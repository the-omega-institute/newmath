\subsection{Nine-coordinate B-star Q6 coupling screen for translation readouts}
\label{sec:bioreality-b-star-q6-coupling-screen}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Record.}
This BioReality record isolates a finite codon-usage screen for the nine synonymous residual coordinates associated with $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$.  The computed coordinate list is
$$
\begin{aligned}
&\mathrm{Arg}_{AGR},\ \mathrm{Ile}_{AUA},\ K_{AAA},\ \mathrm{Leu}_{CUN/UUR},\ \mathrm{Leu}_{UUA/UUG},\\
&\mathrm{Ser}_{UCA/UCG},\ \mathrm{Ser}_{UCR/AGY},\ \mathrm{Thr}_{ACR/ACY},\ f_{3}\text{-stress}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The ambient codon-count space has dimension $61$.  The finite contact used here contains three organism rows: Homo sapiens, Saccharomyces cerevisiae, and Escherichia coli K-12.  The readout in the computed matrix is not a ribosome-profiling, stAI, tAI, or proteomics response.  It is the proxy spectrum given by the tRNA-Leu $CUN$-versus-$UUR$ log-copy contrast.  Thus the result is a codon-usage and tRNA-copy coupling calculation, not a completed translation-survival calculation.

\paragraph{Residual projection.}
The amino-acid quotient is kept explicit.  Each $q_i$ is evaluated only after removing the amino-acid-composition component, so the screen is not allowed to count amino-acid sequence composition as synonymous codon evidence.  For a proxy readout vector $\ell$, the finite-row coupling has the form
$$
\begin{aligned}
A_i = q_i^{T}\Sigma \ell^{\perp},
\end{aligned}
$$
where $\ell^{\perp}$ denotes the readout component after the synonymous projection and $\Sigma$ is the residual count covariance over the finite organism contact.  The null condition for this screen was set as
$$
\begin{aligned}
|A_i| < 0.01 \quad \text{for all nine residual coordinates.}
\end{aligned}
$$
The recorded run did not meet that null condition: all nine coordinates had nonzero entries above the threshold, and the largest absolute coupling was
$$
\begin{aligned}
\max_i |A_i| = 1882464.1106377328.
\end{aligned}
$$
Consequently, the finite proxy matrix shows that the nine-coordinate residual basis is not numerically silent against the Leu tRNA-copy contrast in this three-organism table.

\paragraph{Leu gate contact.}
A separate finite check of the $CUN/UUR$ Leu gate used the same three organism names.  The reported Pearson correlation was $1.0$, and the amino-acid-controlled partial Pearson value was also $1.0$ with $n=3$.  The organism-level log-ratio signs were not uniform:
$$
\begin{aligned}
(1.3514980212896148,\ -0.24869078488829677,\ 1.0086640520051702),
\end{aligned}
$$
with sign pattern
$$
\begin{aligned}
(+1,\ -1,\ +1).
\end{aligned}
$$
This sign reversal is part of the record.  It prevents the three-row table from being read as a universal directionality claim, even though the finite partial correlation in that table is positive.

\paragraph{Survival matrix target.}
The intended survival matrix is a larger object.  For translation readouts $r_j$ such as tAI, stAI, ribosome occupancy, translation efficiency, or protein abundance, the target statistic is
$$
\begin{aligned}
S_{ij}=S_{q_i}(r_j\mid Z),
\end{aligned}
$$
where $Z$ contains amino-acid composition, organism context, GC3, sequence length, and an $M$-density baseline.  A positive survival entry would require a controlled partial-$R^2$ or description-length improvement after this full ablation.  The present computation does not contain such an $S_{ij}$ table.  It contains a proxy coupling matrix $A_i$ against tRNA-Leu copy contrast and a three-organism Leu gate correlation row.  Therefore the correct conclusion is narrower: the proxy contact survives the zero-coupling break condition, while the translation-survival claim remains conditional on organism-matched translation or protein readouts and the declared control set.

\paragraph{Local interpretation boundary.}
No ribosome-mediated translation realization follows from this finite proxy screen alone.  No protein folding, structural order, physical admissibility, biochemical function, pathway role, mechanism claim, or population-level biological law follows from the reported $A_i$ values, from the $n=3$ Pearson value, or from the tRNA gene-copy rows.  tRNA gene copy is treated only as an annotation-level copy-number spectrum; it is not used as tRNA abundance, charging state, ribosome occupancy, translation efficiency, or protein output.  A positive $A_i$ entry is also not promoted to a positive $S_{ij}$ entry.  That promotion would require direct readout rows and the explicit $Z$-controlled survival estimate.

\paragraph{Finite conclusion.}
The finite evidence supports the following bounded statement.  In a $61$-codon count space, after synonymous residualization, the nine $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates have nonzero coupling to the Leu $CUN/UUR$ tRNA-copy proxy in the three-organism contact, with maximum absolute entry $1882464.1106377328$ and all nine entries above the $0.01$ zero-coupling threshold.  The associated Leu gate row has partial Pearson $1.0$ at $n=3$ but has nonuniform organism-level signs.  This is a concrete codon-usage topology contact and a translation-readout screening route.  It is not yet a completed survival matrix for translation realization.
