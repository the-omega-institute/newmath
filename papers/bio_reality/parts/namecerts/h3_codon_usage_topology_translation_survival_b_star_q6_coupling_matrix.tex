\subsection{B-star-Q6 residual survival matrix for translation readouts}
\label{sec:b-star-q6-residual-survival-matrix-translation-readouts}

\paragraph{Record.}
This record isolates a finite matrix question for the nine synonymous-residual coordinates of $B^*_{Q6}$.  The coordinate list used in the computation is
$$
\begin{aligned}
Q=\{&\mathrm{K\_AAA},\mathrm{Arg\_AGR},\mathrm{Ile\_AUA},\mathrm{Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR},\mathrm{Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG},\\
&\mathrm{Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY},\mathrm{Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG},\mathrm{Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY},\mathrm{f3\_stress}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The intended matrix has entries $S_{ij}=S_{q_i}(r_j\mid Z)$, where $q_i\in Q$, $r_j$ is a translation-facing readout, and $Z$ contains amino-acid composition, organism context, $\mathrm{GC3}$, coding-sequence length, and the simpler $M$-density baseline.  The readback demanded by this record is not merely a raw covariance sign.  For each pair $(q_i,r_j)$ one first forms the synonymous projection of the readout weight, denoted $\ell_j^\perp$, then computes a residual covariance coupling
$$
\begin{aligned}
A_{ij}=q_i^{T}\Sigma \ell_j^\perp,
\end{aligned}
$$
and only after the declared controls are removed may one report a survival score such as partial $R^2$ or a description-length improvement.  Thus a nonzero $A_{ij}$ is a contact with the residual geometry, while a positive $S_{ij}$ is the stronger controlled survival statement.

\paragraph{Finite coupling contact.}
The finite proxy run used a $61$-codon count space and loaded three organism rows: $\mathrm{homo\_sapiens}$, $\mathrm{saccharomyces\_cerevisiae}$, and $\mathrm{escherichia\_coli\_k12}$.  The proxy readout was the tRNA-Leu CUN-versus-UUR log-copy spectrum, not a measured ribosome, protein, or direct elongation readout.  Under the declared break rule, the proxy row would have been null if all nine coordinates had $|A_{ij}|<0.01$.  This break condition was not met: all nine entries were nonzero at the proxy level, and the largest absolute coupling was
$$
\begin{aligned}
\max_{i,j}|A_{ij}|=1882464.1106377328.
\end{aligned}
$$
This establishes that the chosen Leu-copy proxy is not orthogonal to the nine-coordinate residual panel in the finite three-organism table.  It does not by itself establish $S_{ij}>0$, since the proxy run did not attach ribosome profiling, observed translation efficiency, stAI, or protein abundance rows with the full control set $Z$.

\paragraph{Modeled translation-weight survival surface.}
A separate rank-powered run used modeled per-codon tAI weights derived from GtRNAdb anticodon copy counts and dos Reis wobble weights.  This run loaded $40$ organism rows and computed a $9\times 9$ modeled translation-readout matrix using the same nine $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinates.  The residualized control design had $24$ control columns, rank $23$, and residual degrees of freedom $17$ after removing $Z$.  The residual coordinate design had rank $9$, so the modeled tAI surface was not the degenerate $n=3$ all-fit surface.  The reported total modeled translation fit was
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{QT}=0.9003849000036579,
\end{aligned}
$$
with positive entries evaluated against the threshold $10^{-12}$.  This is a bounded modeled-translation survival result: it supports a $Q\to T$ matrix for codon-adaptation weights under the stated controls, while still remaining distinct from measured ribosome occupancy, measured elongation speed, proteomics, folding, molecular activity, or phenotype.

\paragraph{Leu gate sanity check.}
The CUN/UUR Leu contact was also checked on the three-organism table.  The partial Pearson statistic against the tRNA-Leu copy ratio was $1.0$ with $n=3$, and the ordinary Pearson value was also $1.0$.  The logged organism-level ratios were
$$
\begin{aligned}
(1.3514980212896148,\;-0.24869078488829677,\;1.0086640520051702),
\end{aligned}
$$
with signs $(1,-1,1)$.  The sign pattern is therefore not uniform across organisms.  This row is useful as a finite contact check, but the sample size and sign reversal prevent reading it as a stable cross-organism direction law.

\paragraph{Local verdict.}
The current evidence supports two bounded statements.  First, the nine $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinates have nonzero finite proxy coupling to the tRNA-Leu CUN-versus-UUR log-copy spectrum in the three-organism table, with maximum absolute coupling $1882464.1106377328$ and threshold $0.01$.  Second, the powered $40$-organism modeled tAI run produces a nondegenerate $9\times 9$ controlled modeled-translation matrix with $R^2_{QT}=0.9003849000036579$, rank-$23$ controls, and $17$ residual degrees of freedom.  These facts justify recording the survival-matrix program as active at the codon-adaptation and modeled-translation boundary.  They do not justify promoting the proxy contact to observed ribosome-mediated translation, nor do they justify protein abundance, folded structure, physical admissibility, biological function, mechanism, phenotype, or universal law.  Any such statement requires its own matched contact and its own controlled statistic.
