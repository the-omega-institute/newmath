\subsection{The $B^{*}_{Q6}$ coupling matrix at the translation boundary}
\label{subsec:bioreality-b-star-q6-coupling-matrix}

\paragraph{Object of the row.}
This record concerns the nine-coordinate $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual panel after quotienting by amino-acid composition.  The coordinates used in the finite computation are
$$
\begin{aligned}
R_{Q6}=\{&\mathrm{Arg}_{AGR},\mathrm{Ile}_{AUA},\mathrm{K}_{AAA},\mathrm{Leu}_{CUN/UUR},\mathrm{Leu}_{UUA/UUG},\\
&\mathrm{Ser}_{UCA/UCG},\mathrm{Ser}_{UCR/AGY},\mathrm{Thr}_{ACR/ACY},f3\text{-}\mathrm{stress}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The carrier is a $9\times K$ survival matrix $S_{ij}$, with rows indexed by these residual coordinates and columns indexed by translation-relevant readouts.  The intended statistic is not the raw covariance alone.  For each coordinate $q_i$ and readout $r_j$, the readout is first projected away from the declared control block $Z$, and the surviving score is the partial $R^2$ or equivalent description-length improvement after controlling for amino-acid composition, organism context, $GC3$, coding-sequence length, and the $M$-density baseline.  In the finite rows currently attached to this packet, $M$ is the support-density baseline formed from the union support of the nine projected $B^{*}_{Q6}$ vectors.

\paragraph{Three-organism proxy coupling.}
The primary finite row is a proxy coupling matrix rather than a completed survival matrix.  It uses codon-count space of dimension $61$ and three organism contacts,
$$
\begin{aligned}
O_3=\{\mathrm{Homo\ sapiens},\mathrm{Saccharomyces\ cerevisiae},\mathrm{Escherichia\ coli\ K12}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The readout in this row is the $\mathrm{tRNA}$-Leu $CUN$ versus $UUR$ log-copy spectrum.  The reported break threshold is $|A_{ij}|<0.01$ for all nine coordinates.  The break condition is not met: all nine entries are nonzero at this threshold, and the largest absolute coupling is
$$
\begin{aligned}
\max_{i,j}|A_{ij}|=1882464.1106377328.
\end{aligned}
$$
This is evidence that the quotient coordinates are not numerically invisible to the selected codon-usage and tRNA-copy proxy.  It is not, by itself, evidence for ribosome-mediated translation, because the row contains neither measured ribosome occupancy nor a $Z$-controlled $S_{ij}$ estimate on a direct translation-efficiency readout.

\paragraph{Leucine gate diagnostic.}
The same three-organism contact gives a sharper diagnostic for the leucine split.  The $CUN/UUR$ codon-use ratio and the tRNA-Leu copy ratio have Pearson correlation $1.0$ in this finite row, and the partial Pearson value after the stated control is also $1.0$ at $n=3$.  The signed log-ratio list is
$$
\begin{aligned}
(1.3514980212896148,\;-0.24869078488829677,\;1.0086640520051702),
\end{aligned}
$$
with signs $(1,-1,1)$.  Thus the row is a positive contact for a small paired table, but it is not a uniform-direction law.  The negative yeast sign is retained as part of the observation rather than normalized away.  The open extension condition is explicit: an eight-organism or larger table must keep the controlled partial correlation above $0.5$ with $p<0.05$ under a nontrivial null before this leucine gate can be treated as a stable cross-organism relation.

\end{aligned}

\paragraph{Local boundary on interpretation.}
The finite rows establish a tiered picture.  First, the three-organism proxy coupling is nonzero above the declared $0.01$ threshold.  Second, modeled tAI rows show strong residual association under the specified controls.  Third, protein-abundance, mRNA-stability, condition, tissue, complement, and candidate-readout tables show that the same nine-coordinate residual panel remains measurable in several downstream statistical projections.  None of these facts alone proves ribosome-mediated translation unless a separate measured translation contact supplies organism- or CDS-matched ribosome occupancy, translation efficiency, or equivalent rows and the declared $Z$-controlled survival statistic is computed.  Likewise, no folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, pathway mechanism, phenotype, or universal biological rule follows from these rows without its own layer-matched measurement and its own statistic.  The present chapter therefore records a bounded translation-adjacent coupling and modeled-readout survival pattern, while keeping measured translation survival and higher biological interpretation as separate empirical obligations.
