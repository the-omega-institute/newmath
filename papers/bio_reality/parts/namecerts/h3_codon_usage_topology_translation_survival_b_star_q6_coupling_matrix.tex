\subsection{A finite coupling matrix for the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ codon-usage residuals}
\label{sec:bioreality-b-star-q6-coupling-matrix}

\paragraph{Object being tested.}
This record concerns the nine-dimensional synonymous-residual coordinate family denoted $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$.  The coordinates used in the finite computation are $K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $Leu_{CUN/UUR}$, $Leu_{UUA/UUG}$, $Ser_{UCR/AGY}$, $Ser_{UCA/UCG}$, $Thr_{ACR/ACY}$, and $f_3$-stress.  The primary table is not a direct protein, structure, or function table.  It is a $9 \times K$ readback matrix whose first realized row family asks whether these residual codon-usage directions have nonzero covariance with a translation-adjacent proxy after the amino-acid quotient has been imposed.  The finite run used a $61$-codon count space and loaded three organism rows, namely Homo sapiens, Saccharomyces cerevisiae, and Escherichia coli K-12.  For the primary contact the proxy readout was the tRNA-Leu $CUN$-versus-$UUR$ log-copy spectrum; true tAI, stAI, ribosome-profiling, and protein-abundance readouts were not present in that particular computation.

\paragraph{Primary finite statistic.}
For each residual coordinate $q_i$ and the proxy readout direction $\ell$, the run computed a coupling entry of the form $A_i = q_i^{T}\Sigma\ell^{\perp}$ on the residual codon-count covariance after projection away from amino-acid composition.  The declared break condition was that all nine coordinates satisfy $|A_i| < 0.01$ against the proxy readout.  That break condition was not met.  The largest absolute entry was $1882464.1106377328$, and all $9$ entries were nonzero above the bookkeeping condition used by the run.  Thus the finite table establishes a bounded codon-usage/topology contact: the nine projected $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ directions are not numerically silent against the tRNA-Leu proxy in this three-organism table.

$$
\begin{aligned}
N_{\mathrm{org}} &= 3,\\
\dim(C) &= 61,\\
\#\{q_i\} &= 9,\\
\max_i |A_i| &= 1882464.1106377328,\\
\#\{i: |A_i| \geq 0.01\} &= 9.
\end{aligned}
$$

This is intentionally weaker than a survival claim.  A positive or large $A_i$ entry is a residual covariance contact, not a controlled survival score $S_i$.  In particular, this row does not assert that a ribosome-mediated translation process has been measured, and it does not identify a protein-output mechanism.

\paragraph{Rank-powered modeled translation comparison.}
A separate modeled-tAI computation supplies a stronger finite comparison while remaining model-bounded.  In that run the same nine residual coordinates were paired with modeled per-codon tAI readouts derived from GtRNAdb anticodon copy counts and dos Reis wobble weights.  The joined panel contained $40$ organisms, the control matrix had $24$ columns with rank $23$, and the residual degrees of freedom after the controls were $17$.  The residualized $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ design had rank $9$, so the fit was not the three-row rank-saturation event.  The modeled translation-readout matrix had shape $9 \times 9$, and the reported aggregate value was $R^2_{QT}=0.9003849000036579$.  The positive-entry threshold was $10^{-12}$, and the degenerate-fit threshold was $0.999999$.

$$
\begin{aligned}
N_{\mathrm{org}} &= 40,\\
\operatorname{rank}(Z) &= 23,\\
\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde X) &= 9,\\
\mathrm{df}_{\perp Z} &= 17,\\
R^2_{QT} &= 0.9003849000036579.
\end{aligned}
$$

This gives evidence that the residual coordinate family can survive against a modeled codon-adaptation readout under amino-acid composition, organism/context, GC3, length, and $M$-density controls.  It remains bounded to the modeled tAI construction: gene-copy-derived tAI is not ribosome occupancy, not measured elongation speed, not proteomics, and not folding or function.

\paragraph{Organism sweep and local ribosome occupancy.}
The modeled-tAI sweep extended the same design over $41$ organisms, with the target of at least $40$ organisms reached.  Across this sweep the $R^2_{QT}$ distribution had minimum $0.1579271837024072$, first quartile $0.26353954700074694$, median $0.33934845811598124$, mean $0.38466555440501393$, third quartile $0.5143951545826744$, and maximum $0.8213457220900503$, using $1000$ permutations.  This shows that the modeled readout is not confined to a single three-organism coincidence, although the organisms are not treated as phylogenetically independent.

A distinct ribosome-occupancy analysis used ordered yeast CDS rows and file-provided codon-window positions.  It joined $5754$ genes after the length check, from $5754$ occupancy genes and $6013$ ordered-CDS genes, with zero length mismatches.  The null shuffled synonymous labels only within the same position/amino-acid bin, and $9$ computed families reached at least $200$ permutations.  With $\lambda_{DL}=0.01$, $DL=2$, and the rule $C_H>0$, the families passing all local-occupancy gates were $K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, and $f_3$-stress.  This is a descriptive codon-window occupancy separation, not a causal elongation model.

\paragraph{Protein-abundance and residual-complement context.}
Several downstream computations show that the same coordinate family also has finite associations with abundance and abundance-adjacent readouts, but these are reported here only as context for the boundary of the coupling claim.  In the six-organism modeled-tAI-complement calculation, the abundance component explained by $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ had complement fractions $d=0.874628721049266$ for Danio rerio, $0.6629960038578532$ for Escherichia coli K-12 MG1655, $0.9400341806302775$ for Gallus gallus, $0.9208949355679455$ for Homo sapiens, $0.8972672018746489$ for Mycobacterium smegmatis str. MC2 155, and $0.21173219572706703$ for Saccharomyces cerevisiae.  The corresponding direct $R^2_{QP}$ values were $0.04613529805760884$, $0.10767526667768725$, $0.03980793595345331$, $0.04454565117192484$, $0.032090091625816136$, and $0.29837624987287115$.  Yeast is therefore the strongest abundance row in this set, whereas several metazoan and bacterial rows leave most of the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ abundance component outside modeled tAI.

A direction-aware candidate ranking then identified organism-specific residual readout supports.  Danio rerio selected measured translation efficiency as $H_o^{\ast}$ with $A_H=0.017594290015070787$, $C_H=0.6573753096184687$, raw product $0.011566051846174291$, score $0.001566051846174291$, and Bonferroni threshold $0.000719994971498518$ on $13140$ joined rows.  Homo sapiens selected complex membership as $H_o^{\ast}$ with $A_H=0.007527199987780841$, $C_H=0.8723071163246565$, and Bonferroni threshold $0.0002646171322365322$ on $18335$ joined rows.  Saccharomyces cerevisiae selected mRNA with $A_H=0.001897605472160837$, $C_H=0.6040571890503664$, and Bonferroni threshold $0.0013014749238591248$ on $5128$ joined rows.  Escherichia coli K-12 MG1655 and Mycobacterium smegmatis str. MC2 155 did not receive a selected $H_o^{\ast}$ in that ranking table.

\paragraph{mRNA stability and abundance decompositions.}
The yeast mRNA half-life row used $5085$ joined rows from the Neymotin table and the same $24$-column control basis with rank $23$.  The $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residuals explained $R^2_{QM}=0.05179429044975085$ for log half-life, while the same join gave $R^2_{QP}=0.2761140373962526$ for protein abundance.  The cosine between $S^{QM}$ and $S^{QP}$ was $0.8040935468384264$, and the dominant mRNA-stability coordinate was $Leu_{CUN/UUR}$.  The mediation-style abundance decomposition reported $R^2_{QP\mid M}=0.24851799532219632$, an absorbed amount $0.027596042074056304$, and mediation fraction $0.09994436477872035$, with a right-tail permutation value $0.000999000999000999$ against a null mean $0.00019674487584783138$ and null $95$th percentile $0.0014571003414947088$.  These numbers are variance-partition statistics; they do not prove an mRNA-decay mechanism.

Condition-specific yeast protein-abundance rows gave $R^2_{QP}=0.3050385538305022$ in SD and $R^2_{QP}=0.3280825603424665$ in YEPD, both with $2209$ shared proteins and dominant coordinate $f_3$-stress.  The condition vectors were nearly aligned, with cosine $0.9993775935182967$ and Pearson correlation $0.9983880256003688$ over $1000$ permutations.  Human tissue-specific protein-abundance rows were smaller but nonzero: Adult B cells had $R^2_{QP}=0.03626117434944674$ with dominant $Arg_{AGR}$ on $9244$ joined rows, Adult Heart had $R^2_{QP}=0.02320896321347296$ with dominant $Ser_{UCR/AGY}$ on $6468$ rows, and Adult Liver had $R^2_{QP}=0.026133456228248052$ with dominant $Ile_{AUA}$ on $8664$ rows.  Pairwise tissue cosines were $0.6883655232187615$, $0.8990093450963941$, and $0.9176793373754919$.

\paragraph{Coordinate-level refinement.}
The per-coordinate reducibility run in yeast used $1258$ joined rows, a five-fold held-out design, $120$ permutations, and a $24$-rank control basis.  It produced all $9$ coordinate rows and identified real measured-abundance sources for $K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $Leu_{CUN/UUR}$, $Leu_{UUA/UUG}$, $Ser_{UCR/AGY}$, $Ser_{ACY/UCG}$, $Thr_{ACR/ACY}$, and $f_3$-stress, with $Ser_{UCA/UCG}$ retained in the table but not listed among the source-passing coordinates.  The $f_3$-stress family decomposition used $1800$ joined rows, $21$ synonymous-family subcoordinates, five folds, and $120$ permutations.  It reported $P_{f3}^2=52.76911713166682$, target norm squared $430.42705205115925$, and $P_{f3}$ fraction of target $0.12259712041843235$.  Nineteen family subcoordinates passed the reported source gate, including $F_{UUU\to UUC}$, $L_{UUA\to UUG}$, $K_{AAA\to AAG}$, $R_{AGA\to AGG}$, and $G_{GGU\to GGG}$.

\paragraph{Local boundary of the claim.}
The finite result supports a specific statement: the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual family has nonzero finite coupling to a tRNA-Leu proxy in the three-organism row, and it has stronger bounded support against modeled tAI and several abundance-adjacent readouts when those separate finite contacts are used.  It does not license a general biological law.  It also does not, by itself, establish measured translation, ribosome-mediated mechanism, protein folding, structural order, physical admissibility, molecular function, pathway function, phenotype, or universality.  Any one of those interpretations requires its own layer-matched contact and its own controlled statistic.  The safe reading is therefore a constrained matrix statement: $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ is not absorbed by amino-acid composition and simple context controls in the finite readouts listed above, but each promoted biological layer remains conditional on the contact that actually measures that layer.
