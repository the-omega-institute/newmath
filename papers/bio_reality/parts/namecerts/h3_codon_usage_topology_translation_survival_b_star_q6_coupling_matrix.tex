\subsection{B-star-Q6 Synonymous Residual Coupling Against Translation-Layer Readbacks}
\label{sec:bioreality-b-star-q6-coupling-matrix}

\paragraph{Claim shape.}
The packet tests whether the nine synonymous residual coordinates of $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ retain measurable contact with translation-adjacent readouts after the amino-acid quotient is imposed.  The coordinate list used in the finite computation is
$\{\mathrm{Arg\_AGR},\mathrm{Ile\_AUA},\mathrm{K\_AAA},\mathrm{Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR},\mathrm{Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG},\mathrm{Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG},\mathrm{Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY},\mathrm{Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY},f3\text{-}\mathrm{stress}\}$.
The primary finite row is classified at the codon-usage-topology layer, not as a completed ribosome-mediated translation claim.  It uses a $61$-codon count space and asks whether the synonymous projection leaves any non-negligible coupling $A_{ij}$ against the proxy readout consisting of the tRNA-Leu CUN-versus-UUR log-copy spectrum.  The declared null for this finite row is that every one of the nine residual coordinates has $|A_{ij}|<0.01$ against that proxy readout.

\paragraph{Finite-row result.}
The three-organism table contains \textit{Homo sapiens}, \textit{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}, and \textit{Escherichia coli} K-12.  In this table the break condition is not met: all nine entries are nonzero above the $0.01$ coupling threshold, and the largest absolute entry is $1{,}882{,}464.1106377328$.  In compact notation the proxy computation reports
$$
\begin{aligned}
\#\{q_i: |A_i|\geq 0.01\} &= 9,\\
\max_i |A_i| &= 1{,}882{,}464.1106377328,\\
\dim(\mathrm{codon\ count\ space}) &= 61.
\end{aligned}
$$
This is evidence that the selected $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ synonymous residual basis is not numerically invisible to the tRNA-Leu copy-ratio proxy on the finite table.  It is not, by itself, a survival matrix $S_{ij}$ for tAI, stAI, ribosome occupancy, protein abundance, or translation efficiency.

\paragraph{Leu gate and sign audit.}
The linked CUN/UUR Leu gate gives the same three organisms a perfect Pearson value and partial Pearson value, both reported as $1.0$ with $n=3$.  The organism-level log-ratio signs are not uniform: the values are $1.3514980212896148$, $-0.24869078488829677$, and $1.0086640520051702$, with signs $+,-,+$.  Thus the finite row is useful as a contact check for the Leu codon/tRNA ratio, but it is rank-small and directionally context-dependent.  The result must not be read as a population estimate or as a universal direction of codon pressure.

\paragraph{Modeled translation-survival comparison.}
A separate powered modeled-tAI computation uses $40$ organisms, the same nine $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates, and $24$ control columns with $\operatorname{rank}(Z)=23$ and residual degrees of freedom $17$.  The modeled per-codon tAI readout has nine projected columns and gives $R^2_{QT}=0.9003849000036579$ with $\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde X)=9$.  This supports a strong modeled-readout association under amino-acid composition, organism context, GC3, length, and M-density controls, while remaining explicitly limited to tRNA gene-copy and dos Reis wobble-weight modeling rather than measured ribosome traffic.  The broader organism sweep reports $41$ organisms, target $40$ reached, and an $R^2_{QT}$ distribution with minimum $0.1579271837024072$, first quartile $0.26353954700074694$, median $0.33934845811598124$, mean $0.38466555440501393$, third quartile $0.5143951545826744$, and maximum $0.8213457220900503$ over $1000$ permutations.

\paragraph{Measured ribosome-position contact.}
The local ribosome-occupancy contact is more direct but narrower.  It joins $5754$ occupancy genes to ordered CDS records after the length check, with $5754$ gene identifiers overlapping the occupancy file and $6013$ ordered CDS genes available.  The position confound is handled by exact file-provided window and codon-index bins together with amino-acid bins, and the synonymous labels are shuffled only within the same bin.  All nine families have at least $200$ within-bin permutations.  With description-length penalty $\lambda_{DL}=0.01$, $DL=2$, and a positive-support rule $C_H>0$, the families passing all gates are $\mathrm{K\_AAA}$, $\mathrm{Arg\_AGR}$, and $f3\text{-}\mathrm{stress}$.  This row supplies a bounded ribosome-occupancy separation statement for local windows, not a causal elongation mechanism.

\paragraph{Mediation and ranking notes.}
In yeast, conditioning the protein-abundance residual on log10 mRNA half-life reduces $R^2_{QP}$ from $0.2761140373962526$ to $0.24851799532219632$, leaving an absorbed component $0.027596042074056304$ and a mediation fraction $0.09994436477872035$.  The permutation mean for this mediation fraction is $0.00019674487584783138$, the permutation $95$th percentile is $0.0014571003414947088$, and the right-tail permutation value is $0.000999000999000999$.  The direction-aware H-candidate ranking is mixed across organisms: \textit{Danio rerio} selects measured translation efficiency as $H_o^{\ast}$ with score $0.001566051846174291$ over $13140$ joined rows, while \textit{E. coli} and \textit{M. smegmatis} have no selected $H_o^{\ast}$.  \textit{Homo sapiens} and \textit{Gallus gallus} select complex membership, and \textit{S. cerevisiae} selects mRNA.  These rankings are descriptive supports for residual directions, not assignments of mechanism.

\paragraph{Local verdict.}
The finite proxy-coupling row is positive for the intended bounded question: the nine-dimensional synonymous residual basis has nonzero contact with the tRNA-Leu copy-ratio proxy on the three-organism table, and downstream modeled or bounded contacts show repeated non-vacuous residual structure under explicit controls.  The stronger survival-matrix statement remains conditional on the readout class.  Modeled tAI and stAI-style rows may support modeled translation-readout survival; local ribosome-occupancy rows support a windowed occupancy separation; protein abundance and mRNA half-life rows support downstream statistical projections.  None of these facts alone establishes ribosome-mediated translation as a causal mechanism, protein folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, phenotype, pathway function, or a universal biological law.  Those conclusions require separate measurements and their own controlled statistics.
