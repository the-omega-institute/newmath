\subsection{B-star-Q6 residual coupling at the translation boundary}
\label{sec:bioreality-b-star-q6-translation-survival-matrix}

\paragraph{Object.}
This record concerns a finite matrix of residual codon-usage coordinates against translation-layer readouts.  The coordinate side consists of the nine synonymous residual directions
$\mathrm{Arg}_{\mathrm{AGR}}$, $\mathrm{Ile}_{\mathrm{AUA}}$, $K_{\mathrm{AAA}}$, $\mathrm{Leu}_{\mathrm{CUN}/\mathrm{UUR}}$, $\mathrm{Leu}_{\mathrm{UUA}/\mathrm{UUG}}$, $\mathrm{Ser}_{\mathrm{UCA}/\mathrm{UCG}}$, $\mathrm{Ser}_{\mathrm{UCR}/\mathrm{AGY}}$, $\mathrm{Thr}_{\mathrm{ACR}/\mathrm{ACY}}$, and the $f_3$ stress coordinate.  The intended survival matrix has entries $S_{ij}=S_{q_i}(r_j\mid Z)$, where $q_i$ is one residual coordinate, $r_j$ is a translation-related readout, and $Z$ includes amino-acid composition, organism context, GC3, coding-sequence length, and the simpler $M$-density baseline.  The finite computation therefore asks whether the residual codon geometry remains informative after the amino-acid quotient and ordinary sequence-composition controls have already been imposed.

\paragraph{Proxy coupling row.}
The directly verified finite proxy contact used three organisms: $\mathrm{Homo\ sapiens}$, $\mathrm{Saccharomyces\ cerevisiae}$, and $\mathrm{Escherichia\ coli}$ K12.  Codon counts were represented in the $61$-codon coding space, and the readout used for the coupling check was the tRNA-Leu CUN-versus-UUR log-copy spectrum.  For this proxy readout, all nine residual coordinates were computed, and the break condition was the null event that every coordinate have $|A_{ij}|<0.01$.  That null event was not met.  The recorded threshold was $0.01$, the number of nonzero matrix entries was $9$, and the largest absolute coupling was $1882464.1106377328$.

$$
\begin{aligned}
\#\{q_i\} &= 9,\\
\#\{\text{codons in the count space}\} &= 61,\\
\max_i |A_{i,\mathrm{Leu}}| &= 1882464.1106377328,\\
\#\{i: A_{i,\mathrm{Leu}}\ne 0\} &= 9,\\
\text{proxy null } \bigl(\forall i\ |A_{i,\mathrm{Leu}}|<0.01\bigr) &= \mathrm{false}.
\end{aligned}
$$

This is a coupling result rather than a survival result.  It shows that the nine residual coordinates are not numerically invisible to the proxy tRNA-Leu spectrum in the three-organism table, but it does not by itself estimate any $S_{ij}$ entry under the full control set $Z$.

\paragraph{Modeled translation-readout survival.}
A separate modeled translation-readout computation used organism-level codon usage, GtRNAdb anticodon copy counts, and dos Reis wobble weights to construct modeled per-codon tAI readouts.  This run used $40$ organism rows and the same nine residual coordinate labels on both the coordinate side and the modeled readout side.  The control design had $24$ columns, rank $23$, and residual degrees of freedom $17$ after residualizing against $Z$.  The powered design retained rank $9$ for the residual coordinate matrix, and the reported threshold for a positive partial-$R^2$ entry was $10^{-12}$.  The resulting modeled translation matrix had shape $9\times 9$ and total modeled $R^2_{QT}=0.9003849000036579$, below the stated degeneracy threshold $0.999999$.

$$
\begin{aligned}
n_{\mathrm{org}} &= 40,\\
\operatorname{rank}(Z) &= 23,\\
\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde X) &= 9,\\
\mathrm{df}_{\mathrm{res}} &= 17,\\
\operatorname{shape}(S^{QT}) &= 9\times 9,\\
R^2_{QT} &= 0.9003849000036579.
\end{aligned}
$$

The forty-organism list includes human, Arabidopsis, grape, zebrafish, budding yeast, multiple bacterial genomes, chicken, and several fungal and cyanobacterial rows.  This broader row count supplies a powered modeled translation contact, but the word modeled is essential: the readout is built from tRNA gene-copy annotations and wobble weights, not from measured ribosome occupancy, elongation speed, matched ribosome profiling, or proteomics.

\paragraph{Leucine gate sanity check.}
The CUN-versus-UUR leucine gate was also checked on the same three-organism finite table used in the proxy contact.  The recorded Pearson correlation was $1.0$, and the recorded partial Pearson correlation with the tRNA-Leu ratio was also $1.0$ for $n=3$.  The organism-level log ratios were $1.3514980212896148$, $-0.24869078488829677$, and $1.0086640520051702$, giving signs $+1$, $-1$, and $+1$.  Thus the sign was not uniform across organisms even though the three-row correlation statistic was maximal.

$$
\begin{aligned}
n &= 3,\\
r_{\mathrm{Pearson}} &= 1.0,\\
r_{\mathrm{partial}} &= 1.0,\\
(\operatorname{sgn}_1,\operatorname{sgn}_2,\operatorname{sgn}_3) &= (+1,-1,+1).
\end{aligned}
$$

This sanity check prevents a directional overreading of the finite row.  The three-row statistic is compatible with a nontrivial codon-usage--tRNA-copy contact, but the observed sign reversal says that no universal positive or negative organism-level direction follows from this row alone.

\paragraph{Interpretation.}
The current finite evidence supports a bounded statement: B-star-Q6 residual coordinates have nonzero contact with a proxy tRNA-Leu spectrum in a three-organism table, and the same nine-coordinate architecture supports a full-rank modeled tAI survival calculation over forty organism rows with $R^2_{QT}=0.9003849000036579$.  Consequently, the amino-acid quotient does not make these residual coordinates automatically disappear from every translation-adjacent readout considered here.  The record is strongest as a codon-usage topology and modeled translation-readout contact; it is not yet a measured ribosome-profiling survival matrix.

\paragraph{Local boundary.}
No measured translation realization follows from the proxy coupling row alone.  tRNA gene-copy counts are not tRNA abundance, tRNA charging, ribosome occupancy, elongation speed, or protein output.  Modeled tAI or stAI weights are translation-layer annotations, not direct ribosome measurements.  Protein folding, structural order, physical admissibility, molecular function, pathway behavior, phenotype, mechanism, and universal biological law are all outside the scope of this finite record unless a separate matched contact supplies the appropriate data and the corresponding controlled statistic is computed.  In particular, a positive or nonzero $A_{ij}$ entry is not promoted to a positive $S_{ij}$ entry without explicit residualization against $Z$ and a partial-$R^2$ or description-length survival estimate.

\paragraph{Status.}
The survival-matrix conjecture is therefore recorded as partially supported at the modeled translation boundary and still open at the measured readout boundary.  The available finite numbers justify reporting the nine-coordinate proxy coupling result, the forty-organism modeled tAI matrix, and the three-organism leucine gate.  They do not justify claiming measured ribosome-mediated survival, protein abundance mediation, folding, function, physical admissibility, mechanism, or universality.
