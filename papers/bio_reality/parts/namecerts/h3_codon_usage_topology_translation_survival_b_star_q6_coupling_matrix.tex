\subsection{Finite proxy coupling for the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ codon-usage residuals}
\label{sec:h3-codon-usage-topology-translation-survival-b-star-q6-coupling-matrix}

\paragraph{Claim surface.}
This record concerns a finite matrix question for the nine synonymous residual coordinates used to test whether the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ codon-usage geometry leaves any detectable trace after amino-acid quotienting and elementary sequence controls. The intended survival matrix has entries $S_{ij}$, where $q_i$ ranges over residual codon-usage coordinates and $r_j$ ranges over translation-related readouts such as tAI, stAI, ribosome occupancy, translation efficiency, or protein abundance. In the fully resolved form, an entry is positive only when the readout residual improves under $q_i$ after controlling for amino-acid composition, organism context, GC3, CDS length, and the $M$-density baseline. The finite computation available here is narrower: it computes a proxy coupling matrix $A_{ij}$ against a tRNA-Leu CUN-versus-UUR log-copy spectrum, not a completed $S_{ij}$ survival matrix.

\paragraph{Residual coordinate carrier.}
The computed residual carrier is the nine-element list
$$
\begin{aligned}
R = \{&\mathrm{Arg\_AGR},\ \mathrm{Ile\_AUA},\ \mathrm{K\_AAA},\ \mathrm{Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR},\\
&\mathrm{Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG},\ \mathrm{Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG},\ \mathrm{Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY},\\
&\mathrm{Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY},\ \mathrm{f3\_stress}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The codon-count ambient space has $61$ codon coordinates. The organism rows loaded for the finite computation are
$$
\begin{aligned}
O = \{\mathrm{Homo\ sapiens},\ \mathrm{Saccharomyces\ cerevisiae},\ \mathrm{Escherichia\ coli\ K12}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the verified row count for the cross-organism contact is $n=3$, and all conclusions below are finite-row statements about these three organism rows. They are not estimates of a population parameter and they do not license a universal direction of effect.

\paragraph{Proxy coupling statistic.}
For a residual coordinate $q_i$ and a proxy readout vector $\ell^{\perp}$, the recorded coupling statistic has the form
$$
\begin{aligned}
A_i = q_i^{T}\Sigma\ell^{\perp},
\end{aligned}
$$
where $\Sigma$ is the residual count covariance after the synonymous projection. The break condition for this proxy test was that every one of the nine coordinates should satisfy $|A_i| < 0.01$. That break condition was not met. The verified scalar summary reports threshold $0.01$, maximum absolute coupling
$$
\begin{aligned}
\max_i |A_i| = 1882464.1106377328,
\end{aligned}
$$
and $9$ nonzero entries among the $9$ residual coordinates. Consequently, the finite proxy matrix is not identically zero at the declared numerical threshold. This supports only the weaker statement that the residual codon coordinates have a nontrivial covariance contact with the selected tRNA-Leu copy-number contrast in the three-row table.

\paragraph{Leu gate finite row.}
A related CUN-versus-UUR Leu contact was computed on the same organism set. The recorded log-ratio row is
$$
\begin{aligned}
(1.3514980212896148,\ -0.24869078488829677,\ 1.0086640520051702),
\end{aligned}
$$
with signs $(1,-1,1)$. Hence the sign is not uniform across organisms. The same finite contact reports Pearson correlation $1.0$ and amino-acid-composition-controlled partial Pearson correlation $1.0$ with $n=3$. These values are exact row-level outputs of the three-organism table, but the sample size is too small to treat the coefficient as a stable cross-organism law. No p-value is present in the verified row, and no eight-organism extension has been supplied.

\paragraph{Survival status.}
The proxy coupling result does not equal a survival result. The intended survival entry is a controlled score $S_{ij}$, for example a partial-$R^2$ or description-length-improvement score, computed after the full control set
$$
\begin{aligned}
Z = (&\mathrm{amino\ acid\ composition},\ \mathrm{organism},\ \mathrm{GC3},\\
&\mathrm{CDS\ length},\ \mathrm{M\mbox{-}density\ baseline}).
\end{aligned}
$$
The present finite data do not contain a completed table of $S_{ij}$ values, do not report partial-$R^2$ entries, and do not provide a sign classification for each $S_{ij}$ survival entry. The available computation instead records that the weaker proxy matrix $A_i$ has all nine entries nonzero and at least one entry above the $0.01$ magnitude threshold. Therefore the survival conjecture remains open: it is not refuted by the proxy break condition, but it is also not established as a positive translation-readout survival matrix.

\paragraph{Local interpretation boundary.}
The biological scope is deliberately narrow. Codon-usage rows and tRNA gene-copy rows can test a sequence-level or annotation-level coupling between synonymous residuals and a tRNA-copy contrast. They do not by themselves measure ribosome occupancy, translation efficiency, tRNA charging, protein abundance, folding, structural order, physical admissibility, biological function, mechanism, phenotype, or a universal biological rule. A ribosome-level translation claim would require a direct ribosome-profiling, TE, tAI, or stAI readout joined to the same coding sequences and evaluated under the declared controls. A protein-output claim would require matched proteomics rows, and any claim about structure, physical admissibility, function, mechanism, or universality would require a separate measurement contact designed for that layer. The current chapter therefore records a nonzero finite proxy coupling in the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual coordinate system, while keeping the stronger survival and higher-layer biological claims unasserted.
