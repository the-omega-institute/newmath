\subsection{B-star-Q6 residual coupling against translation-adjacent readouts}\label{subsec:b-star-q6-residual-coupling-translation-adjacent-readouts}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Object of record.}
This record isolates the finite-row status of the proposed $B^*_{Q6}$ survival matrix before any promotion to a realized translation claim. The intended matrix has entries $S_{ij}=S_{q_i}(r_j\mid Z)$, where $q_i$ ranges over nine synonymous residual coordinates and $r_j$ ranges over translation-related readouts such as tAI, stAI, ribosome occupancy, translation efficiency, and protein abundance. The control family is
$$
\begin{aligned}
Z=\{&\text{amino-acid composition},\ \text{organism context},\ \mathrm{GC3},\\
&\text{coding-sequence length},\ \text{and the simpler }M\text{-density baseline}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
For a true survival entry, the readout must be directly attached and the residual improvement must be estimated after these controls. The present finite row does not yet contain such an $S_{ij}$ matrix. It records a proxy covariance coupling matrix $A_{ij}$ computed from codon-count data and a tRNA-Leu copy-number spectrum, together with the exact boundary that prevents this proxy from being read as translation survival.

\paragraph{Finite carrier.}
The computed residual basis contains the following nine coordinates:
$$
\begin{aligned}
&\mathrm{Arg\_AGR},\ \mathrm{Ile\_AUA},\ \mathrm{K\_AAA},\ \mathrm{Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR},\\
&\mathrm{Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG},\ \mathrm{Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG},\ \mathrm{Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY},\\
&\mathrm{Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY},\ \mathrm{f3\_stress}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The ambient codon-count space has $61$ coding codons. The cross-organism table contains exactly three organisms: \textit{Homo sapiens}, \textit{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}, and \textit{Escherichia coli} K-12. The run identifier for the coupling-matrix computation is $\mathrm{4a783972e74b}$, with verification time $2026$-$06$-$02$ $22{:}48{:}45+00{:}00$.

\paragraph{Proxy coupling statistic.}
For the finite proxy readout, the readout direction is the tRNA-Leu CUN-versus-UUR log-copy spectrum. This is a codon-usage and tRNA-copy contact, not a ribosome-occupancy, tAI, stAI, translation-efficiency, or protein-output measurement. The computed relation is of the form
$$
\begin{aligned}
A_{ij}=q_i^{T}\Sigma\ell_j^{\perp},
\end{aligned}
$$
where $\ell_j^{\perp}$ denotes the synonymous projection of the readout weight after the amino-acid quotient has been kept explicit. The registered break condition for the proxy was that the finite row would be null if all nine coordinates had $|A_{ij}|<0.01$ against the proxy readout. That break condition was not met: all $9$ entries were nonzero above the reporting threshold, and the largest absolute entry was
$$
\begin{aligned}
\max_{i,j}|A_{ij}|=1882464.1106377328.
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the finite codon-count/tRNA-copy contact supports the narrower statement that the selected $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinates are not all orthogonal to this proxy Leu copy-number spectrum under the encoded synonymous projection. It does not compute $S_{ij}$, and it does not establish a positive partial $R^2$ for any direct translation readout.

\paragraph{Leu CUN/UUR finite-row gate.}
A linked three-organism check for the Leu CUN/UUR route used the same organism list and recorded run identifier $\mathrm{675fdddb3187}$. In that row, the Pearson correlation and the amino-acid-controlled partial Pearson statistic were both reported as $1.0$ with $n=3$:
$$
\begin{aligned}
r&=1.0,\\
\rho_{\mathrm{partial}}&=1.0,\\
n&=3.
\end{aligned}
$$
The organism-level log-ratio signs were not uniform. The observed log-ratio vector was
$$
\begin{aligned}
(1.3514980212896148,\ -0.24869078488829677,\ 1.0086640520051702),
\end{aligned}
$$
with sign vector
$$
\begin{aligned}
(+1,\ -1,\ +1).
\end{aligned}
$$
Consequently $\mathrm{sign\_uniform}=\mathrm{false}$. The value $r=1.0$ is therefore a finite three-row alignment, not a stable population estimate and not a universal directionality claim. The sign reversal is part of the observed finite table and must be retained in any later enlargement to at least eight organisms.

\paragraph{Survival condition and current status.}
The conjectural survival matrix would be supported only if at least one pair $(q_i,r_j)$ had
$$
\begin{aligned}
S_{ij}>0
\end{aligned}
$$
after amino-acid composition, organism context, $\mathrm{GC3}$, coding-sequence length, and the $M$-density baseline are all included, and after the simpler-codon baseline has been ablated. Equivalently, one would need a positive Z-controlled partial-$R^2$ or description-length improvement on a direct translation-related readout. The finite row currently lacks that statistic. Its positive information is the non-null proxy coupling $A_{ij}$ against the tRNA-Leu CUN-versus-UUR log-copy spectrum; its negative information is that this proxy cannot be upgraded into translation survival without organism-matched tAI, stAI, ribosome-profiling, translation-efficiency, proteomics, or equivalent readout rows.

\paragraph{Local boundary.}
No realized translation-layer survival follows from the proxy coupling alone. No folding statement, structural-order statement, physical-admissibility statement, biological-function statement, ribosome-mediated mechanism, protein-output statement, or universal biological law follows from these finite rows. tRNA gene-copy ratio is kept as a genome-annotation readout and is not identified with tRNA abundance, tRNA charging, ribosome occupancy, translation efficiency, or mature protein abundance. Matched mRNA abundance, when present, is only a covariate for translation-efficiency residualization. A future positive $S_{ij}$ entry must be computed from a direct readout under the full control family $Z$; it cannot be inferred from identifier routing, codon-window geometry, proxy covariance, or the three-organism Leu gate.
