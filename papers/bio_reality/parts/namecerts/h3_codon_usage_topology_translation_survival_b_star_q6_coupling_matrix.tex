\subsection{B-star-Q6 residual coordinates and bounded translation readbacks}\label{subsec:b-star-q6-residual-translation-readbacks}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Question.}
This chapter records a finite test of whether the nine synonymous residual coordinates in the $B^*_{Q6}$ panel remain visible after the amino-acid quotient and elementary sequence controls have been imposed. The carrier is a rectangular survival array $S_{ij}$, with rows indexed by residual codon-usage coordinates $q_i$ and columns indexed by translation-related readouts $r_j$. The intended statistic is not a raw correlation. For each pair $(q_i,r_j)$ one first separates the readout component orthogonal to the controlled description $Z$, then evaluates whether adding $q_i$ improves the residual description of $r_j$ beyond amino-acid composition, organism context, GC3, coding-sequence length, and the simpler $M$-density baseline. In symbols the bounded readout calculation has the form
$$
\begin{aligned}
A_{ij} &= q_i^{\top}\Sigma \ell_j^{\perp},\\
S_{ij} &= S_{q_i}(r_j\mid Z),\\
Z &= (\hbox{amino-acid composition},\ \hbox{organism},\ \hbox{GC3},\ \hbox{length},\ \hbox{$M$-density baseline}).
\end{aligned}
$$
Here $A_{ij}$ is a covariance coupling against a projected readout direction, while $S_{ij}$ is the controlled survival score. The two quantities have different evidential force: a nonzero $A_{ij}$ is a geometric contact with a readout proxy, whereas a positive $S_{ij}$ requires the full residualized estimation problem.

\paragraph{Residual coordinate panel.}
The finite computation uses the nine $B^*_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates
$$
\begin{aligned}
q_i \in \{&\mathrm{Arg}_{AGR},\ \mathrm{Ile}_{AUA},\ \mathrm{K}_{AAA},\ \mathrm{Leu}_{CUN/UUR},\ \mathrm{Leu}_{UUA/UUG},\\
&\mathrm{Ser}_{UCA/UCG},\ \mathrm{Ser}_{UCR/AGY},\ \mathrm{Thr}_{ACR/ACY},\ f_3\hbox{-stress}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The codon-count ambient space has $61$ sense-codon coordinates before the synonymous projections are applied. The primary finite-row coupling run, identified by run id $4a783972e74b$, loaded three organism contexts: Homo sapiens, Saccharomyces cerevisiae, and Escherichia coli K12. Against the tRNA-Leu CUN-versus-UUR log-copy spectrum used as a proxy readout, the break condition was that every one of the nine residual coordinates satisfy $|A_{ij}|<0.01$. That break condition was not met. All nine entries were nonzero above the stated threshold, and the largest reported absolute coupling was
$$
\begin{aligned}
\max_{i,j}|A_{ij}| = 1882464.1106377328.
\end{aligned}
$$
This finite row therefore rejects the narrow null that the whole $B^*_{Q6}$ residual panel is invisible to this proxy spectrum at threshold $0.01$. It does not, by itself, establish a translation-survival entry, because the proxy row is codon usage joined to tRNA gene-copy information rather than a measured ribosome, translation-efficiency, or protein-output readout.

\paragraph{Modeled translation-weight survival surface.}
A separate modeled-readout computation, run id $09209aa516a0$, expands the panel to $40$ organism rows and constructs per-codon modeled tAI readouts from GtRNAdb anticodon copy counts with dos Reis wobble weights. The same nine residual coordinates appear both as the $B^*_{Q6}$ coordinate panel and as the projected modeled-readout columns, giving a $9\times 9$ surface. The control design had $24$ control columns, rank $23$, and residual degrees of freedom $17$ after residualization. The residualized $B^*_{Q6}$ design had rank $9$. The reported powered-rank check gave
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{QT} &= 0.9003849000036579,\\
\operatorname{rank}(Z) &= 23,\\
\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde X) &= 9,\\
\operatorname{df}_{\mathrm{res}} &= 17.
\end{aligned}
$$
The degenerate-fit threshold used for refusing rank-saturated interpretation was $0.999999$, so the reported $R^2_{QT}$ is below that saturation cutoff. The modeled tAI contact is therefore a stronger bounded readout than the three-organism proxy coupling: it supplies a controlled, non-saturated $9\times 9$ modeled translation-weight surface over $40$ organisms. Its interpretation remains limited to modeled adaptation weights derived from tRNA gene-copy annotations and wobble rules. It is not ribosome profiling, not measured elongation speed, not protein abundance, and not a direct protein-realization measurement.

\paragraph{Leucine CUN/UUR finite-row gate.}
The Leu CUN-versus-UUR subtest records a three-organism finite-row association between the codon-usage ratio and the tRNA-Leu copy ratio. In run id $675fdddb3187$, the loaded organisms were again Homo sapiens, Saccharomyces cerevisiae, and Escherichia coli K12. The reported Pearson value was $1.0$, and the reported partial Pearson value was also $1.0$ at $n=3$. The organism-level log-ratio signs were not uniform:
$$
\begin{aligned}
(1.3514980212896148,\ -0.24869078488829677,\ 1.0086640520051702)
\end{aligned}
$$
with signs $(1,-1,1)$. This row is useful as a small finite contact showing that the Leu split is not silently erased by the projection, but it cannot be read as a population estimate. With only three organisms, the value $1.0$ records a finite-row alignment and not a universal direction, a stable biological law, or a mechanistic statement about tRNA abundance or charging.

\paragraph{Local verdict.}
The chapter supports the following bounded conclusion. The nine-coordinate $B^*_{Q6}$ residual panel is not absorbed by the simplest amino-acid-quotiented codon-description layer when tested against the available proxy and modeled translation-weight contacts. In the three-organism proxy row, all nine coupling entries exceed the absolute threshold $0.01$, with maximum absolute coupling $1882464.1106377328$. In the forty-organism modeled tAI row, the controlled $9\times 9$ surface has $R^2_{QT}=0.9003849000036579$ with residual design rank $9$ and residual degrees of freedom $17$, below the stated rank-saturation refusal threshold $0.999999$. These facts justify retaining the $B^*_{Q6}$ panel as a translation-layer candidate coordinate system and justify reporting modeled translation-weight survival as a bounded result.

\paragraph{Scope boundary.}
No stronger biological conclusion is licensed here. tRNA gene-copy counts are annotation summaries, not tRNA abundance, charging, ribosome occupancy, or elongation-speed measurements. Modeled tAI or stAI weights are adaptation-weight readbacks, not ribosome-profiling observations. The present finite rows do not establish measured translation survival from ribo-seq, do not establish protein abundance, do not establish folding or structural order, do not establish physical admissibility, do not establish molecular function or pathway behavior, and do not establish a universal biological law. Each such conclusion would require its own organism-matched contact and its own controlled statistic. In particular, a positive proxy coupling $A_{ij}$ is not promoted to a positive measured survival score $S_{ij}$ unless the declared $Z$-controlled partial-$R^2$ or description-length calculation has actually been carried out on the corresponding translation readout.
