\subsection{B-star-Q6 codon residuals against translation-layer contacts}
\label{sec:b-star-q6-translation-survival-matrix}

\paragraph{Question.}
This record isolates a finite BioReality question about whether the nine $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates leave any detectable trace on translation-related readouts after the amino-acid quotient and ordinary sequence controls have been imposed. The carrier is a $9 \times K$ matrix whose rows are the residual coordinates $q_i$ and whose columns are readouts $r_j$ such as tAI, stAI, ribosome occupancy, translation efficiency, or protein abundance when organism-matched contacts are present. The intended statistic is not a raw correlation. For each pair $(q_i,r_j)$, the readout weight is first projected to the synonymous residual component $\ell_j^{\perp}$, the proxy coupling is measured as $A_{ij}=q_i^{T}\Sigma\ell_j^{\perp}$ on residual count covariance, and the survival entry $S_{ij}$ is then estimated only after the control block $Z$ has absorbed amino-acid composition, organism context, GC3, coding-sequence length, and the simpler $M$-density baseline.

\paragraph{Finite row.}
The attached finite computation used a $61$-codon count space and computed all nine declared residual coordinates:
$$
\begin{aligned}
Q = \{&\mathrm{Arg}_{AGR},\ \mathrm{Ile}_{AUA},\ K_{AAA},\ \mathrm{Leu}_{CUN/UUR},\ \mathrm{Leu}_{UUA/UUG},\\
&\mathrm{Ser}_{UCA/UCG},\ \mathrm{Ser}_{UCR/AGY},\ \mathrm{Thr}_{ACR/ACY},\ f_3\text{-stress}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The organisms loaded for the finite row were $\mathrm{Homo\ sapiens}$, $\mathrm{Saccharomyces\ cerevisiae}$, and $\mathrm{Escherichia\ coli\ K12}$. Against the proxy readout consisting of the tRNA-Leu CUN-versus-UUR log-copy spectrum, the break rule was: declare the coupling contact null if every row obeys $|A_{ij}|<0.01$. That break condition was not met. The threshold was $0.01$, all $9$ rows had nonzero proxy-coupling entries, and the largest observed absolute entry was
$$
\begin{aligned}
\max_{i,j}|A_{ij}| = 1882464.1106377328.
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the finite row supports only the bounded statement that the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ synonymous residual basis is not numerically silent against this tRNA-copy proxy spectrum in the three-organism table.

\paragraph{CUN/UUR Leu contact.}
A separate organism-level contact tested the Leu CUN-versus-UUR codon-usage ratio against the corresponding tRNA-Leu copy-number ratio in the same three organisms. The reported Pearson coefficient was $1.0$, and the amino-acid-composition-controlled partial Pearson value was also $1.0$ with $n=3$. The organism log-ratio signs were not uniform:
$$
\begin{aligned}
(1.3514980212896148,\ -0.24869078488829677,\ 1.0086640520051702),\qquad (+,-,+).
\end{aligned}
$$
The status of that finite gate was passed for the three loaded organisms, but the sign reversal is part of the data and prevents any reading of the row as a universal direction law. The numerical coincidence $r=1.0$ at $n=3$ is a finite-table fact, not a population estimate.

\paragraph{What is established.}
The established item is a codon-usage and tRNA-copy contact at the codon-usage-topology layer. It shows that the nine residual coordinates can be evaluated in a $61$-codon count space, that the synonymous projection is explicit, and that the proxy matrix $A_{ij}$ contains nonzero entries above the stated $0.01$ threshold in the three-organism row. It also records a Leu CUN/UUR association with Pearson $1.0$ and partial Pearson $1.0$ under the finite control used for that contact. These are finite numerical contacts with concrete row identities and concrete scalar outputs.

\paragraph{What remains unproved.}
The survival matrix $S_{ij}$ itself is not yet present in this finite row. A positive proxy coupling $A_{ij}$ is not the same thing as a positive survival score $S_{ij}$. The survival claim requires direct translation-layer or downstream readout rows, such as organism-matched tAI or stAI weights, ribosome profiling, translation-efficiency estimates, or matched proteomics, followed by the stated control fit against $Z$. In particular, the current row does not contain a Z-controlled partial-$R^2$ or description-length-improvement estimate for any $(q_i,r_j)$ entry. It therefore cannot certify the informal claim that some $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual coordinate satisfies $S_q(r\mid Z)>0$.

\paragraph{Local non-promotion rule.}
No ribosome-mediated translation conclusion follows from the current proxy coupling alone. No protein folding, structural order, physical admissibility, biochemical function, mechanism, pathway evidence, or universal biological law follows from the codon-usage and tRNA-copy rows. tRNA gene-copy ratio is treated here only as an organism-level annotation spectrum; it is not identified with tRNA abundance, charging, ribosome occupancy, translation efficiency, or protein output. Matched mRNA abundance, when supplied, belongs inside the control set for translation-efficiency residualization and does not itself become evidence for translation realization. A protein-abundance row, when supplied, would be a separate downstream measurement and would still require its own controlled statistic.

\paragraph{Break condition.}
For the proxy contact actually computed, the null rule was falsified because the maximum absolute coupling exceeded $0.01$ and the matrix had $9$ nonzero entries. For the intended translation-survival conjecture, the open break rule is stricter: if all $S_{ij}\leq 0$ after amino-acid composition, organism context, GC3, length, and $M$-density baseline controls, then the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual packet is absorbed by the controlled minimal model for the tested translation readouts. The present data do not trigger that survival-level break rule, because the required $S_{ij}$ matrix has not yet been computed.

\paragraph{Readout status.}
The correct reading is therefore narrow but nonempty. The $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates have a concrete finite interaction with a tRNA-Leu CUN/UUR proxy spectrum across three organisms, and the Leu gate has a passed finite contact with $n=3$, Pearson $1.0$, partial Pearson $1.0$, and signs $(+,-,+)$. The survival-matrix conjecture remains open until matched translation readouts are attached and the declared controlled survival statistic is reported entry by entry.
