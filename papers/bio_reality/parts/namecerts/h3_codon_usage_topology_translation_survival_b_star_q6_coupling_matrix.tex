\subsection{Finite Codon-Usage Coupling for the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ Translation-Survival Packet}\label{sec:bioreality-b-star-q6-codon-usage-coupling}\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Coordinate packet.}
The record concerns the nine synonymous residual coordinates used to test whether $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ leaves any residue on translation-facing observables after the amino-acid quotient and ordinary sequence covariates have been imposed.  The codon-count ambient space has $61$ codon coordinates, and the finite computation extracted the following residual list:
$$
\begin{aligned}
R = \{&\mathrm{Arg\_AGR},\ \mathrm{Ile\_AUA},\ \mathrm{K\_AAA},\ \mathrm{Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR},\ \mathrm{Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG},\\
&\mathrm{Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG},\ \mathrm{Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY},\ \mathrm{Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY},\ \mathrm{f3\_stress}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The intended survival matrix has entries $S_{ij}=S_{q_i}(r_j\mid Z)$, where $q_i$ ranges over these nine residual coordinates and $r_j$ ranges over translation-related readouts such as codon-adaptation weights, ribosome occupancy, translation efficiency, and protein abundance.  The control block is
$$
\begin{aligned}
Z=(\mathrm{amino\ acid\ composition},\ \mathrm{organism},\ \mathrm{GC3},\ \mathrm{length},\ \mathrm{M\mbox{-}density\ baseline}).
\end{aligned}
$$
A positive survival entry therefore requires more than a raw codon-usage association: it requires a controlled partial-$R^2$ or description-length improvement after the simpler codon-density baseline has been removed.

\paragraph{Finite rows actually attached.}
The realized table at this stage is a proxy coupling computation, not a completed $S_{ij}$ survival matrix.  The loaded organism set is
$$
\begin{aligned}
O=\{\mathrm{Homo\ sapiens},\ \mathrm{Saccharomyces\ cerevisiae},\ \mathrm{Escherichia\ coli\ K12}\},
\end{aligned}
$$
so the organism count is $n=3$.  The available readout is the $\mathrm{tRNA}$-Leu $\mathrm{CUN}$-versus-$\mathrm{UUR}$ log-copy spectrum, used as a codon-usage and tRNA-copy contact rather than as a direct ribosome or protein-output measurement.  For each residual coordinate $q_i$, the computation formed a proxy coupling coefficient $A_{ij}$ against this log-copy spectrum on residual codon-count covariance.  The break rule for this proxy table was
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{null}\quad \Longleftrightarrow \quad |A_{ij}|<0.01\ \mathrm{for\ all\ nine\ residual\ coordinates}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The observed result did not meet that null rule: all $9$ proxy entries were nonzero relative to the $0.01$ threshold, and the largest absolute coefficient was
$$
\begin{aligned}
\max_i |A_{ij}| = 1882464.1106377328.
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the finite codon-count/tRNA-copy row shows that the nine-coordinate residual packet is not numerically silent against this particular proxy spectrum.  This is a codon-usage topology contact with a tRNA-copy readback; it is not yet a translation-survival estimate.

\paragraph{Leucine gate row.}
A separate finite row tests the $\mathrm{Leu}\ \mathrm{CUN}/\mathrm{UUR}$ relation across the same three organisms.  The reported Pearson coefficient is $1.0$, and the amino-acid-composition-controlled partial Pearson statistic is also $1.0$ with $n=3$.  The organism-level log-ratio list is
$$
\begin{aligned}
(1.3514980212896148,\ -0.24869078488829677,\ 1.0086640520051702),
\end{aligned}
$$
with signs
$$
\begin{aligned}
(+1,\ -1,\ +1).
\end{aligned}
$$
Consequently the sign is not uniform across organisms.  The row supports a finite paired-table association for the three loaded organisms, while simultaneously recording that the direction cannot be promoted to a stable cross-organism direction from these rows alone.  The extension probe asks for at least eight curated organisms and a non-trivial null with partial correlation greater than $0.5$ and $p<0.05$; those eight-organism rows and the associated $p$-value are not present in this finite record.

\paragraph{Survival matrix status.}
The intended matrix entry can be written as a controlled survival score
$$
\begin{aligned}
S_{ij} = \Delta\mathrm{DL}(r_j; q_i\mid Z) \quad \mathrm{or}\quad S_{ij}=R^2(r_j\sim Z+q_i)-R^2(r_j\sim Z),
\end{aligned}
$$
where the exact numerical convention is fixed by the survival-estimation pipeline.  The attached computation supplies $A_{ij}$-type proxy coupling, not the controlled $S_{ij}$ entries.  Direct tAI or stAI weights are named as admissible translation-layer contacts, and ribosome-profiling, matched mRNA abundance, and proteomics contacts are also named as possible readout/control sources.  However, the current finite record contains no organism-matched ribosome-profiling table, no completed $\mathrm{TE}=\mathrm{RPF}/\mathrm{mRNA}$ residualization, no matched protein-abundance regression, and no reported partial-$R^2$ survival matrix entry after the full control block $Z$ and the $M$-density baseline ablation.

\paragraph{Local interpretation.}
The positive result in this chapter is therefore narrow.  In the $61$-codon count space, the nine $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates were computed, the three-organism proxy contact was loaded, and the tRNA-Leu $\mathrm{CUN}$-versus-$\mathrm{UUR}$ log-copy spectrum produced $9$ nonzero proxy coupling entries with maximum absolute magnitude $1882464.1106377328$ under the stated $0.01$ break threshold.  The Leu gate additionally reports Pearson $1.0$ and partial Pearson $1.0$ at $n=3$, together with the non-uniform sign pattern $(+1,-1,+1)$.  These facts justify keeping the translation-survival packet open, because the residual coordinates are visibly coupled to the available proxy readout.  They do not justify declaring a positive survival score $S_{ij}>0$.

\paragraph{Local limitation.}
No translation realization, ribosome-mediated mechanism, protein folding, structural order, physical admissibility, biological function, biochemical pathway conclusion, or universal biological law follows from the rows above.  A tRNA gene-copy ratio is not treated as tRNA abundance, tRNA charging, ribosome occupancy, translation efficiency, or protein output.  A proxy coupling coefficient $A_{ij}$ is not treated as a controlled survival entry $S_{ij}$.  Any future positive survival claim must attach organism-matched translation readouts and compute the declared control-adjusted statistic with amino-acid composition, organism context, GC3, length, and $M$-density baseline controls in place.  Any claim about protein output, folding, physical admissibility, biological function, mechanism, or universality would require its own separately measured contact and its own finite statistic.
