\subsection{B-star Q6 residual coupling against translation-side proxy data}
\label{sec:bioreality-b-star-q6-translation-survival-coupling-matrix}

\paragraph{Finite object.}
This record concerns the nine residual coordinates of the $B^\ast_{Q6}$ codon-usage quotient after the amino-acid quotient has been imposed. The finite coordinate list is
$$
\begin{aligned}
R = \{&\mathrm{Arg\_AGR},\ \mathrm{Ile\_AUA},\ \mathrm{K\_AAA},\ \mathrm{Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR},\ \mathrm{Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG},\\
&\mathrm{Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG},\ \mathrm{Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY},\ \mathrm{Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY},\ \mathrm{f3\_stress}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The codon-count ambient space has $61$ sense-codon coordinates. The attached run, $4a783972e74b$, computed the nine synonymous residual coordinates across the three organism rows
$$
\begin{aligned}
O = \{\mathrm{Homo\ sapiens},\ \mathrm{Saccharomyces\ cerevisiae},\ \mathrm{Escherichia\ coli\ K12}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The measured readout in this row is not a direct ribosome or protein-output measurement. It is the organism-level $\mathrm{tRNA}$-Leu CUN-versus-UUR log-copy spectrum, used as a proxy contact for checking whether the $B^\ast_{Q6}$ residual coordinates are numerically orthogonal to an observed translation-side annotation.

\paragraph{Coupling statistic.}
For a residual coordinate $q_i$ and a readout weight $\ell_j$, the finite coupling row is represented by
$$
\begin{aligned}
A_{ij} = q_i^{T}\Sigma\ell_j^{\perp},
\end{aligned}
$$
where $\ell_j^{\perp}$ denotes the synonymous projection of the readout weight and $\Sigma$ is the residual codon-count covariance on the finite organism table. The break condition for this particular proxy run was that all nine residual coordinates satisfy $|A_{ij}| < 0.01$. That condition was not met. The threshold was $0.01$, the number of nonzero entries was $9$, and the maximum absolute coupling was
$$
\begin{aligned}
\max_i |A_{ij}| = 1882464.1106377328.
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the finite proxy row does not support the null assertion that the nine residual coordinates are all numerically silent against the tRNA-Leu CUN-versus-UUR copy-spectrum contact. This is a codon-usage-topology contact result, not a completed survival matrix on direct translation readouts.

\paragraph{Leucine gate row.}
A related finite row, $675fdddb3187$, evaluates the CUN/UUR leucine gate against tRNA-Leu copy ratio on the same three organism names. In that row the reported Pearson value is $1.0$, and the partial Pearson value after the recorded control is also $1.0$ with $n = 3$. The organism-level log-ratio values are
$$
\begin{aligned}
(1.3514980212896148,\ -0.24869078488829677,\ 1.0086640520051702),
\end{aligned}
$$
with signs
$$
\begin{aligned}
(+1,\ -1,\ +1).
\end{aligned}
$$
The sign is therefore not uniform across the three organisms, even though the finite three-row correlation statistic is reported as passed. This matters for interpretation: the row records a finite association pattern and a sign reversal, not a universal direction for CUN/UUR behavior.

\paragraph{Survival matrix target.}
The intended survival object is a matrix $S_{ij} = S_{q_i}(r_j\mid Z)$, where $q_i$ ranges over the nine residual coordinates and $r_j$ ranges over translation-relevant readouts such as tAI, stAI, ribosome occupancy, translation efficiency, and protein abundance. The declared control family is
$$
\begin{aligned}
Z = (\mathrm{amino\ acid\ composition},\ \mathrm{organism},\ \mathrm{GC3},\ \mathrm{length},\ \mathrm{M\mbox{-}density\ baseline}).
\end{aligned}
$$
A positive entry would require a controlled partial-$R^2$ or description-length improvement after the amino-acid composition, organism context, GC3, length, and simpler-codon baseline have been included. The present finite evidence does not contain such an $S_{ij}$ matrix. It contains a nonzero proxy coupling matrix $A_{ij}$ against a tRNA-copy spectrum and a separate three-organism leucine ratio contact.

\paragraph{Local negative boundary.}
The allowed conclusion is narrow. The current rows show that the nine $B^\ast_{Q6}$ residual coordinates are not all below the stated $0.01$ proxy-coupling threshold against the tRNA-Leu CUN-versus-UUR log-copy contact, and they show a three-organism CUN/UUR leucine association with nonuniform signs. They do not establish ribosome-mediated translation, protein abundance, folding, physical admissibility, biological function, mechanism, or a universal biological law. tRNA gene-copy counts are not tRNA abundance or charging measurements. A positive $A_{ij}$ entry is not a positive $S_{ij}$ survival entry. Direct survival requires organism-matched codon usage, a translation readout such as stAI or ribosome profiling, the stated controls $Z$, and an explicit controlled survival statistic.

\paragraph{Present status.}
The finite proxy coupling test is informative because it blocks the strongest silence null for the available tRNA-copy contact: all nine residual coordinates were nonzero at the recorded threshold, and the largest magnitude was $1882464.1106377328$. The translation-survival claim itself remains conditional on attaching direct readout rows and computing $S_{ij}$ under the declared controls. The mathematically recorded content of this chapter is therefore a bounded finite-row contact: a nonzero residual-coupling spectrum on three organisms, together with a local rule that no higher biological layer is inferred from that spectrum without its own matched measurement.
