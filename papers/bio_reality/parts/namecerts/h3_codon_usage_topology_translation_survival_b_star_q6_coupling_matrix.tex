\subsection{B-star-Q6 residual coupling against translation-layer readouts}
\label{sec:bioreality-b-star-q6-translation-survival-coupling-matrix}

\paragraph{Object and coordinate basis.}
This record concerns the nine-coordinate synonymous residual basis denoted here by $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$.  The finite carrier is a matrix whose rows are the residual coordinates
$$
\begin{aligned}
q_i \in \{&K\_AAA,\ Arg\_AGR,\ Ile\_AUA,\ Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR,\ Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG,\\
&Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY,\ Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG,\ Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY,\ f3\_stress\},
\end{aligned}
$$
and whose columns are translation-facing readouts or controlled proxies.  Each row is evaluated after quotienting out amino-acid composition, with the operational control block $Z$ containing amino-acid proportions, organism context where applicable, $GC3$, coding-sequence length, and the $M$-density baseline.  The numerical question is whether a residual coordinate that is invisible to the amino-acid quotient remains visible to a translation-related readout after these controls are imposed.

\paragraph{Primary finite-row contact.}
The direct coupling computation used a $61$-codon count space and three organisms: \emph{Homo sapiens}, \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}, and \emph{Escherichia coli} K-12.  The readout in that finite table was not ribosome occupancy, protein abundance, measured elongation, or a tAI/stAI row.  It was the tRNA-Leu CUN-versus-UUR log-copy spectrum, so the statistic is a proxy coupling $A_{ij}$ rather than a survival score $S_{ij}$.  With threshold $0.01$, all nine $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual rows had nonzero proxy entries and at least one entry exceeded the break threshold.  The recorded maximum absolute coupling was
$$
\begin{aligned}
\max_{i,j}|A_{ij}| = 1882464.1106377328,
\end{aligned}
$$
with $9$ nonzero entries.  Thus the finite proxy break condition, namely that all nine coordinates have $|A_{ij}|<0.01$, was false.  This is a real codon-usage and tRNA-copy contact, but it is deliberately weaker than a direct translation-survival matrix.

\paragraph{Survival statistic.}
The intended controlled survival score has the form
$$
\begin{aligned}
S_{ij} = S_{q_i}(r_j\mid Z),
\end{aligned}
$$
where $r_j$ may be modeled tAI/stAI, ribosome occupancy, translation efficiency, or protein abundance when the corresponding organism-matched rows exist.  The finite proxy statistic reports $A_{ij}=q_i^{T}\Sigma \ell_j^{\perp}$ on residual count covariance, whereas a positive survival call requires a controlled partial-$R^2$ or description-length improvement after $Z$ and after the simpler-codon-baseline ablation.  Consequently the three-organism proxy result supports non-absorption of the residual basis by the codon/tRNA-copy surface, but it does not itself certify $S_{ij}>0$ for ribosome-mediated translation.

\paragraph{Modeled translation readout.}
A separate powered modeled-tAI computation enlarged the organism panel to $40$ organisms.  The same nine residual coordinates were used, the modeled readout matrix had shape $9\times 9$, the control matrix had $24$ columns, $\operatorname{rank}(Z)=23$, and the residual degrees of freedom after controls were $17$.  The residualized coordinate matrix had rank $9$.  On this modeled per-codon tAI readout, the recorded statistic was
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{QT}=0.9003849000036579.
\end{aligned}
$$
The positive-entry threshold for the partial-$R^2$ surface was $10^{-12}$, and the degeneracy guard was $0.999999$.  Because $R^2_{QT}$ lies below the degeneracy guard while the row rank is full, this computation is a powered modeled-readout survival result, not the three-organism rank-saturated artifact.  It remains bounded by its input: modeled tAI from GtRNAdb anticodon copy counts and dos Reis wobble weights is not measured ribosome occupancy, not measured elongation speed, not proteomics, and not protein folding.

\paragraph{Organism sweep.}
The modeled-tAI organism sweep extended the same coordinate/control design to $41$ organisms, with $40$ target organisms reached.  Across the sweep, the $R^2_{QT}$ distribution was
$$
\begin{aligned}
\min &= 0.1579271837024072,\\
q_{25} &= 0.26353954700074694,\\
\operatorname{median} &= 0.33934845811598124,\\
\operatorname{mean} &= 0.38466555440501393,\\
q_{75} &= 0.5143951545826744,\\
\max &= 0.8213457220900503.
\end{aligned}
$$
The sweep used $1000$ permutations and an $n\geq 500$ gene gate for included organisms.  These values show that the modeled adaptation interface is not confined to one organism, but the organisms are not phylogenetically independent and the readout remains modeled rather than measured ribosome traffic.

\paragraph{Local ribosome occupancy contact.}
A measured ribosome-occupancy contact was evaluated at the codon-window level rather than as the full $9\times K$ survival matrix.  The joined occupancy table had $5754$ genes in overlap, $5754$ genes after the length check, $6013$ ordered CDS genes, and no length mismatches.  Position was controlled by exact file-provided window/codon-index bins together with amino-acid bins; within each bin, occupancy was demeaned and synonymous labels were shuffled for the null.  Nine families were computed, all nine had at least $200$ within-bin permutations, and the description-length gate used $DL=2$ with $\lambda=0.01$.  The families passing the full $C_H>0$ and $\lambda DL$ gate were $K\_AAA$, $Arg\_AGR$, and $f3\_stress$.  This is the strongest measured translation-layer contact in the record, but it is local and descriptive: it reports position-controlled codon-choice association with occupancy separation, not a causal elongation mechanism.

\paragraph{Cross-organism Leu gate.}
The Leu CUN/UUR finite contact used the same three organisms as the primary proxy table.  The partial Pearson correlation between $\log(CUN/UUR)$ usage and the tRNA-Leu copy ratio was $1.0$ at $n=3$.  The recorded log-ratios were $1.3514980212896148$, $-0.24869078488829677$, and $1.0086640520051702$, with signs $+,-,+$.  Therefore the sign was not uniform across organisms.  The entry is a useful finite contact showing that the CUN/UUR direction is context-sensitive, but $n=3$ is not a population estimate and does not define universal directionality.

\paragraph{Protein-abundance and downstream controls.}
Protein-abundance contacts show that the same residual coordinates can be read against abundance surfaces, but these are downstream and must not be confused with direct translation survival.  In the modeled-tAI complement study, the abundance component explained by $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ had complement fractions $d$ equal to $0.874628721049266$ in zebrafish, $0.6629960038578532$ in \emph{E. coli} K-12 MG1655, $0.9400341806302775$ in chicken, $0.9208949355679455$ in human, $0.8972672018746489$ in \emph{Mycobacterium smegmatis}, and $0.21173219572706703$ in yeast.  The corresponding modeled-translation-mediated fractions $m$ were $0.12537127895073397$, $0.3370039961421468$, $0.05996581936972245$, $0.07910506443205445$, $0.10273279812535108$, and $0.788267804272933$.  Yeast had the largest modeled translation-aligned abundance component in this table, while several vertebrate and bacterial entries retained most of their $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ abundance component outside the modeled tAI direction.

\paragraph{Condition, tissue, and mRNA-stability surfaces.}
Yeast condition-specific protein abundance gave $R^2_{QP}=0.3050385538305022$ in SD and $0.3280825603424665$ in YEPD on $2209$ shared proteins, with $f3\_stress$ dominant in both conditions and pairwise stability $S_{QP}$ cosine $0.9993775935182967$. For mRNA stability, yeast gave $R^2_{QM}=0.05179429044975085$ on $5085$ rows with dominant $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$ and permutation $p=0.000999000999000999$; \emph{E. coli} gave $R^2_{QM}=0.02298556640056076$ on $2720$ rows with dominant $Arg\_AGR$ and cosine $-0.7784923375569599$ against the abundance surface; human gave $R^2_{QM}=0.010503670362051522$ on $16165$ rows with dominant $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$ and cosine $0.6351183883924848$.  The yeast abundance mediation audit reported $R^2_{QP}=0.2761140373962526$, $R^2_{QP\mid M}=0.24851799532219632$, absorbed fraction $0.027596042074056304$, mediation fraction $0.09994436477872035$, permutation null mean $0.00019674487584783138$, null $p95=0.0014571003414947088$, and right-tail permutation $p=0.000999000999000999$.

\paragraph{Coordinate and family decomposition.}
A per-coordinate reducibility audit in yeast used $n=1258$, base $n=1800$, five folds, $120$ permutations, and $Z$-basis rank $24$.  The table contained all nine coordinates and found at least one coordinate with held-out support against the permutation gate.  A separate $f3\_stress$ family decomposition used $21$ synonymous-family subcoordinates, $n=1800$, base $n=4759$, five folds, $120$ permutations, and $Z$-basis rank $24$.  The full $f3\_stress$ projection had norm squared $52.76911713166682$ and accounted for $0.12259712041843235$ of the target projection energy, with target norm squared $430.42705205115925$.  Nineteen family subcoordinates passed the recorded source gate, including $K\_AAA\_to\_AAG$, $R\_AGA\_to\_AGG$, $I\_AUU\_to\_AUA$, $T\_ACU\_to\_ACG$, and multiple two-fold third-position families.  This decomposition localizes part of the abundance-facing signal inside synonymous families rather than treating $f3\_stress$ as an indivisible scalar.

\paragraph{Verdict.}
The finite evidence supports a bounded conclusion: the nine $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates are not absorbed by amino-acid composition, simple codon baselines, and context controls on the measured/proxy surfaces reported here.  The primary three-organism table establishes nonzero proxy coupling to a tRNA-Leu CUN/UUR copy spectrum, the powered modeled-tAI panel gives a full-rank controlled modeled survival surface with $R^2_{QT}=0.9003849000036579$, the organism sweep shows broad modeled-readout signal, and the local ribosome-occupancy contact supplies a measured position-controlled codon-window association for $K\_AAA$, $Arg\_AGR$, and $f3\_stress$.  The correct interpretation is therefore a translation-layer contact record with explicit gradations: proxy tRNA-copy coupling, modeled tAI survival, local ribosome-occupancy separation, protein-abundance association, mRNA-stability association, and downstream mediation audits are distinct statistical surfaces.  No translation mechanism, protein folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, pathway effect, phenotype, or universal biological law follows from these numbers unless another layer-matched measurement and its own controlled statistic are supplied.
