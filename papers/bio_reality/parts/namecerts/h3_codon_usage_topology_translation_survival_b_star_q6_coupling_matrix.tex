\subsection{Finite proxy coupling for the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ translation-survival matrix}
\label{sec:b-star-q6-codon-usage-proxy-coupling}

\paragraph{Object of the record.}
This chapter records the finite-row state of the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual-coordinate packet for codon-usage topology and its bounded route toward translation readouts.  The intended survival matrix has entries $S_{ij}=S_{q_i}(r_j\mid Z)$, where $q_i$ ranges over nine synonymous residual coordinates and $r_j$ ranges over translation-related readouts such as tAI, stAI, ribosome occupancy, translation efficiency, or protein abundance.  The present verified row is weaker and more specific: it computes a proxy coupling matrix $A_{ij}$ against the tRNA-Leu CUN-versus-UUR log-copy spectrum, not a fully controlled survival matrix against direct ribosome or protein contacts.

The nine residual coordinates actually computed are
$$
\begin{aligned}
q_i \in \{&\mathrm{Arg\_AGR},\ \mathrm{Ile\_AUA},\ \mathrm{K\_AAA},\ \mathrm{Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR},\ \mathrm{Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG},\\
&\mathrm{Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG},\ \mathrm{Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY},\ \mathrm{Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY},\ \mathrm{f3\_stress}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
They were evaluated in the $61$-codon sense after imposing the amino-acid quotient through the synonymous projection.  The organism list in the finite row is
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathcal O = \{\mathrm{homo\_sapiens},\ \mathrm{saccharomyces\_cerevisiae},\ \mathrm{escherichia\_coli\_k12}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The corresponding break threshold for the proxy contact was $|A_{ij}|<0.01$ for every residual coordinate.  That break condition was not met: all nine proxy entries were nonzero at the stated threshold, and the largest absolute coupling was
$$
\begin{aligned}
\max_{i,j}|A_{ij}| = 1882464.1106377328.
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the finite computation supports the narrower statement that the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ synonymous residual geometry is not numerically silent against this Leu tRNA-copy proxy in the three-organism row.

\paragraph{Proxy coupling and survival are separate statistics.}
For a residual coordinate $q_i$ and a readout weight $\ell_j$, the coupling calculation has the schematic form
$$
\begin{aligned}
A_{ij}=q_i^{\mathsf T}\Sigma\ell_j^{\perp},
\end{aligned}
$$
where $\ell_j^{\perp}$ denotes the synonymous component of the readout weight after the amino-acid quotient and $\Sigma$ is the residual count covariance used by the finite contact.  This is not yet the survival score
$$
\begin{aligned}
S_{ij}=S_{q_i}(r_j\mid Z),
\end{aligned}
$$
because $S_{ij}$ requires a direct translation or protein readout and an explicit control set
$$
\begin{aligned}
Z=(\mathrm{amino\ acid\ composition},\ \mathrm{organism},\ \mathrm{GC3},\ \mathrm{length},\ \mathrm{M\ density\ baseline}).
\end{aligned}
$$
The recorded proxy contact therefore gives an obstruction to the claim that the residual coordinates are trivially zero against every translation-adjacent spectrum, but it does not establish a positive partial $R^2$, a description-length improvement, or any entry $S_{ij}>0$ for tAI, stAI, ribosome occupancy, translation efficiency, or protein abundance.

\paragraph{Leu CUN/UUR row.}
The linked finite Leu gate gives a sharper three-organism diagnostic.  For the CUN/UUR Leu comparison, the recorded log-ratio row is
$$
\begin{aligned}
(1.3514980212896148,\ -0.24869078488829677,\ 1.0086640520051702),
\end{aligned}
$$
with signs
$$
\begin{aligned}
(+1,\ -1,\ +1).
\end{aligned}
$$
The sign is therefore not uniform across the three organisms.  The same finite row reports Pearson correlation $1.0$ and partial Pearson correlation $1.0$ with $n=3$ for the Leu CUN/UUR codon-usage and tRNA-Leu copy-ratio contact.  Since $n=3$, this is best read as an exact finite-row alignment for the attached organisms, not as a population estimate, a stable biological direction, or a universal cross-organism law.  The open extension criterion asks for at least eight curated organisms and a nontrivial null before the Leu gate is treated as cross-organism support rather than a small-row phenomenon.

\paragraph{Local interpretation.}
The finite result is positive only at the proxy-coupling level.  It says that the nine $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates have observable numerical contact with the tRNA-Leu CUN-versus-UUR log-copy spectrum in the three-organism table, with $9$ nonzero entries above the $0.01$ break threshold and maximum absolute coupling $1882464.1106377328$.  It does not say that ribosomes realize the residual coordinates, that tRNA gene copy equals tRNA abundance or charging, or that the sign of a coupling is a universal biological direction.  A genuine survival entry would require matched codon usage, direct translation readouts such as ribosome profiling or tAI/stAI weights, and the full control set $Z$, followed by a partial-$R^2$ or description-length calculation.

\paragraph{Boundary of the claim.}
No translation-survival conclusion follows from the proxy matrix alone.  No folding statement, physical-admissibility statement, functional statement, mechanistic statement, protein-output statement, or universality statement follows from this chapter.  Those stronger readings require their own organism-matched measurements and their own controlled statistics.  The current chapter only fixes the finite proxy geometry: $61$ codon-count coordinates, $9$ computed synonymous residuals, $3$ organisms, a $0.01$ coupling break threshold, a failed all-small break condition, $9$ nonzero proxy entries, and a maximum absolute proxy coupling of $1882464.1106377328$.
