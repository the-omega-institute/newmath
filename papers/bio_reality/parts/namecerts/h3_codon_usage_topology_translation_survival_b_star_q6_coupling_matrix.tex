\subsection{The B-star-Q6 translation-survival coupling matrix}
\label{sec:b-star-q6-translation-survival-coupling-matrix}

\paragraph{Finite carrier.}
The record considered here is the nine-coordinate residual panel obtained after imposing the amino-acid quotient on codon-count space. The codon-count ambient space has $61$ sense-codon coordinates, and the retained residual coordinates are
$$
\begin{aligned}
Q = \{&\mathrm{K\_AAA},\ \mathrm{Arg\_AGR},\ \mathrm{Ile\_AUA},\ \mathrm{Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR},\ \mathrm{Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG},\\
&\mathrm{Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY},\ \mathrm{Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG},\ \mathrm{Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY},\ \mathrm{f3\_stress}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
For a readout family $R$, the intended survival matrix has entries $S_{ij}=S_{q_i}(r_j\mid Z)$, where $Z$ contains amino-acid composition, organism context, $\mathrm{GC3}$, coding-sequence length, and the $M$-density baseline. The local finite-row question is whether any coordinate in $Q$ remains informative for a translation-facing readout after these controls and after the simpler-codon baseline have been accounted for. The matrix is therefore not a free biological interpretation of codon geometry; it is a controlled residual statistic indexed by explicit coordinates, readouts, and row sets.

\paragraph{Observed proxy coupling.}
The direct finite computation for this claim loaded three organism rows, namely $\mathrm{Homo\ sapiens}$, $\mathrm{Saccharomyces\ cerevisiae}$, and $\mathrm{Escherichia\ coli\ K12}$. The readout used in that computation was a proxy spectrum, the tRNA-Leu $\mathrm{CUN}$-versus-$\mathrm{UUR}$ log-copy contrast, not ribosome profiling, measured elongation, protein abundance, or a mature translation-efficiency table. With the threshold $0.01$, the declared break condition was that all nine couplings satisfy $|A_{ij}|<0.01$. This break condition was not met: all nine entries were nonzero above the threshold in the recorded coupling matrix, and the maximum absolute coupling was
$$
\begin{aligned}
\max_{i,j}|A_{ij}| &= 1882464.1106377328,\\
\#\{(i,j): |A_{ij}|\geq 0.01\} &= 9.
\end{aligned}
$$
This establishes a finite proxy contact between the amino-acid-quotiented residual coordinates and a tRNA-copy spectrum. It does not by itself establish any positive survival entry $S_{ij}>0$, since survival requires the controlled partial-$R^2$ or description-length calculation against a declared translation readout.

\paragraph{Rank-powered modeled translation panel.}
A separate modeled tAI panel supplies a stronger, but still bounded, translation-facing calculation. In that panel, $40$ organisms were loaded, the control matrix had $24$ columns, $\operatorname{rank}(Z)=23$, and the residual degrees of freedom after $Z$ were $17$. The residualized nine-coordinate design had $\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde X)=9$, avoiding the three-row saturation pathology. The modeled tAI readout used per-codon tAI derived from GtRNAdb anticodon copy counts and dos Reis wobble weights, then projected onto the same nine residual coordinates. The resulting controlled fit reported
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{QT} &= 0.9003849000036579,\\
\dim(Q) &= 9,\\
\dim(R_{\mathrm{modeled\ tAI}}) &= 9.
\end{aligned}
$$
The positivity threshold for partial $R^2$ entries was $10^{-12}$. This is evidence for modeled translation-readout survival under the stated rank conditions, but it remains a modeled tAI readout. It is not measured ribosome occupancy, measured elongation speed, proteomics, folding, or a pathway claim.

\paragraph{Three-row Leu gate.}
The Leu $\mathrm{CUN}/\mathrm{UUR}$ gate gives a useful diagnostic for the same boundary. Across the same three organisms used by the proxy contact, the partial Pearson association with the tRNA-Leu ratio was recorded as $1.0$ on $n=3$ rows. The organism-level log ratios were
$$
\begin{aligned}
(1.3514980212896148,\ -0.24869078488829677,\ 1.0086640520051702),
\end{aligned}
$$
with signs $(1,-1,1)$. Thus the finite association is not a uniform directionality statement. The row count is too small to support a population-level law, and the sign reversal explicitly prevents reading the proxy gate as a universal biological orientation.

\paragraph{Protein-facing and complement checks.}
The same coordinate family has been tested against downstream protein-abundance and complement readbacks, which are useful for bounding the interpretation of the translation claim. In the six-organism modeled-tAI complement calculation, the complement fraction $d$ for $P_Q^{\perp T}$ was $0.874628721049266$ in zebrafish, $0.6629960038578532$ in $\mathrm{E.\ coli\ K12\ MG1655}$, $0.9400341806302775$ in chicken, $0.9208949355679455$ in human, $0.8972672018746489$ in $\mathrm{Mycobacterium\ smegmatis}$, and $0.21173219572706703$ in yeast. The corresponding direct $R^2_{QP}$ values were $0.04613529805760884$, $0.10767526667768725$, $0.03980793595345331$, $0.04454565117192484$, $0.032090091625816136$, and $0.29837624987287115$. These numbers show that residual protein-abundance projections exist, but the complement vector $a_\perp$ is a coordinate readback of a statistical residual, not a biochemical mechanism.

\paragraph{Condition, tissue, and mRNA stability rows.}
The yeast condition comparison used $2209$ shared proteins, with $2517$ YEPD joined rows and $2442$ SD joined rows before taking the shared set. The controlled protein-abundance fits had $R^2_{QP}=0.3280825603424665$ in YEPD and $R^2_{QP}=0.3050385538305022$ in SD, with $\mathrm{f3\_stress}$ dominant in both conditions and a cosine of $0.9993775935182967$ between the two $S^{QP}$ profiles. Human tissue protein-abundance panels gave $R^2_{QP}=0.03626117434944674$ in adult B cells with $\mathrm{Arg\_AGR}$ dominant, $0.02320896321347296$ in adult heart with $\mathrm{Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY}$ dominant, and $0.026133456228248052$ in adult liver with $\mathrm{Ile\_AUA}$ dominant. The tissue-profile cosines were $0.6883655232187615$, $0.8990093450963941$, and $0.9176793373754919$ over the three pairwise comparisons. In the yeast mRNA half-life panel, the Neymotin join used $5085$ rows, with a reported CDS join hit rate $0.9156038419146592$; the mRNA-stability fit had $R^2_{QM}=0.05179429044975085$, while the same-join protein-abundance comparison had $R^2_{QP}=0.2761140373962526$ and cosine $0.8040935468384264$. Its permutation test used $1000$ permutations and reported $p=0.000999000999000999$ for $R^2_{QM}$. These associated rows show that the nine-coordinate residual family can be read against several bounded omics surfaces, but each surface has its own contact conditions and its own restrictions.

\paragraph{Direction-aware candidate ranking.}
The non-translation residual ranking used the regularized score $A_H C_H - \lambda D_H$ with $\lambda=0.01$ and $D_H=\log_2(1+n_{\mathrm{col}})$. In zebrafish, $\mathrm{measured\_te}$ was selected with $A_H=0.017594290015070787$, $C_H=0.6573753096184687$, $A_HC_H=0.011566051846174291$, score $0.001566051846174291$, $n=13140$, and Bonferroni threshold $0.000719994971498518$. The next zebrafish candidate was $\mathrm{mrna}$ with score $0.000734588577977589$ and threshold $0.000587830414454545$. Human selected $\mathrm{complex\_member}$ as the top candidate with $A_H=0.007527199987780841$, $C_H=0.8723071163246565$, and threshold $0.0002646171322365322$, although its regularized score was negative. Yeast selected $\mathrm{mrna}$ with $A_H=0.001897605472160837$, $C_H=0.6040571890503664$, $n=5128$, and Bonferroni threshold $0.0013014749238591248$. These rankings are descriptive support diagnostics. They do not transform abundance, mRNA, localization, or complex-membership readouts into translation mechanisms.

\paragraph{Local verdict.}
The finite-row status is therefore mixed but informative. The exact three-organism coupling calculation supports nonzero proxy coupling: the null condition $|A_{ij}|<0.01$ for all nine coordinates is false, and the largest absolute coupling is $1882464.1106377328$. The rank-powered modeled tAI calculation supports a controlled modeled-translation surface with $R^2_{QT}=0.9003849000036579$ on $40$ organisms and nondegenerate residual rank. What is not established by the exact proxy matrix is a measured ribosome-profiling survival matrix, a measured translation-efficiency matrix, or a protein-output mediation matrix. No conclusion about translation in the ribosome-measured sense, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, mechanism, phenotype, or universality follows from these numbers unless a separate layer-matched contact supplies the relevant rows and the corresponding controlled statistic is computed.

\paragraph{Refusal of overextension.}
The chapter records a bounded coordinate-to-readout result. tRNA gene-copy ratios are not treated as tRNA abundance or charging. Modeled tAI is not treated as ribosome occupancy. Protein abundance is not treated as folding, function, or pathway evidence. A direct $Q\to P$ association is not treated as translation-conditioned mediation unless a surviving $Q\to T$ step is present on the same joined rows. The survival notation $S_{ij}$ is reserved for controlled partial-$R^2$ or description-length improvement after the declared covariates and baseline ablation; the proxy coupling $A_{ij}$ is reported as coupling only.
