\subsection{B-star-Q6 residual coupling against translation-layer contacts}
\label{subsec:bioreality-b-star-q6-residual-coupling}

\paragraph{Finite-row object.}
This record treats the nine residual coordinates of $B^*_{Q6}$ as a finite codon-usage geometry after the amino-acid quotient has been imposed. The computed coordinate list is
$$
\begin{aligned}
&\mathrm{Arg}_{AGR},\ \mathrm{Ile}_{AUA},\ K_{AAA},\ \mathrm{Leu}_{CUN/UUR},\ \mathrm{Leu}_{UUA/UUG},\\
&\mathrm{Ser}_{UCA/UCG},\ \mathrm{Ser}_{UCR/AGY},\ \mathrm{Thr}_{ACR/ACY},\ f_3\text{-stress}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The ambient codon-count space has $61$ sense-codon coordinates. The finite computation loaded three organism rows, namely Homo sapiens, Saccharomyces cerevisiae, and Escherichia coli K-12. The observed readout used in the completed row is not a direct ribosome, tAI, stAI, or protein-abundance measurement. It is the tRNA-Leu $CUN$-versus-$UUR$ log-copy spectrum, used as a proxy contact for testing whether the residual coordinates have a nontrivial translation-adjacent coupling before any stronger survival statement is made.

\paragraph{Proxy coupling statistic.}
For each residual coordinate $q_i$, the row computes a covariance coupling against the projected proxy readout. In the notation of this chapter, the coupling entry is
$$
\begin{aligned}
A_i = q_i^{T}\Sigma \ell^{\perp},
\end{aligned}
$$
where $\ell^{\perp}$ denotes the readout weight after synonymous projection. The declared break threshold for this finite proxy row is $|A_i| < 0.01$ for every one of the nine coordinates. That break condition was not met. The computation recorded $9$ nonzero entries, with maximum absolute coupling
$$
\begin{aligned}
\max_i |A_i| = 1882464.1106377328,
\end{aligned}
$$
and the coupling threshold was $0.01$. Thus the finite proxy row shows that the nine-coordinate residual package is not numerically invisible to the Leu tRNA-copy contrast under this particular finite contact. This statement is only about the proxy coupling matrix entry $A_i$; it is not yet a controlled survival score $S_{ij}$ on a direct translation readout.

\paragraph{Leu CUN/UUR cross-organism row.}
A second finite row isolates the Leu $CUN/UUR$ route across the same three organisms. The observed log-ratio vector is
$$
\begin{aligned}
(1.3514980212896148,\ -0.24869078488829677,\ 1.0086640520051702),
\end{aligned}
$$
with sign pattern
$$
\begin{aligned}
(+1,\ -1,\ +1).
\end{aligned}
$$
The recorded Pearson value is $1.0$, and the amino-acid-composition-controlled partial Pearson value is also $1.0$ with $n=3$. This is a finite-row contact, not a population estimate. The sign pattern is explicitly nonuniform across organisms, so the row cannot be read as a universal direction of Leu codon preference or as a universal translation law. The value $1.0$ is reported as the exact statistic of the three loaded rows, while the nonuniform signs and small row count keep the interpretation local.

\paragraph{Survival matrix status.}
The intended survival matrix is a $9 \times K$ matrix $S_{ij}=S_{q_i}(r_j\mid Z)$, where the readouts $r_j$ may include tAI, stAI, ribosome occupancy, translation efficiency, or protein abundance, and where the control set is
$$
\begin{aligned}
Z=(\text{amino-acid composition},\ \text{organism context},\ GC3,\ \text{length},\ M\text{-density baseline}).
\end{aligned}
$$
The completed finite row does not yet contain such an $S_{ij}$ matrix. It contains a proxy coupling row $A_i$ and a three-organism Leu ratio contact. A positive proxy coupling is weaker than positive survival: survival requires an explicit partial-$R^2$ or description-length improvement after all controls in $Z$ and after ablation of the simpler codon-density baseline. Consequently, this chapter records a live translation-layer test interface rather than a confirmed survival entry.

\paragraph{Local exclusion boundary.}
No translation-realization claim follows from the proxy row alone. The tRNA gene-copy contact is not treated as tRNA abundance, tRNA charging, ribosome occupancy, translation efficiency, or protein output. The mRNA contact, when present, is only a transcription-layer control for translation-efficiency residualization. Ribosome profiling would provide a bounded translation readout only after organism-matched rows are attached and the controlled statistic is computed. Protein abundance would be a downstream readout, not evidence of folding or function by itself. Nothing in the finite rows establishes protein folding, structural order, physical admissibility, biochemical function, mechanism, or a global biological law. Each of those stronger layers would require its own separate measurement contact and its own controlled statistic.

\paragraph{Current readback.}
The finite evidence supports the following bounded statement. In a $61$-coordinate sense-codon count space, all nine listed $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinates were computed across three organisms, and the proxy Leu tRNA-copy readout produced a nonzero coupling pattern with $\max_i |A_i|=1882464.1106377328$, exceeding the $0.01$ null threshold. The accompanying Leu $CUN/UUR$ row has Pearson $1.0$ and partial Pearson $1.0$ at $n=3$, with log-ratio signs $(+1,-1,+1)$. These rows justify keeping the survival-matrix probe open, because the residual coordinates are not annihilated by the available proxy contact. They do not justify replacing the still-missing controlled survival matrix by the proxy coupling row.
