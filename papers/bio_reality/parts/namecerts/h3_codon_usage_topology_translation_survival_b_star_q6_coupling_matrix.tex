\subsection{B-star Q6 coupling against translation-survival readouts}\label{subsec:b-star-q6-coupling-translation-survival}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite carrier.}
The record studies a nine-coordinate residual codon-usage carrier against translation-related readouts.  The coordinate side is the post-amino-acid-quotient list
$\{\mathrm{Arg}_{\mathrm{AGR}},\mathrm{Ile}_{\mathrm{AUA}},\mathrm{K}_{\mathrm{AAA}},\mathrm{Leu}_{\mathrm{CUN}/\mathrm{UUR}},\mathrm{Leu}_{\mathrm{UUA}/\mathrm{UUG}},\mathrm{Ser}_{\mathrm{UCA}/\mathrm{UCG}},\mathrm{Ser}_{\mathrm{UCR}/\mathrm{AGY}},\mathrm{Thr}_{\mathrm{ACR}/\mathrm{ACY}},f3\text{-}\mathrm{stress}\}$.
These nine rows are evaluated in the $61$-codon count space after the synonymous projection has removed the amino-acid-composition component.  The intended survival matrix is $S_{ij}=S_{q_i}(r_j\mid Z)$, where $q_i$ ranges over the nine residual coordinates, $r_j$ ranges over translation-facing readouts such as tAI, stAI, ribosome occupancy, translation efficiency, or protein abundance, and $Z$ contains amino-acid composition, organism context, GC3, transcript or coding-sequence length, and the simpler $M$-density baseline.  The matrix entry is not merely a raw covariance: a positive entry requires a controlled partial-$R^2$ or description-length improvement after the full control set has been applied.

\paragraph{Observed finite row.}
The verified computation loaded three organism rows: Homo sapiens, Saccharomyces cerevisiae, and Escherichia coli K-12.  In this finite table the available readout was not a direct translation measurement.  It was the tRNA-Leu CUN-versus-UUR log-copy spectrum, used as a proxy contact for codon-usage geometry against a tRNA-copy summary.  The coupling statistic was
$$
\begin{aligned}
A_{ij} &= q_i^{T}\Sigma\ell_j^{\perp},
\end{aligned}
$$
where $\ell_j^{\perp}$ denotes the readout weight after synonymous projection and $\Sigma$ is the residual count covariance.  The break threshold for this proxy coupling run was $|A_{ij}|<0.01$ for all nine rows.  That null condition was not met: all $9$ residual coordinates had nonzero proxy-coupling entries, and the largest absolute entry was $1{,}882{,}464.1106377328$.  Thus the finite contact does show that the nine-coordinate residual basis is not numerically silent against the chosen tRNA-Leu copy-ratio proxy.  It does not by itself produce a positive survival entry $S_{ij}>0$.

\paragraph{Leucine gate row.}
A second finite row isolates the Leu CUN/UUR coordinate against the organism-level tRNA-Leu copy-ratio contact.  The same three organisms were used.  The reported Pearson value was $1.0$, and the reported partial Pearson value after the stated control was also $1.0$ with $n=3$.  The organism-level log-ratio signs were not uniform: the log ratios were $1.3514980212896148$, $-0.24869078488829677$, and $1.0086640520051702$, with signs $+1,-1,+1$.  This is a small finite-row association, not a population estimate.  The sign reversal is important because it prevents reading the row as a universal direction rule even before the absence of a nontrivial $n\geq 8$ test is considered.

\paragraph{Survival test.}
The scientific survival claim is stronger than the available proxy coupling.  For each residual coordinate $q_i$ and each translation readout $r_j$, the required statistic is a controlled comparison between a baseline model using $Z$ and an augmented model using $Z$ together with $q_i$.  In schematic form, the survival entry is positive only when
$$
\begin{aligned}
S_{ij} &> 0
\quad\text{after controlling for amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, length, and the }M\text{-density baseline.}
\end{aligned}
$$
The current rows satisfy a weaker condition: the proxy coupling matrix is nonzero and exceeds the $0.01$ threshold in at least one entry, indeed in all nine listed residual rows.  They do not include organism-matched tAI or stAI weights as the measured readout, ribosome-profiling translation efficiency, matched protein abundance, or matched mRNA abundance used to form translation-efficiency residuals.  Therefore the correct readback is that the B-star Q6 residual basis remains a live candidate for translation-layer testing, while the survival matrix itself remains unfilled.

\paragraph{Local exclusion rule.}
No translation realization follows from the proxy row alone.  A tRNA gene-copy ratio is not tRNA abundance, not tRNA charging, not ribosome occupancy, not translation efficiency, and not protein output.  The proxy coupling also says nothing about folding, structural order, physical admissibility, biochemical function, mechanism, pathway behavior, or biological universality.  Each of those stronger claims would need its own matched measurement contact and its own controlled statistic.  In particular, a positive $A_{ij}$ value may motivate the next test, but it is not a positive $S_{ij}$ value until the declared readout and the full control set have been used.

\paragraph{Status.}
The finite record is suitable as a bounded BioReality chapter because it gives concrete row counts, organism identities, coordinate names, the codon-space dimension, the break threshold, the maximum coupling magnitude, and the three-organism Leu sign pattern.  Its conclusion is deliberately narrow: the proxy coupling break condition failed, so the residual coordinates are not absorbed at the proxy-coupling level; however, the translation-survival statement still awaits direct translation readouts and the full controlled survival computation.
