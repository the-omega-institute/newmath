\subsection{B-Star Q6 Residual Coupling Against Translation Proxies}\label{subsec:b-star-q6-residual-coupling-translation-proxies}\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Record.} This chapter records a bounded BioReality contact for the claim that the nine residual coordinates of $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ can leave a measurable trace at the translation interface after the amino-acid quotient is imposed. The finite object under audit is a $9 \times K$ survival table $S_{ij}$, where the rows are the residual coordinates
$$
\begin{aligned}
&\mathrm{Arg}_{\mathrm{AGR}},\ \mathrm{Ile}_{\mathrm{AUA}},\ \mathrm{K}_{\mathrm{AAA}},\ \mathrm{Leu}_{\mathrm{CUN/UUR}},\ \mathrm{Leu}_{\mathrm{UUA/UUG}},\\
&\mathrm{Ser}_{\mathrm{UCA/UCG}},\ \mathrm{Ser}_{\mathrm{UCR/AGY}},\ \mathrm{Thr}_{\mathrm{ACR/ACY}},\ f3\text{-}\mathrm{stress},
\end{aligned}
$$
and the columns are translation-adjacent readouts such as tAI, stAI, ribosome occupancy, translation efficiency, and protein abundance when those contacts are present. The intended statistic is not the raw codon table itself. For each row $q_i$ and readout $r_j$, the readout weight is first projected into the synonymous residual direction, giving $\ell_j^{\perp}$; then the residual covariance coupling is computed as
$$
\begin{aligned}
A_{ij}=q_i^{T}\Sigma\ell_j^{\perp}.
\end{aligned}
$$
A survival entry $S_{ij}$ is only admitted after controlling for amino-acid composition, organism context, GC3, sequence length, and the simpler $M$-density baseline. Thus a positive proxy coupling $A_{ij}$ is only a diagnostic candidate; it is not itself a positive survival score.

\paragraph{Finite-row computation.} The attached computation used a codon-count space of $61$ sense codons and loaded three organism rows: Homo sapiens, Saccharomyces cerevisiae, and Escherichia coli K12. All nine listed $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates were computed. Against the available proxy readout, namely the tRNA-Leu CUN-versus-UUR log-copy spectrum, the declared break condition was that every residual row satisfy $|A_{ij}|<0.01$. That break condition was not met. The threshold was $0.01$, the number of nonzero entries reported in the proxy coupling matrix was $9$, and the largest absolute coupling was
$$
\begin{aligned}
\max_{i,j}|A_{ij}|=1882464.1106377328.
\end{aligned}
$$
This is a finite proxy-coupling result: it shows that the nine residual directions are not all numerically silent against the attached tRNA-copy contrast in this three-organism table. It does not yet supply the $Z$-controlled partial-$R^2$ or description-length-improvement entries required for a positive $S_{ij}$ survival claim.

\paragraph{Leu CUN/UUR row.} A separate finite row for the CUN/UUR Leu gate used the same three organisms and reported Pearson correlation $1.0$ with a partial Pearson value $1.0$ at $n=3$. The recorded log-ratio values were
$$
\begin{aligned}
(1.3514980212896148,\ -0.24869078488829677,\ 1.0086640520051702),
\end{aligned}
$$
with signs $(1,-1,1)$. The sign was therefore not uniform across organisms. This matters because the observed relation is a context-sensitive finite table rather than a directional law. The row is useful as a stress test for whether the Leu CUN/UUR coordinate continues to interact with organism-level tRNA-Leu copy structure after adding further organisms, but the present evidence has only three organism rows and no nontrivial population-level null at $n\geq 8$.

\paragraph{Translation survival status.} The current evidence supports only the weaker statement that at least one residual coordinate has above-threshold proxy coupling to the attached tRNA-copy contrast. It does not establish that any $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ coordinate survives on tAI, stAI, ribosome occupancy, translation efficiency, or protein abundance after the full control set. The actual survival claim would require organism-matched codon usage, tRNA-copy data, a direct translation readout or codon-adaptation readout, and the covariates
$$
\begin{aligned}
Z=(\mathrm{amino\ acid\ composition},\ \mathrm{organism},\ \mathrm{GC3},\ \mathrm{length},\ M\text{-}\mathrm{density\ baseline}).
\end{aligned}
$$
Only after fitting that controlled model may one report $S_{ij}>0$, a positive partial-$R^2$, or a description-length improvement. The available proxy result is compatible with the survival program, but it remains below the evidential threshold for translation realization.

\paragraph{Local boundary.} No translation mechanism follows from the proxy coupling alone. No ribosome-mediated realization follows from codon-usage and tRNA gene-copy tables alone. No tRNA abundance or charging conclusion follows from gene-copy counts alone. No folding, physical admissibility, biochemical function, pathway-level role, protein-output claim, or universal biological law follows from this finite record. Each of those claims would require its own matched contact and its own statistic. The present chapter only records that the nine residual coordinates were computable in a $61$-codon space, that a three-organism proxy coupling matrix had $9$ nonzero entries above a $0.01$ silence test with maximum absolute coupling $1882464.1106377328$, and that the Leu CUN/UUR sign pattern was nonuniform across the three organism rows.
