\subsection{Residual codon coordinates against translation readouts}\label{subsec:b-star-q6-translation-survival-matrix}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite object.}
The object under test is a finite matrix of residual codon-coordinate scores. Its row set consists of the nine $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates
$$
\begin{aligned}
q_i \in \{&\mathrm{Arg\_AGR},\ \mathrm{Ile\_AUA},\ \mathrm{K\_AAA},\ \mathrm{Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR},\ \mathrm{Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG},\\
&\mathrm{Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG},\ \mathrm{Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY},\ \mathrm{Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY},\ \mathrm{f3\_stress}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
These coordinates live after quotienting by amino-acid identity: a count vector is not read as an amino-acid composition signal, but through the remaining synonymous degrees of freedom. The intended survival matrix has entries $S_{ij}=S_{q_i}(r_j\mid Z)$, where $r_j$ ranges over bounded translation-related readouts and where the control set $Z$ contains amino-acid composition, organism context, GC3, CDS length, and the simpler codon-density baseline. The accompanying coupling statistic is
$$
\begin{aligned}
A_{ij}=q_i^{T}\Sigma \ell_j^{\perp},
\end{aligned}
$$
with $\ell_j^{\perp}$ denoting the readout direction after projection away from the declared controls. A positive coupling entry is only a geometric contact with the readout direction; it is not itself a survival score unless the controlled partial-$R^2$ or description-length statistic is computed.

\paragraph{Three-organism proxy coupling row.}
The finite proxy computation used codon-count space of size $61$ and loaded three organisms: \texttt{homo\_sapiens}, \texttt{saccharomyces\_cerevisiae}, and \texttt{escherichia\_coli\_k12}. The proxy readout was the tRNA-Leu CUN-versus-UUR log-copy spectrum, not ribosome profiling, not stAI measured against elongation, and not protein abundance. With coupling threshold $0.01$, the recorded break condition was that all nine rows should have $|A_{ij}|<0.01$ against that proxy readout. The break condition was not met: the matrix had $9$ nonzero entries above the stated threshold and maximum absolute coupling
$$
\begin{aligned}
\max_{i,j}|A_{ij}|=1882464.1106377328.
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the proxy row says that the nine residual coordinates are not numerically orthogonal to the organism-level tRNA-Leu copy-ratio spectrum in this finite table. It does not say that any coordinate has survived a measured translation-efficiency, ribosome-occupancy, or protein-abundance test.

\paragraph{Leucine gate check.}
The associated CUN/UUR Leu contact also used the same three organisms. The reported Pearson correlation was $1.0$, and the amino-acid-controlled partial Pearson value was also $1.0$ with $n=3$. The organism-level log-ratio row was
$$
\begin{aligned}
(1.3514980212896148,\ -0.24869078488829677,\ 1.0086640520051702),
\end{aligned}
$$
with sign pattern
$$
\begin{aligned}
(+1,\ -1,\ +1).
\end{aligned}
$$
The sign is therefore explicitly non-uniform across organisms. The $n=3$ perfect correlation is a finite-row contact, not a population estimate, and its sign should not be treated as a universal direction of codon usage or tRNA-copy association.

\paragraph{Modeled translation-readout survival.}
A separate rank-powered modeled-tAI computation enlarged the organism panel to $40$ organisms and used the same nine residual coordinates as both coordinate rows and modeled readout columns. The readout was modeled per-codon tAI from GtRNAdb anticodon copy counts and dos Reis wobble weights, projected onto the same synonymous-residual coordinate family. The controlled design residualized $24$ control columns, with $\mathrm{rank}(Z)=23$ and residual degrees of freedom $17$. The powered design had $\mathrm{rank}(\widetilde X)=9$, and the computed $S^{QT}$ surface had matrix shape $9\times 9$. Its aggregate modeled translation-readout score was
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{QT}=0.9003849000036579,
\end{aligned}
$$
with positive-entry threshold $10^{-12}$ and degeneracy threshold $0.999999$. Since $0.9003849000036579<0.999999$, this run is not recorded as a rank-saturated all-one surface. It is evidence for a bounded modeled codon-adaptation readout under the stated controls, while remaining short of measured ribosome occupancy, measured elongation speed, and protein output.

\paragraph{Readback.}
The finite record therefore separates three statements. First, the proxy coupling matrix over three organisms is non-null at threshold $0.01$, with all nine residual rows producing nonzero proxy couplings and maximum absolute entry $1882464.1106377328$. Second, the CUN/UUR Leu subcontact has a perfect three-row partial correlation but a non-uniform sign pattern, so it supports only a small finite association check. Third, the forty-organism modeled-tAI panel gives a non-degenerate controlled modeled-readout surface with $R^2_{QT}=0.9003849000036579$ after the declared residualization. These facts support a chapter-level claim that $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual codon geometry has finite contact with translation-adaptation readouts after amino-acid quotienting and context controls. They do not, by themselves, establish a measured ribosome-mediated translation mechanism.

\paragraph{Local boundary.}
No stronger biological conclusion is licensed by this chapter alone. The proxy tRNA-copy row does not measure tRNA abundance, tRNA charging, ribosome occupancy, translation efficiency, or protein abundance. The modeled-tAI row is a bounded modeled translation-adaptation readout, not ribo-seq, proteomics, folded structure, physical admissibility, biochemical function, pathway activity, phenotype, or a universal biological law. A claim about measured translation, folding, physical admissibility, function, mechanism, or universality requires its own layer-matched contact and its own controlled statistic. In particular, a positive $A_{ij}$ entry is not promoted to a positive $S_{ij}$ entry unless the corresponding $Z$-controlled partial-$R^2$ or description-length-improvement calculation is present for that readout.
