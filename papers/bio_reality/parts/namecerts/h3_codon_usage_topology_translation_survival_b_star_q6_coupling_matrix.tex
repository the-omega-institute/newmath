\subsection{B-star Q6 residual coupling against translation-layer contacts}
\label{sec:b-star-q6-residual-coupling-translation-contacts}

\paragraph{Record.}
This chapter records a finite BioReality test for the claim that the nine residual coordinates of $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ are not all absorbed by amino-acid composition, organism context, GC3, sequence length, and a simpler codon-density baseline when translation-related readouts are attached. The intended survival object is a matrix $S_{ij}$, where $q_i$ ranges over the nine synonymous residual coordinates and $r_j$ ranges over translation-facing readouts such as tAI, stAI, ribosome occupancy, translation efficiency, and protein abundance. In the present finite row, the directly computed object is not yet this survival matrix. The computed object is a proxy coupling matrix against a tRNA-Leu CUN-versus-UUR log-copy spectrum, with true tAI, stAI, ribosome-profiling, and matched protein-abundance readouts still absent from the realized statistic.

\paragraph{Coordinate carrier.}
The codon-count carrier has dimension $61$. The residual coordinate list used in the computation is
$$
\begin{aligned}
R = \{&\mathrm{Arg\_AGR},\ \mathrm{Ile\_AUA},\ \mathrm{K\_AAA},\ \mathrm{Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR},\ \mathrm{Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG},\\
&\mathrm{Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG},\ \mathrm{Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY},\ \mathrm{Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY},\ \mathrm{f3\_stress}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The finite organism list is
$$
\begin{aligned}
O = \{\mathrm{homo\_sapiens},\ \mathrm{saccharomyces\_cerevisiae},\ \mathrm{escherichia\_coli\_k12}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The computation keeps the amino-acid quotient explicit through the synonymous projection $P_{\tau}$, so the residual coordinates are not ordinary codon-count coordinates. They are codon-usage contrasts after the amino-acid composition component has been separated.

\paragraph{Proxy coupling statistic.}
For the available finite row, the readout vector is the tRNA-Leu CUN-versus-UUR log-copy spectrum. The tested coupling has the form
$$
\begin{aligned}
A_{ij} = q_i^{T}\Sigma \ell_j^{\perp},
\end{aligned}
$$
where $\ell_j^{\perp}$ denotes the projected readout weight after removing the synonymous component controlled by the amino-acid quotient. The declared nonzero threshold for the proxy coupling is $0.01$. The break condition for this proxy test was that every one of the nine residual coordinates satisfy $|A_{ij}| < 0.01$ against the proxy readout. That condition was not met. The recorded maximum absolute coupling is
$$
\begin{aligned}
\max_{i,j}|A_{ij}| = 1882464.1106377328,
\end{aligned}
$$
and the finite row reports $9$ nonzero entries above the threshold convention. Thus the proxy codon-usage/tRNA-copy contact gives a nontrivial residual-coupling signal for all nine listed residual coordinates, but only at the codon-usage topology contact level.

\paragraph{Leu CUN/UUR finite row.}
A separate finite row for the Leu CUN/UUR gate uses the same three organisms. It reports a Pearson correlation of $1.0$ and a partial Pearson correlation of $1.0$ with $n=3$ for the relation between the Leu CUN/UUR codon-usage contrast and the corresponding tRNA-Leu copy-ratio contact. The observed log-ratio list is
$$
\begin{aligned}
(1.3514980212896148,\ -0.24869078488829677,\ 1.0086640520051702),
\end{aligned}
$$
with signs
$$
\begin{aligned}
(+1,\ -1,\ +1).
\end{aligned}
$$
The sign is therefore not uniform across the three organisms. This matters mathematically: the finite row records a nontrivial paired contact, but it does not license a population-level direction, a universal sign, or a global law. With $n=3$, the Pearson value $1.0$ is a finite-row statistic rather than an asymptotic biological regularity.

\paragraph{Survival matrix target.}
The survival claim would require an organism-matched matrix $S_{ij}=S_{q_i}(r_j\mid Z)$, where the control set is
$$
\begin{aligned}
Z = (\mathrm{amino\ acid\ composition},\ \mathrm{organism},\ \mathrm{GC3},\ \mathrm{length},\ \mathrm{M\mbox{-}density\ baseline}).
\end{aligned}
$$
A positive entry would mean that at least one residual coordinate $q_i$ improves a translation readout model after these controls and after the simpler baseline ablation. Equivalently, the required statistic is a partial-$R^2$ or description-length improvement for a true translation readout, not merely a proxy covariance against tRNA gene-copy counts. The present row has not computed such an $S_{ij}$ entry. It has computed $A_{ij}$ against a proxy readout and has shown that the proxy null $|A_{ij}|<0.01$ for all nine residual coordinates is false.

\paragraph{Contact scope.}
The attached contact classes have different evidential roles. Per-organism codon-usage tables observe sequence composition. tRNA gene-copy counts observe genome-annotation copy spectra. tAI or stAI weights would be modelled codon-adaptation readouts if attached at organism scope. Ribosome profiling or translation-efficiency rows would be the direct translation-layer contact needed for survival estimation. Matched mRNA abundance belongs in the control set for separating transcript abundance from translation efficiency. Matched proteomics can test downstream abundance only after identifier matching and the relevant covariates are present. These contacts are not interchangeable: mRNA abundance is not ribosome occupancy, tRNA gene copy is not tRNA abundance or charging, and protein abundance is not by itself a ribosome-mediated translation mechanism.

\paragraph{Local restriction on conclusions.}
The finite computation supports the following bounded statement: in a three-organism codon-usage and tRNA-copy proxy contact, the nine $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates have nonzero projected coupling against the Leu CUN-versus-UUR tRNA-copy spectrum, with maximum absolute coupling $1882464.1106377328$ and threshold $0.01$. It does not establish translation survival $S_{ij}>0$, because true tAI, stAI, ribosome-profiling, translation-efficiency, or protein-abundance rows have not yet been paired with the full control set $Z$. It also does not establish translation realization in the ribosome-mediated sense, folding, structural order, physical admissibility, biochemical function, a pathway mechanism, protein output, or any universal biological law. Each of those stronger conclusions would require its own measured contact and its own controlled statistic.

\paragraph{Operational null.}
The correct null state for the survival packet remains open rather than confirmed. The proxy break condition failed, since not all nine residual coordinates had $|A_{ij}|<0.01$. The survival support condition, however, has not yet been met, since no recorded finite row supplies an organism-matched $S_{ij}>0$ under amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, length, and M-density baseline controls. The next decisive computation is therefore not another identifier normalization step and not another restatement of the proxy coupling. It is the construction of the full matrix
$$
\begin{aligned}
S_{ij}=S_{q_i}(r_j\mid Z)
\end{aligned}
$$
for the nine residual coordinates against at least one direct translation readout, with the partial-$R^2$ or description-length improvement reported entry by entry as positive, zero, or negative.
