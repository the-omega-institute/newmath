\subsection{The $B^{*}_{Q6}$ Translation-Survival Coupling Matrix}\label{sec:bioreality-b-star-q6-translation-survival-coupling-matrix}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Object and row space.}
This record concerns a finite matrix of residual codon-usage coordinates tested against translation-adjacent readouts.  The coordinate side is the nine-dimensional $B^{*}_{Q6}$ synonymous residual family
$K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $Leu_{CUN/UUR}$, $Leu_{UUA/UUG}$, $Ser_{UCR/AGY}$, $Ser_{UCA/UCG}$, $Thr_{ACR/ACY}$, and $f_{3}$-stress.  Each coordinate is evaluated only after quotienting away amino-acid composition and after retaining the stated controls: organism context, $GC3$, coding-sequence length, and the $M$-density baseline.  The initial finite computation used a $61$-codon count space and three organisms, namely Homo sapiens, Saccharomyces cerevisiae, and Escherichia coli K-12.  Against the proxy readout given by the tRNA-Leu $CUN$-versus-$UUR$ log-copy spectrum, all nine residual coordinates had nonzero coupling entries, and the largest absolute coupling was $1882464.1106377328$, far above the declared threshold $0.01$.  Thus the proxy break condition,
$$
\begin{aligned}
|A_{ij}| < 0.01\quad \hbox{for all nine }q_i,
\end{aligned}
$$
was not met.  This is a coupling result, not a survival result: the row records that the residual codon coordinates meet a finite tRNA-copy contact, but it does not itself supply ribosome profiling, measured translation efficiency, or protein-output survival.

\paragraph{Controlled survival statistic.}
For a translation readout $r_j$, the intended survival entry is the controlled residual improvement obtained from the pair $(q_i,r_j)$ after the nuisance design $Z$ has been removed.  The reported design uses $Z$ consisting of amino-acid composition, organism context, $GC3$, length, and the $M$-density baseline.  In the powered modeled-tAI computation, $40$ organisms were loaded, the control matrix had $24$ columns with rank $23$, and the residual degrees of freedom after $Z$ were $17$.  The $9\times 9$ modeled-tAI readout matrix was computed from GtRNAdb anticodon copy counts with dos Reis wobble weights, using the same nine coordinate names as both residual coordinates and readout projections.  The resulting modeled translation score was
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{QT}=0.9003849000036579,
\end{aligned}
$$
with residualized coordinate rank $9$ and a degeneracy guard at $0.999999$.  Since $0.9003849000036579<0.999999$, this row is powered and non-saturated under its own rank test.  The positive-entry threshold for partial $R^2$ was $10^{-12}$, and the survival matrix shape was $9\times 9$.  The reading is deliberately bounded: modeled tAI is a codon-adaptation readout derived from gene-copy and wobble weights, not a ribosome footprint, not measured elongation, not measured occupancy, and not proteomics.

\paragraph{Cross-organism checks.}
The three-organism Leu gate gave Pearson correlation $1.0$ and partial Pearson correlation $1.0$ for log $CUN/UUR$ usage against the tRNA-Leu copy ratio, with $n=3$.  The signs of the organism-level log ratios were $+1,-1,+1$, with numerical values $1.3514980212896148$, $-0.24869078488829677$, and $1.0086640520051702$.  Hence the table supports a finite association row but explicitly rejects a uniform-direction reading.  A larger modeled-tAI sweep reached $41$ organisms, with target $40$ reached, and reported the distribution

\begin{aligned}

Q_{25}&=0.26353954700074694,\\
\operatorname{median}&=0.33934845811598124,\\

This sweep is descriptive across non-independent organisms and is not a population law.  The finite outcome is that the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual family remains visible to modeled translation-adaptation readbacks across a broad organism panel, while phylogenetic independence and direct ribosome measurement remain outside this row.

\paragraph{Ribosome-occupancy contact.}
A local occupancy computation joined $5754$ genes after length checking, with $5754$ overlapping gene identifiers, $5754$ occupancy genes, $6013$ ordered-CDS genes, and zero length mismatches.  The test used exact file-provided codon-window position bins and amino-acid bins, then demeaned occupancy within each bin and permuted synonymous labels only inside the same bin.  The null used $200$ requested permutations; all $9$ computed families had at least $200$ permutations.  With the rule $C_H>0$, description-length penalty $DL=2$, and $\lambda=0.01$, the families passing all gates were $K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, and $f_3$-stress.  This row gives a position-confound-controlled local occupancy separation, but it is still descriptive and does not prove a causal ribosome mechanism.

These rows show residual abundance associations under the same codon-derived controls, but abundance is not translation efficiency, folding, physical admissibility, biological function, or mechanism.

The $f_3$ family audit decomposed $21$ synonymous-family subcoordinates at $n=1800$, base $n=4759$, five folds, and $120$ permutations.  It reported

This identifies where the abundance-facing $f_3$ signal resides, without turning the decomposition into a biochemical pathway.

\paragraph{Local boundary.}
The chapter therefore records a graded finite picture.  The three-organism proxy coupling row is non-null for all nine coordinates, the modeled-tAI survival surface is powered with $R^2_{QT}=0.9003849000036579$, the $41$-organism modeled sweep remains nondegenerate, and several protein-abundance, occupancy, complement, and mRNA-stability rows expose residual structure under the declared controls.  At the same time, no statement here may be read as ribosome-mediated translation realization unless a direct ribosome or translation-efficiency contact supplies the matched rows and the controlled statistic; no statement here implies protein folding, physical admissibility, biological function, molecular mechanism, phenotype, or universality.  The safe conclusion is narrower: $B^{*}_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates are not absorbed by the amino-acid, context, $GC3$, length, and $M$-density controls in the reported finite readout panels, but every upward biological interpretation remains conditional on a separate layer-matched measurement.
