\subsection{The B-star-Q6 translation-contact matrix}
\label{sec:bioreality-b-star-q6-translation-contact-matrix}

\paragraph{Packet.}
The record considered here is the nine-coordinate $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual packet tested against translation-adjacent observables after quotienting away amino-acid composition.  The carrier is a $9 \times K$ table whose rows are
$K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $Leu_{CUN/UUR}$, $Leu_{UUA/UUG}$, $Ser_{UCR/AGY}$, $Ser_{UCA/UCG}$, $Thr_{ACR/ACY}$, and $f_{3}$-stress.  Each row is a synonymous residual coordinate, so its intended use is not raw codon preference but codon preference left after the amino-acid sequence class has been held fixed.  The finite computation uses a $61$-codon count space and keeps the synonymous projection explicit through the projection $P_{\tau}$.

\paragraph{Proxy coupling row.}
The direct finite contact for this chapter is the three-organism coupling computation with organisms Homo sapiens, Saccharomyces cerevisiae, and Escherichia coli K-12.  The readout in that run is not ribosome occupancy, not translation efficiency, and not protein abundance; it is the tRNA-Leu CUN-versus-UUR log-copy spectrum.  The coupling test computes entries $A_{ij}$ between each $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual coordinate and that proxy readout.  The declared null was that all nine coordinates satisfy $|A_{ij}| < 0.01$.  The observed finite row rejects that proxy null: all nine coordinates are nonzero by the thresholded matrix summary, and the maximum absolute entry is $1,882,464.1106377328$.

$$
\begin{aligned}
\#\{q_i\} &= 9,\\
\#\{\text{nonzero proxy entries}\} &= 9,\\
\max_i |A_i| &= 1{,}882{,}464.1106377328,\\
\theta_A &= 0.01.
\end{aligned}
$$

This is a real finite-row contact with codon-count and tRNA-copy data, but its scope is narrow.  It says that the residual coordinate packet is not silent against the CUN/UUR Leu proxy in the three-organism table.  It does not by itself compute a controlled survival score $S_{ij}$ for ribosome profiling, measured translation efficiency, stAI, protein abundance, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, or a population-level biological rule.

\paragraph{Modeled translation readout.}
A separate powered modeled-tAI run supplies a stronger translation-adjacent statistic while still remaining a modeled readout.  That experiment uses $40$ organisms, the same nine residual coordinates, modeled per-codon tAI from GtRNAdb anticodon gene-copy counts and dos Reis wobble weights, and $24$ control columns.  The residualized control basis has rank $23$, leaving residual degrees of freedom $17$.  The $9 \times 9$ modeled $S^{QT}$ matrix has $R^2_{QT}=0.9003849000036579$, with rank $9$ in the residualized coordinate design and a degeneracy threshold of $0.999999$.

$$
\begin{aligned}
N_{\mathrm{org}} &= 40,\\
\operatorname{rank}(Z) &= 23,\\
\operatorname{rank}(X_{\perp Z}) &= 9,\\
\mathrm{df}_{\perp Z} &= 17,\\
R^2_{QT} &= 0.9003849000036579.
\end{aligned}
$$

The organism sweep repeats this modeled translation readout over $41$ organisms and reports a descriptive $R^2_{QT}$ distribution with minimum $0.1579271837024072$, lower quartile $0.26353954700074694$, median $0.33934845811598124$, mean $0.38466555440501393$, upper quartile $0.5143951545826744$, and maximum $0.8213457220900503$ under $1000$ permutations.  These values show that the residual packet has substantial modeled tAI structure across a powered organism panel.  They still do not turn tRNA gene-copy or modeled tAI into measured ribosome motion, tRNA charging, or protein output.

\paragraph{Measured ribosome-window contact.}
The ribosome-window analysis gives a bounded measured readout at the local occupancy layer.  Ordered coding sequences and ribosome occupancy windows were joined for $5754$ genes, with $5754$ genes surviving the length check and zero length mismatches.  The occupancy test controls the position confound by demeaning within exact file-provided window/codon-index and amino-acid bins, and its null shuffles synonymous labels only within the same bin.  Nine families were computed, each with at least $200$ within-bin permutations.  With $\lambda_{DL}=0.01$, $DL=2$, and the rule $C_H>0$, the families passing all gates are $K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, and $f_3$-stress.  This supports a position-controlled association between selected synonymous families and local ribosome occupancy, but it remains descriptive and does not establish a causal elongation mechanism.

\paragraph{mRNA-stability and mediation rows.}
The yeast mRNA half-life contact joins $5085$ rows and reports $R^2_{QM}=0.05179429044975085$ for log10 half-life from the Neymotin table, with $R^2_{QP}=0.2761140373962526$ on the same join and cosine $0.8040935468384264$ between the $S^{QM}$ and $S^{QP}$ directions.  The dominant mRNA-stability coordinate is $Leu_{CUN/UUR}$, while the same-join abundance dominant coordinate is $f_3$-stress.  The payload hit rate for the CDS join is $0.9156038419146592$, with $5379$ valid half-life records and $730$ CDS records missing a half-life match.  Conditioning abundance on residualized mRNA half-life gives $R^2_{QP\mid M}=0.24851799532219632$, so the absorbed component is $0.027596042074056304$ and the mediation fraction is $0.09994436477872035$.  Under $1000$ deterministic residual-mediator permutations, the right-tail value is $0.000999000999000999$, the null mean is $0.00019674487584783138$, and the null $95$th percentile is $0.0014571003414947088$.

These rows show that residual codon geometry can be read against mRNA-stability measurements, with organism-dependent direction; they do not make mRNA stability a translation mechanism.

\paragraph{Condition and tissue rows.}
The yeast condition comparison uses Newman/PAXdb GFP abundance rows in YEPD and SD.  The shared protein count is $2209$, with $2517$ YEPD joins and $2442$ SD joins before the shared restriction.  The two condition matrices are nearly collinear: $S^{QP}$ cosine $0.9993775935182967$ and Pearson $0.9983880256003688$ under $1000$ permutations.  YEPD has $R^2_{QP}=0.3280825603424665$ and SD has $R^2_{QP}=0.3050385538305022$; both are dominated by $f_3$-stress.  The human tissue comparison uses Adult Liver, Adult B cells, and Adult Heart.  The joined counts are $8664$, $9244$, and $6468$, respectively.  The corresponding $R^2_{QP}$ values are $0.026133456228248052$, $0.03626117434944674$, and $0.02320896321347296$, with dominant coordinates $Ile_{AUA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, and $Ser_{UCR/AGY}$.  Pairwise tissue cosines are $0.8990093450963941$ for Liver versus B cells, $0.9176793373754919$ for Liver versus Heart, and $0.6883655232187615$ for B cells versus Heart.

\paragraph{Per-coordinate and family decomposition.}
The per-coordinate yeast decomposition uses $n=1258$ joined rows, $5$ held-out folds, $120$ permutations, rank $3$ for the measured readout dictionary, and a $24$-rank control basis.  Eight of the nine coordinates have a reported real source under the held-out/permutation gate: $K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $Leu_{CUN/UUR}$, $Leu_{UUA/UUG}$, $Ser_{UCR/AGY}$, $Thr_{ACR/ACY}$, and $f_3$-stress.  The $Ser_{UCA/UCG}$ row is present in the table but not in the reported real-source list.  The $f_3$-stress family decomposition uses $21$ synonymous-family subcoordinates, $n=1800$ joined rows from a $4759$ base pool, $5$ folds, and $120$ permutations.  Its full $f_3$ projection has $P_{f3}^2=52.76911713166682$, target norm squared $430.42705205115925$, and target fraction $0.12259712041843235$.  Nineteen families pass the real-source family gate, including $K_{AAA\to AAG}$, $R_{AGA\to AGG}$, $I_{AUU\to AUA}$, $T_{ACU\to ACG}$, and $S_{UCU\to UCG}$.

\paragraph{Local verdict.}
The finite evidence supports a bounded conclusion.  The three-organism proxy row shows nonzero $B^{*}_{Q6}$ coupling to a tRNA-Leu CUN/UUR copy-spectrum contact; the modeled tAI runs show a powered residual translation-adaptation surface; the ribosome-window run supplies a position-controlled local occupancy association for selected families; and the abundance, mRNA-stability, condition, tissue, coordinate, and family decompositions show that the same nine-coordinate packet remains measurable after amino-acid composition, GC3, length, organism or context, and M-density controls.  The chapter therefore records a positive codon-usage-topology and modeled-translation-adjacent signal, not a completed universal translation-survival theorem.  No claim of ribosome-mediated mechanism, protein folding, physical admissibility, biological function, phenotype, or universality follows from these rows unless a separate layer-matched contact and its own controlled statistic supply that claim.
