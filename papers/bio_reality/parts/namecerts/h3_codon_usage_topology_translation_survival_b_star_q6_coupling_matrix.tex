\subsection{Codon-Usage Residual Coupling at the Translation Interface}
\label{sec:biorality-b-star-q6-codon-usage-residual-coupling}

\paragraph{Record.}
This record concerns the claim \texttt{h3.codon\_usage\_topology.translation\_survival.b\_star\_q6\_coupling\_matrix}.  Its object is the finite matrix of couplings between nine $B^*_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates and translation-facing readouts, with survival reserved for the later statistic $S_{ij}$ after controls.  The available computation is not yet that survival matrix.  It is a finite codon-count and tRNA-copy contact over three organisms: \texttt{homo\_sapiens}, \texttt{saccharomyces\_cerevisiae}, and \texttt{escherichia\_coli\_k12}.  The codon-count space has size $61$, and the residual coordinate list is
$$
\begin{aligned}
q \in \{&\mathrm{Arg\_AGR},\ \mathrm{Ile\_AUA},\ \mathrm{K\_AAA},\ \mathrm{Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR},\ \mathrm{Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG},\\
&\mathrm{Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG},\ \mathrm{Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY},\ \mathrm{Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY},\ \mathrm{f3\_stress}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The proxy readout is the tRNA-Leu CUN-versus-UUR log-copy spectrum, not a ribosome-profiling, stAI, tAI, protein-abundance, or translation-efficiency row.

\paragraph{Finite matrix contact.}
For experiment run \texttt{4a783972e74b}, all nine residual coordinates produced nonzero proxy-coupling entries above the stated threshold test.  The break condition was: declare the proxy contact null if every one of the nine coordinates had $|A_{ij}| < 0.01$ against the proxy readout.  The recorded threshold is $0.01$, the break condition was not met, the nonzero-entry count is $9$, and the maximum absolute coupling is
$$
\begin{aligned}
\max_{i,j}|A_{ij}| = 1882464.1106377328.
\end{aligned}
$$
This is positive evidence only for a codon-usage-topology coupling against the tRNA-copy proxy.  It is not a positive $S_{ij}$ entry, because no organism-matched translation readout has yet been paired with the full control set $Z$ and no controlled partial-$R^2$ or description-length improvement has been computed for the declared survival matrix.

\paragraph{Leu CUN/UUR finite row.}
The linked contact \texttt{h3.translation\_realization.cross\_organism.cun\_uur\_leu\_gate} records a separate three-organism finite row for the Leu CUN/UUR association.  Experiment run \texttt{675fdddb3187} reports status \texttt{passed}, Pearson correlation $1.0$, and partial Pearson correlation $1.0$ with $n=3$ for the tRNA-Leu ratio contact.  The observed log-ratio row is
$$
\begin{aligned}
(1.3514980212896148,\ -0.24869078488829677,\ 1.0086640520051702),
\end{aligned}
$$
with signs $(1,-1,1)$.  Hence \texttt{sign\_uniform} is false.  The finite row therefore supports a context-sensitive association in this three-organism table and simultaneously rules out reading the sign as a universal direction.  The value $1.0$ is kept as a finite-row statistic, not as a population-level estimate.

\paragraph{Control requirement.}
The intended survival object remains a matrix $S_{ij}=S_{q_i}(r_j\mid Z)$, where $r_j$ ranges over direct translation-facing readouts such as tAI, stAI, ribosome occupancy, translation efficiency, or protein abundance, and where $Z$ includes amino-acid composition, organism context, GC3, length, and the simpler $M$-density baseline.  The present contact preserves the amino-acid quotient through synonymous projection, but it does not yet supply the required residual readout $\ell_j^\perp$ for a direct translation measurement.  In particular, a large proxy value of $A_{ij}$ does not imply positive survival.  The survival assertion would require a separate estimate such as
$$
\begin{aligned}
S_{ij} > 0
\end{aligned}
$$
after the controls and baseline ablation have already been applied.

\paragraph{Boundary of interpretation.}
The finite data justify recording a non-null proxy coupling between the $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinate system and an organism-level tRNA-copy spectrum.  They do not establish ribosome-mediated translation, translation efficiency, protein output, folding, structural order, physical admissibility, biological function, mechanism, or a universal biological law.  tRNA gene-copy counts are annotation-level genomic summaries; they are not tRNA abundance, tRNA charging, ribosome occupancy, or protein abundance.  Ribosome-profiling, proteomics, and matched mRNA contacts are named as required future measurement classes, but their names do not by themselves create rows in the survival matrix.  The correct local conclusion is therefore narrower: the proxy coupling test is non-null over three organisms and nine residual coordinates, while the translation-survival claim remains blocked until direct organism-matched translation readouts and the declared controls produce an explicit positive $S_{ij}$ entry.
