\subsection{Finite Proxy Coupling for the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ Translation-Survival Matrix}
\label{subsec:b-star-q6-translation-survival-proxy-coupling}

\paragraph{Record.}
This BioReality record isolates a finite proxy contact for the proposed $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ survival matrix. The intended matrix has nine residual codon-usage coordinates as rows and translation-related readouts as columns. The row set used in the computation is
$$
\begin{aligned}
Q = \{&\mathrm{Arg\_AGR},\ \mathrm{Ile\_AUA},\ \mathrm{K\_AAA},\ \mathrm{Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR},\ \mathrm{Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG},\\
&\mathrm{Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG},\ \mathrm{Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY},\ \mathrm{Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY},\ \mathrm{f3\_stress}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The codon-count ambient space has $61$ coding codons. The finite organism list contains $3$ rows: Homo sapiens, Saccharomyces cerevisiae, and Escherichia coli K12. The available readout in this record is not a direct ribosome, tAI, stAI, or protein-abundance measurement. It is the tRNA-Leu CUN-versus-UUR log-copy spectrum, used only as a proxy readout for detecting whether the residual coordinates have nonzero coupling against an organism-level translation-associated annotation.

\paragraph{Computed finite-row contact.}
For each residual coordinate $q_i$, the computation forms a synonymous residual projection before comparing it with the proxy readout. The reported coupling threshold is $0.01$, with the null condition specified as $|A_{ij}| < 0.01$ for all nine residual coordinates against the proxy readout. That null condition is not met. The finite contact reports $9$ nonzero coupling entries and a maximum absolute coupling
$$
\begin{aligned}
\max_{i,j} |A_{ij}| = 1882464.1106377328.
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the finite proxy calculation establishes that the nine-coordinate $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual panel is not numerically silent against the tRNA-Leu CUN-versus-UUR log-copy spectrum in this three-organism table. This is a codon-usage-topology contact with an organism-level tRNA gene-copy annotation, not a completed survival estimate on translation output.

\paragraph{Leu gate row.}
A separate finite row for the CUN/UUR Leu gate reports the same three organisms. Its Pearson statistic is $1.0$, and the partial Pearson statistic after the stated control is also $1.0$ with $n=3$. The organism-level log-ratio signs are not uniform: the logged values are $1.3514980212896148$, $-0.24869078488829677$, and $1.0086640520051702$, with signs $+1$, $-1$, and $+1$. This preserves a real finite association in the small table while preventing any reading of the sign as a universal direction. The positive three-row statistic is therefore a contact to be extended, not a population-level conclusion.

\paragraph{Survival matrix target.}
The full intended survival score has the form
$$
\begin{aligned}
S_{ij} = S_{q_i}(r_j \mid Z),
\end{aligned}
$$
where $q_i$ ranges over the nine residual coordinates, $r_j$ ranges over translation-related readouts such as tAI, stAI, ribosome occupancy, translation efficiency, and protein abundance, and $Z$ contains amino-acid composition, organism context, GC3, coding-sequence length, and the simpler $M$-density baseline. The desired support condition is the existence of at least one entry with $S_{ij} > 0$ after those controls and after baseline ablation. The present finite computation does not supply such an entry. It supplies the weaker fact that the proxy coupling matrix has nonzero entries above the declared $0.01$ coupling threshold.

\paragraph{Controls and quotient discipline.}
The amino-acid quotient remains active in this record: the computation keeps the synonymous projection explicit and does not treat amino-acid composition as evidence for codon-choice residual structure. The control set required for survival remains stricter than the proxy coupling calculation. In particular, a positive $A_{ij}$ entry is not promoted to a positive $S_{ij}$ entry unless an organism-matched translation readout is paired with amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, length, and $M$-density controls, followed by an explicit partial-$R^2$ or description-length-improvement estimate. The distinction between a coupling matrix and a survival matrix is essential here: coupling detects alignment in the residual covariance geometry, whereas survival asks whether that alignment improves the controlled description of a biological readout.

\paragraph{Local boundary.}
This chapter records only a bounded finite-row contact. It does not establish ribosome-mediated translation realization, because the present readout is tRNA-Leu gene-copy ratio rather than ribosome occupancy, translation efficiency, tAI, stAI, or protein output. It does not treat tRNA gene copy as tRNA abundance, tRNA charging, ribosome dwell time, or protein abundance. It does not establish protein folding, structural order, physical admissibility, biochemical function, pathway membership, mechanism, or a universal biological law. Those stronger claims require separate organism-matched measurements and their own controlled statistics. The present evidence is therefore a non-null proxy-coupling contact for the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual panel, together with a blocked survival claim awaiting direct translation-readout rows.
