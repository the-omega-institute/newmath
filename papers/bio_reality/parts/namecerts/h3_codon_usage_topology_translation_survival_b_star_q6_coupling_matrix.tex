\subsection{A finite proxy contact for the $B^*_{Q6}$ codon-residual matrix}
\label{subsec:bioreality-b-star-q6-codon-residual-matrix}

\paragraph{Object of record.}
This chapter records a bounded finite-row contact between the nine $B^*_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates and organism-level codon/tRNA readbacks.  The carrier under test is the matrix of residual coordinates
$$(\begin{aligned}
q_i \in \{&\mathrm{Arg}_{AGR},\ \mathrm{Ile}_{AUA},\ K_{AAA},\ \mathrm{Leu}_{CUN/UUR},\ \mathrm{Leu}_{UUA/UUG},\\
&\mathrm{Ser}_{UCA/UCG},\ \mathrm{Ser}_{UCR/AGY},\ \mathrm{Thr}_{ACR/ACY},\ f3\text{-}\mathrm{stress}\},
\end{aligned})$$
placed inside the $61$-codon count space after imposing the amino-acid quotient.  The intended full survival object is a matrix $S_{ij}=S_{q_i}(r_j\mid Z)$ against translation-related readouts $r_j$, with controls $Z$ consisting of amino-acid composition, organism context, $GC3$, coding-sequence length, and the $M$-density baseline.  The finite computation available here does not yet instantiate that full $S_{ij}$ matrix.  Instead, it supplies a proxy coupling matrix $A_{ij}$ against the tRNA-Leu $CUN$-versus-$UUR$ log-copy spectrum, while keeping the distinction between proxy coupling and controlled survival explicit.

\paragraph{Finite rows and measured proxy statistic.}
The verified row loaded three organisms: \emph{Homo sapiens}, \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}, and \emph{Escherichia coli} K-12.  For the $B^*_{Q6}$ residual list above, all nine coordinates were computed in the synonymous residual basis.  The declared proxy break condition was that every coordinate have absolute coupling below $0.01$ against the proxy readout.  That break condition was not met: the finite matrix had $9$ nonzero entries above the threshold and maximum absolute coupling
$$(\begin{aligned}
\max_{i,j}|A_{ij}|=1882464.1106377328,\qquad \theta_A=0.01.
\end{aligned})$$
Thus the proxy contact is not null at the codon-usage topology layer.  It says that, in this three-organism table and for this tRNA-copy proxy, the $B^*_{Q6}$ residual directions are not all orthogonal to the chosen readout spectrum after the synonymous projection.  The result is deliberately weaker than a survival statement: it reports $A_{ij}$ coupling, not a positive value of $S_{ij}$ under the full control set $Z$.

\paragraph{Leucine gate row.}
A companion finite row isolates the Leu $CUN/UUR$ gate.  In the same three-organism scope, the log-ratio signs were
$$(\begin{aligned}
(1.3514980212896148,\ -0.24869078488829677,\ 1.0086640520051702),
\end{aligned})$$
with sign vector $(1,-1,1)$.  The sign is therefore not uniform across organisms.  The reported partial Pearson statistic for the tRNA-Leu ratio row was $1.0$ with $n=3$, and the ordinary Pearson entry was also $1.0$.  Because $n=3$ is a minimal finite table, this value is treated as a contact-level fact about the attached rows, not as a stable population estimate.  The row supports the existence of a nontrivial organism-indexed Leu codon/tRNA-copy contact, while also recording context dependence through the observed sign reversal.

\paragraph{Survival claim still open.}
The survival conjecture asks for at least one positive entry
$$(\begin{aligned}
S_{q_i}(r_j\mid Z)>0
\end{aligned})$$
for a direct translation-layer readout such as tAI, stAI, ribosome occupancy, translation efficiency, or protein abundance, after the amino-acid, organism, $GC3$, length, and $M$-density controls have been applied.  The present finite row does not contain a ribosome-profiling table, matched mRNA abundance table, matched protein-abundance table, or organism-scoped tAI/stAI row from which a controlled partial $R^2$ or description-length improvement can be computed.  Consequently, the correct readback is asymmetric: the proxy coupling matrix is positive in the sense that the all-small-coupling null failed, but the survival matrix is not yet positive in the controlled-readout sense.  No positive $A_{ij}$ entry is promoted to a positive $S_{ij}$ entry without the missing controlled regression or equivalent description-length calculation.

\paragraph{Local boundary.}
This record is confined to codon-usage residual geometry paired with organism-level codon and tRNA-copy summaries.  It does not establish tRNA abundance, tRNA charging, ribosome-mediated translation realization, protein output, folded structure, physical admissibility, biological function, biochemical mechanism, or a universal biological rule.  Each of those stronger claims would require its own matched contact and its own statistic.  In particular, tRNA gene copy is not used as a direct measurement of charged tRNA availability, a three-organism sign pattern is not read as universal directionality, and the absence or presence of proxy coupling is not treated as evidence about folding or function.  The scientifically valid conclusion is that the $B^*_{Q6}$ residual basis has a non-null finite proxy contact with a tRNA-Leu copy spectrum in the attached rows, while the full translation-survival matrix remains an open measurement task until direct readouts and the stated controls are jointly present.
