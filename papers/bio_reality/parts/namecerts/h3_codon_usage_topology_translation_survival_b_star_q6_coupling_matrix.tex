\subsection{B-star Q6 residual coupling against a translation-proxy spectrum}\label{subsec:b-star-q6-residual-coupling-translation-proxy}\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Object of measurement.} This record concerns the finite matrix obtained by pairing the nine synonymous residual coordinates of $B^\ast_{Q6}$ with a translation-adjacent Leu copy-number spectrum. The codon-count ambient space has $61$ sense-codon coordinates, and the amino-acid quotient is imposed before the residual coordinates are read. The measured residual list is
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathcal{Q} = \{&\mathrm{Arg}_{AGR},\ \mathrm{Ile}_{AUA},\ \mathrm{K}_{AAA},\ \mathrm{Leu}_{CUN/UUR},\ \mathrm{Leu}_{UUA/UUG},\\
&\mathrm{Ser}_{UCA/UCG},\ \mathrm{Ser}_{UCR/AGY},\ \mathrm{Thr}_{ACR/ACY},\ f_3\text{-}\mathrm{stress}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The organism row set is the three-element panel
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathcal{O}=\{\mathrm{Homo\ sapiens},\ \mathrm{Saccharomyces\ cerevisiae},\ \mathrm{Escherichia\ coli\ K12}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
For each $q_i \in \mathcal{Q}$, the recorded statistic is a coupling entry $A_{ij}$ against the proxy readout consisting of the tRNA-Leu $CUN$-versus-$UUR$ log-copy spectrum. The declared numerical break condition for this proxy row was that all nine entries satisfy $|A_{ij}| < 0.01$. The computation reports that this break condition is false: all $9$ residual coordinates have nonzero entries above the threshold, and the largest absolute entry is
$$
\begin{aligned}
\max_{i,j}|A_{ij}| = 1882464.1106377328.
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the finite codon-count/tRNA-copy contact does not collapse to the null coupling pattern at threshold $0.01$.

\paragraph{Leu gate subrow.} The attached Leu $CUN/UUR$ gate gives a separate three-organism statistic for the association between the codon-usage log-ratio and the tRNA-Leu copy log-ratio. Its reported Pearson value is $1.0$, and the reported partial Pearson value after the stated amino-acid-composition control is also $1.0$ with $n=3$. The organism-level log-ratio signs are not uniform:
$$
\begin{aligned}
(1.3514980212896148,\ -0.24869078488829677,\ 1.0086640520051702),\qquad
(+1,\ -1,\ +1).
\end{aligned}
$$
The row therefore records a perfectly aligned three-point finite statistic while simultaneously refusing a common sign law across organisms. The sign reversal in the middle row is part of the observed object; it is not an error term to be removed by wording.

\paragraph{What the finite row supports.} The data support a narrow statement about residual codon-usage geometry and an organism-level tRNA-copy proxy: after projection away from amino-acid composition, the nine $B^\ast_{Q6}$ residual coordinates are not all orthogonal, at the declared threshold, to the Leu copy-number spectrum used in this run. In symbols, the verified row supports the finite inequality
$$
\begin{aligned}
\exists q_i \in \mathcal{Q}\quad |A_{ij}| \geq 0.01,
\end{aligned}
$$
and in fact reports $9$ nonzero above-threshold entries for the available proxy readout. This is a codon-usage-topology contact with a translation-adjacent annotation, not a completed survival matrix for direct translation output. The stronger survival score intended for the translation layer would require, for each readout $r_j$, a controlled statistic of the form
$$
\begin{aligned}
S_{ij}=S_{q_i}(r_j\mid Z),\qquad
Z=(\mathrm{amino\ acid\ composition},\ \mathrm{organism},\ GC3,\ \mathrm{length},\ M\text{-}\mathrm{density\ baseline}),
\end{aligned}
$$
with explicit partial-$R^2$ or description-length improvement after the simpler-codon baseline has been ablated. Those $S_{ij}$ entries are not present in the verified row.

\paragraph{Local exclusion clause.} The finite row does not establish ribosome-mediated translation realization. It does not turn tRNA gene-copy counts into tRNA abundance, tRNA charging, ribosome occupancy, translation efficiency, or protein output. It does not provide folding evidence, physical admissibility, biological function, pathway participation, mechanism, or a universal biological law. A positive proxy coupling $A_{ij}$ is not promoted to a positive survival score $S_{ij}$ without organism-matched tAI, stAI, ribosome-profiling, or protein-abundance readouts and the full control set $Z$. Likewise, the three-organism Pearson value $1.0$ is treated as a finite-row statistic only; it is not a population-level estimate, and the observed sign pattern $(+1,-1,+1)$ rules out any local claim of uniform directionality.

\paragraph{Status of the survival-matrix conjecture.} The present computation clears the weak proxy-null condition for the $B^\ast_{Q6}$ residual basis: the nine residual coordinates have detectable coupling to the available Leu copy-number spectrum at threshold $0.01$. The translation-survival version remains open because the matrix of direct readout scores $S_{ij}$ has not been computed from matched translation or protein rows under the full covariate set. The chapter therefore records a bounded positive contact at the codon-usage/proxy-readout interface and a blocked promotion to translation survival until direct readouts and controlled survival estimates are supplied.
