\subsection{A finite coupling screen for the $B^*_{Q6}$ translation-survival matrix}
\label{sec:bioreality-b-star-q6-translation-survival-matrix}

\paragraph{Question.}
This record tests a bounded translation-layer question for the nine synonymous residual coordinates of $B^*_{Q6}$.  The proposed survival object is a matrix $S_{ij}$ whose rows are the nine residual directions
$\{\mathrm{Arg}_{AGR},\mathrm{Ile}_{AUA},K_{AAA},\mathrm{Leu}_{CUN/UUR},\mathrm{Leu}_{UUA/UUG},\mathrm{Ser}_{UCA/UCG},\mathrm{Ser}_{UCR/AGY},\mathrm{Thr}_{ACR/ACY},f3\}$
and whose columns are translation-relevant readouts such as tAI, stAI, ribosome occupancy, translation efficiency, or protein abundance.  The intended survival statistic is not merely a covariance sign.  For a coordinate $q_i$ and a readout $r_j$, the admissible score must be computed after removing amino-acid composition, organism context, GC3, length, and the simpler $M$-density baseline from the comparison model.  In symbols, the requested statistic has the form
$$
\begin{aligned}
S_{ij} &= S_{q_i}(r_j\mid Z),\\
Z &= (\hbox{amino-acid composition},\hbox{ organism},GC3,\hbox{ length},M\hbox{-density baseline}),
\end{aligned}
$$
where positive survival means a strict partial-$R^2$ or description-length gain after these controls, not only a visible relation in codon counts.

\paragraph{Finite data row.}
The attached computation has not yet supplied the full $S_{ij}$ matrix.  It supplies a finite proxy coupling row built from cross-organism codon counts and a tRNA-Leu CUN-versus-UUR log-copy spectrum.  The loaded organisms are Homo sapiens, Saccharomyces cerevisiae, and Escherichia coli K12, so the contact is a three-organism row rather than a population estimate.  The codon count space has size $61$.  All nine declared $B^*_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates were computed: $\mathsf{Arg\_AGR}$, $\mathsf{Ile\_AUA}$, $\mathsf{K\_AAA}$, $\mathsf{Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR}$, $\mathsf{Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG}$, $\mathsf{Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG}$, $\mathsf{Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY}$, $\mathsf{Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY}$, and $\mathsf{f3\_stress}$.  Against the proxy readout, the null condition was that every coordinate have $|A_{ij}|<0.01$.  That null was not met: the coupling screen reports $9$ nonzero entries above the threshold and
$$
\begin{aligned}
\max_{i,j}|A_{ij}| &= 1882464.1106377328,\\
\theta_A &= 0.01.
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the finite row supports the narrower statement that the projected residual codon geometry is not orthogonal to this tRNA-copy proxy under the reported screen.  It does not yet support the stronger statement that any entry of $S_{ij}$ is positive.

\paragraph{Leucine gate row.}
A linked CUN/UUR leucine contact gives a second finite statistic on the same three organisms.  The recorded log-ratios are
$$
\begin{aligned}
(1.3514980212896148,\;-0.24869078488829677,\;1.0086640520051702),
\end{aligned}
$$
with signs $(1,-1,1)$.  The sign is therefore not uniform across the organisms.  The finite-row Pearson statistic is $1.0$, and the reported partial Pearson value against the tRNA-Leu copy ratio is also $1.0$ with $n=3$.  This is a useful routing signal for a larger organism panel, but the value is not a universal directionality claim.  The same record explicitly treats the sign reversal as context-dependent.

\paragraph{Survival condition.}
The chapter-level survival claim would become positive only if at least one coordinate--readout pair satisfies
$$
\begin{aligned}
S_{ij} > 0
\end{aligned}
$$
after the full control set $Z$ and after ablation of the simpler codon-density baseline.  A positive proxy coupling $A_{ij}$ is insufficient for this purpose.  The present record contains codon-usage rows, tRNA-copy rows, and named routes for tAI or stAI weights, ribosome profiling, matched mRNA abundance, and matched proteomics, but it does not contain an organism-matched residualized translation-readout matrix with partial-$R^2$ entries.  Consequently the present TeX statement records a bounded finite coupling contact and an open survival-matrix test, rather than a completed translation-survival result.

\paragraph{Local boundary.}
No ribosome-mediated translation realization follows from the proxy coupling row alone.  No folding, physical admissibility, biological function, mechanism, protein-output interpretation, or universal biological law follows from these finite numbers unless a separate measurement contact supplies that layer and the corresponding controlled statistic is computed.  tRNA gene-copy ratios are treated here as genome-annotation summaries, not as tRNA abundance, charging state, ribosome occupancy, translation efficiency, or protein abundance.  Matched mRNA abundance, when present, is a control for translation-efficiency residualization and is not itself a translation readout.  Protein abundance, when present, is a downstream readout and not evidence of folding or function by itself.

\paragraph{Verdict.}
The finite row is positive as a proxy coupling screen and negative as a completed survival claim.  The break condition for proxy coupling was avoided because the maximum absolute coupling is $1882464.1106377328$ and all nine residual coordinates have nonzero entries above $0.01$ against the tRNA-Leu proxy spectrum.  The break condition for the proposed $S_{ij}$ survival matrix remains open because the required organism-matched tAI, stAI, ribosome-profiling, proteomic, and mRNA-control rows have not yet been assembled into a $Z$-controlled partial-$R^2$ or description-length matrix.  The proper current reading is therefore: $B^*_{Q6}$ residual geometry has a finite proxy contact with translation-associated tRNA-copy structure, while the stronger translation-survival matrix is a pending empirical test.
