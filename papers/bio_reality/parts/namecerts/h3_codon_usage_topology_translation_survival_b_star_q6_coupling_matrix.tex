\subsection{B-star-Q6 codon residuals against translation-layer readouts}\label{sec:bioreality-b-star-q6-translation-survival}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Object.}
The BioReality record studied here is the controlled survival surface for the nine synonymous residual coordinates of $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ against translation-related readouts.  The coordinate list is
$\texttt{K\_AAA}$, $\texttt{Arg\_AGR}$, $\texttt{Ile\_AUA}$, $\texttt{Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR}$, $\texttt{Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG}$, $\texttt{Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY}$, $\texttt{Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG}$, $\texttt{Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY}$, and $\texttt{f3\_stress}$.  Each entry is interpreted only after quotienting by amino-acid composition and after residualizing against organism context, third-position GC content, coding-sequence length, and the simpler $M$-density baseline.  The matrix entry is not the raw codon count association; it is the incremental readout fit of a residual coordinate after the declared controls.  In the finite rows available here, the strongest direct codon-usage contact is a proxy coupling calculation on three organisms, while the powered translation-layer calculation uses modeled tAI rows over a larger organism panel.

\paragraph{Proxy coupling row.}
The three-organism contact loads $\texttt{homo\_sapiens}$, $\texttt{saccharomyces\_cerevisiae}$, and $\texttt{escherichia\_coli\_k12}$ in a $61$-codon count space.  Against the tRNA-Leu CUN-versus-UUR log-copy spectrum, all nine residual coordinates have nonzero proxy coupling entries, and the largest absolute entry is $1882464.1106377328$.  The break threshold in that calculation is $0.01$, and the recorded break condition is false because the maximum coupling is above threshold.  This row is useful as a codon-usage and tRNA-copy contact, but it is not itself a survival matrix entry: the recorded readout is a proxy copy-number spectrum, not a ribosome, TE, stAI, or protein-abundance measurement.

\paragraph{Powered modeled-translation surface.}
The powered modeled-tAI row supplies the current positive translation-layer surface in bounded form.  It loads $40$ organisms and computes a $9\times 9$ modeled readout matrix from per-codon tAI weights derived from tRNA gene-copy counts and wobble weights.  The control design has $24$ columns, rank $23$, and residual degrees of freedom $17$ after the controls.  The residualized $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ design has rank $9$, so the powered row is not the three-organism rank-saturated surface.  Its total modeled translation survival is
$$\begin{aligned}
R^2_{QT} &= 0.9003849000036579,\\
\operatorname{rank}(Z) &= 23,\\
\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde X) &= 9,\\
\operatorname{df}_{\mathrm{res}} &= 17.
\end{aligned}$$
The positivity threshold recorded for individual partial-$R^2$ entries is $10^{-12}$.  A separate organism sweep computes modeled tAI survival for $41$ organisms, reaching the target of $40$ organisms; the per-organism $R^2_{QT}$ distribution has minimum $0.1579271837024072$, first quartile $0.26353954700074694$, median $0.33934845811598124$, mean $0.38466555440501393$, third quartile $0.5143951545826744$, and maximum $0.8213457220900503$ under $1000$ permutations.

\paragraph{Position-resolved ribosome contact.}
A local ribosome-occupancy contact is present for a bounded codon-window question.  It joins $5754$ genes after length checks, with $5754$ genes overlapping between the occupancy file and ordered coding sequences and no length mismatches.  The method demeans occupancy within exact file-provided window and codon-index bins, together with amino-acid bins, and shuffles synonymous labels only inside those bins.  The recorded null uses $200$ requested permutations; all $9$ computed synonymous families have at least $200$ permutations.  With description-length penalty $\lambda_{DL}=0.01$ and $DL=2$, the families passing the combined gate are $\texttt{K\_AAA}$, $\texttt{Arg\_AGR}$, and $\texttt{f3\_stress}$.  This row is a descriptive, position-confound-controlled occupancy separation; it does not identify a causal translation mechanism.

\paragraph{Local verdict.}
The record is ready as a bounded BioReality chapter because it contains a nonzero three-organism proxy coupling, a powered modeled-tAI survival surface over $40$ organisms, an organism sweep over $41$ modeled-tAI panels, and separate downstream checks for ribosome occupancy, mRNA stability, and protein abundance.  The exact finite claim must remain narrower than the informal biological story.  The proxy matrix alone does not prove survival; modeled tAI and stAI weights are not direct ribosome occupancy; tRNA gene copy is not tRNA abundance or charging; protein abundance is not folding, structural order, physical admissibility, molecular function, pathway function, phenotype, or a universal law.  Translation, folding, physical admissibility, function, mechanism, and universality follow only from their own matched contacts and their own controlled statistics.  The present chapter therefore reports a controlled residual-coordinate signal at the modeled translation layer, with measured occupancy and downstream abundance rows kept as separate bounded readouts rather than promoted into a general biological mechanism.
