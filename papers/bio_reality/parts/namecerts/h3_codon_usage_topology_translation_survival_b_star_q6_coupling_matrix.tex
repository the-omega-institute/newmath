\subsection{B-star-Q6 Codon-Usage Residuals Against Translation-Layer Contacts}\label{subsec:bioreality-b-star-q6-translation-survival}\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Question.} This record asks whether the nine residual coordinates of $B^*_{Q6}$ retain measurable predictive content for translation-relevant readouts after the amino-acid quotient and ordinary sequence covariates have been imposed. The intended survival object is a finite matrix $S_{ij}$, with rows indexed by the residual coordinates
$K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $Leu_{CUN/UUR}$, $Leu_{UUA/UUG}$, $Ser_{UCR/AGY}$, $Ser_{UCA/UCG}$, $Thr_{ACR/ACY}$, and the $f_3$ stress-pair coordinate, and with columns indexed by translation readouts such as tAI, stAI, ribosome occupancy, translation efficiency, or matched protein abundance. For each coordinate-readout pair, the target statistic is not the raw coupling alone. It is the residual survival score after controlling for amino-acid composition, organism context, GC3, coding-sequence length, and the simpler $M$-density baseline.

\paragraph{Finite computation already attached.} The current finite row computed the nine synonymous residual coordinates in a $61$-codon count space for three organisms: human, budding yeast, and \emph{E. coli} K-12. The available readout is a proxy spectrum, namely the tRNA-Leu $CUN$-versus-$UUR$ log-copy contrast; direct tAI, stAI, ribosome-profiling, translation-efficiency, and proteomics rows are not yet present. Against this proxy, all nine $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinates had nonzero coupling entries, and the maximum absolute coupling was
$$
\begin{aligned}
\max_{i,j}|A_{ij}| &= 1882464.1106377328,\\
\#\{(i,j): A_{ij}\neq 0\} &= 9,\\
\theta_A &= 0.01.
\end{aligned}
$$
The stated break rule for this proxy calculation was that the packet would be null if every one of the nine residual coordinates had $|A_{ij}|<0.01$ against the proxy readout. That break rule was not met. Thus the finite proxy row says that the residual codon coordinates are not numerically silent against this tRNA-copy contrast. It does not yet say that any entry of the survival matrix $S_{ij}$ is positive.

\paragraph{Leucine gate row.} A second attached finite row isolates the $Leu_{CUN/UUR}$ direction. In the same three organisms, the recorded Pearson statistic between the codon-usage contrast and the tRNA-Leu copy contrast was $1.0$, and the controlled partial Pearson statistic was also $1.0$ with $n=3$. The organism-level log-ratio signs were not uniform: the recorded log ratios were
$$
\begin{aligned}
(1.3514980212896148,\;-0.24869078488829677,\;1.0086640520051702),
\end{aligned}
$$
with sign vector $(1,-1,1)$. The sign reversal is important. It prevents the three-row calculation from being read as a universal direction law, even though the finite partial correlation row is positive. The row is therefore a bounded organism-level contact between codon usage and tRNA-Leu gene-copy spectrum, not a general biological theorem.

\paragraph{Survival statistic.} The survival claim requires a stronger matrix than the proxy coupling matrix. For a residual coordinate $q_i$ and a translation readout $r_j$, the relevant construction first projects the readout weight into the synonymous residual subspace, giving $\ell_j^\perp$. The coupling term has the form
$$
\begin{aligned}
A_{ij} = q_i^{T}\Sigma\ell_j^\perp,
\end{aligned}
$$
where $\Sigma$ is the residual codon-count covariance under the amino-acid quotient. The survival entry $S_{ij}$ must then be computed as a controlled partial-$R^2$ or description-length improvement after regressing out the control block
$$
\begin{aligned}
Z=(\text{amino-acid composition},\;\text{organism},\;GC3,\;\text{length},\;M\text{-density baseline}).
\end{aligned}
$$
A positive $A_{ij}$ is therefore only a screening signal. It is not equivalent to $S_{ij}>0$. The current row crosses the $0.01$ proxy-coupling threshold, but it does not contain a Z-controlled partial-$R^2$ table for tAI, stAI, ribosome occupancy, translation efficiency, or protein abundance.

\paragraph{Local verdict.} The finite evidence supports a narrow statement: within the three-organism codon-count and tRNA-copy proxy contact, the nine $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinates all show nonzero coupling to the tRNA-Leu $CUN/UUR$ log-copy spectrum, with maximum absolute entry $1882464.1106377328$, and the leucine contrast has a recorded three-row partial Pearson value of $1.0$. This is enough to keep the survival-matrix question open rather than null under the proxy break rule. It is not enough to certify the requested translation-realization matrix. The positive-survival statement remains conditional on attaching organism-matched translation readouts and recomputing $S_{ij}$ under the full control set $Z$.

\paragraph{Scientific boundary.} No translation mechanism, ribosome-mediated causal pathway, folded structure, physical admissibility, biochemical function, or universal biological law follows from these rows. tRNA gene-copy counts are genome-annotation summaries and are not tRNA abundance, tRNA charging, ribosome occupancy, or protein output. Ribosome profiling, mRNA abundance, and proteomics contacts, when attached, must be used in their own measured roles: mRNA abundance is a transcription-layer control for translation-efficiency residualization, ribosome profiling is a translation readout, and proteomics is a downstream abundance readout. None of these contacts may be substituted for folding, function, physical admissibility, or universality without a separate measurement designed for that layer.
