\subsection{Finite translation-survival audit for the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates}
\label{sec:bior-h3-codon-usage-topology-translation-survival-b-star-q6-coupling-matrix}

\paragraph{Claim tested.}
The object of this record is the survival matrix $S_{ij}$ for nine synonymous residual coordinates against translation-related readouts.  The intended rows are
$K_{AAA}$, $\mathrm{Arg}_{AGR}$, $\mathrm{Ile}_{AUA}$, $\mathrm{Leu}_{CUN/UUR}$, $\mathrm{Leu}_{UUA/UUG}$, $\mathrm{Ser}_{UCR/AGY}$, $\mathrm{Ser}_{UCA/UCG}$, $\mathrm{Thr}_{ACR/ACY}$, and an $f3$ stress-pair antisymmetric coordinate.  The intended columns are translation-layer quantities such as tAI, stAI, ribosome occupancy, translation efficiency, and protein abundance when those quantities are supplied as organism-matched measurements.  The tested claim is not that a codon coordinate directly causes translation output.  It is the weaker survival question: after amino-acid composition, organism context, GC3, length, and a simpler $M$-density baseline have been controlled, does any residual row still improve the description of at least one translation readout?

\paragraph{Matrix form.}
For a residual coordinate $q_i$ and a readout weight $\ell_j$, the planned statistic separates a coupling entry from a survival entry.  The coupling entry uses the residual covariance of codon counts, while survival uses a controlled readout regression or an equivalent description-length comparison:
$$
\begin{aligned}
A_{ij} &= q_i^{T}\Sigma\ell_j^{\perp},\\
S_{ij} &= S_{q_i}(r_j\mid Z),\\
Z &= (\hbox{amino-acid composition},\ \hbox{organism},\ GC3,\ \hbox{length},\ M\hbox{-density baseline}).
\end{aligned}
$$
The distinction matters: a nonzero $A_{ij}$ is a geometric contact between a residual coordinate and a proxy readout, whereas a positive $S_{ij}$ requires a matched translation or abundance readout and the full control set $Z$.  In this finite record, the observed matrix evidence reaches the coupling level but does not yet reach the controlled survival level.

\paragraph{Finite rows already computed.}
The recorded coupling computation used the $61$-codon count space and loaded three organism rows: \textit{Homo sapiens}, \textit{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}, and \textit{Escherichia coli} K-12.  All nine declared residual coordinates were computed:
$$
\begin{aligned}
R=(&\mathrm{Arg}_{AGR},\ \mathrm{Ile}_{AUA},\ K_{AAA},\ \mathrm{Leu}_{CUN/UUR},\ \mathrm{Leu}_{UUA/UUG},\\
&\mathrm{Ser}_{UCA/UCG},\ \mathrm{Ser}_{UCR/AGY},\ \mathrm{Thr}_{ACR/ACY},\ f3\hbox{-stress}).
\end{aligned}
$$
Against the available proxy readout, namely the tRNA-Leu $CUN$-versus-$UUR$ log-copy spectrum, the break condition was defined as $|A_{ij}|<0.01$ for every one of the nine rows.  That break condition was not met.  The recorded coupling threshold was $0.01$, the number of nonzero entries was $9$, and the largest absolute coupling entry was
$$
\begin{aligned}
\max |A_{ij}|=1{,}882{,}464.1106377328.
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the finite matrix is not null at the proxy-coupling stage.  The same record explicitly states that true tAI, stAI, ribosome-profiling, and protein-abundance readouts were not present in this run, so the result cannot be upgraded to a positive $S_{ij}$ entry.

\paragraph{Leucine gate check.}
A separate three-organism row checked the $CUN/UUR$ leucine association against tRNA-Leu copy ratio.  Its finite table again used \textit{Homo sapiens}, \textit{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}, and \textit{Escherichia coli} K-12.  The recorded Pearson value was $1.0$, and the amino-acid-composition-controlled partial Pearson value was also $1.0$ with $n=3$.  The organism-level log-ratio signs were not uniform:
$$
\begin{aligned}
(1.3514980212896148,\ -0.24869078488829677,\ 1.0086640520051702)
\quad\hbox{with signs}\quad
(+1,-1,+1).
\end{aligned}
$$
This is useful as a finite contact because it shows that the three rows do not all point in the same signed direction, even though the small table has a perfect recorded correlation.  It is not a population estimate.  No universal directionality follows from $n=3$, and the planned extension requires at least eight curated organisms with a nontrivial null before the row can be treated as stable cross-organism evidence.

\paragraph{Readout requirements.}
The survival matrix remains open because the available rows do not contain the complete readout stack.  Codon-usage tables supply per-organism sequence composition.  tRNA gene-copy counts supply genome-annotation summaries for anticodon families.  tAI or stAI weights would supply modelled translation-adaptation readouts.  Ribosome profiling would supply ribosome occupancy or translation efficiency, especially when paired with matched mRNA abundance so that $TE=RPF/mRNA$ can be residualized.  Proteomics would supply downstream protein abundance, but only as a separate abundance contact.  None of these classes may be silently substituted for another: tRNA gene copy is not tRNA abundance or charging, ribosome occupancy is not protein abundance, and protein abundance is not folding or function.

\paragraph{Verdict.}
The finite result supports a narrow statement: in the currently attached three-organism proxy computation, the nine $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual rows are all nonzero against the tRNA-Leu $CUN/UUR$ proxy spectrum, with maximum absolute coupling $1{,}882{,}464.1106377328$ and threshold $0.01$.  It does not support the stronger survival claim $S_{ij}>0$, because the organism-matched translation readout matrix with the full control set $Z$ has not yet been computed.  The correct status is therefore a ready chapter for a bounded proxy-coupling and contact-gap audit, not a completed translation-survival theorem.

\paragraph{Scope boundary.}
No translation-realization conclusion follows from this record unless a direct translation readout such as tAI, stAI, ribosome occupancy, or translation efficiency is attached and analyzed with the stated controls.  No folding, physical admissibility, biological function, mechanism, or universality follows unless a separate measurement contact is supplied for that specific layer.  The present evidence remains a finite codon-usage and tRNA-copy proxy contact together with a precisely stated missing-data condition for the survival matrix.
