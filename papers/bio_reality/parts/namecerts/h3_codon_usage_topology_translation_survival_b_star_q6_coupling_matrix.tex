\subsection{Residual codon-usage coupling before translation survival}
\label{sec:b-star-q6-residual-coupling-before-survival}

\paragraph{Object of the finite row.}
This record concerns the proposed survival matrix $S_{ij}=S_{q_i}(r_j\mid Z)$ for nine $B^*_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates against translation-facing readouts. The intended residual coordinates are $K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $Leu_{CUN/UUR}$, $Leu_{UUA/UUG}$, $Ser_{UCR/AGY}$, $Ser_{UCA/UCG}$, $Thr_{ACR/ACY}$, and the antisymmetric $f_3$ stress coordinate. The control block $Z$ is the amino-acid composition quotient together with organism context, $GC3$, coding-sequence length, and the simpler $M$-density baseline. A full survival entry would require a translation readout $r_j$, such as tAI, stAI, ribosome occupancy, translation efficiency, or matched protein abundance, and would estimate the residual contribution after the whole control block has already been paid for.

\paragraph{Computed proxy contact.}
The finite computation presently attached to this record is not yet the matrix $S_{ij}$. It is a proxy coupling computation in the $61$-codon count space, using three organism rows: Homo sapiens, Saccharomyces cerevisiae, and Escherichia coli K12. The readout used in this contact is the tRNA-Leu $CUN$ versus $UUR$ log-copy spectrum, not a direct ribosome-profiling, stAI, tAI, or protein-abundance row. The computation formed the synonymous residual coordinates and tested their couplings against that proxy spectrum with threshold $0.01$. The break condition for the proxy was that all nine coordinates should satisfy $|A_{ij}|<0.01$. That condition was not met: all nine residual coordinates had nonzero entries above the threshold, and the largest absolute coupling was $1882464.1106377328$.

$$\begin{aligned}
q_i &\in \{Arg_{AGR}, Ile_{AUA}, K_{AAA}, Leu_{CUN/UUR}, Leu_{UUA/UUG}, Ser_{UCA/UCG}, Ser_{UCR/AGY}, Thr_{ACR/ACY}, f_3\text{-stress}\},\\
|\{q_i: |A_{ij}|>0.01\}| &= 9,\\
\max_i |A_{ij}| &= 1882464.1106377328,\\
\text{organism count} &= 3.
\end{aligned}$$

This is evidence that the chosen $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinates are not numerically silent against the available Leu-copy proxy. It is not, by itself, evidence that a translation survival score is positive, because the readout is a tRNA gene-copy contrast and the required $Z$-controlled partial-$R^2$ or description-length comparison has not yet been computed on a direct translation readout.

\paragraph{CUN/UUR Leu gate row.}
A linked three-organism Leu row reports a perfect finite-row Pearson value for the available proxy contact: the Pearson statistic is $1.0$, and the recorded partial Pearson statistic with $n=3$ is also $1.0$. The organism-level log-ratio signs are not uniform. The three log-ratios are $1.3514980212896148$, $-0.24869078488829677$, and $1.0086640520051702$, with signs $+1,-1,+1$. Hence the row supports only a context-dependent finite contact, not a stable direction law.

$$\begin{aligned}
\rho_{\mathrm{Pearson}} &= 1.0,\\
\rho_{\mathrm{partial}} &= 1.0,\\
n &= 3,\\
(\operatorname{sign}_1,\operatorname{sign}_2,\operatorname{sign}_3) &= (+1,-1,+1).
\end{aligned}$$

No $p$-value is present for this three-organism statistic, and no eight-organism extension is present. The row therefore cannot be promoted into a population-level estimate. It only says that, inside the available finite table, the Leu $CUN/UUR$ usage contrast and the tRNA-Leu copy contrast have a nontrivial paired shape, while the signs show organism dependence.

\paragraph{Survival matrix status.}
The target survival claim is stricter than proxy coupling. For each pair $(q_i,r_j)$, the required entry would residualize the translation-facing readout against amino-acid composition, organism, $GC3$, length, and the $M$-density baseline, then ask whether adding $q_i$ gives a positive survival score. Symbolically, the desired row has the form

$$\begin{aligned}
S_{ij} &= S_{q_i}(r_j\mid Z),\\
Z &= (\text{amino-acid composition},\text{ organism},GC3,\text{ length},M\text{-density baseline}),\\
\text{support} &\Longleftrightarrow \exists i,j\; S_{ij}>0.
\end{aligned}$$

The available computation has $A_{ij}$ proxy couplings, but it does not contain the required $S_{ij}$ survival entries. In particular, a positive or large $A_{ij}$ is not counted as positive survival unless the controlled partial-$R^2$ or description-length comparison has been run with the direct translation readout. The current row therefore keeps the survival matrix open: the proxy break condition failed, but the translation-survival support condition has not yet been met.

\paragraph{Local scope boundary.}
The attached contacts are sufficient to discuss codon-usage summaries, tRNA-Leu gene-copy summaries, modelled codon adaptation weights when supplied, ribosome-profiling or translation-efficiency rows when actually attached, matched mRNA abundance as a control, and matched protein abundance as a downstream readout when actually attached. They are not sufficient to infer ribosome-mediated realization from codon counts and tRNA gene-copy ratios alone. They are also not sufficient to infer folding, structural order, physical admissibility, biological function, biochemical pathway participation, a mechanism claim, or a universal biological law. Those conclusions would require separate measurements or separate finite rows addressed to those layers. In the present record, the justified statement is narrower: the nine residual coordinates are nonzero against the available Leu-copy proxy in a three-organism table, while the true $B^*_{Q6}$ translation survival matrix still awaits direct readout rows and full control estimation.
