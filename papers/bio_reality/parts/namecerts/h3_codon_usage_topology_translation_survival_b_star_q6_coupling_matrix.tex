\subsection{A finite proxy row for the B-star-Q6 translation-survival matrix}
\label{sec:bioreality-b-star-q6-translation-survival-matrix}

\paragraph{Record.}
This record concerns the proposed survival matrix $S_{ij}=S_{q_i}(r_j\mid Z)$ for the nine $B^*_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates against translation-adjacent readouts. The intended matrix has rows
$$
\begin{aligned}
q_i \in \{&\mathrm{Arg\_AGR},\ \mathrm{Ile\_AUA},\ \mathrm{K\_AAA},\ \mathrm{Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR},\ \mathrm{Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG},\\
&\mathrm{Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG},\ \mathrm{Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY},\ \mathrm{Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY},\ \mathrm{f3\_stress}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The target readouts are translation-layer quantities such as tAI, stAI, ribosome occupancy, translation efficiency, and matched protein abundance. The control block is $Z=(\text{amino-acid composition},\text{organism},\mathrm{GC3},\text{length},\text{$M$-density baseline})$. The finite computation available here does not yet contain the full $S_{ij}$ table. It contains a proxy coupling row against the tRNA-Leu CUN-versus-UUR log-copy spectrum and a separate three-organism CUN/UUR Leu gate statistic.

\paragraph{Finite carrier.}
The available contact uses codon-count coordinates in the $61$-codon coding alphabet and keeps the amino-acid quotient explicit through the synonymous projection $P_\tau$. Three organism rows are present: Homo sapiens, Saccharomyces cerevisiae, and Escherichia coli K12. For a residual coordinate $q_i$ and a proxy readout vector $\ell^\perp$, the reported coupling is of the form
$$
\begin{aligned}
A_i=q_i^{T}\Sigma\ell^\perp,
\end{aligned}
$$
where $\Sigma$ is the residual count covariance after the synonymous projection. The break rule for the proxy row was: declare a null proxy row if every one of the nine residual coordinates satisfies $|A_i|<0.01$. That break condition was not met. The largest absolute coupling was
$$
\begin{aligned}
\max_i |A_i| = 1882464.1106377328,
\end{aligned}
$$
and all nine residual coordinates had nonzero proxy entries above the $0.01$ coupling threshold.

\paragraph{What the proxy row establishes.}
The proxy row establishes only that the nine $B^*_{Q6}$ residual directions are not all orthogonal, under the stated finite construction, to the tRNA-Leu CUN-versus-UUR log-copy spectrum. It is a codon-usage and tRNA-copy contact, not a ribosome-contact survival estimate. The observed result is therefore a finite positive coupling statement for $A_i$, not a positive survival statement for $S_{ij}$. In particular, the implication
$$
\begin{aligned}
|A_i|>0.01 \quad \Longrightarrow \quad S_{ij}>0
\end{aligned}
$$
is not licensed by this row. A survival entry would require a direct translation readout $r_j$, the full control block $Z$, and a partial-$R^2$ or description-length-improvement calculation after the simpler-codon-baseline ablation.

\paragraph{Three-organism Leu gate contact.}
A second finite row compares the Leu CUN/UUR codon-usage ratio with the tRNA-Leu copy-ratio signal across the same three organisms. The reported Pearson correlation is $1.0$, and the amino-acid-composition-controlled partial Pearson correlation is also $1.0$ with $n=3$. The organism-level log-ratio signs are not uniform: the log-ratio values are
$$
\begin{aligned}
1.3514980212896148,\quad -0.24869078488829677,\quad 1.0086640520051702,
\end{aligned}
$$
with signs $+1,-1,+1$. Thus the finite row gives a sharp three-point association in this small contact table, while simultaneously preventing a universal sign claim. The non-uniform sign pattern is part of the record, not a nuisance to be removed.

\paragraph{Status of the survival conjecture.}
The intended conjecture says that at least one of the nine residual coordinates survives the amino-acid, organism, GC3, length, and $M$-density controls on at least one translation-related readout. The present finite data do not verify that statement. They verify a proxy coupling matrix with nine nonzero entries and a three-organism Leu gate contact, but they do not contain organism-matched ribosome-profiling rows, stAI/tAI survival estimates, matched mRNA residualization, matched proteomics rows, or the declared $Z$-controlled partial-$R^2$ survival scores. The survival matrix remains open until at least one entry satisfies
$$
\begin{aligned}
S_{ij}=S_{q_i}(r_j\mid Z)>0
\end{aligned}
$$
under the stated controls and baseline ablation.

\paragraph{Local boundary.}
No translation realization follows from the proxy coupling alone. No ribosome-mediated mechanism follows from tRNA gene-copy counts. No tRNA abundance, tRNA charging, ribosome occupancy, translation efficiency, protein output, folding, structural order, physical admissibility, biological function, pathway evidence, population-level estimate, or universal biological law follows from the finite rows reported here unless a separate measured contact supplies that layer and the corresponding controlled statistic is computed. The correct reading is narrower: the $B^*_{Q6}$ residual geometry has a non-null finite coupling to a tRNA-Leu proxy spectrum in three organisms, and one Leu CUN/UUR contact has a perfect three-row correlation while showing context-dependent sign behavior. Those facts motivate the survival-matrix test; they do not complete it.
