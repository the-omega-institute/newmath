\subsection{The $B^{*}_{Q6}$ Codon-Usage Coupling Matrix}\label{sec:bioreality-b-star-q6-codon-usage-coupling}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite carrier.}
The record studied here is a finite codon-usage contact for the nine synonymous-residual coordinates
$K\_{AAA}$, $Arg\_{AGR}$, $Ile\_{AUA}$, $Leu\_{CUN/UUR}$, $Leu\_{UUA/UUG}$, $Ser\_{UCR/AGY}$, $Ser\_{UCA/UCG}$, $Thr\_{ACR/ACY}$, and $f3$-stress.  The computed carrier lives in the $61$-codon count space after the amino-acid quotient has been kept explicit by the synonymous projection $P\_{\tau}$.  The finite contact loads three organisms, $Homo\ sapiens$, $Saccharomyces\ cerevisiae$, and $Escherichia\ coli\ K12$, and tests the residual coordinates against a proxy readout: the $tRNA$--Leu $CUN$-versus-$UUR$ log-copy spectrum.  The actual verified matrix is therefore a coupling matrix $A\_{ij}$, not a full survival matrix $S\_{ij}$.

\paragraph{Coupling statistic.}
For each residual coordinate $q\_i$ and the proxy readout vector $\ell$, the finite computation forms a projected readout $\ell^{\perp}$ and evaluates
$$
\begin{aligned}
A\_{i} &= q\_i^{T}\Sigma \ell^{\perp} .
\end{aligned}
$$
The declared break condition for this finite row was that all nine couplings satisfy $|A\_i|<0.01$.  That condition is false: all nine entries are nonzero, and the largest absolute entry is $1882464.1106377328$, well above the threshold $0.01$.  Thus the three-organism codon-count and tRNA-copy contact is not null at the proxy-coupling level.  This says only that the projected codon-residual directions have detectable covariance with the chosen tRNA-copy spectrum in the finite table.  It does not by itself report a positive partial $R^{2}$ survival score for ribosome occupancy, measured translation efficiency, protein abundance, folding, function, or any population-level biological law.

\paragraph{Three-organism Leu gate.}
The companion Leu $CUN/UUR$ contact gives a useful diagnostic for why the finite row must be read narrowly.  Across the same three-organism scale, the partial Pearson statistic between the log $CUN/UUR$ codon ratio and the log tRNA-Leu copy ratio is $1.0$ with $n=3$, while the organism signs are $+1,-1,+1$ with log-ratio values $1.3514980212896148$, $-0.24869078488829677$, and $1.0086640520051702$.  The sign is therefore not uniform even in the small table.  The perfect finite correlation is a rank-limited diagnostic, not a directionality theorem.  In particular, a tRNA gene-copy count is a genome annotation summary; it is not a direct measurement of tRNA abundance, charging, ribosome residence time, elongation speed, or protein output.

\paragraph{Modeled translation readout.}
A separate powered computation replaces the three-organism proxy surface with a modeled per-codon tAI readout derived from GtRNAdb anticodon copy counts and dos Reis wobble weights.  That computation uses $40$ organisms, residualizes $24$ control columns with rank $23$, leaves residual degrees of freedom $17$, and keeps the same nine residual coordinates.  The modeled $S^{QT}$ matrix has shape $9\times 9$, rank $9$ after control residualization, and total $R^{2}\_{QT}=0.9003849000036579$, below the degeneracy guard $0.999999$.  This powered row is a stronger translation-layer model contact than the three-organism proxy table, but it remains modeled tAI.  It is not ribosome profiling, not measured elongation speed, not proteomics, and not evidence for protein folding or function.

\paragraph{Protein-abundance and complement diagnostics.}
The abundance-side computations show that the same nine-coordinate residual system can be joined to large protein-level tables, again with the same core controls: intercept, log CDS length, twenty amino-acid composition proportions, GC3, and the $M$-density baseline.  In the modeled-translation complement computation, the joined row counts are $14410$ for $Danio\ rerio$, $3739$ for $E.\ coli\ K12\ MG1655$, $12418$ for $Gallus\ gallus$, $19053$ for $Homo\ sapiens$, $4129$ for $Mycobacterium\ smegmatis$, and $5815$ for $Saccharomyces\ cerevisiae$, each with $24$ control columns and control rank $23$.  The complement fractions $d$ are $0.874628721049266$, $0.6629960038578532$, $0.9400341806302775$, $0.9208949355679455$, $0.8972672018746489$, and $0.21173219572706703$, respectively; the corresponding modeled-translation-mediated fractions $m=1-d$ are $0.12537127895073397$, $0.3370039961421468$, $0.05996581936972245$, $0.07910506443205445$, $0.10273279812535108$, and $0.788267804272933$.  These values classify how much of the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ abundance component lies outside the modeled tAI component in each organism.  They do not identify a biochemical mechanism.

For $Saccharomyces\ cerevisiae$, the top scored readout is mRNA, with $A\_H=0.001897605472160837$, $C\_H=0.6040571890503664$, $A\_H C\_H=0.0011462622274400686$, score $-0.008853737772559931$, $n=5128$, Bonferroni threshold $0.0013014749238591248$, and null $p95=0.0004745820831535835$.  $E.\ coli\ K12\ MG1655$ and $Mycobacterium\ smegmatis$ have no selected $H^{*}\_o$ in this ranking table.  The module status is therefore mixed: some organisms show ranked residual support in measured TE, mRNA, complex membership, localization, or PTM-density contacts, while other organisms remain weak or empty under the stated scoring rule.

\paragraph{Condition and tissue variation.}
The yeast condition-specific abundance calculation joins $2209$ shared proteins between rich YEPD and minimal SD conditions.  The two $S^{QP}$ values are $0.3280825603424665$ for YEPD and $0.3050385538305022$ for SD, both dominated by $f3$-stress, with vector cosine $0.9993775935182967$ and Pearson $0.9983880256003688$ across $1000$ permutations.  The human tissue calculation uses Kim 2014 SEQUEST PAXdb tissue files and reports $R^{2}\_{QP}=0.03626117434944674$ for adult B cells with dominant $Arg\_{AGR}$ and $n=9244$, $0.02320896321347296$ for adult heart with dominant $Ser\_{UCR/AGY}$ and $n=6468$, and $0.026133456228248052$ for adult liver with dominant $Ile\_{AUA}$ and $n=8664$.  Pairwise tissue cosines are $0.6883655232187615$, $0.8990093450963941$, and $0.9176793373754919$ over $1000$ permutations.  These rows show that the residual coordinates can be read against abundance tables under controls, but the condition and tissue readouts remain descriptive statistical projections.

\paragraph{mRNA-stability contacts.}

It gives $R^{2}\_{QM}=0.05179429044975085$, dominant coordinate $Leu\_{CUN/UUR}$, $R^{2}\_{QP}=0.2761140373962526$ on the same join, cosine $0.8040935468384264$ between $S^{QM}$ and $S^{QP}$, and permutation $p=0.000999000999000999$ across $1000$ permutations.  The mediation decomposition reports $R^{2}\_{QP\mid M}=0.24851799532219632$, absorbed fraction $0.027596042074056304$, and mediation fraction $0.09994436477872035$, with null mean $0.00019674487584783138$ and null $p95=0.0014571003414947088$.  The $E.\ coli$ mRNA-stability calculation joins $2720$ rows to the Esquerr\'e 2015 half-life table at growth rate $0.40\ h^{-1}$, with CDS join hit rate $0.9978649586335735$, $R^{2}\_{QM}=0.02298556640056076$, dominant coordinate $Arg\_{AGR}$, same-join $R^{2}\_{QP}=0.11632311854858134$, cosine $-0.7784923375569599$, and permutation $p=0.000999000999000999$.  These stability rows are statistical mediator and readout contacts; they do not make the codon residuals causal determinants of mRNA decay.

\paragraph{Local conclusion.}
The finite result for this chapter is positive only at its declared level: the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ synonymous-residual coordinates have nonzero proxy coupling to a tRNA-Leu copy spectrum in a three-organism codon-count contact, and the powered modeled-tAI calculation shows a nondegenerate $9\times 9$ modeled translation-readout surface with $R^{2}\_{QT}=0.9003849000036579$ over $40$ organisms.  The broader linked rows show controlled abundance, complement, tissue, condition, mRNA-stability, and ranking statistics with concrete joins and finite nulls.  None of these facts permits promotion to measured ribosome-mediated translation unless a matched ribo-seq or measured TE contact supplies it; none permits promotion to protein folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, biological mechanism, phenotype, or universality unless a separate layer-matched contact and controlled statistic supplies that specific claim.
