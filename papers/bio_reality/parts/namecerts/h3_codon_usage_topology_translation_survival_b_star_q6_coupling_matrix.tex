\subsection{Finite proxy coupling for the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ translation-survival matrix}\label{sec:bioreality-b-star-q6-translation-survival-proxy}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Record.}
This record concerns the proposed survival matrix $S_{ij}=S_{q_i}(r_j\mid Z)$ for the nine synonymous residual coordinates of $B^{*}_{Q6}$ against translation-related readouts. The intended matrix has rows
$K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $Leu_{CUN/UUR}$, $Leu_{UUA/UUG}$, $Ser_{UCR/AGY}$, $Ser_{UCA/UCG}$, $Thr_{ACR/ACY}$, and the antisymmetric $f3$ stress coordinate. The readout side is meant to include tAI or stAI weights, ribosome occupancy or translation efficiency, and matched protein abundance when those measurements are actually attached. The control set is
$$\begin{aligned}
Z = (&\text{amino-acid composition},\ \text{organism context},\ GC3,\\
     &\text{coding length},\ \text{and the }M\text{-density baseline}).
\end{aligned}$$
The finite computation currently available is not yet an $S_{ij}$ survival estimate. It is a proxy coupling computation over a 61-codon count space, using three organisms: Homo sapiens, Saccharomyces cerevisiae, and Escherichia coli K-12. The proxy readout is the tRNA-Leu $CUN$-versus-$UUR$ log-copy spectrum; direct tAI, stAI, ribosome-profiling, and protein-abundance rows are not present in this finite row.

\paragraph{Finite computation.}
For the observed proxy contact, the nine $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual rows were all computed:
$$\begin{aligned}
R = (&Arg_{AGR},\ Ile_{AUA},\ K_{AAA},\ Leu_{CUN/UUR},\ Leu_{UUA/UUG},\\
     &Ser_{UCA/UCG},\ Ser_{UCR/AGY},\ Thr_{ACR/ACY},\ f3\text{-stress}).
\end{aligned}$$
The reported coupling statistic is $A_{ij}=q_i^{T}\Sigma \ell_j^{\perp}$ after the synonymous projection that keeps the amino-acid quotient explicit. The break condition for the proxy run was that all nine rows satisfy $|A_{ij}|<0.01$. That break condition was not met: all $9$ entries were nonzero at the stated threshold, and the maximum absolute coupling was
$$\begin{aligned}
\max_{i,j}|A_{ij}| = 1882464.1106377328.
\end{aligned}$$
Thus the finite proxy contact shows that the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual basis is not numerically orthogonal to the tRNA-Leu $CUN/UUR$ log-copy spectrum in this three-organism table. This is a codon-usage and tRNA-copy coupling statement, not a survival statement.

\paragraph{Leucine gate row.}
A related three-organism CUN/UUR Leu gate computation reports Pearson correlation $1.0$ and partial Pearson correlation $1.0$ with $n=3$ after the stated control route. The organism-level log-ratio signs were not uniform. The recorded log-ratios were
$$\begin{aligned}
(1.3514980212896148,\ -0.24869078488829677,\ 1.0086640520051702),
\end{aligned}$$
with signs $(1,-1,1)$. This makes the row useful as a finite contact for context dependence: it shows that the proxy association can be read in the table, while its direction is not a universal sign claim. The sample size is exactly three organisms, so the row is not a population estimate and is not a cross-organism law.

\paragraph{Survival criterion.}
The conjectural translation-survival claim would require at least one entry with
$$\begin{aligned}
S_{ij}=S_{q_i}(r_j\mid Z)>0
\end{aligned}$$
for a direct translation-layer or downstream abundance readout $r_j$, after controlling for amino-acid composition, organism context, $GC3$, length, and the $M$-density baseline. Equivalently, some $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual row would have to improve the description of a real readout beyond the simpler model containing those controls. The current finite proxy row does not supply that estimate. It supplies $A_{ij}$ coupling against a tRNA-copy spectrum, and it records that the proxy threshold $0.01$ is exceeded. A positive $A_{ij}$ or a large $|A_{ij}|$ is not promoted to positive $S_{ij}$ unless the controlled partial-$R^2$ or description-length statistic is actually computed on the direct readout.

\paragraph{Local evidential boundary.}
The finite row is therefore admissible as a bounded codon-usage and tRNA-copy contact for the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates. It is not, by itself, evidence for ribosome-mediated translation realization. It is also not evidence for protein folding, physical admissibility, biochemical function, pathway participation, mechanism, or universality. Matched ribosome-profiling or translation-efficiency rows would be needed for a translation-readout survival entry. Matched protein-abundance rows would be needed for downstream abundance mediation. Separate structural, physical, functional, or mechanistic contacts would be needed for any claim at those layers. The present record keeps the finite proxy signal, the missing controlled survival matrix, and the prohibited higher-layer readings separate.
