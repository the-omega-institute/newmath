\subsection{B-star-Q6 residual coupling against a Leu-copy proxy}
\label{sec:b-star-q6-residual-coupling-leu-copy-proxy}

\paragraph{Object of the row.}
This record isolates a finite codon-usage calculation for the claim that the nine $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates are not automatically erased by amino-acid composition and organism context before any translation-readout survival test is attempted.  The computed carrier is a $9 \times K$ residual-coupling table in the $61$-codon count space.  In the available row, $K$ is represented by one proxy readout: the organism-level tRNA-Leu CUN-versus-UUR log-copy spectrum.  The nine residual coordinates are
$$
\begin{aligned}
&\mathrm{Arg}_{\mathrm{AGR}},\ \mathrm{Ile}_{\mathrm{AUA}},\ \mathrm{K}_{\mathrm{AAA}},\ \mathrm{Leu}_{\mathrm{CUN}/\mathrm{UUR}},\ \mathrm{Leu}_{\mathrm{UUA}/\mathrm{UUG}},\\
&\mathrm{Ser}_{\mathrm{UCA}/\mathrm{UCG}},\ \mathrm{Ser}_{\mathrm{UCR}/\mathrm{AGY}},\ \mathrm{Thr}_{\mathrm{ACR}/\mathrm{ACY}},\ f_3\text{-stress}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The organism list in the row is $\mathrm{Homo\ sapiens}$, $\mathrm{Saccharomyces\ cerevisiae}$, and $\mathrm{Escherichia\ coli}$ K12.  Thus the result is a three-organism finite table, not a population estimate.

\paragraph{Computed coupling statistic.}
For each residual coordinate $q_i$, the row computes its synonymous residual projection against the Leu-copy proxy and records the coupling entry $A_{ij}$.  The declared null threshold is $|A_{ij}| < 0.01$ for all nine coordinates.  That null condition is not met: all $9$ residual coordinates have nonzero entries above the threshold, and the largest absolute coupling is
$$
\begin{aligned}
\max_{i,j}|A_{ij}| = 1882464.1106377328.
\end{aligned}
$$
Equivalently, the observed finite row has
$$
\begin{aligned}
\#\{(i,j): |A_{ij}| \geq 0.01\} = 9,
\qquad
\#\{q_i\}=9,
\qquad
\#\{\text{codons}\}=61.
\end{aligned}
$$
This is evidence that the chosen $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates retain measurable covariance with the proxy spectrum after the synonymous projection is imposed.  It is not, by itself, a survival score $S_{ij}$ and it is not a partial-$R^2$ result under the full control set.

\paragraph{Leu CUN/UUR cross-organism contact.}
A second finite row gives the specific Leu CUN-versus-UUR comparison against tRNA-Leu copy ratio for the same three organisms.  The ordinary Pearson value is reported as $1.0$, and the amino-acid-composition-controlled partial Pearson value is also reported as $1.0$ with $n=3$.  The organism-level log-ratio signs are not uniform:
$$
\begin{aligned}
(1.3514980212896148,\ -0.24869078488829677,\ 1.0086640520051702),
\qquad
(+,-,+).
\end{aligned}
$$
The sign reversal is important: the finite table supports a bounded association check for the CUN/UUR Leu route, while refusing a universal direction claim.  With only three organisms, the value $1.0$ is a row statistic attached to the listed organisms, not an inference that the relation persists over a wider clade sample.

\paragraph{What the row does and does not establish.}
The row establishes a codon-usage-topology contact: the nine residual coordinates are computed in the $61$-codon count space, the amino-acid quotient is kept explicit through synonymous projection, the coupling threshold is $0.01$, the all-small-coupling break condition is false, and the maximum absolute proxy coupling is $1882464.1106377328$.  The row also records that the available readout is the tRNA-Leu CUN-versus-UUR log-copy spectrum, not tAI, stAI, ribosome occupancy, translation efficiency, or protein abundance.  Therefore the matrix entries currently support proxy coupling $A_{ij}$ only.  A survival entry $S_{ij}>0$ would require an organism-matched translation or protein readout together with amino-acid composition, organism context, GC3, length, and M-density baseline controls, followed by an explicit partial-$R^2$ or description-length-improvement estimate.

\paragraph{Local boundary.}
No translation-realization conclusion follows from this row alone.  In particular, the tRNA gene-copy contact is not treated as tRNA abundance, tRNA charging, ribosome occupancy, translation efficiency, or protein output.  The coupling table also does not imply folded structure, physical admissibility, biochemical function, a ribosome-mediated mechanism, pathway evidence, or a universal biological law.  Each of those stronger readings would need its own matched contact and its own statistic.  The finite result is therefore best read as a disciplined obstruction to the all-zero proxy-coupling null, while the stronger $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ translation-survival matrix remains open until direct translation readouts are attached under the declared controls.
