\subsection{Residual codon coordinates against translation-contact readouts}
\label{sec:bioreality-b-star-q6-translation-survival-matrix}

\paragraph{Record.}
This BioReality record concerns the nine residual codon-usage coordinates associated with $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ after quotienting by amino-acid identity.  The tested coordinate family is
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathcal{Q} = \{&\mathrm{Arg}_{AGR},\ \mathrm{Ile}_{AUA},\ K_{AAA},\ \mathrm{Leu}_{CUN/UUR},\ \mathrm{Leu}_{UUA/UUG},\\
&\mathrm{Ser}_{UCA/UCG},\ \mathrm{Ser}_{UCR/AGY},\ \mathrm{Thr}_{ACR/ACY},\ f3\text{-}\mathrm{stress}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The intended survival matrix has entries $S_{ij}=S_{q_i}(r_j\mid Z)$, where $q_i\in\mathcal{Q}$ and $r_j$ ranges over bounded translation-side readouts such as codon adaptation weights, ribosome profiling translation efficiency, ribosome occupancy, or protein abundance.  The control block is
$$
\begin{aligned}
Z=(\text{amino-acid composition},\ \text{organism context},\ GC3,\ \text{coding length},\ \text{M-density baseline}).
\end{aligned}
$$
A positive entry would mean that the residual coordinate improves the controlled description of a named readout after the simpler codon-density baseline is already present.  The current finite row does not yet contain such an $S_{ij}$ table; it contains a proxy coupling computation against a tRNA-Leu CUN-versus-UUR log-copy spectrum.

\paragraph{Finite computation.}
The recorded run $4a783972e74b$ used a $61$-codon count space and loaded three organisms: Homo sapiens, Saccharomyces cerevisiae, and Escherichia coli K-12.  For the proxy readout, the break rule was that the packet would be null if every one of the nine residual coordinates had $|A_{ij}|<0.01$.  That break rule was not met.  The computation reports $9$ nonzero coupling entries and
$$
\begin{aligned}
\max_{i,j}|A_{ij}|=1882464.1106377328,
\qquad
\tau_A=0.01,
\qquad
\#\{(i,j):A_{ij}\ne 0\}=9.
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the finite proxy row says that the post-amino-acid residual coordinate system is not numerically silent against the attached tRNA-Leu copy-ratio spectrum.  This is a codon-usage topology contact with a modeled tRNA-copy spectrum, not a measured ribosome or protein-output survival result.  In particular, the sign of an individual $A_{ij}$ entry is retained as a finite-row sign only; it is not promoted to a direction of selection, a cross-species invariant, or a causal statement about translation.

\paragraph{Leu gate companion row.}
The companion run $675fdddb3187$ isolates the Leu CUN/UUR contact on the same three-organism list.  It records Pearson correlation $1.0$ and partial Pearson correlation $1.0$ for $n=3$ after the declared control form, with organism-level log-ratio signs
$$
\begin{aligned}
(1.3514980212896148,\ -0.24869078488829677,\ 1.0086640520051702)
\end{aligned}
$$
and sign pattern $(+,-,+)$.  The row therefore has a perfect finite-three-organism correlation in this small table while simultaneously displaying nonuniform organism signs.  The appropriate interpretation is a context-sensitive contact requiring expansion, not a universal Leu rule.  The open extension test asks for at least eight organisms, a partial correlation above $0.5$, and $p<0.05$ under a nontrivial null; those eight-organism and $p$-value data are not present in the current record.

\paragraph{Survival criterion.}
The survival claim remains sharper than the proxy coupling claim.  For a genuine matrix entry, the readout residual $\ell_j^{\perp}$ must be paired with the synonymous residual coordinate and evaluated under the full control block:
$$
\begin{aligned}
A_{ij} &= q_i^{T}\Sigma\ell_j^{\perp},\\
S_{ij} &> 0
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
q_i\ \text{improves the controlled description of}\ r_j\ \text{after}\ Z.
\end{aligned}
$$
The finite row verifies only that a proxy $A_{ij}$ contact is above threshold.  It does not verify a positive partial $R^2$, a description-length improvement, or an ablation result against the M-density baseline for ribosome profiling, tAI, stAI, or protein abundance.  Consequently the current chapter records an open survival matrix with nonzero proxy contact rather than an established translation-survival entry.

\paragraph{Local boundary.}
The attached contacts are deliberately narrow.  Codon-usage tables report sequence composition; tRNA gene-copy counts report annotated genomic copy number; tAI or stAI weights are model-derived codon adaptation annotations; ribosome profiling would supply a translation-efficiency or occupancy contact only when organism-matched rows are attached; matched mRNA abundance is a control for separating transcript abundance from translation efficiency; proteomics is a downstream abundance readout only when identifier-matched protein rows are present.  None of these rows, as currently recorded, establishes ribosome-mediated translation realization for $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$, protein folding, physical admissibility, biological function, mechanism, or universality.  Any one of those conclusions would require its own explicitly attached contact and its own statistic rather than inheritance from the codon residual geometry or from identifier routing.

\paragraph{Status.}
The present state is therefore a bounded positive proxy-contact record and a blocked survival-matrix record.  The data are sufficient to say that all nine named residual coordinates were computed, that the three-organism proxy coupling did not collapse under the $0.01$ threshold, and that the Leu CUN/UUR companion row has $n=3$ Pearson and partial Pearson values equal to $1.0$ with nonuniform signs.  The data are not sufficient to assert $S_{ij}>0$ for any translation readout under $Z$.  The next decisive row is an organism-matched table containing codon usage, direct translation readouts or codon adaptation weights, optional protein abundance, matched mRNA controls, and the declared baseline ablation, from which the signs and magnitudes of $S_{ij}$ can be reported entry by entry.
