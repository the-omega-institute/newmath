\subsection{A bounded residual-codon coupling matrix for the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ translation interface}
\label{subsec:b-star-q6-coupling-matrix}

\paragraph{Finite row.}
This record isolates the nine synonymous-residual coordinates of $B^{*}_{Q6}$ and asks whether any of them remains visible after the amino-acid quotient and ordinary sequence-context controls have been imposed.  The finite carrier is a $9\times K$ matrix whose row coordinates are
$$
\begin{aligned}
&K_{AAA},\quad Arg_{AGR},\quad Ile_{AUA},\quad Leu_{CUN/UUR},\quad Leu_{UUA/UUG},\\
&Ser_{UCR/AGY},\quad Ser_{UCA/UCG},\quad Thr_{ACR/ACY},\quad f3\text{-}stress .
\end{aligned}
$$
For the direct coupling run, the codon-count space had dimension $61$ and the loaded organisms were \textit{Homo sapiens}, \textit{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}, and \textit{Escherichia coli} K12.  The readout in that run was not ribosome profiling and not measured protein output; it was the tRNA-Leu CUN-versus-UUR log-copy spectrum.  The break rule was
$$
\begin{aligned}
\text{break if}\quad |A_{ij}|<0.01\quad\text{for all nine residual coordinates against the proxy readout}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The break condition was not met.  All nine entries were nonzero at the declared threshold, and the largest absolute coupling was
$$
\begin{aligned}
\max_{i,j}|A_{ij}|=1882464.1106377328 .
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the observed finite row says that the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual directions are not numerically silent against this Leu-copy proxy.  It does not, by itself, say that any coordinate survives as a controlled translation readout.

\paragraph{Controlled modeled-readout surface.}
A separate powered row used modeled per-codon tAI weights derived from tRNA gene-copy counts and dos Reis wobble weights.  That row covered $40$ organisms, used the same nine $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates as both coordinate and modeled-readout columns, and produced a $9\times 9$ modeled survival surface.  The control design had $24$ columns, rank $23$, and residual degrees of freedom $17$.  The residualized coordinate block had rank $9$, and the reported modeled translation-readout fit was
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^{2}_{QT}=0.9003849000036579 .
\end{aligned}
$$
The run was rank-sufficient under the declared degeneracy cutoff $0.999999$, since the observed $R^{2}_{QT}$ stayed below that cutoff.  Positive partial-$R^{2}$ entries were judged against threshold $10^{-12}$.  This supplies a bounded modeled tAI survival statement, not a measured ribosome-occupancy statement.  The readout is an adaptation-weight construction from codon usage and tRNA-copy annotation; it is not ribo-seq, not measured elongation speed, not proteomics, and not a protein-folding or function assay.

\paragraph{Complement and mediation diagnostics.}
The complement-support run examined six organism-specific joined matrices with residualized $Q$, modeled $T$, protein-abundance $P$, and controls $Z$.  The joined row counts were $19053$ for \textit{Homo sapiens}, $5815$ for \textit{S. cerevisiae}, $3739$ for \textit{E. coli} K12 MG1655, $14410$ for \textit{Danio rerio}, $12418$ for \textit{Gallus gallus}, and $4129$ for \textit{Mycobacterium smegmatis} str. mc2 155.  Each organism used $24$ control columns of rank $23$.

The modeled complement projection reported the following $R^{2}_{QP}$ values:
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^{2}_{QP}(\textit{H. sapiens})&=0.04454565117192484,\\
R^{2}_{QP}(\textit{S. cerevisiae})&=0.29837624987287115,\\
R^{2}_{QP}(\textit{E. coli})&=0.10767526667768725,\\
R^{2}_{QP}(\textit{D. rerio})&=0.04613529805760884,\\
R^{2}_{QP}(\textit{G. gallus})&=0.03980793595345331,\\
R^{2}_{QP}(\textit{M. smegmatis})&=0.032090091625816136 .
\end{aligned}
$$
The same row reported complement-mediated fractions
$$
\begin{aligned}
m(\textit{H. sapiens})&=0.07910506443205445,\\
m(\textit{S. cerevisiae})&=0.788267804272933,\\
m(\textit{E. coli})&=0.3370039961421468,\\

$$
Only the \textit{M. smegmatis} complement fraction matched the separate mediation reference within $0.02$, with absolute difference $0.018756768926738165$.  The other absolute differences were $0.19295782716913712$ for \textit{H. sapiens}, $0.16088729984876438$ for \textit{S. cerevisiae}, $0.27591825665152014$ for \textit{E. coli}, $0.19033411641073011$ for \textit{D. rerio}, and $0.13772457710273214$ for \textit{G. gallus}.  The complement readback therefore identifies where a modeled-tAI-complement abundance residual lies in the nine-coordinate basis, but it is not a causal mechanism and does not establish that translation mediates abundance in the measured biological sense.

\paragraph{Three-organism Leu gate.}
The Leu CUN/UUR contact gives a deliberately small finite-row check.  Across \textit{H. sapiens}, \textit{S. cerevisiae}, and \textit{E. coli} K12, the reported Pearson value and the amino-acid-controlled partial Pearson value were both $1.0$ with $n=3$.  The organism-level log-ratio signs were not uniform:
$$
\begin{aligned}
(1.3514980212896148,\;-0.24869078488829677,\;1.0086640520051702)
\end{aligned}
$$
with signs $(1,-1,1)$.  The statistic is therefore a finite-row contact, not a population estimate and not a universal direction law.  Its role is to show that the Leu split is experimentally addressable with paired codon-usage and tRNA-copy summaries; it does not transform tRNA gene-copy ratio into tRNA abundance, charging state, ribosome transit, protein output, or biological function.

\paragraph{Local verdict.}
The coupling matrix row is retained as a positive proxy-coupling record: the stated null $|A_{ij}|<0.01$ for all nine coordinates was false, and the maximum absolute coupling was $1882464.1106377328$.  The powered modeled-tAI row supplies a separate, controlled modeled-readout surface with $R^{2}_{QT}=0.9003849000036579$, rank $9$ after residualization, and residual degrees of freedom $17$.  The stronger measured-survival claim remains narrower: a positive $A_{ij}$ is not automatically a positive $S_{ij}$, and modeled tAI is not the same object as ribosome profiling, translation efficiency measured by RPF over mRNA, or protein abundance.  No ribosome-mediated translation realization, folding, physical admissibility, biological function, biochemical mechanism, phenotype, or universal biological law follows from this chapter unless an additional matched contact supplies that layer and the corresponding controlled statistic is computed.
