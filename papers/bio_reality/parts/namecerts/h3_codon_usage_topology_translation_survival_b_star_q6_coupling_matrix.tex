\subsection{B-star Q6 residual coupling against a translation-proxy spectrum}\label{subsec:bioreality-b-star-q6-coupling-matrix}\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite row.}
This record tests a bounded version of the $B^*_{Q6}$ translation-survival question by first computing the synonymous residual coordinates and then asking whether the observed proxy coupling is already forced to vanish. The finite computation used the $61$-codon count space and loaded three organism rows: Homo sapiens, Saccharomyces cerevisiae, and Escherichia coli K12. The nine residual coordinates were
$$\begin{aligned}
&\mathrm{Arg}_{AGR},\ \mathrm{Ile}_{AUA},\ \mathrm{K}_{AAA},\ \mathrm{Leu}_{CUN/UUR},\ \mathrm{Leu}_{UUA/UUG},\\
&\mathrm{Ser}_{UCA/UCG},\ \mathrm{Ser}_{UCR/AGY},\ \mathrm{Thr}_{ACR/ACY},\ \mathrm{and}\ f_3\text{-stress}.
\end{aligned}$$
The readout available in this row was not a ribosome-profiling, protein-abundance, tAI, or stAI measurement. It was the organism-level tRNA-Leu $CUN$-versus-$UUR$ log-copy spectrum, used only as a proxy contact for a first non-vanishing screen. The computation kept the amino-acid quotient explicit through the synonymous projection $P_\tau$, so the reported signal is not a raw codon-composition comparison across amino-acid classes.

\paragraph{Coupling statistic.}
For a residual coordinate $q_i$ and the projected proxy readout vector $\ell^{\perp}$, the finite-row coupling was evaluated as
$$\begin{aligned}
A_i=q_i^{T}\Sigma\ell^{\perp},
\end{aligned}$$
where $\Sigma$ is the residual count covariance on the loaded organism table. The declared break condition for this proxy screen was
$$\begin{aligned}
|A_i| < 0.01\quad\text{for all nine residual coordinates }q_i.
\end{aligned}$$
That break condition was not met. All nine entries were nonzero above the stated threshold, and the largest absolute coupling was
$$\begin{aligned}
\max_i |A_i|=1882464.1106377328.
\end{aligned}$$
Thus the finite row rules out the narrow null in which the nine $B^*_{Q6}$ residual directions are all numerically invisible to this tRNA-Leu proxy spectrum at threshold $0.01$.

\paragraph{Leucine sign check.}
A related three-organism leucine gate compared $\log(CUN/UUR)$ codon usage with the corresponding tRNA-Leu copy-ratio contact. On the three loaded organisms the reported Pearson correlation was $1.0$, and the amino-acid-composition-controlled partial Pearson value was also $1.0$ with $n=3$. The organism-level log-ratio signs were not uniform:
$$\begin{aligned}
(1.3514980212896148,\ -0.24869078488829677,\ 1.0086640520051702),
\end{aligned}$$
with signs $(1,-1,1)$. This is a finite-row contact, not a population estimate. The sign reversal prevents reading the row as a universal directionality statement, even though the three-row partial correlation is positive.

\paragraph{Survival status.}
The matrix sought by the biological conjecture is the controlled survival matrix $S_{ij}=S_{q_i}(r_j\mid Z)$, where $r_j$ ranges over direct translation-related readouts such as tAI, stAI, ribosome occupancy, translation efficiency, or protein abundance, and where
$$\begin{aligned}
Z=(\text{amino-acid composition},\ \text{organism context},\ GC3,\ \text{CDS length},\ M\text{-density baseline}).
\end{aligned}$$
The available finite row supplies a proxy coupling matrix entry family $A_i$, not the controlled survival matrix $S_{ij}$. In particular, no positive $S_{ij}$ entry is asserted here, because the row does not contain organism-matched ribosome-profiling, stAI, tAI, proteomics, and matched mRNA-control data joined to the residual coordinates under the full control set $Z$. The correct readback is therefore asymmetric: the proxy screen is nonzero, while the translation-survival claim remains unpromoted until partial $R^2$ or description-length improvement is computed under the declared controls.

\paragraph{Local boundary.}
This chapter records only the finite non-vanishing of the $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coupling against the tRNA-Leu $CUN/UUR$ proxy and the associated three-organism leucine sign behavior. It does not establish ribosome-mediated translation realization, tRNA abundance, tRNA charging, protein output, folding, structural order, physical admissibility, biochemical function, mechanism, or a universal biological law. Each of those conclusions would require its own matched measurement contact and its own controlled statistic. The present row is useful because it identifies a concrete residual-coordinate interface that survives the first proxy null test, while keeping the stronger translation and downstream biological readings outside the asserted content.
