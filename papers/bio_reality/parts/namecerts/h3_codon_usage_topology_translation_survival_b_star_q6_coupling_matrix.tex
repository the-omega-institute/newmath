\subsection{B-star-Q6 coupling rows before translation-survival promotion}\label{subsec:b-star-q6-coupling-rows-before-translation-survival-promotion}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Question fixed by the finite row.}
The record tests whether the nine synonymous residual coordinates in the $B^*_{Q6}$ codon-usage basis already show a non-trivial coupling against a bounded translation-adjacent proxy before any claim about realized translation survival is made. The codon-count carrier has dimension $61$, and the computed residual coordinate list is
$$
\begin{aligned}
R = (&\mathrm{Arg}_{AGR},\ \mathrm{Ile}_{AUA},\ \mathrm{K}_{AAA},\ \mathrm{Leu}_{CUN/UUR},\ \mathrm{Leu}_{UUA/UUG},\\
&\mathrm{Ser}_{UCA/UCG},\ \mathrm{Ser}_{UCR/AGY},\ \mathrm{Thr}_{ACR/ACY},\ f3\text{-}\mathrm{stress}).
\end{aligned}
$$
The organism row is
$$
\begin{aligned}
O=(\mathrm{homo\_sapiens},\ \mathrm{saccharomyces\_cerevisiae},\ \mathrm{escherichia\_coli\_k12}).
\end{aligned}
$$
For each coordinate $q_i$, the computation forms a synonymous residual projection before reading coupling against the available proxy spectrum. The proxy readout in this row is the tRNA-Leu $CUN$-versus-$UUR$ log-copy spectrum; direct tAI, stAI, ribosome-profiling, and protein-abundance readouts are not present in the row.

\paragraph{Coupling statistic.}
The finite computation uses the proxy coupling entry
$$
\begin{aligned}
A_{ij}=q_i^{\mathsf T}\Sigma\ell_j^{\perp},
\end{aligned}
$$
where $q_i$ is one of the nine residual coordinates, $\Sigma$ is the residual count covariance, and $\ell_j^{\perp}$ is the synonymous projection of the readout weight. The break threshold for the proxy matrix was $|A_{ij}|<0.01$ for all nine coordinates. That break condition was not met: all nine entries were nonzero relative to the threshold, and the largest absolute proxy coupling was
$$
\begin{aligned}
\max_{i,j}|A_{ij}|=1882464.1106377328.
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the finite row records a non-absorbed codon-topology signal at the proxy-coupling level. It does not record a positive survival score $S_{ij}$, because $S_{ij}$ requires a separate controlled translation readout and a partial-$R^2$ or description-length calculation after the declared control set has been imposed.

\paragraph{Leu CUN/UUR gate row.}
A linked three-organism Leu gate row compares the organism-level $CUN/UUR$ codon-usage contrast with the tRNA-Leu copy-ratio contact. In that finite row the Pearson correlation is $1.0$, and the reported partial Pearson value after the available control is also $1.0$ with $n=3$. The observed log-ratio vector is
$$
\begin{aligned}
(1.3514980212896148,\ -0.24869078488829677,\ 1.0086640520051702),
\end{aligned}
$$
with signs
$$
\begin{aligned}
(+1,\ -1,\ +1).
\end{aligned}
$$
The sign is therefore not uniform across organisms. The row is evidence for a finite organism-indexed contact between the Leu $CUN/UUR$ codon contrast and a tRNA-Leu copy-number spectrum, but it is not a population estimate, and it is not a universal direction claim.

\paragraph{Controls required for survival.}
The conjectural survival matrix is the $9\times K$ matrix
$$
\begin{aligned}
S_{ij}=S_{q_i}(r_j\mid Z),
\end{aligned}
$$
where the readouts $r_j$ may include tAI, stAI, ribosome occupancy, translation efficiency, or matched protein abundance, and where the control block is
$$
\begin{aligned}
Z=(\mathrm{amino\ acid\ composition},\ \mathrm{organism},\ GC3,\ \mathrm{CDS\ length},\ M\text{-}\mathrm{density\ baseline}).
\end{aligned}
$$
The present row supplies the nine-coordinate residual basis, the $61$-codon count space, the three-organism contact, the proxy coupling threshold $0.01$, the nonzero proxy-coupling count $9$, and the maximal proxy magnitude $1882464.1106377328$. It does not supply a completed $S_{ij}$ table. A positive survival entry would require at least one direct translation-related readout row and the explicit controlled comparison showing $S_{ij}>0$ after the amino-acid quotient, organism context, $GC3$, length, and $M$-density baseline have all been included.

\paragraph{Local boundary of interpretation.}
This chapter records a codon-usage topology contact and a translation-adjacent proxy coupling. It does not establish ribosome-mediated translation realization, tRNA abundance, tRNA charging, protein output, folded structure, physical admissibility, biochemical function, mechanism, pathway membership, or a universal biological law. The tRNA gene-copy row is treated only as an annotated copy-number spectrum. The ribosome-profiling, tAI or stAI, proteomics, and matched mRNA contacts remain separate requirements for the survival matrix. In particular, a positive $A_{ij}$ entry is not promoted to a positive $S_{ij}$ entry without the declared $Z$-controlled partial-$R^2$ or description-length survival estimate.

\paragraph{Finite-row conclusion.}
The available finite data support the following bounded statement. In the three-organism row, the nine $B^*_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates are not all annihilated by the tRNA-Leu $CUN/UUR$ proxy spectrum under the stated $0.01$ coupling threshold; the maximum absolute proxy coupling is $1882464.1106377328$, and all nine residual-coordinate entries are nonzero at that threshold. A linked Leu $CUN/UUR$ row has Pearson $1.0$ and partial Pearson $1.0$ at $n=3$, while also showing organism-dependent sign reversal through the sign vector $(+1,-1,+1)$. These facts justify keeping the survival-matrix probe open, but they do not by themselves verify translation survival. The next mathematical object needed for promotion is an organism-matched matrix of direct translation readouts with explicit $S_{ij}$ entries computed after the full control block $Z$ and the simpler-codon-baseline ablation.
