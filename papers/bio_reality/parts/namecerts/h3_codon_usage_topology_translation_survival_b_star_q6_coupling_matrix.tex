\subsection{B-star-Q6 Codon-Usage Coupling Against Translation-Layer Readbacks}
\label{subsec:bioreality-b-star-q6-codon-usage-coupling-translation-readbacks}

\paragraph{Question.}
The packet asks whether the nine synonymous-residual coordinates in $B^*_{Q6}$ leave a detectable finite-row trace after the amino-acid quotient has been imposed. The coordinates used in the computation are $\mathrm{Arg\_AGR}$, $\mathrm{Ile\_AUA}$, $\mathrm{K\_AAA}$, $\mathrm{Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR}$, $\mathrm{Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG}$, $\mathrm{Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG}$, $\mathrm{Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY}$, $\mathrm{Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY}$, and $f3\_\mathrm{stress}$. They live in the $61$-codon count space after synonymous projection. For each residual direction $q_i$, the finite test forms a coupling entry against a translation-adjacent readout direction $\ell_j^{\perp}$ by computing a residual-covariance pairing
$$
\begin{aligned}
A_{ij} &= q_i^{T}\Sigma\ell_j^{\perp}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The intended survival statistic is stricter than this coupling: it asks for a controlled partial-$R^2$ or description-length improvement $S_{ij}$ after amino-acid composition, organism identity, GC3, coding-sequence length, and the simpler $M$-density baseline have been included in the control family $Z$.

\paragraph{Finite coupling row.}
The direct coupling run used three organism-level rows: $\mathrm{homo\_sapiens}$, $\mathrm{saccharomyces\_cerevisiae}$, and $\mathrm{escherichia\_coli\_k12}$. The readout was not ribosome profiling, protein abundance, or measured elongation. It was a proxy spectrum: the tRNA-Leu CUN-versus-UUR log-copy contrast. Under the declared break rule, the packet would have been null if all nine residual coordinates had $|A_{ij}| < 0.01$ against this proxy. That break condition was not met. All nine entries were nonzero above the threshold, and the largest absolute coupling was
$$
\begin{aligned}
\max_{i,j}|A_{ij}| &= 1882464.1106377328,\\
\#\{(i,j): |A_{ij}| > 0.01\} &= 9.
\end{aligned}
$$
This is a codon-usage topology contact, not yet a measured translation-survival result. Its value is that the amino-acid quotient did not erase the nine named residual directions before any measured translation row was attached.

\paragraph{Modeled translation-weight survival row.}
A separate modeled tAI computation supplied a stronger but still bounded translation-weight readback. It used $40$ organisms and the same nine residual coordinates. The modeled readout was per-codon tAI derived from GtRNAdb anticodon copy counts and dos Reis wobble weights, then projected onto the $B^*_{Q6}$ synonymous-residual coordinate family. The residualized control design had $24$ control columns, rank $23$, and $17$ residual degrees of freedom after $Z$ was removed. The residual design for the nine $Q$ coordinates had rank $9$, and the reported matrix shape was $9\times 9$. The modeled $Q$-to-$T$ fit was
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{QT} &= 0.9003849000036579,
\end{aligned}
$$
with the degeneracy threshold recorded as $0.999999$. Thus the row is rank-powered relative to the earlier three-organism surface: it is not the all-perfect, rank-saturated case. The positivity threshold for partial-$R^2$ entries was $10^{-12}$. This supports a modeled translation-weight survival reading, but only for the tAI construction from tRNA gene-copy and wobble-weight annotations; it is not ribosome occupancy, measured translation efficiency, proteomics, folding, or function.

\paragraph{Leu CUN/UUR finite-row stress test.}
The Leu CUN-versus-UUR contrast was also tested as a three-organism association against tRNA-Leu copy ratios. The finite row again used $\mathrm{homo\_sapiens}$, $\mathrm{saccharomyces\_cerevisiae}$, and $\mathrm{escherichia\_coli\_k12}$. The reported Pearson value and amino-acid-composition-controlled partial Pearson value were both $1.0$ at $n=3$:
$$
\begin{aligned}
r_{\mathrm{Pearson}} &= 1.0,\\
r_{\mathrm{partial}} &= 1.0,\\
n &= 3.
\end{aligned}
$$
The organism-level log-ratio signs were not uniform. The three log-ratios were $1.3514980212896148$, $-0.24869078488829677$, and $1.0086640520051702$, with signs $+1$, $-1$, and $+1$. This prevents a universal-direction reading. The row is useful as a finite contact showing that the Leu split has a visible organism-scoped tRNA-copy relation, while also showing that sign direction is context-dependent and that the sample size is too small for a population-level claim.

\paragraph{Translation-complement readback.}
A six-organism complement projection further localized which residual coordinates carry the modeled-tAI-complement abundance residual. For $\mathrm{danio\_rerio}$, $\mathrm{escherichia\_coli\_k12\_mg1655}$, $\mathrm{gallus\_gallus}$, $\mathrm{homo\_sapiens}$, $\mathrm{mycobacterium\_smegmatis\_str\_mc2\_155}$, and $\mathrm{saccharomyces\_cerevisiae}$, each $a_{\perp}$ vector had dimension $9$ and total absolute support fraction $1.0$ up to floating-point roundoff. The reported complement fractions were
$$
\begin{aligned}
m_{\mathrm{danio}} &= 0.12537127895073397,\\
m_{\mathrm{ecoli}} &= 0.3370039961421468,\\
m_{\mathrm{gallus}} &= 0.05996581936972245,\\
m_{\mathrm{human}} &= 0.07910506443205445,\\
m_{\mathrm{mycobacterium}} &= 0.10273279812535108,\\
m_{\mathrm{yeast}} &= 0.788267804272933.
\end{aligned}
$$
The corresponding $R^2_{QP}$ values were $0.04613529805760884$, $0.10767526667768725$, $0.03980793595345331$, $0.04454565117192484$, $0.032090091625816136$, and $0.29837624987287115$. The dominant support coordinates varied by organism: for example, human was led by $\mathrm{Arg\_AGR}$, $\mathrm{Ile\_AUA}$, $\mathrm{Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG}$, $\mathrm{K\_AAA}$, and $f3\_\mathrm{stress}$, whereas yeast was led by $\mathrm{Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY}$, $\mathrm{Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR}$, $\mathrm{Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG}$, $\mathrm{Arg\_AGR}$, $\mathrm{Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY}$, and $f3\_\mathrm{stress}$. This again favors an organism-scoped residual-coordinate reading over a single universal coordinate direction.

\paragraph{Interpretation.}
The finite rows support the following bounded statement. The nine-coordinate $B^*_{Q6}$ residual family is not immediately absorbed by the amino-acid quotient and simple context controls. It has a nonzero proxy coupling against a tRNA-Leu copy-spectrum row, a rank-powered modeled-tAI survival surface across $40$ organisms with $R^2_{QT}=0.9003849000036579$, and organism-specific complement support in a six-organism abundance-related projection. These are contacts with codon usage, tRNA-copy annotations, and modeled translation weights. They do not by themselves establish measured ribosome-mediated translation realization. They also do not establish folding, structural order, physical admissibility, molecular function, pathway mechanism, phenotype, or a universal biological law. Any such promotion requires its own layer-matched measurement and its own controlled statistic.

\paragraph{Local verdict.}
The packet is ready as a BioReality record of bounded codon-usage and modeled translation-weight contact. It should not be stated as a completed measured survival matrix for ribosome profiling, translation efficiency, or protein abundance. The correct local conclusion is narrower: at least one finite-row interface between $B^*_{Q6}$ and translation-adjacent readbacks survives the amino-acid quotient in the recorded modeled or proxy settings, while the measured-ribosome and protein-output claims remain outside the demonstrated row set.
