\subsection{B$^{*}_{Q6}$ residual coupling against a translation-adjacent proxy}
\label{sec:bioreality-b-star-q6-coupling-matrix}

\paragraph{Record.}
This record isolates a finite codon-usage contact for the conjecture that the nine B$^{*}_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates are not automatically erased by amino-acid composition and context controls when translation-related readouts are later attached. The available computation is not yet a survival matrix $S_{ij}$ on ribosome profiling, tAI, stAI, or protein abundance. It is a proxy coupling table against the organism-level tRNA-Leu CUN-versus-UUR log-copy spectrum, using codon-count rows from three organisms and keeping the amino-acid quotient explicit through the synonymous projection. The loaded organisms are Homo sapiens, Saccharomyces cerevisiae, and Escherichia coli K12. The codon-count ambient space has $61$ sense-codon coordinates.

\paragraph{Residual coordinates.}
The finite row computes all nine declared B$^{*}_{Q6}$ residual directions:
$$
\begin{aligned}
R = \{&\mathrm{Arg\_AGR},\ \mathrm{Ile\_AUA},\ \mathrm{K\_AAA},\ \mathrm{Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR},\ 
\mathrm{Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG},\\
&\mathrm{Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG},\ \mathrm{Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY},\ \mathrm{Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY},\ \mathrm{f3\_stress}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
For each residual coordinate $q_i$, the computation forms a covariance coupling $A_{ij}$ against the proxy readout direction. The declared null cutoff is $|A_{ij}| < 0.01$ for every one of the nine rows. That cutoff is not met: the number of nonzero entries is $9$, and the largest observed absolute coupling is
$$
\begin{aligned}
\max_i |A_{ij}| = 1882464.1106377328.
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the finite proxy table rejects the narrow null in which every B$^{*}_{Q6}$ residual direction is numerically invisible to this tRNA-Leu copy-ratio spectrum at the $0.01$ threshold.

\paragraph{CUN/UUR Leu gate row.}
A related three-organism row tests the Leu CUN-versus-UUR codon-usage ratio against the tRNA-Leu copy-ratio contact. On this finite table the reported Pearson correlation is $1.0$, and the partial Pearson value after the recorded control is also $1.0$ with $n=3$. The organism-level log-ratio signs are not uniform. The observed log-ratio list is
$$
\begin{aligned}
(1.3514980212896148,\ -0.24869078488829677,\ 1.0086640520051702),
\end{aligned}
$$
with sign pattern
$$
\begin{aligned}
(+1,\ -1,\ +1).
\end{aligned}
$$
The row therefore supplies a finite association contact and, at the same time, records context dependence: the sign pattern is mixed rather than universal. The value $1.0$ is a three-row statistic, not a population estimate.

\paragraph{What is supported.}
The supported statement is limited to proxy-level codon-topology contact. All nine B$^{*}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates were computed in the $61$-codon count space, and at least one coupling exceeds the declared null threshold; in fact all nine entries are nonzero against the chosen proxy readout. The computation also records that the amino-acid composition quotient is not ignored: the synonymous projection is part of the readback. This is meaningful because the conjecture asks whether residual synonymous geometry remains visible after amino-acid content has been quotiented away. At the present finite-row level, the answer is that the proxy tRNA-Leu spectrum is not orthogonal to the residual basis under the declared coupling test.

\paragraph{What is not supported.}
No positive survival entry $S_{ij} > 0$ is established here. The calculation reports proxy couplings $A_{ij}$, not Z-controlled partial $R^2$ or description-length improvement values for ribosome occupancy, translation efficiency, tAI, stAI, or protein abundance. In particular, a positive or large $A_{ij}$ is not promoted to a survival score without fitting the stated control model
$$
\begin{aligned}
Z = (&\mathrm{amino\ acid\ composition},\ \mathrm{organism\ context},\ \mathrm{GC3},\\
&\mathrm{sequence\ length},\ \mathrm{M\mbox{-}density\ baseline}).
\end{aligned}
$$
The mRNA contact, when present, is only a covariate for separating transcript abundance from translation-efficiency readout. The tRNA gene-copy contact is not treated as direct tRNA abundance, tRNA charging, ribosome occupancy, translation efficiency, or protein output.

\paragraph{Local boundary.}
This chapter therefore records a finite proxy coupling and an open survival test. It does not infer ribosome-mediated translation, folding, physical admissibility, biological function, biochemical mechanism, or universality. Each of those conclusions would require its own matched contact and its own statistic. The next admissible survival statement would require organism-matched codon usage, a direct translation readout such as ribosome profiling or tAI/stAI, and the full Z-controlled partial-$R^2$ or description-length calculation for the matrix entries $S_{ij}$.
