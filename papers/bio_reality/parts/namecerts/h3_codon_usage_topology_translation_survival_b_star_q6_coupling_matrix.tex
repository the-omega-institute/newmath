\subsection{Residual codon-usage coordinates against translation-layer readouts}
\label{sec:b-star-q6-translation-survival-coupling-matrix}

\paragraph{Finite object.}
The record concerns the nine synonymous-residual coordinates of $B^{*}_{Q6}$ after quotienting by amino-acid identity. The coordinate list is
$$
\begin{aligned}
&K_{AAA},\quad Arg_{AGR},\quad Ile_{AUA},\quad Leu_{CUN/UUR},\quad Leu_{UUA/UUG},\\
&Ser_{UCR/AGY},\quad Ser_{UCA/UCG},\quad Thr_{ACR/ACY},\quad f3_{stress}.
\end{aligned}
$$
For a translation-related readout $r_j$, the intended survival entry is a controlled residual score $S_{ij}=S_{q_i}(r_j\mid Z)$, where $Z$ contains amino-acid composition, organism context, GC3, coding-sequence length, and the simpler $M$-density baseline. The finite matrix target is therefore a $9\times K$ table of entries indexed by residual coordinate $q_i$ and readout $r_j$, together with a signed coupling statistic
$$
\begin{aligned}
A_{ij}=q_i^{T}\Sigma \ell_j^{\perp},
\end{aligned}
$$
where $\ell_j^{\perp}$ is the readout weight after synonymous projection. A positive matrix entry is not identified with a positive coupling: survival requires a separate controlled partial-$R^2$ or description-length calculation after the declared controls have been applied.

\paragraph{Proxy coupling row.}
One finite contact used three organisms, namely Homo sapiens, Saccharomyces cerevisiae, and Escherichia coli K12. In that row the readout was the tRNA-Leu $CUN$-versus-$UUR$ log-copy spectrum, so the computation tested a codon-usage and tRNA-copy proxy rather than a direct ribosome or protein measurement. The codon-count space had size $61$. All nine $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates were computed, and the break rule was set at $|A_{ij}|<0.01$ for every coordinate against the proxy readout. The break rule was not met: the table reported $9$ nonzero entries and a maximum absolute coupling
$$
\begin{aligned}
\max_{i,j}|A_{ij}|=1882464.1106377328.
\end{aligned}
$$
This is a finite non-null coupling row for the residual codon-usage geometry. It is not a survival row, because the proxy readout is tRNA-copy based and the computation does not contain a ribosome-profiling, stAI, tAI, or protein-abundance survival estimate under the full control set $Z$.

\paragraph{Leucine gate row.}
The same three-organism scale also produced a CUN/UUR leucine association contact. The reported Pearson correlation was $1.0$, and the partial Pearson value against the tRNA-Leu ratio was also $1.0$ with $n=3$. The organism-level log-ratio signs were
$$
\begin{aligned}
(1, -1, 1),
\end{aligned}
$$
with corresponding log-ratio values
$$
\begin{aligned}
1.3514980212896148,\quad -0.24869078488829677,\quad 1.0086640520051702.
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the sign is explicitly not uniform across the organisms. The row is useful as a finite contact showing that the leucine split is visible to organism-level codon and tRNA-copy summaries, but it is too small to infer a population direction, a universal law, or a translation mechanism. The sign reversal is part of the readback and prevents any single-direction interpretation.

\paragraph{Modeled tAI survival row.}
A larger modeled-translation row used $40$ organisms and computed modeled per-codon tAI readouts from GtRNAdb anticodon copy counts with dos Reis wobble weights. The modeled readout was projected onto the same nine $B^{*}_{Q6}$ synonymous-residual coordinates, producing a $9\times 9$ matrix. The control design had $24$ columns, rank $23$, and residual degrees of freedom $17$ after residualizing by $Z$. The residual coordinate design retained rank $9$. The reported global controlled fit was
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{QT}=0.9003849000036579,
\end{aligned}
$$
well below the degeneracy cutoff $0.999999$. The positive-entry threshold was $10^{-12}$. This row supports the statement that the powered modeled-tAI interface is numerically nondegenerate and has enough residual rank to evaluate the declared $9\times 9$ survival table. Its layer is modeled translation readout, not observed ribosome occupancy or protein output.

\paragraph{Boundary of interpretation.}
The bounded conclusion is that $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual codon-usage coordinates survive as nontrivial finite-row objects at two levels: first as a three-organism proxy coupling table with nine nonzero coupling entries above the $0.01$ break threshold, and second as a forty-organism modeled-tAI matrix with rank-$9$ residual coordinates and $R^2_{QT}=0.9003849000036579$ after a rank-$23$ control residualization. These facts do not establish measured ribosome-mediated translation efficiency, protein abundance mediation, folded structure, physical admissibility, biological function, mechanism, phenotype, or universality. A direct translation-survival assertion for ribosome occupancy, ribo-seq translation efficiency, stAI, or proteomics requires organism-matched readout rows and the same controlled partial-$R^2$ or description-length survival statistic. A positive $A_{ij}$ entry alone remains a coupling datum; it is not promoted to $S_{ij}>0$ without the declared controlled survival calculation.
