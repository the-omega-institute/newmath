\subsection{B-Star Q6 Translation-Survival Matrix}\label{sec:b-star-q6-translation-survival-matrix}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Question.}
This record tests whether the nine synonymous-residual coordinates of $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ retain measurable explanatory mass after the amino-acid quotient and ordinary sequence-context controls have already been imposed. The carrier is a $9\times K$ survival matrix $S_{ij}$ whose rows are the residual coordinates
$K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $Leu_{CUN/UUR}$, $Leu_{UUA/UUG}$, $Ser_{UCR/AGY}$, $Ser_{UCA/UCG}$, $Thr_{ACR/ACY}$, and $f3$-stress, and whose columns are translation-relevant readouts such as modeled tAI, stAI, ribosome occupancy, translation efficiency, or protein abundance when the latter is explicitly attached as a downstream readout. The local null is not that codon usage is biologically irrelevant. The local null is sharper: after controlling for amino-acid composition, organism context, $GC3$, coding-sequence length, and the simpler $M$-density baseline, no $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ coordinate improves the description of any permitted translation-side readout.

\paragraph{Statistic.}
For a residual coordinate $q_i$ and readout $r_j$, the computation first projects the readout weight into the synonymous quotient, giving $\ell_j^{\perp}$. It then records the covariance coupling
$$
\begin{aligned}
A_{ij} &= q_i^{T}\Sigma\ell_j^{\perp},
\end{aligned}
$$
and separately estimates the controlled survival score $S_{ij}$ by the partial $R^2$ or equivalent description-length improvement after residualizing against the control block
$$
\begin{aligned}
Z &= (\hbox{amino-acid composition},\ \hbox{organism},\ GC3,\ \hbox{length},\ M\hbox{-density baseline}).
\end{aligned}
$$
The distinction between $A_{ij}$ and $S_{ij}$ is part of the claim. A nonzero covariance entry is only a contact with the coordinate geometry; it is not a survival result unless the controlled score is positive under the declared residualization and baseline ablation.

\paragraph{Finite proxy coupling row.}
The finite proxy computation used a $61$-codon count space and loaded three organisms: Homo sapiens, Saccharomyces cerevisiae, and Escherichia coli K12. All nine listed $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ synonymous residuals were computed. Against the proxy readout given by the tRNA-Leu $CUN$-versus-$UUR$ log-copy spectrum, the break rule was that the contact would be null if all nine rows satisfied $|A_{ij}|<0.01$. That break condition was not met. The recorded threshold was $0.01$, the number of nonzero proxy-coupling entries was $9$, and the maximum absolute coupling was
$$
\begin{aligned}
\max_{i,j}|A_{ij}| &= 1882464.1106377328.
\end{aligned}
$$
This row is therefore a finite positive coupling contact, not a translation-survival conclusion. Its readout is a tRNA-copy proxy spectrum; it is not ribosome profiling, measured elongation, translation efficiency, or protein output.

\paragraph{Leucine gate row.}
A separate three-organism leucine gate checked the $CUN/UUR$ codon-usage ratio against the tRNA-Leu copy-ratio contact. The finite row recorded Pearson correlation $1.0$ and a partial Pearson value $1.0$ with $n=3$. The organism-level log-ratio signs were not uniform:
$$
\begin{aligned}
(1.3514980212896148,\ -0.24869078488829677,\ 1.0086640520051702)
\quad\hbox{with signs}\quad
(1,-1,1).
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the row supports a bounded organism-level association in the available three-row table, while explicitly refusing a universal sign law. The sign reversal is a data feature of the finite row, not a defect to be smoothed away.

\paragraph{Modeled translation-readout row.}
The stronger modeled-readout computation used $40$ organisms and the same nine $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates. It constructed a modeled per-codon tAI readout from GtRNAdb anticodon copy counts and dos Reis wobble weights, then projected the readout onto the nine synonymous-residual directions. The readout column count was $9$, the survival matrix shape was $9\times 9$, and the modeled translation-readout fit recorded
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{QT} &= 0.9003849000036579.
\end{aligned}
$$
The control block had $24$ columns with rank $23$, leaving residual degrees of freedom $17$ after residualization. The residual coordinate design had rank $9$. The degeneracy guard was $R^2<0.999999$, and the positive partial-$R^2$ threshold was $10^{-12}$. Since $0.9003849000036579<0.999999$ and the rank of the residual coordinate block is full at $9$, this row is a powered modeled-tAI survival computation rather than the saturated $n=3$ surface. Its claim remains bounded to modeled translation adaptation weights derived from tRNA gene-copy and wobble rules.

\paragraph{Readback.}
The combined finite evidence has two layers. First, the three-organism proxy matrix shows that the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ rows are not all orthogonal to the tRNA-Leu copy-spectrum contact under the declared $0.01$ coupling cutoff. Second, the forty-organism modeled-tAI computation supplies a nondegenerate $9\times 9$ controlled readout surface with $R^2_{QT}=0.9003849000036579$, rank-$9$ residual coordinates, rank-$23$ controls, and $17$ residual degrees of freedom. In this bounded sense, the amino-acid quotient plus context and $M$-density baseline does not absorb every $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual direction on the modeled translation-adaptation panel.

\paragraph{Scope.}
Nothing in this chapter turns tRNA gene-copy count into tRNA abundance, charging level, ribosome occupancy, elongation speed, or protein output. Nothing here proves folding, structural order, physical admissibility, biological function, mechanism, phenotype, pathway activity, or a universal biological law. Measured ribosome profiling, matched mRNA abundance, matched proteomics, localization, interaction, structure, function, perturbation, or phenotype claims require their own layer-matched contacts and their own controlled statistics. The present record is limited to finite codon-usage, tRNA-copy, proxy-coupling, and modeled-tAI survival evidence under the stated quotient and control design.
