\subsection{The $B^*_{Q6}$ Codon-Usage Coupling Matrix}
\label{sec:b-star-q6-codon-usage-coupling-matrix}

\paragraph{Object of the finite row.}
The record tested here is the codon-usage coupling boundary for the nine synonymous-residual $B^*_{Q6}$ coordinates.  The carrier is a $9 \times K$ matrix whose rows are
$\mathrm{Arg}_{AGR}$, $\mathrm{Ile}_{AUA}$, $K_{AAA}$, $\mathrm{Leu}_{CUN/UUR}$, $\mathrm{Leu}_{UUA/UUG}$, $\mathrm{Ser}_{UCA/UCG}$, $\mathrm{Ser}_{UCR/AGY}$, $\mathrm{Thr}_{ACR/ACY}$, and $f_3$-stress.  These rows live in the $61$-sense-codon count space after the amino-acid quotient is made explicit.  The tested readout in this finite row is not ribosome occupancy or measured translation efficiency; it is the tRNA-Leu $CUN$-versus-$UUR$ log-copy spectrum.  The organisms in the row are $\mathrm{Homo\ sapiens}$, $\mathrm{Saccharomyces\ cerevisiae}$, and $\mathrm{Escherichia\ coli}\ K12$.

\paragraph{Coupling statistic.}
For a residual codon coordinate $q_i$ and a readout direction $\ell_j^{\perp}$, the matrix entry is the residual-covariance coupling
$$
\begin{aligned}
A_{ij} &= q_i^{T}\Sigma\ell_j^{\perp}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The break rule for this finite row was the following: declare the proxy contact null if all nine coordinates have $|A_{ij}| < 0.01$ against the proxy readout.  The observed row did not meet that null condition.  It reported $9$ nonzero entries above the stated coupling threshold, with maximum absolute entry
$$
\begin{aligned}
\max_{i,j}|A_{ij}| &= 1882464.1106377328,
\qquad \theta_A = 0.01.
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the finite table records a nontrivial codon-usage/tRNA-copy coupling in the residual coordinate basis.  The module label for this row is codon-usage topology, not measured translation realization.

\paragraph{Leucine gate check.}
The companion three-organism leucine gate gives a sharper readback of the same boundary.  For the $CUN/UUR$ codon-usage ratio and the tRNA-Leu copy ratio, the finite-row Pearson value is $1.0$, and the partial Pearson value after the stated control is also $1.0$ with $n=3$.  The organism-level log-ratio signs are not uniform:
$$
\begin{aligned}
(1.3514980212896148,\ -0.24869078488829677,\ 1.0086640520051702)
\end{aligned}
$$
with signs $(1,-1,1)$.  This means the row supports a finite coupling contact but does not support a universal sign law.  The sign reversal is part of the observation, not an error to be erased.

\paragraph{Modeled translation readout contrast.}
A separate powered modeled-tAI row used $40$ organisms and the same nine $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinates.  There the residualized control matrix had $24$ control columns, rank $23$, and residual degrees of freedom $17$.  The modeled per-codon tAI readout produced a $9 \times 9$ survival surface with
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{QT} &= 0.9003849000036579,
\qquad \mathrm{rank}(X_{\perp Z}) = 9.
\end{aligned}
$$
This row is important because it is powered enough to avoid the three-organism rank-saturation problem.  Its readout, however, is modeled tAI derived from tRNA gene-copy counts and wobble weights.  It is not ribosome profiling, measured elongation speed, protein output, protein folding, or a pathway-level mechanism.

\paragraph{Protein-abundance and complement rows.}
The cross-layer protein-abundance complement rows show that the $B^*_{Q6}$ abundance component can leave a large modeled-tAI complement in some organisms and a small complement in others.  The reported complement fractions $d$ for $P_Q^{\perp T}$ are $0.874628721049266$ in zebrafish, $0.6629960038578532$ in $E.\ coli$ K12 MG1655, $0.9400341806302775$ in chicken, $0.9208949355679455$ in human, $0.8972672018746489$ in $M.\ smegmatis$, and $0.21173219572706703$ in yeast.  The corresponding modeled-tAI-mediated fractions $m=1-d$ are $0.12537127895073397$, $0.3370039961421468$, $0.05996581936972245$, $0.07910506443205445$, $0.10273279812535108$, and $0.788267804272933$.  These are statistical projection decompositions only.  The yeast row is the strongest translation-complement contrast by this definition, whereas chicken and human leave most of the $B^*_{Q6}$ abundance component outside the modeled-tAI direction.

\paragraph{mRNA-stability and mediation controls.}
The yeast mRNA-stability row joined $5085$ CDS/protein records to a Neymotin half-life table with reported CDS join hit rate $0.9156038419146592$.  For residualized log$_{10}$ mRNA half-life, the $B^*_{Q6}$ matrix achieved
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{QM} &= 0.05179429044975085,
\qquad R^2_{QP} = 0.2761140373962526,
\qquad \cos(S^{QM},S^{QP}) = 0.8040935468384264.
\end{aligned}
$$
The dominant $S^{QM}$ coordinate was $\mathrm{Leu}_{CUN/UUR}$, while the same-join $S^{QP}$ dominant coordinate was $f_3$-stress.  The permutation test used $1000$ permutations and reported $p=0.000999000999000999$ for the $R^2_{QM}$ comparison.  In the mediation row, conditioning on log$_{10}$ mRNA half-life changed the protein-abundance residual association from $R^2_{QP}=0.2761140373962526$ to $R^2_{QP\mid M}=0.24851799532219632$, with absorbed variance $0.027596042074056304$ and mediation fraction $0.09994436477872035$.  The mediation null had mean $0.00019674487584783138$, $95$th percentile $0.0014571003414947088$, and right-tail permutation value $0.000999000999000999$.  These values describe an observational variance partition, not a causal mRNA-stability pathway.

\paragraph{Condition and tissue specificity.}
The yeast condition row compared YEPD and SD protein-abundance readouts on $2209$ shared proteins.  The YEPD statistic was $R^2_{QP}=0.3280825603424665$ and the SD statistic was $R^2_{QP}=0.3050385538305022$; both had $f_3$-stress as the dominant coordinate.  The two condition surfaces had Pearson similarity $0.9983880256003688$ and cosine similarity $0.9993775935182967$ under $1000$ permutations.  In human tissue proteomics, the three tissue rows were Adult B cells with $n=9244$ and $R^2_{QP}=0.03626117434944674$, Adult heart with $n=6468$ and $R^2_{QP}=0.02320896321347296$, and Adult liver with $n=8664$ and $R^2_{QP}=0.026133456228248052$.  Their dominant coordinates were $\mathrm{Arg}_{AGR}$, $\mathrm{Ser}_{UCR/AGY}$, and $\mathrm{Ile}_{AUA}$ respectively.  The pairwise cosine values were $0.6883655232187615$ for B cells versus heart, $0.8990093450963941$ for liver versus B cells, and $0.9176793373754919$ for liver versus heart.  These finite rows show that the same residual basis can be read against different biological measurement panels, but they do not turn a codon-usage coupling into a single organism-independent biological rule.

\paragraph{Candidate-readout ranking.}
The direction-aware candidate table used regularizer $\lambda=0.01$ and description-length penalty $DL_H=\log_2(1+n_{\mathrm{col}})$.  For one-column readouts this gives $DL_H=1.0$; for the twelve-column localization readout the reported value is $3.700439718141092$.  In zebrafish, the top ranked candidate was measured translation efficiency with $A_H=0.017594290015070787$, $C_H=0.6573753096184687$, $A_HC_H=0.011566051846174291$, score $0.001566051846174291$, $n=13140$, Bonferroni threshold $0.000719994971498518$, and null $95$th percentile $0.0003297458343745959$.  The zebrafish mRNA row also exceeded its Bonferroni threshold, with $A_H=0.011973033879985887$, $C_H=0.896563785384548$, and score $0.000734588577977589$.  Human had complex membership as the top ranked row, with $A_H=0.007527199987780841$, $C_H=0.8723071163246565$, and $n=18335$, while yeast had mRNA as the top ranked row, with $A_H=0.001897605472160837$, $C_H=0.6040571890503664$, and $n=5128$.  $E.\ coli$ K12 MG1655 and $M.\ smegmatis$ had no selected $H_o^*$ in this ranking table.  The ranking is therefore organism-specific and readout-specific.

\paragraph{Local conclusion.}
The finite result is a positive residual coupling row: all nine $B^*_{Q6}$ coordinates were computable in the $61$-codon space, the three-organism proxy readout produced $9$ nonzero coupling entries above $0.01$, and the largest absolute coupling was $1882464.1106377328$.  The stronger powered modeled-tAI table, the protein-abundance complement rows, the yeast mRNA-stability rows, the condition/tissue rows, and the candidate-readout ranking show that the same residual basis has measurable statistical contacts in several finite panels.  The conclusion remains bounded.  A proxy tRNA-copy spectrum is not a ribosome-profiled translation readout.  Modeled tAI is not observed elongation.  Protein abundance is not folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, mechanism, phenotype, or universality.  No such higher-layer statement follows from this chapter unless a separate layer-matched measurement supplies it and the corresponding controlled statistic is computed.
