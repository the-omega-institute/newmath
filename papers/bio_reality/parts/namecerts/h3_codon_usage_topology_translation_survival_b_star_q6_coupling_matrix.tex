\subsection{B-star Q6 codon residual coupling at the translation boundary}\label{subsec:b-star-q6-codon-residual-coupling-translation-boundary}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Object.}
This record concerns the nine residual synonymous coordinates
$K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $Leu_{CUN/UUR}$, $Leu_{UUA/UUG}$, $Ser_{UCR/AGY}$, $Ser_{UCA/UCG}$, $Thr_{ACR/ACY}$, and $f_3$-stress, evaluated inside the $61$-codon count space after the amino-acid quotient has been kept explicit.  The finite computation attached to this chapter uses three organism rows,
$homo\_sapiens$, $saccharomyces\_cerevisiae$, and $escherichia\_coli\_k12$, and measures each residual coordinate against a proxy translation-adjacent spectrum: the tRNA-Leu $CUN$-versus-$UUR$ log-copy readout.  The present datum is therefore a codon-usage and tRNA-copy coupling record, not a completed survival matrix for ribosome occupancy, tAI, stAI, translation efficiency, or protein abundance.

\paragraph{Residual coupling statistic.}
Let $q_i$ denote one of the nine synonymous residual coordinates after projection away from amino-acid composition, and let $\ell$ denote the proxy tRNA-Leu log-copy contrast used in the finite row.  The measured coupling has the form
$$
\begin{aligned}
A_i = q_i^{T}\Sigma \ell^{\perp},
\end{aligned}
$$
where $\Sigma$ is the residual count covariance in the finite organism table and $\ell^{\perp}$ is the readout contrast after the same quotient discipline is imposed.  The break test for the coupling row was declared as
$$
\begin{aligned}
|A_i| < 0.01 \quad \text{for all nine residual coordinates.}
\end{aligned}
$$
That break condition was not met.  All nine residual coordinates had nonzero proxy-coupling entries, and the largest absolute entry was
$$
\begin{aligned}
\max_i |A_i| = 1882464.1106377328.
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the finite table does not collapse the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual directions to zero against this proxy spectrum.  It establishes only that the residual codon geometry has a nontrivial covariance contact with the chosen tRNA-copy contrast in the three-organism row.

\paragraph{Leu gate row.}
A second attached row isolates the $Leu_{CUN/UUR}$ contrast against the tRNA-Leu copy-ratio contrast across the same three organisms.  The recorded Pearson coefficient is $1.0$, and the recorded partial Pearson coefficient after the stated amino-acid-composition control is also $1.0$, with $n=3$.  The organism-level log-ratio signs are not uniform:
$$
\begin{aligned}
(1.3514980212896148,\ -0.24869078488829677,\ 1.0086640520051702),
\end{aligned}
$$
and the corresponding sign vector is
$$
\begin{aligned}
(+1,\ -1,\ +1).
\end{aligned}
$$
The row is therefore a finite association contact with sign reversal, not a universal directionality statement.  The perfect coefficient at $n=3$ is recorded as a small-row statistic and must not be read as a population estimate.

\paragraph{Survival matrix still missing.}
The conjectural survival object is not the proxy coupling $A_i$ alone.  The intended survival matrix has entries
$$
\begin{aligned}
S_{ij}=S_{q_i}(r_j\mid Z),
\end{aligned}
$$
where $r_j$ ranges over direct translation-related readouts such as tAI, stAI, ribosome occupancy, translation efficiency, and protein abundance, and where the control set is
$$
\begin{aligned}
Z=(\text{amino-acid composition},\ \text{organism context},\ GC3,\ \text{length},\ \text{M-density baseline}).
\end{aligned}
$$
A positive survival entry requires a controlled partial-$R^2$ or description-length improvement after the simpler-codon baseline has been ablated.  No such $S_{ij}$ entry is present in the finite record.  The available scalar evidence is the proxy coupling threshold $0.01$, the nonzero count $9$, the maximum absolute coupling $1882464.1106377328$, the three loaded organism rows, and the Leu-gate correlations at $n=3$.

\paragraph{Local interpretation.}
The finite result supports a narrow statement: after the amino-acid quotient is respected, the nine $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates are not all annihilated by the three-organism tRNA-Leu proxy contrast.  This keeps the translation-boundary question open and identifies a concrete route for a full survival computation.  It does not yet show that any residual coordinate survives against ribosome profiling, translation efficiency, stAI, tAI, matched proteomics, or mRNA-residualized protein output.

\paragraph{Non-promotion boundary.}
No translation-realization claim follows from this proxy row by itself.  tRNA gene-copy ratios are genome-annotation summaries and are not tRNA abundance, tRNA charging, ribosome occupancy, translation efficiency, or protein output unless a separate contact supplies those measurements.  The codon-count and tRNA-copy computation also gives no evidence for protein folding, structural order, physical admissibility, biological function, biochemical mechanism, pathway membership, or a universal biological law.  Any such statement would require its own organism-matched contact and its own controlled statistic.
