\subsection{B-star Q6 residual coupling against a Leu translation proxy}
\label{subsec:h3-codon-usage-topology-translation-survival-b-star-q6-coupling-matrix}

\paragraph{Question.}
This record asks whether the nine residual coordinates of $B^*_{Q6}$ leave any detectable trace after the amino-acid quotient has already removed ordinary amino-acid composition.  The intended survival object is a matrix $S_{ij}$ whose rows are the nine synonymous residual coordinates and whose columns are translation-related readouts such as $tAI$, $stAI$, ribosome occupancy, translation efficiency, or protein abundance.  A positive entry would mean that one coordinate improves a controlled description of a readout after amino-acid composition, organism context, $GC3$, sequence length, and the simpler $M$-density baseline have already been accounted for.  The finite row presently available is weaker: it computes a proxy coupling matrix $A_{ij}$ against the organism-level $tRNA$-Leu CUN-versus-UUR log-copy spectrum, not a full survival matrix against direct translation readouts.

\paragraph{Finite data used.}
The computation was run on a $61$-codon count space and loaded three organism contexts: Homo sapiens, Saccharomyces cerevisiae, and Escherichia coli K12.  The residual rows were
$Arg\_AGR$, $Ile\_AUA$, $K\_AAA$, $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$, $Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG$, $Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG$, $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$, $Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY$, and $f3\_stress$.  The proxy readout was the $tRNA$-Leu CUN-versus-UUR log-copy spectrum.  With coupling threshold $0.01$, all nine residual rows had nonzero proxy coupling, and the largest absolute coupling reported was $1882464.1106377328$.  In the notation of the experiment,
$$
\begin{aligned}
\#\{q_i : |A_i| > 0.01\} &= 9,\\
\max_i |A_i| &= 1882464.1106377328,\\
\text{break condition met} &= \text{false}.
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the null condition for the proxy contact, namely that all nine residual coordinates have $|A_i| < 0.01$, is not satisfied in this finite row.

\paragraph{Leu gate row.}
A second finite row records the CUN/UUR Leu association across the same three organism contexts.  The reported Pearson value is $1.0$, and the amino-acid-composition-controlled partial Pearson value is also $1.0$ with $n=3$.  The organism-level log-ratio signs are not uniform: the three recorded log-ratios are $1.3514980212896148$, $-0.24869078488829677$, and $1.0086640520051702$, with signs $+1$, $-1$, and $+1$.  Consequently this row is evidence for a finite, context-sensitive proxy association, not for a universal direction.  The sign reversal is important because it prevents the three-row calculation from being read as a stable global law.

\paragraph{What is established.}
The available rows establish that the $B^*_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates are not all orthogonal to the chosen Leu copy-number proxy.  They also establish that the Leu CUN/UUR contact is visible in the three-organism table after an amino-acid-composition control, with a recorded partial Pearson value of $1.0$ at $n=3$.  In the limited notation of the proxy calculation, the positive result is a coupling observation for $A_i$, not a survival observation for $S_{ij}$.  The result therefore supports keeping the $B^*_{Q6}$ translation-survival question open: the residual coordinates have enough contact with the proxy spectrum that the stronger direct-readout test is not vacuous.

\paragraph{What remains unproved.}
No entry of the intended survival matrix $S_{ij}$ has yet been reported as positive under the full control set $Z$.  In particular, the finite rows do not contain an organism-matched ribosome-profiling, $tAI$, $stAI$, translation-efficiency, protein-abundance, or matched mRNA-residualized matrix with partial $R^2$ or description-length improvement values.  A large or nonzero $A_i$ is not interchangeable with a positive $S_{ij}$.  The stronger claim would require explicit estimates of $S_{ij}$ after amino-acid sequence, organism context, $GC3$, length, and $M$-density baseline controls, together with the simpler-codon-baseline ablation.

\paragraph{Local boundary.}
This chapter does not infer ribosome-mediated translation realization from codon usage and $tRNA$ gene-copy rows alone.  It does not identify $tRNA$ gene-copy ratio with $tRNA$ abundance, charging state, ribosome occupancy, translation efficiency, or protein output.  It also does not infer folding, structural order, physical admissibility, biological function, biochemical mechanism, or universality.  Each of those stronger claims would need its own matched measurement contact and its own controlled statistic.  The present conclusion is narrower: in a three-organism proxy table, all nine listed $B^*_{Q6}$ residual rows exceed the stated coupling threshold against the Leu CUN/UUR copy-number spectrum, while the actual translation-survival matrix remains to be computed.
