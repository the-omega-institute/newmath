\subsection{The B-star-Q6 translation-survival coupling matrix}\label{sec:bioreality-b-star-q6-translation-survival-coupling-matrix}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Object of the finite test.}
The record tests whether the nine synonymous residual coordinates of $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ leave any translation-adjacent residue after amino-acid composition and simple sequence controls have been removed.  The coordinate list is
$$
\begin{aligned}
&K_{AAA},\ Arg_{AGR},\ Ile_{AUA},\ Leu_{CUN/UUR},\ Leu_{UUA/UUG},\\
&Ser_{UCR/AGY},\ Ser_{UCA/UCG},\ Thr_{ACR/ACY},\ f_3\text{-stress}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The primary finite-row coupling run used a $61$-codon count space and loaded three organism rows, $\mathrm{Homo\ sapiens}$, $\mathrm{Saccharomyces\ cerevisiae}$, and $\mathrm{Escherichia\ coli}\ K12$.  For that run the readout was not a measured translation-efficiency surface; it was the $tRNA$-Leu $CUN$-versus-$UUR$ log-copy spectrum.  Against that proxy spectrum all nine residual coordinates had nonzero coupling entries, the maximum absolute entry was $1{,}882{,}464.1106377328$, and the declared coupling threshold was $0.01$.  The stated null for this proxy run was that every one of the nine coordinates should satisfy $|A_{ij}|<0.01$; this null was not met.

\paragraph{Controlled modeled-translation surface.}
A separate rank-powered modeled-$tAI$ run supplied a bounded translation-layer statistic rather than a direct ribosome observation.  It used $40$ organisms, nine $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates, and nine modeled per-codon $tAI$ readout coordinates.  The controls had $24$ columns with $\operatorname{rank}(Z)=23$, leaving $17$ residual degrees of freedom after control residualization.  The residualized $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ design had rank $9$, and the modeled translation survival surface had matrix shape $9\times 9$ with
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{QT}=0.9003849000036579.
\end{aligned}
$$
This is strong evidence that the residual coordinate system is not absorbed by amino-acid composition, organism context, $GC3$, length, and the $M$-density baseline when the readout is the explicitly modeled $tAI$ surface.  It is not, by itself, a ribosome-footprint, measured elongation-speed, protein-output, folding, pathway, or function result.

\paragraph{Cross-organism Leu gate.}
The Leu $CUN/UUR$ gate was also tested as a three-row organism-level contact.  The organisms were again human, yeast, and $E.\ coli\ K12$.  The partial Pearson correlation with the $tRNA$-Leu ratio was $1.0$ at $n=3$, and the ordinary Pearson value was also $1.0$.  The log-ratio signs were $+1,-1,+1$, with values
$$
\begin{aligned}
1.3514980212896148,\quad -0.24869078488829677,\quad 1.0086640520051702.
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the row is a finite organism-level contact with a positive partial correlation in a tiny table, while the direction is not uniform across organisms.  It does not support a universal sign law, and it does not treat gene-copy counts as charged $tRNA$ abundance.

\paragraph{Organism sweep for modeled adaptation.}
The larger modeled-$tAI$ sweep reached $41$ organisms, with a target of $40$ organisms met.  Across the organism-specific modeled surfaces the $R^2_{QT}$ distribution had minimum $0.1579271837024072$, first quartile $0.26353954700074694$, median $0.33934845811598124$, mean $0.38466555440501393$, third quartile $0.5143951545826744$, and maximum $0.8213457220900503$, using $1000$ permutations.  These rows show that the modeled adaptation readout is repeatedly aligned with the nine-coordinate residual design, but the organism rows are descriptive rather than phylogenetically independent, and they are still modeled adaptation contacts rather than measured ribosome occupancy.

\paragraph{Measured ribosome-position contact.}
The position-resolved ribosome-occupancy run joined $5754$ genes after the length check, with $5754$ occupancy genes, $6013$ ordered-CDS genes, and zero length mismatches.  It controlled the local-position confound by using the supplied window and codon-index bins together with amino-acid bins; synonymous labels were permuted only within the same bin.  The requested within-bin permutation count was $200$, and all nine computed families had at least $200$ permutations.  The description-length gate used $\lambda=0.01$, $DL=2$, and $C_H>0$.  Three families passed all gates:
$$
\begin{aligned}
K_{AAA},\quad Arg_{AGR},\quad f_3\text{-stress}.
\end{aligned}
$$
This row is the clearest measured ribosome-contact entry in the packet.  It remains descriptive and position-controlled; it is not a causal proof of elongation mechanism.

The residualized control rank was $23$ with $24$ control columns in each organism.  The abundance component explained by $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ had $R^2_{QP}$ values $0.04613529805760884$, $0.10767526667768725$, $0.03980793595345331$, $0.04454565117192484$, $0.032090091625816136$, and $0.29837624987287115$, respectively.  The modeled-$tAI$ complement fractions $d$ were $0.874628721049266$, $0.6629960038578532$, $0.9400341806302775$, $0.9208949355679455$, $0.8972672018746489$, and $0.21173219572706703$.  Equivalently, the modeled-$tAI$ mediated fractions $m$ were $0.12537127895073397$, $0.3370039961421468$, $0.05996581936972245$, $0.07910506443205445$, $0.10273279812535108$, and $0.788267804272933$.  Yeast is therefore the strongest abundance row in this finite list, while the other organisms leave most of the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ abundance component outside the modeled-$tAI$ direction.

\paragraph{mRNA-stability rows.}
The yeast mRNA half-life row joined $5085$ records, with a reported CDS join hit rate $0.9156038419146592$.  The controlled stability surface had
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{QM}=0.05179429044975085,\qquad R^2_{QP}=0.2761140373962526,
\end{aligned}
$$
and cosine $0.8040935468384264$ between $S^{QM}$ and the same-join $S^{QP}$ surface; the dominant stability coordinate was $Leu_{CUN/UUR}$.  Conditioning the abundance row on log half-life reduced $R^2_{QP}$ from $0.2761140373962526$ to $0.24851799532219632$, giving an absorbed component $0.027596042074056304$ and mediation fraction $0.09994436477872035$.  The mediator permutation null had mean $0.00019674487584783138$, $p95=0.0014571003414947088$, and right-tail value $0.000999000999000999$.  These quantities describe a statistical partition in one yeast half-life dataset, not a causal decay pathway.

\paragraph{Coordinate-level decomposition.}
The per-coordinate abundance decomposition used yeast, $n=1258$ joined rows, a base pool of $1800$, $5$ folds, $120$ permutations, and a $Z$-basis rank of $24$.  Eight of the nine listed coordinates had a real-source flag: $K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $Leu_{CUN/UUR}$, $Leu_{UUA/UUG}$, $Ser_{UCR/AGY}$, $Thr_{ACR/ACY}$, and $f_3$-stress.  The full $f_3$ family decomposition used $21$ family subcoordinates, $n=1800$ joined rows, $P_{f3}$ norm squared $52.76911713166682$, target norm squared $430.42705205115925$, and $P_{f3}$ fraction of target $0.12259712041843235$.  Nineteen synonymous family subcoordinates had a real-source flag, including $F_{UUU\to UUC}$, $L_{UUA\to UUG}$, $S_{UCU\to UCG}$, $K_{AAA\to AAG}$, $R_{AGA\to AGG}$, and $G_{GGU\to GGG}$.

\paragraph{Local conclusion and boundary.}
The finite rows support a bounded conclusion: the nine-dimensional $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ synonymous residual system has nonzero proxy coupling, strong controlled modeled-$tAI$ survival, repeated modeled adaptation alignment across many organisms, a position-controlled ribosome-occupancy contact for selected families, and downstream abundance or mRNA-stability associations in several joined datasets.  The evidence does not justify a stronger biological promotion.  No translation mechanism, folded structure, physical admissibility, molecular function, phenotype, pathway, or universal law follows from these rows unless an additional layer-matched contact supplies the relevant observation and the corresponding controlled statistic is computed.
