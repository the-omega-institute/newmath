\subsection{B-star Q6 residual coupling against a translation-proxy spectrum}
\label{sec:bioreality-b-star-q6-coupling-matrix}

\paragraph{Claim under audit.}
This record concerns the proposed survival matrix for nine $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates against translation-related readouts.  The intended matrix has entries $S_{ij}=S_{q_i}(r_j\mid Z)$, where $q_i$ ranges over the residual coordinates and $r_j$ ranges over readouts such as tAI, stAI, ribosome occupancy, or protein abundance.  The control set $Z$ is not optional: it includes amino-acid composition, organism context, GC3, coding-sequence length, and the simpler $M$-density baseline.  The finite computation presently available is not yet an $S_{ij}$ survival matrix.  It is a coupling screen on a proxy readout, namely the tRNA-Leu CUN-versus-UUR log-copy spectrum, with the amino-acid quotient kept explicit through the synonymous projection.

\paragraph{Finite row.}
The computed codon-count ambient space has $61$ codon coordinates.  Inside that space the following nine residual directions were evaluated: Arg\_AGR, Ile\_AUA, K\_AAA, Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR, Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG, Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG, Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY, Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY, and f3\_stress.  The organisms in the finite row are homo sapiens, saccharomyces cerevisiae, and escherichia coli k12.  For the proxy screen, the break condition was that all nine coordinates have $|A_{ij}|<0.01$ against the proxy spectrum.  That condition was not met: all nine entries were nonzero above the coupling threshold, and the largest absolute coupling was $1882464.1106377328$.

$$
\begin{aligned}
N_{\mathrm{codon}} &= 61,\\
N_{q} &= 9,\\
N_{\mathrm{org}} &= 3,\\
\tau_{A} &= 0.01,\\
\max_{i}|A_{ij}| &= 1882464.1106377328,\\
\#\{i:|A_{ij}|\geq \tau_A\} &= 9.
\end{aligned}
$$

The numerical outcome therefore rejects the narrow proxy null ``all nine residual coordinates are uncoupled from the tRNA-Leu CUN-versus-UUR log-copy spectrum at threshold $0.01$''.  It does not, by itself, establish a positive translation-readout survival entry.  The distinction is structural: $A_{ij}$ is a covariance coupling after synonymous projection, while $S_{ij}$ is a controlled survival score or description-length improvement after the full covariate set $Z$ and the $M$-density ablation have been imposed.

\paragraph{Leu CUN/UUR context row.}
A separate three-organism Leu CUN/UUR gate row reports Pearson correlation $1.0$ and partial Pearson correlation $1.0$ with $n=3$ for the association between the Leu CUN/UUR usage ratio and the tRNA-Leu copy-ratio readback.  The organism-level log-ratios in that row are $1.3514980212896148$, $-0.24869078488829677$, and $1.0086640520051702$, with signs $+1,-1,+1$.  Thus the row is not sign-uniform across organisms, even though the finite three-row correlation statistic is maximal.

$$
\begin{aligned}
r_{\mathrm{Pearson}} &= 1.0,\\
r_{\mathrm{partial}} &= 1.0,\\
n &= 3,\\
(\ell_1,\ell_2,\ell_3) &= (1.3514980212896148,-0.24869078488829677,1.0086640520051702),\\
\operatorname{sgn}(\ell_1,\ell_2,\ell_3) &= (+1,-1,+1).
\end{aligned}
$$

This makes the readback useful as a finite contact with codon usage and tRNA gene-copy annotation, but it also prevents any population-level direction from being asserted.  The negative middle sign is part of the data, not an exception to be averaged away.

\paragraph{Interpretation.}
The current chapter-specific conclusion is a bounded one.  The $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual coordinate system is not silent against the selected tRNA-Leu proxy spectrum in the three-organism row: every one of the nine tested residual coordinates has a nonzero coupling above $0.01$, and the largest absolute coupling is about $1.882\times 10^{6}$.  This supports the claim that the residual coordinates deserve a full survival-matrix test with direct translation-layer readouts.  It does not certify that any entry $S_{ij}$ is positive, because the available computation did not attach ribosome profiling, tAI or stAI survival estimation, matched protein abundance, matched mRNA abundance, and the full covariate residualization in one organism-matched matrix.

\paragraph{Local limitation.}
No translation-realization conclusion follows from this row unless a separate direct translation contact supplies the missing readout and the controlled statistic is computed.  No folding, physical admissibility, biological function, molecular mechanism, protein-output mediation, or universal biological law follows from codon usage, tRNA gene-copy ratios, identifier normalization, or proxy coupling alone.  tRNA gene-copy counts are treated here as genome-annotation summaries, not as tRNA abundance, charging state, ribosome occupancy, translation efficiency, or protein abundance.  Likewise, a positive $A_{ij}$ entry is not promoted to a positive $S_{ij}$ entry without the declared partial-$R^2$ or description-length-improvement calculation under $Z$.

\paragraph{Operational verdict.}
The finite evidence is sufficient for an open BioReality chapter recording a proxy coupling contact and a blocked survival upgrade.  The next admissible test is the full matrix computation: for each residual coordinate $q_i$, each translation readout $r_j$, and each organism with matched codon usage, tRNA-copy, tAI or stAI weights, ribosome profiling or translation-efficiency rows, matched mRNA abundance, and optionally matched protein abundance, compute $A_{ij}$ and then estimate $S_{ij}$ after amino-acid composition, organism context, GC3, length, and $M$-density controls.  Support requires at least one strictly positive $S_{ij}$ after baseline ablation; failure occurs if every entry satisfies $S_{ij}\leq 0$ under those controls.
