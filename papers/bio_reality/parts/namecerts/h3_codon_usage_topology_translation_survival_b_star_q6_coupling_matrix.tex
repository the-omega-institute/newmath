\subsection{The $B^*_{Q6}$ Translation-Survival Coupling Matrix}\label{sec:bior-b-star-q6-translation-survival-coupling}\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Claim form.}
This record concerns a finite codon-usage contact for the nine residual coordinates of $B^*_{Q6}$ after the amino-acid quotient has been imposed.  The intended survival matrix has entries $S_{ij}$, where $q_i$ ranges over the nine synonymous residual coordinates and $r_j$ ranges over translation-facing readouts such as tAI, stAI, ribosome occupancy, translation efficiency, or protein abundance.  The biological question is not whether codon geometry alone determines translation output, but whether at least one residual coordinate retains positive explanatory value after the ordinary controls have been imposed.  The declared control family is
$$
\begin{aligned}
Z = (&\text{amino-acid composition},\ \text{organism context},\ \mathrm{GC3},\\
     &\text{coding-sequence length},\ \text{simpler codon-density baseline}).
\end{aligned}
$$
The target statistic is therefore not a raw association.  For each pair $(q_i,r_j)$ it requires the residual readout direction $\ell_j^\perp$, the covariance coupling
$$
\begin{aligned}
A_{ij} = q_i^T\Sigma\ell_j^\perp,
\end{aligned}
$$
and then a controlled survival score $S_{ij}$, read as partial $R^2$ or description-length improvement after $Z$ and the baseline ablation are present.

\paragraph{Finite row actually attached.}
The finite computation presently attached to this claim used a $61$-codon count space and computed all nine declared synonymous residual coordinates:
$$
\begin{aligned}
&\mathrm{Arg}_{AGR},\quad \mathrm{Ile}_{AUA},\quad \mathrm{K}_{AAA},\quad
\mathrm{Leu}_{CUN/UUR},\quad \mathrm{Leu}_{UUA/UUG},\\
&\mathrm{Ser}_{UCA/UCG},\quad \mathrm{Ser}_{UCR/AGY},\quad
\mathrm{Thr}_{ACR/ACY},\quad f3\text{-}\mathrm{stress}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The organisms in the attached row were \emph{Homo sapiens}, \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}, and \emph{Escherichia coli} K-12.  The attached readout was not ribosome profiling, stAI, tAI, translation efficiency, or protein abundance.  It was a proxy spectrum: the tRNA-Leu CUN-versus-UUR log-copy contrast.  Under that proxy contact, the break rule was that the row would be null if every one of the nine coordinates had $|A_{ij}|<0.01$.  The recorded threshold was therefore
$$
\begin{aligned}
\tau_A = 0.01.
\end{aligned}
$$
The null was not met: all nine entries were nonzero relative to the proxy rule, and the largest absolute coupling was
$$
\begin{aligned}
\max_{i,j}|A_{ij}| = 1882464.1106377328.
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the finite row establishes that the chosen residual basis is not invisible to this organism-level tRNA-copy proxy.  It does not yet establish any positive entry of the survival matrix $S_{ij}$, because the required translation readouts and the full control set have not been jointly evaluated.

\paragraph{Leu CUN/UUR gate row.}
A second attached finite row isolates the Leu CUN/UUR contrast across the same three organisms.  The organism-level log-ratio signs were recorded as
$$
\begin{aligned}
(1.3514980212896148,\ -0.24869078488829677,\ 1.0086640520051702),
\end{aligned}
$$
with signs
$$
\begin{aligned}
(+1,\ -1,\ +1).
\end{aligned}
$$
The sign is therefore not uniform across organisms.  The same row records $n=3$, Pearson correlation $1.0$, and partial Pearson correlation $1.0$ for the finite Leu CUN/UUR versus tRNA-Leu ratio contact.  These numbers are reported as a finite three-organism table, not as an estimate of a universal direction.  The perfect correlation is especially fragile because the row count is only three; it is a prompt for a larger controlled panel, not a population-level law.

\paragraph{Survival interpretation.}
The survival conjecture would be supported only if a direct translation-layer readout is paired with codon counts and the control family $Z$, and at least one pair satisfies
$$
\begin{aligned}
S_{ij} > 0
\end{aligned}
$$
after the simpler-codon-density baseline is ablated.  The present proxy matrix gives a nonzero $A_{ij}$ contact against tRNA-Leu copy contrast, but $A_{ij}$ and $S_{ij}$ are different objects.  A positive or large proxy coupling is not promoted to controlled survival unless the readout residual $\ell_j^\perp$ is a genuine translation-facing measurement and the partial-$R^2$ or description-length statistic is computed under the declared controls.  In particular, tRNA gene-copy counts are genome-annotation summaries; they are not, by themselves, tRNA abundance, tRNA charging, ribosome occupancy, translation efficiency, or protein output.

\paragraph{Local exclusion boundary.}
This chapter records a codon-usage and tRNA-copy finite contact only.  It does not infer ribosome-mediated translation realization from the proxy row.  It does not infer folding, structural order, physical admissibility, biochemical function, pathway participation, protein abundance, mechanism, or universality.  Ribosome profiling would test a bounded translation readout only after organism-matched codon counts and matched mRNA controls are attached.  Proteomics would test a downstream abundance readout only after identifier-matched protein rows and the same controls are attached.  Matched mRNA abundance is a covariate for translation-efficiency residualization, not evidence for translation output by itself.  The present status is therefore precise: the nine-coordinate $B^*_{Q6}$ residual basis has a non-null proxy coupling row, while the controlled survival matrix remains open until direct translation or abundance contacts supply the missing readout rows.
