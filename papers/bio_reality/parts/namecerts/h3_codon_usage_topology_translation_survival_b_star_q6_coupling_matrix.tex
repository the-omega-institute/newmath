\subsection{B-star-Q6 Coupling Matrix Against Translation-Layer Contacts}
\label{sec:b-star-q6-coupling-matrix-translation-contacts}

\paragraph{Object of the record.}
This record concerns the survival-matrix question for nine synonymous residual coordinates associated with $B^{*}_{Q6}$. The tested coordinate list is
$$
\begin{aligned}
Q = \{&\mathrm{Arg}_{AGR},\ \mathrm{Ile}_{AUA},\ K_{AAA},\ \mathrm{Leu}_{CUN/UUR},\ \mathrm{Leu}_{UUA/UUG},\\
&\mathrm{Ser}_{UCA/UCG},\ \mathrm{Ser}_{UCR/AGY},\ \mathrm{Thr}_{ACR/ACY},\ f3\text{-}\mathrm{stress}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The intended matrix has one row for each $q_i \in Q$ and one column for each translation-related readout $r_j$, with entries $S_{ij}=S_{q_i}(r_j\mid Z)$. The control set is the amino-acid quotient together with organism context, $GC3$, coding-sequence length, and the simpler $M$-density baseline. A positive survival entry would mean that a residual coordinate improves the controlled description of a measured translation readout after these controls have already been imposed. The finite row currently available is weaker: it computes a proxy coupling matrix $A_{ij}$ against a tRNA-Leu CUN-versus-UUR log-copy spectrum, not a full $S_{ij}$ survival matrix against ribosome occupancy, translation efficiency, stAI, tAI, or protein abundance.

\paragraph{Finite-row computation.}
The computed run used a $61$-codon count space and loaded three organism rows:
$$
\begin{aligned}
O=\{&\mathrm{Homo\ sapiens},\ \mathrm{Saccharomyces\ cerevisiae},\ \mathrm{Escherichia\ coli\ K12}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
For each listed residual coordinate, the amino-acid-composition quotient was kept explicit through the synonymous projection $P_{\tau}$. The recorded break threshold was
$$
\begin{aligned}
|A_{ij}| < 0.01
\end{aligned}
$$
for every one of the nine coordinates against the proxy readout. That break condition was not met. The coupling computation reported $9$ nonzero entries and
$$
\begin{aligned}
\max_{i,j}|A_{ij}| = 1882464.1106377328.
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the finite proxy row rejects the narrow null that all nine $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates have negligible coupling to the attached tRNA-Leu copy-spectrum proxy. It does not, by itself, establish $S_{ij}>0$ for any entry of the intended survival matrix, because the recorded readout is a tRNA-gene-copy spectrum rather than a direct translation-output measurement and because the full $Z$-controlled partial-$R^2$ or description-length comparison has not been recorded for the survival matrix.

\paragraph{Leu CUN/UUR gate row.}
A related three-organism row isolates the Leu CUN/UUR coordinate and compares codon-usage imbalance with tRNA-Leu copy-ratio imbalance. The recorded Pearson value is
$$
\begin{aligned}
r = 1.0,
\end{aligned}
$$
and the recorded partial Pearson value after the available control is
$$
\begin{aligned}
r_{\mathrm{partial}} = 1.0, \qquad n=3.
\end{aligned}
$$
The three log-ratio signs are not uniform across organisms. The recorded log-ratio vector is
$$
\begin{aligned}
(1.3514980212896148,\ -0.24869078488829677,\ 1.0086640520051702),
\end{aligned}
$$
with sign pattern
$$
\begin{aligned}
(+1,\ -1,\ +1).
\end{aligned}
$$
This makes the row useful as a finite contact with context-dependent direction, not as evidence for a universal sign law. The row is compatible with a nontrivial codon-usage/tRNA-copy association in the three loaded organisms, while explicitly retaining the limitation that $n=3$ cannot support a population-level estimate or a stable cross-organism directionality claim.

\paragraph{Survival-matrix status.}
The survival assertion remains open rather than established. The required support condition is the existence of at least one pair $(q_i,r_j)$ such that
$$
\begin{aligned}
S_{q_i}(r_j\mid Z)>0
\end{aligned}
$$
after the amino-acid quotient, organism context, $GC3$, length, and $M$-density baseline have all been included. The present finite row supplies a large nonzero proxy coupling and a three-organism Leu gate, but it lacks organism-matched rows for direct translation readouts under the declared controls. In particular, no recorded entry currently gives a $p$-value for an eight-or-more-organism extension, no row supplies a completed partial-$R^2$ survival score for ribosome profiling or stAI, and no row supplies the baseline-ablation comparison needed to promote $A_{ij}$ into $S_{ij}$.

\paragraph{Local interpretation boundary.}
The allowed reading is narrow. The finite computation says that the nine-coordinate $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual packet is not annihilated by the available tRNA-Leu proxy coupling test at threshold $0.01$, and that the Leu CUN/UUR three-organism row has a perfect recorded correlation in the finite table while reversing sign across organisms. It does not say that ribosomes have realized the coordinate, that tRNA gene copy equals tRNA abundance or charging, that protein output has been explained, or that any folding, physical admissibility, biochemical function, mechanism, or universal biological rule follows. Each of those stronger claims would require its own matched measurement contact and its own controlled statistic. Until such rows are attached, the chapter records a bounded proxy-coupling contact and an explicit survival-matrix gap, not a completed translation-survival theorem.
