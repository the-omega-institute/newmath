\subsection{Proxy coupling for the B$^*_{Q6}$ translation-survival matrix}
\label{sec:bioreality-b-star-q6-translation-survival-proxy-coupling}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Object of the record.}
This record concerns the proposed survival matrix $S_{ij}=S_{q_i}(r_j\mid Z)$ for nine synonymous-residual coordinates of $B^*_{Q6}$ against translation-related readouts. The intended rows are
$$
\begin{aligned}
&\mathrm{Arg}_{AGR},\ \mathrm{Ile}_{AUA},\ K_{AAA},\ \mathrm{Leu}_{CUN/UUR},\ \mathrm{Leu}_{UUA/UUG},\\
&\mathrm{Ser}_{UCA/UCG},\ \mathrm{Ser}_{UCR/AGY},\ \mathrm{Thr}_{ACR/ACY},\ f_3\text{-}\mathrm{stress}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The target readouts are translation-layer quantities such as tAI, stAI, ribosome occupancy, translation efficiency, and protein abundance when these are organism-matched to the coding-sequence rows. The declared control set is $Z=(\mathrm{amino\ acid\ composition},\mathrm{organism},GC3,\mathrm{length},M\text{-}\mathrm{density\ baseline})$. A positive survival entry would require a controlled partial-$R^2$ or description-length improvement after these controls and after the simpler-codon baseline has been ablated.

\paragraph{Finite data actually attached.}
The verified finite row presently contains a proxy coupling computation rather than a completed survival matrix. The codon count space has size $61$, and the organisms loaded are Homo sapiens, Saccharomyces cerevisiae, and Escherichia coli K-12. For the proxy readout, the computation used the tRNA-Leu CUN-versus-UUR log-copy spectrum. Across the nine residual coordinates listed above, all nine entries were nonzero against this proxy. The threshold for a reported coupling was $0.01$, and the maximum observed absolute coupling was
$$
\begin{aligned}
\max_{i,j}|A_{ij}|=1882464.1106377328.
\end{aligned}
$$
The break condition for this proxy row was that every one of the nine residual coordinates should satisfy $|A_{ij}|<0.01$. That condition was not met. Thus the finite proxy contact says that the $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinates are not numerically silent against the attached tRNA-Leu CUN/UUR log-copy spectrum in this three-organism table.

\paragraph{Meaning of the proxy matrix.}
The measured quantity is a coupling $A_{ij}=q_i^T\Sigma\ell_j^\perp$ on residual count covariance, where $q_i$ is one of the nine synonymous-residual coordinates and $\ell_j^\perp$ is the synonymous projection of the readout weight. This is a geometry-of-counts contact: it checks whether the residual coordinate basis interacts with the attached proxy readout after the amino-acid quotient has been kept explicit. It is not yet the survival score $S_{ij}$. The survival score would require the direct translation readout rows and the full control design $Z$, followed by an explicit partial-$R^2$ or description-length comparison. Consequently, a positive or large $A_{ij}$ is only a reason to keep the survival probe open; it is not evidence that any $S_{ij}$ entry is positive.

\paragraph{CUN/UUR Leu side contact.}
A separate finite row records the CUN/UUR Leu relation across the same three organisms. The partial Pearson correlation between $\log(CUN/UUR)$ and the corresponding tRNA-Leu copy-ratio readback is reported as $1.0$ with $n=3$. The organism-level log-ratios are
$$
\begin{aligned}
1.3514980212896148,\quad -0.24869078488829677,\quad 1.0086640520051702,
\end{aligned}
$$
with signs
$$
\begin{aligned}
+1,\quad -1,\quad +1.
\end{aligned}
$$
The sign is therefore not uniform across organisms. This matters because the row can support a finite association check, but it cannot be read as a universal direction, a population-level estimate, or an eight-organism relation. The extension probe remains open: it asks for at least eight curated paired organisms and a non-trivial null test with partial correlation above $0.5$ and $p<0.05$ after amino-acid-composition control. The current table has $n=3$ and no such eight-organism $p$-value.

\paragraph{Contacts and blocked promotions.}
The available contact classes are per-organism codon-usage summaries, organism-level tRNA gene-copy counts, tAI or stAI weights, ribosome-profiling translation-efficiency rows, matched protein-abundance rows, and matched mRNA abundance controls. In the present record, the codon-usage and tRNA-copy contacts are the only contacts realized as finite computed rows. The ribosome-profiling, protein-abundance, and matched-mRNA contacts are named as required routes for the full survival study, but the finite memory does not yet contain organism-matched rows that compute $S_{ij}>0$ under $Z$. Matched mRNA abundance is a covariate for separating translation efficiency from transcript abundance; it is not itself a translation-survival entry. Protein abundance is a downstream readout only when identifier matching and the declared controls are actually present.

\paragraph{Local conclusion.}
The record is therefore a bounded positive proxy-coupling result plus a blocked survival claim. The finite computation shows that all nine $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinates have nonzero proxy coupling against the tRNA-Leu CUN/UUR log-copy spectrum, with maximum absolute coupling $1882464.1106377328$ and threshold $0.01$, across three organisms. It does not show that any entry of $S_{ij}$ is positive. The survival-matrix claim remains open until a direct translation readout such as tAI, stAI, ribosome occupancy, translation efficiency, or matched protein abundance is paired with the codon rows and evaluated under amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, length, and $M$-density baseline controls. No ribosome-level translation conclusion, protein folding conclusion, physical-admissibility conclusion, biological-function conclusion, mechanism claim, or universal biological law follows from this finite row unless an additional matched contact supplies that specific layer.
