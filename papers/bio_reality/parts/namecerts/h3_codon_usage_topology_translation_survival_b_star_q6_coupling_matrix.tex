\subsection{A bounded proxy row for the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ translation-survival matrix}
\label{sec:bior-reality-b-star-q6-coupling-matrix}

\paragraph{Claim form.}
This record concerns the nine synonymous residual coordinates of $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ after the amino-acid quotient has been imposed. The intended survival object is a matrix $S_{ij}$ whose rows are the residual coordinates
$K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $Leu_{CUN/UUR}$, $Leu_{UUA/UUG}$, $Ser_{UCR/AGY}$, $Ser_{UCA/UCG}$, $Thr_{ACR/ACY}$, and an antisymmetric $f3$ stress coordinate, and whose columns are translation-associated readouts such as tAI, stAI, ribosome occupancy, translation efficiency, or matched protein abundance. The survival entry is not a raw covariance. It is the controlled residual contribution of $q_i$ to a readout $r_j$ after amino-acid composition, organism identity, $GC3$, coding length, and a simpler $M$-density baseline have already been charged to the null model.

\paragraph{Finite row actually observed.}
The attached finite computation does not yet contain the full $S_{ij}$ matrix. It contains a proxy coupling row over three organism contexts: Homo sapiens, Saccharomyces cerevisiae, and Escherichia coli K12. The codon-count ambient space has $61$ sense-codon coordinates. All nine declared residual coordinates were computed:
$$
\begin{aligned}
R=\{&Arg_{AGR}, Ile_{AUA}, K_{AAA}, Leu_{CUN/UUR}, Leu_{UUA/UUG},\\
&Ser_{UCA/UCG}, Ser_{UCR/AGY}, Thr_{ACR/ACY}, f3\text{-}stress\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The readout used in this finite row is the organism-level $tRNA$-Leu $CUN$-versus-$UUR$ log-copy spectrum. Thus the observed quantity is a proxy coupling $A_{ij}$, not a translation-survival score. The declared break test for this proxy row was that every residual coordinate should have $|A_{ij}|<0.01$ against the proxy readout. The break condition was not met: all $9$ entries were nonzero above the threshold, and the largest absolute entry was
$$
\begin{aligned}
\max_i |A_{ij}| = 1882464.1106377328.
\end{aligned}
$$
In the narrow sense tested here, the amino-acid quotient did not erase the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates from this tRNA-copy proxy contact.

\paragraph{Leucine gate row.}
A second finite row records the same three organism contexts for the $Leu_{CUN/UUR}$ direction. The Pearson statistic between the codon-usage log ratio and the tRNA-Leu copy log ratio is reported as $1.0$, and the amino-acid-composition-controlled partial Pearson statistic is also reported as $1.0$ with $n=3$. The observed organism-level log ratios are
$$
\begin{aligned}
(1.3514980212896148,\;-0.24869078488829677,\;1.0086640520051702),
\end{aligned}
$$
with signs $(+,-,+)$. This sign pattern is important: the finite row supports a context-sensitive association in the three-row table, not a uniform direction law. No valid population-level inference is obtained from $n=3$, and no $p$-value is recorded for this row.

\paragraph{What survives as a BioReality statement.}
The observed finite fact is that a codon-usage residual construction, evaluated in a $61$-coordinate codon-count space and projected through the amino-acid quotient, has nonzero proxy coupling to an organism-level tRNA-Leu copy spectrum in all nine tested residual directions under the stated numerical threshold. This is enough to keep the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ survival-matrix question open and nontrivial. It is not enough to assert a positive entry $S_{ij}>0$ for tAI, stAI, ribosome occupancy, translation efficiency, or protein abundance, because the Z-controlled partial-$R^2$ or description-length-improvement estimate has not been computed for any direct translation readout.

\paragraph{Local no-promotion boundary.}
The chapter therefore records a bounded codon-usage and tRNA-copy contact, not a completed translation-realization result. tRNA gene-copy number is not treated as tRNA abundance, tRNA charging, ribosome occupancy, translation efficiency, or protein output. A nonzero proxy coupling $A_{ij}$ is not promoted to a positive survival entry $S_{ij}$ without a direct readout and the full control set $Z=(\text{amino-acid composition},\text{organism},GC3,\text{length},M\text{-density baseline})$. Nothing in this row establishes ribosome-mediated translation, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, mechanism, phenotype, or universality. Each of those stronger claims would require its own matched measurement contact and its own controlled statistic.

\paragraph{Data still required for the survival matrix.}
To close the intended matrix claim, the next finite table must attach organism-matched codon counts, direct translation readouts such as tAI, stAI, ribosome profiling, or translation efficiency, and, where protein abundance is used, matched mRNA and proteomics rows. For every residual coordinate $q_i$ and readout $r_j$, the required statistic is
$$
\begin{aligned}
S_{ij}=S_{q_i}(r_j\mid Z),
\end{aligned}
$$
reported together with the corresponding coupling $A_{ij}$ and the sign class positive, zero, or negative. The current record supplies the finite proxy evidence that motivates this table, while preserving the distinction between proxy codon/tRNA coupling and controlled translation-readout survival.
