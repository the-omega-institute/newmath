\subsection{B-star Q6 residual coupling against bounded translation readouts}\label{sec:b-star-q6-residual-coupling-translation-readouts}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Question.} This record tests whether the nine synonymous-residual coordinates assigned to $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ leave any finite trace after quotienting by amino-acid composition and ordinary sequence-context covariates. The carrier is a $9 \times K$ survival-and-coupling table whose rows are the residual coordinates $q_i$ and whose columns are translation-related readouts $r_j$. The row set is
$K_{AAA}$, Arg $AGR$, Ile $AUA$, Leu $CUN/UUR$, Leu $UUA/UUG$, Ser $UCR/AGY$, Ser $UCA/UCG$, Thr $ACR/ACY$, and the antisymmetric $f3$ stress pair. The intended statistic separates a raw covariance coupling $A_{ij}$ from a controlled survival score $S_{ij}$: $A_{ij}$ asks whether a residual coordinate is aligned with a projected readout, while $S_{ij}$ asks whether that alignment improves prediction after the declared controls.

\paragraph{Finite rows used here.} The direct coupling run loaded three organisms: Homo sapiens, Saccharomyces cerevisiae, and Escherichia coli K12. It used a $61$-codon count space and computed all nine $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ synonymous residuals. Against the available proxy readout, namely the tRNA-Leu $CUN$-versus-$UUR$ log-copy spectrum, the break condition was that all nine entries satisfy $|A_{ij}| < 0.01$. That break condition was not met. The recorded coupling table had $9$ nonzero entries above the stated threshold and a maximum absolute coupling
$$\begin{aligned}
\max_{i,j}|A_{ij}| &= 1882464.1106377328,\\
\tau_A &= 0.01.
\end{aligned}$$
Thus the finite row is not a null coupling row. It is, however, only a proxy-contact row: the readout is a tRNA-copy spectrum, not measured ribosome occupancy, measured elongation, measured translation efficiency, or protein output.

\paragraph{Modeled translation-readout panel.} A second finite computation used modeled per-codon tAI derived from tRNA gene-copy counts and dos Reis wobble weights. This run used $40$ organisms, formed a $9 \times 9$ modeled translation matrix, and residualized against a control design with $24$ control columns, rank $23$, and residual degrees of freedom $17$. The residual coordinate design had rank $9$. The recorded controlled fit was
$$\begin{aligned}
R^2_{QT} &= 0.9003849000036579,\\
\operatorname{rank}(Z) &= 23,\\
\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde X) &= 9,\\
\operatorname{df}_{\mathrm{resid}} &= 17.
\end{aligned}$$
The positive-entry threshold for the modeled partial-$R^2$ surface was $10^{-12}$, and the degeneracy guard was $R^2 < 0.999999$. Since $R^2_{QT}=0.9003849000036579$, this row is not rank-saturated under that guard. The correct reading is bounded: the modeled tAI surface gives a controlled modeled translation-readout survival row for the $Q \to T$ interface, not a ribosome-profiling measurement and not protein realization.

\paragraph{Leucine gate row.} The CUN/UUR Leu gate gives an additional three-organism sanity check on directionality. The finite row recorded Pearson $1.0$ and partial Pearson $1.0$ for $n=3$, but the organism-level log ratios were $1.3514980212896148$, $-0.24869078488829677$, and $1.0086640520051702$, with signs $+,-,+$. The sign is therefore not uniform across organisms. This row supports only the existence of a finite association in the small table; it does not license a population-level direction, a universal sign, or an interpretation of tRNA gene copy as tRNA abundance or charging.

\paragraph{Statistic being asserted.} The bounded mathematical statement is that the available rows separate two layers of evidence. First, the three-organism proxy coupling row shows nonzero $A_{ij}$ structure for all nine residual coordinates against the tRNA-Leu copy-spectrum proxy, with maximum absolute coupling $1882464.1106377328$ and threshold $0.01$. Second, the $40$-organism modeled tAI run supplies a non-saturated controlled $9 \times 9$ modeled translation-readout matrix with $R^2_{QT}=0.9003849000036579$ after the stated controls. In symbols, the chapter records the finite implication
$$\begin{aligned}
\exists i,j\; |A_{ij}| &\ge 0.01\quad \text{in the proxy coupling row},\\
R^2_{QT} &= 0.9003849000036579 < 0.999999\quad \text{in the modeled tAI row}.
\end{aligned}$$
It does not assert that the proxy coupling alone is a positive $S_{ij}$ survival entry. A positive survival statement requires the declared $Z$-controlled partial-$R^2$ or description-length comparison for the relevant readout.

\paragraph{Controls and ablation boundary.} The target control set is $Z=$ amino-acid composition, organism context, GC3, coding-sequence length, and the $M$-density baseline, with simpler-codon-baseline ablation kept separate from the residual coordinates. The $40$-organism modeled row records a control rank and residual degrees of freedom, so it is informative about the controlled modeled tAI surface. The three-organism proxy row keeps the amino-acid quotient explicit through the synonymous projection, but it is not a full translation-survival matrix because measured ribosome-profiling, measured TE, or protein-abundance rows are absent from that run.

\paragraph{Local no-promotion rule.} No translation mechanism follows from codon usage or tRNA-copy coupling alone. No ribosome-mediated realization follows unless an organism-matched ribosome or TE contact is attached and the controlled statistic is recomputed. No folding, structural order, physical admissibility, molecular function, pathway evidence, phenotype, mechanism, or universal biological law follows from any row in this chapter. Modeled tAI is a bounded modeled readout; tRNA gene copy is a genome-annotation count; ribosome profiling and proteomics contacts would each need their own matched rows and their own controlled estimates before any stronger layer is asserted.

\paragraph{Chapter verdict.} The finite evidence is sufficient to keep the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ translation-readout packet alive as a bounded BioReality record. It is not a null geometry row: all nine proxy couplings survive the $0.01$ coupling threshold in the three-organism table, and the $40$-organism modeled tAI matrix is nondegenerate under the recorded rank guard. The claim remains bounded to proxy coupling and modeled translation-readout survival. Measured ribosome occupancy, measured TE, protein abundance mediation, function, structure, physical admissibility, mechanism, and universality remain outside the asserted content.
