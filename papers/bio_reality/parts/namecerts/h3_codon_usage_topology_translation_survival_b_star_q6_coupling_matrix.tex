\subsection{B-Star-Q6 Coupling Against Translation-Adjacent Contacts}
\label{subsec:bioreality-b-star-q6-coupling-translation-adjacent}

\paragraph{Finite row.}
This record concerns the finite matrix test attached to the BioReality claim
$h3.codon\_usage\_topology.translation\_survival.b\_star\_q6\_coupling\_matrix$.
The carrier is a $9 \times K$ table whose rows are the nine synonymous residual coordinates
$$
\begin{aligned}
&\mathrm{Arg\_AGR},\ \mathrm{Ile\_AUA},\ \mathrm{K\_AAA},\ \mathrm{Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR},\ \mathrm{Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG},\\
&\mathrm{Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG},\ \mathrm{Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY},\ \mathrm{Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY},\ \mathrm{f3\_stress}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The computation was performed in the $61$-codon count space after keeping the amino-acid quotient explicit through the synonymous projection $P_{\tau}$.  The finite organism list was
$$
\begin{aligned}
\{\mathrm{Homo\ sapiens},\ \mathrm{Saccharomyces\ cerevisiae},\ \mathrm{Escherichia\ coli\ K12}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
For this row the readout is not a direct ribosome, stAI, tAI, or protein-output measurement.  It is the tRNA-Leu CUN-versus-UUR log-copy spectrum, used as a bounded proxy contact for testing whether the residual basis is numerically silent against a translation-adjacent genome annotation.

\paragraph{Coupling matrix outcome.}
For each residual coordinate $q_i$ and the available proxy readout vector $\ell$, the computed entry is the covariance coupling
$$
\begin{aligned}
A_i = q_i^{T}\Sigma \ell^{\perp},
\end{aligned}
$$
where $\ell^{\perp}$ denotes the readout component after synonymous projection.  The null threshold declared for this finite row was $|A_i| < 0.01$ for every one of the nine rows.  That null condition was not met.  The reported matrix summary has $9$ nonzero entries above the stated threshold and
$$
\begin{aligned}
\max_i |A_i| = 1882464.1106377328.
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the nine-row residual basis is not numerically annihilated by the proxy tRNA-Leu copy-ratio contact.  The conclusion is only a coupling statement in the codon-usage topology layer: it says that the residual coordinates have detectable covariance contact with the chosen proxy readout under the finite three-organism table.

\paragraph{Leu CUN/UUR auxiliary row.}
A second finite row records the CUN/UUR Leu gate on the same three-organism support.  The organism-level log-ratio vector was
$$
\begin{aligned}
(1.3514980212896148,\ -0.24869078488829677,\ 1.0086640520051702),
\end{aligned}
$$
with sign pattern
$$
\begin{aligned}
(+1,\ -1,\ +1).
\end{aligned}
$$
The sign is therefore not uniform across organisms.  The finite-row Pearson statistic was reported as $1.0$, and the amino-acid-composition-controlled partial Pearson entry was also $1.0$ with $n=3$.  These numbers are too small in row count to support a population-level law or a universal sign direction.  They are useful here only as an explicit finite contact: the Leu CUN/UUR codon-usage ratio and the tRNA-Leu copy-ratio are aligned in this three-row calculation after the declared control, while the negative middle sign prevents any monotone direction from being stated across organisms.

\paragraph{Survival status.}
The survival matrix intended by the claim is stricter than the proxy coupling matrix.  Its entries have the form
$$
\begin{aligned}
S_{ij}=S_{q_i}(r_j\mid Z),
\end{aligned}
$$
where the control set is
$$
\begin{aligned}
Z=(\mathrm{amino\ acid\ composition},\ \mathrm{organism},\ \mathrm{GC3},\ \mathrm{length},\ \mathrm{M\ density\ baseline}).
\end{aligned}
$$
A positive $A_i$ entry is not the same mathematical object as a positive $S_{ij}$ entry.  The present finite row therefore does not establish that any $B^{*}_{Q6}$ coordinate survives on tAI, stAI, ribosome occupancy, translation efficiency, or protein abundance after the full control set and the simpler-codon-baseline ablation.  What is established is narrower: the residual coordinate list was computed; the three-organism codon-count and tRNA-copy contacts were loaded; the proxy coupling threshold $0.01$ was exceeded; and the strongest absolute proxy coupling was $1882464.1106377328$.

\paragraph{Local boundary.}
No translation realization, ribosome-mediated mechanism, folding claim, physical admissibility claim, biological function claim, or universal biological law follows from these rows alone.  tRNA gene copy is a genome-annotation count, not tRNA abundance or charging.  The tRNA-Leu proxy is not ribosome occupancy, not translation efficiency, and not protein output.  A separate organism-matched contact would be needed for each stronger layer: direct ribosome profiling or stAI/tAI rows for a translation-readout survival entry, matched mRNA rows for TE residualization, proteomics rows for abundance, and independent assays for structure, physical admissibility, mechanism, function, or universality.  Until those contacts are paired with the declared controls and an explicit partial-$R^2$ or description-length survival statistic, the BioReality status is a finite proxy-coupling contact with an open survival test.
