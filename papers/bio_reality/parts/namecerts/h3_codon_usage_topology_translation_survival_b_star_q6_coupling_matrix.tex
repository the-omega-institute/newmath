\subsection{B-star Q6 residual coupling against translation-facing readouts}\label{sec:b-star-q6-residual-coupling-translation-facing-readouts}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Object.}
This record isolates a finite residual-codon calculation before any promotion to a translation-output claim.  The coordinate side consists of the nine synonymous residual directions
$\mathrm{Arg}_{\mathrm{AGR}}$, $\mathrm{Ile}_{\mathrm{AUA}}$, $K_{\mathrm{AAA}}$, $\mathrm{Leu}_{\mathrm{CUN}/\mathrm{UUR}}$, $\mathrm{Leu}_{\mathrm{UUA}/\mathrm{UUG}}$, $\mathrm{Ser}_{\mathrm{UCA}/\mathrm{UCG}}$, $\mathrm{Ser}_{\mathrm{UCR}/\mathrm{AGY}}$, $\mathrm{Thr}_{\mathrm{ACR}/\mathrm{ACY}}$, and $f_3$ stress.  These directions live in the $61$-codon count space after the amino-acid quotient is kept explicit, so the calculation is about synonymous residue rather than amino-acid composition itself.  The finite organism table contains $3$ organisms: Homo sapiens, Saccharomyces cerevisiae, and Escherichia coli K12.  The available readout in the completed row is not a ribosome-profiling, stAI, tAI, or protein-abundance measurement; it is the proxy spectrum given by the tRNA-Leu CUN-versus-UUR log-copy contrast.

\paragraph{Coupling computation.}
For a residual coordinate $q_i$ and a readout direction $\ell_j$, the inspected entry is the residual covariance coupling $A_{ij}=q_i^T\Sigma\ell_j^{\perp}$, where $\ell_j^{\perp}$ denotes the synonymous projection of the readout direction after removing the amino-acid component.  The tested break rule for the proxy row was: declare the proxy contact null if every one of the nine $q_i$ satisfies $|A_{ij}|<0.01$.  The observed finite row did not meet that null rule.  All nine residual directions produced nonzero proxy couplings, and the largest absolute coupling was $1882464.1106377328$.

$$
\begin{aligned}
\#\{q_i\} &= 9,\\
\#\{\hbox{codon coordinates}\} &= 61,\\
\#\{\hbox{organisms}\} &= 3,\\
\tau_A &= 0.01,\\
\max_i |A_{ij}| &= 1882464.1106377328,\\
\#\{i: A_{ij}\ne 0\} &= 9.
\end{aligned}
$$

This is evidence that the chosen synonymous residual basis is not numerically silent against the available Leu-copy proxy.  It is deliberately weaker than a survival statement.  A survival entry $S_{ij}$ would require a direct translation-facing readout and an explicit controlled statistic, for example a partial $R^2$ or a description-length improvement after conditioning on amino-acid composition, organism context, GC3, coding-sequence length, and the simpler $M$-density baseline.

\paragraph{Leu CUN/UUR finite row.}
The linked Leu gate row reports the same $3$ organisms and compares the Leu CUN-versus-UUR codon-usage contrast with the tRNA-Leu copy-ratio contrast.  The finite-row Pearson value is $1.0$, and the reported partial Pearson after amino-acid-composition control is also $1.0$ with $n=3$.  The organism-level log-ratio signs are not uniform: the logged values are $1.3514980212896148$, $-0.24869078488829677$, and $1.0086640520051702$, with signs $+$, $-$, and $+$.  Thus the row contains a strong three-point association and, at the same time, an explicit warning against reading the sign as a universal direction.

$$
\begin{aligned}
r_{\mathrm{Pearson}} &= 1.0,\\
r_{\mathrm{partial}} &= 1.0,\\
n &= 3,\\
(\log \rho_1,\log \rho_2,\log \rho_3) &= (1.3514980212896148,-0.24869078488829677,1.0086640520051702),\\
\operatorname{sgn}(\log \rho_1,\log \rho_2,\log \rho_3) &= (+,-,+).
\end{aligned}
$$

The sign reversal matters for interpretation.  The row can justify keeping the Leu CUN/UUR residual coordinate in the translation-facing test battery, but it does not justify a population-level direction, a conserved biological rule, or a mechanistic claim about tRNA abundance or charging.  Gene-copy counts are annotation-level quantities; they are not measurements of charged tRNA pools, ribosome dwell time, or protein output.

\paragraph{Survival matrix status.}
The intended matrix is $S_{ij}=S_{q_i}(r_j\mid Z)$, where $q_i$ ranges over the nine residual coordinates and $r_j$ ranges over translation-related readouts such as tAI, stAI, ribosome occupancy, translation efficiency, and protein abundance.  The control block is
$Z=(\hbox{amino-acid composition},\hbox{organism},\mathrm{GC3},\hbox{length},M\hbox{-density baseline})$.
The current finite row supplies $A_{ij}$-type proxy coupling and a three-organism Leu gate statistic; it does not supply a completed $S_{ij}$ matrix.  In particular, no recorded entry currently states that any $S_{ij}>0$ after all controls and baseline ablation.  The matrix claim therefore remains a bounded open test: the break condition is that every controlled survival score is non-positive, while support requires at least one strictly positive controlled survival score.

\paragraph{Local boundary.}
The present calculation is confined to codon-count geometry, synonymous residual projection, organism-level tRNA-Leu copy annotations, and a finite proxy coupling statistic.  It does not establish ribosome-mediated translation realization, folded structure, physical admissibility, biological function, biochemical mechanism, protein-output causation, or a universal biological law.  Those conclusions require their own organism-matched contacts and their own controlled readbacks.  A positive proxy coupling $A_{ij}$ is not the same object as a positive survival score $S_{ij}$, and a three-organism sign pattern is not a law across organisms.
