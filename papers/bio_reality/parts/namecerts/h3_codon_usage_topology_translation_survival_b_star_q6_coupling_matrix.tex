\subsection{A finite coupling matrix for residual codon coordinates and translation readouts}
\label{sec:b-star-q6-coupling-matrix}

\paragraph{Object of measurement.}
The record studied here is a finite matrix of residual codon-usage coordinates against translation-adjacent readouts.  The coordinate side consists of the nine synonymous residual directions
$K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $Leu_{CUN/UUR}$, $Leu_{UUA/UUG}$, $Ser_{UCR/AGY}$, $Ser_{UCA/UCG}$, $Thr_{ACR/ACY}$, and the third-position stress coordinate $f_3$.  These coordinates are evaluated after the amino-acid quotient has been imposed, so the statistic is not allowed to recover trivial amino-acid composition.  The intended survival score is denoted $S_{ij}$, where $q_i$ is one of the nine residual coordinates and $r_j$ is a translation-related readout such as tAI, stAI, ribosome occupancy, translation efficiency, or protein abundance.  In the fully observed form, the control family is
$$
\begin{aligned}
Z = (&\text{amino-acid composition},\ \text{organism context},\ GC3,\ \text{coding length},\\
&\text{and the simpler codon-density baseline}).
\end{aligned}
$$
A positive entry means that a residual coordinate improves the controlled description of a readout after the declared controls and baseline have already been applied.  It does not mean that the coordinate is a cause of translation, a protein-output mechanism, or a functional law.

\paragraph{Finite coupling row.}
The directly verified coupling run used experiment id $4a783972e74b$.  It worked in a $61$-codon count space and loaded three organism rows: $Homo\ sapiens$, $Saccharomyces\ cerevisiae$, and $Escherichia\ coli$ K12.  The readout in that run was not ribosome profiling, tAI, stAI, or proteomics.  It was a proxy spectrum, namely the tRNA-Leu $CUN$-versus-$UUR$ log-copy readout.  The computed matrix reported all nine residual coordinates as nonzero against this proxy contact, with
$$
\begin{aligned}
\max_{i,j} |A_{ij}| &= 1882464.1106377328,\\
\#\{(i,j): |A_{ij}| > 0.01\} &= 9,\\
\text{coupling threshold} &= 0.01.
\end{aligned}
$$
The registered null condition for that proxy run was that all nine coordinates have $|A_{ij}| < 0.01$.  Since the maximum absolute coupling was far above threshold and all nine entries were nonzero, that null condition was not met.  This supports the narrow claim that the residual-coordinate basis is not numerically silent on the tRNA-Leu proxy spectrum in the three-row table.

\paragraph{Modeled translation-readout survival.}
A separate modeled-tAI run, experiment id $09209aa516a0$, evaluated a rank-powered $S^{QT}$ surface rather than a measured ribosome or protein-output surface.  It used $40$ organism rows and the same nine residual coordinates.  The modeled readout matrix also had nine columns, one for each residual coordinate after projection through codon adaptation weights derived from tRNA gene-copy counts and wobble weights.  The control design had $24$ control columns with rank $23$, leaving $17$ residual degrees of freedom after control residualization.  The residual coordinate design had rank $9$.  The resulting modeled translation surface had shape $9 \times 9$ and
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{QT} &= 0.9003849000036579,\\
\operatorname{rank}(Z) &= 23,\\
\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde X) &= 9,\\
\text{residual degrees of freedom} &= 17,\\
\text{positive-entry threshold} &= 10^{-12}.
\end{aligned}
$$
This is evidence for a controlled modeled-readout association in the declared residual coordinate system.  Its status is weaker than a direct ribosome-profiling or proteomics contact: modeled tAI is computed from tRNA gene-copy annotations and adaptation weights, so it is not itself ribosome occupancy, elongation speed, mature protein abundance, folded structure, or biological function.

\paragraph{Leucine gate row.}
The cross-organism Leu gate run, experiment id $675fdddb3187$, also used the three organism rows $Homo\ sapiens$, $Saccharomyces\ cerevisiae$, and $Escherichia\ coli$ K12.  For the $CUN/UUR$ leucine contrast, the finite-row Pearson statistic and the controlled partial Pearson statistic were both reported as $1.0$ with $n=3$:
$$
\begin{aligned}
r &= 1.0,\\
r_{\mathrm{partial}} &= 1.0,\\
n &= 3.
\end{aligned}
$$
The organism-level log-ratio signs were not uniform.  The recorded log ratios were $1.3514980212896148$, $-0.24869078488829677$, and $1.0086640520051702$, with signs $+1,-1,+1$.  Thus the row is useful as a finite contact showing that the leucine residual contrast can be read against tRNA-Leu copy ratios, but it is not a population estimate and it is not a universal directionality statement.

\paragraph{Interpretation.}
The coupling matrix and the modeled-tAI surface jointly indicate that the nine-coordinate residual basis has nontrivial translation-adjacent numerical contacts after the amino-acid quotient is made explicit.  The strongest verified finite numbers are the proxy-coupling maximum $1882464.1106377328$, the nine above-threshold proxy entries, and the modeled $R^2_{QT}=0.9003849000036579$ on a $40$-organism design with $17$ residual degrees of freedom.  These facts justify retaining the conjectural survival matrix as an active BioReality object: the coordinate family is not absorbed by amino-acid composition alone, and it is not numerically erased by the declared control geometry in the modeled-tAI contact.

\paragraph{Local boundary.}
The result remains bounded by its contacts.  The three-organism proxy run is a codon-count and tRNA-copy statistic, not a direct ribosome measurement.  The modeled-tAI run is a computed adaptation-weight readout, not ribosome occupancy, translation efficiency measured by ribosome profiling, or protein abundance.  No statement in this section implies translation realization beyond the attached readout class, and no statement implies protein folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, pathway mechanism, phenotype, or a universal biological law.  Those conclusions require separate matched measurements and their own controlled statistics.  In particular, a nonzero coupling $A_{ij}$ is not promoted to a positive survival score $S_{ij}$ unless the readout is present and the $Z$-controlled partial-$R^2$ or description-length improvement is explicitly computed.
