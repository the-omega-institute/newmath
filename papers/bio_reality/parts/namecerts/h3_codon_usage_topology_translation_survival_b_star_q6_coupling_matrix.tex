\subsection{B-star Q6 coupling against translation-layer proxies}\label{subsec:bioreality-b-star-q6-translation-coupling}\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Object of the record.}
This record concerns the nine synonymous residual coordinates associated with the $B^*_{Q6}$ codon-usage packet: $\mathrm{Arg}_{\mathrm{AGR}}$, $\mathrm{Ile}_{\mathrm{AUA}}$, $\mathrm{K}_{\mathrm{AAA}}$, $\mathrm{Leu}_{\mathrm{CUN}/\mathrm{UUR}}$, $\mathrm{Leu}_{\mathrm{UUA}/\mathrm{UUG}}$, $\mathrm{Ser}_{\mathrm{UCA}/\mathrm{UCG}}$, $\mathrm{Ser}_{\mathrm{UCR}/\mathrm{AGY}}$, $\mathrm{Thr}_{\mathrm{ACR}/\mathrm{ACY}}$, and the third-position stress coordinate $f_3$.  The finite computation was carried out in the $61$-codon count space after retaining the amino-acid quotient as an explicit synonymous projection.  The intended survival object is a matrix $S_{ij}=S_{q_i}(r_j\mid Z)$, where $q_i$ ranges over these nine residual coordinates, $r_j$ ranges over translation-related readouts, and $Z$ contains amino-acid composition, organism context, $\mathrm{GC3}$, coding-sequence length, and the simpler $M$-density baseline.  The present finite row does not yet contain that full $S_{ij}$ matrix.  It contains a proxy coupling computation against a tRNA-Leu CUN-versus-UUR log-copy spectrum, together with a separate three-organism CUN/UUR gate row.

\paragraph{Finite proxy coupling.}
The recorded coupling row used three organisms, namely Homo sapiens, Saccharomyces cerevisiae, and Escherichia coli K-12.  For the proxy readout, every one of the nine residual coordinates produced a nonzero coupling entry above the declared absolute threshold $0.01$.  The largest recorded absolute coupling was
$$
\begin{aligned}
\max_{i,j}|A_{ij}| &= 1882464.1106377328,
\end{aligned}
$$
with $9$ nonzero entries in the reported proxy matrix.  Thus the proxy break condition, which would have declared nullity if all nine coordinates had $|A_{ij}|<0.01$, was not met.  This is evidence that the chosen residual coordinates are not numerically invisible to the attached codon-usage and tRNA-copy proxy spectrum in the finite row.  It is not, by itself, a translation-survival estimate: the row explicitly lacks direct tAI, stAI, ribosome-occupancy, translation-efficiency, or protein-abundance observations paired with the full control set $Z$.

\paragraph{Leu CUN/UUR gate row.}
A second finite row isolates the CUN/UUR Leu association across the same three organisms.  The recorded Pearson correlation is $1.0$, and the amino-acid-composition-controlled partial Pearson value is also $1.0$ with $n=3$.  The organism-level log-ratio values are
$$
\begin{aligned}
(1.3514980212896148,\;-0.24869078488829677,\;1.0086640520051702),
\end{aligned}
$$
and their signs are $(1,-1,1)$.  The sign is therefore not uniform across organisms.  This matters because the row supports only a finite association pattern under the recorded contacts; it does not support a universal direction of effect, and it does not justify replacing organism-specific readback by a population-level law.

\paragraph{Survival target and null condition.}
The scientific target is stricter than the proxy rows.  For a genuine survival entry one must compute, for each residual coordinate $q_i$ and readout $r_j$, the residual readout weight $\ell_j^{\perp}$ after the declared controls, the covariance coupling $A_{ij}=q_i^{T}\Sigma\ell_j^{\perp}$, and the controlled survival score $S_{ij}$, for example through partial $R^2$ or an equivalent description-length improvement after baseline ablation.  The support condition is
$$
\begin{aligned}
\exists i,j\quad S_{ij} &> 0,
\end{aligned}
$$
under amino-acid composition, organism, $\mathrm{GC3}$, length, and $M$-density controls.  The corresponding failure condition is that all entries satisfy $S_{ij}\leq 0$ after those controls.  The finite proxy rows reported here do not resolve that condition, because a nonzero $A_{ij}$ against tRNA-copy spectra is not the same statistic as a positive $S_{ij}$ against a direct translation readout.

\paragraph{Local interpretation boundary.}
The chapter records a bounded codon-usage and tRNA-copy contact.  It does not establish ribosome-mediated translation realization, tRNA abundance, tRNA charging, protein abundance, folding, structural order, physical admissibility, biological function, mechanism, or a universal biological rule.  A translation statement requires an organism-matched translation contact such as tAI, stAI, ribosome profiling, or translation efficiency with the declared controls.  A protein-output statement requires matched proteomics or another protein-abundance measurement.  A structure, physical-admissibility, functional, mechanistic, or universality statement requires its own separate contact and its own statistic.  The current row should therefore be read as a finite proxy coupling and gate record: it keeps the amino-acid quotient visible, reports the non-null proxy couplings and the three-organism Leu sign pattern, and leaves the full $B^*_{Q6}$ translation-survival matrix open.
