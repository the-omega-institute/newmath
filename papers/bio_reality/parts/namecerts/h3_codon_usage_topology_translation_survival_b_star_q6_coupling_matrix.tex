\subsection{A finite proxy coupling row for the $B^*_{Q6}$ translation-survival question}
\label{sec:bioreality-b-star-q6-coupling-matrix}

\paragraph{Object under test.}
The record considered here is the nine-coordinate $B^*_{Q6}$ synonymous residual packet tested against a translation-facing proxy readout. The codon count ambient space has $61$ sense-codon coordinates. After imposing the amino-acid quotient, the computed residual coordinates are
$$
\begin{aligned}
R = \{&\mathrm{Arg\_AGR},\ \mathrm{Ile\_AUA},\ \mathrm{K\_AAA},\ \mathrm{Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR},\ \mathrm{Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG},\\
&\mathrm{Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG},\ \mathrm{Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY},\ \mathrm{Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY},\ \mathrm{f3\_stress}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
For this row the available readout is not a direct ribosome or protein measurement. It is the organism-level tRNA-Leu CUN-versus-UUR log-copy spectrum, used as a proxy translation-adjacent contact. The organisms loaded in the finite table are
$$
\begin{aligned}
O = \{\mathrm{homo\_sapiens},\ \mathrm{saccharomyces\_cerevisiae},\ \mathrm{escherichia\_coli\_k12}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the present chapter records a finite coupling computation and a boundary on what may be inferred from it. It does not record a completed survival matrix $S_{ij}$ for tAI, stAI, ribosome occupancy, translation efficiency, or protein abundance.

\paragraph{Computed finite-row coupling.}
For each residual coordinate $q_i$ in the nine-coordinate packet, the computation forms a synonymous projection against the proxy readout and evaluates a covariance coupling $A_{ij}$. The finite run used the null break condition
$$
\begin{aligned}
\text{null if}\quad |A_{ij}| < 0.01\quad \text{for all nine residual coordinates against the proxy readout.}
\end{aligned}
$$
The threshold was $0.01$. The break condition was not met: all $9$ residual coordinates had nonzero proxy-coupling entries, and at least one entry exceeded the threshold. The maximum absolute coupling reported by the run was
$$
\begin{aligned}
\max_{i,j}|A_{ij}| = 1882464.1106377328.
\end{aligned}
$$
This is a strong finite-row signal at the level of codon-usage topology paired with a tRNA-copy spectrum. The correct readback is narrower than a translation-survival claim: the row says that the amino-acid-quotiented $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinates are not invisible to this particular tRNA-Leu proxy coupling statistic in the three loaded organisms.

\paragraph{CUN/UUR Leu gate row.}
A linked finite row isolates the Leu CUN/UUR comparison. In the same three-organism scope, the log-ratio signs are
$$
\begin{aligned}
(1.3514980212896148,\ -0.24869078488829677,\ 1.0086640520051702),
\end{aligned}
$$
with sign pattern
$$
\begin{aligned}
(+1,\ -1,\ +1).
\end{aligned}
$$
The reported Pearson statistic is $1.0$, and the reported partial Pearson statistic against the tRNA-Leu ratio is also $1.0$ with $n=3$. The sign pattern is not uniform across organisms. Consequently, even though the three-row association is numerically perfect in this finite table, it must be read as a small-sample contact rather than a population estimate. No $p$-value is recorded for this row, and no eight-organism extension is present in the supplied data. The finite row therefore supports a concrete follow-up test but not a universal directionality statement.

\paragraph{Survival matrix status.}
The intended survival object is a matrix $S_{ij}=S_{q_i}(r_j\mid Z)$, where the controls include amino-acid composition, organism context, GC3, coding-sequence length, and an $M$-density baseline. The declared translation readouts include tAI, stAI, ribosome occupancy, translation efficiency, and protein abundance. The available verified row does not yet contain those organism-matched readout vectors under the full control set. It also does not contain the partial $R^2$ entries or description-length improvement entries needed to promote a proxy coupling $A_{ij}$ into a positive survival score $S_{ij}>0$.

The distinction can be stated compactly as
$$
\begin{aligned}
A_{ij}\neq 0\quad &\text{records proxy covariance coupling},\\
S_{ij}>0\quad &\text{requires a controlled translation-readout residual improvement under } Z.
\end{aligned}
$$
The finite data establish the first line for the proxy row and leave the second line open. In particular, the current evidence does not show that any $B^*_{Q6}$ coordinate improves a Z-controlled model of tAI, stAI, ribosome occupancy, translation efficiency, or protein abundance.

\paragraph{Local inference boundary.}
The chapter therefore records a bounded result: nine amino-acid-quotiented $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinates were computed in a $61$-codon count space; the three organisms were Homo sapiens, Saccharomyces cerevisiae, and Escherichia coli K12; the proxy tRNA-Leu CUN-versus-UUR log-copy contact produced $9$ nonzero coupling entries; the maximum absolute coupling was $1882464.1106377328$; and the proxy null condition with threshold $0.01$ failed. These facts justify keeping the translation-survival probe open, because the residual geometry has a finite proxy contact rather than collapsing immediately under the amino-acid quotient.

They do not justify a stronger biological conclusion. No ribosome-mediated translation realization follows from codon counts and tRNA gene-copy tables alone. tRNA gene copy is not tRNA abundance, tRNA charging, ribosome occupancy, or protein output. A three-organism sign reversal cannot be used as universal directionality. No folding, structural order, physical admissibility, biological function, biochemical mechanism, pathway claim, or global biological law follows here unless an additional contact directly supplies that layer and a separate statistic is computed for it. The next decisive row is the full matrix $S_{ij}$ under the stated controls, with direct translation or protein readouts and explicit partial-$R^2$ or description-length scores.
