\subsection{B-star Q6 residual coupling against translation-side readouts}
\label{sec:bioreality-b-star-q6-coupling-matrix}

\paragraph{Record.}
This record concerns the claim
$\mathrm{h3.codon\_usage\_topology.translation\_survival.b\_star\_q6\_coupling\_matrix}$.
The object is a proposed survival array $S_{ij}$ whose rows are the nine synonymous residual coordinates of $B^*_{Q6}$ and whose columns are translation-related readouts.  The intended rows are
$\mathrm{Arg\_AGR}$, $\mathrm{Ile\_AUA}$, $\mathrm{K\_AAA}$, $\mathrm{Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR}$, $\mathrm{Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG}$, $\mathrm{Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG}$, $\mathrm{Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY}$, $\mathrm{Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY}$, and $f_3$-stress.  The ambient codon-count space used in the finite computation has $61$ sense-codon coordinates.  The present contact does not yet contain a full $S_{ij}$ table.  It contains a proxy coupling computation against the tRNA-Leu CUN-versus-UUR log-copy spectrum, together with a separate three-organism CUN/UUR Leu gate check.

\paragraph{Finite rows.}
The finite table loaded three organism contexts:
$\mathrm{Homo\ sapiens}$, $\mathrm{Saccharomyces\ cerevisiae}$, and $\mathrm{Escherichia\ coli\ K12}$.
For the nine residual rows listed above, the proxy break test was declared to be null only if every residual coordinate had absolute coupling below $0.01$ against the proxy readout.  In the recorded run, this break condition was not met: all nine entries were nonzero above the threshold test, and the largest absolute proxy coupling was
$$
\begin{aligned}
\max_{i,j} |A_{ij}| &= 1882464.1106377328,\\
\#\{(i,j): |A_{ij}|>0.01\} &= 9.
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the finite codon-count/tRNA-copy contact shows that the nine $B^*_{Q6}$ residual directions are not all numerically silent against this particular proxy spectrum.  The statistic is a coupling statistic $A_{ij}$, not a controlled survival statistic $S_{ij}$.

\paragraph{Leu gate row.}
The associated CUN/UUR Leu gate check used the same three organism contexts.  The recorded log-ratio row was
$$
\begin{aligned}
(1.3514980212896148,\ -0.24869078488829677,\ 1.0086640520051702),
\end{aligned}
$$
with signs $(+,-,+)$.  Hence the sign is not uniform across organisms.  The three-row Pearson statistic and the amino-acid-composition-controlled partial Pearson statistic were both recorded as
$$
\begin{aligned}
\rho &= 1.0,\\
\rho_{\mathrm{partial}} &= 1.0,\\
n &= 3.
\end{aligned}
$$
These numbers certify only the finite three-row association present in the loaded table.  They do not by themselves estimate a population-level direction, and the sign reversal already prevents treating the direction as organism-independent.

\paragraph{Survival interpretation.}
The scientific target is stronger than the proxy table.  For a genuine survival entry one must form, for each residual coordinate $q_i$ and each translation-side readout $r_j$, a residualized readout $\ell_j^{\perp}$ after controlling for amino-acid composition, organism context, GC3, transcript or coding-sequence length, and the simpler $M$-density baseline.  The survival entry is then a controlled improvement score, such as a partial-$R^2$ or description-length reduction, rather than a raw covariance contact.  In symbolic form, the intended distinction is
$$
\begin{aligned}
A_{ij} &= q_i^{T}\Sigma\ell_j^{\perp},\\
S_{ij} &= S_{q_i}(r_j\mid Z),\\
Z &= (\mathrm{amino\ acid\ composition},\ \mathrm{organism},\ \mathrm{GC3},\ \mathrm{length},\ M\text{-}\mathrm{density}).
\end{aligned}
$$
The present evidence supports the statement that the proxy coupling matrix is not numerically zero under the declared threshold.  It does not support the stronger statement that some $S_{ij}$ is positive, because the required organism-matched tAI, stAI, ribosome-profiling, or protein-abundance readout rows have not been paired with the full control set in the recorded memory.

\paragraph{Local claim boundary.}
The contact is therefore bounded as follows.  Codon-usage tables and tRNA gene-copy annotations supply sequence-composition and genome-annotation rows; they do not measure ribosome motion, tRNA charging, mature protein concentration, protein folding, biochemical function, physical admissibility, mechanism, or a universal biological law.  The proxy coupling $A_{ij}$ may motivate a survival test, but it is not itself a survival entry.  A positive translation-realization claim requires a direct translation-side readout and an explicit controlled estimate of $S_{ij}>0$.  Protein abundance, folding, function, physical admissibility, mechanistic interpretation, and cross-organism universality each require their own matched contact and cannot be inferred from the three-organism proxy matrix.
