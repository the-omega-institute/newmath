\subsection{B-star-Q6 residual coordinates against translation readouts}\label{sec:b-star-q6-translation-survival-matrix}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Claim registered by the finite rows.}
This record concerns the nine residual coordinates of $B^*_{Q6}$ after the amino-acid quotient has been imposed.  The coordinates are
$K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $Leu_{CUN/UUR}$, $Leu_{UUA/UUG}$, $Ser_{UCR/AGY}$, $Ser_{UCA/UCG}$, $Thr_{ACR/ACY}$, and the $f_3$ stress coordinate.  The intended survival object is a matrix $S_{ij}$ whose rows are these residual coordinates and whose columns are translation-related readouts such as tAI, stAI, ribosome occupancy, translation efficiency, or protein abundance when those columns are present as matched measurements.  The controls are part of the claim, not an optional adjustment: amino-acid composition, organism context, GC3, CDS length, and the simpler $M$-density baseline must be removed before a positive survival entry is reported.

\paragraph{Matrix statistic.}
For a coordinate $q_i$ and a readout direction $r_j$, the admissible statistic first forms a readout residual $\ell_j^{\perp}$ after the declared controls and then measures the residual covariance coupling
$$
\begin{aligned}
A_{ij} &= q_i^T \Sigma \ell_j^{\perp},\\
S_{ij} &= S_{q_i}(r_j \mid Z),\\
Z &= (\hbox{amino-acid composition},\hbox{ organism}, GC3,\hbox{ length},\hbox{ $M$-density baseline}).
\end{aligned}
$$
The sign and magnitude of $A_{ij}$ are only a coupling readback.  A survival entry requires a separate controlled partial-$R^2$ or description-length improvement calculation for $S_{ij}$.  Thus a nonzero $A_{ij}$ may nominate a row-column pair for testing, but it does not itself certify survival.

\paragraph{Observed proxy coupling row.}
The finite proxy computation used a $61$-codon count space and loaded three organisms: $homo\_sapiens$, $saccharomyces\_cerevisiae$, and $escherichia\_coli\_k12$.  The proxy column was the tRNA-Leu $CUN$ versus $UUR$ log-copy spectrum, not measured ribosome profiling, not protein output, and not a true tAI or stAI panel.  All nine residual coordinates were computed: $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $K_{AAA}$, $Leu_{CUN/UUR}$, $Leu_{UUA/UUG}$, $Ser_{UCA/UCG}$, $Ser_{UCR/AGY}$, $Thr_{ACR/ACY}$, and $f_3$ stress.  The break rule for that proxy row was that the packet would be null if every one of the nine $q_i$ had $|A_{ij}|<0.01$.  The rule was not met: the computation recorded $9$ nonzero entries and a maximum absolute coupling
$$
\begin{aligned}
\max_{i,j}|A_{ij}| &= 1882464.1106377328,\\
\tau_A &= 0.01.
\end{aligned}
$$
This is evidence that the residual-coordinate geometry has a nontrivial proxy contact with a tRNA-copy spectrum.  It is not yet a measured ribosome or protein survival matrix.

\paragraph{Modeled tAI survival panel.}
A separate modeled tAI computation expanded the organism panel to $40$ organisms and formed a $9\times 9$ $S^{QT}$ matrix using modeled per-codon tAI derived from GtRNAdb anticodon copy counts and dos Reis wobble weights.  The nine modeled readout columns matched the nine residual coordinates: $K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $Leu_{CUN/UUR}$, $Leu_{UUA/UUG}$, $Ser_{UCR/AGY}$, $Ser_{UCA/UCG}$, $Thr_{ACR/ACY}$, and $f_3$ stress.  The control design had $24$ control columns, rank $23$, and $17$ residual degrees of freedom after $Z$-residualization.  The residual coordinate design had rank $9$.  The reported modeled translation fit was
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{QT} &= 0.9003849000036579,\\
\operatorname{rank}(Z) &= 23,\\
\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde X) &= 9,\\
\operatorname{df}_{res} &= 17.
\end{aligned}
$$
The degeneracy threshold for refusing the design was $0.999999$, so this modeled panel was rank-powered under the stated criterion.  The positivity threshold for a modeled partial-$R^2$ entry was $10^{-12}$.  This supports the narrower statement that $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinates can survive against modeled tAI-style readouts after the listed controls in a $40$-organism panel.  The result remains model-bounded because the readout is constructed from tRNA gene-copy counts and wobble weights rather than measured elongation speed, ribosome occupancy, or protein abundance.

\paragraph{Leu gate finite row.}
The CUN/UUR Leu gate supplies a smaller organism-level contact.  It used the three organisms $homo\_sapiens$, $saccharomyces\_cerevisiae$, and $escherichia\_coli\_k12$ and reported Pearson correlation $1.0$ and partial Pearson correlation $1.0$ for $n=3$ after the stated finite-row control.  The organism log-ratios were $1.3514980212896148$, $-0.24869078488829677$, and $1.0086640520051702$, with signs $+1,-1,+1$.  Therefore the sign was not uniform across organisms.  This row is useful as a contact check for the Leu coordinate, but the sample size and sign reversal prevent any population-level direction claim.

\paragraph{Local boundary.}
The chapter records three separable levels.  First, the three-organism proxy coupling row shows that the nine residual coordinates are not all zero against a tRNA-copy proxy under the declared $|A_{ij}|$ threshold.  Second, the $40$-organism modeled tAI panel gives a controlled, nondegenerate $9\times 9$ modeled $S^{QT}$ surface with $R^2_{QT}=0.9003849000036579$.  Third, direct measured translation survival remains conditional on organism- or CDS-matched ribosome profiling, translation-efficiency, stAI, or protein-abundance rows with the same controls.  No ribosome-mediated translation event, folding conclusion, physical admissibility claim, biological function, mechanism, or universal law follows from the proxy coupling, the modeled tAI matrix, the identifier routing, or the finite Leu gate unless a separate layer-matched contact supplies that observation and the corresponding controlled statistic is computed.

\paragraph{Operational verdict.}
The finite evidence is sufficient to keep the $B^*_{Q6}$ translation-survival matrix as an active BioReality record rather than a null packet.  The strongest completed support is the modeled $S^{QT}$ panel on $40$ organisms with rank $9$ residual coordinates, rank $23$ controls, $17$ residual degrees of freedom, and $R^2_{QT}=0.9003849000036579$.  The strongest measured-data limitation is that the direct ribosome-profiling, matched mRNA, and matched protein-abundance contacts are still routes for future row attachment rather than completed same-row survival estimates for this matrix.
