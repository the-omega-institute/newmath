\subsection{The $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual coupling matrix for translation-facing readouts}\label{sec:h3-codon-usage-topology-translation-survival-b-star-q6-coupling-matrix}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Question.}
This record asks whether the nine synonymous-residual coordinates in $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ leave any detectable trace after the amino-acid quotient and ordinary sequence-context controls have been imposed.  The finite object is a matrix whose rows are
$K\_AAA$, $Arg\_AGR$, $Ile\_AUA$, $Leu\_{CUN/UUR}$, $Leu\_{UUA/UUG}$, $Ser\_{UCR/AGY}$, $Ser\_{UCA/UCG}$, $Thr\_{ACR/ACY}$, and the antisymmetric $f3$ stress pair.  The intended columns are translation-facing readouts such as tAI, stAI, ribosome occupancy, translation efficiency, or protein abundance when the corresponding organism-matched rows are present.  For a coordinate $q_i$, a readout residual $r_j$, and controls $Z$ consisting of amino-acid composition, organism context, $GC3$, coding-sequence length, and the $M$-density baseline, the relevant survival statistic is a controlled partial contribution rather than a raw association.

\paragraph{Matrix statistic.}
Let $\ell_j^{\perp}$ denote the part of a readout weight vector left after projection away from the synonymous amino-acid quotient and the declared controls.  The coupling entry is computed as
$$
\begin{aligned}
A_{ij} &= q_i^{T}\Sigma\ell_j^{\perp},\\
S_{ij} &= S_{q_i}(r_j\mid Z),
\end{aligned}
$$
where $A_{ij}$ is a residual-covariance coupling and $S_{ij}$ is the corresponding controlled survival score, equivalently a partial-$R^2$ or description-length improvement entry under the same residualization.  The finite-row break condition for the proxy coupling check was that every one of the nine coordinates satisfy $|A_{ij}|<0.01$ against the proxy readout.  That condition was not met: all $9$ row entries were nonzero above the threshold, with maximum absolute coupling
$$
\begin{aligned}
\max_i |A_{ij}| &= 1882464.1106377328.
\end{aligned}
$$
The computation used a $61$-codon count space and three organism rows, namely Homo sapiens, Saccharomyces cerevisiae, and Escherichia coli K12.  The proxy readout in this finite row was the tRNA-Leu CUN-versus-UUR log-copy spectrum, not a direct ribosome-profiling or protein-abundance measurement.

\paragraph{Modelled translation-weight row.}
A separate powered modeled-tAI run attached a larger organism table to the same nine residual coordinates.  It used $40$ organism rows, $9$ residual-coordinate columns, and $9$ modeled readout columns derived from GtRNAdb anticodon copy counts with dos Reis wobble weights.  The residualization design had $24$ control columns, rank $23$, and $17$ residual degrees of freedom after controls.  The residual-coordinate design had rank $9$, and the resulting modeled survival matrix had shape $9\times 9$.  The reported aggregate rank-powered statistic was
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{QT} &= 0.9003849000036579,
\end{aligned}
$$
with the degeneracy guard set at $0.999999$ and the positive-entry threshold set at $10^{-12}$.  This supports a bounded modeled translation-weight survival statement: the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates are not numerically absorbed by the amino-acid, organism, $GC3$, length, and baseline-control model when the readout is the modeled codon-adaptation-weight construction.

\paragraph{Leucine gate finite row.}
The CUN-versus-UUR leucine row gives a small but concrete sign-diagnostic contact.  In the three-organism table, the log-ratio values were
$$
\begin{aligned}
(1.3514980212896148,\;-0.24869078488829677,\;1.0086640520051702),
\end{aligned}
$$
with signs $(1,-1,1)$.  The Pearson statistic and the amino-acid-composition-controlled partial Pearson statistic were both reported as $1.0$ on $n=3$ rows.  This row is therefore a finite contact for the existence of a nonzero residual association, while its sign reversal prevents any reading as a stable cross-organism direction law.

\paragraph{Local interpretation.}
The finite evidence separates three levels.  First, the three-organism tRNA-copy proxy coupling has a nonzero $A_{ij}$ signal above the declared threshold, so the residual coordinate basis has not collapsed to the null proxy geometry.  Second, the $40$-organism modeled-tAI calculation supplies a controlled $9\times 9$ modeled translation-weight survival matrix with $R^2_{QT}=0.9003849000036579$ after rank and residual-degree checks.  Third, the current record does not yet contain an organism-matched ribosome-profiling, translation-efficiency, or measured protein-abundance survival matrix for this exact claim.  Consequently the chapter may report proxy coupling and modeled translation-weight survival, but it may not promote those rows to measured ribosome-mediated translation, protein output, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, mechanism, phenotype, or universality.

\paragraph{Boundary.}
No translation mechanism follows from codon usage or tRNA gene-copy counts alone.  tRNA gene copy is not tRNA abundance, charging, ribosome occupancy, elongation speed, or protein output.  A positive $A_{ij}$ entry is not by itself a positive $S_{ij}$ entry unless the declared controls have been applied and the partial survival statistic is computed.  The modeled-tAI row is a controlled modeled readout, not ribo-seq, proteomics, folding, structure, function, phenotype, or pathway evidence.  Any such stronger assertion requires its own matched measurement contact and its own controlled finite-row statistic.
