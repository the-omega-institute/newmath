\subsection{Finite codon residual coupling for the $B^*_{Q6}$ translation interface}
\label{sec:biorreality-b-star-q6-translation-survival-coupling}

\paragraph{Question.}
The record tests whether the nine synonymous residual coordinates associated with $B^*_{Q6}$ leave any detectable trace at the translation-facing layer after the amino-acid quotient has been imposed. The intended survival object is a matrix $S_{ij}$, with rows indexed by residual codon coordinates $q_i$ and columns indexed by translation-related readouts $r_j$. In its full form the statistic would compare each $q_i$ with readouts such as tAI, stAI, ribosome occupancy, translation efficiency, or protein abundance after controlling for amino-acid composition, organism context, GC3, coding-sequence length, and the simpler $M$-density baseline. The finite row presently available is weaker: it computes a proxy coupling matrix $A_{ij}$ against a tRNA-Leu CUN-versus-UUR log-copy spectrum, not a completed $S_{ij}$ survival matrix.

\paragraph{Coordinate set.}
The computed residual basis contains exactly nine $B^*_{Q6}$ synonymous coordinates:
$$
\begin{aligned}
R = \{&\mathrm{Arg\_AGR},\ \mathrm{Ile\_AUA},\ \mathrm{K\_AAA},\ \mathrm{Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR},\ \\
&\mathrm{Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG},\ \mathrm{Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG},\ \mathrm{Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY},\ \\
&\mathrm{Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY},\ \mathrm{f3\_stress}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The ambient codon-count space has $61$ sense-codon coordinates. The finite contact loads three organism rows: Homo sapiens, Saccharomyces cerevisiae, and Escherichia coli K12. The amino-acid quotient is retained through the synonymous projection, so the calculation is not a raw codon-count comparison across different amino-acid compositions.

\paragraph{Proxy coupling statistic.}
For the available run, the readout column is the tRNA-Leu CUN-versus-UUR log-copy spectrum. The coupling entry is the covariance pairing between a residual coordinate and the projected readout weight,
$$
\begin{aligned}
A_{ij} = q_i^{T}\Sigma \ell_j^{\perp},
\end{aligned}
$$
where $\ell_j^{\perp}$ denotes the synonymous projection of the readout weight and $\Sigma$ is the residual count covariance used by the finite computation. The run used the break rule that the proxy contact would be null if every one of the nine coordinates had $|A_{ij}| < 0.01$. That break condition was not met: the reported threshold was $0.01$, all $9$ entries were nonzero above the declared zero test, and the maximum absolute coupling was
$$
\begin{aligned}
\max_{i,j}|A_{ij}| = 1882464.1106377328.
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the finite row supports a nonzero proxy coupling between the $B^*_{Q6}$ residual basis and the tRNA-Leu CUN/UUR copy spectrum. It does not, by itself, support a positive survival score $S_{ij}$, because $S_{ij}$ requires a direct translation readout and the full control set.

\paragraph{Leu CUN/UUR cross-organism row.}
A linked three-organism Leu gate calculation reports a Pearson correlation of $1.0$ and a partial Pearson correlation of $1.0$ for the CUN/UUR codon-usage ratio against the tRNA-Leu copy-ratio contact. The row count is $n=3$. The reported organism-level log ratios are
$$
\begin{aligned}
(1.3514980212896148,\ -0.24869078488829677,\ 1.0086640520051702),
\end{aligned}
$$
with signs
$$
\begin{aligned}
(+1,\ -1,\ +1).
\end{aligned}
$$
The sign-uniformity flag is false. This matters because the perfect three-row correlation is not a population estimate and is not a universal direction claim. It is a finite alignment in a very small organism table, accompanied by a sign reversal in one row. The row is useful as a targeted contact for the Leu CUN/UUR coordinate, but it cannot replace the intended multi-organism survival matrix.

\paragraph{Survival matrix status.}
The intended survival entry would have the form
$$
\begin{aligned}
S_{ij}=S_{q_i}(r_j\mid Z),\qquad
Z=(\mathrm{amino\ acid\ composition},\ \mathrm{organism},\ \mathrm{GC3},\ \mathrm{length},\ M\mathrm{-density\ baseline}).
\end{aligned}
$$
A positive entry would require a partial-$R^2$ or description-length improvement after those controls and after ablation against the simpler codon baseline. The current finite record does not contain such a controlled $S_{ij}$ table. It contains a proxy $A_{ij}$ contact using codon counts and tRNA-copy annotations, plus a linked three-organism Leu CUN/UUR association. Therefore the correct reading is: $B^*_{Q6}$ is not erased at the proxy coupling level, but translation survival remains open until organism-matched tAI, stAI, ribosome-profiling, translation-efficiency, or protein-abundance rows are joined to the same residual coordinates and controls.

\paragraph{Local claim boundary.}
No ribosome-mediated translation survival follows from the proxy coupling alone. No folding, structural order, physical admissibility, molecular function, pathway role, mechanism, or universal biological law follows from either the $A_{ij}$ matrix or the three-organism Leu row. tRNA gene-copy ratio is not treated as tRNA abundance, tRNA charging, ribosome occupancy, translation efficiency, or protein output. mRNA abundance, when present, is only a transcription-layer control for translation-efficiency residualization. Protein abundance, when present, would be a downstream readout and would still need its own matched controlled statistic. A stronger claim requires a separate contact supplying the missing readout rows and a recorded $Z$-controlled survival or mediation estimate.
