\paragraph{Powered finite-row result.}
The powered boundary-set assay is indexed by claim id \texttt{h3.cross\_layer\_relation.boundary\_set\_trna\_poverty.b\_star\_q6\_window6\_R\_mechanism\_powered}. At level $\alpha=0.05$, using $1000$ permutations and the deterministic seed \texttt{sha256:b\_star\_q6\_boundary\_set\_trna\_poverty\_powered:R-sense-tRNA-poverty-permutation}, the assay passed.  Its recorded call is that $R$ sense codons are systematically tRNA-poor under GtRNAdb tGCN together with dos Reis wobble weights, supporting a tRNA-supply explanation for avoidance of those codons within this bounded codon-supply test.

The present subsection uses them only to locate the boundary-set call within the finite $Q6$ coordinate neighborhood: $f_3$ stress remains visible after amino-acid quotienting, tRNA supply is one bounded contact surface, and the $R$-sense boundary has a permutation-tested shortage signal under the declared wobble-weight model.

\paragraph{Readout boundary.}
The positive call is a codon-supply result, not a direct translation-efficiency result.  Codon-usage tables, tRNA gene-copy annotations, and modeled tAI or stAI weights can support the statement that a finite codon class is supply-poor relative to its matched wobble-weight contact.  They do not by themselves show ribosome occupancy, translation efficiency, protein abundance, folded structure, localization, molecular function, physical admissibility, phenotype, causation, or a universal biological law.  A separate organism- and CDS-matched translation row is required before the $f_3$ stress coordinate may be read as surviving into translation output.

In particular, a translation-readout claim for this same coordinate would require same-row codon counts, wobble-weight asymmetry, ribosome-profiling or translation-efficiency observations, and matched mRNA controls.  The controlled statistic would have to remain positive after amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, CDS length, $M$-density, matched mRNA, rank, and simpler-baseline controls.  If that controlled survival entry is absent, non-positive, or rank-degenerate, the present tRNA-poverty call remains only a supply-side boundary fact for $R$ avoidance.

\paragraph{Local conclusion.}
Within the available finite rows, the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ $f_3$ stress boundary has a passed, powered tRNA-poverty result: $40$ computed organisms out of $46$ requested organisms, $1000$ permutations, $\alpha=0.05$, and a recorded GtRNAdb tGCN plus dos Reis wobble-weight call that $R$ sense codons are systematically tRNA-poor.  The scientifically admissible conclusion is therefore narrow but non-vacuous: the $R$-sense boundary is compatible with a tRNA-supply pressure on codon avoidance in the bounded supply-contact layer.  No stronger statement about measured translation, protein output, structure, physical admissibility, function, mechanism beyond the attached tRNA-supply contact, or universality follows from this row alone.
