\subsection{The $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ boundary set under tRNA poverty}
\label{sec:bioreality-q6-boundary-set-trna-poverty}

\paragraph{Finite coordinate surface.}
This record concerns the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual coordinate surface after amino-acid projection, restricted to the $f_3$-stress coordinate and to the codon rows where a translation-layer readout could in principle be attached.  The finite seed for the coordinate side is explicit.  The set $M=\operatorname{Med}(R)\cup\{\mathrm{ACA},\mathrm{ACG}\}$ has the twenty codons
$$
\begin{aligned}
M=\{&\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{ACA},\mathrm{ACG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\\
&\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The verified $f_3$-saturation of $R$ removes exactly $\mathrm{ACA}$ and $\mathrm{ACG}$ from this displayed set, giving the eighteen-codon saturated row
$$
\begin{aligned}
\operatorname{Sat}_{f_3}(R)=\{&\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\\
&\mathrm{CUU},\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The recorded $f_3$ boundary complement is empty.  Thus the finite codon geometry does not ask for an additional unsaturated third-position edge before the biological-contact question is posed; the remaining question is whether the rows identified by this coordinate are poor in the relevant tRNA or wobble-weight supply and whether any later translation readout survives controls.

\paragraph{Powered tRNA-poverty test.}
The powered boundary-set experiment was run as \texttt{b\_star\_q6\_boundary\_set\_trna\_poverty\_powered}.  It requested $46$ organisms, computed $40$, skipped $6$, and used $1000$ permutations at $\alpha=0.05$.  The finite-row conclusion is that $R$ sense codons are systematically tRNA-poor when organism-level GtRNAdb tRNA gene copy number is combined with dos Reis wobble weights.  In formulas, the tested sign is a supply contrast of the form
$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta_R
  &= \operatorname{Supply}(R\text{ sense rows})
     -\operatorname{Supply}(\text{matched non-}R\text{ sense rows}),\\
\Delta_R &< 0
\end{aligned}
$$
under the declared permutation gate.  The experiment status is \texttt{passed}; the recorded seed is \texttt{sha256:b\_star\_q6\_boundary\_set\_trna\_poverty\_powered:R-sense-tRNA-poverty-permutation}.  The biological content of this pass is narrow: it supports a tRNA-supply explanation for avoidance of the $R$ sense boundary rows under the stated organism panel and wobble model.  It is not a statement about protein abundance, folded structure, localization, function, phenotype, or a global biological law.

\paragraph{Relation to the residual spectrum.}
The surrounding $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ powered spectrum is consistent with treating the tRNA-poverty result as a boundary mechanism rather than as a complete translation-output theorem.  A separate axis-strength survey computed $11$ of $16$ requested organisms with $5$ folds, at least $500$ proteins per organism, and $41$ synonymous design columns.  Its captured spectra were led on average by $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$ and then by $d_{\mathrm{tRNA}}$, with per-organism dominance count $\{d_{\mathrm{resid4}}:11\}$ and an imbalanced domain count $\{\mathrm{archaea}:1,\mathrm{bacteria}:2,\mathrm{eukaryote}:8\}$.  The largest recorded GC3 rank effect in that survey was $\rho=0.136$ for $d_{\mathrm{usage}}$.

Additional powered rows place the boundary in a larger but still finite cross-layer picture.  The universal residual-axis test computed $11$ of $16$ organisms and identified the non-tRNA residual optimum with $f_3$ translation selection.  The two-axis closure test, again on $11$ computed organisms with $5$ folds and $41$ synonymous columns, concluded that a third axis is present because $d_{\perp}$ has systematically positive held-out $R^2$ and same-signed directions across organisms.  The third-axis identity row identified $d_{\perp}$ provisionally with usage frequency within family, with mean cosine $0.128$ and one-sided sign-test $p=0.000488$.  The third-axis mechanism row reported selection-not-GC, not-wobble-clustered, and a CSC or positional identity still requiring external resolution.  The three-axis sufficiency row concluded that a fourth mechanism remains present.  These facts prevent the tRNA-poverty result from being read as a complete basis decomposition; it is a verified boundary pressure inside a larger residual spectrum.

\paragraph{Readout gate.}
The $f_3$-stress coordinate becomes a biological translation-readout claim only after a matched translation row is attached.  The admissible readout is a controlled survival entry $S_{f3\text{-stress},T_j}$ for a bounded translation measurement such as tAI, stAI, ribosome occupancy, or translation efficiency, with amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, CDS length, $M$-density, and mRNA controls present.  The intended support condition is
$$
\begin{aligned}
S_{f3\text{-stress},T_j} &> 0
\end{aligned}
$$
under the declared controls and simpler-codon-baseline ablation.  The present chapter does not assert that inequality for ribosome profiling or translation efficiency.  The matched ribosome-profiling and mRNA contacts describe the kind of data needed for that test, but the same-organism and same-CDS joined matrix is not part of the finite row certified here.

\paragraph{Local refusal boundary.}
The verified claim is therefore the following bounded statement.  On the finite $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ codon surface, the $f_3$-saturated $R$ boundary has no remaining unsaturated $f_3$ complement, and the powered organism panel finds the $R$ sense rows systematically tRNA-poor under tGCN plus dos Reis wobble weighting.  This is enough to support a tRNA-supply account of $R$-row avoidance in the tested boundary set.  It is not enough to claim measured translation survival, protein output, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, phenotype, universality, or any downstream mechanism beyond the stated tRNA-supply contrast.  Each such promotion requires its own layer-matched contact and its own controlled statistic.  In particular, a three-organism all-$1.0$ surface would be rank saturation rather than survival evidence, and a modeled tAI or stAI weight is not the same object as an observed ribosome-occupancy or translation-efficiency row.
