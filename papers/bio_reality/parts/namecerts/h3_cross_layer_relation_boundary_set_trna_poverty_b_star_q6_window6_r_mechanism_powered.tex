\subsection{The $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ boundary set and the tRNA-poverty row}

\paragraph{Finite coordinate being tested.}
The record considered here is the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ third-position stress coordinate after amino-acid projection, restricted to a finite coding-sequence-by-organism residual surface and then paired with translation-side rows.  Its biological carrier is not a protein, a pathway, or a phenotype. The specific claim is that the $R$ boundary of the $Q6$ surface is tRNA-poor under a rank-powered multi-organism panel, and that this supplies a bounded tRNA-supply explanation for avoidance of the $R$ sense region.

\paragraph{Powered tRNA-poverty result.}
The direct finite-row result for this packet is the run `d7728cc8768e`.  The experiment `b\_star\_q6\_boundary\_set\_trna\_poverty\_powered` requested $46$ organisms, computed $40$, and skipped $6$.  It used $1000$ permutations at $\alpha = 0.05$.  The recorded status is `passed`.  Its finite conclusion is that $R$ sense codons are systematically tRNA-poor under GtRNAdb tRNA gene-copy number together with dos Reis wobble weights.  In symbols, the accepted row is a one-sided supply asymmetry statement of the following form:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta_R
  &= \operatorname{Supply}(R\text{-sense codons})
     - \operatorname{Supply}(\text{matched non-}R\text{ sense controls}),\\
\Pr_{\pi}(\Delta_R^{\pi} \leq \Delta_R)
  &< 0.05
\end{aligned}
$$
where the permutation distribution ranges over the declared organism-matched codon and tRNA-weight rows.  The recorded table does not provide the numerical value of $\Delta_R$ or the exact empirical permutation $p$-value, so the paper-level statement must remain at the passed-threshold level rather than reporting an unrecorded scalar.

\paragraph{What the row licenses.}
The valid conclusion is narrow.  Within the finite panel that was actually computed, $R$ sense codons show systematic tRNA poverty under GtRNAdb tGCN and dos Reis wobble weighting.  This supports a supply-pressure interpretation for $R$ avoidance at the codon-usage and tRNA-weight layer.  It also makes the $f_3$-stress coordinate a plausible candidate for a downstream translation-readout test, because third-position asymmetry becomes informative only where context tRNA or wobble weights break the $f_3$ symmetry:
$$
\begin{aligned}
w_c \neq w_{c^{f_3}}
\quad \Longrightarrow \quad
q_{f_3\text{-stress}} \text{ can be paired with a translation row.}
\end{aligned}
$$
Here $w_c$ is the context tRNA or wobble weight of codon $c$, and $c^{f_3}$ denotes its third-position $f_3$ partner.  The pairing is only a test route.  It is not, by itself, a measured survival entry.

\paragraph{Translation-readout boundary.}
The required downstream statistic is a controlled survival entry $S_{f3\text{-stress},T_j}$ for a translation readout $T_j$, such as tAI, stAI, ribosome occupancy, or translation efficiency.  A positive biological readout requires a non-vacuous, non-rank-degenerate statistic after amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, CDS length, $M$-density, matched mRNA, rank, and simpler-baseline controls:
$$
\begin{aligned}
S_{f3\text{-stress},T_j} > 0.
\end{aligned}
$$
The finite records supplied for this chapter do not contain same-organism and same-CDS joined codon-count, wobble-weight, ribosome-profiling or translation-efficiency, and matched mRNA-control rows for this exact $f_3$-stress packet.  Therefore the tRNA-poverty result cannot be promoted to measured translation survival.  It also cannot be promoted to ribosome occupancy, protein abundance, folded structure, physical admissibility, molecular function, phenotype, causal mechanism in the full biological sense, or a universal law.  Each such layer would require its own contact and its own controlled statistic.

\paragraph{Local falsification rule.}
The packet is broken as a translation-readout claim if the controlled surface remains non-positive or rank-degenerate across a powered panel:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\forall T_j,\quad S_{f3\text{-stress},T_j} \leq 0,
\end{aligned}
$$
or if the only apparent support comes from a rank-saturated small panel, such as an all-$1.0$ three-organism matrix.  Such a matrix is a null boundary, not survival.  Conversely, the present $40$-organism tRNA-poverty result is retained as a codon-supply asymmetry even if later translation rows fail.  Failure of $S_{f3\text{-stress},T_j}$ would constrain the readout interface; it would not erase the finite tRNA-supply row.
