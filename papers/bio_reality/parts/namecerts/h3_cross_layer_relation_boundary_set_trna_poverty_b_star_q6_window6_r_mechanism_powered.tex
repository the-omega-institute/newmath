\subsection{A tRNA-poor boundary for the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ $f_3$-stress coordinate}
\label{sec:bioreality-q6-f3-stress-trna-poverty}

\paragraph{Finite carrier.}
This BioReality record concerns a finite CDS-by-organism residual coordinate surface restricted to the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ $f_3$-stress coordinate, together with organism-level codon usage, tRNA-copy or wobble-weight rows, and translation-readout routes. The carrier is not a protein phenotype and not a structural model. It is a controlled codon-coordinate packet whose biological contact is first made through the imbalance between synonymous codons after amino-acid quotienting and the availability of matching tRNA or wobble-weight support. The finite saturation row fixes the relevant codon boundary: the set $M = \mathrm{Med}_R \cup \{\mathrm{ACA}\}$ is recorded as
$$
\begin{aligned}
M=\{&\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{ACA},\mathrm{ACG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\\
&\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The $f_3$ saturation row removes $\mathrm{ACA}$ and $\mathrm{ACG}$ from that list and leaves
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{Sat}_{f_3}(R)=\{&\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\\
&\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The recorded $f_3$ boundary is empty, $\partial_{f_3}R=\varnothing$, so the chapter treats the signal as a residual-coordinate stress surface rather than as a remaining codon-list boundary.

\paragraph{Powered tRNA-poverty row.}
The powered finite-row experiment for this claim has status passed. It requested $46$ organisms, computed $40$, skipped $6$, used $1000$ permutations, and used $\alpha=0.05$. Its recorded mechanism call is that $R$ sense codons are systematically tRNA-poor under GtRNAdb tGCN together with dos Reis wobble weights. This supports a bounded tRNA-supply explanation for avoidance of the $R$ sense boundary in the observed finite panel. In mathematical shorthand, the row supports the finite inequality pattern
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{tRNA\ support}(R\text{-sense}) < \mathrm{matched\ synonymous\ background}
\end{aligned}
$$
under the declared permutation design and wobble-weight scheme. The assertion is deliberately local: it says that the $R$ sense part of the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ stress surface is tRNA-poor in the computed organism panel. It does not say that every organism realizes the same effect size, and it does not turn codon geometry alone into a translation measurement.

\paragraph{Relation to the wider $Q6$ coordinate spectrum.}
The nearby powered rows make the tRNA-poverty call more interpretable but do not enlarge it beyond its contact layer. The axis-strength spectrum computed $11$ of $16$ requested organisms with $5$ folds and $41$ synonymous design columns; in that row, $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$ led the captured spectra on average, followed by $d_{\mathrm{tRNA}}$, and all $11$ per-organism dominant counts were assigned to $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$. The same row notes that domain means are descriptive only because the computed panel was imbalanced, with $1$ archaeal, $2$ bacterial, and $8$ eukaryotic organisms, and that the largest GC3 rank effect was $\rho=0.136$ for $d_{\mathrm{usage}}$. The third-axis identity row computed $11$ of $16$ organisms, used $5$ folds and $41$ synonymous design columns, and reported a candidate usage-frequency-within-family alignment with mean cosine $0.128$ and one-sided sign-test $p=0.000488$. The third-axis mechanism row retained significant cross-species alignment and held-out $R^2$ after GC orthogonalization, and classified the result as selection-not-GC, not-wobble-clustered, with CSC or positional identity still needing a separate outside contact.

\paragraph{Readout boundary.}
The conjectural readout is the controlled survival entry $S_{f_3\text{-stress},T_j}$ for a bounded translation layer such as tAI, stAI, ribosome occupancy, or translation efficiency. The present chapter has a passed tRNA-poverty mechanism row, not a measured translation-survival row. Therefore the legitimate mathematical object here is a mechanism-adjacent boundary condition: $f_3$-paired synonymous imbalance becomes biologically testable only where tRNA or wobble weights distinguish a codon from its third-position $f_3$ partner. A same-row translation claim would require joined codon counts, wobble-breaking tAI or stAI rows, measured ribosome-profiling or translation-efficiency rows, and matched mRNA controls, followed by a controlled statistic after amino-acid composition, organism identity, GC3, CDS length, $M$-density, rank sufficiency, and simpler-baseline ablations. In symbols, promotion to translation survival would require a non-vacuous positive entry
$$
\begin{aligned}
S_{f_3\text{-stress},T_j}>0
\end{aligned}
$$
under those controls. The finite memory supplied here does not contain that joined statistic.

\paragraph{No-promotion rule.}
The chapter therefore allows one conclusion and blocks several tempting stronger ones. The allowed conclusion is that, in the powered panel, $R$ sense codons are systematically tRNA-poor under the declared GtRNAdb tGCN and dos Reis wobble-weight construction. The blocked conclusions are translation realization without a same-row survival statistic, ribosome occupancy from modeled adaptation weights alone, protein abundance from codon or tRNA rows, folded structure from residual coordinates, physical admissibility from sequence geometry, biological function from survival notation, and universality from a finite organism panel. The linked fourth-mechanism rows reinforce this restraint: the mRNA-stability assay concluded that the fourth axis is not mRNA stability in that assay; the codon-pair assay concluded not-codon-pair; the translation-accuracy assay concluded not-accuracy; and the elongation-dwell assay was only partial in Saccharomyces cerevisiae. These negative and partial classifications are useful boundaries, not replacement mechanisms.

\paragraph{Falsification and status.}
This record is falsified as a translation-readout claim if controlled $S_{f_3\text{-stress},T_j}$ remains non-positive or rank-degenerate across a powered joined panel. It remains supported only as a tRNA-supply boundary row so long as the passed finite experiment is read at its actual resolution: $40$ computed organisms from $46$ requested, $6$ skipped organisms, $1000$ permutations, $\alpha=0.05$, and a systematic tRNA-poverty call for $R$ sense codons under the named tRNA-copy and wobble-weight inputs. The mathematical content is thus a finite cross-layer constraint: the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ $f_3$-stress coordinate can become dynamically meaningful only after a context-dependent tRNA or wobble asymmetry breaks third-position symmetry, and any stronger biological interpretation must be supplied by a separate layer-matched contact.
