\subsection{A finite tRNA-poverty boundary for the $B^*_{Q6}$ $f_3$-stress coordinate}\label{subsec:bioreality-q6-f3-trna-poverty-boundary}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Claim surface.}
This record isolates a narrow cross-layer question: whether the $B^*_{Q6}$ $f_3$-stress residual coordinate has a finite supply-side contact in organisms where third-position symmetry is broken by tRNA availability or wobble weighting. The carrier is the finite CDS-by-organism residual coordinate surface restricted to the $B^*_{Q6}$ $f_3$-stress coordinate, together with matched rows for codon usage, tRNA copy number or wobble-weighted adaptation, and translation-layer readout routes. The claim is not that the coordinate already changes protein output or phenotype. It is the smaller assertion that the $R$ sense-codon boundary of the $f_3$ surface is systematically poor in available tRNA support under the assayed tGCN-plus-wobble weighting, and therefore supplies a finite mechanistic pressure for $R$ avoidance at the codon-supply layer.

\paragraph{Finite rows.}
The saturation row fixes the codon sets used by the boundary. The finite set $M=\operatorname{Med}(R)\cup\{\mathrm{ACA}\}$ contains exactly twenty codons:
$$
\begin{aligned}
M={}&\{\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{ACA},\mathrm{ACG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\\
&\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The corresponding $f_3$ saturation row removes the two $ACA/ACG$ entries and leaves eighteen codons:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\operatorname{Sat}_{f_3}(R)={}&\{\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\\
&\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The recorded $f_3$ boundary remainder is empty, so the finite topological boundary is closed at this row: $\partial_{f_3}R=\varnothing$. The powered supply-side assay then evaluates the $R$ sense codons against GtRNAdb tRNA gene-copy numbers and dos Reis wobble weights. In the recorded run $40$ organisms were computed out of $46$ requested organisms, with $6$ skipped organisms, significance level $\alpha=0.05$, and $1000$ permutations under the deterministic seed $\mathrm{sha256{:}b\_star\_q6\_boundary\_set\_trna\_poverty\_powered{:}R\mbox{-}sense\mbox{-}tRNA\mbox{-}poverty\mbox{-}permutation}$. The run status is passed, and its finite conclusion is that $R$ sense codons are systematically tRNA-poor under the declared tGCN-plus-wobble surface.

\paragraph{Local interpretation.}
The result is a supply-asymmetry statement, not a completed translation-survival statement. The coordinate becomes biologically interpretable only at rows where the third-position partner $c^{f_3}$ has unequal contextual weight, written locally as $w_c\ne w_{c^{f_3}}$. On such rows the relevant bounded entry is a controlled survival statistic $S_{f_3\text{-}\mathrm{stress},T_j}$ for a translation readout $T_j$, where $T_j$ may be a tAI row, an stAI row, or an observed ribosome-occupancy or translation-efficiency row. The present finite assay supplies the tRNA-poverty pressure, but it does not by itself supply an observed ribosome row or an mRNA-controlled translation-efficiency row. Thus the strict support condition for translation readback remains:
$$
\begin{aligned}
S_{f_3\text{-}\mathrm{stress},T_j}>0
\end{aligned}
$$
after controls for amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, CDS length, $M$-density, matched mRNA abundance when applicable, rank sufficiency, and simpler codon baselines. Without that positive controlled entry, the $f_3$ coordinate remains a codon-supply boundary rather than an observed translation effect.

The surrounding powered rows support this local reading. \paragraph{Boundary of non-promotion.}
The chapter therefore records a finite, positive tRNA-supply contact for the $R$ sense-codon boundary, but it does not promote that contact to downstream biology. No protein abundance, folded structure, physical admissibility, localization, stability, molecular function, system phenotype, global law, or broader translation mechanism follows from the codon-supply row alone. The only mechanism-level content retained here is the bounded tRNA-supply asymmetry measured by tGCN and wobble weights. Any stronger statement must enter through its own layer-matched contact: a same-organism and same-CDS translation readout for $S_{f_3\text{-}\mathrm{stress},T_j}$, a separate proteomics row for protein abundance, a separate structure or physical assay for admissibility, and separate functional or phenotype assays for higher-layer claims. In particular, a rank-degenerate all-$1.0$ surface on a three-organism panel would be a null boundary, not survival evidence. Directionality is also not inferred from codon topology or modeled adaptation weights alone; the $f_3$ coordinate is treated as dynamically eligible only where the finite tRNA or wobble row breaks the $f_3$ symmetry.
