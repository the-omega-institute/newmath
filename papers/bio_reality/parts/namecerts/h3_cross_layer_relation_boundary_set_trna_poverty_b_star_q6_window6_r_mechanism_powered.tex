\subsection{Boundary-set tRNA poverty for the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ $f_3$-stress coordinate}
\label{sec:bioreality-q6-boundary-set-trna-poverty}

\paragraph{Object of the record.}
This BioReality record concerns the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual coordinate obtained after amino-acid projection of coding-sequence codon counts.  The coordinate is read only as a finite synonymous-residual surface over organisms, with the $f_3$-paired imbalance retained as a candidate stress direction.  The finite row under review is not a protein, structure, localization, stability, function, phenotype, or universal-law row.  It is a codon--tRNA supply row: the test asks whether the $R$ sense codons in the $Q6$ window behave as a systematically tRNA-poor boundary when organism-level tRNA gene-copy counts and dos Reis wobble weights are used as the contact surface.

\paragraph{Finite codon surface.}
The saturated $f_3$ surface records the following finite carrier.  The set $M = \operatorname{Med}_R \cup \{\mathrm{ACA}\}$ is
$$
\begin{aligned}
M = \{&\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{ACA},\mathrm{ACG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\\
&\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
After the $f_3$ saturation step, the residual support removes the two boundary codons $\mathrm{ACA}$ and $\mathrm{ACG}$ and leaves
$$
\begin{aligned}
\operatorname{Sat}_{f_3}(R)=\{&\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\\
&\mathrm{CUU},\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The verified boundary list for $f_3$ is empty, so this row is not driven by an unaccounted finite-boundary remainder.  The biological question is therefore placed on the matched tRNA-contact side: whether the sense members of the $R$ boundary are poor in available tRNA support relative to the organism panel.

\paragraph{Powered tRNA-contact result.}
The powered finite-row run for this claim used $46$ requested organisms and obtained computable rows for $40$ organisms, with $6$ skipped rows.  The permutation test used $1000$ permutations at $\alpha=0.05$ and returned status passed.  Its finite conclusion is that the $R$ sense codons are systematically tRNA-poor under GtRNAdb tRNA gene-copy number together with dos Reis wobble weights.  In symbols, the accepted row is a supply-side inequality over the tested organism panel, not a downstream readout:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\operatorname{Supply}_{\mathrm{tRNA+wobble}}(R_{\mathrm{sense}})
&< \operatorname{Supply}_{\mathrm{tRNA+wobble}}(\text{matched synonymous background})
\end{aligned}
$$
with significance evaluated by the declared permutation scheme.  This is sufficient for a local tRNA-supply interpretation of $R$ avoidance in this finite panel: the boundary is not merely a codon-topology artifact, since its sense codons align with a depleted tRNA-contact surface.

\paragraph{Relation to the residual spectrum.}
The same finite memory places this row inside a larger $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual spectrum.  In the axis-strength survey, $16$ organisms were requested, $11$ were computed, and the held-out design used $5$ folds with $41$ synonymous-design columns and at least $500$ proteins per organism.  The recorded spectrum was led by $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$ on average, followed by $d_{\mathrm{tRNA}}$, with per-organism dominant counts $\{d_{\mathrm{resid4}}:11\}$.  The domain means were explicitly descriptive because the available panel was imbalanced, with $1$ archaeal, $2$ bacterial, and $8$ eukaryotic organisms.  The largest GC3 rank effect in that survey was the $d_{\mathrm{usage}}$ effect with $\rho=0.136$.  These numbers constrain the interpretation: tRNA poverty is a real contact for the boundary-set row, but it is not the only residual axis visible in the powered spectrum.

\paragraph{Residual-axis compatibility.}
Several neighboring finite rows are compatible with the same cautious reading.  The non-tRNA residual optimality row found that the dominant non-tRNA residual direction aligns most with $f_3$ translation selection.  The two-axis closure row concluded that a third axis is present, because the perpendicular direction had systematically positive held-out $R^2$ and mutually same-signed organism directions.  The third-axis identity row provisionally identified $d_{\perp}$ with usage frequency within family, with mean cosine $0.128$ and one-sided sign-test $p=0.000488$.  The third-axis mechanism row reported selection-not-GC and not-wobble-clustered behavior, while leaving the CSC or positional identity as requiring an additional contact.  Thus the tRNA-poverty boundary should be read as one supply-side mechanism row inside a richer residual geometry, not as a complete explanation of the $Q6$ surface.

\paragraph{No promotion past the tested layer.}
This chapter records a positive tRNA-supply result and a bounded interpretation of $R$ avoidance.  It does not record a measured translation-efficiency survival statistic.  A translation-readout claim would require same-organism and same-CDS codon rows, a direct ribosome-profiling or translation-efficiency row, matched mRNA abundance, and controls for amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, coding-sequence length, $M$-density, rank, and simpler baselines.  In the present finite memory, the exact $f_3$-stress translation-readout packet remains blocked because no joined same-row $S^{QT}_{f_3\text{-stress},T_j}$ statistic is present.  Consequently, no translation realization, protein abundance, folded structure, physical admissibility, biological function, phenotype, broader mechanism, or universal biological law follows from this row unless a separate layer-matched contact supplies that evidence and its controlled statistic.

\paragraph{Local verdict.}
The finite claim is accepted at the codon--tRNA contact layer: in a powered $40$-organism computation with $1000$ permutations and $\alpha=0.05$, the $R$ sense boundary is systematically tRNA-poor under GtRNAdb gene-copy counts and dos Reis wobble weights.  The accepted meaning is narrow.  The row supports a tRNA-supply account of $R$ avoidance on the tested boundary set, while preserving the null boundary for translation survival and every higher biological layer.
