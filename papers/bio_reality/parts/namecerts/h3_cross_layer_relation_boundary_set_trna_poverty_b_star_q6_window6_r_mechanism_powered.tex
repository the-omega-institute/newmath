\subsection{Boundary tRNA poverty for the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ $f_3$-stress coordinate}
\label{sec:bioreality-q6-f3-stress-trna-poverty}

\paragraph{Finite carrier.}
The object considered here is a finite coding-sequence residual surface restricted to the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ coordinate that detects third-position $f_3$ stress after amino-acid projection.  The row carrier is not a protein-function table and not a phenotype table.  It consists of codon-residual rows, organism-scoped codon usage, tRNA-gene-copy or wobble-weight rows, and the declared translation-readout routes.  The finite codon boundary used by the row is explicit.  The $M$ carrier obtained from $\mathrm{Med}_R \cup \mathrm{ACA}$ is
$$
\begin{aligned}
M = \{&\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{ACA},\mathrm{ACG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\\
&\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The third-position saturation removes exactly $\mathrm{ACA}$ and $\mathrm{ACG}$ from that carrier, giving
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{Sat}_{f_3}(R)=\{&\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\\
&\mathrm{CUU},\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The recorded $f_3$ boundary set is empty, so the boundary question is not whether an additional codon escapes the finite carrier.  It is whether the residual coordinate becomes biologically visible only when the organism-level tRNA or wobble surface breaks the $f_3$ pairing symmetry.

\paragraph{Observed finite-row result.}
The powered boundary-set assay was run as \texttt{b\_star\_q6\_boundary\_set\_trna\_poverty\_powered}.  It requested $46$ organisms, computed $40$ organisms, and skipped $6$ organisms.  The test used $1000$ permutations at $\alpha=0.05$ with seed \texttt{sha256:b\_star\_q6\_boundary\_set\_trna\_poverty\_powered:R-sense-tRNA-poverty-permutation}.  Its status is \texttt{passed}.  The finite biological call is that the $R$ sense codons are systematically tRNA-poor under GtRNAdb tGCN together with the dos Reis wobble weights.  In this chapter the word \emph{mechanism} is therefore confined to that supplied asymmetry: the row supports a tRNA-supply account of $R$-codon avoidance, not a direct claim about translation efficiency, protein output, folding, localization, stability, molecular function, phenotype, or a universal law.

\paragraph{Residual-coordinate reading.}
The $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ $f_3$-stress coordinate is treated as a controlled synonymous residual after amino-acid quotienting.  Its intended readout is a survival entry against a translation-layer row, for example a tAI, stAI, ribosome-occupancy, or translation-efficiency row.  The finite result above is weaker and more specific: it says that the $R$ sense boundary has tRNA-supply poverty under the stated tGCN and wobble-weight contact.  This is compatible with the residual-axis memory in which the non-tRNA residual optimality axis is reported as $f_3$ translation selection, and with the two-axis result in which a third axis remains after the tRNA and $f_3$ span.  The associated finite scalars are deliberately modest: $11$ of $16$ requested organisms were computed in the residual-axis assays, the synonymous design had $41$ columns, and the third-axis identity was only provisionally aligned to usage frequency within family, with mean cosine $0.128$ and one-sided sign-test $p=0.000488$.

\paragraph{Spectrum position.}
The axis-strength spectrum places this boundary inside a larger residual geometry rather than as an isolated codon-count artifact.  In the powered spectrum run, $11$ organisms were computed from $16$ requested, with $5$ folds, at least $500$ proteins per organism, and $41$ synonymous design columns.  The captured spectra were led on average by $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$, followed by $d_{\mathrm{tRNA}}$; the per-organism dominant-count summary was $\{d_{\mathrm{resid4}}:11\}$.  Domain means were explicitly descriptive under the imbalanced domain counts $\{\mathrm{archaea}:1,\mathrm{bacteria}:2,\mathrm{eukaryote}:8\}$, and the largest reported GC3 rank effect was only $d_{\mathrm{usage}}$ with $\rho=0.136$.  Thus the tRNA-poverty row is not promoted into a universal residual law.  It is a finite organism-panel contact showing that the $R$ boundary is tRNA-poor under a particular tGCN-plus-wobble measurement scheme.

\paragraph{Support statement.}
Let $Q_{f_3}$ denote the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ third-position stress coordinate after amino-acid projection, and let $W_o(c)$ be the organism-scoped tRNA or wobble weight attached to codon $c$ in organism $o$.  The finite row supports the following bounded implication:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\bigl(W_o(c) \neq W_o(c^{f_3})\bigr) \;\wedge\; c \in R
\quad \Longrightarrow \quad
\text{the } R \text{ boundary is testable as a tRNA-supply asymmetry row.}
\end{aligned}
$$
The implication is intentionally not a claim that $Q_{f_3}$ has survived against a measured translation-efficiency response.  A positive translation-readout claim would require a non-vacuous controlled survival entry $S_{f_3\text{-stress},T_j}>0$ after amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, coding-sequence length, $M$-density, matched mRNA, rank, and simpler-baseline controls.  The present row supplies the finite tRNA-poverty side of that test, not the completed same-CDS translation-readout statistic.

\paragraph{Local exclusion boundary.}
No translation realization follows from the codon geometry alone.  No ribosome occupancy, protein abundance, folded structure, physical admissibility, localization, stability, biological function, phenotype, additional mechanism, or universality follows from this row unless a separate layer-matched contact supplies that measurement and the corresponding controlled statistic is positive.  Modelled tAI or stAI weights remain bounded adaptation readbacks, not observed ribosome-footprint rows.  Matched mRNA abundance, when present, is a control surface for translation-efficiency residualization, not itself a translation output.  The only positive content recorded here is the finite, powered tRNA-poverty call for $R$ sense codons under the stated GtRNAdb tGCN and dos Reis wobble-weight construction.
