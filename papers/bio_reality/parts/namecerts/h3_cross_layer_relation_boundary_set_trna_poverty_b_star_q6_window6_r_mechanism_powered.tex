\paragraph{Powered tRNA-poverty call.}
The powered boundary-set assay was run as $\texttt{b\_star\_q6\_boundary\_set\_trna\_poverty\_powered}$ with seed $\texttt{sha256:b\_star\_q6\_boundary\_set\_trna\_poverty\_powered:R-sense-tRNA-poverty-permutation}$. The finite result is positive for the narrow tRNA-supply statement: $R$ sense codons are systematically tRNA-poor under GtRNAdb tRNA gene-copy numbers combined with dos Reis wobble weights. This supports a bounded supply-side interpretation of $R$ avoidance in the assayed panel. The assertion is intentionally local: it is a relation between the residual codon boundary and the tRNA/wobble supply row, not a claim that a protein, phenotype, localization state, folding state, or universal biological principle has been observed.

\paragraph{Relation to the larger $Q6$ residual spectrum.}
The same finite memory places this boundary call inside a nontrivial $Q6$ residual landscape. In the powered axis-strength survey, $11$ organisms were computed out of $16$ requested, with $5$ folds, at least $500$ proteins per organism, and $41$ synonymous design columns. These numbers make the present boundary useful but not self-sufficient: the $R$-poverty result is one mechanism-facing slice through a broader residual spectrum.

\paragraph{Controlled interpretation of the mechanism row.}
A separate powered test identifies the non-tRNA residual optimality direction most strongly with $f_3$ translation selection, again on $11$ computed organisms out of $16$, with $5$ folds, at least $500$ proteins per organism, and $41$ synonymous design columns. The two-axis closure assay did not close the space: it returned a third-axis-present conclusion because $d_{\perp}$ had systematically positive held-out $R^2$ and mutually same-signed directions across organisms. The third-axis identity assay provisionally aligned $d_{\perp}$ with usage frequency within family, with mean cosine $0.128$ and one-sided sign-test $p=0.000488$. The follow-up mechanism test kept GC-orthogonalized $d_{\perp}$ significant and classified the result as selection-not-GC, not-wobble-clustered, with CSC or positional identity still needing a further contact. The three-axis sufficiency test also returned fourth-mechanism-present. Consequently, the $R$ tRNA-poverty assay should be read as a confirmed boundary-set supply signal, not as closure of the full residual mechanism basis.

\paragraph{Translation-readout gate.}
For the $f_3$-stress coordinate to become a translation-layer survival statement, the row must include a nondegenerate controlled entry $S_{f3\text{-}stress,T_j}$ for a translation readout such as tAI, stAI, ribosome occupancy, or translation efficiency. The admissible survival comparison must control for amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, CDS length, M-density, and matched mRNA abundance when translation efficiency is computed from ribosome footprints. $$
\begin{aligned}

Z&=\{\mathrm{amino\ acid\ composition},\mathrm{organism},\mathrm{GC3},\mathrm{length},\mathrm{M\ density},\mathrm{mRNA\ control}\}.

The current powered $R$-poverty result supplies the tRNA/wobble asymmetry side of this gate. It does not by itself supply measured ribosome rows, same-CDS translation-efficiency rows, protein-abundance mediation, structural readouts, physical admissibility assays, functional assays, localization assays, stability assays, phenotype assays, or universality evidence.

\paragraph{Local no-promotion rule.}
The chapter therefore records one positive finite statement and one strict boundary. The positive statement is that, in a powered multi-organism assay, the $R$ sense codon boundary is tRNA-poor under GtRNAdb copy-number and dos Reis wobble weighting. The boundary is that no translation, folding, physical admissibility, function, downstream mechanism, phenotype, or universal law follows from this coordinate unless a layer-matched contact supplies the corresponding rows and the declared controlled statistic is non-vacuous. In particular, a three-organism all-$1.0$ rank-degenerate surface is a null boundary, not survival evidence; modeled tAI or stAI coupling is not observed ribosome occupancy; and a supply-side $R$-poverty call is not a protein-level or organism-level conclusion.
