\subsection{The $B^*_{Q6}$ $f_3$ boundary as a tRNA-poverty surface}\label{subsec:bq6-f3-boundary-trna-poverty}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite coordinate under test.}
The present record isolates one finite synonymous-coordinate surface: coding-sequence residuals restricted to the $B^*_{Q6}$ $f_3$-stress coordinate, after amino-acid projection and after matching the coordinate against organism-scoped tRNA or wobble-weight rows.  The saturated $f_3$ computation gives the ambient finite support explicitly.  The set $M=\mathrm{Med}(R)\cup \mathrm{ACA}$ is
$\{\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{ACA},\mathrm{ACG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\}$.
The $f_3$-saturated $R$ surface removes exactly $\mathrm{ACA}$ and $\mathrm{ACG}$ from that ambient support, leaving
$\{\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\}$.
The recorded $f_3$ boundary is empty, so the finite-row issue is not whether an extra boundary codon remains after saturation.  It is whether the surviving $R$ sense-codon region is poor in tRNA supply under an organism-level wobble-weight readout.

\paragraph{Powered tRNA-poverty readout.}
The boundary-set test was run as experiment $\mathrm{b\_star\_q6\_boundary\_set\_trna\_poverty\_powered}$ with $46$ organisms requested, $40$ organisms computed, and $6$ organisms skipped.  The test used $1000$ permutations at threshold $\alpha=0.05$, with deterministic seed $\mathrm{sha256{:}b\_star\_q6\_boundary\_set\_trna\_poverty\_powered{:}R\mbox{-}sense\mbox{-}tRNA\mbox{-}poverty\mbox{-}permutation}$.  Its finite conclusion is positive: the $R$ sense codons are systematically tRNA-poor under GtRNAdb tRNA gene-copy numbers combined with dos Reis wobble weights.  In compact form the finite row is
$$
\begin{aligned}
N_{\mathrm{requested}}&=46,\\
N_{\mathrm{computed}}&=40,\\
N_{\mathrm{skipped}}&=6,\\
B&=1000,\\
\alpha&=0.05,\\
\mathrm{status}&=\mathrm{passed}.
\end{aligned}
$$
This supports a bounded supply-side interpretation of $R$ avoidance: the codon subset singled out by the $B^*_{Q6}$ $f_3$ coordinate is not merely a syntactic residue of the quotient, but aligns with a finite organism-panel asymmetry in available tRNA or wobble-weight support.

\paragraph{Relation to the broader residual spectrum.}
The same finite memory keeps this boundary result separate from stronger translation claims.  In the powered per-organism axis spectrum, $11$ organisms were computed out of $16$ requested, using $5$ folds, at least $500$ proteins per organism, and $41$ synonymous design columns.  The descriptive spectrum was led on average by $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$, followed by $d_{\mathrm{tRNA}}$; the dominant-count summary recorded $\{d_{\mathrm{resid4}}:11\}$, while the largest GC3 rank effect was only $\rho=0.136$ for $d_{\mathrm{usage}}$.  A separate powered residual-axis call, also on $11$ computed organisms out of $16$ requested with the same $5$-fold and $41$-column design, identified the dominant non-tRNA residual optimality axis as $f_3$ translation selection.  The third-axis identity test assigned $d_\perp$ provisionally to within-family usage frequency, with mean cosine $0.128$ and one-sided sign-test $p=0.000488$.  The third-axis mechanism row then kept the signal GC-orthogonal and selection-facing, while refusing a simple wobble-cluster identity.

\paragraph{Local interpretation.}
The chapter-level claim is therefore narrow.  The $B^*_{Q6}$ $f_3$ boundary has a powered tRNA-poverty result on the $R$ sense-codon subset, and this result is compatible with the wider residual spectrum in which tRNA supply, $f_3$ translation selection, and a residual usage-frequency axis are distinct finite directions.  The supported readback is the supply asymmetry itself: $R$ sense codons are depleted in available tRNA or wobble-weight support across the computed organism panel.  The result does not by itself assert that the coordinate changes ribosome occupancy, translation efficiency, protein abundance, folding, stability, localization, function, phenotype, or a universal biological law.

\paragraph{No-promotion boundary.}
A positive tRNA-poverty row is not a positive $S^{QT}$ translation-survival row.  A translation-readout claim would require same-organism, and where applicable same-CDS, codon-count rows joined to tAI or stAI surfaces, ribosome-profiling or translation-efficiency measurements, matched mRNA abundance, and controls for amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, coding-sequence length, $M$-density, rank, and simpler codon baselines.  In symbols, the missing promotion step is the controlled survival entry
$$
\begin{aligned}
S_{f_3\text{-}\mathrm{stress},T_j}>0
\end{aligned}
$$
for a declared translation readout $T_j$.  The present finite row establishes the tRNA-poverty side of the boundary; it does not supply that survival statistic.  Consequently, no downstream statement about translation dynamics, folded structure, physical admissibility, biological function, mechanism beyond the bounded tRNA-supply asymmetry, or universality follows here unless a separate layer-matched contact supplies the corresponding rows and statistic.
