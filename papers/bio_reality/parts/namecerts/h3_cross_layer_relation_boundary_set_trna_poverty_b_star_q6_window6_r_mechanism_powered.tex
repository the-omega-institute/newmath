\subsection{The $B^{*}_{Q6}$ Boundary Set as a tRNA-Poverty Contact}
\label{sec:bioreality-b-star-q6-boundary-set-trna-poverty}

\paragraph{Finite carrier.}
This record concerns a finite coding-sequence residual surface restricted to the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ coordinate family, with the focal coordinate given by the $f_3$-stress direction after amino-acid projection. The finite codon support used by the saturation contact is explicit. The median-$R$ union with $ACA$ contains
$AAA,AAG,ACA,ACG,AGA,AGG,AUA,AUG,CUA,CUC,CUG,CUU,UAA,UAG,UCA,UCG,UGA,UGG,UUA,UUG$.
After $f_3$ saturation by $R$, the listed support is
$AAA,AAG,AGA,AGG,AUA,AUG,CUA,CUC,CUG,CUU,UAA,UAG,UCA,UCG,UGA,UGG,UUA,UUG$,
so the only two listed median-union codons absent from the saturated support are $ACA$ and $ACG$. The recorded $f_3$ boundary set is empty. Thus the local geometry is not a claim about an unbounded codon universe; it is a finite residual coordinate surface with a named codon list and a stated zero boundary remainder.

\paragraph{Measured finite row.}
The powered tRNA-poverty row tests whether the $R$ sense codons on this boundary-facing surface are depleted in tRNA supply when GtRNAdb tRNA gene-copy numbers are combined with dos Reis wobble weights. The experiment reports status $passed$ at $\alpha=0.05$, with $1000$ permutations, $46$ organisms requested, $40$ organisms computed, and $6$ organisms skipped. In finite-row form the computable panel is therefore
$$
\begin{aligned}
N_{requested} &= 46,\\
N_{computed} &= 40,\\
N_{skipped} &= 6,\\
N_{perm} &= 1000,\\
\alpha &= 0.05.
\end{aligned}
$$
The row-level call is that $R$ sense codons are systematically tRNA-poor under the stated gene-copy and wobble-weight readout. This is a bounded tRNA-supply pressure for $R$ avoidance on the tested surface, not a free-standing claim about translation output or higher biological layers.

\paragraph{Relation to the $f_3$-stress coordinate.}
The intended biological contrast is third-position $f_3$ imbalance after amino-acid quotienting. Such a contrast is silent unless the readout context breaks the paired third-position symmetry. In this chapter the symmetry-breaking contact is the tRNA or wobble-weight asymmetry $w_c \neq w_{c^{f_3}}$, evaluated by the finite tRNA-copy and wobble-weight panel above. The result therefore supports the narrow statement that the $R$-sense portion of the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual surface lies in a tRNA-poor regime under the declared external readout. It does not say that every $f_3$ contrast is dynamic, and it does not turn codon topology alone into a translation-efficiency observation.

\paragraph{Survival gate.}
A downstream translation claim would require a separate row of the form $S_{f3\text{-}stress,T_j}$ for a direct translation readout $T_j$, such as tAI, stAI, ribosome occupancy, or translation efficiency, under the declared controls. The local support condition can be written as
$$
\begin{aligned}
S_{f3\text{-}stress,T_j} &> 0
\end{aligned}
$$
only after amino-acid composition, organism identity, $GC3$, CDS length, $M$-density, matched mRNA abundance, rank sufficiency, and simpler-baseline ablation have been applied. The present tRNA-poverty row is not itself that survival row. It supplies a finite mechanism-facing contact for tRNA supply, while the translation-readout survival entry remains a separate requirement.

\paragraph{Cross-row consistency.}
Other powered rows are consistent with keeping this statement narrow. The per-organism axis-strength spectrum computed $11$ of $16$ requested organisms, used $5$ folds and $41$ synonymous design columns, and found $d_{resid4}$ leading the captured spectra on average, with per-organism dominant counts $\{d_{resid4}:11\}$ and largest $GC3$ rank effect $d_{usage}$ with $\rho=0.136$. The universal residual-axis row also computed $11$ of $16$ organisms with $5$ folds and $41$ synonymous design columns, and identified $f_3$ translation selection as the dominant non-tRNA residual optimality axis. A third-axis identity row reported mean cosine $0.128$ with one-sided sign-test $p=0.000488$ for alignment to within-family usage frequency, while the corresponding mechanism row characterized the signal as selection-not-$GC$, not wobble-clustered, with positional or CSC identity still needing external resolution. These rows reinforce the interpretation that the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ surface carries structured residual signal, but they do not promote the tRNA-poverty result into protein output, structure, function, or a universal biological law.

\paragraph{Local boundary.}
The chapter therefore records a finite, powered, organism-panel contact: under GtRNAdb tGCN plus dos Reis wobble weighting, the $R$ sense codons associated with the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ boundary-facing surface are tRNA-poor in the computed panel. The permitted conclusion is a bounded tRNA-supply pressure for $R$ avoidance on this coordinate. No translation-efficiency survival, ribosome-occupancy realization, protein abundance, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, phenotype, independent downstream mechanism, or universality follows from this row unless another layer-matched contact supplies the corresponding measured rows and a controlled statistic for that layer.
