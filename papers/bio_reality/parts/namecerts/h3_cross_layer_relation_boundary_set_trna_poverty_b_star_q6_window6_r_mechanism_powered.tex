\subsection{Boundary Set tRNA Poverty for the $B^*_{Q6}$ $f_3$-Stress Coordinate}
\label{sec:bioreality-boundary-set-trna-poverty-b-star-q6-window6-r}

\paragraph{Finite coordinate surface.}
This record concerns the finite CDS-by-organism residual coordinate surface restricted to the $B^*_{Q6}$ $f_3$-stress coordinate and to translation-layer rows whose contact is codon use, tRNA copy number, wobble-weighted adaptation, ribosome occupancy, or translation efficiency. The coordinate is not a raw codon count. It is the residual distinction left after amino-acid projection, so the relevant contrast is a synonymous third-position imbalance rather than an amino-acid-composition signal. The finite saturation row fixes the local codon set. The union $M=\operatorname{Med}_R\cup \{\mathrm{ACA}\}$ is
$$
\begin{aligned}
M=\{&\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{ACA},\mathrm{ACG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\\
&\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The $f_3$-saturated $R$ surface removes $\mathrm{ACA}$ and $\mathrm{ACG}$, giving
$$
\begin{aligned}
\operatorname{Sat}_{f_3}(R)=\{&\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\\
&\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The recorded $f_3$ boundary is empty: $\partial_{f_3}R=\varnothing$. Thus the boundary claim is not that an unsaturated codon fringe remains. It is that, within the saturated finite surface, the $R$ sense codons carry a tRNA-supply deficit when read against organism-level tRNA gene copy and wobble-weighted decoding weights.

\paragraph{Powered finite row.}
The powered contact was evaluated in experiment $\mathrm{b\_star\_q6\_boundary\_set\_trna\_poverty\_powered}$ with run id $\mathrm{d7728cc8768e}$. The test requested $46$ organisms, computed $40$, and skipped $6$. The null was assessed with $1000$ permutations at $\alpha=0.05$ under the deterministic seed $\mathrm{sha256:b\_star\_q6\_boundary\_set\_trna\_poverty\_powered:R\mbox{-}sense\mbox{-}tRNA\mbox{-}poverty\mbox{-}permutation}$. The finite verdict was passed: $R$ sense codons are systematically poor in tRNA supply under GtRNAdb tGCN together with dos Reis wobble weights. In local notation, if $w_c$ denotes the context decoding weight of codon $c$ and $c^{f_3}$ denotes its third-position paired codon, the decisive break condition is not merely $c\neq c^{f_3}$ but the asymmetric readout
$$
\begin{aligned}
w_c \neq w_{c^{f_3}}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The experiment therefore supports a bounded tRNA-supply explanation for avoidance of the $R$ sense boundary set within this finite codon/tRNA contact. It does not, by itself, assert measured ribosome speed, protein abundance, folded structure, cellular function, phenotype, or a universal biological rule.

\paragraph{Relation to the translation-readout gate.}
The $B^*_{Q6}$ coordinate becomes a translation-layer candidate only where the synonymous $f_3$ pairing is broken by tRNA or wobble weights. The admissible readout is a controlled survival entry $S_{f3\mbox{-}stress,T_j}$, where $T_j$ is a bounded translation row such as tAI, stAI, ribosome occupancy, or translation efficiency. The local support condition is
$$
\begin{aligned}
S_{f3\mbox{-}stress,T_j} > 0
\end{aligned}
$$
after amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, CDS length, $M$-density, matched mRNA, rank, and simpler-baseline controls. The current powered tRNA-poverty row supplies the finite break in decoding symmetry, not the full measured survival matrix. A three-organism all-$1.0$ controlled surface would be treated as rank saturation rather than survival, because a rank-degenerate panel cannot distinguish a genuine translation signal from a saturated finite fit.

\paragraph{Coordinate context from linked powered rows.}
The surrounding powered rows give the boundary claim its scale without promoting it beyond its contact. In the axis-strength spectrum, $11$ organisms were computed out of $16$ requested with $5$ folds, at least $500$ proteins per organism, and $41$ synonymous design columns; the captured spectra were led on average by $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$, and all $11$ per-organism dominant counts were assigned to $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$. The same spectrum reported the largest GC3 rank effect for $d_{\mathrm{usage}}$ with $\rho=0.136$, so GC3 is a necessary control rather than a substitute explanation. The third-axis identity row, also computed on $11$ of $16$ requested organisms with $5$ folds and $41$ synonymous design columns, provisionally aligned $d_\perp$ to within-family usage frequency with mean cosine $0.128$ and one-sided sign-test $p=0.000488$. Its paired mechanism row concluded that the effect is selection-not-GC and not wobble-clustered, while leaving CSC or positional identity as needing a separate contact. The universal residual-axis row identified the non-tRNA residual optimum with $f_3$ translation selection, and the three-axis sufficiency row reported a fourth mechanism present. These facts make the $R$ tRNA-poverty result a finite, powered boundary within a larger residual-coordinate spectrum, not a standalone law of translation.

\paragraph{No-promotion boundary.}
The chapter's positive content is narrow. It says that the $B^*_{Q6}$ $f_3$-stress coordinate has a powered tRNA-supply break on $R$ sense codons across a $40$-organism computed panel under GtRNAdb tGCN and dos Reis wobble weights. It may be used to motivate a controlled translation-readout survival test. It may not be used as direct evidence for translation realization, ribosome occupancy, protein abundance, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, organismal phenotype, causal mechanism beyond the tRNA-supply contact, or universality. Any one of those higher claims requires its own matched finite contact and its own controlled statistic. In particular, codon topology and modeled tAI or stAI coupling do not determine directionality unless the relevant context weights actually break the $f_3$ symmetry and the corresponding $S_{f3\mbox{-}stress,T_j}$ row is non-vacuous under controls.
