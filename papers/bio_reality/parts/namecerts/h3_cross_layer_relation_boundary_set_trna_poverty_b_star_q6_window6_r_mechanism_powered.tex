\subsection{The $f_3$ boundary set and the tRNA-poverty readout}
\label{sec:bior-reality-q6-f3-boundary-trna-poverty}

\paragraph{Finite coordinate surface.}
This record concerns the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual coordinate restricted to the third-position $f_3$ stress axis after amino-acid projection.  The finite codon surface is not the whole genetic code; it is the closed row set obtained from the $R$ boundary together with the mediated ACA contribution.  In the recorded saturation computation, the carrier row set is
$\{\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{ACA},\mathrm{ACG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\}$.
The $f_3$ saturation row deletes the ACA--ACG pair and leaves
$\{\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\}$.
The recorded $f_3$ boundary residue is empty.  Thus the biological question is not whether an additional boundary codon remains after this closure.  The question is whether the finite residual coordinate becomes visible in a matched translation-layer readout only when the organism-specific tRNA or wobble weights break the $f_3$ pairing symmetry.

\paragraph{Powered tRNA-poverty row.}
The dedicated boundary-set experiment is the strongest finite row attached to this packet.  It computed $40$ organisms out of $46$ requested organisms, skipped $6$, and used $1000$ permutations at $\alpha=0.05$.  The finite conclusion was that $R$ sense codons are systematically tRNA-poor under GtRNAdb tRNA gene-copy numbers combined with dos Reis wobble weights.  This supports a bounded tRNA-supply explanation for avoidance of the $R$ sense boundary within the assayed finite panel.  The support is deliberately local: it attaches an organism-level codon and tRNA-weight asymmetry to the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ boundary set.  It does not by itself measure ribosome occupancy, translation efficiency, protein output, protein folding, physical admissibility, biological function, phenotype, or a universal law.

\paragraph{Cross-layer interpretation.}
The conjectural readout is the survival entry $S_{f3\text{-}stress,T_j}$ for a translation observable $T_j$, where $T_j$ may be a bounded tAI or stAI weight, or a measured ribosome-occupancy or translation-efficiency row when such rows are organism-matched and controlled.  The intended controlled statistic is a positive residual survival after amino-acid composition, organism identity, GC3, CDS length, $M$-density, and matched mRNA controls.  In schematic form, the admissible positive case is
$$
\begin{aligned}
S_{f3\text{-}stress,T_j} &> 0,\\
T_j &\in \{\mathrm{tAI},\mathrm{stAI},\mathrm{TE},\mathrm{RPF/mRNA}\},\\
Z &= \{\mathrm{amino\ acid\ composition},\mathrm{organism},\mathrm{GC3},\mathrm{length},M\text{-}\mathrm{density},\mathrm{mRNA}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The present powered tRNA-poverty row supplies the break condition expected to make this statistic non-vacuous, but it is not the statistic itself.  A three-organism all-$1.0$ controlled surface is therefore a rank-degenerate null boundary rather than evidence of survival.

\paragraph{Relation to the broader $Q6$ spectrum.}
The surrounding finite rows make the boundary-set call biologically sharper but also constrain its scope.  In the axis-strength spectrum, $11$ organisms were computed out of $16$ requested, with $5$ folds, at least $500$ proteins per organism, and $41$ synonymous design columns; the captured spectra were led by $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$ on average, followed by $d_{\mathrm{tRNA}}$, and all $11$ per-organism dominant calls were $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$.  The largest reported GC3 rank effect was $\rho=0.136$ for $d_{\mathrm{usage}}$, so GC3 is present as a covariate but is not allowed to replace the tRNA-poverty test.  The non-tRNA residual optimality row identified $f_3$ translation selection as the dominant residual axis, and the two-axis closure row reported a third-axis-present result with systematically positive held-out $R^2$ and same-signed $d_{\perp}$ directions.  The $d_{\perp}$ identity row aligned that axis to usage frequency within family, with mean cosine $0.128$ and one-sided sign-test $p=0.000488$.  A separate mechanism row kept the $d_{\perp}$ signal after GC orthogonalization and described it as selection-not-GC, not wobble-clustered, with CSC or positional identity still needing an outside contact.

\paragraph{No-promotion rule.}
The finite row permits the following bounded statement: in the assayed organism panel, the $R$ sense boundary is tRNA-poor under the stated tRNA-copy and wobble-weight model, and this makes the $f_3$ stress coordinate a plausible candidate for a controlled translation-readout survival test.  Nothing stronger follows locally.  The chapter does not infer measured translation realization from codon topology, modeled tAI or stAI weights, or tRNA-copy asymmetry alone.  It also does not infer folded structure, physical admissibility, molecular function, localization, stability, phenotype, a ribosome-level mechanism, or universality.  Any such layer requires its own matched contact and its own controlled statistic.  In particular, mRNA abundance is only a control for translation-efficiency residualization, and ribosome profiling becomes relevant only after same-organism and same-CDS rows are joined to codon counts and the declared covariates.

\paragraph{Falsification boundary.}
The packet is falsified as a translation-readout claim if powered organism-matched tests keep $S_{f3\text{-}stress,T_j}\leq 0$ for every declared translation row after the controls above, or if the apparent signal collapses to a rank-degenerate surface.  It is not falsified merely because protein abundance, structure, function, or phenotype is absent from this row; those are different layers.  Conversely, the tRNA-poverty result is not promoted by downstream wording.  Its present evidential content is exactly a finite, controlled tRNA-supply asymmetry for the $R$ sense boundary, positioned as a prerequisite for, not a substitute for, a measured or explicitly bounded translation-survival row.
