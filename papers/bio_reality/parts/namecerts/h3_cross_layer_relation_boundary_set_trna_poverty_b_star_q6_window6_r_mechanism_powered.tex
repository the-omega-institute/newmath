\subsection{Boundary-set tRNA poverty for the $B^*_{Q6}$ $f_3$-stress coordinate}\label{sec:boreality-q6-boundary-set-trna-poverty}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite carrier.}
The object of this record is a finite coding-sequence residual surface, restricted to the $B^*_{Q6}$ coordinate that tests third-position $f_3$ stress after amino-acid projection. The row is not a free biological assertion. It is the finite comparison between a codon-side residual coordinate and organism-scoped tRNA or wobble-weight summaries, with any later translation readout required to appear as its own matched row. The saturation computation fixes the relevant finite support. The marked set $M = \mathrm{Med}(R) \cup \mathrm{ACA}$ is
$$\begin{aligned}
M = \{&\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{ACA},\mathrm{ACG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\\
&\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\}.
\end{aligned}$$
The $f_3$-saturated $R$ surface removes the two codons $\mathrm{ACA}$ and $\mathrm{ACG}$ from this list and leaves
$$\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{Sat}_{f_3}(R) = \{&\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\\
&\mathrm{CUU},\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\}.
\end{aligned}$$
The recorded $f_3$ boundary is empty, $\partial_{f_3}R = \varnothing$. Thus the chapter concerns a closed finite codon boundary after the amino-acid quotient, not an unbounded genomic claim.

\paragraph{Boundary relation.}
The powered boundary-set test asks whether the $R$ sense codons in this finite surface are poor in tRNA support when read through GtRNAdb tRNA gene-copy numbers and dos Reis wobble weights. The completed run used $\alpha = 0.05$ and $1000$ permutations. It requested $46$ organisms, computed $40$, and skipped $6$. Its recorded call is that $R$ sense codons are systematically tRNA-poor under the combined tGCN and wobble-weight readout. In the present chapter this is a bounded supply-side relation: codon residual rows and organism-level tRNA or wobble weights align in the expected direction for $R$ avoidance. It is not, by itself, a measured ribosome-occupancy statement and it is not a protein-output statement.

\paragraph{Coordinate reading.}
The $f_3$-stress distinction is visible only after synonymous codons have been compared inside amino-acid classes. The relevant asymmetry is the failure of the context weight to be invariant under third-position pairing, written locally as $w_c \ne w_{c^{f_3}}$. If that asymmetry is absent, the coordinate has no dynamical translation content. If it is present, the codon-side residual can be compared against tAI, stAI, or a later measured translation-efficiency row, but only as a controlled survival entry. In symbolic form, the admissible survival statistic has the shape
$$\begin{aligned}
S_{f_3,T_j} > 0
\end{aligned}$$
after adjustment for amino-acid composition, organism identity, GC3, coding-sequence length, $M$-density, and matched mRNA controls when translation efficiency is used. This chapter has a positive supply-side tRNA-poverty call, but it does not contain a same-CDS ribosome-profiling matrix establishing such an $S_{f_3,T_j}$ entry.

\paragraph{Rank and spectrum context.}
The boundary result is consistent with the larger $B^*_{Q6}$ spectrum but does not collapse into it. In a related powered axis-spectrum run, $11$ organisms were computed out of $16$ requested, using $5$ folds, a minimum of $500$ proteins per organism, and $41$ synonymous-design columns. The descriptive dominant-axis count was $\{\mathrm{d\_resid4}:11\}$, and the largest GC3 rank effect reported there was $\rho = 0.136$ for $\mathrm{d\_usage}$. A separate third-axis identity run on the same $11$ of $16$ organism panel reported alignment of $d_\perp$ with within-family usage frequency, with mean cosine $0.128$ and one-sided sign-test $p = 0.000488$. The mechanism-side check then kept the cross-species alignment after GC orthogonalization and classified the result as selection-not-GC, not-wobble-clustered, with the CSC or positional identity still requiring an outside row. These numbers locate the boundary-set result inside a finite residual spectrum: the tRNA-poverty result is one supply-side contact, while residual, usage, and orthogonal axes remain distinct finite coordinates.

\paragraph{Supported statement.}
The supported local claim is the following. On the finite $B^*_{Q6}$ $f_3$-stress surface, $R$ sense codons are depleted in tRNA supply under GtRNAdb tGCN plus dos Reis wobble weights across a powered organism panel, with $40$ computed organisms, $6$ skipped organisms, $1000$ permutations, and threshold $\alpha = 0.05$. This supports a bounded tRNA-supply explanation for $R$ avoidance in the codon--tRNA contact layer. It also motivates the next translation-row test: whether the same coordinate survives against tAI, stAI, or measured ribosome-occupancy or translation-efficiency readouts under the declared controls.

\paragraph{Local refusal boundary.}
No stronger biological conclusion is licensed by these rows alone. The contact does not show measured translation efficiency, ribosome occupancy, protein abundance, folded structure, physical admissibility, molecular function, localization, stability, phenotype, or a universal biological rule. A mechanism claim beyond codon--tRNA supply also requires its own contact. The three-organism all-$1.0$ survival surface mentioned in the surrounding memory is treated here as rank-degenerate and therefore null for survival. The only permitted interpretation is a finite supply-side asymmetry: after amino-acid quotienting, the $f_3$ residual coordinate meets a tRNA-poverty boundary for $R$ sense codons, and any downstream translation or protein-level interpretation remains blocked until a separate matched readout supplies a non-vacuous controlled survival statistic.
