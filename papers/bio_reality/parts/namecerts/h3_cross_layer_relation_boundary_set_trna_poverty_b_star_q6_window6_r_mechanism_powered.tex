\subsection{Boundary-set tRNA poverty for the $B^*_{Q6}$ window-six residual}
\label{sec:bioreality-boundary-set-trna-poverty-b-star-q6-window6-r-mechanism-powered}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite coordinate surface.}
This record concerns the finite CDS-by-organism residual coordinate surface obtained after amino-acid projection, restricted to the $B^*_{Q6}$ third-position stress coordinate and to matched translation-readout rows.  The coordinate is not a protein-level or phenotype-level object.  Its carrier is a finite table whose rows join coding-sequence residual coordinates, organism labels, codon-usage summaries, tRNA or wobble weights, and candidate translation readouts.  The particular boundary tested here is the $R$ sense-codon side of the window-six construction: the question is whether the $R$ codons singled out by the coordinate have systematically low tRNA supply under organism-level tRNA gene-copy number and wobble-weight readbacks.

\paragraph{The $f_3$ saturation boundary.}
The finite third-position closure used by the coordinate separates the full marked set
$$
\begin{aligned}
M &= \{\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{ACA},\mathrm{ACG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\\
&\qquad \mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\}
\end{aligned}
$$
from its $f_3$-saturated $R$ part
$$
\begin{aligned}
\operatorname{Sat}_{f_3}(R) &= \{\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\\
&\qquad \mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The two codons present in $M$ but absent from this saturated $R$ list are $\mathrm{ACA}$ and $\mathrm{ACG}$.  The verified finite boundary has empty residual boundary set, recorded as $f_3$-boundary $= \varnothing$.  Thus the biological test is not whether an additional codon lies outside the constructed closure; it is whether the already isolated $R$ sense-codon side is poor in the tRNA-supply readback that would make the coordinate visible to translation-layer assays.

\paragraph{Powered tRNA-poverty test.}
The powered organism panel requested $46$ organisms, computed $40$, and skipped $6$.  The permutation test used $1000$ permutations at $\alpha=0.05$ with deterministic seed \texttt{sha256:b\_star\_q6\_boundary\_set\_trna\_poverty\_powered:R-sense-tRNA-poverty-permutation}.  The finite-row call passed: $R$ sense codons are systematically tRNA-poor when evaluated with GtRNAdb tRNA gene-copy number together with dos Reis wobble weights.  In this bounded sense, the $R$-avoidance side of the $B^*_{Q6}$ coordinate is supply-shaped: the coordinate aligns with a finite organism-level deficit in available cognate or wobble-accessible tRNA support.

\paragraph{Relation to the residual spectrum.}
The surrounding powered residual-basis runs keep this chapter at the coordinate-to-translation boundary rather than above it.  In the per-organism axis-strength spectrum, $11$ organisms were computed out of $16$ requested with $5$ folds and at least $500$ proteins per organism; all $11$ per-organism dominant counts were assigned to $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$, with $41$ synonymous design columns.  The domain counts were imbalanced, with $1$ archaeal, $2$ bacterial, and $8$ eukaryotic organisms, so domain means are descriptive rather than a balanced phylogenetic law.  The largest GC3 rank effect in that spectrum was $\rho=0.136$ for $d_{\mathrm{usage}}$.

The complementary powered residual-axis test also computed $11$ of $16$ requested organisms, again with $5$ folds, at least $500$ proteins per organism, and $41$ synonymous design columns; it identified the dominant non-tRNA residual optimality axis as $f_3$ translation selection.  The third-axis identity run reported alignment of $d_\perp$ to within-family usage frequency, with mean cosine $0.128$ and one-sided sign-test $p=0.000488$.  Its mechanism-side companion kept the axis GC-orthogonal and concluded selection rather than GC, while rejecting a simple wobble-clustered explanation and leaving CSC or positional identity as requiring further data.  These facts make the tRNA-poverty result coherent with the residual spectrum, but they do not replace the direct survival row required for translation-readout claims.

\paragraph{Readout rule.}
For any candidate translation readout $T_j$, the admissible score is a controlled survival entry $S_{f_3\text{-stress},T_j}$ after amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, CDS length, $M$-density baseline, matched mRNA abundance where available, and simpler codon-baseline controls.  A positive table of codon usage, tRNA-copy asymmetry, or modeled tAI/stAI weights is not itself a measured ribosome-occupancy or translation-efficiency row.  The test becomes positive only when a same-organism and, where needed, same-CDS joined panel gives a non-vacuous survival statistic:
$$
\begin{aligned}
S_{f_3\text{-stress},T_j} > 0
\end{aligned}
$$
after the declared controls and baseline ablation.  If every controlled entry is non-positive, or if the matrix is rank-degenerate, the coordinate remains a bounded supply-side and residual-spectrum observation rather than a translation-survival result.

\paragraph{Local boundary on interpretation.}
The verified result supports a finite tRNA-supply explanation for $R$-sense avoidance within the codon-usage, tRNA-copy, and wobble-weight layer.  It does not by itself show ribosome dwell time, translation efficiency, mature protein abundance, folded structure, physical admissibility, localization, molecular function, phenotype, causation, or a universal biological law.  No such higher-layer statement follows from the $B^*_{Q6}$ residual geometry, the $f_3$ saturation table, the tRNA-poverty permutation call, or modeled adaptation weights unless a separate layer-matched contact supplies the relevant rows and the corresponding controlled statistic is computed.  In particular, the tRNA-poverty call may motivate the next translation-readout assay, but it is not a substitute for the mandatory $S_{f_3\text{-stress},T_j}$ row.
