\paragraph{Finite coordinate surface.}
This record isolates one finite cross-layer boundary: the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual coordinate restricted to the $f_3$-stress direction and tested against organism-level codon usage, tRNA gene-copy annotations, and wobble-weighted adaptation rows.  The amino-acid projection is part of the statement: the relevant distinction is not raw codon frequency, but a synonymous residual contrast that remains after the amino-acid class has been quotiented out.  The finite $f_3$ saturation computation gives a concrete boundary set.  The parent mark set is

\paragraph{Powered supply-side readout.}
The powered boundary test was run as experiment $\mathrm{b\_star\_q6\_boundary\_set\_trna\_poverty\_powered}$ with run id $\mathrm{d7728cc8768e}$.  It requested $46$ organisms, computed $40$, skipped $6$, used $1000$ permutations, and applied $\alpha=0.05$.  The finite call passed: $R$ sense codons are systematically tRNA-poor under GtRNAdb tGCN combined with dos Reis wobble weights.  In local terms, the $R$ boundary is not merely a codon-topology artifact; on the available finite panel, it aligns with a supply-side asymmetry in annotated tRNA availability and wobble-weighted decoding capacity.  The appropriate numerical summary is therefore
$$
\begin{aligned}
N_{\mathrm{requested}} &= 46,\\
N_{\mathrm{computed}} &= 40,\\
N_{\mathrm{skipped}} &= 6,\\
B_{\mathrm{perm}} &= 1000,\\
\alpha &= 0.05.
\end{aligned}
$$
This supports a bounded tRNA-supply explanation for $R$ avoidance at the codon-readout layer.  It does not, by itself, establish ribosome occupancy, translation efficiency, protein abundance, folding, physical admissibility, biological function, a full cellular mechanism, or a universal law.

\paragraph{Bounded interpretation.}
The positive $R$-poverty call must be read as a finite annotation-level asymmetry.  It may be combined with codon-usage tables, tRNA-copy summaries, and tAI or stAI weights as a controlled translation-readout boundary, but it is not already a measured $S_{f3\text{-}stress,T_j}$ survival entry.  A downstream translation claim requires a non-vacuous, positive row under amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, CDS length, $M$-density, matched mRNA abundance, rank, and simpler-baseline controls.  The rank-degenerate case is explicitly excluded: an all-$1.0$ three-organism surface is a null boundary rather than survival evidence.  No translation realization, folded structure, physical admissibility, biological function, full mechanism, phenotype, localization, stability, or universality follows from this chapter unless a separate layer-matched contact supplies the relevant finite rows and statistic.

\paragraph{Negative controls from fourth-axis probes.}
Several powered probes narrow the interpretation.  The codon-pair row returned $\mathrm{not\_codon\_pair}$ with $5$ folds, $41$ synonymous design columns, and $50$ shuffle-null samples.  The translation-accuracy row returned $\mathrm{not\_accuracy}$ over $11$ computed organisms out of $16$ requested, with $200$ permutations and $41$ synonymous design columns.  The mRNA-stability row returned $\mathrm{fourth\_axis\_not\_mrna\_stability}$ because the yeast $d_{\mathrm{opt\_halflife}}$ direction had low alignment with abundance $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$ under the fixed gates.  The elongation-dwell row was only partial and was restricted to $\mathrm{saccharomyces\_cerevisiae}$.  These controls prevent promotion of the $R$-poverty result into codon-pair preference, translation-accuracy identity, mRNA half-life identity, or general elongation-dwell law.

\paragraph{Chapter verdict.}
The finite-row verdict is ready at bounded scope.  The chapter records that the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ $f_3$-stress boundary, after amino-acid projection, has a powered $R$-sense tRNA-poverty signature on a $40$-organism computed panel with $1000$ permutations at $\alpha=0.05$.  The strongest scientifically admissible statement is that annotated tRNA supply and wobble-weighted decoding asymmetry provide a finite support surface for $R$ avoidance in the residual codon coordinate.  The statement stops there until direct translation-readout rows, and any higher-layer contacts, are joined to the same finite coordinate surface.
