\subsection{Boundary-set tRNA poverty at the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ $f_{3}$ stress coordinate}
\label{sec:bioreality-boundary-set-trna-poverty-b-star-q6-window6-r-mechanism-powered}

\paragraph{Finite row.}
The finite object considered here is the coding-sequence residual coordinate surface restricted to the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ $f_{3}$-stress coordinate, together with organism-indexed codon usage, tRNA gene-copy, wobble-weight, and translation-readout rows when such rows are present.  The verified boundary row is the $R$-sense tRNA-poverty assay.  In that assay, $46$ organisms were requested, $40$ organisms were computed, and $6$ organisms were skipped.  The permutation count was $1000$, with test level $\alpha = 0.05$.  The recorded outcome is passed: $R$ sense codons are systematically tRNA-poor under GtRNAdb tGCN combined with dos Reis wobble weights.  Thus the local biological interpretation is a supply-side explanation for avoidance of the $R$ boundary set, limited to the codon, tRNA-copy, and wobble-weight rows that enter the computation.

\paragraph{Coordinate boundary.}
The $f_{3}$ saturation row fixes the finite codon support on which the boundary is read.  The union $M = \mathrm{Med}_{R} \cup \{\mathrm{ACA}\}$ is
$$
\begin{aligned}
M = \{&\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{ACA},\mathrm{ACG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\\
&\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
After $f_{3}$ saturation by $R$, the support is the same list with $\mathrm{ACA}$ and $\mathrm{ACG}$ removed:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{Sat}_{f_{3}}(R) = \{&\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\\
&\mathrm{CUU},\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The recorded $f_{3}$ boundary residual is empty, so there is no additional codon outside this finite support that must be promoted into the boundary.  The point of the row is therefore not that an unbounded codon class is being discovered.  It is that the already finite $f_{3}$-paired surface contains a supply asymmetry once the tRNA-copy and wobble-weight rows are joined.

\paragraph{Asymmetry test.}
For a codon $c$ in the finite support, let $c^{f_{3}}$ denote its third-position $f_{3}$ partner where that partner is defined inside the same restricted surface.  The relevant readout is not raw codon identity, but whether the context weight breaks the symmetry
$$
\begin{aligned}
w_{c} \neq w_{c^{f_{3}}}.
\end{aligned}
$$
A positive boundary call requires this broken-pair condition to survive after amino-acid projection; otherwise the coordinate is only a synonymous relabelling artifact.  The verified permutation row supplies exactly this kind of bounded evidence: the $R$ sense codons are systematically poor in the joined tGCN and wobble-weight surface across the $40$ computed organisms.  The assay is therefore a finite cross-layer relation between a residual codon coordinate and a tRNA-supply row, not a direct observation of ribosome motion or protein output.

\paragraph{Relation to the powered $Q6$ panel.}
The same finite panel contains several nearby powered rows that constrain over-reading of the boundary result.  The axis-strength spectrum was computed for $11$ of $16$ requested organisms, with $5$ folds, at least $500$ proteins per organism, and $41$ synonymous design columns; the captured spectra were led by $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$ in all $11$ per-organism dominant calls, while the largest GC3 rank effect was $\rho = 0.136$ for $d_{\mathrm{usage}}$.  The non-tRNA residual optimality row identifies $f_{3}$ translation selection as the dominant residual axis, again on $11$ computed organisms out of $16$ requested.  The two-axis closure row still reports a third axis, and the third-axis identity row aligns $d_{\perp}$ to usage frequency within family with mean cosine $0.128$ and one-sided sign-test $p = 0.000488$.  The third-axis mechanism row records selection-not-GC and not-wobble-clustered, with CSC or positional identity still requiring an additional source of evidence.

\paragraph{Scope of the mechanism wording.}
The word mechanism is used here only in the narrow sense that a tRNA-supply row explains the $R$-boundary avoidance pattern better than a symmetry-preserving null in the available finite table.  It does not by itself assert a measured translation-efficiency effect.  It also does not assert protein abundance, folding, localization, stability, molecular function, phenotype, physical admissibility, or a universal biological law.  Those claims would require layer-matched contacts and explicit statistics at the corresponding layer.  In particular, the f3-stress translation-readout conjecture remains blocked until a same-organism and same-CDS matrix joins codon counts, wobble-breaking tAI or stAI weights, measured ribosome-profiling or translation-efficiency rows, and matched mRNA controls, and then computes a non-vacuous controlled $S_{f3\text{-}stress,T_j}$ statistic.

\paragraph{Readout boundary.}
The allowed readback is therefore the following bounded statement.  In a powered multi-organism panel, the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ $f_{3}$-stress boundary has a positive finite-row tRNA-supply contact: the $R$ sense codons are systematically tRNA-poor under GtRNAdb tGCN plus dos Reis wobble weights, with $40$ computed organisms and $1000$ permutations at $\alpha = 0.05$.  This supports a local supply-side account of $R$ avoidance on the residual codon surface.  It does not promote the coordinate to a downstream translation, protein, structure, function, phenotype, physical-admissibility, or universality claim.  The nearby powered rows sharpen the boundary rather than weakening it: fourth-axis sufficiency reports an additional mechanism beyond the three-axis span, mRNA stability is excluded as the dominant fourth-axis identity in the yeast half-life assay, codon-pair and translation-accuracy explanations are rejected in their respective powered rows, and elongation dwell receives only a partial yeast-specific call.  The chapter-level conclusion is therefore a ready but sharply delimited one: the $R$ boundary is tRNA-poor in the finite weighted supply surface, while every stronger biological interpretation waits for its own matched contact and controlled survival statistic.
