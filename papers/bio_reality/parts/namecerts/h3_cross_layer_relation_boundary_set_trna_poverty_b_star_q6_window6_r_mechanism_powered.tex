\subsection{A powered tRNA-poverty boundary for the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ $f_3$ stress coordinate}
\label{sec:bioreality-q6-f3-trna-poverty-boundary}

\paragraph{Finite coordinate under test.}
This BioReality record concerns the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual coordinate after amino-acid projection, restricted to the $f_3$-stress direction and to translation-layer readouts that can be matched to the same coding rows.  The finite codon surface is not the full codon cube.  The verified $f_3$ saturation computation reports
$$
\begin{aligned}
M &= \{AAA,AAG,ACA,ACG,AGA,AGG,AUA,AUG,CUA,CUC,CUG,CUU,\\
&\qquad UAA,UAG,UCA,UCG,UGA,UGG,UUA,UUG\},\\
\operatorname{Sat}_{f_3}(R) &= \{AAA,AAG,AGA,AGG,AUA,AUG,CUA,CUC,CUG,CUU,\\
&\qquad UAA,UAG,UCA,UCG,UGA,UGG,UUA,UUG\},\\
\partial_{f_3}R &= \varnothing.
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the two codons $ACA$ and $ACG$ lie in $M$ but not in the reported $f_3$-saturated $R$ surface, while the $f_3$ boundary is empty in the finite computation.  The biological question is therefore not whether an extra boundary codon remains after closure; it is whether the surviving residual direction has any measured translation-layer consequence when the third-position symmetry is broken by organism-specific tRNA or wobble weights.

\paragraph{Powered tRNA-poverty result.}
The finite-row mechanism assay for the boundary set was run on a requested panel of $46$ organisms.  It computed $40$ organisms and skipped $6$, using $1000$ permutations at $\alpha=0.05$.  The recorded call is that $R$ sense codons are systematically tRNA-poor under GtRNAdb tRNA gene-copy numbers together with dos Reis wobble weights.  In this chapter, that statement is read narrowly: it supports a tRNA-supply interpretation for avoidance of the $R$ sense-codon surface in the assayed organism panel.  It does not, by itself, establish that the $f_3$-stress coordinate changes ribosome occupancy, translation efficiency, protein abundance, folding, localization, stability, function, phenotype, or any universal biological law.

\paragraph{Residual axis context.}
The surrounding powered assays place the tRNA-poverty result inside a larger $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual geometry.  The axis-strength spectrum was computed for $11$ of $16$ requested organisms, with $5$ folds, at least $500$ proteins per organism, and $41$ synonymous-design columns.  In that panel the captured spectra are led on average by $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$ and then by $d_{\mathrm{tRNA}}$; all $11$ per-organism dominant counts are assigned to $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$.  Domain summaries are descriptive only, since the reported domain counts are imbalanced: $1$ archaeon, $2$ bacteria, and $8$ eukaryotes.  The largest GC3 rank effect in that assay is reported for $d_{\mathrm{usage}}$ with $\rho=0.136$.

\paragraph{Direction of the candidate mechanism.}
The same finite-row memory says that the non-tRNA residual optimality direction aligns most with $f_3$ translation selection.  A two-axis closure test computed $11$ of $16$ requested organisms and concluded that a third axis is present, because $d_{\perp}$ has systematically positive held-out $R^2$ and its directions are mutually same-signed across organisms.  The third-axis identity test, again on $11$ of $16$ requested organisms with $5$ folds, provisionally identifies $d_{\perp}$ with usage frequency within synonymous family: mean cosine $0.128$ and one-sided sign-test $p=0.000488$.  Its mechanism assay reports a selection-not-GC conclusion; after GC orthogonalization, $d_{\perp}$ keeps significant cross-species alignment and held-out $R^2$, while the same record says it is not wobble-clustered and that a CSC or positional identity still needs a separate contact.

\paragraph{Translation-readout gate.}
The support condition for a true translation-layer survival statement is stricter than the tRNA-poverty result.  Let $Q_i$ denote the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ $f_3$-stress residual coordinate and let $T_j$ denote a bounded translation readout such as tAI, stAI, measured ribosome occupancy, or translation efficiency.  A positive survival call requires a nondegenerate statistic of the form
$$
\begin{aligned}
S_{f_3\text{-stress},T_j} > 0
\end{aligned}
$$
after amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, coding-sequence length, $M$-density, matched mRNA abundance where applicable, rank, and simpler-codon-baseline controls.  The present finite rows give a powered tRNA-poverty mechanism call for the $R$ sense-codon surface, and they give modeled or bounded translation-adaptation routes.  They do not yet contain same-organism and same-CDS joined rows for codon counts, wobble-breaking tAI or stAI weights, measured ribosome-profiling or translation-efficiency readouts, and matched mRNA controls for this exact $f_3$-stress subject.  Consequently the chapter records a mechanism-supported boundary, not a measured translation-survival theorem.

\paragraph{Exclusions from adjacent mechanism searches.}
The fourth-axis mechanism records sharpen the local interpretation.  The mRNA-stability assay concludes that the fourth axis is not mRNA stability in its yeast half-life test, because the yeast $d_{\mathrm{opt\_halflife}}$ has low alignment with abundance $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$ under the fixed gates.  The codon-pair assay concludes \emph{not codon pair}.  The translation-accuracy assay concludes \emph{not accuracy} after $200$ permutations over $11$ computed organisms out of $16$ requested organisms.  The elongation-dwell assay in \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae} is marked partial.  These labels do not promote the $f_3$-stress coordinate to a downstream biological function; they only prevent the tRNA-poverty signal from being blurred with several nearby explanatory surfaces.

\paragraph{Local cannot-claim boundary.}
This record permits the following bounded statement: in a powered organism panel, the $R$ sense-codon surface associated with the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual geometry is tRNA-poor under GtRNAdb gene-copy counts and dos Reis wobble weights, and that finite result is compatible with a tRNA-supply explanation for $R$ avoidance.  It does not permit a claim that the $f_3$-stress coordinate has measured translation survival until a nonvacuous $S_{f_3\text{-stress},T_j}$ row is computed under the stated controls.  It also does not permit any inference about protein abundance, folded structure, physical admissibility, localization, stability, molecular function, phenotype, causation, or universality without a separate layer-matched contact and its own controlled statistic.  In particular, an all-$1.0$ rank-degenerate three-organism surface is a null boundary for this purpose, and modeled tAI or stAI coupling is not the same object as observed ribosome occupancy or translation efficiency.
