\subsection{A finite tRNA-poverty boundary for the $B^*_{Q6}$ $f_3$ window}
\label{sec:bioreality-q6-f3-trna-poverty-boundary}

\paragraph{Finite carrier.}
The finite object in this record is a CDS-by-organism residual coordinate surface restricted to the $B^*_{Q6}$ third-position stress coordinate and to rows that can be compared with codon-usage, tRNA-copy, wobble-weight, or translation-readout summaries. The coordinate is not an amino-acid signal: it is formed only after synonymous codons have been compared within amino-acid fibres. Thus the relevant distinction is an $f_3$-paired imbalance that remains after quotienting away amino-acid identity. The carrier is finite at two visible levels. First, the $f_3$ saturation computation gives
$$
\begin{aligned}
M&=\{AAA,AAG,ACA,ACG,AGA,AGG,AUA,AUG,CUA,CUC,CUG,CUU,\\
&\qquad UAA,UAG,UCA,UCG,UGA,UGG,UUA,UUG\},\\
\operatorname{Sat}_{f_3}(R)&=\{AAA,AAG,AGA,AGG,AUA,AUG,CUA,CUC,CUG,CUU,\\
&\qquad UAA,UAG,UCA,UCG,UGA,UGG,UUA,UUG\},\\
\partial_{f_3}R&=\varnothing.
\end{aligned}
$$
The saturation row therefore contains $20$ codons in $M$, $18$ codons in $\operatorname{Sat}_{f_3}(R)$, and an empty $f_3$ boundary. The two codons removed from the saturated $R$ row are $ACA$ and $ACG$. This is the coordinate-level reason that the test is forced to look at matched supply and readout rows rather than at a loose codon-topology statement.

\paragraph{Powered tRNA-poverty row.}
The finite powered row for this chapter is the experiment $b\_star\_q6\_boundary\_set\_trna\_poverty\_powered$ with run id $d7728cc8768e$. It requested $46$ organisms, computed $40$ organisms, and skipped $6$ organisms. The test used $1000$ permutations at $\alpha=0.05$. Its recorded status is passed. The recorded finite call is that $R$ sense codons are systematically tRNA-poor under GtRNAdb tGCN together with dos Reis wobble weights. In symbols, the local reading is that for the relevant $R$ sense codon subset and its matched comparison set, the weighted tRNA-supply statistic is separated in the poverty direction across the computed organism panel:
$$
\begin{aligned}
N_{\mathrm{requested}}&=46,\\
N_{\mathrm{computed}}&=40,\\
N_{\mathrm{skipped}}&=6,\\
B&=1000,\\
\alpha&=0.05.
\end{aligned}
$$
This is a finite supply-side result. It says that the $R$ sense portion of the $B^*_{Q6}$ window is not merely a formal codon subset; it is aligned with a tRNA-copy and wobble-weight scarcity signal in a powered multi-organism panel. The result is still bounded by its row type: it is a codon-to-tRNA-supply comparison, not an observation of ribosome motion, protein abundance, folding, localization, function, phenotype, or a universal law.

\paragraph{Why the $f_3$ break matters.}
The $f_3$ coordinate becomes biologically testable only where third-position symmetry is broken by contextual tRNA or wobble weights. If a codon $c$ and its $f_3$ mate $c^{f_3}$ have identical readout weights, then a residual contrast between them can remain algebraically visible while being dynamically silent at the readout layer. The required asymmetry has the form
$$
\begin{aligned}
w_c \neq w_{c^{f_3}}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The present chapter records a positive finite row for the supply asymmetry side of that condition: the $R$ sense codons in the $B^*_{Q6}$ window are tRNA-poor under the chosen tGCN and wobble-weight surface. This is exactly the kind of break that makes a third-position residual coordinate eligible for later translation-readout testing. It does not, by itself, prove that any translation-efficiency row survives the full control set.

\paragraph{Relation to the surrounding finite spectrum.}
Several adjacent finite rows help locate the result without promoting it beyond its evidence layer. In the axis-strength spectrum row, $11$ organisms were computed out of $16$ requested with $5$ folds and $41$ synonymous design columns; the descriptive spectrum was led by $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$ on average, followed by $d_{\mathrm{tRNA}}$, and all $11$ per-organism dominant counts went to $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$. The same row reports the largest GC3 rank effect as $d_{\mathrm{usage}}$ with $\rho=0.136$. In a separate third-axis identity row, $d_\perp$ was provisionally aligned to within-family usage frequency with mean cosine $0.128$ and one-sided sign-test $p=0.000488$, again with $11$ organisms computed out of $16$ requested, $5$ folds, at least $500$ proteins per organism, and $41$ synonymous design columns. Another finite row identifies the non-tRNA residual optimality axis most strongly with $f_3$ translation selection, again on $11$ computed organisms out of $16$ requested with the same $5$-fold and $41$-column design. These rows are consistent with the poverty result: the $B^*_{Q6}$ coordinate is not exhausted by GC3, not reduced to a raw amino-acid composition effect, and not captured by a single organism anecdote. They do not replace the mandatory translation-readout row.

\paragraph{Local test statement.}
The chapter-specific claim is therefore the following finite assertion. Let $Q$ be the $B^*_{Q6}$ $f_3$-stress residual coordinate after amino-acid projection. Let $W$ be the organism-scoped tRNA-copy and wobble-weight surface. Let $T$ range over bounded translation readouts such as tAI, stAI, ribosome occupancy, or translation efficiency. The powered row supports the supply-side inequality for the $R$ sense component of $Q$ on the computed panel:
$$
\begin{aligned}
Q_{R,f_3}\ \hbox{is separated in the tRNA-poor direction under}\ W.
\end{aligned}
$$
A later translation-readout survival statement would require a separate same-organism, and where needed same-CDS, row giving a controlled survival entry
$$
\begin{aligned}
S_{f_3\text{-stress},T_j}>0
\end{aligned}
$$
after amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, CDS length, $M$-density, matched mRNA abundance, rank, and simpler-baseline controls. The present finite row is upstream of that survival statistic. It justifies carrying the $f_3$-stress coordinate into the readout test; it does not assert that the readout test has already succeeded.

\paragraph{Null boundary.}
The finite result also specifies how the claim can fail at the next layer. If a powered joined panel gives non-positive or rank-degenerate $S_{f_3\text{-stress},T_j}$ entries across tAI, stAI, ribosome-occupancy, or translation-efficiency readouts after the declared controls, then the supply-side poverty row remains only a codon-to-tRNA-weight fact. In particular, an all-$1.0$ rank-degenerate surface on a small organism set is not survival evidence. Likewise, a modelled tAI or stAI surface is not an observed ribosome-occupancy measurement unless a measured contact supplies the corresponding row.

\paragraph{Cannot-claim boundary.}
This chapter permits one bounded statement: the $R$ sense part of the $B^*_{Q6}$ $f_3$ window is systematically tRNA-poor in the powered tGCN-plus-wobble assay with $40$ computed organisms and $1000$ permutations at $\alpha=0.05$. No translation realization, protein abundance, folded structure, localization, physical admissibility, molecular function, phenotype, causal mechanism, or universal biological rule follows from this row alone. Each such statement requires its own layer-matched contact and its own finite statistic. The present result is therefore a ranked and powered supply-boundary record, not a downstream biological realization claim.
