\subsection{Boundary-set tRNA poverty for the $B^*_{Q6}$ $f_3$-stress coordinate}
\label{sec:bioreality-q6-boundary-set-trna-poverty}

\paragraph{Finite coordinate surface.}
The finite object considered here is the coding-sequence residual surface obtained after quotienting by amino-acid identity and then restricting attention to the $B^*_{Q6}$ coordinate that tests third-position $f_3$ stress. The relevant finite saturation computation separates the mediated set
$M=\{\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{ACA},\mathrm{ACG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\}$
from the third-position saturated set
$\operatorname{Sat}_{f_3}(R)=\{\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\}$.
Thus the only displayed difference between the mediated row and the saturated row is the two-codon pair $\{\mathrm{ACA},\mathrm{ACG}\}$, while the recorded $f_3$ boundary is empty. This makes the chapter a finite-row boundary test rather than a claim about all codons or all organisms.

\paragraph{Powered tRNA-poverty row.}
The powered assay attached to this coordinate is the run $\mathrm{d7728cc8768e}$ for the claim $\mathrm{h3.cross\_layer\_relation.boundary\_set\_trna\_poverty.b\_star\_q6\_window6\_R\_mechanism\_powered}$. It requested $46$ organisms, computed $40$, skipped $6$, used $1000$ permutations, and applied $\alpha=0.05$. The finite conclusion was that $R$ sense codons are systematically tRNA-poor when read through GtRNAdb tRNA gene-copy numbers together with dos Reis wobble weights. In the notation of a row-wise test, the result is a supply-asymmetry statement:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta_R &= \operatorname{weight}_{\mathrm{tRNA/wobble}}(R)-\operatorname{weight}_{\mathrm{tRNA/wobble}}(R^{\mathrm{comp}}),\\
\mathrm{run}(\Delta_R) &:\quad 40/46\ \text{organisms computed},\quad 1000\ \text{permutations},\quad \alpha=0.05.
\end{aligned}
$$
The supported interpretation is therefore narrow: the $R$ boundary row is compatible with a tRNA-supply explanation for avoidance of those sense codons under the specified tGCN-plus-wobble contact. It is not, by itself, a measured ribosome-occupancy or translation-efficiency survival result.

\paragraph{Relation to the surrounding spectrum.}
The same finite program places the boundary result inside a broader $B^*_{Q6}$ residual spectrum. In the axis-strength spectrum run, $16$ organisms were requested, $11$ were computed, the design used $41$ synonymous columns and $5$ folds, and every computed organism had $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$ as the dominant axis. The descriptive domain counts were imbalanced, with $1$ archaeal, $2$ bacterial, and $8$ eukaryotic rows, so no domain-universal conclusion is drawn from those means. The largest recorded GC3 rank effect was small, $\rho=0.136$ for $d_{\mathrm{usage}}$. The universal-core tRNA-adaptation row also computed $11/16$ organisms with $41$ synonymous columns and $5$ folds, while the non-tRNA residual optimality row identified $f_3$ translation selection as the dominant residual axis. The two-axis closure row reported a third axis rather than closure by the tRNA and $f_3$ span alone. The third-axis identity row aligned $d_{\perp}$ with usage frequency within family, with mean cosine $0.128$ and one-sided sign-test $p=0.000488$. A separate third-axis mechanism row classified the residual as selection-not-GC, not wobble-clustered, with CSC or positional identity still needing an additional contact.

\paragraph{Finite readout discipline.}
The boundary-set row is positive for tRNA-poverty of $R$ sense codons, but the translation-readout conjecture tied to $f_3$ stress remains conditional. A translation row would require organism- or CDS-matched codon counts, tRNA or wobble weights, ribosome profiling or translation-efficiency measurements, and matched mRNA abundance controls, followed by a controlled survival statistic for $S_{f3\text{-}\mathrm{stress},T_j}$. Until that same-row statistic is present and nondegenerate, the only claim licensed here is the bounded supply-asymmetry relation for the finite $R$ boundary set. In particular, a three-organism all-$1.0$ rank-saturated matrix is a null boundary, not survival evidence.

\paragraph{Non-promotion rule.}
No conclusion about translation realization, ribosome dynamics, protein abundance, folded structure, localization, physical admissibility, biological function, phenotype, or universality follows from the codon geometry or from the tRNA-poverty row alone. A downstream statement at any of those layers requires its own matched biological contact and its own controlled statistic. The present chapter only records that, on the powered finite panel, $R$ sense codons lie on a tRNA-poor boundary under the declared tGCN and wobble-weight measurement scheme, while the $f_3$-stress translation-readout survival row remains an open finite test.
