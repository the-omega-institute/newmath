\subsection{A tRNA-poverty boundary for the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ $f_3$ residual coordinate}
\label{sec:bioreality-q6-f3-trna-poverty-boundary}

\paragraph{Finite coordinate being tested.}
This record concerns the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual coordinate obtained after amino-acid projection, with attention restricted to the $f_3$-paired synonymous imbalance. The finite carrier is a CDS-by-organism residual surface: rows are coding sequences inside organisms, and the active coordinate is the $f_3$-stress axis together with translation-layer readouts such as tAI, stAI, ribosome occupancy, or translation efficiency when those readouts are actually present. The finite codon support is not empty or symbolic. The verified saturation row reports
$$
\begin{aligned}
M = \{&\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{ACA},\mathrm{ACG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\\
&\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\},
\end{aligned}
$$
while the $f_3$-saturated $R$ set after removing the two boundary codons $\mathrm{ACA}$ and $\mathrm{ACG}$ is
$$
\begin{aligned}
\operatorname{Sat}_{f_3}(R)=\{&\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\\
&\mathrm{CUU},\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The same finite-row audit reports an empty $f_3$ boundary set outside this saturation, so the relevant question is not whether the codon-side coordinate exists. It is whether the coordinate survives after biological controls when translation readout rows are attached.

\paragraph{Powered tRNA-poverty result.}
The powered mechanism run for the boundary-set claim was computed on $40$ organisms out of $46$ requested organisms, with $6$ skipped organisms, significance level $\alpha=0.05$, and $1000$ permutations. Its recorded status is passed. The finite mechanism call is that $R$ sense codons are systematically tRNA-poor under GtRNAdb tRNA gene-copy number together with dos Reis wobble weights. In this chapter that statement is read narrowly: the $R$ side of the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ coordinate is supported as a tRNA-supply-poor boundary under the declared copy-number and wobble-weight surface. It is not, by itself, a measured ribosome-footprint statement, a protein-abundance statement, or a phenotype statement.

\paragraph{Why the $f_3$ symmetry break matters.}
The coordinate becomes biologically interrogable only when the third-position $f_3$ pairing is not invisible to the translation contact. Let $c$ be a codon and $c^{f_3}$ its third-position $f_3$ partner. A translation-weight row can distinguish the pair only if the context weight differs:
$$
\begin{aligned}
w_c \ne w_{c^{f_3}}.
\end{aligned}
$$
If $w_c=w_{c^{f_3}}$ throughout an organism or context, then the residual coordinate may still be present in codon geometry, but the translation readout has no local handle on that distinction. The passed tRNA-poverty run supplies the finite reason to keep the mechanism in play: the $R$ sense codons are not merely a formal codon subset, but a subset with a systematic scarcity signal under the declared tRNA and wobble-weight construction.

\paragraph{Relation to the surrounding finite spectrum.}
The same finite audit family reports that the axis-strength spectrum was computed for $11$ organisms out of $16$ requested organisms, with $5$ folds, at least $500$ proteins per organism, and $41$ synonymous design columns. In that spectrum, $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$ leads on average and is dominant in all $11$ computed organisms, while $d_{\mathrm{tRNA}}$ follows; domain means are only descriptive because the domain counts are imbalanced, with $1$ archaeal, $2$ bacterial, and $8$ eukaryotic organisms. The largest GC3 rank effect reported there is $\rho=0.136$ for $d_{\mathrm{usage}}$. Separately, the residual optimality run identifies the dominant non-tRNA residual axis as $f_3$ translation selection across $11$ computed organisms out of $16$ requested organisms, again with $5$ folds, at least $500$ proteins per organism, and $41$ synonymous design columns. The third-axis identity run identifies $d_{\perp}$ provisionally with usage frequency within family, with mean cosine $0.128$ and one-sided sign-test $p=0.000488$; the associated mechanism run reports selection-not-GC, not-wobble-clustered, with CSC or positional identity still requiring outside data.

\paragraph{Controlled survival statement.}
The finite translation-readout claim is therefore conditional. For a coordinate $Q_i$ equal to the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ $f_3$-stress row and a translation readout $T_j$, the supported statistic would have to be a controlled survival entry
$$
\begin{aligned}
S_{f_3\text{-stress},T_j}>0
\end{aligned}
$$
after controlling for amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, CDS length, M-density baseline, matched mRNA abundance where TE is used, and simpler codon baselines. The data in this record do not yet supply a same-CDS joined matrix of codon counts, wobble weights, measured ribosome-profiling or translation-efficiency rows, and matched mRNA controls. Thus the present result supports a tRNA-supply mechanism for $R$ avoidance under a powered organism panel, but it does not close the measured translation-survival row.

\paragraph{Local refusal boundary.}
No translation realization follows merely from codon topology, $S^{QT}$ notation, tAI or stAI model weights, or review eligibility. No folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, localization, stability, phenotype, causation, or universality follows from this packet. A later claim at any one of those layers must attach its own layer-matched finite contact and compute its own controlled statistic. In particular, the three-organism all-$1.0$ matrix is treated as rank degeneracy, not as survival evidence. The positive content of this chapter is narrower and finite: in a powered $40$-organism computation with $1000$ permutations at $\alpha=0.05$, the $R$ sense-codon side of the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ boundary is tRNA-poor under GtRNAdb tGCN plus dos Reis wobble weights, making wobble-broken translation readout a testable next boundary rather than an already established downstream biological conclusion.
