\subsection{A bounded tRNA-supply boundary for the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ $f_3$ residual coordinate}
\label{sec:bioreality-q6-f3-trna-poverty-boundary}

\paragraph{Finite coordinate being tested.}
The record concerns a finite coding-sequence residual surface restricted to the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ coordinate that remains after amino-acid projection and then asks whether the third-position $f_3$ stress direction has any bounded translation-layer contact. The underlying $f_3$ saturation computation is explicit. The row set $M = \mathrm{Med}_R \cup \mathrm{ACA}$ is
$\{\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{ACA},\mathrm{ACG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\}$.
After $f_3$ saturation of $R$, the retained list is
$\{\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\}$,
so the $f_3$ boundary list is empty. This makes the chapter a boundary record rather than a discovery of a new codon class outside the finite surface.

\paragraph{Powered tRNA-poverty test.}
The organism panel test for the $R$ sense-codon boundary used GtRNAdb tRNA gene-copy numbers together with dos Reis wobble weights. It requested $46$ organisms, computed $40$, skipped $6$, and used $1000$ permutations at $\alpha = 0.05$. The finite outcome was passed: $R$ sense codons were systematically tRNA-poor under the tGCN-plus-wobble readout. In formula form, the recorded comparison has the following bounded interpretation:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\text{panel size requested} &= 46,\\
\text{panel size computed} &= 40,\\
\text{panel size skipped} &= 6,\\
N_{\mathrm{perm}} &= 1000,\\
\alpha &= 0.05.
\end{aligned}
$$
The result supports a tRNA-supply explanation for avoidance of the $R$ sense-codon side of the tested surface. It does not, by itself, say that ribosomes moved differently on the same coding sequences; it says that the finite codon boundary is aligned with a measured and modelled tRNA-availability asymmetry.

\paragraph{Relation to the wider $Q6$ residual spectrum.}
Several adjacent finite rows sharpen the interpretation without enlarging the claim. In the per-organism axis-strength spectrum, $11$ organisms were computed out of $16$ requested, with $5$ folds, at least $500$ proteins per organism, and $41$ synonymous-design columns. The captured spectra were led on average by $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$, followed by $d_{\mathrm{tRNA}}$, and the per-organism dominant-count summary was $\{d_{\mathrm{resid4}}:11\}$. The same row reports an imbalanced domain panel with $1$ archaeal, $2$ bacterial, and $8$ eukaryotic organisms, and a largest GC3 rank effect $\rho = 0.136$ for $d_{\mathrm{usage}}$. These figures are descriptive constraints on the panel geometry, not population-level biological laws.

The residual optimality row computed $11$ organisms out of $16$ requested under the same $5$-fold, $500$-protein, $41$-column design and called the dominant non-tRNA residual axis $f_3$ translation selection. The two-axis closure row, again with $11$ organisms computed out of $16$, concluded that a third axis is present because $d_{\perp}$ has systematically positive held-out $R^2$ and same-signed organism directions. The third-axis identity row gave a provisional alignment to usage frequency within family, with mean cosine $0.128$ and one-sided sign-test $p = 0.000488$. A separate mechanism-discrimination row kept the $d_{\perp}$ signal after GC orthogonalization and summarized it as selection-not-GC, not wobble-clustered, with CSC or positional identity still requiring an additional contact. The three-axis sufficiency row concluded that a fourth mechanism is present. These rows show that the tRNA-poverty call sits inside a larger finite residual spectrum, but they do not turn the $R$ boundary into a complete translation, protein, or phenotype claim.

\paragraph{The exact readout boundary.}
The allowed readback here is narrow. The positive finite row is the tRNA-supply asymmetry for $R$ sense codons under GtRNAdb tGCN and dos Reis wobble weights. A translation-survival statement would require matched codon counts, tRNA or wobble weights, a direct translation readout such as tAI, stAI, ribosome occupancy, or translation efficiency, and controls for amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, coding-sequence length, $M$-density, and matched mRNA abundance where the readout is TE. Without that joined matrix, the entry $S_{f_3\text{-stress},T_j}$ remains a target statistic rather than an established survival value.

Consequently, the chapter permits the following bounded conclusion: the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ $f_3$ residual boundary has a powered finite contact with tRNA-poverty on the $R$ sense-codon side of the surface. It does not permit a stronger statement that $f_3$ stress already changes translation efficiency, ribosome occupancy, protein abundance, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, localization, stability, phenotype, or universality. Each of those layers would need its own same-row contact and its own controlled statistic. A three-organism all-$1.0$ rank-saturated surface is also not survival evidence; rank degeneracy is a null boundary for this purpose, not a positive biological readout.

\paragraph{Falsification rule.}
The finite tRNA-poverty row remains compatible with a later negative translation-row outcome. If a powered organism or CDS panel attaches the required translation readouts and the controlled statistic satisfies
$$
\begin{aligned}
S_{f_3\text{-stress},T_j} \leq 0
\end{aligned}
$$
for all declared translation rows $T_j$, or if the design remains rank-degenerate after controls, then the translation-readout conjecture is not supported even though the tRNA-supply boundary row stays valid. The present chapter therefore records a real finite asymmetry at the tRNA-supply boundary and keeps all downstream biological interpretations outside the claim until a layer-matched row supplies them.
