\subsection{Boundary tRNA poverty for the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ $f_3$ stress row}\label{sec:bioreality-boundary-trna-poverty-bstar-q6-f3-stress}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite coordinate surface.}
This record fixes one finite BioReality row for the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual coordinate family: the row in which third-position $f_3$ stress is tested against organism-scoped codon usage, tRNA gene-copy supply, wobble-weight correction, and bounded translation-readout routes.  The finite codon support used by the row is not the full codon cube.  The verified $f_3$ saturation computation reports
$$
\begin{aligned}
M &= \{\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{ACA},\mathrm{ACG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\},\\
\operatorname{Sat}_{f_3}(R) &= \{\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\},\\
\partial_{f_3}R &= \varnothing .
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the saturated row has $20$ codons in $M$, $18$ codons after removing the two $\mathrm{ACA}/\mathrm{ACG}$ entries, and an empty $f_3$ boundary set.  The chapter is therefore not asking whether an unbounded boundary remains after closure.  It asks whether the finite residual direction that survives amino-acid projection aligns with a concrete tRNA-poverty asymmetry in the rank-powered organism panel.

\paragraph{Observed finite assay.}
The boundary-set assay was run as experiment $\mathrm{b\_star\_q6\_boundary\_set\_trna\_poverty\_powered}$ with seed $\mathrm{sha256:b\_star\_q6\_boundary\_set\_trna\_poverty\_powered:R\text{-}sense\text{-}tRNA\text{-}poverty\text{-}permutation}$.  It requested $46$ organisms, computed $40$, skipped $6$, used $1000$ permutations, and applied the declared threshold $\alpha=0.05$.  The finite outcome is recorded as passed.  Its bounded biological reading is that $R$ sense codons are systematically tRNA-poor when GtRNAdb tRNA gene-copy numbers are combined with dos Reis wobble weights.  This supports a tRNA-supply explanation for $R$ avoidance at the codon-supply layer of this assay.

\paragraph{Relation to the $f_3$ stress conjecture.}
The conjectural cross-layer row is narrower than a generic codon-usage association.  It says that, after amino-acid quotienting, the $f_3$-paired synonymous imbalance should become visible only where the local tRNA or wobble surface breaks the $f_3$ symmetry.  In notation local to this chapter, let $q_{f_3}$ denote the residual $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ coordinate and let $T_j$ denote a bounded translation readout such as tAI, stAI, ribosome occupancy, or translation efficiency.  The intended survival statistic has the form
$$
\begin{aligned}
S_{f_3,T_j} &= \Delta \operatorname{Score}(T_j \mid q_{f_3},Z),\\
Z &= \{\hbox{amino-acid composition},\hbox{ organism},\mathrm{GC3},\hbox{ CDS length},M\hbox{-density},\hbox{ mRNA control when measured TE is used}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The verified tRNA-poverty row does not by itself fill this survival entry for a measured translation dataset.  It supplies the finite asymmetry that makes the entry worth testing: the relevant codon-side coordinate is not symmetric after the tRNA-copy and wobble-weight surface is attached.  If every controlled $S_{f_3,T_j}$ remains non-positive, or if the readout matrix is rank-degenerate, the cross-layer conjecture fails even though the codon/tRNA poverty row remains true.

\paragraph{Rank and neighboring finite rows.}
The surrounding powered rows give context for why this boundary is not being promoted beyond its measured scope.  The axis-strength spectrum requested $16$ organisms, computed $11$, used $5$ folds, required at least $500$ proteins per organism, and used $41$ synonymous design columns.  In that row, $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$ led all $11$ per-organism dominant counts; the domain counts were imbalanced as $\{\mathrm{archaea}:1,\mathrm{bacteria}:2,\mathrm{eukaryote}:8\}$, and the largest reported GC3 rank effect was $\rho=0.136$ for $d_{\mathrm{usage}}$.  A separate powered row identified $d_{\perp}$ with usage frequency within family at mean cosine $0.128$ and one-sided sign-test $p=0.000488$, again over $11$ computed organisms from $16$ requested, $5$ folds, at least $500$ proteins per organism, and $41$ synonymous design columns.  The non-tRNA residual optimality row also ran on $11$ computed organisms from $16$ requested and concluded that the dominant non-tRNA residual axis aligns most with $f_3$ translation selection.  These rows make the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual surface biologically pointed, but they do not replace the missing same-row translation-readout survival statistic.

\paragraph{Local admissible reading.}
The admissible conclusion is finite and layer-bounded.  In the powered panel, the $R$ sense codon side of the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ boundary is tRNA-poor under a concrete tRNA-copy plus wobble-weight construction, and this gives a codon-supply route by which $f_3$ stress could become visible to a translation-readout row.  The result is stronger than a purely formal coordinate observation because it uses $40$ computed organisms and a $1000$-permutation test at $\alpha=0.05$.  It is also weaker than a translation-survival theorem because no joined codon, wobble-weight, ribosome or translation-efficiency, and matched mRNA matrix is recorded here.

\paragraph{No-promotion boundary.}
No claim about ribosome occupancy, translation efficiency, protein abundance, folded structure, physical admissibility, biological function, phenotype, global law, or a general molecular mechanism follows from this row alone.  The tRNA-poverty result supplies a bounded codon-supply explanation for $R$ avoidance in the finite assay.  A translation claim requires a separate measured or explicitly bounded translation contact and a positive controlled $S_{f_3,T_j}$ entry.  Protein, localization, stability, RNA half-life, structure, physical admissibility, function, phenotype, causation, and universality each require their own layer-matched contact and statistic.  In particular, a three-organism all-$1.0$ controlled surface is a rank-saturated null boundary rather than evidence of survival, and modeled tAI or stAI coupling cannot be treated as observed ribosome dynamics without a separate calibration row.
