\subsection{The $B^*_{Q6}$ Boundary Set as a tRNA-Poverty Readout}
\label{subsec:bioreality-q6-boundary-set-trna-poverty}

\paragraph{Finite carrier.}
The finite surface considered here is the coding-sequence-by-organism residual coordinate surface restricted to the $B^*_{Q6}$ coordinate, with the $f_3$-stress coordinate read only after amino-acid projection. The carrier is not a protein, a phenotype, or a folding state. It is a finite table whose rows are organism-scoped coding sequences or organism-level codon summaries, whose coordinate columns include the $B^*_{Q6}$ synonymous residual directions, and whose admissible translation-side annotations are codon usage, tRNA gene-copy number, and tAI or stAI style wobble-weight readbacks. The tested boundary asks whether the residual coordinate points toward a special set of sense codons that is systematically poor in tRNA supply under GtRNAdb tGCN together with dos Reis wobble weights.

\paragraph{Saturation geometry.}
The $f_3$ saturation check fixes the finite codon set before any biological interpretation is made. In the recorded run $76d3dc408a57$, the set $M=\mathrm{Med}_R\cup\{\mathrm{ACA}\}$ is
$$
\begin{aligned}
M=\{&\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{ACA},\mathrm{ACG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\\
&\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The saturated $f_3$ image of $R$ removes exactly the two ACA/ACG entries and is
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{Sat}_{f_3}(R)=\{&\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\\
&\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The recorded $f_3$ boundary is empty. Thus the boundary test is not using a leftover codon outside the saturated coordinate. It is testing whether the finite saturated residual set carries a supply-side asymmetry when coupled to organism-level tRNA and wobble annotations.

\paragraph{Observed finite-row result.}
The powered boundary-set run $d7728cc8768e$ evaluated $46$ requested organisms, computed $40$, and skipped $6$. The test used $1000$ permutations at $\alpha=0.05$ and returned status $\mathrm{passed}$. Its finite call is that $R$ sense codons are systematically tRNA-poor under GtRNAdb tGCN plus dos Reis wobble weights, giving a supply-side explanation for $R$ avoidance inside this bounded coordinate surface. In symbols, the tested comparison is a finite contrast between the $R$-marked sense codons and their complement after the $f_3$-saturated coordinate has been fixed:
$$
\begin{aligned}
H_0 &: \Delta_{R,\mathrm{tRNA/wobble}} \leq 0,\\
H_1 &: \Delta_{R,\mathrm{tRNA/wobble}} > 0,
\end{aligned}
$$
where $\Delta_{R,\mathrm{tRNA/wobble}}$ denotes the organism-aggregated depletion of tRNA or wobble support on the $R$ side of the finite contrast. The recorded permutation design is the relevant finite certificate: $40$ computed organisms and $1000$ permutations are enough for this chapter to state a positive tRNA-poverty boundary result, but only at the supply-annotation layer.

\paragraph{Relation to the residual-axis spectrum.}
The same evidence packet sits inside a larger $B^*_{Q6}$ residual-axis survey. In the axis-strength run $0146012d8909$, $16$ organisms were requested, $11$ were computed, every computed organism had at least $500$ proteins, the synonymous design had $41$ columns, and the fold count was $5$. The descriptive spectrum was led on average by $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$, followed by $d_{\mathrm{tRNA}}$, with per-organism dominant counts recorded as $\{d_{\mathrm{resid4}}:11\}$. The largest reported GC3 rank effect in that summary was $\rho=0.136$ for $d_{\mathrm{usage}}$. This does not replace the boundary-set tRNA-poverty test; it locates that test inside a finite residual basis where tRNA-weighted axes and non-tRNA residual axes can both be present.

\paragraph{Third-axis and residual context.}
Two neighboring runs constrain overinterpretation. The third-axis identity run $377671f96e96$ computed $11$ of $16$ organisms with $5$ folds, at least $500$ proteins per organism, and $41$ synonymous design columns. It identified $d_{\perp}$ provisionally with usage frequency within family, with mean cosine $0.128$ and one-sided sign-test $p=0.000488$. The third-axis mechanism run $d0893337f0dd$ reported that the GC-orthogonalized $d_{\perp}$ retained significant cross-species alignment and held-out $R^2$, with the conclusion recorded as selection-not-GC, not-wobble-clustered, and requiring external information for CSC or positional identity. Separately, the universal residual-axis run $2a526c9fb7f4$ reported that non-tRNA residual optimality aligns most with $f_3$ translation selection, while the three-axis sufficiency run $2436cae9d455$ reported fourth-mechanism-present. These neighboring facts keep the present chapter narrow: the $R$ boundary has a tRNA-poverty signal, but the residual system is not exhausted by that one signal.

\paragraph{Translation-readout boundary.}
The positive finite call is a tRNA-supply boundary call, not a measured translation-efficiency survival call. A translation survival entry would require same-organism or same-CDS rows joining the $B^*_{Q6}$ coordinate to a direct translation readout such as tAI, stAI, ribosome occupancy, or translation efficiency after controls for amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, CDS length, $M$-density, and matched mRNA abundance. The allowed bounded statistic has the form
$$
\begin{aligned}
S_{f_3,T_j} &= \mathrm{gain}\bigl(T_j\mid q_{f_3},Z\bigr),\\
Z&=(\mathrm{amino\ acid\ composition},\mathrm{organism},\mathrm{GC3},\mathrm{length},M\text{-}\mathrm{density},\mathrm{mRNA\ control}).
\end{aligned}
$$
This chapter does not assert that such an $S_{f_3,T_j}$ entry is already positive. It states only that the codon-side boundary set is tRNA-poor under the recorded finite organism panel and wobble-weight model. If a later same-row translation matrix is rank-degenerate, all-constant, or non-positive after the declared controls, then the translation-survival reading of the $f_3$-stress coordinate is rejected even though the tRNA-poverty boundary remains a finite supply-side observation.

\paragraph{Local non-promotion rule.}
No conclusion about protein abundance, folded structure, physical admissibility, biological function, phenotype, universality, or a full dynamical translation mechanism follows from this chapter alone. Codon usage, tRNA gene-copy numbers, and modeled tAI or stAI weights are bounded contacts with translation-side annotation; they are not by themselves ribosome-footprint measurements, mature protein measurements, structure assays, functional assays, perturbation experiments, or global biological laws. The chapter therefore permits the following statement and no stronger one: within the finite $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinate surface, the saturated $R$ sense-codon boundary is systematically poor in tRNA/wobble support across the powered organism panel, and any promotion to measured translation survival or downstream biology requires a separate layer-matched contact and an explicit controlled statistic.
