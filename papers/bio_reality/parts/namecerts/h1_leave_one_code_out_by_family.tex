\paragraph{Reality contact and scope.}
The only external contact used here is the curated standard-code table assigning codons to amino-acid or stop labels.  It tests codon-label readback and local label-split behavior.  It does not observe ribosomal realization, folded protein structure, physical admissibility, biochemical function, evolutionary necessity, or a universal biological law.  Consequently the local tile may be reported as a finite code-layer regularity, but it may not be promoted beyond that layer unless another contact independently supplies the higher-level observation.
