\paragraph{Scope of the claim.}
The evidence here is a finite code-readback result.  It says that the curated standard-code labels, when placed on the six-coordinate codon display, produce a local boundary-tile pattern whose leave-one-code-out behavior is strong across the five recorded families.  It does not say that DNA must obey this geometry as a biological necessity.  It does not supply translation dynamics, folded protein structure, physical admissibility, biological function, a molecular mechanism, or a universal law.  Amino-acid label sequences remain label sequences at this stage; no structural or functional conclusion follows unless an independent contact supplies the missing layer.
