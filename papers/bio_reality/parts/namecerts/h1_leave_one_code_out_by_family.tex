\subsection{Leave-One-Code-Out Stability by Genetic-Code Family}\label{sec:h1-leave-one-code-out-by-family}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite object under test.}
This record concerns a window-six codon coordinate model for the standard-code label table.  The carrier is the six-coordinate codon cube $Q_6$, read only at the code-label layer: a codon is treated as a six-bit displayed point with a curated amino-acid or stop label attached by the standard-code table.  The finite row isolates a distinguished set $R$ of thirteen codons,
$$
\begin{aligned}
R = \{&AAA, AGA, AGG, AUA, CUA, CUC, CUG, CUU,\\
     &UAA, UAG, UCA, UGA, UUA\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The row verifies both $|R|=13$ and the punctured-cube face data $f(R)=(13,16,4)$.  Thus the evidence is not a statement about all biochemical behavior of codons; it is a finite statement about a particular local codon tile, its boundary, and its label readback.

\paragraph{Spectral and shape context.}
The same finite packet records the spectral scalars
$$
\begin{aligned}
\lambda_M &= 0.6752479308830213,\\
\lambda_R &= 0.4758352843197002,\\
\mu &= 3.051487585298128.
\end{aligned}
$$
The radial cubic residual is $-6.224354365258478\cdot 10^{-12}$, so the numerical row is internally consistent at the supplied tolerance.  Against $N=2000$ coordinate-preserving null samples with seed $42$, there was one exact shape match, giving $p_{\mathrm{exact}}=0.0005$, and there were $25$ samples with Jaccard similarity at least $0.9$, giving $p_{J\geq 0.9}=0.0125$.  The run required $57377$ distinct-sample attempts and $55377$ duplicate retries, which is a useful warning that the sampled finite space is constrained rather than free-floating.

\paragraph{Code-level recovery.}
The leave-one-code-out experiment evaluated $23$ tables and $63$ targets.  Its baseline hit rate was $0.19821739130434785$.  The observed rate above that baseline was $0.9841269841269841$, with $0.9365079365079365$ of targets landing inside $M-T$ and $0.9841269841269841$ landing inside $M-T$ together with the boundary allowance.  The family-level row is more specific: it groups the tested codes into five families and reports the following hit rates above baseline,
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{alternative\_yeast} &= 0.8333333333333334,\\
\mathrm{bacterial\_plastid} &= 1.0,\\
\mathrm{candidate\_other} &= 1.0,\\
\mathrm{mitochondrial} &= 1.0,\\
\mathrm{nuclear\_ciliate} &= 1.0.
\end{aligned}
$$
The finite claim is therefore that the local tile is unusually stable under held-out code-table variation within these named families.  It is not that the geometry explains translation, folding, viability, or evolutionary mechanism.

\paragraph{Codon-level and module-level checks.}
The leave-one-codon-out row reports recovery rate $1.0$, predictable count $8$, closure-coverage-high count $8$, boundary count $5$, and anchor count $0$.  The module classifier accounts for all thirteen codons of $R$: assigned count $13$, $R$ size $13$, no missing codons, no extra module codons, and no unassigned codons.  The six named modules are
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{agr} &= \{AGA, AGG\},\\
\mathrm{ar\_lys} &= \{AAA\},\\
\mathrm{cun} &= \{CUA, CUC, CUG, CUU\},\\
\mathrm{ile\_met} &= \{AUA\},\\
\mathrm{ssn} &= \{UCA, UUA\},\\
\mathrm{stop\_trp} &= \{UAA, UAG, UGA\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
Every module has either predictable support or boundary support in the recorded classification.  The amino-acid-class-preserving null model gives an additional contrast: over $N=2000$ samples with seed $42$, the observed $M$ size was $20$, the exact shape count was $0$, the lower-tail count at or below the observed $M$ size was $7$, the exact-shape $p$-value was $0.0$, and the lower-tail $p$-value was $0.0035$.

\paragraph{Interpretation.}
The curated standard-code table is the only biological contact used here.  It supplies codon-to-label assignments and thereby permits code-layer readback and finite local split tests.  It does not observe a ribosome, a translation trajectory, a folded protein, a physical admissibility condition, a molecular function, or a universal law of biology.  Consequently the supported conclusion is deliberately narrow: window-six codon coordinates expose a statistically sharp and family-stable local boundary tile for the standard-code label table, with $R$ forming a thirteen-codon punctured-cube pattern and with strong leave-one-code-out behavior across five code families.  Any claim about translation realization, folded structure, physical feasibility, biological function, mechanism, or universality would require a separate contact that measures that higher layer directly.
