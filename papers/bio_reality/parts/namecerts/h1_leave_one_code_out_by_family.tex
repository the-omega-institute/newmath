\subsection{Family-stratified leave-one-code-out recovery in the window-six codon cube}
\label{sec:bioreality-h1-leave-one-code-out-by-family}

\paragraph{Finite coordinate object.}
The record works at the code-read layer of the standard genetic code.  The finite carrier is the six-coordinate codon cube $Q_6$, viewed through the usual codon display over the alphabet $\{A,C,G,U\}$.  Its labels are supplied by a curated standard-code table: the table is used as an observed codon-to-label contact, not as a biological mechanism.  The finite distinctions tracked here are the six-bit display, local tile faces in the cube, singleton label corners, and three-point label classes.  The claim under audit is family-stratified: after leaving one code table out, the local window-six structure is asked whether it still places the held-out target in the same recovered region, measured family by family.

\paragraph{Boundary set and punctured cube shape.}
The boundary set $R$ has cardinality $13$.  In codon notation the supplied row is
$$\begin{aligned}
R = \{&AAA, AGA, AGG, AUA, CUA, CUC, CUG, CUU,\\
     &UAA, UAG, UCA, UGA, UUA\}.
\end{aligned}$$
The same thirteen codons appear in the punctured-cube certificate, with face data summarized by the $f$-vector
$$\begin{aligned}
(f_0,f_1,f_2)=(13,16,4).
\end{aligned}$$
Thus the relevant local object is not an arbitrary subset of the codon cube: it is a thirteen-vertex boundary pattern with sixteen one-dimensional incidences and four two-dimensional faces.  This is the finite shape against which the leave-one-code-out and leave-one-codon-out tests are read.

\paragraph{Reality contact and local verdict.}
The only contact used here is the curated standard-code table, whose observed fact is that codons are assigned amino-acid or stop labels.  It resolves codon label readback and local label-split checks.  It does not observe ribosomal translation, folded protein structure, structural order, thermodynamic admissibility, biological function, organism-level mechanism, or universal biological necessity.  Consequently the ready verdict is local: window-six codon coordinates expose a statistically sharp boundary-and-module pattern for code labels, and the leave-one-code-out test remains strong across five code-table families.  No claim about translation, folding, physical admissibility, function, mechanism, or universality follows from this row unless a separate contact supplies that higher-layer observation.
