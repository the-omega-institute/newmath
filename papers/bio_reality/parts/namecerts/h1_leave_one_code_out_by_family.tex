\subsection{Family-stratified leave-one-code-out recovery for the window-six codon tile}
\label{sec:bioreality-h1-leave-one-code-out-by-family}

\paragraph{Claim.}
This record tests whether the same local window-six codon geometry that isolates a distinguished boundary set remains informative when whole genetic-code tables are withheld by family.  The object under inspection is the standard-code codon table read at the code-label layer: codons are treated as six-bit cube points, and the observed labels are the curated amino-acid or stop assignments.  The claim is deliberately local.  It concerns recovery of codon-label changes from finite table rows and from the window-six tile boundary; it does not assert translation, molecular structure, physical feasibility, biological function, a biochemical mechanism, or a universal biological principle.

\paragraph{Finite carrier and distinguished boundary.}
The codon display has a distinguished set $R$ of thirteen codons:
$$
\begin{aligned}
R = \{&AAA, AGA, AGG, AUA, CUA, CUC, CUG, CUU,\\
     &UAA, UAG, UCA, UGA, UUA\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The row audit reports $|R|=13$ and identifies the same thirteen strings in the cardinality and punctured-cube checks.  As a punctured cube, this local object has face-vector
$$
\begin{aligned}
(f_0,f_1,f_2)=(13,16,4).
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the finite object used in this claim is not a free-form biological narrative; it is a fixed combinatorial region inside the codon cube, with a concrete list of vertices and a measured tile shape.

\paragraph{Spectral and shape checks.}
The same finite region carries a spectral signature.  The audited values are
$$
\begin{aligned}
\lambda_M &= 0.6752479308830213,\\
\lambda_R &= 0.4758352843197002,\\
\mu &= 3.051487585298128.
\end{aligned}
$$
The radial cubic residual is $-6.224354365258478\times 10^{-12}$, reported both as the cubic-polynomial value and as the radial-polynomial value, so the numerical witness is within floating tolerance rather than an independent biological premise.  A separate shape randomization row used $N=2000$ sampled tables with seed $42$.  It found one exact shape match, giving $p_{\mathrm{exact}}=0.0005$, and twenty-five high-overlap cases at Jaccard threshold $0.9$, giving $p_{J\geq 0.9}=0.0125$.  Under the amino-acid-class-preserving null, again with $N=2000$ and seed $42$, the observed module size was $20$; the exact-shape count was $0$, the lower-tail count at or below the observed size was $7$, the exact-shape value was $0.0$, and the lower-tail value was $0.0035$.  These numbers support the view that the window-six tile is a nontrivial finite pattern in the curated code table, while still leaving all higher biological interpretations outside the present contact.

\paragraph{Leave-one-code-out family recovery.}
The family-stratified row evaluates five code families.  Its per-family rates above the baseline are
$$
\begin{aligned}
\operatorname{rate}(\mathrm{alternative\_yeast}) &= 0.8333333333333334,\\
\operatorname{rate}(\mathrm{bacterial\_plastid}) &= 1.0,\\
\operatorname{rate}(\mathrm{candidate\_other}) &= 1.0,\\
\operatorname{rate}(\mathrm{mitochondrial}) &= 1.0,\\
\operatorname{rate}(\mathrm{nuclear\_ciliate}) &= 1.0.
\end{aligned}
$$
The family count is therefore $5$, with four families at perfect above-baseline recovery and the alternative-yeast family at five-sixths recovery.  The pooled leave-one-code-out audit gives additional scale: $23$ tables were evaluated, with $63$ total targets.  The baseline hit rate was $0.19821739130434785$, while the above-baseline hit rate was $0.9841269841269841$.  Restricting to $M\setminus T$ gives $0.9365079365079365$, and adjoining the boundary raises the rate to $0.9841269841269841$.  The family row is therefore consistent with the pooled result: most withheld code-table targets are recovered by the local tile-plus-boundary criterion far above the naive baseline.

\paragraph{Single-codon and module consistency.}
The leave-one-codon-out row gives an orthogonal check on the same local geometry.  It reports $0$ anchors, $5$ boundary cases, $8$ high-closure-coverage cases, $8$ predictable cases, and recovery rate $1.0$.  The module audit partitions all thirteen $R$ codons without leftovers:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{AGR} &= \{AGA,AGG\},\\
\mathrm{AR\_Lys} &= \{AAA\},\\
\mathrm{CUN} &= \{CUA,CUC,CUG,CUU\},\\
\mathrm{Ile\_Met} &= \{AUA\},\\
\mathrm{SSN} &= \{UCA,UUA\},\\
\mathrm{Stop\_Trp} &= \{UAA,UAG,UGA\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The classification row records $R\_size=13$, assigned count $13$, no extra module codons, no missing codons, and no unassigned codons.  Every listed module has a predictable-or-boundary status.  This is the finite reason the family-stratified recovery is interpretable: the recovered targets are not merely counted in bulk, but are organized by a complete module partition of the same thirteen-codon boundary set.

\paragraph{Reality contact.}
The only external contact used here is a curated standard-code table that assigns each codon to an amino-acid or stop label.  This contact is sufficient for code-label readback and for checking whether local tile splits align with observed codon labels.  It is not a measurement of translation dynamics, folded protein geometry, thermodynamic admissibility, organismal function, or universality across biology.  The observed agreement is therefore a code-layer fact: window-six coordinates expose a strong local boundary pattern in curated codon labels, and the leave-one-code-out family rows show that the pattern remains predictive across the five audited families.

\paragraph{Boundary of the conclusion.}
The conclusion is that the six-bit codon tile supplies a finite, testable explanation of local code-label recovery in this dataset.  The strongest audited statement is numerical: across $23$ tables and $63$ targets, the above-baseline recovery is $0.9841269841269841$, and in the family split the five families have rates $0.8333333333333334,1.0,1.0,1.0,1.0$.  No structural or functional conclusion follows from those rates alone.  Any claim about protein folding, physical admissibility, realized translation, biological function, mechanism, or universality would require an additional contact that observes that layer directly.
