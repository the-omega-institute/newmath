\subsection{Leave-One-Code-Out Recovery by Code Family}
\label{sec:bioreality-h1-leave-one-code-out-by-family}

\paragraph{Claim surface.}
The packet records a finite code-read phenomenon for the standard codon table under a window-six codon display. The carrier is the six-coordinate codon cube $Q_6$, read only as a finite coordinate presentation of codon labels. The relevant distinctions are local tile faces, singleton label corners, and three-point label classes. The biological contact is deliberately narrower than the geometry: a curated standard-code table assigns each codon to an amino-acid or stop label, and that contact licenses codon-label readback only. It does not by itself observe translation as a process, folding, physical admissibility, function, a mechanism, or a universal biological law.

\paragraph{Finite residue set.}
The distinguished residue set $R$ has cardinality $13$. Its observed codons are AAA, AGA, AGG, AUA, CUA, CUC, CUG, CUU, UAA, UAG, UCA, UGA, and UUA. In the punctured-cube calculation this same set has face-vector data
$$
\begin{aligned}
(f_0,f_1,f_2) &= (13,16,4).
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the local object is not an arbitrary list of codons: it has a small cubical boundary profile in the window-six coordinate system. The packet keeps this as a code-layer structural fact about finite labels, not as evidence for any higher biological layer.

\paragraph{Spectral row.}
The finite spectrum associated with the same construction gives
$$
\begin{aligned}
\lambda_M &= 0.6752479308830213,\\
\lambda_R &= 0.4758352843197002,\\
\mu &= 3.051487585298128.
\end{aligned}
$$
The radial cubic residual was measured as $-6.224354365258478\times 10^{-12}$, so the row is numerically consistent with the cubic relation at the reported tolerance. The row supplies a stable finite coordinate signature for the local tile; it is not a physical energy, rate, fitness, or folding statistic.

\paragraph{Shape significance.}
Against the reported coordinate null model, the $M$-shape comparison used $N=2000$ samples with seed $42$. There was $1$ exact shape match, giving
$$
\begin{aligned}
 p_{\mathrm{exact}} &= 0.0005,
\end{aligned}
$$
and there were $25$ matches at Jaccard threshold at least $0.9$, giving
$$
\begin{aligned}
 p_{J\geq 0.9} &= 0.0125.
\end{aligned}
$$
The sampling log reports $57377$ distinct sample attempts and $55377$ duplicate retries. Under the amino-acid-class-preserving null, the observed $M$ size was $20$, exact matches were $0$ among $2000$ samples, and $7$ samples fell in the lower tail at or below the observed $M$ size. The corresponding reported values are
$$
\begin{aligned}
 p_{\mathrm{aa\ class,exact}} &= 0.0,\\
 p_{\mathrm{aa\ class,lower\ tail}} &= 0.0035.
\end{aligned}
$$
These rows support the claim that the code-read tile is atypical under the stated finite nulls. They do not identify a biochemical cause.

\paragraph{Leave-one-code-out recovery.}
The all-family leave-one-code-out run evaluated $23$ tables and $63$ total targets. The baseline hit rate was $0.19821739130434785$. The hit rate above baseline was $0.9841269841269841$, the hit rate inside $M\setminus T$ was $0.9365079365079365$, and the hit rate inside $M\setminus T$ plus the boundary was $0.9841269841269841$. The family-stratified row contains $5$ families. Their hit rates above baseline were $0.8333333333333334$ for alternative yeast, $1.0$ for bacterial plastid, $1.0$ for candidate other, $1.0$ for mitochondrial, and $1.0$ for nuclear ciliate. The one sub-perfect family therefore remains high but not saturated, while the other four reported families reach the ceiling value in this finite test.

\paragraph{Codon-module accounting.}
The leave-one-codon-out module classification assigns every residue codon in $R$ to a named finite module. The modules are AGA and AGG in agr; AAA in ar\_lys; CUA, CUC, CUG, and CUU in cun; AUA in ile\_met; UCA and UUA in ssn; and UAA, UAG, and UGA in stop\_trp. The completion row reports $R$ size $13$, assigned count $13$, no extra module codons, no missing codons from the modules, and no unassigned codons. The companion leave-one-codon-out recovery row reports anchor count $0$, boundary count $5$, high closure coverage $8$, predictable count $8$, and recovery rate $1.0$.

\paragraph{Interpretation.}
The finite evidence says that a window-six local tile recovers a strong code-label signal across code-family perturbations, with the strongest row being the by-family result: four of five families have hit rate $1.0$ above baseline and the remaining family has hit rate $0.8333333333333334$. The result is properly read as a local codon-label regularity: curated code labels meet a small coordinate boundary pattern with unusual null-model behavior. The result must not be promoted to a claim about translation being realized by the coordinate system, about amino-acid strings determining folded structure, about physical admissibility, about biological function, about a causal mechanism, or about universality across life. Any such assertion would require a separate contact that directly observes the corresponding higher-layer object.
