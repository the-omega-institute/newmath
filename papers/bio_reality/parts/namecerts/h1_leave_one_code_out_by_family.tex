\paragraph{Finite object under test.}
The record concerns the standard-code codon table viewed through a six-coordinate codon display.  The mathematical carrier is the finite codon cube $Q_6$, while the biological contact is only the curated assignment of each codon to an amino-acid or stop label.  The claim is therefore a code-layer readback claim: window-six coordinates can expose a local boundary pattern in the label table, but the labels themselves remain supplied by the curated biological table.  The test does not observe translation in a cell, peptide production, folding, physical feasibility, selective advantage, or biological function.

The distinguished boundary set $R$ has cardinality $13$.  In the supplied finite row it is exactly
$$
\begin{aligned}
R=\{&AAA,AGA,AGG,AUA,CUA,CUC,CUG,CUU,\\
&UAA,UAG,UCA,UGA,UUA\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The same set is reported as a punctured-cube pattern with $f$-vector $(13,16,4)$.  Thus the object being audited is not an arbitrary narrative grouping of codons: it is a named finite subset of the $64$ codon positions, with thirteen listed vertices, sixteen listed one-dimensional incidences, and four listed two-dimensional faces in the induced local pattern.

\paragraph{Reality contact and boundary of interpretation.}
The only external contact used here is the curated standard genetic-code table.  It supports codon-to-label readback and local label-split checks.  It does not record ribosomal realization, tRNA availability, kinetic accessibility, folded structure, physical admissibility of a peptide, downstream function, organism-level fitness, or a general mechanism.  Consequently the admissible conclusion is narrow: in the supplied finite data, the six-coordinate codon display and the curated standard-code labels determine a robust local tile boundary whose leave-one-code-out behavior remains high across five code-table families.

No stronger biological assertion follows from these rows.  Amino-acid label sequences are not protein structures.  A local codon tile is not a universal law of molecular biology.  Cross-organism wording, tRNA notation, and codon-family naming must remain separate from any claim about folding, translation, physical feasibility, or function unless a new contact directly measures that higher layer.  The present record is therefore a finite code-read witness with explicit stopping conditions: it may be used to discuss label-table geometry, module coverage, family-resolved leave-one-code-out recovery, and the observed finite null-model frequencies, but it must not be promoted beyond the code layer without additional data.
