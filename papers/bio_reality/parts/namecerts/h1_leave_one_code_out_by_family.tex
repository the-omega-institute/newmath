\subsection{Leave-One-Code-Out Stability by Code Family}\label{subsec:h1-leave-one-code-out-by-family}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite display.}
This record treats the standard-code codon table as a six-coordinate codon cube with a curated label readout.  The mathematical object under inspection is not translation itself, but the finite pattern obtained after the codons are displayed as binary-window coordinates and compared with amino-acid or stop labels supplied by the curated code table.  The distinguished residual set $R$ has cardinality $13$ and is exactly
$$
\begin{aligned}
R=\{&AAA, AGA, AGG, AUA, CUA, CUC, CUG, CUU,\\
&UAA, UAG, UCA, UGA, UUA\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The punctured-cube computation returns the same thirteen codons and the face-vector
$$
\begin{aligned}
f(R)=(13,16,4).
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the object is finite at the level of rows: thirteen codon positions, sixteen one-dimensional adjacencies, and four two-dimensional faces in the local tile under the chosen window-six coordinate convention.

\paragraph{Spectral scale.}
The finite row also carries a spectral check.  The observed values are
$$
\begin{aligned}
\lambda_M &= 0.6752479308830213,\\
\lambda_R &= 0.4758352843197002,\\
\mu &= 3.051487585298128.
\end{aligned}
$$
The reported radial cubic residual is $-6.224354365258478\times 10^{-12}$, the same number recorded as the cubic-polynomial residual.  This makes the spectral statement a numerical certificate of the displayed finite matrix computation, not an independent biological mechanism.  Its role is to quantify the local separation seen by the coordinate construction.

\paragraph{Shape rarity.}
Against the supplied randomization run with $N=2000$, seed $42$, and $57377$ distinct-sample attempts, the exact local shape appears once.  The exact-shape probability is therefore reported as $p=0.0005$.  The high-overlap threshold $J\geq 0.9$ appears in $25$ samples, giving $p=0.0125$.  A second amino-acid-class-preserving null run with $N=2000$ reports observed $M$ size $20$, exact-match count $0$, and lower-tail count $7$.  Its corresponding values are $p=0.0$ for exact shape and $p=0.0035$ for the lower tail.  These numbers support a finite code-layer anomaly relative to the declared null models.  They do not identify a biochemical cause.

\paragraph{Leave-one-code-out family test.}
The by-family claim is the family-stratified version of the leave-one-code-out probe.  Across five code families, the hit rates above the baseline are
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{alternative\_yeast} &= 0.8333333333333334,\\
\mathrm{bacterial\_plastid} &= 1.0,\\
\mathrm{candidate\_other} &= 1.0,\\
\mathrm{mitochondrial} &= 1.0,\\
\mathrm{nuclear\_ciliate} &= 1.0.
\end{aligned}
$$
The parent leave-one-code-out row evaluated $23$ tables and $63$ total targets.  Its baseline hit rate is $0.19821739130434785$, while the observed rate above baseline is $0.9841269841269841$.  The hit rate in $M\setminus T$ is $0.9365079365079365$, and the rate in $M\setminus T$ enlarged by the boundary is $0.9841269841269841$.  The family table therefore says that the signal is not confined to a single named family: four families attain full above-baseline recovery, and the remaining alternative-yeast family still remains well above the baseline row.

\paragraph{Module accounting.}
The leave-one-codon-out module classification accounts for all thirteen residual codons.  The modules are
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{agr} &= \{AGA,AGG\},\\
\mathrm{ar\_lys} &= \{AAA\},\\
\mathrm{cun} &= \{CUA,CUC,CUG,CUU\},\\
\mathrm{ile\_met} &= \{AUA\},\\
\mathrm{ssn} &= \{UCA,UUA\},\\
\mathrm{stop\_trp} &= \{UAA,UAG,UGA\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The accounting row reports $R$ size $13$, assigned count $13$, no extra module codons, no missing codons, and no unassigned codons.  The companion leave-one-codon-out row records anchor count $0$, boundary count $5$, high closure coverage $8$, predictable count $8$, and recovery rate $1.0$.  In this chapter's finite interpretation, every module has either a predictable component or a boundary component, and no codon in the thirteen-element residual set is left outside the displayed module partition.

\paragraph{Scientific reading.}
The supported conclusion is deliberately narrow.  Window-six codon coordinates expose a local boundary-tile pattern whose leave-one-code-out behavior remains strong when grouped by code family.  The curated standard-code table supplies the labels needed to read the finite codon positions as amino-acid or stop labels, and the numerical probes measure how that label pattern behaves under deletion and null comparison.  This is a code-read statement: it concerns codon labels in a finite table and the geometry of their displayed positions.

\paragraph{Boundary of the claim.}
Nothing in the finite row shows that this local tile is a law of biology.  The row does not observe translation in a cell, folded protein structure, physical admissibility of a molecule, biological function, a causal mechanism, or universality across life.  Amino-acid label readout is not a structure experiment, and a codon-table contact is not a folding, function, or mechanism contact.  Any assertion crossing from code labels to translation, structure, physical admissibility, function, mechanism, or universality would require a separate observation designed for that higher layer.
