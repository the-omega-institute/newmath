\paragraph{Reality contact and limitation.}
The contact used here is the curated standard-code table, with codon-label resolution.  It can test code readback and local codon-tile label splitting.  It cannot test translation realization, structural order, physical admissibility, or function realization.  Consequently, the chapter asserts only a code-layer fact: window-six coordinates expose a local boundary-tile pattern whose leave-one-code-out behavior is stable across the recorded families.  No statement in this record implies that DNA is forced by the geometry, that a ribosome implements the tile, that an amino-acid label sequence has a folded structure, that a protein is physically admissible, that a biological function follows, or that the pattern is a universal biological mechanism.  Any such promotion would require a separate contact of the appropriate kind, not merely another reading of the codon-label table.
