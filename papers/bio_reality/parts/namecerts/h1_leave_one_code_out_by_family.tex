\subsection{Leave-one-code-out performance by code family}

\paragraph{Record.}
This record concerns a window-six representation of the standard-code codon table.  The carrier is the six-coordinate codon cube $Q_6$, read through a codon-label table whose entries are curated amino-acid or stop assignments.  The claim tested here is family-stratified: after the local codon-tile packet has been calibrated on the standard code, leave-one-code-out targets are grouped by code family, and each family is checked against the same boundary-aware recovery rule.  The result is not a claim about translation as a biochemical process.  It is a claim about label-level readback from a finite codon table under a local cube-coordinate probe.

\paragraph{Boundary of interpretation.}
The contact used here is a curated standard-code table assigning codons to amino-acid or stop labels.  That contact supports codon-label readback and local split checks only.  It does not observe ribosomal translation, folding, structural order, physical admissibility, molecular function, organismal fitness, or a universal biological rule.  In particular, an amino-acid label sequence produced by table readback is still only a label sequence.  No folded structure, admissible conformation, functional phenotype, or mechanistic universality follows from this finite codon-tile calculation unless an additional empirical contact specifically supplies that higher-layer observation.

The family row is ready as a finite mathematical record.  The verified data give a thirteen-codon boundary $R$, a punctured-cube face vector $(13,16,4)$, a complete six-module classification of $R$, spectral values $\lambda_M=0.6752479308830213$, $\lambda_R=0.4758352843197002$, and $\mu=3.051487585298128$, shape-null support at $p_{\mathrm{exact}}=0.0005$ and $p_{J\geq0.9}=0.0125$, amino-acid-class-preserving null support with exact-match probability $0.0$ and lower-tail probability $0.0035$, aggregate leave-one-code recovery $0.9841269841269841$ above baseline, and family rates ranging from $0.8333333333333334$ to $1.0$.  The proper claim is therefore narrow and finite: within the curated codon-label layer, the local window-six boundary packet gives strong family-stratified recovery.  The record must remain at that layer until separate observations are attached for translation, structure, physical admissibility, function, mechanism, or universality.
