\paragraph{Finite record.}
This record concerns the claim identifier \texttt{h1.leave\_one\_code\_out.by\_family}.  The biological contact is the curated standard genetic-code table, used only as a codon-label readback table.  The coordinate carrier is the six-bit codon cube $Q_6$, with distinctions given by the displayed codon, a local tile face, singleton label corners, and three-point label classes.  The chapter-level question is not whether the codon cube explains translation as a mechanism, but whether a finite local coordinate pattern remains visible when one compares standard-code labels against related code tables grouped by code family.

\paragraph{Leave-one-codon and leave-one-code evidence.}
The leave-one-codon row records anchor count $0$, boundary count $5$, high closure coverage $8$, predictable count $8$, and recovery rate $1.0$.  In other words, the codon-level test does not depend on anchors in this row; the recovery is carried by predictable and boundary behavior.  This agrees with the module table above, where every codon in $R$ has a named module assignment.

The leave-one-code aggregate row is stronger at the table-comparison layer.  Among $23$ evaluated tables and $63$ targets, the baseline hit rate is approximately $0.1982$, while the boundary-augmented $M-T$ hit rate is approximately $0.9841$.  The family-resolved row then shows how this aggregate signal distributes over five families: bacterial-plastid, candidate-other, mitochondrial, and nuclear-ciliate families are all recorded at $1.0$, while alternative-yeast is recorded at $5/6=0.8333333333333334$.  The family row therefore acts as a stratified check on the aggregate result, not as an independent biological mechanism claim.

\paragraph{Scope of the biological contact.}
The only external contact in this record is the curated standard-code table.  It supplies codon-to-label assignments, with labels being amino-acid labels or stop labels.  This contact can test code-layer readback and local codon-tile label splits.  It cannot test translation realization, folded structure, structural order, physical admissibility, biological function, or a universal mechanism.  The local tile may be a useful coordinate witness for code-layer organization, but no protein fold, no functional phenotype, no translation apparatus, and no physical admissibility statement follows from this table alone.

This boundary is not a weakness of the row; it is part of the claim.  The row is allowed to say that the six-bit codon coordinate, the punctured $R$ set, the module classification, the spectral diagnostics, the null comparisons, and the family hit rates jointly support a local code-read pattern.  It is not allowed to promote that pattern into a global law of biology.  Any assertion about translation, folding, function, physical realization, or universality would require a separate observation source designed for that layer.
