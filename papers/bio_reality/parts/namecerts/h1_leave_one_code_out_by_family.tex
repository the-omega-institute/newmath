\subsection{Leave-One-Code-Out Family Stratification for the Window-Six Codon Tile}
\label{sec:h1-leave-one-code-out-by-family}

\paragraph{Finite object and scope.}
This record concerns the standard-code codon table only at the label-readback layer. The finite carrier is the six-coordinate codon cube $Q_6$, with codons read as six-bit displays and with labels supplied by a curated standard-code table. The construction does not treat those labels as internally generated biological mechanisms. It tests whether the local window-six tile already singled out by the standard-code row remains predictive when alternative code tables are withheld by family. The only admissible contact in this record is codon-to-label assignment: no translation event, folded structure, physical admissibility constraint, biological function, universal mechanism, or organism-wide law is obtained from these rows unless an independent higher-layer contact is added.

\paragraph{Boundary set.}
The distinguished boundary set $R$ has cardinality $13$. The verified codons are
$$
\begin{aligned}
R=\{&AAA, AGA, AGG, AUA, CUA, CUC, CUG, CUU,\\
&UAA, UAG, UCA, UGA, UUA\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The same finite row verifies that this set is the punctured-cube tile with face data
$$
\begin{aligned}
(f_0,f_1,f_2)=(13,16,4).
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the claim is not about an arbitrary list of codons. It is about a specific local codon face system with $13$ vertices, $16$ one-dimensional incidences, and $4$ two-dimensional faces. The readback is label-level only: it asks whether the codons in this tile organize code-table variation, not whether they implement translation or determine protein form.

\paragraph{Shape significance against null rows.}
Two null checks give the tile a nontrivial finite-row status. In the unrestricted shape comparison, $N=2000$ samples were drawn with seed $42$. The sampling required $57377$ distinct-sample attempts and $55377$ duplicate retries. There was $1$ exact shape match, giving
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{\mathrm{exact}}=0.0005.
\end{aligned}
$$
There were $25$ samples with Jaccard score at least $0.9$, giving
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{\mathrm{Jaccard}\ge 0.9}=0.0125.
\end{aligned}
$$
In the amino-acid-class-preserving null, again with $N=2000$ and seed $42$, the observed comparison set size was $20$. The exact-match count was $0$, and the lower-tail count at or below the observed $M$ size was $7$, giving
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{\mathrm{class\ exact}}&=0.0,\\
p_{\mathrm{class\ lower\ tail}}&=0.0035.
\end{aligned}
$$
These rows support the claim that the observed local codon tile is a statistically sharp code-layer pattern under the tested finite nulls. They still do not promote the pattern to a mechanism of translation, a rule of molecular admissibility, or a claim about protein structure.

This is the main code-layer support for the leave-one-code-out conjecture: withholding code-table information leaves most target labels recoverable from the local window-six organization plus the boundary row.

The verified finite rows support the following claim. In the six-bit codon display, the standard-code labels expose a local boundary-tile organization whose $13$-codon set has punctured-cube face data $(13,16,4)$, a stable spectral coordinate profile, statistically rare shape under the tested nulls, and high leave-one-code-out recovery. The conclusion remains deliberately narrow: these data concern code-label readback in a finite codon table. They do not, by themselves, establish translation realization, protein folding, structural order, physical admissibility, biological function, a mechanism of life, or universality across biology.
