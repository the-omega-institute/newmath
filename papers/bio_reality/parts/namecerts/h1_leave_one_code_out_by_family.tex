\paragraph{Local limits.}
No stronger biological statement follows from this row.  The contact is a curated standard-code table, so it tests codon-to-label readback and local label splits only.  It does not test translation realization, folded protein structure, physical admissibility, biological function, a biochemical mechanism, or universality across biological systems.  Amino-acid label sequences obtained from this table are not folded structures.  Cross-organism wording, CUN/UUR notation, or tRNA-Leu language must remain on a separate organism-matched contact route unless a new contact supplies those observations.  The present record is therefore ready as a finite code-layer chapter: it records a strong local geometric and statistical pattern while keeping all higher-layer biological claims outside the scope of the evidence.
