\paragraph{Codon-window record.}
The packet studies the standard-code codon table through a six-coordinate codon display.  Its carrier is the codon cube $Q_6$, whose vertices are codon words after the usual binary expansion of the four nucleotide symbols across three positions.  The finite observation is not a claim about the whole translation apparatus.  It is a code-layer record: a curated table supplies codon-to-label assignments, and the window-six coordinates expose a local tile, a singleton label corner, and a three-point label class inside that finite table.  The contact therefore tests whether the code labels line up with the local split; it does not test ribosomal realization, folded structure, chemical admissibility, biological function, a mechanism, or universality.

The distinguished boundary row has cardinality $13$.  The verified codon set is

\begin{aligned}

$$
The same row is certified as a punctured cube with face-vector
$$

$$
This is the finite geometric object used by the leave-one probes.  It should be read as a local code-table shape: the contact is the curated standard-code table, not an observation of molecular dynamics or protein structure.

\paragraph{Null comparison.}
The finite shape is not treated as visually suggestive evidence alone.  In the shape-significance row, $N=2000$ sampled null tables were evaluated with seed $42$.  There was $1$ exact shape match, giving $p_{\mathrm{exact}}=0.0005$, and $25$ matches at Jaccard threshold at least $0.9$, giving $p_{J\ge 0.9}=0.0125$.  The sampling log records $57377$ distinct-sample attempts and $55377$ duplicate retries, so the finite null calculation is explicitly tied to the sampled table population.  Under the amino-acid-class-preserving null, again with $N=2000$ and seed $42$, the observed $M$-size was $20$, the exact-match count was $0$, the lower-tail count at or below the observed $M$-size was $7$, the exact-shape probability was $0.0$, and the lower-tail probability was $0.0035$.  Thus the local code-table row is separated from both a broad sampled null and an amino-acid-class-preserving null, while still remaining a code-read observation.

Every module has either a predictable or boundary status.  This is the strongest finite internal check in the row: the same $13$ codons appearing in the punctured-cube boundary are exactly exhausted by the module partition used by the codon-level stress test.

\paragraph{Scope of the claim.}
The claim accepted here is narrow.  Window-six codon coordinates, the punctured-cube boundary $R$, the spectral values, the null-model separations, the family hit rates, and the complete module partition together support a local code-table tile for standard-code label readback.  They do not show that DNA or RNA must obey this geometry as a biological law.  They do not establish translation realization, folded protein structure, structural order, physical admissibility, function, mechanism, or universality.  Any such promotion would require a separate contact whose observed data actually live at that higher layer.  The present record stops at the codon-label table: it is a finite code-read result with explicit boundaries around what the contact can and cannot observe.
