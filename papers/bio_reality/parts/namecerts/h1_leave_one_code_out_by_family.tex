\subsection{Family-Stratified Leave-One-Code-Out Recovery in the Window-Six Codon Cube}
\label{sec:bioreality-h1-leave-one-code-out-by-family}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Claim under test.}
The claim studied here is a code-layer recovery statement for the standard genetic-code label table.  Codons are placed in a six-bit codon cube $Q_6$, with the alphabetic codon display treated as a finite coordinate presentation rather than as a biochemical mechanism.  The relevant local object is the boundary-sensitive tile picked out by code-label changes under leave-one-code-out tests.  Its intended readback is only the amino-acid or stop label assigned by a curated standard-code table.  The contact therefore reaches the code-read layer and no further: it does not observe translation, folded structure, physical admissibility, function, organismal mechanism, or a universal biological law.

\paragraph{Finite codon region.}
The distinguished residual region $R$ has cardinality $13$.  The finite codon list is
$$
\begin{aligned}
R=\{&\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\\
&\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UUA}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The same row asserts that this region is a punctured-cube pattern with face-vector
$$
\begin{aligned}
(f_0,f_1,f_2)=(13,16,4).
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the finite object is not merely a list of exceptional codons.  It carries a local combinatorial shape: thirteen vertices, sixteen one-dimensional adjacencies, and four two-dimensional faces inside the window-six display.  This shape is the object whose family behavior is tested below.

The amino-acid-class-preserving null row supplies a second control: among $N=2000$ trials it records zero exact matches, an observed $M$-side size of $20$, and $7$ lower-tail samples at or below the observed size.  The corresponding values are

\end{aligned}
$$
These numbers support the narrower statement that the window-six local shape is unusually concentrated relative to the recorded null procedures.  They do not convert the pattern into a biochemical explanation.

The reported support persists across the named family partition, with the weakest listed family still at $5/6=0.8333333333333334$.

This closes the local bookkeeping for the code-label tile: the thirteen codons are neither left as an unstructured exception list nor spread across an incomplete module assignment.

\paragraph{Scientific reading.}
The result is a finite, code-layer observation: a window-six coordinate representation isolates a thirteen-codon region with a punctured-cube face-vector, nontrivial spectral data, low null-model frequencies, strong aggregate leave-one-code-out recovery, and family-stratified recovery that remains high across five named code families.  The standard-code table is used only as a curated label contact.  It tells which codon has which amino-acid or stop label; it does not tell whether a transcript is realized, whether a peptide folds, whether a molecule is physically admissible, whether a biological function occurs, or whether a universal mechanism has been found.

\paragraph{Local boundary.}
The admissible conclusion is therefore limited.  The family row supports the statement that the local codon-tile boundary remains predictive for code-label readback under the recorded leave-one-code-out protocol.  Any stronger statement would require a separate contact of the appropriate kind.  Translation would require translation evidence, structure would require structural evidence, physical admissibility would require a physical or chemical admissibility contact, function would require a functional assay or equivalent observation, and universality would require a scope argument not present in the finite rows above.
