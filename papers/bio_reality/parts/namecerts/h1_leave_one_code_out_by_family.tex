\paragraph{Reality contact.}
The external contact is a curated standard genetic-code table assigning codons to amino-acid or stop labels.  In this chapter that contact is used only for code-label readback: it says which labels are attached to codon strings in the curated table and allows local split checks against those labels.  The contact does not observe ribosomal execution, kinetic accessibility, folded structure, organism-level fitness, or biochemical function.  Consequently the family-resolved result is a code-layer result: the codon-window geometry aligns strongly with curated label changes across the tested family partition, but the alignment remains a statement about the finite label table.
