\subsection{Family-resolved leave-one-code-out readback}\label{sec:bioreality-h1-leave-one-code-out-by-family}\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Claim.} This record isolates the family-resolved form of the leave-one-code-out test for the standard-code codon table. The finite object under inspection is the six-coordinate codon cube $Q_6$ equipped with a curated codon-to-label table. The claim is intentionally narrow: window-six codon coordinates expose a local boundary-tile pattern for code-layer readback, while the amino-acid and stop labels remain supplied by the curated table contact. The observation is therefore about label recovery inside a finite codon display, not about translation dynamics, folded structure, physical admissibility, biological function, mechanism, or universality.

\paragraph{Finite codon residue.} The distinguished residue $R$ has cardinality $13$. In the run, the residue was exactly
$$
\begin{aligned}
R = \{&AAA, AGA, AGG, AUA, CUA, CUC, CUG, CUU,\\
     &UAA, UAG, UCA, UGA, UUA\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The punctured-cube check returned the same codon list and the face-vector signature
$$
\begin{aligned}
(f_0,f_1,f_2)=(13,16,4).
\end{aligned}
$$
This is the local tile on which the family-resolved test is evaluated. It is not promoted to a global law of biology; it is a finite coordinate pattern that must be read against a separately curated label table.

\paragraph{Spectral and shape controls.} The spectral run supplied the scalar values
$$
\begin{aligned}
\lambda_M &= 0.6752479308830213,\\
\lambda_R &= 0.4758352843197002,\\
\mu &= 3.051487585298128.
\end{aligned}
$$
The reported cubic residual was $-6.224354365258478\times 10^{-12}$, the same value recorded for the radial polynomial check, so the numerical spectral relation is satisfied to the run tolerance. In the shape-significance control with $N=2000$, one exact shape match was observed after $57377$ distinct-sample attempts and $55377$ duplicate retries, giving $p_{\mathrm{exact}}=0.0005$. The high-overlap control counted $25$ samples with Jaccard score at least $0.9$, giving $p_{\mathrm{Jaccard}\ge 0.9}=0.0125$. Under the amino-acid-class-preserving null with $N=2000$, the observed $M$-size was $20$, the exact-shape count was $0$, and $7$ samples fell in the lower tail at or below the observed $M$-size; the reported probabilities were $p_{\mathrm{class\ exact}}=0.0$ and $p_{\mathrm{lower\ tail}}=0.0035$.

\paragraph{Leave-one-code-out signal.} The aggregate leave-one-code-out run evaluated $23$ tables and $63$ targets. Its baseline hit rate was $0.19821739130434785$. The observed hit rate in $M\setminus T$ was $0.9365079365079365$, while adjoining the boundary raised the hit rate to $0.9841269841269841$. The same value, $0.9841269841269841$, was recorded for the hit rate above baseline. Thus the finite tile is not merely a sparse residue: it supplies a strong code-layer recovery pattern once the boundary codons are permitted, and the run records that gain as a measured finite-row effect.

\paragraph{Family stratification.} The family-resolved run grouped the leave-one-code-out targets into $5$ families. The hit rates above baseline were
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{alternative\ yeast} &= 0.8333333333333334,\\
\mathrm{bacterial\ plastid} &= 1.0,\\
\mathrm{candidate\ other} &= 1.0,\\
\mathrm{mitochondrial} &= 1.0,\\
\mathrm{nuclear\ ciliate} &= 1.0.
\end{aligned}
$$
The non-unit value for the alternative-yeast family is therefore not hidden by the aggregate statistic; it is the single family in this finite report whose family rate is below perfect recovery above baseline. The remaining four families are reported at $1.0$ on this metric.

\paragraph{Codon-module coverage.} The leave-one-codon-out module audit assigned all $13$ residue codons and reported no missing, extra, or unassigned codons. The modules were
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{agr} &= \{AGA,AGG\},\\
\mathrm{ar\_lys} &= \{AAA\},\\
\mathrm{cun} &= \{CUA,CUC,CUG,CUU\},\\
\mathrm{ile\_met} &= \{AUA\},\\
\mathrm{ssn} &= \{UCA,UUA\},\\
\mathrm{stop\_trp} &= \{UAA,UAG,UGA\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The module classification summary recorded $R\_\mathrm{size}=13$, $\mathrm{assigned\_count}=13$, and empty lists for extra module codons, missing module codons, and unassigned codons. The related leave-one-codon-out run reported $0$ anchors, $5$ boundary codons, $8$ high-coverage closures, $8$ predictable codons, and recovery rate $1.0$.

\paragraph{Boundary of interpretation.} The curated standard-code table is sufficient for codon-label readback and for local split checks on the six-bit display. It is not an observation of ribosomal translation, protein folding, thermodynamic admissibility, cellular function, organism-wide mechanism, or a universal biological rule. A sequence of amino-acid labels read from the table remains a code-layer sequence. Any claim about structure or function would require a separate contact that directly observes or validates that higher layer. The present record therefore supports a family-resolved code-read pattern for the finite codon tile and simultaneously blocks promotion of that pattern beyond the code layer.
