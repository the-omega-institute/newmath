\paragraph{Reality contact and boundary.}
The only external contact used here is the curated standard-code table, whose observed fact is that codons are assigned to amino-acid or stop labels.  That contact can test code-read claims and local label splits, but it cannot test translation realization, structural order, physical admissibility, folded structure, biological function, or a general mechanism.  A label-sequence readback is therefore not a protein-structure observation.  Cross-organism wording, CUN/UUR notation, and tRNA-Leu language do not turn the code-layer packet into structural evidence.  To cross that boundary, a separate structure, translation, physical, or functional contact would have to be supplied and analyzed on its own terms.
