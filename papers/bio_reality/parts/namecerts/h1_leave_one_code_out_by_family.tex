\subsection{Family-stratified leave-one-code-out behavior in the codon cube}

\paragraph{Finite coordinate setting.}
The test is carried out on the six-bit codon cube $Q_6$, with each codon treated as a coordinate point whose biological label is supplied by the curated standard-code table. The coordinate layer is therefore finite and explicit, while the amino-acid and stop labels are not inferred from the cube alone. The distinguished residual set has cardinality $13$ and is exactly
$$
\begin{aligned}
R=\{&\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\\
&\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UUA}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
As a cubical subcomplex, this same set has recorded face vector
$$
\begin{aligned}
(f_0,f_1,f_2)=(13,16,4).
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the row is not describing a vague codon-table impression: it fixes a concrete punctured-cube pattern with $13$ vertices, $16$ one-dimensional faces, and $4$ two-dimensional faces.

\mu&=3.051487585298128.

The radial cubic check evaluates to $-6.224354365258478\cdot 10^{-12}$, which is within numerical tolerance for the recorded spectral relation. The sampler required $57377$ distinct-sample attempts and incurred $55377$ duplicate retries, so the small tail counts are attached to a concrete finite sampling trace rather than an informal visual comparison. \paragraph{Interpretation at the code-read layer.}
The evidence supports a narrow code-layer statement: window-six codon coordinates expose a local tile and boundary pattern whose leave-one-out behavior persists across the five recorded code-table families. The curated standard-code table supplies the codon-to-label contact, and the finite tests compare coordinate recovery, residual-cube shape, spectral quantities, and family-stratified hit rates. The result is therefore a local statement about codon-label readback in a finite coordinate display.

\paragraph{Local scope boundary.}
Nothing in this row establishes translation realization, folded structure, physical admissibility, biological function, mechanism, or universality. The standard-code table is a label contact, not an observation of folding or biochemical performance. The cube geometry and the family hit rates may guide a code-read hypothesis, but they do not by themselves show that DNA must obey this pattern, that a translated peptide is realized, that a protein folds, that a molecule is physically admissible, or that any organism-level function follows. Any such stronger claim would require a separate contact at the corresponding biological layer.
