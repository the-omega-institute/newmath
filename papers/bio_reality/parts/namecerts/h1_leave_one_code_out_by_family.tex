\subsection{Leave-one-code-out recovery by genetic-code family}
\label{subsec:h1-leave-one-code-out-by-family}

\paragraph{Claim scope.}
This record concerns a finite code-read experiment on the standard-code codon table.  The object under test is not translation as a cellular process and not protein structure; it is the codon-to-label table viewed through six-bit codon coordinates, local tile faces, singleton label corners, and three-point label classes.  The biological contact used here is a curated standard genetic-code table, which supplies codon labels at the code layer.  The test asks whether the local window-six geometry that already isolates a small boundary region remains predictive when whole alternative-code families are left out.  The family-level statement is therefore deliberately narrow: the row may support code-label readback and local boundary classification, but it does not by itself assert folding, physical admissibility, biological function, a translation mechanism, or a universal biological law.

\paragraph{Finite boundary region.}
The verified boundary set $R$ has cardinality $13$.  The listed codons are
$$
\begin{aligned}
R=\{&\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\\
&\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UUA}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The same computation identifies this set as a punctured-cube region with face-vector data $(13,16,4)$.  The module assignment is complete on this boundary: $\mathrm{AGR}=\{\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG}\}$, $\mathrm{ar\_lys}=\{\mathrm{AAA}\}$, $\mathrm{CUN}=\{\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU}\}$, $\mathrm{ile\_met}=\{\mathrm{AUA}\}$, $\mathrm{SSN}=\{\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UUA}\}$, and $\mathrm{stop\_trp}=\{\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UGA}\}$.  The module audit reports $R\_\mathrm{size}=13$, $\mathrm{assigned\_count}=13$, with no missing codons, no extra module codons, and no unassigned codons.  This is the local finite support on which the family leave-out test is allowed to speak.

\paragraph{Spectral and shape checks.}
The shape data give an observed $M$-tile size of $20$ and a significant finite comparison against sampled alternatives.  In the unrestricted shape null, $N=2000$ samples were evaluated after $57377$ distinct-sample attempts and $55377$ duplicate retries.  There was $1$ exact shape match, giving $p_{\mathrm{exact}}=0.0005$, and $25$ samples reached Jaccard score at least $0.9$, giving $p_{J\geq 0.9}=0.0125$.  In the amino-acid-class-preserving null, again with $N=2000$ and seed $42$, the exact-match count was $0$, the lower-tail count at or below the observed $M$ size was $7$, the exact-shape probability was reported as $0.0$, and the lower-tail probability was $0.0035$.  The spectral row gives
$$
\begin{aligned}
\lambda_M &= 0.6752479308830213,\\
\lambda_R &= 0.4758352843197002,\\
\mu &= 3.051487585298128,
\end{aligned}
$$
with the cubic, equivalently radial, polynomial residual recorded as $-6.224354365258478\times 10^{-12}$.  These values are used only as finite diagnostics for the codon-label geometry; they are not promoted to biochemical causation.

\paragraph{Leave-out evidence.}
The whole-code leave-out experiment evaluated $23$ tables and $63$ total targets.  Its baseline hit rate was $0.19821739130434785$.  The hit rate inside $M\setminus T$ was $0.9365079365079365$, while the hit rate after including the boundary was $0.9841269841269841$; the reported rate above baseline was also $0.9841269841269841$.  The codon-level leave-one-out row separately reports $8$ predictable cases, $5$ boundary cases, $0$ anchors, $8$ cases with high closure coverage, and recovery rate $1.0$.

\paragraph{Family stratification.}
The family-level run isolates $5$ code families.  The above-baseline hit rates by family are
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{alternative\_yeast} &= 0.8333333333333334,\\
\mathrm{bacterial\_plastid} &= 1.0,\\
\mathrm{candidate\_other} &= 1.0,\\
\mathrm{mitochondrial} &= 1.0,\\
\mathrm{nuclear\_ciliate} &= 1.0.
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the finite row supports the following restrained reading: after the local codon boundary has been fixed, most family strata recover the left-out code labels at perfect above-baseline rate, and the alternative-yeast stratum remains strongly above baseline but not perfect.  This is evidence for a robust local code-read pattern across curated family partitions, not evidence that the coordinate system realizes translation or protein-level structure.

\paragraph{Local verdict.}
The claim is accepted as a code-read, family-stratified finite record.  Its positive content is the compatibility of the $13$-codon punctured boundary, complete module partition, significant $M$-shape diagnostics, and high leave-out family recovery rates.  Its negative content is equally part of the record: a curated codon table observes labels, not ribosomal realization, not folded structure, not physical admissibility, not function, and not a universal mechanism.  Any future promotion beyond code labels would need an additional contact designed for that higher layer.
