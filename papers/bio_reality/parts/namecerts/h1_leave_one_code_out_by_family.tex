\subsection{Family-wise leave-one-code-out behavior in the codon cube}
\label{sec:bioreality-h1-leave-one-code-out-by-family}

\paragraph{Object and coordinate regime.}
This record concerns the standard-code codon table at the code-read layer. The finite carrier is the window-six codon cube $Q_6$, where a codon is treated as a six-coordinate display over the four nucleotide positions encoded by two local bits per nucleotide. The observed readback is not a claim about translation machinery; it is the curated assignment of codons to amino-acid or stop labels. The biological contact used here is therefore the standard genetic-code label table, and the mathematical question is whether the finite coordinate geometry exposes a stable local tile boundary around the code labels.

The distinguished residual set $R$ has cardinality $13$ and is exactly
$$
\begin{aligned}
R=\{&AAA, AGA, AGG, AUA, CUA, CUC, CUG, CUU,\\
&UAA, UAG, UCA, UGA, UUA\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The punctured-cube calculation returns the same codon set and the face-count vector
$$
\begin{aligned}
(f_0,f_1,f_2)=(13,16,4).
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the local object is not merely a list of exceptional codons: it carries a finite tile shape with thirteen vertices, sixteen one-dimensional faces, and four two-dimensional faces inside the window-six display.

\paragraph{Family-wise leave-one-code-out test.}
The direct family-wise test evaluates five code families. The number of families is
$$
\begin{aligned}
N_{\mathrm{family}}=5,
\end{aligned}
$$
and the measured hit rates above the baseline are
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{alternative\_yeast}&=0.8333333333333334,\\
\mathrm{bacterial\_plastid}&=1.0,\\
\mathrm{candidate\_other}&=1.0,\\
\mathrm{mitochondrial}&=1.0,\\
\mathrm{nuclear\_ciliate}&=1.0.
\end{aligned}
$$
Four families are fully captured by the local boundary criterion, while the alternative-yeast family remains high but not complete. This is the finite-row content of the claim: the family split is strongly concentrated in the window-six local tile, but it is not promoted to a law of biology.

The companion leave-one-code-out calculation gives the broader row-level scale. It evaluates $23$ tables and $63$ targets. The baseline hit rate is $0.19821739130434785$, while the hit rate above baseline is $0.9841269841269841$. The measured hit rate inside $M\setminus T$ is $0.9365079365079365$, and the hit rate inside $M\setminus T$ together with the boundary is again $0.9841269841269841$. In compact form,
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{tables}&=23,\\
\mathrm{targets}&=63,\\
\mathrm{baseline}&=0.19821739130434785,\\
\mathrm{above\ baseline}&=0.9841269841269841,\\
\mathrm{inside}\ M\setminus T&=0.9365079365079365,\\
\mathrm{inside}\ M\setminus T\ \mathrm{or\ boundary}&=0.9841269841269841.
\end{aligned}
$$
These numbers make the family-wise observation interpretable: the family table does not stand alone, but is one finite slice of a larger leave-one-code-out pattern in which the boundary-enhanced local region recovers almost all targets above the baseline.

\paragraph{Spectral and null-model checks.}
The spectral row gives a second finite signature for the same local shape. The recorded values are
$$
\begin{aligned}
\lambda_M&=0.6752479308830213,\\
\lambda_R&=0.4758352843197002,\\
\mu&=3.051487585298128.
\end{aligned}
$$
The radial cubic residual is $-6.224354365258478\times 10^{-12}$, the same value recorded for the cubic-polynomial check, so the numerical witness is at floating-point tolerance rather than at a visible residual scale.

The shape-significance row uses $N=2000$ sampled controls with seed $42$. Among $57377$ distinct-sample attempts, $55377$ duplicate retries were required. Exactly one sampled control matched the observed shape, giving
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{\mathrm{exact}}=0.0005,
\end{aligned}
$$
while $25$ controls reached Jaccard similarity at least $0.9$, giving
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{\mathrm{Jaccard}\ge 0.9}=0.0125.
\end{aligned}
$$
The amino-acid-class-preserving null model is stricter with respect to label class. In $2000$ samples, it reports $0$ exact shape matches, $7$ samples in the lower tail at or below the observed $M$ size, and observed $|M|=20$. The corresponding values are
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{\mathrm{class\ exact}}&=0.0,\\
p_{\mathrm{class\ lower\ tail}}&=0.0035.
\end{aligned}
$$
These checks support the claim that the tile is not a typical accident under the sampled controls. They do not identify a causal mechanism.

\paragraph{Module-level codon accounting.}
The leave-one-codon-out module classification assigns all thirteen residual codons and leaves no extras. The complete partition is
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{agr}&=\{AGA,AGG\},\\
\mathrm{ar\_lys}&=\{AAA\},\\
\mathrm{cun}&=\{CUA,CUC,CUG,CUU\},\\
\mathrm{ile\_met}&=\{AUA\},\\
\mathrm{ssn}&=\{UCA,UUA\},\\
\mathrm{stop\_trp}&=\{UAA,UAG,UGA\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The classification row records $R_{\mathrm{size}}=13$, assigned count $13$, no extra module codons, no missing codons from the modules, and no unassigned codons. The leave-one-codon-out recovery row records anchor count $0$, boundary count $5$, high closure coverage $8$, predictable count $8$, and recovery rate $1.0$. Thus every module has either predictable or boundary support, and the residual codons are exhausted by the six displayed module classes.

\paragraph{Local interpretation.}
The family-wise row is ready as a code-read statement: curated codon labels, when tested through the window-six local geometry, show strong family-level recovery above baseline. The strongest version supported here is finite and local. The standard-code table supplies labels; the coordinate construction supplies the tile; the sampled null rows supply contrast; and the module row supplies the codon-level accounting.

No stronger biological reading follows from these rows. The result does not establish translation realization, folded structure, physical admissibility, biological function, a mechanism, or universality. In particular, an amino-acid label sequence obtained from the code table is still only a label sequence at this contact. Any claim about folding, structure, function, organism-specific translation machinery, tRNA biology, or a universal biological law would require a separate contact of the appropriate kind. The present record therefore remains a bounded code-layer observation: the local codon tile is empirically sharp against the given finite controls and family rows, while higher biological layers remain outside the evidence supplied here.
