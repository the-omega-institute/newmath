\subsection{Family-resolved leave-one-code-out stability for the codon boundary tile}
\label{sec:bioreality-h1-leave-one-code-out-by-family}

\paragraph{Finite carrier and readback contact.}
The record concerns the standard-code codon table viewed through a six-coordinate codon display.  The finite carrier is the codon cube $Q_6$, with the coordinate readout restricted to codon labels supplied by a curated standard genetic-code table.  This contact tells us which codons carry amino-acid or stop labels in the standard code; it does not by itself observe translation dynamics, protein shape, physical admissibility, biological function, or a universal mechanism.  The local question is therefore deliberately narrower: whether the window-six coordinates expose a stable boundary tile in the code-label layer, and whether the same boundary behavior persists when code tables are withheld by family.

\paragraph{Boundary row.}
The distinguished boundary row $R$ has cardinality $13$.  The verified codons are
$$
\begin{aligned}
R=\{&\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\\
&\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UUA}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The same row is verified as a punctured-cube tile with face data
$$
\begin{aligned}
(f_0,f_1,f_2)=(13,16,4).
\end{aligned}
$$
This is the finite local shape against which the leave-one-code-out tests are read.  The row is not being promoted to a law of biology; it is a code-layer boundary pattern whose empirical meaning is limited to label readback against the curated table.

\paragraph{Spectral and shape evidence.}
The finite spectrum separates the observed tile from random-looking alternatives in the tested coordinate system.  The recorded values are
$$
\begin{aligned}
\lambda_M&=0.6752479308830213,\\
\lambda_R&=0.4758352843197002,\\
\mu&=3.051487585298128.
\end{aligned}
$$
The radial cubic check gives residual $-6.224354365258478\times 10^{-12}$, so the polynomial relation is numerically satisfied at the recorded tolerance.  In the shape-significance run with seed $42$ and $N=2000$ sampled tables, there was $1$ exact shape match after $57377$ distinct-sample attempts and $55377$ duplicate retries, giving
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{\mathrm{exact}}&=0.0005,\\
p_{J\geq 0.9}&=0.0125,
\end{aligned}
$$
with $25$ high-Jaccard matches at threshold $0.9$.  Under the amino-acid-class-preserving null model with the same sample count and seed, the observed $M$ size was $20$, the exact-shape count was $0$, and the lower-tail count at or below the observed size was $7$.  The recorded probabilities are
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{\mathrm{class\ exact}}&=0.0,\\
p_{\mathrm{class\ lower\ tail}}&=0.0035.
\end{aligned}
$$
These numbers support a finite coordinate regularity at the label layer.  They do not supply a physical model of translation or folding.

\paragraph{Leave-one-code-out aggregate.}
The aggregate leave-one-code-out experiment evaluated $23$ code tables and $63$ targets.  The baseline hit rate was $0.19821739130434785$.  The observed hit rate above baseline was $0.9841269841269841$, matching the hit rate in $M\setminus T$ plus boundary, while the hit rate in $M\setminus T$ alone was $0.9365079365079365$:
$$
\begin{aligned}
h_{\mathrm{baseline}}&=0.19821739130434785,\\
h_{\mathrm{above}}&=0.9841269841269841,\\
h_{M\setminus T}&=0.9365079365079365,\\
h_{M\setminus T+\partial}&=0.9841269841269841.
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus, when a table is withheld, the local tile plus its boundary almost always recovers the withheld code-layer target beyond the baseline expectation.  This statement is only about codon-label recovery in the finite table experiment.

\paragraph{Family-level stability.}
The family-resolved run grouped the withheld tables into $5$ families.  The verified family hit rates above baseline are
$$
\begin{aligned}
h_{\mathrm{alternative\ yeast}}&=0.8333333333333334,\\
h_{\mathrm{bacterial\ plastid}}&=1.0,\\
h_{\mathrm{candidate\ other}}&=1.0,\\
h_{\mathrm{mitochondrial}}&=1.0,\\
h_{\mathrm{nuclear\ ciliate}}&=1.0.
\end{aligned}
$$
The only non-perfect family in this finite row is alternative yeast, and it still remains well above the baseline rate.  The other four families have complete recovery in this experiment.  The family split therefore strengthens the interpretation that the codon boundary tile is not a single pooled-table artifact, while keeping the conclusion at the code-read layer.

\paragraph{Leave-one-codon-out and module partition.}
The companion leave-one-codon-out row supplies a local consistency check.  It records $0$ anchors, $5$ boundary codons, $8$ high-coverage closure codons, $8$ predictable codons, and recovery rate $1.0$.  The module classification assigns all $13$ codons of $R$ with no missing, extra, or unassigned codons:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{agr}&=\{\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG}\},\\
\mathrm{ar\_lys}&=\{\mathrm{AAA}\},\\
\mathrm{cun}&=\{\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU}\},\\
\mathrm{ile\_met}&=\{\mathrm{AUA}\},\\
\mathrm{ssn}&=\{\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UUA}\},\\
\mathrm{stop\_trp}&=\{\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UGA}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The assignment count is $13$, equal to the row size, with empty missing, extra, and unassigned lists.  Each module has a predictable-or-boundary witness, so the row is internally covered by the finite module account.

\paragraph{Local conclusion and scope limit.}
The claim is ready at the finite code-read level.  The verified data show a $13$-codon punctured-cube boundary row, a numerically stable spectral relation, low random and amino-acid-class-preserving null probabilities, strong aggregate leave-one-code-out recovery, and family-resolved recovery above baseline across all $5$ families.  The result should be read as a finite table-level regularity: window-six codon coordinates expose a local boundary tile whose labels agree strongly with curated code-table readback.  Nothing in this row licenses claims about actual translation realization, folded structure, physical admissibility, biological function, a mechanism of genetic-code evolution, or universality across biology.  Any such promotion would require a separate contact that observes the higher layer directly.
