\subsection{Family-stratified leave-one-code-out recovery}
\label{sec:bioreality-h1-leave-one-code-out-by-family}

\paragraph{Finite packet.}
This packet tests whether the window-six codon coordinate system keeps its local recovery behavior after the held-out targets are grouped by code family.  The carrier is the six-coordinate codon cube $Q_6$ with the standard codon-label table used only as a label contact.  The relevant boundary set is the thirteen-codon set
$$
\begin{aligned}
R=\{&\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\\
&\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UUA}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The independent cardinality row gives $|R|=13$, and the punctured-cube row gives the face-count signature $(13,16,4)$ for this local region.  Thus the object being tested is not an unconstrained codon table lookup: it is a small finite tile with thirteen labeled vertices, sixteen one-dimensional incidences, and four two-dimensional faces, read against curated codon labels.

The same finite construction has a recorded spectral row

\mu&=3.051487585298128,

$$
with cubic residual $-6.224354365258478\times 10^{-12}$, so the numerical certificate is within the reported tolerance of the radial relation used by the packet.  The shape row used $N=2000$ sampled comparators at seed $42$; among $57377$ distinct-sample attempts with $55377$ duplicate retries, there was one exact shape match and $25$ matches with Jaccard score at least $0.9$.  The corresponding finite estimates were $p_{\mathrm{exact}}=0.0005$ and $p_{J\geq 0.9}=0.0125$.  A second amino-acid-class-preserving null row, again with $N=2000$ and seed $42$, recorded observed $|M|=20$, zero exact matches, seven lower-tail samples at or below the observed size, $p_{\mathrm{class,exact}}=0.0$, and $p_{\mathrm{class,lower}}=0.0035$.  These rows make the family analysis meaningful: the family test is being placed on top of a finite local shape that is already sparse under two registered null comparisons.

The same row reports the above-baseline hit rate as $0.9841269841269841$, so the family split does not erase the local-tile signal; it localizes the residual imperfection to a named family bucket.

\paragraph{Reality contact and boundary.}
The only biological contact used here is the curated standard-code table: it assigns codons to amino-acid or stop labels and thereby supports code-layer readback and local label-split checks.  It does not observe translation realization, folded structure, physical admissibility, biochemical function, a mechanism of code origin, or a universal biological law.  The numerical rows above may justify the statement that window-six coordinates expose a stable local boundary-tile pattern across the recorded code-family split.  They do not justify promoting that pattern beyond codon-label readback.  Any claim about translation, folding, admissibility, function, mechanism, or universality would need a separate empirical contact whose measurement actually lives at that higher layer.
