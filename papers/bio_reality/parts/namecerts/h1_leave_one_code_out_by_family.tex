\subsection{Leave-one-code-out recovery by genetic-code family}

\paragraph{Finite object under test.}
The record studies the standard-code codon table through a six-coordinate codon cube $Q_6$.  The biological contact used here is only the curated codon-to-label table: each codon is read as an amino-acid or stop label, and no cellular process is inferred from that table alone.  The finite boundary set $R$ has cardinality $13$ and is recorded as
$$
\begin{aligned}
R=\{&\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\\
&\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UUA}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The punctured-cube computation gives the same $13$ codons and the face-vector summary $(13,16,4)$.  Thus the finite shape being analyzed is not an unconstrained biological mechanism; it is a local combinatorial tile whose vertices are codons from the curated table.

$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{\mathrm{exact}}&=0.0005,\\
p_{\mathrm{Jaccard}\ge 0.9}&=0.0125,

with $25$ samples at Jaccard similarity at least $0.9$.  The run used seed $42$, required $57377$ distinct-sample attempts, and encountered $55377$ duplicate retries. These numbers support a finite local-code pattern relative to the stated sampling designs, not a claim about translation dynamics or molecular structure.

\paragraph{Boundary of interpretation.}
The curated standard-code table supplies codon-to-label observations only.  From the rows above one may say that the six-bit codon coordinate system, the $13$-codon boundary set, the spectral values, the shape tests, the family hit rates, and the complete module assignment together support a local code-read pattern.  One may not conclude that translation has been realized, that a folded protein structure has been predicted, that a physical molecule is admissible, that biological function follows, that a mechanism has been identified, or that the local tile is universal across biology.  Any such stronger statement would require a separate contact carrying the relevant observation, such as translation data, structure data, physical admissibility evidence, or function assays.  Within the present finite row, the claim remains a bounded code-read result for curated codon labels.
