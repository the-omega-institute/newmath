\subsection{Leave-One-Code-Out Family Stratification for the Window-Six Codon Tile}
\label{subsec:h1-leave-one-code-out-by-family}

\paragraph{Object and scope.}
This record concerns the standard-code codon table as a code-layer object.  The finite carrier is the six-coordinate codon cube $Q_6$, read through a standard codon-to-label table whose entries assign each codon either an amino-acid label or a stop label.  The claim tested here is deliberately local: window-six coordinates expose a boundary-tile pattern around a distinguished codon set, and the same pattern remains visible when code tables are grouped by biological family.  The record does not assert that the tile is a law of translation, a structural rule for proteins, a physical admissibility criterion, a function predictor, a mechanism, or a universal biological principle.  Those stronger statements would require separate observations at the corresponding biological layer.

\paragraph{Distinguished codon set.}
The finite row identifies a thirteen-codon set $R$ inside the codon cube.  Its cardinality check reports
$$
\begin{aligned}
|R| &= 13,
\end{aligned}
$$
and the codons are
$$
\begin{aligned}
R = \{&\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UUA}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The same row describes this set as a punctured-cube pattern with face-vector data
$$
\begin{aligned}
(f_0,f_1,f_2) &= (13,16,4).
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the codons being tracked are not a diffuse list of exceptional table entries: they form a finite local tile with thirteen vertices, sixteen one-dimensional incidences, and four two-dimensional faces in the coordinate representation supplied by the experiment.

\paragraph{Spectral and shape checks.}
The spectral summary supplies three scalar quantities.  The reported values are
$$
\begin{aligned}
\lambda_M &= 0.6752479308830213,\\
\lambda_R &= 0.4758352843197002,\\
\mu &= 3.051487585298128.
\end{aligned}
$$
The radial cubic check is numerically closed at the recorded tolerance, with residual
$$
\begin{aligned}
-6.224354365258478\times 10^{-12}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The same residual is reported for the cubic-polynomial evaluation.  These numbers support the statement that the row has a stable coordinate-spectrum signature, while leaving all biochemical interpretation to the curated codon table contact.

\paragraph{Null comparison.}
Against $2000$ sampled alternatives in the shape-significance check, the exact shape reappears once, giving
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{\mathrm{exact}} &= 0.0005.
\end{aligned}
$$
The number of samples with Jaccard overlap at least $0.9$ is $25$, giving
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{J\geq 0.9} &= 0.0125.
\end{aligned}
$$
The sampling run records $57377$ distinct-sample attempts and $55377$ duplicate retries with seed $42$.  A second null model that preserves amino-acid classes also uses $2000$ samples.  It reports an observed $M$ size of $20$, no exact-shape hits, and $7$ lower-tail samples at or below the observed size:
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{\mathrm{class\ exact}} &= 0.0,\\
p_{\mathrm{class\ lower\ tail}} &= 0.0035.
\end{aligned}
$$
The finite row therefore treats the local tile as statistically non-generic relative to the sampled nulls, but the conclusion remains a code-table regularity rather than an organism-level mechanism.

\paragraph{Leave-one-code-out aggregate result.}
The leave-one-code-out experiment evaluates $23$ code tables and $63$ targets.  The baseline hit rate is
$$
\begin{aligned}
0.19821739130434785.
\end{aligned}
$$
The tile-based readback gives a hit rate above baseline of
$$
\begin{aligned}
0.9841269841269841,
\end{aligned}
$$
with the same value after adding the boundary allowance.  Without the boundary addition, the rate inside $M-T$ is
$$
\begin{aligned}
0.9365079365079365.
\end{aligned}
$$
These values are the immediate predecessor for the family-stratified claim: the aggregate signal is not merely above baseline, and the family split checks whether this behavior is concentrated in only one family of code tables.

\paragraph{Family stratification.}
The family-stratified row contains $5$ families.  The recorded hit rates above baseline are
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{alternative\ yeast} &= 0.8333333333333334,\\
\mathrm{bacterial\ plastid} &= 1.0,\\
\mathrm{candidate\ other} &= 1.0,\\
\mathrm{mitochondrial} &= 1.0,\\
\mathrm{nuclear\ ciliate} &= 1.0.
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus four of the five families reach the full recorded rate, while the alternative-yeast family remains above the aggregate baseline by a large margin.  The point of the family split is not to turn the local tile into a universal law.  It only says that, within this finite panel of curated code-table rows, the leave-one-code-out recovery behavior persists after the tables are grouped by family.

\paragraph{Codon modules.}
The leave-one-codon-out module classification assigns all thirteen codons in $R$ to named modules, with no missing codons, no extra module codons, and no unassigned codons.  The row records
$$
\begin{aligned}
R_{\mathrm{size}} &= 13,\\
\mathrm{assigned\ count} &= 13.
\end{aligned}
$$
The module partition is
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{agr} &= \{\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG}\},\\
\mathrm{ar\_lys} &= \{\mathrm{AAA}\},\\
\mathrm{cun} &= \{\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU}\},\\
\mathrm{ile\_met} &= \{\mathrm{AUA}\},\\
\mathrm{ssn} &= \{\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UUA}\},\\
\mathrm{stop\_trp} &= \{\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UGA}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
Every listed module has either predictable recovery or boundary status in the leave-one-codon-out check.  The same check reports $8$ predictable codons, $5$ boundary codons, $0$ anchor codons, high closure coverage $8$, and recovery rate
$$
\begin{aligned}
1.0.
\end{aligned}
$$
This module accounting explains why the family-level leave-one-code-out result is a finite row claim rather than a loose verbal association: the thirteen codons are exhausted by the module list, and the recovery categories cover the modules without orphan entries.

\paragraph{Reality contact and boundary of interpretation.}
The only observation contact used here is a curated standard genetic-code table.  It supports codon-label readback and local label-split checks.  It does not observe ribosomal translation, folded protein structure, energetic admissibility, organismal function, or a universal generative rule.  Therefore an amino-acid label sequence obtained from the table remains a code-layer readback.  It is not a folded structure, not a physical realization claim, and not a functional annotation.  Any later claim about translation, folding, physical constraints, biological function, or universality must attach a separate contact that actually measures or curates that layer.

\paragraph{Verdict.}
The finite family-stratified result is ready as a BioReality chapter-level record.  The evidence consists of a thirteen-codon punctured-cube tile, a closed numerical spectrum, two sampled null comparisons, aggregate leave-one-code-out performance over $23$ tables and $63$ targets, complete thirteen-codon module accounting, and a five-family split in which every family remains above the stated baseline.  The conclusion is restricted to code-layer table readback: window-six geometry supplies a local coordinate pattern around the standard-code labels, while the biological content of those labels comes from the curated table contact.
