\subsection{Family-resolved leave-one-code-out readback}\label{sec:h1-leave-one-code-out-by-family}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Claim.}
This record tests whether the window-six codon coordinate system gives a stable local account of code-label recovery when one code table is withheld and the comparison is stratified by family.  The object under test is the standard-code codon table as a curated codon-to-label contact.  The coordinate carrier is the six-bit codon cube, and the distinctions used in the row are the codon display, the local tile face, the singleton label corner, and the three-point label class.  The claim is deliberately local: the table supplies amino-acid and stop labels, while the row asks whether the local codon-tile geometry continues to predict code-layer readback after one code table is left out.

\paragraph{Finite support.}
The distinguished reassignment set has thirteen codons.  In the verified row it is
$\{\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UUA}\}$,
so $|R|=13$.  The same row identifies this set with the punctured-cube pattern and gives the face-vector
$$\begin{aligned}
f(R)=(13,16,4).
\end{aligned}$$
The spectral row reports three numerical invariants:
$$\begin{aligned}
\lambda_M &= 0.6752479308830213,\\
\lambda_R &= 0.4758352843197002,\\
\mu &= 3.051487585298128.
\end{aligned}$$
The radial cubic residual is $-6.224354365258478\times 10^{-12}$, so the recorded value is at numerical zero at the precision of the run.  The finite data therefore combine a thirteen-codon boundary set, a four-face punctured-cube signature, and a stable spectral readout.

\paragraph{Shape controls.}
The coordinate shape is not treated as a free narrative fit.  Against $2000$ sampled controls, with seed $42$, the exact-shape match count was $1$, giving $p_{\mathrm{exact}}=0.0005$.  The high-overlap threshold gave $25$ matches with Jaccard score at least $0.9$, hence $p_{J\geq 0.9}=0.0125$.  The same run records $57377$ distinct-sample attempts and $55377$ duplicate retries, which matters because the control space was being sampled for distinct rows rather than merely for repeated draws.  In the amino-acid-class-preserving null, the observed module size was $|M|=20$, the exact match count was $0$ out of $2000$, and the lower-tail count at or below the observed size was $7$.  Thus the recorded probabilities are
$$\begin{aligned}
p_{\mathrm{aa\ class\ exact}} &= 0.0,\\
p_{\mathrm{aa\ class\ lower\ tail}} &= 0.0035.
\end{aligned}$$
These controls support the narrower reading that the observed local geometry is unusually structured relative to the tested finite nulls.

\paragraph{Leave-one-code-out family row.}
The global leave-one-code-out experiment evaluated $23$ tables and $63$ total targets.  Its baseline hit rate was $0.19821739130434785$.  The hit rate inside $M-T$ was $0.9365079365079365$, and the hit rate inside $M-T$ plus the boundary was $0.9841269841269841$.  The family-resolved row keeps the same code-read interpretation and splits the above-baseline behavior into five families:
$$\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{alternative\ yeast} &:\ 0.8333333333333334,\\
\mathrm{bacterial\ plastid} &:\ 1.0,\\
\mathrm{candidate\ other} &:\ 1.0,\\
\mathrm{mitochondrial} &:\ 1.0,\\
\mathrm{nuclear\ ciliate} &:\ 1.0.
\end{aligned}$$
The family count is therefore $5$.  Four families are at full above-baseline recovery in the recorded row, while the alternative-yeast family remains above baseline at five sixths.  This is the chapter-specific content of the claim: family stratification does not erase the local boundary-tile signal.

\paragraph{Reality contact and boundary.}
The only biological contact used here is the curated standard genetic-code table, read as a codon-label table.  It is adequate for testing code-layer readback and local label splits.  It is not a measurement of translation dynamics, folded protein structure, physical admissibility, biological function, a mechanism of codon assignment, or a universal law of biology.  No such higher-layer claim follows from the numbers above unless a separate contact supplies the relevant observation.  In particular, amino-acid label sequences do not become fold data, CUN or UUR wording does not become structural evidence, and tRNA-Leu notation does not by itself supply an organism-matched mechanism.  The proper conclusion is therefore positive but bounded: the finite rows support a family-resolved local code-read pattern in the six-bit codon cube, while blocking promotion to translation, folding, physical admissibility, function, mechanism, or universality.
