\paragraph{Finite carrier and contact.}
The object studied here is the standard-code codon table as a finite label readout on the six-coordinate codon cube $Q_6$.  Each codon is treated as a six-bit display point, with local face incidence and label-class information read against a curated codon-to-amino-acid-or-stop table.  The biological contact used in this section is only the curated standard-code assignment table: it says which codons receive which labels, but it does not observe translation dynamics, molecular folding, physical viability, biological function, or a universal mechanism.  The row is therefore a code-layer recovery test, not a claim about protein realization.

The distinguished residual set $R$ has cardinality $13$.  The verified codons are
$$
\begin{aligned}
R=\{&\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\\
&\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UUA}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The same row verifies that this set has the punctured-cube face data
$$
\begin{aligned}
(f_0,f_1,f_2)=(13,16,4).
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the code-read pattern is not merely a list of exceptional codons; it has a small local cubical shape.  The companion module classification partitions all $13$ codons of $R$ with no omissions and no extras: $\mathrm{agr}=\{\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG}\}$, $\mathrm{ar\_lys}=\{\mathrm{AAA}\}$, $\mathrm{cun}=\{\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU}\}$, $\mathrm{ile\_met}=\{\mathrm{AUA}\}$, $\mathrm{ssn}=\{\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UUA}\}$, and $\mathrm{stop\_trp}=\{\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UGA}\}$.  The verified accounting is
$$
\begin{aligned}
|R|=13,\qquad \mathrm{assigned\_count}=13,\qquad
\mathrm{missing}=0,\qquad \mathrm{extra}=0.
\end{aligned}
$$

\paragraph{Spectral and shape evidence.}
The finite geometry also has a checked spectral profile.  The reported numerical values are
$$
\begin{aligned}
\lambda_M&=0.6752479308830213,\\
\lambda_R&=0.4758352843197002,\\
\mu&=3.051487585298128.
\end{aligned}
$$
The radial cubic check is numerically closed at the displayed tolerance scale: the polynomial value is $-6.224354365258478\times 10^{-12}$, and the recorded spectral-radius value is again $0.6752479308830213$.  This is evidence for a stable finite coordinate pattern inside the codon cube, not evidence for any downstream biological layer.

The shape comparison against sampled alternatives is also finite and explicit.  In the $M$-shape experiment, $N=2000$ sampled tables were considered, with $57377$ distinct-sample attempts and $55377$ duplicate retries.  Exactly one sampled table matched the observed shape, giving
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{\mathrm{exact}}=0.0005.
\end{aligned}
$$
There were $25$ matches at Jaccard score at least $0.9$, giving
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{\mathrm{Jaccard}\ge 0.9}=0.0125.
\end{aligned}
$$
A second null model preserving amino-acid classes used $N=2000$ trials and observed $M$-size $20$.  It produced $0$ exact-shape matches and $7$ lower-tail samples at or below the observed $M$ size, so the recorded finite probabilities are
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{\mathrm{aa\ class\ exact}}=0.0,\qquad
p_{\mathrm{aa\ class\ lower\ tail}}=0.0035.
\end{aligned}
$$
These values support the claim that the local codon-tile geometry is not a typical draw under the tested finite nulls, while still leaving the curated label table as the only biological contact in this row.

\paragraph{Leave-one-code-out recovery.}
The leave-one-code-out experiment evaluated $23$ genetic-code tables and $63$ total targets.  The baseline hit rate was $0.19821739130434785$.  Against that baseline, the verified recovery rates were
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{hit\_rate\_above\_baseline}&=0.9841269841269841,\\
\mathrm{hit\_rate\_in\_M\setminus T}&=0.9365079365079365,\\
\mathrm{hit\_rate\_in\_M\setminus T\ plus\ boundary}&=0.9841269841269841.
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus, when one code-level row is withheld, the finite coordinate and boundary information recover almost all withheld targets above the baseline rate.  The claim is deliberately narrow: the recovery concerns codon-label positions in the code table, not the molecular process that makes a translation event occur.

The family-stratified row sharpens this recovery claim.  There are $5$ code families in the verified family split, with hit rates above baseline
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{alternative\_yeast}&=0.8333333333333334,\\
\mathrm{bacterial\_plastid}&=1.0,\\
\mathrm{candidate\_other}&=1.0,\\
\mathrm{mitochondrial}&=1.0,\\
\mathrm{nuclear\_ciliate}&=1.0.
\end{aligned}
$$
Four of the five families are fully recovered above baseline, and the remaining alternative-yeast family is recovered at five sixths.  This is the chapter-specific content of claim $\mathrm{h1.leave\_one\_code\_out.by\_family}$: the local six-coordinate codon geometry has family-level predictive strength for code-label readback.

\paragraph{Codon-level boundary check.}
A separate leave-one-codon-out row records $0$ anchors, $5$ boundary codons, $8$ high-coverage closure codons, and $8$ predictable codons, with recovery rate
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{recovery\_rate}=1.0.
\end{aligned}
$$
Together with the complete module assignment of $R$, this says that the finite local pattern is closed enough to classify the observed residual modules at the code layer.  In particular, every listed module has either predictable or boundary support.  The result is strongest where it stays finite: it classifies the displayed codon modules and recovers withheld code-table labels under the stated experiments.

\paragraph{Local boundary of the claim.}
The curated standard-code table provides codon-to-label readback only.  No result in this section establishes that DNA must obey this six-coordinate geometry as a biological necessity.  No result here establishes translation realization, folded protein structure, structural order, physical admissibility, biological function, evolutionary mechanism, or universality across all biological systems.  Amino-acid label sequences are not folded proteins, and CUN, UUR, AGR, stop-Trp, Ile-Met, or Lys-related module names are not themselves structure measurements.  Any such stronger assertion would require a separate contact that actually observes the relevant layer.

The verdict of this finite row is therefore precise.  The codon cube, the residual set $R$, the punctured-cube face vector $(13,16,4)$, the spectral values, the shape-null probabilities, the $23$-table leave-one-code-out experiment, and the $5$-family stratification jointly support a local code-read claim: window-six coordinates expose a reproducible boundary-tile structure in the standard-code label table.  The same data also enforce the stopping rule: code-label recovery remains code-label recovery until an independent biological contact supplies translation, folding, physical admissibility, function, mechanism, or universality.
