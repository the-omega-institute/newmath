\paragraph{Finite code-read setting.}
This record studies the standard-code codon table only at the label-readout layer. The carrier is the six-coordinate codon cube $Q_6$, where each codon is read as a six-bit display and the observed labels are supplied by the curated standard-code table. The internal data used here are coordinate position, closure membership, spectral response, and finite relations among the locally exposed codons. The biological datum is therefore a table of assignments from codons to amino-acid or stop labels, not a translation apparatus and not a protein-structure observation.

\begin{aligned}

The table contact fixes the labels assigned to these codons; it does not by itself explain why the contact labels should be realized by any downstream biochemical process.

\paragraph{Local verdict and boundary.}
The finite rows support the following code-layer statement: in the six-bit codon display, the standard-code labels expose a thirteen-codon punctured local tile whose spectrum, null-model behavior, leave-one-code-out recovery, and module classification are mutually consistent with a family-resolved boundary pattern. The curated standard-code table is the only reality contact used here. It supports codon-to-label readback and local split checks. It does not observe translation realization, folded protein structure, structural order, physical admissibility, biological function, universal mechanism, or a law of biology. Any move from this code-layer pattern to those higher claims would require a separate contact designed for that layer.
