\subsection{Leave-one-code-out recovery by code family}
\label{sec:bioreality-h1-leave-one-code-out-by-family}

\paragraph{Claim site.}
This record concerns the finite codon-window claim named \texttt{h1.leave\_one\_code\_out.by\_family}.  The object under test is the standard-code codon table viewed only at the code-read layer: codons are read as curated amino-acid or stop labels, while the six-bit codon display supplies a finite coordinate arena for local comparison.  The claim is not that codon geometry is a biological mechanism.  It is the narrower assertion that, when one genetic-code table is held out and compared against the local tile derived from the standard-code readback, the family-level recovery rates remain high above the reported baseline across the five observed table families.

\paragraph{Finite coordinate carrier.}
The codon carrier is the window-six cube $Q_6$, with codon labels supplied by the curated standard-code table.  In the verified row \texttt{h0.R.cardinality.13}, the distinguished boundary set $R$ has cardinality $13$ and is exactly
$$
\begin{aligned}
R=\{&\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\\
&\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UUA}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The companion row \texttt{h0.R.punctured.cube} reports the same codon list and the face-count vector $(13,16,4)$ for the punctured-cube shape.  Thus the local object being used in this chapter is not an informal cluster: it is a finite codon subset, with a fixed list of thirteen codons and a recorded tile profile.

\paragraph{Shape controls.}
The shape-significance row \texttt{h0.M.shape.significance} reports $N=2000$ randomized trials with $57377$ distinct-sample attempts and $55377$ duplicate retries.  The exact-shape count is $1$, giving $p_{\mathrm{exact}}=0.0005$, while the high-overlap count at Jaccard threshold $0.9$ is $25$, giving $p_{J\geq 0.9}=0.0125$.  A second control, \texttt{h0.null.model.aa\_class\_preserving}, preserves amino-acid class information and reports observed $M$-size $20$, exact-match count $0$, lower-tail count $7$, exact-shape probability $0.0$, and lower-tail probability $0.0035$ over $N=2000$ trials.  These controls say that the displayed tile is a nontrivial finite pattern under the recorded null models.  They do not say that the pattern is a law of molecular biology.

\paragraph{Readback interpretation.}
The curated standard-code table is the reality contact used in this chapter.  It supplies codon-to-label assignments and permits code-layer readback checks.  The contact does not observe ribosomal translation, folded structure, thermodynamic admissibility, cellular function, or any universal generative law.  Consequently, the numerical success of the family-level leave-one-code-out test supports only this bounded statement: the window-six codon coordinates expose a stable local boundary-tile pattern for curated label tables, with high family-stratified recovery in the recorded rows.
