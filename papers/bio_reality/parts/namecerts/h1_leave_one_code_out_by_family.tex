\paragraph{Reality contact and scope.}
The only external contact used here is the curated standard-code table.  It supports code-label readback and local split checks.  It does not observe translation realization, folded structure, physical admissibility, biological function, or a universal mechanism.  The local tile may expose a boundary pattern inside the six-coordinate codon display, and the family leave-one-code-out row shows strong finite recovery across the five recorded families, but those conclusions remain at the code-read layer.  Amino-acid label sequences are labels in the table; they are not protein conformations.  CUN, UUR, stop, tryptophan, lysine, isoleucine, methionine, arginine, and serine groupings are used here as codon-label modules, not as evidence of molecular dynamics.  Any claim about translation, folding, structural order, physical admissibility, function, organism-level necessity, or universality would require a separate contact that directly measures that higher layer.
