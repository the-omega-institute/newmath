\paragraph{Scope boundary.}
The curated standard-code table supplies the only external contact used here.  It supports codon-to-label readback and local split checks.  It does not contain an observation of ribosomal realization, molecular folding, physical admissibility, biological function, or global universality.  Consequently, the result may be stated as follows: in the six-coordinate codon window, the thirteen-codon local boundary set has a stable module decomposition and strong leave-one-code-out recovery across five recorded families, with family hit rates $0.8333333333333334,1.0,1.0,1.0,1.0$ and aggregate above-baseline rate $0.9841269841269841$.  No higher-layer biological conclusion follows from this packet unless another contact, independent of the curated code-label table, supplies the missing translation, structure, physical, functional, or mechanistic evidence.
