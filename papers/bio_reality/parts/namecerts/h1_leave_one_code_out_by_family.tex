\subsection{Leave-One-Code-Out Stability by Code Family}\label{sec:bioreality-h1-leave-one-code-out-by-family}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite question.}
This record tests whether the window-six codon coordinate picture remains predictive when complete code tables are held out by biological family.  The object under inspection is the standard-code label table at the code-read layer: codons are read as amino-acid or stop labels, and no claim is made about the biochemical implementation of translation.  The finite carrier is the six-bit codon cube $Q_6$, equipped here only with coordinate, closure, spectral, and relation data sufficient to compare local tile faces, singleton label corners, and three-point label classes.  The central finite set is the distinguished relation row
$$
\begin{aligned}
R = \{&AAA, AGA, AGG, AUA, CUA, CUC, CUG, CUU,\\
     &UAA, UAG, UCA, UGA, UUA\},
\end{aligned}
$$
with $|R|=13$.  In the punctured-cube computation this same row has the face-vector
$$
\begin{aligned}
(f_0,f_1,f_2)=(13,16,4),
\end{aligned}
$$
so the row is not being treated as an informal codon list, but as a finite subcomplex of the codon cube.

\paragraph{Family-stratified holdout result.}
The by-family leave-one-code-out run evaluated $5$ code families.  Its reported family-level hit rates above the baseline were
$$
\begin{aligned}
&\operatorname{hit}(\mathrm{alternative\_yeast})=0.8333333333333334,\\
&\operatorname{hit}(\mathrm{bacterial\_plastid})=1.0,\\
&\operatorname{hit}(\mathrm{candidate\_other})=1.0,\\
&\operatorname{hit}(\mathrm{mitochondrial})=1.0,\\
&\operatorname{hit}(\mathrm{nuclear\_ciliate})=1.0.
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus four of the five family rows are perfect on this statistic, while the alternative-yeast family is still well above the baseline comparator.  The companion non-stratified leave-one-code-out experiment evaluated $23$ tables and $63$ targets.  Its baseline hit rate was $0.19821739130434785$, while the observed rates were
$$
\begin{aligned}
&\operatorname{hit}(M\setminus T)=0.9365079365079365,\\
&\operatorname{hit}(M\setminus T\;\mathrm{plus\ boundary})=0.9841269841269841,\\
&\operatorname{hit}\_\mathrm{above\ baseline}=0.9841269841269841.
\end{aligned}
$$
The family stratification therefore does not erase the signal seen in the pooled table run; it localizes the only visible weakening to the alternative-yeast row while preserving perfect rates for the other four listed families.

\paragraph{Codon-module accounting.}
The leave-one-codon-out module check partitions all $13$ relation codons without residue.  The modules are
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{AGR} &= \{AGA,AGG\},\\
\mathrm{AR\_Lys} &= \{AAA\},\\
\mathrm{CUN} &= \{CUA,CUC,CUG,CUU\},\\
\mathrm{Ile\_Met} &= \{AUA\},\\
\mathrm{SSN} &= \{UCA,UUA\},\\
\mathrm{Stop\_Trp} &= \{UAA,UAG,UGA\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The classification reports $R\_\mathrm{size}=13$, $\mathrm{assigned\_count}=13$, no missing module codons, no extra module codons, and no unassigned codons.  Every module contains a codon that is either predictable or boundary-classed in the leave-one-codon-out run.  The same run reports $8$ predictable codons, $5$ boundary codons, $0$ anchor codons, $8$ cases of high closure coverage, and recovery rate $1.0$.  This matters for the family holdout because the row is not merely successful as an undifferentiated list: the success survives a named module decomposition of AGR, AR-Lys, CUN, Ile-Met, SSN, and Stop-Trp behavior.

\paragraph{Spectral and null-model checks.}
The finite spectral summary gives
$$
\begin{aligned}
\lambda_M &= 0.6752479308830213,\\
\lambda_R &= 0.4758352843197002,\\
\mu &= 3.051487585298128,
\end{aligned}
$$
and the radial cubic residual is $-6.224354365258478\times 10^{-12}$, the same value reported for the cubic-polynomial check.  The observed $M$ row has size $20$.  Under the shape-significance null run with $N=2000$, seed $42$, and $57377$ distinct-sample attempts, there was $1$ exact shape match and $25$ matches with Jaccard score at least $0.9$, giving
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{\mathrm{exact}} &= 0.0005,\\
p_{\mathrm{Jaccard}\ge 0.9} &= 0.0125.
\end{aligned}
$$
The amino-acid-class-preserving null run, also with $N=2000$ and seed $42$, found $0$ exact matches, $7$ samples in the lower tail at or below the observed $M$ size, and
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{\mathrm{class\ exact}} &= 0.0,\\
p_{\mathrm{class\ lower\ tail}} &= 0.0035.
\end{aligned}
$$
These finite comparisons support the claim that the row geometry and its family-stratified predictive behavior are not explained by the tested randomization baselines alone.

\paragraph{Reality contact and scope.}
The only empirical contact used here is a curated standard genetic-code table assigning codons to amino-acid or stop labels.  This contact tests code-layer readback and local codon-tile label splits.  It does not test ribosomal realization, folded protein geometry, physical admissibility, biological function, mechanistic necessity, or universality across biology.  Consequently the result is a finite code-read statement: window-six coordinates expose a stable local boundary-tile pattern for the standard-code label table and related family holdouts.  Any later assertion about translation, structure, admissibility, function, mechanism, or universal law would require a separate observation channel built for that higher layer.
