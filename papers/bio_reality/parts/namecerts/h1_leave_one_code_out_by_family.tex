\paragraph{Reality contact and scope.}
The only empirical contact used here is the curated standard-code table, treated as a codon-to-label source.  It can test code-layer label readback and the local split of codon labels inside the six-coordinate display.  It does not test translation as a realized biochemical process, folded structure, structural order, physical admissibility, cellular function, evolutionary mechanism, or universality across biology.  An amino-acid label sequence obtained from the table remains a label sequence; it is not a protein structure and it is not evidence of function.  Any passage from this finite code-layer result to structure, mechanism, or organism-level biology would require a separate contact designed for that layer.
