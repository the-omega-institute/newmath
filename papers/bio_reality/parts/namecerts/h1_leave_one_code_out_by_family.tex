\subsection{Family-resolved leave-one-code-out readback for the window-six codon tile}
\label{sec:h1-leave-one-code-out-by-family}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite object under test.}
The record concerns the standard-code codon table as a finite labelled object on the six-coordinate codon cube.  The coordinates are used only as a compact display of codon labels and local tile adjacency.  The reality contact is the curated codon-to-label table: each of the $64$ codons has an externally supplied amino-acid or stop label, and the present test asks whether the local tile pattern continues to recover held-out code-label changes when the alternative tables are grouped into biological families.  The construction therefore lives at the code-read layer.  It does not by itself observe ribosomal translation, folded protein structure, physical admissibility, biological function, a mechanism, or a universal biological rule.

\paragraph{Boundary set and punctured-cube shape.}
The finite boundary set $R$ has cardinality $13$ and is exactly
$$
\begin{aligned}
R=\{&\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\\
&\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UUA}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The same row reports the punctured-cube face count vector
$$
\begin{aligned}
(f_0,f_1,f_2)=(13,16,4),
\end{aligned}
$$
so the selected codons are not being treated as a free list of labels.  They occupy a finite local subcomplex inside the six-bit codon display.  This is the geometric reason for asking whether code-table perturbations concentrate near the selected tile rather than scattering uniformly over all codons.

\paragraph{Spectral and sampling checks.}
The spectral diagnostic attached to the same finite display gives
$$
\begin{aligned}
\lambda_M&=0.6752479308830213,\\
\lambda_R&=0.4758352843197002,\\
\mu&=3.051487585298128,
\end{aligned}
$$
with cubic and radial residual $-6.224354365258478\times 10^{-12}$.  Thus the numerical row is stable at the stated tolerance, not merely a visual codon drawing.  In the shape-randomization check with $N=2000$ samples and seed $42$, there was $1$ exact shape match, giving $p_{\mathrm{exact}}=0.0005$, and $25$ samples with Jaccard overlap at least $0.9$, giving $p_{\mathrm{Jaccard}\ge 0.9}=0.0125$.  The sampler attempted $57377$ distinct draws and recorded $55377$ duplicate retries, so the displayed rarity is tied to a finite sampling run rather than an asymptotic assertion.  Under the amino-acid-class-preserving null with $N=2000$ and seed $42$, the observed $M$ size was $20$, the exact-shape count was $0$, and the lower-tail count at or below the observed size was $7$, giving $p_{\mathrm{exact}}=0$ and $p_{\mathrm{lower\ tail}}=0.0035$.

\paragraph{Leave-one-code-out recovery.}
The global leave-one-code-out row evaluated $23$ alternative code tables with $63$ held-out targets.  The baseline hit rate was $0.19821739130434785$.  The local classifier reached hit rate $0.9365079365079365$ inside $M\setminus T$ and $0.9841269841269841$ inside $M\setminus T$ with the boundary included; the reported hit rate above baseline was also $0.9841269841269841$.  These numbers support a code-layer statement: when one code table is held out, the local codon tile and its boundary usually contain the target code-label shift.  They do not state that the codon geometry causes the biological reassignment or that the reassignment is physically realized by the coordinate system.

\paragraph{Family-resolved row.}
The family analysis partitions the evaluated code-table changes into $5$ families.  The reported family hit rates above the same baseline are
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{alternative\_yeast}&:0.8333333333333334,\\
\mathrm{bacterial\_plastid}&:1.0,\\
\mathrm{candidate\_other}&:1.0,\\
\mathrm{mitochondrial}&:1.0,\\
\mathrm{nuclear\_ciliate}&:1.0.
\end{aligned}
$$
The non-uniform family names matter because the row is not just a pooled success statistic.  Four of the five families are perfect under this finite criterion, while the alternative-yeast family is still far above the pooled baseline but has one unresolved miss in the family-normalized accounting.  The row therefore sharpens the previous leave-one-code-out result: the local tile is broadly stable across named code-table families, with the alternative-yeast family carrying the only visible slack in the family summary.

\paragraph{Module-level localization.}
The leave-one-codon-out module record explains why the family row is a local-boundary result rather than a whole-table theorem.  The $13$ codons of $R$ are completely assigned to six modules:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{agr}&=\{\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG}\},\\
\mathrm{ar\_lys}&=\{\mathrm{AAA}\},\\
\mathrm{cun}&=\{\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU}\},\\
\mathrm{ile\_met}&=\{\mathrm{AUA}\},\\
\mathrm{ssn}&=\{\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UUA}\},\\
\mathrm{stop\_trp}&=\{\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UGA}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The module audit reports $R$ size $13$, assigned count $13$, no extra module codons, no missing codons, and no unassigned codons.  The leave-one-codon-out row has anchor count $0$, boundary count $5$, predictable count $8$, high closure coverage $8$, and recovery rate $1.0$.  Thus every module has either predictable or boundary support, but no module is upgraded to a claim about protein folding, structural order, admissible chemistry, realized function, or organism-independent mechanism.

\paragraph{Local conclusion and blocked promotions.}
The finite evidence supports the following restricted conclusion.  In the six-coordinate codon display, the curated standard-code labels expose a $13$-codon punctured-cube boundary whose shape is rare under the reported null samplers, whose spectrum is numerically stable, and whose leave-one-code-out behavior remains strong after grouping alternative codes into five named families.  The strongest family-resolved statement is that the boundary-based code-read test has family hit rates $1.0,1.0,1.0,1.0$, and $0.8333333333333334$ against a pooled baseline $0.19821739130434785$.  The weakest point is exactly the intended guardrail: all support is label-table support.  Without an additional contact that directly observes translation, folding, physical admissibility, biological function, mechanism, or universality, the chapter asserts only a finite local code-read regularity.
