\subsection{m6A site-load and the mRNA half-life boundary in GSE163500}\label{sec:bioreality-m6a-site-load-mrna-halflife-boundary-gse163500}

\paragraph{Finite panel.}
The record is a finite cross-layer RNA-stability panel, not a claim about protein output or organismal adaptation. Its rows are joined gene-level observations in which an m6A site-load coordinate is read beside an mRNA half-life readout under the GSE163500 perturbation surface. The carrier is the matched table of genes that survive identifier joining, m6A coverage filtering, half-life availability, and length annotation. In the verified run the retained table contains $5837$ genes. The stated coverage rule is that $\min(\mathrm{Mean\ T\ coverage}_{\mathrm{DMSO}},\mathrm{Mean\ T\ coverage}_{\mathrm{STM2457}})$ must lie at or above the $20$th percentile among valid joined genes. Thus the finite object is not an unconstrained transcriptome-wide slogan: it is the set of rows passing this declared coverage threshold and then carrying the paired m6A-load and half-life fields.

\paragraph{Coordinates and readout.}
For each retained row $g$, the coordinate coordinate is the measured m6A site-load summary, and the RNA-layer readout is the log half-life value or perturbation-linked change in log half-life. The relevant finite variables may be written as
$$
\begin{aligned}
R_g &= (L_g, H^{\mathrm{DMSO}}_g, \Delta H_g, \ell_g, C_g),\\
L_g &= \text{m6A site-load coordinate for gene } g,\\
H^{\mathrm{DMSO}}_g &= \text{static log half-life in the DMSO condition},\\
\Delta H_g &= \text{change in log half-life across the STM2457 boundary},\\
\ell_g &= \text{length covariate},\\
C_g &= \text{coverage eligibility indicator under the declared rule}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The verified static statistic is a Spearman association between m6A load and DMSO log half-life, with value $-0.388929$. The perturbation-facing statistic is positive: the Spearman association between m6A load and $\Delta\log_2$ half-life is $0.245804$. After residualizing the length coordinate, the corresponding partial Spearman value is $0.227683$. The two-sided partial permutation value is $0.000999001$. These numbers license a bounded RNA half-life boundary crossing for this finite panel, because the observed relation is not removed by the length-only partialization used in the registered statistic and the permutation tail is below $10^{-3}$ at the reported resolution.

\paragraph{Boundary statistic.}
The finite assertion is the existence of a nonzero row-level RNA-stability readout associated with the m6A-load coordinate under the declared filtering and partialization scheme. A compact way to state the checked finite statistic is
$$
\begin{aligned}
\rho_{\mathrm{static}} &= \rho_{S}(L_g,H^{\mathrm{DMSO}}_g)=-0.388929,\\
\rho_{\mathrm{perturb}} &= \rho_{S}(L_g,\Delta H_g)=0.245804,\\
\rho_{\mathrm{length\ partial}} &= \rho_{S}(L_g,\Delta H_g\mid \ell_g)=0.227683,\\
p_{\mathrm{perm},2\mathrm{sided}} &= 0.000999001,\\
N_{\mathrm{genes}} &= 5837.
\end{aligned}
$$
The sign change between the static DMSO association and the perturbation-linked half-life change is treated only as a finite RNA-layer boundary phenomenon. It is not promoted to an m6A reader mechanism, a translation mechanism, a protein abundance claim, a folding claim, or a biological function claim. The local content is that, on this filtered finite table, m6A site-load is measurably related to RNA half-life behavior.

\paragraph{Relation and trigger.}
The relation component is the ordered comparison between high and low m6A-load labels after the stated row filters and the length partial statistic. The trigger component is the perturbation surface associated with STM2457, read only through the finite half-life change statistic. The event named by the verdict is therefore not the assertion that a particular RNA-binding protein, translation state, protein fold, or cellular phenotype has been identified. It is the weaker and more local assertion that the m6A-load coordinate has crossed from a purely descriptive RNA mark count into a measured RNA half-life boundary on the same joined rows.

\paragraph{Local refusal conditions.}
The positive verdict is broken if the high- and low-coordinate rows become exchangeable for RNA half-life after the declared controls, if the reported joined table cannot be reconstructed under the coverage rule, or if the effect depends only on a batch, transcript-length, identifier-mapping, or abundance artifact. It is also broken by any attempt to read the half-life association as a translation readout. RNA half-life is an RNA-layer quantity. A translation statement would require same-row ribosome occupancy or translation-efficiency data, matched mRNA abundance controls, and an explicitly computed translation statistic. A decay-factor RBP mechanism statement would require measured local RBP occupancy, coordinate-to-occupancy decoupling, target-RBP loss, wild-type rescue, and binding-defective or non-target controls. None of those mechanism rows are supplied by the reported finite statistic.
