\subsection{Methylation load and RNA half-life as a bounded cross-layer RNA-stability record}
\label{subsec:bior-reality-m6a-site-load-half-life-boundary}

\paragraph{Finite row object.}
The record considered here is a finite table of coding-transcript rows in which an mRNA half-life readout is joined to an $m^6A$ site-load coordinate and to coverage filters from the same comparison. The subject is not a free statement about RNA modification in general. It is the narrower claim that, after the declared joins and filters, methylation load has enough residual contact with RNA half-life to cross a pre-registered RNA-stability boundary. The active run retained $5837$ genes. The coverage rule was
$$
\begin{aligned}
\min(\mathrm{Mean\ T\ coverage\ in\ DMSO},\mathrm{Mean\ T\ coverage\ in\ STM2457})
\geq \text{the twentieth percentile among valid joined genes}.
\end{aligned}
$$
This makes the carrier a measured finite-row panel: each retained row supplies an $m^6A$-load coordinate, a DMSO half-life readout, a perturbation-associated half-life difference, and the length control used by the partial statistic.

\paragraph{Observed statistic.}
The run identifier for the site-load half-life boundary is \texttt{7cd189f15c4b}. In the static DMSO surface, the Spearman association between $m^6A$ load and log half-life was $-0.388929$. In the perturbation surface, the Spearman association between $m^6A$ load and $\Delta\log_2$ half-life was $0.245804$. After length residualization, the partial Spearman statistic was $0.227683$, with a two-sided permutation value $0.000999001$. The finite verdict attached to this row set is \texttt{crosses\_boundary}. The mathematical content is therefore a bounded empirical inequality: the observed partial statistic is separated from its permutation null strongly enough to license a row-level RNA-stability contact.

\paragraph{Boundary interpretation.}
The positive boundary does not identify a complete decay mechanism. It says that, in this finite joined panel, methylation load remains associated with RNA half-life after the declared length control and coverage screen. It does not say that translation has been realized, that a protein-output layer has been measured, or that a folded structure, physical admissibility condition, biological function, system phenotype, adaptation, cause, or universal rule follows. Those stronger readings require their own layer-specific observations. In particular, a decay-factor RBP explanation would require the same finite rows, or a deliberately matched extension of them, to carry measured local RBP occupancy, coordinate--occupancy decoupling, target-RBP loss, wild-type rescue, binding-defective or non-target controls, transcription-rate controls, local RNA-structure controls, motif-density controls, isoform state, transcript abundance, batch, and a same-row translation readout kept as a boundary variable rather than as an inferred outcome.

\paragraph{Comparison rows.}
The local half-life boundary is consistent with nearby RNA-stability evidence but is not collapsed into it. A separate human cross-organism methylation-load half-life record used $15845$ genes and $135300$ sites, ran $1000$ permutations, and reported $3$ sign-concordant cases in run \texttt{d7949df4a751}. Another RNA-stability transfer record for endogenous $3'$ UTR AU-rich-element load used $14933$ genes, $2000$ permutations, a baseline $R^2$ of $0.0657771$, a test $R^2$ of $0.0827561$, a gain $\Delta R^2=0.016979$, a negative ARE coefficient $-0.0607953$, and $p=0.00049975$ in run \texttt{e96bf26e626b}. These neighboring finite tables show that RNA-stability contacts can be quantified at row level, but they do not supply the missing RBP-occupancy or perturbation-rescue rows for the present $m^6A$ site-load claim.

\paragraph{Admissible conclusion.}
The admissible conclusion is a constrained cross-layer RNA-stability statement: in the $5837$-gene joined panel, $m^6A$ site load crosses the pre-specified half-life boundary, with static association $-0.388929$, perturbation association $0.245804$, length-partial association $0.227683$, and two-sided permutation value $0.000999001$. The claim remains at RNA-stability scope. It may be used as evidence that the finite row construction has contact with half-life variation, but it may not be promoted to translation, protein abundance, folding, physical admissibility, function, phenotype, mechanism, adaptation, causation, or universality without an additional contact that directly measures the promoted layer.
