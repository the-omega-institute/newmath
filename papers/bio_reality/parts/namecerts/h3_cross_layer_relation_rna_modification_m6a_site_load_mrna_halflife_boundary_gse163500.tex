\subsection{Finite m6A Site-Load Boundary for mRNA Half-Life}
\label{sec:bioreality-m6a-site-load-mrna-halflife-boundary-gse163500}

\paragraph{Finite panel.}
This record concerns a finite joined panel in which coding or transcript-associated RNA rows carry an m6A site-load coordinate and an mRNA half-life readout.  The row object is not a free biological universal: it is a keyed table with gene identifiers, treatment state, joined methylation coverage, transcript-length information, and half-life estimates.  In the audited GSE163500 boundary run, the retained joined panel contains $5837$ genes after applying the declared coverage rule
$$
\begin{aligned}
\min(\operatorname{MeanT}_{\mathrm{DMSO}},\operatorname{MeanT}_{\mathrm{STM2457}})
  \geq \operatorname{percentile}_{20}(\mathrm{valid\ joined\ genes}).
\end{aligned}
$$
The same finite run reports a static DMSO association between m6A load and half-life of $-0.388929$ by Spearman rank correlation, and a treatment-linked association between m6A load and the half-life change of $0.245804$.  After partialing transcript length, the finite statistic remains $0.227683$, with a two-sided permutation value $0.000999001$.  These numbers license a bounded statement about a methylation-load versus RNA-stability readout in the joined rows; they do not by themselves identify the molecular executor of decay.

\paragraph{Readout statistic.}
Let $i$ range over the retained rows, let $A_i$ denote the m6A site-load coordinate, let $L_i^{\mathrm{DMSO}}$ denote the DMSO log half-life readout, and let $\Delta L_i$ denote the treatment-linked log half-life change.  The run supplies the finite signs and magnitudes
$$
\begin{aligned}
\rho(A,L^{\mathrm{DMSO}}) &= -0.388929,\\
\rho(A,\Delta L) &= 0.245804,\\
\rho(A,\Delta L\mid \mathrm{length}) &= 0.227683,\\
p_{\mathrm{perm,2s}} &= 0.000999001,\\
N_{\mathrm{genes}} &= 5837.
\end{aligned}
$$
The interpretation is therefore a boundary crossing in the narrow sense that the methylation-load coordinate remains coupled to an RNA half-life surface under the declared finite statistic.  The reported verdict is $\mathrm{crosses\_boundary}$, with the explicit status semantics that a pass supports the pre-registered boundary statistic and is not a mechanism or function claim.

\paragraph{Cross-panel calibration.}
A separate human m6A/half-life calibration record provides scale information rather than a replacement for the GSE163500 row claim.  That run contains $15845$ genes, $135300$ sites, and $1000$ permutations, with $3$ sign-concordant organism-scoped comparisons.  Its role here is to show that the m6A-load and half-life connection is not being introduced as a single-row anecdote: there is a larger finite human site-load surface in which sign concordance was also recorded.  The chapter nevertheless keeps the GSE163500 statistic primary, because its joined treatment readout is the row set that produced the concrete $5837$-gene boundary verdict.

\paragraph{Coordinate, relation, and trigger.}
The finite coordinate is the m6A load or site-load label attached to each retained RNA row.  The relation is the rank association between that coordinate and the half-life surface, with length-adjusted and permutation-tested forms kept separate.  The trigger is the observed persistence of the association after the declared coverage and length rules are applied.  In symbols, the positive boundary statement is only
$$
\begin{aligned}
(A_i, L_i^{\mathrm{DMSO}}, \Delta L_i, \mathrm{length}_i, \mathrm{coverage}_i)_{i=1}^{5837}
\quad\leadsto\quad
\rho(A,\Delta L\mid \mathrm{length}) = 0.227683,
\end{aligned}
$$
with $p_{\mathrm{perm,2s}}=0.000999001$.  No additional layer is silently attached to this arrow.

\paragraph{Mandatory boundary.}
This chapter does not infer translation from half-life.  It also does not infer protein abundance, folding, physical admissibility, biological function, phenotype, adaptation, causation, or a universal biological rule.  A decay-factor RBP bridge would require same-row measured local RBP occupancy, coordinate--occupancy decoupling, target-RBP loss, wild-type rescue, binding-defective rescue or non-target controls, transcription-rate controls, local RNA-structure controls, motif-density controls, GC and dinucleotide controls, codon-pair context, splice or isoform state, abundance controls, batch controls, and a matched translation readout before downstream interpretation.  The present finite rows supply an RNA half-life boundary with m6A load; they do not supply those RBP perturbation and rescue rows.

\paragraph{Admissible conclusion.}
The admissible conclusion is that the GSE163500 joined finite panel supports a bounded m6A site-load to mRNA half-life readout: $5837$ retained genes, static Spearman $-0.388929$, treatment-linked Spearman $0.245804$, length-partial Spearman $0.227683$, and two-sided permutation value $0.000999001$.  The larger human calibration surface, with $15845$ genes and $135300$ sites, is consistent with treating the methylation-load surface as a real finite RNA-stability contact.  The result remains deliberately local: it is an RNA half-life boundary, not a translation, protein, structure, mechanism, function, or universality claim.
