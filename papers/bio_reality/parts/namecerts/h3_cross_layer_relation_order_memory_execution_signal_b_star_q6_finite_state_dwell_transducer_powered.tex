\paragraph{Carrier and coordinate.}
The carrier is a finite coding-sequence window table.  Each row contains an $f_3$-stress residual coordinate after the amino-acid quotient, local synonymous codon counts, a wobble-weight context, a position-resolved ribosome-occupancy or translation-efficiency readout when available, a matched mRNA control, and amino-acid or position-bin covariates.  The coordinate is not a codon-count summary alone.  It is the residual part that remains after amino-acid identity has been quotiented out, and it is only tested when the wobble surface is capable of breaking the $f_3$ symmetry.  In row notation the admissible entry has the form
$$
\begin{aligned}
  x &= (q_{f_3}, C_{\mathrm{syn}}, w, T_{\mathrm{pos}}, m, Z),\\
  Z &= (\mathrm{AA}, \mathrm{position}, \mathrm{GC3}, \ell_{\mathrm{CDS}}, M, \mathrm{coverage}, \mathrm{mappability}),\\
  w_c &\ne w_c^{f_3}.
\end{aligned}
$$
Here $q_{f_3}$ is the $f_3$-stress residual coordinate, $C_{\mathrm{syn}}$ is the local synonymous-count profile, $w$ is the bounded wobble-weight context, $T_{\mathrm{pos}}$ is the matched position-resolved translation readout, $m$ is the matched mRNA control, and $Z$ is the local control block.  A row lacking $T_{\mathrm{pos}}$ remains a boundary row rather than a positive translation claim.

\paragraph{Survival statistic.}
The proposed readback is a controlled survival entry $S_{f_3}(T_{\mathrm{position}}\mid Z)$.  The statistic is positive only if the $f_3$ coordinate improves held-out prediction of the same-window translation readout after the controls have already absorbed amino-acid composition, position-bin effects, GC3, CDS length, $M$-density, mRNA abundance, simpler codon baselines, coverage, and mappability.  A compact way to state the required comparison is
$$
\begin{aligned}
  S_{f_3}(T_{\mathrm{position}}\mid Z)
    &= \mathcal{L}(T_{\mathrm{position}}\mid Z, B_{\mathrm{simple}})
       - \mathcal{L}(T_{\mathrm{position}}\mid Z, B_{\mathrm{simple}}, q_{f_3}),\\
  \mathrm{pass} &\Longrightarrow S_{f_3}(T_{\mathrm{position}}\mid Z) > 0
       \quad \text{on held-out rows.}
\end{aligned}
$$
The finite-state dwell-transducer row certifies the execution-signal side of this interface.  It does not replace the same-window translation matrix.  The wobble asymmetry condition $w_c \ne w_c^{f_3}$ is an observed-row requirement, not a license to infer directionality from the coordinate definition.

\paragraph{Local boundary.}
The finite-state dwell-transducer pass keeps the order-memory execution signal in scope as a candidate cross-layer coordinate.  The admissible conclusion is only that the row has passed the finite transducer check and may be tested against a same-CDS, same-window translation readout under the stated controls.  No translation realization, protein abundance, folding, structural order, physical admissibility, biological function, mechanism, phenotype, adaptation law, or universality follows from the dwell-transducer row, from $f_3$ residual geometry, from codon/window geometry, from modeled tAI or stAI weights, or from a contact name.  Any such promotion requires a separate layer-matched contact and a separately computed statistic on the same joined rows.

\paragraph{Break condition.}
The row fails as a biological support claim if the text treats review readiness, contact routing, wobble weights, $S_{f_3}(T_{\mathrm{position}}\mid Z)$ notation, or the $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinate as though they already were joined ribosome-footprint data.  It also fails if a modeled adaptation surface is described as observed ribosome occupancy, or if the $f_3$ coordinate is made into a general directionality law without rank sufficiency and a measured wobble-asymmetry surface.  The correct status is a finite passed execution-signal certificate with a still-separate requirement for same-organism, same-CDS, same-window codon counts, wobble weights, matched mRNA abundance, and position-resolved translation readouts before any positive $f_3$ translation-survival statement can be made.
