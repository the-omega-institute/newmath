\paragraph{What the finite certificate does and does not power.}
The passed dwell-transducer row gives a finite order-memory object, not a biological promotion. The $f_3$ coordinate is therefore treated as a candidate coordinate for a joined external panel, rather than as an observed translation mechanism. A valid survival computation must see the same organism, the same CDS, and the same local window in the codon-count surface, the wobble-weight surface, the matched mRNA control, and the ribosome-occupancy or TE readout. The wobble condition is genuinely asymmetric:
$$
\begin{aligned}
  W_c \neq W_c^{f_3},
\end{aligned}
$$
so a symmetric codon table, a modeled tAI or stAI annotation alone, or a three-organism rank-saturated surface is insufficient. The permitted inference is only the bounded implication
$$
\begin{aligned}
  S_{f_3}(T_{\mathrm{position}}\mid Z) > 0
  &\Longrightarrow
  \text{the finite table supports an $f_3$ translation-readout survival entry},\\
  S_{f_3}(T_{\mathrm{position}}\mid Z) \leq 0
  &\Longrightarrow
  \text{the present table does not support that entry}.
\end{aligned}
$$
Neither branch licenses a universal law. In particular, no translation realization, protein abundance, folding, physical admissibility, biological function, phenotype, mechanism, or universality follows from the transducer certificate unless a separate layer-matched contact supplies the corresponding measurement and its own controlled statistic.

\paragraph{Admissible conclusion.}
The admissible conclusion is a finite and conditional one. The passed order-memory execution row makes the $f_3$-stress member of $B^*_{Q6}$ eligible for a bounded position-resolved translation-readout test. It does not yet establish the test outcome. The chapter therefore records a powered transducer plus a sharply delimited empirical demand: build the same-window matrix, observe the wobble asymmetry rather than assuming it, compute $S_{f_3}(T_{\mathrm{position}}\mid Z)$, and report only the survival status obtained under those controls. All downstream claims remain blocked until their own data-bearing contacts are attached.
