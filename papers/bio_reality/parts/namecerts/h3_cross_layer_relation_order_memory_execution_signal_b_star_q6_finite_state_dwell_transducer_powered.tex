\paragraph{Controlled survival statistic.}
Let $W$ be the finite set of CDS windows that survive identifier matching, readout matching, and control attachment.  For $w \in W$, write $q_{f_3}(w)$ for the $f_3$-stress residual coordinate after amino-acid quotienting, $T_{\mathrm{pos}}(w)$ for the matched position-resolved translation readout, and $Z(w)$ for the local control vector containing amino-acid or position-bin controls, GC3, CDS length, $M$-density baseline, local codon-count controls, matched mRNA abundance, coverage and mappability diagnostics, and any simpler baseline used in the ablation.  The admissible statistic is the controlled held-out survival entry
$$
\begin{aligned}
S_{f_3}(T_{\mathrm{pos}} \mid Z)
  &= \operatorname{Score}_{\mathrm{heldout}}(T_{\mathrm{pos}}; Z, q_{f_3})
   - \operatorname{Score}_{\mathrm{heldout}}(T_{\mathrm{pos}}; Z).
\end{aligned}
$$
The row is positive only if this difference is strictly positive on held-out windows after the amino-acid, position, GC3, length, $M$-density, mRNA, codon-count, rank, and simpler-baseline checks have all been applied.  The dwell-transducer certificate says that this is a legitimate finite-state test target; it does not by itself say that the position-resolved ribosome-footprint matrix has already been joined for the $f_3$-stress packet.

\paragraph{Wobble asymmetry condition.}
The proposed $f_3$ coordinate is allowed to enter the cross-layer test only where the wobble-weight surface breaks the $f_3$ symmetry in the row being tested.  In local notation, the admissibility condition is
$$
\begin{aligned}
w_c &\neq w_c^{f_3},\\
(q_{f_3}(w), w_c, w_c^{f_3}, T_{\mathrm{pos}}(w), Z(w)) &\in \mathcal{F}_{\mathrm{row}}.
\end{aligned}
$$
This condition prevents a purely codon-geometric coordinate from being promoted into a translation claim.  A modeled tAI or stAI weight can supply a bounded wobble-weight annotation, but it is not an observed ribosome-occupancy row.  A matched mRNA record can supply a transcription-layer denominator or control, but it is not itself a translation readout.  The finite-state row must contain the same-window translation surface before $S_{f_3}(T_{\mathrm{pos}} \mid Z)$ is a biological statistic rather than a formal request for one.

\paragraph{Local boundary.}
The present chapter therefore records a powered finite-state dwell target, not an unrestricted biological conclusion.  The admissible claim is that the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ order--memory--execution signal has a finite-row transducer form whose $f_3$ member can be tested against a same-window translation readout under explicit controls.  The inadmissible claims are equally important.  No translation realization follows from codon geometry alone.  No protein abundance, folding, structural order, physical admissibility, molecular function, phenotype, mechanism, or universality follows from the $f_3$ coordinate, from wobble-weight asymmetry, from modeled tAI or stAI weights, from mRNA abundance, or from review routing.  Each downstream layer requires its own matched contact and its own controlled statistic.  The break condition for this chapter is any use of the dwell-transducer pass as if it were already a joined ribosome-footprint matrix or as if it licensed a general $f_3$ directionality law.
