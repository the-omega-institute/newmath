\paragraph{Claim surface.}
The record considered here is the BioReality claim
$h3.cross\_layer\_relation.order\_memory\_execution\_signal.b\_star\_q6\_finite\_state\_dwell\_transducer\_powered$.  Its finite verification row reports experiment run $26912bc3e41e$, experiment id $b\_star\_q6\_finite\_state\_dwell\_transducer\_powered$, and status $passed$ at $2026$-$06$-$24$ $05{:}41{:}53+00{:}00$.  The biological reading is deliberately narrow: a finite-state dwell transducer has passed as an order-memory/execution-signal construction for the $B^*_{Q6}$ boundary, not as direct evidence that the $f_3$-stress residual already survives against position-resolved ribosome occupancy.  The row certifies that a finite control object exists for the cross-layer testing program; it does not by itself supply ribosome-footprint rows, translation-efficiency rows, protein-abundance rows, structural assays, physical assays, functional assays, perturbation phenotypes, or a universal biological rule.

\paragraph{Finite carrier.}
The finite-row carrier is a table of coding-sequence windows.  A row may contain an $f_3$-stress residual coordinate after quotienting by amino-acid composition, local synonymous-codon counts, a bounded wobble-weight context, a same-window translation readout when available, a matched mRNA control, and amino-acid or position-bin controls.  The intended statistic is a controlled survival entry $S_{f3}(T_{position}\mid Z)$, where $Z$ contains amino-acid composition, position or amino-acid bin, GC3, CDS length, M-density, matched mRNA, codon-count controls, coverage and mappability limits, rank checks, and simpler-baseline ablations.  In the finite-state dwell view, the transducer separates three roles: the coordinate that records the $f_3$ residual, the relation that compares it with a translation readout, and the rank condition that prevents a saturated or underpowered panel from being misread as survival.

$$
\begin{aligned}
Z &= \{\mathrm{AA},\ \mathrm{position\ bin},\ \mathrm{GC3},\ \mathrm{length},\ \mathrm{M\ density},\ \mathrm{mRNA},\ \mathrm{codon\ counts},\ \mathrm{coverage},\ \mathrm{mappability}\},\\
S_{f3}(T_{position}\mid Z) &> 0
\end{aligned}
$$

is therefore only the form of a possible positive readback.  The present verified row says that the finite-state dwell transducer passed; it does not say that this inequality has been computed on a joined position-resolved ribosome-occupancy matrix for the $f_3$ packet.

\paragraph{Wobble asymmetry and survival gate.}
The $f_3$-stress conjecture is only meaningful where wobble-weight asymmetry is observed rather than assumed.  The relevant distinction is $w_c\neq w_c^{f_3}$ in a context where synonymous codon counts and amino-acid quotienting are already present.  Even then, a modeled tAI or stAI weight is a bounded annotation of codon adaptation, not a ribosome-footprint measurement.  A valid survival row must join the same organism, same CDS, and same local window across ordered coding sequence, codon counts, wobble weights, matched mRNA abundance, and codon-indexed ribosome occupancy or an explicitly bounded translation-efficiency readout.  Only after that join may the statistic test whether the $f_3$ coordinate improves a held-out translation readout after the control set $Z$ and simpler baselines.

\paragraph{Local refusal boundary.}
The chapter therefore makes a positive claim and a refusal claim.  The positive claim is that the finite-state dwell transducer packet has passed as a finite control construction for the $B^*_{Q6}$ order-memory/execution-signal boundary.  The refusal claim is that this pass is not yet a measured $f_3$ translation-survival result.  No translation realization follows from codon-window geometry, $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinates, review readiness, wobble-weight asymmetry, or survival notation alone.  No protein abundance, folding, structural order, physical admissibility, biological function, mechanism, system phenotype, adaptation law, causation claim, or universality claim follows unless a separate layer-matched contact supplies rows and the corresponding controlled statistic is computed.  In particular, position-resolved ribosome-occupancy contact naming is not equivalent to a joined footprint table, matched mRNA contact naming is not equivalent to a TE denominator already evaluated on the same rows, and modeled tAI or stAI weights are not observed ribosome occupancy.

\paragraph{Chapter verdict.}
The record is suitable for the standalone BioReality paper as a bounded finite-row chapter because the core claim has an explicit passed finite-state dwell transducer row and because the nearby finite comparisons show how stronger biological readbacks must be recorded.  Its scientific content is the separation between a passed finite control object and a still-missing same-window translation-survival statistic.  The next admissible strengthening would require a joined position-resolved matrix and a positive held-out improvement for $S_{f3}(T_{position}\mid Z)$ after amino-acid, position-bin, GC3, length, M-density, codon-count, mRNA, coverage, mappability, rank, and simpler-baseline controls.
