\subsection{Finite-state dwell transducer boundary for $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$}\label{subsec:bioreality-bstar-q6-finite-state-dwell-transducer}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite row object.}
The record attached to \texttt{h3.cross\_layer\_relation.order\_memory\_execution\_signal.b\_star\_q6\_finite\_state\_dwell\_transducer\_powered} is a finite-state cross-layer claim about a bounded dwell transducer, not an unrestricted biological law.  Its verified run, \texttt{26912bc3e41e}, reports \texttt{status = passed} for the experiment \texttt{b\_star\_q6\_finite\_state\_dwell\_transducer\_powered}.  The mathematical carrier is a finite table whose rows keep a $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual coordinate, a local codon-window state, a proposed dwell or execution readout, and the control block needed to prevent amino-acid composition, position, GC3, length, methionine density, transcript abundance, or simpler codon baselines from being mistaken for a residual signal.  The claim is therefore local in two senses: it is local to the finite row panel, and it is local to the readout type explicitly attached to those rows.

\paragraph{Controlled survival statistic.}
For a row window $u$, let $q_{f_3}(u)$ denote the $f_3$-stress residual coordinate after quotienting away the amino-acid class, let $T_{\mathrm{pos}}(u)$ be the matched position-resolved ribosome-occupancy or bounded translation-efficiency readout, and let $Z(u)$ collect amino-acid composition, position bin, GC3, CDS length, methionine density, codon-count summaries, matched mRNA abundance, coverage, mappability, and the designated simpler baselines.  The admissible survival entry is not the raw association between $q_{f_3}$ and $T_{\mathrm{pos}}$; it is the held-out improvement that remains after $Z$ is already present.  In the notation of this chapter, the finite test has the form
$$
\begin{aligned}
S_{f_3}(T_{\mathrm{pos}}\mid Z)
  &= \mathcal{L}(T_{\mathrm{pos}}\mid Z)
     - \mathcal{L}(T_{\mathrm{pos}}\mid Z,q_{f_3},w),\\
\mathrm{support}
  &\Longleftrightarrow S_{f_3}(T_{\mathrm{pos}}\mid Z)>0
     \text{ on held-out rows and after simpler-baseline ablation.}
\end{aligned}
$$
Here $w$ is the bounded wobble-weight surface, and the test is only meaningful when the observed context has $w_c\ne w_c^{f_3}$ rather than a symmetric weight assignment imposed by notation.  Rank sufficiency is part of the claim: a saturated panel, a degenerate all-one fit, or a table too small to separate position, amino-acid, and codon effects does not count as powered survival.

\paragraph{Numerical anchors from adjacent finite panels.}
The present dwell-transducer run is linked to a nearby translation-conditioned $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ finite panel that gives the scale of row-level controls required before a cross-layer interpretation is allowed.  In \texttt{h3.cross\_layer\_relation.translation\_conditioned\_structural\_order\_boundary.b\_star\_q6\_powered}, the verified run \texttt{1b97387835f7} used \texttt{fold\_count = 5}, \texttt{min\_joined = 300}, and joined structural-order row counts of $3412$ for \textit{Escherichia coli} K-12 MG1655 and $5119$ for \textit{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}.  Its full-control translation-conditioned order verdict had two positive organism calls, weighted full-control $\Delta R^2 = 0.004764742175882462$, mean aggregate full-control $\Delta R^2 = 0.005284160876443678$, and sign-flip $p = 0.0029296875$ for greater-than-zero behavior; the permuted full-control mean was negative, $-0.00212186632044159$.  These numbers are not imported as proof of the present $f_3$-stress dwell claim.  They are cited only to fix the finite-row discipline: a reported cross-layer survival statement must expose joined counts, held-out folds, control deltas, and a null or permutation comparison instead of relying on geometry alone.

\paragraph{Cross-layer calibration surface.}
Other finite cross-layer records show why the dwell-transducer claim is intentionally narrower than a general biological principle.  The promoter-expression boundary \texttt{h3.cross\_layer\_relation.cis\_regulatory\_grammar.random\_promoter\_tf\_motif\_expression\_boundary\_deboer2020} reports $75111$ designed promoters, $8027$ high-quality rows, an analysis subsample of $2500$ rows, $200$ actual permutations, and runtime $530.4038379192352$ seconds.  The CRISPR position-nucleotide record \texttt{h3.cross\_layer\_relation.crispr\_efficiency.sgrna\_position\_nt\_efficiency\_doench2016} reports $n=5310$, runtime $24.6506$ seconds, and a GC main correlation $r=-0.0582917$.  These are distinct biological surfaces, but they demonstrate the same finite-row standard: contact with biology is recorded through a typed table, row counts, controls, and a declared statistic, not by promoting a symbolic coordinate into a phenotype.

\paragraph{Local interpretation.}
The finite-state dwell transducer should be read as a row-wise order-memory test.  A synonymous residual coordinate can be retained as a candidate state variable only when the same CDS window carries codon counts, a context-specific wobble asymmetry, a matched mRNA control, a position-resolved ribosome-occupancy or translation-efficiency readout, and enough rank to compare against simpler baselines.  The intended biological question is whether the residual state helps predict a local execution signal after the ordinary compositional explanations have already been paid for.  If the joined matrix is absent, if the readout is only modeled tAI or stAI, if mRNA abundance is used as the translation endpoint, or if the rank check collapses, then the chapter records a boundary request rather than a positive translation result.

\paragraph{Cannot-claim boundary.}
No translation realization, ribosome mechanism, protein abundance, folding state, physical admissibility, molecular function, system phenotype, or universal biological rule follows from the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ coordinate, the $f_3$-stress residual, the wobble-weight notation, or the dwell-transducer pass status alone.  Each stronger layer needs its own matched contact and its own finite statistic.  In particular, modeled tAI or stAI weights may be used as bounded codon-adaptation annotations, but they are not observed ribosome footprints.  A position-resolved ribosome-occupancy contact name identifies the required surface; it is not itself the joined same-organism, same-CDS, same-window matrix.  The valid conclusion is therefore precise and limited: the claim is ready as a finite-row BioReality chapter for a powered dwell-transducer boundary, with positive interpretation conditional on an explicit $S_{f_3}(T_{\mathrm{pos}}\mid Z)$ survival entry computed from the required joined rows.
