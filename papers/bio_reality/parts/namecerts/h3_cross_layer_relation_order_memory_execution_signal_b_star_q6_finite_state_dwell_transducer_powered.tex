\paragraph{Claim carrier.}
The record considered here is the finite-state dwell-transducer claim
$h3.cross\_layer\_relation.order\_memory\_execution\_signal.b\_star\_q6\_finite\_state\_dwell\_transducer\_powered$.
Its verified run is $26912bc3e41e$, with experiment identifier

The carrier is a finite coding-sequence window table.  Each admissible row must contain an $f_3$-stress residual coordinate after amino-acid quotienting, local synonymous-codon counts, a bounded wobble-weight context, a matched position-resolved ribosome-occupancy or translation-efficiency readout, an mRNA control, and amino-acid or position-bin controls.  The proposed readback is not a free-standing biological assertion; it is a controlled survival entry $S_{f3}(T_{\mathrm{position}}\mid Z)$, where $Z$ contains amino-acid composition or bins, position bins, $GC3$, coding-sequence length, $M$-density, matched mRNA abundance, codon-count controls, and simpler-baseline ablations.

\paragraph{Finite decision form.}
The chapter uses the passed transducer result as a certification that this packet has a finite internal decision shape: coordinate, relation, and rank are all present at the level needed to ask whether a bounded cross-layer statistic can be formed.  The statistic to be tested has the schematic form
$$
\begin{aligned}
S_{f3}(T_{\mathrm{position}}\mid Z)
  &= \Delta\mathcal{L}(Z,T_{\mathrm{position}})
     - \Delta\mathcal{L}(Z,f_3,T_{\mathrm{position}}), \\
\mathrm{support}
  &\Longleftrightarrow
     S_{f3}(T_{\mathrm{position}}\mid Z)>0
     \quad\text{on held-out rows after baseline ablation.}
\end{aligned}
$$
Here $\Delta\mathcal{L}$ denotes the held-out description-length or predictive-loss contribution of the named covariate set.  The inequality is deliberately local: a positive value would license only a controlled position-resolved translation-readout statement for the joined rows on which it is computed.  It would not, by itself, license protein abundance, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, cellular phenotype, mechanism, or universality.

\paragraph{Wobble asymmetry requirement.}
The $f_3$ coordinate is eligible only when the row records a genuine wobble-weight asymmetry $w_c\ne w_c^{f_3}$.  If the weight surface is symmetric, absent, or only a modeled annotation with no measured or explicitly bounded translation readout, the row cannot contribute a positive translation-survival claim.  Likewise, tAI or stAI weights are permitted as bounded codon-adaptation annotations, but they are not observed ribosome footprints.  Matched mRNA abundance is a control for separating transcriptional abundance from translation-layer signal; it is not itself ribosome occupancy, translation realization, protein output, or mechanism.

\paragraph{Local no-promotion rule.}
The passed finite-state dwell-transducer status establishes that the claim has an admissible finite computational route.  It does not establish that the required same-organism, same-CDS, same-window matrix has already been populated.  Before any positive biological statement is made, the table must contain ordered coding-sequence windows, codon counts, bounded wobble weights, matched mRNA rows, and position-resolved ribosome-footprint or explicitly bounded translation-efficiency rows, all keyed to the same organism, transcript or CDS, and position window.  The failure condition is immediate:
$$
\begin{aligned}
&\text{no joined position-window matrix, or no held-out improvement after } Z, \\
&\hspace{1.5em}\Longrightarrow\quad
\text{no } f_3\text{-stress translation-survival claim.}
\end{aligned}
$$
Even if this condition is later passed, the conclusion remains confined to the tested translation readout.  Folding, physical admissibility, protein abundance, functional realization, system phenotype, mechanistic explanation, and global law each require their own layer-matched contact and their own controlled statistic.
