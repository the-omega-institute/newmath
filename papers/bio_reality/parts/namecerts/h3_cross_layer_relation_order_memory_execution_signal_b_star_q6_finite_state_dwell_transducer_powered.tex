\paragraph{Wobble asymmetry requirement.}
The $f_3$ coordinate is admissible only under a row-local asymmetry condition.  A synonymous residual direction after amino-acid quotienting is not enough; the row must also record a context-specific wobble surface with $w_c \ne w_c^{f_3}$.  In the finite table this distinction matters because a symmetric wobble surface would make the coordinate invisible to the proposed translation-readout test.  The intended statistic asks whether the $f_3$ residual improves held-out position-resolved ribosome occupancy or translation efficiency after the controls in $Z$ and after simpler codon or composition baselines.  Thus the packet has three separate finite ingredients: the residual coordinate, the wobble asymmetry, and the readout survival comparison.  The verified dwell-transducer pass covers the finite execution form; it does not assert that all three ingredients have already been joined in an external ribosome-footprint dataset.

\paragraph{Local refusal boundary.}
This chapter keeps the biological interpretation deliberately narrow.  A passed finite-state dwell-transducer record does not imply translation realization from codon geometry alone.  Modeled tAI or stAI weights are bounded codon-adaptation annotations, not observed ribosome occupancy.  Matched mRNA abundance is a control covariate for separating transcript abundance from translation readout, not a translation measurement by itself.  Position-resolved ribosome occupancy becomes usable only when the same organism, same CDS, same window, ordered codon counts, wobble weights, matched mRNA rows, and footprint rows are all present before the statistic is computed.  No protein abundance, folding, structural order, physical admissibility, molecular function, phenotype, mechanism, adaptation, causation, or universal rule follows from the present packet unless a separate layer-matched contact supplies that readout and its controlled statistic.

\paragraph{Break condition.}
The packet breaks if the finite dwell-transducer pass, the contact names, the $S_{f_3}(T_{\mathrm{position}} \mid Z)$ notation, the wobble-weight route, or the $f_3$ residual geometry is treated as if it were already a computed same-window ribosome-occupancy statistic.  It also breaks if a modeled adaptation weight is reported as measured ribosome dynamics, or if rank language is used before the joined panel has enough nondegenerate rows for held-out survival.  The local admissible statement is therefore only this: the $B^*_{Q6}$ $f_3$-stress order-memory packet has a passed finite-state dwell-transducer certificate, and it names the exact biological rows needed for a future position-resolved translation survival test.  Positive biological survival remains conditional on constructing the joined panel and obtaining a held-out improvement after amino-acid, position, GC3, length, $M$-density, mRNA, codon-count, wobble-weight, coverage, mappability, rank, and simpler-baseline controls.
