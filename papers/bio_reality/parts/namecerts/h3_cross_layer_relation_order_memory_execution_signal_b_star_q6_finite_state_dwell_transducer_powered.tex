\subsection{Finite-state dwell transducer boundary for position-resolved $f_3$ translation survival}\label{sec:biorality-f3-position-resolved-translation-survival}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite-row object.}
The row object considered here is a finite coding-sequence window table.  Each row is keyed by organism, coding sequence or transcript identifier, codon position, and a fixed local window.  The row carries an $f_3$-stress residual coordinate after amino-acid quotient, local synonymous codon counts, a bounded wobble-weight context, a matched position-resolved ribosome-occupancy or translation-efficiency readout, matched mRNA abundance, and amino-acid or position-bin controls.  The associated BioReality claim is not that codon geometry itself is translation.  The claim is narrower: an $f_3$-stress coordinate may be retained as a bounded cross-layer coordinate only when a same-window translation readout improves after the ordinary sequence, abundance, and rank controls have already been attached.

\paragraph{Recorded finite evidence.}
The finite-state dwell transducer record itself is present as claim \texttt{h3.cross\_layer\_relation.order\_memory\_execution\_signal.b\_star\_q6\_finite\_state\_dwell\_transducer\_powered}.  Its run identifier is \texttt{26912bc3e41e}, its experiment identifier is \texttt{b\_star\_q6\_finite\_state\_dwell\_transducer\_powered}, and its recorded status is \texttt{passed}.  This establishes only that the finite-state transducer packet has a passed finite-row status; it does not by itself attach a position-resolved ribosome-footprint matrix for the $f_3$ coordinate.

A nearby translation-conditioned record gives useful scale for what a powered cross-layer table looks like when joined rows and held-out controls are actually present.  In claim \texttt{h3.cross\_layer\_relation.translation\_conditioned\_structural\_order\_boundary.b\_star\_q6\_powered}, the run \texttt{1b97387835f7} used $5$ folds, a minimum joined-row threshold of $300$, and joined structural-order counts of $3412$ for \emph{Escherichia coli} K-12 MG1655 and $5119$ for \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}.  The recorded aggregate full-control $\Delta R^2$ values were positive in both organisms, with minimum $0.002688284523492457$, maximum $0.007880037229394898$, mean $0.005284160876443678$, and weighted value $0.004764742175882462$.  The sign-flip probability for a positive full-control effect was $0.0029296875$, while the permuted full-control $\Delta R^2$ values were negative in both organisms, ranging from $-0.003463794378680385$ to $-0.000779938262202795$.  These numbers are cited here as a finite-row calibration of the project standard: a cross-layer promotion requires an explicitly joined table, a held-out or permutation-sensitive comparison, and a control set strong enough to separate the coordinate from simpler baselines.

Additional measured cross-layer rows show why the present $f_3$ packet cannot be promoted by contact names alone.  The G418 stop-context readthrough record \texttt{h3.cross\_layer\_relation.translation\_termination.stop\_context\_readthrough\_g418\_wangen2020} reports $7658$ joined rows before the nonzero filter, $4982$ rows after the filter, a nonzero readthrough fraction of $0.65056$, and runtime $303.534$ seconds for the endpoint described as a rank-average scaled nonzero G418-induced readthrough score.  The cross-dataset stop-readthrough record \texttt{h3.cross\_layer\_relation.stop\_readthrough.stop\_readthrough\_crossdataset\_wangen2020} reports $55886$ rows, runtime $133.552797$ seconds, and untreated Spearman-type rank signal $0.032718$.  These are not evidence for $f_3$-stress translation survival.  They mark the kind of externally measured readout row that must be present before a symbol such as $S_{f_3}(T_{\mathrm{position}}\mid Z)$ is treated as a computed statistic rather than a requested statistic.

\paragraph{Statistic and controls.}
For a finite panel $\mathcal{T}$ of same-organism, same-CDS, same-window rows, the admissible survival score is a controlled held-out improvement of the position readout over a simpler baseline.  The control vector is
$$
\begin{aligned}
Z = (&\text{amino-acid or position bin},\ \mathrm{GC3},\ \text{CDS length},\ \text{$M$-density baseline},\\
&\text{local codon counts},\ \text{matched mRNA abundance},\ \text{coverage},\ \text{mappability},\ \text{organism and batch when present}).
\end{aligned}
$$
The finite-row claim is allowed to survive only if the $f_3$ coordinate gives a positive held-out improvement after these controls and after ablation against simpler codon-usage or position-only predictors:
$$
\begin{aligned}
S_{f_3}(T_{\mathrm{position}}\mid Z) > 0.
\end{aligned}
$$
The wobble-weight asymmetry condition is likewise observational rather than decorative.  A valid row must distinguish $w_c$ from $w_c^{f_3}$ in the attached organism-specific or explicitly bounded translation-weight surface.  If the apparent asymmetry is only inherited from notation, or if it is not joined to the same window as the ribosome-occupancy or translation-efficiency readout, then it is not a survival datum.

\paragraph{Finite-state interpretation.}
The dwell-transducer language is used here in a bounded sense.  A finite state is a row class obtained after quotienting by amino-acid content, position bin, and the listed controls.  A dwell transition records whether the same local window retains a controlled readout signal when the $f_3$ residual coordinate is exposed to wobble-weight asymmetry and then tested against measured position-level translation data.  Passing the finite-state transducer packet therefore says that the packet has the required finite-row shape for this test.  It does not say that the same-row ribosome-footprint matrix has already been supplied for the $f_3$ survival statistic.

\paragraph{Local boundary.}
The present chapter keeps the claim at translation-readout boundary status.  A positive $f_3$ entry would mean only that a finite same-window statistic survived its stated controls.  It would not imply protein abundance, folding, structural order, physical admissibility, molecular function, cellular phenotype, mechanism, adaptation, causation, or a universal biological law.  Those stronger readings require separate layer-matched contacts and their own finite statistics.  In particular, modeled tAI or stAI weights are allowed as bounded codon-adaptation or wobble-weight annotations, but they are not observed ribosome occupancy.  Matched mRNA abundance is a control covariate for translation-efficiency residualization, not a translation readout by itself.  A position-resolved ribosome-occupancy contact name identifies the required readout surface, but it is not the joined footprint table.

\paragraph{Verdict for the standalone record.}
The finite-row material is sufficient to write the BioReality chapter because the primary dwell-transducer packet has a passed status and the surrounding verified rows supply concrete examples of the row-count, fold, permutation, and scalar-control discipline required for cross-layer promotion.  The chapter therefore records a ready boundary theorem in prose form: $f_3$-stress can be tested as a finite cross-layer coordinate only after same-organism, same-CDS, same-window codon counts, bounded wobble weights, matched mRNA abundance, and position-resolved ribosome-footprint or explicitly bounded translation-efficiency rows are joined.  Until that joined matrix exists and $S_{f_3}(T_{\mathrm{position}}\mid Z)$ is positive under the declared controls, the packet remains a request for a controlled translation-readout statistic rather than evidence of translation realization or any higher biological layer.
