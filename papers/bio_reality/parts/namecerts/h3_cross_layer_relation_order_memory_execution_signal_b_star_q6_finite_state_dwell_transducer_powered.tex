\paragraph{Finite record.}
The claim recorded here is the finite-state dwell-transducer member of the $B^*_{Q6}$ cross-layer family.  Its certified row is keyed by the claim identifier \texttt{h3.cross\_layer\_relation.order\_memory\_execution\_signal.b\_star\_q6\_finite\_state\_dwell\_transducer\_powered} and by the experiment identifier \texttt{b\_star\_q6\_finite\_state\_dwell\_transducer\_powered}. \paragraph{Carrier and row shape.}
The data carrier is a finite table of coding-sequence windows.  A row contains an $f_3$-stress residual coordinate after quotienting by amino-acid identity, local synonymous-codon counts, a wobble-weight context, a matched position-resolved ribosome-occupancy or translation-efficiency readout, a matched mRNA control, and amino-acid or position-bin controls.  The finite-state dwell-transducer assertion is therefore not a statement about an unconstrained sequence geometry.  It is a statement about whether a bounded order-memory coordinate can remain visible after the relevant row has been equipped with the controls that separate codon-window structure from translation-layer readback.

\paragraph{Controlled survival statistic.}
For a window table $W$, let $F_3(w)$ denote the residual $f_3$ coordinate in row $w$, let $T_{\mathrm{pos}}(w)$ denote the matched position-resolved translation readout, and let $Z(w)$ collect amino-acid composition or position-bin controls, GC3, CDS length, M-density, mRNA abundance, coverage and mappability limits, and simpler codon-baseline terms.  The admissible statistic is the held-out controlled gain
$$
\begin{aligned}
S_{f3}(T_{\mathrm{pos}}\mid Z)
  &= \operatorname{Score}(T_{\mathrm{pos}}\mid Z,F_3,\text{wobble})
     - \operatorname{Score}(T_{\mathrm{pos}}\mid Z,\text{baseline}).
\end{aligned}
$$
The break condition is local and finite: the row set does not support the claim if the held-out improvement is non-positive after the amino-acid, position, GC3, length, M-density, mRNA, rank, coverage, mappability, codon-count, wobble-weight, and simpler-baseline checks are imposed.  The wobble asymmetry condition is also part of the row requirement; a presumed asymmetry is not enough unless the relevant surface records $w_c \neq w_c^{f_3}$ in the same finite context used for the readout test.

\paragraph{Local interpretation.}
The chapter-level interpretation is therefore narrow.  The finite-state dwell-transducer test may be reported as a passed finite audit for the named $B^*_{Q6}$ order-memory signal, and it may be positioned as a request for, or boundary around, a same-window translation-readout survival test.  It cannot by itself assert that $f_3$-stress causes translation, that modeled tAI or stAI weights are measured ribosome occupancy, that an aggregate translation-efficiency route is position-resolved occupancy, or that a codon-window coordinate is a biological mechanism.  A stronger claim would require the same organism, same CDS, and same window to carry joined codon counts, wobble weights, matched mRNA abundance, position-resolved ribosome-footprint rows or an explicitly bounded translation-efficiency readout, and a positive held-out statistic after the declared controls.

The cannot-claim boundary is part of the result. Such statements require distinct layer-matched contacts and their own finite statistics.  The present chapter records a passed finite dwell-transducer gate for the $B^*_{Q6}$ order-memory signal and keeps that gate separate from downstream biological realization.
