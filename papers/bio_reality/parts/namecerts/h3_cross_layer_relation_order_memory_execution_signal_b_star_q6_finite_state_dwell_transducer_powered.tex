\paragraph{Finite carrier.}
The intended row type is a bounded coding-sequence window table. \paragraph{Controlled statistic.}
The permitted positive statement has the following form only:
$$
\begin{aligned}
S_{f_3}(T_{\mathrm{position}}\mid Z) &> 0,\\
Z &= \{\mathrm{AA},\mathrm{position},GC3,\mathrm{length},M\text{-}\mathrm{density},\mathrm{mRNA},\mathrm{coverage},\mathrm{mappability},\mathrm{baseline}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the packet asks whether an $f_3$ residual coordinate retains held-out predictive content for a same-window translation readout after the listed controls.  Wobble asymmetry is required as an observed or bounded row feature, written $w_c\ne w_c^{f_3}$, not as an assumed global direction.  If the joined matrix is absent, if the rank check saturates, or if the held-out improvement is nonpositive after ablation, the packet remains only a boundary record for a future measurement.

\paragraph{Null boundary.}
The passed status is a gate on finite transducer admissibility, not a biological promotion.  Modeled $tAI$ or $stAI$ weights may bound a codon-adaptation surface, and codon-usage tables may bound composition, but neither is observed ribosome occupancy.  A position-resolved ribosome-occupancy contact name is likewise only a route to data until same-organism, same-CDS, same-window footprint rows are actually joined.  The packet breaks if review status, contact naming, $S_{f_3}(T_{\mathrm{position}}\mid Z)$ notation, wobble asymmetry, or codon-window geometry is treated as a measured translation statistic without the finite joined matrix.  Any downstream claim about translation realization, folding, physical admissibility, biological function, mechanism, system phenotype, or a general biological law requires a separate row-level contact and its own controlled statistic.
