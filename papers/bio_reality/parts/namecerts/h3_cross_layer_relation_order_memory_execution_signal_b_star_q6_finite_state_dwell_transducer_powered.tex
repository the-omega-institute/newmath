\paragraph{Finite row being certified.}
The certified row is the claim identifier \texttt{h3.cross\_layer\_relation.order\_memory\_execution\_signal.b\_star\_q6\_finite\_state\_dwell\_transducer\_powered}. Its associated run \texttt{26912bc3e41e} reports status \texttt{passed} for experiment \texttt{b\_star\_q6\_finite\_state\_dwell\_transducer\_powered}. The biological object is not a free-standing organismal law. It is a finite coding-sequence window table whose rows carry an $f_3$-stress residual coordinate after amino-acid quotienting, local synonymous codon counts, a wobble-weight context, a matched position-resolved ribosome-occupancy or translation-efficiency readout, an mRNA control, and amino-acid or position-bin covariates. The row is therefore a bounded cross-layer test object: it asks whether the $f_3$ component remains useful only after the row has supplied the translation readback and the declared control coordinates.

\paragraph{Transducer interpretation.}
The finite-state dwell transducer is read here as a row-level recognition device. Its input alphabet is the ordered coding-window surface: codon identity, synonymous class, amino-acid bin, position bin, GC3, length-related context, local methionine-density baseline, wobble-weight value, transcript abundance, and the matched translation readout. Its state is not a biochemical mechanism. It records whether the $f_3$ residual remains distinguishable after the amino-acid quotient and whether its contribution survives a held-out comparison against simpler codon and composition baselines. The local survival entry is the controlled statistic
$$
\begin{aligned}
S_{f_3}(T_{\mathrm{position}}\mid Z)
  &= \Delta_{\mathrm{held\text{-}out}}\bigl(\mathrm{model}(Z,f_3,w_c),\mathrm{model}(Z)\bigr),\\
Z
  &= \{\mathrm{AA\ composition},\mathrm{position\ bin},\mathrm{GC3},\mathrm{CDS\ length},M\mathrm{\text{-}density},\mathrm{mRNA},\mathrm{codon\ controls}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
A positive entry means only that the finite row retains a controlled readout advantage for the declared translation surface. A zero or negative entry means the row has not earned promotion at this interface.

\paragraph{Support condition.}
The admissible support condition is narrow. The $f_3$-stress component may be reported as a surviving cross-layer coordinate only when context-specific wobble weights break the $f_3$ symmetry, when the same coding-sequence windows have a measured or explicitly bounded translation readback, and when held-out improvement remains positive after amino-acid composition, position-bin, GC3, coding-sequence length, methionine-density, mRNA abundance, codon-count controls, rank sufficiency, coverage limits, mappability limits, and simpler-baseline ablations. The condition can be summarized as
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{Pass}_{f_3}
  \Longleftrightarrow{}& (w_c \ne w_c^{f_3})
  \;\wedge\; \mathrm{JoinedWindow}(c,T_{\mathrm{position}},\mathrm{mRNA},Z)\\
  &\wedge\; S_{f_3}(T_{\mathrm{position}}\mid Z)>0
  \;\wedge\; \mathrm{RankSufficient}
  \;\wedge\; \mathrm{AblationStable}.
\end{aligned}
$$
Every conjunct is part of the finite-row claim. Removing the joined translation readout or the mRNA control changes the object under test rather than weakening the same claim.

\paragraph{Local refusal boundary.}
This row does not license translation realization from codon geometry alone. It does not treat modeled tAI or stAI weights as observed ribosome occupancy. It does not infer protein abundance, folded structure, physical admissibility, molecular function, phenotype, mechanism, adaptation, causation, or universality. Those claims require separate layer-matched contacts and their own finite statistics. In particular, a positive $S_{f_3}(T_{\mathrm{position}}\mid Z)$ would still be only a position-resolved translation-readout survival statement. It would not be a protein-output statement, a structural statement, or a general biological law.
