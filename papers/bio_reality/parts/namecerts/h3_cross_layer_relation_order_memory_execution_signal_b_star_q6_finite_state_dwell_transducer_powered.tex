\paragraph{Dwell-transducer interpretation.}
The finite-state dwell transducer keeps three pieces of information separate.  The first is the coordinate value $q_{f_3}$, which is a residual codon-window coordinate and not a biological measurement by itself.  The second is the wobble-weight surface $w$, where the relevant admissibility condition is an observed asymmetry $w_c \neq w_c^{f_3}$ rather than an assumed symmetry break.  The third is the same-window readout $T_{\mathrm{pos}}$, which must be supplied by position-resolved ribosome-footprint occupancy or by an explicitly bounded translation-efficiency row.  In compact form the admissible support condition is
$$
\begin{aligned}
  &\operatorname{rank}(q_{f_3}, w, T_{\mathrm{pos}}, Z) \;\text{is sufficient},\\
  &w_c \neq w_c^{f_3},\\
  &\Delta_{\mathrm{heldout}}\bigl(q_{f_3}; T_{\mathrm{pos}} \mid Z\bigr) > 0.
\end{aligned}
$$
If any one of these three lines is absent, the packet remains a routing record for a possible experiment rather than a positive cross-layer result.
