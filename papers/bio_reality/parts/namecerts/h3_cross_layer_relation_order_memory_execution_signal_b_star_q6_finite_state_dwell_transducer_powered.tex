\subsection{Finite-state dwell transducer boundary for the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ order-memory signal}\label{subsec:bioreality-bstar-q6-finite-state-dwell-transducer}\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Claim surface.} The claim considered here is the finite-state dwell-transducer form of the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ order-memory execution signal. Its finite table is not a free biological assertion: a row is admissible only when it carries a bounded $f_3$-stress residual coordinate, local synonymous-codon counts, a wobble-weight context, a matched translation-layer readout, an mRNA control, and amino-acid or position-bin controls. In the audited row, the exact claim identifier was \texttt{h3.cross\_layer\_relation.order\_memory\_execution\_signal.b\_star\_q6\_finite\_state\_dwell\_transducer\_powered}; the run identifier was \texttt{26912bc3e41e}; the experiment identifier was \texttt{b\_star\_q6\_finite\_state\_dwell\_transducer\_powered}; and the recorded status was \texttt{passed}. Thus the finite-state transducer is admitted as a powered internal row relation, not as a direct claim about protein output or organism-level behavior.

\paragraph{Finite-row relation.} The row type is a controlled window table. Let $q$ denote the $f_3$-stress residual coordinate after amino-acid quotienting, let $c$ denote the local synonymous-codon count vector, let $w$ denote the context-specific wobble-weight surface, let $T_{\mathrm{pos}}$ denote a same-window position-resolved ribosome-occupancy or translation-efficiency readout when present, let $m$ denote the matched mRNA control, and let $Z$ collect the amino-acid, position, GC3, length, M-density, coverage, mappability, and simpler-baseline controls. The admissible survival entry has the schematic form
$$
\begin{aligned}
S_{f_3}(T_{\mathrm{pos}}\mid Z)
  &= \Delta_{\mathrm{heldout}}\bigl(T_{\mathrm{pos}}; q,c,w,m,Z\bigr),\\
\Delta_{\mathrm{heldout}} &> 0 \quad \text{after the declared control and ablation panel.}
\end{aligned}
$$
The asymmetry condition is local: the row must observe a context in which $w_c \neq w_c^{f_3}$ rather than merely assume such a split. Without this observed wobble-weight asymmetry and without the matched translation readout, the $f_3$ coordinate remains a formal residual coordinate on coding-sequence windows.

\paragraph{Readout boundary.} For the present $f_3$-stress dwell-transducer claim, the passed status licenses only the finite-state row relation and its controlled survival requirement. A position-resolved ribosome-occupancy contact name is not itself a footprint matrix; a modeled tAI or stAI weight is not an observed ribosome-occupancy measurement; and a matched mRNA table is a control surface rather than a translation event. The admissible biological test still requires same-organism, same-CDS, same-window joining of ordered coding-sequence coordinates, codon counts, bounded wobble weights, matched mRNA abundance, and codon-indexed ribosome-footprint or explicitly bounded translation-efficiency rows. Only after that join may the statistic $S_{f_3}(T_{\mathrm{pos}}\mid Z)$ be read as a positive translation-layer survival entry.

The chapter therefore records a powered finite-state dwell-transducer boundary: the row relation is accepted, while every downstream biological interpretation remains conditional on separately supplied finite-row evidence.
