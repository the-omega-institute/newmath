\subsection{Target-permutation ceiling for the $B^\ast_{Q6}$ $f_3$-stress translation boundary}
\label{subsec:b-star-q6-f3-target-permutation-ceiling}

\paragraph{Finite table.}
The record considered here is a finite coding-sequence window table whose rows are keyed by organism, CDS identifier, codon-position bin, synonymous amino-acid fiber, the $f_3$ partner class, the $f_3$-stress coordinate, a bounded wobble or tAI weight surface, a matched mRNA control, and a position-resolved ribosome-occupancy readout when such a readout has actually been joined. The mathematical object is therefore not protein output and not a cellular phenotype. It is a residual coordinate problem inside synonymous coding windows: after quotienting by amino-acid identity and subtracting baseline codon-composition effects, one asks whether the $f_3$-stress coordinate remains aligned with a translation-window statistic. The row discipline is strict. A row can contribute to a survival statistic only when the codon counts, weight surface, mRNA control, and position-indexed ribosome readout are all available for the same organism, the same CDS, and the same local window.

\paragraph{Verified finite signal.}
The target-permutation experiment attached to this record used $B=200$ target-null draws. Its deterministic seed was $\mathtt{sha256:b\_star\_q6\_pq\_target\_permutation\_dictionary\_ceiling\_powered|seed=70651|organism|trial|subset|stratum}$, and the recorded runtime was $441.9897983074188$ seconds. The finite verdict was $\mathtt{crosses\_boundary}$: the observed $C^\ast$ statistic exceeded the matched target-null $95\%$ ceiling, with the run summary reporting that $24.5\%$ of the target-permutation signal survived as structure under this ceiling test. This is a dictionary-ceiling result for the finite target-null design. It says that the observed $B^\ast_{Q6}$ target is not fully explained by the matched target permutation used in this experiment. It does not, by itself, identify a measured ribosome pause, a translation mechanism, or any downstream protein-level property.

The other reported weighted increments were smaller or negative: $\mathtt{Arg\_AGR}$ had $0.0020139270616115154$ with sign-flip probability $0.142578125$, $\mathtt{Ile\_AUA}$ had $0.0013067592718559024$ with probability $0.0693359375$, $\mathtt{Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY}$ had $0.0010134090056701938$ with probability $0.2158203125$, $\mathtt{K\_AAA}$ had $0.0005307046179682497$ with probability $0.2294921875$, $\mathtt{Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG}$ had $0.00017615626369389472$ with probability $0.24609375$, $\mathtt{Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG}$ had $0.00004390882136492317$ with probability $0.4873046875$, $\mathtt{Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR}$ had $-0.0003205780368125179$ with probability $0.779296875$, and $\mathtt{Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY}$ had $-0.00045340330387383447$ with probability $0.529296875$. Thus the finite signal is not a broad synonymy claim. In the available computation it concentrates on the $f_3$-stress axis.

\paragraph{Local statistic.}
The admissible readout is a per-window survival statistic, written here as $S_{f3}(T_{\mathrm{window}}\mid Z)$, where $T_{\mathrm{window}}$ is a position-resolved ribosome-occupancy or translation-efficiency window statistic and $Z$ contains the declared controls: amino-acid composition or amino-acid bin, codon position, organism, GC3, CDS length, methionine-density baseline, the wobble or tAI surface, codon-count controls, matched mRNA abundance, coverage, mappability, rank sufficiency, and simpler-baseline ablations. The dynamic case requires an actual asymmetry $w_c\neq w_{c^{f_3}}$ on the same row. A modelled tAI or wobble-weight surface can define a context in which such an asymmetry is meaningful, but it is not itself a ribosome-footprint observation.

\paragraph{Null boundary.}
The present finite evidence is strong enough to justify a BioReality chapter about a target-permutation ceiling and an $f_3$-localized residual coordinate. It is not enough to report a positive translation-readout survival statistic unless the same-row occupancy matrix exists. If the same organism, CDS, local window, codon counts, wobble or tAI weights, matched mRNA control, and position-resolved ribosome-footprint rows are not all present, the only valid return is the null boundary: rank saturation, unmatched rows, or missing contacts prevent promotion beyond the finite coordinate result. In particular, no translation realization, folding claim, physical admissibility claim, biological function, mechanism, phenotype, or universal biological law follows from this record unless a separate layer-matched contact supplies that specific observation and the corresponding controlled statistic is computed.
