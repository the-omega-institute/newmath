\subsection{Target-permutation ceiling for the $B^*_Q6$ $f_3$-stress translation-window boundary}
\label{sec:bioreality-bstar-q6-f3-target-permutation-ceiling}

\paragraph{Finite-row question.}
This record concerns a finite coding-sequence window table in which each row is keyed by organism, CDS identifier, codon-position bin, synonymous amino-acid fiber, $f_3$ partner class, $f_3$-stress coordinate, a bounded wobble or tAI weight surface, a matched mRNA control, and a position-resolved ribosome-occupancy or translation-efficiency window readout. The biological question is deliberately narrower than a general translation claim: after amino-acid quotienting and synonymous residualization, does the $f_3$-stress coordinate remain a bounded predictor of local ribosome-occupancy windows only where the wobble or tAI surface breaks the $f_3$ symmetry? The admissible readout is a per-window survival statistic $S_{f3}(T_{\mathrm{window}}\mid Z)$, with $Z$ containing amino-acid composition, codon-position or bin controls, organism, GC3, CDS length, M-density, wobble or tAI surface, matched mRNA abundance, coverage, mappability, rank sufficiency, and simpler-baseline ablations.

\paragraph{Coordinate audit.}
The supporting structural-order run computed nine residual axes:
$K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $Leu_{CUN\,vs\,UUR}$, $Leu_{UUA\,vs\,UUG}$, $Ser_{UCR\,vs\,AGY}$, $Ser_{UCA\,vs\,UCG}$, $Thr_{ACR\,vs\,ACY}$, and $f_3$-stress. The run used $5$ folds with a minimum joined-row threshold of $300$. Among these coordinates, exactly one axis passed the fold-level sign-flip gate: $f_3$-stress. Its weighted absolute unit loading was $0.5109586606724208$, its weighted held-out $R^2$ increment was $0.0031744502294756994$, and its sign-flip probability for a positive effect was $0.03125$. The other listed axes did not pass this gate: for example, $Arg_{AGR}$ had sign-flip probability $0.142578125$ with weighted held-out $R^2$ increment $0.0020139270616115154$, and $Ile_{AUA}$ had sign-flip probability $0.0693359375$ with increment $0.0013067592718559024$. Thus the coordinate-level evidence identifies $f_3$-stress as the structural carrier of the finite residual signal, but this is still a coordinate result rather than a measured translation outcome.

\paragraph{Permutation ceiling.}
The target-permutation experiment for the $B^*_Q6$ dictionary ceiling used $B=200$ permutation draws. Its deterministic seed was
$\mathrm{sha256{:}b\_star\_q6\_pq\_target\_permutation\_dictionary\_ceiling\_powered\mid seed=70651\mid organism\mid trial\mid subset\mid stratum}$,
and its runtime was $441.9897983074188$ seconds. The reported verdict was that the observed $C^*$ crossed the matched target-null $95\%$ ceiling, with $24.5\%$ of the signal retained as structure under target permutation. In finite-row terms, the result says that the dictionary is not exhausted by the matched target-null construction at the tested boundary. It does not say that ribosome pausing, translation efficiency, protein abundance, or downstream phenotype has already been observed.

$$
\begin{aligned}

\paragraph{Admissible biological contact.}
The result can be read as a request for a joined translation-window panel, not as proof that such a panel has already supplied the survival statistic. The required contact must attach ordered CDS coordinates, same-row codon counts, bounded tAI or stAI weights, matched mRNA abundance, and codon-indexed ribosome-footprint occupancy for the same organism, CDS, and local window. A modeled tAI or wobble-weight asymmetry may define a context surface, but it is not an observed ribosome-occupancy measurement. Similarly, a codon-usage table reports sequence composition and cannot by itself supply local ribosome dynamics. Matched mRNA abundance is a control covariate for separating transcriptional abundance from translation-layer readout; it is not itself a ribosome-occupancy statistic.

\paragraph{Null boundary.}
The positive finite-row ceiling result is therefore bounded by a local null rule. If the joined same-organism, same-CDS, same-window matrix is absent, if the wobble or tAI surface is rank-degenerate, if matched mRNA controls are missing, or if the position-resolved ribosome-occupancy statistic is not computed under the declared controls, the chapter returns the null boundary rather than a biological promotion. In that case the evidence remains a finite coordinate and target-null result: $f_3$-stress is the only passing residual axis in the structural-order run, and the $B=200$ target-permutation experiment reports that observed $C^*$ exceeds the matched $95\%$ ceiling, but no translation realization has been established.

\paragraph{Claim boundary.}
No claim about translation, folding, physical admissibility, function, mechanism, phenotype, universality, protein abundance, protein stability, localization, or structural order as a measured biological state follows from this record unless a separate layer-matched contact supplies the corresponding observation. The strongest local conclusion is narrower: the $B^*_Q6$ $f_3$-stress coordinate survives the finite target-permutation ceiling test and is the only significant residual axis in the companion structural-order audit, so it is eligible for a position-resolved ribosome-occupancy window test under the specified same-row controls. Until that test exists as a joined finite table, the chapter records a bounded cross-layer boundary, not a downstream biological law.
