\subsection{Yeast abundance residual mediation by translation, mRNA stability, and 5-prime CDS folding energy}
\label{sec:bioreality-q6-yeast-abundance-structure-mediation}

\paragraph{Finite panel.}
This record tests whether the nine-dimensional $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual coordinate surface retains a protein-abundance association in yeast after ordinary sequence composition has been removed, and whether that association is materially carried by a 5-prime coding-region RNA-folding proxy beyond translation efficiency and mRNA stability. The finite panel is a five-way join over real yeast CDS codon counts, measured protein abundance, measured translation efficiency, measured mRNA half-life, and a CDS-local structure proxy. The join contains $4759$ genes, with a reported global join hit rate of $0.8818572656921754$ and a CDS join hit rate of $0.9156038419146592$. The TE table contributes $5128$ protein-keyed entries, the mRNA half-life table contributes $5379$ valid systematic-ORF entries, and the structure table contributes $6027$ valid MFE entries. CDS exclusions were finite and explicit: $369$ records missed the mRNA half-life match and $687$ missed the TE match, while duplicate stable identifiers, invalid lengths, empty sense-codon counts, missing protein identifiers, nonpositive abundance, nonpositive mRNA half-life, nonpositive TE, and unparseable yeast ORFs were all recorded as $0$.

\paragraph{Coordinates and controls.}
The tested residual coordinates are
$K\_AAA$, $Arg\_AGR$, $Ile\_AUA$, $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$, $Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG$, $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$, $Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG$, $Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY$, and $f3\_stress$.
The control matrix contains an intercept, $\log(\mathrm{CDS\ length\ in\ nt})$, the $20$ amino-acid composition proportions $A,C,D,E,F,G,H,I,K,L,M,N,P,Q,R,S,T,V,W,Y$, GC3 computed from the same real CDS codon counts, and the $M$-density baseline given by the fraction of sense codons lying on the union support of the nine projected $B^{*}_{Q6}$ vectors. After residualization the rank diagnostics are nondegenerate for the intended comparison: $\operatorname{rank}(Z)=23$, $\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde X)=9$, $\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde T\widetilde M)=2$, $\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde S)=1$, $\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde X\widetilde T\widetilde M)=11$, $\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde X\widetilde S)=10$, and $\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde X\widetilde T\widetilde M\widetilde S)=12$, with residual degrees of freedom $4736$. The residual energies used by the decomposition are $1724.6664873071313$ for protein abundance, $645.3764418081507$ for translation efficiency, $871992.3819246667$ for mRNA stability, and $28554.997251412347$ for the structure proxy.

\paragraph{Structure readout.}
The structure coordinate $S$ is not a folded-protein or physical-state measurement. It is the ViennaRNA RNAfold minimum free energy of the first $39$ nucleotides of the coding sequence, keyed by systematic ORF through the same yeast protein and transcript records. Thus the readout is a 5-prime CDS-local RNA proxy, not a 5-prime UTR structure, not an ensemble folding statistic, and not a measured initiation rate. This distinction matters because a sequence-derived MFE can share variance with GC and codon composition even after the declared controls are included.

\paragraph{Variance partition.}
Let $Q$ denote the residualized nine-coordinate $B^{*}_{Q6}$ block, $P$ the residualized matched protein-abundance readout, $T$ the residualized measured TE readout, $M$ the residualized mRNA-stability readout, and $S$ the residualized 5-prime CDS MFE readout. The computed protein-abundance association before the mediation blocks is
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2(Q \to P) &= 0.2715520808117803.
\end{aligned}
$$
Conditioning on the structure proxy alone gives
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2(Q \to P \mid S) &= 0.2706601505000995,
\end{aligned}
$$
so the structure-only fraction is only $0.003284564452662019$. Conditioning on measured TE together with mRNA stability gives a much larger attenuation,
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2(Q \to P \mid T,M) &= 0.13773967696311287,
\end{aligned}
$$
and adding the structure proxy after $T,M$ changes the value only to
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2(Q \to P \mid T,M,S) &= 0.13759011208318542.
\end{aligned}
$$
The corresponding incremental structure fraction beyond $T,M$ is $0.0005507778820193332$.

\paragraph{Permutation and finite-row verdict.}
The direct relation from the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ block to the 5-prime CDS MFE proxy is small but detectable in the finite panel: $R^2(S \sim Q)=0.003774248455422586$ with right-tail permutation $p=0.027972027972027972$. By contrast, the structure increment in the abundance residual after measured TE and mRNA stability is not supported at the same level: its right-tail permutation value is $p=0.1258741258741259$. The fitted energies agree with that reading. The $Q$ block explains $468.33677333459536$ units of residual protein-abundance energy and $107.77365427073924$ units of residual structure-proxy energy, but the improvement attributable to adding $Q$ after $T,M,S$ is $237.29705529470186$, essentially unchanged from $237.5550048307909$ after $T,M$ alone. In the related cross-organism protein-abundance mediation surface, the yeast entry has $M^{QTP}=0.9491551041216973$, $R^2_{QP}=0.29837624994140827$, $R^2_{QP\mid T}=0.0151709093608293$, and indirect difference $0.28320534058057895$, which is consistent with the present yeast panel assigning the large attenuation to translation-associated and transcript-stability covariates rather than to the 5-prime CDS MFE proxy.

\paragraph{Local interpretation.}
The finite result is therefore a bounded statistical statement. In yeast, on $4759$ joined rows, the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual block has a substantial protein-abundance association after amino-acid composition, CDS length, GC3, and $M$-density controls. Measured TE plus mRNA stability absorbs a large share of that association, while the 5-prime CDS MFE proxy contributes only a very small abundance-residual decrement beyond those readouts and does not pass the recorded permutation test for incremental structure mediation. The positive $Q \to S$ contact is best read as a weak sequence-to-local-MFE association under controls, not as evidence that the protein-abundance relation is structurally mediated.

\paragraph{Boundary of the claim.}
This chapter does not infer translation realization from codon geometry alone, does not infer protein folding from abundance, and does not infer physical admissibility, molecular function, mechanism, selection, phenotype, or universality from the variance partition. The allowed conclusion is confined to the finite yeast matrix and its declared controls: a controlled abundance association exists, its attenuation is mainly aligned with measured TE and mRNA stability, and the tested 5-prime CDS MFE proxy is not a supported downstream mediator beyond that block. Any stronger biological conclusion would require a separate layer-specific measurement and a new finite statistic tied to that layer.
