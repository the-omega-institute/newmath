\subsection{Yeast structure-conditioned abundance residual for $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$}\label{subsec:bioreality-q6-yeast-structure-mediation}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite surface.}
This record evaluates a finite yeast surface whose rows join coding-sequence composition, the nine $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates, measured translation efficiency, mRNA half-life, a local coding-region structure proxy, and matched protein abundance.  The coordinate block is
$K\_AAA$, $Arg\_AGR$, $Ile\_AUA$, $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$, $Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG$, $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$, $Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG$, $Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY$, and $f3\_stress$.
The control block contains an intercept, $\log(\mathrm{CDS\ length\ in\ nt})$, the twenty amino-acid composition proportions $A,C,D,E,F,G,H,I,K,L,M,N,P,Q,R,S,T,V,W,Y$, GC3 computed from real coding-sequence codon counts, and the $M$-density baseline defined by the fraction of sense codons on the union support of the nine projected $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ vectors.  After these controls, the fit has $4736$ residual degrees of freedom and rank diagnostics $\operatorname{rank}(Z)=23$, $\operatorname{rank}(X)=9$, $\operatorname{rank}(S)=1$, $\operatorname{rank}(T,M)=2$, and $\operatorname{rank}(T,M,S)=3$.

\paragraph{Joined empirical panel.}
The yeast panel contains $4759$ joined rows.  The reported overall join hit rate is $0.8818572656921754$, and the coding-sequence join hit rate is $0.9156038419146592$.  The translation-efficiency source contributes $5128$ protein-keyed genes, with $5128$ unique TE gene keys and no ambiguous TE gene keys.  The mRNA half-life table contributes $5379$ valid systematic-ORF records, and the structure table contributes $6027$ valid MFE records.  The structure coordinate is the ViennaRNA RNAfold minimum free energy over the first $39$ coding nucleotides, keyed by systematic ORF; the TE coordinate is the mean footprint normalized density divided by mean mRNA normalized density across the two rich-medium replicates in the Ingolia yeast dataset.  The skipped coding-sequence rows are concentrated in missing half-life and TE contacts: $369$ have no mRNA half-life match and $687$ have no TE match, with zero skipped rows for missing structure, nonpositive abundance, nonpositive TE, nonpositive mRNA half-life, invalid length, or unparseable yeast ORF.

\paragraph{Variance partition.}
Let $Q_e$ be the residualized nine-coordinate $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ matrix after the control block, let $P_e$ be residualized log protein abundance, let $S_e$ be residualized local coding-region MFE, and let $(T_e,M_e)$ denote residualized measured translation efficiency and mRNA stability.  The fitted quantities are read as finite row statistics, not as causal arrows:
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2(Q_e \to P_e) &= 0.2715520808117803,\\
R^2(Q_e \to P_e \mid S_e) &= 0.2706601505000995,\\
R^2(Q_e \to P_e \mid T_e,M_e) &= 0.13773967696311287,\\
R^2(Q_e \to P_e \mid T_e,M_e,S_e) &= 0.13759011208318542.
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus conditioning on the local MFE proxy alone removes only $0.0008919303116808$ absolute $R^2$ from the direct $Q_e$ to $P_e$ residual association, whereas conditioning on measured TE plus mRNA stability removes $0.13381240384866743$ absolute $R^2$.  The structure-only fraction is $0.003284564452662019$, and the structure contribution beyond measured TE plus mRNA stability is $0.0005507778820193332$ of the original $Q_e$ to $P_e$ residual association.  The direct $Q_e$ improvement energy falls from $466.79849101702615$ after conditioning on structure to $237.5550048307909$ after conditioning on TE and mRNA stability, and to $237.29705529470186$ after conditioning on all three readouts.

\paragraph{Structure contact.}
The local structure statistic is detectable but small.  The fitted structure readout satisfies
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2(Q_e \to S_e) &= 0.003774248455422586,\\
p_{\mathrm{right}}(Q_e \to S_e) &= 0.027972027972027972,\\
p_{\mathrm{right}}(\mathrm{structure\ beyond\ TE,M}) &= 0.1258741258741259.
\end{aligned}
$$
The first value says that the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual block has a finite, weak association with the first-$39$-nt MFE proxy after the stated controls.  The second says that this association is right-tail non-null under the recorded residual permutation test.  The third does not support a strong structure-mediated abundance component after TE and mRNA stability have already been conditioned.  The appropriate readback is therefore a controlled variance decomposition: the yeast rows show a large residual $Q_e$ to abundance association, a much larger attenuation through measured TE and mRNA stability than through the MFE proxy, and only a minute additional structure-conditioned fraction.

\paragraph{Relation to the translation-abundance panel.}
The broader translation-mediated abundance run supplies a useful scale check.  In the twelve-organism matrix, the same nine $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ coordinates are used, and the yeast row has $5815$ joined proteins, $275$ all-tRNA copies, and zero codons requiring geometric-mean fill-in for modeled tAI weights.  For yeast, the joint mediation scalar reports $R^2_{QP}=0.29837624994140827$, direct $R^2_{QP\mid T}=0.0151709093608293$, and mediated difference $0.28320534058057895$, giving $M^{QTP}=0.9491551041216973$.  This comparison does not convert the structure row into a mechanism claim; it only shows that, on the same coordinate family, yeast protein abundance has a strong translation-conditioned statistical component while the first-$39$-nt MFE proxy accounts for a much smaller residual share in the structure-conditioned panel.

\paragraph{Coordinate identity check.}
A separate structural-order identity run also identifies the nine-coordinate basis and reports $f3\_stress$ as the only coordinate passing its fold-level sign-flip gate.  Its weighted held-out $R^2$ change is $0.0031744502294756994$ with right-tail sign-flip probability $0.03125$, and its weighted absolute unit loading is $0.5109586606724208$.  The other coordinates do not pass that gate in the supplied run: for example $Arg\_AGR$ has $0.0020139270616115154$ with $p=0.142578125$, $Ile\_AUA$ has $0.0013067592718559024$ with $p=0.0693359375$, and $Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY$ has $-0.00045340330387383447$ with $p=0.529296875$.  This supports a cautious coordinate-level interpretation: the finite yeast structure-abundance surface can be reported as a $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual decomposition, but the dominant axis evidence points to $f3\_stress$ in a separate structural-order readout rather than to a general law over all nine coordinates.

\paragraph{Local boundary.}
The chapter records a bounded cross-layer statistical contact, not a biological mechanism.  The MFE coordinate is a CDS-only first-window proxy, not a measured initiation rate and not an ensemble RNA-structure measurement.  The panel is a single-organism yeast join across single readout sources, and the structure proxy is sequence-derived and partially GC-correlated even after the control block is applied.  No translation realization, folding pathway, physical admissibility, molecular function, phenotype, selection rule, or universal biological law follows from these rows.  Such claims would require separate layer-matched contacts and their own controlled statistics.  In particular, a direct $Q_i \to P_k$ abundance association must not be read as protein realization unless an intervening $Q_i \to T_j$ translation step is measured or explicitly bounded and survives the declared controls.
