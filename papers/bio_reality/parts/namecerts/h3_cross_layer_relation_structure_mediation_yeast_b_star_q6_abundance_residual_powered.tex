\subsection{Yeast structure-mediated abundance residual for $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$}
\label{sec:bioreality-yeast-structure-mediated-abundance-residual}

\paragraph{Finite surface.}
The finite object considered here is the yeast row set in which nine $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates are joined to measured translation efficiency, mRNA half-life, a CDS-local structure proxy, and matched protein abundance.  The coordinate list is
$$
\begin{aligned}
Q = \{&K\_AAA,\ Arg\_AGR,\ Ile\_AUA,\ Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR,\ Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG,\\
&Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY,\ Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG,\ Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY,\ f3\_stress\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The yeast join contains $4759$ genes after matching CDS codon counts, protein abundance, measured translation efficiency, mRNA half-life, and the structure readout.  The reported join hit rate is $0.8818572656921754$, with $5815$ CDS rows available upstream, $5128$ protein-keyed measured-TE rows, $5379$ valid mRNA half-life records, and $6027$ valid structure-MFE records.  The structure coordinate is the ViennaRNA minimum free energy for the first $39$ nucleotides of the coding sequence, keyed by systematic ORF.  The translation coordinate is the mean footprint normalized read density divided by the mean mRNA normalized read density across the Ingolia et al. rich-medium replicates.  The controls are an intercept, $\log(\mathrm{CDS\ length})$, twenty amino-acid composition proportions, GC3, and an $M$-density baseline over the union support of the nine projected residual vectors.  After control residualization, the rank diagnostics are non-vacuous: $\operatorname{rank}(Z)=23$, $\operatorname{rank}(X)=9$, $\operatorname{rank}(S)=1$, $\operatorname{rank}(T,M)=2$, and $4736$ residual degrees of freedom remain.

\paragraph{Decomposition statistic.}
Let $P_e$ denote residualized protein abundance, $Q_e$ the residualized nine-coordinate $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ matrix, $S_e$ the residualized 5-prime CDS MFE coordinate, and $(T_e,M_e)$ the residualized measured-TE and mRNA-stability coordinates.  The four fitted quantities are
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{QP} &= 0.2715520808117803,\\
R^2_{QP\mid S} &= 0.2706601505000995,\\
R^2_{QP\mid T,M} &= 0.13773967696311287,\\
R^2_{QP\mid T,M,S} &= 0.13759011208318542.
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the structure-only attenuation of the $Q\to P$ association is small:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\frac{R^2_{QP}-R^2_{QP\mid S}}{R^2_{QP}} &= 0.003284564452662019,\\
\frac{R^2_{QP\mid T,M}-R^2_{QP\mid T,M,S}}{R^2_{QP}} &= 0.0005507778820193332.
\end{aligned}
$$
The coordinate-to-structure fit is also small, $R^2(S_e\sim Q_e)=0.003774248455422586$.  Its right-tail permutation value is $p=0.027972027972027972$, while the incremental structure fraction beyond measured TE and mRNA stability has $p=0.1258741258741259$.  The first number allows a bounded statement that the residual coordinates carry a detectable but very weak relation to this CDS-local structure proxy.  The second number blocks any stronger claim that this structure proxy is a robust added mediator once measured translation efficiency and mRNA stability have already been included.

\paragraph{Relation to the broader protein-abundance mediation surface.}
The multi-organism translation-conditioned protein-abundance run gives context for the yeast row without replacing it.  In the yeast entry of that surface, the total residual-to-protein fit is $R^2=0.29837624994140827$, the direct fit after modeled tAI conditioning is $0.0151709093608293$, and the inferred mediated fraction is $M^{QTP}=0.9491551041216973$.  That entry is based on $5815$ yeast proteins and uses modeled per-protein tAI rather than measured ribosome occupancy.  The present yeast structure decomposition is stricter in a different direction: it uses measured TE, mRNA stability, and a 5-prime CDS MFE proxy in the same finite join, and it asks whether the structure proxy explains any residual $Q\to P$ association.  The answer is bounded: $Q$ has a large association with $P_e$ before measured TE and mRNA stability are conditioned, but the added structure-proxy increment after those coordinates is tiny and not permutation-supported at the same level.

\paragraph{Axis identity.}
The structural-order axis audit supplies a further finite check on which residual coordinate carries the relevant signal.  Across five folds, exactly one coordinate passes the fold-level sign-flip gate: $f3\_stress$.  Its weighted held-out increment is $0.0031744502294756994$, with sign-flip right-tail probability $0.03125$ and weighted absolute unit loading $0.5109586606724208$.  The other eight coordinates do not pass that gate: for example $Arg\_AGR$ has increment $0.0020139270616115154$ with $p=0.142578125$, $Ile\_AUA$ has $0.0013067592718559024$ with $p=0.0693359375$, and $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$ is negative at $-0.0003205780368125179$ with $p=0.779296875$.  This supports a coordinate-specific readback rather than a claim that every synonymous residual direction carries the same structural or abundance relation.

\paragraph{Boundary of interpretation.}
This chapter records a controlled finite-row variance partition, not a causal pathway.  The structure readout is a CDS-only first-window MFE estimate; it omits the 5-prime UTR, uses a single minimum-free-energy estimate rather than an ensemble, and remains partly tied to GC-rich sequence composition despite the control set.  The measured TE coordinate is a bulk readout, not a translation-initiation rate.  Protein abundance is steady-state abundance and is not isolated from turnover, localization, post-translational modification, pathway context, or measurement bias.  Consequently, no translation mechanism, folding claim, physical-admissibility claim, molecular-function claim, organismal-phenotype claim, selection claim, or universal biological law follows from this row set.  Any such assertion would require a separate layer-matched measurement and its own controlled statistic.  The upstream genetic-code contact is also bounded: the registered Phase 1 table check has $40/40$ successes in the independent replication binomial audit with upper-tail probability $9.094947017729282\times 10^{-13}$ and $0/20$ unexpected successes in the negative-control and ablation checks, but those facts concern codon-table geometry and module-classification contact only; they do not by themselves provide translation, structure, protein-output, function, or mechanism evidence.

\paragraph{Verdict.}
The yeast finite-row record is ready as a bounded cross-layer relation.  It supports the statement that the nine-coordinate $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual matrix has a substantial controlled association with matched protein abundance in the yeast join, that measured TE plus mRNA stability absorb a large part of that association, and that the 5-prime CDS MFE proxy contributes only a very small additional structure-mediated component.  The row also supports a weak coordinate-to-structure signal, mainly consistent with the independent $f3\_stress$ axis audit.  It does not support promotion to protein realization, folding, physical admissibility, function, mechanism, phenotype, selection, or universality.
