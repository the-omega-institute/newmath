\subsection{Yeast structure-conditioned abundance residuals for $B^*_{Q6}$}
\label{subsec:bioreality-yeast-structure-mediated-abundance-residuals}

\paragraph{Finite surface.}
This record studies a finite yeast panel in which each row joins a real coding sequence, a nine-coordinate $B^*_{Q6}$ synonymous-residual vector, a measured translation-efficiency readout, an mRNA-stability readout, a sequence-derived 5-prime coding-region structure proxy, and matched protein abundance. The intended readback is deliberately narrow: it asks whether the $B^*_{Q6}$ residual association with protein abundance is reduced when the model is conditioned on measured translation efficiency, mRNA stability, and the 5-prime coding-region minimum-free-energy proxy. The coordinate list is
$$
\begin{aligned}
Q = \{&K\_AAA,\ Arg\_AGR,\ Ile\_AUA,\ Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR,\ Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG,\\
&Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY,\ Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG,\ Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY,\ f3\_stress\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The control block $Z$ contains an intercept, $\log(\mathrm{CDS\ length\ in\ nt})$, the twenty amino-acid composition proportions computed from the real CDS codon counts, GC3, and an $M$-density baseline defined as the fraction of sense codons lying on the union support of the nine projected $B^*_{Q6}$ vectors. After these controls, the residual degrees of freedom are $4736$, the control rank is $23$, and the residualized $Q$ block has rank $9$. The joined yeast surface contains $4759$ rows. Its reported total join hit rate is $0.8818572656921754$, with $5815$ CDS rows available before the full join, $5128$ translation-efficiency protein identifiers, $5379$ valid mRNA half-life records by systematic ORF, and $6027$ valid 5-prime structure records.

\paragraph{Readout definitions.}
Protein abundance is the residualized response $P_e$. The translation coordinate $T$ is measured translation efficiency from the Ingolia et al. yeast rich-medium data, computed as mean footprint normalized read density divided by mean mRNA normalized read density across two rich-medium replicates. The mRNA-stability coordinate $M$ is the matched half-life readout. The structure coordinate $S$ is the ViennaRNA RNAfold minimum free energy on the first $39$ nucleotides of the CDS; it is a CDS-local folding proxy and is not a 5-prime UTR measurement. The fitted quantities are the incremental $R^2$ values for $Q_e$ after residualization by $Z$ and after additional conditioning on $S$, on $(T,M)$, or on $(T,M,S)$:
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{QP} &= 0.2715520808117803,\\
R^2_{QP\mid S} &= 0.2706601505000995,\\
R^2_{QP\mid T,M} &= 0.13773967696311287,\\
R^2_{QP\mid T,M,S} &= 0.13759011208318542.
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the structure-only attenuation fraction is $0.003284564452662019$, while the incremental structure fraction beyond measured translation efficiency and mRNA stability is only $0.0005507778820193332$. The residualized structure readout is weakly but detectably aligned with the $Q$ coordinates: $R^2(S_e\sim Q_e)=0.003774248455422586$ with right-tail permutation value $0.027972027972027972$. By contrast, the right-tail permutation value for the structure-incremental fraction is $0.1258741258741259$, so the structure term is not promoted to a causal or mechanistic mediator.

\paragraph{Rank and energy diagnostics.}
The rank audit shows that the finite design is not a vacuous saturated fit. The residualized structure block has rank $1$, the residualized $(T,M)$ block has rank $2$, and the residualized $(T,M,S)$ block has rank $3$. Adding $Q$ gives ranks $10$, $11$, and $12$ for $(Q,S)$, $(Q,T,M)$, and $(Q,T,M,S)$ respectively. The residual energies are $28554.997251412347$ for $S$, $645.3764418081507$ for $T$, $871992.3819246667$ for $M$, and $1724.6664873071313$ for $P$. The $Q$ block explains $468.33677333459536$ units of residual energy on $P$ and $107.77365427073924$ units on $S$. Its direct improvement energy after conditioning on $S$ is $466.79849101702615$, after conditioning on $(T,M)$ is $237.5550048307909$, and after conditioning on $(T,M,S)$ is $237.29705529470186$. The large drop from $R^2_{QP}$ to $R^2_{QP\mid T,M}$ is therefore a measured translation-and-mRNA-stability attenuation in this finite yeast panel, whereas the additional structure term is numerically small.

\paragraph{Relation to cross-organism protein-abundance mediation.}
A separate finite protein-abundance mediation audit on twelve organisms gives a useful calibration for the yeast result. In that audit, $Q$ is again the same nine-dimensional $B^*_{Q6}$ block, $T$ is modeled per-protein tAI from GtRNAdb tRNA gene-copy counts and dos Reis wobble weights, and $Y$ is log protein abundance from PAXdb joined to real CDS codon counts. For \textit{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}, the joined surface has $5815$ proteins, a $Z$ rank of $23$, a residual degree count of $5792$, and a $Q$ rank of $9$. The total $Q$ to protein-abundance association is $R^2=0.29837624994140827$, the direct association after conditioning on modeled tAI is $0.0151709093608293$, and the indirect difference is $0.28320534058057895$, giving a mediation fraction $0.9491551041216973$. Across the same audit, the mediation fractions remain in the unit interval for all twelve organisms, including $0.6084594553474505$ in \textit{Bacillus subtilis}, $0.612922252793667$ in \textit{Escherichia coli} K12 MG1655, $0.27206289160119157$ in human, and $0.01850668564589326$ in \textit{Sulfolobus solfataricus}. This comparison supports a bounded interpretation: yeast carries a strong translation-conditioned protein-abundance attenuation signal, while the structure-conditioned increment in the 5-prime MFE panel is much smaller.

\paragraph{Axis identity.}
The structure-mediation chapter should not be read as saying that every $B^*_{Q6}$ coordinate is equally responsible. The held-out structural-order audit identifies $f3\_stress$ as the single significant coordinate: it is both the dominant increment axis and the dominant loading axis, with weighted held-out increment $0.0031744502294756994$, weighted absolute unit loading $0.5109586606724208$, and sign-flip value $0.03125$. Other coordinates have weaker or nonsignificant held-out increments: $Arg\_AGR$ has $0.0020139270616115154$ with sign-flip value $0.142578125$, $Ile\_AUA$ has $0.0013067592718559024$ with sign-flip value $0.0693359375$, and $Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY$ is negative at $-0.00045340330387383447$ with sign-flip value $0.529296875$. The module-level conclusion is therefore that structural-order carriage is concentrated in $f3\_stress$ rather than distributed uniformly across the nine residual coordinates.

\paragraph{Local verdict.}
The finite yeast evidence supports a controlled variance-partition statement. After amino-acid composition, GC3, CDS length, and $M$-density controls, the $B^*_{Q6}$ block has a sizeable residual association with matched protein abundance. Conditioning on measured translation efficiency and mRNA stability removes about half of that association in the yeast structure panel, while conditioning on the 5-prime CDS MFE proxy alone removes only a small fraction. The structure proxy is statistically connected to $Q$ at the $S_e\sim Q_e$ level, but the incremental structure fraction beyond translation efficiency and mRNA stability is not strong enough to be treated as a pathway claim.

\paragraph{Local boundary.}
No translation mechanism, ribosome dynamics, folding pathway, physical admissibility, molecular function, organismal phenotype, selection law, or universal biological rule follows from this chapter by itself. The 5-prime MFE coordinate is sequence-derived and partly composition-correlated; the translation readout is dataset-specific; the protein-abundance readout is steady-state abundance and is not isolated from turnover, localization, post-translational modification, pathway context, or measurement bias. Any claim at those layers requires a separate matched contact and a new controlled statistic at that layer. In this chapter, the defensible statement is only the finite-row yeast variance partition: $B^*_{Q6}$ abundance residuals are strongly attenuated by measured translation efficiency plus mRNA stability, and only weakly further attenuated by the local CDS MFE proxy.
