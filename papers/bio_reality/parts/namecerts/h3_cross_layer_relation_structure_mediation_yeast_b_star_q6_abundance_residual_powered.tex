\subsection{Yeast abundance residual mediation by the B$^\ast_{Q6}$ surface}\label{subsec:bioreality-yeast-bstar-q6-abundance-residual-mediation}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite row surface.}
This record fixes a yeast-only cross-layer mediation surface whose rows are matched systematic ORFs with coding sequence, protein abundance, translation-efficiency, mRNA half-life, and a 5-prime coding-region structure proxy.  The retained join has $4759$ genes.  The reported CDS join hit rate is $0.9156038419146592$, while the full five-way join hit rate is $0.8818572656921754$.  The component finite tables contain $5815$ CDS rows joined to protein abundance before the extra readouts, $5128$ valid translation-efficiency rows keyed by protein identifier, $5379$ valid mRNA half-life rows keyed by systematic ORF, and $6027$ valid 5-prime coding-region MFE rows.  The skipped CDS ledger is also finite: $687$ rows lack the translation-efficiency match and $369$ lack the mRNA half-life match, with no skipped rows from missing structure, nonpositive abundance, nonpositive mRNA half-life, nonpositive translation efficiency, invalid length, duplicate stable identifier, or unparseable yeast ORF.

\paragraph{Coordinates and controls.}
The explanatory block is the nine-coordinate $B^\ast_{Q6}$ residual basis
$K\_AAA$, $Arg\_AGR$, $Ile\_AUA$, $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$, $Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG$, $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$, $Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG$, $Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY$, and $f3\_stress$.  The covariate table uses an intercept, $\log(\mathrm{CDS\ length})$, all twenty amino-acid composition proportions computed from real CDS codon counts, GC3, and the M-density baseline defined by the fraction of sense codons lying on the union support of the nine projected coordinates.  Its diagnostic rank is $23$ for the control matrix, with $24$ control columns before rank reduction.  After control residualization, the residual degrees of freedom are $4736$; the residual energies are $1724.6664873071313$ for protein abundance, $645.3764418081507$ for measured translation efficiency, $871992.3819246667$ for mRNA half-life, and $28554.997251412347$ for the structure proxy.  The residual coordinate block has rank $9$, the residual structure block has rank $1$, the joint translation-efficiency plus mRNA-stability block has rank $2$, and the joint translation-efficiency, mRNA-stability, and structure block has rank $3$.

\paragraph{Mediation decomposition.}
Let $Q$ denote the residualized nine-coordinate block, $P$ the residualized log protein-abundance readout, $T$ the residualized measured translation-efficiency readout, $M$ the residualized mRNA-stability readout, and $S$ the residualized 5-prime CDS MFE readout.  The finite regression audit reports
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2(Q \to P) &= 0.2715520808117803,\\
R^2(Q \to P\mid S) &= 0.2706601505000995,\\
R^2(Q \to P\mid T,M) &= 0.13773967696311287,\\
R^2(Q \to P\mid T,M,S) &= 0.13759011208318542.
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the structure-only attenuation fraction is $0.003284564452662019$, whereas the structure-incremental fraction after measured translation efficiency and mRNA stability is only $0.0005507778820193332$.  The coordinate-to-structure residual association is small but finite, with $R^2(S\sim Q)=0.003774248455422586$ and a right-tail permutation value $p=0.027972027972027972$.  The structure-incremental permutation value is weaker, $p=0.1258741258741259$.  The energy diagnostics agree with this scale: the coordinate block explains $468.33677333459536$ energy units on $P$ and $107.77365427073924$ on $S$, while the direct coordinate improvement on $P$ falls from $466.79849101702615$ after conditioning on $S$ to $237.5550048307909$ after conditioning on $T,M$, and to $237.29705529470186$ after conditioning on $T,M,S$.

\paragraph{Relation to the translation-mediated abundance surface.}
The wider organism panel for the same nine-coordinate family gives a separate bounded comparison for protein abundance mediated by modeled per-protein tAI.  In yeast, that panel has $5815$ joined proteins, rank-sufficient residual blocks, $T$ residual energy $6.90537888244409$, protein-abundance residual energy $2958.1865890925337$, and a mediation fraction $M^{QTP}=0.9491551041216973$ with total $R^2(Q\to P)=0.29837624994140827$, direct conditioned $R^2(Q\to P\mid T)=0.0151709093608293$, and indirect difference $0.28320534058057895$.  The present yeast structure row is different: it uses measured translation efficiency and mRNA stability, then asks whether a CDS-only structure proxy accounts for additional abundance residual association.  Its answer is bounded and asymmetric.  Translation-efficiency plus mRNA-stability conditioning removes a large part of the direct $Q$--$P$ association, while the additional 5-prime MFE term contributes only a very small incremental fraction under the declared controls.

\paragraph{Readback.}
The admissible readback is a controlled variance partition on finite matched yeast rows.  It says that $B^\ast_{Q6}$ residual coordinates have a measurable association with matched protein abundance in this dataset, that measured translation efficiency and mRNA stability account for a substantial part of that association, and that the 5-prime CDS MFE proxy supplies only a small extra decomposition term.  The structure proxy is specifically the ViennaRNA RNAfold minimum free energy of the first $39$ nucleotides of the CDS; it is not a 5-prime UTR assay, not an ensemble structure measurement, and not a measured initiation-rate experiment.  The result is therefore a statistical decomposition of matched yeast rows, not a causal pathway claim.

\paragraph{Local boundary.}
No translation mechanism, folding conclusion, physical admissibility, molecular function, organismal phenotype, selection law, or universal biological rule follows from this row alone.  Protein abundance is a steady-state downstream readout with turnover, localization, post-translational modification, sample-preparation, and ionization confounding.  The CDS MFE readout is sequence-derived and partially GC-correlated, even though GC3 and amino-acid composition are included in the controls.  Any stronger assertion would require a separate layer-matched contact: observed ribosome or translation-rate rows for translation, structure experiments or validated structure predictions for folding, perturbation or rescue data for mechanism, and functional or phenotype assays for biological role.  In the absence of those contacts, this chapter records only the finite controlled mediation surface described above.
