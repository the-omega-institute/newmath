\subsection{Yeast abundance residuals with a translation-and-structure conditioning surface}
\label{sec:bioreality-yeast-abundance-residual-structure-mediation}

\paragraph{Finite row surface.}
This record treats the yeast abundance calculation as a finite cross-layer surface whose rows join coding sequence, $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates, measured translation efficiency, mRNA half-life, a coding-region structure proxy, and matched protein abundance. The joined table contains $4759$ yeast rows. The join uses $5815$ coding sequences with abundance records, $5128$ proteins with translation-efficiency entries, $5379$ valid mRNA half-life records by systematic ORF, and $6027$ valid coding-region MFE records. The reported overall join hit rate is $0.8818572656921754$, and the CDS join hit rate is $0.9156038419146592$. The translation-efficiency readout is the mean footprint normalized read density across two rich-medium replicates divided by the mean mRNA normalized read density across two rich-medium replicates in the Ingolia yeast rich-medium data. The structure readout is a ViennaRNA RNAfold minimum free energy on the first $39$ nucleotides of the coding region, keyed by systematic ORF. The mRNA control is log half-life from the Neymotin table used through the Cheng RNA resource, with payload hash \texttt{cee1ee9a816f0ec1e351bc2d63d4838ae603ea20566fc3ff529514a6ba6cf7d2}.

\paragraph{Residual coordinates and controls.}
The residual coordinate block has $9$ columns: $K\_AAA$, $Arg\_AGR$, $Ile\_AUA$, $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$, $Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG$, $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$, $Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG$, $Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY$, and $f3\_stress$. The control matrix contains an intercept, $\log(\mathrm{CDS\ length\ in\ nt})$, the $20$ amino-acid composition proportions $A,C,D,E,F,G,H,I,K,L,M,N,P,Q,R,S,T,V,W,Y$, GC3 computed from real CDS codon counts, and an M-density baseline given by the fraction of sense codons on the union support of the nine projected $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ coordinate vectors. The numerical rank audit reports $\operatorname{rank}(Z)=23$, $\operatorname{rank}(X)=9$, residual degrees of freedom $4736$, $\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde S)=1$, $\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde T,\widetilde M)=2$, and $\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde T,\widetilde M,\widetilde S)=3$. Thus the fitted surface is not a verbal association: it is a finite controlled regression decomposition with explicit rank checks after residualization.

\paragraph{Variance partition.}
Let $Q_e$ denote the residualized $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ coordinate block, $P_e$ residualized log protein abundance, $T_e$ residualized measured translation efficiency, $M_e$ residualized mRNA half-life, and $S_e$ residualized $5'$ coding-region MFE. The reported quantities are incremental $R^2$ values for $Q_e$ after the stated controls:
$$\begin{aligned}
R^2(Q \to P) &= 0.2715520808117803,\\
R^2(Q \to P \mid S) &= 0.2706601505000995,\\
R^2(Q \to P \mid T,M) &= 0.13773967696311287,\\
R^2(Q \to P \mid T,M,S) &= 0.13759011208318542.
\end{aligned}$$
The structure-only attenuation fraction is $0.003284564452662019$, while the additional structure fraction beyond measured translation efficiency and mRNA half-life is $0.0005507778820193332$. The direct structure association of the residual coordinates is small but nonzero, with $R^2(S_e\sim Q_e)=0.003774248455422586$ and right-tail permutation value $0.027972027972027972$. The right-tail permutation value for the incremental structure fraction is $0.1258741258741259$, so the chapter reads the MFE term as a weak sequence-derived attenuation component rather than as an established causal structural path.

\paragraph{Energy and rank diagnostics.}
The residualized protein-abundance energy is $1724.6664873071313$, the residualized structure energy is $28554.997251412347$, the residualized measured-TE energy is $645.3764418081507$, and the residualized mRNA-half-life energy is $871992.3819246667$. The $Q$ block explains $468.33677333459536$ units of protein-abundance energy and $107.77365427073924$ units of structure-proxy energy. After conditioning on structure, the direct $Q$ improvement energy is $466.79849101702615$; after conditioning on measured TE and mRNA half-life it is $237.5550048307909$; after conditioning on TE, mRNA half-life, and structure together it is $237.29705529470186$. The dominant numerical reduction therefore comes from the measured-TE plus mRNA-half-life side of the surface, not from the $5'$ MFE term alone.

\paragraph{Relation to the broader protein-abundance mediation surface.}
A separate finite protein-abundance mediation calculation over twelve organisms gives a useful comparison point. In that calculation, the yeast organism has $5815$ joined proteins, $275$ all-tRNA copies, no zero raw-weight codons filled by the geometric-mean fallback, and a controlled $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ to protein-abundance total value $c=0.29837624994140827$. Conditioning on modeled per-protein tAI leaves $c'=0.0151709093608293$, giving an indirect component $0.28320534058057895$ and a modeled mediation fraction $0.9491551041216973$. That comparison does not replace the measured yeast TE, mRNA half-life, and MFE calculation above; it says that the yeast abundance residual is numerically compatible with a translation-conditioned readout being the main attenuation surface, while the $5'$ MFE proxy supplies only a small additional partition in the five-way joined yeast table.

\paragraph{Modeled translation contact boundary.}
The modeled tAI survival matrix uses $3$ organisms, $61$ sense codons, $9$ residual coordinates, GtRNAdb tRNA gene-copy counts, and dos Reis wobble weights. Its bounded coupling audit reports $81$ entries above the threshold $0.01$, with maximum absolute $S_{ij}$ and maximum absolute diagonal entry both $3801961.031607896$. This is still explicitly underpowered for a powered survival statement because the organism count is $3$ rather than at least $40$. It can support bounded codon-adaptation readback and rank inspection, but it is not a substitute for same-condition ribosome profiling, matched protein abundance, mRNA control, and the finite mediation matrix used in the yeast abundance record.

\paragraph{Local boundary of interpretation.}
The chapter supports a controlled statistical statement: in yeast, the association between $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates and matched protein abundance is substantially attenuated by measured translation efficiency plus mRNA half-life, while the first-$39$-nt coding-region MFE proxy accounts for only a small additional fraction. The calculation does not prove a causal pathway. It does not establish translation realization from residual geometry alone, does not infer folding from a single MFE proxy, does not establish physical admissibility, molecular function, organismal phenotype, mechanism, selection, or universality, and does not promote a direct $Q_i\to P_k$ association unless the relevant $Q_i\to T_j$ translation-readout step is present and controlled. Any such higher-layer assertion requires its own layer-matched measurement and its own finite statistic.
