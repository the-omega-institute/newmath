\subsection{Yeast abundance residual mediation by a coding-region structure proxy}\label{sec:bsp-q6-yeast-abundance-structure-mediation}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite row surface.}
This record evaluates a yeast-specific cross-layer surface whose rows join coding-sequence codon counts, nine $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates, matched protein abundance, measured translation efficiency, mRNA half-life, and a sequence-derived 5-prime coding-region folding proxy.  The finite join contains $4759$ genes after matching yeast ORF identifiers across the coding-sequence, structure, translation-efficiency, mRNA-stability, and protein-abundance tables.  The reported overall join hit rate is $0.8818572656921754$, and the coding-sequence join hit rate is $0.9156038419146592$.  The translation-efficiency source contributes $5128$ valid protein-keyed genes, the structure table contributes $6027$ valid MFE rows, and the mRNA table contributes $5379$ valid half-life records.  The structure readout is the ViennaRNA RNAfold minimum free energy of the first $39$ coding nucleotides, not a measured folded state and not a 5-prime UTR initiation assay.

\paragraph{Coordinates and controls.}
The residual coordinate block has dimension $9$ and consists of $K\_AAA$, $Arg\_AGR$, $Ile\_AUA$, $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$, $Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG$, $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$, $Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG$, $Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY$, and $f3\_stress$.  The control matrix contains an intercept, $\log(\mathrm{CDS\ length\ in\ nt})$, the $20$ amino-acid composition proportions, GC3 from the same coding sequences, and the M-density baseline, defined as the fraction of sense codons on the union support of the nine projected residual vectors.  After residualizing by this control matrix, the diagnostic ranks are $\mathrm{rank}(Z)=23$, $\mathrm{rank}(X)=9$, $\mathrm{rank}(S)=1$, $\mathrm{rank}(T,M)=2$, and $\mathrm{rank}(T,M,S)=3$, with $4736$ residual degrees of freedom.  Thus the fitted surface is not a one-row mnemonic: it is a finite matrix calculation with nontrivial residual rank for the residual coordinates, the structure proxy, measured translation efficiency, mRNA stability, and protein abundance.

\paragraph{Variance partition.}
Let $Q_e$ denote the control-residualized $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ coordinate matrix, $P_e$ the control-residualized protein-abundance readout, $S_e$ the residualized 5-prime coding-region MFE proxy, and $(T_e,M_e)$ the residualized measured translation-efficiency and mRNA-stability readouts.  The computed abundance association and conditional increments are
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{QP} &= 0.2715520808117803,\\
R^2_{QP\mid S} &= 0.2706601505000995,\\
R^2_{QP\mid T,M} &= 0.13773967696311287,\\
R^2_{QP\mid T,M,S} &= 0.13759011208318542.
\end{aligned}
$$
The structure-only attenuation fraction is therefore $0.003284564452662019$, while the structure increment beyond measured translation efficiency and mRNA stability is $0.0005507778820193332$.  The direct structure association is also small but finite: $R^2(S_e\sim Q_e)=0.003774248455422586$, with right-tail permutation value $p=0.027972027972027972$.  The incremental structure fraction beyond $(T,M)$ has right-tail permutation value $p=0.1258741258741259$, so the structure term is not promoted to a strong independent mediator after the translation-efficiency and mRNA-stability block is already present.

\paragraph{Energy diagnostics.}
The residual-energy ledger gives $P$ residual energy $1724.6664873071313$, structure residual energy $28554.997251412347$, translation-efficiency residual energy $645.3764418081507$, and mRNA-stability residual energy $871992.3819246667$.  The $Q$ block explains $468.33677333459536$ units of energy on protein abundance and $107.77365427073924$ units on the structure proxy.  Conditioning on structure leaves $466.79849101702615$ units of direct $Q$ improvement on protein abundance, whereas conditioning on measured translation efficiency and mRNA stability leaves $237.5550048307909$, and conditioning on all three leaves $237.29705529470186$.  This is the numerical reason the chapter is read as a controlled abundance-residual decomposition rather than as a structural mechanism claim: the structure proxy removes only a very small part of the residual-to-abundance association, and its extra contribution after $(T,M)$ is not permutation-supported at the same level as the direct $Q\to S$ association.

\paragraph{Relation to broader translation-mediated abundance rows.}
The same nine-coordinate block has a broader multi-organism abundance mediation calculation in which modeled per-protein tAI is used as a bounded translation readout.  In yeast, that calculation reports $R^2_{QP}=0.29837624994140827$, direct $R^2_{QP\mid T}=0.0151709093608293$, and an indirect difference of $0.28320534058057895$, giving $M^{QTP}=0.9491551041216973$.  Those numbers support the decision to condition the yeast structure calculation on measured translation efficiency and mRNA stability before reading any structure proxy: direct residual-to-protein abundance is not enough to claim propagation through a protein layer unless the translation readout is explicitly represented.  Separately, across the structural-order audit, the only coordinate passing the fold-level sign-flip gate is $f3\_stress$, with weighted held-out $\Delta R^2=0.0031744502294756994$, unit loading $0.5109586606724208$, and sign-flip value $p=0.03125$.  This supplies a named coordinate identity for follow-up testing, but it does not turn the yeast MFE proxy into a folded-structure observation.

\paragraph{Local interpretation boundary.}
The finite-row result is a statistical variance partition on one yeast matched dataset.  It allows the paper to say that the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual block carries a sizeable controlled association with matched protein abundance, that measured translation efficiency and mRNA stability account for a large portion of that association, and that a first-39-nt coding-region MFE proxy has a small direct relation to the residual block with weak incremental contribution after $(T,M)$.  It does not establish a causal pathway, a translation mechanism, a folded structure, physical admissibility, molecular function, organismal phenotype, selection, or a universal biological rule.  Protein abundance is steady-state abundance; measured translation efficiency is a dataset-specific readout; mRNA half-life is a transcript-level covariate; and the MFE value is a sequence-derived single-structure proxy.  Any stronger biological layer must be supplied by a separate matched contact and a new controlled statistic at that layer.
