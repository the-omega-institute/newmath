\subsection{Yeast structure-conditioned abundance residual for $B^*_{Q6}$}\label{subsec:bioreality-q6-yeast-structure-mediated-abundance}\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite surface.} The finite surface for this record consists of matched yeast rows carrying nine $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinates, a measured translation-efficiency coordinate, an mRNA half-life coordinate, a coding-region structure proxy, and a matched protein-abundance response. The residual coordinates are
$$\begin{aligned}
Q=\{&K\_AAA,\ Arg\_AGR,\ Ile\_AUA,\ Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR,\ Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG,\\
&Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY,\ Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG,\ Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY,\ f3\_stress\}.
\end{aligned}$$
The control block contains an intercept, $\log(\mathrm{CDS\ length})$, the twenty amino-acid composition proportions computed from real CDS codon counts, GC3, and the $M$-density baseline defined by the fraction of sense codons lying on the union support of the nine projected $B^*_{Q6}$ vectors. After these controls, the joined panel has $4759$ genes, residual degrees of freedom $4736$, control rank $23$, residual $Q$ rank $9$, residual structure rank $1$, residual measured-TE plus mRNA-stability rank $2$, and joint residual $Q,T,M,S$ rank $12$. The five-way join is nontrivial rather than symbolic: $5815$ CDS rows were joined before filtering, the final join hit rate was $0.8818572656921754$, the CDS join hit rate was $0.9156038419146592$, $5128$ genes carried valid TE-by-protein keys, $6027$ genes carried valid 5-prime CDS MFE values, and $5379$ valid mRNA half-life records were available by systematic ORF.

\paragraph{Readout definitions.} The protein response $P$ is matched abundance, residualized against the control block. The translation coordinate $T$ is measured translation efficiency from the Ingolia yeast rich-medium footprint and mRNA quantification files, computed as mean footprint normalized read density divided by mean mRNA normalized read density across the two rich-medium replicates. The mRNA-stability coordinate $M$ is the matched half-life readout. The structure coordinate $S$ is a sequence-derived 5-prime coding-region minimum-free-energy proxy from the first $39$ nt of the CDS. It is a CDS-only MFE statistic; it is not a 5-prime UTR measurement, not an ensemble folding summary, and not a measured initiation-rate assay.

\paragraph{Variance partition.} Let $Q_e,T_e,M_e,S_e,P_e$ denote residualized versions of the nine-dimensional residual coordinate matrix, the measured translation-efficiency readout, mRNA stability, the structure proxy, and protein abundance after the control block. The registered decomposition is
$$\begin{aligned}
R^2_{QP} &= R^2(P_e\sim Q_e)=0.2715520808117803,\\
R^2_{QP\mid S} &= 0.2706601505000995,\\
R^2_{QP\mid T,M} &= 0.13773967696311287,\\
R^2_{QP\mid T,M,S} &= 0.13759011208318542.
\end{aligned}$$
Thus conditioning on the measured-TE and mRNA-stability block removes far more of the $Q$-to-protein residual association than conditioning on the 5-prime CDS MFE proxy alone. The structure-only fraction is
$$\begin{aligned}
\frac{R^2_{QP}-R^2_{QP\mid S}}{R^2_{QP}}=0.003284564452662019,
\end{aligned}$$
whereas the structure-incremental fraction beyond measured TE and mRNA stability is
$$\begin{aligned}
\frac{R^2_{QP\mid T,M}-R^2_{QP\mid T,M,S}}{R^2_{QP}}=0.0005507778820193332.
\end{aligned}$$
The direct $Q$-to-structure residual fit is small but detectable in the registered statistic, with $S_Q$ structure $R^2=0.003774248455422586$ and right-tail permutation value $p=0.027972027972027972$. The additional structure-incremental fraction after $T,M$ does not pass the same level of evidence, with right-tail permutation value $p=0.1258741258741259$.

\paragraph{Rank and energy audit.} The rank diagnostics rule out a purely verbal mediation claim. The residual energies were $871992.3819246667$ for $M$, $28554.997251412347$ for $S$, $645.3764418081507$ for $T$, and $1724.6664873071313$ for $P$. The explained energy of $Q$ on $P$ was $468.33677333459536$, while the explained energy of $Q$ on $S$ was $107.77365427073924$. The direct $Q$ improvement energy after conditioning on $S$ was $466.79849101702615$; after conditioning on $T,M$ it was $237.5550048307909$; after conditioning on $T,M,S$ it was $237.29705529470186$. This is the quantitative reason for the chapter verdict: the yeast surface supports a controlled statistical decomposition of the abundance residual, but the structure proxy contributes only a very small additional component once the measured translation and mRNA-stability coordinates are present.

\paragraph{Relation to the broader protein-abundance mediation surface.} A separate multi-organism protein-abundance mediation computation used the same nine $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinates and a modeled per-protein tAI readout. In that broader surface, the yeast row had $5815$ joined proteins, residual degrees of freedom $5792$, residual $Q$ rank $9$, residual $T$ rank $1$, and a mediation fraction $M^{QTP}=0.9491551041216973$ with $R^2_{QP}=0.29837624994140827$, $R^2_{QP\mid T}=0.0151709093608293$, and indirect increment $0.28320534058057895$. This supports the use of translation-conditioned abundance decompositions as finite statistical objects, but it does not replace the yeast structure-conditioned calculation above: the present record uses measured TE, matched mRNA stability, matched abundance, and a 5-prime CDS MFE proxy on the same yeast panel.

\paragraph{Boundary of interpretation.} The admissible conclusion is a controlled variance partition on a finite yeast matrix. A direct $Q_i\to P_k$ association is not read as protein realization unless the corresponding translation-readout step is present and evaluated under the declared controls. A structure proxy association is not a folding claim, and the MFE coordinate is not a physical-state assay. The chapter therefore does not assert molecular mechanism, folded structure, physical admissibility, function, phenotype, selection, or universality. Any such assertion would require a separate layer-matched contact and its own finite statistic. The current result keeps $B^*_{Q6}$ at cross-layer abundance-residual decomposition scope: measured TE and mRNA stability account for a large part of the residual protein-abundance association, while the 5-prime CDS MFE component is small, statistically visible for $Q\to S$, and not sufficient for a causal or structural promotion.
