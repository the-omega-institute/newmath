\subsection{Yeast structure mediation of the $B^*_{Q6}$ abundance residual}\label{sec:biorality-q6-yeast-structure-mediation}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite surface.}
This record concerns a finite yeast join in which the nine $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinates are evaluated against matched translation, mRNA-stability, local coding-structure, and protein-abundance readouts.  The coordinate block is
$K\_AAA$, $Arg\_AGR$, $Ile\_AUA$, $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$, $Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG$, $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$, $Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG$, $Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY$, and $f3\_stress$.  The control block contains an intercept, $\log(\mathrm{CDS\ length})$, the twenty amino-acid composition proportions, GC3, and an $M$-density baseline defined on the union support of the nine projected coordinates.  After these controls, the fitted residual design has $\operatorname{rank}(Z)=23$, $\operatorname{rank}(X)=9$, and residual degrees of freedom $4736$, so the inference is not a one-row or rank-saturated readback.

\paragraph{Joined rows and measured slots.}
The computed yeast surface joins $4759$ rows across CDS sequence, protein abundance, measured translation efficiency, mRNA half-life, and a coding-region structure proxy.  The reported CDS join hit rate is $0.9156038419146592$, and the five-way join hit rate is $0.8818572656921754$.  The translation slot is the mean rich-medium footprint normalized read density divided by mean matched mRNA normalized read density from the Ingolia 2009 BY4741 data; the mRNA-stability slot is the Neymotin half-life table with $5379$ valid systematic-ORF records; the structure slot is the ViennaRNA RNAfold minimum free energy for the first $39$ nt of the CDS, with $6027$ valid structure records.  The resulting finite row set is therefore a yeast, condition-linked statistical surface, not a cross-organism law.

\paragraph{Variance decomposition.}
Let $Q_e$ denote the residualized nine-coordinate $B^*_{Q6}$ block, $P_e$ residualized protein abundance, $S_e$ residualized 5-prime CDS MFE, $T_e$ residualized measured translation efficiency, and $M_e$ residualized mRNA stability.  The run reports the following incremental $R^2$ values:
$$\begin{aligned}
R^2(Q_e \to P_e) &= 0.2715520808117803,\\
R^2(Q_e \to P_e \mid S_e) &= 0.2706601505000995,\\
R^2(Q_e \to P_e \mid T_e,M_e) &= 0.13773967696311287,\\
R^2(Q_e \to P_e \mid T_e,M_e,S_e) &= 0.13759011208318542.
\end{aligned}$$
Thus, conditioning on the local MFE proxy alone removes only a small part of the $Q_e$ to $P_e$ association, while conditioning on measured translation efficiency together with mRNA stability removes a much larger part.  The structure-only fraction is $0.003284564452662019$, and the additional structure fraction beyond $T_e,M_e$ is $0.0005507778820193332$.  The direct structure readback is also small: $R^2(S_e \sim Q_e)=0.003774248455422586$ with right-tail permutation value $0.027972027972027972$, while the incremental structure fraction has right-tail permutation value $0.1258741258741259$.

\paragraph{Rank and energy diagnostics.}
The numerical ranks are consistent with a finite controlled decomposition rather than an unconstrained promotion of sequence geometry.  The residual energies are $871992.3819246667$ for the mRNA-stability slot, $28554.997251412347$ for the structure slot, $645.3764418081507$ for the translation-efficiency slot, and $1724.6664873071313$ for the protein-abundance slot.  The explained energy of $X$ on $P$ is $468.33677333459536$, while the explained energy of $X$ on $S$ is $107.77365427073924$.  After conditioning on $S$, the direct improvement energy of $X$ is $466.79849101702615$; after conditioning on $T,M$, it is $237.5550048307909$; and after conditioning on $T,M,S$, it is $237.29705529470186$.  These numbers give a consistent read: the large attenuation is associated with the measured translation and mRNA-stability block, while the coding-region MFE proxy contributes only a narrow additional partition.

\paragraph{Relation to the broader translation-mediated abundance run.}
A separate matched protein-abundance mediation computation over twelve organisms gives the relevant comparison point for the translation-mediated route.  In that broader run, yeast has $5815$ joined proteins, $275$ all-tRNA gene-copy entries, and no zero raw wobble-weight entries needing geometric-mean filling.  For yeast, the joint nine-dimensional $Q \to T \to P$ decomposition gives total $R^2_{QP}=0.29837624994140827$, direct $R^2_{QP\mid T}=0.0151709093608293$, indirect difference $0.28320534058057895$, and mediated fraction $0.9491551041216973$.  The structure-mediation run is therefore best read as a finer yeast-only partition layered on top of a strong translation-conditioned abundance attenuation pattern, not as independent evidence for folding or molecular function.

\paragraph{Modeled translation-contact boundary.}
The modeled-tAI survival contact supplies only a bounded codon-adaptation readout.  In the three-organism modeled-tAI run, the finite matrix had $81$ entries above threshold $0.01$ and maximum absolute $S_{ij}=3801961.031607896$, but the same record explicitly states that $3$ organisms are insufficient for powered survival and that this is modeled coupling rather than ribosome-profiling or proteomics validation.  This matters locally because the yeast chapter uses measured TE, mRNA stability, protein abundance, and a sequence-derived MFE proxy; none of these slots turns the $B^*_{Q6}$ coordinate system itself into a biological mechanism.

\paragraph{Admissible conclusion.}
The supported chapter-level statement is a controlled yeast variance-partition result: the $B^*_{Q6}$ residual block has a substantial association with protein-abundance residuals after amino-acid composition, GC3, CDS length, and $M$-density controls; measured translation efficiency plus mRNA stability absorbs much of that association; the 5-prime CDS MFE proxy accounts for only a small additional part.  The allowed notation is a finite matrix of residualized associations among $Q_e$, $T_e$, $M_e$, $S_e$, and $P_e$.

\paragraph{Local no-promotion rule.}
No translation mechanism, folding pathway, physical admissibility, molecular function, phenotype, selection principle, or universal rule follows from this chapter by itself.  Protein abundance is a downstream measurement, not a folded-structure assay.  The MFE slot is a CDS-only minimum-free-energy proxy for the first $39$ nt, not a measured initiation rate, ensemble structure, or protein-folding readout.  A stronger claim would require an additional layer-matched contact and a new controlled statistic showing that the same residual coordinate first survives the relevant translation readout and then propagates into that added layer under the stated controls.
