\subsection{Yeast abundance residuals through measured translation and 5-prime coding-region structure}\label{sec:bioreality-q6-yeast-structure-mediation-abundance-residual}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite surface.}
This record evaluates a finite yeast mediation surface whose rows join $B^*_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates, measured translation efficiency, mRNA half-life, a sequence-derived 5-prime coding-region structure proxy, and matched protein abundance. The coordinate block has nine residual axes: $K\_AAA$, $Arg\_AGR$, $Ile\_AUA$, $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$, $Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG$, $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$, $Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG$, $Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY$, and $f3\_stress$. The control block contains an intercept, $\log(\mathrm{CDS\ length\ in\ nt})$, twenty amino-acid composition proportions from the real CDS codon counts, GC3, and an $M$-density baseline defined as the fraction of sense codons on the union support of the nine projected $B^*_{Q6}$ vectors. The joined yeast table contains $4759$ rows, assembled from $5815$ CDS rows, $5128$ proteins with measured translation-efficiency keys by protein id, $5379$ valid mRNA half-life records, and $6027$ valid 5-prime coding-region MFE records. The reported CDS join hit rate is $0.9156038419146592$, and the full five-way join hit rate is $0.8818572656921754$.

\paragraph{Readout definitions.}
The protein readout $P$ is the residualized matched protein-abundance quantity. The translation readout $T$ is measured translation efficiency from the Ingolia et al. yeast rich-medium footprint and mRNA quantification files, computed as mean footprint normalized density divided by mean mRNA normalized density across the two rich-medium replicates. The mRNA-stability readout $M$ is the matched half-life control. The structure proxy $S$ is the ViennaRNA RNAfold minimum free energy of the first $39$ coding nucleotides; it is a CDS-only proxy and does not include the 5-prime UTR. After residualizing against the control block $Z$, the decomposition uses
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{QP} &= R^2(P_e \sim Q_e),\\
R^2_{QP\mid S} &= \Delta R^2(P_e \sim Q_e \mid S_e),\\
R^2_{QP\mid T,M} &= \Delta R^2(P_e \sim Q_e \mid T_e,M_e),\\
R^2_{QP\mid T,M,S} &= \Delta R^2(P_e \sim Q_e \mid T_e,M_e,S_e).
\end{aligned}
$$
The structure-only fraction is $(R^2_{QP}-R^2_{QP\mid S})/R^2_{QP}$, and the structure-incremental fraction is $(R^2_{QP\mid T,M}-R^2_{QP\mid T,M,S})/R^2_{QP}$. These are variance-partition statistics on finite joined rows, not causal path coefficients.

\paragraph{Observed finite-row decomposition.}
The total residual association of the nine-coordinate $Q$ block with protein abundance is
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{QP} &= 0.2715520808117803,\\
R^2_{QP\mid S} &= 0.2706601505000995,\\
R^2_{QP\mid T,M} &= 0.13773967696311287,\\
R^2_{QP\mid T,M,S} &= 0.13759011208318542.
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the measured translation plus mRNA-stability conditioning absorbs a large part of the direct $Q$-to-protein association on this yeast table, while the extra structure conditioning beyond $T$ and $M$ is small. The structure-only fraction is $0.003284564452662019$, and the structure-incremental fraction is $0.0005507778820193332$. The direct $Q$-to-structure relation is also finite but weak: $S_Q\_structure\_R^2=0.003774248455422586$, with a right-tail permutation value $p=0.027972027972027972$. The incremental structure fraction has right-tail permutation value $p=0.1258741258741259$, so the structure-specific increment beyond measured translation efficiency and mRNA stability is not promoted to a strong mediated-effect conclusion.

\paragraph{Rank and residual-energy checks.}
The design is numerically nondegenerate after the declared controls. The control matrix has rank $23$ over $24$ columns, leaving residual degrees of freedom $4736$. The residualized $Q$ block has rank $9$; the residualized structure block has rank $1$; the residualized translation-plus-stability block has rank $2$; and the joint residualized $T,M,S$ block has rank $3$. The combined ranks are $\operatorname{rank}(Q,S)=10$, $\operatorname{rank}(Q,T,M)=11$, and $\operatorname{rank}(Q,T,M,S)=12$. The corresponding residual energies are $871992.3819246667$ for mRNA stability, $645.3764418081507$ for measured translation efficiency, $28554.997251412347$ for the structure proxy, and $1724.6664873071313$ for protein abundance. The $Q$ block explains $468.33677333459536$ units of protein-abundance residual energy and $107.77365427073924$ units of structure-proxy residual energy. The direct improvement energy for $Q$ after conditioning on $S$ is $466.79849101702615$, after conditioning on $T,M$ is $237.5550048307909$, and after conditioning on $T,M,S$ is $237.29705529470186$.

\paragraph{Relation to the broader $Q \to T \to P$ surface.}
The same nine-coordinate $B^*_{Q6}$ block has a separate cross-organism modeled-translation mediation audit. In that audit, the yeast row has $n=5815$ joined proteins, $T$ residual energy $6.90537888244409$, protein-abundance residual energy $2958.1865890925337$, total $R^2_{QP}=0.29837624994140827$, direct $R^2_{QP\mid T}=0.0151709093608293$, indirect difference $0.28320534058057895$, and mediated fraction $0.9491551041216973$. The present yeast structure-mediation table is narrower and more controlled: it replaces modeled tAI-only mediation with measured translation efficiency, mRNA half-life, and a 5-prime coding-region MFE proxy on the same organism. It therefore supports a bounded statement: in these yeast rows, measured translation efficiency and mRNA stability absorb much more of the residual $Q$-to-protein association than the CDS-only MFE proxy does.

\paragraph{Axis-level context.}
A separate structural-order axis audit over the same nine coordinate names identified $f3\_stress$ as the only fold-level significant structural-order axis. Its weighted held-out $R^2$ increment is $0.0031744502294756994$, with sign-flip value $p=0.03125$ and weighted absolute unit loading $0.5109586606724208$. The other listed axes have larger sign-flip values: for example $Ile\_AUA$ has $p=0.0693359375$, $Arg\_AGR$ has $p=0.142578125$, $K\_AAA$ has $p=0.2294921875$, and $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$ has $p=0.779296875$. This axis fact is compatible with the present yeast mediation result but does not by itself identify a folding or mechanism pathway.

\paragraph{Local interpretation boundary.}
This chapter only licenses a controlled finite-row variance partition for yeast protein abundance. It does not assert that a synonymous residual coordinate realizes translation in general, folds a protein, determines physical admissibility, creates molecular function, explains a phenotype, proves a biochemical mechanism, or establishes a universal biological rule. The 5-prime MFE value is a sequence-derived proxy, not an ensemble folding experiment and not a measured translation-initiation rate. Measured translation efficiency is a layer-matched readout in this table, but even that readout does not by itself prove causation. Any claim about translation beyond this bounded measurement, folded structure, physical admissibility, function, mechanism, phenotype, or universality requires a separate layer-specific contact and its own finite statistic.
