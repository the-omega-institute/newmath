\subsection{Yeast abundance residuals through a translation and coding-region structure surface}
\label{sec:bioreality-yeast-q6-structure-mediation-abundance-residual}

\paragraph{Finite panel.}
The yeast mediation surface is a finite joined table with $n=4759$ genes after matching real CDS codon counts, protein abundance, measured translation efficiency, mRNA half-life, and a coding-region structure proxy. The reported join hit rate is $0.8818572656921754$, with $5815$ CDS rows joined to abundance before the five-way restriction, $5128$ genes with translation-efficiency rows keyed by protein id, $5379$ valid mRNA half-life records by systematic ORF, and $6027$ valid structure entries. The structure coordinate is the ViennaRNA RNAfold minimum free energy of the first $39$ nucleotides of the CDS, keyed by locus tag. The translation coordinate is the rich-medium ribosome-footprint read density divided by matched mRNA read density from two replicates, so the analysis does not use transcript abundance alone as a translation readout.

\paragraph{Coordinate and control geometry.}
The residual coordinate block $Q$ has nine columns: $K\_AAA$, $Arg\_AGR$, $Ile\_AUA$, $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$, $Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG$, $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$, $Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG$, $Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY$, and $f3\_stress$. The control block $Z$ contains an intercept, $\log(\mathrm{CDS\ length\ in\ nt})$, the twenty amino-acid composition proportions $A,C,D,E,F,G,H,I,K,L,M,N,P,Q,R,S,T,V,W,Y$, real-CDS $GC3$, and the $M$-density baseline defined on the union support of the nine projected $B^*_{Q6}$ vectors. After residualization against $Z$, the rank diagnostics are non-vacuous: $\operatorname{rank}(Z)=23$, $\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde Q)=9$, $\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde S)=1$, $\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde T\widetilde M)=2$, and $4736$ residual degrees of freedom remain. The residual energies are $1724.6664873071313$ for protein abundance, $645.3764418081507$ for measured translation efficiency, $871992.3819246667$ for mRNA stability, and $28554.997251412347$ for the structure proxy.

\paragraph{Variance partition.}
Let $P_e$ denote residualized log protein abundance, $Q_e$ the residualized nine-dimensional $B^*_{Q6}$ coordinate block, $S_e$ the residualized CDS-structure proxy, and $(T_e,M_e)$ the residualized measured-translation and mRNA-stability pair. The finite-row report estimates the following increments:
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{QP} &= 0.2715520808117803,\\
R^2_{QP\mid S} &= 0.2706601505000995,\\
R^2_{QP\mid T,M} &= 0.13773967696311287,\\
R^2_{QP\mid T,M,S} &= 0.13759011208318542.
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus conditioning only on the CDS-folding proxy removes a small share of the direct $Q\to P$ association, while conditioning on measured translation efficiency and mRNA stability removes a much larger share. The structure-only share is
$$
\begin{aligned}
\frac{R^2_{QP}-R^2_{QP\mid S}}{R^2_{QP}} &= 0.003284564452662019,
\end{aligned}
$$
and the extra reduction after adding structure beyond $(T,M)$ is
$$
\begin{aligned}
\frac{R^2_{QP\mid T,M}-R^2_{QP\mid T,M,S}}{R^2_{QP}} &= 0.0005507778820193332.
\end{aligned}
$$
The direct association of $Q$ with the structure proxy is finite but small, with $R^2(S_e\sim Q_e)=0.003774248455422586$ and right-tail permutation probability $0.027972027972027972$. By contrast, the incremental structure term after measured translation and mRNA stability has right-tail permutation probability $0.1258741258741259$, so this row set does not support a strong structure-mediated interpretation beyond the measured translation and mRNA-stability route.

\paragraph{Relation to the translation-mediated abundance surface.}
The broader protein-abundance mediation run supplies an organism-matched context for the same yeast coordinate family. In that table, yeast has $5815$ joined proteins, $\operatorname{rank}(Z)=23$, $\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde Q)=9$, $\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde T)=1$, and $5792$ residual degrees of freedom. The modeled translation-mediated abundance fraction for yeast is $M^{QTP}=0.9491551041216973$, with total $R^2_{QP}=0.29837624994140827$, conditioned direct $R^2_{QP\mid T}=0.0151709093608293$, and indirect difference $0.28320534058057895$. This does not make the structure proxy causal, but it explains why the present yeast chapter treats measured translation and mRNA stability as the primary blocking covariates before asking whether a coding-region MFE proxy still accounts for residual protein-abundance association.

\paragraph{Axis audit.}
A separate axis-identification audit over the same nine coordinate names marks $f3\_stress$ as the only fold-level sign-flip-positive structural-order axis: its weighted held-out increment is $0.0031744502294756994$, its weighted absolute unit loading is $0.5109586606724208$, and its sign-flip probability is $0.03125$. The other coordinate-level increments do not pass that fold-level sign-flip gate. This supports reading the yeast structure surface as a narrow residual-order audit rather than as a general claim that every synonymous residual direction has a structural consequence.

\paragraph{Local conclusion.}
The finite table supports the following bounded statement. In yeast, the nine-dimensional $B^*_{Q6}$ residual block retains a sizeable association with matched protein abundance after amino-acid composition, CDS length, $GC3$, and $M$-density controls. Most of the removable association is carried by the measured translation-efficiency and mRNA-stability block, not by the first-$39$-nt CDS MFE proxy. The structure proxy has a small direct $Q\to S$ signal, but its incremental mediation beyond measured translation and mRNA stability is not strong on this panel. Therefore the chapter records a controlled statistical decomposition of $Q\to P$ through $(T,M,S)$, not a claim of folding mechanism or causal propagation.

\paragraph{Claim boundary.}
No translation realization, protein folding statement, physical admissibility statement, molecular function statement, mechanism claim, organismal phenotype claim, or universal biological law follows from this chapter by itself. The first-$39$-nt MFE value is a sequence-derived CDS proxy, not an ensemble folding measurement, not a measured initiation-rate assay, and not a structure experiment. Protein abundance is a downstream steady-state measurement that can be affected by mRNA abundance, translation, turnover, localization, sample preparation, ionization, and pathway context. A stronger claim would require a separate layer-matched contact and a finite statistic showing that the same residual coordinate survives the relevant translation step before being propagated to the proposed higher layer.
