\subsection{Yeast abundance residuals with translation and five-prime structure conditioning}
\label{sec:bioreality-q6-yeast-structure-mediation-abundance-residual}

\paragraph{Finite carrier.}
The record is a finite cross-layer surface over nine $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates, one measured yeast translation readout, one matched messenger-RNA stability readout, one coding-region structure proxy, and a matched protein-abundance response.  The coordinate side is
$K\_AAA$, $Arg\_AGR$, $Ile\_AUA$, $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$, $Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG$, $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$, $Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG$, $Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY$, and $f3\_stress$.  The control surface contains an intercept, $\log(\mathrm{CDS\ length\ in\ nt})$, the twenty amino-acid composition proportions $A,C,D,E,F,G,H,I,K,L,M,N,P,Q,R,S,T,V,W,Y$, $GC3$, and the $M$-density baseline defined as the fraction of sense codons lying in the union support of the nine projected residual vectors.  Thus amino-acid sequence, length, third-position base composition, and the residual-coordinate support mass remain controls, not promoted biological coordinates.

\paragraph{Joined yeast rows.}
The yeast table joins coding sequences, protein abundance, measured translation efficiency, messenger-RNA half-life, and a coding-region folding proxy on $4759$ rows.  The reported overall join hit rate is $0.8818572656921754$, while the CDS join hit rate is $0.9156038419146592$.  The joined ingredients are $5815$ CDS rows with reported joins, $5128$ protein-keyed translation-efficiency records, $5379$ valid messenger-RNA half-life records by systematic ORF, and $6027$ valid structure records.  The structure proxy is the ViennaRNA RNAfold minimum free energy of the first $39$ coding nucleotides, keyed by systematic ORF.  The translation-efficiency readout is computed from the Ingolia et al. 2009 rich-medium yeast footprint and mRNA quantifications as mean footprint normalized read density divided by mean mRNA normalized read density across the two rich-medium replicates.  The skipped CDS rows are dominated by $687$ rows without a translation-efficiency match and $369$ rows without an mRNA half-life match; no valid structure row is lost for missing or nonfinite MFE in this finite join.

\paragraph{Rank and residual degrees of freedom.}
After control residualization, the control rank is $23$ with $24$ control columns recorded, and the residual degrees of freedom are $4736$.  The residual coordinate block has rank $9$, the structure block has rank $1$, the measured-translation plus mRNA-stability block has rank $2$, and the joint measured-translation, mRNA-stability, and structure block has rank $3$.  The combined ranks are $10$ for $Q$ with structure, $11$ for $Q$ with measured translation and mRNA stability, and $12$ for all four blocks together.  The residual energies are $28554.997251412347$ for the structure proxy, $645.3764418081507$ for measured translation efficiency, $871992.3819246667$ for messenger-RNA stability, and $1724.6664873071313$ for protein abundance.  The rank surface is therefore nonvacuous for variance partitioning: the residual coordinate block, the translation-stability block, and the structure proxy all retain finite post-control degrees of freedom.

\paragraph{Protein-abundance decomposition.}
Let $Q_e$ denote the nine-dimensional residual-coordinate matrix after the control projection, $P_e$ the residualized protein-abundance vector, $S_e$ the residualized five-prime coding-region MFE proxy, and $(T_e,M_e)$ the residualized measured-translation and mRNA-stability readouts.  The registered decomposition is the incremental $R^2$ of $Q_e$ for $P_e$ after conditioning on different readout blocks:
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{QP} &= 0.2715520808117803,\\
R^2_{QP\mid S} &= 0.2706601505000995,\\
R^2_{QP\mid T,M} &= 0.13773967696311287,\\
R^2_{QP\mid T,M,S} &= 0.13759011208318542.
\end{aligned}
$$
The direct residual-coordinate association with protein abundance is therefore large on this controlled yeast table, but about half of it is removed after the measured translation-efficiency and mRNA-stability block is conditioned on.  Conditioning only on the five-prime structure proxy changes the association only slightly.  In the registered fractions, the structure-only attenuation is $0.003284564452662019$, and the additional attenuation of structure beyond measured translation and mRNA stability is $0.0005507778820193332$.

\paragraph{Structure-coordinate contact.}
The structure proxy itself has a small but finite relation to the residual-coordinate block.  The statistic $R^2(S_e\sim Q_e)$ is $0.003774248455422586$, with right-tail permutation probability $0.027972027972027972$ under the deterministic structure-residual permutation seed.  By contrast, the incremental structure fraction after measured translation and mRNA stability has right-tail permutation probability $0.1258741258741259$.  The finite-row readback is therefore asymmetric: the residual coordinates weakly track the five-prime MFE proxy, but the MFE proxy does not provide strong additional mediation once measured translation efficiency and mRNA stability are already in the conditioning set.

\paragraph{Connection to the wider protein-abundance surface.}
The separate multi-organism protein-abundance mediation run supplies context for why the yeast row is treated as a cross-layer relation rather than a direct protein-realization claim.  In that run, all twelve organism panels had rank-sufficient residualized designs and mediation fractions in $[0,1]$.  Saccharomyces cerevisiae had $5815$ joined proteins, residual degrees of freedom $5792$, $T$-residual energy $6.90537888244409$, protein-abundance residual energy $2958.1865890925337$, total controlled residual-to-protein $R^2=0.29837624994140827$, direct $R^2$ after modeled tAI conditioning $0.0151709093608293$, indirect difference $0.28320534058057895$, and mediation fraction $0.9491551041216973$.  The present yeast structure record is stricter in one direction and weaker in another: it uses measured translation efficiency and matched mRNA stability in the same yeast condition, but its five-prime MFE proxy explains only a very small additional share beyond that translation-stability block.

\paragraph{Translation boundary.}
The modeled translation-survival table is not treated as measured ribosome evidence.  It used only $3$ organisms for bounded modeled tAI coupling, with $81$ entries above threshold $0.01$ and maximum absolute modeled entry $3801961.031607896$; it explicitly reports that $3$ organisms are insufficient for powered survival.  The yeast structure table, by contrast, uses same-condition measured footprint and mRNA quantifications to form translation efficiency.  Even so, the conclusion remains a controlled statistical partition: it does not by itself establish a causal translation mechanism, ribosome dynamics, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, organismal phenotype, selection, or a universal law.

\paragraph{Structural-order caution.}
A linked structural-order audit identified $f3\_stress$ as the only coordinate passing the fold-level sign-flip gate, with weighted held-out $\Delta R^2=0.0031744502294756994$ and sign-flip probability $0.03125$.  That result is compatible with treating $f3\_stress$ as a dominant residual axis for one structural-order readout, but it does not turn the present five-prime MFE variance partition into a folding or physical-admissibility result.  The MFE proxy is sequence-derived, restricted to the first $39$ coding nucleotides, and is a single minimum-free-energy estimate rather than an ensemble or measured structure.  It is also partly coupled to base composition, so the controlled design mitigates but cannot erase all structure-composition entanglement.

\paragraph{Local verdict.}
This finite-row record supports the following bounded statement.  In yeast, after amino-acid composition, $GC3$, CDS length, and $M$-density controls, the nine $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates retain a strong association with matched protein abundance.  Measured translation efficiency together with mRNA stability removes a substantial part of that association, while the five-prime coding-region MFE proxy removes only a small fraction by itself and adds little beyond measured translation and mRNA stability.  The chapter therefore records a yeast-specific controlled variance decomposition for the chain $Q\rightarrow (T,M,S)\rightarrow P$, not a claim that synonymous residual geometry realizes translation, protein folding, physical admissibility, biochemical function, mechanism, phenotype, selection, or universality.  Any such stronger statement would require an additional layer-matched measurement surface and its own controlled statistic.
