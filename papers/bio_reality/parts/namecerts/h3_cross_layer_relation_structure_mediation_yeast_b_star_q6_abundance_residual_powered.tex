\subsection{Yeast abundance residuals and the structure-mediated boundary}
\label{sec:bioreality-q6-yeast-structure-mediation-abundance-residual}

\paragraph{Finite surface.}
This record evaluates a yeast-specific cross-layer surface in which the nine $B^*_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates are kept as controlled coding-sequence covariates and are tested against protein abundance only after the translation and transcript layers have been made explicit.  The coordinate list is
$\texttt{K\_AAA}$, $\texttt{Arg\_AGR}$, $\texttt{Ile\_AUA}$, $\texttt{Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR}$, $\texttt{Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG}$, $\texttt{Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY}$, $\texttt{Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG}$, $\texttt{Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY}$, and $\texttt{f3\_stress}$.  The control block contains an intercept, $\log(\mathrm{CDS\ length\ in\ nt})$, the twenty amino-acid composition proportions $A,C,D,E,F,G,H,I,K,L,M,N,P,Q,R,S,T,V,W,Y$, GC3 from the observed codon counts, and an $M$-density baseline equal to the fraction of sense codons on the union support of the nine projected residual vectors.  Thus amino-acid sequence, length, GC3, organism identity in the yeast panel, and the residual support density remain controls rather than promoted explanatory coordinates.

\paragraph{Joined rows.}
The finite yeast panel joins real CDS codon counts, measured translation efficiency, matched mRNA half-life, matched protein abundance, and a sequence-derived 5-prime coding-region structure proxy.  The joined panel has $4759$ rows.  The reported CDS join hit rate is $0.9156038419146592$, and the overall join hit rate is $0.8818572656921754$.  The translation-efficiency source contains $5128$ valid protein-keyed entries, the mRNA half-life table contains $5379$ valid systematic-ORF entries, and the structure table contains $6027$ valid minimum-free-energy entries.  The structure coordinate is the ViennaRNA RNAfold minimum free energy of the first $39$ coding nucleotides, keyed by yeast locus tag.  Translation efficiency is the mean footprint normalized read density divided by mean mRNA normalized read density across two rich-medium replicates from the Ingolia yeast ribosome-profiling measurements.  The mRNA half-life payload is identified by SHA256 digest \texttt{cee1ee9a816f0ec1e351bc2d63d4838ae603ea20566fc3ff529514a6ba6cf7d2}.  Missingness is finite and visible: among CDS records, $687$ lack a translation-efficiency match and $369$ lack an mRNA half-life match, while no valid CDS row is lost for a missing structure match in this audit.

\paragraph{Residualized model.}
Let $Q_e$ denote the nine-dimensional residual-coordinate block after residualization by the control matrix $Z$, let $P_e$ denote residualized log protein abundance, let $T_e$ denote residualized measured translation efficiency, let $M_e$ denote residualized mRNA stability, and let $S_e$ denote residualized 5-prime coding-region MFE.  The rank diagnostics show that the controlled design is not a rank-saturated toy fit: $\operatorname{rank}(Z)=23$, $\operatorname{rank}(Q_e)=9$, $\operatorname{rank}(S_e)=1$, $\operatorname{rank}(T_e,M_e)=2$, and $\operatorname{rank}(T_e,M_e,S_e)=3$, with $4736$ residual degrees of freedom after $Z$.  The residual energies are $1724.6664873071313$ for protein abundance, $645.3764418081507$ for measured translation efficiency, $871992.3819246667$ for mRNA stability, and $28554.997251412347$ for the structure proxy.

\paragraph{Variance partition.}
The observed association between $Q_e$ and $P_e$ is large at the residual level, but the partition separates a direct residual-to-protein association from translation, mRNA-stability, and structure-conditioned components.  The finite-row values are
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{QP} &= 0.2715520808117803,\\
R^2_{QP\mid S} &= 0.2706601505000995,\\
R^2_{QP\mid T,M} &= 0.13773967696311287,\\
R^2_{QP\mid T,M,S} &= 0.13759011208318542.
\end{aligned}
$$
Conditioning only on the 5-prime CDS MFE proxy therefore removes a small part of the residual-to-protein association, while conditioning on measured translation efficiency and mRNA stability removes a much larger part.  The additional structure contribution beyond translation efficiency and mRNA stability is finite but very small:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{structure\ only\ fraction} &= 0.003284564452662019,\\
\mathrm{structure\ incremental\ fraction} &= 0.0005507778820193332,\\
R^2(S_e \sim Q_e) &= 0.003774248455422586.
\end{aligned}
$$
The right-tail permutation value for $R^2(S_e \sim Q_e)$ is $0.027972027972027972$, whereas the right-tail permutation value for the incremental structure fraction beyond translation efficiency and mRNA stability is $0.1258741258741259$.  The first number supports a weak finite association between the residual coordinates and the 5-prime MFE proxy; the second does not support a strong structure-mediated increment after the measured translation and mRNA-stability block has already been included.

\paragraph{Relation to the translation-protein route.}
A broader multi-organism translation-protein mediation audit gives context for the yeast result without changing the local claim.  In that audit, the yeast protein-abundance join uses $5815$ proteins and a modeled per-protein tAI readout derived from GtRNAdb tRNA copy counts and dos Reis wobble weights.  For yeast the total residual-to-protein value is $c=0.29837624994140827$, the translation-conditioned direct value is $c'=0.0151709093608293$, the indirect difference is $0.28320534058057895$, and the reported mediated fraction is $M^{QTP}=0.9491551041216973$.  This companion result is a statistical decomposition of modeled tAI and PAXdb abundance, not a ribosome-dynamics observation.  Separately, the modeled tAI survival surface over three organisms has $81$ entries above the bounded-contact threshold $0.01$ and maximum absolute entry $3801961.031607896$, but the same audit explicitly records that three organisms are insufficient for a powered survival claim.  The yeast chapter therefore uses the measured-TE and mRNA-stability rows as the relevant local conditioning block rather than treating modeled adaptation weights as measured translation.

\paragraph{Axis identity.}
The nine-coordinate structural-order audit identifies $\texttt{f3\_stress}$ as the dominant structural-order axis in a separate held-out setting: its weighted held-out $\Delta R^2$ is $0.0031744502294756994$, its weighted absolute unit loading is $0.5109586606724208$, and its sign-flip value is $0.03125$.  No other coordinate in that audit passes the same fold-level sign-flip gate.  This axis identity is useful for interpreting which residual direction is most likely to carry a structure-adjacent signal, but it does not promote the present yeast variance partition into folding, physical realization, or function.

\paragraph{Readback.}
The local readback is the controlled matrix $Q \rightarrow (T,M,S) \rightarrow P$ on $4759$ matched yeast rows.  The supported statement is narrow: after amino-acid composition, CDS length, GC3, and $M$-density are controlled, $B^*_{Q6}$ residuals retain a substantial association with matched protein abundance; measured translation efficiency plus mRNA stability accounts for a large part of that association; the 5-prime coding-region MFE proxy is weakly related to the residual block but adds only a small, not permutation-supported, increment beyond measured translation efficiency and mRNA stability.  The result is a finite statistical variance partition.  It is not evidence that the residual coordinates cause translation, determine folding, establish physical admissibility, realize molecular function, define an organismal phenotype, or state a universal biological law.  Each of those interpretations would require its own layer-matched measurement and a separately recorded statistic.
