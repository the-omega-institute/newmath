\subsection{Yeast structure-conditioned abundance residual surface}
\label{sec:bioreality-q6-yeast-structure-mediation-abundance}

\paragraph{Finite row carrier.}
This record concerns a finite yeast surface whose rows are matched coding-sequence entries and whose columns separate three roles: $Q$ for the nine $B^*_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates, $T$ for a measured translation-efficiency readout, $M$ for matched mRNA stability, $S$ for a 5-prime coding-region structure proxy, and $P$ for matched protein abundance. The joined table has $n=4759$ rows after requiring coding sequence counts, protein abundance, translation-efficiency rows, mRNA half-life, and a structure value. The join summary reports $5815$ CDS rows with abundance, $5128$ protein-keyed translation-efficiency rows, $5379$ valid mRNA half-life records, and $6027$ valid structure MFE records; the final hit rate is $0.8818572656921754$ for the five-way join and $0.9156038419146592$ for the CDS join. The translation readout is the rich-medium footprint-to-mRNA ratio from the Ingolia et al. yeast ribosome-profiling quantification, and the structure readout is ViennaRNA RNAfold minimum free energy on the first $39$ nucleotides of the CDS. These facts make the carrier a finite statistical readback table, not a causal pathway model.

\paragraph{Residual coordinates and controls.}
The $Q$ block has coordinate count $9$ with coordinates
$$
\begin{aligned}
Q=\{&K\_AAA,\ Arg\_AGR,\ Ile\_AUA,\ Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR,\ Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG,\\
&Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY,\ Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG,\ Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY,\ f3\_stress\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
All abundance and mediation fits are residualized against the same control block $Z$: an intercept, $\log(\mathrm{CDS\ length\ in\ nt})$, the $20$ amino-acid composition proportions $A,C,D,E,F,G,H,I,K,L,M,N,P,Q,R,S,T,V,W,Y$, GC3 from real CDS codon counts, and the $M$-density baseline defined by the fraction of sense codons lying in the union support of the nine projected $Q$ vectors. The rank diagnostics give $\operatorname{rank}(Z)=23$, control-column count $24$, residual degrees of freedom $4736$, $\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde Q)=9$, $\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde S)=1$, $\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde{T,M})=2$, and $\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde{T,M,S})=3$. The corresponding joined ranks $10,11,12$ for $Q$ with $S$, $T,M$, and $T,M,S$ show that the fitted surfaces are nontrivial finite linear projections rather than saturated copies of the response.

\paragraph{Abundance decomposition.}
Let $P_e$, $Q_e$, $T_e$, $M_e$, and $S_e$ denote the residualized protein abundance, residual-coordinate block, measured translation efficiency, mRNA stability, and structure proxy after removing $Z$. The computed scalar surface is
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{QP} &= 0.2715520808117803,\\
R^2_{QP\mid S} &= 0.2706601505000995,\\
R^2_{QP\mid T,M} &= 0.13773967696311287,\\
R^2_{QP\mid T,M,S} &= 0.13759011208318542.
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus conditioning on the 5-prime CDS MFE alone removes only a small part of the $Q$-to-$P$ association, while conditioning on measured translation efficiency together with mRNA stability removes a much larger part. The structure-only fraction is $0.003284564452662019$, and the additional structure fraction beyond $T,M$ is $0.0005507778820193332$. The finite permutation audit gives $S_Q$-structure $R^2=0.003774248455422586$ with right-tail permutation value $0.027972027972027972$, while the incremental structure fraction beyond $T,M$ has right-tail value $0.1258741258741259$. The local conclusion is therefore narrow: $Q$ has a small but detectable relation to the sequence-derived MFE proxy, but the extra abundance mediation left for $S$ after translation efficiency and mRNA stability is not powered as a strong separate path.

\paragraph{Energy and rank audit.}
The same computation records residual energies $\|P_e\|^2=1724.6664873071313$, $\|T_e\|^2=645.3764418081507$, $\|M_e\|^2=871992.3819246667$, and $\|S_e\|^2=28554.997251412347$. The $Q$ block explains energy $468.33677333459536$ on $P$ and $107.77365427073924$ on $S$. Its direct improvement energy is $466.79849101702615$ after conditioning on $S$, $237.5550048307909$ after conditioning on $T,M$, and $237.29705529470186$ after conditioning on $T,M,S$. These numbers support a finite variance-partition statement: the yeast $B^*_{Q6}$ residual block retains a direct abundance association after the structure proxy is inserted, whereas the measured-TE plus mRNA-stability block accounts for a substantial part of the abundance association. The MFE proxy contributes only a very small additional attenuation once $T$ and $M$ are already present.

\paragraph{Relation to the broader translation-abundance table.}
The yeast row is consistent with the wider modeled-translation abundance surface, where the yeast protein-abundance decomposition had $R^2_{QP}=0.29837624994140827$, direct $R^2_{QP\mid T}=0.0151709093608293$, indirect difference $0.28320534058057895$, and mediated fraction $M^{QTP}=0.9491551041216973$ using modeled per-protein tAI. Across the twelve-organism modeled table, all listed $M^{QTP}$ fractions lie in the unit interval, but the present yeast structure chapter is stricter because it uses measured TE, mRNA stability, and a sequence-derived structure proxy in the same finite yeast rows. The earlier modeled-tAI survival table had only $3$ organisms and was explicitly bounded as modeled coupling rather than measured ribosome or proteomics validation, despite $81$ entries above threshold $0.01$ and maximal absolute coupling $3801961.031607896$. The separate axis-identity audit also found that among the nine coordinates only $f3\_stress$ passed the fold-level sign-flip gate, with weighted held-out $R^2$ increment $0.0031744502294756994$ and sign-flip value $0.03125$. These linked numbers are background constraints on interpretation, not substitutes for the present yeast five-way row join.

\paragraph{Local claim boundary.}
This chapter supports only a controlled yeast variance decomposition on matched finite rows. It does not assert translation realization from codon geometry alone, does not identify a folding pathway, does not prove physical admissibility, does not establish molecular function, does not infer organismal phenotype, and does not state a universal biological law. The 5-prime CDS MFE value is a sequence-derived minimum-free-energy proxy on the first $39$ CDS nucleotides, not a measured initiation rate and not an ensemble structural assay. Protein abundance is a steady-state abundance readout and remains entangled with turnover, localization, post-translational processing, pathway context, and measurement bias. Any stronger statement would require a separate layer-matched contact and a new controlled statistic on the corresponding rows.
