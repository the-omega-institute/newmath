\subsection{Yeast structure-conditioned abundance residual surface}
\label{subsec:h3-cross-layer-relation-structure-mediation-yeast-b-star-q6-abundance-residual-powered}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite object.}
This record studies a finite yeast surface whose rows join coding-sequence composition, the nine $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates, a measured translation-efficiency readout, an mRNA half-life readout, a local coding-region folding proxy, and matched protein abundance.  The coordinate block is
$K\_AAA$, $Arg\_AGR$, $Ile\_AUA$, $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$, $Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG$, $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$, $Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG$, $Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY$, and $f3\_stress$.
The control block contains an intercept, $\log(\mathrm{CDS\ length\ in\ nt})$, twenty amino-acid composition proportions measured from the actual CDS codon counts, $GC3$, and an $M$-density baseline given by the fraction of sense codons lying on the union support of the nine projected $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ vectors.  After this control residualization the design has $4736$ residual degrees of freedom, $\mathrm{rank}(Z)=23$, $\mathrm{rank}(X)=9$, $\mathrm{rank}(S)=1$, $\mathrm{rank}(T,M)=2$, and $\mathrm{rank}(T,M,S)=3$.

\paragraph{Joined rows and readouts.}
The finite panel contains $4759$ joined yeast rows.  The join hit rate is $0.8818572656921754$, with $5815$ CDS rows reported as joined to the base abundance table and a CDS join hit rate of $0.9156038419146592$.  The measured-translation side uses Ingolia et al. BY4741 rich-medium ribosome-footprint and mRNA quantification, with translation efficiency defined as mean footprint normalized read density divided by mean mRNA normalized read density across two rich-medium replicates.  The protein readout is matched abundance on the same ORF-keyed surface.  The structure readout is the ViennaRNA minimum free energy of the first $39$ nt of the CDS, keyed by locus tag.  The RNA half-life side contributes $5379$ valid systematic-ORF half-life records, with no skipped records from missing systematic identifiers, duplicate identifiers, nonpositive half-lives, or non-object rows.

\paragraph{Residual decomposition.}
Let $Q_e$ denote the residualized nine-dimensional $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ block, $P_e$ the residualized protein-abundance readout, $S_e$ the residualized local CDS-MFE proxy, and $(T_e,M_e)$ the residualized measured-translation and mRNA-stability pair.  The fitted increments are reported as
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2(Q_e \to P_e) &= 0.2715520808117803,\\
R^2(Q_e \to P_e \mid S_e) &= 0.2706601505000995,\\
R^2(Q_e \to P_e \mid T_e,M_e) &= 0.13773967696311287,\\
R^2(Q_e \to P_e \mid T_e,M_e,S_e) &= 0.13759011208318542.
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the local structure proxy alone removes only a small part of the residual association, while conditioning on measured translation efficiency together with mRNA stability removes a much larger portion.  The structure-only fraction is $0.003284564452662019$, and the structure increment beyond the measured-translation plus mRNA-stability pair is $0.0005507778820193332$.  The direct $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ explanation energy on protein abundance is $468.33677333459536$, while the direct improvement energy after conditioning on $T,M$ is $237.5550048307909$ and after conditioning on $T,M,S$ is $237.29705529470186$.  In the same residualized coordinates, $Q_e$ explains the structure proxy with $S_Q$ structure $R^2=0.003774248455422586$.

\paragraph{Permutation and rank evidence.}
The right-tail permutation value for $S_Q$ structure association is $0.027972027972027972$, whereas the right-tail permutation value for the structure increment after translation and mRNA stability is $0.1258741258741259$.  This supports a narrow statement: the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ block has a detectable relation to the CDS-MFE proxy, but the added structure-conditioned mediation term is not independently strong once measured translation and mRNA stability are already in the conditioning set.  The residual energies also mark the scale separation: $P_e$ has residual energy $1724.6664873071313$, $S_e$ has residual energy $28554.997251412347$, $T_e$ has residual energy $645.3764418081507$, and $M_e$ has residual energy $871992.3819246667$.

\paragraph{Relation to translation-mediated abundance.}
A separate matched protein-abundance mediation calculation on twelve organisms gives a useful boundary for interpreting the yeast result.  On the yeast row of that surface, the total residual association is $c=0.29837624994140827$, the direct association after modeled per-protein tAI conditioning is $c'=0.0151709093608293$, the difference is $0.28320534058057895$, and the reported mediated fraction is $0.9491551041216973$.  The same calculation reports $5815$ yeast proteins, $275$ all-tRNA copies, zero codons filled by the geometric-mean fallback, $\mathrm{rank}(X)=9$, $\mathrm{rank}(T)=1$, and $5792$ residual degrees of freedom after the same style of controls.  This comparison does not turn the present yeast structure calculation into a causal translation mechanism; it only shows that the yeast abundance residual has an independently recorded translation-conditioned companion surface.

\paragraph{Coordinate identity check.}
The structural-order axis audit identifies $f3\_stress$ as the only coordinate passing its fold-level sign-flip gate.  Its weighted held-out increment is $0.0031744502294756994$, with sign-flip right-tail value $0.03125$ and weighted absolute unit loading $0.5109586606724208$.  Other coordinates have weaker or negative fold-level increments, for example $Arg\_AGR$ has $0.0020139270616115154$ with sign-flip value $0.142578125$, $Ile\_AUA$ has $0.0013067592718559024$ with sign-flip value $0.0693359375$, $Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY$ has $-0.00045340330387383447$, and $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$ has $-0.0003205780368125179$.  The yeast mediation surface therefore should be read as a nine-coordinate residual matrix with one especially visible stress-axis component, not as a law assigning a universal sign to any single synonymous contrast.

\paragraph{Execution-level support.}
The observable execution decomposition supplies an additional finite check on the cross-layer context: in $Saccharomyces\ cerevisiae$, $3289$ genes were evaluated and the final conclusion was an observable execution subblock, meaning that at least one observed rate-proxy slot passed both effective null classes.  This is compatible with using translation and mRNA-stability readouts as conditioning surfaces in the present chapter.  It does not by itself identify the structure proxy as a causal mediator, nor does it promote a residual coordinate to folded-protein structure or molecular function.

\paragraph{Local conclusion.}
The finite-row conclusion is a controlled variance partition.  In yeast, after amino-acid composition, $GC3$, CDS length, and $M$-density controls, the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ block carries a sizeable residual association with matched protein abundance.  Most of the attenuation visible in this chapter is obtained by conditioning on measured translation efficiency and mRNA stability, while the first-$39$-nt CDS-MFE proxy contributes a small additional fraction.  The admissible readback is therefore
$$
\begin{aligned}
Q_i &\longrightarrow (T,M,S) \longrightarrow P,\\
\mathrm{with}\quad S &= \mathrm{CDS\ 5'\ MFE\ proxy},\\
T &= \mathrm{measured\ TE},\\
M &= \mathrm{mRNA\ half\ life},\\
P &= \mathrm{matched\ protein\ abundance}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The result is not evidence that a synonymous residual coordinate determines translation, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, mechanism, phenotype, selection, or universality.  Any such stronger statement would require an additional layer-matched contact and a separately declared statistic.  In particular, the local MFE proxy is CDS-only, lacks the $5'$UTR, is a minimum-free-energy summary rather than an ensemble or in-cell structure measurement, and remains partly entangled with sequence composition despite the controlled residualization.
