\subsection{Yeast structure mediation for B$^*_\mathrm{Q6}$ abundance residuals}
\label{sec:biorality-q6-yeast-structure-mediation-abundance-residual}

\paragraph{Finite surface.}
This chapter records a yeast-only, five-way matched surface for the question whether B$^*_\mathrm{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates retain a protein-abundance association after the association is conditioned through translation and an mRNA-structure proxy.  The rows are coding sequences in \textit{Saccharomyces cerevisiae} for which real CDS codon counts, PAXdb-style protein abundance, measured translation efficiency, mRNA half-life, and a 5-prime CDS folding proxy can all be joined.  The final table contains $4759$ joined rows.  The reported CDS join hit rate is $0.9156038419146592$, and the full join hit rate is $0.8818572656921754$.  The translation-efficiency side starts from $5128$ valid protein-keyed TE records, the mRNA half-life side has $5379$ valid systematic-ORF records, and the structure side has $6027$ genes with valid minimum-free-energy values.  The TE source is the Ingolia et al. 2009 rich-medium ribosome-footprint and mRNA quantification surface; TE is the mean footprint normalized density divided by mean mRNA normalized density across the two rich-medium replicates.  The structure readout is the ViennaRNA RNAfold minimum free energy of the first $39$ CDS nucleotides, keyed by systematic ORF.

\paragraph{Coordinates and controls.}
The finite coordinate matrix uses the nine B$^*_\mathrm{Q6}$ residual axes
$K\_AAA$, $Arg\_AGR$, $Ile\_AUA$, $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$, $Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG$, $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$, $Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG$, $Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY$, and $f3\_stress$.
The control block contains an intercept, $\log(\mathrm{cds\_len\_nt})$, the $20$ amino-acid composition proportions $A,C,D,E,F,G,H,I,K,L,M,N,P,Q,R,S,T,V,W,Y$, GC3 from real CDS codon counts, and an M-density baseline equal to the fraction of sense codons on the union support of the nine projected B$^*_\mathrm{Q6}$ vectors.  After residualizing by this control block, the diagnostic ranks are nondegenerate for the intended regressions: $\operatorname{rank}(Z)=23$, $\operatorname{rank}(X)=9$, $\operatorname{rank}(S)=1$, $\operatorname{rank}(T,M)=2$, and $\operatorname{rank}(T,M,S)=3$, with residual degrees of freedom $4736$.  The corresponding joined-design ranks are $\operatorname{rank}(X,S)=10$, $\operatorname{rank}(X,T,M)=11$, and $\operatorname{rank}(X,T,M,S)=12$.

\paragraph{Variance decomposition.}
Let $Q_e$ denote the residualized nine-coordinate B$^*_\mathrm{Q6}$ block, $P_e$ the residualized log protein-abundance readout, $S_e$ the residualized 5-prime CDS MFE structure proxy, $T_e$ the residualized measured translation-efficiency readout, and $M_e$ the residualized mRNA-stability readout.  The reported decomposition is
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{QP} &= 0.2715520808117803,\\
R^2_{QP\mid S} &= 0.2706601505000995,\\
R^2_{QP\mid T,M} &= 0.13773967696311287,\\
R^2_{QP\mid T,M,S} &= 0.13759011208318542.
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus conditioning on the structure proxy alone removes only a small part of the B$^*_\mathrm{Q6}$ abundance association, while conditioning on measured TE together with mRNA stability removes a much larger part.  The structure-only fraction is $0.003284564452662019$, and the structure increment beyond measured TE plus mRNA stability is $0.0005507778820193332$.  The direct structure readout carried by B$^*_\mathrm{Q6}$ is also small: $R^2(S_e \sim Q_e)=0.003774248455422586$.  Its right-tail permutation value is $0.027972027972027972$, while the right-tail permutation value for the incremental structure fraction is $0.1258741258741259$.  This separates a weak but detectable sequence-derived MFE association from a non-significant incremental structure-mediated contribution once measured TE and mRNA stability are already in the conditioning set.

\paragraph{Rank and energy audit.}
The residual energies give the scale of the finite table: $P_e$ has residual energy $1724.6664873071313$, $S_e$ has residual energy $28554.997251412347$, $T_e$ has residual energy $645.3764418081507$, and $M_e$ has residual energy $871992.3819246667$.  The B$^*_\mathrm{Q6}$ block explains $468.33677333459536$ energy units on $P_e$ and $107.77365427073924$ on $S_e$.  The direct improvement energy from $Q_e$ after conditioning on $S_e$ is $466.79849101702615$; after conditioning on $(T_e,M_e)$ it is $237.5550048307909$; and after conditioning on $(T_e,M_e,S_e)$ it is $237.29705529470186$.  These values support the finite-row reading that the large attenuation is associated with the measured TE and mRNA-stability pair, whereas the MFE proxy contributes only a thin additional slice.

\paragraph{Relation to the broader B$^*_\mathrm{Q6}$ protein-abundance surface.}
A separate multi-organism protein-abundance mediation surface reports positive joint B$^*_\mathrm{Q6}$ to modeled-tAI to abundance mediation fractions for all twelve organisms in that table.  The yeast entry has $c=0.29837624994140827$, $c'=0.0151709093608293$, an indirect component $0.28320534058057895$, and $M^{QTP}=0.9491551041216973$.  The same organism therefore appears as a strong modeled-tAI mediation case in the cross-organism abundance table, while the present yeast-only five-way surface shows that measured TE plus mRNA stability, rather than the CDS MFE proxy alone, accounts for most of the controlled attenuation.  A structural-order audit identifies $f3\_stress$ as the only fold-level sign-flip-passing axis, with weighted held-out $R^2$ increment $0.0031744502294756994$, weighted absolute unit loading $0.5109586606724208$, and sign-flip right-tail value $0.03125$.  That axis identity is useful for naming the residual geometry, but the yeast mediation table remains a variance partition over matched biological readouts, not a statement that the axis realizes protein structure.

\paragraph{Local interpretation boundary.}
The admissible conclusion is narrow.  In the finite yeast table, B$^*_\mathrm{Q6}$ residuals retain a substantial controlled association with protein abundance; that association is strongly attenuated by measured TE plus mRNA stability; and the first-$39$-nt CDS MFE proxy supplies only a small structure-associated component, with a significant $Q$-to-$S$ permutation value but no significant incremental structure fraction after $(T,M)$ conditioning.  The chapter does not infer a causal pathway.  It also does not infer realized translation dynamics, folded protein structure, physical admissibility, molecular function, organismal phenotype, mechanism, adaptation, or universality.  Such claims would require their own layer-matched observations or perturbation contacts.  The present record is a controlled finite-row mediation and residual-partition result for yeast abundance, TE, mRNA stability, and a CDS-local folding proxy.
