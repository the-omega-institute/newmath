\subsection{Yeast abundance residuals with a structure-conditioned Q6 surface}\label{sec:bioreality-q6-yeast-structure-mediation}\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite row surface.}
This record fixes a yeast-only cross-layer surface in which the nine $B^*_Q6$ synonymous residual coordinates are evaluated against matched protein abundance after residualization by a finite control matrix.  The coordinate list is
$$
\begin{aligned}
Q = \{&K\_AAA,\ Arg\_AGR,\ Ile\_AUA,\ Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR,\ Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG,\\
&Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY,\ Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG,\ Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY,\ f3\_stress\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The joined table contains $4759$ Saccharomyces cerevisiae rows.  The join combines real CDS codon counts, protein abundance, measured translation efficiency, mRNA half-life, and a sequence-derived structure readout.  The structure coordinate is the minimum free energy of the first $39$ coding nucleotides, computed as a CDS-local 5-prime folding proxy.  The join summary reports $5815$ CDS rows available before the five-way join, $5128$ valid translation-efficiency protein keys, $6027$ valid structure-MFE records, and $5379$ valid mRNA half-life records.  The final join hit rate is $0.8818572656921754$, with a CDS join hit rate of $0.9156038419146592$.

\paragraph{Controls and rank.}
The control surface $Z$ contains an intercept, $\log(\mathrm{CDS\ length\ in\ nt})$, the twenty amino-acid composition proportions $A,C,D,E,F,G,H,I,K,L,M,N,P,Q,R,S,T,V,W,Y$, GC3 from the same CDS codon counts, and an M-density baseline equal to the fraction of sense codons lying on the union support of the nine projected $B^*_Q6$ vectors.  The numerical rank diagnostics are nondegenerate for the intended residual comparison: $\mathrm{rank}(Z)=23$, the residual degrees of freedom after $Z$ are $4736$, $\mathrm{rank}(\widetilde X)=9$, $\mathrm{rank}(\widetilde S)=1$, $\mathrm{rank}(\widetilde T\widetilde M)=2$, and $\mathrm{rank}(\widetilde T\widetilde M\widetilde S)=3$.  The augmented ranks are $\mathrm{rank}(\widetilde X\widetilde S)=10$, $\mathrm{rank}(\widetilde X\widetilde T\widetilde M)=11$, and $\mathrm{rank}(\widetilde X\widetilde T\widetilde M\widetilde S)=12$, so the residualized $Q$ block is not merely duplicating the control, structure, translation, or mRNA-stability columns.  The reported residual energies are $P:1724.6664873071313$, $S:28554.997251412347$, $T:645.3764418081507$, and $M:871992.3819246667$.

\paragraph{Abundance decomposition.}
Let $P_e$ be residualized protein abundance, $Q_e$ the residualized nine-coordinate $B^*_Q6$ matrix, $S_e$ the residualized 5-prime CDS MFE proxy, and $(T_e,M_e)$ the residualized measured translation-efficiency and mRNA-stability readouts.  The reported finite-row decomposition is
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{QP} &= 0.2715520808117803,\\
R^2_{QP\mid S} &= 0.2706601505000995,\\
R^2_{QP\mid T,M} &= 0.13773967696311287,\\
R^2_{QP\mid T,M,S} &= 0.13759011208318542.
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the direct $Q$-to-protein residual association is large on this yeast table, while conditioning on measured translation efficiency together with mRNA stability removes substantially more of that association than conditioning on the 5-prime CDS MFE proxy alone.  The structure-only fraction is $0.003284564452662019$, whereas the incremental structure fraction beyond measured translation efficiency and mRNA stability is $0.0005507778820193332$.  The residual association from $Q$ to the structure proxy is small but nonzero, with $S_Q\_\mathrm{structure}\_R^2=0.003774248455422586$ and right-tail permutation probability $0.027972027972027972$.  The structure-incremental right-tail probability is $0.1258741258741259$, so the data support a weak finite association between $Q$ and the 5-prime MFE proxy, but do not support a strong structure-mediated explanation of the protein-abundance residual after translation-efficiency and mRNA-stability conditioning.

This comparison places the yeast structure-conditioned result in a table where translation or translation-proxy conditioning is the dominant absorber of the abundance residual, while the 5-prime MFE proxy is only a minor additional partition.

\paragraph{Axis identity and finite verdict.}
The structural-order axis audit identifies $f3\_stress$ as the only coordinate passing its fold-level sign-flip gate.  Its weighted held-out increment is $0.0031744502294756994$, its weighted absolute unit loading is $0.5109586606724208$, and its sign-flip probability is $0.03125$.  The other coordinate increments remain below that gate: $Arg\_AGR$ has $0.0020139270616115154$ with $p=0.142578125$, $Ile\_AUA$ has $0.0013067592718559024$ with $p=0.0693359375$, $K\_AAA$ has $0.0005307046179682497$ with $p=0.2294921875$, $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$ has $0.0010134090056701938$ with $p=0.2158203125$, and $Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY$ is negative at $-0.00045340330387383447$ with $p=0.529296875$.  A separate observable-execution decomposition in yeast uses $3289$ genes and reports the finite conclusion as an observable execution subblock: at least one observed rate-proxy slot passed both effective null classes.  Taken together, the finite rows justify a narrow statement: the yeast $B^*_Q6$ abundance residual is real as a controlled statistical association, it is mostly attenuated by translation-efficiency and mRNA-stability conditioning, and its extra 5-prime CDS MFE partition is small.

\paragraph{Local boundary.}
No stronger biological claim is licensed by this chapter.  The 5-prime MFE coordinate is a CDS-local single-minimum proxy, not a measured folded ensemble and not a translation-initiation rate.  The protein abundance readout is steady-state abundance, not folding, localization, turnover, molecular function, phenotype, or selection.  The measured translation-efficiency and mRNA-stability rows make the abundance decomposition more interpretable, but they do not by themselves prove a causal pathway.  A claim about translation dynamics, protein folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, mechanism, organism-level phenotype, or universality would require an additional layer-matched contact and its own controlled finite-row statistic.  The present record therefore remains a controlled yeast variance partition over $Q$, $T$, $M$, $S$, and $P$, with positive abundance association, weak structure coupling, and explicit refusal to promote that pattern beyond the measured layers.
