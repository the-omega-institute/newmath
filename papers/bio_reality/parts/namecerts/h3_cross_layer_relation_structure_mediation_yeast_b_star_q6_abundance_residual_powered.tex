\subsection{Yeast abundance residual mediation through translation and coding-region structure}
\label{sec:biorreality-q6-yeast-abundance-structure-mediation}

\paragraph{Finite row object.}
This record concerns a finite yeast row table in which nine $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates are joined to matched protein abundance, measured translation efficiency, mRNA stability, and a coding-region structure proxy. The coordinate list is
$K\_AAA$, $Arg\_AGR$, $Ile\_AUA$, $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$, $Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG$, $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$, $Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG$, $Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY$, and $f3\_stress$. The joined yeast table has $4759$ rows after matching CDS codon counts, protein abundance, translation efficiency, mRNA half-life, and the structure readout. The reported CDS join hit rate is $0.9156038419146592$, and the five-way join hit rate is $0.8818572656921754$. The translation-efficiency source contributes $5128$ valid protein-keyed genes, the mRNA half-life source contributes $5379$ valid systematic-ORF records, and the structure table contributes $6027$ valid minimum-free-energy records. After control residualization the available residual degrees of freedom are $4736$.

\paragraph{Controls and admissible coordinates.}
The control block $Z$ contains an intercept, $\log(CDS\ length)$, the twenty amino-acid composition proportions $A,C,D,E,F,G,H,I,K,L,M,N,P,Q,R,S,T,V,W,Y$, GC3 from real CDS codon counts, and an $M$-density baseline defined as the fraction of sense codons on the union support of the nine projected $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ coordinate vectors. These variables remain controls; they are not promoted to the tested residual coordinates. The rank diagnostics are nondegenerate for the residual-coordinate surface: $rank(Z)=23$, $rank(\widetilde{X})=9$, $rank(\widetilde{S})=1$, $rank(\widetilde{T,M})=2$, and $rank(\widetilde{T,M,S})=3$. The augmented ranks are $rank(\widetilde{X,S})=10$, $rank(\widetilde{X,T,M})=11$, and $rank(\widetilde{X,T,M,S})=12$. Thus the reported finite surface has enough row-rank structure to separate the nine residual coordinates from the single structure proxy and the two translation-plus-mRNA covariates within this yeast table.

\paragraph{Measured readouts.}
The protein readout $P$ is log-scaled measured protein abundance. The translation readout $T$ is measured translation efficiency from the Ingolia et al. 2009 yeast rich-medium ribosome-footprint and mRNA quantification files, computed as mean footprint normalized read density divided by mean mRNA normalized read density across the two rich-medium replicates. The mRNA readout $M$ is mRNA stability from the matched half-life table. The structure readout $S$ is the ViennaRNA RNAfold minimum free energy of the first $39$ coding nucleotides, keyed by systematic yeast ORF. This $S$ readout is a CDS-only 5-prime coding-region proxy; it is not a 5-prime UTR measurement, not an ensemble structure distribution, and not a measured initiation-rate assay.

\paragraph{Residual variance partition.}
Let $Q_{e}$ denote the nine-dimensional residual coordinate matrix after removing $Z$, let $P_{e}$ denote residual protein abundance, let $T_{e}$ denote residual measured translation efficiency, let $M_{e}$ denote residual mRNA stability, and let $S_{e}$ denote residual 5-prime CDS minimum free energy. The finite-row decomposition reports
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^{2}_{QP} &= 0.2715520808117803,\\
R^{2}_{QP\mid S} &= 0.2706601505000995,\\
R^{2}_{QP\mid T,M} &= 0.13773967696311287,\\
R^{2}_{QP\mid T,M,S} &= 0.13759011208318542.
\end{aligned}
$$
The structure-only attenuation fraction is $0.003284564452662019$, so conditioning on the residualized 5-prime CDS MFE proxy alone removes only a small part of the $Q_{e}$ to $P_{e}$ association. The structure-incremental fraction beyond measured translation efficiency and mRNA stability is $0.0005507778820193332$, with a right-tail permutation value $p=0.1258741258741259$. The direct $Q_{e}$ to structure association is also small but detectable in this finite table: $S\_Q\_structure\_R2=0.003774248455422586$, with a right-tail permutation value $p=0.027972027972027972$.

\paragraph{Energy diagnostics.}
The same decomposition can be read from the residual-energy diagnostics. The residual protein-abundance energy is $1724.6664873071313$, the residual translation-efficiency energy is $645.3764418081507$, the residual mRNA-stability energy is $871992.3819246667$, and the residual structure energy is $28554.997251412347$. The nine residual coordinates explain $468.33677333459536$ energy units on $P$ and $107.77365427073924$ on $S$. After conditioning on $S$, the direct coordinate-to-protein improvement energy is $466.79849101702615$; after conditioning on $T$ and $M$, it is $237.5550048307909$; and after conditioning on $T$, $M$, and $S$, it is $237.29705529470186$. These numbers support a narrow conclusion: in this yeast table, the dominant attenuation of the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ to protein-abundance residual association is carried by measured translation efficiency plus mRNA stability, while the 5-prime coding-region MFE proxy adds only a very small additional partition.

\paragraph{Relation to cross-organism translation mediation.}
The yeast row is consistent with the broader translation-conditioned protein-abundance audit, where the yeast entry has $5815$ joined protein rows before the stricter five-way structure join. In that cross-organism table the yeast mediation fraction is $M^{QTP}=0.9491551041216973$, with total $R^{2}_{QP}=0.29837624994140827$, direct $R^{2}_{QP\mid T}=0.0151709093608293$, and indirect difference $0.28320534058057895$. Other organisms show positive but smaller mediated fractions, including $0.6084594553474505$ in Bacillus subtilis, $0.612922252793667$ in Escherichia coli K-12 MG1655, $0.4872268357227724$ in Caenorhabditis elegans, $0.3157053953614641$ in Danio rerio, $0.27206289160119157$ in Homo sapiens, and $0.15488799907323988$ in Mus musculus. These values are statistical mediation fractions in matched finite rows; they do not by themselves establish a causal translation mechanism.

\paragraph{Structural-order side information.}
A separate structural-order axis audit on the same nine-coordinate family identifies $f3\_stress$ as the only coordinate passing the fold-level sign-flip gate. Its weighted held-out increment is $0.0031744502294756994$, with sign-flip right-tail value $p=0.03125$ and weighted absolute unit loading $0.5109586606724208$. The other coordinate increments are smaller or not sign-flip significant: $Arg\_AGR$ has $0.0020139270616115154$ with $p=0.142578125$, $Ile\_AUA$ has $0.0013067592718559024$ with $p=0.0693359375$, $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$ has $0.0010134090056701938$ with $p=0.2158203125$, $K\_AAA$ has $0.0005307046179682497$ with $p=0.2294921875$, $Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG$ has $0.00017615626369389472$ with $p=0.24609375$, $Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG$ has $0.00004390882136492317$ with $p=0.4873046875$, $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$ has $-0.0003205780368125179$ with $p=0.779296875$, and $Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY$ has $-0.00045340330387383447$ with $p=0.529296875$. This side information is useful for naming the coordinate that carries the strongest structural-order signal, but it does not turn the yeast MFE partition into a folding, function, or phenotype claim.

\paragraph{Modeled tAI boundary.}
The modeled tAI contact is weaker than the yeast measured-TE table used above. In the modeled tAI survival audit, only $3$ organisms were available for the bounded coupling surface, so powered survival was explicitly not claimed. The model nevertheless computed all $61$ sense-codon weights for Escherichia coli K-12, Homo sapiens, and Saccharomyces cerevisiae, with zero raw-weight fallback count $0$ in each of those three organisms. The bounded coupling matrix had $81$ entries above the $0.01$ threshold and maximum absolute entry $3801961.031607896$. This supports the existence of a modeled codon-adaptation contact, not an observed ribosome-footprint contact and not a protein-abundance mechanism.

\paragraph{Verdict for this finite surface.}
The finite-row evidence is ready for a self-contained BioReality record at the level of controlled statistical mediation. The admissible statement is that, in $4759$ joined yeast rows, nine $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates retain a large association with matched protein abundance after the declared controls, that measured translation efficiency plus mRNA stability account for a substantial part of that association, and that a 5-prime CDS MFE proxy contributes a small additional variance partition. The local numerical summary is
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^{2}_{QP}-R^{2}_{QP\mid T,M} &= 0.13381240384866742,\\
R^{2}_{QP\mid T,M}-R^{2}_{QP\mid T,M,S} &= 0.00014956487992745252,\\
R^{2}_{QP}-R^{2}_{QP\mid S} &= 0.0008919303116808149.
\end{aligned}
$$
This supports a translation-and-mRNA-conditioned abundance-residual partition with a weak structure-proxy increment. It does not license the stronger reading that synonymous residual geometry proves translation, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, mechanism, phenotype, selection, or a general biological rule. Any such stronger statement would require a separate layer-matched contact and its own controlled statistic.
