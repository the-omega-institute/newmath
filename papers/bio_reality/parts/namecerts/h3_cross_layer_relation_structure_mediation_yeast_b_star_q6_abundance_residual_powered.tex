\subsection{Yeast structure mediation of the $B^*_{Q6}$ abundance residual}\label{sec:bioreality-q6-yeast-structure-mediation}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite surface.}
This record uses a yeast finite-row surface whose rows join real coding sequences, $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinates, a translation readout, mRNA stability, a local coding-region structure proxy, and matched protein abundance.  The residual coordinate block $Q$ has nine coordinates: $K\_AAA$, $Arg\_AGR$, $Ile\_AUA$, $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$, $Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG$, $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$, $Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG$, $Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY$, and $f3\_stress$.  The control block $Z$ contains an intercept, $\log(CDS\ length\ in\ nt)$, the twenty amino-acid composition proportions computed from the same CDS codon counts, GC3, and an $M$-density baseline defined as the fraction of sense codons lying on the union support of the nine projected $B^*_{Q6}$ vectors.  After $Z$ residualization the design has $4759$ joined yeast rows, $4736$ residual degrees of freedom, $rank(Z)=23$, $rank(\widetilde Q)=9$, $rank(\widetilde S)=1$, $rank(\widetilde T\widetilde M)=2$, and $rank(\widetilde T\widetilde M\widetilde S)=3$.

\paragraph{Joined biological readouts.}
The five-way join is organism-specific to \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}.  The structure readout is the ViennaRNA RNAfold minimum free energy of the first $39$ nucleotides of the coding sequence, keyed by systematic ORF locus tag.  The translation readout is measured translation efficiency from the Ingolia et al. rich-medium ribosome profiling table, computed as mean footprint normalized read density divided by mean mRNA normalized read density across the two rich-medium replicate pairs.  The mRNA stability control is the Neymotin half-life table, with $5379$ valid half-life records by systematic identifier.  The join contains $4759$ rows, reports a CDS join hit rate $0.9156038419146592$, and reports an overall join hit rate $0.8818572656921754$.  The TE source contributes $5128$ valid protein-keyed rows, the structure source contributes $6027$ valid MFE rows, and the CDS table contributes $5815$ joined CDS records before the final five-way restriction.  The skipped CDS counts are finite and explicit: $369$ rows lack an mRNA half-life match and $687$ lack a TE match, with zero skips for invalid length, missing protein id, nonpositive abundance, nonpositive mRNA half-life, nonpositive TE, duplicate stable id, empty sense-codon counts, or unparseable yeast ORF.

\paragraph{Variance partition.}
The fitted object is a controlled association decomposition, not a causal path assertion.  Let $P_e$ be the residualized protein-abundance readout, $Q_e$ the residualized nine-dimensional $B^*_{Q6}$ block, $S_e$ the residualized $5'$ CDS MFE proxy, and $(T_e,M_e)$ the residualized measured-TE and mRNA-stability pair.  The finite-row regression reports
$$\begin{aligned}
R^2_{QP} &= 0.2715520808117803,\\
R^2_{QP\mid S} &= 0.2706601505000995,\\
R^2_{QP\mid T,M} &= 0.13773967696311287,\\
R^2_{QP\mid T,M,S} &= 0.13759011208318542.
\end{aligned}$$
Thus conditioning on the local MFE proxy alone removes only a small fraction of the $Q$-to-protein residual association, while conditioning on measured TE plus mRNA stability removes a much larger portion.  The recorded structure-only fraction is $0.003284564452662019$.  The additional structure fraction after TE and mRNA stability are already present is $0.0005507778820193332$.  The direct structure relation $R^2(S_e\sim Q_e)$ is $0.003774248455422586$, with a right-tail permutation value $p=0.027972027972027972$ for $S_Q$ structure association, whereas the incremental structure fraction has $p=0.1258741258741259$.  The resulting readback is therefore asymmetric: $Q$ carries a detectable but very small relation to the local MFE proxy, yet that proxy does not materially mediate the abundance residual once measured TE and mRNA stability are included.

\paragraph{Energy and rank diagnostics.}
The finite-rank diagnostics support the interpretation that this is an estimable variance partition rather than a rank artifact.  The residual energies are $p$-residual energy $1724.6664873071313$, $s$-residual energy $28554.997251412347$, $t$-residual energy $645.3764418081507$, and $m$-residual energy $871992.3819246667$.  The $Q$ block explains energy $468.33677333459536$ on $P$ and $107.77365427073924$ on $S$.  The direct improvement energy for $Q$ given $S$ is $466.79849101702615$, while the corresponding value given $(T,M)$ is $237.5550048307909$ and given $(T,M,S)$ is $237.29705529470186$.  These numbers match the scalar $R^2$ pattern: the structure channel is present but small, and the TE--mRNA channel accounts for the larger attenuation of the $Q$-to-protein residual association.

\paragraph{Relation to the modeled translation mediation surface.}
The same yeast coordinate family also appears in the multi-organism modeled translation mediation audit.  In the yeast rows of that audit, $5815$ proteins are joined, the per-protein modeled-tAI residual energy is $6.90537888244409$, and the protein-abundance residual energy is $2958.1865890925337$.  The yeast scalar decomposition there reports total $R^2_{QP}=0.29837624994140827$, direct $R^2_{QP\mid T}=0.0151709093608293$, indirect difference $0.28320534058057895$, and $M^{QTP}=0.9491551041216973$ when the translation readout is modeled per-protein tAI.  This separate finite surface is useful as a consistency check for the translation-conditioned direction, but it remains a modeled tAI decomposition, not ribosome-footprint evidence.  The three-organism modeled-tAI survival audit also records only $3$ organisms for its bounded coupling surface, with $81$ entries above threshold and maximum absolute $S_{ij}=3801961.031607896$ under modeled weights; its own note states that $3$ organisms are insufficient for powered survival.  The present yeast structure chapter therefore relies on the measured yeast TE, mRNA stability, MFE, and abundance join for its local variance partition, while treating modeled tAI as a bounded comparison rather than as an observed translation event.

\paragraph{Local conclusion.}
The ready claim is narrow.  In yeast, a nine-coordinate $B^*_{Q6}$ residual block has a substantial controlled association with matched protein abundance after amino-acid composition, GC3, length, and $M$-density controls.  Conditioning on measured TE plus mRNA stability reduces that association from $0.2715520808117803$ to $0.13773967696311287$.  Conditioning on the $5'$ CDS MFE proxy alone reduces it only to $0.2706601505000995$, and adding the MFE proxy after TE and mRNA stability reduces it only to $0.13759011208318542$.  The local structure proxy is therefore a small, statistically visible sequence-derived covariate, not the main mediator of the abundance residual.  No translation mechanism, folded protein structure, physical admissibility, molecular function, phenotype, selection law, or universal biological rule follows from this chapter.  Such claims require their own layer-matched measurements and controls; a residual coordinate, a local MFE proxy, a modeled codon-adaptation weight, or a steady-state abundance association is not enough.
