\subsection{Yeast abundance residual mediation through translation and 5-prime CDS structure}\label{sec:biorality-q6-yeast-abundance-structure-mediation}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite surface.}
This record tests a finite yeast surface whose rows join B$^*_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates, measured translation efficiency, mRNA stability, a 5-prime coding-region folding proxy, and matched protein abundance. The residual coordinate block has nine columns:
$$
\begin{aligned}
Q=\{&\mathrm{K\_AAA},\mathrm{Arg\_AGR},\mathrm{Ile\_AUA},\mathrm{Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR},\mathrm{Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG},\\
&\mathrm{Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY},\mathrm{Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG},\mathrm{Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY},f_3\text{-}\mathrm{stress}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The control block contains an intercept, $\log(\mathrm{CDS\ length})$, the twenty amino-acid composition proportions, GC3, and an M-density baseline formed from the union support of the nine projected residual vectors. After the control residualization, the fitted ranks were $\operatorname{rank}(Z)=23$, $\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde Q)=9$, $\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde S)=1$, $\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde T,\widetilde M)=2$, and $\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde T,\widetilde M,\widetilde S)=3$, leaving $4736$ residual degrees of freedom. These rank values make the variance-partition statistic non-vacuous rather than a saturated fit.

\paragraph{Joined empirical rows.}
The yeast join used $4759$ matched rows. The reported CDS join hit rate was $0.9156038419146592$, and the five-way join hit rate was $0.8818572656921754$. The translation-efficiency table contributed $5128$ valid protein-keyed TE entries, the mRNA stability table contributed $5379$ valid half-life records by systematic ORF, and the structure table contributed $6027$ valid MFE records. The translation readout is the mean footprint normalized read density across two rich-medium replicates divided by the corresponding mean mRNA normalized read density, from the BY4741 mid-exponential rich-medium Ingolia et al. data. The structure coordinate is not a measured protein fold: it is ViennaRNA RNAfold minimum free energy on the first $39$ nt of the coding sequence, keyed by systematic ORF.

\paragraph{Variance decomposition.}
Let $P_e$ denote the protein-abundance residual after the control block, $Q_e$ the nine-dimensional residual-coordinate matrix after the same controls, $S_e$ the 5-prime CDS MFE residual, and $(T_e,M_e)$ the measured-TE and mRNA-stability residuals. The observed single and joint fits were
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2(Q_e\to P_e)&=0.2715520808117803,\\
R^2(Q_e\to P_e\mid S_e)&=0.2706601505000995,\\
R^2(Q_e\to P_e\mid T_e,M_e)&=0.13773967696311287,\\
R^2(Q_e\to P_e\mid T_e,M_e,S_e)&=0.13759011208318542.
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the isolated 5-prime-CDS-MFE attenuation of the residual-to-protein association is small:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\frac{R^2(Q_e\to P_e)-R^2(Q_e\to P_e\mid S_e)}{R^2(Q_e\to P_e)}&=0.003284564452662019,\\
\frac{R^2(Q_e\to P_e\mid T_e,M_e)-R^2(Q_e\to P_e\mid T_e,M_e,S_e)}{R^2(Q_e\to P_e)}&=0.0005507778820193332.
\end{aligned}
$$
The residual association between $Q_e$ and the structure proxy itself was $R^2(S_e\sim Q_e)=0.003774248455422586$ with a right-tail permutation value $p=0.027972027972027972$. The incremental structural fraction beyond measured TE and mRNA stability had right-tail permutation value $p=0.1258741258741259$, so the stronger mediated-structure reading is not supported by this finite row set.

\paragraph{Energy and rank audit.}
The residual energies were $\|P_e\|^2=1724.6664873071313$, $\|S_e\|^2=28554.997251412347$, $\|T_e\|^2=645.3764418081507$, and $\|M_e\|^2=871992.3819246667$. The energy explained by $Q_e$ on $P_e$ was $468.33677333459536$, while the direct improvement energy for $Q_e$ after conditioning on $S_e$ was $466.79849101702615$. After conditioning on measured TE and mRNA stability, the direct improvement energy was $237.5550048307909$, and after adding the structure proxy it was $237.29705529470186$. This is the numerical reason the record is read as a controlled variance partition: measured TE and mRNA stability absorb a large part of the residual-to-protein association, while the 5-prime coding-region MFE proxy contributes only a narrow additional decrement.

\paragraph{Relation to the broader protein-abundance mediation surface.}
A separate twelve-organism protein-abundance mediation audit places the yeast row in context. For \textit{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}, the modeled-tAI protein-abundance surface used $5815$ joined proteins, had $\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde Q)=9$, $\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde T)=1$, $\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde Q,\widetilde T)=10$, and reported $M^{QTP}=0.9491551041216973$ with total $R^2=0.29837624994140827$, direct conditioned $R^2=0.0151709093608293$, and indirect difference $0.28320534058057895$. That broader statistic uses modeled per-protein tAI rather than the measured TE and mRNA-stability pair used in the yeast structure decomposition above. The two surfaces agree only at the limited level that yeast has a strong controlled residual-to-protein association whose translation-side covariates matter; they do not establish the same molecular mechanism.

\paragraph{Local interpretation.}
The allowed conclusion is finite and statistical. In yeast, after amino-acid composition, GC3, CDS length, and M-density controls, the nine B$^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinates retain a substantial association with matched protein abundance. Conditioning on measured TE plus mRNA stability strongly reduces that association, whereas conditioning on a 5-prime coding-region MFE proxy alone reduces it only slightly. The structure proxy has a small but detectable association with the residual-coordinate block, yet its incremental mediation beyond TE and mRNA stability is not significant in this row set.

\paragraph{No-promotion rule.}
This chapter does not claim that the B$^*_{Q6}$ coordinates cause translation, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, organismal phenotype, selection, or a universal biological law. The MFE field is a sequence-derived coding-region proxy, not an ensemble structure measurement, not a 5-prime UTR measurement, and not a translation-initiation assay. Protein abundance is a steady-state downstream readout and is not separated here from turnover, localization, post-translational modification, pathway context, or direct perturbation response. Any claim at those layers would require its own matched contact and its own controlled statistic after the translation step has survived.
