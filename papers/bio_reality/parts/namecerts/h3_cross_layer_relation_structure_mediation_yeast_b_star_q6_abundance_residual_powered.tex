\subsection{Yeast abundance residuals through translation and local coding-region structure}\label{sec:biorеality-yeast-q6-structure-mediation}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite row surface.}
This record concerns a yeast-only finite panel in which $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates are compared with matched protein abundance after controlling for coding-sequence and transcript-layer covariates.  The row object is not a free association between a codon residual and a protein measurement.  It is a joined table whose finite rows carry a coding sequence, a measured translation-efficiency readout, a matched mRNA half-life readout, a matched protein-abundance readout, and a local coding-region folding proxy.  The computed join contains $4759$ genes, with a reported overall join hit rate of $0.8818572656921754$ and a CDS join hit rate of $0.9156038419146592$.  The translation-efficiency source contributes $5128$ valid protein-keyed genes, the mRNA half-life source contributes $5379$ valid systematic-ORF records, and the structure source contributes $6027$ valid 5-prime coding-region MFE records.  The structure readout is ViennaRNA RNAfold minimum free energy on the first $39$ nt of the CDS; it is therefore a CDS-local sequence proxy, not a direct measurement of initiation kinetics or folded protein state.

\paragraph{Coordinates and controls.}
The residual coordinate block has dimension $9$ and consists of $K\_AAA$, $Arg\_AGR$, $Ile\_AUA$, $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$, $Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG$, $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$, $Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG$, $Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY$, and $f3\_stress$.  The control block contains the intercept, $\log(\mathrm{CDS\ length\ in\ nt})$, the twenty amino-acid composition proportions from real CDS codon counts, GC3, and an M-density baseline equal to the fraction of sense codons on the union support of the nine projected $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ vectors.  The fitted control matrix has rank $23$ with $24$ columns, and the residual degrees of freedom after this control projection are $4736$.  The residualized coordinate matrix has rank $9$, the residualized structure column has rank $1$, the translation-plus-mRNA block has rank $2$, and the combined translation, mRNA, and structure block has rank $3$.  Thus the finite panel has enough rank to ask a variance-partition question, but its single-organism design prevents any population-law reading.

\paragraph{Measured decomposition.}
Let $Q_{e}$ denote the residualized nine-dimensional $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ coordinate block, $P_{e}$ the residualized protein-abundance readout, $S_{e}$ the residualized 5-prime CDS MFE proxy, and $(T_{e},M_{e})$ the measured translation-efficiency and mRNA-stability controls after the same covariate projection.  The computed protein-abundance association before conditioning on the candidate mediators is
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^{2}_{QP} &= 0.2715520808117803.
\end{aligned}
$$
After conditioning on the residualized structure proxy alone, the incremental $Q$ contribution is
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^{2}_{QP\mid S} &= 0.2706601505000995,
\qquad
\frac{R^{2}_{QP}-R^{2}_{QP\mid S}}{R^{2}_{QP}} = 0.003284564452662019.
\end{aligned}
$$
After conditioning on measured translation efficiency and mRNA half-life, the remaining $Q$ contribution is
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^{2}_{QP\mid T,M} &= 0.13773967696311287.
\end{aligned}
$$
Adding the structure proxy to that conditioning set gives
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^{2}_{QP\mid T,M,S} &= 0.13759011208318542,
\qquad
\frac{R^{2}_{QP\mid T,M}-R^{2}_{QP\mid T,M,S}}{R^{2}_{QP}} = 0.0005507778820193332.
\end{aligned}
$$
The direct association between the residual coordinate block and the structure proxy is small but nonzero in this finite table:
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^{2}_{S\leftarrow Q} &= 0.003774248455422586,
\qquad
p_{\mathrm{perm}}(S\leftarrow Q) = 0.027972027972027972.
\end{aligned}
$$
The corresponding right-tail permutation value for the structure-incremental fraction is weaker:
$$
\begin{aligned}
 p_{\mathrm{perm}}(\mathrm{structure\ incremental}) &= 0.1258741258741259.
\end{aligned}
$$
The finite-row verdict is therefore asymmetrical: the residual coordinate block has a detectable but very small relation to the CDS-local MFE proxy, while the extra explanatory contribution of that proxy beyond measured translation efficiency and mRNA stability is not strong in this panel.

\paragraph{Relation to the broader protein-abundance route.}
A separate multi-organism protein-abundance mediation run reports that the same nine $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ coordinates can be decomposed through modeled per-protein tAI and measured PAXdb abundance on $12$ organisms.  In that run, yeast has $5815$ joined proteins and a modeled-tAI mediation fraction $M^{QTP}=0.9491551041216973$, with $c=0.29837624994140827$, $c'=0.0151709093608293$, and indirect $R^{2}=0.28320534058057895$.  That broader result supports the decision to treat translation readouts as a required intermediate surface before discussing protein abundance.  The present yeast structure record is narrower: it asks whether adding a CDS-local MFE proxy explains a portion of the $Q$ to protein-abundance residual relation after translation efficiency and mRNA stability have already been included.  Its answer is bounded and quantitative rather than categorical.

\paragraph{Local verdict.}
The chapter-specific claim is supported only as a controlled statistical decomposition on the joined yeast rows.  The finite data show that $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residuals retain a substantial protein-abundance residual association before mediator conditioning, that translation efficiency plus mRNA stability absorb a large part of that association, and that the additional CDS-local structure proxy contributes only a small residual partition.  This is not evidence that synonymous residual coordinates determine protein folding, molecular function, physical admissibility, organismal phenotype, or a universal biological rule.  It is also not a causal mediation proof: the structure variable is sequence-derived, partly GC-correlated, computed from a single minimum-free-energy structure, restricted to the first $39$ nt of the CDS, and evaluated in one organism with one matched set of readouts.  Any claim about translation dynamics, folded structure, physical admissibility, biochemical function, mechanism, or universality would require a separate layer-matched contact and its own finite statistic.  Within those limits, the ready statement is: in yeast, the nine-dimensional $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual block has a controlled protein-abundance residual association, measured translation efficiency plus mRNA stability account for a large portion of it, and 5-prime CDS MFE supplies at most a small additional structure-proxy partition.
