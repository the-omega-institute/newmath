\subsection{Yeast abundance residuals under translation and CDS-structure conditioning}\label{sec:bioreality-q6-yeast-abundance-structure-mediation}\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite row surface.}
The yeast surface is a finite joined table of $4759$ coding-sequence rows in which each row carries a $9$-coordinate $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual vector, a matched protein-abundance value, a measured translation-efficiency readout, an mRNA-stability readout, and a short-CDS structure proxy.  The residual coordinates are $K\_AAA$, $Arg\_AGR$, $Ile\_AUA$, $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$, $Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG$, $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$, $Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG$, $Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY$, and $f3\_stress$.  The control block contains an intercept, $\log(\mathrm{CDS\ length})$, the $20$ amino-acid composition proportions $A,C,D,E,F,G,H,I,K,L,M,N,P,Q,R,S,T,V,W,Y$, GC3 computed from the same CDS codon counts, and the M-density baseline, defined as the fraction of sense codons lying on the union support of the nine projected $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ vectors.  The control rank is $23$ from $24$ control columns, and the residual degrees of freedom after control removal are $4736$.

\paragraph{Join and measurement audit.}
The row construction is not inferred from codon geometry alone.  It joins real yeast CDS codon counts to protein abundance, measured translation efficiency, mRNA half-life, and a CDS-derived structure proxy.  The reported CDS join count is $5815$, the TE protein-id source contributes $5128$ usable genes, the mRNA half-life source contributes $5379$ valid systematic-ORF records, and the structure table contains $6027$ valid MFE entries.  The final five-way join has $4759$ rows, with reported join hit rate $0.8818572656921754$ and CDS join hit rate $0.9156038419146592$.  The translation-efficiency readout is the mean footprint normalized read density divided by the mean mRNA normalized read density across two rich-medium replicates from the Ingolia et al. yeast ribosome-profiling study.  The structure readout is the ViennaRNA RNAfold minimum free energy of the first $39$ nucleotides of the CDS, keyed by systematic ORF.  The mRNA stability source is the Neymotin half-life table with payload hash $cee1ee9a816f0ec1e351bc2d63d4838ae603ea20566fc3ff529514a6ba6cf7d2$.

\paragraph{Residualized decomposition.}
Let $Q_e$ denote the residualized $9$-coordinate $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ matrix, $P_e$ the residualized log protein-abundance readout, $T_e$ the residualized measured translation-efficiency readout, $M_e$ the residualized mRNA-stability readout, and $S_e$ the residualized $5'$ CDS MFE proxy.  All residualizations use the same control block described above.  The finite table gives the following variance decomposition:
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2(P_e \sim Q_e) &= 0.2715520808117803,\\
R^2(P_e \sim Q_e \mid S_e) &= 0.2706601505000995,\\
R^2(P_e \sim Q_e \mid T_e,M_e) &= 0.13773967696311287,\\
R^2(P_e \sim Q_e \mid T_e,M_e,S_e) &= 0.13759011208318542.
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the protein-abundance association carried by $Q_e$ is large before conditioning on translation and mRNA-stability readouts, is only slightly reduced by the short-CDS MFE proxy alone, and remains almost unchanged when the structure proxy is added after $T_e$ and $M_e$.  Numerically, the structure-only fraction is $0.003284564452662019$, while the added structure fraction after $T_e,M_e$ is $0.0005507778820193332$.  The direct structure relation $R^2(S_e \sim Q_e)$ is $0.003774248455422586$, with right-tail permutation $p=0.027972027972027972$.  The added-after-$T_e,M_e$ structure fraction has right-tail permutation $p=0.1258741258741259$.

\paragraph{Rank and energy checks.}
The residual design has nontrivial but controlled rank.  The rank of $Q_e$ is $9$, the rank of $S_e$ is $1$, the rank of the combined $T_e,M_e$ block is $2$, and the rank of $T_e,M_e,S_e$ is $3$.  Adding $Q_e$ to these blocks gives ranks $10$, $11$, and $12$, respectively.  The protein residual energy is $1724.6664873071313$; the structure residual energy is $28554.997251412347$; the measured-TE residual energy is $645.3764418081507$; and the mRNA-stability residual energy is $871992.3819246667$.  The $Q_e$ explained energy on protein abundance is $468.33677333459536$, while its explained energy on the structure proxy is $107.77365427073924$.  Conditional improvement energies for $Q_e$ are $466.79849101702615$ given $S_e$, $237.5550048307909$ given $T_e,M_e$, and $237.29705529470186$ given $T_e,M_e,S_e$.  These ranks and energies show that the structure proxy is present as a measurable finite-row column, but it does not absorb the main $Q_e$ to protein-abundance association once translation and mRNA-stability readouts are already present.

\paragraph{Relation to translation-mediated protein abundance.}
The same nine-coordinate $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ basis has a broader protein-abundance mediation audit across $12$ organisms using modeled per-protein tAI.  In that audit, all $12$ organism surfaces have sufficient rank and mediation fractions in the unit interval.  Yeast is the strongest case by the modeled-tAI decomposition: $n=5815$ proteins are used, $T$ explains $941.8858618613751$ units of abundance energy, $Q$ explains $882.6526210803958$ units on abundance, $Q$ explains $5.671703404506426$ units on modeled tAI, and the mediated fraction is $0.9491551041216973$ with total $R^2=0.29837624994140827$, conditioned direct $R^2=0.0151709093608293$, and indirect component $0.28320534058057895$.  This broader result motivates reading the yeast structure calculation as a conditioning audit around an already strong yeast translation-associated abundance surface, not as an independent proof of a folding or structure mechanism.

\paragraph{Modeled translation-weight boundary.}
The modeled tAI route itself remains a bounded translation-layer readout.  In the three-organism tAI weight check, the organisms are \textit{Escherichia coli} K-12, \textit{Homo sapiens}, and \textit{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}; each has all $61$ sense codons assigned a modeled weight, and yeast uses $275$ matched tRNA FASTA headers with all $20$ standard amino-acid families represented.  The modeled coupling surface has $81$ entries above threshold $0.01$ and maximum absolute score $3801961.031607896$, but the organism count is only $3$, so that surface is explicitly not a powered ribosome-profiling or proteomics validation.  The present yeast chapter therefore treats measured TE, mRNA stability, protein abundance, and the $5'$ CDS MFE proxy as the operative finite-row measurements, while modeled tAI is only supporting context for why translation-conditioned abundance was worth auditing.

\paragraph{Local scientific reading.}
The finite result supports a narrow statement: in yeast, after amino-acid composition, GC3, CDS length, and the M-density baseline are controlled, the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual block has a strong association with matched protein abundance; measured TE plus mRNA stability remove a large part of that association; the short-CDS MFE proxy is detectably related to $Q_e$ but contributes only a very small share of the remaining protein-abundance decomposition.  The structure proxy is a sequence-derived $5'$ CDS MFE value, not an ensemble folding measurement, not a measured initiation rate, and not a full transcript structure assay.  Because the row surface is one organism and one set of matched datasets, the result is best read as a yeast-specific variance partition over joined observations.

\paragraph{Boundary of claims.}
No claim about translation realization follows from a raw residual-to-protein association unless the same coordinate is first evaluated through a translation readout under the stated controls.  No claim about folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, perturbation mechanism, phenotype, selection, or universality follows from this chapter.  Protein abundance is a downstream abundance measurement, not a folding assay or a function assay.  The MFE column is a CDS-local structure proxy, not a direct observation of the mature folded protein or of a causal pathway.  Any stronger biological statement would require a separate layer-matched measurement and a new controlled statistic on rows joined at that layer.
