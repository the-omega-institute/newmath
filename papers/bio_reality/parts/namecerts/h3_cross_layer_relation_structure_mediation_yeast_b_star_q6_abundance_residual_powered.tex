\subsection{Yeast structure-conditioned abundance residuals}
\label{sec:bior-reality-q6-yeast-structure-mediated-abundance}

\paragraph{Finite row object.}
This record tests a yeast-specific cross-layer question for the nine $B^*_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates
$K\_{AAA}$, $Arg\_{AGR}$, $Ile\_{AUA}$, $Leu\_{CUN\,vs\,UUR}$, $Leu\_{UUA\,vs\,UUG}$, $Ser\_{UCR\,vs\,AGY}$, $Ser\_{UCA\,vs\,UCG}$, $Thr\_{ACR\,vs\,ACY}$, and $f_3\text{-}stress$.
The finite table joins real yeast coding-sequence codon counts, matched measured translation efficiency, matched mRNA half-life, matched protein abundance, and a sequence-derived structure proxy. The structure coordinate $S$ is the ViennaRNA RNAfold minimum free energy for the first $39$ nucleotides of the coding region, keyed by systematic yeast ORF. The protein coordinate $P$ is log protein abundance, and the translation-stability block contains measured translation efficiency $T$ together with mRNA stability $M$. The resulting five-way join contains $4759$ rows. The reported join hit rate is $0.8818572656921754$, with $5815$ CDS rows available before the final joins, $5128$ proteins with measured translation efficiency by protein identifier, $5379$ valid mRNA half-life records by systematic name, and $6027$ valid structure-MFE records.

\paragraph{Controls and rank.}
All residual associations are formed after projecting away the declared control block $Z$. The block contains an intercept, $\log(\mathrm{CDS\ length\ in\ nt})$, the $20$ amino-acid composition proportions computed from real CDS codon counts, GC3, and an $M$-density baseline defined as the fraction of sense codons on the union support of the nine projected $B^*_{Q6}$ vectors. The fitted control matrix has $23$ effective rank with $24$ columns listed before rank reduction. After this projection the residual degrees of freedom are $4736$. The residual energies are
$$
\begin{aligned}
E(P\mid Z) &= 1724.6664873071313,\\
E(T\mid Z) &= 645.3764418081507,\\
E(M\mid Z) &= 871992.3819246667,\\
E(S\mid Z) &= 28554.997251412347.
\end{aligned}
$$
The residualized $B^*_{Q6}$ block has rank $9$. Adding $S$ gives rank $10$, adding $T,M$ gives rank $11$, and adding $T,M,S$ gives rank $12$. Thus the tested surface is not a vacuous rank collapse: the structure proxy contributes one residual direction beyond controls, while the translation-stability block contributes two residual directions beyond controls.

\paragraph{Variance partition.}
Let $Q_e$, $P_e$, $T_e$, $M_e$, and $S_e$ denote the corresponding variables after residualization against $Z$. The direct residual association of the nine-dimensional $Q_e$ block with $P_e$ is
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{QP} &= 0.2715520808117803.
\end{aligned}
$$
Conditioning on the CDS-MFE structure proxy alone changes the incremental contribution of $Q_e$ only slightly:
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{QP\mid S} &= 0.2706601505000995,\\
\frac{R^2_{QP}-R^2_{QP\mid S}}{R^2_{QP}} &= 0.003284564452662019.
\end{aligned}
$$
Conditioning on measured translation efficiency and mRNA stability accounts for a much larger share of the direct $Q_e$ to $P_e$ association:
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{QP\mid T,M} &= 0.13773967696311287,\\
R^2_{QP\mid T,M,S} &= 0.13759011208318542,\\
\frac{R^2_{QP\mid T,M}-R^2_{QP\mid T,M,S}}{R^2_{QP}} &= 0.0005507778820193332.
\end{aligned}
$$
The finite conclusion is therefore narrow. In this yeast table, the nine-coordinate residual block has a strong protein-abundance residual association after the declared controls, but the additional share attributable to the CDS-MFE structure proxy is very small. The structure-only fraction is about $0.328\%$ of the $Q_e$ to $P_e$ signal, and the structure increment beyond measured translation efficiency plus mRNA stability is about $0.055\%$ of that signal.

\paragraph{Structure contact strength.}
The direct residual association from $Q_e$ to the CDS-MFE proxy is nonzero but small:
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2(S_e \sim Q_e) &= 0.003774248455422586.
\end{aligned}
$$
The permutation audit reports a right-tail value $p=0.027972027972027972$ for this $Q_e$ to structure-proxy association. The corresponding right-tail value for the incremental structure fraction beyond $T,M$ is weaker, $p=0.1258741258741259$. This separates two facts. First, the $B^*_{Q6}$ residual block is detectably aligned with the first-$39$-nt CDS-MFE proxy in this finite yeast table. Second, that alignment does not provide strong evidence that the structure proxy is a meaningful mediator of the downstream protein-abundance association once measured translation efficiency and mRNA stability have already been conditioned on.

\paragraph{Coordinate-level context.}
A separate structural-order audit of the same nine-coordinate family identifies $f_3\text{-}stress$ as the only coordinate passing the fold-level sign-flip gate. Its held-out increment is $0.0031744502294756994$, its weighted absolute unit loading is $0.5109586606724208$, and its sign-flip right-tail value is $0.03125$. The other coordinates have positive, null, or negative held-out increments without passing that gate: for example $Arg\_{AGR}$ has $0.0020139270616115154$ with $p=0.142578125$, $Ile\_{AUA}$ has $0.0013067592718559024$ with $p=0.0693359375$, $Thr\_{ACR\,vs\,ACY}$ has $-0.00045340330387383447$ with $p=0.529296875$, and $Leu\_{CUN\,vs\,UUR}$ has $-0.0003205780368125179$ with $p=0.779296875$. This context supports the interpretation that the finite yeast signal is not a uniform codon-family law. It is a low-dimensional residual-order phenomenon in which $f_3\text{-}stress$ is the clearest carried axis.

\paragraph{Translation-mediated abundance comparison.}
The broader cross-organism translation-mediated abundance audit reports a yeast entry with $5815$ joined proteins before the stricter five-way structure join. In that yeast table the joint nine-coordinate mediation decomposition through modeled per-protein tAI gives
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{QP} &= 0.29837624994140827,\\
R^2_{QP\mid T} &= 0.0151709093608293,\\
R^2_{\mathrm{indirect}} &= 0.28320534058057895,\\
M^{QTP} &= 0.9491551041216973.
\end{aligned}
$$
This comparison explains why the yeast structure record is read as a structure-conditioned abundance residual rather than as a free-standing structure mechanism. Translation readouts can absorb a large share of the yeast protein-abundance association in the modeled tAI decomposition, whereas the first-$39$-nt MFE proxy adds only a small increment beyond measured translation efficiency and mRNA stability in the five-way table.

\paragraph{Readback discipline.}
The admissible readback is a controlled matrix of residual associations among $Q$, translation-related readouts, structure proxy, mRNA stability, and matched protein abundance. A direct $Q_i \to P_k$ association is not enough to assert a protein-realization route when the relevant $Q_i \to T_j$ step is absent, untested, rank-saturated, or only represented by a modeled adaptation score. The modeled-tAI survival table confirms why this caution is needed: three organisms were sufficient to compute bounded modeled-tAI couplings, with $81$ entries above the $0.01$ coupling threshold and maximum absolute entry $3801961.031607896$, but the same record states that three organisms are insufficient for powered survival. Thus a modeled codon-adaptation contact is a bounded readout, not a measured ribosome, proteomics, or structure experiment.

\paragraph{Local boundary.}
The result is a statistical variance partition in one yeast setting. It does not establish a causal pathway, a translation-initiation mechanism, a folded-protein structure, physical admissibility, molecular function, organismal phenotype, selection, or a universal biological rule. The CDS-MFE variable is a sequence-derived first-coding-window proxy, not an ensemble folding assay and not a measured initiation rate. Protein abundance remains affected by transcription, translation, degradation, localization, post-translational state, detection bias, and condition-specific biology. Any claim at those layers requires its own matched contact and its own controlled statistic. Within this record the supported statement is only that, after the declared controls, the nine $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinates retain a protein-abundance residual association in yeast, that measured translation efficiency plus mRNA stability remove a large part of it, and that the first-$39$-nt CDS-MFE structure proxy contributes a small but separately measured residual component.
