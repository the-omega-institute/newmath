\subsection{Yeast structure-conditioned abundance residual surface}
\label{subsec:bior-reality-q6-yeast-structure-mediation-abundance-residual}

\paragraph{Finite row object.}
The yeast abundance mediation record is a finite cross-layer table whose rows are matched coding sequences with codon counts, measured translation efficiency, mRNA half-life, a coding-region structure proxy, and protein abundance. After the joins, the usable row count is $4759$. The reported CDS join hit rate is $0.9156038419146592$, and the five-way join hit rate is $0.8818572656921754$. Translation-efficiency rows are keyed by protein identifier, with $5128$ valid protein-level TE entries and no ambiguous TE gene keys. The mRNA half-life side contributes $5379$ valid systematic-gene records. The structure side contributes $6027$ valid MFE records, using the locus-tag systematic ORF as the join route into the yeast proteomics, TE, and half-life table. The structure readout is the ViennaRNA RNAfold minimum free energy for the first $39$ nucleotides of the coding sequence; this is a sequence-derived $5'$ coding-region proxy, not an observed initiation rate.

\paragraph{Coordinate and control surface.}
The residual coordinate block has nine columns:
$$
\begin{aligned}
Q=\{&K\_AAA,\ Arg\_AGR,\ Ile\_AUA,\ Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR,\ Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG,\\
&Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY,\ Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG,\ Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY,\ f3\_stress\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The control matrix $Z$ contains an intercept, $\log(\mathrm{CDS\ length\ in\ nt})$, the twenty amino-acid composition proportions $A,C,D,E,F,G,H,I,K,L,M,N,P,Q,R,S,T,V,W,Y$, $GC3$, and an $M$-density baseline defined as the fraction of sense codons lying in the union support of the nine projected $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ vectors. The effective control rank is $23$ from $24$ control columns. After residualizing by $Z$, the residual degrees of freedom are $4736$. The residual ranks used by the mediation audit are
$$
\begin{aligned}
\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde X)&=9, & \operatorname{rank}(\widetilde S)&=1, & \operatorname{rank}(\widetilde T\widetilde M)&=2,\\
\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde T\widetilde M\widetilde S)&=3, & \operatorname{rank}(X\widetilde S)&=10, & \operatorname{rank}(X\widetilde T\widetilde M\widetilde S)&=12.
\end{aligned}
$$
Here $X$ denotes the nine residual coordinates, $S$ denotes the residualized $5'$ coding-region MFE proxy, $T$ denotes measured TE, $M$ denotes mRNA half-life, and $P$ denotes log protein abundance.

\paragraph{Abundance decomposition.}
The protein-abundance target is residualized log abundance. The direct residual-coordinate association with abundance is substantial in the finite row table: $R^2_{QP}=0.2715520808117803$. Conditioning on the structure proxy alone changes it only slightly, with $R^2_{QP\mid S}=0.2706601505000995$. Conditioning on measured TE and mRNA half-life reduces the residual coordinate increment much more strongly, with $R^2_{QP\mid T,M}=0.13773967696311287$. Adding the structure proxy on top of TE and mRNA half-life gives $R^2_{QP\mid T,M,S}=0.13759011208318542$. Thus the two reported structure fractions are
$$
\begin{aligned}
\frac{R^2_{QP}-R^2_{QP\mid S}}{R^2_{QP}}&=0.003284564452662019,\\
\frac{R^2_{QP\mid T,M}-R^2_{QP\mid T,M,S}}{R^2_{QP}}&=0.0005507778820193332.
\end{aligned}
$$
The residual-coordinate block explains a small but nonzero part of the structure proxy itself: $R^2(S_e\sim Q_e)=0.003774248455422586$. The right-tail permutation value for this $Q$ to structure relation is $0.027972027972027972$. The right-tail permutation value for the extra structure increment after TE and mRNA half-life is $0.1258741258741259$, so the finite table supports a detectable $Q$--structure association but does not support a strong additional structure-mediated abundance increment beyond the measured TE and mRNA-stability block.

\paragraph{Energy and rank checks.}
The diagnostic energies separate the abundance, structure, TE, and mRNA channels. The abundance residual energy is $1724.6664873071313$, the structure residual energy is $28554.997251412347$, the TE residual energy is $645.3764418081507$, and the mRNA half-life residual energy is $871992.3819246667$. The $Q$ block explains $468.33677333459536$ energy units on $P$ and $107.77365427073924$ on $S$. The direct $Q$ improvement energy after conditioning on structure is $466.79849101702615$, whereas after conditioning on $T,M$ it is $237.5550048307909$, and after conditioning on $T,M,S$ it is $237.29705529470186$. These finite-rank diagnostics are consistent with the numerical $R^2$ decomposition: the dominant attenuation of the $Q$--abundance association is carried by measured TE plus mRNA stability, while the $5'$ CDS MFE proxy adds only a small residual adjustment.

\paragraph{Relation to the broader protein-abundance mediation table.}
The same nine-coordinate $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ block appears in the multi-organism protein-abundance mediation audit. In that audit, modeled per-protein tAI is used as $T$, and log PAXdb protein abundance is used as $P$. Yeast has $5815$ joined proteins in that table and an especially large modeled-tAI mediation fraction, $M^{QTP}=0.9491551041216973$, with total $R^2_{QP}=0.29837624994140827$, direct $R^2_{QP\mid T}=0.0151709093608293$, and indirect difference $0.28320534058057895$. Across the twelve listed organisms the mediation fractions range from $0.01850668564589326$ in Sulfolobus solfataricus to $0.9491551041216973$ in Saccharomyces cerevisiae. Those numbers make the yeast row a natural place to ask whether a separate coding-region structure proxy absorbs additional abundance signal. The answer in the finite yeast structure table is sharply bounded: structure is visible as a weak $Q$-associated covariate, but it does not replace the TE and mRNA-stability attenuation pattern.

\paragraph{Coordinate identity check.}
A separate held-out structural-order axis audit on the same nine-coordinate family identifies $f3\_stress$ as the only coordinate passing the fold-level sign-flip gate. Its weighted held-out $R^2$ increment is $0.0031744502294756994$, its weighted absolute unit loading is $0.5109586606724208$, and its sign-flip right-tail value is $0.03125$. The other coordinates have larger sign-flip values, including $0.142578125$ for $Arg\_AGR$, $0.0693359375$ for $Ile\_AUA$, $0.2294921875$ for $K\_AAA$, $0.779296875$ for $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$, $0.24609375$ for $Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG$, $0.2158203125$ for $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$, $0.4873046875$ for $Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG$, and $0.529296875$ for $Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY$. This axis identity result is not a structure-mediation proof, but it gives a finite-row reason to keep $f3\_stress$ visible when reading the yeast structure and abundance surface.

\paragraph{Local no-promotion rule.}
The record is a controlled statistical variance partition, not a causal pathway result. A direct $Q_i\to P_k$ association is not evidence for protein-level realization unless a translation-readout step $Q_i\to T_j$ is separately present and survives the declared controls. The $5'$ coding-region MFE readout is a single minimum-free-energy estimate from coding sequence only; it omits the $5'$ UTR, does not describe an RNA ensemble, and is partially correlated with sequence composition despite the $Z$ controls. The measured TE and mRNA-stability terms are observational covariates from one yeast condition. No translation mechanism, folded protein structure, physical admissibility, molecular function, phenotype, selection claim, or universal biological rule follows from these rows. Each such statement would require its own matched measurement layer and its own finite controlled statistic.
