\subsection{Yeast structure-conditioned abundance residuals for $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$}
\label{sec:bioreality-q6-yeast-structure-conditioned-abundance}

\paragraph{Finite surface.}
The finite object considered here is the yeast row set obtained by joining coding-sequence codon counts, measured translation-efficiency rows, matched mRNA half-life rows, matched protein-abundance rows, and a sequence-derived structure proxy for the first $39$ coding nucleotides. The joined surface has $4759$ genes. The coding-sequence table reports $5815$ joined CDS rows before the five-way intersection, while the translation-efficiency source contributes $5128$ protein-keyed rows, the mRNA half-life source contributes $5379$ valid systematic-ORF rows, and the structure table contributes $6027$ valid MFE rows. The reported CDS join hit rate is $0.9156038419146592$, and the final five-way hit rate is $0.8818572656921754$.

The residual coordinates are the nine projected $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ axes
$$
\begin{aligned}
Q=\{&K\_AAA,\ Arg\_AGR,\ Ile\_AUA,\ Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR,\ Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG,\\
&Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY,\ Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG,\ Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY,\ f3\_stress\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
They are evaluated after residualization against the local control block $Z$: an intercept, $\log(\mathrm{CDS\ length})$, the $20$ amino-acid composition proportions computed from real CDS codon counts, GC3, and the M-density baseline given by the fraction of sense codons lying on the union support of the nine $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ projected vectors. The rank of $Z$ is $23$ with $24$ control columns, and the residual degrees of freedom after $Z$ are $4736$. The residual coordinate block has rank $9$ both as $X_{Q}$ and as the coordinate block used to explain the structure proxy.

\paragraph{Readout definitions.}
Let $P_{e}$ denote residualized log protein abundance, $T_{e}$ measured translation efficiency, $M_{e}$ mRNA stability, and $S_{e}$ the residualized $5'$ coding-region MFE proxy. The registered decomposition uses the following finite-row regression quantities:
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^{2}_{QP} &= R^{2}(P_{e}\sim Q_{e}),\\
R^{2}_{QP\mid S} &= \Delta R^{2}(P_{e}\sim Q_{e}\mid S_{e}),\\
R^{2}_{QP\mid T,M} &= \Delta R^{2}(P_{e}\sim Q_{e}\mid T_{e},M_{e}),\\
R^{2}_{QP\mid T,M,S} &= \Delta R^{2}(P_{e}\sim Q_{e}\mid T_{e},M_{e},S_{e}).
\end{aligned}
$$
On the computed yeast surface these values are
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^{2}_{QP} &= 0.2715520808117803,\\
R^{2}_{QP\mid S} &= 0.2706601505000995,\\
R^{2}_{QP\mid T,M} &= 0.13773967696311287,\\
R^{2}_{QP\mid T,M,S} &= 0.13759011208318542.
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the structure-only attenuation fraction is $0.003284564452662019$, and the structure-incremental fraction beyond measured translation efficiency and mRNA stability is $0.0005507778820193332$. The direct $Q$-to-structure fit is small but nonzero: $R^{2}(S_{e}\sim Q_{e})=0.003774248455422586$, with a right-tail permutation value $p=0.027972027972027972$. The incremental structure fraction has a weaker right-tail permutation value, $p=0.1258741258741259$.

\paragraph{Rank and energy audit.}
The rank diagnostics show that the fitted comparisons are not merely empty column labels. The residual energies are $871992.3819246667$ for the mRNA-stability readout, $28554.997251412347$ for the structure proxy, $645.3764418081507$ for measured translation efficiency, and $1724.6664873071313$ for protein abundance. The structure-only augmented coordinate matrix has rank $10$, the measured-translation plus mRNA-stability matrix has rank $2$, the translation-stability-structure matrix has rank $3$, the coordinate plus translation-stability matrix has rank $11$, and the full coordinate plus translation-stability-structure matrix has rank $12$.

The energy accounting is consistent with a large residual-to-protein association and only a very small structure-mediated component. The coordinate block explains $468.33677333459536$ units of protein-abundance residual energy, while the same coordinates explain $107.77365427073924$ units of structure-proxy residual energy. Conditioning on the structure proxy leaves $466.79849101702615$ units of direct coordinate improvement against protein abundance. Conditioning on measured translation efficiency and mRNA stability leaves $237.5550048307909$, and adding the structure proxy to those two readouts leaves $237.29705529470186$. This is the numerical source of the small structure-incremental fraction.

\paragraph{Relation to the wider $Q\to T\to P$ surface.}
The same $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ coordinate family has a broader modeled translation-conditioned protein-abundance decomposition over $12$ organisms. In that matrix, the yeast row has $5815$ joined proteins, total $R^{2}_{QP}=0.29837624994140827$, direct $R^{2}_{QP\mid T}=0.0151709093608293$, and mediated difference $0.28320534058057895$, giving a modeled $M^{QTP}$ fraction of $0.9491551041216973$. Across the same finite family, other examples include $M^{QTP}=0.6084594553474505$ for \textit{Bacillus subtilis}, $0.612922252793667$ for \textit{Escherichia coli} K12, $0.6165167748739756$ for \textit{Drosophila melanogaster}, and $0.27206289160119157$ for human. Those values show that the yeast abundance residual is not being read from an isolated coordinate fit. It sits inside a larger finite matrix in which translation-conditioned attenuation can be computed row by row, but the present yeast structure analysis asks a narrower question: how much of the yeast $Q\to P$ residual is accounted for by a $5'$ CDS MFE proxy after $T$ and $M$ have already been included.

\paragraph{Local verdict.}
The finite-row verdict is positive only in a restricted variance-partition sense. There is a real joined yeast surface, the controls have full finite rank for the intended residualization, and the $Q$ block has a measurable association with protein-abundance residuals. The direct structure relation $S_{Q}$ is small but permutation-visible at $p=0.027972027972027972$. However, the structure increment after measured translation efficiency and mRNA stability is tiny, $0.0005507778820193332$, and its permutation value $p=0.1258741258741259$ does not support a strong structure-mediated claim.

This chapter therefore records a controlled yeast abundance-residual decomposition, not a causal pathway. The structure readout is the minimum-free-energy estimate for the first $39$ coding nucleotides only; it omits the $5'$ UTR, does not represent an ensemble folding distribution, and remains partly tied to sequence composition even after GC3 and amino-acid controls. No translation-realization, protein folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, mechanistic law, phenotype, selection law, or universality follows from this row set. Such claims would require separate layer-matched measurements and their own finite controlled statistics. Consistently with that boundary, the linked mass-balance audit is negative for a stronger dynamic closure claim, with $\Delta R^{2}=-0.07584438093220836$, $\Delta\mathrm{DL}=-668.8046070381928$ bits, $p_{\mathrm{TEbin}}=0.6831683168316832$, and $p_{\mathrm{timeshuffle}}=0.9306930693069307$ on $120$ genes and $718$ intervals. The turnover audit is also negative beyond expression, TE, pLDDT, and N-end controls, with $\Delta R^{2}=-0.005461979433706483$ and $\Delta\mathrm{DL}=-13.68819707902732$ bits on $3290$ genes. The allowed reading is therefore bounded: yeast $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residuals participate in a controlled protein-abundance residual decomposition, while the present structure proxy contributes only a small and non-decisive additional component after measured translation and mRNA stability are accounted for.
