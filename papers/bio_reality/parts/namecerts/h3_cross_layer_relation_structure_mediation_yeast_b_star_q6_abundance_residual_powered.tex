\subsection{Yeast structure-conditioned abundance residual surface}\label{sec:bior-reality-q6-yeast-structure-mediated-abundance}\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite observational surface.}
The record tests a yeast-only cross-layer surface in which nine $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates are joined to measured translation efficiency, mRNA half-life, a local coding-region structure proxy, and matched protein abundance. The coordinates are $K\_AAA$, $Arg\_AGR$, $Ile\_AUA$, $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$, $Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG$, $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$, $Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG$, $Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY$, and $f3\_stress$. The covariate block contains an intercept, $\log(\mathrm{CDS\ length})$, twenty amino-acid composition proportions, $GC3$, and the $M$-density baseline defined by the fraction of sense codons lying on the union support of the nine projected coordinates. The resulting control design has $24$ columns before numerical rank reduction, rank $23$, and leaves $4736$ residual degrees of freedom after residualization.

\paragraph{Joined rows and readout definitions.}
The finite join contains $4759$ yeast genes. The reported join hit rate is $0.8818572656921754$, with a CDS join hit rate $0.9156038419146592$. Protein abundance and translation-efficiency rows are keyed through the yeast ORF/protein join: $5815$ CDS records are reported joined at the CDS layer, $5128$ protein identifiers carry valid translation-efficiency rows, $5379$ mRNA half-life records are valid by systematic gene name, and $6027$ structure records contain valid MFE values. The translation-efficiency readout is the mean footprint normalized read density across two rich-medium ribosome-footprint replicates divided by the mean mRNA normalized read density across the paired mRNA replicates. The structure coordinate $S$ is the ViennaRNA RNAfold minimum free energy of the first $39$ nucleotides of the coding region, keyed by systematic ORF. The protein readout $P$ is matched abundance on the same joined yeast rows. Thus the row surface is finite, organism-specific, and keyed by matched sequence, translation, mRNA-stability, local-structure, and abundance identifiers rather than by an abstract association alone.

\paragraph{Residual geometry.}
After the control block $Z$ is removed, the residual energies are $P_e:1724.6664873071313$, $T_e:645.3764418081507$, $M_e:871992.3819246667$, and $S_e:28554.997251412347$. The residualized coordinate matrix has rank $9$. Adding structure gives rank $10$, adding measured translation efficiency and mRNA stability gives rank $11$, and adding all three readout blocks gives rank $12$. The structure-only residualized block has rank $1$, the translation-plus-mRNA block has rank $2$, and the combined translation, mRNA, and structure block has rank $3$. These ranks rule out a vacuous one-column collapse of the tested surface: $Q$, $S$, $T$, and $M$ retain distinct finite-row directions after the declared controls.

\paragraph{Abundance association and conditional attenuation.}
The primary decomposition is a controlled variance partition, not a causal path estimate. With $Q_e$ denoting the nine-dimensional residual coordinate block after $Z$ residualization, the total controlled association with protein abundance is
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2(Q_e \to P_e) &= 0.2715520808117803.
\end{aligned}
$$
Conditioning on the residualized structure proxy alone gives
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2(Q_e \to P_e\mid S_e) &= 0.2706601505000995,
\end{aligned}
$$
whereas conditioning on measured translation efficiency and mRNA stability gives
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2(Q_e \to P_e\mid T_e,M_e) &= 0.13773967696311287.
\end{aligned}
$$
Conditioning on translation efficiency, mRNA stability, and the local structure proxy together gives
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2(Q_e \to P_e\mid T_e,M_e,S_e) &= 0.13759011208318542.
\end{aligned}
$$
Equivalently, the structure-only fraction of the original $Q$-to-$P$ association is $0.003284564452662019$, and the incremental structure fraction beyond measured translation efficiency plus mRNA stability is $0.0005507778820193332$. The direct improvement energy for $Q$ given structure is $466.79849101702615$; after conditioning on translation efficiency and mRNA stability it is $237.5550048307909$; after conditioning on all three readout blocks it is $237.29705529470186$.

\paragraph{Structure signal readback.}
The structure proxy is not silent with respect to the residual-coordinate block, but its contribution is small. The controlled fit of residualized structure from the nine residual coordinates is
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2(S_e \sim Q_e) &= 0.003774248455422586,
\end{aligned}
$$
with right-tail permutation value $0.027972027972027972$ under the recorded deterministic structure-residual permutation seed. The incremental abundance partition attributed to structure beyond translation efficiency and mRNA stability is weaker, with right-tail permutation value $0.1258741258741259$. The finite-row conclusion is therefore asymmetric: the coordinates weakly align with the local 5-prime coding-region MFE proxy, but the same proxy explains only a very small additional part of the abundance association once measured translation efficiency and mRNA stability have been included.

\paragraph{Relation to the broader protein-abundance mediation surface.}
The same nine-coordinate family also appears in the cross-organism translation-conditioned protein-abundance decomposition. In that larger table, the yeast entry has $5815$ joined proteins, $T$ residual energy $6.90537888244409$, protein-abundance residual energy $2958.1865890925337$, $R^2_{QP}=0.29837624994140827$, $R^2_{QP\mid T}=0.0151709093608293$, indirect difference $0.28320534058057895$, and mediated fraction $0.9491551041216973$ for modeled per-protein tAI. This comparison is useful only as a bounded consistency check: the present yeast structure chapter uses measured translation efficiency, mRNA stability, local coding-region MFE, and matched abundance rows, and it reports a structure-conditioned variance partition rather than a universal protein-realization law. A separate structural-order axis audit identifies $f3\_stress$ as the only fold-level sign-flip significant coordinate, with weighted held-out increment $0.0031744502294756994$ and sign-flip value $0.03125$; that result motivates attention to structure-adjacent readouts but does not by itself turn the MFE partition into a folding claim.

\paragraph{Bounded conclusion.}
The ready claim is that, on $4759$ matched yeast rows, the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual block has a controlled protein-abundance association that is strongly attenuated by measured translation efficiency plus mRNA stability, while the first-$39$-nt CDS MFE proxy contributes only a small additional conditioning term. The finite numbers support a chapter-level statement of statistical decomposition: $Q$ carries abundance-associated residual variation; $T$ and $M$ absorb a large part of that variation; $S$ is weakly but detectably related to $Q$ and adds little once $T$ and $M$ are present. This does not establish translation realization, folded structure, physical admissibility, molecular function, biological mechanism, selection, phenotype, or universality. Any such stronger statement would require a separate layer-matched contact and its own controlled statistic.
