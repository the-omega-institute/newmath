\subsection{Yeast structure mediation for $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ abundance residuals}\label{sec:bioreality-q6-yeast-structure-mediation}\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite row surface.} The yeast mediation record is a finite cross-layer surface on $4759$ joined Saccharomyces cerevisiae rows.  The joined table couples real CDS codon counts, matched protein abundance, measured translation efficiency, mRNA half-life, and a CDS-local structure proxy.  The translation readout is the mean footprint normalized read density divided by the mean mRNA normalized read density across the two rich-medium replicates of the Ingolia et al. 2009 BY4741 data.  The structure coordinate is the ViennaRNA minimum free energy of the first $39$ nucleotides of the coding sequence, keyed through the systematic ORF locus tag.  The mRNA half-life contact contributes $5379$ valid systematic identifiers, the translation-efficiency contact contributes $5128$ valid protein-keyed rows, and the CDS table contributes $5815$ joined protein rows before the final five-way intersection.  The reported CDS join hit rate is $0.9156038419146592$, and the final five-way join hit rate is $0.8818572656921754$.

\paragraph{Residual coordinates and controls.} The $Q$ side uses the nine $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates
$$
\begin{aligned}
Q=&\{K_{AAA},\ Arg_{AGR},\ Ile_{AUA},\ Leu_{CUN\,vs\,UUR},\ Leu_{UUA\,vs\,UUG},\\
&Ser_{UCR\,vs\,AGY},\ Ser_{UCA\,vs\,UCG},\ Thr_{ACR\,vs\,ACY},\ f3\_stress\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The control block $Z$ consists of an intercept, $\log(\mathrm{CDS\ length\ in\ nt})$, twenty amino-acid composition proportions from the observed CDS codon counts, GC3 from the same CDS rows, and an $M$-density baseline equal to the fraction of sense codons lying on the union support of the nine projected $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ vectors.  After residualization against $Z$, the diagnostic ranks are $\operatorname{rank}(Z)=23$, $\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde Q)=9$, $\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde S)=1$, $\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde T\widetilde M)=2$, $\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde T\widetilde M\widetilde S)=3$, $\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde Q\widetilde S)=10$, $\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde Q\widetilde T\widetilde M)=11$, and $\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde Q\widetilde T\widetilde M\widetilde S)=12$, with $4736$ residual degrees of freedom.  The residual energies are $645.3764418081507$ for translation efficiency, $871992.3819246667$ for mRNA stability, $28554.997251412347$ for the structure proxy, and $1724.6664873071313$ for protein abundance.

\paragraph{Variance partition.} Let $P_e$ denote residualized log protein abundance, $Q_e$ the residualized nine-coordinate $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ matrix, $S_e$ the residualized $5'$ CDS MFE proxy, $T_e$ the residualized measured translation-efficiency readout, and $M_e$ the residualized mRNA-stability readout.  The finite-row decomposition is
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{QP} &= R^2(P_e\sim Q_e)=0.2715520808117803,\\
R^2_{QP\mid S} &= 0.2706601505000995,\\
R^2_{QP\mid T,M} &= 0.13773967696311287,\\
R^2_{QP\mid T,M,S} &= 0.13759011208318542.
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the structure-only share of the $Q$--protein association is
$$
\begin{aligned}
\frac{R^2_{QP}-R^2_{QP\mid S}}{R^2_{QP}}=0.003284564452662019,
\end{aligned}
$$
while the structure increment beyond measured translation efficiency and mRNA stability is
$$
\begin{aligned}
\frac{R^2_{QP\mid T,M}-R^2_{QP\mid T,M,S}}{R^2_{QP}}=0.0005507778820193332.
\end{aligned}
$$
The finite panel also gives $R^2(S_e\sim Q_e)=0.003774248455422586$, with right-tail permutation value $p=0.027972027972027972$ for the $Q$ to structure association.  The right-tail permutation value for the structure increment after translation efficiency and mRNA stability is $p=0.1258741258741259$.  These two numbers separate a weak but detectable $Q$-to-MFE association from a much smaller post-$T,M$ structure contribution to protein-abundance residuals.

\paragraph{Readback.} The admissible readback is a controlled statistical decomposition on matched yeast rows.  The direct $Q$ to protein-abundance residual association is large on this panel, but conditioning on measured translation efficiency and mRNA stability removes about half of the apparent $Q$ contribution: $R^2_{QP}$ drops from $0.2715520808117803$ to $0.13773967696311287$.  Adding the $5'$ CDS MFE proxy after $T$ and $M$ changes the residual $Q$ contribution only from $0.13773967696311287$ to $0.13759011208318542$.  In energy terms, the direct $Q$ improvement against $P$ is $468.33677333459536$, the direct $Q$ improvement after $S$ is $466.79849101702615$, the direct $Q$ improvement after $T,M$ is $237.5550048307909$, and the direct $Q$ improvement after $T,M,S$ is $237.29705529470186$.  The same $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ coordinate family has a broader multi-organism modeled-tAI mediation surface in which Saccharomyces cerevisiae has $5815$ joined protein rows and a modeled $M^{QTP}$ fraction of $0.9491551041216973$, with $R^2_{QP}=0.29837624994140827$ and $R^2_{QP\mid T}=0.0151709093608293$ under the modeled tAI readout.  That comparison is useful only as a bounded consistency check: the present yeast structure packet uses measured TE, mRNA stability, protein abundance, and a sequence-derived MFE proxy on the same finite organismal panel.

\paragraph{Boundary of the claim.} This chapter supports a finite yeast variance partition: $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates remain associated with protein-abundance residuals after the declared $Z$ controls, measured translation efficiency plus mRNA stability account for a substantial share of that association, and the $5'$ coding-region MFE proxy contributes only a small additional partition after those readouts.  The result is not a causal pathway estimate.  The MFE coordinate is computed from the coding sequence, not measured as an in vivo RNA ensemble, and it omits the $5'$ untranslated region.  The table has one organism and one matched collection of readouts, so it does not establish a population law.  No statement about ribosome dynamics beyond the measured TE contact, protein folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, mechanism, phenotype, or universality follows from this record unless an additional layer-matched contact supplies that measurement and the corresponding controlled finite-row statistic.
