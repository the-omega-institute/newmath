\subsection{Yeast abundance residuals with a translation-and-structure mediation surface}
\label{sec:bioreality-yeast-abundance-residual-structure-mediation}

\paragraph{Finite row surface.}
This record concerns a finite yeast table in which each row joins a coding sequence, a protein-abundance readout, a measured translation-efficiency readout, an mRNA-stability readout, and a coding-region structure proxy.  The usable five-way join contains $4759$ genes.  The reported CDS join hit rate is $0.9156038419146592$, and the full join hit rate is $0.8818572656921754$.  The translation table contains $5128$ valid protein-keyed translation-efficiency entries, the structure table contains $6027$ valid minimum-free-energy entries, and the mRNA half-life table contains $5379$ valid systematic-ORF entries.  The structure coordinate is the ViennaRNA minimum free energy of the first $39$ coding nucleotides, keyed by systematic ORF.  The translation coordinate is the mean footprint normalized read density divided by mean mRNA normalized read density across two rich-medium replicates in yeast.  Thus the object tested here is not a free association between synonymous geometry and protein abundance; it is a controlled finite matrix in which the $B^*_{Q6}$ residual block is compared against measured translation, mRNA stability, and a local CDS-folding proxy in matched rows.

\paragraph{Residual coordinates and controls.}
The residual block has $9$ coordinates:
$K\_AAA$, $Arg\_AGR$, $Ile\_AUA$, $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$, $Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG$, $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$, $Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG$, $Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY$, and $f3\_stress$.  Before any cross-layer comparison, the response and readout variables are residualized against the local control family consisting of an intercept, $\log(\mathrm{CDS\ length\ in\ nt})$, the $20$ amino-acid composition proportions $A,C,D,E,F,G,H,I,K,L,M,N,P,Q,R,S,T,V,W,Y$, GC3, and the M-density baseline, defined as the fraction of sense codons lying in the union support of the nine projected $B^*_{Q6}$ vectors.  The diagnostic ranks are nontrivial: the control matrix has rank $23$ from $24$ columns, the residual $B^*_{Q6}$ matrix has rank $9$, the residual structure readout has rank $1$, the measured-translation-plus-mRNA-stability block has rank $2$, and the joint translation, mRNA-stability, and structure block has rank $3$.  After controls, the residual degrees of freedom are $4736$, with residual energies $P=1724.6664873071313$, $T=645.3764418081507$, $M=871992.3819246667$, and $S=28554.997251412347$ for protein abundance, translation efficiency, mRNA stability, and structure proxy respectively.

\paragraph{Variance decomposition.}
Let $Q_e$ denote the controlled $9$-coordinate residual block, $P_e$ the controlled protein-abundance residual, $T_e$ the measured translation-efficiency residual, $M_e$ the mRNA-stability residual, and $S_e$ the coding-region structure residual.  The finite computation reports the following four residual $R^2$ values:
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2(Q_e \to P_e) &= 0.2715520808117803,\\
R^2(Q_e \to P_e \mid S_e) &= 0.2706601505000995,\\
R^2(Q_e \to P_e \mid T_e,M_e) &= 0.13773967696311287,\\
R^2(Q_e \to P_e \mid T_e,M_e,S_e) &= 0.13759011208318542.
\end{aligned}
$$
The structure-only attenuation fraction is therefore $0.003284564452662019$, while the additional structure fraction after measured translation and mRNA stability is only $0.0005507778820193332$.  The direct controlled relation from the residual block to the structure proxy is also small but nonzero, with $R^2(S_e \sim Q_e)=0.003774248455422586$ and a right-tail permutation value $p=0.027972027972027972$.  By contrast, the incremental structure fraction beyond measured translation and mRNA stability has right-tail permutation value $p=0.1258741258741259$.  In finite-row terms, the $B^*_{Q6}$ block carries a detectable but very small relation to the local CDS MFE proxy, while the portion of the protein-abundance residual association assigned uniquely to that structure proxy after translation and mRNA stability is not a powered positive mediation result.

\paragraph{Energy and rank audit.}
The energy diagnostics are consistent with the small structure fractions.  The $B^*_{Q6}$ block explains $468.33677333459536$ units of energy on $P_e$ and $107.77365427073924$ units on $S_e$.  Conditioning only on structure leaves $466.79849101702615$ units of direct improvement energy from $Q_e$ to $P_e$.  Conditioning on measured translation and mRNA stability leaves $237.5550048307909$ units, and conditioning on measured translation, mRNA stability, and structure leaves $237.29705529470186$ units.  The small difference between the last two numbers is the finite-row reason the structure-incremental fraction is tiny.  The rank checks also avoid a saturated explanation: $\mathrm{rank}(Q_e)=9$, $\mathrm{rank}(S_e)=1$, $\mathrm{rank}(T_e,M_e)=2$, $\mathrm{rank}(T_e,M_e,S_e)=3$, $\mathrm{rank}(Q_e,S_e)=10$, $\mathrm{rank}(Q_e,T_e,M_e)=11$, and $\mathrm{rank}(Q_e,T_e,M_e,S_e)=12$, under a degeneracy threshold of $0.999999$.

\paragraph{Relation to the broader translation-mediated abundance screen.}
The same nine-coordinate residual family also appears in a broader protein-abundance mediation computation across $12$ organisms.  In that computation, the yeast row has $5815$ joined proteins, $275$ total tRNA gene-copy entries, no zero raw wobble-weight fallback, and a modeled per-protein tAI readout.  For yeast, the reported total controlled protein-abundance association is $R^2_{QP}=0.29837624994140827$, the direct association after conditioning on modeled tAI is $R^2_{QP\mid T}=0.0151709093608293$, the indirect difference is $0.28320534058057895$, and the mediation fraction is $0.9491551041216973$.  Those numbers are useful as a comparison surface, but they are not substituted for the measured yeast translation-efficiency analysis above.  They show that a translation-conditioned abundance pattern can be large in the modeled tAI table, while the present yeast structure analysis asks a narrower question: whether the residual local CDS MFE proxy explains an additional part of the abundance residual beyond measured translation and mRNA stability.  On that narrower question, the finite-row answer is bounded and small.

\paragraph{Coordinate identity check.}
A separate structural-order audit of the same residual basis identifies $f3\_stress$ as the dominant axis in a held-out structural-order screen.  In that audit, the $f3\_stress$ coordinate has weighted held-out $\Delta R^2=0.0031744502294756994$, weighted absolute unit loading $0.5109586606724208$, and sign-flip value $p=0.03125$, while it is the only coordinate passing the fold-level sign-flip gate.  The other coordinates have weaker or negative increments, for example $Arg\_AGR$ has $\Delta R^2=0.0020139270616115154$ with $p=0.142578125$, $Ile\_AUA$ has $\Delta R^2=0.0013067592718559024$ with $p=0.0693359375$, and $Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY$ has $\Delta R^2=-0.00045340330387383447$ with $p=0.529296875$.  This helps interpret the yeast mediation surface: the structure-adjacent signal is concentrated and small, not a blanket statement that every synonymous residual coordinate carries a structure-level readout.

\paragraph{Local verdict.}
The supported statement is a controlled statistical decomposition.  In yeast, after amino-acid composition, CDS length, GC3, and M-density controls, the $B^*_{Q6}$ residual block has a sizeable association with matched protein-abundance residuals, and that association is strongly reduced when measured translation efficiency and mRNA stability are included.  The same residual block has a small but nonzero association with the first-$39$-nt CDS MFE proxy.  However, the additional structure component beyond measured translation and mRNA stability is numerically tiny and does not pass the reported permutation check.  The local readback is therefore: $B^*_{Q6}$ remains admissible as a controlled cross-layer abundance-residual and translation-conditioned matrix in yeast, while the structure proxy is a weak variance-partition component rather than a causal or mechanistic explanation.

\paragraph{Boundary of interpretation.}
No stronger biological conclusion follows from this table alone.  The CDS MFE readout is computed from sequence, is limited to the first $39$ coding nucleotides, omits the $5'$ UTR, and is a single minimum-free-energy estimate rather than an ensemble or measured initiation-rate assay.  Protein abundance is a steady-state readout and is not isolated from turnover, localization, post-translational modification, pathway membership, or measurement bias.  The finite computation therefore does not establish translation mechanism, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, phenotype, selection, causation, or universality.  Any such claim would require its own layer-matched measurement and a separate controlled statistic.  In this chapter, the admissible conclusion is only the finite controlled variance partition described above.
