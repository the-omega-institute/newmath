\subsection{Yeast Structure-Conditioned Abundance Residual Surface}
\label{sec:bioreality-q6-yeast-structure-mediation}

\paragraph{Claim surface.}
This record concerns the finite yeast surface on which nine $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates are joined to measured translation efficiency, mRNA half-life, a coding-region structure proxy, and matched protein abundance.  The coordinates used in the computation are
$K\_AAA$, $Arg\_AGR$, $Ile\_AUA$, $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$, $Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG$, $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$, $Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG$, $Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY$, and $f3\_stress$.  The control block contains an intercept, $\log(\mathrm{CDS\ length})$, the twenty amino-acid composition proportions $A,C,D,E,F,G,H,I,K,L,M,N,P,Q,R,S,T,V,W,Y$, real-CDS $GC3$, and an $M$-density baseline defined as the fraction of sense codons lying on the union support of the nine projected $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ vectors.  Thus the reported association is not a naked synonymous-coordinate to abundance fit; it is a residualized finite-row variance decomposition after sequence length, amino-acid composition, nucleotide composition, and the projected support baseline have already been removed.

\paragraph{Joined finite rows.}
The yeast row assembly is a five-readout join with $n=4759$ matched rows.  The CDS side reports $5815$ joined CDS entries before the full readout intersection, while the translation-efficiency side contains $5128$ valid protein-keyed entries, the mRNA half-life side contains $5379$ valid systematic-ORF records, and the structure side contains $6027$ valid minimum-free-energy entries.  The reported CDS join hit rate is $0.9156038419146592$ and the final multi-readout join hit rate is $0.8818572656921754$.  The translation readout is the mean footprint normalized read density across two rich-medium replicates divided by the mean mRNA normalized read density across two matched rich-medium replicates.  The structure readout is the ViennaRNA RNAfold minimum free energy for the first $39$ nucleotides of the coding sequence, keyed by yeast systematic ORF.  The mRNA half-life table contributes transcript-stability information, not a translation readout by itself.

\paragraph{Residual ranks and finite algebra.}
After control residualization the design has rank $23$ for the control matrix, rank $9$ for the residual $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ coordinate block, rank $1$ for the residual structure coordinate, rank $2$ for the measured-translation plus mRNA-stability block, and rank $3$ for the joint translation, mRNA-stability, and structure block.  The residual degrees of freedom after the control block are $4736$.  The reported residual energies are
$$
\begin{aligned}
E_P &= 1724.6664873071313,\\
E_T &= 645.3764418081507,\\
E_M &= 871992.3819246667,\\
E_S &= 28554.997251412347.
\end{aligned}
$$
The coordinate block explains $468.33677333459536$ units of residual protein-abundance energy and $107.77365427073924$ units of residual structure energy.  The direct coordinate-improvement energy after conditioning on structure is $466.79849101702615$; after conditioning on measured translation plus mRNA stability it is $237.5550048307909$; after conditioning on all three auxiliary readouts it is $237.29705529470186$.  These rank and energy values make the chapter a finite-row statistical statement rather than an unrestricted biological law.

\paragraph{Protein-abundance decomposition.}
Let $Q_e$ denote the nine residualized $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ coordinates, $P_e$ the residualized log protein-abundance readout, $T_e$ the measured translation-efficiency readout, $M_e$ the mRNA-stability readout, and $S_e$ the coding-region minimum-free-energy readout, all after the same control block.  The computation reports
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{QP} &= 0.2715520808117803,\\
R^2_{QP\mid S} &= 0.2706601505000995,\\
R^2_{QP\mid T,M} &= 0.13773967696311287,\\
R^2_{QP\mid T,M,S} &= 0.13759011208318542.
\end{aligned}
$$
The structure-only attenuation fraction is therefore $0.003284564452662019$, while the incremental structure fraction beyond the measured-translation and mRNA-stability block is $0.0005507778820193332$.  The coordinate-to-structure statistic is $R^2(S_e\sim Q_e)=0.003774248455422586$, with right-tail permutation value $0.027972027972027972$.  The incremental structure fraction beyond $T_e$ and $M_e$ has right-tail permutation value $0.1258741258741259$.  Consequently the finite row set supports a small coordinate--structure association and only a very small additional structure-conditioned reduction of the coordinate--protein association once measured translation efficiency and mRNA stability are already present.

\paragraph{Relation to the broader translation-abundance surface.}
The separate cross-organism protein-abundance mediation run places the yeast case in a larger bounded comparison.  In that run the same nine-coordinate $Q$ block was decomposed through modeled per-protein tAI and protein abundance for twelve organisms.  For \textit{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}, $n=5815$ proteins were joined, the total $Q\to P$ residual association was $R^2=0.29837624994140827$, the direct association after modeled tAI was $R^2=0.0151709093608293$, and the indirect difference was $0.28320534058057895$, giving $M^{QTP}=0.9491551041216973$.  This broader modeled-tAI datum is not the same as the yeast measured-TE and structure decomposition above, but it confirms that yeast is a high-leverage member of the finite protein-abundance surface.  The modeled-tAI contact itself is bounded: the three-organism modeled-tAI survival audit had only $3$ organisms and explicitly reported that this is modeled tAI coupling, not ribosome-seq or proteomics validation.  It nevertheless computed $81$ entries above the bounded-contact threshold $0.01$ and a maximum absolute modeled coupling of $3801961.031607896$.

\paragraph{Admissible readback.}
The admissible conclusion is a controlled variance-partition statement.  In yeast, a nine-dimensional synonymous residual coordinate block has a substantial residual association with matched protein abundance after the declared sequence controls.  Conditioning on measured translation efficiency and mRNA stability removes a large part of that association, while the first-$39$-nt CDS minimum-free-energy readout contributes a small, statistically visible coordinate--structure association and a much smaller additional attenuation of the protein-abundance association after translation and mRNA stability are present.  The finite-row result may therefore be read as evidence for a structure-conditioned statistical surface inside a translation and abundance join, not as causal pathway identification.

\paragraph{Local negative scope.}
No translation mechanism follows merely from the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ coordinates, from the structure proxy, or from a direct $Q_e\to P_e$ association.  The measured-TE row is the only translation-layer readout in this yeast computation, and the mRNA half-life row is a stability covariate rather than a translation measurement.  The first-$39$-nt minimum-free-energy value is a CDS-only, single-structure proxy; it is not a measured initiation rate, not a folded-protein structure, and not an ensemble RNA-structure assay.  Protein abundance does not by itself establish folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, organismal phenotype, selection, or a universal rule.  Any such higher-layer statement would require its own layer-matched measurement surface and its own controlled finite-row statistic before promotion.
