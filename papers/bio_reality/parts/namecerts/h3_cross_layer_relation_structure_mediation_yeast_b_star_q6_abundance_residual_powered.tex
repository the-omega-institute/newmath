\subsection{Yeast abundance residuals with translation and five-prime coding-region structure controls}
\label{sec:bioreality-yeast-q6-structure-abundance-mediation}

\paragraph{Finite row surface.}
This record concerns a yeast-only joined surface in which nine $B^{*}_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates are evaluated against matched protein abundance after controlling for amino-acid composition, coding-sequence length, GC3, and an $M$-density baseline.  The coordinate block is
$K\_AAA$, $Arg\_AGR$, $Ile\_AUA$, $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$, $Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG$, $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$, $Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG$, $Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY$, and $f3\_stress$.  The fitted control design contains an intercept, $\log(\mathrm{CDS\ length})$, the twenty amino-acid composition proportions $A,C,D,E,F,G,H,I,K,L,M,N,P,Q,R,S,T,V,W,Y$, GC3 from real coding-sequence codon counts, and the fraction of sense codons lying on the union support of the nine projected residual vectors.  The five-way yeast join is computed on $4759$ rows.  Its reported CDS join hit rate is $0.9156038419146592$, and the all-source join hit rate is $0.8818572656921754$.  The joined input includes $5128$ valid translation-efficiency protein keys, $5379$ valid mRNA half-life records by systematic ORF, and $6027$ valid five-prime coding-region MFE records.

\paragraph{Readout definitions.}
The protein readout $P$ is the residualized log protein-abundance response after the declared control block.  The translation-stability block $(T,M)$ consists of measured translation efficiency from rich-medium ribosome-footprint and mRNA quantification and mRNA half-life.  The structure proxy $S$ is the ViennaRNA RNAfold minimum-free-energy estimate on the first $39$ nucleotides of the coding sequence, keyed by systematic ORF.  This is a CDS-proximal folding proxy, not a measured initiation rate and not an ensemble structural observable.  The analysis asks how much of the $Q \to P$ association remains after conditioning on $S$, after conditioning on $(T,M)$, and after conditioning on all three readout coordinates.

\paragraph{Variance partition.}
Let $Q$ denote the nine-column residual-coordinate matrix after projection away from the controls, let $P$ denote the protein-abundance residual, and let $S,T,M$ denote the residualized structure, translation-efficiency, and mRNA-stability readouts.  The computed single and joint residual $R^2$ values are
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2(Q \to P) &= 0.2715520808117803,\\
R^2(Q \to P \mid S) &= 0.2706601505000995,\\
R^2(Q \to P \mid T,M) &= 0.13773967696311287,\\
R^2(Q \to P \mid T,M,S) &= 0.13759011208318542.
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the structure-only attenuation fraction is $0.003284564452662019$, while the extra structure fraction after measured translation efficiency and mRNA stability is $0.0005507778820193332$.  The direct $Q \to S$ structure fit has $R^2=0.003774248455422586$ with right-tail permutation $p=0.027972027972027972$.  The extra structure increment after $(T,M)$ has right-tail permutation $p=0.1258741258741259$.  The finite-row result is therefore asymmetric: the residual coordinates have a small but nonzero association with the five-prime MFE proxy, whereas the added attenuation of the protein-abundance association beyond measured translation efficiency and mRNA stability is not supported at the same strength.

\paragraph{Rank and energy diagnostics.}
The residual design is not a one-column accident.  After the control projection, the coordinate block has rank $9$, the control block has rank $23$, and the residual degrees of freedom are $4736$.  The residual energies are $645.3764418081507$ for the measured translation-efficiency coordinate, $871992.3819246667$ for mRNA stability, $28554.997251412347$ for structure, and $1724.6664873071313$ for protein abundance.  The ranks of the conditioned blocks are $\operatorname{rank}(T,M)=2$, $\operatorname{rank}(T,M,S)=3$, $\operatorname{rank}(Q,T,M)=11$, and $\operatorname{rank}(Q,T,M,S)=12$.  The coordinate block explains $468.33677333459536$ units of energy on $P$ and $107.77365427073924$ units on $S$; the direct coordinate improvement energy after conditioning on $S$ is $466.79849101702615$, after conditioning on $(T,M)$ is $237.5550048307909$, and after conditioning on $(T,M,S)$ is $237.29705529470186$.

\paragraph{Relation to the broader protein-abundance mediation table.}
A separate multi-organism protein-abundance mediation computation gives useful scale for the yeast row.  In that table the same nine $B^{*}_{Q6}$ coordinates are joined to per-protein modeled tAI and PAXdb abundance across twelve organisms.  For \textit{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}, $5815$ proteins are used, $R^2(Q \to P)=0.29837624994140827$, $R^2(Q \to P\mid T)=0.0151709093608293$, and the implied mediated fraction is $0.9491551041216973$.  This does not make the structure proxy causal; it only shows that the yeast abundance surface is in the same numerical regime in which translation-conditioned attenuation is large for the modeled-tAI readout.  By contrast, the present five-way yeast analysis separates measured translation efficiency, mRNA stability, and the five-prime MFE proxy, and the structure-specific increment beyond $(T,M)$ is only $0.0005507778820193332$ of the original $Q \to P$ association.

\paragraph{Coordinate identity check.}
The coordinate family is not treated as a homogeneous black box.  In the structural-order axis audit, the same nine axes are computed, and exactly one axis passes the fold-level sign-flip gate: $f3\_stress$.  Its weighted held-out $R^2$ increment is $0.0031744502294756994$, with sign-flip $p=0.03125$, while the other axes do not pass that gate.  The relevant nonzero ordering is therefore carried most clearly by $f3\_stress$ on that structural-order audit, although the present yeast variance partition remains a joint nine-coordinate analysis.

\paragraph{Local interpretation.}
The chapter supports a bounded statement: in yeast, after amino-acid composition, CDS length, GC3, and $M$-density controls, the nine-dimensional $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual block has a substantial protein-abundance residual association, much of that association is attenuated by measured translation efficiency and mRNA stability, and the five-prime coding-region MFE proxy accounts for only a very small additional fraction beyond those readouts.  The positive $Q \to S$ permutation result records contact with a sequence-derived structure proxy, while the larger protein-abundance attenuation is carried by the translation and mRNA-stability block.  No direct $Q \to P$ edge is admitted as protein realization unless the appropriate intermediate readout is present and evaluated.

\paragraph{Local no-promotion boundary.}
These rows do not establish a causal pathway, a folding pathway, physical admissibility, molecular function, organismal phenotype, adaptive mechanism, or universal biological law.  The structure readout is a minimum-free-energy estimate on the first $39$ coding nucleotides and omits the five-prime UTR and ensemble uncertainty.  The translation readout is measured TE from a particular yeast ribosome-profiling study, and the protein readout is an abundance surface rather than a perturbational mechanism assay.  Any claim about translation mechanism, folded protein structure, physical admissibility, function, phenotype, selection, or universality would require a separate layer-matched contact and a new controlled statistic at that layer.
