\subsection{Yeast abundance residual structure mediation for $B^*_{Q6}$}
\label{sec:bioreality-q6-yeast-abundance-structure-mediation}

\paragraph{Finite row surface.}
This record fixes a finite Saccharomyces cerevisiae surface on which nine $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinates are read against matched coding sequence, measured translation efficiency, mRNA half-life, a local coding-region structure proxy, and protein abundance. The coordinate list is
$K\_AAA$, $Arg\_AGR$, $Ile\_AUA$, $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$, $Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG$, $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$, $Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG$, $Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY$, and $f3\_stress$. The control block contains an intercept, $\log(CDS\ length)$, the twenty amino-acid composition proportions, GC3, and an $M$-density baseline defined as the fraction of sense codons lying on the union support of the nine projected $B^*_{Q6}$ vectors. After this control residualization the reported ranks are $rank(Z)=23$, $rank(X)=9$, $rank(S)=1$, $rank(T,M)=2$, and $rank(T,M,S)=3$, with residual degrees of freedom $4736$.

\paragraph{Joined measurements.}
The yeast join contains $4759$ rows, with a reported total join hit rate $0.8818572656921754$ and a CDS join hit rate $0.9156038419146592$. The joined table uses $5815$ CDS rows reported as joined to abundance identifiers, $5128$ genes with translation-efficiency entries, $5379$ valid mRNA half-life records by systematic ORF, and $6027$ valid structure-MFE entries. Translation efficiency is the mean footprint normalized read density divided by the mean mRNA normalized read density across the two rich-medium replicates of the Ingolia et al. yeast data, and the structure coordinate is the ViennaRNA RNAfold minimum free energy of the first $39$ coding nucleotides. The structure readout is therefore a sequence-derived $5'$ coding-region proxy, not a measured initiation-rate or folded-protein observable.

\paragraph{Variance decomposition.}
Let $Q_e$ denote the residualized nine-dimensional $B^*_{Q6}$ block, $P_e$ the residualized protein-abundance readout, $S_e$ the residualized $5'$ coding-region MFE proxy, $T_e$ the residualized measured translation-efficiency readout, and $M_e$ the residualized mRNA-stability readout. The finite regression surface reports
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2(Q_e \to P_e) &= 0.2715520808117803,\\
R^2(Q_e \to P_e \mid S_e) &= 0.2706601505000995,\\
R^2(Q_e \to P_e \mid T_e,M_e) &= 0.13773967696311287,\\
R^2(Q_e \to P_e \mid T_e,M_e,S_e) &= 0.13759011208318542.
\end{aligned}
$$
The structure-only fraction is $0.003284564452662019$, while the structure-incremental fraction beyond measured translation efficiency and mRNA stability is $0.0005507778820193332$. The direct association from $Q_e$ to $P_e$ is therefore large on this controlled surface, but the extra attenuation supplied by the $5'$ coding-region MFE proxy is small. The permutation probability for $S_Q$ structure association is $0.027972027972027972$, whereas the right-tail probability for the incremental structure fraction is $0.1258741258741259$. Thus the finite rows support a bounded statement: $B^*_{Q6}$ residuals have detectable association with the local MFE proxy, but the structure proxy does not by itself supply a strong incremental mediation term after the measured translation and mRNA-stability block has been included.

\paragraph{Energy and rank diagnostics.}
The residual energies keep the decomposition non-vacuous: $P_e$ has residual energy $1724.6664873071313$, $S_e$ has residual energy $28554.997251412347$, $T_e$ has residual energy $645.3764418081507$, and the mRNA-stability residual has energy $871992.3819246667$. The $Q_e$ block explains $468.33677333459536$ units of energy on $P_e$ and $107.77365427073924$ units on $S_e$. Conditioning on $S_e$ leaves $466.79849101702615$ units of direct improvement energy for $Q_e$ on $P_e$; conditioning on $T_e,M_e$ leaves $237.5550048307909$ units; conditioning on $T_e,M_e,S_e$ leaves $237.29705529470186$ units. These numbers are consistent with the same qualitative reading as the $R^2$ table: the measured translation-plus-stability block absorbs a substantial part of the abundance association, while the local MFE proxy changes the remaining direct term only slightly.

\paragraph{Relation to the broader protein-abundance mediation surface.}
The multi-organism modeled-tAI protein-abundance experiment gives an independent comparison point for the same nine-dimensional $Q$ block. In that surface, Saccharomyces cerevisiae has $5815$ joined proteins, a total $Q \to P$ value $c=0.29837624994140827$, a direct value after modeled tAI $c'=0.0151709093608293$, and an indirect difference $c-c'=0.28320534058057895$, giving $M^{QTP}=0.9491551041216973$. Across the broader panel, the modeled-tAI mediation fraction ranges from $0.01850668564589326$ in Sulfolobus solfataricus to $0.9491551041216973$ in yeast. This comparison does not make the yeast MFE proxy causal; it only places the present yeast structure residual surface next to a separate statistical mediation table in which the translation readout has much larger absorbing power for yeast protein abundance.

\paragraph{Coordinate interpretation.}
The structural-order axis audit identifies $f3\_stress$ as the dominant coordinate for a separate structural-order task: its weighted held-out increment is $0.0031744502294756994$, its weighted absolute unit loading is $0.5109586606724208$, and its fold-level sign-flip probability is $0.03125$. No other coordinate passes that exact fold-level sign-flip gate in that audit. This matters here only as a coordinate warning. The yeast abundance residual surface includes $f3\_stress$ among the nine coordinates, but the present table does not isolate a per-coordinate causal chain from $f3\_stress$ to structure to protein abundance. It records a joint $9$-coordinate residual block and its conditioning behavior under the declared controls.

\paragraph{Negative controls from adjacent closure tests.}
The mass-balance closure test supplies a useful no-promotion check. On $120$ fixed-subsample genes and $718$ intervals it reports $R^2_{L0}=0.9960628201518571$, $R^2_{L1}=0.996410628851051$, $R^2_{L2}=0.9712443377743347$, and $R^2_{L3}=0.8953999568421264$, with $\Delta R^2=-0.07584438093220836$, $\Delta DL=-668.8046070381928$ bits, $p_{TEbin}=0.6831683168316832$, and $p_{timeshuffle}=0.9306930693069307$. This adjacent result reinforces the local boundary: abundance and ribosome-related statistical surfaces can be computed, but they do not automatically license a dynamic mass-balance mechanism or a downstream biological mechanism.

\paragraph{Local verdict.}
The finite yeast rows support a controlled variance-partition claim, not a pathway claim. The supported statement is that the $B^*_{Q6}$ residual block has a substantial controlled association with protein abundance on the joined yeast table, that measured translation efficiency plus mRNA stability absorbs a large part of this association, and that the first-$39$-nt coding-region MFE proxy contributes only a small additional attenuation after those readouts are present. The table also supports a weak but nonzero $Q_e \to S_e$ association with $S_Q\ structure\ R^2=0.003774248455422586$ and permutation probability $0.027972027972027972$.

\paragraph{Boundary.}
No translation mechanism, folded protein structure, physical admissibility, molecular function, organismal phenotype, causal pathway, selection law, or universal biological rule follows from this chapter. A direct $Q_i \to P_k$ association is not enough for protein realization unless a same-row $Q_i \to T_j$ translation readout survives the declared controls, and a structure statement is not established by a $5'$ coding-region MFE proxy unless a layer-matched structure contact supplies that evidence. The controls keep amino-acid sequence, organismal background, GC3, CDS length, and $M$-density in the baseline; they are not promoted into biological coordinates.
