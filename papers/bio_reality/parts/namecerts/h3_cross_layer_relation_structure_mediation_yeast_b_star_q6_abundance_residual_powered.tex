\subsection{Yeast abundance residuals through a translation and coding-structure mediation surface}\label{sec:bioreality-yeast-q6-structure-mediation}\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite panel.}
The yeast abundance experiment records a finite five-way panel of coding sequence, measured translation efficiency, mRNA half-life, a CDS-local structure proxy, and matched protein abundance. The joined surface contains $4759$ genes, with a reported overall join hit rate $0.8818572656921754$ and CDS join hit rate $0.9156038419146592$. The translation table contributes $5128$ protein-keyed entries, the mRNA half-life table contributes $5379$ valid systematic-ORF entries, and the structure table contributes $6027$ valid minimum-free-energy entries. The translation readout is measured as mean footprint normalized read density divided by mean mRNA normalized read density across two rich-medium replicates, while the structure readout is the ViennaRNA minimum free energy of the first $39$ coding nucleotides. These are finite joined measurements, not inferred biological layers.

\paragraph{Residual coordinate surface.}
The residual coordinate block has nine coordinates,
$$
\begin{aligned}
Q=\{&K\_AAA,\ Arg\_AGR,\ Ile\_AUA,\ Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR,\ Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG,\\
&Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY,\ Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG,\ Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY,\ f3\_stress\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
All fits are residualized against an intercept, $\log(\mathrm{CDS\ length})$, the $20$ amino-acid composition proportions, GC3, and an M-density baseline defined from the union support of the nine projected residual vectors. The control design has $24$ columns with residual rank $23$, leaving $4736$ residual degrees of freedom. After this residualization the coordinate rank is $9$, the measured-TE plus mRNA-stability rank is $2$, the structure rank is $1$, and the joint measured-TE/mRNA/structure rank is $3$. The residual energies recorded for the joined response surfaces are $645.3764418081507$ for translation efficiency, $871992.3819246667$ for mRNA stability, $28554.997251412347$ for the structure proxy, and $1724.6664873071313$ for protein abundance.

\paragraph{Variance partition.}
Let $Q_e$ denote the residualized nine-coordinate block, $P_e$ residualized protein abundance, $T_e$ residualized measured translation efficiency, $M_e$ residualized mRNA stability, and $S_e$ residualized CDS-local minimum free energy. The computed abundance association and conditioned increments are
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2(Q_e\to P_e) &= 0.2715520808117803,\\
R^2(Q_e\to P_e\mid S_e) &= 0.2706601505000995,\\
R^2(Q_e\to P_e\mid T_e,M_e) &= 0.13773967696311287,\\
R^2(Q_e\to P_e\mid T_e,M_e,S_e) &= 0.13759011208318542.
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the CDS-local structure proxy alone removes only a small fraction of the abundance residual association,
$$
\begin{aligned}
\frac{R^2(Q_e\to P_e)-R^2(Q_e\to P_e\mid S_e)}{R^2(Q_e\to P_e)}
=0.003284564452662019,
\end{aligned}
$$
while the additional structure contribution after conditioning on measured translation efficiency and mRNA stability is still smaller,
$$
\begin{aligned}
\frac{R^2(Q_e\to P_e\mid T_e,M_e)-R^2(Q_e\to P_e\mid T_e,M_e,S_e)}{R^2(Q_e\to P_e)}
=0.0005507778820193332.
\end{aligned}
$$
The coordinate-to-structure association itself is nonzero but weak: $R^2(S_e\sim Q_e)=0.003774248455422586$, with a right-tail permutation value $p=0.027972027972027972$. The right-tail value for the incremental structure fraction is weaker, $p=0.1258741258741259$. This supports a narrow statement: in this yeast panel, the residual coordinates have a detectable but very small relation to the CDS-local folding-energy proxy, and that proxy explains almost none of the residual coordinate-to-protein-abundance association once measured translation efficiency and mRNA stability are already present.

\paragraph{Relation to translation-mediated abundance.}
The broader matched-abundance panel reports a cross-organism $Q\to T\to P$ decomposition with the same nine-coordinate block and a modeled per-protein tAI readout. For yeast specifically, the finite-row counts are $5815$ joined proteins, residual degrees of freedom $5792$, translation residual energy $6.90537888244409$, and protein-abundance residual energy $2958.1865890925337$. Its total residual abundance association is $R^2=0.29837624994140827$, the direct association after conditioning on modeled tAI is $0.0151709093608293$, and the mediated difference is $0.28320534058057895$, giving $M^{QTP}=0.9491551041216973$. This does not make the structure proxy causal; it says that the yeast abundance association is far more strongly absorbed by the translation-readout slot than by the first-$39$-nt minimum-free-energy slot in the separate structure-mediation panel.

\paragraph{Axis identity.}
The structural-order axis audit supplies a useful coordinate-level guardrail. Across the nine coordinates, the only fold-level sign-flip survivor is $f3\_stress$, with weighted held-out increment $0.0031744502294756994$, weighted absolute unit loading $0.5109586606724208$, and sign-flip value $p=0.03125$. Other coordinates do not pass that gate: for example $Arg\_AGR$ has increment $0.0020139270616115154$ with $p=0.142578125$, $Ile\_AUA$ has increment $0.0013067592718559024$ with $p=0.0693359375$, and $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$ is negative at $-0.0003205780368125179$ with $p=0.779296875$. Therefore the finite evidence should not be read as a general codon-family rule or as a universal CUN/UUR direction. The strongest reusable coordinate description is that the $f3\_stress$ direction carries the observed structural-order signal in the available audit.

\paragraph{Local boundary.}
The valid readback is a controlled statistical decomposition on finite yeast rows. It permits the statement that $B^*_Q6$ residual abundance association is partitioned by measured translation efficiency, mRNA stability, and a CDS-local minimum-free-energy proxy under amino-acid composition, GC3, length, and M-density controls. It does not by itself establish translation realization, folding in vivo, physical admissibility, molecular function, mechanism, organismal phenotype, selection, or universality. A direct $Q\to P$ abundance association is not promoted unless the corresponding $Q\to T$ translation step is present and evaluated on matched rows; a structure readout based on first-$39$-nt minimum free energy is not a folded-protein or in-cell RNA-ensemble measurement; and a single-organism yeast sign is not a population law.
