\subsection{Yeast structure mediation for B$^{*}_{Q6}$ abundance residuals}\label{sec:bioreality-q6-yeast-structure-mediation}\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite row surface.} The yeast assay fixes a finite joined table whose rows are coding sequences with matched codon counts, protein abundance, measured translation efficiency, mRNA half-life, and a local coding-region structure proxy. The five-way join contains $4759$ usable rows. The reported CDS join hit rate is $0.9156038419146592$, and the full join hit rate is $0.8818572656921754$. The translation-efficiency source contributes $5128$ protein-keyed genes, the mRNA half-life source contributes $5379$ valid systematic ORF records, and the structure table contributes $6027$ valid MFE records. The structure readout is the ViennaRNA RNAfold minimum free energy of the first $39$ nucleotides of the coding sequence, keyed by systematic ORF; it is therefore a local CDS-derived proxy, not an observed folding trajectory or initiation-rate measurement.

\paragraph{Residual coordinates and controls.} The coordinate block has nine B$^{*}_{Q6}$ residual axes:
$$\begin{aligned}
Q = \{&\mathrm{K\_AAA},\ \mathrm{Arg\_AGR},\ \mathrm{Ile\_AUA},\ \mathrm{Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR},\ \mathrm{Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG},\\
&\mathrm{Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY},\ \mathrm{Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG},\ \mathrm{Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY},\ \mathrm{f3\_stress}\}.
\end{aligned}$$
The control matrix $Z$ uses an intercept, $\log(\mathrm{CDS\ length\ in\ nt})$, the $20$ amino-acid composition proportions computed from real CDS codon counts, GC3, and an M-density baseline defined as the fraction of sense codons lying on the union support of the nine projected B$^{*}_{Q6}$ vectors. The diagnostic rank of $Z$ is $23$ with $24$ control columns, and the residual degrees of freedom after controlling $Z$ are $4736$. After residualization, the B$^{*}_{Q6}$ coordinate rank is $9$, the structure rank is $1$, the joint measured-translation-efficiency plus mRNA-stability rank is $2$, and the joint measured-translation-efficiency plus mRNA-stability plus structure rank is $3$.

\paragraph{Variance decomposition.} Let $Q_e$, $P_e$, $S_e$, $T_e$, and $M_e$ denote the residualized B$^{*}_{Q6}$ coordinate matrix, protein abundance, local CDS MFE proxy, measured translation efficiency, and mRNA stability readouts after projection away from $Z$. The recorded decomposition is
$$\begin{aligned}
R^2_{QP} &= 0.2715520808117803,\\
R^2_{QP\mid S} &= 0.2706601505000995,\\
R^2_{QP\mid T,M} &= 0.13773967696311287,\\
R^2_{QP\mid T,M,S} &= 0.13759011208318542.
\end{aligned}$$
Thus the structure-only attenuation fraction is
$$\begin{aligned}
\frac{R^2_{QP}-R^2_{QP\mid S}}{R^2_{QP}} = 0.003284564452662019,
\end{aligned}$$
whereas the extra structure attenuation after measured translation efficiency and mRNA stability are already conditioned is
$$\begin{aligned}
\frac{R^2_{QP\mid T,M}-R^2_{QP\mid T,M,S}}{R^2_{QP}} = 0.0005507778820193332.
\end{aligned}$$
The direct structure fit from residual coordinates has $R^2(S_e\sim Q_e)=0.003774248455422586$ with right-tail permutation probability $0.027972027972027972$. The incremental structure attenuation beyond measured translation efficiency and mRNA stability has right-tail permutation probability $0.1258741258741259$.

\paragraph{Energy and rank audit.} The residual protein-abundance energy is $1724.6664873071313$, the residual structure energy is $28554.997251412347$, the residual measured-translation-efficiency energy is $645.3764418081507$, and the residual mRNA-stability energy is $871992.3819246667$. The B$^{*}_{Q6}$ block explains $468.33677333459536$ units of protein-abundance energy and $107.77365427073924$ units of structure energy. Conditioning on structure leaves a direct B$^{*}_{Q6}$ improvement energy of $466.79849101702615$ on protein abundance. Conditioning on measured translation efficiency and mRNA stability leaves $237.5550048307909$, and conditioning on all three mediator blocks leaves $237.29705529470186$. These finite-row numbers support a bounded statistical statement: local CDS MFE accounts for a small measurable part of the B$^{*}_{Q6}$ abundance residual surface, but most of the attenuation is carried by measured translation efficiency together with mRNA stability rather than by the local MFE proxy alone.

\paragraph{Relation to the wider translation-abundance surface.} The same nine-coordinate B$^{*}_{Q6}$ panel has a multi-organism translation-conditioned abundance decomposition in which yeast carries a large modeled-tAI-mediated fraction. In the yeast row of that separate surface, $R^2_{QP}=0.29837624994140827$, $R^2_{QP\mid T}=0.0151709093608293$, and the mediated difference is $0.28320534058057895$, giving $M^{QTP}=0.9491551041216973$. That larger cross-organism result uses modeled per-protein tAI rather than the yeast five-way measured-TE, mRNA-stability, and MFE table considered here. The two records are compatible only at the level of finite statistical readouts: the yeast structure table refines the abundance residual into a small local-CDS-MFE component and a much larger translation-plus-mRNA-stability component.

\paragraph{Coordinate identity.} A separate structural-order audit over the same B$^{*}_{Q6}$ coordinate family identifies $\mathrm{f3\_stress}$ as the only fold-level sign-flip-significant axis. Its weighted held-out $R^2$ increment is $0.0031744502294756994$, its weighted absolute unit loading is $0.5109586606724208$, and its sign-flip probability is $0.03125$. The remaining coordinate increments are not fold-level significant in that audit: for example, $\mathrm{Arg\_AGR}$ has increment $0.0020139270616115154$ with probability $0.142578125$, $\mathrm{Ile\_AUA}$ has increment $0.0013067592718559024$ with probability $0.0693359375$, and $\mathrm{Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY}$ has increment $-0.00045340330387383447$ with probability $0.529296875$. This makes the yeast structure-mediation record biologically interpretable as a narrow residual-order contact, with $\mathrm{f3\_stress}$ the most stable named axis, rather than as a population-wide law over all synonymous residual coordinates.

\paragraph{Local conclusion.} The finite-row conclusion is that B$^{*}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates retain a strong association with yeast protein abundance after the declared controls, and that the local $5'$ CDS MFE proxy has a small but nonzero relation to those residual coordinates. The structure-only attenuation is about $0.33\%$ of $R^2_{QP}$, while the incremental structure attenuation after measured translation efficiency and mRNA stability is about $0.055\%$ of $R^2_{QP}$ and is not permutation-significant at the same strength as the direct $Q_e\to S_e$ association. The record is therefore ready as a controlled variance-partition claim for a yeast finite table, not as a causal pathway claim.

\paragraph{Scope limits.} No translation mechanism, folding realization, physical admissibility, molecular function, organismal phenotype, selection claim, or universal biological rule follows from this table alone. The MFE readout is sequence-derived and local to the first $39$ coding nucleotides; it is not a measured folded ensemble. Protein abundance is steady-state abundance and remains entangled with turnover, localization, post-translational state, and assay-specific measurement effects. Any stronger statement would require a separate layer-matched contact and a new controlled statistic at that layer.
