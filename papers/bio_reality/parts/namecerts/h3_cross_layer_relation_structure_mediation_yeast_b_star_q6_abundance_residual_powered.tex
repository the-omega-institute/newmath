\subsection{Yeast structure-conditioned abundance residual for $B^\ast_{Q6}$}
\label{sec:bioreality-q6-yeast-structure-conditioned-abundance}

\paragraph{Finite surface.}
This record studies a yeast finite-row surface in which the nine $B^\ast_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates are evaluated against matched translation, RNA-stability, local coding-region structure, and protein-abundance readouts.  The coordinate list is
$$
\begin{aligned}
Q=\{&K\_AAA,\ Arg\_AGR,\ Ile\_AUA,\ Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR,\ Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG,\\
&Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY,\ Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG,\ Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY,\ f3\_stress\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The joined yeast table has $4759$ rows after requiring coding-sequence codon counts, protein abundance, measured translation efficiency, mRNA half-life, and a structure proxy.  The reported join rate is $0.8818572656921754$, with $5815$ CDS rows available before the final five-way restriction, $5128$ proteins with TE records, $5379$ valid mRNA half-life records, and $6027$ valid structure-MFE records.  The structure coordinate $S$ is the ViennaRNA minimum free energy of the first $39$ coding nucleotides, keyed by systematic ORF; it is therefore a local CDS folding proxy, not a measured initiation rate and not a protein-structure readout.

\paragraph{Controls and rank.}
All association quantities are computed after residualizing against the declared control matrix $Z$: an intercept, $\log(\mathrm{CDS\ length})$, the $20$ amino-acid composition proportions $A,C,D,E,F,G,H,I,K,L,M,N,P,Q,R,S,T,V,W,Y$, GC3 from real CDS codon counts, and the $M$-density baseline given by the fraction of sense codons on the union support of the nine projected $B^\ast_{Q6}$ vectors.  The rank diagnostics show $\operatorname{rank}(Z)=23$, $\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde Q)=9$, $\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde S)=1$, $\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde{T,M})=2$, and $4736$ residual degrees of freedom after $Z$.  The residual energies are $28554.997251412347$ for $S$, $645.3764418081507$ for measured TE, $871992.3819246667$ for mRNA half-life, and $1724.6664873071313$ for protein abundance.  Thus the computation is not rank-vacuous at the $Q$ surface, although the structure readout is one-dimensional.

\paragraph{Variance partition.}
Let $P_e,Q_e,S_e,T_e,M_e$ denote the residualized protein abundance, residual-coordinate matrix, 5-prime CDS MFE, measured translation efficiency, and mRNA half-life after $Z$.  The registered decomposition is
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{QP} &= R^2(P_e\sim Q_e),\\
R^2_{QP\mid S} &= \Delta R^2(P_e\sim Q_e\mid S_e),\\
R^2_{QP\mid T,M} &= \Delta R^2(P_e\sim Q_e\mid T_e,M_e),\\
R^2_{QP\mid T,M,S} &= \Delta R^2(P_e\sim Q_e\mid T_e,M_e,S_e).
\end{aligned}
$$
The finite run gives
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{QP} &= 0.2715520808117803,\\
R^2_{QP\mid S} &= 0.2706601505000995,\\
R^2_{QP\mid T,M} &= 0.13773967696311287,\\
R^2_{QP\mid T,M,S} &= 0.13759011208318542.
\end{aligned}
$$
Conditioning on $S$ alone removes only
$$
\begin{aligned}
0.2715520808117803-0.2706601505000995=0.0008919303116808,
\end{aligned}
$$
which corresponds to the recorded structure-only fraction $0.003284564452662019$.  Beyond measured TE and mRNA half-life, adding $S$ changes the remaining $Q$ increment by
$$
\begin{aligned}
0.13773967696311287-0.13759011208318542=0.00014956487992745,
\end{aligned}
$$
with structure-incremental fraction $0.0005507778820193332$.  The direct $Q$ explanation of $S$ is small but nonzero: $R^2(S_e\sim Q_e)=0.003774248455422586$, with right-tail permutation probability $0.027972027972027972$.  The incremental structure fraction beyond $T,M$ is weaker under the same permutation design, with right-tail probability $0.1258741258741259$.

\paragraph{Readback against the translation-mediated abundance surface.}
The same yeast organism also appears in the cross-organism $Q\to T\to P$ abundance decomposition with $5815$ protein rows before this chapter's stricter structure and half-life joins.  In that table the yeast entry has $T$ residual energy $6.90537888244409$, protein-abundance residual energy $2958.1865890925337$, $T$ explained energy on $Y$ equal to $941.8858618613751$, $Q$ explained energy on $T$ equal to $5.671703404506426$, and $Q$ explained energy on $Y$ equal to $882.6526210803958$.  Its total $Q$--protein abundance value is $R^2=0.29837624994140827$, the direct value after conditioning on modeled per-protein tAI is $0.0151709093608293$, and the recorded mediated fraction is $0.9491551041216973$.  The present yeast structure run is more restrictive: it replaces the modeled-tAI-only mediation scalar with a five-way joined panel and asks whether a local structure proxy explains additional $Q$--protein abundance variation after measured TE and mRNA stability are already in the conditioning set.

\paragraph{Interpretation.}
The allowed conclusion is narrow.  On this finite yeast surface, the $B^\ast_{Q6}$ residual matrix has a sizeable controlled association with protein abundance, a much smaller controlled association with the 5-prime CDS MFE proxy, and an almost negligible extra structure-conditioned contribution after measured TE and mRNA half-life are included.  Numerically, the structure proxy accounts for about $0.33\%$ of the unconditioned $Q$--protein abundance signal and about $0.055\%$ of that signal beyond the $T,M$ block.  The finite evidence is therefore compatible with a weak sequence-derived structure trace, but it does not promote the structure proxy into a causal mediator.

\paragraph{Local limitation.}
This chapter does not infer translation realization, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, mechanism, phenotype, selection, or universality from the residual coordinates.  Translation-layer wording is limited to the attached measured-TE rows in this yeast table, protein-abundance wording is limited to matched abundance measurements, and the 5-prime MFE variable is only a single-sequence computational proxy.  Any assertion about ribosome dynamics, folded protein structure, physical feasibility, biological function, causal pathway, or general biological law would require a separate layer-matched contact and a new finite statistic evaluated under its own controls.
