\subsection{Yeast abundance residuals after translation and local structure conditioning}
\label{sec:bioreality-q6-yeast-abundance-residual-mediation}

\paragraph{Finite row surface.}
The yeast mediation packet is a finite joined surface on $4759$ coding-sequence rows.  Each row carries a nine-coordinate $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual vector, a measured translation-efficiency readout, an mRNA half-life readout, a local coding-region structure proxy, matched protein abundance, and the control block used to keep codon geometry from being promoted into a biological layer by itself.  The nine residual coordinates are
$$
\begin{aligned}
&K\_AAA,\ Arg\_AGR,\ Ile\_AUA,\ Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR,\ Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG,\\
&Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY,\ Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG,\ Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY,\ f3\_stress .
\end{aligned}
$$
The control matrix contains an intercept, $\log(\mathrm{CDS\ length})$, the $20$ amino-acid composition proportions $A,C,D,E,F,G,H,I,K,L,M,N,P,Q,R,S,T,V,W,Y$, real-CDS $GC3$, and an $M$-density baseline defined as the fraction of sense codons lying on the union support of the nine projected $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ vectors.  The realized control rank is $23$ over $24$ control columns, leaving $4736$ residual degrees of freedom.  The residual ranks used by the decomposition are $\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde X)=9$, $\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde S)=1$, $\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde T\widetilde M)=2$, $\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde T\widetilde M\widetilde S)=3$, $\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde X\widetilde S)=10$, $\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde X\widetilde T\widetilde M)=11$, and $\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde X\widetilde T\widetilde M\widetilde S)=12$.

\paragraph{Joined measurements.}
The joined yeast panel is not a symbolic extrapolation.  It joins real CDS codon counts, protein abundance, measured translation efficiency, mRNA half-life, and a CDS-local folding-energy proxy.  The protein and CDS join reports $5815$ CDS-linked abundance rows before the full five-way restriction.  Translation efficiency contributes $5128$ valid protein-keyed rows; mRNA half-life contributes $5379$ valid systematic-ORF rows; the structure table contributes $6027$ valid minimum-free-energy rows.  The final five-way intersection has $4759$ rows, with reported hit rates $0.9156038419146592$ for CDS joining and $0.8818572656921754$ for the full join.  The translation readout is the mean footprint normalized read density divided by mean mRNA normalized read density across two rich-medium replicates from the Ingolia yeast data, and the structure readout is ViennaRNA RNAfold minimum free energy on the first $39$ nucleotides of the coding sequence.  The mRNA half-life payload is identified by SHA-256 hash \texttt{cee1ee9a816f0ec1e351bc2d63d4838ae603ea20566fc3ff529514a6ba6cf7d2}.  Missingness is finite and recorded: $687$ CDS records lack the TE match and $369$ lack the mRNA half-life match; the supplied counts report no duplicate TE protein identifiers, no duplicate mRNA systematic identifiers, no nonpositive TE rows, no nonpositive protein-abundance rows, and no nonfinite structure-MFE rows.

\paragraph{Residual variance decomposition.}
Let $Q$ denote the residualized nine-dimensional $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ block, $P$ the residualized matched protein-abundance readout, $T$ the residualized measured translation-efficiency readout, $M$ the residualized mRNA-stability readout, and $S$ the residualized $5'$ CDS MFE readout.  All residualization is with respect to the control block above.  The total residual association between $Q$ and $P$ is
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2(Q\to P)&=0.2715520808117803.
\end{aligned}
$$
Conditioning on the structure proxy alone leaves
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2(Q\to P\mid S)&=0.2706601505000995,
\end{aligned}
$$
so the structure-only attenuation fraction is $0.003284564452662019$.  Conditioning on measured translation efficiency plus mRNA stability leaves
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2(Q\to P\mid T,M)&=0.13773967696311287,
\end{aligned}
$$
and adding the structure proxy to that conditioning set leaves
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2(Q\to P\mid T,M,S)&=0.13759011208318542.
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the incremental structure fraction beyond $T$ and $M$ is $0.0005507778820193332$.  In energy units, the direct improvement carried by $X$ after $S$ is $466.79849101702615$, after $T,M$ is $237.5550048307909$, and after $T,M,S$ is $237.29705529470186$.  The residual energies are $1724.6664873071313$ for $P$, $645.3764418081507$ for $T$, $871992.3819246667$ for $M$, and $28554.997251412347$ for $S$.

\paragraph{Structure contact strength.}
The finite statistic does detect a small direct relation between the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ block and the local coding-region MFE proxy.  The reported value is
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2(S\sim Q)&=0.003774248455422586,
\end{aligned}
$$
with a right-tail permutation value $p=0.027972027972027972$ under the deterministic structure-residual permutation seed.  The stronger question, whether structure contributes a meaningful additional mediation fraction beyond measured translation efficiency and mRNA stability, is not supported at the same level: its right-tail permutation value is $p=0.1258741258741259$.  The appropriate readback is therefore narrow.  The data support a small sequence-derived local-MFE association with the residual block and a very small structure increment after translation-efficiency and mRNA-stability conditioning; they do not support a causal statement that local folding mediates the abundance residual.

\paragraph{Relation to the broader translation-mediated abundance surface.}
The larger protein-abundance mediation experiment supplies useful context without replacing the yeast-specific statistic.  Across $12$ organisms, the joint nine-coordinate $Q\to T\to P$ decomposition has positive bounded fractions, including $0.9491551041216973$ in \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}, where $R^2(Q\to P)=0.29837624994140827$, $R^2(Q\to P\mid T)=0.0151709093608293$, and the indirect difference is $0.28320534058057895$.  That larger surface uses modeled per-protein tAI rather than the measured TE and mRNA-stability conditioning used in this yeast structure packet.  Separately, the modeled tAI survival matrix was computed on $3$ organisms with $81$ entries above threshold $0.01$ and maximum absolute $S_{ij}=3801961.031607896$, but the same record states that $3$ organisms are insufficient for a powered survival claim.  The structural-order axis audit also reports that among the same nine axes, only $f3\_stress$ passed the fold-level sign-flip gate, with weighted held-out $\Delta R^2=0.0031744502294756994$ and $p=0.03125$.  These surrounding facts sharpen the interpretation: $f3\_stress$ is the only identified structural-order axis in that audit, while the yeast mediation packet here remains a finite variance partition over measured TE, mRNA half-life, local CDS MFE, and protein abundance.

\paragraph{Local no-promotion rule.}
This chapter licenses only the finite statistical statement just described.  A direct $Q\to P$ association is not read as protein realization unless the relevant translation-readout step is present and survives the declared controls.  The $5'$ coding-region MFE value is a CDS-only, single-minimum proxy; it is not a measured initiation rate, not an ensemble structure distribution, and not a folded-protein measurement.  The measured TE and mRNA half-life contacts help separate translation and transcript-stability layers, but they do not by themselves establish a molecular mechanism.  No claim about translation as a universal rule, folded structure, physical admissibility, molecular function, organismal phenotype, selection, causation, or biological universality follows from this packet.  Each of those layers would require its own matched measurement surface and its own finite statistic after the same kind of control discipline.
