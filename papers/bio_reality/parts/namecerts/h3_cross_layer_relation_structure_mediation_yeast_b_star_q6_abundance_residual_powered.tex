\subsection{Yeast abundance residual structure mediation for $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$}
\label{sec:bioreality-q6-yeast-abundance-structure-mediation}

\paragraph{Finite row surface.}
The yeast mediation record is a finite cross-layer regression surface over nine $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates, a measured translation-efficiency readout, an mRNA-stability readout, a sequence-derived 5-prime coding-region structure proxy, and matched protein abundance.  The nine coordinates are $K\_AAA$, $Arg\_AGR$, $Ile\_AUA$, $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$, $Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG$, $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$, $Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG$, $Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY$, and $f3\_stress$.  The join contains $4759$ yeast genes after matching real CDS codon counts, PAXdb-style protein abundance, Ingolia et al. rich-medium translation-efficiency measurements, mRNA half-life, and a ViennaRNA RNAfold minimum-free-energy readout computed on the first $39$ nt of the CDS.  The reported join hit rate is $0.8818572656921754$, with $5815$ CDS rows available before the five-way join, $5128$ valid TE-by-protein rows, $5379$ valid half-life records by systematic ORF, and $6027$ valid structure-MFE records.

\paragraph{Controlled variables.}
All fitted readbacks are residualized against a fixed control block $Z$ rather than against raw codon counts alone.  The control block contains an intercept, $\log(\mathrm{CDS\ length\ in\ nt})$, the $20$ amino-acid composition proportions $A,C,D,E,F,G,H,I,K,L,M,N,P,Q,R,S,T,V,W,Y$, GC3, and an M-density baseline defined as the fraction of sense codons lying on the union support of the nine projected $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ vectors.  The diagnostic rank of $Z$ is $23$ with $24$ control columns, and the residual degrees of freedom after $Z$ are $4736$.  The residualized coordinate block has rank $9$, the residualized structure block has rank $1$, the translation-plus-mRNA block has rank $2$, and the combined translation-plus-mRNA-plus-structure block has rank $3$.  The corresponding joined design ranks are $10$ for $Q$ with structure, $11$ for $Q$ with translation and mRNA stability, and $12$ for $Q$ with all three mediating readout classes.

\paragraph{Variance partition.}
Let $Q_e$ denote the residualized nine-coordinate $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ matrix, $P_e$ the residualized protein-abundance readout, $S_e$ the residualized 5-prime CDS MFE proxy, and $T_e,M_e$ the residualized measured translation-efficiency and mRNA-stability readouts.  The recorded decomposition is
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{QP} &= 0.2715520808117803,\\
R^2_{QP\mid S} &= 0.2706601505000995,\\
R^2_{QP\mid T,M} &= 0.13773967696311287,\\
R^2_{QP\mid T,M,S} &= 0.13759011208318542.
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the direct $Q$-to-protein residual association is large on this finite yeast surface, but most of the reduction is associated with the translation-efficiency plus mRNA-stability conditioning block rather than with the 5-prime CDS structure proxy alone.  The structure-only fraction is $0.003284564452662019$, while the additional structure fraction beyond measured TE and mRNA stability is $0.0005507778820193332$.  The structure proxy itself is weakly but nonzero coupled to the $Q$ block, with
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2(S_e \sim Q_e) &= 0.003774248455422586,\\
p_{\mathrm{perm}}(S_Q\ \mathrm{structure}) &= 0.027972027972027972,\\
p_{\mathrm{perm}}(\mathrm{structure\ incremental}) &= 0.1258741258741259.
\end{aligned}
$$
This supports a narrow readback: the sequence-derived structure proxy is detectable as a $Q$-associated residual surface, but its incremental contribution to explaining protein abundance after measured TE and mRNA stability is not significant at the same strength.

\paragraph{Energy diagnostics.}
The residual energies and improvement energies agree with the same reading.  The protein-abundance residual energy is $1724.6664873071313$, the structure residual energy is $28554.997251412347$, the measured-TE residual energy is $645.3764418081507$, and the mRNA-stability residual energy is $871992.3819246667$.  The $Q$ block explains $468.33677333459536$ units of protein-abundance residual energy and $107.77365427073924$ units of structure residual energy.  The direct $Q$ improvement after conditioning on structure is $466.79849101702615$, while the direct $Q$ improvement after conditioning on measured TE and mRNA stability is $237.5550048307909$, and after conditioning on measured TE, mRNA stability, and structure it is $237.29705529470186$.  These numbers show that the structure proxy alone barely attenuates the $Q$-to-protein association, whereas the measured TE plus mRNA-stability block removes a much larger share of the direct residual association.

\paragraph{Relation to the broader translation-mediated abundance surface.}
The yeast row is consistent with the larger cross-organism $Q \to T \to P$ mediation matrix, where $T$ is modeled per-protein tAI and $P$ is log protein abundance.  In the Saccharomyces cerevisiae row of that matrix, the total $Q$-to-protein $R^2$ is $0.29837624994140827$, the direct $Q$-to-protein $R^2$ after conditioning on modeled tAI is $0.0151709093608293$, and the indirect component is $0.28320534058057895$, giving a mediated fraction $M^{QTP}=0.9491551041216973$.  The same larger matrix includes $12$ organisms and keeps every mediated fraction in the unit interval, with joined protein counts ranging from $1656$ in Sulfolobus solfataricus to $19395$ in Mus musculus.  This broader matrix does not make the yeast structure result causal; it only shows that the yeast abundance-residual surface is not isolated from the finite translation-mediated abundance pattern.

\paragraph{Local interpretation.}
The finite claim is therefore not that a synonymous residual coordinate creates folded structure, function, phenotype, or a universal biological rule.  The claim is that, in a $4759$-gene yeast join with fixed amino-acid, length, GC3, and M-density controls, the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual block has a strong residual association with matched protein abundance, this association is substantially attenuated by measured translation efficiency and mRNA stability, and the 5-prime CDS MFE proxy contributes a much smaller structure-associated residual partition.  The strongest safe summary is a controlled variance decomposition, not a pathway assertion.

\paragraph{Neighboring finite checks.}
Other finite checks constrain how far this row may be promoted.  A modeled-tAI survival matrix over only $3$ organisms has $81$ entries above the bounded contact threshold $0.01$ and maximum absolute entry $3801961.031607896$, but its own record states that $3$ organisms are insufficient for powered survival.  A separate structural-order axis run on $5$ folds identifies $f3\_stress$ as the dominant axis with weighted held-out $\Delta R^2=0.0031744502294756994$ and sign-flip $p=0.03125$.  An observable execution decomposition in Saccharomyces cerevisiae used $3289$ genes and reported an observable execution subblock.  In contrast, the stronger cross-ortholog selection-imprint test over $80$ orthogroups, $240$ rows, and $3$ species is negative for the strong imprint claim: $\Delta R^2=0.03250515015613468$, leave-clade-out $\Delta R^2=-0.0021477729853302424$, and null $95\%$ maximum $\Delta R^2=0.05427157066522958$.  These neighboring checks keep the yeast mediation row in a bounded statistical role.

\paragraph{Local boundary.}
No translation mechanism, folded-protein structure, physical admissibility, molecular function, organismal phenotype, evolutionary selection law, or universal biological principle follows from this chapter by itself.  A translation-efficiency measurement may support a readout-level conditioning variable only on the matched rows where it is present.  The 5-prime CDS MFE value is a sequence-derived proxy, not a measured folding trajectory or initiation-rate assay.  Protein abundance is a steady-state downstream readout and is not isolated from turnover, localization, post-translational modification, pathway context, or assay bias.  Any stronger layer requires its own matched contact and its own controlled statistic before it can be stated.
