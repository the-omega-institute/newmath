\subsection{Leave-one-codon locality in the standard-code cube}
\label{sec:bioreality-h1-leave-one-codon-out}

\paragraph{Claim under test.}
The record tested here is the leave-one-codon-out packet for the standard genetic code at the code-read layer. The carrier is the six-coordinate codon cube $Q_6$, read only as a finite display of codon labels. The contact is a curated standard-code table assigning each codon to an amino-acid or stop label. The claim is therefore deliberately narrow: window-six coordinates can expose a local boundary-tile pattern in the codon table, while the amino-acid labels themselves remain supplied by the curated biological table. The test does not assert that the coordinate geometry is a biological mechanism, a translation process, a fold predictor, a physical-admissibility criterion, a functional assay, or a universal law.

\paragraph{Finite residue set.}
The finite residue set $R$ has cardinality $13$. In codon notation it is
$$
\begin{aligned}
R=\{&\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\\
&\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UUA}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The same run verifies that this set has the punctured-cube face profile
$$
\begin{aligned}
(f_0,f_1,f_2)=(13,16,4).
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the object being tested is not an arbitrary biological narrative about codons, but a specific finite subcomplex inside the six-bit codon display. Its content is still code-layer content: codons are compared as finite coordinate points decorated by curated labels.

\paragraph{Spectral and shape checks.}
The spectral summary separates the residue-side and module-side scales. The reported values are
$$
\begin{aligned}
\lambda_M&=0.6752479308830213,\\
\lambda_R&=0.4758352843197002,\\
\mu&=3.051487585298128.
\end{aligned}
$$
The associated radial cubic residual is $-6.224354365258478\times 10^{-12}$, so the finite spectral computation is numerically coherent at the reported tolerance. The shape comparison against randomized samples used $N=2000$ samples with seed $42$. It found $1$ exact shape match and $25$ matches with Jaccard score at least $0.9$, giving
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{\mathrm{exact}}&=0.0005,\\
p_{\mathrm{Jaccard}\ge 0.9}&=0.0125.
\end{aligned}
$$
The sampling log also records $57377$ distinct-sample attempts and $55377$ duplicate retries, so the small exact-match probability should be read as evidence about this finite sampling protocol rather than as a distribution-free biological theorem.

\paragraph{Class-preserving null comparison.}
The amino-acid-class-preserving null run also used $N=2000$ and seed $42$. It reports observed module size $20$, exact-match count $0$, and $7$ samples in the lower tail at or below the observed module size. The corresponding values are
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{\mathrm{aa\ class\ exact}}&=0.0,\\
p_{\mathrm{aa\ class\ lower\ tail}}&=0.0035.
\end{aligned}
$$
This supports the local claim that the observed code-label pattern is unusual under the stated finite null model. It still does not license promotion from label geometry to translation, molecular structure, physical feasibility, or organismal function.

\paragraph{Leave-one-codon-out recovery.}
For the claim $\mathrm{h1.leave.one.codon.out}$, the finite run reports no anchor cases, five boundary cases, eight high-coverage closure cases, and eight predictable cases:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{anchor\ count}&=0,\\
\mathrm{boundary\ count}&=5,\\
\mathrm{closure\ coverage\ high}&=8,\\
\mathrm{predictable\ count}&=8.
\end{aligned}
$$
The recovery rate is $1.0$, and the above-threshold recovery indicator is also $1.0$. Within this packet, every recovered codon is therefore accounted for by the finite local rule as either predictable or boundary-supported. The word ``recovery'' here has only the finite table meaning: a held-out codon label is recovered from the configured code-layer neighborhood. It is not an experimental statement about ribosomal translation or protein realization.

\paragraph{Module-level accounting.}
The module classification assigns all $13$ residue codons with no missing or extra codons. The six modules are
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{agr}&=\{\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG}\},\\
\mathrm{ar\_lys}&=\{\mathrm{AAA}\},\\
\mathrm{cun}&=\{\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU}\},\\
\mathrm{ile\_met}&=\{\mathrm{AUA}\},\\
\mathrm{ssn}&=\{\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UUA}\},\\
\mathrm{stop\_trp}&=\{\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UGA}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The classification report gives $R$ size $13$, assigned count $13$, empty missing list, empty extra-module list, and empty unassigned list. It also records that every module has at least predictable or boundary status. This is the strongest finite form of the packet: the local boundary split covers the whole residue list without leaving an unclassified codon.

\paragraph{Related leave-one-code-out evidence.}
The broader leave-one-code-out comparison evaluated $23$ genetic-code tables and $63$ total targets. It reports baseline hit rate $0.19821739130434785$, hit rate in $M\setminus T$ equal to $0.9365079365079365$, and hit rate in $M\setminus T$ plus boundary equal to $0.9841269841269841$. The same $0.9841269841269841$ value is reported as the hit rate above baseline. By family, the above-baseline hit rates are $0.8333333333333334$ for alternative yeast and $1.0$ for bacterial plastid, candidate other, mitochondrial, and nuclear ciliate families. These figures place the codon-level packet in a wider table-level pattern, but they do not change the layer: the observed object remains codon-label readback against curated code tables.

\paragraph{Local conclusion and boundary.}
The ready conclusion is that the six-bit codon display contains a finite, testable local tile around the $13$-codon residue set, with a punctured-cube face profile, coherent spectral summary, significant finite-null comparisons, complete module accounting, and perfect reported leave-one-codon recovery under the stated rule. The conclusion is intentionally bounded. From this chapter alone one may claim a code-layer regularity in a curated standard-code table. One may not claim translation realization, folded structure, structural order, physical admissibility, biological function, mechanism, or universality. Any such stronger statement would require a separate contact whose observations live at that higher layer.
