\subsection{Leave-one-codon recovery in the window-six code display}

\paragraph{Finite object.}
The packet is a code-layer record for the standard genetic-code table in a six-coordinate codon display.  Each codon is treated as a finite word over the nucleotide alphabet and is read only through the curated codon-to-label table.  The construction tests whether the local coordinate pattern around a withheld codon is sufficient to recover its label-side placement class, while keeping the biological interpretation at the codon-label level.  In the finite row, the distinguished boundary set $R$ has cardinality $13$ and is exactly
$$
\begin{aligned}
R=\{&\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\\
&\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UUA}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The same row identifies $R$ as a punctured-cube pattern with face data $(13,16,4)$.  Thus the observed object is not merely a count of exceptional codons: it is a finite boundary tile with vertices, edges, and square faces already fixed by the window-six coordinate presentation.

\paragraph{Leave-one-codon result.}
For the codon-level holdout test, the row reports $0$ anchor cases, $5$ boundary cases, $8$ high-coverage closure cases, and $8$ predictable cases.  The recovery rate is $1.0$, and the recorded threshold comparison is also $1.0$.  The operative reading is therefore narrow but sharp: within this finite code table, every tested withheld codon in the reported class is recovered by the local closure-or-boundary diagnosis.  The result is a code-read fact about labels in the standard table, not a claim that the window geometry is itself a biochemical cause.

The row does not supply an elementwise list of $M$ codons, so the text uses only the reported size and probability data for $M$.

\paragraph{Code-table comparison.}
The broader leave-one-code-out row evaluates $23$ tables and $63$ total targets.  Its baseline hit rate is $0.19821739130434785$, while the hit rate above baseline is $0.9841269841269841$.  The two more localized rates are $0.9365079365079365$ inside $M-T$ and $0.9841269841269841$ inside $M-T$ plus boundary.  By family, the five reported groups have hit rates above baseline of $1.0$ for bacterial-plastid, mitochondrial, nuclear-ciliate, and candidate-other cases, and $0.8333333333333334$ for the alternative-yeast family.  These numbers support a finite code-table regularity: the boundary and closure classes remain useful when the table is perturbed by leave-one-code or leave-one-codon tests.

\paragraph{Reality contact.}
The only biological contact used here is the curated standard-code assignment from codons to amino-acid or stop labels.  That contact can test code-read statements and local label splits.  It cannot test whether translation is realized in a cell, whether a peptide folds, whether a molecule is physically admissible, whether a biological function is performed, or whether the pattern is a universal mechanism.  Consequently, the result should be read as follows: the finite six-coordinate display recovers the reported codon-label boundary structure with recovery rate $1.0$, and the supporting null comparisons make the shape non-generic under the recorded finite sampling regimes; no claim about translation, folding, physical admissibility, function, mechanism, or universality follows unless a separate empirical contact supplies that higher-layer observation.
