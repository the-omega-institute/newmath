\subsection{Leave-one-codon boundary recovery in the window-six codon cube}
\label{sec:h1-leave-one-codon-out}

\paragraph{Finite carrier and observed row.}
This record studies the standard-code codon table at the code-read layer. The finite carrier is the six-coordinate codon cube $Q_6$, where each codon is represented as a six-bit display over the two-bit nucleotide coordinates. The empirical contact used here is a curated standard genetic-code table: it assigns each codon to an amino-acid label or to a stop label. The contact is therefore a label table, not a translation apparatus, not a folding experiment, and not a physical or functional assay.

The highlighted residual set $R$ has cardinality $13$. The verified row gives
$$
\begin{aligned}
R = \{&AAA, AGA, AGG, AUA, CUA, CUC, CUG, CUU,\\
     &UAA, UAG, UCA, UGA, UUA\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The punctured-cube check returns the same set and the face-count vector
$$
\begin{aligned}
(f_0,f_1,f_2)=(13,16,4).
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the row is not merely a list of exceptional codons. It is a finite local complex inside $Q_6$, with thirteen vertices, sixteen one-dimensional adjacencies, and four square faces. This local geometry is the object on which the leave-one-codon test is evaluated.

\paragraph{Local leave-one-codon result.}
For the claim \texttt{h1.leave.one.codon.out}, the verified leave-one-codon row reports
$$
\begin{aligned}
\operatorname{anchor\_count} &= 0,\\
\operatorname{boundary\_count} &= 5,\\
\operatorname{predictable\_count} &= 8,\\
\operatorname{closure\_coverage\_high} &= 8,\\
\operatorname{recovery\_rate} &= 1.0.
\end{aligned}
$$
The finite interpretation is direct. Removing one codon from the local code-read row never produces an anchor recovery in this packet; all recoveries are accounted for by either the high-coverage closure class or by explicit boundary behavior. Eight codons are predictable by the closure-side test, and five are retained as boundary codons. Since $8+5=13$, every codon in $R$ is assigned to one of these two finite roles. The recovery rate is $1.0$, and the threshold comparison also reports $1.0$, so the row is complete for this local test.

\paragraph{Scope boundary.}
The contact is a codon-to-label table. Consequently, the chapter makes no claim of translation realization, folded structure, physical admissibility, biological function, mechanism, or universality. Amino-acid label readback is not a protein-structure observation. A CUN, UUR, stop, tryptophan, isoleucine, methionine, arginine, or lysine label comparison remains at the code layer unless a separate observation supplies translation, structure, physical, or functional data. The local tile in $Q_6$ is therefore a finite code-read pattern with a verified boundary partition and recovery rate, not a general law of biological realization.
