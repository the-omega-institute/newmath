\subsection{Leave-One-Codon-Out Local Boundary Test}
\label{sec:h1-leave-one-codon-out}

\paragraph{Object and coordinate level.}
This record concerns the standard-code codon table at the code-read layer.  The finite carrier is the six-coordinate codon cube $Q_6$, with codons displayed as six-bit window positions and then read against the curated standard genetic-code table.  The construction distinguishes a local tile face, a singleton label corner, and a three-point label class.  Its internal data are coordinate, closure, spectrum, and relation data; its empirical contact is only the codon-to-label table.  Thus the carrier may organize a local codon split, but the amino-acid or stop labels are supplied by the curated table rather than by the coordinate geometry alone.

\paragraph{Distinguished residual set.}
The residual codon set $R$ has cardinality $13$.  In lexicographic codon notation the verified list is
$$
\begin{aligned}
R=\{&AAA, AGA, AGG, AUA, CUA, CUC, CUG, CUU,\\
&UAA, UAG, UCA, UGA, UUA\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The same run verifies that this set is the punctured-cube residual with face-vector
$$
\begin{aligned}
(f_0,f_1,f_2)=(13,16,4).
\end{aligned}
$$
These numbers are finite code-layer facts: they say that the window-six residual has $13$ vertices, $16$ one-dimensional adjacencies, and $4$ two-dimensional tile faces in the tested combinatorial presentation.  They do not say that the codon table is forced by geometry as a biological necessity.

\paragraph{Spectral check.}
The spectral row reports three numerical quantities: $\lambda_M=0.6752479308830213$, $\lambda_R=0.4758352843197002$, and $\mu=3.051487585298128$.  The radial cubic evaluation is numerically zero to tolerance,
$$
\begin{aligned}
P_{\mathrm{rad}}(\lambda_M)=-6.224354365258478\cdot 10^{-12},
\end{aligned}
$$
and the recorded spectral radius in tolerance is again $0.6752479308830213$.  This supports a stable finite spectral signature for the residual tile.  The signature is a code-layer descriptor of the finite codon graph; it is not, by itself, a translation mechanism or a physical model of molecular realization.

\paragraph{Shape significance and amino-acid-class null.}
Against the sampled null model for the $M$-shape comparison, the experiment used $N=2000$ accepted samples with seed $42$.  It required $57377$ distinct-sample attempts and recorded $55377$ duplicate retries.  The exact shape matched once, giving
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{\mathrm{exact}}=0.0005,
\end{aligned}
$$
while the high-overlap condition $J\geq 0.9$ matched $25$ times, giving
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{J\geq 0.9}=0.0125.
\end{aligned}
$$
A second null model preserving amino-acid classes also used $N=2000$ and seed $42$.  Its observed $M$-size was $20$, its exact-match count was $0$, and the lower-tail count at or below the observed $M$-size was $7$.  Thus the recorded amino-acid-class-preserving probabilities are
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{\mathrm{aa,class,exact}}=0,
\qquad
p_{\mathrm{aa,class,lower}}=0.0035.
\end{aligned}
$$
These finite comparisons support non-random-looking local code geometry under the tested shuffles.  They still remain comparisons of codon-label data, not observations of folding, admissible chemistry, biological function, or universal evolutionary mechanism.

\paragraph{Leave-one-code and leave-one-codon performance.}
The code-level leave-one experiment evaluated $23$ tables and $63$ targets.  Its baseline hit rate was $0.19821739130434785$.  The recorded hit rate above baseline was $0.9841269841269841$, the hit rate inside $M-T$ was $0.9365079365079365$, and the hit rate inside $M-T$ plus boundary was $0.9841269841269841$.  The codon-level leave-one experiment attached to this chapter has no anchor recoveries and five boundary recoveries:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{anchor\ count}=0,
\qquad
\mathrm{boundary\ count}=5.
\end{aligned}
$$
It records $8$ high-coverage closure cases, $8$ predictable cases, and recovery rate
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{recovery\ rate}=1.0,
\qquad
\mathrm{threshold\ recovery}=1.0.
\end{aligned}
$$
The local interpretation is that the withheld codon is recovered by closure or boundary placement in all tested cases for this row.  Because the anchor count is zero, the row should be read as a boundary-and-closure result rather than as evidence for a privileged singleton biological source.

\paragraph{Family-level distribution.}
The related family split has $5$ families.  The hit rates above baseline are $0.8333333333333334$ for alternative yeast, $1.0$ for bacterial or plastid tables, $1.0$ for candidate other tables, $1.0$ for mitochondrial tables, and $1.0$ for nuclear ciliate tables.  This distribution is consistent with a robust code-read boundary pattern across the finite table families included in the row.  It does not authorize transferring the same statement to organisms, translation systems, protein structures, or functions not represented by a separate empirical contact.

\paragraph{Module classification.}
The module classification is complete on the $13$ residual codons: $R$ has size $13$, the assigned count is $13$, and the lists of extra module codons, missing residual codons, and unassigned codons are all empty.  The verified modules are
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{agr}&=\{AGA,AGG\},\\
\mathrm{ar\_lys}&=\{AAA\},\\
\mathrm{cun}&=\{CUA,CUC,CUG,CUU\},\\
\mathrm{ile\_met}&=\{AUA\},\\
\mathrm{ssn}&=\{UCA,UUA\},\\
\mathrm{stop\_trp}&=\{UAA,UAG,UGA\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
Each module has either predictable or boundary status in the finite classification.  The classification therefore closes the local bookkeeping problem for the residual set: every codon in $R$ is assigned to exactly one displayed module in the reported row, and no reported module contributes an extra codon outside $R$.

\paragraph{Reality contact and scope boundary.}
The only empirical contact used here is the curated standard genetic-code table, treated as a codon-label table.  It supports checking whether a codon receives an amino-acid or stop label and whether the local residual modules align with that code-layer readback.  It does not observe ribosomal translation, folded protein structure, molecular admissibility, biological function, evolutionary mechanism, or universality.  Consequently, the chapter claim is limited to this statement: window-six codon coordinates, together with the curated code labels, expose a finite local boundary-tile pattern with complete residual module assignment and perfect leave-one-codon recovery in the recorded row.  Any stronger biological statement requires an additional contact designed for that stronger layer.
