\paragraph{Scope of the claim.}
The conclusion is deliberately narrow.  The curated standard-code table supplies codon-label readback, and the window-six cube supplies a finite coordinate display in which the thirteen-codon residual tile can be tested.  From those ingredients, the packet supports the statement that local closure and boundary positions recover the leave-one-codon-out labels for this row.  Nothing in the row observes ribosomal translation, molecular realization, folded structure, physical admissibility, biological function, a mechanism of origin, or a universal biological law.  Any such promotion would require a separate contact of the appropriate kind.  In particular, an amino-acid label sequence is still only a label sequence; it is not a folded protein and it is not evidence of function by itself.
