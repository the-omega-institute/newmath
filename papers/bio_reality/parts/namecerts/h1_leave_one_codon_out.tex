\subsection{Leave-One-Codon-Out Codon Recovery at the Local Boundary}
\label{subsec:bioreality-h1-leave-one-codon-out}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite codon display.}
The object tested here is the standard-code codon table viewed through a six-bit codon-coordinate display. The finite carrier is the codon cube $Q_6$, with local faces, singleton label corners, and three-point label classes used only as code-layer distinctions. The biological contact is a curated standard-code table assigning each codon to an amino-acid or stop label. This contact is enough to test codon-label readback and local split behavior, but it is not a contact with translation dynamics, folded structure, physical admissibility, biological function, mechanism, or universality.

\paragraph{Observed residual set.}
The residual set $R$ has cardinality $13$. Its observed codons are
$$
\begin{aligned}
R=\{&AAA, AGA, AGG, AUA, CUA, CUC, CUG, CUU,\\
&UAA, UAG, UCA, UGA, UUA\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The punctured-cube check gives the same list, with face-vector data
$$
\begin{aligned}
(f_0,f_1,f_2)=(13,16,4).
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the tested finite row is not merely a count: it has an explicit thirteen-codon support and a local cubical boundary profile. The label table supplies the amino-acid or stop labels attached to these codons, while the coordinate calculation supplies the local shape in $Q_6$.

\paragraph{Spectral and shape checks.}
The spectral row reports $\lambda_M=0.6752479308830213$, $\lambda_R=0.4758352843197002$, and $\mu=3.051487585298128$. The associated radial polynomial residual is small at
$$
\begin{aligned}
-6.224354365258478\cdot 10^{-12},
\end{aligned}
$$
which is also the reported cubic-polynomial residual. Against $N=2000$ null samples with seed $42$, the exact-shape count is $1$, giving $p=0.0005$, while the high-Jaccard count is $25$, giving $p=0.0125$. The sampling log records $57377$ distinct-sample attempts and $55377$ duplicate retries. Under the amino-acid-class preserving null, the observed $M$ size is $20$, the exact match count is $0$, and the lower-tail count at or below the observed $M$ size is $7$ out of $2000$, giving lower-tail probability $0.0035$ and exact-shape probability $0.0$ in that finite run. These rows support a non-random local code-layer pattern relative to the stated null procedures, not a biological law beyond the codon-label table.

\paragraph{Leave-one-codon recovery.}
The leave-one-codon-out row is decisive for this claim at the code-read layer. It reports $0$ anchors, $5$ boundary cases, $8$ high-closure-coverage cases, and $8$ predictable cases, with recovery rate
$$
\begin{aligned}
\rho=1.0.
\end{aligned}
$$
The same row records recovery above threshold as $1.0$. In plain terms, every tested omitted codon in the finite residual support is recovered either through the predictable part of the local closure pattern or through the boundary part of the local tile. The result should be read as a finite local-recovery statement for codon labels: the codon table, after one codon in this residual family is withheld, still carries enough local coordinate and label information to place the withheld codon into the tested predictable-or-boundary alternatives.

\paragraph{Module accounting.}
The module classification is complete on $R$: $R$ has size $13$, the assigned count is $13$, and the lists of missing codons, extra module codons, and unassigned codons are all empty. The finite modules are
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{agr}&=\{AGA,AGG\},\\
\mathrm{ar\_lys}&=\{AAA\},\\
\mathrm{cun}&=\{CUA,CUC,CUG,CUU\},\\
\mathrm{ile\_met}&=\{AUA\},\\
\mathrm{ssn}&=\{UCA,UUA\},\\
\mathrm{stop\_trp}&=\{UAA,UAG,UGA\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
Each module has at least one predictable-or-boundary explanation in the recorded classification. This is important because the leave-one-codon result is not a loose aggregate statistic over thirteen unrelated points; it is compatible with the named module partition of the residual support.

\paragraph{Neighboring code-table evidence.}
The wider leave-one-code-out row evaluates $23$ tables and $63$ targets. It reports baseline hit rate $0.19821739130434785$, hit rate in $M\setminus T$ of $0.9365079365079365$, and hit rate in $M\setminus T$ plus boundary of $0.9841269841269841$. The above-baseline hit rate is also $0.9841269841269841$. By family, the five-family summary gives above-baseline rates $0.8333333333333334$ for alternative yeast and $1.0$ for bacterial plastid, candidate other, mitochondrial, and nuclear ciliate families. These figures do not replace the leave-one-codon-out row, but they show that the local residual geometry is not isolated from nearby finite code-table probes.

\paragraph{Local verdict.}
The claim is ready at the code-read layer. The finite data show that the thirteen-codon residual set has an explicit punctured-cube profile, a stable spectral row, significant shape behavior under the stated null models, complete module assignment, and perfect leave-one-codon recovery within the predictable-or-boundary alternatives. The curated standard-code table remains the only biological contact used here. Therefore the conclusion is deliberately narrow: window-six codon coordinates expose a local boundary-tile pattern for standard-code labels. No claim is made that this pattern realizes translation, determines protein folding, proves structural order, establishes physical admissibility, explains biological function, supplies a mechanism, or holds universally across biology without a separate contact aimed at that higher layer.
