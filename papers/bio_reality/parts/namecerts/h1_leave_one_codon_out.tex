\paragraph{Finite codon carrier.}
The record studies the standard-code codon table through a six-coordinate codon display, where each codon is treated as a point of the codon cube $Q_6$ and the biological readout is restricted to the curated codon-to-label table.  The finite row singled out by the construction is
$$
\begin{aligned}
R=\{&\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\\
&\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UUA}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The verified cardinality is $|R|=13$.  The same finite row is certified as a punctured-cube pattern with face data
$$
\begin{aligned}
f(R)=(13,16,4).
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the evidence is not a free-form biological story: it is a finite codon subset, a local face pattern, and a label readback against the standard genetic-code table.  The table supplies amino-acid or stop labels only; it does not supply translation dynamics, folded structure, physical realizability, cellular function, a mechanism, or universality.

\paragraph{Observed contact and limits of inference.}
The only external contact used here is the curated standard-code table assigning codons to amino-acid or stop labels.  That contact can test code readback and local codon-label splitting.  It cannot test translation realization, protein structure, physical admissibility, biological function, a mechanistic explanation, or a universal biological law.  Consequently, the scientifically allowed conclusion is narrow: window-six codon coordinates expose a finite local tile with strong recovery and null-model support at the code-read layer.  Any claim about folding, function, translation machinery, cross-organism necessity, or physical admissibility remains blocked until a different contact directly measures that phenomenon.
