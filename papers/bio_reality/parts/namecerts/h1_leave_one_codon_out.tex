\subsection{Leave-one-codon-out recovery at a local code boundary}\label{sec:bioreality-h1-leave-one-codon-out}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Record.}
This record concerns a window-six representation of the standard codon table, viewed as a finite codon cube with curated amino-acid or stop labels supplied by the standard-code table.  The claim is not that the cube geometry explains translation as a biological process.  The narrower claim is that, after withholding one codon-label row at a time, the local coordinate and closure data still identify a sharply bounded recovery region.  The contact used here is the curated standard-code label table: it permits codon-to-label readback and local split tests, but it does not contain observations of ribosomal realization, protein folding, energetic admissibility, organismal function, or a universal mechanism.

\paragraph{Finite carrier and residual set.}
The distinguished residual set $R$ has cardinality $13$.  In the verified row it is exactly
$$
\begin{aligned}
R=\{&\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\\
&\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UUA}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The same run identifies this set as a punctured-cube pattern with face-vector
$$
\begin{aligned}
(f_0,f_1,f_2)=(13,16,4).
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the row is not merely a list of exceptional codons; it carries a small finite cubical shape.  The shape is read only at the label-table level.  Its vertices are codon rows, its local faces are coordinate relations in the six-bit display, and its labels remain curated contacts rather than consequences of the display itself.

\paragraph{Spectral and null checks.}
The associated spectral row gives $\lambda_M=0.6752479308830213$, $\lambda_R=0.4758352843197002$, and $\mu=3.051487585298128$.  The radial cubic check is numerically closed at $-6.224354365258478\times 10^{-12}$, which is within the recorded tolerance for the spectral radius.  Against $N=2000$ sampled comparison shapes with seed $42$, the exact-shape count is $1$, giving $p_{\mathrm{exact}}=0.0005$; the count with Jaccard similarity at least $0.9$ is $25$, giving $p_{J\geq 0.9}=0.0125$.  The sampling log records $57377$ distinct-sample attempts and $55377$ duplicate retries, so the finite comparison is visibly constrained by the small combinatorial universe rather than by an unconstrained continuous null.

A second null row preserves amino-acid class information.  It reports observed $M$ size $20$, exact-match count $0$, and lower-tail count $7$ among $N=2000$ samples.  The resulting values are
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{\mathrm{aa\ class\ exact}}&=0.0,\\
p_{\mathrm{aa\ class\ lower\ tail}}&=0.0035.
\end{aligned}
$$
These values support a local shape signal in the code table, not a promotion to chemistry, folding, physiology, or evolutionary necessity.

\paragraph{Leave-one-code and leave-one-codon behavior.}
At the code-table level, the leave-one-code-out experiment evaluated $23$ tables and $63$ total targets.  The baseline hit rate was $0.19821739130434785$.  The recorded hit rate inside $M-T$ was $0.9365079365079365$, while the rate inside $M-T$ together with the boundary was $0.9841269841269841$.  The hit rate above baseline was also $0.9841269841269841$.  The family split is similarly concentrated: among $5$ families, the above-baseline rates were $0.8333333333333334$ for alternative yeast, and $1.0$ for bacterial-plastid, candidate-other, mitochondrial, and nuclear-ciliate rows.

For the leave-one-codon-out row itself, the finite result is stronger and more local.  The verified row reports anchor count $0$, boundary count $5$, closure coverage high $8$, predictable count $8$, and recovery rate $1.0$.  The threshold comparison also records recovery-rate-above-threshold $1.0$.  In this chapter this means the withheld codon rows are recovered by a combination of predictable closure cases and explicit boundary cases; no separate anchor class is needed in the recorded run.

\paragraph{Module accounting.}
The module classification exhausts $R$.  The six modules are
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{agr}&=\{\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG}\},\\
\mathrm{ar\_lys}&=\{\mathrm{AAA}\},\\
\mathrm{cun}&=\{\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU}\},\\
\mathrm{ile\_met}&=\{\mathrm{AUA}\},\\
\mathrm{ssn}&=\{\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UUA}\},\\
\mathrm{stop\_trp}&=\{\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UGA}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The accounting row gives $R$ size $13$, assigned count $13$, no missing module codons, no extra module codons, and no unassigned codons.  Hence every codon in the residual punctured cube is placed in a named finite module, and every module has either a predictable or a boundary status in the leave-one-codon-out test.

\paragraph{Local verdict.}
The finite data support the following restricted statement: in the standard-code label table, the window-six codon coordinates expose a small local boundary pattern whose leave-one-codon-out recovery rate is $1.0$ in the recorded run, with $8$ predictable cases and $5$ boundary cases across the $13$ residual codons.  The supporting rows also show that the residual set has a punctured-cube shape, that its spectral quantities pass the recorded polynomial check to numerical tolerance, and that two finite null comparisons place the observed shape in a low-probability region.

The same rows also impose the scope limit.  A local codon-tile split is not translation, is not folded protein structure, is not physical admissibility, is not biological function, is not a mechanism, and is not a universal law.  The curated table supplies labels; it does not supply ribosome dynamics, tRNA availability, organism-specific usage, three-dimensional protein structure, thermodynamic viability, or phenotype.  Any claim beyond code-layer readback requires a separate contact that directly observes that higher layer.
