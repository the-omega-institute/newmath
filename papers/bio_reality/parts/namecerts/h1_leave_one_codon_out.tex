\paragraph{Reality contact and scope.}
The only reality contact used here is a curated standard-code table assigning codons to amino-acid or stop labels.  That contact is sufficient for codon-label readback and for checking the local split of singleton label corners, three-point label classes, boundary codons, and module membership.  It is not a contact with translation dynamics, folded structure, physical admissibility, biological function, or a universal mechanism.  Consequently, the finite result is a code-layer statement: window-six coordinates expose a recoverable local boundary-tile pattern for the recorded standard-code labels.  No claim follows here about protein realization, three-dimensional fold, energetic feasibility, organism-wide necessity, or functional biology unless an additional experiment or observation supplies that higher-layer contact.
