\subsection{Leave-one-codon boundary recovery in the window-six codon cube}
\label{sec:h1-leave-one-codon-out}

\paragraph{Finite object under test.}
The object studied here is the standard-code codon table viewed through a six-bit codon display. The coordinate carrier is the codon cube $Q_6$, equipped only with finite distinctions: coordinate position, local closure, spectral scale, and adjacency relation. The biological contact is not a dynamical translation experiment. It is the curated table assigning each codon to an amino-acid label or to stop. Thus the admissible readout is code-layer label readback: which codons receive which labels, and how a local tile behaves when one codon is withheld.

The distinguished residual set $R$ has cardinality $13$ and is exactly
$$
\begin{aligned}
R=\{&AAA, AGA, AGG, AUA, CUA, CUC, CUG, CUU,\\
&UAA, UAG, UCA, UGA, UUA\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The punctured-cube computation gives the same $13$ codons and has face-count vector $(13,16,4)$. This is the finite geometry on which the leave-one-codon test is evaluated. It is not a claim that these codons generate a full biological mechanism; it is only the row set where the local code-read pattern is measured.

\paragraph{Leave-one-codon result.}
For claim $h1.leave.one.codon.out$, the finite run reports no anchor codons, five boundary codons, and eight codons with high closure coverage. The predictable count is $8$, and the recovery rate is $1.0$; the threshold comparison also reports $1.0$. In row form,
$$
\begin{aligned}
\text{anchors}&=0,\\
\text{boundary rows}&=5,\\
\text{high-closure rows}&=8,\\
\text{predictable rows}&=8,\\
\text{recovery rate}&=1.0.
\end{aligned}
$$
The interpretation is deliberately narrow. Removing a single codon from the local window-six table still leaves a complete recovery signal inside the measured boundary-or-closure classification. The result says that the withheld codon is recoverable at the code-label layer from the finite local pattern. It does not say that the organism realizes translation in this coordinate system, nor that a protein fold, thermodynamic admissibility, cellular function, or universal biological principle follows from the pattern.

\paragraph{Module accounting.}
The module classification is complete on $R$: it assigns all $13$ codons, leaves no codon unassigned, has no extra module codons, and has no missing rows. The six modules are
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{agr}&=\{AGA,AGG\},\\
\mathrm{ar\_lys}&=\{AAA\},\\
\mathrm{cun}&=\{CUA,CUC,CUG,CUU\},\\
\mathrm{ile\_met}&=\{AUA\},\\
\mathrm{ssn}&=\{UCA,UUA\},\\
\mathrm{stop\_trp}&=\{UAA,UAG,UGA\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
Every module has a predictable row or a boundary row. This is the main finite-row reason the leave-one-codon recovery is not merely a scattered list of hits: the coverage respects the local module decomposition of the residual cube. The singletons $AAA$ and $AUA$ are still accounted for, while the larger $CUN$ and stop/tryptophan neighborhoods remain within the same boundary-or-closure accounting.

\paragraph{Spectral scale and shape evidence.}
The same finite packet records a spectral separation with $\lambda_M=0.6752479308830213$, $\lambda_R=0.4758352843197002$, and $\mu=3.051487585298128$. The reported radial cubic residual is approximately $-6.224354365258478\times 10^{-12}$, within the numerical tolerance recorded by the run. These values do not create biological content by themselves, but they give a reproducible finite scale for the local codon-tile geometry.

The shape significance run used $N=2000$ sampled controls with seed $42$. It required $57377$ distinct-sample attempts and incurred $55377$ duplicate retries. The exact-shape match count was $1$, giving $p=0.0005$, while the high-Jaccard event $J\geq 0.9$ occurred $25$ times, giving $p=0.0125$. A separate amino-acid-class-preserving null run with $N=2000$ and seed $42$ found $0$ exact matches, observed $|M|=20$, and counted $7$ samples in the lower tail at or below the observed size. Its exact-shape value is $0.0$, and its lower-tail value is $0.0035$. These controls support the claim that the observed local shape is selective relative to the stated finite null models.

\paragraph{Relation to code-table variation.}
The leave-one-code-out study supplies an adjacent code-level check over $23$ evaluated tables and $63$ total targets. Its baseline hit rate is $0.19821739130434785$, while the rate above baseline is $0.9841269841269841$. Inside $M\setminus T$ the hit rate is $0.9365079365079365$, and inside $M\setminus T$ plus boundary it is $0.9841269841269841$. By family, the hit-rate-above-baseline values are $0.8333333333333334$ for alternative yeast, $1.0$ for bacterial/plastid, $1.0$ for candidate-other, $1.0$ for mitochondrial, and $1.0$ for nuclear-ciliate. These numbers are useful context for the codon-level result because they show that the local recovery is aligned with broader finite code-table variation rather than being an isolated singleton computation.

\paragraph{Boundary of the biological reading.}
The contact used in this chapter is the curated standard genetic-code table. It observes codon-to-label assignment and permits local tile checks. It does not observe ribosomal realization, folding, physical admissibility, cellular function, or a universal mechanism. Consequently, the finite recovery result remains at the code-read layer. A future claim about translation, structure, admissibility, function, or universality would need a separate empirical contact designed for that layer. Without such a contact, amino-acid label readback remains a label sequence and not a folded protein structure.
