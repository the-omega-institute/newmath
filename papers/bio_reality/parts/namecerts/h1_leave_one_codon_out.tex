\subsection{Leave-One-Codon Local Boundary Test}
\label{sec:h1-leave-one-codon-out}

\paragraph{Claim under test.}
This record studies a window-six encoding of the standard genetic-code table as a finite codon cube $Q_6$.  The claim is deliberately local: after one codon is withheld, the six-bit coordinate presentation may still place the missing row inside a small predictable region or on an explicit boundary of the tile.  The observed object is not translation itself, but the curated standard-code assignment of codons to amino-acid or stop labels.  The construction therefore tests label readback at the code layer, with no promotion to protein realization or biological mechanism.

\paragraph{Finite codon region.}
The distinguished region $R$ has cardinality $13$.  The verified codon list is
$$
\begin{aligned}
R=\{&\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\\
&\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UUA}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The same row reports that $R$ is a punctured-cube shaped subcomplex with face-vector
$$
\begin{aligned}
(f_0,f_1,f_2)=(13,16,4).
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the finite geometry being tested is not an arbitrary list of exceptional codons: it is a thirteen-vertex boundary-sensitive patch with sixteen one-dimensional incidences and four local square faces.  The codons divide into six named modules, and the assignment is complete: $R_{\mathrm{size}}=13$, $13$ assigned codons, no missing codons, no extra module codons, and no unassigned codons.  The modules are
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{agr}&=\{\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG}\},\\

\mathrm{cun}&=\{\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU}\},\\

$$
Every listed module contains a codon that is either predictable by the local closure rule or lies on the recorded boundary class.

\paragraph{Reality contact and interpretation.}
The only empirical contact used here is the curated standard-code table, understood as a codon-to-label table.  It supports statements about code labels such as amino-acid or stop assignments, and it can test whether a local codon-tile split agrees with that label table.  It does not observe ribosomal execution, molecular folding, energetic admissibility, organismal function, or universality across all biological contexts.  Consequently the chapter records a code-layer regularity: the window-six geometry exposes a stable local boundary pattern for the specified codon rows, while the labels themselves remain supplied by the curated biological table.

\paragraph{Local boundary.}
The permitted conclusion is that this finite codon patch has a strong leave-one-codon recovery signature under the stated coordinate, closure, spectrum, and relation data.  The prohibited conclusion is equally important: no translation event, folded structure, physical feasibility claim, functional effect, universal mechanism, or global biological law follows from these rows unless an additional contact directly measures that higher layer.  In particular, amino-acid label readback is still label readback; it is not a protein-structure observation.
