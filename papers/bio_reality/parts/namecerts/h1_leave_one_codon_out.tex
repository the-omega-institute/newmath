\subsection{Leave-One-Codon Recovery in the Window-Six Codon Cube}
\label{sec:h1-leave-one-codon-out}

\paragraph{Claim.}
The leave-one-codon-out packet tests whether the window-six codon coordinate system can recover held-out code-layer labels from finite local geometry. The carrier is the six-bit codon cube $Q_6$, where each codon is treated as a finite coordinate point and the readback is the curated standard-code table of codon labels. The claim is deliberately local: the row tests boundary-tile recovery inside a codon-label display. It does not assert translation realization, folded structure, physical admissibility, biological function, a molecular mechanism, or a universal law of biology.

\paragraph{Finite codon region.}
The distinguished row set $R$ has cardinality $13$. The verified codons are
$$
\begin{aligned}
R=\{&\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\\
&\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UUA}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The same run verifies that this region has punctured-cube combinatorics with face-vector
$$
\begin{aligned}
(f_0,f_1,f_2)=(13,16,4).
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the finite object under discussion is not an unconstrained sample of codons. It is a specific thirteen-codon boundary-bearing subcomplex of the six-bit display, with four local square faces and sixteen adjacency edges.

\paragraph{Spectral calibration.}
The associated spectral row gives the numerical scale of the local construction. The measured values are
$$
\begin{aligned}
\lambda_M&=0.6752479308830213,\\
\lambda_R&=0.4758352843197002,\\
\mu&=3.051487585298128.
\end{aligned}
$$
The radial cubic residual is $-6.224354365258478\cdot 10^{-12}$, also reported as the cubic-polynomial residual, so the spectral constraint is numerically closed to floating-point tolerance. This supplies a quantitative check that the local geometry is a stable finite coordinate object rather than a loose verbal partition of the codon table.

\paragraph{Shape significance.}
The row set is not treated as significant merely because it is visually suggestive. In the shape-significance experiment with seed $42$ and $N=2000$ samples, the exact shape appeared once, giving
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{\mathrm{exact}}&=0.0005.
\end{aligned}
$$
The high-overlap comparison reported $25$ samples with Jaccard score at least $0.9$, giving
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{J\ge 0.9}&=0.0125.
\end{aligned}
$$
The sampling process required $57377$ distinct-sample attempts and recorded $55377$ duplicate retries, so the row reports both the low exact-match frequency and the finite sampling pressure needed to obtain the comparison set. A second amino-acid-class-preserving null model, also with $N=2000$ and seed $42$, observed $0$ exact shape matches and $7$ lower-tail samples at or below the observed $M$ size $20$. Its reported values are
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{\mathrm{aa\ class\ exact}}&=0.0,\\
p_{\mathrm{aa\ class\ lower\ tail}}&=0.0035.
\end{aligned}
$$
These numbers support a code-layer boundary signal, not a claim that the codon system is generated by the coordinate model.

\paragraph{Leave-one-codon recovery.}
The direct leave-one-codon-out row reports $8$ predictable held-out cases, $5$ boundary cases, and $0$ anchor cases. The high-coverage closure count is $8$. The recovery rate is
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{recovery\ rate}=1.0,
\end{aligned}
$$
and the thresholded recovery indicator is also $1.0$. The appropriate reading is that every held-out case in this finite test is either recovered by the local closure criterion or lands on an explicitly marked boundary. Since the anchor count is zero, the test is not succeeding by appealing to fixed privileged codons; it is operating through the finite neighborhood and boundary organization of the row.

\paragraph{Code-table comparison.}
The companion leave-one-code-out experiment evaluated $23$ code tables and $63$ total targets. The baseline hit rate was $0.19821739130434785$. The hit rate above that baseline was $0.9841269841269841$, the hit rate inside $M\setminus T$ was $0.9365079365079365$, and the hit rate inside $M\setminus T$ plus boundary was $0.9841269841269841$. By family, the finite rows record $5$ families: alternative yeast has hit rate $0.8333333333333334$, while bacterial plastid, candidate other, mitochondrial, and nuclear ciliate each have hit rate $1.0$ above baseline. These family numbers place the codon-level recovery row inside a wider code-label stability pattern, while preserving the same scope restriction: the contact is the curated codon-to-label table, not an observation of translation dynamics or protein behavior.

\paragraph{Module accounting.}
The module classification is complete on the same thirteen-codon region. The assigned count is $13$, the reported $R$ size is $13$, and both the extra-module and missing-module lists are empty. The finite modules are
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{agr}&=\{\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG}\},\\
\mathrm{ar\_lys}&=\{\mathrm{AAA}\},\\
\mathrm{cun}&=\{\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU}\},\\
\mathrm{ile\_met}&=\{\mathrm{AUA}\},\\
\mathrm{ssn}&=\{\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UUA}\},\\
\mathrm{stop\_trp}&=\{\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UGA}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
Every listed module has at least one predictable or boundary codon in the reported classification. This is the strongest local form of the claim: the finite row is fully assigned, the local modules exhaust $R$, and the leave-one-codon test recovers or boundary-marks the held-out behavior.

\paragraph{Reality contact and boundary.}
The only reality contact used here is the curated standard-code table assigning codons to amino-acid or stop labels. That contact can test code readback and local label splits. It cannot test whether translation is physically realized, whether an amino-acid sequence folds, whether a structure is admissible, whether a biological function exists, or whether the observed local geometry is a universal mechanism. Amino-acid label readback is therefore kept at the code layer. Any statement about folded structure, physical admissibility, function, organism-specific translation machinery, or broad biological necessity would require a separate empirical contact designed for that higher layer.
