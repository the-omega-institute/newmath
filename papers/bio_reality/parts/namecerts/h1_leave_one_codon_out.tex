\paragraph{Finite carrier and observed label contact.}

The carrier is the finite codon display induced by three nucleotide positions, each read through a two-bit alphabetic coordinate.  The biological contact used here is a curated standard genetic-code table assigning each codon to an amino-acid or stop label.  This contact is sufficient for codon-label readback and for local comparisons among labels, but it is not a contact with translation kinetics, folded molecular shape, physical admissibility, cellular function, or a universal biological mechanism.

\paragraph{Local conclusion and scope limit.}
The finite evidence supports the following chapter-level reading.  In the six-bit codon cube, the thirteen-codon punctured region $R$ has a concrete cubical boundary profile, a stable numerical spectral summary, significant shape behavior under the reported null comparisons, and complete leave-one-codon recovery by predictable or boundary classes.  The standard-code table supplies the label contact needed to test these facts at the code-read layer.

No stronger biological conclusion is licensed here. Amino-acid label sequences are labels in a code table unless a separate experimental or curated contact supplies the higher-layer observation.  The safe conclusion is therefore local: window-six codon coordinates expose a finite boundary-tile regularity in standard-code label readback, while all claims about mechanism, folding, admissibility, function, and universal biological law remain outside this chapter.
