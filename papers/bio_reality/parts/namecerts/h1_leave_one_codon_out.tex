\paragraph{Local boundary.}
The conclusion is deliberately narrow.  Window-six codon coordinates expose a local tile structure around the standard-code labels, and the leave-one-codon test closes the thirteen-codon exceptional row by predictable or boundary status with recovery rate $1.0$.  The contact used here is a curated standard genetic-code table, so it observes codon-to-amino-acid or codon-to-stop labels.  It does not observe ribosomal realization, folded protein shape, thermodynamic or kinetic admissibility, cellular function, a molecular mechanism, or a universal biological law.  Any statement about translation as a process, protein folding, structural order, physical viability, function, or universality would require a separate empirical contact of the appropriate kind.  The present chapter therefore records a finite code-layer success and stops at that layer.
