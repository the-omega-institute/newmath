\subsection{Leave-One-Codon-Out Recovery in the Window-Six Codon Cube}
\label{sec:bioreality-h1-leave-one-codon-out}

\paragraph{Finite object.}
The record considered here is the standard-code codon table read through a window-six coordinate system.  The carrier is the codon cube $Q_6$, where each codon is displayed as a six-bit coordinate and is then paired with its curated amino-acid or stop label.  The internal question is local: after removing one codon from the distinguished boundary region, does the remaining coordinate-and-label information recover the missing codon as predictable, or at least place it on an explicitly marked boundary?  The claim is not that the coordinate geometry generates the genetic code.  The curated label table is the contact with biological reality, while the cube, local faces, singleton corners, three-point label classes, closure tests, and spectra are finite probes of that contact.

\paragraph{Distinguished region.}
The finite region $R$ has cardinality $13$.  In the verified row it is exactly
$$
\begin{aligned}
R=\{&\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\\
&\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UUA}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The same row identifies $R$ as a punctured-cube shape with face vector
$$
\begin{aligned}
(f_0,f_1,f_2)=(13,16,4).
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the combinatorial datum is not merely a list of exceptional codons; it carries a small two-dimensional face structure inside $Q_6$.  The row also records a companion set $M$ through its measured size and shape statistics: the observed $M$ size is $20$, and the available finite row does not include an ordered codon list for $M$.  The claim therefore uses $M$ only through the supplied numerical and recovery probes, not through an unstated membership table.

\paragraph{Leave-one-codon-out result.}
For the claim $\mathrm{h1.leave.one.codon.out}$, the experiment run $\mathrm{e9e1a6e41633}$ records $8$ predictable removals, $5$ boundary removals, and $0$ anchor removals.  The high-coverage closure count is $8$, and the recovery rate is
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{recovery\ rate}=1.0,
\qquad
\mathrm{recovery\ rate\ above\ threshold}=1.0.
\end{aligned}
$$
Since $|R|=13$, the numerical partition is consistent with $8+5+0=13$.  In this finite test, every removed codon in the region is either recovered by the local closure rule or remains on a declared boundary of the local tile.  The result is therefore a complete local accounting of the thirteen listed codons, not a partial sampling statement.

\paragraph{Spectral and null-model checks.}
The spectral row records $\lambda_M=0.6752479308830213$, $\lambda_R=0.4758352843197002$, and $\mu=3.051487585298128$.  The radial cubic residual is numerically small,
$$
\begin{aligned}

$$
which is the same recorded value for the cubic-polynomial check.  The shape test for $M$ used $N=2000$ samples with $57377$ distinct-sample attempts and $55377$ duplicate retries.  It found $1$ exact shape match, giving $p=0.0005$, and $25$ matches with Jaccard score at least $0.9$, giving $p=0.0125$.  Under the amino-acid-class-preserving null model, again with $N=2000$, the observed $M$ size was $20$, the exact-match count was $0$, and the lower-tail count at or below the observed size was $7$.  The corresponding values were
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{\mathrm{exact}}=0.0,
\qquad
p_{\mathrm{lower\ tail}}=0.0035.
\end{aligned}
$$
These checks support the view that the observed coordinate pattern is not a routine draw from the stated finite null ensembles.  They do not identify a biochemical mechanism.

\paragraph{Reality contact and scope.}
The only biological contact used here is the curated standard-code table, which assigns codons to amino-acid or stop labels.  This contact tests code-layer readback and local label splitting.  It does not observe translation dynamics, folded protein geometry, energetic admissibility, cellular function, a universal biological mechanism, or cross-organism necessity.  Therefore the admissible conclusion is deliberately narrow: the window-six coordinate display exposes a finite local boundary-tile organization in the standard-code label table, with complete leave-one-codon-out coverage for the thirteen-codon region $R$.  Any statement about protein realization, folding, physical viability, biological function, mechanism, or universality would require a separate empirical contact designed for that higher layer.
