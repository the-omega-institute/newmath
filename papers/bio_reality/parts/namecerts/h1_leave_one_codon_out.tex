\paragraph{Claim scope.}
The record \texttt{h1.leave.one.codon.out} concerns a finite recovery test on the standard-code codon table after a single codon label is withheld.  The carrier is the six-coordinate codon cube $Q_6$, where each codon is read as a six-bit display and local faces are used to test whether the missing label lies in a predictable local region or on an explicitly marked boundary.  The biological contact is only the curated standard genetic-code table: it supplies codon-to-amino-acid or codon-to-stop labels, and it does not by itself supply a translation process, a folded structure, a physical admissibility test, a function assay, a mechanism, or a universal biological law.
