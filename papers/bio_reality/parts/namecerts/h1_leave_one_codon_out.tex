\subsection{Leave-One-Codon-Out Boundary Recovery in the Window-Six Codon Cube}
\label{sec:h1-leave-one-codon-out}

\paragraph{Finite carrier and readout.}
This record studies the standard-code codon table through the six-coordinate codon cube $Q_6$, where each codon is represented by a six-bit display and the observable readout is the curated codon-to-label table.  The claim is deliberately local: window-six coordinates may expose a boundary-tile pattern for missing codon labels, but the labels themselves remain supplied by the curated standard-code contact.  The object being tested is therefore the code-read layer, not translation dynamics, molecular folding, physical admissibility, biological function, mechanism, or universality.

\paragraph{Distinguished codon row.}
The finite distinguished row $R$ has cardinality $13$ and is exactly
$$
\begin{aligned}
R=\{&AAA, AGA, AGG, AUA, CUA, CUC, CUG, CUU,\\
&UAA, UAG, UCA, UGA, UUA\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The punctured-cube computation returns the same set and has face-vector
$$
\begin{aligned}
(f_0,f_1,f_2)=(13,16,4).
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the row is not merely a list of exceptional codons; it carries a small cubical boundary shape with $13$ vertices, $16$ edges, and $4$ two-faces inside the window-six display.  This is the geometric datum used by the leave-one-codon-out probe.

\paragraph{Spectral and shape controls.}
The associated spectrum separates the row from a generic random artifact.  The finite calculation gives
$$
\begin{aligned}
\lambda_M&=0.6752479308830213,\\
\lambda_R&=0.4758352843197002,\\
\mu&=3.051487585298128,
\end{aligned}
$$
with the cubic residual reported as $-6.224354365258478\times 10^{-12}$, also recorded as the radial polynomial residual.  In the shape randomization test with $N=2000$, there was $1$ exact shape match, so $p_{\mathrm{exact}}=0.0005$.  There were $25$ samples with Jaccard score at least $0.9$, so $p_{\mathrm{Jaccard}\geq 0.9}=0.0125$.  The run required $57377$ distinct-sample attempts and $55377$ duplicate retries, which is relevant because the finite codon universe makes naive resampling collision-heavy.

\paragraph{Amino-acid-class-preserving null.}
A second null model preserves amino-acid class constraints rather than sampling only by raw shape.  In that model, $N=2000$, the observed $M$ size is $20$, the exact-match count is $0$, and the lower-tail count at or below the observed $M$ size is $7$.  The corresponding values are
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{\mathrm{aa\ class\ exact}}&=0.0,\\
p_{\mathrm{aa\ class\ lower\ tail}}&=0.0035.
\end{aligned}
$$
This supports the narrower claim that the displayed local structure is nontrivial under two finite controls.  It does not turn the tile into a law of biology; it only says that the codon-label table contains a small recoverable pattern at this coordinate scale.

\paragraph{Leave-one-codon-out result.}
For the claim identified as $h1.leave.one.codon.out$, the leave-one-codon-out run reports $0$ anchors, $5$ boundary cases, $8$ high-coverage closure cases, and $8$ predictable cases.  The recovery rate is
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{recovery\ rate}=1.0,
\end{aligned}
$$
and the threshold comparison also reports $1.0$.  The absence of anchors matters: the recovery is not being credited to a trivial singleton label corner already fixed in place.  The successful cases are instead accounted for by closure coverage and boundary placement inside the finite codon tile.

\paragraph{Relation to code-level leave-one-out tests.}
The broader leave-one-code-out row gives a baseline hit rate of $0.19821739130434785$ over $23$ evaluated tables and $63$ targets.  Against that baseline, the hit rate above baseline is $0.9841269841269841$, the hit rate inside $M-T$ is $0.9365079365079365$, and the rate inside $M-T$ together with the boundary is $0.9841269841269841$.  Family stratification gives $5$ families: alternative yeast has hit rate $0.8333333333333334$, while bacterial-plastid, candidate-other, mitochondrial, and nuclear-ciliate each have hit rate $1.0$ above baseline.  These rows are used only as neighboring finite evidence that the codon-level behavior sits inside a larger code-read pattern.

\paragraph{Module classification.}
The $13$ codons in $R$ are fully assigned by the module classification.  The modules are
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{agr}&=\{AGA,AGG\},\\
\mathrm{ar\_lys}&=\{AAA\},\\
\mathrm{cun}&=\{CUA,CUC,CUG,CUU\},\\
\mathrm{ile\_met}&=\{AUA\},\\
\mathrm{ssn}&=\{UCA,UUA\},\\
\mathrm{stop\_trp}&=\{UAA,UAG,UGA\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The classification reports $R\_size=13$, $assigned\_count=13$, no extra module codons, no codons missing from the modules, and no unassigned codons.  Every listed module has either predictable or boundary status.  This is the strongest finite-row form of the claim: the local codon set is completely covered by named modules, and the leave-one-codon-out recovery does not leave a residue outside the classification.

\paragraph{Reality contact and boundary.}
The only contact used here is the curated standard-code table, which observes that codons are assigned to amino-acid or stop labels.  That contact can test code-read and local codon-tile label splits.  It cannot test translation realization, protein structure, physical admissibility, or biological function.  Consequently, amino-acid label readback is not evidence of folded structure, CUN/UUR wording is not structure evidence, and tRNA-related notation would need its own organism-matched contact before it could support a stronger biological claim.  The local pattern is aligned with the code table, but all higher-layer readings remain blocked until a separate observation supplies them.

\paragraph{Verdict.}
The finite data support a ready code-layer chapter: $R$ is a $13$-codon punctured-cube row with face-vector $(13,16,4)$; the spectral row has stable numerical witnesses; two null models give small tail probabilities; the leave-one-codon-out probe recovers all predictable or boundary cases; and the module classification assigns all $13$ codons without leftovers.  The scientific content is therefore a local codon-label boundary recovery statement in $Q_6$, not a claim about mechanism, folding, admissibility, function, translation, or universality.
