\subsection{Leave-One-Codon-Out Boundary Recovery in the Standard Code}\label{sec:h1-leave-one-codon-out}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Question.}
The claim tested here is a code-layer claim about the standard genetic-code table.  Codons are placed in a six-coordinate codon cube $Q_6$, and the curated standard-code table supplies the labels used for readback.  The test asks whether removing one codon at a time from the local codon-tile region leaves enough finite coordinate evidence to recover the omitted codon as a predictable boundary member.  The biological object is therefore not translation as a biochemical process, but the finite table assigning codons to amino-acid or stop labels.

\paragraph{Finite region.}
The distinguished region $R$ has cardinality $13$.  Its observed codons are
$$
\begin{aligned}
R = \{&AAA, AGA, AGG, AUA, CUA, CUC, CUG, CUU,\\
     &UAA, UAG, UCA, UGA, UUA\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The same finite row records that $R$ is the punctured-cube region with face data
$$
\begin{aligned}
(f_0,f_1,f_2)=(13,16,4).
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the object under inspection is a small local tile with thirteen vertices, sixteen one-dimensional incidences, and four two-dimensional faces.  The row does not make this tile a law of biology; it only fixes the coordinate shape whose label readback is compared against the curated table.

\paragraph{Spectral coordinate check.}
The spectral row gives three numerical values for the local configuration:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\lambda_M &= 0.6752479308830213,\\
\lambda_R &= 0.4758352843197002,\\
\mu &= 3.051487585298128.
\end{aligned}
$$
The residual reported for the associated cubic relation is $-6.224354365258478\cdot 10^{-12}$, equal to the radial residual listed in the same row up to the displayed precision.  This is treated as a finite numerical consistency check on the coordinate packet, not as an independent biological mechanism.

\paragraph{Shape significance.}
The shape comparison used $N=2000$ sampled alternatives with seed $42$.  The sampler made $57377$ distinct-sample attempts and $55377$ duplicate retries.  It found $1$ exact shape match and $25$ matches with Jaccard score at least $0.9$, giving
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{\mathrm{exact}} &= 0.0005,\\
p_{\mathrm{Jaccard}\ge 0.9} &= 0.0125.
\end{aligned}
$$
A label-class-preserving null test, also with $N=2000$ and seed $42$, reported observed $M$-size $20$, exact-match count $0$, and $7$ lower-tail samples at or below the observed $M$-size.  Its finite probabilities were
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{\mathrm{class\ exact}} &= 0.0,\\
p_{\mathrm{class\ lower\ tail}} &= 0.0035.
\end{aligned}
$$
These values support the claim that the displayed coordinate pattern is not a routine draw from the tested finite null models.  They do not identify a biochemical cause.

\paragraph{Leave-one-codon-out result.}
The direct leave-one-codon-out row records $0$ anchors, $5$ boundary cases, $8$ high-coverage closure cases, and $8$ predictable cases.  The recovery rate is
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{recovery\ rate}=1.0,
\end{aligned}
$$
with the threshold comparison also equal to $1.0$.  In this chapter the word recovery means that the omitted codon is recovered at the code-label and local-coordinate level.  It does not mean that translation, folding, physical admissibility, function, a universal mechanism, or organism-independent necessity has been recovered.

\paragraph{Module classification.}
The module row partitions all thirteen codons of $R$ with no missing, extra, or unassigned codons.  It reports $R$-size $13$ and assigned count $13$.  The modules are
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{agr} &= \{AGA, AGG\},\\
\mathrm{ar\_lys} &= \{AAA\},\\
\mathrm{cun} &= \{CUA, CUC, CUG, CUU\},\\
\mathrm{ile\_met} &= \{AUA\},\\
\mathrm{ssn} &= \{UCA, UUA\},\\
\mathrm{stop\_trp} &= \{UAA, UAG, UGA\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
Every listed module has either predictable or boundary behavior in the leave-one-codon-out packet.  The singleton module $\mathrm{ar\_lys}=\{AAA\}$ is the singleton label-corner case.  The three-codon module $\mathrm{stop\_trp}=\{UAA,UAG,UGA\}$ is the three-point label-class case.  The four-codon $\mathrm{cun}$ block supplies the local face-like codon family inside the same thirteen-codon region.

\paragraph{Relation to leave-one-code-out evidence.}
The broader leave-one-code-out row evaluated $23$ tables and $63$ total targets.  Its baseline hit rate was $0.19821739130434785$, while the hit rate above baseline was $0.9841269841269841$.  The hit rate inside $M-T$ was $0.9365079365079365$, and the hit rate inside $M-T$ plus boundary was $0.9841269841269841$.  By family, the hit rates above baseline were $1.0$ for bacterial-plastid, mitochondrial, nuclear-ciliate, and candidate-other families, and $0.8333333333333334$ for alternative yeast, across $5$ families.  These companion rows explain why the codon-level recovery is read as a local table phenomenon rather than as an isolated numerical accident.

\paragraph{Reality contact.}
The sole biological contact used here is the curated standard-code table: it says which codons carry which amino-acid or stop labels.  That contact is sufficient for code-readback and for checking whether a local split in codon-label space is present.  It is not a contact with ribosomal realization, protein structure, energetic admissibility, phenotype, molecular function, or organism-level universality.  Any claim at one of those higher layers would require a separate observation with its own resolution and failure conditions.

\paragraph{Verdict.}
The finite evidence supports the following restrained statement.  In the six-coordinate codon display of the curated standard code, the thirteen-codon region $R$ has a punctured-cube shape, stable numerical spectrum, statistically non-routine finite shape profile under the recorded nulls, complete module assignment, and perfect leave-one-codon-out recovery at the code-label boundary level.  The claim is therefore ready as a local codon-table result.  Its scope stops exactly at code-layer readback: no translation process, folded structure, physical possibility, biological function, causal mechanism, or universal law follows from these rows alone.
