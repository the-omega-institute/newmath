\subsection{Leave-One-Codon Local Tile Boundary}
\label{subsec:h1-leave-one-codon-out}

\paragraph{Claim scope.}
This record studies the claim identified by \texttt{h1.leave.one.codon.out}.  The object under inspection is the standard-code codon table after it is placed on a six-bit codon display.  The relevant carrier is the codon cube $Q_6$, with coordinates supplied by the finite codon representation and with labels supplied by the curated standard-code assignment of codons to amino-acid or stop labels.  The claim is deliberately local: window-six codon coordinates can expose a tile-boundary pattern for the code-read layer, but the amino-acid and stop labels are contacts with the curated table, not consequences of the coordinate system alone.

\paragraph{Finite residue row.}
The finite residue set $R$ has cardinality $13$.  In the verified row it is exactly
$$
\begin{aligned}
R=\{&\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\\
&\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UUA}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The same finite computation records $R$ as a punctured-cube pattern with face data
$$
\begin{aligned}
(f_0,f_1,f_2)=(13,16,4).
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the row does not merely report a list of exceptional codons; it reports a finite cubical trace with thirteen vertices, sixteen one-dimensional adjacencies, and four two-dimensional faces in the induced local complex.

\paragraph{Spectral row.}

The calculation is not a physical energy model, a translation model, or a folding model; it is a coordinate-and-spectrum report about the code-label table.

\paragraph{Shape significance row.}
Against the reported randomization row with $N=2000$ samples, the exact shape appeared once, giving
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{\mathrm{exact}}=0.0005.
\end{aligned}
$$
High-overlap matches at Jaccard threshold $0.9$ appeared $25$ times, giving
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{\mathrm{Jaccard}\ge 0.9}=0.0125.
\end{aligned}
$$
The row also records $57377$ distinct-sample attempts and $55377$ duplicate retries under seed $42$.  A second null row preserving amino-acid classes evaluated $2000$ samples, found $0$ exact shape matches, recorded lower-tail count $7$ at observed $M$-size $20$, and assigned
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{\mathrm{class\ exact}}&=0.0,\\
p_{\mathrm{class\ lower\ tail}}&=0.0035.
\end{aligned}
$$
These figures support a code-level shape anomaly in the finite row.  They do not by themselves identify a biochemical mechanism.

\paragraph{Leave-one rows.}

The hit rate inside $M-T$ was $0.9365079365079365$, and the hit rate inside $M-T$ plus the boundary was again $0.9841269841269841$.  The codon-level leave-one row gives the sharper local summary
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{anchor\ count}&=0,\\
\mathrm{boundary\ count}&=5,\\
\mathrm{closure\ coverage\ high}&=8,\\
\mathrm{predictable\ count}&=8,\\
\mathrm{recovery\ rate}&=1.0.
\end{aligned}
$$
The reported recovery-rate-above-threshold value is also $1.0$.  Since the anchor count is zero, the row should be read as a boundary-and-closure phenomenon rather than as a collection of fixed label anchors.

The family-level leave-one row separates five families.  The hit-rate-above-baseline values are $0.8333333333333334$ for alternative yeast, and $1.0$ for bacterial-plastid, candidate-other, mitochondrial, and nuclear-ciliate families.  The module classification accounts for all thirteen residue codons with no extras, no missing module codons, and no unassigned codons.  The finite modules are
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{agr}&=\{\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG}\},\\
\mathrm{ar\_lys}&=\{\mathrm{AAA}\},\\

The equality between assigned count and $R$-size, both $13$, is the finite closure check for the module partition used here.

\paragraph{Reality contact and limit of interpretation.}
The only biological contact used in this row is the curated standard genetic-code table.  It supports codon-to-label readback and local label-split tests at the code-read layer.  It does not observe ribosomal translation, folded structure, structural order, physical admissibility, biological function, a universal biological law, or a general mechanism for all organisms.  Any promotion from this local codon-tile pattern to translation, folding, physical chemistry, function, mechanism, or universality would require a separate contact that measures that higher layer directly.  The present record therefore says that the finite six-bit display and the curated labels align on a local boundary tile; it does not say that the geometry causes the biology.
