\subsection{Leave-one-codon-out recovery in the window-six codon cube}
\label{sec:bioreality-h1-leave-one-codon-out}

\paragraph{Question.}
This record tests whether the window-six display of the standard genetic code has enough local organization to recover omitted codon labels inside the exceptional codon set, while keeping the interpretation at the code-reading layer. The carrier is the six-bit codon cube $Q_6$, where each codon is read as a finite coordinate word and the curated standard-code table supplies the amino-acid or stop label. The relevant distinction is not a biological mechanism. It is the finite split between codons whose local neighborhood gives a stable readback signal and codons that sit on a boundary where the table must still be consulted.

\paragraph{Finite exceptional set.}
The row for $R$ records exactly thirteen codons:
$$
\begin{aligned}
R = \{&\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\\
&\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UUA}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The cardinality check gives $|R|=13$. The punctured-cube check gives the same thirteen codons and records the face data $f=(13,16,4)$, so this row is not merely a list of labels; it also carries a finite local shape inside the codon cube. The shape row therefore supplies a bounded combinatorial object against which a leave-one-codon-out test can be stated without appealing to translation dynamics or protein structure.

\paragraph{Spectral scale.}
The associated spectral row gives
$$
\begin{aligned}
\lambda_M &= 0.6752479308830213,\\
\lambda_R &= 0.4758352843197002,\\
\mu &= 3.051487585298128.
\end{aligned}
$$
The recorded radial polynomial residual is $-6.224354365258478\times 10^{-12}$, the same value reported for the cubic-polynomial check. At the numerical precision of the run, this places the $R$-shape inside a stable finite spectral calculation rather than a hand-selected visual pattern. The spectrum does not identify an amino acid, and it does not claim a biochemical cause. It only fixes a reproducible coordinate signature for the local codon region under study.

\paragraph{Leave-one-codon-out result.}
For the claim $\mathrm{h1.leave.one.codon.out}$, the finite row reports $0$ anchors, $5$ boundary codons, $8$ predictable codons, and $8$ high-coverage closure cases. The recovery rate is
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{recovery\ rate} = 1.0,
\end{aligned}
$$
and the threshold comparison also records $1.0$. Thus every codon counted as predictable is recovered by the local closure criterion used in the run, while five codons remain explicitly marked as boundary cases. The absence of anchors is important: the run is not saying that fixed singleton anchors determine the exceptional set. It is saying that the local tile separates recoverable codons from boundary codons inside a finite region of the code table.

\paragraph{Module accounting.}
The module-classification row assigns all thirteen codons in $R$ and reports no extras, no missing codons, and no unassigned codons. The six modules are
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{agr} &= \{\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG}\},\\
\mathrm{ar\_lys} &= \{\mathrm{AAA}\},\\
\mathrm{cun} &= \{\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU}\},\\
\mathrm{ile\_met} &= \{\mathrm{AUA}\},\\
\mathrm{ssn} &= \{\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UUA}\},\\
\mathrm{stop\_trp} &= \{\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UGA}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The same row records $R\_\mathrm{size}=13$ and $\mathrm{assigned\_count}=13$. It also records that every module has either a predictable codon or a boundary codon. This is the finite reason the leave-one-codon-out result is module-complete: the local split covers the whole exceptional region even though it does not make every codon interior-predictable.

\paragraph{Code-table comparison.}
The related leave-one-code-out row evaluates $23$ code tables and $63$ targets. It records a baseline hit rate of $0.19821739130434785$, a hit rate in $M-T$ of $0.9365079365079365$, and a hit rate in $M-T$ plus boundary of $0.9841269841269841$. The hit rate above baseline is also $0.9841269841269841$. Family-level rows give five families: alternative yeast has hit rate $0.8333333333333334$, while bacterial-plastid, candidate-other, mitochondrial, and nuclear-ciliate each have hit rate $1.0$. These values support the reading that the local codon-tile construction is not confined to a single cherry-picked table row, but the comparison still remains at the label-table level.

\paragraph{Null-model pressure.}
The shape-significance row uses $N=2000$ samples with seed $42$. It reports one exact shape match, $25$ matches with Jaccard score at least $0.9$, $57377$ distinct-sample attempts, and $55377$ duplicate retries. The corresponding probabilities are
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{\mathrm{exact}} &= 0.0005,\\
p_{\mathrm{Jaccard}\ge 0.9} &= 0.0125.
\end{aligned}
$$
The amino-acid-class-preserving null row also uses $N=2000$ and seed $42$. It records observed $M$ size $20$, exact-match count $0$, lower-tail count $7$, exact-shape probability $0.0$, and lower-tail probability $0.0035$. These finite null rows make the observed local shape hard to dismiss as a typical class-preserving random rearrangement, while leaving the biological interpretation deliberately narrow.

\paragraph{Reality contact.}
The only empirical contact used here is the curated standard-code table, whose observed fact is that codons are assigned to amino-acid or stop labels. This contact is adequate for code-label readback and for checking a local label split. It is not a contact with ribosomal realization, folding, thermodynamic admissibility, organismal function, or evolutionary universality. The table can say that $\mathrm{UGA}$ has a stop or reassignment label in a given code table; it cannot by itself say that a folded protein exists, that a peptide is viable, or that a mechanism is forced by the six-bit coordinate geometry.

\paragraph{Local conclusion.}
The finite evidence supports the following bounded claim. In the window-six codon cube, the thirteen-codon exceptional region $R$ has a reproducible punctured-cube shape, a stable spectral signature, complete module accounting, and a leave-one-codon-out split with $8$ predictable codons and $5$ boundary codons. The curated code table then supplies the label readback needed to evaluate the split. No stronger biological conclusion follows from this chapter. In particular, the result does not establish translation, folded structure, physical admissibility, biological function, mechanism, or universality unless another observation, designed for that higher layer, is added.
