\subsection{Leave-one-codon recovery in the local codon tile}
\label{sec:h1-leave-one-codon-out}

\paragraph{Finite carrier and observed label contact.}
The packet uses the six-coordinate codon cube $Q_6$ as its finite carrier.  A codon is read as a six-bit display, with the curated standard genetic-code table supplying the only label contact used here: each codon is assigned an amino-acid or stop label at the code-read layer.  The distinguished residual row $R$ has cardinality $13$ and is exactly
$$
\begin{aligned}
R=\{&\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\\
&\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UUA}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
As a punctured-cube row it has face-vector
$$
\begin{aligned}
(f_0,f_1,f_2)=(13,16,4),
\end{aligned}
$$
so the finite geometry is not merely a list of codon names: it carries vertices, tile edges, and two-faces that can be interrogated locally.  The complementary measured label mass used in the null comparison has observed size $20$; no explicit finite list for that mass is included in this row data, so only its size is asserted here.

\paragraph{Spectral and shape measurements.}
The row has a reproducible spectral signature.  The recorded values are
$$
\begin{aligned}
\lambda_M&=0.6752479308830213,\\
\lambda_R&=0.4758352843197002,\\
\mu&=3.051487585298128.
\end{aligned}
$$
The radial cubic check evaluates to $-6.224354365258478\times 10^{-12}$, the same numerical residual recorded for the cubic-polynomial check, placing the computed value within floating-point tolerance rather than indicating a separate biological mechanism.  In the unconstrained finite null with $N=2000$ samples, there was $1$ exact shape match and $25$ matches with Jaccard score at least $0.9$, after $57377$ distinct-sample attempts and $55377$ duplicate retries.  Thus
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{\mathrm{exact}}&=0.0005,\\
p_{\mathrm{Jaccard}\ge 0.9}&=0.0125.
\end{aligned}
$$
Under the amino-acid-class-preserving null, also with $N=2000$, the exact shape count was $0$, the observed mass size was $20$, and $7$ samples landed in the lower tail at or below that observed size.  The corresponding finite-row probabilities are
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{\mathrm{class\ exact}}&=0.0,\\
p_{\mathrm{class\ lower\ tail}}&=0.0035.
\end{aligned}
$$
These values support a local code-table regularity test; they do not upgrade the finite geometry into a law of molecular realization.

\paragraph{Leave-one-code and leave-one-codon performance.}
The leave-one-code-out experiment evaluated $23$ code tables and $63$ targets.  Its baseline hit rate was $0.19821739130434785$, while the measured hit rate above baseline was $0.9841269841269841$.  The hit rate inside $M\setminus T$ was $0.9365079365079365$, and the rate inside $M\setminus T$ plus the boundary row was again $0.9841269841269841$.  The codon-level leave-one-out row for this chapter then records $0$ anchors, $5$ boundary cases, $8$ high-coverage closure cases, and $8$ predictable cases, with recovery rate
$$
\begin{aligned}
\rho_{\mathrm{recover}}=1.0.
\end{aligned}
$$
The thresholded recovery value is also $1.0$.  The absence of anchors is important: the result is not presented as direct recovery from privileged fixed codons, but as a boundary-and-closure phenomenon inside the finite code-read display.

\paragraph{Module organization.}
The $13$ codons in $R$ are completely assigned to six local modules, with no missing codons, no extra module codons, and no unassigned codons.  The module partition is
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{agr}&=\{\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG}\},\\
\mathrm{ar\_lys}&=\{\mathrm{AAA}\},\\
\mathrm{cun}&=\{\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU}\},\\
\mathrm{ile\_met}&=\{\mathrm{AUA}\},\\
\mathrm{ssn}&=\{\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UUA}\},\\
\mathrm{stop\_trp}&=\{\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UGA}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
Every listed module contains a predictable case or a boundary case.  The family-level leave-one-code-out survey has $5$ families.  The hit rates above baseline are $0.8333333333333334$ for alternative yeast, and $1.0$ for bacterial-plastid, candidate-other, mitochondrial, and nuclear-ciliate families.  This is a finite classification of local codon-label behavior, not a statement that these families share one causal biological pathway.

\paragraph{Local interpretation.}
The evidence supports the following narrow reading.  In the six-bit codon cube, the standard-code label table exposes a small residual row with a punctured-cube face structure, a stable numerical spectrum, statistically uncommon shape behavior under the recorded null models, and perfect recovery in the finite leave-one-codon-out row.  The measured object is therefore a local boundary tile in code-read coordinates.  Its reality contact is the curated codon-to-label table, and that contact can test codon labels and local label splits.

\paragraph{Scope barrier.}
No claim about translation execution, folded protein structure, physical admissibility, biological function, universal mechanism, or organism-independent necessity follows from these data alone.  Amino-acid label readback is still label readback; it is not a protein fold, a thermodynamic feasibility statement, a cellular implementation, or a functional assay.  Any future assertion crossing from code-read geometry to translation, structure, physics, function, mechanism, or universality must introduce a separate observed contact at that layer.  Until such a contact is supplied, the present chapter records only the finite codon-table phenomenon described above.
