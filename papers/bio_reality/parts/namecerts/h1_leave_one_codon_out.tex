\paragraph{Observed contact and negative scope.}
The only observed contact used here is the curated standard-code table, which assigns codons to amino-acid or stop labels.  It can test code readback and a local codon-tile label split.  It cannot test translation realization, protein folding, structural order, physical admissibility, biological function, or a universal mechanism.  Consequently, the amino-acid label sequence obtained from the table remains a label sequence.  It is not a folded structure, not an admissibility certificate, not a function assay, and not a proof that biological systems must instantiate the cube geometry.

The verified result is therefore a bounded code-layer statement: within the supplied standard-code contact, the thirteen-codon distinguished set has the recorded punctured-cube shape, the leave-one-codon recovery row has full recovery at the stated threshold, and the module classification covers every codon in $R$ with no extras and no omissions.  Any move from this local code-read result to translation, folding, physical realization, function, mechanism, or universality requires an additional observed contact designed for that higher layer.
