\paragraph{Finite codon display and reality contact.}

The contact therefore tests label readback and local split behavior, not translation dynamics or protein realization.  The observed boundary set $R$ has exactly thirteen codons,

It says that, within this finite standard-code display, the omitted codon is recovered through the union of local predictable cases and boundary cases.

This is the finite reason the leave-one-codon result is stronger than a single aggregate recovery score: the recovery is distributed across the same codon families that exhaust the observed boundary set.

\paragraph{Interpretive boundary.}
The result is a code-layer statement about curated codon labels.  It does not establish that DNA must obey this geometry as a biological necessity, and it does not infer translation realization, folded structure, structural order, physical admissibility, function, a mechanism, or universality.  The curated standard-code table supplies codon-to-label observations only.  Any claim about translation, folding, physical realization, organism-specific mechanism, or biological function would require an additional contact of the appropriate kind.  In particular, amino-acid label readback is not evidence for a folded protein structure, and CUN/UUR or tRNA-Leu language does not become structure evidence merely by being adjacent to this codon-table pattern.

\paragraph{Conclusion.}
The finite rows support the following narrow conclusion.  In the six-coordinate codon display for the curated standard-code table, the thirteen-codon set $R$ forms a punctured local cube with face vector $(13,16,4)$; its spectral measurements and sampling tests are nontrivial under the stated finite null models; and leave-one-codon omission is recovered at rate $1.0$ through predictable and boundary cases.  The claim is therefore ready as a local code-read record, provided it is kept at that level and not promoted into a statement about translation, folding, physical admissibility, function, mechanism, or universal biological law.
