\subsection{Leave-One-Codon-Out Boundary Recovery in the Window-Six Codon Cube}
\label{subsec:biorality-leave-one-codon-out-boundary-recovery}

\paragraph{Claim and finite carrier.}
The packet records a window-six codon-coordinate claim at the code-read layer.  The finite carrier is the six-bit codon cube $Q_{6}$ equipped with the standard-code label table as the observed readback contact.  The claim is not that the cube geometry is a biological law.  It is that, after one codon is withheld, the local coordinate, closure, spectrum, and relation data still locate the withheld row either in a predictable part of the local tile or on an explicitly marked boundary of that tile.  The biological object under inspection is the standard-code codon table, and the only observed labels used here are codon-to-amino-acid or codon-to-stop assignments.

\paragraph{Reality contact.}
The contact is the curated standard genetic-code table.  It supports statements of the form ``this codon has this standard-code label'' and finite comparisons among such labels.  It does not observe ribosomal translation, molecular folding, thermodynamic admissibility, realized function, evolutionary mechanism, or universality.  Therefore the packet remains at code-read resolution unless a separate empirical contact is attached for a higher layer.  In particular, amino-acid label strings produced by the table are not protein structures, and local CUN, UUR, AGR, or stop-label behavior is not by itself a cross-organism mechanism.

\paragraph{Shape significance checks.}
Against the finite null sample with seed $42$, $N=2000$ sampled alternatives, $57377$ distinct-sample attempts, and $55377$ duplicate retries, the exact shape appeared once.  The exact-shape rate is therefore
$$\begin{aligned}

\end{aligned}$$
The high-overlap event at Jaccard threshold $0.9$ occurred $25$ times, giving
$$\begin{aligned}
p_{J\geq 0.9}=0.0125.
\end{aligned}$$
A second label-class-preserving null used the same $N=2000$ and seed $42$.  It found $0$ exact-shape matches and $7$ lower-tail cases at or below the observed $M$ size.  The observed $M$ size was $20$, with
$$\begin{aligned}
p_{\mathrm{aa\ class\ exact}} &= 0.0,\\
p_{\mathrm{aa\ class\ lower\ tail}} &= 0.0035.
\end{aligned}$$
These checks justify treating the finite local shape as a nontrivial code-layer pattern, while still leaving all mechanistic and structural interpretations outside the present contact.

\paragraph{Leave-one-codon-out result.}
The direct leave-one-codon-out row test has experiment run identifier \texttt{e9e1a6e41633}.  It recorded $0$ anchor rows, $5$ boundary rows, and $8$ predictable rows.  The high-closure coverage count was $8$, and the recovery rate was
$$\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{recovery\ rate}=1.0.
\end{aligned}$$
The threshold comparison also returned $1.0$.  In this packet, every withheld codon in the distinguished row set is recovered either as predictable by the local closure information or as belonging to the boundary part of the tile.  The five boundary rows are not promoted to biological exceptions or mechanistic triggers; they are precisely the rows where the finite local tile says that the code-read split is at an edge of the observed coordinate pattern.
