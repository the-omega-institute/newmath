\subsection{Leave-one-codon recovery in the window-six codon cube}
\label{sec:h1-leave-one-codon-out}

\paragraph{Finite carrier and readout.}
The claim is evaluated on the window-six codon display, where each three-letter codon is treated as a six-coordinate point in the finite codon cube $Q_6$. The biological readout used here is only the curated standard-code assignment of codons to amino-acid or stop labels. Thus the finite object under test is a code-layer table with coordinate, closure, spectral, and relation data; it is not a model of translation chemistry, folding, physical feasibility, organismal function, or universal biological law. The local question is whether the codon-coordinate representation exposes a recoverable boundary pattern when one codon is withheld.

\paragraph{Observed boundary set.}
The distinguished relation row has cardinality $13$. Its observed codons are
$$
\begin{aligned}
R=\{&AAA,AGA,AGG,AUA,CUA,CUC,CUG,CUU,\\
&UAA,UAG,UCA,UGA,UUA\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The same row is reported as a punctured-cube pattern with face-vector
$$
\begin{aligned}
(f_0,f_1,f_2)=(13,16,4).
\end{aligned}
$$
This is the finite local tile used by the leave-one-codon test. The row is not interpreted as a global constraint on DNA or on the genetic code; it is a finite coordinate-and-label configuration whose contact with biology is the curated standard-code table.

\paragraph{Spectral scale.}
The corresponding spectral readout separates the larger measured region from the relation row. The recorded values are
$$
\begin{aligned}
\lambda_M&=0.6752479308830213,\\
\lambda_R&=0.4758352843197002,\\
\mu&=3.051487585298128.
\end{aligned}
$$
The radial polynomial residual is numerically negligible at $-6.224354365258478\times 10^{-12}$, matching the reported cubic-polynomial residual at the same value. These numbers are used only as finite diagnostics of the coordinate construction. They do not supply a mechanistic explanation for how cells translate codons, and they do not upgrade the local tile into a claim about protein structure.

\paragraph{Shape significance against sampled alternatives.}
In the unrestricted sampled comparison, $N=2000$ random draws were evaluated after $57377$ distinct-sample attempts and $55377$ duplicate retries with seed $42$. There was $1$ exact shape match, giving
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{\mathrm{exact}}=0.0005.
\end{aligned}
$$
There were $25$ cases with Jaccard overlap at least $0.9$, giving
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{J\geq 0.9}=0.0125.
\end{aligned}
$$
The amino-acid-class-preserving null gives a stricter comparison for the observed size. With $N=2000$, the observed measured size is $|M|=20$, the exact shape count is $0$, and the lower-tail count at or below the observed measured size is $7$. Hence
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{\mathrm{class\ exact}}&=0.0,\\
p_{\mathrm{class\ lower\ tail}}&=0.0035.
\end{aligned}
$$
These finite-row values support that the observed coordinate pattern is uncommon under the recorded null procedures. They do not, by themselves, identify a physical cause.

\paragraph{Leave-one-codon recovery.}
The direct leave-one-codon row reports no anchor recoveries, $5$ boundary recoveries, and $8$ high-coverage closure recoveries. The predictable count is $8$, and the recovery rate is
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{recovery\ rate}=1.0.
\end{aligned}
$$
The threshold comparison also reports $1.0$. In words, every withheld case counted by the finite test is recovered either through the recorded predictable component or through the boundary classification available inside the local tile. The result is a code-read statement: the six-coordinate codon representation preserves enough local structure to recover the held-out codon placement in this finite table experiment.

\paragraph{Module accounting.}
The module classification exhausts the relation row. The module summary reports $R\_size=13$, $assigned\_count=13$, no extra module codons, no missing codons, and no unassigned codons. The complete module partition is
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{ar\_lys}&=\{AAA\},\\
\mathrm{agr}&=\{AGA,AGG\},\\
\mathrm{ile\_met}&=\{AUA\},\\
\mathrm{cun}&=\{CUA,CUC,CUG,CUU\},\\
\mathrm{stop\_trp}&=\{UAA,UAG,UGA\},\\
\mathrm{ssn}&=\{UCA,UUA\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
Every listed module has either a predictable or boundary status in the leave-one-codon classification. This matters because the recovery claim is not resting on a single isolated codon; it is distributed across singleton, doubleton, tripleton, and four-codon modules inside the same finite relation row.

\paragraph{Related leave-one-code behavior.}
The broader leave-one-code table comparison evaluated $23$ tables and $63$ total targets. Its baseline hit rate is $0.19821739130434785$, while the hit rate above baseline is $0.9841269841269841$. The internal measured-minus-target hit rate is $0.9365079365079365$, and adding the boundary gives $0.9841269841269841$. By family, the hit rates above baseline are $0.8333333333333334$ for alternative yeast and $1.0$ for bacterial-plastid, candidate-other, mitochondrial, and nuclear-ciliate families. These companion rows are not used to assert that the local standard-code tile is universal. They show only that the same finite recovery style remains visible across the reported leave-one-code comparisons.

\paragraph{Local conclusion.}
The finite evidence supports the following restricted conclusion. In the window-six codon cube, the standard-code label table contains a $13$-codon relation row with punctured-cube face-vector $(13,16,4)$, nontrivial spectral separation, low sampled null rates, complete module assignment, and perfect recorded leave-one-codon recovery for the tested row. This is enough for a local code-read claim: the coordinate construction recovers a boundary-sensitive codon-label pattern when a codon is withheld.

\paragraph{Scope boundary.}
No stronger biological claim follows from this row. The curated code table supplies codon-to-label assignments, not ribosomal translation dynamics, folded protein geometry, energetic admissibility, phenotype, organismal function, or a universal mechanism. Amino-acid label readback remains a code-layer observation. Any claim about translation realization, structural order, physical admissibility, function, or universality requires a separate reality contact designed for that layer.
