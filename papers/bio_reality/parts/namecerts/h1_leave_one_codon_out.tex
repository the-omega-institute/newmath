\subsection{Leave-one-codon recovery as a local code-table boundary}
\label{sec:h1-leave-one-codon-out}

\paragraph{Finite codon carrier.}
This record treats the standard-code codon table as a finite code-read object placed on a six-coordinate codon cube $Q_6$.  Each codon is represented by a six-bit display, and the only external biological contact used here is the curated standard-code label table assigning codons to amino-acid or stop labels.  The internal packet has four relevant kinds of structure: coordinate position in the cube, finite closure around selected rows, a spectral summary of the induced row shape, and label relations read from the standard table.  The claim tested in this subsection is therefore deliberately local: after withholding one codon row, the remaining codon-label data may recover whether the omitted codon belongs to the local predictable part or to a boundary class.  It is not a claim about translation being realized, about folded protein structure, about physical admissibility, about biological function, about a mechanism, or about universality.

\paragraph{Punctured-cube row set.}
The finite row set $R$ has cardinality $13$.  The verified codons in $R$ are
$$
\begin{aligned}
R=\{&\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\\
&\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UUA}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The same finite set is recorded as a punctured-cube shape with face-vector
$$
\begin{aligned}
(f_0,f_1,f_2)=(13,16,4).
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the row data are not merely a list of codons; they carry a small cubical incidence profile.  The local readback is the standard-code label assignment on these rows, while the geometry supplies the finite neighborhood in which the leave-one test is evaluated.

\paragraph{Spectral and null-model checks.}
The spectrum attached to the row shape reports $\lambda_M=0.6752479308830213$, $\lambda_R=0.4758352843197002$, and $\mu=3.051487585298128$.  The associated radial cubic residual is numerically negligible at $-6.224354365258478\times 10^{-12}$, with the same value recorded for the cubic check and for the radial polynomial value.  The observed $M$ size is $20$ in the amino-acid-class-preserving null experiment.  Under the shape null with $N=2000$ samples, there was $1$ exact shape match, giving $p=0.0005$, and there were $25$ samples with Jaccard overlap at least $0.9$, giving $p=0.0125$.  The sampling ledger records $57377$ distinct-sample attempts and $55377$ duplicate retries at seed $42$.  Under the amino-acid-class-preserving null, again with $N=2000$ and seed $42$, the exact-shape count was $0$, the lower-tail count at or below the observed $M$ size was $7$, the exact-shape probability was $0.0$, and the lower-tail probability was $0.0035$.  These values support the use of the row shape as a finite code-layer signal, while leaving all higher biological interpretations outside the present contact.

\paragraph{Leave-one-code and leave-one-codon behavior.}
Across the leave-one-code-out experiment, $23$ tables were evaluated and $63$ target rows were tested.  The baseline hit rate was $0.19821739130434785$.  The hit rate above baseline was $0.9841269841269841$, the hit rate inside $M\setminus T$ was $0.9365079365079365$, and the hit rate inside $M\setminus T$ with boundary rows included was $0.9841269841269841$.  The codon-level leave-one run for the present claim has $0$ anchors, $5$ boundary rows, $8$ high-closure-coverage rows, and $8$ predictable rows.  Its recovery rate is $1.0$, and the recorded threshold comparison is also $1.0$.  In finite terms, every omitted codon in the tested packet is recovered either as predictable under the local closure rule or as a boundary row, with no anchor contribution in this run.

\paragraph{Module classification.}
The thirteen codons in $R$ are completely assigned to six finite modules.  The verified module partition is
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{agr}&=\{\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG}\},\\
\mathrm{ar\_lys}&=\{\mathrm{AAA}\},\\
\mathrm{cun}&=\{\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU}\},\\

The module ledger reports $R$ size $13$, assigned count $13$, no extra module codons, no codons missing from the modules, and no unassigned codons.  Each module has at least one predictable or boundary representative.  At the code-family level, five families are recorded: alternative yeast, bacterial or plastid, candidate other, mitochondrial, and nuclear ciliate.  Their hit rates above baseline are respectively $0.8333333333333334$, $1.0$, $1.0$, $1.0$, and $1.0$.  This family-level summary agrees with the codon-level picture: the signal is strong as a finite label-table recovery phenomenon, especially around reassignment-prone local regions.

\paragraph{Local scientific reading.}
The supported conclusion is narrow.  Window-six codon coordinates expose a finite local boundary-and-prediction structure over the standard-code label table.  The row set $R$ has a concrete punctured-cube profile, the spectral summary is stable to numerical tolerance, and the leave-one-codon experiment recovers the withheld codon with rate $1.0$ within the predictable-or-boundary distinction.  The evidence therefore supports a code-read statement: curated codon labels, when placed on this finite cube display, have a non-random local shape that is visible under leave-one withholding.  The evidence does not show that cells implement the geometry, that a peptide is translated, that any amino-acid sequence folds, that a physical configuration is admissible, that a function is realized, or that a general biological law has been found.  Any such promotion would require an additional external contact of the corresponding kind, separate from the standard-code label table used here.
