\subsection{Leave-one-codon recovery at the local codon boundary}
\label{subsec:h1-leave-one-codon-out}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite codon display.}
The record for claim $\mathrm{h1.leave.one.codon.out}$ is a code-layer observation on the six-bit codon cube $Q_6$.  Its biological contact is only the curated standard-code label table: a codon is read as an amino-acid label or as stop, and no higher biological process is observed by that contact.  The distinguished codon subset $R$ has cardinality $13$ and is exactly
$\{\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UUA}\}$.  The same finite set is recorded as a punctured-cube row with face vector $(13,16,4)$, so the object under discussion is not an unrestricted codon table but a small local tile with explicit boundary data.  The available row does not provide an explicit codon-by-codon list for the associated $M$ region; it does provide the observed size $|M|=20$ and the finite significance tests attached to that shape.

\paragraph{Leave-one-codon measurement.}
The leave-one-codon row reports $0$ anchor codons, $5$ boundary codons, $8$ high-coverage closure codons, and $8$ predictable codons.  Its recovery rate is $1.0$, and the threshold flag is also $1.0$.  Interpreted literally, this says that every codon tested by the finite deletion probe is recovered either through the local closure signal or through the boundary accounting supplied by the tile.  It does not say that the codon is biologically forced, translated, folded, or functional; it says that, once the curated label table is fixed as the readout, the local six-bit coordinate structure has complete recovery on this finite row.

\paragraph{Module accounting.}
The module-classification row closes the same $13$ codons without omission.  It assigns all $13$ codons, leaves no missing codons, has no extra module codons, and has no unassigned codons.  The modules are $\mathrm{agr}=\{\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG}\}$, $\mathrm{ar\_lys}=\{\mathrm{AAA}\}$, $\mathrm{cun}=\{\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU}\}$, $\mathrm{ile\_met}=\{\mathrm{AUA}\}$, $\mathrm{ssn}=\{\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UUA}\}$, and $\mathrm{stop\_trp}=\{\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UGA}\}$.  Each listed module has at least one predictable or boundary member, so the recovery is not concentrated in a single exceptional family.  The largest block is the four-codon $\mathrm{cun}$ block, while the singleton blocks $\mathrm{ar\_lys}$ and $\mathrm{ile\_met}$ show that the accounting also covers isolated label corners.

\paragraph{Spectral and null rows.}
The spectral row gives $\lambda_M=0.6752479308830213$, $\lambda_R=0.4758352843197002$, and $\mu=3.051487585298128$.  The recorded cubic residual is $-6.224354365258478\times 10^{-12}$, the same value appearing as the radial residual, so the numerical spectral relation is satisfied to the stored tolerance.  The shape test with $N=2000$ samples reports one exact shape match, $25$ matches at Jaccard score at least $0.9$, $57377$ distinct-sample attempts, and $55377$ duplicate retries.  The corresponding finite p-values are $p_{\mathrm{exact}}=0.0005$ and $p_{\mathrm{Jaccard}\ge 0.9}=0.0125$.  The amino-acid-class preserving null row, also at $N=2000$, has zero exact matches, seven lower-tail samples at or below the observed $|M|=20$, exact-shape p-value $0.0$, and lower-tail p-value $0.0035$.  These rows support the claim that the local tile is unusually shaped relative to the stored finite null ensembles, not that the tile is a universal biological principle.

The rate inside $M-T$ is $0.9365079365079365$, and the rate inside $M-T$ plus the boundary is again $0.9841269841269841$.  Family-level rates are $0.8333333333333334$ for alternative yeast, $1.0$ for bacterial and plastid codes, $1.0$ for the candidate-other family, $1.0$ for mitochondrial codes, and $1.0$ for nuclear-ciliate codes.  These finite comparisons explain why the single-codon deletion row is treated as a strong code-read signal: the same local-boundary pattern remains visible across the supplied table-level probes.

\paragraph{Local boundary of the claim.}
The allowed conclusion is narrow.  Window-six codon coordinates, together with the curated standard-code label contact, expose a finite local boundary tile whose leave-one-codon recovery is complete in the stored row.  The label contact does not observe translation, folded protein structure, physical admissibility, biological function, mechanism, or universality.  Amino-acid label readback remains a code-layer datum.  Any statement about protein realization, folding, organismal mechanism, or function would require a separate observation designed for that higher layer; it is not licensed by this codon-label table or by the local cube calculation.
