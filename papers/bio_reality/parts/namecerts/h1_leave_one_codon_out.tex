\subsection{Leave-One-Codon-Out Recovery in the Window-Six Codon Cube}\label{sec:bioreality-h1-leave-one-codon-out}

\paragraph{Finite codon display.}
This record concerns the standard-code codon table viewed through a six-coordinate codon cube.  The object under test is not translation itself, but the finite readout of codon labels in the curated standard genetic-code table.  In this display the distinguished residual set $R$ has cardinality $13$, with the verified codons
$$
\begin{aligned}
R=\{&\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\\
&\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UUA}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The same row is recorded as a punctured-cube face pattern with $f$-vector $(13,16,4)$.  Thus the tested geometry is finite and explicit: thirteen codon vertices, sixteen local adjacencies, and four two-dimensional tile faces are read inside the window-six coordinate system.  The curated contact supplies the amino-acid-or-stop labels attached to these codons, while the coordinate display supplies only the finite tile arrangement in which those labels are compared.

\paragraph{Spectral and shape evidence.}
The companion spectral row records $\lambda_M=0.6752479308830213$, $\lambda_R=0.4758352843197002$, and $\mu=3.051487585298128$.  The radial cubic check has residual $-6.224354365258478\times 10^{-12}$, so the polynomial relation is satisfied to numerical tolerance in the finite run.  Shape significance was tested against $2000$ samples with seed $42$.  Among those samples there was $1$ exact shape match, giving $p_{\mathrm{exact}}=0.0005$, and $25$ samples had Jaccard similarity at least $0.9$, giving $p_{\mathrm{Jaccard}\geq 0.9}=0.0125$.  The sampling log records $57377$ distinct-sample attempts and $55377$ duplicate retries, so the low exact-match count should be read together with the fact that the finite sample space has many repeated proposals under the sampler.  An amino-acid-class-preserving null model, also with $N=2000$ and seed $42$, found $0$ exact shape matches and $7$ lower-tail cases at or below the observed size $|M|=20$, giving $p_{\mathrm{class\ exact}}=0.0$ and lower-tail value $0.0035$.

\paragraph{Leave-one-code and leave-one-codon rows.}
The broader leave-one-code-out row evaluated $23$ code tables and $63$ targets.  Its baseline hit rate is $0.19821739130434785$, while the hit rate above baseline is $0.9841269841269841$.  The hit rate inside $M\setminus T$ is $0.9365079365079365$, and allowing the boundary raises the same row to $0.9841269841269841$.  The family breakdown keeps the effect concentrated across five family groups: alternative yeast has hit rate above baseline $0.8333333333333334$, while bacterial/plastid, candidate other, mitochondrial, and nuclear ciliate each record $1.0$.

For the codon-level leave-one-out test itself, the verified row gives anchor count $0$, boundary count $5$, high-coverage closure count $8$, predictable count $8$, and recovery rate $1.0$.  The thresholded recovery flag is also $1.0$.  In words, no omitted codon is recovered by a singleton anchor in this row; eight cases are recovered by the high-coverage closure side, and five are assigned to the boundary side.  The complete row therefore supports a local tile-boundary interpretation rather than a global biological conclusion.

\paragraph{Module accounting.}
The module classifier assigns every codon in $R$ without surplus or omission.  The complete accounting has $R$-size $13$, assigned count $13$, no extra module codons, no missing codons, and no unassigned codons.  The six modules are
$$
\begin{aligned}

\mathrm{ar\_lys}&=\{\mathrm{AAA}\},\\

Each module contains either a predictable codon or a boundary codon under the recorded classifier.  This is the strongest local statement licensed by the row: the leave-one-codon test does not merely count recovered codons, but partitions the entire thirteen-codon residual set into named finite modules whose members are all covered by the predictable-or-boundary dichotomy.

\paragraph{Reality contact and scope.}
The contact used here is a curated standard-code table assigning codons to amino-acid or stop labels.  That contact is adequate for code-layer readback and for checking whether a local codon tile separates singleton label corners from three-point label classes.  It is not an observation of translation execution, a protein-folding measurement, a physical-admissibility assay, a function assay, or a universal mechanism.  The numbers above therefore support the following bounded claim: window-six codon coordinates expose a local boundary-tile structure in the standard-code label table, and the leave-one-codon row recovers the omitted labels at rate $1.0$ through predictable or boundary positions.  Nothing in this finite row licenses promotion from codon-label readback to folded structure, biological function, physical necessity, or universality without an additional contact designed for that higher layer.
