\paragraph{Leave-one-code and leave-one-codon recovery.}

Inside $M-T$ the hit rate was $0.9365079365079365$, and after including the boundary it returned to $0.9841269841269841$.  This is the finite evidence that the local codon-tile boundary has predictive force at the code-label level.

The stricter leave-one-codon row reports anchor count $0$, boundary count $5$, high closure coverage $8$, predictable count $8$, and recovery rate $1.0$.  The recovery-rate threshold flag is also $1.0$.  In concrete terms, every recovered target in this test is accounted for either by predictable closure behavior or by an explicit boundary case; none is explained by an anchor count in this row.  The probe therefore describes a local boundary classifier rather than a global biological rule.

\paragraph{Local boundary of the claim.}
The empirical contact is a curated standard genetic-code table.  It observes codon labels and supports local split checks in the six-bit coordinate display.  It does not observe translation realization, folded structure, structural order, physical admissibility, biological function, or a universal mechanism.  In particular, amino-acid label readback is not protein folding, and a CUN/UUR or tRNA-Leu wording pattern is not structure evidence unless a separate organism-matched structural or biochemical contact is attached.  The finite result is therefore this: window-six codon coordinates expose a nontrivial local boundary-tile pattern with complete $13$-codon module accounting and perfect leave-one-codon recovery in the recorded row, while the scientific scope remains confined to curated code-label readback.
