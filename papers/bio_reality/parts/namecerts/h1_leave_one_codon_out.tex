\subsection{Scope boundary}
\label{sec:h1-leave-one-codon-out-boundary}

\paragraph{Claims not licensed by this row.}
Nothing in the finite row observes translation realization, folded structure, physical admissibility, biological function, a universal biological mechanism, or a global law for DNA.  Amino-acid label readback is still label readback.  To move from this chapter to translation, structure, physical chemistry, or function, a separate empirical contact would have to be supplied and checked on its own terms.  Until such a contact is present, the local codon tile is a mathematically organized table phenomenon at the code layer.
