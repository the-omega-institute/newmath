\subsection{Condition-coupled refusal for the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ SD/YEPD interaction packet}
\label{subsec:bioreality-q6-sd-yepd-condition-coupled-refusal}

\paragraph{Finite panel and measured target.}
The record tests whether a finite block of $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates improves a condition-coupled readout for the SD/YEPD contrast after the declared controls have already been assembled.  The measured response is the signed rank-normalized endpoint contrast
$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta P_g = z_{\mathrm{rank}}(P_{\mathrm{SD},g}) - z_{\mathrm{rank}}(P_{\mathrm{YEPD},g}),
\end{aligned}
$$
with $2206$ genes, empirical target mean $-1.1858399826713623\times 10^{-18}$, and empirical target standard deviation $0.4434928347257967$.  The finite row split has fold sizes $447,475,444,451,389$, summing to $2206$ held-out evaluations.  The control design contains an intercept, log CDS sense-codon length, five train-fold principal coordinates from the twenty amino-acid composition proportions $A,C,D,E,F,G,H,I,K,L,M,N,P,Q,R,S,T,V,W,Y$, GC3 from ordered CDS codons, mean pLDDT with a missingness indicator, and an integrated PaxDb $\log_{10}$ baseline abundance covariate.  The complete-case count for the structural-order augmented design is $1654$, with $4$ structural-order missing rows.  Thus the object being audited is a finite controlled prediction problem on matched gene rows, not an unconstrained biological assertion.

\paragraph{Residual coordinate block.}
The candidate residual block has ridge parameter $\lambda=1.0$ for train-only residualization of $Q\sim C$.  In every fold the coefficients and residual scaling are fitted on the training genes and then applied to held-out genes.  The nine coordinates are
$$
\begin{aligned}
&K\_AAA,\quad Arg\_AGR,\quad Ile\_AUA,\quad Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR,\quad Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG,\\
&Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY,\quad Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG,\quad Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY,\quad f3\_stress.
\end{aligned}
$$
The coding cost charged to this block is $38.494680999067235$ nats.  The tested question is therefore sharp: after the controls have already absorbed amino-acid composition, CDS length, GC3, structural-order proxy, and baseline abundance, does this residual coordinate block reduce held-out description length for the signed SD/YEPD endpoint contrast?

\paragraph{Main held-out result.}
The answer is negative.  The baseline model has held-out $R^2=0.008108769333748045$ and SSE $430.68597066859365$, whereas the residual-augmented model has held-out $R^2=0.005545435711181601$ and SSE $431.7989876963185$.  The finite deltas are
$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta R^2 &= -0.002563333622566444,\\
\Delta\log p_D &= -2.936811337346853,\\
\mathrm{residual\ code\ saving} &= -2.8467939090648606\ \mathrm{nats},\\
\mathrm{description\ length\ drop} &= -41.3414749081321\ \mathrm{nats}.
\end{aligned}
$$
Since the description-length drop is negative after charging the $38.494680999067235$-nat coordinate cost, the positive gate is not crossed.  The finite verdict recorded for this packet is therefore $\mathrm{no\_compressible\_q\_interaction}$ rather than a surviving cross-layer interaction.

\paragraph{Null comparisons.}
Three applicable null families were run with $300$ draws each, while the intended publishable count is $1000$ draws.  The condition-sign-flip null has null $95$ thresholds $\Delta\log p_D=-0.05373328681116618$, $\Delta R^2=0.00002359796412121362$, and description-length drop $-38.4687437759017$ nats; its largest observed null improvements are $3.346183710458263$ in $\Delta\log p_D$ and $0.003115206933151926$ in $\Delta R^2$.  The matched-$Q$ permutation null has thresholds $3.7150577222696484$, $0.003501567522117832$, and $-34.593988916260244$ nats, with maxima $9.754186934356767$ and $0.008579527265972553$.  The synonymous-recoding null has thresholds $0.011353124037214002$, $0.00028102266970064793$, and $-38.18213471025496$ nats, with maxima $6.24877932309505$ and $0.0058455213683357155$.  In each of these three null families the joint gate is false: the observed held-out deltas do not exceed the applicable null $95$ boundaries, and the description-length criterion is also false.  A replicate-placebo null is unavailable because the SD/YEPD wrappers expose endpoint condition abundance rather than same-condition replicate abundance columns.

\paragraph{Recoding diagnostics and anti-vacuity checks.}
The synonymous-recoding null preserves amino-acid sequence and per-amino-acid GC3-ending counts exactly.  Its mean absolute GC3 error is $0.0$, its $95$th percentile absolute GC3 error is $0.0$, its mean relative ENC-proxy error is $0.09042494844341317$, and its $95$th percentile relative ENC-proxy error is $0.4059574704858408$.  These diagnostics keep the null close to the controlled codon-composition surface rather than comparing against arbitrary sequence rewrites.  Side checks do not rescue the main target.  For the absolute-delta side target, $\Delta\log p_D=-3.8682101284448436$, $\Delta R^2=-0.0030746447001754884$, residual saving is $-3.5938977348833916$ nats, and the description-length drop is $-42.08857873395063$ nats.  For the signed-delta target with endpoint mean-rank as an extra control, $\Delta\log p_D=-2.7093968327387756$, $\Delta R^2=-0.002354084610949325$, residual saving is $-2.6130013210868746$ nats, and the description-length drop is $-41.10768232015411$ nats.  The mean-rank side evidence has $\Delta\log p_D=7.4632216658321795$ and $\Delta R^2=0.0026536465383733$, but after the same coordinate charge its description-length drop remains negative at $-30.29464161255981$ nats, and it is explicitly not used for the main conclusion.

\paragraph{Local interpretation boundary.}
This chapter records a finite negative interaction result for the declared SD/YEPD signed endpoint contrast.  It does not establish a positive $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ survival entry, and it does not license transfer from residual codon geometry to translation, ribosome occupancy, protein mediation, folding, physical admissibility, biological function, mechanism, phenotype, or universality.  A separate layer-matched contact and a separately computed controlled statistic would be required before any such claim could be made.  Modeled tAI or stAI weights, matched mRNA abundance, baseline protein abundance, structural-order proxies, and routing identifiers remain bounded covariates or candidate contacts; they are not substitutes for an observed translation-efficiency survival matrix.  The present finite row says only that, in this controlled SD/YEPD design and under the charged nine-coordinate residual block, the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ candidate interaction fails to compress the held-out signed endpoint contrast.
