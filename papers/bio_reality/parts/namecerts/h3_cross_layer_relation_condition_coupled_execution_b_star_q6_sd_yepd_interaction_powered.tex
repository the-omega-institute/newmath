\subsection{Condition-coupled $B^*_{Q6}$ residuals do not compress the SD--YEPD endpoint contrast}
\label{sec:h3-cross-layer-relation-condition-coupled-execution-b-star-q6-sd-yepd-interaction-powered}

\paragraph{Finite row object.}
The object tested here is a finite row panel of $2206$ matched coding-sequence entries.  Each row carries a signed endpoint contrast
$\Delta P_g = z_{\mathrm{rank}}(P_{\mathrm{SD}}) - z_{\mathrm{rank}}(P_{\mathrm{YEPD}})$,
with empirical mean $-1.1858399826713623\times 10^{-18}$ and standard deviation $0.4434928347257967$.  The tested coordinate block is the nine-coordinate residualized $Q$ block
$\{K_{AAA}, Arg_{AGR}, Ile_{AUA}, Leu_{CUN\,vs\,UUR}, Leu_{UUA\,vs\,UUG}, Ser_{UCR\,vs\,AGY}, Ser_{UCA\,vs\,UCG}, Thr_{ACR\,vs\,ACY}, f_3\text{-stress}\}$.
For each held-out fold, the residualization $Q\sim C$ is fit only on the training genes, and the held-out residual coordinates use the corresponding training-fit coefficients and training residual scale.  The ridge cost used for the $Q$ block is $\lambda=1.0$, and the charged coordinate encoding cost is $38.494680999067235$ nats.

\paragraph{Control surface.}
The baseline control matrix contains an intercept, log coding-sequence sense-codon length, five train-fold principal components from the twenty amino-acid composition proportions $A,C,D,E,F,G,H,I,K,L,M,N,P,Q,R,S,T,V,W,Y$, GC3 from ordered CDS codons, a mean pLDDT structural-order proxy with a missingness indicator, and an integrated PaxDb $\log_{10}$ baseline abundance covariate.  The five held-out fold sizes are $447$, $475$, $444$, $451$, and $389$, summing to $2206$ test rows.  The complete-case count for the abundance-augmented surface is $1654$, and the structural-order proxy is missing on $4$ rows.  These controls define a narrow finite-row question: does the residual $Q$ block reduce held-out description length for the signed SD--YEPD contrast after the listed baselines are already present?

\paragraph{Held-out comparison.}
The main held-out comparison is negative.  The baseline model has $R^2=0.008108769333748045$ and $\mathrm{SSE}=430.68597066859365$, whereas the model with the residual $Q$ block has $R^2=0.005545435711181601$ and $\mathrm{SSE}=431.7989876963185$.  Thus the fitted residual block lowers predictive fit rather than improving it:
$$\begin{aligned}
\Delta R^2 &= -0.002563333622566444,\\
\Delta\log p_D &= -2.936811337346853,\\
\mathrm{residual\ code\ saving} &= -2.8467939090648606\ \mathrm{nats},\\
\mathrm{description\ length\ drop} &= -41.3414749081321\ \mathrm{nats}.
\end{aligned}$$
Because the description-length drop is negative after paying the $38.494680999067235$ nat coordinate cost, the positive gate is not passed.  The stored verdict for this finite experiment is therefore \emph{no compressible $Q$ interaction}, not a surviving condition-coupled execution effect.

\paragraph{Null comparisons.}
The same conclusion holds against the available null families.  For the condition-sign-flip null, $300$ draws were run; the $95\%$ null levels are $\Delta\log p_D=-0.05373328681116618$, $\Delta R^2=2.359796412121362\times 10^{-5}$, and description-length drop $-38.4687437759017$ nats, while the largest seen null values reached $\Delta\log p_D=3.346183710458263$ and $\Delta R^2=0.003115206933151926$.  For the matched-$Q$ permutation null, also at $300$ draws, the $95\%$ levels are $\Delta\log p_D=3.7150577222696484$, $\Delta R^2=0.003501567522117832$, and description-length drop $-34.593988916260244$ nats, with maxima $9.754186934356767$ and $0.008579527265972553$.  For the synonymous-recoding null, $300$ draws preserve amino-acid sequence and per-amino-acid GC3-ending counts exactly; its $95\%$ levels are $\Delta\log p_D=0.011353124037214002$, $\Delta R^2=0.00028102266970064793$, and description-length drop $-38.18213471025496$ nats, with maxima $6.24877932309505$ and $0.0058455213683357155$.  The mean absolute GC3 error in the recoding diagnostics is $0.0$, and the mean relative ENC-proxy error is $0.09042494844341317$ with $95\%$ value $0.4059574704858408$.  The replicate-placebo family is unavailable because the SD/YEPD wrappers expose endpoint condition abundance rather than same-condition replicate abundance columns.  No applicable null family gives a joint positive pass.

\paragraph{Anti-vacuity side checks.}
The signed contrast is the declared target.  Two side checks show why the conclusion is specific rather than a consequence of a globally inert design.  For the absolute-delta side target, the residual block gives $\Delta\log p_D=-3.8682101284448436$, $\Delta R^2=-0.0030746447001754884$, residual code saving $-3.5938977348833916$ nats, and description-length drop $-42.08857873395063$ nats.  For the signed contrast with endpoint mean-rank as an additional control, the block gives $\Delta\log p_D=-2.7093968327387756$, $\Delta R^2=-0.002354084610949325$, residual code saving $-2.6130013210868746$ nats, and description-length drop $-41.10768232015411$ nats.  By contrast, the endpoint mean-rank side surface has $\Delta\log p_D=7.4632216658321795$ and $\Delta R^2=0.0026536465383733$ before the same coordinate charge, but its description-length drop remains negative at $-30.29464161255981$ nats.  These side surfaces are not used as the main conclusion; they only document that the finite procedure is capable of recording nonzero held-out differences while still rejecting the declared signed SD--YEPD interaction.

\paragraph{Local biological reading.}
The row calculation licenses a narrow negative statement.  In this finite controlled panel, the residual $B^*_{Q6}$ coordinate block does not improve held-out description length for the signed SD--YEPD endpoint contrast after amino-acid composition, length, GC3, structural-order proxy, integrated baseline abundance, and fold-local residualization controls.  The result therefore keeps the coordinate in the status of a codon-residual descriptor for this endpoint contrast, rather than an observed survivor for the declared condition-coupled readout.  It also does not turn modeled codon adaptation, endpoint abundance, or residual codon geometry into ribosome-profiling translation efficiency, mature protein abundance, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, mechanism, phenotype, or a universal biological law.  Any such stronger claim would require a separate layer-matched contact and a new controlled finite-row statistic at that layer.
