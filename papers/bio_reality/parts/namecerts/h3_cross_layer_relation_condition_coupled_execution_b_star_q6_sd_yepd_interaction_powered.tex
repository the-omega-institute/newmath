\subsection{Condition-coupled $B^*_{Q6}$ residuals against the SD--YEPD abundance contrast}\label{sec:bior-claim-q6-sd-yepd-condition-interaction}\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite panel and readout.}
This record tests whether the $B^*_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates carry a condition-coupled signal for the signed SD--YEPD endpoint contrast. The fitted target is
$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta P_g = z_{\mathrm{rank}}(P_{\mathrm{SD},g}) - z_{\mathrm{rank}}(P_{\mathrm{YEPD},g}),
\end{aligned}
$$
with $n=2206$ genes, empirical mean $-1.1858399826713623\cdot 10^{-18}$, and empirical standard deviation $0.4434928347257967$. A log-ratio sensitivity target, $z(\log(P_{\mathrm{SD}})-\log(P_{\mathrm{YEPD}}))$, was recorded as a check on the signed-rank construction, but the main target remained the signed rank-normalized contrast. The run identifier is \texttt{acc5921d7c06}, and the experiment identifier is \texttt{b\_star\_q6\_condition\_interaction\_sd\_yepd\_powered}.

\paragraph{Coordinate and control design.}
The tested residual block contains nine train-only residualized coordinates:
$$
\begin{aligned}
&K\_{AAA},\quad Arg\_{AGR},\quad Ile\_{AUA},\quad Leu\_{CUN\_vs\_UUR},\quad Leu\_{UUA\_vs\_UUG},\\
&Ser\_{UCR\_vs\_AGY},\quad Ser\_{UCA\_vs\_UCG},\quad Thr\_{ACR\_vs\_ACY},\quad f3\_{stress}.
\end{aligned}
$$
For each held-out fold, the residualization model $Q\sim C$ was fitted only on training genes; held-out $Q$ residuals used those train-fit coefficients and train residual scaling. The ridge penalty was $\lambda=1.0$. The baseline control list was the intercept, log CDS sense-codon length, five train-fold PCA coordinates from the twenty amino-acid composition proportions $A,C,D,E,F,G,H,I,K,L,M,N,P,Q,R,S,T,V,W,Y$, GC3 from ordered CDS codons, mean pLDDT with a missingness indicator, and integrated PaxDb $\log_{10}$ baseline abundance. The five fold sizes were $447$, $475$, $444$, $451$, and $389$ genes. The complete-case count for the abundance-augmented model was $1654$, and the structural-order proxy had $4$ missing entries.

\paragraph{Held-out comparison.}
Let $M_0$ denote the controlled baseline and $M_1$ the same baseline with the residualized $Q$ block added. On held-out genes, the baseline model gave $R^2(M_0)=0.008108769333748045$ and $\mathrm{SSE}(M_0)=430.68597066859365$, while the augmented model gave $R^2(M_1)=0.005545435711181601$ and $\mathrm{SSE}(M_1)=431.7989876963185$. Thus
$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta R^2 &= -0.002563333622566444,\\
\Delta \log p_D &= -2.936811337346853,\\
\mathrm{residual\ code\ saving} &= -2.8467939090648606\ \mathrm{nats}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The encoding cost for the nine-coordinate residual block was $38.494680999067235$ nats, so the description-length change was
$$
\begin{aligned}
-2.8467939090648606 - 38.494680999067235 = -41.3414749081321\ \mathrm{nats}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The description-length gate is therefore closed for a positive interaction claim.

\paragraph{Null comparisons.}
The finite null checks agree with the held-out loss. Under the condition-sign-flip null, $B=300$ draws gave $\Delta\log p_D$ null $95\%$ value $-0.05373328681116618$ and $\Delta R^2$ null $95\%$ value $2.359796412121362\cdot 10^{-5}$; the observed values were below both. Under the matched-$Q$ permutation null, where $Q$ rows were permuted within length, amino-acid-PC1, GC3, mean-rank-abundance, and integrated-baseline bins, $B=300$ draws gave null $95\%$ values $3.7150577222696484$ for $\Delta\log p_D$ and $0.003501567522117832$ for $\Delta R^2$; the observed values again failed both gates. Under synonymous recoding, $B=300$ draws gave null $95\%$ values $0.011353124037214002$ for $\Delta\log p_D$ and $0.00028102266970064793$ for $\Delta R^2$. The synonymous-recoding diagnostics preserved per-amino-acid GC3-ending counts exactly, with mean absolute GC3 error $0.0$ and p95 absolute GC3 error $0.0$; the mean relative ENC-proxy error was $0.09042494844341317$ and the p95 relative ENC-proxy error was $0.4059574704858408$. The replicate-placebo null was unavailable because the SD/YEPD wrappers expose endpoint condition abundance rather than same-condition replicate abundance columns.

\paragraph{Side checks and verdict.}
The signed-only main target is not rescued by auxiliary targets. The absolute-delta side check had $\Delta\log p_D=-3.8682101284448436$, $\Delta R^2=-0.0030746447001754884$, and description-length change $-42.08857873395063$ nats. Adding endpoint mean rank as an extra control to the signed-delta target gave $\Delta\log p_D=-2.7093968327387756$, $\Delta R^2=-0.002354084610949325$, and description-length change $-41.10768232015411$ nats. A mean-$P$ side readout improved fit before paying the coordinate cost, with $\Delta\log p_D=7.4632216658321795$ and $\Delta R^2=0.0026536465383733$, but after the same $38.494680999067235$-nat coordinate charge its description-length change was still negative at $-30.29464161255981$ nats and it was not used for the main conclusion. The recorded finite verdict is therefore \texttt{no\_compressible\_q\_interaction}: the controlled $B^*_{Q6}$ residual block does not give a positive, null-clearing description-length survival for the signed SD--YEPD endpoint contrast in this panel.

\paragraph{Local scope of the result.}
This chapter reports only a bounded finite-row failure of the tested residual block to improve the declared controlled condition-contrast readout. It does not show that $B^*_{Q6}$ coordinates are absent from biology, and it does not test ribosome occupancy, translation efficiency, mature protein abundance as a mediated output, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, mechanism, phenotype, or universality. Any such statement would require its own layer-matched empirical contact and its own controlled finite statistic. In particular, modeled tAI or stAI weights, codon-count geometry, endpoint abundance contrast, and the notation $S^{QT}$ must not be treated as direct ribosome-profiling survival unless matched translation-readout and mRNA-control rows are actually joined and evaluated under the declared controls.
