\subsection{Condition-coupled Q6 residuals against SD/YEPD abundance contrast}\label{sec:bior_ality-q6-sd-yepd-interaction-powered}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite row object.}
This record tests whether residual synonymous coordinates from $B^{*}_{Q6}$ survive as a condition-coupled predictor of the signed SD/YEPD abundance contrast after the amino-acid quotient and ordinary sequence-composition controls have already been charged.  The finite carrier is a matched gene table with $2206$ tested genes.  The target is not a protein function, not a folded-state measurement, and not a universal codon rule.  It is the signed rank-normalized endpoint contrast
$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta P_g &= z_{\mathrm{rank}}(P_{SD,g}) - z_{\mathrm{rank}}(P_{YEPD,g}),\\
\bar{\Delta P} &= -1.1858399826713623\times 10^{-18},\\
\mathrm{sd}(\Delta P) &= 0.4434928347257967.
\end{aligned}
$$
The fitted question is whether the residual $Q$ coordinates improve the held-out description of this target beyond the baseline controls.  The baseline control block contains an intercept, log CDS sense-codon length, five train-fold PCA coordinates from the $20$ amino-acid composition proportions, GC3 from ordered CDS codons, a mean pLDDT structural-order proxy with missingness indicator, and an integrated PaxDb log10 baseline abundance covariate.  The fold sizes are $447$, $475$, $444$, $451$, and $389$ genes, and the structural-order proxy is missing for $4$ rows.  The more restricted complete-case count for the auxiliary $m2$ surface is $1654$.

\paragraph{Residual coordinates and train-only fitting.}
The tested residual block has nine coordinates: $K\_AAA$, $Arg\_AGR$, $Ile\_AUA$, $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$, $Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG$, $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$, $Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG$, $Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY$, and $f3\_stress$.  For each fold, $Q\sim C$ is fit only on the training genes; held-out genes receive residual coordinates from the training-fit coefficients and training residual scaling.  The ridge parameter is $\lambda=1.0$, and the charged encoding cost for the residual block is $38.494680999067235$ nats.  Thus the relevant comparison is not whether some codon coordinate has a descriptive name, but whether these nine train-residualized coordinates pay their own complexity cost on held-out SD/YEPD rows.

\paragraph{Held-out result.}
The main held-out comparison is negative.  With the baseline model $m0$ and the residual-coordinate model $m1$, the observed values are
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{m0} &= 0.008108769333748045,\\
R^2_{m1} &= 0.005545435711181601,\\
\Delta R^2 &= -0.002563333622566444,\\
\Delta \log p_D &= -2.936811337346853,\\
\mathrm{SSE}_{m0} &= 430.68597066859365,\\
\mathrm{SSE}_{m1} &= 431.7989876963185.
\end{aligned}
$$
The residual block therefore worsens the held-out fit instead of improving it.  The residual code saving is $-2.8467939090648606$ nats before the coordinate cost is paid, and the final description-length drop is
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{DL\ drop} &= -41.3414749081321\ \mathrm{nats},\\
\mathrm{charged\ cost} &= 38.494680999067235\ \mathrm{nats},\\
\mathrm{gate} &= \mathrm{failed}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The chapter-level verdict is therefore $\mathrm{no\_compressible\_q\_interaction}$: this finite panel does not support a positive $B^{*}_{Q6}$ condition interaction for the signed SD/YEPD endpoint contrast.

\paragraph{Null comparisons.}
The negative call is not rescued by the available null panels.  Three publishable null families were specified with target depth $1000$, and $300$ draws were available for each reported family.  For the condition-sign-flip null, the $95$th-percentile thresholds were $\Delta\log p_D=-0.05373328681116618$ and $\Delta R^2=0.00002359796412121362$; the observed held-out values are below both.  For the matched-$Q$ permutation null, the thresholds were $\Delta\log p_D=3.7150577222696484$ and $\Delta R^2=0.003501567522117832$, again above the observed negative result.  For the synonymous-recoding null, the thresholds were $\Delta\log p_D=0.011353124037214002$ and $\Delta R^2=0.00028102266970064793$, also not met.  The synonymous recodings preserved per-amino-acid GC3-ending counts exactly, with mean absolute GC3 error $0.0$ and $95$th-percentile absolute GC3 error $0.0$; the monitored ENC-proxy relative error had mean $0.09042494844341317$ and $95$th percentile $0.4059574704858408$.  The replicate-placebo family is unavailable here because the SD/YEPD wrappers expose endpoint condition abundance only, not same-condition replicate abundance columns.

\paragraph{Anti-vacuity checks.}
The signed target is the declared main surface.  Side checks confirm that a different response can show weak descriptive movement without authorizing a change of conclusion.  On the mean-abundance side surface, $R^2$ increases from $0.6417254262714966$ to $0.64437907280987$, with $\Delta R^2=0.0026536465383733$, $\Delta\log p_D=7.4632216658321795$, residual code saving $8.200039386507424$ nats, and final description-length drop $-30.29464161255981$ nats after the same cost.  On the absolute-delta side surface, the result is negative: $\Delta R^2=-0.0030746447001754884$, $\Delta\log p_D=-3.8682101284448436$, and final description-length drop $-42.08857873395063$ nats.  With endpoint mean-rank added as an extra control on the signed target, the result remains negative: $\Delta R^2=-0.002354084610949325$, $\Delta\log p_D=-2.7093968327387756$, and final description-length drop $-41.10768232015411$ nats.  These side surfaces are not used for the main conclusion; they only show that the negative signed-interaction result is a specific finite-row finding rather than an empty modeling exercise.
