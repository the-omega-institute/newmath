\subsection{Condition-Coupled Execution Refusal for the $B^*_{Q6}$ SD/YEPD Translation-Linked Surface}
\label{sec:b-star-q6-sd-yepd-condition-coupled-execution}

\paragraph{Finite carrier.}
This record tests whether the residual synonymous-coordinate surface $B^*_{Q6}$ gains a condition-coupled execution signal when the response is the signed endpoint contrast between SD and YEPD protein-abundance ranks.  The finite table has $2206$ gene rows.  Its response is
$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta P_g = z_{\operatorname{rank}}(P_{SD,g}) - z_{\operatorname{rank}}(P_{YEPD,g}),
\end{aligned}
$$
with empirical mean $-1.1858399826713623\times 10^{-18}$ and standard deviation $0.4434928347257967$.  The tested residual-coordinate block contains nine train-residualized coordinates: $K\_AAA$, $Arg\_AGR$, $Ile\_AUA$, $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$, $Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG$, $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$, $Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG$, $Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY$, and $f3\_stress$.  Residualization is fold-local: on each split, $Q\sim C$ is fit only on training genes, and held-out residual coordinates are scaled by the training residual scale.  The ridge cost used for the coordinate block is $\lambda=1.0$, and the encoded-coordinate penalty is $38.494680999067235$ nats.

\paragraph{Controls and row geometry.}
The baseline design includes an intercept, log CDS sense-codon length, five train-fold principal-component coordinates from the twenty amino-acid composition proportions $A,C,D,E,F,G,H,I,K,L,M,N,P,Q,R,S,T,V,W,Y$, GC3 computed from ordered CDS codons, a mean-pLDDT structural-order proxy with a missingness indicator, and integrated PaxDb log10 baseline abundance.  The held-out fold sizes are $447$, $475$, $444$, $451$, and $389$, summing to $2206$ tested genes.  The complete-case size for the structural-order and abundance-augmented block is $1654$, with $4$ missing structural-order entries.  These controls make the experiment a finite conditional-compression test, not a free association between codon residuals and a downstream biological label.

\paragraph{Held-out compression result.}
Let $M_0$ denote the controlled baseline and $M_1$ the same baseline augmented by the residual-coordinate block.  On held-out gene folds, the augmented model does not improve the signed SD/YEPD endpoint contrast.  The observed values are
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2(M_0) &= 0.008108769333748045,\\
R^2(M_1) &= 0.005545435711181601,\\
\Delta R^2 &= -0.002563333622566444,\\
\Delta \log pD &= -2.936811337346853.
\end{aligned}
$$
The sum of squared errors moves in the wrong direction, from $430.68597066859365$ under $M_0$ to $431.7989876963185$ under $M_1$.  The residual block saves only $-2.8467939090648606$ nats before its coding charge, so after the $38.494680999067235$-nat coordinate penalty the description-length drop is $-41.3414749081321$ nats.  The description-length gate is therefore false, and the finite-row verdict is $\operatorname{no\_compressible\_q\_interaction}$.

\paragraph{Null comparisons.}
The refusal is not produced by accepting a weak positive effect below threshold; the observed statistics fail all applicable positive gates.  Under the condition-sign-flip null, using $300$ realized draws toward a publishable target of $1000$, the null $95$ percent thresholds are $\Delta\log pD=-0.05373328681116618$, $\Delta R^2=2.359796412121362\times 10^{-5}$, and description-length drop $-38.4687437759017$ nats, while the largest seen null improvements are $3.346183710458263$ in $\Delta\log pD$ and $0.003115206933151926$ in $\Delta R^2$.  Under matched $Q$ permutation within length, amino-acid-PC1, GC3, mean-rank-abundance, and integrated-baseline bins, the corresponding $95$ percent thresholds are $3.7150577222696484$, $0.003501567522117832$, and $-34.593988916260244$ nats, with maxima $9.754186934356767$ and $0.008579527265972553$.  Under synonymous recoding, preserving amino-acid sequence and per-amino-acid GC3-ending counts exactly, the thresholds are $0.011353124037214002$, $0.00028102266970064793$, and $-38.18213471025496$ nats, with maxima $6.24877932309505$ and $0.0058455213683357155$.  The synonymous-recoding diagnostics report mean absolute GC3 error $0.0$, $95$ percent absolute GC3 error $0.0$, mean relative ENC-proxy error $0.09042494844341317$, and $95$ percent relative ENC-proxy error $0.4059574704858408$.  The replicate-placebo null is unavailable because the SD/YEPD wrappers expose endpoint condition abundance, not same-condition replicate abundance columns.

\paragraph{Anti-vacuity checks.}
The signed endpoint contrast is the main target; side readouts are explicitly not promoted to the conclusion.  For an absolute-delta-only side analysis, the augmented block again worsens held-out fit: $\Delta\log pD=-3.8682101284448436$, $\Delta R^2=-0.0030746447001754884$, residual code saving $-3.5938977348833916$ nats, and description-length drop $-42.08857873395063$ nats.  For the endpoint-mean-rank side analysis, the block has a small apparent held-out gain, with $\Delta\log pD=7.4632216658321795$ and $\Delta R^2=0.0026536465383733$, but the residual code saving $8.200039386507424$ nats remains far below the same $38.494680999067235$-nat encoding charge, giving a net description-length drop of $-30.29464161255981$ nats.  With endpoint mean rank added as an extra control for the signed target, the effect again reverses: $\Delta\log pD=-2.7093968327387756$, $\Delta R^2=-0.002354084610949325$, residual code saving $-2.6130013210868746$ nats, and description-length drop $-41.10768232015411$ nats.  Absolute-delta rank, mean rank, and GO, stress, high-$P$, or low-$P$ subgroup analyses are therefore side evidence only and are not used for the main conclusion.

\paragraph{Local interpretation.}
The finite result is a negative condition-coupled execution call: this $B^*_{Q6}$ residual block does not survive as a compressive descriptor of the SD/YEPD signed endpoint contrast after the declared composition, sequence, structural-proxy, and abundance controls.  It remains a bounded coordinate-and-readout test on $2206$ rows, not evidence for a translation-realization law.  In particular, no positive translation survival, ribosome mechanism, protein mediation, folding conclusion, physical admissibility claim, molecular function, system phenotype, or universal biological rule follows from this experiment.  Any such higher-layer statement would require its own matched contact and its own finite statistic at that layer; it is not licensed by the failed compression gate, by the coordinate names, or by the presence of codon-adaptation, ribosome-profiling, mRNA, or protein-abundance contact routes.
