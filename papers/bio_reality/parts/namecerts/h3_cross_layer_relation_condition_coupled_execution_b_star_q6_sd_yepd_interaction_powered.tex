\subsection{Condition-coupled B*\_Q6 residual coordinates do not survive the SD/YEPD translation-proxy boundary}\label{sec:bioreality-q6-sd-yepd-interaction-powered}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite panel.}
This record tests whether the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates carry a condition-coupled signal across the SD/YEPD boundary once the comparison is forced through a controlled finite row design.  The analyzed panel contains $2206$ genes.  The response is the signed rank-normalized endpoint contrast
$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta P_g = z_{\mathrm{rank}}(P_{\mathrm{SD}}) - z_{\mathrm{rank}}(P_{\mathrm{YEPD}}),
\end{aligned}
$$
with empirical mean $-1.1858399826713623\cdot 10^{-18}$ and standard deviation $0.4434928347257967$.  The held-out folds have sizes $447$, $475$, $444$, $451$, and $389$, totaling $2206$ tested rows.  The complete-case count for the structural-order-augmented control surface is $1654$, with $4$ rows missing the mean-pLDDT proxy.

\paragraph{Coordinates and controls.}
The finite residual coordinate block uses the nine train-fold residualized coordinates $K\_AAA$, $Arg\_AGR$, $Ile\_AUA$, $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$, $Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG$, $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$, $Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG$, $Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY$, and $f3\_stress$.  For each fold, the coordinate matrix is residualized by fitting $Q\sim C$ on the training genes only; held-out residual coordinates use the training coefficients and the training residual scale.  The ridge parameter is $\lambda=1.0$.  The control matrix contains an intercept, log CDS sense-codon length, five training-fold principal coordinates from the twenty amino-acid composition proportions $A,C,D,E,F,G,H,I,K,L,M,N,P,Q,R,S,T,V,W,Y$, GC3 from ordered CDS codons, a mean-pLDDT structural-order proxy with a missingness indicator, and an integrated PaxDb log10 baseline abundance term.  The abundance wrapper has dataset-level coverage and score metadata, but no per-gene reliability score.

\paragraph{Held-out comparison.}
The baseline model $M_0$ achieves held-out $R^2=0.008108769333748045$ with $SSE=430.68597066859365$.  The coordinate-augmented model $M_1$ achieves held-out $R^2=0.005545435711181601$ with $SSE=431.7989876963185$.  Thus the finite-row contrast is negative rather than surviving:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta R^2 &= -0.002563333622566444,\\
\Delta \log p_D &= -2.936811337346853,\\
\mathrm{residual\ code\ saving} &= -2.8467939090648606\ \mathrm{nats}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The coordinate encoding cost is $38.494680999067235$ nats, giving a description-length change of $-41.3414749081321$ nats.  The description-length gate is therefore not passed.  In this finite design, the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates do not improve the signed SD/YEPD endpoint contrast after the stated controls; they increase rather than reduce the held-out error.

\paragraph{Null comparisons.}
The positive gate also fails against all applicable null families.  Under the condition-sign-flip null with $300$ realized draws, the null95 thresholds are $\Delta\log p_D=-0.05373328681116618$, $\Delta R^2=2.359796412121362\cdot 10^{-5}$, and description-length change $-38.4687437759017$ nats; the largest observed null values were $\Delta\log p_D=3.346183710458263$ and $\Delta R^2=0.003115206933151926$.  Under matched coordinate permutation within length, amino-acid-PC1, GC3, mean-rank-abundance, and integrated-baseline bins, the corresponding null95 thresholds are $3.7150577222696484$, $0.003501567522117832$, and $-34.593988916260244$ nats, with maxima $9.754186934356767$ and $0.008579527265972553$.  Under synonymous recoding, preserving amino-acid sequence and exact per-amino-acid GC3-ending counts, the null95 thresholds are $0.011353124037214002$, $0.00028102266970064793$, and $-38.18213471025496$ nats, with maxima $6.24877932309505$ and $0.0058455213683357155$.  The synonymous-recoding diagnostics report mean absolute GC3 error $0.0$, mean relative ENC-proxy error $0.09042494844341317$, p95 absolute GC3 error $0.0$, and p95 relative ENC-proxy error $0.4059574704858408$.  The replicate-placebo arm is unavailable because the SD/YEPD wrappers expose endpoint condition abundance only, not same-condition replicate abundance columns.

\paragraph{Anti-vacuity checks.}
The signed rank-normalized $\Delta P$ target is the main target.  Two side analyses verify that the negative signed result is not merely an artifact of choosing an easy target.  For the absolute-delta-only side evidence, $M_0$ has $R^2=0.05789998072242308$, $M_1$ has $R^2=0.054825336022247595$, $\Delta R^2=-0.0030746447001754884$, $\Delta\log p_D=-3.8682101284448436$, residual code saving $-3.5938977348833916$ nats, and description-length change $-42.08857873395063$ nats.  With endpoint mean rank added as an extra control, the signed-delta side evidence has $R^2$ values $0.007470487595869213$ and $0.005116402984919888$, $\Delta R^2=-0.002354084610949325$, $\Delta\log p_D=-2.7093968327387756$, residual code saving $-2.6130013210868746$ nats, and description-length change $-41.10768232015411$ nats.  The mean-rank side evidence is positive as a separate descriptive check, with $\Delta\log p_D=7.4632216658321795$ and $\Delta R^2=0.0026536465383733$, but it is not used for the main conclusion; neither absolute-delta rank, mean rank, nor GO, stress, high-$P$, or low-$P$ subgroup summaries are promoted into the signed interaction call.

\paragraph{Local verdict.}
The finite-row verdict is $\mathrm{no\_compressible\_q\_interaction}$.  The run identifier is $acc5921d7c06$, and the experiment identifier is $b\_star\_q6\_condition\_interaction\_sd\_yepd\_powered$.  The result is a bounded negative readback: in this controlled SD/YEPD endpoint panel, the tested $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates do not survive as a compressive condition-interaction signal.  The conclusion is limited to the stated signed rank-normalized endpoint contrast and its declared controls.  It does not establish translation efficiency, ribosome occupancy, protein abundance, folded structure, physical admissibility, molecular function, mechanism, phenotype, or universality.  Any such layer would require its own matched measurement rows and its own controlled statistic; the present finite table supplies only the negative condition-coupled residual-coordinate result described above.
