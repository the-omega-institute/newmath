\subsection{Condition-coupled B$^*_{Q6}$ residuals do not survive the SD--YEPD powered interaction test}\label{subsec:b-star-q6-sd-yepd-interaction-powered}\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite panel and tested coordinate.} This record concerns a finite, gene-indexed test of whether B$^*_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates carry a condition-coupled signal for the SD--YEPD protein-abundance contrast. The response row was not a folded-structure, function, phenotype, or mechanism assay. It was the signed endpoint contrast
$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta P_g &= z_{\mathrm{rank}}(P_{\mathrm{SD},g}) - z_{\mathrm{rank}}(P_{\mathrm{YEPD},g}),\\
 n &= 2206,\\
 \overline{\Delta P} &= -1.1858399826713623\times 10^{-18},\\
 \operatorname{sd}(\Delta P) &= 0.4434928347257967.
\end{aligned}
$$
The run identifier was \texttt{acc5921d7c06}, and the finite experiment was \texttt{b\_star\_q6\_condition\_interaction\_sd\_yepd\_powered}, verified at \texttt{2026-06-26T17:54:59+00:00}. The tested residual block had nine train-fold-residualized coordinates:
$$
\begin{aligned}
Q = (&K\_AAA,\ Arg\_AGR,\ Ile\_AUA,\ Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR,\ Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG,\\
&Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY,\ Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG,\ Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY,\ f3\_stress).
\end{aligned}
$$
For every held-out fold, the residualization fit $Q\sim C$ was learned only on the training genes; held-out residual coordinates used the train-fit coefficients and train residual scaling. The ridge penalty was $\lambda=1.0$ for this residualization stage.

\paragraph{Control surface.} The baseline model used an intercept, log CDS sense-codon length, five train-fold PCA coordinates from the twenty amino-acid composition proportions $A,C,D,E,F,G,H,I,K,L,M,N,P,Q,R,S,T,V,W,Y$, GC3 from ordered CDS codons, a mean-pLDDT structural-order proxy with a missingness indicator, and integrated PaxDb log10 baseline abundance. The fold sizes were
$$
\begin{aligned}
(n_0,n_1,n_2,n_3,n_4)=(447,475,444,451,389).
\end{aligned}
$$
The complete-case count for the abundance-plus-structure control surface was $1654$, and the structural-order proxy was missing for $4$ genes. These controls make the test a bounded same-panel compression test: it asks whether the B$^*_{Q6}$ residual block improves held-out description of the signed SD--YEPD endpoint contrast after the declared covariates have already been allowed to explain it.

\paragraph{Held-out compression result.} The main held-out comparison placed the baseline model $M_0$ against the residual-augmented model $M_1$. On $2206$ test genes, the augmented model did not improve the signed target:
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2(M_0) &= 0.008108769333748045,\\
R^2(M_1) &= 0.005545435711181601,\\
\Delta R^2 &= -0.002563333622566444,\\
\Delta \log \operatorname{pd} &= -2.936811337346853,\\
\operatorname{SSE}(M_0) &= 430.68597066859365,\\
\operatorname{SSE}(M_1) &= 431.7989876963185.
\end{aligned}
$$
The residual code saving was negative after charging the coordinate encoding cost:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\operatorname{saving}_{Q} &= -2.8467939090648606\ \mathrm{nats},\\
\operatorname{cost}_{Q,\lambda} &= 38.494680999067235\ \mathrm{nats},\\
\operatorname{description\ length\ drop} &= -41.3414749081321\ \mathrm{nats}.
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the finite row does not support a positive condition-coupled interaction entry. The augmented model is worse on held-out $R^2$, worse on held-out log predictive density, and worse after the explicit coordinate-encoding charge.

\paragraph{Null barriers.} Three applicable null families were evaluated with $300$ realized null samples each, with a publishable target of $1000$ samples. The condition-sign-flip null had a $95\%$ threshold of $-0.05373328681116618$ for $\Delta\log\operatorname{pd}$ and $2.359796412121362\times 10^{-5}$ for $\Delta R^2$; the observed main values were below both. The matched-$Q$ permutation null, binned by length, amino-acid PC1, GC3, mean-rank abundance, and integrated baseline, had thresholds
$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta\log\operatorname{pd}_{0.95} &= 3.7150577222696484,\\
\Delta R^2_{0.95} &= 0.003501567522117832,\\
\Delta L_{0.95} &= -34.593988916260244\ \mathrm{nats}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The synonymous-recoding null, preserving amino-acid sequence and per-amino-acid GC3-ending counts exactly, had thresholds
$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta\log\operatorname{pd}_{0.95} &= 0.011353124037214002,\\
\Delta R^2_{0.95} &= 0.00028102266970064793,\\
\Delta L_{0.95} &= -38.18213471025496\ \mathrm{nats}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The synonymous-recoding diagnostics reported mean absolute GC3 error $0.0$, $95\%$ absolute GC3 error $0.0$, mean relative ENC-proxy error $0.09042494844341317$, and $95\%$ relative ENC-proxy error $0.4059574704858408$. A replicate-placebo null was unavailable because the SD/YEPD wrappers exposed endpoint condition abundance only, not same-condition replicate abundance columns.

\paragraph{Verdict.} The positive gate failed in both required senses: the description-length drop was not positive, and the held-out $\Delta R^2$ and $\Delta\log\operatorname{pd}$ were not above all applicable null $95\%$ thresholds. The finite-row verdict is therefore
$$
\begin{aligned}
\operatorname{verdict} = \mathrm{no\_compressible\_q\_interaction}.
\end{aligned}
$$
This is a negative powered interaction result for the signed rank-normalized SD--YEPD endpoint contrast. It should not be rewritten as evidence that B$^*_{Q6}$ is globally biologically irrelevant; it only says that this controlled finite panel did not find a compressive residual-coordinate contribution to this particular endpoint contrast.

\paragraph{Anti-vacuity side checks.} Two side rows help localize the negative result. The absolute-$\Delta P$ side target was also negative: $\Delta R^2=-0.0030746447001754884$, $\Delta\log\operatorname{pd}=-3.8682101284448436$, residual code saving $-3.5938977348833916$ nats, and description-length drop $-42.08857873395063$ nats on $2206$ test genes. Adding endpoint mean rank as an extra control to the signed target likewise stayed negative: $\Delta R^2=-0.002354084610949325$, $\Delta\log\operatorname{pd}=-2.7093968327387756$, residual code saving $-2.6130013210868746$ nats, and description-length drop $-41.10768232015411$ nats. A mean-abundance side target showed a small positive held-out fit increment, with $\Delta R^2=0.0026536465383733$ and $\Delta\log\operatorname{pd}=7.4632216658321795$, but its charged description-length drop remained negative at $-30.29464161255981$ nats; it is therefore not used as the main conclusion.

\paragraph{Local scope boundary.} The only local readout is a controlled finite-row compression test for a signed SD--YEPD endpoint contrast. It does not establish translation realization, ribosome occupancy, protein mediation, folding, structural order, physical admissibility, molecular function, biological mechanism, organismal phenotype, causation, adaptation, or a universal rule. Any such statement would require its own layer-matched contact and its own controlled statistic. In particular, modeled tAI or stAI rows are not ribosome-profiling rows, mRNA abundance is a control rather than a translation output, and an endpoint protein-abundance contrast is not a folding, function, or mechanism assay.
