\subsection{Condition-coupled $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates against the SD--YEPD protein-abundance contrast}\label{sec:bioreality-q6-sd-yepd-condition-interaction}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite row object.}
The tested object is a finite CDS-level matrix whose rows are yeast genes with matched endpoint protein-abundance measurements in SD and YEPD conditions. The response coordinate is the signed rank-normalized contrast
$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta P_g &= z_{\mathrm{rank}}(P_{\mathrm{SD},g}) - z_{\mathrm{rank}}(P_{\mathrm{YEPD},g}),
\end{aligned}
$$
computed for $2206$ genes. The realized target has empirical mean $-1.1858399826713623\times 10^{-18}$ and standard deviation $0.4434928347257967$. The residual-coordinate side uses nine train-fold residualized coordinates,
$$
\begin{aligned}
Q &= \{\mathrm{K\_AAA},\mathrm{Arg\_AGR},\mathrm{Ile\_AUA},\mathrm{Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR},\mathrm{Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG},\\
&\qquad \mathrm{Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY},\mathrm{Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG},\mathrm{Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY},f_3\text{-}\mathrm{stress}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
For each held-out fold, the regression $Q\sim C$ is fit only on the training genes; held-out residual coordinates are then evaluated with the training coefficients and training residual scale. The ridge parameter for this residual-coordinate block is $\lambda=1.0$.

\paragraph{Controls and fold geometry.}
The control matrix contains an intercept, log CDS sense-codon length, five train-fold PCA coordinates from the twenty amino-acid composition proportions, GC3 from ordered CDS codons, mean pLDDT with a missingness indicator, and an integrated PaxDb log10 baseline abundance. The five held-out fold sizes are $447$, $475$, $444$, $451$, and $389$, summing to $2206$ test rows. The complete-case count for the abundance-linked model is $1654$, and the structural-order proxy is missing for $4$ rows. These controls make the test a bounded finite-row question about whether the residual codon coordinates add held-out description power beyond the declared covariates; they do not by themselves measure ribosome occupancy, translation efficiency, folding, function, or phenotype.

\paragraph{Held-out comparison.}
Let $M_0$ denote the controlled baseline and $M_1$ denote the baseline enlarged by the residual-coordinate block. On the signed SD--YEPD contrast, the held-out result is
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2(M_0)&=0.008108769333748045, & R^2(M_1)&=0.005545435711181601,\\
\Delta R^2&=-0.002563333622566444, & \Delta\log p_{\mathrm{heldout}}&=-2.936811337346853,\\
\mathrm{SSE}(M_0)&=430.68597066859365, & \mathrm{SSE}(M_1)&=431.7989876963185.
\end{aligned}
$$
The residual block therefore worsens the held-out fit. The residual code saving is $-2.8467939090648606$ nats, while the encoding charge for the residual-coordinate block is $38.494680999067235$ nats, giving a description-length change
$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta \mathrm{DL}=-41.3414749081321\ \mathrm{nats}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The positive gate is consequently closed: the description-length criterion fails, and the held-out $\Delta R^2$ and $\Delta\log p$ do not exceed the applicable null boundaries.

\paragraph{Null comparisons.}
Three finite null families were evaluated with $300$ realized draws each. Under condition sign-flip nulls, the $95\%$ thresholds were $\Delta\log p=-0.05373328681116618$ and $\Delta R^2=2.359796412121362\times 10^{-5}$, with maxima $3.346183710458263$ and $0.003115206933151926$ seen across the realized null draws. Under matched $Q$ permutation within length, amino-acid-PC1, GC3, mean-rank-abundance, and integrated-baseline bins, the $95\%$ thresholds were $\Delta\log p=3.7150577222696484$ and $\Delta R^2=0.003501567522117832$, with realized maxima $9.754186934356767$ and $0.008579527265972553$. Under synonymous recoding preserving amino-acid sequence and per-amino-acid GC3-ending counts exactly, the $95\%$ thresholds were $\Delta\log p=0.011353124037214002$ and $\Delta R^2=0.00028102266970064793$, with realized maxima $6.24877932309505$ and $0.0058455213683357155$. The synonymous-recoding diagnostics report mean absolute GC3 error $0.0$, mean relative ENC-proxy error $0.09042494844341317$, and $95$th percentile relative ENC-proxy error $0.4059574704858408$. The replicate-placebo null is unavailable because the SD and YEPD wrappers expose endpoint condition abundance rather than same-condition replicate abundance columns.

\paragraph{Anti-vacuity checks.}
The side checks show that the negative signed-contrast conclusion is not merely a consequence of every alternative target being uninformative. For the absolute-delta side target, the enlarged model has $\Delta R^2=-0.0030746447001754884$, $\Delta\log p=-3.8682101284448436$, and description-length change $-42.08857873395063$ nats. For the endpoint mean-abundance side target, the residual block gives $\Delta R^2=0.0026536465383733$ and $\Delta\log p=7.4632216658321795$, but after the same encoding charge its description-length change remains negative at $-30.29464161255981$ nats. With endpoint mean rank added as an extra control to the signed contrast, the result remains negative: $\Delta R^2=-0.002354084610949325$, $\Delta\log p=-2.7093968327387756$, and description-length change $-41.10768232015411$ nats. The main conclusion uses only the signed rank-normalized $\Delta P$ target; absolute-delta, mean-rank, and subgroup summaries are not promoted into the primary claim.

\paragraph{Verdict.}
The finite-row verdict for experiment run \texttt{acc5921d7c06} is \texttt{no\_compressible\_q\_interaction}. In this panel, the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual-coordinate block does not improve held-out description of the SD--YEPD signed protein-abundance contrast after the declared controls and null comparisons. The result is a controlled non-survival statement for this exact matrix and target: it says that the tested residual coordinates did not pass the positive compression gate here. It is not evidence that the coordinates are biologically irrelevant in general, and it is not a translation-efficiency result. In particular, no claim about translation realization, ribosome profiling, protein folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, mechanism, system phenotype, or universality follows from this run unless a separate layer-matched contact supplies the corresponding measured rows and a new controlled statistic.
