\subsection{Condition-coupled Q6 residuals do not survive the SD/YEPD protein-abundance interaction test}
\label{sec:bireality-q6-sd-yepd-interaction-powered}

\paragraph{Finite object.}
The record considered here is the finite crossed panel formed from the nine residualized $B^{*}_{Q6}$ coordinates against a signed condition-response readout derived from SD and YEPD endpoint protein-abundance ranks. The target row is not a protein-function row, a folding row, or a mechanistic ribosome row. It is the signed rank-normalized endpoint difference
$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta P_g &= z_{\operatorname{rank}}(P_{SD,g}) - z_{\operatorname{rank}}(P_{YEPD,g}),
\end{aligned}
$$
computed for $2206$ genes, with empirical mean $-1.1858399826713623\cdot 10^{-18}$ and standard deviation $0.4434928347257967$. The tested residual coordinate list is
$$
\begin{aligned}
Q=&\{K_{AAA},\operatorname{Arg}_{AGR},\operatorname{Ile}_{AUA},\operatorname{Leu}_{CUN\,vs\,UUR},\operatorname{Leu}_{UUA\,vs\,UUG},\\
&\operatorname{Ser}_{UCR\,vs\,AGY},\operatorname{Ser}_{UCA\,vs\,UCG},\operatorname{Thr}_{ACR\,vs\,ACY},f_{3\text{-}stress}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
For each held-out fold, the coordinates were residualized against the control design on training genes only, then evaluated on held-out rows using the training-fit coefficients and training residual scale, with ridge parameter $\lambda=1.0$ for the residualization step.

\paragraph{Control design.}
The baseline model used an intercept, log CDS sense-codon length, five train-fold PCA coordinates from the twenty amino-acid composition proportions, GC3 from ordered CDS codons, mean pLDDT with a missingness indicator, and integrated PaxDb log10 baseline abundance. The five fold sizes were $447$, $475$, $444$, $451$, and $389$, totaling $2206$ held-out evaluations. The complete-case count for the second-stage controlled design was $1654$, and only $4$ structural-order entries were missing. Thus the test is not a free comparison between codon residuals and protein abundance; it is a controlled held-out comparison asking whether the residual coordinate block adds predictive compression after the declared baseline terms have already been admitted.

\paragraph{Held-out statistic.}
Let $M_0$ denote the controlled baseline model and $M_1$ the model after adding the residualized $Q$ block. The observed held-out comparison was
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2(M_0)&=0.008108769333748045,\\
R^2(M_1)&=0.005545435711181601,\\
\Delta R^2&=-0.002563333622566444,\\
\Delta\log p_D&=-2.936811337346853.
\end{aligned}
$$
The held-out sum of squared error increased from $430.68597066859365$ to $431.7989876963185$. With a coordinate-block encoding cost of $38.494680999067235$ nats, the residual code saving was $-2.8467939090648606$ nats and the resulting description-length change was
$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta L&=-41.3414749081321\ \text{nats}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The sign is decisive for this finite panel: adding the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ block worsened held-out fit and failed to repay its encoding cost.

\paragraph{Null comparisons.}
Three active null families were evaluated with $300$ realized draws each. Under the per-gene deterministic sign-flip null, the observed statistic did not exceed the $95\%$ null thresholds: the null $95\%$ values were $\Delta\log p_D=-0.05373328681116618$ and $\Delta R^2=2.359796412121362\cdot 10^{-5}$. Under matched $Q$ permutation within length, amino-acid-PC1, GC3, mean-rank-abundance, and integrated-baseline bins, the corresponding null $95\%$ values were $3.7150577222696484$ and $0.003501567522117832$. Under synonymous recoding preserving amino-acid sequence and exact per-amino-acid GC3-ending counts, the null $95\%$ values were $0.011353124037214002$ and $0.00028102266970064793$. In that recoding family, the mean absolute GC3 error was $0.0$, the $95\%$ absolute GC3 error was $0.0$, the mean relative ENC-proxy error was $0.09042494844341317$, and the $95\%$ relative ENC-proxy error was $0.4059574704858408$. The replicate-placebo family was unavailable because the SD/YEPD wrappers expose endpoint condition abundance rather than same-condition replicate abundance columns.

\paragraph{Verdict.}
The finite-row result is a controlled non-survival record for condition-coupled execution in this SD/YEPD endpoint panel. The positive gate failed both required parts: the description-length drop was not positive, and the held-out $\Delta R^2$ and $\Delta\log p_D$ did not clear the applicable null thresholds. The recorded verdict is therefore
$$
\begin{aligned}
\operatorname{verdict}&=\operatorname{no\_compressible\_q\_interaction}.
\end{aligned}
$$
This does not say that the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates are biologically irrelevant, nor does it say that no codon-coordinate effect can exist in another design. It says only that, for this finite SD/YEPD matrix with the listed controls, residualized $Q6$ coordinates do not improve the held-out description of the signed endpoint protein-abundance contrast.

\paragraph{Side checks.}
The signed target is the main target. Auxiliary checks do not overturn the result. The absolute-difference side evidence had $\Delta R^2=-0.0030746447001754884$, $\Delta\log p_D=-3.8682101284448436$, residual code saving $-3.5938977348833916$ nats, and description-length change $-42.08857873395063$ nats. Adding endpoint mean-rank as an extra control to the signed target gave $\Delta R^2=-0.002354084610949325$, $\Delta\log p_D=-2.7093968327387756$, residual code saving $-2.6130013210868746$ nats, and description-length change $-41.10768232015411$ nats. A mean-abundance side target showed a small positive held-out association, with $\Delta R^2=0.0026536465383733$ and $\Delta\log p_D=7.4632216658321795$, but after the same coordinate-block cost its description-length change remained negative at $-30.29464161255981$ nats. It is therefore not used as the main conclusion.

\paragraph{Local boundary.}
The readout in this chapter is bounded to the finite controlled entry just described. No translation-realization claim, folding claim, physical-admissibility claim, molecular-function claim, mechanism claim, phenotype claim, or universal biological rule follows from this panel. A separate layer-matched contact would be required for any ribosome-profiling translation-efficiency survival statement, and still other contacts would be required for protein structure, function, physical admissibility, mechanism, phenotype, or universality. The present chapter records a negative controlled interaction result for $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates against the SD/YEPD signed endpoint abundance contrast, not a higher-layer biological promotion.
