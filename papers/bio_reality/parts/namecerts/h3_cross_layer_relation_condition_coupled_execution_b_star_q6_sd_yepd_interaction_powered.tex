\subsection{Condition-coupled B$^*_{Q6}$ residuals against the SD--YEPD contrast}
\label{sec:bioreality-q6-sd-yepd-condition-coupled-execution}

\paragraph{Finite row object.}
This record treats the subject as a finite controlled regression table, not as a free biological interpretation.  The rows are $2206$ matched coding-sequence entries with an endpoint contrast
$\Delta P_g = z_{\mathrm{rank}}(P_{\mathrm{SD}})-z_{\mathrm{rank}}(P_{\mathrm{YEPD}})$.
The target was centered to numerical mean $-1.1858399826713623\times 10^{-18}$ with standard deviation $0.4434928347257967$.  The residual coordinate block was the nine-coordinate train-only residualized list
$\{K\_AAA, Arg\_AGR, Ile\_AUA, Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR, Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG, Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY, Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG, Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY, f3\_stress\}$,
fit with ridge parameter $\lambda=1.0$ for the residualization step.  In each held-out fold, the map $Q\sim C$ was learned on training genes only; held-out residual coordinates used the training coefficients and the training residual scale.  The control matrix contained an intercept, log CDS sense-codon length, five train-fold principal components from the twenty amino-acid composition proportions $A,C,D,E,F,G,H,I,K,L,M,N,P,Q,R,S,T,V,W,Y$, GC3 from ordered CDS codons, a mean-pLDDT structural-order proxy with a missingness indicator, and an integrated PaxDb log$_{10}$ baseline abundance.  The fold sizes were $447,475,444,451,389$, the complete-case count for the second model was $1654$, and the structural-order proxy was missing in $4$ rows.

\paragraph{Controlled survival statistic.}
The intended positive reading would require the residual coordinate block to improve held-out description of the signed SD--YEPD endpoint after the declared controls and after null comparison.  The observed held-out comparison goes in the opposite direction.  For the primary signed rank-normalized endpoint, the baseline model had $R^2=0.008108769333748045$ and SSE $430.68597066859365$, while the residual-augmented model had $R^2=0.005545435711181601$ and SSE $431.7989876963185$.  Thus
$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta R^2 &= -0.002563333622566444,\\
\Delta\log p_{\mathrm{heldout}} &= -2.936811337346853,\\
\mathrm{residual\ code\ saving} &= -2.8467939090648606\ \mathrm{nats},\\
\mathrm{coordinate\ encoding\ cost} &= 38.494680999067235\ \mathrm{nats},\\
\mathrm{description\ length\ drop} &= -41.3414749081321\ \mathrm{nats}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The description-length gate is therefore false.  The result is not merely a small positive effect below threshold; the fitted residual block loses held-out explanatory value on the primary endpoint after accounting for its encoding cost.

\paragraph{Null comparisons.}
Three applicable null families were run with $300$ realized draws each, with $1000$ draws recorded as the publishable target.  Under the condition-sign-flip null, the $95$th-percentile thresholds were $\Delta\log p=-0.05373328681116618$, $\Delta R^2=0.00002359796412121362$, and description-length drop $-38.4687437759017$ nats; the largest sampled null values reached $\Delta\log p=3.346183710458263$ and $\Delta R^2=0.003115206933151926$.  Under the matched-$Q$ permutation null, the thresholds were $\Delta\log p=3.7150577222696484$, $\Delta R^2=0.003501567522117832$, and description-length drop $-34.593988916260244$ nats, with maxima $9.754186934356767$ and $0.008579527265972553$.  Under synonymous recoding with amino-acid sequence preserved and per-amino-acid GC3-ending counts preserved exactly, the thresholds were $\Delta\log p=0.011353124037214002$, $\Delta R^2=0.00028102266970064793$, and description-length drop $-38.18213471025496$ nats; its maxima were $6.24877932309505$ and $0.0058455213683357155$.  The synonymous-recoding diagnostics had mean absolute GC3 error $0.0$, $95$th-percentile absolute GC3 error $0.0$, mean relative ENC-proxy error $0.09042494844341317$, and $95$th-percentile relative ENC-proxy error $0.4059574704858408$.  The replicate-placebo family was unavailable because the SD/YEPD wrappers expose endpoint condition abundance only, not same-condition replicate abundance columns.

\paragraph{Verdict.}
The finite-row verdict is $\mathrm{no\_compressible\_q\_interaction}$.  The positive gate failed both components: the description-length drop was not positive, and the held-out $\Delta R^2$ and $\Delta\log p$ did not exceed all applicable null $95$th-percentile boundaries.  Each recorded per-null joint pass flag is false.  This means that, for this SD--YEPD endpoint contrast and this controlled design, the B$^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinate block does not survive as a positive condition-coupled execution signal.  The bounded readback is a negative controlled-survival entry for the declared finite table, not a biological absence theorem for codon usage in general.

\paragraph{Anti-vacuity checks.}
The signed-only target is the main endpoint.  Several side checks prevent the chapter from being read as an artifact of a completely inert pipeline.  On the mean-$P$ side evidence, the residual block increased held-out fit slightly, with $\Delta\log p=7.4632216658321795$, $\Delta R^2=0.0026536465383733$, $R^2$ moving from $0.6417254262714966$ to $0.64437907280987$, and residual code saving $8.200039386507424$ nats; after the same coordinate cost, its description-length drop remained negative at $-30.29464161255981$ nats.  On the absolute-delta side evidence, the direction was negative: $\Delta\log p=-3.8682101284448436$, $\Delta R^2=-0.0030746447001754884$, and description-length drop $-42.08857873395063$ nats.  With endpoint mean rank added as an extra control for the signed delta, the result also remained negative: $\Delta\log p=-2.7093968327387756$, $\Delta R^2=-0.002354084610949325$, and description-length drop $-41.10768232015411$ nats.  The abs-delta rank, mean-rank, GO, stress, high-$P$, and low-$P$ subgroup readings are not used for the main conclusion.

\paragraph{Layer boundary.}
The chapter closes only the local finite-row question: whether the residual coordinate block compresses the signed SD--YEPD endpoint contrast under the declared controls and nulls.  It does not establish translation-efficiency survival, ribosome occupancy, protein mediation, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, mechanism, phenotype, or universality.  A translation claim would require a separate measured or explicitly bounded translation readout, such as matched ribosome-profiling translation efficiency with same-row mRNA control, joined to the residual coordinates under an adequate rank design.  A protein, structure, function, or phenotype claim would require its own layer-matched contact and its own controlled statistic.  In the present finite table, the correct scientific statement is a local non-survival result for the SD--YEPD signed endpoint, with no promotion beyond that readout.
