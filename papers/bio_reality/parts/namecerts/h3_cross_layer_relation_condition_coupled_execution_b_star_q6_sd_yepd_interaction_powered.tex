\subsection{Condition-coupled $B^*_{Q6}$ residuals do not compress the SD--YEPD protein-abundance contrast}
\label{sec:bioreality-q6-sd-yepd-condition-coupled-execution}

\paragraph{Finite row object.}
This record tests whether a residual synonymous-coordinate panel for $B^*_{Q6}$ contributes held-out information about a condition-coupled abundance contrast after the usual amino-acid and sequence-composition baselines have already been paid for.  The row object is a finite gene-indexed matrix, not a statement about a protein mechanism.  Its response column is
$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta P_g = z_{\mathrm{rank}}(P_{\mathrm{SD},g}) - z_{\mathrm{rank}}(P_{\mathrm{YEPD},g}),
\end{aligned}
$$
computed on $2206$ genes, with empirical mean $-1.1858399826713623\times 10^{-18}$ and standard deviation $0.4434928347257967$.  The fitted residual-coordinate block has nine columns,
$$
\begin{aligned}
Q = (&K\_AAA,\ Arg\_AGR,\ Ile\_AUA,\ Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR,\ Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG,\\
&Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY,\ Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG,\ Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY,\ f3\_stress),
\end{aligned}
$$
where each fold residualizes $Q$ against the control block on training genes only, applies the train-fit coefficients to held-out genes, and scales by the training residual scale.  The ridge value for this residualization is $\lambda=1.0$.  The five held-out folds have sizes $447$, $475$, $444$, $451$, and $389$.

\paragraph{Control surface.}
The baseline model includes an intercept, log CDS sense-codon length, five train-fold PCA coordinates from the twenty amino-acid composition proportions $A,C,D,E,F,G,H,I,K,L,M,N,P,Q,R,S,T,V,W,Y$, GC3 from ordered CDS codons, mean pLDDT with a missingness indicator, and integrated PaxDb log10 baseline abundance.  The structural-order proxy is nearly complete in this row set: only $4$ genes lack the structural-order proxy.  The complete-case count for the model using the fuller structural/protein-abundance surface is $1654$.  Thus the question tested here is deliberately narrow: after these covariates have been fixed, does the $B^*_{Q6}$ residual block improve held-out description of the SD--YEPD signed contrast?

\paragraph{Held-out result.}
The observed comparison is negative.  On all $2206$ held-out genes, the baseline model has $R^2=0.008108769333748045$ and SSE $430.68597066859365$, while the model with the residual-coordinate block has $R^2=0.005545435711181601$ and SSE $431.7989876963185$.  The held-out changes are
$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta R^2 &= -0.002563333622566444,\\
\Delta \log p_{\mathrm{heldout}} &= -2.936811337346853.
\end{aligned}
$$
The residual code saving is $-2.8467939090648606$ nats.  After charging the coordinate encoding cost $38.494680999067235$ nats, the description-length change is
$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta L = -41.3414749081321\ \mathrm{nats},
\end{aligned}
$$
so the description-length gate is not passed.  In this finite row sense, the residual-coordinate block is not a compressive explanation of the signed SD--YEPD contrast.

\paragraph{Null comparison.}
The failure is also stable against the three applicable null families.  The condition-sign-flip null used $300$ realized draws, with publishable target $1000$; its $95\%$ thresholds were $\Delta \log p=-0.05373328681116618$ and $\Delta R^2=2.359796412121362\times 10^{-5}$.  The matched-$Q$ permutation null used $300$ realized draws, again with publishable target $1000$; its $95\%$ thresholds were $\Delta \log p=3.7150577222696484$ and $\Delta R^2=0.003501567522117832$.  The synonymous-recoding null used $300$ realized draws, with publishable target $1000$; its $95\%$ thresholds were $\Delta \log p=0.011353124037214002$ and $\Delta R^2=0.00028102266970064793$.  The actual held-out pair $(-2.936811337346853,-0.002563333622566444)$ is below every applicable joint threshold.  The replicate-placebo null is unavailable here because the SD and YEPD wrappers expose endpoint condition abundance columns rather than same-condition replicate abundance columns.

\paragraph{Side checks.}
Two anti-vacuity side surfaces confirm that the negative result is specific to the declared signed target rather than to a complete absence of measurable signal in the surrounding data.  The absolute-delta-only side surface is also negative, with $\Delta \log p=-3.8682101284448436$, $\Delta R^2=-0.0030746447001754884$, residual code saving $-3.5938977348833916$ nats, and description-length change $-42.08857873395063$ nats.  The signed-delta surface with endpoint mean-rank abundance added as an extra control is negative as well, with $\Delta \log p=-2.7093968327387756$, $\Delta R^2=-0.002354084610949325$, residual code saving $-2.6130013210868746$ nats, and description-length change $-41.10768232015411$ nats.  By contrast, the endpoint mean-abundance side surface has $\Delta \log p=7.4632216658321795$ and $\Delta R^2=0.0026536465383733$, but after the same coordinate encoding cost its description-length change remains negative at $-30.29464161255981$ nats.  That side surface is not used for the main conclusion.

\paragraph{Verdict.}
The finite-row verdict is
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{verdict} = \mathrm{no\_compressible\_q\_interaction}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The positive gate fails both parts: the description-length drop is not positive, and the held-out $\Delta R^2$ and $\Delta\log p$ do not exceed all applicable null $95\%$ thresholds.  The experiment therefore records a bounded refusal: these nine $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinates do not survive as a controlled predictor of the signed SD--YEPD endpoint abundance contrast in this finite panel.

\paragraph{Local interpretation boundary.}
This chapter does not establish a translation-efficiency survivor, because the target row is the signed rank-normalized endpoint abundance contrast rather than a matched ribosome-profiling TE readout.  It also does not establish protein mediation, folding, physical admissibility, biological function, mechanism, phenotype, or a universal rule.  Such claims would require separate layer-matched contacts and their own finite statistics.  The only licensed readback is the negative controlled-compression result for the declared $B^*_{Q6}$ residual block against the SD--YEPD condition-coupled abundance contrast under the listed covariates and nulls.
