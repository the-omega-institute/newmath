\subsection{Condition-coupled B$^*_{Q6}$ residuals against the SD--YEPD translation-abundance contrast}
\label{sec:bioreality-q6-sd-yepd-condition-coupled-execution}

\paragraph{Finite panel and coordinate scope.}
This record tests whether a finite residual-coordinate panel from B$^*_{Q6}$ survives as a condition-coupled predictor of the signed SD--YEPD abundance contrast after the declared biological baselines have already been charged. The target row is not a protein-function row, a folding row, or a phenotype row. It is the signed rank-normalized endpoint contrast
$\Delta P_g = z_{\mathrm{rank}}(P_{\mathrm{SD}})-z_{\mathrm{rank}}(P_{\mathrm{YEPD}})$,
computed on $2206$ genes, with empirical mean $-1.1858399826713623\times 10^{-18}$ and standard deviation $0.4434928347257967$. The residualized coordinate list is finite and explicit:
$K\_AAA$, $Arg\_AGR$, $Ile\_AUA$, $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$, $Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG$, $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$, $Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG$, $Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY$, and $f3\_stress$. For each cross-validation fold, the coordinate regression $Q\sim C$ is fit only on training genes; held-out residual coordinates use the corresponding training-fit coefficients and training residual scale. The ridge penalty used for this residual-coordinate block is $\lambda=1.0$.

\paragraph{Controls and held-out design.}
The control block contains an intercept, log CDS sense-codon length, five train-fold principal components from the twenty amino-acid composition proportions $A,C,D,E,F,G,H,I,K,L,M,N,P,Q,R,S,T,V,W,Y$, GC3 from ordered CDS codons, mean pLDDT with a missingness flag, and integrated PaxDb log$_{10}$ baseline abundance. The fold sizes are $447$, $475$, $444$, $451$, and $389$, summing to the $2206$ held-out test rows. The complete-case count for the second model surface is $1654$, and the structural-order proxy has $4$ missing entries. These controls make the test a finite matched-row comparison: the B$^*_{Q6}$ residual block is allowed to count only if it improves held-out description after amino-acid composition, length, GC3, structural-order proxy, and baseline abundance have been accounted for.

\paragraph{Main held-out result.}
Let $M_0$ denote the controlled baseline model and $M_1$ the same model after adding the residualized B$^*_{Q6}$ coordinate block. On the signed SD--YEPD target, the held-out result is negative rather than compressive: $R^2(M_0)=0.008108769333748045$ and $R^2(M_1)=0.005545435711181601$, so $\Delta R^2=-0.002563333622566444$. The sum of squared errors moves from $430.68597066859365$ under $M_0$ to $431.7989876963185$ under $M_1$. The held-out log predictive density change is $-2.936811337346853$. The residual-code saving is $-2.8467939090648606$ nats, while the coordinate encoding charge is $38.494680999067235$ nats, giving a description-length drop of $-41.3414749081321$ nats. Thus the finite row does not support a positive $S^{QT}_{ij}$ entry for this condition-coupled execution claim.

\paragraph{Null comparisons.}
The signed contrast was also checked against three applicable null families. Under the condition-sign-flip null, $300$ draws were run; the $95$ percent thresholds were $\Delta\log p=-0.05373328681116618$, $\Delta R^2=2.359796412121362\times 10^{-5}$, and description-length drop $-38.4687437759017$, while the actual main result remained below the joint gate. Under matched $Q$ permutation within length, amino-acid-PC1, GC3, mean-rank-abundance, and integrated-baseline bins, $300$ draws gave $95$ percent thresholds $\Delta\log p=3.7150577222696484$, $\Delta R^2=0.003501567522117832$, and description-length drop $-34.593988916260244$. Under synonymous recoding preserving amino-acid sequence and per-amino-acid GC3-ending counts exactly, $300$ draws gave $95$ percent thresholds $\Delta\log p=0.011353124037214002$, $\Delta R^2=0.00028102266970064793$, and description-length drop $-38.18213471025496$; the mean absolute GC3 error was $0.0$, and the mean relative ENC-proxy error was $0.09042494844341317$. The planned publishable count for each of these null families is $1000$, but the finite verified run already records that the observed signed target does not pass any applicable joint null gate. The replicate-placebo null is unavailable because the SD/YEPD wrappers expose endpoint condition abundance only, not same-condition replicate abundance columns.

\paragraph{Side surfaces and anti-vacuity checks.}
The positive-looking side surface is not used for the main conclusion. The mean-$P$ side evidence has $\Delta\log p=7.4632216658321795$, $\Delta R^2=0.0026536465383733$, and residual-code saving $8.200039386507424$ nats, but after the same encoding charge it still has description-length drop $-30.29464161255981$ nats. The absolute-delta side surface is negative, with $\Delta\log p=-3.8682101284448436$, $\Delta R^2=-0.0030746447001754884$, and description-length drop $-42.08857873395063$ nats. Adding endpoint mean-rank as an extra control to the signed-delta surface also remains negative: $\Delta\log p=-2.7093968327387756$, $\Delta R^2=-0.002354084610949325$, and description-length drop $-41.10768232015411$ nats. These side checks prevent the refusal from being an empty bookkeeping outcome: the finite system can detect auxiliary movement, but the declared signed SD--YEPD execution target is not compressed by the residual-coordinate block.

\paragraph{Verdict and local boundary.}
The finite verdict is \texttt{no\_compressible\_q\_interaction}. The positive gate fails both parts: the description-length criterion is false, and the held-out $\Delta R^2$ together with $\Delta\log p$ is not above all applicable null thresholds. Consequently this chapter records a bounded negative readback for the B$^*_{Q6}$ SD--YEPD condition-coupled execution test. It does not say that B$^*_{Q6}$ coordinates are biologically irrelevant, and it does not license any claim about translation realization, ribosome mechanism, protein abundance, folding, physical admissibility, biological function, system phenotype, or universality. Any such higher-layer statement would require its own matched contact and its own finite statistic at that layer.
