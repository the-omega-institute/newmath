\subsection{Condition-coupled $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual execution against the SD--YEPD protein-change readout}
\label{sec:bioreality-q6-sd-yepd-condition-coupled-execution}

\paragraph{Finite-row object.}
The tested object is a finite matched-gene matrix, not a general biological principle.  Its rows are $2206$ coding sequences for which the signed condition target
$\Delta P_g = z_{\mathrm{rank}}(P_{\mathrm{SD},g}) - z_{\mathrm{rank}}(P_{\mathrm{YEPD},g})$
was assembled.  The resulting target has empirical mean $-1.1858399826713623\times 10^{-18}$ and standard deviation $0.4434928347257967$.  The coordinate side contains nine train-only residualized $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ coordinates,
$K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $Leu_{CUN\,\mathrm{vs}\,UUR}$, $Leu_{UUA\,\mathrm{vs}\,UUG}$, $Ser_{UCR\,\mathrm{vs}\,AGY}$, $Ser_{UCA\,\mathrm{vs}\,UCG}$, $Thr_{ACR\,\mathrm{vs}\,ACY}$, and $f_3$-stress.  In each fold the regression $Q\sim C$ is fit only on training genes, and held-out residual coordinates use the corresponding train-fold coefficients and train-fold residual scaling.  The ridge parameter for this residual coordinate block is $\lambda=1.0$.

\paragraph{Control surface.}
The control design contains an intercept, log sense-codon CDS length, five train-fold principal-coordinate directions from the twenty amino-acid proportions $A,C,D,E,F,G,H,I,K,L,M,N,P,Q,R,S,T,V,W,Y$, ordered-CDS $GC3$, a mean-pLDDT structural-order proxy with a missingness indicator, and an integrated PaxDb log$_{10}$ baseline abundance term.  The five held-out folds have sizes $447$, $475$, $444$, $451$, and $389$.  The complete-case count for the second modeled surface is $1654$, and only $4$ genes lack the structural-order proxy.  These controls make the comparison a finite conditional compression test: the coordinate block is allowed to count only if it improves held-out prediction after the declared covariates have already absorbed length, amino-acid composition, nucleotide composition, structural-order proxy information, and baseline abundance.

\paragraph{Main held-out comparison.}
Let $M_0$ denote the control-only model and $M_1$ the model with the residual $Q$ block added.  The held-out scores are
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2(M_0) &= 0.008108769333748045,\\
R^2(M_1) &= 0.005545435711181601,\\
\Delta R^2 &= -0.002563333622566444,\\
\Delta \log p_{\mathrm{heldout}} &= -2.936811337346853.
\end{aligned}
$$
The held-out sum of squared error increases from $430.68597066859365$ to $431.7989876963185$.  Thus the added residual block does not merely fail to clear a conservative threshold; it worsens the held-out fit on the declared target.  The residual code saving is $-2.8467939090648606$ nats, while the coordinate encoding charge is $38.494680999067235$ nats, giving a total description-length change of $-41.3414749081321$ nats.  The description-length gate is therefore false.

\paragraph{Null comparisons.}
Three applicable null families were evaluated with $300$ realized draws each, with a publishable target of $1000$ draws recorded for each family.  The condition-sign-flip null has $95\%$ cutoffs $\Delta \log p=-0.05373328681116618$, $\Delta R^2=2.359796412121362\times 10^{-5}$, and description-length change $-38.4687437759017$ nats; the observed main comparison is below the log-predictive and $R^2$ cutoffs and below the description-length cutoff.  The matched-$Q$ permutation null, stratified within length, amino-acid-PC1, $GC3$, mean-rank-abundance, and integrated-baseline bins, has $95\%$ cutoffs $\Delta \log p=3.7150577222696484$, $\Delta R^2=0.003501567522117832$, and description-length change $-34.593988916260244$ nats.  The synonymous-recoding null, preserving amino-acid sequence and per-amino-acid $GC3$-ending counts exactly, has $95\%$ cutoffs $\Delta \log p=0.011353124037214002$, $\Delta R^2=0.00028102266970064793$, and description-length change $-38.18213471025496$ nats.  Its diagnostic mean absolute $GC3$ error is $0.0$, and its mean relative ENC-proxy error is $0.09042494844341317$, with $95\%$ relative ENC-proxy error $0.4059574704858408$.  The replicate-placebo null is unavailable because the SD/YEPD wrappers expose endpoint condition abundance only, not same-condition replicate abundance columns.

\paragraph{Verdict.}
The finite-row verdict for experiment $\mathrm{acc5921d7c06}$, verified at $2026$-$06$-$22$T$17$:$40$:$58$+00:$00$, is $\mathrm{no\_compressible\_q\_interaction}$.  Both positive gates fail: the description-length drop is not positive, and the held-out $\Delta R^2$ and held-out $\Delta\log p$ do not exceed the applicable null $95\%$ boundaries.  In symbols, the tested survival entry for this SD--YEPD condition-coupled readout is locally negative rather than positive:
$$
\begin{aligned}
S^{QT}_{\mathrm{SD-YEPD}} &\leq 0,\\
\Delta R^2_{\mathrm{heldout}} &= -0.002563333622566444,\\
\Delta \log p_{\mathrm{heldout}} &= -2.936811337346853,\\
\Delta L_{\mathrm{description}} &= -41.3414749081321\;\mathrm{nats}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The result is therefore a refusal of positive condition-coupled execution for the declared finite matrix, not a statement that synonymous residual coordinates are biologically inert in every setting.

\paragraph{Anti-vacuity checks.}
The signed-rank target is the main target.  Side calculations show that the testing machinery can detect signal when the response is changed: for endpoint mean abundance, the side evidence has $\Delta\log p=7.4632216658321795$, $\Delta R^2=0.0026536465383733$, $R^2(M_0)=0.6417254262714966$, $R^2(M_1)=0.64437907280987$, and residual code saving $8.200039386507424$ nats before the same $38.494680999067235$-nat coordinate charge.  By contrast, the absolute-delta side evidence gives $\Delta\log p=-3.8682101284448436$ and $\Delta R^2=-0.0030746447001754884$, while the signed-delta target with endpoint mean rank added as an extra control gives $\Delta\log p=-2.7093968327387756$ and $\Delta R^2=-0.002354084610949325$.  These side calculations are not used to reverse the main conclusion; they show that the negative call is specific to the signed SD--YEPD condition-coupled target under the declared controls.

\paragraph{Local non-promotion rule.}
This chapter records a bounded finite-row compression failure.  It does not license any claim about translation efficiency, ribosome occupancy, folded structure, physical admissibility, molecular function, mechanism, system phenotype, or universality.  A translation-efficiency claim would require a separate same-row ribosome-profiling or matched TE contact with mRNA controls.  A protein-output claim would require a separate matched protein-abundance readout.  Structure, physical admissibility, function, mechanism, and phenotype each require their own layer-matched measurements and their own controlled statistics.  The present evidence says only that the nine residual $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ coordinates did not improve the signed SD--YEPD protein-change readout in this finite controlled design.
