\subsection{Condition-coupled B-star Q6 residuals against the SD/YEPD signed protein-abundance contrast}
\label{sec:bioreality-q6-sd-yepd-condition-coupled}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite row object.}
This record tests whether the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates acquire a condition-coupled readout when they are crossed with a finite yeast SD/YEPD endpoint contrast.  The finite panel has $2206$ genes and uses the signed rank-normalized target
$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta P_g &= z_{\mathrm{rank}}(P_{\mathrm{SD},g}) - z_{\mathrm{rank}}(P_{\mathrm{YEPD},g}),\\
\bar{\Delta P} &= -1.1858399826713623\times 10^{-18},\\
\mathrm{sd}(\Delta P) &= 0.4434928347257967.
\end{aligned}
$$
The coordinate side is the train-residualized $Q$ block with ridge parameter $\lambda=1.0$.  Its nine rows are $K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $Leu_{CUN\,vs\,UUR}$, $Leu_{UUA\,vs\,UUG}$, $Ser_{UCR\,vs\,AGY}$, $Ser_{UCA\,vs\,UCG}$, $Thr_{ACR\,vs\,ACY}$, and $f_3\text{-stress}$.  In each fold, the residual map $Q\sim C$ is fitted only on the training genes; held-out genes receive coefficients and residual scaling learned from the corresponding training split.  The folds have sizes $447$, $475$, $444$, $451$, and $389$.

\paragraph{Controls and admissible readback.}
The baseline control matrix $C$ contains an intercept, log CDS sense-codon length, five train-fold PCA coordinates from the twenty amino-acid composition proportions $A,C,D,E,F,G,H,I,K,L,M,N,P,Q,R,S,T,V,W,Y$, GC3 from ordered CDS codons, a mean-pLDDT structural-order proxy with a missingness indicator, and an integrated PaxDb log10 baseline abundance covariate.  The complete-case count for the model using the assembled controls is $1654$, and the structural-order proxy is missing for $4$ genes.  These rows make the readback finite and bounded: the only claimed measured quantity is whether the residualized $Q$ block improves held-out description of the signed SD/YEPD endpoint contrast after these controls.  The endpoint contrast is not a direct ribosome-profiling translation-efficiency row, and modeled codon-adaptation weights are not treated as observed ribosome occupancy.

\paragraph{Held-out compression result.}
Let $M_0$ be the control-only model and $M_1$ be the model that appends the residualized $Q$ block.  On the held-out gene folds, the $Q$ block worsens the fit rather than compressing the target:
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2(M_0) &= 0.008108769333748045,\\
R^2(M_1) &= 0.005545435711181601,\\
\Delta R^2 &= -0.002563333622566444,\\
\Delta \log p_D &= -2.936811337346853,\\
\mathrm{SSE}(M_0) &= 430.68597066859365,\\
\mathrm{SSE}(M_1) &= 431.7989876963185.
\end{aligned}
$$
The residual code saving is $-2.8467939090648606$ nats.  After charging the $Q$-block encoding cost $38.494680999067235$ nats, the description-length drop is
$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta \mathrm{DL} &= -41.3414749081321\ \mathrm{nats}.
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the positive description-length gate is false.  The finite-row verdict is $\mathrm{no\_compressible\_q\_interaction}$: within this controlled SD/YEPD endpoint panel, $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ does not survive as a condition-coupled residual explanation of the signed endpoint contrast.

\paragraph{Null boundaries.}
Three applicable null families were computed with $300$ draws each, with $1000$ draws reserved as the publishable target count.  The condition sign-flip null has $95\%$ thresholds $\Delta\log p_D=-0.05373328681116618$ and $\Delta R^2=2.359796412121362\times 10^{-5}$; the observed held-out values do not exceed either threshold.  The matched-$Q$ permutation null, where $Q$ rows are permuted within length, amino-acid-PC1, GC3, mean-rank-abundance, and integrated-baseline bins, has thresholds $\Delta\log p_D=3.7150577222696484$ and $\Delta R^2=0.003501567522117832$, again not exceeded.  The synonymous-recoding null preserves amino-acid sequence and per-amino-acid GC3-ending counts exactly; its thresholds are $\Delta\log p_D=0.011353124037214002$ and $\Delta R^2=0.00028102266970064793$, also not exceeded.  The synonymous-recoding diagnostics have mean absolute GC3 error $0.0$, $95\%$ absolute GC3 error $0.0$, mean relative ENC-proxy error $0.09042494844341317$, and $95\%$ relative ENC-proxy error $0.4059574704858408$.  A replicate placebo null is unavailable because the SD/YEPD wrappers expose endpoint condition abundance rather than same-condition replicate abundance columns.

\paragraph{Anti-vacuity checks.}
The signed target is the only main target.  Side targets were recorded to prevent an apparent negative call from hiding a positive conclusion elsewhere.  For the absolute-delta side target, $\Delta R^2=-0.0030746447001754884$, $\Delta\log p_D=-3.8682101284448436$, residual code saving is $-3.5938977348833916$ nats, and the description-length drop is $-42.08857873395063$ nats.  For the signed-delta target with endpoint mean-rank as an additional control, $\Delta R^2=-0.002354084610949325$, $\Delta\log p_D=-2.7093968327387756$, residual code saving is $-2.6130013210868746$ nats, and the description-length drop is $-41.10768232015411$ nats.  The endpoint mean-rank side evidence improves slightly on its own readout, with $\Delta R^2=0.0026536465383733$ and $\Delta\log p_D=7.4632216658321795$, but after the same $38.494680999067235$-nat coordinate charge its description-length drop remains negative at $-30.29464161255981$ nats.  The recorded non-main subgroup and rank summaries are therefore not used to rescue the main conclusion.

\paragraph{Local biological boundary.}
This chapter is a negative finite-row interaction call, not a denial that synonymous residual coordinates may matter in some other layer.  It says only that this controlled SD/YEPD endpoint panel does not support a compressive $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ contribution to the signed condition contrast.  It does not establish translation realization, ribosome occupancy, protein mediation, folded structure, physical admissibility, molecular function, mechanism, system phenotype, or a universal biological rule.  Any such statement would require its own layer-matched measurement rows and its own controlled statistic.  In particular, a future ribosome-profiling translation-efficiency matrix would have to join same-CDS codon counts, RPF rows, matched mRNA abundance, and the declared amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, length, M-density, modeled-baseline, and simpler-baseline controls before a positive $S^{QT}_{ij}$ readback could be reported.
