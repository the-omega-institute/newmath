\subsection{Condition-Coupled B$^*_{Q6}$ Residuals Against SD--YEPD Protein-Endpoint Contrast}
\label{sec:bioreality-q6-sd-yepd-interaction-powered}

\paragraph{Finite row object.}
This record tests whether the B$^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinate family carries a condition-coupled signal for the signed SD--YEPD endpoint contrast.  The finite carrier is the matched gene table used in run \texttt{acc5921d7c06}: $2206$ coding-sequence rows, five held-out gene folds of sizes $447$, $475$, $444$, $451$, and $389$, and a signed target
$\Delta P_g = z_{\mathrm{rank}}(P_{\mathrm{SD}})-z_{\mathrm{rank}}(P_{\mathrm{YEPD}})$.
The constructed response has mean $-1.1858399826713623\cdot 10^{-18}$ and standard deviation $0.4434928347257967$.  The covariate table contains an intercept, log CDS sense-codon length, five train-fold PCA coordinates from the twenty amino-acid composition proportions, GC3 from ordered CDS codons, a mean pLDDT structural-order proxy with a missingness indicator, and an integrated PaxDb log$_{10}$ baseline abundance.  The complete-case count for the abundance-augmented control set is $1654$, and the structural-order proxy is missing in $4$ rows.

\paragraph{Residual coordinates and fitted comparison.}
The residualized coordinate block is fit train-only in each fold, so that $Q\sim C$ is learned on training genes and then applied to held-out genes with training residual scaling.  The ridge parameter is $\lambda=1.0$, and the reported coordinate list is
$\{\mathrm{K\_AAA},\mathrm{Arg\_AGR},\mathrm{Ile\_AUA},\mathrm{Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR},\mathrm{Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG},\mathrm{Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY},\mathrm{Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG},\mathrm{Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY},f_3\text{-stress}\}$.
The main held-out comparison has
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2(M_0)&=0.008108769333748045, & R^2(M_1)&=0.005545435711181601,\\
\Delta R^2&=-0.002563333622566444, & \Delta\log p_D&=-2.936811337346853,\\
\mathrm{SSE}(M_0)&=430.68597066859365, & \mathrm{SSE}(M_1)&=431.7989876963185.
\end{aligned}
$$
The residual-code saving is $-2.8467939090648606$ nats.  With the coordinate encoding cost $38.494680999067235$ nats, the description-length change is $-41.3414749081321$ nats, so the coordinate block does not compress the signed condition contrast.

\paragraph{Null comparisons.}
The positive gate is failed in every declared null comparison.  For the condition-sign-flip null, $B=300$, the null $95\%$ thresholds are $\Delta\log p_D=-0.05373328681116618$, $\Delta R^2=2.359796412121362\cdot 10^{-5}$, and description-length change $-38.4687437759017$ nats; the observed main statistic is below the joint requirement.  For the matched-$Q$ permutation null, $B=300$, the corresponding thresholds are $3.7150577222696484$, $0.003501567522117832$, and $-34.593988916260244$ nats.  For the synonymous-recoding null, $B=300$, the thresholds are $0.011353124037214002$, $0.00028102266970064793$, and $-38.18213471025496$ nats.  The synonymous recodings preserve per-amino-acid GC3-ending counts exactly, with mean absolute GC3 error $0.0$ and mean relative ENC-proxy error $0.09042494844341317$; the $95\%$ relative ENC-proxy error is $0.4059574704858408$.  The replicate placebo is not available because the SD/YEPD wrappers expose endpoint condition abundance rather than same-condition replicate abundance columns.

\paragraph{Anti-vacuity checks.}
The negative call is not caused by absence of numerical variation.  Alternative side calculations move in different directions, but none is the declared main endpoint.  The absolute-delta-only side evidence has $\Delta\log p_D=-3.8682101284448436$, $\Delta R^2=-0.0030746447001754884$, residual-code saving $-3.5938977348833916$ nats, and description-length change $-42.08857873395063$ nats.  The endpoint-mean side evidence has $\Delta\log p_D=7.4632216658321795$ and $\Delta R^2=0.0026536465383733$, but after the same encoding cost its description-length change is still $-30.29464161255981$ nats.  Adding endpoint mean rank as an extra control for the signed-delta target gives $\Delta\log p_D=-2.7093968327387756$, $\Delta R^2=-0.002354084610949325$, residual-code saving $-2.6130013210868746$ nats, and description-length change $-41.10768232015411$ nats.

\paragraph{Local verdict.}
For this finite table the condition-coupled B$^*_{Q6}$ residual block is a tested non-survivor for the signed SD--YEPD contrast.  In the notation of this chapter, the controlled survival entry for the declared target satisfies $S^{QT}_{ij}\leq 0$ for the fitted coordinate block, because held-out fit worsens, log predictive density decreases, and the description-length gate is not met after the coordinate cost.  The appropriate scientific reading is therefore a bounded refusal: the run reports no compressible $Q$ interaction for this condition contrast.  It does not license a claim about translation realization, ribosome occupancy, protein mediation, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, mechanism, phenotype, or universality.  Any such higher-layer conclusion would require a separate layer-matched contact and a separately reported controlled statistic.
