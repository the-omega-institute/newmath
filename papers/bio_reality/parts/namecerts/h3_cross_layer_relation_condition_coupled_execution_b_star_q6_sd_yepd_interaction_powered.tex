\subsection{Condition-coupled $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinates against SD/YEPD abundance contrast}
\label{sec:bioreality-q6-sd-yepd-condition-coupled-execution}

\paragraph{Finite panel.}
This record tests whether the $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinate system has a measurable condition-coupled cross-layer effect on an SD/YEPD protein-abundance contrast after the declared controls are already present. The finite carrier is a gene-indexed matrix with $2206$ held-out test rows. For each gene $g$, the signed endpoint target was constructed as $\Delta P_g = z_{\operatorname{rank}}(P_{\mathrm{SD}})-z_{\operatorname{rank}}(P_{\mathrm{YEPD}})$, with empirical mean $-1.1858399826713623\times 10^{-18}$ and standard deviation $0.4434928347257967$. The target is therefore a rank-normalized signed endpoint contrast, not a protein mechanism, localization assay, folding assay, or phenotype measurement.

\paragraph{Residual coordinates and controls.}
The residual coordinate block contains nine train-fold residualized coordinates: $K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $Leu_{CUN\,\mathrm{vs}\,UUR}$, $Leu_{UUA\,\mathrm{vs}\,UUG}$, $Ser_{UCR\,\mathrm{vs}\,AGY}$, $Ser_{UCA\,\mathrm{vs}\,UCG}$, $Thr_{ACR\,\mathrm{vs}\,ACY}$, and $f_3\text{-stress}$. In every fold, the regression $Q\sim C$ was fit only on training genes, and held-out residuals used the corresponding training coefficients and training residual scale. The ridge penalty for this residual coordinate block was $\lambda=1.0$. The control matrix included an intercept, log CDS sense-codon length, five train-fold PCA coordinates from the twenty amino-acid composition proportions $A,C,D,E,F,G,H,I,K,L,M,N,P,Q,R,S,T,V,W,Y$, GC3 from ordered CDS codons, a mean-pLDDT structural-order proxy with a missingness indicator, and an integrated PaxDb log10 baseline-abundance covariate. The fold sizes were $447$, $475$, $444$, $451$, and $389$, summing to the same $2206$ tested genes. The structural-order proxy had $4$ missing entries, and the complete-case count for the second model family was $1654$.

\paragraph{Held-out comparison.}
Let $M_0$ denote the controlled baseline model and $M_1$ the corresponding model after adding the residual $B^*_{Q6}$ block. The measured held-out change is
$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta R^2 &= R^2(M_1)-R^2(M_0) = 0.005545435711181601-0.008108769333748045 \\
&= -0.002563333622566444,\\
\Delta\log p_{\mathrm{heldout}} &= -2.936811337346853,\\
\operatorname{SSE}(M_0) &= 430.68597066859365,\\
\operatorname{SSE}(M_1) &= 431.7989876963185.
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the residual block worsens the held-out squared-error fit and also loses held-out predictive log density. With residual code saving $-2.8467939090648606$ nats and coordinate-encoding cost $38.494680999067235$ nats, the resulting description-length change is
$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta L &= -41.3414749081321\ \mathrm{nats}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The finite-row decision is therefore not a positive survival statement. The tested block is present, the rows are nonempty, and the calculation ran on the declared folds, but the fitted residual coordinates do not compress the signed SD/YEPD endpoint contrast after the controls.

\paragraph{Null comparisons.}
The positive gate required both description-length improvement and held-out $\Delta R^2$ plus $\Delta\log p$ above all applicable null cutoffs. That gate did not pass. Under the condition-sign-flip null, $300$ null draws gave $\Delta\log p_{95}=-0.05373328681116618$, $\Delta R^2_{95}=0.00002359796412121362$, and description-length-drop cutoff $-38.4687437759017$ nats; the observed pair failed both predictive thresholds. Under the matched-$Q$ permutation null, $300$ draws gave $\Delta\log p_{95}=3.7150577222696484$, $\Delta R^2_{95}=0.003501567522117832$, and description-length-drop cutoff $-34.593988916260244$ nats; the observed values again failed. Under synonymous recoding, $300$ draws gave $\Delta\log p_{95}=0.011353124037214002$, $\Delta R^2_{95}=0.00028102266970064793$, and description-length-drop cutoff $-38.18213471025496$ nats; the observed values again failed. The synonymous-recoding diagnostics preserved per-amino-acid GC3-ending counts exactly, with mean absolute GC3 error $0.0$, p95 absolute GC3 error $0.0$, mean relative ENC-proxy error $0.09042494844341317$, and p95 relative ENC-proxy error $0.4059574704858408$. A same-condition replicate placebo was unavailable because the SD/YEPD wrappers expose endpoint condition abundance rather than replicate abundance columns. Each reported null family used $300$ realized draws, while the publication-intended draw count is $1000$.

\paragraph{Anti-vacuity side checks.}
The signed-rank endpoint was the main target. Side checks show that the negative decision is not merely a consequence of all possible endpoint summaries being empty. For absolute-delta-only side evidence, $R^2$ changed from $0.05789998072242308$ to $0.054825336022247595$, giving $\Delta R^2=-0.0030746447001754884$, $\Delta\log p=-3.8682101284448436$, residual code saving $-3.5938977348833916$ nats, and description-length change $-42.08857873395063$ nats. For endpoint mean-rank side evidence, $R^2$ changed from $0.6417254262714966$ to $0.64437907280987$, giving $\Delta R^2=0.0026536465383733$ and $\Delta\log p=7.4632216658321795$, but the residual code saving of $8.200039386507424$ nats was still below the $38.494680999067235$ nat coordinate cost, so the description-length change remained negative at $-30.29464161255981$ nats. With endpoint mean rank added as an extra control for the signed-delta target, $R^2$ changed from $0.007470487595869213$ to $0.005116402984919888$, with $\Delta R^2=-0.002354084610949325$, $\Delta\log p=-2.7093968327387756$, residual code saving $-2.6130013210868746$ nats, and description-length change $-41.10768232015411$ nats. These side checks are not used as the main conclusion; they record that alternative endpoint summaries either fail directly or fail once coordinate cost is charged.

\paragraph{Decision.}
The finite-row verdict for experiment run $acc5921d7c06$ is $\mathrm{no\_compressible\_q\_interaction}$. In the notation of this paper, the controlled survival entry for the signed SD/YEPD endpoint satisfies $S^{QT}\leq 0$ for the tested residual block, because adding the residual coordinates decreases held-out $R^2$, decreases held-out log predictive density, and loses $41.3414749081321$ nats after the coordinate cost is charged. The statement is deliberately narrow: it is a negative controlled-compression result for these rows, these residual coordinates, these controls, and this signed endpoint contrast.

\paragraph{Local scope limit.}
No broader biological conclusion follows from this record. In particular, the computation does not establish independent translational realization, ribosome mechanism, folding state, physical admissibility, biological function, system phenotype, or universality. It also does not turn modeled codon-adaptation quantities, mRNA controls, structural-order proxies, or baseline abundance covariates into direct measurements at those higher layers. Any such claim would require a separate matched contact and its own finite statistic rather than promotion from the present negative compression test.
