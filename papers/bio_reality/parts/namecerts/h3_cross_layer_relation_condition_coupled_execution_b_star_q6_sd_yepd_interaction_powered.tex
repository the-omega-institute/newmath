\subsection{Condition-coupled refusal for the $B^\ast_{Q6}$ SD--YEPD interaction panel}\label{sec:b-star-q6-sd-yepd-interaction-powered}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite carrier.}
This record is a finite controlled matrix test for whether residual synonymous coordinates from $B^\ast_{Q6}$ compress a condition-coupled readout after the amino-acid and sequence-composition baselines have already been paid for. The empirical panel contains $2206$ genes with the signed target
$\Delta P_g = z_{\mathrm{rank}}(P_{\mathrm{SD}})-z_{\mathrm{rank}}(P_{\mathrm{YEPD}})$, with target mean $-1.1858399826713623\times 10^{-18}$ and target standard deviation $0.4434928347257967$. The readout is therefore a signed SD--YEPD endpoint contrast, not a ribosome-profiling translation-efficiency measurement. The test asks whether adding the train-residualized $Q$ coordinates improves held-out description after the baseline model has absorbed ordinary covariates.

\paragraph{Rows and controls.}
The covariate block $C$ contains an intercept, log CDS sense-codon length, five train-fold PCA coordinates from the twenty amino-acid composition proportions $A,C,D,E,F,G,H,I,K,L,M,N,P,Q,R,S,T,V,W,Y$, GC3 from ordered CDS codons, a mean-pLDDT structural-order proxy with a missingness indicator, and an integrated PaxDb $\log_{10}$ baseline abundance term. Fold sizes are $447,475,444,451,389$, and the complete-case count for the structural-order and integrated-abundance model is $1654$, with $4$ structural-order missing rows. The residual coordinate block is fit only through training-fold information: in each fold $Q\sim C$ is fit on training genes, held-out residuals use the train-fit coefficients and train residual scaling, and the ridge penalty is $\lambda=1.0$. The nine coordinates are $K\_AAA$, $Arg\_AGR$, $Ile\_AUA$, $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$, $Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG$, $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$, $Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG$, $Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY$, and $f3\_stress$.

\paragraph{Held-out comparison.}
Let $M_0$ denote the controlled baseline model and $M_1$ the model with the residual $Q$ block added. On the held-out gene folds the observed comparison is
$$\begin{aligned}
R^2(M_0)&=0.008108769333748045,\\
R^2(M_1)&=0.005545435711181601,\\
\Delta R^2&=-0.002563333622566444,\\
\Delta\log p_{\mathrm{heldout}}&=-2.936811337346853.
\end{aligned}$$
The sum-of-squares increases from $430.68597066859365$ to $431.7989876963185$. The residual-code saving is $-2.8467939090648606$ nats, while the encoding charge for the $Q$ block is $38.494680999067235$ nats, giving a description-length drop of $-41.3414749081321$ nats. Thus the finite panel does not support a positive controlled survival entry: the added residual coordinates make the held-out description worse after the stated controls.

\paragraph{Null calibration.}
Three applicable null families were evaluated with $300$ realized draws each, with a publishable target of $1000$ draws recorded for the null specifications. Under the condition-sign-flip null, the $95\%$ thresholds are $\Delta\log p=-0.05373328681116618$ and $\Delta R^2=0.00002359796412121362$; the actual held-out result is below both thresholds. Under matched $Q$ permutation within length, amino-acid PC1, GC3, mean-rank-abundance, and integrated-baseline bins, the $95\%$ thresholds are $\Delta\log p=3.7150577222696484$ and $\Delta R^2=0.003501567522117832$, again not exceeded. Under synonymous recoding with amino-acid sequence and per-amino-acid GC3-ending counts preserved exactly, the $95\%$ thresholds are $\Delta\log p=0.011353124037214002$ and $\Delta R^2=0.00028102266970064793$, also not exceeded. The synonymous-recoding diagnostics report mean absolute GC3 error $0.0$, mean relative ENC-proxy error $0.09042494844341317$, $95\%$ absolute GC3 error $0.0$, and $95\%$ relative ENC-proxy error $0.4059574704858408$. The replicate-placebo null is unavailable because the SD and YEPD wrappers expose endpoint condition abundance only, not same-condition replicate abundance columns.

\paragraph{Anti-vacuity side checks.}
The signed target is the main target. Two side checks prevent the null result from being read as an artifact of a single target encoding. For the mean-$P$ side evidence, adding the same residual block gives $\Delta\log p=7.4632216658321795$ and $\Delta R^2=0.0026536465383733$, but the description-length drop remains negative at $-30.29464161255981$ nats after the same $38.494680999067235$-nat coordinate cost. For the absolute-delta side evidence, the result is $\Delta\log p=-3.8682101284448436$, $\Delta R^2=-0.0030746447001754884$, and description-length drop $-42.08857873395063$ nats. With endpoint mean rank added as an extra control to the signed-delta target, the result remains negative: $\Delta\log p=-2.7093968327387756$, $\Delta R^2=-0.002354084610949325$, and description-length drop $-41.10768232015411$ nats. The side checks are not used as the main conclusion; they only show that the recorded refusal is not rescued by the tested alternate encodings.

\paragraph{Verdict.}
The finite verdict is $\mathrm{no\_compressible\_q\_interaction}$. The positive gate fails both required parts: the description-length drop is not positive, and the held-out $\Delta R^2$ together with held-out $\Delta\log p$ does not exceed the applicable null thresholds. In the notation of a controlled survival matrix, this row therefore supplies $S^{QT}_{ij}\leq 0$ for the tested condition-coupled endpoint contrast. It is a refusal of compressible $B^\ast_{Q6}$ interaction signal in this finite SD--YEPD panel, not a statement that synonymous residual geometry is biologically irrelevant.

\paragraph{Local scope boundary.}
This chapter does not infer translation realization from the endpoint contrast, because the supplied rows do not contain matched ribosome-profiling translation-efficiency measurements. It also does not infer protein mediation, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, organismal phenotype, mechanism, or universality. Such statements require their own layer-matched empirical contacts and their own controlled finite statistics. The present result is narrower: after the listed amino-acid, GC3, length, structural-order proxy, and abundance controls, the tested $B^\ast_{Q6}$ residual block does not improve held-out compression of the signed SD--YEPD endpoint condition contrast.
