\subsection{Condition-coupled B$^\ast_{Q6}$ residual coordinates against the SD--YEPD abundance contrast}
\label{sec:bioreality-q6-sd-yepd-condition-coupled-execution}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite panel and readout.}
This record tests whether the B$^\ast_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinate system carries a condition-coupled signal into a matched finite row panel for the SD--YEPD abundance contrast.  The tested target is not a structural, functional, or universal biological claim.  It is the finite response
$\Delta P_g=z_{\mathrm{rank}}(P_{\mathrm{SD},g})-z_{\mathrm{rank}}(P_{\mathrm{YEPD},g})$
on $2206$ genes, with empirical mean $-1.1858399826713623\cdot 10^{-18}$ and standard deviation $0.4434928347257967$.  The sensitivity target recorded beside it is $z(\log P_{\mathrm{SD}}-\log P_{\mathrm{YEPD}})$, but the main decision is made on the signed rank-normalized contrast.  The experiment identifier is \texttt{b\_star\_q6\_condition\_interaction\_sd\_yepd\_powered}, run \texttt{acc5921d7c06}, with status \texttt{passed} as a completed finite-row computation rather than as a positive survival result.

\paragraph{Coordinates and controls.}
The residual-coordinate block contains nine train-fold residualized coordinates: $K_{\mathrm{AAA}}$, $\mathrm{Arg}_{\mathrm{AGR}}$, $\mathrm{Ile}_{\mathrm{AUA}}$, $\mathrm{Leu}_{\mathrm{CUN}\,\mathrm{vs}\,\mathrm{UUR}}$, $\mathrm{Leu}_{\mathrm{UUA}\,\mathrm{vs}\,\mathrm{UUG}}$, $\mathrm{Ser}_{\mathrm{UCR}\,\mathrm{vs}\,\mathrm{AGY}}$, $\mathrm{Ser}_{\mathrm{UCA}\,\mathrm{vs}\,\mathrm{UCG}}$, $\mathrm{Thr}_{\mathrm{ACR}\,\mathrm{vs}\,\mathrm{ACY}}$, and $f_3$-stress.  For each fold, $Q\sim C$ is fit only on training genes, and held-out residuals use the training-fit coefficients and training residual scale.  The ridge penalty is $\lambda=1.0$.  The baseline control matrix contains an intercept, log CDS sense-codon length, five train-fold principal-component coordinates from the twenty amino-acid composition proportions A, C, D, E, F, G, H, I, K, L, M, N, P, Q, R, S, T, V, W, Y, GC3 from ordered CDS codons, a mean-pLDDT structural-order proxy with missingness indicator, and an integrated PaxDb log$_{10}$ abundance baseline.  Fold sizes are $447$, $475$, $444$, $451$, and $389$; the complete-case count for the second model family is $1654$, and the structural-order proxy has $4$ missing rows.

\paragraph{Held-out comparison.}
Let $M_0$ denote the controlled baseline and $M_1$ the baseline plus the residual-coordinate block.  On the held-out gene folds, $M_0$ has $R^2=0.008108769333748045$ and $\mathrm{SSE}=430.68597066859365$, while $M_1$ has $R^2=0.005545435711181601$ and $\mathrm{SSE}=431.7989876963185$.  Thus the finite-row deltas are
$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta R^2&=-0.002563333622566444,\\
\Delta\log p_{\mathrm{heldout}}&=-2.936811337346853,\\
\Delta\mathrm{DL}&=-41.3414749081321\ \mathrm{nats}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The coordinate block is charged $38.494680999067235$ nats, while the residual code saving is $-2.8467939090648606$ nats.  The description-length gate is therefore false: the residual coordinates make the finite encoding worse rather than shorter for the signed SD--YEPD contrast.

\paragraph{Null comparisons.}
Three applicable null families were computed with $300$ actual draws each, with $1000$ listed as the publishable draw target for each family.  The condition-sign-flip null has null $95$ thresholds $\Delta\log p=-0.05373328681116618$, $\Delta R^2=2.359796412121362\cdot 10^{-5}$, and $\Delta\mathrm{DL}=-38.4687437759017$ nats; the maximum null values seen were $3.346183710458263$ for $\Delta\log p$ and $0.003115206933151926$ for $\Delta R^2$.  The matched-$Q$ permutation null has null $95$ thresholds $\Delta\log p=3.7150577222696484$, $\Delta R^2=0.003501567522117832$, and $\Delta\mathrm{DL}=-34.593988916260244$ nats; the maximum values seen were $9.754186934356767$ and $0.008579527265972553$.  The synonymous-recoding null preserves amino-acid sequence and per-amino-acid GC3-ending counts exactly, with mean absolute GC3 error $0.0$, $95$th percentile absolute GC3 error $0.0$, mean relative ENC-proxy error $0.09042494844341317$, and $95$th percentile relative ENC-proxy error $0.4059574704858408$.  Its null $95$ thresholds are $\Delta\log p=0.011353124037214002$, $\Delta R^2=0.00028102266970064793$, and $\Delta\mathrm{DL}=-38.18213471025496$ nats, with maxima $6.24877932309505$ and $0.0058455213683357155$.  The replicate-placebo null is unavailable because the SD and YEPD wrappers expose endpoint condition abundance rather than same-condition replicate abundance columns.

\paragraph{Decision.}
The positive gate fails on both required axes.  The description-length drop is not positive, and the held-out $\Delta R^2$ and $\Delta\log p$ are not above all applicable null $95$ boundaries.  Each applicable null family has joint pass equal to false: condition sign flip false, matched-$Q$ permutation false, and synonymous recoding false.  The recorded finite-row verdict is therefore \texttt{no\_compressible\_q\_interaction}.  Equivalently, for this finite SD--YEPD panel, the B$^\ast_{Q6}$ residual block does not yield a positive controlled survival entry for the signed condition contrast after the declared controls and null comparisons.

\paragraph{Anti-vacuity checks.}
The negative decision is not caused by an empty target or an absent numerical response.  The signed target has $2206$ tested rows.  The absolute-delta side readout gives $R^2_0=0.05789998072242308$, $R^2_1=0.054825336022247595$, $\Delta R^2=-0.0030746447001754884$, $\Delta\log p=-3.8682101284448436$, and $\Delta\mathrm{DL}=-42.08857873395063$ nats.  The endpoint-mean-rank extra-control side readout gives $R^2_0=0.007470487595869213$, $R^2_1=0.005116402984919888$, $\Delta R^2=-0.002354084610949325$, $\Delta\log p=-2.7093968327387756$, and $\Delta\mathrm{DL}=-41.10768232015411$ nats.  A mean-abundance side readout is positive before encoding cost, with $\Delta\log p=7.4632216658321795$ and $\Delta R^2=0.0026536465383733$, but its description-length drop remains negative at $-30.29464161255981$ nats after the same coordinate charge.  These side readouts are explicitly not used for the main conclusion, and the excluded side categories include absolute-delta rank, mean rank, and GO, stress, high-$P$, or low-$P$ subgroups.

\paragraph{Local scope.}
This chapter licenses only the finite negative readback just stated.  It does not show that B$^\ast_{Q6}$ coordinates are biologically irrelevant, and it does not establish a general law about codon usage.  More importantly, the failed SD--YEPD survival test does not imply translation realization, folded structure, physical admissibility, molecular function, mechanism, protein abundance, phenotype, or universality.  Any one of those stronger interpretations would require its own layer-matched contact and its own finite statistic.  In this record, ribosome-profiling translation efficiency, modeled tAI or stAI weights, matched mRNA abundance, and proteomics are boundary routes or controls only when explicitly joined and tested; they are not promoted from the present endpoint-abundance contrast.
