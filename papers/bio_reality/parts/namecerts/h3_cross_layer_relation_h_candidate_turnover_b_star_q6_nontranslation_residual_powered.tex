\subsection{Yeast turnover as a bounded candidate for the $B^*_{Q6}$ non-translation remainder}
\label{subsec:bioreality-q6-turnover-candidate}

\paragraph{Finite carrier.}
The record studied here is a finite yeast panel, not a general biological law.  Its rows are coding-sequence entries that simultaneously carry a $B^*_{Q6}$ residual-coordinate readout, the standard control block $Z$, modeled-translation-complement abundance residuals, and a protein half-life value.  The nine residual coordinates are
$K\_AAA$, $Arg\_AGR$, $Ile\_AUA$, $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$, $Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG$, $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$, $Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG$, $Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY$, and $f3\_stress$.  The control matrix has 24 columns with rank $23$: an intercept, $\log(\mathrm{cds\_len\_nt})$, the 20 amino-acid composition proportions $A,C,D,E,F,G,H,I,K,L,M,N,P,Q,R,S,T,V,W,Y$, $GC3$, and the $M$-density baseline defined as the fraction of sense codons on the union support of the nine projected $B^*_{Q6}$ vectors.

\paragraph{Reality contact and join.}
The turnover contact is the yeast protein half-life surface obtained from SGD backend protein-experiment details for Christiano et al. 2014, keyed per locus and mapped to STRING protein identifiers by prefixing the SGD systematic ORF name with $4932.$.  The identified half-life column is the SGD \texttt{data\_value} field in hours, and the identified ORF column is the SGD STRING id.  The loaded turnover payload has 77,817 bytes and SHA-256 hash \texttt{05077d4d73f9dc3f16f331a4ae0c4a4d482e65f17a94de3522b3c0c485335690}.  The finite join reports 6,351 valid abundance proteins, 5,815 CDS rows joined to abundance, 5,128 genes with valid translation-efficiency values, and 3,640 valid turnover proteins.  The turnover-abundance-CDS join contains 3,640 rows; the stricter turnover-abundance-CDS-measured-TE join contains 3,537 rows.  The reported CDS join hit rate is $0.9156038419146592$, and the turnover join hit rate is $0.8818572656921754$.  No abundance record was skipped for missing, duplicate, or nonpositive protein identifiers; no turnover record was skipped for missing, duplicate, or nonpositive half-life; the CDS side lost 2,175 rows for absent turnover and 103 additional rows for absent optional measured translation-efficiency matching.

\paragraph{Statistic.}
The tested quantity is descriptive projection of the modeled-tAI-complement abundance component onto residualized $\log_{10}$ protein half-life.  Let $P_Q$ denote the residual $B^*_{Q6}$ component after the control block, and let $T_{\mathrm{mod}}$ denote the modeled translation surface used to form the non-translation remainder.  On the turnover join, the reported squared norms and complement fraction are
$$
\begin{aligned}
\|P_Q\|^2 &= 457.02479128996873,\\
\|P_{Q\perp T_{\mathrm{mod}}}\|^2 &= 102.04013124265222,\\
d_{\mathrm{modeled}} &= 0.2232704509412724.
\end{aligned}
$$
This value is consistent with the full abundance-CDS complement reference at the declared tolerance: the reference value is $0.21173219572706703$ on 5,815 rows, the turnover-join value is $0.2232704509412724$ on 3,640 rows, the absolute difference is $0.011538255214205373$, and the within-$0.02$ check passes.  After projecting the non-translation complement onto residualized half-life, the turnover absorption is very small:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{absorbed\_by\_turnover} &= 0.0009086031889966327,\\
d_{\mathrm{combined}} &= 0.22306503721327192.
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the half-life contact is available and well joined, but it explains only a minute part of the modeled non-translation complement on this surface.

\paragraph{Relation to measured translation and tRNA contacts.}
This turnover packet is downstream of a measured translation-efficiency panel rather than a substitute for it.  In the yeast measured-TE contact, the same nine coordinates and the same 24-column control design give $n=5,128$ with rank $23$, while the measured-TE absorption of the modeled complement is $0.0002762783754375593$ and the combined fraction is $0.2237928123367028$.  The measured tRNA-gene-copy tAI contact uses 5,815 yeast CDS rows, 273 tRNA genes, and an anticodon table of size 41; its reported tRNA absorption is $0.001530747712165342$ with $d_{\mathrm{combined}}=0.20648621019305421$.  These neighboring finite contacts show that turnover is being evaluated against a specific residual already bounded by modeled translation, measured TE, and tRNA-copy tAI readouts.  The turnover result does not replace the translation-efficiency condition, nor does it identify a causal mediator.

\paragraph{Readback.}
The positive content of the record is narrow.  In \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}, on 3,640 joined turnover rows, the $B^*_{Q6}$ non-translation complement remains close to its reference scale and is barely reduced by residualized protein half-life.  The computed turnover absorption, $0.0009086031889966327$, is compatible with the interpretation that this particular steady-state half-life contact does not materially account for the measured $B^*_{Q6}$ remainder.  Since the upstream yeast complement is already modest, with $d_{\mathrm{modeled}}$ near $0.21$ on the full abundance-CDS reference and $0.2232704509412724$ on the turnover join, the absolute unexplained signal is also modest.  The record is therefore a bounded negative-to-small candidate readback: protein turnover was testable on this finite yeast panel, the join was sufficiently stable against the abundance-CDS reference, and the observed absorption was tiny.

\paragraph{Local boundary.}
No translation, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, organismal phenotype, mechanism, causation, or universality follows from this turnover entry.  The statistic is a projection on one yeast steady-state half-life dataset, not a perturbation, not a mediator test, and not a proof of degradation control.  A protein-stability mechanism would require a separate layer-matched contact such as pulse-chase, degradation-rate perturbation, proteasome or pathway intervention, rescue, or condition-specific turnover evidence with its own controls.  If a later panel joins codon residuals, measured translation, matched mRNA abundance, protein abundance, half-life, localization, complex membership, and perturbation rows on the same biological units, it may test a stronger mediation claim; this finite record only says that the present Christiano-2014 yeast half-life readout absorbs about $9.086\times 10^{-4}$ of the modeled non-translation complement under the declared residualization.
