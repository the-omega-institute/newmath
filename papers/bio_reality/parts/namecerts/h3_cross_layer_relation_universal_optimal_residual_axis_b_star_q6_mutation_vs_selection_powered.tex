\subsection{The $f_3$ residual axis as a bounded translation-readout candidate}
\label{sec:bior-reality-f3-residual-axis}

\paragraph{Finite carrier.}
The record considered here is the finite CDS-by-organism residual coordinate surface restricted to the $B^*_{Q6}$ coordinate named $f_3$-stress.  Its admissible rows are not raw codon counts alone: each row must retain the amino-acid projection, the synonymous codon imbalance paired by third-position $f_3$, the organism label, and a matched translation readout such as tAI, stAI, ribosome occupancy, or translation efficiency.  The coordinate is therefore a residual comparison surface, not a statement that codon topology is already biological mechanism.  The finite saturation row fixes the relevant codon inventory.  The set $M=\operatorname{Med}(R)\cup\{\mathrm{ACA}\}$ contains the 20 codons
$$
\begin{aligned}
M=\{&\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{ACA},\mathrm{ACG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\\
&\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
After $f_3$-saturation of $R$, the finite row removes $\mathrm{ACA}$ and $\mathrm{ACG}$, leaving the 18 codons
$$
\begin{aligned}
\operatorname{Sat}_{f_3}(R)=\{&\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\\
&\mathrm{CUU},\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The reported $f_3$ boundary set is empty.  Thus the chapter starts from a closed finite codon-side coordinate surface, while all biological interpretation remains conditional on separate organism-matched readout rows.

\paragraph{Residual-axis finding.}
In the powered residual-axis assay, the non-tRNA residual optimum aligned most strongly with the axis named $f3\_translation\_selection$.  The run used 5 folds, required at least 500 proteins per organism, computed 11 organisms out of 16 requested organisms, and used 41 synonymous design columns.  Its deterministic seed was recorded as $\mathrm{sha256{:}b\_star\_q6\_universal\_optimal\_residual\_axis\_powered{:}deterministic}$, and the run status was passed.  Formally, the finite conclusion is only the alignment statement
$$
\begin{aligned}
\operatorname{dominant\_axis}(B^*_{Q6}\setminus \mathrm{tRNA})=\mathrm{f3\_translation\_selection},
\end{aligned}
$$
under that assay design.  This is a rank-powered coordinate result: it says that, among the residual axes tested after the stated controls and organism filters, the best non-tRNA residual direction is the $f_3$-translation-selection direction.  It does not by itself say that ribosomes, protein abundance, folding, localization, stability, function, phenotype, or a general biological law have been measured.

\paragraph{Supporting finite geometry.}
Several neighboring finite rows constrain how this axis may be read.  The core tRNA-adaptation assay also passed on 11 computed organisms out of 16 requested organisms, with 5 folds, the 500-protein minimum, and 41 synonymous design columns.  The two-axis closure assay concluded that a third axis is present, with the reason that the residual perpendicular direction has systematically positive held-out $R^2$ and mutually same-signed directions across organisms.  The third-axis identity assay identified $d_\perp$ provisionally with usage frequency within family, reporting mean cosine $0.128$ and one-sided sign-test $p=0.000488$.  The corresponding mechanism assay classified the same direction as selection-not-GC and not-wobble-clustered, while leaving the CSC or positional identity in need of additional contact.  Finally, the three-axis sufficiency assay concluded that a fourth mechanism remains present.  These rows make the $f_3$ residual alignment informative but not final: the finite surface contains a strong residual translation-selection direction, yet the coordinate basis still has unresolved higher-order structure.

\paragraph{Translation row requirement.}
The bounded readback for this chapter is the survival entry $S_{f3\text{-}stress,T_j}$ for a declared translation readout $T_j$.  The admissible readouts are tAI or stAI weights as model-bounded codon-adaptation surfaces, or measured ribosome-occupancy and translation-efficiency rows when same-organism and same-CDS data are actually joined.  The intended statistic has the schematic form
$$
\begin{aligned}
S_{f3\text{-}stress,T_j}>0
\end{aligned}
$$
after amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, CDS length, $M$-density, matched mRNA abundance, rank, and simpler-baseline controls.  The present finite row proves no such measured same-row statistic for ribosome occupancy or translation efficiency.  It records a powered residual-axis alignment and a bounded route for later readout testing.  In particular, a three-organism all-$1.0$ controlled matrix would be treated as rank saturation rather than survival evidence, because it cannot distinguish a real readout from a degenerate fitted surface.

\paragraph{Wobble-breaking condition.}
The biological trigger is not third-position topology alone.  The $f_3$ coordinate becomes a candidate dynamical distinction only in an organism or codon context where tRNA or wobble weights break the symmetry between a codon $c$ and its third-position partner $c^{f_3}$.  The local condition is
$$
\begin{aligned}
w_c\ne w_{c^{f_3}}.
\end{aligned}
$$
Without such asymmetry, amino-acid projection can erase the synonymous contrast for translation-readout purposes.  With such asymmetry, the $f_3$-paired imbalance may be tested against bounded translation readouts, but the result is still only a translation-layer survival entry.  The R-sense tRNA-poverty assay gives mechanism pressure at this layer: with $\alpha=0.05$, 1000 permutations, and 40 computed organisms out of 46 requested organisms, it concluded that R sense codons are systematically tRNA-poor under GtRNAdb tGCN plus dos Reis wobble weights.  That supports a tRNA-supply route for R avoidance, while still not promoting the $f_3$ residual coordinate to a protein, structure, function, phenotype, or universality claim.

\paragraph{Local no-promotion rule.}
The chapter therefore has a precise positive statement and a precise refusal boundary.  The positive statement is that the finite powered panel found the strongest non-tRNA residual optimum along $f3\_translation\_selection$, with 5 folds, 11 computed organisms, a 500-protein organism threshold, and 41 synonymous design columns, against the finite $f_3$ saturation inventory above.  The refusal boundary is equally important: no translation realization beyond the bounded statistic, no ribosome-occupancy fact, no protein-abundance fact, no folded-structure fact, no physical-admissibility fact, no molecular-function fact, no mechanism claim, and no universal biological law follows from this coordinate result unless a separate layer-matched contact supplies the corresponding rows and the declared controlled statistic is non-vacuous and positive.  The current record is thus a disciplined cross-layer candidate: strong enough to motivate the $S_{f3\text{-}stress,T_j}$ test, but not allowed to stand in for that test.
