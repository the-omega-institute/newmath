\subsection{The $f_3$-stress residual axis as a bounded translation-row conjecture}
\label{sec:bior reality-q6-f3-stress-residual-axis}

\paragraph{Finite carrier.}
This record concerns the finite coding-sequence by organism residual surface obtained after amino-acid projection, restricted to the $B^*_{Q6}$ coordinate component called $f_3$-stress.  The coordinate is not treated as a phenotype, a protein property, or a biochemical mechanism.  It is a finite synonymous-codon residual coordinate, paired only with organism-scoped codon usage, tRNA or wobble-weight asymmetry, and matched translation readouts.  The controlling biological distinction is the following: the $f_3$ pairing is invisible after amino-acid quotienting unless the translation context assigns unequal weights to a codon $c$ and its third-position partner $c^{f_3}$.  Thus the row is meant to test whether a residual synonymous imbalance survives as a translation-layer signal only in contexts with $w_c \ne w_{c^{f_3}}$.

\paragraph{Closed codon support.}
The finite $f_3$ saturation record gives an explicit codon-level boundary.  The union $M = \operatorname{Med}(R) \cup \{\mathrm{ACA}\}$ contains the twenty codons
$\mathrm{AAA}, \mathrm{AAG}, \mathrm{ACA}, \mathrm{ACG}, \mathrm{AGA}, \mathrm{AGG}, \mathrm{AUA}, \mathrm{AUG}, \mathrm{CUA}, \mathrm{CUC}, \mathrm{CUG}, \mathrm{CUU}, \mathrm{UAA}, \mathrm{UAG}, \mathrm{UCA}, \mathrm{UCG}, \mathrm{UGA}, \mathrm{UGG}, \mathrm{UUA}, \mathrm{UUG}$.  The saturated $f_3$ image of $R$ removes exactly the two ACA/ACG boundary entries and leaves the eighteen-codon set
$\mathrm{AAA}, \mathrm{AAG}, \mathrm{AGA}, \mathrm{AGG}, \mathrm{AUA}, \mathrm{AUG}, \mathrm{CUA}, \mathrm{CUC}, \mathrm{CUG}, \mathrm{CUU}, \mathrm{UAA}, \mathrm{UAG}, \mathrm{UCA}, \mathrm{UCG}, \mathrm{UGA}, \mathrm{UGG}, \mathrm{UUA}, \mathrm{UUG}$.  The recorded $f_3$ boundary set is empty, so the local codon calculation supplies a closed finite coordinate surface rather than an open-ended codon family.

\paragraph{Observed finite-row result.}
The powered residual-axis run reports status passed for experiment $\mathrm{b\_star\_q6\_universal\_optimal\_residual\_axis\_powered}$.  It computed $11$ of $16$ requested organisms, used $5$ folds, required at least $500$ proteins per organism, and used $41$ synonymous-design columns.  Its conclusion is that the non-tRNA residual optimality direction aligns most strongly with $f_3$ translation selection; the recorded dominant axis is $\mathrm{f3\_translation\_selection}$.  In finite-row notation this is the local readback:
$$
\begin{aligned}
N_{\mathrm{computed}} &= 11, & N_{\mathrm{requested}} &= 16,\\
K_{\mathrm{folds}} &= 5, & p_{\min} &= 500,\\
D_{\mathrm{syn}} &= 41, & d_{\mathrm{dom}} &= \mathrm{f3\_translation\_selection}.
\end{aligned}
$$
This is evidence for a residual-axis identity inside the controlled synonymous design.  It is not, by itself, a measured ribosome-occupancy row or a direct protein-output row.

\paragraph{Neighboring finite checks.}
Several adjacent computations constrain the interpretation.  The tRNA-poverty boundary run computed $40$ of $46$ requested organisms, used $1000$ permutations at $\alpha=0.05$, and called the $R$ sense codons systematically tRNA-poor under GtRNAdb tGCN plus dos Reis wobble weights.  The universal core tRNA-adaptation run also passed on $11$ computed organisms, $16$ requested organisms, $5$ folds, $500$ minimum proteins per organism, and $41$ synonymous-design columns.  The two-axis closure check returned $\mathrm{third\_axis\_present}$ under the same $11/16$, $5$-fold, $500$-protein, $41$-column design.  The third-axis identity check identified $d_{\perp}$ provisionally with within-family usage frequency, with mean cosine $0.128$ and one-sided sign-test $p=0.000488$.  The third-axis mechanism check reported selection-not-GC, not wobble-clustered, and retained GC-orthogonalized alignment and held-out prediction.  The per-organism axis-strength spectrum found $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$ leading on average, with dominant-count summary $\{d_{\mathrm{resid4}}:11\}$, and the largest recorded GC3 rank effect was $\rho=0.136$ for $d_{\mathrm{usage}}$.

\paragraph{Residual scope.}
The three-axis sufficiency check returned $\mathrm{fourth\_mechanism\_present}$ rather than closure by tRNA and $f_3$ alone.  The fourth-axis follow-up rows exclude several tempting promotions: the mRNA-stability run reported $\mathrm{fourth\_axis\_not\_mrna\_stability}$; the codon-pair run reported $\mathrm{not\_codon\_pair}$ with $50$ shuffle-null samples; the translation-accuracy run reported $\mathrm{not\_accuracy}$ using $200$ permutations; and the elongation-dwell run in $\mathrm{saccharomyces\_cerevisiae}$ reported only $\mathrm{partial}$.  Therefore the chapter records a positive residual-axis identification, together with an explicit refusal to collapse the remaining signal into a single downstream interpretation.

\paragraph{Translation-row gate.}
The finite conjecture is supported only at the bounded readout level.  A positive claim about the $f_3$-stress coordinate requires a same-organism, same-CDS joined matrix containing codon counts, wobble-breaking tAI or stAI weights, ribosome-profiling or translation-efficiency rows, matched mRNA abundance, and controls for amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, CDS length, $M$-density, rank, and simpler codon baselines.  The relevant statistic is a controlled survival entry $S_{f3\text{-}\mathrm{stress},T_j}$, not an unconstrained association.  The support condition is
$$
\begin{aligned}
S_{f3\text{-}\mathrm{stress},T_j} &> 0
\end{aligned}
$$
for at least one bounded translation readout $T_j$ after the declared controls and baseline ablations.  The break condition is the finite alternative in which all such entries remain non-positive or rank-degenerate.

\paragraph{Local no-promotion boundary.}
No translation realization beyond the bounded statistic follows from the residual coordinate alone.  No folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, localization, stability, phenotype, mechanism, or universality follows from codon geometry, modeled tAI or stAI weights, review readiness, or the passed residual-axis experiment.  Each higher layer requires its own matched measurement surface and its own controlled statistic.  In particular, the three-organism all-$1.0$ controlled matrix is a rank-saturated null boundary, not survival evidence.  The present chapter therefore certifies a finite $f_3$-stress residual-axis target and its observed rank-powered alignment with $f_3$ translation selection, while keeping downstream biological interpretation blocked until the translation-readout row is non-vacuous under the stated controls.
