\subsection{The $B^*_{Q6}$ residual axis as an $f_3$-stress translation-readout claim}\label{subsec:bioreality-q6-f3-residual-axis}\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite carrier.}
The object considered here is a finite coding-sequence surface indexed by organism, synonymous codon family, and residual coordinate after amino-acid projection.  The distinguished coordinate is the $f_3$-stress component of the $B^*_{Q6}$ residual basis.  Its finite support is not introduced as a protein claim; it is a codon-level contrast that can only be tested at the translation layer after rows have been joined to organism-scoped codon usage, tRNA or wobble weights, and a matched translation readout.  The saturation row gives
$$\begin{aligned}
M &= \{\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{ACA},\mathrm{ACG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\},\\
\operatorname{Sat}_{f_3}(R) &= \{\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\},\\
\partial_{f_3} &= \varnothing .
\end{aligned}$$
Thus the finite $M$ row has $20$ codons, the saturated $f_3$ row has $18$ codons, and the recorded $f_3$ boundary is empty.  The difference between the two displayed lists is exactly the pair $\{\mathrm{ACA},\mathrm{ACG}\}$, so the present claim is not a generic codon-usage assertion; it is a residual third-position stress assertion after the amino-acid quotient has removed direct amino-acid composition.

\paragraph{Powered residual-axis result.}
The finite powered row for the selected claim used $5$ folds, required at least $500$ proteins per organism, requested $16$ organisms, retained $11$ computed organisms, and used $41$ synonymous-design columns.  Its recorded outcome is that non-tRNA residual optimality aligns most strongly with the axis named $\mathrm{f3\_translation\_selection}$.  In local notation, if $q_{f_3}$ is the $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinate and $T_j$ ranges over bounded translation readouts, the intended survival statistic has the form
$$\begin{aligned}
S_{f_3,T_j} &= \Delta \operatorname{score}\bigl(T_j \mid q_{f_3}, Z\bigr),\\
Z &= \{\text{amino-acid composition},\text{ organism},\text{ GC3},\text{ CDS length},\text{$M$-density},\text{mRNA control}\}.
\end{aligned}$$
The powered row supports the direction of residual selection only at this bounded coordinate level: the dominant residual axis is $\mathrm{f3\_translation\_selection}$, not a statement that the coordinate already changes ribosome occupancy, protein abundance, localization, stability, folded structure, function, phenotype, or a universal law.

\paragraph{Relation to neighboring finite rows.}
The same finite audit neighborhood fixes the interpretation of the axis.  The core tRNA-adaptation row also ran on $11$ computed organisms out of $16$ requested organisms, with $5$ folds, at least $500$ proteins per organism, and $41$ synonymous-design columns.  The two-axis closure row reports that a third axis is present because the residual direction has systematically positive held-out $R^2$ and mutually same-signed directions across organisms.  The third-axis identity row identifies $d_\perp$ with usage frequency within family, with mean cosine $0.128$ and one-sided sign-test $p=0.000488$.  Its mechanism row keeps the coordinate GC-orthogonal: the recorded conclusion is selection-not-GC, not wobble-clustered, with CSC or positional identity still needing a separate outside measurement.  The three-axis sufficiency row reports a fourth mechanism present.  The axis-strength spectrum row records $11$ organism-level dominant counts for $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$, descriptive domain sample sizes $\{\mathrm{archaea}:1,\mathrm{bacteria}:2,\mathrm{eukaryote}:8\}$, and the largest GC3 rank effect as $d_{\mathrm{usage}}$ with $\rho=0.136$.

\paragraph{Mechanistic boundary.}
The finite rows sharpen but do not overpromote the biological interpretation.  A separate boundary-set row used $40$ computed organisms out of $46$ requested organisms, skipped $6$, used $1000$ permutations at $\alpha=0.05$, and recorded that $R$ sense codons are systematically tRNA-poor under GtRNAdb tGCN with dos Reis wobble weights.  This supports a tRNA-supply pressure for $R$ avoidance, but it does not by itself establish the $f_3$ residual coordinate as an observed translation-survival row.  The fourth-mechanism checks further limit the positive interpretation: mRNA stability is recorded as not the dominant fourth-axis identity in the yeast half-life assay; codon-pair structure is recorded as not the relevant fourth mechanism; translation accuracy is recorded as not the mechanism; elongation dwell is only partial in Saccharomyces cerevisiae.  These rows leave the present claim as a residual-axis and bounded translation-readout conjecture, not as a completed downstream mechanism.

\paragraph{Readout rule and failure mode.}
The row becomes biologically positive only when a joined finite panel supplies codon counts, wobble-breaking tAI or stAI weights, measured ribosome-profiling or translation-efficiency values, and matched mRNA abundance on the same organism and coding-sequence rows.  The local success condition is $S_{f_3,T_j}>0$ after the full control set and simpler-codon-baseline ablation.  The local failure condition is equally explicit: if all controlled entries remain non-positive, or if the fitted surface is rank-degenerate, then the $f_3$-stress coordinate remains an internal residual contrast with no translation-readout survival.  In particular, an all-$1.0$ three-organism matrix is a rank-saturation boundary, not evidence for survival.  Likewise, a modeled tAI or stAI weight can serve as a bounded codon-adaptation readout, but it is not an observed ribosome-occupancy measurement.

\paragraph{Cannot-claim boundary.}
No translation-realization claim follows from the residual geometry alone.  No protein abundance, folded structure, physical admissibility, localization, stability, molecular function, phenotype, mechanism, or universality follows from the displayed finite rows unless a separate layer-matched contact supplies that measurement and the corresponding controlled statistic is recorded.  The present chapter therefore records a finite, positive residual-axis alignment toward $\mathrm{f3\_translation\_selection}$, together with a strict local rule: downstream biological language is permitted only after the same $q_{f_3}$ coordinate survives as a non-vacuous translation-readout entry under the declared controls.
