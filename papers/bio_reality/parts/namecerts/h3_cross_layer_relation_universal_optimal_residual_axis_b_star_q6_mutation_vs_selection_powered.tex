\subsection{The $B^*_{Q6}$ $f_3$-stress residual axis as a bounded translation-readout conjecture}
\label{sec:bioreality-q6-f3-stress-translation-readout}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite coordinate surface.}
This chapter records a finite BioReality claim about the $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinate after amino-acid quotienting.  The carrier is the CDS-by-organism residual surface restricted to the $f_3$-stress coordinate and to translation-layer rows that can be matched to the same coding sequences.  The coordinate is not interpreted as protein output, folding, function, localization, stability, phenotype, or a universal biological rule.  Its only permitted readout in this chapter is a controlled translation-row survival entry $S_{f_3\text{-stress},T_j}$, where $T_j$ may be a bounded tAI/stAI readback or an organism-matched ribosome-occupancy or translation-efficiency row.

The finite $f_3$ saturation record fixes the relevant codon boundary.  The ambient set $M=\operatorname{Med}(R)\cup\{ACA\}$ is
$$
\begin{aligned}
M=\{&AAA,AAG,ACA,ACG,AGA,AGG,AUA,AUG,CUA,CUC,CUG,CUU,\\
&UAA,UAG,UCA,UCG,UGA,UGG,UUA,UUG\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The saturated $f_3$ image removes precisely $ACA$ and $ACG$ from this display:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\operatorname{Sat}_{f_3}(R)=\{&AAA,AAG,AGA,AGG,AUA,AUG,CUA,CUC,CUG,CUU,\\
&UAA,UAG,UCA,UCG,UGA,UGG,UUA,UUG\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The recorded $f_3$ boundary is empty, so the finite obstruction is not a leftover codon in the boundary set.  The remaining scientific question is whether an $f_3$-paired synonymous imbalance survives after amino-acid projection, organism stratification, GC3, CDS length, $M$-density, and mRNA controls have been imposed.

\paragraph{Axis call from the powered residual panel.}
The powered residual-axis run for the named claim passed on $11$ computed organisms out of $16$ requested organisms, with $5$ folds, a minimum of $500$ proteins per organism, and $41$ synonymous-design columns.  Its reported conclusion is that non-tRNA residual optimality aligns most strongly with $f_3\_translation\_selection$, and the dominant axis is recorded as $f_3\_translation\_selection$.  In the notation of this chapter, the finite claim is therefore not merely that a codon-topological direction exists, but that the leading non-tRNA residual direction selected by the powered design is the $f_3$-translation-selection direction.

This axis call is placed next to the companion finite runs only to bound its interpretation.  The core tRNA-adaptation direction also passed on $11$ computed organisms out of $16$ requested organisms, using $5$ folds, at least $500$ proteins per organism, and $41$ synonymous-design columns.  The two-axis closure run returned \emph{third\_axis\_present} under the same $11/16$, $5$-fold, $500$-protein, $41$-column design, with the reason that $d_\perp$ had systematically positive held-out $R^2$ and mutually same-signed directions across organisms.  Thus the $f_3$ residual axis is not a substitute for the tRNA axis; it is the recorded dominant non-tRNA residual component after the core tRNA direction has been separated.

\paragraph{Spectrum and neighboring mechanism filters.}
The per-organism axis-strength spectrum was also computed for $11$ organisms out of $16$ requested organisms, again with $5$ folds, a minimum of $500$ proteins per organism, and $41$ synonymous-design columns.  Its descriptive summary reports that the captured spectra are led on average by $d_{resid4}$, followed by $d_{tRNA}$, and that all $11$ per-organism dominant counts were assigned to $d_{resid4}$.  The same record warns that domain means are descriptive under the imbalanced panel $n=\{\text{archaea}:1,\text{bacteria}:2,\text{eukaryote}:8\}$, and it reports the largest GC3 rank effect as $\rho=0.136$ for $d_{usage}$.

The third-axis identity run classified $d_\perp$ as aligned to usage frequency within family, with mean cosine $0.128$ and a one-sided sign-test value $p=0.000488$.  The corresponding mechanism run reported \emph{selection-not-GC}, \emph{not-wobble-clustered}, and that the CSC or positional identity still needs external evidence; its stated reason is that GC-orthogonalized $d_\perp$ keeps significant cross-species alignment and held-out $R^2$.  These numbers keep the $f_3$ conjecture in a narrow band: the observed finite pattern supports a residual selection-like direction, but not a completed wobble, positional, CSC, or measured ribosome mechanism by itself.

A separate tRNA-poverty mechanism run for the $R$ sense codons passed on $40$ computed organisms out of $46$ requested organisms, skipped $6$ organisms, used $1000$ permutations, and used $\alpha=0.05$.  Its mechanism call states that $R$ sense codons are systematically tRNA-poor under GtRNAdb tGCN plus dos Reis wobble weights, supporting a tRNA-supply mechanism for $R$ avoidance.  This is relevant background because it supplies a finite tRNA-supply asymmetry near the $B^*_{Q6}$ coordinate system, but it does not by itself compute $S_{f_3\text{-stress},T_j}$ for the present $f_3$-stress readout.

\paragraph{Residual mechanism exclusions.}
The three-axis sufficiency run returned \emph{fourth\_mechanism\_present} on $11$ computed organisms out of $16$ requested organisms, using $5$ folds, at least $500$ proteins per organism, and $41$ synonymous-design columns.  The subsequent fourth-mechanism filters narrow what should not be promoted from the $f_3$ residual call.  The mRNA-stability run concluded \emph{fourth\_axis\_not\_mrna\_stability}, with the stated reason that yeast $d_{opt\_halflife}$ has low alignment with abundance $d_{resid4}$ under the fixed gates.  The elongation-dwell run in \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae} concluded \emph{partial}.  The codon-pair run concluded \emph{not\_codon\_pair} using $5$ folds, at least $500$ proteins per organism, $41$ synonymous-design columns, and $50$ shuffle-null draws.  The translation-accuracy run concluded \emph{not\_accuracy} on $11$ computed organisms out of $16$ requested organisms, with $5$ folds, $41$ synonymous-design columns, and $200$ permutations.

These neighboring exclusions matter because the present chapter is not allowed to convert an $f_3$ residual axis into a broad biological explanation.  The finite evidence says that a non-tRNA residual optimality direction is most aligned with $f_3$ translation selection in the powered panel.  It does not say that the axis is mRNA stability, codon-pair structure, translation accuracy, protein abundance, folded structure, physical admissibility, function, phenotype, or a general law.

\paragraph{Readout condition and break condition.}
Let $Q_i$ be the $B^*_{Q6}$ $f_3$-stress coordinate on a finite CDS-by-organism table, and let $T_j$ be a translation-layer row matched to the same CDS or organism scope.  The chapter permits a positive readout only when the controlled survival statistic is non-vacuous:
$$
\begin{aligned}
S_{f_3\text{-stress},T_j}>0
\end{aligned}
$$
after amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, CDS length, $M$-density, matched mRNA abundance, rank, and simpler-baseline controls.  The same condition must remain meaningful after excluding rank-degenerate surfaces; an all-$1.0$ three-organism matrix is a null boundary, not survival evidence.

The conjecture is therefore sharp.  It predicts that the $f_3$-paired synonymous imbalance can become a translation-readout distinction only where context tRNA or wobble weights break third-position $f_3$ symmetry, for example by $w_c\ne w_{c^{f_3}}$.  It is broken if a powered, organism-matched panel keeps all controlled entries non-positive, or if the only apparent entries are rank-saturated artifacts.  The current finite record contains the powered residual-axis call and several mechanism filters, but it does not contain the same-CDS ribosome or translation-efficiency matrix needed to assert measured translation survival.

\paragraph{Local non-promotion rule.}
The bounded statement is this: the $B^*_{Q6}$ $f_3$-stress coordinate is a finite residual axis whose powered non-tRNA optimality call is $f_3\_translation\_selection$ on the recorded $11/16$ organism panel.  A translation-layer conclusion requires a separate matched translation contact and a positive controlled $S_{f_3\text{-stress},T_j}$ row.  Protein abundance requires its own protein-level contact; localization, stability, folding, physical admissibility, function, phenotype, mechanism, and universality each require their own layer-matched contact and statistic.  None of those downstream conclusions follows from codon geometry, tAI or stAI weights, tRNA-copy summaries, axis labels, workflow readiness, or $S$-notation alone.
