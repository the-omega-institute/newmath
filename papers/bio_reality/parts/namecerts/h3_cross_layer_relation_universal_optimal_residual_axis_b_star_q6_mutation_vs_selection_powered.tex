\paragraph{Finite coordinate surface.}
This record concerns the finite coding-sequence surface obtained after projecting synonymous codon counts through the amino-acid quotient and then restricting attention to the $f_3$-stress coordinate inside the $B^*_{Q6}$ residual basis. The row object is not a whole-genome biological law. It is a controlled coordinate comparison across coding sequences, organisms, codon-usage rows, tRNA or wobble-weight rows, and candidate translation-readout rows. The saturated $f_3$ boundary is finite and explicit: the computed $M=\operatorname{Med}(R)\cup\{\mathrm{ACA}\}$ row is
$$
\begin{aligned}
M=&\{\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{ACA},\mathrm{ACG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\\
&\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The corresponding saturated $f_3$ image removes the two ACA/ACG boundary codons and leaves
$$
\begin{aligned}
\operatorname{Sat}_{f_3}(R)=&\{\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\\
&\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\},
\end{aligned}
$$
with empty residual boundary $\partial_{f_3}=\varnothing$ in the finite computation. This is the combinatorial starting point: the $f_3$ coordinate has no right to be read as dynamics merely because the codon geometry exists.

\paragraph{Powered residual-axis result.}
The powered experiment for this row reports status passed for the non-tRNA residual optimality comparison. It used $5$ folds, required at least $500$ proteins per organism, computed $11$ organisms out of $16$ requested organisms, and used $41$ synonymous-design columns. Its finite conclusion is that the non-tRNA residual optimality direction aligns most strongly with the $f_3$ translation-selection candidate, recorded as dominant axis $\mathrm{f3\_translation\_selection}$. Thus the bounded claim is
$$
\begin{aligned}
\operatorname{argmax}_{a\in A_{\mathrm{resid}}}\;\operatorname{Align}(d_{\mathrm{resid}},a)
=\mathrm{f3\_translation\_selection}
\end{aligned}
$$
inside the declared controlled design, not a statement that every organism realizes the same measured translation effect. The adjacent core tRNA-adaptation row was also passed with the same $5$-fold, $500$-protein, $11/16$ organism, and $41$-column design. Together these two finite rows separate a core tRNA-adaptation direction from a residual $f_3$-stress direction, rather than collapsing all codon-choice signal into one tRNA axis.

\paragraph{Spectrum and residual structure.}
The two-axis closure test did not close the powered panel: it returned the conclusion $\mathrm{third\_axis\_present}$ under the same $5$-fold, $500$-protein, $11/16$ organism, $41$-column design, with the reason that the perpendicular direction has systematically positive held-out $R^2$ and mutually same-signed directions across organisms. The identity test for that perpendicular component gave a provisional alignment to usage frequency within family, with mean cosine $0.128$ and one-sided sign-test $p=0.000488$. Its mechanism row reported selection-not-GC, not wobble-clustered, and left the CSC or positional identity as requiring additional contact; the stated reason was that GC-orthogonalized $d_\perp$ keeps significant cross-species alignment and held-out $R^2$. A separate spectrum row found that captured spectra are led on average by $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$ and then $d_{\mathrm{tRNA}}$; in the per-organism dominant count, $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$ led in all $11$ computed organisms. Domain summaries were explicitly descriptive because the domain counts were imbalanced, with $1$ archaeal, $2$ bacterial, and $8$ eukaryotic organisms, and the largest GC3 rank effect was $d_{\mathrm{usage}}$ with $\rho=0.136$.

\paragraph{Translation-readout gate.}
The finite conclusion is therefore a residual-axis classification, not a promotion to an observed ribosome or protein outcome. A translation interpretation is admitted only when a row such as tAI, stAI, ribosome occupancy, or translation efficiency supplies a non-vacuous survival entry after amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, CDS length, M-density, mRNA, and simpler-codon-baseline controls. In symbols, the admissible readout is a controlled survival statistic of the form
$$
\begin{aligned}
S_{f_3,T_j}>0
\end{aligned}
$$
for a declared translation row $T_j$; a rank-degenerate all-$1.0$ surface, a named contact class, or a modeled coupling alone is not such a statistic. The boundary-set tRNA-poverty row supplies mechanism pressure for $R$-sense codons: across $40$ computed organisms out of $46$ requested organisms, with $6$ skipped, $1000$ permutations, and $\alpha=0.05$, it concluded that $R$ sense codons are systematically tRNA-poor under GtRNAdb tGCN plus dos Reis wobble weights. That row supports a tRNA-supply pressure on the boundary, but it still does not by itself measure ribosome occupancy, translation efficiency, protein abundance, folded structure, localization, stability, molecular function, phenotype, physical admissibility, or universality.

\paragraph{Downstream exclusions.}
The fourth-mechanism rows sharpen the same limitation. The sufficiency row concluded $\mathrm{fourth\_mechanism\_present}$ under the $5$-fold, $500$-protein, $11/16$ organism, $41$-column design. Candidate tests then excluded several naive promotions: the mRNA-stability row concluded $\mathrm{fourth\_axis\_not\_mrna\_stability}$ because yeast $d_{\mathrm{opt\_halflife}}$ had low alignment with abundance $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$ under the fixed gates; the codon-pair row concluded $\mathrm{not\_codon\_pair}$ using $50$ shuffle-null samples; the translation-accuracy row concluded $\mathrm{not\_accuracy}$ with $200$ permutations over the $11/16$ organism design; and the elongation-dwell row for \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae} concluded only $\mathrm{partial}$. These rows keep the chapter at the level of controlled residual geometry plus bounded translation-adaptation evidence. No assertion of translation realization, protein abundance, folding, physical admissibility, biological function, mechanism, phenotype, or universal biological law follows unless a separate layer-matched contact supplies joined rows and the declared controlled statistic is positive.
