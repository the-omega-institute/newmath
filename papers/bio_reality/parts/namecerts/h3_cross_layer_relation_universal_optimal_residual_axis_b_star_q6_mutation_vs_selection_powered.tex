\subsection{The $B^*_{Q6}$ $f_3$ residual axis as a bounded translation-readout target}
\label{sec:bioreality-q6-f3-residual-axis}

\paragraph{Finite coordinate surface.}
This record concerns a finite coding-sequence surface whose rows are organism-matched synonymous residual coordinates, restricted to the $B^*_{Q6}$ third-position $f_3$ stress coordinate and to readout rows that are explicitly translation-facing. The internal coordinate is not a protein phenotype and is not a structure claim. It is a residual direction obtained after amino-acid projection, with the biological test restricted to whether a controlled translation readout can still detect the coordinate once amino-acid composition, organism identity, GC3, CDS length, $M$-density, and matched mRNA abundance have been accounted for. The decisive local distinction is therefore not codon topology alone, but the conjunction of three finite features: an $f_3$-paired synonymous imbalance, a context in which tRNA or wobble weights break the $f_3$ pairing symmetry, and a surviving translation-row statistic.

$$
\begin{aligned}

The nontrivial question is instead whether any matched organism supplies asymmetric tRNA or wobble weights, $w_c\neq w_{c^{f_3}}$, and whether that asymmetry remains visible in a controlled translation readout.

\paragraph{Powered residual-axis result.}
The powered residual-axis run $2a526c9fb7f4$ passed its finite gate for the claim identifier $h3.cross\_layer\_relation.universal\_optimal\_residual\_axis.b\_star\_q6\_mutation\_vs\_selection\_powered$. It used $5$ folds, required at least $500$ proteins per organism, computed $11$ organisms out of $16$ requested organisms, and used $41$ synonymous design columns. Within that finite panel, the recorded conclusion is that non-tRNA residual optimality aligns most strongly with the axis named $f3\_translation\_selection$; the dominant axis field is exactly $f3\_translation\_selection$. This supports a rank-powered residual-axis statement: among the candidate non-tRNA residual directions that survived the assay design, the $f_3$ translation-selection direction is the leading explanation in the computed panel. It does not, by itself, assert measured ribosome occupancy or protein output.

\paragraph{Relation to the adjacent axis rows.}
The local interpretation is strengthened by nearby finite rows but remains bounded by their measured scope. The core tRNA-adaptation row $373843e81379$ also passed on $11$ computed organisms out of $16$, with $5$ folds, a $500$-protein minimum, and $41$ synonymous design columns. The two-axis closure row $99dc7e2d5abf$ did not close the system with only the tRNA and $f_3$ axes: it reported a third-axis-present outcome, with systematically positive held-out $R^2$ for $d_\perp$ and mutually same-signed $d_\perp$ directions across organisms. The third-axis identity row $377671f96e96$ provisionally aligned $d_\perp$ with usage frequency within synonymous family, with mean cosine $0.128$ and one-sided sign-test $p=0.000488$. These rows make the $f_3$ residual axis more specific: it sits in a finite residual spectrum where tRNA adaptation is present, two axes are insufficient, and the remaining residual directions are not reducible to a single GC explanation.

\paragraph{Per-organism spectrum and boundary mechanism.}
The axis-strength spectrum run $0146012d8909$ computed $11$ organisms out of $16$ requested, again with $5$ folds, a $500$-protein minimum, and $41$ synonymous design columns. Its captured spectra were led on average by $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$, followed by $d_{\mathrm{tRNA}}$; the per-organism dominant-count summary was $\{d_{\mathrm{resid4}}:11\}$. The same record reports imbalanced domain counts, with $1$ archaeal, $2$ bacterial, and $8$ eukaryotic organisms, so domain-level averaging is descriptive rather than a balanced phylogenetic law. The largest GC3 rank effect in that row was small, with $d_{\mathrm{usage}}$ having $\rho=0.136$. A separate boundary-set row $d7728cc8768e$ computed $40$ organisms out of $46$ requested, skipped $6$, used $1000$ permutations at $\alpha=0.05$, and passed the claim that $R$ sense codons are systematically tRNA-poor under GtRNAdb tGCN plus dos Reis wobble weights. This supplies a plausible tRNA-supply pressure for the $R$-avoidance boundary, but it is still not a measured same-CDS translation-efficiency statistic.

\paragraph{Translation-readout gate.}
For this chapter, the bounded readback is the controlled survival entry $S_{f3\text{-}stress,T_j}$, where $T_j$ is a translation-facing row such as tAI, stAI, ribosome occupancy, or translation efficiency. The local support condition is
$$
\begin{aligned}
S_{f3\text{-}stress,T_j} > 0
\end{aligned}
$$
under the declared covariates and under ablation against simpler codon baselines. The finite residual-axis experiment supplies a positive rank-powered alignment to $f3\_translation\_selection$ in the computed design, while the tAI or stAI contact can serve only as a bounded modeled translation-weight readout. A measured ribosome-profiling or translation-efficiency conclusion would require same-organism and same-CDS codon-count rows, wobble or tRNA-weight rows, measured RPF or TE rows, and matched mRNA rows joined before estimation. Without that joined matrix, $S^{QT}$ notation names a target statistic rather than an observed translation-survival result.

\paragraph{Local refusal boundary.}
The finite rows justify a chapter-specific statement about a residual axis: the non-tRNA residual optimum in the powered panel is led by the $f_3$ translation-selection direction, and this direction is biologically testable only where third-position symmetry is broken by tRNA or wobble context. They do not justify promotion to protein abundance, folded structure, physical admissibility, molecular function, localization, stability, phenotype, mechanism, or a universal biological law. A positive tAI or stAI association is still a modeled codon-adaptation readout, not observed ribosome occupancy. A three-organism all-$1.0$ controlled surface is rank-degenerate and is treated as a null boundary rather than survival evidence. A future positive claim beyond the present finite rows must first produce a non-vacuous controlled translation entry for the same coordinate, and each higher biological layer must be supplied by its own layer-matched contact and statistic.
