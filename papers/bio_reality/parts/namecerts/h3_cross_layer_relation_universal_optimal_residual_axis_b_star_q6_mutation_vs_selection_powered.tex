\subsection{The $f_3$-stress residual axis as a bounded translation-readout problem}
\label{sec:bior reality-q6-f3-stress-residual-axis}

\paragraph{Finite coordinate surface.}
This record concerns the finite coding-sequence residual surface obtained after amino-acid projection, restricted to the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ coordinate whose stress is the third-position involution $f_3$.  The object under test is not a claim about mature proteins or organismal fitness.  It is the narrower question whether an $f_3$-paired synonymous imbalance, once amino-acid composition has been quotiented away, remains visible in a matched translation-readout row after controls for organism, GC3, coding-sequence length, $M$-density, and the available messenger-RNA baseline.  The finite saturation row fixes the codon support used by the record:
$$
\begin{aligned}
M &= \{\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{ACA},\mathrm{ACG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\\
&\qquad \mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\},\\
\operatorname{Sat}_{f_3}(R) &= \{\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\\
&\qquad \mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\},\\
\partial_{f_3} &= \varnothing .
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the finite contact surface has a twenty-codon $M$ row, an eighteen-codon $f_3$-saturated row, and an empty recorded $f_3$ boundary.  The two codons present in $M$ but absent from the saturated row are $\mathrm{ACA}$ and $\mathrm{ACG}$, so the stress coordinate is evaluated against an explicitly finite asymmetry rather than an open-ended codon universe.

\paragraph{Powered residual-axis result.}
The powered residual-axis assay reports that non-tRNA residual optimality aligns most strongly with the axis named $f_3\_translation\_selection$.  The run used five folds, required at least $500$ proteins per organism, computed $11$ organisms out of $16$ requested organisms, and used $41$ synonymous-design columns.  In the notation of this record, the finite result is summarized as
$$
\begin{aligned}
\operatorname{dominant\_axis}(B^{\ast}_{Q6}\setminus \mathrm{tRNA}) &= f_3\_translation\_selection,\\
N_{\mathrm{computed}} &= 11,\\
N_{\mathrm{requested}} &= 16,\\
K_{\mathrm{folds}} &= 5,\\
D_{\mathrm{syn}} &= 41 .
\end{aligned}
$$
This is a rank-powered residual-axis statement: within the available multi-organism panel, the residual direction left after the core tRNA-adaptation component is most consistent with the $f_3$-translation-selection coordinate.  The companion core result was also computed on $11$ of $16$ requested organisms with five folds, the same $500$-protein minimum, and the same $41$ synonymous-design columns; it supplies the bounded separation between the core tRNA-adaptation axis and the residual $f_3$-stress axis.

\paragraph{Spectrum and remaining axes.}
The two-axis closure assay did not close the surface with only the tRNA and $f_3$ axes.  On the same $11$ of $16$ organism panel, five folds, $500$-protein minimum, and $41$ synonymous-design columns, it returned the finite call $\mathrm{third\_axis\_present}$, with the recorded reason that $d_{\perp}$ has systematically positive held-out $R^2$ and mutually same-signed directions across organisms.  The subsequent identity assay provisionally aligned $d_{\perp}$ with within-family usage frequency, with mean cosine $0.128$ and one-sided sign-test $p=0.000488$.  After GC orthogonalization, the third-axis mechanism row was classified as selection-not-GC, not wobble-clustered, with CSC or positional identity still requiring a separate contact.  A three-axis sufficiency assay then returned $\mathrm{fourth\_mechanism\_present}$, again on $11$ computed organisms out of $16$, five folds, and $41$ synonymous-design columns.  The per-organism spectrum is therefore not a one-axis story: the captured spectra are led on average by $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$ and then by $d_{\mathrm{tRNA}}$, the recorded dominant-count table has $\{d_{\mathrm{resid4}}:11\}$, and the largest GC3 rank effect in that spectrum is $d_{\mathrm{usage}}$ with $\rho=0.136$.

\paragraph{Mechanistic boundary inside the finite rows.}
The finite rows support a specific interpretation and block stronger ones.  The boundary-set tRNA-poverty assay was evaluated on $40$ organisms out of $46$ requested organisms, skipped $6$, and used $1000$ permutations at $\alpha=0.05$.  It reports that the $R$ sense codons are systematically tRNA-poor under GtRNAdb tRNA gene-copy numbers together with dos Reis wobble weights, supporting a tRNA-supply mechanism for $R$ avoidance.  That mechanism row is adjacent to, but not identical with, the $f_3$-stress residual-axis row.  The fourth-mechanism probes further constrain the interpretation: the codon-pair assay returned $\mathrm{not\_codon\_pair}$ with $50$ shuffle nulls; the translation-accuracy assay returned $\mathrm{not\_accuracy}$ with $200$ permutations; the mRNA-stability assay returned $\mathrm{fourth\_axis\_not\_mRNA\_stability}$ because yeast $d_{\mathrm{opt\_halflife}}$ had low alignment with abundance $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$ under the fixed gates; and the elongation-dwell assay returned only $\mathrm{partial}$ in \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}.  These finite outcomes leave the $f_3$ residual direction as a controlled residual-selection signal, not as a completed downstream biological mechanism.

\paragraph{Readout condition.}
The local support condition is a survival entry in a translation row, not a direct biological conclusion.  For a residual coordinate $q_i$ and a translation readout $T_j$, the admissible readback is a controlled score $S_{q_i,T_j}$ computed after the declared covariates.  In this chapter the relevant coordinate is the $f_3$-stress coordinate, so the non-vacuous condition has the form
$$
\begin{aligned}
S_{f_3\text{-stress},T_j} &> 0,\\
T_j &\in \{\mathrm{tAI},\mathrm{stAI},\mathrm{ribosome\ occupancy},\mathrm{translation\ efficiency}\},\\
Z &\supseteq \{\mathrm{amino\ acid\ composition},\mathrm{organism},\mathrm{GC3},\mathrm{length},M\text{-}\mathrm{density},\mathrm{mRNA\ control}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The present finite table supports the rank-powered residual-axis call and records the wobble-breaking contact route, but it does not itself contain same-CDS joined ribosome-profiling rows, matched mRNA rows, and codon-count rows sufficient to certify a measured ribosome or translation-efficiency survival statistic for this exact $f_3$-stress packet.  Modelled tAI or stAI weights may be used as bounded codon-adaptation readouts, provided they are not described as observed ribosome occupancy.

\paragraph{Cannot-claim boundary.}
The finite result therefore licenses only the bounded statement that, in the computed panel, the non-tRNA residual optimality direction is led by an $f_3$-translation-selection axis and is meaningful only where context tRNA or wobble weights break the third-position symmetry.  It does not establish observed translation, ribosome occupancy, protein abundance, folded structure, physical admissibility, molecular function, localization, stability, phenotype, mechanism, or universality.  Any such assertion requires its own layer-matched contact and its own controlled statistic.  In particular, an all-$1.0$ three-organism fitted surface is a rank-degenerate boundary rather than survival evidence, and a positive topology or model-coupling row is not promoted to $S_{f_3\text{-stress},T_j}>0$ without the matched translation-readout computation.
