\subsection{The $f_3$-stress residual axis as a bounded translation-readout claim}\label{sec:bioreality-q6-f3-stress-translation-readout}\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite carrier.}
This record concerns the finite coding-sequence surface obtained after amino-acid projection, restricted to the $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinate that detects third-position $f_3$ imbalance. The coordinate is not read as a protein property. Its biological question is narrower: whether a synonym-paired residual direction can remain visible in a translation-layer row after organism, amino-acid composition, GC3, CDS length, $M$-density, and matched mRNA controls have been imposed. The relevant finite saturation row is explicit. The saturated $f_3$ set is
$$
\begin{aligned}
\operatorname{Sat}_{f_3}(R)=\{&\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\\
&\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The larger row $M=\operatorname{Med}_R\cup\{\mathrm{ACA}\}$ contains the same eighteen codons together with $\mathrm{ACA}$ and $\mathrm{ACG}$. Thus the finite comparison is not an open-ended codon-space assertion: the only named excess pair in the listed $M$ row is $\{\mathrm{ACA},\mathrm{ACG}\}$, and the recorded $f_3$ boundary is empty. This makes the conjecture a statement about a residual coordinate on a fixed codon list, not about arbitrary nucleotide grammar.

\paragraph{Powered residual-axis outcome.}
The powered residual-axis computation reports status passed for the experiment $\mathrm{b\_star\_q6\_universal\_optimal\_residual\_axis\_powered}$. It used $5$ folds, required at least $500$ proteins per organism, computed $11$ organisms out of $16$ requested, and used $41$ synonymous design columns. Its finite conclusion is that the non-tRNA residual optimality direction aligns most strongly with $\mathrm{f3\_translation\_selection}$; the recorded dominant axis is exactly $\mathrm{f3\_translation\_selection}$. The coordinate should therefore be presented as a rank-powered residual-selection candidate: after the tRNA-adaptation component is separated, the best named residual direction in this finite assay is the $f_3$ translation-selection axis.

\paragraph{Relation to the surrounding finite spectrum.}
The same finite panel gives several consistency checks. The core optimal direction assay passed on $11$ of $16$ requested organisms with $5$ folds, at least $500$ proteins per organism, and $41$ synonymous design columns, identifying the core direction with tRNA adaptation. The two-axis closure assay, under the same organism and design-column counts, concluded that a third axis is still present: $d_\perp$ has systematically positive held-out $R^2$ and same-signed organism directions. The separate identity assay provisionally aligns $d_\perp$ with usage frequency within family, with mean cosine $0.128$ and one-sided sign-test $p=0.000488$. The mechanism assay then reports that this third direction is selection-not-GC, not wobble-clustered, and that the CSC or positional identity still needs an outside measurement layer. These rows prevent an over-simple reading in which the $f_3$ residual axis is merely GC content, merely tRNA supply, or already a complete mechanism.

\paragraph{Boundary-set evidence.}
The boundary-set tRNA-poverty assay was run on $40$ computed organisms out of $46$ requested, with $6$ skipped, $1000$ permutations, and $\alpha=0.05$. Its call is that $R$ sense codons are systematically tRNA-poor under GtRNAdb tGCN plus dos Reis wobble weights, supporting a tRNA-supply explanation for $R$ avoidance. This row is compatible with the residual-axis result but does not replace it. The poverty result is a supply-side comparison on a boundary set; the present $f_3$-stress claim asks whether the residual coordinate itself survives in a translation-readout row after controls. Supply asymmetry can motivate the test, while survival must still be computed on the declared readout surface.

\paragraph{Fourth-axis controls.}
The sufficiency assay concluded that a fourth mechanism is present after the three-axis representation, again with $11$ computed organisms out of $16$ requested, $5$ folds, at least $500$ proteins per organism, and $41$ synonymous design columns. Candidate fourth-mechanism probes narrow the interpretation. The mRNA-stability assay calls the fourth axis not mRNA stability because the yeast $d_{\mathrm{opt\_halflife}}$ direction has low alignment with the abundance-side $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$ under the fixed gates. The codon-pair assay returns $\mathrm{not\_codon\_pair}$ using $50$ shuffle nulls. The translation-accuracy assay returns $\mathrm{not\_accuracy}$ on $11$ computed organisms out of $16$ requested with $200$ permutations. The elongation-dwell assay is only partial in \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}. These finite rows make the residual result more specific but also more constrained: the present coordinate is a candidate translation-selection residual, not an omnibus explanation for all remaining biological variance.

\paragraph{Readout rule.}
The bounded readout for this subsection is a controlled survival entry $S_{f_3,T_j}$, where $T_j$ is a translation-layer row such as tAI, stAI, measured ribosome occupancy, or measured translation efficiency. The admissible positive form is
$$
\begin{aligned}
S_{f_3,T_j}>0
\end{aligned}
$$
only after amino-acid composition, organism identity, GC3, CDS length, $M$-density, matched mRNA abundance, rank sufficiency, and simpler codon baselines have been accounted for. A modeled tAI or stAI surface may supply a bounded codon-adaptation readback, but it is not the same object as ribosome footprints or translation-efficiency measurements. A rank-degenerate all-$1.0$ surface on three organisms is therefore a null boundary rather than survival evidence.

\paragraph{Local no-promotion rule.}
Nothing in this finite row proves translation output, ribosome occupancy, protein abundance, folded structure, physical admissibility, molecular function, organismal phenotype, mechanism, or universality by itself. A positive $S_{f_3,T_j}$ row would license only the corresponding controlled translation-readout survival statement. Protein abundance would require a matched protein row; localization, stability, folding, function, phenotype, and physical admissibility would each require a separate layer-matched assay and its own controlled statistic. Conversely, a non-positive or rank-degenerate $S_{f_3,T_j}$ row would falsify this translation-readout conjecture without showing that the $B^*_{Q6}$ coordinate is biologically meaningless in every other layer. The local conclusion is therefore finite and conditional: the powered residual-axis surface identifies $\mathrm{f3\_translation\_selection}$ as the leading non-tRNA residual direction, while the biological interpretation remains bounded to the translation row actually measured or explicitly modeled.
