\subsection{The $B^{*}_{Q6}$ $f_{3}$-stress residual axis}
\label{sec:bioreality-q6-f3-stress-residual-axis}

\paragraph{Finite carrier.}
The record concerns a finite coding-sequence by organism surface, restricted to the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual coordinate that remains after amino-acid projection and then queried against translation-layer rows.  The coordinate is not a free biological narrative: it is the $f_{3}$-paired synonymous imbalance left after codons are compared across the third-position involution, with the comparison made only after amino-acid identity has been quotiented out.  In the finite saturation row, the ambient set $M = \operatorname{Med}_{R} \cup \{ACA\}$ is
$$
\begin{aligned}
M = \{&AAA,AAG,ACA,ACG,AGA,AGG,AUA,AUG,CUA,CUC,CUG,CUU,\\
&UAA,UAG,UCA,UCG,UGA,UGG,UUA,UUG\},
\end{aligned}
$$
whereas the $f_{3}$-saturation of $R$ removes $ACA$ and $ACG$ and leaves
$$
\begin{aligned}
\operatorname{Sat}_{f_{3}}(R)=\{&AAA,AAG,AGA,AGG,AUA,AUG,CUA,CUC,CUG,CUU,\\
&UAA,UAG,UCA,UCG,UGA,UGG,UUA,UUG\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The boundary row for $f_{3}$ is empty in this finite run, so the coordinate is being tested as a closed residual direction rather than as an unresolved boundary codon set.  This is why the chapter treats the $f_{3}$-stress axis as a finite residual coordinate and not as a claim about all synonymous codon biology.

\paragraph{Powered residual-axis row.}
The central powered row is the claim with experiment run $2a526c9fb7f4$.  It used a deterministic seed, five folds, a minimum of $500$ proteins per organism, and $41$ synonymous design columns.  Of $16$ requested organisms, $11$ were computed.  The reported finite conclusion is that non-tRNA residual optimality aligns most strongly with the axis named $f3\_translation\_selection$; in the row language, the dominant axis is $f3\_translation\_selection$.  The bounded mathematical content is therefore the following: after the tRNA-adaptation direction is separated from the residual surface, the leading non-tRNA residual direction selected by the finite panel is an $f_{3}$-stress translation-selection axis.  This does not by itself assert measured ribosome occupancy, mature protein abundance, folded state, function, phenotype, or a universal biological law.  It says that, inside the finite residual coordinate system and the declared organism panel, the best-labelled residual optimality direction is the $f_{3}$-translation-selection direction.

\paragraph{Relation to the surrounding finite spectrum.}
The residual-axis row is coherent with the neighboring powered rows.  The tRNA-adaptation row was also computed on $11$ of $16$ requested organisms with five folds, $500$ proteins per organism, and $41$ synonymous design columns, so the residual-axis conclusion is being read after a comparable core tRNA-adaptation computation.  The two-axis closure row did not close the surface: it reported a third axis, with the reason that $d_{\perp}$ has systematically positive held-out $R^{2}$ and that the $d_{\perp}$ directions are mutually same-signed across organisms.  The third-axis identity row then provisionally identified $d_{\perp}$ with within-family usage frequency, with mean cosine $0.128$ and one-sided sign-test $p=0.000488$.  The mechanism row for that same third direction reported selection-not-GC, not wobble-clustered, and stated that the CSC or positional identity still needs an additional contact.  Thus the $f_{3}$ residual-axis row sits in a spectrum where tRNA adaptation is present, a second residual direction is not sufficient, a third direction survives, and the residual axis named here is the leading non-tRNA optimality label rather than a completed mechanistic explanation.

\paragraph{Rank and organism scope.}
The organism scope is finite and imbalanced.  The axis-strength spectrum row computed $11$ organisms and reported that captured spectra are led by $d_{resid4}$ on average, followed by $d_{tRNA}$; the per-organism dominant-count entry was $\{d_{resid4}:11\}$.  Its domain means were explicitly descriptive under the imbalance $\{\mathrm{archaea}:1,\mathrm{bacteria}:2,\mathrm{eukaryote}:8\}$, and its largest GC3 rank effect was $d_{usage}$ with $\rho=0.136$.  These numbers keep the readback finite: the panel is powered enough to reject the three-organism rank-degenerate all-$1.0$ surface as evidence, but it is not a census of biology.  The row count and fold count support a controlled finite comparison; they do not supply universality outside the sampled organisms or outside the declared residual coordinates.

\paragraph{Wobble breaking and the translation row.}
The biological interpretation is allowed only at the translation-readout boundary.  The $f_{3}$ coordinate becomes dynamically meaningful only when context tRNA or wobble weights break the third-position pairing, so the relevant local contrast is $w_{c}\neq w_{c^{f_{3}}}$.  The boundary-set tRNA-poverty row gives one finite mechanism-side contact: across $40$ computed organisms out of $46$ requested, with $1000$ permutations at $\alpha=0.05$, $R$ sense codons were reported as systematically tRNA-poor under GtRNAdb tGCN plus dos Reis wobble weights.  This supports a tRNA-supply interpretation for $R$ avoidance, but it still remains a mechanism-side bounded contact.  It is not the same as a measured $S_{f3\text{-}stress,T_j}$ translation-survival entry.  A positive translation claim requires an organism-matched row for tAI, stAI, ribosome occupancy, or translation efficiency after controls for amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, CDS length, M-density, and matched mRNA abundance where translation efficiency is computed.

\paragraph{Fourth-axis exclusions.}
The finite rows also constrain over-interpretation of the residual direction.  The three-axis sufficiency row reported fourth\_mechanism\_present on the same $11$ of $16$ requested organisms, with five folds, $500$ proteins per organism, and $41$ synonymous design columns.  The available fourth-mechanism identity rows do not identify that fourth mechanism with several tempting downstream labels.  The mRNA-stability row concluded fourth\_axis\_not\_mrna\_stability because the yeast $d_{opt\_halflife}$ had low alignment with abundance $d_{resid4}$ under the fixed gates.  The codon-pair row concluded not\_codon\_pair using $50$ shuffle nulls.  The translation-accuracy row concluded not\_accuracy on $11$ computed organisms out of $16$, with $200$ permutations.  The elongation-dwell row for \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae} was partial.  These exclusions matter for the present chapter because they stop the $f_{3}$ residual-axis label from being silently promoted to a broad downstream mechanism.

\paragraph{Local test and refusal condition.}
The finite test can be stated without appealing to any other chapter.  For each admissible organism and each matched coding sequence, form the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ $f_{3}$-stress residual coordinate after amino-acid quotienting.  Pair it with a translation readout $T_{j}$ only when the organism supplies codon usage, tRNA or wobble weights, and a matched translation layer such as bounded tAI, bounded stAI, ribosome occupancy, or translation efficiency.  The controlled survival entry is positive only when
$$
\begin{aligned}
S_{f3\text{-}stress,T_j} > 0
\end{aligned}
$$
after amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, CDS length, M-density, matched mRNA, rank, and simpler-baseline controls.  The conjectural relation is falsified for this readout if the controlled entries remain non-positive, rank-degenerate, or indistinguishable from simpler codon baselines across the powered panel.  In particular, an all-$1.0$ matrix on three organisms is a saturated null boundary, not a positive survival result.

\paragraph{No-promotion boundary.}
The chapter therefore records a ready finite residual-axis claim and a still-gated biological readout.  The ready part is the powered finite observation that the non-tRNA residual optimality axis aligns most with $f3\_translation\_selection$ under the declared run.  The gated part is every stronger biological sentence.  No translation realization follows from codon geometry alone; no ribosome occupancy follows from a modelled tAI or stAI weight alone; no protein abundance, folded structure, physical admissibility, localization, stability, molecular function, phenotype, causal mechanism, or universal law follows from the residual-axis row.  Each of those stronger readings requires its own layer-matched contact and its own controlled statistic after the translation row has first become non-vacuous and positive.
