\subsection{The $f_3$-stress residual axis as a bounded translation-readout target}\label{sec:h3-cross-layer-relation-universal-optimal-residual-axis-b-star-q6-mutation-vs-selection-powered}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite carrier.} The finite surface considered here is the coding-sequence-by-organism residual coordinate table restricted to the $B^*_{Q6}$ $f_3$-stress coordinate and to translation-layer rows that can be matched to the same organismal codon system. The coordinate is read only after amino-acid quotienting: synonymous codons are compared inside their amino-acid fibers, and an $f_3$-paired imbalance is meaningful only when the associated context weight is not symmetric, $w_c \ne w_{c^{f_3}}$. In the finite saturation check, the ambient set $M=\operatorname{Med}(R)\cup\{\mathrm{ACA}\}$ contains exactly twenty codons,
$\{\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{ACA},\mathrm{ACG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\}$. The $f_3$-saturated $R$ set removes the two $ACN$ boundary entries $\mathrm{ACA}$ and $\mathrm{ACG}$ and leaves the eighteen-codon list
$\{\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\}$. The recorded $f_3$ boundary set is empty, so the chapter does not introduce an additional boundary codon outside this finite row.

\paragraph{Observed finite-row signal.} The powered residual-axis run reports status passed for experiment \texttt{b\_star\_q6\_universal\_optimal\_residual\_axis\_powered}. It used $5$ folds, required at least $500$ proteins per organism, computed $11$ organisms out of $16$ requested, and used $41$ synonymous-design columns. Its finite conclusion is that non-tRNA residual optimality aligns most strongly with $\mathrm{f3\_translation\_selection}$; equivalently, the dominant residual axis is recorded as $\mathrm{f3\_translation\_selection}$. This is a coordinate-and-readout alignment statement, not a claim that the coordinate has already been observed as ribosome motion or as a protein-level outcome.

\paragraph{Relation to the surrounding axis spectrum.} The same finite panel places the residual-axis result inside a larger $B^*_{Q6}$ axis profile. The core tRNA-adaptation run also passed on $5$ folds, $11/16$ organisms, a $500$-protein minimum, and $41$ synonymous-design columns. The two-axis closure run did not close the picture: it returned \texttt{third\_axis\_present}, with the recorded reason that $d_\perp$ has systematically positive held-out $R^2$ and mutually same-signed directions across organisms. The identity run for $d_\perp$ identified it provisionally with usage frequency within family, with mean cosine $0.128$ and one-sided sign-test $p=0.000488$. The mechanism run then kept this third axis GC-orthogonalized and selection-facing: its conclusion was \texttt{selection-not-GC; not-wobble-clustered; CSC/positional identity needs\_external}. A separate spectrum run found that captured spectra were led on average by $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$ and then by $d_{\mathrm{tRNA}}$; all $11$ computed organisms had $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$ as the dominant count, the domain split was imbalanced with $1$ archaeal, $2$ bacterial, and $8$ eukaryotic organisms, and the largest GC3 rank effect was $\rho=0.136$ for $d_{\mathrm{usage}}$.

\paragraph{Break condition and survival statistic.} The intended bounded readout is a controlled survival entry $S_{f3\text{-}\mathrm{stress},T_j}$ for $T_j$ equal to a tAI row, an stAI row, or an organism-matched ribosome-occupancy or translation-efficiency row. The statistic is not evaluated from codon topology alone. The row is positive only if the $f_3$-stress coordinate improves the translation readout after controls for amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, coding-sequence length, $M$-density, matched mRNA abundance when TE is used, rank sufficiency, and simpler codon baselines. The local decision rule is
$$
\begin{aligned}
S_{f3\text{-}\mathrm{stress},T_j} &> 0 &&\text{supports a bounded translation-row survival statement,}\\
S_{f3\text{-}\mathrm{stress},T_j} &\le 0 &&\text{keeps the coordinate at the null boundary for that readout.}
\end{aligned}
$$
The present finite row supplies a passed residual-axis alignment and related spectrum facts; it does not yet supply a same-CDS measured ribosome or TE matrix for this exact $f_3$-stress packet.

\paragraph{Mechanistic pressure without over-promotion.} The tRNA-poverty boundary run gives a separate mechanism-facing constraint: $R$ sense codons were systematically tRNA-poor under GtRNAdb tGCN plus dos Reis wobble weights, using $40$ computed organisms out of $46$ requested, $6$ skipped organisms, $1000$ permutations, and $\alpha=0.05$. This supports a tRNA-supply explanation for $R$ avoidance, while the residual-axis run points to $\mathrm{f3\_translation\_selection}$ as the dominant non-tRNA residual optimality axis. These statements are compatible, but they are not the same statement: the first concerns tRNA supply at the $R$ boundary, and the second concerns the leading residual optimality direction after tRNA adaptation is separated.

\paragraph{Higher-layer exclusions.} Several powered checks delimit what the chapter may not infer. Three-axis sufficiency returned \texttt{fourth\_mechanism\_present}, so the tRNA plus $f_3$ span is not treated as a complete explanatory closure. The fourth-axis screens exclude several simple promotions: mRNA stability was reported as \texttt{fourth\_axis\_not\_mrna\_stability}, codon-pair identity as \texttt{not\_codon\_pair}, translation accuracy as \texttt{not\_accuracy}, and elongation dwell as only \texttt{partial} in \textit{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}. Therefore the finite row is best read as a controlled residual-axis signal that nominates an $f_3$-translation-selection direction under the available codon and modeled adaptation data.

\paragraph{Local claim boundary.} No assertion about realized translation dynamics, folded structure, physical admissibility, molecular function, organismal phenotype, mechanism beyond the bounded readout, or universality follows from this chapter by itself. A tAI or stAI surface may be used as a bounded translation-adaptation row, and a ribosome-profiling or TE row may test the same coordinate only when same-organism and same-CDS rows are joined with the declared controls. Protein abundance, localization, stability, structure, function, phenotype, and general biological law each require their own layer-matched measurement rows and their own controlled statistic. The finite result here is narrower: in the powered $B^*_{Q6}$ residual panel, the non-tRNA residual optimum is led by $\mathrm{f3\_translation\_selection}$, and the coordinate remains falsifiable by a non-positive or rank-degenerate $S_{f3\text{-}\mathrm{stress},T_j}$ surface.
