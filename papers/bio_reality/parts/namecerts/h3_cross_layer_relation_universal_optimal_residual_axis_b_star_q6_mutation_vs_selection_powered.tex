\subsection{The $B^*_{Q6}$ $f_3$-stress residual axis as a bounded translation-readout question}
\label{sec:bioreality-q6-f3-stress-translation-readout}

\paragraph{Object.}
This record concerns the finite coding-sequence residual surface obtained after amino-acid projection, restricted to the $B^*_{Q6}$ coordinate family and, more specifically, to the $f_3$-stress direction. The biological question is not whether codon geometry alone proves a phenotype. It is whether the synonymous residual coordinate retains a controlled association with a translation-layer readout after amino-acid composition, organism identity, $GC3$, coding length, $M$-density, and matched mRNA controls have been accounted for. The finite row identifies the relevant biological object as a multi-organism coding-sequence panel with codon usage, tRNA or wobble weights, and matched translation readouts, but the present verified run is a powered residual-axis assay rather than a completed measured ribosome-occupancy survival matrix.

\paragraph{Finite codon support.}
The $f_3$ saturation row fixes the codon-side support before any cross-layer interpretation is attempted. The finite set $M={\rm Med}_R\cup\{\mathrm{ACA}\}$ contains 20 codons:
$$
\begin{aligned}
M=\{&\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{ACA},\mathrm{ACG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\\
&\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The saturated $f_3$ image of $R$ removes $\mathrm{ACA}$ and $\mathrm{ACG}$ and leaves 18 codons:
$$
\begin{aligned}
{\rm Sat}_{f_3}(R)=\{&\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\\
&\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The verified boundary list for the $f_3$ row is empty. Thus the codon-side trigger is not an unresolved boundary codon; it is the survival, or failure, of a residual coordinate after quotienting by amino acid and after applying the declared organism and sequence covariates.

\paragraph{Powered residual-axis result.}
The central finite-row computation for this chapter is the powered $B^*_{Q6}$ universal optimal residual-axis run. It was recorded as passed with run id \texttt{2a526c9fb7f4}. The design used 5 folds, required at least 500 proteins per organism, computed 11 organisms out of 16 requested organisms, and used 41 synonymous design columns. Its reported conclusion is that non-tRNA residual optimality aligns most strongly with \texttt{f3\_translation\_selection}; the stored dominant axis is also \texttt{f3\_translation\_selection}. In the notation of this section, the finite result supports the following bounded statement:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\operatorname*{argmax}_{a\in A_{\rm residual}}\; \operatorname{score}(B^*_{Q6},a)
  &= f_3\text{-translation-selection},\\
N_{\rm computed}&=11,\\
N_{\rm requested}&=16,\\
K_{\rm folds}&=5,\\
D_{\rm syn}&=41.
\end{aligned}
$$
This is a residual-axis identification inside the available finite panel. It is not, by itself, a measured translation-efficiency claim, because the row does not contain same-CDS ribosome-footprint or translation-efficiency entries joined to matched mRNA denominators.

\paragraph{Relation to the surrounding axis spectrum.}
The same finite evidence packet gives context for why the $f_3$ residual axis is interpreted as a translation-readout candidate rather than as a generic codon-usage artifact. In the per-organism axis-strength spectrum, 11 organisms were computed from 16 requested organisms, again with 5 folds, a 500-protein minimum, and 41 synonymous design columns. The captured spectra were led on average by \texttt{d\_resid4}, with per-organism dominant counts $\{\texttt{d\_resid4}:11\}$. Domain means were explicitly marked descriptive under the imbalanced domain counts $\{\text{archaea}:1,\text{bacteria}:2,\text{eukaryote}:8\}$, and the largest reported $GC3$ rank effect was only $\rho=0.136$ for \texttt{d\_usage}. The two-axis closure row likewise computed 11 of 16 organisms with 5 folds and concluded \texttt{third\_axis\_present}; its reason was that $d_\perp$ had systematically positive held-out $R^2$ and mutually same-signed directions across organisms. The sufficiency row then concluded \texttt{fourth\_mechanism\_present}, so the $f_3$ residual signal should be read as one member of a structured residual spectrum, not as a complete explanation of synonymous selection.

\paragraph{Mechanistic restrictions already forced by the finite rows.}
The tRNA-poverty assay for the $R$ sense codons used 40 computed organisms out of 46 requested organisms, skipped 6 organisms, and used 1000 permutations at $\alpha=0.05$. Its finite conclusion was that $R$ sense codons are systematically tRNA-poor under GtRNAdb tRNA gene copy number with dos Reis wobble weights, supporting a tRNA-supply explanation for $R$ avoidance. This supplies a plausible route by which wobble or tRNA weights can break the formal $f_3$ pairing symmetry. However, the residual-axis chapter is not allowed to promote that route into a universal mechanism. The third-axis identity row reported mean cosine $0.128$ and one-sided sign-test $p=0.000488$ for alignment to usage frequency within family, while the third-axis mechanism row concluded \texttt{selection-not-GC}, \texttt{not-wobble-clustered}, and that the CSC or positional identity still needs an external contact. These finite rows narrow the interpretation: the $f_3$ stress coordinate is compatible with translation-selection structure, and it is not explained away by the stored $GC3$ summary, but the exact physical readout remains conditional on a joined translation dataset.

\paragraph{Negative controls on higher-layer promotion.}
The fourth-mechanism rows are important because they block several tempting upgrades. The mRNA-stability assay passed but concluded \texttt{fourth\_axis\_not\_mrna\_stability}; its reason was that yeast \texttt{d\_opt\_halflife} has low alignment with abundance \texttt{d\_resid4} under the fixed gates. The elongation-dwell row concluded \texttt{partial} for \textit{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}. The codon-pair row concluded \texttt{not\_codon\_pair} using 5 folds, a 500-protein minimum, 41 synonymous design columns, and 50 shuffle nulls. The translation-accuracy row concluded \texttt{not\_accuracy} with 11 computed organisms out of 16 requested organisms, 5 folds, 41 synonymous design columns, and 200 permutations. These results leave a residual translation-selection axis, but they do not identify protein abundance, folding, localization, stability, molecular function, organismal phenotype, or a general biological law.

\paragraph{Readout rule.}
The local readout rule is therefore deliberately narrow. The coordinate may be interpreted only through a bounded survival entry $S_{f3\text{-stress},T_j}$ for a declared translation readout $T_j$, such as tAI, stAI, or a ribosome-occupancy or translation-efficiency row. A positive result must remain positive after the controls used in the packet: amino-acid composition, organism, $GC3$, coding length, $M$-density, matched mRNA control where translation efficiency is measured, rank sufficiency, and simpler codon-baseline ablation. If the corresponding matrix is rank-degenerate, for example an all-1.0 three-organism controlled surface, then it is a null boundary rather than evidence of survival. In particular, modeled tAI or stAI weights can be used as bounded adaptation readbacks, but they are not themselves observed ribosome footprints.

\paragraph{Falsification boundary.}
The conjecture is falsified at the translation-readout interface if, across a rank-powered panel, all controlled entries satisfy $S_{f3\text{-stress},T_j}\leq 0$, or if the apparent entries collapse to a saturated or rank-degenerate surface after controls. It is supported only if at least one declared translation row gives a non-vacuous positive survival value under the full control set. The present finite facts justify the residual-axis target and the wobble-broken trigger condition, especially the passed powered residual-axis run and the empty $f_3$ boundary list, but they do not yet supply the same-organism and same-CDS measured ribosome or translation-efficiency matrix required for an observed translation claim.

\paragraph{Local no-promotion boundary.}
This chapter makes no claim that the $B^*_{Q6}$ $f_3$-stress coordinate causes measured translation, changes mature protein abundance, determines folded structure, establishes physical admissibility, realizes molecular function, produces a phenotype, or states a universal biological rule. Each of those assertions would require its own layer-matched contact and its own controlled statistic. The finite result here is a sharper and more limited one: in the powered residual-axis assay, the non-tRNA residual optimum is the $f_3$ translation-selection direction, computed on 11 organisms with 5 folds and 41 synonymous design columns, while the measured translation-survival row remains an explicit next contact boundary.
