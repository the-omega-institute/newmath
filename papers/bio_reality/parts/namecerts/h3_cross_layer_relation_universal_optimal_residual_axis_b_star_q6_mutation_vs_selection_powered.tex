\subsection{The $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ $f_3$-Stress Residual Axis as a Bounded Translation-Readout Target}\label{sec:bioreality-q6-f3-stress-translation-readout}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite carrier.}
This record concerns a finite coding-sequence surface indexed by organism and by synonymous codon residual coordinates after amino-acid projection.  The distinguished coordinate is the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ $f_3$-stress axis: it compares codons paired by the third-position operation $f_3$ after the amino-acid identity has already been quotiented out.  The finite saturation calculation fixes the relevant row geometry.  The set $M=\mathrm{Med}\cup R\cup\{\mathrm{ACA}\}$ is
$$
\begin{aligned}
M=\{&\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{ACA},\mathrm{ACG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\\
&\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The $f_3$-saturated $R$ surface removes the two codons $\mathrm{ACA}$ and $\mathrm{ACG}$ and leaves
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{Sat}_{f_3}(R)=\{&\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\\
&\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The reported $f_3$ boundary is empty, so this chapter treats the stress coordinate as a closed finite residual coordinate rather than as an unresolved boundary term.

\paragraph{Rank-powered residual result.}
The powered residual-axis experiment identified the non-tRNA residual optimum with the axis named $\mathrm{f3\_translation\_selection}$.  The run used five folds, required at least $500$ proteins per organism, computed $11$ organisms out of $16$ requested organisms, and used $41$ synonymous-design columns.  Its deterministic seed was the declared SHA-tagged seed for the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ universal optimal residual-axis experiment, and the status was passed.  In the notation of this chapter, the result is summarized as
$$
\begin{aligned}
\operatorname*{argmax}_{d\in D_{\mathrm{non\text{-}tRNA}}} \operatorname{Align}(d,B^{\ast}_{Q6})
=\mathrm{f3\_translation\_selection},
\end{aligned}
$$
where $D_{\mathrm{non\text{-}tRNA}}$ denotes the non-tRNA residual candidate family used by the finite design.  This is a coordinate-level optimality statement.  It does not by itself say that ribosomes, protein abundance, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, phenotype, mechanism, or universality have been observed.

\paragraph{Relation to the tRNA and higher-axis spectrum.}
The companion powered rows locate the $f_3$ residual axis inside a broader finite spectrum.  The tRNA-core direction passed on the same $11$ computed organisms out of $16$, with five folds, at least $500$ proteins per organism, and $41$ synonymous-design columns.  The two-axis closure test concluded $\mathrm{third\_axis\_present}$ rather than closure by tRNA plus $f_3$ alone; its reason was that the perpendicular direction had systematically positive held-out $R^2$ and mutually same-signed organism directions.  The third-axis identity row then aligned $d_{\perp}$ to usage frequency within synonymous family, with mean cosine $0.128$ and one-sided sign-test $p=0.000488$.  The mechanism row kept the third axis GC-orthogonal and classified it as selection rather than GC, not wobble-clustered, with CSC or positional identity still requiring additional measurements.

\paragraph{Strength spectrum.}
The per-organism axis-strength spectrum also used five folds, at least $500$ proteins per organism, $11$ computed organisms out of $16$, and $41$ synonymous-design columns.  Its descriptive conclusion was that $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$ led on average, followed by $d_{\mathrm{tRNA}}$, and that all $11$ per-organism dominant calls were assigned to $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$.  Domain means were explicitly descriptive under the imbalanced panel $n=\{\mathrm{archaea}:1,\mathrm{bacteria}:2,\mathrm{eukaryote}:8\}$, and the largest reported GC3 rank effect was $d_{\mathrm{usage}}$ with $\rho=0.136$.  These rows matter because they prevent the $f_3$ result from being read as an isolated codon-topology artifact: the observed finite panel separates tRNA core, $f_3$-selection residuality, a usage-frequency perpendicular direction, and a fourth residual component.

\paragraph{Mechanism boundaries.}
The boundary-set tRNA-poverty row supplies a finite mechanism contact for $R$-sense avoidance: across $40$ computed organisms out of $46$ requested organisms, with $6$ skipped organisms, $1000$ permutations, and $\alpha=0.05$, $R$ sense codons were systematically tRNA-poor under GtRNAdb tGCN plus dos Reis wobble weights.  That row supports a tRNA-supply explanation for $R$ avoidance, not a direct measurement of the $f_3$ stress coordinate on ribosome output.  The fourth-mechanism rows further restrict promotion.  The mRNA-stability test concluded $\mathrm{fourth\_axis\_not\_mrna\_stability}$, the codon-pair test concluded $\mathrm{not\_codon\_pair}$ using a shuffle null of $50$, the translation-accuracy test concluded $\mathrm{not\_accuracy}$ using $200$ permutations, and the elongation-dwell row was only partial in \textit{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}.  Thus the finite evidence supports a structured residual spectrum, but it does not collapse that spectrum into a single downstream biological mechanism.

\paragraph{Translation-readout condition.}
The intended readout is a controlled survival entry $S_{f3\text{-}\mathrm{stress},T_j}$ where $T_j$ is a bounded translation-layer row such as tAI, stAI, ribosome occupancy, or translation efficiency.  A positive entry is admissible only after amino-acid composition, organism identity, GC3, CDS length, $M$-density, matched mRNA abundance where relevant, rank sufficiency, and simpler-codon baselines have been controlled.  In formula form the admissible claim has the shape
$$
\begin{aligned}
S_{f3\text{-}\mathrm{stress},T_j}>0
\quad\text{after controls }Z
\end{aligned}
$$
for a joined finite table containing the same organism, the same coding sequence or window, the $f_3$ residual coordinate, wobble-breaking weights, and the translation readout.  A rank-degenerate all-$1.0$ surface on three organisms is not survival evidence; it is a null boundary caused by insufficient rank.

\paragraph{Local no-promotion rule.}
The chapter therefore has a narrow positive content and a strict stopping rule.  The positive content is that, in the powered finite residual-axis assay, the non-tRNA optimum aligned most strongly with $\mathrm{f3\_translation\_selection}$, while surrounding rows placed that axis in a larger tRNA, usage-frequency, and fourth-residual spectrum.  The stopping rule is that no measured translation realization follows until a same-row translation contact supplies a non-vacuous positive $S_{f3\text{-}\mathrm{stress},T_j}$ under the stated controls.  Even if that translation row later becomes positive, it still would not establish protein abundance, folded structure, physical admissibility, molecular function, localization, phenotype, mechanism, or a universal biological law without separate layer-matched contacts and statistics.  Codon residual geometry, modeled tAI or stAI weights, contact naming, and workflow readiness are not substitutes for those measurements.
