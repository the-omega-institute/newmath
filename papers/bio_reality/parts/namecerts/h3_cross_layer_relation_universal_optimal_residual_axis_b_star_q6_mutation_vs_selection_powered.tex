\subsection{The $f_3$-stress residual axis as a bounded translation-readout claim}
\label{sec:bioq6-f3-stress-residual-axis}

\paragraph{Finite carrier.}
This record concerns the finite residual coordinate surface obtained from coding sequences after amino-acid projection, organism matching, and restriction to the $B^*_{Q6}$ coordinate family. The selected coordinate is the third-position $f_3$-stress direction: it compares codons paired by the third-position involution after synonymous amino-acid content has already been quotiented out. The carrier is therefore not a claim about a protein, a phenotype, or a whole biological law. It is a row-indexed surface of residual codon coordinates, tRNA or wobble-weight annotations, and matched readout slots.

The finite $f_3$ saturation row fixes the coordinate support. In the recorded run $76d3dc408a57$, the ambient set $M = \operatorname{Med}(R) \cup \{\mathrm{ACA}\}$ contained the twenty codons
$$
\begin{aligned}
M = \{&\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{ACA},\mathrm{ACG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\\
&\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The $f_3$-saturated $R$ part removed $\mathrm{ACA}$ and $\mathrm{ACG}$, leaving eighteen codons:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\operatorname{Sat}_{f_3}(R)=\{&\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\\
&\mathrm{CUU},\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The recorded boundary list for this saturation was empty, so the finite coordinate has no additional $f_3$ boundary codons in that row.

\paragraph{Readout semantics.}
The biological assertion is intentionally narrow. A positive claim is allowed only for a controlled survival entry $S_{f_3,T_j}$ where $T_j$ is a translation-layer readout such as tAI, stAI, ribosome occupancy, or translation efficiency. The relevant asymmetry is not codon topology alone; it is the conjunction of an $f_3$-paired synonymous imbalance and a context-dependent weight break $w_c \neq w_{c^{f_3}}$. Without such a weight break, the coordinate remains a static residual direction rather than a dynamical readout candidate.

For a candidate row, the admissible test has the form
$$
\begin{aligned}
S_{f_3,T_j} > 0
\end{aligned}
$$
after controls for amino-acid composition, organism, $\mathrm{GC3}$, coding-sequence length, $M$-density, matched mRNA abundance when translation efficiency is used, rank sufficiency, and simpler codon baselines. If the controlled score is non-positive, or if the fitted matrix is rank-degenerate, the row is a null boundary rather than evidence for survival.

\subsection{Powered finite evidence around the coordinate}
\label{sec:bioq6-f3-stress-powered-evidence}

\paragraph{Dominant residual axis.}
The central powered row is experiment $2a526c9fb7f4$, named $\mathrm{b\_star\_q6\_universal\_optimal\_residual\_axis\_powered}$. It used five folds, required at least $500$ proteins per organism, computed $11$ organisms out of $16$ requested organisms, and used $41$ synonymous design columns. Its recorded conclusion was that non-tRNA residual optimality aligns most strongly with $f_3\_\mathrm{translation}\_\mathrm{selection}$, and the dominant axis was explicitly stored as $f_3\_\mathrm{translation}\_\mathrm{selection}$.

This is a finite alignment result, not a promotion to an observed ribosome process. It says that, among the residual axes tested in the powered design, the non-tRNA residual optimum was best named by the $f_3$ translation-selection candidate. The statement is still conditional on the readout row: a direct ribosome or translation-efficiency surface must be joined to the same organism or CDS rows before a measured $S_{f_3,T_j}$ can be asserted.

\paragraph{Adjacent axis structure.}
The same finite panel places the selected row inside a larger $B^*_{Q6}$ axis decomposition. The tRNA-adaptation direction was separately passed in experiment $373843e81379$ with five folds, $11$ computed organisms out of $16$, a $500$-protein minimum, and $41$ synonymous design columns. The two-axis closure test $99dc7e2d5abf$ returned the conclusion $\mathrm{third\_axis\_present}$, with the reason that $d_\perp$ retained systematically positive held-out $R^2$ and mutually same-signed directions across organisms. Thus the $f_3$ residual optimum is not replacing the tRNA axis; it is read as a non-tRNA residual component after the tRNA core is accounted for.

The third-axis identity run $377671f96e96$ identified $d_\perp$ provisionally with usage frequency within synonymous family, with mean cosine $0.128$ and one-sided sign-test $p=0.000488$. The mechanism run $d0893337f0dd$ recorded the classification $\mathrm{selection\mbox{-}not\mbox{-}GC}$, $\mathrm{not\mbox{-}wobble\mbox{-}clustered}$, and $\mathrm{CSC/positional\ identity\ needs\ external}$, because the GC-orthogonalized $d_\perp$ kept significant cross-species alignment and held-out $R^2$. These rows are important boundaries: they support residual organization after GC control, but they do not by themselves provide the measured translation-readout matrix required for $S_{f_3,T_j}$.

\paragraph{Spectrum and fourth-axis checks.}
The axis-strength spectrum run $0146012d8909$ computed $11$ organisms out of $16$, again with five folds, a $500$-protein minimum, and $41$ synonymous design columns. It reported that captured spectra were led by $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$ on average, followed by $d_{\mathrm{tRNA}}$; all $11$ per-organism dominant counts were assigned to $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$. The same record warns that domain means are descriptive only because the domain counts were imbalanced: one archaeal organism, two bacterial organisms, and eight eukaryotic organisms. The largest recorded $\mathrm{GC3}$ rank effect was $d_{\mathrm{usage}}$ with $\rho=0.136$.

The three-axis sufficiency run $2436cae9d455$ concluded $\mathrm{fourth\_mechanism\_present}$ on the same $11/16$ organism, five-fold, $500$-protein, $41$-column design. Follow-up fourth-mechanism tests bounded this extra component. The mRNA-stability run $377abd67b8b3$ concluded $\mathrm{fourth\_axis\_not\_mrna\_stability}$ because the yeast $d_{\mathrm{opt\_halflife}}$ had low alignment with abundance $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$ under the fixed gates. The elongation-dwell run $e4edcde459b7$ in \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae} returned $\mathrm{partial}$. The codon-pair run $b519b5ebd34d$ returned $\mathrm{not\_codon\_pair}$ using $50$ shuffle-null samples. The translation-accuracy run $0dc44df59ba7$ returned $\mathrm{not\_accuracy}$ using $200$ permutations. These classifications restrict interpretation of the residual spectrum; they do not replace the missing same-row translation surface.

\subsection{Local reality boundary}
\label{sec:bioq6-f3-stress-boundary}

\paragraph{Required contacts.}
A valid positive readout for this chapter requires organism-matched codon usage, tRNA gene-copy or wobble-weight rows, a bounded tAI or stAI surface or a measured ribosome/translation-efficiency row, and matched mRNA controls when TE is computed. For a ribosome-profiling row, the data must join coding-sequence identifiers to RPF and mRNA measurements so that $\mathrm{TE}=\mathrm{RPF}/\mathrm{mRNA}$ is a row-level readout rather than a contact name. For a modeled tAI or stAI row, the weight is only an adaptation-weight readout unless calibrated against measured occupancy or efficiency.

The tRNA-poverty mechanism row $d7728cc8768e$ supplies a separate finite support point for the $R$ sense-codon background: it computed $40$ organisms out of $46$ requested, skipped $6$, used $1000$ permutations at $\alpha=0.05$, and concluded that $R$ sense codons are systematically tRNA-poor under GtRNAdb tGCN plus dos Reis wobble weights. This supports a tRNA-supply interpretation for $R$ avoidance, but it is not identical to a positive $f_3$ translation-readout survival entry.

\paragraph{What is not inferred.}
No realized translation process, protein abundance, folding state, physical admissibility, molecular function, localization, stability, phenotype, mechanism, or universal biological rule follows from this chapter unless a separate layer-matched contact supplies that row and the declared controlled statistic is positive. In particular, a three-organism all-$1.0$ controlled matrix is a rank-saturated null surface, not survival. Likewise, codon topology and modeled tAI coupling do not determine directionality unless the context weights actually break $f_3$ symmetry and the resulting row survives the stated controls.

The present chapter is therefore ready as a bounded finite record: the powered residual-axis experiment identifies $f_3\_\mathrm{translation}\_\mathrm{selection}$ as the dominant non-tRNA residual optimum on an $11$-organism computed panel with $41$ synonymous design columns, while the observational interpretation remains restricted to a future nondegenerate $S_{f_3,T_j}$ readout row.
