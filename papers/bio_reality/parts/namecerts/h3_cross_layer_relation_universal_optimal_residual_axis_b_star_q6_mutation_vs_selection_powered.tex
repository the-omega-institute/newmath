\subsection{The $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual optimality axis and its $f_3$ translation-selection boundary}\label{subsec:bioreality-bstar-q6-f3-translation-selection}\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite carrier.}  This chapter records a finite CDS-by-organism residual-coordinate surface restricted to the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ coordinate family and, more narrowly, to the third-position $f_3$ stress coordinate after amino-acid projection.  The finite codon-side saturation row is explicit.  The marked set
$$\begin{aligned}
M ={}& \{\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{ACA},\mathrm{ACG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\\
&\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\}
\end{aligned}$$
contains $20$ codons in the recorded $M_{\mathrm{ed}}\cup R\cup\mathrm{ACA}$ surface.  The $f_3$-saturated $R$ row removes exactly $\mathrm{ACA}$ and $\mathrm{ACG}$:
$$\begin{aligned}
\operatorname{Sat}_{f_3}(R) ={}& \{\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\\
&\mathrm{CUU},\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\},
\end{aligned}$$
so the stored $f_3$ boundary row is empty.  The biological question is therefore not whether the codon geometry alone has an empirical interpretation.  It asks whether the finite residual coordinate continues to separate matched translation-readout rows after amino-acid composition, organism identity, GC3, coding length, $M$-density, and mRNA controls are included.

\paragraph{Powered residual-axis row.}  The main powered run for this record is experiment $\mathrm{b\_star\_q6\_universal\_optimal\_residual\_axis\_powered}$, run $\mathrm{2a526c9fb7f4}$.  It passed with $5$ folds, a minimum of $500$ proteins per organism, $11$ organisms computed from $16$ requested organisms, and $41$ synonymous-design columns.  Its finite conclusion is that the non-tRNA residual optimality direction aligns most strongly with $f_3$ translation selection; the recorded dominant axis is $\mathrm{f3\_translation\_selection}$.  This is a residual-axis identification on the powered panel.  It is not, by itself, an observed ribosome-footprint survival row and it is not a protein-output row.

\paragraph{Neighboring powered checks.}  The same finite panel gives several constraints on how this axis may be read.  The universal core tRNA-adaptation run passed with the same $5$ folds, $11/16$ computed organisms, a $500$ protein minimum, and $41$ synonymous-design columns.  The two-axis closure run also passed on $11/16$ organisms and reported $\mathrm{third\_axis\_present}$: $d_{\perp}$ has systematically positive held-out $R^2$ and same-signed directions across organisms.  The third-axis identity run identified $d_{\perp}$ with within-family usage frequency, with mean cosine $0.128$ and one-sided sign-test $p=0.000488$.  Its mechanism-side companion classified the third axis as selection rather than GC, not wobble-clustered, with CSC or positional identity still needing a further contact; the reason stored for that run is that GC-orthogonalized $d_{\perp}$ keeps significant cross-species alignment and held-out $R^2$.

\paragraph{Axis spectrum.}  The axis-strength spectrum run passed with $5$ folds, $11$ computed organisms, $16$ requested organisms, a $500$ protein minimum, and $41$ synonymous-design columns.  Its captured spectra are led by $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$ on average, followed by $d_{\mathrm{tRNA}}$, and the per-organism dominant-count row is $\{d_{\mathrm{resid4}}:11\}$.  Domain summaries in that run are descriptive only because the domain counts are imbalanced: $1$ archaeal, $2$ bacterial, and $8$ eukaryotic organisms.  The largest recorded GC3 rank effect is $d_{\mathrm{usage}}$ with $\rho=0.136$.  These numbers locate the $f_3$ residual axis inside a broader finite spectrum rather than making it a universal biological law.

\paragraph{Fourth-axis exclusion rows.}  The sufficiency run reports $\mathrm{fourth\_mechanism\_present}$ on $11/16$ organisms with $5$ folds, $500$ proteins per organism, and $41$ synonymous-design columns.  The candidate fourth-axis tests narrow what that statement means.  The mRNA-stability run classifies the dominant fourth-axis identity as not mRNA stability, because yeast $d_{\mathrm{opt\_halflife}}$ has low alignment with abundance $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$ under the fixed gates.  The codon-pair run returns $\mathrm{not\_codon\_pair}$ with $50$ shuffle-null draws.  The translation-accuracy run returns $\mathrm{not\_accuracy}$ using $200$ permutations on $11/16$ organisms.  The elongation-dwell run is marked $\mathrm{partial}$ for \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}.  Thus the residual optimality result is not promoted to a closed account of translation dynamics; it remains a constrained finite axis result.

\paragraph{Boundary tRNA row.}  A separate powered boundary-set run on $R$ sense codons used $40$ computed organisms from $46$ requested organisms, skipped $6$, and used $1000$ permutations at $\alpha=0.05$.  It records that $R$ sense codons are systematically tRNA-poor under GtRNAdb tGCN plus dos Reis wobble weights, supporting a tRNA-supply explanation for $R$ avoidance in that finite assay.  This row supplies biological pressure on the codon-side boundary, but it still does not turn the $f_3$ residual coordinate into a measured translation-efficiency row unless a matched translation readout is joined to the same organisms or coding sequences.

\paragraph{Local interpretation.}  The admissible readback is narrow.  The chapter may say that, in a powered $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ panel, the non-tRNA residual optimality axis is classified as $f_3$ translation selection under the recorded synonymous design, and that the surrounding checks constrain nearby alternatives.  It may also say that the $f_3$ coordinate becomes a candidate translation-layer distinction only where context tRNA or wobble weights break third-position symmetry.  The finite support condition for a stronger statement would be a non-vacuous positive entry $S_{f3\text{-}stress,T_j}>0$ for a tAI, stAI, ribosome-occupancy, or translation-efficiency row after amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, length, $M$-density, mRNA, rank, and simpler-baseline controls.

\paragraph{Local refusal boundary.}  No observed translation realization follows from codon topology, modeled tAI or stAI weights, or the powered residual-axis classification alone.  No claim about protein abundance, folded structure, physical admissibility, molecular function, phenotype, mechanism, or universality is licensed here unless a separate layer-matched empirical contact supplies that readout and the corresponding controlled statistic is positive.  A three-organism all-$1.0$ controlled surface is treated as rank saturation rather than survival evidence.  If the controlled $S_{f3\text{-}stress,T_j}$ row remains non-positive or rank-degenerate across a powered panel, the translation-readout form of the conjecture is falsified for that panel, while the codon-coordinate and residual-axis rows remain finite mathematical and statistical records.
