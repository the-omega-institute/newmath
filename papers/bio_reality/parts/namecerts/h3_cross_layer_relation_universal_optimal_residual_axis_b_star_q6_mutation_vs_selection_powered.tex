\subsection{The $f_3$-stress residual axis as a bounded translation-readout claim}
\label{subsec:bior reality-q6-f3-stress-translation-readout}

\paragraph{Finite coordinate surface.}
The record considered here is the finite coding-sequence residual coordinate surface obtained after amino-acid projection, restricted to the $B^*_{Q6}$ $f_3$-stress coordinate and to translation-layer readouts that remain explicitly separated from protein abundance, structure, localization, stability, function, phenotype, mechanism, and universality. The coordinate is not treated as a biological law. It is a row-indexed residual direction whose only allowed biological readback in this chapter is a controlled survival entry against a translation readout such as tAI, stAI, ribosome occupancy, or translation efficiency.

The finite $f_3$ saturation calculation supplies the discrete codon boundary for the coordinate. In run $76d3dc408a57$, the marked union $M = \operatorname{Med}_R \cup \{\mathrm{ACA}\}$ contains exactly twenty codons,
$$
\begin{aligned}
M = \{&\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{ACA},\mathrm{ACG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\\
&\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The $f_3$-saturated $R$ surface is the eighteen-codon set obtained by excluding $\mathrm{ACA}$ and $\mathrm{ACG}$,
$$
\begin{aligned}
\operatorname{Sat}_{f_3}(R) = \{&\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\\
&\mathrm{CUU},\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The recorded $f_3$ boundary residue is empty. Thus the chapter is about a finite residual direction inside a saturated synonymy-aware codon surface, not about an unconstrained whole-genome association.

\paragraph{Observed finite-row support.}
The powered residual-axis run $2a526c9fb7f4$ reports status passed for $11$ computed organisms out of $16$ requested organisms, using $5$ folds, a minimum of $500$ proteins per organism, and $41$ synonymous-design columns. Its finite conclusion is that non-tRNA residual optimality aligns most strongly with the $f_3$ translation-selection axis; the reported dominant axis is $\mathrm{f3\_translation\_selection}$. This is the main positive finite-row entry for the present record.

The surrounding finite-row panel keeps the claim from being read as a single-axis story. The universal core tRNA-adaptation run also passed on $11$ computed organisms out of $16$ requested organisms with $5$ folds, at least $500$ proteins per organism, and $41$ synonymous-design columns. The boundary-set tRNA-poverty run passed on $40$ computed organisms out of $46$ requested organisms, skipping $6$ organisms, with $1000$ permutations at $\alpha = 0.05$; its mechanism-level finite statement is limited to systematic tRNA poverty of $R$ sense codons under GtRNAdb tGCN and dos Reis wobble weights. The two-axis closure run passed on $11$ of $16$ requested organisms and reported that a third axis remains present, because the orthogonal direction has systematically positive held-out $R^2$ and mutually same-signed directions across organisms.

The third-axis identity run passed with the same $11$-organism, $5$-fold, $500$-protein, $41$-column design and identified $d_\perp$ provisionally with usage frequency within family, with mean cosine $0.128$ and one-sided sign-test $p = 0.000488$. The third-axis mechanism run passed and reported the finite classification ``selection-not-GC; not-wobble-clustered; CSC/positional identity needs external'', with the reason that GC-orthogonalized $d_\perp$ keeps significant cross-species alignment and held-out $R^2$. The sufficiency run then passed with conclusion ``fourth mechanism present'', so the three-axis span is not asserted to exhaust the finite residual biology.

\paragraph{Spectrum placement.}
The axis-strength spectrum run $0146012d8909$ computed $11$ organisms out of $16$ requested organisms, again with $5$ folds, a minimum of $500$ proteins per organism, and $41$ synonymous-design columns. Its descriptive spectrum is led by $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$ on average, followed by $d_{\mathrm{tRNA}}$. The per-organism dominant-count table assigns all $11$ computed organisms to $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$. Domain means are only descriptive because the domain counts are imbalanced: $1$ archaeal organism, $2$ bacterial organisms, and $8$ eukaryotic organisms. The largest recorded GC3 rank effect is $d_{\mathrm{usage}}$ with $\rho = 0.136$. These numbers place the $f_3$ residual result inside a finite spectrum rather than allowing it to become a universal direction claim.

\paragraph{Fourth-axis exclusions and partial contacts.}
The finite rows also delimit what the residual axis is not. The mRNA-stability run passed with conclusion ``fourth\_axis\_not\_mrna\_stability'' because the yeast $d_{\mathrm{opt\_halflife}}$ alignment with abundance $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$ is low under the fixed gates. The codon-pair run passed with conclusion ``not\_codon\_pair'' using $50$ shuffle-null samples. The translation-accuracy run passed on $11$ computed organisms out of $16$ requested organisms with $200$ permutations and conclusion ``not\_accuracy''. The elongation-dwell run passed for \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae} with conclusion ``partial''. These entries make the present chapter narrower: it supports a bounded $f_3$ translation-selection residual axis, not a settled downstream identity for every remaining axis.

\paragraph{Translation-readout gate.}
The permitted survival statistic is a controlled entry $S_{f3\text{-}stress,T_j}$, where $T_j$ is a translation-layer readout. The positive form required for promotion is
$$
\begin{aligned}
S_{f3\text{-}stress,T_j} > 0
\end{aligned}
$$
after controls for amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, CDS length, $M$-density baseline, matched mRNA abundance where applicable, rank sufficiency, and simpler codon baselines. The available finite rows support the modeled and rank-powered residual-axis placement, but the same-row measured ribosome or translation-efficiency matrix is not yet present in the supplied data. Therefore the tAI or stAI surface may be used only as a bounded codon-adaptation readout unless it is paired with measured ribosome-profiling or translation-efficiency rows.

\paragraph{Falsification condition.}
The chapter-level conjecture fails at the translation boundary if, across a rank-powered organism panel, every controlled translation-readout entry remains non-positive or rank-degenerate:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\forall T_j,\quad S_{f3\text{-}stress,T_j} \leq 0.
\end{aligned}
$$
The three-organism all-$1.0$ controlled surface is not counted as survival, because rank saturation is a null boundary rather than biological support. Likewise, codon topology and modeled tAI coupling do not by themselves establish directionality. The coordinate becomes a dynamical translation candidate only where context tRNA or wobble weights break the $f_3$ symmetry and a controlled translation row survives.

\paragraph{Local no-promotion rule.}
No translation realization beyond the bounded statistic follows from this coordinate alone. No folded structure, physical admissibility, molecular function, localization, stability, phenotype, mechanism, adaptation claim, causation claim, or universal biological law follows from the $B^*_{Q6}$ residual geometry, the saturation lists, modeled tAI or stAI weights, codon usage tables, or review eligibility. Each higher layer requires its own matched contact and its own finite statistic. In particular, a protein-abundance, structure, function, localization, stability, physical-assay, perturbation, or phenotype statement is outside the present chapter unless a separate layer-matched readout is attached after the $Q_i \to T_j$ translation step has survived under the declared controls.
