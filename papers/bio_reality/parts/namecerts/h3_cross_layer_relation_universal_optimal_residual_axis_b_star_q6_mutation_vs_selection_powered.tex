\subsection{The $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ $f_3$-stress residual axis as a bounded translation-readout conjecture}
\label{sec:bioreality-q6-f3-stress-translation-readout}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite coordinate surface.}
This record isolates one finite residual coordinate in coding-sequence space: the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ $f_3$-stress direction after synonymous codons have first been projected through amino-acid identity.  The point of the construction is not to read a biological output directly from codon geometry.  It is to ask whether a residual codon-coordinate distinction, already visible after amino-acid quotienting, survives when it is paired with a bounded translation-layer row such as tAI, stAI, ribosome occupancy, or translation efficiency.  The carrier is therefore a finite CDS-by-organism residual coordinate surface restricted to the $f_3$-stress row and to matched translation readouts.  The row is admissible only when the organism or local context supplies an asymmetric wobble or tRNA weighting, written here as $w_c \neq w_{c^{f_3}}$, so that the third-position involution is not dynamically invisible.

\paragraph{Saturation boundary.}
The finite $f_3$ saturation computation fixes the relevant codon boundary.  In the recorded run $76d3dc408a57$, the union set $M = \mathrm{Med}_R \cup \mathrm{ACA}$ is
$$
\begin{aligned}
M = \{&\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{ACA},\mathrm{ACG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\\
&\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
After $f_3$ saturation of $R$, the two ACA-family entries are removed, giving
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{Sat}_{f_3}(R) = \{&\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\\
&\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The recorded $f_3$ boundary residue is empty, so the chapter treats $f_3$-stress as a residual coordinate inside a closed finite codon surface rather than as an unaccounted codon class.  This matters for the biological question: if a translation-readout effect is observed later, it must be attributed to a controlled residual direction and not to a missing codon bucket.

\paragraph{Powered residual-axis evidence.}
The powered residual-axis run $2a526c9fb7f4$ used $5$ folds, at least $500$ proteins per organism, $41$ synonymous design columns, and computed $11$ organisms out of $16$ requested organisms.  Its finite conclusion was that non-tRNA residual optimality aligns most strongly with the axis named $f3\_translation\_selection$.  This is the central positive row used here: it does not assert measured ribosome occupancy, protein abundance, or phenotype, but it does identify the leading non-tRNA residual direction in the fitted codon-coordinate panel.  The same $11$-organism, $5$-fold, $41$-column design also reports that the two-axis tRNA-plus-$f_3$ span is insufficient: the two-axis closure run concluded that a third axis is present, with systematically positive held-out $R^2$ and mutually same-signed $d_{\perp}$ directions across organisms.  The third-axis identity run then identified $d_{\perp}$ provisionally with within-family usage frequency, with mean cosine $0.128$ and one-sided sign-test $p=0.000488$.

\paragraph{Relation to the surrounding spectrum.}
The broader axis-strength spectrum, computed on $11$ organisms out of $16$ requested with the same $5$-fold and $41$-column design, reports that captured spectra are led by $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$ on average, followed by $d_{\mathrm{tRNA}}$, and that the per-organism dominant count is $\{d_{\mathrm{resid4}}:11\}$.  The largest GC3 rank effect in that run is small, $\rho=0.136$ for $d_{\mathrm{usage}}$, so the residual-axis statement is not being presented as a mere GC3 ranking artifact.  The mechanism-side tRNA-poverty run supplies a separate finite constraint: across $40$ computed organisms out of $46$ requested, with $1000$ permutations and $\alpha=0.05$, R sense codons are systematically tRNA-poor under GtRNAdb tGCN plus dos Reis wobble weights.  This supports a tRNA-supply interpretation for R avoidance, but it still does not by itself establish the $f_3$-stress translation-readout survival row.  The $f_3$ coordinate becomes a biological translation candidate only after a matched readout row survives the declared controls.

\paragraph{Finite readout statistic.}
For a residual coordinate $q_{f_3}$ and a translation readout $T_j$, the admissible statistic is a controlled survival entry $S_{f_3,T_j}$.  The controls are amino-acid composition, organism identity, GC3, CDS length, M-density, matched mRNA abundance when TE is used, rank sufficiency, and simpler codon baselines.  The intended finite test can be summarized as
$$
\begin{aligned}
S_{f_3,T_j} > 0
\quad &\Longleftrightarrow \quad
q_{f_3}\text{ improves the held-out translation-readout description after all controls},\\
S_{f_3,T_j} \leq 0
\quad &\Longleftrightarrow \quad
q_{f_3}\text{ fails to add controlled translation-readout information}.
\end{aligned}
$$
A rank-degenerate all-$1.0$ matrix on only three organisms is treated as a null boundary, not as survival.  Likewise, modeled tAI or stAI weights are bounded codon-adaptation readbacks; they are not observed ribosome-footprint measurements unless an organism-matched ribosome or TE panel is attached and analyzed.

\paragraph{Negative higher-layer boundary.}
The finite evidence above supports a narrow claim: within the powered codon-coordinate panel, the leading non-tRNA residual optimality direction is the $f_3$-translation-selection axis, and the surrounding spectrum requires more than the tRNA plus $f_3$ span.  It does not follow that $f_3$-stress has been shown to alter translation efficiency, ribosome occupancy, protein abundance, folded structure, physical admissibility, localization, stability, molecular function, phenotype, mechanism, or universality.  Each of those statements requires its own layer-matched contact and its own controlled statistic.  In particular, a positive codon-coordinate association, a tRNA or wobble-weight asymmetry, a contact name, or a review-ready routing status is not a substitute for the finite joined rows containing codon counts, wobble weights, measured or explicitly bounded translation readouts, matched mRNA controls, and the declared covariates.

\paragraph{Current verdict.}
The chapter therefore records a ready but bounded BioReality claim.  The codon-coordinate side is finite and non-vacuous: $M$ has $20$ entries, $\mathrm{Sat}_{f_3}(R)$ has $18$ entries, the $f_3$ boundary residue is empty, and the powered residual-axis run identifies $f3\_translation\_selection$ as the dominant non-tRNA residual optimality axis on $11$ computed organisms with $5$ folds and $41$ synonymous design columns.  The biological readout side remains conditional: the conjecture is supported only at the level of a controlled translation-readout target, and it is falsified for the intended layer if $S_{f_3,T_j}$ remains non-positive or rank-degenerate across a powered matched panel.
