\subsection{Wobble-broken $f_3$ stress as the residual translation axis}
\label{sec:b-star-q6-f3-stress-wobble-broken-translation-readout}

\paragraph{Finite coordinate surface.}
This record isolates the $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinate that remains after synonymous codons are first compared only within their amino-acid fibers.  The coordinate of interest is the third-position $f_3$ stress: two synonymous codons are paired by a third-base involution, projected through the amino-acid quotient, and then tested only through rows that still have a translation-layer readout.  In the finite saturation check, the marked set
$$
\begin{aligned}
M=\{&\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{ACA},\mathrm{ACG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\\
&\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\}
\end{aligned}
$$
has twenty codons.  The $f_3$-saturated sublist removes exactly $\mathrm{ACA}$ and $\mathrm{ACG}$:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\operatorname{Sat}_{f_3}(R)=\{&\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\\
&\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The recorded $f_3$ boundary is empty.  Thus the finite combinatorial part does not by itself supply a biological effect; it supplies a closed residual coordinate on which a translation readout may later be tested.

\paragraph{Rank-powered residual result.}
The powered cross-organism computation identifies the non-tRNA residual optimum with the axis named $f3\_translation\_selection$.  The run used five folds, required at least $500$ proteins per organism, computed $11$ organisms out of $16$ requested organisms, and used $41$ synonymous design columns.  Its status is passed, with deterministic seed $\mathrm{sha256{:}b\_star\_q6\_universal\_optimal\_residual\_axis\_powered{:}deterministic}$.  In the notation of this section, the observed finite-row statement is not that $f_3$ stress changes any downstream phenotype; it is the narrower statement that, after the tRNA-adaptation component is removed, the strongest residual optimality direction in the computed panel is the $f_3$ translation-selection coordinate.

\paragraph{Relation to the adjacent axes.}
The surrounding finite rows make the interpretation more restrictive rather than broader.  The core optimal direction computation also passed on the same scale of $5$ folds, $11$ computed organisms out of $16$ requested, at least $500$ proteins per organism, and $41$ synonymous design columns.  The two-axis closure test concluded $third\_axis\_present$, with the reason that the orthogonal residual direction has systematically positive held-out $R^2$ and mutually same-signed directions across organisms.  The identity run then aligned this residual direction to usage frequency within family, reporting mean cosine $0.128$ and one-sided sign-test $p=0.000488$.  The mechanism run classified the third-axis evidence as selection-not-GC and not-wobble-clustered, while leaving the CSC or positional identity as requiring another source of evidence.  These rows keep $f_3$ stress in a residual translation-selection role: they do not convert it into a general codon-law, a protein-law, or a structural claim.

\paragraph{Wobble-breaking condition.}
The coordinate is biologically testable only where the context weights distinguish a codon $c$ from its third-position partner $c^{f_3}$.  Formally, the admissible readout rows are those for which a context tRNA or wobble weight satisfies $w_c\neq w_{c^{f_3}}$ and the response row is a bounded translation readout such as tAI, stAI, ribosome occupancy, or translation efficiency.  The boundary-set tRNA-poverty run supports the presence of a tRNA-supply mechanism for the $R$ sense codons: it used $40$ computed organisms out of $46$ requested, skipped $6$ organisms, used $1000$ permutations at $\alpha=0.05$, and passed with the mechanism call that $R$ sense codons are systematically tRNA-poor under GtRNAdb tGCN plus dos Reis wobble weights.  This supplies the asymmetry route needed for a meaningful $f_3$ test, but it still does not replace a same-row translation measurement.

\paragraph{Readout equation.}
For a coding sequence row $x$, let $q_{f_3}(x)$ denote the amino-acid-quotiented $f_3$ stress residual, let $T_j(x)$ be a translation readout, and let $Z(x)$ collect amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, coding-sequence length, $M$-density, matched mRNA control where available, rank controls, and simpler synonymous baselines.  The only positive claim allowed by this packet has the form
$$
\begin{aligned}
S_{f_3,T_j} &= \Delta \operatorname{Fit}\bigl(T_j; q_{f_3}\mid Z\bigr),\\
S_{f_3,T_j} &> 0,
\end{aligned}
$$
computed on matched finite rows.  A rank-degenerate all-$1.0$ surface on three organisms is a refusal case, not survival.  A modelled tAI or stAI surface is a bounded adaptation readout, not an observed ribosome-occupancy matrix.  A ribosome-profiling or translation-efficiency contact becomes evidence only after the same coding sequences are joined to codon counts, wobble or tRNA weights, matched mRNA abundance, and the declared controls.

\paragraph{Observed spectrum and remaining axes.}
The per-organism axis spectrum passed on $11$ computed organisms out of $16$ requested, again with five folds, a $500$-protein minimum, and $41$ synonymous design columns.  Its descriptive summary reports $d\_resid4$ as the mean-leading captured spectrum, followed by $d\_tRNA$, with per-organism dominant counts $\{d\_resid4:11\}$, imbalanced domain counts $\{archaea:1,bacteria:2,eukaryote:8\}$, and largest GC3 rank effect $d\_usage$ with $\rho=0.136$.  The three-axis sufficiency test concluded $fourth\_mechanism\_present$.  The fourth-axis checks narrow that remaining component: the mRNA-stability assay concluded $fourth\_axis\_not\_mrna\_stability$, elongation dwell was partial in $\mathrm{saccharomyces\_cerevisiae}$, codon-pair identity concluded $not\_codon\_pair$, and translation accuracy concluded $not\_accuracy$ after $200$ permutations on $11$ computed organisms out of $16$ requested.  These rows explain why the $f_3$ result should be read as a residual-axis result inside a larger finite spectrum, not as an exhaustive mechanism.

\paragraph{Local no-promotion rule.}
The present record licenses only a controlled translation-readout question.  It does not claim that $f_3$ stress directly changes protein abundance, folded structure, physical admissibility, localization, stability, molecular function, organismal phenotype, mechanism, or universality.  Those interpretations require separate layer-matched observations and their own finite statistics.  In particular, codon geometry, tRNA-copy asymmetry, tAI or stAI weights, and review-ready routing are not substitutes for measured ribosome or translation-efficiency rows.  The chapter is falsified at the translation layer if every controlled $S_{f_3,T_j}$ entry is non-positive, vacuous, or rank-degenerate across the powered panel; it remains bounded if the only surviving evidence is a modelled adaptation-weight surface without a matched measured translation row.
