\subsection{The $B^*_{Q6}$ $f_3$ residual axis under broken wobble symmetry}
\label{sec:bioreality-q6-f3-residual-axis}

\paragraph{Object.}
This record concerns the finite coding-sequence residual surface obtained after amino-acid projection, restricted to the $B^*_{Q6}$ coordinate that changes under the third-position involution $f_3$.  The biological rows are organism-indexed coding sequences with codon-usage summaries, tRNA or wobble-weight annotations, and bounded translation-layer readouts.  The relevant distinction is not raw codon identity.  It is the residual imbalance that remains after each synonymous family has been quotiented by amino acid, then compared against context-dependent weights $w_c$ and $w_{c^{f_3}}$.  The coordinate can only become a biological readout candidate where those weights are not symmetric, namely where $w_c \neq w_{c^{f_3}}$.

\paragraph{Finite support.}
The saturated $f_3$ computation gives an empty boundary set: $\mathrm{boundary}_{f_3}=\varnothing$.  In the same run, the union $M=\mathrm{Med}_R\cup\mathrm{ACA}$ contains $20$ codons,
$$
\begin{aligned}
M=\{&\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{ACA},\mathrm{ACG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\\
&\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The $f_3$-saturated $R$ list removes exactly $\mathrm{ACA}$ and $\mathrm{ACG}$ from that displayed carrier and has $18$ codons:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{Sat}_{f_3}(R)=\{&\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\\
&\mathrm{CUU},\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the finite codon geometry supplies a closed residual coordinate system, but it does not by itself supply a measured translation outcome.

\paragraph{Rank-powered residual result.}
The powered residual-axis assay was run with $5$ folds, a minimum of $500$ proteins per organism, $41$ synonymous design columns, and $11$ computed organisms out of $16$ requested.  Its passed conclusion was that the non-tRNA residual optimality direction aligns most strongly with $f_3\_\mathrm{translation}\_\mathrm{selection}$.  In formula language, the tested row is the controlled survival entry
$$
\begin{aligned}
S_{f_3\text{-stress},T_j}>0,
\end{aligned}
$$
where $T_j$ is a bounded translation readout such as tAI, stAI, ribosome occupancy, or translation efficiency.  The displayed inequality is not assumed from codon geometry; it is the required finite-row survival condition after amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, CDS length, $M$-density, matched mRNA, and simpler codon-baseline controls.

\paragraph{Neighboring finite rows.}
The surrounding rank-powered assays constrain how the residual axis may be read.  The universal core tRNA-adaptation assay also passed on $11$ computed organisms out of $16$, with $5$ folds, $500$ minimum proteins per organism, and $41$ synonymous design columns.  The two-axis closure assay passed under the same row counts but concluded that a third axis is present, because the perpendicular residual direction had systematically positive held-out $R^2$ and mutually same-signed directions across organisms.  The third-axis identity assay identified that perpendicular direction provisionally with usage frequency within synonymous family, with mean cosine $0.128$ and one-sided sign-test $p=0.000488$.  After GC orthogonalization, the third-axis mechanism assay retained significant cross-species alignment and held-out $R^2$, summarized as selection rather than GC, not wobble-clustered, and still needing outside CSC or positional identification.  The three-axis sufficiency assay then passed with the conclusion that a fourth mechanism is present.

\paragraph{Fourth-axis exclusions.}
The later assays prevent over-reading the $f_3$ residual row.  Across $11$ computed organisms, the axis-strength spectrum was led by $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$ on average and then by $d_{\mathrm{tRNA}}$; all $11$ per-organism dominant counts were assigned to $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$.  Domain means were descriptive only, with imbalanced counts $n_{\mathrm{archaea}}=1$, $n_{\mathrm{bacteria}}=2$, and $n_{\mathrm{eukaryote}}=8$, and the largest GC3 rank effect was $\rho=0.136$ for $d_{\mathrm{usage}}$.  The mRNA-stability assay passed with the conclusion that the fourth axis is not dominated by mRNA stability in the fixed yeast half-life alignment.  The codon-pair assay concluded \emph{not codon pair} using $50$ shuffle-null draws.  The translation-accuracy assay concluded \emph{not accuracy} with $200$ permutations over $11$ computed organisms.  The elongation-dwell assay in \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae} gave only a partial conclusion.  Separately, the $R$-sense tRNA-poverty assay passed over $40$ computed organisms out of $46$ requested, with $6$ skipped organisms and $1000$ permutations at $\alpha=0.05$, supporting a tRNA-supply explanation for $R$ avoidance.

\paragraph{Interpretation.}
The admissible claim is therefore narrow.  After amino-acid quotienting, the $B^*_{Q6}$ $f_3$ residual coordinate is a candidate translation-readout distinction precisely where wobble or tRNA weights break $f_3$ symmetry.  The finite powered evidence says that, in the available rank-powered panel, the non-tRNA residual optimality direction is best described as $f_3$-translation selection rather than as mutation pressure alone.  The same evidence also says that this axis is not a license to infer protein abundance, folded structure, physical admissibility, molecular function, organismal phenotype, a detailed causal mechanism, or a universal biological law.

\paragraph{Local falsifier.}
The record is falsified at the translation interface if every controlled row remains non-positive or rank-degenerate:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\forall j,\quad S_{f_3\text{-stress},T_j}\leq 0,
\end{aligned}
$$
under the declared controls and baseline ablations.  A three-organism all-$1.0$ matrix is not counted as survival, because rank saturation is a null boundary.  Conversely, one positive controlled entry would only establish a bounded translation-layer survival row for that organism and readout.  Any statement about translation dynamics beyond the declared readout, and any statement about folding, physical admissibility, function, mechanism, phenotype, or universality, requires a separate layer-matched contact and its own finite statistic.
