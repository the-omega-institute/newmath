\subsection{The f\_3-stress residual axis as a bounded translation-readout target}
\label{sec:bioreality-q6-f3-stress-residual-axis}

\paragraph{Finite carrier.}
This record concerns a finite coding-sequence surface indexed by organism and by synonymous codon residual coordinates after amino-acid projection.  The coordinate under test is the $B^*_{Q6}$ residual direction whose dominant finite-row interpretation is $f_3$-translation selection: among the non-tRNA residual candidates in the powered panel, the selected experiment reports that the residual optimum aligns most strongly with the axis named $f3\_translation\_selection$.  The run is not a symbolic extrapolation from codon topology alone.  It is a five-fold finite computation over $11$ computed organisms out of $16$ requested organisms, with at least $500$ proteins per organism and $41$ synonymous design columns.  Its deterministic seed is recorded as $\mathrm{sha256}:b\_star\_q6\_universal\_optimal\_residual\_axis\_powered:deterministic$, and the finite status is passed.

\paragraph{Saturation boundary.}
The local $f_3$ carrier is constrained by the observed saturation lists.  The finite set $M$ used here is the union of the median $R$ component with $\{ACA\}$ and consists of the following $20$ codons:
$$
\begin{aligned}
M=\{&AAA,AAG,ACA,ACG,AGA,AGG,AUA,AUG,CUA,CUC,\\
& CUG,CUU,UAA,UAG,UCA,UCG,UGA,UGG,UUA,UUG\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
After applying the $f_3$ saturation over $R$, the resulting set removes $ACA$ and $ACG$ and has $18$ codons:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\operatorname{Sat}_{f_3}(R)=\{&AAA,AAG,AGA,AGG,AUA,AUG,CUA,CUC,CUG,CUU,\\
&UAA,UAG,UCA,UCG,UGA,UGG,UUA,UUG\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The recorded $f_3$ boundary is empty, written here as $\partial_{f_3}=\varnothing$.  This matters because the tested residual direction is not an arbitrary codon-count contrast: it is a stress coordinate that remains after the amino-acid quotient and after the finite saturation calculation has removed the explicit $ACA,ACG$ edge contribution.

\paragraph{Readout axis.}
The powered residual-axis run says that the non-tRNA residual optimum is best described by $f_3$-translation selection.  In the local notation, the bounded target is a controlled survival entry
$$
\begin{aligned}
S_{f_3,T_j}>0,
\end{aligned}
$$
where $T_j$ is a translation-layer readout such as tAI, stAI, ribosome occupancy, or translation efficiency, and the control family includes amino-acid composition, organism, $GC3$, coding-sequence length, $M$-density, and matched mRNA abundance when a measured translation-efficiency row is used.  The phrase ``survival'' is therefore local and statistical: it means that the residual coordinate continues to improve the declared finite readout after the stated controls and baseline ablations.  It does not mean that the coordinate has become a protein-level, structural, functional, phenotypic, or universal biological law.

\paragraph{Wobble-breaking trigger.}
The conjectural trigger is third-position asymmetry.  The $f_3$ coordinate becomes biologically testable only in an organism or context where the tRNA or wobble weight for a codon differs from the corresponding $f_3$-paired codon weight.  In local notation this is the condition
$$
\begin{aligned}
w_c \ne w_{c^{f_3}}.
\end{aligned}
$$
Without such a broken symmetry, the $f_3$ residual may remain a valid coordinate of the finite coding surface, but it has no permitted route to a translation-readout distinction.  Conversely, the presence of modeled tAI or stAI asymmetry is still only a bounded readout route; it is not by itself an observed ribosome-occupancy matrix.

\paragraph{Powered neighboring facts.}
The same finite-row family supplies several constraints that keep this axis from being overread.  A tRNA-supply mechanism for the $R$ sense codons is supported in a separate powered run: $40$ organisms were computed out of $46$ requested organisms, $6$ were skipped, the test used $1000$ permutations at $\alpha=0.05$, and the mechanism call says that $R$ sense codons are systematically tRNA-poor under GtRNAdb tGCN with dos Reis wobble weights.  The universal-core tRNA-adaptation run also passed with $11$ computed organisms out of $16$, five folds, at least $500$ proteins per organism, and $41$ synonymous design columns.  These facts identify a nearby tRNA axis, but the present chapter is about the non-tRNA residual optimum, whose dominant finite-row label is $f3\_translation\_selection$.

\paragraph{Residual spectrum.}
The two-axis closure check did not close the powered surface: with $11$ computed organisms out of $16$, five folds, at least $500$ proteins per organism, and $41$ synonymous design columns, the recorded conclusion is that a third axis is present.  The stated reason is that $d_{\perp}$ has systematically positive held-out $R^2$ and mutually same-signed directions across organisms.  A separate identity run provisionally aligns $d_{\perp}$ with usage frequency within family, with mean cosine $0.128$ and one-sided sign-test $p=0.000488$.  A mechanism run then records that the $d_{\perp}$ signal remains after GC orthogonalization, with conclusion ``selection-not-GC; not-wobble-clustered; CSC/positional identity needs\_external''.  Thus the $f_3$ residual axis is read inside a multi-axis finite spectrum rather than as a single unrestricted codon law.

\paragraph{Fourth-axis pressure.}
The three-axis sufficiency run reports ``fourth\_mechanism\_present'' on the same finite design scale: five folds, $11$ computed organisms out of $16$, at least $500$ proteins per organism, and $41$ synonymous design columns.  The axis-strength spectrum records that $d_{resid4}$ leads on average, followed by $d_{tRNA}$; all $11$ per-organism dominant counts fall on $d_{resid4}$, while domain means are descriptive only because the domain counts are imbalanced as archaea $=1$, bacteria $=2$, and eukaryote $=8$.  The largest reported $GC3$ rank effect is on $d_{usage}$ with $\rho=0.136$.  These numbers keep the present claim finite and comparative: $f_3$-translation selection is the dominant non-tRNA residual optimum in the named run, not a complete decomposition of every residual mechanism.

\paragraph{Mechanism exclusions.}
Several candidate downstream identities have already been bounded away in the companion finite rows.  The mRNA-stability test concludes ``fourth\_axis\_not\_mrna\_stability'' because yeast $d_{opt\_halflife}$ has low alignment with abundance $d_{resid4}$ under the fixed gates.  The codon-pair run concludes ``not\_codon\_pair'' with five folds, at least $500$ proteins per organism, $41$ synonymous design columns, and $50$ shuffle-null samples.  The translation-accuracy run concludes ``not\_accuracy'' across $11$ computed organisms out of $16$, with $200$ permutations and $41$ synonymous design columns.  The elongation-dwell run is only partial and is restricted to \textit{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}.  Therefore the local reading is narrow: the finite evidence selects a residual translation-selection axis, while refusing to identify it with mRNA half-life, codon-pair structure, translation accuracy, or a fully resolved dwell-time mechanism.

\paragraph{Support and break conditions.}
The support condition for the chapter is the existence of a non-vacuous controlled row in which the $f_3$-stress coordinate survives against the translation readout:
$$
\begin{aligned}
S_{f_3,T_j} &> 0,\\
Z&=\{\mathrm{amino\ acid\ composition},\mathrm{organism},GC3,\mathrm{length},M\mathrm{-density},\mathrm{mRNA\ control}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The break condition is equally finite: if every organism-matched translation readout yields $S_{f_3,T_j}\le 0$, or if the readout matrix is rank-degenerate, then this chapter has no positive translation-readout survival claim.  In particular, an all-$1.0$ three-organism controlled matrix is a rank boundary, not a survival result.  The powered panel described above supplies the residual-axis identification; a measured ribosome or translation-efficiency claim still requires joined codon, wobble-weight, readout, and mRNA-control rows.

\paragraph{Local no-promotion rule.}
Nothing in this chapter licenses an inference from $B^*_{Q6}$ geometry, modeled tAI or stAI weights, codon usage, or the symbol $S_{f_3,T_j}$ to protein abundance, folded structure, physical admissibility, molecular function, localization, stability, phenotype, mechanism, causation, or universality.  Even translation itself is only admitted at the level of the declared readout contact: modeled tAI or stAI is a bounded weight surface, whereas ribosome occupancy or translation efficiency requires an organism-matched measured row with matched mRNA control.  Any statement above that layer must bring its own finite contact and its own controlled statistic.
