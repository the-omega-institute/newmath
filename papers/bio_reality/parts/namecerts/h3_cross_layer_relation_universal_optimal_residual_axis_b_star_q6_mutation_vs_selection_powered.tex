\subsection{The $f_3$-Stress Residual Axis at the Translation Boundary}
\label{subsec:bioreality-q6-f3-stress-translation-boundary}

\paragraph{Finite coordinate surface.}
This record isolates the $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinate whose non-tRNA component is named by the finite assay as $f_3$-translation selection.  The carrier is a finite CDS-by-organism residual surface after amino-acid quotienting, restricted to the $f_3$-stress coordinate and to rows where codon usage, tRNA or wobble weights, and a translation-layer readout are kept as distinct entries.  The relevant third-position saturation computation has
$$
\begin{aligned}
M&=\{\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{ACA},\mathrm{ACG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\},\\
\operatorname{Sat}_{f_3}(R)&=\{\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\},\\
\partial_{f_3}R&=\varnothing .
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the finite topology itself does not supply a remaining $f_3$ boundary row: the biologically meaningful question is whether a controlled translation readout separates paired synonymous codons after amino-acid projection when the context weights satisfy $w_c\ne w_{c^{f_3}}$.

\paragraph{Powered residual-axis evidence.}
The powered residual-axis experiment reports status passed for $11$ computed organisms out of $16$ requested organisms, using $5$ folds, a minimum of $500$ proteins per organism, and $41$ synonymous-design columns.  Its deterministic seed is recorded as $\mathrm{sha256:b\_star\_q6\_universal\_optimal\_residual\_axis\_powered:deterministic}$.  The finite conclusion is that non-tRNA residual optimality aligns most strongly with $\mathrm{f3\_translation\_selection}$, and the dominant axis is exactly $\mathrm{f3\_translation\_selection}$.  This is the positive finite-row reason for retaining the $f_3$-stress coordinate as a BioReality candidate rather than discarding it as a purely codon-topological artifact.

\paragraph{Neighboring axis checks.}
The same finite panel prevents over-reading the axis.  The core tRNA-adaptation check also passed on $11$ computed organisms out of $16$ requested organisms with $5$ folds, at least $500$ proteins per organism, and $41$ synonymous-design columns.  The two-axis closure check passed under the same organism, fold, protein-count, and design-column counts, but its conclusion was $\mathrm{third\_axis\_present}$, with the reason that $d_\perp$ has systematically positive held-out $R^2$ and mutually same-signed directions across organisms.  The third-axis identity check again used $11/16$ organisms, $5$ folds, at least $500$ proteins per organism, and $41$ synonymous-design columns; it identified $d_\perp$ provisionally with usage frequency within family, with mean cosine $0.128$ and one-sided sign-test $p=0.000488$.  The third-axis mechanism check kept significant cross-species alignment and held-out $R^2$ after GC orthogonalization, so the recorded finite call was selection-not-GC, not-wobble-clustered, with CSC or positional identity still needing a separate contact.  These checks place the $f_3$-stress row inside a multi-axis residual spectrum rather than making it a universal law.

\paragraph{Spectrum and mechanism boundary.}
The axis-strength spectrum was computed for $11$ organisms out of $16$ requested organisms with $5$ folds, at least $500$ proteins per organism, and $41$ synonymous-design columns.  The captured spectra were led by $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$ on average, then by $d_{\mathrm{tRNA}}$; the per-organism dominant count was $\{d_{\mathrm{resid4}}:11\}$.  Domain means were explicitly descriptive only, with the imbalanced counts $\{\mathrm{archaea}:1,\mathrm{bacteria}:2,\mathrm{eukaryote}:8\}$, and the largest GC3 rank effect was $d_{\mathrm{usage}}$ with $\rho=0.136$.  A separate boundary-set test for the $R$ sense codons computed $40$ organisms out of $46$ requested, skipped $6$, used $1000$ permutations at $\alpha=0.05$, and called the $R$ sense codons systematically tRNA-poor under GtRNAdb tGCN plus dos Reis wobble weights.  That supports a tRNA-supply interpretation for $R$ avoidance, but it does not by itself prove a measured $f_3$ translation-survival entry.

\paragraph{Fourth-axis exclusions.}
The optimality-axis sufficiency check passed on $11/16$ organisms with $5$ folds, at least $500$ proteins per organism, and $41$ synonymous-design columns, and its finite conclusion was $\mathrm{fourth\_mechanism\_present}$.  The current fourth-axis probes do not collapse the $f_3$-stress row into several tempting alternatives.  The mRNA-stability probe passed with conclusion $\mathrm{fourth\_axis\_not\_mrna\_stability}$, because yeast $d_{\mathrm{opt\_halflife}}$ had low alignment with abundance $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$ under the fixed gates.  The elongation-dwell probe in \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae} passed with conclusion partial.  The codon-pair probe passed with $5$ folds, at least $500$ proteins per organism, $41$ synonymous-design columns, and $50$ shuffle-null samples, with conclusion $\mathrm{not\_codon\_pair}$.  The translation-accuracy probe passed on $11/16$ organisms with $5$ folds, $41$ synonymous-design columns, and $200$ permutations, with conclusion $\mathrm{not\_accuracy}$.  These rows justify a narrow residual-axis statement, not a downstream protein or phenotype claim.

\paragraph{Local readout rule.}
The allowed readback is the controlled survival entry $S_{f3\text{-}\mathrm{stress},T_j}$ for a bounded translation-layer row such as tAI, stAI, or ribosome-occupancy/translation-efficiency.  The support condition is not merely codon topology and not merely a model weight: after amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, CDS length, M-density, matched mRNA, rank, and simpler-baseline controls, the same organism or same CDS rows must show a non-vacuous positive survival statistic for the $f_3$-stress coordinate.  A three-organism all-$1.0$ controlled surface is treated as rank saturation rather than survival.  A positive modeled alignment can nominate the axis, while a measured or explicitly bounded translation row is still required before the word survival is used.

\paragraph{Cannot-claim boundary.}
This chapter does not infer ribosome dynamics from codon geometry alone, does not infer protein abundance from tAI or stAI weights, and does not infer folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, localization, stability, phenotype, mechanism, or universality from the residual coordinate.  Those claims require their own layer-matched contacts and their own finite statistics.  The present record says only this: in the powered finite panel, the non-tRNA residual optimality axis is identified with $f_3$-translation selection, and the next BioReality test is whether wobble-broken, amino-acid-quotiented synonymous imbalance survives in a controlled translation-readout row.
