\subsection{The $f_3$-stress residual axis as a bounded translation-readout conjecture}
\label{sec:biorreality-q6-f3-stress-translation-readout}

\paragraph{Finite coordinate surface.}
The object considered here is a finite CDS-by-organism residual coordinate surface restricted to the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ $f_3$-stress coordinate and to matched translation-readout rows.  The coordinate is formed only after amino-acid projection: synonymous codons are compared inside their amino-acid fibers, and the surviving imbalance is the part paired by the third-position operation $f_3$.  In the finite saturation row, the union $M = \mathrm{Med}(R) \cup \{\mathrm{ACA}\}$ is the twenty-codon set
$$
\begin{aligned}
M=\{&\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{ACA},\mathrm{ACG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\\
&\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The $f_3$-saturated $R$ row removes the two codons $\mathrm{ACA}$ and $\mathrm{ACG}$ and leaves eighteen codons:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{Sat}_{f_3}(R)=\{&\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\\
&\mathrm{CUU},\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The recorded $f_3$ boundary row is empty, so this chapter treats the $f_3$ stress as a residual coordinate rather than as an independently populated boundary class.  The mathematical claim is therefore not that the codon list itself is a biological measurement, but that this finite coordinate can be tested against a separate translation-layer row once organism-matched readouts exist.

\paragraph{Observed finite computation.}
The powered residual-axis computation for the claim passed with experiment run $\mathrm{2a526c9fb7f4}$.  It used five folds, required at least $500$ proteins per organism, computed $11$ organisms out of $16$ requested organisms, and used $41$ synonymous-design columns.  Its conclusion was that non-tRNA residual optimality aligns most with the axis named $f3\_translation\_selection$; the dominant axis field is exactly $f3\_translation\_selection$.  This is a finite-row statement about the residual design matrix and its translation-selection candidate direction.  It is not, by itself, an observed ribosome-occupancy or translation-efficiency survival statistic.

The neighboring powered rows constrain the interpretation.  The tRNA-poverty mechanism test for the $R$ sense codons passed on $40$ computed organisms out of $46$ requested, with $6$ skipped organisms, a permutation count of $1000$, and $\alpha=0.05$; it reports that the $R$ sense codons are systematically tRNA-poor under GtRNAdb tGCN with dos Reis wobble weights.  The universal core tRNA-adaptation row also passed on $11$ computed organisms out of $16$ requested organisms, again with five folds, a minimum of $500$ proteins per organism, and $41$ synonymous-design columns.  The two-axis closure row did not close the story: it passed with the conclusion that a third axis is present, because $d_{\perp}$ has systematically positive held-out $R^2$ and mutually same-signed directions across organisms.  The third-axis identity row provisionally aligned $d_{\perp}$ to usage frequency within family, with mean cosine $0.128$ and one-sided sign-test $p=0.000488$.  The third-axis mechanism row classified the signal as selection-not-GC, not wobble-clustered, and requiring an outside CSC or positional identity to settle the remaining mechanism label.

\paragraph{Spectrum and fourth-axis guardrails.}
The axis-strength spectrum row computed $11$ organisms out of $16$ requested organisms, with five folds, at least $500$ proteins per organism, and $41$ synonymous-design columns.  Its captured spectra were led by $d_{resid4}$ on average, followed by $d_{tRNA}$, and every per-organism dominant count in the captured panel was assigned to $d_{resid4}$, namely $11$ out of $11$.  Domain summaries are explicitly descriptive under the imbalanced domain counts $n=\{\mathrm{archaea}:1,\mathrm{bacteria}:2,\mathrm{eukaryote}:8\}$, and the largest GC3 rank effect reported there is $d_{usage}$ with $\rho=0.136$.  The three-axis sufficiency row concluded that a fourth mechanism is present.  The fourth-mechanism rows then ruled out several tempting promotions: the mRNA-stability test concluded that the fourth axis is not mRNA stability in the yeast assay because $d_{opt\_halflife}$ has low alignment with abundance $d_{resid4}$; the codon-pair row concluded $not\_codon\_pair$ with $50$ shuffle-null draws; the translation-accuracy row concluded $not\_accuracy$ with $200$ permutations; and the elongation-dwell row was only partial in \textit{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}.  Thus the finite computation supports a named residual translation-selection direction while also recording that several downstream explanations are not licensed by this row.

\paragraph{Readout equation.}
Let $Q_{f_3}$ denote the amino-acid-quotiented $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ $f_3$-stress residual coordinate, let $T_j$ be a bounded translation readout such as tAI, stAI, ribosome occupancy, or translation efficiency, and let
$Z$ collect amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, CDS length, $M$-density, matched mRNA abundance when applicable, rank controls, and simpler codon baselines.  The only positive readback permitted here is a controlled survival entry
$$
\begin{aligned}
S_{f_3,T_j} > 0
\end{aligned}
$$
computed on same-organism and same-CDS rows after those controls.  The conjectural support condition is therefore not mere codon topology and not a modeled adaptation weight in isolation.  It requires a non-vacuous translation row in contexts where the wobble or tRNA weights break the $f_3$ symmetry, written locally as $w_c \ne w_{c^{f_3}}$.  If the panel is rank-degenerate, or if every controlled $S_{f_3,T_j}$ is non-positive, the conjecture fails at the translation-readout interface even if the residual-axis computation itself remains internally well defined.

\paragraph{Local biological interpretation.}
The finite result says that, inside the powered $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual surface, the strongest named non-tRNA residual optimality direction is $f3\_translation\_selection$.  The biological reading is deliberately narrower: after amino-acid quotienting, the $f_3$-stress coordinate is a candidate for a translation-readout distinction only in organisms or sequence contexts where tRNA supply or wobble weights distinguish paired third-position codons.  The contacts named by codon usage, tRNA gene copy number, and tAI or stAI weights can define the finite comparison surface.  Ribosome profiling or translation-efficiency rows, together with matched mRNA abundance, are required before the same coordinate can be called an observed translation-survival entry.  A three-organism all-$1.0$ controlled matrix is a rank-saturation boundary, not evidence of survival.

\paragraph{Non-promotion boundary.}
No claim about protein abundance, folded structure, localization, stability, molecular function, physical admissibility, organismal phenotype, mechanism in the strong causal sense, or universality follows from this chapter.  A positive modeled tAI or stAI association remains a bounded translation-weight readback unless a separate measured translation contact supplies same-row ribosome or TE observations.  Even a positive measured $S_{f_3,T_j}$ would license only the controlled translation-readout statement for that coordinate, organism set, readout definition, and covariate set.  Every higher layer requires its own matched contact and its own statistic; the residual coordinate and the codon lists cannot be promoted into those conclusions.
