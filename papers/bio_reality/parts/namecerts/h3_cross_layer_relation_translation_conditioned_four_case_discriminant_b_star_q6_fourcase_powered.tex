\subsection{Translation-conditioned four-case discrimination for $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residuals}\label{subsec:bioreality-q6-fourcase-discriminant}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite matrix being read.}
This record fixes a finite cross-layer matrix over nine $B^{*}_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates, a translation readout $T$, and a protein-abundance readout $P$.  The residual coordinate list is
$K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $Leu_{CUN\_vs\_UUR}$, $Leu_{UUA\_vs\_UUG}$, $Ser_{UCR\_vs\_AGY}$, $Ser_{UCA\_vs\_UCG}$, $Thr_{ACR\_vs\_ACY}$, and $f3\_stress$.  The experiment used real CDS codon counts, measured PAXdb protein abundance as $P=\log_{10}(abundance\_ppm)$, and a bounded translation proxy $T$ equal to the per-protein mean modeled tAI,
$$
\begin{aligned}
T(g) &= \frac{\sum_{c} count_g(c)\,tAI(c)}{\sum_{c} count_g(c)},
\end{aligned}
$$
with GtRNAdb all-tRNA copy counts and dos Reis wobble weights.  The covariate block $Z$ contains an intercept, CDS length, twenty amino-acid composition proportions, $GC3$, and the $M$-density baseline.  The finite rows are powered in both organisms: $rank(Z)=23$, the residualized $Q$ design has rank $9$ for both $P$ and $T$, and all coordinates were classified.

\paragraph{Four labels.}
For each residual coordinate, the row is assigned by the signs of $S^{QT}$ and $S^{QP}$ together with the translation-conditioning test.  A coordinate is not promoted from association to a translation-conditioned protein claim merely because $S^{QP}$ is positive.  The discriminant separates four outcomes: no survival, translation-only survival, direct protein-omics survival without a measured reduction through $T$, and translation-conditioned attenuation of the direct protein-abundance signal.  The classification threshold was $10^{-12}$, so the labels are numerical finite-row labels rather than qualitative impressions.

\paragraph{Modeled-tAI finite outcome.}
Two organisms met the row requirements for this modeled-tAI discriminant.  In \textit{Escherichia coli} K12 MG1655, $n=3739$ CDS/protein rows were joined, the residual degrees of freedom after $Z$ were $3716$, the residual energy of $P$ was $4020.333671802061$, and the residual energy of $T$ was $0.4728468725814029$.  The $Q$ block explained $432.89050065533684$ units of $P$ energy and $0.256128714706293$ units of $T$ energy.  The aggregate finite-row scores were
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{QP} &= 0.10767526677985896,\\
R^2_{QT} &= 0.5416736993691318.
\end{aligned}
$$
The four-case count was $A=0$, $B=0$, $C=0$, $D=7$, with two additional positive-positive coordinates lacking the mediation drop required for the $D$ interpretation.  No coordinate was left unclassified.

In \textit{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}, $n=5815$ CDS/protein rows were joined, the residual degrees of freedom after $Z$ were $5792$, the residual energy of $P$ was $2958.1865890925337$, and the residual energy of $T$ was $6.90537888244409$.  The $Q$ block explained $882.6526210803958$ units of $P$ energy and $5.671703404506426$ units of $T$ energy.  The aggregate finite-row scores were
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{QP} &= 0.29837624994140827,\\
R^2_{QT} &= 0.8213457220900503.
\end{aligned}
$$
The four-case count was $A=0$, $B=0$, $C=0$, $D=9$, with no positive-positive coordinate failing the mediation-drop condition and no unclassified coordinate.

\paragraph{Relation to measured translation rows.}
The modeled-tAI rows are not ribosome-profiling observations.  A separate measured-TE finite row used $T=\log_{10}(TE)$ where $TE=RPF/mRNA$ and reported powered $S^{QT,meas}$ survival in four organisms: $R^2_{QT,meas}=0.009394266754822262$ for \textit{E. coli} with $3415$ genes used and a TE join hit rate of $0.9133458143888741$; $R^2_{QT,meas}=0.13681931343730436$ for yeast with $5128$ genes used and a hit rate of $0.8818572656921754$; $R^2_{QT,meas}=0.018837911344127926$ for human with $11554$ genes used and a hit rate of $0.6064136881331024$; and $R^2_{QT,meas}=0.04034217614654355$ for zebrafish with $13140$ genes used and a hit rate of $0.9118667591950035$.  The measured mediation row then gave measured mediated fractions $0.04091158340950673$ for \textit{E. coli}, $0.4793069656345167$ for yeast, $0.0027966485036786744$ for human, and $0$ for zebrafish after clipping the suppressive raw value $-0.04801486048721136$.  These measured rows support the interpretation that the modeled-tAI discriminant is a bounded translation-readout classification, not a substitute for direct ribosome evidence.

\paragraph{Protein-omics background.}
The direct $Q\to P$ protein-omics survival surface is broad but still non-mechanistic.  In the direct PAXdb row, every one of the nine residual coordinates survived in each of fifteen organisms after the same style of length, amino-acid composition, $GC3$, and $M$-density controls.  Representative aggregate $R^2_{QP}$ values include $0.10767526677985896$ for \textit{E. coli}, $0.29837624994140827$ for yeast, $0.04454565118019448$ for human, $0.049827984062539195$ for mouse, $0.04613529807085213$ for zebrafish, and $0.019256877907572044$ for \textit{Halobacterium salinarum}.  This background explains why the four-case discriminant is needed: direct protein-abundance association alone is too coarse to decide whether translation readout accounts for the association.

\paragraph{Local verdict.}
The finite-row verdict is positive for the discriminant itself.  The experiment produced a complete four-case classification for the modeled-tAI/protein-abundance matrix in \textit{E. coli} and yeast, with full $Q$ rank, $rank(Z)=23$, nondegenerate residual degrees of freedom, no unclassified coordinates, and positive $R^2_{QT}$ and $R^2_{QP}$ in both organisms.  The strongest local call is yeast, where all nine coordinates fall into case $D$ under the declared modeled-tAI conditioning rule.  The \textit{E. coli} call is also informative but mixed: seven coordinates satisfy the case-$D$ rule, while two positive-positive coordinates do not show the required reduction through the modeled translation readout.

\paragraph{Boundary.}
The chapter does not assert translation mechanism, folded structural order, physical admissibility, molecular function, system phenotype, perturbation rescue, evolutionary directionality, or a universal biological law.  Modeled tAI is an organism-scoped codon-adaptation readout, not observed ribosome occupancy.  PAXdb abundance is a steady-state protein-omics readout, not isolated synthesis rate, folding success, localization, turnover, post-translational state, pathway activity, or function.  A stronger biological interpretation would require a separate layer-matched contact, such as matched ribosome profiling, perturbation, localization, turnover, structural, functional, or phenotype data, together with the same residual controls and an explicit demonstration that conditioning on the measured translation readout reduces the relevant downstream association.
