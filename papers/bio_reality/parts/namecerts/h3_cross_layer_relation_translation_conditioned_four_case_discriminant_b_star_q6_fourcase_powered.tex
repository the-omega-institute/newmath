\subsection{Translation-conditioned four-case discriminant for $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates}
\label{sec:bioreality-q6-fourcase-discriminant}

\paragraph{Finite carrier and readouts.}
This record tests whether a finite synonymous-residual surface over $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ can be separated into translation-surviving, protein-omics-surviving, and translation-conditioned protein-abundance classes. The carrier consists of nine residual coordinates,
$K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $Leu_{CUN\,vs\,UUR}$, $Leu_{UUA\,vs\,UUG}$, $Ser_{UCR\,vs\,AGY}$, $Ser_{UCA\,vs\,UCG}$, $Thr_{ACR\,vs\,ACY}$, and $f3_{stress}$, evaluated on matched coding-sequence rows. The discriminant uses a protein readout $P=\log_{10}(\mathrm{abundance\_ppm})$ from measured PAXdb protein abundance and a translation readout $T$ given by per-protein mean modeled tAI,
$\sum_{c} n_c w_c / \sum_c n_c$, with tRNA-copy weights from GtRNAdb and the dos Reis wobble scheme. Both readouts are residualized against the same control family: an intercept, coding-sequence length, amino-acid composition, GC3, and the $M$-density baseline induced by the union support of the nine $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ projected coordinates.

\paragraph{Case rule.}
For each coordinate $q_i$, the row-level design forms residualized quantities $q_i^{\perp Z}$, $T^{\perp Z}$, and $P^{\perp Z}$. The two survival tests are the translation survival $S^{QT}$ and the protein-omics survival $S^{QP}$, each thresholded at $10^{-12}$. The four-case readback is a finite classification rather than a causal interpretation. Case A denotes no positive survival into either readout. Case B denotes translation-only survival. Case C denotes protein-omics survival without an admissible translation-conditioning drop and is therefore only a direct or confounded protein-omics association. Case D denotes the strongest finite label in this run: $S^{QT}>0$, $S^{QP}>0$, and a translation-conditioning reduction of the direct $q_i \to P$ association under the stated controls. Equivalently, the intended discriminant is
$$
\begin{aligned}
A &: S^{QT} \leq 10^{-12}\ \mathrm{and}\ S^{QP} \leq 10^{-12},\\
B &: S^{QT} > 10^{-12}\ \mathrm{and}\ S^{QP} \leq 10^{-12},\\
C &: S^{QT} \leq 10^{-12}\ \mathrm{or}\ \hbox{no translation-conditioning drop},\quad S^{QP}>10^{-12},\\
D &: S^{QT}>10^{-12},\quad S^{QP}>10^{-12},\quad \Delta_{P\mid T}>0.
\end{aligned}
$$
Here $\Delta_{P\mid T}$ records the observed reduction of the direct $Q\to P$ fit after conditioning on the matched translation readout. The label is still descriptive: it says that the finite readout geometry is consistent with translation-conditioned absorption in the chosen readout, not that a perturbative molecular mechanism has been established.

\paragraph{Rank and row support.}
The finite run used two organism panels for the modeled-tAI discriminant. In \emph{Escherichia coli} K12 MG1655, $3739$ coding-sequence/protein rows were joined, with $3739$ proteins used and the translation weights taken from the \emph{E. coli} tRNA table. The control matrix had $24$ columns and rank $23$; the residualized $Q$ design had rank $9$ for both $P$ and $T$; the residual degrees of freedom after controls were $3716$. The residual protein energy was $4020.333671802061$, and the residual modeled-tAI energy was $0.4728468725814029$. The explained energy of the nine-coordinate surface on $P$ was $432.89050065533684$, while the explained energy on $T$ was $0.256128714706293$.

In \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}, $5815$ coding-sequence/protein rows were joined and $5815$ proteins were used, with yeast tRNA weights. The same $24$-column control family again had rank $23$; the residualized $Q$ design had rank $9$ against both readouts; the residual degrees of freedom were $5792$. The residual protein energy was $2958.1865890925337$, and the residual modeled-tAI energy was $6.90537888244409$. The $Q$ surface explained $882.6526210803958$ units of protein residual energy and $5.671703404506426$ units of modeled-tAI residual energy. Both panels therefore pass the declared powered-rank condition rather than relying on a saturated small-$n$ surface.

\paragraph{Observed four-case table.}
The modeled-tAI/protein-abundance discriminant classified all coordinates in both organisms. For \emph{E. coli}, the joint survival summary was $R^2_{QP}=0.10767526677985896$ and $R^2_{QT}=0.5416736993691318$ across the nine-coordinate design. The case counts were
$$
\begin{aligned}
A &= 0,\qquad B = 0,\qquad C = 0,\qquad D = 7,\\
\mathrm{positive\ positive\ without\ mediation\ drop} &= 2.
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus every coordinate had positive signal in the finite sign tests, but two coordinates were explicitly withheld from the translation-conditioned class because the conditioning drop was not present. For yeast, the corresponding summary was $R^2_{QP}=0.29837624994140827$ and $R^2_{QT}=0.8213457220900503$, again over nine coordinates. The case table was
$$
\begin{aligned}
A &= 0,\qquad B = 0,\qquad C = 0,\qquad D = 9,\\
\mathrm{positive\ positive\ without\ mediation\ drop} &= 0.
\end{aligned}
$$
No coordinates were left unclassified in either organism. The yeast panel is therefore the clean finite example in this run: all nine $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates survive both the modeled translation readout and measured protein abundance, and all nine receive the translation-conditioned case-D label under the declared rule.

\paragraph{Relation to measured-TE and protein-omics checks.}
The broader finite evidence shows that the direct protein-omics association is not an isolated artifact of the two modeled-tAI panels. In the direct protein-abundance survival run, the same nine-coordinate surface survived after controls in fifteen organism panels, with $R^2_{QP}$ values ranging from $0.019256877907572044$ in \emph{Halobacterium salinarum} to $0.29837624994140827$ in yeast; every listed organism had all nine coordinates surviving under the $10^{-12}$ positive partial-$R^2$ threshold. The measured ribosome-TE run then supplied a separate translation readout for four organisms: $R^2_{QT,meas}=0.009394266754822262$ for \emph{E. coli}, $0.13681931343730436$ for yeast, $0.018837911344127926$ for human, and $0.04034217614654355$ for zebrafish, again with all nine coordinates surviving in each joined measured-TE panel. These measured-TE rows do not replace the modeled-tAI four-case table above; they show that a ribosome-derived translation readout also carries finite $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual signal after the same style of controls.

\paragraph{Mediation scale from matched measured readouts.}
When measured TE, measured protein abundance, and codon residuals are joined directly, the finite mediation scalar is smaller and organism-dependent. The measured $M^{QTP}$ fractions were $0.04091158340950673$ for \emph{E. coli}, $0.4793069656345167$ for yeast, $0.0027966485036786744$ for human, and $0.0$ for zebrafish after truncating a negative raw value of $-0.04801486048721136$ to the unit interval. The three-way row counts were $3415$ for \emph{E. coli}, $5128$ for yeast, $11554$ for human, and $13140$ for zebrafish. These values sharpen the interpretation of the four-case table: modeled tAI can produce a strong translation-conditioned finite classification, while measured TE confirms a real translation-readout signal but does not make the same mediation magnitude uniform across organisms.

\paragraph{Residual dictionary and additional mediators.}
A separate yeast partial-mediation certificate used a candidate readout family containing measured TE, mRNA stability, and turnover. On a joined yeast base of $4759$ available rows, the selected finite certificate used $n=1800$ rows, five folds, $\lambda_{DL}=0.01$, and $\rho_{join}=0.05$. The raw best coverage over the candidate family was $0.2745002077400602$, the selection-aware coverage was $C^{\ast}=0.24538211439730545$, the selection-aware null $95\%$ level was $0.029149007242956115$, and the unexplained fraction remained $0.7546178856026946$. In the joint TE--mRNA-stability decomposition, the right-tail permutation value was $0.000999000999000999$ for \emph{E. coli}, human, and yeast. The joint mediation fractions were $0.2404669586626661$, $0.16890712099923358$, and $0.5195182225621592$, respectively. These rows support the local reading that translation and transcript or stability readouts absorb a real but incomplete part of the protein-abundance residual surface.

\paragraph{Local boundary.}
The output of this chapter is a finite discriminant over matched rows, not a universal biological law. The modeled-tAI readout is a translation-layer proxy, not ribosome profiling. The PAXdb readout is steady-state protein abundance, not isolated synthesis rate, folded state, activity, localization, turnover, pathway function, phenotype, or physical admissibility. A case-D label is therefore admissible only as a translation-conditioned finite association under the stated controls. No claim about direct translation mechanism, protein folding, molecular function, perturbation rescue, organismal phenotype, physical feasibility, or universality follows from this table unless a separate layer-matched measurement supplies that readout and the corresponding controlled statistic is recomputed on matched rows.
