\subsection{Translation-conditioned four-case readback for $B^*_{Q6}$ protein-abundance residuals}\label{subsec:bioreality-q6-fourcase-powered}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite surface.}
This record tests a finite cross-layer surface whose rows are coding-sequence entries and whose columns are the nine $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinates.  The coordinate list is $K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $Leu_{CUN\,vs\,UUR}$, $Leu_{UUA\,vs\,UUG}$, $Ser_{UCR\,vs\,AGY}$, $Ser_{UCA\,vs\,UCG}$, $Thr_{ACR\,vs\,ACY}$, and $f_3$ stress.  Each row carries real CDS codon counts, a matched modeled translation readout $T$, and a matched protein-abundance readout $P$.  The protein readout is $\log_{10}$ PAXdb abundance in ppm.  The translation readout used by this discriminant is the per-protein codon-count-weighted mean modeled tAI, computed from GtRNAdb all-tRNA copy counts and dos Reis wobble weights.  This is therefore a bounded modeled readout, not ribosome occupancy.

\paragraph{Residualization and rank.}
Both organisms in the finite panel pass the rank gate.  In \emph{Escherichia coli} K12 MG1655, the joined matrix has $3739$ CDS/protein rows, $24$ control columns, $\operatorname{rank}(Z)=23$, residual degrees of freedom $3716$, and full residual rank $9$ for both the $Q\to P$ and $Q\to T$ designs.  The $Q$ block explains $432.89050065533684$ units of residual protein-abundance energy and $0.256128714706293$ units of residual modeled-tAI energy.  In \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}, the joined matrix has $5815$ rows, $24$ control columns, $\operatorname{rank}(Z)=23$, residual degrees of freedom $5792$, and full residual rank $9$ for both designs.  There the $Q$ block explains $882.6526210803958$ units of residual protein-abundance energy and $5.671703404506426$ units of residual modeled-tAI energy.  The control set contains amino-acid composition, CDS length, GC3, organism-level baseline structure, and $M$-density, so the four-case labels are assigned after removing those lower-layer covariates.

\paragraph{Four-case rule.}
The classification threshold is $10^{-12}$.  For each coordinate, the finite readback records whether $S^{QT}$ is positive, whether $S^{QP}$ is positive, and whether conditioning on $T$ removes or reduces the direct $Q\to P$ association.  The aggregate fitted surfaces are
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{QP}(\textit{E. coli}) &= 0.10767526677985896, & R^2_{QT}(\textit{E. coli}) &= 0.5416736993691318,\\
R^2_{QP}(\textit{S. cerevisiae}) &= 0.29837624994140827, & R^2_{QT}(\textit{S. cerevisiae}) &= 0.8213457220900503.
\end{aligned}
$$
The finite module assignment is decisive at the stated threshold: \emph{E. coli} has no case A, B, or C coordinates, seven case D coordinates, and two positive-positive coordinates without the required mediation drop; yeast has no case A, B, or C coordinates and nine case D coordinates.  No coordinates are unclassified in either organism.  Thus, on this modeled-tAI/protein-abundance surface, all classified coordinates fall in the translation-surviving and protein-omics-surviving cell with the recorded attenuation condition; the two \emph{E. coli} positive-positive non-drop entries are kept outside the mediated cell rather than promoted.

\paragraph{Measured-readout comparison.}
The modeled-tAI result should be read beside the measured-ribo-seq TE runs.  In the measured TE survival surface, $R^2_{QT,meas}$ is $0.009394266754822262$ for \emph{E. coli}, $0.13681931343730436$ for yeast, $0.018837911344127926$ for human, and $0.04034217614654355$ for zebrafish, with all four organisms carrying nine surviving coordinates under the positive threshold.  In the measured TE-to-abundance mediation decomposition, the measured mediated fraction is $0.04091158340950673$ for \emph{E. coli}, $0.4793069656345167$ for yeast, $0.0027966485036786744$ for human, and $0.0$ for zebrafish after clipping a negative raw fraction.  These values show why the present four-case discriminant is reported as a finite readout classification, not as a general claim about translation dynamics: the modeled tAI surface is strong, while measured TE mediation is organism- and dataset-dependent.

\paragraph{Abundance-residual context.}
The direct protein-omics signal is real enough to require further partitioning.  In the joint TE and mRNA-stability decomposition, \emph{E. coli} has $R^2_{QP}=0.1181743523064932$ and joint mediation fraction $0.2404669586626661$ with right-tail permutation $p=0.000999000999000999$; human has $R^2_{QP}=0.04274178488511215$ and joint mediation fraction $0.16890712099923358$ with the same right-tail permutation value; yeast has $R^2_{QP}=0.2715520808117803$ and joint mediation fraction $0.5195182225621592$, again with right-tail permutation $p=0.000999000999000999$.  A separate yeast readout-dictionary reduction uses $n=1800$, five folds, $\lambda_{DL}=0.01$, $\rho_{join}=0.05$, and $120$ permutations; its best accepted coverage is $0.24538211439730545$, leaving unexplained fraction $0.7546178856026946$ after a selection-aware null $95\%$ value of $0.029149007242956115$.  The four-case table is therefore a discriminant over a finite matrix, while the larger residual remains only partly reducible to the measured readout dictionary.

\paragraph{Local boundary.}
The present evidence permits a finite statement about $S^{QT}$, $S^{QP}$, and the translation-conditioned attenuation rule on two rank-sufficient modeled-tAI/protein-abundance panels.  It does not by itself establish observed ribosome motion, protein folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, phenotype, causation, perturbation rescue, or a universal biological rule.  A separate layer-matched contact is required before any such assertion is allowed.  In particular, a direct $Q\to P$ association is only a protein-omics association unless a positive $Q\to T$ step is present and the conditioned fit reduces the $Q\to P$ signal on matched rows.  The all-positive classification in the two organisms is a finite readback of this panel, not a license to extrapolate beyond its row joins, controls, modeled translation readout, and abundance assay.
