\subsection{Translation-conditioned four-case discrimination for rank-powered $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residuals}\label{sec:h3-cross-layer-relation-translation-conditioned-four-case-discriminant-b-star-q6-fourcase-powered}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite row surface.}
This record fixes a finite cross-layer readout surface for the claim \texttt{h3.cross\_layer\_relation.translation\_conditioned\_four\_case\_discriminant.b\_star\_q6\_fourcase\_powered}.  The carrier is the nine-coordinate $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual design
$$
\begin{aligned}
Q=\{&K\_{AAA},\ Arg\_{AGR},\ Ile\_{AUA},\ Leu\_{CUN\,vs\,UUR},\ Leu\_{UUA\,vs\,UUG},\\
&Ser\_{UCR\,vs\,AGY},\ Ser\_{UCA\,vs\,UCG},\ Thr\_{ACR\,vs\,ACY},\ f3\_{stress}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The rows are coding sequences joined to two downstream readouts on the same organism-specific protein surface: a translation-weight readout $T$ and a protein-abundance readout $P$.  In this run, $T$ is the per-protein mean modeled tAI, computed as $\sum_c n_c w_c/\sum_c n_c$ from codon counts and GtRNAdb all-tRNA copy counts with dos Reis wobble weights; $P$ is $\log_{10}(\mathrm{abundance\_ppm})$ from PAXdb protein abundance joined to real CDS codon counts.  The computation used two powered organisms: $3739$ joined CDS/protein rows for \textit{Escherichia coli} K12 MG1655, with the tRNA organism keyed as \textit{E. coli}, and $5815$ joined rows for \textit{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}.  The common residualizing design used $24$ control columns, rank $23$ after control projection, and the declared degeneracy threshold $0.999999$.

\paragraph{Controlled readout definition.}
For each organism, the experiment residualizes $Q$, $T$, and $P$ against the same background controls: CDS length, amino-acid composition, GC3, the $M$-density baseline, and the intercept-bearing control span.  The classification threshold is $10^{-12}$.  The matrix is not an unconditioned correlation table; it is a post-control readback in which $S^{QT}$ is the residual association from $Q$ to $T$, $S^{QP}$ is the residual association from $Q$ to $P$, and the case label also records whether conditioning on $T$ removes or reduces the direct $Q\to P$ component.  In compact form, the intended decision surface is
$$
\begin{aligned}
A &: S^{QT}\leq 10^{-12},\quad S^{QP}\leq 10^{-12},\\
B &: S^{QT}>10^{-12},\quad S^{QP}\leq 10^{-12},\\
C &: S^{QT}\leq 10^{-12},\quad S^{QP}>10^{-12},\\
D &: S^{QT}>10^{-12},\quad S^{QP}>10^{-12}\ \text{with a translation-conditioned drop in the direct protein signal}.
\end{aligned}
$$
A separate counter also records positive-positive coordinates for which the drop condition is absent; those are deliberately not promoted to case $D$.

\paragraph{Rank and energy diagnostics.}
Both organisms are rank-powered in the finite matrix actually evaluated.  For \textit{E. coli}, the residualized $Q$ design has rank $9$ for both the $P$ and $T$ fits, residual degrees of freedom $3716$, $T$ residual energy $0.4728468725814029$, and $P$ residual energy $4020.333671802061$.  The $Q$ design explains $0.256128714706293$ units of energy on $T$ and $432.89050065533684$ units on $P$.  The resulting organism-level readout strengths are
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{QT}(\textit{E. coli}) &= 0.5416736993691318,\\
R^2_{QP}(\textit{E. coli}) &= 0.10767526677985896.
\end{aligned}
$$
For yeast, the residualized $Q$ design again has rank $9$ for both fits, residual degrees of freedom $5792$, $T$ residual energy $6.90537888244409$, and $P$ residual energy $2958.1865890925337$.  The $Q$ design explains $5.671703404506426$ units of energy on $T$ and $882.6526210803958$ units on $P$, with
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{QT}(\textit{S. cerevisiae}) &= 0.8213457220900503,\\
R^2_{QP}(\textit{S. cerevisiae}) &= 0.29837624994140827.
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the four-case call is not produced by an underdetermined surface: each organism carries all nine $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ coordinates through a full-rank residualized design.

\paragraph{Four-case outcome.}
The finite classification is sharply concentrated in the translation-positive and protein-positive sector.  For \textit{E. coli}, the case counts are
$$
\begin{aligned}
(A,B,C,D,\mathrm{positive\ positive\ without\ drop})=(0,0,0,7,2),
\end{aligned}
$$
with no unclassified coordinates.  For yeast, the case counts are
$$
\begin{aligned}
(A,B,C,D,\mathrm{positive\ positive\ without\ drop})=(0,0,0,9,0),
\end{aligned}
$$
again with no unclassified coordinates.  The readback therefore supports the narrow finite statement that modeled-tAI-positive $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual structure and measured-protein-abundance association coexist in both evaluated organisms, and that the translation-conditioned drop criterion assigns seven of nine coordinates to case $D$ in \textit{E. coli} and all nine coordinates to case $D$ in yeast.  The two \textit{E. coli} positive-positive coordinates without the drop remain outside the mediated class; they are direct or unresolved protein-omics associations on this readout, not hidden mechanism calls.

\paragraph{Relation to measured translation and abundance panels.}
The modeled-tAI four-case surface is consistent with, but not identical to, the measured-ribosome TE panels.  In measured TE survival, the same nine-coordinate design survived against $\log_{10}(\mathrm{TE})$ in four organisms, with $R^2_{QT,meas}=0.009394266754822262$ for \textit{E. coli}, $0.13681931343730436$ for yeast, $0.018837911344127926$ for human, and $0.04034217614654355$ for zebrafish; each had nine surviving coordinates after the same style of controls.  The direct protein-omics surface is broader: PAXdb abundance joins were computed for fifteen organisms, including $3739$ rows for \textit{E. coli}, $5815$ for yeast, $19053$ for human, $19395$ for mouse, $14410$ for zebrafish, and $4042$ for \textit{Bacillus subtilis}.  The direct $R^2_{QP}$ values include $0.10767526677985896$ for \textit{E. coli}, $0.29837624994140827$ for yeast, $0.04454565118019448$ for human, $0.049827984062539195$ for mouse, and $0.04613529807085213$ for zebrafish, with all nine coordinates surviving in each listed organism.  These numbers justify the four-case discriminator as a sorting device over measured and modeled readout layers, not as a standalone biological explanation.

Thus the modeled-tAI case $D$ outcome should be read as a finite discriminant over the specified modeled translation readout, while the measured-TE mediation surface supplies a separate, lower or organism-dependent attenuation measure.  The joint TE-plus-mRNA-stability decomposition further shows that single readouts do not exhaust the abundance residual: joint mediation fractions are $0.2404669586626661$ for \textit{E. coli}, $0.5195182225621592$ for yeast, and $0.16890712099923358$ for human, each with right-tail permutation value $0.000999000999000999$.  In yeast, a readout-dictionary certificate reports selection-aware coverage $C^{\ast}=0.24538211439730545$ at $\lambda_{DL}=0.01$, with unexplained fraction $0.7546178856026946$, and the $f3\_{stress}$ dwell panel gives held-out $\Delta R^2=0.01856473944057712$ above a permutation null $95$th percentile of $0.0013115362189692937$ with approximate $p=7.873916057196935\times 10^{-9}$.  These companion rows constrain the interpretation: the four-case labels are reliable finite labels, but the residual abundance surface remains only partially reduced by the measured readout dictionary.

\paragraph{Local cannot-claim boundary.}
The chapter does not infer observed ribosome occupancy from modeled tAI, and it does not infer folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, phenotype, perturbation response, evolutionary direction, or a universal biological law from any $Q\to P$ association.  Protein abundance is a cross-sectional omics readout and can absorb mRNA abundance, turnover, localization, PTM state, folding, pathway context, and platform effects.  Case $C$ is a direct or unresolved protein-omics association; case $D$ is only a translation-conditioned finite-row label for this specified design.  A stronger biological claim would require a separate matched contact at the relevant layer, such as ribosome profiling for observed TE, pulse or turnover data for stability, localization rows for compartment readout, structure measurements for folding, functional assays for molecular role, or perturbation and rescue rows for mechanism.  Without such a separate contact and a controlled statistic on the same finite rows, the valid conclusion is limited to the reported $S^{QT}$, $S^{QP}$, and translation-conditioned case assignments.
