\subsection{Translation-conditioned four-case discriminant for the rank-powered $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ panel}
\label{subsec:bioreality-q6-four-case-discriminant}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite surface.}
This record studies a finite matrix surface whose rows are coding-sequence entries and whose columns are nine $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates.  The coordinate list is
$K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $Leu_{CUN\,vs\,UUR}$, $Leu_{UUA\,vs\,UUG}$, $Ser_{UCR\,vs\,AGY}$, $Ser_{UCA\,vs\,UCG}$, $Thr_{ACR\,vs\,ACY}$, and $f_3$-stress.  The four-case experiment uses modeled per-protein tAI as the bounded translation-layer readout $T$ and measured PAXdb protein abundance as the downstream readout $P$.  The modeled tAI value is the codon-count weighted mean
$$
\begin{aligned}
T(g) &= \frac{\sum_c n_{g,c} w_c}{\sum_c n_{g,c}},
\end{aligned}
$$
where $n_{g,c}$ is the CDS codon count and $w_c$ is the GtRNAdb/dos Reis tAI weight.  The protein readout is $\log_{10}(\mathrm{abundance\ ppm})$ after joining measured protein abundance to real CDS codon counts.

\paragraph{Rows and residual controls.}
The computed four-case surface contains two rank-powered organism panels: $3739$ joined CDS/protein rows for \emph{Escherichia coli} K12 MG1655, using the \emph{E. coli} tRNA organism key, and $5815$ joined rows for \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}.  In both panels the control design has $24$ columns and residualized rank $23$.  After residualization, the $Q$ design has rank $9$ both for the protein-abundance target and for the modeled-tAI target.  The residual degrees of freedom are $3716$ in \emph{E. coli} and $5792$ in yeast.  The classification threshold is $10^{-12}$ and the degeneracy cutoff is $0.999999$; both organisms are explicitly marked as powered and classified rather than rank-saturated.

\paragraph{Four-case rule.}
Each coordinate is assigned by the signs of $S^{QT}$ and $S^{QP}$ and by the behavior of the direct $Q \to P$ signal after conditioning on $T$.  The local meanings are: case A has neither translation-readout survival nor protein-readout survival; case B has translation-readout survival without protein-readout survival; case C has protein-readout survival without an admissible translation-conditioned drop; and case D has a positive translation-readout association together with a protein-readout association whose direct component is reduced by the modeled translation readout.  This is a finite readout classification.  It is not a causal mediation theorem and it is not an assertion about ribosome occupancy.

\paragraph{Observed classified surface.}
The modeled-tAI four-case matrix is fully classified for the two organisms.  For \emph{E. coli}, the aggregate residual fits give
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{QP} &= 0.10767526677985896,\\
R^2_{QT} &= 0.5416736993691318,
\end{aligned}
$$
with seven coordinates assigned to case D, no coordinates assigned to cases A, B, or C, and two positive-positive coordinates lacking the mediation-drop condition.  For yeast, the aggregate residual fits give
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{QP} &= 0.29837624994140827,\\
R^2_{QT} &= 0.8213457220900503,
\end{aligned}
$$
with all nine coordinates assigned to case D and no unclassified coordinates.  The energy accounting is also finite-row: in \emph{E. coli}, $Q$ explains $432.89050065533684$ units on $P$ and $0.256128714706293$ units on $T$, against residual energies $4020.333671802061$ and $0.4728468725814029$.  In yeast, $Q$ explains $882.6526210803958$ units on $P$ and $5.671703404506426$ units on $T$, against residual energies $2958.1865890925337$ and $6.90537888244409$.

\paragraph{Relation to direct protein-omics survival.}
The broader direct protein-abundance run shows that the same nine residual coordinates survive as direct $Q \to P$ predictors in all fifteen protein-abundance organism panels.  The direct $R^2_{QP}$ values include $0.10767526677985896$ for \emph{E. coli}, $0.29837624994140827$ for yeast, $0.04454565118019448$ for human, $0.049827984062539195$ for mouse, and $0.04613529807085213$ for zebrafish, with all nine coordinates surviving in each listed organism.  These direct protein-omics numbers justify treating $Q \to P$ as a real finite-row association, but they do not by themselves decide whether the association is translation-conditioned.  The four-case discriminant is the additional sorting step: it asks whether an attached $T$ readout can account for the direct protein-abundance association.

\paragraph{Measured-TE contrast.}
A separate measured-ribo-seq TE run gives a useful calibration boundary.  In that run, the measured readout is $\log_{10}(TE)$ with $TE=RPF/mRNA$, and the joined gene counts are $3415$ for \emph{E. coli}, $5128$ for yeast, $11554$ for human, and $13140$ for zebrafish.  The measured $S^{QT}$ strengths are $0.009394266754822262$ in \emph{E. coli}, $0.13681931343730436$ in yeast, $0.018837911344127926$ in human, and $0.04034217614654355$ in zebrafish.  When protein abundance is conditioned on measured TE rather than modeled tAI, the measured mediated fractions are $0.04091158340950673$ for \emph{E. coli}, $0.4793069656345167$ for yeast, $0.0027966485036786744$ for human, and $0.0$ for zebrafish after truncating a suppressive raw value of $-0.04801486048721136$.  Thus the modeled-tAI four-case result is strongest as a bounded translation-readout classification, while measured TE confirms that the same question has a stricter empirical version with smaller and organism-dependent mediated fractions.

\paragraph{Additional finite mediation context.}
The joint TE and mRNA-stability run further separates translation readout from other post-transcriptional structure.  On four-way joins of $2517$ \emph{E. coli} rows, $11322$ human rows, and $4759$ yeast rows, the joint mediation fractions are $0.2404669586626661$, $0.16890712099923358$, and $0.5195182225621592$, respectively, each with right-tail permutation value $0.000999000999000999$.  The single-mediator decomposition is not uniform: yeast is TE-heavy with $0.4697188319693239$ TE-only fraction and $0.0626618595344251$ stability-only fraction, while \emph{E. coli} is stability-heavy with $0.01975521340963268$ TE-only fraction and $0.23052056324898387$ stability-only fraction.  This supports the local interpretation of the four-case table: a case label is a readout-conditioned variance partition, not a universal biological mechanism.

\paragraph{Verdict and boundary.}
The four-case discriminant is ready as a finite BioReality record for modeled-tAI-conditioned protein-abundance readback.  The ready content is narrow: in the two modeled-tAI panels, all coordinates are classified, the rank diagnostics are nondegenerate, and the dominant empirical outcome is case D, with yeast showing nine of nine case-D coordinates and \emph{E. coli} showing seven of nine plus two positive-positive coordinates without the mediation-drop condition.  No stronger biological conclusion follows from this chapter alone.  Modeled tAI is not observed ribosome movement; PAXdb abundance is not a pure translation-rate measurement; and neither direct $Q \to P$ survival nor a case-D assignment establishes realized translation dynamics, protein folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, phenotype, causal mechanism, or a universal biological law.  Any such stronger statement requires its own layer-matched contact and its own controlled finite-row statistic.
