\subsection{Translation-conditioned four-case readback for $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinates}
\label{sec:bioreality-q6-translation-conditioned-four-case}

\paragraph{Finite panel.}
The object tested here is a finite residual-coordinate surface over nine $B^*_{Q6}$ coordinates:
$K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $Leu_{CUN\,vs\,UUR}$, $Leu_{UUA\,vs\,UUG}$, $Ser_{UCR\,vs\,AGY}$, $Ser_{UCA\,vs\,UCG}$, $Thr_{ACR\,vs\,ACY}$, and $f3_{stress}$.
For each coding sequence row, the analysis compares a residualized codon-coordinate matrix $Q$, a translation readout $T$, and a protein-abundance readout $P$ after removing the declared covariates: intercept, coding-sequence length, amino-acid composition, $GC3$, and the $M$-density baseline.
The four-case run uses per-protein modeled tAI as $T$, computed as a codon-count-weighted mean of GtRNAdb tRNA-copy and dos Reis wobble weights, and uses $\log_{10}$ PAXdb abundance in ppm as $P$.
Two organisms pass the finite-row rank gate for this exact four-case discriminant: $E.\ coli$ K12 MG1655 and $S.\ cerevisiae$.
The joined row counts are $3739$ proteins for $E.\ coli$ and $5815$ proteins for yeast; the tRNA organism keys are respectively $E.\ coli$ and $S.\ cerevisiae$.

\paragraph{Residual rank and energy checks.}
Both organism blocks are nondegenerate at the coordinate level: the rank of the residualized $Q$ matrix is $9$ against the protein readout and also $9$ against the modeled-tAI readout, while the residualized control rank is $23$ from $24$ control columns.
For $E.\ coli$, the residual degrees of freedom after controls are $3716$, the residual protein energy is $4020.333671802061$, and the modeled-tAI residual energy is $0.4728468725814029$.
The same run records $Q$-explained energy $432.89050065533684$ on $P$ and $0.256128714706293$ on $T$.
For yeast, the residual degrees of freedom after controls are $5792$, the residual protein energy is $2958.1865890925337$, and the modeled-tAI residual energy is $6.90537888244409$.
The yeast $Q$-explained energies are $882.6526210803958$ on $P$ and $5.671703404506426$ on $T$.
Thus the classification is not an all-row saturated artifact: each organism has full residual coordinate rank for the nine-coordinate surface and a positive residual-energy target for both readouts.

\paragraph{Four-case rule.}
The discriminant uses threshold $10^{-12}$ and assigns each coordinate by three finite decisions: whether $S^{QT}$ is positive, whether $S^{QP}$ is positive, and whether the apparent $Q\to P$ signal is reduced by conditioning on $T$.
A convenient local notation is
$$
\begin{aligned}
A &: S^{QT} \leq 10^{-12},\quad S^{QP} \leq 10^{-12},\\
B &: S^{QT} > 10^{-12},\quad S^{QP} \leq 10^{-12},\\
C &: S^{QT} \leq 10^{-12},\quad S^{QP} > 10^{-12},\\
D &: S^{QT} > 10^{-12},\quad S^{QP} > 10^{-12}\ \text{and the conditioning test reduces the direct protein signal.}
\end{aligned}
$$
The remaining positive-positive coordinates, when present, are recorded separately because they show both $Q\to T$ and $Q\to P$ positivity without the required attenuation under $T$.
This last bin is intentionally not a mechanism label.
It only says that the modeled translation readout does not absorb the direct protein-abundance association under the fitted residual design.

\paragraph{Observed four-case surface.}
The fitted four-case table is concentrated in the positive translation and positive protein quadrant.
For $E.\ coli$, the run classifies $0$ coordinates as case $A$, $0$ as case $B$, $0$ as case $C$, and $7$ as case $D$, with $2$ additional positive-positive coordinates that fail the mediation-drop condition.
There are no unclassified coordinates.
For yeast, the run classifies all $9$ coordinates as case $D$, with $0$ in cases $A$, $B$, and $C$, and with no positive-positive coordinate left outside the mediation-drop condition.
There are again no unclassified coordinates.
At the joint coordinate level, the recorded modeled-tAI and abundance survival magnitudes are
$$
\begin{aligned}
E.\ coli &: R^2_{QT}=0.5416736993691318,\quad R^2_{QP}=0.10767526677985896,\\
S.\ cerevisiae &: R^2_{QT}=0.8213457220900503,\quad R^2_{QP}=0.29837624994140827.
\end{aligned}
$$
These values justify a finite readback statement for the modeled-tAI discriminant: the tested organisms show strong residual association of $B^*_{Q6}$ coordinates with the bounded translation proxy and simultaneous association with measured protein abundance, and the yeast block is entirely assigned to the attenuation-positive case.

\paragraph{Relation to direct protein-omics survival.}
The four-case result sits inside a larger direct protein-abundance surface in which all $15$ tested organisms have full residual coordinate rank $9$ after the same $23$-rank control space.
The direct $S^{QP}$ run reports $9$ surviving coordinates in every organism and gives, for example, $R^2_{QP}=0.10767526677985896$ in $E.\ coli$, $0.29837624994140827$ in yeast, $0.04454565118019448$ in human, $0.049827984062539195$ in mouse, and $0.04613529807085213$ in zebrafish.
The protein-abundance join rates in that broader surface are high but not identical: $E.\ coli$ joins $3739$ proteins at hit rate $0.9978649586335735$, yeast joins $5815$ at $0.9156038419146592$, human joins $19053$ at $0.9779294769799313$, mouse joins $19395$ at $0.9808334176190958$, and zebrafish joins $14410$ at $1.0$.
These direct protein-omics positives do not by themselves establish translation mediation.
They only show that the residual coordinates retain predictive mass against measured abundance under the finite control design.
The four-case table is therefore the stricter readback because it asks whether a translation readout participates in the same row-wise signal.

\paragraph{Measured-TE comparison.}
The modeled-tAI discriminant should be read beside the measured ribosome-profiling TE contacts, not substituted for them.
A separate measured-TE run uses $\log_{10}(TE)$ with $TE=RPF/mRNA$ and reports full rank $9$ for four organisms.
The measured $R^2_{QT}$ values are $0.009394266754822262$ for $E.\ coli$, $0.13681931343730436$ for yeast, $0.018837911344127926$ for human, and $0.04034217614654355$ for zebrafish, again with $9$ surviving coordinates in each organism.
The joined measured-TE row counts are $3415$ for $E.\ coli$, $5128$ for yeast, $11554$ for human, and $13140$ for zebrafish, with ribosome-TE join hit rates $0.9133458143888741$, $0.8818572656921754$, $0.6064136881331024$, and $0.9118667591950035$ respectively.
A measured mediation run against PAXdb abundance then gives mediated fractions $0.04091158340950673$ for $E.\ coli$, $0.4793069656345167$ for yeast, $0.0027966485036786744$ for human, and $0.0$ for zebrafish after clipping a negative raw fraction $-0.04801486048721136$.
These numbers preserve the central asymmetry of the evidence: modeled tAI yields a sharp two-organism four-case table, while measured TE gives a condition-specific, smaller, but still finite same-row translation readout.

\paragraph{Joint stability control.}
A further three-organism decomposition conditions the $Q\to P$ signal on measured TE, mRNA stability, and both jointly.
It reports right-tail permutation values $0.000999000999000999$ for $E.\ coli$, human, and yeast under the joint TE--stability pair permutation.
The joint mediation fractions are $0.2404669586626661$ for $E.\ coli$, $0.16890712099923358$ for human, and $0.5195182225621592$ for yeast.
The single-mediator split is organism-specific: $E.\ coli$ has TE-only fraction $0.01975521340963268$ and stability-only fraction $0.23052056324898387$; human has $0.00549490864190343$ and $0.15314679870183337$; yeast has $0.4697188319693239$ and $0.0626618595344251$.
The overlap diagnostic is approximately independent in all three cases by the declared tolerance $0.05$.
This extra control does not replace the four-case table, but it shows that the residual abundance association can be partitioned against measured translation and transcript-stability variables without collapsing to a single universal explanation.

\paragraph{Local verdict.}
The finite claim supported here is narrow.
For the two-organism modeled-tAI four-case surface, $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinates survive both translation-proxy and protein-abundance readouts after the stated controls, and the coordinate-level classification is complete: $7$ of $9$ $E.\ coli$ coordinates and all $9$ yeast coordinates fall into the attenuation-positive class, with the remaining $2$ $E.\ coli$ coordinates explicitly held outside that class.
The result is therefore ready as a bounded BioReality record for a translation-conditioned discriminant over modeled tAI and PAXdb abundance.
It is not ready as a causal mechanism, universal biological law, protein-folding claim, physical-admissibility claim, molecular-function claim, phenotype claim, or proof that modeled tAI is ribosome occupancy.
Those stronger statements would require additional layer-matched contacts and, for mechanism language, perturbational or otherwise temporally identifying evidence rather than the present cross-sectional residual fits.
