\subsection{Translation-conditioned four-case separation for the $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinates}
\label{sec:biorality-q6-translation-conditioned-four-case}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite carrier.}
This record uses a finite matched matrix whose rows are coding-sequence/protein entries and whose columns are the residual $B^*_{Q6}$ coordinates $q_i$, a translation readout $T$, and a protein-omics readout $P$.  The translation readout is the per-protein mean modeled tAI,
$\sum_c \operatorname{codon\_count}(c)\operatorname{tAI\_weight}(c)/\sum_c \operatorname{codon\_count}(c)$, using GtRNAdb all-tRNA copy counts with dos Reis wobble weights.  The protein readout is measured PAXdb $\log_{10}(\operatorname{abundance\_ppm})$ joined to real CDS codon counts.  The finite panel contains two organism-scoped blocks: \emph{Escherichia coli} K12 MG1655 has $3739$ joined CDS/protein rows and $3739$ proteins used, with the tRNA organism recorded as \emph{Escherichia coli}; \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae} has $5815$ joined rows and $5815$ proteins used, with its own tRNA organism.  In both blocks the $B^*_{Q6}$ coordinate count is $9$.

\paragraph{Controlled residualization and rank gate.}
The discriminant is evaluated only after residualizing against the declared control family: amino-acid composition, organism scope, GC3, CDS length, M-density, mRNA-related control where available, and simpler codon baselines.  In the finite run $d74f9f0e39cf$, each organism block used $24$ control columns with control rank $23$.  The residual degrees of freedom were $3716$ for \emph{E. coli} and $5792$ for yeast.  The rank check is non-vacuous in both readout directions: for \emph{E. coli}, $\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde X_T)=9$ and $\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde X_P)=9$; for yeast, $\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde X_T)=9$ and $\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde X_P)=9$.  The recorded degeneracy threshold is $0.999999$, and both organism blocks are marked powered-rank sufficient.  Thus the four-case call is not the rank-saturated $n=3$ all-$1.0$ boundary; it is a finite-row, rank-powered classification over matched rows.

\paragraph{Readout energy and aggregate $R^2$ values.}
The residual translation energy and protein-energy scales are highly different across readouts and organisms, so the signs used below should be read as controlled survival/readout signs rather than as common-scale effect sizes.  For \emph{E. coli}, the translation residual energy is $0.4728468725814029$, the protein residual energy is $4020.333671802061$, the $B^*_{Q6}$ coordinates explain $0.256128714706293$ energy on $T$, and they explain $432.89050065533684$ energy on $P$.  The aggregate controlled coefficients are $R^2_{QT}=0.5416736993691318$ and $R^2_{QP}=0.10767526677985896$ over $9$ coordinates.  For yeast, the translation residual energy is $6.90537888244409$, the protein residual energy is $2958.1865890925337$, the explained energy is $5.671703404506426$ on $T$ and $882.6526210803958$ on $P$, with $R^2_{QT}=0.8213457220900503$ and $R^2_{QP}=0.29837624994140827$ over the same $9$ coordinates.

\paragraph{Four-case discriminant.}
For each matched coordinate/readout cell, let $S^{QT}$ denote the controlled survival sign for $q_i$ against the translation readout, let $S^{QP}$ denote the controlled survival sign for $q_i$ against the protein-omics readout, and let $M^{QTP}$ record whether conditioning on $T$ reduces the direct $q_i$ to $P$ association.  The classification threshold is $10^{-12}$.  The four cases are local to this measured table:
$$
\begin{aligned}
A &: S^{QT}\le 0 \text{ and } S^{QP}\le 0, \\
B &: S^{QT}>0 \text{ and } S^{QP}\le 0, \\
C &: S^{QT}\le 0 \text{ and } S^{QP}>0, \\
D &: S^{QT}>0 \text{ and } S^{QP}>0 \text{ with the direct } q_i\to P \text{ term reduced after conditioning on } T.
\end{aligned}
$$
Case $A$ is compression away from both readouts.  Case $B$ is translation-level irreducibility without a surviving protein-omics propagation call.  Case $C$ is protein-omics prediction not mediated by the measured translation readout.  Case $D$ is the translation-conditioned propagation pattern: the coordinate survives into $T$, survives into $P$, and the direct $q_i\to P$ term is reduced when $T$ is placed in the conditioning set.

\paragraph{Finite classification result.}
Both organism blocks were classified with no unclassified coordinates.  In \emph{E. coli}, the case counts are $A=0$, $B=0$, $C=0$, $D=7$, with $2$ positive-positive coordinates that do not satisfy the mediation-drop condition.  In yeast, the counts are $A=0$, $B=0$, $C=0$, $D=9$, with $0$ positive-positive coordinates lacking the mediation drop.  Therefore the observed finite-row result is not merely a direct $Q$ to $P$ association.  It separates the protein-omics signal by first asking whether the same residual coordinate survives into the translation readout, and then asking whether the protein association is attenuated when that translation readout is included.

\paragraph{Local interpretation.}
The result supports a bounded cross-layer discriminant for this finite matched panel: $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinates can be sorted into explicit $A/B/C/D$ cells after the declared controls and rank checks.  The strongest observed pattern is case $D$, especially in yeast where all $9$ coordinates satisfy the translation-conditioned propagation criterion.  In \emph{E. coli}, $7$ coordinates satisfy the same criterion, while $2$ coordinates remain positive-positive without the required attenuation and therefore are not counted as case $D$.  This distinction is essential: a positive $q_i\to P$ readout is not enough to claim translation-mediated propagation unless the $q_i\to T$ step survives and conditioning on $T$ reduces the direct protein association.

\paragraph{Boundary of the claim.}
The classification is a controlled finite-matrix readback, not a causal mechanism.  The modeled tAI readout is not ribosome profiling, and the joined rows are cross-sectional.  Protein abundance is not isolated from mRNA abundance, turnover, localization, post-translational modification, folding state, or pathway context.  Consequently, this chapter does not infer folded structure, physical admissibility, molecular function, phenotype, perturbation rescue, or a universal biological law.  Those conclusions would require additional layer-specific measurements and their own controlled statistics.  Case $C$, when it occurs in any later panel, must remain a protein-omics residual not mediated by the measured $T$; it is not a translation mechanism by itself.
