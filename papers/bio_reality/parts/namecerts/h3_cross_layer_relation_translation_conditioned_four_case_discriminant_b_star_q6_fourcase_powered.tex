\subsection{Translation-conditioned four-case discriminant for the powered $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ panel}\label{sec:bioreality-q6-four-case-discriminant}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite surface.}
The record tests a finite matrix surface whose rows are coding-sequence entries and whose columns are the nine residual $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ coordinates
$K\_AAA$, $Arg\_AGR$, $Ile\_AUA$, $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$, $Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG$, $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$, $Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG$, $Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY$, and $f3\_stress$.
The rows are joined to a translation readout $T$ and a protein-abundance readout $P$ after the common control surface is removed.
For the exact four-case run, $T$ is a per-protein modeled tAI mean computed as
$\sum_{c} n_c w_c / \sum_c n_c$ from codon counts, GtRNAdb all-tRNA copy counts, and dos Reis wobble weights; $P$ is $\log_{10}(abundance\_ppm)$ from measured PAXdb protein abundance joined to real CDS codon counts.
The classification threshold is $10^{-12}$, so a positive entry means a strictly positive controlled residual association above that numerical boundary, not a qualitative biological mechanism.

\paragraph{Rank and row support.}
Two organism surfaces were classified in the modeled-tAI four-case run.
For $E.\ coli$ K12 MG1655, $3739$ CDS/protein rows were loaded, with $3739$ proteins used and the tRNA organism key $escherichia\_coli$.
After residualization, the control design had $24$ columns, $rank(Z)=23$, residual degrees of freedom $3716$, and both $rank(\widetilde X_P)$ and $rank(\widetilde X_T)$ equal to $9$.
The protein residual energy was $4020.333671802061$, the modeled-tAI residual energy was $0.4728468725814029$, the $Q$-explained energy on $P$ was $432.89050065533684$, and the $Q$-explained energy on $T$ was $0.256128714706293$.
For $S.\ cerevisiae$, $5815$ CDS/protein rows were loaded and used, the control design again had $24$ columns with $rank(Z)=23$, residual degrees of freedom $5792$, and both residual $Q$ ranks equal to $9$.
Its protein residual energy was $2958.1865890925337$, its modeled-tAI residual energy was $6.90537888244409$, the $Q$-explained energy on $P$ was $882.6526210803958$, and the $Q$-explained energy on $T$ was $5.671703404506426$.
The degenerate-fit cutoff was $0.999999$, and both organisms passed the powered-rank sufficiency check.

\paragraph{Four-case rule.}
Each coordinate is assigned by the signs of $S^{QT}$ and $S^{QP}$ and by whether the direct $Q \to P$ signal is reduced after conditioning on the translation readout.
The local readback can be written as
$$
\begin{aligned}
A &: S^{QT} \leq 0,\quad S^{QP} \leq 0,\\
B &: S^{QT} > 0,\quad S^{QP} \leq 0,\\
C &: S^{QT} \leq 0,\quad S^{QP} > 0,\\
D &: S^{QT} > 0,\quad S^{QP} > 0,\quad Q \to P\ \hbox{is reduced after conditioning on }T.
\end{aligned}
$$
A coordinate with positive $S^{QT}$ and positive $S^{QP}$ but no mediation drop is kept separate from $D$; it is a direct or unresolved protein-omics association rather than a translation-conditioned propagation call.
This is why the run reports a fifth bookkeeping count, $positive\_positive\_without\_mediation\_drop$, in addition to the four named cases.

\paragraph{Observed classifications.}
The modeled-tAI discriminant is fully classified for the two powered organisms and leaves no unclassified coordinate.
For $E.\ coli$ K12 MG1655, the case vector is
$$
\begin{aligned}
A&=0, & B&=0, & C&=0, & D&=7,
\end{aligned}
$$
with $2$ positive-positive coordinates lacking a mediation drop.
The total controlled associations are $R^2_{QP}=0.10767526677985896$ and $R^2_{QT}=0.5416736993691318$ across the nine-coordinate residual design.
For $S.\ cerevisiae$, the case vector is
$$
\begin{aligned}
A&=0, & B&=0, & C&=0, & D&=9,
\end{aligned}
$$
with no positive-positive coordinate left outside the mediation-drop class.
The corresponding totals are $R^2_{QP}=0.29837624994140827$ and $R^2_{QT}=0.8213457220900503$.
Thus the finite-row result is not merely a protein-abundance association: in this bounded modeled-tAI readout, all yeast coordinates and seven of nine $E.\ coli$ coordinates enter the translation-conditioned $D$ class, while the remaining two $E.\ coli$ coordinates are explicitly retained as unresolved positive-positive entries.

\paragraph{Relation to measured translation rows.}
The modeled-tAI result should be read beside the measured ribosome-TE surface, because the latter supplies an observed translation-efficiency readout rather than a tRNA-copy model.
The measured TE run used the same nine-coordinate source and reported $R^2_{QT,measured}=0.009394266754822262$ for $E.\ coli$ with $3415$ genes used, $R^2_{QT,measured}=0.13681931343730436$ for yeast with $5128$ genes used, $R^2_{QT,measured}=0.018837911344127926$ for human with $11554$ genes used, and $R^2_{QT,measured}=0.04034217614654355$ for zebrafish with $13140$ genes used.
The measured mediation run also reports nonnegative clamped mediated fractions of $0.04091158340950673$ for $E.\ coli$, $0.4793069656345167$ for yeast, $0.0027966485036786744$ for human, and $0.0$ for zebrafish, where the zebrafish raw fraction was suppressive at $-0.04801486048721136$.
These measured-TE numbers do not replace the exact modeled-tAI four-case table above; they show that the same coordinate family also survives against ribosome-derived TE readouts in joined finite rows, with weaker and more condition-specific mediation behavior.

\paragraph{Protein-abundance boundary.}
Direct $Q \to P$ survival is broad in the PAXdb protein-abundance surface: all $9$ coordinates survive in each of the $15$ organisms in the direct protein-omics run.
Representative controlled $R^2_{QP}$ values are $0.10767526677985896$ for $E.\ coli$, $0.29837624994140827$ for yeast, $0.04454565118019448$ for human, $0.049827984062539195$ for mouse, $0.10217906758013379$ for $C.\ elegans$, and $0.019256877907572044$ for $Halobacterium\ salinarum$.
This broad protein-abundance survival is not by itself a translation mechanism.
The four-case table is needed precisely because a direct $Q \to P$ association can remain direct, confounded, mediated by unmeasured variables, or only partly absorbed by translation and transcript-side covariates.
The joint TE/stability decomposition makes this point concrete: in yeast, $R^2_{QP}=0.2715520808117803$ drops to $R^2_{QP\mid T,M}=0.13047582645538838$, giving a joint mediation fraction $0.5195182225621592$; in $E.\ coli$, $R^2_{QP}=0.1181743523064932$ drops to $0.08975732521542036$, giving $0.2404669586626661$; in human, $R^2_{QP}=0.04274178488511215$ drops to $0.0355223930537993$, giving $0.16890712099923358$.
The right-tail permutation value was $0.000999000999000999$ in each of those three organism decompositions, but the result remains a variance-partition statistic across matched observational readouts.

\paragraph{Local cannot-claim boundary.}
The finite classification licenses only the following statement: after the declared controls, the nine-coordinate $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual surface has positive modeled-translation and protein-abundance readbacks in the powered two-organism table, with seven $E.\ coli$ coordinates and nine yeast coordinates assigned to the translation-conditioned $D$ class under the modeled-tAI rule.
It does not imply observed ribosome occupancy for the modeled-tAI entries, causal translation control, folded structure, physical admissibility, molecular function, system phenotype, evolutionary direction, perturbation rescue, or a universal biological law.
A stronger biological interpretation would require a separate layer-matched contact and a fresh finite statistic at that layer: observed ribosome profiling for translation dynamics, perturbation or rescue for mechanism, structure or stability measurements for physical state, and functional or phenotype assays for downstream biological action.
The direct protein-omics surface is therefore kept as prediction and variance accounting unless the matched translation readout survives and absorbs the corresponding $Q \to P$ signal in the same controlled row system.
