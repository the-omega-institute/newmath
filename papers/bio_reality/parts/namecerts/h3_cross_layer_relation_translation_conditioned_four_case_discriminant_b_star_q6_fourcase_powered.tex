\subsection{Translation-conditioned four-case readback for $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates}
\label{sec:bioreality-q6-translation-conditioned-four-case}

\paragraph{Finite carrier and readouts.}
This chapter records a finite cross-layer test over matched coding-sequence, modeled translation, transcript-control, and protein-abundance rows.  The carrier is the table of $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates $q_i$, modeled translation readouts $T_j$, and protein-omics readouts $P_k$ after residualization against amino-acid composition, organism scope, GC3, coding-sequence length, $M$-density, matched mRNA abundance, and simpler codon-baseline controls.  The translation readout used here is not ribosome profiling: it is per-protein mean modeled tAI,
$$
\begin{aligned}
T = \frac{\sum_{c} \operatorname{count}(c)\operatorname{tAI}(c)}{\sum_{c} \operatorname{count}(c)},
\end{aligned}
$$
where the weights are derived from organism-scoped tRNA gene-copy counts and dos Reis wobble weights.  The protein readout is measured PAXdb abundance, represented as $\log_{10}(\operatorname{abundance\_ppm})$, joined to real coding-sequence codon counts.  Thus the finite object tested here is a controlled same-row matrix over sequence-derived coordinates, a bounded modeled translation proxy, matched mRNA control, and measured steady-state protein abundance.

\paragraph{Rank gate.}
Positive classification is only admitted after the residualized coordinate matrix has full $q$-rank for both the translation and protein regressions.  In the Escherichia coli K12 MG1655 panel, $3739$ joined proteins were used.  The control matrix had $24$ columns with $\operatorname{rank}(Z)=23$, the residual degrees of freedom after controls were $3716$, and the residualized $B^{*}_{Q6}$ design had $9$ coordinates with
$$
\begin{aligned}
\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde X_T) &= 9,\\
\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde X_P) &= 9.
\end{aligned}
$$
For Saccharomyces cerevisiae, $5815$ joined proteins were used.  The control matrix again had $24$ columns with $\operatorname{rank}(Z)=23$, the residual degrees of freedom after controls were $5792$, and the same rank condition held:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde X_T) &= 9,\\
\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde X_P) &= 9.
\end{aligned}
$$
Both organisms therefore pass the finite rank-admissibility test for the nine residual coordinates.  The degeneracy guard was $R^2<0.999999$, and both organism panels were marked rank sufficient before the four-case labels were read.

\paragraph{Controlled energies and aggregate association surfaces.}
After residualization, the Escherichia coli panel retained translation residual energy $0.4728468725814029$ and protein-abundance residual energy $4020.333671802061$.  The $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual design explained energy $0.256128714706293$ on $T$ and $432.89050065533684$ on $P$.  The aggregate controlled surfaces were
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{QT} &= 0.5416736993691318,\\
R^2_{QP} &= 0.10767526677985896.
\end{aligned}
$$
For Saccharomyces cerevisiae, the retained translation residual energy was $6.90537888244409$ and the protein-abundance residual energy was $2958.1865890925337$.  The $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual design explained energy $5.671703404506426$ on $T$ and $882.6526210803958$ on $P$, giving
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{QT} &= 0.8213457220900503,\\
R^2_{QP} &= 0.29837624994140827.
\end{aligned}
$$
These numbers say that the residual coordinates retain controlled association with the modeled translation readout and with measured protein abundance in both panels.  They do not by themselves identify a causal pathway; they only justify applying the coordinate-level sign and mediation-discriminant rule on a rank-powered matrix.

\paragraph{Four-case rule.}
For each matched coordinate/readout cell, the classification uses the signs of the controlled $Q\to T$ and $Q\to P$ surfaces, followed by a translation-conditioning check when both signs are positive.  With classification threshold $10^{-12}$, the four labels are
$$
\begin{aligned}
A &: S^{QT}\leq 0 \text{ and } S^{QP}\leq 0,\\
B &: S^{QT}>0 \text{ and } S^{QP}\leq 0,\\
C &: S^{QT}\leq 0 \text{ and } S^{QP}>0,\\
D &: S^{QT}>0 \text{ and } S^{QP}>0 \text{ with reduced direct } Q\to P \text{ after conditioning on } T.
\end{aligned}
$$
Case $A$ is compression of the residual coordinate away from both readouts.  Case $B$ is a translation-level residual signal without propagation into the measured protein-abundance readout.  Case $C$ is a protein-omics residual prediction not mediated by the measured modeled-translation readout.  Case $D$ is the only positive-positive class in which the conditioning test supports a translation-mediated attenuation pattern.  The word ``mediated'' here names this finite regression relation only; it is not a perturbational or biochemical mechanism claim.

\paragraph{Observed case inventory.}
The Escherichia coli K12 MG1655 finite table classified all nine coordinates with no unclassified entries.  The case counts were
$$
\begin{aligned}
A&=0, & B&=0, & C&=0, & D&=7,
\end{aligned}
$$
with $2$ additional positive-positive coordinates that did not pass the mediation-drop condition and therefore were not promoted to case $D$.  Thus the Escherichia coli readback is not a blanket nine-coordinate propagation call; it is a seven-coordinate case-$D$ call plus two positive-positive non-mediation-drop cells held outside the propagated class.

For Saccharomyces cerevisiae, the finite table also classified all nine coordinates with no unclassified entries.  The case counts were
$$
\begin{aligned}
A&=0, & B&=0, & C&=0, & D&=9,
\end{aligned}
$$
with $0$ positive-positive coordinates lacking the mediation drop.  On this panel the nine tested $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates all fall into the translation-conditioned propagation class relative to the modeled tAI readout and measured protein abundance.

\paragraph{Local interpretation.}
The finite-row conclusion is a two-organism, rank-powered, controlled discriminant: under the stated controls, $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates survive the modeled-translation and protein-abundance readbacks strongly enough to assign explicit four-case labels.  Escherichia coli supplies $7$ case-$D$ cells out of $9$ coordinates, while Saccharomyces cerevisiae supplies $9$ case-$D$ cells out of $9$ coordinates.  The aggregate $R^2_{QT}$ values, $0.5416736993691318$ and $0.8213457220900503$, show that the modeled translation readout is not rank-degenerate noise in either organism; the aggregate $R^2_{QP}$ values, $0.10767526677985896$ and $0.29837624994140827$, show a controlled protein-abundance association that is weaker than the translation surface but still finite and classified.

\paragraph{No-promotion rule.}
This chapter does not establish observed ribosome occupancy, translation causality, folding behavior, physical admissibility, molecular function, organismal phenotype, perturbation rescue, or universality.  The translation readout is modeled tAI, not ribosome profiling.  Protein abundance is steady-state abundance and is not isolated from mRNA abundance, turnover, localization, post-translational modification, folding, or pathway context.  A direct $Q\to P$ association is not accepted as translation-mediated propagation unless the $Q\to T$ step survives and conditioning on $T$ reduces the direct $Q\to P$ relation.  Case $C$, if it appears in later panels, must be read only as protein-omics prediction not mediated by the measured translation readout.  Any realized translation process, folding claim, physical-admissibility statement, function claim, mechanism claim, phenotype claim, or global biological law requires a separate layer-appropriate measurement and its own controlled statistic.
