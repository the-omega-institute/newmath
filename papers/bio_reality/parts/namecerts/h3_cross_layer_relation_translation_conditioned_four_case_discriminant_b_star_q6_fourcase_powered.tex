\subsection{Translation-conditioned four-case readback for the $B^*_{Q6}$ surface}
\label{sec:bioreality-q6-fourcase-readback}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite carrier.}
The record considered here is a finite matrix surface whose rows are coding-sequence units and whose columns are nine residual $B^*_{Q6}$ coordinates.  The active coordinates are $K\_AAA$, $Arg\_AGR$, $Ile\_AUA$, $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$, $Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG$, $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$, $Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG$, $Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY$, and $f3\_stress$.  Each coordinate is residualized against the same control family: intercept, coding length, amino-acid composition, $GC3$, and the $M$-density baseline induced by the union support of the nine projected coordinates.  The four-case readback uses two matched outputs on the same coding-sequence rows: a translation-side score $T$ and a protein-abundance score $P$.  In this finite run $T$ is the per-protein mean modeled tAI, computed as $\sum_c n_c w_c / \sum_c n_c$ from codon counts, GtRNAdb all-tRNA copy counts, and dos Reis wobble weights; $P$ is $\log_{10}$ PAXdb abundance in ppm joined to real CDS codon counts.  The case assignment is therefore a readout classification over bounded modeled-translation and measured-abundance columns, not a biochemical mechanism.

\paragraph{Rank and row support.}
Two organisms enter the four-case matrix.  For \emph{Escherichia coli} K-12 MG1655, the run joins $3739$ CDS/protein rows and uses the \emph{E. coli} tRNA organism table.  For \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}, the run joins $5815$ CDS/protein rows and uses the matched yeast tRNA table.  The residualized control rank is $23$ in both organisms from $24$ control columns.  The residual $Q$ design has rank $9$ against $P$ and rank $9$ against $T$ in both organisms, so the nine-coordinate surface is not rank-collapsed at the tested boundary.  The post-control residual degrees of freedom are $3716$ for \emph{E. coli} and $5792$ for yeast.  The residual energies also make the finite nature of the readback explicit:
$$
\begin{aligned}
E_{P,\mathrm{coli}} &= 4020.333671802061, & E_{T,\mathrm{coli}} &= 0.4728468725814029, \\
E_{P,\mathrm{yeast}} &= 2958.1865890925337, & E_{T,\mathrm{yeast}} &= 6.90537888244409.
\end{aligned}
$$
The energy explained by the residual $Q$ surface is $432.89050065533684$ on $P$ and $0.256128714706293$ on $T$ for \emph{E. coli}; for yeast it is $882.6526210803958$ on $P$ and $5.671703404506426$ on $T$.  The classification threshold is $10^{-12}$, and both organisms are marked powered-rank sufficient.

\paragraph{Observed four-case output.}
The four labels separate translation-null coordinates, protein-omics-null coordinates, direct protein-omics coordinates, and translation-conditioned coordinates.  In the finite readback, no coordinate lands in case $A$, $B$, or $C$ for either organism.  \emph{E. coli} assigns $7$ of $9$ coordinates to case $D$ and records $2$ positive-positive coordinates without the required mediation drop.  Yeast assigns all $9$ coordinates to case $D$ and has no positive-positive coordinate lacking the mediation drop.  There are no unclassified coordinates in either organism.  At the organism level, the residual survival summaries are
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{QT,\mathrm{coli}} &= 0.5416736993691318, & R^2_{QP,\mathrm{coli}} &= 0.10767526677985896, \\
R^2_{QT,\mathrm{yeast}} &= 0.8213457220900503, & R^2_{QP,\mathrm{yeast}} &= 0.29837624994140827.
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the finite surface supports a strong modeled-tAI association and a direct protein-abundance association in both tested organisms.  The case-$D$ call is narrower: it says that, under this modeled translation readout, the corresponding direct $q_i \to P$ signal is reduced by the translation-side conditioning rule used by the classifier.  It does not say that ribosomes, elongation, folding, localization, turnover, or function have been causally identified.

\paragraph{Measured-translation comparison.}
A separate measured-TE run replaces modeled tAI with ribosome-profiling translation efficiency, where $TE=RPF/mRNA$ in the local source files.  That run covers four organisms and keeps the same nine residual coordinates.  The measured translation-side survival values are $R^2_{QT,meas}=0.009394266754822262$ for \emph{E. coli} on $3415$ joined TE genes, $0.13681931343730436$ for yeast on $5128$ joined TE genes, $0.018837911344127926$ for human on $11554$ joined TE genes, and $0.04034217614654355$ for zebrafish on $13140$ joined TE genes.  These numbers are compatible with a real translation-layer signal, but they are condition-specific observational readouts.  They do not convert the modeled-tAI four-case table into a universal ribosome law.

\paragraph{Protein-abundance comparison.}
The direct protein-omics surface was also computed across a broader $15$-organism panel, again with nine residual coordinates and the same control family.  The PAXdb joins include $4042$ rows for \emph{Bacillus subtilis}, $11661$ for \emph{Caenorhabditis elegans}, $14410$ for zebrafish, $6974$ for \emph{Dictyostelium discoideum}, $13295$ for \emph{Drosophila melanogaster}, $3739$ for \emph{E. coli}, $12418$ for chicken, $1747$ for \emph{Halobacterium salinarum}, $19053$ for human, $19395$ for mouse, $4129$ for \emph{Mycobacterium smegmatis}, $5006$ for \emph{Pseudomonas aeruginosa}, $13322$ for rat, $5815$ for yeast, and $1656$ for \emph{Sulfolobus solfataricus}.  The direct $R^2_{QP}$ values range from $0.019256877907572044$ in \emph{Halobacterium} to $0.29837624994140827$ in yeast, with all nine coordinates surviving at the positive threshold in every listed organism.  This broader result justifies treating $q_i \to P$ as a real finite protein-omics association, while still leaving mechanism open unless a translation readout absorbs the direct signal.

\paragraph{Mediation and reduction controls.}
Measured-TE mediation supplies the closest independent check on the four-case interpretation.  In the four-way measured run, the mediated fractions are $0.04091158340950673$ for \emph{E. coli}, $0.4793069656345167$ for yeast, $0.0027966485036786744$ for human, and $0.0$ for zebrafish after clipping a raw suppressive value of $-0.04801486048721136$.  The same measured run reports $S_{QP}=0.10941535951828074$ and $S_{QT,meas}=0.009394266754822262$ for \emph{E. coli}; $S_{QP}=0.3146818503437042$ and $S_{QT,meas}=0.13681931343730436$ for yeast; $S_{QP}=0.04288464996165745$ and $S_{QT,meas}=0.018837911344127926$ for human; and $S_{QP}=0.04926578454614652$ and $S_{QT,meas}=0.04034217614654355$ for zebrafish.  A joint TE-plus-mRNA-stability decomposition gives joint mediation fractions $0.2404669586626661$ in \emph{E. coli}, $0.16890712099923358$ in human, and $0.5195182225621592$ in yeast, each with right-tail permutation value $0.000999000999000999$.  A separate partial-mediation certificate on the yeast context uses $\lambda_{DL}=0.01$, $\rho_{join}=0.05$, $1800$ joined rows, $5$ folds, and $120$ permutations; its selected readout dictionary covers $0.24538211439730545$ of the target and leaves $0.7546178856026946$ unexplained, above a selection-aware null $95\%$ value of $0.029149007242956115$.  These finite reductions show that the abundance signal is partly carried by measured readouts, but not exhausted by them.

\paragraph{Layer boundary.}
The permitted conclusion is a four-case finite-row classification for the $B^*_{Q6}$ residual surface under modeled-tAI translation readout and measured PAXdb abundance, with supporting measured-TE and reduction checks.  No stronger biological layer follows automatically.  A positive $S^{QT}$, a positive $S^{QP}$, or a case-$D$ assignment does not by itself establish observed translation dynamics, folded structure, physical admissibility, molecular function, organismal phenotype, causal mechanism, evolutionary direction, or a universal biological rule.  Those claims require their own matched contacts and their own controlled statistics.  The f3-stress dwell records illustrate the same discipline: yeast dwell mediation on $1257$ joined rows gives held-out $\Delta R^2=0.01856473944057712$ against a permutation null $95\%$ value of $0.0013115362189692937$ and $p\approx 7.873916057196935\times 10^{-9}$ for the f3-dwell correlation, while the positional record identifies the $5'$ segment as leading.  Even there, a structural-order ramp test concludes only $\text{ramp\_positive\_but\_not\_localized}$, with weighted ramp $\Delta R^2=0.0011165640103326878$, body $\Delta R^2=-0.00026157313923957926$, and paired ramp-minus-body sign-flip value $0.05859375$.  The local rule is therefore conservative: finite translation-conditioned and protein-omics associations may be reported, but translation mechanism, folding, physical admissibility, function, and universality remain unclaimed without layer-specific evidence.
