\subsection{Translation-conditioned four-case discriminant for the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual surface}
\label{sec:bioreality-q6-four-case-discriminant}

\paragraph{Finite-row object.}
This chapter records a finite discriminant for the nine-dimensional $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ synonymous residual surface.  The coordinates are $K\_AAA$, $Arg\_AGR$, $Ile\_AUA$, $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$, $Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG$, $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$, $Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG$, $Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY$, and $f3\_stress$.  Each row is a coding sequence with codon counts, a matched protein-abundance readout $P$, and a matched translation-style readout $T$.  In the discriminant run, $P$ is $\log_{10}$ PAXdb abundance in ppm, and $T$ is the per-protein mean modeled tAI, computed as codon-count-weighted tAI using GtRNAdb all-tRNA copy counts and dos Reis wobble weights.  The row joins are organism-specific: $3739$ Escherichia coli K12 MG1655 CDS/protein rows with the Escherichia coli tRNA source, and $5815$ Saccharomyces cerevisiae CDS/protein rows with the Saccharomyces cerevisiae tRNA source.

\paragraph{Residualized design.}
For each organism the readouts and the nine $Q$ coordinates are residualized against the same covariate family: intercept, CDS length, amino-acid composition, GC3, and an $M$-density baseline.  The control design has $24$ columns and rank $23$ in both organisms.  The residual $Q$ design keeps rank $9$ for both the protein-abundance fit and the tAI fit, so the classification is not a rank-degenerate all-one surface.  The residual degrees of freedom after controls are $3716$ in Escherichia coli and $5792$ in yeast.  The residual protein energies are $4020.333671802061$ and $2958.1865890925337$, while the residual tAI energies are $0.4728468725814029$ and $6.90537888244409$.  The explained energies by the $Q$ block are $432.89050065533684$ on $P$ and $0.256128714706293$ on $T$ in Escherichia coli, and $882.6526210803958$ on $P$ and $5.671703404506426$ on $T$ in yeast.

\paragraph{Four-case rule.}
The discriminant assigns each coordinate by the signs of $S^{QT}$ and $S^{QP}$ together with whether conditioning on the measured or explicitly bounded translation readout attenuates the direct $Q \to P$ association.  The numerical threshold used for positive classification is $10^{-12}$.  The aggregate readbacks are
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{QP}(\mathrm{E.coli}) &= 0.10767526677985896, & R^2_{QT}(\mathrm{E.coli}) &= 0.5416736993691318,\\
R^2_{QP}(\mathrm{yeast}) &= 0.29837624994140827, & R^2_{QT}(\mathrm{yeast}) &= 0.8213457220900503.
\end{aligned}
$$
The finite classification is therefore completed for all coordinates in both organisms.  Escherichia coli has no coordinates in cases A, B, or C, seven coordinates in case D, and two additional positive-positive coordinates without a mediation drop.  Yeast has no coordinates in cases A, B, or C and all nine coordinates in case D.  No coordinate is left unclassified in either organism.

\paragraph{Interpretation of the cases.}
Case D is the only local label in this run that supports the translation-conditioned reading: the coordinate survives against the modeled translation readout and the direct protein-abundance association is reduced after conditioning on that translation readout.  The two Escherichia coli positive-positive coordinates without a mediation drop are deliberately not promoted to the same status; they are direct or confounded protein-omics associations under this finite panel.  The point of the four-case surface is thus not to claim that every $Q \to P$ association is translational.  It separates translation-surviving signal from protein-omics survival that remains direct, non-mediated, or unresolved by the chosen $T$ readout.

\paragraph{Measured-readout comparison.}
The modeled-tAI discriminant is bounded by separate measured-TE evidence.  In the measured ribosome-TE survival run, the same nine-coordinate $Q$ block survived against $\log_{10}(TE)$ in four organisms, with $R^2_{QT,meas}=0.009394266754822262$ in Escherichia coli, $0.13681931343730436$ in yeast, $0.018837911344127926$ in human, and $0.04034217614654355$ in zebrafish; each organism retained $9$ surviving coordinates.  The corresponding measured mediation fractions for protein abundance are smaller and organism-specific: $0.04091158340950673$ in Escherichia coli, $0.4793069656345167$ in yeast, $0.0027966485036786744$ in human, and $0.0$ in zebrafish after clipping a negative raw fraction of $-0.04801486048721136$.  These numbers keep the present chapter honest: the modeled-tAI four-case readout is a finite discriminant, while ribosome-derived TE supplies a distinct measured translation surface.

\paragraph{Protein-omics and readout dictionary boundaries.}
The direct protein-abundance surface is strong but not by itself mechanistic.  In the direct PAXdb run, all $15$ organism panels were powered for rank and all $9$ coordinates survived in each organism; the direct $R^2_{QP}$ values ranged from $0.019256877907572044$ in Halobacterium salinarum to $0.29837624994140827$ in yeast, with examples $0.10767526677985896$ in Escherichia coli, $0.04454565118019448$ in human, and $0.049827984062539195$ in mouse.  A separate yeast dictionary reduction, using $\lambda_{DL}=0.01$, $\rho_{join}=0.05$, $n=1800$, five folds, and $120$ permutations, obtained coverage $C^{\ast}=0.24538211439730545$ with a selection-aware null $95$ percentile of $0.029149007242956115$, leaving unexplained fraction $0.7546178856026946$.  The registered irreducibility-style test on the same yeast scale used $\lambda_{DL}=0.01$, $\rho_{join}=0.05$, $n=1800$, five folds, and $120$ permutations, and its current status is a reduction-family refutation of the strongest irreducibility reading rather than a certificate of a universal primitive.

\paragraph{Additional layer checks.}
The joint TE and mRNA-stability decomposition also supports a cautious reading.  Joint mediation fractions are $0.2404669586626661$ in Escherichia coli, $0.16890712099923358$ in human, and $0.5195182225621592$ in yeast, each with right-tail permutation value $0.000999000999000999$.  The single-mediator pieces are not identical across organisms: yeast is TE-dominant with TE-only fraction $0.4697188319693239$ and stability-only fraction $0.0626618595344251$, while human is stability-heavy with $0.00549490864190343$ TE-only and $0.15314679870183337$ stability-only.  A yeast $f3\_stress$ dwell analysis gives a further local example: with $n=1257$, five folds, and $120$ deterministic row-label permutations, the held-out $\Delta R^2$ is $0.01856473944057712$ against a null $95$ percentile of $0.0013115362189692937$, with $f3$-dwell correlation $0.1615441450901653$ and approximate $p=7.873916057196935\times 10^{-9}$.  These auxiliary surfaces are compatible with partial mediation, not with a single universal explanation.

\paragraph{Local no-promotion boundary.}
The chapter does not assert translation causality, folded structural order, physical admissibility, molecular function, phenotype realization, perturbation rescue, or universality.  Modeled tAI is not ribosome profiling.  PAXdb abundance is a steady-state protein-omics readout, not a direct translation-rate measurement.  A positive $Q \to P$ association is not a mechanism unless a layer-matched $Q \to T$ step survives and conditioning on $T$ removes or reduces the direct $Q \to P$ signal on the same finite row surface.  Any stronger biological conclusion must be supplied by a separate matched contact at the relevant layer, with its own controls and rank diagnostics.
