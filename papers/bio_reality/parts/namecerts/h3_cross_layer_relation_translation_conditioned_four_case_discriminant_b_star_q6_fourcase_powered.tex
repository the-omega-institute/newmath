\subsection{Translation-conditioned four-case discriminant for the powered $B^{*}_{Q6}$ surface}
\label{sec:h3-cross-layer-relation-translation-conditioned-four-case-discriminant-b-star-q6-fourcase-powered}

\paragraph{Finite surface.}
The tested object is a finite row-matched surface whose rows are coding sequences and whose columns are the nine residual $B^{*}_{Q6}$ coordinates
$K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $Leu_{CUN\,vs\,UUR}$, $Leu_{UUA\,vs\,UUG}$, $Ser_{UCR\,vs\,AGY}$, $Ser_{UCA\,vs\,UCG}$, $Thr_{ACR\,vs\,ACY}$, and $f3_{stress}$.  The readout pair used for the four-case call is deliberately narrow.  The translation-side column $T$ is not ribosome profiling: it is the per-protein mean modeled tAI,
$$
\begin{aligned}
T(g) &= \frac{\sum_{c} n_{g,c} w_c}{\sum_c n_{g,c}},
\end{aligned}
$$
with $w_c$ supplied by all-tRNA gene-copy counts and dos Reis wobble weights.  The protein-side column $P$ is $\log_{10}$ PAXdb abundance in ppm, joined to real CDS codon counts.  Both $Q$, $T$, and $P$ are read after residualization against the same covariate family: intercept, CDS length, amino-acid composition, GC3, and the $M$-density baseline.  The classification threshold is $10^{-12}$, so positive entries are numerical finite-row events rather than informal labels.

\paragraph{Rows, rank, and residual energy.}
Two organisms have a complete modeled-tAI and protein-abundance join for this discriminant.  In \textit{Escherichia coli} K-12 MG1655, the calculation uses $3739$ joined CDS/protein rows; the corresponding protein-abundance contact reports a CDS join hit rate of $0.9978649586335735$.  After the $Z$ controls, $rank(Z)=23$, the $Q$ matrix has rank $9$ for both the $P$ and $T$ regressions, and the residual degrees of freedom are $3716$.  The $Q$ block explains $432.89050065533684$ units of residual protein-abundance energy and $0.256128714706293$ units of modeled-tAI energy, with residual energies $4020.333671802061$ for $P$ and $0.4728468725814029$ for $T$.  In \textit{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}, the calculation uses $5815$ joined rows; the protein-abundance contact reports a CDS join hit rate of $0.9156038419146592$.  Here $rank(Z)=23$, the two residualized $Q$ designs again have rank $9$, the residual degrees of freedom are $5792$, and the $Q$ block explains $882.6526210803958$ units of residual protein-abundance energy and $5.671703404506426$ units of modeled-tAI energy.  The corresponding residual energies are $2958.1865890925337$ for $P$ and $6.90537888244409$ for $T$.  Thus the call is not an $n=3$ rank-saturation artifact: each classified organism has thousands of CDS rows and a full-rank nine-coordinate residual surface.

\paragraph{Four-case rule.}
For each coordinate $q_i$, the finite readback distinguishes four outcomes.  Case A means no positive $q_i \to T$ survival and no positive $q_i \to P$ survival.  Case B means a positive translation-side entry without a positive protein-side entry.  Case C means a positive direct protein-side entry without a positive translation-side entry.  Case D means both $q_i \to T$ and $q_i \to P$ are positive and the protein-side association is reduced by conditioning on the matched translation readout.  A separate counter is kept for positive-positive coordinates that do not show the required reduction; such entries are not promoted to a translation-mediated call.  In aggregate form,
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{QT}(E.\ coli) &= 0.5416736993691318, & R^2_{QP}(E.\ coli) &= 0.10767526677985896,\\
R^2_{QT}(S.\ cerevisiae) &= 0.8213457220900503, & R^2_{QP}(S.\ cerevisiae) &= 0.29837624994140827.
\end{aligned}
$$
These values show that both organisms carry strong modeled-tAI structure and a positive direct protein-abundance association on the residual $B^{*}_{Q6}$ surface.

\paragraph{Classification result.}
The yeast surface is cleanly classified: all nine coordinates fall in case D, with no unclassified coordinate and no positive-positive coordinate failing the mediation-drop condition.  The \textit{E. coli} surface is mixed but still finite and explicit: seven coordinates fall in case D, no coordinate falls in A, B, or C, and two coordinates are positive on both $S^{QT}$ and $S^{QP}$ while failing the mediation-drop requirement.  No coordinate is unclassified in either organism.  The resulting case-count table is therefore
$$
\begin{aligned}
E.\ coli &: A=0,\ B=0,\ C=0,\ D=7,\ PP_{no\ drop}=2,\\
S.\ cerevisiae &: A=0,\ B=0,\ C=0,\ D=9,\ PP_{no\ drop}=0.
\end{aligned}
$$
The discriminant is consequently ready as a finite classification of the modeled-tAI/protein-abundance join.  It supports the statement that, on these two powered joins, most or all residual $B^{*}_{Q6}$ coordinates that survive directly against abundance also survive against the modeled tAI readout, and that the yeast instance satisfies the stricter translation-conditioned attenuation criterion for every coordinate.

\paragraph{Relation to measured translation evidence.}
The modeled-tAI result is not the same as ribosome profiling.  The measured-TE run supplies a separate finite check: $R^2_{QT,meas}=0.009394266754822262$ in \textit{E. coli}, $0.13681931343730436$ in yeast, $0.018837911344127926$ in human, and $0.04034217614654355$ in zebrafish, each with nine surviving coordinates after the same style of controls.  The measured protein-abundance mediation run gives mediated fractions $0.04091158340950673$ for \textit{E. coli}, $0.4793069656345167$ for yeast, $0.0027966485036786744$ for human, and $0.0$ for zebrafish after truncating a negative raw fraction.  A wider TE-plus-mRNA-stability partition gives joint mediation fractions $0.2404669586626661$ for \textit{E. coli}, $0.5195182225621592$ for yeast, and $0.16890712099923358$ for human, with right-tail permutation values $0.000999000999000999$ in all three organisms.  These companion rows make the boundary sharper: modeled tAI gives the two-organism four-case table above, while measured TE and stability readouts provide separate non-identical contacts whose numbers should not be collapsed into the modeled-tAI classification.

\paragraph{Local no-promotion rule.}
The chapter makes no claim that the residual coordinates themselves prove translation, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, biological mechanism, phenotype, or universality.  The $T$ column in the four-case table is a modeled tAI average; it is not observed ribosome occupancy.  The $P$ column is steady-state protein abundance; it is not isolated from mRNA abundance, turnover, localization, post-translational modification, folding state, pathway context, or experimental condition.  Case D is therefore a controlled finite-row attenuation label, not a causal pathway.  Case C, if it appears in a later organism or readout, should be read only as direct protein-omics prediction unless a matched translation readout survives and conditioning on that readout reduces the direct $q_i \to P$ signal.  Any statement about real translation dynamics requires measured RPF/mRNA or an equivalent translation contact; any statement about folding, physical feasibility, function, mechanism, or a general biological law requires a separate layer-matched observation and its own controlled statistic.
