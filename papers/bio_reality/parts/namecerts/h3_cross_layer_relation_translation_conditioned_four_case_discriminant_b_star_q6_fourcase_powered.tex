\subsection{Translation-conditioned four-case discriminant for $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$}
\label{sec:bq6-translation-conditioned-four-case-discriminant}

\paragraph{Finite row surface.}
The discriminant is evaluated on a finite matrix whose rows are coding sequences with matched codon counts, a bounded translation-layer readout, and measured protein abundance.  The source coordinates are the nine $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates $K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $Leu_{CUN\,vs\,UUR}$, $Leu_{UUA\,vs\,UUG}$, $Ser_{UCR\,vs\,AGY}$, $Ser_{UCA\,vs\,UCG}$, $Thr_{ACR\,vs\,ACY}$, and $f_3$-stress.  The discriminant run used per-protein mean modeled tAI,
$$
\begin{aligned}
T &= {\sum_c n_c w_c \over \sum_c n_c},
\end{aligned}
$$
where $n_c$ is the CDS codon count and $w_c$ is the GtRNAdb/dos Reis tAI weight.  The protein readout was $P=\log_{10}(\mathrm{abundance\ ppm})$ from measured PAXdb abundance joined to the same CDS rows.  For \emph{Escherichia coli} K12 MG1655 the finite join contained $3739$ CDS/protein rows and used the \emph{E. coli} tRNA table; for \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae} the finite join contained $5815$ rows and used the yeast tRNA table.  These are finite-row classifications, not asymptotic claims.

\paragraph{Residualization and rank.}
In each organism, $Q$, $T$, and $P$ were tested after the common control residualization against CDS length, amino-acid composition, GC3, and the $M$-density baseline.  The control design had $24$ columns and residual rank $\operatorname{rank}(Z)=23$ in both organisms.  The $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual design retained full coordinate rank for both target fits:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde X_T)&=9, & \operatorname{rank}(\widetilde X_P)&=9.
\end{aligned}
$$
The remaining residual degrees of freedom were $3716$ for \emph{E. coli} and $5792$ for yeast.  The fitted $Q$ energy against $P$ was $432.89050065533684$ for \emph{E. coli} and $882.6526210803958$ for yeast.  The fitted $Q$ energy against modeled tAI was $0.256128714706293$ for \emph{E. coli} and $5.671703404506426$ for yeast.  The corresponding residual energies were $4020.333671802061$ and $2958.1865890925337$ for $P$, and $0.4728468725814029$ and $6.90537888244409$ for $T$.  The degeneracy cutoff was $0.999999$, and both organisms passed the powered-rank gate.

\paragraph{Four cases.}
The classification threshold was $10^{-12}$.  Each coordinate receives a finite label from the sign pattern of $S^{QT}$ and $S^{QP}$ together with whether conditioning on $T$ reduces the direct $Q\to P$ contribution.  Case A denotes no translation-layer survival and no protein-abundance survival.  Case B denotes translation-layer survival without protein-abundance survival.  Case C denotes direct protein-abundance survival without the measured or bounded translation-readout condition carrying the signal.  Case D denotes simultaneous $S^{QT}>0$ and $S^{QP}>0$ with the direct $Q\to P$ term reduced after conditioning on $T$.  Thus the discriminant separates a translation-conditioned protein-abundance pattern from a merely direct or confounded protein-omics association.

\paragraph{Observed classification.}
The modeled-tAI/protein-abundance discriminant produced no unclassified coordinates.  In \emph{E. coli}, $R^2_{QT}=0.5416736993691318$ and $R^2_{QP}=0.10767526677985896$ over the nine-coordinate residual design.  Seven coordinates were assigned to case D, while two coordinates were positive for both $S^{QT}$ and $S^{QP}$ but did not show the required mediation drop.  The case count was therefore
$$
\begin{aligned}
(A,B,C,D,\mathrm{positive\ without\ drop})&=(0,0,0,7,2).
\end{aligned}
$$
In yeast, $R^2_{QT}=0.8213457220900503$ and $R^2_{QP}=0.29837624994140827$.  All nine coordinates were assigned to case D, with no positive-positive coordinate lacking the conditioning drop:
$$
\begin{aligned}
(A,B,C,D,\mathrm{positive\ without\ drop})&=(0,0,0,9,0).
\end{aligned}
$$
The finite readback is therefore strong for a bounded translation-conditioned abundance pattern on the two modeled-tAI organism panels, with yeast giving the cleanest four-case surface.

\paragraph{Relation to direct protein-omics survival.}
The same $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ coordinate family also had direct protein-abundance survival in the broader measured PAXdb screen.  The direct $S^{QP}$ run reported $R^2_{QP}=0.10767526677985896$ for \emph{E. coli} and $0.29837624994140827$ for yeast, matching the two organisms used in the four-case discriminant.  In that broader screen, all nine coordinates survived against $P$ in each listed organism, including $R^2_{QP}=0.04454565118019448$ for human, $0.049827984062539195$ for mouse, $0.04613529807085213$ for zebrafish, $0.0770952887136557$ for fly, and $0.10217906758013379$ for \emph{C. elegans}.  Those direct values are useful as the protein-output side of the comparison, but by themselves they do not determine mechanism.  The four-case run adds the bounded $T$ conditioning step and records whether the protein-abundance association is absorbed by that readout.

\paragraph{Measured-TE contrast.}
The modeled-tAI discriminant must be kept separate from measured ribosome-profiling TE.  A separate measured-TE survival run found positive $S^{QT,\mathrm{meas}}$ values after the same style of controls: $0.13681931343730436$ for yeast, $0.009394266754822262$ for \emph{E. coli}, $0.018837911344127926$ for human, and $0.04034217614654355$ for zebrafish, with nine surviving coordinates in each organism.  A separate measured-TE/protein-abundance mediation fit gave measured mediated fractions $0.4793069656345167$ for yeast, $0.04091158340950673$ for \emph{E. coli}, $0.0027966485036786744$ for human, and $0$ for zebrafish after truncating a negative raw fraction.  These measured-TE numbers support the plausibility of asking the same four-case question with ribosome-derived $T$, but they are not the same finite surface as the modeled-tAI four-case table above.

\paragraph{Additional mediator context.}
The joint TE and mRNA-stability decomposition gives a useful guardrail against over-reading the four-case labels.  On the three-organism joint join, the $Q\to P$ association had $R^2_{QP}=0.2715520808117803$ in yeast, $0.1181743523064932$ in \emph{E. coli}, and $0.04274178488511215$ in human.  Conditioning jointly on measured TE and mRNA stability reduced the corresponding values to $0.13047582645538838$, $0.08975732521542036$, and $0.0355223930537993$, giving joint mediation fractions $0.5195182225621592$, $0.2404669586626661$, and $0.16890712099923358$.  The right-tail permutation value was $0.000999000999000999$ in all three organisms.  This shows that translation readout and transcript stability can both carry portions of the abundance association, so a four-case D label is a controlled statistical classification rather than a complete biological pathway.

\paragraph{Local boundary.}
The chapter claims only the finite discriminant just described: on the modeled-tAI panels, $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates show positive $Q\to T$ and $Q\to P$ surfaces, and most or all positive-positive coordinates are assigned to the translation-conditioned case D under the declared residualization.  Modeled tAI is not ribosome profiling.  PAXdb abundance is steady-state protein abundance, not folding, localization, turnover, function, phenotype, or causal response.  No translation mechanism, folded structural order, physical admissibility, molecular function, system phenotype, perturbation rescue, evolutionary direction, or universal biological law follows from this table unless a separate layer-matched contact supplies that readout and the same kind of controlled finite-row statistic is computed there.
