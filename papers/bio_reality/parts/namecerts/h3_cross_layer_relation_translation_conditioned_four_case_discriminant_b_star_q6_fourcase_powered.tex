\paragraph{Readouts and controls.}
The translation readout is not ribosome profiling. It is a per-protein modeled tAI readout computed as the codon-count-weighted mean of codon tAI weights, using GtRNAdb all-tRNA copy counts and dos Reis wobble weights. The protein readout is measured PAXdb protein abundance, used as $\log_{10}(\mathrm{abundance\_ppm})$, joined to real coding-sequence codon counts. Before any positive case is allowed, the $q_i$ block is residualized against amino-acid composition, organism identity within the relevant panel construction, GC3, CDS length, M-density, mRNA control information when available in the design, and simpler codon-baseline controls. This chapter therefore reads the finite matrix as a bounded cross-layer discriminant, not as a direct mechanistic assay.

\paragraph{Controlled matrices.}
For each coordinate $q_i$, the discriminant compares three controlled quantities. The first is $S^{QT}$, the controlled association between the residual coordinate and the translation readout. The second is $S^{QP}$, the controlled association between the residual coordinate and the protein-omics readout. The third is $M^{QTP}$, the translation-conditioned reduction test for the direct $q_i$ to $P_k$ association after conditioning on $T_j$. With the classification threshold fixed at $10^{-12}$, the four cases are read as follows:
$$
\begin{aligned}
A &: S^{QT} \leq 0 \ \text{and}\ S^{QP} \leq 0, \\
B &: S^{QT} > 0 \ \text{and}\ S^{QP} \leq 0, \\
C &: S^{QT} \leq 0 \ \text{and}\ S^{QP} > 0, \\
D &: S^{QT} > 0 \ \text{and}\ S^{QP} > 0\ \text{with a reduced direct }q_i \to P_k\text{ term after conditioning on }T_j.
\end{aligned}
$$
Case $A$ is a compressed-away coordinate for both readouts. Case $B$ is a translation-level residual. Case $C$ is a protein-omics residual not mediated by the measured translation readout. Case $D$ is the only positive-positive translation-conditioned propagation case, and it requires both survival of the $q_i \to T_j$ step and attenuation of the direct $q_i \to P_k$ term after $T_j$ is included.

\paragraph{Four-case outcome.}

It assigns $7$ coordinates to case $D$ and records $2$ positive-positive coordinates without the mediation drop required for a case-$D$ call. Thus the bacterial readback is not simply a positive $q_i \to P_k$ association: only $7$ of the $9$ coordinates satisfy the stricter translation-conditioned propagation criterion, while $2$ are explicitly withheld from that label. The yeast panel assigns no coordinate to case $A$, case $B$, or case $C$, assigns all $9$ coordinates to case $D$, and records $0$ positive-positive coordinates without mediation drop. Both panels have empty unclassified-coordinate lists.
