\subsection{Translation-conditioned four-case discriminant for $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates}
\label{sec:biorality-q6-translation-conditioned-four-case-discriminant}

\paragraph{Finite carrier and readout layout.}
The finite object tested here is a matched row carrier whose rows are coding-sequence/protein entries and whose columns split into three named blocks: $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates $q_i$, a translation-layer readout $T$, and a protein-omics readout $P$.  The translation readout is the per-protein mean modeled tAI,
$$
\begin{aligned}
T &= \frac{\sum_{c} n_c w_c}{\sum_c n_c},
\end{aligned}
$$
where $n_c$ is the codon count in the coding sequence and $w_c$ is the organism-scoped tAI weight derived from all-tRNA gene-copy counts with dos Reis wobble weights.  The protein-omics readout is $P=\log_{10}(\mathrm{abundance\_ppm})$ from measured PAXdb abundance joined to the same coding-sequence rows.  The run identifier for the finite computation is \texttt{d74f9f0e39cf}.  The joined panels contain $3739$ proteins for \emph{Escherichia coli} K12 MG1655 and $5815$ proteins for \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}.  The $E.\ coli$ coding-sequence organism is \texttt{escherichia\_coli\_k12\_mg1655} and its tRNA organism key is \texttt{escherichia\_coli}; the yeast panel uses \texttt{saccharomyces\_cerevisiae} for both coding-sequence and tRNA keys.

\paragraph{Controlled matrices.}
For each organism, the $Q$ block has $9$ residual coordinates.  The control block has $24$ columns before residualization and rank $23$ after the declared controls are assembled.  The controls include amino-acid composition, organism identity where applicable, GC3, coding-sequence length, M-density, matched mRNA control information when present in the design, and simpler-baseline terms.  Let $\widetilde Q_T$ and $\widetilde Q_P$ denote the residualized $Q$ matrices used against $T$ and $P$, respectively.  The powered-rank check passes in both panels:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde Q_T) &= 9, & \operatorname{rank}(\widetilde Q_P) &= 9
\end{aligned}
$$
for both organisms.  The residual degrees of freedom after controls are $3716$ for $E.\ coli$ and $5792$ for yeast.  The residual energies are also finite and nonzero: in $E.\ coli$, $T$ has residual energy $0.4728468725814029$ and $P$ has residual energy $4020.333671802061$; in yeast, $T$ has residual energy $6.90537888244409$ and $P$ has residual energy $2958.1865890925337$.  The $Q$ block explains finite residual energy against both readouts: $E.\ coli$ has $0.256128714706293$ against $T$ and $432.89050065533684$ against $P$, while yeast has $5.671703404506426$ against $T$ and $882.6526210803958$ against $P$.

\paragraph{Four-case rule.}
Each coordinate is assigned by the signs of the controlled $Q\to T$ and $Q\to P$ surfaces and by whether the direct $Q\to P$ term is reduced after conditioning on $T$.  With the classification threshold fixed at $10^{-12}$, the four cases are:
$$
\begin{aligned}
A &: S^{QT}\leq 0\ \text{and}\ S^{QP}\leq 0,\\
B &: S^{QT}>0\ \text{and}\ S^{QP}\leq 0,\\
C &: S^{QT}\leq 0\ \text{and}\ S^{QP}>0,\\
D &: S^{QT}>0\ \text{and}\ S^{QP}>0\ \text{with a reduced direct }Q\to P\text{ term after conditioning on }T.
\end{aligned}
$$
Case $A$ is compression away from both readouts.  Case $B$ is a translation-readout residual without propagation to the measured protein abundance.  Case $C$ is protein-omics prediction not mediated by the measured translation readout.  Case $D$ is the bounded propagation pattern: the coordinate reaches the translation readout, reaches the protein-abundance readout, and the direct protein-abundance association is reduced when $T$ is included.

\paragraph{Observed finite-row outcome.}
The controlled coefficient-of-determination summaries are positive in both organisms.  For $E.\ coli$,
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{QT} &= 0.5416736993691318, & R^2_{QP} &= 0.10767526677985896,
\end{aligned}
$$
and for yeast,
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{QT} &= 0.8213457220900503, & R^2_{QP} &= 0.29837624994140827.
\end{aligned}
$$
The case counts are organism-specific.  In $E.\ coli$, no coordinate falls in cases $A$, $B$, or $C$; seven coordinates fall in case $D$; two further coordinates are positive-positive but fail the mediation-drop requirement and therefore are not promoted to case $D$.  The unclassified-coordinate list is empty.  In yeast, all nine coordinates fall in case $D$, with zero coordinates in cases $A$, $B$, and $C$, zero positive-positive coordinates lacking the mediation drop, and no unclassified coordinates.  Thus the finite readback is not a rank-saturated three-row artifact: both panels have $9$ residual-coordinate rank after controls, thousands of residual degrees of freedom, and explicit case allocation for every coordinate.

\paragraph{Interpretation allowed by the rows.}
The result supports only the finite discriminant over the declared rows: after the stated controls, the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual block retains rank, has positive controlled association with the modeled translation readout and the measured protein-abundance readout, and assigns every tested coordinate to one of the stated four cases or to the explicit positive-positive non-mediated remainder.  The yeast panel is a clean all-$D$ panel.  The $E.\ coli$ panel is mostly $D$ but keeps two positive-positive coordinates outside the mediated-propagation call because conditioning on $T$ does not reduce the direct $Q\to P$ term enough under the rule.  This distinction is essential: a direct $Q\to P$ association alone is not treated as translation-mediated propagation.

\paragraph{Local no-promotion boundary.}
The computation uses modeled tAI as a bounded translation-layer proxy and measured PAXdb abundance as a protein-omics readout.  It does not observe ribosome occupancy, elongation dynamics, perturbational causality, protein folding, structural order, physical admissibility, molecular function, phenotype realization, or a universal biological law.  Protein abundance is not separated here from turnover, localization, post-translational modification, folding state, pathway context, or residual mRNA effects beyond the declared controls.  Case $C$, if it appears in a later panel, must be read only as protein-omics prediction not mediated by the measured $T$ variable; it is not a translation mechanism.  Case $D$ is likewise a controlled mediation-shaped finite-row classification, not a causal mechanism claim.  Any stronger statement about realized translation, folding, physical admissibility, function, mechanism, or universality requires a separate layer-matched contact and its own controlled statistic.
