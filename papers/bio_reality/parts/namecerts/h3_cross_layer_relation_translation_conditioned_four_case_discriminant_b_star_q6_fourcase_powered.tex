\subsection{Translation-conditioned four-case discriminant for $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinates}
\label{sec:bioreality-q6-fourcase-discriminant}

\paragraph{Finite-row surface.}
The tested object is a finite residual-coordinate panel over nine $B^*_{Q6}$ synonymous coordinates,
$$
\begin{aligned}
Q=\{&K_{AAA},\operatorname{Arg}_{AGR},\operatorname{Ile}_{AUA},\operatorname{Leu}_{CUN\,\mathrm{vs}\,UUR},\operatorname{Leu}_{UUA\,\mathrm{vs}\,UUG},\\
&\operatorname{Ser}_{UCR\,\mathrm{vs}\,AGY},\operatorname{Ser}_{UCA\,\mathrm{vs}\,UCG},\operatorname{Thr}_{ACR\,\mathrm{vs}\,ACY},f_3\text{-stress}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
Each coding sequence row carries real codon counts, a matched modeled translation readout $T$, and a matched measured protein-abundance readout $P$. The translation readout in this run is not ribosome profiling: it is the per-protein mean modeled tAI, computed as the codon-count-weighted mean of GtRNAdb all-tRNA copy-count weights with dos Reis wobble weights. The protein readout is $\log_{10}(\mathrm{abundance\_ppm})$ from measured PAXdb abundance. The control design $Z$ has $24$ columns before rank reduction and residualizes against length, amino-acid composition, GC3, and the $M$-density baseline. After residualization, both tested organisms have $\operatorname{rank}(Z)=23$ and $\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde X)=9$ for both the protein and modeled-tAI fits.

\paragraph{Rank-powered rows.}
The finite joins are non-vacuous. For \emph{Escherichia coli} K12 MG1655 the discriminant uses $3739$ joined CDS/protein rows, with the CDS organism recorded as \texttt{escherichia\_coli\_k12\_mg1655} and the tRNA organism as \texttt{escherichia\_coli}. The residual degrees of freedom after $Z$ are $3716$, the protein residual energy is $4020.333671802061$, and the modeled-tAI residual energy is $0.4728468725814029$. The $B^*_{Q6}$ design explains $432.89050065533684$ units of protein residual energy and $0.256128714706293$ units of modeled-tAI residual energy. The corresponding aggregate readbacks are
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{QP}(\mathrm{E.\ coli})&=0.10767526677985896,\\
R^2_{QT}(\mathrm{E.\ coli})&=0.5416736993691318.
\end{aligned}
$$
For \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae} the discriminant uses $5815$ joined CDS/protein rows, with matched yeast tRNA weights. The residual degrees of freedom after $Z$ are $5792$, the protein residual energy is $2958.1865890925337$, and the modeled-tAI residual energy is $6.90537888244409$. The $B^*_{Q6}$ design explains $882.6526210803958$ units of protein residual energy and $5.671703404506426$ units of modeled-tAI residual energy. The aggregate readbacks are
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{QP}(\mathrm{yeast})&=0.29837624994140827,\\
R^2_{QT}(\mathrm{yeast})&=0.8213457220900503.
\end{aligned}
$$
The direct protein-abundance run supplies the surrounding row quality: the PAXdb-CDS join hit rate is $0.9978649586335735$ for \emph{E. coli}, with $3739$ joined proteins, and $0.9156038419146592$ for yeast, with $5815$ joined proteins. In that direct protein-omics surface all nine coordinates survive the positive partial-$R^2$ threshold in both organisms, with $R^2_{QP}=0.10767526677985896$ for \emph{E. coli} and $R^2_{QP}=0.29837624994140827$ for yeast.

\paragraph{Four-case rule.}
The classification threshold is $10^{-12}$. A coordinate is read as translation-null or translation-surviving by the sign of its controlled $Q\to T$ contribution, and protein-omics-null or protein-omics-surviving by the sign of its controlled $Q\to P$ contribution. The final case then asks whether the direct $Q\to P$ signal is reduced by conditioning on the translation readout. Thus the finite table separates four empirical possibilities: no survival in either readout, translation-only survival, protein-only or unabsorbed protein-omics survival, and translation-conditioned protein-omics survival. This is a classification of residual linear readbacks, not an assertion that tAI is a physical cause.

\paragraph{Observed classification.}
The finite classification is complete in both organisms: no coordinate is left unclassified. For \emph{E. coli}, the case count is
$$
\begin{aligned}
A&=0,&B&=0,&C&=0,&D&=7,
\end{aligned}
$$
with two additional positive-positive coordinates recorded as lacking the required mediation drop. For yeast, the case count is
$$
\begin{aligned}
A&=0,&B&=0,&C&=0,&D&=9,
\end{aligned}
$$
with no positive-positive coordinate outside the mediation-drop condition. In the terminology of this chapter, yeast is therefore the cleaner finite surface: every one of the nine residual coordinates is both modeled-translation-surviving and protein-omics-surviving, and the direct protein-abundance association is reduced by the modeled-tAI conditioning step. The \emph{E. coli} surface is still strongly positive but less closed: seven coordinates satisfy the same translation-conditioned pattern, while two positive-positive coordinates remain outside the drop criterion.

\paragraph{Measured-TE comparison.}
The same notation must not hide the difference between modeled tAI and measured ribosome-efficiency data. A separate measured-TE run gives $R^2_{QT,\mathrm{meas}}=0.009394266754822262$ for \emph{E. coli} on $3415$ TE genes and $R^2_{QT,\mathrm{meas}}=0.13681931343730436$ for yeast on $5128$ TE genes. The measured-TE mediation run reports measured mediated fractions of $0.04091158340950673$ for \emph{E. coli} and $0.4793069656345167$ for yeast, using three-way joins of $3415$ and $5128$ rows respectively. These measured-TE values are smaller than the modeled-tAI $R^2_{QT}$ values in the four-case run, so the chapter keeps two readings separate: modeled-tAI classification is the discriminant actually reported here, while ribosome-profiling TE is an independent contact that can support a stricter measured translation statement only in its own row-matched analysis.

\paragraph{Local conclusion.}
The finite evidence supports a ready four-case discriminant over the modeled-tAI/protein-abundance surface. The row counts, residual ranks, and residual degrees of freedom rule out the rank-degenerate all-fit boundary for the two organisms tested. The result is strongest in yeast, where all nine coordinates fall in case $D$ under the modeled-tAI conditioning rule; it is partial but still non-null in \emph{E. coli}, where seven coordinates fall in case $D$ and two positive-positive coordinates do not satisfy the mediation-drop condition. The conclusion is therefore not merely that $B^*_{Q6}$ predicts protein abundance, but that the finite modeled-tAI surface can discriminate whether a protein-omics association is translation-conditioned or remains unabsorbed by the translation readout.

\paragraph{Claim boundary.}
No stronger biological layer is inferred from this table. Modeled tAI is a codon-adaptation readout, not direct ribosome occupancy. PAXdb abundance is a steady-state protein-omics measurement, not a translation-rate measurement, folding assay, localization assay, perturbation, rescue, or functional experiment. The four-case labels do not by themselves establish translation mechanism, folded structure, physical admissibility, molecular function, phenotype, causality, evolutionary direction, or a universal biological rule. Any such claim would require a separate layer-matched contact and its own finite statistic.
