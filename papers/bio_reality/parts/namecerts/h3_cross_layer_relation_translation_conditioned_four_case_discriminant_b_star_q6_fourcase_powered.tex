\subsection{Translation-conditioned four-case discrimination for $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residuals}\label{subsec:bioreality-q6-fourcase-discriminant}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite panel and coordinate surface.}
This record evaluates a finite cross-layer matrix whose rows are coding sequences and whose columns are the nine $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates $K\_{AAA}$, $Arg\_{AGR}$, $Ile\_{AUA}$, $Leu\_{CUN\,vs\,UUR}$, $Leu\_{UUA\,vs\,UUG}$, $Ser\_{UCR\,vs\,AGY}$, $Ser\_{UCA\,vs\,UCG}$, $Thr\_{ACR\,vs\,ACY}$, and $f_3\text{-}stress$. The tested readouts are a protein-abundance column $P$, given by $\log_{10}(\mathrm{abundance\_ppm})$ from measured PAXdb abundance joined to real CDS codon counts, and a bounded translation-readout column $T$, given by per-protein mean modeled tAI, $\sum_c n_c w_c/\sum_c n_c$, using GtRNAdb all-tRNA copy counts with dos Reis wobble weights. The two organisms retained for this four-case run were \emph{Escherichia coli} K12 MG1655 and \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}. The joined row counts were $3739$ CDS/protein rows for \emph{E. coli} and $5815$ rows for yeast; the tRNA organisms were recorded as \emph{escherichia coli} and \emph{saccharomyces cerevisiae}, respectively.

\paragraph{Residualization and rank checks.}
For each organism, the design residualizes $Q$, $T$, and $P$ against a $24$-column control surface consisting of intercept, CDS length, amino-acid composition, GC3, and the $M$-density baseline. The effective control rank was $23$ in both organisms. After this residualization, both the $P$ and $T$ residualized $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ designs had full coordinate rank $9$, so the four-case labels are not produced by a rank-deficient coordinate collapse. The finite diagnostics are
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{rank}(Z) &= 23,\\
\mathrm{rank}(\widetilde Q_P) &= 9,\\
\mathrm{rank}(\widetilde Q_T) &= 9,\\
\mathrm{threshold} &= 10^{-12}.
\end{aligned}
$$
For \emph{E. coli}, the residual degrees of freedom after $Z$ were $3716$, $P$ residual energy was $4020.333671802061$, $T$ residual energy was $0.4728468725814029$, $Q$ explained energy on $P$ was $432.89050065533684$, and $Q$ explained energy on $T$ was $0.256128714706293$. For yeast, the corresponding values were residual degrees of freedom $5792$, $P$ residual energy $2958.1865890925337$, $T$ residual energy $6.90537888244409$, $Q$ explained energy on $P$ $882.6526210803958$, and $Q$ explained energy on $T$ $5.671703404506426$.

\paragraph{Four-case rule.}
Each coordinate is classified by the signs of two controlled survivals and by whether the direct $Q \to P$ component is reduced after conditioning on $T$. In the notation of this chapter, $S^{QT}$ is the residual association between the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ coordinate block and the modeled tAI readout, $S^{QP}$ is the residual association between the same coordinate block and measured protein abundance, and the translation-conditioned comparison asks whether the $Q \to P$ signal is attenuated when $T$ is included. The computed organism-level surfaces were
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{QP}(\textit{E. coli}) &= 0.10767526677985896,\\
R^2_{QT}(\textit{E. coli}) &= 0.5416736993691318,\\
R^2_{QP}(\textit{S. cerevisiae}) &= 0.29837624994140827,\\
R^2_{QT}(\textit{S. cerevisiae}) &= 0.8213457220900503.
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus both organisms show positive aggregate $Q \to T$ and $Q \to P$ readback at the controlled matrix level, but the per-coordinate case call depends on whether the translation-conditioned comparison absorbs the direct protein-abundance signal.

\paragraph{Observed case counts.}
The finite four-case output is concentrated in case $D$ under this modeled-tAI readout. In \emph{E. coli}, the classification returned $0$ case-$A$ coordinates, $0$ case-$B$ coordinates, $0$ case-$C$ coordinates, $7$ case-$D$ coordinates, and $2$ coordinates that were positive for both $S^{QT}$ and $S^{QP}$ but did not show the required mediation drop. No coordinates were left unclassified. In yeast, the classification returned $0$ case-$A$, $0$ case-$B$, $0$ case-$C$, and $9$ case-$D$ coordinates, again with no unclassified coordinates and no positive-positive coordinates lacking the mediation drop. The module-level classification is therefore a bounded four-case discrimination result, not a universal biological law: the matrix separates direct protein-omics association, modeled translation-readout association, and translation-conditioned attenuation only inside the two joined, rank-sufficient organism panels.

\paragraph{Comparison with measured-ribosome and abundance facts.}
The broader finite row set gives important scale information for interpreting this four-case run. A direct protein-omics survival run over $15$ organisms found all nine $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ coordinates surviving against PAXdb protein abundance after the same core controls; examples include $R^2_{QP}=0.10767526677985896$ for \emph{E. coli}, $0.29837624994140827$ for yeast, $0.04454565118019448$ for human, and $0.049827984062539195$ for mouse. The measured ribosome-profiling TE run, which uses $\log_{10}(\mathrm{TE})$ with $\mathrm{TE}=\mathrm{RPF}/\mathrm{mRNA}$, retained four organism panels and reported $R^2_{QT,meas}=0.009394266754822262$ for \emph{E. coli} on $3415$ TE-matched genes, $0.13681931343730436$ for yeast on $5128$ genes, $0.018837911344127926$ for human on $11554$ genes, and $0.04034217614654355$ for zebrafish on $13140$ genes. The measured $Q \to T \to P$ decomposition gave mediated fractions $0.04091158340950673$ for \emph{E. coli}, $0.4793069656345167$ for yeast, $0.0027966485036786744$ for human, and $0.0$ for zebrafish after truncating a raw suppressive fraction $-0.04801486048721136$ to the unit interval. These measured-readout numbers confirm that modeled tAI is not interchangeable with ribosome-profiling TE; it is a bounded readout surface whose four-case labels must be read as finite matrix classifications.

\paragraph{Related mediation scale.}
A joint TE and mRNA-stability decomposition further constrains the interpretation of a case-$D$ call. On matched four-way joins, the row counts were $2517$ for \emph{E. coli}, $4759$ for yeast, and $11322$ for human. The joint mediation fractions were $0.2404669586626661$, $0.5195182225621592$, and $0.16890712099923358$, respectively, each with right-tail permutation probability $0.000999000999000999$. In yeast, the single-mediator split was $0.4697188319693239$ for measured TE alone and $0.0626618595344251$ for mRNA stability alone; in \emph{E. coli}, the analogous fractions were $0.01975521340963268$ and $0.23052056324898387$; in human they were $0.00549490864190343$ and $0.15314679870183337$. A separate yeast readout-dictionary certificate on $n=1800$ sampled rows used $\lambda_{DL}=0.01$, $\rho_{join}=0.05$, and $120$ permutations, with coverage $C^{\ast}=0.24538211439730545$, raw best coverage $0.2745002077400602$, selection-aware null $95\%$ value $0.029149007242956115$, and unexplained fraction $0.7546178856026946$. These values show that the direct protein-abundance residual is only partly reducible to the measured readout dictionary, even when a positive four-case or mediation label is present.

\paragraph{Boundary of the claim.}
The ready result is the following finite statement: in the two modeled-tAI panels, the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual matrix is rank-sufficient after controls, has positive controlled aggregate association with both $T$ and $P$, and assigns all classified yeast coordinates and seven of nine classified \emph{E. coli} coordinates to the case in which the modeled translation readout absorbs the direct protein-abundance association. This does not by itself assert observed translation dynamics, causal mediation, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, phenotype, or universality. Modeled tAI is not ribosome occupancy, PAXdb abundance is not isolated protein production, and cross-sectional joins do not establish temporal order. Any stronger statement at the translation, post-translational, structural, physical, functional, mechanistic, or universal layer requires a separate matched contact and its own controlled finite statistic.
