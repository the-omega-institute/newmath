\subsection{Translation-conditioned four-case discriminant for $B^*_{Q6}$ residuals}
\label{subsec:bioreality-q6-fourcase-discriminant}

\paragraph{Finite surface.}
The finite object tested here is a two-organism, nine-coordinate residual surface for the $B^*_{Q6}$ synonymous coordinates.  Each coding sequence is represented after residualizing against length, amino-acid composition, GC3, and the $M$-density baseline.  The tested coordinates are $K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $Leu_{CUN\,vs\,UUR}$, $Leu_{UUA\,vs\,UUG}$, $Ser_{UCR\,vs\,AGY}$, $Ser_{UCA\,vs\,UCG}$, $Thr_{ACR\,vs\,ACY}$, and $f_3$-stress.  The translation-side readout in the four-case run is per-protein modeled tAI, computed as a codon-count-weighted mean from GtRNAdb tRNA copy counts and dos Reis wobble weights.  The protein-side readout is $\log_{10}$ PAXdb abundance in ppm, joined to the same CDS codon-count rows.  The classification threshold is $10^{-12}$, so the labels are not visual cluster names: they are finite sign and attenuation decisions on residualized regression surfaces.

\paragraph{Rank and row support.}
The experiment run $f37bd98e0739$ contains two powered organisms.  In \emph{Escherichia coli} K-12 MG1655, $3739$ CDS/protein rows are joined, the control matrix has $24$ columns with rank $23$, and the residual degrees of freedom after controls are $3716$.  The residual $B^*_{Q6}$ design has rank $9$ both for the protein-abundance fit and for the modeled-tAI fit.  The explained energies are $432.89050065533684$ on $P$ and $0.256128714706293$ on $T$, with residual energies $4020.333671802061$ and $0.4728468725814029$ respectively.  In \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}, $5815$ rows are joined, the same $24$-column control design has rank $23$, and the residual degrees of freedom are $5792$.  The residual coordinate rank is again $9$ for both targets; the explained energies are $882.6526210803958$ on $P$ and $5.671703404506426$ on $T$, with residual energies $2958.1865890925337$ and $6.90537888244409$.

\paragraph{Four-case readback.}
The four labels separate four finite situations: no survival on either readout, translation-only survival, protein-only direct survival, and translation-surviving protein-side attenuation.  In the computed matrix, both organisms are fully classified and no coordinate is left unassigned.  The organism-level residual strengths are
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{QP}(E.\ coli) &= 0.10767526677985896, & R^2_{QT}(E.\ coli) &= 0.5416736993691318,\\
R^2_{QP}(S.\ cerevisiae) &= 0.29837624994140827, & R^2_{QT}(S.\ cerevisiae) &= 0.8213457220900503.
\end{aligned}
$$
The resulting counts are sharply concentrated in the translation-attached class: \emph{E. coli} has $A=0$, $B=0$, $C=0$, and $D=7$, with two additional positive-positive coordinates for which the recorded mediation-drop test does not support the $D$ promotion.  Yeast has $A=0$, $B=0$, $C=0$, and $D=9$, with no positive-positive coordinate left outside the attenuation-supported class.  Thus the finite readback is not merely that the residual coordinates correlate with protein abundance.  On this modeled-tAI surface, every yeast coordinate and seven of nine \emph{E. coli} coordinates pass the stricter translation-attached protein-side classification.

\paragraph{Relation to direct protein-omics survival.}
The direct protein-omics survival run $104db736a756$ shows why the four-case conditioning is necessary.  Across a broader fifteen-organism PAXdb panel, every organism has all nine residual coordinates surviving against protein abundance after the same style of sequence controls.  Examples include $R^2_{QP}=0.10767526677985896$ in \emph{E. coli}, $0.29837624994140827$ in yeast, $0.04454565118019448$ in human, and $0.049827984062539195$ in mouse.  The broader direct result therefore supplies a real downstream association surface, but it is not by itself a translation account.  The four-case discriminant adds a separate modeled translation coordinate and asks whether the direct $q_i\to P_k$ signal is reduced when that translation readout is included.

\paragraph{Measured-TE comparison.}
The modeled-tAI result must be read beside measured ribosome-profiling TE rows, because modeled tAI is not an observed ribosome measurement.  In the powered measured-TE run $58611670ec19$, the measured $S^{QT}$ surface is positive but weaker and study-dependent: $R^2_{QT,meas}=0.009394266754822262$ for \emph{E. coli}, $0.13681931343730436$ for yeast, $0.018837911344127926$ for human, and $0.04034217614654355$ for zebrafish, with all nine coordinates surviving in each organism under the positive threshold.  The measured mediation run $d71384d16e71$ then reports bounded cross-sectional fractions rather than causal effects: the measured mediated fractions are $0.04091158340950673$ in \emph{E. coli}, $0.4793069656345167$ in yeast, $0.0027966485036786744$ in human, and $0.0$ in zebrafish after truncating a negative raw value.  These measured rows support the same qualitative discipline as the four-case run: translation readouts can absorb part of the protein-abundance association, but the amount is organism- and assay-specific.

\paragraph{Residual mediators beyond TE.}
A second check comes from the joint TE and mRNA-stability decomposition $81f728964641$.  The single and joint residual fractions show that measured TE is not the only downstream route.  In yeast, $R^2_{QP}=0.2715520808117803$ falls to $R^2_{QP\mid T,M}=0.13047582645538838$, giving a joint mediation fraction $0.5195182225621592$, with TE-only fraction $0.4697188319693239$ and stability-only fraction $0.0626618595344251$.  In \emph{E. coli}, $R^2_{QP}=0.1181743523064932$ falls to $0.08975732521542036$, giving joint fraction $0.2404669586626661$.  In human, $R^2_{QP}=0.04274178488511215$ falls to $0.0355223930537993$, giving joint fraction $0.16890712099923358$.  The permutation right-tail value is $0.000999000999000999$ in all three organisms.  This makes the four-case call more precise: a $D$ label is evidence for translation-conditioned attenuation on the declared readout, not a claim that translation is the sole route from synonymous residuals to abundance.

\paragraph{Local negative scope.}
The chapter licenses only a finite classification of residual coordinate behavior on the specified joined rows.  Modeled tAI is a bounded translation proxy, PAXdb abundance is steady-state protein abundance, and the rows are cross-sectional.  No translation mechanism, ribosome dwell mechanism, folding state, physical admissibility, molecular function, phenotype, evolutionary direction, or universal biological law follows from this matrix unless a separate layer-matched measurement supplies that fact.  Case $C$ remains direct or confounded protein-omics prediction, and even case $D$ remains an attenuation result on the declared readout rather than a perturbational mechanism.  The admissible conclusion is therefore narrower and stronger: in a rank-powered finite panel, $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinates survive on both modeled translation and protein-abundance surfaces, and many of those direct protein-side associations are reduced when the modeled translation readout is included.
