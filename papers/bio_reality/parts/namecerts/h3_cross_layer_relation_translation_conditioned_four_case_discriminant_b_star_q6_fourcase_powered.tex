\subsection{Translation-Conditioned Four-Case Discriminant for $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ Residual Coordinates}\label{sec:biorreality-q6-translation-conditioned-four-case-discriminant}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite carrier and matched rows.}
This record treats the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ synonymous-residual coordinates as a finite matrix carrier over coding-sequence rows, modeled translation readouts, and matched protein-abundance readouts.  The finite run $f37bd98e0739$ joined two organism panels.  For \emph{Escherichia coli} K12 MG1655, the joined panel contains $3739$ CDS/protein rows, with $3739$ proteins used.  For \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}, the joined panel contains $5815$ CDS/protein rows, with $5815$ proteins used.  In both panels the coordinate block has $9$ $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ coordinates after residualization against the declared controls.  The translation readout $T$ is the per-protein mean modeled tAI,
$$
\begin{aligned}
T(g) &= \frac{\sum_{c} n_{g,c} w_c}{\sum_{c} n_{g,c}},
\end{aligned}
$$
where $n_{g,c}$ is the codon count for gene $g$ and $w_c$ is the organism-scoped tAI weight derived from tRNA gene-copy counts and wobble weights.  The protein-omics readout $P$ is $\log_{10}(\mathrm{abundance\_ppm})$ from measured PAXdb abundance joined to real CDS codon counts.  Thus the row object is not a pure sequence summary: it is a same-row codon, modeled-translation, and protein-abundance table with explicit residual controls.

\paragraph{Residualized rank gate.}
Let $X$ be the $n\times 9$ matrix of $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ coordinates, let $Z$ be the control matrix containing amino-acid composition, organism-scope covariates, GC3, CDS length, M-density, mRNA where available, and simpler-baseline controls, and let $\widetilde X=(I-\Pi_Z)X$.  The discriminant is only admissible when $\widetilde X$ keeps enough rank to separate coordinates after controls.  In the \emph{E. coli} panel, the control block has $24$ columns, rank $23$, and residual degrees of freedom $3716$; the residual coordinate ranks are $\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde X_T)=9$ and $\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde X_P)=9$.  In the yeast panel, the control block also has $24$ columns, rank $23$, and residual degrees of freedom $5792$; again $\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde X_T)=9$ and $\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde X_P)=9$.  Both panels therefore pass the powered-rank sufficiency gate.  The degenerate-$R^2$ refusal threshold was $0.999999$, so the reported surface is not the excluded all-fitted boundary.  The residual energies also remain nonzero: for \emph{E. coli}, $\|\widetilde T\|^2=0.4728468725814029$ and $\|\widetilde P\|^2=4020.333671802061$; for yeast, $\|\widetilde T\|^2=6.90537888244409$ and $\|\widetilde P\|^2=2958.1865890925337$.

\paragraph{Controlled association surfaces.}
The finite discriminant computes two controlled association surfaces, one from residual coordinates to the translation readout and one from residual coordinates to the protein-omics readout.  In compact notation,
$$
\begin{aligned}
S^{QT}_i &> 0 &&\text{means that coordinate } q_i \text{ survives as a controlled predictor of } T,\\
S^{QP}_i &> 0 &&\text{means that coordinate } q_i \text{ survives as a controlled predictor of } P.
\end{aligned}
$$
The run records organism-level explanatory strength for the full $Q$ block after the same residualization.  For \emph{E. coli}, the $Q\to T$ surface has $R^2_{QT}=0.5416736993691318$, while the $Q\to P$ surface has $R^2_{QP}=0.10767526677985896$.  The corresponding explained energies are $0.256128714706293$ on $T$ and $432.89050065533684$ on $P$.  For yeast, $R^2_{QT}=0.8213457220900503$ and $R^2_{QP}=0.29837624994140827$, with explained energies $5.671703404506426$ on $T$ and $882.6526210803958$ on $P$.  These numbers are finite-row regression readbacks on the joined panels; they are not population laws and are not causal estimates.

\paragraph{Four-case discriminant.}
Each coordinate is assigned by the signs of $S^{QT}$ and $S^{QP}$ and, in the positive-positive quadrant, by whether conditioning on $T$ reduces the direct $q_i\to P$ association.  The four cases are:
$$
\begin{aligned}
A &: S^{QT}\leq 0,\; S^{QP}\leq 0,\\
B &: S^{QT}>0,\; S^{QP}\leq 0,\\
C &: S^{QT}\leq 0,\; S^{QP}>0,\\
D &: S^{QT}>0,\; S^{QP}>0 \text{ with a mediation-condition drop after } T.
\end{aligned}
$$
The numerical classification threshold was $10^{-12}$.  In \emph{E. coli}, the run classified all $9$ coordinates: $0$ in case A, $0$ in case B, $0$ in case C, $7$ in case D, and $2$ positive-positive coordinates without the required mediation drop.  In yeast, all $9$ coordinates were classified: $0$ in case A, $0$ in case B, $0$ in case C, $9$ in case D, and $0$ positive-positive coordinates without the mediation drop.  There were no unclassified coordinates in either organism.  The finite readback therefore supports a bounded statement: in these two joined panels, most or all rank-admissible $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates occupy the translation-positive, protein-positive, mediation-drop quadrant, while the \emph{E. coli} panel also contains two positive-positive coordinates that fail the final case-D mediation condition.

\paragraph{Local interpretation boundary.}
The case labels are statistical discriminants on residualized finite matrices.  They do not by themselves identify a biochemical mechanism.  In particular, case C would only be a protein-omics predictive residual not mediated by the measured translation readout, and even case D only says that the direct $q_i\to P$ association is reduced after conditioning on the modeled translation readout.  The translation coordinate here is modeled tAI from tRNA gene-copy and wobble weights, not ribosome profiling or a direct ribosome-occupancy measurement.  The protein readout is steady-state abundance, not folding, localization, post-translational state, molecular function, perturbation response, or phenotype.  Therefore no observed translation process, folded structure, physical admissibility, biological function, mechanism, or universal biological rule follows from this discriminant unless a separate layer-matched contact supplies that measurement and the corresponding controlled statistic is recomputed.  The admissible conclusion is narrower: run $f37bd98e0739$ gives a powered, same-row, translation-conditioned four-case readback for $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates in two organism panels, with rank sufficiency verified before positive case survival is reported.
