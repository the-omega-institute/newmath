\paragraph{Claim form under audit.}
The biological question is whether a high-versus-low $B^*_{Q6}$ synonymous residual label, after quotienting by amino-acid identity, can be connected to an RNA half-life residual only through a translation-conditioned and RBP-perturbation-supported bridge.  The admissible positive form is narrow: the same row must attach a translation readout, an RNA half-life readout, matched transcription and abundance controls, measured local occupancy of a prespecified decay-factor RBP, target-RBP loss, wild-type target-RBP rescue, and binding-defective or non-target rescue controls.
