\subsection{Translation-conditioned RNA half-life and the absent RBP bridge}
\label{sec:bioreality-q6-translation-conditioned-rna-half-life-rbp-bridge}

\paragraph{Finite row carrier.}
This record concerns a finite matched-panel question: whether synonymous residual coordinate windows of type $B^*_{Q6}$ can be connected to an RNA half-life effect only after the amino-acid quotient, same-row translation readout, matched transcript controls, and an explicitly measured decay-factor RBP intervention are all present in the same finite carrier. The intended row type is not merely a transcript table. A row must carry a high- or low-coordinate synonymous residual label, the corresponding coding or transcript identifier, a same-row translation readout or translation-efficiency boundary, an RNA half-life readout, measured local occupancy of a prespecified decay-factor RBP at the editable RNA window, and perturbation rows separating target-RBP loss, wild-type rescue, and binding-defective or non-target rescue controls. In symbols, the positive carrier would have rows of the form $(q_i,T_i,H_i,O_i,L_i,W_i,D_i,N_i,Z_i)$, where $q_i$ is the residual coordinate label, $T_i$ is the translation readout, $H_i$ is RNA half-life, $O_i$ is local decay-factor RBP occupancy, $L_i$ is target-RBP loss, $W_i$ is wild-type rescue, $D_i$ is binding-defective rescue, $N_i$ is a non-target control, and $Z_i$ contains the declared controls.

\paragraph{Observed finite surfaces.}
The available finite evidence does not yet instantiate that row type. One checked finite run for the methylation flanking-context relation used $656$ individuals and $20000$ probes, with baseline $R^2=0.0129735$ and no cross-cohort value reported. This is useful as a scale marker for finite-row cross-layer auditing, but it is not an RNA half-life, RBP occupancy, perturbation, or rescue table. A separate RBP-binding finite run reports $900$ total peaks with $3$ cross-RBP-concordant cases and runtime $223.648043$ seconds. That contact shows that finite RBP binding surfaces can be represented, but it does not supply same-window decay-factor occupancy for the $B^*_{Q6}$ RNA half-life rows. A translation-readout run for the mTOR translation surface contains $6494$ rows from the stated GEO source and runtime $9.469$ seconds; it supplies a finite translation contact class, but it is not joined to RNA half-life, measured RBP occupancy, target-RBP loss, or rescue rows for the present conjecture.

\paragraph{Declared statistic.}
The admissible bridge statistic is conditional rather than direct. A half-life contrast may be considered only after conditioning on amino-acid sequence, transcription rate, local RNA structure, motif density, GC and dinucleotide composition, codon-pair context, splice or isoform state, transcript abundance, batch, and the same-row translation readout. The null hypothesis is exchangeability of high- and low-coordinate labels with respect to translation-conditioned RNA half-life:
$$
\begin{aligned}
H_0:\quad
(q_i=\mathrm{high}) \perp H_i\mid Z_i,T_i
\quad\text{against}\quad
H_1:\quad
(q_i=\mathrm{high}) \not\perp H_i\mid Z_i,T_i .
\end{aligned}
$$
Even rejection of this null would still be only an RNA-stability association unless the RBP bridge also satisfies the occupancy and intervention clauses:
$$
\begin{aligned}
&\Delta H(q,T,Z)\neq 0,\quad
\Delta O(q,T,Z)\neq 0,\quad
\Delta H\text{ collapses under target-RBP loss},\\
&\Delta H\text{ returns under wild-type rescue},\quad
\Delta H\text{ does not return under binding-defective or non-target rescue}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The present finite facts do not provide these five quantities in a joined matrix. Therefore the strongest licensed statement is a bounded target specification: the coordinate can be tested against RNA half-life only after translation conditioning, and the RBP explanation remains unavailable until same-window occupancy and loss/rescue rows are attached.

\paragraph{Local exclusion boundary.}
The chapter does not infer translation realization from RNA half-life alone, and it does not infer an RNA decay mechanism from the mere presence of a translation contact. It also does not treat RBP peak counts, mRNA abundance controls, or ribosome-profiling contacts as measured decay-factor occupancy at the relevant editable RNA window. No protein output, folding, physical admissibility, biological function, phenotype, adaptation, causation, mechanism, or universal biological rule follows from the current data. A positive bridge would require a new finite panel joining the $B^*_{Q6}$ coordinate, same-row translation readout, RNA half-life, local decay-factor RBP occupancy, target-RBP loss, wild-type rescue, binding-defective rescue, non-target control, and the full control set in the same organism and window.
