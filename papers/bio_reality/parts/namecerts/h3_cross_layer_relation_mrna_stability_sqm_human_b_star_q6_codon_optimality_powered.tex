\subsection{Human consensus mRNA half-life as a B$^*_{{Q6}}$ residual readout}
\label{sec:bioreality-human-mrna-stability-q6}

\paragraph{Finite row under test.}
This record tests whether the nine-dimensional B$^*_{{Q6}}$ synonymous residual coordinate system retains measurable information about human mRNA stability after the amino-acid quotient and the usual coding-sequence controls have already been imposed.  The readout is the unlogged mean normalized relative mRNA half-life consensus assembled from the Agarwal--Kelley human panel, with $54$ dataset columns contributing to the consensus table.  The row is therefore not a raw minute-scale decay measurement; it is a normalized cross-dataset relative half-life statistic, and the interpretation is restricted to that scale.  The joined design contains $16165$ coding-sequence rows.  The half-life table contains $19480$ consensus records, and the coding-sequence bridge reports $19053$ CDS rows before the half-life join.  The reported CDS join hit rate is $0.9779294769799313$.  Of the joined half-life rows, $16150$ are obtained through Ensembl gene identifiers after version stripping, and $15$ additional rows are obtained through the unique gene-symbol fallback.  No ambiguous symbol fallback key is reported.

\paragraph{Controlled design.}
For each joined coding sequence, the source computation forms the nine residual coordinates
$K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $Leu_{CUN/UUR}$, $Leu_{UUA/UUG}$, $Ser_{UCR/AGY}$, $Ser_{UCA/UCG}$, $Thr_{ACR/ACY}$, and $f3$-stress.  These coordinates are tested only after projecting away a $24$-column control matrix whose numerical rank is $23$.  The controls are the intercept, $\log(\mathrm{CDS\ length})$, the $20$ amino-acid composition proportions computed from real CDS codon counts, GC3, and the M-density baseline defined as the fraction of sense codons on the union support of the nine projected B$^*_{{Q6}}$ vectors.  Thus the residual degrees of freedom after the control projection are $16142$, and the test is not a raw codon-usage comparison.  The operative statistic can be summarized as
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{QM} &= 0.010503670362051522,\\
R^2_{QP}\text{ on the same human join} &= 0.04095215557813418,\\
\operatorname{rank}(Z) &= 23,\\
\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde Q) &= 9.
\end{aligned}
$$
The finite row is therefore positive but small: the residual B$^*_{{Q6}}$ block explains about one percent of the residualized human consensus half-life variance under the declared controls.

\paragraph{Dominant coordinate and permutation readback.}
The computed human $S^{QM}$ row has dominant coordinate $Leu_{CUN/UUR}$.  On the same human join, the corresponding protein-abundance row has dominant coordinate $Arg_{AGR}$, so the mRNA-stability and protein-abundance directions are not identical even though their finite-row vectors have positive alignment.  The reported cosine between the human $S^{QM}$ vector and the same-join human $S^{QP}$ vector is $0.6351183883924848$, with positive sign.  The permutation audit uses $1000$ permutations and reports a right-tail value
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{\mathrm{perm}} &= 0.000999000999000999.
\end{aligned}
$$
The underlying energy accounting is also finite and explicit: the residual half-life energy is $1422.0291144063049$, and the explained energy attributed to the nine residual coordinates is $14.936525062963877$.  The degenerate-fit threshold is $0.999999$, far above the reported $R^2_{QM}$, so this row is not being accepted through a saturated design.

\paragraph{Cross-organism anchors.}
The human row is most naturally read beside two already computed mRNA-stability anchors.  In yeast, the analogous B$^*_{{Q6}}$ to mRNA half-life statistic has $R^2_{QM}=0.05179429044975085$, $n=5085$, dominant coordinate $Leu_{CUN/UUR}$, same-join protein statistic $R^2_{QP}=0.2761140373962526$, and cosine $0.8040935468384264$ between the $S^{QM}$ and $S^{QP}$ directions.  In the E. coli K-12 MG1655 row, the analogous statistic has $R^2_{QM}=0.02298556640056076$, $n=2720$, dominant coordinate $Arg_{AGR}$, same-join protein statistic $R^2_{QP}=0.11632311854858134$, and cosine $-0.7784923375569599$.  The human result therefore agrees with yeast in the sign of the mRNA--protein directional comparison and in the $Leu_{CUN/UUR}$ mRNA-stability dominance, while it differs from the E. coli row, where $Arg_{AGR}$ dominates and the comparison direction is negative.  These anchors are descriptive only.  They are not a phylogenetic model, and they do not license a universal direction for any coordinate.

\paragraph{Local interpretation.}
This chapter records a controlled association between B$^*_{{Q6}}$ synonymous residual coordinates and a human consensus relative mRNA half-life readout.  The positive finite-row statistic means that the nine-coordinate residual block is not fully absorbed by amino-acid composition, coding-sequence length, GC3, and M-density baseline on this joined human half-life panel.  The strongest readback is the $Leu_{CUN/UUR}$ coordinate, and the human mRNA-stability direction has positive but incomplete alignment with the human protein-abundance direction on the same coding-sequence join.  The row is a statistical projection statement, not a decay-pathway statement.  It does not say that the codon residual causes mRNA stabilization, that codon optimality is the biochemical mechanism, or that the reported consensus readout is a direct raw half-life in minutes.

\paragraph{Boundary of the claim.}
No translation realization follows from this row by itself.  The readout is mRNA stability, not ribosome occupancy, translation efficiency, modeled or measured tAI, or protein synthesis rate.  No protein folding, structural order, physical admissibility, molecular function, cellular phenotype, mechanistic pathway, or universal biological law follows from the statistic unless a separate layer-matched contact supplies that evidence and is evaluated under its own controls.  In particular, the positive $R^2_{QM}$ value cannot be promoted to a ribosome-mediated mechanism, and the positive cosine with the human protein-abundance row cannot be promoted to translation-conditioned protein realization.  The only supported conclusion is the finite controlled human statement: on $16165$ joined CDS rows, the nine B$^*_{{Q6}}$ residual coordinates carry a small but nonzero association with normalized human consensus relative mRNA half-life after the declared coding-sequence controls.
