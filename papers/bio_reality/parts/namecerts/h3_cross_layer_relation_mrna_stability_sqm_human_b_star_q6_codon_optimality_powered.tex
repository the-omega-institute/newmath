\subsection{Human mRNA-Stability Survival of $B^*_{Q6}$ Codon Residuals}
\label{sec:bior-reality-human-mrna-stability-b-star-q6}

\paragraph{Finite object.}
This record tests whether the nine-dimensional $B^*_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinate system has a measurable statistical relation to human mRNA stability after the amino-acid quotient and the standard coding-sequence controls have already been imposed.  The carrier is the finite joined table obtained by matching human CDS-derived codon counts to the Agarwal--Kelley consensus mRNA half-life matrix.  The usable join contains $16165$ rows.  The consensus source contains $19480$ valid Ensembl-indexed records and $54$ dataset columns; the CDS side reports $19053$ joined coding-sequence records before the half-life join.  The reported CDS join hit rate is $0.9779294769799313$.  Of the final half-life matches, $16150$ are joined through Ensembl gene identifiers without version suffixes, while $15$ use the unique gene-symbol fallback; no ambiguous symbol fallback keys are used.  The payload checksum for the half-life table is \texttt{ddf9441d1fe85d091d4b7f0a444b8dcf3658aa1d4158f66948bff00d5406de98}.

\paragraph{Coordinates and controls.}
The tested coordinate list is
$$
\begin{aligned}
Q = (&\texttt{K\_AAA},\ \texttt{Arg\_AGR},\ \texttt{Ile\_AUA},\ \texttt{Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR},\ \texttt{Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG},\\
&\texttt{Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY},\ \texttt{Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG},\ \texttt{Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY},\ \texttt{f3\_stress}).
\end{aligned}
$$
The control design has $24$ columns and rank $23$.  It consists of an intercept, $\log(\mathrm{CDS\ length\ in\ nt})$, the $20$ amino-acid composition proportions $A,C,D,E,F,G,H,I,K,L,M,N,P,Q,R,S,T,V,W,Y$ computed from real CDS codon counts, GC3, and the $M$-density baseline defined as the fraction of sense codons lying on the union support of the nine projected $B^*_{Q6}$ coordinate vectors.  After residualization against this design, the residual degrees of freedom are $16142$.  The readout is the unlogged mean normalized relative mRNA half-life across the $54$ Agarwal--Kelley human dataset columns, with missing and empty cells skipped.  Its unit is therefore a normalized cross-dataset relative half-life, not raw minutes.

\paragraph{Statistic.}
Let $X$ denote the residualized $16165 \times 9$ coordinate matrix and let $m$ denote the residualized human consensus mRNA-stability readout.  The fitted survival statistic is the partial coefficient of determination of $m$ on the nine residual coordinates after the control projection has already been removed:
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{QM} &= 0.010503670362051522,\\
\mathrm{rank}(X) &= 9,\\
\Vert m\Vert^2_{Z\perp} &= 1422.0291144063049,\\
\Vert \widehat m_Q\Vert^2 &= 14.936525062963877.
\end{aligned}
$$
The degeneracy threshold used by the computation is $0.999999$, and the fit is far below that threshold.  The reported permutation comparison uses $1000$ permutations and gives
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{\mathrm{perm}}(R^2_{QM}) &= 0.000999000999000999.
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the finite human row supports a small but nonzero $B^*_{Q6}$ association with residualized relative mRNA half-life under the declared controls.  The dominant coordinate in the mRNA-stability surface is \texttt{Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR}.

\paragraph{Same-row protein-abundance comparison.}
On the same human join, the companion protein-abundance projection has
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{QP} &= 0.04095215557813418,\\
\cos(S^{QM}_{\mathrm{human}},S^{QP}_{\mathrm{human}}) &= 0.6351183883924848.
\end{aligned}
$$
The sign of this cosine is positive, but the leading coordinate differs: the human mRNA-stability surface is led by \texttt{Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR}, while the same-row protein-abundance surface is led by \texttt{Arg\_AGR}.  The protein-abundance readout used for this comparison is the PAXdb human whole-organism integrated file, with payload checksum \texttt{dbc2566f94d85117adb686a5acd99fc9fd1bad0ed7db7e803e332e0320f76a4a}.  This comparison is a geometric comparison of two residual projection surfaces on matched rows; it is not a mediation result.

\paragraph{Cross-organism calibration.}
Two descriptive anchors accompany the human row.  In yeast, the corresponding $B^*_{Q6}$ mRNA-stability fit reports $R^2_{QM}=0.052$, permutation $p=0.001$, cosine with the same-row protein-abundance surface near $0.8$, and dominant coordinate \texttt{Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR}.  In \textit{E. coli} K-12 MG1655, the corresponding fit reports $R^2_{QM}=0.023$, permutation $p=0.001$, cosine with the same-row protein-abundance surface near $-0.78$, and dominant coordinate \texttt{Arg\_AGR}.  These two rows are used only as finite comparative anchors.  They do not define a phylogenetic model, do not establish conserved directionality, and do not convert the human row into a cross-organism law.

\paragraph{Interpretation.}
The human result is a controlled statistical survival entry for the mRNA-stability readout: after amino-acid composition, coding length, GC3, and the $M$-density baseline are removed, the nine $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinates explain about $1.05\%$ of the residual variance of the normalized relative half-life consensus.  The effect is small in magnitude but nonzero under the finite-row permutation test, and the leading contribution is localized to the Leu CUN/UUR residual contrast.  Because the readout is a consensus relative half-life rather than a direct biochemical decay-rate measurement in minutes, the chapter records a codon-residual association with normalized mRNA stability, not a molecular decay mechanism.

\paragraph{Local boundary.}
No translation claim follows from this row by itself.  The readout is mRNA half-life, not ribosome occupancy, translation efficiency, tAI, stAI, protein abundance, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, phenotype, or a universal biological law.  The positive $R^2_{QM}$ and the positive human $S^{QM}$--$S^{QP}$ cosine do not show that codon choice causes stability, that stability mediates translation, that translation mediates protein output, or that any downstream structure or function is realized.  Such claims require their own matched layer-specific contacts and their own controlled statistics.  This chapter therefore records only the bounded statement that the human $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinate system has a finite, controlled, nonzero association with a normalized consensus mRNA-stability readout.
