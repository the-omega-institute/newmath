\subsection{Human mRNA-stability survival of the $B^*_{Q6}$ codon residual}
\label{sec:bioreality-human-mrna-stability-bstar-q6}

\paragraph{Object and row set.}
This record tests whether the nine-coordinate $B^*_{Q6}$ synonymous residual system retains measurable information about human mRNA stability after the ordinary sequence-level covariates have already been removed.  The response variable is the unlogged mean normalized relative mRNA half-life consensus assembled across $54$ human dataset columns from the Agarwal--Kelley panel.  The join uses Ensembl gene identifiers with version suffixes removed, with a unique gene-symbol fallback only where needed.  The resulting finite row set contains $16165$ joined CDS/readout rows, with $16150$ matches through Ensembl gene identifiers and $15$ through the symbol fallback.  The source panel reports $19480$ consensus records, and the CDS-side hit rate is $0.9779294769799313$.  No ambiguous symbol fallback keys are used.  On the CDS side, $2876$ records fail to find a half-life match and $12$ duplicate genes are skipped; the half-life table contributes no skipped records for missing identifiers, duplicate Ensembl identifiers, non-numeric consensus values, or non-object rows.

\paragraph{Residual design.}
The fitted coordinate block is the ordered list
$$
\begin{aligned}
Q=(&K_{AAA},\ Arg_{AGR},\ Ile_{AUA},\ Leu_{CUN/UUR},\ Leu_{UUA/UUG},\\
&Ser_{UCR/AGY},\ Ser_{UCA/UCG},\ Thr_{ACR/ACY},\ f3\text{-}stress).
\end{aligned}
$$
The control matrix $Z$ has $24$ columns and rank $23$.  It contains an intercept, $\log(\mathrm{CDS\ length})$, the $20$ amino-acid composition proportions $A,C,D,E,F,G,H,I,K,L,M,N,P,Q,R,S,T,V,W,Y$, GC3 computed from the real CDS codon counts, and the $M$-density baseline given by the fraction of sense codons on the union support of the nine projected $B^*_{Q6}$ coordinate vectors.  After residualization, the available degrees of freedom are $16142$, and the $Q$ block has residualized rank $9$.  Thus the positive statistic is not a three-row or rank-saturated artifact; it is a high-row-count human CDS panel with the amino-acid quotient, GC3, length, and simpler support-density baseline already imposed.

\paragraph{Human $S^{QM}$ statistic.}
For the human mRNA-stability readout $M$, the residualized nine-coordinate block explains
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{QM} &= 0.010503670362051522,\\
\mathrm{explained\ energy} &= 14.936525062963877,\\
\mathrm{residual\ response\ energy} &= 1422.0291144063049.
\end{aligned}
$$
The dominant coordinate in the $S^{QM}$ vector is $Leu_{CUN/UUR}$.  The permutation audit uses $1000$ permutations and gives a right-tail value
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{\mathrm{perm}} = 0.000999000999000999.
\end{aligned}
$$
The statistic is therefore retained as a finite positive cross-layer association between $B^*_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates and the human consensus relative half-life readout, not merely as an identifier join or a codon-count restatement.

\paragraph{Comparison with matched protein abundance.}
On the same human join, the paired protein-abundance residual statistic is
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{QP} &= 0.04095215557813418.
\end{aligned}
$$
The protein-side dominant coordinate on this join is $Arg_{AGR}$, while the mRNA-stability dominant coordinate is $Leu_{CUN/UUR}$.  The cosine between the human $S^{QM}$ and $S^{QP}$ directions is positive:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\cos(S^{QM},S^{QP}) = 0.6351183883924848.
\end{aligned}
$$
This is a directional comparison of two residual association vectors on a matched human context.  It does not say that mRNA stability mediates protein abundance, nor that the codon residual causes either readout.  It says that the human mRNA-stability residual vector is not orthogonal to the same-join protein-abundance residual vector, while still having a different leading coordinate.

\paragraph{Cross-organism anchors.}
The human row is best read beside the already computed yeast and \emph{E. coli} mRNA-stability panels.  In the yeast half-life panel, $R^2_{QM}=0.05179429044975085$, the dominant coordinate is again $Leu_{CUN/UUR}$, and the same-join protein comparison reports $R^2_{QP}=0.2761140373962526$ with cosine $0.8040935468384264$.  In the \emph{E. coli} panel at growth rate $0.40\,h^{-1}$, $R^2_{QM}=0.02298556640056076$, the dominant coordinate is $Arg_{AGR}$, and the comparison with the same-join protein statistic has cosine $-0.7784923375569599$.  The human statistic therefore occupies an intermediate descriptive position: its mRNA-stability association is smaller than the yeast and \emph{E. coli} mRNA-stability associations in $R^2$ magnitude, but its direction is positively aligned with the human protein-abundance vector and shares the yeast $Leu_{CUN/UUR}$ mRNA-stability leader.

\paragraph{Local interpretation.}
The finite conclusion is that the human consensus relative half-life readout leaves a positive residual trace of the $B^*_{Q6}$ synonymous coordinate system after amino-acid composition, GC3, length, and $M$-density controls.  In compact form,
$$
\begin{aligned}
S^{QM}_{\mathrm{human}}(B^*_{Q6}\mid Z) &> 0,\\
R^2_{QM} &= 0.010503670362051522,\\
p_{\mathrm{perm}} &= 0.000999000999000999,\\
\operatorname*{argmax}_{q_i}|S^{QM}_{i}| &= Leu_{CUN/UUR}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The claim is deliberately narrow.  It is a controlled residual association between codon-choice geometry and a normalized human mRNA half-life consensus.  It is not a direct translation-efficiency measurement, not ribosome occupancy, not tRNA charging, not protein folding, not physical admissibility, not biological function, not a mechanistic decay model, and not a universal law.  Any assertion about translation, folding, biochemical function, organismal phenotype, or mechanism would require a separate layer-matched measurement and its own controlled statistic.
