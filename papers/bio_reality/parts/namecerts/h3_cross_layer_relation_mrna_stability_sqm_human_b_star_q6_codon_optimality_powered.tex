\subsection{Human mRNA-stability readback for the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual basis}
\label{sec:bioreality-human-mrna-stability-bstar-q6}

\paragraph{Claim.}
This record tests whether the nine-dimensional $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ synonymous-residual basis retains measurable association with a human mRNA-stability readout after codon-length, base-composition, amino-acid, and simpler codon-density controls are imposed.  The readout is the unlogged mean normalized relative mRNA half-life consensus assembled across $54$ Agarwal--Kelley human dataset columns.  The claim is therefore a bounded cross-layer statistical statement: $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual codon coordinates are tested against residualized human relative mRNA half-life, not against ribosome motion, protein folding, function, phenotype, or a universal biological rule.

\paragraph{Finite row set and join.}
The human contact joins coding-sequence codon counts to the consensus relative half-life table by Ensembl gene identifier after version stripping, with a unique gene-symbol fallback.  The finite join contains $16165$ usable rows.  The source table contributes $19480$ consensus records, all valid by Ensembl gene identifier, and the CDS side reports $19053$ joined coding-sequence records before the half-life match.  The reported CDS join hit rate is $0.9779294769799313$.  Of the final joined rows, $16150$ are matched through Ensembl gene identifiers and $15$ through the gene-symbol fallback; there are no ambiguous fallback keys.  The skipped CDS records are concentrated in the no-half-life-match category, with $2876$ such records and $12$ duplicate-gene exclusions, while empty codon counts, invalid length, missing CDS headers, missing gene keys, non-object records, and nonpositive abundance records are all reported as zero.

\paragraph{Residual design.}
The predictor block is the fixed nine-coordinate $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual basis
$K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $Leu_{CUN/UUR}$, $Leu_{UUA/UUG}$, $Ser_{UCR/AGY}$, $Ser_{UCA/UCG}$, $Thr_{ACR/ACY}$, and $f_3$-stress.  The control design has $24$ columns and rank $23$.  It contains an intercept, $\log(\mathrm{CDS\ length\ in\ nt})$, the $20$ amino-acid composition proportions $A,C,D,E,F,G,H,I,K,L,M,N,P,Q,R,S,T,V,W,Y$, GC3 computed from the same real CDS codon counts, and an $M$-density baseline equal to the fraction of sense codons lying in the union support of the nine projected $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ vectors.  After the control projection, the residual degrees of freedom are $16142$, and the residualized $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ block has rank $9$.

\paragraph{Human survival statistic.}
Let $X_Q$ be the nine residualized $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ coordinates, $Z$ the control design, and $M_H$ the human normalized relative half-life readout.  The tested quantity is the partial description gain, reported as a partial $R^2$, from adding $X_Q$ after projecting out $Z$:
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{QM}(\mathrm{human}) &= 0.010503670362051522,\\
\operatorname{rank}(X_Q \perp Z) &= 9,\\
\|M_H \perp Z\|^2 &= 1422.0291144063049,\\
\|\operatorname{Proj}_{X_Q \perp Z}(M_H \perp Z)\|^2 &= 14.936525062963877.
\end{aligned}
$$
The dominant coordinate in this human mRNA-stability readback is $Leu_{CUN/UUR}$.  The statistic is positive but small: the nine-coordinate residual basis explains about $1.05\%$ of the control-residualized variance in the normalized human half-life readout.  A $1000$-permutation comparison reports $p=0.000999000999000999$, so the finite-row result is not being read as a zero row under that permutation procedure.

\paragraph{Comparison with the same-row human protein-abundance surface.}
On the same human join, the corresponding protein-abundance readback has
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{QP}(\mathrm{human};\ \mathrm{same\ join}) &= 0.04095215557813418,\\
\cos(S^{QM}_{\mathrm{human}},S^{QP}_{\mathrm{human}}) &= 0.6351183883924848.
\end{aligned}
$$
The half-life surface and the protein-abundance surface therefore point in a positively aligned direction in the nine-coordinate residual space, but they are not identical.  The dominant half-life coordinate is $Leu_{CUN/UUR}$, whereas the same-join protein-abundance surface is dominated by $Arg_{AGR}$.  This distinction matters: the human result supports a statistical mRNA-stability association for the residual codon basis, while the protein-abundance contact remains a separate downstream readout with its own measured surface.

\paragraph{Cross-organism anchors.}
The same mRNA-stability protocol gives larger partial $R^2$ values in two supplied comparison rows.  In yeast, $n=5085$, $R^2_{QM}=0.05179429044975085$, the dominant coordinate is again $Leu_{CUN/UUR}$, and the yeast half-life/protein-abundance cosine is $0.8040935468384264$.  In \emph{E. coli} K-12 MG1655, $n=2720$, $R^2_{QM}=0.02298556640056076$, the dominant coordinate is $Arg_{AGR}$, and the half-life/protein-abundance cosine is $-0.7784923375569599$.  These two comparison rows show that the human row is not a verbatim copy of either reference pattern: it shares the $Leu_{CUN/UUR}$ half-life dominance with yeast, but its same-join protein-abundance dominance is $Arg_{AGR}$, and its half-life/protein-abundance alignment is positive but weaker than the yeast value.

\paragraph{Interpretation.}
The finite human result is a positive, controlled mRNA-stability readback for the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates.  It says that, after amino-acid composition, CDS length, GC3, and $M$-density are accounted for, the residual synonymous-coordinate block still improves the description of normalized human relative mRNA half-life.  The effect is statistically nonzero under the recorded permutation procedure and numerically modest in variance terms.  The appropriate biological reading is therefore narrow: the human coding-sequence residual basis carries a detectable association with a consensus mRNA-stability measurement.

\paragraph{Local boundary.}
No translation claim follows from this row by itself.  The readout is normalized relative mRNA half-life, not ribosome occupancy, elongation rate, tAI, stAI, or translation efficiency.  No folding, structural order, physical admissibility, molecular function, pathway mechanism, phenotype, or universal biological law is inferred from the positive $R^2_{QM}$ value.  A translation statement would require a separate translation-layer contact such as matched ribosome profiling or explicitly bounded tAI/stAI readouts with its own controlled statistic.  A protein-output or mediation statement would require a separate protein-abundance surface and an explicit conditioning analysis.  A function or mechanism statement would require an additional layer-matched assay rather than this mRNA-stability row.
