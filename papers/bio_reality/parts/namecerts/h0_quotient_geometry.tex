\paragraph{ORF-boundary interpretation.}

\paragraph{Local boundary.}
The finite quotient geometry explains only the enumerated codon pattern and its quotient representatives.  The curated interval row tests one restricted ORF-eligibility layer against a finite external annotation contact.  Neither row establishes protein production, folded molecular structure, thermodynamic or cellular admissibility, phenotypic role, causal mechanism, or a universal biological principle.  Those stronger claims would require their own contacts and their own tests.  The self-check row agrees with this separation: three gates are checked, and the stated boundary is that no codon geometry is carried across biological layers merely by being available as a finite coordinate pattern.
