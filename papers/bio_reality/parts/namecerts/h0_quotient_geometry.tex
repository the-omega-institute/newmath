\paragraph{ORF-eligibility interpretation.}
The biological object attached to the row is open-reading-frame eligibility from start and stop codon enumeration.  The geometry just displayed can state which finite codon windows would be eligible under a start/stop-window classifier, but that classifier is a boundary test unless an observed interval is supplied separately.  In the seed record itself, the carrier and readback are marked as awaiting contact with biological data, the distinctions are likewise awaiting such contact, and the internal structure is recorded as none.  This is why the row may support a proposed test of ORF eligibility while still refusing to assert that an ORF has been observed.

\paragraph{Local non-promotion rule.}
The boundary is part of the mathematical content of the chapter.  Start/stop-window enumeration does not imply translation realization.  It does not imply a folded protein structure, physical admissibility, biological function, a causal mechanism, or a universal law over genomes.  The quotient data, the $20$-element codon set, the $10$-row image $\pi(M)$, the face-vectors, and the saturation audit only describe a finite combinatorial organization.  A further claim about expression, structure, function, or mechanism must be supported by a separate contact whose measurement class is appropriate to that stronger layer.  The self-check row agrees with this restriction: it records three checked gates and states that no codon geometry is promoted across biological layers.
