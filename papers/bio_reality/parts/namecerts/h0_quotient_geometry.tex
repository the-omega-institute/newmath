\paragraph{ORF-eligibility reading.}
The biological object named by this chapter is open-reading-frame eligibility from start/stop codon enumeration.  The row therefore has a natural testable question: whether a proposed nucleotide interval is externally present and satisfies the declared start/stop-window classifier.  The supplied start/stop-window check reports one external window, one loaded window, and one classified window, with scope explicitly restricted to external window start/stop enumeration.  Its recorded classification boundary is \texttt{orf\_eligibility\_only}.  Consequently, the geometry row can at most organize the finite codon side of the question. \paragraph{Boundary verdict.}
For the present claim row, the attached contact list is empty, while the finite quotient and codon rows are populated.  The correct status is therefore a boundary record for ORF-eligibility testing: the data specify what a start/stop-window eligibility contact would have to check, and the linked curated-contact evidence shows how such a check can be bounded when an external interval dataset is supplied.  The self-check records three checked gates and states that no codon geometry is promoted across biological layers.  The chapter therefore accepts the finite quotient geometry and the listed codon equalities, but rejects every stronger biological reading unless a separate contact is explicitly supplied for that stronger layer.
