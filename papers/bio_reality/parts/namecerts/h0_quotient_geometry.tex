\paragraph{Finite coordinate packet.}
The finite row named $h0.quotient.geometry$ records a combinatorial quotient geometry over codon-level coordinates. Its verified finite carrier has an $M$-level face vector
$$
\begin{aligned}

\end{aligned}
$$
and the quotient object has face vector
$$
\begin{aligned}

The quotient has $10$ listed classes. In the finite encoding supplied by the experiment, these classes are

\begin{aligned}

The row also records an empty failure list for the product-decomposition check, so within this finite packet there is no recorded obstruction to the stated decomposition test. This is a purely finite combinatorial statement. It is not, by itself, an observation of a biological open reading frame.

\paragraph{ORF eligibility status.}
The biological layer named here is open-reading-frame eligibility from start and stop codon enumeration. The seed row itself has no attached contact in the supplied top-level contact list, and its minimal biological form is explicitly marked as awaiting contact. Accordingly, the finite quotient data can only formulate what would have to be checked by a separate biological record. A window may be eligible only after a sequence interval, transcript record, or curated annotation supplies the interval being tested. Without such contact, start and stop codon geometry remains a boundary condition rather than an observed ORF.

\paragraph{Layer boundary.}
The curated contact can support only the named ORF-eligibility layer for the tested interval dataset. It does not establish translation realization, folded molecular structure, physical admissibility, biochemical function, mechanism, or a universal biological law. The start and stop codon enumeration classifies windows under the frozen finite rules; it does not show that ribosomes translate a product, that a peptide folds, that the product is physically viable, or that an organism uses it. Those stronger assertions require their own contacts and tests. The self-check row reinforces this boundary by recording $3$ checked gates and stating that no codon geometry is promoted across biological layers.

\paragraph{Local conclusion.}
The quotient-geometry row is therefore ready as a finite BioReality record for an ORF-eligibility boundary. Its verified content is the $20$-element $M$ set, the $13$-element $R$ motif-compression set, the quotient size $10$, the two face vectors $(20,38,26,8,1)$ and $(10,14,6,1)$, the listed quotient classes, and the empty product-decomposition and saturation-boundary failure records. The later curated interval contact shows that the frozen ORF-window classifier can be tested against a finite external annotation dataset, with $168$ rows and exact success against its declared positive, decoy, boundary, and split controls. The scientific reading is narrow: finite quotient geometry can organize an eligibility test, and curated interval contact can support eligibility for the tested records, but neither the geometry nor the eligibility result promotes itself to translation, folding, physical viability, function, mechanism, or universality.
