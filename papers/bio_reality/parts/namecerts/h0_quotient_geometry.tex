\paragraph{Local boundary.}
The self-check records three checked gates and states that no codon geometry is promoted across biological layers.  Therefore the conclusion of this chapter is intentionally narrow.  The quotient $\pi(M)$, the face vectors $(20,38,26,8,1)$ and $(10,14,6,1)$, the $13$-element motif set $R$, and the $20$-element set $M$ form a finite geometric packet that can organize start/stop-window tests.
