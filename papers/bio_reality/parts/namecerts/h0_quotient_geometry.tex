\paragraph{ORF boundary interpretation.}
The biological object attached to this packet is open-reading-frame eligibility from start/stop codon enumeration.  In this chapter the word ``eligibility'' is used only for the following restricted question: whether an externally supplied nucleotide interval can be classified by a frozen start/stop enumeration rule.  The present quotient-geometric row has no attached contact in its own contact list and no listed reality-contact reference.  Therefore it remains a boundary record: it can say what kind of external observation would be needed, but it cannot assert that such an observation has occurred.

\paragraph{Local non-promotion rule.}
The finite quotient geometry and the curated interval contact must not be merged into a stronger biological conclusion.
