\paragraph{Contact discipline.}
Two boundary probes govern the local reading.  The first says that a start--stop-window eligibility seed remains only a testable boundary record unless a sequence, transcript, or curated-annotation contact supplies an observed biological interval.  The second says that the packet must not record an ORF-eligibility claim while no sequence database record, transcript evidence, or curated annotation can test the proposed interval.  Both probes forbid treating start--stop geometry as an observed open reading frame.

There is a separate finite row for start--stop-window enumeration. Its recorded classification boundary is \texttt{orf\_eligibility\_only}.  That row is compatible with the present geometry only because it refuses promotion: enumeration of a start and a stop does not assert translation, folding, physical admissibility, mechanism, biological function, or a universal law.
