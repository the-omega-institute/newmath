\paragraph{ORF-eligibility contact.}
The local biological layer is restricted to start/stop-window eligibility.  The boundary seed explicitly begins without a reality contact: its carrier, distinctions, and readback are all waiting for an external interval observation, and its internal structure is recorded as none.  A small start/stop-window enumeration row classifies $1$ input window, loads $1$ external window datum, and marks the scope as external window start/stop enumeration only, with snapshot date $2026$-$05$-$25$.  That finite row is useful only for checking whether a supplied interval matches the predeclared start/stop rule; it is not a license to treat codon geometry as an observed open reading frame.

\paragraph{Boundary of interpretation.}
The chapter therefore has two separate readings.  First, the quotient geometry row is a checked finite combinatorial fact: $20$ codons, quotient size $10$, face vectors $(20,38,26,8,1)$ and $(10,14,6,1)$, plus the displayed quotient image.  Second, ORF eligibility appears only when an external curated interval contact is present; without such contact, the seed remains a boundary probe.  No translation realization, folded structure, physical admissibility, biological function, causal mechanism, or universal biological law follows from the quotient coordinates, from start/stop-window enumeration alone, or from the curated interval statistics.  Any claim at one of those higher layers would require a distinct contact whose data are suitable for that layer.  The self-check row records the same discipline: three gates are checked, and no codon geometry is promoted across biological layers.
