\paragraph{Finite claim.}
The record \texttt{h0.quotient.geometry} isolates a finite quotient geometry inside the codon packet and then records exactly how far that geometry can be used in a biological reading.  The biological object under discussion is open-reading-frame eligibility as seen through start and stop codon enumeration.  The claim is deliberately narrow: the finite codon geometry can organize an eligibility test, but it does not by itself assert that an observed biological interval exists.  In the seed state, the carrier, distinctions, and readback of the biological layer remain at contact-gap status; the only internal structure recorded for the seed packet is absence of further biological content.  Thus the geometry is a typed finite coordinate object, not an observation of translation.

\paragraph{Boundary interpretation.}
The quotient geometry therefore supplies a finite organization of start/stop-window eligibility data, but it is not a biological contact.  A start codon or stop codon pattern can mark an eligibility window only after a separate sequence, transcript, or curated interval record supplies the interval being tested.  Without that extra contact, the admissible statement is conditional: if an interval is presented by an appropriate biological annotation or sequence observation, then the frozen start/stop enumeration can classify that interval at the eligibility layer.  The geometry alone does not assert that the interval is present in a genome, expressed in a transcript, translated into a peptide, folded into a structure, physically admissible in a cell, functional, mechanistic, or universal across organisms.

\paragraph{Negative boundary.}
The self-check row records that three gates were checked and that no codon geometry is promoted across biological layers.  This local boundary is part of the mathematical content of the chapter.  The curated interval contact can support ORF eligibility for its finite interval dataset, but it still does not establish translation, folding, physical admissibility, biological function, a molecular mechanism, or a universal biological law.  The matched decoys and boundary controls do not classify windows outside the curated dataset, and the RefSeq contact remains an external biological annotation input rather than kernel content of the quotient geometry.
