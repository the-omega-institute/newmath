\paragraph{Local boundary.}
The chapter therefore has two distinct parts. The first is the finite quotient calculation: $20$ codons in $M$, $10$ quotient representatives in $\pi_M$, face vector $f(M)=(20,38,26,8,1)$, and quotient face vector $f(\pi_M)=(10,14,6,1)$. The second is the contact-limited ORF-eligibility evidence: $168$ curated rows, $72$ positive successes, $72$ matched decoy rejections, three stable held-out folds, and no promotion of $24$ boundary rows. This boundary is strict. No translation event, folded protein structure, physical admissibility, biological function, molecular mechanism, or universal genomic law follows from the quotient geometry, from start--stop enumeration alone, or from the curated interval contact unless a further independent biological contact supplies that stronger layer. The self-check row confirms the same discipline by recording $3$ checked gates and the boundary statement that codon geometry is not promoted across biological layers.
