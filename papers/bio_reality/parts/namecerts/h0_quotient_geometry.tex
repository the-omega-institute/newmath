\paragraph{Finite coordinate content.}

The audit reports no missing and no extra rows for this motif union. A separate saturation calculation records
$$
\begin{aligned}

&UAA,UAG,UCA,UCG,UGA,UGG,UUA,UUG\},

Thus the coordinate packet distinguishes a small closed combinatorial configuration, but the packet itself contains no sequence interval, no transcript evidence, and no curated biological annotation.

\paragraph{Boundary interpretation for ORF eligibility.}
The biological object attached to this record is open-reading-frame eligibility from start and stop codon enumeration. The local meaning is deliberately narrow: a start/stop-window rule can mark a candidate interval shape only after a separate biological contact supplies an observed interval to test. Without that contact, the geometry remains a boundary condition for a future test. It is not itself a biological observation.

The ORF window enumeration check records one external input window and one classified window, with scope limited to external start/stop enumeration. Its source is a curated finite nucleotide-window manifest, and its snapshot date is $2026$-$05$-$25$. That row is useful because it shows what kind of contact can instantiate the boundary, but it also records the scope string $external\_window\_start\_stop\_enumeration\_only$ and the promotion guard $orf\_eligibility\_only$. Thus even when a window is classified, the claim remains at the ORF-eligibility layer.

\paragraph{Local non-promotion rule.}
The chapter therefore has a sharp negative boundary. The quotient support of size $10$, the face vectors $(20,38,26,8,1)$ and $(10,14,6,1)$, the $20$-codon set $M$, the $13$-codon motif set $R$, and the empty obstruction lists are coordinate facts. They do not establish translation, protein folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, mechanism, or universality. A curated interval contact may test ORF eligibility for its own finite rows; it still does not establish translation, structure, physical admissibility, function, a global biological law, or classification outside its dataset. The self-check records the same guard: three gates are checked, but no codon geometry is promoted across biological layers.
