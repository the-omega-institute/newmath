\paragraph{Local cannot-claim boundary.}
The quotient geometry and the curated interval contact have different roles.  The quotient calculation supplies a finite codon geometry with $20$ rows, a $10$-row quotient, and the displayed $f$-vectors.  The curated interval contact supplies a controlled outside annotation table for ORF-eligibility testing on the stated dataset.  Neither component, alone or together, entails translation realization, folded protein structure, thermodynamic or cellular admissibility, biological function, mechanism, or universality.  Any one of those stronger biological layers would require a further contact designed for that layer.  The self-check row records the same restriction with $3$ checked gates: no codon geometry is promoted across biological layers.  Consequently this section certifies a finite quotient calculation and records a separately scoped ORF-eligibility contact, while keeping all stronger biological assertions outside the local claim.
