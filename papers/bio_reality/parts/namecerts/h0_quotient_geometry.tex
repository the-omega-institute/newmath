\paragraph{Local cannot-claim boundary.}
The quotient geometry by itself does not establish translation, folding, physical admissibility, biological function, mechanism, or a universal biological law. The finite codon sets, the ten quotient representatives, and the $f$-vectors are mathematical enumeration data. The start/stop-window row adds only an eligibility classifier for supplied windows. Therefore the admissible statement is: this packet fixes a finite quotient geometry that can participate in ORF-eligibility testing once an independent interval contact is supplied.
