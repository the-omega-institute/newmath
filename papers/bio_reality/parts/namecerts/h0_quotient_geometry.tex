\subsection{Quotient Geometry and the ORF-Eligibility Boundary}\label{sec:h0-quotient-geometry-orf-boundary}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite coordinate record.}
The record considered here is the finite quotient-geometry packet with claim identifier $\mathrm{h0.quotient.geometry}$. Its biological reading is deliberately narrow: it concerns whether a start/stop codon enumeration can supply an open-reading-frame eligibility boundary, not whether a coding event is realized in a cell. The finite codon set $M$ has cardinality $20$ and is recorded as
$$
\begin{aligned}
M=\{&AAA,AAG,ACA,ACG,AGA,AGG,AUA,AUG,CUA,CUC,\\
& CUG,CUU,UAA,UAG,UCA,UCG,UGA,UGG,UUA,UUG\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The motif-compressed subrecord $R$ is the $13$-element set
$$
\begin{aligned}
R=\{&AAA,AGA,AGG,AUA,CUA,CUC,CUG,CUU,UAA,UAG,UCA,UGA,UUA\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The verification row reports no missing and no extra elements for this motif compression. The companion equality check records $M$ as the same $20$ codons obtained from the union construction labelled by the finite row as $WNR\cup CUN$.

\paragraph{Saturation and boundary shape.}
A third finite operation saturates $R$ and records
$$
\begin{aligned}
\operatorname{Sat}_{f3}(R)=\{&AAA,AAG,AGA,AGG,AUA,AUG,CUA,CUC,CUG,CUU,\\
&UAA,UAG,UCA,UCG,UGA,UGG,UUA,UUG\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
This is the $18$-element part of $M$ obtained by excluding $ACA$ and $ACG$. The same row records the equality $M=\operatorname{Med}(R)\cup\{ACA,ACG\}$ and an empty $f3$ boundary list. In this chapter, that emptiness is read only as a finite combinatorial audit result: the chosen codon packet closes under the recorded saturation test in the stated coordinate system. It is not a claim that a biological interval has been observed, transcribed, translated, folded, stabilized, or assigned a function.

The product-decomposition check is recorded with an empty exception list. Thus the local mathematical content is a finite quotient calculation over the displayed codon packet: $20$ codon vertices in the prequotient record, $10$ quotient vertices after the recorded projection, and the corresponding face counts listed above.

\paragraph{Biological reading.}
The ORF-eligibility interpretation attached to this packet is a boundary reading. A start/stop enumeration can specify the kind of interval evidence that would be needed for an ORF-eligibility test, but the enumeration itself is not an observed ORF. The finite quotient geometry supplies an organized coordinate constraint over codon symbols. It does not supply a genome interval, a transcript, ribosome occupancy, peptide production, folded molecular structure, thermodynamic admissibility, biochemical role, organismal function, causal mechanism, or universal biological law. Each of those claims would require an additional contact with biological data at the appropriate layer.

\paragraph{External interval contact.}
A separate curated-interval contact is present elsewhere in the finite row and is relevant only as a scoped comparison point. That contact loads $168$ rows from a curated ORF interval dataset based on NCBI RefSeq GenBank XML CDS intervals for Escherichia coli K-12 MG1655 accession $NC\_000913.3$, with snapshot date $2026$-$05$-$30$. The input classes present are boundary cases, matched non-ORF decoys, and positive curated ORFs. The finite statistics are $72$ accepted positives out of $72$, $72$ rejected matched decoys out of $72$, and $24$ boundary cases with $0$ promotions. The positive and decoy exact one-sided binomial rows both report
$$
\begin{aligned}
p=2.117582368135751\cdot 10^{-22},
\end{aligned}
$$
with a paired baseline sign-test advantage of $72$ wins in $72$ pairs and the same reported upper-tail value. Three held-out splits are recorded: left, middle, and right locus folds, each with $24$ positives accepted at rate $1.0$ and $24$ decoys rejected at rate $1.0$.

\paragraph{Local verdict.}
The finite quotient-geometry packet is ready as a self-contained mathematical boundary record for ORF-eligibility testing. Its verified content is the displayed codon set $M$, the motif subrecord $R$, the saturation relation excluding $ACA$ and $ACG$, the $M$ and quotient $f$-vectors, and the ten quotient vertices. Its biological content remains conditional: without a separate curated interval, sequence, or transcript contact, it makes no ORF-eligibility assertion. Even when the separate curated contact is considered, the claim remains restricted to interval-level eligibility in that dataset; it still does not assert translation realization, folding, physical admissibility, biological function, mechanism, or universality.
