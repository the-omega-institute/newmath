\paragraph{Local boundary.}
The scientific conclusion is therefore local and conditional.  The quotient geometry row $h0.quotient.geometry$ gives a finite codon quotient with $|M|=20$, $|\pi_M|=10$, face-vector $(20,38,26,8,1)$ before quotienting, and face-vector $(10,14,6,1)$ after quotienting.  Together with the motif, saturation, and curated interval contact rows, it supports an ORF-eligibility classification inside the finite RefSeq-derived interval audit.  It does not establish that any eligible interval is translated, that a protein folds, that a molecule is physically admissible, that a biological function is present, that a mechanism has been identified, or that a universal rule holds outside the audited rows.  Those stronger claims require independent biological contacts appropriate to those layers.  The self-check row records the same restraint: three gates were checked, and no codon geometry was promoted across biological layers.
