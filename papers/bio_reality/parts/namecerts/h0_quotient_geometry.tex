\paragraph{Finite codon support.}

& CUG,CUU,UAA,UAG,UCA,UCG,UGA,UGG,UUA,UUG\}.

$$

\end{aligned}
$$
with no missing and no additional codons in the finite comparison against the declared motif union.  A separate saturation row reports that $\mathrm{Sat}_{f_3}(R)$ is exactly $M\setminus\{ACA,ACG\}$, namely

\begin{aligned}

&UCA,UCG,UGA,UGG,UUA,UUG\},

\paragraph{ORF-eligibility interpretation.}
These finite geometry rows are not, by themselves, biological interval observations.  They only prepare a boundary test for open-reading-frame eligibility under start/stop enumeration.  In the one-window enumeration contact, the external nucleotide-window dataset was loaded with $1$ input window, and all $1$ window was classified by the start/stop rule.  That row is explicitly scoped as external-window start/stop enumeration only, with the classifier output marked as $\mathrm{orf\_eligibility\_only}$.  Hence the quotient geometry may organize the combinatorial inputs used by an ORF-eligibility classifier, but it does not turn a codon pattern into a translated molecule or a biological role.

\paragraph{Curated interval contact.}
A later curated-interval contact supplies the biological interval layer for this packet.  The contact dataset contains $168$ rows from NCBI RefSeq GenBank XML CDS intervals for Escherichia coli K-12 MG1655 accession $NC\_000913.3$, with snapshot date 2026-05-30.  The required row classes are present: positive curated ORF intervals, matched non-ORF decoys, and boundary cases.  The frozen classifier is $\mathrm{orf\_start\_stop\_window\_eligibility}$, and its rules were not modified after the protocol was fixed.  The positive interval test accepted $72$ of $72$ positives, with accept rate $1.0$ and exact one-sided binomial upper-tail value $2.117582368135751\cdot 10^{-22}$.  The matched decoy test rejected $72$ of $72$ decoys, also with rate $1.0$ and the same upper-tail value $2.117582368135751\cdot 10^{-22}$.  The paired baseline comparison records $72$ wins in $72$ pairs, again with win rate $1.0$ and upper-tail value $2.117582368135751\cdot 10^{-22}$.  Boundary control rows remain non-promoted: among $24$ boundary cases, $0$ are promoted, so the promoted fraction is $0.0$.

\paragraph{Held-out stability.}
The curated contact is not a single undivided fit.  It reports three held-out splits.  In the left-locus split, $24$ positives are accepted at rate $1.0$ and $24$ decoys are rejected at rate $1.0$.  The middle-locus split has the same counts and rates, and the right-locus split also has $24$ positives accepted and $24$ decoys rejected.  All three split records are marked as passed.  This supports the limited claim that, for this finite curated interval dataset and this frozen classifier, the start/stop eligibility rule separates the declared positives from matched decoys and does not promote the declared boundary cases.

\paragraph{Local non-promotion rule.}
The scientific conclusion is deliberately bounded.  The quotient geometry verifies a finite ten-vertex image of the twenty-codon support, and the curated interval contact verifies ORF-eligibility classification behavior on $168$ external rows.  Nothing in this packet establishes translation realization, folded protein structure, thermodynamic or kinetic admissibility, molecular mechanism, organism-level function, or a universal biological law.  Those stronger layers require their own observations or curated contacts.  The self-check row records this same separation: three gates are checked, and no codon geometry is promoted across biological layers.
