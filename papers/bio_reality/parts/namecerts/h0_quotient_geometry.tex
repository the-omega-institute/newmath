\paragraph{ORF-eligibility seed status.}
The biological object attached to this packet is open-reading-frame eligibility from start and stop codon enumeration. At the seed stage the carrier, distinctions, and readback are explicitly not yet biological observations: they await an independent contact with curated sequence, transcript, or annotation data. The local meaning is therefore narrow. A start/stop-window enumeration may specify which finite windows would be eligible under the chosen syntactic rule, but it does not by itself say that an open reading frame occurs in a genome, that a transcript exists, or that a product is made. In the linked start/stop-window check, one external nucleotide-window record was loaded, and all one of one input windows was classified by the enumeration rule. Its declared scope is only external-window start/stop enumeration, with the non-promotion value recorded as $orf\_eligibility\_only$.

\paragraph{Curated interval contact.}
A separate curated interval contact supplies the stronger empirical test for this layer. That contact loaded $168$ rows from a finite interval dataset derived from NCBI RefSeq GenBank XML CDS intervals for Escherichia coli K-12 MG1655 complete genome accession $NC\_000913.3$, with snapshot date $2026$-$05$-$30$. The frozen protocol was recorded before the external evidence was observed: the protocol time was $2026$-$05$-$29T19{:}11{:}13+00{:}00$, and the external evidence observation time was $2026$-$05$-$30T10{:}29{:}31+00{:}00$. The required row classes were present: boundary cases, matched non-ORF decoys, and positive curated ORFs. The classifier identifier was $orf\_start\_stop\_window\_eligibility$, and its rules were not modified during the contact.

\paragraph{Local interpretation.}
The finite quotient geometry supplies a compact, verified combinatorial coordinate system for the $20$-codon set and its $10$-row quotient. The curated ORF interval contact supplies a separate biological observation layer for interval eligibility, and the two parts must not be conflated. The quotient rows and face vectors do not imply translation, folded protein structure, thermodynamic or kinetic admissibility, molecular mechanism, organismal function, or a universal biological law. The curated interval contact also remains limited to its stated dataset and protocol: it supports ORF-eligibility classification for the observed interval records and matched controls, but it does not classify all possible nucleotide windows and does not turn start/stop syntax into evidence of translation or function. The self-check reports three checked gates and states the same boundary: codon geometry is not promoted across biological layers without the relevant independent contact.
