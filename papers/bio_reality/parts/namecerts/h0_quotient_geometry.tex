\paragraph{Local claim boundary.}
This chapter therefore records two different things and keeps them separated. First, the finite quotient geometry of $M$ is reproducible: the carrier, quotient rows, face vectors, motif rows, saturation rows, and empty boundary residual are all finite-row data. Second, a later curated-interval contact can test start/stop eligibility against external annotation rows, and in the recorded dataset it separates $72$ positives from $72$ matched decoys with the stated exact binomial values and stable held-out folds. Neither statement says that a quotient coordinate is an observed transcript, that an eligible interval is translated, that a protein folds, that a molecular structure is physically admissible, that a biological function occurs, that a mechanism has been established, or that a universal biological law follows. Those stronger layers require their own contacts and their own tests. The self-check run \texttt{0f47d2f0cf41} records the same boundary at the packet level: three gates were checked, and no codon geometry was promoted across biological layers.
