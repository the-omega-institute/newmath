$$
\begin{aligned}

Thus the row-level geometry is not merely a loose codon catalogue: it comes with a certified compression list, a saturation list, and quotient coordinates that agree with their declared finite cardinalities.

\paragraph{Local non-promotion rule.}
These numbers justify a finite eligibility statement only when the outside interval contact is present.  The quotient geometry by itself does not observe a transcript, does not prove that translation occurs, does not determine a folded molecular structure, does not establish physical admissibility, does not assign biological function, does not provide a mechanism, and does not state a universal biological law.  Even the curated interval contact is an annotation-level contact; it confirms that the frozen start/stop-window classifier separates the supplied positive intervals, matched decoys, and boundary cases in this finite dataset, while leaving translation, structure, physical realization, function, and generalization to other datasets outside the claim.  The self-check agrees with this boundary: it records three checked gates and states that no codon geometry is promoted across biological layers.
