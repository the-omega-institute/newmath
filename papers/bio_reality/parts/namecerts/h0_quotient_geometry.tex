\paragraph{ORF-eligibility boundary.}
The biological layer attached to this record is open-reading-frame eligibility from start/stop codon enumeration.  The quotient rows and codon lists may suggest a start/stop-window classifier, but the record itself contains no sequence interval, transcript observation, curated coding annotation, ribosome evidence, folding assay, physical model, functional assay, mechanistic account, or universality theorem.  Consequently, the local rule is strict: the quotient table may organize a proposed eligibility test, but it does not by itself establish translation, folded structure, physical admissibility, biological function, mechanism, or a general biological law.  Those stronger claims require a separate biological contact capable of testing the relevant layer.
