\paragraph{Local scientific boundary.}
The claim established here is therefore narrow.  The finite rows support a quotient geometry for the $20$-codon set $M$, a $10$-point quotient projection, and an ORF-eligibility screening relation when an appropriate curated interval contact is present.  They do not establish translation realization, folded protein structure, physical admissibility, molecular function, causal mechanism, or a universal biological law.  No such conclusion follows from codon geometry, quotient incidence counts, start/stop enumeration, matched decoy performance, or the curated interval row unless an additional contact supplies evidence at that specific biological layer.
