\subsection{Dynamic mass balance closes the measured RPF--protein route negatively}
\label{sec:bioreality-q6-rpf-protein-dynamic-mass-balance}

\paragraph{Finite surface.}
This chapter records the finite-row test in which $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinates are asked to pass through a measured translation surface before being read against matched protein abundance.  The carrier is the finite mediation surface with entries $(q_i,t_j,p_k)$, where $q_i$ is one of the nine residual coordinates, $t_j$ is a translation readout, and $p_k$ is a matched protein-abundance readout.  The declared biological object is therefore not a direct synonymous-residual to protein-abundance association.  It is the narrower question whether a residual coordinate can survive a translation step and then improve the downstream mass-balance description after amino-acid composition, organism, $GC3$, coding-sequence length, $M$-density baseline, and matched mRNA abundance are held as controls.

\paragraph{Dynamic row set.}
The executed dynamic closure test used $4434$ eligible genes before the fixed subsample rule and then fixed the analyzed panel at $120$ genes.  Across those genes the intervalized dynamic surface contained $718$ intervals.  The run identifier was $6a956431a53f$, and the recorded runtime was $441.440662$ seconds.  The fitted sequence of models was explicitly finite:
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{L0,M\text{-only}} &= 0.9960628201518571,\\
R^2_{L1,M+E_{\mathrm{stat}}} &= 0.996410628851051,\\
R^2_{L2} &= 0.9712443377743347,\\
R^2_{L3} &= 0.8953999568421264.
\end{aligned}
$$
The registered contrast from $L2$ to $L3$ was negative:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta R^2 &= -0.07584438093220836,\\
\Delta DL_{\mathrm{bits}} &= -668.8046070381928.
\end{aligned}
$$
The effective null checks also did not support promotion of the residual coordinates into a protein-realization claim: the translation-efficiency-bin null gave $p=0.6831683168316832$, and the time-shuffle null gave $p=0.9306930693069307$.

\paragraph{Readout discipline.}
The negative dynamic contrast is read locally as a mass-balance closure result, not as evidence that $B^*_{Q6}$ has no biological contact anywhere.  Other finite runs show that the same residual family can touch bounded lower-layer or adjacent readouts.  A modeled-tAI survival run computed nine coordinates, namely $K\_AAA$, $Arg\_AGR$, $Ile\_AUA$, $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$, $Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG$, $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$, $Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG$, $Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY$, and $f3\_stress$, on three organisms.  That run reported $81$ entries above the bounded coupling threshold $0.01$ and maximum absolute modeled coupling $3801961.031607896$, while also recording that $3$ organisms are insufficient for a powered survival claim requiring at least $40$ matched species.  A separate observable-execution run in \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae} used $3289$ genes and reached the finite verdict that at least one observed rate-proxy slot passed both effective null classes.  These facts preserve a translation-adjacent contact surface, but they do not overturn the dynamic mass-balance closure reported here.

\paragraph{Mediation context.}
The broader protein-abundance mediation audit supplies a useful comparison because it measured the finite $Q \to T \to P$ decomposition across twelve organisms using modeled per-protein tAI and PAXdb abundance.  Its mediated fractions were all in the unit interval, with values $0.6084594553474505$ for \emph{Bacillus subtilis}, $0.4872268357227724$ for \emph{Caenorhabditis elegans}, $0.3157053953614641$ for \emph{Danio rerio}, $0.6165167748739756$ for \emph{Drosophila melanogaster}, $0.612922252793667$ for \emph{Escherichia coli} K-12 MG1655, $0.1976903964724546$ for \emph{Gallus gallus}, $0.27206289160119157$ for \emph{Homo sapiens}, $0.15488799907323988$ for \emph{Mus musculus}, $0.08397602919861291$ for \emph{Mycobacterium smegmatis}, $0.17489681798543982$ for \emph{Rattus norvegicus}, $0.9491551041216973$ for \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}, and $0.01850668564589326$ for \emph{Sulfolobus solfataricus}.  The joined protein counts ranged from $1656$ proteins in \emph{Sulfolobus solfataricus} to $19395$ proteins in \emph{Mus musculus}; the yeast entry used $5815$ proteins.  That cross-sectional mediation surface is a statistical decomposition under modeled tAI, whereas the present chapter asks a different dynamic question: whether the RPF--protein mass-balance closure improves when the $B^*_{Q6}$ residual block is carried into the stronger dynamic setting.  The answer recorded by this run is no.

\paragraph{Closure statement.}
The admissible local conclusion is therefore a bounded negative.  In this finite dynamic panel, after the $M$-only, $M+E_{\mathrm{stat}}$, $L2$, and $L3$ fits are compared, the $B^*_{Q6}$ block does not improve the RPF--protein mass-balance description.  The observed decreases $\Delta R^2=-0.07584438093220836$ and $\Delta DL_{\mathrm{bits}}=-668.8046070381928$, together with $p_{TE\mathrm{bin}}=0.6831683168316832$ and $p_{\mathrm{timeshuffle}}=0.9306930693069307$, keep the claim at a closed current-data boundary rather than a positive protein-realization result.  A related turnover audit is consistent with this restraint: on $3290$ genes it reported $R^2_{L2}=0.055740851626933985$, $R^2_{L3}=0.050278872193227464$, $\Delta R^2=-0.005461979433706483$, $\Delta DL_{\mathrm{bits}}=-13.68819707902732$, $p_{\mathrm{matchedbin}}=0.8855721393034826$, and $p_{\mathrm{recoded}}=1.0$.

\paragraph{Local boundary.}
No stronger biological interpretation is licensed by this chapter.  The finite rows do not establish translation realization, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, mechanism, phenotype, selection, or a universal biological law.  A direct $Q_i \to P_k$ association is not accepted here unless the same coordinate first survives an explicit translation readout $Q_i \to T_j$, and the dynamic closure run gives a negative result for the stronger RPF--protein mass-balance route.  Any future positive statement at a higher layer must attach its own layer-matched contact and recompute the relevant controlled statistic on finite joined rows.
