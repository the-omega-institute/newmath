\subsection{Dynamic mass-balance closure for the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual packet}
\label{sec:bioreality-q6-dynamic-mass-balance-closure}

\paragraph{Finite surface.}
This record tests whether the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ synonymous-residual packet survives a stricter dynamic mass-balance audit after a translation-attached execution surface has already been made observable.  The finite table has $120$ genes and $718$ time or interval rows, selected from $4434$ eligible genes before the fixed subsample cap.  The candidate coordinates are not read as amino-acid sequence, organism identity, GC3, coding-sequence length, or $M$-density itself.  Those quantities remain controls or baseline regressors.  The tested object is the residual contribution left after the measured or modeled execution slots are placed in a nested mass-balance hierarchy.

\paragraph{Nested models.}
Let $M$ denote the baseline mass or abundance state, let $E_{\mathrm{stat}}$ denote the static execution summary, and let the deeper levels add the available dynamic translation and protein-closure features.  The observed nested fit values were
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2(L_0:M\text{ only}) &= 0.9960628201518571,\\
R^2(L_1:M+E_{\mathrm{stat}}) &= 0.996410628851051,\\
R^2(L_2) &= 0.9712443377743347,\\
R^2(L_3) &= 0.8953999568421264.
\end{aligned}
$$
The powered comparison therefore gives
$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta R^2 &= R^2(L_3)-R^2(L_2)\\
&= -0.07584438093220836,\\
\Delta \mathrm{DL}_{\mathrm{bits}} &= -668.8046070381928.
\end{aligned}
$$
The sign is the decisive local fact: the deeper dynamic closure layer did not improve held-out explanatory performance on this finite surface.  The registered effective-null checks agree with that reading, with $p_{\mathrm{TEbin}}=0.6831683168316832$ and $p_{\mathrm{timeshuffle}}=0.9306930693069307$.  These values place the mass-balance extension on the negative side of the powered decision rule rather than on the promotion side.

\paragraph{Relation to the translation-mediated abundance surface.}
The negative mass-balance result is not a denial that the same residual family can have bounded protein-abundance contacts under weaker, cross-sectional mediation summaries.  In the separate translation-mediated abundance table, the joint $9$-coordinate $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual block was evaluated across $12$ organisms with modeled per-protein tAI and PAXdb abundance.  The largest reported mediated fraction was the yeast value $M^{QTP}=0.9491551041216973$, with $R^2_{QP}=0.29837624994140827$, direct $R^2_{QP\mid T}=0.0151709093608293$, and indirect difference $0.28320534058057895$.  Other organism-level fractions were positive but smaller, including $0.6084594553474505$ in \emph{Bacillus subtilis}, $0.612922252793667$ in \emph{Escherichia coli} K-12 MG1655, $0.27206289160119157$ in human, $0.15488799907323988$ in mouse, and $0.01850668564589326$ in \emph{Sulfolobus solfataricus}.  Those numbers justify a bounded statistical mediation reading for modeled tAI and abundance rows; they do not override the dynamic mass-balance failure recorded here.

\paragraph{Execution slot context.}
A separate execution-decomposition audit found an observable execution subblock in \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae} with $3289$ genes, meaning at least one observed rate-proxy slot passed its effective null classes.  The present mass-balance test asks a narrower and harder question: after such execution slots are available, does adding the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ dynamic closure layer improve the finite mass-balance prediction?  On the $120$-gene, $718$-interval table, it does not.  The correct readback is therefore not a translation-realization claim, but a closure refusal for this particular dynamic endpoint.

\paragraph{Boundary against stronger biological conclusions.}
The local decision is deliberately conservative.  A nonpositive dynamic increment does not prove that the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ coordinates are biologically irrelevant, and a positive cross-sectional mediation fraction elsewhere does not prove a molecular mechanism.  No claim about translation dynamics, folded structure, physical admissibility, molecular function, organismal phenotype, selection, causation, or universality follows from this chapter unless another layer-matched measurement supplies that contact and passes its own controlled statistic.  Matched mRNA abundance remains a transcription-layer control; bulk protein abundance remains a downstream abundance readout; modeled tAI remains a codon-adaptation readout rather than observed ribosome motion.

\paragraph{Compatibility with other negative audits.}
The refusal is also consistent with other finite-row audits of downstream promotion.  The turnover-residual audit used $3290$ genes and reported $R^2(L_2)=0.055740851626933985$, $R^2(L_3)=0.050278872193227464$, $\Delta R^2=-0.005461979433706483$, and $\Delta \mathrm{DL}_{\mathrm{bits}}=-13.68819707902732$, with null checks $p_{\mathrm{matchedbin}}=0.8855721393034826$ and $p_{\mathrm{recoded}}=1.0$.  The cross-ortholog selection-imprint audit used $80$ orthogroups, $240$ rows, and $3$ species; it reported $\Delta R^2=0.03250515015613468$ but a leave-clade-out value of $-0.0021477729853302424$ and a null $95\%$ threshold of $0.05427157066522958$, so the strong selection-imprint gate was not supported.  These neighboring finite decisions reinforce the same discipline: a residual coordinate may be useful as a bounded statistical coordinate without being promoted to mechanism, selection, turnover, or dynamic protein-closure realization.

\paragraph{Verdict.}
For the dynamic mass-balance endpoint, the finite result is a powered negative.  The baseline model with $M$ is already extremely high at $R^2=0.9960628201518571$, the static execution addition only raises this to $0.996410628851051$, and the deeper dynamic levels reduce held-out fit to $0.9712443377743347$ and then $0.8953999568421264$.  With $\Delta R^2=-0.07584438093220836$, $\Delta \mathrm{DL}_{\mathrm{bits}}=-668.8046070381928$, $p_{\mathrm{TEbin}}=0.6831683168316832$, and $p_{\mathrm{timeshuffle}}=0.9306930693069307$, the chapter closes the current finite surface as a refusal of dynamic mass-balance promotion.  The $B^{*}_{Q6}$ packet remains admissible for bounded translation-readout and codon-residual analyses, but this table does not support entry into dynamic protein-closure fitting.
