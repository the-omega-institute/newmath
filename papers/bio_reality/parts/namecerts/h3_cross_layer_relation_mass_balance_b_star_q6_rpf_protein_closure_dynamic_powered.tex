\paragraph{Finite surface.}
This record tests whether the B$^*_Q6$ residual coordinates improve a dynamic mass-balance readout once the experiment is forced through matched translation and protein-abundance rows rather than through a direct residual-to-protein association. The finite surface has rows indexed by genes and intervals. Each row carries a coding-sequence residual coordinate $q_i$, a translation-side readout built from ribosome footprint or translation-efficiency information, a matched protein-abundance readout, and controls for amino-acid composition, organism identity, GC3, coding-sequence length, M-density baseline, and matched mRNA abundance. The admissible readback is therefore a mediated matrix of the form $Q_i \to T_j \to P_k$; a bare $Q_i \to P_k$ fit is not treated as protein realization unless the same coordinate survives the translation-readout stage.

\paragraph{Observed row counts.}
The dynamic closure run is finite and bounded. It began with $4434$ eligible genes before the fixed subsample rule, then evaluated $120$ genes after applying the cap of $120$ genes. These numbers matter because the verdict is not a symbolic promotion of codon geometry; it is a finite comparison of nested empirical models on a small, explicitly capped dynamic panel.

\paragraph{Local verdict.}
For \texttt{h3.cross\_layer\_relation.mass\_balance.b\_star\_q6\_rpf\_protein\_closure\_dynamic\_powered}, the proper readback is current-data closure at the dynamic mass-balance boundary, not protein-realization success. The experiment ran, the finite row counts are explicit, and the decisive scalar increments are nonpositive. The packet should therefore be reported as a bounded negative audit: B$^*_Q6$ residuals are not promoted from translation-readout or codon-count residual status into dynamic protein mass-balance realization on this evidence.

\paragraph{Boundary of interpretation.}
No translation mechanism, protein folding pathway, physical admissibility claim, molecular function, organismal phenotype, selective law, or universal biological rule follows from this chapter. A positive claim at any such layer would require a separate layer-matched contact and its own controlled statistic. In particular, a direct association between a residual coordinate and protein abundance is not sufficient here; the coordinate must first survive a controlled translation readout, and the downstream protein-abundance or mass-balance term must then improve under the matched controls. The registered dynamic run does not meet that stronger condition.
