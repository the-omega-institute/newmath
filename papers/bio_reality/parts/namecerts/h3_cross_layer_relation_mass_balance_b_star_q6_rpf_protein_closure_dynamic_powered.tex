\subsection{Dynamic mass-balance closure for the $B^*_{Q6}$ translation--protein route}
\label{sec:bior eality-q6-dynamic-mass-balance-closure}

\paragraph{Finite surface.}
This record treats the $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinates as a finite cross-layer mediation surface over triples $(q_i,t_j,p_k)$, where $q_i$ is one of the nine residual coordinates, $t_j$ is a translation-facing readout, and $p_k$ is a matched protein-abundance or mass-balance readout. The admissible coordinates are $K\_AAA$, $Arg\_AGR$, $Ile\_AUA$, $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$, $Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG$, $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$, $Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG$, $Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY$, and $f3\_stress$. The finite controls stay as controls: amino-acid composition, organism identity, $GC3$, coding-sequence length, the $M$-density baseline, and matched mRNA abundance are not promoted to residual coordinates. The admissible readback is therefore not a direct $q_i \to p_k$ assertion. A protein-side statement is allowed only after a translation-facing step has been evaluated and after the matched transcript and sequence-composition controls have been held fixed.

\paragraph{Mass-balance experiment.}
The dynamic mass-balance run used experiment id $6a956431a53f$ and fixed the powered subsample at $120$ genes from $4434$ eligible genes, giving $718$ fitted intervals. The four nested fits place the mass-only baseline at $R^2=0.9960628201518571$, the mass plus estimated translation layer at $R^2=0.996410628851051$, the next residual layer at $R^2=0.9712443377743347$, and the full $B^*_{Q6}$-augmented layer at $R^2=0.8953999568421264$. Thus the registered increment from the comparison layer to the final layer is negative: $\Delta R^2=-0.07584438093220836$ and $\Delta DL=-668.8046070381928$ bits. The two effective-null checks also fail to support promotion: the translation-efficiency-bin null gives $p=0.6831683168316832$, and the time-shuffle null gives $p=0.9306930693069307$. In compact form, the observed finite row is
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{M} &= 0.9960628201518571,\\
R^2_{M,E} &= 0.996410628851051,\\
R^2_{L2} &= 0.9712443377743347,\\
R^2_{L3} &= 0.8953999568421264,\\
\Delta R^2 &= -0.07584438093220836,\\
\Delta DL &= -668.8046070381928.
\end{aligned}
$$
The readback is therefore a closed negative result for dynamic mass-balance promotion: the registered $B^*_{Q6}$ layer does not improve the finite mass-balance model under the declared powered design.

\paragraph{Relation to the positive translation--protein mediation surface.}
The negative mass-balance result does not erase the separate bounded mediation row. In the multi-organism $q_i \to T \to P$ protein-abundance decomposition, the same nine-coordinate residual system was joined to measured PAXdb abundance and modeled per-protein tAI across twelve organisms. Those rows had sufficient residual rank after the $24$-column control design. The joint mediation fractions were finite and positive but heterogeneous: $M^{QTP}=0.9491551041216973$ in \textit{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}, $0.612922252793667$ in \textit{Escherichia coli} K12 MG1655, $0.6165167748739756$ in \textit{Drosophila melanogaster}, $0.4872268357227724$ in \textit{Caenorhabditis elegans}, $0.27206289160119157$ in human, $0.15488799907323988$ in mouse, and $0.01850668564589326$ in \textit{Sulfolobus solfataricus}. The yeast entry had $c=R^2_{QP}=0.29837624994140827$, $c'=R^2_{QP\mid T}=0.0151709093608293$, and indirect increment $0.28320534058057895$; the human entry had $c=0.04454565118019448$, $c'=0.03242643251185274$, and indirect increment $0.012119218668341744$. These numbers justify a bounded statistical mediation statement for the modeled-tAI surface, not a dynamic mass-balance claim.

\paragraph{Why the closure is negative.}
The dynamic mass-balance row asks a stricter question than cross-sectional modeled-tAI mediation. It asks whether the residual layer improves a time-indexed or interval-indexed mass-balance closure once the mass and translation estimates already explain nearly all variation. Since $R^2_M$ is already $0.9960628201518571$ and the final registered layer drops to $0.8953999568421264$, the finite row points away from a useful residual correction in this design. The negative description-length increment, $-668.8046070381928$ bits, is treated as decisive for this packet because both null comparisons also remain nonsignificant. The appropriate conclusion is not that the residual coordinates are biologically empty; it is that this particular powered mass-balance route is closed as non-promoting.

\paragraph{Neighboring finite contacts.}
Other contacts locate the boundary. The observable-execution decomposition in yeast ran on $3289$ genes and concluded that at least one observed rate-proxy slot formed an observable execution subblock. The structural-order identity run over five folds found the dominant axis to be $f3\_stress$, with weighted held-out increment $0.0031744502294756994$ and sign-flip $p=0.03125$. The evolutionary-selection-imprint run, however, stayed below its strong gate on $80$ orthogroups and $240$ rows: $\Delta R^2=0.03250515015613468$, leave-clade-out $\Delta R^2=-0.0021477729853302424$, and maximum null-$95$ $\Delta R^2=0.05427157066522958$, so the positive selection-imprint flag is false. The turnover audit likewise refuses promotion beyond the current data domain: with $3290$ genes, $R^2_{L2}=0.055740851626933985$, $R^2_{L3}=0.050278872193227464$, $\Delta R^2=-0.005461979433706483$, $\Delta DL=-13.68819707902732$ bits, $p_{matched\ bin}=0.8855721393034826$, and $p_{recoded}=1.0$. These neighboring rows agree with the mass-balance verdict: $B^*_{Q6}$ can have bounded translation-facing and protein-abundance decompositions, but the dynamic closure route tested here does not gain support.

\paragraph{Local scope boundary.}
This chapter does not claim translation realization from sequence geometry alone. It does not claim folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, organismal phenotype, mechanism, adaptation, causation, or universality. Modeled tAI is a bounded translation-facing annotation, not observed ribosome occupancy. PAXdb abundance is a protein-abundance measurement surface, not a functional assay or a folding assay. Matched mRNA abundance is a control layer, not a translation or protein readout by itself. A direct residual-to-protein association is not admissible here unless the residual first survives a controlled translation readout, and even then the resulting statement remains a statistical mediation or bounded mass-balance statement unless a separate layer-matched measurement supplies the next contact.

\paragraph{Verdict.}
The finite-row verdict is ready and negative for the named dynamic mass-balance claim. The powered experiment completed, the row counts and nested fits are concrete, and the null checks do not support promotion. The correct present-tense statement is: $B^*_{Q6}$ remains available as a controlled residual coordinate system with bounded translation-facing and protein-abundance decompositions, while the dynamic mass-balance closure tested in experiment $6a956431a53f$ is closed against positive promotion because the registered final layer decreases fit and fails both null comparisons.
