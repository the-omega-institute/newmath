\subsection{Mass balance at the $B^\ast_{Q6}$ translation-protein boundary}
\label{subsec:h3-cross-layer-relation-mass-balance-b-star-q6-rpf-protein-closure-dynamic-powered}

\paragraph{Finite regression panel.}
The mass-balance row is evaluated on a finite panel with $4434$ eligible genes before subsampling, a fixed cap of $120$ genes, and $718$ intervals. The simplest mass-only baseline already explains almost all variance: $R^2_{L0}=0.9960628201518571$, while the mass-plus-Estat baseline remains similar at $R^2_{L1}=0.996410628851051$. The richer closures then deteriorate: $R^2_{L2}=0.9712443377743347$ and $R^2_{L3}=0.8953999568421264$. Written as a finite comparison,
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{L0} &= 0.9960628201518571, \\
R^2_{L1} &= 0.996410628851051, \\
R^2_{L2} &= 0.9712443377743347, \\
R^2_{L3} &= 0.8953999568421264, \\
\Delta R^2 &= -0.07584438093220836.
\end{aligned}
$$
The description-length shift is likewise unfavorable, with $\Delta \mathrm{DL}=-668.8046070381928$. The two finite null checks stay unremarkable: $p_{\mathrm{TEbin}}=0.6831683168316832$ and $p_{\mathrm{timeshuffle}}=0.9306930693069307$. This is a bounded fit result, not a closure upgrade.

\paragraph{Coordinate class.}
The $B^\ast_{Q6}$ coordinate bundle is itself finite and structured. The companion axis audit names nine coordinates: $\mathrm{K\_AAA}$, $\mathrm{Arg\_AGR}$, $\mathrm{Ile\_AUA}$, $\mathrm{Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR}$, $\mathrm{Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG}$, $\mathrm{Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY}$, $\mathrm{Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG}$, $\mathrm{Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY}$, and $\mathrm{f3\_stress}$. That audit singles out $\mathrm{f3\_stress}$ as the unique dominant increment and loading axis, with sign-flip $p=0.03125$, weighted held-out $R^2=0.0031744502294756994$, and the conclusion structural\_order\_carried\_by\_f3\_stress. By contrast, the other coordinates remain smaller: $\mathrm{Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR}$ has weighted held-out $R^2=-0.0003205780368125179$, and $\mathrm{Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY}$ has $-0.00045340330387383447$.

\paragraph{Translation-layer context.}
The translation side is not empty, but it also stays bounded. In the modeled translation-survival panel, the $B^\ast_{Q6}$ coordinate set above is paired with a finite readout surface that reports $81$ entries above threshold $0.01$, with $\max |S_{ij}|=3801961.031607896$ and the same value on the diagonal. The same record also says that three organisms are insufficient for powered survival, so the contact is a bounded translation-readout test rather than a universal translation law. The local tRNA payload is complete for human and yeast and nearly complete for E. coli K12, with matched counts $(432,432,20,0)$ for Homo sapiens, $(275,275,20,0)$ for Saccharomyces cerevisiae, and $(89,87,20,2)$ for Escherichia coli K12 in the order total headers, matched headers, standard amino-acid families, and unmatched headers.

\paragraph{Mediation against abundance.}
A separate cross-organism mediation panel keeps the translation-to-protein route finite and controlled. Across twelve organisms, the mediation fraction $M^{QTP}$ always remains in the unit interval, for example $0.6084594553474505$ in Bacillus subtilis, $0.4872268357227724$ in Caenorhabditis elegans, $0.3157053953614641$ in Danio rerio, $0.6165167748739756$ in Drosophila melanogaster, $0.612922252793667$ in Escherichia coli K12, $0.27206289160119157$ in Homo sapiens, $0.9491551041216973$ in Saccharomyces cerevisiae, and $0.01850668564589326$ in Sulfolobus solfataricus. The organism-level panels range from $1656$ proteins in Sulfolobus to $19395$ in Mus musculus, with $19053$ in Homo sapiens and $5815$ in yeast. In yeast, the mediated partition is substantial but still statistical: $c_{\mathrm{total}}=0.29837624994140827$, $c'_{\mathrm{direct}}=0.0151709093608293$, and $c_{\mathrm{indirect}}=0.28320534058057895$. This reads as a mediation decomposition, not as proof of a molecular mechanism.

\paragraph{Auxiliary structure residualization.}
A separate yeast residualization panel joins $4759$ CDS rows, with reported join hit rates of $0.9156038419146592$ and $0.8818572656921754$. There, $R^2_{QP}=0.2715520808117803$, $R^2_{QP\mid S}=0.2706601505000995$, $R^2_{QP\mid T,M}=0.13773967696311287$, and $R^2_{QP\mid T,M,S}=0.13759011208318542$; the structure-only fraction is $0.003284564452662019$ and the structure-incremental fraction is $0.0005507778820193332$. The structure side therefore stays a small residual partition, with permutation $p$-values $0.027972027972027972$ and $0.1258741258741259$, rather than a substitute for the mass-balance test.

\paragraph{Interpretation boundary.}
The right reading of this chapter is conservative. The finite rows support a bounded statistical statement: the added dynamic layer does not improve the mass-balance fit, the translation-readout contact is nonzero but underpowered for a universal survival claim, and the protein-abundance mediation panel stays within controlled regression geometry. Nothing in these rows justifies a jump to translation mechanism, folded structure, physical admissibility, biological function, or universality. Those higher-level claims would require a distinct contact at that layer, with its own matched observations and its own finite test.
