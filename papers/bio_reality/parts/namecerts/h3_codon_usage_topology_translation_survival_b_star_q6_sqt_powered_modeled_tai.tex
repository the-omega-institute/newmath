\subsection{Modeled tAI survival for the nine $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates}
\label{sec:bior reality-b-star-q6-modeled-tai-survival}

\paragraph{Finite row and coordinate system.}
This record tests whether the nine $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ synonymous-residual coordinates retain translation-layer explanatory power after the amino-acid quotient and the declared covariates have already been imposed.  The finite carrier is a $9\times 9$ survival surface $S^{QT}$ whose row coordinates are
$K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $Leu_{CUN/UUR}$, $Leu_{UUA/UUG}$, $Ser_{UCR/AGY}$, $Ser_{UCA/UCG}$, $Thr_{ACR/ACY}$, and $f3$-stress.  The readout side uses the same nine residual directions after projecting modeled per-codon tAI weights, computed from GtRNAdb anticodon copy counts with dos Reis wobble weights, into the synonymous residual coordinate space.  The joined panel contains $40$ organism rows.  The control design has $24$ columns, numerical rank $23$, and $17$ residual degrees of freedom after residualizing by amino-acid composition, organism context, GC3, coding-sequence length, and the $M$-density baseline.

\paragraph{Statistic.}
Let $Q$ denote the $40\times 9$ matrix of residual codon-usage coordinates and let $T$ denote the $40\times 9$ modeled tAI readout matrix in the same residual coordinate frame.  With $Z$ the declared control matrix, the tested surface is obtained by replacing both sides by their residuals after projection away from $Z$ and then fitting the remaining translation readout energy by the remaining $Q$ directions.  In this finite row the rank diagnostics are
$$
\begin{aligned}
 n_{org} &= 40,\\
 \operatorname{rank}(Z) &= 23,\\
 \operatorname{rank}(\widetilde Q) &= 9,\\
 df_Z &= 17,\\
 \dim(S^{QT}) &= 9\times 9 .
\end{aligned}
$$
The survival fit reports
$$
\begin{aligned}
 R^2_{QT} &= 0.9003849000036579,\\
 \tau_{+} &= 10^{-12},\\
 \tau_{deg} &= 0.999999 .
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the observed modeled-tAI survival is far above the positive-entry tolerance while remaining below the declared degeneracy ceiling.  The computation is therefore not the three-row saturated surface that the earlier refusal records warned against; it is a rank-powered $40$-organism finite row with a full nine-dimensional residual design after controls.

\paragraph{Interpretation of the positive modeled translation contact.}
The result says that the amino-acid-composition, organism, GC3, length, and $M$-density baselines do not absorb the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual geometry when the readout is modeled tAI in the declared coordinate frame.  Equivalently, the finite matrix has positive controlled translation-readout survival in the modeled-tAI sense: at least one residual direction improves the residual description of the modeled tAI surface after the controls have already been paid for.  The statement is deliberately about modeled codon-adaptation weights, not about observed ribosome motion.  Its evidential content is a controlled cross-organism codon-usage/tRNA-copy readout, with $R^2_{QT}=0.9003849000036579$ on a $40$-organism panel, not a claim about measured elongation speed, ribosome occupancy, translation efficiency from RPF/mRNA, or protein output.

\paragraph{Relation to the smaller proxy coupling row.}
A separate three-organism proxy row computed a coupling matrix against a tRNA-Leu CUN-vs-UUR log-copy spectrum.  That row had $61$ codon-count coordinates, all nine $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ synonymous residuals present, $9$ nonzero coupling entries, and maximum absolute coupling
$$
\begin{aligned}
 \max_{i,j}|A_{ij}| &= 1882464.1106377328,
\end{aligned}
$$
with the break threshold set at $0.01$.  The break condition was not met, but that earlier contact was only a proxy coupling contact; it explicitly did not provide $S_{ij}$ survival.  The present $40$-organism modeled-tAI row is stronger in rank and in readout geometry because it computes the controlled $S^{QT}$ surface directly, but it still remains bounded to modeled tAI rather than measured ribosome profiling.

\paragraph{Leucine sign context.}
The CUN/UUR leucine finite row gives a useful warning about directionality.  In the three-organism leucine table, the partial Pearson value was $1.0$ for $n=3$, while the organism-level log-ratio signs were $(1,-1,1)$, so sign uniformity was false.  Consequently, the modeled-tAI survival row should be read as a positive controlled survival statement for the finite $40$-organism panel, not as a universal positive or negative direction attached to any one codon family.  A positive $S^{QT}$ entry reports residual explanatory survival; it does not turn the sign of a coordinate into a global biological rule.

\paragraph{Downstream comparisons that remain separate.}
Other finite rows show that $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residuals can also be projected against downstream readouts, but those rows do not change the meaning of the modeled-tAI result.  In the translation-complement abundance analysis, the modeled-tAI-complement fraction $d$ was $0.874628721049266$ for danio rerio, $0.6629960038578532$ for Escherichia coli K12 MG1655, $0.9400341806302775$ for Gallus gallus, $0.9208949355679455$ for Homo sapiens, $0.8972672018746489$ for Mycobacterium smegmatis strain mc2 155, and $0.21173219572706703$ for Saccharomyces cerevisiae.  The same analysis reported corresponding $R^2_{QP}$ values $0.04613529805760884$, $0.10767526667768725$, $0.03980793595345331$, $0.04454565117192484$, $0.032090091625816136$, and $0.29837624987287115$.  These are abundance-side projections, not evidence that modeled tAI is a measured translation mediator.

For yeast mRNA half-life, the codon-optimality row joined $5085$ proteins and found $R^2_{QM}=0.05179429044975085$, $R^2_{QP}=0.2761140373962526$ on the same join, cosine $0.8040935468384264$ between the $S^{QM}$ and $S^{QP}$ surfaces, and a permutation $p$-value of $0.000999000999000999$ for $1000$ permutations; the dominant mRNA-stability coordinate was $Leu_{CUN/UUR}$.  This supports a separate mRNA-stability projection, not translation realization.  In yeast protein-abundance condition rows, $R^2_{QP}=0.3280825603424665$ in YEPD and $0.3050385538305022$ in SD, both with $2209$ shared proteins and dominant coordinate $f3$-stress; the YEPD--SD surface cosine was $0.9993775935182967$ and Pearson similarity was $0.9983880256003688$ over $1000$ permutations.  In human tissue protein-abundance rows, Adult B cells, heart, and liver had $n=9244$, $6468$, and $8664$, with $R^2_{QP}=0.03626117434944674$, $0.02320896321347296$, and $0.026133456228248052$, and dominant coordinates $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ser_{UCR/AGY}$, and $Ile_{AUA}$ respectively.  These finite rows show that the coordinate system has several downstream statistical interfaces, but each interface is its own contact and none substitutes for measured ribosome evidence.

\paragraph{Scope restriction.}
The modeled-tAI survival row licenses only the following bounded claim: after the declared controls, the nine $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates explain a large finite fraction of modeled tAI readout energy across $40$ organisms, with $R^2_{QT}=0.9003849000036579$ and nondegenerate residual rank.  It does not show measured translation efficiency, ribosome occupancy, elongation speed, protein abundance, folding, structural order, physical admissibility, molecular function, pathway mechanism, phenotype, or a universal biological law.  Those statements require their own organism-matched contacts and their own controlled statistics.  In particular, tRNA gene-copy and modeled wobble-weight readbacks are not tRNA abundance, tRNA charging, RPF density, proteomics, or functional assay data.  The chapter therefore records a strong modeled translation-readout survival surface, while keeping measured translation, protein, structure, function, mechanism, and universality outside the conclusion.
