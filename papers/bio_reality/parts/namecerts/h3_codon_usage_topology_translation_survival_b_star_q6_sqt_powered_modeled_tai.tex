\subsection{Rank-powered modeled-tAI survival for the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates}
\label{sec:bioreality-b-star-q6-modeled-tai-survival}

\paragraph{Finite contact.}
The modeled-tAI contact tests whether the nine $B^{*}_{Q6}$ synonymous-residual coordinates retain explanatory mass after quotienting by amino-acid composition and by coarse sequence-context controls.  The finite row is the experiment with run identifier $09209aa516a0$.  It uses $40$ organism-level rows and computes the residual coordinate panel
$K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $Leu_{CUN/UUR}$, $Leu_{UUA/UUG}$, $Ser_{UCR/AGY}$, $Ser_{UCA/UCG}$, $Thr_{ACR/ACY}$, and $f_{3}$-stress.  The modeled translation-weight panel has the same nine named columns, obtained from per-codon tAI weights derived from GtRNAdb anticodon gene-copy counts with dos Reis wobble weights, then projected onto the same synonymous-residual coordinate system.  Thus the computed surface is a $9 \times 9$ $S^{QT}$ matrix rather than a single hand-picked contrast.

\paragraph{Controlled residualization.}
Let $Q$ denote the $40 \times 9$ matrix of $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates, let $T$ denote the $40 \times 9$ modeled-tAI residual readout matrix, and let $Z$ collect the declared controls: amino-acid composition, organism context, GC3, length, and the simpler codon-density baseline.  The recorded control design has $24$ columns and rank $23$, leaving $17$ residual degrees of freedom after projecting away $Z$.  The residualized coordinate block has rank $9$, so the test is not the three-row saturated surface that would make every controlled statistic vacuous.  In the notation of this chapter, the finite computation is
$$
\begin{aligned}
\widetilde Q &= (I-P_Z)Q,\\
\widetilde T &= (I-P_Z)T,\\
\operatorname{rank}(Z) &= 23,\\
\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde Q) &= 9,\\
\operatorname{df}_{\perp Z} &= 17.
\end{aligned}
$$
The positive-entry threshold recorded for partial $R^2$ is $10^{-12}$, so survival is not being inferred merely from a nonzero symbolic coefficient; it is evaluated as residual explanatory improvement after the stated controls and baseline ablation.

\paragraph{Observed finite statistic.}
The computed modeled translation surface has total controlled explanatory strength
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{QT} = 0.9003849000036579.
\end{aligned}
$$
The associated rank check records organism count $40$, residualized coordinate rank $9$, control rank $23$, and residual degrees of freedom $17$, against a degeneracy cutoff $0.999999$.  This places the run on the powered side of the rank boundary: the calculation is still finite and organism-level, but it is not the earlier $n=3$ all-correlation artefact.  The chapter therefore records a positive modeled-readout survival result for the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ panel against projected tAI weights under the declared controls.

\paragraph{Relation to the proxy coupling row.}
A separate coupling computation, run $4a783972e74b$, supplies a weaker codon-usage/tRNA-copy proxy contact.  It computed all nine synonymous residual coordinates in a $61$-codon count space and found that the break condition $|A_{ij}|<0.01$ for every coordinate was not met.  The maximum absolute proxy coupling was $1882464.1106377328$, with $9$ nonzero entries above the stated $0.01$ coupling threshold.  That row justifies keeping the translation-readout direction under test, but it is not itself the survival statistic used here.  The present chapter relies on the modeled-tAI $S^{QT}$ run for survival, while the proxy row remains only a supporting indication that the residual coordinates are not numerically silent against a tRNA-copy spectrum.

\paragraph{Leucine finite-row warning.}
The CUN/UUR leucine gate, run $675fdddb3187$, gives a useful caution about directionality.  In the three-organism row for Homo sapiens, Saccharomyces cerevisiae, and Escherichia coli K12, the reported partial Pearson value is $1.0$, but the log-ratio signs are $1,-1,1$ with values $1.3514980212896148$, $-0.24869078488829677$, and $1.0086640520051702$.  Hence the sign is explicitly not uniform across organisms.  This warning is carried into the $40$-organism modeled-tAI result: a positive $S^{QT}$ surface says that the residual coordinate panel survives a controlled modeled translation-weight readout; it does not license a universal sign law for any individual codon-family contrast.

\paragraph{Local interpretation.}
The ready claim is narrow.  After removing the declared $Z$ controls, the nine-coordinate $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual panel retains positive modeled-tAI explanatory content, with $R^2_{QT}=0.9003849000036579$ on a $40$-organism matrix and a nondegenerate residual design.  Equivalently, the modeled tAI readout is not completely absorbed by amino-acid composition, organism context, GC3, length, and the simpler codon-density baseline.  This is a translation-weight survival statement at the modeled codon-adaptation layer, not a direct ribosome-occupancy measurement.

\paragraph{Boundary of the claim.}
The same finite row does not establish measured translation efficiency, ribosome traffic, protein abundance, folding, physical admissibility, biological function, molecular mechanism, phenotype, or a universal biological law.  Modeled tAI from tRNA gene-copy counts and wobble weights is not measured elongation speed, not ribosome profiling, not proteomics, and not a protein-realization assay.  Any move from this $S^{QT}$ result to measured translation, to $S^{QP}$ protein abundance, or to a mediated $M^{QTP}$ chain requires a separate organism-matched contact and its own controlled statistic.  The result is therefore recorded as a ready modeled-translation survival entry, with all higher biological layers left unclaimed.
