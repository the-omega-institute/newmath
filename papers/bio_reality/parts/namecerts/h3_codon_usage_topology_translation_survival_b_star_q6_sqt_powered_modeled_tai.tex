\subsection{Rank-powered modeled tAI survival of the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual panel}
\label{sec:bioreality-b-star-q6-modeled-tai-survival}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Question fixed by the finite row.}
This record tests whether the nine-coordinate $B^{*}_{Q6}$ synonymous-residual panel is absorbed by ordinary sequence and context covariates when the readout is a modeled codon-adaptation surface.  The finite carrier is a $9 \times 9$ survival matrix $S^{QT}$, with rows
$K\_AAA$, $Arg\_AGR$, $Ile\_AUA$, $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$, $Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG$, $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$, $Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG$, $Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY$, and $f3\_stress$.
The readout side uses the same nine named modeled-tAI columns, computed from GtRNAdb anticodon gene-copy counts with dos Reis wobble weights and then projected onto the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ synonymous-residual coordinate system.  The fitted panel covers $40$ organisms, with $24$ control columns in $Z$, $\operatorname{rank}(Z)=23$, residual degrees of freedom $17$, and $\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde X)=9$ after residualization.  The controls include amino-acid composition, organism context, GC3, CDS length, and the $M$-density baseline, so the row is not a raw codon-count association.

\paragraph{Statistic.}
For each residual coordinate $q_i$ and modeled-tAI readout $r_j$, the experiment compares the residual readout against the residualized $B^{*}_{Q6}$ design after removing $Z$.  In matrix form the finite check is summarized by
$$
\begin{aligned}
S^{QT}_{ij} &= S(q_i,r_j\mid Z),\\
\dim(S^{QT}) &= 9\times 9,\\
R^2_{QT} &= 0.9003849000036579,\\
\tau_{+} &= 10^{-12}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The degeneracy guard for the powered row is $0.999999$, and the observed $R^2_{QT}$ is below that guard while the residualized design has full nine-coordinate rank.  Thus the row is treated as a powered modeled-readout survival result rather than the earlier three-organism rank-saturated shape.  The organism panel contains representative eukaryotic and bacterial rows, including $homo\_sapiens$, $arabidopsis\_thaliana$, $danio\_rerio$, $saccharomyces\_cerevisiae$, $escherichia\_coli$, $gallus\_gallus$, several $burkholderia$ rows, several $mycobacterium$ rows, and $anabaena\_variabilis\_atcc\_29413$.

\paragraph{Finite verdict.}
The modeled-tAI matrix is positive in the only sense asserted here: after the declared controls and baseline ablation, the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual panel retains substantial explanatory mass on the modeled adaptation readout, with $R^2_{QT}=0.9003849000036579$ on the $9\times 9$ surface.  The finite row therefore supports the bounded statement that $B^{*}_{Q6}$ is not fully absorbed by the amino-acid, context, GC3, length, and $M$-density description when the readout is modeled per-codon tAI.  A companion organism sweep computed modeled tAI for $41$ organisms and reached the target panel of $40$, with per-organism $R^2_{QT}$ values ranging from $0.1579271837024072$ to $0.8213457220900503$, mean $0.38466555440501393$, median $0.33934845811598124$, lower quartile $0.26353954700074694$, and upper quartile $0.5143951545826744$ under $1000$ permutations.  This sweep supports the rank-powered character of the modeled-tAI interface, but it remains descriptive because the organisms are not phylogenetically independent experimental replicates.

\paragraph{Relation to weaker and adjacent rows.}
The same coordinate family had an earlier proxy coupling contact against a tRNA-Leu CUN-versus-UUR log-copy spectrum in only three organisms.  That proxy row reported $9$ nonzero entries and maximum absolute coupling $1882464.1106377328$ above the $0.01$ threshold, but it explicitly did not supply $S_{ij}$ survival.  A separate three-organism Leu CUN/UUR gate had partial Pearson $1.0$ with signs $(1,-1,1)$ for log-ratio entries $1.3514980212896148$, $-0.24869078488829677$, and $1.0086640520051702$; that row is kept as a finite contact, not as a universal direction claim.  Downstream protein-abundance decompositions also remain separate: for the modeled-tAI complement of the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ abundance component, the complement fraction $d$ was $0.9208949355679455$ in human, $0.21173219572706703$ in yeast, $0.6629960038578532$ in E. coli K-12 MG1655, $0.874628721049266$ in zebrafish, $0.9400341806302775$ in chicken, and $0.8972672018746489$ in $mycobacterium\_smegmatis\_str\_mc2\_155$.  Those $d$ values describe residual abundance geometry relative to modeled tAI; they do not reclassify the modeled-tAI survival row as a protein-realization result.

\paragraph{Boundary of interpretation.}
The readout in this row is modeled tAI from tRNA gene-copy annotations and wobble weights.  It is not ribosome profiling, not measured elongation speed, not ribosome occupancy, not proteomics, and not protein folding.  The positive $S^{QT}$ surface also does not establish a gene-level causal mechanism, a pathway rule, a folded structural state, physical admissibility, biological function, phenotype, or a universal biological law.  Those stronger claims require separate row-matched contacts.  For example, the local ribosome-occupancy contact in yeast reported a position-bin-controlled descriptive signal for $K\_AAA$, $Arg\_AGR$, and $f3\_stress$ under a $200$-permutation within-bin null and a $\lambda_{DL}=0.01$ penalty with $DL=2$, but that is a distinct measured-occupancy row.  Likewise, direction-aware non-translation readout ranking used a stated $\lambda=0.01$ and $DL=\log_2(1+n_{col})$ regularizer and ranked organism-specific candidates such as measured TE, mRNA, PTM density, localization, and complex membership; those rankings do not change what this modeled-tAI chapter is allowed to claim.

\paragraph{Local conclusion.}
Within its own measurement boundary, the finite result is a powered modeled-translation-readout survival statement: the nine $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates retain a nonzero, high-rank, $Z$-controlled relation to modeled codon adaptation across the declared organism panel.  The result is ready for inclusion as a BioReality chapter only with the readout qualifier kept explicit: it supports modeled $Q\to T$ survival, while measured translation, protein abundance mediation, folding, physical admissibility, function, mechanism, phenotype, and universality remain outside this row unless supplied by their own matched contacts and statistics.
