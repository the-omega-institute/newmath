\subsection{Rank-powered modeled tAI survival for the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual panel}
\label{subsec:h3-codon-usage-topology-translation-survival-b-star-q6-sqt-powered-modeled-tai}

\paragraph{Finite row and readout.}
The record tests whether the nine synonymous-residual coordinates of $B^{*}_{Q6}$ retain explanatory mass against a modeled translation-layer readout after quotienting away the simpler compositional and context variables. The finite table contains $40$ organism rows. Its readout is a modeled per-codon tAI construction from GtRNAdb anticodon copy counts together with the dos Reis wobble weights, then projected onto the same nine synonymous-residual coordinates used on the codon-usage side. The coordinate list is
$$
\begin{aligned}
&K_{AAA},\; Arg_{AGR},\; Ile_{AUA},\; Leu_{CUN/UUR},\; Leu_{UUA/UUG},\\
&Ser_{UCR/AGY},\; Ser_{UCA/UCG},\; Thr_{ACR/ACY},\; f3_{stress}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The modeled tAI readout panel has the same nine coordinate columns, so the evaluated survival object is a $9\times 9$ matrix $S^{QT}$ indexed by residual codon coordinates on one side and projected modeled-tAI readouts on the other.

\paragraph{Controlled residualization.}
The control block $Z$ contains amino-acid composition, organism context, $GC3$, CDS length, and the $M$-density baseline. In the verified finite run, the control design had $24$ columns and rank $23$. After residualizing against $Z$, the remaining degrees of freedom were $17$. The powered residual design retained rank $9$, matching the number of $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates rather than collapsing to a lower-dimensional artifact. Thus the computation is not merely reporting raw codon composition, raw tRNA copy number, or an organism label effect; it is reporting the residual relationship after the declared quotient and baseline ablation have been imposed.

\paragraph{Survival statistic.}
Let $\widetilde Q$ denote the $Z$-residualized matrix of the nine $B^{*}_{Q6}$ coordinates and let $\widetilde T$ denote the corresponding $Z$-residualized modeled-tAI readout matrix. The run computes the rank-powered survival matrix $S^{QT}$ from the residual association between these two panels. The reported aggregate coefficient was
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^{2}_{QT} &= 0.9003849000036579.
\end{aligned}
$$
The degeneracy cutoff for treating the powered relation as rank-saturated was $0.999999$, and the observed value is below that cutoff. The positive-entry threshold for partial $R^{2}$ survival was $10^{-12}$. Since the matrix shape is $9\times 9$ and the residual design rank is $9$, the finite result supports a modeled translation-readout survival statement at the declared boundary: the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual panel is not absorbed by the amino-acid, organism, $GC3$, length, and $M$-density controls when the readout is the projected modeled-tAI panel.

\paragraph{Relation to proxy coupling checks.}
A separate three-organism proxy contact computed all nine $B^{*}_{Q6}$ synonymous residuals against a tRNA-copy-derived Leu spectrum. That proxy coupling check reported $9$ nonzero entries, maximum absolute coupling $1882464.1106377328$, and did not meet the null condition that all nine couplings have magnitude below $0.01$. Another finite Leu $CUN/UUR$ row over Homo sapiens, Saccharomyces cerevisiae, and Escherichia coli K12 reported Pearson $1.0$ for the three-row partial contact, with log-ratio signs $1,-1,1$, so its direction was explicitly not uniform across organisms. These two checks are useful consistency pressure on the codon-usage and tRNA-copy interface, but they are not substituted for the controlled $S^{QT}$ computation. The present chapter uses them only to mark that the modeled-tAI survival calculation sits beyond a mere proxy-coupling sign report.

\paragraph{Interpretation.}
The supported claim is narrow. In this finite $40$-organism modeled-readout table, the nine-coordinate $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual panel has positive controlled survival against a projected modeled-tAI readout, with $R^{2}_{QT}=0.9003849000036579$, residual degrees of freedom $17$, control rank $23$, and powered residual rank $9$. This is a translation-realization-modeled result, not a measured ribosome-profiling result. The modeled tAI values come from tRNA gene-copy annotations and wobble weights; they are not direct measurements of elongation speed, ribosome occupancy, ribosome density, protein abundance, folding, molecular function, or organismal phenotype.

\paragraph{Local no-promotion rule.}
Nothing in this finite record establishes measured translation efficiency, protein output, folded structure, physical admissibility, biochemical mechanism, biological function, phenotype, or a universal biological law. A ribosome-profiling, proteomics, functional-assay, perturbation, structural, or physical-admissibility conclusion would require its own matched contact and its own controlled statistic. In particular, tRNA gene-copy counts are not treated as tRNA abundance or charging, a positive modeled $S^{QT}$ entry is not promoted to protein abundance, and no sign from the finite proxy rows is read as population-level directionality. The result is therefore confined to the modeled translation-readout interface: after the declared controls, the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates retain measurable description-length or partial-$R^{2}$ value against projected modeled tAI.
