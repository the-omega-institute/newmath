\subsection{Modeled tAI survival of the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates}
\label{sec:bioreality-b-star-q6-modeled-tai-survival}

\paragraph{Finite object.}
This record concerns the finite matrix obtained by pairing nine $B^{*}_{Q6}$ synonymous-residual coordinates with nine modeled codon-adaptation readouts.  The coordinate list is
$K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $Leu_{CUN/UUR}$, $Leu_{UUA/UUG}$, $Ser_{UCR/AGY}$, $Ser_{UCA/UCG}$, $Thr_{ACR/ACY}$, and $f3$-stress.  The readout side uses the same nine coordinate directions after a modeled tAI construction from organism-level tRNA anticodon copy counts and dos Reis wobble weights.  The finite row contains $40$ organism entries and a $9\times 9$ survival matrix.  The loaded organism count is therefore large enough to distinguish this record from the earlier three-organism Leu contact, but it remains a finite cross-organism table rather than a population theorem.

\paragraph{Residualization design.}
The controlled model removes amino-acid composition, organism context, GC3, length, and the simpler $M$-density baseline before testing the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates against the modeled tAI readout.  The control block has $24$ columns and rank $23$.  After the control projection, the residual degrees of freedom are $17$, and the residualized $B^{*}_{Q6}$ design has rank $9$.  Thus the finite row is not a rank-collapsed comparison: the nine residual coordinates remain simultaneously visible after the declared controls.  In symbols, if $Z$ denotes the control span, $X$ the nine-coordinate residual design, and $T$ the modeled tAI readout block, the tested statistic is the controlled comparison
$$
\begin{aligned}
X_{\perp Z} &= (I-P_Z)X,\\
T_{\perp Z} &= (I-P_Z)T,\\
S^{QT} &= S(X_{\perp Z},T_{\perp Z}).
\end{aligned}
$$
The recorded matrix shape is $9\times 9$, and the positive-entry threshold for partial-$R^2$ survival is $10^{-12}$.

\paragraph{Numerical readback.}
The finite computation reports $R^2_{QT}=0.9003849000036579$ for the modeled tAI readout block after the declared controls.  The powered-rank check records organism count $40$, rank$(Z)=23$, rank$(X_{\perp Z})=9$, residual degrees of freedom $17$, and degeneracy cutoff $0.999999$.  Since $0.9003849000036579<0.999999$, the reported value is not classified as a degenerate exact-fit artifact by the stated cutoff.  The local support statement is therefore narrow: in the modeled tAI table, the nine-coordinate $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual block retains controlled explanatory power against the modeled codon-adaptation readout block.  Equivalently, the tested $B^{*}_{Q6}$ block is not absorbed by the amino-acid, organism, GC3, length, and $M$-density control model for this modeled readout.

\paragraph{Boundary of interpretation.}
The word ``modeled'' is essential here.  The tAI block is computed from tRNA gene-copy counts and wobble weights; it is not ribosome profiling, measured elongation speed, ribosome occupancy, proteomics, folding evidence, or a direct protein-realization measurement.  Positive modeled $S^{QT}$ entries, if inspected entrywise inside the matrix, support only the controlled modeled-translation-readout relation for the declared finite table.  They do not imply measured translation, folded structure, physical admissibility, molecular function, mechanism, pathway activity, protein abundance, or a universal biological rule.  Any such conclusion would require its own matched contact and its own controlled statistic.  In particular, tRNA copy number is not treated as tRNA abundance or charging, a modeled tAI readout is not treated as ribosome occupancy, and a cross-organism residual association is not promoted to a gene-level mechanism.

\paragraph{Local conclusion.}
For the standalone BioReality record, the finite claim is ready at the modeled-translation-readout level.  The carrier is the $9\times 9$ controlled $S^{QT}$ matrix over $40$ organisms; the controls have rank $23$ with $17$ residual degrees of freedom; the residual coordinate block has rank $9$; and the reported block score is $R^2_{QT}=0.9003849000036579$ under a degeneracy cutoff of $0.999999$.  This supports the bounded statement that the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ synonymous residual geometry survives the declared controls against modeled tAI coordinates in this finite table.  It does not close any claim about measured ribosome dynamics, protein abundance, folding, physical admissibility, biological function, mechanism, or universality.
