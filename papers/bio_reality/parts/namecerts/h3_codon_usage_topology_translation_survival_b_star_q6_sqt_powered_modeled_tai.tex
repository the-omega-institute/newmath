\subsection{Rank-powered modeled tAI survival for the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual panel}
\label{sec:b-star-q6-sqt-powered-modeled-tai}

\paragraph{Finite row.}
The experiment indexed by run $09209aa516a0$ tests whether the nine synonymous-residual coordinates of $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ retain predictive mass on a bounded translation-layer readout after the amino-acid quotient and contextual covariates are imposed. The readout is modeled per-codon tAI, computed from GtRNAdb anticodon copy counts with dos Reis wobble weights, and then projected onto the same nine residual directions. The organism panel contains $40$ organism rows, including human, Arabidopsis, grape, zebrafish, yeast, $E.\ coli$, frog, chicken, multiple Burkholderia and Mycobacterium entries, and additional bacterial and eukaryotic contexts. The residual coordinate list is
$$
\begin{aligned}
Q=\{&K\_AAA,\ Arg\_AGR,\ Ile\_AUA,\ Leu\_{CUN/UUR},\ Leu\_{UUA/UUG},\\
&Ser\_{UCR/AGY},\ Ser\_{UCA/UCG},\ Thr\_{ACR/ACY},\ f3\text{-}stress\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The modeled readout panel has the same cardinality: $9$ readout columns are projected against $9$ residual coordinates, producing a $9\times 9$ $S^{QT}$ surface.

\paragraph{Residualized design.}
The control matrix $Z$ contains $24$ declared control columns and has rank $23$ on this finite panel. After residualizing by amino-acid composition, organism context, GC3, sequence length, and the simpler codon-density baseline, the remaining degrees of freedom are $17$. The powered-rank check reports
$$
\begin{aligned}
\operatorname{rank}(Z)&=23,\\
\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde X)&=9,\\
\operatorname{df}_{\mathrm{res}}&=17,\\
R^{2}_{QT}&=0.9003849000036579.
\end{aligned}
$$
The row is therefore not the three-organism saturated surface that was separately refused: the experiment records $40$ organisms, full residual rank $9$ for the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ panel, and $R^{2}_{QT}$ below the declared degeneracy cutoff $0.999999$. The positivity floor for a partial-$R^{2}$ survival entry is $10^{-12}$, so the reported matrix is evaluated as a rank-powered modeled-tAI survival computation rather than as a rank artefact.

\paragraph{Modeled survival statement.}
Within this finite design, the claim supported is narrow: the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ synonymous-residual panel is not fully absorbed by the amino-acid, organism, GC3, length, and baseline codon-density controls when the response is modeled tAI projected onto the same residual coordinate family. In symbols, the computed surface is the modeled translation-readout score
$$
\begin{aligned}
S^{QT}_{ij}=S(q_i,t_j\mid Z),\qquad i,j\in\{1,\ldots,9\},
\end{aligned}
$$
with matrix shape $(9,9)$ and aggregate fitted value $R^{2}_{QT}=0.9003849000036579$ after the stated residualization. This is evidence for a modeled codon-adaptation survival interface: at least at the level of tRNA-copy-derived tAI weights, the residual $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ directions retain nontrivial explanatory geometry after the declared controls are imposed.

\paragraph{Relation to earlier finite contacts.}
The modeled-tAI row should be read together with two bounded predecessor contacts. First, run $4a783972e74b$ computed the nine $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ synonymous residuals against a proxy tRNA-Leu CUN-versus-UUR log-copy spectrum for $3$ organisms and found the break condition false: all nine residual directions were not below the $|A_{ij}|<0.01$ threshold, with $9$ nonzero entries and maximum absolute coupling $1882464.1106377328$. That row supplies coupling information only; it does not by itself supply a controlled survival score. Second, run $675fdddb3187$ reports the three-organism Leu CUN/UUR gate with Pearson $1.0$ and partial Pearson $1.0$, while also recording sign variation across organisms through log-ratios $1.3514980212896148$, $-0.24869078488829677$, and $1.0086640520051702$. That sign variation prevents any reading of the Leu row as a universal directional law. The $40$-organism modeled-tAI row is stronger in rank and scope than those $3$-organism contacts, but it remains a modeled readout rather than a direct ribosome or protein measurement.

\paragraph{Scientific boundary.}
The data used here are codon usage, tRNA gene-copy annotations, and modeled tAI weights. They are appropriate for testing a bounded codon-adaptation readout, not for asserting measured elongation, ribosome occupancy, protein output, folding, physical admissibility, biochemical function, pathway mechanism, phenotype, or a universal biological rule. A ribo-seq or translation-efficiency contact would be needed for measured translation survival. A proteomics contact would be needed for protein-abundance mediation. Separate structural, physical, perturbational, or functional assays would be needed for any higher-layer biological interpretation. Thus the present row supports only the rank-powered modeled $S^{QT}$ statement: $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates survive a controlled modeled-tAI projection on a $40$-organism panel with $\operatorname{rank}(Z)=23$, $\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde X)=9$, $17$ residual degrees of freedom, and $R^{2}_{QT}=0.9003849000036579$.
