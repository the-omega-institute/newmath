\subsection{Modeled-tAI Survival of the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ Synonymous Residual Coordinates}
\label{subsec:bioreality-b-star-q6-modeled-tai-survival}

\paragraph{Question.}
This record asks whether the nine $B^{*}_{Q6}$ synonymous-residual coordinates retain explanatory mass on a bounded translation-layer readout after the amino-acid quotient and the declared sequence controls have already been imposed.  The tested coordinates are
$$
\begin{aligned}
&K_{AAA},\quad Arg_{AGR},\quad Ile_{AUA},\quad Leu_{CUN/UUR},\quad Leu_{UUA/UUG},\\
&Ser_{UCR/AGY},\quad Ser_{UCA/UCG},\quad Thr_{ACR/ACY},\quad f3\text{-}stress.
\end{aligned}
$$
The readout is not a ribosome-profiling measurement.  It is a modeled per-codon tAI table assembled from GtRNAdb anticodon gene-copy counts with dos Reis wobble weights, then projected onto the same nine synonymous-residual directions.  Thus the contact tests a codon-usage/tRNA-copy modeled adaptation surface, not measured elongation speed or protein output.

\paragraph{Finite rows.}
The powered matrix computation used $40$ organisms.  The residual-control block had $24$ columns with $rank(Z)=23$, leaving $17$ residual degrees of freedom after the controls.  The residual coordinate block had $rank(\widetilde X)=9$, so all nine $B^{*}_{Q6}$ directions were estimable rather than collapsed by the controls.  The modeled-tAI readout side also had $9$ projected columns, giving a $9\times 9$ survival matrix.  The finite-row fit reported
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^{2}_{QT} &= 0.9003849000036579,\\
rank(\widetilde X) &= 9,\\
rank(Z) &= 23,\\
df_{res}(Z) &= 17,\\
S^{QT}\text{ matrix shape} &= 9\times 9.
\end{aligned}
$$
The positivity threshold for a partial-$R^{2}$ entry was $10^{-12}$, and the degeneracy cutoff was $0.999999$.  Since $R^{2}_{QT}=0.9003849000036579<0.999999$, the result is recorded as rank-powered rather than as a saturated all-fit artifact.  The organism list includes human, Arabidopsis, grape, zebrafish, budding yeast, chicken, E. coli, Xenopus, several Burkholderia and Mycobacterium entries, Pseudomonas, Nostoc, Leptospira, and Anabaena; the important numerical point is the $40$-organism panel rather than a three-row sign coincidence.

\paragraph{Readout geometry.}
For each residual coordinate $q_i$ and modeled-tAI direction $r_j$, the computation first residualizes both sides against amino-acid composition, organism context as present in the finite design, GC3, CDS length, and the $M$-density baseline.  It then evaluates the residual projection of the modeled tAI surface onto the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ coordinate system.  In compact notation, the finite-row object is
$$
\begin{aligned}
S^{QT}_{ij} &= S_{q_i}(r_j\mid Z),\\
Z &= (AA\text{-composition},\ organism/context,\ GC3,\ length,\ M\text{-density}),\\
q_i &\in \{K_{AAA},Arg_{AGR},Ile_{AUA},Leu_{CUN/UUR},Leu_{UUA/UUG},\\
&\qquad Ser_{UCR/AGY},Ser_{UCA/UCG},Thr_{ACR/ACY},f3\text{-}stress\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The reported aggregate $R^{2}_{QT}$ says that the nine residual coordinates jointly explain a large part of the residualized modeled-tAI projection under these controls.  It does not identify a single universal sign for any codon family, nor does it license replacing the matrix by one scalar biological law.

\paragraph{Relation to earlier coupling checks.}
A separate proxy coupling calculation on three organisms found nonzero $A_{ij}$ entries for all nine residual coordinates, with maximum absolute coupling $1882464.1106377328$ against a tRNA-Leu CUN-vs-UUR log-copy spectrum and a threshold $0.01$.  That proxy computation had $61$ codon-count dimensions and explicitly did not compute $S_{ij}$ survival.  The present $40$-organism modeled-tAI run is therefore the stronger finite-row statistic for this chapter: it uses a powered residual design and reports a controlled $S^{QT}$ matrix rather than only a covariance coupling.  The older Leu CUN/UUR finite contact had $n=3$, Pearson $1.0$, partial Pearson $1.0$, and signs $(+,-,+)$ for the logged ratios, so it remains useful only as a small-row orientation check, not as population-level directionality.

\paragraph{Organism sweep comparison.}
A companion organism-level modeled-tAI sweep reached $41$ organisms with an $n\geq 500$ gene gate.  Across that sweep the per-organism $R^{2}_{QT}$ distribution had minimum $0.1579271837024072$, first quartile $0.26353954700074694$, median $0.33934845811598124$, mean $0.38466555440501393$, third quartile $0.5143951545826744$, and maximum $0.8213457220900503$, with $1000$ permutations used in the sweep summaries.  This supports the interpretation that the modeled-tAI contact is not confined to one organism row, while still leaving phylogenetic non-independence and model-readout status explicit.

\paragraph{Local boundary.}
The finding is bounded to modeled translation-readout survival.  It does not show measured ribosome occupancy, measured elongation rate, translation efficiency from RPF/mRNA rows, protein abundance, protein folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, pathway mechanism, phenotype, or a universal biological rule.  tRNA gene copy is also not treated as tRNA abundance or charging.  Any such stronger interpretation would require its own matched contact and its own controlled statistic.  Within that boundary, the finite record is positive: after the amino-acid quotient and the $Z$ controls, the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ coordinate block is not absorbed by the simpler amino-acid/context/$M$-density model for the modeled-tAI surface.
