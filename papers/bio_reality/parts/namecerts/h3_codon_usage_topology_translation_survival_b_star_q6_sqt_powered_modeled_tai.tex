\subsection{Modeled tAI survival of the nine $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates}
\label{sec:bioreality-q6-modeled-tai-survival}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Question isolated by the finite row.}
This record tests whether the nine synonymous-residual coordinates of $B^{*}_{Q6}$ retain explanatory power for a modeled translation-layer readout after the amino-acid quotient and the declared sequence-context controls have already been imposed.  The coordinate list is
$K\_AAA$, $Arg\_AGR$, $Ile\_AUA$, $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$, $Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG$, $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$, $Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG$, $Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY$, and $f3\_stress$.  The readout is not measured ribosome flow.  It is a modeled per-codon tAI surface built from GtRNAdb anticodon gene-copy counts and dos Reis wobble weights, then projected onto the same nine synonymous-residual coordinates.  Thus the finite claim is deliberately narrower than a translation-mechanism claim: it asks whether residual codon-usage geometry survives against a bounded tAI-style readout under controls, not whether ribosomes, proteins, structures, or functions have been observed.

\paragraph{Observed design matrix.}
The powered row uses $40$ organism rows.  The organism panel contains Homo sapiens, Arabidopsis thaliana, Vitis vinifera, Danio rerio, Saccharomyces cerevisiae, Escherichia coli, Gallus gallus, Xenopus tropicalis, Ostreococcus lucimarinus, Debaryomyces hansenii, Yarrowia lipolytica, Anabaena variabilis, Nostoc sp. PCC 7120, several Burkholderia and Pseudomonas entries, and multiple Mycobacterium entries, among other bacterial and eukaryotic contexts.  The residualized control block has $24$ columns and rank $23$, leaving $17$ residual degrees of freedom after $Z$.  The controlled model therefore separates the nine $B^{*}_{Q6}$ coordinates from amino-acid composition, organism context, GC3, coding-sequence length, and the $M$-density baseline.  In this finite panel the residual coordinate block has full rank $9$, so the test is not the earlier three-organism rank-saturated surface.

\paragraph{Statistic.}
Let $X$ denote the $40 \times 9$ residual-coordinate matrix after projection away from $Z$, and let $T$ denote the corresponding $40 \times 9$ modeled tAI readout matrix after the same control removal.  The reported survival statistic is the controlled multivariate coefficient of determination
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^{2}_{QT} &= 0.9003849000036579,\\
\operatorname{rank}(Z) &= 23,\\
\operatorname{rank}(X\_{\perp Z}) &= 9,\\
\operatorname{df}\_{\perp Z} &= 17.
\end{aligned}
$$
The matrix shape of the computed $S^{QT}$ surface is $9 \times 9$, matching the nine residual coordinates against nine modeled tAI readout coordinates.  The positive-entry threshold recorded for partial $R^{2}$ is $10^{-12}$, and the degenerate-fit refusal threshold is $0.999999$.  Since $R^{2}_{QT}=0.9003849000036579$ is below the degeneracy threshold while the residual coordinate block has rank $9$, this row supports a modeled-readout survival statement rather than a rank-refusal statement.

\paragraph{Finite interpretation.}
The survival score is positive at the level of the joint $Q \to T$ modeled surface: the nine residual coordinates are not absorbed by the amino-acid, organism, GC3, length, and $M$-density control model when the bounded tAI-style readout is used.  The cross-organism sweep gives the same conclusion in a broader descriptive form: $41$ organisms were swept for modeled tAI, with $40$ target organisms reached, and the per-organism $R^{2}_{QT}$ distribution had minimum $0.1579271837024072$, first quartile $0.26353954700074694$, median $0.33934845811598124$, mean $0.38466555440501393$, third quartile $0.5143951545826744$, and maximum $0.8213457220900503$ under $1000$ permutations.  These values show that the modeled tAI contact is not a one-row accident of the primary $40$-organism panel, although the sweep remains descriptive rather than phylogenetically independent.

\paragraph{Relation to weaker contacts.}
The earlier proxy coupling row computed all nine $B^{*}_{Q6}$ synonymous residuals against a tRNA-Leu CUN-versus-UUR log-copy spectrum in only three organisms.  Its break condition was not met: all nine proxy couplings were not below $0.01$, and the maximum absolute coupling was $1882464.1106377328$ with $9$ nonzero entries.  That row is useful as a coordinate-contact precursor, but it is not itself the $S^{QT}$ survival statistic, because a proxy coupling $A_{ij}$ is not promoted to controlled survival without the explicit $Z$-residualized partial-$R^{2}$ computation.  The present modeled-tAI row supplies that computation for the bounded modeled readout and records the rank diagnostics that make the result nondegenerate.

\paragraph{Position-resolved contact boundary.}
A separate ribosome-occupancy finite row reports a descriptive local-occupancy separation with exact file-provided window and codon-index bins plus amino-acid bins.  In that row $9$ families were computed, all $9$ had at least $200$ within-position-bin permutations, and the families passing the $\lambda\_{DL}=0.01$, $DL=2$, and $C_H>0$ gate were $K\_AAA$, $Arg\_AGR$, and $f3\_stress$.  This is relevant only as a neighboring measured-readout boundary: it uses position-bin-controlled occupancy and synonymous-label permutations, whereas the present row uses organism-level modeled tAI derived from tRNA gene-copy and wobble weights.  The two contacts should therefore not be merged into one mechanism claim.

\paragraph{Local no-promotion rule.}
The conclusion is exactly a modeled translation-readout survival statement for $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates under the declared controls.  It does not show measured elongation speed, ribosome occupancy, translation efficiency, protein abundance, protein folding, structural order, physical admissibility, molecular function, pathway mechanism, phenotype, causation, or a universal biological law.  A tRNA gene-copy based tAI surface is not tRNA abundance, tRNA charging, ribosome dwell time, proteomics, or protein realization.  Any stronger statement would require its own matched contact and its own controlled statistic.  In particular, positive $S^{QT}$ entries in this row are not evidence for $S^{QP}$ protein-abundance survival and are not evidence for a mediated $M^{QTP}$ chain unless matched protein, mRNA, and measured or explicitly bounded translation rows are joined and tested separately.
