\subsection{Modeled tAI survival of the $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinates}
\label{subsec:b-star-q6-modeled-tai-survival}

\paragraph{Claim.}
This record concerns the residual codon-usage coordinates left after quotienting by amino-acid identity.  The tested carrier is a $9\times 9$ finite matrix $S^{QT}$ whose rows are the nine $B^*_{Q6}$ synonymous-residual coordinates and whose columns are modeled tAI readouts projected onto the same residual coordinate system.  The row set is
$K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $Leu_{CUN/UUR}$, $Leu_{UUA/UUG}$, $Ser_{UCR/AGY}$, $Ser_{UCA/UCG}$, $Thr_{ACR/ACY}$, and the $f_3$ stress coordinate.  The finite computation reports that all nine residual coordinates were computed and that the modeled readout table also has nine columns with the same coordinate names.  Thus the tested object is square, with matrix shape $[9,9]$, and the result is not a one-coordinate anecdote.

\paragraph{Finite row.}
The modeled tAI experiment has run identifier $09209aa516a0$ and was verified at $2026$-$06$-$05$T$07{:}22{:}14{+}00{:}00$.  It loads $40$ organism rows, including $homo\_sapiens$, $arabidopsis\_thaliana$, $vitis\_vinifera$, $danio\_rerio$, $saccharomyces\_cerevisiae$, $escherichia\_coli$, $gallus\_gallus$, $yarrowia\_lipolytica\_clib122$, and $anabaena\_variabilis\_atcc\_29413$, together with additional bacterial, fungal, plant, and vertebrate entries.  The control design has $24$ control columns, rank $23$, and leaves residual degrees of freedom $17$ after residualization.  The residualized $B^*_{Q6}$ design has rank $9$, so the nine-coordinate residual family is not collapsed by the declared controls.  The reported modeled translation fit is
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{QT} &= 0.9003849000036579,\\
\operatorname{rank}(Z) &= 23,\\
\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde X) &= 9,\\
\operatorname{df}_{\mathrm{resid}} &= 17,\\
\operatorname{shape}(S^{QT}) &= 9\times 9.
\end{aligned}
$$
The degeneracy threshold recorded for the powered-rank check is $0.999999$, while the observed $R^2_{QT}$ is below that value.  The positive-entry threshold for partial $R^2$ is $10^{-12}$.  These finite numbers support the narrow statement that a modeled tAI residual survival matrix has been computed under the stated controls and that the residual design retains full nine-coordinate rank in this row.

\paragraph{Statistic.}
For each residual coordinate $q_i$ and modeled tAI readout column $r_j$, the intended survival entry compares the residual readout after control projection with and without the $q_i$ coordinate.  In the notation used here, $Z$ contains amino-acid composition, organism context, GC3, coding-sequence length, and the simpler $M$-density baseline.  The local statistic is read as a controlled improvement score:
$$
\begin{aligned}
S^{QT}_{ij} &> 0
\quad \Longleftrightarrow \quad
q_i \text{ improves the residual description of } r_j \text{ after projecting away } Z,\\
Z &= (\text{amino-acid composition},\ \text{organism},\ GC3,\ \text{length},\ M\text{-density baseline}).
\end{aligned}
$$
The claim is therefore not that raw codon frequency predicts biology directly.  It is that the modeled tAI row leaves a nontrivial residual interface between the nine synonymous codon coordinates and the projected codon-adaptation readout after the named controls have already been charged against the model.

\paragraph{Coupling check.}
A separate finite coupling computation, run $4a783972e74b$, used three organisms: $homo\_sapiens$, $saccharomyces\_cerevisiae$, and $escherichia\_coli\_k12$.  It computed the same nine residual coordinates against a tRNA-Leu CUN-versus-UUR log-copy proxy.  The break condition for that row was that all nine couplings satisfy $|A_{ij}|<0.01$.  The break condition was not met: the row reports $9$ nonzero entries above the stated threshold and a maximum absolute coupling
$$
\begin{aligned}
\max_{i,j}|A_{ij}| &= 1882464.1106377328,\\
\theta_A &= 0.01.
\end{aligned}
$$
This coupling result is not itself the survival matrix.  Its role in the present chapter is narrower: it shows that the residual coordinate system is capable of making nonzero contact with a translation-adjacent tRNA-copy proxy before the stronger $Z$-controlled modeled tAI survival row is considered.

\paragraph{Leucine gate.}
The CUN/UUR leucine gate row, run $675fdddb3187$, also uses $homo\_sapiens$, $saccharomyces\_cerevisiae$, and $escherichia\_coli\_k12$.  It reports Pearson correlation $1.0$ and partial Pearson correlation $1.0$ for the three-row log-ratio contact, but it also records sign non-uniformity across organisms.  The observed log ratios are $1.3514980212896148$, $-0.24869078488829677$, and $1.0086640520051702$, with signs $+1,-1,+1$.  This row is therefore useful as a finite warning: a small table can expose a strong contact while still failing to justify a universal direction.  In the present chapter it supports only the existence of a bounded cross-organism contact route for one leucine coordinate, not a population law.

\paragraph{Interpretation.}
The modeled tAI row is a translation-readout model built from GtRNAdb tRNA gene-copy counts and dos Reis wobble weights, then projected onto $B^*_{Q6}$ synonymous-residual coordinates.  It is legitimate to report it as modeled $Q\to T$ survival under the declared control design.  It is not legitimate to re-describe it as ribosome profiling, measured elongation speed, ribosome occupancy, protein abundance, protein realization, folding, physical admissibility, biological function, mechanism, or universality.  The row also does not establish $S^{QP}$ or $M^{QTP}$: positive modeled $S^{QT}$ entries, if inspected entrywise, remain within the modeled translation-readout layer.

\paragraph{Local boundary.}
No translation, folding, physical admissibility, function, mechanism, or universal biological law follows from this chapter unless a distinct measurement contact supplies that layer and the corresponding controlled statistic is recorded.  tRNA gene-copy counts are not tRNA abundance or charging measurements.  Modeled tAI is not ribosome occupancy.  Ribosome-profiling, proteomics, and matched mRNA contacts are named as possible future measurement classes, but the verified row used here is the $40$-organism modeled tAI computation with rank $9$ residual coordinates, rank $23$ controls, residual degrees of freedom $17$, and $R^2_{QT}=0.9003849000036579$.
