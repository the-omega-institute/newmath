\subsection{Rank-powered modeled tAI survival for the $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinates}
\label{sec:bioreality-q6-modeled-tai-survival}

\paragraph{Finite object.}
This record tests whether the nine synonymous-residual coordinates of $B^*_{Q6}$ retain explanatory weight against a bounded modeled codon-adaptation readout after quotienting away coarse sequence and organism context.  The coordinate list is
$K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $Leu_{CUN/UUR}$, $Leu_{UUA/UUG}$, $Ser_{UCR/AGY}$, $Ser_{UCA/UCG}$, $Thr_{ACR/ACY}$, and the $f_3$ stress coordinate.  The readout side has the same nine projected modeled-tAI columns, giving a $9 \times 9$ $S^{QT}$ surface.  The powered panel uses $40$ organism rows, including human, Arabidopsis, grape, zebrafish, budding yeast, Escherichia coli, chicken, several Burkholderia and Mycobacterium rows, and other bacterial and eukaryotic contexts.  The control design has $24$ columns, rank $23$, and leaves $17$ residual degrees of freedom after removing the declared covariates.

\paragraph{Controlled statistic.}
Let $q_i$ range over the nine residual codon coordinates and let $t_j$ range over the nine modeled per-codon tAI projections.  The computation first residualizes both sides against the control block $Z$, which includes amino-acid composition, organism context, GC3, CDS length, and the simpler $M$-density baseline.  The reported surface is a partial-$R^2$ survival matrix, with positivity threshold $10^{-12}$.  In this finite run the combined controlled modeled-translation surface satisfies
$$
\begin{aligned}
\operatorname{rank}(Z) &= 23,\\
\operatorname{df}_{\mathrm{res}} &= 17,\\
\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde X) &= 9,\\
R^2_{QT} &= 0.9003849000036579.
\end{aligned}
$$
The row is therefore not the earlier three-organism saturated surface: the stated degeneracy cutoff was $0.999999$, while the powered statistic is below that cutoff and keeps all nine residual directions active.

\paragraph{Readout interpretation.}
The positive result is a modeled translation-adaptation survival claim, not a ribosome-occupancy measurement.  The readout is computed from organism-scoped tRNA gene-copy contacts and dos Reis wobble weights and then projected into the $B^*_{Q6}$ synonymous-residual basis.  Thus the finite-row conclusion is that the amino-acid quotient, organism controls, GC3, length, and the $M$-density baseline do not absorb the whole $B^*_{Q6}$ residual panel when the target is this modeled tAI surface.  Numerically, the row records a $9 \times 9$ survival matrix and a global modeled $Q \to T$ fit of $R^2_{QT}=0.9003849000036579$ on $40$ organisms.

\paragraph{Relation to the proxy coupling row.}
A separate proxy coupling computation used three organisms and a tRNA-Leu CUN-versus-UUR log-copy spectrum rather than the powered modeled-tAI panel.  That row computed the same nine residual coordinates, found that the null condition $|A_{ij}|<0.01$ for all coordinates was not met, and reported $9$ nonzero entries with maximum absolute coupling $1882464.1106377328$.  This supports the decision to run the powered survival test, but it is not itself a survival statistic: $A_{ij}$ records a covariance-style coupling, whereas $S_{ij}$ requires the controlled partial-$R^2$ or description-length calculation after $Z$ residualization.

\paragraph{Sign boundary.}
The Leu CUN/UUR gate row gives a useful warning about directionality.  In the three-organism table the reported partial Pearson value was $1.0$, but the organism signs were $+$, $-$, and $+$, with log-ratio entries $1.3514980212896148$, $-0.24869078488829677$, and $1.0086640520051702$.  The powered modeled-tAI row should therefore be read as evidence for controlled residual survival in the declared modeled readout, not as a universal sign law for any codon family or organism class.

\paragraph{Local boundary.}
This chapter does not claim direct ribosome-mediated translation, measured elongation speed, ribosome occupancy, protein abundance, folding, structural order, physical admissibility, biological function, molecular mechanism, phenotype, or a universal biological law.  Those conclusions require separate matched contacts, such as ribosome profiling with mRNA controls for measured translation efficiency, proteomics for protein abundance, and independent assays for structure, function, physical admissibility, or mechanism.  The present claim stops at the finite, controlled, modeled-tAI survival surface for $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinates.
