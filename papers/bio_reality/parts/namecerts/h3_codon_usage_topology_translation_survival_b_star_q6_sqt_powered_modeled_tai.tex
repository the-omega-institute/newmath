\subsection{Modeled tAI survival of the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual panel}
\label{sec:bioreality-b-star-q6-modeled-tai-survival}

\paragraph{Question.}
This record tests whether the nine-coordinate $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ synonymous-residual panel is absorbed by a codon-composition baseline once a modeled translation-adaptation readout is attached. The tested layer is \emph{modeled translation realization}: the readout is not ribosome profiling, elongation speed, protein abundance, folding, function, or a perturbation mechanism. It is a codon-level tAI surface computed from GtRNAdb anticodon copy counts and dos Reis wobble weights, then projected onto the same nine synonymous-residual directions used for the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ panel.

\paragraph{Finite carrier.}
The carrier is a $9\times 9$ survival table comparing nine residual coordinates against nine modeled tAI readout coordinates. The coordinate list is
$K\_AAA$, $Arg\_AGR$, $Ile\_AUA$, $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$, $Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG$, $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$, $Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG$, $Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY$, and $f3\_stress$.
The readout side uses the same nine labels after the modeled tAI projection. The organism panel has $40$ rows: $homo\_sapiens$, $arabidopsis\_thaliana$, $vitis\_vinifera$, $danio\_rerio$, $saccharomyces\_cerevisiae$, $burkholderia\_xenovorans\_lb400$, $burkholderia\_xenovorans\_lb400\_2$, $mycobacterium\_ulcerans\_agy99$, $escherichia\_coli$, $xenopus\_tropicalis$, $bradyrhizobium\_sp\_btai1$, $myxococcus\_xanthus\_dk\_1622$, $mesorhizobium\_loti\_maff303099$, $burkholderia\_pseudomallei\_668$, $saccharopolyspora\_erythraea\_nrrl\_2338$, $burkholderia\_pseudomallei\_1106a$, $burkholderia\_pseudomallei\_1106a\_2$, $rhizobium\_leguminosarum\_bv\_viciae\_3841$, $burkholderia\_cenocepacia\_hi2424$, $hahella\_chejuensis\_kctc\_2396$, three $mycobacterium\_smegmatis\_str\_mc2\_155$ entries, $burkholderia\_cenocepacia\_au\_1054$, $sphingopyxis\_alaskensis\_rb2256$, $burkholderia\_pseudomallei\_1710b$, $pseudomonas\_aeruginosa\_pa7$, $nostoc\_sp\_pcc\_7120$, $sinorhizobium\_medicae\_wsm419$, $ostreococcus\_lucimarinus\_cce9901$, $debaryomyces\_hansenii\_cbs767$, $mycobacterium\_vanbaalenii\_pyr\_1$, $gallus\_gallus$, $mycobacterium\_sp\_kms$, $yarrowia\_lipolytica\_clib122$, $nocardia\_farcinica\_ifm\_10152$, $pseudomonas\_aeruginosa\_ucbpp\_pa14$, two $leptospira\_borgpetersenii\_serovar\_hardjo\_bovis$ entries, and $anabaena\_variabilis\_atcc\_29413$.

\paragraph{Controls and rank.}
The regression removes a control block $Z$ with $24$ columns and numerical rank $23$. The covariate block includes amino-acid composition, organism/context terms, $GC3$, sequence length, and the $M$-density baseline. After residualizing by $Z$, the remaining degrees of freedom are $17$. The residualized $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ coordinate matrix has rank $9$, so the tested nine-coordinate panel is not numerically collapsed after the controls. The run records a degeneracy guard at $0.999999$ and a positive partial-$R^2$ threshold of $10^{-12}$.

\paragraph{Statistic.}
Let $Q$ denote the residualized $40\times 9$ matrix of $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ coordinates and let $T$ denote the residualized $40\times 9$ matrix of modeled tAI readout coordinates. The reported survival statistic is the controlled multivariate improvement obtained by adding $Q$ after $Z$ when predicting $T$. In scalar summary form the finite-row result is
$$
\begin{aligned}
  n_{organisms} &= 40,\\
  \operatorname{rank}(Z) &= 23,\\
  \operatorname{rank}(Q_{\perp Z}) &= 9,\\
  \operatorname{df}_{\perp Z} &= 17,\\
  R^2_{QT} &= 0.9003849000036579.
\end{aligned}
$$
The associated matrix has shape $9\times 9$. Since $R^2_{QT}$ is far below the degeneracy cutoff $0.999999$ and far above the positive threshold $10^{-12}$, this row supports a modeled $S^{QT}$ survival call for the bounded tAI readout class. The result is not a single-coordinate universal law; it is a finite controlled survival surface over the declared organism panel.

\paragraph{Coupling check.}
A preceding proxy coupling table used three organisms, $homo\_sapiens$, $saccharomyces\_cerevisiae$, and $escherichia\_coli\_k12$, and tested the same nine residual coordinates against a tRNA-Leu CUN-versus-UUR log-copy spectrum. In that lower-resolution contact, all $9$ coordinates had nonzero proxy coupling entries and the largest absolute entry was $1882464.1106377328$, above the declared $0.01$ break threshold. That result did not itself establish survival, because proxy coupling $A_{ij}$ is not the controlled partial-$R^2$ statistic. Its role here is diagnostic: it motivated the $40$-organism powered modeled-tAI run, while the survival claim rests on the later $R^2_{QT}=0.9003849000036579$ computation.

\paragraph{Leucine sign boundary.}
The CUN/UUR leucine gate also has a separate three-organism contact. The logged ratios in that row were $1.3514980212896148$, $-0.24869078488829677$, and $1.0086640520051702$, with signs $+,-,+$. The partial Pearson value in that three-row table was $1.0$, but sign uniformity was false. This matters for interpretation: the modeled tAI survival result is not read as a universal positive or negative leucine direction. It records that the residual coordinate panel survives a controlled modeled-readout test; it does not assign a global biological sign law.

\paragraph{Relation to abundance and mRNA stability rows.}
Several downstream rows show that the same residual coordinate family can be evaluated against other readout classes, but those rows are not imported as evidence for the modeled tAI claim. In yeast mRNA half-life, the $S^{QM}$ row joined $5085$ records, had $R^2_{QM}=0.05179429044975085$, reported $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$ as the dominant coordinate, and used a permutation count of $1000$ with right-tail value $0.000999000999000999$. The same join reported $R^2_{QP}=0.2761140373962526$ for protein abundance. In the yeast abundance residual mediation row, conditioning on log half-life changed $R^2_{QP}$ from $0.2761140373962526$ to $0.24851799532219632$, giving an absorbed component $0.027596042074056304$ and a mediation fraction $0.09994436477872035$; the permutation-null mean was $0.00019674487584783138$ and its $95$th percentile was $0.0014571003414947088$. These numbers are useful for layer comparison only. They do not convert modeled tAI survival into measured translation, protein output, or mechanism.

\paragraph{Verdict.}
The finite-row conclusion is that $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ is not absorbed by the amino-acid, organism/context, $GC3$, length, and $M$-density baseline when the readout is the modeled tAI projection built from tRNA gene-copy and wobble weights. The positive object is the controlled modeled $S^{QT}$ surface with $40$ organisms, $9$ residual coordinates, $9$ readout coordinates, $24$ controls, rank-$23$ control block, rank-$9$ residual coordinate block, $17$ residual degrees of freedom, and $R^2_{QT}=0.9003849000036579$.

\paragraph{Local boundary.}
This chapter does not infer measured translation from modeled tAI. It does not infer ribosome occupancy, elongation speed, protein abundance, folding, physical admissibility, biochemical function, causal mechanism, pathway membership, phenotype, or a universal biological rule. tRNA gene-copy counts are not treated as tRNA abundance or charging. A separate matched contact would be needed for each stronger layer: ribosome profiling or TE rows for measured translation, proteomics rows for protein abundance, structure or assay rows for folding and physical admissibility, perturbation or function rows for mechanism and function, and a broader design for population-level or universal claims.
