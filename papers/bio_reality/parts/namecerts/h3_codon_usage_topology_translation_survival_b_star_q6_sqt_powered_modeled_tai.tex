\subsection{Rank-powered modeled-tAI survival of the $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinates}
\label{subsec:h3-codon-usage-topology-translation-survival-b-star-q6-sqt-powered-modeled-tai}

\paragraph{Finite object.}
This record tests the nine-coordinate $B^*_{Q6}$ synonymous-residual system against a bounded translation-layer readout built from modeled tAI weights.  The carrier is a $9 \times 9$ survival surface whose row and column names are the same residual-coordinate list:
$K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $Leu_{CUN/UUR}$, $Leu_{UUA/UUG}$, $Ser_{UCR/AGY}$, $Ser_{UCA/UCG}$, $Thr_{ACR/ACY}$, and $f_3$-stress.  The readout is modeled per-codon tAI from GtRNAdb anticodon copy counts with dos Reis wobble weights, projected back onto these nine synonymous-residual directions.  The finite run used $40$ organisms, not a three-row saturated fit, and reported a full $9 \times 9$ $S^{QT}$ matrix.

\paragraph{Controlled statistic.}
The residualization set $Z$ had $24$ columns and rank $23$, leaving $17$ residual degrees of freedom after controls.  The control family included amino-acid composition, organism context, $GC3$, coding-sequence length, and the $M$-density baseline, so the modeled translation readout was not allowed to win merely by restating amino-acid usage or a simpler codon-density contrast.  The powered design had $rank(\widetilde X)=9$, while the declared degeneracy guard was $R^2<0.999999$.  The observed modeled-tAI survival energy was
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{QT} &= 0.9003849000036579,\\
rank(Z) &= 23,\\
rank(\widetilde X) &= 9,\\
\operatorname{df}_{\mathrm{resid}} &= 17.
\end{aligned}
$$
The positive-entry threshold was $10^{-12}$, and the matrix shape was exactly $9 \times 9$.  Thus the finite-row verdict is that the $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinates are not absorbed by the amino-acid, organism, $GC3$, length, and $M$-density description when the bounded readout is modeled tAI.

\paragraph{Organism panel.}
The organism panel contains $40$ rows, including human, Arabidopsis, grape, zebrafish, budding yeast, several Burkholderia strains, Mycobacterium entries, $E.\ coli$, Xenopus, Gallus, Yarrowia, Nostoc, Leptospira, and Anabaena.  This is a powered cross-organism modeled-readout contact, not a population-genetic law.  A companion organism sweep over modeled tAI reported $41$ organisms and an $R^2_{QT}$ distribution with minimum $0.1579271837024072$, first quartile $0.26353954700074694$, median $0.33934845811598124$, mean $0.38466555440501393$, third quartile $0.5143951545826744$, and maximum $0.8213457220900503$ under $1000$ permutations.  Those numbers show that the modeled-tAI contact is not a single-organism artifact, but they still remain descriptive statistics over the assembled panel.

\paragraph{Relation to measured translation contacts.}
A separate ribosome-occupancy analysis reached a narrower measured-contact boundary: $5754$ genes joined after length checks, $9$ families had at least $200$ within-position-bin permutations, and the families passing the local occupancy gate with $C_H>0$, $DL=2$, and $\lambda_{DL}=0.01$ were $K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, and $f_3$-stress.  That result supports a position-controlled local occupancy separation for selected synonymous families, but it is not the same object as the $40$-organism modeled-tAI matrix.  The present chapter therefore records modeled translation-readout survival and keeps measured ribosome occupancy as a separate, narrower contact.

\paragraph{Downstream comparison without promotion.}
The protein-abundance complement runs show why the boundary matters.  For example, the modeled-tAI complement fraction $d$ of the abundance component was $0.9208949355679455$ in human, $0.9400341806302775$ in chicken, $0.8972672018746489$ in $M.\ smegmatis$, $0.874628721049266$ in zebrafish, $0.6629960038578532$ in $E.\ coli$, and $0.21173219572706703$ in yeast.  The yeast abundance association was much larger, with $R^2_{QP}=0.29837624987287115$, while human had $R^2_{QP}=0.04454565117192484$ and chicken had $R^2_{QP}=0.03980793595345331$.  These values show that modeled translation complement and abundance residuals vary sharply by organism; they do not license a single universal direction.

\paragraph{mRNA-stability side contacts.}
The mRNA-stability rows also remain side contacts rather than translation mechanisms.  In yeast, the half-life join used $5085$ rows, with a reported CDS join hit rate of $0.9156038419146592$, and gave $R^2_{QM}=0.05179429044975085$, $R^2_{QP}=0.2761140373962526$, cosine $0.8040935468384264$, dominant $Leu_{CUN/UUR}$ for $S^{QM}$, and a permutation right-tail value $0.000999000999000999$.  In $E.\ coli$, $2720$ rows joined with hit rate $0.9978649586335735$, $R^2_{QM}=0.02298556640056076$, $R^2_{QP}=0.11632311854858134$, cosine $-0.7784923375569599$, dominant $Arg_{AGR}$, and the same reported permutation resolution $0.000999000999000999$.  In human, $16165$ rows joined with hit rate $0.9779294769799313$, $R^2_{QM}=0.010503670362051522$, $R^2_{QP}=0.04095215557813418$, cosine $0.6351183883924848$, and dominant $Leu_{CUN/UUR}$.  These contacts demonstrate residual codon association with mRNA-stability readouts, but they do not convert the modeled-tAI matrix into a causal decay model.

\paragraph{Coordinate decomposition.}
The coordinate-decomposition audit used a yeast join with $n=1258$, five folds, $120$ permutations, and a $24$-rank control basis.  It reported that $8$ of the $9$ coordinates had real source support under the stated held-out and permutation gate: all listed coordinates except $Ser_{UCA/UCG}$ appeared in the support list.  The $f_3$-stress family decomposition used $n=1800$, $21$ synonymous-family subcoordinates, five folds, $120$ permutations, $rank(f_3)=1$, and $P_{f3}$ energy $52.76911713166682$, which was $0.12259712041843235$ of the target energy $430.42705205115925$.  Nineteen of the twenty-one family subcoordinates were marked as having real source support.  These decompositions make the survival surface inspectable at coordinate and family resolution, rather than treating $B^*_{Q6}$ as an unanalyzed block.

\paragraph{Local boundary.}
The finite conclusion is deliberately narrow.  The row establishes a controlled, rank-powered modeled-tAI survival surface for the nine $B^*_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates, with $R^2_{QT}=0.9003849000036579$ on a $40$-organism panel and a nondegenerate $9$-rank residual design.  It does not show measured elongation speed, measured ribosome occupancy for the whole matrix, protein abundance mediation, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, pathway mechanism, phenotype, or a universal biological rule.  Any such stronger statement requires its own matched contact and its own controlled statistic.  In particular, tRNA gene copy and modeled tAI weights remain bounded translation annotations; they are not tRNA abundance, charging measurements, direct ribosome dynamics, or protein-output evidence by themselves.
