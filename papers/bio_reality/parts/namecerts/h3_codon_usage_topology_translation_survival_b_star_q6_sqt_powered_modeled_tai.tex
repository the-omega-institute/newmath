\subsection{Rank-powered modeled-tAI survival for the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual panel}
\label{sec:bioreality-h3-codon-usage-topology-translation-survival-b-star-q6-sqt-powered-modeled-tai}

\paragraph{Finite row.}
The record studied here is the finite cross-organism matrix test for the nine residual coordinates in the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ codon-usage panel against a modeled translation-adaptation readout.  The coordinate side contains exactly nine rows:
$K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $Leu_{CUN/UUR}$, $Leu_{UUA/UUG}$, $Ser_{UCR/AGY}$, $Ser_{UCA/UCG}$, $Thr_{ACR/ACY}$, and the $f3$ stress coordinate.  The readout side also contains nine modeled tAI columns with the same coordinate names after projection onto synonymous residual directions.  The experiment therefore computes a $9\times 9$ surface $S^{QT}$ rather than a single scalar association.

\paragraph{Controls and residualization.}
The computation uses $40$ organism rows.  The control block $Z$ has $24$ columns and effective rank $23$, leaving $17$ residual degrees of freedom after removing amino-acid composition, organism context, $GC3$, CDS length, and the simpler $M$-density baseline.  The powered design has residual coordinate rank $9$, so the test is not the three-organism saturated surface that would make every controlled score look perfect by construction.  In the finite row, the reported global fit of the modeled translation-adaptation surface is
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{QT} &= 0.9003849000036579,\\
\operatorname{rank}(Z) &= 23,\\
\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde X) &= 9,\\
\operatorname{df}_{\mathrm{res}} &= 17.
\end{aligned}
$$
The positive-entry threshold used for the partial-$R^2$ survival call is $10^{-12}$, and the degenerate-rank refusal threshold is $0.999999$.  Since $0.9003849000036579 < 0.999999$, this row is treated as rank-powered enough to report modeled survival scores rather than as a saturated refusal.

\paragraph{Readout definition.}
The translation-side readout is modeled per-codon tAI computed from GtRNAdb anticodon copy counts together with dos Reis wobble weights.  It is then projected onto the same nine synonymous-residual directions as the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ coordinate side.  Thus the matrix entry has the form
$$
\begin{aligned}
S^{QT}_{ij}
  &= S(q_i,r_j\mid Z),\\
q_i &\in \{K_{AAA},Arg_{AGR},Ile_{AUA},Leu_{CUN/UUR},Leu_{UUA/UUG},Ser_{UCR/AGY},Ser_{UCA/UCG},Thr_{ACR/ACY},f3\},\\
r_j &\in \{K_{AAA},Arg_{AGR},Ile_{AUA},Leu_{CUN/UUR},Leu_{UUA/UUG},Ser_{UCR/AGY},Ser_{UCA/UCG},Thr_{ACR/ACY},f3\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
A positive entry means that, after the declared controls have been residualized, the corresponding $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual coordinate improves the modeled tAI description of the paired readout direction by a strictly positive partial-$R^2$ amount above $10^{-12}$.

\paragraph{Organism panel.}
The row is not confined to the earlier three-organism Leu gate.  The loaded organism panel has $40$ entries: $homo\_sapiens$, $arabidopsis\_thaliana$, $vitis\_vinifera$, $danio\_rerio$, $saccharomyces\_cerevisiae$, $burkholderia\_xenovorans\_lb400$, $burkholderia\_xenovorans\_lb400\_2$, $mycobacterium\_ulcerans\_agy99$, $escherichia\_coli$, $xenopus\_tropicalis$, $bradyrhizobium\_sp\_btai1$, $myxococcus\_xanthus\_dk\_1622$, $mesorhizobium\_loti\_maff303099$, $burkholderia\_pseudomallei\_668$, $saccharopolyspora\_erythraea\_nrrl\_2338$, $burkholderia\_pseudomallei\_1106a$, $burkholderia\_pseudomallei\_1106a\_2$, $rhizobium\_leguminosarum\_bv\_viciae\_3841$, $burkholderia\_cenocepacia\_hi2424$, $hahella\_chejuensis\_kctc\_2396$, $mycobacterium\_smegmatis\_str\_mc2\_155$, $mycobacterium\_smegmatis\_str\_mc2\_155\_2$, $mycobacterium\_smegmatis\_str\_mc2\_155\_3$, $burkholderia\_cenocepacia\_au\_1054$, $sphingopyxis\_alaskensis\_rb2256$, $burkholderia\_pseudomallei\_1710b$, $pseudomonas\_aeruginosa\_pa7$, $nostoc\_sp\_pcc\_7120$, $sinorhizobium\_medicae\_wsm419$, $ostreococcus\_lucimarinus\_cce9901$, $debaryomyces\_hansenii\_cbs767$, $mycobacterium\_vanbaalenii\_pyr\_1$, $gallus\_gallus$, $mycobacterium\_sp\_kms$, $yarrowia\_lipolytica\_clib122$, $nocardia\_farcinica\_ifm\_10152$, $pseudomonas\_aeruginosa\_ucbpp\_pa14$, $leptospira\_borgpetersenii\_serovar\_hardjo\_bovis\_l550$, $leptospira\_borgpetersenii\_serovar\_hardjo\_bovis\_jb197$, and $anabaena\_variabilis\_atcc\_29413$.  The older three-row Leu contact remains useful as a warning example: its partial Pearson value was $1.0$ with $n=3$, while the logged organism signs were $+,-,+$, so that row did not justify a universal direction.  The present forty-organism modeled-tAI row is the stronger finite panel, but it is still a modeled readout panel.

\paragraph{Coupling context.}
A prior synonymous-residual coupling computation found all nine $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ coordinates present against a proxy tRNA-Leu CUN-versus-UUR log-copy spectrum, with maximum absolute coupling $1882464.1106377328$ and a $0.01$ break threshold not met.  That fact supports the decision to run the survival matrix, but it is not itself the survival result.  Coupling $A_{ij}$ reports residual covariance against a proxy spectrum; survival $S^{QT}_{ij}$ reports controlled modeled-readout improvement after $Z$ and the baseline ablation.

\paragraph{Verdict.}
For this chapter, the finite row is ready as a modeled translation-adaptation survival record.  The evidence says that the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual panel is not absorbed by the amino-acid, organism, $GC3$, length, and $M$-density control model when the target is the bounded modeled tAI surface.  The support is the computed $9\times 9$ $S^{QT}$ matrix over $40$ organisms with $R^2_{QT}=0.9003849000036579$, $\operatorname{rank}(Z)=23$, $\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde X)=9$, and $17$ residual degrees of freedom.

\paragraph{Local limitation.}
This row must not be read as measured ribosome occupancy, elongation speed, proteomic output, folded structure, physical admissibility, biochemical function, pathway mechanism, or a universal biological rule.  The tAI values are modeled from tRNA gene-copy and wobble-weight inputs; tRNA gene copy is not the same object as tRNA abundance or charging, and modeled adaptation is not ribo-seq.  Any claim about measured translation, protein abundance, folding, physical admissibility, function, mechanism, phenotype, or universality requires a separate matched contact and its own controlled statistic.
