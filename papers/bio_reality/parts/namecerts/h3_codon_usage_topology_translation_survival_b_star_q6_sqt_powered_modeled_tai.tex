\subsection{Modeled tAI survival of the nine $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ synonymous residuals}
\label{sec:bioreality-b-star-q6-modeled-tai-survival}

\paragraph{Finite carrier and coordinate content.}
This record concerns the finite matrix whose rows and columns are both indexed by the nine synonymous-residual coordinates
$K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $Leu_{CUN/UUR}$, $Leu_{UUA/UUG}$, $Ser_{UCR/AGY}$, $Ser_{UCA/UCG}$, $Thr_{ACR/ACY}$, and $f3$-stress.  The observed row panel contains $40$ organism entries, including human, yeast, zebrafish, chicken, \emph{Arabidopsis thaliana}, \emph{Escherichia coli}, several \emph{Burkholderia}, \emph{Mycobacterium}, and \emph{Pseudomonas} entries, together with other bacterial, fungal, plant, and animal rows.  For each organism the readout is not ribosome occupancy and not measured elongation speed; it is a modeled per-codon tAI value built from GtRNAdb anticodon copy counts together with dos Reis wobble weights, then projected onto the same nine $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates.

\paragraph{Controlled statistic.}
Let $Q$ denote the $40 \times 9$ matrix of residual codon-coordinate values and let $T$ denote the $40 \times 9$ modeled-tAI readout matrix after the same coordinate projection.  The nuisance design $Z$ has $24$ control columns, with fitted rank $23$, leaving $17$ residual degrees of freedom after residualization.  The statistic reported here is the controlled survival surface $S^{QT}$, with shape $9 \times 9$, obtained after removing amino-acid composition, organism-level context, GC3, length, and the $M$-density baseline.  In matrix notation the fitted controlled surface is read as
$$
\begin{aligned}
  \widetilde Q &= (I-P_Z)Q,\\
  \widetilde T &= (I-P_Z)T,\\
  S^{QT}_{ij} &> 0 \quad \text{only when the residual coordinate } \widetilde q_i
  \text{ improves the controlled modeled-tAI description of } \widetilde t_j .
\end{aligned}
$$
The run reports full rank for the residual coordinate block, with $\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde Q)=9$, and the aggregate controlled modeled-tAI fit has
$$
\begin{aligned}
  R^2_{QT} &= 0.9003849000036579,\\
  \operatorname{rank}(Z) &= 23,\\
  \operatorname{df}_{\mathrm{res}} &= 17,\\
  \dim(S^{QT}) &= 9 \times 9 .
\end{aligned}
$$
The declared positivity threshold for a partial-$R^2$ entry is $10^{-12}$, and the rank-saturation refusal threshold is $0.999999$.  Since $R^2_{QT}=0.9003849000036579$ is below the saturation threshold and the residual coordinate block has rank $9$, this finite row is treated as a powered modeled-readout computation rather than as the earlier degenerate small-$n$ surface.

\paragraph{What the positive modeled surface says.}
The supported statement is narrow: after quotienting out amino-acid composition and the declared organism, GC3, length, and $M$-density controls, the nine-coordinate $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual block is not absorbed by the controlled null description of the modeled tAI readout.  Equivalently, the computation supplies a finite $S^{QT}$ surface for modeled codon-adaptation readouts and records a positive controlled modeled-translation signal somewhere in the $9 \times 9$ coordinate grid.  This is stronger than a raw codon-count association because the statistic is computed after residualization against $Z$, but it remains weaker than a measured ribosome or protein-output result because the readout itself is built from tRNA gene-copy annotations and wobble weights.

\paragraph{Relation to the proxy coupling row.}
A separate three-organism proxy calculation used codon counts and a tRNA-Leu CUN-versus-UUR log-copy spectrum.  In that row all nine $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ synonymous residuals were computed, the codon-count space had dimension $61$, and the proxy coupling matrix had $9$ nonzero entries with maximum absolute coupling
$$
\begin{aligned}
  \max_{i,j}|A_{ij}| &= 1882464.1106377328 .
\end{aligned}
$$
The break condition for that proxy row would have required all nine couplings to satisfy $|A_{ij}|<0.01$; it was not met.  This coupling fact is useful as a coordinate-contact check, but it is not promoted to survival by itself.  The modeled-tAI row is the bounded survival computation because it attaches the projected readout matrix and the $Z$-controlled partial-$R^2$ surface.

\paragraph{Leucine gate context.}
The older CUN/UUR Leu contact used only three organisms: human, yeast, and \emph{E. coli} K12.  It reported a partial Pearson value of $1.0$ for the log CUN/UUR codon-usage ratio against the tRNA-Leu copy-ratio proxy, with log-ratio signs $(1,-1,1)$ and therefore no uniform sign across organisms.  That row is retained only as a finite contact hint.  It is not a population estimate, and its sign pattern is explicitly context-dependent.  The forty-organism modeled-tAI computation is the relevant powered row for this chapter.

\paragraph{Protein-readout comparison kept outside the claim.}
There is also a yeast protein-abundance condition comparison with $2209$ shared proteins between YEPD and SD conditions.  It reports $R^2_{QP}=0.3280825603424665$ in YEPD and $R^2_{QP}=0.3050385538305022$ in SD, with $f3$-stress dominant in both conditions and a condition-surface cosine of $0.9993775935182967$ over $1000$ permutations.  Those numbers show that the same coordinate family can be read against a protein-abundance layer in a separate finite panel, but they do not alter the present modeled-tAI conclusion.  The present section is an $S^{QT}$ modeled-readout record, not a protein-mediation or functional-assay record.

\paragraph{Local non-promotion boundary.}
The chapter therefore permits the following statement and no stronger one: a forty-organism, rank-sufficient, $Z$-controlled modeled-tAI survival surface exists for the nine $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates, with aggregate $R^2_{QT}=0.9003849000036579$ and residual degrees of freedom $17$.  It does not establish measured ribosome-mediated translation, measured elongation speed, ribosome occupancy, proteomics output, folding, structural order, physical admissibility, biological function, causal mechanism, pathway participation, phenotype, or universality.  Any such higher claim requires its own matched contact and its own controlled statistic.  In particular, tRNA gene-copy input is not treated as tRNA abundance or charging, modeled tAI is not treated as ribo-seq, and a positive $S^{QT}$ entry is not treated as evidence for $S^{QP}$ or $M^{QTP}$ without a separate same-row protein or mediation calculation.
