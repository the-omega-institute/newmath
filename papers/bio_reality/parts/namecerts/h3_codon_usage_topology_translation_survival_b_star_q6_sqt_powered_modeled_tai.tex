\subsection{Modeled tAI Survival of the Nine $B^{*}_{Q6}$ Residual Coordinates}
\label{sec:bioreality-b-star-q6-modeled-tai-survival}

\paragraph{Finite row.}
The record tests whether the nine synonymous-residual coordinates selected by $B^{*}_{Q6}$ retain explanatory mass against a modeled codon-adaptation readout after the amino-acid quotient and context controls are imposed. The verified computation used $40$ organism rows and the coordinate list
$$
\begin{aligned}
&K_{AAA},\ \mathrm{Arg}_{AGR},\ \mathrm{Ile}_{AUA},\ \mathrm{Leu}_{CUN/UUR},\ \mathrm{Leu}_{UUA/UUG},\\
&\mathrm{Ser}_{UCR/AGY},\ \mathrm{Ser}_{UCA/UCG},\ \mathrm{Thr}_{ACR/ACY},\ f3\text{-}\mathrm{stress}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The same nine names were used as the modeled tAI readout columns, producing a $9\times 9$ survival surface. The organism panel contained $40$ entries, including human, yeast, \textit{Escherichia coli}, zebrafish, chicken, \textit{Arabidopsis thaliana}, \textit{Vitis vinifera}, \textit{Xenopus tropicalis}, and bacterial or fungal rows such as \textit{Mycobacterium smegmatis}, \textit{Pseudomonas aeruginosa}, \textit{Nostoc} sp. PCC 7120, and \textit{Yarrowia lipolytica}. The readout was not a ribosome experiment: it was modeled per-codon tAI derived from anticodon copy counts and dos Reis wobble weights, then projected onto the same synonymous-residual coordinate grammar.

\paragraph{Controlled statistic.}
The control matrix $Z$ contained amino-acid composition, organism context, GC3, length, and the simpler codon-density baseline. In the recorded run, $Z$ had $24$ columns, rank $23$, and left $17$ residual degrees of freedom after residualization. The residual coordinate design had rank $9$, so the survival calculation was not the three-row rank-saturated surface that had to be rejected elsewhere. The verified scalar for the modeled tAI panel was
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^{2}_{QT} &= 0.9003849000036579,\\
\operatorname{rank}(Z) &= 23,\\
\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde X) &= 9,\\
\mathrm{df}_{\mathrm{residual}} &= 17,\\
\dim(S^{QT}) &= 9\times 9.
\end{aligned}
$$
The positive-entry threshold used for partial-$R^{2}$ survival was $10^{-12}$, and the degeneracy guard was $R^{2}<0.999999$. Since $R^{2}_{QT}=0.9003849000036579$ lies below that guard, the finite row is usable as a powered modeled-readout survival record rather than a rank-saturation artefact.

\paragraph{Relation to the earlier coupling row.}
A separate proxy calculation on three organisms computed all nine $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residuals against a tRNA-Leu CUN-versus-UUR log-copy spectrum. That earlier row reported $9$ nonzero coupling entries and maximum absolute coupling
$$
\begin{aligned}
\max_{i,j}|A_{ij}| &= 1882464.1106377328,
\end{aligned}
$$
above the declared $0.01$ break threshold. That coupling row is useful only as a coordinate-contact diagnostic: it shows that the residual directions are not numerically silent against the proxy spectrum, but it does not by itself imply a controlled survival score. The modeled tAI result is the stronger finite row because it computes a $9\times 9$ $S^{QT}$ surface after residualizing the declared controls. The two rows therefore serve different roles: $A_{ij}$ records covariance-scale contact, while $S^{QT}_{ij}$ records controlled modeled-readout survival.

\paragraph{Leucine sign boundary.}
The CUN/UUR leucine gate supplies a small-row check on directionality. In the three-organism contact, the logged CUN/UUR ratios were
$$
\begin{aligned}
1.3514980212896148,\quad -0.24869078488829677,\quad 1.0086640520051702,
\end{aligned}
$$
with signs $+,-,+$. The reported Pearson and partial Pearson statistics were both $1.0$ on $n=3$, but the sign pattern was explicitly non-uniform across organisms. This prevents reading the modeled survival surface as a universal directional rule about leucine codon preference. The $40$-organism modeled tAI row can support the weaker conclusion that some $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual information survives the specified controls in a modeled translation-readout coordinate system; it cannot turn a context-dependent finite row into a population law.

\paragraph{Interpretation.}
The supported statement is narrow. After amino-acid quotienting and the stated context controls, the nine-dimensional $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual package is not fully absorbed by the amino-acid-plus-context-plus-baseline description when the response is the modeled tAI projection. The operative finite numbers are the $40$ organism rows, the $9$ residual coordinates, the $9$ modeled readout columns, the $24$ control columns of rank $23$, the $17$ residual degrees of freedom, and the controlled modeled-readout value $R^{2}_{QT}=0.9003849000036579$. These values justify recording the packet as a modeled translation-readout survival result, not merely as a proxy-coupling or routing result.

\paragraph{Local exclusion.}
No measured translation event follows from this row alone. Modeled tAI from tRNA gene-copy annotations and wobble weights is not ribosome occupancy, elongation speed, ribosome profiling, protein abundance, protein folding, physical admissibility, biological function, mechanism, phenotype, or a universal biological law. A measured translation claim would require a separate ribosome-profiling or translation-efficiency contact paired to the same controls. A protein-output claim would require matched proteomics and, for mediation, an explicit $Q_i\to T_j\to P_k$ calculation. Structure, admissibility, function, phenotype, and mechanism each require their own measurements and cannot be inferred from this modeled $S^{QT}$ surface.
