\subsection{Rank-powered modeled-tAI survival of the nine residual codon coordinates}
\label{subsec:bioreality-b-star-q6-modeled-tai-survival}

\paragraph{Finite object.}
The record concerns a bounded translation-layer calculation for the nine synonymous-residual coordinates
$K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $Leu_{CUN/UUR}$, $Leu_{UUA/UUG}$, $Ser_{UCR/AGY}$, $Ser_{UCA/UCG}$, $Thr_{ACR/ACY}$, and $f3$-stress.  These coordinates are tested against nine modeled per-codon tAI readout columns built from GtRNAdb anticodon copy counts with dos Reis wobble weights.  The finite matrix therefore has shape $9 \times 9$: nine residual codon coordinates by nine modeled adaptation readouts.  The calculation is not a claim about measured ribosome occupancy.  It is a controlled survival calculation for a modeled codon-adaptation contact, with the contact kept at the translation-readout layer and with amino-acid quotienting kept explicit.

\paragraph{Controls and rank.}
The run contains $40$ organisms.  The control design has $24$ columns, rank $23$, and leaves $17$ residual degrees of freedom after residualization.  The residual coordinate block has rank $9$, so the nine tested coordinates remain visible after the declared controls are imposed.  The rank check reports
$$
\begin{aligned}
 n &= 40,\\
 \operatorname{rank}(Z) &= 23,\\
 \operatorname{rank}(\widetilde X) &= 9,\\
 df_{\mathrm{res}} &= 17.
\end{aligned}
$$
The global modeled translation-readout fit is reported as
$$
\begin{aligned}
 R^2_{QT} &= 0.9003849000036579,
\end{aligned}
$$
with the degeneracy refusal threshold fixed at $0.999999$.  Thus this row is not the earlier saturated small-sample surface: the powered design has enough residual rank to distinguish a high modeled-tAI association from a rank-one or all-ones artefact.  The positivity threshold for partial-$R^2$ survival is $10^{-12}$, so the numerical row is interpreted only after the full control residualization, not from an unadjusted codon-count association.

\paragraph{Modeled survival statement.}
Let $q_i$ range over the nine residual coordinates and let $r_j$ range over the nine modeled tAI readout columns.  The computed object is the matrix $S^{QT}_{ij}=S(q_i,r_j\mid Z)$ after residualizing against amino-acid composition, organism context, GC3, length, and the simpler codon-density baseline.  In this finite row the matrix shape is $9\times 9$, the residual coordinate count is $9$, the readout count is $9$, and the reported joint modeled readout score is $R^2_{QT}=0.9003849000036579$.  The appropriate readback is therefore: the nine-coordinate residual codon block survives as a modeled tAI-readout predictor under the declared controls, at powered cross-organism rank, for this finite panel of $40$ organisms.  The result is a modeled translation-readout survival entry, not a direct ribosome or protein measurement.

\paragraph{Relation to the proxy coupling rows.}
A separate finite coupling row had already computed the same nine residual coordinates against a tRNA-Leu CUN-versus-UUR log-copy spectrum in three organisms.  That earlier proxy row loaded human, budding yeast, and \emph{E. coli} K12, found all nine coupling entries nonzero, and reported maximum absolute coupling
$$
\begin{aligned}
 \max_{i,j}|A_{ij}| &= 1882464.1106377328,
\end{aligned}
$$
against a null threshold $0.01$.  That row did not itself establish survival, because a nonzero covariance coupling $A_{ij}$ is weaker than a $Z$-controlled partial-$R^2$ or description-length improvement.  The present modeled-tAI row supplies the stronger controlled modeled-readout calculation: $40$ organisms, rank-$23$ controls, $17$ residual degrees of freedom, and a $9\times 9$ survival surface.  The proxy coupling should be read as a precursor contact, while the powered modeled-tAI computation is the row that carries the modeled $S^{QT}$ claim.

\paragraph{Sign and organism dependence.}
The small Leu CUN/UUR gate remains informative only as a warning against over-reading direction.  In the three-organism contact, the CUN/UUR log-ratio signs were $(1,-1,1)$, with numerical log-ratios $1.3514980212896148$, $-0.24869078488829677$, and $1.0086640520051702$.  The reported Pearson and partial Pearson values were both $1.0$ for $n=3$, but the sign was explicitly non-uniform across organisms.  This is why the modeled-tAI survival row is framed as a finite, controlled, powered readout calculation rather than as a universal sign law for codon usage.  The forty-organism modeled row raises the rank and sample scale, but it still does not turn any individual coordinate sign into a population-level biological direction without a separately reported entrywise statistic and null distribution.

\paragraph{Local boundary of interpretation.}
The supported statement stops at modeled translation-readout survival.  Modeled tAI from tRNA gene-copy counts and wobble weights is not ribosome profiling, measured elongation speed, ribosome occupancy, protein abundance, protein folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, pathway mechanism, phenotype, or a universal biological law.  Positive modeled $S^{QT}$ behavior must not be promoted to protein-output survival $S^{QP}$ or translation-conditioned mediation $M^{QTP}$ unless same-organism and same-CDS protein or ribosome contacts are attached and recomputed under the same controls.  Matched mRNA abundance, when present, is only a covariate for separating transcript-level abundance from translation-layer readout; it is not itself translation realization.  Likewise, tRNA gene-copy ratio is a genome-annotation contact and must not be renamed as tRNA abundance, tRNA charging, or ribosome dynamics without an independent measurement.

\paragraph{Verdict for this row.}
Within its stated scope, the row is ready: it records a powered $40$-organism modeled-tAI survival calculation for the nine $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinates, using a $24$-column control design of rank $23$, retaining $17$ residual degrees of freedom, testing a $9\times 9$ modeled readout surface, and reporting $R^2_{QT}=0.9003849000036579$ below the declared saturation cutoff $0.999999$.  The conclusion is deliberately narrow: the residual codon-coordinate block is not absorbed by the amino-acid, organism, GC3, length, and simpler-baseline controls for this modeled tAI readout.  Nothing in this row, by itself, establishes measured translation, folding, physical admissibility, function, mechanism, phenotype, protein abundance, or universality.
