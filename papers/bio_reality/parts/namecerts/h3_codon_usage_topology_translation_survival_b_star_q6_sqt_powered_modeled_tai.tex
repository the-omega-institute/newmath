\subsection{Modeled tAI survival of the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual panel}\label{sec:b-star-q6-modeled-tai-survival}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Question.}
This record tests whether the nine-dimensional $B^{*}_{Q6}$ synonymous-residual panel keeps a detectable translation-layer trace after quotienting away amino-acid composition and ordinary sequence covariates. The tested readout is not measured ribosome motion. It is a modeled per-codon tAI surface built from GtRNAdb anticodon copy counts together with dos Reis wobble weights, then projected onto the same nine synonymous-residual coordinates used for the codon-usage side. The finite row is therefore a bounded translation-readout contact: it can report survival against a modeled adaptation surface, but it cannot by itself report ribosome occupancy, elongation speed, proteomics, folding, biochemical function, a mechanism, or a general biological law.

\paragraph{Finite design.}
The computation used experiment run \texttt{09209aa516a0}. The carrier is a $9\times 9$ survival surface, with rows indexed by
$K\_AAA$, $Arg\_AGR$, $Ile\_AUA$, $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$, $Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG$, $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$, $Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG$, $Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY$, and $f3\_stress$. The readout columns have the same nine labels, because the modeled tAI surface is read back through the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual-coordinate basis. The organism panel contains $40$ organisms, including \textit{Homo sapiens}, \textit{Arabidopsis thaliana}, \textit{Vitis vinifera}, \textit{Danio rerio}, \textit{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}, \textit{Escherichia coli}, \textit{Xenopus tropicalis}, \textit{Gallus gallus}, several \textit{Burkholderia} and \textit{Mycobacterium} records, and additional bacterial and eukaryotic entries.

The control design contains $24$ columns. Its residualized rank is $23$, leaving residual degrees of freedom $17$ after the control block. The controls include amino-acid composition, organism context, GC3, CDS length, and the $M$-density baseline on the union support of the nine projected residual coordinates. With $X$ denoting the residualized $B^{*}_{Q6}$ coordinate matrix, $T$ the projected modeled-tAI readout matrix, and $Z$ the control block, the reported scalar is the controlled multivariate survival coefficient
$$
\begin{aligned}
S^{QT} &= R^2\bigl(T \mid Z,X\bigr)-R^2\bigl(T \mid Z\bigr),\\
R^2_{QT} &= 0.9003849000036579.
\end{aligned}
$$
The stated positivity threshold for partial $R^2$ entries is $10^{-12}$, and the degeneracy threshold is $0.999999$. The fitted rank of the residualized $B^{*}_{Q6}$ block is $9$, so the positive result is not an all-columns-collapsed rank artifact in this forty-organism design.

\paragraph{Result.}
The finite computation reports a $9\times 9$ modeled-translation survival matrix and an aggregate controlled value $R^2_{QT}=0.9003849000036579$. On the paper's local scale, this means that the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual panel is not absorbed by the amino-acid, organism, GC3, length, and $M$-density control model when the target is the modeled tAI surface. The result is specifically about $S^{QT}$: codon residuals to modeled translation-adaptation readouts. It is not a statement about $S^{QP}$ protein abundance or $M^{QTP}$ translation-conditioned protein mediation.

A separate organism sweep of the same modeled-tAI idea reports $41$ organisms swept, with target coverage reached for $40$ organisms. In that sweep the per-organism $R^2_{QT}$ distribution has minimum $0.1579271837024072$, first quartile $0.26353954700074694$, median $0.33934845811598124$, mean $0.38466555440501393$, third quartile $0.5143951545826744$, and maximum $0.8213457220900503$, using $1000$ permutations. This surrounding finite row supports the interpretation that the modeled-tAI contact is not confined to a single named organism, while still leaving phylogenetic dependence and dataset heterogeneity outside the claim.

\paragraph{Relation to weaker contacts.}
An earlier proxy coupling row computed the same nine residual coordinates in a $61$-codon count space for three organisms: \textit{Homo sapiens}, \textit{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}, and \textit{Escherichia coli} K-12. That proxy used a tRNA-Leu CUN-vs-UUR log-copy spectrum, reported all $9$ coupling entries nonzero, and had maximum absolute coupling $1882464.1106377328$ against a threshold $0.01$. Its own break condition, all $|A_{ij}|<0.01$, was not met. That row is weaker than the modeled-tAI survival result because nonzero $A_{ij}$ is only a covariance contact and does not substitute for the controlled $S^{QT}$ estimate. The present row is the stronger finite statement because the controls are residualized and a partial-$R^2$ survival statistic is computed.

A narrower CUN/UUR Leu contact also reported a three-organism Pearson value $1.0$ and partial Pearson value $1.0$ for log CUN/UUR against the tRNA-Leu copy ratio, with observed signs $(1,-1,1)$ across the three organism rows. The sign non-uniformity matters: this chapter does not turn the CUN/UUR finite row into a universal direction claim. It only records that the broader forty-organism modeled-tAI design preserves a controlled signal for the nine-coordinate residual panel.

\paragraph{Measured-readout boundary.}
The modeled-tAI row must be kept separate from direct ribosome and protein contacts. A distinct ribosome-occupancy finite row on yeast joined $5754$ genes after the length check, with $5754$ genes overlapping the occupancy source and $6013$ ordered CDS genes available. It used exact file-provided window or codon-index bins and amino-acid bins, with synonymous labels shuffled within bins. In that local-occupancy analysis, the families $K\_AAA$, $Arg\_AGR$, and $f3\_stress$ passed the stated gates: $C_H>0$, $\lambda_{DL}=0.01$, and $DL=2$, with $200$ requested permutations and all $9$ computed families having at least $200$ permutations. That is a separate occupancy contact, not evidence that the modeled-tAI matrix itself measured ribosome speed.

Thus the chapter's cannot-claim boundary is local and strict. The $R^2_{QT}=0.9003849000036579$ result licenses a positive modeled translation-readout survival statement for $B^{*}_{Q6}$ under the named controls. It does not license measured translation efficiency, ribosome occupancy, protein abundance, protein folding, structural order, physical admissibility, biological function, causal mechanism, phenotype, or universality. Each of those stronger readings would require its own matched contact rows and its own controlled statistic.
