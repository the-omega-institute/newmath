\subsection{Rank-powered modeled-tAI survival of $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates}\label{sec:bioreality-q6-modeled-tai-survival}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite row under test.}
This record tests whether the nine synonymous-residual coordinates of $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ retain predictive content for a bounded modeled translation-readout surface after the amino-acid quotient and elementary sequence covariates have already been imposed.  The finite carrier is a $9\times 9$ survival matrix $S^{QT}$ whose rows and columns are the same nine residual coordinates:
$$
\begin{aligned}
&K\_AAA,\ Arg\_AGR,\ Ile\_AUA,\ Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR,\ Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG,\\
&Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY,\ Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG,\ Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY,\ f3\_stress.
\end{aligned}
$$
The readout is modeled per-codon tAI derived from GtRNAdb anticodon copy counts with dos Reis wobble weights and then projected onto the same residual coordinate basis.  Thus the row is a modeled $Q\to T$ contact, not a direct ribosome-footprint, elongation-rate, protein-abundance, folding, function, or phenotype measurement.

\paragraph{Controls and rank.}
The powered run uses $40$ organisms.  The residualization basis $Z$ has $24$ control columns and rank $23$; after removing $Z$, the residual degrees of freedom are $17$.  The covariates include the amino-acid composition quotient, organism context, GC3, coding-sequence length, and the $M$-density baseline given by the fraction of sense codons lying on the union support of the nine projected $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ vectors.  The residualized $Q$ design has rank $9$, so the tested coordinate surface is not the three-organism saturated case.  The recorded diagnostic is
$$
\begin{aligned}
 n_{org} &= 40,\\
 \operatorname{rank}(Z) &= 23,\\
 \operatorname{rank}(\widetilde X) &= 9,\\
 df_{res}(Z) &= 17,\\
 \dim(S^{QT}) &= 9\times 9.
\end{aligned}
$$
The degenerate-fit threshold was $0.999999$, while the observed modeled-tAI survival fit was $R^2_{QT}=0.9003849000036579$.  This is below the degeneracy cutoff and above the positive-entry threshold $10^{-12}$, so the finite row supports nonzero modeled-readout survival in the bounded rank-powered sense.

\paragraph{Observed survival statistic.}
The survival surface reports $R^2_{QT}=0.9003849000036579$ for the $9\times 9$ modeled-tAI matrix.  The statement read from this number is narrow: after quotienting amino-acid composition and residualizing the stated controls, the nine-dimensional $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual coordinate system is not absorbed by the amino-acid, context, GC3, length, and $M$-density description when the target is the modeled tAI surface.  Equivalently, at least part of the modeled anticodon-copy/wobble-weight readout remains aligned with the residual coordinate system.

\paragraph{Organism panel.}
The primary run loaded $40$ organism rows, including human, yeast, zebrafish, chicken, Arabidopsis, grape, several Burkholderia and Mycobacterium entries, Escherichia coli, Xenopus, Nostoc, Yarrowia, Nocardia, and Anabaena.  A companion organism sweep reports $41$ organisms with per-organism modeled-tAI surfaces.  In that sweep the $R^2_{QT}$ distribution has minimum $0.1579271837024072$, first quartile $0.26353954700074694$, median $0.33934845811598124$, mean $0.38466555440501393$, third quartile $0.5143951545826744$, and maximum $0.8213457220900503$ over $1000$ permutations.  These numbers show that the modeled-tAI contact is not confined to one hand-picked organism row, although the organisms are not phylogenetically independent and the sweep is descriptive rather than a population law.

\paragraph{Coupling and local occupancy checks.}
A separate proxy coupling computation on three organisms found all nine $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates computable and at least one proxy coupling above the declared $0.01$ threshold, with $9$ nonzero entries and maximum absolute coupling $1882464.1106377328$ against a tRNA-Leu CUN-vs-UUR log-copy spectrum.  That coupling run is not itself survival, since it lacks the full modeled-tAI survival matrix and the complete $Z$-controlled partial-$R^2$ estimate.  A position-controlled ribosome-occupancy check gives a different bounded contact: exact file-provided window/codon-index and amino-acid bins were used, synonymous labels were shuffled only within the same bin, $200$ permutations were requested, and $9$ families reached at least $200$ permutations.  Under the stated $C_H>0$ rule with description-length penalty $DL=2$ and $\lambda_{DL}=0.01$, $K\_AAA$, $Arg\_AGR$, and $f3\_stress$ passed the local occupancy gate.  That result supplies a compatible position-level readout boundary, but it remains a descriptive occupancy separation rather than a causal translation mechanism.

\paragraph{Complement and finite-row caution.}
The modeled-translation complement computation shows that the part of the abundance projection not explained by modeled tAI varies sharply by organism.  The complement fractions $d$ are $0.874628721049266$ for zebrafish, $0.6629960038578532$ for Escherichia coli K-12 MG1655, $0.9400341806302775$ for chicken, $0.9208949355679455$ for human, $0.8972672018746489$ for Mycobacterium smegmatis, and $0.21173219572706703$ for yeast.  The corresponding modeled-tAI-mediated fractions $m$ are $0.12537127895073397$, $0.3370039961421468$, $0.05996581936972245$, $0.07910506443205445$, $0.10273279812535108$, and $0.788267804272933$.  These values prevent promoting the modeled-tAI row to a uniform protein-output story.  The older three-organism CUN/UUR Leu gate has Pearson and partial Pearson equal to $1.0$ for $n=3$, but its log-ratio signs are $1,-1,1$ with values $1.3514980212896148$, $-0.24869078488829677$, and $1.0086640520051702$; this is a finite-row contact with sign reversal, not universal directionality.

\paragraph{Local interpretation.}
The supported conclusion is that $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ has a positive, rank-admissible modeled $S^{QT}$ entry after the declared controls, and that the full modeled-tAI coordinate matrix has strong residual alignment with the nine-coordinate basis.  The row is therefore evidence for a bounded modeled translation-readout survival surface.  It is not evidence that tRNA gene copy equals tRNA abundance or charging, not evidence of measured ribosome-mediated translation, not evidence of protein abundance, and not evidence of protein folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, organismal phenotype, mechanism, or a universal biological law.  Any one of those stronger claims would require its own matched contact and its own controlled statistic, separate from the modeled anticodon-copy/wobble-weight surface reported here.
