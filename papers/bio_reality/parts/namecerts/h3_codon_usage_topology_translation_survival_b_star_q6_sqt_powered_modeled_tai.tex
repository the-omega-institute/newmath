\paragraph{Finite carrier.}

\paragraph{Residualized survival statistic.}
Let $Q$ denote the $40\times 9$ matrix of residual synonymous coordinates and let $T$ denote the $40\times 9$ modeled tAI readout matrix after projection onto the same coordinate vocabulary.  The controls $Z$ include amino-acid composition, organism context, GC3, coding-sequence length, and the simpler codon-density baseline.  With $Q_{\perp Z}$ and $T_{\perp Z}$ denoting residuals after regression on $Z$, the checked finite-row statement is that the translation-readout surface is computed on the residualized design rather than read directly from raw codon counts:
$$
\begin{aligned}
S^{QT} &= S(Q_{\perp Z},T_{\perp Z}),\\
\operatorname{shape}(S^{QT}) &= 9\times 9,\\
R^2_{QT} &= 0.9003849000036579.
\end{aligned}
$$
The positive-entry threshold used for the partial-$R^2$ decision is $10^{-12}$.  The recorded scalar $R^2_{QT}=0.9003849000036579$ lies below the declared degeneracy cutoff $0.999999$, so the result is reported as a powered modeled-readout survival surface rather than as a saturated fit.

\paragraph{Scope boundary.}
The readout here is modeled tAI from tRNA gene-copy counts and wobble weights.  It is not ribosome profiling, measured elongation speed, ribosome occupancy, protein abundance, protein folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, pathway mechanism, phenotype, or a universal biological law.  No measured translation, folding, physical admissibility, function, mechanism, or universality follows from this record unless a separate layer-matched contact supplies that measurement and the corresponding controlled statistic is computed.  Positive $S^{QT}$ entries are therefore bounded modeled-readout survival entries only; they do not imply $S^{QP}$ protein-output survival or $M^{QTP}$ translation-conditioned protein mediation.
