\subsection{Rank-Powered Modeled tAI Survival for the Nine-Coordinate B Star Q6 Residual System}\label{subsec:bioreality-b-star-q6-sqt-powered-modeled-tai}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Claim scope.}
This record tests whether the nine synonymous-residual coordinates of the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ codon-usage system retain modeled translation-layer information after the amino-acid quotient and the declared control family have been imposed.  The biological object is not raw codon usage alone, and it is not a direct ribosome or protein measurement.  It is the controlled survival matrix
$$
\begin{aligned}
S^{QT}_{ij} &= S_{q_i}(r_j \mid Z),\\
q_i &\in \{\mathrm{K\_AAA},\mathrm{Arg\_AGR},\mathrm{Ile\_AUA},\mathrm{Leu\_CUN/UUR},\mathrm{Leu\_UUA/UUG},\\
&\qquad \mathrm{Ser\_UCR/AGY},\mathrm{Ser\_UCA/UCG},\mathrm{Thr\_ACR/ACY},\mathrm{f3\_stress}\},\\
r_j &\in \{\mathrm{K\_AAA},\mathrm{Arg\_AGR},\mathrm{Ile\_AUA},\mathrm{Leu\_CUN/UUR},\mathrm{Leu\_UUA/UUG},\\
&\qquad \mathrm{Ser\_UCR/AGY},\mathrm{Ser\_UCA/UCG},\mathrm{Thr\_ACR/ACY},\mathrm{f3\_stress}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
Here the readout side is modeled per-codon tAI assembled from GtRNAdb anticodon copy counts with dos Reis wobble weights and then projected onto the same synonymous-residual coordinate family.  The control family $Z$ contains amino-acid composition, organism context, GC3, coding-sequence length, and the simpler $M$-density baseline.  Thus the tested question is whether the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual basis is fully absorbed by amino-acid composition plus context plus baseline codon-density information, or whether a controlled modeled-tAI component remains.

\paragraph{Finite-row table.}
The completed row used $40$ organism entries.  The organism list includes human, Arabidopsis, grape, zebrafish, yeast, Escherichia coli, chicken, Xenopus, Ostreococcus, Debaryomyces, Yarrowia, Nostoc, Anabaena, multiple Burkholderia rows, multiple Mycobacterium rows, and additional bacterial and eukaryotic genomes in the same finite table.  The residual coordinate count was $9$, and the modeled-tAI readout count was also $9$, giving a recorded survival-matrix shape of $9\times 9$.  The residualization design contained $24$ control columns, had control rank $23$, and left $17$ residual degrees of freedom after projecting away $Z$.  The powered coordinate design had rank
$$
\begin{aligned}
\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde X) &= 9,
\end{aligned}
$$
so the nine-coordinate residual system was not collapsed by the control projection.  The recorded aggregate modeled translation score was
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{QT} &= 0.9003849000036579,
\end{aligned}
$$
with a positive-entry threshold of $10^{-12}$ for partial-$R^2$ survival and a degeneracy cutoff of $0.999999$.  Since $0.9003849000036579 < 0.999999$, the table is not classified as a degenerate perfect-fit row by the stated cutoff.

\paragraph{Modeled translation interpretation.}
The finite row supports a bounded modeled-translation statement: after the declared controls have been residualized, the nine-coordinate $B^{*}_{Q6}$ system retains substantial modeled-tAI signal in the recorded $S^{QT}$ computation.  The row is specifically a $Q\to T$ modeled-readout contact, where $Q$ denotes the synonymous-residual codon coordinate and $T$ denotes the modeled tAI coordinate.  It is therefore legitimate to report that the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual system is not absorbed by the amino-acid, organism, GC3, length, and $M$-density controls in this modeled tAI experiment.  The strongest scalar summary available in the row is the aggregate $R^2_{QT}=0.9003849000036579$ over the $9\times 9$ powered modeled-tAI matrix.

This coupling information is useful as coordinate-level pressure, but it is weaker than the modeled survival row because it does not by itself estimate $S_{ij}$ under the full control family.  In the present chapter it is used only as supporting context for why the same nine residual directions were plausible candidates for the modeled-tAI survival computation.

\paragraph{Leu CUN/UUR finite contact.}
The Leu CUN/UUR contact gives an additional small finite-row check on the same translation-facing coordinate family.  Across three organisms, the recorded log-ratio signs were
$$
\begin{aligned}
(1.3514980212896148,\;-0.24869078488829677,\;1.0086640520051702),
\end{aligned}
$$
with sign pattern $(1,-1,1)$.  The recorded Pearson value and partial Pearson value were both $1.0$ at $n=3$, while the sign-uniformity flag was false.  This is not a population-level estimate.  Its role here is narrower: it shows that the Leu CUN/UUR direction is a real finite coordinate contact but that its sign is context-dependent in the observed small row.  The forty-organism modeled-tAI survival table is therefore the more relevant row for the present claim.

\paragraph{Boundary of the result.}
The row does not contain measured ribosome profiling, measured elongation speed, ribosome occupancy, proteomics, mature protein abundance, folding, physical admissibility, biochemical function, phenotype, perturbation mechanism, or a universal biological law.  Modeled tAI from tRNA gene-copy counts and wobble weights is a translation-layer model readout, not a direct ribosome trace.  Positive modeled $Q\to T$ survival, even with $R^2_{QT}=0.9003849000036579$, does not imply $Q\to P$ protein-abundance survival and does not establish a mediated $Q\to T\to P$ chain.  Any such higher-layer statement would require its own organism-matched measurement table and a separate controlled statistic.  Likewise, tRNA gene-copy counts are not treated as tRNA abundance or charging unless a separate contact supplies that measurement.

\paragraph{Audit verdict.}
Within its stated scope, the finite row is ready for inclusion as a modeled translation-realization record.  The accepted statement is that a $40$-organism, $9\times 9$ powered modeled-tAI computation retained a nondegenerate controlled modeled-translation signal after a rank-$23$ control projection, with $17$ residual degrees of freedom and aggregate $R^2_{QT}=0.9003849000036579$.  The accepted statement is not a claim about measured ribosome behavior, protein output, folding, function, physical admissibility, mechanism, or universality.
