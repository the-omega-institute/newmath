\subsection{Rank-powered modeled-tAI survival of the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates}
\label{sec:bioreality-b-star-q6-modeled-tai-survival}

\paragraph{Question.}
The tested object is the nine-coordinate synonymous-residual surface $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$, evaluated against a modeled translation-adaptation readout rather than against folded protein state, biochemical function, or organismal phenotype. The coordinate list is
$K\_AAA$, $Arg\_AGR$, $Ile\_AUA$, $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$, $Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG$, $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$, $Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG$, $Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY$, and $f3\_stress$.
For each organism row, codon usage is projected to the synonymous residual coordinates after removing amino-acid composition, organism context, $GC3$, coding-sequence length, and the simpler $M$-density baseline. The readout is a modeled per-codon tAI surface derived from GtRNAdb anticodon copy counts and dos Reis wobble weights, then projected onto the same nine residual coordinate names. This contact is deliberately narrow: it asks whether the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual geometry has remaining description power for a modeled translation-adaptation panel once the declared covariates have already been removed.

\paragraph{Finite-row design.}
The powered modeled-tAI run used $40$ organism rows and produced a $9\times 9$ $S^{QT}$ matrix. The residual-control matrix had $24$ columns, numerical rank $23$, and residual degrees of freedom $17$ after the $Z$ controls. The residualized coordinate design had rank $9$, so the test was not the three-row saturated surface that would make every controlled contrast formally perfect. The positive-entry threshold was $10^{-12}$ for partial $R^2$, and the recorded degenerate-fit cutoff was $0.999999$. The main fitted quantity was
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{QT} &= 0.9003849000036579,\\
\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde X) &= 9,\\
\operatorname{rank}(Z) &= 23,\\
\operatorname{df}_{\mathrm{resid}\mid Z} &= 17.
\end{aligned}
$$
The organism panel contained human, yeast, zebrafish, chicken, $E.\ coli$, Arabidopsis, grape, Xenopus, multiple Burkholderia and Mycobacterium entries, Pseudomonas entries, Nostoc, Anabaena, and additional bacterial and eukaryotic rows, for a total recorded organism count of $40$ in the powered run. The readout column count was also $9$, with columns matched namewise to the nine residual coordinates.

\paragraph{Result.}
The powered modeled-tAI contact supports a positive modeled translation-adaptation survival surface for $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ under the stated controls. In concrete terms, the null in which all nine residual coordinates are absorbed by amino-acid composition, organism identity, $GC3$, coding-sequence length, and $M$-density is not the recorded outcome: the $S^{QT}$ matrix exists, has shape $9\times 9$, and the aggregate modeled-tAI fit reports $R^2_{QT}=0.9003849000036579$ with sufficient residual rank. The associated broader organism sweep independently reports $41$ organisms swept, target $40$ reached, and a per-organism modeled-tAI $R^2_{QT}$ distribution with minimum $0.1579271837024072$, first quartile $0.26353954700074694$, median $0.33934845811598124$, mean $0.38466555440501393$, third quartile $0.5143951545826744$, and maximum $0.8213457220900503$ across $1000$ permutations. These numbers place the modeled-tAI survival claim on a powered cross-organism panel rather than on the earlier three-organism Leu contact.

\paragraph{Relation to the earlier coupling contact.}
A separate codon-usage and tRNA-copy proxy contact computed all nine $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ synonymous residuals on a $61$-codon count space for human, yeast, and $E.\ coli$ K-12. Its break condition was that all nine $|A_{ij}|$ values remain below $0.01$ against the proxy readout; that break condition was not met. The largest absolute proxy coupling was $1882464.1106377328$, with $9$ nonzero entries. That earlier contact is useful as a direction-finding statistic, but it is not promoted to survival by itself. The present modeled-tAI run is stronger because it reports the controlled $S^{QT}$ matrix after the $Z$ residualization and rank checks; the earlier $A_{ij}$ matrix remains only a coupling contact unless the survival statistic is computed.

\paragraph{Leu gate and measured ribosome boundary.}
The Leu $CUN/UUR$ contact remains a small finite-row association: across three organisms it recorded Pearson $1.0$ and partial Pearson $1.0$ for log $CUN/UUR$ usage versus the tRNA-Leu copy ratio, while the organism signs were $[1,-1,1]$ and therefore not uniform. That row is not used here as a universal direction claim. A separate yeast ribosome-occupancy analysis gives a measured local contact boundary: $5754$ ribosome-occupancy genes joined ordered CDS windows without length mismatches, $9$ families were computed, all $9$ had at least $200$ within-position-bin permutations, and the families passing the $\lambda_{DL}=0.01$, $DL=2$, and $C_H>0$ gates were $K\_AAA$, $Arg\_AGR$, and $f3\_stress$. This measured-occupancy contact is compatible with the modeled-tAI result as a nearby translation-layer readout, but it is not the same statistic and does not convert the modeled tAI surface into observed ribosome speed or measured elongation.

\paragraph{Scope.}
The conclusion is therefore specific: $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ has a positive, rank-powered modeled translation-adaptation survival surface after the declared sequence and context controls. The modeled readout comes from tRNA gene-copy and wobble-weight annotation; it is not ribosome profiling, measured elongation speed, ribosome occupancy, proteomics, protein abundance, folding, physical admissibility, biological function, causal mechanism, pathway evidence, or a universal biological law. A later claim at any of those layers would need its own matched rows and its own statistic. In particular, tRNA gene-copy is not treated as tRNA abundance or charging, positive $S^{QT}$ entries are not treated as $S^{QP}$ protein-abundance entries, and the cross-organism modeled-tAI panel is not read as a gene-level mechanism.
