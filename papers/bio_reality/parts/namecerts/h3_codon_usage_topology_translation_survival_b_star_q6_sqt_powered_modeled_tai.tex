\subsection{Modeled tAI survival of the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ synonymous residual surface}
\label{sec:bioreality-b-star-q6-modeled-tai-survival}

\paragraph{Claimed object.}
This record concerns a finite cross-organism matrix test for whether the nine $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates retain translation-facing information after the amino-acid quotient and the declared sequence controls have been imposed.  The tested coordinates are
$K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $Leu_{CUN/UUR}$, $Leu_{UUA/UUG}$, $Ser_{UCR/AGY}$, $Ser_{UCA/UCG}$, $Thr_{ACR/ACY}$, and $f_3$-stress.  The readout side is not measured ribosome traffic.  It is a modeled tAI surface computed from GtRNAdb anticodon copy counts together with dos Reis wobble weights, then projected onto the same nine synonymous-residual coordinate names.  Thus the tested readout list is again
$K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $Leu_{CUN/UUR}$, $Leu_{UUA/UUG}$, $Ser_{UCR/AGY}$, $Ser_{UCA/UCG}$, $Thr_{ACR/ACY}$, and $f_3$-stress.

\paragraph{Finite-row design.}
The powered modeled-tAI run uses $40$ organism rows.  The organism panel is
Homo sapiens, Arabidopsis thaliana, Vitis vinifera, Danio rerio, Saccharomyces cerevisiae, Escherichia coli, Xenopus tropicalis, Gallus gallus, Debaryomyces hansenii, Yarrowia lipolytica, Nostoc sp. PCC 7120, Anabaena variabilis ATCC 29413, and a bacterial panel including Burkholderia, Mycobacterium, Pseudomonas, Rhizobium, Mesorhizobium, Bradyrhizobium, Sinorhizobium, Leptospira, Nocardia, Hahella, Sphingopyxis, Myxococcus, Saccharopolyspora, and Ostreococcus entries.  The control block $Z$ has $24$ columns and rank $23$, leaving residual degrees of freedom $17$ after residualization.  The residualized coordinate design has rank $9$, so the fitted surface is not the three-row rank-saturated case.  The matrix actually computed has shape $9 \times 9$.

\paragraph{Statistic.}
For a residual coordinate $q_i$ and modeled tAI readout coordinate $r_j$, the survival score is treated as a controlled partial-$R^2$ entry after projecting away $Z$.  The global finite-row statistic reported for the modeled-tAI surface is
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{QT} &= 0.9003849000036579,\\
\operatorname{rank}(Z) &= 23,\\
\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde X) &= 9,\\
\operatorname{df}_{\mathrm{residual}} &= 17,\\
\tau_{+} &= 10^{-12},\\
\tau_{\mathrm{degenerate}} &= 0.999999.
\end{aligned}
$$
The reported $R^2_{QT}$ is below the stated degeneracy threshold and above the positivity threshold.  On this finite modeled-readout boundary, the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual block is therefore not absorbed by the amino-acid, organism-context, GC3, length, and $M$-density baseline controls.

\paragraph{Coordinate and readout classification.}
The module classification of the main run is translation realization in the modeled-readout sense: the stored layer is $translation\_realization\_modeled$, not measured ribosome occupancy, not measured elongation speed, and not protein realization.  The design is nevertheless a translation-facing contact because the readout is derived from codon-specific tRNA-copy and wobble-weight annotations rather than from protein abundance, structure, or phenotype.  A separate preliminary coupling run used three organisms, Homo sapiens, Saccharomyces cerevisiae, and Escherichia coli K-12, and found that all nine residual coordinates had nonzero proxy coupling against a tRNA-Leu CUN-versus-UUR log-copy spectrum, with maximum absolute coupling $1882464.1106377328$ against a break threshold $0.01$.  That earlier $A_{ij}$ proxy contact is kept as coupling evidence only; it is not used as a substitute for the controlled $S^{QT}$ statistic above.

\paragraph{Cross-organism sweep context.}
A later organism sweep keeps the same nine-coordinate $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual block and the same $24$-column control family while evaluating modeled tAI organism by organism.  It reports $41$ organisms swept, a target of $40$ organisms reached, and $1000$ permutations for the per-organism modeled-tAI surface.  The distribution of $R^2_{QT}$ across that sweep is
$$
\begin{aligned}
\min R^2_{QT} &= 0.1579271837024072,\\
Q_1(R^2_{QT}) &= 0.26353954700074694,\\
\operatorname{median}(R^2_{QT}) &= 0.33934845811598124,\\
\operatorname{mean}(R^2_{QT}) &= 0.38466555440501393,\\
Q_3(R^2_{QT}) &= 0.5143951545826744,\\
\max R^2_{QT} &= 0.8213457220900503.
\end{aligned}
$$
This sweep supports the interpretation that the modeled-tAI signal is not confined to a single row choice.  It still remains a modeled adaptation-weight result, not a measured ribosome-profiling result.

\paragraph{Adjacent measured-contact checks.}
The closest measured translation-contact check is a descriptive ribosome-occupancy analysis in which synonymous labels are permuted within exact position and amino-acid bins.  That check joined $5754$ genes after length validation, with $5754$ genes overlapping between the occupancy file and ordered CDS table and $0$ length mismatches.  It requested $200$ within-bin permutations and obtained nine computed families with at least $200$ permutations.  Under the stated gate $C_H>0$ and description-length penalty $\lambda_{DL}=0.01$ with $DL=2$, the families passing all gates were $K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, and $f_3$-stress.  This does not replace the modeled-tAI survival matrix, but it shows that measured ribosome-window contacts can be kept in a separate, position-controlled finite-row analysis without promoting the modeled tAI result beyond its own readout type.

\paragraph{Downstream abundance and mRNA comparisons.}
The same nine-coordinate residual family also has bounded downstream comparisons that should not be collapsed into the modeled tAI result.  In yeast mRNA half-life data, the $S^{QM}$ statistic for log$_{10}$ half-life has $n=5085$, $R^2_{QM}=0.05179429044975085$, dominant coordinate $Leu_{CUN/UUR}$, permutation count $1000$, and permutation right-tail value $0.000999000999000999$.  On the same yeast join, the protein-abundance comparison reports $R^2_{QP}=0.2761140373962526$, while conditioning on mRNA half-life gives $R^2_{QP\mid M}=0.24851799532219632$ and an absorbed fraction $0.09994436477872035$ with permutation null mean $0.00019674487584783138$ and null $95$th percentile $0.0014571003414947088$.  These numbers show that abundance and stability readouts are separately measurable, but they do not turn modeled tAI into proteomics evidence or causal mediation.

\paragraph{Per-coordinate decomposition context.}
A separate yeast coordinate decomposition used $n=1258$ joined rows, base size $1800$, five folds, $120$ permutations, and a $24$-rank $Z$ basis to test held-out reducibility of individual residual coordinates against a measured readout dictionary.  It reports nine decomposed coordinates and identifies real-source support for $K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $Leu_{CUN/UUR}$, $Leu_{UUA/UUG}$, $Ser_{UCR/AGY}$, $Thr_{ACR/ACY}$, and $f_3$-stress, while leaving $Ser_{UCA/UCG}$ outside that real-source list for this specific decomposition.  The $f_3$ family decomposition then expands $f_3$-stress into $21$ synonymous-family subcoordinates on $n=1800$ yeast rows, with base size $4759$, five folds, $120$ permutations, $P_{f3}$ norm squared $52.76911713166682$, target norm squared $430.42705205115925$, and $P_{f3}$ fraction of target $0.12259712041843235$.  Nineteen listed families pass the real-source gate there, including $K_{AAA\to AAG}$, $R_{AGA\to AGG}$, $I_{AUU\to AUA}$, $T_{ACU\to ACG}$, and $L_{UUA\to UUG}$.  These decompositions are useful consistency checks for the coordinate system, not replacements for the modeled-tAI survival matrix.

\paragraph{Local boundary.}
The ready claim is narrowly this: under the declared controls and the powered $40$-organism modeled-tAI design, the $9 \times 9$ $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual surface has positive controlled modeled-translation survival with $R^2_{QT}=0.9003849000036579$ and nondegenerate rank diagnostics.  No measured translation mechanism follows from this chapter alone.  No ribosome-mediated elongation claim, protein folding claim, physical admissibility claim, molecular function claim, pathway mechanism claim, phenotype claim, or universal biological law follows unless a separate layer-matched contact supplies the relevant rows and a separate controlled statistic is recorded.
