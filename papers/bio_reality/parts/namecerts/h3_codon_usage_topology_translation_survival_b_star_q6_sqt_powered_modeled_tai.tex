\subsection{Modeled-tAI survival of the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual panel}
\label{subsec:bior reality-bstar-q6-modeled-tai-survival}

\paragraph{Finite readout packet.}
This record tests whether the nine-coordinate $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ synonymous-residual panel retains translation-layer explanatory power after the amino-acid quotient and the standard sequence/context controls are imposed.  The readout is explicitly modeled tAI, not measured ribosome occupancy: codon adaptation weights are computed from GtRNAdb anticodon copy counts using the dos Reis wobble weighting convention and then projected onto the same synonymous-residual coordinate system as the codon-usage panel.  The tested coordinates are
$$
\begin{aligned}
&K\_AAA,\ Arg\_AGR,\ Ile\_AUA,\ Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR,\ Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG,\\
&Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY,\ Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG,\ Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY,\ f3\_stress.
\end{aligned}
$$
The readout side has the same nine named columns, so the computed survival object is a $9\times 9$ $S^{QT}$ surface.  The finite row set contains $40$ organisms, including human, Arabidopsis, grape, zebrafish, budding yeast, chicken, Escherichia coli, Xenopus, Yarrowia, Nostoc, several Burkholderia and Mycobacterium entries, and other bacterial or eukaryotic contacts.  The observed organism count is therefore not the earlier three-organism diagnostic surface; it is a powered cross-organism modeled-readout panel.

\paragraph{Controlled residual model.}
The control block $Z$ contains $24$ columns and has rank $23$.  It includes amino-acid composition, organism/context structure, GC3, CDS length, and the $M$-density baseline, so the reported score is not a raw codon-count association.  After residualizing against $Z$, the remaining degrees of freedom are $17$, and the residualized $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ design has rank $9$.  The computation reports
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{QT} &= 0.9003849000036579,\\
\operatorname{rank}(Z) &= 23,\\
\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde X) &= 9,\\
\operatorname{df}_{\perp Z} &= 17.
\end{aligned}
$$
The degeneracy guard is $0.999999$, so $R^2_{QT}=0.9003849000036579$ is high but not classified as rank-saturated.  The positivity threshold for partial $R^2$ entries is $10^{-12}$.  Under that convention, the packet supports modeled translation-readout survival: the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual panel is not absorbed by the amino-acid, context, GC3, length, and $M$-density description of the modeled tAI surface.

\paragraph{Readback of the survival assertion.}
The local assertion is narrow.  It says that a nine-dimensional synonymous-residual codon-usage panel explains a large controlled fraction of a modeled codon-adaptation readout across $40$ organism rows.  It does not say that any single coordinate has a universal direction, and it does not identify a gene-level mechanism.  The result is best read as a finite controlled survival statement for $S^{QT}$ with modeled tAI as $T$, rather than as a measured translation-efficiency, protein-output, structure, or functional phenotype result.  In formula form, the supported statement is
$$
\begin{aligned}
S^{QT}(B^{\ast}_{Q6}\mid Z) &> 0\quad\text{for the modeled tAI panel},\\
Z &= \{\text{amino-acid composition, organism/context, GC3, length, $M$-density}\},\\
\dim(B^{\ast}_{Q6}) &= 9,\qquad \dim(T\_{\mathrm{modeled}})=9.
\end{aligned}
$$
The survival score is therefore a controlled statistical readback of codon-adaptation geometry.  It is not a statement that tRNA gene copy equals tRNA abundance, charging state, elongation speed, or ribosome dwell time.

\paragraph{Consistency with adjacent finite contacts.}
A wider organism sweep using the same modeled-tAI interface reports $41$ organisms and an $R^2_{QT}$ distribution with minimum $0.1579271837024072$, first quartile $0.26353954700074694$, median $0.33934845811598124$, mean $0.38466555440501393$, third quartile $0.5143951545826744$, and maximum $0.8213457220900503$ over $1000$ permutations.  This companion surface shows that the modeled-tAI signal is not confined to a single row selection, although the present packet remains the canonical $40$-organism $9\times 9$ matrix with $R^2_{QT}=0.9003849000036579$.  A separate ribosome-occupancy dwell check is more direct biologically but different in scope: it uses exact codon-position and amino-acid bins, requests $200$ within-bin synonymous-label permutations, and applies a $\lambda_{DL}=0.01$ gate with $DL=2$ and $C_H>0$.  In that check the families passing all gates are $K\_AAA$, $Arg\_AGR$, and $f3\_stress$.  These numbers are useful as neighboring evidence that the coordinate vocabulary can meet stricter local-readout gates, but they do not replace the modeled-tAI survival computation in this subsection.

\paragraph{Downstream boundaries.}
The same coordinate system appears in protein-abundance and mRNA-stability analyses, but those rows must not be folded back into the present $S^{QT}$ claim.  For example, a yeast mRNA half-life mediation row joins $5085$ records and reports $R^2_{QP}=0.2761140373962526$, $R^2_{QP\mid M}=0.24851799532219632$, an absorbed component $0.027596042074056304$, mediation fraction $0.09994436477872035$, permutation null mean $0.00019674487584783138$, null $p95=0.0014571003414947088$, and right-tail permutation $p=0.000999000999000999$.  A yeast condition comparison on protein abundance joins $2209$ shared proteins, with $R^2_{QP}=0.3050385538305022$ in SD and $0.3280825603424665$ in YEPD, both dominated by $f3\_stress$, with cosine $0.9993775935182967$ and Pearson $0.9983880256003688$ over $1000$ permutations.  These are downstream or cross-layer statistics, not proof that modeled tAI is measured ribosome motion or that protein abundance is mediated by translation in this packet.

\paragraph{Local cannot-claim boundary.}
The chapter-level conclusion is therefore ready but bounded.  The finite evidence supports a controlled modeled-translation survival matrix for $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ against modeled tAI.  It does not establish measured translation efficiency, ribosome-mediated mechanism, protein realization, folding, structural order, physical admissibility, biological function, phenotype, or universality.  Any such stronger statement requires its own matched contact and its own controlled statistic.  In particular, modeled tAI from tRNA gene-copy and wobble weights is not ribo-seq, not proteomics, not measured elongation speed, and not a mechanism assay.  The correct finite conclusion is that the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual panel survives the declared controls on a bounded modeled translation-readout surface, with $40$ organisms, a $9\times 9$ matrix, $\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde X)=9$, $\operatorname{rank}(Z)=23$, residual degrees of freedom $17$, and $R^2_{QT}=0.9003849000036579$.
