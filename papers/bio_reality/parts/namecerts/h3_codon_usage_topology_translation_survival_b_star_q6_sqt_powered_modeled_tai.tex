\subsection{Modeled translation survival of the $B^\ast_{Q6}$ synonymous residual basis}
\label{sec:biorality-b-star-q6-modeled-tai-survival}

\paragraph{Question.}
This record asks whether the nine $B^\ast_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates retain any predictive mass on a translation-adaptation readout after the amino-acid quotient and the standard sequence/context controls have already been imposed.  The tested coordinates are $K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $Leu_{CUN/UUR}$, $Leu_{UUA/UUG}$, $Ser_{UCR/AGY}$, $Ser_{UCA/UCG}$, $Thr_{ACR/ACY}$, and the $f_3$ stress coordinate.  The readout is a modeled per-codon tAI surface derived from GtRNAdb anticodon gene-copy counts together with dos Reis wobble weights, then projected onto the same nine synonymous-residual coordinates.  The result is therefore a modeled translation-layer contact, not a measured ribosome-profiling or proteomics contact.

\paragraph{Finite-row carrier.}
The finite table contains $40$ organism rows and a $9\times 9$ survival surface.  The organism list includes human, Arabidopsis, grape, zebrafish, budding yeast, chicken, $E.\ coli$, multiple Burkholderia entries, multiple Mycobacterium entries, Pseudomonas, Nostoc, Ostreococcus, Debaryomyces, Yarrowia, Nocardia, Leptospira, and Anabaena rows.  The control design has $24$ columns and rank $23$, leaving $17$ residual degrees of freedom after residualizing the readout and the nine-coordinate design against amino-acid composition, organism context, GC3, CDS length, and the $M$-density baseline.  The powered-rank check reports $rank(\widetilde X)=9$, so the nine-coordinate residual design is not collapsed by the control projection.

\paragraph{Statistic.}
Let $Q$ denote the residualized $40\times 9$ matrix of $B^\ast_{Q6}$ coordinates, let $T$ denote the residualized modeled-tAI readout matrix, and let $Z$ denote the declared control matrix.  The tested survival statistic is the partial explained fraction of $T$ by $Q$ after the projection away from $Z$:
$$
\begin{aligned}
S^{QT} &= R^2(T;Q\mid Z),\\
R^2_{QT} &= 0.9003849000036579.
\end{aligned}
$$
The degeneracy guard is $0.999999$, so the observed value is large but not treated as a saturated all-one fit.  The positive-entry threshold used by the finite-row run is $10^{-12}$.  The survival matrix shape is $[9,9]$, with nine modeled tAI readout columns named by the same coordinate labels as the $B^\ast_{Q6}$ residual basis.

\paragraph{Readback.}
The finite result supports the bounded statement that $B^\ast_{Q6}$ is not absorbed by the amino-acid, organism-context, GC3, length, and $M$-density baseline model when the readout is this modeled tAI surface.  In the language of this chapter, the positive object is a controlled modeled-translation survival entry, summarized by $R^2_{QT}=0.9003849000036579$ on $40$ organisms with residual degrees of freedom $17$.  A prior coupling contact on only three organisms reported nonzero proxy couplings for all nine coordinates and a maximum absolute coupling of $1882464.1106377328$, with the break condition $|A_{ij}|<0.01$ for all nine coordinates not met.  That earlier row is weaker: it used a tRNA-Leu CUN-versus-UUR log-copy proxy, not a controlled survival matrix.  The present $40$-organism modeled-tAI computation is the first powered row in this packet that supplies the declared controlled $S^{QT}$ surface.

\paragraph{Local consistency checks.}
The three-organism Leu CUN/UUR contact has Pearson and partial Pearson equal to $1.0$ over $n=3$, but its log-ratio signs are $(1,-1,1)$, so it cannot be read as a uniform cross-organism direction.  The powered modeled-tAI row avoids that rank-deficient setting by using $40$ organism rows.  Downstream protein-abundance and non-translation rows also stay separate.  For example, yeast protein abundance under growth-condition readouts gives $R^2_{QP}=0.3050385538305022$ in SD and $0.3280825603424665$ in YEPD, both with $2209$ shared proteins and dominant coordinate $f_3$ stress; the condition vectors have cosine $0.9993775935182967$ and Pearson $0.9983880256003688$.  Human tissue protein-abundance rows give $R^2_{QP}=0.03626117434944674$ in adult B cells, $0.02320896321347296$ in adult heart, and $0.026133456228248052$ in adult liver, with dominant coordinates $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ser_{UCR/AGY}$, and $Ile_{AUA}$ respectively.  These numbers describe separate protein-abundance contacts and do not enlarge the modeled-tAI claim.

\paragraph{Complement and mediation context.}
The modeled-tAI complement analysis shows that the abundance component outside the modeled-tAI direction varies strongly by organism: the complement fraction $d$ is $0.874628721049266$ for zebrafish, $0.6629960038578532$ for $E.\ coli$ K12 MG1655, $0.9400341806302775$ for chicken, $0.9208949355679455$ for human, $0.8972672018746489$ for $M.\ smegmatis$, and $0.21173219572706703$ for budding yeast.  The corresponding modeled-tAI-mediated complement $m=1-d$ is therefore especially large in yeast, $0.788267804272933$, but this is a projection decomposition, not a causal chain.  The H-candidate ranking uses the stated regularizer $\lambda=0.01$ with $DL_H=\log_2(1+n_{col})$; for example, zebrafish ranks measured TE first with $A_H=0.017594290015070787$, $C_H=0.6573753096184687$, $A_HC_H=0.011566051846174291$, and Score $0.001566051846174291$, while human ranks complex membership first with $A_H=0.007527199987780841$, $C_H=0.8723071163246565$, and Score $-0.0034339698846599055$.  These auxiliary rows help locate what remains after modeled-tAI projection, but they do not change the main interpretation of $S^{QT}$.

\paragraph{mRNA stability side rows.}
The yeast mRNA half-life contact joins $5085$ CDS/protein rows, with a reported CDS join hit rate of $0.9156038419146592$ and payload hash recorded for the Neymotin table.  On that join, the codon-residual to mRNA-half-life surface has $R^2_{QM}=0.05179429044975085$, dominant coordinate $Leu_{CUN/UUR}$, and permutation $p=0.000999000999000999$ over $1000$ permutations.  The same join gives $R^2_{QP}=0.2761140373962526$, and conditioning protein abundance on mRNA half-life gives $R^2_{QP\mid M}=0.24851799532219632$, so the absorbed fraction is $0.027596042074056304$ and the mediation fraction is $0.09994436477872035$.  These rows support a separate statistical decomposition of yeast abundance and mRNA stability; they are not used to promote modeled tAI survival into a biochemical mechanism.

\paragraph{Exclusion boundary.}
The positive modeled-tAI survival row does not assert measured ribosome occupancy, measured elongation speed, protein abundance, protein folding, structural order, physical admissibility, molecular function, pathway activity, phenotype, mechanism, or a universal biological rule.  tRNA gene-copy counts are not tRNA abundance, tRNA charging, or ribosome dynamics.  A positive $S^{QT}$ entry in this chapter means only that the nine-coordinate $B^\ast_{Q6}$ residual design improves a controlled modeled translation-adaptation readout on the stated finite organism panel.  Any stronger statement would require its own matched contact and its own controlled statistic.
