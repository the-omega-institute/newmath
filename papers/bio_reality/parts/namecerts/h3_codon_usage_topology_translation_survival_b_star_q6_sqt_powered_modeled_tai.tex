The residualized $B^{*}_{Q6}$ design has rank $9$, so the nine-coordinate test is not rank-collapsed. $$
\begin{aligned}

\end{aligned}
$$

Positive entries in the $S^{QT}$ surface are judged against the stated partial-$R^{2}$ threshold $10^{-12}$; the packet records that the modeled survival matrix was computed, with $R^{2}_{QT}=0.9003849000036579$ and matrix shape $[9,9]$.

\paragraph{Statistic being asserted.}
For a coordinate $q_i$ and modeled adaptation readout $t_j$, the local statistic is the controlled contribution of $q_i$ to $t_j$ after removing $Z$.  In schematic form, with tildes denoting residuals after projection away from $Z$,
$$
\begin{aligned}
\widetilde q_i &= q_i - \Pi_Z q_i,\\
\widetilde t_j &= t_j - \Pi_Z t_j,\\
S^{QT}_{ij} &> 0
\end{aligned}
$$
means that the residual coordinate supplies positive modeled-tAI description beyond the amino-acid, organism, $\mathrm{GC}3$, length, and simpler-density controls.  Equivalently, the tested model is not fully absorbed by the controlled baseline on the modeled adaptation layer.  The run establishes this only for modeled per-codon tAI projected into the nine-coordinate synonymous-residual frame; it does not by itself assert measured elongation speed or ribosome occupancy.

This supports the weaker claim that the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ coordinates are not numerically silent against that proxy spectrum.  It is not the same as the powered $S^{QT}$ statistic: proxy coupling $A_{ij}$ can motivate the survival test, but the positive modeled-tAI survival row depends on the controlled partial-$R^{2}$ computation described above.

The earlier Leu CUN/UUR finite contact reported a three-row partial Pearson value $1.0$ for the tRNA-Leu ratio comparison, with signs $[1,-1,1]$ across the three organisms and therefore no uniform sign law. A separate complement-projection computation also read modeled-tAI-complement abundance residuals back into the nine-coordinate frame for six organisms. The finite conclusion is that the nine-coordinate $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual panel has a non-vacuous, rank-powered modeled-tAI survival surface after the declared controls. \paragraph{Boundary of the claim.}
This chapter does not infer measured ribosome traffic, translation efficiency from ribosome profiling, protein abundance, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, pathway mechanism, phenotype, or a universal biological rule.  Modeled tAI is not ribo-seq, not measured elongation speed, not ribosome occupancy, not proteomics, and not protein realization.  tRNA gene-copy counts are also not tRNA abundance or charging measurements.  Any measured translation statement would require an organism-matched ribosome or translation-efficiency contact; any protein statement would require matched proteomics; any function, structure, physical-admissibility, mechanism, phenotype, or universality statement would require its own layer-matched measurement and a separate controlled statistic.
