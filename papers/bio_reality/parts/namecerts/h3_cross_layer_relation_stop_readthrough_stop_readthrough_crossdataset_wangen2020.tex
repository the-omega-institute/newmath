\subsection{Stop-readthrough cross-dataset boundary for position-resolved translation survival}
\label{sec:bioreality-stop-readthrough-crossdataset-wangen}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite row surface.}
The record tests a narrow cross-layer question: whether a finite coding-window coordinate derived from the $f_3$-stress residual can be promoted only as far as a same-row translation-readout statistic when the rows also carry stop-context or position-resolved readout information. The carrier is not an abstract biological law. It is a finite table whose rows must contain a coding-window identifier, the $f_3$ residual after amino-acid quotienting, synonymous codon counts in the local window, a bounded wobble-weight context, a matched position-resolved ribosome-occupancy or translation-efficiency readout, mRNA abundance as a control, and amino-acid or position-bin covariates. The admissible statistic is a controlled survival entry
$$
\begin{aligned}
S_{f_3}(T_{\mathrm{position}}\mid Z),
\end{aligned}
$$
where $Z$ includes amino-acid composition or position bins, GC3, length, $M$-density, codon-count baselines, matched mRNA abundance, coverage, mappability, rank sufficiency, and simpler translation baselines.

\paragraph{Observed stop-readthrough contact.}
The Wangen stop-readthrough cross-dataset row supplies a real finite readout surface for the stop-readthrough side of the claim. The verified run `7c85e126c888` records $n=55886$ rows, runtime $133.552797$ seconds, and an untreated rank correlation
$$
\begin{aligned}
\rho_{\mathrm{untreated}} &= 0.032718.
\end{aligned}
$$
This is a small but concrete scalar on a large cross-dataset panel. It can be cited as evidence that the stop-context readout has been joined and evaluated as finite rows, not as evidence that the $f_3$-stress coordinate has already survived the full position-resolved translation test. A second Wangen-derived G418 termination surface, run `fe18fff6841f`, gives a more condition-specific positive-readout subset: $7658$ joined rows before nonzero filtering, $4982$ rows after filtering, nonzero fraction $0.65056$, runtime $303.534$ seconds, and endpoint ``G418-induced RRTS nonzero subset, rank-average scaled to [0,1]'' on sheet ``G418 2.0mg|mL 24hr merged''. These numbers show that a finite stop-readthrough readout can be assembled and filtered, but they do not by themselves attach the missing $f_3$-stress, wobble-weight, matched mRNA, and same-window footprint coordinates required for the present survival statistic.

\paragraph{Coordinate requirement.}
The proposed $f_3$ member is admissible only after amino-acid quotienting has removed direct amino-acid-composition explanations. The residual coordinate must then be tested under a wobble-weight asymmetry condition, written locally as
$$
\begin{aligned}
w_c \neq w_c^{f_3},
\end{aligned}
$$
with the inequality observed or computed from the row-level weight surface rather than assumed from codon labels. The same row must then carry the translation readout. A modeled tAI or stAI weight can contribute a bounded codon-adaptation covariate, but it is not an observed ribosome-footprint row. Conversely, a stop-readthrough or ribosome-footprint row can provide translation-layer contact, but it does not contain the $f_3$ residual coordinate unless the coding window has been explicitly joined to the residual-basis table.

\paragraph{Break condition.}
The chapter accepts only the following finite promotion rule. Let $T_{\mathrm{position}}$ denote the matched position-resolved occupancy, readthrough, or translation-efficiency readout and let $Z$ denote the full control set. The survival claim is supported only if the held-out improvement of the $f_3$ coordinate over the simpler baselines is positive after all controls:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta_{\mathrm{heldout}}(f_3;T_{\mathrm{position}}\mid Z) &> 0.
\end{aligned}
$$
The current verified Wangen cross-dataset fact establishes a concrete stop-readthrough surface with $55886$ rows and $\rho_{\mathrm{untreated}}=0.032718$; the G418 fact establishes a condition-specific nonzero subset with $4982$ analyzed rows. Neither fact, as stated here, supplies the full same-window matrix containing $f_3$ residuals, local synonymous codon counts, wobble weights, matched mRNA abundance, and position-controlled footprint readouts. Therefore the correct local interpretation is boundary-level readiness for the statistic, not a positive $f_3$ survival call.

\paragraph{Local no-promotion rule.}
No claim about translation realization follows from the $f_3$ residual coordinate, stop-context readthrough rows, modeled wobble weights, or codon-window geometry alone. No protein abundance, folding, physical admissibility, biological function, mechanism, phenotype, or universal law follows from the finite rows reported here. Those layers require their own matched contacts and their own controlled statistics. The present chapter records a finite stop-readthrough readout surface and the exact row-level conditions under which it could participate in an $f_3$-stress translation-survival test.
