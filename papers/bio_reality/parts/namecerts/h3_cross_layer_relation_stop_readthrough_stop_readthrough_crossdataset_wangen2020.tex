\subsection{Stop-context readthrough as a bounded cross-dataset translation readout}\label{sec:bioreality-stop-readthrough-crossdataset-wangen2020}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite row surface.}
This record treats stop-codon readthrough as a finite translation-layer readout rather than as a general claim about protein fate.  The cross-dataset Wangen-style table has $n=55886$ joined rows and reports an untreated rank correlation $\rho=0.032718$ for the stop-context readthrough coordinate under the recorded run $7c85e126c888$.  The computation time for that row audit was $133.552797$ seconds.  A second Wangen readthrough contact, restricted to the G418-induced RRTS nonzero subset, used the sheet named G418 2.0mg|mL 24hr merged, joined $7658$ rows before the nonzero filter, retained $n=4982$ rows after filtering, and recorded a nonzero RRTS fraction of $0.65056$ with runtime $303.534$ seconds.  These numbers give the chapter its empirical scale: the untreated cross-dataset association is large in row count but small in monotone effect size, while the drug-induced subset is a narrower, explicitly filtered readout panel.

\paragraph{Coordinate and readout.}
The biological object is a stop-context readthrough row keyed by local coding-sequence context and a measured readthrough response.  The row is allowed to carry synonymous or codon-window coordinates, including an $f_3$-stress residual coordinate when that coordinate has already been formed after amino-acid quotienting.  It is not allowed to turn that coordinate into translation realization by itself.  The only admissible readback in this chapter is a same-row readthrough statistic whose outcome is observed in the Wangen-style readout surface.  In finite-row notation the relevant boundary is:
$$
\begin{aligned}
T_{\mathrm{stop}} &= \text{measured stop-context readthrough response},\\
Z &= \text{amino-acid, position, GC3, length, M-density, mRNA, and simpler-baseline controls},\\
S_{f3}(T_{\mathrm{stop}}\mid Z) &> 0
\end{aligned}
$$
only when a held-out or otherwise controlled statistic improves over the declared controls.  The observed untreated value $\rho=0.032718$ is therefore evidence of a weak finite association in the recorded table, not a completed survival theorem for an $f_3$-stress mechanism.

\paragraph{Cross-dataset interpretation.}
The cross-dataset entry is useful because it separates three layers that are often conflated.  First, the stop-context readthrough response is an observed translation-layer readout.  Second, codon-window or residual coordinates are explanatory candidates attached to the same finite rows.  Third, downstream protein abundance, folding, physical admissibility, function, mechanism, phenotype, and universality are not present in the row surface.  The $n=55886$ untreated panel can support a bounded statement that the recorded stop-context coordinate has a small nonzero rank relation to untreated readthrough.  The G418 subset can support a separate bounded statement that the induced readthrough sheet contains a filtered response surface with $4982$ nonzero rows out of $7658$ joined rows before filtering.  Neither panel, by these numbers alone, supplies a protein-level or systems-level conclusion.

\paragraph{Break condition.}
The chapter breaks if the readthrough association is promoted beyond the finite row surface.  In particular, it breaks if $\rho=0.032718$ is described as a law of translation, as a ribosome mechanism, as protein abundance, as folding evidence, or as a universal property of stop codons.  It also breaks if modeled wobble weights or codon geometry are treated as observed ribosome occupancy without a same-row measured readout.  The correct local claim is narrower: Wangen-style stop-context rows provide a measured readthrough contact, and the recorded cross-dataset audit supplies a finite association size and row count for that contact.

\paragraph{Admissible conclusion.}
This chapter is therefore a bounded cross-layer record.  It may say that the stop-context readthrough contact has a verified untreated cross-dataset surface with $n=55886$ and $\rho=0.032718$, and that a related G418-induced nonzero RRTS subset retains $4982$ rows from $7658$ joined rows with nonzero fraction $0.65056$.  It may also say that these rows are suitable candidates for a controlled survival test of an $f_3$-stress or synonymous-context coordinate, provided that amino-acid composition, position or binning, GC3, length, M-density, matched mRNA, rank sufficiency, and simpler baselines are included.  It may not claim translation realization from geometry alone, and it may not infer folding, physical admissibility, biological function, mechanism, phenotype, or a global biological rule unless a separate layer-matched contact supplies the corresponding finite rows and a controlled statistic.
