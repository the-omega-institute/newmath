\subsection{Stop-readthrough cross-dataset boundary for an $f_3$-stress translation-survival claim}
\label{sec:bioreality-stop-readthrough-crossdataset-wangen2020}

\paragraph{Finite row object.}
The record considered here is a finite cross-layer row table for stop-context readthrough in which each admissible row is allowed to carry an $f_3$-stress residual coordinate, local synonymous-codon counts, a bounded wobble-weight context, a matched translation readout, an mRNA control, and amino-acid or position-bin controls.  The intended statistic is not a free-standing codon-geometry score.  It is a controlled survival entry of the form $S_{f3}(T_{\mathrm{position}}\mid Z)$, where $T_{\mathrm{position}}$ is a position-resolved ribosome-occupancy or explicitly bounded translation-efficiency readout and $Z$ includes amino-acid composition, position bin, GC3, coding-sequence length, methionine-density baseline, codon-count controls, matched mRNA abundance, coverage, mappability, and simpler-baseline ablations.  The chapter therefore treats the stop-readthrough evidence as a finite reality check on a translation-layer boundary, not as a general law about codons.

\paragraph{Observed Wangen-style readthrough rows.}
The cross-dataset stop-readthrough record supplies a large finite panel: the verified run $7c85e126c888$ reports $n=55886$ rows and an untreated rank correlation $\rho=0.032718$, with runtime $133.552797$ seconds.  A related Wangen termination contact gives a more specific G418-induced readthrough subset: run $fe18fff6841f$ uses the endpoint ``G418-induced RRTS nonzero subset, rank-average scaled to $[0,1]$'', on sheet ``G418 2.0mg|mL 24hr merged'', with $7658$ joined rows before the nonzero filter, $4982$ retained rows after that filter, nonzero fraction $0.65056$, and runtime $303.534$ seconds.  These numbers make the row surface non-vacuous: there is enough finite readthrough material to discuss a controlled boundary.  They do not by themselves supply the full same-window $f_3$-stress matrix required for a positive $S_{f3}$ statement.

\paragraph{Statistic being bounded.}
The admissible positive statement would have the following shape.  Let $Q_{f3}$ be the amino-acid-quotiented $f_3$-stress residual coordinate, let $W$ denote the wobble-weight surface with observed asymmetry $w_c \ne w_c^{f_3}$, and let $T$ be the matched readthrough or ribosome-occupancy readout for the same coding window.  The chapter asks whether the row table supports a held-out improvement after controls:
$$
\begin{aligned}
S_{f3}(T\mid Z) &> 0,\\
Z &= (\mathrm{AA},\mathrm{position},\mathrm{GC3},\mathrm{length},\mathrm{M\ density},\mathrm{codon\ counts},\mathrm{mRNA},\mathrm{coverage},\mathrm{mappability}).
\end{aligned}
$$
The verified untreated value $\rho=0.032718$ is therefore only a weak descriptive association on the available cross-dataset stop-readthrough surface.  It is not reported here as the survival statistic above, because the required same-row joins to $Q_{f3}$, wobble weights, position-resolved footprint occupancy, mRNA abundance, and the full ablation set have not all been recorded for this exact packet.

\paragraph{Cross-dataset interpretation.}
The finite evidence is useful because it separates two things that are often conflated.  First, the Wangen-derived surfaces show that stop-context readthrough can be represented as a finite ranked readout: $55886$ rows in the cross-dataset panel and $4982$ nonzero G418-induced rows after filtering are concrete biological rows, not symbolic labels.  Second, the proposed $f_3$-stress claim asks for an additional controlled survival step on the same or properly matched coding windows.  The row counts and the nonzero fraction $0.65056$ show that the readout side is not empty, while the small untreated $\rho$ and the missing same-window residual-coordinate join prevent promotion to a directionality law.  Thus the chapter records a bounded opportunity for testing, not a completed mechanism.

\paragraph{Local refusal boundary.}
No translation-realization claim follows from the $f_3$ coordinate alone.  No protein abundance, folding, structural order, physical admissibility, biological function, phenotype, mechanism, or universal biological rule follows from the stop-readthrough rows, from wobble-weight asymmetry, or from codon/window geometry unless a separate layer-matched contact supplies the relevant measurement and the corresponding controlled statistic is computed.  In particular, modeled tAI or stAI weights may help define a bounded codon-adaptation context, but they are not observed ribosome occupancy.  Likewise, matched mRNA abundance is a control for translation-efficiency residualization, not a substitute for a ribosome-footprint or readthrough statistic.

\paragraph{Chapter verdict.}
The correct reading is a boundary-positive and survival-unproven result.  The stop-readthrough contacts provide finite row material with explicit sample sizes and measured rank summaries.  They justify keeping the $f_3$-stress position-resolved translation-survival packet in the testable cross-layer class.  They do not yet justify the stronger statement $S_{f3}(T_{\mathrm{position}}\mid Z)>0$ under the declared controls.  The next admissible promotion would require a same-organism, same-CDS, same-window matrix joining ordered coding sequence, codon counts, wobble-weight rows, matched mRNA abundance, position-resolved ribosome-footprint or readthrough occupancy, coverage and mappability checks, and a held-out simpler-baseline ablation.
