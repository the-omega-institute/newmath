\subsection{Cross-dataset stop-context readthrough as a bounded translation readout}
\label{sec:bioreality-stop-readthrough-crossdataset-wangen2020}

\paragraph{Finite row surface.}
This record concerns the finite Wangen-style stop-context panel identified by
\texttt{h3.cross\_layer\_relation.stop\_readthrough.stop\_readthrough\_crossdataset\_wangen2020}.  The verified finite-row run is \texttt{7c85e126c888}, with $n=55886$ joined rows, runtime $133.552797$ seconds, and untreated rank correlation
$\rho=0.032718$.  The numerical entry is deliberately read as a weak but nonzero cross-dataset readout of stop-context organization under the untreated condition, not as a complete mechanism.  In the notation of this chapter, the observed row table is a finite set
$$
\begin{aligned}
T=\{(s_a,y_a,z_a):1\leq a\leq 55886\},
\end{aligned}
$$
where $s_a$ is the local stop-context or derived sequence coordinate available for row $a$, $y_a$ is the untreated readthrough-aligned response used by the verified run, and $z_a$ denotes the finite set of controls and row identifiers available to the cross-dataset join.  The reported statistic is summarized as
$$
\begin{aligned}
\rho_{\mathrm{untreated}}(s,y)=0.032718.
\end{aligned}
$$
The chapter therefore records a bounded readout contact: stop-context rows do carry a measurable cross-dataset association in this run, but the magnitude is small and must remain attached to the exact finite table and endpoint.

\paragraph{Coordinate being tested.}
The surrounding vision-level question is whether a residual synonymous coordinate, especially an $f_3$-stress coordinate after amino-acid quotienting, can survive as a cross-layer signal only after the same coding windows are joined to translation readouts.  For this stop-context run, the finite evidence is narrower.  It supplies a cross-dataset stop-readthrough statistic, not a full same-window $f_3$-stress survival matrix.  A valid promoted statistic would need rows containing, on the same finite support, an $f_3$-stress residual coordinate, local synonymous codon counts, wobble-weight context, a matched position-resolved ribosome-occupancy or translation-efficiency readout, matched mRNA abundance, and amino-acid or position-bin controls.  The intended survival entry can be written as
$$
\begin{aligned}
S_{f_3}(T_{\mathrm{position}}\mid Z)>0,
\end{aligned}
$$
where $Z$ includes amino-acid composition, position-bin or amino-acid-bin controls, GC3, length, M-density, codon-count terms, matched mRNA abundance, coverage and mappability limits, rank sufficiency, and simpler-baseline ablations.  The available stop-readthrough statistic does not by itself instantiate this inequality.

\paragraph{What the verified number supports.}
The verified value $\rho=0.032718$ supports only the following local statement: in the finite cross-dataset stop-readthrough panel with $55886$ rows, the untreated endpoint has a small positive rank association with the row-level stop-context coordinate used by the run.  This is enough to keep the stop-context readthrough contact scientifically live as a bounded cross-layer readout.  It is not enough to assert that $f_3$-stress geometry has survived full translation controls, because the finite proof object reported here does not include the required same-window $f_3$ residual coordinate, wobble-asymmetry surface, matched mRNA denominator, position-resolved ribosome-footprint matrix, and complete ablation panel.  Thus the row table can be used as a stop-readthrough contact and as an empirical warning that translation-layer endpoints are not invisible to local sequence context, but it cannot be substituted for a position-resolved $f_3$ survival test.

\paragraph{Rank and break condition.}
The admissible promotion rule is finite and negative by default.  Let $M_0$ be the simpler baseline using amino-acid, position, GC3, length, M-density, mRNA, and coarse codon-usage controls, and let $M_1$ add the candidate $f_3$-stress residual coordinate with its wobble-weight context.  The chapter would count the $f_3$ entry as surviving only if a held-out comparison satisfies
$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta_{\mathrm{heldout}}
  &= \operatorname{score}(M_1)-\operatorname{score}(M_0) \\
  &>0,
\end{aligned}
$$
with sufficient rank after the stated controls.  The present run reports the stop-readthrough row count and untreated association, but not such a held-out $\Delta_{\mathrm{heldout}}$ for the $f_3$ coordinate.  Consequently, any attempted reading of the $55886$-row stop-readthrough statistic as a full $f_3$ translation-survival entry breaks the local rule of interpretation.

\paragraph{Local exclusion boundary.}
No claim about translation realization follows from codon or window geometry alone.  No protein abundance, folding state, physical admissibility, molecular function, system phenotype, mechanism, or universal biological law follows from the reported stop-readthrough association.  Modeled tAI or stAI weights, when present in neighboring packets, are codon-adaptation annotations rather than observed ribosome-occupancy rows.  Likewise, the existence of a stop-readthrough contact is not a substitute for same-row position-resolved ribosome occupancy, matched mRNA abundance, or downstream protein-layer measurements.  The only positive statement retained here is the finite and bounded one: a Wangen-style cross-dataset stop-context readthrough table with $n=55886$ has an untreated rank association of $0.032718$ in run \texttt{7c85e126c888}, and this supports continued testing of local stop-context translation readouts under stricter same-row controls.
