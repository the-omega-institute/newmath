\subsection{Stop-context readthrough as a bounded cross-dataset contact}\label{sec:bioreality-stop-readthrough-crossdataset-wangen2020}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite row carrier.}
The BioReality object in this chapter is a finite table of stop-context rows, not a free-standing claim about translation in general.  Each row is keyed by a local termination context and by the readout surface used in the Wangen stop-readthrough assays.  The cross-dataset contact currently contributes $55886$ joined rows for the untreated comparison, with a measured rank correlation of $0.032718$ on the untreated readthrough surface.  A second Wangen-derived termination contact supplies the drug-conditioned finite subset: before restriction to nonzero signal it has $7658$ joined rows, and after the nonzero RRTS filter it has $4982$ rows, with nonzero fraction $0.65056$ on the G418 $2.0\,\mathrm{mg}/\mathrm{mL}$, $24\,\mathrm{hr}$ merged sheet.  Thus the available evidence is finite, row-indexed, and assay-specific:
$$
\begin{aligned}
N_{\mathrm{untreated}} &= 55886,\\
\rho_{\mathrm{untreated}} &= 0.032718,\\
N_{\mathrm{G418, joined}} &= 7658,\\
N_{\mathrm{G418, nonzero}} &= 4982,\\
\pi_{\mathrm{G418, nonzero}} &= 0.65056.
\end{aligned}
$$
The two row systems are useful because they keep the chapter anchored to measured stop-context readback rather than to codon geometry alone.

\paragraph{Local claim.}
The admissible statement is narrow.  Stop-context sequence features have a small but directly measured cross-dataset association with readthrough in the untreated Wangen surface, and a separate G418-conditioned surface provides a nonzero subset suitable for termination-context analysis.  This supports a bounded cross-layer contact at the translation-termination readout level: local sequence context can be tested against observed stop-readthrough rows after the table is joined, filtered, and ranked.  The untreated statistic is deliberately reported as a weak scalar, not as a mechanistic law.  Its value, $\rho=0.032718$, is positive but small, so the chapter treats it as a row-level survival signal that must remain tied to the exact finite assay surface.

\paragraph{Relation to the proposed $f_3$-stress window test.}
The broader conjectural packet asks whether an $f_3$-stress residual coordinate can survive as a controlled position-resolved translation coordinate when synonymous codon counts, wobble-weight context, matched mRNA abundance, amino-acid or position-bin controls, and a same-window ribosome-occupancy or translation-efficiency readout are all present in the same finite table.  The stop-readthrough contact does not yet supply that full matrix.  It supplies termination-context readthrough rows, not a same-CDS, same-window panel containing $f_3$ residual coordinates, wobble-weight asymmetry, codon-indexed ribosome footprints, and matched mRNA controls.  Consequently the Wangen cross-dataset result can be used as a measured termination readout contact, while the $f_3$ position-resolved survival statistic remains unpromoted until its own joined window table exists.

\paragraph{Controlled statistic boundary.}
The chapter therefore separates two quantities.  The observed stop-readthrough contact is the finite Wangen readout summarized above.  The still-missing $f_3$ statistic would have the schematic form
$$
\begin{aligned}
S_{f_3}(T_{\mathrm{position}}\mid Z) &> 0,\\
Z &= \{\mathrm{AA\ composition},\ \mathrm{position\ bin},\ \mathrm{GC3},\ \mathrm{length},\ \mathrm{M\ density},\ \mathrm{mRNA},\ \mathrm{simpler\ baselines}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
No such positive value is recorded in the supplied finite rows.  The stop-readthrough surface can motivate the readout side of the test, but it cannot stand in for the missing same-window occupancy matrix or for the ablation sequence needed to show held-out improvement.

\paragraph{No-promotion rule.}
Nothing in this chapter licenses a claim about general translation realization, protein abundance, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, phenotype, mechanism, or universality.  The untreated Wangen association and the G418 nonzero subset are measured readthrough contacts only.  Modeled tAI or stAI weights, codon-window coordinates, wobble-weight asymmetry, and $f_3$ residual geometry do not become biological readouts unless the same rows also contain the appropriate measured translation surface and the controlled survival statistic remains positive after the stated covariates and simpler-baseline checks.  In the present finite record, the safe conclusion is that Wangen stop-context readthrough is a concrete termination-layer contact, while the $f_3$ position-resolved cross-layer survival claim remains a bounded target awaiting a joined window-level dataset.
