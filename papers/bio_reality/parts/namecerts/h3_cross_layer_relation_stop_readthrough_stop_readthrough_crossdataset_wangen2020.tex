\subsection{Cross-dataset stop-readthrough boundary in Wangen-style termination data}
\label{sec:bioreality-stop-readthrough-crossdataset-wangen2020}

\paragraph{Finite row under examination.}
This record concerns a finite table of stop-context readthrough measurements in which the observable layer is a termination readout, not a protein-function, folding, or organism-level phenotype readout.  The cross-dataset row indexed by \texttt{h3.cross\_layer\_relation.stop\_readthrough.stop\_readthrough\_crossdataset\_wangen2020} contains $n=55886$ joined entries and reports an untreated rank correlation
$\rho=0.032718$ after a run time of $133.552797$ seconds.  The companion G418-focused termination row, \texttt{h3.cross\_layer\_relation.translation\_termination.stop\_context\_readthrough\_g418\_wangen2020}, uses the sheet \texttt{G418 2.0mg|mL 24hr merged}; it begins from $7658$ joined rows before the nonzero-filter step, retains $4982$ rows, and records a nonzero RRTS fraction of $0.65056$ with endpoint described as the G418-induced nonzero RRTS subset rank-averaged onto $[0,1]$.  These numbers make the chapter a concrete finite-row contact: there is an observed termination/readthrough surface, and the measured association is small but explicitly numerical.

\paragraph{Local mathematical form.}
Let $T$ denote the finite row set of the untreated cross-dataset readthrough table, with $|T|=55886$.  Each row $t\in T$ carries a stop-context identifier, a readthrough-associated response coordinate, and whatever matched covariates were retained by the finite analysis.  The observed scalar in this chapter is not a universal law; it is the single finite correlation statistic
$$
\begin{aligned}
|T| &= 55886,\\
\rho_{\mathrm{untreated}}(T) &= 0.032718.
\end{aligned}
$$
For the G418 termination contact, the finite retained set $G$ is described by
$$
\begin{aligned}
|G_{\mathrm{joined}}| &= 7658,\\
|G_{\mathrm{nonzero}}| &= 4982,\\
\frac{|G_{\mathrm{nonzero}}|}{|G_{\mathrm{joined}}|} &= 0.65056.
\end{aligned}
$$
The interpretation is therefore bounded: the rows support the existence of a measurable stop-context/readthrough readout and a weak untreated cross-dataset association.  They do not by themselves establish a mechanistic explanation of termination, a protein-abundance consequence, or a general translation rule.

\paragraph{Relation to the proposed $f_3$-stress coordinate.}
The proposed $f_3$-stress survival statement requires a stronger joined object than the stop-readthrough row alone.  Its admissible carrier is a finite coding-sequence window table in which every row simultaneously contains an $f_3$-stress residual coordinate after amino-acid quotienting, local synonymous codon counts, a bounded wobble-weight context, a matched position-resolved ribosome-occupancy or translation-efficiency readout, an mRNA control, and amino-acid or position-bin controls.  The Wangen-style stop-readthrough result supplies a termination-layer reality contact, but it does not yet supply that full same-window matrix.  In particular, the observed $\rho=0.032718$ is a stop-readthrough statistic, whereas the requested $f_3$ statistic would have to be a controlled survival entry of the form
$$
\begin{aligned}
S_{f_3}(T_{\mathrm{position}}\mid Z) > 0,
\end{aligned}
$$
where $Z$ includes amino-acid composition or bin, position, GC3, coding-sequence length, methionine density, matched mRNA abundance, local codon-count baselines, coverage limits, mappability limits, and simpler-baseline ablations.  No such positive held-out improvement is contained in the finite row values reported here.

\paragraph{What the contact supports.}
The cross-dataset termination row supports a modest but non-null observable contact between stop-context data and readthrough-associated response in a large finite table.  The G418 row separately shows that, under the named treatment sheet, the nonzero RRTS subset is substantial enough to define a rank-scaled endpoint on $4982$ retained rows.  Thus the record is useful as a termination-readout boundary: it tells us that readthrough can be represented as a finite measured response and that the table is large enough to be used in downstream checks.  It also constrains interpretation: any claim promoted beyond stop-context/readthrough association must attach its own matched rows and controls rather than borrowing force from the existence of this table.

\paragraph{What remains blocked.}
No translation-realization claim follows from the numbers above unless a separate same-row translation readout is joined and survives the declared controls.  No folding, structural order, physical admissibility, biological function, system phenotype, mechanism, or universality follows from the stop-readthrough table.  Modeled tAI or stAI weights, wobble-weight asymmetry, stop-context geometry, and $f_3$-stress notation are not observations of ribosome occupancy by themselves.  A later positive claim would need the same organism, the same coding sequence or local window, joined codon counts, bounded wobble weights, position-resolved footprint or translation-efficiency rows, matched mRNA abundance, rank sufficiency, and a positive held-out improvement over simpler codon and position baselines.  Until that matrix exists, this chapter records a measured termination contact and a precise non-promotion boundary, not a general biological law.
