\subsection{Stop-context readthrough as a bounded cross-dataset control surface}\label{subsec:bioreality-stop-readthrough-crossdataset-wangen2020}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite row surface.}
The Wangen stop-readthrough record is a finite cross-dataset translation-termination contact, not a free-standing law about codon geometry.  Its largest joined table contains $55{,}886$ rows and reports an untreated rank correlation
$\rho = 0.032718$ for the stop-context readthrough cross-dataset statistic.  A second Wangen-derived termination surface, using the G418-induced RRTS nonzero subset, begins from $7{,}658$ joined rows before the nonzero filter and retains $4{,}982$ rows after filtering.  The retained fraction of nonzero RRTS rows is $0.65056$, and the endpoint is the G418-induced RRTS nonzero subset rank-averaged and scaled to $[0,1]$.  These two finite surfaces give the chapter a concrete translation-termination readout, but they do not by themselves provide a position-resolved coding-window ribosome-occupancy matrix for an $f_3$-stress residual coordinate.

\paragraph{Name-certificate carrier.}
The certificate carrier is the finite table whose rows are keyed by the stop-context construct or joined sequence row and whose observable columns include the measured readthrough readout, the treatment or untreated endpoint indicator, and any available local stop-neighborhood sequence annotations.  For the cross-dataset contact the numeric certificate is
$$
\begin{aligned}
N_{\mathrm{cross}} &= 55{,}886,\\
\rho_{\mathrm{untreated}} &= 0.032718.
\end{aligned}
$$
For the G418 nonzero subset the corresponding finite certificate is
$$
\begin{aligned}
N_{\mathrm{joined}} &= 7{,}658,\\
N_{\mathrm{nonzero}} &= 4{,}982,\\
\frac{N_{\mathrm{nonzero}}}{N_{\mathrm{joined}}} &= 0.65056.
\end{aligned}
$$
The certificate therefore records a measured stop-readthrough response surface with a nontrivial finite row count and a separately filtered induced endpoint.  It is admissible as a translation-termination contact because the response is a measured readthrough readout rather than an inferred consequence of symbolic codon coordinates.

\paragraph{Relation to the $f_3$-stress survival question.}
The $f_3$-stress conjecture asks for a stricter row type than the Wangen stop-readthrough rows currently certify.  The requested row must simultaneously contain an amino-acid-quotiented $f_3$ residual coordinate, synonymous codon counts in a fixed coding-window, a bounded wobble-weight context, matched mRNA abundance, amino-acid or position-bin controls, and a same-window position-resolved ribosome-occupancy or translation-efficiency response.  The Wangen stop-readthrough surface supplies a measured termination readout, but the finite facts cited here do not assert that each readthrough row is joined to the $f_3$ coordinate, the wobble-weight asymmetry $w_c \neq w_c^{f_3}$, or the full control set required for a positive survival entry.

\paragraph{Controlled statistic.}
The intended survival statistic for the $f_3$ packet is a controlled held-out improvement of a translation-position response after nuisance variables are included.  In local notation, the only admissible positive call has the form
$$
\begin{aligned}
S_{f_3}(T_{\mathrm{position}}\mid Z) &> 0,\\
Z &= \{\mathrm{AA\ composition},\ \mathrm{position\ bin},\ \mathrm{GC3},\ \mathrm{length},\ \mathrm{M\ density},\ \mathrm{mRNA},\ \mathrm{simpler\ baselines}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The Wangen rows can participate in a termination-specific control analysis only after the required columns are present in the same finite table.  The observed $\rho_{\mathrm{untreated}}=0.032718$ is therefore a measured cross-dataset scalar for the stop-readthrough contact, not a proof that the $f_3$ coordinate improves a position-resolved ribosome-occupancy predictor under the full control set.

\paragraph{Rank and break condition.}
The rank condition is local to the joined design matrix.  A positive result requires enough nondegenerate rows after controls for the $f_3$ column to be estimable and for held-out improvement to survive simpler codon-count, GC3, length, M-density, mRNA, amino-acid, and position-bin alternatives.  The break condition is triggered if review status, contact naming, modeled wobble weights, or symbolic survival notation is treated as a computed same-row statistic before the joined matrix exists.  The finite Wangen facts keep the stop-readthrough readout real, while leaving the $f_3$ position-window survival claim unpromoted until the stricter row join is supplied.

\paragraph{Local no-promotion boundary.}
This chapter supports only a bounded readout statement: the Wangen surfaces give finite measured stop-readthrough data, including $55{,}886$ cross-dataset rows and a $4{,}982$-row G418 nonzero subset.  No translation mechanism, protein abundance, folding, structural order, physical admissibility, molecular function, system phenotype, or universal biological rule follows from these numbers.  Those interpretations require separate layer-matched contacts and their own controlled finite-row statistics.  In particular, modeled tAI or stAI weights cannot be substituted for observed ribosome occupancy, and a stop-context readthrough table cannot be reinterpreted as an $f_3$-stress position-resolved ribosome-occupancy table unless the required same-row codon-window, wobble-weight, mRNA, and position controls are explicitly present.
