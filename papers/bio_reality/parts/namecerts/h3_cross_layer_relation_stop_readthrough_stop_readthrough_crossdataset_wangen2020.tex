\subsection{Stop-readthrough cross-dataset boundary for an $f_3$-stress translation-survival record}
\label{sec:bioreality-stop-readthrough-crossdataset-wangen2020}

\paragraph{Finite row under audit.}
The record considered here is the cross-dataset stop-readthrough row keyed by the claim identifier $\mathrm{h3.cross\_layer\_relation.stop\_readthrough.stop\_readthrough\_crossdataset\_wangen2020}$.  Its measured finite table contains $55{,}886$ joined entries, with runtime $133.552797$ seconds and an untreated rank correlation
$$
\begin{aligned}
\rho_{\mathrm{untreated}} &= 0.032718 .
\end{aligned}
$$
This number is intentionally read as a small finite-row association, not as a general law of codon biology.  The same evidence bundle also contains a treatment-specific stop-context readthrough contact from the Wangen 2020 G418 surface: the sheet is $\mathrm{G418\ 2.0mg|mL\ 24hr\ merged}$, the endpoint is the nonzero-subset G418-induced RRTS value rank-averaged onto $[0,1]$, the inner join contains $7{,}658$ rows before the nonzero filter, the retained nonzero subset has $4{,}982$ rows, the nonzero fraction is $0.65056$, and the runtime is $303.534$ seconds.  These two row counts give the local scale of the stop-readthrough reality contact: one wide untreated cross-dataset table and one smaller induced nonzero readout table.

\paragraph{Coordinate being tested.}
The mathematical object proposed by the record is an $f_3$-stress synonymous residual coordinate in coding-sequence windows.  The intended carrier is a finite CDS-window table in which each row carries an $f_3$-stress residual coordinate after quotienting by amino-acid identity, local synonymous codon counts, a wobble-weight context, a matched translation readout, an mRNA control, and amino-acid or position-bin covariates.  The proposed survival statistic is not the raw stop-context correlation above.  It is a controlled per-window entry of the form
$$
\begin{aligned}
S_{f_3}(T_{\mathrm{position}}\mid Z) > 0,
\end{aligned}
$$
where $Z$ contains amino-acid composition or position-bin controls, GC3, CDS length, M-density, matched mRNA abundance, codon-count controls, coverage and mappability restrictions, rank sufficiency checks, and simpler-baseline ablations.  The finite Wangen rows therefore serve as reality contact for stop-readthrough measurement, while the stronger $f_3$-stress translation-survival assertion remains conditional on the construction of the same-window matrix.

\paragraph{What the current rows support.}
The cross-dataset Wangen entry supports a bounded stop-readthrough contact: there is a large finite row set, $n=55{,}886$, and the observed untreated association is positive but small, $\rho=0.032718$.  The G418 entry supports a related termination-readthrough surface in which $4{,}982$ nonzero RRTS rows survive a nonzero filter from $7{,}658$ joined rows.  Together, these numbers justify treating stop-readthrough as an experimentally addressable translation-termination readout.  They do not by themselves certify that an $f_3$-stress residual coordinate survives as a position-resolved ribosome-occupancy or translation-efficiency predictor.  The missing step is the rowwise join that places, in the same finite record, the residual coordinate, wobble-weight asymmetry $w_c \neq w_c^{f_3}$, local codon counts, matched mRNA abundance, and a position-resolved translation readout.

\paragraph{Local null boundary.}
The present chapter therefore records a bounded cross-layer boundary rather than a completed promotion.  The stop-readthrough tables show that the readthrough phenotype can be organized as finite rows, and they provide concrete scales and scalar readbacks for Wangen 2020.  However, neither the $55{,}886$-row untreated table nor the $4{,}982$-row G418 nonzero subset is yet a same-CDS, same-window $f_3$-stress survival matrix.  Modeled tAI or stAI weights, wobble asymmetry, codon-window geometry, and notation for $S_{f_3}(T_{\mathrm{position}}\mid Z)$ are admissible only as ingredients for a future controlled statistic.  They are not observations of ribosome occupancy unless the joined footprint or translation-efficiency rows are present.

\paragraph{Cannot-claim boundary.}
No translation realization follows from the residual coordinate alone.  No protein abundance, folding state, physical admissibility, molecular function, phenotype, mechanism, adaptation claim, or universal biological rule follows from the stop-readthrough row counts, the untreated $\rho$, the G418 nonzero fraction, or the proposed $f_3$-stress coordinate.  A downstream claim would require its own layer-matched contact: measured translation rows for translation survival, proteomics rows for protein abundance, structure or stability contacts for physical claims, functional assays for function, and phenotype or perturbation rows for system-level interpretation.  Until those contacts are separately joined and controlled, the finite Wangen data should be reported only as a stop-readthrough reality surface and as a candidate boundary for testing whether $f_3$-stress survives after the declared controls.
