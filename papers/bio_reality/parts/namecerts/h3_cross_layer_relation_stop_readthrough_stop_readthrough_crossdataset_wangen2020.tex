\subsection{Stop-context readthrough as a bounded cross-dataset relation}\label{sec:bioreality-stop-readthrough-crossdataset-wangen2020}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite row surface.}
The stop-readthrough record is treated here as a finite table claim, not as a general statement about translation termination.  The audited cross-dataset Wangen2020 run has run identifier \texttt{7c85e126c888}, contains $55{,}886$ joined rows, and reports an untreated rank correlation
$\rho = 0.032718$ after a runtime of $133.552797$ seconds.  A related G418-conditioned Wangen2020 termination run, \texttt{fe18fff6841f}, uses the endpoint ``G418-induced RRTS nonzero subset, rank-average scaled to $[0,1]$'', begins from $7{,}658$ joined rows before the nonzero filter, retains $4{,}982$ rows after filtering, and has nonzero RRTS fraction $0.65056$ with runtime $303.534$ seconds.  These numbers make the record finite and inspectable: the chapter asserts only that the named stop-context relation was evaluated on the stated rows and produced the stated scalar readbacks.

\paragraph{Coordinate and readout.}
Each row is a stop-context entry whose response is a readthrough-related measurement or rank-transformed readout.  The cross-dataset statistic is summarized by a small untreated association rather than by a mechanistic law.  In local notation, let $x_r$ denote the stop-context coordinate for row $r$, let $y_r$ denote the matched untreated readout used by the cross-dataset run, and let $R$ be the audited finite row set.  The reported scalar is recorded as
$$
\begin{aligned}
|R| &= 55{,}886,\\
\rho_{\mathrm{untreated}}(x,y) &= 0.032718.
\end{aligned}
$$
The G418-conditioned companion panel is kept separate:
$$
\begin{aligned}
|R_{\mathrm{joined}}| &= 7{,}658,\\
|R_{\mathrm{nonzero}}| &= 4{,}982,\\
\Pr(y_{\mathrm{RRTS}} > 0) &= 0.65056.
\end{aligned}
$$
The two panels therefore support a narrow statement: stop-context sequence information can be organized into finite rows whose readthrough-associated response admits reproducible scalar summaries under the declared condition and filtering rule.

\paragraph{Relation to the f\_3-stress packet.}
The present claim is not promoted to a position-resolved $f_3$-stress translation-survival claim.  The supplied conjectural carrier for the $f_3$ packet requires a finite CDS-window table containing an $f_3$-stress residual coordinate, local synonymous codon counts, wobble-weight context, matched ribosome-occupancy or translation-efficiency readout, mRNA control, and amino-acid or position-bin controls.  Those joined rows are not part of the audited stop-readthrough scalar above.  The stop-context table may be a useful cross-layer warning surface because it shows that termination-adjacent sequence features can be tested against readthrough readouts, but it does not by itself supply same-CDS, same-window ribosome-footprint occupancy, wobble-weight asymmetry, or the controlled statistic $S_{f3}(T_{\mathrm{position}}\mid Z)$.

\paragraph{Local survival criterion.}
A future $f_3$-stress readout would have to be evaluated on the same finite row where the coordinate and the translation readout are both present.  With $q_r$ the $f_3$-stress residual coordinate, $T_r$ a position-resolved translation readout, and $Z_r$ the declared controls, the relevant local test has the form
$$
\begin{aligned}
S_{f3}(T_{\mathrm{position}}\mid Z)
&= \Delta_{\mathrm{held\text{-}out}}(q;T\mid Z),\\
Z
&= \{\mathrm{AA\ composition},\ \mathrm{position\ bin},\ \mathrm{GC3},\ \mathrm{length},\ \mathrm{M\ density},\ \mathrm{mRNA},\ \mathrm{simpler\ baselines}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The break condition is strict: the entry must remain positive under held-out evaluation and after the listed controls and ablations.  The current Wangen2020 stop-readthrough rows do not certify that condition for $f_3$-stress.  They certify only the stated stop-context cross-dataset association and the companion G418-filtered row counts.

\paragraph{Claim boundary.}
No translation mechanism, protein abundance, folding, physical admissibility, biological function, phenotype, or universal rule follows from this chapter.  The untreated value $\rho=0.032718$ is a bounded finite-row association, not a termination law and not evidence that BEDC residual geometry has been observed in biology.  The G418 panel likewise records a condition-specific readthrough endpoint and a nonzero subset; it is not a substitute for a same-row ribosome-occupancy matrix, an mRNA-controlled translation-efficiency survival statistic, or downstream protein-layer evidence.  Any such stronger claim must be supplied by a separate layer-matched dataset and by a controlled finite-row statistic computed on that dataset.
