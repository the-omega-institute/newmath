\subsection{Stop-context readthrough as a bounded cross-dataset translation-termination readout}
\label{sec:bioreality-stop-readthrough-crossdataset-wangen2020}

\paragraph{Finite row surface.}
The Wangen readthrough surface gives a finite translation-termination panel rather than a general rule about coding-sequence geometry.  In the cross-dataset run recorded for this claim, the joined table contains $n = 55886$ rows and the untreated rank association is $\rho = 0.032718$, with run time $133.552797$ seconds under run id $7c85e126c888$.  A separate Wangen G418 termination-contact run uses the sheet named ``G418 2.0mg|mL 24hr merged'', joins $7658$ rows before the nonzero filter, retains $n = 4982$ rows after filtering to the nonzero RRTS subset, and records a nonzero fraction $0.65056$ with run time $303.534$ seconds under run id $fe18fff6841f$.  These two finite contacts are useful because they keep the readthrough endpoint anchored to measured termination-context rows, while also showing that the untreated cross-dataset association is small and must be treated as a bounded readout rather than as a broad biological law.

\paragraph{Coordinate being tested.}
The proposed coordinate is the $f_3$-stress residual direction after quotienting amino-acid composition.  Its admissible carrier is a finite CDS-window table whose rows contain the residual coordinate, local synonymous codon counts, a wobble-weight context, a matched position-resolved ribosome-occupancy or translation-efficiency readout, matched mRNA abundance, and amino-acid or position-bin controls.  The stop-readthrough Wangen rows provide a termination-layer contact, but they do not by themselves supply the full same-window $f_3$-stress table.  The local statistic required for the stronger claim has the form
$$
\begin{aligned}
S_{f3}(T_{\mathrm{position}} \mid Z) &> 0,\\
Z &= \{\mathrm{AA\ composition},\ \mathrm{position\ bin},\ \mathrm{GC3},\ \mathrm{length},\ \mathrm{M\ density},\ \mathrm{mRNA},\ \mathrm{simpler\ baselines}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The observed Wangen quantities therefore certify a finite termination readout surface, not the full survival statistic for $f_3$-stress in position-resolved CDS windows.

\paragraph{What the finite numbers support.}
The value $\rho = 0.032718$ on $55886$ cross-dataset rows is compatible with a weak but measurable untreated readthrough association at the row scale used by the Wangen-derived surface.  The G418 contact supplies an independent finite termination context with $4982$ nonzero RRTS rows after a documented nonzero filter, so it can be used as a separate stress-conditioned termination readout when the analysis is explicitly restricted to that endpoint.  These data are enough for a BioReality chapter to record that stop-context readthrough is externally measured in finite rows and that the claim can be evaluated as a bounded cross-layer relation at the translation-termination boundary.

\paragraph{What remains unproved for the $f_3$-stress conjecture.}
The numbers above do not yet establish a same-CDS, same-window $f_3$-stress survival entry.  To promote the conjecture, each analysis row would need ordered CDS position, local codon counts, an observed or explicitly bounded wobble-weight surface satisfying $w_c \neq w_c^{f_3}$ where the contrast is used, matched mRNA abundance, and a position-resolved ribosome-footprint or translation-efficiency readback.  The promoted statistic must then survive amino-acid, position, GC3, length, M-density, mRNA, rank, coverage, mappability, and simpler-baseline controls.  Without that joined matrix, $S_{f3}(T_{\mathrm{position}} \mid Z)$ is a declared target statistic rather than a computed positive survival result.

\paragraph{Local no-promotion boundary.}
The stop-readthrough rows are termination-readout evidence only.  They do not imply translation realization from codon geometry alone, do not convert modeled tAI or stAI weights into ribosome occupancy, and do not license claims about protein abundance, folding, structural order, physical admissibility, molecular function, mechanism, phenotype, or universality.  Any such higher-layer statement would require its own matched contact and its own finite statistic.  The present chapter therefore records a measured stop-context readthrough surface and a bounded route toward testing $f_3$-stress, while keeping the stronger position-resolved translation-survival claim conditional on the missing same-window join.
