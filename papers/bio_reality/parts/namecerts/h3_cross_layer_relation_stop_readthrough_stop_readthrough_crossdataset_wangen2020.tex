\subsection{Stop-Context Readthrough As A Bounded Translation-Readout Contact}\label{sec:bioreality-stop-readthrough-crossdataset-wangen2020}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite table.}
The BioReality record for \emph{stop readthrough crossdataset Wangen2020} is a finite-row contact with an explicit translation-layer endpoint, not a general statement about protein output or organismal state.  The crossdataset run keyed by \texttt{h3.cross\_layer\_relation.stop\_readthrough.stop\_readthrough\_crossdataset\_wangen2020} reports $n=55886$ joined rows, runtime $133.552797$ seconds, and an untreated rank correlation $\rho=0.032718$.  A second Wangen stop-context readthrough contact, keyed by \texttt{h3.cross\_layer\_relation.translation\_termination.stop\_context\_readthrough\_g418\_wangen2020}, uses the endpoint ``G418-induced RRTS nonzero subset, rank-average scaled to $[0,1]$'' on the sheet \texttt{G418 2.0mg|mL 24hr merged}.  Its inner-join count before the nonzero filter is $7658$, the analyzed nonzero subset has $n=4982$, the nonzero readthrough fraction is $0.65056$, and the runtime is $303.534$ seconds.  These numbers are the only quantitative commitments made here.

\paragraph{Coordinate under test.}
The proposed coordinate is the $f_3$-stress synonymous residual coordinate on coding-sequence windows after amino-acid quotienting.  The intended finite row has, at minimum, a local $f_3$ residual value, synonymous codon counts, a bounded wobble-weight context, a matched position-resolved ribosome-occupancy or translation-efficiency readout, an mRNA control, and amino-acid or position-bin controls.  The Wangen stop-context runs show that stop-codon-context readthrough can be represented by finite rank rows, but they do not by themselves supply the complete $f_3$ position-window matrix required for a positive $f_3$ survival claim.

\paragraph{Statistic.}
The admissible statistic is a controlled survival entry for the same coding window, written locally as
$$
\begin{aligned}
S_{f_3}(T_{\mathrm{position}}\mid Z)
  &= \Delta\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{held\ out}}\bigl(T_{\mathrm{position}};
      f_3\text{-stress}, Z\bigr),\\
Z
  &= \{\mathrm{AA\ composition},\ \mathrm{position\ bin},\ \mathrm{GC3},\ \mathrm{length},
      \mathrm{M\ density},\ \mathrm{mRNA},\ \mathrm{codon\ controls}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
A positive chapter-level readback requires held-out improvement after the controls in $Z$ and after simpler-baseline ablation.  The recorded crossdataset value $\rho=0.032718$ is therefore only a weak finite-row association for the Wangen stop-readthrough surface.  It is not promoted here to a powered $f_3$-stress survival entry, because the supplied finite facts do not include same-window $f_3$ residuals, wobble-weight asymmetry rows, matched mRNA residuals, and position-bin or amino-acid-bin controls in one joined design matrix.

\paragraph{Wobble asymmetry gate.}
The biological hypothesis is directional only when the local wobble weights break the formal $f_3$ symmetry.  In local notation the gate is
$$
\begin{aligned}
w_c &\neq w_c^{f_3},\\
S_{f_3}(T_{\mathrm{position}}\mid Z) &> 0
\end{aligned}
$$
under held-out evaluation.  The finite Wangen rows establish that stop-context readthrough can be used as a measured translation-termination readout, and the G418 subset establishes a nonzero induced readthrough panel with $4982$ analyzed rows.  They do not establish the wobble-asymmetry condition for $f_3$-stress windows.  The correct use of these rows is therefore as a bounded readout contact and a target surface for a later same-row residual test.

\paragraph{Interpretation boundary.}
This chapter makes no claim that $f_3$-stress causes translation, predicts protein abundance, controls folding, supplies physical admissibility, realizes biological function, gives a molecular mechanism, or states a universal biological law.  The finite stop-readthrough rows are translation-readout evidence only at the level of the recorded assays.  Any stronger claim would require a separately joined contact at the relevant layer: ribosome-position rows for position-resolved occupancy, matched mRNA rows for translation-efficiency residualization, proteomics rows for protein abundance, structural assays or predictions for folding and order, perturbation or functional assays for biological function, and phenotype rows for system-level effects.

\paragraph{Chapter verdict.}
The record is usable as a chapter-specific finite BioReality contact because it contains concrete row counts and scalar readouts from Wangen stop-context experiments: $55886$ crossdataset rows with untreated $\rho=0.032718$, and a G418 nonzero readthrough subset of $4982$ rows from $7658$ joined candidates with nonzero fraction $0.65056$.  The chapter remains deliberately narrow: it records a measured readthrough surface and states the exact additional same-row data required before an $f_3$-stress position-resolved translation-survival claim can be made.
