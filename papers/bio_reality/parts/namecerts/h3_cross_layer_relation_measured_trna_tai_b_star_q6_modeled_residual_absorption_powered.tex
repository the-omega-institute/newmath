\subsection{Measured tRNA-copy tAI as a bounded absorption readout for the $B^*_{Q6}$ modeled complement}
\label{subsec:bioreality-q6-measured-trna-tai-modeled-residual-absorption}

\paragraph{Finite surface.}
This record tests a narrow cross-layer question: after the $B^*_{Q6}$ residual basis has been formed against amino-acid composition and sequence-composition controls, does a measured tRNA gene-copy tAI surface absorb any of the modeled-tAI-complement abundance component?  The tested coordinate set has nine rows,
$K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $Leu_{CUN\,vs\,UUR}$, $Leu_{UUA\,vs\,UUG}$, $Ser_{UCR\,vs\,AGY}$, $Ser_{UCA\,vs\,UCG}$, $Thr_{ACR\,vs\,ACY}$, and $f_3\text{-stress}$.  The control design uses an intercept, $\log(\mathrm{CDS\ length})$, the twenty amino-acid composition proportions $A,C,D,E,F,G,H,I,K,L,M,N,P,Q,R,S,T,V,W,Y$, GC3 from the same CDS codon counts, and an $M$-density baseline defined on the union support of the nine projected $B^*_{Q6}$ vectors.  Each organism has $24$ control columns and rank $23$, so the reported quantities are not produced by an under-rank control block.

\paragraph{Rows and tRNA-copy contact.}
The full-join tRNA-copy calculation is available for three organisms.  For \textit{Danio rerio}, the joined CDS count is $14{,}410$, the parsed tRNA-gene count is $8{,}846$, the anticodon gene-copy table has $57$ entries, and the measured-tAI calculation has $8{,}607$ usable records.  For \textit{Homo sapiens}, the joined CDS count is $19{,}053$, the parsed tRNA-gene count is $431$, the anticodon table has $47$ entries, and the measured-tAI calculation has $422$ usable records.  For \textit{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}, the joined CDS count is $5{,}815$, the parsed tRNA-gene count is $273$, the anticodon table has $41$ entries, and the measured-tAI calculation has $268$ usable records.  No zero raw $W$ values were filled by a geometric mean in any of the three organisms.  The parser excluded $3$ selenocysteine, $25$ suppressor, and $5$ undetermined-anticodon headers in \textit{Danio rerio}; $1$ selenocysteine header in \textit{Homo sapiens}; and $2$ undetermined-anticodon headers in \textit{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}.

\paragraph{Modeled-complement stability.}
The tRNA-copy join preserves the modeled-complement target exactly relative to the abundance-CDS complement reference.  In all three organisms the absolute discrepancy between the reference modeled-complement value and the current full-join value is $0.0$.  The finite values are
$$
\begin{aligned}
 d_{\mathrm{modeled}}(\textit{Danio rerio}) &= 0.874628721049266,\\
 d_{\mathrm{modeled}}(\textit{Homo sapiens}) &= 0.9208949355679455,\\
 d_{\mathrm{modeled}}(\textit{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}) &= 0.21173219572706703.
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the tRNA-copy surface is not changing the residual target by reducing the joined abundance-CDS panel.  It measures absorption against the same modeled-complement residual used by the full abundance-CDS reference in these three organisms.

\paragraph{Absorption values.}
The measured tRNA gene-copy tAI surface absorbs only a small fraction of the modeled-tAI-complement abundance component.  The computed absorption and combined residual values are
$$
\begin{aligned}
 A_{\mathrm{tRNA}}(\textit{Danio rerio}) &= 0.020817102845637092, & d_{\mathrm{combined}} &= 0.835657382343872,\\
 A_{\mathrm{tRNA}}(\textit{Homo sapiens}) &= 0.00005426229300865148, & d_{\mathrm{combined}} &= 0.92041342656924,\\
 A_{\mathrm{tRNA}}(\textit{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}) &= 0.001530747712165342, & d_{\mathrm{combined}} &= 0.20648621019305421.
\end{aligned}
$$
The largest observed absorption is in \textit{Danio rerio}, about $2.08\%$ of the modeled-complement component.  The human and yeast values are much smaller: approximately $5.43\times 10^{-5}$ and $1.53\times 10^{-3}$, respectively.  This supports a bounded descriptive conclusion: measured tRNA gene-copy tAI does not substantially explain the modeled-tAI-complement residual in this finite panel, although it is computable and nonzero in all three organisms.

\paragraph{Comparison with measured translation-efficiency absorption.}
A separate measured-translation-efficiency calculation gives a useful scale for this record.  On the measured-TE all-readout joins, the absorption values are $0.01759429001506974$ for \textit{Danio rerio}, $0.0010982956489726373$ for \textit{Escherichia coli} K-12 MG1655, $0.0028947149257963897$ for \textit{Homo sapiens}, and $0.0002762783754375593$ for \textit{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}.  The corresponding measured-TE joins have $13{,}140$, $3{,}415$, $11{,}554$, and $5{,}128$ rows, each with control rank $23$.  The tRNA-copy calculation therefore sits in the same descriptive scale as the measured-TE calculation: both are finite projections of the $B^*_{Q6}$ residual surface, and neither by itself licenses a causal or mechanistic interpretation.  The measured-TE calculation also shows that readout availability can alter the joined panel: the modeled-complement discrepancy is $0.03219747984141397$ in \textit{Danio rerio} and $0.06427590857052146$ in \textit{Homo sapiens}, whereas the tRNA-copy calculation keeps the full abundance-CDS join exactly in the three reported organisms.

\paragraph{Cross-organism context.}
The broader organism-specific summary remains exploratory.  In the twelve-organism meta table with complete features, the dominant-coordinate contingency has Pearson chi-square $9.399999999999999$, Cramer's $V=0.6258327785172862$, and permutation right-tail $p=0.5814185814185814$ over $1000$ trials.  The $M^{QTP}$ fraction has weak and non-resolving correlations with the continuous organism-level proxies: with GC3, $r=-0.3062616500469447$ and two-sided permutation $p=0.35264735264735264$; with log proteome size, $r=-0.033628115789504955$ and $p=0.913086913086913$; with log tRNA-gene count, $r=0.06029405206037823$ and $p=0.8621378621378621$.  The domain comparison has variance ratio $0.8889980452277374$ and right-tail permutation $p=0.4155844155844156$.  These values do not resolve a cross-organism law for the residual basis; they only locate the tRNA-copy absorption result inside a heterogeneous finite panel.

\paragraph{Local boundary.}
The conclusion is intentionally limited.  tRNA gene copy number is a proxy for tRNA abundance, not a direct tRNA-seq measurement.  The tAI calculation uses wobble parameters from a modeling convention, and the absorbed fraction is a descriptive projection rather than a mediation test.  A positive or nonzero tRNA-copy absorption entry does not establish ribosome occupancy, translation realization, protein abundance, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, organismal phenotype, mechanism, adaptation, causation, or universality.  Those stronger statements require separate layer-matched contacts and their own controlled finite-row statistics.  Within this chapter, the supported statement is only that the measured tRNA gene-copy tAI surface can be joined at full abundance-CDS size for three organisms and absorbs a small, explicitly reported fraction of the modeled-tAI-complement $B^*_{Q6}$ abundance residual.
