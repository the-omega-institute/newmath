\subsection{Measured tRNA-copy tAI Absorption of the Modeled Q6 Residual}\label{sec:bioreality-q6-measured-trna-tai-modeled-residual-absorption}\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite row surface.} This record tests whether an organism-specific tRNA gene-copy implementation of tAI absorbs the part of the $B^*_{Q6}$ abundance residual that remains after the modeled-tAI projection has already been removed. The finite surface is not a protein-function, folding, or phenotype surface. Its rows are coding-sequence readouts joined to organism labels, codon-count-derived residual coordinates, a control matrix $Z$, and a measured tRNA-copy tAI readback. The nine tested coordinates are $K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $Leu_{CUN\,vs\,UUR}$, $Leu_{UUA\,vs\,UUG}$, $Ser_{UCR\,vs\,AGY}$, $Ser_{UCA\,vs\,UCG}$, $Thr_{ACR\,vs\,ACY}$, and $f_3$-stress. The control basis has $24$ columns with rank $23$ in each organism; it consists of an intercept, $\log(\mathrm{cds\_len\_nt})$, the $20$ amino-acid composition proportions $A,C,D,E,F,G,H,I,K,L,M,N,P,Q,R,S,T,V,W,Y$, GC3 from the same CDS codon counts, and an $M$-density baseline measuring the fraction of sense codons lying on the union support of the nine projected $B^*_{Q6}$ vectors.

\paragraph{Join sizes and tRNA readback.} The test is rank-powered on three full-join organism panels. For \emph{Danio rerio}, the CDS join has $n=14410$ rows, $8846$ parsed tRNA genes, an anticodon gene-copy table of size $57$, and $8607$ usable measured-tAI records; selenocysteine, suppressor, and undetermined-anticodon headers are excluded with counts $3$, $25$, and $5$. For \emph{Homo sapiens}, the join has $n=19053$ rows, $431$ parsed tRNA genes, an anticodon table of size $47$, and $422$ usable measured-tAI records; one selenocysteine header is excluded. For \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}, the join has $n=5815$ rows, $273$ parsed tRNA genes, an anticodon table of size $41$, and $268$ usable measured-tAI records; two undetermined-anticodon headers are excluded. In all three organisms the raw tAI weight table required no geometric-mean filling: the zero-raw-$W$ replacement count is $0$.

\paragraph{Projection statistic.} Let $P_{Q\perp T_{mod}}$ denote the abundance component carried by the $B^*_{Q6}$ residual basis after removing the modeled-tAI component under the control matrix $Z$. The descriptive absorption statistic compares the modeled-complement distance before and after adding the measured tRNA-copy tAI readback. In this finite surface the computed values are
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{Danio\ rerio}:&\quad d_{modeled}=0.874628721049266,\quad a_{tRNA}=0.020817102845637092,\quad d_{combined}=0.835657382343872,\\
\mathrm{Homo\ sapiens}:&\quad d_{modeled}=0.9208949355679455,\quad a_{tRNA}=0.00005426229300865148,\quad d_{combined}=0.92041342656924,\\
\mathrm{Saccharomyces\ cerevisiae}:&\quad d_{modeled}=0.21173219572706703,\quad a_{tRNA}=0.001530747712165342,\quad d_{combined}=0.20648621019305421.
\end{aligned}
$$
Here $a_{tRNA}$ is the fraction of the modeled-tAI-complement residual absorbed by the measured tRNA-copy tAI scalar. The largest measured value is in \emph{Danio rerio}, about $2.08\%$; yeast is about $0.153\%$; human is essentially zero at about $5.43\times 10^{-5}$. Thus the measured tRNA-copy readback is a small absorber of the non-modeled component in this panel, not a dominating explanation of the residual.

\paragraph{Full-join stability check.} The modeled-complement target is checked against the earlier abundance-complement surface on the same full CDS joins. The equality is exact at the recorded precision in all three organisms: \emph{Danio rerio} has reference join $14410$, current join $14410$, and absolute difference $0.0$; \emph{Homo sapiens} has reference join $19053$, current join $19053$, and absolute difference $0.0$; \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae} has reference join $5815$, current join $5815$, and absolute difference $0.0$. This matters because the protein-feature and measured-TE companion checks show that changing the available readout rows can alter the residual target. In the measured-TE companion fact, for example, the all-readout joins are $13140$ for \emph{Danio rerio}, $3415$ for \emph{Escherichia coli} K-12 MG1655, $11554$ for \emph{Homo sapiens}, and $5128$ for \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}; the modeled-complement deviations from the full abundance join are $0.03219747984141397$, $0.00017488612263594217$, $0.06427590857052146$, and $0.012133464712697745$, respectively. The tRNA-copy test avoids that particular instability because it is computed on the full abundance-CDS join for each of its three organisms.

\paragraph{Organism specificity.} The tRNA-copy absorption does not resolve a universal translation rule. The broader organism-specific table reports only exploratory cross-organism summaries: the complete-feature meta panel has $12$ rows, while the assembled organism table has $16$ rows with four organisms still needing data for the $M^{QTP}$ summary. Its domain test for $M^{QTP}$ fraction has variance ratio $0.8889980452277374$ and permutation right-tail $p=0.4155844155844156$; the dominant-coordinate-by-domain contingency has $\chi^2=9.399999999999999$, Cramer's $V=0.6258327785172862$, and permutation right-tail $p=0.5814185814185814$. Continuous exploratory checks also remain non-decisive: $M^{QTP}$ fraction versus GC3 gives $r=-0.3062616500469447$ with two-sided permutation $p=0.35264735264735264$, versus log tRNA gene count gives $r=0.06029405206037823$ with $p=0.8621378621378621$, and versus log proteome size gives $r=-0.033628115789504955$ with $p=0.913086913086913$. These numbers support a bounded, organism-indexed descriptive readback rather than a domain law.

\paragraph{Local boundary.} The chapter licenses only the following finite statement: on the three full-join organism panels, measured tRNA gene-copy tAI absorbs a small, explicitly recorded fraction of the modeled-tAI-complement $B^*_{Q6}$ abundance residual after the declared $Z$ controls. It does not show that a $Q_i$ coordinate causes translation efficiency, ribosome occupancy, protein abundance, folding, physical admissibility, function, mechanism, phenotype, or universality. tRNA gene copy number is a proxy for tRNA abundance, not direct tRNA-seq; the tAI wobble parameters come from a modeling convention; and the absorption statistic is a projection summary, not a causal mediation test. Any stronger claim at the translation, protein, structure, physical, functional, mechanistic, phenotype, or universal layer requires a separate layer-matched contact and its own controlled statistic.
