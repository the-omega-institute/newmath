\subsection{Measured tRNA-copy tAI absorption of the Q6 modeled-complement residual}
\label{sec:bioreality-q6-measured-trna-tai-modeled-residual-absorption}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Question.}
This finite record asks whether an organism-matched tRNA gene-copy tAI readout absorbs the part of the $B^*_{Q6}$ abundance residual that remained after the modeled tAI contact.  The object under test is not a protein, phenotype, folding state, or causal mechanism.  It is a finite CDS-by-organism readout surface whose rows carry nine residual coordinates,
$K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $Leu_{CUN\_vs\_UUR}$, $Leu_{UUA\_vs\_UUG}$, $Ser_{UCR\_vs\_AGY}$, $Ser_{UCA\_vs\_UCG}$, $Thr_{ACR\_vs\_ACY}$, and $f3\_stress$, together with organism-specific controls and a measured tRNA-copy tAI score.  The target quantity is descriptive absorption: after the $Q6$ residual component $\widehat P_{Q\perp T_{mod}}$ is formed, the measured tRNA-copy tAI coordinate is tested for how much of that remaining component it removes.

\paragraph{Rows and controls.}
The control design uses an intercept, $\log(\mathrm{cds\_len\_nt})$, the twenty amino-acid composition proportions $A,C,D,E,F,G,H,I,K,L,M,N,P,Q,R,S,T,V,W,Y$, $GC3$ from the real CDS codon counts, and an $M$-density baseline equal to the fraction of sense codons on the union support of the nine projected $B^*_{Q6}$ vectors.  Thus the recorded control-column count is $24$ in every organism and the rank of the control matrix is $23$ in every organism.  The joined surfaces are organism-specific: $Danio\ rerio$ has $14410$ joined CDS rows, $8846$ parsed tRNA genes, and $57$ anticodon gene-copy entries; $Homo\ sapiens$ has $19053$ joined CDS rows, $431$ parsed tRNA genes, and $47$ anticodon entries; $Saccharomyces\ cerevisiae$ has $5815$ joined CDS rows, $273$ parsed tRNA genes, and $41$ anticodon entries.  The usable measured-tAI records are $8607$, $422$, and $268$ respectively, with zero raw-$W$ geometric-mean fills in all three organisms.

\paragraph{Definition of the reported absorption.}
For each organism, let $P_Q$ denote the controlled $Q6$ residual component and let $P_{Q\perp T_{mod}}$ denote its component not explained by the modeled-tAI readout.  The modeled-complement ratio is the normalized squared length
$$
\begin{aligned}
d_{modeled} &= \frac{\lVert P_{Q\perp T_{mod}}\rVert^2}{\lVert P_Q\rVert^2}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The measured tRNA-copy tAI contact is then projected against this remaining component.  The reported number $d_{combined}$ is the normalized residual left after adding the measured tRNA-copy tAI readout, and $absorbed\_by\_trna$ is the corresponding descriptive decrease.  This is a projection statistic on the joined finite rows; it is not a perturbation result and it is not a causal mediation estimate.

\paragraph{Consistency of the modeled-complement join.}
The full-join self-check exactly matches the earlier modeled-complement experiment in all three organisms for which measured tRNA-copy data were attached.  In $Danio\ rerio$, the current full-join value and the complement reference are both $0.874628721049266$ on $14410$ rows.  In $Homo\ sapiens$, both are $0.9208949355679455$ on $19053$ rows.  In $Saccharomyces\ cerevisiae$, both are $0.21173219572706703$ on $5815$ rows.  The absolute difference is therefore $0.0$ in each of the three organisms, and each comparison passes the $0.02$ tolerance check.  This matters because the tRNA-copy test is being applied to the same modeled-complement surface rather than to a changed join.

\paragraph{Observed finite-row values.}
The measured tRNA-copy tAI contact absorbs only a small fraction of the modeled-complement residual.  In $Danio\ rerio$, the absorbed fraction is $0.020817102845637092$ and the combined residual ratio is $0.835657382343872$.  In $Homo\ sapiens$, the absorbed fraction is $5.426229300865148\times 10^{-5}$ and the combined residual ratio is $0.92041342656924$.  In $Saccharomyces\ cerevisiae$, the absorbed fraction is $0.001530747712165342$ and the combined residual ratio is $0.20648621019305421$.  Equivalently, the strongest recorded absorption is the zebrafish value, about $2.08\%$ of the modeled-complement residual, while the human and yeast values are much smaller on this surface.

\paragraph{Readback.}
The finite readback is therefore asymmetric.  The measured tRNA-copy tAI coordinate is not empty: the zebrafish rows show a measurable positive descriptive absorption, and yeast also shows a positive but small value.  However, the absorption is not large enough to erase the modeled-complement residual in any organism, and the human value is essentially negligible at the scale of the reported ratios.  The result is best read as a bounded contact between $B^*_{Q6}$ residual geometry and a tRNA gene-copy adaptation proxy: it says that the proxy explains a small part of the modeled-tAI-complement residual in the available joined rows, while most of that residual remains outside this tRNA-copy tAI readout.

\paragraph{Data provenance inside the finite packet.}
The tRNA records were fetched from genome-specific GtRNAdb FASTA payloads.  The $Danio\ rerio$ payload has $1747886$ bytes and SHA-256 digest \texttt{e81e605548df44e88ca0c3778b315202bd826c377ed9173e1a8575e21eb4090a}; the $Homo\ sapiens$ payload has $82720$ bytes and SHA-256 digest \texttt{50bd6fe75f60ce7461864dc1604a3c51481f1adfae7ad74198a1f389c211ac89}; the $Saccharomyces\ cerevisiae$ payload has $56478$ bytes and SHA-256 digest \texttt{273c3519053ef469cf4e43063ce634187a943a5d53ea4e87879b2224860d82c8}.  The parser excludes nonstandard header classes before forming the anticodon gene-copy table: zebrafish excludes $3$ selenocysteine, $25$ suppressor, and $5$ undetermined-anticodon headers; human excludes $1$ selenocysteine header; yeast excludes $2$ undetermined-anticodon headers.

\paragraph{Negative scope.}
No direct translation realization, ribosome occupancy law, protein abundance, folding conclusion, physical admissibility statement, molecular function, mechanism, phenotype, or universality follows from these absorption entries alone.  tRNA gene copy number is a proxy for tRNA abundance rather than direct tRNA-seq measurement, the tAI wobble values use the dos Reis modeling convention, and the absorbed fraction is a descriptive projection quantity.  Any claim about measured translation efficiency requires same-CDS ribosome-profiling and matched mRNA rows; any claim beyond translation requires its own layer-matched contact and its own statistic.

\paragraph{Verdict.}
The finite-row verdict is positive but narrow: the measured tRNA-copy tAI contact computes successfully and gives bounded absorption values on three rank-sufficient organism joins.  The largest absorption is $0.020817102845637092$ in $Danio\ rerio$, followed by $0.001530747712165342$ in $Saccharomyces\ cerevisiae$ and $5.426229300865148\times 10^{-5}$ in $Homo\ sapiens$.  Thus the $B^*_{Q6}$ modeled-complement residual is not compressed away by measured tRNA-copy tAI on this panel.  The residual remains a live unexplained component for later contacts such as matched measured translation efficiency, mRNA control, turnover, localization, or protein-level readouts.
