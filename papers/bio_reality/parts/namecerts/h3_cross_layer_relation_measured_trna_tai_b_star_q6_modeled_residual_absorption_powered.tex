\subsection{Measured tRNA-copy tAI absorption of the modeled translation complement}
\label{sec:bioreality-q6-measured-trna-tai-modeled-residual-absorption}

\paragraph{Finite readout surface.}
This record tests whether a measured tRNA-gene-copy tAI surface absorbs the modeled-tAI-complement component of the $B^*_{Q6}$ abundance residual.  The finite carrier is the organism-indexed CDS join on which nine residual coordinates are evaluated after the common covariate block has been removed.  The coordinate list is
$K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $Leu_{CUN\_vs\_UUR}$, $Leu_{UUA\_vs\_UUG}$, $Ser_{UCR\_vs\_AGY}$, $Ser_{UCA\_vs\_UCG}$, $Thr_{ACR\_vs\_ACY}$, and $f3\_stress$.  The control block contains the intercept, $\log(\mathrm{cds\_len\_nt})$, the twenty amino-acid composition proportions from real CDS codon counts, GC3, and the $M$-density baseline defined as the fraction of sense codons lying on the union support of the nine projected $B^*_{Q6}$ vectors.  In each of the three measured-tRNA organisms the design reports $24$ control columns and rank $23$, so the computation is not the rank-saturated three-row refusal case.

\paragraph{Joined rows and tRNA-copy readback.}
The full CDS joins are organism-specific and unequal in scale: zebrafish contributes $14410$ joined CDS rows, human contributes $19053$, and budding yeast contributes $5815$.  The tRNA-copy side is also finite: zebrafish has $8846$ parsed tRNA genes over $57$ anticodon GCN entries, human has $431$ parsed tRNA genes over $47$ anticodon entries, and budding yeast has $273$ parsed tRNA genes over $41$ anticodon entries.  The usable measured-tAI record counts after applying the gene-copy table are $8607$ for zebrafish, $422$ for human, and $268$ for budding yeast, with zero raw $W$ entries filled by a geometric mean in all three organisms.  The header exclusions are explicit rather than hidden: zebrafish excludes $3$ selenocysteine, $25$ suppressor, and $5$ undetermined-anticodon headers; human excludes $1$ selenocysteine header; budding yeast excludes $2$ undetermined-anticodon headers.

\paragraph{Modeled-complement stability under the tRNA join.}
The first check is that the tRNA-copy join does not move the modeled complement being tested.  For all three organisms the current full-join $d_{modeled}$ exactly matches the complement reference to the displayed precision:
$$
\begin{aligned}
 d_{modeled}^{Drerio} &= 0.874628721049266,\\
 d_{modeled}^{Hsapiens} &= 0.9208949355679455,\\
 d_{modeled}^{Scerevisiae} &= 0.21173219572706703.
\end{aligned}
$$
The reported absolute difference from the complement reference is $0.0$ for zebrafish, human, and budding yeast, with the same reference rank $23$ throughout.  This matters because the tRNA-copy absorption is being read against the same residual target as the modeled-complement experiment, not against a smaller availability-biased target.

\paragraph{Absorption result.}
Projecting the modeled-tAI-complement abundance component onto the measured tRNA-copy tAI surface gives a small but finite descriptive absorption in all three organisms:
$$
\begin{aligned}
 \mathrm{absorbed}_{tRNA}^{Drerio} &= 0.020817102845637092, & d_{combined}^{Drerio} &= 0.835657382343872,\\
 \mathrm{absorbed}_{tRNA}^{Hsapiens} &= 0.00005426229300865148, & d_{combined}^{Hsapiens} &= 0.92041342656924,\\
 \mathrm{absorbed}_{tRNA}^{Scerevisiae} &= 0.001530747712165342, & d_{combined}^{Scerevisiae} &= 0.20648621019305421.
\end{aligned}
$$
The largest measured-tRNA-copy absorption occurs in zebrafish, where the residual fraction drops from $0.874628721049266$ to $0.835657382343872$.  Human is essentially unchanged at this boundary, and budding yeast shows only a small decrement.  Thus the tRNA-copy tAI contact is a bounded absorber of the modeled translation complement, not a wholesale explanation of the $B^*_{Q6}$ residual.

\paragraph{Relation to measured translation efficiency.}
The measured-TE companion computation supplies a useful comparison because it uses organism-matched ribosome-profiling translation-efficiency values and matched abundance joins.  Its joined TE rows are $13140$ in zebrafish, $3415$ in \emph{E. coli} K-12 MG1655, $11554$ in human, and $5128$ in budding yeast, again with rank $23$ for the common control block.  The TE absorption values are $0.01759429001506974$ in zebrafish, $0.0010982956489726373$ in \emph{E. coli}, $0.0028947149257963897$ in human, and $0.0002762783754375593$ in budding yeast.  This comparison keeps measured tRNA-copy tAI and ribosome-profiling TE separate: both are descriptive projections against controlled residuals, but neither one is promoted into a causal translation mechanism by this chapter.

\paragraph{Cross-organism caution.}
The wider organism-specificity survey reinforces the same bounded reading.  Among $12$ complete-feature organisms, the dominant-coordinate versus domain contingency has permutation right-tail $p=0.5814185814185814$ despite Cramer's $V=0.6258327785172862$, so the displayed heterogeneity is not a resolved domain law.  The $M_{QTP}$ fraction correlations are also weak under the deterministic permutation checks: versus GC3, $r=-0.3062616500469447$ with two-sided permutation $p=0.35264735264735264$; versus log proteome size, $r=-0.033628115789504955$ with $p=0.913086913086913$; and versus log tRNA-gene count, $r=0.06029405206037823$ with $p=0.8621378621378621$.  These numbers license an organism-indexed finite report, not a universal biological rule.

\paragraph{Local interpretation boundary.}
The result is therefore a controlled absorption measurement: measured tRNA gene-copy tAI can be tested as a finite external readout for whether it reduces the modeled-tAI-complement abundance residual.  The calculation does not say that tRNA gene copy number is direct tRNA abundance, does not identify a causal mediator, and does not turn codon-coordinate residual geometry into observed ribosome dynamics.  No translation-realization claim beyond the explicitly computed bounded projection follows here.  No protein abundance, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, phenotype, mechanism, adaptation claim, or universal law follows unless a separate layer-matched contact supplies its own rows and statistic.
