\subsection{Measured tRNA-copy tAI absorption of the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ modeled-complement residual}
\label{sec:bioreality-measured-trna-tai-q6-residual-absorption}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite surface.}
The record tested here is a bounded cross-layer readout: the nine-coordinate $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ synonymous residual surface is compared with a measured tRNA-gene-copy tAI surface, not with protein amount, molecular structure, functional output, or phenotype.  The residual basis consists of
$K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $Leu_{CUN\,vs\,UUR}$, $Leu_{UUA\,vs\,UUG}$, $Ser_{UCR\,vs\,AGY}$, $Ser_{UCA\,vs\,UCG}$, $Thr_{ACR\,vs\,ACY}$, and $f_3$-stress.  The nuisance design $Z$ used for the residual projection has 24 listed columns with rank 23 in every organism: an intercept, $\log(\mathrm{cds\_len\_nt})$, the 20 amino-acid composition proportions computed from real CDS codon counts, GC3, and the $M$-density baseline defined as the fraction of sense codons on the union support of the nine projected $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ vectors.  Thus the finite object is a same-CDS joined matrix with one modeled-complement residual target and one measured tRNA-copy tAI readout per organism.

\paragraph{Joined rows and tRNA tables.}
The full-join check is exact for all three organisms in this packet.  For \emph{Danio rerio}, the joined panel contains $n=14410$ CDS rows, the tRNA table has 8846 parsed tRNA genes, 57 anticodon gene-copy entries, and 8607 usable measured-tAI records; the header parser excluded 3 selenocysteine records, 25 suppressor records, and 5 records with undetermined anticodon.  For \emph{Homo sapiens}, the joined panel contains $n=19053$ rows, with 431 tRNA genes, 47 anticodon entries, and 422 usable measured-tAI records; one selenocysteine record was excluded.  For \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}, the joined panel contains $n=5815$ rows, with 273 tRNA genes, 41 anticodon entries, and 268 usable measured-tAI records; two records with undetermined anticodon were excluded.  No raw $W$ value was filled by a geometric mean in any organism, so the measured-tAI vector used in the projection is not supported by imputed zero-repair at this step.

\paragraph{Modeled-complement preservation.}
Before asking whether measured tRNA-copy tAI absorbs the modeled-complement residual, the packet verifies that the residual target is the same one used by the complement experiment on the full abundance-CDS join.  The equality check is exact:
$$
\begin{aligned}
 d_{\mathrm{modeled}}(\mathrm{Danio}) &= 0.874628721049266,\\
 d_{\mathrm{modeled}}(\mathrm{human}) &= 0.9208949355679455,\\
 d_{\mathrm{modeled}}(\mathrm{yeast}) &= 0.21173219572706703.
\end{aligned}
$$
In each case the current full join has the same row count as the reference join and absolute difference $0.0$.  This matters because other candidate contacts in the same research family can change the protein-id join and thereby change the target residual.  For example, the measured-translation-efficiency candidate uses $n=13140$ rows in \emph{Danio rerio}, $n=11554$ rows in human, $n=5128$ rows in yeast, and $n=3415$ rows in \emph{E. coli}; its modeled-complement values on those all-readout joins are $0.8424312412078521$, $0.8566190269974241$, $0.22386566043976477$, and $0.6628211177352172$, respectively.  The tRNA-copy tAI packet is therefore interpreted on the stable full join for the three organisms where the measured tRNA-copy surface was attached.

\paragraph{Absorption readout.}
The absorption statistic is descriptive: it asks how much of the modeled-tAI-complement abundance component $\widehat P_{Q\perp T_{\mathrm{mod}}}$ is removed after projection against the residualized measured tRNA-copy tAI surface.  The computed values are
$$
\begin{aligned}
 \mathrm{absorbed}_{\mathrm{tRNA}}(\mathrm{Danio}) &= 0.020817102845637092,
 & d_{\mathrm{combined}}(\mathrm{Danio}) &= 0.835657382343872,\\
 \mathrm{absorbed}_{\mathrm{tRNA}}(\mathrm{human}) &= 0.00005426229300865148,
 & d_{\mathrm{combined}}(\mathrm{human}) &= 0.92041342656924,\\
 \mathrm{absorbed}_{\mathrm{tRNA}}(\mathrm{yeast}) &= 0.001530747712165342,
 & d_{\mathrm{combined}}(\mathrm{yeast}) &= 0.20648621019305421.
\end{aligned}
$$
The largest finite-row absorption is the \emph{Danio rerio} entry, about $2.08\%$ of the residual component under this projection.  Human is essentially unchanged at the displayed scale, and yeast shows a small positive absorption.  The finite conclusion is therefore not that tRNA copy number explains the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual.  It is that a measured tRNA-gene-copy tAI surface can be attached and tested on a rank-sufficient same-CDS join, and that the observed absorption remains small in all three organisms.

\paragraph{Cross-organism context.}
The wider organism-specific summary keeps this result from being promoted into a universal rule.  The meta table has 16 assembled organism rows, with 12 complete-feature rows used for the reported tests.  The dominant-coordinate versus domain contingency has $n=12$, Pearson $\chi^2=9.399999999999999$, Cramer's $V=0.6258327785172862$, and permutation right-tail $p=0.5814185814185814$ over 1000 trials.  The continuous tests for the $M^{QTP}$ fraction are likewise descriptive: versus GC3, $r=-0.3062616500469447$ with two-sided permutation $p=0.35264735264735264$; versus log tRNA-gene count, $r=0.06029405206037823$ with $p=0.8621378621378621$; versus log proteome size, $r=-0.033628115789504955$ with $p=0.913086913086913$.  These values support heterogeneity of the finite panels rather than a resolved domain law or a conserved mechanistic explanation.

\paragraph{Local verdict.}
The chapter is ready as a bounded BioReality record because the finite rows, ranks, tRNA-copy tables, modeled-complement equality checks, and absorption scalars are all explicit.  Its scientific content is deliberately narrow.  A tRNA-gene-copy tAI proxy is not direct tRNA abundance, and the tAI wobble parameters are a modeling choice inherited by the readout.  The computed absorption fractions are statistical projections only; they do not establish causation, a ribosome mechanism, translation realization, protein abundance, folded structure, physical admissibility, molecular function, phenotype, or universality.  Any one of those stronger statements would require a separate layer-matched contact and its own controlled finite-row statistic.
