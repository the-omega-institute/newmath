\subsection{Measured tRNA-copy tAI absorption of the $B^*_{Q6}$ modeled-complement residual}
\label{sec:bior-reality-measured-trna-tai-q6-absorption}

\paragraph{Finite surface.}
This record tests a bounded cross-layer contact: whether an organism-specific tRNA gene-copy implementation of tAI absorbs part of the $B^*_{Q6}$ abundance residual that remains after the modeled-tAI component has already been removed.  The carrier is a finite CDS-by-organism table with nine $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinates,
$K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $Leu_{CUN\,vs\,UUR}$, $Leu_{UUA\,vs\,UUG}$, $Ser_{UCR\,vs\,AGY}$, $Ser_{UCA\,vs\,UCG}$, $Thr_{ACR\,vs\,ACY}$, and $f_3$-stress.  The control block has $24$ columns and rank $23$ in each organism, consisting of an intercept, $\log(\mathrm{CDS\ length})$, twenty amino-acid composition proportions, GC3, and the M-density baseline defined on the union support of the nine projected $B^*_{Q6}$ vectors.  The joined row counts are $14{,}410$ for \emph{Danio rerio}, $19{,}053$ for \emph{Homo sapiens}, and $5{,}815$ for \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}; these are the same full-join row counts used by the modeled-complement reference, so the current projection does not change the target residual by changing the CDS universe.

\paragraph{tRNA-copy readout.}
The measured readout is not direct tRNA abundance.  It is a tRNA gene-copy tAI surface computed from organism-specific tRNA FASTA records and dos Reis-style wobble weights.  The parsed tRNA gene counts are $8{,}846$ for \emph{D. rerio}, $431$ for human, and $273$ for yeast, with anticodon GCN table sizes $57$, $47$, and $41$, respectively.  The usable measured-tAI record counts are $8{,}607$, $422$, and $268$, and no zero raw $W$ entries required geometric-mean filling in any of the three organisms.  Header exclusions were finite and explicit: \emph{D. rerio} excluded $3$ selenocysteine, $25$ suppressor, and $5$ undetermined-anticodon records; human excluded $1$ selenocysteine record; yeast excluded $2$ undetermined-anticodon records.

\paragraph{Projection statistic.}
Let $P_Q$ denote the controlled $B^*_{Q6}$ residual surface and let $P_{Q\perp T_{mod}}$ denote the abundance component orthogonal to the modeled-tAI readout under the same control design.  The test asks whether adding the measured tRNA-copy tAI coordinate reduces the remaining modeled-complement distance.  The full-join self-check is exact in all three organisms:
$$
\begin{aligned}
 d_{mod}(\mathrm{D.\ rerio}) &= 0.874628721049266,\\
 d_{mod}(\mathrm{human}) &= 0.9208949355679455,\\
 d_{mod}(\mathrm{yeast}) &= 0.21173219572706703,
\end{aligned}
$$
with absolute difference $0.0$ from the complement reference in each case.  The measured tRNA-copy tAI absorption is small but finite in all three rows:
$$
\begin{aligned}
 \mathrm{absorb}_{tRNA}(\mathrm{D.\ rerio}) &= 0.020817102845637092, & d_{combined} &= 0.835657382343872,\\
 \mathrm{absorb}_{tRNA}(\mathrm{human}) &= 0.00005426229300865148, & d_{combined} &= 0.92041342656924,\\
 \mathrm{absorb}_{tRNA}(\mathrm{yeast}) &= 0.001530747712165342, & d_{combined} &= 0.20648621019305421.
\end{aligned}
$$
The largest finite reduction is in \emph{D. rerio}, where the measured tRNA-copy readout absorbs about $2.08\%$ of the modeled-complement residual.  Human is effectively unchanged at this resolution, while yeast shows a small reduction on a smaller upstream residual.

\paragraph{Relation to measured translation efficiency.}
A separate measured-ribo-seq TE contact was computed on the all-readout joins, with row counts $13{,}140$ for \emph{D. rerio}, $3{,}415$ for \emph{E. coli} K-12 MG1655, $11{,}554$ for human, and $5{,}128$ for yeast.  Under the same nine-coordinate residual basis and a matched mRNA-aware TE definition, measured TE absorption of the modeled complement was $0.01759429001506974$ in \emph{D. rerio}, $0.0010982956489726373$ in \emph{E. coli}, $0.0028947149257963897$ in human, and $0.0002762783754375593$ in yeast.  The TE join changes the residual target in \emph{D. rerio} and human: the modeled-complement reference differs by $0.03219747984141397$ and $0.06427590857052146$, exceeding the $0.02$ self-check tolerance, while \emph{E. coli} and yeast remain within tolerance at $0.00017488612263594217$ and $0.012133464712697745$.  Thus the tRNA-copy result is best read as a full-CDS codon-adaptation absorption check, whereas the ribo-seq TE check is a measured-translation contact whose finite availability can alter the analyzed CDS population.

\paragraph{Cross-organism context.}
The broader organism-specific meta surface remains descriptive rather than law-like.  Across the complete-feature organism panel, $12$ organisms were available for the M-QTP fraction tests, while $16$ organisms were present in the assembled meta table and $4$ organisms still needed data for M-QTP.  The permutation tests did not produce a resolved organism-class rule: M-QTP fraction versus domain had variance ratio $0.8889980452277374$ and right-tail permutation $p=0.4155844155844156$; versus GC3 it had Pearson $r=-0.3062616500469447$ and two-sided permutation $p=0.35264735264735264$; versus log tRNA-gene count it had $r=0.06029405206037823$ and $p=0.8621378621378621$; versus log proteome size it had $r=-0.033628115789504955$ and $p=0.913086913086913$.  The dominant-coordinate contingency had $n=12$, Pearson chi-square $9.399999999999999$, Cramer's $V=0.6258327785172862$, and right-tail permutation $p=0.5814185814185814$.  These finite numbers support heterogeneity and bounded readout separation, not a domain-wide biological rule.

\paragraph{Boundary of the claim.}
The result licenses only a descriptive projection statement: measured tRNA gene-copy tAI can be inserted as a finite readout and its absorption of $P_{Q\perp T_{mod}}$ can be measured under the declared controls.  It does not establish causality, direct tRNA abundance, ribosome occupancy, translation realization, protein abundance, folded structure, physical admissibility, molecular function, phenotype, mechanism, adaptation, or universality.  Those claims require their own matched contacts and statistics.  The yeast turnover contact illustrates the same boundary: on $3{,}640$ joined turnover rows, with join hit rate $0.8818572656921754$ and CDS join hit rate $0.9156038419146592$, the turnover absorption was only $0.0009086031889966327$ even though the modeled-complement self-check stayed within tolerance at absolute difference $0.011538255214205373$.  A small descriptive absorption, whether by tRNA-copy tAI, measured TE, or turnover, is therefore a finite contact statistic rather than a biological mechanism claim.
