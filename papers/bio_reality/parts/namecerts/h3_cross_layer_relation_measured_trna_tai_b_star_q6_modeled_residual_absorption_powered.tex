\subsection{Measured tRNA-copy tAI absorption of the $B^*_{Q6}$ modeled-residual component}
\label{sec:bioreality-q6-measured-trna-tai-absorption}

\paragraph{Finite readout surface.}
This record tests whether an organism-matched tRNA gene-copy tAI surface absorbs the component of the $B^*_{Q6}$ abundance residual that remains after the modeled-tAI projection has been removed. The finite surface contains three organisms: \emph{Danio rerio}, \emph{Homo sapiens}, and \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}. The residual coordinate list has nine entries,
$K\_{AAA}$, $Arg\_{AGR}$, $Ile\_{AUA}$, $Leu\_{CUN\_vs\_UUR}$, $Leu\_{UUA\_vs\_UUG}$, $Ser\_{UCR\_vs\_AGY}$, $Ser\_{UCA\_vs\_UCG}$, $Thr\_{ACR\_vs\_ACY}$, and $f3\_stress$.
The control design has $24$ columns and rank $23$ in every organism. Its columns are the intercept, $\log(\mathrm{CDS\ length})$, the $20$ amino-acid composition proportions computed from real CDS codon counts, GC3, and the $M$-density baseline, where $M$-density is the fraction of sense codons lying on the union support of the nine projected $B^*_{Q6}$ coordinate vectors.

\paragraph{Matched rows and tRNA tables.}
The full CDS join is preserved for this measured tRNA-copy tAI contact. In \emph{Danio rerio}, the joined panel has $14{,}410$ CDS rows, $8{,}846$ parsed tRNA genes, an anticodon gene-copy table of size $57$, and $8{,}607$ usable measured-tAI records. In \emph{Homo sapiens}, the joined panel has $19{,}053$ CDS rows, $431$ parsed tRNA genes, an anticodon table of size $47$, and $422$ usable measured-tAI records. In \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}, the joined panel has $5{,}815$ CDS rows, $273$ parsed tRNA genes, an anticodon table of size $41$, and $268$ usable measured-tAI records. No raw codon weight was filled by a geometric mean in any of the three organisms: the zero-fill count is $0$ for each table.

\paragraph{Rank and complement self-check.}
The modeled-complement target is stable on the tRNA-copy join. The current join size equals the complement-reference join size in all three organisms, so the absolute difference between the current $d_{\mathrm{modeled}}$ and the complement-reference value is exactly $0.0$ in each case. The checked values are
$$
\begin{aligned}
 d_{\mathrm{modeled}}(\emph{D. rerio}) &= 0.874628721049266,\\
 d_{\mathrm{modeled}}(\emph{H. sapiens}) &= 0.9208949355679455,\\
 d_{\mathrm{modeled}}(\emph{S. cerevisiae}) &= 0.21173219572706703.
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the tRNA-copy calculation is not changing the residual target by losing CDS rows. It measures absorption against the same modeled-complement component already present on the abundance-CDS join.

\paragraph{Absorption readback.}
Let $P_{Q\perp T_{\mathrm{mod}}}$ denote the component of the $B^*_{Q6}$ abundance projection orthogonal to the modeled-tAI readout after the stated controls. The tRNA-copy tAI contact is evaluated by projecting this component against the measured gene-copy tAI surface. The observed absorbed fractions are
$$
\begin{aligned}
 A_{\mathrm{tRNA}}(\emph{D. rerio}) &= 0.020817102845637092,\\
 A_{\mathrm{tRNA}}(\emph{H. sapiens}) &= 0.00005426229300865148,\\
 A_{\mathrm{tRNA}}(\emph{S. cerevisiae}) &= 0.001530747712165342.
\end{aligned}
$$
The corresponding combined residual levels are
$$
\begin{aligned}
 d_{\mathrm{combined}}(\emph{D. rerio}) &= 0.835657382343872,\\
 d_{\mathrm{combined}}(\emph{H. sapiens}) &= 0.92041342656924,\\
 d_{\mathrm{combined}}(\emph{S. cerevisiae}) &= 0.20648621019305421.
\end{aligned}
$$
The largest absorption is therefore the \emph{D. rerio} value, about $2.08\%$ of the modeled-complement residual component. The human value is effectively negligible at this scale, and the yeast value is small but nonzero. These values support only a descriptive projection statement: measured tRNA gene-copy tAI removes a small part of the modeled-complement abundance residual in this finite panel, with organism-specific magnitude.

\paragraph{Relation to measured translation efficiency.}
A companion ribosome-profiling TE run on the same nine-coordinate residual basis gives a stricter readout boundary. The measured-TE joined panels contain $13{,}140$ rows for \emph{D. rerio}, $3{,}415$ rows for \emph{E. coli} K-12 MG1655, $11{,}554$ rows for \emph{H. sapiens}, and $5{,}128$ rows for \emph{S. cerevisiae}, again with control rank $23$. Its measured-TE absorptions are $0.01759429001506974$, $0.0010982956489726373$, $0.0028947149257963897$, and $0.0002762783754375593$, respectively. The tRNA-copy contact and the measured-TE contact are therefore not interchangeable: one is a gene-copy adaptation proxy, while the other is a matched ribosome-profiling translation-efficiency readout. The fact that the largest tRNA-copy absorption occurs in \emph{D. rerio} does not by itself identify a ribosome-level mechanism, because the TE contact changes row availability in several organisms and supplies a separate measured layer.

\paragraph{Cross-organism caution.}
The broader organism-specificity survey contains $16$ assembled rows, of which $12$ have the complete feature set used for the domain and continuous covariate tests. On those $12$ complete rows, the $M^{QTP}$ fraction versus GC3 test has Pearson $r=-0.3062616500469447$ with two-sided permutation $p=0.35264735264735264$ over $1000$ trials. The same response versus $\log$ tRNA gene count has $r=0.06029405206037823$ with $p=0.8621378621378621$, and versus $\log$ proteome size has $r=-0.033628115789504955$ with $p=0.913086913086913$. The domain test reports a variance ratio $0.8889980452277374$ with right-tail permutation $p=0.4155844155844156$. The dominant-coordinate-by-domain contingency has Pearson chi-square $9.399999999999999$, Cramer $V=0.6258327785172862$, and right-tail permutation $p=0.5814185814185814$. These finite-row tests do not resolve a domain-wide law for the tRNA-copy absorption pattern; they keep the readout at the level of measured organism-specific projection summaries.

\paragraph{Local verdict.}
The finite calculation is ready as a bounded cross-layer relation. It establishes that the measured tRNA gene-copy tAI surface can be joined without changing the modeled-complement residual target in the three reported organisms, and that its absorption of $P_{Q\perp T_{\mathrm{mod}}}$ is small: approximately $0.0208$ in \emph{D. rerio}, $0.000054$ in \emph{H. sapiens}, and $0.00153$ in \emph{S. cerevisiae}. The result is not a causal mediator test. tRNA gene copy number is a proxy for tRNA abundance, the wobble parameters used by tAI are modeling choices, and the absorbed fraction is a descriptive projection quantity. No claim about realized translation dynamics, protein abundance, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, mechanism, phenotype, or universality follows from this entry unless a separate layer-matched contact supplies the corresponding observations and statistic.
