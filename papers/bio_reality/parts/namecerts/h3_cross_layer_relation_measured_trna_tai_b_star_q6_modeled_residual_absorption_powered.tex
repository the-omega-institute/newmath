\subsection{Measured tRNA-copy tAI absorption of the $B^*_{Q6}$ modeled-complement residual}
\label{sec:bioreality-q6-measured-trna-tai-absorption}

\paragraph{Finite readout surface.}
The measured-tRNA contact tests whether an organism-specific tRNA gene-copy tAI surface absorbs any of the abundance residual component left after the modeled-tAI direction has already been removed.  The finite carrier is the CDS-by-organism table formed from the nine $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinates
$K\_AAA$, $Arg\_AGR$, $Ile\_AUA$, $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$, $Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG$, $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$, $Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG$, $Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY$, and $f3\_stress$, together with organism-matched tRNA gene-copy codon-adaptation weights.  The control design has $24$ columns before rank reduction: an intercept, $\log(cds\_len\_nt)$, the $20$ amino-acid composition proportions $A,C,D,E,F,G,H,I,K,L,M,N,P,Q,R,S,T,V,W,Y$, GC3, and the $M$-density baseline defined by the fraction of sense codons on the union support of the nine projected $B^*_{Q6}$ vectors.  In each organism used here the realized control rank is $23$, so the residualization step is not treated as a full-rank $24$-degree regression surface.

\paragraph{Joined rows and tRNA tables.}
The full-join check is exact for the three organisms in this measured-tRNA run.  For \emph{Danio rerio}, the CDS join contains $14410$ rows, the anticodon gene-copy table has $57$ entries, and $8846$ tRNA genes are parsed; after removing headers marked as selenocysteine, suppressor, or undetermined anticodon, $8607$ measured-tAI records remain usable, with no zero raw $W$ value filled by a geometric mean.  For \emph{Homo sapiens}, the join contains $19053$ CDS rows, the anticodon table has $47$ entries, $431$ tRNA genes are parsed, and $422$ measured-tAI records remain usable, again with zero geometric-mean fills.  For \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}, the join contains $5815$ CDS rows, the anticodon table has $41$ entries, $273$ tRNA genes are parsed, and $268$ measured-tAI records remain usable, also with zero geometric-mean fills.  The corresponding downloaded FASTA payload sizes are $1747886$, $82720$, and $56478$ bytes, respectively, so the three organism rows are finite records rather than symbolic contact names.

\paragraph{Complement-stability check.}
The residual target used for this tRNA-copy test is the same full-join modeled-complement target used by the abundance-complement computation.  The equality check is literal in all three cases: the absolute difference between the current full-join $d_{modeled}$ value and the complement reference is $0.0$ for \emph{D. rerio}, \emph{H. sapiens}, and \emph{S. cerevisiae}.  The modeled-complement fractions are
$$
\begin{aligned}
 d_{modeled}(\textit{D. rerio}) &= 0.874628721049266,\\
 d_{modeled}(\textit{H. sapiens}) &= 0.9208949355679455,\\
 d_{modeled}(\textit{S. cerevisiae}) &= 0.21173219572706703.
\end{aligned}
$$
This matters because the tRNA-copy absorption numbers can then be read against a stable residual target rather than against a feature-availability subset.  It is not a causal mediation result; it is only a controlled descriptive projection on the declared rows.

\paragraph{Observed absorption magnitudes.}
The measured tRNA gene-copy tAI surface removes a small fraction of the modeled-complement residual in all three organisms.  The strongest entry is the zebrafish row, where the absorbed fraction is $0.020817102845637092$ and the combined residual fraction is $0.835657382343872$.  The human row is essentially unchanged at this scale, with absorbed fraction $0.00005426229300865148$ and combined residual fraction $0.92041342656924$.  The yeast row has absorbed fraction $0.001530747712165342$ and combined residual fraction $0.20648621019305421$.  Thus the finite result is not that tRNA-copy tAI explains the $B^*_{Q6}$ complement.  The result is narrower: after the stated covariates and the modeled-tAI complement are fixed, the tRNA-copy tAI contact has a measurable but small descriptive overlap, largest in \emph{D. rerio} and minimal in the other two organisms.

\paragraph{Measured-TE comparison.}
A separate measured-translation-efficiency run gives the immediate boundary condition for interpreting this tRNA-copy result.  In the measured-TE panel, the all-readout joins are $13140$ rows for \emph{D. rerio}, $3415$ for \emph{E. coli} K12 MG1655, $11554$ for \emph{H. sapiens}, and $5128$ for \emph{S. cerevisiae}, each with control rank $23$.  The measured-TE absorption fractions are $0.01759429001506974$, $0.0010982956489726373$, $0.0028947149257963897$, and $0.0002762783754375593$, respectively.  These values are comparable in scale to the tRNA-copy absorption values, but they are not interchangeable: tRNA gene copy number is a proxy for the codon-adaptation surface, whereas measured TE is a ribosome-profiling ratio with matched mRNA abundance in its construction.  Therefore the tRNA-copy contact may be reported as a bounded translation-adaptation readback, not as measured ribosome occupancy and not as protein output.

\paragraph{Cross-organism scope.}
The broader organism-specific summary also blocks any upgrade from these three rows to a general biological rule.  In the twelve-row meta table, the dominant-coordinate versus domain contingency has Pearson chi-square $9.399999999999999$, Cramer's $V=0.6258327785172862$, and right-tail permutation probability $0.5814185814185814$ across $1000$ trials.  The $M_{QTP}$ fraction has weak and nonsignificant continuous associations in that table: versus GC3, $r=-0.3062616500469447$ with two-sided permutation probability $0.35264735264735264$; versus log tRNA gene count, $r=0.06029405206037823$ with probability $0.8621378621378621$; versus log proteome size, $r=-0.033628115789504955$ with probability $0.913086913086913$.  The domain variance-ratio test for $M_{QTP}$ gives $0.8889980452277374$ with right-tail permutation probability $0.4155844155844156$.  These checks support heterogeneity as the honest finite reading: the current rows show bounded organism-specific absorption, not a resolved phylogenetic, domain-level, or universal pattern.

\paragraph{Local interpretation boundary.}
The chapter therefore licenses only the following statement.  On the declared finite rows, tRNA gene-copy tAI can be projected against the modeled-tAI-complement abundance residual after amino-acid composition, CDS length, GC3, and $M$-density controls, and the resulting absorbed fractions are finite and small.  No statement about translation realization follows from this alone unless a direct measured translation contact supplies the corresponding row and statistic.  No statement about folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, organismal phenotype, mechanism, or universality follows from this chapter.  The tRNA-copy surface is a bounded codon-adaptation proxy; it is not direct tRNA-seq, not direct ribosome occupancy, not protein abundance, and not a higher-layer biological assay.
