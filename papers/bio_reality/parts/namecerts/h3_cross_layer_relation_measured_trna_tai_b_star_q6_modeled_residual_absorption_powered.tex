\subsection{Measured tRNA-copy tAI absorption of the $B^*_{Q6}$ modeled residual}
\label{sec:bioreality-q6-measured-trna-tai-absorption}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Question.}
This record tests a bounded cross-layer contact between the nine $B^*_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates and an organism-level tRNA gene-copy tAI surface.  The object being measured is not mature protein abundance, ribosome dwell time, folding, function, phenotype, or a causal mechanism.  It is the following finite projection question: after the residual coordinates have already been controlled by amino-acid composition, coding-sequence length, GC3, and the $M$-density baseline, how much of the previously modeled-tAI-complement abundance component is absorbed by replacing the modeled adaptation surface with a tRNA gene-copy-derived tAI readout?

\paragraph{Finite rows and coordinates.}
The finite coordinate list has nine entries,
$$
\begin{aligned}
R_{Q6}=\{&K\_AAA,\ Arg\_AGR,\ Ile\_AUA,\ Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR,\ Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG,\\
&Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY,\ Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG,\ Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY,\ f3\_stress\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
For each organism, the control matrix contains an intercept, $\log(\mathrm{cds\_len\_nt})$, twenty amino-acid composition proportions computed from real CDS codon counts, GC3, and the $M$-density baseline defined as the fraction of sense codons on the union support of the nine projected $B^*_{Q6}$ vectors.  The reported control-column count is $24$, with rank $23$, for each organism in this tRNA-copy tAI contact.  Thus the readout is not a free unconstrained fit: it is evaluated after a fixed residualization boundary and on finite joined coding-sequence rows.

\paragraph{Joined organism panels.}
The three joined organisms are zebrafish, human, and budding yeast.  The zebrafish row has $n=14410$ joined CDS records, $8846$ parsed tRNA genes, and an anticodon gene-copy table of size $57$.  The human row has $n=19053$ joined CDS records, $431$ parsed tRNA genes, and an anticodon table of size $47$.  The budding-yeast row has $n=5815$ joined CDS records, $273$ parsed tRNA genes, and an anticodon table of size $41$.  The usable measured-tAI record counts are respectively $8607$, $422$, and $268$, and no raw $W$ value required geometric-mean filling in any of the three organisms.  The parser excluded three selenocysteine, twenty-five suppressor, and five undetermined-anticodon headers in zebrafish; one selenocysteine header in human; and two undetermined-anticodon headers in yeast.

\paragraph{Full-join stability against the modeled-complement reference.}
The modeled-complement value is exactly preserved against the reference full join in all three organisms.  The comparison is
$$
\begin{aligned}
D_{\mathrm{modeled}}(\mathrm{zebrafish})&=0.874628721049266,\\
D_{\mathrm{modeled}}(\mathrm{human})&=0.9208949355679455,\\
D_{\mathrm{modeled}}(\mathrm{yeast})&=0.21173219572706703.
\end{aligned}
$$
In each case the absolute difference from the complement-experiment reference is $0.0$, the current join size equals the complement-reference join size, and the reported match-within-$0.02$ check is true.  This matters because the tRNA-copy tAI absorption is computed on the same full CDS join as the complement surface, rather than on a sparse feature subset that changes the residual target.

\paragraph{Absorption values.}
The measured tRNA gene-copy tAI projection produces small but finite descriptive absorption values:
$$
\begin{aligned}
A_{\mathrm{tRNA}}(\mathrm{zebrafish})&=0.020817102845637092,\\
A_{\mathrm{tRNA}}(\mathrm{human})&=0.00005426229300865148,\\
A_{\mathrm{tRNA}}(\mathrm{yeast})&=0.001530747712165342.
\end{aligned}
$$
The corresponding combined residual levels are
$$
\begin{aligned}
D_{\mathrm{combined}}(\mathrm{zebrafish})&=0.835657382343872,\\
D_{\mathrm{combined}}(\mathrm{human})&=0.92041342656924,\\
D_{\mathrm{combined}}(\mathrm{yeast})&=0.20648621019305421.
\end{aligned}
$$
The strongest finite absorption in this panel is therefore the zebrafish value, about $2.08\%$ of the modeled-complement residual component.  Yeast is much smaller, about $0.153\%$, and human is nearly zero on this statistic, about $0.0054\%$.  The result is heterogeneous across organisms and is not a population-level law.

\paragraph{Comparison with measured translation-efficiency rows.}
A separate measured translation-efficiency contact tests ribosome-profiling TE as a descriptive H-candidate on four organism joins.  Its TE absorption values are $0.01759429001506974$ for zebrafish, $0.0010982956489726373$ for \emph{Escherichia coli} K12 MG1655, $0.0028947149257963897$ for human, and $0.0002762783754375593$ for budding yeast.  The TE contact uses measured TE rows with joined counts $13140$, $3415$, $11554$, and $5128$ respectively, again with $24$ control columns and rank $23$.  These TE rows are not interchangeable with the tRNA-copy tAI rows: zebrafish has comparable tRNA-copy and TE absorption magnitudes, human has higher TE absorption than tRNA-copy absorption, and yeast has higher tRNA-copy absorption than TE absorption, but all of these are descriptive projections rather than causal mediation tests.

\paragraph{Cross-organism scope.}
The broader organism-specific survey reports twelve complete-feature organisms for several meta summaries, with $16$ rows assembled overall and four organisms still needing data for the $M^{QTP}$ meta surface.  In that survey, the dominant-coordinate contingency against domain has $n=12$, Pearson chi-square $9.399999999999999$, Cramer's $V=0.6258327785172862$, and a right-tail permutation value $p=0.5814185814185814$ over $1000$ trials.  The $M_{QTP}$ fraction has weak continuous associations in that finite survey: $r=-0.3062616500469447$ against GC3 with two-sided permutation $p=0.35264735264735264$, $r=-0.033628115789504955$ against log proteome size with $p=0.913086913086913$, and $r=0.06029405206037823$ against log tRNA-gene count with $p=0.8621378621378621$.  These numbers support a conservative reading of the present tRNA-copy tAI contact: organism-specific structure is visible in finite rows, but the available panel does not license a resolved domain preference or a universal rule.

\paragraph{Interpretation.}
The finite readout supports the following bounded statement.  A tRNA gene-copy-derived tAI surface can be joined to the $B^*_{Q6}$ residual panel for zebrafish, human, and budding yeast without changing the modeled-complement full join, and it absorbs a small organism-dependent fraction of the modeled-tAI-complement abundance component.  The largest absorption is in zebrafish, while human is nearly unchanged and yeast is modestly changed.  This is evidence for a bounded relation between a synonymous residual coordinate system and a tRNA-copy adaptation proxy, not evidence that the residual basis directly realizes translation dynamics.

\paragraph{Local refusal boundary.}
The contact must be read as a statistical projection on finite rows.  tRNA gene copy number is only a proxy for tRNA abundance, not direct tRNA-seq; the tAI wobble parameters are inherited modeling choices; and the absorbed fraction is descriptive rather than a causal mediator estimate.  No claim about translation realization, ribosome occupancy, protein abundance, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, biological mechanism, phenotype, or universality follows from this chapter unless another layer-matched contact supplies that measurement and its own controlled statistic.  If the residual remains after this tRNA-copy tAI projection, the unabsorbed component remains open to other bounded contacts rather than being promoted to a biological mechanism.
