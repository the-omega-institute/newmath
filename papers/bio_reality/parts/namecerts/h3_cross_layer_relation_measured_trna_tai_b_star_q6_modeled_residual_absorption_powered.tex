\subsection{Measured tRNA-copy tAI absorption of the $B^*_{Q6}$ modeled residual}
\label{sec:bioreality-measured-trna-tai-q6-residual-absorption}

\paragraph{Finite readout surface.}
This record tests whether a measured tRNA-gene-copy tAI surface absorbs any part of the $B^*_{Q6}$ modeled-tAI-complement abundance residual.  The finite carrier is the per-CDS organism-matched table obtained after joining coding-sequence codon counts, the nine $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinates, and organism-level tRNA gene-copy anticodon counts.  The nine coordinates are $K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $Leu_{CUN\,vs\,UUR}$, $Leu_{UUA\,vs\,UUG}$, $Ser_{UCR\,vs\,AGY}$, $Ser_{UCA\,vs\,UCG}$, $Thr_{ACR\,vs\,ACY}$, and $f_3\_stress$.  The control block used for the residual coordinates contains an intercept, $\log(\mathrm{cds\_len\_nt})$, the twenty amino-acid composition proportions $A,C,D,E,F,G,H,I,K,L,M,N,P,Q,R,S,T,V,W,Y$, GC3 from the same CDS codon counts, and an $M$-density baseline defined as the fraction of sense codons lying on the union support of the nine projected $B^*_{Q6}$ vectors.  The control design has $24$ columns and rank $23$ in every reported organism.

\paragraph{Joined organism rows and tRNA-copy readback.}
The measured tRNA-copy contact is finite and fully enumerated for three organisms.  In \emph{Danio rerio}, the joined CDS surface has $n=14410$ rows, an anticodon gene-copy table of size $57$, and $8846$ parsed tRNA genes; $8607$ measured-tAI records are usable, with $0$ raw $W$ entries filled by a geometric mean.  In \emph{Homo sapiens}, the joined surface has $n=19053$ rows, an anticodon table of size $47$, and $431$ parsed tRNA genes; $422$ measured-tAI records are usable, again with $0$ geometric-mean fills.  In \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}, the joined surface has $n=5815$ rows, an anticodon table of size $41$, and $273$ parsed tRNA genes; $268$ measured-tAI records are usable, also with $0$ geometric-mean fills.  The parsing exclusions are explicit: zebrafish excludes $3$ selenocysteine, $25$ suppressor, and $5$ undetermined-anticodon headers; human excludes $1$ selenocysteine header; yeast excludes $2$ undetermined-anticodon headers.

\paragraph{Reference residual magnitude.}
Before tRNA-copy tAI is added, the modeled-tAI-complement residual fraction $d_{modeled}$ exactly matches the complement reference on the same full join in all three organisms.  The zebrafish value is $0.874628721049266$ on $14410$ rows, the human value is $0.9208949355679455$ on $19053$ rows, and the yeast value is $0.21173219572706703$ on $5815$ rows.  In each case the reported absolute difference against the complement reference is $0.0$, so this test is not a sparse-feature reindexing artifact.  The tested projection can be summarized as
$$
\begin{aligned}
P_Q &\longmapsto \widehat{P}_{Q\perp T_{mod}},\\
\widehat{P}_{Q\perp T_{mod}} &\longmapsto \widehat{P}_{Q\perp T_{mod}\perp T_{trna}},
\end{aligned}
$$
where $T_{mod}$ denotes the modeled-tAI component already removed and $T_{trna}$ denotes the measured tRNA-gene-copy tAI readout.

\paragraph{Absorption values.}
The measured tRNA-copy tAI surface absorbs only a small fraction of the modeled residual.  In zebrafish, $absorbed\_by\_trna=0.020817102845637092$ and the combined remaining fraction is $d_{combined}=0.835657382343872$.  In human, $absorbed\_by\_trna=0.00005426229300865148$ and $d_{combined}=0.92041342656924$.  In yeast, $absorbed\_by\_trna=0.001530747712165342$ and $d_{combined}=0.20648621019305421$.  Thus the largest finite-row absorption is the zebrafish value, about $2.08\%$ of the modeled residual component, while the human and yeast absorptions are near zero on this scale.  The test therefore records bounded descriptive absorption rather than closure of the residual by measured tRNA copy number.

\paragraph{Relation to measured translation-efficiency rows.}
A separate measured-translation-efficiency computation gives a useful boundary for interpretation.  On the all-readout TE join, zebrafish has $n=13140$ rows and measured-TE absorption $0.01759429001506974$ with $d_{combined}=0.8276090159036916$; \emph{E. coli} K12 MG1655 has $n=3415$ rows and absorption $0.0010982956489726373$ with $d_{combined}=0.6620871850159149$; human has $n=11554$ rows and absorption $0.0028947149257963897$ with $d_{combined}=0.854133502300174$; yeast has $n=5128$ rows and absorption $0.0002762783754375593$ with $d_{combined}=0.2237928123367028$.  These rows are not substituted for the tRNA-copy tAI surface.  They show that the same residual family remains mostly outside both the measured TE readout and the measured tRNA-copy proxy in the available panels.

\paragraph{Cross-organism context.}
The broader cross-organism summary is exploratory and does not supply a phylogenetic correction.  Among the complete-feature organisms, the meta table has $12$ rows, while the assembled organism list has $16$ rows with missing $M^{QTP}$ data for \emph{Pseudomonas aeruginosa} PAO1, \emph{Halobacterium salinarum}, \emph{Dictyostelium discoideum}, and \emph{Arabidopsis thaliana}.  The $M^{QTP}$ fraction versus domain test reports a variance ratio of $0.8889980452277374$ with right-tail permutation $p=0.4155844155844156$ over $1000$ trials.  The continuous tests give $r=-0.3062616500469447$ for $M^{QTP}$ fraction versus GC3 with two-sided permutation $p=0.35264735264735264$, $r=0.06029405206037823$ versus log tRNA-gene count with $p=0.8621378621378621$, and $r=-0.033628115789504955$ versus log proteome size with $p=0.913086913086913$.  The dominant-coordinate contingency has $n=12$, Pearson $\chi^2=9.399999999999999$, Cramer's $V=0.6258327785172862$, and right-tail permutation $p=0.5814185814185814$.  These numbers place the measured tRNA-copy tAI absorption result inside a heterogeneous organism panel, not inside a resolved universal rule.

\paragraph{Verdict.}
The finite rows support a narrow statement: measured tRNA-gene-copy tAI can be projected against the modeled-tAI-complement $B^*_{Q6}$ abundance residual, and the resulting absorptions are $0.020817102845637092$, $0.00005426229300865148$, and $0.001530747712165342$ for zebrafish, human, and yeast respectively.  The contact is therefore a bounded cross-layer readout for residual absorption, with the strongest observed effect still small and with the residual largely remaining after the projection.  Because tRNA gene copy number is only a proxy for tRNA abundance, and because the wobble parameters are a modeling choice rather than a direct ribosome measurement, this section does not infer translation realization, protein abundance, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, mechanism, phenotype, or universality.  Any such stronger conclusion would require its own layer-matched observation and its own finite statistic.
