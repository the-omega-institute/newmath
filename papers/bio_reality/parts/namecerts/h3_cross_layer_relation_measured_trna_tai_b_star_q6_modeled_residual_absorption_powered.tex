\subsection{Measured tRNA-copy tAI absorption of the $B^*_{Q6}$ modeled-complement residual}
\label{sec:bioreality-measured-trna-tai-q6-absorption}

\paragraph{Finite surface.}
This record tests a bounded cross-layer question: after the nine $B^*_{Q6}$ synonymous-residual coordinates are projected against the declared control surface, does an organism-specific tRNA gene-copy tAI readout absorb any of the abundance component that remained outside the modeled-tAI projection?  The tested residual basis has coordinates
$K\_AAA$, $Arg\_AGR$, $Ile\_AUA$, $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$, $Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG$, $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$, $Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG$, $Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY$, and $f3\_stress$.  The control matrix contains an intercept, $\log(\mathrm{cds\_len\_nt})$, the twenty amino-acid composition proportions $A,C,D,E,F,G,H,I,K,L,M,N,P,Q,R,S,T,V,W,Y$, GC3 computed from real CDS codon counts, and the $M$-density baseline defined on the union support of the nine projected $B^*_{Q6}$ vectors.  In every organism used here the control column count is $24$ and the realized control rank is $23$, so the contact is not a rank-saturated three-row fit.

\paragraph{Joined rows and tRNA-copy readout.}
The finite panel contains three organisms with full CDS joins for this test.  In $Danio\ rerio$, $n=14410$ CDS rows are joined to an anticodon gene-copy table of size $57$ with $8846$ parsed tRNA genes; $8607$ measured-tAI records are usable and no zero raw $W$ entry is filled by the geometric mean.  In $Homo\ sapiens$, $n=19053$ CDS rows are joined to an anticodon table of size $47$ with $431$ parsed tRNA genes; $422$ measured-tAI records are usable and again no zero raw $W$ entry is imputed.  In $Saccharomyces\ cerevisiae$, $n=5815$ CDS rows are joined to an anticodon table of size $41$ with $273$ parsed tRNA genes; $268$ measured-tAI records are usable and the zero-fill count is $0$.  Header exclusions are also finite: zebrafish excludes $3$ selenocysteine, $25$ suppressor, and $5$ undetermined-anticodon entries; human excludes $1$ selenocysteine entry; yeast excludes $2$ undetermined-anticodon entries.

\paragraph{Modeled-complement check.}
The modeled-complement target is stable under the full join used for the tRNA-copy contact.  The complement distance copied into this contact exactly matches the reference abundance-CDS join in all three organisms:
$$
\begin{aligned}
 d_{\mathrm{modeled}}(Danio\ rerio) &= 0.874628721049266,\\
 d_{\mathrm{modeled}}(Homo\ sapiens) &= 0.9208949355679455,\\
 d_{\mathrm{modeled}}(Saccharomyces\ cerevisiae) &= 0.21173219572706703.
\end{aligned}
$$
The absolute reference difference is $0.0$ for each organism, with current and reference join counts equal to $14410$, $19053$, and $5815$ respectively.  This matters because the absorption statistic is then read against the same modeled-complement surface, rather than against a shifted protein-id subset.

\paragraph{Absorption result.}
The tRNA-copy tAI surface has only a small descriptive absorption of the modeled-complement residual.  The computed absorbed fractions and combined distances are
$$
\begin{aligned}
 \mathrm{absorb}_{tRNA}(Danio\ rerio) &= 0.020817102845637092, & d_{\mathrm{combined}} &= 0.835657382343872,\\
 \mathrm{absorb}_{tRNA}(Homo\ sapiens) &= 0.00005426229300865148, & d_{\mathrm{combined}} &= 0.92041342656924,\\
 \mathrm{absorb}_{tRNA}(Saccharomyces\ cerevisiae) &= 0.001530747712165342, & d_{\mathrm{combined}} &= 0.20648621019305421.
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus zebrafish shows the largest tRNA-copy absorption, about $2.08\%$ of the modeled-complement target, while human and yeast are nearly unchanged at about $5.43\times 10^{-5}$ and $1.53\times 10^{-3}$.  The result is therefore a powered finite-row calculation, but it is not a strong absorption event across the panel.  Its useful content is narrower: tRNA gene-copy tAI can be placed beside the $B^*_{Q6}$ modeled-complement residual without changing the target join, and the observed absorption is small, organism-dependent, and descriptive.

\paragraph{Measured-TE comparison.}
A separate ribosome-profiling translation-efficiency calculation gives a useful scale for the boundary.  On all-readout joins with matched abundance and TE rows, the measured-TE absorption values are $0.01759429001506974$ for zebrafish, $0.0010982956489726373$ for $E.\ coli\ K12\ MG1655$, $0.0028947149257963897$ for human, and $0.0002762783754375593$ for yeast.  Those joins are smaller for the eukaryotic TE panel: $13140$ zebrafish rows rather than $14410$, $11554$ human rows rather than $19053$, and $5128$ yeast rows rather than $5815$; $E.\ coli$ contributes $3415$ TE-valid rows from $3739$ abundance-CDS joins.  The TE contact therefore cannot be silently substituted for the tRNA-copy contact, and the tRNA-copy statistic cannot be reported as measured ribosome occupancy.  The two surfaces are different finite readouts with different availability boundaries.

\paragraph{Cross-organism context.}
The broader organism-specific meta surface also warns against reading these values as a universal translation law.  In the available meta panel, the complete-feature row count is $12$ inside a larger $16$-organism assembly, with missing $M^{QTP}$ data for $Pseudomonas\ aeruginosa\ PAO1$, $Halobacterium\ salinarum$, $Dictyostelium\ discoideum$, and $Arabidopsis\ thaliana$.  The dominant-coordinate by domain contingency has $n=12$, Pearson $\chi^2=9.4$, Cramer $V=0.6258327785172862$, and a right-tail permutation value $p=0.5814185814185814$ over $1000$ trials.  Continuous tests are likewise not decisive: $M^{QTP}$ fraction versus GC3 has $r=-0.3062616500469447$ and two-sided permutation $p=0.35264735264735264$; versus log tRNA-gene count has $r=0.06029405206037823$ and $p=0.8621378621378621$; versus log proteome size has $r=-0.033628115789504955$ and $p=0.913086913086913$.  These numbers support heterogeneity and bounded comparison, not a resolved phylogenetic or domain-level rule.

\paragraph{Interpretation boundary.}
The accepted statement is only that tRNA gene-copy tAI was computed from finite organism-specific anticodon tables and projected as a descriptive absorber of $\widehat P_{Q\perp Tmod}$ under the stated control matrix.  Gene copy number is a proxy for tRNA abundance, not direct tRNA-seq; the tAI wobble coefficients are a modeling choice; and the absorbed fraction is not a causal mediator estimate.  No translation-realization claim, folding claim, physical-admissibility claim, molecular-function claim, mechanism claim, phenotype claim, or universal biological law follows from this contact unless a separate layer-matched measurement supplies that evidence.  In particular, a low absorbed fraction leaves the $B^*_{Q6}$ complement largely outside this tRNA-copy tAI readout; it does not prove biological irrelevance, and it does not license promotion to protein abundance, structure, function, or mechanism.
