\subsection{Measured tRNA-copy tAI absorption of the $B^*_{Q6}$ modeled-complement residual}
\label{sec:bioreality-q6-measured-trna-tai-absorption}

\paragraph{Finite readout surface.}
This record tests whether an organism-specific tRNA gene-copy tAI readout absorbs the part of the $B^*_{Q6}$ abundance residual that remains after the modeled-tAI component has already been removed. The carrier is a finite CDS-by-organism table with nine residual coordinates,
$K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $Leu_{CUN\_vs\_UUR}$, $Leu_{UUA\_vs\_UUG}$, $Ser_{UCR\_vs\_AGY}$, $Ser_{UCA\_vs\_UCG}$, $Thr_{ACR\_vs\_ACY}$, and $f3_{stress}$, together with organism-matched tRNA gene-copy counts converted to a tAI-style readout. The control block contains an intercept, $\log(\mathrm{cds\_len\_nt})$, the 20 amino-acid composition proportions computed from real CDS codon counts, GC3, and the $M$-density baseline defined as the fraction of sense codons on the union support of the nine projected $B^*_{Q6}$ vectors. In each organism the control matrix has 24 columns and rank 23, so the reported quantities are not an all-column saturated fit.

\paragraph{Joined rows and tRNA tables.}
The tRNA-copy contact is evaluated on the same full abundance-CDS joins used by the modeled-complement reference. For \emph{Danio rerio}, the joined surface has $n=14410$ CDS rows, 8846 parsed tRNA genes, 57 anticodon GCN entries, and 8607 usable measured-tAI records; three selenocysteine, 25 suppressor, and five undetermined-anticodon headers are excluded. For \emph{Homo sapiens}, the joined surface has $n=19053$ CDS rows, 431 parsed tRNA genes, 47 anticodon GCN entries, and 422 usable measured-tAI records; one selenocysteine header is excluded. For \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}, the joined surface has $n=5815$ CDS rows, 273 parsed tRNA genes, 41 anticodon GCN entries, and 268 usable measured-tAI records; two undetermined-anticodon headers are excluded. No organism required geometric-mean filling for zero raw $W$ values.

\paragraph{Reference stability of the modeled-complement target.}
The modeled-complement residual is evaluated on the identical join used by the abundance-side reference in all three organisms. The full-join equality check is exact at the displayed precision:
$$
\begin{aligned}
 d_{\mathrm{modeled}}(\mathrm{danio}) &= 0.874628721049266, & |\Delta| &= 0.0,\\
 d_{\mathrm{modeled}}(\mathrm{human}) &= 0.9208949355679455, & |\Delta| &= 0.0,\\
 d_{\mathrm{modeled}}(\mathrm{yeast}) &= 0.21173219572706703, & |\Delta| &= 0.0.
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the tRNA-copy tAI calculation is not shifting the residual target by changing the protein or CDS join. This is important because a neighboring measured-translation-efficiency run used smaller TE-available joins: 13140 rows in \emph{Danio rerio}, 11554 rows in human, 5128 rows in yeast, and 3415 rows in \emph{Escherichia coli} K12 MG1655. In that TE-available run the modeled-complement mismatch against the full abundance-CDS reference was 0.03219747984141397 in \emph{Danio rerio}, 0.06427590857052146 in human, 0.012133464712697745 in yeast, and 0.00017488612263594217 in \emph{E. coli}. The present tRNA-copy calculation therefore has a cleaner target-stability status than the measured-TE availability surface for the vertebrate rows.

\paragraph{Absorption estimates.}
The descriptive absorption by measured tRNA-copy tAI is small in every organism. The finite-row output is
$$
\begin{aligned}
 \mathrm{absorbed}_{\mathrm{tRNA}}(\mathrm{danio}) &= 0.020817102845637092, & d_{\mathrm{combined}} &= 0.835657382343872,\\
 \mathrm{absorbed}_{\mathrm{tRNA}}(\mathrm{human}) &= 0.00005426229300865148, & d_{\mathrm{combined}} &= 0.92041342656924,\\
 \mathrm{absorbed}_{\mathrm{tRNA}}(\mathrm{yeast}) &= 0.001530747712165342, & d_{\mathrm{combined}} &= 0.20648621019305421.
\end{aligned}
$$
The largest absorption is the \emph{Danio rerio} value, about 2.08 percent of the tested modeled-complement residual. Human is effectively unchanged at this scale, and yeast is also small. The result therefore supports a bounded statement only: measured tRNA gene-copy tAI, as operationalized here with dos Reis-style wobble weights and organism-specific GCN tables, does not substantially remove the modeled-tAI-complement residual in this three-organism finite panel.

\paragraph{Relation to measured translation efficiency.}
The measured-TE companion computation also reports low descriptive absorption after residualization on the TE-available joins: 0.01759429001506974 for \emph{Danio rerio}, 0.0028947149257963897 for human, 0.0002762783754375593 for yeast, and 0.0010982956489726373 for \emph{E. coli}. Those values are not interchangeable with the tRNA-copy tAI values because the TE panel has a different availability boundary in several organisms and includes matched mRNA abundance in the translation-efficiency construction. The shared message is narrower: neither a tRNA-copy tAI readout nor the available measured-TE projection, in these finite rows, warrants promotion to a causal translation mechanism or a downstream protein claim.

\paragraph{Cross-organism context.}
A separate organism-specificity summary has 16 total rows, with 12 complete-feature organisms available for the reported domain and continuous-covariate tests. Its permutation checks do not resolve a strong domain or continuous predictor for the $M^{QTP}$ fraction: the domain test has right-tail permutation $p=0.4155844155844156$, the dominant-coordinate-by-domain contingency has $p=0.5814185814185814$, $M^{QTP}$ versus GC3 has two-sided permutation $p=0.35264735264735264$ with $r=-0.3062616500469447$, $M^{QTP}$ versus log tRNA gene count has $p=0.8621378621378621$ with $r=0.06029405206037823$, and $M^{QTP}$ versus log proteome size has $p=0.913086913086913$ with $r=-0.033628115789504955$. These finite meta-tests are exploratory and not phylogenetically corrected. They give useful caution for this chapter: an organism-specific tRNA-copy absorption estimate should be read as a bounded projection on the available rows, not as a universal codon-usage or translation law.

\paragraph{Local no-promotion rule.}
The permitted conclusion is only that the descriptive projection quantities were computed and that tRNA gene-copy tAI absorbs little of the modeled-tAI-complement residual in the three full-join organisms. The tRNA variable is a proxy for tRNA abundance, not direct tRNA-seq. The wobble parameters are a modeling choice, not an observed ribosome-dynamics measurement. No translation realization, ribosome mechanism, protein abundance, folding, physical admissibility, biological function, phenotype, or universality follows from this chapter unless a separate layer-matched contact supplies rows and a statistic at that layer. If the $B^*_{Q6}$ residual remains after the tRNA-copy tAI readout, the correct interpretation is an unresolved cross-layer remainder, not evidence for a higher biological mechanism.
