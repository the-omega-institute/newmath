\subsection{Measured tRNA--tAI absorption of the $B^*_{Q6}$ modeled-residual component}
\label{subsec:measured-trna-tai-b-star-q6-modeled-residual-absorption}

\paragraph{Question.}
This record tests a narrow cross-layer question: after the nine $B^*_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates have been formed and the modeled-tAI component has been separated, how much of the remaining modeled-complement abundance component is absorbed by a tRNA gene-copy tAI readout?  The tested coordinates are
$K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $Leu_{CUN\,vs\,UUR}$, $Leu_{UUA\,vs\,UUG}$, $Ser_{UCR\,vs\,AGY}$, $Ser_{UCA\,vs\,UCG}$, $Thr_{ACR\,vs\,ACY}$, and $f3_{stress}$.  The control frame has $24$ columns and rank $23$ in every organism considered here, consisting of an intercept, $\log(\mathrm{cds\_len\_nt})$, the $20$ amino-acid composition proportions from real CDS codon counts, $GC3$, and the $M$-density baseline on the union support of the nine projected $B^*_{Q6}$ vectors.

\paragraph{Finite readout surface.}
The finite carrier is the organism-by-CDS join on which codon counts, the nine residual coordinates, the control frame, and the tRNA gene-copy tAI values are simultaneously defined.  Three organisms enter the powered tRNA-copy test.  In \emph{Danio rerio}, the full CDS join contains $14410$ rows, the parsed tRNA table contains $8846$ tRNA genes over $57$ anticodon gene-copy entries, and $8607$ measured-tAI records are usable.  In \emph{Homo sapiens}, the corresponding values are $19053$ joined CDS rows, $431$ tRNA genes, $47$ anticodon entries, and $422$ usable measured-tAI records.  In \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}, the corresponding values are $5815$ joined CDS rows, $273$ tRNA genes, $41$ anticodon entries, and $268$ usable measured-tAI records.  No zero raw $W$ value required replacement by a geometric mean in any of the three organisms.

\paragraph{Join stability.}
The tRNA-copy contact preserves the full abundance-CDS join used by the modeled-complement reference.  The reference modeled-complement distances and the current full-join distances coincide exactly in the recorded precision:
$$
\begin{aligned}
 d_{\mathrm{modeled}}(\mathrm{Danio}) &= 0.874628721049266,\\
 d_{\mathrm{modeled}}(\mathrm{human}) &= 0.9208949355679455,\\
 d_{\mathrm{modeled}}(\mathrm{yeast}) &= 0.21173219572706703.
\end{aligned}
$$
For each organism the absolute difference from the complement reference is $0.0$, and each match is within the $0.02$ tolerance.  This matters because the same residual target is being tested across the tRNA-copy readout, rather than a shifted target induced by missing rows.  The matched translation-efficiency experiment does not have this property in all organisms: the measured-TE all-readout join has $13140$ rows in \emph{Danio rerio}, $3415$ rows in \emph{E. coli} K12 MG1655, $11554$ rows in human, and $5128$ rows in yeast, with complement-reference differences $0.03219747984141397$, $0.00017488612263594217$, $0.06427590857052146$, and $0.012133464712697745$, respectively.  Thus the tRNA-copy calculation is the cleaner full-join absorption test, while measured TE is a separate and partly row-shifted contact.

\paragraph{Absorption values.}
The descriptive absorption fractions are small but positive in all three tested organisms.  The recorded values are
$$
\begin{aligned}
 \mathrm{absorb}_{\mathrm{tRNA}}(\mathrm{Danio}) &= 0.020817102845637092,
 & d_{\mathrm{combined}}(\mathrm{Danio}) &= 0.835657382343872,\\
 \mathrm{absorb}_{\mathrm{tRNA}}(\mathrm{human}) &= 0.00005426229300865148,
 & d_{\mathrm{combined}}(\mathrm{human}) &= 0.92041342656924,\\
 \mathrm{absorb}_{\mathrm{tRNA}}(\mathrm{yeast}) &= 0.001530747712165342,
 & d_{\mathrm{combined}}(\mathrm{yeast}) &= 0.20648621019305421.
\end{aligned}
$$
The largest recorded absorption is the \emph{Danio rerio} value, about $2.08\%$ of the modeled-complement residual component under this projection.  Yeast shows a much smaller but nonzero value, about $0.153\%$.  Human is essentially unchanged at this scale, about $0.0054\%$.  These numbers support a bounded readback: tRNA gene-copy tAI can account for a small part of the modeled-tAI-complement abundance residual in this finite surface, but it does not erase the modeled-complement component.

\paragraph{Data provenance and parsing.}
The tRNA input files were fetched from GtRNAdb FASTA endpoints.  The \emph{Danio rerio} payload has $1747886$ bytes and SHA256 hash \texttt{e81e605548df44e88ca0c3778b315202bd826c377ed9173e1a8575e21eb4090a}; headers excluded $3$ selenocysteine entries, $25$ suppressor entries, and $5$ undetermined-anticodon entries.  The human payload has $82720$ bytes and SHA256 hash \texttt{50bd6fe75f60ce7461864dc1604a3c51481f1adfae7ad74198a1f389c211ac89}; one selenocysteine header was excluded.  The yeast payload has $56478$ bytes and SHA256 hash \texttt{273c3519053ef469cf4e43063ce634187a943a5d53ea4e87879b2224860d82c8}; two undetermined-anticodon headers were excluded.  The calculation therefore uses finite, auditable tRNA-copy tables rather than an unrecorded adaptation score.

\paragraph{Comparison with adjacent contacts.}
The measured-TE contact absorbs $0.01759429001506974$ in \emph{Danio rerio}, $0.0010982956489726373$ in \emph{E. coli} K12 MG1655, $0.0028947149257963897$ in human, and $0.0002762783754375593$ in yeast, but its row join differs from the full abundance-CDS complement reference in \emph{Danio rerio} and human.  The yeast turnover contact uses $3640$ joined rows, matches the complement reference within $0.011538255214205373$, and absorbs $0.0009086031889966327$.  These adjacent contacts help locate the tRNA-copy result: it is not a general translation-efficiency result and not a turnover result.  It is specifically a tRNA gene-copy tAI projection on the modeled-complement component, computed on the full CDS join for the three organisms where the tRNA tables are present.

\paragraph{Local conclusion.}
The finite result is positive but modest.  The tRNA gene-copy tAI readout absorbs a small fraction of the modeled-tAI-complement abundance residual in the tested organisms, with the strongest finite value in \emph{Danio rerio} and near-zero absorption in human.  Because the readout is gene-copy tAI, not direct tRNA-seq, and because the wobble weights are a modeling choice, the result is descriptive rather than causal.  It does not by itself establish realized translation dynamics, ribosome occupancy, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, mechanism, phenotype, or universality.  Any such interpretation would require a separate layer-matched measurement and its own controlled statistic.  The present chapter licenses only the bounded statement that, on the recorded finite joins and under the stated controls, measured tRNA gene-copy tAI accounts for the listed fractions of the modeled-complement residual component.
