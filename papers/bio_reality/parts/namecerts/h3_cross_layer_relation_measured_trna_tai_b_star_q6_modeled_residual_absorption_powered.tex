\subsection{Measured tRNA-copy tAI as a bounded absorber of the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ modeled-complement residual}
\label{sec:bior reality-q6-measured-trna-tai-absorber}

\paragraph{Finite contact.}
The readout tested here is not ribosome occupancy, protein abundance, folding, function, or phenotype.  It is a finite per-CDS projection test asking whether a tRNA-gene-copy version of tAI absorbs any of the modeled-tAI-complement abundance residual already carried by the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ quotient coordinates.  The coordinate panel has nine named residual directions,
$K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $Leu_{CUN\,vs\,UUR}$, $Leu_{UUA\,vs\,UUG}$, $Ser_{UCR\,vs\,AGY}$, $Ser_{UCA\,vs\,UCG}$, $Thr_{ACR\,vs\,ACY}$, and $f3_{stress}$.
The control surface contains an intercept, $\log(\mathrm{cds\_len\_nt})$, twenty amino-acid composition proportions computed from real CDS codon counts, GC3, and an $M$-density baseline defined on the union support of the nine projected $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ vectors.  In each organism the reported control-column count is $24$ and the realized control rank is $23$.

\paragraph{Rows and tRNA-copy surface.}
The organism panel is finite and fully joined for the three tRNA-copy organisms.  Danio rerio contributes $14410$ joined CDS rows, $8846$ parsed tRNA genes, an anticodon GCN table of size $57$, and $8607$ usable measured-tAI records.  Homo sapiens contributes $19053$ joined CDS rows, $431$ parsed tRNA genes, an anticodon table of size $47$, and $422$ usable measured-tAI records.  Saccharomyces cerevisiae contributes $5815$ joined CDS rows, $273$ parsed tRNA genes, an anticodon table of size $41$, and $268$ usable measured-tAI records.  No zero raw $W$ value required geometric-mean filling in any of the three organisms.  Header exclusions were organism-specific: Danio rerio excluded $3$ selenocysteine, $25$ suppressor, and $5$ undetermined-anticodon records; Homo sapiens excluded $1$ selenocysteine record; Saccharomyces cerevisiae excluded $2$ undetermined-anticodon records.

\paragraph{Projection statistic.}
For each organism, the target quantity is the residual fraction $d_{modeled}$ left after removing the modeled tAI component, and the measured-copy tAI test reports how much of that modeled-complement residual is descriptively absorbed.  The finite values are
$$
\begin{aligned}
 d_{modeled}(\mathrm{danio}) &= 0.874628721049266, & a_{tRNA}(\mathrm{danio}) &= 0.020817102845637092, & d_{combined}(\mathrm{danio}) &= 0.835657382343872,\\
 d_{modeled}(\mathrm{human}) &= 0.9208949355679455, & a_{tRNA}(\mathrm{human}) &= 0.00005426229300865148, & d_{combined}(\mathrm{human}) &= 0.92041342656924,\\
 d_{modeled}(\mathrm{yeast}) &= 0.21173219572706703, & a_{tRNA}(\mathrm{yeast}) &= 0.001530747712165342, & d_{combined}(\mathrm{yeast}) &= 0.20648621019305421.
\end{aligned}
$$
The full-join self-check is exact for the three organisms: the current full-join $d_{modeled}$ equals the complement-experiment reference with absolute difference $0.0$ in Danio rerio, Homo sapiens, and Saccharomyces cerevisiae.  Thus the tRNA-copy tAI absorber is evaluated on the same abundance-CDS join used by the modeled-complement reference, rather than on a changed row set.

\paragraph{Readout interpretation.}
The largest measured-copy tAI absorption is the Danio rerio value $0.020817102845637092$, followed by the Saccharomyces cerevisiae value $0.001530747712165342$ and the Homo sapiens value $0.00005426229300865148$.  In the terms of this contact, the Danio rerio row says that measured tRNA gene-copy tAI removes about two percent of the modeled-complement residual fraction; the yeast row removes about fifteen hundredths of a percent; the human row is effectively negligible at this scale.  Because the copy-number surface is a proxy for tRNA abundance and because the tAI wobble coefficients are a modeling choice inherited from the dos Reis construction, these numbers are descriptive projections, not a causal mediation test.

\paragraph{Comparison with measured translation-efficiency availability.}
A separate measured-TE calculation gives a useful boundary check.  On the all-readout TE join, Danio rerio has $13140$ rows and measured-TE absorption $0.01759429001506974$ with $d_{combined}=0.8276090159036916$; Escherichia coli K12 MG1655 has $3415$ rows and absorption $0.0010982956489726373$ with $d_{combined}=0.6620871850159149$; Homo sapiens has $11554$ rows and absorption $0.0028947149257963897$ with $d_{combined}=0.854133502300174$; Saccharomyces cerevisiae has $5128$ rows and absorption $0.0002762783754375593$ with $d_{combined}=0.2237928123367028$.  The measured-TE join changes the row set for Danio rerio and Homo sapiens relative to the full abundance-CDS reference: the absolute $d_{modeled}$ differences are $0.03219747984141397$ and $0.06427590857052146$, respectively, while Escherichia coli and yeast stay within $0.02$ with differences $0.00017488612263594217$ and $0.012133464712697745$.  This comparison keeps the tRNA-copy result from being read as a direct substitute for measured ribosome profiling: the tRNA-copy absorber is row-stable on the three-organism copy-number join, whereas the TE contact has its own organism-specific availability boundary.

\paragraph{Cross-organism caution.}
The broader organism-specific summary remains exploratory.  A twelve-organism meta table reports $M_{QTP}$ fraction tests against GC3, proteome size, tRNA gene count, and domain.  The continuous tests have permutation two-sided $p$-values $0.35264735264735264$ for GC3 with $r=-0.3062616500469447$, $0.913086913086913$ for log proteome size with $r=-0.033628115789504955$, and $0.8621378621378621$ for log tRNA gene count with $r=0.06029405206037823$.  The domain comparison has variance ratio $0.8889980452277374$ and right-tail permutation $p=0.4155844155844156$, with group means $0.01850668564589326$ for archaea, $0.4351192457799102$ for bacteria, and $0.3960177769015294$ for eukaryotes.  The dominant-coordinate contingency has $n=12$, chi-square $9.399999999999999$, Cramer's $V=0.6258327785172862$, and right-tail permutation $p=0.5814185814185814$.  These values do not support a resolved phylogenetic or domain law; they only show that the finite panel was assembled and tested under deterministic permutation nulls.

\paragraph{Local verdict.}
The measured tRNA-copy tAI contact is therefore a bounded positive absorber for one organism and a weak or negligible absorber for the other two organisms in this finite row surface.  The strongest row is Danio rerio, but even there the absorbed fraction is small relative to the remaining modeled-complement residual.  The correct statement is that tRNA gene-copy tAI can be projected against the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ modeled-complement abundance residual on a rank-$23$ controlled finite panel, with exact join agreement to the complement reference in all three organisms.  The result does not establish observed translation, ribosome occupancy, protein abundance, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, mechanism, phenotype, or universality.  Any such claim would require a separate layer-matched contact and its own finite statistic.
