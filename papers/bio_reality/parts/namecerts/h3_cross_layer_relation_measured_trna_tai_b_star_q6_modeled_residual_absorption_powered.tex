\subsection{Measured tRNA-copy tAI against the modeled-tAI-complement residual}
\label{sec:bior-gcn-tai-q6-modeled-residual-absorption}

\paragraph{Finite surface.}
This record tests whether organism-specific tRNA gene-copy adaptation scores absorb the residual abundance component left after the modeled-tAI projection has already been removed from the $B^*_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates.  The carrier is a finite coding-sequence readout surface: rows are coding sequences joined to organism-specific codon counts and tRNA gene-copy tables, columns include the nine residual coordinates
$K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $Leu_{CUN\_vs\_UUR}$, $Leu_{UUA\_vs\_UUG}$, $Ser_{UCR\_vs\_AGY}$, $Ser_{UCA\_vs\_UCG}$, $Thr_{ACR\_vs\_ACY}$, and $f3_{stress}$, and the control block is the rank-checked matrix $Z$ consisting of an intercept, $\log(\mathrm{cds\_len\_nt})$, the twenty amino-acid composition proportions $A,C,D,E,F,G,H,I,K,L,M,N,P,Q,R,S,T,V,W,Y$, $GC3$, and the $M$-density baseline defined by the fraction of sense codons lying on the union support of the nine projected $B^*_{Q6}$ vectors.  In the run $a1fcdda313fd$, the declared coordinate count is $9$, the control-column count is $24$ in each organism, and the realized control rank is $23$.

\paragraph{Joined organism rows.}
The tRNA-copy contact is available for three organisms.  For \textit{Danio rerio}, the joined coding-sequence count is $14410$, the tRNA gene count is $8846$, and the parsed anticodon gene-copy table has $57$ anticodon entries. \paragraph{Modeled-complement check.}
The first numerical guard is that the modeled-tAI-complement residual is evaluated on the same full coding-sequence join as the reference complement experiment.  This check is exact in all three organisms in the present tRNA-copy run:
$$
\begin{aligned}
 d_{\mathrm{modeled}}(\textit{D. rerio}) &= 0.874628721049266,\\
 d_{\mathrm{modeled}}(\textit{H. sapiens}) &= 0.9208949355679455,\\
 d_{\mathrm{modeled}}(\textit{S. cerevisiae}) &= 0.21173219572706703.
\end{aligned}
$$
The absolute difference between the current full-join value and the complement-reference value is $0.0$ for each organism, with reference rank $23$ in all cases.  Thus the absorption calculation is not being driven by a changed coding-sequence join in this contact.

\paragraph{Absorption values.}
The measured tRNA-copy tAI contact absorbs only a small part of the modeled-tAI-complement abundance component.  The computed absorbed fractions are
$$
\begin{aligned}
 \alpha_{\mathrm{tRNA}}(\textit{D. rerio}) &= 0.020817102845637092,\\
 \alpha_{\mathrm{tRNA}}(\textit{H. sapiens}) &= 0.00005426229300865148,\\
 \alpha_{\mathrm{tRNA}}(\textit{S. cerevisiae}) &= 0.001530747712165342.
\end{aligned}
$$
The corresponding combined residual values are
$$
\begin{aligned}
 d_{\mathrm{combined}}(\textit{D. rerio}) &= 0.835657382343872,\\
 d_{\mathrm{combined}}(\textit{H. sapiens}) &= 0.92041342656924,\\
 d_{\mathrm{combined}}(\textit{S. cerevisiae}) &= 0.20648621019305421.
\end{aligned}
$$
The largest observed absorption is therefore approximately $2.08\%$ in \textit{D. rerio}; human is essentially unchanged at approximately $0.0054\%$ absorption; yeast is approximately $0.153\%$.  The finite-row conclusion is descriptive: gene-copy tAI does not collapse the modeled-tAI-complement component in these joined panels.

\paragraph{Relation to measured translation-efficiency and turnover contacts.}
The paired measured-translation-efficiency run $bbd448cac2b4$ gives a useful boundary check because its join changes after requiring ribosome-profiling TE rows.  The TE join sizes are $13140$ for \textit{D. rerio}, $3415$ for \textit{E. coli} K-12 MG1655, $11554$ for human, and $5128$ for yeast, again with control rank $23$ and $24$ control columns.  Its measured-TE absorption values are $0.01759429001506974$, $0.0010982956489726373$, $0.0028947149257963897$, and $0.0002762783754375593$, respectively.  The complement-join self-check fails the $0.02$ tolerance for \textit{D. rerio} and human because the measured-TE availability changes the joined protein-id surface, while it passes for \textit{E. coli} and yeast.  The yeast turnover run $c48bd41e6202$ supplies a separate downstream comparison on $3640$ joined yeast proteins, with a turnover absorbed fraction $0.0009086031889966327$ and combined residual $0.22306503721327192$.  These neighboring contacts show that the present tRNA-copy calculation should be read as one bounded projection among several layer-specific projections, not as a general explanation of the residual.

\paragraph{Verdict.}
On the finite rows available here, the measured tRNA gene-copy tAI contact is rank-admissible and exactly matched to the modeled-complement reference join for the three organisms tested.  It produces positive but small descriptive absorption in all three organisms.  The result supports the narrower statement that tRNA-copy tAI can be evaluated as an organism-level readout against the modeled-tAI-complement residual and that, in this run, it leaves most of that residual intact.  It does not support a causal mediator interpretation.  It also does not establish observed ribosome occupancy, translation-efficiency survival, protein abundance, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, mechanism, phenotype, or universality.  Any one of those stronger claims would require a separate layer-matched contact and its own controlled statistic on joined rows.
