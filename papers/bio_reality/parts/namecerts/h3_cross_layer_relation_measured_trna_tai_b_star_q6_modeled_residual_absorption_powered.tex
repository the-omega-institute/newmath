\subsection{Measured tRNA-copy tAI absorption of the modeled residual component}\label{subsec:measured-trna-tai-q6-modeled-residual-absorption}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite readout surface.}
This record tests a bounded cross-layer question: whether a measured tRNA gene-copy tAI readout absorbs any part of the $B^*_{Q6}$ abundance component that remains after the modeled-tAI projection has been removed. The finite carrier is the organism-indexed CDS surface whose rows are protein-matched coding sequences, whose residual coordinates are the nine projected $B^*_{Q6}$ directions
$K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $Leu_{CUN\,vs\,UUR}$, $Leu_{UUA\,vs\,UUG}$, $Ser_{UCR\,vs\,AGY}$, $Ser_{UCA\,vs\,UCG}$, $Thr_{ACR\,vs\,ACY}$, and $f3_{stress}$, and whose readout is a gene-copy tAI value computed from organism-specific tRNA anticodon tables. The controls are the intercept, $\log(\mathrm{CDS\ length})$, the twenty amino-acid composition proportions from real CDS codon counts, GC3, and the $M$-density baseline defined on the union support of the nine projected $B^*_{Q6}$ vectors. In each organism the control matrix has $24$ columns and rank $23$, so the computation is not the rank-degenerate three-row refusal case.

\paragraph{Joined rows and tRNA-copy tables.}
The three organism panels are full joins against the complement reference used by the modeled residual calculation. For \emph{Danio rerio}, $n=14410$ CDS rows are joined, with $8846$ parsed tRNA genes and $57$ anticodon gene-copy entries. For \emph{Homo sapiens}, $n=19053$ CDS rows are joined, with $431$ parsed tRNA genes and $47$ anticodon entries. For \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}, $n=5815$ CDS rows are joined, with $273$ parsed tRNA genes and $41$ anticodon entries. The usable measured-tAI record counts are $8607$, $422$, and $268$, respectively, and no zero raw $W$ value is replaced by a geometric-mean fill in any of the three organisms. The parser excludes nonstandard headers locally: in zebrafish, $3$ selenocysteine, $25$ suppressor, and $5$ undetermined-anticodon headers are removed; in human, $1$ selenocysteine header is removed; in yeast, $2$ undetermined-anticodon headers are removed.

\paragraph{Complement stability.}
The residual target is stable under this tRNA-copy readout join. The modeled-complement distance $d_{modeled}$ is exactly reproduced relative to the abundance/CDS complement reference in all three organisms:
$$
\begin{aligned}
 d_{modeled}^{Drerio} &= 0.874628721049266,\\
 d_{modeled}^{Hsapiens} &= 0.9208949355679455,\\
 d_{modeled}^{Scerevisiae} &= 0.21173219572706703.
\end{aligned}
$$
The absolute difference between the current full join and the complement-reference join is $0.0$ for all three organisms. Thus the tRNA-copy test is not changing the row universe of the modeled residual component; it is evaluating an added readout against the same finite residual target.

\paragraph{Absorption readback.}
The measured tRNA gene-copy tAI absorbs only a small fraction of the modeled-tAI-complement component. The absorbed fractions are
$$
\begin{aligned}
 \mathrm{Abs}_{tRNA}^{Drerio} &= 0.020817102845637092,\\
 \mathrm{Abs}_{tRNA}^{Hsapiens} &= 0.00005426229300865148,\\
 \mathrm{Abs}_{tRNA}^{Scerevisiae} &= 0.001530747712165342.
\end{aligned}
$$
The corresponding combined residual distances are
$$
\begin{aligned}
 d_{combined}^{Drerio} &= 0.835657382343872,\\
 d_{combined}^{Hsapiens} &= 0.92041342656924,\\
 d_{combined}^{Scerevisiae} &= 0.20648621019305421.
\end{aligned}
$$
The strongest measured tRNA-copy absorption occurs in zebrafish, at about $2.08\%$ of the modeled-complement component. Human is essentially unchanged, at about $5.43\times 10^{-5}$, and yeast is also low, at about $1.53\times 10^{-3}$. The biological reading is therefore narrow: measured tRNA gene copy number can be used as a bounded codon-adaptation readout, but it does not, in this computation, explain most of the residual component left after modeled tAI.

\paragraph{Relation to measured translation efficiency.}
A separate measured-translation-efficiency computation tests ribosome-profiling TE as another candidate readout. Its joined TE rows are $13140$ for zebrafish, $3415$ for \emph{E. coli} K12 MG1655, $11554$ for human, and $5128$ for yeast, again with $24$ control columns and rank $23$. In that contact the measured-TE absorbed fractions are $0.01759429001506974$, $0.0010982956489726373$, $0.0028947149257963897$, and $0.0002762783754375593$, respectively. Those TE values are reported only as a comparison of bounded readout strength. They do not turn the tRNA-copy calculation into a ribosome-occupancy claim, because the tRNA-copy calculation uses gene-copy tAI and dos Reis-style wobble modeling rather than direct ribosome footprints.

\paragraph{Cross-organism context.}
The broader organism-specific screen remains exploratory. In a twelve-organism meta table, the dominant-coordinate-versus-domain contingency has Pearson $\chi^2=9.399999999999999$, Cramer's $V=0.6258327785172862$, and permutation right-tail $p=0.5814185814185814$ over $1000$ trials. Continuous comparisons of $M_{QTP}$ fraction give $r=-0.3062616500469447$ versus GC3 with two-sided permutation $p=0.35264735264735264$, $r=-0.033628115789504955$ versus log proteome size with $p=0.913086913086913$, and $r=0.06029405206037823$ versus log tRNA gene count with $p=0.8621378621378621$. The domain variance-ratio test has statistic $0.8889980452277374$ and right-tail permutation $p=0.4155844155844156$. These finite numbers support heterogeneity rather than a resolved domain law.

\paragraph{Local interpretation boundary.}
This chapter licenses only a descriptive projection statement: measured tRNA gene-copy tAI has been computed on full joined organism panels and has low absorption of the modeled-tAI-complement $B^*_{Q6}$ abundance component. It does not establish a causal mechanism, direct tRNA abundance, ribosome occupancy, translation-efficiency survival, protein abundance, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, phenotype, or universality. A claim at any one of those layers requires a separate layer-matched contact and its own controlled statistic. In particular, the mRNA-controlled ribosome-profiling TE survival question remains a distinct contact: matched mRNA abundance may be used as a control for TE residualization, but transcript abundance by itself is not a translation readout, and tRNA gene-copy tAI is not a substitute for same-CDS ribosome-profiling rows.
