\subsection{Measured tRNA-gene-copy tAI as a bounded absorber of the $B^*_{Q6}$ modeled residual}
\label{sec:biorreality-q6-measured-trna-tai-modeled-residual-absorption}

\paragraph{Finite readout surface.}
This record tests whether a tRNA-gene-copy-derived tAI surface absorbs the modeled-tAI-complement component of the $B^*_{Q6}$ residual signal. The coordinate side is the same nine-dimensional quotient residual basis used throughout the $Q6$ packet:
$$
\begin{aligned}
Q=\{&K\_AAA,\ Arg\_AGR,\ Ile\_AUA,\ Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR,\ Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG,\\
&Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY,\ Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG,\ Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY,\ f3\_stress\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
For each organism, CDS codon counts are projected against controls consisting of an intercept, $\log(\mathrm{cds\_len\_nt})$, the twenty amino-acid composition proportions $A,C,D,E,F,G,H,I,K,L,M,N,P,Q,R,S,T,V,W,Y$, GC3, and the $M$-density baseline defined on the union support of the nine projected $B^*_{Q6}$ vectors. The resulting control design has $24$ columns and rank $23$ in each organism used here. The tested readout is not direct tRNA-seq abundance and not ribosome occupancy; it is a gene-copy tAI construction from parsed genomic tRNA records with dos Reis-style wobble weights.

\paragraph{Joined rows and rank condition.}
The full-CDS joins are rank-powered for the three organisms in the measured-tRNA contact. In \emph{Danio rerio}, $n=14410$ CDS rows are joined, the parsed tRNA table has $8846$ tRNA genes and $57$ anticodon-GCN entries, and $8607$ measured-tAI records are usable. In \emph{Homo sapiens}, $n=19053$ CDS rows are joined, the parsed tRNA table has $431$ tRNA genes and $47$ anticodon-GCN entries, and $422$ measured-tAI records are usable. In \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}, $n=5815$ CDS rows are joined, the parsed tRNA table has $273$ tRNA genes and $41$ anticodon-GCN entries, and $268$ measured-tAI records are usable. No zero raw $W$ value required geometric-mean filling in any of the three organisms. The header exclusions are organism-specific: zebrafish excludes $3$ selenocysteine, $25$ suppressor, and $5$ undetermined-anticodon records; human excludes $1$ selenocysteine record; yeast excludes $2$ undetermined-anticodon records.

\paragraph{Full-join stability check.}
The modeled residual target is stable under this contact because the current full join exactly matches the complement-experiment reference join in all three organisms. The modeled-complement distances are
$$
\begin{aligned}
d_{\mathrm{model}}(\mathrm{Danio}) &= 0.874628721049266,\\
d_{\mathrm{model}}(\mathrm{human}) &= 0.9208949355679455,\\
d_{\mathrm{model}}(\mathrm{yeast}) &= 0.21173219572706703.
\end{aligned}
$$
For each of these, the absolute difference from the reference complement experiment is $0.0$, with current join sizes $14410$, $19053$, and $5815$ respectively. This matters because an absorption calculation on a shifted join would test a different residual target. Here the tRNA-gene-copy contact preserves the full modeled-complement surface before the additional readout is projected.

\paragraph{Absorption result.}
Let $d_{Q\perp T_{\mathrm{mod}}}$ denote the modeled-tAI-complement residual distance before adding the measured tRNA-gene-copy tAI readout, and let $d_{\mathrm{combined}}$ denote the distance after the tRNA-derived tAI projection is included. The descriptive absorbed fractions are
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{absorb}_{\mathrm{tRNA}}(\mathrm{Danio}) &= 0.020817102845637092, & d_{\mathrm{combined}} &= 0.835657382343872,\\
\mathrm{absorb}_{\mathrm{tRNA}}(\mathrm{human}) &= 0.00005426229300865148, & d_{\mathrm{combined}} &= 0.92041342656924,\\
\mathrm{absorb}_{\mathrm{tRNA}}(\mathrm{yeast}) &= 0.001530747712165342, & d_{\mathrm{combined}} &= 0.20648621019305421.
\end{aligned}
$$
The largest measured-tRNA absorption occurs in zebrafish, where the tRNA-derived projection accounts for about $2.08\%$ of the modeled-complement component. Yeast has a small but nonzero descriptive absorption, about $0.153\%$. Human is essentially unchanged at this readout, with absorption near $5.43\times 10^{-5}$. Thus the finite table does not support a uniform cross-organism collapse of the $B^*_{Q6}$ modeled residual into tRNA gene-copy tAI. It supports a bounded readout: the tRNA-gene-copy tAI surface can absorb a small fraction of the residual in some organisms, while most of the modeled-complement residual remains outside this readout.

\paragraph{Relation to measured translation efficiency.}
A separate ribosome-profiling translation-efficiency calculation with matched mRNA abundance controls gives a different contact boundary. On the all-readout TE join, the row counts are $13140$ for zebrafish, $3415$ for \emph{E. coli} K-12 MG1655, $11554$ for human, and $5128$ for yeast, each again using $24$ control columns with rank $23$. The measured-TE absorption values are $0.01759429001506974$ for zebrafish, $0.0010982956489726373$ for \emph{E. coli}, $0.0028947149257963897$ for human, and $0.0002762783754375593$ for yeast. Those entries show that measured TE is a distinct bounded readout with its own join and mRNA-control condition. They do not turn the tRNA-gene-copy tAI result into observed ribosome motion, and the tRNA result does not replace the TE-plus-mRNA calculation.

\paragraph{Local interpretation.}
The finite result is a descriptive projection statement. It says that, after amino-acid composition, CDS length, GC3, and $M$-density controls, a tRNA-gene-copy tAI coordinate surface absorbs only a small part of the modeled-tAI-complement $B^*_{Q6}$ abundance residual: strongest in zebrafish, weak in yeast, and nearly absent in human. The computation is therefore compatible with a residual component that remains open after this tRNA proxy is supplied. It is not evidence for a causal mediator, because tRNA gene copy number is only a proxy for tRNA abundance and the wobble coefficients are a modeling choice. It is also not a protein-abundance result, not a folding result, not a physical-admissibility assay, not a function assay, not a mechanism proof, and not a universal biological law. Any such stronger statement would require a separate layer-matched contact and its own controlled finite-row statistic.
