\subsection{Measured tRNA-copy tAI absorption of the $B^*_{Q6}$ modeled-complement residual}
\label{sec:bioreality-measured-trna-tai-q6-absorption}

\paragraph{Question.}
This record asks whether an empirically instantiated tRNA-copy tAI surface removes any appreciable part of the $B^*_{Q6}$ abundance component that remains after the modeled-tAI direction has already been removed. The tested object is not a protein phenotype, a folding state, or a causal mechanism. It is a finite row-level projection problem: for each coding sequence in an organism panel, a nine-coordinate $B^*_{Q6}$ residual basis is formed, a modeled-tAI abundance component is separated, and a tRNA-gene-copy tAI readout is then tested against the remaining component $\widehat P_{Q\perp T_{\mathrm{mod}}}$.

\paragraph{Finite carrier.}
The coordinate family has nine entries:
$K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $Leu_{CUN\,vs\,UUR}$, $Leu_{UUA\,vs\,UUG}$, $Ser_{UCR\,vs\,AGY}$, $Ser_{UCA\,vs\,UCG}$, $Thr_{ACR\,vs\,ACY}$, and $f3_{stress}$.  The control surface contains an intercept, $\log(\mathrm{cds\_len\_nt})$, twenty amino-acid composition proportions computed from CDS codon counts, GC3, and the $M$-density baseline given by the fraction of sense codons on the union support of the nine projected $B^*_{Q6}$ vectors.  Each organism has $24$ control columns and control rank $23$.

\paragraph{Joined row counts.}
The tRNA-copy tAI computation used the full abundance-CDS join for the three organisms where the measured tRNA-copy table was attached.  The finite joins were $14410$ CDS rows for \emph{Danio rerio}, $19053$ for \emph{Homo sapiens}, and $5815$ for \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}.  Their tRNA inventories had respectively $8846$, $431$, and $273$ parsed tRNA genes, with anticodon GCN table sizes $57$, $47$, and $41$.  Usable measured-tAI record counts were $8607$, $422$, and $268$, and no zero raw $W$ value required geometric-mean filling in any of the three organisms.

\paragraph{Reference modeled-complement scale.}
The modeled-complement residual fraction was exactly preserved relative to the complement reference on these full joins.  The reported values were
$$
\begin{aligned}
 d_{\mathrm{modeled}}(\mathrm{Danio}) &= 0.874628721049266,\\
 d_{\mathrm{modeled}}(\mathrm{human}) &= 0.9208949355679455,\\
 d_{\mathrm{modeled}}(\mathrm{yeast}) &= 0.21173219572706703.
\end{aligned}
$$
For all three organisms the absolute difference between the current full join and the complement reference was $0.0$, with matching reference rank $23$.  This matters because the tRNA-copy test is not being performed on a sparse or shifted feature join; the residual target is the same full-join target used for the modeled-complement comparison.

\paragraph{Absorption readout.}
The tRNA-copy tAI readout absorbed only a small fraction of $\widehat P_{Q\perp T_{\mathrm{mod}}}$.  The measured absorption values were
$$
\begin{aligned}
 A_{\mathrm{tRNA}}(\mathrm{Danio}) &= 0.020817102845637092,\\
 A_{\mathrm{tRNA}}(\mathrm{human}) &= 0.00005426229300865148,\\
 A_{\mathrm{tRNA}}(\mathrm{yeast}) &= 0.001530747712165342.
\end{aligned}
$$
The corresponding combined residual fractions were
$$
\begin{aligned}
 d_{\mathrm{combined}}(\mathrm{Danio}) &= 0.835657382343872,\\
 d_{\mathrm{combined}}(\mathrm{human}) &= 0.92041342656924,\\
 d_{\mathrm{combined}}(\mathrm{yeast}) &= 0.20648621019305421.
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus, the largest finite-row effect in this packet is the \emph{Danio} value, where measured tRNA-copy tAI removes about $2.08\%$ of the modeled-complement component.  Human and yeast are much smaller, at about $0.0054\%$ and $0.153\%$ respectively.  The readout is therefore a bounded absorption result, not a broad explanation of the $B^*_{Q6}$ residual.

\paragraph{Relation to measured translation efficiency.}
A separate measured-TE projection used ribosome-profiling translation efficiency with matched mRNA abundance controls on four organism joins.  Its finite rows were $13140$ for \emph{Danio rerio}, $3415$ for \emph{Escherichia coli} K-12 MG1655, $11554$ for human, and $5128$ for yeast, again with $24$ control columns and rank $23$.  The measured-TE absorption values were $0.01759429001506974$ for \emph{Danio}, $0.0010982956489726373$ for \emph{E. coli}, $0.0028947149257963897$ for human, and $0.0002762783754375593$ for yeast.  These numbers are close in scale to the tRNA-copy absorption result: finite, computable, and mostly small.  The TE contact also shows that join availability matters: the modeled-complement self-check matched within $0.02$ for \emph{E. coli} and yeast, but not for \emph{Danio} or human when the measured-TE availability restricted the join.

\paragraph{Cross-organism context.}
A broader organism-specific survey assembled $16$ organism rows, of which $12$ had complete feature rows for the $M^{QTP}$ meta tests.  The domain comparison of $M^{QTP}$ fraction had group means $0.01850668564589326$ for archaea, $0.4351192457799102$ for bacteria, and $0.3960177769015294$ for eukaryotes, but the permutation right-tail value was $0.4155844155844156$ over $1000$ trials.  Continuous tests were also weak: $M^{QTP}$ fraction versus GC3 had $r=-0.3062616500469447$ with two-sided permutation value $0.35264735264735264$; versus log tRNA gene count had $r=0.06029405206037823$ with value $0.8621378621378621$; and versus log proteome size had $r=-0.033628115789504955$ with value $0.913086913086913$.  Dominant-coordinate contingency by domain gave $\chi^2=9.399999999999999$, Cramer's $V=0.6258327785172862$, and permutation right-tail value $0.5814185814185814$ on $12$ complete rows.  These meta-results support heterogeneity of the finite readbacks, not a universal codon-translation law.

\paragraph{Interpretation.}
The supported conclusion is narrow.  Measured tRNA gene-copy tAI can be attached to the $B^*_{Q6}$ modeled-complement residual on full organism joins, and the resulting projection quantities are finite, rank-checked, and reproducible from the recorded rows.  The strongest absorption is small and organism-specific.  In this packet, tRNA-copy tAI behaves as a bounded explanatory covariate for a small part of the modeled-complement residual, while most of the residual remains outside this measured-tRNA proxy.

\paragraph{Local scope limit.}
No translation mechanism, ribosome-occupancy realization, protein abundance, folding state, physical admissibility, molecular function, phenotype, causal claim, or universal biological rule follows from these projection values.  tRNA gene copy number is a proxy for tRNA abundance rather than direct tRNA-seq, and the tAI wobble parameters are a modeling choice.  A stronger translation claim would require a separate measured translation contact, matched mRNA controls, and a declared survival statistic on joined rows.  A protein, structure, function, phenotype, or mechanism claim would require its own layer-matched measurement and its own finite statistic.
