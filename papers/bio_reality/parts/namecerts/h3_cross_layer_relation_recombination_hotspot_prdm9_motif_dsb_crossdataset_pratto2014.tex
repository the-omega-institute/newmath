\paragraph{Local null model.}
The null condition is row exchangeability of high-coordinate and low-coordinate synonymous residual labels after the mandated controls. In local notation, let $q$ denote the residual-coordinate label, $T$ the same-row translation readout, $O$ the measured decay-factor RBP occupancy state, $P$ the perturbation or rescue condition, and $H$ the controlled RNA half-life residual. Let $Z$ collect transcription rate, transcript abundance, local RNA structure, motif density, GC and dinucleotide composition, codon-pair context, splice or isoform state, amino-acid composition, batch, and simpler baseline terms. The bridge is not supported by a marginal shift in $H$ alone. It requires that the coordinate-associated half-life contrast remain after $T$ and $Z$, follow measured $O$ under coordinate--occupancy decoupling, disappear under target-RBP loss, return under wild-type rescue, and fail to return under binding-defective or non-target controls.

\paragraph{Support criterion.}
A positive finite-row claim is licensed only if the declared statistic separates the following cases on the same matched panel. First, the high-coordinate and low-coordinate labels must differ in controlled RNA half-life residuals after $T$ and $Z$. Second, the same residual contrast must track measured local occupancy of the prespecified decay-factor RBP, rather than merely the coordinate label. Third, removing the target RBP must collapse the effect. Fourth, restoring wild-type RBP must recover the effect. Fifth, a binding-defective rescue or a non-target control must not recover the effect. Written schematically, the desired pattern is
$$
\begin{aligned}
  H &\not\perp q \mid T,Z, \\
  H &\text{ follows } O \text{ after coordinate--occupancy decoupling}, \\
  \Delta H_{\mathrm{target\ loss}} &\approx 0, \\
  \Delta H_{\mathrm{wildtype\ rescue}} &\text{ returns}, \\
  \Delta H_{\mathrm{binding\ defective}} &\approx 0.
\end{aligned}
$$
This display is a local description of the admissible finite statistic, not a universal biological law.

\paragraph{No-promotion boundary.}
The present record therefore remains bounded to a finite test design and its recorded finite-run scalars. No translation realization, protein abundance, folded structure, physical admissibility, molecular function, system phenotype, RNA-decay mechanism, adaptation, causation, or universal rule follows from the $21636$ and $21512$ row counts, from the runtime, from a half-life contact name, from a ribosome-profiling route, from a matched mRNA control, or from $B^*_{Q6}$ coordinate geometry. A half-life difference without measured RBP occupancy is only an RNA-stability association. An occupancy association without coordinate--occupancy decoupling is not an RBP-mediated coordinate bridge. A target-RBP-loss effect without wild-type rescue and binding-defective or non-target controls is not a licensed mechanism claim. The admissible conclusion is narrower: this chapter specifies the finite row shape and the failure conditions required before a translation-bounded, RBP-conditioned RNA half-life bridge may be asserted.
