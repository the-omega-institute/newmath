\paragraph{Licensed mathematical form.}
Let $W$ be the finite set of editable coding RNA windows retained after identifier matching.  Each row $w\in W$ may carry a tuple
$$
\begin{aligned}
  x(w) = (q(w),T(w),H(w),O(w),P(w),Z(w)),
\end{aligned}
$$
where $q(w)$ is the residual-coordinate label, $T(w)$ is the same-row translation readout, $H(w)$ is the controlled RNA half-life residual, $O(w)$ is measured local occupancy by the prespecified decay-factor RBP, $P(w)$ is the perturbation or rescue condition, and $Z(w)$ is the finite control vector.  The control vector includes transcription rate or abundance controls, local RNA structure, motif density, GC and dinucleotide composition, codon-pair context, splice or isoform state, batch, amino-acid matching, and the translation readout itself.  The admissible positive statement is therefore conditional rather than global: a coordinate-associated half-life difference may be read as a bounded cross-layer bridge only if it survives the matched controls, tracks measured RBP occupancy after coordinate and occupancy are experimentally separated, collapses under target-RBP loss, returns under wild-type rescue, and fails to return under binding-defective or non-target controls.

\paragraph{Matched-null statistic.}
The finite null treats high-coordinate and low-coordinate synonymous residual labels as exchangeable once the amino acid, transcript context, and same-row translation boundary have been fixed.  A compact way to state the tested contrast is
$$
\begin{aligned}
  \Delta_H
  &= E\{H(w)\mid q(w)=\mathrm{high},Z(w)\}
     - E\{H(w)\mid q(w)=\mathrm{low},Z(w)\},\\
  \Delta_{H\mid O,P}
  &= E\{H(w)\mid q(w),O(w),P(w),Z(w)\}
     - E\{H(w)\mid O(w),P(w),Z(w)\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
A finite association in $\Delta_H$ is not enough.  The record is promoted only when the occupancy and perturbation coordinates explain the half-life contrast in the direction required by the prespecified RBP-decay model.  In particular, an RNA half-life difference without measured decay-factor RBP occupancy remains an RNA-stability association.  An occupancy association without coordinate/occupancy decoupling remains a binding correlation.  A target-RBP loss effect without wild-type rescue and binding-defective or non-target controls remains an incomplete perturbation pattern.

\paragraph{Local no-promotion rule.}
No translation realization follows from RNA half-life alone.  No protein abundance, folded structure, physical admissibility, molecular function, phenotype, adaptation, causal mechanism, or universal biological law follows from the residual-coordinate geometry or from the existence of the row-matching computation.  Ribosome profiling, mRNA abundance, and RNA half-life contacts are separate readout surfaces; none of them substitutes for same-window decay-factor RBP occupancy or for target-RBP perturbation and rescue.  The finite claim remains valid only as a bounded conditional form: if a later joined panel supplies same-organism same-window RBP occupancy, coordinate/occupancy decoupling, target-RBP loss, wild-type rescue, binding-defective or non-target controls, and the declared matched statistic, then the same schema can certify a local cross-layer bridge for that panel.  Until those rows exist, the present record is a precise finite boundary around what the RNA half-life data can and cannot say.
