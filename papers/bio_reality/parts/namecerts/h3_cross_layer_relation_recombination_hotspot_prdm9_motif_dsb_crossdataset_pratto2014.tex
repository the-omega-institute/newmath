\subsection{Translation-Conditioned RBP RNA Half-Life Boundary}\label{sec:translation-conditioned-rbp-rna-half-life-boundary}\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite record.} The record considered here is a bounded BioReality certificate for synonymously editable coding RNA windows whose rows carry a $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual-coordinate label, a same-row translation readout, a measured RNA half-life readout, a local decay-factor RBP occupancy state, and matched transcriptional or mRNA controls. The finite audit payload names experiment run $a5e9df27ecd0$, verified at $2026$-$06$-$27$T$08{:}56{:}11+00{:}00$, with two finite row counts reported as $n_a=21636$ and $n_c=21512$. The same audit records a runtime of $187.044$ seconds. These numbers are used only as provenance for the finite checked payload: they do not by themselves supply RBP occupancy, perturbation, rescue, or RNA-decay mechanism data.

\paragraph{Object under test.} A row is eligible only when it joins a coding-window coordinate to a translation readout, an RNA half-life residual, and an RBP-state annotation for the same local window. The intended comparison is not a raw high-versus-low coordinate comparison. It is the controlled contrast between high-coordinate and low-coordinate synonymous residual labels inside amino-acid-matched editable RNA windows, with an additional split by whether the prespecified decay-factor RBP is locally occupied. The local statistic must treat transcription rate, transcript abundance, local RNA structure, motif density, GC and dinucleotide composition, codon-pair context, splice or isoform state, batch, amino-acid composition, and the same-row translation readout as covariates or matching variables. In symbolic form, the positive bridge is not the marginal contrast
$$\begin{aligned}
  B^{\ast}_{Q6} &\longrightarrow H,
\end{aligned}$$
where $H$ denotes RNA half-life. The admissible target is the controlled and experimentally supported relation
$$\begin{aligned}
  B^{\ast}_{Q6} \times O_{\mathrm{RBP}} \times C_{\mathrm{pert}} &\longrightarrow H_{\mathrm{res}},
\end{aligned}$$
where $O_{\mathrm{RBP}}$ is measured local decay-factor occupancy, $C_{\mathrm{pert}}$ distinguishes target-RBP loss, wild-type rescue, binding-defective rescue, and non-target controls, and $H_{\mathrm{res}}$ is the RNA half-life residual after the declared controls.

\paragraph{Licensing condition.} The record licenses a cross-layer RNA-decay bridge only if five finite-row conditions hold together. First, the high-coordinate and low-coordinate synonymous windows must remain amino-acid matched and must be tested inside the same organismal or cellular panel. Second, the RNA half-life contrast must survive the translation-readout boundary: the same row must include a ribosome-occupancy or translation-efficiency measurement, and the half-life statistic must still be present after this readout is included. Third, the apparent coordinate effect must track measured occupancy of the prespecified decay-factor RBP when coordinate and occupancy are experimentally separated. Fourth, target-RBP loss must collapse the half-life effect and wild-type rescue must restore it. Fifth, binding-defective rescue or a non-target control must fail to restore the coordinate-associated half-life behavior. Without all five conditions, the finite table may describe an RNA-stability association, but not an RBP-mediated bridge.

\paragraph{Observed audit content.} The available finite evidence is narrower than the biological bridge being tested. The checked payload supplies the run identifier $a5e9df27ecd0$, the row counts $21636$ and $21512$, and the runtime $187.044$ seconds for the named audit key. It also records that the relevant contact classes include RNA half-life readouts, ribosome-profiling or translation-efficiency readouts, and matched mRNA-abundance controls. These contacts bound the surfaces on which a future statistic could be computed. They do not include, in the present finite record, same-window decay-factor RBP occupancy, coordinate/occupancy decoupling, target-RBP depletion, wild-type rescue, binding-defective rescue, or non-target control rows. Therefore the record is ready as a scoped boundary statement, not as a positive RNA-decay mechanism claim.

\paragraph{Null model.} The local null is exchangeability of high-coordinate and low-coordinate labels for RNA half-life once the declared controls are attached. Equivalently, after residualization against transcription rate, abundance, local structure, motif burden, nucleotide composition, codon-pair context, splice or isoform state, batch, amino-acid composition, and same-row translation readout, the coordinate label should not predict $H_{\mathrm{res}}$ better than the matched null. A concise form is
$$\begin{aligned}
  H_{\mathrm{res}} \perp B^{\ast}_{Q6}\;\mid\; Z, T, O_{\mathrm{RBP}}, C_{\mathrm{pert}},
\end{aligned}$$
where $Z$ denotes the non-translation controls, $T$ is the same-row translation readout, $O_{\mathrm{RBP}}$ is measured occupancy, and $C_{\mathrm{pert}}$ is the perturbation and rescue condition. If the half-life contrast appears before $O_{\mathrm{RBP}}$ and $C_{\mathrm{pert}}$ are attached, the most it can support is a finite RNA half-life association. If it persists without following measured RBP occupancy, or if target-RBP loss and wild-type rescue do not behave as required, it remains outside the bridge claim.

\paragraph{Cannot-claim boundary.} The chapter does not infer translation, protein abundance, folded structure, physical admissibility, molecular function, phenotype, adaptation, causation, mechanism, or universality from RNA half-life alone. A translation readout is required to keep the RNA half-life statistic from being promoted across layers, but the existence of that readout does not itself prove an RNA-decay mechanism. Likewise, an RNA half-life contact is not an RBP-occupancy assay; a ribosome-profiling contact is not an RNA half-life assay; and an mRNA-abundance contact is only a transcriptional control unless a joined statistic explicitly uses it. The $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ coordinate is treated as an internal finite label for organizing matched rows, not as external biological evidence by itself.

\paragraph{Finite verdict.} With the present data, the BioReality status is a ready boundary certificate for the conjecture $q6.translation\text{-}conditioned.rbp\text{-}rna\text{-}half\text{-}life.cross\text{-}layer$. The finite audit gives concrete provenance and row counts, but the positive bridge remains blocked until a same-organism, same-window panel supplies measured decay-factor RBP occupancy, coordinate/occupancy decoupling, target-RBP loss, wild-type rescue, binding-defective or non-target controls, matched mRNA abundance, same-row translation readout, RNA half-life, and the declared controlled statistic. The admissible conclusion is therefore: the chapter defines the exact finite-row test needed for a bounded translation-conditioned RBP RNA half-life bridge, while the present record does not yet establish that bridge as a biological mechanism.
