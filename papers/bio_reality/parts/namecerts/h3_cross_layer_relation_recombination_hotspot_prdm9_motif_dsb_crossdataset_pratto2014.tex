\subsection{Claim tested by the packet}\label{sec:q6-rbp-rna-half-life-claim}

\paragraph{Permitted statement.} The claim can be stated in bounded form as follows. In a finite matched RNA/CDS window panel, a $B^*_{Q6}$ residual-coordinate RNA-decay relation may be reported only if the RNA half-life residual survives transcription-rate, transcript-abundance, local RNA-structure, motif-density, GC, dinucleotide, codon-pair, splice or isoform, batch, amino-acid, and same-row translation-readout controls; if the effect tracks measured local occupancy of a named decay-factor RBP when coordinate and occupancy are experimentally decoupled; if the effect collapses under loss of the target RBP; if wild-type rescue restores it; and if binding-defective or non-target controls fail to restore it. The relation is finite-panel, finite-RBP, and finite-condition in scope.

\paragraph{Null model.} The local null is exchangeability of high-coordinate and low-coordinate residual labels with respect to controlled RNA half-life once the required covariates and matched readouts have been attached. In symbols, the positive bridge would require a nonzero residual association after the declared controls, followed by intervention behavior that assigns the association to the prespecified decay-factor RBP:
$$
\begin{aligned}
 H_0 &: \operatorname{HalfLifeResidual}(Q_{\mathrm{high}}) \sim \operatorname{HalfLifeResidual}(Q_{\mathrm{low}})\mid Z,\\
 Z &:=(\mathrm{transcription},\mathrm{mRNA},\mathrm{structure},\mathrm{motif},\mathrm{GC},\mathrm{dinucleotide},\mathrm{codon\ pair},\\
 &\qquad \mathrm{splice/isoform},\mathrm{batch},\mathrm{amino\ acid},\mathrm{translation\ readout}).
\end{aligned}
$$
If a half-life difference is present without measured decay-factor RBP occupancy, the result remains an RNA-stability association. If occupancy is present but does not respond to coordinate/occupancy decoupling, the RBP mechanism is not licensed. If target-RBP loss and wild-type rescue are absent, the cross-layer relation is not licensed.

\subsection{Empirical contacts and their limits}\label{sec:q6-rbp-rna-half-life-contacts}

\paragraph{RNA half-life contact.} The half-life contact supplies organism-scoped transcript or gene rows with curated mRNA half-life estimates or normalized consensus half-life readouts. This is a necessary readout surface for the present packet, but it is not sufficient for a positive $B^*_{Q6}$ RBP-decay claim. Platform, growth condition, cell type, isoform ambiguity, gene/CDS mapping loss, normalization, and half-life estimation method can all shift the readout. In the supplied record, this contact is explicitly described as lacking same-window RBP occupancy and perturbation/rescue panels.

\paragraph{Translation and mRNA contacts.} The ribosome-profiling translation-efficiency contact can test bounded translation-layer quantities when ribosome footprint or translation-efficiency rows are joined to the same coding units. The matched mRNA abundance contact provides the transcription-layer denominator or covariate needed to separate translation efficiency from transcript abundance. Neither contact is an RNA half-life measurement, neither is an RBP-occupancy assay, and neither supplies target-RBP perturbation or rescue behavior. Their role in this packet is to prevent promotion from RNA half-life to translation or mechanism unless the same-row controls are actually joined.

\paragraph{Missing RBP intervention surface.} The decisive empirical surface is absent from the supplied finite-row data: same-organism, same-window measured decay-factor RBP occupancy, coordinate/occupancy decoupling, target-RBP loss, wild-type rescue, and binding-defective or non-target controls. Without those rows, the packet can define a testable boundary but cannot report an RBP-mediated RNA half-life bridge. The finite run \texttt{a5e9df27ecd0} therefore supports a scoped audit statement, not a mechanistic conclusion.

\subsection{No-promotion boundary}\label{sec:q6-rbp-rna-half-life-no-promotion}

\paragraph{What the row counts do not prove.} The counts $21636$ and $21512$ show the scale of the finite comparison stored with the packet. They do not show translation realization, protein abundance, protein folding, physical admissibility, biological function, system phenotype, adaptation, causation, or a universal biological law. They also do not select the RBP. A residual-coordinate label is a coordinate label; it becomes biologically probative only through the attached empirical fields and the declared finite statistic.

\paragraph{Allowed conclusion.} The correct conclusion is a conditional boundary statement. The packet specifies the exact kind of panel required to make a bounded RNA-decay bridge claim: matched synonymous editable windows, residual-coordinate labels, same-row translation readout, matched mRNA control, RNA half-life, measured decay-factor RBP occupancy, perturbation, rescue, and a declared matched-null statistic. In the present finite data, the positive bridge is not established because the RBP occupancy and intervention rows are missing. The packet is nevertheless useful because it states where a positive claim would have to enter and which promotions are blocked until that evidence is supplied.
