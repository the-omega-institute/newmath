\paragraph{Finite panel.}
This record concerns a finite matched RNA/CDS window panel whose rows carry synonymously editable coding-window labels, a $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual-coordinate class, a same-row translation readout, a measured RNA half-life readout, a declared local state for a prespecified decay-factor RNA-binding protein, and matched transcription or mRNA controls. It is a bounded cross-layer assertion for rows in which high-coordinate and low-coordinate synonymous residual labels can be compared inside amino-acid-matched editable windows while the local RBP occupancy state and perturbation condition are also read.

\paragraph{Row variables and statistic.}
For each admissible window row $w$, write $q(w)$ for the residual-coordinate label, $T(w)$ for the same-row translation readout, $H(w)$ for the controlled RNA half-life residual, $O(w)$ for measured local occupancy of the named decay-factor RBP, and $P(w)$ for the perturbation or rescue condition. The chapter licenses a positive bridge only if the declared finite statistic separates high-coordinate from low-coordinate labels after the required controls and only along the measured RBP-occupancy route. In schematic form, the tested residual is
$$
\begin{aligned}
H(w) &= \lambda_q q(w)+\lambda_T T(w)+\lambda_O O(w)+\lambda_P P(w)+\lambda_Z Z(w)+\varepsilon(w),
\end{aligned}
$$
where $Z(w)$ includes transcription rate, transcript abundance, local RNA structure, motif density, GC and dinucleotide composition, codon-pair context, splice or isoform state, amino-acid composition, batch, rank sufficiency, and simpler-baseline controls. The present finite data report gives the row counts and runtime above, but it does not provide numerical $\lambda$ estimates, $p$-values, matched-null scores, hit rates, or module classifications for this RBP-half-life statistic. Consequently the chapter is written as a boundary certificate rather than as a positive biological result.

\paragraph{Support rule.}
A positive reading requires four finite-row conditions at once. First, the RNA half-life residual must remain associated with $q(w)$ after the translation readout $T(w)$ and all matched controls are included. Second, the association must track the measured local occupancy $O(w)$ of the prespecified decay-factor RBP when coordinate and occupancy are experimentally decoupled. Third, target-RBP loss must collapse the half-life effect. Fourth, wild-type rescue must restore it, while binding-defective rescue or a non-target control must fail to restore it. In symbols, the intended support pattern is
$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta H_{\mathrm{WT}} &\neq 0,\\
\Delta H_{\mathrm{target\ loss}} &\approx 0,\\
\Delta H_{\mathrm{WT\ rescue}} &\approx \Delta H_{\mathrm{WT}},\\
\Delta H_{\mathrm{binding\ defective}} &\approx 0.
\end{aligned}
$$
The displayed pattern is a gate on interpretation, not a result asserted by the current row-count record. The available contacts name half-life, translation-efficiency, and mRNA-abundance readout surfaces, but they explicitly do not include same-window measured RBP occupancy, coordinate/occupancy decoupling, target-RBP loss, wild-type rescue, or binding-defective controls.

\paragraph{Null interpretation.}
The finite null is exchangeability of high-coordinate and low-coordinate synonymous residual labels with respect to RNA half-life after transcription, abundance, local RNA structure, motif, GC/dinucleotide, codon-pair, splice/isoform, batch, amino-acid, and translation-readout controls. If a half-life difference is found without measured RBP occupancy, the result remains an RNA-stability association. If occupancy changes but target-RBP loss does not collapse the effect, the result remains an occupancy-associated correlation. If loss collapses the effect but wild-type rescue does not restore it, or if a binding-defective or non-target control restores it, the proposed RBP route is not licensed.

\paragraph{Local no-promotion rule.}
This chapter does not infer translation, protein abundance, folded structure, physical admissibility, biological function, phenotype, adaptation, causation, mechanism, or universality from RNA half-life. It also does not treat $B^{*}_{Q6}$ geometry, codon-window coordinates, modeled translation weights, ribosome-profiling contact names, or mRNA-control contact names as biological measurements of an RNA-decay mechanism. A stronger claim would require a separate same-organism, same-window panel containing the residual-coordinate label, translation readout, matched mRNA abundance, RNA half-life, measured decay-factor RBP occupancy, coordinate/occupancy decoupling, target-RBP loss, wild-type rescue, binding-defective or non-target controls, and the declared finite matched-null statistic.
