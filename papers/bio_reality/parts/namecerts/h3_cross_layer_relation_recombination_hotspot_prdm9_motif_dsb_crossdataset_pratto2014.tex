\subsection{Translation-conditioned RNA half-life as a bounded RBP bridge}
\label{subsec:bioreality-q6-rbp-rna-half-life-bridge}

\paragraph{Finite panel.}
The record concerns a finite matched RNA/CDS window panel in which synonymously editable coding windows carry $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual-coordinate labels, a same-row translation readout, an RNA half-life readout, a measured local occupancy state for a prespecified decay-factor RNA-binding protein, and matched transcription and mRNA controls. The intended biological object is not a transcriptome-wide law. It is the finite per-window tuple
$$
\begin{aligned}
  w \longmapsto (&q(w),\ T(w),\ O_R(w),\ E(w),\ H(w),\ Z(w),\ \Delta(w)),
\end{aligned}
$$
where $q(w)$ is the residual-coordinate label, $T(w)$ is the translation readout attached to the same row, $O_R(w)$ is measured occupancy by the chosen decay-factor RBP, $E(w)$ records perturbation or rescue condition, $H(w)$ is the controlled RNA half-life residual, $Z(w)$ is the declared control bundle, and $\Delta(w)$ is the matched-null statistic. The finite audit row used for this chapter is the run $\mathrm{a5e9df27ecd0}$, verified at 2026-06-26T17:54:59+00:00. Its available scalar row counts are $n_a=21636$ and $n_c=21512$, with runtime $187.044$ seconds. These numbers support the existence of a finite audited comparison surface, but they do not by themselves supply RBP occupancy, perturbation, or rescue measurements.

\paragraph{Claim licensed by the row.}
The only positive form admitted here is conditional. A $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual-coordinate RNA-decay readout may be described as a bounded cross-layer bridge only if the half-life contrast remains after transcription-rate, abundance, local RNA-structure, motif-density, GC and dinucleotide composition, codon-pair context, splice or isoform state, batch, amino-acid composition, and same-row translation-readout controls. In addition, the residual must track measured local occupancy of the prespecified decay-factor RBP when coordinate and occupancy are experimentally separated; it must collapse under target-RBP loss; it must return under wild-type rescue; and it must not return under a binding-defective rescue or a non-target control. In symbols, the admissible evidence pattern has the shape
$$
\begin{aligned}
  &H_{\mathrm{res}}(q, O_R, E \mid Z,T) \neq 0,\\
  &H_{\mathrm{res}}(q, O_R, E_{\mathrm{loss}} \mid Z,T) \approx 0,\\
  &H_{\mathrm{res}}(q, O_R, E_{\mathrm{WT\ rescue}} \mid Z,T) \approx H_{\mathrm{res}}(q, O_R, E_{\mathrm{WT}} \mid Z,T),\\
  &H_{\mathrm{res}}(q, O_R, E_{\mathrm{binding\ defective}} \mid Z,T) \approx 0.
\end{aligned}
$$
This is a finite-row license, not a promotion from coordinate notation to mechanism. The row counts $21636$ and $21512$ indicate the scale of the audited comparison surfaces available for this record, while the absence of same-window RBP occupancy and rescue rows prevents a positive mechanistic assertion from being made solely from the stored half-life and translation boundary contacts.

\paragraph{Matched null.}
The local null is exchangeability of high-coordinate and low-coordinate synonymous residual labels with respect to controlled RNA half-life once the mandatory covariates and the same-row translation readout have been accounted for. Equivalently, the finite statistic must fail if the apparent RNA half-life contrast can be absorbed by transcription, abundance, RNA structure, motif density, composition, codon-pair effects, splice or isoform state, batch, amino-acid composition, or the translation readout. A half-life contrast without measured RBP occupancy remains only an RNA-stability association. An occupancy contrast without coordinate/occupancy decoupling remains only a co-occurrence pattern. A perturbation effect without wild-type rescue and binding-defective or non-target controls remains insufficient for a decay-factor RBP bridge.

\paragraph{Boundary of interpretation.}
The contact named by RNA half-life supplies a transcript-level decay readout. The translation-efficiency contact supplies, at most, a bounded translation-layer readout when same-organism rows are actually joined. The matched mRNA contact supplies a transcription-layer control for residualization. None of these contacts, separately or by name alone, measures decay-factor RBP occupancy, target-RBP loss, wild-type rescue, binding-defective rescue, or coordinate/occupancy decoupling. Therefore no claim about translation realization, protein abundance, protein folding, physical admissibility, biological function, phenotype, adaptation, causation, mechanism, or universality follows from this record unless a separate layer-matched contact supplies the corresponding finite rows and a declared statistic is computed on them.
