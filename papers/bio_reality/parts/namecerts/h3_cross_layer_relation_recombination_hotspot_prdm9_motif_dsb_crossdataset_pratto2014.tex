\subsection{Translation-Conditioned RBP RNA Half-Life Boundary}\label{sec:bioreality-q6-translation-conditioned-rbp-rna-half-life}\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite carrier.}
This record concerns a finite panel of synonymously editable coding-RNA windows.  Each row carries a residual-coordinate label of type $B^*_{Q6}$, an amino-acid-matched coding window, a same-row translation readout, an mRNA abundance or transcription control, an RNA half-life readout, and the intended state of a decay-factor RNA-binding protein.  The finite audit surface available for this chapter is the run $a5e9df27ecd0$, with row counts $n_a=21636$ and $n_c=21512$ and elapsed runtime $187.044$ seconds.  These are the only scalar measurements supplied by the verified payload.  They certify that the present record is row-indexed and finite, but they do not by themselves provide an RNA-decay mechanism, an RBP occupancy matrix, or a perturbation/rescue experiment.

\paragraph{Question tested by the row panel.}
The proposed cross-layer question is whether a residual coordinate on synonymous coding windows can be licensed as a bounded RNA-half-life bridge after the translation boundary has already been paid.  The admissible comparison is not a raw high-versus-low coordinate contrast.  It is a controlled finite-row statistic in which high-coordinate and low-coordinate synonymous windows are compared only after amino-acid identity, transcription, abundance, local RNA structure, motif density, GC and dinucleotide composition, codon-pair context, splice or isoform state, batch, and the same-row translation readout have been absorbed into the null model.  In schematic form, the intended residual readout has the form
$$
\begin{aligned}
H_{\mathrm{res}} &= H_{\mathrm{RNA\ half\ life}} - \widehat{H}(Z,T,A),\\
Z &= \{\text{transcription, abundance, structure, motif, composition, codon-pair, isoform, batch}\},
\end{aligned}
$$
where $T$ is the translation readout and $A$ denotes the amino-acid-matched coding-window constraint.  A coordinate-associated half-life difference outside this residualized comparison is only an RNA-stability association on the available rows.

\paragraph{Positive bridge rule.}
A positive chapter-level bridge would require three finite-row alignments on the same organism and the same local windows.  First, the half-life residual must remain after the translation-readout boundary and the matched transcription or abundance controls.  Second, the signal must track measured local occupancy of a prespecified decay-factor RBP when coordinate state and occupancy state are experimentally separated.  Third, the effect must collapse under loss of the target RBP, return under wild-type rescue, and fail to return under a binding-defective rescue or a non-target control.  In compact notation, the licensed pattern is
$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta_H(B^*_{Q6}) &\neq 0 \quad \text{after } Z,T,A \text{ controls},\\
\Delta_H(B^*_{Q6}) &\text{ follows measured RBP occupancy under decoupling},\\
\Delta_H &\to 0 \text{ under target-RBP loss},\\
\Delta_H &\text{ returns under wild-type rescue only.}
\end{aligned}
$$
The verified row counts $21636$ and $21512$ do not instantiate these four lines; they only give the finite audit scale on which such a test would have to be computed.

\paragraph{Observed boundary from the supplied contacts.}
The supplied half-life contact supports organism-scoped gene or transcript rows with curated mRNA half-life estimates.  The translation contact supports per-CDS ribosome occupancy or translation efficiency when matched ribosome-profiling and mRNA rows are available.  The mRNA contact supports abundance control for separating transcription-layer signal from translation-layer signal.  None of these contacts, as supplied here, contains same-window decay-factor RBP occupancy, coordinate/occupancy decoupling, target-RBP loss, wild-type rescue, binding-defective rescue, or non-target rescue.  Therefore the present finite record has enough structure to state the test and to reject over-reading, but not enough structure to assert a measured RBP-mediated RNA-half-life mechanism.

\paragraph{Null model.}
The local null is exchangeability of the high-coordinate and low-coordinate residual labels with respect to RNA half-life once the required controls are included.  Equivalently, any coordinate term must fail unless it improves the declared finite statistic after translation, abundance, transcription, sequence-composition, structure, isoform, and batch effects have been accounted for.  The null can be written as
$$
\begin{aligned}
H_{\mathrm{res}} \perp B^*_{Q6}\mid Z,T,A,\mathrm{RBP},
\end{aligned}
$$
with the additional experimental condition that the RBP term is measured rather than inferred from coordinate geometry.  If a half-life difference appears without measured RBP occupancy and perturbation/rescue behavior, the result remains a bounded RNA-half-life association on the finite panel, not an RBP bridge.

\paragraph{Local no-promotion rule.}
This chapter makes no claim that residual-coordinate geometry is itself a biological measurement.  A half-life association does not imply translation realization, protein abundance, folded structure, physical admissibility, molecular function, phenotype, adaptation, causation, or a universal biological rule.  A same-row translation readout is a required boundary variable, not proof of an RNA-decay mechanism.  Likewise, mRNA abundance is a control surface rather than evidence for RNA half-life, and ribosome profiling is a translation readout rather than an RBP occupancy or rescue experiment.  Any claim at one of those higher or different layers requires its own matched contact and its own finite statistic.

\paragraph{Chapter verdict.}
The finite evidence is sufficient for a ready boundary chapter because the claim is stated with concrete row scale and with explicit refusal conditions.  The verified run $a5e9df27ecd0$ supplies $n_a=21636$, $n_c=21512$, and runtime $187.044$ seconds.  The available contacts do not supply the decisive RBP occupancy and perturbation/rescue rows.  Therefore the current scientific content is a negative licensing result: the proposed bridge is admissible as a testable finite-row target, but the present payload does not support a positive RBP-mediated RNA-half-life conclusion.
