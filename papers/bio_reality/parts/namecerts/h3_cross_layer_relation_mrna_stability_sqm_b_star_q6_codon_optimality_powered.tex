\subsection{B-star Q6 codon residuals and yeast mRNA half-life}
\label{sec:bioreality-b-star-q6-mrna-stability-sqm}

\paragraph{Claim.}
This record tests whether the nine $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ synonymous-residual coordinates retain measurable information about yeast mRNA stability after the ordinary sequence-composition explanations have already been removed. The readout is $\log_{10}(t_{1/2})$ in minutes from the Neymotin et al. 2014 yeast 4tU metabolic-labeling half-life table. The fitted object is the controlled survival surface $S^{QM}$ from the residual codon-coordinate block $Q$ to the residual mRNA-stability readout $M$, with controls
$$
\begin{aligned}
Z = (&1,\log(\mathrm{CDS\ length}),\mathrm{AA}_{A},\ldots,\mathrm{AA}_{Y},\mathrm{GC3},\mathrm{M\mbox{-}density}).
\end{aligned}
$$
Here the amino-acid terms are the twenty amino-acid composition proportions computed from the same CDS codon counts, and the M-density term is the fraction of sense codons lying in the union support of the nine projected $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ coordinate vectors. Thus the statistic does not ask whether amino-acid composition, CDS length, or GC3 explain half-life; it asks whether the remaining synonymous-coordinate directions still account for residual half-life variation.

\paragraph{Finite row and join.}
The yeast half-life contact is a matched CDS-level table. The join key is the CDS protein identifier after stripping the $4932.$ prefix against the Neymotin \texttt{Syst} identifier. The computation reports $5815$ CDS rows joined to the yeast codon-count/protein table, $5379$ valid half-life records by \texttt{Syst}, and $5085$ rows in the final three-way table used for the $S^{QM}$ estimate. The reported CDS join hit rate is $0.9156038419146592$. No CDS rows were lost for empty sense-codon counts, invalid length, missing protein id, nonpositive abundance, non-object payloads, or unparseable yeast ORF names; $730$ CDS rows lacked a half-life match. The half-life table had no duplicate, missing, non-object, or nonpositive half-life records in the retained keyed set. The payload hash for the half-life input is \texttt{cee1ee9a816f0ec1e351bc2d63d4838ae603ea20566fc3ff529514a6ba6cf7d2}.

\paragraph{Coordinate basis.}
The residual coordinate block has nine columns:
$$
\begin{aligned}
Q = (&K\_AAA,\ Arg\_AGR,\ Ile\_AUA,\ Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR,\ Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG,\\
&Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY,\ Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG,\ Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY,\ f3\_stress).
\end{aligned}
$$
The control matrix has $24$ columns and rank $23$, leaving $5062$ residual degrees of freedom. After residualizing both $Q$ and the half-life readout against $Z$, the $Q$ block has rank $9$, so the fitted $S^{QM}$ surface is not a rank-zero artifact. The half-life residual energy is $229.94736158731112$, and the energy explained by the nine-coordinate block is $11.909960434207074$.

\paragraph{Observed statistic.}
The controlled mRNA-stability statistic is
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^{2}_{QM} &= 0.05179429044975085,\\
\mathrm{rank}(\widetilde Q) &= 9,\\
\mathrm{dominant\ coordinate} &= Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR.
\end{aligned}
$$
The permutation audit used $1000$ permutations and reports a right-tail value $p=0.000999000999000999$ for $R^{2}_{QM}$. On the same yeast join, the protein-abundance surface has $R^{2}_{QP}=0.2761140373962526$, with $f3\_stress$ rather than $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$ as the dominant coordinate. The cosine between the yeast $S^{QM}$ direction and the same-row $S^{QP}$ direction is $0.8040935468384264$. This means the half-life and protein-abundance readbacks are aligned but not identical: the mRNA-stability record is not merely a relabeling of the protein-abundance surface.

\paragraph{Mediation check.}
A separate yeast decomposition conditions the protein-abundance residual on residualized mRNA half-life. On the same $5085$ joined rows, the direct protein-abundance statistic is $R^{2}_{QP}=0.2761140373962526$, while conditioning on $M$ gives $R^{2}_{QP\mid M}=0.24851799532219632$. The absorbed component is therefore
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^{2}_{\mathrm{absorbed\ by\ }M} &= 0.027596042074056304,\\
\mathrm{mediation\ fraction} &= 0.09994436477872035.
\end{aligned}
$$
The mediation permutation audit reports mean $0.00019674487584783138$, null $95$th percentile $0.0014571003414947088$, and right-tail value $p=0.000999000999000999$. This supports a descriptive variance-partition statement: a nonzero part of the yeast $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ protein-abundance association is shared with the mRNA half-life readout. It does not establish a directional biochemical pathway from codon choice to decay to protein abundance.

\paragraph{Cross-organism comparison.}
The same mRNA-stability design has bounded comparison rows in two other organisms. In \emph{Escherichia coli} K-12 MG1655, the selected growth-rate half-life table gives $n=2720$, $R^{2}_{QM}=0.02298556640056076$, permutation value $p=0.000999000999000999$, dominant coordinate $Arg\_AGR$, and same-row $R^{2}_{QP}=0.11632311854858134$. The cosine between the E. coli $S^{QM}$ and $S^{QP}$ directions is $-0.7784923375569599$, so the yeast positive alignment is not promoted to a universal sign rule. In the human consensus relative half-life table, the joined row count is $16165$, $R^{2}_{QM}=0.010503670362051522$, permutation value $p=0.000999000999000999$, dominant coordinate $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$, same-row $R^{2}_{QP}=0.04095215557813418$, and $\cos(S^{QM},S^{QP})=0.6351183883924848$. The human readout is a normalized consensus across $54$ dataset columns, not raw half-life minutes. These comparison rows make the yeast record more interpretable: the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ half-life signal is reproducible as a controlled statistical surface in multiple bounded contacts, while the direction and dominant coordinate remain organism- and readout-dependent.

\paragraph{Boundary.}
This chapter records a controlled association between synonymous codon-coordinate residuals and mRNA half-life. It does not by itself claim measured translation efficiency, ribosome occupancy, protein folding, structural order, physical admissibility, molecular function, organismal phenotype, causal mechanism, or a universal biological law. The yeast half-life measurement is a steady-state 4tU estimate, not a direct decay-rate mechanism, and the statistic is observational. Any translation-layer, protein-layer, structure-layer, function-layer, physical-assay, perturbation, or universality statement requires its own matched contact and its own controlled statistic. Within that boundary, the finite result is positive: after the declared sequence-composition controls and the simpler M-density baseline, the nine-dimensional $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual block explains a nonzero part of residual yeast mRNA half-life, with $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$ as the leading coordinate and a permutation-supported $R^{2}_{QM}$ of $0.05179429044975085$.
