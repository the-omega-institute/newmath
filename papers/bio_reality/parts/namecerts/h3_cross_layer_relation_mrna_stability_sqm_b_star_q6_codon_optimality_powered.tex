\subsection{Yeast mRNA half-life as a controlled $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ readout}
\label{sec:bioreality-q6-mrna-stability-yeast}

\paragraph{Finite row.}
This record tests whether the nine synonymous-residual coordinates of $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ retain predictive mass on a measured yeast mRNA-stability readout after the usual coding-sequence covariates are removed.  The readout is $\log_{10}(t_{1/2}\,\mathrm{minutes})$ from the Neymotin 4tU metabolic-labeling half-life table, joined to real Saccharomyces cerevisiae coding sequences by stripping the $4932.$ prefix from the CDS protein identifier and matching it to the Neymotin Syst key.  The finite join contains $5085$ rows, with $5379$ valid half-life records and $5815$ coding-sequence rows available before the half-life join; the reported CDS join hit rate is $0.9156038419146592$.  The source payload is fixed by the digest \texttt{cee1ee9a816f0ec1e351bc2d63d4838ae603ea20566fc3ff529514a6ba6cf7d2}.

\paragraph{Residual design.}
The tested $Q$ block has nine columns: $\mathrm{K\_AAA}$, $\mathrm{Arg\_AGR}$, $\mathrm{Ile\_AUA}$, $\mathrm{Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR}$, $\mathrm{Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG}$, $\mathrm{Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY}$, $\mathrm{Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG}$, $\mathrm{Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY}$, and $f3\_\mathrm{stress}$.  The nuisance block $Z$ has $24$ columns before rank reduction: an intercept, $\log(\mathrm{cds\_len\_nt})$, the $20$ amino-acid composition proportions computed from the real CDS codon counts, GC3, and the $M$-density baseline defined as the fraction of sense codons lying on the union support of the nine projected $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ vectors.  Its numerical rank is $23$, leaving $5062$ residual degrees of freedom.  The residualized $Q$ block has rank $9$, so the mRNA-stability statistic is not a saturated fit.

\paragraph{Measured statistic.}
Let $M$ denote the residualized yeast mRNA half-life vector and let $Q$ denote the residualized nine-column coordinate block.  The reported survival statistic is the partial coefficient of determination for $M$ after conditioning on $Z$:
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{QM} &= 0.05179429044975085,\\
\mathrm{explained\ energy} &= 11.909960434207074,\\
\lVert M_{\perp Z}\rVert^2 &= 229.94736158731112.
\end{aligned}
$$
The dominant entry of the $S^{QM}$ vector is $\mathrm{Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR}$.  Against $1000$ label permutations, the reported right-tail value is $p=0.000999000999000999$, so the finite row supports a positive controlled association between the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual coordinate block and yeast mRNA half-life.  This is a statistical readout of residual half-life, not a decay mechanism.

\paragraph{Protein-abundance comparison on the same join.}
The same joined yeast rows also carry a protein-abundance comparison.  On that matched surface, the reported abundance statistic is
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{QP} &= 0.2761140373962526,\\
\cos(S^{QM},S^{QP}) &= 0.8040935468384264,\\
\mathrm{dominant}(S^{QP}) &= f3\_\mathrm{stress}.
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the mRNA-stability direction is not identical to the protein-abundance direction: half-life is led by $\mathrm{Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR}$, while abundance on the same rows is led by $f3\_\mathrm{stress}$.  The positive cosine says that the two nine-dimensional summaries are aligned in aggregate, while the distinct dominant coordinate prevents collapsing the two readouts into a single biological explanation.

\paragraph{Mediation check.}
A companion variance partition on the same yeast half-life and abundance rows reports $R^2_{QP}=0.2761140373962526$ before conditioning on the half-life readout and $R^2_{QP\mid M}=0.24851799532219632$ after conditioning on it.  The absorbed part is $0.027596042074056304$, giving a mediation fraction of $0.09994436477872035$.  The permutation reference for that fraction has mean $0.00019674487584783138$, $95$th percentile $0.0014571003414947088$, and right-tail value $0.000999000999000999$.  This supports a finite statistical decomposition in which residualized mRNA half-life accounts for about ten percent of the yeast $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ abundance association, but it does not establish the direction of causation.

\paragraph{Cross-organism anchors.}
The yeast row is not treated as a universal sign rule.  The same $S^{QM}$ style computation gives $R^2_{QM}=0.02298556640056076$ for Escherichia coli K-12 MG1655 at growth rate $0.40\,\mathrm{h}^{-1}$, with $2720$ joined rows, $2697$ residual degrees of freedom, dominant coordinate $\mathrm{Arg\_AGR}$, and $\cos(S^{QM},S^{QP})=-0.7784923375569599$.  For the human consensus relative half-life table, the corresponding values are $R^2_{QM}=0.010503670362051522$, $16165$ joined rows, $16142$ residual degrees of freedom, dominant coordinate $\mathrm{Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR}$, and $\cos(S^{QM},S^{QP})=0.6351183883924848$.  These anchors show that the yeast result is one organism-specific controlled row inside a broader, sign-sensitive family of readbacks.
