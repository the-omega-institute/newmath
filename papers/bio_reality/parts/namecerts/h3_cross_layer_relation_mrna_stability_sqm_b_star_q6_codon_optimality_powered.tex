\subsection{Yeast mRNA-Stability Survival of the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ Codon Residual}
\label{sec:bioreality-q6-mrna-stability-sqm-yeast}

\paragraph{Measured object.}
This record tests whether the nine-dimensional $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ synonymous codon residual has a measurable association with yeast mRNA half-life after the amino-acid quotient and sequence-level covariates are imposed.  The carrier is the residual design matrix $Q$ with coordinates $K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $Leu_{CUN/UUR}$, $Leu_{UUA/UUG}$, $Ser_{UCR/AGY}$, $Ser_{UCA/UCG}$, $Thr_{ACR/ACY}$, and $f_3$-stress, joined to the Neymotin yeast 4tU metabolic-labeling half-life table.  The measured response is $\log_{10}(t_{1/2}\text{ minutes})$, and the control block $Z$ contains an intercept, $\log(\mathrm{CDS\ length})$, the twenty amino-acid composition proportions, GC3, and the $M$-density baseline given by the fraction of sense codons lying on the union support of the nine projected $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ vectors.

\paragraph{Finite row and identifier join.}
The half-life table contributes $5379$ valid Syst rows, and the CDS-side join contributes $5815$ codon-count rows.  After stripping the $4932.$ protein-id prefix and matching to Neymotin Syst identifiers, the final joined row count is $n=5085$, with a reported CDS join hit rate of $0.9156038419146592$.  The skipped CDS records are concentrated in the unmatched half-life key class: $730$ have no half-life match, while empty codon counts, invalid length, missing protein id, nonpositive abundance, non-object rows, and unparseable yeast ORF labels all have count $0$.  The half-life side has no duplicate Syst identifiers, no missing Syst identifiers, no non-object rows, and no nonpositive half-lives.  The source payload hash is \texttt{cee1ee9a816f0ec1e351bc2d63d4838ae603ea20566fc3ff529514a6ba6cf7d2}.

\paragraph{Residual model.}
Let $X$ denote the $5085 \times 9$ $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual-coordinate matrix and let $y$ be the joined half-life readout.  The control matrix has $24$ columns and rank $23$, leaving residual degrees of freedom $5062$ after projection away from $Z$.  The computed survival statistic is the partial explanatory fraction of the $Z$-residualized half-life vector carried by the $Z$-residualized $Q$ coordinates:
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{QM} &= 0.05179429044975085,\\
\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde X) &= 9,\\
\|\widetilde y\|^2 &= 229.94736158731112,\\
\|\operatorname{Proj}_{\widetilde X}(\widetilde y)\|^2 &= 11.909960434207074.
\end{aligned}
$$
The entry count is $9$, the degeneracy cutoff used by the audit is $0.999999$, and the dominant coordinate for $S^{QM}$ is $Leu_{CUN/UUR}$.  Against a $1000$-permutation null, the reported right-tail value is $p=0.000999000999000999$, so the finite row is not a null routing artifact under this design.

\paragraph{Protein-abundance comparison on the same rows.}
On the same yeast join, the sibling protein-abundance statistic is $R^2_{QP}=0.2761140373962526$, with dominant coordinate $f_3$-stress.  The cosine between the $S^{QM}$ and same-join $S^{QP}$ coordinate profiles is $0.8040935468384264$.  Thus the mRNA-stability component is smaller than the protein-abundance component but not orthogonal to it; the readout changes the leading coordinate from $f_3$-stress to $Leu_{CUN/UUR}$ while preserving a strongly positive profile alignment.  The corresponding mediation run reports $R^2_{QP\mid M}=0.24851799532219632$, an absorbed component $R^2_{QP}-R^2_{QP\mid M}=0.027596042074056304$, and mediation fraction $0.09994436477872035$ against a residual-mediator permutation null with mean $0.00019674487584783138$, null $95\%$ value $0.0014571003414947088$, and right-tail value $0.000999000999000999$.  This supports a statistical partition of abundance association through the measured half-life coordinate, not a causal path.

\paragraph{Cross-organism calibration.}
The same $S^{QM}$ design has organism-specific finite rows outside yeast.  In \textit{Escherichia coli} K-12 MG1655, the Esquerré half-life row at growth rate $0.40\,\mathrm{h}^{-1}$ has $n=2720$, $R^2_{QM}=0.02298556640056076$, dominant coordinate $Arg_{AGR}$, $R^2_{QP}=0.11632311854858134$, cosine $S^{QM}$ versus $S^{QP}$ equal to $-0.7784923375569599$, and permutation value $0.000999000999000999$.  In the human consensus half-life row, $n=16165$, $54$ dataset columns are used, $R^2_{QM}=0.010503670362051522$, the dominant coordinate is again $Leu_{CUN/UUR}$, $R^2_{QP}=0.04095215557813418$, the profile cosine is $0.6351183883924848$, and the permutation value is $0.000999000999000999$.  These rows make the yeast result interpretable as one member of a small measured family: the existence of an $S^{QM}$ signal is reproducible across these finite contacts, but its magnitude, leading coordinate, and direction relative to protein abundance are organism- and dataset-dependent.

\paragraph{Module boundary.}
This mRNA-stability row is classified at the cross-layer-relation level: it connects a codon-residual coordinate system to a transcript half-life readout.  It is not a translation-realization row.  The neighboring direction-aware $H$ ranking uses a regularization choice $\lambda=0.01$ with $DL=\log_2(1+n_{\mathrm{col}})$ and reports mRNA as the selected yeast $H_o^{\ast}$ candidate; in that ranking, the yeast mRNA entry has $A_H=0.001897605472160837$, $C_H=0.6040571890503664$, $A_H C_H=0.0011462622274400686$, $DL_H=1.0$, score $-0.008853737772559931$, Bonferroni threshold $0.0013014749238591248$, null $95\%$ value $0.0004745820831535835$, and $n=5128$.  A separate position-controlled wobble-dwell analysis also uses $\lambda=0.01$ with $DL=2$ and has passing families $K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, and $f_3$-stress.  Those neighboring rows show that transcript, protein, and local ribosome-occupancy contacts are distinct modules with their own gates; their lambda choices do not convert this half-life statistic into a mechanism.

\paragraph{Local conclusion.}
For the yeast half-life contact, the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual survives the declared amino-acid, GC3, length, and $M$-density controls with $R^2_{QM}=0.05179429044975085$ and permutation value $0.000999000999000999$.  The strongest coordinate is $Leu_{CUN/UUR}$, and the $S^{QM}$ profile is positively aligned with the same-row protein-abundance profile at cosine $0.8040935468384264$.  The correct reading is narrow: this is a measured association between synonymous codon residual coordinates and residualized yeast mRNA half-life.  It does not show ribosome-mediated translation, protein folding, physical admissibility, biological function, molecular mechanism, phenotype, or a universal biological law.  Any such stronger claim requires a separate layer-matched contact and its own controlled statistic.
