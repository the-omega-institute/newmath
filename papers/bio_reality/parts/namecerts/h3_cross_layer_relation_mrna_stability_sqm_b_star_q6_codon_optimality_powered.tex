\subsection{B-star-Q6 codon residuals and yeast mRNA half-life}
\label{sec:bioreality-b-star-q6-yeast-mrna-stability}

\paragraph{Record.}
This BioReality record tests whether the nine $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates retain measurable explanatory power for yeast mRNA half-life after the ordinary coding-sequence covariates have been removed.  The finite row is the run \texttt{e9294fb2f491}.  The organism is \textit{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}; the readout is $\log_{10}(t_{1/2})$ in minutes from the Neymotin metabolic-labeling half-life table; and the joined table contains $5085$ coding-sequence rows.  The half-life table contributes $5379$ valid records keyed by Syst identifier, while the CDS side contributes $5815$ matched records after stripping the \texttt{4932.} prefix from protein identifiers.  The reported CDS join hit rate is $0.9156038419146592$, with $730$ CDS records skipped for missing half-life matches and no skipped rows from empty codon counts, invalid length, missing protein identifier, nonpositive abundance, or unparseable yeast ORF labels.

\paragraph{Residual design.}
The tested coordinate block has dimension $9$ and consists of $K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $Leu_{CUN/UUR}$, $Leu_{UUA/UUG}$, $Ser_{UCR/AGY}$, $Ser_{UCA/UCG}$, $Thr_{ACR/ACY}$, and $f3\_stress$.  The control matrix has $24$ columns and rank $23$: an intercept, $\log(\mathrm{CDS\ length})$, the $20$ amino-acid composition proportions $A,C,D,E,F,G,H,I,K,L,M,N,P,Q,R,S,T,V,W,Y$, the empirical $GC3$ value, and an $M$-density baseline defined by the fraction of sense codons lying on the union support of the nine projected $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ vectors.  After this residualization the row reports $5062$ residual degrees of freedom.  Thus the tested statement is not a raw codon-usage correlation: it asks whether the nine-dimensional synonymous residual subspace explains the remaining half-life variation after amino-acid composition, length, $GC3$, and the simpler support-density baseline have already been charged to the control side.

\paragraph{Statistic.}
Let $X_{Q}$ be the $5085 \times 9$ matrix of $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates, let $Z$ be the $5085 \times 24$ control matrix, and let $m$ be the yeast half-life readout.  The experiment first forms the residualized variables $\widetilde X_{Q}$ and $\widetilde m$ after projection away from $Z$, and then reports the partial coefficient of determination for the nine-coordinate block:
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^{2}_{QM}
  &= 0.05179429044975085,\\
\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde X_{Q})
  &= 9,\\
\|\widehat m_{Q}\|^{2}
  &= 11.909960434207074,\\
\|\widetilde m\|^{2}
  &= 229.94736158731112.
\end{aligned}
$$
The dominant half-life coordinate in this run is $Leu_{CUN/UUR}$.  The permutation test uses $1000$ permutations and reports $p = 0.000999000999000999$ for the $R^{2}_{QM}$ statistic.  The comparison table computed on the same joined rows reports $R^{2}_{QP}=0.2761140373962526$ for protein abundance and a cosine similarity of $0.8040935468384264$ between the $S^{QM}$ and $S^{QP}$ coordinate profiles.  On that same join, the protein-abundance dominant coordinate is $f3\_stress$, so the mRNA half-life readout and the protein-abundance readout are aligned but not identical in their leading coordinate.

\paragraph{Interpretation.}
The finite result supports a bounded cross-layer relation: on this yeast table, the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ synonymous residual block is not fully absorbed by amino-acid composition, sequence length, $GC3$, and the $M$-density baseline when the target is residualized $\log_{10}$ mRNA half-life.  The effect size is modest but nonzero: about $5.18\%$ of the remaining half-life variance is assigned to the nine-coordinate block.  The row is therefore a positive $S^{QM}$ contact for mRNA stability, not merely a codon-composition artifact and not merely a protein-abundance echo.  Its dominant $Leu_{CUN/UUR}$ coordinate also gives the contact a specific coordinate readback rather than only a block-level pass.

\paragraph{Relation to the abundance mediation row.}
The companion abundance-residual mediation run \texttt{fb4b0c5b925a} uses the same $5085$ joined yeast rows and the same control rank $23$.  It reports $R^{2}_{QP}=0.2761140373962526$ before conditioning on mRNA half-life and $R^{2}_{QP\mid M}=0.24851799532219632$ after conditioning on the residualized half-life mediator, so the absolute absorbed component is
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^{2}_{\mathrm{absorbed\ by\ }M}
  &= 0.027596042074056304,\\
\frac{R^{2}_{QP}-R^{2}_{QP\mid M}}{R^{2}_{QP}}
  &= 0.09994436477872035.
\end{aligned}
$$
That mediation row has a right-tail permutation value $p = 0.000999000999000999$, a permutation-null mean $0.00019674487584783138$, and a permutation-null $95\%$ point $0.0014571003414947088$.  These numbers make the mRNA-stability row relevant to the protein-abundance row, but they do not turn the variance partition into a causal pathway.  The only supported statement is that residualized yeast half-life absorbs about $10.0\%$ of the measured $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ protein-abundance association on this observational join.

\paragraph{Cross-organism check.}
The same mRNA-stability construction has two comparison rows.  In \textit{Escherichia coli} K-12 MG1655, run \texttt{9c91117c69c4} joins $2720$ rows from the Esquerr\'e half-life table at growth rate $0.40\,h^{-1}$ and reports $R^{2}_{QM}=0.02298556640056076$, $R^{2}_{QP}=0.11632311854858134$, dominant coordinate $Arg_{AGR}$, and $1000$-permutation value $p=0.000999000999000999$; its cosine between $S^{QM}$ and $S^{QP}$ is negative, $-0.7784923375569599$.  In the human consensus half-life row, run \texttt{97aeba49c2d2} joins $16165$ rows across $54$ normalized half-life columns and reports $R^{2}_{QM}=0.010503670362051522$, $R^{2}_{QP}=0.04095215557813418$, dominant coordinate $Leu_{CUN/UUR}$, $p=0.000999000999000999$, and positive cosine $0.6351183883924848$.  The three rows therefore agree that a controlled $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ mRNA-stability statistic is computable and positive in these finite datasets, while the dominant coordinate and the relation to protein abundance are organism-dependent.

\paragraph{Boundary.}
This chapter does not claim that codon residuals cause mRNA decay or that the $Leu_{CUN/UUR}$ entry is a biochemical decay mechanism.  The Neymotin yeast contact is a single-organism, steady-state $4tU$ metabolic-labeling estimate, and the statistic is a residual projection.  It is not ribosome profiling, not a matched translation-efficiency experiment, and not a direct decay-rate assay.  No translation realization, protein folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, organismal phenotype, mechanism, or universal biological law follows from this row unless a separate layer-matched contact supplies that measurement and its own controlled statistic.  The local conclusion is narrower: under the stated yeast controls, the nine $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates carry a finite, reproducible mRNA-half-life association, with $Leu_{CUN/UUR}$ leading the $S^{QM}$ profile and with a related but non-identical protein-abundance profile on the same joined rows.
