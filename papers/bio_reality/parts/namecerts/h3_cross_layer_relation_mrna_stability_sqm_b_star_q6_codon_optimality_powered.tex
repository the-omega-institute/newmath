\subsection{Yeast mRNA-Stability Survival of the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ Codon-Residual Coordinates}
\label{sec:bioreality-q6-mrna-stability-sqm-yeast}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Question.}
This chapter records a bounded cross-layer test in which the nine $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates are evaluated against a yeast mRNA-stability readout. The target is not translation efficiency and not protein function. It is the residual association between codon-choice coordinates and $\log_{10}$ mRNA half-life after the same CDS-derived control block used in the sibling protein-abundance calculation. The tested coordinate list is
$$
\begin{aligned}
&K\_AAA,\ Arg\_AGR,\ Ile\_AUA,\ Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR,\ Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG,\\
&Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY,\ Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG,\ Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY,\ f3\_stress.
\end{aligned}
$$
The readout is $\log_{10}(t_{1/2}\ \mathrm{minutes})$ from the Neymotin yeast 4tU metabolic-labeling half-life table, joined to real yeast CDS codon counts by stripping the $4932.$ prefix from the CDS protein identifier and matching it to the Neymotin Syst key.

\paragraph{Finite row set and controls.}
The joined table contains $5085$ valid rows. The source half-life table contributes $5379$ valid Syst-keyed records, while the CDS side reports $5815$ joined coding-sequence records before the half-life intersection; the reported CDS join hit rate is $0.9156038419146592$. The half-life payload hash is \texttt{cee1ee9a816f0ec1e351bc2d63d4838ae603ea20566fc3ff529514a6ba6cf7d2}. The skipped CDS records are concentrated in the no-half-life-match bucket: $730$ CDS records have no matched half-life entry, and the other listed CDS skip modes are zero. On the half-life side, missing Syst, duplicate Syst, non-object rows, and nonpositive half-lives are all zero.

The control matrix $Z$ has $24$ columns and rank $23$, leaving residual degrees of freedom $5062$. Its columns consist of an intercept, $\log(\mathrm{CDS\ length\ in\ nt})$, the twenty amino-acid composition proportions $A,C,D,E,F,G,H,I,K,L,M,N,P,Q,R,S,T,V,W,Y$ computed from real CDS codon counts, GC3, and the $M$-density baseline, defined as the fraction of sense codons lying on the union support of the nine projected $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ coordinate vectors. Thus the mRNA-stability statistic is not a raw codon-usage correlation; it is measured only after amino-acid sequence composition, CDS length, GC3, and the simpler union-support density have already been removed.

\paragraph{Survival statistic.}
Let $X$ be the $5085\times 9$ matrix of the nine residual codon coordinates and let $y$ be the joined $\log_{10}$ half-life vector. Both are residualized against $Z$ before estimating the codon-coordinate contribution. The finite-row calculation gives
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{QM} &= 0.05179429044975085,\\
\mathrm{rank}(\widetilde X) &= 9,\\
\|\widehat y_{QM}\|^2 &= 11.909960434207074,\\
\|\widetilde y\|^2 &= 229.94736158731112.
\end{aligned}
$$
The dominant coordinate for the mRNA-stability surface is $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$. A $1000$-permutation comparison reports right-tail $p=0.000999000999000999$ for the $R^2_{QM}$ statistic, so the finite row does not collapse to the permutation null under the declared controls. The associated protein-abundance computation on the same join gives $R^2_{QP}=0.2761140373962526$, with dominant coordinate $f3\_stress$. The cosine between the mRNA-stability surface $S^{QM}$ and the same-join abundance surface $S^{QP}$ is $0.8040935468384264$, indicating that the two residual surfaces are aligned but not identical.

\paragraph{Mediation comparison.}
The related abundance-residual mediation calculation uses the same $5085$-row yeast three-way join and the same nine-coordinate design. It reports $R^2_{QP}=0.2761140373962526$ before conditioning on the mRNA half-life mediator and $R^2_{QP\mid M}=0.24851799532219632$ after conditioning on it. The absorbed component is therefore $0.027596042074056304$, and the reported mediation fraction is $0.09994436477872035$. Its residual-mediator permutation audit gives null mean $0.00019674487584783138$, null $95$th percentile $0.0014571003414947088$, and right-tail $p=0.000999000999000999$. This comparison supports only a variance-partition statement: part of the yeast protein-abundance residual association is shared with residualized mRNA half-life, but the calculation does not establish a causal route from codon choice through decay to abundance.

\paragraph{Cross-organism orientation.}
The yeast result is not being promoted to a universal direction. Two companion mRNA-stability rows show that the same family of tests is organism-dependent. In \emph{Escherichia coli} K-12 MG1655, the comparable half-life row has $n=2720$, $R^2_{QM}=0.02298556640056076$, dominant coordinate $Arg\_AGR$, same-join $R^2_{QP}=0.11632311854858134$, and cosine $-0.7784923375569599$ against the abundance surface. In the human consensus relative-half-life row, the comparable calculation has $n=16165$, $R^2_{QM}=0.010503670362051522$, dominant coordinate $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$, same-join $R^2_{QP}=0.04095215557813418$, and positive cosine $0.6351183883924848$. These rows make the yeast observation sharper rather than broader: the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ mRNA-stability signal survives in the yeast table, but its coordinate emphasis and relation to abundance are not invariant across organisms.

\paragraph{Local interpretation.}
The finite conclusion is that, in the Neymotin yeast half-life join, the nine $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates retain a positive residual mRNA-stability association after the declared $Z$ controls and the $M$-density baseline. Numerically, this is the statement $R^2_{QM}=0.05179429044975085$ with full-rank residualized coordinate design, dominant $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$, and permutation $p=0.000999000999000999$. The chapter also records that the mRNA-stability surface is aligned with, but distinct from, the same-join protein-abundance surface, whose dominant coordinate is $f3\_stress$.

\paragraph{Boundary.}
No translation, folding, physical admissibility, biochemical function, molecular mechanism, phenotype, or universal biological rule follows from this row. The readout is a single-organism yeast mRNA half-life table from a 4tU metabolic-labeling experiment, and the statistic is a residual projection. It is not ribosome profiling, not measured elongation, not direct decay-rate biophysics, not matched translation control, and not a perturbation experiment. A separate ribosome-occupancy row exists for local codon-window analysis, where the families $K\_AAA$, $Arg\_AGR$, and $f3\_stress$ pass a position-bin-controlled occupancy gate under $\lambda_{DL}=0.01$ and $C_H>0$; that row is descriptive and does not convert the mRNA-stability result into a translation mechanism. Likewise, the protein-abundance mediation row remains a statistical partition and does not prove codon-to-stability-to-abundance causation. The only supported statement here is the finite controlled survival of $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ codon-residual information in the yeast mRNA half-life readout.
