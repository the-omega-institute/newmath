\subsection{Curated Interval Contact for ORF Eligibility}
\label{sec:h2-orf-eligibility-curated-interval-contact}

\paragraph{Question isolated by the finite record.}
The layer considered here is not translation, protein folding, biochemical admissibility, cellular function, molecular mechanism, or a universal biological law.  It is the narrower question of whether a finite start--stop interval can be treated as eligible for open-reading-frame status after contact with an external curated nucleotide annotation.  The seed packet before contact had only a boundary role: start and stop codons can be enumerated in a finite geometry, but that enumeration by itself does not show that any biological interval has been observed.  The present contact supplies one finite curated interval dataset and asks only whether the already fixed start--stop classifier separates the curated positives from matched non-ORF decoys and from boundary cases.

\paragraph{External interval contact.}
The contact used a curated finite nucleotide-window manifest derived from NCBI RefSeq GenBank XML CDS intervals for \emph{Escherichia coli} K-12 MG1655, complete genome accession NC\_000913.3.  The snapshot date was 2026-05-30.  The loaded dataset path was \texttt{tools/bio\_reality/data/curated\_orf\_interval\_contact\_dataset.json}, and the row count was $168$.  The required input classes were exactly positive curated ORF intervals, matched non-ORF decoys, and boundary cases.  The classifier was \texttt{orf\_start\_stop\_window\_eligibility}; its rules were recorded as unmodified.  The protocol froze the classifier, the input classes, the held-out splits, and the baselines before the external observation: the freeze time was 2026-05-29T19:11:13+00:00, while the external evidence was observed at 2026-05-30T10:29:31+00:00.  The analysis channel also recorded that no translation or structure input was used.

\paragraph{Local interpretation.}
The contact upgrades the seed from a mere start--stop boundary packet to a dataset-bounded ORF-eligibility contact: within these $168$ curated rows, the frozen geometry-only classifier separates the supplied positive intervals from the supplied decoys and does not promote the boundary cases.  The result is finite, dated, and tied to the stated RefSeq source.  It does not license any broader biological conclusion outside that manifest, and it does not turn codon geometry into a translation event.  In particular, no conclusion about translation realization, folded protein structure, physical admissibility, molecular function, mechanism of action, or universality follows from these rows unless another independent contact supplies the corresponding evidence.
