\paragraph{Claim scope.}
This record concerns open-reading-frame eligibility only at the level of finite start--stop interval enumeration.  Its biological object is not translation, peptide realization, protein folding, cellular fitness, or gene function.  The claim is that a predeclared start--stop-window classifier, supplied with a curated interval annotation contact, separates curated coding-sequence intervals from matched non-ORF decoys on the finite dataset described below, while leaving boundary cases outside the promoted class.  The admissible readout is therefore an interval-level eligibility statement tied to this specific curated contact.

\paragraph{Held-out stability and boundary cases.}
The held-out split audit contains three folds.  In the left-locus fold, $24$ positives were accepted at rate $1.0$ and $24$ decoys were rejected at rate $1.0$.  The middle-locus fold has the same $24$ positive and $24$ decoy counts with rates $1.0$ and $1.0$.  The right-locus fold also has $24$ positives and $24$ decoys with the same rates.  All three folds are marked as passed.  Boundary cases are separately capped: $24$ boundary rows were tested, $0$ were promoted, and the promoted fraction was $0.0$.  This is the local guard that prevents the interval classifier from extending its verdict to rows outside the curated positive/decoy design.

\paragraph{Local cannot-claim boundary.}
The conclusion is an ORF-eligibility contact statement for this curated interval dataset.  It does not establish that any interval is translated in vivo, does not establish a folded protein structure, does not establish thermodynamic or physical admissibility, does not establish biological function, does not identify a molecular mechanism, and does not assert a universal law over genomes or transcripts.  The matched decoy and boundary controls classify only the finite rows in this contact design.  Any claim about translation, folding, physical realization, function, mechanism, or broad biological generality requires a further independent observation suited to that layer.
