\paragraph{Question fixed by the contact.}
The object tested here is not protein production, molecular function, or a cellular mechanism.  The tested layer is only open-reading-frame eligibility as seen through finite start/stop codon interval enumeration.  The biological input is a curated interval table extracted from NCBI RefSeq GenBank XML CDS annotations for \textit{Escherichia coli} K-12 MG1655 complete genome accession NC\_000913.3, with snapshot date 2026-05-30. $$
\begin{aligned}

\end{aligned}

The scope is therefore external curated interval contact only.  The result says that a frozen start/stop-window classifier separates these finite curated positives from matched decoys and keeps the boundary cases unpromoted.
