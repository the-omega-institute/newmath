\paragraph{Local boundary of interpretation.}
The result supports only the statement that this frozen start/stop-window eligibility classifier makes the recorded positive/decoy distinctions on this curated RefSeq interval dataset and leaves the boundary rows unpromoted.  It does not establish that an accepted interval is translated in vivo, that a peptide folds, that any folded state is physically admissible, that a mechanism is present, that the interval has biological function, or that a universal biological law has been discovered.  Those stronger statements require separate contacts of the appropriate kind.  The NCBI RefSeq annotation remains curated biological input to this test; it is not produced by the codon geometry, and the matched decoy and boundary controls do not classify windows outside the supplied dataset.
