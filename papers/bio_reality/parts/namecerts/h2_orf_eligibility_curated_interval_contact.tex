\paragraph{Finite contact under study.}
This record concerns open-reading-frame eligibility as a finite interval test, not as a claim about translation or protein production.  The biological object is the recognition of curated CDS-like nucleotide intervals by a start/stop-window classifier whose rules were fixed before the NCBI RefSeq observation was read.  The contact is confined to Escherichia coli K-12 MG1655 accession NC\_000913.3, snapshot date 2026-05-30, using a curated interval table with 168 rows at \texttt{tools/bio\_reality/data/curated\_orf\_interval\_contact\_dataset.json}.  The three declared input classes are \texttt{positive\_curated\_orf}, \texttt{matched\_non\_orf\_decoy}, and \texttt{boundary\_case}.  The classifier identifier is \texttt{orf\_start\_stop\_window\_eligibility}, and its rule set is reported as unmodified during the contact test.  The earlier one-window start/stop enumeration contact had scope \texttt{external\_window\_start\_stop\_enumeration\_only}, with one input window and one classified window; the present contact is stronger because it uses an independent curated interval table rather than only a syntactic boundary enumeration.

\paragraph{Protocol ordering.}
The protocol timestamps separate rule choice from biological observation.  The classifier rules, held-out splits, input classes, and baseline comparisons were all declared at 2026-05-29T19:11:13+00:00, while the external curated interval evidence was observed at 2026-05-30T10:29:31+00:00. The self-check records three gate checks and states that no codon geometry is carried upward across biological layers.

\paragraph{Local claim boundary.}
The admissible conclusion is narrow: within this curated RefSeq interval table and its matched controls, the fixed start/stop-window classifier separates the positive curated intervals from the matched non-ORF decoys, while leaving boundary cases below the eligibility threshold.  Nothing in this record asserts translation, ribosomal realization, folded protein structure, physical admissibility, biochemical function, mechanism, genome-wide law, or universal biological validity.  Those stronger layers would require their own contact data.  The NCBI RefSeq annotation remains a curated biological input to the test, not a consequence of the codon geometry itself.
