\paragraph{Local conclusion and boundary.}
The chapter-level conclusion is narrow.  In this finite contact, a frozen start/stop-window eligibility classifier separates $72$ curated positive CDS intervals from $72$ matched non-ORF decoys, remains stable across three held-out locus folds of $24$ positive and $24$ decoy examples each, and refuses promotion on $24$ boundary examples.  This supports ORF eligibility only for the supplied curated interval contact.  It does not establish translation, folded protein structure, physical admissibility, biological function, mechanism, or universality.  It also does not classify windows outside the $168$-row dataset.  The RefSeq annotation is an outside curated biological contact used to test the finite rule; it is not part of the codon-enumeration kernel and it does not turn start/stop geometry alone into an observed biological process.
