\subsection{Curated interval contact for ORF eligibility}
\label{subsec:h2-orf-eligibility-curated-interval-contact}

\paragraph{Object and local claim.}
This record concerns open-reading-frame eligibility as tested by finite start and stop codon enumeration together with an independently curated interval annotation. The biological object is not protein production and not molecular function. It is the narrower question whether a nucleotide interval, already supplied by a curated biological annotation, is accepted by the fixed start--stop-window classifier used in this finite row. The pre-contact packet therefore had only the status of a boundary seed: before an annotated interval was attached, the coordinate data could describe what must be tested, but it could not itself assert that an observed open reading frame existed.

The classifier is \texttt{orf\_start\_stop\_window\_eligibility}, and the row states that the classifier rules were not modified. The protocol was frozen at $2026$-$05$-$29\,19{:}11{:}13+00{:}00$, before the external evidence time $2026$-$05$-$30\,10{:}29{:}31+00{:}00$. The frozen fields include predeclared baselines, fixed input classes, fixed held-out splits, fixed classifier rules, and analysis with no translation or structure input. The required input classes are present: \texttt{boundary\_case}, \texttt{matched\_non\_orf\_decoy}, and \texttt{positive\_curated\_orf}.

This is an interval-contact result, not a general biological theorem.

\paragraph{Local verdict and limits.}
The finite row supports the following restricted statement: after an independently curated RefSeq CDS interval contact is attached, the frozen start--stop-window eligibility classifier accepts all $72$ positive curated intervals, rejects all $72$ matched decoys, passes all three held-out locus splits, and leaves $24$ boundary cases unpromoted. The self-check row records three checked gates and states that no codon geometry is promoted across biological layers. Consequently, the contact establishes only dataset-scoped ORF-eligibility separation for this curated interval manifest. It does not establish translation, protein folding, physical admissibility, biological function, biochemical mechanism, organism-level effect, or a universal rule for windows outside this dataset. Any such stronger claim would require its own independent contact and its own finite audit.
