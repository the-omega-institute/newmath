\paragraph{Local conclusion.}
Within this row, the admissible conclusion is that the frozen start--stop-window classifier has a curated interval contact for $\mathsf{orf\_eligibility}$ on the stated RefSeq-derived finite dataset.  The row is not a statement that the codon geometry alone observes an ORF.  It is also not a statement about translation realization, folding, physical admissibility, biochemical mechanism, biological function, or universality.  Any one of those stronger layers would require its own independent contact with data capable of testing that stronger predicate.  The self-check row agrees with this boundary: it records $3$ checked gates and states that no codon geometry is promoted across biological layers.
