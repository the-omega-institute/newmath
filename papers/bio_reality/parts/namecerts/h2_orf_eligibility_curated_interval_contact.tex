\paragraph{Claim admitted by the contact.}
The admitted claim is narrow. In this row, ORF eligibility means that the frozen start--stop-window classifier separates curated positive interval annotations from matched non-ORF decoys in the recorded finite dataset, while refusing to promote boundary cases. The support is therefore a dataset-level interval-contact result: $168$ rows, $72$ positive acceptances, $72$ matched decoy rejections, $24$ boundary non-promotions, three stable held-out splits, and predeclared paired advantage over the baseline. The self-check row independently records that three gates were checked and that no codon geometry was promoted across biological layers.

\paragraph{Local non-promotion rule.}
Nothing in this chapter asserts translation realization, folded protein structure, physical admissibility, biological function, mechanism, or universality. The curated interval contact says that the named RefSeq CDS intervals can test this ORF-eligibility layer for this finite dataset. It does not turn the codon quotient into a ribosomal event, does not infer a protein fold, does not establish thermodynamic or kinetic feasibility, does not identify function, and does not state a global law about all genomes or all intervals. Any such stronger layer would require its own separately recorded contact capable of testing that stronger biological assertion.
