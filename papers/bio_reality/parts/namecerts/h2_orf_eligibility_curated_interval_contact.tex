\paragraph{Non-promotion boundary.}
No stronger biological conclusion is licensed here.  The curated interval contact does not show that any accepted interval is translated.  It does not show a folded protein structure, a physically admissible molecular state, a biochemical mechanism, a biological function, or a universal law about genomes.  The matched decoy and boundary controls do not classify windows outside the supplied dataset.  The RefSeq contact remains an outside curated biological annotation used by the experiment; it is not converted into kernel content or into a general rule.  Any claim about translation realization, folding, physical admissibility, mechanism, function, or universality would require an additional contact designed for that layer.
