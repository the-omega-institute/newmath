\subsection{Curated interval contact for open-reading-frame eligibility}\label{sec:h2-orf-eligibility-curated-interval-contact}

\paragraph{Question.}
This record asks whether a finite start--stop enumeration rule, after being fixed before biological inspection, can make contact with curated genomic interval data at the layer of open-reading-frame eligibility. The biological object is not a protein, a transcript, a folded conformation, or a functional unit. It is only the eligibility predicate induced by interval boundaries: a nucleotide window is accepted when its externally supplied interval data satisfy the frozen start--stop rule, and it is rejected when matched non-ORF controls fail that rule. The relevant dataset is the curated interval contact table at \texttt{tools/bio\_reality/data/curated\_orf\_interval\_contact\_dataset.json}, loaded with $168$ rows from NCBI RefSeq GenBank XML CDS intervals for \emph{Escherichia coli} K-12 MG1655 accession NC\_000913.3, with snapshot date $2026$-$05$-$30$.

\paragraph{Protocol separation.}
The interval-contact experiment records that the classifier identifier was \texttt{orf\_start\_stop\_window\_eligibility}, that classifier rules were not modified, and that the protocol was frozen at $2026$-$05$-$29$ $19{:}11{:}13+00{:}00$ before the external evidence was observed at $2026$-$05$-$30$ $10{:}29{:}31+00{:}00$. This is the central control: the contact tests whether an externally curated interval table agrees with the predeclared boundary rule, not whether the codon geometry itself generates biological reality.

\paragraph{Held-out stability.}
The held-out split table has three folds. In \texttt{refseq\_nc\_000913\_left\_locus\_fold}, the positive count is $24$ with accept rate $1.0$, and the decoy count is $24$ with reject rate $1.0$. \paragraph{Local conclusion.}
Within this contact table, the open-reading-frame eligibility layer is supported as an externally tested interval-boundary classification. The supported statement is narrow: curated CDS intervals from the stated RefSeq source align with the frozen start--stop eligibility rule, matched decoys are rejected, boundary cases are not promoted, and three held-out locus splits preserve the same separation. The self-check row reports three checked gates and states that no codon geometry is promoted across biological layers. Accordingly, this chapter makes no claim of translation realization, folded structure, physical admissibility, biological function, molecular mechanism, species-wide law, or universal genomic principle. Any such stronger assertion would require a distinct contact whose measured object is that stronger layer rather than the interval-eligibility row used here.
