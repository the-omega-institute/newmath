\paragraph{Local decision rule.}
The admissible test is therefore narrow.  For each organism and CDS window, compute the amino-acid-quotient residual $f_3$-stress coordinate, attach same-window wobble weights, attach matched mRNA abundance, and then estimate the position-resolved ribosome-occupancy or translation-efficiency residual under the full control set $Z$.  A positive chapter-level readout would require a nondegenerate design matrix, a strictly positive held-out or residual survival statistic, and failure of simpler baselines to absorb the signal.  If the rank check fails, if the controlled increment is non-positive, or if the signal is explained by GC3, length, $M$-density, mRNA, amino-acid bin, position, or codon-count controls, the coordinate remains a codon-topology residual.

\paragraph{Boundary of interpretation.}
No translation realization follows from the codon lists, quotient vectors, APA/PAS row counts, modeled tAI or stAI weights, or the name of a ribosome-occupancy contact.  No claim is made about protein abundance, protein folding, structural order, physical admissibility, molecular function, phenotype, mechanism, adaptation, causation, or universality.  Each of those layers would require its own matched contact and its own finite statistic.  The present chapter records a precise request for a same-organism, same-CDS, same-window translation-readout table and states that the $f_3$-stress coordinate may move downstream only if that table yields a non-vacuous controlled survival entry.
