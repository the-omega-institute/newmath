\subsection{Polyadenylation Row Count and Translation-Readout Boundary}
\label{sec:bioreality-apa-pas-polyadb-f3-translation-boundary}

\paragraph{Finite row surface.}
The PolyADB alternative-polyadenylation contact attached to this record is a finite table rather than a mechanistic conclusion.  Its verified surface contains $24740$ genes and $296950$ polyadenylation-site rows, with the recorded run reporting $0.0$ seconds of runtime for the row-count certificate.  These numbers are large enough to certify that the named APA table is not empty, but they do not by themselves supply codon-window rows, wobble-weight rows, matched mRNA rows, or position-resolved ribosome-footprint rows.  Consequently the APA count can serve as a finite catalogue boundary for transcript-end usage, while the proposed $f_3$-stress translation readout remains unfilled at the required joined-row level.

\paragraph{Coordinate content already fixed.}
The codon-topology side of the record has finite BEDC-side coordinates that can be stated exactly.  The saturated $f_3$ region records
$$
\begin{aligned}

\end{aligned}
$$
The recorded boundary list for $f_3$ is empty, and the two codons present in $M$ but absent from the saturated list are $\mathrm{ACA}$ and $\mathrm{ACG}$.  The quotient certificate separately gives $|\pi_M|=10$, an $M$-vector $(20,38,26,8,1)$, and a quotient vector $(10,14,6,1)$.  These are finite coordinate facts about the codon residual geometry; they are not measurements of translation, ribosome pausing, protein abundance, folding, physical admissibility, function, mechanism, phenotype, or universality.

\paragraph{Readout requested by the claim.}
The biological readout requested here is narrower than the APA row count and narrower than a modeled codon-adaptation table.  A positive entry would require a same-organism, same-CDS, same-window table containing ordered coding-sequence coordinates, amino-acid-quotient-surviving synonymous-codon residuals, local $f_3$-paired codon counts, a bounded wobble-weight surface, matched mRNA abundance, and a position-resolved ribosome-occupancy or explicitly residualized translation-efficiency readout.  The admissible statistic has the form
$$
\begin{aligned}
S_{f_3\text{-stress}}(T_{\mathrm{pos}}\mid Z)>0,
\end{aligned}
$$
where $T_{\mathrm{pos}}$ denotes the position-resolved translation readout and $Z$ includes organism, amino-acid composition or amino-acid bins, $\mathrm{GC3}$, CDS length, $M$-density, matched mRNA abundance, position controls, codon-count controls, coverage and mappability limits, rank checks, and simpler-baseline ablations.  The supplied data do not yet contain that matrix, so the survival expression is a required test, not an observed effect.

\paragraph{Controls suggested by nearby finite probes.}
A nearby structural-order probe shows why the boundary must remain local and readout-specific.  In that record, the primary ramp window $50$ had weighted held-out $\Delta R^2=0.0011165640103326878$ for the ramp and $-0.00026157313923957926$ for the body, with ramp sign-flip $p=0.0478515625$, body sign-flip $p=0.54296875$, and paired ramp-minus-body sign-flip $p=0.05859375$.  Its conclusion was $\mathrm{ramp\_positive\_but\_not\_localized}$, not a broad structural or mechanistic law.  That finite outcome is useful here as a cautionary calibration: even when a bounded signal is positive in one window statistic, the result may fail the localization or mediation gate needed for promotion to another biological layer.

\paragraph{Local verdict.}
The chapter therefore records a nonempty APA finite-row surface and a precise codon-coordinate boundary, but not a completed cross-layer translation statistic.  The current evidence can justify the statement that the named PolyADB table has $24740$ gene rows and $296950$ PAS rows, and that the $f_3$ codon coordinate has the finite saturated lists and quotient sizes stated above.  It cannot justify the stronger statement that $f_3$-stress predicts position-resolved ribosome occupancy, translation efficiency, protein output, structural order, physical admissibility, biological function, mechanism, phenotype, or a universal biological rule.  A later positive claim must be based on the joined finite matrix described in this subsection and must remain positive after the specified controls, rank sufficiency checks, null comparison, and simpler-baseline ablation.
