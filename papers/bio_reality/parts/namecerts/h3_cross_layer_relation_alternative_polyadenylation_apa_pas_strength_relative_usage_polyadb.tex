\subsection{Alternative-polyadenylation contact and the $f_3$ translation boundary}

\paragraph{Finite row surface.}
The record considered here has two logically different finite components.  The first is a curated alternative-polyadenylation surface: the PolyADB-style pass reports $24740$ genes and $296950$ polyadenylation sites for the row named
$\texttt{h3.cross\_layer\_relation.alternative\_polyadenylation.apa\_pas\_strength\_relative\_usage\_polyadb}$.
This count is an external row-count certificate for an APA/PAS table; it is not, by itself, a codon-window, ribosome-footprint, or translation-efficiency table.  The second component is an $f_3$ residual coordinate proposed for a possible translation-layer test.  Its admissible biological use is narrower: the coordinate may be tested only after same-organism, same-CDS, same-window codon rows, bounded wobble-weight rows, matched mRNA abundance, and position-resolved ribosome-occupancy rows are placed in one finite design matrix.

\paragraph{Readout that would make the cross-layer claim testable.}
Let $W$ range over matched CDS windows and let $Z$ collect organism, amino-acid composition or amino-acid bin, GC3, CDS length, $M$-density, matched mRNA abundance, position, coverage, mappability, and simpler codon-count baselines.  The only admissible survival statistic for the present packet has the form
$$
\begin{aligned}
S_{f_3}(T_{\mathrm{pos}}\mid Z)
  &= \Delta\mathcal{L}\bigl(T_{\mathrm{pos}}; Z,\rho_{f_3}(W)\bigr)
     -\Delta\mathcal{L}\bigl(T_{\mathrm{pos}}; Z\bigr),
\end{aligned}
$$
where $T_{\mathrm{pos}}$ is a position-resolved ribosome-occupancy or explicitly bounded translation-efficiency residual, and $\rho_{f_3}(W)$ is computed only after synonymous codon residualization has removed amino-acid composition.  A positive interpretation requires a nondegenerate matrix, a rank check, a null comparison, and an ablation showing that the coordinate still contributes after the simpler baselines.  If $S_{f_3}(T_{\mathrm{pos}}\mid Z)\leq 0$, or if the joined matrix is rank-degenerate, the row remains a codon-topology residual rather than a promoted translation readout.

A nearby powered structural-order run is useful here only as a warning about scope. It shows instead why this chapter keeps finite coordinate support, row-count evidence, and downstream biological interpretation in separate registers.

\paragraph{Local boundary.}
The APA table supplies $24740$ gene rows and $296950$ PAS rows; the $f_3$ records supply finite codon-support and quotient-geometry facts; neither supplies a same-window ribosome-occupancy statistic.  Consequently this chapter admits only the following narrow statement: an $f_3$ stress coordinate is eligible for a controlled, position-resolved translation-readout test once the required row joins exist.  No conclusion about translation realization, protein abundance, folding, structural order, physical admissibility, molecular function, mechanism, phenotype, adaptation, causation, or universality follows from the codon support, the APA/PAS row count, modeled tAI or stAI weights, or the existence of a contact route.  Each such downstream layer requires its own matched finite contact and its own non-vacuous controlled statistic.
