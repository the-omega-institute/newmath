\paragraph{Finite certificate.}

$\mathrm{h3.cross\_layer\_relation.alternative\_polyadenylation.apa\_pas\_strength\_relative\_usage\_polyadb}$, read here through the associated $f_3$-stress translation-boundary packet.  The available finite row for the alternative-polyadenylation surface contains $24740$ genes and $296950$ polyadenylation-site rows. The present chapter therefore treats the row as a finite contact-bearing routing certificate and states the exact additional finite matrix that would be needed before any $f_3$-stress coordinate is promoted from codon-topology residual to position-resolved translation readout.

\paragraph{Admissible translation statistic.}
A positive BioReality readout would require a same-organism, same-CDS, same-window table whose rows contain ordered coding-sequence coordinates, codon counts, the bounded wobble-weight surface, matched mRNA abundance, and codon-indexed or local-window ribosome-footprint occupancy.  On such a table one may form a controlled survival entry
$$
\begin{aligned}
S_{f_3}(T_{\mathrm{pos}}\mid Z)>0,
\end{aligned}
$$
where $T_{\mathrm{pos}}$ is position-resolved ribosome occupancy or an explicitly bounded translation-efficiency residual, and $Z$ includes organism, amino-acid bin or position bin, GC3, CDS length, $M$-density, matched mRNA abundance, codon-count baselines, coverage, mappability, rank sufficiency, and simpler-baseline ablation.  If this entry is non-positive, rank-degenerate, or absent, then the $f_3$ coordinate remains a finite code-read residual and not a translation readout.

\paragraph{Why the present finite rows are not enough.}
The available alternative-polyadenylation row count, $n_{\mathrm{genes}}=24740$ and $n_{\mathrm{PAS}}=296950$, confirms a large finite transcript-end table.  It does not contain the required same-window codon-indexed ribosome-occupancy rows.  The associated contacts name the necessary measurement surfaces: per-organism codon usage, tAI or stAI weights, matched mRNA abundance, and position-resolved ribosome occupancy.  Naming those surfaces is weaker than joining them into the same finite matrix.  In particular, modeled tAI or stAI weights can be used only as bounded codon-adaptation annotations; they are not observed ribosome occupancy.  Matched mRNA abundance can enter the control set or the denominator for translation-efficiency residualization; it is not itself translation.  Position-resolved ribosome occupancy is the relevant readout only after ordered CDS coordinates, local codon windows, wobble weights, mRNA controls, and position or amino-acid-bin controls are attached on the same rows.

\paragraph{Local no-promotion rule.}
The present packet allows only the following assertion.  The $f_3$-stress coordinate is a finite residual coordinate on a specified codon quotient, and it may be tested against position-resolved ribosome occupancy once a same-organism, same-CDS, same-window matrix exists.  The certificate does not claim translation realization from codon geometry, wobble-weight asymmetry, modeled adaptation weights, review routing, or notation for $S^{QT}$.  It also does not claim protein abundance, folding, structural order, physical admissibility, molecular function, system phenotype, biological mechanism, or a universal law.  Each of those stronger layers would require its own layer-matched finite contact and its own controlled survival or mediation statistic.

\paragraph{Break condition.}
The certificate fails as a BioReality statement if the prose treats the current contacts as already joined ribosome-footprint rows, treats the $24740$ gene and $296950$ polyadenylation-site counts as a translation-occupancy matrix, treats tAI or stAI weights as observed ribosome dynamics, or reads the closed $f_3$ codon geometry as protein, structure, function, mechanism, or universality.  It also fails if rank-degenerate or underpowered matrices are reported as positive survival.  The only admissible next finite test is the controlled same-window statistic $S_{f_3}(T_{\mathrm{pos}}\mid Z)$ with explicit baseline ablation and a declared null comparison.
