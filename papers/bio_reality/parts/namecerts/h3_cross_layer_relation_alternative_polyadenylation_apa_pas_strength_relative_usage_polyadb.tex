\subsection{Alternative polyadenylation as a bounded cross-layer row test}
\label{sec:bioreality-apa-pas-strength-relative-usage-polyadb}

\paragraph{Finite surface.}
The present record is a finite-row BioReality entry for alternative polyadenylation, keyed by the claim identifier \texttt{h3.cross\_layer\_relation.alternative\_polyadenylation.apa\_pas\_strength\_relative\_usage\_polyadb}.  Its directly verified external table contains $24740$ genes and $296950$ annotated polyadenylation sites.  These numbers are the only positive APA-specific row counts available in the packet, and they license a bounded statement about the size of the available PAS surface, not a mechanistic statement about translation, folding, protein output, or organismal phenotype.

\paragraph{Coordinate discipline.}
The associated coordinate memory asks whether an $f_3$-stress residual in coding windows can be compared with a position-resolved translation readout after amino-acid projection and baseline controls.  This is not an APA mechanism by itself.  The $f_3$ saturation facts record
$$
\begin{aligned}
M &= \{\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{ACA},\mathrm{ACG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\},\\
\operatorname{Sat}_{f_3}(R) &= \{\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus $M$ has $20$ listed codon-level entries, while the saturated $f_3$ image omits $\mathrm{ACA}$ and $\mathrm{ACG}$ and has $18$ listed entries.  The recorded $f_3$ boundary list is empty, so this packet does not supply a non-empty boundary-row table that could be read as a biological effect without additional rows.  The quotient geometry check gives $\pi_M$ size $10$, with $M$-vector $(20,38,26,8,1)$ and quotient vector $(10,14,6,1)$.  These are finite coordinate facts; they are not measurements of PAS choice.

\paragraph{APA readout and separation from translation.}
The APA surface contributes a large finite polyadenylation-site table: $24740$ genes and $296950$ PAS rows in the PolyADB-style usage surface.  The mathematically admissible claim is therefore narrow: the APA table can serve as a downstream readout candidate only after it is joined on compatible gene or transcript identifiers to the relevant coding-window residuals and after PAS-strength or relative-usage statistics are residualized against appropriate covariates.  The packet currently provides no same-row matrix linking PAS strength or PAS relative usage to $f_3$-stress windows, local codon counts, wobble weights, matched mRNA abundance, and position-resolved ribosome occupancy.

\paragraph{Control requirements.}
A positive cross-layer statement would require a finite design matrix whose rows simultaneously contain organism, coding sequence or transcript identifier, local codon-window coordinates, amino-acid-bin or position-bin controls, GC3, CDS length, $M$-density, matched mRNA abundance, PAS strength or relative PAS usage, and a position-resolved ribosome-occupancy or translation-efficiency residual when translation mediation is invoked.  The survival entry would have to remain non-vacuous after rank checking, simpler-baseline ablation, and null comparison.  In schematic form, the admissible statistic has the form
$$
\begin{aligned}
S_{f_3}(T_{\mathrm{pos}} \mid Z) &> 0,\\
Z &= \{\mathrm{organism},\mathrm{amino\ acid\ composition},\mathrm{GC3},\mathrm{length},M\text{-}\mathrm{density},\mathrm{mRNA},\mathrm{position/bin\ controls}\},
\end{aligned}
$$
where $T_{\mathrm{pos}}$ is a measured or explicitly bounded translation readout.  The present APA row count does not compute this statistic.

\paragraph{Evidence already in memory.}
A related structural-order run is useful only as a calibration warning.  In that run, the primary ramp setting used $50$ positions, $5$ folds, and a minimum joined count of $300$.  The recorded weighted held-out increments were $0.0011165640103326878$ for the $f_3$ ramp and $-0.00026157313923957926$ for the body, with a ramp-minus-body difference of $0.001378137149572267$.  The sign-flip probabilities were $0.0478515625$ for the ramp, $0.54296875$ for the body, and $0.05859375$ for the paired ramp-minus-body contrast.  The verdict there was ramp-positive but not localized.  Those values do not prove an APA relation; they show why any positive cross-layer readout must pass a local, controlled, nondegenerate design rather than relying on coordinate geometry alone.

\paragraph{Local no-promotion rule.}
From the present finite rows one may say that an APA/PAS table of $24740$ genes and $296950$ PAS entries is available, and that the relevant codon-coordinate facts are finite and explicitly enumerated.  One may not conclude that $f_3$-stress affects translation, APA choice, protein abundance, folding, structural order, physical admissibility, molecular function, mechanism, phenotype, or a universal biological rule.  Those conclusions would require separate layer-matched contacts and computed statistics.  In particular, modeled tAI or stAI weights are not ribosome-footprint measurements, a position-resolved ribosome-occupancy contact name is not a joined footprint matrix, and PAS row availability is not evidence of translation mediation unless the same-row controlled statistic is actually present.

\paragraph{Resulting status.}
The chapter is therefore a bounded finite-row registration rather than a positive biological mechanism.  The APA claim is ready to state as a row-surface and boundary condition: the PolyADB-style PAS table is large enough to define a candidate readout surface, while the current packet still lacks the joined coding-window, wobble-weight, mRNA-control, translation-readout, and APA-usage matrix needed for a survival claim.  Any future positive statement must be expressed as a controlled finite statistic over that joined matrix, with rank sufficiency and simpler-baseline failure modes reported alongside the coefficient or score.
