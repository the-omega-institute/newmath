\paragraph{Admissible statistic.}
For a window $w$ in coding sequence $c$ of organism $o$, let $Q_{o,c,w}$ denote the amino-acid-quotient-surviving $f_3$ residual coordinate, let $T_{o,c,w}$ denote the position-resolved ribosome-occupancy or explicitly bounded translation-efficiency readout, and let $Z_{o,c,w}$ collect the mandatory controls.  The only admissible survival entry has the form
$$\begin{aligned}
S_{f_3}(T\mid Z) &= \Delta \operatorname{score}\bigl(T_{o,c,w}; Q_{o,c,w}, Z_{o,c,w}\bigr),\\
Z_{o,c,w} &= \{\mathrm{organism},\mathrm{AA\ composition},\mathrm{GC3},\mathrm{CDS\ length},M\text{-}\mathrm{density},\mathrm{mRNA},\mathrm{position\ or\ AA\ bin}\}.
\end{aligned}$$
The statistic must remain non-vacuous after rank checks and after ablation against simpler codon-count, composition, and position baselines.  If the joined matrix is rank-degenerate, if the held-out improvement is non-positive, or if the signal disappears under the baseline ablation, the row returns a null or needs-data result rather than a translation claim.
