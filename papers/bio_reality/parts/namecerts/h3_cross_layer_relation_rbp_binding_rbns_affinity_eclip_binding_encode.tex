\subsection{Translation-conditioned RNA half-life and a bounded RBP bridge}\label{subsec:bioreality-q6-rna-half-life-rbp-bridge}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite carrier.}
The record considered here is a finite matched panel of transcript or CDS rows.  Each row carries a synonymous residual coordinate label of type $B^{*}_{Q6}$ after quotienting by amino-acid identity, a same-row translation readout or explicitly declared translation boundary, an RNA half-life readout, measured occupancy information for a prespecified decay-factor RNA-binding protein when available, and matched controls for transcription, abundance, local composition, and window context.  The scientific question is not whether a codon-window coordinate can be promoted into an RNA-decay mechanism by itself.  The question is whether a coordinate-associated half-life residual remains after the required controls and is accompanied by the experimentally separated RBP pattern: local occupancy differs at the editable RNA window, target-RBP loss removes the effect, wild-type target-RBP rescue restores it, and binding-defective or non-target controls fail to restore it.

\paragraph{Observed finite rows.}
The finite RBP binding contact presently attached to this claim is the RBNS/eCLIP/ENCODE-style binding surface recorded under the claim identifier $\mathrm{h3.cross\_layer\_relation.rbp\_binding.rbns\_affinity\_eclip\_binding\_encode}$.  Its verified run is $\mathrm{b225890251c2}$, with $900$ total peak rows and $3$ cross-RBP concordant rows, computed in $223.648043$ seconds at timestamp $2026$-$06$-$22\,19{:}44{:}29+00{:}00$.  Thus the concrete finite statistic available for the RBP contact is
$$
\begin{aligned}
N_{\mathrm{peak}} &= 900,\\
N_{\mathrm{concordant}} &= 3,\\
\widehat h_{\mathrm{RBP}} &= \frac{3}{900}=\frac{1}{300}\approx 0.003333.
\end{aligned}
$$
This number is a binding-surface concordance statistic only.  It is not a same-window RNA half-life perturbation statistic and it is not a rescue statistic.  The same audit bundle also contains two comparison records from other cross-layer contacts: a methylation flanking-context run $\mathrm{bd781331dc42}$ with $20{,}000$ probes, $656$ individuals, and baseline $R^{2}=0.0129735$, and a meiotic recombination contact run $\mathrm{e57a09da690c}$ with $52{,}963$ hotspot rows and runtime $168.443$ seconds.  These linked finite counts show that the present chapter is being handled as a row-bounded empirical record, but they do not supply RNA half-life, RBP loss, or rescue evidence for the present RBP-bridge claim.

\paragraph{Controlled null.}
The null model keeps high-coordinate and low-coordinate synonymous residual labels exchangeable with respect to RNA half-life once transcription rate, local RNA structure, motif density, GC content, dinucleotide composition, codon-pair context, splice or isoform state, transcript abundance, batch, amino-acid composition, and the same-row translation readout have been conditioned.  In symbols, if $q\in\{\mathrm{high},\mathrm{low}\}$ denotes the residual coordinate label and $H$ denotes the RNA half-life readout, then the admissible null is the row-finite conditional exchangeability assertion
$$
\begin{aligned}
H \perp q \;\mid\; Z_{\mathrm{tx}},Z_{\mathrm{struct}},Z_{\mathrm{motif}},Z_{\mathrm{comp}},Z_{\mathrm{iso}},Z_{\mathrm{abund}},Z_{\mathrm{batch}},Z_{\mathrm{aa}},T,
\end{aligned}
$$
where $T$ is the same-row translation readout and the $Z$ terms name the declared control blocks.  A half-life difference that survives this conditioning is still only an RNA-stability association unless the RBP bridge is separately observed in the same organism and same window.

\paragraph{RBP bridge criterion.}
A positive bridge requires more than the $3$ concordant binding rows among $900$ peak rows.  It requires a finite joined table in which the same coordinate window has measured occupancy of a prespecified decay-factor RBP, an occupancy contrast decoupled from coordinate discovery, target-RBP loss, wild-type rescue, and binding-defective or non-target rescue controls.  The bridge statistic is therefore a conjunction of four row-level tests: an RNA half-life residual, a local occupancy shift, collapse under target-RBP loss, and return under wild-type rescue with failure under the negative rescue arms.  If any one of these four surfaces is absent, the chapter may report a bounded RNA half-life boundary or a bounded RBP binding contact, but it may not report an RNA-decay mechanism.

\paragraph{Current finite verdict.}
The present data are sufficient to record a finite RBP binding contact and to state the required RNA half-life/RBP/rescue design, but they are not sufficient to assert a positive translation-conditioned RNA half-life RBP bridge.  The observed hit fraction $1/300$ is a real finite-row scalar for the binding contact; it is not a measured local decay-factor occupancy response inside a matched RNA half-life panel.  No protein output, folded structure, physical admissibility, biological function, phenotype, adaptation, causal mechanism, or universal sequence law follows from this chapter.  Those claims require separate layer-matched contacts and their own finite statistics.  Likewise, same-row translation conditioning prevents promotion from RNA half-life alone to translation realization: the translation readout is a boundary condition for the row model, not evidence that the coordinate has been realized as protein abundance or protein state.

\paragraph{Admissible readback.}
The only admissible readback at this stage is negative-to-bounded: the record supports a finite binding-contact summary with $900$ peak rows and $3$ cross-RBP concordant rows, together with a precise design requirement for a future same-window RNA half-life/RBP perturbation panel.  A later positive claim would have to attach the missing joined rows and report the declared finite statistic under the full control set.  Until then, the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ coordinate remains a controlled synonymous residual descriptor, the RNA half-life surface remains an RNA-stability boundary, and the RBP binding surface remains a binding-contact boundary rather than a demonstrated decay bridge.
