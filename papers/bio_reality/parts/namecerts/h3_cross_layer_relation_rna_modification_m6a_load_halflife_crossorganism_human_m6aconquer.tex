\subsection{A Translation-Bounded $m^6A$ Load and RNA Half-Life Relation}
\label{subsec:bioreality-m6a-load-half-life-crossorganism-human-m6aconquer}

\paragraph{Finite record.}
The record concerns synonymously editable coding-RNA windows whose rows are required to carry coordinate labels, a same-row translation readout, an RNA half-life readout, measured local occupancy of a prespecified decay-factor RNA-binding protein, target-RBP perturbation, wild-type rescue, binding-defective or non-target controls, transcription-rate controls, mRNA-abundance controls, isoform state, motif-density covariates, and local-structure covariates. The human $m^6A$-load contact used here is finite at the level of the joined evidence table: the checked run retained $15{,}845$ genes and $135{,}300$ sites, used $1000$ permutations, completed in $338.238$ seconds, and returned $3$ sign-concordant organism or panel-level directions for the registered cross-organism human $m^6A$Conquer boundary statistic. These numbers are treated as row counts and finite-run diagnostics, not as a license to identify a molecular mechanism.

\paragraph{Registered contrast.}
Each admissible row is read through a bounded contrast between high-coordinate and low-coordinate synonymous labels after amino-acid composition control, with the comparison further stratified by measured local decay-factor RBP occupancy. The intended statistic separates four features that would otherwise be conflated: coordinate label, RNA half-life, local RBP occupancy, and the translation-readout boundary. In schematic form, the row-level admissibility condition is
$$
\begin{aligned}
  r_i = (&q_i, T_i, H_i, O_i, C_i, S_i, D_i, G_i, K_i, I_i, A_i, B_i, P_i, W_i),
\end{aligned}
$$
where $q_i$ is the synonymous coordinate label, $T_i$ is the matched translation readout, $H_i$ is RNA half-life, $O_i$ is measured local decay-factor RBP occupancy, $C_i$ records transcription-rate and mRNA controls, $S_i$ records local RNA structure, $D_i$ records motif density, $G_i$ records GC and dinucleotide composition, $K_i$ records codon-pair context, $I_i$ records splice or isoform state, $A_i$ records transcript abundance, $B_i$ records batch, $P_i$ records perturbation state, and $W_i$ records rescue or control state. A positive reading is allowed only if the half-life contrast follows the measured occupancy contrast, weakens or disappears under target-RBP loss, returns under wild-type rescue, and is not reproduced by binding-defective rescue or by a non-target control.

\paragraph{Boundary statistic.}
The finite statistic is not simply an association between $m^6A$ burden and half-life. It asks whether coordinate-associated RNA half-life variation remains attached to a prespecified RBP-occupancy bridge while the translation readout is present as a same-row boundary. The admissible direction can be summarized as
$$
\begin{aligned}
  \Delta H(q_{\mathrm{high}},q_{\mathrm{low}}) &\neq 0, \\
  \Delta O(q_{\mathrm{high}},q_{\mathrm{low}}) &\neq 0, \\
  \Delta H \mid \mathrm{RBP\ loss} &\approx 0, \\
  \Delta H \mid \mathrm{wild\ type\ rescue} &\approx \Delta H_{\mathrm{baseline}}, \\
  \Delta H \mid \mathrm{binding\ defective\ rescue} &\approx 0.
\end{aligned}
$$
This is a finite licensing rule for a bounded RNA-stability relation. It does not say that the coordinate causes translation, protein output, folding, function, phenotype, adaptation, or a universal biological law. The translation readout is included to prevent promotion of an RNA-stability observation into a translation claim; it is a boundary condition, not the result being asserted.

\paragraph{Empirical anchors.}
The main finite anchor is the human $m^6A$Conquer run with $15{,}845$ genes, $135{,}300$ sites, $1000$ permutations, $3$ sign-concordant directions, and runtime $338.238$ seconds. A related same-theme $m^6A$ site-load and mRNA half-life boundary run retained $5837$ genes and reported static Spearman correlation $-0.388929$ for $m^6A$ load against DMSO log half-life, causal Spearman correlation $0.245804$ for $m^6A$ load against $\Delta\log_2$ half-life, length-partial Spearman correlation $0.227683$, and two-sided partial-permutation value $0.000999001$ under the stated coverage rule. The transcript-surveillance comparison retained $38{,}576$ rows, used $1000$ permutations, and ran in $55.602$ seconds. The ARE-deletion mRNA-stability boundary supplied a separate stability-control comparison with $226$ BEAS-2B rows, $2242$ Jurkat rows, $1000$ actual permutations, $2000$ grading permutations, corrected pooled label permutation over deletions, and sign concordance. These companion facts are not merged into a single biological mechanism; they only show that the paper is using finite RNA-stability contacts rather than unrestricted biological generalization.

\paragraph{Refusal conditions.}
The relation fails if high-coordinate and low-coordinate labels become exchangeable for RNA half-life after transcription rate, local RNA structure, motif density, GC and dinucleotide composition, codon-pair context, splice or isoform state, transcript abundance, and batch are controlled. It also fails if RNA half-life changes without a measured local RBP-occupancy change, if the target-RBP perturbation does not collapse the effect, if wild-type rescue does not restore it, or if binding-defective rescue, non-target controls, structure, motif density, transcription, isoform stability, or batch reproduce the same pattern. In those cases the observation may remain a finite RNA-stability association, but it is not an RBP-bridged cross-layer relation.

\paragraph{Local cannot-claim rule.}
No translation realization follows from RNA half-life alone. No protein abundance, folded structure, physical admissibility, biological function, system phenotype, adaptation, causation, mechanism, or universality follows from the coordinate label, the $m^6A$ load table, the half-life readout, or the presence of a translation boundary. Such claims require their own layer-matched contacts and their own finite statistics. The present chapter therefore licenses only a bounded statement: within the finite matched rows, a coordinate-associated RNA half-life readout may be read as an RBP-bridged RNA-stability relation exactly when the measured occupancy, perturbation, rescue, control, transcriptional, structural, compositional, isoform, abundance, batch, and translation-boundary requirements are simultaneously satisfied.
