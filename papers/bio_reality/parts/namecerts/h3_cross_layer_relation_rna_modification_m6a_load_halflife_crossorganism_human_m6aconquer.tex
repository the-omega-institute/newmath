\subsection{A bounded m6A-load and RNA half-life certificate}
\label{sec:bioreality-m6a-load-half-life-crossorganism-human-m6aconquer}

\paragraph{Finite panel.}
The record concerns a finite panel of coding-RNA rows in which the biological coordinate is not a protein-level object but an RNA-level annotation: a measured m6A site-load or site-count signal is paired with an RNA half-life readout after identifier matching.  The row universe is therefore a finite set of transcript or gene identifiers, not an unrestricted class of cellular processes.  In the human m6AConquer-linked run, the retained panel contains $15845$ genes and $135300$ m6A sites.  The finite computation used $1000$ permutations, completed in $338.238$ seconds, and reported $3$ sign-concordant organism or condition summaries under run identifier \texttt{d7949df4a751}.  These numbers are part of the certificate: the claim is only about the matched rows that survived the stated joins and filters.

\paragraph{Readout relation.}
For each retained row $i$, the admissible data have the form
$$
\begin{aligned}
  x_i &= \text{m6A load or local m6A-site burden},\\
  h_i &= \text{RNA half-life readout},\\
  z_i &= \text{available transcript-level controls and join metadata}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The empirical relation being licensed is the bounded statement that variation in $x_i$ is evaluated against $h_i$ on the same finite joined surface.  A positive row-level association is not promoted to a translation claim.  It is also not promoted to a decay-factor RBP mechanism unless the same rows contain measured local occupancy of a prespecified decay-factor RBP, coordinate--occupancy separation, target-RBP loss, wild-type rescue, and binding-defective or non-target controls.  The present m6AConquer finite-row certificate records an RNA half-life boundary, not that stronger mechanistic bridge.

\paragraph{Comparison anchor.}
A second m6A half-life boundary run gives the scale of a more explicit same-dataset statistic.  In run \texttt{7cd189f15c4b}, $5837$ genes passed the coverage rule \texttt{min(Mean T coverage DMSO, STM2457) >= 20th percentile among valid joined genes}.  The static Spearman relation between m6A load and DMSO log half-life was $-0.388929$.  The perturbation-oriented statistic had causal Spearman value $0.245804$, length-partial Spearman value $0.227683$, and a two-sided partial-permutation value $0.000999001$.  Its verdict was \texttt{crosses\_boundary}, with the stated semantics that the dataset supports the pre-registered boundary statistic and does not by itself assert mechanism or function.  This comparison is used here only to calibrate the kind of finite RNA-stability evidence that the m6AConquer record can enter; it does not import any broader biological conclusion.

\paragraph{Licensed statement.}
The chapter-specific assertion is the following finite implication.  If a matched m6A-load panel supplies the RNA half-life rows described above, then it may support a bounded cross-layer RNA-stability relation exactly at the RNA-readout surface:
$$
\begin{aligned}
  \{(x_i,h_i,z_i) : i \in I\}
  \quad &\leadsto \quad
  \text{finite m6A-load/RNA-half-life association},\\
  |I|_{\mathrm{human}} &= 15845\ \text{genes},\\
  N_{\mathrm{sites}} &= 135300,\\
  N_{\mathrm{perm}} &= 1000.
\end{aligned}
$$
The sign-concordant count $3$ is read as a finite concordance statistic over the declared summaries, not as a universal rule about m6A biology.  The row counts and permutation count delimit the computation; they do not license extrapolation to unobserved organisms, unmeasured isoforms, or unjoined regulatory states.

\paragraph{Refusal conditions.}
The claim fails as an RBP-mediated decay bridge if the high- and low-coordinate labels are exchangeable for RNA half-life after transcription-rate, local RNA-structure, motif-density, GC and dinucleotide composition, codon-pair context, splice or isoform state, transcript abundance, and batch controls.  It also fails as a mechanism claim if RNA half-life differs but measured decay-factor RBP occupancy is absent or unchanged; if target-RBP loss does not collapse the effect; if wild-type rescue does not restore it; or if binding-defective rescue, non-target controls, structure, motif density, transcription, isoform stability, or batch reproduce the pattern.  The same-row translation readout, when present, is a boundary condition preventing layer confusion; it is not itself evidence that RNA half-life has become translation realization.

\paragraph{Local claim boundary.}
No translation output, protein abundance, folding, physical admissibility, biological function, phenotype, adaptation, causation, RNA-decay mechanism, or universal biological law follows from these finite RNA half-life rows.  Such conclusions require distinct layer-matched contacts with their own rows and statistics.  The present record is therefore a bounded empirical certificate for a m6A-load/RNA-half-life relation on the stated finite panels, with the RBP-occupancy and perturbation/rescue requirements kept outside the positive claim until those measurements are attached on the same matched window rows.
