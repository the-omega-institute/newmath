\subsection{Human m6A Site Load and the RNA Half-Life Boundary}
\label{sec:bioreality-human-m6a-load-halflife-m6aconquer}

\paragraph{Finite panel.}
This record concerns a finite human coding-RNA panel in which the coordinate side is an m6A site-load label and the readout side is an RNA half-life measurement.  The working rows are gene-level or transcript-level objects joined to curated m6A site annotations, with the declared statistic restricted to whether the sign of the m6A-load association with RNA half-life is reproducible across the available organism-scoped or dataset-scoped contrasts.  In the m6Aconquer human run, the finite surface contains $15845$ genes and $135300$ m6A sites.  The null was evaluated with $1000$ permutations, and $3$ eligible contrasts were sign-concordant.  The recorded runtime was $338.238$ seconds.  These numbers are part of the finite row certificate, not a claim that the same rows already identify an RNA-binding-protein mechanism.

\paragraph{Readout equation.}
For each retained row $i$, let $L_i$ denote the m6A-load coordinate, let $H_i$ denote the RNA half-life readout, and let $Z_i$ collect the available nuisance controls.  The boundary statistic is only a finite association test of the form
$$
\begin{aligned}
  H_i &= \alpha + \beta L_i + \gamma^{\mathsf T} Z_i + \varepsilon_i,\\
  \operatorname{sgn}(\widehat\beta)&\text{ is compared across the declared finite contrasts.}
\end{aligned}
$$
The supporting m6A half-life contact from the GSE163500 boundary run gives an independent calibration point for the same kind of layer boundary.  In that run, $5837$ genes passed the stated coverage rule, the static Spearman association between m6A load and DMSO half-life was $-0.388929$, the causal Spearman association between m6A-load change and half-life change was $0.245804$, the length-partial Spearman value was $0.227683$, and the two-sided partial-permutation value was $0.000999001$.  The coverage rule was $\min(\mathrm{Mean\ T\ coverage\ DMSO},\mathrm{STM2457})$ at least the twentieth percentile among valid joined genes.  This companion fact is used only to locate the kind of readout being measured: m6A load can cross an RNA half-life boundary under explicit finite controls, but this does not by itself supply downstream biological interpretation.

\paragraph{Licensed interpretation.}
The human m6Aconquer row set licenses a bounded cross-layer statement: in the finite joined panel, m6A site-load labels and RNA half-life readouts are close enough to define a reproducible RNA-stability boundary statistic.  The positive content is deliberately narrow.  It says that an RNA modification coordinate can be read against an RNA decay-time surface, and that the finite computation has enough rows and permutation structure to report a sign-concordance count.  It does not say that the coordinate is translated into protein output, nor that it determines folding, cellular function, phenotype, selection, adaptation, or a universal biological law.

\paragraph{RBP bridge refusal.}
A stronger decay-factor RBP bridge would require same-window measured occupancy for a prespecified decay-factor RNA-binding protein, coordinate--occupancy decoupling, target-RBP loss, wild-type rescue, binding-defective rescue or non-target controls, and matched same-row translation and mRNA controls.  Those fields are not present in the verified m6Aconquer fact.  Therefore the result remains an RNA half-life boundary rather than an RBP-mechanism claim.  If one writes $O_i$ for measured local decay-factor RBP occupancy and $P_i$ for perturbation or rescue state, the stronger bridge would require a nondegenerate finite table of the form
$$
\begin{aligned}
  (L_i,H_i,O_i,P_i,T_i,A_i,S_i,B_i)_{i=1}^n,
\end{aligned}
$$
where $T_i$ is a same-row translation readout, $A_i$ is matched mRNA abundance or transcription-rate control, $S_i$ records local structure and motif context, and $B_i$ records batch or isoform state.  The present fact supplies $L_i$ and $H_i$ at finite scale, but not the occupancy, perturbation, rescue, and translation-boundary columns needed for that stronger claim.

\paragraph{Cannot-claim boundary.}
No translation realization follows from RNA half-life alone.  No protein abundance, folded structure, physical admissibility, biochemical function, system phenotype, mechanism, causation, adaptation, or general law follows from the m6A-load statistic.  A translation statement would need a separate same-row translation-efficiency or ribosome-profiling contact.  A protein-output statement would need matched protein abundance.  A folding or physical-admissibility statement would need its own structure or physical assay.  A decay-factor RBP mechanism would need measured occupancy and perturbation-rescue rows.  Without those additional contacts, the finite result is exactly an m6A-load/RNA-half-life boundary with $15845$ genes, $135300$ annotated sites, $1000$ permutations, and $3$ sign-concordant contrasts.
