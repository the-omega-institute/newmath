\subsection{Human m$^6$A Load and Half-Life in a Cross-Organism Panel}

\paragraph{Finite-row record.}
This chapter records a bounded observational panel for the human m$^6$A load and half-life claim across synonymously editable coding RNA windows. The measured panel contains $15845$ genes and $135300$ sites, and the permutation check was run with $1000$ shuffles. Only $3$ rows were sign-concordant, and the runtime was $338.238\ \mathrm{s}$. In compact form,
$$\begin{aligned}
 n_{\mathrm{genes}} &= 15845, \\
 n_{\mathrm{sites}} &= 135300, \\
 n_{\mathrm{perm}} &= 1000, \\
 n_{\mathrm{sign}} &= 3, \\
 t_{\mathrm{run}} &= 338.238\ \mathrm{s}.
\end{aligned}$$
The row count fixes a finite empirical witness for the claim, but only at the level of observed association on the stated panel. It does not, by itself, identify translation, folding, physical admissibility, function, mechanism, or any universal rule.

\paragraph{Interpretive scope.}
The relation is read only as a cross-layer association between coding-window coordinates and RNA half-life under the attached control structure. That means the result is a local observational statement, not a mechanistic explanation. A stronger reading would require a separate contact that directly measures the missing layer on the same finite row structure, such as a translation readout, a protein-state assay, or a perturbation-rescue panel. Without that extra contact, the panel remains confined to the recorded RNA modification and half-life surfaces, and no inference to protein output, folded structure, physical admissibility, function, mechanism, or general validity is licensed.

\paragraph{Nearby boundary contact.}
A separate half-life boundary panel in the same observation family, keyed to $5837$ genes, reported a causal Spearman value of $0.245804$, a length-partial Spearman value of $0.227683$, a static Spearman value of $-0.388929$, and a permutation $p$-value of $0.000999001$. Written as a compact summary,
$$\begin{aligned}
 n_{\mathrm{genes}} &= 5837, \\
 \rho_{\mathrm{causal}} &= 0.245804, \\
 \rho_{\mathrm{length\text{-}partial}} &= 0.227683, \\
 \rho_{\mathrm{static}} &= -0.388929, \\
 p &= 0.000999001.
\end{aligned}$$
That nearby contact sharpens the empirical neighborhood of the claim, but it still stays at the level of a boundary statistic. It describes how m$^6$A load co-varies with half-life under the stated controls; it does not explain why the relation exists or convert it into a statement about downstream molecular state.

\paragraph{Boundary statement.}
The correct reading is narrow and disciplined. The present finite panel supports a locally grounded observational relation between m$^6$A load and half-life in the human cross-organism setting, with the explicit counts above. It does not, on its own, establish translation, protein folding, physical admissibility, function, mechanism, or any universal law. Those layers remain outside the claim until a separate contact at that layer is attached and checked on the same finite row structure.
