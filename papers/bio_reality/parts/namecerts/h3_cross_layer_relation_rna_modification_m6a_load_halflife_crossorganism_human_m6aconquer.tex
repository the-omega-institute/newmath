\subsection{m6A load and half-life as a bounded cross-organism readout}\label{subsec:m6a-load-halflife-crossorganism}

\paragraph{Finite witness.} The human cross-organism panel for this claim is explicitly finite. The retained summary records $n_{\mathrm{genes}}=15845$, $n_{\mathrm{sites}}=135300$, a permutation budget of $1000$, a sign-concordant count of $3$, and a runtime of $338.238\ \mathrm{s}$. In the companion half-life boundary panel, the retained summary over $5837$ genes reports a static Spearman correlation of $-0.388929$, a causal Spearman of $0.245804$, a length-partial Spearman of $0.227683$, and a two-sided permutation value of $0.000999001$, with the panel verdict recorded as crosses\_boundary. The resulting witness is concrete enough to support a narrow statement about load-to-half-life association, and nothing broader.

\paragraph{Observable pattern.} $$\begin{aligned}
 n_{\mathrm{genes}} &= 15845, \qquad n_{\mathrm{sites}} = 135300, \qquad \text{permutation budget} = 1000, \\
 \text{sign-concordant count} &= 3, \qquad \text{runtime} = 338.238\ \mathrm{s}.
\end{aligned}$$ A second finite row set gives $$\begin{aligned}
 n_{\mathrm{genes}} &= 5837, \qquad \rho_{\mathrm{static}} = -0.388929, \qquad \rho_{\mathrm{causal}} = 0.245804, \\
 \rho_{\mathrm{length\text{-}partial}} &= 0.227683, \qquad p_{\mathrm{perm}} = 0.000999001.
\end{aligned}$$ Taken together, these rows say that m6A load and RNA half-life are not independent bookkeeping fields in the panel. The sign pattern is stable enough to survive the finite permutation budget, and the smaller half-life panel still leaves a residual relationship after the length adjustment recorded in the summary.

\paragraph{Scope of the claim.} That is the full extent of the claim made here. The chapter records a bounded external readout: m6A load tracks RNA half-life in the specific finite panels above, under the recorded control structure. It does not elevate that relation into translation, folded structure, physical admissibility, function, mechanism, or universality. It also does not treat the permutation result as a license to erase transcript abundance, batch, gene identity, or the other measured covariates that sit inside the panel design. Those layers remain separate and would require their own contact with their own rows before any stronger statement could be made.

\paragraph{Interpretive boundary.} The safe reading is therefore narrow and local. The evidence supports a finite, data-bound association between m6A load and RNA half-life in the observed panels, with the larger human cross-organism packet carrying $15845$ genes and $135300$ sites and the boundary packet carrying $5837$ genes and $p_{\mathrm{perm}}=0.000999001$. No conclusion about protein output, translated realization, stable folding, physical admissibility, biological function, or a general rule of nature follows from this chapter alone. Those claims sit beyond the present witness and require a separate measured contact at the relevant layer.
