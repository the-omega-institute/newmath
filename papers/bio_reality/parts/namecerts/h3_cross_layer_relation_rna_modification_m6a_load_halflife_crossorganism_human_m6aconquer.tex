\subsection{Cross-organism m6A load and RNA half-life boundary}
\label{sec:bioreality-m6a-load-halflife-crossorganism-human-m6aconquer}

\paragraph{Finite panel.}
The record concerns a finite panel of human coding-RNA and transcript-level rows in which an m6A site-load coordinate is compared with an RNA half-life readout.  The principal finite run retained $15845$ genes and $135300$ m6A sites, used $1000$ permutations, and reported sign concordance in $3$ organism-scoped or dataset-scoped comparisons.  The role of these rows is deliberately narrow: they let the paper ask whether an m6A-load descriptor is reproducibly aligned with RNA half-life under the available joined identifiers.  They do not by themselves supply local decay-factor RBP occupancy, synonymous editing, target-RBP perturbation, wild-type rescue, binding-defective rescue, or non-target rescue.  Thus the panel is suitable for a bounded RNA-stability boundary and not for a decay-factor mechanism claim.

\paragraph{Observed statistic.}
The verified cross-organism run is recorded as experiment $d7949df4a751$.  Its scalar evidence is
$$
\begin{aligned}
  n_{\mathrm{genes}} &= 15845,\\
  n_{\mathrm{sites}} &= 135300,\\
  B_{\mathrm{perm}} &= 1000,\\
  n_{\mathrm{sign\ concordant}} &= 3.
\end{aligned}
$$
These numbers license the following statement and no stronger one: within the finite joined matrix available to the run, the m6A-load coordinate has a reproducible direction of association with RNA half-life across the checked comparison surfaces.  The result is a reality-facing association because the half-life values and m6A site calls are external measurements joined through finite gene or transcript keys.  It is not a proof that m6A load causes decay, that translation realizes the coordinate, or that a specific RBP executes the effect.

\paragraph{Same-layer comparison.}
A second finite RNA-modification run supplies a useful calibration point for the boundary.  In experiment $7cd189f15c4b$, the joined m6A and half-life matrix retained $5837$ genes after the stated coverage rule.  The static DMSO association between m6A load and log half-life was $-0.388929$ by Spearman correlation, while the perturbation-oriented comparison gave a Spearman value of $0.245804$ for m6A-load change against $\Delta\log_2$ half-life.  The length-partial value was $0.227683$, and the two-sided partial permutation value was $0.000999001$.  The local meaning of this comparison is that m6A-to-half-life contact can be made finite, signed, and statistically nontrivial when a declared coverage and control rule is applied.  It still remains an RNA half-life readout, not a translation, protein, structure, function, or mechanism readout.

\paragraph{Coordinate and readout separation.}
The coordinate side of the claim is a finite m6A-load or synonymous-window label.  The readout side is RNA half-life, with mRNA abundance, transcriptional state, isoform state, local sequence composition, local structure, motif density, codon-pair context, batch, and translation readout treated as required separators before any cross-layer interpretation is attempted.  In this chapter the verified rows establish only the m6A-load--half-life boundary.  They do not contain the stronger same-window panel that would be needed to assert a decay-factor RBP bridge: measured local RBP occupancy, coordinate--occupancy decoupling, target-RBP loss, wild-type rescue, binding-defective rescue, and non-target controls are absent from the verified scalar payload.

\paragraph{Break rule.}
The claim breaks if the m6A-load label is interpreted as protein output, translation realization, folded structure, physical admissibility, biological function, adaptation, causation, or a universal biological law.  It also breaks if an RNA half-life association is promoted to an RBP-mediated mechanism without the missing occupancy and perturbation rows.  A positive RBP bridge would require a finite matched panel in which the half-life shift follows measured local occupancy of a prespecified decay-factor RBP, collapses under target-RBP loss, returns under wild-type rescue, and fails to return under binding-defective or non-target controls while transcription, abundance, isoform, structure, motif, composition, codon-pair, translation, and batch alternatives remain separated.

\paragraph{Chapter verdict.}
The finite evidence is sufficient for a bounded BioReality statement: m6A site load can be recorded as a finite coordinate whose association with RNA half-life is externally checked in the cited human m6AConquer-oriented run, with $15845$ genes, $135300$ sites, $1000$ permutations, and $3$ concordant signed comparisons.  The independent m6A half-life boundary run, with $5837$ genes and partial permutation value $0.000999001$, supports the same local discipline: RNA-stability contact is real at the level of finite joined rows, but all higher biological layers remain closed unless their own matched measurements and controls are attached.
