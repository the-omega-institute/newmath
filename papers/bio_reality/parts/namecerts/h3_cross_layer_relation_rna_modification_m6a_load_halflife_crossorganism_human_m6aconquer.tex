\subsection{m6A Load, RNA Half-Life, and the Translation Boundary}
\label{sec:bioreality-m6a-load-halflife-crossorganism-human-m6aconquer}

\paragraph{Finite panel.}
This record concerns a finite panel of coding-RNA windows in which synonymous coordinate labels are compared only after the amino-acid content has already been treated as a control surface.  The biological readout is RNA half-life, not protein output.  The intended higher-resolution carrier would contain, in each row, a coordinate label, a same-row translation readout, an RNA half-life value, measured local occupancy of a prespecified decay-factor RNA-binding protein, transcription rate, local RNA structure, motif density, GC and dinucleotide composition, codon-pair context, splice or isoform state, transcript abundance, batch, perturbation state, rescue state, and a declared failure condition.  The available finite evidence reaches the RNA-stability boundary but does not contain the full occupancy, perturbation, and rescue panel needed for a decay-factor RBP mechanism claim.

\paragraph{Observed finite counts.}
The human m6AConquer-linked run for the cross-organism m6A-load and half-life record used experiment run $\mathrm{d7949df4a751}$.  It retained $15{,}845$ genes and $135{,}300$ m6A sites, used $1{,}000$ permutations, and reported $3$ sign-concordant organism-level or dataset-level directions, with runtime $338.238$ seconds.  In finite-row notation, the observed support surface for this chapter is therefore summarized as
$$
\begin{aligned}
N_{\mathrm{genes}} &= 15{,}845,\\
N_{\mathrm{sites}} &= 135{,}300,\\
N_{\mathrm{perm}} &= 1{,}000,\\
N_{\mathrm{sign\ concordant}} &= 3.
\end{aligned}
$$
A separate human m6A half-life boundary run, experiment $\mathrm{7cd189f15c4b}$, supplies a more explicit same-layer stability statistic: $5{,}837$ genes passed the stated coverage rule, the static Spearman association between m6A load and DMSO log half-life was $-0.388929$, the causal Spearman association between m6A-load change and half-life change was $0.245804$, the length-partial Spearman association was $0.227683$, and the two-sided partial permutation value was $0.000999001$.  These values justify treating m6A load as a finite RNA half-life boundary signal under the declared readout, not as a direct statement about translation or protein state.

\paragraph{Boundary statistic.}
The chapter-level relation is the following bounded implication: if high-coordinate and low-coordinate synonymous labels remain separated for RNA half-life after transcription rate, local RNA structure, motif density, GC and dinucleotide composition, codon-pair context, splice or isoform state, transcript abundance, and batch are controlled, then the coordinate may be retained as a finite descriptor of RNA-stability association.  It may be promoted to an RBP-mediated cross-layer relation only if the same rows also contain measured local occupancy of the prespecified decay-factor RBP, coordinate--occupancy decoupling, target-RBP loss, wild-type rescue, binding-defective rescue or non-target control behavior, and the same-row translation readout used only as a boundary condition.  The intended controlled contrast has the form
$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta H &= H(\mathrm{high\ coordinate}) - H(\mathrm{low\ coordinate}),\\
\Delta O &= O(\mathrm{high\ coordinate}) - O(\mathrm{low\ coordinate}),\\
\Delta H_{\mathrm{loss}} &\approx 0 \quad \text{under target-RBP loss},\\
\Delta H_{\mathrm{rescue}} &\approx \Delta H \quad \text{under wild-type rescue},\\
\Delta H_{\mathrm{binding\ defective}} &\not\approx \Delta H.
\end{aligned}
$$
Here $H$ denotes RNA half-life and $O$ denotes local occupancy of the prespecified decay-factor RBP.  The available m6AConquer finite counts support only the $H$ side of this display; the $O$, loss, rescue, and binding-defective rows are requirements for a stronger interpretation, not observed components of the present run.

\paragraph{Translation boundary.}
The same-row translation readout is mandatory because a coding-window coordinate can otherwise be confused with downstream protein interpretation.  In this chapter, however, translation is a guardrail rather than the measured result.  RNA half-life differences alone do not establish ribosome occupancy, translation efficiency, mature protein abundance, folded structure, physical admissibility, biological function, phenotype, adaptation, causation, or any universal sequence law.  The mRNA abundance contact is likewise a control for transcript-level separation rather than a protein-output observation.  The ribosome-profiling contact, when absent from the joined rows, cannot be inferred from half-life, m6A load, codon geometry, or coordinate notation.

\paragraph{Comparison with a neighboring stability surface.}
The endogenous 3-prime-UTR ARE transfer run gives a useful calibration for the scale of RNA-stability evidence without changing the m6A claim.  Experiment $\mathrm{e96bf26e626b}$ used $14{,}933$ genes and $2{,}000$ permutations, with baseline $R^2=0.0657771$, test $R^2=0.0827561$, $\Delta R^2=0.016979$, $\beta_{\mathrm{ARE}}=-0.0607953$, and $p_{\mathrm{perm}}=0.00049975$.  This comparison shows that finite transcript-stability contacts can be numerically sharp while still remaining layer-local.  It therefore supports the discipline used here: an RNA half-life signal may be real at the transcript layer without licensing a decay-factor RBP mechanism, translation realization, protein-state claim, structural conclusion, function statement, phenotype conclusion, or global law.

\paragraph{Local refusal conditions.}
The positive statement is refused if high- and low-coordinate labels are exchangeable for RNA half-life after the listed controls; if RNA half-life differs but measured decay-factor RBP occupancy does not; if target-RBP loss fails to collapse the effect; if wild-type rescue does not restore it; if a binding-defective rescue or non-target control reproduces the pattern; or if local structure, motif density, transcription, isoform state, transcript abundance, codon-pair context, or batch explains the contrast.  Under those outcomes the panel remains a finite RNA-stability association.  It is not a mechanism result, not a translation result, not a protein-output result, not a folding result, not a physical-admissibility result, not a function result, and not a universal biological principle.
