\subsection{Cross-organism m6A load and RNA half-life as a bounded BioReality row system}
\label{sec:bioreality-m6a-load-halflife-crossorganism-human-m6aconquer}

\paragraph{Finite carrier.}
The finite object considered here is a matched panel of coding-RNA rows in which an m6A-site or m6A-load coordinate is joined to an RNA half-life readout.  The usable rows are not treated as a free-standing law about coding sequence.  They are only the rows on which the same gene or transcript identifier carries an m6A-derived coordinate, a half-life measurement, and the declared bookkeeping needed to separate abundance, organism, and platform effects from the half-life comparison.  In the human m6Aconquer run recorded for this claim, the joined panel contains $15845$ genes and $135300$ m6A sites.  The finite check used $1000$ permutations, ran for $338.238$ seconds, and reported $3$ sign-concordant organism-level or dataset-level directions in the registered comparison.

\paragraph{Readout.}
The readout is an RNA-stability boundary statistic, not a translation or protein statistic.  A row may be read as evidence that an m6A-load coordinate has crossed into an RNA half-life measurement only after the row has been joined to the half-life surface and after the statistic remains finite under the declared comparison.  The local form of the statistic may be written schematically as
$$
\begin{aligned}
  \Delta_{\mathrm{HL}}(g) &= \log \mathrm{HL}_{\mathrm{high\ m6A}}(g)-\log \mathrm{HL}_{\mathrm{low\ m6A}}(g),\\
  S_{\mathrm{m6A,HL}} &= \operatorname{stat}\{(L_g,\Delta_{\mathrm{HL}}(g),Z_g):g\in G\},
\end{aligned}
$$
where $L_g$ is the finite m6A-load label and $Z_g$ denotes the controls actually present in the joined table.  For the associated human METTL3-inhibition half-life boundary check, the joined GSE163500 surface retained $5837$ genes.  Its static Spearman association between m6A load and DMSO log half-life was $-0.388929$, while the causal load-versus-$\Delta\log_2$ half-life Spearman statistic was $0.245804$.  After length adjustment the partial Spearman statistic was $0.227683$, with two-sided permutation value $0.000999001$ under the stated coverage rule $\min(\mathrm{Mean\ T\ coverage\ DMSO},\mathrm{STM2457})$ at least the twentieth percentile among valid joined genes.

\paragraph{Boundary interpretation.}
The registered human m6Aconquer result therefore supports a finite RNA-half-life boundary: m6A load can be compared with transcript stability on a joined finite surface.  The sign-concordant count $3$ is a bounded concordance result over the available rows, not a mechanistic derivation.  It does not by itself say that translation has been realized, that a protein output has changed, or that a decay-factor RNA-binding protein is the operative cause.  The required mechanism bridge would need the same finite rows to carry measured local occupancy of a prespecified decay-factor RBP, coordinate--occupancy decoupling, target-RBP loss, wild-type rescue, a binding-defective rescue or non-target control, transcription-rate controls, local RNA-structure and motif-density controls, GC and dinucleotide controls, codon-pair context, isoform state, transcript abundance, batch, and a same-row translation readout used only as a boundary covariate.

\paragraph{Comparison to neighboring RNA-stability contacts.}
The same boundary discipline is visible in the neighboring RNA-decay contacts.  The NMD position-rule run retained $38576$ rows, used $1000$ permutations, and ran for $55.602$ seconds; it supports a transcript-surveillance boundary rather than a universal law of coding sequence.  The ARE-deletion stability check retained $226$ BEAS2B rows and $2242$ Jurkat rows, used $1000$ actual permutations and $2000$ grading permutations, and found a sign-concordant deletion effect under a corrected pooled-label permutation over deletions.  The endogenous $3'$ UTR transfer check retained $14933$ genes, used $2000$ permutations, gave baseline $R^2=0.0657771$, test $R^2=0.0827561$, $\Delta R^2=0.016979$, $\beta_{\mathrm{ARE}}=-0.0607953$, and $p=0.00049975$.  These numbers show that finite RNA-stability surfaces can be made concrete, but each surface remains limited to the layer it actually measures.

\paragraph{Refusal conditions.}
The claim is refused as an RBP-mediated RNA-decay bridge if the half-life contrast survives without measured decay-factor RBP occupancy, if occupancy is not decoupled from the coordinate label, if target-RBP loss does not collapse the effect, if wild-type rescue does not restore it, or if binding-defective rescue, non-target controls, RNA structure, motif density, transcription, isoform state, transcript abundance, codon-pair context, or batch reproduce the pattern.  It is also refused if the same rows lack the translation-readout boundary required to prevent a half-life statistic from being promoted into translation language.  Thus the present finite result is a bounded m6A-load/RNA-half-life record.  No translation realization, protein abundance, folded structure, physical admissibility, biological function, phenotype, adaptation, mechanism, causation, or universal biological rule follows unless an additional layer-matched contact supplies that measurement and the corresponding finite statistic.
