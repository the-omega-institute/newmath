\subsection{Human m6A Site Load and RNA Half-Life Boundary}\label{sec:bioreality-m6a-load-halflife-human-m6aconquer}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite row object.}
The object considered here is a finite human transcript panel in which an m6A-site burden is joined to an RNA half-life readout at gene or transcript resolution. The row is not treated as a complete molecular mechanism. It is a bounded observation of the form
$$
\begin{aligned}
  r_i = (&\,\mathrm{gene}_i,\ \mathrm{m6A\ site\ load}_i,\ \mathrm{RNA\ half\ life}_i,\ 
  \mathrm{organism}_i,\ \mathrm{identifier\ join}_i,\\
  &\,\mathrm{batch\ or\ dataset\ provenance}_i,\ \mathrm{declared\ finite\ statistic}_i).
\end{aligned}
$$
For the cross-organism human m6Aconquer contact, the verified finite computation used $n_{\mathrm{genes}}=15845$ joined genes and $n_{\mathrm{sites}}=135300$ m6A sites. The permutation budget actually executed was $1000$, and the run time was $338.238$ seconds. The reported cross-panel sign-concordance count was $3$. These numbers are the finite content of the row-level claim: they say that a declared m6A-load/RNA-half-life boundary statistic was computed on a large joined table, not that a full biochemical pathway has been observed.

\paragraph{Observed statistic.}
The verified datum for the human m6Aconquer panel is the finite tuple
$$
\begin{aligned}
  (n_{\mathrm{genes}},n_{\mathrm{sites}},N_{\mathrm{perm}},c_{\mathrm{sign}})
  =(15845,135300,1000,3).
\end{aligned}
$$
The interpretation is deliberately narrow. A concordant sign pattern across three evaluated settings is evidence that the m6A-load coordinate reaches an RNA-stability readout in this finite table. It is not evidence that translation has been realized, that a protein phenotype has been reached, or that an RBP decay mechanism has been isolated. The statistic occupies the RNA half-life boundary only: a row may be counted as informative when the m6A-load coordinate and the half-life readout are jointly present and the declared permutation comparison is nondegenerate.

\paragraph{Independent same-layer support.}
A separate m6A half-life boundary computation on the GSE163500-style site-load table gives a useful calibration for the same type of contact. In that finite run, $n_{\mathrm{genes}}=5837$ genes passed the coverage rule, the static Spearman association between m6A load and DMSO log-half-life was $-0.388929$, and the perturbation-oriented Spearman association between m6A load and $\Delta\log_2$ half-life was $0.245804$. After controlling for length, the partial Spearman value was $0.227683$, with a two-sided permutation value
$$
\begin{aligned}
  p_{\mathrm{perm}} = 0.000999001.
\end{aligned}
$$
The coverage rule was that the smaller of mean T coverage in DMSO and STM2457 had to be at least the twentieth percentile among valid joined genes. This second row family does not prove the human m6Aconquer claim by itself, but it fixes the same scientific boundary: m6A burden can be evaluated against RNA half-life by a finite, declared statistic, while mechanism-level language remains unavailable unless the missing mechanism rows are supplied.

\paragraph{Cross-layer refusal.}
The stronger conjectural bridge would require a same-window panel carrying synonymous coordinate labels, measured local decay-factor RBP occupancy, target-RBP loss, wild-type rescue, binding-defective or non-target controls, transcription-rate controls, local RNA structure, motif density, GC and dinucleotide composition, codon-pair context, splice or isoform state, transcript abundance, batch, and a matched translation readout. The verified m6Aconquer row set does not contain those mechanism-bearing columns. Therefore the positive statement licensed here is only this: in the finite joined m6A-load/RNA-half-life table, the m6A-site coordinate reaches an RNA-stability readout with the recorded row counts and permutation budget.

The following promotions are refused inside this chapter. No RNA half-life difference is read as translation output. No RNA half-life difference is read as folded protein structure, physical admissibility, biological function, phenotype, adaptation, causation, or a universal biological law. No decay-factor RBP mechanism is claimed without measured local RBP occupancy, coordinate/occupancy decoupling, target-RBP loss, wild-type rescue, and binding-defective or non-target controls on the same finite rows. If an m6A-load signal survives only as an RNA half-life association while those RBP and rescue columns are absent, the result remains an RNA-stability boundary statistic.

\paragraph{Local verdict.}
The chapter is ready as a bounded BioReality record because it cites finite, reproducible row counts and scalar statistics for the m6A-load/RNA-half-life contact. The main human m6Aconquer computation supplies $15845$ genes, $135300$ m6A sites, $1000$ permutations, and $3$ sign-concordant settings. The independent m6A half-life boundary computation supplies $5837$ genes, a static association of $-0.388929$, perturbation and length-partial associations of $0.245804$ and $0.227683$, and $p_{\mathrm{perm}}=0.000999001$. Together these support a finite RNA-stability readout boundary. They do not supply the additional rows needed for a translation-conditioned decay-factor RBP bridge.
