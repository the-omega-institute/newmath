\paragraph{Finite witness.}
This subsection records the human panel that supports the cross-organism m6A load and half-life claim. The checked run \texttt{d7949df4a751} was verified at \texttt{2026-06-25T13:52:57+00:00} on 15{,}845 genes and 135{,}300 m6A sites, with 1{,}000 permutations and 3 sign-concordant rows. The run completed in 338.238 seconds. In this finite row set, the relation is bounded and empirical: it says that the measured m6A load and RNA half-life cross the pre-registered boundary under the recorded controls, not that the observation has been promoted into a general biological rule.

$$
\begin{aligned}
 n_{\mathrm{genes}} &= 15845, \\
 n_{\mathrm{sites}} &= 135300, \\
 N_{\mathrm{perm}} &= 1000, \\
 n_{\mathrm{sign}} &= 3.
\end{aligned}
$$

\paragraph{Control envelope.}
The panel is interpreted only after matching mRNA abundance, transcription rate, local RNA structure, motif density, GC and dinucleotide composition, codon-pair context, splice or isoform state, transcript abundance, batch, perturbation state, rescue state, and the paired translation readout. Those covariates belong to the measurement surface and are not optional background. The related GSE163500 half-life record reports a causal Spearman value of 0.245804, a length-partial Spearman value of 0.227683, a static Spearman value of -0.388929, a two-sided permutation p-value of 0.000999001, and 5{,}837 genes. These numbers support a finite half-life signal that survives the pre-registered controls.

$$
\begin{aligned}
 \mathrm{causal\ Spearman} &= 0.245804, \\
 \mathrm{length\ partial\ Spearman} &= 0.227683, \\
 \mathrm{static\ Spearman} &= -0.388929, \\
 p_{\mathrm{perm}} &= 0.000999001.
\end{aligned}
$$

\paragraph{Scope limit.}
Nothing here licenses a jump from RNA half-life to translation realization, protein output, folded structure, physical admissibility, function, mechanism, phenotype, adaptation, or a universal biological rule. The same caution applies to the prespecified decay-factor RBP contact: if measured occupancy does not change, or if target-RBP loss, wild-type rescue, binding-defective rescue, or non-target control does not preserve the pattern, the result remains a finite RNA-stability association. This chapter therefore records a row-finite cross-layer relation with explicit controls, not a mechanistic closure statement.
