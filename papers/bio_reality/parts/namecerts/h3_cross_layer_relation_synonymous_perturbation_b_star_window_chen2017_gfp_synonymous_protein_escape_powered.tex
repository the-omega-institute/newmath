\subsection{Chen 2017 GFP Synonymous-Window Protein Escape}
\label{sec:bioreality-chen2017-gfp-synonymous-protein-escape}

\paragraph{Finite observation surface.}
This record treats the Chen 2017 GFP synonymous-variant table as a finite perturbation surface rather than as a general law about codon usage.  The rows are coding variants whose amino-acid sequence is held fixed while synonymous windows change the local $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinates.  The registered run `32de547f1ca8' used $3556$ variants, partitioned into $1124$ rows in region $1$ and $2432$ rows in region $2$, with $B=200$ resampling units and a runtime of $447.6606512069702$ seconds.  The available scalar that directly couples the protein channel to the out-of-frame mRNA channel is
$$
\begin{aligned}
  n_{\mathrm{variants}} &= 3556,\\
  n_{\mathrm{region}\,1} &= 1124,\\
  n_{\mathrm{region}\,2} &= 2432,\\
  B &= 200,\\
  \lambda_{\mathrm{protein}\leftarrow\mathrm{oof\ mRNA}} &= 0.016523671594873352.
\end{aligned}
$$
The last number is read only as a small fitted slope on the available row surface.  It does not by itself certify a translational mechanism, a folding consequence, or a fitness interpretation.

\paragraph{Coordinate role.}
The biological object is the finite family of synonymous perturbations together with their $B^*_{Q6}$ residual-window coordinates and the measured GFP output channel.  The admissible interpretation is a controlled escape test: after amino-acid identity has been fixed by construction, one asks whether residual synonymous-window coordinates retain predictive contact with the protein-output readout.  The row split matters because a single pooled slope can hide region dependence; here the registered surface exposes both region counts explicitly, so any later model comparison must account for the $1124/2432$ imbalance rather than treating the two regions as exchangeable by assertion.

\paragraph{Relation to the translation-gated protein surface.}
The broader cross-layer mediation run for $B^*_{Q6}$ protein abundance supplies the reference shape for what would count as a stronger protein-channel claim.  In that run, the coordinate set had $9$ coordinates, namely $K\_AAA$, $Arg\_AGR$, $Ile\_AUA$, $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$, $Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG$, $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$, $Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG$, $Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY$, and $f3\_stress$.  The cross-organism mediated fractions were positive but heterogeneous: for example $0.9491551041216973$ in \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}, $0.612922252793667$ in \emph{Escherichia coli} K12 MG1655, $0.27206289160119157$ in human, and $0.01850668564589326$ in \emph{Sulfolobus solfataricus}.  These values are useful as a guardrail for the GFP perturbation table: a direct synonymous-window association with protein output is not automatically a $Q_i \to T_j \to P_k$ mediation claim.  A protein-readout statement becomes mediation only when the same finite rows contain a surviving translation readout $T_j$ and when conditioning on $T_j$ changes the direct $Q_i \to P_k$ relation under the declared controls.

\paragraph{Translation-contact boundary.}
The modeled translation-survival audit supplies a second guardrail.  It computed $9$ $B^*_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates across $3$ organisms and reported $81$ modeled tAI coupling entries above threshold $0.01$, with maximum absolute modeled coupling $3801961.031607896$.  The same audit explicitly kept this as modeled tAI bounded coupling rather than ribosome-profiling or proteomics validation, because $3$ organisms are insufficient for powered survival under the stated rule requiring at least $40$ matched species for a powered survival request.  Therefore the Chen GFP surface can be reported as a finite synonymous perturbation contact with protein output, but it cannot be promoted to observed ribosome dynamics unless matched ribosome occupancy or translation-efficiency rows are attached to the same variant or coding-window surface.

\paragraph{Local claim.}
The finite claim supported here is narrow.  On the registered Chen 2017 GFP row surface, synonymous-window perturbations provide a concrete protein-output test bed with $3556$ variants and a measured protein-versus-out-of-frame-mRNA slope of $0.016523671594873352$.  This is enough to record a bounded cross-layer protein-output contact for synonymous perturbations, because the row table is not merely a codon-count table: it is a perturbation panel with a downstream GFP readout.  The claim is not that every $B^*_{Q6}$ coordinate realizes protein abundance, nor that the GFP result establishes a population rule.  The admissible mathematical readback is a finite matrix whose rows are variants and whose columns include region, synonymous-window residual coordinates, the protein readout, and the available out-of-frame mRNA channel.

\paragraph{Non-promotion rule.}
No translation, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, mechanism, organismal phenotype, selection, or universality follows from this chapter alone.  Translation requires a separate translation readout such as ribosome occupancy, translation efficiency, tAI, or stAI evaluated under the appropriate controls.  Folding requires a structure-level contact.  Function and phenotype require assays at those layers.  The Chen GFP table is therefore used as a finite protein-output perturbation contact, with the $B^*_{Q6}$ coordinates kept as controlled synonymous-window regressors rather than treated as biological causes by definition.

\paragraph{Verdict.}
The row evidence is sufficient for a ready BioReality entry at the protein-output perturbation boundary: $3556$ synonymous GFP variants, $1124$ and $2432$ rows in the two registered regions, $B=200$, and the finite fitted slope $0.016523671594873352$ give a concrete, auditable surface.  The chapter remains bounded because the available row facts do not include a same-row translation mediator, a folding assay, a functional assay, or a universality test.
