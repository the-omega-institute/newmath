\paragraph{Coordinate discipline.}
The admissible coordinate carrier is the finite set of triples $(q_i,t_j,p_k)$ in which $q_i$ is one of the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates, $t_j$ is a translation-layer readout, and $p_k$ is a matched protein-abundance or protein-output readout. In the modeled translation-abundance audit, the finite cross-organism panel used $12$ organism-specific CDS/protein joins, including $3739$ joined proteins for \emph{Escherichia coli} K-12 MG1655, $5815$ for \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}, $19053$ for human, and $19395$ for mouse. \paragraph{Translation gate.}
The admissible readback is the controlled matrix
$$
\begin{aligned}
q_i &\longrightarrow t_j \longrightarrow p_k,
\end{aligned}
$$
where $t_j$ may be a bounded translation readout such as tAI, stAI, ribosome occupancy, or translation efficiency, and $p_k$ is a matched protein-output or abundance measurement.  The modeled-tAI survival audit computed $81$ entries above the bounded-contact threshold $0.01$ across $3$ organisms, with maximum absolute score $3801961.031607896$. Consequently, the Chen GFP result can be discussed as a finite perturbation contact with a protein-output response and an out-of-frame mRNA control slope, while translation survival remains a separate condition that must be supplied by a measured or explicitly bounded $t_j$ surface.

\paragraph{Local interpretation.}
The chapter-specific evidence is positive only in the limited sense that the registered computation ran on a nontrivial synonymous-variant panel and retained a concrete protein-versus-out-of-frame-mRNA scalar.  The slope $0.016523671594873352$ is small enough that the control channel cannot be ignored, and the row split $1124+2432=3556$ matters because any region effect must be read through the two-window design rather than through a single pooled codon-count story.  The correct mathematical object is therefore a controlled finite matrix whose rows are synonymous GFP variants and whose columns separate residual-coordinate labels, region membership, mRNA-control readback, and protein-output readback.  The record is not a statement about folding, mature molecular function, physical admissibility, organismal fitness, or a general selection law.

\paragraph{No-promotion boundary.}
No claim of translation realization, ribosome mechanism, folding, physical admissibility, biological function, phenotype, causation, or universality follows from the Chen GFP synonymous-window row counts alone.  A translation claim requires a separate translation contact; a protein-abundance mediation claim requires that the same residual coordinate first survive the translation readout and then show a downstream matched protein-output relation under amino-acid sequence, organism or construct context, GC3, CDS length, $M$-density baseline, and matched mRNA controls.  If the $q_i \to t_j$ step is null or untested, any apparent $q_i \to p_k$ relation remains a bounded protein-output association on this perturbation panel rather than evidence that $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ has entered a protein-realization layer.

The finite row record is ready for inclusion as a bounded BioReality chapter because it has an identified claim id, a completed run id, $3556$ variant rows, two explicit region counts, $B=200$, a retained mRNA-control slope, and a clear layer boundary.  The accepted readback is: synonymous GFP perturbation windows can be audited for whether a $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual coordinate contributes to protein-output variation after the relevant controls.
