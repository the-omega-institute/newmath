\subsection{Chen 2017 GFP Synonymous Protein-Escape Window}
\label{sec:bioreality-chen2017-gfp-synonymous-protein-escape}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite surface.}
The Chen 2017 GFP synonymous-perturbation packet is read as a finite cross-layer table whose rows are synonymous GFP variants and whose columns separate codon-residual geometry, translation-adjacent readouts, and protein-output observations.  The finite run contains $3556$ synonymous variants, split into two reported analysis regions with $1124$ variants in region $1$ and $2432$ variants in region $2$.  The registered resampling budget is $B=200$, and the recorded execution time is $447.6606512069702$ seconds.  The only scalar coupling reported inside this packet is a protein-on-out-of-frame-mRNA slope of $0.016523671594873352$.  In this chapter this number is treated as a finite perturbation readback, not as a mechanism claim.

$$
\begin{aligned}
 n_{\mathrm{variants}} &= 3556,\\
 n_{\mathrm{region}\,1} &= 1124,\\
 n_{\mathrm{region}\,2} &= 2432,\\
 B &= 200,\\
 \widehat\lambda_{P\mid \mathrm{oof\,mRNA}} &= 0.016523671594873352.
\end{aligned}
$$

The equality $1124+2432=3556$ is the row-accounting check for the two reported windows.  The packet therefore has enough finite row support to state a bounded perturbation contact: synonymous edits in the GFP coding sequence can be placed on a finite table and compared with a protein-output readout while keeping the out-of-frame mRNA slope visible as a nuisance or leakage-sensitive scalar.

\paragraph{Coordinate interpretation.}
The biological object under audit is not an amino-acid substitution series.  It is a synonymous coding-window perturbation table in which the amino-acid sequence is held fixed while codon-level residual coordinates are allowed to vary.  The relevant residual family is the nine-coordinate $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ list used by the surrounding finite programs:
$$
\begin{aligned}
Q=\{&K\_AAA,\ Arg\_AGR,\ Ile\_AUA,\ Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR,\ Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG,\\
&Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY,\ Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG,\ Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY,\ f3\_stress\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
These coordinates may be used as controlled codon-window summaries.  They are not themselves observations of translation, folding, molecular function, cellular phenotype, or evolutionary selection.  Their admissible use in this chapter is narrower: they organize synonymous residual variation and ask whether the resulting finite rows remain informative after the relevant controls are kept in place.

\paragraph{Translation-mediated comparison.}
The broader $Q\to T\to P$ mediation audit supplies the finite shape that this GFP packet must respect.  In that audit the same nine $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates were paired with a modeled translation readout $T$, namely per-protein mean modeled tAI, and a protein-abundance readout $P$, namely $\log_{10}$ abundance in ppm.  Across the twelve organism-level joined panels, every reported mediated fraction lay in the unit interval.  The largest reported mediated fraction was for \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae},
$$
\begin{aligned}
M^{QTP}_{\mathrm{yeast}} &= 0.9491551041216973,\\
R^2_{QP} &= 0.29837624994140827,\\
R^2_{QP\mid T} &= 0.0151709093608293,\\
R^2_{\mathrm{indirect}} &= 0.28320534058057895,
\end{aligned}
$$
while the smallest reported mediated fraction was for \emph{Sulfolobus solfataricus},
$$
\begin{aligned}
M^{QTP}_{\mathrm{sulfolobus}} &= 0.01850668564589326,\\
R^2_{QP} &= 0.06397514251295629,\\
R^2_{QP\mid T} &= 0.06279117466131778,\\
R^2_{\mathrm{indirect}} &= 0.0011839678516385038.
\end{aligned}
$$
These numbers are not imported as conclusions about GFP.  They define the local standard of evidence: a direct $Q\to P$ association is not enough for protein-realization language unless the same table also contains a surviving $Q\to T$ step and a controlled reduction of the direct protein-output association after conditioning on $T$.

\paragraph{Modeled-readout boundary.}
The available translation-survival run also records a bounded modeled-tAI contact.  It used $3$ organisms, a codon count space of $61$, a coupling threshold of $0.01$, and reported $81$ entries above threshold with maximum absolute $S_{ij}$ equal to $3801961.031607896$.  The same record explicitly says that $3$ organisms are insufficient for powered survival and that the readout is modeled tAI from GtRNAdb tRNA gene-copy data and dos Reis wobble weights, not ribosome profiling or proteomics validation.  Thus the GFP packet may inherit the discipline of the translation-readout route, but it does not inherit a measured ribosome-occupancy result.

$$
\begin{aligned}
 n_{\mathrm{organisms}} &= 3,\\
 |\mathcal C_{\mathrm{sense}}| &= 61,\\
 \tau_{\mathrm{coupling}} &= 0.01,\\
 \#\{|S_{ij}|>\tau_{\mathrm{coupling}}\} &= 81,\\
 \max |S_{ij}| &= 3801961.031607896.
\end{aligned}
$$

The correct reading is therefore a bounded one.  The GFP synonymous table is a perturbation contact with $3556$ rows and a measured protein-associated output surface; it is not, by itself, a completed translation-mediated protein-abundance matrix.

\paragraph{Local verdict.}
The finite evidence supports a chapter-level record that synonymous GFP coding-window variation has been organized into a protein-output perturbation surface with explicit row counts, regional partitioning, and an out-of-frame mRNA slope.  The result is useful because it gives a concrete perturbation setting in which $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$-style residual coordinates can be tested against downstream output without changing amino-acid sequence.  The strongest statement licensed here is that the packet is ready as a finite protein-output perturbation audit: the data table exists at nontrivial size, the two region counts close exactly, and the registered bootstrap budget is explicit.

It does not license a stronger biological conclusion.  No claim of translation realization follows unless a matched translation readout such as ribosome occupancy, translation efficiency, tAI, or stAI is joined to the same synonymous rows and survives the declared controls.  No claim of folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, mechanism, phenotype, selection, or universality follows from the protein-output surface alone.  A positive protein-output association would remain a downstream association until the same coordinate first passes a controlled translation-readout step and then shows the declared $Q\to T\to P$ attenuation pattern under amino-acid composition, organism or experimental background, GC3, coding-sequence length, $M$-density baseline, and matched mRNA controls.
