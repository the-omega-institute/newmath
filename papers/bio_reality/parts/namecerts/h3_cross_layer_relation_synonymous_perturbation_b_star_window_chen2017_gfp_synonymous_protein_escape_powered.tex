\subsection{Chen 2017 GFP Synonymous Windows as a Bounded Protein-Escape Perturbation Surface}
\label{sec:bior reality-chen2017-gfp-synonymous-protein-escape}

\paragraph{Finite perturbation carrier.}
The Chen 2017 GFP synonymous-window record is read here as a finite perturbation surface rather than as a general law about synonymous sequence.  Its rows are the assayed synonymous variants, partitioned into two reported regions with $1124$ rows in the first region and $2432$ rows in the second region, for a total of $3556$ variants.  The admissible coordinates are window-level synonymous residual summaries, the available mRNA control readout, and the measured protein-escape or protein-output response attached to the same variant row.  The verified run used $B=200$ resampling or null-comparison units and completed in $447.6606512069702$ seconds under the registered analysis.

\paragraph{Local numerical readback.}
The finite statistic retained by the paper is deliberately small.  The observed slope of protein response on out-of-frame mRNA was
$$
\begin{aligned}
\lambda_{\mathrm{protein}\mid \mathrm{oof\;mRNA}} &= 0.016523671594873352,\\
N_{\mathrm{variants}} &= 3556,\\
N_{\mathrm{region\;1}} &= 1124,\\
N_{\mathrm{region\;2}} &= 2432,\\
B &= 200.
\end{aligned}
$$
This is enough to record a row-grounded perturbation contact: the protein readout is not being inferred from codon geometry alone, and the mRNA-side control is explicitly present in the finite panel.  The magnitude of $\lambda_{\mathrm{protein}\mid \mathrm{oof\;mRNA}}$ is small and is therefore used as a guarded adjustment term, not as a claim that transcript abundance explains the protein escape response.

\paragraph{Mediation discipline.}
The claim is accepted only at the bounded perturbation layer.  A direct residual-to-protein association is not promoted to translation realization unless the same residual first has an admissible translation readout.  The broader translation-mediated protein-abundance audit gives the intended separation rule: with $12$ organism panels, the joint $9$-coordinate $B^*_{Q6}$ surface had positive statistical mediation fractions in the bounded modeled-tAI decomposition, including $0.9491551041216973$ in \textit{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}, $0.612922252793667$ in \textit{Escherichia coli} K12 MG1655, $0.6165167748739756$ in \textit{Drosophila melanogaster}, and $0.27206289160119157$ in \textit{Homo sapiens}.  Those values are useful here only as calibration of the rule that protein abundance must be routed through a translation readout when such a route is claimed.  They do not make the GFP synonymous-window result a multi-organism protein law.

\paragraph{Translation-readout boundary.}
The modeled translation-survival audit computed nine residual coordinates, namely $K\_AAA$, $Arg\_AGR$, $Ile\_AUA$, $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$, $Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG$, $Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG$, $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$, $Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY$, and $f3\_stress$.  In the bounded modeled-tAI contact, only $3$ organisms were present, while powered survival required at least $40$ matched species.  The same audit nevertheless found $81$ entries above the bounded-contact threshold $0.01$, with maximum absolute modeled coupling $3801961.031607896$.  For the Chen GFP panel this means that modeled adaptation weights may motivate a translation-layer check, but they are not themselves ribosome occupancy, translation efficiency, or protein abundance.  The GFP evidence is therefore kept at a perturbation readout level unless a same-row translation measurement is supplied.

\paragraph{Admissible interpretation.}
The row set supports the following narrow statement.  In the Chen 2017 GFP synonymous-window experiment, a finite variant panel with $3556$ rows gives a measured protein-output response together with an mRNA-side control, and the registered analysis records the small protein-on-out-of-frame-mRNA slope $0.016523671594873352$ under $B=200$ null units.  This permits a bounded statement that synonymous windows can be tested for protein-output escape after transcript-side adjustment.  It does not by itself establish that a $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinate has survived a direct ribosome or translation-efficiency readout.

\paragraph{Local refusal line.}
No claim of translation realization, folded protein structure, physical admissibility, molecular function, mechanism, organismal phenotype, selection, or universality follows from this chapter alone.  Those stronger readings require separate layer-matched measurements: ribosome profiling or translation-efficiency rows for translation, structure or stability assays for folding and physical state, functional or phenotype assays for biological effect, and multi-organism controlled panels for population-level directionality.  The Chen GFP record is therefore a finite perturbation contact with protein readout and transcript control, not a license to turn synonymous residual geometry into a general biological mechanism.
