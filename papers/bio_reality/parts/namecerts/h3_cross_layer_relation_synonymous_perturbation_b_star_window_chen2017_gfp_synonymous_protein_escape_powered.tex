\subsection{Chen GFP Synonymous-Window Protein-Escape Audit}\label{sec:chen-gfp-synonymous-window-protein-escape}\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite surface.}
This record isolates a finite synonymous-perturbation surface from the Chen GFP assay and asks a deliberately narrow question: whether a $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ window coordinate can be read as a protein-output residual only after the perturbation rows have been kept separate from amino-acid change, transcript abundance, and lower-layer codon-count explanations. The finite row set contains $3556$ synonymous variants, split into $1124$ rows in the first analyzed region and $2432$ rows in the second analyzed region. The permutation budget recorded for the run is $B=200$, and the completed wall-clock time is $447.6606512069702$ seconds. The only directly reported cross-readout scalar in this finite run is the slope of protein signal on out-of-frame mRNA, namely $0.016523671594873352$.

\paragraph{Controlled readback.}
The intended empirical object is not a bare association $Q_i \to P_k$. The admissible form is a controlled matrix in which a synonymous residual coordinate first survives a translation-facing or expression-facing readout and only then may be compared with protein abundance. In symbols, the allowed finite-chain shape is
$$
\begin{aligned}
q_i &\in B^{\ast}_{Q6},\\
t_j &\in \{\mathrm{tAI},\mathrm{stAI},\mathrm{ribosome\ occupancy},\mathrm{translation\ efficiency}\},\\
p_k &\in \{\mathrm{matched\ protein\ abundance\ or\ protein\ reporter\ output}\},\\
q_i &\longrightarrow t_j \longrightarrow p_k.
\end{aligned}
$$
The Chen GFP run therefore supports a bounded protein-output audit only at the row level stated above. It does not, by itself, identify a folding pathway, a molecular function, an organismal phenotype, or a general rule of translation.

\paragraph{B-star coordinates.}
The residual coordinate menu used by the surrounding finite program has nine named directions:
$$
\begin{aligned}
&K\_AAA,\ Arg\_AGR,\ Ile\_AUA,\ Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR,\ Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG,\\
&Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY,\ Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG,\ Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY,\ f3\_stress.
\end{aligned}
$$
These directions are residual coordinates after amino-acid identity has been held fixed; they are not themselves empirical measurements. The controls kept out of the promoted coordinate set are amino-acid sequence, organism identity, $GC3$, coding-sequence length, and the $M$-density baseline. Matched mRNA abundance is treated as a control channel rather than as a protein-output substitute.

\paragraph{Why the Chen run is a protein-output audit rather than a mechanism claim.}
The primary Chen GFP row count is large enough for a concrete perturbation audit, but the reported finite facts are intentionally sparse: $n=3556$ variants, two region counts, $B=200$, and the out-of-frame mRNA slope. No finite $p$-value, confidence interval, translation-efficiency matrix, ribosome-occupancy panel, or fitted mediation coefficient is reported inside this specific run. Consequently the chapter may state that the Chen surface was executed as a bounded synonymous-window protein-output test, but it may not infer the missing translation step from the existence of the protein reporter readout.

\paragraph{Relation to the translation-mediation control surface.}
The larger translation-mediated protein-abundance audit supplies the finite template against which this GFP record is constrained. In that audit the same nine $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ directions were evaluated on $12$ organism-matched protein-abundance panels, with joined protein counts ranging from $1656$ in \emph{Sulfolobus solfataricus} to $19395$ in mouse and $19053$ in human. The reported mediation fractions remained inside the unit interval for all organisms. For example, the yeast surface had
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{QP} &= 0.29837624994140827,\\
R^2_{QP\mid T} &= 0.0151709093608293,\\
R^2_{\mathrm{indirect}} &= 0.28320534058057895,\\
M^{QTP} &= 0.9491551041216973,
\end{aligned}
$$
while the \emph{E. coli} K-12 MG1655 surface had
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{QP} &= 0.10767526677985896,\\
R^2_{QP\mid T} &= 0.04167869969498872,\\
R^2_{\mathrm{indirect}} &= 0.06599656708487024,\\
M^{QTP} &= 0.612922252793667.
\end{aligned}
$$
Those numbers are not imported as Chen GFP effects. They set the local standard: a protein-output association is allowed to climb only when a translation or expression readout is present and the direct residual-to-protein edge is separated from lower-layer controls.

\paragraph{Modeled translation contact.}
A separate modeled-tAI contact also explains why this Chen record is not automatically a ribosome-dynamics claim. The modeled-tAI matrix used three organisms, not a powered multi-organism survival panel: \emph{E. coli} K-12, human, and budding yeast. It computed $61$ sense-codon weights per organism from GtRNAdb tRNA copy counts and dos Reis wobble weights, with zero raw-weight fallback count equal to $0$ for all three organisms in that modeled readout. The finite coupling table contained $81$ entries above the threshold $0.01$, with maximum absolute entry
$$
\begin{aligned}
\max |S_{ij}| = 3801961.031607896.
\end{aligned}
$$
That large modeled coupling is still not a Chen GFP ribosome-footprint measurement. It remains a bounded codon-adaptation readout unless same-row ribosome profiling, matched mRNA abundance, and the GFP perturbation rows are joined and re-estimated.

\paragraph{Comparison with a protein-output perturbation positive control.}
A related \emph{E. coli} mRFP synonymous-output audit gives a useful scale for what a finite protein-output claim looks like when more scalar evidence is available. That run used $1459$ variants with $B=200$, reported $R^2=0.12648386762689323$ for the control layer, $R^2=0.1798829276988051$ after adding the residual window, and a residual increment
$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta R^2 &= 0.05339906007191191,\\
\Delta DL &= 19.089327473282296\ \mathrm{bits},\\
p_{\mathrm{matched}} &= 0.004975124378109453,\\
p_{\mathrm{within}} &= 0.004975124378109453.
\end{aligned}
$$
The Chen GFP record does not report an analogous $\Delta R^2$, $\Delta DL$, or permutation tail probability. This is why the Chen chapter is marked as ready for bounded inclusion but not as a full mechanistic promotion.

\paragraph{Local verdict.}
The finite conclusion is therefore conditional and narrow. The Chen GFP synonymous-window assay contributes a completed finite-row protein-output audit with $3556$ variants, two analyzed regions, $B=200$, and a measured protein-on-out-of-frame-mRNA slope of $0.016523671594873352$. The evidence is compatible with keeping $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ in the cross-layer protein-output ledger, but it does not close the translation-mediated chain on its own. The admissible statement is: if a same-row translation readout is later attached and survives the declared controls, then the Chen GFP protein-output rows may enter a $q_i \to t_j \to p_k$ mediation analysis; without that readout, the record remains a bounded synonymous-perturbation protein-output audit.

\paragraph{Cannot-claim boundary.}
No translation realization, ribosome occupancy, folding state, physical admissibility, molecular function, mechanism, selection law, phenotype, or universal biological rule follows from this record alone. A direct residual-to-protein association is not sufficient unless the translation-facing step is measured or explicitly bounded, matched mRNA abundance is controlled, and the lower-layer codon-count and amino-acid baselines remain in the conditioning set.
