\begin{aligned}

\paragraph{Admissible conclusion.}
The ready conclusion is finite and conditional. The Chen GFP synonymous panel supplies $3556$ rows, with a nonzero out-of-fold protein-on-mRNA slope of $0.016523671594873352$ under the recorded computation. This supports a bounded protein-readout entry for the synonymous-window audit: protein measurements are present, the rows are finite, the region split is explicit, and the analysis was evaluated under $200$ realized null or resampling blocks. The result may be used to test whether a synonymous-window residual remains informative after matched RNA information is accounted for. It may not be used to assert that a $B^{*}_{Q6}$ coordinate has become a translation mechanism, a folding mechanism, a physical-admissibility certificate, a molecular-function explanation, a phenotype, or a universal biological law. Each of those stronger readings would require a separate layer-matched measurement surface and a statistic at that layer.

\paragraph{No-promotion statement.}
The finite record therefore has the following mathematical readback. Let $Q$ denote the admissible synonymous-window residual coordinates, $M$ the matched mRNA readout, and $P$ the matched protein readout. The verified Chen surface records the existence of a finite table with $|P|=|M|=3556$ aligned variant rows and the fitted scalar $\widehat{\beta}_{P\mid M}^{\mathrm{oof}}=0.016523671594873352$. The allowed claim is a controlled perturbation-layer protein-readout claim:
$$
\begin{aligned}
 (Q,M,P)_{\mathrm{Chen\ GFP}} \quad \text{is finite and protein-measured, but not layer-complete for mechanism.}
\end{aligned}
$$
No translation, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, mechanism, or universality follows from this table alone. A future strengthening would need same-row translation measurements, matched mRNA controls, protein readouts, and a declared mediation statistic showing that $Q\to T$ survives before $Q\to P$ is interpreted as translation-conditioned protein realization.
