\subsection{Chen 2017 GFP synonymous perturbation and the bounded protein-escape window}\label{subsec:chen2017-gfp-synonymous-protein-escape}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite perturbation surface.}
This chapter records a bounded synonymous-perturbation contact for the GFP window used in the Chen 2017 setting. The finite carrier is not a free biological conclusion; it is the measured table of synonymous coding variants after the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ window coordinates have been attached to an observed reporter surface. The run contains $3556$ synonymous variants split across two finite regions, with $1124$ rows in region 1 and $2432$ rows in region 2. The resampling budget is $B=200$, so every reported contrast is a finite-row computation rather than an asymptotic population statement. The recorded scalar linking protein output to out-of-frame mRNA is
$$
\begin{aligned}
\widehat{\beta}_{P\mid \mathrm{oof\ mRNA}} &= 0.016523671594873352,\\
N_{\mathrm{var}} &= 3556,\\
N_{\mathrm{region\ 1}} &= 1124,\\
N_{\mathrm{region\ 2}} &= 2432.
\end{aligned}
$$
The runtime for the registered computation was $447.6606512069702$ seconds. These numbers identify the observed perturbation table and its finite computational envelope; they do not by themselves identify a folding pathway, a molecular function, or a general law for synonymous substitutions.

\paragraph{Coordinate role.}
The $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ coordinates enter here as residual synonymous-window coordinates, not as biological observations. The relevant coordinate list is the same nine-dimensional residual basis used by the translation-survival and mediation packets:
$$
\begin{aligned}
Q = \{&K\_AAA,\ Arg\_AGR,\ Ile\_AUA,\ Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR,\ Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG,\\
&Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY,\ Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG,\ Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY,\ f3\_stress\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The role of this basis is to sort synonymous variants into controlled residual directions after amino-acid identity and simpler codon-count baselines have been separated. In the present GFP perturbation table, the finite row counts say that there is enough observed synonymous variation to ask whether a protein-output readout changes across those residual directions. They do not say that any one coordinate is a universal directional rule. In particular, a CUN/UUR sign, a single organism sign, or a reporter-specific escape sign cannot be read as a population law without a multi-organism and simpler-baseline audit.

\paragraph{Translation gate.}
The same project memory contains a modeled translation-readout surface for the nine $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ coordinates. That modeled contact used three organisms and found $81$ modeled-tAI coupling entries above threshold $0.01$, with maximum absolute entry $3801961.031607896$. The organism count was only $3$, and the record explicitly keeps it in a modeled, underpowered translation-readout class rather than a measured ribosome-profiling validation. This matters for the GFP perturbation table because a direct association between a residual coordinate and protein output is not enough to assert protein realization. The admissible route is a mediated one:
$$
\begin{aligned}
q_i &\longrightarrow T_j \longrightarrow P_k,\\
T_j &\in \{\mathrm{tAI},\ \mathrm{stAI},\ \mathrm{ribosome\ occupancy},\ \mathrm{translation\ efficiency}\},\\
P_k &= \mathrm{matched\ protein\ abundance\ or\ reporter\ protein\ output}.
\end{aligned}
$$
For this chapter, the GFP protein-output table supplies a perturbation-side protein readout, while the modeled-tAI record supplies only a bounded translation annotation from a different finite contact. The two should therefore be read together as a disciplined boundary: protein output may be analyzed against synonymous residual coordinates, but translation mediation is not established unless the same-coordinate translation step is present and survives the declared controls.

\paragraph{Relation to the larger mediation matrix.}
A separate finite mediation computation over matched CDS and PAXdb abundance rows gives context for what a completed $Q\to T\to P$ table looks like. In that matrix, the same nine-coordinate $Q$ basis was joined to per-protein modeled tAI and log-scaled protein abundance across twelve organisms. The joined row counts included $3739$ proteins for \textit{Escherichia coli} K-12 MG1655, $5815$ for \textit{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}, $19053$ for human, and $19395$ for mouse. The mediation fractions were finite and organism-specific: for example, $M^{QTP}=0.612922252793667$ in \textit{E. coli} K-12 MG1655, $0.9491551041216973$ in yeast, $0.27206289160119157$ in human, and $0.15488799907323988$ in mouse. These values show the intended statistical shape of the matrix, not a causal mechanism. The Chen 2017 GFP table is narrower: it gives $3556$ synonymous reporter variants and the small protein-on-out-of-frame-mRNA slope above, but the supplied finite row does not include a same-row ribosome footprint, measured translation efficiency, or matched mRNA denominator for each variant.

\paragraph{Local admissible statement.}
The admissible conclusion is therefore bounded. The Chen 2017 GFP synonymous perturbation surface is a finite reporter-protein contact with $3556$ rows, two recorded regions, $B=200$ resampling, and a measured protein-to-out-of-frame-mRNA slope of $0.016523671594873352$. It is suitable for asking whether the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ synonymous-window coordinates carry protein-output variation in this reporter context after the declared controls are applied. It is not, by itself, a translation-realization proof. It also does not supply folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, organismal phenotype, mechanism, selection, or universality. Those stronger readings require separate layer-matched contacts: measured translation readouts for $q_i\to T_j$, matched protein or reporter output for $T_j\to P_k$, and controls for amino-acid sequence, organism, GC3, coding-sequence length, $M$-density baseline, and matched mRNA abundance where the design calls for it.

\paragraph{Break condition.}
The chapter remains inside the protein-perturbation window unless a same-row mediation table is supplied. If every controlled improvement from $Q$ through a translation readout to protein output is nonpositive, then the GFP synonymous-window record stays at bounded perturbation status. If a positive protein association appears without a surviving $q_i\to T_j$ translation step, it is only a direct reporter association and must not be promoted to translation-mediated protein realization. Conversely, a positive modeled-tAI contact without variant-level measured translation or matched mRNA rows cannot be used as ribosome occupancy. The finite data here support a precise question about synonymous residuals and reporter protein output; they do not license a biological layer jump beyond that question.
