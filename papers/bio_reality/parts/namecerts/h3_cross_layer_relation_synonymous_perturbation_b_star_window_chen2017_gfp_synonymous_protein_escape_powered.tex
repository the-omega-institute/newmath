\paragraph{No-promotion rule.}
The finite GFP result must remain layer-bounded.  Synonymous perturbation rows, $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates, modeled tAI contacts, mRNA controls, and protein-side escape measurements do not imply protein folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, organismal phenotype, causation, selection, or a universal biological rule.  A translation claim would require a separate same-row translation readout such as ribosome occupancy or translation efficiency.  A folding claim would require a structure-level contact.  A function or phenotype claim would require an assay at that layer.  The present chapter only records that the Chen 2017 GFP synonymous-window panel has enough finite row structure to support a controlled protein-readout boundary test, with $3556$ variants, region counts $1124$ and $2432$, a finite budget $B=200$, and an out-of-fold mRNA slope of $0.016523671594873352$.
