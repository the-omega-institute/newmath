\subsection{Chen 2017 GFP synonymous escape window}
\label{subsec:chen2017-gfp-synonymous-protein-escape-window}

\paragraph{Finite row surface.}
The Chen 2017 GFP packet is treated as a bounded synonymous-perturbation surface, not as a general law about codon usage.  The finite table contains $3556$ synonymous GFP variants, split into $1124$ rows in the first assayed region and $2432$ rows in the second assayed region.  The registered computation used $200$ resampling or null draws, and the observed out-of-fold mRNA-to-protein slope was $0.016523671594873352$.  Thus the row object for this chapter is the finite matrix
$$
\begin{aligned}
R_{\mathrm{GFP}} &= R_1 \sqcup R_2,\\
|R_1| &= 1124,\\
|R_2| &= 2432,\\
|R_{\mathrm{GFP}}| &= 3556,\\
B &= 200,\\
\lambda_{\mathrm{oof}} &= 0.016523671594873352.
\end{aligned}
$$
Here $\lambda_{\mathrm{oof}}$ is read only as the fitted held-out slope from matched mRNA to protein signal on this finite perturbation table.  Its small positive value is useful for auditing whether protein output can be separated from transcript-level movement, but it is not by itself a translation mechanism, a folding measurement, or a fitness statement.

\paragraph{Coordinate role.}
The chapter attaches the GFP synonymous perturbation rows to the $B^*_{Q6}$ residual-coordinate vocabulary only through controlled coding-sequence readbacks.  The available $B^*_{Q6}$ coordinate list contains nine coordinates, namely $K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $Leu_{CUN\_vs\_UUR}$, $Leu_{UUA\_vs\_UUG}$, $Ser_{UCR\_vs\_AGY}$, $Ser_{UCA\_vs\_UCG}$, $Thr_{ACR\_vs\_ACY}$, and $f3_{\mathrm{stress}}$.  A separate modeled translation-readout calculation on three organisms recorded $81$ entries above the bounded coupling threshold $0.01$, with maximum absolute entry $3801961.031607896$, but that contact is explicitly a modeled tAI coupling surface rather than a measured ribosome-footprint or proteomics validation.  Its organism count is $3$, below the stated powered-survival requirement of at least $40$ matched species for a powered survival claim.

\paragraph{Protein-output reading.}
The GFP table is therefore admitted only as a perturbation-level protein-output audit.  The finite slope $\lambda_{\mathrm{oof}}=0.016523671594873352$ says that out-of-fold transcript movement and protein movement are not identical on this surface; it does not say that a $B^*_{Q6}$ coordinate has already crossed the translation layer.  For a genuine mediation assertion one would need a same-row chain $q_i \to T_j \to P_k$, where $T_j$ is a translation readout such as tAI, stAI, ribosome occupancy, or translation efficiency, and $P_k$ is matched protein abundance.  The broader multi-organism mediation audit provides such a statistical template: it used a nine-coordinate $X_Q$ design and protein abundance rows across organisms, with examples such as $n=5815$ for \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}, $n=3739$ for \emph{Escherichia coli} K12 MG1655, $n=19053$ for human, and $n=19395$ for mouse.  In that separate matrix the mediated fractions included $0.9491551041216973$ for yeast, $0.612922252793667$ for \emph{E. coli}, $0.27206289160119157$ for human, and $0.15488799907323988$ for mouse.  Those values show how a protein-abundance mediation surface is to be bounded, but they do not replace the GFP perturbation-specific evidence.

\paragraph{Local verdict.}
The Chen 2017 GFP record is ready as a finite perturbation chapter because the row counts, region split, null-count parameter, runtime, and held-out transcript-to-protein slope are all fixed.  The supported statement is narrow: on this GFP synonymous-variant panel, a protein-output readout can be audited against transcript movement, and the registered finite table contains enough rows to describe a controlled protein-output perturbation surface.  The chapter does not promote the result to translation survival, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, mechanism, selection, phenotype, or universality.  A positive downstream interpretation would require an additional layer-matched contact, for example measured ribosome occupancy or translation efficiency on the same variants, followed by matched protein abundance and mRNA controls in the same finite rows.  Without that extra contact, any direct $Q_i \to P_k$ association remains a bounded perturbation association rather than evidence for realized translation-mediated protein production.

\paragraph{No-promotion rule.}
The local boundary is strict.  Synonymous GFP variants may change fluorescence or protein proxy readouts, but fluorescence or abundance alone does not identify ribosome transit, cotranslational folding, mature folded structure, physical admissibility, molecular function, mechanism, or a biological law.  Likewise, modeled codon-adaptation weights and $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinates are coordinate-side instruments until the same rows contain a controlled translation readout.  The finite claim is therefore a controlled GFP protein-output escape-window record with $3556$ variants and $\lambda_{\mathrm{oof}}=0.016523671594873352$, not a universal codon-translation theorem.
