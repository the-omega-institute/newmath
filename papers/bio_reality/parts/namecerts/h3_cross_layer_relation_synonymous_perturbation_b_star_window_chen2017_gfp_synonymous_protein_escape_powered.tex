\subsection{Chen 2017 GFP synonymous protein-escape window}
\label{subsec:bioreality-chen2017-gfp-synonymous-protein-escape}

\paragraph{Finite row surface.}
This record isolates a synonymous-perturbation window in the Chen 2017 GFP system as a finite cross-layer audit, not as a general law about synonymous coding. The subject is the measured variant table itself: $3556$ synonymous GFP variants split into two finite regions, with $1124$ rows in region $1$ and $2432$ rows in region $2$. The bootstrap or resampling budget recorded for the run is $B=200$, and the completed computation took $447.6606512069702$ seconds. The observed scalar connecting the protein readout to the out-of-frame mRNA channel is small but nonzero:
$$
\begin{aligned}
  n_{\mathrm{variants}} &= 3556,\\
  n_{\mathrm{region}\,1} &= 1124,\\
  n_{\mathrm{region}\,2} &= 2432,\\
  B &= 200,\\
  \lambda_{\mathrm{protein}\mid \mathrm{oof\ mRNA}} &= 0.016523671594873352.
\end{aligned}
$$
Here $\lambda_{\mathrm{protein}\mid \mathrm{oof\ mRNA}}$ denotes the finite-row slope recorded by the run, not a mechanistic constant. The row counts are part of the claim: the window is allowed to speak only about this measured GFP variant panel and only about the attached protein-output and mRNA-related channels present in that panel.

\paragraph{Allowed readback.}
The biological reading is deliberately narrow. A synonymous edit can be entered into the audit as a perturbation of coding-window coordinates, and the finite GFP measurement can be used to ask whether that perturbation is associated with protein escape after the recorded mRNA-side channel is accounted for. The record therefore supports the existence of a concrete perturbation surface with thousands of rows and a measured protein-associated scalar. It does not by itself identify codon-specific causality, a universal translation rule, or a population-level selection law. The correct mathematical object is a finite relation among rows, regions, and readouts:
$$
\begin{aligned}
  q_i &\longrightarrow t_j \longrightarrow p_k,\\
  q_i &\in B^{\ast}_{Q6}\ \text{or a synonymous-window coordinate},\\
  t_j &\in \{\text{translation or mRNA-side readout admitted by the dataset}\},\\
  p_k &\in \{\text{matched GFP protein output}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
For this particular table, the recorded scalar is best treated as an adjustment or leakage diagnostic: the protein channel has a slope of $0.016523671594873352$ against the out-of-frame mRNA channel, so any protein-escape statement must remain conditioned on that finite mRNA-side association rather than treating protein output as a clean downstream realization.

\paragraph{Relation to the broader translation-mediated audit.}
The nearby multi-organism mediation run supplies the background discipline for interpreting this GFP perturbation surface. In that independent mediation matrix, the coordinate family has $9$ entries: $K\_AAA$, $Arg\_AGR$, $Ile\_AUA$, $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$, $Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG$, $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$, $Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG$, $Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY$, and $f3\_stress$. It also records positive bounded mediation fractions for organism-level protein abundance, for example $M^{QTP}=0.612922252793667$ in \emph{Escherichia coli} K12 MG1655, $M^{QTP}=0.9491551041216973$ in \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}, and $M^{QTP}=0.27206289160119157$ in human. Those values are not imported as evidence for the GFP variant table. They state the admissible form of a cross-layer claim: a residual or perturbation coordinate must first pass through an explicit translation-side or expression-side readout before a protein-output statement is allowed. The Chen 2017 GFP record is therefore placed on the perturbation branch of the same discipline: a direct $q_i \to p_k$ assertion is not promoted unless the mediating readout surface is present and controlled.

\paragraph{Comparison with other synonymous perturbation panels.}
The Chen 2017 GFP table is smaller than the human prime-editor synonymous panel with $9000$ synonymous rows and $1822$ genes, and larger than the \emph{E. coli} mRFP panel with $1459$ variants. The mRFP audit reports a finite protein-output improvement of $\Delta R^2=0.05339906007191191$ and $\Delta DL=19.089327473282296$ bits with matched and within null probabilities both equal to $0.004975124378109453$. By contrast, this GFP record stores the row counts and the protein-on-out-of-frame-mRNA slope, but it does not store an analogous $\Delta R^2$, $\Delta DL$, or null-tail probability for the GFP window. A separate boundary check on the mRFP system reports that a downstream-window term was not stable: $\Delta R^2=-0.001126409133973234$, $\Delta LL=-2.082967365015851$, and block-null $p=0.1691542288557214$. This comparison is useful because it prevents the GFP row count from being mistaken for powered support. The finite GFP table is real enough to write down; the strength of any positive protein-escape conclusion remains limited by the missing null-calibrated statistic.

\paragraph{No-promotion boundary.}
The local rule is simple. The Chen 2017 GFP surface may be cited as a measured synonymous-variant protein-output table with $3556$ rows, two recorded regions, $B=200$, and a protein-to-out-of-frame-mRNA slope of $0.016523671594873352$. It may not be cited as evidence for translation dynamics unless a separate translation contact supplies ribosome occupancy, translation efficiency, tAI, stAI, or another explicitly bounded translation readout on matched rows. It may not be cited as evidence for folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, organismal phenotype, mechanism, selection, or universality. Those claims require their own layer-matched measurements and their own finite statistics. In particular, codon-window geometry and synonymous perturbation labels are coordinates for organizing the audit; they are not biological observations by themselves.

\paragraph{Verdict.}
The chapter is accepted as a bounded finite-row record for the Chen 2017 GFP synonymous protein-escape window. The usable numerical content is the row partition $1124+2432=3556$, the resampling budget $B=200$, the runtime $447.6606512069702$ seconds, and the slope $0.016523671594873352$ connecting protein output to the out-of-frame mRNA channel. The positive content is therefore a controlled data-surface claim, not a mechanism claim. The unresolved content is the absence of a recorded GFP-specific $\Delta R^2$, description-length gain, null-tail probability, and same-row translation-readout mediation statistic.
