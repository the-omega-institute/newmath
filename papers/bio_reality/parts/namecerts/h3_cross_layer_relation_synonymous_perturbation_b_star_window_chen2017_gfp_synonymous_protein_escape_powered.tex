The modeled translation survival audit confirms that $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ coordinates can be projected into a bounded codon-adaptation readout, but it also records the boundary of that projection. The local tRNA payload covered all $20$ standard amino-acid families for \emph{E. coli} K12, human, and yeast, with matched FASTA header counts $87$, $432$, and $275$ respectively.

$$
\begin{aligned}

For the Chen GFP panel this means that the perturbation readout may be placed next to the modeled translation route, but it is not automatically a measured ribosome-occupancy contact.  A translation-realization claim would require same-variant or same-CDS translation rows, such as ribosome occupancy or translation efficiency, plus matched mRNA controls.  Without that additional contact, the Chen evidence remains a protein-output perturbation contact inside a synonymous-window audit.

\paragraph{Local verdict.}
The Chen GFP synonymous perturbation record is ready as a bounded BioReality chapter because its finite row counts and control-slope diagnostic are present, and because its interpretation is constrained by already computed translation-mediated and modeled-translation audits.  The admissible statement is that the Chen panel contributes a finite synonymous-variant protein-output contact with $3556$ variants and a recorded out-of-frame mRNA slope of $0.016523671594873352$.  It may be compared to the $Q \to T \to P$ mediation grammar and to independent synonymous perturbation panels, but it does not by itself establish that any $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ coordinate has survived a measured translation readout in the Chen rows.

The cannot-claim boundary is local and strict.  No translation mechanism follows from the Chen GFP protein escape table unless a same-row translation contact supplies it.  No folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, organismal phenotype, selection principle, causal mechanism, or universal biological rule follows from the synonymous residual coordinates or from the protein readout alone.  A direct $Q_i \to P_k$ association remains below protein-realization mediation unless a $Q_i \to T_j$ step is measured or explicitly bounded and then shown to carry the downstream protein-abundance or protein-output contact under amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, coding-sequence length, $M$-density baseline, and matched mRNA controls.
