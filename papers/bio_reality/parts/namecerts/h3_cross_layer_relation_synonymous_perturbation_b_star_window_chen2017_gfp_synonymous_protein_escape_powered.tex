\subsection{Chen GFP synonymous perturbation window and protein escape}
\label{sec:chen-gfp-synonymous-protein-escape}

\paragraph{Finite perturbation surface.}
The Chen GFP packet is a finite perturbation record for synonymous edits in a protein-output assay.  Its observed row set contains $3556$ synonymous variants, split into two assay regions with $1124$ rows in region $1$ and $2432$ rows in region $2$.  The computation used $B=200$ null or resampling blocks and completed in $447.6606512069702$ seconds.  The scalar retained by the registered run is the slope of protein output on out-of-frame mRNA, namely
$$
\begin{aligned}
  \widehat\beta_{P\mid m_{\mathrm{oof}}} &= 0.016523671594873352,\\
  n_{\mathrm{region}\,1} &= 1124,\\
  n_{\mathrm{region}\,2} &= 2432,\\
  n_{\mathrm{variants}} &= 3556,\\
  B &= 200.
\end{aligned}
$$
The small positive slope is read here only as a finite assay coupling between a measured mRNA-side nuisance coordinate and the reported protein-side readout.  It is not by itself a translation-efficiency statistic, a folding statistic, a molecular-function statistic, or a fitness statistic.

\paragraph{Coordinate role.}
The relevant coordinate family is the $9$-axis $B^*_{Q6}$ residual basis used throughout the perturbation and mediation computations:
$$
\begin{aligned}
  Q = \{&K\_AAA,\ Arg\_AGR,\ Ile\_AUA,\ Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR,\ Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG,\\
       &Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY,\ Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG,\ Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY,\ f3\_stress\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
For this chapter, a coordinate does not enter the biological layer merely because it is computable from codon counts.  It enters only through a finite row design in which synonymous sequence changes, mRNA-side controls, and protein-side measurements are jointly present.  The Chen GFP run supplies that row design and its row counts, but the retained scalar is an mRNA-to-protein nuisance slope rather than a complete mediation statistic of the form $Q_i \to T_j \to P_k$.

\paragraph{Perturbation readback.}
The assay is therefore interpreted as a perturbation-window contact at the protein-output boundary.  The positive coefficient $0.016523671594873352$ says that the out-of-frame mRNA readout has a measured association with the protein readout on the finite Chen GFP rows.  The chapter does not promote that association to a claim that any one $B^*_{Q6}$ coordinate causes protein output.  The admissible readback is narrower: the finite synonymous window has enough rows to audit whether protein-output variation can remain after mRNA-side variation is visible, and the run records that this audit was actually executed with $3556$ variants and $200$ blocks.

\paragraph{Relation to the mediated protein-abundance matrix.}
A separate cross-organism matrix gives the standing mediation scale against which this perturbation record is interpreted.  In that matrix, the same $9$ coordinates were decomposed against modeled per-protein tAI and matched protein abundance across $12$ organisms.  The mediated fractions were in the unit interval for every organism: $0.6084594553474505$ in \emph{Bacillus subtilis}, $0.4872268357227724$ in \emph{Caenorhabditis elegans}, $0.3157053953614641$ in \emph{Danio rerio}, $0.6165167748739756$ in \emph{Drosophila melanogaster}, $0.612922252793667$ in \emph{Escherichia coli} K12 MG1655, $0.1976903964724546$ in chicken, $0.27206289160119157$ in human, $0.15488799907323988$ in mouse, $0.08397602919861291$ in \emph{Mycobacterium smegmatis}, $0.17489681798543982$ in rat, $0.9491551041216973$ in yeast, and $0.01850668564589326$ in \emph{Sulfolobus solfataricus}.  The Chen GFP record is not identified with that matrix.  Instead, it is a perturbation-side check that a synonymous edit surface with a protein readout is available, while the broader matrix specifies what would be needed to call a translation-mediated protein-abundance chain.

\paragraph{Modeled translation boundary.}
The modeled tAI survival computation used only $3$ organisms for the bounded coupling audit and explicitly did not provide powered survival: the organism count was $3$, the coupling threshold was $0.01$, $81$ entries exceeded that threshold, and the maximum absolute modeled coupling was $3801961.031607896$.  This matters for the Chen GFP interpretation because a protein-output perturbation surface is not automatically a translation-readout surface.  A claim of the form $Q_i \to T_j$ would require a translation readout such as tAI, stAI, ribosome occupancy, or translation efficiency in the same inferential slot.  A claim of the form $Q_i \to T_j \to P_k$ would additionally require that the $Q_i \to T_j$ step survive before the downstream protein readout is used as mediated evidence.

\paragraph{Neighboring perturbation calibration.}
The Chen GFP packet sits beside other finite perturbation records, which help set the numerical scale without changing the local claim.  The \emph{E. coli} mRFP perturbation run used $1459$ variants and $B=200$, with $\Delta R^2=0.05339906007191191$, $\Delta DL=19.089327473282296$ bits, and matched and within-null probabilities both $0.004975124378109453$.  The yeast synonymous-mutant run used $1783$ synonymous mutants over $20$ genes with $B=200$, and its real codon-stability coefficient correlation against the proxy was $0.39717918221186976$.  The human prime-editor run used $9000$ synonymous edits, a compact surface of $24000$ synonymous rows, $1822$ genes, and $B=200$.  These neighboring records show that the perturbation program can support powered finite-row tests when effect, null, and control scalars are retained.  The Chen GFP record is more limited: it retains row counts and the protein-on-out-of-frame-mRNA slope, but not a local $p$-value, $\Delta R^2$, or description-length improvement for a named $B^*_{Q6}$ axis.

\paragraph{Local verdict.}
The ready claim is therefore a bounded one.  The Chen GFP finite surface supports inclusion of this assay as a synonymous protein-output perturbation window with $3556$ rows, two explicit regions, $B=200$, and a recorded mRNA-side coupling coefficient.  It does not support a stronger statement that $B^*_{Q6}$ has been shown, in this assay alone, to realize translation, protein folding, molecular function, physical admissibility, mechanism, phenotype, selection, or universality.  Those conclusions require their own matched contacts and finite statistics.  In particular, a direct $Q_i \to P_k$ association, if later computed on this surface, must remain below the protein-realization line unless a same-row translation readout $T_j$ is present and the $Q_i \to T_j$ step survives the declared controls.
