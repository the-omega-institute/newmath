\subsection{Chen GFP Synonymous Window Escape}\label{subsec:chen-gfp-synonymous-window-escape}\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite surface.} The Chen GFP synonymous-perturbation record is a finite-row protein-output boundary for a coding-window perturbation assay. The row carrier is not the full biological mechanism of GFP expression. It is the finite table of synonymous variants, a split into two assayed regions, the out-of-frame mRNA covariate, and the observed protein-output readout. In the registered run, the table contains $3556$ synonymous variants, with $1124$ rows in region $1$ and $2432$ rows in region $2$. The empirical control slope of protein output on out-of-frame mRNA is
$$
\begin{aligned}
\widehat\beta_{P\mid \mathrm{oof\ mRNA}} &= 0.016523671594873352,
\end{aligned}
$$
and the randomization depth is $B=200$. The run identifier is \texttt{32de547f1ca8}, with runtime $447.6606512069702$ seconds. These numbers are the whole admissible finite contact for this chapter: they permit a bounded protein-output audit for synonymous GFP windows, not a claim that the coordinate has become a universal translation law.

\paragraph{Coordinate discipline.} The relevant residual coordinates are the nine $B^*_{Q6}$ axes used throughout the registered finite analyses:
$$
\begin{aligned}
Q=\{&K\_AAA,\ Arg\_AGR,\ Ile\_AUA,\ Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR,\ Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG,\\
&Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY,\ Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG,\ Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY,\ f3\_stress\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
In this chapter they remain coordinates attached to synonymous coding variation. The GFP experiment supplies a perturbation readout and an mRNA control covariate, but it does not by itself supply a same-row ribosome-profiling matrix, a folded-structure assay, a physical-admissibility measurement, a functional mechanism assay, or an organism-wide selection statistic. Consequently a direct association between a synonymous window coordinate and GFP protein output is read only as a finite perturbation signal until a translation step $q_i\to T_j$ is separately observed or bounded in the same design.

\paragraph{Readout relation.} The admissible relation has the shape
$$
\begin{aligned}
q_i &\longrightarrow P_k \mid Z,\mathrm{oof\ mRNA},
\end{aligned}
$$
where $q_i$ is a synonymous-window residual coordinate, $P_k$ is the GFP protein-output readout, and $Z$ contains the sequence and assay controls available to the finite perturbation panel. The positive finite fact is the existence of a powered row set large enough for a window-level protein-output audit: $n=3556$ variants and $B=200$ null resamples. The chapter does not replace the missing translation mediator by notation. Without a measured or explicitly bounded translation readout $T_j$, the stronger chain
$$
\begin{aligned}
q_i &\longrightarrow T_j \longrightarrow P_k
\end{aligned}
$$
remains an unfilled route for this specific GFP panel.

\paragraph{Cross-dataset calibration.} The restriction is not a rejection of the $B^*_{Q6}$ program; it is the local boundary imposed by the Chen GFP rows. In a separate multi-organism protein-abundance mediation run, the same nine-coordinate system was evaluated with modeled per-protein tAI and PAXdb protein abundance across $12$ organisms. There the joined row counts included $3739$ proteins for \textit{E. coli} K-12 MG1655, $5815$ for \textit{S. cerevisiae}, $19053$ for human, and $19395$ for mouse. The mediated fraction $M^{QTP}$ was in $[0,1]$ for every organism; examples include $0.612922252793667$ for \textit{E. coli}, $0.9491551041216973$ for yeast, $0.27206289160119157$ for human, and $0.15488799907323988$ for mouse. Those numbers show that the coordinate family has a finite route into a translation-conditioned protein-abundance decomposition when a translation readout is present. They do not transfer the mediator into the Chen GFP panel, whose verified rows currently report variant counts, region counts, a protein-on-out-of-frame-mRNA slope, and randomization depth rather than a same-row $T_j$ surface.

\paragraph{Axis information.} The strongest cross-layer axis identity presently available is also bounded. In the structural-order axis audit, the dominant coordinate was $f3\_stress$, with weighted held-out increment $0.0031744502294756994$ and sign-flip right-tail probability $0.03125$. Other axes did not pass that fold-level sign-flip gate: for example $Arg\_AGR$ had increment $0.0020139270616115154$ with probability $0.142578125$, and $Ile\_AUA$ had increment $0.0013067592718559024$ with probability $0.0693359375$. This supports treating $f3\_stress$ as a serious candidate axis when a row table has the right readout, but it does not license a claim that the Chen GFP synonymous escape rows measure folding, molecular function, or physical admissibility.

\paragraph{Perturbation comparison.} A nearby \textit{E. coli} mRFP synonymous-output panel gives a useful contrast. That finite run used $1459$ variants and $B=200$ randomizations, with base $R^2=0.12648386762689323$, full $R^2=0.1798829276988051$, increment $\Delta R^2=0.05339906007191191$, description-length gain $19.089327473282296$ bits, and matched-null probability $0.004975124378109453$. The Chen GFP record is larger at $3556$ variants, but its stored finite summary does not include the corresponding held-out $\Delta R^2$, description-length gain, or matched-null probability. Therefore the local conclusion is more conservative: the GFP table is powered as a finite perturbation surface, and it records a small out-of-frame mRNA slope, but the currently exported row summary is not enough to promote the panel to a quantified translation-mediated protein-realization result.

\paragraph{Local verdict.} The chapter-level readback is a bounded synonymously perturbed GFP protein-output audit. The finite facts establish $3556$ variants split as $1124+2432$, a protein-on-out-of-frame-mRNA slope of $0.016523671594873352$, and $200$ randomization draws. These support a controlled protein-output surface for the exact Chen GFP synonymous-window claim. They do not establish translation realization, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, mechanism, organismal phenotype, selection, or universality. Any such claim would require a separate layer-matched contact: ribosome occupancy or translation efficiency for translation, structural measurement or validated structural prediction for folding, a physical assay for admissibility, a biochemical or cellular assay for function, and multi-context evidence before any population-level law is asserted.
