\paragraph{Readout grammar.}
The object being read is not the amino-acid sequence of GFP, because the perturbations are synonymous.  The finite surface instead asks whether a B-star window coordinate can remain visible in protein output after the obvious transcript-side leakage route is measured.  The local numerical summary is therefore deliberately modest:
$$
\begin{aligned}
N_{\mathrm{variants}} &= 3556,\\
N_{\mathrm{region\ 1}} &= 1124,\\
N_{\mathrm{region\ 2}} &= 2432,\\
B &= 200,\\
\widehat{\lambda}_{P\leftarrow m_{\mathrm{oof}}} &= 0.016523671594873352.
\end{aligned}
$$
Here $m_{\mathrm{oof}}$ denotes the out-of-frame mRNA control readout and $P$ denotes the matched protein-output readout for the reporter.  The small positive slope is treated as a control diagnostic, not as a mechanistic coefficient.  It says that the protein channel is not being read in complete isolation from the transcript-side measurement, so any protein-facing interpretation must keep the mRNA route in the conditioning set.

\paragraph{Local verdict.}
The finite Chen2017 row surface is usable for a BioReality chapter because it has concrete variant counts, a region split, a fixed resampling budget, a matched mRNA-control scalar, and a protein-output endpoint.  Its legitimate readback is narrow: a synonymous GFP window can be audited for residual protein-output escape while the out-of-frame mRNA channel is kept visible.  The recorded slope $0.016523671594873352$ prevents a clean story in which protein output is read independently of transcript-side leakage, but it is also not large enough, by itself, to erase the reporter surface.  The chapter should therefore state the object as a controlled perturbational protein-output audit, not as a claim that the residual coordinate has reached translation, folding, molecular function, cellular mechanism, or an organism-level phenotype.

\paragraph{No-promotion statement.}
No translation realization follows from this GFP reporter surface unless a separate translation contact supplies ribosome occupancy, translation efficiency, tAI or stAI calibration, or another explicitly bounded translation readout on matched rows.  No folded-structure, physical-admissibility, molecular-function, causal-mechanism, selection, phenotype, or universal-law conclusion follows from the $3556$ variant rows, the $1124/2432$ region split, the $B=200$ audit, or the protein-on-out-of-frame-mRNA slope.  Those numbers support a finite synonymous perturbation readout with a transcript-side control; every higher biological layer requires its own measured contact and its own controlled finite statistic.
