\subsection{Synonymous GFP Perturbation Window at the Protein-Escape Boundary}
\label{sec:biorality-chen2017-gfp-synonymous-protein-escape}

\paragraph{Finite row surface.}
This record isolates a synonymous-perturbation test for the Chen 2017 GFP escape surface.  The finite table has $3556$ synonymous variants, split into two assayed regions with $1124$ rows in region $1$ and $2432$ rows in region $2$.  The computation used $B=200$ null or resampling units and completed in $447.6606512069702$ seconds.  The only scalar coupling retained from the run is the observed protein-on-out-of-frame-mRNA slope,
$$
\begin{aligned}
  n_{\mathrm{var}} &= 3556,\\
  n_{R_1} &= 1124,\\
  n_{R_2} &= 2432,\\
  B &= 200,\\
  \lambda_{P\mid \mathrm{oof\ mRNA}} &= 0.016523671594873352.
\end{aligned}
$$
These numbers make the record a finite perturbation surface, not a general statement about synonymous biology.  The rows are interpreted as local coding-window perturbations whose readout includes protein escape and an out-of-frame mRNA channel; they are not promoted to folding, molecular function, organismal phenotype, mechanism, selection, or universality.

\paragraph{Coordinate being tested.}
The BioReality coordinate under audit is a $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ synonymous residual window, treated as a controlled residual coordinate rather than as a biological object by itself.  A positive downstream protein association is admissible only when the same coordinate first survives a translation-layer or translation-adjacent readout.  In this chapter the retained Chen 2017 finite rows are therefore read as a perturbation-window contact with protein output and an out-of-frame mRNA diagnostic, while amino-acid sequence, organism, GC3, CDS length, and the $M$-density baseline remain controls.  The local test does not license a direct inference $Q_i \to P_k$ unless the intermediate readout slot $T_j$ is present and non-null.

\paragraph{Controlled matrix form.}
The admissible readback is the mediated matrix
$$
\begin{aligned}
  Q_i &\longrightarrow T_j \longrightarrow P_k,\\
  T_j &\in \{\mathrm{tAI},\ \mathrm{stAI},\ \mathrm{ribosome\ occupancy},\ \mathrm{translation\ efficiency},\ \mathrm{oof\ mRNA\ diagnostic}\},\\
  P_k &= \mathrm{matched\ protein\ abundance\ or\ protein\ escape\ readout}.
\end{aligned}
$$
For the Chen 2017 GFP surface, the finite report gives the region sizes, total variant count, resampling count, runtime, and the small positive slope $\lambda_{P\mid \mathrm{oof\ mRNA}}=0.016523671594873352$.  It does not, by itself, give a complete mediated $Q_i \to T_j \to P_k$ decomposition with a per-coordinate survival table, a region-specific $p$-value, or a held-out $\Delta R^2$ for each $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ coordinate.  The paper-level statement is therefore deliberately narrower: the GFP synonymous perturbation surface supplies a finite protein-escape contact that can participate in a translation-gated protein-readout audit, but the finite numbers reported here do not turn the coordinate into a mechanism.

\paragraph{Connection to the broader protein-abundance audit.}
The surrounding powered protein-abundance audit supplies the relevant comparison class.  In that matrix, nine $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ coordinates were used: $K\_AAA$, $Arg\_AGR$, $Ile\_AUA$, $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$, $Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG$, $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$, $Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG$, $Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY$, and $f3\_stress$.  Across $12$ organism-level protein-abundance joins, the reported mediation fractions remained inside the unit interval.  The largest listed mediated fraction was yeast with $M^{QTP}=0.9491551041216973$, with total $R^2_{QP}=0.29837624994140827$, direct conditioned $R^2_{QP\mid T}=0.0151709093608293$, and indirect difference $0.28320534058057895$.  Escherichia coli K12 MG1655 had $n=3739$ joined proteins, $M^{QTP}=0.612922252793667$, total $R^2_{QP}=0.10767526677985896$, conditioned direct $R^2_{QP\mid T}=0.04167869969498872$, and indirect difference $0.06599656708487024$.  Those cross-organism values justify the matrix grammar used here, but they do not replace the missing Chen-specific per-coordinate mediation table.

\paragraph{Relation to modeled translation readouts.}
A separate modeled-tAI contact computed the same nine residual coordinates from $61$ sense codons in $3$ organisms.  That contact found $81$ entries above the bounded coupling threshold $0.01$, with maximum absolute modeled coupling $3801961.031607896$, but explicitly remained underpowered for a strong survival claim because only $3$ organisms were available rather than at least $40$ matched species.  The GFP perturbation surface is read under the same discipline: modeled tAI, stAI, ribosome occupancy, translation efficiency, mRNA controls, and protein escape are distinct readouts.  A modeled weight or a protein-output association cannot substitute for a controlled survival step.

\paragraph{Perturbation comparison.}
A nearby synonymous perturbation contact in E. coli mRFP used $1459$ variants and $B=200$, reporting $\Delta R^2=0.05339906007191191$, $\Delta \mathrm{DL}=19.089327473282296$ bits, and matched-null and within-null probabilities both equal to $0.004975124378109453$.  That comparison shows the kind of scalar evidence needed when a protein-output claim is pushed beyond a row-count and slope report.  The Chen 2017 GFP record does not expose the same $\Delta R^2$, description-length, or null-probability fields in the finite row supplied here.  Consequently, the local chapter keeps the Chen surface at a bounded protein-escape readout: it is useful evidence for auditing synonymous perturbation windows, while the stronger mediated protein-realization claim requires the missing row-level statistic.

\paragraph{Local verdict.}
The finite evidence supports a ready but bounded BioReality chapter.  The row counts are concrete, the region split is explicit, and the observed protein-on-out-of-frame-mRNA slope is finite and small.  The admissible conclusion is that the Chen 2017 GFP synonymous perturbation window is a real finite surface for testing whether $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual windows can participate in a translation-gated protein-output audit.  The conclusion is not that $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ causes protein escape, changes folding, realizes molecular function, supplies a physical-admissibility constraint, or establishes a universal synonymous-code law.  Any such stronger statement would require a separate layer-matched contact and a controlled statistic showing that the same coordinate survives the translation-readout step before downstream protein association is interpreted.
