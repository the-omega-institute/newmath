\subsection{Chen GFP synonymous-window protein-escape audit}
\label{subsec:biorReality-chen-gfp-synonymous-protein-escape}

\paragraph{Finite row surface.}
This record treats the Chen GFP synonymous perturbation table as a finite windowed surface rather than as a free-standing claim about protein realization.  The observed table contains $3556$ synonymous variants, partitioned into $1124$ rows in the first assayed region and $2432$ rows in the second assayed region.  The registered computation used $200$ resampling blocks and completed in $447.6606512069702$ seconds.  The scalar finite-row readout retained for this chapter is the fitted slope from out-of-frame mRNA signal into the protein readout,
$$
\begin{aligned}
 n_{\mathrm{variants}} &= 3556,\\
 n_{\mathrm{region\ 1}} &= 1124,\\
 n_{\mathrm{region\ 2}} &= 2432,\\
 B &= 200,\\
 \widehat\beta_{P\leftarrow \mathrm{oof\ mRNA}} &= 0.016523671594873352.
\end{aligned}
$$
The slope is intentionally read as a nuisance-link scalar on the perturbation surface.  It says that the protein response is not being handled as an isolated sequence-only coordinate: an mRNA-linked channel was estimated and retained in the finite audit.  It does not, by itself, identify a translation mechanism or a protein-state mechanism.

\paragraph{Coordinate role.}
The biological object in this chapter is a controlled perturbation matrix whose rows are synonymous GFP edits and whose columns include region membership, an out-of-frame mRNA-linked quantity, and a downstream protein readout.  The B$^*_{Q6}$ coordinates are allowed to enter only as residual coding-sequence coordinates on this matrix.  They are not promoted to amino-acid substitutions, folding coordinates, molecular-function coordinates, or organismal phenotype coordinates.  The admissible readback has the form
$$
\begin{aligned}
 q_i &\longrightarrow t_j \longrightarrow p_k,\\
 t_j &\in \{\mathrm{tAI},\mathrm{stAI},\mathrm{ribosome\ occupancy},\mathrm{translation\ efficiency}\},\\
 p_k &= \mathrm{matched\ protein\ abundance\ or\ assayed\ protein\ output}.
\end{aligned}
$$
For the Chen GFP table, the finite-row evidence presently exposed by the audit is the perturbation size, the regional split, the resampling count, runtime, and the small positive out-of-frame mRNA-to-protein slope.  The row table is therefore suitable for a bounded protein-output audit, but not for a claim that a B$^*_{Q6}$ coordinate has independently crossed a measured translation step.

\paragraph{Relation to the larger mediation surface.}
The broader protein-abundance mediation computation supplies a separate cross-organism calibration of what a controlled $Q\to T\to P$ calculation looks like.  In that computation the B$^*_{Q6}$ coordinate list has $9$ entries, namely $K\_AAA$, $Arg\_AGR$, $Ile\_AUA$, $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$, $Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG$, $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$, $Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG$, $Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY$, and $f3\_stress$.  The joined organism panel includes, for example, $5815$ yeast proteins, $3739$ Escherichia coli K12 MG1655 proteins, $19053$ human proteins, and $19395$ mouse proteins.  Its mediated fractions remain bounded between $0$ and $1$, with representative values $0.9491551041216973$ in yeast, $0.612922252793667$ in Escherichia coli K12 MG1655, $0.27206289160119157$ in human, and $0.15488799907323988$ in mouse.  These numbers show the intended finite mediation grammar: a direct $Q\to P$ association is not the desired object unless a translation readout $T$ is present and is explicitly conditioned.

\paragraph{Translation-readout boundary.}
The modeled translation-survival contact confirms that the residual coordinate vocabulary is computable without granting it a measured ribosome interpretation.  The modeled tAI contact used $3$ organisms, $61$ sense codons, and $9$ B$^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinates, with $81$ entries above the bounded coupling threshold $0.01$ and maximum absolute modeled coupling $3801961.031607896$.  The same audit states that $3$ organisms are insufficient for powered survival and that at least $40$ matched species would be required for a powered survival request.  Thus the Chen GFP perturbation rows can be placed beside a modeled translation-readout vocabulary, but the chapter does not convert modeled tAI, out-of-frame mRNA association, or synonymous-window geometry into observed ribosome occupancy.

\paragraph{Local verdict.}
The finite Chen GFP audit is ready as a bounded synonymous-perturbation protein-output record.  It supports the statement that a $3556$-variant synonymous GFP table has been processed with a two-region split and a retained out-of-frame mRNA-to-protein slope of $0.016523671594873352$.  It does not support the stronger statement that a B$^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinate has realized protein output through a validated translation readout.  A positive protein association in this chapter remains inadmissible as protein realization unless the same finite rows also contain, or are joined to, a controlled translation readout and the path $q_i\to t_j\to p_k$ survives the stated covariates.

\paragraph{No-promotion rule.}
Nothing in this chapter licenses claims about translation dynamics, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, perturbation mechanism, selection, phenotype, or universality.  Translation would require a separate matched ribosome-occupancy or translation-efficiency contact, folding would require a structure-level contact, physical admissibility would require a physical or stability assay, function would require a functional assay, and universality would require multi-organism controls with simpler-baseline ablation.  The present chapter remains a finite, controlled protein-output audit for a specific synonymous GFP perturbation surface.
