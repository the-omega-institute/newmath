\subsection{Chen 2017 GFP Synonymous Escape Window}
\label{subsec:brioreality-chen2017-gfp-synonymous-escape-window}

\paragraph{Finite row surface.}
This record treats the Chen 2017 GFP synonymous-variant table as a bounded perturbation surface rather than as a direct claim about folded protein state or organismal adaptation.  The finite panel contains $3556$ synonymous variants split across two declared windows, with $1124$ variants in the first region and $2432$ variants in the second region.  The run identifier was \texttt{32de547f1ca8}, and the randomized audit budget was $B=200$.  The scalar readout available for this chapter is the fitted slope from out-of-frame mRNA to protein, recorded as $0.016523671594873352$.  Thus the chapter may say that the variant table has enough finite rows for a synonym-window protein-output audit and that the observed protein readout is not simply replaced by the out-of-frame mRNA covariate.  It may not say, from these rows alone, that a B$^*_Q6$ coordinate has been promoted to a molecular mechanism.

\paragraph{Coordinates and readout discipline.}
The admissible coordinates are the nine residual axes used by the surrounding B$^*_Q6$ packet: $K\_AAA$, $Arg\_AGR$, $Ile\_AUA$, $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$, $Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG$, $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$, $Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG$, $Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY$, and $f3\_stress$.  In this chapter they are not treated as biochemical causes.  They are finite coding-window coordinates tested against a synonymous perturbation readout.  A downstream protein signal is admissible only after the row design keeps amino-acid sequence fixed and keeps transcript abundance, codon composition, window position, and simpler codon baselines from being silently promoted into the residual coordinates.  The local datum $\lambda_{\mathrm{oof\ mRNA}\to P}=0.016523671594873352$ is therefore read as a control-slope diagnostic, not as an independent protein-realization proof.

\paragraph{Relation to the wider translation mediation audit.}
The larger matched Q--T--P mediation packet supplies the comparison scale for interpreting this perturbation table.  In that audit, $12$ organisms were joined with real CDS codon counts and measured PAXdb protein abundance; the joined protein counts ranged from $1656$ in \emph{Sulfolobus solfataricus} to $19395$ in mouse.  The modeled per-protein tAI route produced positive statistical mediation fractions in all listed organisms, for example $M^{QTP}=0.9491551041216973$ in yeast, $0.612922252793667$ in \emph{E. coli} K12 MG1655, $0.27206289160119157$ in human, and $0.01850668564589326$ in \emph{Sulfolobus}.  Those values show that B$^*_Q6$ residuals can enter a controlled protein-abundance mediation calculation when a translation readout is explicitly present.  They do not by themselves certify the Chen GFP perturbation as a causal translation mechanism, because the Chen finite row recorded here contributes the variant count, regional split, audit budget, runtime, and out-of-frame mRNA-to-protein slope, but not a full per-coordinate mediation matrix.

\paragraph{Comparison with a powered perturbation control.}
A useful bounded comparison is the Nieuwkoop \emph{E. coli} mRFP synonymous protein-output run.  That run used $1459$ variants and the same audit budget $B=200$, with $R^2=0.12648386762689323$ for controls, $R^2=0.1798829276988051$ after adding the B$^*_Q6$ residual block, $\Delta R^2=0.05339906007191191$, $\Delta DL=19.089327473282296$ bits, and matched and within-null probabilities both equal to $0.004975124378109453$.  The Chen GFP chapter is more conservative: it records a finite synonymous perturbation surface and a protein readout with a small out-of-frame mRNA slope, but it does not import the Nieuwkoop significance numbers into the Chen row.  The comparison only fixes the evidential grammar: a perturbation chapter can become stronger when it carries its own baseline $R^2$, residual increment, description-length gain, and null probabilities.

\paragraph{Translation boundary.}
The separate modeled-tAI survival audit computed the same nine B$^*_Q6$ residual coordinates in a $61$-sense-codon space across $3$ organisms.  It found $81$ entries above the bounded coupling threshold $0.01$ and a maximum absolute modeled entry of $3801961.031607896$, but it also explicitly refused powered translation survival because $3$ organisms are not enough for the requested powered survival criterion of at least $40$ matched species.  This matters locally because the Chen GFP synonymous table is a protein-output perturbation record, not an automatic translation-readout survival record.  A direct $Q_i\to P_k$ association cannot be read as protein realization unless the corresponding $Q_i\to T_j$ step is present or the perturbation design supplies an equivalent bounded translation contact.

\paragraph{Local finite claim.}
The chapter-specific assertion is therefore the following finite statement.  A synonymous GFP perturbation table with $3556$ variants, split as $1124+2432$, admits a B$^*_Q6$ window audit against a protein readout while retaining an out-of-frame mRNA control slope of $0.016523671594873352$ under a $B=200$ audit budget.  This is evidence that the row surface is suitable for a controlled protein-output residual analysis.  It is not, by itself, evidence for translation dynamics, folding, physical admissibility, biological function, mechanism, selection, or universality.  Those stronger readings require distinct row-matched contacts at the relevant layer: ribosome or translation-efficiency rows for translation, structural or physical assays for folding and admissibility, functional assays for molecular function, perturbation or phenotype panels for system phenotype, and multi-context controls before any general biological rule is entertained.

\paragraph{Break condition.}
The local positive reading breaks if the protein association is absorbed by mRNA, region, position, codon-count, amino-acid-preserving, or simpler codon-adaptation baselines, or if no same-coordinate translation readout can be supplied for a claimed mediation path.  In that case the Chen GFP table remains a finite synonymous perturbation audit with a recorded protein readout and control slope, but it does not enter the stronger Q--T--P mediation class.  The current finite row values are sufficient for a bounded chapter claim and insufficient for mechanism, folding, physical admissibility, function, phenotype, or universal-law promotion.
