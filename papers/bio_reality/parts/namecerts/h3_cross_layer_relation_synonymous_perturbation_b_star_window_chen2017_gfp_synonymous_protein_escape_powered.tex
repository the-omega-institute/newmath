\subsection{Chen 2017 GFP synonymous escape window}
\label{sec:bioreality-chen2017-gfp-synonymous-protein-escape}

\paragraph{Finite perturbation surface.}
This record treats the Chen 2017 GFP synonymous library as a finite perturbation surface rather than as a general law about synonymous coding.  The measured table contributes $3556$ synonymous variants, split by the registered window audit into $1124$ variants in region 1 and $2432$ variants in region 2.  The resampling budget was $B=200$, and the run completed in $447.6606512069702$ seconds under experiment id $32de547f1ca8$.  The scalar used here is the fitted protein-on-out-of-frame-mRNA slope
$$
\begin{aligned}
\widehat{\lambda}_{\mathrm{protein}\mid \mathrm{oof\ mRNA}} &= 0.016523671594873352.
\end{aligned}
$$
The slope is small and positive on this finite surface.  It is therefore recorded as a bounded protein-output contact for a synonymous-perturbation window, not as a statement that the residual coordinate by itself has reached translation, folding, function, phenotype, or selection.

\paragraph{Coordinate interpretation.}
The finite carrier is the set of observed synonymous GFP edits together with the registered B$^*_Q6$ window residual coordinates and the measured output slots available in the dataset.  The readout is local: one may compare protein-output variation against the out-of-frame mRNA control inside the same perturbation table.  A direct $Q_i \to P_k$ reading is not admitted as a protein-realization claim unless the translation-facing part of the chain has also been supplied.  In symbolic form the admissible relation is the mediated surface
$$
\begin{aligned}
Q_i &\longrightarrow T_j \longrightarrow P_k,\\
T_j &\in \{\hbox{tAI},\ \hbox{stAI},\ \hbox{ribosome occupancy},\ \hbox{translation efficiency}\},\\
P_k &= \hbox{matched protein abundance or protein-output readout}.
\end{aligned}
$$
For the Chen GFP table, the finite-row result supplies a perturbational protein-output slope and variant counts.  It does not by itself supply a matched ribosome-profiling matrix, a measured translation-efficiency denominator, a folding assay, a physical-admissibility assay, or a functional rescue assay.

\paragraph{Relation to the larger Q-to-T-to-P audit.}
The broader cross-layer audit gives the comparison class for interpreting this GFP surface.  In the organism-level protein-abundance mediation run, the B$^*_Q6$ coordinate list had $9$ coordinates, and the joined protein counts included, for example, $3739$ proteins in Escherichia coli K12 MG1655, $5815$ in Saccharomyces cerevisiae, $19053$ in Homo sapiens, and $19395$ in Mus musculus.  On that separate mediation surface the reported mediated fractions included $0.612922252793667$ for Escherichia coli K12 MG1655, $0.9491551041216973$ for Saccharomyces cerevisiae, $0.27206289160119157$ for Homo sapiens, and $0.15488799907323988$ for Mus musculus.  Those values show that a controlled $Q \to T \to P$ decomposition is a meaningful finite-row object when the joined rows exist.  They are not imported as proof that the Chen GFP slope has a measured translation mediator; they only fix the admissible shape of the claim.

\paragraph{Translation-readout boundary.}
The modeled translation-survival audit computed the B$^*_Q6$ residual coordinates $\mathrm{Arg\_AGR}$, $\mathrm{Ile\_AUA}$, $\mathrm{K\_AAA}$, $\mathrm{Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR}$, $\mathrm{Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG}$, $\mathrm{Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG}$, $\mathrm{Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY}$, $\mathrm{Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY}$, and $f_3\text{-}\mathrm{stress}$.  It used $61$ sense codons and $3$ organisms, with $81$ modeled entries above threshold $0.01$ and maximum absolute modeled coupling $3801961.031607896$.  The same audit explicitly records that $3$ organisms are insufficient for a powered survival claim requiring at least $40$ matched species.  Thus the Chen GFP perturbation table is not promoted through the modeled-tAI surface alone.  The local GFP result remains a finite perturbation output, while any translation-realization statement still requires an organism-matched translation readout and the corresponding controlled statistic.

\paragraph{Controls and residual status.}
The standing control set is amino-acid sequence or composition, organism, GC3, CDS length, M-density baseline, and matched mRNA abundance when the data allow it.  The yeast structure-mediation audit illustrates why this restriction matters: in a $4759$-gene five-way join, the direct B$^*_Q6$ to protein-abundance residual association had $R^2_{QP}=0.2715520808117803$, while conditioning on measured translation efficiency and mRNA stability reduced the residual increment to $R^2_{QP\mid T,M}=0.13773967696311287$; adding the structure proxy gave $R^2_{QP\mid T,M,S}=0.13759011208318542$.  The Chen GFP slope is therefore read with the same discipline: it is a measured finite perturbation scalar, but it is not a license to skip the translation and mRNA-control layers.

\paragraph{Comparable perturbation scale.}
A separate E. coli mRFP synonymous perturbation audit gives a useful scale check without replacing the Chen table.  That run used $1459$ variants, reported $\Delta R^2=0.05339906007191191$, $\Delta\mathrm{DL}=19.089327473282296$ bits, and matched-null and within-null $p$-values both equal to $0.004975124378109453$.  The Chen GFP table has a larger variant count, $3556$, but the registered finite-row output currently exposes only the region counts, the resampling budget, runtime, and the protein-on-out-of-frame-mRNA slope.  Since no matched $p$-value, description-length gain, or held-out $\Delta R^2$ is present for this exact Chen record, the conclusion is intentionally narrower.

\paragraph{Local verdict.}
The finite evidence supports the existence of a registered synonymous GFP perturbation surface with $3556$ variants and a small positive protein-output association after the out-of-frame mRNA comparison, summarized by $\widehat{\lambda}=0.016523671594873352$.  The admissible readback is a bounded protein-output perturbation entry for this table.  It does not establish translation realization, ribosome dynamics, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, organismal phenotype, mechanism, selection, or universality.  Any such higher-layer statement requires a separate layer-matched contact and a controlled finite-row statistic that first shows the relevant $Q_i \to T_j$ survival step before interpreting downstream protein-output variation.
