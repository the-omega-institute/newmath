\paragraph{What the finite result says.}
The near-zero collinearity means that the ESE hexamer perturbation coordinate is not merely a duplicate of the MaxEnt splice-site perturbation coordinate on the audited finite rows.  A useful local reading is therefore:
$$
\begin{aligned}
&\text{ESE coordinate present} \\
&\quad\wedge\; \text{splice-site coordinate present} \\
&\quad\wedge\; \operatorname{corr}(\Delta ESEseq,\Delta MaxEnt)=-0.008429245619 \\
&\quad\Longrightarrow\; \text{non-collinearity of these two finite exon-level coordinates.}
\end{aligned}
$$

The finite claim is especially narrow because the supplied number is a coordinate-correlation statistic; it is not a regression coefficient for inclusion, a causal perturbation estimate, or a conserved splicing law.
