\paragraph{Finite row.}

$\mathrm{h3.cross\_layer\_relation.exon\_inclusion.ese\_hexamer\_exon\_inclusion\_adamson2018}$

$-0.008429245619$ between the ESE-sequence direction and the MaxEnt splice-strength direction.  The run identifier is

Thus the row is not empty, not merely a named route, and not a statement about a missing biological surface: it is a concrete finite comparison between two splice-regulatory coordinates on the same exon-inclusion problem.

\paragraph{Mathematical object.}
Let $X$ denote the finite set of $1037$ rows.  Each row carries an exon-inclusion readout together with two scalar coordinates: an ESE-hexamer score change, written $E(x)$, and a splice-site MaxEnt score change, written $M_{\mathrm{splice}}(x)$.  The table-level relation recorded here is the signed collinearity
$$
\begin{aligned}
\rho(E,M_{\mathrm{splice}}) &= -0.008429245619,\\
|X| &= 1037.
\end{aligned}
$$
The magnitude is close to zero, and the sign is weakly negative.  The appropriate finite-row interpretation is therefore separation, not reinforcement: the ESE-hexamer coordinate and the MaxEnt coordinate are both splice-layer descriptors, but this run does not show that they collapse to the same one-dimensional direction.

\paragraph{Splice-layer reading.}
The admissible biological reading is narrow.  The row supports the statement that, in this Adamson-style exon-inclusion surface, the ESE-hexamer score direction is nearly orthogonal to the MaxEnt splice-strength direction at the level of the recorded finite comparison.  A useful way to phrase the result is that the ESE coordinate is not merely a disguised MaxEnt splice-site score in this table.  It may be used as a separate splice-regulatory axis for finite-row bookkeeping, provided that any downstream claim remains attached to a matched exon-inclusion readout and to the same finite data surface.

\paragraph{No-promotion boundary.}
The finite splice result may be used only as a splice-regulatory relation.  A statement about translation would require a separate position-resolved ribosome-footprint or translation-efficiency contact joined to the relevant coding-sequence rows.  A statement about protein output would require a matched protein-abundance contact.  A statement about folding, physical admissibility, biological function, system phenotype, mechanism, or a universal biological law would require its own layer-matched evidence surface and a new finite statistic on that surface.  The present row supplies none of those contacts.  Its strength is exactly its boundedness: $1037$ finite exon-inclusion rows show near-zero ESE-versus-MaxEnt collinearity, and nothing in that fact licenses promotion beyond the splice-inclusion layer.
