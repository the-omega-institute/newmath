\paragraph{Exon-inclusion verdict.}
For the Adamson ESE-hexamer row, the finite conclusion is narrow.  The panel supports the statement that the ESE perturbation coordinate and the splice-site-strength perturbation coordinate are not collinear in the recorded $1037$-row exon-inclusion table.  It does not support a positive $f_3$-stress survival statistic for position-resolved ribosome occupancy, because the required same-organism, same-CDS, same-window codon counts, wobble weights, matched mRNA abundance, and ribosome-footprint rows are absent from this row.  A legitimate statistic would have to estimate
$$
\begin{aligned}
S_{f_3\text{-}\mathrm{stress}}(T_{\mathrm{position}}\mid Z)
\end{aligned}
$$
where $T_{\mathrm{position}}$ is a position-resolved translation readout and $Z$ includes amino-acid composition or amino-acid-bin controls, organism, GC3, CDS length, $M$-density, matched mRNA abundance, coverage, mappability, rank sufficiency, and simpler-baseline ablation.  If this controlled survival is non-positive, rank-degenerate, or not computable, the $f_3$ coordinate remains a coding-topology residual.
