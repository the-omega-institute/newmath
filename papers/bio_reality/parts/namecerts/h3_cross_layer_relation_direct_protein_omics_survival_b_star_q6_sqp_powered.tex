\subsection{Direct protein-omics survival of $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates}
\label{sec:bioreality-q6-direct-protein-omics-survival}

\paragraph{Finite carrier.}
The finite panel for this record is a CDS-by-protein table in which each row carries real coding-sequence codon counts, nine synonymous residual coordinates, organism-specific abundance joins, and a measured protein-omics readout. The residual coordinates are
$K\_AAA$, $Arg\_AGR$, $Ile\_AUA$, $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$, $Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG$, $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$, $Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG$, $Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY$, and $f3\_stress$. They are used only after the amino-acid quotient: the row does not ask whether lysine, arginine, leucine, serine, threonine, or global amino-acid composition explains abundance, but whether the remaining synonymous coordinates retain predictive mass after the declared baseline controls.

\paragraph{Readout and controls.}
The protein readout is $\log_{10}(\mathrm{abundance\_ppm})$ from measured PAXdb protein abundance, joined to real CDS codon-count records. The fitted baseline contains an intercept, CDS length, amino-acid composition, GC3, and the $M$-density baseline induced by the support of the nine projected coordinates. In every organism listed below the residualized coordinate matrix has rank $9$, while the baseline matrix has rank $23$ with $24$ control columns reported. The positive-entry threshold for a coordinate contribution is $10^{-12}$, and the degeneracy guard for $R^2$ is $0.999999$.

\paragraph{Observed survival surface.}
Let $Q$ denote the residualized $9$-coordinate block and let $P$ denote the residualized protein-abundance column. The reported direct protein-omics survival score is the controlled partial protein-abundance readback
$$
\begin{aligned}
S^{QP} &= R^2(P \sim Q \mid Z),\\
Z &= \{\mathrm{length},\mathrm{amino\ acid\ composition},\mathrm{GC3},M\text{-}\mathrm{density}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The finite run reports a positive $S^{QP}$ in all fifteen joined organisms. The largest observed value is in $Saccharomyces\ cerevisiae$, where $R^2_{QP}=0.29837624994140827$ on $5815$ joined proteins with join hit rate $0.9156038419146592$. The next largest values are $Escherichia\ coli\ K12\ MG1655$ with $R^2_{QP}=0.10767526677985896$ on $3739$ joined proteins and hit rate $0.9978649586335735$, and $Caenorhabditis\ elegans$ with $R^2_{QP}=0.10217906758013379$ on $11661$ joined proteins and hit rate $0.8913774652193854$. Other positive entries are $Dictyostelium\ discoideum$ at $0.08141786718177903$ on $6974$ rows, $Drosophila\ melanogaster$ at $0.0770952887136557$ on $13295$ rows, $Pseudomonas\ aeruginosa\ PAO1$ at $0.0646471273126996$ on $5006$ rows, $Sulfolobus\ solfataricus$ at $0.06397514251295629$ on $1656$ rows, $Bacillus\ subtilis\ subsp.\ subtilis\ str.\ 168$ at $0.061906245886660105$ on $4042$ rows, $Mus\ musculus$ at $0.049827984062539195$ on $19395$ rows, $Danio\ rerio$ at $0.04613529807085213$ on $14410$ rows, $Homo\ sapiens$ at $0.04454565118019448$ on $19053$ rows, $Gallus\ gallus$ at $0.03980793596647631$ on $12418$ rows, $Mycobacterium\ smegmatis\ str.\ mc2\ 155$ at $0.03209009170649934$ on $4129$ rows, $Rattus\ norvegicus$ at $0.02763998603083845$ on $13322$ rows, and $Halobacterium\ salinarum$ at $0.019256877907572044$ on $1747$ rows. In each case all nine residual coordinates are reported as surviving coordinates.

\paragraph{Rank and residual energy.}
The strongest yeast row is not a small-rank artifact: after controls it has residual degrees of freedom $5792$, $\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde X)=9$, $\operatorname{rank}(Z)=23$, residual protein-abundance energy $2958.1865890925337$, and explained energy $882.6526210803958$. The human row has residual degrees of freedom $19030$, residual energy $30769.5367150478$, and explained energy $1370.6490494847067$. The mouse row has residual degrees of freedom $19372$, residual energy $48806.47856410273$, and explained energy $2431.9284360407714$. The smallest joined organism, $Sulfolobus\ solfataricus$, still reports residual degrees of freedom $1633$, residual energy $980.7615792975887$, explained energy $62.74436180679531$, and full residual coordinate rank. Thus the direct protein-omics statistic is not being read from the three-organism modeled-tAI surface; it is computed on per-protein rows with organism-specific abundance joins.

\paragraph{Translation boundary.}
This record is deliberately narrower than a translation-mechanism claim. A separate modeled translation contact computed nine $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ synonymous residuals in a three-organism modeled-tAI panel and found $81$ bounded-coupling entries above threshold $0.01$, with maximum absolute $S_{ij}=3801961.031607896$, but that panel had only $3$ organisms and explicitly remained underpowered for powered survival. It supplied modeled tAI weights from tRNA gene copy and dos Reis wobble weights, not measured ribosome occupancy. Consequently the present $S^{QP}$ table cannot be promoted to a $Q\to T\to P$ mediation result. A positive protein-abundance survival entry says that a synonymous residual coordinate retains direct protein-omics predictive mass after the listed CDS controls; it does not say that translation efficiency mediates the signal.

\paragraph{Relation to RNA half-life contacts.}
The neighboring mRNA-stability rows show that the same coordinate family also has controlled RNA-half-life readbacks, but those rows do not repair the missing protein-mediation step. In yeast, the $S^{QM}$ half-life run reports $R^2_{QM}=0.05179429044975085$, same-join $R^2_{QP}=0.2761140373962526$, cosine $0.8040935468384264$, and permutation $p=0.000999000999000999$, with $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$ dominant. In $E.\ coli$, the corresponding half-life value is $R^2_{QM}=0.02298556640056076$, same-join $R^2_{QP}=0.11632311854858134$, cosine $-0.7784923375569599$, and the dominant coordinate is $Arg\_AGR$. In human consensus relative half-life, the value is $R^2_{QM}=0.010503670362051522$, same-join $R^2_{QP}=0.04095215557813418$, cosine $0.6351183883924848$, and $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$ is dominant. These RNA rows are useful cross-layer diagnostics, but they are not ribosome-footprint or translation-efficiency mediators for the protein-abundance table.

\paragraph{Local interpretation.}
The finite result supports a direct protein-omics association: under the declared baseline, the residual synonymous $Q6$ coordinate block explains nonzero residual variation in measured protein abundance across all joined organism panels. The result is strongest in yeast, moderate in several bacterial and metazoan panels, and smallest but still positive in halophilic archaeal and rat panels. The correct readback is therefore DNA-to-protein-omics predictive irreducibility at the row level. It is not evidence for a universal codon law, a causal perturbation effect, ribosome-speed mediation, protein folding, localization, post-translational modification, protein stability, biochemical function, organismal phenotype, physical admissibility, or a general mechanism. Any such stronger statement would require an additional same-row contact: measured translation readout $T$, matched mRNA abundance, and a mediation fit showing that conditioning on $T$ removes or reduces the direct $Q\to P$ signal.

\paragraph{Verdict.}
This chapter is ready as a bounded direct protein-omics survival record. It passes the finite-row criterion for $S^{QP}>0$ with full residual coordinate rank in every organism listed, and it keeps the interpretation at direct residual protein-abundance prediction. It remains blocked, by design, for translation mediation and for all higher biological layers until separate layer-matched contacts are attached and evaluated.
