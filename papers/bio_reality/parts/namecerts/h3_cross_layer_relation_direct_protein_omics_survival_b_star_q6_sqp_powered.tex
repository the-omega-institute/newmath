\subsection{Direct Protein-Omics Survival of $B^*_{Q6}$ Synonymous Residual Coordinates}
\label{sec:bior_reality-q6-direct-protein-omics-survival}

\paragraph{Finite carrier.}
The finite record considered here is a CDS-by-organism table in which each coding sequence is assigned a $B^*_{Q6}$ synonymous residual vector, a controlled protein-omics readout, and the baseline covariates needed to keep the readout from being mistaken for amino-acid composition or simple sequence composition.  The residual coordinates used in the protein-omics fit are
$$
\begin{aligned}
q \in \{&K\_AAA,\ Arg\_AGR,\ Ile\_AUA,\ Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR,\ Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG,\\
&Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY,\ Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG,\ Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY,\ f3\_stress\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the coordinate dimension is $9$.  These coordinates are not raw codon frequencies.  They are residual codon-topology coordinates retained after the amino-acid quotient, so their intended question is whether synonymous structure leaves information in a downstream measured table after standard compositional explanations have been removed.

\paragraph{Observed protein-abundance rows.}
The protein-omics contact uses measured PAXdb protein abundance, read as $\log_{10}(\mathrm{abundance\_ppm})$, joined to real CDS codon counts.  Seven organism-level joined tables were available.  The joined row counts and hit rates were $14410$ rows at hit rate $1.000000$ for \emph{Danio rerio}, $3739$ rows at hit rate $0.997865$ for \emph{Escherichia coli} K-12 MG1655, $12418$ rows at hit rate $1.000000$ for \emph{Gallus gallus}, $19053$ rows at hit rate $0.977929$ for \emph{Homo sapiens}, $4129$ rows at hit rate $1.000000$ for \emph{Mycobacterium smegmatis} str. MC$^2$ 155, $5006$ rows at hit rate $0.994438$ for \emph{Pseudomonas aeruginosa} PAO1, and $5815$ rows at hit rate $0.915604$ for \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}.  These are finite observed rows, not a universal biological population.

\paragraph{Controlled survival statistic.}
For each organism, the protein-abundance response was residualized against the baseline controls before testing the $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinates.  The control design used $24$ columns, with $\operatorname{rank}(Z)=23$ in every organism, and the residual coordinate matrix retained full coordinate rank $\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde X)=9$.  The residual degrees of freedom after the control fit were $14387$, $3716$, $12395$, $19030$, $4106$, $4983$, and $5792$ for \emph{D. rerio}, \emph{E. coli}, \emph{G. gallus}, \emph{H. sapiens}, \emph{M. smegmatis}, \emph{P. aeruginosa}, and \emph{S. cerevisiae}, respectively.  The degeneracy threshold was $R^2=0.999999$, and each organism was marked rank-sufficient for the finite fit.  The positive-entry threshold for partial $R^2$ was $10^{-12}$.

\paragraph{Finite readback.}
The direct protein-omics survival score $S^{QP}$ was positive in every available organism-level table.  The fitted $R^2_{QP}$ values were
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{QP}(\emph{D.\ rerio}) &= 0.04613529807085213,\\
R^2_{QP}(\emph{E.\ coli}\ K\text{-}12) &= 0.10767526677985896,\\
R^2_{QP}(\emph{G.\ gallus}) &= 0.03980793596647631,\\
R^2_{QP}(\emph{H.\ sapiens}) &= 0.04454565118019448,\\
R^2_{QP}(\emph{M.\ smegmatis}) &= 0.03209009170649934,\\
R^2_{QP}(\emph{P.\ aeruginosa}) &= 0.0646471273126996,\\
R^2_{QP}(\emph{S.\ cerevisiae}) &= 0.29837624994140827.
\end{aligned}
$$
In each case all $9$ residual coordinates had positive survival under the declared threshold.  The largest organism-level value in this finite panel was the yeast value $0.29837624994140827$, while the smallest was the \emph{M. smegmatis} value $0.03209009170649934$.  The result is therefore a finite, controlled association between amino-acid-quotient synonymous residual coordinates and measured protein abundance.

\paragraph{Energy and rank checks.}
The controlled fits were not accepted merely because rows existed.  The response residual energies after the control projection were $24915.244071805108$ for \emph{D. rerio}, $4020.333671802061$ for \emph{E. coli}, $18586.77768412634$ for \emph{G. gallus}, $30769.5367150478$ for \emph{H. sapiens}, $5729.194538214237$ for \emph{M. smegmatis}, $5221.627438262674$ for \emph{P. aeruginosa}, and $2958.1865890925337$ for \emph{S. cerevisiae}.  The corresponding explained energies of the $B^*_{Q6}$ residual block were $1149.47221176076$, $432.89050065533684$, $739.9012558728322$, $1370.6490494847067$, $183.85037813566998$, $337.5632137808526$, and $882.6526210803958$.  These finite values support the reading that the positive $S^{QP}$ entries are measured residual associations rather than a rank-saturated all-positive artifact.

\paragraph{Translation boundary.}
This chapter records a direct $q_i \to P_k$ protein-omics association.  It does not by itself record a measured translation path $q_i \to T_j \to P_k$.  The available companion translation record used only three organisms for modelled tAI readout, with nonzero bounded coupling above threshold $0.01$ in $81$ entries and maximum absolute coupling $3801961.031607896$; that three-organism contact was explicitly underpowered for a rank-powered survival claim.  Consequently, the protein-omics result here is not a translation-rate result, not ribosome occupancy, and not mediation.  Conditioning on an actually measured translation readout $T_j$ could remove, reduce, or leave the direct $q_i \to P_k$ association, but that mediation surface is not present in this finite table.

\paragraph{Local no-promotion rule.}
The only supported statement is that, in the seven joined PAXdb-CDS tables listed above, $B^*_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates retain positive direct protein-abundance association after the declared sequence-composition controls.  No claim about translation mechanism, folding, physical admissibility, localization, post-translational modification, protein stability, biochemical function, phenotype, causality, or biological universality follows from this score.  Any such stronger interpretation requires its own layer-matched measurement and its own controlled statistic.  In particular, a future translation-mediated claim must join the same CDS or organism rows to measured translation readouts and matched mRNA controls, then show whether the $q_i \to T_j$ step survives and whether conditioning on $T_j$ attenuates the $q_i \to P_k$ association.
