\subsection{Direct protein-omics survival of $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinates}
\label{sec:bgioreality-q6-direct-protein-omics-survival}

\paragraph{Finite carrier.}
The finite object in this record is a joined CDS-by-protein table in which each row carries a real coding sequence, nine $B^*_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates, organism-local baseline controls, and a measured protein-abundance readout.  The residual coordinates are
$K\_AAA$, $Arg\_AGR$, $Ile\_AUA$, $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$, $Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG$, $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$, $Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG$, $Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY$, and $f3\_stress$.  These coordinates are interpreted only after the amino-acid quotient: they are not amino-acid composition effects, but synonymous residual directions retained after the residue identity itself has been accounted for.  The measured protein readout is $\log_{10}(\mathrm{abundance\_ppm})$ from PAXdb protein abundance joined to CDS codon counts.  The row counts are large enough for a non-saturated residual fit in each organism: $3739$ joined proteins for \emph{Escherichia coli} K-12 MG1655, $5815$ for \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}, $19053$ for \emph{Homo sapiens}, $14410$ for \emph{Danio rerio}, $12418$ for \emph{Gallus gallus}, and $4129$ for \emph{Mycobacterium smegmatis} str. MC2 155.  The corresponding join hit rates are $0.9978649586335735$, $0.9156038419146592$, $0.9779294769799313$, $1.0$, $1.0$, and $1.0$.

\paragraph{Residualization protocol.}
For each organism, the protein-abundance column was residualized against a control block containing length, amino-acid composition, GC3, M-density, and organism-local baseline covariates as encoded in a $24$-column design.  The effective control rank was $23$ in all six organisms, while the residualized $B^*_{Q6}$ design retained full coordinate rank $9$.  Thus the protein-omics surface is not an all-positive artifact caused by loss of coordinate rank.  The residual degrees of freedom after the control block were $3716$ in \emph{E. coli}, $5792$ in yeast, $19030$ in human, $14387$ in zebrafish, $12395$ in chicken, and $4106$ in \emph{M. smegmatis}.  The degenerate-$R^2$ cutoff used by the audit was $0.999999$, and every organism was marked rank-sufficient.

Let $Q$ denote the nine-column residual-coordinate matrix after projection away from the baseline controls, and let $P$ denote the residualized protein-abundance readout.  The direct protein-omics survival statistic reported here is the controlled partial protein-abundance score $S^{QP}$, summarized by the organism-level $R^2_{QP}$ values

The positive-entry threshold was $10^{-12}$, and all nine $B^*_{Q6}$ coordinates survived in each organism-level fit.  The residual protein-abundance energies after controls were $4020.333671802061$ for \emph{E. coli}, $2958.1865890925337$ for yeast, $30769.5367150478$ for human, $24915.244071805108$ for zebrafish, $18586.77768412634$ for chicken, and $5729.194538214237$ for \emph{M. smegmatis}. This is finite evidence for a direct codon-residual to protein-abundance association after the stated controls.

\paragraph{Translation boundary.}
The positive $S^{QP}$ surface is deliberately not promoted to a translation mechanism.  The measured PAXdb column is a steady-state protein-omics readout, not a ribosome-occupancy, elongation-rate, or translation-efficiency measurement.  A separate earlier modeled-tAI contact computed nine synonymous residual coordinates over three organisms and found nonzero bounded coupling above threshold, with $81$ entries above $0.01$ and maximum absolute modeled score $3801961.031607896$, but that contact was explicitly underpowered for survival: $n=3$ organisms cannot support a powered claim, and the recorded condition requires at least $40$ matched species before a powered modeled-tAI survival statement is allowed.  Therefore the present protein-omics record is read as direct predictive irreducibility from $Q$ to $P$, not as a mediated statement $Q \to T \to P$.

\paragraph{Local cannot-claim boundary.}
This chapter establishes only a controlled association between $B^*_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates and measured protein abundance in the finite joined rows described above.  It does not show that the signal is caused by translation, that ribosome dynamics carry the signal, that a measured translation mediator absorbs it, or that the protein-abundance effect is functional.  No conclusion about folding, structural order, physical admissibility, localization, post-translational state, molecular function, phenotype, mechanism, or universality follows from $S^{QP} > 0$.  To make any such stronger statement, one must attach a layer-matched contact: measured translation readouts for a $Q \to T$ claim, a joint mediation fit for $Q \to T \to P$, structural or physical assays for admissibility and folding, and functional or perturbational assays for biological role.  Without those added rows and their own controlled statistics, the correct interpretation remains direct protein-omics survival with an explicit translation boundary.
