\subsection{Direct protein-omics survival for the $B^*_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates}\label{subsec:bioq6-direct-protein-omics-survival}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite carrier.}
This record tests a finite CDS-by-organism table in which each coding sequence carries nine $B^*_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates after quotienting by amino-acid identity.  The coordinate list is
$K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $Leu_{CUN\,vs\,UUR}$, $Leu_{UUA\,vs\,UUG}$, $Ser_{UCR\,vs\,AGY}$, $Ser_{UCA\,vs\,UCG}$, $Thr_{ACR\,vs\,ACY}$, and $f3_{stress}$.  These coordinates are not protein measurements.  They are codon-topology residual columns attached to real CDS rows and then tested against a matched protein-omics readout.  The protein readout is $\log_{10}(\mathrm{abundance\_ppm})$ from measured PAXdb protein abundance joined to CDS codon counts.  The finite rows are organism scoped: Escherichia coli K-12 MG1655 contributes $3739$ joined proteins with join hit rate $0.9978649586335735$, Mycobacterium smegmatis str. MC2 155 contributes $4129$ joined proteins with hit rate $1.0$, and Saccharomyces cerevisiae contributes $5815$ joined proteins with hit rate $0.9156038419146592$.

\paragraph{Controlled statistic.}
For each organism, the protein-abundance vector was residualized against the declared baseline controls before asking whether the nine $B^*_{Q6}$ coordinates still explain any residual protein-omics variation.  The control set includes length, amino-acid composition, GC3, and M-density terms; in the recorded finite run this produced $24$ control columns and rank $23$ for the control design in each organism.  The residualized $Q$ design retained full coordinate rank: $\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde X)=9$ for all three organisms.  The residual degrees of freedom after controls were $3716$ for E. coli, $4106$ for M. smegmatis, and $5792$ for S. cerevisiae.  The residual protein-abundance energies were $4020.333671802061$, $5729.194538214237$, and $2958.1865890925337$, respectively.  The corresponding explained energies attributed to the controlled $Q$ block were $432.89050065533684$, $183.85037813566998$, and $882.6526210803958$.

\paragraph{Survival readout.}
The direct protein-omics survival surface is summarized by the organism-level controlled $R^2_{QP}$ values
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{QP}(\mathrm{E.\ coli\ K12}) &= 0.10767526677985896,\\
R^2_{QP}(\mathrm{M.\ smegmatis}) &= 0.03209009170649934,\\
R^2_{QP}(\mathrm{S.\ cerevisiae}) &= 0.29837624994140827.
\end{aligned}
$$
In all three organisms the surviving coordinate count is $9$ at the recorded positive partial-$R^2$ threshold $10^{-12}$.  The rank-sufficiency flag is true for each organism under the stated degeneracy threshold $0.999999$.  Thus the finite-row result supports a narrow statement: after the specified controls, the nine synonymous residual coordinates retain direct predictive contact with measured steady-state protein abundance in these joined rows.  This is a $Q\to P$ association, not a completed $Q\to T\to P$ account.

\paragraph{Translation boundary.}
The same data bundle also records a separate modeled translation-readout contact based on tRNA gene-copy information and dos Reis wobble weights.  That contact has three organisms, $61$ sense codons, zero raw-weight fallback events in each organism, and nonzero bounded coupling above threshold $0.01$ with $81$ entries above threshold.  Its maximum absolute coupling is $3801961.031607896$, equal to the maximum absolute diagonal entry.  This modeled tAI contact is useful as a bounded translation-layer readout, but the three-organism panel is explicitly underpowered for rank-powered survival; it is not ribosome profiling, not measured translation efficiency, and not proteomics validation.  The protein-omics run reported here also records that it does not use perturbation, rescue, ribosome profiling, or matched transcript controls.  Therefore the positive $R^2_{QP}$ values may be read only as direct codon-residual to protein-abundance association after the available controls.  They do not establish that a measured translation mediator $T_j$ carries the signal.

\paragraph{Local no-promotion rule.}
No translation mechanism, folded-structure conclusion, physical-admissibility statement, biochemical-function claim, localization claim, post-translational-state claim, phenotype claim, causal mechanism, or universal biological law follows from this chapter.  Such claims require separate layer-matched measurements and a statistic appropriate to that layer.  In particular, a mediation claim would require same-CDS rows containing $Q_i$, a measured translation readout $T_j$, matched mRNA controls, and protein abundance $P_k$, followed by a fit showing whether conditioning on $T_j$ removes, reduces, or leaves the direct $Q_i\to P_k$ signal.  Until that matrix exists, the result remains a controlled direct protein-omics survival record with an explicit translation boundary.
