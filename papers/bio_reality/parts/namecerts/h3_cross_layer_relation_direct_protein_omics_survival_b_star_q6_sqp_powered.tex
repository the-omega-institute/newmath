\subsection{Direct protein-omics survival for $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinates}
\label{sec:direct-protein-omics-survival-bstar-q6}

\paragraph{Finite carrier.}
The record is a finite CDS-by-organism table whose rows join real coding-sequence codon counts to measured PAXdb protein abundance.  The codon side is projected to nine amino-acid-quotient residual coordinates,
$K\_AAA$, $Arg\_AGR$, $Ile\_AUA$, $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$, $Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG$, $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$, $Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG$, $Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY$, and $f3\_stress$.  The protein side is $\log_{10}(\mathrm{abundance\_ppm})$ from measured PAXdb abundance, joined to the same CDS records.  The fitted control block contains an intercept, $\log(\mathrm{CDS\ length})$, the twenty amino-acid composition proportions, GC3, and an $M$-density baseline defined from the union support of the nine projected coordinates.  In every organism listed below the residualized $q$-block has rank $9$, while the control block has rank $23$ out of $24$ named control columns.

\paragraph{Residual survival statistic.}
For an organism $o$, let $X_o$ be the $n_o \times 9$ matrix of $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinates, let $Z_o$ be the control matrix, and let $y_o$ be the measured protein-abundance readout.  The reported statistic is the partial protein-omics survival of $X_o$ after removing $Z_o$ from both sides:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\widetilde X_o &= (I-\Pi_{Z_o})X_o,\\
\widetilde y_o &= (I-\Pi_{Z_o})y_o,\\
R^2_{QP}(o) &= \frac{\|\Pi_{\widetilde X_o}\widetilde y_o\|^2}{\|\widetilde y_o\|^2}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The positivity threshold for a surviving entry is $10^{-12}$.  The calculation is therefore a direct $q_i \rightarrow P_k$ residual association after composition and sequence controls; it is not a translation-rate measurement and is not a mediation fit.

\paragraph{Boundary against promotion.}
The interpretation stops at direct DNA-to-protein-omics predictivity after the stated controls.  A positive $S^{QP}_{ik}$ entry does not by itself identify translation as the mechanism, because the protein-abundance readout is cross-sectional abundance rather than a ribosome or translation-efficiency readout.  No conclusion about folding, localization, post-translational modification, stability, biochemical function, phenotype, physical admissibility, causal intervention, or universal biological law follows from these rows.  A translation-mediated claim would require matched rows containing a measured translation readout $T_j$, matched mRNA abundance, and the same CDS coordinates, followed by an explicit mediation comparison showing whether conditioning on $T_j$ removes, reduces, or leaves the direct $q_i \rightarrow P_k$ signal.

\paragraph{Relation to mRNA stability contacts.}
Separate mRNA-stability calculations show that the same nine-coordinate basis can also be tested against transcript-layer readouts, but those contacts remain distinct from the direct protein-omics statistic.  In yeast, the matched half-life table gives $R^2_{QM}=0.051794$, same-join $R^2_{QP}=0.276114$, cosine $0.804094$, and permutation $p=0.000999$ with dominant coordinate $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$.  In \textit{E. coli}, the half-life contact gives $R^2_{QM}=0.022986$, same-join $R^2_{QP}=0.116323$, cosine $-0.778492$, and permutation $p=0.000999$, with $Arg\_AGR$ dominant.  In human consensus half-life data, the contact gives $R^2_{QM}=0.010504$, same-join $R^2_{QP}=0.040952$, cosine $0.635118$, and permutation $p=0.000999$, again with a transcript-layer readout rather than a translation readout.  These comparisons support the decision to keep transcript abundance, translation efficiency, and protein abundance as separately measured layers rather than treating any one of them as a substitute for the others.

\paragraph{Readback.}
The finite result is therefore positive for direct protein-omics survival: under the named controls, each organism in the joined panel has $R^2_{QP}>0$ and all nine $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinates survive the organism-specific partial fit.  The result is ready as a bounded cross-layer association record.  It remains explicitly unresolved as a translation-mediated mechanism until a matched $Q,T,P$ matrix with mRNA controls is attached and fitted.
