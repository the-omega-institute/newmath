\subsection{Direct protein-omics survival of $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates}
\label{sec:biorreality-q6-direct-protein-omics-survival}

\paragraph{Finite carrier.}
This record uses a finite coding-sequence by organism table in which each row is a joined protein-coding entry with real CDS codon counts and measured PAXdb protein abundance. The coordinate side is the nine-dimensional $B^{*}_{Q6}$ synonymous residual block
$K\_AAA$, $Arg\_AGR$, $Ile\_AUA$, $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$, $Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG$, $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$, $Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG$, $Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY$, and $f3\_stress$. These coordinates are evaluated after the amino-acid quotient, so the test is not a restatement of amino-acid composition. The measured response is $\log_{10}(\mathrm{abundance\_ppm})$ from PAXdb abundance joined to CDS rows. The baseline control matrix contains an intercept, CDS length, the twenty amino-acid composition proportions, GC3, and an M-density covariate supported on the union of the nine projected coordinate supports. In every organism listed below the control matrix has $24$ columns, rank $23$, and the residualized coordinate block has rank $9$.

\paragraph{Statistic.}
For each organism $o$, let $Y_o$ be residualized log protein abundance, $Z_o$ the baseline controls, and $Q_o$ the residualized $B^{*}_{Q6}$ coordinate matrix. The reported direct protein-omics survival score is the partial coefficient of determination of $Q_o$ against $Y_o$ after projection away from $Z_o$:
$$
\begin{aligned}
S^{QP}(o) &= R^2\bigl( Y_o \sim Q_o \mid Z_o \bigr),\\
Z_o &= \{1,\log(\mathrm{CDS\ length}),\mathrm{AA\ composition},GC3,M\hbox{-}\mathrm{density}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The positivity threshold used by the run is $10^{-12}$, and all fitted surfaces are far below the degeneracy cutoff $R^2=0.999999$. Thus the positive readout is not an all-positive rank artifact of a saturated three-row design. The row-level residual degrees of freedom range from $1633$ in \emph{Sulfolobus solfataricus} to $19372$ in mouse, while the smallest joined protein table has $1656$ rows and the largest has $19395$ rows.

\paragraph{Interpretation of the positive surface.}
The finite result supports a direct codon-residual to protein-abundance association after the CDS-derived controls. Its strongest organism-level value is in budding yeast, where $R^2_{QP}=0.29837624994140827$ on $5815$ joined protein rows, and its weakest positive value is in \emph{Halobacterium salinarum}, where $R^2_{QP}=0.019256877907572044$ on $1747$ joined rows. The result is still a direct association surface, not a translation mechanism. It shows that the nine residual coordinates retain measurable predictive content for protein abundance in these joined finite tables, but the fitted response is steady-state protein abundance and not a ribosome-occupancy, elongation-rate, or translation-efficiency measurement.

\paragraph{Translation boundary.}
The same coordinate family has a modeled translation-readout contact based on tAI weights from tRNA gene copy and dos Reis wobble weights in three organisms. That modeled run used $61$ sense codons, recovered all $20$ standard amino-acid families in the local tRNA payloads, and found $81$ entries above the bounded coupling threshold $0.01$, with maximum absolute coupling $3801961.031607896$. However the modeled translation contact has only $3$ organisms, so it is explicitly underpowered for rank-based survival and is not a measured ribosome-profiling validation. Therefore the present $S^{QP}$ table must not be rewritten as $Q\to T\to P$ mediation. A mediation statement would require same-row measured translation readouts $T_j$, matched mRNA controls, and an explicit fit showing that the $Q_i\to T_j$ step survives and that conditioning on $T_j$ removes or reduces the direct $Q_i\to P_k$ association.

\paragraph{mRNA comparison rows.}
The mRNA half-life contacts give useful contrast without changing the direct protein-omics claim. In budding yeast, the same coordinate family reaches $R^2_{QM}=0.05179429044975085$ on $5085$ joined half-life rows, with permutation $p=0.000999000999000999$ and cosine similarity $0.8040935468384264$ to the same-join protein-omics vector. In \emph{E. coli}, the half-life surface has $R^2_{QM}=0.02298556640056076$ on $2720$ joined rows, permutation $p=0.000999000999000999$, and cosine $-0.7784923375569599$ to the protein-omics vector. In human, the consensus relative half-life surface has $R^2_{QM}=0.010503670362051522$ on $16165$ joined rows, permutation $p=0.000999000999000999$, and cosine $0.6351183883924848$. These rows show that the coordinate family also contacts transcript-stability readouts, but they do not supply the missing measured translation mediator for the protein-abundance association.

\paragraph{Local refusal boundary.}
The admissible claim is narrow: $B^{*}_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates have positive direct survival against measured protein abundance in finite organism-matched CDS/proteomics tables after length, amino-acid composition, GC3, and M-density controls. No translation mechanism, folding claim, physical admissibility claim, biochemical function, localization, post-translational state, phenotype, causality, adaptation law, or universality follows from this table alone. Those interpretations require separate layer-matched contacts and their own controlled statistics. In the absence of matched measured translation readouts and mRNA controls in the same rows, this chapter reports direct protein-omics irreducibility only.
