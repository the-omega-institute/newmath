\subsection{Direct protein-omics survival of $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates}\label{sec:bioreality-q6-direct-protein-omics-survival}

\paragraph{Finite carrier.}
The carrier for this record is a finite CDS-by-organism panel in which each coding sequence row carries nine synonymous residual coordinates after amino-acid quotienting, a measured protein-omics readout, and the covariates needed to remove the obvious compositional explanations.  The nine coordinates are
$$
\begin{aligned}
&K\_AAA,\ Arg\_AGR,\ Ile\_AUA,\ Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR,\ Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG,\\
&Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY,\ Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG,\ Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY,\ f3\_stress.
\end{aligned}
$$
For each organism the protein readout is $\log_{10}(\mathrm{abundance\_ppm})$ from measured PAXdb abundance joined to real CDS codon counts.  The control block has $24$ columns, with rank $23$ after the intercept and compositional dependencies are taken into account; after projection away from this block the residual coordinate matrix has rank $9$ in every organism.  Thus the tested object is not raw codon usage, but the residual map $Q \to P$ after length, amino-acid composition, GC3, and the $M$-density baseline have been removed.

\paragraph{Statistic.}
Let $Z$ denote the organism-specific control matrix, $Q$ the nine-column residual-coordinate matrix, and $P$ the measured protein-abundance vector.  The reported direct survival score is the partial $R^2$ of $Q$ against $P$ after residualization against $Z$:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\widetilde Q &= (I-\Pi_Z)Q,\\
\widetilde P &= (I-\Pi_Z)P,\\
R^2_{QP} &= 1 - \frac{\|\widetilde P-\Pi_{\widetilde Q}\widetilde P\|^2}{\|\widetilde P\|^2}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The positivity threshold for a coordinate-level survival entry is $10^{-12}$, and all nine residual coordinates survive in every organism under this threshold.  At the block level, the finite values are $R^2_{QP}=0.0619062459$ for \emph{B. subtilis}, $0.1021790676$ for \emph{C. elegans}, $0.0461352981$ for zebrafish, $0.0814178672$ for \emph{D. discoideum}, $0.0770952887$ for fly, $0.1076752668$ for \emph{E. coli}, $0.0398079360$ for chicken, $0.0192568779$ for \emph{H. salinarum}, $0.0445456512$ for human, $0.0498279841$ for mouse, $0.0320900917$ for \emph{M. smegmatis}, $0.0646471273$ for \emph{P. aeruginosa}, $0.0276399860$ for rat, $0.2983762499$ for yeast, and $0.0639751425$ for \emph{S. solfataricus}.  The strongest block score is the yeast panel, where the nine residual coordinates explain about $29.84\%$ of the residual protein-abundance energy after the control projection.  The weakest is \emph{H. salinarum}, still positive at about $1.93\%$.

\paragraph{Residual-energy check.}
The control projection is not empty notation: the residual protein-abundance energy remains finite and substantial after $Z$ is removed.  Representative residual energies are $4020.333671802061$ for \emph{E. coli}, $30769.5367150478$ for human, $48806.47856410273$ for mouse, $2958.1865890925337$ for yeast, and $980.7615792975887$ for \emph{S. solfataricus}.  The corresponding explained energies by the $Q$ block are $432.89050065533684$, $1370.6490494847067$, $2431.9284360407714$, $882.6526210803958$, and $62.74436180679531$, respectively.  The relation therefore has finite numerical contact with measured protein abundance in every listed organism, while remaining explicitly a residual association rather than an asserted biochemical route.

\paragraph{Translation boundary.}
This chapter records a direct $Q \to P$ protein-omics survival surface, not a $Q \to T \to P$ mediation surface.  The earlier modeled translation contact used three organisms and tAI weights computed from GtRNAdb tRNA gene-copy data with dos Reis wobble weights; it found $81$ bounded-coupling entries above threshold $0.01$ and a maximum absolute modeled value $3801961.031607896$, but that $n=3$ setting was explicitly underpowered for a rank-based survival claim.  The present PAXdb panel is much larger and rank-sufficient for the direct protein-abundance statistic, yet it still does not include a matched measured translation readout $T_j$ or a fitted mediation matrix.  Consequently a positive $R^2_{QP}$ is read only as residual predictive contact from synonymous-coordinate geometry to measured protein abundance.  It is not, by itself, evidence of ribosome-rate mediation.

\paragraph{Nearby RNA-layer checks.}
The same nine-coordinate family also has finite RNA half-life contacts, which help locate the protein-omics result without upgrading its meaning.  In yeast, the residual half-life statistic has $R^2_{QM}=0.05179429045$ on $5085$ joined rows, with $R^2_{QP}=0.2761140374$ on the same join and cosine $0.8040935468$ between the $S^{QM}$ and $S^{QP}$ vectors.  In \emph{E. coli}, the half-life statistic has $R^2_{QM}=0.02298556640$ on $2720$ joined rows, same-join $R^2_{QP}=0.1163231185$, and cosine $-0.7784923376$.  In human, the consensus half-life statistic has $R^2_{QM}=0.01050367036$ on $16165$ joined rows, same-join $R^2_{QP}=0.04095215558$, and cosine $0.6351183884$.  These RNA-layer values show that the $Q$ coordinates can survive different measured readouts, but they do not turn protein abundance into a translation mechanism or RNA decay mechanism.

\paragraph{Local verdict.}
The finite result is ready at the direct protein-omics layer: across $15$ organism panels, the residualized nine-coordinate $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ block has positive $R^2_{QP}$ against measured protein abundance after the declared CDS-derived controls, and each panel retains rank $9$ for the residual coordinate block.  The local scientific reading is deliberately narrow.  No translation claim, folded-structure claim, physical-admissibility claim, molecular-function claim, causal mechanism, phenotype claim, or universal biological law follows from these numbers unless a separate layer-matched measurement supplies that object and a new controlled statistic is computed for it.
