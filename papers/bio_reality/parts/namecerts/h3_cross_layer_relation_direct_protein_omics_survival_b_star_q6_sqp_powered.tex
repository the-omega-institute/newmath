\paragraph{Finite row object.}
This record tests whether synonymous residual coordinates from the $B^\ast_{Q_6}$ codon-topology packet retain direct predictive contact with measured protein abundance after amino-acid-level and sequence-composition controls have already been removed.  The carrier is a finite CDS-by-organism table whose rows are protein-coding sequences matched to measured PAXdb abundance and real CDS codon counts.  The residual coordinate block has nine columns:
$$
\begin{aligned}
Q=\{&K_{AAA},\operatorname{Arg}_{AGR},\operatorname{Ile}_{AUA},\operatorname{Leu}_{CUN\;vs\;UUR},\operatorname{Leu}_{UUA\;vs\;UUG},\\
&\operatorname{Ser}_{UCR\;vs\;AGY},\operatorname{Ser}_{UCA\;vs\;UCG},\operatorname{Thr}_{ACR\;vs\;ACY},f3_{stress}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The response variable is $\log_{10}(\operatorname{abundance\_ppm})$ from measured PAXdb protein abundance.  The control block contains length, amino-acid composition, $GC3$, $M$-density, and organism-specific baseline columns as implemented in the finite run.  The statistic reported here is direct protein-omics survival $S^{QP}_{ik}$, summarized per organism by the controlled partial $R^2_{QP}$ and by the number of $Q$ coordinates with strictly positive post-control contribution above the threshold $10^{-12}$.

\paragraph{Translation boundary.}
This chapter does not identify a translation mechanism.  A direct $S^{QP}_{ik}$ entry is a protein-omics association after controls, not a measured $q_i\to T_j$ translation step and not a mediation surface $q_i\to T_j\to P_k$.  The finite evidence explicitly says that the PAXdb abundance readout is cross-sectional protein abundance, not a ribosome-occupancy or translation-rate measurement, and that the run does not use perturbation, rescue, ribosome profiling, or matched transcript controls.  Therefore positive $R^2_{QP}$ values may be read only as DNA-to-protein-omics predictive irreducibility under the stated covariates.  They may not be promoted to translation, folding, localization, post-translational modification, pathway function, stability, physical admissibility, organismal phenotype, causal mechanism, or universal biological law.

That modeled contact is useful as a translation-readout boundary, but it does not absorb or explain the direct $Q\to P$ protein-omics association reported here.

\paragraph{Local verdict.}
The finite table is ready as a direct protein-omics survival record for $B^\ast_{Q_6}$ residual coordinates.  Its positive content is narrow: seven organism-specific controlled fits, all with $\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde X)=9$, all with positive $R^2_{QP}$, and all with nine surviving coordinates.  Its negative content is equally important: without matched measured translation readouts $T_j$, matched mRNA controls inside the same mediation design, and an explicit mediation fit, the result remains a direct protein-abundance association and not a translation-mediated biological mechanism.
