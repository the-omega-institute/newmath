\subsection{Direct Protein-Omics Survival of $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ Residual Coordinates}
\label{subsec:b-star-q6-direct-protein-omics-survival}

\paragraph{Finite carrier.}
The finite object considered here is a CDS-by-organism table in which each coding sequence carries a synonymous residual coordinate vector $q$ from the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ amino-acid quotient, a measured protein-abundance readout $P$, and a control block $Z$.  The residual coordinates are not amino-acid composition itself: they are codon-topology directions remaining after the amino-acid quotient has been taken.  The computed coordinate list has cardinality $9$:
$$
\begin{aligned}
Q=\{&K\_AAA,\ Arg\_AGR,\ Ile\_AUA,\ Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR,\ Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG,\\
&Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY,\ Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG,\ Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY,\ f3\_stress\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The protein readout is $\log_{10}(\mathrm{abundance\_ppm})$ from measured PAXdb protein abundance joined to real CDS codon counts.  The coordinate vectors were imported from the same $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual computation used for the translation-survival route, but the present statistic is a direct $q_i$ to protein-omics readout, not a translation-rate measurement.

\paragraph{Observed rows and joins.}
Three organism-level finite tables were loaded.  For \emph{Escherichia coli} K-12 MG1655, $3739$ proteins were used with $3739$ joined CDS rows and join hit rate $0.9978649586335735$.  For \emph{Mycobacterium smegmatis} str. MC2 155, $4129$ proteins were used with $4129$ joined rows and hit rate $1.0$.  For \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}, $5815$ proteins were used with $5815$ joined rows and hit rate $0.9156038419146592$.  These are finite row contacts against measured abundance, not simulated protein-output labels.  The survival test therefore has enough per-CDS rows to avoid the three-organism rank-saturation failure that blocks cross-organism all-positive surfaces.

\paragraph{Residualization and rank.}
Within each organism the control block $Z$ contains $24$ columns, including length, amino-acid-composition, GC3, and M-density controls.  The fitted control rank is $23$ in all three organism tables.  After removing $Z$, the residual degrees of freedom are $3716$ for \emph{E. coli}, $4106$ for \emph{M. smegmatis}, and $5792$ for yeast.  The residualized $Q$ design has rank $9$ in every organism, matching the number of residual coordinates.  The degeneracy threshold used for the control fit is $R^2=0.999999$, and the reported powered-rank flag is true for all three rows of the organism panel.

\paragraph{Finite statistic.}
Let $X_Q$ be the $9$-column residual-coordinate matrix, let $y_P$ be the measured protein-abundance vector, and let $\Pi_Z^{\perp}$ denote residualization against the finite control block.  The direct protein-omics survival surface is the partial fit of $\Pi_Z^{\perp} y_P$ by $\Pi_Z^{\perp} X_Q$.  In the run recorded here, the positive-entry threshold was $10^{-12}$ for partial $R^2$.  The aggregate direct survival values were:
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{QP}(\mathrm{E.\ coli\ K12})&=0.10767526677985896,\\
R^2_{QP}(\mathrm{M.\ smegmatis})&=0.03209009170649934,\\
R^2_{QP}(\mathrm{S.\ cerevisiae})&=0.29837624994140827.
\end{aligned}
$$
Each organism table reports $9$ surviving coordinates.  The corresponding residual-response energies after $Z$ were $4020.333671802061$, $5729.194538214237$, and $2958.1865890925337$, respectively; the energy explained by the control block was $432.89050065533684$, $183.85037813566998$, and $882.6526210803958$.  Thus the finite result is a reproducible direct association between synonymous residual codon topology and measured protein abundance after the stated baseline controls.

\paragraph{Translation boundary.}
This chapter does not promote $S^{QP}$ to a translation mechanism.  The linked modeled-translation contact used tRNA gene-copy and dos Reis wobble weights to compute modeled tAI weights for three organisms, with all standard amino-acid tRNA families present in the local payloads.  The modeled-tAI coupling surface had $81$ entries above threshold $0.01$ and maximum absolute entry $3801961.031607896$, but that run had only $3$ organisms and was explicitly underpowered for powered survival requiring at least $40$ matched species.  It was a bounded modeled-readout check, not ribosome profiling, not translation efficiency, and not a protein-abundance mediation matrix.

\paragraph{Interpretation.}
The supported statement is narrow: in the finite PAXdb-CDS joins, the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates retain direct predictive information for measured protein abundance after the declared sequence and composition controls.  This is DNA-to-protein-omics predictive irreducibility at the level of cross-sectional abundance association.  It is not evidence that any $q_i$ changes translation rate, folding, localization, post-translational modification, stability, biochemical activity, pathway role, organism fitness, or a universal biological rule.

\paragraph{Local refusal of stronger claims.}
No translation, folding, physical admissibility, function, mechanism, phenotype, or universality follows from the present statistic.  A translation interpretation would require a measured translation readout $T_j$, matched mRNA controls, and a same-CDS mediation fit showing $q_i \to T_j \to P_k$ with attenuation of the direct $q_i \to P_k$ signal after conditioning on $T_j$.  Without that joined translation-and-protein table, the finite result remains a direct protein-omics survival record only.  A negative future mediation result would not erase the direct $S^{QP}$ association; it would classify the association as not absorbed by the measured translation boundary.  A positive mediation result would still stop at protein abundance unless another layer supplies its own measurement contact.
