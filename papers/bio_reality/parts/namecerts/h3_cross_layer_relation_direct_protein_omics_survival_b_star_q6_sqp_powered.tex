\subsection{Direct B-star Q6 protein-omics survival under a translation boundary}
\label{subsec:b-star-q6-direct-protein-omics-survival}

\paragraph{Finite row carrier.}
The record is a finite CDS-by-organism table in which each coding sequence carries nine $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates and one matched protein-omics readout.  The coordinates are
$K\_AAA$, $Arg\_AGR$, $Ile\_AUA$, $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$, $Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG$, $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$, $Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG$, $Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY$, and $f3\_stress$.  They are used only after the amino-acid quotient: the row-level signal is a synonymous residual coordinate, not a raw amino-acid composition effect.  The protein readout is $\log_{10}(\mathrm{abundance\_ppm})$ from measured PAXdb protein abundance joined to real CDS codon counts.  Three organism panels enter the finite computation: $3739$ joined proteins for \emph{Escherichia coli} K-12 MG1655 with join hit rate $0.9978649586335735$, $4129$ joined proteins for \emph{Mycobacterium smegmatis} str. MC2 155 with join hit rate $1.0$, and $5815$ joined proteins for \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae} with join hit rate $0.9156038419146592$.

\paragraph{Controlled statistic.}
For each organism, let $X_Q$ be the matrix of the nine $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates, let $y_P$ be the protein-abundance vector, and let $Z$ contain the baseline controls used in the finite row computation: amino-acid composition, CDS length, GC3, methionine density, and the associated organism-scoped nuisance columns present in the design.  The reported survival score is the direct protein-omics partial fit after residualizing both $X_Q$ and $y_P$ against $Z$.  In the three panels the control design has $24$ columns and residualized control rank $23$.  The residual coordinate rank is full in every panel:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde X_Q) &= 9,\\
\operatorname{rank}(Z) &= 23.
\end{aligned}
$$
The residual degrees of freedom after the control projection are $3716$ for \emph{E. coli}, $4106$ for \emph{M. smegmatis}, and $5792$ for yeast.  The corresponding residual protein-readout energies are $4020.333671802061$, $5729.194538214237$, and $2958.1865890925337$.  The finite row also records the baseline explained energies $432.89050065533684$, $183.85037813566998$, and $882.6526210803958$, with degeneracy threshold $0.999999$ and positive partial-$R^2$ threshold $10^{-12}$.

\paragraph{Observed direct survival.}
The direct protein-omics survival surface is positive in all three organism panels under the stated residualization.  The finite values are
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{QP}(\mathrm{E.\ coli\ K\mbox{-}12\ MG1655}) &= 0.10767526677985896,\\
R^2_{QP}(\mathrm{M.\ smegmatis\ str.\ MC2\ 155}) &= 0.03209009170649934,\\
R^2_{QP}(\mathrm{S.\ cerevisiae}) &= 0.29837624994140827.
\end{aligned}
$$
Each panel reports $9$ surviving coordinates, so the row is not an $n=3$ organism-level rank-saturated all-positive surface.  The effective rows are per-CDS or per-protein rows inside each organism panel, and the fitted residual coordinate block has full rank after the baseline projection.  This supports the narrow claim that the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ synonymous residual basis retains direct predictive information for measured protein abundance in these finite panels after the stated sequence-composition controls.

\paragraph{Translation boundary.}
The same record does not promote the direct $q_i \to P_k$ association to a translation mechanism.  A separate modeled translation contact exists for a smaller three-organism tAI calculation: it used modeled codon adaptation weights from GtRNAdb tRNA gene copy counts and dos Reis wobble weights, with $61$ sense codons in each organism and zero raw-weight fallback count $0$ in \emph{E. coli}, human, and yeast.  That modeled-tAI contact found $81$ bounded coupling entries above threshold $0.01$ and maximum absolute coupling $3801961.031607896$, but it is explicitly a modeled codon-adaptation readout rather than measured ribosome occupancy or translation efficiency.  It also records that $3$ organisms are insufficient for powered survival when a powered translation-survival panel is requested.  Therefore the present direct protein-omics result remains a $q_i \to P_k$ association unless a matched measured translation readout $T_j$ is joined to the same CDS rows and a mediation fit shows a surviving $q_i \to T_j$ step followed by attenuation or absorption of the $q_i \to P_k$ signal after conditioning on $T_j$.

\paragraph{Local non-promotion rule.}
The permitted readback is deliberately narrow: positive $S^{QP}_{ik}$ means direct codon-residual to protein-abundance survival after the finite control projection.  It does not assert translation realization, ribosome-mediated causality, folded structure, protein localization, post-translational state, physical admissibility, molecular function, organismal phenotype, or a universal biological rule.  None of those claims follows from cross-sectional PAXdb abundance, from the codon residual coordinates, or from modeled tAI weights.  They require their own matched layer-specific measurements and their own controlled statistics.  In particular, a translation-mediated protein-omics claim would need same-row codon counts, measured translation readouts such as ribosome profiling or translation efficiency, matched mRNA controls, matched protein abundance, and an explicit $Q \to T \to P$ mediation matrix.  Until that matrix is present, the chapter records direct protein-omics irreducibility only.
