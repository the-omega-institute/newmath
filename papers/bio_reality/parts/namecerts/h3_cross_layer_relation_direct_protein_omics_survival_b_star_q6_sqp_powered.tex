\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite row object.}
The finite object considered here is a CDS-by-organism panel carrying nine amino-acid-quotient synonymous residual coordinates, organism-matched protein abundance rows, and a fixed CDS-derived control block.  The coordinate list is
$K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $Leu_{CUN\,vs\,UUR}$, $Leu_{UUA\,vs\,UUG}$, $Ser_{UCR\,vs\,AGY}$, $Ser_{UCA\,vs\,UCG}$, $Thr_{ACR\,vs\,ACY}$, and $f_3$-stress.  The protein readout is measured PAXdb $\log_{10}(\mathrm{abundance\ ppm})$ joined to real CDS codon counts.  The fitted direct surface is therefore a $Q\to P$ readout, denoted here by $S^{QP}$, not a translation-rate assay and not a mediation model.

\paragraph{Control geometry.}
For each organism, the protein abundance vector was residualized against a 24-column baseline whose effective rank was $23$.  The baseline includes an intercept, CDS length, the 20 amino-acid composition proportions, GC3, and the M-density baseline induced by the support of the nine projected $B^{*}_{Q6}$ coordinates.  After this residualization, the residualized $Q$ design retained full rank $9$ in every organism, and every listed organism was marked rank-sufficient rather than degenerate.  The positivity threshold for a coordinate contribution was $10^{-12}$ in partial-$R^2$ units, and the degeneracy guard was $R^2=0.999999$.

\paragraph{Rank and residual energy checks.}
The rank guard is also visible in the residual degrees of freedom.  The residual degrees of freedom after $Z$ are $4019$, $11638$, $14387$, $6951$, $13272$, $3716$, $12395$, $1724$, $19030$, $19372$, $4106$, $4983$, $13299$, $5792$, and $1633$ in the same organism order.  Thus the positive $S^{QP}$ calls are not produced by the earlier three-organism saturation problem: the powered protein-omics rows have thousands to tens of thousands of protein-level observations per organism, while the modeled translation-weight contact had only $3$ organisms and was explicitly classified as modeled-tAI bounded coupling rather than powered survival.  In that earlier translation-weight contact, $81$ modeled entries exceeded the $0.01$ bounded-coupling threshold, but the organism count was $3$, so the result did not license a measured translation-survival claim.

\paragraph{Boundary against translation promotion.}
The present positive surface is a direct codon-residual to protein-abundance association.  It does not say that $q_i$ changes ribosome speed, does not say that a translation mediator $T_j$ carries the signal, and does not establish a causal path $q_i\to T_j\to P_k$.  Such a statement would require same-CDS measured translation rows, such as ribosome occupancy or translation efficiency, joined to the same protein-abundance and mRNA-control rows, followed by an explicit mediation fit testing whether conditioning on $T_j$ removes or reduces the direct $q_i\to P_k$ association.  The available protein-omics run itself records that the measured PAXdb abundance is cross-sectional protein abundance rather than a translation-rate measurement, and that the experiment did not use perturbation, rescue, ribosome profiling, or matched transcript controls.

These RNA contacts sharpen the cross-layer picture, but they do not replace the absent same-row translation mediator required for a $Q\to T\to P$ claim.

\paragraph{Interpretation.}
The supported statement is therefore narrow and positive: in the finite PAXdb/CDS table, $B^{*}_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates retain direct predictive information about residualized steady-state protein abundance after amino-acid composition, length, GC3, and M-density controls.  The result is a protein-omics residual-survival observation at the $Q\to P$ surface.  No translation mechanism, folded structure, physical admissibility, biochemical function, localization, post-translational state, phenotype, organism-level fitness statement, causal mechanism, or universal biological law follows from this table alone.  Each of those stronger statements would require its own layer-matched measurement and a new finite statistic evaluated under the relevant controls.
