\subsection{Direct protein-omics survival of $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinates}\label{sec:bioreality-direct-protein-omics-survival-bstar-q6}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite row object.}
This record tests whether amino-acid-quotient synonymous coordinates in $B^*_{Q6}$ retain predictive contact with measured protein abundance after the usual sequence-composition baselines have been removed.  The finite carrier is a CDS-by-organism table whose rows join real coding-sequence codon counts to a measured PAXdb protein-abundance readout.  The coordinate block contains nine residual coordinates,
$K\_AAA$, $Arg\_AGR$, $Ile\_AUA$, $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$, $Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG$, $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$, $Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG$, $Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY$, and $f3\_stress$.  For each organism, the protein readout is $\log_{10}(\mathrm{abundance\_ppm})$, and the residualization block contains an intercept, CDS length, the twenty amino-acid composition proportions, GC3, and the $M$-density baseline.  The contact therefore asks for a direct residual association $q_i \to P_k$ and not for a translation-rate mechanism.

\paragraph{Residualized statistic.}
Let $Y$ be the measured protein-abundance column, $Q$ the nine-column $B^*_{Q6}$ residual-coordinate matrix, and $Z$ the control matrix.  For each organism the reported score is the partial coefficient of determination after projecting both $Y$ and $Q$ away from $Z$:
$$
\begin{aligned}
S^{QP} &= R^2\bigl((I-\Pi_Z)Y, (I-\Pi_Z)Q\bigr),\\
Z &= \{1,\log(\mathrm{CDS\ length}),\mathrm{AA\ composition},GC3,M\text{-density}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The control matrix has 24 columns and numerical rank 23 in every organism, while the residualized coordinate block keeps rank 9.  The positive-entry threshold is $10^{-12}$, and every listed organism has powered rank sufficiency for the nine-coordinate fit.

\paragraph{Direct protein-omics survival.}
All fifteen organism fits retain all nine residual coordinates above the positive threshold.  The strongest direct $S^{QP}$ value is in $Saccharomyces\ cerevisiae$, where $R^2_{QP}=0.298376$ and 9 coordinates survive.  The next largest values are $Escherichia\ coli$ K-12 MG1655 with $R^2_{QP}=0.107675$, $Caenorhabditis\ elegans$ with $R^2_{QP}=0.102179$, $Dictyostelium\ discoideum$ with $R^2_{QP}=0.081418$, and $Drosophila\ melanogaster$ with $R^2_{QP}=0.077095$.  Midrange positive contacts include $Pseudomonas\ aeruginosa$ PAO1 with $R^2_{QP}=0.064647$, $Sulfolobus\ solfataricus$ with $R^2_{QP}=0.063975$, $Bacillus\ subtilis$ 168 with $R^2_{QP}=0.061906$, $Mus\ musculus$ with $R^2_{QP}=0.049828$, $Danio\ rerio$ with $R^2_{QP}=0.046135$, and $Homo\ sapiens$ with $R^2_{QP}=0.044546$. \paragraph{Energy and rank checks.}
The residual energy scale varies substantially across organisms, but the rank condition is stable.  For example, $Mus\ musculus$ has residual protein-abundance energy $48806.478564$ and explained energy $2431.928436$ after the control projection, while $Homo\ sapiens$ has residual energy $30769.536715$ and explained energy $1370.649049$.  In yeast the corresponding values are $2958.186589$ and $882.652621$, matching the much larger $R^2_{QP}$ value in that organism.  In $Halobacterium\ salinarum$, the residual energy is $1047.656789$ and the explained energy is $20.174599$, consistent with the smaller but still positive direct contact.  No organism in this panel is a rank-degenerate three-row surface: every organism-level regression has thousands of residual degrees of freedom after $Z$, from 1633 up to 19372.

\paragraph{Translation boundary.}
The result is a direct DNA-to-protein-omics residual association, not a translation-realization claim.  The matched translation boundary is mandatory because a protein-abundance association can arise from transcriptional abundance, protein turnover, measurement bias, organism-specific regulation, or other downstream effects.  The linked modeled translation-readout run used only three organisms for tAI-style codon adaptation weights and explicitly classified that surface as modeled and underpowered for rank-powered survival; its contact had 81 entries above the bounded coupling threshold $0.01$, but $n=3$ was not enough to promote the fitted surface to a powered biological survival claim.  Consequently, positive $S^{QP}$ entries here do not imply a mediator $q_i \to T_j \to P_k$.  A mediation statement would require same-row translation measurements $T_j$ and an explicit fit showing that conditioning on $T_j$ removes or reduces the $q_i \to P_k$ association.

\paragraph{Relation to mRNA half-life controls.}
Separate mRNA-stability contacts show that the same coordinate family can also touch transcript-layer readouts, but these contacts do not explain the protein-omics panel by themselves.  In yeast, the mRNA half-life statistic has $R^2_{QM}=0.051794$, same-join $R^2_{QP}=0.276114$, cosine $0.804094$, dominant $S^{QM}$ coordinate $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$, and permutation value $p=0.000999$ with 1000 permutations.  In $E.\ coli$, $R^2_{QM}=0.022986$, same-join $R^2_{QP}=0.116323$, cosine $-0.778492$, dominant coordinate $Arg\_AGR$, and the same permutation value $p=0.000999$.  In human consensus half-life data, $R^2_{QM}=0.010504$, same-join $R^2_{QP}=0.040952$, cosine $0.635118$, dominant coordinate $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$, and $p=0.000999$.  These numbers support a layered interpretation: the $B^*_{Q6}$ coordinates have finite residual contact with protein abundance and with some transcript half-life surfaces, but the route between DNA, mRNA, translation, and protein output remains an empirical question for joined multi-readout designs.

\paragraph{Local cannot-claim boundary.}
This chapter establishes a finite protein-omics survival record for $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinates under sequence-composition controls.  It does not establish translation, ribosome occupancy, causal mediation, protein folding, physical admissibility, localization, post-translational modification, pathway function, phenotype, adaptation, or universality.  PAXdb abundance is a cross-sectional protein measurement, not a translation-rate readout; the fit is associative, not perturbational; and no rescue, ribosome-profiling, matched translation-efficiency, or explicit mediation matrix is part of the reported protein-omics statistic.  The correct reading is therefore narrow: the nine-coordinate synonymous residual block retains direct predictive information for measured protein abundance in the finite joined panel, while every mechanistic or higher-layer interpretation requires a separate layer-matched contact and its own controlled statistic.
