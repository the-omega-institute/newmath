\paragraph{Translation boundary.}
This chapter records $S^{QP}$, not $S^{QT}$ and not $M^{QTP}$.  The measured PAXdb abundance column is a cross-sectional protein-omics readout; it is not a ribosome-occupancy, elongation-rate, or translation-efficiency measurement.  A separate modeled translation contact exists only at bounded tAI scope: for three organisms it computed modeled tAI codon weights from GtRNAdb tRNA gene copy data and dos Reis wobble weights, with 81 entries above the $0.01$ coupling threshold and maximum absolute modeled coupling $3801961.031608$.  That same record explicitly has organism count $3$ and is not powered survival; it does not supply a measured $T_j$ mediator for the present protein-abundance result.  Consequently, the correct interpretation is direct DNA-to-protein-omics predictive irreducibility after CDS-derived controls, not a translation mechanism.

\paragraph{Local verdict.}
The finite row panel supports the claim that at least one B*\_Q6 amino-acid-quotient residual coordinate block has positive direct survival against measured protein abundance under the recorded controls, and in the computed panel all nine coordinates survive for each of the fifteen organisms.  The statement remains intentionally narrow.  It does not imply translation realization, protein folding, biochemical function, localization, post-translational modification, physical admissibility, phenotype, causation, mechanism, or universality.  Those stronger readings require their own layer-matched contacts: in particular, a same-row translation readout $T_j$ and an explicit mediation fit are required before any $q_i\to T_j\to P_k$ interpretation is allowed.  Without that mediator, a positive $S^{QP}$ entry is a controlled protein-omics association and nothing more.
