\subsection{Direct Protein-Omics Survival of $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ Residual Coordinates}\label{subsec:biorality-direct-protein-omics-survival-bstar-q6}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite carrier.}
This record tests whether synonymous residual coordinates in the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ family retain predictive contact with measured protein abundance after the amino-acid quotient and after ordinary CDS-level composition controls have been removed.  The finite carrier is a CDS-by-organism table whose rows are real coding sequences joined to PAXdb protein abundance, and whose columns include nine residual coordinates,
$K\_AAA$, $Arg\_AGR$, $Ile\_AUA$, $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$, $Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG$, $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$, $Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG$, $Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY$, and $f3\_stress$.
The protein readout is $\log_{10}(\mathrm{abundance\_ppm})$ from measured PAXdb abundance joined to real CDS codon counts.  The controlled design uses an intercept, $\log(\mathrm{CDS\ length})$, amino-acid composition, $GC3$, and the $M$-density baseline induced by the union support of the nine projected coordinates.  For every organism below, the residualized $Q$ block has rank $9$ after the control block has rank $23$, so the positive entries are not produced by an empty or collapsed residual coordinate space.

\paragraph{Controlled statistic.}
For an organism $o$, let $P_o$ be the residualized protein-abundance vector and let $Q_o$ be the residualized nine-column $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ coordinate matrix after the same control block $Z_o$ has been projected out.  The reported survival score is the partial protein-omics coefficient of determination
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{QP}(o) = 1 - \frac{\lVert P_o - \Pi_{Q_o}P_o\rVert^2}{\lVert P_o\rVert^2},
\end{aligned}
$$
with a positive-entry threshold of $10^{-12}$.  This is a direct $q_i$ to $P_k$ association score.  It is not a translation-rate measurement, not a mediation statistic, and not a causal perturbation result.

\paragraph{Rank and residual-energy checks.}
The control residualization is numerically nondegenerate in each organism.  The residual degrees of freedom after $Z$ range from $1633$ in Sulfolobus solfataricus to $19372$ in Mus musculus.  Representative residual protein-abundance energies are $4020.333671802061$ for Escherichia coli K-12 MG1655, $30769.5367150478$ for Homo sapiens, $48806.47856410273$ for Mus musculus, and $2958.1865890925337$ for Saccharomyces cerevisiae.  The corresponding explained energies assigned to the residual $Q$ block are $432.89050065533684$, $1370.6490494847067$, $2431.9284360407714$, and $882.6526210803958$.  These values are consistent with a finite residual association rather than a rank-saturated artifact: the degenerate $R^2$ refusal threshold is $0.999999$, while the observed $R^2_{QP}$ values remain well below that boundary.

\paragraph{Translation boundary.}
The same research line contains a separate modeled translation readout based on tRNA gene copy and dos Reis wobble weights in three organisms.  That modeled-tAI calculation has $81$ bounded-coupling entries above threshold $0.01$, with maximum absolute entry $3801961.031607896$, but its organism count is only $3$.  It is therefore explicitly not a powered measured translation-survival result.  The present chapter does not use that modeled readout to promote $S^{QP}$ into a translation mechanism.  A translation-mediated statement would require a same-row matrix with a measured or otherwise explicitly bounded translation readout $T_j$, matched mRNA control, and an attenuation or mediation fit showing that conditioning on $T_j$ removes or reduces the direct $q_i$ to $P_k$ association.

\paragraph{Local interpretation.}
The accepted claim is narrow: after amino-acid composition, length, $GC3$, and $M$-density controls, the nine $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates retain direct predictive association with measured protein abundance in the joined PAXdb/CDS panels.  The claim is an omics-level residual association, not a statement about ribosome occupancy, elongation, folding, localization, post-translational state, biochemical function, physical admissibility, organismal phenotype, causal mechanism, adaptation, or universality.  Any one of those stronger readings would need its own layer-matched contact and a statistic computed on the corresponding rows.  In particular, a positive $S^{QP}$ entry does not become $q_i \to T_j \to P_k$ unless a measured translation surface is attached and the mediation matrix supports that path under the same controls.
