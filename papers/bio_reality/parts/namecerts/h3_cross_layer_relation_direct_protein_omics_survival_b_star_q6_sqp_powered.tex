\subsection{Direct protein-omics survival of $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates}\label{sec:bq6-direct-protein-omics-survival}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite carrier.}
The record considered here is a finite CDS-by-organism table whose rows are coding sequences with matched codon-count coordinates and measured protein-abundance readouts.  The synonymous coordinates are the nine $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual directions
$K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $Leu_{CUN\,vs\,UUR}$, $Leu_{UUA\,vs\,UUG}$, $Ser_{UCR\,vs\,AGY}$, $Ser_{UCA\,vs\,UCG}$, $Thr_{ACR\,vs\,ACY}$, and $f3_{stress}$.  Each coordinate is formed after the amino-acid quotient, so the test is not a disguised amino-acid composition comparison.  The protein-omics column is $\log_{10}(abundance\_ppm)$ from measured PAXdb protein abundance joined to real CDS codon counts.  The finite joins used $3739$ proteins for \emph{Escherichia coli} K-12 MG1655 with hit rate $0.9978649586335735$, $4129$ proteins for \emph{Mycobacterium smegmatis} str. MC2 155 with hit rate $1.0$, and $5815$ proteins for \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae} with hit rate $0.9156038419146592$.

\paragraph{Residualized test statistic.}
For each organism, the protein-abundance vector $P$ and the coordinate matrix $Q$ are tested only after removing the baseline control space $Z$.  In this finite run, $Z$ contains length, amino-acid composition, GC3, and M-density controls, with $24$ control columns and numerical rank $23$ in each organism.  Let $\widetilde Q=(I-\Pi_Z)Q$ and $\widetilde P=(I-\Pi_Z)P$.  The reported score is the direct controlled protein-omics survival value
$$
\begin{aligned}
S^{QP} &= R^2(\widetilde P\,;\,\widetilde Q),\\
S^{QP} &> 10^{-12}\quad\hbox{counts as positive direct survival in this finite audit.}
\end{aligned}
$$
The residual design is rank-sufficient in all three organisms: $rank(\widetilde Q)=9$ with $9$ tested coordinates.  After projecting away $Z$, the residual degrees of freedom are $3716$ for \emph{E. coli}, $4106$ for \emph{M. smegmatis}, and $5792$ for yeast.  The corresponding residual abundance energies are $4020.333671802061$, $5729.194538214237$, and $2958.1865890925337$.  The baseline explained energies are $432.89050065533684$, $183.85037813566998$, and $882.6526210803958$, respectively.  The degeneracy guard is $R^2<0.999999$, so the reported positives are not accepted from an all-explained or rank-collapsed surface.

\paragraph{Finite readback.}
The direct controlled protein-omics survival score is positive in every organism in the finite panel.  For \emph{E. coli} K-12 MG1655, the fitted value is $R^2_{QP}=0.10767526677985896$, with all $9$ residual coordinates surviving the positivity threshold.  For \emph{M. smegmatis} str. MC2 155, the fitted value is $R^2_{QP}=0.03209009170649934$, again with $9$ surviving coordinates.  For \emph{S. cerevisiae}, the fitted value is $R^2_{QP}=0.29837624994140827$, with $9$ surviving coordinates.  Thus the finite row calculation supports a direct association between synonym-residual codon topology and measured protein abundance after the stated sequence-composition controls.  The strongest of the three organism-level scores in this audit is the yeast value $0.29837624994140827$, while the weakest is the \emph{M. smegmatis} value $0.03209009170649934$; both remain above the declared positive threshold.

\paragraph{Translation boundary.}
This chapter does not identify the direct $Q\to P$ association with translation mechanism.  The run did not use perturbation, rescue, ribosome profiling, or a joint mediation fit.  The measured PAXdb abundance column is a cross-sectional protein-omics readout, not a translation-rate measurement.  A translation-mediated statement would require a matched translation readout $T$, matched mRNA control rows, and an explicit comparison of the direct route with a mediation chain
$$
\begin{aligned}
q_i &\longrightarrow T_j \longrightarrow P_k,\\
q_i &\longrightarrow P_k\mid T_j.
\end{aligned}
$$
Only such a joined design could say whether conditioning on measured translation removes, reduces, or leaves the $q_i$ to $P_k$ signal.  The present finite table therefore records direct protein-omics irreducibility under the stated controls, not translation realization.

\paragraph{Local cannot-claim boundary.}
No claim about translation dynamics, ribosome occupancy, folding, localization, post-translational state, biochemical function, physical admissibility, organismal phenotype, causal mechanism, or universal biological law follows from the positive $S^{QP}$ values above.  Those layers require their own matched observations and their own controlled statistics.  In particular, positive protein-abundance survival does not show that a codon residual changes translation rate, stabilizes a folded protein, controls localization, supplies a pathway function, or improves fitness.  The supported statement is narrower: in three finite organism-specific CDS-protein tables, the nine $B^{*}_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates retain positive predictive relation to measured PAXdb protein abundance after length, amino-acid composition, GC3, and M-density controls, with the translation-mediation boundary still open.
