\subsection{Direct protein-omics survival for $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates under a translation boundary}
\label{sec:biorreality-q6-direct-protein-omics-survival}

\paragraph{Finite carrier.}
The finite carrier is the matched CDS-by-protein table obtained by joining real coding-sequence codon counts to measured PAXdb protein abundance and then evaluating the nine amino-acid-quotient residual coordinates of $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$.  The coordinate list is
$$
\begin{aligned}
Q=\{&K\_AAA,\ Arg\_AGR,\ Ile\_AUA,\ Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR,\ Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG,\\
&Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY,\ Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG,\ Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY,\ f3\_stress\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
For each protein row, these coordinates are residual codon-topology quantities after quotienting by amino-acid identity; they are not amino-acid composition effects.  The response column is $\log_{10}(\mathrm{abundance\_ppm})$ from measured PAXdb protein abundance.  The baseline matrix $Z$ contains an intercept, $\log(\mathrm{CDS\ length})$, the twenty amino-acid composition proportions, GC3, and the $M$-density baseline on the union support of the nine projected coordinates.  Thus the tested statistic is a direct $Q \to P$ protein-omics association after CDS-derived controls, not a translation-rate statistic.

\paragraph{Residualized model.}
Within each organism, the protein-abundance vector $P$ and the coordinate matrix $Q$ are first residualized against $Z$.  The score reported here is the partial protein-omics survival
$$
\begin{aligned}
S^{QP} &= R^{2}\bigl(P_{\perp Z}\sim Q_{\perp Z}\bigr),\\
\mathrm{positive\ entry} &\Longleftrightarrow S^{QP}>10^{-12},\\
\mathrm{degenerate\ fit\ cutoff} &= 0.999999.
\end{aligned}
$$
The finite-row audit reports $24$ control columns with $\operatorname{rank}(Z)=23$ in every organism and $\operatorname{rank}(Q_{\perp Z})=9$ in every organism.  Hence the nine-coordinate residual surface is not collapsed by the control projection in these rows.  The run identifier for the direct protein-omics computation is $104db736a756$, verified at 2026-06-12T11:06:10+00:00.

\paragraph{Translation boundary.}
This chapter records direct protein-omics survival only.  The finite rows do not contain a same-row measured translation mediator $T$, so a positive $S^{QP}$ entry is not evidence for a $Q\to T\to P$ mechanism.  A translation-mediated statement would require matched codon counts, matched mRNA controls, a measured translation readout such as ribosome occupancy or translation efficiency, matched protein abundance, and an explicit mediation surface showing that the $Q\to T$ step survives and that conditioning on $T$ removes or reduces the direct $Q\to P$ association.  The available modeled-tAI result is also not a replacement for measured translation: it used only $3$ organisms, computed modeled tAI from GtRNAdb tRNA gene copy and dos Reis wobble weights, found $81$ modeled coupling entries above threshold $0.01$, and reached maximum absolute coupling $3801961.031608$, but it explicitly remains underpowered for powered survival because $3$ organisms are below the requested $40$-species boundary.

\paragraph{Local cannot-claim boundary.}
The admissible conclusion is narrow: after controlling for length, amino-acid composition, GC3, and $M$-density, the nine $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates retain measurable direct association with matched PAXdb protein abundance in all fifteen finite organism tables.  Nothing in this record establishes translation realization, ribosome dynamics, protein folding, physical admissibility, localization, post-translational state, biochemical function, phenotype, causal mechanism, or a universal biological law.  Each of those stronger readings would require its own layer-matched contact and its own controlled statistic.  In particular, absent a measured translation readout $T$ on the same rows, this chapter must be read as direct DNA-to-protein-omics predictive irreducibility under stated controls, not as a translation-mediated explanation.
