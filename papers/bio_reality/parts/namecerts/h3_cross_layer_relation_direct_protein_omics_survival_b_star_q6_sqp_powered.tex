\paragraph{Finite carrier.}
The finite panel consists of real CDS codon-count rows joined to measured PAXdb protein abundance rows, with nine amino-acid-quotient residual coordinates carried as the $Q$ side of the test.  The coordinates are $K\_AAA$, $Arg\_AGR$, $Ile\_AUA$, $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$, $Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG$, $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$, $Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG$, $Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY$, and $f3\_stress$.  Each protein-abundance response is the joined value $\log_{10}(\mathrm{abundance\_ppm})$, and the reported statistic is a direct residual protein-omics survival score $S^{QP}$ after removing the declared CDS-derived baseline.  The baseline contains an intercept, CDS length, amino-acid composition, $GC3$, and an $M$-density support control; in the realized matrices this is recorded as $24$ control columns with $\operatorname{rank}(Z)=23$.  After residualization, the $Q$ block retains full rank, $\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde X)=9$, in every organism listed below, so the positive entries are not a rank-zero artifact.

\paragraph{Translation boundary.}
This result is deliberately narrower than a translation mechanism claim.  The response variable is measured protein abundance, not ribosome occupancy, elongation rate, translation efficiency, or a perturbational translation readout.  A separate modeled-tAI contact exists for three organisms, with $81$ entries above threshold $0.01$ and maximum absolute modeled coupling $3801961.031607896$, but that earlier contact is explicitly underpowered for survival because $n=3$ is insufficient for a powered claim requiring at least $40$ matched species.  The present protein-omics panel repairs the row-count problem for direct $Q\to P$ association, but it does not by itself attach a matched $T$ mediator.  Therefore the mathematical readback is
$$
\begin{aligned}
Q_i &\longrightarrow P_k \quad \text{is observed as controlled direct protein-omics association},\\
Q_i &\longrightarrow T_j \longrightarrow P_k \quad \text{is not established by this panel}.
\end{aligned}
$$
A mediation statement would require same-CDS rows carrying the residual coordinate $Q_i$, a measured translation readout $T_j$, matched mRNA abundance, and the protein-omics response $P_k$, followed by an explicit attenuation or absorption fit.

\paragraph{Local interpretation.}
The finite result supports the statement that B$^*_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates retain predictive information for measured protein abundance after amino-acid composition, length, $GC3$, and $M$-density controls.  It should be read as DNA-to-protein-omics irreducibility in the joined PAXdb panels.  It should not be read as evidence for translation rate, folding, localization, post-translational modification, biochemical function, phenotype, physical admissibility, causation, mechanism, or a universal biological law.  Those interpretations require separate layer-matched contacts and separate statistics.  In particular, a positive $S^{QP}$ entry remains a direct omics correlation unless a measured translation mediator is attached and shown to carry the $Q_i\to P_k$ signal through a controlled $Q_i\to T_j\to P_k$ mediation surface.
