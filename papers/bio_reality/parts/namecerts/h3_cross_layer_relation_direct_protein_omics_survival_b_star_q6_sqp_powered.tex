\subsection{Direct Protein-Omics Survival of $B^{*}_{Q6}$ Residual Coordinates}
\label{subsec:biorreality-direct-protein-omics-survival-b-star-q6}

\paragraph{Finite carrier and readout.}
This record tests whether synonymous residual coordinates from $B^{*}_{Q6}$ retain predictive mass against measured protein abundance after the amino-acid quotient has already removed the primary coding-content layer.  The finite carrier is a CDS-by-organism table with nine residual coordinates,
$$
\begin{aligned}
Q=\{&K\_AAA,\ Arg\_AGR,\ Ile\_AUA,\ Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR,\ Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG,\\
&Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY,\ Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG,\ Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY,\ f3\_stress\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
For each organism row block, the protein readout is $\log_{10}(\mathrm{abundance\_ppm})$ from measured PAXdb protein abundance joined to real CDS codon counts.  The control matrix contains 24 columns: intercept, log CDS length, twenty amino-acid composition proportions, GC3, and the $M$-density baseline defined on the union support of the nine projected residual coordinates.  Across all reported organism blocks, the residualized coordinate matrix has $\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde X)=9$, the control matrix has $\operatorname{rank}(Z)=23$, and the powered-rank flag is true.  Thus the direct statistic is not an $n=3$ saturated readback: it is a per-protein within-organism residual association computed on joined protein rows.

\paragraph{Rank and residual-energy checks.}
The finite rows also record nondegenerate residual degrees of freedom after the control projection.  Representative values include $4019$ residual degrees of freedom for $\mathrm{B.\ subtilis}$, $3716$ for $\mathrm{E.\ coli}$, $5792$ for $\mathrm{S.\ cerevisiae}$, $19030$ for $\mathrm{H.\ sapiens}$, and $19372$ for $\mathrm{M.\ musculus}$.  The residual protein-abundance energies after controls are likewise nonzero: $3165.2372866126684$ for $\mathrm{B.\ subtilis}$, $4020.333671802061$ for $\mathrm{E.\ coli}$, $2958.1865890925337$ for $\mathrm{S.\ cerevisiae}$, $30769.5367150478$ for $\mathrm{H.\ sapiens}$, and $48806.47856410273$ for $\mathrm{M.\ musculus}$.  These numbers support the narrow claim that the $Q$-coordinates retain direct protein-abundance signal after the stated CDS-derived controls.  They do not by themselves identify a biochemical path from codon choice to protein amount.

\paragraph{Translation boundary.}
The same data package records why the interpretation must stay narrow.  A separate modeled-tAI contact used only three organisms and explicitly reports that three organisms are insufficient for powered survival.  That modeled translation readout is bounded coupling, not ribosome profiling or proteomics validation, even though it contains nonzero modeled coupling entries above threshold: $81$ entries exceed $0.01$, and the maximum absolute modeled score is $3801961.031607896$.  The direct protein-omics run itself states that measured PAXdb protein abundance is cross-sectional abundance, not a translation-rate measurement, and that positive $S^{QP}$ entries are not a mediation matrix $M^{QTP}$.  Therefore the present result is a DNA-to-protein-omics residual association, not a translation mechanism.

\paragraph{Comparison with mRNA-stability contacts.}
Linked finite rows show that the same nine residual coordinates can also be projected against mRNA half-life readouts, but those rows remain separate contacts rather than explanations of the protein-abundance result.  In yeast, the mRNA half-life statistic reports $R^2_{QM}=0.05179429044975085$, $R^2_{QP}=0.2761140373962526$ on the same join, cosine $0.8040935468384264$ between $S^{QM}$ and $S^{QP}$, dominant $Q$ coordinate $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$, and permutation $p=0.000999000999000999$ with $1000$ permutations.  In $\mathrm{E.\ coli}$, the corresponding mRNA statistic reports $R^2_{QM}=0.02298556640056076$, same-join $R^2_{QP}=0.11632311854858134$, cosine $-0.7784923375569599$, dominant coordinate $Arg\_AGR$, and the same permutation $p=0.000999000999000999$.  In human, the consensus relative half-life row reports $R^2_{QM}=0.010503670362051522$, same-join $R^2_{QP}=0.04095215557813418$, cosine $0.6351183883924848$, dominant $Q$ coordinate $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$, and permutation $p=0.000999000999000999$.  These comparisons indicate that $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residuals meet multiple omics surfaces with organism-specific signs and dominant coordinates, but they do not collapse the RNA, translation, and protein-abundance layers into one mechanism.

\paragraph{Local verdict and forbidden promotions.}
The chapter-level verdict is that direct $S^{QP}$ protein-omics survival is observed in the finite PAXdb-CDS table under the declared controls, with fifteen organism blocks, nine surviving coordinates in every block, and powered rank sufficiency in every reported block.  The chapter does not claim translation realization, ribosome occupancy, protein folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, causal mechanism, organism-level phenotype, or universality.  Any such statement would require a separate layer-matched contact: for translation, same-row ribosome or translation-efficiency measurements with matched mRNA controls; for mediation, a joint $q_i \to T_j \to P_k$ fit showing that the translation readout survives and absorbs or reduces the direct $q_i \to P_k$ association; for structure, function, localization, stability, phenotype, or physical admissibility, an assay or prediction contact at that specific layer.  Without those additional contacts, the result remains a controlled direct protein-abundance association of synonymous residual coordinates after amino-acid composition, GC3, length, and $M$-density have been removed.
