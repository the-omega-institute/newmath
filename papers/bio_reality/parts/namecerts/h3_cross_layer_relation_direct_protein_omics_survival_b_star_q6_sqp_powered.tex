\subsection{Direct $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ Protein-Omics Survival With a Translation Boundary}\label{sec:biorality-q6-direct-protein-omics-survival}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite carrier.}
This record tests whether synonymous residual coordinates in $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ retain direct predictive contact with matched protein-omics readouts after amino-acid quotienting and baseline residualization. The finite carrier is a CDS-by-organism table whose rows are coding sequences joined to measured protein abundance, whose residual coordinate columns are the nine $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ coordinates
$K\_AAA$, $Arg\_AGR$, $Ile\_AUA$, $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$, $Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG$, $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$, $Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG$, $Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY$, and $f3\_stress$, and whose response column is $\log_{10}(\mathrm{abundance\_ppm})$ from measured PAXdb protein abundance. The coordinate source is the same finite $q$-vector construction used for the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ translation-survival computation, so the protein-omics test is performed on residual codon-topology coordinates rather than raw codon counts.

\paragraph{Rows and joins.}
The protein-abundance contact is not symbolic: it is a finite joined table for three organisms. For \emph{Escherichia coli} K-12 MG1655, $3739$ proteins are used with join hit rate $0.9978649586335735$. For \emph{Mycobacterium smegmatis} str. mc2 155, $4129$ proteins are used with join hit rate $1.0$. For \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}, $5815$ proteins are used with join hit rate $0.9156038419146592$. Thus the test is a per-CDS association against measured steady-state abundance, not a claim inferred from codon-usage tables alone.

\paragraph{Residualization design.}
For each organism the response and the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ columns are residualized against baseline controls including length, amino-acid composition, GC3, and M-density controls. The recorded control design has $24$ control columns and rank $23$ in each organism, with the degeneracy threshold fixed at $0.999999$. The residualized $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ design remains full rank: $\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde X)=9$ for all three organisms. The remaining response degrees of freedom are $3716$ in \emph{E. coli}, $4106$ in \emph{M. smegmatis}, and $5792$ in yeast. The corresponding residual response energies are $4020.333671802061$, $5729.194538214237$, and $2958.1865890925337$, while the explained energies assigned by the controlled fit are $432.89050065533684$, $183.85037813566998$, and $882.6526210803958$.

\paragraph{Survival statistic.}
The finite readout computes a direct protein-omics survival surface $S^{QP}$ after the stated controls, with a positivity threshold for partial $R^2$ equal to $10^{-12}$. The organism-level controlled $R^2_{QP}$ values are
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{QP}(\mathrm{E.\ coli\ K\mbox{-}12\ MG1655}) &= 0.10767526677985896,\\
R^2_{QP}(\mathrm{M.\ smegmatis\ str.\ mc2\ 155}) &= 0.03209009170649934,\\
R^2_{QP}(\mathrm{S.\ cerevisiae}) &= 0.29837624994140827.
\end{aligned}
$$
In all three organism tables, the surviving coordinate count is $9$. This supports the narrow statement that the nine $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates retain nonzero direct association with measured protein abundance after the listed sequence and composition controls. It does not by itself identify which biochemical process carries the signal.

\paragraph{Translation boundary.}
A separate translation-layer calculation exists only as a bounded modeled-readout contact. In that finite contact, $3$ organisms were used, $61$ sense codons were represented, and the local tRNA payloads contained all $20$ standard amino-acid families for \emph{E. coli}, human, and yeast. The modeled tAI readout from GtRNAdb tRNA gene copy and dos Reis wobble weights had no zero raw-weight fallback among the $61$ sense codons in any of the three organisms. The bounded modeled-tAI coupling table recorded $81$ entries above threshold $0.01$, with maximum absolute coupling $3801961.031607896$. This translation contact is deliberately not promoted to measured ribosome occupancy or measured translation efficiency: the organism count is $3$, and the record itself refuses powered survival when at least $40$ matched species are required.

\paragraph{Interpretation.}
The present claim is therefore a direct DNA-to-protein-omics association under controls, not a translation-mechanism result. The matrix $S^{QP}$ records that the controlled codon-residual coordinates still explain some measured protein-abundance variance. It does not show $q_i \to T_j \to P_k$ mediation, because the protein-abundance run did not attach a matched measured translation readout $T_j$ and did not fit a mediation matrix $M^{QTP}$. The correct readback is: conditioning on the available sequence and composition controls leaves a positive $q_i$ to $P_k$ signal in each of the three organism tables; whether a measured translation readout removes, reduces, or leaves that signal is still a separate finite test.

\paragraph{Local no-promotion rule.}
No translation-rate, ribosome-dynamics, folding, physical-admissibility, localization, post-translational-state, molecular-function, phenotype, causal-mechanism, or universal-law conclusion follows from this protein-omics survival score. Steady-state PAXdb abundance is a protein-omics measurement, not a ribosome-profiling measurement, not a perturbation, not a rescue experiment, and not a structural assay. A translation claim requires matched $T_j$ rows and a controlled $q_i \to T_j$ statistic. A mediation claim requires a joint $q_i,T_j,P_k$ table and an explicit $M^{QTP}$ fit showing how conditioning on $T_j$ changes the direct $q_i \to P_k$ association. Structure, physical admissibility, function, localization, stability, phenotype, and universality each require their own layer-matched contact and cannot be inferred from the positive $S^{QP}$ entries reported here.
