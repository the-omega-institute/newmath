\paragraph{Finite carrier.}
The record is a finite coding-sequence by organism table whose columns include nine amino-acid-quotient $B^*_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates and one measured protein-omics readout.  The residual coordinates are
$$
\begin{aligned}
&K\_AAA,\ Arg\_AGR,\ Ile\_AUA,\ Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR,\ Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG,\\
&Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY,\ Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG,\ Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY,\ f3\_stress.
\end{aligned}
$$
The protein readout is $\log_{10}(\mathrm{abundance\_ppm})$ from measured PAXdb protein abundance joined to real CDS codon counts.  The reported statistic is $S^{QP}$: the residual predictive contribution of the $q_i$ codon-coordinate block to the protein-omics readout $P_k$ after removing the declared CDS-derived baseline controls.  The control block contains an intercept, $\log(\mathrm{CDS\ length})$, twenty amino-acid composition proportions, GC3, and an $M$-density baseline defined from the union support of the nine projected $B^*_{Q6}$ vectors.  In each organism the control design has 24 columns and rank $23$, while the residualized $Q$ block has rank $9$.

\paragraph{Rank and residual energy checks.}
The finite-row test avoids the three-organism rank-saturation failure seen in the modeled translation readout.  The earlier modeled tAI contact used $3$ organisms, had a nonzero bounded coupling surface with $81$ entries above threshold $0.01$, and reached maximum absolute $S_{ij}=3801961.031607896$ under a modeled tRNA-copy and dos Reis wobble-weight construction.  That $n=3$ surface was explicitly not a powered survival result.  By contrast, the protein-omics table contains thousands of CDS-protein rows per organism, and every organism has residual degrees of freedom far above the rank of the $Q$ block: for example $3716$ in \emph{E. coli}, $5792$ in yeast, $19030$ in human, and $19372$ in mouse.  The residual response energies after the baseline controls are also finite and nonzero: $4020.333671802061$ in \emph{E. coli}, $2958.1865890925337$ in yeast, $30769.5367150478$ in human, and $48806.47856410273$ in mouse.  The rank condition therefore records an actual residual regression surface rather than a saturated all-positive artifact.

\paragraph{Boundary against translation mediation.}
The present statistic is $S^{QP}$, not $S^{QT}$ and not $M^{QTP}$.  The measured PAXdb abundance column is a cross-sectional protein-abundance readout.  It is not a ribosome-footprint column, not a translation-efficiency column, not a perturbation assay, and not a rescue experiment.  Consequently a positive $Q \to P$ entry says only that synonymous residual coordinates retain direct predictive information for the measured protein-omics table after the stated CDS controls.  It does not say that the route is translational.  A translation-mediated interpretation would require a same-row translation readout $T_j$ and a mediation fit testing whether $q_i \to T_j \to P_k$ survives, and whether conditioning on measured $T_j$ removes or reduces the direct $q_i \to P_k$ association.  Without that added column and statistic, the record remains a direct protein-omics association.

\paragraph{mRNA comparison rows.}
The same nine-coordinate block has also been evaluated against mRNA half-life readouts in three finite settings, which provides useful scale but not a protein-translation mechanism.  In yeast, $5085$ joined rows give $R^2_{QM}=0.05179429044975085$, $R^2_{QP}=0.2761140373962526$ on the same join, cosine $0.8040935468384264$, and permutation $p=0.000999000999000999$ with dominant coordinate $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$.  In \emph{E. coli}, $2720$ joined rows give $R^2_{QM}=0.02298556640056076$, same-join $R^2_{QP}=0.11632311854858134$, cosine $-0.7784923375569599$, and permutation $p=0.000999000999000999$ with dominant coordinate $Arg\_AGR$.  In human, $16165$ joined rows across $54$ consensus dataset columns give $R^2_{QM}=0.010503670362051522$, same-join $R^2_{QP}=0.04095215557813418$, cosine $0.6351183883924848$, and permutation $p=0.000999000999000999$ with dominant coordinate $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$.  These comparisons show that the $Q$ block is not only tracking one protein-abundance table shape, but they remain statistical projections onto mRNA or protein readouts rather than decay mechanisms, translation mechanisms, or causal assertions.

\paragraph{Local cannot-claim boundary.}
This chapter licenses the following finite statement: in the listed organism-specific PAXdb joins, the nine $B^*_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates have positive direct residual association with measured protein abundance after length, amino-acid composition, GC3, and $M$-density controls.  It does not license translation realization, ribosome mechanism, protein folding, physical admissibility, localization, post-translational modification, biochemical function, phenotype, adaptation, causation, or universality.  Each of those stronger readings requires its own measured contact and its own finite statistic.  In particular, protein abundance cannot substitute for ribosome profiling, and a codon residual cannot become a mechanism merely because its direct protein-omics score is positive.

\paragraph{Verdict.}
The direct $S^{QP}$ surface is ready as a finite protein-omics association record.  The translation-mediated reading is not ready inside this record: it needs matched translation readouts, matched mRNA controls on the same rows, and an explicit $M^{QTP}$ mediation matrix.  The correct interpretation is therefore positive direct protein-omics survival with a hard boundary against mechanism promotion.
