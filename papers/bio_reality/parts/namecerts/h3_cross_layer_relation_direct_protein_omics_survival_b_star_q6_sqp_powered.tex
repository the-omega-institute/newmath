\subsection{Direct protein-omics survival of $B^*_{Q6}$ residual codon coordinates}\label{subsec:bioreality-q6-direct-protein-omics-survival}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite panel and coordinate carrier.}
The record tests whether synonymous residual coordinates retained after the amino-acid quotient still predict a measured protein-omics readout once ordinary coding-sequence covariates have been removed. The finite carrier is a CDS-by-protein table joined to PAXdb abundance and equipped with nine $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinates: $K\_AAA$, $Arg\_AGR$, $Ile\_AUA$, $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$, $Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG$, $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$, $Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG$, $Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY$, and $f3\_stress$. The readout is $\log_{10}(\mathrm{abundance\_ppm})$ from measured PAXdb protein abundance, joined to real CDS codon counts. The fitted score is the direct $S^{QP}$ surface: a codon-residual-to-protein-abundance association after CDS-derived controls, not a translation-rate measurement and not a causal mediation statistic.

\paragraph{Residualization design.}
For every organism the baseline matrix contains 24 control columns, with numerical rank $23$ after residualization. The nine $B^*_{Q6}$ columns retain rank $9$ after projection away from the controls. The controls include coding length, amino-acid composition, GC3, and an $M$-density baseline on the union support of the nine projected coordinates. The residual degrees of freedom after the baseline range from $1633$ in \emph{S. solfataricus} to $19372$ in mouse. The positive-entry threshold is $10^{-12}$ in partial-$R^2$ units, and the degeneracy cutoff is $R^2=0.999999$; all 15 organism-specific fits are rank-sufficient under these finite-row checks.

\paragraph{Relation to translation and RNA controls.}
A separate modeled-tAI contact exists for three organisms, with 61 sense codons, organism-level tRNA gene-copy readouts, dos Reis wobble weights, and 81 entries above the bounded coupling threshold $0.01$; its maximum absolute modeled coupling is $3801961.031608$. That three-organism modeled surface is explicitly underpowered for powered survival because $n=3$ is below the required $40$ matched species. The present $S^{QP}$ result therefore must not be re-described as measured translation survival. In the RNA-stability comparison records, yeast gives $R^2_{QM}=0.051794$ with $R^2_{QP}=0.276114$ on the same join and cosine $0.804094$ between $S^{QM}$ and $S^{QP}$; \emph{E. coli} gives $R^2_{QM}=0.022986$, same-join $R^2_{QP}=0.116323$, and cosine $-0.778492$; human gives $R^2_{QM}=0.010504$, same-join $R^2_{QP}=0.040952$, and cosine $0.635118$. Each of these RNA records has permutation $p=0.000999$ where reported. These auxiliary finite-row facts show that the same residual coordinate block can meet several omics surfaces, but they do not supply the missing joint $q_i \to T_j \to P_k$ mediation matrix for the protein-abundance panel.

\paragraph{Local interpretation.}
The supported statement is narrow: after the stated CDS-derived controls, the nine-dimensional $B^*_{Q6}$ synonymous residual block retains nonzero direct association with measured protein abundance in every organism-specific table in this finite panel. The association is a protein-omics predictive residual. It may be useful as a downstream readout of codon-topology information that is not erased by amino-acid composition, length, GC3, or the $M$-density baseline.

\paragraph{Boundary of the claim.}
No translation mechanism follows from this chapter. PAXdb abundance is a cross-sectional protein-omics measurement, not a ribosome-occupancy, elongation-rate, or translation-efficiency readout. A positive $S^{QP}$ entry is not an $M^{QTP}$ mediation entry. Translation mediation would require matched rows carrying $q_i$, measured $T_j$, protein abundance $P_k$, mRNA abundance, and the same baseline controls, followed by an explicit test of whether conditioning on $T_j$ removes or reduces the direct $q_i \to P_k$ signal. Likewise, the present result says nothing by itself about protein folding, localization, post-translational modification, physical admissibility, biochemical function, phenotype, causation, adaptation, or universality. Each of those claims would require a separate layer-matched measurement and its own finite statistic.
