\subsection{Direct protein-omics survival of $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates}
\label{sec:bq6-direct-protein-omics-survival}

\paragraph{Finite carrier.}
This record fixes a finite CDS-by-organism table in which each coding sequence carries a synonymous residual coordinate vector, baseline covariates, and a measured protein-abundance response.  The coordinate side is the nine-dimensional $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual basis
$$
\begin{aligned}
Q=\{&K\_AAA,\ Arg\_AGR,\ Ile\_AUA,\ Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR,\ Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG,\\
&Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY,\ Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG,\ Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY,\ f3\_stress\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
These coordinates are synonymous residual coordinates after the amino-acid quotient: they are not amino-acid composition effects, and they are not promoted to protein function or translation mechanism by their notation alone.  The response is $P=\log_{10}(\mathrm{abundance\_ppm})$ from measured PAXdb protein abundance joined to real CDS codon counts.  The controlled design removes length, amino-acid composition, GC3, and M-density effects before testing the residual $Q \to P$ association.  The experiment identifier for the protein-omics run is $5cd589f63a85$, verified at $2026$-$06$-$06$ $15{:}15{:}56+00{:}00$.

\paragraph{Joined finite rows.}
The joined matrices are not symbolic examples.  In \emph{Escherichia coli} K-12 MG1655, $3739$ proteins were used after joining CDS and abundance rows, with join hit rate $0.9978649586335735$.  In \emph{Mycobacterium smegmatis} str. MC2 155, $4129$ proteins were used and the join hit rate was $1.0$.  In \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}, $5815$ proteins were used and the join hit rate was $0.9156038419146592$.  Thus the finite carrier has organism-specific row counts
$$
\begin{aligned}
N_{\mathrm{Eco}}&=3739, &N_{\mathrm{Msm}}&=4129, &N_{\mathrm{Sce}}&=5815,
\end{aligned}
$$
with the same nine residual coordinates evaluated inside each organism-specific protein-abundance table.

\paragraph{Residualization ranks.}
For each organism, the control matrix had $24$ columns and rank $23$, while the residualized $Q$ matrix retained rank $9$.  The post-control residual degrees of freedom were $3716$ for \emph{E. coli}, $4106$ for \emph{M. smegmatis}, and $5792$ for yeast.  The corresponding response residual energies were $4020.333671802061$, $5729.194538214237$, and $2958.1865890925337$.  The explained energies attributed to the control fit were $432.89050065533684$, $183.85037813566998$, and $882.6526210803958$, respectively.  The degeneracy threshold used to reject saturated fits was $0.999999$, and all three organism-specific designs were marked rank-sufficient for the nine-coordinate residual test:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\operatorname{rank}(Z)&=23,\\
\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde Q)&=9,\\
\operatorname{df}_{\mathrm{Eco}}&=3716,\quad
\operatorname{df}_{\mathrm{Msm}}=4106,\quad
\operatorname{df}_{\mathrm{Sce}}=5792.
\end{aligned}
$$
This rank statement is important because the same program explicitly refuses small, saturated surfaces: the related modeled-tAI contact has only $3$ organisms and is recorded as underpowered for powered survival, even though its bounded modeled coupling contains $81$ entries above threshold $0.01$ and a maximum absolute entry $3801961.031607896$.

\paragraph{Protein-omics survival statistic.}
The direct controlled protein-omics statistic is denoted $S^{QP}$.  For this finite table, the test returned a positive direct $Q \to P$ surface in all three organisms under the declared residualization.  The organism-level partial-$R^2$ values were
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{QP}(\mathrm{Eco})&=0.10767526677985896,\\
R^2_{QP}(\mathrm{Msm})&=0.03209009170649934,\\
R^2_{QP}(\mathrm{Sce})&=0.29837624994140827.
\end{aligned}
$$
The positivity threshold for partial $R^2$ was $10^{-12}$.  Each organism retained all $9$ residual coordinates as surviving coordinates against the measured protein-abundance readout.  The finite conclusion is therefore a direct protein-omics survival statement: after the baseline controls represented by $Z$, the synonymous residual coordinates retain predictive information for measured steady-state protein abundance in the joined rows.

\paragraph{Translation boundary.}
The same data do not establish a translation-mediated mechanism.  A translation readout $T_j$ is a mandatory boundary object before a $Q_i \to T_j \to P_k$ mediation statement can be made.  In this finite protein-omics run, the response $P$ is measured PAXdb abundance, not ribosome occupancy, not translation efficiency, and not a perturbative translation-rate assay.  The record explicitly states that the experiment is cross-sectional and does not use perturbation, rescue, ribosome profiling, or matched transcript controls.  Hence a positive $S^{QP}$ entry means only that the residual codon-topology coordinates improve prediction of protein abundance after the available baseline controls.  It does not decide whether the path is translational, transcriptional, degradation-related, organism-specific, or a mixture of unmeasured processes.

\paragraph{Comparison with the modeled translation contact.}
A separate modeled translation contact computed tAI weights from GtRNAdb tRNA gene-copy data and dos Reis wobble weights for \emph{E. coli} K-12, human, and yeast.  That contact loaded all standard amino-acid families: \emph{E. coli} had $89$ FASTA headers, $87$ matched headers, and $20$ standard amino-acid families; human had $432$ matched headers across $20$ families; yeast had $275$ matched headers across $20$ families.  Each organism had all $61$ sense codons represented and zero fallback counts for raw modeled weights.  However, its organism count was $3$, so it is retained only as bounded modeled readout evidence, not as a powered measured translation survival panel.  This separation is what prevents the present $S^{QP}$ protein-omics result from being described as a translation mechanism.

\paragraph{Local cannot-claim boundary.}
The supported finite statement is narrow.  The table supports direct residual codon-coordinate survival against matched measured protein abundance for the three organism-specific CDS-abundance matrices listed above.  It does not imply translation realization, ribosome dynamics, folding, localization, post-translational modification, protein stability, physical admissibility, molecular function, phenotype, causality, mechanism, or a universal biological law.  Any such assertion requires a separate layer-matched contact and a statistic computed at that layer.  In particular, a mediation claim requires same-row codon counts, measured translation readouts $T_j$, matched protein abundance $P_k$, matched mRNA controls, and an explicit fit showing that $Q_i \to T_j$ survives and that conditioning on $T_j$ removes or reduces the direct $Q_i \to P_k$ association.

\paragraph{Readback.}
The current readback is therefore: $B^{*}_{Q6}$ has a finite, rank-sufficient, organism-resolved direct protein-omics survival signal in the PAXdb abundance contact, with $R^2_{QP}$ equal to $0.10767526677985896$, $0.03209009170649934$, and $0.29837624994140827$ for the three joined organism tables.  The result is useful as DNA-to-protein-omics predictive irreducibility after the declared baseline controls.  It remains blocked from translation-mediated interpretation until a measured translation boundary is attached and the corresponding mediation matrix is computed.
