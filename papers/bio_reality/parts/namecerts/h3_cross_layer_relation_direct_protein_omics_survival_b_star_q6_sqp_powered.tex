\paragraph{Translation boundary.}
This chapter deliberately stops at direct $Q\to P$ prediction. The protein-abundance readout is cross-sectional abundance, not a translation-rate measurement. A positive $S^{QP}$ value is therefore not a claim that codon residuals act through ribosome speed, translation efficiency, or a measured mediator $T_j$. The linked modeled-tAI run used only $3$ organisms, namely \textit{E. coli} K12, \textit{H. sapiens}, and \textit{S. cerevisiae}; it computed modeled codon weights from GtRNAdb tRNA gene copy numbers and dos Reis wobble weights, with all $61$ sense codons assigned a nonzero raw-weight fallback count of $0$ in each organism. That run found $81$ modeled bounded-coupling entries above threshold $0.01$ and maximum absolute value $3801961.031607896$, but it was explicitly underpowered for survival because $3$ organisms are not enough for the requested powered panel. The direct protein-abundance result here must therefore remain separate from any mediated $Q\to T\to P$ statement until same-row measured translation readouts and matched mRNA controls are fitted in a mediation matrix.

\paragraph{Local interpretation.}
The accepted finite statement is narrow: in six organism-specific PAXdb/CDS joins, the nine $B^{*}_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates retain positive direct protein-abundance association after length, amino-acid composition, GC3, methionine density, and related controls. This is evidence for DNA-sequence residual coordinates carrying protein-omics predictive information beyond the listed baselines. It is not evidence for folding, stability, localization, post-translational modification, biochemical activity, phenotype, physical admissibility, causal mechanism, or a universal biological law. Those interpretations require separate layer-matched measurements and their own controlled statistics. In particular, a translation mechanism requires a measured $T_j$ column and an explicit test that conditioning on $T_j$ removes or reduces the direct $q_i\to P_k$ association after the same baseline controls.

\paragraph{Null and continuation condition.}
The null condition for this direct protein-omics chapter would have been $S^{QP}_{ik}\leq 0$ for all tested residual coordinates after the full baseline projection, or loss of residual rank through an overfit control design. The observed finite rows do not satisfy that null: every organism reports $9$ surviving coordinates and positive organism-level $R^2_{QP}$. The remaining open condition is not direct survival, but mediation. The next finite table must join the same CDS rows to measured translation-efficiency or ribosome-profiling columns, matched mRNA abundance, and protein abundance, then evaluate whether the path $q_i\to T_j\to P_k$ explains the direct $q_i\to P_k$ surface.
