\subsection{Direct protein-omics survival for $B^*_{Q6}$ residual codon coordinates}
\label{sec:direct-protein-omics-survival-b-star-q6-sqp-powered}

\paragraph{Finite row system.}
This record tests whether amino-acid-quotient synonymous codon coordinates retain predictive contact with measured protein abundance after the ordinary sequence-composition explanations have already been removed. The carrier is a finite CDS-by-organism table. Each row carries a real coding sequence, a measured protein-omics readout $P$, and nine $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinates:
$$
\begin{aligned}
Q = \{&K\_AAA,\ Arg\_AGR,\ Ile\_AUA,\ Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR,\ Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG,\\
&Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY,\ Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG,\ Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY,\ f3\_stress\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The measured readout is $\log_{10}(\mathrm{abundance\_ppm})$ from PAXdb protein abundance joined to real CDS codon counts. The control block $Z$ contains an intercept, $\log(\mathrm{CDS\ length})$, the twenty amino-acid composition proportions, GC3, and an $M$-density baseline defined on the union support of the nine projected coordinates. The fitted test is therefore not a raw codon-usage contrast. It is a residual question: after $Z$ has absorbed length, amino-acid composition, third-position GC, and broad coordinate-support density, does the residual $Q$ block still explain residual protein abundance?

\paragraph{Controlled score.}
For each organism $o$, the statistic is the partial protein-omics survival score
$$
\begin{aligned}
S^{QP}(o) = R^2\bigl(P_o - \Pi_{Z_o}P_o,\ (I-\Pi_{Z_o})Q_o\bigr),
\end{aligned}
$$
with positivity threshold $10^{-12}$ and degeneracy guard $R^2 < 0.999999$. The residualized $Q$ block has rank $9$ in every reported organism, while the control block has rank $23$ despite containing $24$ columns. Thus the reported positive entries are not artifacts of a collapsed coordinate matrix. The smallest residual degrees of freedom occur in \textit{Sulfolobus solfataricus}, with $1633$ residual degrees of freedom after $Z$; the largest occur in \textit{Mus musculus}, with $19372$ residual degrees of freedom. Every organism in the panel is therefore rank-powered for this direct $Q$ to $P$ calculation.

\paragraph{Translation boundary.}
The same finite memory contains a modeled translation-contact precursor: three organisms, $61$ sense codons, nine computed synonymous residual coordinates, and modeled tAI weights from tRNA gene copy and dos Reis wobble values. That three-organism run has $81$ bounded coupling entries above threshold $0.01$ and maximum absolute coupling $3801961.031607896$, but its organism count is only $3$. It is therefore explicitly not a powered survival panel. The protein-omics calculation in this section does not turn that modeled tAI contact into observed ribosome occupancy, translation efficiency, or a mediation chain. A positive $S^{QP}$ entry says that residual codon geometry predicts residual protein abundance; it does not say that a measured translation readout $T$ carries the signal.

\paragraph{Local boundary on interpretation.}
This chapter licenses only the following finite statement: in the reported PAXdb-CDS joins, the nine-coordinate $B^*_{Q6}$ residual block has positive direct survival against residualized measured protein abundance in every organism after length, amino-acid composition, GC3, and $M$-density controls. No translation mechanism follows from this. No folding claim follows from this. No physical admissibility, biochemical function, localization, post-translational state, phenotype, causal mechanism, or universal biological law follows from this. A stronger claim would require a separate same-row contact containing measured translation readouts $T$, matched mRNA controls, and an explicit mediation calculation showing whether conditioning on $T$ removes, reduces, or preserves the direct $Q$ to $P$ signal. Until such a matrix is attached, the correct reading is direct DNA-to-protein-omics predictive irreducibility under the stated finite controls, not translation-mediated realization.
