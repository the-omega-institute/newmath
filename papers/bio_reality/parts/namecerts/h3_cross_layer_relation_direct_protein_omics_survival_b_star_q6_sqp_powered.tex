\subsection{Direct protein-omics survival of the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates}
\label{sec:bioq6-direct-protein-omics-survival}

\paragraph{Finite carrier.}
The finite object in this chapter is a CDS-by-protein table in which each row carries real coding-sequence codon counts, a measured protein-abundance readout, and nine synonymous residual coordinates obtained after quotienting by amino-acid identity.  The coordinate list is
$K\_AAA$, $Arg\_AGR$, $Ile\_AUA$, $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$, $Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG$, $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$, $Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG$, $Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY$, and $f3\_stress$.  The protein readout is $\log_{10}(\mathrm{abundance\_ppm})$ from measured PAXdb protein abundance joined to real CDS codon counts.  For each organism, the residual design removes the baseline columns consisting of an intercept, CDS length, twenty amino-acid composition proportions, GC3, and the $M$-density baseline on the union support of the nine projected coordinates.  The recorded control design has $24$ control columns with rank $23$, while the residualized $Q$ block has rank $9$ in every organism listed below.

\paragraph{Statistic.}
For an organism $o$, let $P_o$ be the measured protein-abundance vector, $Z_o$ the baseline control matrix, and $Q_o$ the nine-column residual-coordinate matrix.  Write $\widetilde P_o$ and $\widetilde Q_o$ for the residuals after projection away from $Z_o$.  The direct protein-omics survival score is the incremental residual coefficient of determination
$$
\begin{aligned}
S^{QP}(o) &= R^2\bigl(\widetilde P_o \sim \widetilde Q_o\bigr),\\
\mathrm{survival}(o) &\Longleftrightarrow S^{QP}(o) > 10^{-12}\quad\text{and}\quad \operatorname{rank}(\widetilde Q_o)=9.
\end{aligned}
$$
This is a direct $Q\to P$ association statistic.  It is not a translation-rate statistic, a mediation statistic, or a causal perturbation statistic.

\paragraph{Relation to translation readouts.}
A separate modeled translation contact computed nine $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates on only $3$ organisms using modeled tAI weights from tRNA gene copy and dos Reis wobble weights.  That contact had $81$ entries above the bounded coupling threshold $0.01$ and maximum absolute score $3801961.031607896$, but it explicitly recorded that $3$ organisms are insufficient for powered survival.  The present protein-omics chapter is therefore stronger in row count for $P$ than the modeled tAI panel is for measured translation, but it still does not contain a matched measured mediator $T_j$.  A positive $S^{QP}$ value is consequently read as DNA-to-protein-omics predictive irreducibility, not as proof that the path is $q_i\to T_j\to P_k$.

\paragraph{Decision.}
The finite decision for this chapter is positive for direct protein-omics survival and negative for mechanistic promotion.  The direct statement is that the nine $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates explain nonzero residual variance in measured PAXdb protein abundance after the declared CDS-derived controls, with $R^2_{QP}$ ranging from $0.019256877907572044$ to $0.29837624994140827$ across $15$ organisms and with all nine coordinates retained in every organism-specific fit.  The local boundary is equally part of the result: no translation claim, folding claim, physical-admissibility claim, biochemical-function claim, causal mechanism, organism-level fitness claim, or universal law follows from this table.  Such a claim would require a separate layer-matched contact: measured translation readouts for $q_i\to T_j$, a joint mediation matrix for $q_i\to T_j\to P_k$, perturbation or rescue rows for causality, structure assays for folding, physical assays for admissibility, and functional or phenotype assays for biological action.
