\subsection{Direct protein-omics survival for $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual codon coordinates}
\label{sec:q6-direct-protein-omics-survival}

\paragraph{Finite carrier and readout.}
This record concerns a finite CDS-by-organism table in which each coding sequence is assigned synonymous residual coordinates from the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ amino-acid quotient and is then matched to a measured protein-omics abundance column.  The experiment uses nine residual coordinates,
$K\_AAA$, $Arg\_AGR$, $Ile\_AUA$, $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$, $Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG$, $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$, $Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG$, $Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY$, and $f3\_stress$.  These coordinates are not amino-acid composition coordinates: they are the remaining codon-topology directions after quotienting by amino-acid identity and then testing whether any residue of synonymous usage still predicts a downstream protein abundance readout.

The protein readout is $\log_{10}(\mathrm{abundance\_ppm})$ from measured PAXdb protein abundance joined to real CDS codon counts.  The finite joined panels are organism-specific.  For \emph{Escherichia coli} K-12 MG1655 the table contains $3739$ joined proteins with join hit rate $0.9978649586335735$.  For \emph{Mycobacterium smegmatis} str. MC2 155 it contains $4129$ joined proteins with join hit rate $1.0$.  For \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae} it contains $5815$ joined proteins with join hit rate $0.9156038419146592$.  Thus the result is not an organism-count survival claim over three rows; it is a per-CDS protein-abundance association computed inside three large organism-specific finite panels.

\paragraph{Controlled statistic.}
For each organism, the residual coordinate matrix $Q$ is tested against the residualized protein abundance response after controlling for the declared baseline covariates available in the finite run: CDS length, amino-acid composition, GC3, and methionine-density controls.  The statistic reported here is the direct protein-omics survival score $S^{QP}$, summarized by the partial explanatory strength of the nine residual coordinates after the control projection.  In schematic form, the computation is
$$
\begin{aligned}
  \widetilde Q &= (I-\Pi_Z)Q,\\
  \widetilde P &= (I-\Pi_Z)P,\\
  S^{QP} &> 0 \quad \text{when the fitted } \widetilde Q \to \widetilde P \text{ relation has positive controlled partial } R^2.
\end{aligned}
$$
The positivity threshold used by the run is $10^{-12}$ for partial $R^2$.  The control design is not rank-degenerate in any of the three organism panels: each panel has $24$ control columns, $\operatorname{rank}(Z)=23$, and $\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde Q)=9$, equal to the number of tested $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ coordinates.  The residual degrees of freedom after the control projection are $3716$ for \emph{E. coli}, $4106$ for \emph{M. smegmatis}, and $5792$ for yeast.  These values separate the present protein-omics test from the earlier three-organism modeled-tAI surface, where $n=3$ was explicitly insufficient for powered survival and rank saturation could make controlled matrices biologically uninformative.

\paragraph{Finite-row outcome.}
All three organism-specific panels show positive direct protein-omics survival for the nine-coordinate $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ block.  The controlled protein-abundance partial strengths are
$$
\begin{aligned}
  R^2_{QP}(\textit{E. coli K-12 MG1655}) &= 0.10767526677985896,\\
  R^2_{QP}(\textit{M. smegmatis str. MC2 155}) &= 0.03209009170649934,\\
  R^2_{QP}(\textit{S. cerevisiae}) &= 0.29837624994140827.
\end{aligned}
$$
In each case the surviving coordinate count is $9$, so the finite run reports survival for the full residual coordinate block rather than for a single isolated codon contrast.  The residualized response energies are also finite and nonzero: $4020.333671802061$ for \emph{E. coli}, $5729.194538214237$ for \emph{M. smegmatis}, and $2958.1865890925337$ for yeast.  The explained energies attributed to the controlled $Q$ block are $432.89050065533684$, $183.85037813566998$, and $882.6526210803958$, respectively.  The largest apparent protein-omics association in this record is therefore the yeast panel, followed by \emph{E. coli}, then \emph{M. smegmatis}; this ordering is an empirical ordering of this finite contact, not a universal biological ranking.

\paragraph{Translation boundary.}
The result is a direct $q_i \to P_k$ protein-omics association after baseline controls.  It is not a measured translation mechanism.  The run itself records that measured PAXdb abundance is a cross-sectional protein-omics readout rather than a translation-rate measurement, and that the experiment does not use perturbation, rescue, ribosome profiling, or matched transcript controls.  Consequently the positive $S^{QP}$ entries cannot be promoted to a mediation surface $M^{QTP}$.  A translation-mediated interpretation would require a joined table containing the same CDS identifiers, the same $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates, a measured translation readout $T_j$, matched protein abundance $P_k$, and matched mRNA controls, followed by an explicit test of whether conditioning on $T_j$ removes or reduces the direct $q_i \to P_k$ association.

The available modeled-tAI contact remains useful only as a bounded translation-layer readout.  In that separate finite contact, three organisms were available, all standard amino-acid tRNA families were present in the local GtRNAdb payload, modeled dos Reis wobble weights produced nonzero codon weights for all $61$ sense codons, and $81$ modeled coupling entries exceeded threshold $0.01$ with maximum absolute score $3801961.031607896$.  However, that contact also records that $3$ organisms are insufficient for powered survival and that modeled tAI is not ribosome profiling or proteomics validation.  It therefore supplies context for why the protein-omics boundary must remain explicit, not a proof of translation mediation.

\paragraph{Local claim boundary.}
The finite evidence supports this restricted statement: in three organism-specific CDS-protein panels, the nine $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates retain positive direct association with measured PAXdb protein abundance after the available baseline controls, with full residual coordinate rank and large residual degrees of freedom.  Nothing stronger follows from this table.  In particular, the result does not establish translation realization, ribosome occupancy, causal mechanism, protein folding, physical admissibility, localization, post-translational state, pathway function, organismal phenotype, or a universal biological law.  Each of those layers would need its own matched measurement contact and its own controlled statistic.  The present chapter therefore records a direct codon-residual to protein-omics survival contact and keeps translation-mediated, structural, functional, mechanistic, and universal readings outside the claim.
