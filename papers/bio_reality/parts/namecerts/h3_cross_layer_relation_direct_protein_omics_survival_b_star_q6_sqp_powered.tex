\subsection{Direct protein-omics survival of B$^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinates}
\label{sec:bior reality-q6-direct-protein-omics-survival}

\paragraph{Finite record.}
The finite carrier is a CDS-by-organism table in which each coding sequence has codon-count coordinates, a residual B$^*_{Q6}$ coordinate vector after the amino-acid quotient, a measured protein-abundance readout, and the baseline covariates needed to prevent amino-acid composition or simple coding-sequence scale from being mistaken for a synonymous signal.  The tested residual basis has nine coordinates:
$$
\begin{aligned}
Q=\{&\texttt{K\_AAA},\texttt{Arg\_AGR},\texttt{Ile\_AUA},\texttt{Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR},\texttt{Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG},\\
&\texttt{Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY},\texttt{Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG},\texttt{Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY},\texttt{f3\_stress}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The readout is $\log_{10}(\mathrm{abundance\_ppm})$ from measured PAXdb protein abundance joined to real CDS codon counts.  The fitted quantity is a direct $S^{QP}$ protein-omics survival score: it asks whether the residualized B$^*_{Q6}$ coordinates add predictive energy for protein abundance after the declared controls have already been removed.  It is not a translation-rate measurement and it is not a mediation matrix.

\paragraph{Controls and rank.}
For every organism the control block contains 24 columns: an intercept, $\log(\mathrm{CDS\ length})$, the 20 amino-acid composition proportions, GC3, and an M-density baseline defined on the union support of the nine projected B$^*_{Q6}$ vectors.  The control rank is $23$ in every organism, while the residualized B$^*_{Q6}$ block has rank $9$ in every organism.  The rank check is therefore nondegenerate for this direct protein-omics test.  The residual degrees of freedom after the control projection range from $1633$ in \textit{Sulfolobus solfataricus} to $19372$ in \textit{Mus musculus}; representative values include $3716$ for \textit{Escherichia coli} K-12 MG1655, $5792$ for \textit{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}, $19030$ for \textit{Homo sapiens}, and $4019$ for \textit{Bacillus subtilis} subsp. subtilis str. 168.  The positivity threshold used for partial $R^2$ survival is $10^{-12}$, and the degeneracy threshold for explained variance is $0.999999$.

\paragraph{Translation boundary.}
The protein-omics result is deliberately lower than a mechanism claim.  A separate modeled translation-readout record exists for three organisms using GtRNAdb tRNA gene copy and dos Reis wobble weights; it computed 81 entries above the bounded-coupling threshold $0.01$ and reported maximum absolute coupling $3801961.031607896$, but it also recorded that three organisms are insufficient for powered survival.  That modeled tAI contact is useful as a bounded translation-layer comparison, not as observed ribosome occupancy.  In the present protein-omics table, conditioning on measured translation readouts cannot be evaluated because the same-row ribosome or translation-efficiency mediator $T_j$ is absent.  Consequently the positive $S^{QP}$ entries mean direct DNA-to-protein-omics predictive irreducibility after CDS-derived controls; they do not establish $q_i\to T_j\to P_k$ mediation.

\paragraph{mRNA comparison.}
The nearby mRNA stability contacts sharpen the boundary rather than replacing it.  In yeast, the same nine-coordinate style gives $R^2_{QM}=0.05179429044975085$ for log half-life and $R^2_{QP}=0.2761140373962526$ on the same join, with cosine $0.8040935468384264$ and permutation $p=0.000999000999000999$; the dominant mRNA coordinate is \texttt{Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR}.  In \textit{E. coli}, $R^2_{QM}=0.02298556640056076$, same-join $R^2_{QP}=0.11632311854858134$, cosine $-0.7784923375569599$, permutation $p=0.000999000999000999$, and dominant coordinate \texttt{Arg\_AGR}.  In human, $R^2_{QM}=0.010503670362051522$, same-join $R^2_{QP}=0.04095215557813418$, cosine $0.6351183883924848$, permutation $p=0.000999000999000999$, and dominant mRNA coordinate \texttt{Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR}.  These comparisons show that mRNA and protein abundance can carry related but organism-dependent residual projections; they do not turn the protein-abundance association into a translation mechanism.

\paragraph{Local boundary.}
The claim supported here is restricted to a finite direct association: B$^*_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates survive as predictors of measured protein abundance after length, amino-acid composition, GC3, and M-density controls in the listed organism panels.  Nothing in this chapter implies translation realization, ribosome kinetics, folding, physical admissibility, localization, post-translational state, biochemical function, phenotype, causality, or universality.  Each of those statements would need its own matched contact and its own controlled statistic.  In particular, a translation interpretation requires same-CDS rows carrying a measured or explicitly bounded $T_j$ readout and a mediation fit showing that conditioning on $T_j$ removes or reduces the direct $q_i\to P_k$ signal.  Until that matrix is present, the correct reading is direct protein-omics survival under controls, with the translation boundary left open.
