\subsection{Direct protein-omics survival for $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates}
\label{sec:biorality-q6-direct-protein-omics-survival}

\paragraph{Finite row object.}
This record tests whether amino-acid-quotient synonymous coordinates in $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ retain measurable information about protein abundance after standard sequence-composition controls have been removed.  The finite carrier is a CDS-by-organism data table whose rows are coding sequences joined to measured protein abundance, and whose coordinate block contains nine residual coordinates:
$K\_AAA$, $Arg\_AGR$, $Ile\_AUA$, $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$, $Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG$, $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$, $Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG$, $Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY$, and $f3\_stress$.
The protein readout is $\log_{10}(\mathrm{abundance\_ppm})$ from measured PAXdb abundance joined to real CDS codon counts.  The reported statistic is not a translation-rate statistic.  It is a direct $q_i \to P_k$ protein-omics association after residualizing against baseline covariates, and it is therefore read only as a bounded downstream omics contact.

\paragraph{Controls and residual ranks.}
For each organism, the response and the nine $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ coordinates were residualized against a control block containing length, amino-acid composition, GC3, and M-density terms.  In the three organism-specific tables the control block had $24$ columns and rank $23$.  The residualized $Q$ block retained rank $9$ in every organism, so the nine tested coordinates were not collapsed by the baseline projection.  The Escherichia coli K-12 MG1655 table used $3739$ joined proteins with join hit rate $0.9978649586335735$, residual degrees of freedom $3716$, explained control energy $432.89050065533684$, and response residual energy $4020.333671802061$.  The Mycobacterium smegmatis str. MC2 155 table used $4129$ joined proteins with join hit rate $1.0$, residual degrees of freedom $4106$, explained control energy $183.85037813566998$, and response residual energy $5729.194538214237$.  The Saccharomyces cerevisiae table used $5815$ joined proteins with join hit rate $0.9156038419146592$, residual degrees of freedom $5792$, explained control energy $882.6526210803958$, and response residual energy $2958.1865890925337$.

\paragraph{Protein-omics survival statistic.}
Let $Q$ denote the nine-column residual codon-topology block, $P$ the measured protein-abundance readout, and $Z$ the baseline control block.  The fitted quantity is the additional protein-abundance variance explained by $Q$ after projection away from $Z$:
$$
\begin{aligned}
S^{QP} &> 0
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
R^2(P \mid Z,Q) - R^2(P \mid Z) > 10^{-12}.
\end{aligned}
$$
Under this finite-row criterion all three organism tables show positive direct protein-omics survival.  Escherichia coli K-12 MG1655 has $R^2_{QP}=0.10767526677985896$ with $9$ surviving coordinates.  Mycobacterium smegmatis str. MC2 155 has $R^2_{QP}=0.03209009170649934$ with $9$ surviving coordinates.  Saccharomyces cerevisiae has $R^2_{QP}=0.29837624994140827$ with $9$ surviving coordinates.  The threshold for a positive partial-$R^2$ entry was $10^{-12}$, and the degeneracy guard used for the residual fits was $R^2=0.999999$.  These numbers support a direct finite-row relation between the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual block and measured protein abundance under the stated baseline controls.

\paragraph{Translation boundary.}
The same coordinate family also has a modeled translation-readout contact through tAI weights computed from GtRNAdb tRNA gene-copy data and dos Reis wobble weights.  That modeled contact covered $61$ sense codons in each of Escherichia coli K-12, Homo sapiens, and Saccharomyces cerevisiae, with zero raw-weight fallback count $0$ in each case.  The local tRNA payload contained all $20$ standard amino-acid families for each of the three organisms: Escherichia coli K-12 had $89$ FASTA headers, $87$ matched headers, and $2$ unmatched headers; Homo sapiens had $432$ headers and $432$ matched headers; Saccharomyces cerevisiae had $275$ headers and $275$ matched headers.  The modeled tAI coupling table had $81$ entries above threshold $0.01$, with maximum absolute value $3801961.031607896$.  This modeled translation contact is useful as a bounded readout, but its organism count is only $3$, so it is not a powered measured translation-survival study.  The direct protein-omics calculation reported here also did not include matched ribosome profiling, perturbation, rescue, or matched transcript controls.  Therefore the observed $S^{QP}$ values cannot be promoted to a $q_i \to T_j \to P_k$ mediation matrix.

\paragraph{Local interpretation.}
The finite result is that the nine $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ amino-acid-quotient residual coordinates retain direct predictive signal for measured protein abundance in three organism-specific CDS-protein tables after the stated composition controls.  The strongest reported direct association is in Saccharomyces cerevisiae, where $R^2_{QP}=0.29837624994140827$, followed by Escherichia coli K-12 MG1655 with $R^2_{QP}=0.10767526677985896$, and Mycobacterium smegmatis str. MC2 155 with $R^2_{QP}=0.03209009170649934$.  Since all three residualized $Q$ blocks have rank $9$ and all three tables have thousands of joined proteins, the positive entries are not an artifact of the three-organism rank saturation problem that affects organism-level survival summaries.

\paragraph{Boundary of the claim.}
This chapter makes no claim that the direct $q_i \to P_k$ association is a translation mechanism.  Measured protein abundance is a cross-sectional protein-omics readout, not a ribosome-rate measurement.  A separate matched translation contact would be required to show a surviving $q_i \to T_j$ step, and a joint mediation fit would be required to decide whether conditioning on $T_j$ removes, reduces, or leaves the $q_i \to P_k$ signal.  Likewise, no statement about folding, localization, post-translational modification, protein stability, biochemical function, physical admissibility, phenotype, causality, or universality follows from these $S^{QP}$ values.  Those stronger readings require their own layer-matched measurements and cannot be inferred from codon residual geometry or bulk protein abundance alone.
