\subsection{Direct protein-omics survival of $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates}
\label{sec:bioreality-q6-direct-protein-omics-survival}

\paragraph{Finite matrix.}
The tested object is a finite coding-sequence matrix whose rows are CDS entries matched to measured protein-abundance records and whose columns contain nine $B^{*}_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates.  The coordinate list is
$K\_AAA$, $Arg\_AGR$, $Ile\_AUA$, $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$, $Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG$, $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$, $Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG$, $Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY$, and $f3\_stress$.
The response column is $\log_{10}(\mathrm{abundance\_ppm})$ from measured PAXdb protein abundance joined to real CDS codon counts.  The baseline design contains an intercept, $\log(\mathrm{CDS\ length})$, the twenty amino-acid composition proportions, GC3, and the $M$-density baseline given by the fraction of sense codons on the union support of the nine projected coordinates.  In every organism reported below the residualized coordinate rank is $\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde X)=9$, with $\operatorname{rank}(Z)=23$ and twenty-four control columns before rank reduction.

\paragraph{Definition of the reported score.}
For an organism $o$, let $P_o$ denote the residual protein-abundance vector after projecting out the baseline controls $Z_o$, and let $Q_o$ denote the nine-column $B^{*}_{Q6}$ coordinate matrix after the same projection.  The direct protein-omics survival number is the partial coefficient of determination of $P_o$ on $Q_o$ after those controls:
$$
\begin{aligned}
S^{QP}(o) &= R^2\bigl(P_o \sim Q_o \mid Z_o\bigr),\\
Z_o &= \{1,\log(\mathrm{CDS\ length}),\mathrm{AA\ composition},\mathrm{GC3},M\mathrm{-density}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
A strictly positive entry is read only as residual predictive contact between synonymous codon geometry and the protein-abundance readout.  It is not a translation-rate measurement, not a causal effect, and not a mediation statement of the form $q_i\to T_j\to P_k$.

\paragraph{Rank and residual-energy checks.}
The design is not reported from a saturated three-organism fit.  Each organism has $\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde X)=9$, $\operatorname{rank}(Z)=23$, and positive residual degrees of freedom after the control projection.  Representative residual degrees of freedom after $Z$ are $3716$ in \emph{E. coli}, $5792$ in yeast, $19030$ in human, and $19372$ in mouse.  The residual response energies remain nonzero: $4020.333671802061$ in \emph{E. coli}, $2958.1865890925337$ in yeast, $30769.5367150478$ in human, and $48806.47856410273$ in mouse.  The positive entries therefore come from finite residualized regressions rather than from an all-positive saturated surface.

\paragraph{Translation boundary.}
A neighboring translation contact based on modeled tAI was computed only for three organisms and therefore does not by itself supply powered translation survival.  That finite modeled-tAI contact used all sixty-one sense codons, recovered twenty standard amino-acid tRNA families in the local payloads for \emph{E. coli}, human, and yeast, and produced $81$ modeled entries above the bounded coupling threshold $0.01$, with maximum absolute $S_{ij}$ equal to $3801961.031607896$.  Because the organism count there is $3$, it remains a bounded modeled adaptation readout rather than a measured ribosome or translation-efficiency validation.  Consequently the direct protein-omics score above must be read as $Q\to P$ residual predictivity, not as evidence that a translation mediator $T$ has carried the signal.

\paragraph{Local verdict.}
The direct protein-omics claim is supported at the level of residualized finite-row association: measured PAXdb abundance joined to real CDS codon counts remains predictable from the nine $B^{*}_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates after length, amino-acid composition, GC3, and $M$-density controls.  The local result is therefore a direct omics-survival statement for $S^{QP}$.
It does not establish translation realization, ribosome dynamics, folding, physical admissibility, biochemical function, post-translational state, mechanism, organismal phenotype, causality, adaptation, or universality.  Any such stronger statement would require its own matched biological readout and its own controlled statistic.  In particular, a translation-mediated interpretation requires same-row measured $T_j$ data and a mediation surface showing that the $q_i\to T_j$ step survives and that conditioning on $T_j$ removes or materially reduces the $q_i\to P_k$ association.
