\subsection{Direct protein-omics survival of $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates}
\label{subsec:bq6-direct-protein-omics-survival}

\paragraph{Finite object.}
This record concerns a finite CDS-by-organism table in which each protein-coding entry carries nine $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates after the amino-acid quotient, a measured protein-omics column, and the baseline covariates used to keep the synonymous signal separate from simpler sequence composition effects.  The nine residual coordinates are
$K\_AAA$, $Arg\_AGR$, $Ile\_AUA$, $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$, $Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG$, $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$, $Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG$, $Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY$, and $f3\_stress$.  The protein readout is $\log_{10}(\mathrm{abundance\_ppm})$ from measured PAXdb protein abundance joined to real CDS codon counts.  For each organism the $Q$ block has rank $9$ after projection away from the control space, while the control design has $24$ columns and rank $23$.  The controls include amino-acid composition, CDS length, GC3, and M-density terms, so the tested quantity is not raw codon usage but residual codon-topology information left after the main compositional alternatives have been removed.

\paragraph{Residual statistic.}
For an organism $o$, let $X_o$ be the matrix of the nine $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ coordinates, let $Z_o$ be the baseline control matrix, and let $y_o$ be the measured protein-abundance vector.  The direct protein-omics survival score is the additional residual variance explained by $X_o$ after both $X_o$ and $y_o$ are residualized against $Z_o$:
$$
\begin{aligned}
S^{QP}(o) &= R^2\bigl(\Pi_{Z_o}^{\perp} y_o \sim \Pi_{Z_o}^{\perp} X_o\bigr),\\
\operatorname{rank}(\Pi_{Z_o}^{\perp} X_o) &= 9.
\end{aligned}
$$
The positivity threshold recorded for per-coordinate survival is $10^{-12}$, and every organism in the finite panel is rank-sufficient under the reported residual design.  The statistic is therefore a direct $Q \to P$ protein-omics association after baseline controls.  It is not a translation-rate measurement, not a causal perturbation result, and not a mediation matrix.

\paragraph{Translation boundary.}
A separate translation-contact memory exists for a three-organism modeled-tAI calculation, where $61$ sense codons were assigned dos Reis wobble-weighted modeled tAI values in $Escherichia\ coli$ K12, $Homo\ sapiens$, and $Saccharomyces\ cerevisiae$.  That smaller calculation reports $81$ bounded-coupling entries above threshold $0.01$ and a maximum absolute modeled coupling of $3801961.031607896$, but it is explicitly underpowered for survival because the organism count is $3$, below the stated $40$-species requirement for powered survival.  Consequently the protein-omics result in this subsection is not allowed to borrow a translation interpretation from the modeled-tAI contact.  A measured translation readout $T_j$, such as ribosome occupancy or translation efficiency with matched mRNA control, must be joined on the same rows before any $q_i \to T_j \to P_k$ mediation statement can be made.

\paragraph{Local interpretation.}
The finite conclusion is that $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates carry direct protein-abundance predictive information in the reported PAXdb-CDS joins after the declared baseline controls.  The permissible reading is DNA-sequence residual information reaching a measured protein-omics column as a controlled association.  The impermissible readings are equally important: this does not establish translation-rate mediation, ribosome mechanism, folded structure, physical admissibility, molecular function, localization, post-translational state, organismal phenotype, or a universal biological rule.  Those claims require layer-matched measurements and their own controlled statistics.  In particular, a later mediation test must compare $S^{QT}$, $S^{QP}$, and a controlled $M^{QTP}$ surface on matched codon, translation, mRNA, and protein rows, and must report whether conditioning on measured $T_j$ removes, reduces, or leaves the direct $q_i \to P_k$ association.
