\subsection{Direct protein-omics survival of $B^*_{Q6}$ synonymous residuals}
\label{sec:bq6-direct-protein-omics-survival}

\paragraph{Finite row object.}
The finite object tested here is a CDS-by-organism table whose rows join real coding-sequence codon counts to measured PAXdb protein abundance.  The synonymous coordinates are the nine $B^*_{Q6}$ residual directions
$K\_AAA$, $Arg\_AGR$, $Ile\_AUA$, $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$, $Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG$, $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$, $Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG$, $Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY$, and $f3\_stress$.  Each coordinate is read after the amino-acid quotient, so the tested signal is not ordinary amino-acid composition.  For each organism the protein readout is $\log_{10}(\mathrm{abundance\_ppm})$, and the baseline covariates are an intercept, CDS length, the twenty amino-acid composition proportions, GC3, and an $M$-density baseline on the union support of the nine projected residual vectors.  The control design has $24$ columns and rank $23$ in every organism, while the residualized coordinate block has rank $9$ in every organism.  The positive-entry threshold for a coordinate to count as surviving is $10^{-12}$ in partial $R^2$.

\paragraph{Controlled statistic.}
For an organism $o$, let $P_o$ be the residualized protein-abundance vector after the baseline controls, and let $Q_o$ be the residualized $9$-column $B^*_{Q6}$ coordinate matrix after the same controls.  The reported survival score is the finite least-squares gain
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{QP}(o)
  &= 1 - \frac{\lVert P_o - \Pi_{Q_o}P_o\rVert^2}{\lVert P_o\rVert^2},\\
S^{QP}_{ik}(o) &> 0
\quad\text{only when the corresponding partial contribution is above }10^{-12}.
\end{aligned}
$$
This statistic is a direct codon-residual to protein-omics association after sequence-derived controls.  It is not a translation-rate measurement, not a causal estimate, and not a mediation matrix.

These values certify that the displayed $R^2_{QP}$ entries are controlled finite-row fits rather than labels attached to an unranked table.

\paragraph{Translation boundary.}
The same coordinate family previously had a bounded modeled-tAI contact over only three organisms, with $81$ modeled coupling entries above threshold $0.01$ and maximum absolute modeled score $3801961.031607896$.  That contact is explicitly not powered survival: $3$ organisms are insufficient for a rank-powered translation-survival claim.  The present protein-omics table is different in kind.  It has many per-protein rows per organism and it supports direct $Q\to P$ survival after sequence-derived controls, but it still lacks a same-row measured translation readout $T_j$ and a fitted $Q\to T\to P$ mediation surface.  Therefore the result is a direct protein-omics irreducibility record, not a translation mechanism.

\paragraph{Comparison with mRNA half-life contacts.}
Three mRNA-stability contacts show that the same coordinate family also has transcription-layer associations, but those contacts do not convert the protein-omics statistic into a translation claim.  In yeast, the half-life fit has $n=5085$, $R^2_{QM}=0.0517942904$, $R^2_{QP}=0.2761140374$ on the same join, cosine $0.8040935468$, dominant $Q\to M$ coordinate $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$, and permutation value $0.000999000999000999$ from $1000$ permutations.  In \emph{E. coli}, the half-life fit has $n=2720$, $R^2_{QM}=0.0229855664$, same-join $R^2_{QP}=0.1163231185$, cosine $-0.7784923376$, dominant coordinate $Arg\_AGR$, and the same finite permutation value.  In human, the consensus relative half-life fit has $n=16165$, $R^2_{QM}=0.0105036704$, same-join $R^2_{QP}=0.0409521556$, cosine $0.6351183884$, dominant coordinate $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$, and uses $54$ consensus dataset columns.  These contacts demonstrate that the residual coordinates can touch mRNA and protein readouts with different signs and magnitudes; they do not by themselves identify a ribosome-mediated path.

\paragraph{Local cannot-claim boundary.}
The chapter supports only the following finite statement: after length, amino-acid composition, GC3, and $M$-density controls, the nine $B^*_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates have positive direct association with measured protein abundance in each of the fifteen joined organism tables.  No translation realization follows from this chapter unless a separate measured translation contact supplies same-CDS $T_j$ rows and the mediation fit shows $Q_i\to T_j\to P_k$.  No folding, physical admissibility, localization, post-translational state, molecular function, phenotype, causal mechanism, or universal biological law follows from $S^{QP}$ alone.  Bulk protein abundance is a downstream omics readout, and a positive direct residual-to-protein association must remain at that level until a layer-matched contact and statistic are added.
