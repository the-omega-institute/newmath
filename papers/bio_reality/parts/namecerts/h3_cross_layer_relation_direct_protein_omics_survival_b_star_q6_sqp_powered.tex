\subsection{Direct protein-omics survival for $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinates}
\label{sec:biorality-q6-direct-protein-omics-survival}

\paragraph{Finite carrier.}
This record uses a finite CDS-by-organism table in which each protein-coding row carries three separated kinds of columns.  The first block is the $B^*_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinate block after the amino-acid quotient.  The computed coordinate list has nine entries,
$$
\begin{aligned}
q \in \{&K\_AAA,\ Arg\_AGR,\ Ile\_AUA,\ Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR,\ Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG,\\
&Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY,\ Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG,\ Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY,\ f3\_stress\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The second block is a required translation boundary block: a measured or explicitly bounded translation readout $T_j$ is needed before any statement about translation mediation is permitted.  The third block is the protein-omics readout block.  In the present finite run the protein readout is $\log_{10}(\mathrm{abundance\_ppm})$ from measured PAXdb protein abundance joined to real CDS codon counts.  The reported statistic is therefore a direct $q_i$ to $P_k$ protein-omics survival surface, denoted here by $S^{QP}$, not a translation-mediated matrix.

\paragraph{Control geometry.}
For each organism, the response was residualized against the declared CDS-derived controls: an intercept, $\log(\mathrm{CDS\ length})$, twenty amino-acid composition proportions, GC3, and the $M$-density baseline defined by the union support of the nine projected $B^*_{Q6}$ vectors.  This gives $24$ control columns with rank $23$ in every organism-level fit.  After these controls, the residualized $q$-block keeps full coordinate rank, $\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde X)=9$, in all fifteen organism panels.  The positive partial-$R^2$ threshold is $10^{-12}$, and the degeneracy guard is $R^2<0.999999$.

\paragraph{Translation boundary.}
The protein-abundance run is deliberately interpreted below translation mechanism.  Measured PAXdb abundance is a steady-state protein-omics readout; it is not a ribosome-rate column, not a translation-efficiency measurement, and not a mediator.  A positive $S^{QP}$ entry therefore means that the residual codon-topology coordinate block predicts protein abundance after the stated sequence controls.  It does not mean that $q_i$ changes translation, and it does not identify a path $q_i \to T_j \to P_k$.  To make that stronger statement one would need same-row codon counts, measured translation readouts such as ribosome profiling or translation efficiency, matched mRNA abundance, matched protein abundance, and a mediation fit showing that conditioning on $T_j$ removes or reduces the direct $q_i \to P_k$ signal.

\paragraph{Relation to bounded translation readouts.}
A separate modeled translation contact used three organisms and dos Reis wobble-weighted tAI values from tRNA gene-copy data.  That run found $81$ entries above a $0.01$ bounded-coupling threshold and a maximum absolute modeled coupling of $3801961.031607896$, but it explicitly had only $3$ organisms.  Consequently it is a modeled readout contact, not a powered survival claim.  The present protein-omics run has far larger per-CDS panels, yet it still lacks the same-row measured $T_j$ mediator required for translation-mechanism language.  These two finite facts are compatible: modeled tAI can show a bounded codon-adaptation surface, and PAXdb abundance can show direct protein-omics survival, while neither alone supplies a measured translation-mediation chain.

\paragraph{Local verdict.}
The finite-row verdict is positive for direct protein-omics survival and negative for mechanism promotion.  The chapter records a cross-layer association from synonymous residual coordinates to measured protein abundance after standard sequence controls, with all nine residual coordinates surviving in all fifteen organism panels.  It does not claim translation realization, causal mediation, folding, physical admissibility, localization, post-translational state, biochemical function, phenotype, adaptation, or universality.  Any such statement requires a separate layer-matched contact and a statistic computed on the corresponding rows.  In particular, a future translation-mediated claim must report the measured $T_j$ column and must state whether conditioning on $T_j$ removes, reduces, or leaves the $q_i$ to $P_k$ signal.
