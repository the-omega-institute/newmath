\subsection{Direct protein-omics survival of $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates}\label{sec:biorality-q6-direct-protein-omics-survival}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite carrier.}
This record uses a finite CDS-by-organism table whose rows are coding sequences with measured protein abundance and whose columns include the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates computed after amino-acid quotienting.  The coordinate block has nine columns,
$K\_AAA$, $Arg\_AGR$, $Ile\_AUA$, $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$, $Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG$, $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$, $Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG$, $Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY$, and $f3\_stress$.  The protein-omics readout is $\log_{10}(\mathrm{abundance\_ppm})$ from measured PAXdb protein abundance joined to real CDS codon counts.  The fitted protein-abundance residual is controlled for CDS length, amino-acid composition, GC3, and M-density; the experiment explicitly does not contain perturbation, rescue, ribosome profiling, or matched transcript-control rows.  Thus the statistic below is a direct $q_i \to P_k$ association statistic, not a translation-rate, mediation, or causal statistic.

In every organism the residualized $Q$ block has rank $9$ after the control block is removed.  The control rank is $23$ against $24$ supplied control columns, and the residual degrees of freedom remain large: $3716$ in $\textit{E. coli}$, $5792$ in yeast, $19030$ in human, $19372$ in mouse, and $14387$ in zebrafish, with the remaining organisms ranging from $4019$ to $13272$.

\paragraph{Statistic.}
For each organism, the fitted quantity is the partial protein-omics survival score of the residual coordinate block after the control projection.  In notation, if $P$ is the protein-abundance vector, $Q$ is the nine-column synonymous residual block, and $Z$ is the baseline control matrix, the reported finite-row statistic is the residual partial coefficient of determination
$$
\begin{aligned}
S^{QP} &= R^2\bigl((I-\Pi_Z)P,\,(I-\Pi_Z)Q\bigr),\\
\mathrm{pass} &\Longleftrightarrow S^{QP} > 10^{-12}\ \text{and}\ \operatorname{rank}((I-\Pi_Z)Q)=9.
\end{aligned}
$$
The threshold $10^{-12}$ is only a positivity threshold for the direct protein-abundance association.  It is not a biological mechanism threshold and it is not a claim that codon usage determines protein output in a universal way.

\paragraph{Observed survival.}
All eleven organism-level protein-omics fits are positive under the stated controls, and all nine $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ coordinates survive in each organism.  The strongest observed protein-abundance survival is in $\textit{S. cerevisiae}$, where $R^2_{QP}=0.29837624994140827$ with $9$ surviving coordinates.  The next largest values are $R^2_{QP}=0.10767526677985896$ for $\textit{E. coli}$ K12 MG1655 and $R^2_{QP}=0.10217906758013379$ for $\textit{C. elegans}$.  The other positive entries are $0.0770952887136557$ for $\textit{D. melanogaster}$, $0.0646471273126996$ for $\textit{P. aeruginosa}$ PAO1, $0.061906245886660105$ for $\textit{B. subtilis}$ 168, $0.049827984062539195$ for $\textit{M. musculus}$, $0.04613529807085213$ for $\textit{D. rerio}$, $0.04454565118019448$ for $\textit{H. sapiens}$, $0.03980793596647631$ for $\textit{G. gallus}$, and $0.03209009170649934$ for $\textit{M. smegmatis}$ MC2 155.  The smallest positive value is still many orders of magnitude above the declared positivity threshold, and no organism has rank loss in the residualized $Q$ block.

\paragraph{Relation to the translation boundary.}
The companion modeled-tAI contact supplies only bounded codon-adaptation readout.  It used three organisms, standard tRNA families were present in the local GtRNAdb payloads, and $81$ modeled coupling entries exceeded the contact threshold $0.01$, with maximum absolute modeled value $3801961.031607896$.  That contact was explicitly underpowered for survival because $3$ organisms are insufficient for the requested powered panel.  The present protein-omics fit is different: it uses per-CDS protein-abundance rows and has adequate residual rank inside each organism, but it still does not contain a measured translation readout $T_j$.  Therefore a positive $S^{QP}$ is read only as DNA-to-protein-omics predictive irreducibility after the named controls.  It does not establish $q_i \to T_j$, and it does not establish a mediation chain $q_i \to T_j \to P_k$.

\paragraph{Local boundary.}
The record supports the narrow statement that $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates retain direct explanatory power for measured steady-state protein abundance in the finite PAXdb-joined CDS tables after length, amino-acid composition, GC3, and M-density controls.  It does not support translation realization, ribosome dynamics, protein folding, physical admissibility, protein function, localization, post-translational modification, phenotype, mechanism, causality, or universality.  Any such promotion requires a separate matched contact at that layer: ribosome profiling or translation-efficiency rows for translation, matched mRNA rows for transcript control, explicit mediation fits for $q_i \to T_j \to P_k$, and independent structural, physical, functional, perturbational, localization, or phenotype readouts for higher biological interpretations.
