\subsection{Direct protein-omics survival of $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates}
\label{subsec:biorality-q6-direct-protein-omics-survival}

\paragraph{Finite row carrier.}
This record uses a finite CDS-by-organism table in which each coding sequence carries synonymous residual coordinates from the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ amino-acid-quotient basis and a matched protein-abundance readout.  The coordinate side has nine residual columns,
$$
\begin{aligned}
Q=\{&K\_AAA,\ Arg\_AGR,\ Ile\_AUA,\ Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR,\ Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG,\\
&Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY,\ Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG,\ Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY,\ f3\_stress\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The readout is $\log_{10}(\mathrm{abundance\_ppm})$ from measured PAXdb protein abundance joined to real CDS codon counts.  The joined finite rows are organism-specific: Escherichia coli K12 MG1655 contributes $3739$ proteins with join hit rate $0.9978649586335735$, Mycobacterium smegmatis str. MC2 155 contributes $4129$ proteins with join hit rate $1.0$, and Saccharomyces cerevisiae contributes $5815$ proteins with join hit rate $0.9156038419146592$.  These are not abstract species-level summaries; the score is computed on per-protein rows after the coordinate table, baseline covariates, and protein-abundance column have been joined.

\paragraph{Controlled statistic.}
For each organism, the protein readout $P$ is residualized against the declared baseline controls before the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual block is tested.  The control set contains length, amino-acid composition, GC3, and M-density terms, with $24$ control columns and numerical rank $23$ in each organism.  The residualized coordinate block retains rank $9$ in all three organisms, so the nine-coordinate test is not rank-collapsed after the baseline projection.  The finite residual degrees of freedom after controls are $3716$ for Escherichia coli K12 MG1655, $4106$ for Mycobacterium smegmatis str. MC2 155, and $5792$ for Saccharomyces cerevisiae.  The corresponding residual protein-readout energies are $4020.333671802061$, $5729.194538214237$, and $2958.1865890925337$, while the baseline-explained energies are $432.89050065533684$, $183.85037813566998$, and $882.6526210803958$.  Thus the computation has enough finite row support for a direct residual association test inside each organism, with degenerate $R^2$ cutoff $0.999999$ and positive partial-$R^2$ threshold $10^{-12}$.

\paragraph{Protein-omics survival readback.}
Let $X_Q$ denote the residualized nine-column $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ coordinate block and let $P_Z$ denote the protein-abundance readout after projection away from the baseline control space $Z$.  The reported direct protein-omics survival score is the incremental controlled fit of $X_Q$ against $P_Z$, written here as $R^2_{QP}$ rather than as a mediation statistic.  The finite computation gives
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{QP}(\mathrm{E.\ coli\ K12\ MG1655})&=0.10767526677985896,\\
R^2_{QP}(\mathrm{M.\ smegmatis\ str.\ MC2\ 155})&=0.03209009170649934,\\
R^2_{QP}(\mathrm{S.\ cerevisiae})&=0.29837624994140827.
\end{aligned}
$$
All nine residual coordinates have positive direct survival under the stated positivity threshold in each organism, so the finite-row claim supported by this run is that the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ synonymous residual block retains measurable protein-abundance association after the local sequence-composition controls have been removed.  The largest reported block-level direct survival among these three finite contacts is the Saccharomyces cerevisiae value $0.29837624994140827$; the smallest is the Mycobacterium smegmatis value $0.03209009170649934$.  The conclusion is therefore not a single-organism anomaly in the row accounting: every organism has full coordinate rank $9$, positive block survival, and thousands of residual degrees of freedom.

\paragraph{Translation boundary.}
The direct protein-omics score is deliberately not interpreted as a translation mechanism.  The same research ledger contains a modeled translation-readout contact based on GtRNAdb tRNA gene-copy data and dos Reis wobble weights for Escherichia coli K12, Homo sapiens, and Saccharomyces cerevisiae.  That modeled-tAI run computed all $61$ sense codon weights in each of the three organisms, used local tRNA payloads with $89$, $432$, and $275$ FASTA headers respectively, and found $81$ bounded-coupling entries above threshold $0.01$, with maximum absolute coupling $3801961.031607896$.  However, that run has only $3$ organisms and explicitly remains underpowered for powered translation survival, which requires at least $40$ matched species for that mode.  It is also modeled tAI rather than a measured ribosome-profiling or translation-efficiency table.  Therefore the present protein-abundance association is kept downstream of a boundary: it records $Q \to P$ predictive survival, not $Q \to T \to P$ mediation.

\paragraph{No promotion beyond the measured layer.}
The finite rows support a direct omics correlation between synonymous residual codon topology and measured protein abundance after sequence-composition controls.  They do not by themselves show that a ribosome-rate mediator absorbs the signal, because the direct protein-omics run does not include matched ribosome profiling, measured translation efficiency, perturbation, rescue, or matched transcript controls.  A stronger mediated claim would require same-CDS rows carrying $q_i$, a measured translation readout $T_j$, protein abundance $P_k$, and mRNA abundance, followed by an explicit fit testing whether conditioning on $T_j$ removes or reduces the $q_i$ to $P_k$ signal.  Until that contact exists, positive $S^{QP}_{ik}$ values remain evidence of DNA-to-protein-omics predictive irreducibility only.

\paragraph{Local cannot-claim boundary.}
No translation mechanism, folding claim, physical admissibility claim, biochemical function, localization claim, stability claim, phenotype claim, causal statement, or universal biological law follows from this record.  PAXdb abundance is a cross-sectional protein-omics measurement, not a translation-rate instrument.  The score $S^{QP}$ is consequently a controlled direct association from $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual codon usage to protein abundance.  It is not a statement about protein folding, post-translational modification, pathway role, organismal fitness, or molecular function.  Any such interpretation would need an additional layer-specific measurement and a separately recorded finite statistic at that layer.

\paragraph{Finite verdict.}
Within its declared layer, the record is positive: all three organism-specific finite matrices are joined at high hit rate, retain full residual coordinate rank, and produce positive direct protein-omics survival with $R^2_{QP}$ values $0.10767526677985896$, $0.03209009170649934$, and $0.29837624994140827$.  The result is therefore accepted only as a bounded cross-layer relation from $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates to matched protein-abundance readouts.  The same record remains open at the translation-mediation boundary, because a measured $T_j$ table and matched mRNA controls have not yet been joined to the protein-omics rows for an explicit $Q \to T \to P$ mediation matrix.
