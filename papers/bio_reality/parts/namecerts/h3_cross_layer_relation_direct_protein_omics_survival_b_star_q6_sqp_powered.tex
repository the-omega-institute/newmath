\subsection{Direct protein-omics survival of $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinates}\label{subsec:bior reality-q6-direct-protein-omics-survival}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite row object.}
The finite object considered here is a coding-sequence by organism table in which each CDS row carries nine synonymous residual coordinates after the amino-acid quotient, a measured protein-abundance readout, and the covariates used to remove the most immediate compositional alternatives.  The nine coordinates are $K\_AAA$, $Arg\_AGR$, $Ile\_AUA$, $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$, $Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG$, $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$, $Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG$, $Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY$, and $f3\_stress$.  The protein readout is $\log_{10}(\mathrm{abundance\_ppm})$ from measured PAXdb abundance joined to real CDS codon counts.  The control matrix contains an intercept, CDS length, twenty amino-acid composition coordinates, GC3, and an $M$-density baseline, giving $24$ control columns with residual control rank $23$ in every organism-specific fit.  The reported statistic is the direct residual protein-omics survival score $S^{QP}$: it asks whether a residual codon coordinate still explains protein abundance after these sequence-derived controls have been removed.

\paragraph{Controlled statistic.}
For each organism $o$, let $y_o$ be the residualized protein-abundance vector, let $Z_o$ be the control matrix, and let $Q_o$ be the nine-column residual-coordinate matrix.  The fitted surface is the partial residual regression
$$
\begin{aligned}
\widetilde y_o &= (I-\Pi_{Z_o})y_o,\\
\widetilde Q_o &= (I-\Pi_{Z_o})Q_o,\\
R^2_{QP}(o) &= 1-\frac{\lVert \widetilde y_o-\Pi_{\widetilde Q_o}\widetilde y_o\rVert^2}{\lVert \widetilde y_o\rVert^2}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The finite run uses the positive threshold $10^{-12}$ for coordinate survival and the degeneracy guard $R^2<0.999999$.  Every organism has $\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde Q_o)=9$, so the nine-coordinate surface is not collapsed by the control projection.  The residual degrees of freedom after $Z$ range from $1633$ in $Sulfolobus\ solfataricus$ to $19372$ in $Mus\ musculus$, and every row-level organism fit is marked rank-sufficient.

\paragraph{Translation boundary.}
This chapter deliberately does not identify the direct $q_i\to P_k$ association with translation.  A separate translation row would have to attach a measured ribosome-occupancy or translation-efficiency readout $T_j$, matched mRNA abundance, and a mediation fit testing whether $q_i\to T_j\to P_k$ absorbs the direct $q_i\to P_k$ signal.  The available modeled translation contact is only a bounded tAI readback on $3$ organisms: it computes $81$ entries above threshold $0.01$ and has maximum absolute $S_{ij}=3801961.031607896$, but the same record states that $3$ organisms are insufficient for powered survival.  Thus modeled tAI coupling can mark a translation-facing boundary, while it cannot by itself promote the protein-abundance association to a measured translation mechanism.

The nearby RNA half-life checks show why this chapter keeps protein abundance, transcript stability, and translation as separate readout layers.  In yeast, the same coordinate family gives $R^2_{QM}=0.05179429044975085$ for log half-life, $R^2_{QP}=0.2761140373962526$ on the same join, cosine $0.8040935468384264$, dominant coordinate $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$, $5085$ joined rows, and permutation $p=0.000999000999000999$. In human, the consensus relative half-life surface has $R^2_{QM}=0.010503670362051522$, same-join $R^2_{QP}=0.04095215557813418$, cosine $0.6351183883924848$, dominant coordinate $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$, $16165$ joined rows, and permutation $p=0.000999000999000999$.  These values support the narrower reading that $B^*_{Q6}$ residuals have finite cross-layer statistical contacts, not a single undifferentiated biological mechanism.

\paragraph{Local interpretation.}
The chapter is ready as a protein-omics association result because the observed readout is measured abundance, the joined row counts are explicit, the control ranks are nondegenerate, and each organism retains a positive nine-coordinate residual surface.  Its scope is nevertheless intentionally bounded.  No claim about translation rate, ribosome dynamics, folding, structural order, physical admissibility, localization, post-translational state, biochemical function, phenotype, causal mechanism, adaptation, or universality follows from $S^{QP}$ alone.  Those statements require their own layer-matched contacts and their own controlled finite statistics.  The present result says only that after amino-acid composition, GC3, length, and $M$-density controls, the $B^*_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates still carry direct predictive information for measured protein abundance in the finite PAXdb-joined panels.
