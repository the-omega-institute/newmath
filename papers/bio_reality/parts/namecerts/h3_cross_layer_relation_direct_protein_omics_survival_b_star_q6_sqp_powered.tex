\subsection{Direct protein-omics survival of $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ synonymous residuals}
\label{sec:bior reality-q6-direct-protein-omics-survival}

\paragraph{Finite panel.}
The experiment places nine $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates on real coding sequences and asks whether they retain direct predictive mass against matched protein-abundance measurements after sequence-composition controls.  The nine coordinates are
$K\_AAA$, $Arg\_AGR$, $Ile\_AUA$, $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$, $Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG$, $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$, $Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG$, $Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY$, and $f3\_stress$.  The response variable is $\log_{10}(abundance\_ppm)$ from measured PAXdb protein abundance joined to real CDS codon counts.  The covariate block has $24$ columns: an intercept, coding length, the $20$ amino-acid composition proportions, $GC3$, and the $M$-density baseline formed from the union support of the nine projected $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ vectors.  In every organism listed below, the residualized coordinate matrix has rank $9$ after the control projection, while the control matrix has rank $23$; hence the protein-omics fits are not vacuous coordinate-rank artifacts.

\paragraph{Controlled survival statistic.}
For each organism $o$, let $Y_o$ be the centered protein-abundance vector, $Z_o$ the baseline control matrix, and $Q_o$ the nine-column synonymous residual coordinate matrix.  The reported statistic is the partial coefficient of determination of $Q_o$ after residualizing both $Y_o$ and $Q_o$ against $Z_o$:
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{QP}(o)
  &= 1 - \frac{\| (I-\Pi_{\widetilde Q_o})(I-\Pi_{Z_o})Y_o\|^2}{\|(I-\Pi_{Z_o})Y_o\|^2}, \\
\widetilde Q_o
  &= (I-\Pi_{Z_o})Q_o .
\end{aligned}
$$
The positive-entry threshold used in the run is $10^{-12}$, and the degeneracy guard is $R^2 < 0.999999$.  All fifteen organism panels pass the rank check with $rank(\widetilde Q_o)=9$ and with all nine coordinates surviving the positive partial-$R^2$ threshold.

\paragraph{Relation to translation and RNA readouts.}
A separate modeled-translation contact computed $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residuals for three organisms using GtRNAdb tRNA gene copies and dos Reis wobble weights.  That contact found $81$ entries above threshold $0.01$ and maximum $|S_{ij}|=3801961.031607896$, but it also records that $3$ organisms are insufficient for powered survival and that the readout is modeled tAI rather than measured ribosome profiling.  The protein-omics result in this subsection therefore remains a direct $Q \to P$ association.  It is not a measured $Q \to T$ translation result and it is not a mediated $Q \to T \to P$ result.

The same coordinate family also has RNA-half-life contacts on separate finite panels.  In yeast, $5085$ joined rows give $R^2_{QM}=0.05179429044975085$, same-join $R^2_{QP}=0.2761140373962526$, cosine $0.8040935468384264$, and permutation $p=0.000999000999000999$, with $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$ dominant for $S^{QM}$.  In \emph{E. coli}, $2720$ joined rows give $R^2_{QM}=0.02298556640056076$, same-join $R^2_{QP}=0.11632311854858134$, cosine $-0.7784923375569599$, and the same permutation resolution $p=0.000999000999000999$, with $Arg\_AGR$ dominant.  In human, $16165$ joined rows across $54$ consensus half-life columns give $R^2_{QM}=0.010503670362051522$, same-join $R^2_{QP}=0.04095215557813418$, cosine $0.6351183883924848$, and $p=0.000999000999000999$, with $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$ dominant for RNA half-life and $Arg\_AGR$ dominant for the same-join protein-abundance projection.  These RNA contacts support the interpretation that the $Q$ coordinates carry cross-layer statistical information, but they do not turn the protein-abundance association into a translation mechanism.

\paragraph{Local verdict.}
The finite evidence supports a bounded direct protein-omics survival statement: after length, amino-acid composition, $GC3$, and $M$-density controls, every tested organism has a positive $S^{QP}$ surface with all nine $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ coordinates surviving the numerical threshold, and the largest observed direct protein-abundance partial $R^2$ is $0.29837624994140827$ in yeast.  The result is deliberately narrower than a biological mechanism claim.  Protein abundance is a steady-state omics readout, not a translation-rate measurement.  No translation, ribosome-occupancy, folding, physical-admissibility, localization, post-translational-state, biochemical-function, phenotype, causation, mechanism, or universal biological law follows from these $S^{QP}$ values unless a separate layer-matched contact supplies that readout and a joint statistic verifies the required boundary.  In particular, a $Q_i \to P_k$ signal is not promoted to $Q_i \to T_j \to P_k$ mediation until measured translation rows, matched mRNA controls, and matched protein-abundance rows are joined on the same finite panel and conditioning on $T_j$ is shown to remove or reduce the direct $Q_i \to P_k$ association.
