\subsection{Three-axis insufficiency for the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ optimality surface}
\label{sec:bioreality-q6-three-axis-sufficiency}

\paragraph{Finite carrier.}
This BioReality record concerns the finite residual coordinate surface obtained from coding-sequence rows after amino-acid projection, organism matching, and synonymous-design restriction to the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ family.  The coordinate under inspection is the $f_3$-stress residual, read only through rows that can compare codon usage, tRNA or wobble-weight asymmetry, and translation-layer quantities.  The finite row used here is not a claim about protein state or organismal phenotype.  It is a row-level statement about whether the already named optimality axes close the controlled synonymous surface, or whether a further independent direction remains visible after the declared controls.

The saturation row fixes the relevant $f_3$ boundary.  The ambient marked set is
$$
\begin{aligned}
M &= \{\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{ACA},\mathrm{ACG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\\
&\qquad \mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\},
\end{aligned}
$$
while the $f_3$-saturated set removes precisely $\mathrm{ACA}$ and $\mathrm{ACG}$:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{Sat}_{f_3}(R) &= \{\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\\
&\qquad \mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\},\\
\partial_{f_3} &= \varnothing .
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the coordinate is finite and non-asymptotic: the boundary supplied to this row is the empty $f_3$ boundary, and all subsequent strength calls are made on the controlled synonymous design rather than on an unrestricted codon universe.

\paragraph{Three-axis test.}
The principal finite result is the powered optimality-axis run \texttt{b\_star\_q6\_optimality\_axis\_sufficiency\_powered}.  It requested $16$ organisms, computed $11$ organisms, required at least $500$ proteins per organism, used $41$ synonymous-design columns, and used $5$ folds.  Its recorded conclusion is \texttt{fourth\_mechanism\_present}.  In row terms, the three named axes do not exhaust the residual optimality surface on the computed panel.  This is a sufficiency failure, not a biological promotion: it says that the finite controlled surface still carries a fourth direction after the tested three-axis span has been accounted for.

The surrounding powered rows make the same reading more precise.  The two-axis closure run also computed $11$ of $16$ requested organisms with $5$ folds, $41$ synonymous-design columns, and a $500$-protein floor, and concluded \texttt{third\_axis\_present}.  The residual-axis run on the same organism count and design width reported that the non-tRNA residual optimality direction aligns most with \texttt{f3\_translation\_selection}.  The third-axis identity run identified $d_{\perp}$ with \texttt{usage\_frequency\_within\_family}, with mean cosine $0.128$ and one-sided sign-test value $p=0.000488$.  The third-axis mechanism row then kept the signal after GC orthogonalization and reported \texttt{selection-not-GC; not-wobble-clustered; CSC/positional identity needs\_external}.  These rows support a finite hierarchy: one tRNA-linked axis is insufficient, the tRNA plus $f_3$ span is insufficient, and the three-axis model is still insufficient on the powered panel.

\paragraph{Axis spectrum.}
The per-organism spectrum row gives the finite shape of the remaining variation.  It computed $11$ organisms out of $16$ requested, again with $5$ folds, a $500$-protein floor, and $41$ synonymous-design columns.  Its descriptive conclusion was that captured spectra are led by $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$ on average, then by $d_{\mathrm{tRNA}}$, and that the per-organism dominant counts are $\{d_{\mathrm{resid4}}:11\}$.  Domain means were explicitly marked descriptive under the imbalanced counts $\{\mathrm{archaea}:1,\mathrm{bacteria}:2,\mathrm{eukaryote}:8\}$.  The largest GC3 rank effect was reported as $\rho=0.136$ for $d_{\mathrm{usage}}$.  The spectrum therefore does not license a taxonomic law; it records that, on every computed organism in this finite panel, the residual fourth direction dominates the captured axis-strength summary.

\paragraph{Mechanism exclusions and partial contacts.}
The mechanism rows restrict what the fourth direction can presently mean.  The mRNA-stability assay, with $11$ computed organisms, $16$ requested organisms, $5$ folds, a $500$-protein floor, and $41$ design columns, concluded \texttt{fourth\_axis\_not\_mrna\_stability}; its reason was that the yeast $d_{\mathrm{opt\_halflife}}$ direction has low alignment with abundance $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$ under the fixed gates.  The codon-pair assay concluded \texttt{not\_codon\_pair} with $50$ shuffle-null draws.  The translation-accuracy assay computed $11$ of $16$ organisms, used $200$ permutations, and concluded \texttt{not\_accuracy}.  The elongation-dwell assay is narrower: it was run for \textit{Saccharomyces cerevisiae} and concluded \texttt{partial}.  These calls leave a real fourth-axis remainder, but they do not identify it as mRNA half-life, codon-pair structure, or translation accuracy in the tested designs; elongation dwell remains only a partial row.

A separate boundary-set row supplies a mechanism-adjacent constraint on the $R$ sense codons.  It requested $46$ organisms, computed $40$, skipped $6$, used $1000$ permutations at $\alpha=0.05$, and concluded that $R$ sense codons are systematically tRNA-poor under GtRNAdb tGCN with dos Reis wobble weights.  This supports a tRNA-supply interpretation for $R$ avoidance, but it is still not a direct translation-efficiency, ribosome-occupancy, protein-output, or phenotype row for the $f_3$-stress coordinate.

\paragraph{Bounded readback.}
For this chapter the admissible readback is the controlled entry $S_{f_3\text{-}\mathrm{stress},T_j}$, where $T_j$ is a bounded translation readout such as tAI, stAI, or an explicitly joined ribosome-occupancy or translation-efficiency row.  The support condition is not mere codon topology and not the existence of a modeled weight.  A positive translation row would require a same-organism or same-CDS panel joining codon counts, the $f_3$-stress coordinate, tRNA or wobble weights, a translation readout, matched mRNA abundance where needed, and controls for amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, coding-sequence length, $M$-density, rank, and simpler baselines.  Without that joined statistic, an all-$1.0$ rank-saturated surface on a small organism panel is a null boundary rather than evidence of survival.

\paragraph{Local no-promotion rule.}
The finite rows above justify the following local statement and no more: on the computed powered panel, the three-axis optimality description is insufficient, and a fourth residual direction is present under the declared synonymous design.  The row does not establish observed translation, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, mechanism, phenotype, or universality.  Modeled tAI or stAI weights may serve as bounded translation-layer annotations only when kept separate from direct ribosome measurements.  Ribosome profiling or translation efficiency would need its own joined, controlled statistic before any positive $S_{f_3\text{-}\mathrm{stress},T_j}$ claim is made.  Protein abundance, localization, stability, structure, function, physical assay, perturbation, or phenotype conclusions each require separate layer-matched contacts and their own finite statistics.  Thus the chapter records a controlled three-axis failure and a fourth-axis remainder, while holding every downstream biological interpretation outside the claim until the corresponding contact is supplied.
