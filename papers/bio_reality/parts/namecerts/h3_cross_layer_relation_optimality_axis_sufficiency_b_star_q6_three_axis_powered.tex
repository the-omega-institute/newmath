\subsection{Three-axis insufficiency of the $B^*_{Q6}$ translation surface}
\label{sec:biorreality-q6-three-axis-insufficiency}

\paragraph{Finite carrier.}
The record considered here is the finite coding-sequence surface obtained after amino-acid projection, organism matching, and restriction to the $f_3$-stress coordinate of $B^*_{Q6}$.  Its biological rows are not protein rows, structural rows, localization rows, or phenotype rows.  They are codon-usage, tRNA or wobble-weight, and bounded translation-readout rows.  The claim tested in this section is narrower than a universal codon law: it asks whether the three-axis description formed from the core tRNA direction, the $f_3$ translation-selection residual direction, and the previously detected third residual direction is sufficient for the powered multi-organism surface.

The saturated $f_3$ row is finite.  The ambient set used for the saturation computation is
$$\begin{aligned}
M={}&\{\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{ACA},\mathrm{ACG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\\
&\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\}.
\end{aligned}$$
The $f_3$-saturated residual subset removes the two $\mathrm{AC}$ boundary codons and is
$$\begin{aligned}
\operatorname{Sat}_{f_3}(R)={}&\{\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\\
&\mathrm{CUU},\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\}.
\end{aligned}$$
The finite boundary list is empty, $\partial_{f_3}R=\varnothing$.  Thus the tested object is not a loose codon narrative: it is a residual coordinate surface with a declared twenty-codon ambient list, an eighteen-codon saturated list, and a zero boundary entry for the $f_3$ saturation check.

\paragraph{Powered three-axis test.}
The direct optimality-axis sufficiency assay was run as \texttt{b\_star\_q6\_optimality\_axis\_sufficiency\_powered}.  It used $5$ folds, required at least $500$ proteins per organism, computed $11$ organisms out of $16$ requested organisms, and used $41$ synonymous-design columns.  The deterministic seed was \texttt{sha256:b\_star\_q6\_optimality\_axis\_sufficiency\_powered:deterministic}.  The row status was \texttt{passed}, and the finite conclusion was
$$\begin{aligned}
\text{three-axis sufficiency call} &= \texttt{fourth\_mechanism\_present}.
\end{aligned}$$
This is a negative sufficiency statement for the three-axis model, not a positive identification of a downstream biological layer.  The assay says that, in the powered panel actually computed, the three declared axes do not exhaust the surviving residual structure.  It does not say that the remaining structure is protein abundance, folding, localization, molecular function, physical admissibility, phenotype, or a general biological principle.

\paragraph{Relation to the lower-dimensional axes.}
The insufficiency call is coherent with the surrounding finite rows.  The two-axis closure assay \texttt{b\_star\_q6\_universal\_optimal\_two\_axis\_closure\_powered} used the same $5$ folds, $500$-protein minimum, $11/16$ organism computation, and $41$ synonymous-design columns, and returned \texttt{third\_axis\_present}.  Its stated reason was that $d_{\perp}$ had systematically positive held-out $R^2$ and that the $d_{\perp}$ directions were mutually same-signed across organisms.  The third-axis identity assay then provisionally aligned $d_{\perp}$ with usage frequency within family, with mean cosine $0.128$ and one-sided sign-test $p=0.000488$.  A separate mechanism row classified the same third direction as selection-not-GC, not wobble-clustered, with the CSC or positional identity still requiring an additional contact.

These rows give the following finite progression:
$$\begin{aligned}
\text{two-axis span} &\longrightarrow \texttt{third\_axis\_present},\\
\text{third-axis identity} &\longrightarrow \text{usage-frequency-within-family alignment},\\
\text{three-axis span} &\longrightarrow \texttt{fourth\_mechanism\_present}.
\end{aligned}$$
The progression is not a hierarchy of biological realization.  It is a sequence of residual-coordinate failures of sufficiency under the declared organism and design gates.

\paragraph{Axis spectrum.}
The per-organism axis-strength spectrum further constrains the interpretation.  In the powered spectrum run, $11$ organisms were computed out of $16$ requested, using $5$ folds, a $500$-protein minimum, and $41$ synonymous-design columns.  The captured spectra were led on average by $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$, followed by $d_{\mathrm{tRNA}}$, and the per-organism dominant-count summary was $\{\texttt{d\_resid4}:11\}$.  Domain means were explicitly descriptive under the imbalanced domain counts $\{\texttt{archaea}:1,\texttt{bacteria}:2,\texttt{eukaryote}:8\}$.  The largest reported GC3 rank effect was small, with $d_{\mathrm{usage}}$ having $\rho=0.136$.

This spectrum supports the local reading of the sufficiency failure.  The finite surface is not merely rediscovering a single GC3 axis, nor is it licensed to report a universal domain contrast from the imbalanced domain counts.  The data instead say that, among the computed organisms, the residual fourth direction is repeatedly dominant in the captured spectrum while the GC3 rank effect is bounded by the reported $0.136$ value.

\paragraph{Translation readout boundary.}
The $f_3$-stress coordinate becomes biologically testable only where the translation row is present and nondegenerate.  A modeled tAI or stAI weight can be used as a bounded codon-adaptation readback; a ribosome-profiling or translation-efficiency row can test a measured translation surface only after same-organism and same-CDS joining with codon counts and matched mRNA controls.  The controlled survival entry has the form
$$\begin{aligned}
S_{f_3\text{-stress},T_j}>0
\end{aligned}$$
after amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, coding-sequence length, $M$-density, matched mRNA, rank, and simpler-baseline controls.  Without that row, there is no assertion of translation realization beyond the bounded modeled readback.  In particular, the three-organism all-$1.0$ rank-degenerate surface is a null boundary rather than survival evidence.

\paragraph{Local no-promotion rule.}
The finite conclusion \texttt{fourth\_mechanism\_present} is therefore a residual-coordinate statement.  It is compatible with the tRNA-poverty row for $R$ sense codons, which was tested across $40$ computed organisms out of $46$ requested with $1000$ permutations at $\alpha=0.05$, and it is compatible with the non-tRNA residual optimality row whose dominant axis was \texttt{f3\_translation\_selection}.  But compatibility is not promotion.  No claim about ribosome occupancy, protein abundance, folded structure, physical admissibility, molecular function, localization, stability, phenotype, mechanism, or universality follows from this section unless a separate layer-matched contact supplies the corresponding rows and statistic.  The present record only establishes that, on the finite powered coordinate surface with $11$ computed organisms and $41$ synonymous-design columns, the declared three-axis span is insufficient and a fourth residual direction remains to be explained.
