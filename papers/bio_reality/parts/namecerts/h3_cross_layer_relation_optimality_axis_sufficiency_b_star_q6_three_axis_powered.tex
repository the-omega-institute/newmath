\subsection{Three-axis insufficiency for the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ translation residual surface}
\label{sec:bior-h3-q6-three-axis-powered}

\paragraph{Finite coordinate surface.}
This record concerns the finite coding-sequence residual surface obtained after amino-acid projection, restricted to the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ coordinate family and to the $f_3$-stress direction. The $f_3$ operation leaves no unused boundary row in the recorded saturation table: the listed boundary complement is empty. The finite codon support used by the coordinate packet is not an informal codon-class slogan; it is the explicit row set
$$
\begin{aligned}
M &= \{\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{ACA},\mathrm{ACG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\},\\
\operatorname{Sat}_{f_3}(R) &= \{\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\},\\
M\setminus \operatorname{Sat}_{f_3}(R) &= \{\mathrm{ACA},\mathrm{ACG}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the chapter is about a finite row system in which the stress coordinate is already saturated except for the two recorded $\mathrm{ACN}$ rows. The biological interpretation is deliberately narrower than the notation may suggest: this surface is allowed to ask whether synonymous imbalance survives into a translation readout after controls, but it does not by itself assert a protein, structure, phenotype, or universal biological rule.

\paragraph{Rank-powered sufficiency test.}
The direct sufficiency claim tested whether the previously retained three axes close the optimal residual surface. The powered run for this chapter used $5$ folds, required at least $500$ proteins per organism, computed $11$ organisms out of $16$ requested organisms, and used $41$ synonymous-design columns. Its finite verdict was
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{experiment} &= \mathrm{b\_star\_q6\_optimality\_axis\_sufficiency\_powered},\\
\mathrm{status} &= \mathrm{passed},\\
\mathrm{conclusion} &= \mathrm{fourth\_mechanism\_present}.
\end{aligned}
$$
Consequently the three-axis span is not sufficient on this finite powered panel. The result should be read as an insufficiency statement about the controlled residual design, not as an immediate identification of the missing biology. In the companion axis-strength computation on the same design scale, $11$ organisms were computed from $16$ requested, again with $5$ folds, $500$ minimum proteins per organism, and $41$ synonymous-design columns; all $11$ organism-level dominance calls were led by $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$, while the descriptive domain counts were imbalanced with $1$ archaeal, $2$ bacterial, and $8$ eukaryotic organisms. The largest reported GC3 rank effect in that spectrum was small, $\rho=0.136$ for $d_{\mathrm{usage}}$, so the observed insufficiency is not summarized by a large GC3 rank artifact in the recorded scalar surface.

\paragraph{Relation to the prior residual axes.}
The failure of three-axis closure sits after two earlier finite separations. First, the two-axis closure test reported a third axis: $d_{\perp}$ had systematically positive held-out $R^2$ and same-signed organism-level directions on a panel with $11$ computed organisms, $16$ requested organisms, $5$ folds, $500$ minimum proteins per organism, and $41$ synonymous-design columns. Second, the identity run for this third axis provisionally aligned $d_{\perp}$ to within-family usage frequency, with mean cosine $0.128$ and one-sided sign-test $p=0.000488$. A mechanism check then kept the same scale and reported selection-not-GC, not-wobble-clustered, with CSC or positional identity still requiring a separate contact; its stated reason was that GC-orthogonalized $d_{\perp}$ retained significant cross-species alignment and held-out $R^2$. These finite facts make the present chapter specific: it is not discovering that one or two coordinates are missing in the abstract, but recording that even after the tRNA-related and $f_3$-selection axes and the third residual direction have been admitted, an additional mechanism remains visible in the powered residual surface.

\paragraph{Negative identifications for the fourth direction.}
The missing component is constrained by several finite exclusions. The mRNA-stability assay returned $\mathrm{fourth\_axis\_not\_mrna\_stability}$ under the fixed gates, because the yeast $d_{\mathrm{opt\_halflife}}$ alignment with abundance $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$ was low. The codon-pair assay returned $\mathrm{not\_codon\_pair}$ using $5$ folds, a $500$ protein minimum per organism, $41$ synonymous-design columns, and $50$ shuffle-null draws. The translation-accuracy assay returned $\mathrm{not\_accuracy}$ on $11$ computed organisms out of $16$, with $200$ permutations, $5$ folds, and the same $41$ synonymous-design columns. The elongation-dwell assay was the only listed fourth-mechanism run that did not reject its candidate outright: in \textit{Saccharomyces cerevisiae} it returned $\mathrm{partial}$ with $5$ folds. The conservative finite read is therefore that the fourth mechanism is present, is not identified by the recorded mRNA-stability, codon-pair, or accuracy tests, and has only partial support from the available elongation-dwell row.

\paragraph{Translation-readout gate.}
For the $f_3$-stress conjecture, a translation row is mandatory. A modeled tAI or stAI surface may supply a bounded codon-adaptation readout, and a ribosome-profiling or translation-efficiency table may supply a measured translation readout, but neither becomes survival merely by being named. The admissible statistic has the form
$$
\begin{aligned}
S_{f_3\text{-}\mathrm{stress},T_j} &> 0
\end{aligned}
$$
after amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, CDS length, $M$-density, matched mRNA, rank, and simpler-baseline controls. If the joined same-organism or same-CDS matrix is absent, or if the matrix is rank-degenerate, the entry is null for this chapter. In particular, an all-$1.0$ three-organism surface is a saturation boundary rather than evidence of translation survival. The positive fourth-mechanism result above therefore does not promote the $f_3$ coordinate to a downstream biological claim until the translation-readout row is non-vacuous and positive under the declared controls.

\paragraph{Local no-promotion rule.}
The chapter records a finite cross-layer pressure, not a general biological law. Codon counts, tRNA-copy summaries, wobble or adaptation weights, and residual coordinates can be used to construct the finite residual design. They do not, without a separate layer-matched measurement, establish ribosome occupancy, protein abundance, folded structure, physical admissibility, molecular function, localization, stability, phenotype, mechanism, causation, or universality. The only claim carried here is the controlled finite one: on the powered $B^{*}_{Q6}$ synonymous residual design, $11$ computed organisms, $5$ folds, $500$ protein minimum, and $41$ synonymous-design columns were sufficient to reject three-axis closure and to record a fourth residual mechanism, while the available fourth-mechanism probes constrain but do not fully identify that mechanism.
