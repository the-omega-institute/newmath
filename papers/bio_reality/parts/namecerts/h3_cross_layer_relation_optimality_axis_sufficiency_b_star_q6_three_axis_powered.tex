\subsection{Three-axis insufficiency for the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ optimality surface}\label{sec:bioreality-q6-three-axis-sufficiency}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite coordinate under test.}
This record concerns the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ synonymous residual surface after amino-acid projection, with special attention to the $f_3$-stress coordinate and its possible translation-row readout.  The finite carrier is not a protein-level object: it is a table of coding-sequence residual coordinates, organism labels, codon-usage summaries, tRNA or wobble-weight annotations, and matched translation-readout rows when those rows are actually present.  The tested optimality question is whether the already isolated axes suffice for the powered cross-organism surface, or whether a further residual mechanism remains after the declared synonymous design is fit.

The $f_3$ saturation fact fixes the relevant codon boundary.  The marked set $M={\rm Med}_R\cup\{ACA\}$ is
$$
\begin{aligned}
M=\{&AAA,AAG,ACA,ACG,AGA,AGG,AUA,AUG,CUA,CUC,CUG,CUU,\\
&UAA,UAG,UCA,UCG,UGA,UGG,UUA,UUG\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
After applying $f_3$ saturation to $R$, the surviving list is
$$
\begin{aligned}
{\rm Sat}_{f_3}(R)=\{&AAA,AAG,AGA,AGG,AUA,AUG,CUA,CUC,CUG,CUU,\\
&UAA,UAG,UCA,UCG,UGA,UGG,UUA,UUG\},
\end{aligned}
$$
with no separate $f_3$ boundary residue recorded.  Thus the finite row geometry removes the $ACA,ACG$ pair from the saturated $R$ surface and leaves an $18$-codon saturated support inside the $20$-codon marked set.  The biological reading is deliberately narrow: this is a synonymous-coordinate and organism-row construction, not by itself an observation of ribosome dwell time, protein abundance, structure, function, phenotype, or a universal biological rule.

\paragraph{Powered optimality verdict.}
The powered optimality-axis test for claim \texttt{h3.cross\_layer\_relation.optimality\_axis\_sufficiency.b\_star\_q6\_three\_axis\_powered} passed on experiment run \texttt{2436cae9d455}.  The assay used $5$ folds, required at least $500$ proteins per organism, computed $11$ organisms out of $16$ requested organisms, and used $41$ synonymous design columns.  Its finite conclusion is \texttt{fourth\_mechanism\_present}.  In the notation of the residual design, the bounded statement is
$$
\begin{aligned}
{\rm three\_axis\_span}(B^{\ast}_{Q6})\;\hbox{is not sufficient for the powered optimality surface.}
\end{aligned}
$$
Equivalently, after fitting the declared tRNA-related, $f_3$-related, and third residual axes in the powered organism panel, a remaining axis is still detected by the held-out design.  This justifies the chapter-level conclusion that the optimality surface is at least four-mechanism in this assay, but it does not identify that fourth mechanism as translation efficiency, mRNA stability, codon-pair bias, elongation dwell, translation accuracy, localization, folding, or phenotype.

The surrounding finite facts sharpen that interpretation.  The two-axis closure test concluded \texttt{third\_axis\_present} on $11$ computed organisms, with $5$ folds, $500$ proteins per organism, and $41$ synonymous design columns; its reason was that $d_{\perp}$ had systematically positive held-out $R^2$ and same-signed directions across organisms.  The third-axis identity test then provisionally aligned $d_{\perp}$ with within-family usage frequency, with mean cosine $0.128$ and one-sided sign-test $p=0.000488$.  The third-axis mechanism test reported \texttt{selection-not-GC; not-wobble-clustered; CSC/positional identity needs\_external}, because GC-orthogonalized $d_{\perp}$ retained significant cross-species alignment and held-out $R^2$.  These facts make the fourth-axis result nontrivial: the fourth signal is not merely the first appearance of a third residual direction, nor is it automatically explained by GC rank or wobble clustering.

\paragraph{Axis spectrum and negative mechanism screens.}
The per-organism axis spectrum also passed on $11$ computed organisms out of $16$ requested, again with $5$ folds, at least $500$ proteins per organism, and $41$ synonymous design columns.  Its captured spectra were led by $d_{\rm resid4}$ on average, followed by $d_{\rm tRNA}$; the per-organism dominant count was \texttt{\{'d\_resid4': 11\}}.  Domain means were explicitly descriptive only, because the domain counts were imbalanced: $1$ archaeal organism, $2$ bacterial organisms, and $8$ eukaryotic organisms.  The largest reported GC3 rank effect was $d_{\rm usage}$ with $\rho=0.136$.  This places the fourth-axis call in a finite, imbalanced, organism-ranked surface: strong enough to record a powered residual axis, but not strong enough to license a domain-general biological law.

Several candidate identities are already constrained by separate powered screens.  The mRNA-stability assay concluded \texttt{fourth\_axis\_not\_mrna\_stability}: in yeast, $d_{\rm opt\_halflife}$ had low alignment with abundance $d_{\rm resid4}$ under the fixed gates.  The codon-pair assay concluded \texttt{not\_codon\_pair} using $5$ folds and $50$ shuffle-null samples.  The translation-accuracy assay concluded \texttt{not\_accuracy} over $11$ computed organisms out of $16$ requested, using $200$ permutations and $41$ synonymous design columns.  The elongation-dwell assay, run in \textit{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}, concluded only \texttt{partial}.  Thus the clean local statement is not that the fourth mechanism has been named; it is that the three-axis span is insufficient, while several simple fourth-mechanism identifications have been rejected or left incomplete by finite tests.

\paragraph{Translation-row boundary.}
The $f_3$-stress conjecture becomes a translation-layer claim only where context tRNA or wobble weights break the third-position $f_3$ symmetry and where a matched translation readout survives the declared controls.  The required controlled row is $S_{f3\text{-}{\rm stress},T_j}$ for a bounded $T_j$, such as tAI, stAI, ribosome occupancy, or translation efficiency.  The required control set includes amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, CDS length, $M$-density, and mRNA abundance where translation efficiency is computed.  In finite terms, the admissible positive statement has the form
$$
\begin{aligned}
S_{f3\text{-}{\rm stress},T_j}>0
\end{aligned}
$$
only after the same organism or same CDS rows are joined and the simpler codon baselines are ablated.  A rank-degenerate all-$1.0$ surface on three organisms is not survival evidence; it is a null boundary caused by insufficient rank.  Likewise, modeled tAI or stAI weights can be used as bounded adaptation readbacks, but they are not observed ribosome occupancy or protein output.

The boundary-set tRNA-poverty experiment supplies a mechanism-adjacent finite fact but not a translation-survival row.  It passed with $40$ organisms computed out of $46$ requested, $6$ organisms skipped, $1000$ permutations, and $\alpha=0.05$; its call was that $R$ sense codons are systematically tRNA-poor under GtRNAdb tGCN plus dos Reis wobble weights, supporting a tRNA-supply explanation for $R$ avoidance.  That result is compatible with an $f_3$-stress translation hypothesis, because wobble-weight asymmetry can break the relevant symmetry.  It still does not replace the mandatory same-row $S_{f3\text{-}{\rm stress},T_j}$ estimate.

\paragraph{Local conclusion.}
The chapter-level verdict is therefore a bounded optimality result.  The powered $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ design detects that the three-axis span is insufficient and records a fourth residual mechanism on an $11$-organism, $5$-fold, $41$-column synonymous design with at least $500$ proteins per computed organism.  The surrounding axis tests show that $d_{\perp}$ is already present, provisionally usage-frequency aligned, and not simply GC or wobble clustering; the spectrum is led by $d_{\rm resid4}$ in all $11$ computed organisms.  Separate screens reject mRNA stability, codon-pair bias, and translation accuracy as the dominant fourth identity in their respective assays, while elongation dwell remains partial.

No stronger biological promotion is made here.  The coordinate result does not establish translation realization unless the translation-readout row survives the stated controls.  It does not establish folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, localization, protein stability, phenotype, mechanism, causation, or universality unless a separate layer-matched measurement supplies that claim and its own controlled statistic.  The present record is ready as a finite mathematical-biological chapter for the fourth-axis insufficiency claim, with the translation and downstream biological interpretations held behind their required contact rows.
