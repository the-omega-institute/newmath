\subsection{Three-axis optimality is insufficient for the powered $B^{*}_{Q6}$ surface}\label{sec:bioreality-q6-three-axis-optimality-sufficiency}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite object.}
This record concerns a finite synonymous-residual surface over coding sequences, restricted to the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ coordinate system and read only through organism-matched translation-facing rows.  The coordinate under inspection is the $f_{3}$-stress direction after amino-acid quotienting: synonymous codons are compared only after the amino-acid projection has removed amino-acid-composition effects.  The finite carrier is therefore not a protein-structure, phenotype, or biochemical-function object.  It is a controlled codon-coordinate table with organism, GC3, CDS length, $M$-density, and amino-acid-composition controls available as covariates.

The base $f_{3}$ saturation check gives an explicit finite boundary.  The set $M = R \cup \{\mathrm{ACA}\}$ contains
$$
\begin{aligned}
M=\{&\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{ACA},\mathrm{ACG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\\
&\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The $f_{3}$-saturated $R$ set removes exactly the two boundary codons $\mathrm{ACA}$ and $\mathrm{ACG}$:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\operatorname{Sat}_{f_{3}}(R)=\{&\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\\
&\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The reported $f_{3}$ boundary is empty after this saturation step.  This matters because the powered analysis is not allowed to create a new uncontrolled boundary by moving from codon topology to translation interpretation.

\paragraph{Powered three-axis test.}
The powered sufficiency run for the claim used $5$ folds, required at least $500$ proteins per organism, computed $11$ organisms out of $16$ requested organisms, and used $41$ synonymous-design columns.  Its finite conclusion is
$$
\begin{aligned}
\text{three-axis sufficiency verdict} = \text{fourth mechanism present}.
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the three-axis span is not accepted as a sufficient description of the powered $B^{*}_{Q6}$ optimality surface.  The earlier two-axis closure run already reported a surviving third axis: it used the same $5$ folds, the same $500$-protein minimum, the same $11$ of $16$ organism count, and the same $41$ synonymous-design columns, with the conclusion that a third axis is present because the orthogonal component has systematically positive held-out $R^{2}$ and mutually same-signed organism directions.  The current row is stronger in a different direction: after the third axis is admitted, the three-axis model still leaves a fourth component detectable under the powered gate.

\paragraph{Axis context.}
The residual-axis run identifies the dominant non-tRNA residual optimality direction as $f_{3}$ translation selection.  The third-axis identity run assigns the orthogonal direction provisionally to usage frequency within synonymous family, with mean cosine $0.128$ and one-sided sign-test $p=0.000488$ across the powered panel.  The third-axis mechanism run then reports that this direction remains aligned after GC orthogonalization, giving the local reading
$$
\begin{aligned}
\text{third-axis mechanism} = \text{selection-not-GC; not-wobble-clustered; CSC/positional identity needs external contact}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The axis-strength spectrum is also finite and panel-bound.  It computed $11$ organisms out of $16$, used $5$ folds, kept the $500$-protein minimum, and used $41$ synonymous-design columns.  Its descriptive spectrum is led on average by $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$, followed by $d_{\mathrm{tRNA}}$; the per-organism dominant count is $\{d_{\mathrm{resid4}}:11\}$.  The domain split is imbalanced, with $1$ archaeal organism, $2$ bacterial organisms, and $8$ eukaryotic organisms, so domain-level comparisons are descriptive rather than a separate universal law.  The largest reported GC3 rank effect is $d_{\mathrm{usage}}$ with $\rho=0.136$, which is small enough that the chapter keeps the axis statement as a controlled residual claim rather than a GC3 explanation.

\paragraph{Fourth-axis discrimination.}
The powered row does not by itself identify the fourth mechanism.  It only states that a three-axis explanation is insufficient on the finite panel.  Candidate follow-up rows narrow this statement without promoting it beyond their assays.  The mRNA-stability candidate reports $\text{fourth\_axis\_not\_mrna\_stability}$: in yeast, the $d_{\mathrm{opt\_halflife}}$ direction has low alignment with abundance $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$ under the fixed gates.  The elongation-dwell candidate is marked partial for \textit{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}.  The codon-pair candidate reports $\text{not\_codon\_pair}$ using $5$ folds, a $500$-protein minimum, $41$ synonymous-design columns, and $50$ shuffle-null replicates.  The translation-accuracy candidate reports $\text{not\_accuracy}$ with $11$ computed organisms out of $16$ requested organisms, $5$ folds, $41$ synonymous-design columns, and $200$ permutations.  These rows make the local interpretation asymmetric: the fourth component is present, but it is not licensed here as mRNA stability, codon-pair structure, translation accuracy, or a fully resolved elongation-dwell mechanism.

\paragraph{Bounded readout rule.}
The only permitted readback for this record is a controlled translation-facing survival entry for the same finite row system.  If $Q_{i}$ denotes a $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual coordinate and $T_{j}$ denotes a translation readout such as bounded tAI, stAI, ribosome occupancy, or translation efficiency, then the support condition has the form
$$
\begin{aligned}
S_{ij} > 0
\end{aligned}
$$
only after amino-acid composition, organism identity, GC3, CDS length, $M$-density, matched mRNA abundance where used, rank sufficiency, and simpler-codon baselines have been controlled.  A rank-degenerate all-$1.0$ surface on three organisms is a null boundary, not evidence of survival.  A modeled tAI or stAI surface can be used as a bounded translation-facing weight, but it is not the same thing as observed ribosome occupancy.  A ribosome-profiling or translation-efficiency row must be organism-matched and CDS-matched before it can support a measured survival statement.

\paragraph{Local cannot-claim boundary.}
The chapter proves no downstream biological realization.  The finite result says that, in the powered $B^{*}_{Q6}$ synonymous-residual panel, three optimality axes do not exhaust the controlled residual surface, and a fourth component remains.  It does not say that the $f_{3}$-stress coordinate changes protein abundance, folding, localization, stability, molecular function, organismal phenotype, or any global biological rule.  It also does not establish a mechanism by codon topology alone.  Those stronger conclusions require separate layer-matched contacts and their own controlled statistics.  Until a same-row translation readout supplies a non-vacuous positive $S_{ij}$ entry, the fourth-component result stays a finite residual-coordinate fact with a translation-facing test condition, not a claim about translation realization itself.
