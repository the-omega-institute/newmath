\subsection{Rank-powered three-axis insufficiency on the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ $f_3$-stress surface}
\label{sec:bioreality-q6-three-axis-sufficiency}

\paragraph{Finite coordinate surface.}
This record isolates the finite coding-sequence surface obtained after amino-acid projection, restricted to the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual coordinate carrying third-position $f_3$ stress.  The coordinate is not treated as a protein-level or organism-level claim.  It is a finite residual direction on synonymous codon rows, paired with organism-scoped codon usage, tRNA or wobble-weight summaries, and candidate translation readouts.  The support question is whether three axes are enough to span the observed optimality surface once the panel is rank-powered, or whether a fourth residual mechanism remains visible after the declared controls.

The $f_3$ saturation row fixes the local codon boundary.  In the supplied finite run, the median-$R$ union with ACA is
$$
\begin{aligned}
M &= \{\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{ACA},\mathrm{ACG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\},\\
\operatorname{Sat}_{f_3}(R) &= \{\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the $f_3$ boundary list is empty, while ACA and ACG mark the two codons present in $M$ but absent from the saturated $R$ row.  This is the finite row on which the later cross-layer test is allowed to speak: it supplies a residual codon-coordinate surface, not a biological conclusion by itself.

\paragraph{Three-axis test.}
The rank-powered sufficiency assay used $5$ folds, required at least $500$ proteins per organism, computed $11$ organisms out of $16$ requested organisms, and used $41$ synonymous-design columns.  Its deterministic seed was recorded as \texttt{sha256:b\_star\_q6\_optimality\_axis\_sufficiency\_powered:deterministic}.  The finite verdict of that assay is \texttt{fourth\_mechanism\_present}.  In local notation, if $d_{\mathrm{tRNA}}$, $d_{f_3}$, and $d_{\perp}$ denote the first three controlled residual axes, the fitted panel rejects the claim that these three axes exhaust the observed optimality surface:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\operatorname{span}(d_{\mathrm{tRNA}},d_{f_3},d_{\perp}) &\not\simeq \operatorname{Obs}_{Q6},\\
\operatorname{resid}\bigl(\operatorname{Obs}_{Q6}\mid d_{\mathrm{tRNA}},d_{f_3},d_{\perp}\bigr) &\neq 0
\end{aligned}
$$
within the finite assay boundary.  This statement is deliberately narrower than a mechanism claim: it says that the three-axis description is insufficient in the powered panel, not that the remaining axis has already been biologically identified.

\paragraph{Relation to the surrounding finite spectrum.}
The adjacent axis-spectrum run, also on $5$ folds with the same $500$-protein floor and $41$ synonymous-design columns, computed $11$ of $16$ requested organisms.  Its descriptive spectrum was led by \texttt{d\_resid4} on average, followed by \texttt{d\_tRNA}; all $11$ per-organism dominant calls were assigned to \texttt{d\_resid4}.  Domain means were reported only descriptively because the domain sample was imbalanced, with $1$ archaeal, $2$ bacterial, and $8$ eukaryotic organisms.  The largest GC3 rank effect in that spectrum was reported for \texttt{d\_usage}, with $\rho=0.136$.  These numbers are consistent with the sufficiency assay: the residual fourth direction is not a marginal label introduced after failure, but the dominant captured direction in the powered finite spectrum.

The two-axis closure assay supplies the preceding boundary.  It computed the same $11$ of $16$ organism panel, again with $5$ folds, a $500$-protein minimum, and $41$ synonymous-design columns, and returned \texttt{third\_axis\_present}.  Its reason was that $d_{\perp}$ had systematically positive held-out $R^2$ and that the $d_{\perp}$ directions were mutually same-signed across organisms.  The next residual-axis assay then reported that the non-tRNA residual optimality direction aligned most strongly with \texttt{f3\_translation\_selection}.  The third-axis identity assay provisionally identified $d_{\perp}$ with usage frequency within synonymous family, with mean cosine $0.128$ and one-sided sign-test $p=0.000488$.  The mechanism-side test retained cross-species alignment after GC orthogonalization and classified the third axis as selection-not-GC, not wobble-clustered, while leaving the CSC or positional identity as needing an additional contact.

\paragraph{Fourth-axis exclusions.}
The finite rows also constrain what the fourth direction is not.  The mRNA-stability identity assay returned \texttt{fourth\_axis\_not\_mrna\_stability}; its stated reason was that yeast \texttt{d\_opt\_halflife} had low alignment with abundance \texttt{d\_resid4} under the fixed gates.  The codon-pair assay returned \texttt{not\_codon\_pair} using $5$ folds, the $500$-protein floor, $41$ synonymous-design columns, and $50$ shuffle-null samples.  The translation-accuracy assay computed $11$ of $16$ organisms, used $200$ permutations, and returned \texttt{not\_accuracy}.  The elongation-dwell assay was more limited: in \textit{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}, with $5$ folds, it returned \texttt{partial}.  The finite classification is therefore not a single named mechanism.  It is a positive insufficiency result for the three-axis model, together with several bounded negative identifications and one partial dwell-time contact.

\paragraph{Readout boundary.}
The coordinate becomes a translation-readout claim only at rows where a controlled survival entry $S_{f_3\text{-}\mathrm{stress},T_j}$ is non-vacuous and positive for a bounded readout such as tAI, stAI, ribosome occupancy, or translation efficiency.  A three-organism all-$1.0$ controlled matrix is not treated as survival, because rank saturation is a null boundary.  Likewise, modeled tAI or stAI weights may provide a bounded codon-adaptation readback, but they do not by themselves observe ribosome occupancy.  The present finite result permits the statement that the powered optimality surface still contains a fourth residual direction after the three named axes are included.  It does not permit a claim about translation realization, folded structure, physical admissibility, molecular function, phenotype, mechanism, or universality unless a separate layer-matched contact supplies the corresponding measured rows and the declared controlled statistic is positive.

\paragraph{Local conclusion.}
Within this finite BioReality record, the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ $f_3$-stress surface is a rank-powered residual codon-coordinate assay.  The relevant powered sufficiency run passed and returned \texttt{fourth\_mechanism\_present} on $11$ computed organisms, $5$ folds, $41$ synonymous-design columns, and a $500$-protein organism floor.  The surrounding finite rows support the same local reading: two axes are insufficient, a third axis is present, the non-tRNA residual direction aligns with $f_3$ translation selection, $d_{\perp}$ has a provisional usage-frequency identity with $p=0.000488$, and the fourth captured direction is not reduced to mRNA stability, codon-pair structure, or translation accuracy in the supplied tests.  The result is therefore ready as a bounded, finite, cross-layer optimality record, with the higher biological interpretations held outside the claim until their own contacts are present.
