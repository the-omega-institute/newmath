The surrounding finite rows constrain how the insufficiency should be read. A separate boundary-set tRNA-poverty row used $40$ computed organisms out of $46$ requested, skipped $6$, used $1000$ permutations at $\alpha=0.05$, and concluded that $R$ sense codons are systematically tRNA-poor under GtRNAdb tGCN plus dos Reis wobble weights. These records justify treating wobble-weight asymmetry and tRNA supply as mandatory context for the $f_3$-stress question, but they do not by themselves create a measured ribosome-occupancy or translation-efficiency survival entry.

\paragraph{Fourth-axis exclusions.}
The finite follow-up rows narrow, but do not finish, the interpretation of the fourth component.  The mRNA-stability row passed with conclusion \texttt{fourth\_axis\_not\_mrna\_stability}; its stated reason was that yeast $d_{\mathrm{opt\_halflife}}$ had low alignment with the abundance-side $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$ under the fixed gates.  The codon-pair row passed with conclusion \texttt{not\_codon\_pair} using $50$ shuffle nulls.  The translation-accuracy row passed with conclusion \texttt{not\_accuracy}, using $200$ permutations on the $11/16$ organism panel.  The elongation-dwell row for \textit{Saccharomyces cerevisiae} returned \texttt{partial}.  The axis-strength spectrum row reported that captured spectra were led by $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$ on average, followed by $d_{\mathrm{tRNA}}$; its per-organism dominant count was $\{d_{\mathrm{resid4}}:11\}$, with domain counts imbalanced as $\{\mathrm{archaea}:1,\mathrm{bacteria}:2,\mathrm{eukaryote}:8\}$ and largest GC3 rank effect $\rho=0.136$ for $d_{\mathrm{usage}}$.  These finite rows support a fourth-component presence call while keeping its biological identity bounded.

\paragraph{Readout condition and local null.}
For this chapter, the only admissible bounded readout is a controlled survival entry $S_{f_3\text{-}\mathrm{stress},T_j}$ for a translation-facing row such as tAI, stAI, ribosome occupancy, or translation efficiency.  The row must survive amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, CDS length, $M$-density, matched mRNA, rank, and simpler-baseline controls.  In symbols, a positive translation-facing entry would have to satisfy
$$
\begin{aligned}
S_{f_3\text{-}\mathrm{stress},T_j}>0
\end{aligned}
$$
on a nondegenerate matched panel.  The present finite record does not contain same-organism and same-CDS joined codon, wobble-weight, measured ribosome or translation-efficiency, and matched mRNA rows for this exact subject.  Consequently, the three-axis insufficiency result is a powered residual-geometry result, not a measured translation-survival result.

\paragraph{Claim boundary.}
The chapter therefore records a precise finite conclusion: the powered $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ optimal residual surface is not closed by the first three axes on the $11$-organism computed panel, and the remaining component is not explained by the tested mRNA-stability, codon-pair, or translation-accuracy alternatives, with elongation dwell only partial in the yeast row.  No claim about translation realization, ribosome occupancy, protein abundance, folded structure, physical admissibility, biological function, mechanism, phenotype, or universality follows from this chapter alone.  Each such assertion requires its own layer-matched contact and its own controlled statistic.  The current record is strongest as a finite, falsifiable direction for the next panel: attach matched translation-readout rows and ask whether $S_{f_3\text{-}\mathrm{stress},T_j}$ remains positive after the declared controls, or whether the fourth component collapses at the translation boundary.
