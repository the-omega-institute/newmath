\subsection{Rank-powered insufficiency of the three-axis B*\_Q6 translation surface}\label{sec:bioreality-q6-three-axis-sufficiency}\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite coordinate surface.} The object tested here is the finite CDS-by-organism residual coordinate surface obtained after amino-acid projection, with attention restricted to the $f_3$-stress coordinate of $B^*_{Q6}$ and to translation-layer rows such as tAI, stAI, ribosome occupancy, or translation efficiency. The finite codon support used by the saturation record is explicit. The union $M=\mathrm{Med}(R)\cup\{\mathrm{ACA}\}$ is
$$\begin{aligned}
M=\{&\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{ACA},\mathrm{ACG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\\
&\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\}.
\end{aligned}$$
The recorded $f_3$ saturation removes the ACA/ACG pair from that displayed carrier:
$$\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{Sat}_{f_3}(R)=\{&\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\\
&\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\},
\end{aligned}$$
and the listed $f_3$ boundary set is empty. Thus the chapter is not asking whether an unconstrained codon list can be made to vary; it asks whether a residual coordinate left after the amino-acid quotient has enough rank and readout contact to survive as a translation-layer distinction.

\paragraph{Powered three-axis test.} The finite powered assay for the three-axis sufficiency question was run as experiment $\mathrm{b\_star\_q6\_optimality\_axis\_sufficiency\_powered}$ with deterministic seed $\mathrm{sha256{:}b\_star\_q6\_optimality\_axis\_sufficiency\_powered{:}deterministic}$. It computed $11$ organisms out of $16$ requested organisms, used $5$ folds, required at least $500$ proteins per organism, and used $41$ synonymous-design columns. Its recorded status is passed, and its conclusion is
$$\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{three\mbox{-}axis\ sufficiency}\quad \longmapsto \quad \mathrm{fourth\_mechanism\_present}.
\end{aligned}$$
This is a negative sufficiency result, not a downstream biological promotion. The first three axes carry real finite structure, but the powered surface says that the three-axis span does not exhaust the controlled residual readout geometry. The appropriate scientific statement is therefore: the $B^*_{Q6}$ residual surface contains a further direction that remains visible under the declared finite design, rather than being closed by the tRNA and $f_3$ components alone.

\paragraph{Axis evidence surrounding the failure of sufficiency.} The linked two-axis closure test used the same scale of $11$ computed organisms out of $16$, $5$ folds, a minimum of $500$ proteins per organism, and $41$ synonymous-design columns. Its conclusion was $\mathrm{third\_axis\_present}$, with the reason that the perpendicular direction has systematically positive held-out $R^2$ and mutually same-signed directions across organisms. The separate identity test for that perpendicular direction again used $11/16$ organisms, $5$ folds, the $500$-protein floor, and $41$ synonymous-design columns; it identified $d_\perp$ with usage frequency within family, with mean cosine $0.128$ and one-sided sign-test $p=0.000488$. A mechanism-side assay then recorded $\mathrm{selection\mbox{-}not\mbox{-}GC}$ and $\mathrm{not\mbox{-}wobble\mbox{-}clustered}$, while leaving the CSC/positional identity as requiring further contact. These rows make the sufficiency verdict interpretable: a third axis exists and has a provisional usage-frequency identity, yet the optimality surface still reports a fourth mechanism.

\paragraph{Spectrum and tRNA boundary.} The per-organism axis-strength spectrum gives a consistent finite picture of which direction dominates the available powered panel. It computed $11$ organisms out of $16$, again with $5$ folds, a $500$-protein minimum, and $41$ synonymous-design columns. The captured spectra are led by $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$ on average, followed by $d_{\mathrm{tRNA}}$, and the per-organism dominant count is $\{d_{\mathrm{resid4}}:11\}$. The domain means are descriptive only because the panel is imbalanced, with $n=\{\mathrm{archaea}:1,\mathrm{bacteria}:2,\mathrm{eukaryote}:8\}$; the largest GC3 rank effect is $d_{\mathrm{usage}}$ with $\rho=0.136$. A separate tRNA-poverty assay over the $R$ sense codons computed $40$ organisms out of $46$ requested, skipped $6$, used $1000$ permutations at $\alpha=0.05$, and called the $R$ sense codons systematically tRNA-poor under GtRNAdb tGCN plus dos Reis wobble weights. These data support a tRNA-supply pressure at the boundary, but they do not absorb the fourth direction reported by the three-axis optimality test.

\paragraph{Translation-readout gate.} The bounded readback for this chapter is a controlled entry $S_{f3\mbox{-}\mathrm{stress},T_j}$ for a translation row $T_j$, where $T_j$ may be tAI, stAI, ribosome occupancy, or translation efficiency, and where the controls include amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, CDS length, M-density, and matched mRNA when the readout is a translation-efficiency row. The present finite facts establish a powered residual-axis result and a fourth-mechanism call; they do not, by themselves, supply a same-CDS measured ribosome or TE matrix for the exact $f_3$-stress row. The local admissible implication is therefore
$$\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{passed\ three\mbox{-}axis\ assay}+\mathrm{fourth\_mechanism\_present}
&\Rightarrow \mathrm{three\mbox{-}axis\ span\ insufficient\ on\ the\ powered\ residual\ surface},\\
&
Rightarrow \mathrm{measured\ translation\ survival\ without\ a\ matched\ readout\ row}.
\end{aligned}$$
A positive translation claim would require a non-vacuous controlled $S_{f3\mbox{-}\mathrm{stress},T_j}>0$ under the declared controls and baseline ablations. A rank-degenerate or all-constant surface is a null boundary, even if its notation resembles a survival matrix.

\paragraph{Local boundary.} No claim about translation realization, ribosome dynamics, protein abundance, folded structure, physical admissibility, molecular function, phenotype, mechanism, or universality follows from this chapter alone. Codon-usage rows, tRNA-copy rows, and modelled tAI or stAI weights can bound a translation-layer readout, but they are not observed ribosome occupancy and they are not protein output. The chapter records a finite rank-powered insufficiency result for the $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinate system: the three-axis description is not enough, the fourth direction is present in the powered assay, and any movement from that residual fact to translation or higher biology must pass through a separate layer-matched contact with its own controlled statistic.
