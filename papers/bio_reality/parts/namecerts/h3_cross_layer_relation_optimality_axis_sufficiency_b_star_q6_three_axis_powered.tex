\subsection{Three-axis insufficiency for the $B^*_{Q6}$ translation surface}
\label{sec:bioreality-q6-three-axis-sufficiency}

\paragraph{Finite object.}
This record concerns a finite codon-coordinate surface restricted to the $B^*_{Q6}$ synonymous residual basis, with special attention to the $f_3$-stress direction after quotienting by amino-acid identity. The codon carrier used by the saturation row is not an open-ended genetic-code narrative: it is the explicit twenty-codon set
$\{\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{ACA},\mathrm{ACG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\}$.
The corresponding $f_3$ saturation row removes exactly $\mathrm{ACA}$ and $\mathrm{ACG}$, leaving the eighteen-codon surface
$\{\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\}$.
The recorded $f_3$ boundary is empty. Thus the coordinate under discussion is a finite residual surface, not a continuous latent variable and not a direct biological measurement.

\paragraph{Rank-powered optimality test.}
The powered sufficiency assay for the three named axes was run as the experiment $\mathrm{b\_star\_q6\_optimality\_axis\_sufficiency\_powered}$ with five folds, at least $500$ proteins per organism, and $41$ synonymous design columns. It computed $11$ organisms out of $16$ requested organisms and passed with the conclusion $\mathrm{fourth\_mechanism\_present}$. In the notation of the finite rows, the verdict is
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{status} &= \mathrm{passed},\\
\mathrm{organisms\ computed} &= 11,\\
\mathrm{organisms\ requested} &= 16,\\
\mathrm{folds} &= 5,\\
\mathrm{synonymous\ design\ columns} &= 41,\\
\mathrm{conclusion} &= \mathrm{fourth\ mechanism\ present}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The point is negative for sufficiency: the three-axis span is not treated as closed under the observed residual readback. It is also positive for search direction: after the universal two-axis closure row reports a third axis, the three-axis sufficiency row still requires a fourth component on the same controlled finite surface.

\paragraph{Relation to the neighboring axis rows.}
The axis-strength spectrum was computed on the same $11$ of $16$ organism panel, again with five folds, $500$ minimum proteins per organism, and $41$ synonymous design columns. Its captured spectra are led by $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$ on average, followed by $d_{\mathrm{tRNA}}$; the per-organism dominance count is $\{d_{\mathrm{resid4}}:11\}$. The domain means are descriptive only because the panel is imbalanced, with $1$ archaeal, $2$ bacterial, and $8$ eukaryotic organisms. The largest reported GC3 rank effect is $\rho=0.136$ for $d_{\mathrm{usage}}$.
The prior two-axis closure row also computed $11$ of $16$ organisms and concluded $\mathrm{third\_axis\_present}$, with the reason that $d_\perp$ has systematically positive held-out $R^2$ and same-signed directions across organisms. The identity row then aligns $d_\perp$ to usage frequency within family, with mean cosine $0.128$ and one-sided sign-test $p=0.000488$. The mechanism row keeps the $d_\perp$ signal after GC orthogonalization and classifies it as selection-not-GC and not wobble-clustered, while leaving the CSC or positional identity dependent on additional contact rows. These linked rows make the sufficiency result sharp: the failure is not a failure to find a third direction, but a failure of the first three controlled directions to exhaust the organism-level synonymous residual surface.

\paragraph{$f_3$-stress readout rule.}
The $f_3$-stress coordinate becomes biologically interpretable only through a bounded translation row. A valid readout has the form $S_{f3\text{-}stress,T_j}$, where $T_j$ is a tAI, stAI, ribosome-occupancy, or translation-efficiency surface and where the row is estimated after amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, CDS length, $M$-density, mRNA abundance when applicable, rank, and simpler-baseline controls. The local support condition is therefore not that the codon topology is elegant, and not that a wobble model can be written down. The local support condition is a non-vacuous, non-rank-degenerate survival entry:
$$
\begin{aligned}
S_{f3\text{-}stress,T_j} &> 0,\\
\mathrm{rank}(T_j \mid Z) &> \mathrm{null\ boundary},\\
w_c &\neq w_{c^{f_3}}
\end{aligned}
$$
for at least one controlled translation readout $T_j$ in a powered panel. If the controlled surface remains non-positive, or if the fitted matrix collapses to an all-$1.0$ rank-saturated boundary on a three-organism panel, the correct interpretation is refusal rather than survival.

\paragraph{Fourth-mechanism exclusions.}
The finite rows also constrain what the fourth component is not allowed to become by assertion. The codon-pair assay passed with conclusion $\mathrm{not\_codon\_pair}$, using five folds, $500$ minimum proteins per organism, $41$ synonymous design columns, and $50$ shuffle-null samples. The translation-accuracy assay computed $11$ of $16$ organisms, used $200$ permutations, and concluded $\mathrm{not\_accuracy}$. The mRNA-stability assay passed with conclusion $\mathrm{fourth\_axis\_not\_mrna\_stability}$ because the yeast $d_{\mathrm{opt\_halflife}}$ direction has low alignment with abundance $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$ under the fixed gates. The elongation-dwell row is weaker: it reports $\mathrm{partial}$ in $\mathrm{Saccharomyces\ cerevisiae}$ under the five-fold assay. Thus the fourth component is present in the powered residual geometry, but these rows do not license a single resolved mechanism. They rule out several tempting promotions and leave the positive identity bounded by the available rows.

\paragraph{Local boundary.}
This chapter records an ai-discovered vision-level finite surface whose current strongest claim is three-axis insufficiency for $B^*_{Q6}$ under controlled synonymous design. It does not say that $f_3$-stress causes translation output. It does not say that a modeled tAI or stAI row is observed ribosome occupancy. It does not say that the residual coordinate determines protein abundance, folding, structural order, physical admissibility, molecular function, localization, stability, phenotype, mechanism, or universality. Each such higher layer requires its own matched contact and its own statistic. The present result is narrower and cleaner: on the finite powered panel, the three-axis optimality basis is insufficient, a fourth residual component is present, and any biological promotion must first pass through a controlled, nondegenerate translation-readout survival row.
