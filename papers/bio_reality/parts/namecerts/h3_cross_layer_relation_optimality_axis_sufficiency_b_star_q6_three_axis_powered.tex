This supports a tRNA-supply interpretation for R avoidance, but it does not identify the fourth residual direction by itself.  The powered fourth-mechanism assays further narrow the local reading: mRNA stability was excluded as the dominant fourth-axis identity in the yeast half-life alignment test, codon-pair structure was called $\mathrm{not\_codon\_pair}$ with $50$ shuffle-null draws, translation accuracy was called $\mathrm{not\_accuracy}$ using $200$ permutations across $11$ computed organisms, and elongation dwell gave only a partial call in $\mathrm{saccharomyces\_cerevisiae}$.  Consequently, the chapter records a real finite insufficiency of the three-axis model, while withholding a completed biological identity for the fourth direction.
