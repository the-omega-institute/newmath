\subsection{A fourth translation-facing coordinate beyond the three-axis residual basis}
\label{sec:bioreality-q6-optimality-axis-sufficiency}

\paragraph{Finite object.}
The record considered here is a finite coding-sequence residual surface on the $B^*_{Q6}$ coordinate system, restricted to synonymous variation after amino-acid projection and organized around the $f_3$-stress coordinate.  The row object is not a claim about proteins, phenotypes, or universal biological law.  It is a controlled finite panel asking whether a three-axis explanation of $B^*_{Q6}$ translation-facing residuals is sufficient, or whether a further axis is required once organism, amino-acid composition, GC3, coding-sequence length, $M$-density, and simpler codon baselines are accounted for.

The finite $f_3$ saturation row fixes the codon support on which this question is posed.  The ambient marked set was
$$
\begin{aligned}
M = \{&AAA,AAG,ACA,ACG,AGA,AGG,AUA,AUG,CUA,CUC,CUG,CUU,\\
&UAA,UAG,UCA,UCG,UGA,UGG,UUA,UUG\},
\end{aligned}
$$
while the $f_3$-saturated residual set removed the two $ACA/ACG$ boundary entries and gave
$$
\begin{aligned}
\operatorname{Sat}_{f_3}(R)=\{&AAA,AAG,AGA,AGG,AUA,AUG,CUA,CUC,CUG,CUU,\\
&UAA,UAG,UCA,UCG,UGA,UGG,UUA,UUG\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The recorded boundary difference was empty, $\partial_{f_3}=\varnothing$, so the row is a saturated finite-coordinate test rather than a boundary-leakage event.  The distinction carried by the row is specifically the survival of synonymous residual structure after amino-acid quotienting; the row does not license interpretation of folded structure, localization, stability, molecular function, organismal phenotype, physical admissibility, mechanism, or universality.

\paragraph{Three-axis null.}
The tested null hypothesis says that the translation-facing residual surface is exhausted by the already identified three-axis span: the tRNA-adaptation direction, the $f_3$ translation-selection residual direction, and the perpendicular third direction.  Under this null, adding a fourth coordinate should not improve held-out description of the organism-level synonymous design once the declared controls are imposed.  In finite-row notation, if $Q$ denotes the residual coordinate surface and $T$ denotes a bounded translation-facing readout, the admissible comparison is only the controlled increment
$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta_4
  &= \operatorname{Score}(T \mid Z,Q_{1},Q_{2},Q_{3},Q_{4})
     - \operatorname{Score}(T \mid Z,Q_{1},Q_{2},Q_{3}),
\end{aligned}
$$
where $Z$ includes amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, coding-sequence length, $M$-density, and simpler synonymous baselines.  The finite experiment for this row was run with $5$ folds, a minimum of $500$ proteins per organism, $41$ synonymous-design columns, $16$ requested organisms, and $11$ organisms computed.  The deterministic seed was $\mathrm{sha256{:}b\_star\_q6\_optimality\_axis\_sufficiency\_powered{:}deterministic}$.  The recorded status was passed, and the finite conclusion was
$$
\begin{aligned}
\text{three-axis sufficiency} = \text{false},\qquad
\text{finite call} = \text{fourth mechanism present}.
\end{aligned}
$$
This is a scoped statistical statement about the finite organism panel and its controlled synonymous design.  It does not identify the fourth mechanism by itself.

\paragraph{Relation to the surrounding finite spectrum.}
The same audit packet contains the adjacent spectrum needed to keep the fourth-axis statement narrow.  The per-organism axis-strength experiment used $5$ folds, the same $500$-protein minimum, $41$ synonymous-design columns, $16$ requested organisms, and $11$ computed organisms.  Its descriptive spectrum was led by $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$ on average, followed by $d_{\mathrm{tRNA}}$, and all $11$ per-organism dominant counts were assigned to $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$.  The domain split was imbalanced, with $n=1$ archaeal, $n=2$ bacterial, and $n=8$ eukaryotic organisms, so the domain means are not promoted to a domain law.  The largest reported GC3 rank effect was $\rho=0.136$ for $d_{\mathrm{usage}}$, which is small enough to keep the fourth-axis call separate from a simple GC3 explanation.

The two-axis closure test also used $5$ folds, $41$ synonymous-design columns, $16$ requested organisms, and $11$ computed organisms.  It returned the finite conclusion $\text{third\_axis\_present}$, with the reason that $d_{\perp}$ had systematically positive held-out $R^2$ and mutually same-signed directions across organisms.  The third-axis identity row then identified $d_{\perp}$ provisionally with usage frequency within synonymous family, reporting mean cosine $0.128$ and one-sided sign-test $p=0.000488$.  A separate mechanism row kept the third axis GC-orthogonalized and called it selection-not-GC, not wobble-clustered, with the CSC or positional identity still requiring an additional measured contact.  These rows matter because the fourth-axis call is not being confused with the already recorded third-axis residual.

\paragraph{Mechanism exclusions and non-exclusions.}
The finite row says that a fourth mechanism is present relative to the three-axis baseline, but the available mechanism tests do not turn that statement into a completed causal identification.  The mRNA-stability assay concluded $\text{fourth\_axis\_not\_mRNA\_stability}$ because the yeast $d_{\mathrm{opt\_halflife}}$ alignment with abundance $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$ was low under the fixed gates.  The elongation-dwell assay was run in \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae} with $5$ folds and returned $\text{partial}$.  The codon-pair assay used $5$ folds, $41$ synonymous-design columns, the $500$-protein minimum, and $50$ shuffle-null draws, and returned $\text{not\_codon\_pair}$.  The translation-accuracy assay used $5$ folds, $41$ synonymous-design columns, $16$ requested organisms, $11$ computed organisms, and $200$ permutations, and returned $\text{not\_accuracy}$.  Thus the row supports the existence of a fourth finite residual axis under the declared design, while excluding several candidate identities in the tested forms and leaving the remaining identity bounded by the contact actually measured.

The tRNA-poverty contact is also relevant but not decisive for this row.  The boundary-set experiment used $40$ computed organisms out of $46$ requested, skipped $6$, used $1000$ permutations at $\alpha=0.05$, and recorded that $R$ sense codons are systematically tRNA-poor under GtRNAdb tGCN plus dos Reis wobble weights.  This supports a tRNA-supply interpretation for $R$ avoidance, but it is not by itself the fourth-axis identity and it is not an observed ribosome-occupancy measurement.

\paragraph{Readout boundary.}
The admissible biological readback is confined to a translation-facing row such as tAI, stAI, ribosome occupancy, or translation efficiency, and only after the relevant same-organism or same-CDS controls have been joined.  Modeled tAI or stAI weights may be used as bounded codon-adaptation readouts; they are not observed ribosome footprints.  Ribosome-profiling or translation-efficiency contacts would be stronger, but only if they are identifier-matched to coding sequences and paired with matched mRNA abundance so that a translation-efficiency residual is computed rather than a transcript-abundance association.

Accordingly, the row has the following local no-promotion rule.  A positive fourth-axis call does not entail translation realization unless a controlled translation-readout statistic is supplied.  It does not entail folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, localization, stability, protein abundance, phenotype, mechanism, causation, or universality unless a separate layer-matched contact supplies that layer and the declared controls are applied.  The present finite result is therefore a powered insufficiency result for the three-axis residual model: in the computed panel, the three-axis description does not exhaust the controlled $B^*_{Q6}$ synonymous residual surface, and the residual spectrum requires a fourth translation-facing coordinate whose biological interpretation remains bounded by the available readout rows.
