\paragraph{Three-axis sufficiency test.}
The powered optimality-axis experiment was run with $5$ folds, at least $500$ proteins per organism, $41$ synonymous design columns, and $11$ computed organisms out of $16$ requested organisms. Its recorded status is passed, with deterministic seed $\mathrm{sha256{:}b\_star\_q6\_optimality\_axis\_sufficiency\_powered{:}deterministic}$, and the finite conclusion is that a fourth mechanism is present. In symbols, if the fitted three-axis span is denoted by $U_3$ and the held-out residual component by $d_4$, the empirical decision is not that $U_3$ closes the surface, but that the held-out residual remains nontrivial under the powered design:
$$

This is the central finite-row content of the chapter. The three-axis model is strong enough to identify structured residual behavior, but not sufficient to exhaust the $B^*_{Q6}$ optimality surface.

\paragraph{Surrounding powered rows.}
The insufficiency call is coherent with the neighboring finite rows. The two-axis closure experiment, also on $11$ computed organisms out of $16$ requested with $5$ folds, reports a third axis present; its reason states that $d_\perp$ has systematically positive held-out $R^2$ and same-signed directions across organisms. These rows place the three-axis insufficiency call in a finite spectrum rather than in a single isolated fit.

\paragraph{Mechanistic narrowing without promotion.}
The available mechanism-discrimination rows make the fourth component more constrained, but not biologically promoted. The boundary-set tRNA-poverty experiment computed $40$ organisms out of $46$ requested, skipped $6$, used $1000$ permutations at $\alpha=0.05$, and passed with the call that $R$ sense codons are systematically tRNA-poor under GtRNAdb tGCN plus dos Reis wobble weights. The fourth-mechanism rows then reject several proposed identities: the mRNA-stability assay calls $\mathrm{fourth\_axis\_not\_mRNA\_stability}$ because yeast $d_{\rm opt\_halflife}$ has low alignment with abundance $d_{\rm resid4}$ under the fixed gates; the codon-pair assay calls $\mathrm{not\_codon\_pair}$ using $50$ shuffle nulls; the translation-accuracy assay calls $\mathrm{not\_accuracy}$ using $200$ permutations on $11$ computed organisms out of $16$ requested; and the elongation-dwell assay on \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae} calls only $\mathrm{partial}$. The resulting finite statement is therefore narrow: a fourth component is present in the powered $B^*_{Q6}$ optimality surface, but the recorded discriminants do not identify it as mRNA stability, codon-pair preference, translation accuracy, or a complete elongation-dwell explanation.

\paragraph{Readout boundary.}
The biological readback allowed here is bounded. Codon-usage tables, tRNA gene-copy counts, and tAI or stAI weights can support finite codon-adaptation and wobble-asymmetry rows. Ribosome profiling or translation-efficiency data, if joined to the same organism and CDS rows, could test a controlled translation-readout survival entry. In this packet, however, the same-row ribosome or TE matrix for the $f_3$-stress coordinate is not present. Therefore the valid conclusion is a powered finite-coordinate statement about the insufficiency of three axes and the presence of a fourth residual component. No claim follows here about measured translation efficiency, ribosome occupancy, protein abundance, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, phenotype, mechanism in the causal sense, or universality. Any such assertion would require its own layer-matched contact and a recorded controlled statistic.

\paragraph{Local falsification rule.}
Let $q_{f_3}$ denote the $f_3$-stress residual coordinate and let $T_j$ denote a bounded translation readout such as tAI, stAI, ribosome occupancy, or translation efficiency. The desired positive readback would be a controlled survival entry $S_{q_{f_3},T_j}>0$ after amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, CDS length, M-density, mRNA, rank, and simpler-baseline controls. The present finite rows do not supply that entry. The chapter is therefore falsifiable at the readout interface: if a powered same-row panel keeps every admissible $S_{q_{f_3},T_j}$ non-positive, or if the surface is rank-degenerate as in an all-$1.0$ three-organism matrix, the translation-readout interpretation is refused even though the internal residual geometry and powered fourth-axis call remain recorded.
