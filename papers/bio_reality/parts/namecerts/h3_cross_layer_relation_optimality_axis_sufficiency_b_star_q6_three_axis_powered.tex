\subsection{Three-axis insufficiency for the powered $B^{*}_{Q6}$ panel}
\label{sec:bioreality-q6-three-axis-insufficiency}

\paragraph{Finite object.}
This record concerns a finite CDS-by-organism residual coordinate surface after amino-acid projection, restricted to the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ synonymous-coordinate family and, more sharply, to the $f_{3}$-stress direction where third-position pairing can be broken by context-specific wobble or tRNA weights. The finite saturated codon row has
$$
\begin{aligned}
M &= \{\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{ACA},\mathrm{ACG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\\
&\qquad \mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\},
\end{aligned}
$$
with $20$ listed codons. Its $f_{3}$ saturation removes the two-row pair $\{\mathrm{ACA},\mathrm{ACG}\}$ and leaves $18$ codons; the recorded $f_{3}$ boundary list is empty. Thus the row is not being introduced as an open-ended codon vocabulary. It is a finite residual coordinate packet whose admissible stress has already been filtered through the amino-acid quotient and the stated saturation operation.

\paragraph{Powered axis test.}
The optimality-axis sufficiency assay is recorded under experiment \texttt{b\_star\_q6\_optimality\_axis\_sufficiency\_powered}, run \texttt{2436cae9d455}. It used $5$ folds, required at least $500$ proteins per organism, computed $11$ organisms from $16$ requested organisms, and used $41$ synonymous-design columns. The status is \texttt{passed}, and the finite conclusion field is \texttt{fourth\_mechanism\_present}. Read literally, this is not a promotion to a biochemical cause. It says that the tested three-axis description does not exhaust the held-out residual structure of the powered $B^{*}_{Q6}$ surface under the stated design. In symbols, the observed finite-row verdict is the inequality of explanatory closure,
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{span}(d_{\mathrm{tRNA}}, d_{f_{3}}, d_{\perp}) \;\not\equiv\; \mathrm{held\mbox{-}out\ }B^{*}_{Q6}\mathrm{\ residual\ surface}
\end{aligned}
$$
for this powered panel and this feature construction. The word ``present'' is therefore interpreted as an additional residual axis requirement in the statistical coordinate system, not as an identified cellular process.

\paragraph{Relation to the lower-axis rows.}
The two-axis closure row independently reports \texttt{third\_axis\_present} on the same scale of $5$ folds, $11$ computed organisms out of $16$, at the $500$-protein threshold and $41$ synonymous columns. The residual-axis row identifies the dominant non-tRNA residual direction as \texttt{f3\_translation\_selection}, again on $11/16$ organisms, $5$ folds, and $41$ columns. The third-axis identity row then aligns $d_{\perp}$ to usage frequency within codon family, with mean cosine $0.128$ and one-sided sign-test $p=0.000488$. These three rows make the present sufficiency result more specific: it is not merely that a tRNA axis is too small, and not merely that an $f_{3}$ direction must be added. Even after the tRNA, $f_{3}$, and third usage-frequency-like directions have been separated, the powered held-out surface still records an additional component.

\paragraph{Spectrum and boundary evidence.}
The per-organism spectrum row reports that captured spectra are led on average by $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$, followed by $d_{\mathrm{tRNA}}$, with dominant-count summary $\{d_{\mathrm{resid4}}:11\}$ over the $11$ computed organisms. The same row gives the domain balance as $1$ archaeal, $2$ bacterial, and $8$ eukaryotic organisms, so domain means remain descriptive rather than population-level biological laws. Its largest GC3 rank effect is the $d_{\mathrm{usage}}$ value $\rho=0.136$. Separately, the boundary-set row tests the $R$ sense codons under tRNA gene copy and dos Reis wobble weights over $40$ computed organisms from $46$ requested organisms, with $6$ skipped organisms, $1000$ permutations, and $\alpha=0.05$. That row supports systematic tRNA poverty for $R$ sense codons. These numbers justify treating the extra axis as a real finite-row demand inside the assay, while still keeping the result conditional on the sampled organisms, design matrix, and modeled readout surfaces.

\paragraph{Negative identity filters.}
The auxiliary fourth-component identity probes narrow the interpretation. The mRNA-stability row concludes \texttt{fourth\_axis\_not\_mrna\_stability}: the yeast half-life alignment is low enough under the fixed gates to exclude mRNA stability as the dominant identity in that assay. The codon-pair row concludes \texttt{not\_codon\_pair} with $5$ folds, $41$ synonymous columns, and $50$ shuffle-null draws. The translation-accuracy row concludes \texttt{not\_accuracy} over $11$ computed organisms from $16$ requested, with $200$ permutations and $41$ synonymous columns. The elongation-dwell row is weaker: in \textit{Saccharomyces cerevisiae} it reports \texttt{partial}. Thus the finite state is neither a universal biological mechanism nor a named fourth process. It is a constrained residual fact: three axes are insufficient, several candidate identities are rejected or only partial, and the surviving row should be treated as an unresolved additional coordinate demand.

\paragraph{Translation-readout boundary.}
The $f_{3}$-stress conjecture becomes a translation-layer statement only when a same-organism or same-CDS readout row is present. The required bounded readback is a controlled entry $S_{f3\text{-}stress,T_j}$ for tAI, stAI, ribosome occupancy, or translation efficiency, after amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, CDS length, $M$-density, matched mRNA, rank, and simpler-baseline controls. A modeled tAI or stAI surface can supply a bounded translation-weight contact; it is not by itself an observed ribosome-footprint row. Likewise, codon usage, tRNA-copy spectra, and wobble weights do not imply protein abundance, protein structure, localization, stability, molecular function, phenotype, or a general biological rule.

\paragraph{Local verdict.}
The finite-row conclusion is that the powered $B^{*}_{Q6}$ optimality surface requires more than the tRNA, $f_{3}$, and third usage-frequency-like axes. The strongest supported wording is: in the present rank-powered panel, the three-axis model is insufficient, and a fourth residual component is detected by the held-out design. The chapter does not assert translation realization unless a controlled $S_{f3\text{-}stress,T_j}$ row is non-vacuous and positive. It does not assert folding, physical admissibility, biological function, causal mechanism, phenotype, or universality without a separate layer-matched contact and statistic.
