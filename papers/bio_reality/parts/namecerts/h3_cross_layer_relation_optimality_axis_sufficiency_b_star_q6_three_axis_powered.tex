\subsection{Three-axis insufficiency for the $B^*_{Q6}$ translation-readout surface}
\label{sec:bioreality-q6-three-axis-insufficiency}

\paragraph{Finite coordinate under test.}
The row studied here is the finite CDS-by-organism residual coordinate surface obtained after amino-acid projection and then restricted to the $f_3$-stress component of the $B^*_{Q6}$ coordinate family.  Its intended readout is deliberately narrow: the only admissible biological readback is a controlled survival entry $S_{f3\text{-}stress,T_j}$ for a translation-layer row such as tAI, stAI, ribosome occupancy, or translation efficiency.  The coordinate is not interpreted as protein abundance, protein structure, localization, stability, molecular function, organismal phenotype, or a general biological law.  Those layers require their own matched contacts and their own controlled statistics.

\paragraph{Saturation boundary.}
The finite $f_3$ saturation computation fixes the codon support on which the later cross-layer rows are allowed to speak.  The ambient marked set is
$$\begin{aligned}
M={}&\{AAA,AAG,ACA,ACG,AGA,AGG,AUA,AUG,CUA,CUC,CUG,CUU,\\
&\quad UAA,UAG,UCA,UCG,UGA,UGG,UUA,UUG\},
\end{aligned}$$
while the saturated $f_3$ image removes the two $ACA/ACG$ entries:
$$\begin{aligned}
\operatorname{Sat}_{f_3}(R)={}&\{AAA,AAG,AGA,AGG,AUA,AUG,CUA,CUC,CUG,CUU,\\
&\quad UAA,UAG,UCA,UCG,UGA,UGG,UUA,UUG\}.
\end{aligned}$$
The recorded boundary set is empty, so the finite row contains no additional $f_3$ boundary codons beyond this displayed difference.  This matters because the later claim is not that every synonymous codon contrast is dynamically active; the test concerns whether the surviving residual axes are sufficient once codon usage, tRNA or wobble weighting, and translation-readout rows are brought into the same finite design.

\paragraph{Three-axis result.}
The powered optimality-axis sufficiency run returned status $passed$ with conclusion $fourth\_mechanism\_present$.  The experiment used $5$ folds, required at least $500$ proteins per organism, computed $11$ organisms out of $16$ requested organisms, and used $41$ synonymous-design columns.  In the notation of the row, the intended three-axis span can be summarized as a tRNA-adaptation axis, an $f_3$ translation-selection residual axis, and a third orthogonal residual component $d_\perp$.  The finite result says that this span is not sufficient for the observed optimality surface in the powered panel:
$$\begin{aligned}
\operatorname{span}(d_{tRNA},d_{f_3},d_{\perp})
  &\not\equiv \operatorname{all\ retained\ optimality\ signal},\\
\operatorname{status} &= passed,\\
\operatorname{conclusion} &= fourth\_mechanism\_present.
\end{aligned}$$
The seed was deterministic, recorded as $sha256{:}b\_star\_q6\_optimality\_axis\_sufficiency\_powered{:}deterministic$, so the row is a finite reproducibility claim about this specified design rather than an open-ended biological assertion.

\paragraph{Placement among neighboring finite rows.}
The insufficiency call is not isolated.  The two-axis closure run, also using $5$ folds, at least $500$ proteins per organism, $11$ computed organisms out of $16$, and $41$ synonymous-design columns, concluded $third\_axis\_present$.  The residual-axis row then identified the dominant non-tRNA residual direction as $f3\_translation\_selection$ on the same $11/16$ powered organism panel.  The third-axis identity row reported that $d_\perp$ is provisionally aligned to usage frequency within family, with mean cosine $0.128$ and one-sided sign-test $p=0.000488$.  A separate third-axis mechanism row kept this direction GC-orthogonalized and concluded $selection\text{-}not\text{-}GC;\ not\text{-}wobble\text{-}clustered$, while marking CSC or positional identity as needing an outside assay.  Together these rows justify a precise finite reading: the three displayed axes are real within the powered residual design, but they do not exhaust the optimality surface.

\paragraph{Spectrum evidence for the missing direction.}
The per-organism strength spectrum gives the strongest local clue about the extra component.  Across the $11$ computed organisms, the captured spectra were led on average by $d_{resid4}$, followed by $d_{tRNA}$, and the per-organism dominance count was $\{d_{resid4}:11\}$.  The same row cautions that domain means are descriptive only because the domain counts were imbalanced, with $n=\{archaea:1,bacteria:2,eukaryote:8\}$.  The largest GC3 rank effect was small, $\rho=0.136$ for $d_{usage}$.  Thus the fourth component is not licensed as a universal mechanism, but it is a stable finite feature of the current powered matrix: every computed organism in the panel assigned dominance to the residual-fourth direction under the recorded spectrum calculation.

\paragraph{Mechanistic exclusions already available.}
Several candidate identities for the fourth component are already bounded by finite rows.  The mRNA-stability run concluded $fourth\_axis\_not\_mrna\_stability$: in yeast, $d_{opt\_halflife}$ had low alignment with abundance $d_{resid4}$ under the fixed gates, excluding mRNA stability as the dominant fourth-axis identity in that assay.  The codon-pair run concluded $not\_codon\_pair$ with $5$ folds, at least $500$ proteins per organism, $41$ synonymous-design columns, and a shuffle null size of $50$.  The translation-accuracy run concluded $not\_accuracy$ on $11$ computed organisms out of $16$, with $200$ permutations and the same $41$ synonymous-design columns.  The elongation-dwell row was weaker: it concluded $partial$ for $Saccharomyces\ cerevisiae$ under $5$ folds.  These exclusions and partials do not identify the fourth axis completely, but they prevent the chapter from collapsing the missing component into mRNA stability, codon-pair bias, or translation accuracy.

\paragraph{tRNA-poverty contact.}
The boundary-set tRNA-poverty row supplies a separate finite contact with the codon-level asymmetry.  On $40$ computed organisms out of $46$ requested organisms, with $6$ skipped organisms, $1000$ permutations, and $\alpha=0.05$, the run concluded that $R$ sense codons are systematically tRNA-poor under GtRNAdb tGCN plus dos Reis wobble weights.  This supports a tRNA-supply explanation for $R$ avoidance at the bounded codon-supply layer.  It does not by itself prove ribosome occupancy, translation efficiency, protein abundance, folding, function, physical admissibility, or universality.  Its role in this chapter is narrower: it shows that the finite codon surface can meet an organism-level tRNA or wobble-weight asymmetry row, which is a prerequisite for making the $f_3$ coordinate biologically testable rather than purely geometric.

\paragraph{Local support statement.}
The finite evidence supports the following chapter-level statement.  In the powered $B^*_{Q6}$ panel, the tRNA axis, the $f_3$ residual selection axis, and the identified $d_\perp$ usage-frequency-within-family axis are insufficient to explain the retained optimality surface; a fourth residual component is present.  The result is supported by the $5$-fold, $11$-organism, $41$-column optimality-sufficiency run and is consistent with the axis-strength spectrum in which $d_{resid4}$ dominates all $11$ computed organisms.  The result is also constrained by the negative identity tests for mRNA stability, codon pairs, and translation accuracy, and by the partial yeast-specific elongation-dwell row.

\paragraph{No-promotion boundary.}
This chapter does not claim that $f_3$-stress has an observed translation effect in the absence of a non-vacuous controlled $S_{f3\text{-}stress,T_j}$ entry.  Modeled tAI or stAI weights may provide a bounded codon-adaptation readout, and ribosome-profiling or translation-efficiency rows may test the next layer when same-organism and same-CDS joins exist.  Until such rows are joined with codon counts, wobble or tRNA weights, matched mRNA controls, and the declared controls for amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, CDS length, $M$-density, rank, and simpler baselines, the chapter remains a finite optimality-surface statement.  No realized translation outcome, protein abundance, folded structure, physical admissibility, molecular function, downstream mechanism, phenotype, or universal biological rule follows from the three-axis insufficiency row alone.
