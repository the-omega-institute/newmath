\subsection{Three-axis optimality is not sufficient on the powered $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual surface}
\label{sec:b-star-q6-three-axis-optimality-sufficiency}

\paragraph{Finite carrier.}
The record studied here is the finite CDS-by-organism residual coordinate surface obtained after amino-acid projection and baseline control.  The active coordinate family is $B^{*}_{Q6}$, with synonymous design restricted to $41$ columns and with organism rows retained only when the row has at least $500$ proteins.  The powered optimality-axis experiment requested $16$ organisms and computed $11$ organism rows under a deterministic seed, using $5$ folds.  Its finite conclusion is not that three axes close the surface, but the opposite: the recorded call is \texttt{fourth\_mechanism\_present}.  Thus the powered row set rejects the sufficiency of the three-axis model on this controlled surface.

\paragraph{Boundary set and $f_{3}$ saturation.}
The $f_{3}$ boundary calculation fixes the relevant codon support before any cross-layer wording is allowed.  The finite set $M=\operatorname{Med}(R)\cup\{\mathrm{ACA}\}$ is
$$
\begin{aligned}
M=\{&\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{ACA},\mathrm{ACG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\\
&\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
After $f_{3}$ saturation of $R$, the two codons $\mathrm{ACA}$ and $\mathrm{ACG}$ are absent, giving
$$
\begin{aligned}
\operatorname{Sat}_{f_{3}}(R)=\{&\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\\
&\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The recorded $f_{3}$ boundary is empty.  This matters because the stress coordinate is not being inferred from a loose codon-name contrast: it is being read inside a finite saturated support where third-position pairing has already been quotient-checked.

\paragraph{Axis sequence.}
The surrounding powered rows identify a staged failure of low-dimensional closure.  The tRNA plus $f_{3}$ span test also requested $16$ organisms, computed $11$, used $5$ folds, and concluded \texttt{third\_axis\_present}, with the reason that $d_{\perp}$ keeps systematically positive held-out $R^{2}$ and that the $d_{\perp}$ directions are mutually same-signed across organisms.  The third-axis identity row then identified $d_{\perp}$ as aligned with usage frequency within family, with mean cosine $0.128$ and one-sided sign-test $p=0.000488$.  A separate mechanism row retained the selection interpretation after GC control: its conclusion was \texttt{selection-not-GC; not-wobble-clustered; CSC/positional identity needs\_external}, with the stated reason that GC-orthogonalized $d_{\perp}$ keeps significant cross-species alignment and held-out $R^{2}$.

\paragraph{Three-axis insufficiency.}
Against that background, the optimality-axis sufficiency row asks whether the already available axes close the controlled synonymous surface.  The finite answer is no.  With $11$ computed organisms out of $16$ requested, $5$ folds, a $500$-protein row threshold, and $41$ synonymous design columns, the row is marked \texttt{passed} and returns \texttt{fourth\_mechanism\_present}.  The associated spectrum row reports that captured spectra are led on average by $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$, followed by $d_{\mathrm{tRNA}}$, and that all $11$ per-organism dominant counts fall on $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$.  Domain summaries are explicitly descriptive only because the retained panel is imbalanced, with $1$ archaeal, $2$ bacterial, and $8$ eukaryotic organisms.  The largest GC3 rank effect reported in that spectrum row is the $d_{\mathrm{usage}}$ effect, with $\rho=0.136$.

\paragraph{What the fourth direction is not.}
The finite records do not identify the fourth direction as an arbitrary downstream biological state.  Several candidate mechanisms were separately screened.  The mRNA-stability row concluded \texttt{fourth\_axis\_not\_mrna\_stability}, because yeast $d_{\mathrm{opt\_halflife}}$ has low alignment with abundance $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$ under the fixed gates.  The codon-pair row concluded \texttt{not\_codon\_pair} using $5$ folds, the same $500$-protein threshold, $41$ synonymous design columns, and a shuffle null of $50$.  The translation-accuracy row concluded \texttt{not\_accuracy} with $11$ computed organisms out of $16$ requested, $41$ synonymous design columns, and $200$ permutations.  The elongation-dwell row is the only weaker positive among these identity probes: in \textit{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}, using $5$ folds, it returned \texttt{partial}.  Therefore the finite reading is a fourth controlled residual mechanism with partial elongation-dwell contact in yeast, not a closed molecular explanation.

\paragraph{Readout rule.}
The admissible cross-layer readback is narrow.  A $B^{*}_{Q6}$ coordinate may be compared with a translation row such as tAI, stAI, ribosome occupancy, or translation efficiency only after amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, CDS length, M-density, and matched mRNA or simpler baseline controls are applied.  A positive notation such as $S_{f3\text{-}\mathrm{stress},T_j}$ means only that this bounded translation row survives those controls.  It does not by itself assert protein abundance, protein folding, physical admissibility, localization, stability, biological function, phenotype, mechanism, or universality.  Those claims require separate layer-matched measurements and their own controlled statistics.

\paragraph{Local verdict.}
The powered finite row is therefore a sufficiency refusal for the three-axis account.  The row does not dissolve the already observed tRNA, $f_{3}$, or usage-frequency structure; instead it says that those axes leave a reproducible residual direction.  The current best finite summary is

&\text{three-axis controlled model}\quad \not\Rightarrow\quad \text{closed }B^{*}_{Q6}\text{ optimality surface},\\
&\text{powered call}=\texttt{fourth\_mechanism\_present},\\

This establishes a bounded BioReality record at the translation-coordinate interface: the fourth direction is statistically present in the finite panel, its present identity is constrained by the negative mechanism screens, and any promotion beyond controlled translation readout remains disallowed until a separate reality row supplies it.
