\paragraph{Finite coordinate under test.}
This record concerns the finite coding-sequence residual surface obtained after amino-acid projection, with attention restricted to the $f_3$-paired synonymous imbalance coordinate and to translation-layer readout rows that can be matched to the same organismal or CDS support. The coordinate is not interpreted as a protein, phenotype, or mechanism. It is a finite residual direction on synonymous codon usage, and its biological contact is allowed only through codon usage, tRNA or wobble weights, and explicitly bounded translation readouts. The relevant $f_3$ saturation row records
$$\begin{aligned}
M &= \{\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{ACA},\mathrm{ACG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\},\\

\end{aligned}$$
Thus the finite saturation calculation leaves no separate $f_3$ boundary row: the test must come from controlled survival or failure of readout coordinates, not from a remaining boundary codon class.

\paragraph{Powered three-axis result.}
The powered optimality-axis experiment used five folds, required at least $500$ proteins per organism, computed $11$ organisms out of $16$ requested organisms, and used $41$ synonymous design columns. Its deterministic seed was recorded as $\mathrm{sha256:b\_star\_q6\_optimality\_axis\_sufficiency\_powered:deterministic}$, and the finite conclusion was $\mathrm{fourth\_mechanism\_present}$. In the local notation, the three tested axes do not close the observed residual surface:
$$\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{status} &= \mathrm{passed},\\
\mathrm{folds} &= 5,\\
\mathrm{organisms\ computed} &= 11,\\
\mathrm{organisms\ requested} &= 16,\\
\mathrm{synonymous\ columns} &= 41,\\
\mathrm{conclusion} &= \mathrm{fourth\_mechanism\_present}.
\end{aligned}$$
This is a sufficiency failure, not a promotion to a higher biological layer. It says that the measured finite design still contains residual structure after the named axes are used; it does not say what organismal function, folded structure, physical feasibility, or phenotype follows from that residual.

\paragraph{Relation to the earlier axes.}
The same powered panel gives a coherent ordering of nearby residual tests. The two-axis closure experiment also used five folds, $11$ computed organisms out of $16$, at least $500$ proteins per organism, and $41$ synonymous design columns; its conclusion was $\mathrm{third\_axis\_present}$, with the reason that the perpendicular direction had systematically positive held-out $R^2$ and mutually same-signed organismal directions. The third-axis identity experiment then identified $d_{\perp}$ provisionally with usage frequency within family, with mean cosine $0.128$ and one-sided sign-test $p=0.000488$. The mechanism test for that axis reported $\mathrm{selection\mbox{-}not\mbox{-}GC}$, $\mathrm{not\mbox{-}wobble\mbox{-}clustered}$, and an unresolved CSC or positional identity requiring additional contact data; the stated reason was that GC-orthogonalized $d_{\perp}$ retained significant cross-species alignment and held-out $R^2$. These facts place the three-axis experiment in a finite progression: two axes were insufficient, a third axis was detectable and partially named, and the three-axis design still failed to exhaust the residual surface.

\paragraph{Axis-strength spectrum.}
The powered axis-strength spectrum computed the same $11$ organisms out of $16$ requested organisms under five-fold evaluation and $41$ synonymous design columns. Its captured spectra were led by $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$ on average, followed by $d_{\mathrm{tRNA}}$, and the per-organism dominant-count summary was $\{d_{\mathrm{resid4}}:11\}$. The domain counts were imbalanced, with $1$ archaeal, $2$ bacterial, and $8$ eukaryotic organisms, so the domain means are descriptive rather than domain-balanced evidence. The largest GC3 rank effect reported in this spectrum was $d_{\mathrm{usage}}$ with $\rho=0.136$. This matters for interpretation: the fourth residual component is not a cosmetic tail of the model. It dominates every computed organism in this panel, while the GC3 rank effect remains explicitly reported and bounded.

\paragraph{Mechanistic exclusions around the fourth component.}
The fourth-component follow-up rows do not collapse to a single named mechanism. The mRNA-stability assay reported $\mathrm{fourth\_axis\_not\_mrna\_stability}$ because yeast $d_{\mathrm{opt\_halflife}}$ had low alignment with abundance $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$ under the fixed gates. The codon-pair assay reported $\mathrm{not\_codon\_pair}$ using five folds, at least $500$ proteins per organism, $41$ synonymous design columns, and a shuffle null of $50$. The translation-accuracy assay reported $\mathrm{not\_accuracy}$ over $11$ computed organisms out of $16$, with $200$ permutations and $41$ synonymous design columns. The elongation-dwell assay, restricted here to $\mathrm{saccharomyces\_cerevisiae}$, reported $\mathrm{partial}$ under five folds. Taken together, the finite rows support the narrower statement that a fourth residual component is present and not explained by the tested mRNA-stability, codon-pair, or translation-accuracy identities, while elongation dwell remains only a partial contact in the available organismal row.

\paragraph{tRNA-poverty contact and the $R$-sense boundary.}
A separate powered boundary test computed $40$ organisms out of $46$ requested organisms, skipped $6$, used $1000$ permutations, and set $\alpha=0.05$. Its mechanism call was that $R$ sense codons are systematically tRNA-poor under GtRNAdb tGCN plus dos Reis wobble weights, supporting a tRNA-supply explanation for $R$ avoidance. This row is relevant because the $f_3$ coordinate becomes biologically testable only where tRNA or wobble weights distinguish the paired third-position contexts. The tRNA-poverty result supplies a finite organism-level asymmetry contact, but it remains a supply-side readback; by itself it is not a same-CDS ribosome-occupancy measurement and not a proof of protein output.

\paragraph{Local verdict.}
The finite-row verdict is that the three named axes are not sufficient for the powered $B^*_{Q6}$ synonymous residual surface. The strongest local statement is
$$\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{three\mbox{-}axis\ sufficiency} &\longrightarrow \mathrm{rejected\ on\ the\ powered\ finite\ panel},\\
\mathrm{fourth\ residual\ component} &\longrightarrow \mathrm{present\ in\ the\ fitted\ coordinate\ surface},\\
\mathrm{translation\ realization} &\longrightarrow \mathrm{requires\ a\ separate\ matched\ readout\ statistic}.
\end{aligned}$$
No translation, folding, physical admissibility, function, mechanism, phenotype, or universality follows from this coordinate result alone. A positive translation-layer claim would require organism-matched codon rows, tRNA or wobble rows, ribosome-profiling or translation-efficiency rows, matched mRNA controls, and a nondegenerate controlled survival entry $S_{f_3\text{-}\mathrm{stress},T_j}>0$ after amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, CDS length, $M$-density, matched mRNA, rank, and simpler-baseline controls. The three-organism all-$1.0$ rank-saturated case is therefore a null boundary rather than survival evidence. Until the matched translation row exists, the powered conclusion stays at the residual-coordinate level: three axes are insufficient, a fourth component is present, and higher-layer biological interpretation remains blocked.
