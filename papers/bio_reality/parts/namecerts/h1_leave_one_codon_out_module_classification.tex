\subsection{Leave-One-Codon-Out Module Classification in the Window-Six Codon Cube}
\label{sec:h1-leave-one-codon-out-module-classification}

\paragraph{Finite coordinate setting.}
This record treats the standard genetic-code table as a curated code-label contact and asks what a window-six codon coordinate can and cannot recover from that contact. The finite carrier is the six-bit codon cube $Q_6$, read as a codon display with local tile faces, singleton label corners, and three-point label classes. The object under inspection is not translation as a biochemical process. It is the code-layer table assigning each codon to an amino-acid or stop label. The relevant finite row is the exceptional set $R$ detected in the coordinate scan:
$$
\begin{aligned}
R &= \{AAA, AGA, AGG, AUA, CUA, CUC, CUG, CUU, UAA, UAG, UCA, UGA, UUA\},\\
|R| &= 13.
\end{aligned}
$$
The punctured-cube computation returns the same thirteen codons and has face-vector $$(13,16,4)$$ in the recorded cell count convention. Thus the local object is not an informal cluster: it is a specific finite subset of the codon cube, with thirteen vertices, sixteen one-dimensional incidences, and four two-dimensional faces in the computed local tile.

\paragraph{Spectral and null-model evidence.}
The finite spectrum attached to the same coordinate package separates the local row from a generic reshuffling baseline. The measured values are
$$
\begin{aligned}
\lambda_M &= 0.6752479308830213,\\
\lambda_R &= 0.4758352843197002,\\
\mu &= 3.051487585298128.
\end{aligned}
$$
The radial cubic check is numerically closed at $-6.224354365258478\cdot 10^{-12}$, matching the reported tolerance slot for the spectral radius. Against $N=2000$ sampled alternatives with seed $42$, the exact-shape count is $1$, giving $p_{\mathrm{exact}}=0.0005$. The high-Jaccard count at threshold $0.9$ is $25$, giving $p_{J\geq 0.9}=0.0125$. The sampling log records $57377$ distinct-sample attempts and $55377$ duplicate retries, so the finite row is being compared against a constrained discrete space rather than against an unconstrained continuum. Under the amino-acid-class-preserving null with the same $N=2000$ and seed $42$, the observed module size is $20$, the exact-match count is $0$, and the lower-tail count at or below the observed size is $7$. This gives $p_{\mathrm{class\ exact}}=0.0$ and $p_{\mathrm{lower\ tail}}=0.0035$. These numbers support a code-layer distinction in the finite table, while not elevating the distinction into a biological mechanism.

\paragraph{Leave-one-code and leave-one-codon behavior.}
The leave-one-code-out probe evaluated $23$ code tables and $63$ targets. Its baseline hit rate is $0.19821739130434785$, while the observed hit rate above baseline is $0.9841269841269841$. The hit rate inside $M-T$ is $0.9365079365079365$, and the rate after adding the local boundary is again $0.9841269841269841$. By family, the hit rates above baseline are $1.0$ for bacterial/plastid, mitochondrial, nuclear ciliate, and candidate-other rows, and $0.8333333333333334$ for the alternative-yeast family. The leave-one-codon-out row is sharper: it reports $0$ anchors, $5$ boundary cases, $8$ high-coverage closure cases, $8$ predictable cases, and recovery rate $1.0$. Thus the finite data support a local recovery pattern in the code-label table, not a claim that the window geometry performs translation or explains why a cell realizes a protein.

\paragraph{Module partition.}
The module-classification row assigns all thirteen codons in $R$ to named modules with no leftovers. The complete partition is
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{AGR} &= \{AGA, AGG\},\\
\mathrm{AR\_Lys} &= \{AAA\},\\
\mathrm{CUN} &= \{CUA, CUC, CUG, CUU\},\\
\mathrm{Ile\_Met} &= \{AUA\},\\
\mathrm{SSN} &= \{UCA, UUA\},\\
\mathrm{Stop\_Trp} &= \{UAA, UAG, UGA\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The accounting check gives $R$-size $13$, assigned count $13$, no extra module codons, no codons missing from the modules, and no unassigned codons. The row therefore closes the finite module classification for the exceptional set: every codon in $R$ is either predictable in the local closure sense or sits on the recorded boundary side of the tile. The singleton modules $\{AAA\}$ and $\{AUA\}$ are important because they prevent the classification from being merely a family-size restatement; the four-codon CUN block and the three-codon stop/tryptophan block show that larger local faces are also represented.

\paragraph{Reality contact and scope.}
The only external contact used here is the curated standard-code table, whose observed fact is that codons have amino-acid or stop labels. That contact can test code readback and local codon-tile label splits. It cannot test translation realization, folded structure, structural order, physical admissibility, biological function, or universal mechanism. The local tile may expose a finite boundary pattern in the window-six display, but the table itself supplies labels rather than chemistry. In particular, amino-acid label sequences are not folded protein structures, and a CUN/UUR or tRNA-Leu wording does not become structure evidence without an organism-matched structural contact. The correct conclusion is therefore local and finite: the thirteen-codon exceptional set admits a complete module classification, the leave-one probes recover the code-layer rows at high rate, and the null models make the observed shape non-generic in the sampled spaces. No claim about translation, folding, physical admissibility, function, mechanism, or universality follows unless a separate observation supplies that higher-layer fact.
