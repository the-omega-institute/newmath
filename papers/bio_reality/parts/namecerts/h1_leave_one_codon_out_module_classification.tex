\paragraph{Reality contact and local boundary.}
The only external contact used here is a curated standard genetic-code table assigning codons to amino-acid or stop labels.  That contact can test code-read statements and local codon-tile label splits.  It cannot test translation realization, folded structure, structural order, physical admissibility, biological function, or a universal mechanism.  Consequently, the module classification remains a code-layer statement: a window-six coordinate pattern, a local tile boundary, a singleton-corner behavior, and a three-point label-class behavior can be read against the standard-code labels, but no higher biological layer follows from this evidence alone.
