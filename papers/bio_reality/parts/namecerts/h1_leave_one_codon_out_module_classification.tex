\subsection{Leave-One-Codon-Out Module Classification}\label{sec:bioreality-h1-leave-one-codon-out-module-classification}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite claim.}
This record concerns the standard-code codon table at the code-read layer only.  The carrier is the window-six codon cube $Q_6$, where a codon is treated as a six-bit coordinate display rather than as a biochemical mechanism.  The observed object is the curated standard-code assignment from codons to amino-acid or stop labels.  The claim is that the codons singled out by the leave-one-codon-out procedure are not an unstructured list: they partition into named local modules, and every codon in the residual set is accounted for by exactly one of those modules.  The empirical contact is therefore a finite label-table contact, not an observation of translation dynamics, folded structure, physical admissibility, biological function, universal mechanism, or evolutionary necessity.

\paragraph{Residual codon set.}
The residual set $R$ has cardinality $13$.  The finite row h0.R.cardinality.13 reports
$$
\begin{aligned}
R=\{&\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\\
&\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UUA}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The companion punctured-cube row h0.R.punctured.cube returns the same codon list and the face-vector summary $(13,16,4)$.  Thus the data used here are finite and inspectable: thirteen vertices, sixteen one-dimensional adjacencies, and four local tile faces in the codon-coordinate display.  No conclusion in this chapter depends on a hidden continuum, a learned embedding, or an unobserved biochemical process.

\paragraph{Module assignment.}
The module-classification row h1.leave\_one\_codon\_out.module\_classification assigns all thirteen residual codons and reports no extras, no omissions from the modules, and no unassigned codons.  The complete module table is
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{agr} &= \{\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG}\},\\
\mathrm{ar\_lys} &= \{\mathrm{AAA}\},\\
\mathrm{cun} &= \{\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU}\},\\

\mathrm{ssn} &= \{\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UUA}\},\\

$$
The associated completeness counters are $R\_\mathrm{size}=13$, $\mathrm{assigned\_count}=13$, $\mathrm{extra\_module\_codons}=\emptyset$, $\mathrm{missing\_from\_modules}=\emptyset$, and $\mathrm{unassigned\_codons}=\emptyset$.  The module statement is therefore exact at the finite-row level: every codon in $R$ lies in one and only one displayed module, and the displayed modules contain no codon outside $R$.

\paragraph{Prediction row.}
The leave-one-codon-out prediction row h1.leave.one.codon.out reports $0$ anchors, $5$ boundary codons, $8$ high-coverage closure codons, and $8$ predictable codons, with recovery rate $1.0$ and recovery-rate-above-threshold value $1.0$. It does not support replacing the curated label table with the geometry itself.  The label table remains the finite contact that tells us which codon has which standard-code label; the cube geometry supplies a local organization of the row after that contact is fixed.

\paragraph{Shape and spectrum checks.}
The supporting shape row h0.M.shape.significance used $N=2000$ samples with seed $42$.  It made $57377$ distinct-sample attempts and $55377$ duplicate retries, found $1$ exact shape match, found $25$ matches with Jaccard score at least $0.9$, and reported $p\_\mathrm{exact}=0.0005$ and $p\_{\mathrm{Jaccard}\geq 0.9}=0.0125$.  The amino-acid-class-preserving null row h0.null.model.aa\_class\_preserving also used $N=2000$ and seed $42$; it reports observed $M$ size $20$, exact-match count $0$, lower-tail count $7$, exact-shape $p$-value $0.0$, and lower-tail $p$-value $0.0035$.  The spectrum row h0.spectrum reports $\lambda_M=0.6752479308830213$, $\lambda_R=0.4758352843197002$, $\mu=3.051487585298128$, radial-polynomial value $-6.224354365258478\times 10^{-12}$, and the same value for the cubic-polynomial residual.  These numerical checks support a non-random finite shape signal in the code-coordinate representation, with the residual set $R$ occupying a measurable local pattern relative to the larger observed shape $M$.

\paragraph{Boundary of interpretation.}
The chapter's conclusion is deliberately local.  The standard-code table is a curated bridge from codons to labels, and the finite rows show that the leave-one-codon-out residual codons form a complete module partition inside the six-bit codon-coordinate display.  The result may be used as a code-read claim about local boundary tiles, singleton corners, three-point label classes, closures, spectra, and relations in the finite codon cube.  It must not be promoted to a claim about translation realization, folded protein structure, physical admissibility, biological function, universal law, or biological mechanism unless another contact supplies observations at that higher layer.  In particular, amino-acid label readback is not a structure experiment, CUN or UUR wording is not folded-structure evidence, and a local tile in $Q_6$ is not by itself a law governing living systems.
