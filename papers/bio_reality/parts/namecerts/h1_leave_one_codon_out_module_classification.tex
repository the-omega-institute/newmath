\subsection{Leave-One-Codon Module Classification in the Window-Six Codon Cube}
\label{sec:h1-leave-one-codon-out-module-classification}

\paragraph{Claim.}
This record concerns the finite codon-cube packet in which the standard genetic-code table is read only as a curated codon-to-label assignment. The carrier is the six-coordinate codon display $Q_6$, and the tested object is the local boundary behavior of the thirteen-codon residual set
$$
\begin{aligned}
R=\{&AAA, AGA, AGG, AUA, CUA, CUC, CUG, CUU,\\
&UAA, UAG, UCA, UGA, UUA\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The verified row reports $|R|=13$. The same row identifies $R$ as a punctured-cube shaped subcomplex with finite face data
$$
\begin{aligned}
f(R)=(13,16,4).
\end{aligned}
$$
The claim is not that this geometry explains translation. The narrower claim is that the window-six coordinates organize the standard-code residual codons into local modules whose missing-codon behavior is either predictable from closure or lies on a boundary recognized by the finite probe.

\paragraph{Module partition.}
The module classification run assigns all thirteen residual codons and leaves no unassigned residue. Its completion row is
$$
\begin{aligned}
R\_\mathrm{size}&=13,\\
\mathrm{assigned}&=13,\\
\mathrm{extra\ module\ codons}&=0,\\
\mathrm{missing\ from\ modules}&=0,\\
\mathrm{unassigned\ codons}&=0.
\end{aligned}
$$
The six reported modules are concrete and exhaustive:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{AGR}&=\{AGA,AGG\},\\
\mathrm{AR\_Lys}&=\{AAA\},\\
\mathrm{CUN}&=\{CUA,CUC,CUG,CUU\},\\
\mathrm{Ile\_Met}&=\{AUA\},\\
\mathrm{SSN}&=\{UCA,UUA\},\\
\mathrm{Stop\_Trp}&=\{UAA,UAG,UGA\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
For this finite row, every listed module contains at least one codon classified as predictable or boundary-supported by the leave-one-codon probe. The classification is therefore not a free narrative grouping: it is a finite assignment over the displayed residual set, checked against the local boundary rule.

\paragraph{Spectral and shape diagnostics.}
The residual geometry is accompanied by a spectral row in which the reported parameters are
$$
\begin{aligned}
\lambda_M&=0.6752479308830213,\\
\lambda_R&=0.4758352843197002,\\
\mu&=3.051487585298128.
\end{aligned}
$$
The same row gives a radial cubic residual of $-6.224354365258478\times 10^{-12}$, recorded both as the cubic-polynomial value and as the radial-polynomial value. At the precision of the run, this is a numerical witness that the finite spectral summary is internally consistent with the reported cubic constraint. The shape-significance experiment used $N=2000$ samples with seed $42$. It found one exact shape match and twenty-five matches with Jaccard score at least $0.9$, giving
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{\mathrm{exact}}&=0.0005,\\
p_{\mathrm{Jaccard}\geq 0.9}&=0.0125.
\end{aligned}
$$
The sampling row also records $57377$ distinct-sample attempts and $55377$ duplicate retries, so the finite null comparison is not being reported as an unlimited random search. Under the amino-acid-class-preserving null, again with $N=2000$ and seed $42$, the exact-shape count is $0$, the lower-tail count at or below the observed module size is $7$, the observed $M$ size is $20$, and the reported lower-tail probability is $0.0035$.

\paragraph{Leave-one recovery.}
The leave-one-code-out row evaluates $23$ code tables and $63$ total targets. Its baseline hit rate is $0.19821739130434785$, while the hit rate above baseline is $0.9841269841269841$. The same run reports a hit rate of $0.9365079365079365$ inside $M-T$ and $0.9841269841269841$ inside $M-T$ together with the boundary allowance. In the leave-one-codon-out row, the anchor count is $0$, the boundary count is $5$, the closure-high coverage is $8$, the predictable count is $8$, and the recovery rate is $1.0$. These numbers support a local code-read statement: when a codon is withheld from the finite table context, the window-six module rule recovers the withheld label position either through closure-predictable behavior or through the stated boundary class.

\paragraph{Family-level check.}
The family summary reports five code-table families. The hit rates above baseline are $0.8333333333333334$ for alternative yeast, $1.0$ for bacterial plastid, $1.0$ for candidate other, $1.0$ for mitochondrial, and $1.0$ for nuclear ciliate. This family row is useful because it keeps the observation from depending on a single aggregate score alone. The weaker alternative-yeast value is still above the baseline reported for the leave-one-code-out aggregate, while the other four families reach full above-baseline recovery in the finite table set.

\paragraph{Scope.}
The biological contact used here is the curated standard genetic-code table: a codon is assigned to an amino-acid label or to stop. That contact tests code-layer readback and local label splitting; it does not observe ribosomal translation, protein folding, physical realizability, biological activity, organismal fitness, or a universal mechanism. Consequently, the module classification may be used as evidence for a finite local codon-label pattern in $Q_6$, but it must not be promoted into a claim about folded structure, functional protein behavior, admissible molecular physics, or a general law of biology without an additional experiment or data source aimed at that higher layer.
