\subsection{Leave-One-Codon-Out Module Classification}
\label{sec:h1-leave-one-codon-out-module-classification}

\paragraph{Claim.}
The window-six codon display supplies a finite coordinate arena in which the standard-code label table can be read as a local code-layer object.  The present claim concerns only the thirteen-codon residual region $R$ and asks whether the leave-one-codon-out cases fall into a complete set of named codon modules.  The audited outcome is affirmative at the code-read layer: every codon in $R$ is assigned to one of six modules, and every listed module contains at least one codon whose leave-one-codon-out status is predictable or boundary-supported.  This is not a claim about translation dynamics, folded protein structure, biochemical admissibility, phenotype, mechanism, or universality.

\paragraph{Finite codon region.}
The residual set has cardinality $13$.  The audited row reports
$$
\begin{aligned}
R=\{&AAA, AGA, AGG, AUA, CUA, CUC, CUG, CUU,\\
    &UAA, UAG, UCA, UGA, UUA\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The same row identifies this set with a punctured cube pattern and gives the finite face data
$$
\begin{aligned}
f(R)=(13,16,4).
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the classification problem is not over all $64$ codons at once; it is a finite residual classification over thirteen named codons after the window-six coordinate construction has isolated the local tile boundary.

\paragraph{Module partition.}
The module classification is complete.  The audit reports $R\_size=13$, $assigned\_count=13$, and the three error lists are empty: no extra module codons, no missing codons, and no unassigned codons.  The actual module map is
$$
\begin{aligned}
agr&=\{AGA,AGG\},\\
ar\_lys&=\{AAA\},\\
cun&=\{CUA,CUC,CUG,CUU\},\\
ile\_met&=\{AUA\},\\
ssn&=\{UCA,UUA\},\\
stop\_trp&=\{UAA,UAG,UGA\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
This is the main finite-row witness for the present claim.  It says that the residual codon tile is not merely counted correctly; it is exhausted by biologically legible code-label modules that cover all thirteen codons without residue.

\paragraph{Leave-one-codon-out recovery.}
The leave-one-codon-out audit supplies the local predictive status behind the module claim.  It reports $anchor\_count=0$, $boundary\_count=5$, $predictable\_count=8$, and $closure\_coverage\_high=8$.  The recovery rate is $1.0$, and the threshold comparison also records $recovery\_rate\_above\_threshold=1.0$.  Consequently, the module partition is not a post hoc name list disconnected from the local tile test: each module is represented inside the predictable-or-boundary regime, with the thirteen codons exhausted by the six named classes above.

These numbers support the narrower reading of the result: the six module classes behave as stable local code-read compartments under the finite leave-out probes, but the support remains tied to codon-label tables rather than to any downstream molecular realization.

\paragraph{Shape and spectral checks.}
The shape-significance audit used $N=2000$ with seed $42$.  It observed one exact shape match, giving $p\_exact=0.0005$, and $25$ high-Jaccard matches, giving $p\_Jaccard\geq 0.9=0.0125$.  The run also records $57377$ distinct sample attempts and $55377$ duplicate retries.  Under the amino-acid-class-preserving null, again with $N=2000$ and seed $42$, the observed module-size statistic was $20$, exact matches were $0$, and the lower-tail count at or below the observed size was $7$, yielding $p\_exact=0.0$ and $p\_lower\_tail=0.0035$.  The spectral row reports
$$
\begin{aligned}
\lambda\_M&=0.6752479308830213,\\
\lambda\_R&=0.4758352843197002,\\
\mu&=3.051487585298128,
\end{aligned}
$$
with the radial cubic residual equal to $-6.224354365258478\times 10^{-12}$.  These scalar checks give independent finite summaries of the same local pattern: the residual tile has a nonrandom-looking shape signature and a numerically stable spectral readout within the audited tolerance.

\paragraph{Reality contact.}
The biological contact used here is the curated standard genetic-code table.  It supplies codon-to-label assignments for the code-read layer: codons are attached to amino-acid or stop labels.  The contact does not observe ribosomal execution, folding, physical realizability, cellular function, or cross-organism necessity.  The local tile test is therefore a classification of codon-label readback inside a finite coordinate display.  It is not evidence that DNA must obey this geometry as a biological law, and it is not evidence that a label sequence determines a folded protein.

\paragraph{Boundary of the result.}
The result may be stated as follows.  In the supplied finite rows, the thirteen-codon residual region
$$
\begin{aligned}
R=\{AAA,AGA,AGG,AUA,CUA,CUC,CUG,CUU,UAA,UAG,UCA,UGA,UUA\}
\end{aligned}
$$
is completely partitioned into six named modules, and those modules are covered by the predictable-or-boundary leave-one-codon-out statuses.  The supporting rows include $R\_size=13$, $assigned\_count=13$, an empty unassigned list, recovery rate $1.0$, code-out hit rate above baseline $0.9841269841269841$, and significant shape comparisons with $p=0.0005$ for exact shape and $p=0.0035$ for the amino-acid-class-preserving lower tail.  What follows is a code-layer local module classification.  What does not follow is translation, folding, biochemical admissibility, biological function, mechanism, or universality; each of those would require its own separate empirical contact.
