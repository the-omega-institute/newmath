\paragraph{Scientific reading and boundary.}
The biological contact used here is deliberately narrow.  It says that a curated standard-code table assigns codons to amino-acid or stop labels, and that those labels can be read against the six-bit codon carrier.  Within that scope, the finite evidence supports a local boundary-tile classification: the set $R$ has size $13$, has the displayed punctured-cube face-vector, satisfies the recorded spectral check, is statistically uncommon under the sampled null models, and admits a complete six-module classification under the leave-one-codon probe.
