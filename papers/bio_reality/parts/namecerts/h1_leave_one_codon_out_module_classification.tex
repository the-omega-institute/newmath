\subsection{Leave-one-codon-out module classification}
\label{sec:h1-leave-one-codon-out-module-classification}

\paragraph{Claim.}
This packet records a code-layer regularity in the standard-code codon table.  The carrier is the six-coordinate codon cube $Q_6$, read as a finite display of the $64$ codons by binary nucleotide coordinates.  The relevant distinctions are local tile faces, singleton label corners, and three-point label classes.  The claim is not that the cube geometry generates biology by itself.  The claim is narrower: after the curated standard-code labels are placed on the codon cube, the leave-one-codon-out residual set is exhausted by a small list of codon modules, and every listed module contains a codon that is either recovered by the local closure test or lies on the stated boundary.

\paragraph{Residual codon row.}
The residual row has cardinality $13$.  The verified codons are
$$
\begin{aligned}
R=\{&AAA,AGA,AGG,AUA,CUA,CUC,CUG,CUU,\\
&UAA,UAG,UCA,UGA,UUA\},\qquad |R|=13.
\end{aligned}
$$
The same row is also reported as a punctured cube with face vector $(13,16,4)$.  Thus the finite object being classified is not an arbitrary prose list: it is a fixed thirteen-codon subset together with a local cubical incidence profile.  The construction uses the curated code table only as the source of amino-acid or stop labels; the finite geometry supplies the coordinate display, closure behavior, spectrum, and relation data.

\paragraph{Module partition.}
The module audit assigns every element of $R$ and no codon outside $R$.  The classification is
$$
\begin{aligned}
AGR&=\{AGA,AGG\},& AR\_Lys&=\{AAA\},\\
CUN&=\{CUA,CUC,CUG,CUU\},& Ile\_Met&=\{AUA\},\\
SSN&=\{UCA,UUA\},& Stop\_Trp&=\{UAA,UAG,UGA\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The completion row reports $R\_size=13$, $assigned\_count=13$, and empty lists for extra module codons, missing module codons, and unassigned codons.  This is the finite classification statement for the packet: the six named modules cover exactly the thirteen residual codons, and each module has a predictable or boundary member.  The statement is intentionally local.  It does not say that these modules are mechanisms, that they are universal biological constraints, or that the codon cube alone explains translation.

\paragraph{Closure and boundary row.}
The leave-one-codon-out probe reports $predictable\_count=8$, $boundary\_count=5$, $anchor\_count=0$, and $closure\_coverage\_high=8$. The module statement uses this split only inside the code-read layer: the verified condition is that every module meets the predictable-or-boundary test, not that each codon has a downstream structural or functional explanation.

These rows are evidence that the local codon-tile behavior is not confined to a single unstratified table comparison, but they still remain code-label comparisons rather than claims about realized molecular dynamics.

\paragraph{Reality contact.}
The only external contact used here is the curated standard genetic-code table.  It supplies the observed fact that codons are assigned amino-acid or stop labels under the standard code.  Its resolution is codon label readback.  It can test code-layer label assignments and local codon-tile splits.  It cannot test translation realization, folded structure, structural order, physical admissibility, biological function, or a universal mechanism.  Therefore amino-acid label-sequence readback remains a code-layer observation.  No protein fold, no physical admissibility claim, no organism-level function, and no general law of biology follows from the finite row unless a separate contact supplies that higher-layer observation.

\paragraph{Verdict.}
The packet is ready as a finite code-read record.  The verified thirteen-codon residual set is exactly covered by six modules; each module contains predictable or boundary evidence; the closure, spectral, null-model, leave-one-code-out, and family rows all support the local classification.  The correct scientific reading is a bounded one: window-six codon coordinates expose a local boundary-tile structure after the curated standard-code labels are attached, while the curated table remains the reality source for labels and blocks promotion to translation, folding, physical admissibility, function, mechanism, or universality.
