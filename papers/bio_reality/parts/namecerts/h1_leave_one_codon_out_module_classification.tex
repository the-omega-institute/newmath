\subsection{Leave-One-Codon-Out Module Classification}\label{sec:h1-leave-one-codon-out-module-classification}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Object and coordinate frame.}
This record treats the standard genetic-code table as a finite codon-label object placed on the six-bit codon cube $Q_6$.  The six displayed coordinates are used only as a discrete codon display: they support local adjacency, local closure, and finite row comparisons, but they are not taken as a biological mechanism.  The biological contact is the curated standard-code assignment of codons to amino-acid or stop labels.  The layer under test is therefore code readback: codon strings may be compared with labels, boundary positions, and local modules, while translation dynamics, folded structure, physical admissibility, biochemical function, and universality remain outside the scope of this contact.

\paragraph{Local interpretation and blocked promotions.}
The result is a code-read result.  It says that the curated standard-code labels, when displayed on $Q_6$, admit a compact thirteen-codon boundary tile with a complete six-module classification and strong leave-one-out recovery statistics.  It does not say that the cube display is a law of DNA, that the codon table realizes translation by itself, that the amino-acid label sequence folds, that the labels determine physical admissibility, or that biological function follows.  Any such stronger statement would require a separate contact: for example a translation assay for translation realization, a structure observation or structure-prediction contact for folding, a biochemical contact for physical admissibility, and a functional assay for function.  Without those additional contacts, the present record remains a finite code-layer classification of the standard-code label table.
