\subsection{Leave-One-Codon-Out Module Classification in the Window-Six Codon Cube}
\label{subsec:h1-leave-one-codon-out-module-classification}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite setting.}
The packet studies the standard-code codon table through a six-bit codon display, where each codon is treated as a point of the codon cube $Q_6$ and the only biological readout used here is the curated codon-to-label table. The finite residue set selected by the local tile calculation has cardinality $13$:
$$
\begin{aligned}
R = \{&AAA, AGA, AGG, AUA, CUA, CUC, CUG, CUU,\\
&UAA, UAG, UCA, UGA, UUA\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The same row records that this set is a punctured-cube shaped object with $f$-vector $(13,16,4)$. The point of the chapter is not that this local object is a law of biology. It is a finite coordinate pattern whose labels can be compared with the curated standard-code table, while stronger biological assertions remain outside the tested contact.

\paragraph{Spectral and shape controls.}
The spectral row gives three numerical scalars for the local construction:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\lambda_M &= 0.6752479308830213,\\
\lambda_R &= 0.4758352843197002,\\
\mu &= 3.051487585298128.
\end{aligned}
$$
The associated radial cubic residual is $-6.224354365258478\times 10^{-12}$, which is numerically at zero for the recorded tolerance. Shape significance was tested against $2000$ sampled alternatives with seed $42$. Among $57377$ distinct-sample attempts, $55377$ duplicate retries were needed; exactly one sampled alternative matched the observed shape, giving $p=0.0005$, and $25$ alternatives reached Jaccard similarity at least $0.9$, giving $p=0.0125$. The amino-acid-class-preserving null gives a second control: with $2000$ samples, the observed $M$ size was $20$, the exact-shape count was $0$, and the lower-tail count at or below the observed size was $7$, giving lower-tail probability $0.0035$. These finite rows support the claim that the displayed tile is not an arbitrary coding of the table, but they do not promote the tile to translation physics, protein structure, admissibility, function, mechanism, or universality.

\paragraph{Leave-one-code and leave-one-codon checks.}
The leave-one-code-out row evaluated $23$ code tables and $63$ targets. The baseline hit rate was $0.19821739130434785$, while the hit rate above baseline was $0.9841269841269841$. The hit rate inside $M-T$ was $0.9365079365079365$, and the hit rate inside $M-T$ together with the boundary was again $0.9841269841269841$. The leave-one-codon-out row gives a sharper local accounting: there were $8$ predictable codons, $5$ boundary codons, no anchors, and $8$ high-coverage closure cases, with recovery rate $1.0$. Thus the evidence is finite, rowwise, and code-layer specific: removed codon labels are recovered or placed on the boundary inside the same local window, not inferred from an organismal mechanism.

\paragraph{Module classification.}
The module row partitions all $13$ codons of $R$ into six named modules, with no missing, extra, or unassigned codons. The assignment is
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{agr} &= \{AGA,AGG\},\\
\mathrm{ar\_lys} &= \{AAA\},\\
\mathrm{cun} &= \{CUA,CUC,CUG,CUU\},\\
\mathrm{ile\_met} &= \{AUA\},\\
\mathrm{ssn} &= \{UCA,UUA\},\\
\mathrm{stop\_trp} &= \{UAA,UAG,UGA\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The completeness counters are $R\_size=13$, $assigned\_count=13$, $extra\_module\_codons=\emptyset$, $missing\_from\_modules=\emptyset$, and $unassigned\_codons=\emptyset$. Each module contains a predictable or boundary member, so the leave-one-codon behavior is not concentrated in a single accidental codon family. Family-level leave-one-code-out data are consistent with this reading: bacterial/plastid, candidate-other, mitochondrial, and nuclear-ciliate families each have hit rate above baseline equal to $1.0$, while the alternative-yeast family has hit rate $0.8333333333333334$.

\paragraph{Reality contact and boundary of interpretation.}
The only contact used here is the curated standard genetic-code table, taken as an observation that codons have amino-acid or stop labels in the standard code. That contact can test code readout and local label splits. It cannot test whether translation is realized in a cell, whether a label sequence folds, whether a molecule is physically admissible, whether a biological function occurs, or whether the local pattern is a universal mechanism. In particular, amino-acid label readback is not folded protein structure. Cross-organism wording, tRNA notation, or a local CUN/UUR split would require their own matched contacts before becoming organismal or structural claims. The present verdict is therefore confined to the code-read layer: within the finite window-six cube, the $13$-codon residue set is completely classified into modules, every module is covered by predictable or boundary behavior, and the numerical controls support a nontrivial local tile without crossing into higher biological layers.
