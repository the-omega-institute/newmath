\subsection{Leave-one-codon-out module classification for the codon boundary tile}
\label{sec:h1-leave-one-codon-out-module-classification}

\paragraph{Finite coordinate packet.}
The packet studies the standard-code codon table through a six-bit codon display on the codon cube $Q_6$.  The mathematical object under inspection is not a biochemical mechanism, but a finite coordinate-and-label record: codons are placed in the window-six cube, local faces and corners are read as finite tile data, and the curated standard-code table supplies the amino-acid or stop labels.  The distinguished residue set is the thirteen-codon region
$$
\begin{aligned}
R = \{&AAA, AGA, AGG, AUA, CUA, CUC, CUG, CUU,\\
     &UAA, UAG, UCA, UGA, UUA\},
\end{aligned}
$$
with $|R|=13$.  The punctured-cube check gives the same list and the face-vector record $(13,16,4)$, so the local shape is a concrete finite subcomplex rather than a verbal grouping of codons.  The surrounding distinguished set $M$ is observed with size $20$ in the amino-acid-class-preserving null experiment, and the leave-one-code-out checks report hit rates inside $M-T$ and its boundary enlargement.  Thus the packet has three separate finite layers: a thirteen-codon residue set $R$, a twenty-point observed set $M$, and label data imported from the curated code table.

\paragraph{Spectral and null controls.}
The local coordinate data also carries a numerical spectral signature.  The recorded values are
$$
\begin{aligned}
\lambda_M &= 0.6752479308830213,\\
\lambda_R &= 0.4758352843197002,\\
\mu &= 3.051487585298128,
\end{aligned}
$$
and the radial cubic residual is $-6.224354365258478\times 10^{-12}$, within the recorded tolerance for the same value of $\lambda_M$.  The null checks are finite and explicit.  In $N=2000$ random trials for the shape test, there was $1$ exact shape match after $57377$ distinct-sample attempts and $55377$ duplicate retries, giving $p_{\mathrm{exact}}=0.0005$.  The high-overlap threshold $J\geq 0.9$ produced $25$ matches and $p_{J\geq 0.9}=0.0125$.  Under the amino-acid-class-preserving null model with $N=2000$, the exact-shape count was $0$, the observed size was $20$, the lower-tail count at or below that size was $7$, and the lower-tail value was $0.0035$.  These numbers support a real finite regularity at the code-label layer, while still leaving the labels themselves dependent on the curated biological table.

\paragraph{Leave-one-code and leave-one-codon behavior.}
The leave-one-code-out experiment evaluated $23$ code tables over $63$ targets.  Its baseline hit rate was $0.19821739130434785$, whereas the hit rate above baseline was $0.9841269841269841$.  Inside $M-T$ the hit rate was $0.9365079365079365$, and inside $M-T$ together with the boundary it was again $0.9841269841269841$.  The family-level split gives five families: alternative yeast has rate $0.8333333333333334$, and bacterial/plastid, candidate other, mitochondrial, and nuclear ciliate each have rate $1.0$.  The leave-one-codon-out experiment separately records $0$ anchors, $5$ boundary codons, $8$ predictable codons, $8$ high-coverage codons, and recovery rate $1.0$.  The interpretation is therefore local and finite: the window-six geometry helps classify boundary and predictable positions in a code table, not a realized translation process.

\paragraph{Module classification.}
The module classification is complete on the thirteen-codon residue set.  The assigned modules are
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{AGR} &= \{AGA, AGG\},\\
\mathrm{AR\_Lys} &= \{AAA\},\\
\mathrm{CUN} &= \{CUA, CUC, CUG, CUU\},\\
\mathrm{Ile\_Met} &= \{AUA\},\\
\mathrm{SSN} &= \{UCA, UUA\},\\
\mathrm{Stop\_Trp} &= \{UAA, UAG, UGA\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The audit row reports $R\_size=13$ and $assigned\_count=13$, with no extra module codons, no codons missing from the modules, and no unassigned codons.  Every module has at least one predictable or boundary codon under the leave-one-codon-out classification.  This is the strongest finite claim in the packet: the six displayed groups exactly partition the observed residue set and the classification test covers every module.  It is not merely that a few codons behave well; the module accounting closes over all thirteen listed codons.

\paragraph{Reality contact and local boundary.}
The only biological contact used here is the curated standard-code table, understood as a codon-to-label table.  It can test code readback and local codon-tile label splits.  It cannot test whether translation is realized, whether a protein folds, whether a sequence is physically admissible, whether a function is achieved, or whether the local tile is a universal mechanism.  The statement supported by the finite rows is therefore this: window-six codon coordinates expose a stable local boundary-tile organization for the standard-code label table, with a complete thirteen-codon module partition and strong leave-one-code and leave-one-codon recovery statistics.  The statement not supported is equally important: no conclusion about translation, folding, physical admissibility, function, mechanism, or universality follows unless a separate observation supplies that higher-layer contact.
