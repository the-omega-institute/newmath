\subsection{Leave-one-codon module classification in a window-six codon cube}
\label{sec:bioreality-h1-leave-one-codon-out-module-classification}

\paragraph{Finite coordinate setting.}
The finite carrier is the six-coordinate codon cube $Q_6$, read at the code-label level through the curated standard genetic-code table. The claim studied here is not that the cube explains translation, chemistry, protein folding, or organismal function. It is the narrower assertion that a window-six coordinate display can expose a local boundary-tile pattern among codons whose labels are unstable under leave-one-codon or leave-one-code probes. In the present row, the distinguished residual set $R$ has exactly thirteen codons:
$$
\begin{aligned}
R=\{&\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\\
&\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UUA}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The same thirteen entries are reported by the punctured-cube check, whose face data have $f$-vector $(13,16,4)$. Thus the row is not using an open-ended biological vocabulary: it is testing a fixed finite codon set, a fixed local cube geometry, and a fixed curated label readback.

\paragraph{Spectral and sampling evidence.}
The finite spectral row records $\lambda_M=0.6752479308830213$, $\lambda_R=0.4758352843197002$, and $\mu=3.051487585298128$. The associated cubic residual is $-6.224354365258478\cdot 10^{-12}$, also reported as the radial polynomial value, so the numerical certificate is effectively at floating-point zero for this run. The observed $M$-shape comparison used $N=2000$ samples with seed $42$. Among $57377$ distinct-sample attempts, $55377$ duplicate retries were needed; only $1$ exact shape match occurred, giving $p_{\mathrm{exact}}=0.0005$, while $25$ samples reached Jaccard similarity at least $0.9$, giving $p_{\mathrm{Jaccard}\geq 0.9}=0.0125$. The amino-acid-class-preserving null also used $N=2000$ and seed $42$; it had observed $M$-size $20$, exact-match count $0$, lower-tail count $7$, $p_{\mathrm{class\ exact}}=0.0$, and $p_{\mathrm{class\ lower\ tail}}=0.0035$. These numbers support a finite pattern claim about the code-label display; they do not by themselves identify a molecular mechanism.

\paragraph{Leave-one-code and leave-one-codon readback.}
The leave-one-code-out row evaluated $23$ code tables and $63$ total targets. Its baseline hit rate was $0.19821739130434785$, while the hit rate above baseline was $0.9841269841269841$. The same row reports hit rate $0.9365079365079365$ inside $M\setminus T$ and $0.9841269841269841$ inside $M\setminus T$ plus the boundary. The leave-one-codon-out row records no anchors, with anchor count $0$, boundary count $5$, closure-coverage-high count $8$, predictable count $8$, and recovery rate $1.0$. In this chapter, those figures mean that the finite cube display is useful as a local recovery and boundary classifier for code labels, not that it has crossed into a translation or protein-structure observation.

\paragraph{Module classification.}
The module classification row partitions all thirteen $R$ codons into six named modules, with no missing and no extra codons:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{agr} &= \{\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG}\},\\
\mathrm{ar\_lys} &= \{\mathrm{AAA}\},\\
\mathrm{cun} &= \{\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU}\},\\
\mathrm{ile\_met} &= \{\mathrm{AUA}\},\\
\mathrm{ssn} &= \{\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UUA}\},\\
\mathrm{stop\_trp} &= \{\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UGA}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The completeness counters are $R_{\mathrm{size}}=13$, assigned count $13$, extra module codons $\varnothing$, missing-from-modules $\varnothing$, and unassigned codons $\varnothing$. Hence every codon in the residual set is accounted for by one of the named modules, and every listed module contains at least one codon classified as predictable or boundary-supported in the finite row. The family-level leave-one-code-out check gives five families: alternative yeast has hit rate above baseline $0.8333333333333334$, while bacterial plastid, candidate other, mitochondrial, and nuclear ciliate each have hit rate above baseline $1.0$.

\paragraph{Reality contact and scope.}
The only biological contact used here is the curated standard-code table: it supplies codon-to-amino-acid or codon-to-stop labels. That contact can test code-layer readback and local codon-tile label splits. It cannot test translation realization, folded structure, structural order, physical admissibility, biological function, a universal biological law, or a mechanism for why organisms use a given assignment. The chapter therefore records a bounded result: window-six coordinates, together with the curated label table, organize a thirteen-codon residual set into a complete local module classification with strong finite recovery statistics. Any claim about translation, folding, admissibility, function, mechanism, or universality would require a separate observation of that higher layer before it could be asserted.
