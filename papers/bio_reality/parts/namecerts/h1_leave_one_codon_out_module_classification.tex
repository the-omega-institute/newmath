\paragraph{Finite carrier and observed label contact.}
This record works at the code-read layer of the standard genetic-code table.  The finite carrier is the codon cube $Q_6$, where a codon is represented as a six-bit coordinate display over the four nucleotide symbols.  The observed contact is a curated standard-code table assigning each codon to an amino-acid label or to a stop label.  That contact supplies label readback only: it does not observe translation in a cell, folded structure, physical admissibility, biological function, a biochemical mechanism, or a universal law.  The present claim is therefore a finite classification statement about a local codon tile and its label boundary, not a higher-layer biological assertion.
