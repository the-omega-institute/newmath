\subsection{Leave-One-Codon-Out Module Classification in the Window-Six Codon Cube}
\label{sec:h1-leave-one-codon-out-module-classification}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite setting.}
The record studies the standard-code codon table through a six-coordinate codon display, where each codon is read as a point of the codon cube $Q_6$. The biological contact is deliberately narrow: the curated standard-code table supplies codon-to-label assignments, and the internal geometry supplies local coordinates, closure, spectral measurements, and relation data. The claim is not that the cube geometry is a biological cause. It is that a finite leave-one-codon experiment, when restricted to code-label readback, organizes the exceptional codons into a complete module partition with no unassigned residue.

\paragraph{Exceptional region.}
The punctured-cube row gives the exceptional set $R$ with cardinality $13$. The listed codons are $AAA$, $AGA$, $AGG$, $AUA$, $CUA$, $CUC$, $CUG$, $CUU$, $UAA$, $UAG$, $UCA$, $UGA$, and $UUA$. The same row reports an $f$-vector $(13,16,4)$ for this region. Thus the finite object being classified is not an informal collection of examples: it is a thirteen-point subconfiguration with sixteen one-dimensional incidences and four two-dimensional faces in the local codon geometry.

\paragraph{Module partition.}
The leave-one-codon-out module-classification row assigns every element of $R$ to one of six named modules. The $AGR$ module is $\{AGA,AGG\}$; the $AR/Lys$ module is $\{AAA\}$; the $CUN$ module is $\{CUA,CUC,CUG,CUU\}$; the $Ile/Met$ module is $\{AUA\}$; the $SSN$ module is $\{UCA,UUA\}$; and the $Stop/Trp$ module is $\{UAA,UAG,UGA\}$. The completeness counters are $R\_size=13$ and $assigned\_count=13$, with $extra\_module\_codons=\varnothing$, $missing\_from\_modules=\varnothing$, and $unassigned\_codons=\varnothing$. This is the central finite-row conclusion: the leave-one-codon-out boundary analysis does not merely find several recognizable families, but covers the whole thirteen-codon region exactly.

\paragraph{Predictability and boundary status.}
The leave-one-codon-out row reports $predictable\_count=8$, $boundary\_count=5$, $anchor\_count=0$, $closure\_coverage\_high=8$, and $recovery\_rate=1.0$, with $recovery\_rate\_above\_threshold=1.0$. In module language, every module contains codons that are either predictable or boundary-supported. The finite statement is therefore weaker than deterministic biological reconstruction but stronger than a loose visual analogy: each module is accounted for by the local leave-one-codon procedure or by an explicitly marked boundary role.

\paragraph{Code-table comparison.}
The leave-one-code-out comparison used $23$ tables and $63$ total targets. Against a baseline hit rate of $0.19821739130434785$, the hit rate above baseline is $0.9841269841269841$. The hit rate inside $M-T$ is $0.9365079365079365$, and the rate inside $M-T$ plus boundary is $0.9841269841269841$. The family-level row separates five families: alternative yeast has hit rate $0.8333333333333334$, while bacterial/plastid, candidate other, mitochondrial, and nuclear ciliate each have hit rate $1.0$. These numbers support the code-read layer interpretation: the observed module coverage is stable across a finite comparison regime, while still being evaluated only against label assignments.

\paragraph{Shape and spectrum checks.}
The geometric control rows give additional finite context. In the shape-significance run with $N=2000$ samples, there was $1$ exact shape match, so $p\_exact=0.0005$, and there were $25$ high-Jaccard matches, so $p\_{J\ge 0.9}=0.0125$. The sampling record also reports $57377$ distinct-sample attempts and $55377$ duplicate retries at seed $42$. In the amino-acid-class-preserving null model with $N=2000$, the observed size was $|M|=20$, exact-match count was $0$, the lower-tail count was $7$, $p\_value\_aa\_class\_exact\_shape=0.0$, and $p\_value\_aa\_class\_lower\_tail=0.0035$. The spectral row records $\lambda_M=0.6752479308830213$, $\lambda_R=0.4758352843197002$, and $\mu=3.051487585298128$, with the radial polynomial residual $-6.224354365258478\times 10^{-12}$. These controls do not create biological mechanism; they show that the finite geometry being used for the code-layer readback is numerically constrained and nontrivial under the stated null comparisons.

\paragraph{Local verdict.}
The ready claim is a code-read claim. The standard-code label table supplies the observed codon labels, and the window-six geometry supplies a local tile and boundary organization. The thirteen exceptional codons form a complete six-module classification, every module has predictable or boundary support, and the comparison rows show high recovery relative to the reported baselines. No conclusion about translation realization, folded protein structure, physical admissibility, biological function, universal mechanism, or organism-wide necessity follows from these rows. Any such stronger statement would require a separate contact that directly observes that stronger layer rather than only codon-to-label readback.
