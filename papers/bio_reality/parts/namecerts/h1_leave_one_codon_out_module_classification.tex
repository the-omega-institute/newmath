\subsection{Leave-One-Codon-Out Module Classification}
\label{sec:bioreality-h1-leave-one-codon-out-module-classification}

\paragraph{Finite setting.}
The claim concerns a six-coordinate codon cube $Q_6$ whose rows are read as codon labels through the curated standard genetic-code table. The empirical object is not translation itself, but the finite table assigning each codon to an amino-acid or stop label. Within this setting the distinguished residual row set $R$ has cardinality $13$ and is exactly
$$
\begin{aligned}
R = \{&\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\\
&\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UUA}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The same finite set is certified as a punctured-cube row pattern with face-count vector $(13,16,4)$. Thus the row evidence is not merely a list of exceptional codons: it also carries a local cubical shape, with $13$ vertices, $16$ one-dimensional adjacencies, and $4$ two-dimensional faces in the induced tile.

\paragraph{Spectral and null checks.}
The associated spectral calculation gives
$$
\begin{aligned}
\lambda_M &= 0.6752479308830213,\\
\lambda_R &= 0.4758352843197002,\\
\mu &= 3.051487585298128,
\end{aligned}
$$
with the radial cubic residual recorded as $-6.224354365258478\times 10^{-12}$, which is numerically at tolerance for the finite computation. The shape null experiment with $N=2000$ trials observed one exact shape match, giving $p_{\mathrm{exact}}=0.0005$, and $25$ samples with Jaccard score at least $0.9$, giving $p_{\mathrm{Jac}\ge 0.9}=0.0125$. The sampler attempted $57377$ distinct draws and rejected $55377$ duplicates, so the small exact-tail value is attached to a concrete finite sampling run rather than to an asymptotic statement. A second amino-acid-class-preserving null, also with $N=2000$, observed $0$ exact matches, $7$ lower-tail cases at or below the observed module size $20$, and hence recorded $p_{\mathrm{class\ exact}}=0.0$ and $p_{\mathrm{class\ lower\ tail}}=0.0035$.

\paragraph{Module assignment.}
The leave-one-codon-out classification is complete on the residual set. All $13$ residual codons are assigned, no extra codons appear in the module cover, and the missing and unassigned lists are empty. The six modules are
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{AGR} &= \{\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG}\},\\
\mathrm{AR/Lys} &= \{\mathrm{AAA}\},\\
\mathrm{CUN} &= \{\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU}\},\\
\mathrm{Ile/Met} &= \{\mathrm{AUA}\},\\
\mathrm{SSN} &= \{\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UUA}\},\\
\mathrm{Stop/Trp} &= \{\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UGA}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The row-level conclusion is therefore sharp: every module contains at least one codon classified as predictable or as boundary-supported, and the module cover accounts for exactly the whole residual set $R$.

\paragraph{Scientific scope.}
The contact used here is the curated standard-code table. It supplies codon-to-label readback and permits finite checks about local label splits, residual-row geometry, module cover, and recovery under leave-one-out probes. It does not observe ribosomal realization, kinetic translation, folded protein structure, thermodynamic admissibility, biological function, organism-level mechanism, or universality across life. Consequently the classification may be stated only at the code-read layer: the window-six codon geometry exposes a local boundary-tile pattern whose labels are fixed by the curated table. No claim about translation, folding, physical admissibility, function, mechanism, or universal biological law follows from this packet unless a distinct experiment or observation supplies that higher-layer contact.
