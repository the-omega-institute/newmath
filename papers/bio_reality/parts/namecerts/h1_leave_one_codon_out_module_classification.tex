\subsection{Leave-One-Codon Module Classification in the Standard Code}
\label{sec:h1-leave-one-codon-out-module-classification}

\paragraph{Finite codon display.}
The record studied here is the standard-code codon table viewed through a six-bit codon display.  The finite carrier is the codon cube $Q_6$, with codon labels supplied by a curated standard-code table.  The relevant row is not a claim about molecular execution.  It is a code-layer readback: a finite coordinate system is compared with the codon-to-label assignment in the table, and the resulting local tile is tested for boundary behavior.  The distinguished residual set has cardinality $13$ and is exactly
$$
\begin{aligned}
R = \{&AAA, AGA, AGG, AUA, CUA, CUC, CUG, CUU,\\
     &UAA, UAG, UCA, UGA, UUA\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The same finite set is reported as a punctured-cube pattern with face vector
$$
\begin{aligned}
(f_0,f_1,f_2)=(13,16,4).
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the object under discussion is a local codon-label configuration with thirteen residual codons, sixteen one-dimensional incidences, and four two-dimensional tile faces inside the codon cube.

\paragraph{Module partition.}
The leave-one-codon-out classification assigns all thirteen residual codons to named modules, with no unassigned codon and no extra codon outside the module list.  The complete assignment is
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{AGR} &= \{AGA,AGG\},\\
\mathrm{AR/Lys} &= \{AAA\},\\
\mathrm{CUN} &= \{CUA,CUC,CUG,CUU\},\\
\mathrm{Ile/Met} &= \{AUA\},\\
\mathrm{SSN} &= \{UCA,UUA\},\\
\mathrm{Stop/Trp} &= \{UAA,UAG,UGA\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The finite audit reports $R$-size $13$, assigned count $13$, missing-from-module count $0$, unassigned count $0$, and extra-module count $0$.  Moreover each module contains codons classified as predictable or boundary-supported in the leave-one-codon-out run.  This is the core row-level assertion: the residual codons do not merely form a thirteen-point set; they split into six biologically legible code-table modules, and every module participates in the predictable-or-boundary classification.

\paragraph{Interpretation boundary.}
The scientific reading is deliberately limited.  The curated standard-code table supplies codon-to-amino-acid or codon-to-stop labels, and therefore supports code-layer readback and local label-split tests.  It does not observe ribosomal translation, folded protein structure, physical admissibility, biological function, or a universal mechanism.  The module classification therefore cannot be promoted to a statement that DNA must obey this geometry as a biological necessity.  It also cannot turn an amino-acid label sequence into a structural or functional protein claim.  Any claim crossing that boundary would require a separate contact: for example a translation assay for execution, a structural observation or validated structure-prediction contact for folding, a physical model for admissibility, and functional experiments for biological effect.

\paragraph{Verdict.}
For the claim identified by the leave-one-codon-out module classification row, the finite evidence is ready at the code-read layer.  The residual set is completely listed, the punctured-cube face vector is finite and explicit, the thirteen codons are exhausted by six named modules, and every module has predictable-or-boundary support.  The recovery, family-hit, null-shape, and spectral values are all concrete finite-row numbers.  The result should therefore be stated as a local codon-window classification of the standard-code label table, with a strict boundary against translation, folding, physical admissibility, function, mechanism, and universality.
