\paragraph{Finite object.}
The record is a code-read packet for the standard-code codon table. Its carrier is the six-bit codon cube $Q_6$, read as the four-letter codon alphabet with three positions and two binary coordinates per position. The finite distinctions used here are the six-bit display, local tile faces, singleton label corners, and three-point label classes. The observed contact is only a curated codon-to-label table: it assigns each codon an amino-acid or stop label, and it gives no direct observation of translation dynamics, protein folding, physical admissibility, biological function, mechanism, or universality.

\paragraph{Shape significance.}
Against the sampled null model for the $M$ shape, the run used $N=2000$ samples, seed $42$, and $57377$ distinct-sample attempts, with $55377$ duplicate retries. There was $1$ exact shape match, giving $p_{\mathrm{exact}}=0.0005$, and there were $25$ matches with Jaccard similarity at least $0.9$, giving $p_{J\geq 0.9}=0.0125$. Under the amino-acid-class-preserving null, the observed $M$ size was $20$, the exact-match count was $0$, and the lower-tail count at or below the observed size was $7$ out of $2000$, giving
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{\mathrm{aa\ class,exact}}&=0.0,\\
p_{\mathrm{aa\ class,lower\ tail}}&=0.0035.
\end{aligned}
$$
These numbers make the local pattern nontrivial relative to the tested randomizations. They remain finite-row evidence about codon-label organization, not evidence about folded molecular structure.

\paragraph{Boundary of interpretation.}
The conclusion is a local code-layer classification: the six-bit window and its boundary modules organize a finite set of standard-code labels with strong recovery behavior in the tested rows. The curated table is the contact that supplies the labels; the coordinate geometry supplies the finite patterning. Nothing in this packet observes ribosomal realization, folding, structural order, physical admissibility, biological function, a universal mechanism, or a necessity law for DNA. Any such stronger assertion would require a separate contact at the appropriate biological layer.
