\subsection{Leave-one-codon module classification}
\label{subsec:h1-leave-one-codon-out-module-classification}

\paragraph{Claim scope.}
This record concerns the standard-code codon table viewed through a six-coordinate codon display. The carrier is the finite codon cube $Q_6$, and the biological readout used here is only the curated assignment of codons to amino-acid or stop labels. The claim is therefore a code-layer claim: window-six coordinates may expose a local boundary-tile organization among codons whose labels are sensitive under leave-one-codon or leave-one-code perturbations. The claim does not assert that the coordinate pattern is a law of biology, nor that the pattern realizes translation, folding, physical admissibility, function, mechanism, or universality. Those stronger assertions would require independent contacts beyond the codon-label table.

\paragraph{Finite support set.}
The distinguished residual set $R$ has cardinality $13$ and is exactly
$\{\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UUA}\}$. The punctured-cube audit reports the same codon list and the face-count vector $(13,16,4)$, so the row is not merely a label inventory: it also carries a finite local shape. The spectral row gives $\lambda_M=0.6752479308830213$, $\lambda_R=0.4758352843197002$, and $\mu=3.051487585298128$, with the radial cubic residual $-6.224354365258478\times 10^{-12}$. These values are used as numerical checks on the coordinate construction, not as mechanistic biological causes.

\paragraph{Module partition.}
The leave-one-codon module classification assigns all $13$ codons of $R$ and leaves no residue outside the listed modules: the audit reports $R\_size=13$, $assigned\_count=13$, $extra\_module\_codons=\emptyset$, $missing\_from\_modules=\emptyset$, and $unassigned\_codons=\emptyset$. The modules are
$$\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{agr} &= \{\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG}\},\\
\mathrm{ar\_lys} &= \{\mathrm{AAA}\},\\
\mathrm{cun} &= \{\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU}\},\\
\mathrm{ile\_met} &= \{\mathrm{AUA}\},\\
\mathrm{ssn} &= \{\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UUA}\},\\
\mathrm{stop\_trp} &= \{\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UGA}\}.
\end{aligned}$$
Each module contains codons that the leave-one-codon audit classifies as predictable or boundary-supported. The same audit records $predictable\_count=8$, $boundary\_count=5$, $anchor\_count=0$, $closure\_coverage\_high=8$, and $recovery\_rate=1.0$. Thus the finite row closes the local classification problem for this $R$-set: every codon in the residual set is accounted for by one of the six named modules, and every module has a predictable-or-boundary certificate inside the finite computation.

\paragraph{Cross-table behavior.}
The leave-one-code-out audit evaluated $23$ code tables and $63$ targets. It reports a baseline hit rate of $0.19821739130434785$, a hit rate in $M-T$ of $0.9365079365079365$, and a hit rate in $M-T$ plus boundary of $0.9841269841269841$. The row also records $hit\_rate\_above\_baseline=0.9841269841269841$. By family, the above-baseline rates are $1.0$ for bacterial-plastid, mitochondrial, nuclear-ciliate, and candidate-other families, and $0.8333333333333334$ for alternative-yeast. These numbers support the narrow statement that the window-six local tile is effective as a code-label classifier under the tested perturbation regime.

\paragraph{Null comparisons.}
The coordinate shape is not treated as a free interpretation of the table. Against $N=2000$ null samples with seed $42$, the exact shape count is $1$, giving $p\_exact=0.0005$, while the count with Jaccard similarity at least $0.9$ is $25$, giving $p\_{\mathrm{Jaccard}\ge 0.9}=0.0125$. In the amino-acid-class-preserving null, again with $N=2000$ and seed $42$, the observed $M$ size is $20$, the exact-match count is $0$, the lower-tail count at or below the observed size is $7$, the exact-shape probability is $0.0$, and the lower-tail probability is $0.0035$. These null rows say that the finite shape and its class-preserving behavior are statistically nontrivial within the stated tests. They do not upgrade the result into a biological mechanism.

\paragraph{Local readout boundary.}
The only reality contact used here is a curated standard-code table assigning codons to amino-acid or stop labels. That contact can test code readback and local codon-tile label splits. It cannot test translation realization, folded structure, structural order, physical admissibility, biological function, or universality. The codon-label readout therefore remains at the label layer. A future structural or functional statement would need a separate observation stream, such as an organism-matched translation assay, structure contact, physical admissibility test, or functional experiment. Without that added contact, the finite module classification is a complete local code-table result and nothing stronger.
