\paragraph{Finite codon carrier.}
This record concerns the standard-code codon table viewed through a six-bit codon display.  The finite carrier is the codon cube $Q_6$, while the observed label contact is the curated assignment of codons to amino-acid or stop labels in the standard code.  The claim is deliberately local: window-six coordinates may reveal a boundary-tile pattern in the code-label table, but the label table itself is the contact that supplies biological names.  The distinguished residual set has cardinality thirteen.  In the recorded row it is
$$
\begin{aligned}
R=\{&AAA,AGA,AGG,AUA,CUA,CUC,CUG,CUU,\\
&UAA,UAG,UCA,UGA,UUA\},
\end{aligned}
$$
with $|R|=13$.  The same row identifies $R$ as a punctured-cube shaped subset with face-vector $(13,16,4)$, so the combinatorial support is not merely a list of codons but a small local tile with vertices, edges, and two-dimensional faces.

In the amino-acid-class-preserving null row, again with $N=2000$ and seed $42$, the observed module size was $20$, the exact-shape count was $0$, and the lower-tail count at or below the observed size was $7$. These numbers support a finite code-layer anomaly relative to the sampled nulls; they do not by themselves identify a molecular mechanism.

This is the finite content of the claim: when one codon is withheld, the local coordinate-and-boundary organization still covers the residual modules without leaving a codon outside the module ledger.

\paragraph{Local boundary of interpretation.}
The observed contact is a curated standard-code table.  It says which codon receives which amino-acid or stop label, and it can test code readback and local label splits.  It does not observe ribosomal translation, folding, structural order, physical admissibility, biochemical function, or a universal biological law.  Consequently, an amino-acid label sequence produced from this table is not a folded protein structure, and a window-six tile is not a global law of DNA or protein realization.  Any future statement about translation, structure, admissibility, function, mechanism, or universality must bring a separate contact that directly measures that layer.  Without such a contact, the present claim remains a finite code-read classification: a thirteen-codon punctured-cube residual set, a complete six-module assignment, and leave-one recovery statistics tied to the standard-code label table.
