\subsection{Leave-One-Codon-Out Module Classification in the Six-Bit Codon Cube}
\label{subsec:h1-leave-one-codon-out-module-classification}

\paragraph{Finite object under test.}
The record concerns the standard-code codon table viewed through a six-coordinate codon display.  Each codon is treated as a vertex of the window-six cube, while the curated standard genetic-code table supplies only the amino-acid or stop label attached to that vertex.  The object tested here is therefore a code-layer readback problem: whether the finite coordinate pattern isolates a small boundary set and whether the leave-one-codon-out rows fall into complete local modules.  The observed boundary set is
$$
\begin{aligned}
R=\{&\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\\
&\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UUA}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
Its measured cardinality is $|R|=13$, and the punctured-cube face count is $(13,16,4)$.  Thus the row is not merely a list of exceptional codons; it is a finite local shape with thirteen vertices, sixteen one-dimensional incidences, and four two-dimensional faces inside the displayed codon geometry.

\paragraph{Spectral and sampling checks.}
The associated spectral summary separates the boundary set from the larger measured module $M$.  The recorded values are

\begin{aligned}
\lambda_M &= 0.6752479308830213,\\
\lambda_R &= 0.4758352843197002,\\
\mu &= 3.051487585298128,
\end{aligned}
$$
with radial-polynomial residual $-6.224354365258478\cdot 10^{-12}$, so the numerical certificate is at floating-point zero for the polynomial relation used in the finite run.  In the shape-significance run with $N=2000$ random draws, there was $1$ exact shape match, giving $p_{\mathrm{exact}}=0.0005$, and $25$ samples with Jaccard similarity at least $0.9$, giving $p_{\mathrm{Jaccard}\ge 0.9}=0.0125$.  The sampler needed $57377$ distinct-sample attempts and rejected $55377$ duplicates, so the reported denominator is not an unfiltered stream count but the retained sample size.  A second null model preserving amino-acid class gave observed $|M|=20$, $0$ exact-shape matches among $2000$ samples, and $7$ lower-tail samples at or below the observed $M$ size, with lower-tail probability $0.0035$.

\paragraph{Leave-one-code and leave-one-codon behavior.}
Across the leave-one-code-out test, $23$ genetic-code tables supplied $63$ total targets.  The baseline hit rate was $0.19821739130434785$, while the hit rate above baseline was $0.9841269841269841$.  The stricter interior count $M\setminus T$ reached $0.9365079365079365$, and allowing the boundary recovered the same $0.9841269841269841$ rate.  The leave-one-codon-out row then sharpened the result: there were $0$ anchors, $5$ boundary cases, $8$ high-coverage closure cases, and $8$ predictable cases, with recovery rate $1.0$.  This makes the row a local classification statement rather than a global biological mechanism: every recovered codon is accounted for by a finite code-coordinate relation, but the relation remains a label-table relation.

\paragraph{Module classification.}
The module partition is complete on the thirteen codons of $R$.  The recorded modules are
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{agr} &= \{\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG}\},\\
\mathrm{ar\_lys} &= \{\mathrm{AAA}\},\\
\mathrm{cun} &= \{\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU}\},\\
\mathrm{ile\_met} &= \{\mathrm{AUA}\},\\
\mathrm{ssn} &= \{\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UUA}\},\\
\mathrm{stop\_trp} &= \{\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UGA}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The completeness counters are $R\_size=13$ and $assigned\_count=13$, with no extra module codons, no missing codons, and no unassigned codons.  Thus the row classifies every boundary codon into exactly the named finite modules supplied by the run.  The family-level leave-one-code-out check is compatible with this picture: the hit-rate-above-baseline values are $1.0$ for bacterial-plastid, mitochondrial, nuclear-ciliate, and candidate-other families, and $0.8333333333333334$ for alternative-yeast.

\paragraph{Reality contact and local verdict.}
The only biological contact used here is the curated standard-code table, whose observed fact is that codons are assigned amino-acid or stop labels.  Consequently the verified conclusion is restricted to code-layer readback: the six-bit coordinate display exposes a local boundary-tile organization, the row recovers the leave-one-codon-out cases at rate $1.0$, and the thirteen boundary codons are fully assigned to six named modules.  Nothing in this record establishes translation realization, folded structure, physical admissibility, biological function, a universal mechanism, or a law governing DNA.  Any such promotion would require a separate contact that directly observes that higher layer.  In particular, reading back an amino-acid label sequence from the codon table is still not a protein-structure observation.
