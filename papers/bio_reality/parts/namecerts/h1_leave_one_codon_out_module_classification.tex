\subsection{Leave-one-codon module classification in the window-six codon cube}
\label{subsec:h1-leave-one-codon-out-module-classification}

\paragraph{Finite coordinate object.}
The record studied here is the standard-code codon table viewed through a six-coordinate codon display.  The carrier is the $64$-vertex codon cube $Q_6$, with codons read as finite words over the four RNA bases and then placed in the six-bit coordinate presentation induced by base distinctions.  The relevant local object is not the whole cube but a punctured codon region $R$ detected by the code-label readback.  In this run the region has cardinality $13$, and the complete row list is
$$
\begin{aligned}
R=\{&\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\\
&\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UUA}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The same finite set is recorded as a punctured cube with face vector $(13,16,4)$.  Thus the object under discussion is a local combinatorial tile with $13$ vertices, $16$ one-dimensional incidences, and $4$ two-dimensional faces.  The row data are therefore small enough that the classification claim is a finite census statement, not an asymptotic or model-selection assertion.

This is the narrow mathematical content of the claim: the punctured $13$-row codon tile admits a complete finite module census whose rows are covered by the predictable-or-boundary dichotomy observed in the leave-one-codon-out test.

\paragraph{Reality contact and scope.}
The only biological contact used here is the curated standard genetic-code table, understood as a codon-to-label table assigning RNA codons to amino-acid or stop labels.  It supplies the label readback needed to name the singleton corners, the three-row label classes, and the module names above.  It does not supply a translation apparatus, a folding experiment, a molecular-physics admissibility test, a function assay, or a universal mechanism.  Consequently the chapter asserts a finite code-layer classification inside the window-six codon coordinate representation and nothing stronger.  If a later argument wants to speak about translation realization, folded structure, physical viability, function, or biological universality, it must attach a separate empirical contact appropriate to that higher layer.  The present row data support only the local codon-tile split, its spectrum, its finite module census, and its measured code-label recovery behavior.
