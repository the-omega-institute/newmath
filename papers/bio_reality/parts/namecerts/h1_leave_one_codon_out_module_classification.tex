\subsection{Leave-One-Codon-Out Module Classification}\label{sec:bioreality-h1-leave-one-codon-out-module-classification}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite codon carrier.}
The object studied here is the standard-code codon table viewed through a six-coordinate codon display.  Each codon is treated as a point of the finite cube $Q_6$ obtained from three nucleotide positions with two binary coordinates per position.  The biological readout is not inferred from the cube itself: the amino-acid and stop labels are supplied by the curated standard-code table.  The finite question is therefore whether a window-six coordinate geometry organizes the codons whose labels become unstable under leave-one-codon or leave-one-code perturbations, while the label table remains an external contact at the code-read layer.

\paragraph{Recovered residual set.}
The finite residual set $R$ has cardinality $13$.  Its members are
$$
\begin{aligned}
R=\{&AAA, AGA, AGG, AUA, CUA, CUC, CUG, CUU,\\
&UAA, UAG, UCA, UGA, UUA\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The same set is witnessed as a punctured cubical tile with face-vector
$$
\begin{aligned}
(f_0,f_1,f_2)=(13,16,4).
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the observed residual is not merely a count.  It carries a finite local face structure: thirteen vertices, sixteen one-dimensional adjacencies, and four two-dimensional faces inside the codon display.  This is the geometric content of the local-tile reading.  It does not by itself assert any biological law; it records that the codons singled out by the finite experiment occupy a structured region of the coordinate carrier.

\paragraph{Leave-one-code and leave-one-codon behavior.}
The leave-one-code experiment evaluated $23$ code tables and $63$ total targets.  Its baseline hit rate was

\begin{aligned}

\end{aligned}
$$
whereas the hit rate above baseline was
$$
\begin{aligned}
0.9841269841269841.
\end{aligned}
$$
The hit rate inside $M-T$ was $0.9365079365079365$, and the hit rate inside $M-T$ together with the boundary was $0.9841269841269841$.  The leave-one-codon experiment reported $0$ anchors, $5$ boundary cases, $8$ high-coverage closure cases, $8$ predictable cases, and recovery rate
$$
\begin{aligned}
1.0.
\end{aligned}
$$
The finite reading is that the codon-coordinate tile has predictive and boundary roles for code-label recovery under the tested leave-one regimes.  The boundary cases are essential: they prevent the local coordinate result from being promoted into a statement about all biological layers.

\paragraph{Module classification.}
The module classification is complete for the residual set.  It assigns all $13$ residual codons and leaves no extra module codons, no missing residual codons, and no unassigned codons:
$$
\begin{aligned}
R_{\mathrm{size}} &= 13,\\
\mathrm{assigned} &= 13,\\
\mathrm{extra} &= 0,\\
\mathrm{missing} &= 0,\\
\mathrm{unassigned} &= 0.
\end{aligned}
$$
The six observed modules are
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{agr} &= \{AGA,AGG\},\\
\mathrm{ar\_lys} &= \{AAA\},\\
\mathrm{cun} &= \{CUA,CUC,CUG,CUU\},\\
\mathrm{ile\_met} &= \{AUA\},\\
\mathrm{ssn} &= \{UCA,UUA\},\\
\mathrm{stop\_trp} &= \{UAA,UAG,UGA\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
Every listed module has either a predictable member or a boundary member in the leave-one-codon analysis.  This is the local classification result: the residual codons decompose into named code-label modules that exhaust the observed tile and preserve the predictable-or-boundary status required by the finite probe.

\paragraph{Reality contact and scope.}
The only reality contact used here is the curated standard genetic-code table, which supplies codon-to-label assignments at codon-label resolution.  That contact can test code-read behavior and local codon-tile label splits.  It cannot test translation realization, folded structure, structural order, physical admissibility, biological function, or a universal mechanism.  Consequently, the result of this subsection is a finite code-layer classification: the window-six codon carrier exposes a local residual tile, the tile is spectrally and combinatorially constrained, and the thirteen residual codons are completely assigned to six predictable-or-boundary modules.  No conclusion about translation, folding, physical admissibility, function, mechanism, or universality follows from these data unless an additional reality contact supplies observations at that higher layer.
