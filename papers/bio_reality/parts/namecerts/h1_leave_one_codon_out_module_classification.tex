\subsection{Leave-one-codon-out modules in the window-six codon cube}

\paragraph{Claim.}
This record concerns the standard-code codon table read at the code-label layer.  The finite carrier is the six-coordinate codon cube $Q_6$, with codons displayed as six-bit words after the usual two-bit expansion of the four RNA bases.  The observed object is not translation itself, but the table-level assignment of a codon to an amino-acid label or to stop.  The claim is that the leave-one-codon-out locus has a complete module classification: every codon in the distinguished residual set $R$ belongs to one of six named finite modules, and every such module is covered by the predictable-or-boundary certificate reported by the row.

\paragraph{Residual set.}
The residual set has cardinality $13$.  Its codons are
$$
\begin{aligned}
R=\{&\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\\
&\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UUA}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The punctured-cube check reports the same set and gives the finite face-count vector
$$
\begin{aligned}
(f_0,f_1,f_2)=(13,16,4).
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the row is not merely counting exceptional codons; it records a local cubical subcomplex with thirteen vertices, sixteen edges, and four square faces inside the six-coordinate codon display.

\paragraph{Null-model separation.}

$$
\begin{aligned}

\end{aligned}
$$
An amino-acid-class-preserving null run, also with $N=2000$ and seed $42$, reports observed $M$-size $20$, exact-match count $0$, and lower-tail count $7$.  Its tabulated probabilities are
$$
\begin{aligned}

The finite evidence therefore supports a non-random local code-label pattern under the tested nulls, while remaining confined to the codon-label table and its derived coordinate checks.

\paragraph{Reality contact.}
The empirical contact used here is the curated standard genetic-code table.  It supplies codon-to-label readback at the code layer: codons are assigned to amino-acid labels or to stop labels.  That contact is adequate for checking the local label split, the residual codon set, the module assignment, and the finite recovery statistics.  It is not a contact with ribosomal realization, folding dynamics, thermodynamic admissibility, organism-level function, or a universal causal mechanism.  The chapter therefore reads the codon table as a finite code-label object and treats all higher biological layers as absent from this packet unless another observation is attached.

\paragraph{Boundary of interpretation.}
The finite results do not imply that biological translation is generated by the window-six geometry.  They do not imply that an amino-acid label sequence has a folded structure, that a protein is physically admissible, that a biological function is realized, or that the same local tile is a law across organisms.  In particular, the CUN, UUR-related, AGR, Ile--Met, and Stop--Trp groupings are code-label modules in this row.  They are not evidence for folded structure or for a mechanism of reassignment.  A separate structure experiment, translation contact, physical model, or functional assay would be needed before any such statement could be made.

\paragraph{Conclusion.}
For the claim $\mathrm{h1.leave\_one\_codon\_out.module\_classification}$, the finite data are complete at the code-read layer.  The residual set has $13$ codons, its punctured-cube vector is $(13,16,4)$, all $13$ residual codons are assigned to six modules, and no extra or missing module codons are reported.  The accompanying spectral, null-model, leave-one-code-out, and leave-one-codon-out rows give mutually consistent numerical support for a local boundary-tile classification of the standard-code label table.  The correct reading is therefore a finite, reality-contacted code-label result with an explicit stop at the translation, folding, physical-admissibility, function, mechanism, and universality boundaries.
