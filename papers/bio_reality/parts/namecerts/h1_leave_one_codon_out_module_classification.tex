\paragraph{Finite codon window.}
This record studies the standard-code codon table through a six-bit codon display on the codon cube $Q_6$. The coordinate view distinguishes a local tile face, singleton label corners, and three-point label classes; the readback is the curated standard-code assignment of codons to amino-acid or stop labels. \end{aligned}

The reported cubic residual is $-6.224354365258478\times 10^{-12}$, the same value recorded as the radial polynomial residual, so the numerical check is within the stated tolerance. These rows support the claim that the window-six tile is a non-generic finite feature of the code-label table, while still leaving the biological meaning of the labels to the curated contact.

\paragraph{Module classification.}
The module classification is complete on the thirteen-codon region: $R\_size=13$, $assigned\_count=13$, with no extra module codons, no missing codons, and no unassigned codons.  The six modules are
$$
\begin{aligned}
AGR &= \{AGA,AGG\},\\
AR\_Lys &= \{AAA\},\\
CUN &= \{CUA,CUC,CUG,CUU\},\\
Ile\_Met &= \{AUA\},\\
SSN &= \{UCA,UUA\},\\
Stop\_Trp &= \{UAA,UAG,UGA\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
Every listed module has either a predictable member or a boundary member in the leave-one-codon test.  This is the specific content of the classification claim: the finite exceptional region is exhausted by six named modules, and the leave-one-codon behavior covers those modules without residue.  The classification does not assert that the modules are universal biological mechanisms; it records a finite partition of the code-layer boundary pattern supported by the standard-code label table.

\paragraph{Scope of the contact.}
The only reality contact used here is the curated standard genetic-code bridge, whose observed fact is that codons receive amino-acid or stop labels.  This contact can test code readback and local codon-tile label splits.  It cannot test whether translation is realized in a cell, whether an amino-acid string folds, whether a molecule is physically admissible, whether a protein has a function, or whether a universal mechanism is present.  Therefore an amino-acid label sequence read from this table remains a code-layer object.  Any step from these finite codon labels to translation, folded structure, physical admissibility, biological function, mechanism, or universality would require an additional independent contact with the relevant phenomenon.

\paragraph{Conclusion.}
The finite rows support a ready code-layer claim.  In the standard-code table, the thirteen-codon region $R$ forms the recorded punctured-cube tile, has the stated spectral signature, survives two finite null comparisons, and is completely assigned to six leave-one-codon modules.  The correct scientific reading is local and bounded: window-six codon coordinates expose a recoverable boundary-tile organization of curated labels.  They do not, by themselves, promote the table into a theory of translation, folding, physical chemistry, function, mechanism, or biological necessity.
