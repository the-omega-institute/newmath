\subsection{Residual codon topology after the amino-acid quotient}\label{sec:bior-reality-q6-residual-codon-topology}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Object of record.}
This record treats codon usage as an organism-indexed finite table and asks which synonymous coordinates remain visible after amino-acid identity has already been held fixed.  The carrier is the per-organism CDS codon-count table projected to the amino-acid-residual basis $B^{*}_{Q6}$.  In this basis the recorded coordinates are
$$
\begin{aligned}
B^{*}_{Q6}=\{&K_{AAA},\ Arg_{AGR},\ Ile_{AUA},\ Leu_{CUN/UUR},\ Leu_{UUA/UUG},\\
&Ser_{UCR/AGY},\ Ser_{UCA/UCG},\ Thr_{ACR/ACY},\ f_{3}\text{-stress antisymmetric}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The intended readback is conditional: for a basis element $q$ and readout $r$, estimate a survival contrast
$$
\begin{aligned}
S_Q(r\mid Z)=J_0(Z)-J_Q(Z,q),
\end{aligned}
$$
where $Z$ includes the amino-acid sequence $y$, local context $c$, and simpler codon-feature baselines.  A positive value is therefore not a free-standing biological law; it is a conditional improvement over the chosen baseline and control set.

\paragraph{Finite coordinate facts.}
The finite computation named \texttt{residual\_basis\_q6\_topology\_validation} reports that the residual basis has $9$ elements.  In the organism sample, $7$ of these $9$ residual coordinates have nonzero variation above the variance threshold $0.01$, giving nonzero fraction
$$
\begin{aligned}
\frac{7}{9}=0.7777777777777778.
\end{aligned}
$$
The organism contact is deliberately small: $3$ organisms are present, namely \texttt{homo\_sapiens}, \texttt{saccharomyces\_cerevisiae}, and \texttt{escherichia\_coli\_k12}.  Each organism contributes one codon-count table, and the NCBI genetic-code interpretation is recorded as loaded.  This is a finite contact with three named rows, not a population estimate over organisms.

The product-decomposition residue for $M$ is recorded as empty.  Thus the finite data distinguish two levels: the $M$ quotient has a compact ten-class geometry, while the amino-acid-residual basis keeps nine explicit synonymous directions that are not collapsed to $M$-membership.

\paragraph{Biological reading.}
The scientific question is whether any coordinate in $B^{*}_{Q6}$ participates in DNA-to-protein realization after amino-acid composition has already been controlled.  The admissible test is not raw codon-count variation.  It is a paired statistic asking whether some residual coordinate improves a readout such as tAI, ribosome occupancy, or protein abundance relative to an $M$-density baseline and the controls in $Z$.  Equivalently, the counterexample search asks whether every organism and every readout satisfies
$$
\begin{aligned}
S_Q(r\mid Z,\Delta_M)\leq 0
\end{aligned}
$$
for all $Q\in B^{*}_{Q6}$, or whether at least one residual coordinate breaks that inequality.  The Leu $CUN/UUR$ contrast is especially diagnostic because an $M$-only model cannot directly test the split between the two Leu codon families; similarly, the $AAA/AAG$ distinction is not resolved by local $M$ density alone.

\paragraph{Contact status.}
Three contact classes are available in the record: per-organism codon-usage summaries, organism-level tRNA gene-copy annotations, and organism-scoped tAI or stAI weights.  These are suitable categories for a bounded translation-layer readout when paired correctly.  They are not yet the paired controlled statistic itself.  The current mismatch record says that no organism-matched table has yet combined codon usage, relevant tRNA-copy spectra, and tAI or stAI readouts into estimates of $S_Q(r\mid Z,\Delta_M)$.  For that reason, the finite result is a coordinate-level and contact-planning result, not yet an empirical survival result for a translation readout.

\paragraph{Local boundary.}
The verified finite facts show that the residual codon coordinates are computable, nontrivial in $7$ of $9$ slots over the three named organisms, and properly separated from the ten-class $M$ quotient.  They do not by themselves establish a translation-efficiency effect, ribosome-mediated mechanism, protein abundance relation, folded structure, physical admissibility, biological function, biochemical mechanism, or organism-universal rule.  Any such stronger assertion requires an additional contact that directly measures or validly models the corresponding layer and includes the amino-acid-composition control.
