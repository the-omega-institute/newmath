\subsection{Codon-Usage Residual Topology After the Amino-Acid Quotient}
\label{sec:bior-q6-residual-topology-aa-quotient}

\paragraph{Finite row under examination.}
This record concerns organism-level coding-sequence codon counts after the amino-acid sequence has already been held fixed as the primary compositional control.  The finite contact table contains one codon-count summary for each of three organisms: \emph{Homo sapiens}, \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}, and \emph{Escherichia coli} K-12.  The NCBI genetic code is loaded in the finite computation, and the row is explicitly scoped as finite contact rather than as a population estimate.  The biological coordinate is the amino-acid-residual codon basis
$B^{*}_{Q6}$, consisting of the nine named residual coordinates
$K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $Leu_{CUN/UUR}$, $Leu_{UUA/UUG}$, $Ser_{UCR/AGY}$, $Ser_{UCA/UCG}$, $Thr_{ACR/ACY}$, and the antisymmetric $f_{3}$-stress pair.  These coordinates are not treated as a replacement for amino-acid sequence.  They are the part of codon usage left visible after the amino-acid quotient has removed the direct amino-acid identity signal.

\paragraph{Observed residual support.}
The finite computation reports that all nine residual coordinates of $B^{*}_{Q6}$ were constructed, and that seven of the nine had nonzero organism-level residual variance above the stated threshold $0.01$.  Thus the measured finite nonzero fraction is
$$
\begin{aligned}
  \frac{7}{9} &= 0.7777777777777778.
\end{aligned}
$$
This number is a structural observation about the three organism-level codon-count rows.  It says that the amino-acid quotient does not collapse the selected $Q6$ residual basis to zero in this finite panel.  It does not, by itself, say that any coordinate improves translation, predicts protein abundance, or identifies a molecular mechanism.  The only conclusion carried by this row is that the residual codon coordinates remain numerically distinguishable after amino-acid composition is controlled at the level of the selected count summaries.

\paragraph{Motif compression contact.}
A separate finite check identifies the relevant motif set $R$ exactly as the following thirteen codons:
$AAA$, $AGA$, $AGG$, $AUA$, $CUA$, $CUC$, $CUG$, $CUU$, $UAA$, $UAG$, $UCA$, $UGA$, and $UUA$.  The same check reports no missing codons and no extra codons relative to the motif-union computation.  This matters here because several members of $B^{*}_{Q6}$ are not single-codon indicators but contrasts between codon families.  In particular, the leucine and serine coordinates require contrasts such as $CUN$ against $UUR$, $UUA$ against $UUG$, $UCR$ against $AGY$, and $UCA$ against $UCG$.  The finite motif compression therefore supplies a bounded codon-level vocabulary in which the residual coordinates can be named without collapsing them into a single synonymous-density statistic.

The quotient row therefore motivates a residual test rather than closing the biological claim.

\paragraph{Readout statistic.}
For a readout $r$ such as tAI, ribosome occupancy, or protein abundance, the intended survival comparison for a residual coordinate $q \in B^{*}_{Q6}$ is
$$
\begin{aligned}
  S_{Q}(r\mid Z) &= J_{0}(Z)-J_{Q}(Z,q),
\end{aligned}
$$
where $Z$ includes the amino-acid sequence $y$, organism and gene context, and simpler codon-feature baselines.  When an $M$-density baseline is explicitly included, the corresponding test is
$$
\begin{aligned}
  S_{Q}(r\mid Z,\Delta_{M}) &> 0.
\end{aligned}
$$
A positive value would mean only that the residual coordinate improved the selected bounded readout relative to the stated baseline and controls.  A nonpositive value would mean that this coordinate did not survive that particular controlled comparison.  The current finite row has not yet supplied the paired organism-matched tAI, stAI, ribosome-occupancy, or abundance statistic needed to evaluate this inequality.

\paragraph{Contact scope.}
Three contact classes are available for the eventual test.  Per-organism codon-usage tables can test codon usage distribution but not folded structure, physical admissibility, function, or a universal rule.  Organism-level tRNA gene-copy spectra can test a translation-layer relation at the level of annotation summaries, but they do not observe ribosome motion or protein output directly.  tAI or stAI weights can provide a bounded codon-adaptation readout, but they are themselves modeled annotations and must not be confused with a direct mechanism.  Therefore the present finite row establishes residual coordinate availability and finite nontriviality, while the stronger translation-realization comparison remains conditional on a paired controlled readout table.

\paragraph{Local claim boundary.}
The row does not assert ribosome-mediated translation realization from codon counts alone.  It does not infer protein folding, structural order, physical admissibility, biological function, biochemical mechanism, or universal biological law.  It also does not interpret the signs of the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residuals as universal directionality across organisms.  The valid local statement is narrower: in the three-organism codon-count contact, the amino-acid-residual $Q6$ basis is computable, has nine named coordinates, and shows seven nonzero residual coordinates above threshold after the amino-acid quotient.  Any promotion from this coordinate fact to translation, abundance, mechanism, structure, function, or universality requires a separate contact that supplies the relevant controlled readout.
