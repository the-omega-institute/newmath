\paragraph{Finite object.}
The finite object considered here is not a protein-folding claim.  It is a row-level request for a coding-window statistic in which the $f_3$ stress coordinate is evaluated only after synonymous codon variation has been quotiented by amino-acid composition.  The admissible row is keyed by organism, coding sequence, codon position modulo $3$, local paired-codon counts, a bounded wobble-weight surface, matched mRNA abundance, and a position-resolved ribosome-occupancy or translation-efficiency readout.  Its intended readback is a residual survival entry
$$
\begin{aligned}
S_{f_3}(T_{\mathrm{pos}}\mid Z),
\end{aligned}
$$
where $T_{\mathrm{pos}}$ is a codon-indexed or local-window ribosome-footprint statistic and $Z$ includes organism, amino-acid composition or amino-acid bin, GC3, coding-sequence length, $M$-density, matched mRNA abundance, positional coverage, mappability, rank sufficiency, and simpler codon-count baselines.  If this joined matrix is absent, rank-degenerate, or non-positive after ablation, the coordinate remains a sequence-topology residual and is not promoted to a translation, protein, structure, function, mechanism, phenotype, or universal-law statement.

The codon-side finite support is explicitly recorded.  The set $M=\operatorname{Med}(R)\cup\{\mathrm{ACA},\mathrm{ACG}\}$ has the twenty codons

$$
The recorded $f_3$ saturation of $R$ removes exactly the two added codons $\mathrm{ACA}$ and $\mathrm{ACG}$ and leaves the eighteen-codon set
$$
\begin{aligned}
\operatorname{Sat}_{f_3}(R)=\{&\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\\

\end{aligned}
$$
The recorded boundary set for $f_3$ is empty.  Thus the finite coordinate is not being introduced by an unaccounted boundary codon; it is a controlled residual coordinate whose biological admissibility depends entirely on whether a separate position-resolved translation readout can be joined on the same organism, coding sequence, and window.

A positive biological readout would still require an externally supplied window table and an explicit residual statistic.

\paragraph{Decision rule.}
The admissible statistic is a bounded survival comparison, not a narrative association.  On the joined table one would fit the baseline and augmented descriptions
$$
\begin{aligned}
T_{\mathrm{pos}}&\sim Z+\mathrm{simple\ codon\ baselines},\\
T_{\mathrm{pos}}&\sim Z+\mathrm{simple\ codon\ baselines}+f_3\text{-}\mathrm{stress},
\end{aligned}
$$
and then retain the packet only if the augmented model gives a non-vacuous positive survival after rank checks and ablation of simpler alternatives.  The current finite records do not contain the required same-organism, same-CDS, same-window codon counts, wobble-weight rows, matched mRNA rows, and codon-indexed footprint-occupancy rows for this exact statistic.  Therefore the correct local verdict is needs-more-data: the coordinate and several neighboring biological surfaces are finite and auditable, but the translation-readout matrix that would connect them has not been supplied.

No protein abundance, folding stability, thermal stability, structural order, physical admissibility, molecular function, mechanism, phenotype, or universality follows from the $f_3$ stress coordinate, from the twenty-codon $M$ support, from the eighteen-codon saturation support, from the ten-point quotient, from modeled tAI or stAI weights, or from the existence of downstream protein datasets.
