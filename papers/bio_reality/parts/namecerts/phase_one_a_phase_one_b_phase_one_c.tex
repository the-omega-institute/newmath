\paragraph{Local boundary.}
The admissible conclusion is narrow.  The phase-one contact is a strong finite contact with the genetic-code table, and the later $Q\to T\to P$ rows give a controlled statistical mediation surface for $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinates, modeled tAI, and matched protein abundance.  A direct $Q_i\to P_k$ association is not read as protein realization unless the corresponding $Q_i\to T_j$ step is present and the stated controls have been applied.  No translation realization follows from the phase-one codon-table row alone; no ribosome occupancy follows from modeled tAI alone; no folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, biological mechanism, phenotype, selection law, or universal rule follows from any residual coordinate unless an additional layer-matched measurement supplies that specific contact and its own finite statistic.
