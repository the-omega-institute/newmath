\subsection{Phase-One Genetic-Code Contact and Translation-Mediated Protein-Abundance Boundary}
\label{sec:bioreality-phase-one-q6-translation-protein-boundary}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite claim surface.}
This record concerns the finite mediation surface whose rows are triples $(q_i,t_j,p_k)$.  The $q_i$ coordinates are the nine $B^*_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates
$K\_AAA$, $Arg\_AGR$, $Ile\_AUA$, $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$, $Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG$, $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$, $Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG$, $Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY$, and $f3\_stress$.  The translation readout $t_j$ is allowed to be a bounded codon-adaptation or translation-efficiency readout, including modeled tAI, stAI, ribosome occupancy, or translation efficiency, and the protein readout $p_k$ is matched protein abundance.  The admissible readback is therefore not a direct residual-to-protein assertion, but a controlled matrix of the form
$$
\begin{aligned}
q_i \longrightarrow t_j \longrightarrow p_k,
\end{aligned}
$$
where amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, coding-sequence length, the $M$-density baseline, and matched mRNA abundance remain controls rather than promoted coordinates.  A residual-to-protein association is counted only after the corresponding residual-to-translation step has a non-null finite statistic.

\paragraph{Phase-one table contact.}
The locked confirmatory contact against the NCBI genetic-code table $gc.prt$ represented all three declared target families: $h0.null.model.aa\_class\_preserving$, $h1.leave\_one\_code\_out.by\_family$, and $h1.leave\_one\_codon\_out.module\_classification$.  The independent replication statistic had $40$ successes in $40$ trials, with rate $1.0$ and exact one-sided binomial upper-tail value
$$
\begin{aligned}
 p &= 9.094947017729282\times 10^{-13}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The boundary-probe statistic had the same finite count, again $40$ successes in $40$ trials with rate $1.0$ and the same upper-tail value $9.094947017729282\times 10^{-13}$.  Negative controls had $20$ rows and $0$ unexpected successes, and the critical ablation table also had $20$ rows and $0$ unexpected successes.  The observation snapshot is dated $2026$-$06$-$16$ $18{:}30{:}05+00{:}00$, with preregistration before observation at $2026$-$05$-$29$ $20{:}11{:}31+00{:}00$.  These numbers support the finite codon-table contact only; they do not by themselves establish translation, protein structure, physical admissibility, biological function, mechanism, or a universal biological rule.

\paragraph{Modeled translation readout.}
A bounded modeled-tAI surface was computed from GtRNAdb tRNA gene-copy counts and dos Reis wobble weights for three organisms: $Escherichia\ coli$ K-12, $Homo\ sapiens$, and $Saccharomyces\ cerevisiae$.  The contact covered $61$ sense codons.  The local tRNA payload contained all $20$ standard amino-acid families for each organism, with $89$ FASTA headers in $E.\ coli$ K-12, $432$ in human, and $275$ in yeast; the matched header counts were $87$, $432$, and $275$, respectively.  Zero raw-weight fallback counts were $0$ for all three modeled-tAI codon tables.  The bounded coupling surface had $81$ entries above threshold $0.01$, and the largest absolute entry was
$$
\begin{aligned}
\max_{i,j}|S_{ij}| &= 3801961.031607896 .
\end{aligned}
$$
This finite matrix is useful as a bounded translation-layer readout, but the organism count was $3$, below the stated powered-survival requirement of at least $40$ matched species.  Consequently it is not ribosome profiling, not proteomics validation, and not a measured translation-realization claim.

\paragraph{Protein-abundance mediation rows.}
The protein-abundance mediation run joined real coding sequences, modeled per-protein tAI, and PAXdb protein abundance for $12$ organisms.  The joined row counts were $4042$ for $Bacillus\ subtilis$ strain $168$, $11661$ for $Caenorhabditis\ elegans$, $14410$ for $Danio\ rerio$, $13295$ for $Drosophila\ melanogaster$, $3739$ for $Escherichia\ coli$ K-12 MG1655, $12418$ for $Gallus\ gallus$, $19053$ for $Homo\ sapiens$, $19395$ for $Mus\ musculus$, $4129$ for $Mycobacterium\ smegmatis$ MC2 155, $13322$ for $Rattus\ norvegicus$, $5815$ for $Saccharomyces\ cerevisiae$, and $1656$ for $Sulfolobus\ solfataricus$.  Each organism had rank-sufficient residualized design matrices with $9$ $q$ coordinates, $24$ control columns, and a residualized $T$ rank of $1$.  The modeled tAI readout used per-protein means
$$
\begin{aligned}
T &= \frac{\sum_c n_c w_c}{\sum_c n_c},
\end{aligned}
$$
where $n_c$ is the codon count and $w_c$ is the tAI weight.

The mediated fractions $M^{QTP}$ were $0.6084594553474505$ in $B.\ subtilis$, $0.4872268357227724$ in $C.\ elegans$, $0.3157053953614641$ in $D.\ rerio$, $0.6165167748739756$ in $D.\ melanogaster$, $0.612922252793667$ in $E.\ coli$, $0.1976903964724546$ in chicken, $0.27206289160119157$ in human, $0.15488799907323988$ in mouse, $0.08397602919861291$ in $M.\ smegmatis$, $0.17489681798543982$ in rat, $0.9491551041216973$ in yeast, and $0.01850668564589326$ in $S.\ solfataricus$.  The corresponding total residual-to-protein $R^2$ values $c$ and direct-after-translation values $c'$ satisfy
$$
\begin{aligned}

\paragraph{Axis identity.}
The structural-order axis audit computed the same nine coordinates and found exactly one coordinate passing the fold-level sign-flip gate.  The dominant loading and dominant increment coordinate was $f3\_stress$, with weighted absolute unit loading $0.5109586606724208$, weighted held-out increment $R^2$ of $0.0031744502294756994$, and sign-flip value $0.03125$.  The other reported weighted held-out increments were $0.0020139270616115154$ for $Arg\_AGR$, $0.0013067592718559024$ for $Ile\_AUA$, $0.0005307046179682497$ for $K\_AAA$, $-0.0003205780368125179$ for $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$, $0.00017615626369389472$ for $Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG$, $4.390882136492317\times 10^{-5}$ for $Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG$, $0.0010134090056701938$ for $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$, and $-0.00045340330387383447$ for $Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY$.  This identifies a finite residual axis in the available structural-order statistic; it does not promote the residual axis to protein folding, molecular function, phenotype, mechanism, or universality.

\paragraph{Local boundary.}
The chapter therefore permits the following narrow conclusion: within the finite matched rows described above, the nine-coordinate $B^*_{Q6}$ residual system has a controlled statistical decomposition through modeled translation readout into matched protein abundance, and the strongest reported structural-order axis is $f3\_stress$.  The conclusion remains bounded by its contacts.  A modeled tAI value is not observed ribosome occupancy.  A protein-abundance row is not a folding, localization, function, phenotype, or physical assay.  A positive mediated fraction is not a causal mechanism.  A direct $q_i\to p_k$ association is not admissible as protein realization when the $q_i\to t_j$ step is absent, null, rank-degenerate, or untested.  Any translation-efficiency, folding, physical-admissibility, function, mechanism, or universal-law assertion requires a separate layer-matched measurement and its own controlled statistic.
