\paragraph{Local cannot-claim boundary.}
The chapter therefore has a ready finite verdict only for the bounded surfaces explicitly measured here: phase-one genetic-code table agreement, modeled-tAI coupling, controlled modeled-tAI protein-abundance decomposition, and the listed auxiliary audits.  No translation realization follows from the phase-one code-table result alone.  No ribosome occupancy follows from modeled tAI alone.  No folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, mechanism, organismal phenotype, selection law, or universal biological law follows from the residual coordinates, from a direct residual-to-protein association, or from a positive mediated fraction.  Those claims require separate layer-specific contacts and a same-row controlled statistic before they can be stated as biological support.
