\paragraph{Local cannot-claim rule.}
The chapter therefore has a two-level verdict.  First, the phase-one genetic-code table contact is ready as a locked finite confirmation of the registered codon-geometry tasks: $40/40$ and $40/40$ positives with exact values $9.094947017729282\times 10^{-13}$, plus $0/20$ failures in each of the negative and ablation controls.  Second, the protein-abundance extension is ready only as a bounded statistical readback through the declared translation and abundance contacts.  The admissible assertion is that the nine-coordinate residual matrix can be evaluated on finite rows of codon counts, modeled or measured translation readouts, matched mRNA controls, and matched protein abundance, and that several computed surfaces show nonzero controlled mediation fractions.  No statement of translation realization, folded structure, physical admissibility, biological function, organismal phenotype, causal mechanism, evolutionary selection law, or universality follows from these rows unless a separate layer-matched contact supplies the corresponding measurement and the same controlled finite-row test supports it.
