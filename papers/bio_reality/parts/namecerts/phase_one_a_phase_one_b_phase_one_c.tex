\subsection{Phase-One Table Contact and Translation-Bounded Residual Readback}
\label{sec:bioreality-phase-one-q6-translation-bounded-contact}

\paragraph{Finite surface.}
The record considered here is a finite table-level contact, not an open-ended biological law.  Its primary row set is the locked confirmatory readback of three codon-geometry targets against the NCBI genetic-code table snapshot: the amino-acid-class preserving null model, the leave-one-code-out family test, and the leave-one-codon-out module-classification test.  The finite surface is therefore indexed by the registered target identifiers
$h0.null.model.aa\_class\_preserving$, $h1.leave\_one\_code\_out.by\_family$, and $h1.leave\_one\_codon\_out.module\_classification$, with the readout restricted to curated genetic-code table agreement and its negative controls.  The reported source is the NCBI $gc.prt$ table readback against the Phase 1 codon-geometry claims, with a data snapshot at $2026$-$06$-$16$ $18{:}30{:}05+00{:}00$ and a registered-before-observation time of $2026$-$05$-$29$ $20{:}11{:}31+00{:}00$.

\paragraph{Primary table statistics.}
The finite confirmatory row has two independent exact-binomial supports.  The boundary-probe statistic has $40$ successes in $40$ trials, hence rate $1.0$ and one-sided upper-tail probability
$$
\begin{aligned}
  p_{\mathrm{boundary}} &= 9.094947017729282\times 10^{-13},\\
  n_{\mathrm{boundary}} &= 40,\\
  s_{\mathrm{boundary}} &= 40.
\end{aligned}
$$
The independent replication statistic has the same finite count and probability,
$$
\begin{aligned}
  p_{\mathrm{replication}} &= 9.094947017729282\times 10^{-13},\\
  n_{\mathrm{replication}} &= 40,\\
  s_{\mathrm{replication}} &= 40.
\end{aligned}
$$
The two absence checks are also finite: the negative-control panel has $20$ rows and $0$ unexpected successes, and the critical-ablation panel has $20$ rows and $0$ unexpected successes.  Thus the table contact supports a codon-geometry readback at the genetic-code-table layer while simultaneously recording that the same evidence does not move upward to translation, protein structure, physical admissibility, biological function, mechanism, or universality.

\paragraph{Residual coordinates placed behind a translation gate.}
The associated $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual surface contains exactly nine named coordinates:
$$
\begin{aligned}
  Q=\{&K\_AAA,\ Arg\_AGR,\ Ile\_AUA,\ Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR,\\
      &Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG,\ Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY,\ Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG,\\
      &Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY,\ f3\_stress\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
These coordinates are not read as direct protein-abundance variables.  The admissible cross-layer matrix has the form $q_i\to t_j\to p_k$, where $t_j$ is a translation readout such as modeled tAI, stAI, ribosome occupancy, or translation efficiency, and $p_k$ is matched protein abundance.  A direct $q_i\to p_k$ association is therefore not promoted unless a same-coordinate $q_i\to t_j$ step is present under the declared controls.  The controls remain amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, coding-sequence length, the $M$-density baseline, and matched mRNA abundance; they are not promoted into residual coordinates.

\paragraph{Modeled tAI bounded contact.}
A modeled tAI survival surface was computed from GtRNAdb tRNA gene-copy counts and dos Reis wobble weights for three organisms.  The organism count is $3$, the codon-count space is $61$, and the coupling threshold is $0.01$.  The finite matrix contains $81$ entries above threshold, with
$$
\begin{aligned}
  \max |S_{ij}| &= 3801961.031607896,\\
  \max |S_i| &= 3801961.031607896,\\
  \tau_{\mathrm{coupling}} &= 0.01.
\end{aligned}
$$
The organism-specific tRNA payloads cover all $20$ standard amino-acid families.  Escherichia coli K12 has $89$ FASTA headers, $87$ matched headers, and $2$ unmatched headers; Homo sapiens has $432$ headers and $432$ matched headers; Saccharomyces cerevisiae has $275$ headers and $275$ matched headers.  The modeled tAI weights were finite for all $61$ sense codons in each of these three organisms, with zero raw-weight fallback count $0$ in all three cases.  This is a bounded modeled-readout contact.  Because only $3$ organisms are present, it is explicitly not a powered survival claim; the finite row itself records that powered survival requires at least $40$ matched species.

Each organism panel used a $9$-coordinate residual block and $24$ control columns, with residual rank diagnostics satisfying the reported powered-rank condition.

\paragraph{Negative higher-layer checks.}
Several linked computations constrain over-reading of the table and mediation contacts.  The cross-ortholog selection-imprint panel used $80$ orthogroups, $240$ rows, and $3$ species; its positive selection-imprint gate is false, with $\Delta R^2=0.03250515015613468$, leave-clade-out $\Delta R^2=-0.0021477729853302424$, maximum null-$95$ $\Delta R^2=0.05427157066522958$, and description-length change $-258.6010084301837$ bits.  The mass-balance closure computation used $120$ genes and $718$ intervals; its terminal comparison has $\Delta R^2=-0.07584438093220836$, $\Delta\mathrm{DL}=-668.8046070381928$ bits, time-shuffle probability $0.9306930693069307$, and TE-bin probability $0.6831683168316832$.  The turnover residual audit used $3290$ genes and $200$ null draws, with $\Delta R^2=-0.005461979433706483$, $\Delta\mathrm{DL}=-13.68819707902732$ bits, matched-bin probability $0.8855721393034826$, and recoded probability $1.0$.  These negative or bounded rows keep the claim within its measured layer.

\paragraph{Axis identity without promotion.}
The structural-order axis computation evaluated the same nine $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ coordinates over $5$ folds with minimum joined count $300$.  Exactly one coordinate passed the fold-level sign-flip gate: $f3\_stress$.  Its weighted held-out $R^2$ increment is $0.0031744502294756994$, its weighted absolute unit loading is $0.5109586606724208$, and its sign-flip probability is $0.03125$.  The other coordinates did not pass this finite gate: for example, $Arg\_AGR$ has sign-flip probability $0.142578125$, $Ile\_AUA$ has $0.0693359375$, and $Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY$ has $0.529296875$.  This identifies a finite statistical axis in the available rows; it does not make a folding, physical-admissibility, functional, phenotypic, mechanistic, or universal claim.

\paragraph{Local claim boundary.}
The chapter therefore has a two-level verdict.  First, the Phase 1 genetic-code-table contact is ready as a finite codon-geometry confirmation: two $40/40$ exact-binomial contacts have $p=9.094947017729282\times 10^{-13}$, and both $20$-row null panels have zero unexpected successes.  Second, the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual material may be read only through controlled translation and protein-abundance surfaces.  Modeled tAI and protein-abundance mediation rows provide bounded statistical contacts, including $12$ organism-level $M^{QTP}$ fractions and a $4759$-row yeast decomposition, but no translation realization, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, causal mechanism, organismal phenotype, selection law, or universal biological rule follows from these rows without a separate layer-matched measurement and the corresponding controlled statistic.
