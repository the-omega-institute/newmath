\subsection{Phase-One Genetic-Code Contact and the Translation-Mediated Protein-Abundance Surface}\label{sec:phase-one-q6-translation-mediated-protein-abundance}\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite record.}
This chapter records a finite BioReality contact for the compound claim $\mathrm{phase.one.a}+\mathrm{phase.one.b}+\mathrm{phase.one.c}$ and its downstream use as a bounded guide for the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ translation-mediated protein-abundance question.  The finite surface is made of triples $(q_i,t_j,p_k)$, where $q_i$ is one of the nine $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates, $t_j$ is a translation readout, and $p_k$ is a matched protein-abundance readout.  The admissible residual coordinates are
$$
\begin{aligned}
Q=\{&K\_AAA,\ Arg\_AGR,\ Ile\_AUA,\ Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR,\ Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG,\\
&Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY,\ Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG,\ Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY,\ f3\_stress\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The readout $T$ used in the powered mediation table is a per-protein modeled tAI value, computed as a codon-count-weighted mean of GtRNAdb tRNA-copy and dos Reis wobble weights.  The protein readout $P$ is $\log_{10}(\mathrm{abundance\_ppm})$ from measured PAXdb abundance joined to real CDS codon counts.  The controls are kept in $Z$: amino-acid composition, organism identity, GC3, CDS length, the $M$-density baseline on the support of the nine projected vectors, and matched mRNA abundance where the readout requires it.

\paragraph{Locked genetic-code contact.}
The phase-one contact is a confirmatory genetic-code-table readback against NCBI $gc.prt$, not a translation or proteomics assay.  It represented the three target claim families $h0.null.model.aa\_class\_preserving$, $h1.leave\_one\_code\_out.by\_family$, and $h1.leave\_one\_codon\_out.module\_classification$.  The independent replication table has $40$ successes in $40$ trials, with exact one-sided binomial upper-tail probability $9.094947017729282\times 10^{-13}$; the boundary probe has the same $40/40$ rate and the same probability.  The negative-control table has $20$ rows and $0$ unexpected successes, and the critical-ablation table also has $20$ rows and $0$ unexpected successes.  The preregistration time is $2026$-$05$-$29$T$20{:}11{:}31+00{:}00$, and the confirmatory snapshot time is $2026$-$06$-$16$T$18{:}30{:}05+00{:}00$.  These numbers make the finite genetic-code contact sharp, but its scope remains the curated codon table and the locked codon-geometry claims.  It does not by itself observe ribosomes, proteins, folded conformations, assays of physical viability, molecular function, mechanism, phenotype, selection, or a universal biological law.

\paragraph{Modeled translation surface.}
Before protein mediation is considered, the translation readout must be separated from direct residual-to-protein association.  In the modeled-tAI survival run over $3$ organisms, all standard amino-acid tRNA families were present: Escherichia coli K12 had $89$ FASTA headers, $87$ matched headers, and $2$ unmatched headers; Homo sapiens had $432$ headers and $432$ matched headers; Saccharomyces cerevisiae had $275$ headers and $275$ matched headers.  The modeled tAI codon table covered $61$ sense codons in each organism, with zero raw-$W$ fallback count $0$ in the three-organism contact.  The bounded coupling table had $81$ entries above threshold, threshold $0.01$, and maximum absolute $S_{ij}=3801961.031607896$.  This is a bounded modeled-weight contact: the organism count is $3$, so it is not a powered ribosome-profiling or proteomics validation panel.

\paragraph{Yeast structure-side check.}
The yeast five-way join adds measured translation efficiency, mRNA half-life, $5'$ coding-region MFE, protein abundance, and codon counts on $4759$ joined rows.  The reported CDS join hit rate is $0.9156038419146592$, and the overall join hit rate is $0.8818572656921754$.  In the residualized abundance partition, $R^2_{QP}=0.2715520808117803$, $R^2_{QP\mid S}=0.2706601505000995$, $R^2_{QP\mid T,M}=0.13773967696311287$, and $R^2_{QP\mid T,M,S}=0.13759011208318542$.  The structure-only fraction is $0.003284564452662019$, while the structure-incremental fraction after measured TE and mRNA stability is $0.0005507778820193332$.  The $Q\to S$ structure readback has $R^2=0.003774248455422586$ and permutation right-tail probability $0.027972027972027972$; the incremental structure probability is $0.1258741258741259$.  This is a variance partition over a CDS-only MFE proxy, not a folding experiment and not a physical-admissibility assay.

\paragraph{Axis identity.}
The structural-order axis audit over the same nine-coordinate list identifies exactly one fold-level significant coordinate: $f3\_stress$.  Its weighted absolute unit loading is $0.5109586606724208$, its weighted held-out $R^2$ increment is $0.0031744502294756994$, and its sign-flip probability for being greater than zero is $0.03125$.  The other coordinates do not pass that gate: for example, $Arg\_AGR$ has increment $0.0020139270616115154$ with probability $0.142578125$, $Ile\_AUA$ has increment $0.0013067592718559024$ with probability $0.0693359375$, and $Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY$ is negative at $-0.00045340330387383447$ with probability $0.529296875$.  The chapter therefore records $f3\_stress$ as the dominant finite axis in this audit, but this axis identity still remains a statistical readback unless an independent layer-matched contact supplies a stronger biological endpoint.

\paragraph{Local boundary.}
The valid conclusion is narrow.  The phase-one table gives strong locked contact with the curated genetic code, and the $Q\to T\to P$ table gives a finite mediated protein-abundance decomposition when modeled tAI is used as the translation covariate.  The chapter does not claim translation realization from the genetic-code contact, does not claim ribosome occupancy from tAI weights, does not claim folding or physical admissibility from abundance or MFE, does not claim biological function or phenotype from the residual coordinates, and does not claim a universal mechanism from any one organism or sign.  A stronger claim would require a separate organism- and CDS-matched contact at the relevant layer, with codon counts, translation readout, mRNA control, protein or structure rows as appropriate, rank sufficiency, and the stated residual controls computed on the same finite rows.
