\subsection{Phase-one codon geometry and the translation-mediated protein-abundance boundary}
\label{subsec:bioreality-phase-one-q6-translation-mediated-protein-abundance}

\paragraph{Finite surface.}
The record treated here is a finite surface of rows $(q_i,t_j,p_k)$.  The $q_i$ coordinates are the nine $B^*_{Q6}$ synonymous residual directions
$
K_{AAA},\ \mathrm{Arg}_{AGR},\ \mathrm{Ile}_{AUA},\ \mathrm{Leu}_{CUN\;vs\;UUR},\ \mathrm{Leu}_{UUA\;vs\;UUG},\ \mathrm{Ser}_{UCR\;vs\;AGY},\ \mathrm{Ser}_{UCA\;vs\;UCG},\ \mathrm{Thr}_{ACR\;vs\;ACY},\ f_3\text{-stress}
$.
The $t_j$ layer is restricted to translation readouts such as modeled tAI, stAI, ribosome occupancy, or translation efficiency.  The $p_k$ layer is matched protein abundance.  A row is admissible only in the mediated order $q_i \to t_j \to p_k$: a direct $q_i \to p_k$ association is not interpreted as protein realization unless the same coordinate first survives a controlled translation-readout step.  The control surface keeps amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, CDS length, and the M-density baseline as covariates rather than as promoted coordinates, and matched mRNA abundance separates a translation-mediated edge from a transcription-only edge.

\paragraph{Locked phase-one contact.}
The finite genetic-code contact for the phase-one row was evaluated against the NCBI genetic-code table readback.  The confirmatory dataset was loaded from \texttt{tools/bio\_reality/data/phase2\_confirmatory\_falsification\_dataset.json}, with the protocol registered before observation at \texttt{2026-05-29T20:11:31+00:00} and a snapshot dated \texttt{2026-06-16T18:30:05+00:00}.  All three target claim families were represented: the amino-acid-class preserving null model, leave-one-code-out by family, and leave-one-codon-out module classification.  The independent replication statistic had $40$ successes in $40$ trials, with rate $1.0$ and exact one-sided binomial upper-tail probability $9.094947017729282\times 10^{-13}$.  The boundary probe had the same finite strength: $40$ successes in $40$ trials, rate $1.0$, and exact one-sided binomial upper-tail probability $9.094947017729282\times 10^{-13}$.  The negative controls had $20$ rows and $0$ unexpected successes, and the critical ablation controls also had $20$ rows and $0$ unexpected successes.  Thus the phase-one row is a locked confirmatory contact for codon-geometry claims, not a claim about translation, protein structure, physical admissibility, molecular function, or biological universality.

\paragraph{Translation readout.}
The modeled-tAI survival audit computed all nine residual coordinates above on $61$ sense codons.  It used three organisms, so its own record marks the matrix as underpowered for a population-level survival claim.  Nevertheless, the bounded modeled readout was nonzero: $81$ entries exceeded the threshold $0.01$, with maximum absolute entry $3801961.031607896$ and the same maximum absolute diagonal entry.  The tRNA payload had all $20$ standard amino-acid families for \textit{Escherichia coli} K12, \textit{Homo sapiens}, and \textit{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}; the respective FASTA header counts were $89$, $432$, and $275$, with matched header counts $87$, $432$, and $275$.  For each of these three organisms the modeled tAI calculation covered $61$ sense codons and had zero raw-$W$ fallback count $0$.  This establishes a bounded codon-adaptation contact.  It does not establish measured ribosome occupancy, measured translation efficiency, protein output, folding, physical admissibility, function, mechanism, or a universal law.

\paragraph{Protein-abundance mediation.}
The powered $q_i \to t_j \to p_k$ audit joined real CDS codon counts, GtRNAdb tRNA gene-copy readouts, and PAXdb protein abundance for $12$ organisms.  The joined protein counts were $4042$ for \textit{Bacillus subtilis} strain 168, $11661$ for \textit{Caenorhabditis elegans}, $14410$ for \textit{Danio rerio}, $13295$ for \textit{Drosophila melanogaster}, $3739$ for \textit{Escherichia coli} K12 MG1655, $12418$ for \textit{Gallus gallus}, $19053$ for \textit{Homo sapiens}, $19395$ for \textit{Mus musculus}, $4129$ for \textit{Mycobacterium smegmatis} MC2 155, $13322$ for \textit{Rattus norvegicus}, $5815$ for \textit{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}, and $1656$ for \textit{Sulfolobus solfataricus}.  The fitted mediation scalar was in $[0,1]$ for every organism.  The mediated fractions were $0.6084594553474505$, $0.4872268357227724$, $0.3157053953614641$, $0.6165167748739756$, $0.612922252793667$, $0.1976903964724546$, $0.27206289160119157$, $0.15488799907323988$, $0.08397602919861291$, $0.17489681798543982$, $0.9491551041216973$, and $0.01850668564589326$, in the organism order just listed.  The yeast row was the largest, with total $R^2_{QP}=0.29837624994140827$, direct $R^2_{QP\mid T}=0.0151709093608293$, and indirect difference $0.28320534058057895$.

\paragraph{Control algebra.}
The mediation fit residualized against $24$ control columns, with rank $23$ for $Z$ and rank $9$ for the $B^*_{Q6}$ coordinate matrix in every organism-level block.  The translation-readout matrix had rank $1$, while the joint $X,T$ design had rank $10$.  The residual degrees of freedom after $Z$ ranged from $1633$ in \textit{Sulfolobus solfataricus} to $19372$ in \textit{Mus musculus}.  The measured protein readout was $\log_{10}(\mathrm{abundance\ ppm})$, and the translation readout was per-protein mean modeled tAI,
$$
\begin{aligned}
T &= \frac{\sum_c n_c w_c}{\sum_c n_c},
\end{aligned}
$$
where $n_c$ is the CDS codon count and $w_c$ is the codon tAI weight from all-tRNA copy counts and dos Reis wobble weights.  These finite ranks make the decomposition estimable; they do not make it causal.

\paragraph{Yeast structural and abundance decomposition.}
The yeast five-way join for abundance, measured TE, mRNA half-life, CDS sequence, and a $5'$ coding-region folding proxy had $4759$ joined rows.  Its reported CDS join hit rate was $0.9156038419146592$, and the overall join hit rate was $0.8818572656921754$.  The single and joint $R^2$ values were $R^2_{QP}=0.2715520808117803$, $R^2_{QP\mid S}=0.2706601505000995$, $R^2_{QP\mid T,M}=0.13773967696311287$, and $R^2_{QP\mid T,M,S}=0.13759011208318542$.  The structure-only fraction was $0.003284564452662019$, the structure-incremental fraction was $0.0005507778820193332$, and the residual structure readout from $Q$ had $S_Q$ structure $R^2=0.003774248455422586$ with right-tail permutation probability $0.027972027972027972$.  The structure-incremental right-tail probability was $0.1258741258741259$.  This is a statistical variance partition for a CDS-only MFE proxy, not a folded-protein or causal pathway result.

\paragraph{Axis identity and rate-slot evidence.}
A separate structural-order axis audit found exactly one significant coordinate under the fold-level sign-flip gate: $f_3$-stress.  Its weighted held-out increment was $0.0031744502294756994$, its weighted absolute unit loading was $0.5109586606724208$, and its sign-flip probability for greater-than-zero effect was $0.03125$.  The other eight coordinates did not pass that gate.  The observable execution decomposition for \textit{Saccharomyces cerevisiae} used $3289$ genes and concluded that at least one observed rate-proxy slot passed both effective null classes.  This supports an observable execution subblock, while leaving protein folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, phenotype, mechanism, and universality outside the claim.

\paragraph{Negative and bounded downstream rows.}
Several linked rows keep the mediation claim bounded.  The dynamic mass-balance audit used $120$ genes and $718$ intervals; adding the final block reduced $R^2$ from $0.9712443377743347$ to $0.8953999568421264$, with $\Delta R^2=-0.07584438093220836$, $\Delta\mathrm{DL}=-668.8046070381928$ bits, $p_{TE\,bin}=0.6831683168316832$, and $p_{time\,shuffle}=0.9306930693069307$.  The turnover audit used $3290$ genes and reported $R^2_{L2}=0.055740851626933985$, $R^2_{L3}=0.050278872193227464$, $\Delta R^2=-0.005461979433706483$, $\Delta\mathrm{DL}=-13.68819707902732$ bits, $p_{matched\,bin}=0.8855721393034826$, and $p_{recoded}=1.0$.  The cross-ortholog selection-imprint audit used $80$ orthogroups, $240$ rows, and $3$ species; its positive-selection gate was false, with $\Delta R^2=0.03250515015613468$, leave-clade-out $\Delta R^2=-0.0021477729853302424$, and null $95\%$ maximum $\Delta R^2=0.05427157066522958$.

\paragraph{Perturbation rows.}
The perturbation evidence is mixed and layer-specific.  The \textit{E. coli} mRFP row had $1459$ variants and a positive protein-output increment, with base $R^2=0.092995095023503$, full $R^2=0.16725111921465707$, $\Delta R^2=0.07425602419115408$, $\Delta\mathrm{DL}=42.597002054209426$ bits, and both matched and within-null probabilities $0.004975124378109453$.  The yeast synonymous-mutation row had $20$ genes, $1783$ synonymous mutants, $21$ CDS-map hits, and real CSC robustness with real CSC not absorbing the effect.  The GFP synonymous protein-escape row had $3556$ variants, split as $1124$ and $2432$ across its two regions, with protein-on-out-of-frame mRNA slope $0.016523671594873352$.  The human prime-editor row had $9000$ synonymous rows, $24000$ compact synonymous rows, and $1822$ genes under gene-block folds.  These rows contact protein output or fitness-like surfaces only under their own finite designs; they do not retroactively convert codon residual geometry into a general mechanism.

\paragraph{Verdict.}
The chapter-level verdict is therefore positive only for a finite, controlled mediation readback: phase-one codon geometry is externally confirmed at the genetic-code-table level, the $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinates have bounded modeled translation-readout contacts, and the powered multi-organism audit gives a controlled statistical decomposition from residual coordinates through modeled tAI to matched protein abundance.  The claim remains explicitly non-causal.  No translation realization follows from phase-one genetic-code confirmation alone; no measured ribosome dynamics follows from modeled tAI or stAI alone; no protein folding, physical admissibility, biological function, mechanism, phenotype, selection law, or universality follows unless a separate layer-matched contact supplies that measurement and the corresponding controlled statistic is computed.
