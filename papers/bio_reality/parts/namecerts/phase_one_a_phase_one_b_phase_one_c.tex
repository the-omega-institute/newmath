\paragraph{What the phase-one row does not promote.}
The row deliberately stops at the genetic-code table.  It does not establish translation dynamics, ribosome occupancy, protein structure, physical admissibility, biological function, mechanism, phenotype, selection, or a universal biological law.  This local boundary is part of the scientific content of the row rather than a footnote: a codon table can witness a finite symbolic contact, but it cannot by itself observe an mRNA molecule, a ribosome, a protein abundance vector, a folding process, or a function assay.  Any later claim above the code-table layer must therefore attach a separate measurement contact and a statistic defined on matched rows.
