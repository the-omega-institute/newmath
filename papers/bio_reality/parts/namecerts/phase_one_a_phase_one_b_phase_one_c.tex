\paragraph{Protein-abundance mediation rows.}

\paragraph{Local no-promotion rule.}
The finite evidence supports three bounded statements.  First, the Phase One genetic-code-table contact strongly confirms the locked codon-geometry readback under its own finite protocol.  Second, the nine-coordinate $B^*\_{Q6}$ residual basis can be carried into modeled translation-readout and protein-abundance matrices with explicit controls and rank diagnostics.  Third, some joined rows show positive statistical mediation fractions through modeled tAI, especially in yeast.  None of these statements by itself establishes observed translation, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, mechanism, phenotype, selection, or universality.  Observed translation requires a separate ribosome or TE contact with matched mRNA control; protein realization requires matched protein rows after a surviving translation step; structure, function, physical admissibility, localization, stability, and phenotype each require their own layer-matched measurements.  Direct $Q_i\to P_k$ association is therefore not treated as protein realization unless the corresponding $Q_i\to T_j$ step is present and controlled.

\paragraph{Finite verdict.}
The chapter-level readback is ready at bounded cross-layer status.  The strong Phase One result is a confirmed finite genetic-code contact, while the $Q\to T\to P$ material is a controlled statistical surface whose strongest entries are modeled-tAI protein-abundance mediation entries, not causal biology.  The appropriate conclusion is therefore not a universal codon law, but a ranked finite program of residual coordinates, translation readouts, and protein-abundance outcomes, with the higher biological layers kept outside the claim unless a separate measured contact supplies them.
