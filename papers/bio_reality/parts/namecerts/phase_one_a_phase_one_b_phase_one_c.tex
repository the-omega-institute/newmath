\subsection{Phase-One Confirmatory Contact and Translation-Mediated Protein-Abundance Boundary}
\label{sec:bioreality-phase-one-q6-translation-mediated-protein-abundance}

\paragraph{Finite row carrier.}
The record is a finite surface of triples $(q_i,t_j,p_k)$.  The first coordinate $q_i$ ranges over the nine $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates
$K\_AAA$, $Arg\_AGR$, $Ile\_AUA$, $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$, $Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG$, $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$, $Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG$, $Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY$, and $f3\_stress$.  The middle coordinate $t_j$ is a translation readout, here represented by bounded modeled tAI weights and by per-protein mean modeled tAI where each protein receives
$$
\begin{aligned}
T &= \frac{\sum_c n_c w_c}{\sum_c n_c},
\end{aligned}
$$
with $n_c$ the codon count and $w_c$ the organism-specific tAI weight obtained from tRNA gene-copy counts and dos Reis wobble weights.  The third coordinate $p_k$ is matched protein abundance, specifically $\log_{10}(\mathrm{abundance\_ppm})$ after joining measured PAXdb abundance to real CDS codon-count rows.  Amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, CDS length, and the M-density baseline are covariates, not promoted coordinates; matched mRNA abundance is a control separating transcription-layer variation from the translation-mediated chain.

It uses the NCBI genetic-code table readback against locked codon-geometry claims, with the protocol declared before observation at 2026-05-29T20:11:31+00:00 and the finite snapshot dated 2026-06-16T18:30:05+00:00. $$
\begin{aligned}
P(X \geq 40 \mid n=40,p=1/2) &= 9.094947017729282\times 10^{-13}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The negative-control table has $20$ rows and $0$ unexpected successes, and the critical-ablation table has $20$ rows and $0$ unexpected successes.  This is strong contact with the curated genetic-code table, but it remains bounded to that contact: it does not by itself establish translation, protein structure, physical admissibility, biological function, mechanism, universality, or any organism-level law.

The corresponding FASTA header counts were $89$, $432$, and $275$, with matched header counts $87$, $432$, and $275$ respectively.  The modeled tAI readout covered all $61$ sense codons in each organism, with zero raw-weight fallback counts $0$, $0$, and $0$.  The finite modeled coupling matrix had $81$ entries above the $0.01$ bounded-contact threshold, with maximum absolute entry
$$
\begin{aligned}
\max |S_{ij}| &= 3801961.031607896.
\end{aligned}
$$
This is not powered measured translation survival: the organism count is $3$, and the row explicitly records that a powered survival claim would require at least $40$ matched species or an organism-matched measured translation panel.  The result is therefore a bounded modeled-tAI coupling contact, not a ribosome-profiling, proteomics, or causal translation measurement.

\paragraph{Local no-promotion rule.}
The admissible conclusion is narrow.  At finite row level, there is a computed $Q_i\to T_j\to P_k$ matrix in which the $Q$ block consists of nine $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates, $T$ is a modeled translation-readout scalar, and $P$ is matched measured protein abundance.  Direct $Q_i\to P_k$ associations are not interpreted as protein realization unless the translation readout is explicitly present and conditioned in the same row design.  Even then, the result is a statistical decomposition under controls, not a causal mechanism.  No claim about measured ribosome occupancy, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, phenotype, selection, perturbational rescue, or universality follows from these rows unless a separate layer-matched contact supplies that measurement and a controlled statistic is computed for it.
