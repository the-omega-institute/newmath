\subsection{Finite genetic-code confirmation and translation-gated protein readout}\label{sec:bioreality-phase-one-q6-translation-mediated-protein-readout}

\paragraph{Finite row surface.}
This record is a finite-row BioReality chapter for the claim $\mathrm{phase.one.a+phase.one.b+phase.one.c}$, read together with the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ translation-mediated protein-abundance packet.  The carrier is the finite mediation surface of triples $(q_i,t_j,p_k)$, where $q_i$ ranges over the nine residual coordinates
$$
\begin{aligned}
q_i \in \{&\mathrm{K\_AAA},\ \mathrm{Arg\_AGR},\ \mathrm{Ile\_AUA},\ \mathrm{Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR},\ \mathrm{Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG},\\
&\mathrm{Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY},\ \mathrm{Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG},\ \mathrm{Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY},\ f_3\text{-}\mathrm{stress}\},
\end{aligned}
$$
$t_j$ ranges over bounded translation readouts such as modeled tAI, stAI, ribosome occupancy, or translation efficiency, and $p_k$ is a matched protein-abundance readout.  The local admissibility rule is asymmetric: a direct $q_i \to p_k$ association is not promoted unless a same-coordinate $q_i \to t_j$ translation readout has first survived the declared controls.  Amino-acid sequence, organism, GC3, CDS length, and the $M$-density baseline remain covariates, not new coordinates.  Matched mRNA abundance is used to separate translation-layer signal from transcription-only signal.

\paragraph{Locked finite confirmation at the genetic-code boundary.}
The genetic-code contact is finite and exact.  The registered table readback loaded the confirmatory dataset `tools/bio\_reality/data/phase2\_confirmatory\_falsification\_dataset.json' and represented the three target claim families $\mathrm{h0.null.model.aa\_class\_preserving}$, $\mathrm{h1.leave\_one\_code\_out.by\_family}$, and $\mathrm{h1.leave\_one\_codon\_out.module\_classification}$.  The independent replication statistic had $n=40$ trials, $40$ successes, rate $1.0$, and one-sided binomial upper-tail probability $9.094947017729282\times 10^{-13}$.  The boundary-probe statistic used the same finite count, again with $n=40$, $40$ successes, rate $1.0$, and one-sided binomial upper-tail probability $9.094947017729282\times 10^{-13}$.  The negative-control surface had $n=20$ with $0$ unexpected successes, and the critical-ablation surface also had $n=20$ with $0$ unexpected successes.  Thus the finite table contact supports the codon-geometry claims at the curated genetic-code layer only.

\paragraph{Modeled translation-readout contact.}
The first translation contact is modeled tAI from GtRNAdb tRNA gene-copy counts with dos Reis wobble weights.  It is a bounded readout, not an observed ribosome-footprint measurement.  The finite tRNA payload contains all standard amino-acid families for the three organisms in the modeled survival run: Escherichia coli K12 had $89$ FASTA headers, $87$ matched headers, $2$ unmatched headers, and $20$ standard amino-acid families; Homo sapiens had $432$ headers, all $432$ matched, and $20$ families; Saccharomyces cerevisiae had $275$ headers, all $275$ matched, and $20$ families.  For all three organisms the modeled tAI surface covered $61$ sense codons with zero raw-weight fallback count $0$.  The bounded-coupling matrix had $81$ entries above threshold $0.01$, with maximum absolute entry $3801961.031607896$ and the same maximum on the diagonal.  This is a strong modeled-readout contact, but the organism count is only $3$, so it is explicitly not a powered multi-organism translation-survival result.

\paragraph{Perturbation contacts.}
Finite perturbation surfaces give narrower evidence.  The Nieuwkoop Escherichia coli mRFP protein-output audit used $1459$ variants and found base $R^2=0.12648386762689323$, full $R^2=0.1798829276988051$, $\Delta R^2=0.05339906007191191$, $\Delta\mathrm{DL}=19.089327473282296$ bits, and matched and within probabilities both $0.004975124378109453$.  The yeast synonymous-mutant audit used $20$ genes, $1783$ synonymous mutants, and $21$ CDS-map hits, with real CSC robustness retained and real-versus-proxy correlation $0.39717918221186976$.  The five-prime boundary audit on $1459$ variants found a start-window $\Delta R^2=1.7624020537495221\times 10^{-9}$ and downstream $\Delta R^2=-0.001126409133973234$, so the downstream boundary was not sign-stable.  These perturbation rows support bounded protein-output or RNA-stability contacts only where the finite design supplies the relevant measurement.

\paragraph{Local cannot-claim boundary.}
The chapter therefore has a positive finite confirmation at the genetic-code table layer and a bounded, statistically quantified translation-mediated protein-abundance decomposition for matched finite rows.  It does not claim translation realization from codon geometry alone.  It does not claim ribosome dynamics from modeled tAI or stAI alone.  It does not claim protein folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, phenotype, mechanism, selection, or universality from a residual coordinate, from a protein-abundance association, or from a mediated fraction.  A higher-layer statement becomes admissible only when that layer has its own matched contact and its own controlled statistic.  In particular, a $q_i \to p_k$ association is held below protein-realization status unless the same coordinate first survives a $q_i \to t_j$ translation-readout step under amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, CDS length, $M$-density baseline, matched mRNA abundance, and simpler-baseline controls.
