\subsection{Phase-one table contact and translation-bounded protein mediation}\label{sec:bioreality-phase-one-a-b-c-q6-mediation}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite contact surface.}
The BioReality record considered here attaches the phase-one codon-geometry contact to a bounded cross-layer mediation question.  The finite surface consists of triples $(q_i,t_j,p_k)$, where $q_i$ ranges over nine $B^*_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates, $t_j$ is a translation readout such as modeled tAI, stAI, ribosome occupancy, or translation efficiency, and $p_k$ is matched protein abundance.  The residual coordinates used in the computed rows are
$$
\begin{aligned}
Q=\{&\mathrm{K\_AAA},\mathrm{Arg\_AGR},\mathrm{Ile\_AUA},\mathrm{Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR},\mathrm{Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG},\\
&\mathrm{Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY},\mathrm{Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG},\mathrm{Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY},\mathrm{f3\_stress}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The allowed readback is not a direct $q_i\to p_k$ assertion.  It is a controlled chain $q_i\to t_j\to p_k$, and the direct protein-abundance term is admissible only after the translation-readout term has been evaluated under amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, CDS length, an $M$-density baseline, and matched mRNA abundance controls.  Amino-acid sequence, organism identity, GC3, CDS length, and the $M$-density baseline remain controls in this chapter; they are not promoted into the residual coordinate set.

\paragraph{Phase-one finite-row result.}
The phase-one contact itself is a locked confirmatory falsification readback against the NCBI genetic-code table file \texttt{gc.prt}.  It represented all three target claim families: \texttt{h0.null.model.aa\_class\_preserving}, \texttt{h1.leave\_one\_code\_out.by\_family}, and \texttt{h1.leave\_one\_codon\_out.module\_classification}.  The independent-replication statistic and the boundary-probe statistic both had $40$ successes in $40$ trials, rate $1.0$, with exact one-sided binomial upper-tail probability $9.094947017729282\times 10^{-13}$.  The negative controls had $20$ rows and $0$ unexpected successes, and the critical ablation controls also had $20$ rows and $0$ unexpected successes.  Thus the finite table contact is positive for the registered codon-table geometry checks, but only at that layer.
$$
\begin{aligned}
&n_{\mathrm{rep}}=40,\quad s_{\mathrm{rep}}=40,\quad p_{\mathrm{rep}}=9.094947017729282\times 10^{-13},\\
&n_{\mathrm{boundary}}=40,\quad s_{\mathrm{boundary}}=40,\quad p_{\mathrm{boundary}}=9.094947017729282\times 10^{-13},\\
&n_{\mathrm{neg}}=20,\quad u_{\mathrm{neg}}=0,\quad n_{\mathrm{abl}}=20,\quad u_{\mathrm{abl}}=0.
\end{aligned}
$$
This establishes a finite external contact for the codon-table layer.  It does not by itself establish translation, protein structure, physical admissibility, biological function, a mechanism, or a universal biological law.

\paragraph{Modeled translation boundary.}
A separate modeled-tAI computation supplies a bounded translation-layer readout, not a ribosome-observation claim.  In the three-organism modeled-tAI surface, the organism count was $3$, the codon-count space was $61$, and the coupling threshold was $0.01$.  The finite matrix had $81$ entries above threshold, with maximum absolute entry $3801961.031607896$ and the same value on the maximum absolute diagonal entry.  All three modeled-tAI readouts used $61$ sense codons and had zero raw-$W$ fallback count $0$ for \textit{Escherichia coli} K12, \textit{Homo sapiens}, and \textit{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}.  The tRNA family payload covered all $20$ standard amino-acid families in each of those organisms, with matched FASTA header counts $87$ of $89$ for \textit{E. coli} K12, $432$ of $432$ for human, and $275$ of $275$ for yeast.  The record is therefore a bounded modeled-adaptation contact.  Because the organism count is $3$, it is underpowered for a strong survival claim requiring a larger matched-species panel.

\paragraph{Matched mRNA and structure-conditioned yeast audit.}
The yeast five-way join used $4759$ joined genes, with a reported CDS join hit rate $0.9156038419146592$ and an overall join hit rate $0.8818572656921754$.  The residualized protein-abundance association had $R^2_{QP}=0.2715520808117803$.  Conditioning on the $5'$ CDS MFE proxy alone gave $R^2_{QP\mid S}=0.2706601505000995$, while conditioning on measured translation efficiency and mRNA stability gave $R^2_{QP\mid T,M}=0.13773967696311287$; conditioning on all three gave $R^2_{QP\mid T,M,S}=0.13759011208318542$.  The structure-only fraction was $0.003284564452662019$, and the structure-incremental fraction was $0.0005507778820193332$.  The $Q\to S$ structure proxy statistic had $R^2=0.003774248455422586$ with permutation right-tail probability $0.027972027972027972$, whereas the incremental structure fraction had permutation right-tail probability $0.1258741258741259$.  This audit keeps the structure proxy in a statistical-variance role and does not turn it into folded-structure evidence.

\paragraph{Negative and bounded downstream audits.}
Several linked finite-row checks constrain the interpretation.  The cross-ortholog selection-imprint experiment used $80$ orthogroups, $240$ rows, and $3$ species.  Its positive selection-imprint gate had $\Delta R^2=0.03250515015613468$, description-length change $-258.6010084301837$ bits, leave-clade-out $\Delta R^2=-0.0021477729853302424$, and null $95\%$ maximum $\Delta R^2=0.05427157066522958$, so the strong selection-imprint call was not supported.  The mass-balance audit used $120$ genes and $718$ intervals; adding the full residual block reduced performance from $R^2_{L2}=0.9712443377743347$ to $R^2_{L3}=0.8953999568421264$, with $\Delta R^2=-0.07584438093220836$, $\Delta\mathrm{DL}=-668.8046070381928$ bits, $p_{\mathrm{TEbin}}=0.6831683168316832$, and $p_{\mathrm{timeshuffle}}=0.9306930693069307$.  The turnover audit used $3290$ genes and $200$ null draws; it had $R^2_{L2}=0.055740851626933985$, $R^2_{L3}=0.050278872193227464$, $\Delta R^2=-0.005461979433706483$, $\Delta\mathrm{DL}=-13.68819707902732$ bits, $p_{\mathrm{matchedbin}}=0.8855721393034826$, and $p_{\mathrm{recoded}}=1.0$.  These rows are not failures of the finite codon-table or modeled-mediation contacts; they delimit where the present data stop.

\paragraph{Axis identity and local admissible claim.}
The structural-order axis audit computed the same nine residual axes and identified exactly one fold-level significant coordinate: \texttt{f3\_stress}.  Its weighted held-out increment was $0.0031744502294756994$, its weighted absolute unit loading was $0.5109586606724208$, and its sign-flip probability for a positive effect was $0.03125$.  Other axes did not pass that gate, for example \texttt{Ile\_AUA} had sign-flip probability $0.0693359375$ and \texttt{Arg\_AGR} had $0.142578125$.  The local claim is therefore narrow: the chapter records a finite codon-table contact, a bounded modeled-translation surface, and a controlled statistical $Q\to T\to P$ decomposition across matched rows.  It does not license a direct residual-to-protein interpretation when the translation step is absent, null, or untested.  No translation realization beyond the measured or explicitly bounded readout, no protein folding, no physical admissibility, no biological function, no molecular mechanism, no phenotype, and no universality follows without an additional layer-matched contact and a corresponding finite statistic.
