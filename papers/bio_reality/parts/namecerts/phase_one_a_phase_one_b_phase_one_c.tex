\subsection{Phase-one confirmatory boundary and translation-gated protein readback}
\label{sec:phase-one-confirmatory-translation-gated-protein-readback}

\paragraph{Finite surface.}
The record considered here is a finite mediation surface whose rows are triples $(q_i,t_j,p_k)$.  The $q_i$ coordinates are the nine $B^*_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates
$K\_AAA$, $Arg\_AGR$, $Ile\_AUA$, $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$, $Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG$, $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$, $Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG$, $Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY$, and $f3\_stress$.  The translation coordinate $t_j$ is allowed to be a bounded translation readout such as modeled tAI, stAI, ribosome occupancy, or translation efficiency.  The downstream coordinate $p_k$ is a matched protein-abundance readout.  The admissible readback is therefore not a direct residual-to-protein statement.  It is the controlled chain $Q_i \to T_j \to P_k$, with amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, CDS length, an $M$-density baseline, and matched mRNA abundance retained as controls.

\paragraph{Confirmatory finite rows.}
The phase-one contact uses the NCBI genetic-code table readback against locked codon-geometry claims.  The represented targets are $h0.null.model.aa\_class\_preserving$, $h1.leave\_one\_code\_out.by\_family$, and $h1.leave\_one\_codon\_out.module\_classification$.  The independent replication panel reports $40$ successes in $40$ trials, with rate $1.0$ and exact one-sided binomial upper-tail value $9.094947017729282\times 10^{-13}$.  The boundary-probe panel has the same finite shape: $40$ successes in $40$ trials, rate $1.0$, and exact one-sided binomial upper-tail value $9.094947017729282\times 10^{-13}$.  The negative-control panel has $20$ rows with $0$ unexpected successes, and the critical-ablation panel also has $20$ rows with $0$ unexpected successes.  In compact form, the finite readout is
$$
\begin{aligned}
R_{\mathrm{rep}}&=40/40=1.0, & p_{\mathrm{rep}}&=9.094947017729282\times 10^{-13},\\
R_{\mathrm{bdry}}&=40/40=1.0, & p_{\mathrm{bdry}}&=9.094947017729282\times 10^{-13},\\
M_{\mathrm{neg}}&=0/20, & M_{\mathrm{abl}}&=0/20.
\end{aligned}
$$
These rows certify the genetic-code-table contact for the phase-one module-classification and boundary tests.  They do not by themselves certify translation, protein output, structure, function, or a population-level biological rule.

\paragraph{Modeled translation contact.}
A bounded modeled-tAI contact was computed for three organisms: $Escherichia\ coli$ K-12, $Homo\ sapiens$, and $Saccharomyces\ cerevisiae$.  The tRNA payload covered all $20$ standard amino-acid families in each organism.  The FASTA header counts were $89$ for $E.\ coli$ K-12, $432$ for human, and $275$ for yeast; the matched header counts were $87$, $432$, and $275$, respectively.  The modeled tAI readout used $61$ sense codons in each organism and had zero raw-$W$ fallback count $0$ for all three.  The finite modeled coupling matrix had $81$ entries above the threshold $0.01$, with maximum absolute score $3801961.031607896$ and the same maximum on the diagonal.  This is a bounded codon-adaptation readback, not an observed ribosome-footprint measurement.  The three-organism panel is explicitly underpowered for a powered survival claim, because $3$ organisms are far below the declared $40$-species condition.

\paragraph{Cross-layer protein-abundance mediation.}
The powered protein-abundance decomposition uses the same nine residual coordinates and joins real CDS codon counts to PAXdb protein abundance in $12$ organisms.  The joined protein counts are $4042$ for $Bacillus\ subtilis$ strain 168, $11661$ for $Caenorhabditis\ elegans$, $14410$ for $Danio\ rerio$, $13295$ for $Drosophila\ melanogaster$, $3739$ for $Escherichia\ coli$ K-12 MG1655, $12418$ for $Gallus\ gallus$, $19053$ for human, $19395$ for mouse, $4129$ for $Mycobacterium\ smegmatis$, $13322$ for rat, $5815$ for yeast, and $1656$ for $Sulfolobus\ solfataricus$.  In every organism the rank diagnostics mark the powered rank as sufficient, with $9$ residual coordinates and $24$ control columns.

\paragraph{Mediation fractions.}
The joint $9$-dimensional decomposition reports positive indirect $R^2$ fractions in all $12$ organisms.  The mediated fractions $M^{QTP}$ are $0.6084594553474505$ in $Bacillus\ subtilis$, $0.4872268357227724$ in $C.\ elegans$, $0.3157053953614641$ in zebrafish, $0.6165167748739756$ in fly, $0.612922252793667$ in $E.\ coli$, $0.1976903964724546$ in chicken, $0.27206289160119157$ in human, $0.15488799907323988$ in mouse, $0.08397602919861291$ in $M.\ smegmatis$, $0.17489681798543982$ in rat, $0.9491551041216973$ in yeast, and $0.01850668564589326$ in $S.\ solfataricus$.  The corresponding total and direct protein-abundance readbacks include the yeast case
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{QP}&=0.29837624994140827,\\
R^2_{QP\mid T}&=0.0151709093608293,\\
R^2_{QP}-R^2_{QP\mid T}&=0.28320534058057895,\\
M^{QTP}&=0.9491551041216973.
\end{aligned}
$$
This is the strongest finite mediated fraction in the panel.  The weakest mediated fraction is $0.01850668564589326$ in $S.\ solfataricus$, where $R^2_{QP}=0.06397514251295629$ and $R^2_{QP\mid T}=0.06279117466131778$.  These numbers support a statistical mediation surface through modeled per-protein tAI; they do not establish causal translation dynamics.

\paragraph{Yeast structure-proxy split.}
The yeast five-way join supplies $4759$ rows across CDS counts, protein abundance, modeled translation efficiency, mRNA half-life, and a $5'$ CDS folding proxy.  The reported CDS join hit rate is $0.9156038419146592$, and the full join hit rate is $0.8818572656921754$.  In this yeast-only split, the direct residual-to-protein association after controls is $R^2_{QP}=0.2715520808117803$.  Conditioning on the $5'$ CDS MFE proxy gives $R^2_{QP\mid S}=0.2706601505000995$, while conditioning on measured translation efficiency plus mRNA stability gives $R^2_{QP\mid T,M}=0.13773967696311287$, and conditioning on all three gives $R^2_{QP\mid T,M,S}=0.13759011208318542$.  The structure-only fraction is $0.003284564452662019$, the structure-incremental fraction is $0.0005507778820193332$, and the residual structure readback $S_Q$ has $R^2=0.003774248455422586$ with permutation right-tail value $0.027972027972027972$.  The incremental structure permutation value is $0.1258741258741259$.  This is a variance partition using a CDS-only MFE proxy, not a folding-pathway or physical-admissibility result.

\paragraph{Axis identity.}
The held-out structural-order audit identifies $f3\_stress$ as the sole significant coordinate among the nine $B^*_{Q6}$ axes.  It has weighted held-out increment $0.0031744502294756994$, weighted absolute unit loading $0.5109586606724208$, sign-flip value $0.03125$, and rank $1$ by weighted delta.  The other coordinate increments are smaller or negative: $Arg\_AGR$ gives $0.0020139270616115154$ with sign-flip value $0.142578125$, $Ile\_AUA$ gives $0.0013067592718559024$ with $0.0693359375$, $K\_AAA$ gives $0.0005307046179682497$ with $0.2294921875$, $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$ gives $0.0010134090056701938$ with $0.2158203125$, $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$ gives $-0.0003205780368125179$ with $0.779296875$, and $Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY$ gives $-0.00045340330387383447$ with $0.529296875$.  Thus the finite axis call is not a general directional law for every synonymous residual; it is a coordinate-specific held-out signal dominated by $f3\_stress$.

\paragraph{Local boundary.}
The admissible conclusion is narrow.  The finite data support a phase-one genetic-code-table contact, a bounded modeled-tAI translation-readout contact, and a controlled statistical $Q_i\to T_j\to P_k$ protein-abundance decomposition across matched organism rows.  A direct $Q_i\to P_k$ association is not enough for protein-level promotion unless the $Q_i\to T_j$ step is present in the same controlled design.  No translation realization from ribosome dynamics, folded structure, physical admissibility, molecular function, biological mechanism, phenotype, selection claim, or universal biological law follows from these rows unless a separate layer-matched measurement supplies that contact and the same residual coordinate survives the declared controls.
