\paragraph{Finite surface.}
The record considered here is the finite mediation surface whose rows have the form $(q_i,t_j,p_k)$.  The $q_i$ coordinates are the nine projected $B^*_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates
$$
\begin{aligned}
Q=\{&K\_AAA,\ Arg\_AGR,\ Ile\_AUA,\ Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR,\ Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG,\\
&Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY,\ Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG,\ Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY,\ f3\_stress\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The translation slot $t_j$ is allowed to contain a bounded translation readout, such as modeled tAI, stAI, ribosome occupancy, or translation efficiency.  The protein slot $p_k$ is restricted to matched protein-abundance readouts.  A direct $q_i\to p_k$ association is therefore not a realization claim unless the same row also carries a surviving $q_i\to t_j$ step.  Amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, CDS length, and the $M$-density baseline remain controls.  Matched mRNA abundance is also a control separating translation-mediated signal from transcription-only signal.

This is a positive finite modeled-contact result, but it is not a powered ribosome-profiling or proteomics validation: the row count is only $3$ organisms, and the declared powered-survival route requires at least $40$ matched species.

\paragraph{Local verdict.}
The finite-row conclusion is that $B^*_{Q6}$ has a reproducible genetic-code contact and a bounded translation-to-protein mediation surface under explicit controls.  The strongest downstream table is the modeled-tAI protein-abundance mediation matrix, especially the yeast row, and the strongest coordinate-specific structural-order row is $f3\_stress$.  The result should be read as controlled cross-layer association and statistical mediation on finite joined tables.  It does not establish ribosome dynamics unless measured ribosome or translation-efficiency rows are joined at the same identifiers; it does not establish folding, physical admissibility, biological function, a molecular mechanism, organismal phenotype, selection, or universality unless a separate layer-matched contact supplies that measurement and the corresponding controlled statistic.
