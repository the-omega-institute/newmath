\subsection{Phase-one codon geometry and translation-conditioned protein abundance}
\label{sec:bioreality-phase-one-q6-translation-protein}

\paragraph{Finite surface.}
The record considered here is the finite mediation surface whose rows join three kinds of entries: a synonymous residual coordinate $q_i$ in the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ family, a translation readout $t_j$, and a matched protein-abundance readout $p_k$. The intended readback is not a direct map from synonymous residual geometry to protein abundance. It is the controlled chain
$$
\begin{aligned}
q_i \longrightarrow t_j \longrightarrow p_k,
\end{aligned}
$$
where $t_j$ is a bounded translation-layer quantity such as modeled tAI, stAI, ribosome occupancy, or translation efficiency, and $p_k$ is matched protein abundance. Amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, coding-sequence length, and the $M$-density baseline remain covariates. Matched mRNA abundance is also a control, so that a transcription-only association is not confused with a translation-mediated one. The coordinate list used in the powered protein-abundance run has nine entries: $K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $Leu_{CUN\,vs\,UUR}$, $Leu_{UUA\,vs\,UUG}$, $Ser_{UCR\,vs\,AGY}$, $Ser_{UCA\,vs\,UCG}$, $Thr_{ACR\,vs\,ACY}$, and $f_3$-stress.

\paragraph{Independent codon-table contact.}
The phase-one finite row is already supported at the codon-table layer. The locked confirmatory readback used the NCBI genetic-code table against the three target claims $h0.null.model.aa\_class\_preserving$, $h1.leave\_one\_code\_out.by\_family$, and $h1.leave\_one\_codon\_out.module\_classification$. Both the boundary probe and the independent replication recorded $40$ successes in $40$ trials, with exact one-sided binomial upper-tail probability $9.094947017729282\times 10^{-13}$ in each case. The negative-control panel had $0$ unexpected successes in $20$ trials, and the critical-ablation panel likewise had $0$ unexpected successes in $20$ trials. This establishes a curated genetic-code-table contact for the phase-one geometry, not a translation, protein, folding, function, mechanism, or universality claim.

\paragraph{Modeled translation contact.}
A bounded modeled-tAI surface was computed for three organisms using GtRNAdb tRNA gene-copy counts and dos Reis wobble weights. The organism count is $3$, with modeled weights for all $61$ sense codons in each organism and no zero raw-weight fallback in the three modeled-tAI readouts. The local GtRNAdb payload contained all $20$ standard amino-acid families for \textit{Escherichia coli} K-12, \textit{Homo sapiens}, and \textit{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}; the matched FASTA header counts were $87$ of $89$, $432$ of $432$, and $275$ of $275$, respectively. The bounded-coupling matrix had $81$ entries above threshold $0.01$, and the maximum absolute entry was $3801961.031607896$. This is a modeled codon-adaptation contact. Since only $3$ organisms are present, it is not a powered multi-organism survival claim and does not observe ribosome movement.

\paragraph{Protein-abundance mediation rows.}
The powered mediation run joined real coding sequences, modeled per-protein tAI, and PAXdb protein abundance across $12$ organism panels. Joined protein counts were $4042$ for \textit{Bacillus subtilis} strain 168, $11661$ for \textit{Caenorhabditis elegans}, $14410$ for \textit{Danio rerio}, $13295$ for \textit{Drosophila melanogaster}, $3739$ for \textit{Escherichia coli} K-12 MG1655, $12418$ for \textit{Gallus gallus}, $19053$ for \textit{Homo sapiens}, $19395$ for \textit{Mus musculus}, $4129$ for \textit{Mycobacterium smegmatis}$\,$str. MC2 155, $13322$ for \textit{Rattus norvegicus}, $5815$ for \textit{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}, and $1656$ for \textit{Sulfolobus solfataricus}. In every organism panel the residualized design had rank $9$ for the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ coordinates, rank $1$ for the modeled translation readout, and rank $10$ for the joint $Q,T$ design, with $23$ control-rank columns after residualization. The residual degrees of freedom after controls ranged from $1633$ in \textit{S. solfataricus} to $19030$ in human.

\paragraph{Mediation scalar.}
The scalar decomposition used
$$
\begin{aligned}
M^{QTP} &= \frac{R^2(Q\to P)-R^2(Q\to P\mid T)}{R^2(Q\to P)}.
\end{aligned}
$$
All $12$ organism panels had $M^{QTP}$ inside the unit interval. The largest mediated fraction was in yeast, where $R^2(Q\to P)=0.29837624994140827$, $R^2(Q\to P\mid T)=0.0151709093608293$, the indirect component was $0.28320534058057895$, and $M^{QTP}=0.9491551041216973$. Other large fractions occurred in \textit{D. melanogaster} with $M^{QTP}=0.6165167748739756$, \textit{E. coli} with $M^{QTP}=0.612922252793667$, and \textit{B. subtilis} with $M^{QTP}=0.6084594553474505$. Smaller but positive decompositions included mouse with $M^{QTP}=0.15488799907323988$, \textit{M. smegmatis} with $M^{QTP}=0.08397602919861291$, and \textit{S. solfataricus} with $M^{QTP}=0.01850668564589326$. These numbers support a statistical translation-conditioned protein-abundance decomposition on the finite rows, while leaving causal mechanism unasserted.

\paragraph{Yeast structure and abundance partition.}
A yeast five-way join supplied $4759$ matched rows for coding sequence, measured translation efficiency, protein abundance, mRNA half-life, and a $5'$ coding-region minimum-free-energy proxy. The reported CDS join hit rate was $0.9156038419146592$, and the broader join hit rate was $0.8818572656921754$. After residualizing the same control family, the total $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ to protein-abundance value was $R^2_{QP}=0.2715520808117803$. Conditioning only on the $5'$ coding-region MFE proxy gave $R^2_{QP\mid S}=0.2706601505000995$. Conditioning on measured translation efficiency and mRNA stability gave $R^2_{QP\mid T,M}=0.13773967696311287$, and conditioning on all three gave $R^2_{QP\mid T,M,S}=0.13759011208318542$. The structure-only fraction was $0.003284564452662019$, the incremental structure fraction beyond translation and mRNA stability was $0.0005507778820193332$, and $R^2(S\sim Q)=0.003774248455422586$ with right-tail permutation probability $0.027972027972027972$. The incremental structure probability was $0.1258741258741259$, so the structure proxy is a small statistical partition, not a folding readout.

\paragraph{Axis and execution audits.}
The structural-order axis audit selected exactly one significant coordinate: $f_3$-stress. Its weighted held-out $R^2$ increment was $0.0031744502294756994$, its weighted absolute unit loading was $0.5109586606724208$, and the sign-flip probability for a positive increment was $0.03125$. The other eight coordinates did not pass the same fold-level sign-flip gate. A separate observable-execution decomposition in \textit{S. cerevisiae} used $3289$ genes and returned the finite verdict that at least one observed rate-proxy slot passed both effective null classes. These audits sharpen the finite coordinate identity: the strongest structural-order signal is carried by $f_3$-stress, while rate-proxy execution remains a bounded observable subblock.

\paragraph{Negative and bounded results.}
The cross-ortholog selection-imprint experiment used $80$ orthogroups, $240$ rows, and $3$ species. Its positive selection-imprint gate was not passed: $\Delta R^2=0.03250515015613468$, description-length change was $-258.6010084301837$ bits, leave-clade-out $\Delta R^2=-0.0021477729853302424$, and the largest null $95$th-percentile $\Delta R^2$ was $0.05427157066522958$. The honest conclusion is codon-bias expression correlation, not a strong evolutionary selection-imprint claim. The dynamic mass-balance run used $120$ genes and $718$ intervals after a fixed subsample from $4434$ eligible genes. Its baseline $M$-only value was $R^2=0.9960628201518571$, adding estimated expression gave $R^2=0.996410628851051$, while the deeper layers had $R^2=0.9712443377743347$ and $R^2=0.8953999568421264$. The resulting $\Delta R^2=-0.07584438093220836$, $\Delta DL=-668.8046070381928$ bits, $p_{TEbin}=0.6831683168316832$, and $p_{timeshuffle}=0.9306930693069307$ do not promote the dynamic closure claim.

\paragraph{Local boundary.}
The chapter therefore records a finite, controlled cross-layer association: phase-one codon geometry has a strong genetic-code-table contact; $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates have bounded modeled translation contacts; and, on matched organism rows, the residual-to-protein abundance association can be decomposed through modeled per-protein tAI. The allowed statement is statistical and layer-limited. No translation realization follows from codon geometry alone. No ribosome occupancy follows from tAI or stAI weights alone. No protein folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, organismal phenotype, causal mechanism, evolutionary selection law, or universal biological rule follows from these rows unless a distinct layer-matched contact supplies the relevant measurement and the controlled statistic is computed on the same finite surface.
