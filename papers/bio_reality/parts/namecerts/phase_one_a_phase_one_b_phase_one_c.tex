\paragraph{Local no-promotion rule.}
The admissible conclusion is that the phase-one genetic-code contact is strongly replicated at the table level, and that later $B^*_{Q6}$ rows define a controlled finite route from residual coordinates through translation readouts into matched protein-abundance decompositions.  The chapter does not claim translation from the phase-one table alone, does not claim ribosome dynamics from modeled tAI alone, does not claim folding or physical admissibility from abundance, does not claim biological function from a residual coordinate, does not claim a molecular mechanism from a mediation fraction, and does not claim universality from any single organism or finite panel.  Any such stronger assertion requires its own layer-matched observational contact, its own matched rows, and its own controlled statistic after amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, CDS length, M-density baseline, matched mRNA abundance, and simpler-baseline controls are present.
