\paragraph{Finite surface.}
This record concerns a finite mediation surface whose rows are triples $(q_i,t_j,p_k)$.  The $q_i$ coordinates are the nine $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates
$$
\begin{aligned}
&K\_AAA,\ Arg\_AGR,\ Ile\_AUA,\ Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR,\ Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG,\\
&Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY,\ Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG,\ Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY,\ f3\_stress.
\end{aligned}
$$
The $t_j$ readouts are bounded translation readouts, here represented by modeled per-protein tAI and, in the wider contact design, by tAI, stAI, ribosome occupancy, or translation efficiency.  The $p_k$ readout is matched protein abundance, represented in the powered mediation run by $\log_{10}$ abundance ppm from PAXdb joined to real CDS codon counts.  The admissible readback is therefore not a direct residual-to-protein statement.  It is the controlled chain $Q_i \to T_j \to P_k$, where the translation slot must be evaluated before the protein-abundance slot is interpreted.

\paragraph{Phase-one table contact.}
The locked genetic-code-table contact was tested against the NCBI genetic-code table readback.  The finite audit represented the targets $h0.null.model.aa\_class\_preserving$, $h1.leave\_one\_code\_out.by\_family$, and $h1.leave\_one\_codon\_out.module\_classification$.  Both the independent replication probe and the boundary probe returned $40$ successes in $40$ trials, with exact one-sided binomial upper-tail probability $9.094947017729282\times 10^{-13}$ in each case.  The negative-control panel had $20$ rows and $0$ unexpected successes, and the critical-ablation panel also had $20$ rows and $0$ unexpected successes.  Thus the phase-one row is a strong finite contact with the curated genetic-code table, but its own scope is only the codon-geometry contact.  It does not by itself assert translation, protein structure, physical admissibility, biological function, a mechanism, or a universal biological law.

\paragraph{Modeled translation survival boundary.}
The modeled-tAI survival surface computed the same nine $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates in three organisms: \emph{Escherichia coli} K-12, \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}, and \emph{Homo sapiens}.  The modeled readout used GtRNAdb tRNA gene-copy counts and dos Reis wobble weights, producing $61$ sense-codon weights per organism with zero raw-weight fallback count $0$ in all three organisms.  The local tRNA payload covered all $20$ standard amino-acid families in each organism, with matched FASTA header counts $87$ of $89$ for \emph{E. coli}, $275$ of $275$ for yeast, and $432$ of $432$ for human.  At threshold $0.01$, the bounded modeled-coupling matrix had $81$ entries above threshold and maximum absolute entry $3801961.031607896$.  This is a nonzero bounded modeled readout, not a measured ribosome-profiling validation: the organism count is $3$, whereas the declared powered-survival route requires at least $40$ matched species for that stronger claim.

\paragraph{Protein-abundance mediation rows.}

After controls for intercept, CDS length, amino-acid composition, GC3, and the $M$-density baseline, all organisms were rank-sufficient for the joint nine-coordinate design.  The mediated fractions $M^{QTP}$ were in the unit interval in all $12$ organisms:

\begin{aligned}

Yeast had the largest mediated fraction, with $c=0.29837624994140827$, $c'=0.0151709093608293$, and indirect increment $0.28320534058057895$. These finite rows support a controlled statistical mediation decomposition through modeled tAI; they do not certify a causal translation mechanism.

\paragraph{Matched mRNA and structure controls.}
A yeast five-way join supplied $4759$ rows linking CDS, protein abundance, measured TE, mRNA half-life, and a $5'$ CDS folding-free-energy proxy.  The reported CDS join hit rate was $0.9156038419146592$ and the overall join hit rate was $0.8818572656921754$.  In that panel, the total residual association was $R^2_{QP}=0.2715520808117803$.  Conditioning on the structure proxy alone gave $R^2_{QP\mid S}=0.2706601505000995$, while conditioning on measured TE plus mRNA stability gave $R^2_{QP\mid T,M}=0.13773967696311287$, and conditioning on all three gave $R^2_{QP\mid T,M,S}=0.13759011208318542$.  The structure-only fraction was $0.003284564452662019$, the incremental structure fraction beyond TE and mRNA stability was $0.0005507778820193332$, and the right-tail permutation probability for the structure incremental statistic was $0.1258741258741259$.  The structure-contact row is therefore a variance partition over a sequence-derived proxy, not a folded-structure experiment.
