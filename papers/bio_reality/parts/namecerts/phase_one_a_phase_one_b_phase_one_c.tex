\subsection{Translation-Gated Protein-Abundance Surface for the $B^*_{Q6}$ Residual Coordinates}
\label{sec:bioreality-q6-translation-gated-protein-abundance}

\paragraph{Finite surface.}
This record treats the $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinates as a finite row system whose admissible readback has three layers: a residual coordinate $q_i$, a translation readout $t_j$, and a matched protein-abundance readout $p_k$. The active coordinate list is the nine-coordinate family
$$
\begin{aligned}
Q=\{&K\_AAA,\ Arg\_AGR,\ Ile\_AUA,\ Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR,\ Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG,\\
&Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY,\ Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG,\ Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY,\ f3\_stress\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The admissible interpretation is therefore not a direct $q_i\to p_k$ assertion. A protein-abundance association may be discussed only through the controlled route $q_i\to t_j\to p_k$, where $t_j$ is a translation-layer quantity such as modeled tAI, stAI, ribosome occupancy, or translation efficiency, and where matched mRNA abundance is used to separate translation from transcriptional abundance. Amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, coding-sequence length, and the M-density baseline remain controls rather than promoted biological coordinates.

\paragraph{Confirmatory genetic-code contact.}
The phase-one finite contact was run as a locked confirmatory falsification surface against the NCBI genetic-code table $gc.prt$ snapshot dated 2026-06-16T18:30:05+00:00. The registered targets were $h0.null.model.aa\_class\_preserving$, $h1.leave\_one\_code\_out.by\_family$, and $h1.leave\_one\_codon\_out.module\_classification$. Both the boundary probe and the independent replication gave $40$ successes in $40$ trials, with exact one-sided binomial upper-tail probability $9.094947017729282\times 10^{-13}$ in each case. The negative control had $0$ unexpected successes in $20$ trials, and the critical ablation had $0$ unexpected successes in $20$ trials. These numbers support the finite codon-geometry contact at the genetic-code table layer only. They do not by themselves observe translation, protein abundance, folded structure, physical admissibility, molecular function, mechanism, phenotype, or a universal biological rule.

\paragraph{Modeled translation readout.}
A bounded modeled-tAI surface was computed from GtRNAdb tRNA gene-copy counts and dos Reis wobble weights for $3$ organisms. The codon-count space had $61$ sense codons, and the local payload covered all $20$ standard amino-acid families in Escherichia coli K12, Homo sapiens, and Saccharomyces cerevisiae. The modeled coupling table had $81$ entries above the threshold $0.01$, with maximum absolute entry $3801961.031607896$. This is a finite modeled-adaptation contact, not a measured ribosome-profiling result. The row count $3$ is explicitly below the $\geq 40$ matched-species condition requested for powered survival, so the output is read as bounded coupling rather than population-level translation survival.

\paragraph{Local boundary.}
The chapter-level claim is therefore limited to a finite, controlled, cross-layer statistical surface: $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinates can be placed into a $Q\to T\to P$ mediation matrix when translation readouts and protein-abundance rows are organism-matched and control-residualized. The admissible positive reading is a bounded mediation statistic on finite rows. No translation mechanism, ribosome dynamics, folded structure, physical admissibility, molecular function, organismal phenotype, causal pathway, evolutionary law, or universal biological principle follows from this chapter unless a separate layer-matched measurement supplies that contact and the corresponding controlled statistic is computed.
