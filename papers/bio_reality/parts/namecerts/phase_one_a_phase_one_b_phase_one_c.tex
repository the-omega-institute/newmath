\subsection{Translation-Gated Protein-Abundance Mediation for $B^*_{Q6}$ Residual Coordinates}
\label{sec:bioreality-q6-translation-mediated-protein-abundance}

\paragraph{Finite surface.}
The claim is represented on a finite mediation surface whose rows are triples $(q_i,T_j,P_k)$.  The coordinate $q_i$ ranges over the nine $B^*_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates
$K\_AAA$, $Arg\_AGR$, $Ile\_AUA$, $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$, $Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG$, $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$, $Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG$, $Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY$, and $f3\_stress$.  The middle coordinate $T_j$ is a translation readout, here instantiated either as a bounded modeled tAI readout or, where available, as measured translation efficiency.  The downstream coordinate $P_k$ is matched protein abundance, read as $\log_{10}(\mathrm{abundance\_ppm})$ in the multi-organism protein table.  The admissible readback is not a direct residual-to-protein assertion.  It is the controlled chain
$$
\begin{aligned}
q_i &\longrightarrow T_j \longrightarrow P_k,\\
Z &= \{\hbox{amino-acid composition},\ \hbox{organism},\ GC3,\ \hbox{CDS length},\ M\hbox{-density baseline},\ \hbox{matched mRNA where available}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
A row is therefore promoted only to a protein-abundance mediation row when the translation coordinate has survived its own readout test and the remaining protein association is evaluated after the declared controls.  Amino-acid sequence, organism identity, $GC3$, length, and $M$-density stay in the control block; they are not promoted to residual coordinates.

\paragraph{Confirmatory finite contact.}
The finite genetic-code contact supporting the surrounding BioReality packet used an independent confirmatory table readback from the NCBI genetic-code table.  The locked target family was represented by $h0.null.model.aa\_class\_preserving$, $h1.leave\_one\_code\_out.by\_family$, and $h1.leave\_one\_codon\_out.module\_classification$.  The independent replication statistic had $40$ successes in $40$ trials, with exact one-sided binomial upper-tail probability $9.094947017729282\times 10^{-13}$ and rate $1.0$.  The boundary probe used the same finite count, again $40/40$ with probability $9.094947017729282\times 10^{-13}$.  The negative-control panel had $20$ trials and $0$ unexpected successes, and the critical-ablation panel also had $20$ trials and $0$ unexpected successes.  These numbers certify a finite codon-table contact for the original geometry claim, but they do not by themselves certify translation, protein realization, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, mechanism, phenotype, or universality.

\paragraph{Translation readout before protein readout.}
A modeled tAI contact was computed for three organisms: \emph{Escherichia coli} K12, \emph{Homo sapiens}, and \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}.  The tRNA payload contained all $20$ standard amino-acid families in each organism.  The matched FASTA-header counts were $87$ of $89$ for \emph{E. coli} K12, $432$ of $432$ for human, and $275$ of $275$ for yeast.  Each modeled tAI table covered $61$ sense codons, and the raw-weight fallback count was $0$ for all three organisms in that bounded calculation.  The modeled coupling audit found $81$ entries above the threshold $0.01$, with maximal absolute $S_{ij}$ equal to $3801961.031607896$.  This is a bounded modeled-readout result, not a measured ribosome-profiling result: the organism count was $3$, below the declared powered-survival requirement of at least $40$ matched species.  Thus the modeled tAI computation supplies a translation-layer contact only at bounded scope.

\paragraph{Local verdict.}
The finite-row evidence supports a ready BioReality chapter for a controlled translation-gated protein-abundance mediation surface.  The positive statement is narrow: $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinates can be organized into a finite $Q\to T\to P$ matrix, and the available multi-organism protein table contains positive mediated fractions after the declared controls, with all ranks and row counts explicit.  The negative statement is equally part of the claim: a direct $Q\to P$ association is not admissible as protein realization unless the $Q\to T$ step is present at the corresponding readout layer, and none of these rows establishes translation mechanism, folding, physical admissibility, biological function, phenotype, selection, or universality without an additional layer-matched contact.
