\subsection{Locked Genetic-Code Readback and Translation-Bounded Protein Mediation}
\label{subsec:biorereality-locked-genetic-code-and-q6-translation-mediation}

\paragraph{Finite surface.}
The finite record considered here has two coupled surfaces.  The first is the locked genetic-code readback for the three registered codon-geometry targets $h0.null.model.aa\_class\_preserving$, $h1.leave\_one\_code\_out.by\_family$, and $h1.leave\_one\_codon\_out.module\_classification$.  The second is the downstream $B^{*}_{Q6}$ mediation surface whose rows are triples $(q_i,t_j,p_k)$: $q_i$ ranges over the nine residual coordinates
$$
\begin{aligned}
&K\_AAA,\ Arg\_AGR,\ Ile\_AUA,\ Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR,\ Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG,\\
&Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY,\ Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG,\ Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY,\ f3\_stress,
\end{aligned}
$$
$t_j$ is a bounded translation readout such as modeled tAI, stAI, ribosome occupancy, or translation efficiency, and $p_k$ is matched protein abundance.  The admissible readback is therefore not a direct residual-to-protein claim.  It is a controlled mediation matrix in which a residual coordinate must first survive a translation-readout step before any downstream protein-abundance association may be interpreted as translation-conditioned.

\paragraph{Confirmatory code-table contact.}
The locked confirmatory readback used the NCBI genetic-code table $gc.prt$ against the registered codon-geometry claims.  The registered protocol was declared before observation at $2026$-$05$-$29T20{:}11{:}31{+}00{:}00$, and the snapshot time was $2026$-$06$-$16T18{:}30{:}05{+}00{:}00$.  The independent replication statistic had $n=40$ trials, $40$ successes, rate $1.0$, and exact one-sided binomial upper-tail probability $9.094947017729282\times 10^{-13}$.  The boundary probe used the same doubled trial count, again with $n=40$, $40$ successes, rate $1.0$, and probability $9.094947017729282\times 10^{-13}$.  The negative-control surface had $n=20$ and $0$ unexpected successes, and the critical ablation surface also had $n=20$ and $0$ unexpected successes.  These numbers support a locked table-level contact for the three named codon-geometry targets; they do not by themselves report translation, protein structure, physical admissibility, function, mechanism, or a universal biological rule.

\paragraph{Modeled translation-contact boundary.}
For the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ translation-readout layer, the modeled-tAI audit used $61$ sense codons and three organisms.  The local GtRNAdb payload covered all $20$ standard amino-acid families in each organism: Escherichia coli K12 had $89$ FASTA headers with $87$ matched and $2$ unmatched headers, Homo sapiens had $432$ matched headers, and Saccharomyces cerevisiae had $275$ matched headers.  Dos Reis wobble-weight readouts gave zero raw-$W$ fallback count $0$ for each of the three organisms.  The bounded-coupling surface had $81$ entries above threshold $0.01$, with maximum absolute $S_{ij}=3801961.031607896$ and maximum absolute diagonal $S_i=3801961.031607896$.  This is a modeled codon-adaptation contact with $organism\_n=3$; it is explicitly underpowered for a powered survival claim requiring at least $40$ matched species.

\paragraph{Axis identity and selection boundary.}
The structural-order axis audit again computed the same nine $B^{*}_{Q6}$ axes.  Exactly one coordinate passed the fold-level sign-flip gate: $f3\_stress$.  Its weighted held-out $R^2$ increment was $0.0031744502294756994$, weighted absolute unit loading was $0.5109586606724208$, rank by weighted increment was $1$, and sign-flip probability was $0.03125$.  By contrast, the stronger cross-ortholog selection-imprint computation over $80$ orthogroups, $240$ rows, and $3$ species did not pass the positive selection-imprint gate: $\Delta R^2=0.03250515015613468$, description-length change $-258.6010084301837$ bits, leave-clade-out $\Delta R^2=-0.0021477729853302424$, and maximum null-$95$ $\Delta R^2=0.05427157066522958$.  The honest conclusion for that surface is codon-bias expression correlation, not a strong evolutionary selection-imprint result.

\paragraph{Local interpretation rule.}
The finite evidence supports a table-level codon-geometry contact and a bounded set of modeled or matched translation/protein-abundance decompositions.  It also enforces a strict layer boundary.  A $Q_i\to P_k$ association is not admissible as protein realization unless the same coordinate has first passed a controlled $Q_i\to T_j$ translation-readout step.  Matched mRNA abundance is a transcription-layer control, not a translation readout.  Modeled tAI and stAI are codon-adaptation annotations, not direct ribosome-occupancy measurements.  Protein abundance is not folding, molecular function, perturbational mechanism, physical admissibility, phenotype, or universality.  Any such higher-layer conclusion requires a separate matched contact and its own finite statistic after amino-acid composition, organism, $GC3$, CDS length, $M$-density baseline, and matched mRNA abundance have been controlled.
