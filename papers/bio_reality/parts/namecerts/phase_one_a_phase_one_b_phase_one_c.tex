\subsection{Phase-One Codon Geometry and the Translation-Gated Protein-Abundance Boundary}
\label{subsec:bioreality-phase-one-q6-translation-mediated-protein-abundance}

\paragraph{Finite object.}
The finite surface considered here is the controlled table of triples $(q_i,t_j,p_k)$ in which $q_i$ is one of the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates, $t_j$ is a translation readout, and $p_k$ is a matched protein-abundance readout. The residual coordinate list used in the translation and protein-abundance audits is
$$
\begin{aligned}
Q=\{&\mathrm{K\_AAA},\ \mathrm{Arg\_AGR},\ \mathrm{Ile\_AUA},\ \mathrm{Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR},\ \\
&\mathrm{Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG},\ \mathrm{Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY},\ \mathrm{Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG},\ \\
&\mathrm{Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY},\ f_3\text{-}\mathrm{stress}\} .
\end{aligned}
$$
The phase-one contact is narrower: it confirms that codon-window geometry has a reproducible table-level relation to curated genetic-code data. It does not by itself measure translation, protein output, folded structure, physical admissibility, biological function, mechanism, selection, or universality. Those claims require layer-matched measurements rather than coordinate geometry alone.

\paragraph{Phase-one finite confirmation.}
The locked confirmatory run for \texttt{phase.one.a+phase.one.b+phase.one.c} used the NCBI genetic-code table \texttt{gc.prt} against the phase-one codon-geometry claims. The represented target claims were \texttt{h0.null.model.aa\_class\_preserving}, \texttt{h1.leave\_one\_code\_out.by\_family}, and \texttt{h1.leave\_one\_codon\_out.module\_classification}. The independent replication statistic and the boundary-probe statistic both had $40$ successes in $40$ trials, rate $1.0$, and exact one-sided binomial upper-tail value
$$
\begin{aligned}
 p &= 9.094947017729282\times 10^{-13},\\
 n &= 40,\\
 \mathrm{successes} &= 40 .
\end{aligned}
$$
The two zero-tolerance checks were also finite and explicit: the negative-control table had $20$ rows with $0$ unexpected successes, and the critical-ablation table had $20$ rows with $0$ unexpected successes. Thus the phase-one row is a strong codon-table confirmation and a strict no-promotion boundary at the same time. The observed phase-one fact is about genetic-code table contact, not about translation dynamics or protein abundance.

\paragraph{Modeled translation readout.}
A separate modeled-tAI audit computed $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates in a $61$-sense-codon count space for three organisms: \textit{Escherichia coli} K-12, \textit{Homo sapiens}, and \textit{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}. The readout used GtRNAdb tRNA gene-copy counts and dos Reis wobble weights to form codon-level modeled tAI weights. Each of the three organisms had all $61$ sense codons assigned a finite modeled weight with zero raw-weight fallback count $0$. The local tRNA payload contained all $20$ standard amino-acid families in each organism; the matched FASTA header counts were $87$ of $89$ for \textit{E. coli} K-12, $432$ of $432$ for human, and $275$ of $275$ for yeast.

The bounded modeled coupling was nonzero: $81$ entries exceeded the declared threshold $0.01$, and the maximum absolute entry was
$$
\begin{aligned}
\max |S_{ij}|&=3801961.031607896,\\
\max |S_i^{\mathrm{diag}}|&=3801961.031607896,\\
\theta&=0.01 .
\end{aligned}
$$
This is still a modeled-readout result, not ribosome profiling and not proteomics validation. The organism count was $3$, below the powered-survival rule requiring at least $40$ matched species for the requested powered claim. Consequently the finite result is a bounded modeled translation contact: it shows that the residual coordinates can be evaluated against a modeled tAI surface, while the measured translation-survival claim remains unpromoted.

\paragraph{Adjacent finite outcomes.}
The structural-order audit identified a single dominant coordinate, $f_3$-stress. It was rank $1$ by weighted increment, had weighted held-out $\Delta R^2=0.0031744502294756994$, weighted absolute unit loading $0.5109586606724208$, and fold-level sign-flip value $p=0.03125$. The full $B^{*}_{Q6}$ signal passed the reported sign-flip gate, while the other listed coordinates did not pass that same exact gate.

The observable execution decomposition in yeast reported $3289$ genes and concluded that at least one observed rate-proxy slot passed both effective null classes. The evolutionary selection-imprint audit had $80$ orthogroups, $240$ rows, and $3$ species; its positive selection-imprint gate was not supported, with $\Delta R^2=0.03250515015613468$, leave-clade-out $\Delta R^2=-0.0021477729853302424$, and maximum null $95\%$ $\Delta R^2=0.05427157066522958$. The mass-balance dynamic audit used $120$ genes and $718$ intervals; its final added layer had $\Delta R^2=-0.07584438093220836$, $\Delta\mathrm{DL}=-668.8046070381928$ bits, $p_{\mathrm{TEbin}}=0.6831683168316832$, and $p_{\mathrm{timeshuffle}}=0.9306930693069307$. The turnover-residual audit used $3290$ genes and reported $\Delta R^2=-0.005461979433706483$, $\Delta\mathrm{DL}=-13.68819707902732$ bits, $p_{\mathrm{matchedbin}}=0.8855721393034826$, and $p_{\mathrm{recoded}}=1.0$. These adjacent rows sharpen the boundary: some rate and structural-order contacts produce finite positive summaries, while selection imprint, mass-balance closure, and turnover prediction remain negative or bounded in the current data.

\paragraph{Local verdict.}
The finite evidence supports three statements. First, phase-one codon geometry is strongly confirmed at the curated genetic-code table layer, with two independent $40/40$ exact-binomial confirmations and zero failures in two $20$-row exclusion checks. Second, the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates have nonzero bounded contact with modeled tAI surfaces and finite statistical decompositions against matched protein-abundance rows. Third, a protein-abundance association is admissible here only as a controlled $Q\to T\to P$ decomposition in which the translation readout remains explicit. No direct $Q\to P$ association may be read as protein realization when the $Q\to T$ step is absent, null, rank-saturated, or untested. No translation, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, biological mechanism, evolutionary law, or organismal universality follows from these rows unless a separate measurement surface at that layer supplies the contact and the corresponding controlled statistic is computed.
