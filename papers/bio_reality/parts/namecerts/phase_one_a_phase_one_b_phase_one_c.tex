\paragraph{Finite surface.}
This record packages the phase-one genetic-code contact together with the later $B^*_{Q6}$ translation and protein-abundance rows as a single finite mediation surface.  The coordinates are triples $(q_i,t_j,p_k)$, where $q_i$ ranges over the nine computed $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinates, $t_j$ is a bounded translation readout, and $p_k$ is matched protein abundance.  The admissible residual coordinates are
$$
\begin{aligned}
&K\_AAA,\ Arg\_AGR,\ Ile\_AUA,\ Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR,\ Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG,\\
&Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY,\ Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG,\ Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY,\ f3\_stress.
\end{aligned}
$$
The readout is not a direct residual-to-protein assertion.  A row is allowed to reach the protein-abundance coordinate only through a translation entry $q_i\to t_j$ that remains visible after the declared controls.  Amino-acid composition, organism identity, GC3, coding-sequence length, and the $M$-density baseline remain controls; they are not promoted into the residual coordinate set.  Matched mRNA abundance is used to distinguish translation-layer signal from transcription-layer signal, rather than as a translation observation by itself.

\paragraph{Phase-one contact.}
The finite external table contact represented the locked phase-one codon-geometry claims against the NCBI genetic-code table readback.  The three represented target claims were the amino-acid-class-preserving null model, the leave-one-code-out family test, and the leave-one-codon-out module-classification test.  The confirmatory surface had $40$ successes in $40$ independent-replication trials and $40$ successes in $40$ boundary-probe trials, each with exact one-sided binomial upper-tail probability $9.094947017729282\times 10^{-13}$.  The negative-control panel had $20$ rows with $0$ unexpected successes, and the critical-ablation panel likewise had $20$ rows with $0$ unexpected successes.  The preregistration timestamp was 2026-05-29T20:11:31+00:00, and the table snapshot used here is 2026-06-16T18:30:05+00:00.  These numbers justify only the curated genetic-code-table contact for the phase-one claims; they do not by themselves observe translation, protein structure, physical admissibility, function, mechanism, or a universal biological law.

\paragraph{Modeled translation readout.}
The first translation-layer contact is a modeled tAI surface computed from GtRNAdb tRNA gene-copy rows with dos Reis wobble weights.  It used $3$ organisms: \emph{Escherichia coli} K12, \emph{Homo sapiens}, and \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}.  The organism payloads covered all $20$ standard amino-acid families.  The FASTA header counts were $89$, $432$, and $275$, with matched header counts $87$, $432$, and $275$, respectively.  For each organism the modeled tAI readout covered $61$ sense codons, and the zero raw-weight fallback count was $0$ in all three cases.  The bounded coupling audit reported $81$ entries above threshold $0.01$, with maximum absolute entry
$$
\begin{aligned}
\max |S_{ij}|=3{,}801{,}961.031607896.
\end{aligned}
$$
This is a finite modeled-coupling contact, not a powered translation-survival experiment: with only $3$ organisms it does not meet the stated threshold of at least $40$ matched species for powered survival.  Its role is therefore to keep the $q_i\to t_j$ route open while refusing claims about measured ribosome occupancy or downstream protein realization from modeled weights alone.

\paragraph{Local verdict.}
The chapter therefore records a bounded positive statement: the finite surface contains a controlled $B^*_{Q6}$ residual family, a modeled translation-readout route, and organism-matched protein-abundance decompositions in which the mediated fraction is nonnegative and in $[0,1]$ across all $12$ powered organism panels.  The strongest protein-abundance mediation fraction appears in yeast at $0.9491551041216973$, while the smallest appears in \emph{S. solfataricus} at $0.01850668564589326$.  The phase-one genetic-code table contact is also strong on its own finite terms, with $40/40$ successes in each of two confirmatory panels and no unexpected successes in either $20$-row control panel.  The readback must remain narrow: no translation mechanism, ribosome dynamics, folded structure, physical admissibility, biological function, organismal phenotype, causal effect, or universal rule follows from these rows unless a separate layer-matched contact supplies that observation and the corresponding controlled statistic.
