\paragraph{Local boundary.}
The admissible reading is narrow.  The phase-one rows establish a locked genetic-code-table contact for the named codon-geometry targets, and the later rows show bounded statistical decompositions through modeled translation weights, measured translation-efficiency proxies, abundance tables, structure proxies, turnover, and perturbation screens.  They do not by themselves establish translation realization, folded structure, physical admissibility, molecular function, biological mechanism, organismal phenotype, selection as a population law, or universality.  A direct $Q_i\to P_k$ association is not promoted unless a same-coordinate $Q_i\to T_j$ translation-readout step is present and evaluated under amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, coding-sequence length, $M$-density baseline, matched mRNA abundance, and simpler-baseline controls.  Where the available readout is modeled tAI or stAI, it remains a bounded codon-adaptation annotation; where the readout is PAXdb abundance, it remains a steady-state abundance measurement entangled with expression, turnover, localization, sample handling, and identifier reconciliation.  Higher biological layers require their own matched measurements before any positive statement can be made.
