\subsection{Phase-one codon geometry and the translation-mediated protein-abundance boundary}\label{sec:bioreality-phase-one-q6-translation-protein-boundary}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite row surface.}
This chapter records a finite BioReality surface whose first layer is the locked phase-one codon-geometry contact and whose later layer is a bounded $B^*_{Q6}$ mediation audit.  The phase-one run was evaluated against the NCBI genetic-code table readback, with the confirmatory dataset loaded from \texttt{tools/bio\_reality/data/phase2\_confirmatory\_falsification\_dataset.json}.  The preregistration timestamp was $2026$-$05$-$29$T$20$:$11$:$31$+00:$00$, and the snapshot timestamp was $2026$-$06$-$16$T$18$:$30$:$05$+00:$00$.  The finite target list represented exactly three registered codon-geometry claims: \texttt{h0.null.model.aa\_class\_preserving}, \texttt{h1.leave\_one\_code\_out.by\_family}, and \texttt{h1.leave\_one\_codon\_out.module\_classification}.  Both confirmatory binomial checks had $40$ successes in $40$ trials, rate $1.0$, and one-sided upper-tail probability $9.094947017729282\times 10^{-13}$.  The negative-control panel had $20$ trials and $0$ unexpected successes, and the critical-ablation panel likewise had $20$ trials and $0$ unexpected successes.  Thus the finite code-table contact is positive for the registered phase-one geometry claims, while remaining silent about translation, protein state, or biological mechanism.

\paragraph{Coordinate list and admissible readout.}
The downstream surface keeps the $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinates as a finite coordinate system rather than as biological observations.  The nine coordinates used in the powered audits are
$$
\begin{aligned}
Q = \{&\mathrm{K\_AAA},\ \mathrm{Arg\_AGR},\ \mathrm{Ile\_AUA},\ \mathrm{Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR},\ \mathrm{Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG},\\
&\mathrm{Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY},\ \mathrm{Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG},\ \mathrm{Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY},\ \mathrm{f3\_stress}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
A residual-to-protein association is admitted only through a translation readout $T$.  In the supplied finite rows, the broad survival surface used modeled tAI, computed from GtRNAdb tRNA gene-copy counts and dos Reis wobble weights.  The preliminary modeled-tAI contact covered $3$ organisms, all with $61$ sense codons and zero raw-weight fallback count $0$; it produced $81$ entries above the coupling threshold $0.01$ and maximum absolute modeled coupling $3801961.031607896$.  This is a bounded codon-adaptation readout, not a ribosome-profiling measurement.  The same record explicitly reports that $3$ organisms are insufficient for powered survival and that a powered survival request requires at least $40$ matched species.

\paragraph{Protein-abundance mediation matrix.}
The powered mediation audit joined real CDS codon counts, modeled per-protein tAI, matched PAXdb protein abundance, and control covariates across $12$ organisms.  The joined protein counts were: $4042$ for \emph{Bacillus subtilis} str. $168$, $11661$ for \emph{Caenorhabditis elegans}, $14410$ for \emph{Danio rerio}, $13295$ for \emph{Drosophila melanogaster}, $3739$ for \emph{Escherichia coli} K-$12$ MG$1655$, $12418$ for \emph{Gallus gallus}, $19053$ for \emph{Homo sapiens}, $19395$ for \emph{Mus musculus}, $4129$ for \emph{Mycobacterium smegmatis} mc$^2$ $155$, $13322$ for \emph{Rattus norvegicus}, $5815$ for \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}, and $1656$ for \emph{Sulfolobus solfataricus}.  Each organism had a rank-sufficient residualized design with $9$ residual coordinates, one modeled translation column, and $24$ control columns before the residual degrees of freedom count.  The control set included amino-acid composition, CDS length, GC3, and the $M$-density baseline, while matched mRNA abundance remains a control rather than a translation readout.

\paragraph{Mediation fractions.}
For each organism the finite matrix reports a total residual protein-abundance association $c=R^2_{QP}$, a direct association after conditioning on modeled translation $c'=R^2_{QP\mid T}$, and an indirect component $c-c'$.  The corresponding mediated fractions $M^{QTP}$ were $0.6084594553474505$ for \emph{B. subtilis}, $0.4872268357227724$ for \emph{C. elegans}, $0.3157053953614641$ for \emph{D. rerio}, $0.6165167748739756$ for \emph{D. melanogaster}, $0.612922252793667$ for \emph{E. coli}, $0.1976903964724546$ for \emph{G. gallus}, $0.27206289160119157$ for human, $0.15488799907323988$ for mouse, $0.08397602919861291$ for \emph{M. smegmatis}, $0.17489681798543982$ for rat, $0.9491551041216973$ for yeast, and $0.01850668564589326$ for \emph{S. solfataricus}.  All listed mediation fractions lie in the unit interval.  Yeast had the largest mediated fraction, with $c=0.29837624994140827$, $c'=0.0151709093608293$, and $c-c'=0.28320534058057895$.  Human had $c=0.04454565118019448$, $c'=0.03242643251185274$, and $c-c'=0.012119218668341744$.  The finite readback is therefore a controlled statistical matrix
$$
\begin{aligned}
q_i \longrightarrow T_{\mathrm{modeled\ tAI}} \longrightarrow P_{\mathrm{PAXdb\ abundance}},
\end{aligned}
$$
not a claim that a synonymously encoded coordinate has been causally realized in a protein.

These rows support a variance-partition statement in yeast, not a folding-pathway or causal-structure statement.

\paragraph{Bounded positive and negative checks.}
Several linked finite audits constrain the interpretation.  The structural-order axis audit found exactly one coordinate passing the fold-level sign-flip gate: \texttt{f3\_stress}, with weighted held-out $\Delta R^2=0.0031744502294756994$ and sign-flip probability $0.03125$.  The evolutionary selection-imprint audit over $80$ orthogroups, $240$ rows, and $3$ species reported $\Delta R^2=0.03250515015613468$, description-length change $-258.6010084301837$ bits, leave-clade-out $\Delta R^2=-0.0021477729853302424$, null-$95$ threshold $0.05427157066522958$, and a negative strong-imprint verdict.  The dynamic mass-balance audit used $120$ genes and $718$ intervals; its final increment was $\Delta R^2=-0.07584438093220836$, $\Delta DL=-668.8046070381928$ bits, $p_{\mathrm{TEbin}}=0.6831683168316832$, and $p_{\mathrm{timeshuffle}}=0.9306930693069307$.  The turnover audit used $3290$ genes and found $\Delta R^2=-0.005461979433706483$, $\Delta DL=-13.68819707902732$ bits, $p_{\mathrm{matchedbin}}=0.8855721393034826$, and $p_{\mathrm{recoded}}=1.0$.  A perturbation panel in \emph{E. coli} mRFP is positive at the protein-output layer, with $1459$ variants, $\Delta R^2=0.05339906007191191$, $\Delta DL=19.089327473282296$ bits, and matched and within null probabilities both $0.004975124378109453$; that result remains a separate perturbation readout and is not folded back into the phase-one code-table claim.

\paragraph{Local boundary.}
The chapter therefore has two separate conclusions.  First, the phase-one codon-geometry claims are confirmed on the locked genetic-code table surface with $40/40$ successes in each strong binomial check and no negative-control or critical-ablation failures.  Second, the $B^*_{Q6}$ protein-abundance surface contains a finite, controlled mediation matrix through modeled tAI and matched protein abundance, with all reported $M^{QTP}$ fractions inside $[0,1]$ across $12$ organisms.  Neither conclusion licenses a stronger biological assertion by itself.  Translation is not established by the code-table contact; observed ribosome dynamics are not established by modeled tAI; folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, mechanism, phenotype, selection, or universality do not follow from any residual coordinate, survival notation, protein-abundance association, or mediation fraction unless a separate layer-matched measurement supplies that contact and the declared controls are evaluated on the same finite rows.
