\subsection{Phase-one codon geometry and translation-conditioned protein readback}
\label{subsec:bioreality-phase-one-q6-translation-protein-readback}

\paragraph{Finite row surface.}
This record concerns a finite mediation surface whose rows are triples $(q_i,t_j,p_k)$.  The first coordinate $q_i$ ranges over the nine computed $B^*_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates,
$$
\begin{aligned}
Q=\{&K\_AAA, Arg\_AGR, Ile\_AUA, Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR, Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG,\\
&Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY, Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG, Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY, f3\_stress\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The second coordinate $t_j$ is a translation-layer readout, here including explicitly bounded modeled tAI weights and, where available, measured translation-efficiency routes.  The third coordinate $p_k$ is matched protein abundance.  The admissible readback is therefore not a direct $Q\to P$ assertion.  A protein-abundance association is allowed to enter this chapter only through a declared $Q\to T\to P$ calculation or through a separately reported direct residual association that remains marked as direct rather than mechanistic.

\paragraph{Locked phase-one contact.}
The phase-one confirmatory contact is the NCBI genetic-code table readback against locked codon-geometry claims.  The represented target claims are $h0.null.model.aa\_class\_preserving$, $h1.leave\_one\_code\_out.by\_family$, and $h1.leave\_one\_codon\_out.module\_classification$.  The independent replication statistic used $40$ trials with $40$ successes, giving rate $1.0$ and exact one-sided binomial upper-tail value $9.094947017729282\times 10^{-13}$.  The boundary-probe statistic has the same finite count and the same value,
$$
\begin{aligned}
 n&=40, & s&=40, & \widehat r&=1.0, & p&=9.094947017729282\times 10^{-13}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The negative-control panel had $20$ rows and $0$ unexpected successes, and the critical-ablation panel also had $20$ rows and $0$ unexpected successes.  These numbers certify a codon-table contact for the phase-one geometry.  They do not by themselves certify translation, protein structure, physical admissibility, function, mechanism, or a universal biological rule.

\paragraph{Modeled translation readout.}
The bounded translation contact uses modeled tAI weights computed from GtRNAdb tRNA gene-copy counts and dos Reis wobble weights.  In the finite modeled-tAI survival matrix, $3$ organisms were used: \emph{Escherichia coli} K12, \emph{Homo sapiens}, and \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}.  The codon-count space has $61$ sense codons.  The bounded coupling threshold is $0.01$, and $81$ entries exceed this threshold.  The maximum absolute matrix entry is $3801961.031607896$, equal to the maximum absolute diagonal entry.  The local tRNA payload contains all $20$ standard amino-acid families for each of the three organisms; the FASTA header counts are $89$ for \emph{E. coli} K12, $432$ for human, and $275$ for yeast, with matched counts $87$, $432$, and $275$ respectively.  Zero raw tAI fallback counts are $0$ in all three modeled organism readouts.  Because the organism count is only $3$, this is a bounded modeled-readout contact, not a powered measured-ribosome or proteomics validation.

\paragraph{Local interpretation.}
The chapter therefore supports the following finite statement.  Phase-one codon geometry has a strong genetic-code-table contact; the $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinates have a bounded modeled-tAI translation readout; and a $12$-organism protein-abundance matrix reports positive statistical $Q\to T\to P$ mediated fractions under amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, CDS length, and $M$-density controls.  In yeast, an additional structure proxy explains only a small part of the abundance association, while measured translation efficiency plus mRNA stability absorb a much larger share of the direct residual association.

\paragraph{No-promotion boundary.}
The finite calculations above do not authorize stronger biological conclusions.  Modeled tAI is not observed ribosome occupancy.  A positive mediated fraction is not a causal translation mechanism.  A protein-abundance association is not protein folding, molecular function, physical admissibility, phenotype, selection, or universality.  Those layers require their own matched contact surfaces and their own controlled statistics.  Until such contacts are attached, this chapter stops at codon-table confirmation, bounded translation-readout coupling, statistical translation-conditioned protein-abundance mediation, and the explicitly stated residual decompositions.
