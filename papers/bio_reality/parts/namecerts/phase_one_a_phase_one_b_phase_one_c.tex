\subsection{Locked genetic-code contact and the translation-mediated protein boundary}

\paragraph{Finite surface.}
The record considered here is the finite table surface for the claim id \texttt{phase.one.a+phase.one.b+phase.one.c}, read together with the later $B^*_{Q6}$ translation and protein-abundance rows that test how far the same codon-coordinate memory may be carried.  The phase-one table itself is not a protein experiment.  Its empirical contact is the NCBI genetic-code table readback against locked codon-geometry claims, with the scope explicitly limited to confirmatory falsification on a genetic-code table.  The represented target list contains three finite tests: \texttt{h0.null.model.aa\_class\_preserving}, \texttt{h1.leave\_one\_code\_out.by\_family}, and \texttt{h1.leave\_one\_codon\_out.module\_classification}.  The preregistration timestamp is $2026$-$05$-$29\,20{:}11{:}31+00{:}00$, the snapshot timestamp is $2026$-$06$-$16\,18{:}30{:}05+00{:}00$, and the last verified timestamp is $2026$-$06$-$20\,20{:}35{:}45+00{:}00$.

\paragraph{Primary finite counts.}
The phase-one confirmation has two exact one-sided binomial contacts and two zero-tolerance absence controls.  The independent-replication contact has $40$ successes in $40$ trials, success rate $1.0$, and upper-tail probability $9.094947017729282\times 10^{-13}$.  The boundary-probe contact has the same count, rate, and probability.  The negative-control contact has $20$ rows and $0$ unexpected successes, and the critical-ablation contact also has $20$ rows and $0$ unexpected successes.  In compact notation, the finite evidence table is
$$
\begin{aligned}
R_{\mathrm{rep}} &= 40/40, & p_{\mathrm{rep}} &= 9.094947017729282\times 10^{-13},\\
R_{\mathrm{boundary}} &= 40/40, & p_{\mathrm{boundary}} &= 9.094947017729282\times 10^{-13},\\
M_{\mathrm{neg}} &= 0/20, & M_{\mathrm{abl}} &= 0/20.
\end{aligned}
$$
Here $R_{\mathrm{rep}}$ and $R_{\mathrm{boundary}}$ are finite hit-rate rows, not rate constants for translation.  The $M$ rows are absence checks, not molecular mechanism parameters.  No lambda-valued kinetic row is present in the supplied phase-one table.

\paragraph{Readback rule.}
The admissible readback is narrow.  The phase-one result says that the registered codon-geometry claims survived the curated genetic-code table challenge under the stated finite tests.  It does not say that the codon coordinates are translation measurements, ribosome occupancy measurements, protein-abundance measurements, folding measurements, physical-admissibility measurements, functional assays, mechanism assays, phenotype assays, or universal biological laws.  The local rule is that a coordinate $q_i$ may enter a protein-abundance discussion only after a separate translation readout $T_j$ has been attached and survived the declared control surface.  A direct association $q_i\to P_k$ is therefore insufficient for protein realization unless the mediated chain has a non-null translation step:
$$
\begin{aligned}
q_i &\longrightarrow T_j \longrightarrow P_k,\\
T_j &\in \{\mathrm{tAI},\mathrm{stAI},\mathrm{ribosome\ occupancy},\mathrm{translation\ efficiency}\},\\
P_k &= \mathrm{matched\ protein\ abundance}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The controls that remain controls, rather than promoted coordinates, are amino-acid composition, organism, $GC3$, coding-sequence length, the $M$-density baseline, and matched mRNA abundance.

\paragraph{Modeled translation contact.}
The modeled-tAI survival contact supplies a bounded translation-layer readout, not a measured ribosome experiment.  It computed $9$ residual coordinates, namely \texttt{Arg\_AGR}, \texttt{Ile\_AUA}, \texttt{K\_AAA}, \texttt{Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR}, \texttt{Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG}, \texttt{Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG}, \texttt{Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY}, \texttt{Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY}, and \texttt{f3\_stress}.  The modeled surface used $61$ sense codons and $3$ organisms.  The finite bounded-coupling matrix had $81$ entries above the threshold $0.01$, with maximum absolute entry $3801961.031607896$.  The organism count remains too small for a powered multi-species survival claim: $3$ organisms are not the same thing as $40$ or more matched species.  Thus the modeled-tAI contact is positive as a bounded coupling surface while remaining underpowered as a general translation-survival result.

\paragraph{Perturbation contacts.}
The perturbation rows show that the boundary is not uniformly null once suitable experimental surfaces exist.  The \textit{E. coli} mRFP synonymous protein-output table used $1459$ variants and reported $\Delta R^2=0.05339906007191191$, $\Delta\mathrm{DL}=19.089327473282296$ bits, $p_{\mathrm{matched}}=0.004975124378109453$, and $p_{\mathrm{within}}=0.004975124378109453$, with real CAI not absorbing the effect.  The associated $5'$ boundary audit on the same variant count did not show stable downstream support: downstream $\Delta R^2=-0.001126409133973234$, downstream $\Delta\mathrm{LL}=-2.082967365015851$, block probability $0.1691542288557214$, and sign stability false.  The yeast synonymous-mutant row used $1783$ synonymous mutants across $20$ genes, with $21$ coding-sequence map hits and real CSC not absorbing the effect; the real-versus-proxy CSC correlation change was $0.39717918221186976$.

\paragraph{Local verdict.}
The phase-one finite table is ready as a codon-geometry confirmation: $40/40$ and $40/40$ positive rows, both with exact one-sided probability $9.094947017729282\times 10^{-13}$, plus two $0/20$ absence controls.  Its permissible biological interpretation is narrower than the later translation and protein-abundance rows.  The later $B^*_{Q6}$ records show that controlled translation and protein-abundance mediation can be computed on finite matched rows, and in some organisms the mediated statistical fraction is large.  The chapter therefore records a guarded positive status: the genetic-code table contact is confirmed at its own layer, the $Q\to T\to P$ mediation matrix is available as a bounded statistical surface, and every stronger claim remains blocked unless a layer-matched contact supplies it.  In particular, translation, ribosome dynamics, protein folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, mechanism, phenotype, selection, and universality do not follow from the phase-one codon table or from a direct $Q\to P$ association alone.
