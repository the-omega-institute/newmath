\subsection{Phase-one genetic-code contact and the translation boundary}
\label{subsec:phase-one-genetic-code-contact-translation-boundary}

\paragraph{Finite contact surface.}
The finite record for the phase-one packet is a locked comparison between codon-window geometry claims and the curated NCBI genetic-code table \texttt{gc.prt}.  Its declared scope is the confirmatory falsification surface only: the contact checks whether the previously specified codon-geometry claims meet a fresh table readback, and it does not enlarge the biological layer of the claim.  The represented target classes are \texttt{h0.null.model.aa\_class\_preserving}, \texttt{h1.leave\_one\_code\_out.by\_family}, and \texttt{h1.leave\_one\_codon\_out.module\_classification}.  The preregistration timestamp is \texttt{2026-05-29T20:11:31+00:00}, the snapshot timestamp is \texttt{2026-06-16T18:30:05+00:00}, and the recorded verification timestamp is \texttt{2026-06-27T19:56:12+00:00}.  The table contact is therefore finite in the operative sense used here: each row is a codon-table comparison row, each pass or failure is evaluated against a fixed table source, and the readback is bounded to genetic-code table geometry rather than translation output.

\paragraph{Phase-one readback.}
The independent replication and boundary-probe components both report exact one-sided binomial evidence with $40$ successes in $40$ trials.  In both cases the upper-tail probability is
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{\mathrm{binom}} &= 9.094947017729282 \times 10^{-13},\\
n &= 40,\\
s &= 40,\\
s/n &= 1.0.
\end{aligned}
$$
The negative-control surface reports $0$ unexpected successes in $20$ trials, and the critical-ablation surface also reports $0$ unexpected successes in $20$ trials.  Thus the finite phase-one readback is not a soft qualitative impression: it consists of two $40/40$ success surfaces with the same exact binomial scalar, together with two $0/20$ absence surfaces.  The accompanying confirmatory dataset is \texttt{tools/bio\_reality/data/phase2\_confirmatory\_falsification\_dataset.json}, and the recorded source is the NCBI genetic-code table read against the locked phase-one codon-geometry claims.

\paragraph{What the phase-one result licenses.}
The licensed conclusion is that the codon-geometry packet survives the finite genetic-code table contact.  More explicitly, the supported statement is a table-level codon and amino-acid readback: the three represented target classes remain aligned with the curated genetic-code surface under the stronger statistic using doubled minimum independent trial counts, zero-tolerance negative controls, and zero-tolerance ablation controls.  This is a strong statement about finite code-table contact, but it is intentionally narrow.  It says that the codon-window geometry is not merely an unconstrained formal artifact at the code-table layer; it does not say that the geometry has already been observed as ribosome motion, protein concentration, folded structure, physical feasibility, molecular function, organismal phenotype, or a universal rule.

\paragraph{Local cannot-claim boundary.}
The chapter licenses two statements and blocks several stronger ones.  First, the phase-one genetic-code contact is strongly supported at the finite table layer by two $40/40$ success surfaces and two $0/20$ control-ablation absence surfaces.  Second, the later $B^*_{Q6}$ matrices provide bounded statistical readbacks into modeled tAI, protein abundance, and selected residual decompositions, including a positive modeled mediation surface across $12$ organisms.  Nothing in these rows permits a direct claim of translation realization from codon geometry alone.  No folding claim follows without a separate structure-level contact; no physical-admissibility claim follows without a separate physical or stability assay; no molecular-function, phenotype, causal mechanism, adaptation, or universal biological law follows from the genetic-code table contact, the modeled tAI contact, or the protein-abundance association alone.  A residual-to-protein edge is admissible only as a controlled statistical association unless the residual first survives the declared translation-readout step under amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, coding-sequence length, $M$-density, matched mRNA abundance, and simpler-baseline controls.
