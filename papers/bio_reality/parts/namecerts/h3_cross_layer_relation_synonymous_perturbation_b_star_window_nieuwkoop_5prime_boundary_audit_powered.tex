\subsection{Nieuwkoop five-prime boundary audit for B-star window residuals}
\label{sec:bior-h3-nieuwkoop-fiveprime-boundary-audit}

\paragraph{Finite row.}
The Nieuwkoop mRFP perturbation table gives a direct finite-row test of whether the B-star window residual carries protein-output information specifically through the start-proximal boundary, or whether the recoverable signal is better described by broader design and downstream-coding covariates. The audited row contains $1459$ synonymous variants and was evaluated with $B=200$ block-null resamples. The registered three-step fit separates a first control surface, a strong design-control upgrade, and a final B-star downstream increment. The observed values are
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{\mathrm{step\ 1}} &= 0.06748192482378434,\\
R^2_{\mathrm{step\ 2}} &= 0.6060702157093043,\\
R^2_{\mathrm{step\ 3}} &= 0.6049438075310105.
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the second surface dominates the explanatory gain, with $\Delta\ell_{2-1}=628.6217154500001$, while the final B-star downstream term does not improve the held model: $\Delta R^2_{3-2}=-0.001126409133973234$ and $\Delta\ell_{3-2}=-2.082965600280344$. The directly registered downstream increment agrees with this negative direction, $\Delta\ell_{B,\mathrm{downstream}}=-2.082967365015851$, and the stricter downstream window after position $16$ is also negative, $\Delta\ell_{B,\mathrm{downstream},16+}=-2.1989730953428164$.

\paragraph{Boundary statistic.}
The start-proximal audit does not produce a usable positive B-star boundary effect. The start term is numerically negligible, with
$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta R^2_{B,\mathrm{start}} &= 1.7624020537495221\times 10^{-9},\\
\Delta\ell_{B,\mathrm{start}} &= 3.26370933437133\times 10^{-6}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The downstream block test gives $p_{\mathrm{block}}=0.1691542288557214$ against a null-$95$ reference value of $0.5254770343527547$, and the sign-stability flag is false. In this row the finite contact therefore records a refusal of start-boundary promotion: the data support a strong non-B-star design transition from step $1$ to step $2$, but they do not support a stable B-star residual contribution at the five-prime boundary after that transition is present.

\paragraph{Relation to the broader Nieuwkoop mRFP protein-output row.}
The boundary audit is not a contradiction of the broader Nieuwkoop mRFP protein-output contact. In the companion finite row on the same $1459$ variants, the full B-star perpendicular residual improves the control model from $R^2=0.12648386762689323$ to $R^2=0.1798829276988051$, with $\Delta R^2=0.05339906007191191$, $\Delta\mathrm{DL}=19.089327473282296$ bits, and matched and within-null probabilities both equal to $0.004975124378109453$. That broader row is robust to real CAI control, with real-CAI absorption false and real-CAI robustness true. The five-prime boundary audit asks a sharper question: whether the recoverable synonymous perturbation signal localizes to the start-proximal or downstream boundary after the stronger design layer has already been admitted. Its answer is negative for this boundary-specific claim.

\paragraph{Admissible readback.}
The finite object read back from this audit is a bounded perturbation matrix over synonymous variants, control surfaces, and protein-output measurements. Its admissible conclusion is that the Nieuwkoop five-prime boundary does not by itself carry a stable positive B-star window increment in the audited protein-output fit. The large step-$2$ gain remains a design-level and control-level fact, while the B-star boundary terms are either nearly zero or negative once the stronger surface is present. This keeps the row in a boundary-audit role: it refines where the mRFP perturbation signal may be located, but it does not license a start-boundary mechanism.

\paragraph{Local no-promotion rule.}
No translation-realization claim follows from this row alone. The assay observes protein-output variation under synonymous perturbation, not ribosome occupancy, codon-by-codon elongation, folding dynamics, physical admissibility, biochemical function, organismal phenotype, or a universal rule. A direct $Q_i\to P_k$ association is not enough to promote a residual coordinate to translation mediation unless a separate translation readout supplies the intervening $Q_i\to T_j$ step. Likewise, no folding, molecular mechanism, function, selection, or cross-organism law is inferred from the five-prime boundary audit. Those layers require their own matched contacts and their own controlled finite statistics.
