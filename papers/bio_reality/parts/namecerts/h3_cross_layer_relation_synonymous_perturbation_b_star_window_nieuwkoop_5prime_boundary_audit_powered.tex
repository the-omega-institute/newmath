\subsection{Nieuwkoop five-prime boundary audit for the $B^{\ast}$ window}
\label{sec:bioreality-nieuwkoop-five-prime-boundary-audit}

\paragraph{Finite row surface.}
This record tests whether the $B^{\ast}$ synonymous-window coordinate is still carrying protein-output information after the known five-prime boundary structure of the Nieuwkoop \emph{E. coli} mRFP variant panel is made explicit.  The finite surface has $1459$ synonymous variants and a deterministic block-null audit with $B=200$ resamples.  The measured response is not treated as a general biological layer; it is used only as the bounded protein-output readout present in this perturbation panel.  The row set is therefore a three-stage comparison: a first model with ordinary controls, a second model that admits the dominant five-prime boundary term, and a third model that asks whether the downstream $B^{\ast}$ window still improves fit after that boundary has been accounted for.

\paragraph{Nested audit statistic.}
Let $Y$ denote the finite protein-output readout on the $1459$ rows, let $C$ denote the non-window controls, let $U$ denote the five-prime boundary coordinate, and let $B_d$ denote the downstream $B^{\ast}$ window coordinate after the boundary.  The recorded audit values are
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2(Y\sim C) &= 0.06748192482378434,\\
R^2(Y\sim C+U) &= 0.6060702157093043,\\
R^2(Y\sim C+U+B_d) &= 0.6049438075310105.
\end{aligned}
$$
The boundary term is therefore the large finite-row jump in this panel, with
$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta \ell(C+U:C) &= 628.6217154500001,\\
\Delta \ell(C+U+B_d:C+U) &= -2.082965600280344,\\
\Delta R^2(C+U+B_d:C+U) &= -0.001126409133973234.
\end{aligned}
$$
The start-window component itself is effectively null on the held audit scale, with $\Delta \ell=3.26370933437133\times 10^{-6}$ and $\Delta R^2=1.7624020537495221\times 10^{-9}$.  The downstream component is also not a stable positive increment: the downstream log-likelihood change is $-2.082967365015851$, the stricter downstream $16+$ statistic is $-2.1989730953428164$, the block-null $95\%$ reference is $0.5254770343527547$, and the block-null probability is $p=0.1691542288557214$.  The sign-stability flag is false.

\paragraph{Interpretation of the boundary.}
The same Nieuwkoop mRFP panel has a separate powered protein-output result in which the broader $B^{\ast}$ window signal improves held-out fit before this boundary dissection: $R^2$ moves from $0.12648386762689323$ to $0.1798829276988051$, with $\Delta R^2=0.05339906007191191$, $\Delta\mathrm{DL}=19.089327473282296$ bits, and both matched and within-panel probabilities equal to $0.004975124378109453$.  The five-prime audit does not erase that row-level contact; it localizes its admissible reading.  Once the dominant first-region boundary is made part of the finite model, the downstream $B^{\ast}$ increment is nonpositive and not sign-stable.  Thus the panel supports a bounded perturbation readout at the protein-output surface, but it does not support promoting the downstream synonymous residual into an independent translation-mediated or mechanism-level claim.

\paragraph{Relation to the broader mediated protein-abundance matrix.}
The larger cross-organism mediation matrix gives context for why this audit is a boundary test rather than a universal law.  In the $12$-organism protein-abundance decomposition, the joint $9$-coordinate $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ surface has positive statistical mediation fractions in all listed organisms, for example $M^{QTP}=0.612922252793667$ in \emph{E. coli} K12 MG1655, $0.9491551041216973$ in \emph{S. cerevisiae}, $0.27206289160119157$ in human, and $0.01850668564589326$ in \emph{Sulfolobus solfataricus}.  Those entries are finite statistical decompositions using modeled per-protein tAI and matched protein abundance; they do not convert the Nieuwkoop downstream window into a separate mechanism.  The local audit instead says that this particular perturbation panel is dominated by a five-prime boundary term, and that the downstream $B^{\ast}$ window has no positive finite-row increment after the boundary is admitted.

\paragraph{Allowed claim.}
The allowed statement is therefore narrow.  On the $1459$-variant Nieuwkoop mRFP surface, the five-prime boundary accounts for the main protein-output improvement, while the downstream $B^{\ast}$ window fails the powered boundary audit after the boundary term is included.  The result is a locality constraint on the synonymous perturbation signal: the broad protein-output contact remains real for the panel, but its downstream-window reading is bounded by the five-prime audit.

\paragraph{Non-promotion boundary.}
No translation realization, ribosome dynamics, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, causal mechanism, organismal phenotype, selection law, or universality follows from this audit.  A direct $Q_i\to P_k$ association is not enough for a translation-mediated claim unless the same coordinate first survives a controlled $Q_i\to T_j$ readout and then shows the declared downstream attenuation or mediation on matched rows.  Here the recorded finite numbers instead mark a boundary: the downstream window is not a stable positive addition once the five-prime structure is represented.
