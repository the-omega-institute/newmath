\subsection{Nieuwkoop five-prime boundary audit for the $B^{\ast}$ window}
\label{sec:bioreality-nieuwkoop-fiveprime-boundary-audit}

\paragraph{Finite surface.}
This record audits whether the $B^{\ast}$ synonymous-window signal in the Nieuwkoop bacterial reporter panel is concentrated at the five-prime boundary or persists as a downstream coding-sequence signal after the boundary has been isolated.  The finite table has $1459$ synonymous variants.  Each row carries the measured reporter output, local synonymous recoding information, the five-prime start-window coordinate, and the downstream $B^{\ast}$ window coordinate.  The intended readout is not a direct declaration that a codon residual has become a protein mechanism.  It is a bounded comparison of nested statistical surfaces: a first surface with ordinary controls, a second surface that accounts for the five-prime boundary term, and a third surface that asks whether the downstream $B^{\ast}$ window still explains output after that boundary term is already present.

\paragraph{Nested readout.}
The three reported fits have
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_1 &= 0.06748192482378434,\\
R^2_2 &= 0.6060702157093043,\\
R^2_3 &= 0.6049438075310105.
\end{aligned}
$$
The move from the first to the second surface is large on the reported likelihood scale, with $\Delta \ell_{2,1}=628.6217154500001$.  This is the empirical signature that the five-prime boundary term is carrying the dominant detectable response in this finite panel.  By contrast, after the boundary term is included, the downstream $B^{\ast}$ coordinate does not improve the fit: the reported downstream change is
$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta R^2_{3,2} &= -0.001126409133973234,\\
\Delta \ell_{3,2} &= -2.082965600280344.
\end{aligned}
$$
The directly reported downstream audit gives the same negative direction, $\Delta \ell=-2.082967365015851$, with block-null $p=0.1691542288557214$ and null $95\%$ reference value $0.5254770343527547$.  When the downstream window is restricted to the $16+$ region, the likelihood change remains negative, $\Delta \ell=-2.1989730953428164$.

\paragraph{Boundary verdict.}
The finite-row conclusion is therefore asymmetric.  The five-prime start coordinate is effectively neutral on its own reported coefficient scale, with $\Delta R^2=1.7624020537495221\times 10^{-9}$ and $\Delta \ell=3.26370933437133\times 10^{-6}$ for the start term as a separate small audit quantity, but the nested model comparison shows that the major output separation is already captured when the boundary layer is admitted.  Once that boundary layer is present, the downstream $B^{\ast}$ window has no positive residual evidence in this panel.  The sign-stability flag is false, so the result is not a stable downstream-direction claim.  The honest readback is a boundary-localized report: the Nieuwkoop five-prime panel supports a strong start-region statistical partition, while the post-boundary $B^{\ast}$ residue is not promoted to a downstream protein-output explanation.

\paragraph{Relation to the reporter output audit.}
The same Nieuwkoop mRFP variant count, $1459$, appears in the protein-output audit for the broader reporter panel.  There the controls-only surface has $R^2=0.12648386762689323$, the controls-plus-$B^{\ast}_{\perp}$ surface has $R^2=0.1798829276988051$, and the reported gain is $\Delta R^2=0.05339906007191191$ with $\Delta {\rm DL}=19.089327473282296$ bits.  The matched and within-null probabilities are both $0.004975124378109453$, and the real-CAI robustness flag is true with real-CAI versus proxy correlation $0.17884920449487354$.  The five-prime boundary audit does not contradict that bounded reporter-output contact; it localizes the support.  The broad reporter output statistic can be positive while the post-boundary downstream window is negative after the five-prime term is accounted for.

\paragraph{Admissible interpretation.}
This chapter records a finite empirical boundary, not a molecular pathway.  A direct $Q_i\to P_k$ association is not enough to assert protein realization unless a same-row translation readout $T_j$ is observed or explicitly bounded and the mediated surface $Q_i\to T_j\to P_k$ is evaluated under matched controls.  Here the available row data show a five-prime reporter boundary and a nonpositive downstream residual after that boundary.  No translation realization, ribosome dynamics, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, mechanism, organismal phenotype, selection law, or universal biological rule follows from this audit.  Any such assertion would require a separate layer-matched contact, such as measured translation efficiency, ribosome occupancy, matched protein abundance with mRNA control, structural readout, perturbation rescue, or functional assay rows, joined to the same coordinate surface and tested under amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, coding-sequence length, M-density baseline, and matched mRNA controls.

\paragraph{Status.}
The finite verdict is closed for this dataset: $n=1459$, $B=200$ block-null draws, $R^2_1=0.06748192482378434$, $R^2_2=0.6060702157093043$, $R^2_3=0.6049438075310105$, downstream $\Delta R^2=-0.001126409133973234$, downstream $\Delta \ell=-2.082967365015851$, block-null $p=0.1691542288557214$, and sign stability is false.  The appropriate BioReality claim is a five-prime boundary audit with downstream refusal, not a downstream synonymous-window mechanism.
