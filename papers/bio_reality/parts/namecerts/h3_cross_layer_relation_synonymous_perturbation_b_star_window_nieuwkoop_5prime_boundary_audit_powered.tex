\subsection{Nieuwkoop five-prime boundary audit for the synonymous-window signal}
\label{sec:bioreality-nieuwkoop-5prime-boundary-audit}

\paragraph{Finite row surface.}
This record audits whether the Nieuwkoop \textit{E. coli} mRFP synonymous-variant signal is better read as a generic downstream synonymous-window effect or as a sharply localized five-prime boundary effect.  The finite surface has $1459$ variants.  Each row carries a protein-output readout together with the controlled synonymous-window coordinates used in the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ audit.  The relevant comparison is not a free residual-to-protein promotion: the audit first asks whether the explanatory mass sits at the start-proximal coding boundary, and then asks whether downstream $B^{\ast}$ coordinates add stable information after that boundary has already been admitted.

\paragraph{Nested audit.}
The fitted sequence of finite regressions has three reported stages.  The initial control stage explains
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{1} &= 0.06748192482378434 .
\end{aligned}
$$
Adding the five-prime boundary block gives the dominant gain,
$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta \ell_{2\mid 1} &= 628.6217154500001,\\
R^2_{2} &= 0.6060702157093043 .
\end{aligned}
$$
After that boundary block is present, adding the downstream $B^{\ast}$ block decreases the held fit rather than improving it:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta \ell_{3\mid 2} &= -2.082965600280344,\\
R^2_{3} &= 0.6049438075310105,\\
\Delta R^2_{\mathrm{downstream}} &= -0.001126409133973234 .
\end{aligned}
$$
The corresponding downstream log-likelihood audit is also negative, with $\Delta \ell_{\mathrm{downstream}}=-2.082967365015851$ and the stricter downstream-$16+$ check giving $-2.1989730953428164$.  The block randomization reference places the downstream statistic below its positive threshold: the reported null-$95$ cutoff is $0.5254770343527547$, while the block-tail value is $p=0.1691542288557214$.  The sign-stability flag is false.

\paragraph{Start-boundary diagnosis.}
The start-local $B^{\ast}$ increment is numerically negligible once the five-prime boundary design is separated from the wider synonymous-window block:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta \ell_{\mathrm{start}} &= 3.26370933437133\times 10^{-6},\\
\Delta R^2_{\mathrm{start}} &= 1.7624020537495221\times 10^{-9} .
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the audit does not support a claim that the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ coordinate itself provides an independent start-window perturbation law in this finite table.  It supports a narrower statement: in this mRFP variant panel, the five-prime boundary term carries the large detectable protein-output association, and the residual downstream $B^{\ast}$ term fails the positive-improvement and sign-stability checks after that boundary is controlled.

\paragraph{Relation to the mRFP protein-output run.}
The negative downstream-boundary audit should be read beside the paired Nieuwkoop mRFP protein-output run, not as a denial that the panel contains a protein-output signal.  In the paired run on the same $1459$ variants, the control-only model has $R^2=0.12648386762689323$, the full controlled-plus-$B_{\perp}$ model has $R^2=0.1798829276988051$, and the reported increment is
$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta R^2 &= 0.05339906007191191,\\
\Delta \mathrm{DL}_{\mathrm{bits}} &= 19.089327473282296,\\
p_{\mathrm{matched}} &= p_{\mathrm{within}} = 0.004975124378109453 .
\end{aligned}
$$
That paired result says that the mRFP synonymous panel contains a reproducible protein-output association under its own controlled design.  The present boundary audit says that, once the five-prime boundary is explicitly modeled, the downstream $B^{\ast}$ residue is not the stable explanatory component.  The two statements are compatible: one locates a protein-output association in the panel; the other prevents that association from being over-read as a downstream-window or general $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ propagation law.

\paragraph{Allowed readback.}
The finite readback is therefore a boundary-localization verdict.  The admissible statement is that the Nieuwkoop mRFP data favor a five-prime boundary account over an independent downstream synonymous-window account for this particular $1459$-variant surface.  The observed finite numbers are strong for the boundary step, null or negative for downstream $B^{\ast}$ after that step, and not sign-stable for a downstream promotion.  The coordinate remains useful as an audit coordinate, but the current rows do not authorize a downstream mechanistic interpretation.

\paragraph{Local non-promotion rule.}
No measured translation process, folding state, physical admissibility condition, molecular function, cellular mechanism, organismal phenotype, selection law, or universal biological rule follows from this audit alone.  A direct $Q_i\to P_k$ association is not enough to promote a protein-realization claim unless a separate layer-matched contact supplies the intervening translation readout $Q_i\to T_j$ and the controlled mediation chain $Q_i\to T_j\to P_k$ survives the declared covariates.  Here the finite audit is narrower: it separates a start-proximal boundary effect from a downstream synonymous-window residual and records that the downstream residual does not pass the positive boundary test.
