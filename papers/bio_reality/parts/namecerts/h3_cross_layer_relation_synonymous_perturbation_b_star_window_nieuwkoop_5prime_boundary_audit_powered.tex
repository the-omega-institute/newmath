\subsection{Nieuwkoop mRFP five-prime boundary audit for the B-star window}
\label{sec:bioreality-nieuwkoop-five-prime-boundary-audit}

\paragraph{Finite surface.}
This record tests whether the synonymous B-star window signal in the Nieuwkoop \emph{E. coli} mRFP perturbation panel survives after the early coding-region boundary is made explicit.  The finite surface has $1459$ synonymous variants.  Each row carries a protein-output readout for the mRFP construct, a start-proximal summary, and a downstream B-star window summary.  The admissible question is not whether a synonymous edit can change fluorescence or protein output in general, but whether the downstream B-star coordinates retain predictive information once the five-prime boundary has already absorbed the dominant variation.

\paragraph{Nested audit statistic.}
Let $Y$ denote the measured protein-output readout, $C$ the baseline construct and mutation controls, $F$ the start-proximal five-prime block, and $B_{\mathrm{down}}$ the downstream B-star window block.  The audit uses the nested sequence
$$
\begin{aligned}
L_1 &: Y \sim C,\\
L_2 &: Y \sim C + F,\\
L_3 &: Y \sim C + F + B_{\mathrm{down}}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The finite run gives
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2(L_1) &= 0.06748192482378434,\\
R^2(L_2) &= 0.6060702157093043,\\
R^2(L_3) &= 0.6049438075310105,\\
\Delta \ell(L_2,L_1) &= 628.6217154500001,\\
\Delta \ell(L_3,L_2) &= -2.082965600280344.
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the start-proximal block accounts for a large improvement over baseline, while adding the downstream B-star block after that boundary slightly worsens the fitted score.  The direct downstream summary agrees with this reading: $\Delta R^2(B_{\mathrm{down}})=-0.001126409133973234$ and $\Delta \ell(B_{\mathrm{down}})=-2.082967365015851$.

\paragraph{Boundary verdict.}
The block-null calibration reports a downstream null $95\%$ reference value of $0.5254770343527547$ and a block probability $p=0.1691542288557214$.  The downstream sixteen-plus-codon audit is also negative, with $\Delta \ell=-2.1989730953428164$.  The start-specific B-star increment is numerically negligible, with $\Delta R^2=1.7624020537495221\times 10^{-9}$ and $\Delta \ell=3.26370933437133\times 10^{-6}$.  The sign-stability flag is false.  These finite quantities force a local reading: the Nieuwkoop mRFP panel contains a strong five-prime boundary component, but this audit does not support promotion of the downstream B-star window to an independent protein-output mediator once that boundary is controlled.

\paragraph{Relation to the broader mRFP protein-output run.}
A companion Nieuwkoop mRFP protein-output run on the same $1459$-variant scale reported a positive broad-window signal before this boundary separation: $R^2$ increased from $0.12648386762689323$ under the level-two controls to $0.1798829276988051$ after adding the B-perpendicular block, giving $\Delta R^2=0.05339906007191191$, $\Delta\mathrm{DL}=19.089327473282296$ bits, and matched and within-panel probabilities both equal to $0.004975124378109453$.  The real-CAI robustness flag was true, with real-CAI versus proxy correlation $0.17884920449487354$.  The present audit does not erase that earlier bounded protein-output association; it refines its location.  Once the five-prime boundary is represented explicitly, the downstream B-star window no longer earns an independent positive increment in this finite panel.

\paragraph{Allowed readback.}
The permitted conclusion is a boundary-local one.  The finite data support a statement of the form: in the Nieuwkoop \emph{E. coli} mRFP synonymous-variant panel, protein-output variation is strongly absorbed by a five-prime/start-proximal component, and the downstream B-star window fails the post-boundary increment test.  The row set therefore remains useful as a controlled perturbation surface, but the downstream coordinates must be read as bounded residual features rather than as a demonstrated translation-mediated protein-realization route.

\paragraph{No higher-layer promotion.}
No claim about translation dynamics, ribosome occupancy, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, mechanism, selection, phenotype, or universality follows from this audit alone.  A direct $Q_i \to P_k$ association is not enough for protein realization unless the same coordinate first survives a controlled translation-readout step $Q_i \to T_j$ and the downstream protein readout is evaluated as $Q_i \to T_j \to P_k$ under matched mRNA abundance, amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, coding-sequence length, and M-density controls.  Here the measured result is the opposite of a downstream promotion: after the five-prime boundary enters the model, the downstream B-star increment is negative and sign stability is absent.
