\subsection{Nieuwkoop Five-Prime Boundary Audit for a Synonymous Protein-Output Perturbation}
\label{sec:bior reality-nieuwkoop-five-prime-boundary-audit}

\paragraph{Finite row surface.}
This record audits whether the Nieuwkoop synonymous-variant signal should be localized to a five-prime start-window effect or allowed to propagate as a downstream $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual protein-output claim. The finite table contains $1459$ synonymous variants and evaluates a three-step regression chain. Step 1 contains the baseline controls and has $R^2=0.06748192482378434$. Step 2 introduces the strong ordinary sequence and design covariates and raises the fit to $R^2=0.6060702157093043$, with a log-likelihood gain of $628.6217154500001$ against Step 1. Step 3 then tests the downstream $B^{*}$ residual after the Step 2 controls; the fit falls slightly to $R^2=0.6049438075310105$, and the downstream increment is negative.

$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{\mathrm{step1}} &= 0.06748192482378434, \\
R^2_{\mathrm{step2}} &= 0.6060702157093043, \\
\Delta \ell_{2-1} &= 628.6217154500001, \\
R^2_{\mathrm{step3}} &= 0.6049438075310105, \\
\Delta R^2_{B,\mathrm{downstream}} &= -0.001126409133973234, \\
\Delta \ell_{B,\mathrm{downstream}} &= -2.082967365015851.
\end{aligned}
$$

The finite-row conclusion is therefore not a positive downstream residual mediation call. The start-window residual is also numerically absent at the fitted scale: $\Delta R^2_{B,\mathrm{start}}=1.7624020537495221\times 10^{-9}$ with $\Delta \ell_{B,\mathrm{start}}=3.26370933437133\times 10^{-6}$. The downstream block test gives $p=0.1691542288557214$, while the block null $95\%$ reference is $0.5254770343527547$; the sign-stability flag is false. These finite quantities place the specific five-prime boundary packet below the promotion threshold.

\paragraph{Boundary verdict.}
The audit separates a strong ordinary predictor layer from the residual coordinate layer. The ordinary Step 2 model carries almost all of the observed improvement over the baseline, while the $B^{*}$ downstream residual has negative incremental evidence once that layer is present. In the more restrictive downstream-$16+$ check the log-likelihood increment is also negative, $\Delta \ell=-2.1989730953428164$. Thus the packet is read as a controlled refusal of a five-prime boundary residual signal, not as a positive synonymous protein-output mechanism.

This verdict is narrower than the separate Nieuwkoop mRFP protein-output fit on the same variant count. In that companion finite table, $n=1459$ variants gave $R^2=0.12648386762689323$ for the controlled baseline and $R^2=0.1798829276988051$ after adding the $B_{\perp}$ residual, with $\Delta R^2=0.05339906007191191$, $\Delta\mathrm{DL}=19.089327473282296$ bits, and matched-null and within-null probabilities both $0.004975124378109453$. The present chapter does not erase that protein-output contact; it says that the five-prime boundary audit, when conditioned through the stricter stepwise surface above, does not identify a stable additional start-window or downstream boundary effect.

\paragraph{Relation to the translation-mediated matrix.}
The broader $Q \to T \to P$ mediation surface is already represented elsewhere in finite rows by a twelve-organism protein-abundance matrix. That matrix uses nine $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates and reports, for example, mediated fractions $M^{QTP}=0.612922252793667$ for \emph{Escherichia coli} K-12 MG1655, $0.9491551041216973$ for \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}, $0.27206289160119157$ for human, and $0.15488799907323988$ for mouse, all within the unit interval. It also records rank sufficiency after controls for amino-acid composition, GC3, coding-sequence length, and the $M$-density baseline. A separate modeled-tAI contact computes nine synonymous residual coordinates across three organisms and reports $81$ modeled coupling entries above threshold $0.01$, with maximum absolute $S_{ij}=3801961.031607896$, while also stating that three organisms are insufficient for a powered survival claim requiring at least forty matched species.

The five-prime boundary audit should therefore be placed as a local constraint inside a larger family of translation and protein-abundance readbacks. It does not license the statement that a particular five-prime $B^{*}$ window is a stable causal source of protein output. It also does not license the converse statement that all $B^{*}_{Q6}$ protein-output contacts vanish. Its role is to remove one tempting interpretation: the Nieuwkoop signal should not be described as a start-boundary residual effect when the finite row table shows essentially zero start increment, negative downstream increment, and non-stable sign behavior.

\paragraph{Layer discipline.}
The admissible statement is a controlled statistical one: in this $1459$-variant Nieuwkoop boundary audit, the measured protein-output response is explained by the baseline and ordinary sequence/design covariates, and the tested $B^{*}$ boundary residual contributes no positive downstream increment after those controls. No translation realization, ribosome-dynamics mechanism, folding conclusion, physical-admissibility conclusion, molecular-function conclusion, phenotype claim, selection law, or universal biological rule follows from this audit alone. Each such stronger statement would require its own matched measurement surface and its own finite statistic at the corresponding biological layer. In particular, a direct $Q_i \to P_k$ association is not enough for a protein-realization claim unless the $Q_i \to T_j$ translation-readout step survives under the declared controls, and this boundary audit does not supply that surviving step.
