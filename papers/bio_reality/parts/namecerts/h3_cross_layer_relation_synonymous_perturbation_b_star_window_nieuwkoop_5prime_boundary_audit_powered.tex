\subsection{Nieuwkoop five-prime boundary audit for synonymous mRFP output}
\label{sec:bioreality-nieuwkoop-five-prime-boundary-audit}

\paragraph{Finite surface.}
This audit treats the Nieuwkoop synonymous mRFP perturbation table as a finite surface of variant rows, sequence-window residual coordinates, and measured protein-output readouts.  The active carrier is not an unrestricted sequence-to-protein map.  It is the finite table of $1459$ synonymous variants in which the proposed $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ window residual is tested only after ordinary sequence and expression baselines have had first access to the readout.  The local question is whether the apparent downstream synonymous signal has a privileged five-prime boundary form: a start-proximal contribution, a downstream contribution, or a stable sign-separated contrast between them.  The readback is therefore a boundary audit inside a perturbation experiment, not a promotion of codon geometry into translation, folding, function, or organismal phenotype.

\paragraph{Nested fits.}
The registered audit reports three nested predictive surfaces.  The first baseline reaches $R^2=0.06748192482378434$.  Adding the main sequence and context block gives a large likelihood gain, with
$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta \ell_{2\mid 1} &= 628.6217154500001,\\
R^2_2 &= 0.6060702157093043.
\end{aligned}
$$
The subsequent $B^{\ast}$ downstream boundary term does not improve the fit.  Its reported likelihood increment is negative, $\Delta \ell_{3\mid 2}=-2.082965600280344$, and the final fit falls slightly to $R^2_3=0.6049438075310105$.  Equivalently, the downstream boundary increment is $\Delta R^2=-0.001126409133973234$ with block permutation $p=0.1691542288557214$ against a null $95\%$ value of $0.5254770343527547$.  The separate downstream $16+$ audit is also negative, with $\Delta \ell=-2.1989730953428164$.  The start-proximal term is numerically negligible rather than supportive: $\Delta \ell=0.00000326370933437133$ and $\Delta R^2=0.0000000017624020537495221$.  The sign-stability flag is false.

\paragraph{Boundary verdict.}
These numbers support a narrow negative conclusion.  The Nieuwkoop table contains enough signal for the broad experimental model to fit protein-output variation, but the five-prime boundary decomposition does not isolate a stable $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ start or downstream window effect after the stronger fitted surface is present.  The accepted statement is therefore not that the residual has no biological contact anywhere in the mRFP experiment.  A companion mRFP protein-output run on the same $1459$ variants reports a positive residual increment after controls, with $R^2$ rising from $0.12648386762689323$ to $0.1798829276988051$, $\Delta R^2=0.05339906007191191$, $\Delta$ description-length gain $19.089327473282296$ bits, and matched and within-null $p=0.004975124378109453$.  It also records that real CAI does not absorb the residual signal, with real-CAI robustness true and correlation between real CAI and the proxy only $0.17884920449487354$.  The present boundary audit says something more specific: that this protein-output contact should not be redescribed as a five-prime boundary law.

\paragraph{Relation to mediated protein abundance.}
The broader cross-layer mediation surface remains a controlled $Q_i \to T_j \to P_k$ object.  In the multi-organism protein-abundance mediation run, the finite coordinate set has $9$ residual coordinates, and each organism keeps the translation readout as a separate modeled per-protein tAI coordinate before protein abundance is read.  The mediated fractions span a wide range: for example $M^{QTP}=0.9491551041216973$ in Saccharomyces cerevisiae, $0.612922252793667$ in Escherichia coli K12 MG1655, $0.27206289160119157$ in Homo sapiens, and $0.01850668564589326$ in Sulfolobus solfataricus.  Those entries justify retaining the mediated matrix as a real statistical object, but they do not rescue the five-prime boundary call in this perturbation audit.  Here the registered finite rows say that the boundary term is not a positive, stable increment once the stronger fitted baseline is in place.

\paragraph{Local cannot-claim rule.}
The chapter therefore records a bounded protein-output perturbation audit, not a mechanism.  No translation-realization claim follows from the five-prime boundary numbers, because no separate ribosome or translation-efficiency contact is used here to establish a surviving $Q_i \to T_j$ step.  No folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, organismal phenotype, selection rule, or universal biological law follows from the residual or from the mRFP protein-output association.  A direct $Q_i \to P_k$ association remains only a controlled protein-output association unless the translation readout is separately joined, tested, and shown to survive under amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, coding-sequence length, M-density baseline, and matched mRNA controls.
