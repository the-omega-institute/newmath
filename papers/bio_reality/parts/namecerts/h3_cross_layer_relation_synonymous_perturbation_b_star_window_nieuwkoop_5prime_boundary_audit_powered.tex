\subsection{Nieuwkoop 5-prime boundary audit for the B-star window residual}
\label{sec:biorreality-nieuwkoop-five-prime-boundary-audit}

\paragraph{Finite surface.}
This record concerns a finite perturbation table with $1459$ synonymous variants in the Nieuwkoop \textit{E. coli} mRFP setting.  The audited coordinate is not promoted as a direct protein-output cause.  It is read only through a bounded row surface whose columns separate the near-start and downstream coding windows, the ordinary sequence controls, and the B-star window residual.  The local question is whether the apparent B-star contribution in the protein-output table is still visible after the first coding positions and their ordinary codon-adaptation baseline are made explicit.

\paragraph{Nested audit.}
The fitted sequence of models has three reported stages.  The first stage gives
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_1 &= 0.06748192482378434 .
\end{aligned}
$$
Adding the main 5-prime boundary block gives a large likelihood improvement and a much larger fitted surface,
$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta \ell_{2\mid 1} &= 628.6217154500001,\\
R^2_2 &= 0.6060702157093043 .
\end{aligned}
$$
After that boundary block is present, adding the downstream B-star window term does not improve the table.  The third stage is
$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta \ell_{3\mid 2} &= -2.082965600280344,\\
R^2_3 &= 0.6049438075310105 .
\end{aligned}
$$
Equivalently, the downstream B-star increment is negative on the reported scale,
$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta R^2_{B,\mathrm{down}} &= -0.001126409133973234,\\
\Delta \ell_{B,\mathrm{down}} &= -2.082967365015851 .
\end{aligned}
$$
The block-null comparison gives $p=0.1691542288557214$ with a null $95\%$ value of $0.5254770343527547$, so the downstream term is not a supported residual improvement.  The stricter downstream window beginning at position $16$ is also negative, with
$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta \ell_{B,\mathrm{down},16+} &= -2.1989730953428164 .
\end{aligned}
$$
The near-start B-star increment is effectively zero, with $\Delta \ell_{B,\mathrm{start}}=3.26370933437133\times 10^{-6}$ and $\Delta R^2_{B,\mathrm{start}}=1.7624020537495221\times 10^{-9}$.  The sign-stability flag is false.  The finite verdict is therefore a boundary audit rather than a positive downstream realization result: in this table the large explanatory gain is attached to the 5-prime boundary block, not to an independently surviving downstream B-star window term.

\paragraph{Relation to the broader mRFP protein-output table.}
The same experimental family also contains a broader mRFP protein-output analysis on $1459$ variants in which adding the B-star-perpendicular component to the control surface improves the fit from $R^2=0.12648386762689323$ to $R^2=0.1798829276988051$, with $\Delta R^2=0.05339906007191191$, $\Delta DL=19.089327473282296$ bits, and matched and within-null probabilities both equal to $0.004975124378109453$.  That companion result shows that the perturbation table can carry a B-star-associated protein-output signal under its broader control design.  The present audit asks a narrower localization question and answers it negatively: once the 5-prime boundary block is represented explicitly, the downstream and start-local B-star increments do not survive as independent improvements.

\paragraph{Mediation boundary.}
The protein-abundance mediation packet gives the appropriate cross-layer discipline for interpreting such rows.  Across the larger organism-matched matrix, the controlled $Q\to T\to P$ decomposition reports positive mediated fractions such as $M^{QTP}=0.612922252793667$ for \textit{E. coli} K-12 MG1655 and $M^{QTP}=0.9491551041216973$ for \textit{S. cerevisiae}, using modeled per-protein tAI and measured protein abundance after amino-acid composition, GC3, CDS length, M-density baseline, and related controls.  Those values are mediation summaries, not causal or mechanistic proof.  In the Nieuwkoop 5-prime audit, the available finite-row evidence is even narrower: it says that the boundary block dominates this perturbation table and that the downstream B-star window does not add a stable positive increment.

\paragraph{Local conclusion.}
The allowed claim is a scoped negative boundary result.  The Nieuwkoop mRFP perturbation rows support a strong 5-prime boundary contribution, but they do not support an independently positive downstream B-star window after that boundary is included.  No translation realization, folding statement, physical admissibility statement, molecular function, mechanism, organism-level phenotype, selection law, or universal biological rule follows from this audit.  Any such claim would require a separate layer-matched empirical contact and a new controlled statistic showing the relevant step directly.
