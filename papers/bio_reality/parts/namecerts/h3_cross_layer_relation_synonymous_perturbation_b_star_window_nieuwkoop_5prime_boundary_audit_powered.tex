\subsection{Nieuwkoop five-prime boundary audit for synonymous-window protein output}
\label{sec:bioreality-nieuwkoop-fiveprime-boundary-audit}

\paragraph{Finite row surface.}
This record treats the Nieuwkoop synonymous-variant panel as a finite perturbation surface for asking whether the $B^{\ast}$ window signal is concentrated at the coding-sequence start or whether it persists into downstream coding positions.  The observed table contains $1459$ variants.  The audit is intentionally narrower than a full biological mechanism claim: the rows are used to test whether a residual synonymous-window coordinate improves protein-output prediction after the declared covariates, and then whether the improvement remains stable away from the five-prime boundary.  The registered resampling depth was $B=200$, so the finite comparison has enough repeated block draws to distinguish a boundary-local improvement from a robust downstream signal, but it is still a statistical perturbation audit rather than a direct measurement of translation dynamics.

\paragraph{Nested finite models.}
The audit compares three nested fits.  The first fit gives the covariate-only boundary surface, with
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_1 &= 0.06748192482378434 .
\end{aligned}
$$
The second fit adds the major start-region explanatory block and moves the model to
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_2 &= 0.6060702157093043,\\
\Delta \ell_{2-1} &= 628.6217154500001 .
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the dominant predictive gain in this perturbation table is associated with the start-proximal block.  The downstream $B^{\ast}$ window term then gives
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_3 &= 0.6049438075310105,\\
\Delta \ell_{3-2} &= -2.082965600280344,\\
\Delta R^2_{\mathrm{downstream}} &= -0.001126409133973234 .
\end{aligned}
$$
The downstream term is therefore not a positive improvement over the start-region model in this finite run.  Its block-null comparison gives
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{\mathrm{block}} &= 0.1691542288557214,\\
Q_{0.95}^{\mathrm{null}} &= 0.5254770343527547,
\end{aligned}
$$
and the sign-stability flag is false.  A stricter downstream interval beginning at position $16$ gives $\Delta \ell=-2.1989730953428164$, reinforcing the same boundary verdict.  By contrast, the start-specific $B$ term itself is numerically negligible in this audit, with
$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta \ell_{\mathrm{start},B} &= 3.26370933437133\times 10^{-6},\\
\Delta R^2_{\mathrm{start},B} &= 1.7624020537495221\times 10^{-9} .
\end{aligned}
$$
The finite-row conclusion is therefore not that $B^{\ast}$ explains the start effect; it is that once the start-region block is included, the downstream synonymous-window residual does not carry a stable positive increment in this panel.

\paragraph{Relation to the companion mRFP output fit.}
The same Nieuwkoop variant count, $1459$, also appears in the companion protein-output fit for the Escherichia coli mRFP table.  There, the controls-only level has $R^2=0.12648386762689323$, while the controls plus $B_{\perp}$ level has $R^2=0.1798829276988051$, so
$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta R^2 &= 0.05339906007191191,\\
\Delta \mathrm{DL}_{\mathrm{bits}} &= 19.089327473282296,\\
p_{\mathrm{matched}} &= p_{\mathrm{within}} = 0.004975124378109453 .
\end{aligned}
$$
That companion result shows that the synonymous-window coordinate can be predictive for protein output in the same variant scale, and the real-CAI audit reports that the signal is not absorbed by the real CAI control.  The five-prime boundary audit separates this broader protein-output association from a downstream-position claim: the positive companion result does not license a claim that the downstream portion of the window remains active after the start-region block has been fitted.  The row-level lesson is a localization of evidential support, not a promotion to a general coding-sequence law.

\paragraph{Mediation context.}
The wider $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ mediation surface keeps the appropriate biological order explicit: residual coordinates may be compared with a translation readout $T$ and then with matched protein abundance $P$, rather than being read as a direct residual-to-protein mechanism.  In the twelve-organism modeled-tAI/protein-abundance mediation run, all organisms had rank-sufficient residualized designs and finite mediated fractions in the unit interval.  The reported mediated fractions included $0.612922252793667$ for Escherichia coli K-12 MG1655, $0.9491551041216973$ for Saccharomyces cerevisiae, $0.27206289160119157$ for Homo sapiens, and $0.01850668564589326$ for Sulfolobus solfataricus.  The Escherichia coli entry used $3739$ joined proteins, while the yeast entry used $5815$ joined proteins.  Those numbers show that the project has a finite matrix language for $Q\to T\to P$ decomposition, but the Nieuwkoop five-prime audit itself does not measure ribosome occupancy, translation efficiency, or a matched mediation chain.  Its own verdict is only about whether a downstream synonymous-window term survives the finite perturbation boundary test after the start-region explanatory block.

\paragraph{Local verdict.}
The finite calculation supports a boundary-limited reading.  The start-region block gives a large improvement, $\Delta \ell_{2-1}=628.6217154500001$, but the downstream $B^{\ast}$ term is negative by likelihood and by $R^2$ after that block is present.  The downstream block is also not significant against its finite null at $p_{\mathrm{block}}=0.1691542288557214$, and its sign is not stable.  Consequently this chapter records a positive perturbation-scale protein-output context for the Nieuwkoop table only together with a refusal to promote the downstream five-prime-window residual into a stable translation-mediated effect.

\paragraph{Claim boundary.}
No claim of translation realization follows from this audit alone.  No folded structure, physical admissibility, molecular function, organismal phenotype, causal mechanism, adaptive selection law, or universal biological rule follows from the row statistics above.  Such statements would require separate layer-matched contacts, for example a same-CDS translation-efficiency or ribosome-footprint surface for $Q\to T$, matched protein abundance for $Q\to T\to P$, and additional assays for structure, function, phenotype, or physical admissibility.  In the present finite table, the admissible statement is only that the downstream synonymous-window increment is not supported after the start-region model, despite the companion protein-output fit showing a broader $B_{\perp}$ association in the same Nieuwkoop variant scale.
