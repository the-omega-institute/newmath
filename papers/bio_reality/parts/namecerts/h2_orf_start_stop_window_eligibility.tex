The finite run attached to this claim loaded one curated window manifest, classified all supplied windows by the start--stop enumeration rule, and reported
$$
\begin{aligned}

\text{classified windows} &= 1,\\

Thus the row is a finite eligibility classifier over a supplied window, not an autonomous biological observation.  The row can say that the one input window passed through the finite start--stop rule; it cannot, from that fact alone, say that a transcript was expressed, that a peptide was produced, that a folded product exists, that the product is physically admissible, that it has function, that it supplies a mechanism, or that it states a universal genomic law.

\paragraph{Reality-contact gate.}
The seed record contains no direct contact entries of its own.  Its probes require a curated ORF interval annotation before an ORF eligibility assertion can be made beyond boundary status.  The reason is simple: start and stop codons are necessary ingredients for a candidate interval rule, but the existence of a start--stop pattern inside a finite enumeration is not the same as observing a biological interval in a genome, transcript, or curated annotation table.  The row is therefore admissible as a boundary test: if a pipeline promotes start--stop enumeration alone into ORF eligibility, translation realization, folded structure, physical admissibility, biological function, mechanism, or universality, the packet breaks its own stated scope.

\paragraph{Local verdict.}
The finite evidence is therefore sufficient for a narrow statement: the start--stop-window packet is a contact-gated ORF-eligibility record, and the one-window manifest run classified its single supplied window under the frozen enumeration rule.  The related curated interval row supplies an external annotation contact with $168$ rows and strong finite positive-versus-decoy behavior, but even that contact is limited to external ORF interval contact.  Neither row licenses a claim of translation, folding, physical admissibility, biological function, mechanism, or universality.  The self-check attached to the packet records three checked gates and states that no codon geometry is promoted across biological layers.  The correct reading is consequently layer-local: finite start--stop enumeration may organize an externally supplied interval test, while any stronger biological assertion must arrive through an additional contact with evidence appropriate to that stronger layer.
