\subsection{Universal core tRNA adaptation in the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual direction}\label{subsec:bioreality-q6-universal-core-trna-adaptation}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite object.}
The record studied here is the finite coding-sequence residual surface obtained after amino-acid projection, restricted to the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ coordinate family and read only through organism-matched codon-usage, tRNA-copy, and codon-adaptation rows. The concrete question is whether the optimal direction in this residual coordinate system is already accounted for by a common tRNA-adaptation component, before any interpretation is made at the level of ribosome occupancy, protein abundance, folded structure, molecular function, phenotype, or universality. The computation named $\mathrm{b\_star\_q6\_universal\_core\_is\_trna\_adaptation\_powered}$ was run with $5$ folds, a minimum of $500$ proteins per organism, $41$ synonymous-design columns, and a deterministic seed recorded as $\mathrm{sha256:b\_star\_q6\_universal\_core\_is\_trna\_adaptation\_powered:deterministic}$. It requested $16$ organisms and retained $11$ organisms for the powered finite panel. Its status is passed. Thus the finite claim supported by this chapter is not that translation has been measured directly, but that the cross-organism residual direction selected by the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ design is compatible with a universal tRNA-adaptation core on the available codon-usage and tRNA-weight rows.

\paragraph{The $f_3$ boundary surface.}
The accompanying $f_3$ saturation computation fixes the discrete support on which the residual interpretation is allowed to operate. The set $M=\mathrm{Med}(R)\cup\mathrm{ACA}$ is
$$
\begin{aligned}
M=\{&\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{ACA},\mathrm{ACG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\\
&\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
After removing the two $\{\mathrm{ACA},\mathrm{ACG}\}$ entries, the $f_3$ saturation of $R$ is
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{Sat}_{f_3}(R)=\{&\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\\
&\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The finite boundary reported for $f_3$ is empty, $\partial_{f_3}=\varnothing$. This matters because the coordinate being tested is not an unconstrained codon-frequency contrast. It is the synonymously projected imbalance remaining after the amino-acid quotient has already removed the first-order amino-acid-composition explanation. In this chapter the empty boundary is used as a local admissibility condition: it permits a clean residual coordinate, but it does not itself supply a biological measurement.

\paragraph{Powered residual result.}
On the powered organism panel, the primary tRNA-adaptation run passed with $11$ computed organisms out of $16$ requested, $5$ folds, $41$ synonymous-design columns, and the $500$-protein organism threshold. The nearby residual-axis runs constrain the interpretation of that pass. The non-tRNA residual optimality run reported that the dominant non-tRNA residual direction aligns most with $f_3$ translation selection. The two-axis closure run concluded that a third axis is still present: the $d_{\perp}$ directions have systematically positive held-out $R^2$ and mutually same-signed directions across organisms. The third-axis identity run identified $d_{\perp}$ provisionally with usage frequency within synonymous family, with mean cosine $0.128$ and one-sided sign-test $p=0.000488$. After GC orthogonalization, the third-axis mechanism run kept significant cross-species alignment and held-out $R^2$, and classified the residual as selection-not-GC, not wobble-clustered, with CSC or positional identity still requiring an additional contact.

\paragraph{Spectrum and mechanism checks.}
The axis-strength spectrum gives the local rank of the effect. Among the $11$ computed organisms, the captured spectra were led on average by $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$, followed by $d_{\mathrm{tRNA}}$, and the per-organism dominant-count summary was $\{d_{\mathrm{resid4}}:11\}$. Domain means were explicitly descriptive because the retained panel was imbalanced, with $1$ archaeal, $2$ bacterial, and $8$ eukaryotic organisms. The largest reported GC3 rank effect in that spectrum was $d_{\mathrm{usage}}$ with $\rho=0.136$. Separately, the boundary-set tRNA-poverty permutation test was run on $40$ computed organisms out of $46$ requested, skipped $6$, used $1000$ permutations at $\alpha=0.05$, and passed with the mechanism call that $R$ sense codons are systematically tRNA-poor under GtRNAdb tGCN together with dos Reis wobble weights. This supports a tRNA-supply interpretation for $R$ avoidance, but only at the level of codon and tRNA-weight contacts.

\paragraph{Why three axes are not enough.}
The finite panel does not collapse to a two-axis tRNA-plus-$f_3$ story. The three-axis sufficiency run concluded $\mathrm{fourth\_mechanism\_present}$ on $11$ computed organisms, again with $5$ folds, $41$ synonymous-design columns, and the $500$-protein threshold. The fourth-mechanism exclusion checks sharpen that statement. In yeast, the elongation-dwell run was classified as partial. The mRNA-stability run concluded $\mathrm{fourth\_axis\_not\_mrna\_stability}$ because yeast $d_{\mathrm{opt\_halflife}}$ had low alignment with abundance $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$ under the fixed gates. The codon-pair run concluded $\mathrm{not\_codon\_pair}$ using $50$ shuffle-null samples. The translation-accuracy run concluded $\mathrm{not\_accuracy}$ using $200$ permutations on $11$ computed organisms out of $16$ requested. These negative identifications prevent the chapter from reducing the residual spectrum to a single named downstream mechanism.

\paragraph{Local statistical reading.}
The finite reading is therefore the following. There is a passed powered computation for the universal tRNA-adaptation core in the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ optimal direction. It sits on a codon and tRNA-weight panel with $11$ retained organisms, $5$ folds, $41$ synonymous-design columns, and a $500$-protein organism threshold. It is consistent with a broader residual geometry in which $f_3$ translation selection, a same-signed $d_{\perp}$ axis, and a fourth residual component remain visible after amino-acid projection and baseline controls. The $f_3$ support lists above show that the coordinate is finite and explicit, and the tRNA-poverty permutation result gives a concrete supply-side bridge for the $R$ sense codons. These facts justify the local phrase ``universal core tRNA adaptation'' only for the bounded codon-adaptation layer.

\paragraph{No-promotion boundary.}
No claim in this chapter says that $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual geometry has been observed as ribosome occupancy, translation efficiency, protein abundance, folded structure, physical admissibility, molecular function, phenotype, mechanism, or a universal biological law. A modeled tAI or stAI row is a codon-adaptation contact, not an observed ribosome-footprint row. A codon-usage table is a sequence-composition contact, not a protein-output assay. A matched mRNA row is a transcription-layer control for later translation-efficiency residualization, not a translation-readout by itself. A positive or passed entry in the finite codon/tRNA computation may motivate a translation-readout test, but it does not become such a test until same-organism and same-CDS codon counts, wobble or tRNA weights, measured ribosome-profiling or translation-efficiency rows, and matched mRNA controls are joined and a controlled survival statistic is computed.

\paragraph{Falsification route.}
The bounded conjecture would lose its biological reading if the same coordinate failed once moved to a rank-powered direct translation panel. In the intended follow-up matrix, each $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual coordinate $q_i$ must be paired with a translation readout $T_j$ and controls for amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, CDS length, $M$-density, matched mRNA abundance, rank, and simpler codon baselines. The admissible survival claim has the form
$$
\begin{aligned}
S_{ij}>0
\end{aligned}
$$
for at least one nondegenerate $(q_i,T_j)$ entry after those controls. If every controlled $S_{ij}$ is non-positive, or if the apparent matrix is rank-saturated in the manner of an all-$1.0$ three-organism surface, then the translation-readout interpretation is refused. Under that outcome the present chapter would still record a finite codon/tRNA residual computation, but it would not support translation realization beyond the bounded adaptation-weight layer.
