\paragraph{Layer verdict.}
The finite-row conclusion is therefore positive only at the declared layer.  The one-window run confirms that a curated external window can be classified by the start/stop enumeration, and the $168$-row curated interval run supplies a larger external contact with perfect positive acceptance, perfect matched-decoy rejection, stable held-out folds, and no boundary promotion.  The local statement is
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{contacted\ interval\ data} &\Longrightarrow \mathrm{orf\_eligibility\ screen\ result},\\
\mathrm{codon\ geometry\ alone} &\nRightarrow \mathrm{orf\_eligibility\ contact},\\
\mathrm{orf\_eligibility} &\nRightarrow \mathrm{translation,\ folding,\ admissibility,\ function,\ mechanism,\ or\ universality}.
\end{aligned}

The ORF packet is therefore ready as a finite, externally checked eligibility record, with its scope explicitly restricted to start/stop interval screening.
