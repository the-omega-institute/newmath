\paragraph{Local non-promotion boundary.}
The admissible conclusion is intentionally narrow.  Start--stop enumeration may certify that a supplied window satisfies the finite eligibility rule, and the curated-interval row may certify that the frozen classifier separated positive curated intervals from matched decoys and boundary cases in the recorded dataset.  No translation event follows from this.  No folded structure, physical admissibility, biochemical mechanism, organism-level function, or universal biological rule follows from it.  Such claims require a separate contact that is able to test the corresponding biological layer.  The self-check row states the same restriction numerically: it checked $3$ gates and recorded that no codon geometry is promoted across biological layers.  Therefore this chapter records a finite, contact-limited ORF eligibility result: one externally supplied window is classified in the window-enumeration run, and the related curated-interval contact gives $168$ rows of stronger interval evidence, while all higher biological interpretations remain outside the certified scope.
