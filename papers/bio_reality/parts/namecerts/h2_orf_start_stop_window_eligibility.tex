\paragraph{Contact gap.}
The claim row itself has no attached reality-contact references. The local admissible reading is therefore narrow: a start/stop-window eligibility seed may remain as a boundary test for what would have to be checked, but it cannot by itself become an ORF eligibility assertion about a real biological interval. The two probe records state this boundary in complementary ways. First, a start/stop-window seed must remain at boundary status until an external sequence, transcript, or curated interval annotation supplies the observed interval. Second, recording an ORF eligibility claim without such a testable biological interval would break the row. Thus the finite enumeration is a classifier over a supplied window, not evidence that the window is realized in a cell.
