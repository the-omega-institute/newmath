\subsection{Joint translation-efficiency and stability partition for $B^*_{Q6}$ abundance residuals}
\label{sec:bioreality-q6-joint-te-stability-mediation}

\paragraph{Finite surface.}
The finite surface is a matched per-CDS table in which nine $B^*_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates are compared with measured protein abundance, measured ribosome-derived translation efficiency, and an mRNA-stability readout after the same covariate residualization.  The coordinate block is
$K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $Leu_{CUN\,vs\,UUR}$, $Leu_{UUA\,vs\,UUG}$, $Ser_{UCR\,vs\,AGY}$, $Ser_{UCA\,vs\,UCG}$, $Thr_{ACR\,vs\,ACY}$, and $f_3$-stress.  The control block has $24$ columns before numerical rank reduction and consists of an intercept, $\log(\mathrm{CDS\ length})$, the $20$ amino-acid composition proportions, GC3, and an $M$-density baseline defined on the union support of the nine projected coordinates.  In the three organisms with the complete four-way join, the residualized control rank is $23$ and the residualized $Q$-rank is $9$ in every organism, so the reported entries are not the all-saturated small-panel boundary.

\paragraph{Joined rows.}
The computed panel contains three organism-specific joins.  In \textit{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}, $4759$ CDS rows are joined, with a reported CDS join hit rate $0.9156038419146592$ and a four-way join hit rate $0.8818572656921754$; the TE readout uses $5128$ genes and the mRNA half-life table reports $5379$ valid systematic-gene records.  In \textit{Escherichia coli} K-12 MG1655, $2517$ rows are joined, with reported hit rates $0.9978649586335735$ for CDS-to-abundance and $0.9133458143888741$ for the TE join; the TE table supplies $3415$ protein-keyed genes and the mRNA half-life table supplies $2947$ valid gene-id records at growth rate $0.40\,\mathrm{h}^{-1}$.  In \textit{Homo sapiens}, $11322$ rows are joined, with a reported CDS-to-abundance hit rate $0.9779294769799313$ and TE join hit rate $0.6064136881331024$; the TE table supplies $11554$ genes and the mRNA consensus table reports $19480$ valid Ensembl-gene records.  The finite-row claim is therefore scoped to these three joined surfaces, not to all organisms in the larger protein-abundance scan.

\paragraph{Variance partition.}
For each organism let $P_e$ denote protein abundance after the $Z$ controls, let $Q_e$ denote the residualized nine-coordinate block, let $T_e$ denote residualized measured translation efficiency, and let $M_e$ denote residualized mRNA stability.  The recorded definition is
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{QP} &= R^2(P_e \sim Q_e),\\
R^2_{QP\mid T} &= R^2(P_e \sim Q_e\mid T_e),\\
R^2_{QP\mid M} &= R^2(P_e \sim Q_e\mid M_e),\\
R^2_{QP\mid T,M} &= R^2(P_e \sim Q_e\mid T_e,M_e),\\
\mu_{T,M} &= \frac{R^2_{QP}-R^2_{QP\mid T,M}}{R^2_{QP}}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The scalar $\mu_{T,M}$ is a variance-partition fraction: it measures how much of the $Q$-to-abundance association is removed by adding the measured TE and mRNA-stability readouts to the residualized regression.  It is not a causal mediation effect.

\paragraph{Observed partition.}
The yeast surface has the largest direct abundance association and the largest joint attenuation.  Its entries are
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{QP} &= 0.2715520808117803,\\
R^2_{QP\mid T} &= 0.1439989545940314,\\
R^2_{QP\mid M} &= 0.25453612246767165,\\
R^2_{QP\mid T,M} &= 0.13047582645538838,\\
\mu_{T,M} &= 0.5195182225621592.
\end{aligned}
$$
The single-readout fractions are $0.4697188319693239$ for measured TE alone and $0.0626618595344251$ for mRNA stability alone.  Their sum is $0.532380691503749$, and the joint-minus-single-sum value is $-0.012862468941589822$, inside the declared descriptive tolerance $0.05$; the finite row is therefore classified as approximately independent overlap between the two readout axes.  The right-tail deterministic pair-permutation value is $0.000999000999000999$.

\paragraph{Rank diagnostics.}
The residual-energy diagnostics show that the regressions were computed on nonzero residual surfaces.  For yeast, the residual degrees of freedom after $Z$ are $4736$, with $p$-residual energy $1724.6664873071313$, $t$-residual energy $645.3764418081507$, and $m$-residual energy $194.99141167236996$; the $Q$ block explains $468.33677333459536$ energy on $P$, while the combined $T,M$ block explains $470.61694807139054$.  For \textit{E. coli}, the residual degrees of freedom are $2494$, with $p$-, $t$-, and $m$-residual energies $2323.813305213946$, $672.36799431777$, and $198.6753916649337$; the $Q$ block explains $274.6151322248693$ energy on $P$.  For human, the corresponding residual degrees of freedom are $11299$, with residual energies $12615.307912483846$, $1866.9940287020145$, and $647.920297431553$, and the $Q$ block explains $539.2007770548378$ energy on $P$.  In all three organisms the residualized ranks are $\mathrm{rank}(T_e)=1$, $\mathrm{rank}(M_e)=1$, $\mathrm{rank}(T_e,M_e)=2$, $\mathrm{rank}(Q_e,T_e,M_e)=11$.

\paragraph{Relation to measured TE and direct protein scans.}
The broader measured-TE survival computation supplies the separate translation-readout contact.  It reports positive $Q$-to-measured-TE partial $R^2$ values for four organisms: $0.13681931343730436$ in yeast, $0.009394266754822262$ in \textit{E. coli}, $0.018837911344127926$ in human, and $0.04034217614654355$ in zebrafish, with all nine coordinates surviving above the positive threshold in those rows.  The direct protein-omics scan is broader still: across $15$ organisms it reports positive direct $Q$-to-protein abundance partial $R^2$ values after the same family of controls, including $0.29837624994140827$ in yeast, $0.10767526677985896$ in \textit{E. coli}, and $0.04454565118019448$ in human.  These two finite facts justify treating the joint TE--stability run as a downstream partition of an already detected abundance association, while also showing why a direct $Q\to P$ association is not by itself a mechanism statement.

\paragraph{Four-case comparison.}
A separate two-organism four-case discriminant over modeled tAI and measured protein abundance classifies yeast as case $D$ for all $9$ coordinates and \textit{E. coli} as case $D$ for $7$ coordinates, with $2$ positive-positive entries lacking a mediation drop.  In that auxiliary surface, the modeled-tAI $R^2_{QT}$ values are $0.8213457220900503$ for yeast and $0.5416736993691318$ for \textit{E. coli}, while the corresponding direct $R^2\_{QP}$ values are $0.29837624994140827$ and $0.10767526677985896$.  The joint measured-TE and stability partition sharpens that readback: in yeast, measured TE absorbs a large fraction of the direct abundance signal; in \textit{E. coli} and human, mRNA stability accounts for most of the joint attenuation.

\paragraph{Reduction boundary.}
The yeast dictionary-style reduction certificate gives an additional scale for the residual.  On a yeast join with $n=1800$, $5$ folds, $\lambda\_{DL}=0.01$, and $120$ deterministic permutations, the best selection-aware coverage by at most three readouts among measured TE, mRNA stability, and turnover is $0.24538211439730545$, above a selection-aware null $95$th percentile of $0.029149007242956115$ and leaving unexplained fraction $0.7546178856026946$.  This is compatible with the joint TE--stability result: measured readouts remove a reproducible part of the abundance residual, but they do not collapse the nine-coordinate $Q$ signal to a fully explained layer.

\paragraph{Local verdict.}
The finite-row verdict is that $B^*\_{Q6}$ residual coordinates retain a direct protein-abundance association in the joined panels, and measured TE plus mRNA stability remove a positive, permutation-separated fraction of that association in yeast, \textit{E. coli}, and human.  The organism-specific pattern is not uniform: yeast is TE-dominated, while \textit{E. coli} and human are stability-dominated.  The overlap classification is approximately independent in all three organisms at the fixed tolerance $0.05$.  This supports a bounded cross-layer statistical statement: the abundance residual is partly read through measured TE and mRNA stability on finite matched rows.

\paragraph{Claim boundary.}
No causal ordering is inferred from these rows.  The abundance, TE, and mRNA-stability measurements come from organism-matched but not necessarily condition-identical data sources, and the regressions are observational variance partitions.  A positive $Q\to T$ association supplies a measured translation-readout surface, but it does not by itself establish a ribosome mechanism, elongation model, perturbation response, folded structure, physical admissibility, molecular function, phenotype, adaptation direction, or universal biological rule.  A direct $Q\to P$ association remains a protein-omics prediction unless conditioning on the measured readout reduces the signal on the same finite surface; any higher-layer interpretation requires a separate layer-matched contact and its own controlled statistic.
