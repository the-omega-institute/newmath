\subsection{Position-resolved dwell evidence for the f3-stress abundance component}\label{sec:bioreality-f3-stress-dwell-positional}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite surface.}
This record concerns the $f3\_stress$ coordinate inside the nine-coordinate $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ synonymous residual surface.  The tested readout is not a global protein-abundance association and not a structural claim.  It is the projection $P_{f3}$ of $Z$-residualized $\log_{10}$ protein abundance onto the $Z$-residualized $f3\_stress$ direction on a yeast join where positional ribosome-occupancy summaries are available.  The organism is \textit{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}; the active join contains $n=1257$ genes, the base sampling frame has $1800$ rows, the residual control basis has rank $24$, and the coordinate block tested here has $\operatorname{rank}(f3)=1$.  The target component has squared norm
$$
\begin{aligned}
\lVert P_{f3}\rVert^2 &= 44.20095750389323,\\
P_{f3}\text{ fraction of target} &= 0.1999063916718794.
\end{aligned}
$$
The row is therefore a finite positional-discrimination test for a specific abundance component, not an unrestricted statement about translation or protein biology.

\paragraph{Readout construction.}
The dwell variables are per-gene relative ribosome-occupancy summaries computed separately from file-provided $5'$ head and $3'$ tail A-site occupancy windows.  The fit compares a base design against base-plus-segment dwell designs under five held-out folds.  Its null is a deterministic row-label permutation of $P_{f3}$ with base and full designs and folds fixed, run separately for each segment and for the joint segment design.  The permutation count is $120$, and the deterministic seed is \texttt{sha256:b\_star\_q6\_f3\_stress\_dwell\_positional\_powered:deterministic}.  The registered finite verdict is \texttt{passed}: the leading segment by held-out increment is \texttt{5prime}, the dominant segment is \texttt{5prime}, the $5'$ ramp gate passed, and the $3'$ body gate did not pass.  In compact form,
$$
\begin{aligned}
\text{organism} &= \textit{S. cerevisiae},\\
n_{\mathrm{join}} &= 1257,\\
\text{folds} &= 5,\\
\text{permutations} &= 120,\\
\text{leading segment} &= 5',\\
\text{5' ramp passed} &= \mathrm{true},\\
\text{3' body passed} &= \mathrm{false}.
\end{aligned}
$$
This is a directional positional statement: the observed increment localizes to the head-window side of the dwell readout rather than to the tail-window side.

\paragraph{Relation to the non-positional dwell certificate.}
The same $P_{f3}$ abundance component has an independent dwell-mediation row on the same organismal context.  That companion fit used $n_{\mathrm{join}}=1257$, base frame size $1800$, five folds, $120$ deterministic permutations, and the same target norm $44.20095750389323$.  Its base held-out $R^2$ was $0.1855578757644517$, its dwell-alone $R^2$ was $0.034336580953121976$, its full held-out $R^2$ was $0.20412261520502883$, and the held-out dwell increment was
$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta R^2_{\mathrm{dwell}} &= 0.01856473944057712,\\
\mathrm{Null}_{95} &= 0.0013115362189692937,\\
\mathrm{Null}_{\max} &= 0.005001350039772926,\\
\mathrm{Null}_{\mathrm{mean}} &= -0.0032911642620130563,\\
\mathrm{Null}_{\min} &= -0.013799622026666476.
\end{aligned}
$$
The observed $f3\_stress$--dwell correlation in that row was $0.1615441450901653$ with approximate $p=7.873916057196935\times 10^{-9}$.  Thus the positional result refines a previously positive dwell increment: the finite table says that the usable dwell increment is not merely present, but is carried by the $5'$ segment in the positional decomposition.

\paragraph{Relation to ramp-body structural-order screening.}
A separate ramp-versus-body screen on the same coordinate family also reports a positive ramp-side signal without granting a structural interpretation.  In that row the primary ramp size was $50$ codons, the weighted $f3$ ramp held-out increment was $0.0011165640103326878$, the weighted $f3$ body increment was $-0.00026157313923957926$, and the paired ramp-minus-body increment was $0.001378137149572267$.  The sign-flip probabilities were
$$
\begin{aligned}
p(f3\text{ ramp}>0) &= 0.0478515625,\\
p(f3\text{ body}>0) &= 0.54296875,\\
p(f3\text{ ramp}-f3\text{ body}>0) &= 0.05859375.
\end{aligned}
$$
That screen classified the outcome as \texttt{ramp\_positive\_but\_not\_localized}: the ramp signal was positive, while the stronger paired localization gate did not pass.  The present dwell-positional row is sharper in its own measurement family: within measured positional dwell summaries, the $5'$ segment is the passing segment and the $3'$ body segment is not.

\paragraph{Cross-layer scope.}
The direct protein-omics surface shows that $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residuals can associate with measured PAXdb protein abundance after controls across multiple organisms, but those entries are direct associations.  For example, the yeast protein-abundance row used $5815$ joined proteins with join hit rate $0.9156038419146592$ and reported $R^2_{QP}=0.29837624994140827$ with all $9$ residual coordinates surviving.  Human used $19053$ joined proteins with hit rate $0.9779294769799313$ and reported $R^2_{QP}=0.04454565118019448$; mouse used $19395$ joined proteins with hit rate $0.9808334176190958$ and reported $R^2_{QP}=0.049827984062539195$; \textit{E. coli} used $3739$ joined proteins with hit rate $0.9978649586335735$ and reported $R^2_{QP}=0.10767526677985896$.  These values motivate the abundance projection but do not by themselves identify a translation mechanism.

\paragraph{Translation-conditioned comparison.}
Measured ribosome-footprint translation efficiency gives a separate translation readout.  In yeast, the measured-TE survival row used $5128$ genes with ribosome-TE join hit rate $0.8818572656921754$ and reported $R^2_{QT,\mathrm{meas}}=0.13681931343730436$ with $9$ surviving coordinates.  The measured TE--abundance mediation row for yeast used a three-way join of $5128$ rows and reported
$$
\begin{aligned}
S_{QP} &= 0.3146818503437042,\\
S_{QT,\mathrm{meas}} &= 0.13681931343730436,\\
M^{QTP}_{\mathrm{meas}} &= 0.4793069656345167.
\end{aligned}
$$
For comparison, \textit{E. coli} reported $S_{QP}=0.10941535951828074$, $S_{QT,\mathrm{meas}}=0.009394266754822262$, and $M^{QTP}_{\mathrm{meas}}=0.04091158340950673$ on $3415$ joined rows; human reported $S_{QP}=0.04288464996165745$, $S_{QT,\mathrm{meas}}=0.018837911344127926$, and $M^{QTP}_{\mathrm{meas}}=0.0027966485036786744$ on $11554$ joined rows.  The positional dwell result is consistent with this measured-readout framing: it supplies a yeast, row-matched positional ribosome-occupancy discriminator for the $f3\_stress$ abundance component, while leaving causal language outside the finite table.

\paragraph{Reduction and residual status.}
The partial readout-dictionary certificate for yeast used base frame size $1800$, five folds, $120$ permutations, $\lambda_{\mathrm{DL}}=0.01$, and $\rho_{\mathrm{join}}=0.05$.  It found selection-aware significant coverage
$$
\begin{aligned}
C^{\ast} &= 0.24538211439730545,\\
\mathrm{unexplained} &= 0.7546178856026946,\\
\mathrm{selection\ aware\ Null}_{95} &= 0.029149007242956115,\\
\mathrm{naive\ fixed\ subset\ Null}_{95} &= 0.00598374993486675.
\end{aligned}
$$
This prevents the dwell result from being overstated.  The $5'$ positional dwell signal explains a finite part of the $f3\_stress$ abundance component, and it improves the localization of that part within ribosome-occupancy windows, but a large residual component remains outside the listed readout dictionary.

\paragraph{Local conclusion.}
The ready claim is narrow.  On a yeast panel with $1257$ joined genes, five held-out folds, $120$ deterministic permutations, and a rank-one $f3\_stress$ target component, positional dwell summaries distinguish the $5'$ segment from the $3'$ body segment: the $5'$ ramp passes and is the leading segment, while the $3'$ body does not pass.  The result is a finite cross-layer readout about where the dwell-associated increment for the $f3\_stress$ abundance projection is located.  It does not assert that $f3\_stress$ causes translation changes, that the effect is universal, that the signal specifies folding or physical admissibility, or that it realizes molecular function.  Any such statement would require a separate layer-matched measurement and a new controlled statistic for that layer.
