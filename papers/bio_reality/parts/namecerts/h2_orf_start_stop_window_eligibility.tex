\paragraph{Contact requirement.}
The two boundary probes state the local admissibility rule.  A start--stop window can remain in the record as a proposed eligibility test only while it is paired with, or waiting for, an external sequence, transcript, or curated interval annotation capable of testing the interval.  Without such contact, the packet is a boundary question rather than an ORF claim.  With such contact, the outcome still remains at \texttt{orf\_eligibility}; it does not become a claim about translation, folding, physical admissibility, function, mechanism, or universality.
