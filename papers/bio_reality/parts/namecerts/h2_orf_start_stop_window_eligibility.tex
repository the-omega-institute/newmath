\paragraph{Local non-promotion rule.}
The decisive boundary is local and non-negotiable. Start--stop enumeration may classify supplied windows as ORF-eligible under the frozen rule, and the curated interval contact may support that classifier on its finite rows. Neither result entails translation realization, protein folding, thermodynamic or kinetic admissibility, organismal function, molecular mechanism, or universality across organisms and datasets. Those higher layers require their own separate contacts, such as transcript evidence, translation evidence, protein evidence, physical measurement, functional assay, or a broader independently sampled biological table. In their absence, the present record remains an ORF-window eligibility contact, not a biological completion theorem.
