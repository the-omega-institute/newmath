\subsection{Start--Stop Window Eligibility for Open Reading Frame Screening}
\label{sec:h2-orf-start-stop-window-eligibility}

\paragraph{Object of the record.}
This record concerns only the finite screening question obtained by enumerating nucleotide windows with start and stop codon boundaries.  The biological object is open-reading-frame eligibility at the window level: a sequence interval is admitted to the screening class when the recorded finite input satisfies the declared start/stop boundary rule.  The object is deliberately weaker than translation, weaker than a folded molecule, weaker than physical realizability, and weaker than biological function.  Its purpose is to keep the row-level enumeration separate from every higher biological layer unless another curated contact supplies the missing biological interval evidence.

\paragraph{Local boundary.}
The self-check row records three checked gates and states that no codon geometry is promoted across biological layers.  This chapter therefore makes the following local boundary explicit.  Start/stop-window enumeration may record ORF eligibility only for the finite windows or curated intervals actually supplied to the experiment.  It does not show that a ribosome translated the interval, that a protein folded, that the molecule is physically admissible, that the interval has a function, that a causal mechanism has been identified, or that the classifier is universal.  Each of those stronger assertions would require a separate contact with evidence appropriate to that layer.
