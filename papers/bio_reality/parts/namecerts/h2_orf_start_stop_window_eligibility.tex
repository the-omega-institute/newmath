\paragraph{Boundary rule.}
The local claim is therefore intentionally weak.  A start/stop-window enumeration may say that the one supplied window was classified by the enumeration rule, and it may record the layer name $\mathrm{orf\_eligibility\_only}$.  It may not say that translation occurred, that a protein structure exists, that the interval is physically admissible, that it performs a biological function, that a molecular mechanism has been established, or that a universal biological law follows.  Those stronger readings require their own contacts and tests.  In particular, codon geometry alone is insufficient for promotion beyond ORF eligibility.
