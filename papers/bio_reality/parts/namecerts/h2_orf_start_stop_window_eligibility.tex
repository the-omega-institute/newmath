\paragraph{Layer boundary.}
The finite rows justify a narrow statement: a supplied nucleotide window or curated interval can be assessed by the start/stop-window eligibility rule inside the declared manifest.  They do not justify translation realization, folded molecular structure, thermodynamic or kinetic admissibility, cellular function, biochemical mechanism, organismal effect, or universality across unobserved sequence populations.  Those stronger claims would require a different contact that observes the appropriate layer.  A sequence or annotation contact can support interval eligibility; a transcript contact would be needed for expression; translation evidence would be needed for realized peptide production; structure evidence would be needed for folding; physical measurements would be needed for admissibility; and biological-function assays would be needed for function.  The present record keeps these demands separate.

The result is not a free-standing biological mechanism; it is a contact-limited ORF eligibility record whose force ends exactly at the layer named in the experiment.
