\subsection{Position-resolved dwell localization for the $f_3$-stress abundance component}
\label{sec:bioreality-f3-stress-dwell-positional-ramp-body}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Claim surface.}
This record tests whether the $f_3$-stress coordinate in the $B^*_{Q6}$ residual basis has a positionally localized dwell contribution to its measured protein-abundance component.  The finite surface is restricted to \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}, with the target fixed as the projection $P_{f3}$ of $Z$-residualized $
\log_{10}$ protein abundance onto the $Z$-residualized $f_3$-stress coordinate on the active base-plus-positional-dwell join.  The control rank is nontrivial: the control basis has rank $24$, the tested $f_3$ source has rank $1$, the fitted base size is $1800$, and the active joined positional-dwell panel contains $1257$ genes.  The readout is not a global ribosome-dynamics object; it is a pair of per-gene relative ribosome-occupancy summaries computed from file-provided $5'$ head-window and $3'$ tail-window A-site occupancy rows.

\paragraph{Finite statistic.}
The experiment separates two segment designs, one for the $5'$ ramp/head window and one for the $3'$ body/tail window, and compares each against a deterministic row-label permutation null for $P_{f3}$ with the base design, full design, and five cross-validation folds held fixed.  The registered run used $120$ permutations with seed \texttt{sha256:b\_star\_q6\_f3\_stress\_dwell\_positional\_powered:deterministic}.  The observed $f_3$ abundance component has squared norm
$$
\begin{aligned}
\|P_{f3}\|^2 &= 44.20095750389323,\\
\frac{\|P_{f3}\|^2}{\|P_{\mathrm{target}}\|^2} &= 0.1999063916718794 .
\end{aligned}
$$
The run status is \texttt{passed}: the leading segment by held-out increment is the $5'$ segment, the dominant segment label is \texttt{5prime}, the $5'$ ramp gate passed, and the $3'$ body gate did not pass.  In finite-row terms, the result is a localized positive dwell increment for the $f_3$ abundance component, not a two-sided positional equivalence between head and tail windows.

\paragraph{Readback.}
The chapter-level readback is therefore asymmetric.  The $5'$ window carries the registered positional increment, while the $3'$ window remains below the same pass rule.  This supports the narrower statement that at least one positional occupancy window contributes held-out predictive information for the $f_3$-aligned abundance component after the base controls.  It does not support the stronger statement that body-window dwell is positive, that the $f_3$ coordinate is globally position-localized across every window, or that the $5'$ signal alone identifies a molecular mechanism.

The companion non-positional dwell run fixes the scale of the same object.  On the yeast active join with $n=1257$, five folds, rank $1$ source, and $Z$-basis rank $24$, the base model has held-out $R^2=0.1855578757644517$, dwell alone has $R^2=0.034336580953121976$, and the full model has $R^2=0.20412261520502883$.  The resulting held-out increment is
$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta R^2_{\mathrm{dwell}} &= 0.01856473944057712,\\
\mathrm{Null}_{95} &= 0.0013115362189692937,\\
\mathrm{Null}_{\max} &= 0.005001350039772926,\\
\mathrm{Null}_{\mathrm{mean}} &= -0.0032911642620130563,\\
\mathrm{Null}_{\min} &= -0.013799622026666476.
\end{aligned}
$$
The observed $f_3$--dwell correlation is $0.1615441450901653$ with approximate $p=7.873916057196935\times 10^{-9}$.  These numbers establish that the positional run is not an isolated label: it is a refinement of a positive dwell-mediated abundance component, then split into head and tail occupancy summaries.

\paragraph{Relation to ramp/body structural-order evidence.}
A separate structural-order ramp/body computation gives useful context but does not replace the dwell statistic.  For the primary ramp length $50$, the weighted $f_3$ ramp held-out increment is $0.0011165640103326878$, the weighted $f_3$ body increment is $-0.00026157313923957926$, and the weighted ramp-minus-body increment is $0.001378137149572267$.  The sign-flip probabilities are
$$
\begin{aligned}
 p(\Delta R^2_{\mathrm{ramp}}>0) &= 0.0478515625,\\
 p(\Delta R^2_{\mathrm{body}}>0) &= 0.54296875,\\
 p(\Delta R^2_{\mathrm{ramp-body}}>0) &= 0.05859375 .
\end{aligned}
$$
That companion run is classified as \texttt{ramp\_positive\_but\_not\_localized}: the ramp signal is positive, but the paired ramp-body localization gate does not pass.  The present dwell-positional result is consequently sharper in one respect and narrower in another.  It is sharper because the $5'$ dwell window passes while the $3'$ window does not; it is narrower because the passed object is a positional ribosome-occupancy dwell readout for $P_{f3}$, not a structural-order conclusion.

\paragraph{Background survival scale.}
The yeast protein-abundance surface is large enough to make the positional claim non-vacuous.  In the direct protein-omics survival computation, yeast has $5815$ joined proteins with a codon/protein join hit rate of $0.9156038419146592$, all nine $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinates survive the positive threshold, and the joint direct protein-abundance statistic is $R^2_{QP}=0.29837624994140827$.  The measured ribosome-TE survival run gives yeast $5128$ genes with TE readouts, a ribosome-TE join hit rate of $0.8818572656921754$, all nine coordinates surviving, and $R^2_{QT,\mathrm{meas}}=0.13681931343730436$.  These background entries show that the yeast rows have both protein-abundance and measured-translation contacts, but they do not by themselves prove the positional dwell claim.  The positional claim rests on the $1257$-row dwell window join and its segment-specific permutation comparison.

\paragraph{Reduction boundary.}
The related readout-dictionary computation reports that the yeast abundance residual is partly reducible to the measured readout family \texttt{['measured\_te', 'mrna\_stability', 'turnover']}.  In that run the active join has $n=1800$, five folds, source rank inherited from the nine-dimensional $B^*_{Q6}$ design, $\lambda_{\mathrm{DL}}=0.01$, $\rho_{\mathrm{join}}=0.05$, raw best coverage $0.2745002077400602$, selection-aware null $95\%$ level $0.029149007242956115$, corrected coverage $C^*=0.24538211439730545$, and unexplained fraction $0.7546178856026946$.  Thus the positional dwell result should be read as one finite component in a partially reducible protein-abundance residual, not as a complete reduction of the $B^*_{Q6}$ signal.

\paragraph{Admissible conclusion.}
The admissible conclusion is finite and local: in the registered yeast panel, the $f_3$-stress-aligned abundance component has a positive positional dwell increment led by the $5'$ occupancy window, with the $3'$ body window failing the corresponding pass condition.  The result is a cross-layer relation between a synonymous residual coordinate, measured positional ribosome-occupancy summaries, and the $f_3$ projection of residualized protein abundance.  It is not a causal mediation effect, not a universal translation law, not a folding or structural-order proof, not a physical-admissibility assay, and not a molecular-function or phenotype claim.  Any such stronger interpretation would require a separate layer-matched contact and its own finite statistic; the present rows only certify the bounded positional-dwell readback described above.
