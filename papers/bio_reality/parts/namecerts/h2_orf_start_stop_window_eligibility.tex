\subsection{Start--stop window eligibility as a finite biological boundary record}
\label{sec:bioreality-orf-start-stop-window-eligibility}

\paragraph{Object of the record.}
This chapter isolates a single finite assertion about open-reading-frame eligibility: a nucleotide window may be sorted by the presence of an admissible start boundary and a compatible stop boundary, but that sorting is only a window-level eligibility predicate. The biological object is not protein production, not a folded molecule, not a physically admissible molecular trajectory, and not organism-level function. The finite experiment for claim $\mathrm{h2.orf.start\_stop.window\_eligibility}$ loaded one curated nucleotide-window manifest, with $\mathrm{window\_count}=1$, $\mathrm{input\_windows}=1$, and $\mathrm{classified}=1$. Its declared scope is external window start--stop enumeration only, and its recorded classification label is $\mathrm{orf\_eligibility\_only}$. Thus the row supports only the claim that the supplied window can be placed in the start/stop-window eligibility class under the frozen enumeration rule.

\paragraph{Boundary predicate.}
For the present row, the admissible mathematical content is the following finite implication. Given the supplied curated window manifest, the frozen start/stop rule assigns every supplied row to its start/stop-window class; here the entire manifest consists of one row and that row is classified. Formally, the experiment supports the finite statement
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{loaded}=1,\quad \mathrm{input\_windows}=1,\quad \mathrm{classified}=1
\quad \Longrightarrow \quad
\mathrm{all\ supplied\ windows\ receive\ an\ eligibility\ label}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The implication is deliberately conditional on the supplied manifest. It does not quantify over all genomes, all transcripts, all organisms, or all possible nucleotide strings.

\paragraph{Contact discipline.}
The two local probes state the negative side of the same rule. A start/stop-window seed remains a boundary test until a separate biological interval contact is attached. In the present row, the source is a curated finite nucleotide-window manifest with snapshot date $2026$-$05$-$25$, and the source text explicitly says that the records are external annotation inputs to the experiment, not facts derived from the codon geometry alone. Therefore the enumeration may confirm that an input window has the prescribed boundary pattern, but it may not certify translation, folded structure, physical admissibility, molecular mechanism, biological function, or a universal law.

\paragraph{Scope of the conclusion.}
The linked contact check shows that the frozen start/stop-window classifier can agree perfectly with the supplied curated interval dataset and matched decoys under the recorded protocol. It still does not move the present claim beyond ORF-eligibility classification. The local self-check records three checked gates and states that no codon geometry is promoted across biological layers. Consequently, the chapter-level conclusion is narrow: the finite data establish one classified supplied window for start/stop-window eligibility, situated against explicit codon sets and a finite quotient bookkeeping row, with a linked curated-interval contact showing $168$ rows of stronger interval evidence. Nothing in these rows establishes translation realization, folding, physical admissibility, biological function, mechanistic causation, or universality without another contact designed for that higher biological layer.
