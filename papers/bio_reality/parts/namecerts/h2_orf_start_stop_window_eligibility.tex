\paragraph{Local admissible conclusion.}
The admissible conclusion is therefore narrow but nonempty.  In the finite start/stop-window row, the one supplied window was classified by the enumeration rule, and the recorded scope is explicitly $\mathrm{external\_window\_start\_stop\_enumeration\_only}$.  In the linked curated-interval contact, the same style of classifier was tested on a finite external CDS interval dataset with $168$ rows, including positives, matched decoys, and boundary cases.  The strongest local evidence is the simultaneous perfect acceptance of $72$ positive curated ORFs, perfect rejection of $72$ matched decoys, passing stability across three held-out folds, and non-promotion of $24$ boundary cases.  The self-check row records $3$ checked gates and states that no codon geometry is promoted across biological layers.

\paragraph{Boundary of what is not established.}
Nothing in this packet establishes translation realization, folded protein structure, physical admissibility, biochemical activity, organismal role, mechanism, or a universal biological law.  The start/stop rule is an eligibility filter over supplied finite windows and curated intervals.  It remains dependent on the attached biological contact for any statement about an observed interval, and even that contact only supports the stated $\mathrm{orf\_eligibility}$ layer.  A later claim about translation, folding, physical behavior, function, mechanism, or generality would require its own independent contact and its own finite audit record.
