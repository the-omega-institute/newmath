The present claim is ready only at the stated eligibility level. It has one external window, one start/stop enumeration classification, a loaded finite manifest, and a recorded refusal to promote geometry into translation. The supporting finite codon tables give $|M|=20$, $|R|=13$, $|\pi_M|=10$, the face vectors $(20,38,26,8,1)$ and $(10,14,6,1)$, and an empty $f_3$ boundary row.
