\paragraph{Local non-promotion boundary.}
The present start--stop window row and the curated-interval row have different evidential roles.  The one-window row certifies only that a supplied external manifest row was enumerated by start and stop codons.  The $168$-row curated contact supplies a stronger external biological comparison, but even that stronger row explicitly refuses promotion to translation, protein structure, physical admissibility, biological function, a mechanism of action, or a universal biological law.  No codon geometry, quotient vector, saturation list, start codon, stop codon, interval endpoint, decoy rejection rate, held-out split, or binomial value changes that boundary.  Such conclusions require an additional contact of the corresponding kind.  In particular, translation would require evidence of translation, folding would require structural evidence, physical admissibility would require a physical model or measurement, function would require functional observation, mechanism would require causal or process-level evidence, and universality would require a scope-expanding biological survey.

The finite record is therefore ready as an ORF-eligibility statement with a narrow interpretation.  It may say that the supplied external nucleotide window was classified by start--stop enumeration, and it may be compared with the separate $168$-row curated interval contact whose positives, decoys, boundary cases, and held-out splits are numerically specified.  It may not treat the coordinate facts $M$, $R$, saturation, or quotient geometry as biological realization.  The mathematically stable content is a finite external-window eligibility classification plus an explicit refusal to cross into translation, folding, physical admissibility, function, mechanism, or universal biology without further observation.
