\paragraph{Local exclusion of stronger biological claims.}
The finite row explicitly blocks promotion from start/stop-window enumeration to translation.  The same exclusion must be read more broadly: no folded structure, no physical admissibility, no molecular mechanism, no organismal function, and no universal biological law follows from this row.  A start codon and stop codon can delimit a candidate interval, but they do not by themselves show that a transcript exists, that a ribosome traverses the interval, that a peptide is produced, that the peptide folds, that the fold is physically realized in a cell, or that the product has a biological role.  The self-check row reinforces the same point with checked gate count $3$ and boundary text stating that no codon geometry is promoted across biological layers.
