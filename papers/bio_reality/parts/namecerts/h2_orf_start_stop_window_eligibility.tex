The verified run $\mathrm{c26a0d6656f7}$ loaded one curated finite nucleotide-window record, used the snapshot dated $2026$-$05$-$25$, and classified all loaded windows by start/stop enumeration:

The source statement for this row is a curated finite nucleotide-window manifest for BioReality ORF start/stop enumeration. The records are external annotation inputs to the experiment. They are not produced by the codon-coordinate geometry alone.

\paragraph{Layer discipline.}
The verified scope is $\mathrm{external\_window\_start\_stop\_enumeration\_only}$ for the one-window run and $\mathrm{external\_orf\_interval\_contact\_only}$ for the curated-interval run. The recorded promotion field is $\mathrm{orf\_eligibility\_only}$. Consequently, the chapter makes no claim that the accepted intervals are translated in vivo, no claim that any protein fold is realized, no claim of physical admissibility, no claim of biological function, no mechanism claim, and no universal biological law. Such stronger assertions would require a different contact supplying the relevant kind of observation. The start/stop enumeration is a finite eligibility test on externally supplied interval records; it is not a route from codon geometry to translation, structure, function, mechanism, or universality.
