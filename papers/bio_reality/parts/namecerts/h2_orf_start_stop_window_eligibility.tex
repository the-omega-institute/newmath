\paragraph{Local boundary on interpretation.}
The permissible conclusion is narrow. A finite external window was loaded, and every loaded window in that one-row manifest was classified by the start--stop enumeration procedure. That supports only ORF-window eligibility within the stated external-window scope. It does not establish that translation occurred; it does not establish a folded protein, a thermodynamically or physically admissible molecule, a biochemical function, a causal mechanism, or a universal biological law. Those stronger readings require their own contact records with the relevant biological measurements or curated annotations. Codon lists, quotient vectors, saturation rows, and start--stop-window enumeration are useful finite controls, but none of them by themselves supplies transcript evidence, ribosome engagement, protein abundance, structure, activity, phenotype, or organism-level universality.
