\subsection{Position-resolved dwell localization for the $f_3$ stress abundance component}
\label{sec:bioreality-f3-stress-dwell-positional-ramp-body}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite-row question.}
This record tests whether the $f_3$ stress coordinate in the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual basis has a position-localized ribosome-dwell readback against the protein-abundance component aligned with that coordinate.  The carrier is a finite joined surface over synonymous residual coordinates, control-residualized protein abundance, and position-separated ribosome occupancy summaries in \textit{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}.  The tested target is not total protein abundance and not total translation efficiency.  It is the one-dimensional component
$P_{f_3}$ obtained by projecting $Z$-residualized $\log_{10}$ protein abundance onto the $Z$-residualized $f_3$ stress axis on the active positional-dwell join.  The control basis has rank $24$, the tested $f_3$ source has rank $1$, and the active finite join contains $1257$ genes inside a base design size of $1800$ rows.

\paragraph{Readouts and controls.}
The dwell readout consists of per-gene relative ribosome occupancy summaries computed separately from file-provided $5'$ head windows and $3'$ tail windows.  The same base and full designs are kept fixed while the target component $P_{f_3}$ is permuted by deterministic row-label shuffling.  The positional test uses $5$ folds and $120$ permutations for the segment-specific and joint-segment designs.  The run identifier is \texttt{6db78e1bf746}; the deterministic seed is \texttt{sha256:b\_star\_q6\_f3\_stress\_dwell\_positional\_powered:deterministic}.  The measured target component has
$$
\begin{aligned}
\lVert P_{f_3}\rVert^2 &= 44.20095750389323,\\
\frac{\lVert P_{f_3}\rVert^2}{\lVert P_{\mathrm{target}}\rVert^2} &= 0.1999063916718794.
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the positional analysis is performed on a nonzero abundance component rather than on a vacuous fitted direction.

\paragraph{Segment decision.}
The positional result passes on the $5'$ segment and does not pass on the $3'$ segment.  The leading segment by held-out increment is $5'$; the registered dominant segment is also $5'$.  In the finite-row decision table this is recorded as
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{five\_prime\_ramp\_passed} &= \mathrm{true},\\
\mathrm{three\_prime\_body\_passed} &= \mathrm{false},\\
\mathrm{leading\_segment\_by\_delta\_r^2} &= 5',\\
\mathrm{dominant\_segment} &= 5'.
\end{aligned}
$$
The scientific content is therefore a localized dwell increment: at least one positional window supplies held-out improvement above the fixed permutation boundary, and the active window is the $5'$ head/ramp side rather than the $3'$ body/tail side.

\paragraph{Relation to the unsegmented dwell fit.}
The unsegmented dwell mediation run provides the scalar context for the same $f_3$ abundance component.  On $1257$ joined yeast rows, with the same $P_{f_3}$ norm and fraction, the base model has held-out $R^2=0.1855578757644517$, the dwell-alone model has $R^2=0.034336580953121976$, and the full model has $R^2=0.20412261520502883$.  The dwell increment is
$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta R^2_{\mathrm{held\ out}} &= 0.01856473944057712,\\
\mathrm{Null}_{0.95} &= 0.0013115362189692937,\\
\mathrm{Null}_{\max} &= 0.005001350039772926,\\
\mathrm{Null}_{\mathrm{mean}} &= -0.0032911642620130563.
\end{aligned}
$$
The observed $f_3$--dwell correlation is $0.1615441450901653$, with approximate $p=7.873916057196935\times 10^{-9}$.  This unsegmented result says that dwell carries a reproducible increment for the $f_3$-aligned abundance component; the positional run localizes that increment to the $5'$ side of the dwell surface.

\paragraph{Ramp-body comparison.}
A companion ramp/body positional check gives the numerical direction of the same localization.  At primary ramp length $50$, the weighted $f_3$ ramp increment is $0.0011165640103326878$, while the weighted $f_3$ body increment is $-0.00026157313923957926$.  The ramp-minus-body contrast is $0.001378137149572267$.  The one-sided sign-flip probabilities are $0.0478515625$ for the ramp signal, $0.54296875$ for the body signal, and $0.05859375$ for the paired ramp-minus-body contrast.  The finite-row classification is therefore \texttt{ramp\_positive\_but\_not\_localized}: the ramp side is positive, but the stricter paired localization gate does not pass.  This is consistent with the main dwell-position result, but it is weaker than a universal positional law.

\paragraph{Cross-layer placement.}
The surrounding measured-TE and abundance facts keep the interpretation bounded.  In yeast, measured ribosome TE survival for the nine $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ coordinates has $R^2_{QT,\mathrm{meas}}=0.13681931343730436$ on $5128$ genes with a ribosome-TE join hit rate of $0.8818572656921754$.  The measured TE to protein-abundance decomposition in yeast reports $S_{QP}=0.3146818503437042$, $S_{QT,\mathrm{meas}}=0.13681931343730436$, and a measured mediation fraction $0.4793069656345167$ on $5128$ three-way joined rows.  These values justify treating measured translation readouts as relevant covariates in the yeast surface, but they do not turn a dwell-position increment into a causal mechanism.

\paragraph{Reduction status.}
The broader yeast residual dictionary places the result in a partially reducible, not fully explained, region.  With $\lambda_{\mathrm{dl}}=0.01$, $\rho_{\mathrm{join}}=0.05$, $5$ folds, $120$ permutations, $1800$ joined rows, and $4758$ available joined rows, the readout dictionary covers $C^{\ast}=0.24538211439730545$ of the source signal and leaves $0.7546178856026946$ unexplained.  The selection-aware null $95$ percent boundary is $0.029149007242956115$, while the naive fixed-subset null $95$ percent boundary is $0.00598374993486675$.  The irreducibility certificate run on the same yeast context uses the same $\lambda_{\mathrm{dl}}=0.01$, $\rho_{\mathrm{join}}=0.05$, $5$ folds, $120$ permutations, $1800$ joined rows, and $4758$ available rows; it records that the registered claim was tested and that at least one listed reduction family refutes the original irreducibility hypothesis.  The correct reading is partial reduction with a large residual remainder, not a proof that $f_3$ stress is primitive in every biological layer.

\paragraph{Local conclusion.}
The finite-row verdict is positive for a position-resolved dwell increment.  The $f_3$-aligned abundance component in yeast has a reproducible dwell contribution, and the segment-specific decision assigns the leading contribution to the $5'$ head/ramp window while the $3'$ tail/body window does not pass.  The result supports a bounded statement: on this yeast joined surface, $f_3$ stress is linked to a position-separated ribosome-occupancy dwell readout for the $P_{f_3}$ abundance component.  It does not by itself establish translation causality, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, phenotype, evolutionary direction, or a universal biological rule.  Those claims would require separate matched contacts and finite-row statistics at the corresponding layer.
