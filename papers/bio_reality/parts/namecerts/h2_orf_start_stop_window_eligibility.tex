\subsection{Joint measured-TE and mRNA-stability mediation for $B^*_{Q6}$ abundance residuals}
\label{sec:bioreality-q6-joint-te-stability-mediation}

\paragraph{Finite surface.}
This record tests whether the $B^*_{Q6}$ synonymous residual surface reaches measured protein abundance through two explicitly named intermediate readouts: ribosome-derived translation efficiency and mRNA stability.  The finite carrier is the matrix of nine residual coordinates
$K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $Leu_{CUN\,vs\,UUR}$, $Leu_{UUA\,vs\,UUG}$, $Ser_{UCR\,vs\,AGY}$, $Ser_{UCA\,vs\,UCG}$, $Thr_{ACR\,vs\,ACY}$, and $f3\_stress$, residualized together with protein abundance $P$, measured translation efficiency $T$, and mRNA stability $M$ against the same control family.  The control family contains an intercept, $\log(\mathrm{CDS\ length})$, the twenty amino-acid composition proportions, $GC3$, and the $M$-density baseline over the union support of the nine projected coordinates.  The computation was run on three joined organism panels: \emph{Escherichia coli} K-12 MG1655 with $2517$ four-way rows, human with $11322$ four-way rows, and budding yeast with $4759$ four-way rows.

\paragraph{Joined readout provenance.}
The bacterial panel joined RefSeq CDS rows to Li--Burkhardt--Gross--Weissman ribosome profiling and mRNA data with reported CDS hit rate $0.9978649586335735$ and TE join hit rate $0.9133458143888741$; its TE value is mean CDS footprint coverage divided by mean CDS mRNA coverage on the annotated coding strand.  The human panel joined $19053$ reported CDS rows to $11554$ TE genes and $19480$ mRNA consensus records, with CDS hit rate $0.9779294769799313$ and TE join hit rate $0.6064136881331024$; its TE readout is the positive RPFdb CDS translation-efficiency value from the GSE56887 processed table, averaged across positive columns per Ensembl gene.  The yeast panel joined $5815$ reported CDS rows to $5128$ TE genes and $5379$ mRNA half-life records, with CDS hit rate $0.9156038419146592$ and TE join hit rate $0.8818572656921754$; its TE readout is mean footprint normalized read density divided by mean mRNA normalized read density across the rich-medium Ingolia replicates.

\paragraph{Rank and residual diagnostics.}
After control residualization, all three organism panels retained the full nine-dimensional $Q$ rank, with $\operatorname{rank}(Q)=9$, $\operatorname{rank}(T)=1$, $\operatorname{rank}(M)=1$, and joint $\operatorname{rank}(Q,T,M)=11$.  The residual degrees of freedom after controls were $2494$ in \emph{E. coli}, $11299$ in human, and $4736$ in yeast.  The residual protein energies were $2323.813305213946$, $12615.307912483846$, and $1724.6664873071313$, respectively.  The translation residual energies were $672.36799431777$, $1866.9940287020145$, and $645.3764418081507$; the mRNA-stability residual energies were $198.6753916649337$, $647.920297431553$, and $194.99141167236996$.  These numbers rule out the rank-degenerate all-entry readback: the surface is evaluated on powered within-organism joins, not on an undersized three-organism saturated table.

\paragraph{Variance decomposition.}
The measured quantity is the residual association of $Q$ with $P$ before and after conditioning on $T$, on $M$, and on both.  The computation uses
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{QP} &= R^2(P_e \sim Q_e),\\
R^2_{QP\mid T} &= R^2(P_e \sim Q_e \mid T_e),\\
R^2_{QP\mid M} &= R^2(P_e \sim Q_e \mid M_e),\\
R^2_{QP\mid T,M} &= R^2(P_e \sim Q_e \mid T_e,M_e),\\
\mathrm{joint\ fraction} &= \frac{R^2_{QP}-R^2_{QP\mid T,M}}{R^2_{QP}}.
\end{aligned}
$$
For \emph{E. coli}, the list is $R^2_{QP}=0.1181743523064932$, $R^2_{QP\mid T}=0.11583979275713331$, $R^2_{QP\mid M}=0.09093273405121653$, and $R^2_{QP\mid T,M}=0.08975732521542036$.  For human, the corresponding list is $0.04274178488511215$, $0.04250692268197657$, $0.036196017359154814$, and $0.0355223930537993$.  For yeast, the list is $0.2715520808117803$, $0.1439989545940314$, $0.25453612246767165$, and $0.13047582645538838$.  Thus the joint fraction is $0.2404669586626661$ in \emph{E. coli}, $0.16890712099923358$ in human, and $0.5195182225621592$ in yeast.

\paragraph{Single-mediator readback.}
The TE-only component is small in \emph{E. coli} and human, at $0.01975521340963268$ and $0.00549490864190343$, but large in yeast, at $0.4697188319693239$.  The mRNA-stability-only component is $0.23052056324898387$ in \emph{E. coli}, $0.15314679870183337$ in human, and $0.0626618595344251$ in yeast.  The overlap diagnostic classifies all three panels as \texttt{approximately\_independent}: the joint-minus-single-sum values are $-0.009808817995950458$, $0.010265413655496769$, and $-0.012862468941589822$, each within the fixed tolerance $0.05$.  The deterministic right-tail permutation value is $p=0.000999000999000999$ for each organism-specific joint mediator pair.  In words, the finite rows support a statistical split: yeast is dominated by measured TE absorption, while \emph{E. coli} and human are dominated by the mRNA-stability side of the same variance partition.

\paragraph{Relation to measured-TE and direct protein-abundance surfaces.}
The same coordinate family has a measured-TE survival surface in four organisms: $R^2_{QT,meas}=0.009394266754822262$ in \emph{E. coli}, $0.018837911344127926$ in human, $0.13681931343730436$ in yeast, and $0.04034217614654355$ in zebrafish, with all nine coordinates surviving the positive threshold in each panel.  The direct protein-omics surface is also nonzero across a broader protein-abundance panel; examples include $R^2_{QP}=0.10767526677985896$ in \emph{E. coli}, $0.04454565118019448$ in human, $0.29837624994140827$ in yeast, $0.049827984062539195$ in mouse, and $0.06397514251295629$ in \emph{Sulfolobus solfataricus}, again with nine surviving coordinates.  The joint mediation row is therefore not a discovery of a new coordinate basis.  It is the stricter conditioning test that asks how much of an already present $Q \to P$ residual association remains once measured TE and mRNA stability are allowed to absorb it.

\paragraph{Four-case interpretation.}
The four-case discriminant supplies a bounded companion classification on modeled-tAI and measured-abundance rows.  In the two panels where that finite discriminant was computed, \emph{E. coli} had seven case-D coordinates and two positive-positive coordinates without a mediation drop, while yeast had nine case-D coordinates.  Here, case-D should be read only as the local pattern in which both translation-readout and protein-abundance signals are present with the specified conditioning behavior; it is not a biochemical mechanism label.  The measured-mediation calculation using ribosome-derived TE reports measured mediated fractions $0.04091158340950673$ in \emph{E. coli}, $0.0027966485036786744$ in human, $0.4793069656345167$ in yeast, and $0.0$ in zebrafish after truncating a negative raw fraction.  These finite rows agree with the joint TE--stability record in the main qualitative point: measured TE is a strong absorber in yeast, weak in human, and partial in \emph{E. coli}; mRNA stability supplies an additional distinct absorber in the three-organism joint panel.

\paragraph{Dictionary and irreducibility boundary.}
A separate yeast readout-dictionary certificate tested whether the residual abundance signal can be compressed by a small measured-readout family.  On $1800$ joined yeast rows, with $5$ folds, $\lambda\_{DL}=0.01$, $\rho\_{join}=0.05$, and $120$ deterministic permutations, the best selected family from \texttt{measured\_te}, \texttt{mrna\_stability}, and \texttt{turnover} covered $C^*=0.24538211439730545$ of the residual signal.  The selection-aware null $95$th percentile was $0.029149007242956115$, the naive fixed-subset null $95$th percentile was $0.00598374993486675$, and the unexplained fraction remained $0.7546178856026946$.  This places the present chapter between two extremes: the residual is not erased by the named readouts, but the positive coverage is large enough that the direct protein-omics association cannot be presented as primitive or mechanism-free.

\paragraph{Local verdict.}
The finite-row verdict is ready at the level of statistical cross-layer decomposition.  The record supports the following bounded statement: in powered joined panels, $B^*\_{Q6}$ residual protein-abundance association is partly absorbed by measured TE and mRNA stability, with organism-specific weights and a significant deterministic pair-permutation readback.  It does not establish a causal path, a universal translation law, protein folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, phenotype, or a general mechanism.  A translation claim beyond the named measured-TE readout would require an additional contact at that layer; any folding, physical, functional, perturbational, or universal claim would require its own separate measurement surface and its own finite statistic.
