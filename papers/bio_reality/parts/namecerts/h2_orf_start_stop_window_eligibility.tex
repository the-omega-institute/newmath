\paragraph{Layer boundary.}
The conclusion is deliberately local. The finite rows justify only start--stop-window eligibility relative to the frozen classifier and the external interval records just described. No conclusion about translation, folding, physical admissibility, biological function, biochemical mechanism, organism-wide law, or universal genomic behavior follows from the codon geometry, from the one-window enumeration run, or from the $168$-row curated interval contact. Such stronger statements would require their own reality contacts and their own finite tests. In particular, the start--stop window should not be read as a translated product, a stable three-dimensional structure, a feasible physical molecule, or an explanation of biological role. The verified content is exactly the bounded ORF-eligibility classification supplied by finite annotation rows and finite start--stop enumeration.
