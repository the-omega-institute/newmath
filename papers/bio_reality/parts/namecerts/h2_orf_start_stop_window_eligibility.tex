\paragraph{Finite codon base.}
The ORF-eligibility packet concerns a narrow biological question: whether a finite nucleotide window is eligible under explicit start/stop enumeration. The input is not treated as a translated molecule, a folded product, a physically realized species, or a functional gene product. It is only an enumerated window whose status is checked against external annotation data. The codon-side finite support used by this packet is fixed by the verified motif set
$$
\begin{aligned}
R = \{&\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\\
&\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UUA}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The corresponding medium set has cardinality $20$ and is exactly
$$
\begin{aligned}
M = \{&\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{ACA},\mathrm{ACG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\\
&\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\\
&\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The compression audit records no missing and no extra motif entries for $R$. The equality audit records $M = \mathrm{WNR} \cup \mathrm{CUN}$ with size $20$. Thus the codon alphabet used here is a finite enumerated object, not an inferred biological process.

\paragraph{Window enumeration contact.}
The direct start/stop-window audit loaded one curated finite nucleotide-window manifest dated $2026$--$05$--$25$. The record does not transform codon geometry into a claim of translation, protein structure, thermodynamic admissibility, molecular mechanism, phenotype, organismal role, or universal biological law.

The classifier id was \texttt{orf\_start\_stop\_window\_eligibility}; its rules were not modified after the protocol was fixed. The protocol was fixed at $2026$--$05$--$29\,19{:}11{:}13+00{:}00$, before the external evidence time $2026$--$05$--$30\,10{:}29{:}31+00{:}00$. Given the enumerated codon support, the one-window start/stop manifest, and the curated interval contact, the packet supports ORF-window eligibility only for the supplied finite contact regime. Its strongest statistical entries are exact finite counts: $72/72$ positives accepted, $72/72$ matched decoys rejected, $72/72$ pairwise wins, three held-out splits each passing with $24$ positives and $24$ decoys, and $0/24$ boundary promotions.
