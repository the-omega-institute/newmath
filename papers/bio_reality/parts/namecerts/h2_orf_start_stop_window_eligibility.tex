\subsection{Per-coordinate reducibility of the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ abundance projection}
\label{sec:b-star-q6-per-coordinate-reducibility}

\paragraph{Question.}
The object tested here is the nine-coordinate synonymous residual system $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ after quotienting by amino-acid composition and after applying the shared covariate basis $Z$.  The finite question is not whether codon geometry is biologically universal, nor whether it realizes a biochemical mechanism.  It is narrower: after the common controls have been applied, can the abundance component carried by each one-dimensional residual coordinate be recovered from a fixed measured-readout dictionary on held-out yeast rows?  For each coordinate $x_i$, the target component is the orthogonal projection $P_i=\operatorname{Proj}_{x_i}(P)$ of the residualized protein-abundance target onto that coordinate.  The reported coverage statistic asks whether the fixed dictionary spans enough of $P_i$ to beat a deterministic target-label permutation boundary.

\paragraph{Finite design.}
The run used the Saccharomyces cerevisiae unified context join.  The base table contained $1800$ proteins, and the decomposition table was evaluated on $n=1258$ joined rows after the shared row restrictions.  The organism gate required at least $500$ proteins per organism.  The control basis had rank $24$ and used the same sibling covariates as the surrounding abundance computations: amino-acid composition, CDS length, GC3, and the $M$-density baseline.  The measured-readout dictionary had residual rank $\operatorname{rank}(D_e)=3$.  The source side was the nine-dimensional $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ synonymous residual design
$$
\begin{aligned}
Q=&\{\mathrm{K\_AAA},\mathrm{Arg\_AGR},\mathrm{Ile\_AUA},\mathrm{Leu\_CUN/UUR},\mathrm{Leu\_UUA/UUG},\\
&\mathrm{Ser\_UCR/AGY},\mathrm{Ser\_UCA/UCG},\mathrm{Thr\_ACR/ACY},f_3\text{-}\mathrm{stress}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The audit records $9$ computed rows in the per-coordinate table, with $5$ held-out folds and $120$ deterministic permutations.  The seed was fixed as \texttt{sha256:b\_star\_q6\_coordinate\_decomposition\_powered:deterministic}, so the fold and null construction are part of the finite row, not an informal description.

\paragraph{Test statistic.}
For each coordinate $x_i$, the single-coordinate projection $P_i$ was compared with its reconstruction from the residualized dictionary $D_e$.  The positive-source decision used held-out $R^2$ against a deterministic target-label permutation Null95 boundary with fixed folds and fixed design.  In compact form, the statistic is
$$
\begin{aligned}
P_i &= \operatorname{Proj}_{x_i}(P),\\
\widehat P_i &= \operatorname{Proj}_{D_e}(P_i),\\
\operatorname{Surv}_i &= \mathbf{1}\{R^2_{\mathrm{heldout}}(P_i,\widehat P_i)>\operatorname{Null95}_i\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The supplied row states that the full $9$-coordinate decomposition was produced and that at least one coordinate crossed this held-out/permutation gate as a real abundance source.  The coordinates listed as having a real source were
$$
\begin{aligned}
&\mathrm{K\_AAA},\ \mathrm{Arg\_AGR},\ \mathrm{Ile\_AUA},\ \mathrm{Leu\_CUN/UUR},\ \mathrm{Leu\_UUA/UUG},\\
&\mathrm{Ser\_UCR/AGY},\ \mathrm{Thr\_ACR/ACY},\ f_3\text{-}\mathrm{stress}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The coordinate $\mathrm{Ser\_UCA/UCG}$ is present in the nine-row table but is not in the recorded positive-source list.  Thus the finite result is a coordinate-resolved separation: the abundance-side $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ signal is not merely a single pooled projection, and the positive dictionary coverage is not uniformly assigned to every coordinate.

\paragraph{Relation to the surrounding abundance rows.}
The per-coordinate decomposition is consistent with the broader yeast abundance surface.  In the yeast translation-complement row, the same organism has $n=5815$ proteins, $\operatorname{rank}(Z)=23$, $R^2_{QP}=0.29837624987287115$, complement fraction $d=0.21173219572706703$, and modeled-translation-aligned fraction $m=0.788267804272933$.  The complementary support vector in that row is spread across $\mathrm{Ser\_UCR/AGY}$, $\mathrm{Leu\_CUN/UUR}$, $\mathrm{Ser\_UCA/UCG}$, $\mathrm{Arg\_AGR}$, $\mathrm{Thr\_ACR/ACY}$, and $f_3$-stress, with absolute support fractions summing to $1.0$.  The growth-condition abundance row also gives the same dominant coordinate in rich and minimal media: in Saccharomyces cerevisiae, $R^2_{QP}=0.3280825603424665$ in YEPD and $R^2_{QP}=0.3050385538305022$ in SD, with $f_3$-stress dominant in both and condition-vector similarity $0.9983880256003688$ by Pearson correlation and $0.9993775935182967$ by cosine.  These numbers do not prove the present held-out dictionary result, but they give a finite consistency check: the coordinate-level decomposition sits inside a nontrivial yeast abundance surface rather than inside a rank-empty design.

The mediation audit reports $R^2_{QP\mid M}=0.24851799532219632$, absorbed component $0.027596042074056304$, mediation fraction $0.09994436477872035$, permutation mean $0.00019674487584783138$, permutation Null95 $0.0014571003414947088$, and right-tail permutation $p=0.000999000999000999$. These rows are not substituted for the per-coordinate dictionary test; they show that several coordinate names appearing in the positive-source list also recur in independently bounded yeast readouts.

\paragraph{Stress-coordinate refinement.}
The $f_3$-stress coordinate is not treated as a featureless scalar in the attached finite rows.  Its family decomposition used a base table of $4759$ rows, an evaluated join of $1800$ rows, $5$ folds, $120$ permutations, and the same rank-$24$ control basis.  The full $f_3$ projection had norm squared $52.76911713166682$, accounting for $0.12259712041843235$ of the target norm.  The decomposition produced $21$ synonymous-family subcoordinates, with $19$ families listed as real sources under the held-out/permutation gate.  The positive families included $\mathrm{K\_AAA\to K\_AAG}$, $\mathrm{R\_AGA\to R\_AGG}$, $\mathrm{I\_AUU\to I\_AUA}$, $\mathrm{T\_ACU\to T\_ACG}$, and multiple two-codon terminal-position contrasts.  This supports reading the $f_3$ entry in the per-coordinate result as an internally decomposable abundance source, not as a label attached only to the aggregate coordinate.

\paragraph{Conclusion.}
The finite verdict is that the per-coordinate reducibility packet is positive at the stated bounded level.  The run produced all $9$ coordinate rows, used $5$ held-out folds and $120$ deterministic permutations, worked on a yeast join with $n=1258$ and a rank-$24$ covariate basis, and reported a positive-source list containing $8$ of the $9$ coordinates.  The result is therefore a coordinate-level abundance decomposition for $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ under the declared controls.  It does not assert that any coordinate directly causes translation, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, a biological mechanism, or a universal law.  Those stronger readings would require separate layer-matched contacts and their own controlled statistics.  Here the established claim is only that, in the supplied yeast finite row, the residual abundance projection contains coordinate-resolved components that survive the held-out measured-dictionary and permutation check.
