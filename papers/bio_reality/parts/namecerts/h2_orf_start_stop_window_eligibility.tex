\paragraph{Boundary of the claim.}
The present claim remains a contact-limited ORF-eligibility record.  Start and stop enumeration can classify the one supplied window within the manifest, and the broader curated-interval contact can be reported as a distinct finite observation with $168$ rows and strong matched controls.  Neither statement establishes translation realization, folded molecular structure, physical admissibility, biochemical function, mechanism, organism-level relevance, or a universal biological law.  Those stronger assertions would require an additional contact whose measurement type is appropriate to the asserted layer.  In particular, no conclusion about protein production follows from the one-window enumeration, no conclusion about folding follows from the codon sets $R$ and $M$, and no functional annotation follows from the exact binomial values in the curated-interval contact.  The self-check run recorded checked gate count $3$ and stated that no codon geometry is promoted across biological layers.  The scientific content is therefore deliberately narrow: a finite manifest row was classified at the ORF-eligibility boundary, while all higher biological meanings remain outside the local claim unless supplied by separate evidence.
