\paragraph{Local cannot-claim boundary.}
The permitted conclusion is narrow.  The one-row window run establishes only that an externally supplied finite nucleotide window was loaded and classified by start/stop enumeration.  The $168$-row curated interval contact supports the bounded $\mathsf{orf\_eligibility}$ classifier on its declared external interval dataset and controls.  Neither contact turns codon geometry into translation realization.  Neither contact proves folding, physical admissibility, biochemical mechanism, biological function, a universal law about genomes, or classification of windows outside the named datasets.  The self-check records three gates and states that no codon geometry is promoted across biological layers.  Any claim beyond window eligibility would require a further reality contact whose data type can test that higher layer directly.
