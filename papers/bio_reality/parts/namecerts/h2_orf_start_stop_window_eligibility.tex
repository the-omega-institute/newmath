\subsection{Positional dwell localization of the $f_3$ stress abundance component}
\label{sec:bioreality-f3-stress-dwell-positional-ramp-body}

\paragraph{Finite surface.}
This record tests whether the $f_3$ stress component of the $B^*_{Q6}$ synonymous residual basis has a position-specific ribosome-dwell signature in the same yeast rows used for the abundance-side projection.  The carrier is the finite matrix whose active rows are Saccharomyces cerevisiae coding sequences with codon counts, the sibling control design, measured protein abundance, and file-provided A-site occupancy summaries split into a $5'$ head window and a $3'$ tail or body window.  The response is not total abundance itself.  It is the one-dimensional projection $P_{f_3}$ of $Z$-residualized $\log_{10}$ protein abundance onto the $Z$-residualized $f_3$ coordinate on the active positional-dwell join.  The realized row count is $n=1257$ inside a base cap of $1800$ rows.  The sibling control basis has rank $24$, the tested residual coordinate has rank $1$, and the held-out comparison uses $5$ folds.

\paragraph{Measured quantities.}
The run reports
$$
\begin{aligned}
\|P_{f_3}\|^2 &= 44.20095750389323,\\
\frac{\|P_{f_3}\|^2}{\|P_{\mathrm{target}}\|^2} &= 0.1999063916718794,\\
 n_{\mathrm{join}} &= 1257,\\
 n_{\mathrm{base}} &= 1800,\\
 \operatorname{rank}(f_3) &= 1,\\
 \operatorname{rank}(Z) &= 24.
\end{aligned}
$$
The null model is a deterministic row-label permutation of $P_{f_3}$ with the base and full designs and the cross-validation folds held fixed.  It is run separately for each positional segment and for the joint segment design, with $120$ permutations under the seed \texttt{sha256:b\_star\_q6\_f3\_stress\_dwell\_positional\_powered:deterministic}.  The finite readout dictionary is therefore not a free biological narrative: it consists of two named positional dwell summaries, one for the $5'$ head region and one for the $3'$ body or tail region, evaluated against the same target projection and the same controlled row surface.

\paragraph{Positional verdict.}
The positional result is asymmetric.  The $5'$ segment passes the fixed permutation gate, while the $3'$ body segment does not:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{five\_prime\_ramp\_passed} &= \mathrm{true},\\
\mathrm{three\_prime\_body\_passed} &= \mathrm{false},\\
\mathrm{leading\_segment\_by\_delta\_R^2} &= 5',\\
\mathrm{dominant\_segment} &= 5'.
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the finite conclusion is not merely that dwell summaries can add held-out signal.  The signal is localized, within this joined yeast panel and this target projection, to the head-window readout.  The body-window readout is part of the tested alternative and is explicitly negative at the same gate.  This matters because a non-positional dwell association would allow the two windows to be interchangeable evidence for a generic ribosome-occupancy effect.  Here the registered finite outcome separates them: the leading increment is at the $5'$ ramp/head side, not in the $3'$ body side.

\paragraph{Relation to the non-positional dwell mediation run.}
The companion dwell-abundance computation on the same biological organism gives the scale of the effect before the positional split.  In that run the active join is again $n=1257$, the base cap is $1800$, the fold count is $5$, the same $P_{f_3}$ norm is $44.20095750389323$, and the same fractional target mass is $0.1999063916718794$.  The base model has held-out $R^2=0.1855578757644517$, the dwell-only model has $R^2=0.034336580953121976$, and the full model has $R^2=0.20412261520502883$.  The held-out dwell increment is
$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta R^2_{\mathrm{dwell}} &= 0.01856473944057712,\\
\mathrm{Null}_{95} &= 0.0013115362189692937,\\
\mathrm{Null}_{\max} &= 0.005001350039772926,\\
\mathrm{Null}_{\mathrm{mean}} &= -0.0032911642620130563,\\
\mathrm{Null}_{\min} &= -0.013799622026666476.
\end{aligned}
$$
The recorded $f_3$--dwell correlation in that non-positional run is $0.1615441450901653$, with approximate right-tail probability $7.873916057196935\times 10^{-9}$.  The positional run refines this finite statement: the generic dwell increment is not evenly assigned to all coding-sequence positions, and the registered lead is the $5'$ dwell segment.

\paragraph{Relation to ramp-versus-body structural-order testing.}
A separate ramp-versus-body structural-order experiment gives a useful boundary check but not an upgrade in claim type.  At primary ramp length $50$, the weighted $f_3$ ramp increment is $0.0011165640103326878$, while the weighted $f_3$ body increment is $-0.00026157313923957926$, so the ramp-minus-body difference is $0.001378137149572267$.  The sign-flip probabilities are $0.0478515625$ for positive ramp signal, $0.54296875$ for positive body signal, and $0.05859375$ for positive paired ramp-minus-body localization.  That experiment classifies the outcome as \texttt{ramp\_positive\_but\_not\_localized}: the ramp signal is positive, but the stronger paired localization gate does not pass.  The present dwell-position chapter should therefore be read narrowly.  It supports a $5'$-leading ribosome-dwell increment for the abundance projection target, not a general structural-order localization theorem.

\paragraph{Protein and translation context.}
The same yeast background has strong direct protein-omics survival in the broad $B^*_{Q6}$ surface: the PAXdb abundance join has $5815$ coding sequences, join hit rate $0.9156038419146592$, and direct $R^2_{QP}=0.29837624994140827$ with all $9$ residual coordinates surviving the positive partial-$R^2$ threshold.  Measured ribosome translation-efficiency survival in yeast uses $5128$ genes with a ribosome-TE join hit rate $0.8818572656921754$ and reports $R^2_{QT,\mathrm{meas}}=0.13681931343730436$, again with $9$ surviving coordinates.  The measured translation-conditioned abundance decomposition in yeast records $S_{QP}=0.3146818503437042$, $S_{QT,\mathrm{meas}}=0.13681931343730436$, and measured mediated fraction $0.4793069656345167$.  These numbers explain why the $f_3$ dwell-position test is biologically worth registering, but they do not by themselves identify the causal mechanism of the positional dwell effect.

\paragraph{Reduction and classification context.}
The yeast readout dictionary also shows that the global $B^*_{Q6}$ abundance signal is only partially reducible to a small measured-readout family.  With $\lambda_{\mathrm{DL}}=0.01$, $\rho_{\mathrm{join}}=0.05$, $n_{\mathrm{join}}=1800$, $5$ folds, and $120$ permutations, the partial mediation certificate reports selection-aware coverage $C^*=0.24538211439730545$ and unexplained fraction $0.7546178856026946$; the selection-aware null $95$th percentile is $0.029149007242956115$.  The irreducibility certificate uses the same $\lambda_{\mathrm{DL}}=0.01$, $\rho_{\mathrm{join}}=0.05$, $n_{\mathrm{join}}=1800$, and $120$ permutations, and records the current claim as a real source signal with at least one listed reduction family refuting the stronger irreducibility hypothesis.  The modeled-tAI four-case discriminant over yeast assigns all $9$ coordinates to case $D$ with no positive-positive-without-drop coordinates, using $R^2_{QT}=0.8213457220900503$ and $R^2_{QP}=0.29837624994140827$.  These surrounding finite facts are compatible with the present chapter: the $f_3$ dwell-position signal is a localized measured-readout increment inside a larger, partly reducible, partly unexplained codon-residual abundance surface.

\paragraph{Local no-promotion rule.}
The admissible claim is exactly the finite one: on the registered yeast join, the $f_3$ abundance projection has a positional dwell increment whose leading passing segment is the $5'$ head window, while the $3'$ body window does not pass the same gate.  This does not establish translation realization in a causal sense, does not identify elongation speed, initiation control, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, phenotype, adaptation, universality, or a biological law.  It also does not show that direct $q_i\to P_k$ association is mechanistically mediated by translation in every coordinate.  Those stronger readings require separate layer-matched contacts, perturbation or rescue evidence where mechanism is claimed, and a controlled statistic showing that the proposed readout both survives and absorbs or reduces the downstream association under the declared covariates.  In the present chapter, the row-level result remains a measured positional ribosome-dwell readback for one yeast abundance-projection target.
