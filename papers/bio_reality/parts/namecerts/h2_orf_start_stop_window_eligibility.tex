\paragraph{Finite object.}
The object studied here is not a translated gene, not a protein, and not a biological function.  It is the much narrower record obtained when a finite nucleotide window is checked against a start--stop enumeration rule.  The verified run for claim $h2.orf.start\_stop.window\_eligibility$ loaded one curated nucleotide-window manifest, with $1$ input window and $1$ classified window.  Thus the observed finite count is
$$
\begin{aligned}
  N_{\mathrm{windows}} &= 1,\\
  N_{\mathrm{classified}} &= 1,\\
  N_{\mathrm{loaded}} &= 1.
\end{aligned}
$$
The stated scope of that run is external-window start--stop enumeration only, with snapshot date $2026$-$05$-$25$.  The data source is a curated finite nucleotide-window manifest whose rows are annotation inputs to the experiment.  The enumeration therefore supplies an eligibility classification for the supplied window, not a derivation of an expressed open reading frame from codon geometry alone.

\paragraph{Reality contact supplied by curated intervals.}

It loaded $168$ curated rows from NCBI RefSeq GenBank XML coding-sequence intervals for \emph{Escherichia coli} K-12 MG1655, accession $NC\_000913.3$, with snapshot date $2026$-$05$-$30$.  The required input classes were present: boundary cases, matched non-ORF decoys, and positive curated ORFs.  The protocol record states that the classifier rules, baselines, input classes, and held-out splits were fixed before the external observation time $2026$-$05$-$30T10{:}29{:}31+00{:}00$, with protocol time $2026$-$05$-$29T19{:}11{:}13+00{:}00$.

For the positive curated intervals, the observed count was $72$ accepted out of $72$, with one-sided binomial upper-tail probability
$$
\begin{aligned}

\end{aligned}

\paragraph{Local verdict.}
The finite evidence supports the following bounded statement.  Given the supplied curated nucleotide-window manifest, the start--stop enumeration classified all supplied windows in the manifest, namely $1$ out of $1$.  Given the separate curated interval-contact dataset, the same style of eligibility classifier agrees with $72$ curated positive intervals, rejects $72$ matched decoys, does not promote $24$ boundary cases, and remains stable across three held-out folds of $24$ positives and $24$ decoys each.  This is an ORF-eligibility contact result for the stated finite rows only.

The local non-claim is equally important.  No translation event, folded protein, physical admissibility, biological function, mechanistic explanation, genome-wide law, or universal rule follows from these counts unless another contact specifically measuring that higher layer is supplied.  The self-check record reinforces this limitation: three gates were checked, and no codon geometry was promoted across biological layers.  The chapter therefore records a finite start--stop eligibility result with curated interval support, while keeping all higher biological interpretations outside the proved scope.
