\paragraph{Local boundary of interpretation.}
The self-check row states that no codon geometry is promoted across biological layers and that $3$ gates were checked.  The local consequence is strict.  Start/stop-window enumeration may say that a supplied finite interval satisfies the chosen ORF-boundary rule.  It may not say that the interval is translated, that a protein folds, that a physical molecule is admissible, that a function is present, that a mechanism has been identified, or that a universal biological law follows.  Any such stronger statement requires a separate contact that directly supplies the missing biological evidence.  Without that additional contact, the present claim remains a finite eligibility classification for the supplied window manifest and nothing higher.
