\paragraph{Finite object.}
The object recorded here is not an observed protein-coding event.  It is a finite start--stop-window eligibility record whose biological layer is restricted to $\mathrm{orf\_eligibility}$.  The row reports one externally supplied nucleotide window and one classified window, so the enumeration check has the finite size summary
$$
\begin{aligned}
  \#\mathrm{input\ windows} &= 1,\\
  \#\mathrm{classified\ windows} &= 1,\\
  \#\mathrm{loaded\ external\ window\ datasets} &= 1.
\end{aligned}
$$
The snapshot date for this enumeration row is $2026$-$05$-$25$.  The source is a curated finite nucleotide-window manifest for BioReality ORF start/stop enumeration.  The records are external annotation inputs to the experiment, not facts generated by the BEDC coordinate system.  The local scope is therefore exactly external finite-window start/stop enumeration, and the recorded layer is $\mathrm{orf\_eligibility\ only}$.

\paragraph{Contact discipline.}
The local row has no attached contact object in its own contact list.  For that reason, its positive content is limited to the statement that the supplied finite window can be classified by the frozen start--stop-window enumeration rule.  A separate curated-interval experiment shows what a stronger biological contact looks like without changing the meaning of this row.  That experiment loaded $168$ curated interval records from NCBI RefSeq GenBank XML CDS intervals for \textit{Escherichia coli} K-12 MG1655 accession $\mathrm{NC\_000913.3}$, with snapshot date $2026$-$05$-$30$.  Its input classes were boundary cases, matched non-ORF decoys, and positive curated ORFs.  It reported $72$ accepted positive intervals with accept rate $1.0$, $72$ rejected matched decoys with reject rate $1.0$, and $24$ boundary cases with $0$ promotions.  The one-sided exact binomial upper-tail value reported for the positive interval statistic was $2.117582368135751\times 10^{-22}$; the matched decoy statistic and the predeclared paired advantage statistic reported the same value.  Three held-out splits were recorded, each with $24$ positives accepted at rate $1.0$ and $24$ decoys rejected at rate $1.0$.

\paragraph{Boundary of interpretation.}
Those contact statistics are cited only to delimit the difference between a bare start--stop enumeration and a contacted biological interval.  The present row does not infer translation realization.  It does not infer folded structure, physical admissibility, biological function, molecular mechanism, regulatory role, organism-level effect, or a universal law about open reading frames.  The self-check row makes the same boundary explicit: it records a BioReality self-check with checked gate count $3$ and states that no codon geometry is promoted across biological layers.  Consequently the valid conclusion for this chapter is narrow: one externally supplied finite nucleotide window was loaded and classified by start--stop enumeration at the $\mathrm{orf\_eligibility}$ layer, while all stronger biological readings require their own independent contact data.
