\subsection{Window-Six Codon Boundary Row}\label{sec:bioreality-h0-r-cardinality-13}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite object.}
This record concerns the standard-code codon table viewed through a six-bit codon display. The finite carrier is the codon cube $Q_6$, with codons read as vertices and with local faces used only as a coordinate device for code-layer inspection. The distinguished row $R$ has cardinality $13$. In the verified run $5d4a3459997d$, the row is exactly
$$
\begin{aligned}
R=\{&\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\\
&\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UUA}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the measured value $|R|=13$ is not a parameter fitted after inspection; it is the size of the listed finite row. The contact used here is a curated standard genetic-code table assigning codons to amino-acid labels or stop labels. That contact supports label readback and local split checks, but it does not observe translation dynamics, molecular structure, physical admissibility, biological function, a mechanism, or a universal law.

\paragraph{Punctured-cube shape.}
The companion shape audit $e19917012c60$ identifies the same row $R$ with the punctured-cube codon set and returns the face-count vector $(13,16,4)$. The first entry repeats the vertex count, while the remaining entries record the induced local tile geometry in this finite coordinate presentation. This is a boundary-tile fact: it says that the selected codons form a particular finite pattern inside the window-six cube. It does not say that DNA or translation is governed by that geometry as a biological necessity. The coordinate pattern is therefore retained as a code-read regularity, not promoted beyond the curated label table.

\paragraph{Spectral measurement.}
The spectral row $1026324eff41$ supplies numerical invariants for the same finite construction. The recorded values are $\lambda_M=0.6752479308830213$, $\lambda_R=0.4758352843197002$, and $\mu=3.051487585298128$. The radial polynomial check gives $-6.224354365258478\times 10^{-12}$, and the same residual is reported for the cubic polynomial evaluation. Within numerical tolerance, the finite row therefore satisfies the intended cubic spectral relation. These numbers strengthen the claim that the row is a reproducible coordinate feature of the standard-code label table, but they do not supply an experimental observation of folding, protein activity, or biochemical feasibility.

\paragraph{Null comparisons.}
The shape-significance audit $0b267c88f8e4$ sampled $N=2000$ distinct alternatives using seed $42$. It required $57377$ sampling attempts, with $55377$ duplicate retries. The exact shape appeared once, giving $p=0.0005$, and $25$ samples reached Jaccard similarity at least $0.9$, giving $p=0.0125$. The amino-acid-class-preserving null audit $e8d5de747a0c$ also used $N=2000$ and seed $42$. There the observed module size was $20$, the exact-shape count was $0$, and the lower-tail count at or below the observed size was $7$, giving lower-tail probability $0.0035$. The reported exact-shape probability in that constrained null was $0.0$ at this sample size. These finite null rows make the local tile hard to dismiss as an arbitrary display artifact, while still leaving its biological interpretation bounded by the available contact.

\paragraph{Readback tests.}
Two leave-out probes test whether the coordinate row carries predictive pressure at the code-label level. In run $429e6e2c4eb4$, $23$ code tables were evaluated against $63$ total targets. The baseline hit rate was $0.19821739130434785$, while the hit rate above baseline was $0.9841269841269841$. The rate inside $M-T$ was $0.9365079365079365$, and the rate inside $M-T$ together with the boundary was $0.9841269841269841$. In run $e9e1a6e41633$, the leave-one-codon probe found $0$ anchors, $5$ boundary cases, $8$ high-coverage closure cases, and $8$ predictable cases, with recovery rate $1.0$. These figures support the claim that the local codon-tile row is informative for code-label readback under the tested finite probes. They do not convert label recovery into translation realization or protein behavior.

\paragraph{Family and module accounting.}
The family audit $7dda978c6c97$ divides the leave-one-code-out row into $5$ families. The hit rates above baseline are $0.8333333333333334$ for alternative yeast, and $1.0$ for bacterial plastid, candidate other, mitochondrial, and nuclear ciliate families. The module-classification run $bbe8a4d0455b$ assigns all $13$ row codons with no extra module codons, no missing row codons, and no unassigned codons. The six modules are $\mathrm{agr}=\{\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG}\}$, $\mathrm{ar\_lys}=\{\mathrm{AAA}\}$, $\mathrm{cun}=\{\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU}\}$, $\mathrm{ile\_met}=\{\mathrm{AUA}\}$, $\mathrm{ssn}=\{\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UUA}\}$, and $\mathrm{stop\_trp}=\{\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UGA}\}$. This exhaustive finite partition is the strongest local form of the claim: the row is counted, geometrically located, spectrally measured, null-tested, and module-classified. Its scope remains the code layer. Any assertion about translation, folding, physical admissibility, biological function, mechanism, or universality requires an additional contact that directly measures that higher layer.
