\paragraph{Reality contact and blocked promotions.}
The only reality contact used here is the curated standard genetic-code table, at codon-label resolution.  It observes that codons are assigned to amino-acid or stop labels.  It does not observe ribosomal realization, molecular structure, thermodynamic admissibility, organismal function, or a universal mechanism.  Consequently, the verified local tile may be used to discuss a code-layer split and its finite statistics, but it must not be promoted to translation, folding, physical admissibility, biological function, mechanism, or universality unless another contact supplies that higher-layer evidence.  In particular, amino-acid label readback is still label readback; it is not a folded protein and not a structural-order measurement.
