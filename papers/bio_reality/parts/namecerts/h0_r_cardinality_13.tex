\paragraph{Reality contact and local verdict.}
The contact used here is the curated standard genetic-code table: it supplies codon-to-amino-acid or codon-to-stop labels.  The table is sufficient for checking code-layer label readback and local tile splits.  It is not an observation of translation dynamics, folded structure, physical admissibility, biological function, evolutionary mechanism, or a universal law.  Consequently, the chapter-level conclusion is deliberately limited.  The window-six coordinates expose a thirteen-codon boundary row with punctured-cube incidence, reproducible spectral numbers, significant randomization contrasts, high recovery under code-table and codon deletion tests, and a complete six-module accounting.  Those finite facts justify the claim that the standard-code label table contains a local boundary-tile pattern at the code-read layer.  No stronger biological interpretation follows from this contact alone; any claim about protein realization, folding, admissible chemistry, organism-level function, mechanism, or universality would require an independent contact specifically measuring that higher layer.
