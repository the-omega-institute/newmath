\subsection{Window-six codon boundary record}
\label{sec:bior-h0-r-cardinality-13}

\paragraph{Coordinate object.}
The record studies the standard-code codon table through a six-bit codon display, where each codon is treated as a point of the codon cube $Q_6$ and the only admitted readback is the curated assignment of codons to amino-acid or stop labels.  The observed boundary set $R$ has cardinality $13$.  In the verified row, the set is
$$
\begin{aligned}
R=\{&\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\\
&\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UUA}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the finite claim is not merely that a boundary exists, but that this particular collection of codons is the recovered row-level boundary, with $|R|=13$.  The accompanying punctured-cube check reports the same codon list and the face-count vector $(13,16,4)$, so the local tile is recorded as a thirteen-vertex, sixteen-edge, four-face pattern in the window-six geometry.

\paragraph{Spectral and shape checks.}
The spectrum row supplies the numerical invariants

\begin{aligned}

\lambda_R&=0.4758352843197002,\\
\mu&=3.051487585298128.

$$
The reported cubic residual is $-6.224354365258478\times 10^{-12}$, which is the same value listed for the radial polynomial residual.  This is treated as a numerical tolerance check on the finite codon-tile computation, not as an independent biological mechanism.  A separate randomized shape comparison used $N=2000$ trials with seed $42$.  It found $1$ exact shape match, giving $p_{\mathrm{exact}}=0.0005$, and $25$ high-Jaccard matches at threshold $0.9$, giving $p_{J\ge 0.9}=0.0125$.  The run required $57377$ distinct-sample attempts and $55377$ duplicate retries, so the small empirical tail probabilities must be read together with the sampling strain of the finite null experiment.

\paragraph{Amino-acid-class preserving null row.}
A stricter label-class preserving null model also used $N=2000$ trials with seed $42$.  The observed module size was $20$, the exact-match count was $0$, and the lower-tail count at or below the observed size was $7$.  Consequently the recorded probabilities are
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{\mathrm{class\ exact}}&=0.0,\\
p_{\mathrm{class\ lower\ tail}}&=0.0035.
\end{aligned}
$$
This supports the statement that the local shape is unusual under the tested finite nulls.  It does not convert the codon cube calculation into a claim about translation dynamics, folded structure, biochemical admissibility, organismal function, a causal mechanism, or a universal biological law.

\paragraph{Scope of the record.}
The only external contact used here is the curated standard-code table, understood as a codon-to-label table.  It observes that codons receive amino-acid or stop labels; it does not observe ribosomal realization, folding, conformational order, energetic admissibility, biological function, or a law valid across all biological settings.  For that reason the conclusion is deliberately narrow: window-six coordinates expose a finite local boundary-tile structure in the code-read layer, with $R$ equal to the thirteen codons displayed above.  Any assertion about translation, protein structure, physical feasibility, functional biology, mechanism, or universality would require another contact whose observations live at that higher layer.
