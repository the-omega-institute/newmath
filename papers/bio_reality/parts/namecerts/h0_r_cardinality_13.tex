\subsection{The thirteen-codon boundary row in the window-six codon cube}\label{sec:h0-r-cardinality-13}\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite object.} The record concerns a six-coordinate codon display in which the standard genetic-code table is used only as a curated label contact. The row singled out by the computation is the set $R$ of codons whose local tile behavior marks the boundary under the window-six coordinate presentation. The verified row has cardinality $13$ and is exactly
$$
\begin{aligned}
R=\{&AAA, AGA, AGG, AUA, CUA, CUC, CUG, CUU,\\
&UAA, UAG, UCA, UGA, UUA\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the local boundary packet contains one lysine codon $AAA$, the two arginine codons $AGA,AGG$, one isoleucine codon $AUA$, the four leucine codons $CUA,CUC,CUG,CUU$, two stop codons $UAA,UAG$, one serine codon $UCA$, one stop codon $UGA$, and one leucine codon $UUA$. The relevant reality contact is only that the curated standard-code table assigns each codon to an amino-acid or stop label. No physical translation process is observed by this row itself.

\paragraph{Punctured-cube shape.} The same run verifies that $R$ is the punctured-cube boundary row with face-vector data
$$
\begin{aligned}
(f_0,f_1,f_2)=(13,16,4).
\end{aligned}
$$
The scalar $f_0=13$ agrees with the explicit list above. The remaining two entries record $16$ local one-dimensional incidences and $4$ local two-dimensional faces inside the finite codon-coordinate shape. This is a combinatorial statement about the coordinate presentation and its label readback; it is not a claim that the codon cube supplies a universal biological mechanism.

The spectral radius reported in tolerance was $0.6752479308830213$, matching $\lambda_M$. These numbers support the stability of the finite coordinate calculation, but they do not add a molecular dynamics interpretation.

\paragraph{Shape significance against finite null rows.} In the shape-significance experiment, $N=2000$ distinct null samples were requested from seed $42$. The sampler made $57377$ distinct-sample attempts and encountered $55377$ duplicate retries. Among the $2000$ accepted samples, there was $1$ exact shape match and $25$ samples with Jaccard score at least $0.9$. Therefore
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{\mathrm{exact}}&=0.0005,\\
p_{\mathrm{Jaccard}\ge 0.9}&=0.0125.
\end{aligned}
$$
A second null computation preserving amino-acid class used $N=2000$ and seed $42$. It found $0$ exact matches, $7$ lower-tail samples at or below the observed $M$ size, and an observed $M$ size of $20$. Its reported probabilities were
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{\mathrm{class\ exact}}&=0.0,\\
p_{\mathrm{class\ lower\ tail}}&=0.0035.
\end{aligned}
$$
These finite rates say that the observed local shape is uncommon under the tested row models. They do not identify a causal evolutionary pathway, a biochemical admissibility criterion, or a functional constraint.

\paragraph{Leave-out readback.} The leave-one-code-out experiment evaluated $23$ tables with $63$ total targets. Its baseline hit rate was $0.19821739130434785$, while the hit rate above baseline was $0.9841269841269841$. The rate inside $M-T$ was $0.9365079365079365$, and the rate inside $M-T$ plus the boundary was again $0.9841269841269841$. The leave-one-codon-out experiment reported $0$ anchors, $5$ boundary codons, $8$ high-coverage closure cases, $8$ predictable cases, and recovery rate $1.0$. The family split has $5$ families, with hit rates above baseline

\begin{aligned}

\mathrm{bacterial\ plastid}&:1.0,\\
\mathrm{candidate\ other}&:1.0,\\

$$
These leave-out rows show that the boundary neighborhood has strong code-layer recovery behavior under the tested finite perturbations. The evidence remains label-level evidence.

\paragraph{Module accounting.} The module classification is complete: $R$ has size $13$, all $13$ codons are assigned, and there are no extra module codons, no missing codons from the modules, and no unassigned codons. The verified modules are

Every module has either predictable or boundary status in the finite record. The split is therefore internally closed for this row: the thirteen listed codons, their module partition, and the local punctured-cube face data agree.

\paragraph{Scope of the claim.} The supported statement is narrow. Window-six codon coordinates expose a thirteen-codon local boundary row whose standard-code labels can be read through the curated table, and the accompanying finite probes show non-random local shape and strong leave-out recovery in the tested settings. The same data do not establish translation realization, folded structure, physical admissibility, biological function, universal mechanism, or global law. Any such promotion would require a separate contact that directly observes that higher layer. In particular, an amino-acid label sequence is still only a label sequence here; it is not a folded protein structure and not a function measurement.
