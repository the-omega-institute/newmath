\paragraph{Scope boundary.}
The reality contact used here is the curated standard genetic-code table. That contact observes codon-to-label assignments and supports local split checks in the code layer. It does not observe ribosomal translation, folded structure, physical admissibility, biochemical function, evolutionary mechanism, or universality across all biological settings. Consequently, this chapter may state that the window-six coordinate system exposes a reproducible local residual tile in the standard-code label table, with the finite counts, spectra, null comparisons, recovery rates, and module partition listed above. It may not state that the tile is a law of biology, a mechanism of translation, a protein-structure predictor, a physical constraint, or a functional explanation unless another independently specified contact supplies that higher-layer evidence.
