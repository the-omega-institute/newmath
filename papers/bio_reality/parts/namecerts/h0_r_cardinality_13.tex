\subsection{Window-six codon boundary tile}
\label{sec:bioreality-h0-r-cardinality-13}

\paragraph{Finite codon display.}
This record concerns the standard-code codon table only at the label-readout level. The finite carrier is the six-coordinate codon cube obtained by writing each RNA codon as a three-letter word over $\{U,C,A,G\}$ and then using a two-bit coordinate for each letter. On this carrier the distinguished boundary set $R$ has cardinality $13$. The verified row is
$$
\begin{aligned}
R=\{&AAA, AGA, AGG, AUA, CUA, CUC, CUG, CUU,\\
&UAA, UAG, UCA, UGA, UUA\}, \qquad |R|=13.
\end{aligned}
$$
The same finite row identifies this set as the punctured-cube boundary pattern with face-vector
$$
\begin{aligned}
(f_0,f_1,f_2)=(13,16,4).
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the object being described is not an abstract biological mechanism. It is a finite coordinate pattern whose readout is compared against the curated standard-code table assigning codons to amino-acid or stop labels.

\paragraph{Local tile and numerical spectrum.}
The boundary set is paired with an observed local tile $M$ of size $20$ in the supplied computations. The spectral row reports
$$
\begin{aligned}
\lambda_M &= 0.6752479308830213,\\
\lambda_R &= 0.4758352843197002,\\
\mu &= 3.051487585298128.
\end{aligned}
$$
The radial cubic check is numerically closed at the recorded tolerance: the value of the tested cubic expression is $-6.224354365258478\cdot 10^{-12}$. These numbers justify treating the window-six pattern as a stable finite signature for this dataset, while still leaving all causal and biochemical interpretations outside the claim.

\paragraph{Shape significance against finite null rows.}
The shape test with seed $42$ used $N=2000$ accepted samples after $57377$ distinct-sample attempts and $55377$ duplicate retries. It found $1$ exact shape match, giving $p_{\mathrm{exact}}=0.0005$, and $25$ samples with Jaccard score at least $0.9$, giving $p_{J\geq 0.9}=0.0125$. A separate amino-acid-class-preserving null row, also with $N=2000$ and seed $42$, found $0$ exact matches for the observed shape and $7$ samples in the lower tail at or below the observed $M$ size $20$. Its reported values are
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{\mathrm{aa\ class\ exact}}&=0.0,\\
p_{\mathrm{aa\ class\ lower\ tail}}&=0.0035.
\end{aligned}
$$
These finite tests support the claim that the local coordinate tile is atypical under the sampled null procedures. They do not turn the coordinate tile into a law of translation or into a statement about protein structure.

\paragraph{Leave-one readout checks.}
The leave-one-code-out row evaluated $23$ tables and $63$ total targets. Its baseline hit rate was $0.19821739130434785$, while the hit rate above baseline was $0.9841269841269841$. The hit rate inside $M-T$ was $0.9365079365079365$, and the rate inside $M-T$ together with the boundary was $0.9841269841269841$. By family, the five reported groups were alternative yeast, bacterial or plastid, candidate other, mitochondrial, and nuclear ciliate. Their hit rates above baseline were respectively
$$
\begin{aligned}
&0.8333333333333334,\quad 1.0,\quad 1.0,\quad 1.0,\quad 1.0.
\end{aligned}
$$
The leave-one-codon-out row reports no anchor cases, $5$ boundary cases, $8$ high-coverage closure cases, $8$ predictable cases, and recovery rate $1.0$. These are readout-level checks: they say that the finite tile and its boundary recover many held-out code labels within this curated comparison regime.

\paragraph{Module accounting.}
The module-classification row assigns all $13$ boundary codons, with no missing, extra, or unassigned codons. The six modules are
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{agr}&=\{AGA,AGG\},\\
\mathrm{ar\_lys}&=\{AAA\},\\
\mathrm{cun}&=\{CUA,CUC,CUG,CUU\},\\
\mathrm{ile\_met}&=\{AUA\},\\
\mathrm{ssn}&=\{UCA,UUA\},\\
\mathrm{stop\_trp}&=\{UAA,UAG,UGA\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The accounting identity is therefore $R_{\mathrm{size}}=13$ and $\mathrm{assigned\ count}=13$. This matters because the boundary is not merely a count: the listed codons are partitioned into biologically named label-change modules, while the computation still remains a finite comparison against the standard-code label table.

\paragraph{Scope of the contact.}
The reality contact used here is the curated standard genetic-code table. It supplies codon-to-label assignments and allows the local split to be checked at code-readout resolution. It does not observe ribosomal translation, folded structure, physical admissibility, biological function, or a universal mechanism. Consequently, the present record permits the following limited conclusion: window-six codon coordinates expose a statistically sharp local boundary pattern for the standard-code label table, with $|R|=13$, face-vector $(13,16,4)$, the spectral values above, and strong leave-one recovery rows. Any assertion about translation realization, folding, physical feasibility, function, mechanism, or universality requires an additional contact designed for that layer.
