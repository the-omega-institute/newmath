\subsection{The Thirteen-Codon Boundary Row in the Standard Code}\label{sec:bioreality-h0-r-cardinality-13}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite codon row.}
The record considered here is a window-six coordinate analysis of the standard-code codon table. The finite carrier is the six-bit codon cube $Q_6$, with readout supplied by the curated standard genetic-code table. The distinguished boundary row $R$ has exactly thirteen codons:
$$
\begin{aligned}
R=\{&\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\\
&\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UUA}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The direct cardinality computation gives $|R|=13$, with the same thirteen-element list appearing in the punctured-cube check. In that check the row has face data
$$
\begin{aligned}
(f_0,f_1,f_2)=(13,16,4),
\end{aligned}
$$
so the observed object is not merely a label list: it is a small subcomplex inside the codon cube, carrying vertices, edges, and two-dimensional faces. The biological contact, however, is only the codon-to-label table. It says which codons read as amino-acid or stop labels in the standard code; it does not observe ribosomal translation, folded structure, chemical feasibility, organismal fitness, or a universal mechanism.

\paragraph{Spectral signature.}
The associated finite spectrum supplies a second numerical check on the same row. The recorded scalars are
$$
\begin{aligned}
\lambda_M&=0.6752479308830213,\\
\lambda_R&=0.4758352843197002,\\
\mu&=3.051487585298128.
\end{aligned}
$$
The cubic residual is $-6.224354365258478\times 10^{-12}$, also reported as the radial polynomial value, so the row satisfies the intended finite spectral equation to numerical tolerance. The spectral radius entry is $0.6752479308830213$, matching $\lambda_M$ in the recorded row. This is a coordinate-level and spectrum-level regularity of the finite codon display. It should be read as a constraint on the local window-six pattern, not as a claim that the genetic code is forced by this geometry.

\paragraph{Null comparisons.}
Two randomized controls give the row its statistical scale. In the shape-significance run with seed $42$, $N=2000$ samples were evaluated after $57377$ distinct-sample attempts and $55377$ duplicate retries. There was $1$ exact shape match, hence
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{\mathrm{exact}}=0.0005.
\end{aligned}
$$
There were $25$ samples with Jaccard score at least $0.9$, giving
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{\mathrm{Jaccard}\ge 0.9}=0.0125.
\end{aligned}
$$
The amino-acid-class-preserving control, also with $N=2000$ and seed $42$, reports observed $M$-size $20$, exact match count $0$, and $7$ samples in the lower tail at or below the observed $M$-size. Thus the class-preserving exact-shape value is $0.0$ in the sampled row, and the lower-tail value is $0.0035$. These controls do not prove a biological law; they say that the finite geometry is rare under the tested reshufflings.

\paragraph{Leave-one-codon structure of the row.}
The leave-one-codon-out row gives $0$ anchors, $5$ boundary codons, $8$ predictable codons, closure coverage high $8$, and recovery rate $1.0$. The module classification accounts for all thirteen codons of $R$: $R$-size $13$, assigned count $13$, no extra module codons, no missing codons from the modules, and no unassigned codons. The modules are
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{agr}&=\{\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG}\},\\
\mathrm{ar\_lys}&=\{\mathrm{AAA}\},\\
\mathrm{cun}&=\{\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU}\},\\
\mathrm{ile\_met}&=\{\mathrm{AUA}\},\\
\mathrm{ssn}&=\{\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UUA}\},\\
\mathrm{stop\_trp}&=\{\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UGA}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
Every module has either predictable or boundary status in the recorded classification. The row therefore has a complete internal assignment at the code-label level. The singleton and three-point label classes are not promoted into protein shape, admissible chemistry, cellular function, or cross-organism universality by this record alone.

\paragraph{Local scope.}
The finite result is a local codon-tile statement. The curated table supports standard-code label readback and local split checks; it does not by itself supply a separate contact for translation dynamics, folded-protein geometry, thermodynamic admissibility, functional phenotype, or a general biological mechanism. Any claim crossing one of those boundaries would require an additional observation of the relevant kind. In the present row, the scientifically licensed statement is narrower: within $Q_6$, the standard-code labels determine a thirteen-codon boundary row with punctured-cube face vector $(13,16,4)$, a stable numerical spectral signature, rare shape under the sampled null models, and complete module accounting for the thirteen listed codons.
