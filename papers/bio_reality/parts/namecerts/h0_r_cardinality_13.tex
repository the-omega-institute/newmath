\paragraph{Reality contact and scope.}
The only reality contact used here is the curated standard genetic-code table.  It supplies codon-to-label assignments and permits tests at the code-read layer, including local codon-tile label splits.  It does not supply a translation experiment, a folded-structure observation, a physical-admissibility measurement, a function assay, or a universal mechanism.  Consequently, the allowed conclusion is narrow: the window-six coordinate display exposes a reproducible local boundary pattern in the standard-code labels, with $13$ named boundary codons and strong finite null-model contrasts.  The row does not license any claim that DNA must obey this geometry as a biological necessity, that an amino-acid label sequence is a protein structure, or that the local tile is a general law of organisms.  Any such stronger assertion would require an additional contact designed for that layer.
