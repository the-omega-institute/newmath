\subsection{A thirteen-codon boundary row in the window-six codon cube}
\label{subsec:h0-r-cardinality-thirteen}

\paragraph{Finite coordinate setting.}
This record concerns the standard-code codon table only at the level of code labels.  The carrier is the six-bit codon cube $Q_6$, read as a finite display of codon coordinates rather than as a mechanism of translation.  The distinguished row $R$ is the observed local boundary row selected by the window-six coordinate construction.  In the verified finite run with experiment identifier $5d4a3459997d$, the row has exactly thirteen codons:
$$
\begin{aligned}
R=\{&AAA, AGA, AGG, AUA, CUA, CUC, CUG, CUU,\\
&UAA, UAG, UCA, UGA, UUA\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the finite cardinality statement is
$$
\begin{aligned}
|R|=13.
\end{aligned}
$$
The row is not introduced as a biological universal.  It is a finite coordinate fact about a local codon-label pattern inside the window-six display, with the curated standard-code table serving as the contact that supplies codon-to-label assignments.

Under that null, $N=2000$ samples produced zero exact matches to the observed shape, and seven samples landed in the lower tail at or below the observed $M$ size.  The reported values are
$$
\begin{aligned}

p_{\mathrm{exact}}&=0.0,\\
\#\{\text{lower-tail samples}\}&=7,\\
p_{\mathrm{lower\ tail}}&=0.0035.
\end{aligned}
$$
A separate shape-significance run $0b267c88f8e4$ used $N=2000$ accepted samples, requiring $57377$ distinct-sample attempts and $55377$ duplicate retries.  It found one exact shape match and twenty-five samples with Jaccard similarity at least $0.9$:

\begin{aligned}

p_{\mathrm{Jaccard}\geq 0.9}&=0.0125.
\end{aligned}
$$
These numbers support a finite rarity claim for the observed local arrangement among the tested randomized alternatives.  They do not license a claim that the geometry alone explains the standard code or that it determines biological implementation.

\paragraph{Local interpretation and boundary.}
The scientific content of this chapter is therefore a narrow finite statement.  The window-six codon coordinate exposes a thirteen-codon boundary row, the same row has punctured-cube face data $(13,16,4)$, the spectral calculation has the displayed stable values, the observed shape is rare under the tested null models, and the leave-one tests recover the code-layer neighborhood at high rates.  The contact is the curated standard-code label table.  That contact observes codon-to-label assignments; it does not observe translation dynamics, ribosomal realization, folded structure, physical admissibility, biological function, mechanism, or universality.  Any such higher-layer statement requires a separate contact designed for that layer.  Without that separate contact, the row $R$ is a local codon-label boundary pattern and nothing stronger.
