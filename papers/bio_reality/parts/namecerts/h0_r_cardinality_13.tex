\paragraph{Finite object.}
The finite object in this record is the standard-code codon table viewed through a six-coordinate codon display. The display is not treated as a biological mechanism. It is a bounded coordinate scheme for asking whether the curated codon-to-label table contains a reproducible local boundary pattern. The carrier is the codon cube $Q_6$, whose points are read as codon labels after the usual four-letter nucleotide alphabet has been encoded into six binary coordinates. The distinctions used here are the coordinate position, the local tile face, the singleton label corner, and the three-point label class. The biological contact is only the curated standard-code assignment of codons to amino-acid or stop labels.
