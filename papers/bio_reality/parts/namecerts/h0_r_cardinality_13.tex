\paragraph{Finite row.}

\paragraph{Code-layer contact.}
The only external contact used here is the curated standard genetic-code table, viewed as a codon-to-label assignment table.  It supports questions at the code-read layer: whether the displayed codons carry amino-acid or stop labels, how singleton label corners and three-point label classes sit against the local tile, and how a leave-one readback behaves when code labels are withheld. These finite comparisons support the claim that the displayed tile is not a generic random appearance under the sampled controls.  They still remain controls over codon-label geometry, not evidence for a biochemical pathway or a structural fold.
