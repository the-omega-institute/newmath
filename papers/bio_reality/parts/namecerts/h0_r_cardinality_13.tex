\subsection{A Window-Six Boundary Tile in the Standard Codon Table}
\label{sec:bioreality-h0-r-cardinality-13}

\paragraph{Finite coordinate record.}
The object considered here is the standard-code codon table read at the code-label layer.  Each codon is displayed as a six-bit window coordinate, with the curated table supplying the amino-acid or stop label attached to that coordinate.  The row under audit is the boundary set $R$ singled out by the local tile computation.  Its verified cardinality is exactly thirteen, and the verified members are
$$
\begin{aligned}
R=\{&\mathtt{AAA},\mathtt{AGA},\mathtt{AGG},\mathtt{AUA},\mathtt{CUA},\mathtt{CUC},\mathtt{CUG},\mathtt{CUU},\mathtt{UAA},\mathtt{UAG},\mathtt{UCA},\mathtt{UGA},\mathtt{UUA}\},\\
|R|&=13.
\end{aligned}
$$
This is not a verbal grouping inferred after the fact: the finite run records both $R\_cardinality\_equals\_13=13$ and $size=13$, with the same thirteen codons listed as the expected coordinate hits.  The biological contact is therefore narrow and concrete.  It says that, inside the curated standard-code table, these thirteen codons occupy the local boundary row selected by the window-six display.

\paragraph{Punctured-cube shape.}
The same row is also verified as a punctured-cube tile.  The finite face count is recorded by the $f$-vector

\begin{aligned}

\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the row has thirteen vertices, sixteen one-dimensional adjacencies, and four two-dimensional faces in the local coordinate complex used by the computation.  This is a shape statement about the codon-coordinate display and the standard-code labels attached to it.  It does not say that the genome, ribosome, tRNA pool, folding landscape, or biochemical viability is forced by this cube fragment.  Those would require separate observations at those layers.

$$
\begin{aligned}

p_{\mathrm{Jaccard}\ge 0.9}&=0.0125.
\end{aligned}
$$
In the amino-acid-class-preserving null, again with $N=2000$ and seed $42$, the observed comparison set had size $20$.  The exact-match count was $0$, the lower-tail count at or below the observed $M$ size was $7$, and the recorded probabilities were

\begin{aligned}

p_{\mathrm{aa\ class\ lower\ tail}}&=0.0035.

These finite figures make the row nontrivial relative to the tested null procedures.  They do not remove the dependence on the curated code-label table, and they do not license claims about physical realization, folding, or biological function.
