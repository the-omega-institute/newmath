\paragraph{Object of record.}
This record concerns the standard-code codon table viewed through a six-bit codon display.  The carrier is the full codon cube $Q_6$, while the observed row $R$ is a local boundary set selected by the window-six coordinate construction and then read against curated codon labels.  The relevant distinctions are the six-bit codon display, local tile faces, singleton label corners, and three-point label classes.  The internal bookkeeping uses coordinates, closure, spectrum, and relations, but the biological labels themselves are supplied by a curated standard genetic-code table.  Thus the mathematical object is a finite codon-window configuration with an external label readout, not a claim about translation dynamics.

\end{aligned}
$$

$$
\begin{aligned}

\paragraph{Reality contact and limitation.}
The contact used here is the curated standard-code table, which assigns codons to amino-acid or stop labels.  That contact can test code readback and local codon-tile label splitting.  It cannot test translation realization, folded structure, structural order, physical admissibility, biological function, mechanism, or universality.  The safe conclusion is therefore narrow: in the standard-code table, the six-bit coordinate construction isolates a thirteen-codon local boundary row with the listed punctured-cube, spectral, sampling, recovery, and module-classification statistics.  No claim about protein folding, organism-level function, biochemical admissibility, or universal biological necessity follows from this row unless a separate experiment or data contact is supplied for that higher layer.
