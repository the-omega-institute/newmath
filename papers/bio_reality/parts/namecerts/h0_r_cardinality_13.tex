\paragraph{Finite coordinate object.}
This record concerns the standard-code codon table viewed only at the code-label layer.  The carrier is the six-bit codon cube $Q_6$, with codons displayed as three nucleotide positions and read against a curated standard-code table assigning each codon to an amino-acid or stop label.  The finite row singled out by the computation is the boundary set
$$
\begin{aligned}
R=\{&AAA, AGA, AGG, AUA, CUA, CUC, CUG, CUU,\\
&UAA, UAG, UCA, UGA, UUA\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The verified cardinality is $|R|=13$.  The same finite run reports the row as exactly the listed codons, with size $13$, under experiment run $5d4a3459997d$ at timestamp $2026$-$06$-$03T13{:}21{:}46+00{:}00$.  No inference is made from the spelling of these codons alone; the biological contact is the curated standard-code label table, and the mathematical carrier only organizes the codon coordinates in which this label split is inspected.

\paragraph{Scope of the biological contact.}

It does not observe translation realization, folded structure, structural order, physical admissibility, biological function, or a universal mechanism.
