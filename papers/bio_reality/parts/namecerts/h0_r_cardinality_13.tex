\paragraph{Finite object.}
The record concerns the standard-code codon table at the code-read layer. The finite carrier is the six-bit codon cube $Q_6$, read as a display of $64$ codon positions with a curated amino-acid-or-stop label attached to each position. The tested subset $R$ has cardinality $13$. Its observed members are exactly $AAA$, $AGA$, $AGG$, $AUA$, $CUA$, $CUC$, $CUG$, $CUU$, $UAA$, $UAG$, $UCA$, $UGA$, and $UUA$. Thus the finite row records the equality $|R|=13$ rather than merely a qualitative boundary description. The same rows also identify $R$ with a punctured-cube pattern whose face-count vector is $(13,16,4)$, so the local tile has $13$ vertices, $16$ recorded edges, and $4$ two-dimensional faces in the extracted combinatorial presentation.

\paragraph{Local label split.}
The codons in $R$ concentrate the readback positions where the window-six coordinate display separates singleton label corners from a three-point label class and nearby local faces. This statement is only a finite codon-table statement: the source contact is a curated standard genetic-code table, and the observed fact is a codon-to-label assignment. The table can test code labels and local codon-tile splits. It does not observe translation chemistry, folded structure, thermodynamic admissibility, organismal function, a molecular mechanism, or a universal biological principle. Those stronger statements would require additional contacts of the appropriate type.

Among those samples, the exact extracted shape appeared once, giving $p_{\mathrm{exact}}=0.0005$. A high-overlap criterion with Jaccard value at least $0.9$ appeared $25$ times, giving $p_{\mathrm{Jaccard}\ge 0.9}=0.0125$. A second null preserving amino-acid classes used $N=2000$ samples with seed $42$, found $0$ exact shape matches, and recorded $7$ samples in the lower tail at or below the observed $M$ size. The observed $M$ size was $20$, the exact-shape value was $0.0$, and the lower-tail value was $0.0035$. These rows make the local tile non-generic relative to the sampled baselines, while still leaving its interpretation at the finite code-label level.
