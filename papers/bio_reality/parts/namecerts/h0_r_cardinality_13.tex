\paragraph{Scope boundary.}
The reality contact is the curated standard-code label table.  Therefore the only biological reading licensed here is code-layer readback: codons are associated with amino-acid or stop labels, and the coordinate probe detects a local boundary tile among those labels.  No claim about translation realization, folded structure, physical admissibility, biochemical function, mechanism, or universality follows from these rows.  Any such higher-layer statement would require a separate contact measuring that layer directly.  In particular, reading an amino-acid label sequence from codons is not an observation of a folded protein, and a local codon tile is not a global biological principle.
