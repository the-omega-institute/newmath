\paragraph{Finite carrier and observed row.}
The object considered here is the standard-code codon table observed only at the code-label layer. The finite carrier is the six-coordinate codon cube $Q_6$, with each codon treated as a six-bit display position for a nucleotide triple. The relevant distinguished row $R$ is the local boundary set singled out by the window-six tile calculation. In the verified run $5d4a3459997d$, the row has exactly thirteen codons:
$$
\begin{aligned}
R=\{&\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\\
&\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UUA}\},\\
|R|&=13.
\end{aligned}
$$
The same row is also verified as a punctured-cube pattern in run $e19917012c60$, with face-vector data
$$
\begin{aligned}
f(R)&=(13,16,4).
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the finite observation is not merely a count. It has a local cubical shape: thirteen displayed codon positions, sixteen one-dimensional incidences, and four local two-dimensional faces inside the window-six coordinate presentation.

\paragraph{Code-label contact.}
The reality contact used here is the curated standard genetic-code table. It assigns each codon to an amino-acid or stop label, and it is used only as a code readout. This contact is strong enough to test whether the finite row and its neighboring tile structure align with the standard-code label table. It is not a translation experiment, not a folding observation, not a thermodynamic admissibility test, and not a functional assay. Consequently the row $R$ may be discussed as a local code-table structure, but no statement about protein realization, folded geometry, physical realizability, biological function, mechanism, or universality is licensed by this contact alone.

\paragraph{Shape significance under finite randomization.}
The local shape is non-generic under the recorded null sampling. In run $0b267c88f8e4$, $N=2000$ sampled tables required $57377$ distinct-sample attempts, with $55377$ duplicate retries. The exact shape appeared once, giving
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{\mathrm{exact}}&=0.0005,\\
p_{J\geq 0.9}&=0.0125.
\end{aligned}
$$
Here $25$ samples reached Jaccard similarity at least $0.9$. These values support the claim that the observed local tile is a finite code-layer regularity rather than an ordinary outcome of the sampled comparison class. A second null model preserved amino-acid class. In run $e8d5de747a0c$, again with $N=2000$, the exact-match count was $0$, the observed $M$-size was $20$, and the lower-tail count at or below the observed $M$-size was $7$. The corresponding values were
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{\mathrm{class\ exact}}&=0.0,\\
p_{\mathrm{class\ lower\ tail}}&=0.0035.
\end{aligned}
$$
This class-preserving comparison reinforces the same finite conclusion: the local row and its companion $M$-shape are unusual inside the tested code-label randomizations.

\paragraph{Leave-one recovery evidence.}
Two finite recovery tests measure whether the local tile has predictive structure at the code-label level. In the leave-one-code-out experiment $429e6e2c4eb4$, $23$ code tables were evaluated and $63$ targets were tested. The baseline hit rate was $0.19821739130434785$. The hit rate in $M-T$ was $0.9365079365079365$, and the hit rate in $M-T$ together with the boundary was $0.9841269841269841$. The reported rate above baseline was also $0.9841269841269841$. In the leave-one-codon-out experiment $e9e1a6e41633$, the anchor count was $0$, the boundary count was $5$, the predictable count was $8$, and the recovery rate was $1.0$. The high-coverage count was $8$, matching the predictable count. These data say that the row is useful for local code-label recovery in the tested finite setting. They do not say that the codon system is generated by the window geometry as a biological necessity.

This is the strongest chapter-local form of the claim: the window-six coordinate display isolates a complete thirteen-codon boundary row whose module decomposition is finite, explicit, and checked against the standard-code label table.

\paragraph{Local conclusion and limit.}
The finite-row evidence supports a code-read statement: in the standard-code table, the thirteen-codon row $R$ forms a local window-six boundary tile with punctured-cube shape, non-generic randomization statistics, stable spectral values, high leave-one recovery, and complete module assignment. The conclusion remains at the codon-label layer. A separate empirical contact would be required before asserting translation dynamics, folded protein structure, physical admissibility, biological function, a causal mechanism, or a universal law across biological systems.
