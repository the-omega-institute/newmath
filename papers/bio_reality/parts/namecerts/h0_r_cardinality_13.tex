\paragraph{Finite carrier and label contact.}
The record uses the six-coordinate codon cube $Q_6$ as its finite display space.  A codon is read as a six-bit coordinate, and the only contact with biological labeling in this row is the curated standard-code assignment of codons to amino-acid or stop labels.  The contact is therefore a code-label contact: it can test whether a local tile split agrees with the standard codon table, but it does not observe translation dynamics, folded structure, physical admissibility, biological function, mechanism, or universality.  Those higher layers would require independent measurements or curated contacts of their own.

\paragraph{Local conclusion and blocked promotion.}
The verified content is a finite code-layer statement.  Window-six coordinates isolate a thirteen-codon row in the standard-code label table; that row has the punctured-cube summary $(13,16,4)$, a reported spectral signature with $\lambda_M=0.6752479308830213$ and $\lambda_R=0.4758352843197002$, rare sampled shape matches with $p_{\mathrm{exact}}=0.0005$, strong leave-one-code recovery with hit rate $0.9841269841269841$ above baseline, and complete six-module assignment.  The conclusion stops there.  A codon-label tile is not a protein-fold observation, not a physical admissibility test, not a function assay, not a translation mechanism, and not a universal biological law.  Promotion beyond code readback is blocked until a separate contact directly measures the higher layer being claimed.
