\subsection{Window-six codon boundary record}\label{sec:h0-r-cardinality-13-window-six-codon-boundary}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite object.}
This record concerns the standard-code codon table only at the label-read layer. The carrier is the six-coordinate codon cube $Q_6$, where each codon is read as a finite word over the usual nucleotide alphabet and then displayed through six binary coordinates. The relevant distinctions are the six-bit codon display, the local tile face, the singleton label corner, and the three-point label class. The finite row named $h0.R.cardinality.13$ isolates the boundary set
$$
\begin{aligned}
R=\{&AAA, AGA, AGG, AUA, CUA, CUC, CUG, CUU,\\
&UAA, UAG, UCA, UGA, UUA\},
\end{aligned}
$$
with cardinality $|R|=13$. The same row reports the size field as $13$, so the named cardinality assertion and the enumerated codon list agree exactly. The construction is therefore not a numerical summary detached from the table: the record fixes the thirteen codons themselves and treats the count as a consequence of that finite enumeration.

\paragraph{Local cube geometry.}
The companion finite row $h0.R.punctured.cube$ identifies the same codon set as a punctured-cube shaped region. Its reported face data are
$$
\begin{aligned}
(f_0,f_1,f_2)=(13,16,4),
\end{aligned}
$$
so the local tile has thirteen vertices, sixteen one-dimensional adjacencies, and four two-dimensional faces in the chosen codon-cube presentation. This is a boundary statement about the code table display. It says that the codons in $R$ occupy a coherent local tile in $Q_6$ after the standard-code labels are read back. It does not say that a cell implements this geometry as a causal device, that translation is explained by the tile, or that protein structure is determined by it.

\paragraph{Spectral finite row.}
The spectrum row supplies a numerical check on the same finite object. It reports
$$
\begin{aligned}
\lambda_M&=0.6752479308830213,\\
\lambda_R&=0.4758352843197002,\\
\mu&=3.051487585298128,\\
p(\mu)&=-6.224354365258478\times 10^{-12}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The residual is also recorded as the radial polynomial value and as the cubic-polynomial residual, so the row is internally consistent at numerical tolerance. The value of $\lambda_M$ is the larger listed spectral scale, while $\lambda_R$ is the boundary-set scale. These numbers support a concrete finite comparison between the main label region and the thirteen-codon boundary set. They do not turn the coordinate comparison into an account of ribosomal dynamics, folding, energetic admissibility, phenotype, or evolutionary necessity.

\paragraph{Shape significance.}
The finite randomization row $h0.M.shape.significance$ evaluates the observed shape against $N=2000$ sampled alternatives with seed $42$. It records $57377$ distinct-sample attempts and $55377$ duplicate retries, so the experiment had to reject many repeated draws while forming the comparison set. Among the $2000$ accepted samples, there was $1$ exact shape match and $25$ samples with Jaccard overlap at least $0.9$. The reported probabilities are
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{\mathrm{exact}}&=0.0005,\\
p_{\mathrm{Jaccard}\geq 0.9}&=0.0125.
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the observed shape is rare under this finite null model, both for exact equality and for high-overlap comparison. A second null row preserving amino-acid class reports $N=2000$, observed $|M|=20$, exact-match count $0$, and lower-tail count $7$. Its probabilities are
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{\mathrm{class\ exact}}&=0.0,\\
p_{\mathrm{class\ lower\ tail}}&=0.0035.
\end{aligned}
$$
These are evidence for a non-generic finite label pattern in the standard table. They are not evidence that window-six geometry is a law of biology.

\paragraph{Code-out and codon-out tests.}
The leave-one-code-out row evaluates $23$ tables and $63$ total targets. It gives baseline hit rate $0.19821739130434785$, hit rate in $M-T$ equal to $0.9365079365079365$, and hit rate in $M-T$ plus boundary equal to $0.9841269841269841$. The reported rate above baseline is also $0.9841269841269841$. The family-level row groups the comparison into $5$ families and reports hit-rate-above-baseline values: alternative yeast $0.8333333333333334$, bacterial/plastid $1.0$, candidate other $1.0$, mitochondrial $1.0$, and nuclear ciliate $1.0$. The leave-one-codon-out row gives anchor count $0$, boundary count $5$, closure-coverage-high count $8$, predictable count $8$, and recovery rate $1.0$. These rows say that the local code-read pattern has finite predictive strength for held-out code labels and held-out codons within the tested table family. They still remain code-layer tests.

\paragraph{Module accounting.}
The module-classification row assigns all thirteen codons in $R$ with no missing and no extra entries. It reports $R$-size $13$, assigned count $13$, empty extra-module codon list, empty missing-from-modules list, and empty unassigned list. The modules are
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{agr}&=\{AGA,AGG\},\\
\mathrm{ar\_lys}&=\{AAA\},\\
\mathrm{cun}&=\{CUA,CUC,CUG,CUU\},\\
\mathrm{ile\_met}&=\{AUA\},\\
\mathrm{ssn}&=\{UCA,UUA\},\\
\mathrm{stop\_trp}&=\{UAA,UAG,UGA\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
Every module has either predictable or boundary status in the supplied row. This gives a closed finite accounting of the thirteen-codon boundary set: the row does not merely count codons, but sorts all of them into named local modules without leftover elements.

\paragraph{Reality contact and scope.}
The only contact used here is the curated standard genetic-code table, whose observed fact is that codons are assigned to amino-acid or stop labels. That contact is strong enough for codon-label readback and for local split checks in the window-six display. It is not a contact with translation realization, folded structure, physical admissibility, biological function, mechanism, or universality. The boundary condition for this chapter is therefore local and strict: the data support a finite code-read pattern in $Q_6$ with a thirteen-codon region $R$, the punctured-cube face vector $(13,16,4)$, the listed spectral numbers, rare-shape null-model values, and complete module classification. Any statement about translation, folding, physical feasibility, function, causal mechanism, or universal biological law requires a separate empirical contact that is not present in this row.
