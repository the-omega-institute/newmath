\paragraph{Local biological scope.}
The supported claim is narrow.  Window-six coordinates expose a thirteen-codon local boundary tile in the standard-code label table, with punctured-cube incidence data, numerical spectral values, null-model significance, recovery rows, and complete module assignment.  The curated table is a contact for codon-to-label readback only.  It does not observe a ribosome, a tRNA process, an amino-acid chain realization, a folded structure, an energetic admissibility condition, a phenotype, or a universal law across life.  Therefore no translation claim, folding claim, physical-admissibility claim, function claim, mechanism claim, or universality claim follows from this chapter unless a separate biological contact supplies that higher-layer observation.  In particular, amino-acid label sequences remain labels at the code-read layer; they are not protein structures, and CUN, UUR, AGR, stop, tryptophan, lysine, isoleucine, or methionine module names do not by themselves become structural or functional evidence.
