\subsection{Window-six boundary row for the standard codon table}\label{sec:bioreality-h0-r-cardinality-13}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Object and contact.}
This record concerns the standard-code codon table only at the code-read layer. The carrier is the six-coordinate codon cube $Q_6$, read as a finite display of $64$ codon positions with labels supplied by a curated standard genetic-code table. The row does not treat the table as a mechanism for translation. It treats the table as a finite contact assigning each codon an amino-acid or stop label, and then asks which local boundary positions are selected by the window-six tile rule.

\paragraph{Selected boundary row.}
The verified row \texttt{h0.R.cardinality.13} identifies a distinguished set $R$ of exactly $13$ codons. The finite set is
$$
\begin{aligned}
R=\{&\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\\
&\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UUA}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the cardinality check is
$$
\begin{aligned}
|R|=13.
\end{aligned}
$$
The companion punctured-cube computation returns the same $13$ codons and records the face-count summary
$$
\begin{aligned}
(f_0,f_1,f_2)=(13,16,4).
\end{aligned}
$$
This is a local combinatorial profile of the selected codon tile, not a claim that the codon table is forced by geometry.

\paragraph{Spectral readout.}
The spectral row gives three scalar values for the associated finite operator calculation:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\lambda_M&=0.6752479308830213,\\
\lambda_R&=0.4758352843197002,\\
\mu&=3.051487585298128.
\end{aligned}
$$
The reported radial cubic residual is $-6.224354365258478\times 10^{-12}$, the same number recorded as the cubic-polynomial residual. This is within numerical tolerance for the finite-row calculation. The spectral information is therefore a numerical signature of this codon-label row. It is not evidence for folding, energetic admissibility, or biological function.

\paragraph{Shape significance.}
The null computation \texttt{h0.M.shape.significance} used $N=2000$ samples with seed $42$. It required $57377$ distinct-sample attempts and incurred $55377$ duplicate retries. Among those samples there was $1$ exact shape match, giving
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{\mathrm{exact}}=0.0005.
\end{aligned}
$$
There were $25$ samples with Jaccard score at least $0.9$, giving
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{\mathrm{Jaccard}\geq 0.9}=0.0125.
\end{aligned}
$$
The amino-acid-class-preserving null used the same $N=2000$ and seed $42$. It observed $0$ exact matches, an observed $M$-size of $20$, and $7$ lower-tail samples at or below that observed size. Its reported exact-shape value is $0.0$, and its lower-tail value is
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{\mathrm{lower\ tail}}=0.0035.
\end{aligned}
$$
These numbers support the statement that the finite row is unusual under the sampled label-preserving comparisons. They do not convert the row into a universal property of genetic codes.

\paragraph{Leave-out behavior.}
The leave-one-code-out test evaluated $23$ tables and $63$ targets. The baseline hit rate was $0.19821739130434785$. The hit rate above baseline was $0.9841269841269841$, the hit rate inside $M-T$ was $0.9365079365079365$, and the hit rate inside $M-T$ plus the boundary was $0.9841269841269841$. By family, the row reports $5$ families: alternative yeast at $0.8333333333333334$, bacterial plastid at $1.0$, candidate other at $1.0$, mitochondrial at $1.0$, and nuclear ciliate at $1.0$. These leave-out results say that the finite code-read profile is stable across the tested table family partition. They do not say that the profile realizes translation dynamics.

\paragraph{Leave-one-codon behavior.}
The leave-one-codon-out row reports $0$ anchors, $5$ boundary codons, $8$ high-coverage closure cases, $8$ predictable cases, and recovery rate $1.0$. The module classification assigns all $13$ selected codons with no extras, no missing selected codons, and no unassigned codons. The modules are

\begin{aligned}

\mathrm{ar\_lys}&=\{\mathrm{AAA}\},\\
\mathrm{cun}&=\{\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU}\},\\

The module audit records $R$-size $13$ and assigned count $13$. This closes the finite bookkeeping for the row: each codon selected by $R$ appears in exactly one of the named modules, and the module inventory introduces no additional codons.

\paragraph{Local interpretation.}
The supported claim is narrow. Window-six coordinates expose a local codon-tile boundary pattern in the curated standard-code label table. The row can be used for code-layer readback, local split checks, sampled null comparisons, and leave-out diagnostics. It cannot be promoted to a statement about translation realization, folded protein structure, physical admissibility, biological function, mechanism, or universality without a separate contact that directly observes that higher layer. In particular, amino-acid label sequences obtained from the code table are still label sequences. They are not folded structures, not structural order measurements, and not function assays.
