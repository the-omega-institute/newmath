\subsection{Window-Six Codon Boundary Packet}
\label{sec:bioreality-h0-r-cardinality-13}

\paragraph{Claim domain.}
This record concerns the standard-code codon table only at the code-read layer. The finite object is the six-coordinate codon cube $Q_6$, with each RNA codon represented by its window-six coordinate display and then compared with the curated standard-code label table. The relevant local set $R$ has cardinality $13$ and is exactly
$$
\begin{aligned}
R=\{&\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\\
&\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UUA}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The audited row reports $|R|=13$, and the same row gives no additional codons outside this list. Thus the claim is not that geometry determines biology, but that a specified finite coordinate construction isolates a local codon-label boundary subset whose label readback can be checked against the standard table.

\paragraph{Local geometry.}
The punctured-cube computation identifies the same $13$ codons as a local tile with face data
$$
\begin{aligned}
(f_0,f_1,f_2)=(13,16,4).
\end{aligned}
$$
Here $f_0=13$ counts the listed codon vertices, $f_1=16$ counts the local one-step coordinate adjacencies retained by the tile, and $f_2=4$ counts the square faces present in the finite cell complex. The name ``punctured cube'' is therefore used in a literal finite-combinatorial sense: the observed object is a partial cube-shaped tile in $Q_6$, not a global law for all codon behavior.

It does not add a mechanistic interpretation; it only supplies a quantitative invariant attached to the codon tile.

\paragraph{Shape rarity under finite null models.}
The coordinate-shape experiment sampled $N=2000$ null instances with seed $42$. It required $57377$ distinct-sample attempts and encountered $55377$ duplicate retries. The exact tile shape appeared once, giving
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{\mathrm{exact}}&=0.0005,\\
p_{J\geq 0.9}&=0.0125,
\end{aligned}
$$
where the high-overlap count was $25$. A second null model preserving amino-acid classes used the same sample size $N=2000$ and seed $42$. In that model the exact match count was $0$, the observed module-size statistic was $20$, and the lower-tail count was $7$, giving
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{\mathrm{class\ exact}}&=0.0,\\
p_{\mathrm{class\ lower\ tail}}&=0.0035.
\end{aligned}
$$
These numbers justify treating the tile as a nontrivial finite pattern at the code-label level. They do not by themselves identify translation dynamics, folding, physical feasibility, biochemical function, a universal mechanism, or any organism-independent necessity.

\paragraph{Leave-one readback checks.}
The leave-one-code-out row evaluated $23$ code tables and $63$ total targets. Its baseline hit rate was $0.19821739130434785$, while the hit rate above baseline was $0.9841269841269841$. The hit rate inside $M-T$ was $0.9365079365079365$, and the hit rate inside $M-T$ plus the boundary was again $0.9841269841269841$. The leave-one-codon-out row reports $8$ predictable codons, $5$ boundary codons, $0$ anchors, high closure coverage $8$, and recovery rate $1.0$. These are readback statistics for omitted labels and omitted codons; they say that the local coordinate packet recovers code-layer entries unusually well, not that the entries realize a protein structure or biological role.

\paragraph{Reality contact and stopping rule.}
The only contact used here is a curated standard genetic-code table assigning codons to amino-acid or stop labels. That contact is adequate for checking codon-label readback and local label splits. It is not an observation of ribosomal translation, molecular folding, structural order, thermodynamic admissibility, cellular function, or a universal biological mechanism. Consequently, the packet is accepted as a finite code-read boundary record: $R$ is the verified $13$-codon tile, the punctured-cube face vector is $(13,16,4)$, the spectral scalars and null-model statistics are as reported above, and all higher-layer biological interpretations remain blocked until an independent contact at that higher layer is supplied.
