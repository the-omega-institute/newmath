\paragraph{Finite claim.}
The record concerns the standard-code codon table viewed through a six-coordinate codon display.  The carrier is the codon cube $Q_6$, where a codon is represented by its finite window-six coordinate, and the readback contact is the curated standard-code assignment of codons to amino-acid or stop labels.  The claim is deliberately local: the coordinate construction isolates a boundary tile in the code-label layer, while the biological labels themselves are supplied by the curated table.  The finite boundary set is
$$
\begin{aligned}
R=\{&\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\\
&\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UUA}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The audited row gives
$$
\begin{aligned}
|R|=13.
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the finite object is not an unspecified region of codon space: it is the thirteen-codon set displayed above, with a code-label readback attached through the standard table.

\paragraph{Combinatorial shape.}
The same row verifies that $R$ is the punctured-cube boundary tile detected by the window-six construction.  Its finite face data are

The count is useful because it separates the observed boundary tile from an arbitrary codon subset of the same size: the claim is about the particular codon set together with its induced local face structure, not merely about the number thirteen.

\paragraph{Null-model pressure.}
Two finite randomization checks make the shape nontrivial relative to the sampled baselines.  In the shape-preserving Monte Carlo row with $N=2000$ and seed $42$, there was $1$ exact shape match, giving
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{\mathrm{exact}}=0.0005.
\end{aligned}
$$
The same run found $25$ samples with Jaccard score at least $0.9$, giving
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{J\geq 0.9}=0.0125.
\end{aligned}
$$
The run records $57377$ distinct-sample attempts and $55377$ duplicate retries, which is consistent with a constrained finite sampling space rather than an unconstrained independent draw at each attempt.  Under the amino-acid-class-preserving null model, also with $N=2000$ and seed $42$, the observed module size is $20$, the exact-match count is $0$, and the lower-tail count at or below the observed module size is $7$.  The reported values are
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{\mathrm{aa\ class\ exact}}&=0.0,\\
p_{\mathrm{aa\ class\ lower\ tail}}&=0.0035.
\end{aligned}
$$
These numbers support the narrow statement that the local tile is unusually shaped relative to the recorded finite null families.  They do not promote the tile to a general law of molecular biology.

\paragraph{Biological contact and boundary.}
The only biological contact used here is the curated standard genetic-code table, at codon-label resolution.  It supplies the fact that codons are assigned amino-acid or stop labels in the standard code.  It does not supply a ribosomal realization trace, a folding experiment, a physical-admissibility assay, a protein-function measurement, or a proof of universality across all biological settings.  Therefore the valid conclusion is confined to the code-read layer: the window-six coordinate system, together with the curated label table, exposes a thirteen-codon local boundary tile with the finite shape, spectrum, null-model scores, prediction rates, and module partition recorded above.  No claim about translation, folded structure, physical admissibility, biological function, mechanism, or universal biological necessity follows unless a separate contact for that higher layer is added.
