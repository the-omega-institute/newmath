\paragraph{Finite carrier and observed row.}
The record fixes a six-bit codon display as the finite coordinate object.  Its biological contact is only the curated standard genetic-code table: a codon is read as an amino-acid or stop label because that table supplies the label, not because the cube geometry by itself supplies a biochemical event.  In this row the distinguished local region $R$ has exactly thirteen codons:
$$
\begin{aligned}
R = \{&\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\\
&\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UUA}\},\\
|R| &= 13.
\end{aligned}
$$
The same computation reports $R\_{\mathrm{cardinality\ equals\ 13}}=13$ and size $13$ in experiment run $5d4a3459997d$.  Thus the finite claim in this chapter is not that a biological mechanism has been explained, but that a particular window-six coordinate selection gives the stated codon list and cardinality when evaluated against the standard-code label table.

\paragraph{Local boundary of interpretation.}
The support condition for this chapter is narrow.  The curated standard-code table supplies codon-to-label assignments, and the coordinate computations expose a local thirteen-codon boundary pattern with shape, spectrum, null-model, recovery, family, and module statistics.  The result may be stated as a code-read claim: window-six codon coordinates detect a structured local tile whose labels can be checked against the standard-code table.  It may not be promoted to translation realization, folded structure, physical admissibility, biological function, mechanism, or universality without an additional contact that directly observes that higher layer.  Amino-acid label sequences are still labels in this chapter; they are not protein folds, physical objects, functions, or universal mechanisms.
