\subsection{Window-six local codon boundary record}
\label{sec:h0-r-cardinality-13}

\paragraph{Claim and finite object.}
This record concerns the finite codon set detected in the window-six codon coordinate cube $Q_6$ when the standard-code label table is used only as a curated codon-to-label contact. The claim is not that the genetic code is forced by the coordinate cube. The claim is narrower: the six-bit codon display separates a local tile boundary whose exceptional region $R$ has exactly thirteen codons, and those codons can be read as a finite code-layer pattern against the standard-code table. The verified row for this claim reports
$$
\begin{aligned}
R=\{&AAA, AGA, AGG, AUA, CUA, CUC, CUG, CUU,\\
&UAA, UAG, UCA, UGA, UUA\},
\end{aligned}
$$
with $|R|=13$. The same row identifies the size field as $13$, so the cardinality assertion and the displayed codon list agree exactly.

\paragraph{Local shape.}
A second finite check records the same set $R$ as a punctured-cube object. Its face data are summarized by the vector $(13,16,4)$: thirteen vertices, sixteen one-dimensional adjacencies, and four two-dimensional faces in the local tile representation. This is a structural statement about the finite codon display. It does not assert a biochemical mechanism, and it does not convert the displayed codons into a universal law of coding. The table contact supplies labels such as amino-acid or stop assignments; the window-six object supplies the coordinate boundary on which those labels can be inspected.

\paragraph{Spectral row.}
The spectral audit gives a numerical consistency check for the same local split. The recorded values are

\lambda_R &= 0.4758352843197002,\\

$$
The associated cubic residual is $-6.224354365258478\times 10^{-12}$, recorded both as the cubic-polynomial value and as the radial-polynomial value. At this resolution the residual is numerically negligible for the finite-row audit, and the spectral-radius entry repeats $0.6752479308830213$ for the $M$ side. These numbers support the internal coherence of the local coordinate split, not any claim about translation kinetics, folding, physical chemistry, or function.

\paragraph{Shape significance.}
The shape-significance experiment sampled $N=2000$ null draws with seed $42$. It required $57377$ distinct-sample attempts and incurred $55377$ duplicate retries. Among those draws, there was $1$ exact shape match, giving $p_{\mathrm{exact}}=0.0005$. There were $25$ samples with Jaccard score at least $0.9$, giving $p_{\mathrm{Jaccard}\ge 0.9}=0.0125$. Thus the observed local shape is rare under the reported finite null model, while still remaining a code-layer observation. The statistic says that the finite coordinate pattern is nontrivial under the chosen randomization; it does not say that the pattern alone performs translation or explains protein structure.

\paragraph{Amino-acid-class preserving null.}

The result is still a comparison among code-label tables and coordinate tiles; no fold, admissible molecule, activity, or organism-level mechanism is measured here.

\paragraph{Reality contact and boundary of assertion.}
The only reality contact used here is the curated standard-code table, whose observed fact is that codons are assigned to amino-acid or stop labels. That contact is sufficient for label readback and local split checking. It is not evidence for translation realization, folded structure, physical admissibility, biological function, or a universal coding mechanism. In particular, amino-acid label sequences are not protein structures, and a local codon tile is not a global biological law. Any claim beyond code-layer readback would need a separate contact that directly observes the higher layer being asserted.

\paragraph{Conclusion.}
The finite evidence supports the following bounded statement. In the six-bit codon coordinate cube, the standard-code label contact singles out a thirteen-codon local boundary set $R$ with the displayed codons, punctured-cube face vector $(13,16,4)$, coherent spectral values, rare-shape behavior under the reported null models, and complete module accounting. The support is therefore ready for a code-read claim about a local boundary tile. The same evidence blocks promotion to translation, folding, physical admissibility, function, mechanism, or universality unless an additional reality contact is supplied for that separate layer.
