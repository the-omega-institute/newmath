\paragraph{Reality contact and scope.}
The only biological contact used here is the curated standard-code label table, which assigns each codon to an amino-acid label or a stop label. That contact is sufficient for code-layer readback and for testing the local split of labels across the window-six tile. It is not a measurement of translation dynamics, folded structure, physical admissibility, realized function, or a universal biological mechanism. Amino-acid label sequences obtained from the table remain label sequences; no folded-protein structure follows from them. Likewise, the local CUN, UUR, stop, tryptophan, arginine, lysine, isoleucine, and methionine groupings in the module row are code-table observations, not claims that organisms must implement the same geometry as a biological necessity.
