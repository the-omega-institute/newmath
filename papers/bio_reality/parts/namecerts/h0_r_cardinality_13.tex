A second null model preserving amino-acid classes also used $N=2000$ and seed $42$.  In that control the observed comparison size was $20$, the exact-match count was $0$, the lower-tail count at or below the observed $M$ size was $7$, the exact-shape value was $0.0$, and the lower-tail value was $0.0035$.  These finite-row values support the claim that the thirteen-codon pattern is not a routine draw under the recorded controls.  They do not, by themselves, promote the pattern beyond a code-label observation.
