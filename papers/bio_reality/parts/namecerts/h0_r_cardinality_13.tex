\paragraph{Coordinate record.}
The record concerns the standard-code codon table viewed through a six-bit codon display.  The carrier is the codon cube $Q_6$, whose rows are the $64$ codons represented by three nucleotide positions with four choices per position.  The finite distinguished row $R$ is not introduced as a biological mechanism.  It is the set of codon labels selected by the local window-six tile test against the curated standard-code label table.  The verified cardinality computation gives
$$
\begin{aligned}
|R| &= 13.
\end{aligned}
$$
The row is exactly
$$
\begin{aligned}
R = \{&\texttt{AAA},\texttt{AGA},\texttt{AGG},\texttt{AUA},\texttt{CUA},\texttt{CUC},\texttt{CUG},\texttt{CUU},\\
&\texttt{UAA},\texttt{UAG},\texttt{UCA},\texttt{UGA},\texttt{UUA}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The same finite computation records the punctured-cube face data
$$
\begin{aligned}
(f_0,f_1,f_2)=(13,16,4),
\end{aligned}
$$
so the selected codons form a local boundary object with $13$ vertices, $16$ one-dimensional adjacencies, and $4$ two-dimensional faces in the induced codon-cube geometry.

It does not identify a ribosomal process, a protein structure, a physical energy model, or a functional phenotype.

\paragraph{Shape significance against shuffled controls.}
The finite null comparison for the $M$-shape used $N=2000$ samples with seed $42$.  The sampler attempted $57377$ distinct draws and rejected $55377$ duplicates.  Among the accepted controls, the exact shape was seen once, giving
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{\mathrm{exact}} &= 0.0005,
\end{aligned}
$$
and the high-overlap event with Jaccard score at least $0.9$ appeared $25$ times, giving
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{J\ge 0.9} &= 0.0125.
\end{aligned}
$$
A second null model preserving amino-acid classes evaluated $N=2000$ samples with the same seed.  It reported no exact shape match, so $p_{\mathrm{aa\ class\ exact}}=0.0$, and it reported $7$ samples in the lower tail at or below the observed $M$-side size.  The observed $M$-side size is $20$, and the lower-tail probability is
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{\mathrm{aa\ class\ lower\ tail}} &= 0.0035.
\end{aligned}
$$
These numbers say that the finite codon-label shape is rare under the stated shuffles.  They do not say that the shape is an evolutionary law or that the geometry by itself performs translation.

\paragraph{Leave-one tests.}
The leave-one-code-out experiment evaluated $23$ code tables and $63$ total targets.  Its baseline hit rate was $0.19821739130434785$.  The hit rate above baseline was $0.9841269841269841$, the hit rate inside $M-T$ was $0.9365079365079365$, and the hit rate inside $M-T$ together with the boundary was $0.9841269841269841$.  By family, the hit rates above baseline were $0.8333333333333334$ for alternative yeast, $1.0$ for bacterial plastid, $1.0$ for candidate other, $1.0$ for mitochondrial, and $1.0$ for nuclear ciliate, across $5$ families.  The leave-one-codon-out experiment reported recovery rate $1.0$, with $8$ predictable codons, $5$ boundary codons, $8$ cases of high closure coverage, and no anchor codons.  These tests support the use of the local row as a code-readback discriminator under the specified finite perturbations.

\paragraph{Reality contact and boundary.}
The only biological contact used here is the curated standard-code table assigning codons to amino-acid or stop labels.  That contact is sufficient for code-layer readback and for checking the local tile split.  It is not an observation of translation realization, folded structure, physical admissibility, biological function, mechanism, or universality.  In particular, an amino-acid label sequence read from the table is still a label sequence; no protein fold, stability condition, organismal role, or universal biological necessity follows from this finite row.  Any such stronger assertion would require a separate contact that directly measures the relevant higher layer.  The present chapter therefore records a finite, self-contained code-level boundary object: $|R|=13$, a punctured-cube profile $(13,16,4)$, spectral values $(\lambda_M,\lambda_R,\mu)$ as above, rare-shape null statistics, strong leave-one recovery, and a complete six-module partition, all restricted to the standard-code label table.
