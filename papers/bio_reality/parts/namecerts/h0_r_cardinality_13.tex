\paragraph{Reality contact and scope.}
The only biological contact used here is the curated standard-code label table. It supports the statement that these codons have assigned amino-acid or stop labels in that table, and it allows local split checks against those labels. It does not observe ribosomal translation, folded protein shape, thermodynamic admissibility, downstream biological function, causal machinery, or universality across biology. Consequently the conclusion is deliberately narrow: window-six codon coordinates expose a finite boundary tile whose listed codons, face vector, spectral values, null-model scores, module partition, and leave-out behavior are all recorded at the code-read layer.
