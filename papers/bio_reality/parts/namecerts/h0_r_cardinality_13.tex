\paragraph{Reality contact and boundary of assertion.}
The only biological contact used here is the curated standard-code table assigning codons to amino-acid or stop labels.  That contact can test code-level readback and the local label split of the tile, but it does not observe translation dynamics, folding, structural order, physical admissibility, biological function, or a universal mechanism.  The row therefore supports the narrow statement that window-six codon coordinates expose a finite local boundary pattern in the standard-code label table.  It does not license promotion to protein realization, folded structure, functional biology, physical law, or organism-independent necessity.  Any such stronger claim would require an additional contact that directly measures the relevant layer rather than reusing codon-to-label readback.
