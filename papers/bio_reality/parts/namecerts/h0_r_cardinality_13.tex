\paragraph{Finite codon window.}
This packet treats the standard-code codon table as a code-layer readback problem on the six-bit codon cube $Q_6$. The coordinate object is not a biochemical mechanism. It is a finite display in which codons occupy vertices, local tile faces record adjacency constraints, singleton label corners mark isolated label behavior, and three-point label classes record small codon families visible in the curated standard-code table. The biological contact used here is only the curated codon-to-label assignment: each codon is assigned an amino-acid label or a stop label. No ribosome dynamics, folding observation, thermodynamic admissibility, cellular function, or cross-organism law is contained in that contact.

\paragraph{Local scope.}
The claim is therefore narrow. Window-six codon coordinates expose a local boundary-tile structure in the standard-code label table, with $|R|=13$, face vector $(13,16,4)$, spectral values $\lambda_M=0.6752479308830213$ and $\lambda_R=0.4758352843197002$, significant sampled null behavior, and complete module accounting. From this packet alone one may not infer translation realization, folded protein structure, physical admissibility, biological function, a molecular mechanism, or universality across life. Any such assertion would require a separate contact of the corresponding kind. Amino-acid label readback remains label readback; it is not a structure experiment.
