\subsection{Growth-condition stability of the $B^*_{Q6}$ protein-abundance projection}\label{sec:bioreality-q6-growth-condition-sqp}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite row.}
This record tests whether the nine-dimensional $B^*_{Q6}$ synonymous-residual coordinate packet retains a comparable protein-abundance readback across two yeast growth conditions.  The organism is $\mathit{Saccharomyces\ cerevisiae}$, the measured response is $\log_{10}(\mathrm{abundance\_ppm})$, and the two condition rows are YEPD rich medium and SD minimal medium in the Newman GFP-tagged yeast abundance files as distributed through PAXdb.  The downloaded file headers identify the source as Newman, Nature, 2006, while the task label names Newman et al. 2009; the numerical row is therefore reported with this provenance caveat rather than promoted to a broader condition law.

The coordinate family is
$K_{AAA}$, $\mathrm{Arg}_{AGR}$, $\mathrm{Ile}_{AUA}$, $\mathrm{Leu}_{CUN/UUR}$, $\mathrm{Leu}_{UUA/UUG}$, $\mathrm{Ser}_{UCR/AGY}$, $\mathrm{Ser}_{UCA/UCG}$, $\mathrm{Thr}_{ACR/ACY}$, and $f_3$-stress.  In both conditions the residualization basis has $24$ control columns and rank $23$.  The controls are the intercept, $\log(\mathrm{cds\_len\_nt})$, the $20$ amino-acid composition proportions computed from real CDS codon counts, GC3, and the $M$-density baseline defined as the fraction of sense codons on the union support of the nine projected $B^*_{Q6}$ vectors.  The condition-specific joins contain $2442$ SD rows and $2517$ YEPD rows before taking the shared condition intersection; the paired comparison uses $2209$ shared proteins.

\paragraph{Controlled projection statistic.}
For each condition $c \in \{\mathrm{SD},\mathrm{YEPD}\}$, let $Z_c$ denote the control design, $X_c$ the residualized $9$-coordinate $B^*_{Q6}$ design, and $y_c$ the residualized protein-abundance readout.  The reported survival statistic is the controlled protein-abundance projection score
$$
\begin{aligned}
S^{QP}_c &= R^2(y_c \mid Z_c, X_c)-R^2(y_c \mid Z_c).
\end{aligned}
$$
Both condition fits are nondegenerate under the stated gate: the degenerate-$R^2$ threshold is $0.999999$, $\operatorname{rank}(X_c)=9$, $\operatorname{rank}(Z_c)=23$, and the residual degrees of freedom after $Z_c$ are $2186$ in each shared-row condition fit.  In SD minimal medium, the $B^*_{Q6}$ increment explains $131.4599852310053$ residual-energy units out of $430.9618688529857$, giving
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{QP}(\mathrm{SD}) &= 0.3050385538305022.
\end{aligned}
$$
In YEPD rich medium, the corresponding explained energy is $149.3320327478236$ out of $455.16601855320954$, giving
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{QP}(\mathrm{YEPD}) &= 0.3280825603424665.
\end{aligned}
$$
The dominant coordinate is $f_3$-stress in both conditions.  Thus the finite row supports a condition-stable direction for this one yeast abundance dataset: the strongest coordinate does not change when the same residual coordinate packet is read against SD and YEPD abundance on the $2209$ shared proteins.

\paragraph{Condition comparison.}
The two condition-specific $S^{QP}$ vectors are almost collinear.  The vector cosine is $0.9993775935182967$, the Pearson correlation is $0.9983880256003688$, the same coordinate is dominant in both rows, and the comparison used $1000$ permutations.  This is stronger than a mere positive-entry result: it says that, within this paired yeast abundance panel and under the same amino-acid, GC3, length, and $M$-density controls, the $B^*_{Q6}$ abundance projection has nearly the same coordinate geometry in rich and minimal media.

This condition row is also compatible with neighboring finite checks on the same coordinate packet.  A per-coordinate decomposition on yeast protein abundance reports $n=1258$ joined rows after the stated gates, a $5$-fold held-out design, $120$ permutations, and real-source hits for $K_{AAA}$, $\mathrm{Arg}_{AGR}$, $\mathrm{Ile}_{AUA}$, $\mathrm{Leu}_{CUN/UUR}$, $\mathrm{Leu}_{UUA/UUG}$, $\mathrm{Ser}_{UCR/AGY}$, $\mathrm{Thr}_{ACR/ACY}$, and $f_3$-stress.  A finer $f_3$-stress family decomposition reports $21$ family subcoordinates, $n=1800$ joined yeast rows, $120$ permutations, $P_{f3}$ norm squared $52.76911713166682$, target norm squared $430.42705205115925$, and $P_{f3}$ fraction of target $0.12259712041843235$.  The mRNA-stability mediation row on yeast reports $R^2_{QP}=0.2761140373962526$, $R^2_{QP\mid M}=0.24851799532219632$, an absorbed component $0.027596042074056304$, mediation fraction $0.09994436477872035$, and right-tail permutation value $0.000999000999000999$ on $5085$ joined rows.  A local ribosome-occupancy window analysis reports a description-length gate with $\lambda=0.01$, $DL=2$, and passing families $K_{AAA}$, $\mathrm{Arg}_{AGR}$, and $f_3$-stress.  These adjacent rows do not replace the growth-condition statistic, but they make clear that the present condition comparison is not an isolated spelling artifact of the coordinate names.

\paragraph{Local interpretation.}
The supported claim is deliberately narrow.  In the Newman/PAXdb yeast condition files, after amino-acid composition, CDS length, GC3, and $M$-density controls, the $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinate packet retains a positive protein-abundance association in both SD and YEPD, with $R^2_{QP}$ about $0.305$ and $0.328$, respectively.  The readback vectors are almost identical by cosine and Pearson comparison, and $f_3$-stress is the shared leading coordinate.  The finite-row conclusion is therefore condition stability of a controlled protein-abundance projection inside one organism and one abundance-study family.

\paragraph{Boundary of the claim.}
No translation mechanism follows from this row.  The response is GFP/PAXdb protein abundance, not ribosome profiling, not measured translation efficiency, and not a matched mRNA-controlled translation-efficiency statistic.  The row also does not establish folding, structural order, physical admissibility, molecular function, pathway function, causal mediation, or a universal biological rule.  A positive $S^{QP}$ value is a controlled statistical projection onto abundance; it is not a biochemical mechanism.  Any claim about ribosome-mediated translation, protein structure, biological function, perturbational causality, or cross-organism universality would require its own matched measurement layer and its own controlled statistic.
