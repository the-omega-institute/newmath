\subsection{Condition-specific protein-abundance survival for the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates}\label{sec:bioreality-q6-condition-specific-sqp-growth-condition}\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite row and readout.}
This record tests whether the nine $B^{*}_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates retain protein-abundance explanatory power in \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae} when the readout is separated by growth condition.  The measured response is $\log_{10}(\mathrm{abundance\_ppm})$ from Newman GFP-tagged yeast abundance files as served through the PAXdb condition tables.  The two condition labels are YEPD, a rich-medium condition, and SD, a minimal-medium condition.  The downloaded table headers identify the source as Newman, Nature, 2006 with \texttt{paxdb\_publication\_year=2006}, while the experiment label attached to the task names Newman et al. 2009; this chapter therefore treats the provenance as the finite downloaded PAXdb condition row rather than as a bibliographic assertion beyond that row.

\paragraph{Design matrix.}
For each joined yeast coding sequence, the source design uses the nine residual coordinates
$\texttt{K\_AAA}$, $\texttt{Arg\_AGR}$, $\texttt{Ile\_AUA}$, $\texttt{Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR}$, $\texttt{Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG}$, $\texttt{Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY}$, $\texttt{Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG}$, $\texttt{Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY}$, and $\texttt{f3\_stress}$.  The nuisance design $Z$ has 24 columns in both SD and YEPD before rank reduction, consisting of an intercept, $\log(\mathrm{cds\_len\_nt})$, the 20 amino-acid composition proportions computed from real CDS codon counts, GC3, and an $M$-density baseline equal to the fraction of sense codons lying on the union support of the nine projected $B^{*}_{Q6}$ vectors.  The fitted control rank is 23 in both condition-specific models, and the residual degrees of freedom on the shared condition join are 2186.

\paragraph{Condition-specific survival statistics.}
The finite join contains 2442 SD condition hits and 2517 YEPD condition hits before restricting to the paired comparison.  The shared condition table has 2209 proteins.  On that shared row set, the residual $B^{*}_{Q6}$ block explains a substantial fraction of the residual protein-abundance energy in both conditions:
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^{2}_{QP}(\mathrm{SD}) &= 0.3050385538305022,\\
R^{2}_{QP}(\mathrm{YEPD}) &= 0.3280825603424665.
\end{aligned}
$$
The SD fit has explained energy $131.4599852310053$ against residual response energy $430.9618688529857$.  The YEPD fit has explained energy $149.3320327478236$ against residual response energy $455.16601855320954$.  In both cases the residualized $B^{*}_{Q6}$ coordinate block has rank 9, so the positive values are not a rank-zero or collapsed-design artifact.  The dominant coordinate is $\texttt{f3\_stress}$ in both growth conditions.

\paragraph{Condition comparison.}
The two condition-specific $S^{QP}$ profiles are nearly collinear on this finite yeast table.  The cosine similarity between the two profile vectors is
$$
\begin{aligned}
\cos\bigl(S^{QP}_{\mathrm{SD}},S^{QP}_{\mathrm{YEPD}}\bigr) &= 0.9993775935182967,
\end{aligned}
$$
and the Pearson correlation is $0.9983880256003688$.  The condition comparison used 1000 permutations and retained the same dominant coordinate in both conditions.  Thus the finite result is not merely that each condition has a positive residual abundance association; it is that the direction of the nine-coordinate abundance profile is almost unchanged between the rich-medium and minimal-medium condition rows used here.

\paragraph{Coordinate-level anchoring.}
A neighboring yeast decomposition row gives a narrower coordinate audit on a unified yeast context join.  There, a 5-fold held-out design with 120 deterministic target-label permutations tested the nine single-coordinate projections against the same style of $Z$ residualization.  The decomposition table has 9 rows, base size 1800, joined size 1258, $Z$-basis rank 24, and reports real-source support for $\texttt{K\_AAA}$, $\texttt{Arg\_AGR}$, $\texttt{Ile\_AUA}$, $\texttt{Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR}$, $\texttt{Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG}$, $\texttt{Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY}$, $\texttt{Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY}$, and $\texttt{f3\_stress}$.  This does not replace the condition-specific $S^{QP}$ test, but it shows that the yeast abundance signal is not treated solely as an opaque nine-dimensional aggregate.

\paragraph{$f3$ stress decomposition.}
Because $\texttt{f3\_stress}$ is the dominant coordinate in both SD and YEPD, the family-level $f3$ audit is relevant to interpreting the finite row.  In the yeast family decomposition, $P_{f3}^{2}=52.76911713166682$, the $f3$ projection accounts for $0.12259712041843235$ of the target projection, and the full target norm is $430.42705205115925$.  The decomposition covers 21 synonymous-family subcoordinates with 5 folds and 120 permutations on $n=1800$ joined rows.  Nineteen family subcoordinates pass the real-source gate: $\texttt{F\_UUU\_to\_UUC}$, $\texttt{L\_UUA\_to\_UUG}$, $\texttt{S\_UCU\_to\_UCG}$, $\texttt{Y\_UAU\_to\_UAC}$, $\texttt{C\_UGU\_to\_UGC}$, $\texttt{P\_CCU\_to\_CCG}$, $\texttt{H\_CAU\_to\_CAC}$, $\texttt{Q\_CAA\_to\_CAG}$, $\texttt{R\_CGU\_to\_CGG}$, $\texttt{I\_AUU\_to\_AUA}$, $\texttt{T\_ACU\_to\_ACG}$, $\texttt{N\_AAU\_to\_AAC}$, $\texttt{K\_AAA\_to\_AAG}$, $\texttt{R\_AGA\_to\_AGG}$, $\texttt{V\_GUU\_to\_GUG}$, $\texttt{A\_GCU\_to\_GCG}$, $\texttt{D\_GAU\_to\_GAC}$, $\texttt{E\_GAA\_to\_GAG}$, and $\texttt{G\_GGU\_to\_GGG}$.  The condition-specific result therefore has a concrete internal support pattern: the stable dominant $f3$ coordinate is itself decomposed into many synonymous-family contributions under a related yeast abundance target.

\paragraph{Interpretation.}
The finite conclusion is a descriptive cross-layer abundance relation: after controlling for amino-acid composition, CDS length, GC3, and the $M$-density baseline, the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual coordinate block retains positive protein-abundance explanatory power in both SD and YEPD yeast condition rows, with a nearly identical nine-coordinate profile and $\texttt{f3\_stress}$ as the shared dominant coordinate.  The row supports condition-stability of the $S^{QP}$ profile within one organism, one abundance technology class, and one paired condition comparison.

\paragraph{Local boundary.}
Nothing in this finite row by itself establishes ribosome-mediated translation, protein folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, cellular mechanism, phenotype, or a universal biological rule.  The readout is GFP-based protein abundance, not ribosome profiling and not a matched mRNA-controlled translation-efficiency statistic.  The quantities above are residual projection and partial-explanation statistics on joined finite tables.  Any claim about translation dynamics, folding, biochemical function, causality, or cross-organism universality would require a separate layer-appropriate contact and its own controlled statistic.
