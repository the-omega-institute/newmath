\subsection{Growth-condition stability of the yeast $B^*_{Q6}$ protein-abundance projection}
\label{sec:biorality-q6-growth-condition-sqp}

\paragraph{Finite row and coordinate system.}
This record tests a condition-specific protein-abundance readout for the nine-dimensional $B^*_{Q6}$ synonymous-residual coordinate family in \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}.  The measured response is $\log_{10}(\mathrm{abundance\_ppm})$ from GFP-tagged yeast PAXdb condition files for YEPD rich medium and SD minimal medium.  The design uses the same nine residual coordinates in both conditions: $K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $Leu_{CUN\,/\,UUR}$, $Leu_{UUA\,/\,UUG}$, $Ser_{UCR\,/\,AGY}$, $Ser_{UCA\,/\,UCG}$, $Thr_{ACR\,/\,ACY}$, and $f3$-stress.  The covariate block contains an intercept, $\log(\mathrm{cds\_len\_nt})$, the twenty amino-acid composition proportions computed from real CDS codon counts, GC3, and an $M$-density baseline equal to the fraction of sense codons lying in the union support of the nine projected $B^*_{Q6}$ vectors.  Thus each condition has $24$ declared control columns, with control rank $23$ in the fitted row set.

\paragraph{Condition joins.}
The two condition files first produce different condition-specific joins: $2442$ rows for SD and $2517$ rows for YEPD.  The comparison is then restricted to the shared condition support, giving $2209$ common proteins.  On this shared support the residual degrees of freedom after the control block are $2186$ in each condition, and the residualized $B^*_{Q6}$ design keeps rank $9$.  The computation is therefore not a rank-saturated three-row contact and not a spelling or identifier-routing event; it is a finite protein-abundance projection on thousands of matched yeast proteins.

\paragraph{Survival scores by condition.}
Let $X_Q$ denote the residualized nine-coordinate $B^*_{Q6}$ design and let $P_c$ denote the residualized abundance vector in condition $c$.  The condition-specific score is the controlled protein-abundance projection
$$
\begin{aligned}
S^{QP}_c &= R^2(P_c \mid X_Q, Z) , \\
Z &= (1, \log(\mathrm{length}), \mathrm{AA}_{20}, \mathrm{GC3}, M\text{-density}).
\end{aligned}
$$
For SD minimal medium, the measured value is $R^2_{QP}=0.3050385538305022$, with explained energy $131.4599852310053$ against residual response energy $430.9618688529857$.  For YEPD rich medium, the measured value is $R^2_{QP}=0.3280825603424665$, with explained energy $149.3320327478236$ against residual response energy $455.16601855320954$.  In both conditions the dominant coordinate is $f3$-stress.  The finite-row result therefore says that, after amino-acid composition, length, GC3, and $M$-density have been removed, the $B^*_{Q6}$ residual family still explains a nonzero protein-abundance component in the same yeast study under two growth conditions.

\paragraph{Condition comparison.}
The two fitted $S^{QP}$ profiles are almost collinear.  The condition-comparison calculation reports cosine $0.9993775935182967$ and Pearson correlation $0.9983880256003688$ between the SD and YEPD profile vectors, using $1000$ permutations for the comparison audit.  The dominant coordinate is unchanged: $f3$-stress is the leading coordinate in both media.  In the narrow language of this record, the growth-condition contrast does not erase the $B^*_{Q6}$ abundance projection; it leaves a highly similar nine-coordinate profile across the two yeast media tested here.

\paragraph{Relation to sibling abundance and stability rows.}
The condition-specific row is consistent with the single-context yeast abundance decomposition in which the same nine-coordinate system is used on a unified yeast context join.  That decomposition reports $n=1258$ for the held-out per-coordinate abundance audit, a five-fold design, $120$ target-label permutations, $z$-basis rank $24$, and identifies real-source support for eight of the nine coordinates: $K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $Leu_{CUN\,/\,UUR}$, $Leu_{UUA\,/\,UUG}$, $Ser_{UCR\,/\,AGY}$, $Thr_{ACR\,/\,ACY}$, and $f3$-stress.  The finer $f3$-stress family decomposition uses $1800$ joined yeast proteins, $21$ synonymous-family subcoordinates, five folds, and $120$ permutations; it reports $P_{f3}$ norm squared $52.76911713166682$ and a target-fraction value $0.12259712041843235$, with $19$ named synonymous families passing the real-source gate.  These companion finite rows make the dominant $f3$-stress coordinate in the SD/YEPD comparison less opaque: the dominant coordinate is not merely a label in the condition contrast, but a coordinate family that has been decomposed into many synonymous blocks in a separate yeast abundance audit.

\paragraph{Relation to mRNA-stability rows.}
The same yeast abundance setting has an mRNA half-life mediation audit on $5085$ joined rows.  In that row, the direct $B^*_{Q6}$ abundance score is $R^2_{QP}=0.2761140373962526$, conditioning on residualized mRNA half-life gives $R^2_{QP\mid M}=0.24851799532219632$, and the absorbed portion is $0.027596042074056304$, corresponding to mediation fraction $0.09994436477872035$.  The permutation right-tail value for that mediation fraction is $0.000999000999000999$, with permutation-null mean $0.00019674487584783138$ and null $95\%$ value $0.0014571003414947088$.  A separate yeast mRNA-stability score gives $R^2_{QM}=0.05179429044975085$ on the same $5085$-row join, with cosine $0.8040935468384264$ between $S^{QM}$ and the same-join $S^{QP}$ profile and with $Leu_{CUN\,/\,UUR}$ rather than $f3$-stress as the dominant mRNA-stability coordinate.  These rows do not turn the SD/YEPD abundance result into a causal pathway.  They only show that the yeast abundance projection lives next to a measurable, but partial, mRNA-stability component.

\paragraph{Relation to ribosome-occupancy contact.}
A separate position-controlled ribosome-occupancy row joins $5754$ yeast genes after length checking, with $5754$ gene identifiers overlapping between the occupancy file and ordered CDS records and no length mismatches.  Its within-position-bin permutation audit requests $200$ permutations and obtains at least $200$ permutations for all $9$ computed families.  Under the stated $C_H>0$ rule and description-length penalty $DL=2$ with $\lambda_{DL}=0.01$, the families passing all local occupancy gates are $K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, and $f3$-stress.  This is useful context because $f3$-stress also dominates the condition-specific protein-abundance comparison, but it is not a proof that the abundance result is caused by ribosome dwell behavior.  It is a separate finite readout with its own position-bin control and its own local interpretation.

\paragraph{Local boundary of the claim.}
This chapter-level claim is deliberately narrow.  It says that the $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinate family has a positive controlled association with yeast protein abundance in two growth conditions, and that the two condition profiles are nearly identical on the shared $2209$-protein support.  It does not establish translation realization, ribosome-mediated mechanism, protein folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, phenotype, or a universal biological law.  The condition files are from a single yeast study, and the provenance note records that the task labels the contrast as Newman et al. 2009 while the downloaded PAXdb headers report Newman, \emph{Nature}, 2006 with publication year $2006$.  The readout is GFP-based protein abundance rather than ribo-seq, and the condition-specific row is not matched to an mRNA-control denominator.  Any claim about translation, folding, physical feasibility, function, mechanism, or cross-organism universality would require a separate layer-matched contact and its own controlled statistic.

\paragraph{Verdict.}
The finite row is ready for inclusion as a bounded BioReality result.  The controlled values $R^2_{QP}=0.3050385538305022$ in SD and $R^2_{QP}=0.3280825603424665$ in YEPD are both positive; the dominant coordinate is $f3$-stress in both conditions; the shared-support comparison has cosine $0.9993775935182967$ and Pearson $0.9983880256003688$; and the computation is made on $2209$ shared yeast proteins with a full amino-acid, length, GC3, and $M$-density control block.  The scientific content is therefore a condition-stable yeast protein-abundance projection for $B^*_{Q6}$, not a higher-layer biological promotion.
