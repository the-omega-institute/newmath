\subsection{Condition-specific protein-abundance survival of $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ in yeast growth media}
\label{sec:bioreality-q6-condition-specific-sqp-growth-condition}

\paragraph{Finite row set.}
This record tests whether the nine synonymous residual coordinates of $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ remain visible in a yeast protein-abundance readout after the amino-acid quotient and the standard codon-composition controls have already been imposed.  The biological rows are \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae} coding sequences joined to GFP-based protein-abundance measurements in two growth contexts, YEPD rich medium and SD minimal medium.  The readout in both cases is $\log_{10}(\mathrm{abundance\_ppm})$.  The shared comparison set contains $2209$ proteins, after condition-specific joins of $2517$ rows in YEPD and $2442$ rows in SD.  The source files are labelled in the task as Newman et al. 2009, while the downloaded PAXdb condition headers report Newman, \emph{Nature}, 2006 with \texttt{paxdb\_publication\_year=2006}; the calculation therefore treats the provenance as a concrete downloaded condition pair rather than as a bibliographic claim.

\paragraph{Coordinates and controls.}
The tested coordinate block has dimension $9$ and consists of
$\texttt{K\_AAA}$, $\texttt{Arg\_AGR}$, $\texttt{Ile\_AUA}$, $\texttt{Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR}$, $\texttt{Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG}$, $\texttt{Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY}$, $\texttt{Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG}$, $\texttt{Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY}$, and $\texttt{f3\_stress}$.  These are imported from the same $q$-vector construction used for the powered $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ translation-survival computations.  Each condition uses the same $24$-column control design: an intercept, $\log(\mathrm{cds\_len\_nt})$, the $20$ amino-acid composition proportions $A,C,D,E,F,G,H,I,K,L,M,N,P,Q,R,S,T,V,W,Y$ computed from real CDS codon counts, GC3, and the $M$-density baseline defined as the fraction of sense codons lying on the union support of the nine projected $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ coordinate vectors.  In both conditions the control rank is $23$, the residual coordinate rank is $9$, and the residual degrees of freedom after the controls are $2186$.

\paragraph{Condition-wise survival statistic.}
Let $X$ denote the $9$-column residual coordinate matrix, let $Z$ denote the control matrix, and let $P_c$ denote the condition-specific protein-abundance vector for condition $c \in \{\mathrm{SD},\mathrm{YEPD}\}$.  The reported statistic is the residualized abundance fraction explained by $X$ after projecting away $Z$:
$$
\begin{aligned}
S^{QP}_{c} &= R^2\!\left(P_c; X \mid Z\right),\\
S^{QP}_{\mathrm{SD}} &= 0.3050385538305022,\\
S^{QP}_{\mathrm{YEPD}} &= 0.3280825603424665.
\end{aligned}
$$
For SD, the explained residual energy is $131.4599852310053$ against a residual readout energy of $430.9618688529857$.  For YEPD, the explained residual energy is $149.3320327478236$ against a residual readout energy of $455.16601855320954$.  Both finite estimates are well below the stated degeneracy cutoff $0.999999$, so the positive values are not rank-saturated perfect fits.  In both media the dominant coordinate is $\texttt{f3\_stress}$, giving a condition-stable leading direction for this particular abundance contact.

\paragraph{Between-condition geometry.}
The two nine-entry $S^{QP}$ profiles are almost collinear across the two growth contexts.  The finite comparison reports
$$
\begin{aligned}
\cos\!\left(S^{QP}_{\mathrm{SD}},S^{QP}_{\mathrm{YEPD}}\right) &= 0.9993775935182967,\\
\mathrm{Pearson}\!\left(S^{QP}_{\mathrm{SD}},S^{QP}_{\mathrm{YEPD}}\right) &= 0.9983880256003688,
\end{aligned}
$$
with $1000$ permutations used for the condition-profile comparison and with the dominant coordinate unchanged.  Thus the finite observation is not that the two media select unrelated codon-residual modes, but that the same $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ abundance direction remains visible in both environments, with a modest increase of total residual abundance fraction in YEPD relative to SD.

\paragraph{Coordinate-level corroboration.}
A separate per-coordinate reducibility calculation on the unified yeast abundance join reports $n=1258$ joined rows, a $5$-fold held-out design, $120$ deterministic target-label permutations, and a $24$-rank sibling control basis.  In that analysis, at least one single $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ coordinate passes the held-out/permutation gate as a real abundance source, and the coordinates with real-source support are $\texttt{K\_AAA}$, $\texttt{Arg\_AGR}$, $\texttt{Ile\_AUA}$, $\texttt{Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR}$, $\texttt{Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG}$, $\texttt{Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY}$, $\texttt{Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY}$, and $\texttt{f3\_stress}$.  The missing member from that real-source list is $\texttt{Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG}$, which remains present in the nine-coordinate table but is not listed among the held-out abundance sources in that finite audit.  This supports the interpretation that the condition-specific result is not merely a joint-coordinate artifact: yeast abundance contains recoverable signal along individual residual axes as well as in the full nine-dimensional block.

\paragraph{Fine structure of the $f3$ leading direction.}
Because $\texttt{f3\_stress}$ is the dominant coordinate in both SD and YEPD, the family decomposition is relevant to the local reading of the condition result.  The yeast $f3$ family calculation decomposes the coordinate into $21$ synonymous-family subcoordinates on $n=1800$ joined rows, with $5$ folds and $120$ deterministic permutations.  The full $f3$ projection has squared norm $52.76911713166682$ and accounts for $0.12259712041843235$ of the target energy in that decomposition, against target squared norm $430.42705205115925$.  Nineteen family subcoordinates are reported as real abundance sources: $\texttt{F\_UUU\_to\_UUC}$, $\texttt{L\_UUA\_to\_UUG}$, $\texttt{S\_UCU\_to\_UCG}$, $\texttt{Y\_UAU\_to\_UAC}$, $\texttt{C\_UGU\_to\_UGC}$, $\texttt{P\_CCU\_to\_CCG}$, $\texttt{H\_CAU\_to\_CAC}$, $\texttt{Q\_CAA\_to\_CAG}$, $\texttt{R\_CGU\_to\_CGG}$, $\texttt{I\_AUU\_to\_AUA}$, $\texttt{T\_ACU\_to\_ACG}$, $\texttt{N\_AAU\_to\_AAC}$, $\texttt{K\_AAA\_to\_AAG}$, $\texttt{R\_AGA\_to\_AGG}$, $\texttt{V\_GUU\_to\_GUG}$, $\texttt{A\_GCU\_to\_GCG}$, $\texttt{D\_GAU\_to\_GAC}$, $\texttt{E\_GAA\_to\_GAG}$, and $\texttt{G\_GGU\_to\_GGG}$.  The leading coordinate in the condition comparison therefore has a broad synonymous-family footprint rather than being reducible to a single codon pair in the recorded yeast abundance contact.

\paragraph{Relation to mRNA-stability mediation.}
The condition result is an abundance association, not a causal path.  A separate yeast mediation calculation joins $5085$ rows with $\log_{10}$ protein abundance and $\log_{10}$ mRNA half-life, using the same nine $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ coordinates and the same $24$-column control family.  There the direct abundance fraction is $R^2_{QP}=0.2761140373962526$, the abundance fraction after conditioning on mRNA half-life is $R^2_{QP\mid M}=0.24851799532219632$, and the absorbed fraction is $0.027596042074056304$.  The reported mediation fraction is $0.09994436477872035$, with a deterministic residual-mediator permutation mean $0.00019674487584783138$, null $95$th percentile $0.0014571003414947088$, and right-tail permutation value $0.000999000999000999$ over $1000$ permutations.  This adjacent finite calculation shows that part of the yeast abundance association can be shared with mRNA half-life, but it does not convert the growth-condition comparison into a causal codon-to-mRNA-to-protein mechanism.

\paragraph{Local verdict.}
The finite row supports a ready BioReality statement at the protein-abundance association layer: in one yeast study and two growth conditions, the nine-dimensional $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual block remains predictive of residualized protein abundance after amino-acid composition, GC3, length, and $M$-density controls, with $S^{QP}_{\mathrm{SD}}=0.3050385538305022$ and $S^{QP}_{\mathrm{YEPD}}=0.3280825603424665$.  The condition profiles are nearly identical in direction, with cosine $0.9993775935182967$, Pearson correlation $0.9983880256003688$, and the same dominant coordinate $\texttt{f3\_stress}$.  The appropriate reading is therefore a condition-stable statistical protein-abundance residual in this yeast contact.  No direct translation readout, ribosome-mediated process, folding state, physical admissibility, molecular function, biochemical mechanism, or universal biological law follows from these rows unless a separate matched contact supplies the corresponding measurement and its own controlled statistic.
