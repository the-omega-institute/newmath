\subsection{Condition-specific survival of the $B^*_{Q6}$ protein-abundance projection}
\label{sec:bioreality-q6-condition-specific-sqp}

\paragraph{Finite object under test.}
This record tests whether the nine synonymous-residual coordinates collected as $B^*_{Q6}$ retain a condition-stable protein-abundance association in one measured yeast study.  The organism is \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}.  The response variable is $\log_{10}(\mathrm{abundance\_ppm})$ from GFP-tagged yeast protein-abundance tables, compared between the rich-medium condition YEPD and the minimal-medium condition SD.  The computation uses the same nine coordinate names in both conditions:
$$
\begin{aligned}
Q=\{&K\_AAA,\ Arg\_AGR,\ Ile\_AUA,\ Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR,\ Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG,\\
&Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY,\ Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG,\ Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY,\ f3\_stress\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The control design has $24$ columns in each condition: an intercept, $\log(\mathrm{cds\_len\_nt})$, the $20$ amino-acid composition proportions computed from real CDS codon counts, GC3, and the $M$-density baseline defined as the fraction of sense codons lying on the union support of the nine projected $B^*_{Q6}$ vectors.  The fitted control rank is $23$ in each condition, and after the shared join the residual degrees of freedom are $2186$.

\paragraph{Joined rows and survival statistic.}
The condition-specific input tables contain $2517$ joined YEPD rows and $2442$ joined SD rows before enforcing the common protein set.  The comparison is then made on $2209$ shared proteins.  For each condition $c\in\{\mathrm{YEPD},\mathrm{SD}\}$, the reported scalar is the $Z$-controlled projection fraction
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{QP}(c)
  = \frac{\|\operatorname{Proj}_{\widetilde Q_c}(\widetilde P_c)\|^2}
         {\|\widetilde P_c\|^2},
\end{aligned}
$$
where $\widetilde Q_c$ and $\widetilde P_c$ denote the residualized $B^*_{Q6}$ coordinate matrix and residualized protein-abundance vector after the declared controls.  The finite values are
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{QP}(\mathrm{SD}) &= 0.3050385538305022,\\
R^2_{QP}(\mathrm{YEPD}) &= 0.3280825603424665.
\end{aligned}
$$
The SD fit has explained energy $131.4599852310053$ against response residual energy $430.9618688529857$.  The YEPD fit has explained energy $149.3320327478236$ against response residual energy $455.16601855320954$.  In both conditions the residualized coordinate rank is $9$, so the nine-coordinate comparison is not a collapsed one-dimensional fit.

\paragraph{Condition comparison.}
The survival-vector comparison is sharply stable across the two growth conditions.  The coordinatewise $S^{QP}$ vectors have cosine
$$
\begin{aligned}
\cos(S^{QP}_{\mathrm{SD}},S^{QP}_{\mathrm{YEPD}})=0.9993775935182967
\end{aligned}
$$
and Pearson correlation
$$
\begin{aligned}
\rho(S^{QP}_{\mathrm{SD}},S^{QP}_{\mathrm{YEPD}})=0.9983880256003688.
\end{aligned}
$$
The dominant coordinate is $f3\_stress$ in both SD and YEPD, and the comparison used $1000$ permutations for the reported condition-contrast diagnostic.  The finite-row verdict is therefore not merely that each condition has a positive projection fraction; it is that the direction of the nine-coordinate abundance projection is almost unchanged between the two media on the common $2209$-protein panel.

\paragraph{Coordinate-level support.}
A separate per-coordinate decomposition on the unified yeast context join gives $n=1258$ with $5$ held-out folds, $120$ deterministic target-label permutations, and a $24$-rank $Z$ basis. This matters because the condition result is not only a whole-block statistic; it is compatible with a coordinate audit in which most individual axes have held-out support against permutation.

\begin{aligned}

\end{aligned}
$$

\paragraph{Local ribosome-contact context.}
A distinct ribosome-occupancy contact tests synonymous choices in supplied $5'$ and $3'$ codon-window arrays using position-bin and amino-acid-bin control.  That run joins $5754$ genes after length checking, has no length mismatches, requests $200$ within-bin permutations, and obtains computable nulls for all $9$ families.  Under the stated $C_H>0$ rule and the description-length penalty with $\lambda_{DL}=0.01$ and $DL=2$, the families passing all gates are $K\_AAA$, $Arg\_AGR$, and $f3\_stress$.  This supports a local occupancy separation for some synonymous families, but it is not the same statistic as the condition-specific protein-abundance $S^{QP}$ comparison.  It is included here only to delimit the nearby translation-readout contact: the abundance comparison above is not converted into ribosome-mediated causality by the existence of a separate occupancy statistic.

\paragraph{Boundary of the claim.}
The finite conclusion is restricted to a measured yeast protein-abundance comparison across two growth conditions.  The result says that, after amino-acid composition, GC3, CDS length, and $M$-density controls, the nine-dimensional $B^*_{Q6}$ residual block explains a positive fraction of residual protein-abundance variance in both SD and YEPD, with $f3\_stress$ dominant in both and with nearly identical coordinate direction across conditions.  It does not establish a ribosome mechanism, a translation-efficiency mechanism, protein folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, phenotype, or a universal biological law.  Such stronger statements would require their own layer-matched measurements and their own controlled statistics.  The source provenance is also finite: the task labels the contrast as Newman et al. 2009, while the downloaded PAXdb condition-file headers report Newman, Nature, 2006 and $\mathrm{paxdb\_publication\_year}=2006$; the mathematical record therefore relies on the downloaded condition rows and reports that provenance discrepancy without upgrading it into a biological interpretation.
