\subsection{Translation-conditioned RNA half-life bridge for the Crown-seq m6Am coordinate packet}
\label{subsec:bior-reality-m6am-crownseq-rna-half-life-rbp-bridge}

\paragraph{Finite panel.}
This record concerns a finite matched RNA panel in which coding-transcript or CDS rows carry a $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinate label, a same-row translation readout boundary, an RNA half-life readout boundary, matched abundance controls, and a proposed decay-factor RNA-binding-protein bridge.  The biological subject is not m6Am methylation alone, but the stricter question of whether a coordinate-associated RNA half-life effect can be read as a bounded cross-layer RBP-mediated decay relation after translation conditioning.  The row carrier therefore has four separations built into its meaning: high-coordinate versus low-coordinate synonymous residual labels after amino-acid quotienting; measured decay-factor RBP occupancy present versus absent or reduced at the editable RNA window; wild-type target-RBP rescue versus binding-defective or non-target control; and a translation-readout-conditioned half-life residual rather than an abundance, transcription, or isoform artifact.

\paragraph{Observed coordinate surface.}
The Crown-seq m6Am run supplies a concrete finite coordinate surface.  The parsed panel contains $n=31886$ rows and $19406$ distinct sites, with $20942$ rows in the variable subset and $3230$ rows in the low-tail subset below $0.8$.  The saturated high-stoichiometry fraction is $0.343223$.  The run is cross-cell concordant and classifies the m6Am verdict as crossing the finite statistical boundary for the methylation coordinate itself.  The main reported comparison uses $1200$ observed blocks and a GC-matched null with
$$
\begin{aligned}
  p &= 0.000833,\\
  p_{\mathrm{full}} &= 0.000833,\\
  p_{\mathrm{lowtail}} &= 0.000833.
\end{aligned}
$$
The corresponding partial correlations are $0.268094$ on the full panel, $0.168335$ on the variable subset, and $0.098666$ on the low-tail subset.  The null means are $0.003666$ for the full panel, $0.002173$ for the variable subset, and $0.001263$ for the low-tail subset.  Positive-control structure is also present: PCIF1 knockout is represented on $31886$ rows with mean $0.009592$ and median $0.0$; the plus-two $G$ stratum has $14263$ rows with mean $0.901842$, while the plus-two $A$ stratum has $6902$ rows with mean $0.837479$, giving a $G$ versus $A$ contrast of $0.064362$.  The SSCA/GC comparison has $707$ rows at level $0.957884$, and the VA/RR comparison has $1086$ rows at level $0.71252$, with an SSCA/GC minus VA/RR relative response of $0.245364$.

\paragraph{Bridge requirement.}
These values establish a finite m6Am positional and stoichiometric readout, not by themselves an RNA-decay mechanism.  The proposed bridge is allowed only if the same rows also contain a translation readout, RNA half-life, measured occupancy of a prespecified decay-factor RBP at the relevant local window, target-RBP loss, wild-type rescue, and a failure of binding-defective or non-target controls to restore the effect.  The auxiliary RBP-binding contact available in the surrounding finite record is much weaker than that: it reports $900$ total peaks and $3$ cross-RBP concordant cases in the RBNS/eCLIP binding route.  That contact can show that RBP binding surfaces exist as measurable finite objects, but it is not the same-organism same-window decay-factor occupancy/loss/rescue matrix demanded here.

\paragraph{Null model.}
The operational null is exchangeability of high- and low-coordinate labels with respect to RNA half-life after the declared controls are applied.  Those controls include transcription rate, local RNA structure, motif density, GC and dinucleotide composition, codon-pair context, splice or isoform state, transcript abundance, batch, amino-acid composition, and the same-row translation readout.  In symbols, the intended residual comparison is only meaningful after conditioning on the control block $Z$:
$$
\begin{aligned}
  H_0:\quad
  \Delta_{\mathrm{half\mbox{-}life}}(Q_{\mathrm{high}},Q_{\mathrm{low}}\mid Z,T)
  &=0,\\
  Z&=(\mathrm{transcription},\mathrm{structure},\mathrm{motif},\mathrm{GC},\mathrm{dinucleotide},\\
    &\quad \mathrm{codon\ pair},\mathrm{isoform},\mathrm{abundance},\mathrm{batch},\mathrm{amino\ acid}).
\end{aligned}
$$
A nonzero half-life residual is still not sufficient for the RBP bridge.  The positive bridge condition also requires that the residual follows measured local occupancy of the prespecified decay-factor RBP, collapses when that RBP is lost, returns under wild-type rescue, and fails to return under binding-defective or non-target rescue.  If any of those finite-row tests are absent or fail, the coordinate remains a bounded descriptor or a finite RNA-stability association rather than an RBP-mediated cross-layer relation.

\paragraph{Boundary.}
The Crown-seq numbers license a finite statement about m6Am flanking identity and stoichiometry under the reported GC-matched and cross-cell checks.  They do not license translation realization, protein output, protein folding, physical admissibility, biological function, phenotype, adaptation, causation, RNA-decay mechanism, or a universal biological law.  The same-row translation readout is a conditioning boundary, not proof of translation as the mechanism.  The RNA half-life contact is a half-life surface, not measured RBP occupancy.  The RBP-binding contact is a binding surface, not target-RBP loss or rescue.  A positive BioReality bridge for this packet therefore remains conditional on adding the missing finite rows for local decay-factor RBP occupancy, coordinate/occupancy decoupling, target-RBP loss, wild-type rescue, and binding-defective or non-target controls, followed by the declared translation-conditioned half-life statistic on the joined panel.
