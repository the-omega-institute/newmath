\subsection{Crown-seq $m^{6}A_m$ flanking identity as a finite stoichiometry row system}
\label{sec:bioreality-m6am-flanking-identity-crownseq}

\paragraph{Finite carrier.}
The row system considered here is a finite Crown-seq panel of transcript-end windows with measured $m^{6}A_m$ stoichiometry, frozen local position-identity labels, GC-aware flanking sequence controls, and cell-context replication.  The principal verified table contains $n=31886$ assayed rows and $19406$ distinct coordinate-bearing entries.  A variable-stoichiometry subpanel contains $20942$ rows, and the low-tail subpanel below the $0.8$ stoichiometry cutoff contains $3230$ rows.  The empirical high-saturation mass is not total: the fraction at or above $0.95$ is $0.343223$, so the panel contains both saturated and variable sites.  This matters because the claimed object is not merely the presence of an annotated cap-proximal modification; it is the finite relation between local flanking identity and measured modification level after the declared controls are applied.

\paragraph{Observed finite statistic.}
The Crown-seq run records a positive finite signal for flanking identity.  The main statistic was computed with $1200$ null draws.  The full-panel partial correlation after the stated controls is $0.268094$, the variable-subset partial correlation is $0.168335$, and the low-tail partial correlation is $0.098666$.  The GC-matched null gives
$$
\begin{aligned}
  p &= 0.000833,\\
  p_{\mathrm{full}} &= 0.000833,\\
  p_{\mathrm{lowtail}} &= 0.000833.
\end{aligned}
$$
The corresponding null means are small in the scale of the statistic: $0.003666$ on the full panel, $0.002173$ on the variable subset, and $0.001263$ on the low-tail subpanel.  The recorded verdict for this finite panel is therefore a boundary crossing for the $m^{6}A_m$ flanking-identity stoichiometry claim: the row-wise sequence identity signal survives the GC-matched comparison and remains visible in the non-saturated rows.

\paragraph{Positive-control calibration.}
The same record contains a biochemical calibration check rather than only a model score.  The positive-control fields reproduce the expected cap-proximal identity behavior: the plus-two guanine group has mean $0.901842$ over $14263$ rows, the plus-two adenine group has mean $0.837479$ over $6902$ rows, and the recorded plus-two $G$ versus $A$ difference is $0.064362$.  The minus-one cytosine contrast is $0.046475$.  The SSCA-GC class has level $0.957884$ over $707$ rows, while the VA/RR comparison class has level $0.71252$ over $1086$ rows, giving an SSCA-GC minus VA/RR contrast of $0.245364$.  The PCIF1-knockout control is also carried as finite data: $31886$ rows are present, with mean $0.009592$ and median $0.0$.  These numbers make the chapter a measured stoichiometry record, not a free-standing sequence grammar.

\paragraph{Relation to a nearby $m^{6}A$ row system.}
A separate RNA-modification panel for $m^{6}A$ stoichiometry over exon architecture reports $n=60000$ rows, $49668$ distinct entries, a coverage-confound correlation of $-0.135922814$, and cross-condition concordance.  That second panel is not pooled with the Crown-seq $m^{6}A_m$ table.  Its role here is comparative only: both records show that finite RNA-modification stoichiometry can be attached to local transcript architecture while keeping coverage and condition checks explicit.  The $m^{6}A_m$ claim in this chapter remains specific to Crown-seq cap-proximal windows and the verified flanking-identity statistic above.

\paragraph{Null model.}
The matched null says that, after GC and coverage-aware controls and after freezing the position-identity encoding, the local flanking labels should be exchangeable with respect to measured $m^{6}A_m$ stoichiometry.  The observed panel rejects that exchangeability at the recorded finite resolution: every reported GC-matched $p$-value is $0.000833$, and the positive-control contrasts move in the expected biochemical direction.  The row system therefore supports a bounded cross-layer statement from local RNA sequence context to measured $m^{6}A_m$ stoichiometry.  It does not by itself identify a universal sequence law, nor does it turn the coordinate labels into a causal mechanism.

\paragraph{Boundary of interpretation.}
The claim stops at measured RNA-modification stoichiometry in a finite Crown-seq table.  It does not imply translation output, ribosome occupancy, RNA half-life, decay-factor RBP occupancy, target-RBP perturbation behavior, protein abundance, folding, physical admissibility, biological function, phenotype, adaptation, causation, or universality.  In particular, a separate RNA half-life or decay-factor RBP bridge would require same-row translation readout, matched mRNA controls, local RBP occupancy at the editable RNA window, target-RBP loss, wild-type rescue, and binding-defective or non-target rescue controls.  None of those missing rows is supplied by the Crown-seq $m^{6}A_m$ stoichiometry panel.  Thus the positive result here is a finite flanking-identity-to-$m^{6}A_m$ stoichiometry readout, while RNA-decay mechanism and translation-conditioned RBP mediation remain outside the licensed conclusion.
