\subsection{m6Am Flanking Identity Stoichiometry and the RNA-Decay Bridge Boundary}\label{sec:bioreality-m6am-flanking-identity-stoichiometry-crownseq}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite panel.}
The finite object for this record is a CROWN-seq methylation panel indexed by transcript-level $m^6Am$ candidate rows, local hg38 flanking sequence, methylation stoichiometry, cell-condition provenance, GC-matched controls, and positive-control diagnostics. The verified run contains $31{,}886$ rows and $19{,}406$ distinct row identities. The variable-stoichiometry subset has $20{,}942$ rows, and the low-tail subset with stoichiometry below $0.8$ has $3{,}230$ rows. The saturated component, defined by stoichiometry at least $0.95$, occupies fraction $0.343223$ of the finite panel. These numbers make the chapter a bounded row statement: it is about measured $m^6Am$ stoichiometry and local flanking identity in a parsed CROWN-seq table, not about protein output or an unconstrained sequence law.

\paragraph{Observed stoichiometric contrast.}
The main statistic is a position-identity association that survives the declared GC and coverage checks. In the full panel the partial rank correlation is $0.268094$ with a GC-matched null probability $0.000833$. On the low-tail subset the partial rank correlation is $0.098666$, again with GC-matched null probability $0.000833$. On the variable subset the partial rank correlation is $0.168335$. The permutation scale used by the run is visible in the finite counts: the observed statistic exceeded the GC-matched null in $1{,}200$ effective comparisons, while the recorded null means were $0.003666$ for the full panel, $0.001263$ for the low-tail panel, and $0.002173$ for the variable panel. Thus the claim licensed here is not merely that a motif was noticed; it is that a fixed local identity readout remains nonexchangeable against the matched nulls on several finite row restrictions.

$$
\begin{aligned}
N_{\mathrm{rows}} &= 31{,}886,\\
N_{\mathrm{distinct}} &= 19{,}406,\\
N_{\mathrm{variable}} &= 20{,}942,\\
N_{\mathrm{lowtail}<0.8} &= 3{,}230,\\
\rho_{\mathrm{full}\mid GC} &= 0.268094,\\
\rho_{\mathrm{lowtail}\mid GC} &= 0.098666,\\
\rho_{\mathrm{variable}\mid GC} &= 0.168335,\\
p_{\mathrm{GC\ null}} &= 0.000833.
\end{aligned}
$$

\paragraph{Positive controls.}
The positive-control block anchors the methylation assay to known $m^6Am$ behavior rather than to a purely formal sequence partition. PCIF1-knockout rows number $31{,}886$, with mean signal $0.009592$ and median signal $0.0$. The $+2G$ class has $14{,}263$ rows with mean $0.901842$, while the $+2A$ class has $6{,}902$ rows with mean $0.837479$; their recorded contrast is $0.064362$. The $-1C$ versus non-$C$ contrast is $0.046475$. The SSCA/GC positive-control level is $0.957884$ on $707$ rows, while the VA/RR level is $0.71252$ on $1{,}086$ rows, giving an SSCA/GC minus VA/RR contrast of $0.245364$. The run therefore reports both a principal flanking-identity statistic and an assay-specific control pattern, with the control verdict marked as reproduced.

\paragraph{Finite verdict.}
For this chapter the finite verdict is that the $m^6Am$ flanking-identity row system crosses the methylation-stoichiometry boundary. The recorded checks are all affirmative: the CROWN-seq table was parsed, the hg38 flanks were computed chromosome-wise, positional identity was fixed before testing, methylation was treated as a continuous readout, GC coverage was controlled, the GC-matched null was evaluated, the positive control reproduced, and cross-cell concordance was true. In symbols, if $x_i$ denotes the frozen local identity feature for row $i$, $s_i$ the measured $m^6Am$ stoichiometry, and $G_i$ the GC and coverage control block, the licensed statement is the finite nonexchangeability of $s_i$ with respect to $x_i$ after conditioning on $G_i$:

$$
\begin{aligned}
H_0 &: (s_i \mid G_i, x_i = \mathrm{high}) \sim (s_i \mid G_i, x_i = \mathrm{low}),\\
\widehat{\rho}_{\mathrm{full}} &= 0.268094,\\
p_{\mathrm{matched}} &= 0.000833.
\end{aligned}
$$

This is a stoichiometry statement over a finite methylation table. It does not by itself identify a decay-factor RBP, a translation-conditioned RNA half-life mechanism, a protein-level consequence, or an adaptive explanation.

\paragraph{RNA-decay bridge test.}
The supplied conjectural bridge asks for a stronger object than the CROWN-seq row system supplies. A positive RNA-decay bridge would require rows that simultaneously carry a $B^*_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinate label, a same-row translation readout, matched mRNA abundance, RNA half-life, measured local occupancy of a prespecified decay-factor RBP, target-RBP loss, wild-type target-RBP rescue, and binding-defective or non-target controls. Those rows are not present in the verified CROWN-seq fact. The separate RBP-binding audit confirms that RBP data are a different finite surface: the RBNS/eCLIP contact contains $900$ peak rows in total and reports cross-RBP concordance count $3$, but it is not joined here to the same transcript windows, translation rows, half-life rows, RBP-loss rows, or rescue rows. Likewise, the TOPScore/mTOR translation audit records $6{,}494$ rows for a translation readout source, but those rows are not a same-row RNA half-life and RBP perturbation panel for this $m^6Am$ chapter.

\paragraph{Local null.}
The null for the methylation part is row exchangeability of high- and low-coordinate local identity labels after GC and coverage matching. That null is rejected at the recorded finite scale. The null for the RNA-decay RBP bridge is different: after transcription-rate, local RNA-structure, motif-density, GC and dinucleotide composition, codon-pair context, splice or isoform state, transcript abundance, batch, amino-acid composition, and same-row translation-readout controls, high- and low-coordinate labels remain exchangeable with respect to RNA half-life unless a prespecified decay-factor RBP occupancy and perturbation/rescue pattern is also observed. The current finite facts do not reject that stronger bridge null.

\paragraph{Permitted and excluded readings.}
The permitted reading is narrow and concrete: local flanking identity is a finite predictor of measured $m^6Am$ stoichiometry in the parsed CROWN-seq panel, with $31{,}886$ rows, cross-cell concordance, positive-control reproduction, and GC-matched null probability $0.000833$. The excluded readings are equally important. No translation realization follows from the methylation table alone. No ribosome occupancy, RNA half-life mechanism, decay-factor RBP mechanism, protein abundance, folding, physical admissibility, biological function, phenotype, adaptation, causation, or universal sequence law follows unless a separate layer-matched contact supplies the corresponding rows and the declared finite statistic is computed on the joined panel. In particular, an RNA half-life association without measured local decay-factor RBP occupancy, collapse after target-RBP loss, wild-type rescue return, and failure of binding-defective or non-target rescue remains only an RNA-stability association, not an RBP bridge.
