\subsection{CROWN-seq m6Am Flanking Identity as a Finite Stoichiometry Record}
\label{sec:bioreality-m6am-flanking-identity-crownseq}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite rows.}
The record considered here is a finite CROWN-seq panel of transcript-end methylation rows, not a general law about RNA chemistry.  The row set for
$h3.cross\_layer\_relation.rna\_m6am\_methylation.m6am\_flanking\_identity\_stoichiometry\_crownseq$ contains $31{,}886$ assayed rows and $19{,}406$ distinct sites after the declared parsing and identity filters.  The finite scalar summary reports $20{,}942$ rows in the variable subset, $3{,}230$ rows in the low-tail set with methylation below $0.8$, and a saturated-site fraction of $0.343223$ at the threshold $0.95$.  Cross-cell agreement is recorded as true, so the chapter treats the phenomenon as a reproducible finite association between local flanking identity and measured m6Am stoichiometry in the parsed CROWN-seq table.

\paragraph{Measured statistic.}
The primary finite statistic is the residual association of position identity with continuous m6Am level after the declared GC and coverage controls.  The stored computation gives a full-panel partial correlation of $0.268094$, a variable-subset partial correlation of $0.168335$, and a low-tail partial correlation of $0.098666$.  The matched null is explicitly finite: the observed count is $1200$, while the GC-matched null means are $0.003666$ for the full panel, $0.002173$ for the variable subset, and $0.001263$ for the low-tail subset.  The recorded Monte Carlo tail probability is
$$
\begin{aligned}
  p_{\mathrm{GC\ matched}} &= 0.000833,\\
  p_{\mathrm{full}} &= 0.000833,\\
  p_{\mathrm{low\ tail}} &= 0.000833.
\end{aligned}
$$
These values license only the bounded conclusion that flanking identity contributes measurable information about m6Am stoichiometry within this finite CROWN-seq surface.

\paragraph{Positive-control behavior.}
The same run includes internal biochemical sanity checks.  The positive-control block reports a PCIF1-knockout mean of $0.009592$ and median $0.0$ over $31{,}886$ rows, consistent with the intended methyltransferase-sensitive readout.  At the $+2$ position, $G$ rows have mean $0.901842$ over $14{,}263$ rows, while $A$ rows have mean $0.837479$ over $6{,}902$ rows, giving a $G$ versus $A$ difference of $0.064362$.  The minus-one $C$ versus non-$C$ contrast is $0.046475$.  The SSCA-GC subgroup has level $0.957884$ over $707$ rows, while the VA-RR subgroup has level $0.71252$ over $1{,}086$ rows; the recorded SSCA-GC minus VA-RR readback is $0.245364$.  These controls make the finite m6Am panel scientifically interpretable as a methylation-stoichiometry record rather than merely an identifier table.

\paragraph{Cross-record calibration.}
The related RNA-modification record
$h3.cross\_layer\_relation.rna\_m6a\_stoichiometry.m6a\_stoichiometry\_exon\_architecture\_glori$ supplies an independent finite comparison class: it contains $60{,}000$ rows, $49{,}668$ distinct entries, cross-condition concordance recorded as true, and a coverage-confound correlation of $-0.135922814$.  This comparison does not prove the m6Am claim, but it shows that the BioReality table can separately encode another RNA methylation stoichiometry surface with its own row count and confound diagnostic.  The RBP-contact record
$h3.cross\_layer\_relation.rbp\_binding.rbns\_affinity\_eclip\_binding\_encode$ is also distinct: it contains $900$ total peaks and records cross-RBP concordance $3$.  That RBP record is relevant only as a separate binding-contact class; it is not a decay-factor occupancy, perturbation, or rescue panel for the present CROWN-seq m6Am rows.

\paragraph{Finite assertion.}
Within this chapter, the admissible claim is the following bounded statement: among the $31{,}886$ parsed CROWN-seq rows, local flanking identity retains measurable association with continuous m6Am stoichiometry after the recorded GC and coverage controls, with cross-cell concordance and positive-control behavior preserved.  In compact form, the accepted finite readout is
$$
\begin{aligned}
  N &= 31{,}886,\\
  D &= 19{,}406,\\
  \rho_{\mathrm{partial,full}} &= 0.268094,\\
  \rho_{\mathrm{partial,variable}} &= 0.168335,\\
  \rho_{\mathrm{partial,lowtail}} &= 0.098666,\\
  p &= 0.000833.
\end{aligned}
$$
The module classification is therefore a finite cross-layer RNA-modification stoichiometry record, not a translation, protein-output, or phenotype record.

\paragraph{Local boundary.}
Nothing in this finite CROWN-seq table by itself establishes translation realization, RNA half-life mediation, decay-factor RBP mechanism, protein abundance, protein folding, physical admissibility, biological function, system phenotype, adaptation, causation, or universality.  A translation-conditioned RNA half-life or RBP-bridge claim would require a separate same-organism and same-window panel carrying translation readout, matched mRNA abundance, RNA half-life, measured decay-factor RBP occupancy, target-RBP loss, wild-type rescue, and binding-defective or non-target controls.  The present evidence does not contain those rows.  Thus the m6Am result crosses the finite stoichiometry boundary for flanking identity in CROWN-seq, while all mechanism and higher-layer interpretations remain outside the licensed conclusion.
