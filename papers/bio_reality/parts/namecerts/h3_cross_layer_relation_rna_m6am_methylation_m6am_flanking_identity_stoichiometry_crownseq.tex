\subsection{CROWN-seq m6Am flanking identity as a finite cross-layer contact}
\label{sec:bioreality-m6am-flanking-identity-crownseq}

\paragraph{Finite panel.}
The record considered here is a finite transcript-window panel for cap-adjacent RNA methylation, with CROWN-seq-derived $m6Am$ stoichiometry attached to local flanking identity labels.  The verified run used $n=31886$ rows and $19406$ distinct genomic or transcript-window identities.  The variable-stoichiometry subset contained $20942$ rows, and the low-tail subset with stoichiometry below $0.8$ contained $3230$ rows.  A saturated high-stoichiometry region was present but not universal: the fraction of rows with stoichiometry at least $0.95$ was $0.343223$.  The finite readout is therefore not a binary presence call; it is a bounded stoichiometric surface over matched local sequence windows.

\paragraph{Observed statistic.}
The principal finite statistic records a positive flanking-identity association after GC and coverage controls.  The run reported cross-cell concordance as true, with the CROWN-seq parser, chromwise hg38 flank extraction, continuous $m6Am$ scoring, position-identity freezing, GC-coverage control, GC-matched null, positive control, and final $m6Am$ verdict all passing.  The main observed statistic used $B=1200$ matched null draws and returned
$$
\begin{aligned}
  p &= 0.000833,\\
  p_{\mathrm{full}} &= 0.000833,\\
  p_{\mathrm{lowtail}} &= 0.000833,\\
  \rho_{\mathrm{full}\mid GC} &= 0.268094,\\
  \rho_{\mathrm{variable}\mid GC} &= 0.168335,\\
  \rho_{\mathrm{lowtail}\mid GC} &= 0.098666.
\end{aligned}
$$
The corresponding null means were $0.003666$ on the full panel, $0.002173$ on the variable subset, and $0.001263$ on the low-tail subset.  These numbers license a finite statement about local identity and measured $m6Am$ stoichiometry.  They do not by themselves identify a decay pathway, a translation mechanism, or a protein-level consequence.

\paragraph{Positive-control calibration.}
The same finite run contains an internal calibration against expected cap-adjacent sequence behavior and perturbation direction.  The positive-control block reported $31886$ PCIF1KO rows, with mean $0.009592$ and median $0.0$ under that perturbation readout.  At the $+2$ position, the $G$ group had mean $0.901842$ over $14263$ rows, while the $A$ group had mean $0.837479$ over $6902$ rows, giving a $G$ versus $A$ difference of $0.064362$.  At the $-1$ position, the $C$ versus non-$C$ contrast was $0.046475$.  The SSCA-GC level was $0.957884$ over $707$ rows, while the VA/RR level was $0.71252$ over $1086$ rows, with a difference of $0.245364$.  These controls make the flanking-identity contact empirically non-vacuous: the panel sees the expected cap-window signal and retains it after the declared matching tests.

\paragraph{Cross-layer status.}
The finite verdict for this chapter is a bounded cross-layer contact between local transcript-window identity and RNA chemical stoichiometry.  In compact notation, if $x_i$ is a finite row, $s_i$ is its local flanking identity class, $y_i$ is the CROWN-seq $m6Am$ stoichiometry, and $Z_i$ collects the declared GC, coverage, and matched-null controls, the supported assertion has the form
$$
\begin{aligned}
  (s_i, Z_i)_{i=1}^{31886} &\longmapsto (y_i)_{i=1}^{31886},\\
  s_i &\not\perp y_i \mid Z_i
\end{aligned}
$$
with the observed finite evidence summarized by $p=0.000833$ and positive partial correlations on the full, variable, and low-tail panels.  The phrase ``cross-layer'' here means only that a sequence-window coordinate is read against an RNA-modification measurement.  It does not mean that translation, RNA decay, RBP recruitment, protein abundance, folding, physical admissibility, biological function, phenotype, adaptation, causation, or universality has been established.

\paragraph{Relation to the RBP bridge conjecture.}
The surrounding conjecture asks for a stricter object: a same-organism, same-window panel in which a $B^*_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinate is joined to same-row translation readout, RNA half-life, measured decay-factor RBP occupancy, target-RBP loss, wild-type rescue, and binding-defective or non-target controls.  The CROWN-seq $m6Am$ finite run does not contain that perturbation-and-rescue panel.  It supplies a strong RNA-modification identity-stoichiometry contact, not a measured RBP-decay bridge.  For comparison, a separate RBP-binding finite record contains $900$ total peaks and $3$ cross-RBP-concordant cases, while a separate translation-readout record contains $6494$ rows from the mTOR translation contact.  Those facts show that RBP-binding and translation-readout surfaces exist as distinct kinds of finite evidence, but they are not joined here to the CROWN-seq $m6Am$ rows as a same-row loss/rescue statistic.

\paragraph{Local null and stopping rule.}
The local null is that, after the declared composition, GC, coverage, and matched-window controls, flanking identity is exchangeable with respect to $m6Am$ stoichiometry.  The verified CROWN-seq statistic rejects that null for the finite panel.  A different null governs the RNA half-life RBP-bridge conjecture: high-coordinate and low-coordinate synonymous residual labels remain exchangeable for translation-conditioned RNA half-life unless measured local decay-factor RBP occupancy, target-RBP loss collapse, wild-type rescue return, and binding-defective or non-target rescue failure are all present on the same finite rows.  That second null is not rejected by the CROWN-seq record, because the necessary half-life, occupancy, loss, and rescue fields are absent.

\paragraph{Claim boundary.}
The chapter therefore records a ready finite RNA-modification result and a blocked mechanistic promotion.  The ready part is the identity-conditioned $m6Am$ stoichiometry surface over $31886$ rows, with cross-cell concordance and GC-matched null support.  The blocked part is any claim that this surface proves translation realization, RNA decay mechanism, decay-factor RBP mediation, protein output, folded structure, physical admissibility, biological function, phenotype, adaptation, causation, or a universal biological law.  Such conclusions require separate layer-matched contacts and a declared same-row statistic; they are not consequences of the CROWN-seq identity-stoichiometry panel alone.
