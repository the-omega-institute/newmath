\subsection{CROWN-seq m6Am flanking identity as a bounded RNA-modification record}
\label{sec:bioreality-m6am-flanking-identity-crownseq}

\paragraph{Finite panel.}
The record considered here is a finite CROWN-seq panel for m6Am-bearing RNA starts, indexed by transcript-local rows with sequence-context annotations, stoichiometric readouts, cell-context replication, and composition controls.  The certified run for \texttt{h3.cross\_layer\_relation.rna\_m6am\_methylation.m6am\_flanking\_identity\_stoichiometry\_crownseq} contains $31886$ assayed rows and $19406$ distinct sites.  The variable subset contains $20942$ rows, and the low-tail subset below stoichiometry $0.8$ contains $3230$ rows.  A saturated component, using the threshold $0.95$, occupies fraction $0.343223$ of the panel.  These numbers define the finite surface on which the claim is allowed to speak: local m6Am stoichiometry and flanking identity are observed, but no translation, RNA-decay, RBP-perturbation, protein-output, folding, function, or universality statement is produced by these rows alone.

\paragraph{Observed sequence identity signal.}
The principal CROWN-seq statistic reports a non-exchangeable relation between flanking identity and m6Am stoichiometry after the declared GC and coverage controls.  The run gives $p=0.000833$ with the same finite permutation value for the full GC-matched null, $p_{\mathrm{full}}=0.000833$, and for the low-tail GC-matched null, $p_{\mathrm{low}}=0.000833$.  The observed finite statistic is recorded as $B=1200$, while the corresponding null means are $0.003666$ on the full panel, $0.001263$ on the low-tail panel, and $0.002173$ on the variable subset.  The controlled rank correlations are $0.268094$ on the full panel, $0.098666$ on the low-tail subset, and $0.168335$ on the variable subset.  In compact form, the certified finite readout is
$$
\begin{aligned}
 n &= 31886, & d &= 19406, & n_{\mathrm{var}} &= 20942, & n_{<0.8} &= 3230, \\
 p_{\mathrm{GC}} &= 0.000833, & \rho_{\mathrm{full}} &= 0.268094, & \rho_{\mathrm{var}} &= 0.168335, & \rho_{<0.8} &= 0.098666 .
\end{aligned}
$$
The biological reading is therefore narrow but real: the finite panel supports a local RNA-modification association between flanking nucleotide identity and measured m6Am stoichiometry.

\paragraph{Positive controls.}
The same run carries internal assay checks.  PCIF1 knockout rows are present for all $31886$ assayed entries, with mean signal $0.009592$ and median $0.0$.  The plus-two nucleotide contrast is strong in the recorded positive-control layer: $6902$ plus-two-A rows have mean $0.837479$, while $14263$ plus-two-G rows have mean $0.901842$, giving a plus-two G-versus-A contrast of $0.064362$.  The minus-one C-versus-not-C contrast is $0.046475$.  The SSCA-GC and VA/RR positive-control classes contain $707$ and $1086$ rows respectively, with levels $0.957884$ and $0.71252$, and their difference is $0.245364$.  These checks are important because they show that the assay is reading an RNA-start modification context rather than only an unconstrained abundance artifact.

\paragraph{Cross-cell and neighboring modification context.}
The CROWN-seq record is cross-cell concordant, and its machine-readable verdict is \texttt{crosses\_boundary} for the m6Am flanking-identity stoichiometry surface.  A separate linked RNA-modification finite panel, \texttt{h3.cross\_layer\_relation.rna\_m6a\_stoichiometry.m6a\_stoichiometry\_exon\_architecture\_glori}, gives an m6A stoichiometry record with $60000$ rows, $49668$ distinct entries, cross-condition concordance equal to true, and coverage-confound correlation $-0.135922814$.  That second panel is not evidence for this m6Am mechanism, but it is useful as a neighboring RNA-modification calibration: it shows that the BioReality ledger distinguishes RNA-modification stoichiometry records from translation, protein, and phenotype records even when the layer is still RNA.

\paragraph{RBP and translation boundaries.}
The conjectural language names a possible bridge through translation-conditioned RNA half-life and decay-factor RBP occupancy.  The present finite CROWN-seq rows do not contain that bridge.  In particular, this chapter does not have a same-row RNA half-life matrix, a same-row ribosome-profiling or translation-efficiency matrix, local measured occupancy for a prespecified decay-factor RBP, target-RBP loss, wild-type rescue, or binding-defective and non-target rescue controls.  A separate RBP-binding reference record, \texttt{h3.cross\_layer\_relation.rbp\_binding.rbns\_affinity\_eclip\_binding\_encode}, contains $900$ total peaks and $3$ cross-RBP-concordant cases, but those rows are not joined here to the CROWN-seq m6Am sites, nor do they provide the loss-and-rescue intervention required for a decay-factor RBP mechanism.  Thus RBP language remains a proposed next contact, not a conclusion of the current m6Am panel.

\paragraph{Permitted conclusion.}
The permitted conclusion is that a finite CROWN-seq panel detects a reproducible and composition-controlled association between m6Am stoichiometry and local flanking identity, with $31886$ rows, $19406$ distinct sites, cross-cell concordance, and a GC-matched null value $p=0.000833$.  The claim is a bounded RNA-modification readout.  It may be compared with other RNA-layer stoichiometry records, but it must not be promoted to translation realization, RNA-decay causation, RBP-mediated half-life control, protein abundance, folded structure, physical admissibility, biological function, phenotype, adaptation, mechanism, or a general sequence law without a separately joined finite contact at that layer.
