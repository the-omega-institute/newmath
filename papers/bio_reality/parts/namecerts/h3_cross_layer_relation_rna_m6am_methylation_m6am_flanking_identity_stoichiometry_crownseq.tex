\subsection{CROWN-seq m6Am Identity Rows as a Bounded Cross-Layer Readout}
\label{sec:bioreality-m6am-crownseq-identity-stoichiometry}

\paragraph{Finite panel.}
The CROWN-seq record is treated here as a finite matched-row biological panel, not as an unbounded sequence principle.  The row carrier consists of transcript-associated m6Am calls with local flanking identity labels, continuous stoichiometry values, GC-matched controls, cross-cell concordance status, and a fixed positional identity readout.  The verified panel contains $31886$ total rows and $19406$ distinct transcript or site-level entries.  Within that finite carrier, the variable subset contains $20942$ rows, and the low-tail stratum below stoichiometry $0.8$ contains $3230$ rows.  The saturated high-stoichiometry region, defined by stoichiometry at least $0.95$, occupies fraction $0.343223$ of the panel.  These numbers are the row support for the present claim.

\paragraph{Observed identity effect.}
The primary statistic compares the observed flanking-identity association with GC-matched null resampling.  The finite run used $B=1200$ null draws.  Its reported full-panel partial correlation is $0.268094$, the variable-subset partial correlation is $0.168335$, and the low-tail partial correlation is $0.098666$.  The corresponding GC-matched null probability is $0.000833$ for the main panel, with the same value reported for the full and low-tail null checks.  The observed null means are small: $0.003666$ for the full panel, $0.002173$ for the variable subset, and $0.001263$ for the low-tail subset.  In the notation of this chapter, the finite evidence surface is therefore summarized by
$$
\begin{aligned}
N &= 31886,\qquad D = 19406,\qquad N_{\mathrm{var}} = 20942,\qquad N_{<0.8}=3230,\\
\rho_{\mathrm{full}} &= 0.268094,\qquad \rho_{\mathrm{var}}=0.168335,\qquad \rho_{<0.8}=0.098666,\\
p_{\mathrm{GC-null}} &= 0.000833,\qquad f_{\geq 0.95}=0.343223.
\end{aligned}
$$
This supports a bounded statement: in this panel, local identity around the m6Am site carries reproducible information about measured m6Am stoichiometry after the declared GC and coverage checks.  It does not by itself establish a translation, protein, folding, physical, functional, adaptive, or universal biological law.

\paragraph{Positive controls.}
The same run reports internal positive-control behavior consistent with known m6Am sequence context.  The plus-two comparison gives mean stoichiometry $0.901842$ for $G$ at plus two over $14263$ rows and $0.837479$ for $A$ at plus two over $6902$ rows, for a plus-two $G$ versus $A$ difference of $0.064362$.  The minus-one $C$ versus non-$C$ contrast is $0.046475$.  The SSCA-GC class appears at level $0.957884$ over $707$ rows, while the VA/RR class appears at level $0.71252$ over $1086$ rows, giving an SSCA-GC minus VA/RR readout of $0.245364$.  The PCIF1 knockout control has $31886$ rows, mean $0.009592$, and median $0.0$.  These finite controls are important because they show that the observed signal is not merely a named coordinate label: the measured CROWN-seq surface responds in the expected direction for local identity classes and collapses under the PCIF1-linked perturbation control.

\paragraph{Boundary against overpromotion.}
The panel crosses a BioReality boundary only for m6Am flanking-identity stoichiometry.  It does not contain same-row ribosome-profiling translation efficiency, RNA half-life, measured decay-factor RBP occupancy, target-RBP loss, wild-type rescue, or binding-defective rescue rows.  Consequently the same rows cannot be promoted into a translation-conditioned RNA-decay mechanism or an RBP-mediated half-life bridge.  A separate RBP-binding contact exists elsewhere as a finite readout: the RBNS/eCLIP comparison records $900$ total peaks and $3$ cross-RBP concordant cases.  That fact is useful as a reminder that RBP occupancy must be measured as its own biological surface; it is not supplied by CROWN-seq m6Am stoichiometry.  Likewise, a separate translation contact records $6494$ rows for the mTOR translation panel, but that panel is not joined here to the m6Am CROWN-seq rows.  The m6Am identity statistic therefore remains a methylation-stoichiometry result, not a same-row translation or decay-factor RBP result.

\paragraph{Null interpretation.}
The matched null for this chapter is that, after GC and coverage controls and the declared local identity freezing, high- and low-identity coordinate classes are exchangeable with respect to m6Am stoichiometry.  The verified run rejects that exchangeability within the CROWN-seq panel at $p=0.000833$, with cross-cell concordance reported as true and the run verdict recorded as crossing the boundary.  The rejected null is deliberately narrow: it concerns flanking identity and m6Am stoichiometry in finite measured rows.  It does not reject nulls about protein output, folded structure, physical admissibility, biological function, phenotype, adaptation, causation, global codon law, or RNA half-life RBP mediation.

\paragraph{Local conclusion.}
The chapter claim is therefore accepted in bounded form.  The finite CROWN-seq evidence supports a cross-layer relation between local sequence identity and m6Am stoichiometry, with $31886$ rows, $19406$ distinct entries, cross-cell concordance, positive-control reproduction, a saturated fraction of $0.343223$, and GC-matched null probability $0.000833$.  Any stronger statement would require a new joined panel carrying the missing layer-specific measurements: same-row translation readout for translation claims, RNA half-life for RNA-stability claims, measured RBP occupancy plus perturbation and rescue rows for an RBP mechanism, and independent contacts for protein abundance, folding, physical admissibility, function, phenotype, or universality.
