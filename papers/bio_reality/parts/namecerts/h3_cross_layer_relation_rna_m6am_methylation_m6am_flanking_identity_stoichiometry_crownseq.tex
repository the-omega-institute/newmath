\subsection{CROWN-seq $m^6A_m$ Flanking Identity as a Bounded RNA-Surface Readout}
\label{sec:bioreality-m6am-flanking-identity-crownseq}

\paragraph{Finite carrier.}
This record is built from finite transcript-window rows in which the measured surface is CROWN-seq $m^6A_m$ stoichiometry, indexed by genomic flank identity and evaluated against matched controls for positional identity and GC coverage. The row set for the principal run contains $31886$ assayed RNA windows, of which $19406$ have distinct finite identities after row normalization. The variable subset contains $20942$ rows, and the low-tail subset defined by stoichiometry below $0.8$ contains $3230$ rows. The associated fact key is \texttt{h3.cross\_layer\_relation.rna\_m6am\_methylation.m6am\_flanking\_identity\_stoichiometry\_crownseq}; it reports run \texttt{eb689b7dc73c} and classifies the finite surface as \texttt{crosses\_boundary} for the RNA-modification readout itself.

\paragraph{Measured RNA-modification signal.}
The finite statistic is not a generic codon or transcript descriptor: it is a measured stoichiometric RNA-modification surface with positive-control structure. In the main CROWN-seq readout, the full partial correlation after the declared controls is $0.268094$, the variable-subset partial correlation is $0.168335$, and the low-tail partial correlation is $0.098666$. The reported GC-matched null probability is $0.000833$ for the main statistic, with the same value reported for the full and low-tail tests. The null means are $0.003666$ for the full statistic, $0.002173$ for the variable subset, and $0.001263$ for the low-tail statistic. Thus the finite rows support a bounded claim that local flanking identity carries reproducible information about measured $m^6A_m$ stoichiometry under the recorded control design.

\paragraph{Positive controls and finite calibration.}
The same run records a positive-control pattern consistent with known $m^6A_m$ sequence context rather than a free-floating coordinate artifact. The plus-two $G$ subset has mean $0.901842$ across $14263$ rows, while the plus-two $A$ subset has mean $0.837479$ across $6902$ rows, giving a plus-two $G$ versus $A$ contrast of $0.064362$. The minus-one $C$ versus non-$C$ contrast is $0.046475$. The SSCA-GC positive-control level is $0.957884$ over $707$ rows, while the VA/RR level is $0.71252$ over $1086$ rows, giving an SSCA-GC minus VA/RR contrast of $0.245364$. PCIF1 knockout rows remain present in the payload, with $31886$ rows, mean $0.009592$, and median $0.0$. These values make the chapter a finite RNA-modification readout, not a statement about protein production or downstream phenotype.

\paragraph{Cross-cell concordance.}
The row audit reports \texttt{cross\_cell\_concordant = true}. The chapter therefore treats the $m^6A_m$ surface as a reproducible RNA-layer measurement across the recorded cell-context comparison. In compact form, the finite evidence used here is
$$
\begin{aligned}
N_{\mathrm{rows}} &= 31886, \\
N_{\mathrm{distinct}} &= 19406, \\
N_{\mathrm{variable}} &= 20942, \\
N_{\mathrm{lowtail}<0.8} &= 3230, \\
\rho_{\mathrm{full}} &= 0.268094, \\
\rho_{\mathrm{variable}} &= 0.168335, \\
\rho_{\mathrm{lowtail}} &= 0.098666, \\
p_{\mathrm{GC\ matched}} &= 0.000833.
\end{aligned}
$$
The saturated fraction at stoichiometry at least $0.95$ is $0.343223$, so the readout includes both highly occupied and lower-tail windows rather than being confined to a single saturated class.

\paragraph{Relation to the RBP-bridge target.}
The same packet asks whether a B$^*_{Q6}$ residual-coordinate label can be promoted to a translation-conditioned RNA half-life bridge through a prespecified decay-factor RBP. The finite CROWN-seq result does not by itself supply that bridge. It supplies an RNA-modification stoichiometry surface. A separate linked finite RBP-binding contact, \texttt{h3.cross\_layer\_relation.rbp\_binding.rbns\_affinity\_eclip\_binding\_encode}, records run \texttt{b225890251c2} with $900$ total peaks and $3$ cross-RBP concordant cases, but that row set is still only an RBP-binding boundary unless joined to the same transcript windows, the same organism and condition, the same translation readout, the same RNA half-life readout, and an explicit loss-and-rescue panel. Consequently, the present record does not assert that the CROWN-seq $m^6A_m$ flank effect is mediated by any decay-factor RBP.

\paragraph{Null model.}
The local null is exchangeability of high-coordinate and low-coordinate labels with respect to RNA half-life after controlling transcription rate, local RNA structure, motif density, GC and dinucleotide composition, codon-pair context, splice or isoform state, transcript abundance, batch, amino-acid composition, and the same-row translation readout. The CROWN-seq statistic breaks a narrower RNA-modification null: flank identity remains associated with measured $m^6A_m$ stoichiometry under the recorded GC and positional controls. It does not break the stronger RBP-mediated RNA half-life null, because the finite rows lack same-window decay-factor RBP occupancy, target-RBP depletion, wild-type rescue, and binding-defective or non-target rescue controls.

\paragraph{Allowed conclusion.}
The supported conclusion is that the finite CROWN-seq panel contains a reproducible, control-aware RNA-modification surface in which flanking identity predicts measured $m^6A_m$ stoichiometry. The empirical strength is carried by $31886$ rows, $19406$ distinct windows, cross-cell concordance, a GC-matched null probability of $0.000833$, and the positive-control contrasts at plus-two and minus-one positions. This permits a bounded cross-layer RNA-surface statement: sequence-window identity can be read against measured RNA-modification stoichiometry in the finite panel.

\paragraph{Local boundary.}
No translation claim follows from this chapter unless a same-row translation contact is actually joined and conditioned. No RNA-decay mechanism follows unless the same-window half-life rows are joined to measured decay-factor RBP occupancy, target-RBP loss, wild-type rescue, and binding-defective or non-target controls. No protein output, folded structure, physical admissibility, biological function, phenotype, adaptation, causation, mechanism beyond the measured RNA-modification surface, or universal biological law follows from the CROWN-seq statistic. Those claims require their own layer-matched contacts and finite statistics.
