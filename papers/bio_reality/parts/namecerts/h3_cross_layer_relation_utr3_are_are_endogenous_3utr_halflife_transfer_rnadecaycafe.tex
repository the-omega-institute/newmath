\subsection{Endogenous 3UTR ARE Load as a Bounded RNA Half-Life Transfer Record}
\label{sec:bioreality-utr3-are-endogenous-halflife-transfer}

\paragraph{Finite panel.}
The record concerns a finite organism-scoped transcript panel in which an AU-rich-element coordinate in the endogenous $3'$ untranslated region is compared with an externally curated mRNA half-life readout.  The biological object is not a protein, a folded molecule, or a phenotype.  It is a joined row table whose admissible columns are a gene or transcript identifier, an ARE-load or ARE-presence coordinate, RNA half-life, transcript-level controls, and declared nuisance structure such as identifier mapping, batch, and covariates used to prevent the coordinate from standing in for a simpler transcript feature.  In this panel the evaluated endogenous half-life transfer run kept $14933$ genes.  The baseline model had
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{\mathrm{base}} &= 0.0657771,
\end{aligned}
$$
while the model with the ARE coordinate had
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{\mathrm{ARE}} &= 0.0827561.
\end{aligned}
$$
The finite improvement was therefore
$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta R^2 &= 0.0827561-0.0657771\\
&=0.016979.
\end{aligned}
$$
The fitted ARE coefficient was negative,
$$
\begin{aligned}
\beta_{\mathrm{ARE}}&=-0.0607953,
\end{aligned}
$$
so the finite readout points in the direction expected for lower RNA persistence among rows with stronger ARE signal.  The reported permutation calculation used $2000$ permutations and gave
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{\mathrm{perm}}&=0.00049975.
\end{aligned}
$$
The computation is recorded as run \texttt{e96bf26e626b}, with runtime $256.449$ seconds and verification time \texttt{2026-06-25T01:51:51+00:00}.  The row statistic is explicitly dependent on the earlier synthetic ARE reporter claim, so it is read here as an endogenous transfer check rather than as an origin claim for the whole ARE program.

\paragraph{Readout interpretation.}
The positive content of the record is a bounded RNA-stability relation: in the finite joined table, an endogenous $3'$UTR ARE coordinate improves held-model explanation of RNA half-life beyond the baseline encoded in the run, and the sign of the coefficient is negative.  This licenses the statement that the ARE coordinate survives contact with a real RNA half-life surface in the measured rows.  The statement is deliberately narrower than a decay mechanism.  The table does not by itself contain local decay-factor RBP occupancy, coordinate--occupancy decoupling, target-RBP loss, wild-type rescue, a binding-defective rescue, or a non-target rescue.  It also does not contain a same-row translation-efficiency measurement that would permit any promotion from RNA half-life to translation output.  Thus the row statistic supports an endogenous RNA half-life transfer boundary, not an RBP-mediated causal bridge.

\paragraph{Comparison with perturbative ARE deletion.}
A separate finite ARE deletion boundary run provides useful calibration for scope.  In that record the deletion panel kept $226$ BEAS2B rows and $2242$ Jurkat rows, used $1000$ actual permutations, and recorded a $2000$-permutation grading calculation with sign concordance.  That comparison shows that the endogenous half-life transfer result is not an isolated naming artifact: an ARE-directed perturbative surface and an endogenous half-life surface both point to RNA-stability behavior.  The two records are still not interchangeable.  The deletion calculation tests a deletion boundary, while the endogenous transfer calculation tests whether naturally occurring ARE load carries half-life information in a larger joined panel.  Neither calculation, standing alone, measures a decay-factor RBP bridge.

\paragraph{Finite criterion.}
Let $x_i$ denote the ARE coordinate for retained row $i$, let $h_i$ denote the RNA half-life readout, and let $z_i$ denote the baseline covariate vector used by the finite run.  The accepted finite fact is the improvement of a controlled half-life predictor when $x_i$ is included:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta R^2(x;h\mid z)&=0.016979,\\
\beta_{\mathrm{ARE}}&=-0.0607953,\\
p_{\mathrm{perm}}&=0.00049975,\\
N&=14933.
\end{aligned}
$$
This is enough to retain the ARE coordinate as an RNA half-life descriptor in the finite panel.  It is not enough to assert that the coordinate is the active cause, that a named decay-factor RBP is the mediator, or that the same rows instantiate translation-conditioned RNA decay.  A stronger bridge would require a table in which the same windows carry synonymous coordinate labels, same-row translation readout, RNA half-life, measured local decay-factor RBP occupancy, target-RBP perturbation, wild-type rescue, binding-defective rescue or non-target control, transcription-rate control, mRNA abundance control, isoform state, motif-density and local-structure covariates, composition controls, codon-pair context, batch, and a declared break condition.

\paragraph{Local refusal boundary.}
No translation claim follows from this RNA half-life result.  No protein-output, folded-structure, physical-admissibility, biological-function, phenotype, adaptation, mechanism, causation, or universal-law claim follows from the reported $\Delta R^2$, coefficient, row count, or permutation value.  If high-ARE and low-ARE rows become exchangeable after the declared transcript controls, the coordinate is only a bounded descriptor.  If RNA half-life differs but measured decay-factor RBP occupancy does not change, or if target-RBP loss and wild-type rescue do not control the effect, the result remains an RNA-stability association.  If binding-defective rescue, non-target controls, RNA structure, motif density, transcription rate, isoform state, abundance, or batch reproduce the same pattern, the mechanism bridge is rejected.  The present finite record therefore closes only the following claim: an endogenous $3'$UTR ARE coordinate has a measured, sign-directed, permutation-supported association with RNA half-life in the retained panel.
