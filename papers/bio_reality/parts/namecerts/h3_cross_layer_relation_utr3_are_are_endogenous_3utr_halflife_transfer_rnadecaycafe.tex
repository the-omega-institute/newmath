\subsection{Endogenous ARE Load as a Bounded RNA Half-Life Transfer}
\label{sec:bioreality-endogenous-are-halflife-transfer-rnadecaycafe}

\paragraph{Finite row object.}
The finite object for this BioReality record is a matched transcript table in which each retained row carries an endogenous $3'$UTR AU-rich element signal, an RNA half-life readout, and the covariates needed to keep the readout at RNA-stability scope.  The row type is not a free biological law.  It is a finite panel of gene or transcript observations with an ARE term, half-life response, baseline predictors, and a declared permutation test.  In the verified run for the endogenous RNAdecayCafe transfer statistic, the usable table contained $n_{\mathrm{genes}}=14933$ genes.  The baseline model had $R^2=0.0657771$, and the model containing the ARE term had test $R^2=0.0827561$, so the observed improvement was
$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta R^2 &= 0.0827561-0.0657771 \\
           &= 0.016979 .
\end{aligned}
$$
The ARE coefficient was negative, with $\beta_{\mathrm{ARE}}=-0.0607953$, so the direction recorded by the finite rows is shorter RNA half-life at higher endogenous ARE load, after the controls represented in the fitted statistic.  The permutation audit used $2000$ permutations and returned $p_{\mathrm{perm}}=0.00049975$.  The run identifier for this computation is \texttt{e96bf26e626b}, verified at \texttt{2026-06-27T19:56:13+00:00}.

\paragraph{What the statistic licenses.}
This chapter therefore licenses only the following bounded statement: in the finite endogenous panel, the ARE coordinate contributes a negative RNA half-life term beyond the recorded baseline, with a nonzero held-out improvement and a permutation-supported tail probability.  The numerical content is a row-level RNA-stability readout, not a direct observation of translation, protein abundance, folded structure, organismal function, adaptation, or a universal sequence principle.  The statistic may be written as a finite comparison between a baseline predictor family $Z$ and an ARE-augmented family $Z+\mathrm{ARE}$:
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2(Z) &= 0.0657771, \\
R^2(Z+\mathrm{ARE}) &= 0.0827561, \\
R^2(Z+\mathrm{ARE})-R^2(Z) &= 0.016979, \\
\beta_{\mathrm{ARE}} &= -0.0607953, \\
p_{\mathrm{perm}} &= 0.00049975 .
\end{aligned}
$$
The interpretation is deliberately narrow.  A negative ARE coefficient is evidence that this finite endogenous panel contains an ARE-associated half-life transfer signal.  It is not, by itself, evidence that a specified decay-factor RNA-binding protein occupies the relevant local sequence, that such occupancy is changed by synonymous coordinates, or that perturbation and rescue establish an RBP-mediated decay mechanism.

\paragraph{Relation to the reporter-side boundary.}
The endogenous transfer record depends on the earlier synthetic ARE reporter claim in the data lineage.  The paired reporter-side finite run, \texttt{a41440ab2792}, used a corrected pooled label permutation over deletions, with $1000$ actual permutations and $2000$ grading permutations.  It retained $226$ BEAS-2B deletion rows and $2242$ Jurkat deletion rows, and the sign-concordance flag was true.  That reporter-side result is useful here only as a separate finite stability boundary: it supports that ARE perturbation can be read through mRNA stability in a controlled reporter or deletion design.  It does not merge with the endogenous RNAdecayCafe statistic into an RBP-mechanism proof.  The two facts together justify a stable chapter-level reading: ARE sequence features can be attached to RNA half-life surfaces in finite data, and the endogenous transfer statistic preserves the expected negative direction, but the mechanism layer still requires additional rows.

\paragraph{Required bridge rows not supplied here.}
The stronger cross-layer conjecture would require same-window rows carrying measured local decay-factor RBP occupancy, target-RBP loss, wild-type rescue, binding-defective rescue or non-target control arms, transcription-rate controls, mRNA abundance controls, isoform state, motif density, local structure, GC and dinucleotide composition, codon-pair context, batch, and a same-row translation readout used only as a boundary condition.  Those rows are not present in the verified RNAdecayCafe transfer statistic.  The available payload gives an RNA half-life association with an ARE term; it does not provide the occupancy and intervention columns that would distinguish an RBP-mediated bridge from a motif-associated stability signal.

\paragraph{Local refusal rule.}
The chapter therefore refuses every promotion beyond the finite RNA-stability surface.  No translation realization follows from the half-life statistic.  No protein-output, folding, physical-admissibility, phenotype, function, mechanism, causation, adaptation, or universality claim follows from the ARE coefficient or from the permutation result.  No decay-factor RBP bridge is accepted unless a separate finite panel supplies measured RBP occupancy in the same windows, shows coordinate--occupancy decoupling, loses the half-life effect under target-RBP perturbation, restores it under wild-type rescue, and fails to restore it under binding-defective or non-target controls.  Until those rows exist, the certified content is exactly the bounded endogenous ARE-to-RNA-half-life transfer recorded by the $14933$-gene statistic and its reporter-side stability companion.
