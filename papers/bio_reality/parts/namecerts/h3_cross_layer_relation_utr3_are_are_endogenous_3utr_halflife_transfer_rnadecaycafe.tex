\subsection{Endogenous ARE Load as an RNA Half-Life Transfer Boundary}
\label{sec:bioreality-are-endogenous-utr3-halflife-transfer-rnadecaycafe}

\paragraph{Finite row carrier.}
The object in this chapter is a finite organism-scoped table of endogenous transcript rows in which an AU-rich element coordinate is joined to an RNA half-life readout.  The row type is deliberately narrower than a mechanism experiment: each retained row may record a transcript or gene identifier, an endogenous $3'$ UTR ARE count or load, an mRNA half-life value, baseline transcript covariates, and the fitted residual surface used for the declared statistic.  The verified run contains $14933$ joined gene rows.  On these rows the baseline model has test coefficient of determination $0.0657771$, while the model including the ARE term has test coefficient of determination $0.0827561$.  The finite gain is therefore
$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta R^2 &= 0.0827561 - 0.0657771 \\
&= 0.016979.
\end{aligned}
$$
The fitted ARE coefficient is $-0.0607953$, with negative sign, and the permutation test used $2000$ permutations with $p = 0.00049975$.  The run identifier is \texttt{e96bf26e626b}, and the recorded runtime is $256.449$ seconds.  These numbers support a bounded statement about endogenous ARE-load transfer to RNA half-life prediction; they do not by themselves identify a decay-factor RBP, a translation effect, or a downstream protein-layer event.

\paragraph{Declared relation.}
Let $i$ range over the $14933$ finite rows.  Write $a_i$ for the endogenous ARE coordinate, $h_i$ for the RNA half-life readout, and $z_i$ for the baseline covariates used before the ARE term is admitted.  The empirical boundary is the comparison between a baseline predictor $B(z_i)$ and an augmented predictor $B(z_i,a_i)$.  In the verified run the augmented surface improves held-out prediction by $\Delta R^2 = 0.016979$ and the fitted ARE direction is negative.  The chapter therefore records the finite implication
$$
\begin{aligned}
\text{ARE coordinate present in the joined table}
&\Longrightarrow \text{nonzero half-life prediction gain on these rows},\\
\beta_{\mathrm{ARE}} &= -0.0607953,\\
p_{\mathrm{perm}} &= 0.00049975.
\end{aligned}
$$
The word ``transfer'' here means only that a coordinate measured in an endogenous $3'$ UTR table survives as a finite RNA half-life predictor after the declared baseline.  It is not a claim that the ARE coordinate is the sole cause of the half-life value, and it is not a claim that the observed half-life difference is a translation readout.

\paragraph{Comparison to neighboring RNA-layer contacts.}
The same verification packet records several neighboring RNA-layer checks, which help fix the scope of this chapter.  An ARE deletion stability boundary has $226$ BEAS2B rows and $2242$ Jurkat rows, uses $1000$ actual permutations and $2000$ grading permutations, and records sign concordance.  A site-level m6A half-life boundary retains $5837$ genes, reports static Spearman correlation $-0.388929$, causal Spearman correlation $0.245804$, length-partial Spearman correlation $0.227683$, and two-sided partial permutation value $0.000999001$.  A cross-organism human m6A load check records $15845$ genes, $135300$ sites, $1000$ permutations, and $3$ sign-concordant cases.  An NMD position-rule boundary keeps $38576$ rows with $1000$ permutations.  A miRNA repression boundary records $55170$ rows, aggregate per-miRNA partial median $\rho=-0.224966$, and $7$ replicated gradient cases.  These companion numbers show that the present ARE half-life entry belongs to a family of finite RNA-readout boundary tests: each may support a row-level RNA association under its own statistic, but none is promoted across layers without a separately matched contact.

\paragraph{RBP bridge gate.}
The stronger biological object named by this chapter would require synonymously editable coding-RNA windows, same-row translation readout, measured local decay-factor RBP occupancy, target-RBP loss, wild-type rescue, binding-defective or non-target controls, transcription-rate controls, mRNA abundance controls, isoform state, motif density, local RNA structure, GC and dinucleotide composition, codon-pair context, abundance, and batch.  The verified RNAdecaycafe run does not contain those same-window RBP occupancy and perturbation rows.  Thus the current finite evidence licenses only the following bounded statement: endogenous ARE load is a statistically supported RNA half-life predictor in the retained table, with negative fitted direction and permutation support.  It does not license the claim that a prespecified decay-factor RBP mediates the effect.  In particular, a measured occupancy change, collapse under target-RBP loss, restoration under wild-type rescue, and failure of binding-defective or non-target rescue remain separate requirements.

\paragraph{Translation boundary.}
A same-row translation readout is part of the intended cross-layer design because it prevents RNA half-life from being silently interpreted as translation.  The current half-life table does not provide a completed ribosome-profiling or translation-efficiency matrix for the same finite windows.  Therefore the half-life result stays at the RNA-stability layer.  No translation realization, ribosome occupancy, protein abundance, folded structure, physical admissibility, molecular function, phenotype, adaptation, causal mechanism, or universal biological law follows from the $14933$-row ARE statistic.  Such claims would need their own layer-matched rows and their own declared statistics.

\paragraph{Refusal conditions.}
The chapter refuses the bridge interpretation under any of the following finite failures.  If high- and low-coordinate labels become exchangeable for RNA half-life after transcription, local structure, motif density, composition, codon-pair context, isoform state, abundance, and batch are controlled, the coordinate is only a descriptor.  If RNA half-life differs but measured local decay-factor RBP occupancy is absent or unchanged, the evidence is only an RNA-stability association.  If target-RBP loss does not collapse the effect, if wild-type rescue does not restore it, or if binding-defective or non-target controls reproduce the same pattern, the RBP mechanism reading is rejected.  If local RNA structure, motif density, transcription, isoform stability, abundance, or batch explains the pattern, the coordinate remains a finite covariate rather than a bridge.

\paragraph{Chapter conclusion.}
The verified row set gives a concrete RNA-layer boundary: $n=14933$, baseline $R^2=0.0657771$, augmented $R^2=0.0827561$, $\Delta R^2=0.016979$, $\beta_{\mathrm{ARE}}=-0.0607953$, and $p=0.00049975$ under $2000$ permutations.  This is enough to record a finite endogenous ARE-to-half-life transfer surface.  It is not enough to record a decay-factor RBP bridge, a translation event, or any protein-layer or organism-layer consequence.
