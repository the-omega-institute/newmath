\subsection{Endogenous 3UTR ARE Load as a Bounded RNA Half-Life Readout}
\label{subsec:bioreality-endogenous-utr3-are-halflife-rnadecaycafe}

\paragraph{Finite row carrier.}
This record concerns a finite panel of transcript-level rows in which each row carries an endogenous $3'$UTR AU-rich-element coordinate, an RNA half-life readout, and control covariates sufficient to keep the observation at RNA-stability scope. The carrier is not a protein-output carrier and not a perturbation-rescue RBP mechanism carrier. Its row type is the finite tuple
$$
\begin{aligned}
 r_g = (&\mathrm{gene}_g,\ \mathrm{ARE}_g,\ H_g,\ Z_g,\ B_g),
\end{aligned}
$$
where $\mathrm{ARE}_g$ is the local AU-rich-element burden or label, $H_g$ is the RNA half-life readout, $Z_g$ contains the declared baseline controls used by the finite statistic, and $B_g$ records batch or dataset provenance. The realized panel contains $14933$ gene rows. The readout is therefore a finite endogenous $3'$UTR stability comparison, not an unlimited rule about synonymous coordinates, translation, RBP binding, or organism-wide biology.

\paragraph{Observed finite statistic.}
On the $14933$ joined rows, the baseline regression surface explains $R^2_{\mathrm{base}}=0.0657771$ of the RNA half-life variation. After adding the ARE term, the test surface gives $R^2_{\mathrm{test}}=0.0827561$, hence
$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta R^2
  &= R^2_{\mathrm{test}}-R^2_{\mathrm{base}} \\
  &= 0.0827561-0.0657771 \\
  &= 0.016979.
\end{aligned}
$$
The fitted ARE coefficient is negative, with $\beta_{\mathrm{ARE}}=-0.0607953$. The finite permutation audit used $2000$ permutations and returned $p_{\mathrm{perm}}=0.00049975$. The local numerical reading is therefore: within this finite RNA half-life panel, the endogenous $3'$UTR ARE term contributes a small but nonzero additional held-out explanatory component beyond the baseline surface, and its signed direction is toward shorter RNA half-life.

\paragraph{Relation to the synthetic deletion anchor.}
The endogenous transfer statistic is explicitly dependent on the earlier synthetic ARE reporter claim. That dependency is used only as a biological orientation for the direction and identity of the ARE coordinate. The separate deletion panel contains $226$ BEAS2B deletion rows and $2242$ Jurkat deletion rows, uses $1000$ actual permutations with a $2000$-permutation grading route, and reports sign concordance under a pooled label-permutation method over deletions. This does not turn the endogenous RNA half-life panel into a perturbation experiment; it only says that the endogenous coefficient is read against a finite reporter-supported ARE stability direction.

\paragraph{Permitted interpretation.}
The allowed conclusion is narrow. A finite endogenous transcript panel supports a bounded RNA half-life association for $3'$UTR ARE load: the ARE coordinate improves the RNA half-life readout surface by $\Delta R^2=0.016979$ over a baseline with $R^2=0.0657771$, has a negative fitted sign, and passes the declared permutation threshold with $p_{\mathrm{perm}}=0.00049975$ on $14933$ rows. In symbols, the only licensed transition is
$$
\begin{aligned}
(\mathrm{ARE}_g, Z_g) \longmapsto H_g
\end{aligned}
$$
inside the finite joined table. The result is a stability readout about RNA half-life, not a statement that the coordinate realizes translation, protein abundance, folded structure, cellular function, adaptation, or a general sequence law.

\paragraph{RBP bridge refusal.}
The stronger decay-factor RBP bridge is not established by these rows. The present finite statistic does not contain measured local decay-factor RBP occupancy, coordinate--occupancy decoupling, target-RBP loss, wild-type rescue, binding-defective rescue, or non-target controls. Consequently, even though the ARE coefficient is negative and the permutation statistic is strong, the mechanism-level bridge remains refused. A positive RBP bridge would require same-row evidence that the half-life effect follows measured local RBP occupancy, collapses after loss of the prespecified RBP, returns with wild-type rescue, and is not restored by binding-defective or non-target controls. Without those rows, the proper reading is an endogenous RNA-stability association, not an RBP-mediated decay mechanism.

\paragraph{Translation boundary.}
The claim also stays below translation scope. RNA half-life, mRNA abundance, and ribosome-profiling contact names are distinct readout surfaces. This panel does not provide a same-row translation-efficiency matrix that licenses any statement about ribosome occupancy, protein output, or translation realization. A translation-conditioned RNA-stability claim would require the same finite rows to carry the coordinate label, RNA half-life, matched mRNA abundance, and an explicit translation readout before interpretation. Even then, the conclusion would still remain a translation-bounded RNA-half-life statistic unless a separate protein-level or function-level contact were attached.

\paragraph{Local cannot-claim boundary.}
No conclusion about translation, protein abundance, protein folding, physical admissibility, biological function, phenotype, adaptation, causation, RBP mechanism, or universality follows from this chapter. Those layers require their own finite contacts and their own declared statistics. The current chapter licenses only the following bounded sentence: in an endogenous $3'$UTR RNA half-life table of $14933$ genes, ARE load adds a negative RNA-stability term with $\Delta R^2=0.016979$ and $p_{\mathrm{perm}}=0.00049975$ under the declared finite permutation audit.
