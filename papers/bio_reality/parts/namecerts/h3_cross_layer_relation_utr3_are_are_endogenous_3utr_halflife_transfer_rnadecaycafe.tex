\subsection{Endogenous ARE Half-Life Transfer With a Translation Boundary}\label{sec:bioreality-endogenous-are-halflife-transfer}\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite record.}
The record concerns a finite panel of endogenous human transcript rows in which an AU-rich element indicator is evaluated against an RNA half-life readout. The verified run for the claim is \texttt{e96bf26e626b}. It retains $14933$ gene-level rows, uses $2000$ label permutations, and reports a negative ARE coefficient, $\beta_{\mathrm{ARE}}=-0.0607953$. The baseline model has $R^2=0.0657771$, the ARE-augmented test model has $R^2=0.0827561$, and the observed improvement is $\Delta R^2=0.016979$ with permutation value $p=0.00049975$. The sign is therefore read locally as shorter RNA half-life for ARE-bearing rows after the declared baseline adjustment, not as a translation, protein, structure, function, phenotype, adaptation, or universal-law result.

\paragraph{Row semantics.}
Each row is treated as a bounded transcript-stability row, not as a complete mechanistic experiment. The row carries an endogenous transcript or gene identifier, the ARE-side covariate used by the fitted statistic, the RNA half-life response, the baseline covariates available to the run, and the declared permutation test. The admissible readout is the finite contrast
$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta R^2
  &= R^2_{\mathrm{test}}-R^2_{\mathrm{baseline}} \\
  &= 0.0827561-0.0657771 \\
  &= 0.016979 .
\end{aligned}
$$
Together with $\beta_{\mathrm{ARE}}<0$ and $p=0.00049975$, this licenses a restricted stability-side statement: ARE status carries reproducible information about endogenous RNA half-life in this finite joined panel. It does not by itself identify the decay factor, prove binding, prove perturbation response, or show that synonymous coding-window coordinates have produced a biological mechanism.

\paragraph{Transfer from the reporter-side record.}
The run declares its dependence on an original synthetic ARE reporter claim. That dependence is not used as a substitute for the endogenous statistic; it is used only to explain why the endogenous test is a transfer target rather than an unconstrained screen. A separate ARE-deletion stability record gives useful finite context: the deletion run \texttt{a41440ab2792} uses $1000$ actual permutations, $2000$ grading permutations, $226$ BEAS2B deletion rows, and $2242$ Jurkat deletion rows, with sign concordance reported as true. The deletion result supports the direction that ARE perturbation can touch RNA stability, while the endogenous transfer result supplies the larger $14933$-row half-life surface. These are still two bounded stability contacts. Neither one supplies measured local decay-factor RBP occupancy, target-RBP loss, wild-type rescue, binding-defective rescue, or a non-target rescue control.

\paragraph{Translation boundary.}
The cross-layer form of the claim explicitly requires a same-row translation readout before any interpretation that mentions translation-conditioned transfer. In the present finite evidence, the verified half-life statistic is real, but the available contacts do not attach a same-window ribosome-occupancy or translation-efficiency row to the same ARE/RNA-half-life row. Therefore the chapter records the half-life side as supported and keeps translation outside the supported conclusion. A translation statement would require a finite joined panel with coding-window coordinate labels, same-row translation efficiency or ribosome occupancy, matched mRNA abundance, RNA half-life, transcription-rate control, local RNA-structure covariates, motif density, GC and dinucleotide composition, codon-pair context, splice or isoform state, transcript abundance, batch, perturbation state, rescue state, and a declared break condition.

\paragraph{RBP mechanism gate.}
The endogenous half-life transfer is not an RBP mechanism result. The mechanism claim would need measured local occupancy of a prespecified decay-factor RBP, a coordinate--occupancy decoupling design, loss of the target RBP, restoration by wild-type rescue, failure of restoration by a binding-defective rescue or non-target control, and persistence after transcription, abundance, isoform, motif-density, local-structure, composition, and batch controls. If the half-life association remains but RBP occupancy is unchanged, the record stays at RNA-stability association. If target-RBP loss does not collapse the effect, or if wild-type rescue does not restore it, the RBP bridge is refused. If the binding-defective rescue, non-target control, local RNA structure, motif density, transcription, isoform stability, or batch reproduces the same pattern, the mechanism reading is also refused.

\paragraph{Adjacent stability calibration.}
A separate mRNA-stability boundary record for m6A provides calibration for the scale of finite transcript half-life tests without merging the biological mechanisms. In run \texttt{7cd189f15c4b}, $5837$ genes pass the coverage rule, the static Spearman association between m6A load and DMSO log half-life is $-0.388929$, the causal Spearman association between m6A load and $\Delta\log_2$ half-life is $0.245804$, the length-partial Spearman value is $0.227683$, and the two-sided partial permutation value is $0.000999001$. This neighboring record shows that finite RNA-modification or motif-linked half-life surfaces can be quantitatively checked while remaining separate from protein output, folding, physical admissibility, function, phenotype, mechanism, and universality. The ARE chapter follows the same discipline: it reports the measured half-life boundary and does not promote it across unsupported layers.

\paragraph{Admissible conclusion.}
The admissible conclusion is narrow. In the finite endogenous panel, ARE status contributes a negative RNA-half-life signal with $14933$ rows, $\Delta R^2=0.016979$, and $p=0.00049975$ under the stated run. This is enough for a bounded BioReality record of endogenous ARE-associated RNA half-life transfer from the reporter-side direction into a larger curated half-life surface. It is not enough for translation realization, protein abundance, folded structure, physical admissibility, function realization, system phenotype, adaptation, causation, or a general biological law. Those claims require separate row-matched contacts at their own layer, and none is supplied by the half-life statistic alone.
