\subsection{Endogenous ARE Transfer as an RNA-Half-Life Boundary}
\label{sec:bioreality-are-endogenous-3utr-halflife-transfer-rnadecaycafe}

\paragraph{Finite panel.}
The record concerns a finite panel of endogenous transcript rows in which an AU-rich element coordinate is evaluated against RNA half-life after baseline transcript covariates are already present. The biological object is not a protein-output assay and not a direct translation assay. It is a same-organism RNA-stability readout keyed by gene or transcript identifiers, with the declared coordinate standing for an ARE-associated 3UTR feature. The usable row schema is a tuple
$$\begin{aligned}
 r_i = (&\operatorname{ARE}_i, H_i, X_i, G_i, D_i, I_i, B_i),
\end{aligned}$$
where $\operatorname{ARE}_i$ is the finite ARE indicator or score, $H_i$ is the RNA half-life readout, $X_i$ is the baseline covariate vector already admitted by the audit, $G_i$ is the gene identifier, $D_i$ is dataset provenance, $I_i$ records identifier-join state, and $B_i$ records the break condition for rows that cannot support the intended interpretation. The verified endogenous transfer run retained $14933$ genes, used $2000$ permutations, and reported
$$\begin{aligned}
 R^2_{\mathrm{baseline}} &= 0.0657771,\\
 R^2_{\mathrm{test}} &= 0.0827561,\\
 \Delta R^2 &= 0.016979,\\
 \beta_{\mathrm{ARE}} &= -0.0607953,\\
 p_{\mathrm{perm}} &= 0.00049975.
\end{aligned}$$
The sign entry for the fitted ARE term is negative. Thus the finite readout supports the narrow claim that the ARE-bearing coordinate contributes a measurable RNA-half-life signal beyond the stated baseline model in this joined dataset.

\paragraph{Preceding deletion anchor.}
A second finite row source anchors the interpretation as an ARE-to-RNA-stability boundary rather than as an isolated regression artifact. The deletion audit used a corrected pooled label permutation over deletions because the within-region permutation was degenerate for the per-parent predictor. It retained $226$ BEAS2B rows and $2242$ Jurkat rows, ran $1000$ actual permutations with a $2000$-permutation grading pass, and found sign concordance. This is not imported here as a universal law. It supplies a compatible finite perturbation-shaped contact: when ARE sequence is removed in that separate row system, the direction of the mRNA-stability signal agrees with the endogenous transfer sign pattern. The present chapter therefore treats the endogenous $14933$-gene result as a transfer boundary depending on the earlier synthetic ARE reporter/deletion claim, not as an autonomous mechanism proof.

\paragraph{Statistic licensed by the rows.}
The licensed statistic is the improvement from the baseline half-life model to the ARE-augmented half-life model. In compact form, the admitted comparison is
$$\begin{aligned}
 H_i &= f_0(X_i) + \varepsilon_i,\\
 H_i &= f_1(X_i,\operatorname{ARE}_i) + \eta_i,\\
 \Delta R^2 &= R^2(f_1)-R^2(f_0)=0.016979.
\end{aligned}$$
The finite permutation probability $p_{\mathrm{perm}}=0.00049975$ is the row-level evidence that the observed ARE contribution is not being accepted merely because the expanded model has an additional coordinate. The direction $\beta_{\mathrm{ARE}}=-0.0607953$ is read only as the fitted direction of the half-life association under this encoding. The audit does not license an arbitrary extrapolation from all AU-rich elements to every cell type, transcript class, assay platform, or decay regime.

\paragraph{Translation boundary.}
The same record names translation readout and matched mRNA abundance control as necessary boundary surfaces for stronger cross-layer claims, but those surfaces are not present here as a completed same-row translation matrix. RNA half-life alone is not a translation measurement. A finite ARE association with $H_i$ cannot be reworded as ribosome occupancy, translation efficiency, protein abundance, folded protein state, physical admissibility, biological function, phenotype, adaptation, or a general biological law. Any such promotion would require a separate row set containing the relevant measurement on the same biological units and a declared statistic that survives its own controls.

\paragraph{RBP bridge not yet closed.}
The stronger bridge would require measured local occupancy of a prespecified decay-factor RNA-binding protein on the same windows, a coordinate/occupancy decoupling design, loss of the target RBP, wild-type rescue, binding-defective rescue or non-target controls, transcription-rate controls, isoform diagnostics, local RNA-structure and motif-density covariates, GC and dinucleotide composition, codon-pair context, transcript abundance, batch, and an explicit break rule. Those data are not contained in the verified endogenous transfer run. Consequently the present chapter accepts a bounded ARE-to-RNA-half-life transfer surface and refuses the stronger RBP-mechanism reading. If the ARE term remains predictive only because of unmeasured RBP occupancy, transcriptional coupling, isoform instability, abundance bias, motif-density confounding, or batch structure, the positive interpretation must be reduced to a finite RNA-stability association.

\paragraph{Local conclusion.}
The finite evidence is strong enough for a chapter-level BioReality record at RNA-half-life scope: $14933$ joined genes, $\Delta R^2=0.016979$, negative ARE coefficient, and $p_{\mathrm{perm}}=0.00049975$ give a concrete endogenous transfer signal, while the separate ARE deletion audit supplies a compatible finite perturbation-shaped anchor with $226$ BEAS2B rows, $2242$ Jurkat rows, and sign concordance. The accepted claim is therefore deliberately narrow. ARE-associated 3UTR information crosses into an RNA-half-life readout under the declared finite statistic, but no translation, folding, physical admissibility, function, mechanism, causation, adaptation, phenotype, or universality follows without a distinct contact that directly measures that layer.
