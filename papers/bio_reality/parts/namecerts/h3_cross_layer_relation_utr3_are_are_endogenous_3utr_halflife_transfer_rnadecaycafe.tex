\subsection{Endogenous AU-rich 3UTR signal as a bounded RNA half-life transfer}
\label{sec:bioreality-are-endogenous-3utr-halflife-transfer-rnadecaycafe}

\paragraph{Finite row object.}
The record concerns a finite panel of transcript-level rows in which the molecular coordinate is an AU-rich element burden in a 3UTR context and the readout is an endogenous RNA half-life estimate. The biological question is not whether the coordinate is a universal decay law, but whether a bounded RNA-stability signal remains after baseline transcript covariates are carried in the same statistical object. In the supplied run, the endogenous transfer panel contains $14933$ genes. The baseline model has $R^2 = 0.0657771$, while the ARE-augmented test model has $R^2 = 0.0827561$. The observed gain is therefore $\Delta R^2 = 0.016979$. The ARE coefficient is negative, $\beta_{ARE} = -0.0607953$, and the sign field records the same direction as negative. With $2000$ permutations, the reported permutation value is $p = 0.00049975$, and the run time is $256.449$ seconds. These numbers license a finite RNA half-life association: in this joined panel, the AU-rich 3UTR coordinate improves prediction of the half-life readout, and the fitted direction is toward shorter half-life for higher ARE burden.

\paragraph{Allowed mathematical reading.}
The chapter uses the row set only as a finite relation between a 3UTR sequence descriptor and an RNA half-life scalar. A compact way to state the accepted content is
$$
\begin{aligned}
N &= 14933,\\
R^2_{base} &= 0.0657771,\\
R^2_{ARE} &= 0.0827561,\\
\Delta R^2 &= 0.016979,\\
\beta_{ARE} &= -0.0607953,\\
p_{perm} &= 0.00049975.
\end{aligned}
$$
The row-level conclusion is consequently narrow: the 3UTR ARE coordinate carries finite predictive information for the RNA half-life readout in this endogenous RNA-decay panel. The entry also declares that this result depends on the original synthetic ARE reporter claim, so the interpretation is a transfer boundary rather than a free-standing mechanistic reconstruction. The finite rows make contact with RNA stability, not with protein output or cell-level consequence.

\paragraph{Relation to neighboring RNA-stability rows.}
Two nearby RNA-stability records help calibrate the scope without changing the claim. The ARE-deletion stability boundary reports sign concordance with $2242$ Jurkat rows and $226$ BEAS2B rows under $1000$ actual permutations and $2000$ grading permutations, with a corrected pooled-label permutation method over deletions. That companion result supports the idea that ARE perturbation can sit at an RNA-stability boundary, but it does not add measured decay-factor RBP occupancy to this endogenous transfer panel. A separate m6A site-load half-life row reports $5837$ genes, a static Spearman value of $-0.388929$ between m6A load and DMSO half-life, a causal Spearman value of $0.245804$ for m6A load versus $\Delta\log_2$ half-life, a length-partial Spearman value of $0.227683$, and a two-sided partial permutation value $p = 0.000999001$. That row shows another RNA-modification-to-half-life boundary, but it remains a distinct molecular coordinate. These comparisons locate the present ARE row among finite RNA-stability contacts rather than promoting it to a general transcript-control theorem.

\paragraph{RBP bridge condition.}
The supplied conjecture asks for a stronger cross-layer relation: a coordinate-associated half-life effect would become an RBP-mediated decay bridge only if the same finite rows also contained measured local occupancy of a prespecified decay-factor RBP, target-RBP loss, wild-type rescue, binding-defective control, non-target control, transcription-rate control, mRNA abundance control, isoform state, motif-density and local-structure covariates, and a same-row translation readout used only as a boundary condition. The present finite numbers do not report measured decay-factor RBP occupancy, target-RBP perturbation, rescue behavior, binding-defective rescue failure, or coordinate/occupancy decoupling. Therefore the positive finite statistic is accepted as an endogenous ARE--RNA-half-life transfer signal, while the RBP-mechanism bridge remains unlicensed for this panel.

\paragraph{Local refusal boundary.}
No translation realization follows from the RNA half-life statistic. No ribosome occupancy, protein abundance, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, system phenotype, adaptation, causation, mechanism, or universal biological rule follows from the ARE coefficient, from the permutation value, or from the improvement in $R^2$. The same-row translation readout named by the conjecture would serve only to prevent an RNA-stability association from being confused with a translation claim; it would not itself prove an RNA-decay mechanism. A mechanistic RBP statement would require measured occupancy and perturbation/rescue rows at the same finite granularity. A protein or phenotype statement would require a separate layer-matched contact and its own statistic. Thus the chapter records a bounded endogenous 3UTR ARE half-life relation and refuses every higher-layer interpretation not supplied by rows of that kind.
