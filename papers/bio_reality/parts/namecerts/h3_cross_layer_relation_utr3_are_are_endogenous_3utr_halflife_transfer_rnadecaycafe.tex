\subsection{Endogenous 3UTR ARE transfer into RNA half-life}
\label{sec:bioreality-utr3-are-endogenous-halflife-transfer}

\paragraph{Finite record.}
This section records a bounded BioReality contact for AU-rich element structure in endogenous 3UTRs.  The finite panel is not a free-standing law about codons, translation, or function.  Each row is read as a transcript-level tuple carrying an ARE-side descriptor, an RNA half-life readout, baseline transcript covariates, and the declared regression and permutation statistic.  The completed run used $14933$ genes and compared a baseline half-life model with a model carrying the ARE term.  The baseline coefficient of determination was $R^2_{0}=0.0657771$, while the ARE-augmented model reached $R^2_{1}=0.0827561$.  Thus the measured improvement was
$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta R^2 &= R^2_{1}-R^2_{0} \\
           &= 0.0827561-0.0657771 \\
           &= 0.016979.
\end{aligned}
$$
The fitted ARE coefficient was $\beta_{\mathrm{ARE}}=-0.0607953$, with negative sign, and the finite permutation calculation used $2000$ permutations with $p=0.00049975$.  The run identifier was \texttt{e96bf26e626b}, and the recorded runtime was $256.449$ seconds.  These numbers license only the statement that the finite endogenous dataset preserves a small but permutation-supported ARE contribution to RNA half-life prediction after the stated baseline fit.

\paragraph{What is being transferred.}
The object being transferred is a 3UTR ARE-side stability signal, not a claim that the coding coordinate itself realizes translation or protein output.  The run is explicitly dependent on an original synthetic ARE reporter claim, but the present record is endogenous: the observed statistic is computed on gene rows with endogenous half-life values.  The negative sign of $\beta_{\mathrm{ARE}}$ is the local biological orientation of the record: higher ARE-side burden is associated with shorter RNA half-life under the fitted finite model.  The support is therefore a constrained RNA-stability boundary.  It does not by itself identify a decay-factor RBP, prove target-RBP recruitment, or establish that a particular coordinate label causes the observed half-life difference.

\paragraph{Same-layer comparison.}
A separate ARE-deletion stability run supplies a useful same-layer calibration.  That run recorded $226$ BEAS2B rows and $2242$ Jurkat rows, used $1000$ actual permutations with a $2000$-permutation grading setting, and reported sign concordance under a pooled label-permutation procedure corrected for the degeneracy of within-region permutation for the per-parent predictor.  This supports the local interpretation that ARE perturbation and ARE burden can both be represented as finite RNA-stability contacts, but the two records are not interchangeable.  The deletion run is an intervention-side stability boundary; the endogenous transfer run is a predictive half-life transfer boundary with $n=14933$, $\Delta R^2=0.016979$, and $p=0.00049975$.  Their agreement is used only to keep the ARE direction biologically plausible within RNA stability, not to promote the claim to translation, protein abundance, phenotype, or mechanism.

\paragraph{RNA-half-life neighborhood.}
The m6A site-load half-life boundary gives a second independent RNA-stability comparator.  In that record, $5837$ genes crossed the boundary statistic, with static Spearman association $-0.388929$ between m6A load and DMSO log2 half-life, causal Spearman association $0.245804$ for m6A load against delta log2 half-life, length-partial Spearman association $0.227683$, and a two-sided partial permutation value $0.000999001$.  This comparator is not used as evidence for ARE biology.  It only shows that the BioReality table is treating RNA half-life as its own empirical layer: ARE and m6A contacts can both be finite half-life readouts without collapsing into a shared mechanism or a universal RNA-stability rule.

\paragraph{Cross-layer gate.}
The stronger conjectural form asks for a matched coding-window panel with synonymous coordinate labels, same-row translation readout, RNA half-life, local decay-factor RBP occupancy, target-RBP perturbation, wild-type rescue, binding-defective control, non-target control, transcription-rate control, mRNA abundance control, isoform state, motif-density covariates, and local-structure covariates.  The present endogenous ARE run does not contain that full panel.  It contains the RNA half-life transfer statistic and its finite regression improvement, but it does not contain same-window measured decay-factor RBP occupancy, coordinate/occupancy decoupling, target-RBP loss, rescue, or binding-defective rescue rows.  Consequently the admissible conclusion is narrower than the conjectural bridge: ARE burden is a finite endogenous RNA-half-life predictor in this dataset; the missing RBP and perturbation rows block any positive RBP-mediated decay claim.

\paragraph{Refusal conditions.}
The result breaks if high- and low-coordinate labels become exchangeable for half-life after transcription rate, local RNA structure, motif density, GC and dinucleotide composition, codon-pair context, splice or isoform state, transcript abundance, and batch are controlled.  It also breaks if the observed half-life difference is reproduced by RNA structure, motif density, isoform stability, transcriptional differences, abundance artifacts, or batch.  Most importantly, even a stable half-life association is not a mechanism bridge unless measured decay-factor RBP occupancy moves with the finite rows, the effect collapses under target-RBP loss, returns under wild-type rescue, and is not restored by binding-defective or non-target controls.  The recorded $p=0.00049975$ is therefore evidence for the declared endogenous ARE half-life statistic, not evidence for the missing RBP-control circuit.

\paragraph{Local boundary.}
No translation realization, ribosome occupancy, protein abundance, folded structure, physical admissibility, biological function, system phenotype, adaptation, causation, or universal biological law follows from this half-life statistic.  A same-row translation readout is required before interpreting the record as translation-bounded, and a separate layer-matched contact is required for every higher layer.  The current finite numbers support a disciplined RNA-stability statement: in $14933$ endogenous gene rows, adding the ARE term improves half-life prediction by $\Delta R^2=0.016979$ with negative ARE coefficient $-0.0607953$ and permutation value $0.00049975$.  Everything beyond that remains outside the claim until the missing occupancy, perturbation, rescue, translation, and control rows are supplied.
