\subsection{Endogenous 3-prime UTR ARE Load as a Bounded RNA Half-Life Transfer}
\label{sec:bioreality-endogenous-utr3-are-halflife-transfer}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite row object.}
This record concerns a finite transcript-level panel in which each retained row carries an endogenous 3-prime UTR AU-rich element annotation, an RNA half-life readout, and the covariates needed to keep the readout at RNA-stability scope. The intended positive contact is not a protein-output or functional contact. It is a finite RNA half-life boundary: a row may say that an ARE-bearing 3-prime UTR context is associated with shorter measured mRNA persistence after controls, but it may not by itself say that translation, folding, physical admissibility, biological function, phenotype, adaptation, or a general law has been observed. The usable row coordinates are therefore transcript or gene identifiers with matched half-life estimates, ARE status or ARE burden, abundance and normalization context, and whatever transcript-structure or isoform information is available from the payload. The coordinate is read only through the declared finite statistic; geometry, motif naming, or review eligibility is not treated as biological evidence.

\paragraph{Observed finite statistic.}
The endogenous 3-prime UTR half-life transfer run retained $14933$ genes and used $2000$ permutations. The baseline model had $R^2 = 0.0657771$, and adding the ARE term produced test $R^2 = 0.0827561$. The finite gain was therefore $\Delta R^2 = 0.016979$. The fitted ARE coefficient was $\beta_{ARE} = -0.0607953$, with negative sign, and the permutation value was $p = 0.00049975$. In compact form, the checked row-level summary is
$$
\begin{aligned}
 n &= 14933,\\
 R^2_{base} &= 0.0657771,\\
 R^2_{ARE} &= 0.0827561,\\
 \Delta R^2 &= 0.016979,\\
 \beta_{ARE} &= -0.0607953,\\
 p_{perm} &= 0.00049975.
\end{aligned}
$$
These numbers license a bounded statement: in this finite joined panel, the endogenous 3-prime UTR ARE coordinate transfers to an RNA half-life readout with a negative direction and a nonzero controlled contribution. The runtime for the computation was $256.449$ seconds, and the run is explicitly dependent on the earlier synthetic ARE reporter claim rather than replacing it with a universal rule.

\paragraph{Relation to the deletion and methylation boundary checks.}
The direction of the endogenous ARE result is consistent with a separate ARE-deletion stability boundary run. That supporting run used $1000$ actual permutations, a $2000$-permutation grading pass, $226$ BEAS-2B rows, and $2242$ Jurkat rows; its corrected null method was pooled label permutation over deletions, and the sign-concordance flag was true. This second fact does not make the endogenous half-life transfer mechanistic, but it reduces the chance that the present record is merely an idiosyncratic encoding of the endogenous table. A separate m6A half-life boundary supplies a useful contrast: in $5837$ genes, the static m6A-load association with DMSO half-life was $-0.388929$, the causal Spearman statistic for m6A load against $\Delta \log_2$ half-life was $0.245804$, the length-partial statistic was $0.227683$, and the two-sided partial permutation value was $0.000999001$. The contrast matters because both packets touch RNA half-life, yet neither permits promotion to protein, structure, function, mechanism, or universality without a layer-matched contact that actually measures that layer.

\paragraph{Licensed interpretation.}
The present finite record supports a cross-layer relation only in a narrow sense: a coding- or transcript-associated coordinate can be carried to an RNA half-life readout when the rows are kept finite, the statistic is declared, the control surface is named, and the result remains inside RNA stability. The negative $\beta_{ARE}$ means that the ARE-bearing direction is associated with shorter RNA persistence in the retained endogenous panel. The $\Delta R^2$ value is modest, so the result should be read as a bounded residual contribution rather than a complete explanation of half-life. The permutation value says that the observed gain is not easily reproduced by the declared label randomization; it does not identify a decay factor, an RBP, a causal pathway, or a biological purpose.

\paragraph{Boundary against RBP mechanism.}
The stronger RBP-bridge formulation would require same-organism and same-window measured decay-factor RBP occupancy, coordinate--occupancy decoupling, target-RBP loss, wild-type rescue, binding-defective rescue or non-target controls, transcription-rate controls, local RNA-structure controls, motif-density controls, GC and dinucleotide controls, codon-pair context, isoform state, transcript abundance, batch, and a same-row translation readout held as a boundary variable. Those rows are not supplied by the present endogenous half-life run. Consequently, a changed half-life associated with ARE load is not reported here as measured RBP occupancy, RBP-mediated decay, translation realization, protein abundance, folded structure, physical admissibility, function realization, phenotype, adaptation, causation, or a general biological law. If future rows show that high- and low-coordinate labels are exchangeable after transcription, structure, motif, composition, codon-context, isoform, abundance, and batch controls, the coordinate must remain descriptive. If half-life changes without a matching decay-factor RBP occupancy shift, or if target-RBP loss and wild-type rescue fail to control the effect, the record remains an RNA-stability association only.

\paragraph{Declared break conditions.}
This record breaks if the ARE term is used as a direct translation readout, as a protein-output readout, as a folding or physical-admissibility readout, or as evidence of biological function. It also breaks if an RBP mechanism is claimed without measured local occupancy and perturbation-rescue rows in the same finite panel. A binding-defective rescue, non-target control, RNA-structure covariate, motif-density covariate, transcription-rate covariate, isoform-stability effect, transcript-abundance effect, or batch effect that reproduces the pattern would refuse the bridge interpretation. The surviving statement is therefore deliberately small: in the verified endogenous RNA half-life panel, the ARE coordinate adds a negative, permutation-supported RNA-stability signal, while every stronger layer remains outside the claim until separately measured.
