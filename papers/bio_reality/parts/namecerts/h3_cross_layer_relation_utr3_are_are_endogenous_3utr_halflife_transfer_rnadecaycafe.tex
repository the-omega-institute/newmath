\subsection{Endogenous ARE Load as a Bounded Three-Prime-UTR Half-Life Transfer}\label{sec:bioreality-endogenous-are-halflife-transfer}\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite record.}
This record concerns a finite panel of transcript rows in which an AU-rich element load in the three-prime untranslated region is tested against an endogenous RNA half-life readout. The registered subject is \texttt{h3.cross\_layer\_relation.utr3\_are.are\_endogenous\_3utr\_halflife\_transfer\_rnadecaycafe}. The biological object is not a whole-organism law and not a protein-level claim: it is a table of gene or transcript rows carrying an ARE-derived coordinate, half-life data, transcript-level covariates, and the declared statistic used to decide whether the ARE coordinate transfers into a bounded RNA-stability readout. The present finite run contains $14933$ genes, uses $2000$ label permutations, and reports a negative ARE coefficient,
$$
\begin{aligned}
 n_{\mathrm{genes}} &= 14933,\\
 N_{\mathrm{perm}} &= 2000,\\
 \beta_{\mathrm{ARE}} &= -0.0607953.
\end{aligned}
$$
The sign is biologically directional only at the RNA-stability surface: larger ARE burden is associated with shorter endogenous half-life in the fitted finite panel. This sign does not by itself identify the decay factor, prove direct binding, or assert a translation, protein, structure, function, phenotype, adaptation, or universal sequence rule.

\paragraph{Statistic.}
The baseline model explains $R^2=0.0657771$ of the held finite response under the registered controls, while the ARE-augmented model reaches $R^2=0.0827561$. The observed gain is
$$
\begin{aligned}
 R^2_{\mathrm{baseline}} &= 0.0657771,\\
 R^2_{\mathrm{ARE}} &= 0.0827561,\\
 \Delta R^2 &= 0.016979,\\
 p_{\mathrm{perm}} &= 0.00049975.
\end{aligned}
$$
The permutation value is computed from the same finite experiment, with runtime $256.449$ seconds. The recorded dependence on the original synthetic ARE reporter claim is treated as lineage of the tested motif hypothesis, not as a promotion of the endogenous table into a mechanism statement. The accepted reading is therefore narrow: in this RNAdecaycafe-style endogenous half-life panel, the ARE coordinate contributes measurable half-life information beyond the baseline variables used in the run.

\paragraph{Transfer interpretation.}
The transfer licensed here is from a motif-level three-prime-UTR coordinate to an endogenous RNA half-life readout. It is not a transfer to translation realization. It is also not a transfer to protein abundance, folded structure, physical admissibility, biological function, system phenotype, adaptation, causation, or general biological law. In the notation of the finite run, the admissible conclusion is the bounded implication that the ARE coordinate carries residual predictive mass for RNA half-life inside the declared row set:
$$
\begin{aligned}
 \mathrm{ARE\ coordinate} + \mathrm{controls}
 &\longmapsto \mathrm{RNA\ half\!\mbox{-}\!life\ readout},\\
 \Delta R^2 &= 0.016979,\\
 \beta_{\mathrm{ARE}} &< 0.
\end{aligned}
$$
The mandatory translation boundary remains present as a guardrail: a translation readout may be required before a higher cross-layer interpretation is considered, but the half-life statistic itself is not a translation statistic. Matched mRNA abundance controls, transcription-rate controls, isoform state, local RNA structure, motif density, GC and dinucleotide composition, codon-pair context, transcript abundance, and batch are part of the finite discipline separating the RNA-stability surface from downstream biological claims.

\paragraph{Relation to deletion evidence.}
A second finite ARE-linked fact, \texttt{h3.cross\_layer\_relation.utr3\_are.are\_deletion\_mrna\_stability\_boundary\_siegel2022}, supplies a companion deletion boundary rather than a replacement for the endogenous half-life statistic. That run records $2242$ Jurkat rows and $226$ BEAS2B rows, with $1000$ actual permutations, $2000$ grading permutations, and sign concordance reported as true. Its null method is a pooled label permutation over deletions, corrected because the within-region route is degenerate for a per-parent predictor. These numbers support a local consistency check: both finite ARE surfaces point in an RNA-stability direction, but they remain distinct row systems and do not jointly prove an RBP-mediated mechanism.

\paragraph{Mechanism refusal.}
The RBP bridge is deliberately not closed by the present evidence. A positive decay-factor mechanism would require the same finite rows, or a declared matched extension, to carry measured local decay-factor RBP occupancy, coordinate--occupancy decoupling, target-RBP loss, wild-type rescue, binding-defective rescue or non-target controls, and the finite statistic showing that the half-life effect tracks those rows. The present record lacks those mechanism-bearing rows. Therefore the result remains an endogenous RNA half-life transfer with a negative ARE coefficient and a nonzero permutation-supported gain in explained variance, not a proof that a specified RBP caused the decay effect.

\paragraph{Local boundary.}
If high-ARE and low-ARE labels become exchangeable for half-life after the declared controls, the coordinate is only a bounded descriptor. If RNA half-life differs but measured RBP occupancy does not change, the result is an RNA-stability association rather than an RBP bridge. If target-RBP loss, wild-type rescue, binding-defective rescue, non-target controls, RNA structure, motif density, transcription, isoform behavior, abundance, or batch can reproduce the pattern, the mechanism claim is refused. No translation, folding, physical admissibility, function, phenotype, adaptation, causation, or universality follows from this chapter unless a separate layer-matched contact supplies the corresponding finite rows and statistic.
