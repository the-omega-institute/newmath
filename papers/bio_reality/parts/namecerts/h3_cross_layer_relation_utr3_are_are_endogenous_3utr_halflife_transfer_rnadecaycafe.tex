\subsection{Endogenous AU-rich 3UTR signal as a bounded RNA half-life transfer}
\label{sec:bioreality-are-endogenous-3utr-halflife-transfer-rnadecaycafe}

\paragraph{Finite row object.}
The record considered here is a finite gene-level RNA-stability table in which an AU-rich element indicator is evaluated against endogenous mRNA half-life.  The carrier is not a free sequence law: each row is a measured transcript or gene row with a half-life readout, a baseline feature set, and an explicit finite statistic.  The positive datum is the RNA-decay-cafe run \texttt{e96bf26e626b}, in which $14933$ genes were retained.  A baseline model attained held-out $R^2=0.0657771$, while the model with the ARE term attained held-out $R^2=0.0827561$.  Thus the observed increment was
$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta R^2 &= 0.0827561-0.0657771 \\
           &= 0.016979 .
\end{aligned}
$$
The fitted ARE coefficient was negative, $\beta_{\mathrm{ARE}}=-0.0607953$, with the recorded sign classified as negative.  The permutation audit used $2000$ permutations and returned $p=0.00049975$.  The runtime recorded for the finite computation was $256.449$ seconds.  This row set therefore supports a bounded statement: within the measured endogenous panel, the ARE label adds reproducible predictive information for RNA half-life beyond the declared baseline, and the direction of the fitted term is toward shorter half-life.

\paragraph{Relation to the synthetic deletion boundary.}
The endogenous half-life transfer is not interpreted in isolation.  A separate ARE deletion boundary, run \texttt{a41440ab2792}, supplies a nearby finite control surface: $2242$ Jurkat rows and $226$ BEAS2B rows were retained, with $1000$ actual permutations, $2000$ grading permutations, and a sign-concordant outcome.  Its null method was a corrected pooled label permutation over deletions, because a within-region permutation was degenerate for the per-parent predictor.  The deletion result and the endogenous transfer result are not duplicates.  The deletion table tests whether removing ARE-bearing segments perturbs mRNA stability in an edited or deletion-style setting; the endogenous table asks whether naturally occurring ARE burden transfers into a held-out half-life statistic across a larger gene panel.  Their agreement is used only as finite reinforcement for an RNA-stability boundary.  It does not turn the ARE coordinate into a translation readout, protein-output measurement, folded-structure statement, phenotypic assay, adaptive explanation, or universal rule.

\paragraph{Cross-layer status.}
The licensed cross-layer relation is deliberately narrow.  The coordinate side is an ARE-bearing 3UTR descriptor; the readout side is RNA half-life.  The accepted finite statistic is the observed negative ARE coefficient together with the positive held-out increment and the permutation value.  In symbols, the row certificate has the form
$$
\begin{aligned}
N &= 14933, \\
R^2_{\mathrm{base}} &= 0.0657771, \\
R^2_{\mathrm{ARE}} &= 0.0827561, \\
\Delta R^2 &= 0.016979, \\
\beta_{\mathrm{ARE}} &= -0.0607953, \\
p_{\mathrm{perm}} &= 0.00049975 .
\end{aligned}
$$
This is enough to say that the finite RNA half-life panel detects an ARE-associated stability signal.  It is not enough to say that the signal is produced by a specified decay-factor RNA-binding protein.  The RBP bridge would require same-row measured local occupancy of the prespecified factor, coordinate--occupancy decoupling, target-factor loss, wild-type rescue, and binding-defective or non-target rescue controls.  Those rows are not part of this certificate.  Consequently, the present chapter states an RNA half-life transfer result, not an RNA-binding-protein mechanism.

\paragraph{Translation boundary.}
The finite statement also stays below the translation layer.  A same-row translation readout is required before any downstream interpretation involving translation efficiency, ribosome occupancy, or protein output can be made.  The current RNA half-life contact records an endogenous stability statistic, and the matched mRNA-abundance and ribosome-profiling contacts are only routing conditions for a stronger panel.  They are not themselves a joined matrix with coordinate labels, translation readout, half-life, RBP occupancy, perturbation state, and rescue state.  Therefore a half-life difference is not promoted to translation realization.  No claim is made about protein abundance, protein folding, structural order, physical admissibility, biological function, system phenotype, adaptation, causation, or a general biological law.

\paragraph{Comparison with another RNA-stability surface.}
The m6A half-life boundary gives an independent example of the same discipline.  In run \texttt{7cd189f15c4b}, $5837$ genes passed the coverage rule, the static Spearman association between m6A load and DMSO half-life was $-0.388929$, the length-partial Spearman association for m6A load against $\Delta \log_2$ half-life was $0.227683$, the causal Spearman statistic was $0.245804$, and the two-sided partial permutation value was $0.000999001$.  That record was explicitly marked as a boundary statistic rather than a mechanism or function claim.  The ARE chapter follows the same standard: a finite RNA-stability statistic may cross the boundary into measured half-life only at the row resolution actually present in the data.  It may not borrow mechanism, translation, function, or universality from nearby biological vocabulary.

\paragraph{Break condition.}
The claim breaks if the ARE term becomes exchangeable with the null after the declared controls, if the held-out increment disappears, if batch or identifier mapping explains the fitted sign, or if the result is described as a decay-factor RBP bridge without measured occupancy and perturbation-rescue rows.  It also breaks if the chapter treats ARE geometry, 3UTR membership, or the half-life association as evidence for translation, protein output, folding, physical admissibility, function, phenotype, adaptation, causation, or universality.  The surviving assertion is only this: in the finite RNA-decay-cafe endogenous panel, an ARE-associated 3UTR descriptor contributes a negative, permutation-supported RNA half-life signal, and a separate ARE deletion surface points in a concordant RNA-stability direction.
