\subsection{Endogenous ARE Load as a Bounded RNA-Half-Life Readout}
\label{sec:bioreality-endogenous-are-halflife-transfer-rnadecaycafe}

\paragraph{Finite row object.}
The row object is a finite, organism-scoped table of endogenous transcript records carrying a $3'$ UTR AU-rich element indicator or load, an RNA half-life readout, and the covariates needed to keep the readout at RNA-stability scope.  The operative biological object is not a free sequence law.  It is a joined panel in which each retained gene row has a declared ARE coordinate, a measured or curated half-life value, and enough transcript-level context to ask whether ARE-bearing rows remain separated from non-ARE rows after a baseline model has already absorbed simpler explanatory terms.  In the verified run for \texttt{h3.cross\_layer\_relation.utr3\_are.are\_endogenous\_3utr\_halflife\_transfer\_rnadecaycafe}, the retained table contains $14933$ genes and uses $2000$ permutations for the declared boundary statistic.  The baseline model has test $R^2=0.0657771$; the ARE-augmented model has test $R^2=0.0827561$; the added explanatory mass is therefore
$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta R^2 &= 0.0827561 - 0.0657771 \\
           &= 0.016979.
\end{aligned}
$$
The fitted ARE coefficient is negative, $\beta_{\mathrm{ARE}}=-0.0607953$, and the permutation value is $p=0.00049975$.  These numbers license a bounded statement: in this finite endogenous table, the ARE coordinate contributes a reproducible RNA-half-life separation beyond the encoded baseline.  They do not by themselves identify a decay-factor RBP, a translation mechanism, or a downstream protein-level outcome.

\paragraph{Statistic and readback.}
Let $i$ range over the retained gene rows.  Each row carries an ARE coordinate $A_i$, an RNA half-life readout $H_i$, and baseline covariates $Z_i$ representing the available transcript-level controls.  The verified comparison is a finite predictive contrast between a baseline surface and an ARE-augmented surface:
$$
\begin{aligned}
H_i &\sim f_0(Z_i), \\
H_i &\sim f_1(Z_i,A_i), \\
\Delta R^2 &= R^2(f_1)-R^2(f_0)=0.016979.
\end{aligned}
$$
The negative sign of $\beta_{\mathrm{ARE}}$ records that the ARE-bearing direction is associated with shorter half-life in the fitted coordinate convention.  The permutation result, $p=0.00049975$ over $2000$ permutations, makes the readout non-null at the finite-table level.  The correct interpretation is therefore a same-layer RNA-stability boundary: endogenous $3'$ UTR ARE information transfers from a prior synthetic reporter setting into an endogenous half-life panel as a measurable predictor of transcript decay behavior.  The phrase \textit{depends on the original synthetic ARE reporter claim} is part of the provenance of the statistic, not an extra biological mechanism.

\paragraph{Relation to nearby RNA-stability checks.}
The ARE deletion boundary run \texttt{h3.cross\_layer\_relation.utr3\_are.are\_deletion\_mrna\_stability\_boundary\_siegel2022} supplies a separate finite check at the perturbation-of-element level: it reports $226$ BEAS2B rows, $2242$ Jurkat rows, $1000$ actual permutations, $2000$ grading permutations, and sign concordance under a pooled label-permutation method corrected for the degeneracy of within-region labels.  That result is not the same table as the endogenous half-life transfer run, but it gives a neighboring RNA-stability contact: changing ARE-bearing sequence context can be read at mRNA-stability scope when the finite comparison is declared in advance.  A second nearby RNA-stability check, \texttt{h3.cross\_layer\_relation.rna\_modification.m6a\_site\_load\_mrna\_halflife\_boundary\_gse163500}, keeps the same discipline for a different RNA feature: $5837$ genes, a DMSO static Spearman value of $-0.388929$, a causal Spearman value of $0.245804$ for m6A load versus $\Delta\log_2$ half-life, a length-partial value of $0.227683$, and a two-sided partial permutation value of $0.000999001$.  These neighboring records support the paper's broader rule that RNA-feature coordinates may cross into RNA half-life only through finite matched readouts and declared controls; they do not merge into a single universal rule.

\paragraph{What is licensed.}
The licensed claim is deliberately narrow.  The endogenous ARE run supports the statement that, in a finite joined gene table with $14933$ retained rows, an ARE coordinate adds measurable RNA-half-life information beyond the encoded baseline: $\Delta R^2=0.016979$, $\beta_{\mathrm{ARE}}=-0.0607953$, and $p=0.00049975$.  This is enough for a bounded cross-layer relation from a transcript feature coordinate to an RNA half-life readout, because the coordinate is not merely named but evaluated against a finite external half-life surface.  The relation remains a readout relation, not a declaration that AREs are sufficient causes of decay in every cell type, condition, isoform, or organism.  Batch, isoform ambiguity, transcript abundance, local structure, motif density, dinucleotide composition, transcription rate, and identifier mapping can still move the estimate, and the row object must be treated as a finite panel rather than as an unrestricted biological theorem.

\paragraph{What is not licensed.}
No translation, ribosome occupancy, protein abundance, folding, physical admissibility, biological function, phenotype, adaptation, causation, decay-factor mechanism, or universal biological law follows from the verified ARE half-life statistic alone.  In particular, a decay-factor RBP interpretation would require same-window measured RBP occupancy, coordinate--occupancy decoupling, target-RBP loss, wild-type rescue, binding-defective or non-target controls, and a declared finite statistic showing that the half-life effect follows that RBP route.  The current verified ARE transfer row does not contain those occupancy and perturbation-rescue fields.  Likewise, a translation-conditioned claim would require a same-row translation readout and matched mRNA controls before RNA half-life could be discussed as translation-attached.  The present result therefore stops at RNA half-life separation: it is a concrete endogenous ARE-to-half-life readout, with a negative ARE coefficient and a non-null permutation value, but it does not promote RNA stability into translation, protein structure, function, mechanism, or universality.
