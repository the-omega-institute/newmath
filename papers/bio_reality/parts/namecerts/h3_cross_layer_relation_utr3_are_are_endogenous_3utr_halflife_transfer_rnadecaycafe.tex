\subsection{Endogenous ARE signal as a bounded RNA half-life transfer record}\label{subsec:bioreality-are-endogenous-halflife-transfer}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite panel.}
The record concerns a finite gene-level panel in which AU-rich-element annotation is tested against endogenous RNA half-life after ordinary expression and sequence controls have been attached. The verified row count is $14933$ genes. The tested statistic compares a baseline model with test $R^2=0.0657771$ to a model that includes the ARE term and reaches $R^2=0.0827561$, giving
$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta R^2 &= 0.0827561-0.0657771 \\
           &= 0.016979 .
\end{aligned}
$$
The fitted ARE coefficient is $\beta_{\mathrm{ARE}}=-0.0607953$, and the stored sign label is negative. The permutation calculation used $2000$ permutations and reports $p=0.00049975$, with runtime $256.449$ seconds. These numbers make the row set a concrete RNA-stability contact: in the joined endogenous panel, the ARE coordinate contributes a small but nonzero half-life prediction increment beyond the baseline covariates represented in the finite statistic.

\paragraph{Interpretation of the negative coefficient.}
The negative sign is read only at the RNA half-life layer. In this chapter, an ARE-positive row is not being promoted to a translation event, a protein-output event, a folded-structure event, or a cellular-function event. The numerical claim is narrower: within the finite panel, the ARE coordinate is associated with shorter RNA half-life in the controlled endogenous readout. The value $\Delta R^2=0.016979$ is therefore a bounded improvement in half-life prediction, not a law of RNA decay and not a mechanism assignment. The permutation value $p=0.00049975$ certifies that the observed improvement is separated from the label-randomized null used in the run; it does not identify the molecular agent that executes degradation.

\paragraph{Relation to the deletion surface.}
The same bounded reading is reinforced by the separate ARE-deletion stability record. That record keeps $226$ BEAS-2B rows and $2242$ Jurkat rows, runs $1000$ actual permutations, records a grading permutation count of $2000$, and stores sign concordance as true under a corrected pooled-label permutation over deletions. The deletion result and the endogenous transfer result point in the same RNA-stability direction, but their scopes remain distinct. The deletion rows test a perturbative ARE-removal surface; the endogenous transfer rows test whether ARE annotation carries predictive half-life information in an endogenous joined panel. Their combination supports a stable RNA-layer pattern, not a claim that a particular decay-factor RBP has been measured or causally isolated in the same rows.

\paragraph{Required bridge data not present here.}
A stronger RBP-mediated bridge would require same-row measured occupancy for a prespecified decay-factor RBP, coordinate--occupancy decoupling, loss of the target RBP, wild-type rescue, binding-defective rescue or non-target controls, and matched transcription, abundance, isoform, structure, motif-density, composition, codon-context, batch, and translation-boundary controls. The stored endogenous half-life run does not provide those finite rows. Consequently, the present chapter licenses the following statement and no more: ARE annotation contributes a negative endogenous RNA half-life term in a $14933$-gene panel, with $\Delta R^2=0.016979$ and $p=0.00049975$ under $2000$ permutations. It does not license the statement that the effect is carried by any named RBP, that translation realizes the RNA-stability signal, or that protein abundance, folding, physical admissibility, biological function, adaptation, causation, or universality follows.

\paragraph{Break condition.}
The record breaks if the ARE term is used as a substitute for measured decay-factor RBP occupancy or perturbation-rescue evidence. It also breaks if the half-life association is described as translation, protein output, structure, function, phenotype, mechanism, adaptation, or a general biological rule. A later panel could strengthen the claim only by adding the missing same-row occupancy and perturbation columns and then recomputing the finite RBP-decay statistic under the declared controls. Until those columns exist, the correct classification is a bounded endogenous RNA half-life transfer record with a concordant ARE-deletion companion surface.
