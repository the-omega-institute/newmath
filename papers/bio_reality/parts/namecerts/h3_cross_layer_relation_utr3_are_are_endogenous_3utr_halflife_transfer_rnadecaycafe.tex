\subsection{Endogenous ARE Load as a Bounded RNA Half-Life Transfer Record}
\label{sec:bioreality-utr3-are-endogenous-halflife-transfer-rnadecaycafe}

\paragraph{Finite row carrier.}
This record concerns a finite transcript-indexed panel in which an AU-rich element coordinate is read against an RNA half-life surface.  The operative rows are not treated as a general law about codons, translation, protein state, or organismal function.  They are transcript or gene rows carrying an endogenous $3'$ UTR ARE descriptor, an externally curated RNA half-life readout, and the covariates needed to prevent the ARE coordinate from being promoted beyond the RNA-stability layer.  In the finite run used here, the joined panel contains $14933$ genes.  The fitted baseline explains $R^2=0.0657771$ of the held-out half-life variation, while the model with the ARE term explains $R^2=0.0827561$.  Thus the finite increment recorded for this packet is
$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta R^2 &= 0.0827561-0.0657771 \\
           &= 0.016979 .
\end{aligned}
$$
The estimated ARE coefficient is negative, $\beta_{\mathrm{ARE}}=-0.0607953$, and the sign audit records the coefficient as negative.  The permutation audit used $2000$ permutations and returned $p=0.00049975$.  The run identifier for this finite computation is \texttt{e96bf26e626b}, with runtime $256.449$ seconds and verification timestamp \texttt{2026-06-24T17:56:44+00:00}.

\paragraph{Bounded interpretation.}
The empirical content is a same-layer RNA half-life transfer: endogenous ARE load carries a reproducible negative association with RNA half-life after the declared baseline has been applied.  The finite statistic is therefore a licensed RNA-stability boundary, not a translation statement.  The record may say that the ARE coordinate contributes a small but permutation-supported increment to an RNA half-life predictor on the joined panel.  It may not say that the coordinate realizes translation, protein output, folded structure, physical admissibility, biological function, phenotype, adaptation, causation, or a universal biological rule.  Those layers require their own matched contacts, and none of those contacts is supplied by the RNA half-life table itself.

\paragraph{Relation to the synthetic deletion control.}
A separate finite ARE deletion boundary gives a useful scope check for the endogenous transfer.  In that record, the pooled-label permutation audit over deletion rows used $1000$ actual permutations and $2000$ grading permutations, with $226$ BEAS-2B rows and $2242$ Jurkat rows.  The sign was concordant, and the audit explicitly classifies the within-region permutation as degenerate for the per-parent predictor.  This companion fact supports the narrower conclusion that ARE-bearing regions can be read at the RNA-stability layer in finite assays, but it does not turn the endogenous RNAdecayCafe transfer into a mechanism claim.  The two facts agree only at the level of a bounded RNA half-life surface: one is an endogenous transfer over $14933$ genes, and the other is a deletion-based stability boundary over two finite cellular panels.

\paragraph{Layer separation against nearby RNA half-life evidence.}
The same discipline is visible in the RNA-modification half-life boundary.  In the m6A site-load audit, $5837$ genes were retained, the static Spearman association between m6A load and DMSO log half-life was $-0.388929$, and the causal Spearman association between m6A load and $\Delta \log_2$ half-life was $0.245804$.  After length partialing, the corresponding statistic was $0.227683$, with two-sided partial permutation value $0.000999001$.  These numbers are not evidence for the ARE coordinate; they serve as an internal comparison showing how the paper treats different RNA-layer contacts.  Both the ARE and m6A records are allowed to speak about finite RNA half-life readouts.  Neither record licenses translation realization, protein folding, protein function, phenotype, or a general biological law without a separate layer-matched measurement.

\paragraph{Required break conditions.}
The positive ARE half-life transfer breaks if the high-ARE and low-ARE labels become exchangeable for RNA half-life after the declared controls, or if identifier mapping, transcript isoform ambiguity, batch, local RNA structure, motif density, GC and dinucleotide composition, codon-pair context, transcription rate, or transcript abundance reproduces the effect.  It also breaks if the result is rephrased as a decay-factor RBP mechanism without the additional rows needed for that claim.  A decay-factor RBP bridge would require same-organism, same-window measured RBP occupancy, coordinate--occupancy decoupling, target-RBP loss, wild-type rescue, binding-defective rescue or non-target controls, and an explicit finite statistic tying those perturbation states to RNA half-life.  Those rows are absent from the verified finite table summarized here.

\paragraph{Local conclusion.}
The chapter records a finite, reproducible RNA-stability association: endogenous $3'$ UTR ARE load improves a baseline RNA half-life predictor by $\Delta R^2=0.016979$ on $14933$ genes, with $\beta_{\mathrm{ARE}}=-0.0607953$ and $p=0.00049975$ under $2000$ permutations.  The correct biological reading is deliberately modest.  ARE load is licensed here as a bounded RNA half-life coordinate, supported by a finite external half-life panel and compatible with a separate deletion-based ARE stability boundary.  No translation, folding, physical-admissibility, function, mechanism, phenotype, adaptation, causation, or universality claim follows from this evidence alone.
