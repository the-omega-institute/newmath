\subsection{Endogenous 3 exorpdfstring{$'$}{'} UTR ARE Transfer to RNA Half-Life}\label{sec:bioreality-utr3-are-endogenous-halflife-transfer}\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite row carrier.} This record concerns a finite panel of coding-RNA windows whose synonymous coordinate labels are matched to RNA-level readouts rather than to protein-level outcomes.  A row is admitted only when it can carry, at minimum, a coordinate label, an RNA half-life value, matched mRNA abundance or transcription-rate controls, local sequence-composition controls, isoform state, batch state, and an explicit break condition.  The intended stronger panel also carries same-row translation readout, measured occupancy of a prespecified decay-factor RNA-binding protein, target-RBP loss, wild-type rescue, binding-defective rescue or non-target control, local RNA-structure covariates, motif density, GC and dinucleotide composition, codon-pair context, transcript abundance, and splice or isoform state.  The chapter therefore treats the endogenous ARE signal as a bounded RNA-stability transfer surface, not as a direct statement about translation, protein output, folding, physical admissibility, function, adaptation, causation, or a universal biological rule.

\paragraph{Observed endogenous transfer statistic.} The finite statistic used here is the run attached to \texttt{h3.cross\_layer\_relation.utr3\_are.are\_endogenous\_3utr\_halflife\_transfer\_rnadecaycafe}.  Its carrier contains $14933$ gene rows and uses $2000$ label permutations.  The fitted baseline has $R^2=0.0657771$, while the ARE-augmented test surface has $R^2=0.0827561$.  The recorded improvement is therefore
$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta R^2 &= 0.0827561 - 0.0657771 \\
           &= 0.016979.
\end{aligned}
$$
The ARE coefficient is negative, $\beta_{\mathrm{ARE}}=-0.0607953$, with recorded sign \texttt{negative}; the permutation probability is $p=0.00049975$.  The runtime for this finite computation is $256.449$ seconds.  These numbers license the narrow statement that endogenous 3 exorpdfstring{$'$}{'} UTR ARE burden carries a reproducible finite association with mRNA half-life after the declared baseline surface is applied.  They do not, by themselves, identify a decay-factor RBP, establish an RBP perturbation mechanism, or show that synonymous coding-window geometry caused the half-life difference.

\paragraph{Relation to the deletion boundary.} The endogenous transfer is read alongside the finite deletion packet \texttt{h3.cross\_layer\_relation.utr3\_are.are\_deletion\_mrna\_stability\_boundary\_siegel2022}.  That earlier boundary contains $226$ BEAS2B rows and $2242$ Jurkat rows, uses $1000$ actual permutations, records a $2000$-permutation grading run, and reports sign concordance under a corrected pooled label-permutation test over deletions.  In this chapter the deletion result is not reused as a mechanistic proof.  It serves only as the finite companion showing that an ARE-directed perturbation surface and an endogenous ARE half-life surface point in a compatible RNA-stability direction.  The two records together justify a transfer question: whether an ARE-associated half-life pattern seen in an endogenous transcript panel can be treated as the RNA-stability side of a cross-layer relation once the missing same-window translation and RBP-control rows are supplied.

\paragraph{Cross-layer license.} A positive cross-layer reading requires more than the endogenous half-life association.  The row must also include a same-row translation readout so that RNA stability is not silently promoted to translation realization.  It must include measured local occupancy of the nominated decay-factor RBP, and the occupancy relation must survive coordinate-to-occupancy decoupling rather than merely tracking motif density, local structure, or nucleotide composition.  The half-life effect must collapse under target-RBP loss and return under wild-type rescue.  It must not return under a binding-defective rescue or under a non-target control.  It must remain separated from transcription rate, mRNA abundance, local RNA structure, motif density, GC and dinucleotide composition, codon-pair context, splice or isoform state, transcript abundance, batch, and simpler baseline explanations.

\paragraph{Local refusal conditions.} The claim is refused if high-coordinate and low-coordinate labels are exchangeable for RNA half-life after the declared controls.  It is also refused if RNA half-life differs while measured decay-factor RBP occupancy is unchanged, if target-RBP loss and wild-type rescue fail to track the effect, or if binding-defective rescue, non-target control, local structure, motif density, transcriptional variation, isoform stability, transcript abundance, or batch reproduces the pattern.  Even when the RNA half-life statistic is accepted, no translation effect, ribosome-occupancy realization, protein abundance, protein folding, physical admissibility, biological function, system phenotype, adaptation, mechanism, causation, or universal law follows without an additional layer-matched measurement surface and its own finite statistic.

\paragraph{Chapter verdict.} The ready portion of the record is an endogenous ARE-to-half-life transfer boundary: $n=14933$, $\Delta R^2=0.016979$, $\beta_{\mathrm{ARE}}=-0.0607953$, and $p=0.00049975$ under $2000$ permutations.  The result is strengthened in scope by the separate ARE deletion boundary with $226$ BEAS2B rows, $2242$ Jurkat rows, and sign-concordant deletion behavior, but it remains below a full decay-factor RBP mechanism bridge.  The finite-row evidence is sufficient for a bounded RNA half-life chapter and for a sharply stated next experiment: attach the same-window translation readout, RBP occupancy, target-RBP loss, wild-type rescue, binding-defective rescue, and non-target controls before any stronger biological interpretation is allowed.
