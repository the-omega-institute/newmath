\subsection{Endogenous 3UTR ARE Half-Life Transfer With a Translation Boundary}
\label{sec:bioreality-utr3-are-endogenous-halflife-transfer-rnadecaycafe}

\paragraph{Finite row object.}
This record concerns a finite panel of endogenous transcript rows in which AU-rich element burden in the 3UTR is tested against an RNA half-life readout. The row object is not a free sequence law and not a direct mechanism assay. Each usable row is interpreted as a transcript-indexed tuple containing an ARE descriptor, the endogenous RNA half-life measurement, the baseline covariates used by the finite regression, and the declared exclusion of downstream protein, structure, function, and phenotype interpretations. The intended stronger bridge would require a same-window coding-coordinate label, same-row translation readout, measured local decay-factor RBP occupancy, target-RBP perturbation, wild-type rescue, binding-defective or non-target controls, transcription-rate controls, mRNA-abundance controls, isoform state, motif-density and local-structure covariates, GC and dinucleotide composition, codon-pair context, batch, and a declared break condition. The finite verification available here reaches the endogenous RNA half-life transfer surface, not that full RBP-perturbation bridge.

\paragraph{Observed scalar surface.}
The primary finite run for \texttt{h3.cross\_layer\_relation.utr3\_are.are\_endogenous\_3utr\_halflife\_transfer\_rnadecaycafe} used experiment run \texttt{e96bf26e626b}. It kept $14933$ genes and used $2000$ permutations. The baseline model had $R^2 = 0.0657771$, while the ARE-augmented test model had $R^2 = 0.0827561$, giving
$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta R^2 &= 0.0827561 - 0.0657771 \\
           &= 0.016979 .
\end{aligned}
$$
The fitted ARE coefficient was $\beta_{ARE} = -0.0607953$, with recorded sign \texttt{negative}, and the two-sided permutation level was $p = 0.00049975$. The measured direction therefore says that, in this finite endogenous table and under the declared baseline, higher ARE burden is associated with shorter RNA half-life. This is a bounded RNA-stability readout: the value is a row-level half-life transfer statistic, not an observation of translation, protein abundance, folding, localization, molecular function, phenotype, adaptation, causation, or a universal biological rule.

\paragraph{Relation to the deletion boundary.}
A separate finite ARE deletion boundary, \texttt{h3.cross\_layer\_relation.utr3\_are.are\_deletion\_mrna\_stability\_boundary\_siegel2022}, supplies the closest paired context for this endogenous transfer record. That run, \texttt{a41440ab2792}, used $1000$ actual permutations, a $2000$-permutation grading pass, $226$ BEAS2B deletion rows, and $2242$ Jurkat deletion rows; its sign-concordance flag was true. Its null method is recorded as a corrected pooled label permutation over deletions because the within-region route was degenerate for the per-parent predictor. This deletion evidence does not become the endogenous RNA-decay mechanism by itself, but it gives a finite companion boundary: one surface reports perturbation of ARE-containing 3UTR segments, while the present surface reports endogenous half-life transfer over $14933$ genes. The two rows of evidence agree only at the limited level that ARE-related 3UTR information can be tested against RNA stability in finite data.

\paragraph{Permitted conclusion.}
The permitted conclusion is narrow. Within the available endogenous half-life table, the ARE descriptor improves the finite prediction surface from $R^2 = 0.0657771$ to $R^2 = 0.0827561$, with $\Delta R^2 = 0.016979$ and $p = 0.00049975$ under $2000$ permutations. The negative coefficient $\beta_{ARE} = -0.0607953$ records the direction of the association. This licenses a bounded cross-layer statement from 3UTR ARE annotation to RNA half-life readout, provided it is kept as a transcript-stability association and not promoted into a translation or protein-layer statement.

\paragraph{Local refusal conditions.}
The chapter refuses any stronger claim whenever the finite rows lack a same-row translation readout, matched transcription or mRNA controls, measured local decay-factor RBP occupancy, target-RBP loss, wild-type rescue, binding-defective rescue, non-target controls, local RNA-structure controls, motif-density controls, isoform diagnostics, transcript-abundance controls, and batch controls. If high- and low-coordinate labels are exchangeable after those controls, the coordinate remains only a descriptor. If RNA half-life differs but measured decay-factor RBP occupancy does not change, or if target-RBP loss and wild-type rescue do not control the effect, the result remains an RNA-stability association rather than an RBP mechanism bridge. If binding-defective rescue, non-target controls, RNA structure, motif density, transcription, isoform state, abundance, or batch reproduce the pattern, the bridge claim is rejected.

\paragraph{Cannot-claim boundary.}
No translation realization follows from the half-life statistic alone. No protein output, protein folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, system phenotype, adaptation, causation, RNA-decay mechanism, decay-factor RBP mechanism, or universality follows from the reported $R^2$, $\Delta R^2$, $\beta_{ARE}$, or permutation value. Such claims require separate layer-matched contacts and their own finite statistics. In this chapter, the scientific content is the observed endogenous 3UTR ARE to RNA half-life transfer, supported by $14933$ gene rows and a significant finite permutation result, with the stronger RBP and translation-conditioned mechanism boundary explicitly left unlicensed.
