\subsection{Window-six shape significance for the standard codon table}
\label{subsec:h0-m-shape-significance}

\paragraph{Claim.}
The claim tested here is that the standard-code codon table, when read through a six-coordinate codon display, presents a small local tile whose boundary shape is statistically atypical among sampled label-preserving alternatives. The biological object is the curated standard-code codon table: each codon is assigned an amino-acid or stop label, and the present record uses that table only as a code-layer contact. The mathematical carrier is the codon cube $Q_6$, with distinctions for six-bit codon display, local tile face, singleton label corner, and a three-point label class. The internal reading uses coordinate, closure, spectrum, and relation data; the biological readout is only the standard codon-to-label table.

\paragraph{Observed relation.}
The finite relation $R$ has cardinality $13$. Its codons are
$$
\begin{aligned}
R=\{&\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\\
&\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UUA}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The same row audit reports that this relation is a punctured-cube pattern with face-vector $(13,16,4)$. Thus the code-layer evidence is not merely a count: it records a specific thirteen-codon subset together with a finite incidence profile. The row also records an observed $M$-tile size of $20$ in the amino-acid-class preserving null comparison.

\paragraph{Shape-significance audit.}
In the main Monte Carlo row for $h0.M.shape.significance$, the run used seed $42$ and $N=2000$ sampled null objects. To obtain these $2000$ distinct samples, the generator made $57377$ distinct-sample attempts and encountered $55377$ duplicate retries. The exact observed shape appeared once among the $2000$ null samples, giving $p_{\mathrm{exact}}=0.0005$. A relaxed test at Jaccard threshold $0.9$ found $25$ matches, giving $p_{J\geq 0.9}=0.0125$. In compact form, the finite row is
$$
\begin{aligned}
N&=2000, & \#\mathrm{exact}&=1, & p_{\mathrm{exact}}&=0.0005,\\
\#\{J\geq 0.9\}&=25, & p_{J\geq 0.9}&=0.0125, & \mathrm{seed}&=42.
\end{aligned}
$$
This supports the narrow statement that the window-six local tile is unusual under the sampled nulls used by the experiment. It does not show that the geometry is a law of biology; it shows that this particular finite code-layer readback has a low-frequency local shape in the tested ensemble.

\paragraph{Amino-acid-class preserving null.}
A separate null row preserved amino-acid class structure. It again used $N=2000$ and seed $42$. In that comparison, exact shape matches were $0$, so the exact-shape empirical value was $0.0$. The observed $M$ size was $20$, and the lower-tail count at or below that observed size was $7$, giving lower-tail value $0.0035$. The recorded values are
$$
\begin{aligned}
\#\mathrm{exact}_{\mathrm{aa\ class}}&=0, & p_{\mathrm{exact,aa}}&=0.0,\\
\#\mathrm{lower\ tail}\leq |M|_{\mathrm{obs}}&=7, & |M|_{\mathrm{obs}}&=20, & p_{\mathrm{lower\ tail}}&=0.0035.
\end{aligned}
$$
This strengthens the local shape observation because the comparison is not a wholly unconstrained shuffle: it keeps a biologically meaningful label-class constraint while still failing to reproduce the observed local tile except in the lower-tail sense just stated.

\paragraph{Spectral finite row.}
The spectrum row gives $\lambda_M=0.6752479308830213$, $\lambda_R=0.4758352843197002$, and $\mu=3.051487585298128$. The radial cubic check and the cubic polynomial check both have residual $-6.224354365258478\times 10^{-12}$, which is within numerical tolerance for the recorded root relation. Written as the finite audit line,
$$
\begin{aligned}
\lambda_M&=0.6752479308830213, & \lambda_R&=0.4758352843197002,\\
\mu&=3.051487585298128, & \mathrm{residual}&=-6.224354365258478\times 10^{-12}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The spectral row is used only as a numerical coordinate of the same finite pattern. It is not treated as a mechanistic derivation of translation or folding.

\paragraph{Leave-one evidence.}
The leave-one-code-out row evaluated $23$ tables with $63$ total targets. The baseline hit rate was $0.19821739130434785$. The observed hit rate above baseline was $0.9841269841269841$; the hit rate inside $M\setminus T$ was $0.9365079365079365$; with boundary included it was $0.9841269841269841$. By family, $5$ families were recorded: alternative yeast had hit rate $0.8333333333333334$, while bacterial plastid, candidate other, mitochondrial, and nuclear ciliate each had hit rate $1.0$. The leave-one-codon-out row reports recovery rate $1.0$, predictable count $8$, boundary count $5$, anchor count $0$, and high closure coverage $8$. These rows indicate that the local code-layer tile has predictive stability under the tested leave-one deletions, but the prediction is still a codon-label recovery statement.

\paragraph{Module accounting.}
The thirteen codons in $R$ are completely assigned to six modules: $\mathrm{agr}=\{\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG}\}$, $\mathrm{ar\_lys}=\{\mathrm{AAA}\}$, $\mathrm{cun}=\{\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU}\}$, $\mathrm{ile\_met}=\{\mathrm{AUA}\}$, $\mathrm{ssn}=\{\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UUA}\}$, and $\mathrm{stop\_trp}=\{\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UGA}\}$. The module audit records $R$ size $13$, assigned count $13$, no extra module codons, no missing codons from modules, and no unassigned codons. This gives a closed finite bookkeeping check for the local relation used in the shape-significance claim.

\paragraph{Scope limit.}
The contact used here is the curated standard-code table, whose observed fact is a codon-to-amino-acid-or-stop assignment. That contact can test code readback and local codon-tile label splitting. It cannot test translation realization, protein structure, physical admissibility, biological function, a universal mechanism, or any claim that DNA must obey the six-coordinate geometry as a biological necessity. Amino-acid label sequences are not folded structures, and a local codon-tile split is not a protein-function result. Any later assertion about translation, folding, admissibility, function, mechanism, or universality would require a separate empirical contact whose measurement actually reaches that layer.

\paragraph{Verdict.}
The finite rows support the claim at the code-read layer: the standard-code table yields a thirteen-codon punctured-cube relation with face-vector $(13,16,4)$, a statistically rare local shape under the sampled nulls, a compatible spectral coordinate, complete module assignment, and strong leave-one recovery behavior. The chapter therefore records a ready local-boundary result for $h0.M.shape.significance$, while keeping all higher biological interpretations outside the claim.
