\subsection{Window-Six Local Tile Significance for the Standard Code}
\label{subsec:bioreality-h0-m-shape-significance}

\paragraph{Finite object under test.}
The record concerns the standard-code codon table as a code-layer object.  The finite carrier is the six-coordinate codon cube $Q_6$, read as a binary display of the three nucleotide positions, with curated amino-acid and stop labels attached only through the standard-code table contact.  The tested claim is deliberately local: window-six coordinates expose a boundary-tile pattern in the codon-label table.  They do not, by themselves, assert translation, folding, physical admissibility, biological function, a molecular mechanism, or a universal biological law.

\paragraph{Exceptional residual set.}
The finite residual set $R$ has cardinality $13$ and is exactly
$$
\begin{aligned}
R = \{&\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\\
&\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UUA}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The same row set is reported as a punctured-cube shape with face data
$$
\begin{aligned}
(f_0,f_1,f_2)=(13,16,4).
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the observed object is not a vague enrichment of the codon table: it is a specific thirteen-codon subset, with a fixed edge-and-face count, inside the finite six-bit codon display.

\paragraph{Monte Carlo shape test.}
The primary significance check used seed $42$ and $N=2000$ distinct sampled shapes.  Producing those distinct samples required $57377$ sample attempts, of which $55377$ were duplicate retries.  In this finite comparison, the exact observed shape appeared once, and shapes with Jaccard overlap at least $0.9$ appeared $25$ times.  The empirical tail values are
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{\mathrm{exact}} &= 0.0005,\\
p_{\mathrm{Jaccard}\geq 0.9} &= 0.0125.
\end{aligned}
$$
The numerical reading is that the standard-code residual tile is uncommon under this finite sampling regime.  The result supports a local code-read pattern; it does not convert the coordinate geometry into a biological necessity.

\paragraph{Amino-acid-class preserving null.}
A second null model preserved amino-acid class structure.  Under this comparison, again with $N=2000$ and seed $42$, the observed local object had $|M|=20$.  There were no exact shape matches among the samples, so the reported exact-shape count was $0$ and the corresponding finite empirical value was $0.0$.  The lower-tail count at or below the observed $M$ size was $7$, giving
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{\mathrm{aa\ class\ exact}} &= 0.0,\\
p_{\mathrm{aa\ class\ lower\ tail}} &= 0.0035.
\end{aligned}
$$
This null check is important because it asks whether the phenomenon disappears after respecting label-class constraints.  It does not disappear in the finite rows supplied here; the observed object remains in a small lower tail.

The spectral row is still only a numerical descriptor of the code-layer geometry; it is not an observation of protein structure or molecular action.

\paragraph{Reality contact and local boundary.}
The only reality contact used here is the curated standard genetic-code table.  It supplies codon-to-label assignments at codon-label resolution.  It does not contain a translation experiment, a folding observation, an energetic admissibility test, a function assay, or a universality theorem.  Consequently, the supported statement is narrow: the standard code exhibits an unusual window-six local tile and a thirteen-codon residual boundary with strong finite-row support under the supplied null comparisons.  Any claim about translation realization, folded protein structure, physical feasibility, function, mechanism, or universality requires a separate contact of the appropriate kind before it can be stated as observed.
