\subsection{Window-six local codon tile significance}
\label{sec:bioreality-h0-m-shape-significance}

\paragraph{Finite object under test.}
The record studies the standard-code codon table through the six-coordinate codon cube $Q_6$.  A codon is treated here as a finite coordinate word, and the curated standard-code table is used only as the source of amino-acid or stop labels.  The relevant distinctions are the six-bit display, local tile faces, singleton label corners, and three-point label classes.  The biological contact is therefore a code-label contact: it says which codon receives which standard-code label, but it does not say that a molecular mechanism, a fold, a physical constraint, or a functional role has been observed.

$$
This is the concrete shape datum: the local boundary is not merely a verbal grouping of codons, but a finite subconfiguration with thirteen vertices, sixteen one-dimensional adjacencies, and four two-dimensional faces inside the window-six coordinate presentation.

\paragraph{Shape significance.}
The primary significance run for claim $h0.M.shape.significance$ used seed $42$ and $N=2000$ distinct sampled comparison rows.  Constructing those distinct samples required $57377$ attempts and produced $55377$ duplicate retries, so the null exploration was collision-heavy.  Against that sample, the observed shape had one exact match and twenty-five high-overlap matches at Jaccard threshold $0.9$:
$$
\begin{aligned}
N&=2000,\\
\#\{\hbox{exact shape matches}\}&=1,\\
\#\{\hbox{Jaccard}\geq 0.9\hbox{ matches}\}&=25,\\
p_{\mathrm{exact}}&=0.0005,\\
p_{\mathrm{Jaccard}\geq 0.9}&=0.0125.
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the finite row supports a narrow statement: within the stated coordinate and sampling protocol, the observed local codon-tile shape is rare under the sampled comparison distribution.  The row does not by itself identify a cause, a universal law, or an evolutionary mechanism.

\paragraph{Class-preserving null comparison.}
A second null row preserves amino-acid class information while testing the shape.  In that run, again with seed $42$ and $N=2000$, the observed $M$-size was $20$.  The exact-shape count was zero, while the lower-tail count at or below the observed $M$-size was seven:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\#\{\hbox{class-preserving exact matches}\}&=0,\\
\#\{\hbox{lower-tail samples}\leq |M|\}&=7,\\
|M|&=20,\\
p_{\mathrm{class\ exact}}&=0.0,\\
p_{\mathrm{class\ lower\ tail}}&=0.0035.
\end{aligned}
$$
This strengthens the local reading: the pattern is not explained away merely by preserving the broad label-class inventory in the reported null model.  The finite record gives the size of $M$ but not the element-by-element codon list for $M$, so the chapter cites $M$ by the available size and test statistics rather than by an omitted enumeration.

\paragraph{Local conclusion.}
The supported conclusion is deliberately small.  The window-six codon coordinate system exposes a rare local boundary-tile pattern in the curated standard-code label table; the evidence includes the exact thirteen-codon boundary row, the punctured-cube face-vector $(13,16,4)$, the shape-significance values $0.0005$ and $0.0125$, the class-preserving lower-tail value $0.0035$, the spectral values $\lambda_M=0.6752479308830213$, $\lambda_R=0.4758352843197002$, $\mu=3.051487585298128$, and the leave-one-out recovery rows.  The contact remains at the code-read layer.  No translation realization, folded structure, physical admissibility, biological function, mechanistic explanation, or universal biological law follows from these rows unless another independently specified contact supplies that higher-layer observation.
