\subsection{Window-Six Codon Tile Significance}
\label{subsec:bioreality-h0-m-shape-significance}

\paragraph{Claim scope.}
The claim concerns a finite code-read phenomenon in the standard genetic-code table.  Codons are treated as six-bit window coordinates in the codon cube $Q_6$, with curated codon labels used only as readback labels.  The tested object is not translation as a biochemical process and not a model of protein structure.  It is a local question about whether the observed label-shaped tile has unusually specific finite geometry when compared with randomized label-preserving or label-derived alternatives.  The external contact used here is the curated standard-code table: it supplies codon-to-amino-acid or codon-to-stop labels, and it supplies no observation of folding, physical admissibility, cellular function, mechanism, or universal biological necessity.

\paragraph{Finite carrier and observed boundary set.}
The verified finite boundary row identifies a set $R$ of thirteen codons:
$$
\begin{aligned}
R=\{&\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UUA}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The cardinality check reports $|R|=13$.  The same row verifies that this set realizes the punctured-cube boundary pattern with face-vector
$$
\begin{aligned}
(f_0,f_1,f_2)=(13,16,4).
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the finite geometry is not being inferred from biological mechanism; it is read from a concrete set of codon labels after the standard table has supplied the code-layer labeling.  The observed tile $M$ is represented in the finite rows by its size, with $|M|=20$ in the amino-acid-class-preserving null experiment; the row data supplied here do not include an explicit codon-by-codon list for $M$.

\paragraph{Randomization evidence.}
The shape-significance experiment used seed $42$ and $N=2000$ sampled alternatives.  It required $57377$ distinct-sample attempts and recorded $55377$ duplicate retries.  Among the $2000$ alternatives, exactly one matched the observed shape, giving
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{\mathrm{exact}}=0.0005.
\end{aligned}
$$
The high-overlap threshold also remained uncommon: $25$ alternatives had Jaccard overlap at least $0.9$, giving
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{J\geq 0.9}=0.0125.
\end{aligned}
$$
These numbers support a narrow finite statement: the standard-code label table induces a window-six local tile whose exact shape and near-shape are rare under the sampled null family.  They do not support promotion from codon labels to realized translation dynamics, protein conformation, organismal function, or lawlike biological inevitability.

\paragraph{Amino-acid-class-preserving null row.}
A second finite test preserves amino-acid class structure while resampling the relevant shape.  It used the same seed $42$ and $N=2000$.  The exact observed shape appeared zero times, so the reported exact-shape value is
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{\mathrm{class\ exact}}=0.0.
\end{aligned}
$$
The lower-tail count at or below the observed size $|M|=20$ was $7$, hence
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{\mathrm{class\ lower\ tail}}=0.0035.
\end{aligned}
$$
This is a stronger check against the worry that the tile is merely an artifact of preserving coarse amino-acid classes.  It remains, however, a code-table result: the evidence is about finite label geometry, not about the biochemical realization of those labels.

\paragraph{Spectral check.}
The spectrum row gives numerical invariants for the same finite configuration.  The reported values are
$$
\begin{aligned}
\lambda_M&=0.6752479308830213,\\
\lambda_R&=0.4758352843197002,\\
\mu&=3.051487585298128.
\end{aligned}
$$
The radial polynomial evaluation is
$$
\begin{aligned}
-6.224354365258478\times 10^{-12},
\end{aligned}
$$
which is recorded in the row both as the cubic-polynomial residual and the radial-polynomial value.  The residual is numerically near zero at the stated tolerance scale, so the spectral row supplies an additional finite consistency check for the codon-tile configuration.  The eigenvalue data are internal numerical witnesses for this finite graph-like object; they are not measurements of molecular energy, folding stability, or translation kinetics.

\paragraph{Leave-one-code-out behavior.}
The leave-one-code-out row evaluated $23$ code tables and $63$ total targets.  Its baseline hit rate was
$$
\begin{aligned}
0.19821739130434785,
\end{aligned}
$$
while the hit rate above baseline was
$$
\begin{aligned}
0.9841269841269841.
\end{aligned}
$$
The hit rate inside $M\setminus T$ was $0.9365079365079365$, and the hit rate inside $M\setminus T$ together with the boundary was $0.9841269841269841$.  Broken down by family, five families were recorded: alternative yeast has rate $0.8333333333333334$, while bacterial-plastid, candidate-other, mitochondrial, and nuclear-ciliate each have rate $1.0$.  These values say that the local codon-tile geometry has strong finite recovery behavior across the sampled code-table family.  They do not say that every organism implements the same mechanism or that a code-table comparison observes protein-level structure.

\paragraph{Leave-one-codon-out behavior and modules.}
The leave-one-codon-out row records anchor count $0$, boundary count $5$, closure coverage high $8$, predictable count $8$, and recovery rate $1.0$.  The module classification assigns all thirteen boundary codons and leaves no missing, extra, or unassigned codons.  The six modules are
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{agr}&=\{\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG}\},\\
\mathrm{ar\_lys}&=\{\mathrm{AAA}\},\\
\mathrm{cun}&=\{\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU}\},\\
\mathrm{ile\_met}&=\{\mathrm{AUA}\},\\
\mathrm{ssn}&=\{\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UUA}\},\\
\mathrm{stop\_trp}&=\{\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UGA}\}.

$$
The completeness row reports $R$-size $13$, assigned count $13$, no extra module codons, no missing codons from the modules, and no unassigned codons.  Each module has predictable-or-boundary status, so the finite module cover is complete for this observed boundary set.

\paragraph{Local conclusion.}
The finite rows jointly support a chapter-specific code-read conclusion.  In the standard-code table, the window-six codon coordinates expose a local boundary-tile pattern with $|R|=13$, face-vector $(13,16,4)$, rare exact-shape frequency $0.0005$ under the primary null, rare high-Jaccard frequency $0.0125$, and a class-preserving lower-tail value $0.0035$.  The recovery rows add cross-table and leave-one-codon stability at the code-label level.  The conclusion is therefore that the curated code labels instantiate a significant finite local tile in $Q_6$.  No translation process, folded structure, physical admissibility, biological function, universal mechanism, or organism-independent biological law follows from these rows unless an additional contact directly observes that higher layer.
