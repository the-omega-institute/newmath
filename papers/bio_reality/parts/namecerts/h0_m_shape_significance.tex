\paragraph{Finite object.}
This record treats the standard-code codon table as a finite code-read contact placed on the six-bit codon cube $Q_6$.  The cube coordinate is not used as a biological cause.  It is used as a finite display on which a local label pattern can be tested against curated codon-to-label assignments.  The relevant distinctions are the six-bit codon display, a local tile face, singleton label corners, and a three-point label class.  The observed readback is the standard-code label table: each codon is assigned an amino-acid or stop label by the curated table, and the geometry is then asked only whether it organizes that table in a locally detectable way.

\paragraph{Shape significance test.}
The primary significance row samples $N=2000$ distinct comparison shapes with seed $42$.  Obtaining those $2000$ distinct samples required $57377$ sample attempts and $55377$ duplicate retries, so the null space was repeatedly revisited before the accepted comparison set was formed.  Among the accepted comparisons there was exactly one exact shape match and $25$ matches with Jaccard score at least $0.9$.  The corresponding empirical probabilities are
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{\mathrm{exact}}&=0.0005,\\
p_{\mathrm{Jaccard}\geq 0.9}&=0.0125.
\end{aligned}
$$
Both reported significance fields repeat these values: the exact-shape significance value is $0.0005$, and the high-Jaccard significance value is $0.0125$.  The finite conclusion is therefore modest but sharp: within this sampling design, the observed local codon-tile shape is rare under the tested comparison distribution.

\paragraph{Local verdict.}
The supported claim is that window-six codon coordinates expose a statistically unusual local boundary-tile organization of the curated standard-code table.  The finite evidence includes the exact $R$ list, $|R|=13$, the punctured-cube face-vector $(13,16,4)$, empirical probabilities $0.0005$ and $0.0125$ for the primary shape test, amino-acid-class preserving null values $0.0$ and $0.0035$, the spectral values $\lambda_M=0.6752479308830213$, $\lambda_R=0.4758352843197002$, $\mu=3.051487585298128$, the $23$-table and $63$-target leave-one-code-out row, and the complete six-module partition of $R$.  These rows justify a code-layer reading of a local tile boundary.  They do not justify promotion to translation in a cell, folded protein structure, physical admissibility, biological function, a mechanism of code formation, or a universal biological law.  Any such higher-layer statement would require a separate contact measuring that layer directly.
