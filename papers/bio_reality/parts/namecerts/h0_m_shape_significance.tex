\subsection{Window-six codon tile shape significance}\label{sec:bioreality-h0-m-shape-significance}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Claim.} This record concerns the claim identifier \texttt{h0.M.shape.significance}.  The biological object is the standard-code codon table, read only at the code-label layer.  The finite carrier is the six-coordinate codon cube $Q_6$, with codons displayed as six-bit positions and then compared against a local tile face, a singleton label corner, and a three-point label class.  The observed readout is not a mechanism of translation; it is the curated assignment of codons to amino-acid or stop labels.  The question is whether the observed $M$-shape behaves like an accidental labeling pattern under finite randomization, or whether the window-six coordinates isolate a local boundary tile with measurable rarity.

\paragraph{Finite codon boundary.} The finite boundary set $R$ has cardinality $13$.  The verified codons are
$$
\begin{aligned}
R=\{&\texttt{AAA},\texttt{AGA},\texttt{AGG},\texttt{AUA},\texttt{CUA},\texttt{CUC},\texttt{CUG},\texttt{CUU},\\
&\texttt{UAA},\texttt{UAG},\texttt{UCA},\texttt{UGA},\texttt{UUA}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The punctured-cube check reports the same thirteen codons and the face-vector signature
$$
\begin{aligned}
(f_0,f_1,f_2)=(13,16,4).
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the boundary object used by the shape test is a concrete finite subcomplex of the codon cube, not an unbounded genomic or organism-level assertion.  The row also records an observed $M$ size of $20$ for the amino-acid-class-preserving null comparison, while the finite row supplied here does not include the membership list of $M$ itself.

\paragraph{Shape significance.} The principal randomization row used $N=2000$ samples with seed $42$.  Among these samples, there was exactly $1$ exact shape match, giving
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{\mathrm{exact}}=0.0005.
\end{aligned}
$$
For the high-overlap threshold, $25$ samples reached Jaccard similarity at least $0.9$, giving
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{J\geq 0.9}=0.0125.
\end{aligned}
$$
The distinct-sample bookkeeping is also finite: $57377$ distinct sample attempts were made and $55377$ duplicate retries were recorded.  These counts matter because the rarity statement is about the enumerated randomized comparison, not about an asymptotic population model.  The result supports the narrower statement that the observed local codon-label geometry is unusually shaped under the tested null, with exact-shape frequency $1/2000$ and high-Jaccard frequency $25/2000$.

\paragraph{Class-preserving null comparison.} A second null row preserved amino-acid label classes across $N=2000$ trials, again with seed $42$.  It found $0$ exact matches to the observed shape, so the recorded exact-shape value is
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{\mathrm{class\ exact}}=0.0.
\end{aligned}
$$
For the lower-tail comparison against the observed $M$ size $20$, the row counted $7$ samples at or below the observed size, giving
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{\mathrm{class\ lower}}=0.0035.
\end{aligned}
$$
This strengthens the finite claim in a specific way: even when the randomization respects amino-acid class structure, the observed local shape remains rare in the sampled comparison.  It does not, by itself, identify a biochemical cause.

\paragraph{Spectral check.} The shape packet also carries a numerical spectrum.  The recorded values are
$$
\begin{aligned}
\lambda_M&=0.6752479308830213,\\
\lambda_R&=0.4758352843197002,\\
\mu&=3.051487585298128.
\end{aligned}
$$
The radial cubic residual is $-6.224354365258478\times 10^{-12}$, reported both as the cubic-polynomial check and the radial-polynomial value.  The spectral-radius entry agrees with $\lambda_M=0.6752479308830213$.  This gives a numerical consistency check for the finite coordinate object: the boundary and the observed $M$-shape are accompanied by a stable local spectral signature, with residual at numerical roundoff scale.

\paragraph{Leave-one evidence.} The code-table perturbation rows test whether the local packet keeps predictive contact when one table or one codon is withheld.  In the leave-one-code-out row, $23$ tables and $63$ total targets were evaluated.  The baseline hit rate was $0.19821739130434785$, while the hit rate above baseline was $0.9841269841269841$.  The hit rate inside $M\setminus T$ was $0.9365079365079365$, and the hit rate inside $M\setminus T$ plus boundary was $0.9841269841269841$.  By family, the above-baseline rates were $0.8333333333333334$ for alternative yeast and $1.0$ for bacterial/plastid, candidate-other, mitochondrial, and nuclear-ciliate families.  In the leave-one-codon-out row, the anchor count was $0$, the boundary count was $5$, the predictable count was $8$, the high-closure-coverage count was $8$, and the recovery rate was $1.0$.  These rows are finite robustness checks for codon-label readback, not evidence for protein folding or organismal function.

The classification row reports $R$ size $13$, assigned count $13$, no extra module codons, no missing codons, and no unassigned codons.  Therefore every boundary codon in this finite packet is accounted for by the module partition used in the local shape analysis.

\paragraph{Scientific boundary.} The only contact used here is the curated standard genetic-code table, which assigns codons to amino-acid or stop labels.  That contact supports code-layer readback and local label-split checks.  It does not observe ribosomal realization, kinetic accessibility, folded structure, physical admissibility, biological function, evolutionary mechanism, or universality across life.  A codon-label sequence read from this table is still a label sequence; it is not a protein structure, a functional molecule, or a global biological law.  Any claim crossing from this local codon-tile result into translation, folding, admissibility, function, mechanism, or universality would require a separate experimental or curated contact at that higher layer.

\paragraph{Conclusion.} The finite rows support a ready code-read claim: in the six-coordinate codon cube, the standard-code label table determines a thirteen-codon boundary with face-vector $(13,16,4)$, complete module assignment, rare exact-shape frequency $0.0005$, high-Jaccard frequency $0.0125$, class-preserving lower-tail value $0.0035$, and a stable spectral signature with $\lambda_M=0.6752479308830213$.  The result is a local geometric and statistical statement about codon-label readback.  Its force is strongest precisely because its scope is limited: the packet isolates a finite boundary-tile phenomenon without promoting it into a claim about translation, folding, physical realization, function, mechanism, or universal biological necessity.
