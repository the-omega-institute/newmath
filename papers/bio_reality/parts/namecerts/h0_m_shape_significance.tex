\subsection{Window-six codon shape significance}
\label{subsec:bioreality-h0-m-shape-significance}

\paragraph{Finite display.}
The packet is a code-layer record for the standard genetic-code table, expressed on the six-coordinate codon cube $Q_6$.  Each codon is read as a six-bit window, so the carrier has the local geometry of a cube while the labels still come from a curated codon-to-amino-acid-or-stop table.  The finite distinction under test is not the whole biology of translation.  It is the narrower question whether the standard-code labels determine a local boundary-tile shape that is unusually hard to reproduce under finite randomizations respecting the declared comparison rule.

The distinguished boundary set $R$ has cardinality $13$ and is exactly
$$
\begin{aligned}
R=\{&\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\\
&\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UUA}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The same row certifies that this set has the punctured-cube face count
$$
\begin{aligned}
(f_0,f_1,f_2)=(13,16,4).
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the observation is not merely a count of exceptional codons.  It is a finite cubical shape with $13$ vertices, $16$ edges, and $4$ square faces inside the window-six coordinate system.

\paragraph{Shape-frequency test.}
The main finite-row run used seed $42$ and evaluated $N=2000$ distinct sampled tables after $57377$ sample attempts, with $55377$ duplicate retries.  Among these $2000$ distinct samples, exactly one matched the observed shape, while $25$ had Jaccard similarity at least $0.9$ to the observed shape.  The empirical frequencies are therefore
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{\mathrm{exact}}&=\frac{1}{2000}=0.0005,\\
p_{J\geq 0.9}&=\frac{25}{2000}=0.0125.
\end{aligned}
$$
These values are the finite significance content of claim $\mathrm{h0.M.shape.significance}$.  They say that the observed local shape is rare in the sampled null ensemble, and that even near-matches at the $0.9$ Jaccard threshold occur only $1.25\%$ of the time.  The row does not assert an asymptotic theorem or a biological necessity law; it records a reproducible finite comparison against the sampled coordinate-preserving background.

\paragraph{Amino-acid-class preserving null.}
A stricter control preserves amino-acid class information.  In that run, again with seed $42$ and $N=2000$, the observed set size was $|M|=20$.  The exact-shape count was $0$, giving
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{\mathrm{aa\mbox{-}class,exact}}=0.0.
\end{aligned}
$$
The lower-tail count for samples with size at most the observed $M$ size was $7$, hence
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{\mathrm{aa\mbox{-}class,lower\ tail}}=\frac{7}{2000}=0.0035.
\end{aligned}
$$
This second null row is important because it shows that the shape signal is not exhausted by a completely unconstrained relabeling comparison.  Even after preserving amino-acid-class information, the exact observed shape is absent from the $2000$ sampled controls, and only $0.35\%$ of controls fall in the lower tail by the recorded $M$-size comparison.  The finite rows supplied here give the size of $M$ but not a codon-by-codon listing of $M$.

\paragraph{Spectral readout.}
The local shape also carries a numerical spectral summary.  The recorded values are
$$
\begin{aligned}
\lambda_M&=0.6752479308830213,\\
\lambda_R&=0.4758352843197002,\\
\mu&=3.051487585298128.
\end{aligned}
$$
The corresponding cubic residual is $-6.224354365258478\times 10^{-12}$, reported both as the cubic-polynomial check and as the radial-polynomial value.  At this tolerance the spectral-radius entry is $0.6752479308830213$, matching $\lambda_M$.  This supplies an independent finite numeric signature for the same code-layer object: the cubical boundary pattern is accompanied by a stable spectral value, not only by a vertex list and a Monte Carlo frequency.

\paragraph{Leave-one-code and leave-one-codon probes.}
The leave-one-code-out row evaluated $23$ tables and $63$ total targets.  Its baseline hit rate was $0.19821739130434785$, while the recorded hit rate above baseline was $0.9841269841269841$.  The hit rate inside $M-T$ was $0.9365079365079365$, and the hit rate inside $M-T$ together with the boundary was again $0.9841269841269841$.  By family, the hit-rate-above-baseline values were $1.0$ for bacterial-plastid, mitochondrial, nuclear-ciliate, and candidate-other families, and $0.8333333333333334$ for alternative-yeast.  These numbers support a code-layer recovery pattern across finite table families, while still remaining tied to the label table and not to a molecular mechanism.

The leave-one-codon-out row gives a complementary local recovery check.  It reports $0$ anchors, $5$ boundary cases, $8$ predictable cases, $8$ high-coverage closure cases, and recovery rate $1.0$.  The module row partitions all $13$ boundary codons without omission or surplus: $R$ has size $13$, the assigned count is $13$, and the extra, missing, and unassigned lists are empty.  The six modules are
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{agr}&=\{\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG}\},\\
\mathrm{ar\_lys}&=\{\mathrm{AAA}\},\\
\mathrm{cun}&=\{\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU}\},\\
\mathrm{ile\_met}&=\{\mathrm{AUA}\},\\
\mathrm{ssn}&=\{\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UUA}\},\\
\mathrm{stop\_trp}&=\{\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UGA}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
Every module has either predictable or boundary status in the finite classification row.  This is the strongest local organization statement available from the supplied data: the thirteen-codon boundary is not an unstructured residue, but a complete module decomposition inside the observed code-table readout.

\paragraph{Reality contact and scope.}
The biological contact used here is the curated standard-code table: it assigns each codon to an amino-acid label or to stop.  That contact is sufficient for code-read comparison, local label splitting, cubical boundary enumeration, finite null sampling, and the recovery probes just stated.  It is not a contact with ribosomal dynamics, a contact with folded protein structure, a contact with energetic admissibility, or a contact with organism-level function.  Therefore no conclusion in this subsection promotes the window-six tile to translation, folding, physical realization, functional phenotype, universal mechanism, or global biological law.  Such claims would require separate observations at those layers.  The present record is confined to a finite, reproducible code-table phenomenon: the standard-code labels determine a rare local cubical boundary pattern with $|R|=13$, face vector $(13,16,4)$, exact-shape frequency $0.0005$, high-Jaccard frequency $0.0125$, amino-acid-class lower-tail frequency $0.0035$, spectral radius $0.6752479308830213$, and complete thirteen-codon module coverage.
