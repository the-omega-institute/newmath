\subsection{Window-six codon tile significance}\label{sec:bioreality-h0-m-shape-significance}

\paragraph{Claim.}
The packet tests a code-layer claim about the standard genetic-code table after codons are displayed as a six-coordinate cube $Q_6$.  The biological datum used here is only the curated assignment of the $64$ codons to amino-acid or stop labels.  The internal object is a local tile cut out by the window-six coordinates: it has coordinate data, a closure operation, a spectral summary, and a relation boundary.  The intended readback is therefore the standard-code label table, not translation dynamics and not protein structure.

The relation row $R$ has cardinality $13$.  In codon notation the recovered row is

\begin{aligned}

&\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UUA}\}.

Thus the finite object is not merely a count: it specifies thirteen named codons together with a small two-dimensional boundary profile inside the six-bit codon display.

\paragraph{Shape significance.}
The primary significance run used seed $42$ and sampled $N=2000$ comparison rows.  The run required $57377$ distinct-sample attempts and encountered $55377$ duplicate retries.  Among the $2000$ sampled rows, exactly one had the same shape as the observed row, giving
$$
\begin{aligned}
\#\{\mathrm{exact\ shape\ matches}\}&=1, & p_{\mathrm{exact}}&=0.0005.
\end{aligned}
$$
For a high-overlap comparison threshold, $25$ sampled rows reached Jaccard score at least $0.9$, giving
$$
\begin{aligned}
\#\{J\geq 0.9\}&=25, & p_{J\geq 0.9}&=0.0125.
\end{aligned}
$$
The finite-row evidence therefore says that the observed local tile is rare under this sampling procedure, both at exact shape and at high Jaccard overlap.  This is a code-table regularity claim only: the numbers support the local codon-label split tested by the packet, not a claim that biology must instantiate this geometry as a law.

\#\{\mathrm{class\ preserving\ exact\ matches}\}&=0, & p_{\mathrm{class,exact}}&=0.0.

\begin{aligned}
\#\{\mathrm{lower\ tail}\leq 20\}&=7, & p_{\mathrm{class,lower\ tail}}&=0.0035.

This strengthens the local reading: even when the randomized comparison keeps amino-acid class information, the observed finite tile remains unusual in the recorded sample.

\paragraph{Local biological interpretation.}
The curated standard-code table supplies a codon-to-label contact: it tells us which codons read as amino-acid labels or stop labels in the standard code.  It does not observe ribosomal realization, folding, admissible molecular physics, biological function, or a universal mechanism.  The tile significance, punctured-cube row, spectral diagnostics, and leave-one-out behavior therefore justify only a narrow statement: the standard-code label table contains a statistically unusual local window-six boundary pattern, and that pattern is recoverable in the recorded finite probes.

\paragraph{Boundary of the claim.}
No conclusion about translation, folded protein structure, physical admissibility, biological function, mechanism, or universality follows from this packet unless another contact supplies that higher-layer observation.  Amino-acid label readback is still label readback; it is not a structure measurement.  Cross-organism wording, CUN/UUR notation, or tRNA-Leu terminology would also require its own organism-matched contact before being used as evidence for structure or function.  Within the present data, the scientifically valid conclusion is confined to the code-read layer: a local codon-tile pattern in $Q_6$ is rare under the stated finite null rows and aligns with the curated standard-code table as a label-level phenomenon.
