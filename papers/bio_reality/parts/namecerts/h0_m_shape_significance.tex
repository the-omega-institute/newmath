\subsection{Window-Six Shape Significance for the Standard-Code Codon Tile}
\label{subsec:h0-m-shape-significance}

\paragraph{Finite carrier and measured contact.}
The record uses the six-bit codon cube $Q_6$ as its finite carrier. Each codon is read as a three-letter nucleotide word, while the coordinate view treats the word as a position in a six-coordinate display. The only biological contact used here is the curated standard genetic-code table: it assigns each codon to an amino-acid label or to a stop label. This contact is sufficient for code-layer label readback and for checking local tile splits. It is not a translation experiment, not a folding experiment, not a physical-admissibility measurement, and not a functional assay.

\paragraph{Distinguished boundary set.}
The finite row identifies the boundary set $R$ with exactly $13$ codons:
$$
\begin{aligned}
R=\{&\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\\
&\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UUA}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The cardinality check reports $|R|=13$. The same row reports that $R$ has the punctured-cube face data
$$
\begin{aligned}
f(R)=(13,16,4),
\end{aligned}
$$
so the observed boundary is not merely a list of labels; it carries a finite local face profile inside the codon cube. The module classification partitions all $13$ members without omissions or extras: $\mathrm{AGR}=\{\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG}\}$, $\mathrm{ar\_lys}=\{\mathrm{AAA}\}$, $\mathrm{CUN}=\{\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU}\}$, $\mathrm{ile\_met}=\{\mathrm{AUA}\}$, $\mathrm{SSN}=\{\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UUA}\}$, and $\mathrm{stop\_trp}=\{\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UGA}\}$. The assigned count is $13$, the recorded $R$ size is $13$, and both the missing-from-modules and extra-module-codons lists are empty.

\paragraph{Shape-significance experiment.}
The shape-significance run used seed $42$ and sampled $N=2000$ distinct comparison rows. Because duplicate samples were rejected, the run made $57{,}377$ distinct-sample attempts and recorded $55{,}377$ duplicate retries. The exact observed shape appeared once among the $2000$ accepted comparisons, giving
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{\mathrm{exact}}=0.0005.
\end{aligned}
$$
The high-overlap comparison counted $25$ rows with Jaccard similarity at least $0.9$, giving
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{J\geq 0.9}=0.0125.
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the local standard-code tile is finite and statistically sharp at the code-label layer: exact shape recurrence is rare in the sampled null population, while near-shape recurrence is still uncommon but less restrictive than exact equality.

\paragraph{Amino-acid-class preserving null.}
A second null keeps amino-acid class constraints in view. In that run, again with seed $42$ and $N=2000$, the observed $M$ size is $20$. The row reports $0$ exact matches, hence $p=0.0$ for exact-shape recurrence under this class-preserving null. The lower-tail count at or below the observed $M$ size is $7$, giving a lower-tail value $0.0035$. These values support the same conclusion at a stricter label-respecting comparison level: the observed local shape is not common under the recorded finite null.

\paragraph{Spectral coordinates.}
The spectral row gives $\lambda_M=0.6752479308830213$, $\lambda_R=0.4758352843197002$, and $\mu=3.051487585298128$. The radial cubic residual is recorded as $-6.224354365258478\times 10^{-12}$, and the same value is reported for the cubic-polynomial residual. This near-zero residual is a numerical certificate that the coordinate summary, the boundary set, and the radial relation agree to the tolerance used by the run. The number $\lambda_M$ is also reported as the spectral radius in tolerance, so the finite local tile has a stable numerical signature in the coordinate computation.

\paragraph{Leave-one readback checks.}
The leave-one-code-out row evaluated $23$ tables and $63$ total targets. Its baseline hit rate is $0.19821739130434785$. The hit rate above baseline is $0.9841269841269841$, the hit rate in $M\setminus T$ is $0.9365079365079365$, and the hit rate in $M\setminus T$ together with the boundary is $0.9841269841269841$. The family split keeps the same pattern visible: alternative yeast gives $0.8333333333333334$, while bacterial-plastid, candidate-other, mitochondrial, and nuclear-ciliate families each give $1.0$ for family hit rate above baseline. The leave-one-codon-out row reports anchor count $0$, boundary count $5$, closure coverage high $8$, predictable count $8$, and recovery rate $1.0$, with the threshold comparison also $1.0$. These rows say that the observed local codon tile is useful for code-layer recovery checks, especially around boundary and closure behavior.

\paragraph{Local conclusion.}
The finite evidence supports a precise code-read claim: window-six codon coordinates expose a statistically distinctive local tile in the standard-code label table, with $R$ forming a $13$-codon punctured-cube boundary and with exact-shape recurrence $0.0005$ in the sampled null. The conclusion remains local. It does not assert that living translation is produced by the coordinate geometry, that amino-acid labels determine folded structure, that the tile certifies physical admissibility, that protein function follows, that a mechanism has been measured, or that a universal biological law has been found. Any such promotion would require a separate contact at the relevant biological layer.
