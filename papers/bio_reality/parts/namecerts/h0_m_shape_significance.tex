\subsection{Window-six local codon shape and code-label contact}
\label{sec:h0-m-shape-significance}

\paragraph{Claim scope.}
The claim concerns a six-coordinate display of the standard-code codon table. Its mathematical object is the codon cube $Q_6$, read as a finite array of codon positions with local tile faces, singleton label corners, and three-point label classes. The biological contact used here is narrower: a curated standard-code table assigns each codon to an amino-acid or stop label. Thus the chapter studies code-label geometry, not a biochemical implementation of translation. The working conjecture is that the window-six coordinates expose a local boundary-tile pattern whose shape is unusually specific under finite randomization tests, while the amino-acid and stop labels remain supplied by the curated code table.

\paragraph{Observed boundary set.}
The boundary set $R$ has cardinality $13$. In the verified row, the codons are
$$\begin{aligned}
R=\{&AAA, AGA, AGG, AUA, CUA, CUC, CUG, CUU,\\
&UAA, UAG, UCA, UGA, UUA\}.
\end{aligned}$$
The same row records that this set has the punctured-cube face data
$$\begin{aligned}
(f_0,f_1,f_2)=(13,16,4).
\end{aligned}$$
This is the finite combinatorial footprint used by the chapter. It says that the observed code-label boundary occupies thirteen codon vertices, sixteen one-dimensional adjacencies, and four local two-dimensional faces in the induced codon-cube arrangement. The statement is intentionally local: the data identify a shaped region in the code-label table, not a law governing all biological codon usage or all genetic-code evolution.

\paragraph{Shape significance row.}
The direct shape test used $N=2000$ sampled comparison tables with seed $42$. The sampling process required $57377$ distinct-sample attempts and encountered $55377$ duplicate retries, so the finite run is visibly concentrated around a constrained comparison space rather than an unconstrained independent draw. Against this run, the exact observed shape appeared once, giving
$$\begin{aligned}
p_{\mathrm{exact}}=0.0005.
\end{aligned}$$
The relaxed high-overlap condition, measured by Jaccard threshold at least $0.9$, appeared $25$ times, giving
$$\begin{aligned}
p_{J\geq 0.9}=0.0125.
\end{aligned}$$
The claim supported by these numbers is that the window-six tile boundary is not a typical finite-row outcome under the sampled null. The claim is not that the coordinate system itself causes translation, protein folding, chemical admissibility, organismal function, or universality.

\paragraph{Amino-acid-class preserving null.}
A second null row preserves amino-acid class information. In that run, again with $N=2000$ and seed $42$, the observed $M$ size was $20$. The exact-shape count was $0$, giving
$$\begin{aligned}
p_{\mathrm{class,exact}}=0.0.
\end{aligned}$$
The lower-tail count at or below the observed $M$ size was $7$, giving
$$\begin{aligned}
p_{\mathrm{class,lower}}=0.0035.
\end{aligned}$$
This second row is important because it keeps more label structure than a fully unconstrained shuffle. The boundary pattern still lands in the small tail of the comparison distribution. The available row gives the size of $M$ but not an explicit codon list for $M$, so this chapter records the numeric support without inventing a membership table.

\paragraph{Spectral row.}
The spectral calculation supplies three finite scalars:
$$\begin{aligned}
\lambda_M&=0.6752479308830213,\\
\lambda_R&=0.4758352843197002,\\
\mu&=3.051487585298128.
\end{aligned}$$
The recorded cubic residual is $-6.224354365258478\times 10^{-12}$, also appearing as the radial-polynomial value. At the precision of the finite run, this residual is numerical zero. This does not by itself create a biological mechanism. It only says that the computed window-six geometry has a stable spectral signature matching the finite algebraic check used by the experiment.

\paragraph{Leave-one-code-out readback.}
The leave-one-code-out row evaluated $23$ tables and $63$ total targets. Its baseline hit rate was $0.19821739130434785$. The hit rate in $M\setminus T$ was $0.9365079365079365$, and the hit rate after including the boundary was $0.9841269841269841$. The same value, $0.9841269841269841$, is recorded as the rate above baseline. By family, the hit rates above baseline were
$$\begin{aligned}
&0.8333333333333334 &&\text{for alternative yeast},\\
&1.0 &&\text{for bacterial plastid},\\
&1.0 &&\text{for candidate other},\\
&1.0 &&\text{for mitochondrial},\\
&1.0 &&\text{for nuclear ciliate}.
\end{aligned}$$
These finite rows support code-layer predictability across the tested table family split. They do not show that a codon label sequence folds, performs a function, or is physically realized in any given cellular context.

\paragraph{Leave-one-codon-out and module classification.}
The leave-one-codon-out row records $0$ anchors, $5$ boundary cases, $8$ predictable cases, high closure coverage $8$, and recovery rate $1.0$. The module row assigns all $13$ elements of $R$ with no missing or extra codons: $R$ size $13$, assigned count $13$, empty extra-module list, empty missing-from-modules list, and empty unassigned list. The modules are
$$\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{agr}&=\{AGA,AGG\},\\
\mathrm{ar\_lys}&=\{AAA\},\\
\mathrm{cun}&=\{CUA,CUC,CUG,CUU\},\\
\mathrm{ile\_met}&=\{AUA\},\\
\mathrm{ssn}&=\{UCA,UUA\},\\
\mathrm{stop\_trp}&=\{UAA,UAG,UGA\}.
\end{aligned}$$
Every module therefore has either predictable or boundary status in the finite classification. This strengthens the local code-read account because the thirteen boundary codons are not left as a loose residue: they are completely partitioned into named finite modules.

\paragraph{Contact and limitation.}
The reality contact for the chapter is the curated standard genetic-code table. It verifies that codons have amino-acid or stop labels at the code-read layer. It does not observe ribosomal translation, tertiary or quaternary folded structure, thermodynamic admissibility, biological function, organism-level necessity, or a universal mechanism. Consequently, the supported conclusion is deliberately bounded: the window-six codon coordinates expose an unusually sharp local boundary shape in the standard-code label table, with exact-shape probability $0.0005$, high-Jaccard probability $0.0125$, amino-acid-class lower-tail probability $0.0035$, a thirteen-codon punctured-cube boundary, and strong leave-one-out code-label recovery. Any claim beyond code-label readback requires a separate empirical contact designed for that higher layer.
