\subsection{Window-Six Local Tile Significance for the Standard-Code Table}
\label{sec:bior-h0-m-shape-significance}

\paragraph{Object and coordinate display.}
This packet studies the standard-code codon table at the code-read layer. The carrier is the six-coordinate codon cube $Q_6$, where a codon is viewed as a six-bit display rather than as a biochemical mechanism. The finite distinctions used here are a local tile face, a singleton label corner, and a three-point label class. The internal bookkeeping is coordinate, closure, spectrum, and relation data, while the observed labels are taken from a curated standard-code table assigning codons to amino-acid or stop labels. The contact therefore tests codon-label readback and local split behavior only.

\paragraph{Boundary relation.}
The relation row $R$ contains exactly thirteen codons. In lexicographic display order the observed set is
$$
\begin{aligned}
R=\{&AAA, AGA, AGG, AUA, CUA, CUC, CUG, CUU,\\
&UAA, UAG, UCA, UGA, UUA\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The cardinality check records $|R|=13$, and the same row is also certified as the punctured-cube relation with face-vector
$$
\begin{aligned}
(f_0,f_1,f_2)=(13,16,4).
\end{aligned}
$$
This is the finite local geometry being tested. It is not a statement that DNA, translation, or protein realization is forced by the cube geometry.

\paragraph{Shape significance.}
The observed $M$-shape row was compared against $N=2000$ sampled alternatives with seed $42$. The run needed $57377$ distinct-sample attempts and rejected $55377$ duplicate retries before completing the comparison set. Exactly one sampled alternative matched the observed shape, and $25$ alternatives had Jaccard overlap at least $0.9$. Thus the reported tail probabilities are
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{\mathrm{exact}}&=0.0005,\\
p_{J\geq 0.9}&=0.0125.
\end{aligned}
$$
Both figures are small enough to mark the observed local shape as statistically notable under this finite sampling protocol. The result says that the local shape is uncommon among the sampled alternatives; it does not turn the tile into a biological law.

\paragraph{Amino-acid-class preserving null.}
A second null model preserves amino-acid class structure. In that comparison, again with $N=2000$ and seed $42$, the observed $M$ size is $20$. The exact-shape count is $0$, giving an exact-shape reported value $0.0$, while $7$ sampled alternatives fall in the lower tail at or below the observed $M$ size. The lower-tail probability is therefore
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{\mathrm{lower\ tail}}=0.0035.
\end{aligned}
$$
This null row supports the same reading: the local codon-label shape is not behaving like a routine class-preserving random draw, but the evidence remains confined to codon labels and their finite local arrangement.

These rows show that the finite local split has strong code-read recovery behavior across the evaluated table family; they do not show a universal mechanism of translation.

\paragraph{Leave-one-codon-out behavior.}
The leave-one-codon-out row records no anchors, $5$ boundary cases, $8$ high-coverage closure cases, and $8$ predictable cases. The module classification is complete with $R_{\mathrm{size}}=13$ and assigned count $13$. This is a code-layer closure and boundary classification, not a structural or functional annotation of the proteins that might be read from the labels.

\paragraph{Local verdict and limits.}
The finite rows jointly support a precise code-read conclusion. The standard-code label table contains a thirteen-codon punctured-cube relation, a statistically uncommon $M$-shape under two finite null protocols, a stable spectral row, strong leave-one-code-out recovery, and complete module classification for the relation codons. The admissible claim is therefore that window-six codon coordinates expose a significant local boundary-tile pattern in the curated standard-code label table. Nothing in this packet establishes translation realization, folded structure, physical admissibility, biological function, a mechanism, or universality. Any such higher-layer statement would need a separate observation source designed for that layer.
