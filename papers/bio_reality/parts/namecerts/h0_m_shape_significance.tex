\subsection{Window-six codon tile significance at the code-read layer}
\label{subsec:h0-m-shape-significance}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite object and observational contact.}
The record studied here is a window-six representation of the standard genetic-code table.  The carrier is the six-coordinate codon cube $Q_6$, used only as a finite coordinate display for codon labels.  Its biological contact is the curated standard-code table, namely the assignment of each codon to an amino-acid label or stop label.  The contact therefore tests code-label readback and local tile separation.  It does not observe translation as a cellular process, folded structure, physical admissibility, function, mechanism, or universality.  Any assertion crossing those layers would require an additional observation of the relevant layer.

\paragraph{Boundary set.}
The verified boundary set $R$ has cardinality $13$.  The listed codons are
$$
\begin{aligned}
R=\{&\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\\
&\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UUA}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The same finite row verifies that this set is a punctured-cube tile with face-vector data
$$
\begin{aligned}
(f_0,f_1,f_2)=(13,16,4).
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the observed object is not merely a list of exceptional codons; it is a finite subcomplex of the window-six cube with thirteen vertices, sixteen one-dimensional incidences, and four square faces.  The geometric assertion is local: it concerns this tile and its label boundary, not a law governing biological realization.

\paragraph{Shape significance against finite null sampling.}
For the primary shape test, $N=2000$ distinct null samples were used with seed $42$.  Constructing those samples required $57377$ distinct-sample attempts and produced $55377$ duplicate retries, so the sampled null space was already highly collision-prone under the imposed constraints.  Among the $2000$ distinct samples, exactly one had the observed shape.  The exact-shape tail estimate is therefore
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{\mathrm{exact}}=0.0005.
\end{aligned}
$$
A relaxed comparison using Jaccard threshold at least $0.9$ found $25$ matches, giving
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{J\geq 0.9}=0.0125.
\end{aligned}
$$
These two finite values support the claim that the observed local tile is unusually specific among the sampled alternatives.  They do not by themselves identify a causal biological process; they establish significance for a codon-label shape under the stated finite sampling design.

\paragraph{Amino-acid-class preserving null check.}
A second null row preserves amino-acid class constraints.  In this run $N=2000$ and the observed comparison size was $20$.  The exact observed shape appeared $0$ times, so the finite empirical exact-shape value is
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{\mathrm{aa\ class\ exact}}=0.0.
\end{aligned}
$$
The lower-tail count at or below the observed $M$ size was $7$, giving
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{\mathrm{aa\ class\ lower\ tail}}=0.0035.
\end{aligned}
$$
This strengthens the code-layer reading: even after preserving amino-acid class information, the observed local organization is rare in the sampled null collection.  The row reports the size of the relevant $M$ object as $20$, but the finite data supplied here does not include an explicit codon-by-codon enumeration of $M$.

\paragraph{Local conclusion.}
The finite rows support the following bounded conclusion.  In the six-coordinate codon cube, the curated standard-code labels determine a thirteen-codon boundary tile with face vector $(13,16,4)$, rare exact shape under the primary sampled null at $p=0.0005$, high-Jaccard rarity at $p=0.0125$, amino-acid-class preserving exact count $0$ out of $2000$, amino-acid-class lower-tail value $0.0035$, coherent spectral values with near-zero polynomial residual, strong leave-one-code-out and leave-one-codon-out recovery, and a complete six-module classification of the boundary.  The conclusion remains deliberately narrow: the packet supports a significant local code-label shape.  It does not authorize claims about translation realization, folded protein structure, physical admissibility, biological function, mechanism, or universality unless a separate contact directly observes that higher layer.
