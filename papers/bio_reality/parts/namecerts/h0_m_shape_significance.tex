\subsection{Window-six shape significance for the standard-code boundary tile}
\label{sec:h0-m-shape-significance}

\paragraph{Claim surface.}
The packet records a code-layer observation about the standard genetic-code table under a six-coordinate codon display.  The biological object is the curated standard-code codon-to-label table, and the claimed layer is only label readback.  The finite carrier is the codon cube $Q_6$, with local distinctions coming from the six-bit codon display, a local tile face, singleton label corners, and three-point label classes.  The row does not assert that the displayed geometry is a law of biology.  It says that, after the standard-code labels are read against this finite coordinate system, a distinguished local boundary set has a sharply constrained shape under the sampled null models.

\paragraph{Reality contact.}
The only external contact used here is the curated standard-code table: each codon is assigned an amino-acid or stop label.  That contact is sufficient for code-layer readback and local split tests.  It is not a contact with translation dynamics, folded structure, physical admissibility, biological function, a mechanism, or a universal biological principle.  Any move across those boundaries would require another observation source, separate from the codon-label table.

\paragraph{Shape significance.}
The main significance row used $N=2000$ distinct samples with seed $42$.  Because the sampling avoided duplicate shapes, it required $57377$ distinct-sample attempts and rejected $55377$ duplicate retries.  Among the $2000$ retained samples, the exact observed shape occurred once, and $25$ samples had Jaccard overlap at least $0.9$ with the observed shape.  The recorded empirical rates are
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{\mathrm{exact}}&=0.0005,\\
p_{J\ge 0.9}&=0.0125.
\end{aligned}
$$
The row therefore treats the exact finite shape as rare under this null, while the high-overlap neighborhood is still small but less extreme.  The finite evidence is a sampled shape-comparison result, not a mechanistic explanation.

The finite rows support the following narrow conclusion.  In the six-coordinate codon display, the curated standard-code table yields a thirteen-codon punctured boundary set with face-vector $(13,16,4)$, a complete six-module classification, rare sampled shape statistics with $p_{\mathrm{exact}}=0.0005$ and $p_{J\ge 0.9}=0.0125$, a class-preserving lower-tail value $0.0035$, and strong leave-one-out recovery behavior.
