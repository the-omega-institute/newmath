\subsection{Window-six codon tile significance}
\label{sec:h0-m-shape-significance}

\paragraph{Finite codon display.}
The record studies the standard genetic-code table only at the codon-label level.  The carrier is the six-coordinate codon cube $Q_6$, where a codon is read as a six-bit window and the observed labels are the curated amino-acid or stop assignments of the standard code.  The relevant local object is a codon tile with a singleton corner and a three-point label class.  Its mathematical content is a finite coordinate pattern, a closure operation on the selected codons, a spectral summary, and a relation separating the locally exposed boundary from the rest of the cube.  The empirical contact is therefore limited to label readback: it tests whether the standard-code labels induce the reported local tile, not whether translation, molecular folding, physical feasibility, biological function, mechanism, or universality has been observed.

\paragraph{Boundary set.}
The distinguished residual set $R$ has cardinality $13$.  In codon notation the verified row is
$$
\begin{aligned}
R=\{&\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\\
&\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UUA}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The same row identifies $R$ as a punctured-cube shaped boundary with face-vector
$$
\begin{aligned}
(f_0,f_1,f_2)=(13,16,4).
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the claim is not that all codons are geometrically forced by biology.  It is the narrower assertion that the standard-code label table, when placed on the six-bit codon cube, singles out this thirteen-codon boundary pattern as the readback-relevant residual component.  The complementary local tile $M$ is reported with observed size $20$ in the class-preserving null experiment; the finite row supplied here does not list the individual codons of $M$, so the chapter uses the available size and shape statistics rather than inventing an element list.

\paragraph{Shape test.}
For the primary shape-significance experiment, the run used seed $42$ and sampled $N=2000$ distinct randomized candidates.  Obtaining those candidates required $57377$ distinct-sample attempts and incurred $55377$ duplicate retries.  The exact shape matched once, giving
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{\mathrm{exact}}=\frac{1}{2000}=0.0005.
\end{aligned}
$$
The high-overlap threshold counted $25$ samples with Jaccard score at least $0.9$, so
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{J\geq 0.9}=\frac{25}{2000}=0.0125.
\end{aligned}
$$
These finite counts support a local significance statement: the observed codon-label tile is rare under the sampled comparison model.  They do not promote the tile into an explanation of protein realization, folding, physical admissibility, function, or a universal genetic-code principle.

\paragraph{Class-preserving null.}
A second null model preserved amino-acid class structure.  With the same seed $42$ and $N=2000$, it found $0$ exact matches to the observed shape.  It also recorded $7$ samples in the lower tail at or below the observed $M$-size of $20$.  The corresponding reported values are
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{\mathrm{class,exact}}&=0.0,\\
p_{\mathrm{class,lower\ tail}}&=\frac{7}{2000}=0.0035.
\end{aligned}
$$
The null comparison is useful because it asks whether the pattern remains unusual after retaining coarse label-class information.  The answer supplied by the row is yes at the finite-sampling resolution of the run: exact recurrence was absent, and the lower-tail event occurred only seven times.

\paragraph{Spectral summary.}
The spectral row gives three scalars for the same local geometry:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\lambda_M&=0.6752479308830213,\\
\lambda_R&=0.4758352843197002,\\
\mu&=3.051487585298128.
\end{aligned}
$$
The recorded cubic residual is $-6.224354365258478\times 10^{-12}$, the same value reported for the radial polynomial evaluation.  The spectral radius in tolerance is $0.6752479308830213$, matching $\lambda_M$ to the displayed precision.  This supplies a numerical consistency check for the local tile spectrum, but it remains a coordinate-level invariant of the codon-label display.  It is not a thermodynamic, structural, or evolutionary measurement.

\paragraph{Leave-one-code-out check.}
The leave-one-code-out row evaluated $23$ code tables and $63$ total targets.  Its baseline hit rate was $0.19821739130434785$.  The reported hit rate above baseline was $0.9841269841269841$, the hit rate inside $M-T$ was $0.9365079365079365$, and the rate inside $M-T$ plus the boundary was $0.9841269841269841$.  By family, the five reported groups had hit rates above baseline
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{alternative\ yeast}&:0.8333333333333334,\\
\mathrm{bacterial\ plastid}&:1.0,\\
\mathrm{candidate\ other}&:1.0,\\
\mathrm{mitochondrial}&:1.0,\\
\mathrm{nuclear\ ciliate}&:1.0.
\end{aligned}
$$
This supports the narrower reading that the local codon tile has predictive contact with code-table label changes in the tested collection.  The evidence still stops at code-layer labels.

\paragraph{Leave-one-codon-out check.}
The leave-one-codon-out row reports anchor count $0$, boundary count $5$, predictable count $8$, high closure coverage $8$, and recovery rate $1.0$.  Its module classification assigns all $13$ residual codons, with $R\_\mathrm{size}=13$, assigned count $13$, no extra module codons, no missing residual codons, and no unassigned codons.  The modules are
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{agr}&=\{\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG}\},\\
\mathrm{ar\_lys}&=\{\mathrm{AAA}\},\\
\mathrm{cun}&=\{\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU}\},\\
\mathrm{ile\_met}&=\{\mathrm{AUA}\},\\
\mathrm{ssn}&=\{\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UUA}\},\\
\mathrm{stop\_trp}&=\{\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UGA}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
Every module has either predictable or boundary status in the reported classification.  This makes the packet internally coherent as a finite codon-label study: the residual set is fully partitioned, the leave-one-codon probe recovers the held-out cases at rate $1.0$, and the code-table comparison shows strong enrichment over the reported baseline.

\paragraph{Local scope.}
The conclusion is deliberately modest.  Window-six coordinates expose a rare local boundary-tile pattern in the standard-code label table, with exact-shape probability $0.0005$ under the primary sample and no exact matches under the amino-acid-class-preserving sample of size $2000$.  The punctured residual cube, the spectral values, the leave-one-code-out rates, and the complete thirteen-codon module partition jointly support a code-read claim about codon labels.  No statement in this chapter establishes translation dynamics, folded protein structure, physical admissibility, realized biological function, a mechanism of code origin, or a law valid across all biological contexts.  Any such stronger claim would require a separate contact measuring the relevant layer rather than an inference from the codon-label table alone.
