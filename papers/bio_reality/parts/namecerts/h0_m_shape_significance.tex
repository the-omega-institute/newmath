\subsection{Window-six local shape significance for the standard-code table}\label{subsec:h0-m-shape-significance}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite codon display.}
The record fixes a six-coordinate codon display in which the standard-code table is read only as a codon-to-label assignment.  The carrier is the finite codon cube $Q_6$, and the visible distinctions are a six-bit codon coordinate, local tile faces, singleton label corners, and three-point label classes.  The biological contact is deliberately narrow: a curated standard-code table assigns each codon to an amino-acid or stop label.  This contact is enough to test code-layer label readback and local codon-tile splits, but it is not a measurement of translation dynamics, folded structure, physical admissibility, molecular function, a mechanism, or a universal biological law.

\paragraph{Boundary set.}
The distinguished boundary row $R$ has cardinality $13$.  The observed membership is
$$
\begin{aligned}
R=\{&\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\\
&\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UUA}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The same finite row is certified as a punctured-cube shape with face-vector
$$
\begin{aligned}
f(R)=(13,16,4).
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the local object is not merely a counted subset of codons: it has a recorded incidence profile with $13$ vertices, $16$ one-dimensional incidences, and $4$ two-dimensional tile faces.  This is the geometric datum used by the code-read packet; no stronger biological layer is inferred from it.

\paragraph{Observed shape statistic.}
The shape-significance run used seed $42$ and $N=2000$ samples.  It required $57377$ distinct-sample attempts, of which $55377$ were duplicate retries, so the finite sampler was operating in a heavily constrained discrete space.  The exact observed shape occurred once among the $2000$ sampled comparisons, giving
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{\mathrm{exact}}=0.0005.
\end{aligned}
$$
The high-overlap threshold also remained uncommon: $25$ sampled rows had Jaccard overlap at least $0.9$, giving
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{J\geq 0.9}=0.0125.
\end{aligned}
$$
These numbers support a code-layer claim that the window-six local tile is statistically non-generic under the recorded finite sampling procedure.  They do not identify a causal mechanism, and they do not convert the local shape into a global law of biology.

\paragraph{Amino-acid-class preserving null.}
A second null model preserves amino-acid class data while testing the same shape.  In that run the observed comparison size for $M$ was $20$, the exact shape count was $0$ over $N=2000$, and the lower-tail count at or below the observed $M$ size was $7$.  The resulting finite-row probabilities are
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{\mathrm{aa\ class\ exact}}&=0.0,\\
p_{\mathrm{aa\ class\ lower\ tail}}&=0.0035.
\end{aligned}
$$
The membership list for $M$ is not present in the finite row supplied here, so the claim is phrased in terms of the recorded $M$ size and the certified null counts rather than as a displayed codon set.  The relevant conclusion is still local: the standard-code labels give a sharp code-read shape relative to this class-preserving null, not evidence of folding or function.

\paragraph{Leave-one tests.}
The leave-one-code-out run evaluated $23$ code tables and $63$ total targets.  Its baseline hit rate was $0.19821739130434785$, while the hit rate above baseline was $0.9841269841269841$.  The hit rate inside $M-T$ was $0.9365079365079365$, and the hit rate inside $M-T$ plus the boundary was again $0.9841269841269841$.  Family-level rows remained high: alternative yeast had rate $0.8333333333333334$, while bacterial plastid, candidate other, mitochondrial, and nuclear ciliate families each had rate $1.0$.  The leave-one-codon-out run recorded $0$ anchors, $5$ boundary cases, $8$ high-coverage closure cases, $8$ predictable cases, and recovery rate $1.0$.  These tests show that the local code-read shape is stable under the recorded omissions; they do not show that a ribosome, tRNA pool, folding environment, or organism-level function must realize the same pattern.

Any assertion about translation realization, folded structure, structural order, physical admissibility, biological function, mechanism, or universality would require a separate empirical contact beyond the curated standard-code table used here.
