\subsection{Window-six codon tiles and the standard-code label split}\label{subsec:bioreality-h0-m-shape-significance}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite claim.}
This record concerns a window-six presentation of the standard genetic code at the code-read layer.  The finite carrier is the codon cube $Q_6$, read as a six-bit display of the three nucleotide positions.  The observed biological contact is only the curated standard-code assignment of codons to amino-acid or stop labels.  Within that limited contact, the claim is that the window-six coordinates expose a local boundary-tile pattern whose finite shape is unusual under the sampled null models.  The claim is not that the geometry produces translation, protein structure, admissible chemistry, organismal function, or a universal biological mechanism.

\paragraph{Observed punctured region.}
The distinguished residual set $R$ has cardinality $13$.  The verified row gives
$$
\begin{aligned}
R=\{&AAA, AGA, AGG, AUA, CUA, CUC, CUG, CUU,\\
&UAA, UAG, UCA, UGA, UUA\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The same computation identifies this set as a punctured-cube pattern with $f$-vector $(13,16,4)$.  Thus the finite object is not merely a list of exceptional codons: it has thirteen vertices, sixteen one-dimensional adjacencies, and four two-dimensional faces in the induced local cubical view.  This is the concrete tile whose boundary behavior is being compared against code-label randomizations and leave-out tests.

\paragraph{Shape significance.}
For the main shape comparison, the verified run used seed $42$ and $N=2000$ sampled distinct tables.  Producing those samples required $57377$ distinct-sample attempts and $55377$ duplicate retries, which is important because the null ensemble is finite and collision-heavy.  Exactly one sampled table matched the observed shape, while twenty-five sampled tables had Jaccard similarity at least $0.9$.  The finite-run estimates are
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{\mathrm{exact}}&=0.0005,\\
p_{\mathrm{Jaccard}\ge 0.9}&=0.0125.
\end{aligned}
$$
The exact-shape row and the high-Jaccard row therefore both mark the observed local tile as rare in the sampled comparison class.  This rarity is a code-layer observation: it says that the codon-label table is unusually aligned with the window-six local shape, not that the local geometry is a sufficient biological law.

\paragraph{Class-preserving null.}
A second null model preserves amino-acid class information.  In that row the observed $M$-size is $20$, again with seed $42$ and $N=2000$.  The exact class-preserving shape count is $0$, so the recorded exact-shape value is $p=0.0$ at this finite resolution.  The lower-tail count at or below the observed $M$-size is $7$, giving
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{\mathrm{class\ exact}}&=0.0,\\
p_{\mathrm{lower\ tail}}&=0.0035.
\end{aligned}
$$
This strengthens the code-read interpretation: even when the null keeps coarse label classes fixed, the observed local shape remains in a small tail of the sampled population.  The result is still a statement about the curated label table and the induced finite geometry, not about peptide folding or biochemical realizability.

\paragraph{Spectral check.}
The same finite pattern has a numerical spectral signature.  The verified spectral row reports
$$
\begin{aligned}
\lambda_M&=0.6752479308830213,\\
\lambda_R&=0.4758352843197002,\\
\mu&=3.051487585298128.
\end{aligned}
$$
The recorded cubic residual is $-6.224354365258478\times 10^{-12}$, identical to the radial-polynomial residual in the finite-row report up to the displayed precision.  The spectral-radius tolerance row records $0.6752479308830213$.  These values give a second, non-enumerative read on the same local shape: the tile is accompanied by a stable numerical profile, but that profile is still attached only to the finite codon-label graph supplied by the standard-code contact.

\paragraph{Leave-one-code-out behavior.}
The leave-one-code-out test evaluated $23$ tables and $63$ total targets.  The baseline hit rate was $0.19821739130434785$.  The hit rate above baseline was $0.9841269841269841$, the hit rate inside $M\setminus T$ was $0.9365079365079365$, and the hit rate inside $M\setminus T$ plus boundary was $0.9841269841269841$.  By family, five families were recorded: alternative yeast has hit rate $0.8333333333333334$, while bacterial-plastid, candidate-other, mitochondrial, and nuclear-ciliate each have hit rate $1.0$.  These rows show that the local tile is predictive for many standard-code-near label changes, but the prediction target is still a codon-label placement target.  It is not a claim that the inferred label is translated in vivo, folded into a stable protein, or assigned a biological function.

\paragraph{Leave-one-codon-out behavior.}
The leave-one-codon-out row records $0$ anchors, $5$ boundary cases, $8$ high-coverage closure cases, and $8$ predictable cases.  The recovery rate is $1.0$, and the above-threshold recovery rate is also $1.0$.  The module-classification row accounts for every member of $R$ with no missing or extra module codons: $R$-size is $13$, assigned count is $13$, extra module codons are empty, missing-from-modules is empty, and unassigned codons is empty.  The recorded modules are
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{agr}&=\{AGA,AGG\},\\
\mathrm{ar\_lys}&=\{AAA\},\\
\mathrm{cun}&=\{CUA,CUC,CUG,CUU\},\\
\mathrm{ile\_met}&=\{AUA\},\\
\mathrm{ssn}&=\{UCA,UUA\},\\
\mathrm{stop\_trp}&=\{UAA,UAG,UGA\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus every codon in the residual punctured region is placed in a named finite module, and each module has either predictable or boundary behavior in the leave-one-codon-out report.

\paragraph{Scope of the contact.}
The only biological contact used here is the curated standard-code table, understood as a codon-to-label observation.  It can test whether a local codon tile agrees with code-read labels and whether a boundary split is present in the finite table.  It cannot test translation realization, folded structure, physical admissibility, function, mechanism, or universality.  Amino-acid label readback therefore remains a label-level statement.  Any future claim about protein structure, organism-specific translation, tRNA-copy mechanism, molecular admissibility, or biological function would need an additional contact of the appropriate kind and cannot be obtained from this codon-tile row alone.

\paragraph{Local conclusion.}
The finite rows support a narrow conclusion.  In $Q_6$, the standard-code label table determines a thirteen-codon punctured region with $f$-vector $(13,16,4)$, a rare exact shape under the sampled shape null with $p=0.0005$, a rare high-overlap tail with $p=0.0125$, a class-preserving lower-tail value $0.0035$, and a stable spectral profile with $\lambda_M=0.6752479308830213$ and $\lambda_R=0.4758352843197002$.  The leave-out rows further show strong code-label recovery across $23$ tables, $63$ targets, and a complete six-module classification of the thirteen residual codons.  The result is therefore ready as a finite code-read record, with its biological reach explicitly confined to the curated codon-label table.
