\paragraph{Finite coordinate object.}
This record concerns the standard-code codon table only at the label-readback layer.  The finite carrier is the six-coordinate codon cube $Q_6$, interpreted as a binary window over the three displayed nucleotide positions.  The observed finite packet separates a singleton label corner, a three-point label class, and a local tile face inside the window-six display.  The codon labels are supplied by the curated standard-code table: the table tells which codon carries which amino-acid or stop label, but it does not by itself describe translation dynamics, folded structure, energetic admissibility, phenotype, or function.

The distinguished residual set $R$ has cardinality $13$.  Its listed codons are
$$

&\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UUA}\}.

$$
\begin{aligned}

Thus the local object is not merely a count of codons.  It is a small cubical subconfiguration with thirteen vertices, sixteen one-dimensional adjacencies, and four two-dimensional faces.  The finite-row data supplied here do not give the full membership list of the complementary observed set $M$; they do give its observed size, $|M|=20$, and its randomized shape statistics.

\paragraph{Randomized shape test.}
The primary significance row uses $N=2000$ distinct sampled comparison shapes with seed $42$.  To obtain those distinct samples the run made $57377$ attempts and rejected $55377$ duplicates, so the finite test was sampling a highly repetitive finite search space rather than drawing independent unconstrained shapes from an unlimited population.  Within the accepted sample, the exact observed shape occurred once:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\#\{\text{exact shape matches}\}&=1,\\
p_{\mathrm{exact}}&=0.0005.
\end{aligned}
$$
A looser comparison using Jaccard overlap at least $0.9$ found $25$ matches:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\#\{J\geq 0.9\}&=25,\\
p_{J\geq 0.9}&=0.0125.
\end{aligned}
$$
These values support the narrower statement that the observed local codon-window shape is uncommon under the recorded finite randomization procedure.  They do not turn the local tile into a biological law.  The exact-shape row and the high-Jaccard row are evidence about a finite codon-label geometry, not about translation occurring in a cell, protein folding, physical viability, molecular mechanism, or universality across life.

\paragraph{Amino-acid-class preserving null.}
The amino-acid-class preserving control strengthens the code-layer reading because it keeps the relevant label-class structure in view while randomizing the shape question.  In $N=2000$ class-preserving samples, the exact observed shape was never reproduced:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\#\{\text{class-preserving exact matches}\}&=0,\\
p_{\mathrm{class\ exact}}&=0.0.
\end{aligned}
$$
The same run reports $|M|=20$ and a lower-tail count of $7$ samples at or below the observed $M$ size:
$$
\begin{aligned}

This is a finite negative-control result: the observed geometry remains exceptional even after preserving the amino-acid class boundary used by the label table.  The result is still only a code-read result.  The curated table supplies codon-to-label assignments, and the null model probes the arrangement of those assignments inside $Q_6$; neither supplies a ribosome-level, thermodynamic, structural, functional, or evolutionary mechanism.
