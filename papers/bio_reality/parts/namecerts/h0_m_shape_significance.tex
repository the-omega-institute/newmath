\subsection{Window-six codon tile significance}\label{subsec:h0-m-shape-significance}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Object and layer.}
The record concerns the standard-code codon table viewed only at the code-read layer.  The finite carrier is the six-coordinate codon cube $Q_6$, where a codon is displayed as a six-bit local coordinate and then compared with the curated standard-code assignment of codons to amino-acid or stop labels.  The distinctions used in this row are the six-bit codon display, a local tile face, a singleton label corner, and a three-point label class.  The internal operations are coordinate placement, closure, spectrum, and relation extraction, while the readback is the standard-code label table.  This is a finite claim about a local codon-label pattern, not a claim that geometry by itself realizes translation, folding, physical admissibility, biological function, mechanism, or universality.

\begin{aligned}

&\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UUA}\},

\begin{aligned}
|R|&=13, & f(R)&=(13,16,4).

Thus the relation row is not merely a label-name inventory: it also carries a punctured-cube face count.  The finite row identifies the same thirteen codons in both the cardinality check and the punctured-cube check, so the set-level and cell-complex summaries agree on the object being measured.

\paragraph{Shape significance.}
The shape row compares the observed local pattern against $N=2000$ sampled alternatives with seed $42$.  Distinct sampling required $57377$ attempts, with $55377$ duplicate retries.  The exact observed shape appeared once, giving
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{\mathrm{exact}}&=0.0005, & p_{\mathrm{Jaccard}\ge 0.9}&=0.0125.
\end{aligned}
$$
The high-overlap count was $25$ among the $2000$ samples.  These values make the local shape unusual under the finite sampling procedure used in the row.  The row should be read as evidence that the window-six encoding isolates a non-generic local codon-label boundary, not as evidence that the boundary is a biological law.

\paragraph{Amino-acid-class preserving null.}
A second null keeps amino-acid class information fixed and tests whether the observed shape still appears.  In $N=2000$ trials with seed $42$, the exact shape count was $0$, the observed $M$ size was $20$, and the lower-tail count at or below the observed size was $7$.  The reported values are
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{\mathrm{class\ exact}}&=0.0, & p_{\mathrm{class\ lower\ tail}}&=0.0035.
\end{aligned}
$$
This strengthens the reading that the local boundary is not reproduced by a simple class-preserving randomization.  It does not identify a causal translation mechanism, a structural protein property, or a functional adaptation.

The fingerprint is a code-layer invariant of the measured table contact; it is not a fold, energy, kinetics, or organism-level phenotype.

\paragraph{Leave-one checks.}
The leave-one-code-out test evaluated $23$ tables and $63$ targets.  Its baseline hit rate was $0.19821739130434785$, while the hit rate above baseline was $0.9841269841269841$.  The hit rate inside $M-T$ was $0.9365079365079365$, and the hit rate inside $M-T$ plus the boundary was $0.9841269841269841$.  By family, the hit-rate-above-baseline values were $0.8333333333333334$ for alternative yeast, and $1.0$ for bacterial-plastid, candidate-other, mitochondrial, and nuclear-ciliate rows.  These leave-one results show that the local code-read organization is stable under the finite table-withholding experiment.

\paragraph{Reality contact and scope.}
The only reality contact used here is the curated standard-code table assigning codons to amino-acid or stop labels.  That contact can test codon-label readback and local tile label splits.  It cannot test translation realization, protein folding, structural order, physical admissibility, biological function, or a universal mechanism.  Consequently, the correct conclusion is narrow: window-six codon coordinates expose a statistically rare and module-complete local boundary in the standard-code label table.  Any claim about translation, fold structure, physical realizability, biological function, or universality requires a separate contact that directly observes that higher layer.
