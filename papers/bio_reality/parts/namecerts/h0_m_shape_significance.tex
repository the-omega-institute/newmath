\paragraph{Object and coordinate frame.}
The record concerns the standard-code codon table viewed through a six-bit codon display.  The biological contact used here is the curated assignment of each codon to an amino-acid or stop label.  The coordinate object is the codon cube $Q_6$, with local tile faces, singleton label corners, and three-point label classes used as finite distinctions.  The readout is only the standard-code label table: it records which codon has which label, and it does not by itself record ribosomal realization, molecular folding, physical admissibility, biological function, mechanism, or universality.

\paragraph{Shape significance experiment.}
The main shape-significance run used seed $42$ and sampled $N=2000$ distinct comparison shapes.  Obtaining those distinct samples required $57377$ sample attempts and $55377$ duplicate retries, which is itself a warning that the comparison space contains many repeated draws under the sampling procedure.  Against that finite null ensemble, the exact observed shape appeared once, while $25$ samples reached Jaccard similarity at least $0.9$.  The resulting empirical rates were
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{\mathrm{exact}}&=0.0005,\\
p_{J\geq 0.9}&=0.0125.
\end{aligned}
$$
The exact-shape and high-Jaccard statistics therefore mark the observed local codon tile as unusual under this finite randomization, but the conclusion remains a code-layer conclusion about labels on codons.

\paragraph{Local claim and exclusion zone.}
The admissible conclusion is that window-six codon coordinates expose a significant local boundary-tile pattern in the curated standard-code label table.  The evidence is numerical and finite: $|R|=13$, face-vector $(13,16,4)$, exact-shape rate $0.0005$, high-Jaccard rate $0.0125$, amino-acid-class lower-tail rate $0.0035$, spectral radius value $0.6752479308830213$, and recovery rates up to $1.0$ in the leave-one-codon-out probe.  No stronger biological claim follows from these rows.  In particular, this chapter does not infer translation realization, folded protein structure, physical admissibility, biological function, a mechanism, or a universal biological law unless a separate observation supplies that higher-layer contact.
