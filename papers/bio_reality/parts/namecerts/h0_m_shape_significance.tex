\subsection{Window-Six Codon Tile Significance}
\label{subsec:h0-m-shape-significance}

\paragraph{Finite codon display.}
The object of this record is the standard-code codon table viewed through a six-coordinate codon display $Q_6$.  The biological contact is deliberately narrow: a curated standard genetic-code table supplies codon-to-label assignments, where each codon is read only as an amino-acid or stop label.  The coordinate layer then asks whether the observed label split has a finite local shape inside the codon cube, rather than whether the cube geometry is itself a biological mechanism.

The distinguished boundary row $R$ has cardinality $13$.  In the verified finite table it is exactly
$$
\begin{aligned}
R=\{&AAA, AGA, AGG, AUA, CUA, CUC, CUG, CUU,\\
&UAA, UAG, UCA, UGA, UUA\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The same row is recorded as a punctured-cube boundary with $f$-vector
$$
\begin{aligned}
(f_0,f_1,f_2)=(13,16,4).
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the local tile is not being inferred from a verbal grouping of genetic-code entries.  It is a finite coordinate object: thirteen displayed codons, sixteen one-dimensional incidences, and four two-dimensional faces in the verified row geometry.

\paragraph{Observed shape and null comparison.}
The main shape test used $N=2000$ random comparison samples with seed $42$.  To obtain those $2000$ distinct samples the run made $57377$ distinct-sample attempts and encountered $55377$ duplicate retries.  Against this finite null ensemble, the exact observed shape appeared once, giving
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{\mathrm{exact}}=\frac{1}{2000}=0.0005.
\end{aligned}
$$
A softer comparison by high Jaccard overlap, with threshold at least $0.9$, produced $25$ matches, giving
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{J\geq 0.9}=\frac{25}{2000}=0.0125.
\end{aligned}
$$
The amino-acid-class-preserving null gives a separate check.  In that run the observed $M$ row had size $20$, the exact-shape count was $0$ out of $2000$, and the lower-tail count at or below the observed $M$ size was $7$.  Hence the recorded values are
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{\mathrm{aa\ class,exact}}&=0,\\
p_{\mathrm{aa\ class,lower\ tail}}&=\frac{7}{2000}=0.0035.
\end{aligned}
$$
These numbers support the claim that the displayed standard-code row is unusually shaped under the supplied finite comparisons.  They do not say that translation, folding, function, or admissible molecular dynamics have been derived from the six-coordinate display.

\paragraph{Spectral check.}
The same finite row has a spectral signature.  The recorded values are
$$
\begin{aligned}
\lambda_M&=0.6752479308830213,\\
\lambda_R&=0.4758352843197002,\\
\mu&=3.051487585298128.
\end{aligned}
$$
The radial cubic evaluation was $-6.224354365258478\times 10^{-12}$, recorded both as the cubic-polynomial residual and as the radial-polynomial residual.  At this tolerance the spectral row is internally consistent with the finite coordinate computation.  The spectral radius value associated to the $M$ side was also recorded as $0.6752479308830213$.  This is a numerical certificate of the coordinate construction; it is not a statement about protein structure or physical realizability.

The recorded hit rate above baseline was likewise $0.9841269841269841$.  Family-level stratification used $5$ families and recorded the following hit rates above baseline:

\begin{aligned}

The leave-one-codon-out row gives a complementary local recovery check.  It recorded anchor count $0$, boundary count $5$, predictable count $8$, high closure coverage $8$, and recovery rate $1.0$.  These values say that the finite local row is stable under the tested omissions at the code-label level.

\paragraph{Module accounting.}
The thirteen codons of $R$ are exhausted by the verified module classification.  The complete module accounting reports $R$ size $13$, assigned count $13$, no extra module codons, no missing codons, and no unassigned codons.  The modules are
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{agr}&=\{AGA,AGG\},\\
\mathrm{ar\_lys}&=\{AAA\},\\
\mathrm{cun}&=\{CUA,CUC,CUG,CUU\},\\
\mathrm{ile\_met}&=\{AUA\},\\
\mathrm{ssn}&=\{UCA,UUA\},\\
\mathrm{stop\_trp}&=\{UAA,UAG,UGA\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
Every listed module has either a predictable or boundary role in the recorded finite computation.  This closes the finite accounting for the local tile: the thirteen-codon boundary is neither under-assigned nor enlarged by hidden module entries.

\paragraph{Scope of the biological contact.}
The curated standard-code table is sufficient for code-layer readback: codons can be mapped to amino-acid or stop labels, and the resulting label split can be compared with the coordinate tile.  It is not a contact with translation realization, folded structure, physical admissibility, biological function, or a universal biological mechanism.  The local conclusion is therefore restricted to this form: the window-six coordinate display exposes an unusually shaped local boundary tile for the standard-code label table, with exact-shape probability $0.0005$, high-overlap probability $0.0125$, a thirteen-codon punctured-cube boundary, stable leave-one behavior, and complete module accounting.  Any claim beyond codon-label readback would require a separate observation tied to that higher layer.
