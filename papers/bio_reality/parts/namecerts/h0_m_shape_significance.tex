\subsection{Window-six local codon tile significance}\label{subsec:h0-m-shape-significance}\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite coordinate object.} The claim concerns the standard-code codon table after it has been read at the code-label layer through a six-bit codon display. The carrier is the codon cube $Q_6$, with local observations organized by coordinate, closure, spectrum, and relation. The relevant relation row is the finite set
$$
\begin{aligned}
R=\{&\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\\
&\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UUA}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
It has cardinality $13$, and the punctured-cube check records the face vector $(13,16,4)$. Thus the object is not merely a verbal grouping of codons: it is a finite subconfiguration of the six-dimensional codon cube, with vertices, edges, and two-faces counted in the coordinate geometry. The associated $M$ row is observed with size $20$ in the amino-acid-class preserving null experiment; the finite row supplied here gives this size but not the full displayed codon list for $M$.

\paragraph{Shape test.} The direct significance run used seed $42$ and sampled $N=2000$ distinct randomized shapes. Because the sampler had to reject repeated samples, it reports $57377$ distinct-sample attempts and $55377$ duplicate retries. Against that null, the observed shape occurred exactly once:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\#\{\text{exact matches}\}&=1, & p_{\mathrm{exact}}&=0.0005,\\
\#\{\text{Jaccard}\geq 0.9\}&=25, & p_{J\geq 0.9}&=0.0125.
\end{aligned}
$$
The exact-shape number is therefore a finite empirical small-tail event in this sampling regime, while the high-Jaccard number shows that near-neighbor shapes are uncommon but not absent. The interpretation is local: the window-six representation exposes a boundary-tile pattern in the codon-label table. It does not by itself explain why translation exists, how a ribosome realizes it, whether a peptide folds, or whether any biological function follows.

\paragraph{Class-preserving null comparison.} A stricter amino-acid-class preserving null used the same seed $42$ and again used $N=2000$ samples. In that run, the exact match count was $0$, so the recorded exact-shape frequency is $0.0$. The lower-tail statistic for the observed size $|M|=20$ counted $7$ samples at or below the observed size, giving
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{\mathrm{class\ exact}}&=0.0, & p_{\mathrm{class\ lower\ tail}}&=0.0035.
\end{aligned}
$$
This comparison matters because the null keeps amino-acid class information rather than scrambling all labels without regard to class. Even under that more conservative finite-row comparison, the observed local shape remains a low-frequency event. The result is a code-read statement: curated codon-to-label assignments support the split being tested, but the table contains no structural, kinetic, or functional measurement.

\paragraph{Spectral profile.} The spectral row gives a second numerical view of the same local object. The recorded values are
$$
\begin{aligned}
\lambda_M&=0.6752479308830213,\\
\lambda_R&=0.4758352843197002,\\
\mu&=3.051487585298128.
\end{aligned}
$$
The radial cubic check evaluates to $-6.224354365258478\times 10^{-12}$, and the same residual is recorded for the cubic-polynomial check. At the precision of the run, this is a numerical zero certificate for the stated spectral relation. The spectral radius entry repeats $0.6752479308830213$, matching $\lambda_M$ in the supplied row. These numbers do not create a mechanism; they only say that the finite coordinate object has a stable numerical profile under the recorded matrix calculation.

\paragraph{Recovery probes.} Two leave-one-out probes test whether the local tile is predictive inside the code-label layer. In the leave-one-code-out experiment, $23$ code tables and $63$ total targets were evaluated. The baseline hit rate was $0.19821739130434785$, whereas the hit rate above baseline was $0.9841269841269841$. The hit rate inside $M\setminus T$ was $0.9365079365079365$, and the hit rate inside $M\setminus T$ plus the boundary was again $0.9841269841269841$. In the leave-one-codon-out row, the anchor count is $0$, the boundary count is $5$, the predictable count is $8$, the high-closure coverage count is $8$, and the recovery rate is $1.0$. These figures support a local reconstruction phenomenon for codon labels: removed label information is often recovered by the finite neighborhood and boundary data. They do not promote the statement to a claim about protein structure or organism-level adaptation.

\paragraph{Family and module accounting.} The family-level leave-one-code-out row partitions the evaluated cases into $5$ families. The above-baseline hit rates are $0.8333333333333334$ for alternative yeast, and $1.0$ for bacterial-plastid, candidate-other, mitochondrial, and nuclear-ciliate families. The module classification is complete for the relation row: $R$ has size $13$, all $13$ codons are assigned, and there are no extra module codons, no codons missing from the modules, and no unassigned codons. The modules are
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{agr}&=\{\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG}\},\\
\mathrm{ar\_lys}&=\{\mathrm{AAA}\},\\
\mathrm{cun}&=\{\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU}\},\\
\mathrm{ile\_met}&=\{\mathrm{AUA}\},\\
\mathrm{ssn}&=\{\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UUA}\},\\
\mathrm{stop\_trp}&=\{\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UGA}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
Every module has either predictable or boundary status in the recorded classification. This gives the local shape a concrete module-level decomposition rather than a single aggregate statistic.

\paragraph{Local scientific boundary.} The only biological contact used here is a curated standard-code table assigning codons to amino-acid or stop labels. That contact can test code-layer readback and the local split of codon labels. It cannot test translation realization, folded structure, structural order, physical admissibility, biological function, or a universal mechanism. Consequently, the chapter records a significant finite codon-label geometry in $Q_6$ and blocks every higher-layer promotion unless a separate experiment supplies the missing contact. Amino-acid label sequences are not folded proteins, and a local codon tile is not a global law of biology.
