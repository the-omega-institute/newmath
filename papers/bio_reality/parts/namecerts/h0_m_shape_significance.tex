\paragraph{Finite carrier and observed contact.}
The record concerns the standard-code codon table viewed through a six-bit codon display.  The finite carrier is the codon cube $Q_6$, and the only biological contact used here is a curated table assigning each codon to an amino-acid or stop label.  The contact is therefore a code-layer observation: it supplies codon-to-label readback and local label comparisons, but it does not observe translation dynamics, folded protein geometry, physical admissibility, biological function, mechanism, or universality.  Any claim crossing those layers requires a separate contact of the appropriate kind.

\paragraph{Shape significance.}
The principal shape experiment sampled $N=2000$ distinct comparison shapes using seed $42$.  Because the sampling was constrained to distinct trials, the run made $57377$ sample attempts and rejected $55377$ duplicates.  Among the $2000$ distinct samples, exactly one matched the observed shape exactly, giving
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{\mathrm{exact}}=\frac{1}{2000}=0.0005.
\end{aligned}
$$
High-overlap matches at Jaccard threshold $0.9$ occurred $25$ times, giving
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{J\geq 0.9}=\frac{25}{2000}=0.0125.
\end{aligned}
$$
Both values are small enough to record that the observed local tile is unusual under this finite null comparison.  The conclusion is deliberately local: the codon-coordinate pattern has nontrivial finite shape signal relative to the chosen sampler, while the amino-acid and stop labels still enter through the curated standard-code table.

\paragraph{Amino-acid-class-preserving control.}
A second null model preserves amino-acid class information and again uses $N=2000$ samples with seed $42$.  In this control, the exact observed shape appeared $0$ times, so the finite estimate for exact shape recurrence is $0.0$.  The lower-tail count for samples with $M$-size at most the observed value $20$ was $7$, giving
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{\mathrm{lower\ tail}}=\frac{7}{2000}=0.0035.
\end{aligned}
$$
This supports the same local reading from a stricter comparison: after respecting label-class information, the observed $M$-side size and shape still sit in a sparse region of the sampled finite space.  The result does not convert the table into a mechanism; it says only that the code-layer labels have an unexpectedly organized local boundary profile in the chosen coordinates.
