\subsection{Window-six codon shape significance}
\label{sec:h0-m-shape-significance}

\paragraph{Finite coordinate record.}
The claim concerns the standard-code codon table read at the code-label layer.  The carrier is the codon cube $Q_6$, understood as the six binary coordinates obtained from a three-position codon display.  The observed packet separates a local tile face, singleton label corners, and a three-point label class inside that finite cube.  The contact is the curated standard genetic-code table: it supplies codon-to-label assignments for the standard code, and nothing in this chapter treats that table as a mechanism of translation or as evidence about folded proteins.  The finite distinguished boundary set is
$$
\begin{aligned}
R=\{&AAA, AGA, AGG, AUA, CUA, CUC, CUG, CUU,\\
&UAA, UAG, UCA, UGA, UUA\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The verified size is $|R|=13$.  In the punctured-cube check the same set carries the face-count vector $(13,16,4)$, so the row data isolate thirteen vertices, sixteen one-dimensional adjacencies, and four two-dimensional square faces in the local object being tested.  This is a finite coordinate statement about the standard-code label table, not a claim that biology must realize the geometry as a law.

\paragraph{Observed shape and sampling comparison.}
The finite experiment attached to the main claim used seed $42$ and $N=2000$ sampled comparison shapes.  Because repeated samples occurred, the run made $57377$ distinct-sample attempts and recorded $55377$ duplicate retries.  The observed shape had one exact match among the $2000$ sampled alternatives, giving
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{\mathrm{exact}} &= 0.0005,\\
p_{J\geq 0.9} &= 0.0125,
\end{aligned}
$$
with $25$ sampled alternatives reaching Jaccard similarity at least $0.9$.  These values support a narrow finite-row statement: the standard-code local tile is uncommon under the sampled comparison procedure.  They do not identify a causal origin of the code, a molecular translation process, or any universal biological principle.  The amino-acid-class-preserving comparison gives a second control.  In that run the observed $M$-size was $20$, exact shape count was $0$ out of $2000$, the lower-tail count at or below the observed $M$-size was $7$, and the corresponding values were
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{\mathrm{class\ exact}} &= 0.0,\\
p_{\mathrm{class\ lower\ tail}} &= 0.0035.
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the row evidence is stronger than a purely unconstrained visual coincidence, while still remaining a code-readback statistic anchored to the curated table.

\paragraph{Local interpretation.}
The chapter-level conclusion is therefore deliberately narrow.  The standard-code table contains a finite window-six local shape whose exact sampled match rate is $0.0005$, whose high-Jaccard sampled match rate is $0.0125$, whose boundary set is the thirteen-codon punctured cube listed above, and whose leave-one protocols show strong code-layer recovery against the recorded baselines.  The empirical contact is sufficient for codon-label readback and local split checks.  It is not sufficient for translation realization, folding, structural order, physical admissibility, biological function, mechanism, or universality.  Any step across those boundaries would require an additional reality contact designed for that layer, such as an experiment or curated dataset about translation dynamics, molecular structure, biochemical feasibility, or functional phenotype.
