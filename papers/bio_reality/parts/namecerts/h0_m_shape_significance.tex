\subsection{Window-six codon shape significance}
\label{subsec:h0-m-shape-significance}

\paragraph{Claim record.}
The claim concerns the standard-code codon table viewed through a six-bit codon display.  The carrier is the codon cube $Q_6$, and the readout is the curated assignment of each codon to an amino-acid or stop label in the standard genetic code.  The finite distinction package used here separates coordinate data, closure data, spectral data, and relation data.  Its biological interpretation is deliberately narrow: the table contact can test codon-label readback and local tile splitting, but it does not observe translation as a cellular process, folded structure, physical admissibility, biochemical function, a mechanism, or a universal biological law.

\paragraph{Observed boundary region.}
The distinguished boundary set $R$ has cardinality $13$.  In the supplied finite row it is exactly
$$
\begin{aligned}
R=\{&AAA, AGA, AGG, AUA, CUA, CUC, CUG, CUU,\\
&UAA, UAG, UCA, UGA, UUA\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The same row records that this set is a punctured-cube shape with face-vector
$$
\begin{aligned}
(f_0,f_1,f_2)=(13,16,4).
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the local codon window is not being treated as a loose verbal analogy: the boundary region is a concrete finite subset of $Q_6$, and the incidence count attached to it is part of the data.  The row also gives an observed module partition of all $13$ boundary codons.  The modules are $\{AGA,AGG\}$ for $agr$, $\{AAA\}$ for $ar\_lys$, $\{CUA,CUC,CUG,CUU\}$ for $cun$, $\{AUA\}$ for $ile\_met$, $\{UCA,UUA\}$ for $ssn$, and $\{UAA,UAG,UGA\}$ for $stop\_trp$.  The module audit is complete: $R$ has size $13$, the assigned count is $13$, and the missing, extra, and unassigned lists are empty.

\paragraph{Shape null experiment.}
The direct shape test used seed $42$ and sampled $N=2000$ distinct surrogate rows.  Producing those distinct samples required $57377$ attempts, with $55377$ duplicate retries.  The exact shape match count was $1$, giving
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{\mathrm{exact}}=0.0005.
\end{aligned}
$$
The high-overlap criterion counted $25$ samples with Jaccard score at least $0.9$, giving
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{J\geq 0.9}=0.0125.
\end{aligned}
$$
These numbers support the local statement that the observed tile shape is uncommon under the sampled surrogate ensemble.  They do not by themselves identify a biochemical cause, and they do not license any claim that six-bit cube geometry is sufficient for translation, folding, physical realizability, function, or evolutionary universality.

\paragraph{Amino-acid-class preserving null.}
A second finite test preserves amino-acid class while checking whether the observed local geometry still appears.  With $N=2000$ and seed $42$, the observed $M$ size is $20$.  The exact class-preserving shape match count is $0$, so the recorded exact-shape probability is
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{\mathrm{class\ exact}}=0.0.
\end{aligned}
$$
The lower-tail count at or below the observed $M$ size is $7$, giving
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{\mathrm{class\ lower\ tail}}=0.0035.
\end{aligned}
$$
This strengthens the reading that the standard-code labels carry a nontrivial local arrangement in the window-six display.  The result remains a code-layer result: the test uses codon labels and finite local geometry, not ribosomal dynamics, thermodynamic constraints, protein folding observations, or organism-level function.

\paragraph{Spectral row.}
The spectral data attach three scalars to the same local configuration.  The row records
$$
\begin{aligned}
\lambda_M &= 0.6752479308830213,\\
\lambda_R &= 0.4758352843197002,\\
\mu &= 3.051487585298128.
\end{aligned}
$$
The associated radial cubic residual is $-6.224354365258478\times 10^{-12}$, also reported as the cubic-polynomial residual.  At the numerical tolerance of the run, this is effectively zero for the finite computation.  The spectral row therefore gives a quantitative internal signature of the observed split between the $M$ side and the $R$ side.  Its role is evidential and geometric: it measures a finite arrangement of codon labels, not a physical spectrum of a molecule or a direct measurement of a biological apparatus.

\paragraph{Leave-one-code-out behavior.}
The leave-one-code-out experiment evaluated $23$ tables and $63$ targets.  Its baseline hit rate is $0.19821739130434785$.  The hit rate above baseline is $0.9841269841269841$, the hit rate inside $M-T$ is $0.9365079365079365$, and the hit rate inside $M-T$ plus boundary is $0.9841269841269841$.  By family, the five recorded groups have hit rates above baseline as follows:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{alternative\ yeast} &= 0.8333333333333334,\\
\mathrm{bacterial\ plastid} &= 1.0,\\
\mathrm{candidate\ other} &= 1.0,\\
\mathrm{mitochondrial} &= 1.0,\\
\mathrm{nuclear\ ciliate} &= 1.0.
\end{aligned}
$$
The high rates show that the local code-layer geometry has predictive reach across the curated table comparison used by the run.  The result is still bounded by the kind of contact supplied: curated code tables compare labels, and label comparison is not the same as observing translation realization or biological function.

\paragraph{Leave-one-codon-out behavior.}
The leave-one-codon-out row records $0$ anchors, $5$ boundary cases, $8$ predictable cases, and $8$ high-coverage closure cases.  The recovery rate is $1.0$, and the above-threshold recovery rate is also $1.0$.  This supports the interpretation that the boundary and closure information are not merely decorative summaries of the table: they recover omitted codon-label positions in the finite test setting.  The complete module record matches this interpretation because every listed module has either predictable or boundary status, with no missing codons from the module assignment.

\paragraph{Local verdict.}
The finite evidence supports the following restricted conclusion.  In a six-bit display of the standard genetic code, the observed codon-label table contains a statistically uncommon local shape, a concrete $13$-codon boundary set, a punctured-cube incidence profile $(13,16,4)$, a stable module assignment, a spectral signature with $\lambda_M=0.6752479308830213$ and $\lambda_R=0.4758352843197002$, and strong leave-one-out recovery behavior over the supplied code-table comparisons.  The evidence is strongest as a claim about code-layer readback from a curated standard-code table.  It does not establish that DNA or RNA must obey this geometry as a law of nature, and it does not establish translation, protein structure, physical admissibility, function, mechanism, or universality.  Any movement to those higher biological layers would require a separate contact whose observed rows actually measure that layer.
