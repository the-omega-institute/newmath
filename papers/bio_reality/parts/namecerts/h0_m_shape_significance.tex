\subsection{Window-Six Codon Shape Significance}\label{subsec:h0-m-shape-significance}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Claim and finite setting.}
The record concerns the claim \texttt{h0.M.shape.significance}: the standard-code codon table, when read as a six-bit codon display on the codon cube $Q_6$, exhibits a local tile boundary that is statistically uncommon under the sampled comparison rows. The intended biological layer is only code readback. The data used here are finite: codons are treated as table labels, the coordinate carrier is the six-bit codon cube, and the readback contact is a curated standard-code assignment of codons to amino-acid or stop labels. The distinctions carried by the record are a coordinate display, a local tile face, a singleton label corner, and a three-point label class. These distinctions are enough to test a local label-split pattern, but they are not enough to infer translation dynamics, molecular structure, energetic feasibility, biological function, a mechanism, or universality.

\paragraph{Shape significance.}
The direct null comparison for \texttt{h0.M.shape.significance} used seed $42$ and $N=2000$ sampled rows. The sampler required $57377$ distinct-sample attempts and encountered $55377$ duplicate retries. Among the $2000$ retained comparison rows, exactly $1$ matched the observed shape exactly, giving
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{\mathrm{exact}}=\frac{1}{2000}=0.0005.
\end{aligned}
$$
For the high-overlap criterion, $25$ rows achieved Jaccard similarity at least $0.9$, giving
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{J\geq 0.9}=\frac{25}{2000}=0.0125.
\end{aligned}
$$
The finite row records these same values as $p\_value\_exact\_shape\_significant=0.0005$ and $p\_value\_high\_jaccard\_significant=0.0125$. A second, amino-acid-class-preserving null model, also with seed $42$ and $N=2000$, reports $0$ exact shape matches, so the recorded exact-shape $p$-value is $0.0$ at this sampling resolution. In that class-preserving null, the observed $M$-size is $20$, the lower-tail count at or below the observed size is $7$, and the lower-tail value is
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{\mathrm{lower\ tail}}=\frac{7}{2000}=0.0035.
\end{aligned}
$$
These numbers support a narrow statistical reading: the observed local codon-coordinate shape is uncommon in the sampled comparison ensembles. They do not convert the coordinate shape into a law of genetic coding.

\paragraph{Local boundary of interpretation.}
The curated standard-code table is a contact for codon-to-label readback. It says that a codon is assigned to an amino-acid or stop label in the standard code. It does not observe translation realization, folded protein structure, structural order, physical admissibility, biological function, or a universal mechanism. Consequently, the present record may say that a window-six coordinate display exposes a statistically uncommon local label-tile pattern in the standard-code table. It may not say that DNA must obey this geometry as a biological necessity, that the local tile explains translation, that amino-acid label sequences determine folded structure, or that cross-organism codon-family wording supplies structure evidence. Any such stronger claim would require an additional contact of the appropriate kind, separate from the curated codon-label table used here.
