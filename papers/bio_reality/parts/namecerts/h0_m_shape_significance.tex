\subsection{Window-six codon tile significance}
\label{sec:bior-h0-m-shape-significance}

\paragraph{Finite codon coordinate record.}
The record studied here is a six-bit codon display on the codon cube $Q_6$, read only at the code-label layer.  The finite carrier is not being treated as a molecular machine.  It is a coordinate packet whose observable contact is the curated standard-code table assigning codons to amino-acid or stop labels.  Within that contact, the relevant distinctions are a local tile face, a singleton label corner, and a three-point label class.  The internal bookkeeping separates coordinate, closure, spectrum, and relation data, and the only readback used in this section is the standard-code label table.

\paragraph{Boundary set and punctured-cube shape.}
The finite boundary relation $R$ has cardinality $13$.  The supplied row gives the exact codon list
$$
\begin{aligned}
R=\{&\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\\
&\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UUA}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The same row identifies this set as a punctured-cube pattern with $f$-vector $(13,16,4)$.  Thus the finite observation is not merely a list of exceptional codons: it is a structured local subcomplex with $13$ vertices, $16$ one-dimensional incidences, and $4$ two-dimensional faces.  The module classification also accounts for all $13$ codons with no omissions and no extras: $\mathrm{AGR}=\{\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG}\}$, $\mathrm{AR\_Lys}=\{\mathrm{AAA}\}$, $\mathrm{CUN}=\{\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU}\}$, $\mathrm{Ile\_Met}=\{\mathrm{AUA}\}$, $\mathrm{SSN}=\{\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UUA}\}$, and $\mathrm{Stop\_Trp}=\{\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UGA}\}$.  The module row reports $R_{\mathrm{size}}=13$, assigned count $13$, missing-from-module list empty, extra-module-codon list empty, and unassigned-codon list empty.

\paragraph{Shape significance under finite resampling.}
The primary significance row uses $N=2000$ sampled alternatives with seed $42$.  It required $57377$ distinct-sample attempts, with $55377$ duplicate retries, before obtaining the finite comparison population.  In that population the exact local shape appeared once, while a high-overlap comparison at Jaccard threshold $0.9$ appeared $25$ times.  The finite $p$-values are therefore
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{\mathrm{exact}}&=0.0005,\\
p_{J\geq 0.9}&=0.0125.
\end{aligned}
$$
The recorded significance fields repeat these values as $p\_value\_exact\_shape\_significant=0.0005$ and $p\_value\_high\_jaccard\_significant=0.0125$.  This supports the narrower statement that the window-six coordinate system exposes a rare local boundary-tile shape relative to the tested finite null ensemble.  It does not by itself turn the tile into a biological law, and it does not identify a mechanism of translation.

\paragraph{Amino-acid-class-preserving null comparison.}
A separate null row preserves amino-acid class information and again uses $N=2000$ with seed $42$.  In that row the observed $M$ size is $20$.  The exact-shape count is $0$, giving $p\_value\_aa\_class\_exact\_shape=0.0$ in the finite run.  The lower-tail count at or below the observed $M$ size is $7$, giving
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{\mathrm{aa\ class,exact}}&=0.0,\\
p_{\mathrm{aa\ class,lower\ tail}}&=0.0035.
\end{aligned}
$$
The exact-shape and lower-tail significance fields record the same values, $0.0$ and $0.0035$.  The result is stronger than a generic codon shuffle because it keeps label-class information fixed while still finding the observed local shape and size behavior rare in the supplied finite population.

The cubic and radial polynomial residuals are both $-6.224354365258478\times 10^{-12}$, and the spectral-radius field repeats $0.6752479308830213$ as within tolerance.  Equivalently, the finite row records
$$
\begin{aligned}
\lambda_M&=0.6752479308830213,\\
\lambda_R&=0.4758352843197002,\\
\mu&=3.051487585298128,\\
\varepsilon_{\mathrm{poly}}&=-6.224354365258478\cdot 10^{-12}.

$$
This is a numerical consistency check for the coordinate packet.  It is not evidence for a folded structure or for physical admissibility of any peptide product.

\paragraph{Leave-one-code and leave-one-codon probes.}
The leave-one-code-out row evaluates $23$ tables and $63$ total targets.  Its baseline hit rate is $0.19821739130434785$.  The hit rate above baseline is $0.9841269841269841$, the hit rate in $M-T$ is $0.9365079365079365$, and the hit rate in $M-T$ plus boundary is $0.9841269841269841$.  By family, the finite row reports five families: alternative yeast has hit rate $0.8333333333333334$, while bacterial/plastid, candidate other, mitochondrial, and nuclear ciliate each have hit rate $1.0$.  The leave-one-codon-out row reports anchor count $0$, boundary count $5$, high closure coverage $8$, predictable count $8$, and recovery rate $1.0$, with the threshold comparison also equal to $1.0$.  These probes show that the local coordinate packet has predictive stability for code-label recovery under the tested deletions.  The statement remains a code-read statement: it is about codon-label behavior under finite perturbations of the table.

\paragraph{Local interpretation.}
The combined finite record supports the following chapter-level reading.  The set $R$ is a $13$-codon punctured-cube boundary with complete module accounting, the observed $M$ size is $20$ in the amino-acid-class-preserving null row, the shape has exact finite-run probability $0.0005$ under the primary resampling experiment, the high-Jaccard event has probability $0.0125$, and the class-preserving lower-tail probability is $0.0035$.  The spectral scalars are numerically coherent to about $10^{-11}$ residual scale, and the deletion probes recover code-label targets at rates far above the stated baseline.  Therefore the finite data justify treating the window-six pattern as a significant local codon-label tile in the curated standard-code table.

\paragraph{Scope boundary.}
Nothing in this chapter claims that the local tile realizes translation, determines folding, proves structural order, establishes physical admissibility, predicts biological function, gives a universal mechanism, or states a global biological law.  The curated table contact supplies codon-to-label readback only.  Any move from code labels to translation realization, folded protein structure, physical admissibility, function, or universality would require a separate observational contact with data at that layer.  In particular, amino-acid label sequences are not folded structures, and CUN/UUR or tRNA-Leu wording does not become structure evidence merely by appearing near the codon-label packet.
