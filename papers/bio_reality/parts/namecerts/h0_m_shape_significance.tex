\subsection{Window-Six Shape Significance for the Standard Code}
\label{sec:h0-m-shape-significance}

\paragraph{Finite object under test.}
The record concerns the standard-code codon table viewed only at the code-read layer. The finite carrier is the six-coordinate codon cube $Q_6$, where each codon is treated as a six-bit display point and the curated standard-code table supplies the label readback into amino-acid or stop labels. The construction distinguishes a local tile face, singleton label corners, and three-point label classes; its internal evidence is organized by coordinate placement, closure, spectrum, and relation data. The biological object is therefore not translation itself, and not protein realization. It is the finite codon-to-label table together with a window-six geometry that can be checked against that table.

\paragraph{Observed relational set.}
The relation side of the packet isolates a thirteen-codon set $R$. The verified cardinality claim gives
$$
\begin{aligned}
|R| &= 13,\\
R &= \{\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\\
&\qquad \mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UUA}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The same codon list is verified again as a punctured-cube pattern. Its face-vector data are
$$
\begin{aligned}
f(R)=(13,16,4),
\end{aligned}
$$
so the row does not merely count labels; it records a local cubical shape with thirteen vertices, sixteen one-dimensional incidences, and four two-dimensional faces. This is the concrete shape whose significance is tested against shuffled or constrained alternatives.

\paragraph{Shape significance.}
The finite Monte Carlo row for the shape test used seed $42$ and $N=2000$ sampled tables. Because duplicate samples were rejected, the run required $57377$ distinct-sample attempts and recorded $55377$ duplicate retries. The observed exact-shape match count was $1$, giving
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{\mathrm{exact}} &= 0.0005.
\end{aligned}
$$
For the high-overlap criterion, the run found $25$ samples with Jaccard score at least $0.9$, giving
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{J\geq 0.9} &= 0.0125.
\end{aligned}
$$
These two numbers make the local tile statistically non-generic under the sampled comparison class. The claim is deliberately narrow: the result says that the standard-code labels, when read through this six-coordinate window, reproduce a rare local boundary shape. It does not say that the geometry is a biological necessity.

\paragraph{Amino-acid-class preserving null.}
A second null row keeps amino-acid class information fixed while testing the same local geometry. In that run, again with seed $42$ and $N=2000$, the exact-shape count was $0$. The observed $M$-side size was $20$, and only $7$ samples landed in the lower tail at or below the observed size. Thus
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{\mathrm{aa\ class,exact}} &= 0.0,\\
p_{\mathrm{aa\ class,lower\ tail}} &= 0.0035,\\
|M|_{\mathrm{observed}} &= 20.
\end{aligned}
$$
This strengthens the finite-row reading: the signal is not erased when the comparison respects amino-acid class. The chapter still treats the result as a code-table fact, not as a claim about biochemical dynamics.

In this chapter the spectrum is used only as a finite diagnostic of the codon-label geometry. It is not promoted to an energy model, folding constraint, or mechanistic law.

The hit rate inside $M\setminus T$ was $0.9365079365079365$, and the rate inside $M\setminus T$ together with the boundary was $0.9841269841269841$. By family, five families were recorded: alternative yeast at $0.8333333333333334$, bacterial plastid at $1.0$, candidate other at $1.0$, mitochondrial at $1.0$, and nuclear ciliate at $1.0$. These rows show that the window-six tile has strong recovery behavior for codon-label readback across the tested table family. They do not convert table-to-table label recovery into a universal account of translation.

The leave-one-codon-out row gives a more local recovery check. It records anchor count $0$, boundary count $5$, high closure coverage $8$, predictable count $8$, and recovery rate $1.0$. The module-classification row reports $R$-size $13$, assigned count $13$, no extra module codons, no missing codons, and no unassigned codons. Every module has either predictable or boundary status. This makes the local boundary interpretation concrete: the significant shape is not an undifferentiated thirteen-point anomaly, but a classified set of codon modules inside the standard-code readback.

\paragraph{Contact discipline.}
The only reality contact used here is the curated standard genetic-code table, treated as a bridge for codon labels. It supports the assertion that codons receive amino-acid or stop labels in the standard code. It does not observe ribosomal realization, kinetic selection, folded protein structure, physical admissibility, organismal function, or evolutionary universality. Consequently, the verified rows authorize a statement about a rare and recoverable local codon-label tile in $Q_6$, together with its spectrum and module classification. They do not authorize the stronger statements that DNA must obey this geometry, that the window-six carrier implements translation, that the amino-acid labels specify a folded structure, that the resulting sequence has biological function, or that the tile is a universal mechanism. Any such higher-layer assertion would require a separate contact built for that layer.

\paragraph{Chapter verdict.}
The finite evidence supports the claim $\mathrm{h0.M.shape.significance}$ at the code-read layer. The strongest local statement is that the curated standard code, when represented in the six-coordinate codon cube, contains a thirteen-codon punctured-cube boundary set $R$ with face vector $(13,16,4)$, rare exact occurrence under the sampled null with $p=0.0005$, high-overlap probability $0.0125$, amino-acid-class-preserving lower-tail probability $0.0035$, spectral values $\lambda_M=0.6752479308830213$, $\lambda_R=0.4758352843197002$, and $\mu=3.051487585298128$, plus complete thirteen-codon module classification and strong leave-one-code and leave-one-codon recovery. The result is therefore ready as a finite code-layer shape-significance record, provided the boundary against translation, folding, physical admissibility, function, mechanism, and universality remains part of the claim itself.
