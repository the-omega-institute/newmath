\subsection{Direction-aware ranking of the modeled-translation complement}
\label{sec:bq6-h-candidate-ranking-non-translation-residual}

\paragraph{Object of the finite computation.}
This record tests what remains of the $B^*_{Q6}$ abundance component after the modeled translation-adaptation component has been removed.  For each organism $o$, let $P_Q(o)$ denote the residualized protein-abundance component explained by the nine $B^*_{Q6}$ synonymous coordinates, and let $P_{Q\perp T_{\mathrm{mod}}}(o)$ denote the component of $P_Q(o)$ orthogonal to the modeled-tAI direction.  The experiment computes both the complement size
$$
\begin{aligned}
  d(o) &= \frac{\|P_{Q\perp T_{\mathrm{mod}}}(o)\|^2}{\|P_Q(o)\|^2},
\end{aligned}
$$
and a direction-aware ranking of candidate readouts $H$.  Each candidate receives a magnitude score $A_H$, a coordinate-support alignment $C_H$, and the penalized score
$$
\begin{aligned}
  \operatorname{Score}(H) &= A_H C_H - \lambda \log_2(1+n_H),\qquad \lambda=0.01.
\end{aligned}
$$
The nine coordinates are $K_{AAA}$, $\operatorname{Arg}_{AGR}$, $\operatorname{Ile}_{AUA}$, $\operatorname{Leu}_{CUN/UUR}$, $\operatorname{Leu}_{UUA/UUG}$, $\operatorname{Ser}_{UCR/AGY}$, $\operatorname{Ser}_{UCA/UCG}$, $\operatorname{Thr}_{ACR/ACY}$, and $f_3$-stress.  The control design has $24$ columns with rank $23$ in every organism in the ranking table; the joined row counts are $n=14410$ for zebrafish, $3739$ for E. coli K-12 MG1655, $12418$ for chicken, $19053$ for human, $4129$ for Mycobacterium smegmatis, and $5815$ for yeast.

\paragraph{Complement sizes.}
The modeled-tAI complement is not uniform across organisms.  In yeast the complement is small relative to the modeled translation component: $\|P_Q\|^2=882.6526208776509$, $\|P_{Q\perp T_{\mathrm{mod}}}\|^2=186.88597748267557$, and $d=0.21173219572706714$.  In E. coli the corresponding values are $432.8905002445726$, $287.00467177017873$, and $d=0.6629960038578534$.  The vertebrate rows are larger: zebrafish has $d=0.874628721049266$, chicken has $d=0.9400341806302775$, and human has $d=0.9208949355679455$.  Mycobacterium has $d=0.8972672018746484$.  Thus the table is not a claim that modeled tAI absorbs the $B^*_{Q6}$ abundance component in general.  It says that the residual direction is organism-dependent and should be interrogated by explicit candidate readouts rather than promoted to a biological pathway.

Zebrafish also has Bonferroni-positive entries for PTM density and measured tRNA-tAI, but their penalized scores are negative.

For E. coli K-12 MG1655 no selected $H_o^*$ is reported.  The computable entries are mRNA with $A_H=0.0017706954554909877$, $C_H=0.8639529831258776$, $\operatorname{Score}=-0.008470202379021127$, $n=3415$, Bonferroni threshold $0.001860998420625413$, and Null95 $0.0011620497987012218$, and measured translation efficiency with $A_H=0.0010982956489728746$, $C_H=0.5041139753888787$, $\operatorname{Score}=-0.009446333814243976$, $n=3415$, Bonferroni threshold $0.0013123286537543308$, and Null95 $0.0011834571310485437$.  This is a negative or underpowered ranking outcome for this candidate set, not a failure of the residual construction.

For yeast, the selected candidate is mRNA: $A_H=0.001897605472160837$, $C_H=0.6040571890503664$, $A_HC_H=0.0011462622274400686$, $\operatorname{Score}=-0.008853737772559931$, $n=5128$, Bonferroni threshold $0.0013014749238591248$, and Null95 $0.0004745820831535835$. Mycobacterium has no computable ranking entries in this table.

\paragraph{Relation to the modeled translation surface.}
The same coordinate family has a rank-powered modeled-tAI surface across $40$ organisms.  There the readout matrix has shape $9\times 9$, residual degrees of freedom $17$ after rank-$23$ controls, and $R^2_{QT}=0.9003849000036579$ with rank $9$ in the residualized $B^*_{Q6}$ design.  A broader organism sweep over modeled tAI reports $41$ organisms and an $R^2_{QT}$ distribution with minimum $0.1579271837024072$, first quartile $0.26353954700074694$, median $0.33934845811598124$, mean $0.38466555440501393$, third quartile $0.5143951545826744$, and maximum $0.8213457220900503$.  These numbers justify treating modeled translation adaptation as a substantial bounded readout, but they do not turn the complement ranking into a ribosome mechanism.

\paragraph{Relation to mRNA and abundance decompositions.}
The yeast mRNA-stability rows give a separate check on the mRNA side of the ranking.  On the same yeast join, $n=5085$, the half-life join hit rate is $0.9156038419146592$, and the B$^*_{Q6}$ to mRNA half-life statistic is $R^2_{QM}=0.05179429044975085$ with dominant coordinate $\operatorname{Leu}_{CUN/UUR}$.  The same join gives $R^2_{QP}=0.2761140373962526$ for protein abundance, and the cosine between $S^{QM}$ and $S^{QP}$ is $0.8040935468384264$ with permutation $p=0.000999000999000999$.  In the mediation decomposition, conditioning protein abundance on mRNA half-life reduces $R^2_{QP}$ from $0.2761140373962526$ to $R^2_{QP\mid M}=0.24851799532219632$, so the absorbed part is $0.027596042074056304$ and the mediation fraction is $0.09994436477872035$ against a permutation Null95 of $0.0014571003414947088$ with right-tail $p=0.000999000999000999$.  These values make the yeast mRNA ranking scientifically interpretable as a statistical residual companion to abundance, while keeping it distinct from a causal mRNA-decay pathway.

\paragraph{Position-resolved ribosome contact.}
A separate ribosome-occupancy computation supplies a local translation contact for selected synonymous families.  Its position control demeans occupancy within exact file-provided window and codon-index bins together with amino-acid bins, and its null shuffles synonymous labels only within those bins.  The joined ordered-CDS and occupancy table contains $5754$ genes, with no length mismatches.  With $200$ requested permutations, all $9$ computed families have at least $200$ permutations, and the families passing the $C_H>0$ rule, description-length gate $DL=2$, and $\lambda_{DL}=0.01$ are $K_{AAA}$, $\operatorname{Arg}_{AGR}$, and $f_3$-stress.  This local ribosome result is compatible with the existence of translation-layer contacts, but it is not the same object as the organism-level complement ranking and is not used as a proof of the ranked $H$ candidates.

\paragraph{Verdict and boundary.}
The finite rows support the following bounded statement: after amino-acid composition, GC3, length, organism context where applicable, and the $M$-density baseline are controlled, the $B^*_{Q6}$ abundance component leaves a modeled-tAI complement whose candidate explanations differ sharply by organism.  Zebrafish points first to measured translation efficiency, chicken and human point first to complex membership, yeast points first to mRNA, E. coli has no selected positive candidate under the scoring rule, and Mycobacterium has no computable candidate entries.  The result is a direction-aware statistical ranking of residual readout support, not a causal or mechanistic assignment.  No translation mechanism, protein folding statement, physical admissibility statement, biological function, pathway claim, or universal biological law follows from this table unless an additional layer-matched contact supplies that claim and the corresponding controlled statistic is computed.
