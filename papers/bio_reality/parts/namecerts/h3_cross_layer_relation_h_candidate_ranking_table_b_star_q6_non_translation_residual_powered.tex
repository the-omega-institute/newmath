\subsection{Direction-Aware Ranking of the Modeled-tAI Complement}
\label{sec:bioreality-q6-h-candidate-ranking-table}

\paragraph{Object measured.}
This record studies the part of the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ abundance component that remains after projecting away the modeled-tAI direction.  The nine residual coordinates are
$K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $Leu_{CUN/UUR}$, $Leu_{UUA/UUG}$, $Ser_{UCR/AGY}$, $Ser_{UCA/UCG}$, $Thr_{ACR/ACY}$, and $f3$-stress.  For each organism $o$, the computation first forms the residualized abundance component $P_Q(o)$ and the modeled-tAI complement $P_{Q\perp T_{\rm mod}}(o)$.  It then reads this complement back in the same nine-dimensional $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ coordinate system as $a_{\perp}(o)$ and ranks candidate readouts $H$ by a direction-aware score.  The module label for the finite row is \emph{cross-layer relation}; this is a statistical ranking of residual support, not a biological mechanism.

\paragraph{Controls and finite rows.}
All six organism panels report the same control shape: $24$ control columns with rank $23$.  The joined row counts are $14410$ for \emph{Danio rerio}, $3739$ for \emph{Escherichia coli} K-12 MG1655, $12418$ for \emph{Gallus gallus}, $19053$ for \emph{Homo sapiens}, $4129$ for \emph{Mycobacterium smegmatis} str. mc2 155, and $5815$ for \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}.  The controls include amino-acid composition, organism context where applicable, GC3, CDS length, and the $M$-density baseline.  The ranking regularizer is fixed at $\lambda=0.01$, with description length $DL_H=\log_2(1+n_{\rm col})$; hence one-column readouts have $DL_H=1.0$, while the twelve-column localization readout has $DL_H=3.700439718141092$.  The reported score is the regularized quantity
$$
\begin{aligned}
Score(H,o) &= A_H(o)C_H(o)-\lambda DL_H .
\end{aligned}
$$
The table therefore keeps the raw alignment product $A_HC_H$ visible while penalizing wider readout modules.

\paragraph{Modeled-tAI complement.}
The complement fraction $d=\|P_{Q\perp T_{\rm mod}}\|^2/\|P_Q\|^2$ varies sharply by organism.  Yeast is the least complemented case: $\|P_Q\|^2=882.6526208776509$, $\|P_{Q\perp T_{\rm mod}}\|^2=186.88597748267557$, and $d=0.21173219572706714$.  In contrast, the complement is high in chicken and human, with $d=0.9400341806302775$ for chicken and $d=0.9208949355679455$ for human.  The other finite rows are $d=0.874628721049266$ for zebrafish, $d=0.6629960038578534$ for \emph{E. coli}, and $d=0.8972672018746484$ for \emph{M. smegmatis}.  These values agree exactly with the complement-function reference values in the audit row and remain within $0.02$ of the stated roadmap anchors in every organism.  Written as an unexplained-modeled-tAI fraction $m=1-d$, the list is
$$
\begin{aligned}
(m_{Dr},m_{Ec},m_{Gg},m_{Hs},m_{Ms},m_{Sc})
&=(0.12537127895073397,0.3370039961421466,0.05996581936972245, \\
&\quad 0.0791050644320545,0.1027327981253516,0.7882678042729329).
\end{aligned}
$$
This makes yeast the organism in which the modeled-tAI direction absorbs the largest share of the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ abundance component, while chicken and human retain large modeled-tAI-complement components.

Thus the zebrafish finite row selects measured translation efficiency as $H_o^{\ast}$ and keeps mRNA as a close positive companion.

\paragraph{Interpretation of the table.}
The finite row supports a narrow claim: the modeled-tAI complement of the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ abundance component can be decomposed into direction-aware candidate readouts, and the resulting preferred readout is not stable across organisms.  Zebrafish is the only case with positive regularized scores, selecting measured TE and mRNA.  Human and chicken select complex membership but with negative regularized scores.  Yeast selects mRNA with a negative regularized score, while \emph{E. coli} and \emph{M. smegmatis} provide no selected positive readout.  This pattern is scientifically useful because negative or organism-specific rows are retained rather than forced into a common biological story.

\paragraph{Boundary of the result.}
The computation does not by itself establish translation realization, ribosome-mediated mechanism, protein folding, physical admissibility, biological function, pathway membership, or universality.  Measured TE, modeled tAI, mRNA, protein abundance, localization, complex membership, ptm-density, and transmembrane count are separate readout classes with their own noise and matching conditions.  A positive $A_HC_H$, a Bonferroni exceedance, or a selected $H_o^{\ast}$ is not a causal statement.  It is only a finite-row residual-alignment statistic under the declared controls.  Any stronger statement about translation, structure, function, mechanism, phenotype, or universal biological law requires its own layer-matched contact and its own controlled statistic.
