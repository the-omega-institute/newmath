\subsection{Direction-aware ranking of residual readout candidates}\label{sec:bioreality-q6-h-candidate-ranking}\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Object measured.} This record tests how much of the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ abundance component remains outside the modeled-translation direction, and then asks which measured readout family $H$ best aligns with that remaining component. For each organism the construction starts from the same nine synonymous residual coordinates,
$K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $Leu_{CUN/UUR}$, $Leu_{UUA/UUG}$, $Ser_{UCR/AGY}$, $Ser_{UCA/UCG}$, $Thr_{ACR/ACY}$, and $f_3$-stress. The abundance component $P_Q$ is projected away from the modeled tAI component, giving $P_{Q\perp T_{mod}}$. The scalar
$$
\begin{aligned}
d_{mod} = \frac{\lVert P_{Q\perp T_{mod}}\rVert^2}{\lVert P_Q\rVert^2}
\end{aligned}
$$
records the fraction of the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ abundance component not absorbed by modeled tAI. The finite rows are strongly organism-dependent: $d_{mod}=0.2117321957$ in \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}, $0.6629960039$ in \emph{Escherichia coli} K-12 MG1655, $0.8746287210$ in \emph{Danio rerio}, $0.8972672019$ in \emph{Mycobacterium smegmatis}, $0.9208949356$ in \emph{Homo sapiens}, and $0.9400341806$ in \emph{Gallus gallus}. Thus yeast is the row in which modeled tAI absorbs the largest share of the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ abundance component, while bird and human retain a much larger modeled-translation complement.

\paragraph{Ranking statistic.} For every candidate readout family $H$, the row reports a magnitude $A_H$, a coordinate-support alignment $C_H$, the raw product $A_H C_H$, and the penalized score
$$
\begin{aligned}
Score(H) = A_H C_H - 0.01\,\log_2(1+n_H),
\end{aligned}
$$
where $n_H$ is the number of columns in the candidate readout block. The single-column candidates therefore have $DL_H=1.0$, while localization has $DL_H=3.7004397181$ because it uses twelve columns. This regularization matters: localization can have a large raw product and still rank below compact one-column readouts. The statistic is descriptive and direction-aware; it is not a causal decomposition.

\paragraph{Finite organism rows.} In \emph{Danio rerio}, the top candidate is measured translation efficiency with $A_H=0.0175942900$, $C_H=0.6573753096$, $A_HC_H=0.0115660518$, $Score=0.0015660518$, $n=13140$, Null95 $=0.0003297458$, and Bonferroni threshold $=0.0007199950$. The mRNA row is also positive after the same penalty, with $A_H=0.0119730339$, $C_H=0.8965637854$, $A_HC_H=0.0107345886$, $Score=0.0007345886$, $n=13140$, Null95 $=0.0002356009$, and Bonferroni threshold $=0.0005878304$. In \emph{E. coli} K-12 MG1655, no candidate is selected as $H_o^{\ast}$: mRNA has $Score=-0.0084702024$ with $n=3415$ and measured translation efficiency has $Score=-0.0094463338$ with the same row count. In \emph{Gallus gallus}, complex membership is the top labelled candidate, with $A_H=0.0074424743$, $C_H=0.5699065405$, $A_HC_H=0.0042415148$, $Score=-0.0057584852$, $n=1906$, Null95 $=0.0027087119$, and Bonferroni threshold $=0.0055416579$; ptm density also exceeds its Bonferroni threshold, but its smaller support alignment gives $Score=-0.0075542561$.

\paragraph{Human and yeast rows.} In \emph{Homo sapiens}, the selected candidate is complex membership, with $A_H=0.0075272000$, $C_H=0.8723071163$, $A_HC_H=0.0065660301$, $Score=-0.0034339699$, $n=18335$, Null95 $=0.0002334420$, and Bonferroni threshold $=0.0002646171$. The human measured-TE row is deliberately not promoted despite exceeding its Bonferroni threshold: its support alignment is negative, $C_H=-0.1081353722$, so $A_HC_H=-0.0003130211$ and the penalized score is $-0.0103130211$. In \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}, mRNA is selected, with $A_H=0.0018976055$, $C_H=0.6040571891$, $A_HC_H=0.0011462622$, $Score=-0.0088537378$, $n=5128$, Null95 $=0.0004745821$, and Bonferroni threshold $=0.0013014749$. Yeast localization exceeds its Bonferroni threshold and has $A_HC_H=0.0047683241$, but the twelve-column description penalty lowers it to $Score=-0.0322360731$. \emph{Mycobacterium smegmatis} has a valid nine-dimensional $a_{\perp}$ coordinate readback but no assembled candidate ranking, so it contributes to the complement table but not to $H_o^{\ast}$ selection.

\paragraph{Relation to nearby readouts.} The same finite system reports a direct complement-support analysis in which $R^2_{QP}=0.2983762499$ for yeast, $0.1076752667$ for \emph{E. coli}, $0.0461352981$ for zebrafish, $0.0445456512$ for human, $0.0398079360$ for chicken, and $0.0320900916$ for \emph{M. smegmatis}. The yeast mRNA mediation row gives $R^2_{QP}=0.2761140374$, $R^2_{QP\mid M}=0.2485179953$, and an absorbed fraction $0.0999443648$ with right-tail permutation value $0.0009990010$ over 1000 permutations. A separate ribosome-occupancy row is narrower still: it reports nine computed families, 200 requested within-position-bin permutations, and three families passing the stated $\lambda_{DL}=0.01$ plus $C_H>0$ gate, namely $K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, and $f_3$-stress. These neighboring rows show that the ranking table is not a free-standing biological mechanism; it is one descriptive layer among abundance, modeled translation, mRNA stability, and local occupancy contacts.

\paragraph{Interpretation.} The finite conclusion is that the modeled-tAI complement of the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ abundance component is measurable and rankable in five organisms with candidate rows, but it does not collapse to a single universal readout. Zebrafish selects measured translation efficiency and mRNA with positive penalized scores; human and chicken select complex membership despite negative penalized scores; yeast selects mRNA with a negative penalized score; \emph{E. coli} and \emph{M. smegmatis} do not supply a selected positive candidate. The result therefore supports a bounded residual-ranking statement: after the amino-acid, GC3, length, organism-context, and $M$-density controls used in the surrounding finite rows, some organism-specific non-modeled-translation residual structure remains visible, but its best readout class depends on the organism and on the description-length penalty.

\paragraph{Local boundary.} No translation realization, folding statement, physical admissibility statement, biological function statement, mechanism, or universal biological law follows from this chapter. A positive $A_HC_H$, a Bonferroni exceedance, or the existence of a selected $H_o^{\ast}$ is only a finite statistical readback for the declared joined rows. Claims about ribosome-mediated translation require a separate measured translation contact; claims about protein output require matched protein-abundance rows; claims about structure, physical admissibility, function, or mechanism require their own layer-matched contacts and statistics.
