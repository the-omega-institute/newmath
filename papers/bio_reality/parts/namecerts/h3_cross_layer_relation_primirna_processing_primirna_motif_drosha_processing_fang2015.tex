\subsection{A pri-miRNA processing contact does not promote the $f_3$-stress translation boundary}
\label{sec:bioreality-primirna-processing-f3-stress-boundary}

\paragraph{Finite row type.}
The finite table considered here has rows keyed by a coding-sequence window and by the biological assay surface that would be needed to make an $f_3$-stress translation statement.  A row is admissible for the $f_3$-stress question only when it contains, in the same finite record, an $f_3$-stress residual coordinate after amino-acid quotienting, local synonymous codon counts, a bounded wobble-weight context, a matched position-resolved ribosome-occupancy or translation-efficiency readout, a matched mRNA control, and amino-acid or position-bin controls.  The coordinate part alone is not a translation measurement.  The wobble-weight part alone is not a ribosome measurement.  The mRNA part alone is only a transcription-layer control.  Thus the intended survival entry has the local form
$$
\begin{aligned}
S_{f_3}(T_{\mathrm{position}} \mid Z) > 0,
\end{aligned}
$$
where $Z$ includes amino-acid composition or amino-acid-bin controls, position-bin controls when the window is position-resolved, GC3, CDS length, $M$-density, codon-count baselines, matched mRNA abundance, coverage and mappability restrictions, rank checks, and simpler-baseline ablations.

\paragraph{Observed pri-miRNA processing row.}
The verified Fang2015 pri-miRNA processing contact is a real finite assay row, but it is not a coding-window translation row.  In the recorded run \texttt{9cfc036ef9a5}, the wild-type consensus was obtained from input-count-weighted bases.  Mature 5p and 3p sequences were matched exactly inside each fixed scaffold.  Motif windows were anchored to scaffold landmarks: the basal flank at the 5p Drosha end, the downstream basal flank after the 3p Drosha end, and the apical loop between mature arms.  No de-novo RNA-structure inference was used.  The scalar control association recorded for the main GC term was
$$
\begin{aligned}
 r_{\mathrm{GC,main}} &= -0.0479934,
\end{aligned}
$$
with runtime $409.285$ seconds.  This is a bounded processing-contact statement about motif placement and Drosha-related pri-miRNA processing in fixed scaffolds.  It does not supply codon-indexed CDS windows, synonymous codon counts, wobble weights, matched mRNA abundance for translation-efficiency residualization, or ribosome-footprint occupancy at codon or local-window resolution.

\paragraph{What the row can certify.}
The Fang2015 row can certify that a finite motif-processing analysis was anchored to explicit pri-miRNA scaffold landmarks and that the reported GC-control scalar was small and negative in that run.  The row is therefore admissible as evidence for a pri-miRNA processing contact, not as evidence for $f_3$-stress survival.  Its assay coordinates live on pri-miRNA scaffold landmarks, while the $f_3$-stress translation question lives on ordered coding-sequence windows.  These carriers are not interchangeable: a basal-flank or apical-loop motif row cannot be retyped as a synonymous-codon window without an explicit join that creates the coding-window fields.

\paragraph{Cross-layer comparison.}
A separate translation-conditioned structural-order analysis shows what a powered cross-layer row looks like when the necessary finite joins are actually present.  In run \texttt{1b97387835f7}, the structural-order joined counts were $3412$ for \textit{Escherichia coli} K-12 MG1655 and $5119$ for \textit{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}, with $5$ folds and a minimum joined threshold of $300$.  Its weighted full-control $\Delta R^2$ was $0.004764742175882462$, the aggregate full-control $\Delta R^2$ ranged from $0.002688284523492457$ to $0.007880037229394898$ across $n=2$ organisms, and the full-control sign-flip probability for being greater than zero was $0.0029296875$.  Those numbers are not imported into the present pri-miRNA row as support for $f_3$-stress translation; they only illustrate the kind of same-panel, controlled readback that is missing here.

\paragraph{Local survival condition.}
For the present $f_3$-stress packet, a positive claim would require a finite matrix whose rows contain the same organism, the same CDS, the same local window, ordered codon positions, synonymous codon counts, bounded tAI or stAI wobble weights, matched mRNA abundance, and position-resolved ribosome-footprint occupancy or an explicitly bounded translation-efficiency readout.  Only after those fields are present may the statistic be evaluated as
$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta_{f_3}
  &= \operatorname{score}(T_{\mathrm{position}} \mid Z, f_3)
   - \operatorname{score}(T_{\mathrm{position}} \mid Z),\\
S_{f_3}(T_{\mathrm{position}} \mid Z)
  &> 0.
\end{aligned}
$$
The required break condition is failure to improve held-out prediction after amino-acid or position-bin controls, GC3, length, $M$-density, matched mRNA, codon-count controls, rank sufficiency, coverage and mappability limits, and simpler-baseline ablations.  If $\Delta_{f_3} \leq 0$ under those controls, the coordinate remains a bounded internal covariate for the tested panel and does not survive as a translation-readout entry.

\paragraph{Boundary of interpretation.}
The present chapter therefore records a non-promotion boundary.  Review eligibility, a pri-miRNA processing assay, a modeled wobble-weight surface, or $S_{f_3}(T_{\mathrm{position}} \mid Z)$ notation does not create measured ribosome occupancy.  A position-resolved ribosome-occupancy contact name names the needed assay surface; it is not itself the joined footprint table.  No translation realization, protein abundance, folding claim, physical-admissibility claim, biological function claim, mechanism claim, phenotype claim, or universal rule follows from the $f_3$-stress coordinate unless a separate layer-matched contact supplies the corresponding finite rows and the controlled statistic survives there.
