\subsection{Anchored pri-miRNA motif windows and Drosha-processing readout}
\label{sec:bioreality-primirna-motif-drosha-processing-fang2015}

\paragraph{Finite row object.}
The finite object for this record is an anchored pri-miRNA scaffold table rather than a free RNA-structure search. Each admissible row is keyed by a fixed scaffold, the mature-arm identity inside that scaffold, and a motif window whose coordinate is defined relative to Drosha-relevant landmarks. The verified run used input-count-weighted bases to derive the wild-type consensus, matched mature $5p$ and $3p$ sequences exactly inside each fixed scaffold, and then placed motif windows at three local sites: the basal flank at the $5p$ Drosha end, the downstream basal flank after the $3p$ Drosha end, and the apical loop between the mature arms. This makes the carrier a finite landmark-indexed motif table with a processing readout, not a de-novo RNA-folding carrier.

r_{\mathrm{GC,main}} &= -0.0479934 .
\end{aligned}
$$
The computation completed in

The negative value of $r_{\mathrm{GC,main}}$ is small in magnitude, so the record should be read as an anchored motif-processing contact with a weak GC-direction scalar, not as evidence that GC content alone explains Drosha processing. The important mathematical point is the finite anchoring: all motif coordinates are tied to scaffold landmarks before any association is interpreted.

\paragraph{Relation encoded by the table.}
Let $W$ denote the finite set of accepted scaffold-window rows, and let each row $w \in W$ carry a motif coordinate $m(w)$, a landmark class $\ell(w)$, a scaffold identifier $s(w)$, and a processing readout $D(w)$. The relevant relation is not a universal motif law, but the bounded finite-row question
$$
\begin{aligned}
(m(w),\ell(w),s(w)) \longmapsto D(w)
\end{aligned}
$$
under the restriction that $\ell(w)$ is one of the verified landmark anchors. Because the mature $5p$ and $3p$ sequences were matched exactly in each fixed scaffold, the rows are controlled against a major coordinate ambiguity: motif windows are not allowed to drift across unrelated pri-miRNA geometries. Because no de-novo structure inference was used, the record also avoids treating predicted secondary structure as an observed processing measurement.

\paragraph{Interpretive status.}
This chapter records a cross-layer contact between local pri-miRNA sequence grammar and Drosha-processing behavior in a finite scaffold experiment. Its support is strongest at the level of anchored motif-window association: a motif or base-composition term may be tested only after the window has been placed at the basal $5p$ flank, downstream $3p$ flank, or apical-loop coordinate defined by the scaffold landmarks. The observed $r_{\mathrm{GC,main}}=-0.0479934$ is therefore a bounded scalar trace from that finite table. It is not, by itself, a claim that GC content is a Drosha-processing mechanism, nor that a motif term transfers to arbitrary pri-miRNA contexts.

\paragraph{Local no-promotion rule.}
No translation, protein folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, cellular mechanism, organismal phenotype, or universality follows from this record. The row table concerns pri-miRNA motif placement and Drosha-processing readout only. Any claim about downstream mature-miRNA abundance, target repression, translation, protein output, folding, physical feasibility, function, phenotype, causation, or a general biological law would require a separate finite contact with its own matched rows and controls. In particular, the fact that the windows are scaffold-anchored does not turn motif geometry into a mechanism; it only makes the sequence-processing association well posed on the rows actually analyzed.

\paragraph{Break condition.}
The record breaks if the anchored-window scalar is treated as a de-novo structure result, if the scaffold-specific mature-arm matching is ignored, if the small GC association is promoted to a dominant explanatory law, or if the motif-processing relation is used to infer translation or protein-layer consequences without an independent downstream readout. The admissible conclusion is narrower: in the verified finite run, the pri-miRNA rows were anchored to Drosha-relevant scaffold landmarks, the main GC association was $-0.0479934$, and the computation finished in $409.285$ seconds. That is sufficient for a bounded BioReality chapter on anchored pri-miRNA motif processing, and insufficient for any higher-layer biological promotion.
