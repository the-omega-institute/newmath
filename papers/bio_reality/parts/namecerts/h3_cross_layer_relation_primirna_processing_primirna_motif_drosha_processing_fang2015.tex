\subsection{Drosha-anchored pri-miRNA motif contact and the f\_3-stress boundary}
\label{subsec:bioreality-primirna-fang2015-f3-boundary}

\paragraph{Finite row.}
The row indexed by \texttt{h3.cross\_layer\_relation.primirna\_processing.primirna\_motif\_drosha\_processing\_fang2015} is a finite BioReality contact for motif-position effects in pri-miRNA processing.  Its verified run is \texttt{9cfc036ef9a5}, with runtime $409.285$ seconds and main GC association $r_{\mathrm{GC}}=-0.0479934$.  The biological object is not a coding-sequence translation window.  It is a fixed-scaffold pri-miRNA processing panel in which the wild-type consensus was obtained from input-count-weighted bases, mature $5p$ and $3p$ sequences were matched exactly inside each scaffold, and motif windows were anchored to scaffold landmarks: the basal flank at the $5p$ Drosha end, the downstream basal flank after the $3p$ Drosha end, and the apical loop between mature arms.  No de novo RNA-structure inference enters this row.

\paragraph{Local mathematical carrier.}
For this chapter the finite carrier is a table $F_{\mathrm{Drosha}}$ whose rows have the form
$$
\begin{aligned}
  x &= (s, a, u, y, z),
\end{aligned}
$$
where $s$ is a fixed scaffold identifier, $a$ is one of the scaffold-anchored motif windows, $u$ is the local sequence or motif state in that window, $y$ is the measured pri-miRNA processing readout, and $z$ records the finite nuisance coordinates used to keep motif position separate from gross sequence composition.  The observed GC scalar $r_{\mathrm{GC}}=-0.0479934$ is therefore interpreted only as a local composition diagnostic for this pri-miRNA row.  It is not a sign law for synonymous coding windows, and it is not a translation readout.

\paragraph{Claim admitted by the row.}
The row supports the following bounded statement: within the fixed-scaffold Fang-style pri-miRNA panel, motif windows can be tested as Drosha-anchored processing coordinates because the mature-arm landmarks and basal/apical anchors are part of the same finite table.  The row is admissible as a cross-layer RNA-processing contact because the measured endpoint is attached to the same designed scaffold instances that define the motif positions.  The finite statistic has the schematic form
$$
\begin{aligned}
  \Delta_{\mathrm{Drosha}}(a) =
  \operatorname{score}(y \mid u_a,z) - \operatorname{score}(y \mid z),
\end{aligned}
$$
with the understanding that the actual row records the verified finite run rather than a universal mechanistic formula.

\paragraph{Why this is not an f\_3 translation survival row.}
The conjectural $f_3$-stress object requires a different carrier: coding-sequence windows with an $f_3$ residual coordinate after amino-acid quotienting, synonymous codon counts, a wobble-weight context, matched position-resolved ribosome occupancy or translation efficiency, matched mRNA abundance, and amino-acid or position-bin controls.  The Fang pri-miRNA row supplies none of those same-CDS translation fields.  In particular, it has scaffold-anchored pri-miRNA motif windows, not ordered CDS codon windows; it has Drosha-processing readout, not ribosome-footprint occupancy; and its GC scalar is a composition diagnostic, not a wobble-asymmetry survival statistic.

The corresponding $f_3$ translation survival quantity would have to be computed on a separate joined panel as
$$
\begin{aligned}
  S_{f_3}(T_{\mathrm{position}} \mid Z)
  = \operatorname{gain}(T_{\mathrm{position}}; f_3 \mid Z),
\end{aligned}
$$
where $Z$ contains amino-acid composition or position bins, GC3, CDS length, M-density, mRNA abundance, codon-count controls, coverage and mappability diagnostics, and simpler-baseline ablations.  The present pri-miRNA row does not instantiate $T_{\mathrm{position}}$, does not define a same-window CDS translation matrix, and does not meet the rank condition for an $f_3$ readout.

\paragraph{Comparison with a powered translation row.}
The separation is not merely verbal.  A different verified table entry, \texttt{h3.cross\_layer\_relation.translation\_conditioned\_structural\_order\_boundary.b\_star\_q6\_powered}, records a passed translation-conditioned structural-order boundary with run \texttt{1b97387835f7}.  That row reports two positive organisms for the $B^*_ {Q6}$ structural-order predictor summary, with mean $0.09855549686555976$, minimum $0.03664544696481631$, and maximum $0.1604655467663032$ over $n=2$.  Its translation-conditioned verdict records weighted full-control $\Delta R^2=0.004764742175882462$, aggregate full-control mean $0.005284160876443678$, and sign-flip probability $p=0.0029296875$ after five folds and joined structural-order sample sizes $3412$ for \textit{Escherichia coli} K-12 MG1655 and $5119$ for \textit{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}.  Those numbers illustrate the kind of same-row, controlled readout evidence that is absent from the pri-miRNA Fang row.  They do not transfer to the pri-miRNA contact; they only clarify the evidentiary threshold required before a coordinate is promoted beyond a local boundary.

\paragraph{Cannot-claim boundary.}
The Fang pri-miRNA contact may be used to discuss scaffold-anchored motif effects on Drosha-associated processing.  It may not be used to infer translation realization, ribosome occupancy, translation efficiency, protein abundance, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, cellular phenotype, mechanism, or universality.  It also may not be used to validate the $f_3$-stress member of $B^*_{Q6}$, because that object requires a same-organism, same-CDS, same-window translation-readout panel with wobble-weight asymmetry and matched mRNA controls.  Any later positive claim about $f_3$ must come from such a panel and must survive held-out improvement after amino-acid composition, position-bin, GC3, length, M-density, mRNA, codon-count, rank, and simpler-baseline controls.

\paragraph{Local verdict.}
This chapter therefore records a positive finite contact for Drosha-anchored pri-miRNA motif processing and a negative promotion result for $f_3$ translation survival.  The verified finite numbers belong to the pri-miRNA processing row: runtime $409.285$ seconds and $r_{\mathrm{GC}}=-0.0479934$ under the stated scaffold anchoring.  They do not supply the missing CDS-window table required for $S_{f_3}(T_{\mathrm{position}}\mid Z)>0$.
