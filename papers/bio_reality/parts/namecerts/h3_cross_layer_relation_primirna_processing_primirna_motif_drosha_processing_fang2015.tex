\subsection{Pri-miRNA Motif Anchoring at the Drosha Processing Boundary}\label{sec:primirna-motif-drosha-processing-fang2015}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite row.}
This record treats pri-miRNA processing as a finite row problem rather than as an inferred structural mechanism. The row is keyed by fixed precursor scaffolds, mature-arm landmarks, local motif windows, input-count-weighted sequence summaries, and a processing readout attached to the same designed molecule. The verified processing row for \texttt{h3.cross\_layer\_relation.primirna\_processing.primirna\_motif\_drosha\_processing\_fang2015} reports run \texttt{9cfc036ef9a5}, runtime $409.285$ seconds, and a main GC association $r=-0.0479934$. The audit note fixes the anchoring rule: the wild-type consensus was computed from input-count-weighted bases, mature $5p$ and $3p$ sequences were matched exactly within each fixed scaffold, and the motif windows were placed relative to scaffold landmarks. In particular, the basal flank is anchored at the $5p$ Drosha end, the downstream basal flank is anchored after the $3p$ Drosha end, and the apical-loop window lies between the mature arms. No de novo RNA-structure inference is part of this row.

\paragraph{Coordinate.}
Let $T$ be the finite table of designed pri-miRNA variants that survive the scaffold and mature-arm matching rule. A row $t\in T$ carries a scaffold identifier, an input-count weight, the matched mature-arm coordinates, the basal and apical motif-window indicators, a GC covariate, and the processing readout. The coordinate used here is not a free RNA-folding variable. It is the landmark-resolved motif vector obtained after the two mature arms are exactly located in the fixed scaffold. In symbols, the admissible coordinate has the form
$$
\begin{aligned}
q(t)=(b_{5p}(t), b_{3p}(t), a(t), g(t), c(t)),
\end{aligned}
$$
where $b_{5p}(t)$ is the basal-flank entry at the $5p$ Drosha end, $b_{3p}(t)$ is the downstream basal-flank entry after the $3p$ Drosha end, $a(t)$ is the apical-loop entry between the mature arms, $g(t)$ records the GC component, and $c(t)$ records the fixed-scaffold and position controls. The finite audit gives $r_{GC}=-0.0479934$, so the GC term is retained as a control coordinate, not promoted to the biological explanation.

\paragraph{Relation.}
The supported relation is a bounded cross-layer association from landmark-anchored sequence motifs to the measured pri-miRNA processing readout. The row does not assert that a motif sequence alone determines Drosha behavior. It asserts only that, once the fixed scaffold, exact mature-arm matching, input-count weighting, and local landmark placement are respected, the finite table is eligible for a controlled motif-processing statistic. A compact way to state the permitted statistic is
$$
\begin{aligned}
S_{\mathrm{Drosha}}(q\mid Z)
  &= \Delta \mathcal{L}\bigl(Y_{\mathrm{proc}}; q, Z\bigr) \\
  &= \mathcal{L}\bigl(Y_{\mathrm{proc}}; Z\bigr)
     -\mathcal{L}\bigl(Y_{\mathrm{proc}}; q, Z\bigr),
\end{aligned}
$$
where $Y_{\mathrm{proc}}$ is the processing readout and $Z$ contains the scaffold, mature-arm position, input-count, and composition controls available in the finite row. Positive support requires that the motif coordinate improve the held-out description of the processing readout after these controls. The observed $r_{GC}=-0.0479934$ is small in magnitude and is treated as a guard against composition-only explanation, not as a standalone processing law.

\paragraph{Rank and comparison surface.}
The row is kept separate from broader sequence-design successes in the audit bundle. For example, the companion promoter-grammar finite run \texttt{h3.cross\_layer\_relation.cis\_regulatory\_grammar.random\_promoter\_tf\_motif\_expression\_boundary\_deboer2020} reports $75111$ designed sequences, $8027$ high-quality rows, an analysis subset of $2500$ rows, $200$ permutations, seed $20260239$, and runtime $530.4038379192352$ seconds. Those numbers are not imported as evidence about pri-miRNA processing. They serve only to make the audit discipline explicit: each biological boundary is judged on its own finite table, its own row keys, and its own held-out statistic. The Drosha-processing record therefore remains tied to the Fang-style scaffold landmarks and the run \texttt{9cfc036ef9a5}; it is not generalized from promoter grammar, CRISPR efficiency, translation, stop-readthrough, or any other neighboring finite-row task.

\paragraph{What the row supports.}
Within this chapter, the admissible claim is narrow. The finite evidence supports a scaffold-landmark motif coordinate for pri-miRNA processing, with exact mature-arm matching and no inferred RNA secondary-structure reconstruction. The basal and apical windows are experimentally meaningful because they are attached to the $5p$ Drosha end, the downstream flank after the $3p$ Drosha end, and the loop between mature arms. A positive result for $S_{\mathrm{Drosha}}(q\mid Z)$ would mean that the landmark-anchored motif entries carry processing information beyond the retained controls on this finite panel. The current scalar audit entry records the GC association and the anchoring rule; it does not by itself provide a full list of motif coefficients, permutation $p$-values, module labels, or per-window hit rates.

\paragraph{Local cannot-claim boundary.}
No translation claim follows from this pri-miRNA-processing row. No protein abundance, protein folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, cellular mechanism, phenotype, or universal biological principle follows from the motif coordinate unless a separate finite contact at that layer supplies its own matched rows and controlled statistic. The absence of de novo RNA-structure inference is also a boundary: the record may discuss scaffold-landmark motif placement and processing readout, but it may not claim an inferred RNA folding pathway, an energetic folding mechanism, or a general Drosha mechanism from this row alone. The chapter therefore records a finite pri-miRNA processing contact: landmark-anchored motif coordinates may be tested against a matched processing readout, while every higher biological layer remains outside scope until independently measured.
