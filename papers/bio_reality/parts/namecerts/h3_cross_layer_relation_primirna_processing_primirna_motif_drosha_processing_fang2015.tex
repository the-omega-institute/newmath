\subsection{Finite Pri-miRNA Motif Anchoring at the Drosha-Processing Boundary}
\label{sec:bioreality-primirna-motif-drosha-processing-fang2015}

\paragraph{Finite object.}
This record isolates a finite primary-miRNA processing surface rather than a general law of sequence-to-function transfer. The row object is a fixed-scaffold pri-miRNA table in which mature arm landmarks, basal-flank windows, downstream basal-flank windows, and apical-loop windows are all read against the same designed precursor context. The verified run for \texttt{h3.cross\_layer\_relation.primirna\_processing.primirna\_motif\_drosha\_processing\_fang2015} has run identifier \texttt{9cfc036ef9a5}, runtime $409.285$ seconds, and a main GC association reported as $r=-0.0479934$. The operational point is not that GC content is absent; it is that the reported scalar is small enough to force the motif-processing claim to be stated as a controlled local-window readout rather than as a composition-only explanation.

\paragraph{Landmark construction.}
The finite anchoring rule is explicit. The wild-type consensus was computed from input-count-weighted bases. Mature $5p$ and $3p$ sequences were matched exactly inside each fixed scaffold. Motif windows were then placed by scaffold landmarks: the basal flank at the $5p$ Drosha end, the downstream basal flank after the $3p$ Drosha end, and the apical loop between the two mature arms. No de-novo RNA-structure inference was used. Thus the carrier is not a free structural model; it is a finite table of sequence-window positions whose coordinates are pinned by mature-arm identity and Drosha-end geometry.

\paragraph{Statistic carried by the chapter.}
Let $W$ denote the finite set of anchored windows and let $Y_w$ denote the processing readout attached to window $w$. Let $G_w$ be the local GC coordinate and let $M_w$ be the motif coordinate anchored at the mature-arm landmarks. The local claim is the existence of a bounded processing-readout statistic of the following form:
$$
\begin{aligned}
S_{\mathrm{Drosha}}(M \mid Z)
  &= \Delta \operatorname{score}(Y; Z,M) - \Delta \operatorname{score}(Y; Z),\\
Z
  &= \{\text{fixed scaffold},\ \text{mature-arm match},\ \text{window identity},\ \text{local composition controls}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The verified scalar $r=-0.0479934$ for the GC main coordinate is treated as a local audit value on the control surface, not as an effect-size claim for a biological mechanism. A positive statement in this chapter therefore has the form: the anchored motif table is admissible for testing whether finite pri-miRNA windows carry Drosha-processing signal after the stated controls. It does not have the form: a nucleotide motif alone determines processing, function, phenotype, or mechanism.

\paragraph{Comparison scale.}
The same finite-row registry contains larger expression and translation-readout surfaces, and those numbers help calibrate scope. The random-promoter expression record \texttt{h3.cross\_layer\_relation.cis\_regulatory\_grammar.random\_promoter\_tf\_motif\_expression\_boundary\_deboer2020} reports $75111$ designed sequences, $8027$ high-quality rows, an analysis subset of $2500$ rows, $200$ permutations, subsample seed $20260239$, and runtime $530.4038379192352$ seconds. The translation-initiation record \texttt{h3.cross\_layer\_relation.translation\_initiation.utr5\_features\_ribosome\_load\_sample2019} reports $80000$ rows and runtime $172.864$ seconds, with cross-sample status recorded as null. These registry facts show that the present pri-miRNA chapter is not being promoted by analogy to promoter expression or ribosome-load panels. Its support is the Fang-style fixed-scaffold Drosha-processing table and its landmark construction, not a borrowed conclusion from another layer.

\paragraph{Relation to the proposed $f_3$-stress translation-survival coordinate.}
The broader conjectural packet asks whether an $f_3$-stress synonymous residual coordinate can survive against position-resolved ribosome occupancy or translation efficiency under wobble-weight asymmetry and full controls. This pri-miRNA record does not supply that matrix. It has no same-CDS codon-indexed ribosome-footprint rows, no matched mRNA denominator for translation efficiency, no same-window synonymous codon counts, no wobble-weight surface, and no held-out $S_{f3}(T_{\mathrm{position}}\mid Z)$ statistic. Consequently the Fang-style Drosha-processing fact can only be used as an RNA-processing landmark example: finite windows can be anchored to biochemical processing landmarks and audited against composition controls. It cannot be used as evidence that $f_3$-stress survives in translation readouts.

\paragraph{Local admissible claim.}
The admissible claim is narrow. A finite fixed-scaffold pri-miRNA table, anchored by exact mature-arm matching and Drosha-end landmarks, supports a local test of whether motif windows near basal and apical regions improve a Drosha-processing readout beyond scaffold and composition controls. The reported run \texttt{9cfc036ef9a5} supplies a concrete audit point: runtime $409.285$ seconds and $r=-0.0479934$ for the GC main coordinate. This is enough to record a bounded processing-contact surface for the standalone BioReality paper.

\paragraph{Cannot-claim boundary.}
No translation, ribosome occupancy, protein abundance, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, mechanism, phenotype, or universality follows from this chapter. The fixed-scaffold pri-miRNA rows concern RNA processing landmarks. Any claim about translation would require a separate same-row translation contact with measured ribosome occupancy or translation efficiency, matched mRNA controls, rank checks, and simpler-baseline ablations. Any claim about protein-level or organism-level consequences would require its own layer-matched contact. The present chapter therefore closes only the finite pri-miRNA motif-to-Drosha-processing boundary and explicitly refuses promotion beyond that boundary.
