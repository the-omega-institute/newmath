\paragraph{Finite row surface.}
The record named \texttt{h3.cross\_layer\_relation.primirna\_processing.primirna\_motif\_drosha\_processing\_fang2015} is a finite processing-surface certificate for a fixed-scaffold pri-miRNA experiment.  Its rows are not free RNA sequences and not inferred secondary structures.  The operative row key is a scaffold-anchored motif position: the mature 5p and 3p sequences are matched exactly inside each fixed scaffold, the basal flank is anchored at the 5p Drosha end, the downstream basal flank is anchored after the 3p Drosha end, and the apical-loop window lies between the mature arms.  The verified run reports that the wild-type consensus was derived from input-count-weighted bases and that no de-novo RNA-structure inference was used.  This makes the contact a finite positional motif table tied to a Drosha-processing readout, not a general RNA-folding surface.

\paragraph{Admissible statement.}
The admissible claim is that anchored pri-miRNA motif windows in the Fang fixed-scaffold setting form a bounded cross-layer processing coordinate for Drosha-processing readout.  The coordinate is grounded by exact mature-arm matching, landmark-based basal and apical placement, an input-count-weighted wild-type consensus, and the recorded GC-control scalar.  The evidence is therefore local to motif placement and processing in the fixed scaffold.  It is not evidence for translation, ribosome occupancy, protein abundance, protein folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, organismal phenotype, mechanism, or universality.  Any such higher-layer statement would require a separate matched contact with its own finite rows and its own controlled statistic.

\paragraph{Break condition.}
The certificate breaks if the scaffold-landmark motif table is treated as de-novo RNA-structure inference, as a general pri-miRNA grammar, or as a mechanistic statement about Drosha cleavage.  It also breaks if the GC scalar is promoted into proof that all sequence-composition effects are absent.  The row remains valid only as a finite processing-surface entry: fixed scaffold, exact mature-arm matching, anchored basal and apical motif windows, observed Drosha-processing readout, and explicitly recorded control diagnostics.
