\subsection{Finite Drosha-Landmark Motif Certificate for the Fang Pri-miRNA Surface}
\label{sec:bioreality-primirna-motif-drosha-processing-fang2015}

\paragraph{Row carrier.}
The Fang pri-miRNA processing record is treated here as a finite landmark-anchored table, not as a free RNA-folding model.  A row is a designed or observed pri-miRNA scaffold entry whose mature $5p$ and $3p$ arms are matched exactly inside a fixed scaffold, together with local sequence windows placed relative to the Drosha-processing landmarks.  The usable coordinates are the basal flank at the $5p$ Drosha end, the downstream basal flank after the $3p$ Drosha end, and the apical-loop interval between the mature arms.  The finite certificate records that the wild-type consensus was derived from input-count-weighted bases, that the mature $5p/3p$ sequences were matched exactly in each fixed scaffold, and that no de-novo RNA-structure inference was used.  Thus the scientific object certified by this row is a motif-position processing surface for pri-miRNA landmarks, not a structural folding surface.

\paragraph{Observed finite scalars.}
The verified run for the pri-miRNA processing claim is $9cfc036ef9a5$.  Its recorded runtime is $409.285$ seconds, and the reported main GC correlation is
$$
\begin{aligned}
 r_{\mathrm{GC,main}} &= -0.0479934 .
\end{aligned}
$$
This scalar is small in magnitude and is therefore used only as a local control diagnostic: it says that the finite motif-processing readout is not being summarized as a strong GC-only effect.  The row certificate instead depends on the scaffold-consensus construction and on the landmark anchoring described above.  As a calibration point for what counts as a separate finite experimental surface in this paper, the same audit ledger contains a random-promoter transcription record with $n_{\mathrm{designed}}=75111$, $n_{\mathrm{HQ}}=8027$, an analysis subsample of $2500$, $200$ actual permutations, seed $20260239$, and runtime $530.4038379192352$ seconds.  Those promoter numbers are not evidence for Drosha processing; they are cited only to make explicit that each BioReality row family must carry its own finite counts, run identifier, and scalar readout.

\paragraph{Processing claim.}
Within this chapter the admissible claim is bounded: local pri-miRNA sequence motifs, when indexed against the fixed Drosha landmarks, define a finite processing-readout surface for the Fang scaffold system.  The anchoring rule is part of the claim.  Moving the basal or apical windows to a different coordinate system would define a different row family.  In notation, the chapter certifies a finite map of the form
$$
\begin{aligned}
  (\text{scaffold},\;5p/3p\text{ match},\;\text{landmark window},\;\text{local motif state},\;Z)
  \longmapsto
  \text{Drosha-processing readout},
\end{aligned}
$$
where $Z$ includes the scaffold identity and local composition controls needed to keep motif position distinct from a broad sequence-composition surrogate.  The scalar $r_{\mathrm{GC,main}}=-0.0479934$ is kept in the chapter precisely to prevent over-reading a GC trend as the processing mechanism.

\paragraph{Name certificate.}
The name certified by this finite row is \texttt{h3.cross\_layer\_relation.primirna\_processing.primirna\_motif\_drosha\_processing\_fang2015}.  Its content is a pri-miRNA motif-to-processing relation under fixed-scaffold landmark anchoring.  The certificate does not assert that the motif windows reconstruct RNA secondary structure, that they identify a universal Microprocessor rule, or that they transfer unchanged to unrelated pri-miRNA architectures.  The phrase ``Drosha processing'' is therefore read operationally: a row is admissible only when the mature-arm matches and the basal or apical landmark window are present in the same finite scaffold record.

\paragraph{No-promotion boundary.}
No translation, protein abundance, protein folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, phenotype, mechanism, or universality follows from this certificate.  The Fang row family concerns pri-miRNA processing landmarks.  It does not provide ribosome occupancy, translation efficiency, codon-adaptation weights, protein-state measurements, physical assays, or perturbation-rescue evidence.  Any later claim at those layers would require a separate finite contact with its own rows, controls, and held-out readout.  In particular, a motif association at the Drosha-processing surface is not a general RNA mechanism unless an independent perturbation or rescue surface is attached, and it is not a translation-layer result under any reading of the present finite data.

\paragraph{Break condition.}
The chapter would fail its local certificate if the scaffold landmarks were replaced by inferred RNA structure without the fixed mature-arm match, if the motif windows were allowed to drift away from the $5p$ basal, $3p$ downstream basal, or apical-loop landmarks, or if the small GC diagnostic were promoted into the biological explanation.  It would also fail if the row were cited as evidence for translation, folding, physical admissibility, function, phenotype, or a universal law.  The supported reading is narrower and sufficient: a finite, landmark-anchored pri-miRNA motif surface was run under the Fang processing packet, with run id $9cfc036ef9a5$, runtime $409.285$ seconds, and $r_{\mathrm{GC,main}}=-0.0479934$.
