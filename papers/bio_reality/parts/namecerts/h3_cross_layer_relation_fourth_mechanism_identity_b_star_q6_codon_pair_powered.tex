\subsection{Codon-pair refusal for the fourth B$^*_{Q6}$ mechanism}
\label{sec:bioreality-q6-codon-pair-fourth-mechanism}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite object.}
The record considered here is the finite coding-sequence residual surface obtained after amino-acid quotienting, organism matching, and restriction to the $B^*_{Q6}$ coordinate family.  The specific candidate under test is whether the fourth surviving mechanism behind the $B^*_{Q6}$ residual axis is explained by codon-pair context.  The computed packet is not a broad claim about translation, protein abundance, structure, function, phenotype, or a universal law.  It is a finite negative identity test: among the controlled synonymous designs available in the powered panel, the codon-pair hypothesis is not the identity of the fourth mechanism.

The finite $f_3$ saturation boundary used by the surrounding coordinate system is explicit.  The marked union $M=\mathrm{Med}(R)\cup\{\mathrm{ACA}\}$ contains
$\{\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{ACA},\mathrm{ACG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\}$, while the $f_3$-saturated $R$ set removes $\mathrm{ACA}$ and $\mathrm{ACG}$ and is
$\{\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\}$.  The reported $f_3$ boundary residue is empty.  Thus the codon-pair test is evaluated against a closed finite codon surface rather than against an unspecified codon vocabulary.

\paragraph{Controlled negative call.}
The codon-pair experiment reports status $\mathrm{passed}$ with experiment id $\mathrm{b\_star\_q6\_fourth\_mechanism\_codon\_pair\_powered}$, run id $\mathrm{b519b5ebd34d}$, five folds, at least $500$ proteins per organism, $41$ synonymous design columns, and $50$ shuffle-null draws.  Its finite conclusion is
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{FourthMechanismCandidate}(B^*_{Q6},\mathrm{codon\ pair}) &= \mathrm{not\_codon\_pair}.
\end{aligned}
$$
This is a refusal of one mechanistic identity, not a refusal of the fourth mechanism itself.  The separate sufficiency test on the three-axis model reports $\mathrm{fourth\_mechanism\_present}$ with $11$ computed organisms out of $16$ requested, five folds, at least $500$ proteins per organism, and $41$ synonymous design columns.  The codon-pair result therefore narrows the identity of the remaining axis: it says that the fourth signal is present in the powered setting but is not captured by the codon-pair candidate under the declared design.

\paragraph{Context among neighboring finite rows.}
Other powered mechanism rows sharpen the refusal.  The mRNA-stability identity test concludes $\mathrm{fourth\_axis\_not\_mrna\_stability}$, with the reason that yeast $d_{\mathrm{opt\_halflife}}$ has low alignment with abundance $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$ under the fixed gates.  The translation-accuracy identity test concludes $\mathrm{not\_accuracy}$ on $11$ computed organisms out of $16$ requested with $200$ permutations, five folds, and $41$ synonymous design columns.  The elongation-dwell test is only $\mathrm{partial}$ and is organism-scoped to $\mathrm{saccharomyces\_cerevisiae}$.  Consequently the codon-pair row should be read as one member of a finite exclusion-and-localization pattern: codon-pair context is excluded as the dominant fourth-axis identity, mRNA stability and translation accuracy are also excluded in their respective tests, and elongation dwell remains only partially supported in a narrower organism-level contact.

The axis-strength spectrum is consistent with this interpretation.  In the powered spectrum, $11$ organisms were computed out of $16$ requested, the captured spectra were led on average by $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$ and then by $d_{\mathrm{tRNA}}$, and the per-organism dominant count was $\{d_{\mathrm{resid4}}:11\}$.  The domain counts were imbalanced, with $1$ archaeon, $2$ bacteria, and $8$ eukaryotes, so domain-level contrasts are descriptive only.  The largest GC3 rank effect was $d_{\mathrm{usage}}$ with $\rho=0.136$.  These numbers keep the codon-pair refusal finite: the row belongs to a residual-axis spectrum dominated by $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$, not to an unrestricted mechanistic law.

\paragraph{Relation to the $f_3$ translation boundary.}
The nearby $f_3$ readout conjecture asks for survival only where context tRNA or wobble weights break third-position $f_3$ symmetry and where a translation row remains after amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, length, $M$-density, and mRNA controls.  The present codon-pair row does not supply that survival statistic.  It supplies a negative mechanism-identity datum for codon-pair context within the fourth-axis search.  If a translation readout is later attached, the admissible statistic has the form
$$
\begin{aligned}
S_{f3,T_j} &> 0
\end{aligned}
$$
after the declared controls and rank checks.  Without such a nondegenerate same-row statistic, no translation realization is asserted here.

The broader powered rows give the allowed orientation.  The non-tRNA residual optimality row identifies $f_3\_\mathrm{translation\_selection}$ as the dominant non-tRNA residual optimality axis on $11$ computed organisms out of $16$ requested.  The two-axis closure test reports $\mathrm{third\_axis\_present}$ because $d_{\perp}$ has systematically positive held-out $R^2$ and mutually same-signed organism directions.  The third-axis identity row aligns $d_{\perp}$ to usage frequency within family with mean cosine $0.128$ and one-sided sign-test $p=0.000488$.  The third-axis mechanism row reports $\mathrm{selection\text{-}not\text{-}GC}$, not wobble-clustered, and requiring an external CSC or positional identity.  Finally, the boundary-set tRNA-poverty row reports that $R$ sense codons are systematically tRNA-poor under GtRNAdb tGCN plus dos Reis wobble weights, using $40$ computed organisms out of $46$ requested, $6$ skipped organisms, and $1000$ permutations.  These rows motivate a translation-facing residual search, but they do not turn the codon-pair refusal into a positive translation mechanism.

\paragraph{Cannot-claim boundary.}
The finite conclusion is deliberately narrow.  From the codon-pair row one may say that the powered fourth-mechanism identity test returned $\mathrm{not\_codon\_pair}$ under five-fold evaluation, $41$ synonymous design columns, a $500$-protein organism threshold, and a $50$-draw shuffle null.  One may also place that refusal beside the independently recorded exclusions of mRNA stability and translation accuracy, and beside the partial elongation-dwell row.  One may not infer from this chapter that $B^*_{Q6}$ changes ribosome occupancy, translation efficiency, protein abundance, folding, physical admissibility, localization, stability, function, phenotype, causation, or universality.  Those claims require their own layer-matched measurements and their own controlled finite statistics.  In particular, modeled tAI or stAI weights and codon-pair geometry are not ribosome-footprint observations, and a rank-degenerate all-$1.0$ surface on three organisms is a null boundary rather than survival evidence.
