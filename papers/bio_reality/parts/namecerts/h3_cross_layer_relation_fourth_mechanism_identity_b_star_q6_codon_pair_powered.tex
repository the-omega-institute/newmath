\subsection{Codon-pair exclusion for the fourth $B^*_{Q6}$ mechanism}\label{subsec:bior reality-q6-codon-pair-exclusion}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite carrier.}
This record concerns the finite coding-sequence residual surface obtained after amino-acid projection, restricted to the $B^*_{Q6}$ coordinate family and to rows for which organism-level codon usage, tRNA or wobble weights, and translation-layer readouts can be kept as distinct finite columns. The particular question tested here is not whether the $f_3$-stress coordinate exists as a sequence coordinate; it is whether the additional fourth-axis signal in the powered $B^*_{Q6}$ panel is explained by a codon-pair mechanism. The finite row used for this chapter has run id \texttt{b519b5ebd34d}, experiment id \texttt{b\_star\_q6\_fourth\_mechanism\_codon\_pair\_powered}, status \texttt{passed}, five folds, a minimum of $500$ proteins per organism, $41$ synonymous design columns, and $50$ shuffle-null replicates. Its recorded conclusion is \texttt{not\_codon\_pair}.

\paragraph{Coordinate boundary.}
The $f_3$ saturation row fixes the local codon vocabulary used by the residual construction. The row $M=\mathrm{Med}(R)\cup\mathrm{ACA}$ contains
$\{\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{ACA},\mathrm{ACG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\}$,
while the saturated $f_3$ row removes the two ACA/ACG entries and leaves
$\{\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\}$.
The recorded $f_3$ boundary set is empty. Thus the codon-pair test is not being asked to invent the residual coordinate; it is asked whether adjacent-codon context accounts for the already separated fourth mechanism.

\paragraph{Powered codon-pair test.}
Let $Q_{f3}$ denote the $f_3$-paired synonymous imbalance after amino-acid quotienting, and let $C_{pair}$ denote the finite codon-pair feature block evaluated against the same controlled synonymous design. The chapter-level decision is the sign of the held-out comparison between the fourth residual direction and the codon-pair block, under the fixed folds and shuffle-null scheme. The recorded row is summarized as
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{run} &= \texttt{b519b5ebd34d},\\
\mathrm{folds} &= 5,\\
\mathrm{synonymous\ design\ columns} &= 41,\\
\mathrm{shuffle\ nulls} &= 50,\\
\mathrm{minimum\ proteins\ per\ organism} &= 500,\\
\mathrm{decision} &= \texttt{not\_codon\_pair}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The finite verdict is therefore negative for codon-pair identity: in this powered assay, codon-pair context is not the recovered identity of the fourth $B^*_{Q6}$ mechanism.

\paragraph{Relation to the surrounding finite rows.}
The negative codon-pair call is not an isolated absence claim. The three-axis sufficiency row, run \texttt{2436cae9d455}, reports \texttt{fourth\_mechanism\_present} over $11$ computed organisms out of $16$ requested, with five folds, a $500$-protein minimum, and $41$ synonymous design columns. The axis-strength spectrum row, run \texttt{0146012d8909}, also uses $11$ computed organisms out of $16$ requested and records that $d_{resid4}$ leads the captured spectra on average, with per-organism dominant counts $\{\texttt{d\_resid4}:11\}$ and largest GC3 rank effect $\rho=0.136$ for $d_{usage}$. The codon-pair result therefore says that the observed fourth-axis presence is not identified with the codon-pair candidate in the tested finite design, not that the fourth axis disappears.

\paragraph{Mechanism comparison.}
The adjacent fourth-mechanism rows constrain the interpretation. The mRNA-stability assay, run \texttt{377abd67b8b3}, concluded \texttt{fourth\_axis\_not\_mrna\_stability}; the translation-accuracy assay, run \texttt{0dc44df59ba7}, concluded \texttt{not\_accuracy} with $11$ computed organisms out of $16$ requested and $200$ permutations; the elongation-dwell assay, run \texttt{e4edcde459b7}, returned \texttt{partial} for \textit{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}. Together with the codon-pair row, these facts leave the fourth mechanism as present but not explained by codon-pair context, not explained as the dominant mRNA-stability axis in the assayed yeast comparison, and not explained as translation accuracy in the powered panel. The partial elongation-dwell row remains a bounded candidate contact, not a general mechanism law.

\paragraph{Cross-layer readout condition.}
The $f_3$-stress coordinate becomes a biological readout only when a translation row is joined to the coordinate under the declared controls. Formally, a positive row would have to survive as a controlled entry such as $S_{f3,T_j}>0$ for tAI, stAI, ribosome occupancy, or translation efficiency after amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, length, M-density, mRNA, rank, and simpler-baseline controls. Without that joined row, the record is a finite residual-coordinate and mechanism-exclusion statement. The three-organism all-$1.0$ surface is treated as rank-degenerate and supplies no survival evidence. A modeled tAI or stAI asymmetry can bound a translation-weight readback, but it is not an observed ribosome-occupancy row by itself.

\paragraph{Local cannot-claim boundary.}
This chapter does not claim that $B^*_{Q6}$ or $f_3$-stress affects protein abundance, folding, physical admissibility, localization, molecular function, organismal phenotype, causation, mechanism, or universality. It also does not claim that codon topology, wobble-weight asymmetry, or a positive modeled-adaptation association alone proves translation realization. The only positive statement made here is finite and local: in the powered codon-pair assay with five folds, $41$ synonymous design columns, $50$ shuffle nulls, and a $500$-protein organism threshold, the fourth $B^*_{Q6}$ mechanism is classified as \texttt{not\_codon\_pair}. Any stronger biological interpretation requires a separate layer-matched contact and a recorded controlled statistic at that layer.
