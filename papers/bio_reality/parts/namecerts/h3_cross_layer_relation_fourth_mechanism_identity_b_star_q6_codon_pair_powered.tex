\subsection{Codon-pair refusal for the fourth residual mechanism}
\label{sec:bioreality-q6-codon-pair-fourth-mechanism}

\paragraph{Finite coordinate surface.}
The record concerns the $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinate surface on coding sequences, after amino-acid projection and after the usual organism-level codon summaries have been separated from translation readout rows. The finite stress coordinate used here is the $f_3$-paired synonymous imbalance coordinate: codons paired by the third-position operation are compared only after amino-acid identity has been quotiented away. In the saturation row, the marked set
$$
\begin{aligned}
M = \{&AAA,AAG,ACA,ACG,AGA,AGG,AUA,AUG,CUA,CUC,\\
     &CUG,CUU,UAA,UAG,UCA,UCG,UGA,UGG,UUA,UUG\}
\end{aligned}
$$
contains $20$ codons, while the $f_3$-saturated residual set deletes $ACA$ and $ACG$ and is
$$
\begin{aligned}
\operatorname{Sat}_{f_3}(R)=\{&AAA,AAG,AGA,AGG,AUA,AUG,CUA,CUC,CUG,CUU,\\
&UAA,UAG,UCA,UCG,UGA,UGG,UUA,UUG\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The boundary list for the $f_3$ operation is empty in the recorded row, so this packet is not using an unpaired boundary codon as the source of the effect. The biological question is narrower: whether the residual direction that remains after the lower axes have been fitted is explained by codon-pair usage, rather than by a translation-weight or dwell-related distinction.

\paragraph{Placement among nearby mechanism rows.}
The refusal is not isolated from the rest of the $B^*_{Q6}$ evidence table. The three-axis sufficiency row was passed on $11$ computed organisms out of $16$ requested organisms, again with $5$ folds, at least $500$ proteins per organism, and $41$ synonymous design columns, and its conclusion was \texttt{fourth\_mechanism\_present}. The axis-strength spectrum row used the same $11/16$ organism panel and recorded that $d\_{resid4}$ led the captured spectra on average, with per-organism dominant counts $\{d\_{resid4}:11\}$; the largest GC3 rank effect reported there was $\rho=0.136$ for $d\_{usage}$. These two rows say that a fourth residual direction is present and repeatedly visible in the finite panel. The codon-pair row says that this direction should not be named as a codon-pair mechanism on the present evidence.

\paragraph{Discriminating neighboring candidates.}
Other fourth-mechanism identity rows sharpen the local interpretation. The mRNA-stability assay, run as $b\_star\_q6\_fourth\_mechanism\_mrna\_stability\_powered$, passed with conclusion \texttt{fourth\_axis\_not\_mrna\_stability}; its reason states that yeast $d\_{opt\_halflife}$ has low alignment with the abundance-side $d\_{resid4}$ under the fixed gates. The translation-accuracy assay passed with conclusion \texttt{not\_accuracy}, using $11$ computed organisms out of $16$ requested, $200$ permutations, $5$ folds, and $41$ synonymous design columns. By contrast, the elongation-dwell row passed with conclusion \texttt{partial} in \textit{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}. The codon-pair refusal therefore has a precise role: it removes codon-pair structure from the fourth-axis name, while leaving open a bounded translation-dwell component where a separate row actually supports it.

\paragraph{Relation to the $f_3$ stress picture.}
The broader $f_3$ stress interpretation is also finite-row constrained. A separate third-axis identity row identified $d_{\perp}$ provisionally with usage frequency within synonymous family, reporting mean cosine $0.128$ and one-sided sign-test $p=0.000488$ across the powered organism panel. A boundary-set tRNA-poverty row computed $40$ organisms out of $46$ requested, skipped $6$, used $1000$ permutations at $\alpha=0.05$, and recorded that $R$ sense codons are systematically tRNA-poor under GtRNAdb tRNA gene-copy numbers plus dos Reis wobble weights. These facts make the $f_3$ and tRNA-weight setting scientifically relevant, but they do not turn codon-pair imbalance into the fourth mechanism. In this chapter the codon-pair explanation is specifically the rejected candidate.

\paragraph{Readout boundary.}
The admissible readback is bounded to the translation row actually supplied by a matching contact, such as tAI, stAI, ribosome occupancy, or translation efficiency, and only after amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, CDS length, $M$-density, mRNA control, rank, and simpler-baseline controls have been handled. The present codon-pair assay supplies a mechanism-identity refusal, not a direct measured translation-survival matrix. In symbols, the downstream claim would require a non-vacuous controlled entry
$$
\begin{aligned}
S_{f_3\text{-stress},T_j} > 0,
\end{aligned}
$$
for an explicitly named translation readout $T_j$. The recorded codon-pair row does not by itself provide such an entry, and the three-organism all-$1.0$ rank-saturated matrix remains a null boundary rather than survival evidence.

\paragraph{Cannot-claim boundary.}
No statement in this chapter licenses a claim about protein abundance, folded structure, localization, stability, molecular function, physical admissibility, phenotype, mechanism beyond the assayed rows, or a universal biological law. Codon-pair non-identity is a finite negative call inside a controlled residual-coordinate assay. Translation realization still requires a same-organism or same-CDS translation contact and a positive controlled survival statistic. Folding, physical admissibility, function, localization, stability, phenotype, and universality each require their own layer-matched measurement rather than an inference from $B^*_{Q6}$ geometry, tAI or stAI weights, ribosome-contact naming, mRNA controls, or codon-pair topology.
