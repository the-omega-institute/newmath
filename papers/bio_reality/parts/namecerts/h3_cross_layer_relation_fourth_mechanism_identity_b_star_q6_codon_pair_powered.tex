\paragraph{Powered codon-pair test.}
The codon-pair mechanism assay was run as experiment \texttt{b\_star\_q6\_fourth\_mechanism\_codon\_pair\_powered} with run identifier \texttt{b519b5ebd34d}. The finite design used $41$ synonymous design columns, required at least $500$ proteins per organism, used $5$ folds, and compared the candidate codon-pair signal against $50$ shuffle-null draws. The recorded status is \texttt{passed}, and the recorded conclusion is \texttt{not\_codon\_pair}. In the local notation of this chapter, if $Q_{f_3}$ denotes the B$^*_{Q6}$ $f_3$-stress residual coordinate and $C_{\mathrm{pair}}$ denotes the codon-pair candidate readout, the assay rejects the interpretation
$$
\begin{aligned}
Q_{f_3} \leadsto C_{\mathrm{pair}}
\end{aligned}
$$
as the identity of the powered fourth mechanism under the fixed finite-row design. The result is not a statement that codon-pair context is biologically absent. It is narrower: within this controlled B$^*_{Q6}$ residual assay, codon-pair structure did not supply the fourth-axis identity.

\paragraph{Relation to the fourth-axis need.}
The exclusion is meaningful because the companion finite-row sufficiency result records a remaining fourth-mechanism requirement: the three-axis optimality test, run with $41$ synonymous design columns, $5$ folds, at least $500$ proteins per organism, and $11$ computed organisms out of $16$ requested organisms, concluded \texttt{fourth\_mechanism\_present}. Therefore the codon-pair test is not eliminating a redundant candidate after the surface has already closed. It is separating one proposed identity from a still-present fourth direction. The local logical form is
$$
\begin{aligned}
\text{three-axis closure is insufficient} \quad &\wedge \quad \text{codon-pair candidate fails the fourth-axis identity test}\\
&\Longrightarrow \quad \text{the remaining fourth direction is not identified by this codon-pair assay.}
\end{aligned}
$$
This keeps the row-level conclusion finite and negative: the powered panel supports a codon-pair exclusion, not a replacement mechanism.

\paragraph{Translation-readout gate.}
The broader biological conjecture asks whether the $f_3$-paired synonymous imbalance survives as a translation-readout distinction only where tRNA or wobble weights break third-position $f_3$ symmetry. That question requires a non-vacuous controlled survival entry $S_{f_3\text{-stress},T_j}$ for a translation row $T_j$, such as tAI, stAI, ribosome occupancy, or translation efficiency. The codon-pair exclusion does not by itself provide such an entry. In particular, the finite result \texttt{not\_codon\_pair} cannot be promoted to translation realization, ribosome dynamics, protein abundance, folded structure, physical admissibility, molecular function, organismal phenotype, a mechanism claim, or a universal biological law. Those claims require their own layer-matched contact rows and their own controlled statistics.

\paragraph{Local verdict.}
The chapter-level readback is therefore an exclusion record. On the saturated $f_3$ codon support, with the empty boundary set and a powered finite design using $41$ synonymous columns, $5$ folds, a $500$-protein organism floor, and $50$ shuffle-null comparisons, codon-pair context is not the observed identity of the fourth B$^*_{Q6}$ mechanism in this assay. The result leaves open the translation-readout conjecture and the identity of the remaining fourth direction; it only rules out this codon-pair candidate under the recorded finite-row conditions.
