\subsection{Codon-pair exclusion for the fourth $B^*_{Q6}$ mechanism}
\label{sec:bioq6-codon-pair-fourth-mechanism}

\paragraph{Finite row.}
This record tests whether the fourth residual mechanism detected on the $B^*_{Q6}$ coding-sequence surface is accounted for by codon-pair structure. The carrier is the finite CDS-by-organism residual coordinate surface restricted to the $f_3$-stress and matched synonymous-design rows: the computation uses $41$ synonymous design columns, requires at least $500$ proteins per organism, and evaluates the codon-pair candidate under $5$ folds with $50$ shuffle-null replicates. The resulting finite row is passed with experiment run \texttt{b519b5ebd34d}; its recorded conclusion is \texttt{not\_codon\_pair}. Thus the codon-pair hypothesis is not promoted to the identity of the fourth residual mechanism in this powered assay.

\paragraph{Coordinate boundary.}
The tested coordinate sits after amino-acid quotienting. It is not a raw amino-acid composition effect and it is not a direct codon-count table. The relevant $f_3$ saturation memory fixes the finite codon surface: the set $M=\operatorname{Med}(R)\cup\{\mathrm{ACA}\}$ is
$$
\begin{aligned}
M=\{&\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{ACA},\mathrm{ACG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\\
&\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The saturated $f_3$ image removes the two explicit boundary codons $\mathrm{ACA}$ and $\mathrm{ACG}$, giving
$$
\begin{aligned}
\operatorname{Sat}_{f_3}(R)=\{&\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\\
&\mathrm{CUU},\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\},
\end{aligned}
$$
with empty recorded $f_3$ boundary. The codon-pair test therefore acts on a residual surface whose finite support is already organized by this $f_3$ closure geometry; a positive codon-pair result would have had to explain the fourth mechanism beyond amino-acid identity and the baseline codon-coordinate controls.

\paragraph{Decision statistic.}
The codon-pair assay is a mechanism-identification test, not a discovery of a downstream phenotype. In the available row the assay status is \texttt{passed}, the shuffle-null count is $50$, and the conclusion is negative for codon-pair identity. The local interpretation is therefore
$$
\begin{aligned}
\text{fourth mechanism} \neq \text{codon-pair mechanism}
\end{aligned}
$$
within the declared finite design, not in every conceivable organism, assay platform, or codon-pair parametrization. The powered row is strong enough to block codon-pair promotion for this packet because the same coordinate family already shows that a fourth mechanism is present: the three-axis sufficiency assay records \texttt{fourth\_mechanism\_present} across $11$ computed organisms out of $16$ requested, again with $5$ folds, at least $500$ proteins per organism, and $41$ synonymous design columns.

\paragraph{Neighboring mechanism rows.}
The exclusion is sharper when read against the companion finite rows. The per-organism axis-strength spectrum computes $11$ organisms out of $16$ requested and reports $5$ folds, $41$ synonymous design columns, and a descriptive dominance count of $\{\texttt{d\_resid4}:11\}$, with $\texttt{d\_resid4}$ leading on average before $\texttt{d\_tRNA}$. Domain means are explicitly descriptive because the panel is imbalanced with $1$ archaeon, $2$ bacteria, and $8$ eukaryotes; the largest GC3 rank effect recorded there is $\rho=0.136$ for $\texttt{d\_usage}$. The mRNA-stability row, also run with $5$ folds, at least $500$ proteins per organism, and $41$ synonymous design columns, concludes \texttt{fourth\_axis\_not\_mrna\_stability}. The translation-accuracy row computes $11$ organisms out of $16$ requested, uses $200$ permutations, and concludes \texttt{not\_accuracy}. By contrast, the elongation-dwell row is only \texttt{partial} and is organism-scoped to \textit{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}. These finite outcomes leave the fourth mechanism present but not identified as codon pair, not identified as mRNA stability in the tested abundance--half-life alignment, and not identified as translation accuracy in the powered accuracy assay.

\paragraph{Relation to the translation-selection axis.}
The broader residual-basis memory gives the negative codon-pair call a specific location. The non-tRNA residual optimality row computes $11$ organisms out of $16$ requested and records \texttt{f3\_translation\_selection} as the dominant axis, with $5$ folds, at least $500$ proteins per organism, and $41$ synonymous design columns. The third-axis identity row identifies $d_{\perp}$ with usage frequency within family, with mean cosine $0.128$ and one-sided sign-test $p=0.000488$. The corresponding mechanism row reports that GC-orthogonalized $d_{\perp}$ keeps significant cross-species alignment and held-out $R^2$, concluding \texttt{selection-not-GC; not-wobble-clustered; CSC/positional identity needs\_external}. The codon-pair result must be read within that landscape: the residual geometry contains selection-like and position-sensitive structure, but the fourth mechanism is not explained by the codon-pair candidate tested here.

\paragraph{Readout boundary.}
No translation-realization claim follows from the codon-pair exclusion alone. The available finite row says that the fourth residual mechanism is not codon-pair identity under the stated powered design. It does not by itself establish ribosome occupancy, translation efficiency, protein abundance, localization, folding, stability, molecular function, phenotype, physical admissibility, or a universal biological rule. A translation row would require same-organism or same-CDS codon counts, tRNA or wobble weights, a direct translation readout such as tAI, stAI, ribosome occupancy, or translation efficiency, matched mRNA control where TE is used, and an explicitly controlled survival statistic after amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, CDS length, $M$-density, rank, and simpler-baseline controls. Without such a non-vacuous positive survival entry, the result remains a bounded mechanism-exclusion statement on the residual coordinate surface.

\paragraph{Local conclusion.}
The finite evidence supports the following local statement: in the powered $B^*_{Q6}$ fourth-mechanism assay with $5$ folds, $50$ shuffle-null replicates, at least $500$ proteins per organism, and $41$ synonymous design columns, the codon-pair candidate is rejected as the identity of the fourth mechanism. This does not erase the fourth mechanism; the companion sufficiency and spectrum rows keep it present. It narrows the interpretation by removing codon-pair identity from the admissible mechanism set for this coordinate packet while preserving the requirement that any biological readout beyond the residual surface be supplied by its own matched external assay and controlled statistic.
