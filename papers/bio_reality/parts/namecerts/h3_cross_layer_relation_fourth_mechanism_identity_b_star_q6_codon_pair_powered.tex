\paragraph{Translation-facing boundary.}
The biologically relevant readout for this chapter is not downstream protein state.  A positive biological translation statement would require a non-vacuous survival entry $S_{f3\text{-}\mathrm{stress},T_j}$ for a translation-facing row such as tAI, stAI, ribosome occupancy, or translation efficiency after amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, length, M-density, and mRNA controls.  The present codon-pair row does not provide that survival entry; it only rejects one candidate identity for the fourth mechanism.  The tRNA-poverty row supplies a separate finite mechanism contact: $40$ organisms computed out of $46$ requested, $6$ skipped, $1000$ permutations, and $\alpha = 0.05$, with the call that $R$ sense codons are systematically tRNA-poor under GtRNAdb tGCN plus dos Reis wobble weights.  That supports a tRNA-supply boundary for $R$ avoidance, but it does not turn the codon-pair exclusion into a measured ribosome-occupancy or translation-efficiency survival result.

The universal residual-axis row said that non-tRNA residual optimality aligns most with $f_{3}$ translation selection, and the two-axis closure row found $\mathrm{third\_axis\_present}$ because $d_{\perp}$ kept systematically positive held-out $R^{2}$ and mutually same-signed directions across organisms.  These rows place the codon-pair exclusion inside a larger finite residual spectrum: the remaining signal is real enough to survive the model screens, but this chapter does not identify it as codon-pair power.

\paragraph{Local refusal of overclaiming.}
The conclusion is therefore a negative mechanism identity statement: in the powered fourth-mechanism screen, the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual fourth direction is not explained as codon-pair signal under the finite design just described.  Nothing in this chapter establishes translation realization, ribosome occupancy, protein abundance, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, organismal phenotype, causal mechanism, or universality.  Those stronger statements would require their own layer-matched contacts and controlled finite statistics.  In particular, modeled tAI or stAI weights are bounded codon-adaptation readbacks unless joined to measured translation rows, and a rank-degenerate all-$1.0$ surface in a three-organism panel is a null boundary rather than survival evidence.
