\paragraph{Finite coordinate under test.}
This chapter records the finite codon-pair test for the B*\_Q6 fourth-mechanism identity. The carrier is the finite CDS-by-organism residual coordinate surface restricted to synonymous codon contrasts after amino-acid projection, with the f\_3-stress coordinate kept separate from any downstream readout. \begin{aligned}

$$
\begin{aligned}

Thus the mechanism question is not whether the f\_3 coordinate exists as a finite residual coordinate; it is whether codon-pair context supplies the empirical identity of the fourth residual mechanism after the usual amino-acid and sequence-composition baselines have been imposed.

\paragraph{Powered codon-pair assay.}
The codon-pair mechanism assay is the finite row with run id $\mathrm{b519b5ebd34d}$. It was recorded as passed, used $5$ folds, required at least $500$ proteins per organism, used $41$ synonymous design columns, and compared against $50$ shuffle-null draws. Its mechanism conclusion is the negative call
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{conclusion} = \mathrm{not\_codon\_pair}.
\end{aligned}
$$
This is a substantive identity refusal. The fourth residual component is present elsewhere in the finite record, since the three-axis sufficiency assay reports $\mathrm{fourth\_mechanism\_present}$ with $5$ folds, $11$ computed organisms out of $16$ requested organisms, and the same $41$ synonymous design columns. However, the codon-pair assay does not identify that fourth component with codon-pair power under its powered comparison. In the same surrounding finite panel, the axis-strength spectrum also used $5$ folds, $11$ computed organisms out of $16$ requested organisms, and $41$ synonymous design columns; all $11$ computed organisms were led by $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$ in the recorded dominant-count summary, while the domain counts were imbalanced with $1$ archaeal, $2$ bacterial, and $8$ eukaryotic organisms. That spectrum says that the fourth residual direction is empirically visible in the panel, but the codon-pair row says that its identity is not codon-pair structure in this assay.

\paragraph{Relation to neighboring mechanism tests.}
The codon-pair refusal is not an isolated absence of data. Neighboring fourth-mechanism rows separate several candidate identities. The mRNA-stability row passed with $5$ folds, at least $500$ proteins per organism, and $41$ synonymous design columns, and concluded $\mathrm{fourth\_axis\_not\_mrna\_stability}$. The translation-accuracy row passed with $5$ folds, $11$ computed organisms out of $16$ requested organisms, $200$ permutations, and $41$ synonymous design columns, and concluded $\mathrm{not\_accuracy}$. The elongation-dwell row, restricted to $\mathrm{saccharomyces\_cerevisiae}$, passed with $5$ folds and concluded $\mathrm{partial}$. Taken together, these finite rows place the codon-pair candidate in a specific logical position: codon-pair context is rejected as the powered identity of the fourth mechanism, mRNA stability and translation accuracy are also rejected in their assays, and elongation dwell remains only partial rather than a completed identification.

\paragraph{Readout discipline.}
The negative codon-pair call does not erase the f\_3-stress coordinate, and it does not by itself create a positive translation claim. A bounded translation readout would require a non-vacuous survival entry $S_{f_3\text{-stress},T_j}$ for a declared translation layer such as tAI, stAI, ribosome occupancy, or translation efficiency, after controls for amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, CDS length, M-density baseline, matched mRNA abundance where applicable, rank sufficiency, and simpler codon baselines. A rank-degenerate all-$1.0$ surface over three organisms would remain a null boundary rather than survival. Likewise, a modeled tAI or stAI weight is a bounded codon-adaptation readback, not an observed ribosome-footprint row. The finite codon-pair result should therefore be read as a mechanism-identity refusal inside the B*\_Q6 residual panel: it says that codon-pair power is not the fourth-mechanism identity in the powered assay, while leaving the translation-readout question dependent on separate joined rows and an explicit controlled statistic.

\paragraph{Local boundary.}
No claim about translation realization, ribosome dynamics, protein abundance, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, phenotype, mechanism, or universality follows from the codon-pair refusal alone. Those layers require their own organism-matched contacts and their own finite statistics. The present chapter only supports the following local conclusion: within the recorded powered B*\_Q6 fourth-mechanism comparison, the codon-pair candidate is rejected, with $5$ folds, a $500$-protein minimum, $41$ synonymous design columns, and $50$ shuffle-null draws, while the fourth residual direction itself remains part of the broader finite residual spectrum.
