\subsection{Codon-pair exclusion for the fourth Q6 mechanism}
\label{sec:bioreality-q6-codon-pair-exclusion}

\paragraph{Finite coordinate surface.}
This record concerns the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual surface after amino-acid projection, with attention to the $f_3$-stress coordinate and its possible passage into translation-layer readouts. The finite codon base used by the packet has the union carrier
$$
\begin{aligned}
M &= \{\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{ACA},\mathrm{ACG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\\
&\qquad \mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The $f_3$ saturation removes the two boundary codons $\mathrm{ACA}$ and $\mathrm{ACG}$, leaving
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{Sat}_{f_3}(R) &= \{\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\\
&\qquad \mathrm{CUU},\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\},
\end{aligned}
$$
and the recorded $f_3$ boundary residual is empty. Thus the finite row is not a free-form biological narrative: it is a bounded residual-coordinate test over a named codon carrier, with the codon-pair question asked only after synonymous and amino-acid controls have been imposed.

\paragraph{Powered codon-pair test.}
The powered assay for the claim \texttt{h3.cross\_layer\_relation.fourth\_mechanism\_identity.b\_star\_q6\_codon\_pair\_powered} returned status \texttt{passed} with conclusion \texttt{not\_codon\_pair}. The run identifier is \texttt{b519b5ebd34d}; the experiment identifier is \texttt{b\_star\_q6\_fourth\_mechanism\_codon\_pair\_powered}. The design used $5$ folds, at least $500$ proteins per organism, $41$ synonymous-design columns, and $50$ shuffle-null replicates. In the finite-row reading, this is a negative identity result: the residual fourth signal present in the surrounding Q6 axis analysis is not identified with the codon-pair mechanism under this codon-pair assay.

\paragraph{Relation to the neighboring mechanism rows.}
The codon-pair exclusion is meaningful because the same finite dossier contains positive or discriminating structure around it. The three-axis sufficiency row, computed on $11$ of $16$ requested organisms with $5$ folds and $41$ synonymous-design columns, reported \texttt{fourth\_mechanism\_present}. The per-organism axis-strength spectrum was also computed on $11$ of $16$ requested organisms, with $5$ folds and a $500$-protein minimum, and reported that $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$ led the captured spectra on average and dominated all $11$ per-organism calls; the same row recorded $d_{\mathrm{tRNA}}$ next and a largest GC3 rank effect of $\rho=0.136$ for $d_{\mathrm{usage}}$. These facts make the codon-pair row a targeted exclusion rather than absence of any fourth residual structure. Other fourth-mechanism probes sharpen the boundary: the mRNA-stability row concluded \texttt{fourth\_axis\_not\_mrna\_stability}; the translation-accuracy row concluded \texttt{not\_accuracy}; the elongation-dwell row in \textit{Saccharomyces cerevisiae} concluded \texttt{partial}. The finite readback is therefore: codon-pair structure is not the identified fourth mechanism in this powered test, while the wider residual family still requires a separate translation-layer row before being treated as observed translation biology.

\paragraph{Third-axis and residual context.}
The third-axis rows give additional scale to the codon-pair exclusion. The $d_{\perp}$ identity assay, again on $11$ of $16$ requested organisms with $5$ folds, $41$ synonymous-design columns, and a $500$-protein minimum, aligned $d_{\perp}$ to usage frequency within family with mean cosine $0.128$ and one-sided sign-test $p=0.000488$. The mechanism row for $d_{\perp}$ reported \texttt{selection-not-GC; not-wobble-clustered; CSC/positional identity needs\_external}, with the reason that GC-orthogonalized $d_{\perp}$ kept significant cross-species alignment and held-out $R^2$. This matters for the codon-pair chapter because it prevents a collapse of all residual structure into an unconstrained codon-pair story. The finite rows separate at least three statements: a usage-frequency-associated third axis, a present fourth residual mechanism, and a failed codon-pair identity call for that fourth mechanism.

\paragraph{Translation-readout gate.}
For the $f_3$-stress conjectural interface, the only admissible biological readback is a controlled survival entry $S_{f3\text{-}stress,T_j}$ for a translation-layer row such as tAI, stAI, ribosome occupancy, or translation efficiency. The present codon-pair run does not itself supply such a same-row translation statistic. A valid positive readout would require organism- and CDS-matched codon counts, wobble or tRNA weights when used, a direct translation readout or explicitly bounded modeled translation score, and matched mRNA controls. The statistic must survive amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, CDS length, $M$-density baseline, rank, and simpler-baseline controls. In particular, a rank-degenerate all-$1.0$ three-organism matrix is a null boundary for this purpose, not a survival result.

\paragraph{Local cannot-claim boundary.}
This chapter supports only the finite codon-pair exclusion stated above. It does not show that $f_3$-stress affects translation, ribosome occupancy, protein abundance, folding, physical admissibility, localization, stability, molecular function, phenotype, mechanism, or universality. tAI or stAI weights remain modeled codon-adaptation readbacks unless calibrated against measured translation rows. Ribosome-profiling or translation-efficiency contacts become evidential only after same-organism and same-CDS joining with the declared controls. Matched mRNA abundance is a covariate for separating transcription from translation; by itself it is not a translation-output observation. Thus the local conclusion is deliberately narrow: within the powered Q6 fourth-mechanism identity assay, codon-pair structure is excluded as the identified fourth mechanism, while every higher biological interpretation remains blocked until supplied by a separate layer-matched contact and a nondegenerate controlled survival statistic.
