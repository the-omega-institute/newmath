\subsection{Codon-pair exclusion for the fourth $B^{*}_{Q6}$ mechanism}\label{subsec:bioreality-q6-codon-pair-not-identity}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite carrier.}
This record concerns a finite coding-sequence surface indexed by organism, controlled synonymous residual coordinates, and matched translation-layer readout classes. The coordinate of interest is the $f_{3}$-stress component of $B^{*}_{Q6}$ after amino-acid projection. Its finite saturation background is not an open-ended codon vocabulary: the marked set $M = \mathrm{Med}(R) \cup \{\mathrm{ACA}\}$ is
$$
\begin{aligned}
M = \{&\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{ACA},\mathrm{ACG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\\
&\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The $f_{3}$-saturated $R$ set removes the ACA/ACG pair and is
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{Sat}_{f_{3}}(R)=\{&\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\\
&\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The finite boundary is empty in the recorded saturation check, $\partial_{f_{3}}R=\varnothing$. Thus the codon-pair question is asked on a closed finite codon surface rather than on an unspecified genomic universe.

\paragraph{Tested identity.}
The candidate identity under audit was that the fourth residual mechanism in the powered $B^{*}_{Q6}$ panel is explained by codon-pair context. The powered codon-pair experiment used a deterministic seed, five folds, a minimum of 500 proteins per organism, 41 synonymous design columns, and 50 shuffled null draws. Its recorded status is passed, but its biological call is negative: the conclusion is \texttt{not\_codon\_pair}. In mathematical shorthand, the tested surface distinguishes the residual fourth axis $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$ from the codon-pair candidate $c_{\mathrm{pair}}$ under the declared controls:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\operatorname{Id}(d_{\mathrm{resid4}},c_{\mathrm{pair}}) = 0
\end{aligned}
$$
where the equality is an empirical finite-row verdict, not a theorem about all organisms or all possible coding sequences. The experiment therefore excludes codon-pair context as the dominant identity of the fourth mechanism in this assay; it does not exclude weaker codon-pair effects elsewhere.

\paragraph{Relation to the surrounding powered panel.}
The negative codon-pair call is sharpened by the other finite rows. The three-axis sufficiency test on 11 computed organisms out of 16 requested organisms, with five folds, at least 500 proteins per organism, and 41 synonymous design columns, concluded that a fourth mechanism is present. The axis-strength spectrum on the same 11-of-16 organism panel reported that captured spectra are led on average by $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$, followed by the tRNA axis, with per-organism dominant counts $\{d_{\mathrm{resid4}}:11\}$. Domain means were explicitly descriptive under the imbalanced domain count $\{\mathrm{archaea}:1,\mathrm{bacteria}:2,\mathrm{eukaryote}:8\}$, and the largest GC3 rank effect was small, $\rho=0.136$ for the usage axis. These numbers make the codon-pair exclusion nontrivial: the fourth axis is not absent from the finite panel, but the codon-pair candidate fails as its identity.

\paragraph{Mechanism boundary.}
The same panel separates several nearby explanations. The mRNA-stability identity test passed with the finite call \texttt{fourth\_axis\_not\_mrna\_stability}; in that assay yeast $d_{\mathrm{opt\_halflife}}$ had low alignment with abundance $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$. The translation-accuracy identity test passed with the call \texttt{not\_accuracy} on 11 computed organisms out of 16 requested organisms, using 200 permutations, five folds, and 41 synonymous design columns. The elongation-dwell row is different: it passed with a \texttt{partial} conclusion for \textit{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}. Thus the finite mechanism ledger reads as a constrained exclusion pattern: codon-pair identity is rejected, mRNA stability is rejected as dominant in its assay, translation accuracy is rejected, and elongation dwell remains only partially aligned in one organism-specific row.

\paragraph{Translation-readout gate.}
The residual coordinate becomes biologically interpretable only where an organism-matched translation row exists. The declared readout family is bounded to tAI, stAI, ribosome occupancy, or translation efficiency after controls for amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, CDS length, $M$-density, and mRNA abundance where the readout is TE-like. A modeled tAI or stAI weight can be used as a bounded translation-adaptation surface, but it is not an observed ribosome-footprint measurement. Likewise, a ribosome-profiling contact name is not itself a computed survival statistic. The survival entry for the $f_{3}$-stress coordinate is the controlled value $S_{f_{3}\text{-}\mathrm{stress},T_{j}}$; without a non-vacuous positive entry, the coordinate remains a residual geometric signal rather than a realized translation claim.

\paragraph{Local cannot-claim boundary.}
This chapter makes one positive finite-row statement and several negative exclusions. The positive statement is that a fourth residual mechanism is present in the powered panel, while the codon-pair candidate is not its identity in the recorded experiment. The chapter does not assert protein abundance, folded structure, physical admissibility, localization, molecular function, phenotype, causation, or a universal biological law. No such assertion follows from codon usage, tRNA copy spectra, tAI or stAI weights, ribosome-contact routing, or the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ coordinate alone. Any translation, protein, structure, function, mechanism, or universality claim requires its own layer-matched finite contact and its own controlled statistic.
