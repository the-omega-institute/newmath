\paragraph{Codon-pair decision.}
Let $Q$ denote the finite $B^*_{Q6}$ residual design and let $C_{pair}$ denote the candidate codon-pair readout block used by the powered discriminant.  The codon-pair identity claim would require the fourth residual direction to survive as a codon-pair effect after the finite synonymous design, folds, and shuffle-null comparison are imposed.  The recorded decision is the opposite:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{FourthMechanism}(B^*_{Q6}) \neq C_{pair}
\end{aligned}
$$
within the tested finite row.  The statement is deliberately narrow.  It says that the codon-pair block is not the identified fourth mechanism in this assay; it does not say that codon-pair context is absent from coding biology, that local codon adjacency is irrelevant in every organism, or that every possible windowed pair model has been ruled out.  The claim is only the finite powered refusal attached to the run above.

\paragraph{Translation-readout boundary.}
The $f_3$-stress conjecture remains a translation-readout boundary problem rather than a codon-pair identity theorem.  A positive biological readback would require same-organism or same-CDS rows joining codon counts, wobble or tRNA-weight asymmetry, a bounded translation readout such as tAI, stAI, ribosome occupancy, or translation efficiency, and matched mRNA controls.  The survival statistic must be computed after amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, CDS length, $M$-density, rank, matched mRNA, and simpler-baseline controls.  In the notation of the packet, the desired survival row has the form
$$
\begin{aligned}
S_{f_3\text{-stress},T_j}>0,
\end{aligned}
$$
for an explicitly named translation readout $T_j$.  The codon-pair experiment does not supply such a row; it only rejects codon-pair identity for the fourth mechanism in the powered finite assay.

\paragraph{Local cannot-claim boundary.}
No observed ribosome motion, translation-efficiency survival, protein abundance, folded structure, localization, stability, molecular function, phenotype, physical admissibility, general mechanism, or universal biological rule follows from this codon-pair refusal.  Codon usage, tRNA-copy summaries, and tAI or stAI weights can bound a translation-layer question only when kept as separate empirical contacts.  A downstream assertion requires its own layer-matched rows and its own controlled statistic.  The present chapter therefore records a specific finite conclusion: within the powered $B^*_{Q6}$ fourth-mechanism assay, codon-pair structure is not the identified mechanism, while the translation-readout survival question remains open until a nondegenerate controlled $S_{f_3\text{-stress},T_j}$ row is attached.
