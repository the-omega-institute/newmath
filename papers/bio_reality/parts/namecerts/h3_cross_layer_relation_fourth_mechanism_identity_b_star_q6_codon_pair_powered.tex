\subsection{Codon-pair refusal for the fourth residual mechanism}
\label{sec:bior reality-q6-codon-pair-refusal}

\paragraph{Finite subject.}
The record considered here is the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ fourth-mechanism codon-pair test on a finite coding-sequence surface.  The carrier is not a protein phenotype, a folded-state readout, or a direct ribosome trace.  It is a controlled residual coordinate surface on synonymous coding data: coding sequences are grouped by organism, projected through amino-acid composition controls, and tested against a candidate codon-pair explanation for the residual axis left after the stronger translation-adaptation and residual-optimality axes have been accounted for.  The powered codon-pair assay used $5$ folds, required at least $500$ proteins per organism, used $41$ synonymous design columns, and compared the fitted signal against $50$ shuffle-null replicates.  Its finite conclusion is \emph{not codon pair}: the codon-pair surface does not identify the fourth residual mechanism in this assay.

\paragraph{Underlying codon boundary.}
The finite $f_3$ saturation calculation fixes the codon support on which the residual discussion is allowed to live.  The ambient set $M=\operatorname{Med}(R)\cup\{\mathrm{ACA}\}$ is
$\{\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{ACA},\mathrm{ACG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\}$.
After $f_3$ saturation of $R$, the retained set is
$\{\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\}$,
with $\mathrm{ACA}$ and $\mathrm{ACG}$ absent from the saturated residual set and with empty $f_3$ boundary.  Thus the codon-pair question is not asked on an open-ended codon universe; it is asked after a finite amino-acid-quotiented residual construction has already determined which third-position stress coordinates remain visible.

\paragraph{Local test statistic.}
The codon-pair candidate is treated as a proposed identity for the fourth residual component rather than as a general law of coding sequence organization.  In the finite assay the relevant comparison can be summarized as follows:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\text{design columns} &= 41,\\
\text{folds} &= 5,\\
\text{minimum proteins per organism} &= 500,\\
\text{shuffle-null replicates} &= 50,\\
\text{finite call} &= \text{not\_codon\_pair}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The entry is therefore a negative identification result.  It does not say that codon-pair context is biologically meaningless, and it does not exclude codon-pair effects in other assays.  It says that, under the declared finite residual design and its shuffle-null comparison, codon-pair structure is not the powered identity of the fourth $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual mechanism.

\paragraph{Position among neighboring residual tests.}
The negative codon-pair call is consistent with the surrounding finite-row picture.  The three-axis sufficiency test reports that a fourth mechanism remains present on the powered panel, using $5$ folds, $41$ synonymous design columns, and $11$ computed organisms out of $16$ requested.  The per-organism axis-strength spectrum also computed $11$ organisms and found $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$ leading on average, with per-organism dominant counts $\{d_{\mathrm{resid4}}:11\}$; the domain counts were imbalanced, with $1$ archaeal, $2$ bacterial, and $8$ eukaryotic organisms, so those domain means are descriptive only.  The largest GC3 rank effect in that spectrum was reported for $d_{\mathrm{usage}}$ with $\rho=0.136$.  In the same neighborhood, the universal residual-optimality assay identified the dominant non-tRNA residual axis as $f_3$ translation selection, while the third-axis identity assay aligned $d_{\perp}$ to usage frequency within family with mean cosine $0.128$ and one-sided sign-test $p=0.000488$.  These facts locate the codon-pair refusal: the fourth residual slot is real enough to be tested, but the codon-pair candidate fails this particular identity test.

\paragraph{Competing fourth-mechanism rows.}
Other finite mechanism rows do not rescue a codon-pair interpretation.  The mRNA-stability assay concluded \emph{fourth-axis not mRNA stability}, because the yeast $d_{\mathrm{opt\_halflife}}$ direction had low alignment with abundance $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$ under the fixed gates.  The translation-accuracy assay, run with $5$ folds, $41$ synonymous design columns, $11$ computed organisms out of $16$ requested, and $200$ permutations, concluded \emph{not accuracy}.  The elongation-dwell assay in \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae} concluded \emph{partial}.  The resulting local map is sharp: codon-pair identity is refused, mRNA-stability identity is refused, translation-accuracy identity is refused, and elongation-dwell remains only partial.  The finite data therefore support a constrained exclusion statement, not a completed positive naming of the fourth mechanism.

\paragraph{Readout boundary.}
This chapter makes no downstream biological promotion from the codon-pair result.  A negative codon-pair identity call is not a translation-efficiency measurement, not a ribosome-occupancy measurement, not a protein-abundance measurement, and not evidence about protein folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, localization, stability, phenotype, mechanism in vivo, or universality.  Any such statement would require its own layer-matched contact: for example organism- and CDS-matched translation readouts, matched mRNA controls, proteomics rows, localization rows, structure rows, perturbation rows, or functional assays, together with the declared covariates.  In particular, modeled tAI or stAI weights may serve only as bounded adaptation-weight readbacks unless joined measured translation rows and controls are present.

\paragraph{Verdict.}
The finite row is a ready negative chapter.  The powered codon-pair assay passed its computational gate and returned \emph{not codon pair}.  The correct scientific wording is therefore: codon-pair context is excluded as the identified fourth $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual mechanism under the stated finite design, but the fourth residual problem itself remains open to other bounded readouts, with elongation dwell currently only partially aligned and with all downstream biological interpretations blocked until separate measured contacts supply them.
