\subsection{Codon-Pair Refusal for the Fourth Residual Mechanism}\label{sec:bioreality-q6-codon-pair-fourth-mechanism-refusal}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite surface.}
This record concerns the finite coding-sequence residual surface obtained after amino-acid projection, organism stratification, and the restriction to the $B^*_{Q6}$ coordinate family. The tested row asks whether the fourth residual mechanism detected in the powered $Q6$ panel is explained by synonymous codon-pair context. The finite codon support is anchored by the $f_3$ saturation row. The carrier set $M=\operatorname{Med}(R)\cup\operatorname{ACA}$ is the twenty-codon list
$$
\begin{aligned}
M=\{&\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{ACA},\mathrm{ACG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\\
&\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The $f_3$-saturated $R$ face removes exactly $\mathrm{ACA}$ and $\mathrm{ACG}$ from this support, giving the eighteen-codon row
$$
\begin{aligned}
\operatorname{Sat}_{f_3}(R)=\{&\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\\
&\mathrm{CUU},\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\},
\end{aligned}
$$
with empty $f_3$ boundary. Thus the codon-pair test is not a free-floating dinucleotide or adjacency claim; it is a finite residual-coordinate test on a fixed amino-acid-quotiented codon surface.

\paragraph{Powered codon-pair test.}
The codon-pair mechanism row was run as experiment $\mathrm{b\_star\_q6\_fourth\_mechanism\_codon\_pair\_powered}$ with run id $\mathrm{b519b5ebd34d}$. The design used $5$ folds, at least $500$ proteins per organism, $41$ synonymous design columns, and $50$ shuffle-null replicates. Its recorded status is passed, and its finite conclusion is
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{FourthMechanism}_{Q6}\neq \mathrm{codon\_pair}.
\end{aligned}
$$
Equivalently, codon-pair context does not identify the powered fourth mechanism under this assay. This is a negative mechanism identity result, not a failure of the residual-coordinate program. The same powered family reports that three axes are insufficient: the optimality-axis sufficiency row, also at $5$ folds with $41$ synonymous design columns and at least $500$ proteins per organism, records $11$ computed organisms out of $16$ requested and concludes that a fourth mechanism is present. The codon-pair row therefore narrows the identity of that fourth mechanism rather than erasing it.

\paragraph{Relation to the surrounding residual spectrum.}
The exclusion is consistent with the broader powered spectrum. In the per-organism axis-strength row, $11$ organisms were computed out of $16$ requested, all $11$ per-organism dominant counts were assigned to $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$, and the descriptive domain counts were $1$ archaeal, $2$ bacterial, and $8$ eukaryotic organisms. The largest reported GC3 rank effect was small, $\rho=0.136$ for $d_{\mathrm{usage}}$, so the leading fourth residual direction is not reduced here to a simple GC3-rank account. The third-axis identity row identified $d_{\perp}$ with usage frequency within family, with mean cosine $0.128$ and one-sided sign-test $p=0.000488$; the third-axis mechanism row further records selection-not-GC, not-wobble-clustered, and a CSC or positional identity still needing an external row. The non-tRNA residual optimality row identifies the dominant non-tRNA residual direction as $f_3$ translation selection. These neighboring rows make the codon-pair refusal sharper: the finite panel retains structured residual axes, but codon-pair context is not the observed fourth-axis identity in this powered test.

\paragraph{Mechanism boundary.}
The powered comparison separates several candidate interpretations. The mRNA-stability row concludes $\mathrm{fourth\_axis\_not\_mrna\_stability}$ because the yeast $d_{\mathrm{opt\_halflife}}$ alignment with abundance $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$ is low under the fixed gates. The translation-accuracy row, run over $11$ computed organisms out of $16$ requested with $200$ permutations, concludes $\mathrm{not\_accuracy}$. The elongation-dwell row in $\mathit{Saccharomyces\ cerevisiae}$ concludes only partial. The codon-pair row adds the separate conclusion $\mathrm{not\_codon\_pair}$. Taken together, these rows force the fourth residual mechanism to remain a constrained unknown rather than being promoted to any one downstream biological explanation. The tRNA-poverty row supplies a distinct positive mechanism contact for $R$ sense codons: $40$ organisms were computed out of $46$ requested, $6$ were skipped, the permutation count was $1000$, $\alpha=0.05$, and the call states that $R$ sense codons are systematically tRNA-poor under GtRNAdb tGCN plus dos Reis wobble weights. That positive tRNA-supply row does not rescue the codon-pair hypothesis; it only says that a separate supply-side mechanism exists on the relevant boundary set.

\paragraph{Translation-readout gate.}
For the $f_3$-stress formulation, the only admissible bounded readback is a controlled survival entry $S_{f_3\text{-}\mathrm{stress},T_j}$ for a translation-layer row such as tAI, stAI, ribosome occupancy, or translation efficiency. The required statistic is not a codon-pair score by itself. It must be computed after amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, length, $M$-density, mRNA, rank, and simpler-baseline controls. In schematic finite-row form, the relevant admissible test is
$$
\begin{aligned}
S_{f_3\text{-}\mathrm{stress},T_j}>0
\quad\text{after controls}\quad
Z=\{\mathrm{AA},\mathrm{organism},\mathrm{GC3},\mathrm{length},M\text{-}\mathrm{density},\mathrm{mRNA},\mathrm{rank},\mathrm{baseline}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The current codon-pair row does not provide such a same-row translation-efficiency or ribosome-footprint matrix. It says that, within the powered synonymous residual design, codon-pair context is not the fourth mechanism identity. Therefore the surviving scientific statement is negative and local: the fourth residual axis is present in the surrounding powered panel, but codon-pair context is not its identified mechanism in this finite assay.

\paragraph{Local no-promotion rule.}
No claim about translation realization, ribosome dynamics, folded structure, physical admissibility, molecular function, organismal phenotype, mechanism closure, or universality follows from this codon-pair refusal. A separate layer-matched contact must supply each such interpretation. In particular, modeled tAI or stAI weights are bounded translation-readout annotations rather than observed ribosome occupancy; matched mRNA abundance is a control rather than a translation output; codon usage and codon-pair geometry are sequence-derived surfaces rather than protein, structure, function, or phenotype measurements. The finite result here is exactly the codon-pair exclusion under the stated powered design, together with the constraint that any positive $f_3$-stress translation statement must pass through a nondegenerate controlled $S_{f_3\text{-}\mathrm{stress},T_j}$ row.
