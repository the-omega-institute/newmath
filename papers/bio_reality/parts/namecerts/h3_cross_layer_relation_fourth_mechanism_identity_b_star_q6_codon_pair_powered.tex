\paragraph{Question tested.}
The biological question is whether the residual direction left after the established three-axis explanation is best read as a codon-pair or adjacent-codon effect. The powered codon-pair assay is therefore not a claim about protein abundance, folding, localization, stability, molecular function, phenotype, or a universal translation law. It is a finite discrimination test inside the translation-facing residual basis: if codon-pair structure were the fourth mechanism, then the held-out residual direction should be recovered by codon-pair features more strongly than by the declared null shuffles under the same synonym-aware design.

\paragraph{Powered finite-row result.}
The codon-pair identity experiment $b\_star\_q6\_fourth\_mechanism\_codon\_pair\_powered$ was recorded as run $b519b5ebd34d$ and passed its execution gate. It used $5$ folds, required at least $500$ proteins per organism, retained $41$ synonymous design columns, and compared against $50$ shuffle-null replicates. Its recorded conclusion is
$$
\begin{aligned}
\operatorname{conclusion}=\operatorname{not\_codon\_pair}.
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the finite evidence does not identify the fourth residual mechanism with codon-pair structure in this assay. The negative call is a powered exclusion within the declared feature family, not a statement that codon-pair biology never matters. It says only that the fourth $B^*_{Q6}$ residual mechanism, as represented on this controlled finite surface, is not explained by the tested codon-pair feature family.

Taken together, the codon-pair result is best read as a targeted exclusion: codon-pair structure is not the recorded identity of the fourth mechanism, while a translation-facing $f_3$ residual component remains the live finite direction.

\paragraph{Readout boundary.}
The bounded readback for this chapter is the controlled survival entry $S_{f3\text{-}stress,T_j}$ for a translation readout such as tAI, stAI, ribosome occupancy, or translation efficiency. A modelled tAI or stAI row can bound a translation-facing readout, but it is not by itself a measured ribosome-occupancy row. A ribosome-profiling or translation-efficiency row can test translation-layer survival only after same-organism and same-CDS joining to codon counts, wobble or tRNA weights, and matched mRNA controls. The three-organism all-$1.0$ rank-saturated surface is a null boundary, not positive survival. Therefore this codon-pair exclusion does not promote the $B^*_{Q6}$ coordinate to protein abundance, folded structure, physical admissibility, molecular function, cellular phenotype, mechanism, or universality. Those interpretations require separate layer-matched contacts and separately recorded statistics. The present finite result is narrower and stronger: on the powered synonymous residual surface with $5$-fold testing, $50$ shuffle nulls, $41$ synonymous design columns, and the recorded finite $f_3$ codon support, the fourth $B^*_{Q6}$ residual mechanism is not identified as codon-pair structure.
