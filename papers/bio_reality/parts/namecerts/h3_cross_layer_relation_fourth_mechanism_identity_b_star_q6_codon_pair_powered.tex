\paragraph{Local interpretation rule.}
The chapter-level conclusion is a refusal, not a promotion.  The finite result says that, under the powered assay described above, codon-pair context is not the identity of the fourth $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ candidate.  It does not say that the $f_3$ residual coordinate changes protein abundance, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, localization, stability, organismal phenotype, or a general biological law.  It also does not establish a biological mechanism merely from codon geometry, tAI or stAI modeling, or the existence of matched control variables.  Such statements require separate layer-matched contacts and explicit controlled statistics.  In the present finite row, the only positive content is the powered codon-pair exclusion together with the bounded placement of that exclusion inside the surrounding $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual-coordinate assays.
