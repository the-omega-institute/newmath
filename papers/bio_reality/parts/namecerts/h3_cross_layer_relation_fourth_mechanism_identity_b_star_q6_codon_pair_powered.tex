\subsection{Codon-pair exclusion for the fourth B*\_Q6 mechanism}\label{sec:bioreality-q6-codon-pair-fourth-mechanism}\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite coordinate being tested.}
The finite object in this record is the coding-sequence residual surface restricted to the B*\_Q6 $f_3$-stress coordinate after amino-acid projection, organism matching, GC3 control, length control, M-density control, and matched translation-readout routing.  The coordinate is not treated as a protein, phenotype, or causal mechanism.  It is a finite residual column whose proposed cross-layer content is evaluated only through explicitly attached readout rows.  The $f_3$ saturation datum fixes the discrete codon substrate: the set $M=\mathrm{Med}\cup R\cup\{\mathrm{ACA}\}$ is
$$\begin{aligned}
M=\{&\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{ACA},\mathrm{ACG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\\
&\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\},
\end{aligned}$$
while the saturated $f_3$-closure removes exactly the pair $\mathrm{ACA},\mathrm{ACG}$ and gives
$$\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{Sat}_{f_3}(R)=\{&\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\\
&\mathrm{CUU},\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\}.
\end{aligned}$$
The recorded boundary $\partial_{f_3}R=\varnothing$ is therefore a finite null boundary rather than an empirical effect.  It licenses a coordinate test but does not itself produce a biological observation.

\paragraph{Fourth-axis setting.}
The powered three-axis sufficiency experiment reports $\mathrm{conclusion}=\mathrm{fourth\_mechanism\_present}$ on $11$ computed organisms out of $16$ requested organisms, using $5$ folds, at least $500$ proteins per organism, and $41$ synonymous-design columns.  The accompanying organism-level strength spectrum also used $5$ folds and $41$ synonymous-design columns, with $11$ computed organisms out of $16$ requested; its descriptive summary says that $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$ led on average, followed by $d_{\mathrm{tRNA}}$, and that all $11$ per-organism dominant counts were assigned to $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$.  The domain counts were imbalanced, with $1$ archaeal, $2$ bacterial, and $8$ eukaryotic organisms, so those domain means are not promoted to a domain law.  The largest GC3 rank effect reported in that spectrum was the $d_{\mathrm{usage}}$ effect with $\rho=0.136$.

\paragraph{Codon-pair test.}
The codon-pair identity assay for the fourth mechanism returned $\mathrm{status}=\mathrm{passed}$ and $\mathrm{conclusion}=\mathrm{not\_codon\_pair}$ in run $\mathrm{b519b5ebd34d}$.  The assay used $5$ folds, a minimum of $500$ proteins per organism, $41$ synonymous-design columns, and $50$ shuffle-null replicates.  In finite-row terms the observation is not that codon pairs are absent from coding sequences, nor that codon-pair context is biologically irrelevant.  The narrower statement is that, under the declared residual design, fold structure, protein-count threshold, synonymous-column basis, and shuffle-null comparison, the fourth residual axis is not identified as the codon-pair mechanism.  A compact readout of the exclusion is
$$\begin{aligned}
\mathcal{D}_{\mathrm{pair}}&=(5\ \mathrm{folds},\ 500\ \mathrm{minimum\ proteins},\ 41\ \mathrm{synonymous\ columns},\ 50\ \mathrm{shuffle\ nulls}),\\
\mathrm{Call}(\mathcal{D}_{\mathrm{pair}})&=\mathrm{not\_codon\_pair}.
\end{aligned}$$
This is a negative mechanism-identity call for one proposed explanation of $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$, not a negative result for the whole B*\_Q6 residual program.

\paragraph{Relation to the neighboring finite calls.}
The codon-pair exclusion is coherent with the surrounding fourth-mechanism screen.  The mRNA-stability assay passed with $5$ folds, at least $500$ proteins per organism, and $41$ synonymous-design columns, but its call was $\mathrm{fourth\_axis\_not\_mrna\_stability}$ because the yeast $d_{\mathrm{opt\_halflife}}$ direction had low alignment with abundance $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$ under the fixed gates.  The translation-accuracy assay passed with $11$ computed organisms out of $16$ requested, $5$ folds, $41$ synonymous-design columns, and $200$ permutations, and returned $\mathrm{not\_accuracy}$.  The elongation-dwell assay, by contrast, passed on $\mathrm{saccharomyces\_cerevisiae}$ with $5$ folds and returned $\mathrm{partial}$.  Thus the local finite pattern is not a generic rejection of translation-layer hypotheses.  It is a discriminant: codon-pair identity is rejected for this fourth-axis role, mRNA stability and translation accuracy are also rejected in their declared assays, and elongation dwell remains only partial.

\paragraph{Translation-row gate.}
The f\_3-stress conjecture remains a translation-readout conjecture only where a same-organism and same-CDS row attaches codon counts, wobble-breaking tAI or stAI weights, ribosome occupancy or translation efficiency, and matched mRNA abundance.  The finite data supplied here do not contain a nondegenerate $S^{QT}$ matrix for that exact joined panel.  The appropriate survival statistic is still bounded by
$$\begin{aligned}
S_{f_3,T_j}>0
\end{aligned}$$
after amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, CDS length, M-density, matched mRNA abundance, rank, and simpler-baseline controls.  The three-organism all-$1.0$ surface mentioned in the packet is a rank-saturated null boundary, not survival.  Modelled tAI or stAI weights may define a bounded translation-weight surface, but they are not measured ribosome occupancy, protein abundance, folding, localization, function, or phenotype.

\paragraph{Mechanistic boundary.}
The strongest local conclusion is therefore an exclusion statement: codon-pair context is not the identified fourth mechanism in the powered B*\_Q6 residual assay recorded here.  No observed translation effect follows from this exclusion unless a separate translation contact supplies a positive, controlled, non-rank-degenerate survival entry.  No claim about protein folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, localization, stability, phenotype, causation, mechanism in the biochemical sense, or universality follows from the residual coordinate, from the codon-pair exclusion, from the $f_3$ saturation table, or from tAI/stAI weights alone.  Each such layer would require its own matched measurement surface and its own controlled statistic.  Within those limits, the record is ready for the standalone paper as a finite negative identity result: among the screened candidates for the fourth B*\_Q6 residual mechanism, the codon-pair explanation is ruled out by the declared powered assay, while the translation-readout conjecture remains open pending the required same-row survival matrix.
