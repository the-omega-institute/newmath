\subsection{Translation-conditioned RNA half-life with an RBP-occupancy gate}
\label{sec:bioreality-q6-translation-conditioned-rna-half-life-rbp-bridge}

\paragraph{Finite row carrier.}
The record considered here is a finite matched panel whose rows carry coding-transcript or CDS identifiers, synonymous residual-coordinate labels of type $B^*_{Q6}$ after quotienting by amino-acid sequence, a same-row translation readout, an RNA half-life readout, matched mRNA abundance or transcription controls, and a proposed local occupancy surface for an externally named decay-factor RNA-binding protein. The coordinate label is not itself a biological measurement. It is only a row label that partitions synonymous windows into high-coordinate and low-coordinate classes after amino-acid content has been held fixed. The biological question is therefore not whether a coding-window coordinate exists, but whether a controlled half-life residual survives the required same-row translation boundary and then follows a separately measured RBP-occupancy and perturbation pattern.

\paragraph{Available finite audit evidence.}
The current row ledger contains one run for the named DNA-mechanics handle, with run id \texttt{f8464f8328f6} and runtime $196.505904$ seconds. That run supplies execution provenance for the BioReality audit surface but does not expose a positive RNA half-life coefficient, an RBP occupancy contrast, a loss-of-function collapse statistic, a rescue statistic, a $p$-value, an $R$ list, an $M$ list, or a $\lambda$ value for this particular RNA-decay bridge. Related finite panels in the same audit family show the intended row discipline: the methylation-flanking-context run \texttt{bd781331dc42} used $20000$ probes from $656$ individuals and reported baseline $R^2=0.0129735$; the RBP-binding run \texttt{b225890251c2} counted $900$ total peaks and found $3$ cross-RBP concordant cases; the m6A stoichiometry run \texttt{2e8583415e99} used $60000$ rows with $49668$ distinct sites, a coverage-confound correlation of $-0.135922814$, and cross-condition concordance marked true; the m6Am run \texttt{eb689b7dc73c} used $31886$ rows with $19406$ distinct sites, $3230$ low-tail rows below $0.8$, $20942$ rows in the variable subset, saturated fraction $0.343223$, and cross-cell concordance marked true. These numbers are not imported as evidence for the present RBP bridge. They only fix the standard that a positive BioReality claim must name finite rows, declared controls, scalar readouts, and a non-ambiguous boundary between coordinate memory and measured biology.

\paragraph{Controlled null.}
Let $q_i$ denote the $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinate label for row $i$, let $T_i$ denote the same-row translation readout, let $H_i$ denote the RNA half-life readout, let $O_i$ denote measured local occupancy of the prespecified decay-factor RBP in the editable RNA window, and let $Z_i$ collect transcription rate, local RNA structure, motif density, GC and dinucleotide composition, codon-pair context, splice or isoform state, transcript abundance, batch, amino-acid composition, and the matched mRNA control. The finite null is the exchangeability statement that high-coordinate and low-coordinate labels do not change the translation-conditioned RNA half-life residual once $T_i$ and $Z_i$ are attached:
$$
\begin{aligned}
H_i \perp q_i \mid T_i,Z_i .
\end{aligned}
$$
A weaker half-life association, even if numerically nonzero, remains only an RNA-stability association unless the RBP bridge also passes its own finite test. The bridge test requires measured local occupancy, coordinate-versus-occupancy decoupling, target-RBP loss, wild-type rescue, and binding-defective or non-target controls on the same finite panel. In particular, the admissible positive pattern is not just $H_i$ changing with $q_i$; it is the ordered row-level pattern
$$
\begin{aligned}
q_i &\leadsto H_i \quad \text{after conditioning on } T_i,Z_i,\\
q_i &\leadsto O_i \quad \text{at the prespecified RBP window},\\
\text{target-RBP loss} &\leadsto \text{collapse of the } q_i\text{-half-life residual},\\
\text{wild-type rescue} &\leadsto \text{return of the residual},\\
\text{binding-defective or non-target rescue} &\not\leadsto \text{return of the residual}.
\end{aligned}
$$
All five clauses must be read as finite-row requirements. Missing any one of them leaves the record below the mechanism boundary.

\paragraph{Decision rule.}
The chapter therefore licenses a bounded conditional statement rather than a positive mechanistic conclusion. If a future same-organism, same-window panel supplies rows for $q_i$, $T_i$, $H_i$, $O_i$, target-RBP loss, wild-type rescue, and binding-defective or non-target controls, then the declared statistic may test whether the coordinate-associated RNA half-life residual is carried through the measured RBP occupancy route. If the half-life term survives without occupancy change, without target-loss collapse, without wild-type rescue, or with restoration by binding-defective or non-target controls, the result is rejected as an RBP bridge and remains a finite RNA-stability association at most. The present verified record does not contain the required RBP perturbation and rescue rows, so it cannot be read as translation realization, RNA-decay mechanism, protein output, folding, physical admissibility, biological function, adaptation, phenotype, causation, or a universal sequence law.

\paragraph{Local boundary.}
The same-row translation readout is mandatory because RNA half-life alone does not promote a synonymous coordinate into a translation-layer claim. The matched mRNA control is mandatory because transcript abundance can otherwise masquerade as either half-life or translation efficiency. The RBP-occupancy and rescue clauses are mandatory because a decay-factor mechanism cannot be inferred from local sequence geometry, motif density, RNA structure, codon-pair context, or residual coordinate labels. Thus the current record is scientifically useful as a precise finite-row specification: it states what would count as a translation-conditioned RNA half-life RBP bridge and what must remain outside the claim until separate layer-matched measurements supply it.
