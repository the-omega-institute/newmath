\subsection{Translation-conditioned RNA half-life and the RBP-bridge boundary}
\label{sec:bioreality-q6-rna-half-life-rbp-bridge}

\paragraph{Finite panel.}
This record concerns finite matched transcript or CDS rows in which synonymous residual-coordinate labels are compared only after the amino-acid sequence has been quotiented away. The intended rows carry a high-coordinate or low-coordinate $B^*_{Q6}$ residual label, a same-row translation readout, an RNA half-life readout, a matched mRNA-abundance control, local sequence and structure controls, and an externally fixed decay-factor RNA-binding protein candidate. The finite data currently attached to the record do not contain that full joined table. The claim identifier h3.cross\_layer\_relation.dna\_mechanics.dna\_cyclizability\_helical\_phasing\_basu2021 is verified only as an executed finite run with experiment id f8464f8328f6 and runtime $196.505904$ seconds; no $R$ list, $M$ list, helical-phase scalar, half-life coefficient, local RBP-occupancy contrast, loss coefficient, rescue coefficient, hit rate, p-value, or row count is present in the supplied payload for this claim. This is therefore a boundary statement rather than a positive mechanistic result.

\paragraph{Readout equation.}
For a joined row $i$, let $q_i \in \{\mathrm{high},\mathrm{low}\}$ denote the residual-coordinate label after amino-acid quotienting, let $T_i$ denote the same-row translation readout, let $H_i$ denote the RNA half-life residual, let $O_i$ denote measured local occupancy of the prespecified decay-factor RBP at the editable RNA window, and let $Z_i$ collect transcription rate, transcript abundance, local RNA structure, motif density, GC and dinucleotide composition, codon-pair context, splice or isoform state, batch, and the remaining declared controls. A positive bridge would require the finite contrast to be estimable in a joined panel of the form
$$
\begin{aligned}
H_i &= \alpha + \beta_Q q_i + \beta_T T_i + \gamma Z_i + \varepsilon_i,\\
O_i &= \alpha_O + \theta_Q q_i + \theta_T T_i + \gamma_O Z_i + \eta_i,
\end{aligned}
$$
and the half-life contrast must track the measured occupancy contrast rather than a structure-only, motif-only, transcription-only, abundance-only, isoform-only, composition-only, codon-context-only, or batch explanation. The finite bridge is not licensed by $\beta_Q \neq 0$ alone. It also needs target-RBP loss to collapse the half-life effect, wild-type rescue to restore it, and binding-defective or non-target rescue to fail. In the supplied rows those perturbation and rescue columns are absent.

\paragraph{Attached finite facts.}
The available companion evidence establishes only nearby surfaces. The RBP-binding companion run h3.cross\_layer\_relation.rbp\_binding.rbns\_affinity\_eclip\_binding\_encode has experiment id b225890251c2, $900$ total peaks, $3$ cross-RBP concordant cases, and runtime $223.648043$ seconds. This confirms that finite RBP-binding rows can be audited as binding evidence, but it does not supply the same transcript windows, the same $B^*_{Q6}$ labels, the decay-factor occupancy values, or the perturbation and rescue responses needed here. The translation companion run h3.cross\_layer\_relation.mtor\_translation.topscore\_mtor\_translation\_thoreen2012 has experiment id 0c2bbfd3e2d9, $n=6494$ readout rows, source GSE36892 RAW, and runtime $9.469$ seconds. This confirms that finite translation readouts can be carried as an observable layer, but it does not by itself provide RNA half-life, RBP occupancy, RBP loss, or rescue columns. These two companion facts are useful as row-type witnesses, not as support for the present RBP-decay mechanism.

\paragraph{Null.}
The matched null is exchangeability of high-coordinate and low-coordinate labels for RNA half-life after conditioning on amino-acid sequence, transcription rate, local RNA structure, motif density, GC and dinucleotide composition, codon-pair context, splice or isoform state, transcript abundance, batch, and the same-row translation readout. In symbols, the finite audit target is the failure of the coordinate label to improve the controlled half-life readout unless the same rows also show the prespecified RBP-occupancy and perturbation pattern:
$$
\begin{aligned}
q_i \perp H_i \mid T_i,Z_i
\end{aligned}
$$
for the negative case, and for a positive bridge the coordinate-associated half-life contrast must disappear under target-RBP loss and reappear only under wild-type rescue. If the half-life contrast survives without measured local occupancy change, or if binding-defective or non-target rescue restores it, the result is not an RBP bridge under this record. It remains at most a bounded RNA-stability association or a residual-coordinate descriptor.

\paragraph{Local boundary.}
No translation realization follows from RNA half-life alone. No RNA-decay mechanism follows from the presence of a same-row translation readout. No measured half-life difference is to be read as decay-factor RBP action unless measured local RBP occupancy, target-RBP loss, wild-type rescue, and binding-defective or non-target controls are all present in the same finite panel. Codon-window geometry and $B^*_{Q6}$ labels do not provide protein output, folding, physical admissibility, biological function, phenotype, adaptation, causation, or a universal biological law. Each of those statements would require a separate layer-matched contact and its own finite statistic.

\paragraph{Status.}
The record is complete as a self-contained negative boundary chapter: it specifies the finite carrier, the required columns, the controlled null, the perturbation-and-rescue gate, and the local non-promotion rule. It is not complete as a positive biological bridge. The present finite facts report one executed claim run with runtime only, one finite RBP-binding companion surface with $900$ peaks and $3$ cross-RBP concordant cases, and one finite translation companion surface with $6494$ rows. They do not yet form the joined RNA half-life, RBP occupancy, loss, and rescue matrix required to cross the RNA-decay RBP-bridge boundary.
