\subsection{Translation-conditioned RNA half-life as a bounded RBP bridge}\label{sec:h3-cross-layer-relation-dna-mechanics-dna-cyclizability-helical-phasing-basu2021}\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite panel.} The record considered here is a finite matched transcript or CDS panel whose rows carry four simultaneously indexed quantities: a synonymous residual-coordinate label after amino-acid quotienting, a same-row translation readout, an RNA half-life readout, and a measured local occupancy readout for a prespecified decay-factor RNA-binding protein. The operative question is not whether synonymous coding geometry by itself is biological mechanism. It is whether a bounded cross-layer relation may be certified when the coordinate-associated RNA half-life effect is observed only in the presence of the matched translation boundary and an experimentally separated RBP occupancy, loss, and rescue pattern. The completed run for the present claim identifier is recorded as experiment run \texttt{f8464f8328f6} with runtime $196.505904$ seconds. That run record supplies execution provenance but does not expose an $R$ list, $M$ list, lambda parameter, $p$-value, row count, module call, or hit-rate scalar for this specific DNA-mechanics-named surface.

\paragraph{Row semantics.} Each admissible row has a coordinate label $q_i$, a translation readout $T_i$, an RNA half-life readout $H_i$, a local RBP occupancy readout $O_i$, and controls $Z_i$ for transcription rate, local RNA structure, motif density, GC and dinucleotide composition, codon-pair context, splice or isoform state, transcript abundance, batch, and amino-acid composition. The intended finite statistic has the form
$$
\begin{aligned}
(q_i,T_i,H_i,O_i,Z_i) \longmapsto \Delta_H(q_i \mid T_i,Z_i),\qquad
\Delta_O(q_i \mid T_i,Z_i),
\end{aligned}
$$
with the bridge allowed only when the half-life residual follows the measured occupancy residual and the perturbation arms behave in the required direction. A high-coordinate versus low-coordinate half-life contrast without the same-row translation readout is only an RNA-stability association. A half-life contrast with unchanged measured occupancy is not an RBP bridge. A contrast that persists after target-RBP loss, fails to return under wild-type rescue, or returns under a binding-defective or non-target rescue is also not an RBP bridge.

\paragraph{Bridge test.} The positive pattern is therefore a finite conjunction, not a single association. First, the high-coordinate and low-coordinate labels must remain compared after amino-acid quotienting and after the declared controls are attached. Second, the half-life effect must be conditioned on the same-row translation readout rather than inferred from transcript abundance alone. Third, a prespecified decay-factor RBP must show measured local occupancy at the editable RNA window. Fourth, experimentally decoupled target-RBP loss must collapse the coordinate-associated half-life effect. Fifth, wild-type target-RBP rescue must restore the effect, while binding-defective rescue or non-target rescue must fail. In symbols, the admissible readback is the finite pattern
$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta_H(q_i \mid T_i,Z_i) \neq 0,
\quad \Delta_O(q_i \mid T_i,Z_i) \neq 0,
\quad \Delta_H^{\mathrm{loss}} \approx 0,
\quad \Delta_H^{\mathrm{WT\ rescue}} \approx \Delta_H,
\quad \Delta_H^{\mathrm{defective\ rescue}} \approx 0.
\end{aligned}
$$
The approximations here are not asymptotic promises; they denote the declared finite tolerance of the matched panel.

\paragraph{Linked finite evidence.} The broader ledger contains a directly relevant RBP-binding finite run, \texttt{h3.cross\_layer\_relation.rbp\_binding.rbns\_affinity\_eclip\_binding\_encode}, recorded as experiment run \texttt{b225890251c2}. Its exposed scalars are $900$ total peaks, $3$ cross-RBP-concordant cases, and runtime $223.648043$ seconds. Those numbers show that an RBP occupancy surface can be represented as a finite external readout, but they do not by themselves identify the decay-factor RBP required for this RNA half-life bridge, nor do they supply loss and rescue rows. The ledger also contains a translation-readout run, \texttt{h3.cross\_layer\_relation.mtor\_translation.topscore\_mtor\_translation\_thoreen2012}, with $n=6494$ rows and runtime $9.469$ seconds. That scalar supports the availability of finite translation-row readouts in the surrounding BioReality ledger, but it is not the same joined panel as the present RNA half-life/RBP bridge. These linked facts are used only to delimit the row types that the present claim requires: translation readout is a separate row surface, and RBP occupancy is a separate row surface.

\paragraph{Null model.} The local null says that, after conditioning on transcription rate, local RNA structure, motif density, GC and dinucleotide composition, codon-pair context, splice or isoform state, transcript abundance, batch, amino-acid composition, and the same-row translation readout, high-coordinate and low-coordinate labels are exchangeable with respect to RNA half-life. A failure of exchangeability is still not enough for a mechanism claim. The bridge is broken unless the same rows also show the specified measured occupancy, target-RBP loss collapse, wild-type rescue return, and binding-defective or non-target rescue failure. Thus RNA half-life, translation readout, and RBP occupancy remain three separated finite surfaces until the joined perturbation panel is present.

\paragraph{Local boundary.} This chapter licenses only a bounded RNA half-life/RBP-bridge readback under the finite conditions just stated. It does not license translation realization from RNA half-life alone, protein output from translation conditioning, folded structure from codon-window geometry, physical admissibility from an RNA decay statistic, biological function from an RBP occupancy pattern, a causal mechanism beyond the explicitly perturbed RBP bridge, adaptation, phenotype, or any universal biological law. If the measured occupancy, loss, rescue, and negative-control arms are absent, the coordinate remains a bounded descriptor or at most a finite RNA-stability association under the declared controls.
