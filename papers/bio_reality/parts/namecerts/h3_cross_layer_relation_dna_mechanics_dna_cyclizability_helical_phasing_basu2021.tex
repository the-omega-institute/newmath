\subsection{Translation-conditioned RNA half-life and a finite RBP bridge gate}
\label{sec:bioreality-q6-rna-half-life-rbp-bridge}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite rows and declared target.}
The object of this record is a finite matched panel whose rows carry a synonymous residual coordinate label $B^{*}_{Q6}$, a coding-transcript or CDS identifier, a same-row translation readout, an RNA half-life readout, matched mRNA abundance or transcription controls, local sequence and RNA-structure covariates, and an explicitly prespecified decay-factor RBP route.  The intended positive reading is deliberately narrow: a high-coordinate versus low-coordinate contrast may be reported as a bounded cross-layer RBP bridge only when the half-life residual follows measured local occupancy of the nominated RBP, collapses under loss of that RBP, returns under wild-type rescue, and is not restored by binding-defective or non-target rescue controls.  The row named h3.cross\_layer\_relation.dna\_mechanics.dna\_cyclizability\_helical\_phasing\_basu2021 supplies only the local audit scalar $196.505904$ seconds of runtime for the corresponding finite run.  It does not supply an $R$ list, an $M$ list, a lambda value, a p-value, a module class, a hit rate, a row count, or a target-specific occupancy/loss/rescue table.  Therefore this subsection records the gate conditions and the negative boundary of interpretation rather than a completed positive RBP-mediated RNA-decay result.

\paragraph{Statistic shape.}
For each matched row $i$, let $q_i$ denote the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual coordinate label after amino-acid quotienting, let $T_i$ denote the attached translation readout, let $H_i$ denote the RNA half-life readout, let $O_i$ denote measured local occupancy of the prespecified decay-factor RBP, and let $Z_i$ collect transcription rate, mRNA abundance, local RNA structure, motif density, GC and dinucleotide composition, codon-pair context, splice or isoform state, amino-acid composition, batch, and simpler sequence baselines.  The admissible bridge test is not the marginal contrast $q_i \mapsto H_i$.  It is the finite joint requirement that the residual half-life contrast remains after $Z_i$ and $T_i$ are attached, that the same coordinate window has a decoupled occupancy readout $O_i$, and that perturbation and rescue arms move in the required directions.  In compact notation the positive bridge would require the following four readbacks on the same finite panel:
$$
\begin{aligned}
q_i &\not\sim H_i \;\text{under the matched null after conditioning on}\; Z_i,T_i, \\
q_i &\sim H_i \;\text{only with a same-window change in}\; O_i, \\
\text{target-RBP loss} &\Rightarrow \text{collapse of the coordinate-associated half-life effect}, \\
\text{wild-type rescue} &\Rightarrow \text{return of the effect, while defective or non-target rescue fails.}
\end{aligned}
$$
The supplied rows do not instantiate these four lines; they specify what would have to be present before the association can cross from a bounded RNA-stability pattern into an RBP-bridge claim.

The surrounding finite ledger shows that the BioReality table can carry real row counts and scalar statistics when they exist.  The RBP binding calibration entry h3.cross\_layer\_relation.rbp\_binding.rbns\_affinity\_eclip\_binding\_encode reports $900$ total peaks, $3$ cross-RBP-concordant outcomes, and $223.648043$ seconds of runtime. The N-terminal acetylation entry h3.cross\_layer\_relation.nterminal\_acetylation.nterminal\_acetylation\_occupancy\_kron2018 reports $657$ rows, $426$ NatA rows, $622$ high-low rows for AUROC, and AUROC $0.942081$.  These examples matter here only as audit comparators: they demonstrate the expected form of a completed finite scalar payload.  The present RNA half-life/RBP bridge row lacks the matching occupancy, perturbation, rescue, and target-specific statistic fields that those completed payloads expose for their own subjects.

\paragraph{Local null and interpretation boundary.}
The local null is exchangeability of high-coordinate and low-coordinate synonymous labels with respect to RNA half-life after conditioning on transcription rate, mRNA abundance, local RNA structure, motif density, GC and dinucleotide composition, codon-pair context, splice or isoform state, amino-acid composition, batch, and the mandatory same-row translation readout.  A half-life difference without the prespecified RBP occupancy and perturbation pattern remains a finite RNA-stability association at most.  A translation readout attached to the same row does not by itself establish translation realization, RNA-decay mechanism, protein output, folding, physical admissibility, biological function, phenotype, adaptation, causation, or universality.  Each of those stronger readings would require its own layer-matched contact and its own finite statistic.  In the present record, the scientifically valid conclusion is therefore a bounded readiness statement: the coordinate, translation, abundance-control, and RNA half-life surfaces can be arranged into the required panel shape, but the RBP bridge is not licensed until measured local RBP occupancy, target-RBP loss, wild-type rescue, and binding-defective or non-target controls are supplied for the same editable RNA windows.
