\subsection{Cross-dataset axis transfer for synonymous perturbation windows}
\label{sec:bioreality-q6-cross-dataset-axis-transfer}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite surface.}
This record fixes a finite mediation and perturbation surface whose rows are not free biological narratives, but matched triples of the form $(q_i,t_j,p_k)$ and perturbation-window readouts.  The $q_i$ coordinates are the nine $B^*_{Q6}$ residual axes
$$
\begin{aligned}
&K\_AAA,\; Arg\_AGR,\; Ile\_AUA,\; Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR,\; Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG,\\
&Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY,\; Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG,\; Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY,\; f3\_stress .
\end{aligned}
$$
The meta-run for the cross-dataset transfer audit used $B=200$ resampling draws and completed in $974.407$ seconds.  Its role is to ask whether the same residual-axis family remains usable when the organism, assay, local synonymous-edit design, and output scale are changed.  It is therefore a transfer audit across finite rows, not a license to replace a layer-specific measurement with the codon residual itself.

\paragraph{Perturbation panel.}
The panel contains several non-equivalent perturbation contacts.  The yeast synonymous-mutation run used $20$ genes, $1783$ synonymous mutants, $21$ CDS-map hits, and a real codon-stability-coefficient comparison with correlation $0.39717918221186976$ between the real and proxy contrasts; the real coefficient did not absorb the residual signal and was reported as robust.  The human prime-editor run used $1822$ genes, $9000$ synonymous rows in the main analysis, and $24000$ synonymous rows in the compact representation, with held-out folds blocked by gene and a maximum of $3000$ rows per cell line.  The Chen GFP synonymous protein-escape audit used $3556$ variants split as $1124$ in region 1 and $2432$ in region 2, and reported an out-of-frame mRNA to protein slope of $0.016523671594873352$.  The Nieuwkoop E. coli mRFP protein-output run used $1459$ variants and found
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{\mathrm{controls}}&=0.12648386762689323,\\
R^2_{\mathrm{controls}+B^\perp}&=0.1798829276988051,\\
\Delta R^2&=0.05339906007191191,\\
\Delta DL&=19.089327473282296\;\mathrm{bits},\\
p_{\mathrm{matched}}&=0.004975124378109453,\qquad p_{\mathrm{within}}=0.004975124378109453.
\end{aligned}
$$
The same assay family also has a five-prime boundary audit on $1459$ variants: after the step from $R^2=0.06748192482378434$ to $R^2=0.6060702157093043$, adding the downstream $B$ term gave $\Delta R^2=-0.001126409133973234$ and $\Delta LL=-2.082967365015851$, while the start-window term gave only $\Delta R^2=1.7624020537495221\times 10^{-9}$.  This negative boundary result is part of the transfer surface: a reusable axis must survive the assay-specific nulls, not merely be named in the coordinate system.

\paragraph{Endogenous and distributed recoding rows.}
The Yang E. coli endogenous-fitness contact contributes $30$ genes and $46686$ synonymous edits.  The matched distributed-recoding contact contributes $47$ AmpR rows, $53$ GmR rows, and $19$ multi-row $D$ strata.  In that run the AmpR slope was $0.0$ with independent $p=1.0$, so it supplies a bounded negative or non-supporting finite row rather than a positive propagation claim.  These rows matter because the cross-dataset claim would be too weak if it only repeated one reporter construct; they also prevent promotion from a reporter-specific positive result to a general rule.

\paragraph{Axis identity and translation-conditioned context.}
A separate structural-order axis audit computed all nine residual axes and identified $f3\_stress$ as the only fold-level sign-flip survivor.  Its weighted held-out increment was $0.0031744502294756994$ with sign-flip right-tail probability $0.03125$, while examples of non-surviving axes include $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$ with increment $-0.0003205780368125179$ and $Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY$ with increment $-0.00045340330387383447$.  The transfer paper therefore treats axis identity as empirical and finite: one axis may dominate a particular held-out readout, and the remaining axes are not silently upgraded.

\paragraph{Mediation numbers.}
The matched protein-abundance mediation panel gives a larger cross-organism context for why a direct $q_i\to p_k$ association is not enough.  Across twelve organisms, the controlled joint $9$-coordinate mediation decomposition reported positive bounded fractions, for example $M^{QTP}=0.9491551041216973$ in \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}, $0.612922252793667$ in \emph{Escherichia coli} K12 MG1655, $0.6084594553474505$ in \emph{Bacillus subtilis}, $0.27206289160119157$ in human, and $0.01850668564589326$ in \emph{Sulfolobus solfataricus}.  The yeast row had total residual association $c=0.29837624994140827$, direct association after modeled tAI conditioning $c'=0.0151709093608293$, and indirect difference $0.28320534058057895$.  These values justify keeping the chain notation $q_i\to t_j\to p_k$ in the chapter, but they do not turn modeled tAI into observed ribosome motion or turn steady-state protein abundance into folding, localization, or function.

\paragraph{Structure and turnover guards.}
The yeast structure-mediation row joined $4759$ genes and reported $R^2_{QP}=0.2715520808117803$, $R^2_{QP\mid S}=0.2706601505000995$, $R^2_{QP\mid T,M}=0.13773967696311287$, and $R^2_{QP\mid T,M,S}=0.13759011208318542$.  Its structure-only fraction was $0.003284564452662019$, the structure-incremental fraction was $0.0005507778820193332$, and the right-tail permutation probabilities were $0.027972027972027972$ for $S_Q$ structure association and $0.1258741258741259$ for the incremental structure fraction.  In contrast, the turnover audit used $3290$ genes and gave $R^2_{L2}=0.055740851626933985$, $R^2_{L3}=0.050278872193227464$, $\Delta R^2=-0.005461979433706483$, $\Delta DL=-13.68819707902732$ bits, $p_{\mathrm{matchedbin}}=0.8855721393034826$, and $p_{\mathrm{recoded}}=1.0$.  The mass-balance row likewise remained negative for the added layer, with $R^2_{L2}=0.9712443377743347$, $R^2_{L3}=0.8953999568421264$, $\Delta R^2=-0.07584438093220836$, $\Delta DL=-668.8046070381928$ bits, $p_{TEbin}=0.6831683168316832$, and $p_{\mathrm{timeshuffle}}=0.9306930693069307$ on $120$ genes and $718$ intervals.  These guards are part of the mathematical object: transfer is permitted only where the finite rows survive their controls.

\paragraph{Local verdict.}
The chapter supports a narrow statement: the $B^*_{Q6}$ synonymous-window axis family has a reusable finite audit surface across yeast, human, E. coli reporter, E. coli endogenous, GFP, and distributed-recoding rows, and at least some rows show residual improvement or mediation-compatible attenuation after the declared controls.  It does not support a stronger biological statement by itself.  No translation realization follows without a measured or explicitly bounded translation readout; no protein-realization statement follows from a direct $q_i\to p_k$ association when the $q_i\to t_j$ step is absent, null, or untested; and no folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, mechanism, organismal phenotype, selection law, or universal biological rule follows unless a separate layer-matched contact supplies that readout and the corresponding controlled statistic.
