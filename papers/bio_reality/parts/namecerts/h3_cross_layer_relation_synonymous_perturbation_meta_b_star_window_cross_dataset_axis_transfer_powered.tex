\subsection{Cross-dataset transfer of the synonymous-window axis}
\label{sec:b-reality-cross-dataset-axis-transfer}

\paragraph{Finite row surface.}
The record considered here is the finite mediation surface formed by triples $(q_i,t_j,p_k)$, where $q_i$ ranges over the nine $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates, $t_j$ ranges over bounded translation readouts such as modeled tAI, ribosome occupancy, or translation efficiency, and $p_k$ ranges over matched downstream protein-abundance or perturbation-output measurements.  The coordinate list used by the finite runs is
$$
\begin{aligned}
Q=\{&K\_AAA,\ Arg\_AGR,\ Ile\_AUA,\ Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR,\ Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG,\\
&Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY,\ Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG,\ Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY,\ f3\_stress\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The meta-run itself used $B=200$ finite resampling or null draws and completed in $974.407$ seconds.  Its purpose is not to assert a universal synonymous-code rule, but to ask whether a window-level residual axis that survives in one perturbation surface remains visible when carried across independently keyed datasets with different organisms, endpoints, and nuisance structure.

\paragraph{Dataset rows.}
The transfer packet is supported by a heterogeneous finite panel.  In the \emph{E. coli} mRFP synonymous-output assay, $n=1459$ variants gave a controlled improvement from $R^2=0.12648386762689323$ to $R^2=0.1798829276988051$, hence
$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta R^2 &= 0.05339906007191191,\\
\Delta DL &= 19.089327473282296\ \text{bits},\\
p\_{\mathrm{matched}}&=p\_{\mathrm{within}}=0.004975124378109453.
\end{aligned}
$$
The corresponding real-CAI check had correlation $0.17884920449487354$ with the proxy and did not absorb the residual signal.  The separate 5-prime boundary audit on the same variant scale used $n=1459$ rows and found that downstream windows did not inherit a stable effect: the downstream increment was $\Delta R^2=-0.001126409133973234$, $\Delta LL=-2.082967365015851$, with block probability $p=0.1691542288557214$ and null95 value $0.5254770343527547$.  Thus the positive mRFP readout is local to the tested window geometry rather than an unrestricted coding-sequence law.

\paragraph{Perturbation panel.}
The yeast synonymous-mutation surface used $20$ genes and $1783$ synonymous mutants, with $21$ CDS map hits; the real CSC check had correlation $0.39717918221186976$ with its proxy and likewise did not absorb the residual.  The human prime-editor surface used $9000$ synonymous edits, a compact synonymous panel of $24000$ rows, $1822$ genes, and a per-cell-line cap of $3000$ rows, with held-out folds blocked by gene.  The Chen GFP surface used $3556$ variants split into $1124$ region-one and $2432$ region-two variants, and the fitted protein-on-out-of-frame-mRNA slope was $0.016523671594873352$.  The endogenous \emph{E. coli} fitness surface used $30$ genes and $46686$ synonymous edits.  A later distributed-recoding surface had $47$ AmpR rows, $53$ GmR rows, and $19$ multi-strata $D$ classes, but the AmpR independent probability was $1.0$ and the fitted AmpR slope was $0.0$.  These rows are therefore not read as a monotone direction shared by all assays; they are a finite stress test for whether the same residual vocabulary remains meaningful under several endpoint maps.

\paragraph{Axis identity check.}
The structural-order audit identifies $f3\_stress$ as the only coordinate passing the fold-level sign-flip gate.  Its weighted held-out increment was $0.0031744502294756994$ with sign-flip probability $0.03125$ and weighted absolute unit loading $0.5109586606724208$.  The next largest positive increments were $Arg\_AGR$ at $0.0020139270616115154$ with probability $0.142578125$, $Ile\_AUA$ at $0.0013067592718559024$ with probability $0.0693359375$, and $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$ at $0.0010134090056701938$ with probability $0.2158203125$.  The finite axis-transfer reading is therefore asymmetric: the record supports a recurring residual coordinate system with one especially stable stress axis, not a claim that every codon-family contrast transfers with the same sign or the same biological interpretation.

\paragraph{Translation and protein-abundance mediation.}
The broad protein-abundance mediation run joined modeled per-protein tAI, real CDS codon counts, and PAXdb abundance for $12$ organisms.  The joined row counts were $4042$ for \emph{Bacillus subtilis}, $11661$ for \emph{Caenorhabditis elegans}, $14410$ for \emph{Danio rerio}, $13295$ for \emph{Drosophila melanogaster}, $3739$ for \emph{Escherichia coli}, $12418$ for \emph{Gallus gallus}, $19053$ for human, $19395$ for mouse, $4129$ for \emph{Mycobacterium smegmatis}, $13322$ for rat, $5815$ for yeast, and $1656$ for \emph{Sulfolobus solfataricus}.  In that surface the mediated fractions remained in $[0,1]$ for all organisms; examples include $0.9491551041216973$ in yeast, $0.612922252793667$ in \emph{E. coli}, $0.6084594553474505$ in \emph{B. subtilis}, $0.27206289160119157$ in human, and $0.01850668564589326$ in \emph{S. solfataricus}.  These are statistical decompositions of protein-abundance association after amino-acid composition, GC3, CDS length, organism context, and $M$-density controls.  They do not by themselves turn modeled tAI into observed ribosome motion or convert abundance into a causal mechanism.

\paragraph{Boundary calculations.}
The modeled tAI survival check used $3$ organisms, $61$ sense codons, and nine residual coordinates.  It produced $81$ entries above the bounded-contact threshold $0.01$, with maximum absolute coupling $3801961.031607896$, but it also explicitly marked the panel as underpowered for a powered survival claim because $3$ organisms are not the requested $40$ matched species.  A separate evolutionary-imprint audit used $80$ orthogroups and $240$ rows across $3$ species; its strong imprint gate failed, with $\Delta R^2=0.03250515015613468$, leave-clade-out $\Delta R^2=-0.0021477729853302424$, null95 $\Delta R^2=0.05427157066522958$, and $\Delta DL=-258.6010084301837$ bits.  The positive finite contacts therefore remain bounded to their tested surfaces and do not license a cross-ortholog selection mechanism.

\paragraph{Local verdict.}
The finite verdict is that the $B^{\ast}$ synonymous-window axis is reusable across several perturbation and abundance surfaces, with the clearest positive protein-output row in the \emph{E. coli} mRFP assay and a separately identified $f3\_stress$ axis in the structural-order audit.  The record also contains negative and boundary-preserving rows: downstream 5-prime propagation is not stable in the mRFP boundary audit, the distributed-recoding AmpR slope is zero, modeled tAI has only bounded underpowered survival status at $n=3$, and the evolutionary-imprint gate is negative under its stronger test.  Consequently the admissible claim is finite and conditional: the coordinate system has cross-dataset transfer value as a controlled residual readout, while translation realization, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, mechanism, phenotype, selection, and universality remain outside the chapter unless a separate layer-matched contact supplies the relevant observations and the declared controls are passed.
