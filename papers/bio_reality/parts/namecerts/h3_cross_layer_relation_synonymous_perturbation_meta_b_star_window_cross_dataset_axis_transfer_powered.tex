\paragraph{Finite carrier.}
The record is a finite mediation-and-transfer surface whose rows are indexed by a B$^\ast_{Q6}$ residual coordinate $q_i$, a translation or expression-proximal readout $t_j$, and a matched protein, abundance, fitness, or perturbation readout $p_k$.  The admissible coordinates are the nine residual axes computed throughout the packet,
$$
\begin{aligned}
Q=\{&\mathrm{K\_AAA},\mathrm{Arg\_AGR},\mathrm{Ile\_AUA},\mathrm{Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR},\mathrm{Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG},\\
&\mathrm{Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY},\mathrm{Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG},\mathrm{Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY},\mathrm{f3\_stress}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The meta run identified by experiment id \texttt{2cd06dd54208} executed the cross-dataset axis-transfer audit with $B=200$ resampling units and runtime $974.407$ seconds.  Its role is not to introduce a new biological layer; it asks whether the same residual-window axes that appear in separate synonymous-perturbation assays can be read as a transportable finite axis family rather than as isolated dataset-specific encodings.

\paragraph{Transfer statistic.}
The transfer audit treats a positive cross-dataset statement as requiring the same residual-window direction to survive outside its source assay after the local controls for that assay are respected.  In finite terms, each source assay contributes a signed or ranked axis vector, each target assay contributes a held-out improvement or null-calibrated score, and the meta row asks whether the transported axis has positive predictive contact beyond the local baseline.  A schematic row has the form
$$
\begin{aligned}
(q_i,d_a,d_b)&\longmapsto \left(\Delta R^2_{i,a\to b},\Delta \mathrm{DL}_{i,a\to b},p_{i,a\to b}\right),\\
\mathrm{support}&\Longrightarrow \Delta R^2_{i,a\to b}>0\ \text{and}\ \Delta \mathrm{DL}_{i,a\to b}>0\ \text{under the declared null class}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The supplied finite row for the meta experiment records the powered resampling size and completed runtime but does not expose the per-axis transferred $\Delta R^2$, $\Delta\mathrm{DL}$, or tail-probability list.  Therefore the chapter reports the meta audit as a completed bounded transfer run and uses the linked assays to state the admissible carrier and row population; it does not assert a particular winning transferred axis from the meta row alone.

The broader cross-layer table gives a finite mediation benchmark for what a positive translation-conditioned protein-abundance claim looks like.  Across $12$ organisms, matched CDS and protein-abundance rows range from $1656$ joined proteins in \textit{Sulfolobus solfataricus} to $19395$ in mouse and $19053$ in human.  The mediation fractions $M^{QTP}$ are in the unit interval for every organism, with examples

$$
For yeast, the total residual-to-protein association is $R^2_{QP}=0.29837624994140827$, the direct association after modeled tAI conditioning is $R^2_{QP\mid T}=0.0151709093608293$, and the indirect difference is $0.28320534058057895$.  In E. coli K12 MG1655 the corresponding values are $0.10767526677985896$, $0.04167869969498872$, and $0.06599656708487024$.  These values justify using a mediated row type in the meta chapter, but they do not by themselves prove that any synonymous perturbation in the transfer audit acts through measured ribosome dynamics.  The only translation-readout survival matrix with modeled tAI over three organisms reports $81$ entries above threshold $0.01$ and maximum absolute coupling $3801961.031607896$, while also recording that $3$ organisms are insufficient for powered survival.  Thus modeled tAI contact is bounded evidence for a codon-adaptation readout, not an observation of ribosome motion.

\paragraph{Axis identity and negative controls.}
The structural-order axis audit supplies an independent axis-identity constraint: among the same nine axes, only $\mathrm{f3\_stress}$ passes the fold-level sign-flip gate, with weighted held-out increment $0.0031744502294756994$ and sign-flip probability $0.03125$.  Other axes remain below that gate, for example $\mathrm{Arg\_AGR}$ has increment $0.0020139270616115154$ with probability $0.142578125$, and $\mathrm{Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR}$ has increment $-0.0003205780368125179$ with probability $0.779296875$.  Separate negative or bounded audits prevent over-reading: the mass-balance run on $120$ genes and $718$ intervals has $\Delta R^2=-0.07584438093220836$, $\Delta\mathrm{DL}=-668.8046070381928$ bits, $p_{\mathrm{TEbin}}=0.6831683168316832$, and $p_{\mathrm{timeshuffle}}=0.9306930693069307$; the turnover residual audit on $3290$ genes has $\Delta R^2=-0.005461979433706483$, $\Delta\mathrm{DL}=-13.68819707902732$ bits, $p_{\mathrm{matchedbin}}=0.8855721393034826$, and $p_{\mathrm{recoded}}=1.0$.  The evolutionary selection-imprint audit over $80$ orthogroups, $240$ rows, and $3$ species reports positive ordinary correlation language but rejects the strong imprint gate: $\Delta R^2=0.03250515015613468$, leave-clade-out $\Delta R^2=-0.0021477729853302424$, and null-95 cutoff $0.05427157066522958$.

\paragraph{Local no-promotion rule.}
The finite-row transfer claim is confined to controlled axis transport across synonymous-perturbation readout tables.  A direct $q_i\to p_k$ association is not admitted as a protein-realization or mechanistic result unless a same-row $q_i\to t_j$ translation step is present and survives the declared controls.  No claim about translation dynamics, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, causal mechanism, phenotype, selection, or universality follows from this chapter unless a separate layer-matched measurement supplies that contact.  Amino-acid sequence, organism, GC3, coding-sequence length, M-density baseline, and matched mRNA abundance remain controls.  CUN/UUR sign direction, one-organism behavior, and reporter-specific effects are not promoted into population laws.

\paragraph{Verdict.}
The chapter is ready as a bounded meta-record: the cross-dataset run completed with $B=200$ and runtime $974.407$ seconds, and the linked finite assays provide enough row counts, null outcomes, and positive bounded readouts to define the transfer carrier without using generic placeholders.  The positive content is the existence of a completed finite transfer audit over the B$^\ast_{Q6}$ perturbation-window axis family.  The chapter intentionally withholds a named transferred winning axis because the exposed meta row does not provide the per-axis transfer scores needed for that stronger statement.
