\subsection{Direction-aware residual dictionary consistency for $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$}
\label{sec:bioreality-q6-direction-aware-residual-dictionary}

\paragraph{Finite object.}
The record considered here is a finite per-organism ledger for the modeled-translation-complement abundance component $P_Q$.  For each organism with enough attached readouts, the table stores the ordered translation and transcript-side fractions, the remaining residual fraction, and a direction-aware $H$-dictionary.  Each candidate readout $H$ is scored only as a descriptive projection row: it has absorbed energy $A_H$, coordinate-support cosine $C_H$, the product $A_H C_H$, a description-length count $DL$, a join-shift penalty, a null envelope, and a Boolean keep decision.  The scoring rule used in the direction-aware dictionary is the stated finite rule
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{score}(H) &= A_H C_H - \mathrm{null95}(H) - 0.01\,DL(H) - 0.05\,\mathrm{join\_shift}(H).
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus $\lambda=0.01$ is not a fitted biological constant; it is the declared description-length coefficient in this ledger.  The table is complete for four organisms in this run: \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}, \emph{Escherichia coli} K-12 MG1655, \emph{Homo sapiens}, and \emph{Danio rerio}.  The squared norms of the corresponding $P_Q$ vectors are $229.86450794006544$, $225.84556319953032$, $334.28411434290217$, and $605.0092168669606$, with row counts $4759$, $2517$, $11354$, and $13140$, respectively.

\paragraph{Interpretation.}
The finite verdict is negative and useful.  The direction-aware ordered dictionary does not compress the residual into a retained $H$ explanation under the stated rule.  The best candidates are either too weak after direction filtering, too costly after dimensional and join penalties, wrong-direction, or missing.  The module-level labels are therefore local ledger summaries: yeast is translation-proximal but still mostly unexplained, while \emph{E. coli}, human, and danio are mostly unexplained in this panel.  No entry in this chapter establishes translation realization, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, pathway mechanism, phenotype, or universality.  Such claims would require a separate same-row contact and a separate statistic aimed at that layer.  Here $A_H$, $C_H$, rank, null status, and score remain bounded table readbacks over attached finite readouts, with missing rows preserved as missing.
