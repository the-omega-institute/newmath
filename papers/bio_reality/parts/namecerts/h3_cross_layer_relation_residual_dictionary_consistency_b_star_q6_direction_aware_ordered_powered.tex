\subsection{Direction-aware residual dictionaries for the non-translation abundance component}
\label{sec:biorality-q6-direction-aware-residual-dictionary}

\paragraph{Finite object.}
The finite row considered here is the direction-aware residual dictionary for the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ abundance component after the modeled translation readout has been separated. For each organism, the target vector is $P_Q=\Pi_{Q_e}P_e$, where the nine $B^{*}_{Q6}$ coordinates are residualized against the same control family: an intercept, $\log(\mathrm{CDS\ length})$, the $20$ amino-acid composition coordinates, $GC3$, and the $M$-density baseline. The dictionary then records an ordered split into a measured-translation part $T$, an mRNA-stability or transcript-side part $S$ when available, and a residual panel of candidate $H$ readouts. Each candidate row carries absorbed energy $A_H$, coordinate-support cosine $C_H$, the product $A_H C_H$, a null threshold, a description length, a join-shift penalty, and a Boolean decision about whether the readout is retained. The scoring convention uses the stated regularization scale $\lambda=0.01$ for one-column readouts, while multi-column readouts carry their reported dimensional description length; in the broader ranking table this is the same convention summarized by $DL(H)=\log_2(1+n_{\mathrm{columns}})$.

$$
\begin{aligned}
C_H &= \frac{\langle a_H,a_{\perp}\rangle}{\lVert a_H\rVert\,\lVert a_{\perp}\rVert},\\
\mathrm{raw}(H) &= A_H C_H,\\
\mathrm{score}(H) &\text{ is the declared direction-aware product penalized by the null, description length, and join shift.}
\end{aligned}
$$

The finite table is therefore a ranked projection ledger, not a biological pathway model. The readout named by $H$ is allowed to enter only as an attached measured or curated row; the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates do not by themselves create a translation, localization, turnover, complex-membership, structural, functional, or causal statement.

\paragraph{Ordered residual split.}
The four computed organisms have exact sum diagnostics equal to $1.0$ after the ordered split. In \textit{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}, $T=0.11271394500088064$, $S=0.019627420469677935$, and the unexplained residual is $U=0.8676586345294415$ on $n=4759$ rows with $\lVert P_Q\rVert^2=229.86450794006544$. This is the only row in the emitted dictionary table whose qualitative label is translation-proximal, but even there no positive $H$ readout is retained. In \textit{Escherichia coli} K-12 MG1655, $T=0.00035964766234144823$, $S=0.005357973825835613$, and $U=0.9942823785118229$ on $n=2517$ rows with $\lVert P_Q\rVert^2=225.84556319953032$. In \textit{Homo sapiens}, $T=0.015365036507318318$, $S=0.004768342590437625$, and $U=0.9798666209022441$ on $n=11354$ rows with $\lVert P_Q\rVert^2=334.28411434290217$. In \textit{Danio rerio}, $T=0.02436809579891462$, the $S$ slot is skipped, and $U=0.9756319042010854$ on $n=13140$ rows with $\lVert P_Q\rVert^2=605.0092168669606$. Thus the dominant empirical fact is not the discovery of a shared downstream readout, but the persistence of a large residual after the measured translation and transcript-side cuts.

In \textit{Homo sapiens}, PTM density has the largest listed energy in the direction-aware dictionary, $A_H=0.014354648896209531$, but its cosine is only $C_H=0.4249168128961486$, giving $A_HC_H=0.006099531659220571$ and score $=-0.004684322133066079$. \paragraph{Cross-organism context.}
The same finite record sits against two independent background checks. First, the Window6 boundary $R$ set has a strong avoided abundance-preference signature: among $15$ computed organisms, $15$ are avoided, $0$ preferred, and $0$ zero, with one-sided binomial $p=3.0517578125\times10^{-5}$ and two-sided $p=6.103515625\times10^{-5}$. Among individually significant organisms, $6$ of $6$ are avoided. This says that the fixed $R$ boundary has a reproducible abundance-preference direction, not that the residual dictionary has found a mechanism. Second, the optimal-codon direction comparison is classified as clustered, with mean pairwise cosine $0.2629282074265475$ across $15$ computed organisms and $41$ synonymous-design columns. These background facts make the residual dictionary worth reporting, but they also reinforce its finite status: coherent codon-side geometry can coexist with an unexplained downstream abundance component.

\paragraph{Local interpretation boundary.}
The conclusion of this finite row is negative and informative. The ordered table leaves $86.8\%$ to $99.4\%$ of $P_Q$ unexplained across the four computed organisms, and every direction-aware $H$ entry in the emitted dictionary has \texttt{kept=false}. Large energy is not enough; the sign and magnitude of $C_H$, the dof-matched null, the description length, and the join shift all matter. Missing rows remain unavailable rather than being counted as zero or negative evidence. A positive cosine is only coordinate agreement, not a biological regulation channel. From this table alone one may report the finite tuples $(A_H,C_H,A_HC_H,\mathrm{score})$, the ordered fractions $(T,S,U)$, the null diagnostics, the row counts, and the absence of a shared retained $H$. One may not infer translation realization, folding, physical admissibility, protein function, localization mechanism, turnover causation, organism-universal circuitry, or a global biological law unless a separate layer-matched contact supplies that stronger claim.
