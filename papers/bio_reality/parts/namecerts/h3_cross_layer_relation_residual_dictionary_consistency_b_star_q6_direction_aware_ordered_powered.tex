\paragraph{Finite carrier and scope.}
The finite object tested here is a per-organism ledger over the nine $B^\ast_{Q6}$ coordinates
$K\_AAA$, $Arg\_AGR$, $Ile\_AUA$, $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$, $Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG$, $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$, $Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG$, $Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY$, and $f3\_stress$.  For each organism, the abundance component $P_Q=\Pi_{Q_e}P_e$ is first cut by the fixed measured-translation and stability order, and the remaining component is tested against an $H$ dictionary.  The $H$ row is not accepted merely because it has projection energy.  It is evaluated by the signed coordinate-support agreement
$$
\begin{aligned}
C_H &= \frac{\langle a_H,a_\perp\rangle}{\lVert a_H\rVert\lVert a_\perp\rVert},\\
\operatorname{score}(H) &= A_H C_H-\lambda DL(H)-\operatorname{null95}(H)-\operatorname{join\_shift}(H),
\end{aligned}
$$
with the recorded scoring scale using $\lambda=0.01$.  The verified row is therefore a descriptive ordered projection table, not a biological mechanism.  Its declared layer is \texttt{cross\_layer\_relation}, and the associated finite statement says that $P_Q$ is decomposed in the order measured TE, mRNA stability where available, and then a greedy direction-aware $H$ dictionary scored by $A_HC_H$ after null, description-length, and join-shift penalties.

The computed organisms close their ordered fractions exactly at the row level.  Saccharomyces cerevisiae has $T=0.1127139450$, $S=0.0196274205$, and unexplained $U=0.8676586345$.  Escherichia coli K-12 MG1655 has $T=0.0003596477$, $S=0.0053579738$, and $U=0.9942823785$.  Homo sapiens has $T=0.0153650365$, $S=0.0047683426$, and $U=0.9798666209$.  Danio rerio has $T=0.0243680958$, skipped $S$, and $U=0.9756319042$.  The emitted sum diagnostics are $1.0$ for all four organisms, with zero reported sum error in every case.  The corresponding $P_Q$ energies and row counts are $229.8645079401$ over $4759$ yeast rows, $225.8455631995$ over $2517$ E. coli rows, $334.2841143429$ over $11354$ human rows, and $605.0092168670$ over $13140$ danio rows.  Thus the table is numerically closed as a finite variance ledger, while its residual component remains large in all four organisms.

\paragraph{Local interpretation boundary.}
The result is ready as a bounded table claim.  It says that, under the stated finite joins, nulls, direction cosines, description-length penalties, and join penalties, no positive $H$ readout is retained in the ordered direction-aware dictionary for the four emitted organisms.  The residuals remain mostly unexplained: $U=0.868$ in yeast, $U=0.994$ in E. coli, $U=0.980$ in human, and $U=0.976$ in danio.  This does not imply that no biological explanation exists.  It only says that these measured readouts, under this scoring rule and these row joins, do not supply a shared explanatory absorber.  No translation realization, folding state, physical admissibility, biological function, pathway mechanism, phenotype, universal $H$, or global biological law follows from $A_H$, $C_H$, rank, score, null status, or the coordinate geometry unless a separate layer-matched contact and statistic establish that stronger statement.
