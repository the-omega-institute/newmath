\paragraph{Finite object under review.}
The finite record for this packet is a per-organism ledger attached to the modeled-translation-complement abundance component $P_Q=\Pi_{Q_e}P_e$.  Each row stores the squared norm of $P_Q$, the usable row count, the ordered translation and transcript-side fractions, the remaining fraction $U$, and a direction-aware dictionary over fetchable readouts $H$.  For every readout row the ledger records absorbed energy $A_H$, directional agreement $C_H$, raw directional support $A_HC_H$, a null reference, a description count, a join-shift penalty, and a final score.  The score is a descriptive ranking statistic; it is not a biological channel.

The emitted scores are consistent with the finite rule
$$
\begin{aligned}
\operatorname{raw}_H &= A_H C_H,\\
\operatorname{Score}_H &= A_HC_H-\operatorname{null}_{95}(H)-0.01\,DL(H)-0.05\,\operatorname{join\_shift}(H).
\end{aligned}
$$
The accounting table has no retained $H$ term, so the checked identity is simply the reported partition with $H_1=H_2=0$:
$$
\begin{aligned}
T_o+S_o+H_{o,1}+H_{o,2}+U_o=1.
\end{aligned}
$$
The sum diagnostics are exact at displayed precision for all four organisms: Saccharomyces cerevisiae, Escherichia coli K-12 MG1655, Homo sapiens, and Danio rerio each has reported sum $1.0$ and sum error $0.0$.

\paragraph{Local interpretation boundary.}

Large raw products such as yeast localization $0.01517908257625157$ and Danio localization $0.013628837966018246$ do not become retained dictionary entries after null, description-length, and join-shift penalties.  Wrong-direction rows, missing rows, and zero placeholders remain separate from within-null evidence: missing data are not counted as biological absence, and wrong direction is not a mechanism.
