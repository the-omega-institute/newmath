\paragraph{Axis separation.}

$$

\paragraph{Controlled interpretation.}
The phrase structural order in this chapter names a tested readout axis on the finite joined panel, not an assertion of folded-state causation.  The positive statement is that the full $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ signal was significant in the powered run and that the fold-level identity of the surviving coordinate was $f3\_stress$.  This is consistent with the separate modeled translation contact, where $3$ organisms produced a bounded modeled-tAI coupling surface with $81$ entries above threshold $0.01$ and maximum absolute $S_{ij}=3801961.031607896$, while also recording that $3$ organisms are insufficient for powered survival requiring at least $40$ matched species.  It is also distinct from the twelve-organism protein-abundance mediation surface, where the same nine $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ coordinates were decomposed through modeled per-protein tAI and matched PAXdb abundance; for example, the yeast mediation fraction was $0.9491551041216973$ with total $R^2_{QP}=0.29837624994140827$ and direct $R^2_{QP\mid T}=0.0151709093608293$, whereas human had mediation fraction $0.27206289160119157$ with total $R^2_{QP}=0.04454565118019448$.  Those mediation rows are useful finite context, but they do not convert the structural-order axis call into translation mechanism, protein folding, or function.

These rows support a restrained reading: there is a measurable axis identity and there are bounded structure-adjacent statistical decompositions, but neither is a folded protein structure measurement.

The chapter therefore licenses only the following finite claim: on the powered structural-order panel, $f3\_stress$ is the unique $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ coordinate that passes the fold-level sign-flip gate, with $p=0.03125$ and weighted held-out increment $0.0031744502294756994$.
