\paragraph{Readout interpretation.}
The word structural in this chapter names the registered order readout of the experiment, not a folded-protein structure contact.  The result says that the $f3\_stress$ coordinate is the unique significant axis inside the tested $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual family.  It does not say that $f3\_stress$ directly causes folding, creates a molecular function, or supplies a universal rule of codon biology.  The finite result is narrower: after the declared fold procedure, exactly one coordinate survives the sign-flip filter, and that coordinate is the same one selected by the dominant-increment and dominant-loading diagnostics.
