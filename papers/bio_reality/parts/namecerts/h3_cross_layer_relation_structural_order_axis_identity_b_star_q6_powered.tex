\paragraph{Translation-readout boundary.}
The modeled-tAI survival contact is informative but narrow. However, the same record says that three organisms are insufficient for powered survival and classifies the layer as modeled tAI bounded coupling rather than ribosome-profiling or proteomics validation.  Thus the structural-order axis identity may be placed next to modeled translation readouts, but it is not transformed into an observed ribosome-occupancy claim.

\paragraph{Local boundary.}
The finite conclusion is the following: on the supplied rows, $f3\_stress$ is the unique coordinate that carries the powered structural-order axis signal, with $p=0.03125$ and weighted held-out increment $0.0031744502294756994$.
