\subsection{B-star-Q6 structural-order axis identity under translation-gated protein-abundance controls}
\label{sec:bioreality-b-star-q6-structural-order-axis-identity}

\paragraph{Finite carrier.}
The record considered here is a finite table of rows whose coordinates are B-star-Q6 synonymous residual coordinates, translation readouts, and matched protein-abundance or structure-adjacent abundance readouts. The residual coordinate list is
$K\_AAA$, $Arg\_AGR$, $Ile\_AUA$, $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$, $Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG$, $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$, $Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG$, $Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY$, and $f3\_stress$.
A direct residual-to-protein edge is not admitted as a protein-level interpretation unless the same residual coordinate is first evaluated through a translation readout such as modeled tAI, stAI, ribosome occupancy, or translation efficiency, and unless matched mRNA abundance is kept as a control rather than confused with translation output. Amino-acid composition, organism identity, GC3, CDS length, and the M-density baseline are controls in this record, not promoted biological coordinates.

\paragraph{Powered axis test.}
The structural-order axis identity run used five folds, a minimum joined-row requirement of $300$, and the deterministic seed $sha256:8da7c86c999e5bee370c0c1a60a84722d81290b54b4dc2d4bd43c1cbd29fb394$. The finite run status was passed. The full B-star-Q6 signal was significant, but the fold-level sign-flip gate selected exactly one coordinate: $f3\_stress$. The selected axis was simultaneously the dominant increment axis and the dominant loading axis, with weighted absolute unit loading $0.5109586606724208$, held-out weighted $R^2$ increment $0.0031744502294756994$, and sign-flip probability $0.03125$ for the positive direction.

$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta R^2_{f3\_stress} &= 0.0031744502294756994,\\
p_{f3\_stress} &= 0.03125,\\
|u_{f3\_stress}|_{weighted} &= 0.5109586606724208.
\end{aligned}
$$

The remaining coordinates did not pass the same gate. Their held-out weighted increments and sign-flip probabilities were $0.0020139270616115154$ and $0.142578125$ for $Arg\_AGR$, $0.0013067592718559024$ and $0.0693359375$ for $Ile\_AUA$, $0.0005307046179682497$ and $0.2294921875$ for $K\_AAA$, $-0.0003205780368125179$ and $0.779296875$ for $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$, $0.00017615626369389472$ and $0.24609375$ for $Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG$, $0.0010134090056701938$ and $0.2158203125$ for $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$, $0.00004390882136492317$ and $0.4873046875$ for $Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG$, and $-0.00045340330387383447$ and $0.529296875$ for $Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY$. Thus the finite readout does not support a broad all-coordinate structural-order claim; it identifies a single residual axis whose sign and held-out contribution survive the stated gate.

In that mediation surface, the largest modeled translation-mediated protein-abundance fraction was observed in Saccharomyces cerevisiae, where $M^{QTP}=0.9491551041216973$, total $R^2_{QP}=0.29837624994140827$, direct $R^2_{QP\mid T}=0.0151709093608293$, and indirect difference $0.28320534058057895$. These are statistical decompositions of matched rows, not perturbation effects.

\paragraph{Yeast abundance and structure-adjacent partition.}
A separate yeast five-way join contained $4759$ rows after joining CDS counts, protein abundance, measured translation efficiency, mRNA half-life, and a CDS structure proxy. The reported CDS join hit rate was $0.9156038419146592$, and the overall join hit rate was $0.8818572656921754$. In the residualized abundance partition, $R^2_{QP}=0.2715520808117803$, $R^2_{QP\mid S}=0.2706601505000995$, $R^2_{QP\mid T,M}=0.13773967696311287$, and $R^2_{QP\mid T,M,S}=0.13759011208318542$. The structure-only fraction was $0.003284564452662019$, while the structure-incremental fraction beyond measured translation efficiency and mRNA stability was $0.0005507778820193332$. The residualized structure fit from Q to the 5-prime CDS MFE proxy had $S\_Q$ structure $R^2=0.003774248455422586$, with right-tail permutation probability $0.027972027972027972$ for that structure fit and $0.1258741258741259$ for the structure-incremental fraction.

These finite values give a narrow interpretation: the structural-order identity record is consistent with $f3\_stress$ carrying the detectable axis in the powered table, and the yeast partition shows only a small structure-proxy component relative to the larger abundance association. The structure proxy is first-39-nt CDS minimum free energy, not an experimental folded-protein structure readout.

\paragraph{Modeled translation-readout boundary.}
The modeled-tAI contact used three organisms for a bounded codon-adaptation readout: Escherichia coli K12, Homo sapiens, and Saccharomyces cerevisiae. The tRNA-family payload covered all $20$ standard amino-acid families in each organism, with $87$ matched headers out of $89$ in Escherichia coli K12, $432$ out of $432$ in Homo sapiens, and $275$ out of $275$ in Saccharomyces cerevisiae. The modeled tAI weights covered $61$ sense codons in each organism. The bounded-coupling matrix had $81$ entries above threshold $0.01$ and maximum absolute entry $3801961.031607896$, but the organism count was only $3$; therefore this is modeled coupling and not a powered measured-ribosome survival result.

\paragraph{Local no-promotion rule.}
The admissible conclusion is finite and axis-specific: among the nine B-star-Q6 residual coordinates tested in the powered structural-order table, $f3\_stress$ is the only coordinate passing the fold-level sign-flip gate and it is the dominant loading and increment axis. No translation realization follows from this axis identity unless a separate translation contact supplies measured or explicitly bounded translation rows. No folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, mechanism, phenotype, selection claim, or universal biological rule follows from the residual coordinate, the modeled-tAI mediation surface, the yeast abundance partition, or the sign-flip result alone. The Phase 1 confirmatory table reinforces this discipline: it reported $40$ successes out of $40$ under independent replication and $40$ successes out of $40$ under the boundary probe, each with one-sided binomial upper-tail probability $9.094947017729282\times 10^{-13}$, while also recording $0$ unexpected successes out of $20$ negative controls and $0$ unexpected successes out of $20$ critical ablations. Those finite results support codon-table boundary contact only; they do not license movement into higher biological layers without their own matched measurements.
