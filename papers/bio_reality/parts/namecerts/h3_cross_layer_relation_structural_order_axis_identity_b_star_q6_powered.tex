\subsection{Structural-order axis identity for the powered $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ panel}\label{sec:h3-cross-layer-relation-structural-order-axis-identity-b-star-q6-powered}

\paragraph{Finite surface.}
This record concerns the finite panel of nine $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates computed on coding-sequence rows: $K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $Leu_{CUN\,vs\,UUR}$, $Leu_{UUA\,vs\,UUG}$, $Ser_{UCR\,vs\,AGY}$, $Ser_{UCA\,vs\,UCG}$, $Thr_{ACR\,vs\,ACY}$, and $f_{3}\text{-stress}$.  The tested question is not whether the whole residual basis becomes a general biological rule.  It is the narrower finite-row question of whether a single coordinate carries the identifiable structural-order increment once the comparison is made by held-out folds and fold-level sign-flip calibration.  The registered run used $5$ folds, required at least $300$ joined rows in the relevant joins, and returned status passed for experiment id \texttt{b\_star\_q6\_structural\_order\_axis\_identity\_powered} with deterministic seed \texttt{sha256:8da7c86c999e5bee370c0c1a60a84722d81290b54b4dc2d4bd43c1cbd29fb394}.

\paragraph{Axis call.}
The finite verdict is that structural-order signal, as measured by this design, is carried by $f_{3}\text{-stress}$.  The dominant-axis audit identified $f_{3}\text{-stress}$ as both the dominant increment axis and the dominant loading axis.  Its weighted absolute unit loading was $0.5109586606724208$, its held-out weighted $R^{2}$ increment was $0.0031744502294756994$, and its fold-level sign-flip right-tail value was $p=0.03125$.  The same audit marked the full $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ signal significant, but the coordinate-level reason was more restrictive: exactly one coordinate passed the fold-level sign-flip gate.  In finite notation, the accepted axis identity is
$$
\begin{aligned}
q_{\mathrm{struct}} &= f_{3}\text{-stress},\\
\Delta R^{2}_{\mathrm{heldout}}(q_{\mathrm{struct}}) &= 0.0031744502294756994,\\
p_{\mathrm{signflip}}(q_{\mathrm{struct}}) &= 0.03125 .
\end{aligned}
$$
This is a coordinate identity result, not a license to read every $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ coordinate as structural evidence.

\paragraph{Negative coordinates.}
The other eight coordinates remain below the same coordinate-level gate.  Their held-out weighted increments and sign-flip right-tail values were: $Arg_{AGR}$, $0.0020139270616115154$ with $p=0.142578125$; $Ile_{AUA}$, $0.0013067592718559024$ with $p=0.0693359375$; $K_{AAA}$, $0.0005307046179682497$ with $p=0.2294921875$; $Leu_{CUN\,vs\,UUR}$, $-0.0003205780368125179$ with $p=0.779296875$; $Leu_{UUA\,vs\,UUG}$, $0.00017615626369389472$ with $p=0.24609375$; $Ser_{UCR\,vs\,AGY}$, $0.0010134090056701938$ with $p=0.2158203125$; $Ser_{UCA\,vs\,UCG}$, $0.00004390882136492317$ with $p=0.4873046875$; and $Thr_{ACR\,vs\,ACY}$, $-0.00045340330387383447$ with $p=0.529296875$.  These values matter because the claim is an identity claim: the result is not that synonymous residual structure is diffusely present everywhere, but that the resolved finite panel selects $f_{3}\text{-stress}$ under the stated audit.

\paragraph{Relation to translation and protein rows.}
The structural-order axis is also bounded by the available translation and protein-abundance contacts.  A separate modeled-tAI survival run computed the same nine residual coordinates over $3$ organisms, with $81$ modeled coupling entries above the $0.01$ threshold and maximum absolute $S_{ij}$ equal to $3801961.031607896$; however, that run explicitly remained modeled-tAI bounded coupling, not powered measured ribosome profiling or proteomics validation, because $3$ organisms are insufficient for powered survival.  A separate translation-mediated protein-abundance run joined real CDS codon counts, modeled per-protein tAI, and PAXdb protein abundance across $12$ organism panels.  It found all organism-level $M^{QTP}$ fractions in the unit interval, including $0.9491551041216973$ for \textit{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}, $0.612922252793667$ for \textit{Escherichia coli} K12 MG1655, $0.4872268357227724$ for \textit{Caenorhabditis elegans}, and $0.27206289160119157$ for \textit{Homo sapiens}.  Those values show that the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual family has finite downstream abundance decompositions in matched rows, but they do not turn the structural-order axis call into a causal translation mechanism or a folded-protein statement.

\paragraph{Yeast mediation check.}
The yeast five-way join supplies a useful guardrail because it includes $4759$ joined rows with CDS codon counts, protein abundance, measured translation efficiency, mRNA half-life, and a $5'$ coding-region MFE proxy.  In that joined surface, the nine-coordinate residual family had $R^{2}_{QP}=0.2715520808117803$ after controls, $R^{2}_{QP\mid S}=0.2706601505000995$, $R^{2}_{QP\mid T,M}=0.13773967696311287$, and $R^{2}_{QP\mid T,M,S}=0.13759011208318542$.  The structure-only fraction was $0.003284564452662019$, the structure-incremental fraction was $0.0005507778820193332$, and the $S_{Q}$ structure $R^{2}$ was $0.003774248455422586$ with right-tail permutation value $0.027972027972027972$.  This supports a small finite structural-order association in the controlled yeast surface, while also showing why the chapter must stay inside variance-partition language rather than causal pathway language.

\paragraph{Local boundary.}
The permitted statement is therefore the following: within the finite powered axis-identity audit, $f_{3}\text{-stress}$ is the only $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual coordinate that passes the fold-level sign-flip gate for structural-order increment, with held-out weighted increment $0.0031744502294756994$ and $p=0.03125$.  The statement does not assert measured translation realization, ribosome dynamics, protein folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, organismal phenotype, evolutionary selection, mechanism, or universality.  Any such stronger statement would require its own layer-matched contact and its own controlled statistic.  In particular, a direct residual-to-protein or residual-to-structure association is not enough unless the intervening translation readout is separately tested and survives the declared controls, including amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, CDS length, the $M$-density baseline, and matched mRNA abundance where the readout requires it.
