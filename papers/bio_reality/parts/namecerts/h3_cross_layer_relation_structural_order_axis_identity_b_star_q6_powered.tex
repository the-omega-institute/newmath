\paragraph{Local no-promotion rule.}
The chapter's positive statement is only this: in the powered structural-order audit, $f3\_stress$ is the unique $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ coordinate that carries the structural-order axis under the fold-level sign-flip gate, and the surrounding protein-abundance and structure-proxy rows keep the coordinate inside controlled cross-layer matrices.  No claim of translation realization, folded structure, physical admissibility, molecular function, biological mechanism, phenotype, selection, or universality follows from the axis identity alone.  Such a claim would require its own layer-matched measurement contact and its own controlled statistic.  In particular, a $q_i\to p_k$ protein-abundance association cannot be read as protein realization when the $q_i\to t_j$ translation step is null, untested, modeled-only beyond its declared scope, or not joined to the relevant rows.  CUN/UUR directionality and any single-organism sign are likewise kept as finite-row observations unless a multi-organism controlled design and simpler-baseline ablation support the stronger reading.
