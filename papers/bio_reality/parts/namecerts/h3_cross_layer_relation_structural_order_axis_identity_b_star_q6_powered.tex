\subsection{Structural-order axis identity for the powered $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual surface}
\label{subsec:bioreality-bstar-q6-structural-order-axis-identity}

\paragraph{Finite row surface.}
The finite object in this record is a joined row surface on nine $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates, evaluated against a structural-order readout after the residual coordinates have already been separated from amino-acid identity.  The computed coordinate list is
$$
\begin{aligned}
R_{Q6} = \{&K\_AAA,\ Arg\_AGR,\ Ile\_AUA,\ Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR,\ Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG,\\
&Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY,\ Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG,\ Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY,\ f3\_stress\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The powered audit used five folds, required at least $300$ joined rows per fold unit, and completed under experiment run \texttt{fac1206f6516}.  The declared result is not that all synonymous residual coordinates have the same structural role.  The finite comparison selected a single dominant coordinate: $f3\_stress$ was both the dominant loading axis and the dominant increment axis.

\paragraph{Axis-rank readback.}
The row-level conclusion is an axis-identity statement: the detectable structural-order contribution in this finite powered surface is carried by $f3\_stress$.  Its held-out increment and exact fold-level sign-flip value were
$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta R^2_{\mathrm{heldout}}(f3\_stress) &= 0.0031744502294756994,\\
p_{\mathrm{signflip}}(f3\_stress > 0) &= 0.03125,\\
|u_{f3\_stress}|_{\mathrm{weighted}} &= 0.5109586606724208.
\end{aligned}
$$
This is the only coordinate that passed the fold-level sign-flip gate.  The other eight residual coordinates were retained in the audit and did not pass that gate: $K\_AAA$ had $\Delta R^2=0.0005307046179682497$ with $p=0.2294921875$; $Arg\_AGR$ had $\Delta R^2=0.0020139270616115154$ with $p=0.142578125$; $Ile\_AUA$ had $\Delta R^2=0.0013067592718559024$ with $p=0.0693359375$; $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$ had $\Delta R^2=-0.0003205780368125179$ with $p=0.779296875$; $Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG$ had $\Delta R^2=0.00017615626369389472$ with $p=0.24609375$; $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$ had $\Delta R^2=0.0010134090056701938$ with $p=0.2158203125$; $Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG$ had $\Delta R^2=0.00004390882136492317$ with $p=0.4873046875$; and $Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY$ had $\Delta R^2=-0.00045340330387383447$ with $p=0.529296875$.

\paragraph{Local mathematical statement.}
Let $Q$ denote the nine-column residual matrix after the amino-acid quotient and the stated sequence controls, and let $Y_{\mathrm{ord}}$ denote the structural-order target used by the finite audit.  The finite statement supported here is
$$
\begin{aligned}
\operatorname*{argmax}_{q_i \in R_{Q6}}\ \Delta R^2_{\mathrm{heldout}}(q_i;Y_{\mathrm{ord}}) &= f3\_stress,\\
p_{\mathrm{signflip}}(f3\_stress > 0) &= 0.03125,\\
\#\{q_i : p_{\mathrm{signflip}}(q_i > 0) \leq 0.05\} &= 1.
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the result is an axis identity, not a blanket promotion of the whole nine-dimensional residual basis.  The full $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ signal was reported as significant, but the interpretable coordinate-level carrier is the $f3\_stress$ axis alone under this gate.

\paragraph{Controls and mediation context.}
The associated protein-abundance mediation audit used the same nine coordinates and controlled for an intercept, coding-sequence length, twenty amino-acid composition proportions, $GC3$, and the $M$-density baseline.  In the cross-organism mediation surface, $12$ organisms had joined CDS, modeled tAI, and PAXdb abundance rows, ranging from $1656$ joined proteins in \textit{Sulfolobus solfataricus} to $19395$ in mouse and $19053$ in human.  The mediation fractions were finite and organism-specific: yeast had $M^{QTP}=0.9491551041216973$ with total $R^2_{QP}=0.29837624994140827$ and direct $R^2_{QP\mid T}=0.0151709093608293$; \textit{Escherichia coli} K12 had $M^{QTP}=0.612922252793667$ with total $R^2_{QP}=0.10767526677985896$; human had $M^{QTP}=0.27206289160119157$ with total $R^2_{QP}=0.04454565118019448$; and \textit{Sulfolobus solfataricus} had $M^{QTP}=0.01850668564589326$ with total $R^2_{QP}=0.06397514251295629$.  These numbers show that translation-conditioned protein-abundance decompositions exist as finite statistical surfaces, but they do not by themselves explain the structural-order axis call.  The structural-order chapter keeps the axis identity separate from abundance mediation.

\paragraph{Structure-proxy comparison.}
A yeast structure-mediation run provides an additional local check on overinterpretation.  In that five-way join, $4759$ yeast rows connected CDS counts, protein abundance, measured translation efficiency, mRNA half-life, and a $5'$ coding-region MFE proxy.  The reported residual association between $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ and protein abundance was $R^2_{QP}=0.2715520808117803$.  Conditioning on the structure proxy alone changed it only to $R^2_{QP\mid S}=0.2706601505000995$, giving a structure-only fraction of $0.003284564452662019$.  The $Q$ to structure-proxy readout was small, $S\_Q\_structure\_R^2=0.003774248455422586$, with permutation right-tail value $0.027972027972027972$.  These finite values are compatible with the present axis finding only in the narrow sense that a small, controlled structure-related residual can be computed.  They are not a folding experiment and they do not turn $f3\_stress$ into a protein-structure mechanism.

\paragraph{Translation-readout boundary.}
The translation-survival precursor computed modeled tAI weights for three organisms, using GtRNAdb tRNA gene-copy counts and dos Reis wobble weights.  The payload contained all standard amino-acid tRNA families for yeast, human, and \textit{E. coli}; the modeled coupling surface had $81$ entries above threshold $0.01$, with maximum absolute $S_{ij}=3801961.031607896$.  However, the same record explicitly reports only $3$ organisms, below the requested powered survival level of at least $40$ matched species.  It is therefore a bounded modeled-tAI coupling surface, not a measured ribosome-profiling, proteomics, or universality result.  This matters for the present chapter because a structural-order axis may be named only after the finite structural-order audit; it is not licensed by modeled tAI alone.

\paragraph{Admissible readback.}
The admissible conclusion is the following finite-row claim.  Among the nine residual coordinates in the powered $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ surface, $f3\_stress$ is the unique coordinate that passes the fold-level sign-flip test for the structural-order readout, with rank $1$ by weighted held-out increment and $p=0.03125$.  The observed effect size is small in held-out $R^2$ units, so the result should be read as a stable coordinate identity rather than as a large predictive law.  Negative or nonpassing coordinates remain in the matrix as audited contrasts, not as failed biology.

\paragraph{Local non-promotion rule.}
No claim about translation, ribosome occupancy, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, organismal phenotype, causal mechanism, adaptation, or universality follows from this axis call alone.  A direct $Q_i \to P_k$ association is also not enough for protein realization unless a same-coordinate $Q_i \to T_j$ translation-readout step survives under the declared controls.  Protein abundance, mRNA abundance, modeled tAI, ribosome profiling, localization, turnover, folding, and function each require their own matched contact surface and their own finite statistic.  The present chapter therefore records a controlled structural-order coordinate identity: $f3\_stress$ is the supported axis in this finite powered audit, while every higher biological layer remains outside the claim unless independently measured and tested.
