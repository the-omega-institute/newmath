\paragraph{Finite surface.} The record studied here is the finite residual panel with nine $B^*_{Q6}$ coordinates,
$\texttt{K\_AAA}$, $\texttt{Arg\_AGR}$, $\texttt{Ile\_AUA}$, $\texttt{Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR}$, $\texttt{Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG}$, $\texttt{Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY}$, $\texttt{Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG}$, $\texttt{Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY}$, and $\texttt{f3\_stress}$. Each coordinate is kept as a residual coding-sequence coordinate, not as a direct protein-state observable. The experiment $\texttt{b\_star\_q6\_structural\_order\_axis\_identity\_powered}$ used five folds, required at least $300$ joined rows per evaluated fold surface, and used the deterministic seed $\texttt{sha256:8da7c86c999e5bee370c0c1a60a84722d81290b54b4dc2d4bd43c1cbd29fb394}$. Its recorded status is $\texttt{passed}$, with conclusion $\texttt{structural\_order\_carried\_by\_f3\_stress}$.

\paragraph{Axis test.} The test asks whether the structural-order readout carried by the powered residual panel is diffuse across synonymous residual coordinates or localized on one identifiable coordinate. The fold-level sign-flip gate selected exactly one significant coordinate. The selected coordinate was $\texttt{f3\_stress}$, and it was simultaneously the dominant increment axis and the dominant loading axis. The numerical assessment was
$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta R^2_{\mathrm{heldout}}(\texttt{f3\_stress}) &= 0.0031744502294756994,\\
p_{\mathrm{signflip}}(\texttt{f3\_stress}) &= 0.03125,\\
|u_{\mathrm{weighted}}(\texttt{f3\_stress})| &= 0.5109586606724208.
\end{aligned}
$$
The coordinate ranked first by weighted held-out increment and first by weighted loading. The full $B^*_{Q6}$ signal was significant in the registered gate, but the coordinate-level interpretation is narrower: the finite table identifies $\texttt{f3\_stress}$ as the carrier of the structural-order axis on this row set.

\paragraph{Non-dominant coordinates.} The remaining eight coordinates did not pass the same fold-level sign-flip gate. Their held-out increments and right-tail sign-flip values were: $\texttt{Arg\_AGR}$ with $\Delta R^2=0.0020139270616115154$ and $p=0.142578125$; $\texttt{Ile\_AUA}$ with $\Delta R^2=0.0013067592718559024$ and $p=0.0693359375$; $\texttt{K\_AAA}$ with $\Delta R^2=0.0005307046179682497$ and $p=0.2294921875$; $\texttt{Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR}$ with $\Delta R^2=-0.0003205780368125179$ and $p=0.779296875$; $\texttt{Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG}$ with $\Delta R^2=0.00017615626369389472$ and $p=0.24609375$; $\texttt{Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY}$ with $\Delta R^2=0.0010134090056701938$ and $p=0.2158203125$; $\texttt{Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG}$ with $\Delta R^2=0.00004390882136492317$ and $p=0.4873046875$; and $\texttt{Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY}$ with $\Delta R^2=-0.00045340330387383447$ and $p=0.529296875$. Thus the finite decision is not that every synonymous residual participates equally. The observed identity is sparse: one axis passes, and the others remain below the declared coordinate-level gate on this run.

Representative values were $0.9491551041216973$ in yeast, $0.612922252793667$ in $\textit{E. coli}$, $0.6165167748739756$ in fly, $0.27206289160119157$ in human, $0.15488799907323988$ in mouse, and $0.01850668564589326$ in $\textit{S. solfataricus}$. These numbers show that the residual panel has a bounded mediated-abundance surface under modeled tAI and protein-abundance rows, but they do not turn the structural-order axis into a causal translation mechanism.

\paragraph{Structure-related control context.} A yeast five-way join gave another finite control surface, with $4759$ joined rows across CDS codon counts, measured translation efficiency, mRNA stability, protein abundance, and a $5'$ coding-region folding proxy. The direct residual-to-protein association after controls was $R^2_{QP}=0.2715520808117803$. Conditioning on the structure proxy alone gave $R^2_{QP\mid S}=0.2706601505000995$; conditioning on measured TE and mRNA stability gave $R^2_{QP\mid T,M}=0.13773967696311287$; conditioning on all three gave $R^2_{QP\mid T,M,S}=0.13759011208318542$. The structure-only fraction was $0.003284564452662019$, the structure-incremental fraction was $0.0005507778820193332$, and the residual structure readout explained by $Q$ had $S_Q$ structure $R^2=0.003774248455422586$ with permutation $p=0.027972027972027972$. This is useful as a finite statistical neighbor for the axis call, but it remains a variance-partition result using a CDS folding proxy, not a direct measurement of folded protein structure.

\paragraph{Translation-readout boundary.} The modeled tAI survival contact used $3$ organisms and computed $81$ bounded-coupling entries above threshold $0.01$, with maximum absolute $S_{ij}=3801961.031607896$. The codon-count space contained $61$ sense codons, and all three local tRNA payloads covered the $20$ standard amino-acid families. This establishes a modeled translation-readout contact surface only. The record explicitly notes that $3$ organisms are insufficient for powered survival and that the result is modeled-tAI bounded coupling, not ribosome-profiling or proteomics validation.

\paragraph{Local interpretation.} The chapter-level claim is therefore the following finite statement. On the powered structural-order axis experiment, the $B^*_{Q6}$ panel has a significant full residual signal, and the coordinate-level identity of that signal is $\texttt{f3\_stress}$ with held-out increment $0.0031744502294756994$, sign-flip value $0.03125$, and weighted unit loading $0.5109586606724208$. The claim is intentionally narrower than a biological mechanism claim. It identifies which residual coordinate carries the structural-order readout on the registered finite rows. It does not assert that synonymous geometry directly realizes translation, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, phenotype, selection, mechanism, or a universal biological rule. Those stronger readings require separate layer-matched measurements and their own controlled finite statistics before they can be asserted.
