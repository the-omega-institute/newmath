&Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY,\ Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG,\ Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY,\ f3\_stress\}.

The finite experiment was run under identifier $b\_star\_q6\_structural\_order\_axis\_identity\_powered$ with seed $sha256:8da7c86c999e5bee370c0c1a60a84722d81290b54b4dc2d4bd43c1cbd29fb394$. The reported status is passed, but the pass is an axis-identification result, not a license to promote the coordinate to translation, folding, physical admissibility, function, mechanism, phenotype, selection, or universality.

\paragraph{Controlled interpretation.}
The result is best read as an axis-identity statement.  It says that, among the available residual coordinates, the third-position stress coordinate is the only coordinate that survives the finite fold-level discrimination used here.  It does not say that $f3\_stress$ is a universal law of coding sequence, nor that the signs observed in one organism or one panel can be exported to a population-wide rule.  The negative or weak increments for $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$, $Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY$, $Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG$, $Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG$, $K\_AAA$, $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$, $Ile\_AUA$, and $Arg\_AGR$ are part of the claim: they prevent the chapter from treating the entire residual basis as a single homogeneous structural-order driver.

\paragraph{Relation to translation and protein readouts.}
A separate translation-mediated protein-abundance computation used the same nine-coordinate family on $12$ organism surfaces and found positive statistical mediation fractions for the joint $Q\to T\to P$ decomposition.  Those fractions ranged from $0.01850668564589326$ in $Sulfolobus\ solfataricus$ to $0.9491551041216973$ in $Saccharomyces\ cerevisiae$, with examples including $0.612922252793667$ in $Escherichia\ coli$ K-12 MG1655, $0.27206289160119157$ in $Homo\ sapiens$, and $0.15488799907323988$ in $Mus\ musculus$.  The same computation used modeled per-protein tAI from GtRNAdb tRNA copy counts and dos Reis wobble weights, not observed ribosome occupancy.  A still smaller modeled-tAI survival calculation had only $3$ organisms and explicitly reported that $3$ organisms are insufficient for powered survival, even though $81$ bounded-coupling entries exceeded the $0.01$ threshold in the modeled matrix.  These companion facts constrain the present structural-order axis result: they support a finite coordinate identity and a bounded statistical relationship to modeled translation readouts, but they do not by themselves supply a measured translation mechanism or a structural experiment.

\paragraph{Scope boundary.}
The chapter therefore makes one positive claim and several local exclusions.  The positive claim is that the finite structural-order axis identity test identifies $f3\_stress$ as the unique significant $B^*_{Q6}$ coordinate on the recorded surface, with $p=0.03125$ and weighted held-out increment $0.0031744502294756994$.  The exclusions are equally important: no translation realization follows without a matched translation contact; no folding or structural mechanism follows without an independent structure-level contact; no physical admissibility, molecular function, organismal phenotype, selection mechanism, or universal biological rule follows from the residual coordinate or from the axis test alone.  Direct residual-to-protein or residual-to-structure language is admissible only as a controlled finite association unless the intermediate readout and the downstream layer are separately measured on matched rows.
