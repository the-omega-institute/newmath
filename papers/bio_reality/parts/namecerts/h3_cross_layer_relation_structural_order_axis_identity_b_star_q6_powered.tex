\paragraph{Finite row under audit.}
The record considered here is the finite panel of nine $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinates
$K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $Leu_{CUN\,vs\,UUR}$, $Leu_{UUA\,vs\,UUG}$, $Ser_{UCR\,vs\,AGY}$, $Ser_{UCA\,vs\,UCG}$, $Thr_{ACR\,vs\,ACY}$, and $f_3$-stress.  The powered audit asks whether the structural-order signal in this coordinate family is distributed across many synonymous axes or is concentrated on a single identifiable residual coordinate.  The finite run used five folds, required at least $300$ joined rows per evaluated surface, and reported status \emph{passed} for experiment run $fac1206f6516$ with seed $sha256:8da7c86c999e5bee370c0c1a60a84722d81290b54b4dc2d4bd43c1cbd29fb394$.  The reported conclusion is that structural order is carried by $f_3$-stress in this finite audit, because exactly one coordinate passed the fold-level sign-flip gate.

\paragraph{Axis decision rule.}
For each coordinate $q_i$, the run reports a held-out weighted increment $\Delta_i$ and a fold-level sign-flip right-tail probability $p_i$.  The finite decision is not a claim that the coordinate is a universal biological cause.  It is only the row-level assertion that one coordinate is the dominant loading and dominant held-out increment within the audited nine-coordinate panel.  The coordinate-wise surface is

\paragraph{Translation and protein-abundance context.}
The structural-order row is evaluated beside, not substituted for, translation and protein-abundance contacts.  The bounded translation-readout matrix computed modeled tAI weights from GtRNAdb tRNA gene copy counts and dos Reis wobble weights for three organisms.  It found $81$ modeled-tAI bounded-coupling entries above threshold $0.01$, with maximum absolute $S_{ij}=3801961.031607896$, while explicitly recording that $3$ organisms are insufficient for powered survival and that this is modeled-tAI coupling rather than ribosome-profiling or proteomics validation.  The same coordinate list appears in the larger protein-abundance mediation run, where per-protein modeled tAI and PAXdb abundance rows were joined across twelve organisms: $4042$ rows for \emph{Bacillus subtilis} strain 168, $11661$ for \emph{Caenorhabditis elegans}, $14410$ for \emph{Danio rerio}, $13295$ for \emph{Drosophila melanogaster}, $3739$ for \emph{Escherichia coli} K12 MG1655, $12418$ for \emph{Gallus gallus}, $19053$ for \emph{Homo sapiens}, $19395$ for \emph{Mus musculus}, $4129$ for \emph{Mycobacterium smegmatis}, $13322$ for \emph{Rattus norvegicus}, $5815$ for \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}, and $1656$ for \emph{Sulfolobus solfataricus}.  In that mediation surface, all twelve organism-level rank diagnostics report sufficient rank, and the mediated fraction $M^{QTP}$ ranges from $0.01850668564589326$ in \emph{S. solfataricus} to $0.9491551041216973$ in yeast.  These rows support a bounded statistical mediation description, not a causal translation mechanism.

\paragraph{Matched abundance and structure-proxy controls.}
The yeast five-way abundance decomposition provides a separate check that protein-abundance association is not allowed to stand alone as structural-order evidence.  It joined $4759$ yeast rows across CDS, protein abundance, translation efficiency, mRNA half-life, and a $5'$ coding-region MFE proxy, with reported CDS join hit rate $0.9156038419146592$ and overall join hit rate $0.8818572656921754$.  In that row set, $R^2_{QP}=0.2715520808117803$, $R^2_{QP\mid S}=0.2706601505000995$, $R^2_{QP\mid T,M}=0.13773967696311287$, and $R^2_{QP\mid T,M,S}=0.13759011208318542$.  The structure-only fraction is only $0.003284564452662019$, the structure-incremental fraction beyond measured translation efficiency and mRNA stability is $0.0005507778820193332$, and the permutation probability for the structure-incremental right tail is $0.1258741258741259$.  This is consistent with using the structural-order axis identity as a finite statistical classification of the residual panel, while refusing to convert it into a folded-structure pathway or physical-admissibility statement.

\paragraph{Negative and bounded companion rows.}
Several linked finite audits further constrain the interpretation.  The cross-ortholog selection-imprint run used $80$ orthogroups, $240$ rows, and $3$ species; it found $\Delta R^2=0.03250515015613468$ but a leave-clade-out $\Delta R^2=-0.0021477729853302424$ and maximum null95 $\Delta R^2=0.05427157066522958$, so the positive selection-imprint gate is false.  The dynamic mass-balance run used $120$ genes and $718$ intervals, with $R^2_{L0}=0.9960628201518571$, $R^2_{L1}=0.996410628851051$, $R^2_{L2}=0.9712443377743347$, $R^2_{L3}=0.8953999568421264$, and $\Delta R^2=-0.07584438093220836$, giving no positive closure beyond the declared bounded surface.  The turnover audit used $3290$ genes and $200$ null draws; $R^2_{L2}=0.055740851626933985$, $R^2_{L3}=0.050278872193227464$, $\Delta R^2=-0.005461979433706483$, $p_{matched\,bin}=0.8855721393034826$, and $p_{recoded}=1.0$, so the row reports no evidence that $B^*_{Q6}$ predicts measured turnover beyond expression, translation efficiency, pLDDT, N-end, and related controls.  The early finite-code contact remains separate as well: its module-classification family is represented among the target claims, the independent replication and boundary probes each have $40$ successes out of $40$ with one-sided binomial upper-tail probability $9.094947017729282\times 10^{-13}$, and the negative and ablation controls have $0$ unexpected successes out of $20$ each.  Those finite code-table successes do not raise the structural-order row into a higher biological layer.

\paragraph{Local no-promotion boundary.}
The accepted statement is therefore narrow.  In the audited nine-coordinate panel, $f_3$-stress is the unique dominant structural-order axis by held-out increment, loading, and sign-flip gate.  Nothing in this chapter asserts that $f_3$-stress causes translation, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, organismal phenotype, selection, mechanism, or universality.  A direct $Q_i\to P_k$ association is not sufficient for protein realization unless the same coordinate first survives a controlled $Q_i\to T_j$ translation-readout step and the downstream $Q_i\to T_j\to P_k$ surface is evaluated with amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, CDS length, M-density baseline, matched mRNA abundance, and simpler baseline controls.  Likewise, a modeled tAI or stAI contact remains a bounded codon-adaptation readout; ribosome profiling, matched proteomics, localization, turnover, stability, folded structure, physical assay, function assay, perturbation, and phenotype data each require their own layer-matched contact before any such statement is made.
