\subsection{B-star-Q6 structural-order axis identity after translation and abundance controls}
\label{sec:bioreality-bstar-q6-structural-order-axis-identity}

\paragraph{Finite surface.}
The record is a finite cross-layer surface whose rows are triples $(q_i,t_j,p_k)$.  The $q_i$ coordinates are the nine projected $B^*_{Q6}$ synonymous residual axes
$K\_AAA$, $Arg\_AGR$, $Ile\_AUA$, $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$, $Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG$, $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$, $Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG$, $Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY$, and $f3\_stress$.  The translation slot $t_j$ is read only through a bounded translation annotation or measurement, such as modeled tAI, stAI, ribosome occupancy, or translation efficiency.  The protein slot $p_k$ is a matched abundance readout, not a structural, functional, or phenotypic endpoint.  The admissible readback is therefore a controlled matrix $Q_i \to T_j \to P_k$, with amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, coding-sequence length, the $M$-density baseline, and matched mRNA abundance kept as controls rather than promoted to coordinates.

\paragraph{Axis identity statistic.}
The powered structural-order identity run used five folds, required at least $300$ joined rows per fold, and was seeded by $\mathrm{sha256{:}8da7c86c999e5bee370c0c1a60a84722d81290b54b4dc2d4bd43c1cbd29fb394}$.  The full nine-coordinate $B^*_{Q6}$ signal was significant, but the coordinate-level gate selected exactly one axis.  The dominant loading axis and the dominant held-out increment axis were both $f3\_stress$.  Its weighted absolute unit loading was $0.5109586606724208$, its held-out weighted $R^2$ increment was $0.0031744502294756994$, and its one-sided sign-flip probability was $0.03125$.

$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta R^2_{f3\_stress} &= 0.0031744502294756994,\\
p_{f3\_stress} &= 0.03125,\\
\lvert \lambda_{f3\_stress}\rvert &= 0.5109586606724208.
\end{aligned}
$$

All other axes remained below the fold-level sign-flip gate.  The next largest positive increments were $Arg\_AGR$ with $\Delta R^2=0.0020139270616115154$ and $p=0.142578125$, $Ile\_AUA$ with $\Delta R^2=0.0013067592718559024$ and $p=0.0693359375$, and $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$ with $\Delta R^2=0.0010134090056701938$ and $p=0.2158203125$.  $K\_AAA$ gave $\Delta R^2=0.0005307046179682497$ with $p=0.2294921875$; $Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG$ gave $0.00017615626369389472$ with $p=0.24609375$; $Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG$ gave $0.00004390882136492317$ with $p=0.4873046875$.  The negative increments were $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$ at $-0.0003205780368125179$ with $p=0.779296875$ and $Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY$ at $-0.00045340330387383447$ with $p=0.529296875$.  Thus the finite-row conclusion is not a diffuse nine-axis structural-order claim; it is the narrower statement that this fitted surface identifies $f3\_stress$ as the single surviving structural-order coordinate.

\paragraph{Translation and protein-abundance mediation context.}
The same coordinate list was evaluated in a twelve-organism protein-abundance mediation panel using per-protein modeled tAI as the translation readout.  The joined protein counts were $4042$ for \textit{Bacillus subtilis} 168, $11661$ for \textit{Caenorhabditis elegans}, $14410$ for \textit{Danio rerio}, $13295$ for \textit{Drosophila melanogaster}, $3739$ for \textit{Escherichia coli} K-12 MG1655, $12418$ for \textit{Gallus gallus}, $19053$ for human, $19395$ for mouse, $4129$ for \textit{Mycobacterium smegmatis}, $13322$ for rat, $5815$ for yeast, and $1656$ for \textit{Sulfolobus solfataricus}.  In every organism the residualized design retained rank sufficiency for the nine $q_i$ coordinates after the $23$-rank control block.  The scalar mediation fraction $M^{QTP}$ stayed inside $[0,1]$ in all twelve cases: $0.6084594553474505$, $0.4872268357227724$, $0.3157053953614641$, $0.6165167748739756$, $0.612922252793667$, $0.1976903964724546$, $0.27206289160119157$, $0.15488799907323988$, $0.08397602919861291$, $0.17489681798543982$, $0.9491551041216973$, and $0.01850668564589326$, in the organism order just listed.  The largest mediated fraction occurred in yeast, where $c=R^2_{QP}=0.29837624994140827$, $c'=R^2_{QP\mid T}=0.0151709093608293$, and $c-c'=0.28320534058057895$.

\paragraph{Structure-proxy decomposition in yeast.}
A separate yeast decomposition joined $4759$ rows across coding sequence, protein abundance, measured translation efficiency, mRNA half-life, and a sequence-derived structure proxy.  The structure readout was the ViennaRNA minimum-free-energy estimate for the first $39$ nucleotides of the coding region, not a folded-protein measurement.  After residualizing the $24$ control columns, the joint $B^*_{Q6}$ to protein-abundance association was $R^2_{QP}=0.2715520808117803$.  Conditioning on the structure proxy alone gave $R^2_{QP\mid S}=0.2706601505000995$, while conditioning on measured translation efficiency plus mRNA stability gave $R^2_{QP\mid T,M}=0.13773967696311287$ and adding the structure proxy gave $R^2_{QP\mid T,M,S}=0.13759011208318542$.  The structure-only fraction was $0.003284564452662019$, the incremental structure fraction beyond translation and mRNA stability was $0.0005507778820193332$, and $R^2(S\sim Q)$ was $0.003774248455422586$ with a right-tail permutation probability of $0.027972027972027972$.  These numbers are compatible with a small sequence-derived structure-proxy association, not with a direct folded-structure realization claim.

\paragraph{Readout boundary.}
The translation readout supporting the broader route was also tested in a smaller modeled-tAI contact using three organisms: \textit{E. coli} K-12, human, and yeast.  The tRNA payload contained all $20$ standard amino-acid families in each organism, with $87$ matched FASTA headers out of $89$ for \textit{E. coli}, $432$ out of $432$ for human, and $275$ out of $275$ for yeast.  The modeled-tAI coupling surface had $81$ entries above the $0.01$ threshold and maximum absolute entry $3801961.031607896$, but $n=3$ organisms is not a powered species panel.  It is therefore a bounded modeled-tAI contact, not ribosome-profiling validation.

\paragraph{Local conclusion.}
The finite-row verdict is that $f3\_stress$ is the only $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinate that passes the powered axis-identity gate for this structural-order surface.  The claim remains a controlled association and mediation record over finite rows.  A direct $Q_i\to P_k$ association is not admissible as protein realization unless the $Q_i\to T_j$ translation step is present and survives the declared controls.  No translation mechanism, folded structure, physical admissibility, molecular function, organismal phenotype, selection law, or universality follows from these rows alone; any such assertion would require a separate layer-matched contact and its own controlled statistic.
