\subsection{Powered structural-order axis identity for $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residuals}
\label{sec:bioreality-q6-structural-order-axis-identity}

\paragraph{Finite row object.}
The record is a finite cross-layer table whose rows are triples $(q_i,t_j,p_k)$, with $q_i$ drawn from the nine $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates, $t_j$ drawn from bounded translation readouts, and $p_k$ drawn from matched protein-abundance measurements.  The nine residual axes computed in the powered run were
$$
\begin{aligned}
Q=\{&K\_{AAA},\ Arg\_{AGR},\ Ile\_{AUA},\ Leu\_{CUN\,vs\,UUR},\ Leu\_{UUA\,vs\,UUG},\\
&Ser\_{UCR\,vs\,AGY},\ Ser\_{UCA\,vs\,UCG},\ Thr\_{ACR\,vs\,ACY},\ f_3\text{-stress}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The experiment identified the structural-order direction by five-fold held-out comparison with a fold-level sign-flip gate.  The finite run used $5$ folds, required at least $300$ joined rows per fitted surface, and reported status $\mathrm{passed}$.  The conclusion is not that every synonymous residual is structurally meaningful; it is the sharper finite claim that exactly one coordinate passed the fold-level sign-flip filter, and that coordinate was $f_3$-stress.

\paragraph{Axis identity.}
The powered axis identity is the assertion that the structural-order contribution carried by the computed $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ panel concentrates on the $f_3$-stress coordinate.  In the run, $f_3$-stress was simultaneously the dominant increment axis and the dominant loading axis.  Its weighted held-out increment was positive, its loading was the largest recorded weighted unit loading, and its sign-flip test passed the declared one-sided gate:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta R^2_{\mathrm{heldout}}(f_3\text{-stress})&=0.0031744502294756994,\\
p_{\mathrm{signflip}}(f_3\text{-stress})&=0.03125,\\
|u_{\mathrm{weighted}}(f_3\text{-stress})|&=0.5109586606724208.
\end{aligned}
$$
The remaining eight axes did not pass that fold-level gate.  Their weighted held-out increments and sign-flip probabilities were: $Arg\_{AGR}$, $0.0020139270616115154$ with $p=0.142578125$; $Ile\_{AUA}$, $0.0013067592718559024$ with $p=0.0693359375$; $K\_{AAA}$, $0.0005307046179682497$ with $p=0.2294921875$; $Leu\_{CUN\,vs\,UUR}$, $-0.0003205780368125179$ with $p=0.779296875$; $Leu\_{UUA\,vs\,UUG}$, $0.00017615626369389472$ with $p=0.24609375$; $Ser\_{UCA\,vs\,UCG}$, $0.00004390882136492317$ with $p=0.4873046875$; $Ser\_{UCR\,vs\,AGY}$, $0.0010134090056701938$ with $p=0.2158203125$; and $Thr\_{ACR\,vs\,ACY}$, $-0.00045340330387383447$ with $p=0.529296875$.  Thus the chapter records an axis identity, not a diffuse multi-axis law.

\paragraph{Translation and abundance conditioning.}
The same coordinate family also has a bounded translation-to-protein mediation surface, but that surface is used here only to keep the structural-order axis from being read as a direct residual-to-protein assertion.  In the multi-organism protein-abundance mediation run, $12$ organisms were joined to real CDS codon counts, modeled per-protein tAI, and PAXdb abundance.  Joined protein counts ranged from $1656$ for \emph{Sulfolobus solfataricus} to $19395$ for mouse, with $19053$ human rows and $5815$ yeast rows.  After residualizing amino-acid composition, $GC3$, CDS length, and the $M$-density baseline, all organism-specific rank checks reported $\mathrm{powered\_rank\_sufficient}=\mathrm{true}$ for the nine-coordinate $Q$ panel.

The mediation scalar $M^{QTP}$ stayed inside the unit interval for every organism.  The largest fraction was yeast, $M^{QTP}=0.9491551041216973$, with total $R^2_{QP}=0.29837624994140827$, direct $R^2_{QP\mid T}=0.0151709093608293$, and indirect difference $0.28320534058057895$.  Other positive bounded fractions included \emph{Escherichia coli} $0.612922252793667$, \emph{Bacillus subtilis} $0.6084594553474505$, \emph{Drosophila melanogaster} $0.6165167748739756$, \emph{Caenorhabditis elegans} $0.4872268357227724$, human $0.27206289160119157$, mouse $0.15488799907323988$, rat $0.17489681798543982$, chicken $0.1976903964724546$, zebrafish $0.3157053953614641$, \emph{Mycobacterium smegmatis} $0.08397602919861291$, and \emph{Sulfolobus solfataricus} $0.01850668564589326$.  These numbers justify retaining the finite mediation ordering $Q\to T\to P$ as a statistical decomposition surface, while still forbidding a direct interpretation of $Q\to P$ as protein realization when the translation step is absent or untested.

\paragraph{Modeled translation contact.}
A separate modeled-tAI contact establishes only a bounded translation readout, not observed ribosome dynamics.  The local tRNA payload covered all $20$ standard amino-acid families for \emph{E. coli} K-12, human, and yeast.  The corresponding FASTA header counts were $89$, $432$, and $275$, with matched counts $87$, $432$, and $275$.  The modeled-tAI matrix used $61$ sense codons in each organism and had zero raw-weight fallback count $0$ in all three organisms.  It produced $81$ entries above the coupling threshold $0.01$, with maximum absolute entry $3801961.031607896$.  Because the organism count was only $3$, the record explicitly remains modeled and underpowered for a powered survival claim requiring at least $40$ matched species.

\paragraph{Structure-proxy decomposition in yeast.}
The yeast five-way join provides an additional finite check against confusing structural-order wording with causal folding evidence.  The joined table contained $4759$ rows and combined real CDS codon counts, protein abundance, measured translation efficiency, mRNA half-life, and a CDS-derived $5'$ coding-region RNAfold MFE proxy.  The join hit rate reported for the five-way table was $0.8818572656921754$, and the CDS join hit rate was $0.9156038419146592$.  In that table, the residualized $Q$ panel had
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{QP}&=0.2715520808117803,\\
R^2_{QP\mid S}&=0.2706601505000995,\\
R^2_{QP\mid T,M}&=0.13773967696311287,\\
R^2_{QP\mid T,M,S}&=0.13759011208318542.
\end{aligned}
$$
The structure-only fraction was $0.003284564452662019$, the structure-incremental fraction beyond measured translation efficiency and mRNA stability was $0.0005507778820193332$, and $S_Q$ structure $R^2$ was $0.003774248455422586$ with permutation right-tail probability $0.027972027972027972$.  These values are finite variance-partition evidence for a weak structure-proxy association, not an observation of folding, stability, physical feasibility, or molecular function.

\paragraph{Null boundary.}
The admissible reading is therefore narrow.  A $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ coordinate may enter this chapter only as a controlled residual coordinate, and a protein-abundance association may be discussed only through the ordered matrix $Q_i\to T_j\to P_k$ when the translation readout is present.  Amino-acid sequence, organism, $GC3$, CDS length, and $M$-density remain controls rather than promoted coordinates; matched mRNA abundance remains a transcription-layer control rather than a translation readout.  No translation realization, ribosome mechanism, folded structure, physical admissibility, molecular function, phenotype, selection claim, or universal biological law follows from the powered $f_3$-stress axis identity unless a separate layer-matched measurement supplies that specific contact and the corresponding controlled statistic.

\paragraph{Verdict.}
The finite-row verdict is ready for the standalone BioReality paper.  The powered structural-order run supports the claim that the detected structural-order signal in this $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ panel is carried by $f_3$-stress: it was the only significant axis, with $p=0.03125$ and weighted held-out increment $0.0031744502294756994$.  The connected translation and protein-abundance rows keep the direction of interpretation constrained to a controlled mediation matrix rather than a direct residual-to-protein assertion.  The record is strong enough to state a powered axis identity, but only under the stated finite-row controls and no-promotion boundary.
