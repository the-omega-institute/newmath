It does not assert that codon geometry has become a direct measurement of translation, folded structure, physical admissibility, molecular function, organismal phenotype, or a universal biological law.

\paragraph{Local verdict.}
The ready claim is an axis-identity claim on a finite controlled surface.  The structural-order increment is detected for the full $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual set, and the only coordinate passing the fold-level sign-flip gate is $f3\_stress$ with $\Delta R^2=0.0031744502294756994$, $p=0.03125$, and weighted absolute unit loading $0.5109586606724208$.  The claim should therefore be read as: on the tested finite row surface, structural-order signal is concentrated on the $f3\_stress$ residual axis.  No direct statement about translation, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, mechanism, phenotype, selection, or universality follows from this row surface alone.  Those stronger readings require their own layer-matched measurements and controls, and direct $Q_i \to P_k$ associations remain inadmissible as protein-realization evidence unless the same coordinate first survives an appropriate $Q_i \to T_j$ translation-readout step.
