\paragraph{Finite mediation surface.}
The finite object in this record is the controlled row surface whose coordinates are the nine $B^*_{Q6}$ residual axes
$K\_{AAA}$, $Arg\_{AGR}$, $Ile\_{AUA}$, $Leu\_{CUN\,vs\,UUR}$, $Leu\_{UUA\,vs\,UUG}$, $Ser\_{UCR\,vs\,AGY}$, $Ser\_{UCA\,vs\,UCG}$, $Thr\_{ACR\,vs\,ACY}$, and $f_3$-stress.  The powered structural-order run evaluated these axes on five folds with a minimum joined-row requirement of $300$ and used the deterministic seed $sha256:8da7c86c999e5bee370c0c1a60a84722d81290b54b4dc2d4bd43c1cbd29fb394$.  The reported conclusion is not that every synonymous residual has structural content.  It is the sharper finite statement that, inside this particular controlled axis panel, exactly one coordinate passed the fold-level sign-flip gate and that the winning coordinate was $f_3$-stress.

\paragraph{Axis statistics.}
The fold-level increment table separates the dominant coordinate from the rest of the residual panel.  The $f_3$-stress coordinate had weighted held-out $R^2$ increment $0.0031744502294756994$ with right-tail sign-flip probability $0.03125$.  Its weighted absolute unit loading was $0.5109586606724208$, and it ranked first both by increment and by loading.  The other eight coordinates did not pass the same fold-level gate: $Arg\_{AGR}$ had increment $0.0020139270616115154$ and $p=0.142578125$; $Ile\_{AUA}$ had increment $0.0013067592718559024$ and $p=0.0693359375$; $K\_{AAA}$ had increment $0.0005307046179682497$ and $p=0.2294921875$; $Leu\_{CUN\,vs\,UUR}$ had increment $-0.0003205780368125179$ and $p=0.779296875$; $Leu\_{UUA\,vs\,UUG}$ had increment $0.00017615626369389472$ and $p=0.24609375$; $Ser\_{UCR\,vs\,AGY}$ had increment $0.0010134090056701938$ and $p=0.2158203125$; $Ser\_{UCA\,vs\,UCG}$ had increment $0.00004390882136492317$ and $p=0.4873046875$; and $Thr\_{ACR\,vs\,ACY}$ had increment $-0.00045340330387383447$ and $p=0.529296875$.  Thus the finite readout is an axis-identity result, not a broad promotion of all $B^*_{Q6}$ coordinates.

\paragraph{Translation and protein-abundance context.}
The structural-order axis call is read together with bounded translation and protein-abundance contacts, but it is not identical to either of them. That run explicitly remained underpowered for powered survival because $3$ organisms are below the required $40$ matched species. The controls used around the related protein and structure contacts keep amino-acid composition, GC3, coding-sequence length, organism identity, and the $M$-density baseline out of the promoted coordinate set.  In the yeast structure-mediation audit, the five-way join contained $4759$ genes, with reported CDS join hit rate $0.9156038419146592$ and overall join hit rate $0.8818572656921754$. These values support a cautious reading: there is a measurable axis identity for structural order, but the finite rows do not license a broad claim that synonymous residuals determine folded structure.

\paragraph{Negative and bounded companion results.}
Several companion finite-row runs restrict the interpretation.  The cross-ortholog selection-imprint audit used $80$ orthogroups, $240$ rows, and $3$ species; it found $\Delta R^2=0.03250515015613468$ but failed the positive selection-imprint gate because the leave-clade-out increment was $-0.0021477729853302424$ and the maximum null $95$ percent increment was $0.05427157066522958$.  The dynamic mass-balance run used $120$ genes and $718$ intervals, but its later layer decreased held-out fit from $R^2=0.9712443377743347$ to $0.8953999568421264$, giving $\Delta R^2=-0.07584438093220836$ and $\Delta DL=-668.8046070381928$ bits.  The turnover-residual audit used $3290$ genes and found $\Delta R^2=-0.005461979433706483$ with matched-bin $p=0.8855721393034826$ and recoded $p=1.0$.  By contrast, an observable execution decomposition in yeast reported an observed rate-proxy subblock on $3289$ genes.  The Phase-one genetic-code contact is even more upstream: it had $40$ of $40$ successes in its confirmatory and replication binomial tests, both with upper-tail probability $9.094947017729282\times 10^{-13}$, and $0$ unexpected successes among $20$ negative controls and $20$ ablations.  Those finite rows are useful boundary checks, not licenses to collapse codon geometry, translation, protein abundance, structure, function, and selection into one claim.

\paragraph{Local readback.}
The allowed statement is therefore local and finite.  In the powered structural-order panel, the full $B^*_{Q6}$ signal was significant, but the identity of the structural-order direction was carried by $f_3$-stress alone.  Direct residual-to-protein wording is not admitted unless the same residual first survives an explicitly bounded translation-readout step.  Transcription-only effects are separated by matched mRNA abundance controls.  Amino-acid sequence, organism, GC3, coding-sequence length, and the $M$-density baseline remain controls rather than promoted biological coordinates.  No translation realization, ribosome mechanism, folded structure, physical admissibility, molecular function, phenotype, selection law, or universality follows from this chapter unless a separate layer-matched contact supplies rows for that layer and the declared controlled statistic is computed there.
