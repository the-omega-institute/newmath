\subsection{Axis identity for the $B^\ast_{Q6}$ structural-order signal}
\label{sec:bioreality-bstar-q6-structural-order-axis-identity}

\paragraph{Finite surface.}
The empirical object in this chapter is a finite row surface in which each coding sequence is represented by nine $B^\ast_{Q6}$ residual coordinates and then tested against a structural-order readout under held-out evaluation.  The coordinates are
$$
\begin{aligned}
Q=\{&K_{AAA},\ Arg_{AGR},\ Ile_{AUA},\ Leu_{CUN\,vs\,UUR},\ Leu_{UUA\,vs\,UUG},\\
&Ser_{UCR\,vs\,AGY},\ Ser_{UCA\,vs\,UCG},\ Thr_{ACR\,vs\,ACY},\ f_3\text{-stress}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The run used five folds, required at least $300$ joined rows per fold-level test, and completed with status \emph{passed}.  The finite question is not whether all synonymous residuals behave alike.  It is whether the structural-order increment carried by the nine-dimensional residual packet can be assigned to a particular coordinate after fold-level sign-flip testing.

\paragraph{Axis test.}
For each coordinate $q\in Q$, the statistic was the weighted held-out $R^2$ increment obtained by adding that coordinate to the controlled structural-order fit, paired with a sign-flip probability for the one-sided greater-than-zero test.  The observed increments were
$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta R^2(K_{AAA})&=0.0005307046179682497,& p&=0.2294921875,\\
\Delta R^2(Arg_{AGR})&=0.0020139270616115154,& p&=0.142578125,\\
\Delta R^2(Ile_{AUA})&=0.0013067592718559024,& p&=0.0693359375,\\
\Delta R^2(Leu_{CUN\,vs\,UUR})&=-0.0003205780368125179,& p&=0.779296875,\\
\Delta R^2(Leu_{UUA\,vs\,UUG})&=0.00017615626369389472,& p&=0.24609375,\\
\Delta R^2(Ser_{UCR\,vs\,AGY})&=0.0010134090056701938,& p&=0.2158203125,\\
\Delta R^2(Ser_{UCA\,vs\,UCG})&=0.00004390882136492317,& p&=0.4873046875,\\
\Delta R^2(Thr_{ACR\,vs\,ACY})&=-0.00045340330387383447,& p&=0.529296875,\\
\Delta R^2(f_3\text{-stress})&=0.0031744502294756994,& p&=0.03125.
\end{aligned}
$$
Exactly one coordinate passed the fold-level sign-flip gate.  The same coordinate, $f_3$-stress, was also the dominant loading axis, with weighted absolute unit loading $0.5109586606724208$ and rank $1$ by weighted increment.  Thus the chapter-level readout is an axis-identity claim: within this finite tested surface, the detectable structural-order component of the $B^\ast_{Q6}$ packet is carried by $f_3$-stress, not by the leucine, serine, threonine, lysine, arginine, or isoleucine residual coordinates individually.

\paragraph{Matrix form.}
The finite result can be summarized as a controlled rank selection over the coordinate list.  Let $I(q)$ denote the held-out increment and let $P(q)$ denote the sign-flip probability.  The observed decision rule was
$$
\begin{aligned}
q_{\mathrm{dom}}&=\arg\max_{q\in Q} I(q),\\
q_{\mathrm{dom}}&=f_3\text{-stress},\\
I(q_{\mathrm{dom}})&=0.0031744502294756994,\\
P(q_{\mathrm{dom}})&=0.03125.
\end{aligned}
$$
Since no other coordinate crossed the same fold-level gate, the positive statement is deliberately narrow: the nine-dimensional packet has a significant structural-order signal, and the finite axis carrying that signal is $f_3$-stress.  The negative part is equally important: a positive packet-level result is not a license to promote every synonymous residual direction into a structural-order coordinate.

\paragraph{Relation to translation and abundance contacts.}
Separate finite contacts show why the result must remain layered.  A modeled translation-contact run computed the same nine residual coordinates for three organisms and found $81$ modeled-tAI coupling entries above threshold $0.01$, with maximum absolute $S_{ij}=3801961.031607896$; however, that run explicitly had only $3$ organisms and did not constitute powered measured translation survival.  A larger protein-abundance mediation run joined $12$ organisms, including $5815$ proteins for \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}, $19053$ for \emph{Homo sapiens}, $19395$ for \emph{Mus musculus}, $14410$ for \emph{Danio rerio}, and $3739$ for \emph{Escherichia coli} K12 MG1655.  In that protein-abundance analysis, the $B^\ast_{Q6}$ packet had positive statistical mediation fractions through modeled per-protein tAI, for example $M^{QTP}=0.9491551041216973$ in yeast, $0.612922252793667$ in \emph{E. coli}, $0.27206289160119157$ in human, and $0.15488799907323988$ in mouse.  These numbers show that downstream abundance decompositions exist as separate finite tables, but they do not convert the structural-order axis call into translation mechanism, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, phenotype, or universality.

\paragraph{Yeast structure-adjacent decomposition.}
A yeast-specific decomposition also illustrates the same boundary.  In a five-way joined yeast table with $4759$ rows, the residualized $B^\ast_{Q6}$ packet explained protein-abundance variance with $R^2_{QP}=0.2715520808117803$.  Conditioning on a $5'$ coding-region MFE proxy changed this only to $R^2_{QP\mid S}=0.2706601505000995$, while conditioning on measured translation efficiency and mRNA stability gave $R^2_{QP\mid T,M}=0.13773967696311287$, and conditioning on all three gave $R^2_{QP\mid T,M,S}=0.13759011208318542$.  The structure-only fraction was $0.003284564452662019$, the structure-incremental fraction was $0.0005507778820193332$, and the permutation probabilities were $0.027972027972027972$ for the $Q$-to-structure proxy association and $0.1258741258741259$ for the incremental structure fraction.  This is a statistical variance partition around a sequence-derived MFE proxy, not a folding experiment.  It is consistent with keeping the $f_3$-stress result as an axis identity on a finite structural-order readout rather than as a molecular structure claim.

\paragraph{Local claim boundary.}
The admissible conclusion is therefore the following.  On the tested finite row surface, the $B^\ast_{Q6}$ structural-order signal is significant as a packet and is axis-resolved to $f_3$-stress, with held-out increment $0.0031744502294756994$, sign-flip probability $0.03125$, and weighted absolute unit loading $0.5109586606724208$.  The chapter does not claim that $f_3$-stress is a universal biological law, a translation mechanism, a ribosome-occupancy measurement, a folding determinant, a physical-admissibility certificate, a function realization, or an organismal phenotype.  Those stronger readings require their own layer-matched measurements and controls.  A direct residual-to-protein or residual-to-structure association is not promoted here unless the relevant intermediate readout is separately supplied and survives its own controlled finite test.
