It is an axis-identity test inside the controlled residual surface: a coordinate may be named as the structural-order carrier only if the fold-level increment and loading diagnostics isolate that coordinate under the declared controls.

The full $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ signal was marked significant, but the coordinate-level gate admitted only $f_3$-stress.  This matters because the claim is not that the whole residual basis has an undifferentiated structural-order effect.  The finite-row reading is narrower: after the fold-level sign-flip test, the only coordinate permitted to carry the structural-order axis identity in this run is $f_3$-stress.

\paragraph{Translation-readout boundary.}
The modeled-tAI survival matrix supplies a bounded translation-layer contact, not an observed ribosome trace.  In the three-organism modeled-tAI computation, $61$ sense codons were used, $9$ residual coordinates were computed, the coupling threshold was $0.01$, and $81$ entries exceeded that threshold.  The maximum absolute entry was $3801961.031607896$, equal to the maximum absolute diagonal entry in that run.  The organism count was $3$, with standard amino-acid family coverage of $20$ in \textit{E. coli} K12, human, and yeast; matched FASTA header counts were $87$, $432$, and $275$, respectively.  The run explicitly stayed underpowered for a powered survival claim because $3$ organisms are insufficient for the requested multi-organism survival level.  This is why the structural-order axis identity cannot be read as observed translation realization.  It is a controlled axis call, while measured translation requires organism- or CDS-matched rows from a translation contact such as ribosome profiling, translation efficiency, or an explicitly bounded weight surface.

\paragraph{Local no-promotion rule.}
The chapter therefore permits the following statement and no stronger one: in the powered finite-row structural-order experiment, $f_3$-stress is the unique $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual coordinate that passed the fold-level sign-flip gate and simultaneously served as the dominant increment and loading axis.  Nothing in this chapter establishes translation, folded structure, physical admissibility, molecular function, organismal phenotype, mechanism, selection, or universality.  Those interpretations require their own layer-matched contacts and their own controlled statistics.  The Phase 1 confirmation record illustrates the same discipline at a lower layer: $40$ of $40$ independent replication trials and $40$ of $40$ boundary-probe trials succeeded with one-sided binomial upper-tail values $9.094947017729282\times 10^{-13}$, while $20$ negative controls and $20$ critical ablations had $0$ unexpected successes.  That strong codon-geometry contact still did not license translation, protein structure, physical admissibility, function, or a global law.  The present axis identity is likewise bounded: it names a finite residual coordinate that survived the declared structural-order axis test, and it stops there.
