\subsection{Structural-Order Axis Identity for the Powered $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ Residual Surface}
\label{sec:h3-cross-layer-relation-structural-order-axis-identity-b-star-q6-powered}

\paragraph{Finite surface.}
This record concerns a finite mediation surface whose rows are triples $(q_i,t_j,p_k)$.  The $q_i$ coordinates are the nine projected $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ synonymous residual axes
$K\_AAA$, $Arg\_AGR$, $Ile\_AUA$, $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$, $Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG$, $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$, $Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG$, $Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY$, and $f3\_stress$.  The admissible readout is not a direct residual-to-protein arrow.  A protein-abundance entry is read only after a translation-coordinate stage, with amino-acid composition, organism, $GC3$, coding-sequence length, the $M$-density baseline, and matched mRNA abundance kept in the conditioning set rather than promoted to the residual coordinate list.  The coordinate rank is finite: in the translation-mediated protein-abundance computation the residual block has coordinate count $9$, the control block has column count $24$, and, after residualization, $rank(X)=9$, $rank(T)=1$, and $rank(X,T)=10$ in every organism-specific row block reported for the twelve-organism panel.

\paragraph{Axis identity statistic.}
The powered structural-order audit used five held-out folds, a minimum joined-row requirement of $300$, and deterministic seed $sha256:8da7c86c999e5bee370c0c1a60a84722d81290b54b4dc2d4bd43c1cbd29fb394$.  Exactly one coordinate passed the fold-level sign-flip gate.  The dominant increment axis and the dominant loading axis are both $f3\_stress$, with weighted absolute unit loading $0.5109586606724208$, weighted held-out $\Delta R^2=0.0031744502294756994$, and one-sided sign-flip probability $p=0.03125$.  The remaining axes did not pass that gate: $K\_AAA$ has $\Delta R^2=0.0005307046179682497$ and $p=0.2294921875$; $Arg\_AGR$ has $\Delta R^2=0.0020139270616115154$ and $p=0.142578125$; $Ile\_AUA$ has $\Delta R^2=0.0013067592718559024$ and $p=0.0693359375$; $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$ has $\Delta R^2=-0.0003205780368125179$ and $p=0.779296875$; $Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG$ has $\Delta R^2=0.00017615626369389472$ and $p=0.24609375$; $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$ has $\Delta R^2=0.0010134090056701938$ and $p=0.2158203125$; $Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG$ has $\Delta R^2=0.00004390882136492317$ and $p=0.4873046875$; and $Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY$ has $\Delta R^2=-0.00045340330387383447$ and $p=0.529296875$.  In compact form, the selected axis is therefore
$$
\begin{aligned}
q_{\mathrm{selected}} &= f3\_stress,\\
\Delta R^2_{\mathrm{held\text{-}out}} &= 0.0031744502294756994,\\
p_{\mathrm{signflip}} &= 0.03125,\\
|\ell|_{\mathrm{weighted}} &= 0.5109586606724208.
\end{aligned}
$$
This is an axis-identity statement inside the finite residual surface: the positive result is that the structural-order signal carried by the powered audit localizes to $f3\_stress$, not to the leucine, serine, threonine, lysine, arginine, or isoleucine synonymous contrasts individually.

\paragraph{Translation and abundance rows.}
The downstream protein-abundance mediation panel supplies the row context for why the axis identity is cross-layer rather than purely codon-count internal.  The panel joins real CDS codon counts, modeled per-protein tAI, and measured PAXdb abundance for twelve organisms: $4042$ joined rows in \emph{Bacillus subtilis} subsp. subtilis str. 168, $11661$ in \emph{Caenorhabditis elegans}, $14410$ in \emph{Danio rerio}, $13295$ in \emph{Drosophila melanogaster}, $3739$ in \emph{Escherichia coli} K-12 MG1655, $12418$ in \emph{Gallus gallus}, $19053$ in \emph{Homo sapiens}, $19395$ in \emph{Mus musculus}, $4129$ in \emph{Mycobacterium smegmatis} str. mc2 155, $13322$ in \emph{Rattus norvegicus}, $5815$ in \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}, and $1656$ in \emph{Sulfolobus solfataricus}.  The modeled tAI readout is the per-protein codon-count mean of tAI weights built from GtRNAdb all-tRNA copy counts and dos Reis wobble weights.  It is a bounded translation annotation, not a ribosome-footprint observation.

\paragraph{Mediation magnitudes.}
Within that twelve-organism matrix, the joint nine-dimensional residual block has positive total residual-to-protein association in every organism, and conditioning on modeled tAI reduces that association by an organism-specific fraction $M^{QTP}$.  The largest reported mediated fraction is in \emph{S. cerevisiae}, where $n=5815$, $R^2_{QP}=0.29837624994140827$, $R^2_{QP\mid T}=0.0151709093608293$, and $M^{QTP}=0.9491551041216973$.  Other high fractions occur in \emph{D. melanogaster} with $M^{QTP}=0.6165167748739756$, \emph{E. coli} K-12 MG1655 with $M^{QTP}=0.612922252793667$, and \emph{B. subtilis} with $M^{QTP}=0.6084594553474505$.  Lower but still positive fractions appear in \emph{S. solfataricus} with $M^{QTP}=0.01850668564589326$, \emph{M. smegmatis} with $M^{QTP}=0.08397602919861291$, and \emph{M. musculus} with $M^{QTP}=0.15488799907323988$.  These values justify keeping the matrix as a controlled mediation readback rather than collapsing it to a direct $q_i \to p_k$ association.

\paragraph{Yeast structure-side audit.}
The yeast five-way join gives a separate finite check on the abundance residual setting.  It joins $4759$ rows across protein abundance, measured translation efficiency, mRNA half-life, CDS counts, and a $5'$ coding-region folding-free-energy proxy.  The residualized block has $rank(Z)=23$, $rank(X)=9$, $rank(T,M)=2$, $rank(T,M,S)=3$, and residual degrees of freedom $4736$.  The abundance association is $R^2_{QP}=0.2715520808117803$; conditioning on the structure proxy alone gives $R^2_{QP\mid S}=0.2706601505000995$; conditioning on measured translation efficiency plus mRNA stability gives $R^2_{QP\mid T,M}=0.13773967696311287$; and conditioning on all three gives $R^2_{QP\mid T,M,S}=0.13759011208318542$.  The structure-only fraction is $0.003284564452662019$, while the incremental structure fraction beyond translation efficiency and mRNA stability is $0.0005507778820193332$.  The $S_Q$ structure readback has $R^2=0.003774248455422586$ with permutation right-tail probability $0.027972027972027972$, whereas the incremental structure fraction has permutation right-tail probability $0.1258741258741259$.  Thus the same finite row family contains a weak structure-proxy association, but not a basis for treating the $f3\_stress$ axis as folded-structure realization.

\paragraph{Modeled translation boundary.}
A smaller modeled-tAI survival contact was also computed for three organisms, with standard amino-acid tRNA families present for \emph{E. coli} K-12, \emph{H. sapiens}, and \emph{S. cerevisiae}.  It reports $81$ entries above the bounded coupling threshold $0.01$, maximum absolute $S_{ij}=3801961.031607896$, and maximum absolute diagonal $S_i=3801961.031607896$.  However, $n=3$ organisms is insufficient for powered survival, and the computation itself records that powered survival requires at least $40$ matched species.  The broader Phase One confirmatory table has $40$ successes out of $40$ in the independent replication statistic, one-sided binomial upper-tail probability $9.094947017729282\times 10^{-13}$, and zero unexpected successes in $20$ negative controls and $20$ critical ablations; that earlier contact supports codon-table geometry only and does not promote this chapter to a universal biological rule.

\paragraph{Local verdict and boundary.}
The chapter verdict is that the powered finite-row audit identifies $f3\_stress$ as the unique significant $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ structural-order axis in the tested residual surface, with the result embedded in a controlled translation-mediated protein-abundance setting.  The result remains a statistical finite-row identity: it permits the statement that the powered axis signal is concentrated on $f3\_stress$ and that downstream abundance associations should be read through the translation stage when that stage is present.  It does not, by itself, establish observed translation dynamics, ribosome occupancy, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, biological mechanism, phenotype, selection, or universality.  Any such assertion would require a separate layer-matched measurement and its own controlled statistic before it could enter the interpretation.
