\subsection{Structural-order axis identity for the powered $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual surface}\label{sec:bioreality-h3-structural-order-axis-identity-b-star-q6-powered}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite row surface.}
The object tested here is a finite residual-coordinate surface on nine $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ coordinates,
$$
\begin{aligned}
Q=\{&\mathrm{K\_AAA},\ \mathrm{Arg\_AGR},\ \mathrm{Ile\_AUA},\ \mathrm{Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR},\ \mathrm{Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG},\\
&\mathrm{Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY},\ \mathrm{Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG},\ \mathrm{Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY},\ f_3\text{-}\mathrm{stress}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The powered structural-order audit was run as experiment \texttt{b\_star\_q6\_structural\_order\_axis\_identity\_powered} with run id \texttt{fac1206f6516}, five held-out folds, and minimum joined-row requirement $300$. The finite conclusion is not that every synonymous residual coordinate carries structural-order information. The row-level conclusion is sharper: exactly one coordinate passed the fold-level sign-flip gate, and that coordinate was $f_3\text{-}\mathrm{stress}$. In the audit output, $f_3\text{-}\mathrm{stress}$ was both the dominant increment axis and the dominant loading axis, with weighted absolute unit loading $0.5109586606724208$, weighted held-out $\Delta R^2=0.0031744502294756994$, rank $1$ by weighted increment, and sign-flip right-tail probability $p=0.03125$.

\paragraph{Axis discrimination.}
The coordinate comparison is important because the claim is an axis-identity claim, not a generic positive signal claim. The remaining eight coordinates did not pass the same gate. Their weighted held-out increments and sign-flip probabilities were
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{Arg\_AGR}:&\quad \Delta R^2=0.0020139270616115154,\quad p=0.142578125,\\
\mathrm{Ile\_AUA}:&\quad \Delta R^2=0.0013067592718559024,\quad p=0.0693359375,\\
\mathrm{K\_AAA}:&\quad \Delta R^2=0.0005307046179682497,\quad p=0.2294921875,\\
\mathrm{Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR}:&\quad \Delta R^2=-0.0003205780368125179,\quad p=0.779296875,\\
\mathrm{Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG}:&\quad \Delta R^2=0.00017615626369389472,\quad p=0.24609375,\\
\mathrm{Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY}:&\quad \Delta R^2=0.0010134090056701938,\quad p=0.2158203125,\\
\mathrm{Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG}:&\quad \Delta R^2=0.00004390882136492317,\quad p=0.4873046875,\\
\mathrm{Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY}:&\quad \Delta R^2=-0.00045340330387383447,\quad p=0.529296875.
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the finite certificate identifies a single structural-order axis rather than a broad codon-family effect. The observed ordering also blocks a simpler interpretation in which any one of the named synonymous contrasts could be substituted for the result: two coordinates have negative held-out increments, several have small positive increments, and only $f_3\text{-}\mathrm{stress}$ crosses the declared sign-flip gate.

\paragraph{Relation to translation and protein-abundance contacts.}
The structural-order axis is read alongside, but not collapsed into, the translation-mediated protein-abundance audit. In the matched protein-abundance experiment \texttt{b\_star\_q6\_mQTP\_powered}, twelve organisms were joined to real coding sequences, modeled per-protein tAI, and PAXdb protein abundance. The joined protein counts were $4042$ for \emph{Bacillus subtilis} 168, $11661$ for \emph{Caenorhabditis elegans}, $14410$ for \emph{Danio rerio}, $13295$ for \emph{Drosophila melanogaster}, $3739$ for \emph{Escherichia coli} K12 MG1655, $12418$ for \emph{Gallus gallus}, $19053$ for \emph{Homo sapiens}, $19395$ for \emph{Mus musculus}, $4129$ for \emph{Mycobacterium smegmatis}, $13322$ for \emph{Rattus norvegicus}, $5815$ for \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}, and $1656$ for \emph{Sulfolobus solfataricus}. In that separate finite matrix, the mediation fractions $M^{QTP}$ stayed inside the unit interval for all twelve organisms, with examples ranging from $0.01850668564589326$ in \emph{Sulfolobus solfataricus} to $0.9491551041216973$ in \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}. Those numbers support a bounded cross-layer statistical readout, but they do not by themselves explain the structural-order axis; they only show that a controlled $Q\to T\to P$ surface exists for protein abundance under the stated covariates.

\paragraph{Yeast structure-adjacent decomposition.}
A second neighboring audit joined yeast protein abundance, modeled or measured translation-related covariates, mRNA half-life, and a coding-region folding proxy. The five-way yeast join contained $4759$ rows, with reported CDS join hit rate $0.9156038419146592$ and overall join hit rate $0.8818572656921754$. In that decomposition, the residualized $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ to protein-abundance association had $R^2_{QP}=0.2715520808117803$; conditioning on the 5-prime coding-region MFE proxy gave $R^2_{QP\mid S}=0.2706601505000995$, while conditioning on measured translation efficiency plus mRNA stability gave $R^2_{QP\mid T,M}=0.13773967696311287$, and adding the structure proxy after those terms gave $R^2_{QP\mid T,M,S}=0.13759011208318542$. The structure-only fraction was $0.003284564452662019$, the incremental structure fraction beyond translation and mRNA stability was $0.0005507778820193332$, and $R^2(S\sim Q)=0.003774248455422586$ with right-tail permutation probability $0.027972027972027972$. These values are compatible with a small structure-adjacent component, but they are not a folding-mechanism claim.

\paragraph{Local verdict and scope.}
The accepted finite statement is therefore the following. On the audited structural-order surface, the only $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ coordinate that survived the fold-level sign-flip criterion was $f_3\text{-}\mathrm{stress}$, with $p=0.03125$ and weighted held-out $\Delta R^2=0.0031744502294756994$. The appropriate readback is an axis-identity result for a controlled residual surface, not a universal codon law and not a direct molecular mechanism. A related modeled-tAI survival matrix was computed on $3$ organisms and $61$ sense codons, with $81$ entries above threshold $0.01$ and maximum absolute $S_{ij}=3801961.031607896$, but that audit explicitly reports that $3$ organisms are insufficient for powered survival requiring at least $40$ matched species. Consequently, this chapter may retain the structural-order axis identity as a finite positive result, while keeping translation realization, folding, physical admissibility, function, mechanism, phenotype, selection, and universality outside the claim unless separate layer-matched contacts and controlled statistics supply them.
