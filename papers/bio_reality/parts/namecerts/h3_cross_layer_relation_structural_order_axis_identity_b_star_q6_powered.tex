\paragraph{Controlled interpretation.}

$$
\begin{aligned}
\operatorname{Pass}(q_i)
&:= \bigl(\Delta R^2_i > 0\bigr) \wedge \bigl(p_i \leq 0.05\bigr),\\
\{q_i \in Q : \operatorname{Pass}(q_i)\}

This makes the chapter an identity result for the structural-order axis, not a broad biological-law result.
