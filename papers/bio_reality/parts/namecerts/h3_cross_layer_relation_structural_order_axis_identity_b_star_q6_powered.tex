\subsection{B-star-Q6 structural-order axis identity}
\label{subsec:bioreality-bstar-q6-structural-order-axis-identity}

\paragraph{Finite object.}
The object tested here is a finite cross-layer surface whose residual coordinates are the nine $B^*_{Q6}$ axes
$K\_AAA$, $Arg\_AGR$, $Ile\_AUA$, $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$, $Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG$, $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$, $Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG$, $Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY$, and $f3\_stress$.  The readout is not a direct biological promotion of these coordinates.  It is a finite held-out axis-identification test asking whether the structural-order signal carried by the full residual packet has a distinguishable coordinate direction after the declared baseline controls have already kept amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, coding-sequence length, and the $M$-density baseline outside the promoted coordinate list.  The experiment was registered under run $fac1206f6516$ with experiment id $b\_star\_q6\_structural\_order\_axis\_identity\_powered$, used $5$ folds, and imposed a minimum joined-row condition of $300$.

\paragraph{Statistic.}
For each coordinate $q_i$, the statistic recorded a weighted held-out increment and an exact fold-level sign-flip right-tail probability for being greater than zero.  The finite audit therefore reads the packet as a ranked coordinate identity problem rather than as a free biological mechanism.  In the recorded table, the increments were $0.0005307046179682497$ for $K\_AAA$ with $p=0.2294921875$, $0.0020139270616115154$ for $Arg\_AGR$ with $p=0.142578125$, $0.0013067592718559024$ for $Ile\_AUA$ with $p=0.0693359375$, $-0.0003205780368125179$ for $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$ with $p=0.779296875$, $0.00017615626369389472$ for $Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG$ with $p=0.24609375$, $0.0010134090056701938$ for $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$ with $p=0.2158203125$, $0.00004390882136492317$ for $Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG$ with $p=0.4873046875$, $-0.00045340330387383447$ for $Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY$ with $p=0.529296875$, and $0.0031744502294756994$ for $f3\_stress$ with $p=0.03125$.  Thus the only coordinate passing the fold-level sign-flip gate was $f3\_stress$.

\paragraph{Axis readback.}
The resulting finite conclusion is that the structural-order signal observed by this audit is carried by the $f3\_stress$ coordinate.  The same coordinate was both the dominant increment axis and the dominant loading axis.  Its weighted absolute unit loading was $0.5109586606724208$, and its weighted held-out $R^2$ increment was $0.0031744502294756994$.  The full $B^*_{Q6}$ signal was marked significant, but the reason was deliberately narrow: exactly one coordinate passed the fold-level sign-flip gate.  In display form, the accepted identity is only
$$
\begin{aligned}
q_{\mathrm{dominant}} &= f3\_stress,\\
\Delta R^2_{\mathrm{held\text{-}out}}(f3\_stress) &= 0.0031744502294756994,\\
p_{\mathrm{signflip}}(f3\_stress > 0) &= 0.03125.
\end{aligned}
$$
No other axis is promoted to the structural-order identity slot by this finite table.

\paragraph{Relation to translation and protein-abundance contacts.}
The coordinate identity is interpreted only inside a controlled mediation discipline.  A separate modeled-tAI contact had already computed the same family of nine synonymous residual coordinates and found nonzero bounded coupling above threshold in $81$ entries, with maximum absolute $S_{ij}$ equal to $3801961.031607896$ at threshold $0.01$, but that contact used only $3$ organisms and explicitly remained an underpowered modeled-tAI readout rather than ribosome profiling or proteomics validation.  A larger protein-abundance mediation run joined $12$ organisms and found per-organism $M^{QTP}$ fractions in the unit interval, including $0.9491551041216973$ for \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}, $0.612922252793667$ for \emph{Escherichia coli} K12 MG1655, $0.6084594553474505$ for \emph{Bacillus subtilis} 168, $0.4872268357227724$ for \emph{Caenorhabditis elegans}, $0.3157053953614641$ for \emph{Danio rerio}, and $0.27206289160119157$ for human.  Those numbers support a bounded statistical decomposition of residual, translation-readout, and protein-abundance rows; they do not turn the present structural-order axis into a causal translation mechanism.

\paragraph{Yeast structure-proxy context.}
A yeast five-way join supplied $4759$ joined rows for a related structure-mediated abundance-residual audit.  In that audit, the direct residual-to-protein association after baseline residualization was $R^2_{QP}=0.2715520808117803$, the value after conditioning on the 5-prime coding-region MFE proxy was $R^2_{QP\mid S}=0.2706601505000995$, the value after conditioning on measured translation efficiency and mRNA stability was $R^2_{QP\mid T,M}=0.13773967696311287$, and the value after all three was $R^2_{QP\mid T,M,S}=0.13759011208318542$.  The structure-only fraction was $0.003284564452662019$, the incremental structure fraction beyond translation and mRNA stability was $0.0005507778820193332$, and $S_Q$ structure $R^2$ was $0.003774248455422586$ with permutation right-tail probability $0.027972027972027972$.  This context is compatible with the $f3\_stress$ axis being the finite coordinate selected by the structural-order identity audit, but it is still a proxy-level variance partition, not a proof of folded structure or physical feasibility.

\paragraph{Local boundary.}
The admissible statement is therefore: among the nine computed $B^*_{Q6}$ residual axes, the finite held-out sign-flip audit identifies $f3\_stress$ as the single coordinate carrying the recorded structural-order axis signal.  The statement stops there.  It does not assert translation realization from codon geometry, does not infer ribosome dynamics from modeled tAI or stAI weights, does not infer mature protein abundance without the mediated $Q_i \to T_j \to P_k$ route, and does not infer folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, organismal phenotype, mechanism, selection, or universality.  Any such claim would require its own layer-matched contact and its own finite statistic after the same controls, with direct residual-to-protein association rejected whenever the translation-readout step is null or untested.
