\paragraph{Finite question.}
The record tests whether the nine-coordinate $B^*_{Q6}$ synonymous-residual panel carries a reproducible structural-order signal through one identifiable coordinate, rather than through an undifferentiated residual block. The finite surface consists of residual coordinates $q_i$, translation readouts $T_j$, and matched downstream protein-abundance or structure-proxy readouts $P_k$, with amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, CDS length, and the $M$-density baseline kept as controls. The coordinate list used in the powered structural-order run is
$$
\begin{aligned}
Q = \{&\mathrm{K\_AAA},\mathrm{Arg\_AGR},\mathrm{Ile\_AUA},\mathrm{Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR},\mathrm{Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG},\\
&\mathrm{Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY},\mathrm{Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG},\mathrm{Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY},f_3\text{-}\mathrm{stress}\}.

$$
The experiment used five folds, a minimum joined-row requirement of $300$, and deterministic seed \texttt{sha256:8da7c86c999e5bee370c0c1a60a84722d81290b54b4dc2d4bd43c1cbd29fb394}. The reported status is \texttt{passed}, and the finite conclusion is that structural order is carried by the $f_3$-stress axis.

\paragraph{Modeled translation boundary.}
The modeled-tAI survival contact was computed only for three organisms in its bounded coupling run: \emph{Escherichia coli} K12, human, and yeast. The codon-count space has $61$ sense codons, the coupling threshold is $0.01$, and there are $81$ entries above threshold. The maximum absolute $S_{ij}$ and maximum absolute diagonal $S_i$ are both $3801961.031607896$. The local GtRNAdb payload contains all $20$ standard amino-acid families in each of the three organisms, with FASTA header counts $89$, $432$, and $275$, respectively. This confirms a finite modeled-readout contact, but the organism count is $3$, so the record explicitly refuses powered survival from that small panel and requires at least $40$ matched species before a powered survival claim is made from modeled tAI alone.

\paragraph{Boundary of interpretation.}
The structural-order axis identity is a finite statistical ordering statement over the tested residual coordinates. It does not by itself establish translation realization, ribosome occupancy, folded structure, physical admissibility, molecular function, organismal phenotype, mechanism, selection, or universality. A translation statement requires a separate translation readout, such as measured TE or an explicitly bounded modeled-tAI contact, and a protein-abundance statement requires matched protein rows. Folded structure, localization, post-translational state, function, physical feasibility, perturbation response, and population-level law each require their own layer-matched contact and statistic. In this chapter the admissible positive readback is narrower: under the powered structural-order audit, $f_3$-stress is the only tested $B^*_{Q6}$ coordinate that passes the fold-level sign-flip gate, with $p=0.03125$ and held-out increment $0.0031744502294756994$.
