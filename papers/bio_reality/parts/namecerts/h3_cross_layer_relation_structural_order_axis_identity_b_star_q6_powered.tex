\subsection{Structural-order axis identity for the powered $B^*_{Q6}$ residual surface}\label{sec:bioreality-structural-order-axis-identity-b-star-q6-powered}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite surface.}
This record concerns a finite cross-layer surface whose rows are residual coordinates in the $B^*_{Q6}$ basis and whose tested response is a structural-order readout. The computed coordinate list is
$K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $Leu_{CUN\,vs\,UUR}$, $Leu_{UUA\,vs\,UUG}$, $Ser_{UCR\,vs\,AGY}$, $Ser_{UCA\,vs\,UCG}$, $Thr_{ACR\,vs\,ACY}$, and $f_3$-stress. The experiment used five folds, required at least $300$ joined rows, and was run under the deterministic seed $sha256:8da7c86c999e5bee370c0c1a60a84722d81290b54b4dc2d4bd43c1cbd29fb394$. The finite question is not whether the whole codon geometry becomes a biological law. It is whether one of the nine named residual coordinates carries the held-out structural-order increment after the declared controls and fold-level sign-flip test are applied.

\paragraph{Axis-level result.}
The powered run returned status $passed$ and classified the structural-order signal as carried by $f_3$-stress. The full $B^*_{Q6}$ signal was significant, but exactly one coordinate passed the fold-level sign-flip gate. The dominant increment axis and the dominant loading axis were both $f_3$-stress. Its weighted held-out $R^2$ increment was $0.0031744502294756994$, its weighted absolute unit loading was $0.5109586606724208$, its rank by weighted increment was $1$, and its right-tail sign-flip value was $0.03125$. Thus the positive statement licensed by the finite row is narrow: within this tested surface, the structural-order readout identifies $f_3$-stress as the single surviving coordinate among the nine $B^*_{Q6}$ residual axes.

\paragraph{Competing coordinates.}
The non-dominant coordinates did not pass the same gate. Their held-out increments and sign-flip values were: $Arg_{AGR}$, $0.0020139270616115154$ with $p=0.142578125$; $Ile_{AUA}$, $0.0013067592718559024$ with $p=0.0693359375$; $K_{AAA}$, $0.0005307046179682497$ with $p=0.2294921875$; $Leu_{CUN\,vs\,UUR}$, $-0.0003205780368125179$ with $p=0.779296875$; $Leu_{UUA\,vs\,UUG}$, $0.00017615626369389472$ with $p=0.24609375$; $Ser_{UCA\,vs\,UCG}$, $0.00004390882136492317$ with $p=0.4873046875$; $Ser_{UCR\,vs\,AGY}$, $0.0010134090056701938$ with $p=0.2158203125$; and $Thr_{ACR\,vs\,ACY}$, $-0.00045340330387383447$ with $p=0.529296875$. In compact form, the accepted coordinate is determined by
$$
\begin{aligned}
q^* &= f_3\text{-stress},\\
\Delta R^2_{held\text{-}out}(q^*) &= 0.0031744502294756994,\\
p_{signflip}(q^*) &= 0.03125,\\
\#\{q_i: p_{signflip}(q_i) \leq 0.05\} &= 1.
\end{aligned}
$$
This is an axis-identity statement, not a promotion of every codon-family contrast in the basis.

\paragraph{Relation to translation and protein-abundance rows.}
The broader $B^*_{Q6}$ memory contains bounded translation and abundance contacts, but they remain separate finite panels. A modeled tAI run over $3$ organisms computed all nine residuals and found $81$ entries above the $0.01$ bounded-coupling threshold, with maximum absolute $S_{ij}=3801961.031607896$; the same record states that $3$ organisms are insufficient for powered survival and that a powered survival request needs at least $40$ matched species. A distinct protein-abundance mediation panel joined $12$ organisms and used per-protein modeled tAI with matched protein abundance. In that panel the organism-level joined counts included $3739$ proteins for $Escherichia$ $coli$ K12 MG1655, $5815$ for $Saccharomyces$ $cerevisiae$, $19053$ for $Homo$ $sapiens$, and $19395$ for $Mus$ $musculus$. Its mediation fractions ranged from $0.01850668564589326$ in $Sulfolobus$ $solfataricus$ to $0.9491551041216973$ in yeast, with the yeast total residual-to-protein $R^2$ equal to $0.29837624994140827$ and the direct value after conditioning on modeled tAI equal to $0.0151709093608293$. These rows show that translation and protein-abundance surfaces can be finite and measurable, but they do not by themselves identify structural order for the present chapter; the structural-order axis call above is the local result.

\paragraph{Checks against broader biological promotion.}
Several linked finite runs sharpen the boundary. The yeast structure-mediation audit used $4759$ joined rows and decomposed a $B^*_{Q6}$ to protein-abundance association with $R^2_{QP}=0.2715520808117803$, $R^2_{QP\mid S}=0.2706601505000995$, $R^2_{QP\mid T,M}=0.13773967696311287$, and $R^2_{QP\mid T,M,S}=0.13759011208318542$; its structure-only fraction was only $0.003284564452662019$, and its structure-incremental permutation value was $0.1258741258741259$. The cross-ortholog selection-imprint run used $80$ orthogroups and $240$ rows, found $\Delta R^2=0.03250515015613468$, but failed the positive selection-imprint gate because the leave-clade-out increment was $-0.0021477729853302424$ and the null $95\%$ maximum was $0.05427157066522958$. The dynamic mass-balance run used $120$ genes and $718$ intervals and returned $\Delta R^2=-0.07584438093220836$ with $p_{timeshuffle}=0.9306930693069307$. The turnover audit used $3290$ genes and returned $\Delta R^2=-0.005461979433706483$, $p_{recoded}=1.0$, and $p_{matchedbin}=0.8855721393034826$. These numbers prevent the structural-order axis identity from being read as selection, dynamic mass balance, turnover prediction, or a general protein-state law.

\paragraph{Local claim boundary.}
The chapter therefore licenses one finite statement: among the tested $B^*_{Q6}$ residual axes, $f_3$-stress is the unique fold-level sign-flip survivor for the structural-order readout in the powered run. No translation realization, ribosome mechanism, folding pathway, physical admissibility, molecular function, organismal phenotype, evolutionary law, or universal mechanism follows from that axis identity alone. A claim at any of those layers would require its own matched contact surface and its own controlled statistic. In particular, a direct $Q_i\to P_k$ association is not evidence for protein realization unless the same coordinate first survives a controlled $Q_i\to T_j$ translation-readout step; transcript abundance is a control for separating transcription from translation, not a translation readout; and a modeled tAI or stAI weight is a bounded adaptation readback rather than an observed ribosome-occupancy measurement.
