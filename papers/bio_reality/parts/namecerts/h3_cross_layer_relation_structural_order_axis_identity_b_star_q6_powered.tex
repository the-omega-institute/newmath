\paragraph{Finite surface.}

The tested object is not raw codon usage and not a direct protein-structure assertion. The finite run labelled \texttt{b\_star\_q6\_structural\_order\_axis\_identity\_powered} used five folds, required at least $300$ joined rows per admissible fold block, and used deterministic seed \texttt{sha256:8da7c86c999e5bee370c0c1a60a84722d81290b54b4dc2d4bd43c1cbd29fb394}. \paragraph{Axis call.}
The dominant-loading and dominant-gain coordinates agree: both are $f3\_stress$.  Its weighted absolute unit loading is $0.5109586606724208$, its held-out weighted $R^{2}$ gain is $0.0031744502294756994$, and its exact sign-flip right-tail probability is $0.03125$.  The fold-level sign-flip gate reports this coordinate as significant, with rank $1$ by weighted gain.  No second coordinate passes the same gate, so the finite call is an axis-identity statement rather than a broad claim that every $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual participates in structural ordering.

\paragraph{Modeled translation contact.}
The translation-contact audit used modeled tAI weights computed from GtRNAdb tRNA gene-copy counts and dos Reis wobble values.  In the three-organism bounded-coupling run, the payload contained all $20$ standard amino-acid families for \emph{E. coli} K12, human, and yeast.  The modeled tAI surface had $81$ entries above threshold $0.01$, with maximum absolute entry $3801961.031607896$.  This is a codon-adaptation readout, not observed ribosome occupancy.  The three-organism count is explicitly insufficient for powered survival, so that contact can constrain the translation interface but cannot by itself establish measured translation realization or protein realization.

\paragraph{Boundary of the claim.}
The chapter-level assertion is therefore the following finite-row statement.  On the powered structural-order audit, $f3\_stress$ is the unique significant axis among the nine $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates, with $\Delta R^{2}=0.0031744502294756994$ and sign-flip probability $0.03125$.  This axis call may be read together with the translation-mediated protein-abundance decomposition and the yeast structure-proxy partition, but it remains a controlled statistical identity of a residual coordinate.  It does not establish translation from codon geometry alone, does not establish folding, does not establish physical admissibility, does not establish molecular function, does not establish a mechanism, and does not establish universality.  Each of those stronger interpretations would require its own layer-matched measurement surface and its own controlled finite statistic.  Direct $Q_i\to P_k$ association is also insufficient for protein-realization language unless the same coordinate has first survived a controlled $Q_i\to T_j$ translation-readout step, with matched mRNA abundance retained as a control rather than treated as a translation readout.
