\subsection{Finite axis identity for the B$^{*}_{Q6}$ structural-order readout}
\label{subsec:bioreality-bstar-q6-structural-order-axis-identity}

\paragraph{Question.}
This record asks whether the nine computed B$^{*}_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates behave as an undifferentiated codon-usage block, or whether a single coordinate carries the observable structural-order increment after ordinary coding-sequence covariates have been held fixed.  The finite surface is the row set on which each protein-coding entry has a B$^{*}_{Q6}$ coordinate vector, the baseline controls for amino-acid composition, GC3, coding-sequence length, organism, and the M-density baseline, and a structural-order readout used only as a statistical response.  The tested coordinates were
$$
\begin{aligned}
Q=\{&K\_AAA,\ Arg\_AGR,\ Ile\_AUA,\ Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR,\ Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG,\\
&Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY,\ Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG,\ Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY,\ f3\_stress\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The experiment used five folds, required at least $300$ joined rows in the finite joins, and was run under the deterministic seed \texttt{sha256:8da7c86c999e5bee370c0c1a60a84722d81290b54b4dc2d4bd43c1cbd29fb394}.  Its reported status was \texttt{passed}, meaning that the registered computation completed and returned a bounded finite verdict, not that a biological mechanism was established.

\paragraph{Axis scan.}
The fold-level sign-flip scan separated the coordinates sharply.  The only coordinate passing the sign-flip gate was $f3\_stress$, with weighted held-out $\Delta R^2=0.0031744502294756994$ and one-sided sign-flip probability $p=0.03125$.  It was also the largest loading axis, with weighted absolute unit loading $0.5109586606724208$, and it was ranked first by weighted increment.  The remaining coordinates had weaker or negative held-out increments: $Arg\_AGR$ had $\Delta R^2=0.0020139270616115154$ with $p=0.142578125$; $Ile\_AUA$ had $\Delta R^2=0.0013067592718559024$ with $p=0.0693359375$; $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$ had $\Delta R^2=0.0010134090056701938$ with $p=0.2158203125$; $K\_AAA$ had $\Delta R^2=0.0005307046179682497$ with $p=0.2294921875$; $Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG$ had $\Delta R^2=0.00017615626369389472$ with $p=0.24609375$; $Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG$ had $\Delta R^2=0.00004390882136492317$ with $p=0.4873046875$; $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$ had $\Delta R^2=-0.0003205780368125179$ with $p=0.779296875$; and $Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY$ had $\Delta R^2=-0.00045340330387383447$ with $p=0.529296875$.  Thus the finite conclusion is not that every synonymous residual coordinate contributes to structural order.  The recorded identity is narrower: among these nine axes, the observed structural-order increment is carried by $f3\_stress$.

\paragraph{Finite verdict.}
The dominant-axis test reports two matching facts.  First, the dominant increment axis is $f3\_stress$.  Second, the dominant loading axis is also $f3\_stress$.  In local notation, the accepted finite statement is
$$
\begin{aligned}
\operatorname{argmax}_{q_i\in Q}\ \Delta R^2_{\mathrm{heldout}}(q_i)&=f3\_stress,\\
\operatorname{argmax}_{q_i\in Q}\ |\ell_i|_{\mathrm{weighted}}&=f3\_stress,\\
p_{\mathrm{signflip}}(f3\_stress)&=0.03125.
\end{aligned}
$$
The phrase ``structural order carried by $f3\_stress$'' is therefore a finite-row identity claim about the tested matrix and its held-out score.  It does not say that the B$^{*}_{Q6}$ system as a whole is a universal structural law.  It also does not promote $f3\_stress$ into a folding pathway, a physical admissibility certificate, a molecular-function annotation, or an organismal phenotype.

\paragraph{Yeast decomposition context.}
A yeast five-way join gives a useful scale check.  The joined surface contained $4759$ genes, with a CDS join hit rate $0.9156038419146592$ and an overall join hit rate $0.8818572656921754$.  After residualization against $23$ effective control rank columns, the B$^{*}_{Q6}$ block had $R^2_{QP}=0.2715520808117803$ against protein abundance, $R^2_{QP\mid S}=0.2706601505000995$ after conditioning on the 5-prime CDS MFE proxy, $R^2_{QP\mid T,M}=0.13773967696311287$ after measured translation efficiency and mRNA stability, and $R^2_{QP\mid T,M,S}=0.13759011208318542$ after all three.  The structure-only fraction was $0.003284564452662019$, the incremental structure fraction beyond translation and mRNA stability was $0.0005507778820193332$, and the permutation probabilities were $p=0.027972027972027972$ for the $S\sim Q$ structure readout and $p=0.1258741258741259$ for the incremental structure fraction.  These values keep the structural-order axis result in proportion: the visible structural-order signal is small, concentrated, and row-dependent, not a broad claim that synonymous residual geometry determines folded state.

The turnover residual audit used $3290$ genes and reported $R^2_{L2}=0.055740851626933985$, $R^2_{L3}=0.050278872193227464$, $\Delta R^2=-0.005461979433706483$, $\Delta DL=-13.68819707902732$ bits, $p_{matchedbin}=0.8855721393034826$, and $p_{recoded}=1.0$.  These negative or bounded outcomes prevent the structural-order axis identity from being read as a general protein-output, turnover, or mass-balance principle.

\paragraph{Local boundary.}
The admissible conclusion is limited to a finite controlled identity: in the registered structural-order computation, exactly one B$^{*}_{Q6}$ coordinate passed the fold-level sign-flip gate, and that coordinate was $f3\_stress$.  No translation-realization statement follows from this chapter unless a separate translation contact supplies measured or explicitly bounded translation rows.  No folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, mechanism, phenotype, selection law, or universal biological rule follows from this chapter unless a distinct layer-matched contact supplies that measurement and the corresponding controlled statistic.  The Phase-one table-contact checks, including the module-classification target \texttt{h1.leave\_one\_codon\_out.module\_classification}, reported $40$ successes out of $40$ under exact one-sided binomial tests with $p=9.094947017729282\times 10^{-13}$ and $0$ unexpected successes in $20$ negative-control and $20$ critical-ablation trials; those checks establish a separate finite codon-table contact discipline, not a license to treat the present structural-order axis as a mechanism.  The chapter therefore closes at the axis-identity level: $f3\_stress$ is the observed carrier of the structural-order increment on this finite surface, and every higher biological reading remains outside the claim unless independently measured.
