\subsection{Structural-order axis identity for the powered $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual panel}
\label{sec:bioreality-structural-order-axis-identity-bstar-q6}

\paragraph{Finite row surface.}
The BioReality record in this section concerns a finite mediation surface whose rows carry synonymous residual coordinates, translation-layer readouts, and matched downstream abundance measurements. The coordinate side is the nine-dimensional $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual panel
$K\_AAA$, $Arg\_AGR$, $Ile\_AUA$, $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$, $Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG$, $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$, $Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG$, $Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY$, and $f3\_stress$.
The row discipline is deliberately narrow: a residual-to-protein association is not counted as a protein-level realization unless the same coordinate first survives a translation readout, and any direct protein-abundance relation is separated from transcription-only signal by matched mRNA control. Amino-acid composition, organism identity, GC3, coding-sequence length, and the $M$-density baseline remain control columns rather than promoted biological coordinates. The target readback is therefore a controlled matrix of the form $Q_i \to T_j \to P_k$, where $T_j$ may be a modeled tAI value, stAI value, ribosome occupancy, or translation efficiency, and $P_k$ is matched protein abundance.

\paragraph{Axis identity result.}
The powered structural-order audit computed all nine residual axes and found a single fold-level surviving coordinate. Across $5$ folds with a minimum joined-row requirement of $300$, the full $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ signal was significant, but the dominant increment axis and dominant loading axis were both $f3\_stress$. Its weighted absolute unit loading was $0.5109586606724208$, its weighted held-out $R^2$ increment was $0.0031744502294756994$, and its sign-flip right-tail probability was $0.03125$. The other coordinate-level increments did not pass the same gate: $Arg\_AGR$ had held-out increment $0.0020139270616115154$ with $p=0.142578125$; $Ile\_AUA$ had $0.0013067592718559024$ with $p=0.0693359375$; $K\_AAA$ had $0.0005307046179682497$ with $p=0.2294921875$; $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$ had $0.0010134090056701938$ with $p=0.2158203125$; $Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG$ had $0.00017615626369389472$ with $p=0.24609375$; $Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG$ had $4.390882136492317\times 10^{-5}$ with $p=0.4873046875$; $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$ had $-0.0003205780368125179$ with $p=0.779296875$; and $Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY$ had $-0.00045340330387383447$ with $p=0.529296875$.
Thus the finite row verdict is not that the whole synonymous residual panel becomes a broad structural law. It is the sharper statement that the tested structural-order increment, under this row design and null class, is carried by $f3\_stress$ alone.

$$
\begin{aligned}
\operatorname{Axis}_{\mathrm{survive}} &= \{f3\_stress\},\\
\Delta R^2_{\mathrm{heldout}}(f3\_stress) &= 0.0031744502294756994,\\
p_{\mathrm{signflip}}(f3\_stress) &= 0.03125.
\end{aligned}
$$

\paragraph{Relation to translation-mediated protein abundance.}
The protein-abundance mediation audit gives a separate finite-row check on how the same nine-coordinate panel behaves when matched protein abundance and modeled translation readout are present. In that audit, $12$ organisms were joined, with $3739$ proteins for $Escherichia\ coli$ K12 MG1655, $5815$ for $Saccharomyces\ cerevisiae$, $19053$ for $Homo\ sapiens$, $19395$ for $Mus\ musculus$, $13295$ for $Drosophila\ melanogaster$, $14410$ for $Danio\ rerio$, $11661$ for $Caenorhabditis\ elegans$, $12418$ for $Gallus\ gallus$, $13322$ for $Rattus\ norvegicus$, $4042$ for $Bacillus\ subtilis$ 168, $4129$ for $Mycobacterium\ smegmatis$ mc2 155, and $1656$ for $Sulfolobus\ solfataricus$. The mediation fractions $M^{QTP}$ stayed in the unit interval in every listed organism. They ranged from $0.01850668564589326$ in $Sulfolobus\ solfataricus$ and $0.08397602919861291$ in $Mycobacterium\ smegmatis$ to $0.9491551041216973$ in yeast. The yeast row had total residual-to-protein $R^2=0.29837624994140827$, direct $R^2$ after conditioning on modeled translation $0.0151709093608293$, and indirect difference $0.28320534058057895$. The $E.\ coli$ row had total $R^2=0.10767526677985896$, direct conditioned $R^2=0.04167869969498872$, and indirect difference $0.06599656708487024$.
These numbers justify using the translation-mediated abundance surface as a bounded cross-layer companion to the structural-order axis audit. They do not, by themselves, convert $f3\_stress$ into a folding coordinate, a molecular-function coordinate, or a causal translation mechanism.

\paragraph{Yeast structure-mediated abundance cross-check.}
A yeast five-way join supplies an additional bounded check on whether residual abundance signal remains separable under structure and translation covariates. The join retained $4759$ rows, with a reported CDS join hit rate of $0.9156038419146592$ and a broader join hit rate of $0.8818572656921754$. After residualizing controls, the nine-coordinate $Q$ panel gave $R^2_{QP}=0.2715520808117803$. Conditioning on the 5-prime coding-region MFE proxy gave $R^2_{QP\mid S}=0.2706601505000995$, while conditioning on measured translation efficiency plus mRNA stability gave $R^2_{QP\mid T,M}=0.13773967696311287$, and conditioning on all three gave $R^2_{QP\mid T,M,S}=0.13759011208318542$. The structure-only fraction was $0.003284564452662019$, the structure-incremental fraction beyond translation and mRNA stability was $0.0005507778820193332$, and the $Q$-to-structure $R^2$ was $0.003774248455422586$ with right-tail permutation probability $0.027972027972027972$ for the $Q$-structure component.
This supports the conservative interpretation: the structural-order axis identity is a finite statistical identity in the declared row surface, not a claim that the synonymous residual directly measures folded protein structure.

\paragraph{Auxiliary reality checks.}
Several companion audits bound the claim from above. The modeled-tAI contact used only $3$ organisms, computed $61$ sense-codon weights in each organism, found $81$ entries above the $0.01$ bounded-coupling threshold, and explicitly did not provide powered survival because $3$ organisms are below the $40$-species requirement. The observable-execution audit in yeast used $3289$ genes and concluded that at least one observed rate-proxy slot passed both effective null classes, but this is an execution-subblock result rather than a structural mechanism. The evolutionary-selection audit used $80$ orthogroups and $240$ rows; its positive selection-imprint gate was negative, with $\Delta R^2=0.03250515015613468$, leave-clade-out $\Delta R^2=-0.0021477729853302424$, and null $95\%$ maximum $0.05427157066522958$. The mass-balance audit used $120$ genes and $718$ intervals and was negative for the stronger closure, with $\Delta R^2=-0.07584438093220836$ and $\Delta DL=-668.8046070381928$ bits. The turnover audit used $3290$ genes and likewise stayed negative beyond its controls, with $\Delta R^2=-0.005461979433706483$, $p_{matchedbin}=0.8855721393034826$, and $p_{recoded}=1.0$.

\paragraph{Local cannot-claim boundary.}
The accepted readback is only this: within the finite powered structural-order audit, $f3\_stress$ is the unique surviving $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ axis, and companion mediation tables show that the nine-coordinate panel can be evaluated on controlled translation and protein-abundance surfaces. No translation-realization claim follows from this section unless a translation contact supplies a same-row readout and survives the declared controls. No folded-structure, physical-admissibility, molecular-function, organismal-phenotype, mechanism, selection, or universality claim follows from the axis identity. Those higher layers require their own layer-matched measurements and null tests. A direct $Q_i \to P_k$ association remains insufficient when the $Q_i \to T_j$ step is absent, null, or untested.
