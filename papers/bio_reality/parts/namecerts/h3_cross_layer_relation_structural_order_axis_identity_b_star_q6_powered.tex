\paragraph{Interpretation of the positive axis call.}
The phrase structural-order axis is used here in the narrow statistical sense supplied by the run: among the nine tested $B^*_{Q6}$ coordinates, one coordinate supplied the dominant held-out increment for the structural-order readout.  The evidence does not identify a folding pathway, a physical admissibility law, or a molecular function.  It also does not show that $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$, $Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG$, $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$, $Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG$, $Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY$, $K\_AAA$, $Arg\_AGR$, or $Ile\_AUA$ individually carry the same status.  In fact, two coordinates had negative weighted held-out increments, $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$ at $-0.0003205780368125179$ and $Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY$ at $-0.00045340330387383447$, and every non-$f3\_stress$ coordinate had sign-flip probability at least $0.0693359375$.
