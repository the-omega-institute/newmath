\paragraph{Layer boundary.}
The modeled tAI contact was computed on only three organisms in the earlier translation-readout matrix, with organism count $3$ and $81$ entries above threshold $0.01$; the maximum absolute coupling was $3801961.031607896$.  That contact is therefore a bounded modeled-readout calculation, not measured ribosome occupancy.  The confirmatory genetic-code table audit likewise had $40$ successes in $40$ independent replication trials with one-sided binomial upper-tail probability $9.094947017729282\times 10^{-13}$, $40$ successes in $40$ boundary-probe trials with the same probability, and $0$ unexpected successes in $20$ negative controls and $20$ ablation controls.  Those numbers support finite codon-table contact, not a higher-layer biological conclusion.

\paragraph{Verdict.}
The finite result is ready at the structural-order axis-identity level: among the nine powered $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ coordinates, only $f_3\text{-}\mathrm{stress}$ passes the sign-flip gate, has rank $1$ by weighted increment, and carries the dominant loading.  The chapter may therefore state that the powered structural-order axis in this finite surface is identified with $f_3\text{-}\mathrm{stress}$.  It must not state that this proves translation, protein folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, causal mechanism, organismal phenotype, selection, or a universal biological law.  Any such reading would require a separate layer-matched measurement contact and its own controlled statistic; direct $Q_i\to P_k$ association is not enough when the translation or other intervening layer is null, unmeasured, or only modeled.
