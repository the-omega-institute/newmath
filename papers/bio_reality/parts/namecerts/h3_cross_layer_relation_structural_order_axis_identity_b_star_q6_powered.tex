\subsection{Structural-order axis identity for $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates}
\label{sec:bioreality-structural-order-axis-identity-b-star-q6}

\paragraph{Finite surface.}
The BioReality record considered here is the finite surface whose rows are controlled residual coordinates $q_i$ from the nine-coordinate $B^{*}_{Q6}$ panel, together with translation-side and protein-side readouts kept as separate measured or modelled coordinates. The computed residual axes are $K\_AAA$, $Arg\_AGR$, $Ile\_AUA$, $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$, $Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG$, $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$, $Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG$, $Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY$, and $f3\_stress$. The finite claim is not that all synonymous residual axes carry higher-order biological information. It is the sharper assertion that, after the declared controls and held-out folds are applied, the structural-order increment has a single dominant coordinate, namely $f3\_stress$.

\paragraph{Axis test.}
The powered axis-identity run used $5$ folds with a minimum joined-row condition of $300$ and evaluated fold-level sign-flip evidence for each of the nine residual coordinates. The full $B^{*}_{Q6}$ signal was significant, but exactly one coordinate passed the coordinate-level sign-flip gate. The dominant increment axis and the dominant loading axis were both $f3\_stress$. Its weighted held-out $R^2$ increment was $0.0031744502294756994$, its weighted absolute unit loading was $0.5109586606724208$, and its sign-flip right-tail value was $p=0.03125$. The remaining coordinates did not pass the same gate: $Arg\_AGR$ had increment $0.0020139270616115154$ with $p=0.142578125$, $Ile\_AUA$ had increment $0.0013067592718559024$ with $p=0.0693359375$, $K\_AAA$ had increment $0.0005307046179682497$ with $p=0.2294921875$, $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$ had increment $0.0010134090056701938$ with $p=0.2158203125$, $Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG$ had increment $0.00017615626369389472$ with $p=0.24609375$, and $Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG$ had increment $0.00004390882136492317$ with $p=0.4873046875$. Two coordinates were negative in held-out increment: $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$ at $-0.0003205780368125179$ with $p=0.779296875$, and $Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY$ at $-0.00045340330387383447$ with $p=0.529296875$.

\paragraph{Finite interpretation.}
The supported readback is therefore an axis identity statement, not a universal codon law. The finite matrix separates one coordinate from the rest: $f3\_stress$ is the only tested $B^{*}_{Q6}$ coordinate whose fold-level increment remains positive against the sign-flip null at the stated gate. Equivalently, the observed ordering is
$$
\begin{aligned}
  f3\_stress &> Arg\_AGR > Ile\_AUA > Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY > K\_AAA \\
  &> Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG > Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG > Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR > Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY
\end{aligned}
$$
when ordered by the reported weighted held-out increment. The term structural order is used here only for this finite tested response surface. It does not by itself assert folded protein structure, physical feasibility, molecular function, phenotype, mechanism, or species-wide invariance.

\paragraph{Relation to the translation-mediated abundance surface.}
A separate cross-layer protein-abundance decomposition supplies the main guardrail for interpreting direct $q_i \to P_k$ associations. In that run, the same nine $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates were joined to per-protein modelled tAI and measured protein abundance in $12$ organisms. The organism-specific joined protein counts were $4042$ for \textit{Bacillus subtilis}, $11661$ for \textit{Caenorhabditis elegans}, $14410$ for \textit{Danio rerio}, $13295$ for \textit{Drosophila melanogaster}, $3739$ for \textit{Escherichia coli} K12 MG1655, $12418$ for chicken, $19053$ for human, $19395$ for mouse, $4129$ for \textit{Mycobacterium smegmatis}, $13322$ for rat, $5815$ for yeast, and $1656$ for \textit{Sulfolobus solfataricus}. The mediation scalar $M^{QTP}$ stayed in the unit interval for every organism, ranging from $0.01850668564589326$ in \textit{Sulfolobus solfataricus} to $0.9491551041216973$ in yeast. Human had total $R^2_{QP}=0.04454565118019448$, direct $R^2_{QP\mid T}=0.03242643251185274$, and indirect component $0.012119218668341744$; yeast had total $R^2_{QP}=0.29837624994140827$, direct $R^2_{QP\mid T}=0.0151709093608293$, and indirect component $0.28320534058057895$. These values support a controlled statistical decomposition of residual-to-protein abundance through a translation readout, but they do not convert the structural-order axis result into a causal translation mechanism.

\paragraph{Structure-proxy audit.}
The yeast structure-mediation run gives a second local check on over-reading the axis identity. On $4759$ joined yeast rows, after controls for coding length, amino-acid composition, $GC3$, and the $M$-density baseline, the direct residual-to-protein-abundance value was $R^2_{QP}=0.2715520808117803$. Conditioning on the 5-prime coding-region MFE proxy changed this to $R^2_{QP\mid S}=0.2706601505000995$, while conditioning on measured translation efficiency and mRNA stability gave $R^2_{QP\mid T,M}=0.13773967696311287$, and conditioning on all three gave $R^2_{QP\mid T,M,S}=0.13759011208318542$. The structure-only fraction was $0.003284564452662019$, the structure-incremental fraction was $0.0005507778820193332$, and the permutation value for the structure-incremental right tail was $0.1258741258741259$. This audit is compatible with a small sequence-derived structure proxy, but it is not a folded-structure experiment and does not license a structural mechanism.

\paragraph{Local no-promotion rule.}
The admissible conclusion is confined to finite-row identity: among the tested $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates, $f3\_stress$ is the only coordinate that passes the fold-level sign-flip gate for the structural-order response, with held-out increment $0.0031744502294756994$ and $p=0.03125$. A direct residual-to-protein association is not promoted unless the same coordinate first survives an explicit translation-readout step, and even then the result remains a controlled statistical chain rather than proof of translation dynamics. No translation realization, folding claim, physical admissibility claim, molecular function claim, mechanism claim, organismal phenotype claim, selection claim, or universal biological law follows from this chapter unless a separate layer-matched contact supplies that specific measurement and the finite statistic is recomputed under the stated controls.
