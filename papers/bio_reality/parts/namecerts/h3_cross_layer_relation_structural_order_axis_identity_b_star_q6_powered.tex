\subsection{Structural-order axis identity for the powered $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ panel}
\label{sec:bioreality-structural-order-axis-identity-bstar-q6}

\paragraph{Finite row surface.}
This record concerns a finite cross-layer matrix whose rows carry nine synonymous-residual coordinates, a structural-order response slot, and fold-held-out scoring metadata.  The tested coordinate list was $\{\texttt{K\_AAA},\texttt{Arg\_AGR},\texttt{Ile\_AUA},\texttt{Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR},\texttt{Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG},\texttt{Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY},\texttt{Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG},\texttt{Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY}, f_3\text{-stress}\}$.  The run used five folds with a minimum joined support of $300$ rows per accepted fold surface, and the registered status was \texttt{passed}.  The readout is not a direct claim about translation, folding physics, molecular function, organismal phenotype, or a universal biological rule.  It is a finite axis-identification test: among the residual coordinates that remain after amino-acid and simpler composition controls, which coordinate carries the observed structural-order increment on the evaluated held-out surface?

\paragraph{Dominant-axis computation.}
The powered structural-order run identified exactly one coordinate passing the fold-level sign-flip gate.  The selected coordinate was $f_3\text{-stress}$, and it was simultaneously the dominant loading axis and the dominant incremental axis.  Its weighted absolute unit loading was $0.5109586606724208$, its weighted held-out increment was $0.0031744502294756994$, and its sign-flip upper-tail value was $0.03125$.  In compact form, the decisive row is
$$
\begin{aligned}
q_{\mathrm{dom}} &= f_3\text{-stress},\\
\Delta R^2_{\mathrm{heldout}}(q_{\mathrm{dom}}) &= 0.0031744502294756994,\\
p_{\mathrm{signflip}}(q_{\mathrm{dom}}) &= 0.03125,\\
|u_{\mathrm{dom}}|_{\mathrm{weighted}} &= 0.5109586606724208.
\end{aligned}
$$
The full $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ signal was significant on this criterion, but the coordinate-level conclusion is deliberately narrower: the structural-order signal in this finite panel is carried by the $f_3$-stress axis rather than by the leucine, serine, threonine, isoleucine, lysine, or arginine residual contrasts.

\paragraph{Axis comparison.}
The remaining eight coordinate increments did not pass the same gate.  Their held-out increments and sign-flip values were as follows: $\texttt{Arg\_AGR}$ had $\Delta R^2=0.0020139270616115154$ with $p=0.142578125$; $\texttt{Ile\_AUA}$ had $\Delta R^2=0.0013067592718559024$ with $p=0.0693359375$; $\texttt{K\_AAA}$ had $\Delta R^2=0.0005307046179682497$ with $p=0.2294921875$; $\texttt{Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY}$ had $\Delta R^2=0.0010134090056701938$ with $p=0.2158203125$; $\texttt{Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG}$ had $\Delta R^2=0.00017615626369389472$ with $p=0.24609375$; $\texttt{Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG}$ had $\Delta R^2=0.00004390882136492317$ with $p=0.4873046875$; $\texttt{Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR}$ had $\Delta R^2=-0.0003205780368125179$ with $p=0.779296875$; and $\texttt{Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY}$ had $\Delta R^2=-0.00045340330387383447$ with $p=0.529296875$.  Thus the positive ordering is not enough by itself: several coordinates have positive numerical increments, but only $f_3\text{-stress}$ remains non-null under the registered fold-sign perturbation check.

\paragraph{Relation to translation-mediated protein abundance.}
A separate powered mediation surface supplies the finite context in which this structural-order axis should be read.  The protein-abundance mediation panel used the same nine $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates and joined organism-specific CDS, modeled tAI, and measured PAXdb abundance rows.  The organism row counts were $4042$ for \textit{Bacillus subtilis} 168, $11661$ for \textit{Caenorhabditis elegans}, $14410$ for \textit{Danio rerio}, $13295$ for \textit{Drosophila melanogaster}, $3739$ for \textit{Escherichia coli} K-12 MG1655, $12418$ for \textit{Gallus gallus}, $19053$ for \textit{Homo sapiens}, $19395$ for \textit{Mus musculus}, $4129$ for \textit{Mycobacterium smegmatis} mc2 155, $13322$ for \textit{Rattus norvegicus}, $5815$ for \textit{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}, and $1656$ for \textit{Sulfolobus solfataricus}.  The joint mediation fractions were in the unit interval for all twelve organisms, ranging from $0.01850668564589326$ in \textit{S. solfataricus} to $0.9491551041216973$ in yeast.  The yeast row had total residual-to-protein $R^2=0.29837624994140827$, direct residual-to-protein after modeled tAI $R^2=0.0151709093608293$, and indirect difference $0.28320534058057895$.  These numbers show that the structural-order axis identity is compatible with a broader translation-conditioned protein-abundance surface, but they do not by themselves convert the structural-order axis into a causal translation mechanism.

\paragraph{Matched structure and expression controls.}
The yeast structure-mediation run provides a second guard against over-reading the axis.  In the five-way yeast join, $4759$ genes were matched across CDS, protein abundance, measured translation efficiency, mRNA half-life, and a $5'$ coding-region folding proxy.  The reported CDS join hit rate was $0.9156038419146592$, and the overall join hit rate was $0.8818572656921754$.  After residualization, the direct $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ to protein-abundance value was $R^2_{QP}=0.2715520808117803$; conditioning on the structure proxy gave $R^2_{QP\mid S}=0.2706601505000995$; conditioning on measured translation efficiency plus mRNA stability gave $R^2_{QP\mid T,M}=0.13773967696311287$; and conditioning on all three gave $R^2_{QP\mid T,M,S}=0.13759011208318542$.  The structure-only fraction was $0.003284564452662019$, the structure-incremental fraction beyond translation and mRNA stability was $0.0005507778820193332$, and $R^2(S\sim Q)$ was $0.003774248455422586$ with right-tail permutation value $0.027972027972027972$.  These finite values justify mentioning a structural-order contact surface, but only as a controlled statistical partition over a proxy, not as folded-state observation.

\paragraph{Translation-readout boundary.}
The modeled tAI survival matrix was computed for three organisms, with $61$ sense codons, $9$ residual coordinates, a coupling threshold of $0.01$, $81$ entries above threshold, and maximum absolute modeled coupling $3801961.031607896$.  The three-organism panel is explicitly not powered for a population-level survival statement: the recorded organism count is $3$, and the registered rule states that powered survival requires at least $40$ matched species.  Therefore the present structural-order chapter may use modeled tAI, ribosome-occupancy routes, matched mRNA control, and protein-abundance rows only as bounded readout surfaces.  A translation claim requires a separate same-row translation contact; a folding claim requires a separate structure contact; physical admissibility, function, mechanism, and universality each require their own layer-matched evidence.

\paragraph{Negative and adjacent audits.}
Several adjacent finite audits sharpen the scope.  The observable execution decomposition in yeast ran on $3289$ genes and reported an observable execution subblock, meaning at least one observed rate-proxy slot passed both effective null classes.  The cross-ortholog selection-imprint run used $80$ orthogroups, $240$ rows, and $3$ species; its positive-selection gate was not supported, with $\Delta R^2=0.03250515015613468$, leave-clade-out $\Delta R^2=-0.0021477729853302424$, and null $95\%$ maximum $0.05427157066522958$.  The dynamic mass-balance run used $120$ genes and $718$ intervals, with $R^2$ moving from $0.996410628851051$ at the mass-plus-estimated-expression layer to $0.8953999568421264$ at the fuller layer and $\Delta R^2=-0.07584438093220836$.  The turnover audit used $3290$ genes and found no positive residual turnover improvement after expression, translation-efficiency, confidence, and N-end controls, with $\Delta R^2=-0.005461979433706483$ and $\Delta\mathrm{DL}=-13.68819707902732$ bits.  These neighboring rows keep the structural-order axis identity from being inflated into selection, mass-balance closure, turnover prediction, or molecular mechanism.

\paragraph{Local verdict.}
The finite verdict is that the powered $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ structural-order axis identity is ready as a controlled, coordinate-level statement: among the nine tested residual coordinates, $f_3\text{-stress}$ is the unique fold-significant structural-order carrier in the registered surface.  The statement remains intentionally local.  It supports neither direct residual-to-protein realization nor translation mechanism unless the residual first survives a layer-matched translation readout.  It also supplies no standalone evidence for folded structure, physical admissibility, biological function, organismal phenotype, selection, or a universal biological law.  Those claims would require distinct finite panels with their own measured contacts, controls, ranks, and null checks.
