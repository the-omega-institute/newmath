\subsection{Rank-powered joint translation-efficiency and stability mediation of the $B^*_{Q6}$ abundance residual}
\label{sec:biorality-q6-joint-te-stability-mediation}

\paragraph{Finite surface.}
This record concerns a finite row surface in which the nine $B^*_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates are read on the same coding-sequence rows as measured translation efficiency, an mRNA-stability readout, and measured protein abundance. The coordinate list is
$K\_AAA$, $Arg\_AGR$, $Ile\_AUA$, $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$, $Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG$, $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$, $Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG$, $Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY$, and $f3\_stress$.  Each organism uses the same residualizing covariate family: an intercept, $\log(\mathrm{CDS\ length})$, the twenty amino-acid composition proportions, $GC3$, and the $M$-density baseline given by the fraction of sense codons on the union support of the nine projected coordinates.  The empirical panel is rank-powered rather than a three-row saturation artifact: after residualization, $\operatorname{rank}(Z)=23$ and $\operatorname{rank}(X)=9$ in all three organisms, with residual degrees of freedom $2494$ for $E.\ coli$, $11299$ for human, and $4736$ for yeast.

\paragraph{Joined empirical rows.}
The four-way join is explicitly finite.  In $Escherichia\ coli\ K12\ MG1655$, the CDS contact reports $3739$ joined coding sequences and the four-way TE--mRNA-stability--protein-abundance panel keeps $2517$ rows, with TE matched for $3415$ protein ids and mRNA half-life matched for $2947$ gene ids; the reported four-way hit rate is $0.9133458143888741$.  In human, the CDS contact reports $19053$ joined coding sequences and the four-way panel keeps $11322$ rows, with $11554$ TE-bearing genes and $19480$ valid consensus mRNA records; the reported hit rate is $0.6064136881331024$.  In yeast, the CDS contact reports $5815$ joined coding sequences and the four-way panel keeps $4759$ rows, with $5128$ TE-bearing proteins and $5379$ mRNA half-life records; the reported hit rate is $0.8818572656921754$.  The TE definitions are not interchangeable proxies: $E.\ coli$ uses mean CDS footprint wiggle coverage divided by mean CDS mRNA wiggle coverage, human uses positive RPFdb CDS translation-efficiency values averaged by Ensembl gene, and yeast uses the mean rich-medium footprint rpkM divided by the mean rich-medium mRNA rpkM.

\paragraph{Variance decomposition.}
Let $Q_e$ denote the residualized nine-coordinate design, $T_e$ the residualized measured-TE column, $M_e$ the residualized mRNA-stability column, and $P_e$ the residualized log protein abundance.  The finite statistic first measures the direct abundance association
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{QP} &= R^2(P_e \sim Q_e),
\end{aligned}
$$
and then recomputes the incremental contribution of $Q_e$ after conditioning on $T_e$, on $M_e$, and on both columns together:
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{QP\mid T} &= R^2(P_e \sim Q_e \mid T_e),\\
R^2_{QP\mid M} &= R^2(P_e \sim Q_e \mid M_e),\\
R^2_{QP\mid T,M} &= R^2(P_e \sim Q_e \mid T_e,M_e),\\
\mu_{T,M} &= \frac{R^2_{QP}-R^2_{QP\mid T,M}}{R^2_{QP}}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The observed values are organism-specific.  For $E.\ coli$,
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{QP} &= 0.1181743523064932,\\
R^2_{QP\mid T} &= 0.11583979275713331,\\
R^2_{QP\mid M} &= 0.09093273405121653,\\
R^2_{QP\mid T,M} &= 0.08975732521542036,\\
\mu_{T,M} &= 0.2404669586626661.
\end{aligned}
$$
For human,
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{QP} &= 0.04274178488511215,\\
R^2_{QP\mid T} &= 0.04250692268197657,\\
R^2_{QP\mid M} &= 0.036196017359154814,\\
R^2_{QP\mid T,M} &= 0.0355223930537993,\\
\mu_{T,M} &= 0.16890712099923358.
\end{aligned}
$$
For yeast,
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{QP} &= 0.2715520808117803,\\
R^2_{QP\mid T} &= 0.1439989545940314,\\
R^2_{QP\mid M} &= 0.25453612246767165,\\
R^2_{QP\mid T,M} &= 0.13047582645538838,\\
\mu_{T,M} &= 0.5195182225621592.
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus all three panels show a positive finite attenuation of the direct $Q_e \to P_e$ association after the two measured readouts are included, but the split differs sharply by organism.  The TE-only fractions are $0.01975521340963268$ in $E.\ coli$, $0.00549490864190343$ in human, and $0.4697188319693239$ in yeast.  The stability-only fractions are $0.23052056324898387$, $0.15314679870183337$, and $0.0626618595344251$, respectively.

\paragraph{Overlap classification and permutation scale.}
The joint term is classified as approximately independent in each organism under the fixed descriptive tolerance $0.05$.  The joint-minus-single-sum values are $-0.009808817995950458$ for $E.\ coli$, $0.010265413655496769$ for human, and $-0.012862468941589822$ for yeast, so the two single-readout fractions nearly add to the joint fraction on this diagnostic scale.  The right-tail pair-permutation value is the same finite-grid value in all three cases,
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{\mathrm{right}} &= 0.000999000999000999,
\end{aligned}
$$
with deterministic seeds tied to organism-specific joint $T,M$ pair permutations.  The energy diagnostics are consistent with the same reading: in yeast, for example, $T$ alone explains $440.4712858729507$ units of $P$ energy and $T,M$ together explain $470.61694807139054$, while the residualized abundance energy is $1724.6664873071313$; in human, $T$ alone explains only $6.781948682039624$ units whereas $M$ explains $768.267193808668$ units, against $12615.307912483846$ residual abundance energy.

\paragraph{Relation to direct and dictionary readouts.}
The joint-mediation computation should be read against two neighboring finite facts.  The direct protein-omics screen found $S^{QP}$-type survival for all nine coordinates in fifteen protein-abundance panels, with examples including $R^2_{QP}=0.10767526677985896$ for $E.\ coli$, $0.04454565118019448$ for human, and $0.29837624994140827$ for yeast.  The measured ribosome-TE screen separately reports positive measured-TE survival for the same nine-coordinate family, including $R^2_{QT,\mathrm{meas}}=0.009394266754822262$ for $E.\ coli$, $0.018837911344127926$ for human, and $0.13681931343730436$ for yeast.  A readout-dictionary certificate on the yeast context used $\lambda_{DL}=0.01$, $n_{join}=1800$, $5$ folds, and $120$ deterministic permutations; its selection-aware null $95\%$ level is $0.029149007242956115$, while the recorded coverage is $0.24538211439730545$, leaving unexplained fraction $0.7546178856026946$.  These neighboring numbers keep the present chapter from collapsing the abundance residual into a single readout: the direct signal is real on the finite rows, measured TE and stability remove a finite part of it, and a substantial remainder remains outside the listed small readout family.

\paragraph{Local verdict.}
The finite-row verdict is positive for statistical joint mediation in the limited variance-partition sense.  The panel supplies same-row codon, measured-TE, mRNA-stability, and protein-abundance readouts for three rank-sufficient organisms; the direct $B^*_{Q6}$ abundance association is reduced after conditioning on measured TE and mRNA stability; and the observed joint fractions are $0.2404669586626661$, $0.16890712099923358$, and $0.5195182225621592$ for $E.\ coli$, human, and yeast.  This is not a causal pathway result.  The finite rows combine organism-specific datasets, experimental conditions, platforms, and identifier joins, so the calculation establishes a controlled descriptive decomposition of protein-abundance variance, not temporal ordering, perturbation response, rescue, elongation mechanism, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, phenotype, or a general biological law.  Any claim at those layers requires a separate layer-matched measurement and its own controlled finite statistic.
