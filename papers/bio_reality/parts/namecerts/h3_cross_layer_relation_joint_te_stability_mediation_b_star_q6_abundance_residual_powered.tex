\subsection{Joint measured-translation and mRNA-stability mediation of the $B^\ast_{Q6}$ abundance residual}
\label{sec:bioreality-q6-joint-te-stability-mediation}

\paragraph{Finite surface.}
The record is a finite matched-row test of whether the $B^\ast_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates retain a protein-abundance association after two measured downstream readouts are inserted: a measured translation-efficiency coordinate $T$ and an mRNA-stability coordinate $M$.  The source coordinate matrix has nine columns,
$K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $Leu_{CUN\,vs\,UUR}$, $Leu_{UUA\,vs\,UUG}$, $Ser_{UCR\,vs\,AGY}$, $Ser_{UCA\,vs\,UCG}$, $Thr_{ACR\,vs\,ACY}$, and $f_3$-stress.  All protein-abundance, translation-efficiency, and stability readouts are first residualized against the same finite control design $Z$: intercept, $\log(\mathrm{CDS\ length})$, the twenty amino-acid composition proportions from real CDS codon counts, $GC3$, and an $M$-density baseline equal to the fraction of sense codons on the union support of the nine projected $B^\ast_{Q6}$ vectors.  In each of the three computed organisms the control rank is $\operatorname{rank}(Z)=23$, the residualized $B^\ast_{Q6}$ rank is $\operatorname{rank}(Q\mid Z)=9$, and the single-readout ranks are $\operatorname{rank}(T\mid Z)=1$ and $\operatorname{rank}(M\mid Z)=1$.  The joint readout rank is $\operatorname{rank}(T,M\mid Z)=2$, while the augmented ranks are $10$ for $(Q,T)$ and $(Q,M)$ and $11$ for $(Q,T,M)$.

\paragraph{Joined rows and source contacts.}
The finite row joins are organism-specific and should be read as measurement surfaces, not as a universal law.  For \emph{Escherichia coli} K-12 MG1655, the four-way join contains $2517$ CDS rows, with reported CDS join hit rate $0.9978649586335735$ and TE join hit rate $0.9133458143888741$.  The measured-TE surface uses the Li--Burkhardt--Gross--Weissman 2014 random-fragment control data, with TE defined as mean CDS footprint wiggle coverage on the annotated strand divided by mean CDS mRNA wiggle coverage on the same strand; $3415$ TE genes were reported and $2947$ mRNA half-life records were available at growth rate $0.40\,h^{-1}$.  For \emph{Homo sapiens}, the four-way join contains $11322$ CDS rows, with reported CDS join hit rate $0.9779294769799313$ and TE join hit rate $0.6064136881331024$; the measured-TE field is the positive RPFdb CDS TE value from the GSE56887 surface, averaged across positive TE columns per Ensembl gene, and the mRNA stability field is the consensus RNA-stability table with $19480$ reported records.  For \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}, the four-way join contains $4759$ CDS rows, with reported CDS join hit rate $0.9156038419146592$ and TE join hit rate $0.8818572656921754$; measured TE is computed from Ingolia et al. 2009 rich-medium footprint and mRNA normalized read densities, and $5379$ mRNA half-life records are available from the Neymotin table.

\paragraph{Decomposition statistic.}
Let $P_e$, $Q_e$, $T_e$, and $M_e$ denote the residualized abundance, residual-coordinate, measured-TE, and mRNA-stability columns after projection away from $Z$.  The direct abundance score is
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{QP} &= R^2(P_e \sim Q_e).
\end{aligned}
$$
The conditioned scores are the incremental $Q_e$ contributions after adding $T_e$, $M_e$, or both mediators to the design.  The registered joint attenuation statistic is
$$
\begin{aligned}
J_{TM} &= \frac{R^2_{QP}-R^2_{QP\mid T,M}}{R^2_{QP}}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The corresponding single-readout attenuation fractions are obtained by replacing the joint denominator design with $T$ alone or $M$ alone.  This definition is a variance partition on finite rows.  It does not assert a temporal pathway, a perturbational intervention, or a molecular mechanism.

\paragraph{Mediation fractions and module classification.}
The joint attenuation is positive in every computed organism.  In \emph{E. coli}, the TE-only fraction is $0.01975521340963268$, the stability-only fraction is $0.23052056324898387$, and the joint fraction is $0.2404669586626661$.  The joint-minus-single-sum value is $-0.009808817995950458$, within the fixed descriptive tolerance $0.05$, so the overlap module is classified as approximately independent.  In human, the TE-only fraction is $0.00549490864190343$, the stability-only fraction is $0.15314679870183337$, and the joint fraction is $0.16890712099923358$; the joint-minus-single-sum value is $0.010265413655496769$, again classified as approximately independent.  In yeast, the TE-only fraction is much larger, $0.4697188319693239$, the stability-only fraction is $0.0626618595344251$, and the joint fraction is $0.5195182225621592$; the joint-minus-single-sum value is $-0.012862468941589822$, also within the approximately independent module.  The fixed right-tail permutation probability is $0.000999000999000999$ in each organism under the deterministic pair-permutation test.

The residual-energy diagnostics show that the attenuation is not an all-rank artifact. The explained energy on $P$ is $274.6151322248693$ for $Q$, $22.699433610195115$ for $T$, $376.8458226543232$ for $M$, and $388.6777658316693$ for $(T,M)$.  In human, the corresponding residual energies are $12615.307912483846$ for $P$, $1866.9940287020145$ for $T$, and $647.920297431553$ for $M$, with explained energies $539.2007770548378$ for $Q$, $6.781948682039624$ for $T$, $768.267193808668$ for $M$, and $798.0659074156708$ for $(T,M)$.  In yeast, the residual energies are $1724.6664873071313$ for $P$, $645.3764418081507$ for $T$, and $194.99141167236996$ for $M$, with explained energies $468.33677333459536$ for $Q$, $440.4712858729507$ for $T$, $33.14864736606459$ for $M$, and $470.61694807139054$ for $(T,M)$.  Thus the yeast row is TE-dominant, while the bacterial and human rows are stability-dominant under this finite decomposition.

\paragraph{Relation to direct abundance and measured-TE surfaces.}
The broader finite evidence packet also contains direct $Q\to P$ abundance survival across a larger protein-abundance panel and measured $Q\to T$ survival across measured ribosome-TE rows.  The direct protein-abundance run used fifteen organisms and found positive $R^2_{QP}$ entries after the same style of length, amino-acid composition, $GC3$, and $M$-density controls; examples include yeast at $R^2_{QP}=0.29837624994140827$, \emph{E. coli} at $0.10767526677985896$, and human at $0.04454565118019448$.  The measured-TE survival run reported positive measured $Q\to T$ readouts in yeast, \emph{E. coli}, human, and zebrafish, with $R^2_{QT,\mathrm{meas}}=0.13681931343730436$, $0.009394266754822262$, $0.018837911344127926$, and $0.04034217614654355$, respectively.  A separate measured-TE abundance mediation run found measured mediated fractions $0.4793069656345167$ in yeast, $0.04091158340950673$ in \emph{E. coli}, $0.0027966485036786744$ in human, and $0.0$ in zebrafish after clipping a suppressive raw fraction.  The present joint record refines that picture by showing that mRNA stability absorbs a substantial part of the direct abundance residual in \emph{E. coli} and human, whereas measured TE absorbs a larger part in yeast.

\paragraph{Dictionary comparison.}
The related finite readout-dictionary certificate on the yeast join used $n=1800$ rows from $4758$ available rows, five folds, $\lambda_{DL}=0.01$, $\rho_{join}=0.05$, and $120$ deterministic permutations.  Its best selection-aware coverage was $C^\ast=0.24538211439730545$ with unexplained fraction $0.7546178856026946$ and selection-aware null $95\%$ value $0.029149007242956115$.  That certificate is consistent with the present chapter's interpretation: measured readouts explain a real, finite part of the abundance residual, but they do not collapse the whole $B^\ast_{Q6}$ abundance association into a single completed biological pathway.

\paragraph{Local conclusion.}
The finite verdict is that the $B^\ast_{Q6}$ abundance residual is jointly attenuated by measured translation efficiency and mRNA stability in all three computed organism rows.  The strongest TE component appears in yeast, where $J_{TM}=0.5195182225621592$ and the TE-only fraction is $0.4697188319693239$.  The strongest stability components appear in \emph{E. coli} and human, where the stability-only fractions are $0.23052056324898387$ and $0.15314679870183337$.  The overlap module is approximately independent in all three cases, with right-tail permutation probability $0.000999000999000999$ for each row.  This supports a finite cross-layer statistical readback: a direct $Q\to P$ abundance residual partly decomposes through measured TE and mRNA stability when those readouts are available on matched CDS rows.

\paragraph{Boundary.}
The conclusion is deliberately narrow.  It does not by itself establish translation mechanism, folding state, physical admissibility, molecular function, system phenotype, evolutionary directionality, or universality.  The measured-TE and mRNA-stability columns are observational readouts from organism-specific datasets, and the protein-abundance column is a steady-state proteomics measurement.  A stronger claim would require a separate layer-matched contact, such as perturbation, rescue, direct mechanistic translation dynamics, folding or stability assay, localization assay, functional assay, or phenotype measurement, evaluated on the same finite row surface with its own controls.  Without that added contact, this chapter reports a controlled finite variance partition and nothing higher.
