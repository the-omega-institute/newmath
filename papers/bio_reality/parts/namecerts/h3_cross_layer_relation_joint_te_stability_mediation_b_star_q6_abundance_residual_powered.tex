\subsection{Joint measured-TE and mRNA-stability mediation for $B^{*}_{Q6}$ abundance residuals}\label{sec:bior-joint-te-stability-mediation-bstar-q6}\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite-row object.}
This record tests whether the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ synonymous residual surface reaches protein abundance through measured translation efficiency, mRNA stability, or both.  The finite carrier is a row-matched matrix whose rows are coding sequences with codon counts, a protein-abundance readout $P$, a measured translation-efficiency readout $T$, an mRNA-stability readout $M$, and the nine residual coordinates
$K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $Leu_{CUN\,vs\,UUR}$, $Leu_{UUA\,vs\,UUG}$, $Ser_{UCR\,vs\,AGY}$, $Ser_{UCA\,vs\,UCG}$, $Thr_{ACR\,vs\,ACY}$, and $f3_{stress}$.  Before the cross-layer comparison, $Q$, $T$, $M$, and $P$ are read after the same covariate removal: an intercept, $\log(\mathrm{cds\_len\_nt})$, the twenty amino-acid composition proportions, $GC3$, and the $M$-density baseline on the union support of the nine projected $B^{*}_{Q6}$ coordinates.  In all three organism panels the residualized coordinate rank is $9$, the control rank is $23$, the measured-TE rank is $1$, the mRNA-stability rank is $1$, and the joint $T,M$ rank is $2$.

\paragraph{Joined panels.}
Three finite panels were computed.  For \textit{Escherichia coli} K12 MG1655, the four-way join has $2517$ rows, with reported CDS join hit rate $0.9978649586$ and measured-TE join hit rate $0.9133458144$; $3415$ genes carry measured TE and $2947$ genes carry mRNA half-life records.  For \textit{Homo sapiens}, the four-way join has $11322$ rows, with reported CDS join hit rate $0.9779294770$ and measured-TE join hit rate $0.6064136881$; $11554$ genes carry measured TE and $19480$ consensus mRNA records are available.  For \textit{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}, the four-way join has $4759$ rows, with reported CDS join hit rate $0.9156038419$ and measured-TE join hit rate $0.8818572657$; $5128$ genes carry measured TE and $5379$ mRNA half-life records are available.  The residual degrees of freedom after $Z$ are respectively $2494$, $11299$, and $4736$.

\paragraph{Variance decomposition.}
Let $Q_e$, $T_e$, $M_e$, and $P_e$ denote the residualized coordinate, translation-efficiency, mRNA-stability, and protein-abundance columns after the shared control projection.  The direct abundance statistic is $R^2_{QP}=R^2(P_e\sim Q_e)$.  The conditional statistics $R^2_{QP\mid T}$, $R^2_{QP\mid M}$, and $R^2_{QP\mid T,M}$ are the remaining incremental $Q_e$ contributions after adjoining measured TE, mRNA stability, or both.  The joint attenuation fraction is
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{Med}_{T,M}(Q,P)&=\frac{R^2_{QP}-R^2_{QP\mid T,M}}{R^2_{QP}}.
\end{aligned}
$$
For \textit{E. coli}, the row gives $R^2_{QP}=0.1181743523$, $R^2_{QP\mid T}=0.1158397928$, $R^2_{QP\mid M}=0.0909327341$, and $R^2_{QP\mid T,M}=0.0897573252$.  Thus the joint attenuation fraction is $0.2404669587$, split descriptively as TE-only fraction $0.0197552134$ and stability-only fraction $0.2305205632$.  For human, $R^2_{QP}=0.0427417849$, $R^2_{QP\mid T}=0.0425069227$, $R^2_{QP\mid M}=0.0361960174$, and $R^2_{QP\mid T,M}=0.0355223931$, giving joint attenuation $0.1689071210$, TE-only fraction $0.0054949086$, and stability-only fraction $0.1531467987$.  For yeast, $R^2_{QP}=0.2715520808$, $R^2_{QP\mid T}=0.1439989546$, $R^2_{QP\mid M}=0.2545361225$, and $R^2_{QP\mid T,M}=0.1304758265$, giving joint attenuation $0.5195182226$, TE-only fraction $0.4697188320$, and stability-only fraction $0.0626618595$.

\paragraph{Overlap classification and finite significance check.}
The two-mediator overlap was classified as approximately independent in each organism.  The joint-minus-single-sum values were $-0.0098088180$ for \textit{E. coli}, $0.0102654137$ for human, and $-0.0128624689$ for yeast, all within the fixed descriptive tolerance $0.05$.  The paired $T,M$ permutation check used deterministic row-label permutations and returned the same right-tail value $p=0.000999000999$ for each organism.  This is not a claim that TE and mRNA stability are universally independent mediators; it says only that, in these three finite joined panels under this projection rule, the joint attenuation is numerically close to the sum of the two single-mediator attenuations.

\paragraph{Relation to adjacent finite rows.}
The measured-TE survival row reports positive $B^{*}_{Q6}$ survival against log measured TE in four organisms: $R^2_{QT,meas}=0.0093942668$ for \textit{E. coli}, $0.0188379113$ for human, $0.1368193134$ for yeast, and $0.0403421761$ for zebrafish, with all nine coordinates surviving under the declared positive threshold. The measured-TE abundance decomposition gives measured mediated fractions $0.0409115834$ for \textit{E. coli}, $0.0027966485$ for human, $0.4793069656$ for yeast, and $0$ for zebrafish after truncating a negative raw fraction.  A separate readout-dictionary certificate on the yeast context used $\lambda_{DL}=0.01$, $\rho_{join}=0.05$, $n_{join}=1800$, five folds, and $120$ permutations, with selection-aware null $95\%$ value $0.0291490072$, raw best coverage $0.2745002077$, accepted coverage $C^{*}=0.2453821144$, and unexplained fraction $0.7546178856$.  These neighboring rows support the interpretation that the abundance residual is real and only partly reducible to the measured readout dictionary.

\paragraph{Local verdict.}
The finite evidence supports a bounded cross-layer statement: in three rank-sufficient organism panels, the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ abundance association is attenuated after conditioning on measured TE and mRNA stability, with the strongest joint attenuation in yeast, a stability-heavy pattern in human and \textit{E. coli}, and a consistently positive residual component after both mediators.  The result is a variance partition on matched observational rows.  It does not establish a causal pathway, temporal ordering, translation mechanism, protein folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, phenotype, evolutionary direction, or a universal biological law.  Any such stronger statement would require a separate layer-matched contact and its own finite statistic.
