\subsection{Joint TE--stability mediation of the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ abundance residual}
\label{sec:bioreality-q6-joint-te-stability-mediation}

\paragraph{Finite surface.}
This record tests a finite cross-layer surface whose rows are coding sequences and whose columns are the nine $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates
$K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $Leu_{CUN\,vs\,UUR}$, $Leu_{UUA\,vs\,UUG}$, $Ser_{UCR\,vs\,AGY}$, $Ser_{UCA\,vs\,UCG}$, $Thr_{ACR\,vs\,ACY}$, and $f_3$-stress.  The response $P$ is residualized log protein abundance, $T$ is residualized measured translation efficiency, and $M$ is residualized mRNA-stability or abundance control on the same joined rows.  The control design $Z$ has 24 columns before rank reduction: an intercept, $\log$ CDS length, twenty amino-acid composition proportions, GC3, and an $M$-density baseline on the union support of the nine projected coordinates.  In each computed organism the effective control rank is $23$, the residual coordinate rank is $9$, the measured-TE rank is $1$, the mRNA-control rank is $1$, the joint $T,M$ rank is $2$, and the augmented ranks are $10$ for $Q,T$ and $Q,M$ and $11$ for $Q,T,M$.

\paragraph{Joined measured panels.}
The four-way join is finite and organism-specific.  For \emph{Escherichia coli} K12 MG1655, the joined panel contains $2517$ CDS rows; the reported CDS join hit rate is $0.9978649586335735$ and the TE join hit rate is $0.9133458143888741$.  The TE readout is mean CDS footprint wiggle coverage divided by mean CDS mRNA wiggle coverage from the Li--Burkhardt--Gross--Weissman 2014 random-fragment control data, with $3415$ genes carrying TE values and $2947$ valid mRNA half-life records at growth rate $0.40\,h^{-1}$.  For \emph{Homo sapiens}, the joined panel contains $11322$ rows; the reported CDS join hit rate is $0.9779294769799313$ and the TE join hit rate is $0.6064136881331024$.  The measured TE values come from the RPFdb processed GSE56887 table, with $11554$ TE genes and $19480$ mRNA consensus records.  For \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}, the joined panel contains $4759$ rows; the reported CDS join hit rate is $0.9156038419146592$ and the TE join hit rate is $0.8818572656921754$.  The yeast TE readout is the mean footprint normalized read density divided by the mean mRNA normalized read density across two rich-medium replicates from the Ingolia 2009 data, with $5128$ TE genes and $5379$ valid mRNA half-life records.

\paragraph{Variance partition.}
The statistic is not a causal mediation coefficient.  It is the finite least-squares variance partition obtained after projecting $Q$, $T$, $M$, and $P$ off the same $Z$ controls.  The direct residual abundance association is
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{QP}(E.\ coli) &= 0.1181743523064932,\\
R^2_{QP}(H.\ sapiens) &= 0.04274178488511215,\\
R^2_{QP}(S.\ cerevisiae) &= 0.2715520808117803.
\end{aligned}
$$
Conditioning on measured TE alone gives
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{QP\mid T}(E.\ coli) &= 0.11583979275713331,\\
R^2_{QP\mid T}(H.\ sapiens) &= 0.04250692268197657,\\
R^2_{QP\mid T}(S.\ cerevisiae) &= 0.1439989545940314,
\end{aligned}
$$
whereas conditioning on the mRNA-control coordinate alone gives
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{QP\mid M}(E.\ coli) &= 0.09093273405121653,\\
R^2_{QP\mid M}(H.\ sapiens) &= 0.036196017359154814,\\
R^2_{QP\mid M}(S.\ cerevisiae) &= 0.25453612246767165.
\end{aligned}
$$
The joint conditioning fit gives
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{QP\mid T,M}(E.\ coli) &= 0.08975732521542036,\\
R^2_{QP\mid T,M}(H.\ sapiens) &= 0.0355223930537993,\\
R^2_{QP\mid T,M}(S.\ cerevisiae) &= 0.13047582645538838.
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the finite joint attenuation fractions are $0.2404669586626661$ for \emph{E. coli}, $0.16890712099923358$ for human, and $0.5195182225621592$ for yeast.  The right-tail permutation value is $0.000999000999000999$ in all three organisms under the deterministic paired $T,M$ row-label permutation used for this run.

\paragraph{Mediator split.}
The split between the two conditioning coordinates is organism-dependent.  In \emph{E. coli}, the measured-TE-only fraction is $0.01975521340963268$, while the mRNA-stability-only fraction is $0.23052056324898387$; the joint-minus-single-sum value is $-0.009808817995950458$, classified as approximately independent under the fixed tolerance $0.05$.  In human, the measured-TE-only fraction is $0.00549490864190343$, the mRNA-control fraction is $0.15314679870183337$, and the joint-minus-single-sum value is $0.010265413655496769$, again approximately independent.  In yeast, the measured-TE-only fraction is much larger, $0.4697188319693239$, while the mRNA-stability-only fraction is $0.0626618595344251$; the joint-minus-single-sum value is $-0.012862468941589822$, still inside the same approximately independent tolerance.  This is the main finite-row discrimination: yeast routes much more of the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}\to P$ residual through the measured TE coordinate, while \emph{E. coli} and human route more of the attenuation through the mRNA-control coordinate.

\paragraph{Energy diagnostics.}
The residual abundance energies after $Z$ are $2323.813305213946$ for \emph{E. coli}, $12615.307912483846$ for human, and $1724.6664873071313$ for yeast.  The direct $Q$ explained energies on $P$ are $274.6151322248693$, $539.2007770548378$, and $468.33677333459536$, respectively.  The measured TE coordinate alone explains $22.699433610195115$ on $P$ in \emph{E. coli}, $6.781948682039624$ in human, and $440.4712858729507$ in yeast; the mRNA-control coordinate alone explains $376.8458226543232$, $768.267193808668$, and $33.14864736606459$.  The joint $T,M$ explained energies are $388.6777658316693$, $798.0659074156708$, and $470.61694807139054$.  These numbers agree with the preceding fraction split: the yeast panel has a large measured-TE abundance component, whereas the bacterial and human panels have stronger mRNA-control absorption in this particular joined surface.

\paragraph{Relation to companion finite rows.}
The measured-TE survival row confirms that the same nine-coordinate $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ design has positive measured-TE association after the same class of controls: $R^2_{QT,meas}=0.009394266754822262$ in \emph{E. coli}, $0.018837911344127926$ in human, and $0.13681931343730436$ in yeast, with all nine coordinates surviving in each of those panels.  The direct protein-abundance row is also positive in broad proteomics panels, for example $R^2_{QP}=0.10767526677985896$ in \emph{E. coli}, $0.04454565118019448$ in human, and $0.29837624994140827$ in yeast when the direct PAXdb abundance readout is tested without the joint $T,M$ conditioning surface.  A separate measured-TE mediation row reports measured mediated fractions $0.04091158340950673$ for \emph{E. coli}, $0.0027966485036786744$ for human, and $0.4793069656345167$ for yeast, matching the same qualitative pattern: yeast has the strongest measured-TE attenuation of the codon residual abundance association.  A related finite dictionary certificate on yeast uses $\lambda_{DL}=0.01$, $n=1800$, five folds, and $120$ permutations; its selected readout family covers $0.24538211439730545$ of the residual signal, above the selection-aware null $95$ percentile $0.029149007242956115$, leaving unexplained fraction $0.7546178856026946$.

\paragraph{Local verdict.}
The ready finite statement is therefore the following: on the three organism panels where codon counts, measured TE, mRNA-control readouts, and protein abundance are joined on the same CDS units, the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual abundance signal is positive and is attenuated by conditioning on measured TE and mRNA-control coordinates.  The attenuation is statistically strong under the registered permutation comparison, with right-tail value $0.000999000999000999$ in each panel, and the split of the attenuation is biologically nonuniform across organisms.  This supports a finite, controlled cross-layer association and a variance-partition mediation summary.  It does not by itself assert a translation mechanism, a temporal causal path, folded structural order, physical admissibility, molecular function, phenotype, or a universal biological rule.  Those stronger readings require separate matched measurements at the relevant layer, and direct $Q\to P$ survival is kept as prediction rather than mechanism unless the measured $Q\to T$ step survives and conditioning on $T$ reduces the $Q\to P$ association on the same joined rows.
