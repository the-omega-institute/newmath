\subsection{Joint measured-TE and mRNA-stability mediation for the $B^*_{Q6}$ abundance residual}
\label{sec:bioreality-q6-joint-te-stability-mediation}

\paragraph{Finite row object.}
This record concerns the cross-layer finite matrix whose rows are coding-sequence units with matched codon counts, measured translation efficiency, an mRNA-stability readout, and measured protein abundance.  The source design is the nine-dimensional $B^*_{Q6}$ synonymous residual surface with coordinates $K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $Leu_{CUN\,vs\,UUR}$, $Leu_{UUA\,vs\,UUG}$, $Ser_{UCR\,vs\,AGY}$, $Ser_{UCA\,vs\,UCG}$, $Thr_{ACR\,vs\,ACY}$, and $f_3$-stress.  Every organism-specific design is residualized against the same covariate family: intercept, $\log(\mathrm{CDS\ length})$, the twenty amino-acid composition proportions, GC3, and the $M$-density baseline on the union support of the nine projected coordinates.  The verified finite run contains three four-way joins: $2517$ joined coding rows for \textit{Escherichia coli} K-12 MG1655, $11322$ rows for \textit{Homo sapiens}, and $4759$ rows for \textit{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}.  The corresponding reported join hit rates are $0.9133458143888741$, $0.6064136881331024$, and $0.8818572656921754$ for the translation-efficiency side of the four-way join.  The codon-to-abundance contact itself is therefore not a symbolic association: it is a finite row table with observed $P$, observed or processed TE, and an mRNA-stability covariate on the same coding units.

\paragraph{Residualized ranks and measured readouts.}
After control residualization, the rank diagnostics are nondegenerate in all three organisms.  For \textit{E. coli}, the control rank is $23$, the residual degrees of freedom are $2494$, $\operatorname{rank}(Q)=9$, $\operatorname{rank}(T)=1$, $\operatorname{rank}(M)=1$, and $\operatorname{rank}(Q,T,M)=11$.  For human, the same rank pattern appears with residual degrees of freedom $11299$.  For yeast, the same rank pattern appears with residual degrees of freedom $4736$.  The residual protein-abundance energies are $2323.813305213946$, $12615.307912483846$, and $1724.6664873071313$, while the residual measured-TE energies are $672.36799431777$, $1866.9940287020145$, and $645.3764418081507$.  The mRNA-stability residual energies are $198.6753916649337$, $647.920297431553$, and $194.99141167236996$.  These values matter because the mediation statement is only meaningful when the $Q$, $T$, and $M$ columns retain rank after the same covariates have been removed.

\paragraph{Variance partition.}
The statistic is a controlled variance partition.  Let $P_e$ be the residualized protein-abundance vector, let $Q_e$ be the residualized nine-coordinate $B^*_{Q6}$ design, let $T_e$ be the residualized measured-TE column, and let $M_e$ be the residualized mRNA-stability column.  The reported quantities are
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{QP} &= R^2(P_e \sim Q_e),\\
R^2_{QP\mid T} &= \Delta R^2(P_e \sim Q_e \mid T_e),\\
R^2_{QP\mid M} &= \Delta R^2(P_e \sim Q_e \mid M_e),\\
R^2_{QP\mid T,M} &= \Delta R^2(P_e \sim Q_e \mid T_e,M_e),\\
J_{T,M} &= \frac{R^2_{QP}-R^2_{QP\mid T,M}}{R^2_{QP}}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The computed $R^2$ list is organism-specific.  In \textit{E. coli}, $R^2_{QP}=0.1181743523064932$, $R^2_{QP\mid T}=0.11583979275713331$, $R^2_{QP\mid M}=0.09093273405121653$, and $R^2_{QP\mid T,M}=0.08975732521542036$.  In human, the corresponding list is $0.04274178488511215$, $0.04250692268197657$, $0.036196017359154814$, and $0.0355223930537993$.  In yeast, the list is $0.2715520808117803$, $0.1439989545940314$, $0.25453612246767165$, and $0.13047582645538838$.  Thus the joint mediator pair absorbs $24.04669586626661\%$ of the controlled $Q\to P$ abundance association in \textit{E. coli}, $16.890712099923358\%$ in human, and $51.95182225621592\%$ in yeast.

\paragraph{Single-mediator structure.}
The two mediator columns do not contribute equally.  The TE-only fractions are $0.01975521340963268$ for \textit{E. coli}, $0.00549490864190343$ for human, and $0.4697188319693239$ for yeast.  The mRNA-stability-only fractions are $0.23052056324898387$, $0.15314679870183337$, and $0.0626618595344251$.  In this finite row panel, yeast is the case where measured TE carries most of the joint reduction, whereas \textit{E. coli} and human are dominated by the stability column.  The overlap diagnostic classifies all three organisms as approximately independent at the fixed descriptive tolerance $0.05$: the joint-minus-single-sum values are $-0.009808817995950458$, $0.010265413655496769$, and $-0.012862468941589822$.  The right-tail permutation value is $0.000999000999000999$ in each organism, using the deterministic joint $T,M$ pair-permutation scheme recorded by the run.

\paragraph{Relation to the measured-TE and direct-protein contacts.}
The joint mediation run is consistent with the separate measured-TE survival surface.  In the measured-TE contact, the $B^*_{Q6}$ residuals have positive controlled $Q\to T$ readout values in the organisms used here: $R^2_{QT,\mathrm{meas}}=0.009394266754822262$ for \textit{E. coli}, $0.018837911344127926$ for human, and $0.13681931343730436$ for yeast, with nine surviving coordinates in each case.  The direct protein-omics contact is also positive: the same coordinate family has direct $Q\to P$ survival values $R^2_{QP}=0.10767526677985896$ for \textit{E. coli}, $0.04454565118019448$ for human, and $0.29837624994140827$ for yeast in the broader PAXdb abundance table, again with all nine coordinates counted as surviving under the stated positive threshold.  These companion surfaces justify asking the mediation question, but they do not by themselves answer it; the present record uses the same-row four-way table and measures how much of the abundance association remains after $T_e$ and $M_e$ are explicitly conditioned.

\paragraph{Readout dictionary and residual remainder.}
The finite-row evidence is not a complete reduction of the $B^*_{Q6}$ abundance residual.  A related yeast readout-dictionary certificate on the unified context join reports $n_{\mathrm{join}}=1800$ from $4758$ available rows, fold count $5$, $\lambda_{DL}=0.01$, $\rho_{\mathrm{join}}=0.05$, raw best coverage $0.2745002077400602$, selection-aware null $95\%$ value $0.029149007242956115$, and accepted coverage $C^*=0.24538211439730545$.  Its unexplained fraction is $0.7546178856026946$.  Thus the same data family supports partial reduction to a small measured-readout dictionary, but it also leaves a large residual component.  The $f_3$-stress dwell contact gives a more localized example inside yeast: with $n_{\mathrm{join}}=1257$, fold count $5$, and $120$ deterministic permutations, the base+dwell held-out increment is $\Delta R^2=0.01856473944057712$, above the null $95\%$ value $0.0013115362189692937$, with $f_3$-dwell correlation $0.1615441450901653$ and approximate $p=7.873916057196935\times 10^{-9}$.  The positional structural-order contact remains more limited: its primary ramp window has weighted $f_3$ ramp increment $0.0011165640103326878$, body increment $-0.00026157313923957926$, sign-flip value $0.0478515625$ for the ramp, and the finite conclusion is ramp-positive but not localized.  These companion finite rows sharpen the interpretation: the abundance residual has measurable translation and stability components, yet the remaining component cannot be silently promoted to structure, localization, function, or a universal biological rule.

\paragraph{Scientific verdict.}
The finite-row result is ready as a statistical cross-layer record.  It supports the following bounded statement: in the three rank-sufficient organism tables, the controlled association from the nine-dimensional $B^*_{Q6}$ residual design to measured protein abundance is reduced after conditioning on measured TE and mRNA stability, with joint mediation fractions $0.2404669586626661$, $0.16890712099923358$, and $0.5195182225621592$.  The organism pattern is heterogeneous: yeast shows a strong TE-associated reduction, while \textit{E. coli} and human show larger stability-associated reductions.  The verified object is not a causal pathway, not a perturbation rescue, and not a proof that synonymous residual coordinates determine protein output.  It is a finite same-row variance decomposition after amino-acid composition, length, GC3, and $M$-density controls.

\paragraph{Local boundary.}
No translation mechanism follows merely from a positive $Q\to P$ association, and no folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, organismal phenotype, or universal law follows from this chapter.  Measured TE and mRNA stability are noisy condition- and study-specific readouts; the abundance, TE, and stability measurements are observational and need not share a causal ordering.  A stronger biological claim would require a separate layer-matched contact: direct perturbation or rescue for mechanism, structure-level data for folding or physical admissibility, functional assays for molecular function, and phenotype assays for organism-level consequences.  The admissible conclusion here is exactly the controlled finite-row mediation decomposition reported above.
