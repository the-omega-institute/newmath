\subsection{Joint Translation-Efficiency and mRNA-Stability Mediation of the $B^*_{Q6}$ Abundance Residual}\label{sec:bioreality-q6-joint-te-stability-mediation}\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite surface.}  This record fixes a finite matched surface whose rows are coding sequences and whose columns are the nine residual $B^*_{Q6}$ coordinates, a measured translation-efficiency readout $T$, an mRNA-stability readout $M$, a measured protein-abundance readout $P$, and the shared covariates $Z$.  The residual coordinates are
$K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $Leu_{CUN\,vs\,UUR}$, $Leu_{UUA\,vs\,UUG}$, $Ser_{UCR\,vs\,AGY}$, $Ser_{UCA\,vs\,UCG}$, $Thr_{ACR\,vs\,ACY}$, and $f_3\_stress$.  The control basis contains an intercept, $\log$ CDS length, twenty amino-acid-composition proportions from real CDS codon counts, GC3, and an $M$-density baseline defined on the union support of the nine projected coordinates.  In every evaluated organism the residualized coordinate design has rank $9$ and the control design has rank $23$, so the readback is not an all-fit boundary artifact.

\paragraph{Matched rows.}  Three organisms have the four-way join required for this chapter.  In \textit{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}, the join contains $4759$ rows, with a reported CDS join hit rate of $0.9156038419146592$ and a TE join hit rate of $0.8818572656921754$; $5128$ genes have TE records and $5379$ mRNA half-life records are available.  In \textit{Escherichia coli} K12 MG1655, the join contains $2517$ rows, with a reported CDS join hit rate of $0.9978649586335735$ and a TE join hit rate of $0.9133458143888741$; $3415$ genes have TE records and $2947$ mRNA half-life records are available.  In \textit{Homo sapiens}, the join contains $11322$ rows, with a reported CDS join hit rate of $0.9779294769799313$ and a TE join hit rate of $0.6064136881331024$; $11554$ genes have TE records and $19480$ consensus mRNA records are available.  These are large finite joins rather than a three-row organism-level regression.

\paragraph{Variance partition.}  The statistic decomposes the residual protein-abundance association of the nine-dimensional coordinate matrix $Q$ after $Z$ residualization.  The direct quantity is $R^2_{QP}$, and the conditioned quantities are the incremental $Q$ contributions after adding measured translation efficiency, mRNA stability, or both.  The reported joint fraction is
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{JointFraction} = \frac{R^2_{QP}-R^2_{QP\mid T,M}}{R^2_{QP}}.
\end{aligned}
$$
For \textit{S. cerevisiae}, $R^2_{QP}=0.2715520808117803$, $R^2_{QP\mid T}=0.1439989545940314$, $R^2_{QP\mid M}=0.25453612246767165$, and $R^2_{QP\mid T,M}=0.13047582645538838$.  The resulting joint mediation fraction is $0.5195182225621592$, with TE-only fraction $0.4697188319693239$ and stability-only fraction $0.0626618595344251$.  For \textit{E. coli}, $R^2_{QP}=0.1181743523064932$, $R^2_{QP\mid T}=0.11583979275713331$, $R^2_{QP\mid M}=0.09093273405121653$, and $R^2_{QP\mid T,M}=0.08975732521542036$, giving a joint fraction of $0.2404669586626661$, a TE-only fraction of $0.01975521340963268$, and a stability-only fraction of $0.23052056324898387$.  For human, $R^2_{QP}=0.04274178488511215$, $R^2_{QP\mid T}=0.04250692268197657$, $R^2_{QP\mid M}=0.036196017359154814$, and $R^2_{QP\mid T,M}=0.0355223930537993$, giving a joint fraction of $0.16890712099923358$, a TE-only fraction of $0.00549490864190343$, and a stability-only fraction of $0.15314679870183337$.

\paragraph{Readout direction.}  The same coordinate system also survives against measured ribosome-derived TE in the separate measured-TE run: $R^2_{QT,meas}=0.13681931343730436$ in yeast, $0.009394266754822262$ in \textit{E. coli}, and $0.018837911344127926$ in human, with all nine coordinates surviving in each of these three organisms.  The measured abundance mediation run gives compatible directional scale: the measured mediated fraction is $0.4793069656345167$ in yeast, $0.04091158340950673$ in \textit{E. coli}, and $0.0027966485036786744$ in human, while danio is reported as $0.0$ after clipping a negative raw fraction.  Thus the joint TE--stability chapter should be read as a refinement of an already positive measured-TE boundary, not as a claim that a direct abundance association alone identifies a translation mechanism.

\paragraph{Overlap class.}  The single-mediator fractions nearly add to the joint fraction under the fixed descriptive tolerance $0.05$.  Yeast has single-mediator sum $0.532380691503749$ and joint-minus-single-sum $-0.012862468941589822$; \textit{E. coli} has $0.25027577665861656$ and $-0.009808817995950458$; human has $0.1586417073437368$ and $0.010265413655496769$.  All three are classified as approximately independent under the stated tolerance.  The right-tail permutation value is $0.000999000999000999$ in each organism, using the deterministic joint $T,M$ pair-permutation seed recorded for that organism.

\paragraph{Interpretation.}  The ready conclusion is finite and descriptive: in three large matched panels, the residual $B^*_{Q6}$ protein-abundance association is reduced when measured TE and mRNA stability are included, and the reduction is organism-specific.  Yeast is dominated by measured TE; \textit{E. coli} and human are dominated by mRNA stability, with measured TE contributing only a small fraction.  This supports a cross-layer variance-partition statement for matched rows, not a causal pathway statement.

\paragraph{Local boundary.}  No translation realization, folded structure, physical admissibility, molecular function, organism phenotype, mechanism, evolutionary direction, or universal biological rule follows from this chapter alone.  Measured TE and mRNA stability are observational readouts from particular studies, and the protein-abundance readout is cross-sectional.  A stronger claim would require a separate layer-matched contact, perturbation or rescue design, and controls showing that the relevant readout changes in the predicted direction.  The admissible statement here is only that the finite matched matrices exhibit a reproducible residual variance partition under the declared controls.
