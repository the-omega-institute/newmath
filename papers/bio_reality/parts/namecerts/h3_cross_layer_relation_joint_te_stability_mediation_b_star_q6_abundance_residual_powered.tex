\subsection{Joint Translation-Efficiency and Stability Mediation for the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ Abundance Residual}
\label{sec:bioreality-q6-joint-te-stability-mediation}

\paragraph{Finite panel.}
This record tests whether the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ synonymous residual surface that predicts protein abundance can be decomposed through two measured intermediate readouts: a translation-efficiency readout $T$ and an mRNA-stability readout $M$.  The carrier is a finite joined table of coding sequences, residual coordinates, measured translation-efficiency rows, matched mRNA-stability rows, and matched protein-abundance rows.  The nine residual coordinates are
$K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $Leu_{CUN\,vs\,UUR}$, $Leu_{UUA\,vs\,UUG}$, $Ser_{UCR\,vs\,AGY}$, $Ser_{UCA\,vs\,UCG}$, $Thr_{ACR\,vs\,ACY}$, and $f3_{stress}$.
Before the mediation calculation, $Q$, $T$, $M$, and $P$ are residualized against an intercept, $\log(\mathrm{CDS\ length})$, twenty amino-acid composition proportions, $GC3$, and the $M$-density baseline on the union support of the nine projected $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ coordinates.  In each of the three computed organisms the residualized $Q$ design has rank $9$, the residualized $T$ and $M$ designs each have rank $1$, the joint $T,M$ design has rank $2$, and the control design has rank $23$ from $24$ control columns.

\paragraph{Joined readout rows.}
The four-way joins are nondegenerate.  In \textit{Escherichia coli} K12 MG1655, $2517$ rows enter the joint calculation; the codon/protein join reports $3739$ CDS rows with hit rate $0.9978649586$, and the translation-efficiency source reports $3415$ usable genes with join hit rate $0.9133458144$.  The stability readout is a half-life table with $2947$ valid gene records at growth rate $0.40\,h^{-1}$.  In \textit{Homo sapiens}, $11322$ rows enter; the codon/protein join reports $19053$ CDS rows with hit rate $0.9779294770$, and the translation-efficiency source reports $11554$ usable genes with join hit rate $0.6064136881$.  The stability side uses $19480$ valid consensus mRNA records.  In \textit{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}, $4759$ rows enter; the codon/protein join reports $5815$ CDS rows with hit rate $0.9156038419$, and the translation-efficiency source reports $5128$ usable genes with join hit rate $0.8818572657$.  The stability table contributes $5379$ valid mRNA half-life records.

\paragraph{Definition of the decomposition.}
Let $Q_e$, $T_e$, $M_e$, and $P_e$ denote the residualized coordinate matrix, measured translation-efficiency vector, mRNA-stability vector, and protein-abundance vector after the shared control projection.  The direct abundance association is $R^2_{QP}=R^2(P_e\sim Q_e)$.  The conditioned quantities $R^2_{QP\mid T}$, $R^2_{QP\mid M}$, and $R^2_{QP\mid T,M}$ are the incremental $R^2$ values of $Q_e$ after adding $T_e$, $M_e$, or both mediators first.  The joint absorbed fraction is
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mu_{T,M}(Q,P) &= \frac{R^2_{QP}-R^2_{QP\mid T,M}}{R^2_{QP}} .
\end{aligned}
$$
This is a finite variance partition.  It is not a causal mediation effect, since abundance, translation-efficiency, and stability are measured from per-organism single-study or cross-study surfaces rather than from perturbation and rescue experiments.

\paragraph{Mediation fractions and module class.}
The joint absorbed fractions are $0.2404669587$ in \textit{E. coli}, $0.1689071210$ in human, and $0.5195182226$ in yeast.  The corresponding single-readout components are:

The overlap audit classifies all three organisms as approximately independent modules under tolerance $0.05$: the joint-minus-single-sum values are $-0.0098088180$, $0.0102654137$, and $-0.0128624689$, respectively.  Each right-tail paired-permutation test reports $p=0.000999001$, using deterministic seeds of the form $\mathrm{sha256}(\mathrm{claim},\mathrm{organism},\mathrm{joint\ pair\ permutation},\mathrm{index},n)$.

\paragraph{Relation to measured translation survival.}
The measured translation-efficiency survival calculation gives positive $Q\to T$ readout association in the organisms where this record can be compared with the TE surface: $R^2_{QT,meas}=0.0093942668$ for \textit{E. coli}, $0.0188379113$ for human, and $0.1368193134$ for yeast, with nine surviving coordinates in each case.  The broader measured-TE abundance mediation run reports measured mediated fractions $0.0409115834$ for \textit{E. coli}, $0.0027966485$ for human, and $0.4793069656$ for yeast, with the yeast value aligning with the large TE-only component in the joint $T,M$ decomposition.  These numbers support the finite statement that the abundance residual is partly read through measured translation-efficiency and mRNA-stability covariates, with organism-specific weights.

\paragraph{Residual not exhausted.}
The partial mediation dictionary check on the yeast context keeps the residual from being promoted into a complete mechanism.  On a unified yeast join with $n=1800$, $4758$ available joined rows, $5$ folds, $\lambda_{DL}=0.01$, and $120$ permutations, the best small readout dictionary $[\mathrm{measured\_te},\mathrm{mrna\_stability},\mathrm{turnover}]$ covers $C^{\ast}=0.2453821144$ of the source signal.  The raw best coverage is $0.2745002077$, the selection-aware null $95$ percentile is $0.0291490072$, and the unexplained fraction remains $0.7546178856$.  A separate irreducibility audit on the same yeast scale also uses $n=1800$, $5$ folds, $\lambda_{DL}=0.01$, $\rho_{join}=0.05$, and $120$ permutations; its status means that the source signal is real and at least one listed reduction family refutes the strongest irreducibility form, not that a global biological law has been found.

\paragraph{Local claim boundary.}
The admissible conclusion is a controlled finite-row statement: in the three computed organisms, the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ abundance residual has a positive direct protein-abundance association, and part of that association is absorbed by measured translation-efficiency and mRNA-stability readouts after the declared controls.  The strongest yeast case is translation-heavy, with $\mu_T=0.4697188320$ and $\mu_{T,M}=0.5195182226$; the \textit{E. coli} and human cases are stability-heavy, with $\mu_M=0.2305205632$ and $0.1531467987$.  No stronger biological assertion follows from these rows alone.  The calculation does not establish causal translation, protein folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, phenotype, evolutionary direction, or universality.  Any such assertion would require an additional layer-matched measurement and a separate finite statistic rather than the present variance partition.
