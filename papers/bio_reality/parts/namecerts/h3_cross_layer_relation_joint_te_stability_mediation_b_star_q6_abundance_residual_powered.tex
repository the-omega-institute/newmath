\subsection{Joint translation-efficiency and mRNA-stability mediation for $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ abundance residuals}
\label{sec:bioreality-q6-joint-te-stability-mediation}

\paragraph{Finite surface.}
This record concerns a finite matched matrix whose rows are coding sequences and whose columns consist of nine $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates, one measured translation-efficiency readout $T$, one mRNA-stability readout $M$, and one measured protein-abundance readout $P$.  The nine residual coordinates are $K\_AAA$, $Arg\_AGR$, $Ile\_AUA$, $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$, $Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG$, $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$, $Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG$, $Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY$, and $f3\_stress$.  Before fitting the cross-layer surface, $Q$, $T$, $M$, and $P$ are residualized against a 24-column control design: intercept, log CDS length, the 20 amino-acid composition proportions, GC3, and an M-density baseline defined by the union support of the nine projected $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ vectors.  In the three organisms where the four-way join is available, the residual design has rank $rank(Z)=23$ and $rank(Q\_{\perp Z})=9$.

\paragraph{Joined organisms and row counts.}
The finite panel is not a broad phylogenetic survey; it is a three-organism matched readout surface.  In \textit{E. coli} K-12 MG1655, the four-way join contains 2517 rows, with a reported CDS join hit rate of 0.9978649586335735 and a TE join hit rate of 0.9133458143888741.  The measured-TE source uses Li, Burkhardt, Gross, and Weissman 2014 footprint and mRNA wiggle coverage, and the stability source supplies 2947 valid mRNA half-life records at growth rate $0.40\,h^{-1}$.  In human, the four-way join contains 11322 rows, with CDS hit rate 0.9779294769799313 and TE hit rate 0.6064136881331024; the TE side is RPFdb processed GSE56887, and the mRNA side has 19480 valid consensus records.  In budding yeast, the four-way join contains 4759 rows, with CDS hit rate 0.9156038419146592 and TE hit rate 0.8818572656921754; the TE side uses Ingolia et al. 2009 rich-medium footprint and mRNA quantification, and the mRNA-stability side has 5379 valid half-life records.

\paragraph{Variance partition.}
The measured quantity is a controlled variance decomposition, not a causal mediation effect.  For each organism, $R^2\_{QP}$ is the fraction of residual protein-abundance variance explained by the nine-dimensional residual coordinate block after $Z$-residualization.  The conditioned values $R^2\_{QP\mid T}$, $R^2\_{QP\mid M}$, and $R^2\_{QP\mid T,M}$ are the incremental $Q$ contributions after adding measured TE, mRNA stability, or both.  The joint attenuation fraction is
$$
\begin{aligned}
J &= \frac{R^2\_{QP}-R^2\_{QP\mid T,M}}{R^2\_{QP}} .
\end{aligned}
$$
For \textit{E. coli}, the fitted values are $R^2\_{QP}=0.1181743523064932$, $R^2\_{QP\mid T}=0.11583979275713331$, $R^2\_{QP\mid M}=0.09093273405121653$, and $R^2\_{QP\mid T,M}=0.08975732521542036$, giving $J=0.2404669586626661$.  The single-readout split is TE-only $0.01975521340963268$ and stability-only $0.23052056324898387$.  For human, $R^2\_{QP}=0.04274178488511215$, $R^2\_{QP\mid T}=0.04250692268197657$, $R^2\_{QP\mid M}=0.036196017359154814$, and $R^2\_{QP\mid T,M}=0.0355223930537993$, giving $J=0.16890712099923358$, with TE-only $0.00549490864190343$ and stability-only $0.15314679870183337$.  For yeast, $R^2\_{QP}=0.2715520808117803$, $R^2\_{QP\mid T}=0.1439989545940314$, $R^2\_{QP\mid M}=0.25453612246767165$, and $R^2\_{QP\mid T,M}=0.13047582645538838$, giving $J=0.5195182225621592$, with TE-only $0.4697188319693239$ and stability-only $0.0626618595344251$.

\paragraph{Readback by organism.}
The three organisms separate into different finite readback regimes.  Yeast is the strongest measured-TE attenuation case: the direct $Q \rightarrow P$ residual association is large, and conditioning on measured TE removes nearly half of it before the joint TE-plus-stability fit removes slightly more than half.  Human is the stability-dominant case: the measured-TE fraction is only $0.00549490864190343$, while mRNA stability accounts for $0.15314679870183337$ of the direct residual signal.  \textit{E. coli} is also stability-dominant, with a small TE-only fraction and a larger stability-only fraction.  In all three organisms, the joint fraction is close to the sum of the two single-readout fractions within the fixed descriptive tolerance $0.05$: the joint-minus-single-sum values are $-0.009808817995950458$ for \textit{E. coli}, $0.010265413655496769$ for human, and $-0.012862468941589822$ for yeast.  The right-tail paired-readout permutation value is $0.000999000999000999$ in each organism, using the deterministic seed keyed by organism and the joint $T,M$ pair permutation design.

\paragraph{Rank and energy diagnostics.}
The finite-row fits are nondegenerate under the declared rank checks.  In \textit{E. coli}, after $Z$-residualization the residual degrees of freedom are 2494, with residual energies $\|T\_{\perp Z}\|^2=672.36799431777$, $\|M\_{\perp Z}\|^2=198.6753916649337$, and $\|P\_{\perp Z}\|^2=2323.813305213946$.  The ranks are $rank(T\_{\perp Z})=1$, $rank(M\_{\perp Z})=1$, $rank((T,M)\_{\perp Z})=2$, and $rank((Q,T,M)\_{\perp Z})=11$.  In human, the residual degrees of freedom are 11299, with $\|T\_{\perp Z}\|^2=1866.9940287020145$, $\|M\_{\perp Z}\|^2=647.920297431553$, and $\|P\_{\perp Z}\|^2=12615.307912483846$.  In yeast, the residual degrees of freedom are 4736, with $\|T\_{\perp Z}\|^2=645.3764418081507$, $\|M\_{\perp Z}\|^2=194.99141167236996$, and $\|P\_{\perp Z}\|^2=1724.6664873071313$.  The degenerate-$R^2$ threshold is $0.999999$ throughout, and the fitted rank block expands from 9 for $Q$ alone to 11 for $Q,T,M$.

\paragraph{Relation to nearby finite tests.}
The same coordinate block has positive measured-TE survival in a separate powered TE test: $R^2\_{QT,measured}=0.009394266754822262$ in \textit{E. coli}, $0.018837911344127926$ in human, and $0.13681931343730436$ in yeast, with all nine coordinates surviving under the positive partial-$R^2$ threshold $10^{-12}$.  The direct protein-omics surface is also positive in the same organisms: $R^2\_{QP}=0.10767526677985896$ in \textit{E. coli}, $0.04454565118019448$ in human, and $0.29837624994140827$ in yeast when computed on the broader direct protein-abundance join.  A measured TE-to-abundance mediation fit reports mediated fractions $0.04091158340950673$ in \textit{E. coli}, $0.0027966485036786744$ in human, and $0.4793069656345167$ in yeast, with danio recorded separately as a suppressive or null measured-TE mediation case clipped to zero.  A yeast readout-dictionary certificate gives selection-aware coverage $0.24538211439730545$ at $\lambda\_{DL}=0.01$, with unexplained fraction $0.7546178856026946$ and selection-aware null95 $0.029149007242956115$; this supports partial reducibility without erasing the remaining residual.  The paired irreducibility check uses the same $\lambda\_{DL}=0.01$, $\rho\_{join}=0.05$, five folds, and 120 permutations on a yeast join of 1800 rows from 4758 available rows, and its own status semantics explicitly reject a global irreducibility certificate.

\paragraph{Local interpretation.}
The finite conclusion is that $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates retain measurable association with protein abundance after sequence-composition controls, and that the amount absorbed by measured TE and mRNA stability is organism-specific.  Yeast shows a large measured-TE component; human and \textit{E. coli} are dominated by mRNA-stability attenuation.  This is enough to justify a cross-layer statistical readback over the matched rows, but it is not enough to assert a biochemical pathway, a synonymous-edit rescue mechanism, protein folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, organismal phenotype, or a universal biological law.  The attached data supply measured TE, stability, abundance, and codon-coordinate rows for this variance partition only.  Any stronger biological statement would require a separate layer-matched contact and its own finite statistic.
