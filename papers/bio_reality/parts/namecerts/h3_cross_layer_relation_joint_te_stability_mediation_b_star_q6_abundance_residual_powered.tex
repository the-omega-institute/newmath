\paragraph{Finite surface.}
This record tests a finite cross-layer surface whose rows are coding sequences and whose columns combine nine $B^*_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates, a measured translation-efficiency readout $T$, an mRNA-stability readout $M$, matched protein abundance $P$, and the control design $Z$.  The nine residual coordinates are $K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $Leu_{CUN\,vs\,UUR}$, $Leu_{UUA\,vs\,UUG}$, $Ser_{UCR\,vs\,AGY}$, $Ser_{UCA\,vs\,UCG}$, $Thr_{ACR\,vs\,ACY}$, and $f3\_stress$.  The control columns are an intercept, $\log(\mathrm{CDS\ length})$, the twenty amino-acid composition proportions, $GC3$, and the $M$-density baseline on the union support of the nine projected coordinates.  In all three computed organisms the residualized $Q$ design has rank $9$ and the control design has rank $23$ with $24$ supplied control columns, so the reported numbers are not the rank-degenerate three-organism all-$1.0$ boundary.

\paragraph{Variance partition.}
For each organism, $P_e$, $Q_e$, $T_e$, and $M_e$ denote the corresponding residuals after projection away from $Z$.  The direct abundance association is $R^2_{QP}=R^2(P_e\sim Q_e)$.  The conditioned quantities $R^2_{QP\mid T}$, $R^2_{QP\mid M}$, and $R^2_{QP\mid T,M}$ are the incremental explanatory fractions for $Q_e$ after conditioning on measured TE, on mRNA stability, or on both readouts.  The joint absorbed fraction is
$$
\begin{aligned}
  J_{T,M} = \frac{R^2_{QP}-R^2_{QP\mid T,M}}{R^2_{QP}} .
\end{aligned}
$$
The numerical readback is organism-specific.  For \emph{E. coli},
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{QP} &= 0.1181743523064932,\\
R^2_{QP\mid T} &= 0.11583979275713331,\\
R^2_{QP\mid M} &= 0.09093273405121653,\\
R^2_{QP\mid T,M} &= 0.08975732521542036.
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus $J_{T,M}=0.2404669586626661$, with a TE-only fraction $0.01975521340963268$ and an mRNA-stability-only fraction $0.23052056324898387$.  For human,
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{QP} &= 0.04274178488511215,\\

R^2_{QP\mid M} &= 0.036196017359154814,\\
R^2_{QP\mid T,M} &= 0.0355223930537993.
\end{aligned}
$$
The corresponding joint fraction is $0.16890712099923358$, with TE-only fraction $0.00549490864190343$ and stability-only fraction $0.15314679870183337$.  For yeast,

\begin{aligned}

The yeast joint fraction is $0.5195182225621592$, with TE-only fraction $0.4697188319693239$ and stability-only fraction $0.0626618595344251$.

\paragraph{Overlap classification.}
The overlap readback compares the joint absorbed fraction with the sum of the two single-readout absorbed fractions.  In \emph{E. coli}, the single-readout sum is $0.25027577665861656$ and the joint-minus-sum value is $-0.009808817995950458$.  In human, the corresponding values are $0.1586417073437368$ and $0.010265413655496769$.  In yeast, they are $0.532380691503749$ and $-0.012862468941589822$.  With the fixed descriptive tolerance $0.05$, all three organisms are classified as approximately independent overlap surfaces rather than strong redundancy or strong antagonism.  The paired readout permutation right-tail value is $0.000999000999000999$ in each organism under the registered deterministic seeds, so the finite-row record supports a nonzero statistical decomposition of the $Q\to P$ residual through the two measured readout columns.

\paragraph{Layer interpretation.}
The strongest readback is in yeast: measured TE absorbs a large part of the direct $B^*_{Q6}$ abundance association, while mRNA stability contributes a smaller additional share.  In \emph{E. coli} and human, mRNA stability accounts for most of the absorbed part and TE is much weaker.  This gives a three-organism table of finite variance partitions, not a universal biological law.  The result is also narrower than a mechanism claim: the TE and mRNA-stability measurements are observational, condition-specific, and assembled from organism-level datasets that need not share a perturbation design or time ordering.  Conditioning on $T$ or $M$ can reduce a direct $Q\to P$ association, but it does not by itself prove a causal pathway, a translation mechanism, protein folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, system phenotype, or evolutionary direction.  Any such higher-layer claim would require an additional layer-matched contact and its own finite statistic.

\paragraph{Status.}
This chapter is therefore ready as a bounded BioReality record for the conjecture $q6.joint\text{-}te\text{-}stability\text{-}mediation.abundance\text{-}residual.cross\text{-}layer$.  It establishes that the finite matched panels support a measured statistical partition of the $B^*_{Q6}$ protein-abundance residual into direct, TE-conditioned, mRNA-stability-conditioned, and jointly conditioned components.  It does not upgrade the direct protein-omics survival surface into translation, structure, function, physical-admissibility, mechanism, or universality.
