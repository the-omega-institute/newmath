\subsection{Joint measured-TE and mRNA-stability partition of the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ abundance residual}
\label{sec:bioreality-q6-joint-te-stability-mediation}

\paragraph{Finite row surface.}
The record tested here is a finite cross-layer table over nine $B^{*}_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates, a measured translation-efficiency readout $T$, an mRNA-stability readout $M$, and a matched protein-abundance readout $P$.  The coordinate list is
$K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $Leu_{CUN\,vs\,UUR}$, $Leu_{UUA\,vs\,UUG}$, $Ser_{UCR\,vs\,AGY}$, $Ser_{UCA\,vs\,UCG}$, $Thr_{ACR\,vs\,ACY}$, and $f3\_stress$.  Each row first removes the same control family $Z$: intercept, $\log(\mathrm{CDS\ length})$, twenty amino-acid composition proportions, $GC3$, and the $M$-density baseline defined by the union support of the nine projected $B^{*}_{Q6}$ vectors.  The matrix therefore asks a narrow question: after these controls, how much of the $Q \to P$ association is lost when the residualized abundance readout is additionally conditioned on measured translation efficiency, on mRNA stability, or on both together?

\paragraph{Joined panels.}
The current powered table contains three organisms with four-way joins.  In \emph{Escherichia coli} K12 MG1655, $2517$ CDS rows were retained; the reported CDS join hit rate was $0.9978649586335735$, the TE join hit rate was $0.9133458143888741$, and the TE source used $3415$ valid protein-keyed TE rows.  The mRNA half-life source supplied $2947$ valid gene-id rows at growth rate $0.40\,h^{-1}$.  In human, $11322$ rows were retained; the reported CDS join hit rate was $0.9779294769799313$, the TE join hit rate was $0.6064136881331024$, and the TE source supplied $11554$ valid protein-keyed TE rows.  The mRNA consensus table supplied $19480$ Ensembl-gene rows and used $17$ gene-symbol fallbacks in the final join.  In \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}, $4759$ rows were retained; the reported CDS join hit rate was $0.9156038419146592$, the TE join hit rate was $0.8818572656921754$, the TE source supplied $5128$ valid protein-keyed TE rows, and the mRNA half-life table supplied $5379$ valid systematic-name rows.  These row counts are large enough for the residualized nine-coordinate $Q$ block to have rank $9$ in every organism after the $Z$ projection.

\paragraph{Decomposition.}
Let $Q_e$, $T_e$, $M_e$, and $P_e$ denote the residualized coordinate, translation-efficiency, stability, and protein-abundance columns after removing $Z$.  The finite statistic is the variance partition
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{QP} &= R^2(P_e \sim Q_e),\\
R^2_{QP\mid T} &= R^2(P_e \sim Q_e \mid T_e),\\
R^2_{QP\mid M} &= R^2(P_e \sim Q_e \mid M_e),\\
R^2_{QP\mid T,M} &= R^2(P_e \sim Q_e \mid T_e,M_e),\\
\mu_{T,M} &= \frac{R^2_{QP}-R^2_{QP\mid T,M}}{R^2_{QP}}.
\end{aligned}
$$
For \emph{E. coli}, the values are $R^2_{QP}=0.1181743523064932$, $R^2_{QP\mid T}=0.11583979275713331$, $R^2_{QP\mid M}=0.09093273405121653$, and $R^2_{QP\mid T,M}=0.08975732521542036$, giving joint mediation fraction $0.2404669586626661$.  For human, the values are $0.04274178488511215$, $0.04250692268197657$, $0.036196017359154814$, and $0.0355223930537993$, giving joint fraction $0.16890712099923358$.  For yeast, the values are $0.2715520808117803$, $0.1439989545940314$, $0.25453612246767165$, and $0.13047582645538838$, giving joint fraction $0.5195182225621592$.

\paragraph{Mediator profile.}
The two single-mediator fractions are highly organism-specific.  In \emph{E. coli}, the measured-TE-only fraction is $0.01975521340963268$ while the stability-only fraction is $0.23052056324898387$; the joint fraction is therefore mostly associated with the mRNA-stability side of the table.  In human, the measured-TE-only fraction is only $0.00549490864190343$, while the stability-only fraction is $0.15314679870183337$.  In yeast, the pattern is reversed: the measured-TE-only fraction is $0.4697188319693239$, the stability-only fraction is $0.0626618595344251$, and the joint fraction passes one half of the direct abundance association.  The right-tail permutation value is $0.000999000999000999$ in all three organism rows under the deterministic paired $T,M$ permutation design.

\paragraph{Rank and energy diagnostics.}
The diagnostic ranks are nondegenerate in each row: $rank(Z)=23$, $rank(Q_e)=9$, $rank(T_e)=1$, $rank(M_e)=1$, $rank(T_e,M_e)=2$, $rank(Q_e,T_e)=10$, $rank(Q_e,M_e)=10$, and $rank(Q_e,T_e,M_e)=11$.  The abundance residual energies are $2323.813305213946$ for \emph{E. coli}, $12615.307912483846$ for human, and $1724.6664873071313$ for yeast.  The direct $Q$ explained energies on $P$ are $274.6151322248693$, $539.2007770548378$, and $468.33677333459536$, respectively.  Conditioning on both mediators leaves direct $Q$ improvement energies of $208.57926657600905$, $448.1259261619555$, and $225.02728529130957$.  The translation residual energies are $672.36799431777$, $1866.9940287020145$, and $645.3764418081507$; the stability residual energies are $198.6753916649337$, $647.920297431553$, and $194.99141167236996$.

\paragraph{Overlap classification.}
The overlap module classifies all three rows as approximately independent at descriptive tolerance $0.05$.  In \emph{E. coli}, the single-mediator sum is $0.25027577665861656$ and the joint-minus-single-sum value is $-0.009808817995950458$.  In human, the corresponding values are $0.1586417073437368$ and $0.010265413655496769$.  In yeast, they are $0.532380691503749$ and $-0.012862468941589822$.  Thus the finite table does not show a large antagonistic or redundant collapse between $T$ and $M$; within this tolerance, the two conditioning routes add nearly linearly even though their relative weights differ sharply by organism.

\paragraph{Relation to neighboring finite checks.}
The same nine-coordinate source has measured-TE survival in four organisms: $R^2_{QT,meas}=0.009394266754822262$ for \emph{E. coli}, $0.018837911344127926$ for human, $0.13681931343730436$ for yeast, and $0.04034217614654355$ for zebrafish.  The direct protein-abundance survival table gives positive $R^2_{QP}$ entries over a broader fifteen-organism panel, including $0.10767526677985896$ for \emph{E. coli}, $0.04454565118019448$ for human, and $0.29837624994140827$ for yeast.  A measured-TE-to-abundance mediation table gives measured mediated fractions $0.04091158340950673$, $0.0027966485036786744$, and $0.4793069656345167$ for these same three organisms, matching the present result that yeast is the row where measured translation efficiency absorbs the largest part of the abundance residual.  A separate readout-dictionary check on the yeast context used $n=1800$, $\lambda_{DL}=0.01$, $\rho_{join}=0.05$, $120$ permutations, and found coverage $C^{*}=0.24538211439730545$ with selection-aware null $95\%$ value $0.029149007242956115$; this supports partial reducibility but leaves unexplained fraction $0.7546178856026946$.  The irreducibility certificate uses the same yeast source design with $n_{join}=1800$, $n_{join\ available}=4758$, $\lambda_{DL}=0.01$, $\rho_{join}=0.05$, five folds, and $120$ permutations, and should be read as a test of listed reductions rather than as a claim of a final biological primitive.

\paragraph{Verdict.}
The finite-row result is ready at the level of statistical cross-layer partitioning.  The table supports the statement that the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ abundance residual is partly absorbed by measured TE and mRNA stability, with yeast showing a large measured-TE component, human and \emph{E. coli} showing stronger stability components, and all three rows giving significant paired-permutation evidence for the joint $T,M$ conditioning surface.  It does not show a universal organism-independent rule: the mediator weights are different in each organism, and the data are drawn from separate per-organism datasets with different platforms and conditions.

\paragraph{Local no-promotion rule.}
This chapter does not infer translation mechanism, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, phenotype, evolutionary direction, or universality from the $Q \to P$ association.  Measured TE and mRNA stability are noisy observational readouts, and the fractions above are variance partitions, not causal effects.  Any claim about ribosome mechanism, protein structure, localization, turnover causality, function, phenotype, or a general biological law requires its own matched measurement layer and a separately evaluated statistic.  A direct $Q \to P$ term that remains after conditioning is only an unresolved abundance residual in this chapter.
