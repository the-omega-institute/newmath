\subsection{Joint TE--Stability Mediation of the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ Abundance Residual}

\paragraph{Finite-row surface.}
This record studies a finite matched surface in which the nine residual coordinates $Q$ are paired with three per-organism rows containing coding-sequence counts, measured translation efficiency $T$, mRNA-stability or half-life readout $M$, and measured protein abundance $P$.  The coordinates are $\texttt{K\_AAA}$, $\texttt{Arg\_AGR}$, $\texttt{Ile\_AUA}$, $\texttt{Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR}$, $\texttt{Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG}$, $\texttt{Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY}$, $\texttt{Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG}$, $\texttt{Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY}$, and $\texttt{f3\_stress}$.  The controls are an intercept, $\log$ CDS length, the twenty amino-acid composition proportions, GC3, and an $M$-density baseline on the union support of the projected $B^{*}_{Q6}$ vectors.  In all three rows the control rank is $23$, the residualized $Q$ rank is $9$, and the joint $Q,T,M$ rank is $11$, so the reported decompositions are not rank-degenerate all-one surfaces.  The joined organism rows are $2517$ for \emph{Escherichia coli} K12 MG1655, $11322$ for \emph{Homo sapiens}, and $4759$ for \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}; the corresponding reported four-way join hit rates are $0.9133458143888741$, $0.6064136881331024$, and $0.8818572656921754$.

\paragraph{Variance partition.}
After residualizing by the shared control matrix, the direct abundance association is measured by $R^{2}_{QP}$, and the conditioned quantities $R^{2}_{QP\mid T}$, $R^{2}_{QP\mid M}$, and $R^{2}_{QP\mid T,M}$ measure the remaining incremental $Q$ contribution after adding measured TE, stability, or both readouts.  The finite decomposition is
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{joint\;fraction} &= \frac{R^{2}_{QP}-R^{2}_{QP\mid T,M}}{R^{2}_{QP}}.
\end{aligned}
$$
For \emph{E. coli}, $R^{2}_{QP}=0.1181743523064932$, $R^{2}_{QP\mid T}=0.11583979275713331$, $R^{2}_{QP\mid M}=0.09093273405121653$, and $R^{2}_{QP\mid T,M}=0.08975732521542036$, giving joint mediation fraction $0.2404669586626661$.  The TE-only fraction is $0.01975521340963268$ and the stability-only fraction is $0.23052056324898387$.  For human, the corresponding list is $0.04274178488511215$, $0.04250692268197657$, $0.036196017359154814$, and $0.0355223930537993$, with joint fraction $0.16890712099923358$, TE-only fraction $0.00549490864190343$, and stability-only fraction $0.15314679870183337$.  For yeast, the list is $0.2715520808117803$, $0.1439989545940314$, $0.25453612246767165$, and $0.13047582645538838$, with joint fraction $0.5195182225621592$, TE-only fraction $0.4697188319693239$, and stability-only fraction $0.0626618595344251$.

\paragraph{Overlap classification and finite null.}
The three rows have deterministic pair-permutation right-tail values $p=0.000999000999000999$.  Their overlap mode is classified as approximately independent under the stated tolerance $0.05$: the joint-minus-single-sum values are $-0.009808817995950458$ for \emph{E. coli}, $0.010265413655496769$ for human, and $-0.012862468941589822$ for yeast.  This classification is important because it says that, in these rows, the TE and stability readouts do not simply duplicate one another as finite explanatory columns.  The rank diagnostics support the same reading: for \emph{E. coli}, $T$ explains $22.699433610195115$ units of residual protein-abundance energy, $M$ explains $376.8458226543232$, and $T,M$ together explain $388.6777658316693$; for human, the corresponding energies are $6.781948682039624$, $768.267193808668$, and $798.0659074156708$; for yeast they are $440.4712858729507$, $33.14864736606459$, and $470.61694807139054$.

\paragraph{Relation to measured-TE survival.}
The measured-TE survival row supplies the separate $Q \to T$ contact needed to keep this chapter from reading a direct protein-omics association as a translation statement.  In the powered measured-TE run, the residualized $Q$ rank is $9$ after control rank $23$, and the measured $R^{2}_{QT}$ values are $0.009394266754822262$ for \emph{E. coli}, $0.018837911344127926$ for human, and $0.13681931343730436$ for yeast, with an additional danio row at $0.04034217614654355$.  The measured abundance mediation run gives the corresponding measured-mediated fractions $0.04091158340950673$ for \emph{E. coli}, $0.0027966485036786744$ for human, and $0.4793069656345167$ for yeast, with danio clipped to $0.0$ from raw value $-0.04801486048721136$.  Thus the joint TE--stability chapter is not using tAI wording or protein abundance alone as a hidden translation surrogate; it is conditioning the abundance residual by measured TE and by a distinct stability readout.

\paragraph{Readback.}
The biological readback is a three-row, four-column statement: $Q$ has a direct abundance residual in each row, measured TE removes only a small part in \emph{E. coli} and human but a large part in yeast, stability removes a large part in \emph{E. coli} and human but a smaller part in yeast, and the joint model removes more than either single readout alone.  The yeast row is TE-dominant, with $0.4697188319693239$ TE-only fraction and $0.5195182225621592$ joint fraction.  The bacterial and human rows are stability-dominant, with stability-only fractions $0.23052056324898387$ and $0.15314679870183337$.  A separate partial-mediation dictionary run on yeast reports $\lambda_{\mathrm{DL}}=0.01$, $\rho_{\mathrm{join}}=0.05$, $n_{\mathrm{join}}=1800$, coverage $C^{*}=0.24538211439730545$, selection-aware null $95$ value $0.029149007242956115$, and unexplained fraction $0.7546178856026946$, so the TE--stability pair is a substantial but incomplete readout family rather than a full reduction of the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ abundance residual.  A related dwell-mediated yeast check reports $n_{\mathrm{join}}=1257$, base $R^{2}=0.1855578757644517$, full $R^{2}=0.20412261520502883$, held-out $\Delta R^{2}=0.01856473944057712$, null $95$ value $0.0013115362189692937$, and $f3$--dwell approximate $p=7.873916057196935\times 10^{-9}$, giving a concrete downstream readout for one coordinate without making it a universal mechanism.

\paragraph{Local boundary.}
The finite rows justify a statistical variance-partition claim: in the matched rows above, residualized measured TE and residualized mRNA stability jointly absorb a positive fraction of the residual $B^{*}_{Q6}$ association with measured protein abundance, with organism-specific allocation between the two readouts.  They do not establish causation, temporal ordering, ribosome mechanism, folded structure, physical admissibility, molecular function, phenotype, or universality.  The abundance, TE, and stability contacts come from per-organism datasets and can differ in platform and condition.  A stronger biological assertion would require a separate layer-matched contact and a new finite statistic at that layer; without such a contact, the present chapter remains a controlled cross-layer association and attenuation record.
