\subsection{Joint measured-translation and stability conditioning for the $B^*_{Q6}$ abundance residual}
\label{subsec:biorality-joint-te-stability-mediation-b-star-q6-abundance-residual-powered}

\paragraph{Finite surface.}
The finite object is a three-organism matched matrix whose rows are coding sequences and whose columns are residualized $B^*_{Q6}$ coordinates, measured translation-efficiency readouts, mRNA-stability readouts, protein-abundance readouts, and sequence controls.  The $B^*_{Q6}$ block has nine residual coordinates,
$K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $Leu_{CUN\,vs\,UUR}$, $Leu_{UUA\,vs\,UUG}$, $Ser_{UCR\,vs\,AGY}$, $Ser_{UCA\,vs\,UCG}$, $Thr_{ACR\,vs\,ACY}$, and $f3\_stress$.  The control block $Z$ has an intercept, $\log(\mathrm{CDS\ length})$, twenty amino-acid composition proportions, GC3, and the $M$-density baseline on the union support of the nine projected coordinates.  After residualizing against $Z$, the measured question is not whether codon geometry is a biological cause.  It is only whether the direct $Q \to P$ abundance association is reduced when the same rows also contain measured translation efficiency $T$ and mRNA stability $M$.

\paragraph{Joined rows and rank.}
The joined four-way table was computed for $\mathrm{E.\ coli}$ K12 MG1655, human, and budding yeast.  In $\mathrm{E.\ coli}$ the four-way join contains $2517$ CDS rows, with reported CDS hit rate $0.9978649586335735$ and translation-readout join hit rate $0.9133458143888741$; the TE source contributes $3415$ protein-keyed entries and the mRNA-stability source contributes $2947$ valid gene records.  In human the joined table contains $11322$ rows, with reported CDS hit rate $0.9779294769799313$ and translation-readout join hit rate $0.6064136881331024$; the TE table contributes $11554$ gene entries and the mRNA consensus table contributes $19480$ valid Ensembl records.  In yeast the joined table contains $4759$ rows, with reported CDS hit rate $0.9156038419146592$ and translation-readout join hit rate $0.8818572656921754$; the TE table contributes $5128$ protein-keyed entries and the mRNA half-life table contributes $5379$ valid systematic-gene records.

For all three organisms the rank diagnostics are nondegenerate for the intended residual regression: $\operatorname{rank}(Z)=23$, $\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde Q)=9$, $\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde T)=1$, $\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde M)=1$, $\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde T,\widetilde M)=2$, $\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde Q,\widetilde T,\widetilde M)=11$.  The residual degrees of freedom after $Z$ are $2494$ for $\mathrm{E.\ coli}$, $11299$ for human, and $4736$ for yeast.  The residual protein-abundance energies are respectively $2323.813305213946$, $12615.307912483846$, and $1724.6664873071313$; the residual measured-TE energies are $672.36799431777$, $1866.9940287020145$, and $645.3764418081507$; and the residual mRNA-stability energies are $198.6753916649337$, $647.920297431553$, and $194.99141167236996$.

\paragraph{Decomposition statistic.}
For each organism, let $P_e$, $Q_e$, $T_e$, and $M_e$ denote the residualized protein abundance, nine-coordinate $B^*_{Q6}$ design, measured translation efficiency, and mRNA stability after removing $Z$.  The statistic compares the direct abundance fit $R^2_{QP}$ with the incremental $Q$ contribution after conditioning on $T$, on $M$, and on both readouts:
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{QP} &= R^2(P_e \sim Q_e),\\
R^2_{QP\mid T} &= R^2(P_e \sim Q_e,T_e)-R^2(P_e \sim T_e),\\
R^2_{QP\mid M} &= R^2(P_e \sim Q_e,M_e)-R^2(P_e \sim M_e),\\
R^2_{QP\mid T,M} &= R^2(P_e \sim Q_e,T_e,M_e)-R^2(P_e \sim T_e,M_e),\\
\mu_{T,M} &= \frac{R^2_{QP}-R^2_{QP\mid T,M}}{R^2_{QP}}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The resulting finite rows are as follows.  For $\mathrm{E.\ coli}$, $R^2_{QP}=0.1181743523064932$, $R^2_{QP\mid T}=0.11583979275713331$, $R^2_{QP\mid M}=0.09093273405121653$, and $R^2_{QP\mid T,M}=0.08975732521542036$, giving joint mediated fraction $\mu_{T,M}=0.2404669586626661$.  For human, $R^2_{QP}=0.04274178488511215$, $R^2_{QP\mid T}=0.04250692268197657$, $R^2_{QP\mid M}=0.036196017359154814$, and $R^2_{QP\mid T,M}=0.0355223930537993$, giving $\mu_{T,M}=0.16890712099923358$.  For yeast, $R^2_{QP}=0.2715520808117803$, $R^2_{QP\mid T}=0.1439989545940314$, $R^2_{QP\mid M}=0.25453612246767165$, and $R^2_{QP\mid T,M}=0.13047582645538838$, giving $\mu_{T,M}=0.5195182225621592$.

\paragraph{Single-readout split and overlap class.}
The single-readout fractions separate the three organisms into different measured regimes.  In $\mathrm{E.\ coli}$ the translation-only fraction is $0.01975521340963268$ while the stability-only fraction is $0.23052056324898387$, so the joint reduction is mostly carried by the mRNA-stability readout.  In human the translation-only fraction is $0.00549490864190343$ and the stability-only fraction is $0.15314679870183337$, again giving a stability-dominated reduction.  In yeast the translation-only fraction is $0.4697188319693239$ and the stability-only fraction is $0.0626618595344251$, so the same construction identifies a translation-dominated reduction.

The overlap diagnostic classifies all three organisms as approximately independent at the fixed descriptive tolerance $0.05$.  The joint-minus-single-sum values are $-0.009808817995950458$ for $\mathrm{E.\ coli}$, $0.010265413655496769$ for human, and $-0.012862468941589822$ for yeast.  The corresponding single-mediator sums are $0.25027577665861656$, $0.1586417073437368$, and $0.532380691503749$.  Each organism has deterministic pair-permutation right-tail value $0.000999000999000999$ for the joint $T,M$ reduction statistic, using the fixed seed family recorded for the organism-specific joint-mediator permutation.

\paragraph{Relation to measured translation survival and direct abundance survival.}
The same coordinate family has measured-TE survival on four organisms in the companion measured-TE table: $R^2_{QT,measured}=0.009394266754822262$ for $\mathrm{E.\ coli}$, $0.018837911344127926$ for human, $0.13681931343730436$ for yeast, and $0.04034217614654355$ for zebrafish, with nine surviving coordinates in each reported row.  Direct protein-omics survival is also positive on the broad PAXdb abundance panel: for example $R^2_{QP}=0.10767526677985896$ in $\mathrm{E.\ coli}$, $0.04454565118019448$ in human on the broader abundance-only join, $0.29837624994140827$ in yeast, and positive entries across all fifteen listed organisms.  The present finite surface is stricter than either one-readout table because it asks how much of the direct $Q\to P$ association remains after measured translation efficiency and mRNA stability are both present on the same rows.

A separate yeast readout-dictionary certificate reports $n_{join}=1800$ drawn from $4758$ available joined rows, $5$ folds, permutation count $120$, description-length penalty $\lambda_{DL}=0.01$, join threshold $\rho_{join}=0.05$, raw best coverage $0.2745002077400602$, selection-aware null $95\%$ value $0.029149007242956115$, and certified coverage $C^*=0.24538211439730545$.  That companion statistic is consistent with the joint-conditioning picture: measured readouts can account for a nonzero but incomplete part of the residual abundance signal.

\paragraph{Readback.}
The ready readback is a bounded variance-partition claim.  On the three computed organisms, the direct residual $B^*_{Q6}$ to protein-abundance association is reduced after conditioning on measured translation efficiency and mRNA stability.  The reduction is largest in yeast, intermediate in $\mathrm{E.\ coli}$, and smaller but still positive in human.  The dominant mediator differs by organism: yeast is translation-heavy, while $\mathrm{E.\ coli}$ and human are stability-heavy.  The overlap class is approximately independent in all three rows, so the finite matrix does not require a strong interaction term between measured TE and mRNA stability to describe the observed reduction.

This is not a causal pathway result.  The abundance, TE, and stability measurements come from organism-specific data sources and conditions; they are observational, noisy, and not perturbation-rescue experiments.  The calculation does not establish translation as a mechanism, and it does not imply folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, phenotype, evolutionary direction, or a universal biological rule.  Those stronger readings require their own matched contacts and their own finite statistics.  Here the only admitted conclusion is that, on the joined rows listed above, conditioning on measured TE and mRNA stability removes a quantified fraction of the $B^*_{Q6}$ residual association with protein abundance.
