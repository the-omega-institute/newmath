\subsection{Joint measured-TE and mRNA-stability conditioning for the $B^*_{Q6}$ abundance residual}\label{sec:bioreality-q6-joint-te-stability-mediation}\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite panel.}
The finite surface consists of three organism-level four-way joins in which the same coding-sequence rows carry $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinates, measured translation-efficiency readouts, mRNA-stability or abundance-control readouts, and measured protein abundance.  The three computed organisms are \emph{Escherichia coli} K12 MG1655, \emph{Homo sapiens}, and \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}.  The active $Q$ block has nine residual coordinates: $K\_AAA$, $Arg\_AGR$, $Ile\_AUA$, $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$, $Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG$, $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$, $Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG$, $Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY$, and $f3\_stress$.  The common control design has $24$ columns before rank reduction and includes an intercept, $\log$ CDS length, the twenty amino-acid composition proportions, GC3, and the M-density baseline on the union support of the nine projected $B^*_{Q6}$ vectors.  In the three active joins the residualized control rank is $23$, the residualized $Q$ rank is $9$, the residualized measured-TE rank is $1$, the residualized mRNA-stability rank is $1$, and the joint measured-TE plus stability rank is $2$.

\paragraph{Joined rows and contact quality.}
The four-way joins are non-vacuous and are not rank-degenerate.  \emph{E. coli} joins $2517$ rows, with reported CDS join hit rate $0.9978649586335735$ and TE join hit rate $0.9133458143888741$; its measured-TE readout is mean CDS footprint wiggle coverage divided by mean CDS mRNA wiggle coverage from the Li, Burkhardt, Gross, and Weissman 2014 random-fragment-control ribosome-profiling study, while mRNA stability uses $2947$ valid half-life records at growth rate $0.40\,h^{-1}$.  Human joins $11322$ rows, with reported CDS join hit rate $0.9779294769799313$ and TE join hit rate $0.6064136881331024$; its measured-TE readout comes from RPFdb processed GSE56887 CDS translation-efficiency rows, and its mRNA-control table reports $19480$ valid consensus records.  Yeast joins $4759$ rows, with reported CDS join hit rate $0.9156038419146592$ and TE join hit rate $0.8818572656921754$; its measured-TE readout is the Ingolia et al. 2009 rich-medium footprint density divided by mRNA density, and its mRNA-stability side contributes $5379$ valid half-life records.  These counts are large enough for the declared residual ranks: residual degrees of freedom after $Z$ are $2494$ in \emph{E. coli}, $11299$ in human, and $4736$ in yeast.

\paragraph{Variance decomposition.}
For each organism, let $P_e$ be the $Z$-residualized log protein-abundance vector, $Q_e$ the $Z$-residualized nine-coordinate codon-residual matrix, $T_e$ the $Z$-residualized measured-TE vector, and $M_e$ the $Z$-residualized mRNA-stability vector.  The reported quantities are the direct $Q$-to-protein abundance fit and the incremental $Q$ contribution after conditioning on $T_e$, $M_e$, or both:
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{QP} &= R^2(P_e \sim Q_e),\\
R^2_{QP\mid T} &= \Delta R^2(P_e \sim Q_e \mid T_e),\\
R^2_{QP\mid M} &= \Delta R^2(P_e \sim Q_e \mid M_e),\\
R^2_{QP\mid T,M} &= \Delta R^2(P_e \sim Q_e \mid T_e,M_e),\\
\mu_{T,M} &= \frac{R^2_{QP}-R^2_{QP\mid T,M}}{R^2_{QP}}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The computed values are organism-specific rather than universal.  In \emph{E. coli}, $R^2_{QP}=0.1181743523064932$, $R^2_{QP\mid T}=0.11583979275713331$, $R^2_{QP\mid M}=0.09093273405121653$, and $R^2_{QP\mid T,M}=0.08975732521542036$, giving joint mediated fraction $0.2404669586626661$.  In human, $R^2_{QP}=0.04274178488511215$, $R^2_{QP\mid T}=0.04250692268197657$, $R^2_{QP\mid M}=0.036196017359154814$, and $R^2_{QP\mid T,M}=0.0355223930537993$, giving joint mediated fraction $0.16890712099923358$.  In yeast, $R^2_{QP}=0.2715520808117803$, $R^2_{QP\mid T}=0.1439989545940314$, $R^2_{QP\mid M}=0.25453612246767165$, and $R^2_{QP\mid T,M}=0.13047582645538838$, giving joint mediated fraction $0.5195182225621592$.

\paragraph{Single-mediator split.}
The split is asymmetric across organisms.  In \emph{E. coli}, the TE-only fraction is $0.01975521340963268$ and the stability-only fraction is $0.23052056324898387$; thus the attenuation of the direct $Q\to P$ association is mostly carried by the mRNA-stability readout in this panel.  In human, the TE-only fraction is $0.00549490864190343$ and the stability-only fraction is $0.15314679870183337$, again making the stability side the dominant single mediator.  In yeast, the TE-only fraction is $0.4697188319693239$ and the stability-only fraction is $0.0626618595344251$, so the measured-TE side carries most of the attenuation.  The pair-permutation right-tail value is $0.000999000999000999$ in all three organisms under the deterministic pair-permutation design, so the joint fractions are not treated as row-order artifacts.

\paragraph{Overlap classification.}
The overlap diagnostic compares the joint mediated fraction to the sum of the single-mediator fractions with fixed descriptive tolerance $0.05$.  The three organisms all fall into the approximately independent overlap class.  In \emph{E. coli}, the single-mediator sum is $0.25027577665861656$ and the joint-minus-sum value is $-0.009808817995950458$.  In human, the single-mediator sum is $0.1586417073437368$ and the joint-minus-sum value is $0.010265413655496769$.  In yeast, the single-mediator sum is $0.532380691503749$ and the joint-minus-sum value is $-0.012862468941589822$.  These signed gaps are small relative to the declared tolerance, so the finite readback is not a strong redundancy or suppression claim; it is a descriptive statement that measured TE and mRNA stability remove largely distinct portions of the direct $Q\to P$ variance within each joined surface.

\paragraph{Relation to measured-TE survival and protein abundance.}
The same coordinate family has bounded measured-TE survival in a broader measured-ribo-seq panel: $R^2_{QT,meas}=0.009394266754822262$ for \emph{E. coli}, $0.018837911344127926$ for human, $0.13681931343730436$ for yeast, and $0.04034217614654355$ for zebrafish, each with all nine coordinates surviving the positive partial-$R^2$ threshold.  The direct protein-omics surface is also nonzero in the broader PAXdb abundance panel, with $R^2_{QP}=0.10767526677985896$ for \emph{E. coli}, $0.04454565118019448$ for human, and $0.29837624994140827$ for yeast among the reported organism-wide direct abundance fits.  A measured-TE-only mediation computation gives mediated fractions $0.04091158340950673$ for \emph{E. coli}, $0.0027966485036786744$ for human, and $0.4793069656345167$ for yeast, with zebrafish clipped to $0$ from raw suppressive value $-0.04801486048721136$.  The present joint TE-plus-stability chapter therefore sharpens the direct protein-omics association: yeast is the case where measured TE carries a large share, while \emph{E. coli} and human require the mRNA-stability axis to explain a substantial portion of the abundance residual.

\paragraph{Compression and residual remainder.}
A separate yeast readout-dictionary certificate gives a selection-aware partial reduction benchmark for the same broad target family.  On a yeast join with $n=1800$ sampled from $4758$ available rows, fold count $5$, $\lambda_{DL}=0.01$, $\rho_{join}=0.05$, and $120$ permutations, the best selected readout family $\{\mathrm{measured\_te},\mathrm{mrna\_stability},\mathrm{turnover}\}$ has $C^*=0.24538211439730545$, raw best coverage $0.2745002077400602$, selection-aware null $95$th percentile $0.029149007242956115$, and unexplained fraction $0.7546178856026946$.  This is consistent with the joint-mediation picture: measured TE and mRNA stability are real finite readouts for part of the abundance residual, but they do not exhaust the $B^*_{Q6}$ association with protein abundance.

\paragraph{Local verdict and limits.}
The finite-row verdict is that the claim is ready as a statistical variance-partition record.  The panel supports a non-vacuous direct $B^*_{Q6}$ abundance residual, positive measured-TE contact in the relevant coordinate family, and organism-specific attenuation after conditioning on measured TE, mRNA stability, or both.  The strongest TE-mediated case is yeast, where the joint fraction is $0.5195182225621592$ and the TE-only fraction is $0.4697188319693239$; the stronger stability-mediated cases are \emph{E. coli} and human, with stability-only fractions $0.23052056324898387$ and $0.15314679870183337$.  The claim must remain at the level of observational readout decomposition.  The data do not establish causation, temporal ordering, a molecular mechanism, universal biology, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, phenotype realization, or a general translation law.  A stronger statement would require a separate layer-matched contact, ideally perturbational or condition-matched, that supplies the missing biological operation rather than only the cross-sectional abundance, measured-TE, and mRNA-stability rows used here.
