\subsection{Joint Translation-Efficiency and Stability Mediation for $B^*_{Q6}$ Abundance Residuals}
\label{sec:bioreality-q6-joint-te-stability-mediation}

\paragraph{Finite surface.}
This record tests a finite cross-layer surface in which nine $B^*_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates are read against matched protein abundance, measured translation efficiency, and mRNA-stability rows. The coordinate block is
$K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $Leu_{CUN\,vs\,UUR}$, $Leu_{UUA\,vs\,UUG}$, $Ser_{UCR\,vs\,AGY}$, $Ser_{UCA\,vs\,UCG}$, $Thr_{ACR\,vs\,ACY}$, and $f3\_stress$. Each organism is first residualized against the same control family: intercept, $\log(\mathrm{CDS\ length})$, the twenty amino-acid composition proportions, $GC3$, and the $M$-density baseline given by the fraction of sense codons lying on the union support of the nine projected $B^*_{Q6}$ vectors. The resulting finite design has rank $23$ for the control block and rank $9$ for the residualized coordinate block in each computed organism. The computation covers three organisms, not a phylogenetic law: \emph{Escherichia coli} K12 MG1655, \emph{Homo sapiens}, and \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}.

\paragraph{Matched rows and readout construction.}
The four-way join is concrete in each organism. For \emph{E. coli}, the CDS hit rate is $0.9978649586335735$, the TE/mRNA join hit rate is $0.9133458143888741$, and the finite panel contains $2517$ joined rows after intersecting CDS, protein abundance, measured TE, and mRNA half-life. The TE surface uses Li, Burkhardt, Gross, and Weissman 2014 Cell data, with TE defined as mean CDS footprint coverage divided by mean CDS mRNA coverage on the annotated coding strand. For human, the CDS hit rate is $0.9779294769799313$, the TE/mRNA join hit rate is $0.6064136881331024$, and the joined panel contains $11322$ rows; the TE values come from the RPFdb processed GSE56887 surface, averaged over positive TE columns for each Ensembl gene. For yeast, the CDS hit rate is $0.9156038419146592$, the TE/mRNA join hit rate is $0.8818572656921754$, and the joined panel contains $4759$ rows; the TE readout is the Ingolia et al. 2009 rich-medium footprint density divided by matched mRNA density. The mRNA-stability mediator is represented by the matched half-life or consensus-stability route available for the same joined units.

\paragraph{Variance partition.}
Let $Q_e$ denote the residualized nine-dimensional coordinate matrix, $P_e$ the residualized protein-abundance readout, $T_e$ the residualized measured-TE readout, and $M_e$ the residualized mRNA-stability readout. The reported quantities are finite least-squares increments:
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{QP} &= R^2(P_e \sim Q_e),\\
R^2_{QP\mid T} &= \Delta R^2(P_e \sim Q_e \mid T_e),\\
R^2_{QP\mid M} &= \Delta R^2(P_e \sim Q_e \mid M_e),\\
R^2_{QP\mid T,M} &= \Delta R^2(P_e \sim Q_e \mid T_e,M_e),\\
\mu_{T,M} &= \frac{R^2_{QP}-R^2_{QP\mid T,M}}{R^2_{QP}}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The $R^2$ list is organism-specific. In \emph{E. coli},
$R^2_{QP}=0.1181743523064932$, $R^2_{QP\mid T}=0.11583979275713331$, $R^2_{QP\mid M}=0.09093273405121653$, and $R^2_{QP\mid T,M}=0.08975732521542036$. In human,
$R^2_{QP}=0.04274178488511215$, $R^2_{QP\mid T}=0.04250692268197657$, $R^2_{QP\mid M}=0.036196017359154814$, and $R^2_{QP\mid T,M}=0.0355223930537993$. In yeast,
$R^2_{QP}=0.2715520808117803$, $R^2_{QP\mid T}=0.1439989545940314$, $R^2_{QP\mid M}=0.25453612246767165$, and $R^2_{QP\mid T,M}=0.13047582645538838$.

\paragraph{Mediator fractions and overlap class.}
The finite mediation fractions separate the translation-efficiency component, the mRNA-stability component, and the joint removal by both mediators. In \emph{E. coli}, the TE-only fraction is $0.01975521340963268$, the stability-only fraction is $0.23052056324898387$, and the joint fraction is $0.2404669586626661$. In human, the corresponding list is $0.00549490864190343$, $0.15314679870183337$, and $0.16890712099923358$. In yeast, it is $0.4697188319693239$, $0.0626618595344251$, and $0.5195182225621592$. The overlap classifier is `approximately\_independent' in all three organisms: the joint-minus-single-sum value is $-0.009808817995950458$ in \emph{E. coli}, $0.010265413655496769$ in human, and $-0.012862468941589822$ in yeast, each within the fixed descriptive tolerance $0.05$. The right-tail permutation value is $0.000999000999000999$ for each organism, using deterministic pair permutation seeds tied to the organism and the joint mediator test.

\paragraph{Rank and energy diagnostics.}
The row-level regressions are not rank-null artifacts. After the control residualization, \emph{E. coli} has residual degrees of freedom $2494$, $p$-residual energy $2323.813305213946$, $t$-residual energy $672.36799431777$, and $m$-residual energy $198.6753916649337$. Its coordinate energy on $P$ is $274.6151322248693$, while the direct $Q$ improvement after conditioning on both $T$ and $M$ is $208.57926657600905$. Human has residual degrees of freedom $11299$, $p$-residual energy $12615.307912483846$, $t$-residual energy $1866.9940287020145$, $m$-residual energy $647.920297431553$, coordinate energy on $P$ equal to $539.2007770548378$, and direct $Q$ improvement after both mediators equal to $448.1259261619555$. Yeast has residual degrees of freedom $4736$, $p$-residual energy $1724.6664873071313$, $t$-residual energy $645.3764418081507$, $m$-residual energy $194.99141167236996$, coordinate energy on $P$ equal to $468.33677333459536$, and direct $Q$ improvement after both mediators equal to $225.02728529130957$. The mediator ranks are $\operatorname{rank}(T)=1$, $\operatorname{rank}(M)=1$, and $\operatorname{rank}(T,M)=2$ in all three panels; the augmented ranks are $10$, $10$, and $11$ for $Q+T$, $Q+M$, and $Q+T+M$.

\paragraph{Relation to the readout dictionary.}
A related yeast dictionary run gives a useful scale for the same family of residuals. On $1800$ joined yeast rows drawn from $4758$ available rows, with five folds, $\lambda_{DL}=0.01$, $\rho_{join}=0.05$, and $120$ deterministic permutations, the best measured-readout dictionary of size at most three covers $C^*=0.24538211439730545$ of the source signal after selection correction. The raw best coverage over the candidate family is $0.2745002077400602$, the selection-aware null $95$ percent value is $0.029149007242956115$, and the unexplained fraction remains $0.7546178856026946$. This supports the local interpretation used here: measured TE and mRNA stability absorb a real finite part of the $B^*_{Q6}$ abundance residual surface, but they do not exhaust it.

\paragraph{Verdict.}
The finite evidence supports a ready statistical statement: in the three computed organism panels, the $B^*_{Q6}$ abundance association remains positive, and measured TE plus mRNA stability remove a nonzero fraction of the direct coordinate-to-protein-abundance signal. The pattern is strongest for measured TE in yeast, strongest for mRNA stability in \emph{E. coli} and human, and approximately additive between the two mediators under the declared tolerance. This is a variance-partition result on finite joined rows. It is not a causal pathway proof, not a universal biological law, and not a statement about folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, localization, phenotype, or perturbation rescue. The only translation-layer content licensed here is the use of the measured TE readout already present in the joined matrices; any stronger translation mechanism, structural claim, functional claim, or universal statement would require a separate layer-matched contact and a new finite statistic.
