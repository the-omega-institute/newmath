\subsection{Joint measured-TE and mRNA-stability attenuation of the B$^*_{Q6}$ abundance residual}\label{sec:bioreality-q6-joint-te-stability-mediation}\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite surface.}
This record tests a rank-powered finite matrix whose rows are coding-sequence units and whose columns are the nine B$^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinates
$K\_AAA$, $Arg\_AGR$, $Ile\_AUA$, $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$, $Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG$, $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$, $Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG$, $Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY$, and $f3\_stress$.  The response $P$ is residualized log$_{10}$ protein abundance, $T$ is a measured ribosome-profiling translation-efficiency readout, and $M$ is an mRNA-stability or half-life readout.  The common covariate block $Z$ contains an intercept, log CDS length, twenty amino-acid composition proportions, GC3, and an M-density baseline on the union support of the nine projected coordinates.  The computation is a variance-partition test, not a causal experiment: it asks how much of the observed $Q \to P$ association is attenuated after adding $T$, after adding $M$, and after adding both.

\paragraph{Joined rows and rank.}
Three organisms passed the four-way join and rank checks.  In \textit{Escherichia coli} K-12 MG1655, the joined panel has $n=2517$ rows, with reported CDS hit rate $0.9978649586335735$ and measured-TE join hit rate $0.9133458143888741$; the matched mRNA table contributes $2720$ primary-key rows and the TE table contributes $3415$ protein-id rows.  In human, the joined panel has $n=11322$ rows, with reported CDS hit rate $0.9779294769799313$ and measured-TE join hit rate $0.6064136881331024$; the mRNA side contributes $16160$ primary-key rows plus $17$ symbol-fallback rows, and the TE side contributes $11554$ protein-id rows plus $8$ gene-key rows.  In budding yeast, the joined panel has $n=4759$ rows, with reported CDS hit rate $0.9156038419146592$ and measured-TE join hit rate $0.8818572656921754$; the mRNA side contributes $5085$ systematic-name rows and the TE side contributes $5128$ protein-id rows.  All three panels have $rank(Z)=23$, $rank(Q_Z)=9$, $rank(T_Z)=1$, $rank(M_Z)=1$, and $rank(Q,T,M\mid Z)=11$, so the attenuation estimates are not rank-saturated all-one artifacts.

\paragraph{R$^2$ decomposition.}
The controlled direct abundance association is positive in all three finite panels.  The fitted quantities are
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{QP}(E.\ coli) &= 0.1181743523064932, & R^2_{QP\mid T,M}(E.\ coli) &= 0.08975732521542036,\\
R^2_{QP}(human) &= 0.04274178488511215, & R^2_{QP\mid T,M}(human) &= 0.0355223930537993,\\
R^2_{QP}(yeast) &= 0.2715520808117803, & R^2_{QP\mid T,M}(yeast) &= 0.13047582645538838.
\end{aligned}
$$
Single-condition values separate translation-efficiency attenuation from mRNA-stability attenuation.  For \textit{E. coli}, $R^2_{QP\mid T}=0.11583979275713331$ and $R^2_{QP\mid M}=0.09093273405121653$.  For human, $R^2_{QP\mid T}=0.04250692268197657$ and $R^2_{QP\mid M}=0.036196017359154814$.  For yeast, $R^2_{QP\mid T}=0.1439989545940314$ and $R^2_{QP\mid M}=0.25453612246767165$.  Thus yeast is dominated by measured-TE attenuation, whereas the bacterial and human panels are dominated by mRNA-stability attenuation.

\paragraph{Mediation fractions.}
Using the registered scalar
$$
\begin{aligned}
\operatorname{joint\_fraction} = \frac{R^2_{QP}-R^2_{QP\mid T,M}}{R^2_{QP}},
\end{aligned}
$$
the joint attenuation fractions are $0.2404669586626661$ for \textit{E. coli}, $0.16890712099923358$ for human, and $0.5195182225621592$ for yeast.  The corresponding single-readout fractions are $(TE,M)=(0.01975521340963268,0.23052056324898387)$ for \textit{E. coli}, $(0.00549490864190343,0.15314679870183337)$ for human, and $(0.4697188319693239,0.0626618595344251)$ for yeast.  The overlap classifier is approximately independent in all three organisms: the joint-minus-single-sum values are $-0.009808817995950458$, $0.010265413655496769$, and $-0.012862468941589822$, each within the fixed descriptive tolerance $0.05$.  The right-tail permutation value is $0.000999000999000999$ in each organism under the deterministic paired $T,M$ permutation design.

\paragraph{Readout consistency.}
The same coordinate family also survives the measured-TE contact in four organism panels: $R^2_{QT,meas}=0.009394266754822262$ in \textit{E. coli}, $0.018837911344127926$ in human, $0.13681931343730436$ in yeast, and $0.04034217614654355$ in zebrafish, with all nine coordinates counted as surviving by the positive-threshold rule.  Direct protein-omics survival is broader across the PAXdb panels, including $R^2_{QP}=0.10767526677985896$ for \textit{E. coli}, $0.04454565118019448$ for human, and $0.29837624994140827$ for yeast in the direct abundance screen.  A measured-TE abundance decomposition gives measured mediated fractions $0.04091158340950673$ for \textit{E. coli}, $0.0027966485036786744$ for human, $0.4793069656345167$ for yeast, and $0.0$ for zebrafish after clipping the suppressive raw value.  A separate yeast readout-dictionary certificate with $\lambda_{DL}=0.01$ reports coverage $C^*=0.24538211439730545$ over the candidate family, with selection-aware null $95\%$ level $0.029149007242956115$ and unexplained fraction $0.7546178856026946$; this supports partial reducibility without closing the residual.

\paragraph{Interpretation.}
The finite-row result is therefore a positive, organism-specific attenuation record.  B$^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinates predict protein-abundance residuals after composition and length controls, and a measurable part of that prediction is removed by the pair consisting of measured TE and mRNA stability.  The pattern is not uniform: yeast assigns most attenuation to measured TE, while \textit{E. coli} and human assign most attenuation to mRNA stability.  The approximately independent overlap class says that, at the registered tolerance, the joint fraction behaves close to the sum of the two single-readout fractions rather than as a strong redundancy or antagonism module.

\paragraph{Local no-promotion boundary.}
This chapter does not claim a causal translation pathway, ribosome mechanism, folding effect, physical admissibility, molecular function, phenotype, evolutionary direction, or universal biological law.  The abundance, TE, and mRNA-stability sources are observational, per-organism, and drawn from separate measurement contexts; the mediation numbers are finite variance partitions.  A stronger biological statement would require a separate layer-matched contact, such as perturbation, rescue, structural assay, localization assay, stability assay, functional assay, or controlled phenotype readout, with the same coordinate rows and its own finite statistic.
