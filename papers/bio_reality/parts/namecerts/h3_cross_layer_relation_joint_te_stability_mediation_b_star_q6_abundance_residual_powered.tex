\subsection{Joint measured-TE and mRNA-stability attenuation of the $B^*_{Q6}$ abundance residual}\label{sec:bioreality-q6-joint-te-stability-mediation}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite surface.}
The row object is a matched finite matrix over nine $B^*_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates, one measured translation-efficiency readout $T$, one mRNA-stability readout $M$, and a measured protein-abundance readout $P$ on the same coding-sequence units whenever the join exists.  The coordinate list is
$K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $Leu_{CUN\_vs\_UUR}$, $Leu_{UUA\_vs\_UUG}$, $Ser_{UCR\_vs\_AGY}$, $Ser_{UCA\_vs\_UCG}$, $Thr_{ACR\_vs\_ACY}$, and $f3\_stress$.  Each organism first removes the same control family $Z$: intercept, $\log(\mathrm{CDS\ length})$, the twenty amino-acid composition proportions from real CDS codon counts, GC3, and the M-density baseline defined on the union support of the nine projected coordinates.  After this removal, the experiment fits $Q_e$, $T_e$, $M_e$, and $(T_e,M_e)$ against the residual protein abundance $P_e$.

\paragraph{Definition of the reported fractions.}
For each organism, $R^2_{QP}$ denotes the finite least-squares fraction of residual protein-abundance energy explained by the nine-dimensional residual coordinate block $Q_e$.  The conditioned quantities $R^2_{QP\mid T}$, $R^2_{QP\mid M}$, and $R^2_{QP\mid T,M}$ are the incremental explanatory fractions of $Q_e$ after the measured-TE column, the mRNA-stability column, or both columns have already been included.  The joint attenuation fraction is therefore read as
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mu_{T,M}(Q,P)
  &= \frac{R^2_{QP}-R^2_{QP\mid T,M}}{R^2_{QP}},\\
\mu_T(Q,P)
  &= \frac{R^2_{QP}-R^2_{QP\mid T}}{R^2_{QP}},\\
\mu_M(Q,P)
  &= \frac{R^2_{QP}-R^2_{QP\mid M}}{R^2_{QP}}.
\end{aligned}
$$
These are variance-partition summaries on finite rows, not causal effects.  The row calculation deliberately asks whether the direct $Q\to P$ association becomes smaller after adding measured TE and RNA stability; it does not claim that the molecules have been intervened on.

\paragraph{Readback.}
The four-case interpretation is local to the available rows.  A coordinate or coordinate block with neither translation-readout survival nor abundance survival is a null readback.  A block with translation survival but no downstream abundance signal remains translation-layer only.  A block with abundance survival but no surviving translation step is direct or confounded protein-omics prediction.  A block is allowed to enter the mediated class only when the $Q\to T$ surface is positive and conditioning on $T$, or on $(T,M)$ in this chapter, reduces the $Q\to P$ surface.  The present joint panel supports the last, attenuated, finite-row reading at the block level: measured TE and mRNA stability together reduce the direct abundance association in all three organisms, with the largest joint fraction in yeast, an intermediate fraction in \textit{E. coli}, and the smallest but still positive fraction in human.

\paragraph{Consistency with companion finite checks.}
The direct protein-omics surface supplies the larger background: across fifteen organisms, every listed organism has all nine coordinates surviving against measured PAXdb protein abundance after the same family of controls, with $R^2_{QP}$ ranging from $0.019256877907572044$ in \textit{Halobacterium salinarum} to $0.29837624994140827$ in yeast.  The measured ribosome-TE surface separately reports positive $S^{QT,\mathrm{meas}}$ in four organisms: $0.009394266754822262$ for \textit{E. coli}, $0.018837911344127926$ for human, $0.04034217614654355$ for zebrafish, and $0.13681931343730436$ for yeast, with nine surviving coordinates in each case.  The measured-TE abundance decomposition gives measured mediated fractions $0.04091158340950673$ in \textit{E. coli}, $0.0027966485036786744$ in human, and $0.4793069656345167$ in yeast, while zebrafish is truncated to $0.0$ from a raw suppressive value of $-0.04801486048721136$.  A separate yeast reduction-family audit at $\lambda_{DL}=0.01$ reports selected coverage $C^*=0.24538211439730545$ over $n=1800$ rows and leaves unexplained fraction $0.7546178856026946$; this is compatible with the joint TE/stability result because attenuation is partial rather than exhaustive.  The yeast $f3\_stress$ dwell audit also gives a positive held-out increment $0.01856473944057712$ over a fixed permutation $95\%$ boundary $0.0013115362189692937$, with an approximate $f3$--dwell correlation $0.1615441450901653$ and $p\approx 7.873916057196935\times 10^{-9}$.

\paragraph{Boundary.}
This chapter records a finite observational decomposition of a residual protein-abundance association.  It does not license a causal translation pathway, a general translation law, a folded-structure claim, physical admissibility, molecular function, phenotype realization, evolutionary direction, or universality.  The measured TE and mRNA-stability columns are noisy, condition-specific readouts, and the abundance, TE, and half-life measurements come from organism-level study joins that need not share perturbation timing or platform.  Any stronger statement about translation dynamics, folding, function, mechanism, or universal biology requires a separate layer-matched contact and a new finite statistic at that layer.
