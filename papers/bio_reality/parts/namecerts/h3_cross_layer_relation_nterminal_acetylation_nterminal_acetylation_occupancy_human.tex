It does not itself provide codon-indexed ribosome-footprint rows, matched mRNA denominators, wobble-weight matrices, or a computed $f_3$-stress translation-window statistic.

\paragraph{Local coordinate and admissible statistic.}
For a window $i$, the intended coordinate separates third-position topology from amino-acid composition before asking whether any translation readout remains.  The admissible comparison has the form
$$
\begin{aligned}
T_i &= \alpha + \beta S_i^{f_3} + \gamma^\top Z_i + \varepsilon_i,\\
Z_i &= (\text{amino-acid bin},\ \text{position bin},\ \mathrm{GC3},\ \text{length},\ \text{methionine density},\ \text{mRNA control},\ \text{coverage controls}),
\end{aligned}
$$
where $T_i$ is a matched position-resolved ribosome-occupancy or explicitly bounded translation-window residual, and $S_i^{f_3}$ is the residual third-position stress coordinate after amino-acid projection.  The coefficient $\beta$ is interpretable only inside rows where the wobble surface is asymmetric, for example where $w_c \neq w_{c^{f_3}}$, and only when the design matrix has adequate rank after the control columns are included.  If the same finite window lacks joined footprint occupancy, matched mRNA abundance, or wobble weights, then $S_i^{f_3}$ remains a sequence-coordinate descriptor and not a translation-layer measurement.

The acetylation result and the translation-window proposal occupy different layers. The chapter breaks if review eligibility, a contact name, modeled tAI or stAI weights, wobble asymmetry, $S^{QT}$ notation, or codon-window geometry is treated as measured position-resolved ribosome occupancy.  It also breaks if the human acetylation finite row is read as evidence for translation mechanism, folding, physical admissibility, localization, stability, function, phenotype, or universality.
