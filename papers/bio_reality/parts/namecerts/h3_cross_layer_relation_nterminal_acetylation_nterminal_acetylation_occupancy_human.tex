\subsection{Human N-terminal acetylation occupancy as a bounded cross-layer record}

\paragraph{Finite row under audit.}
The row named \texttt{h3.cross\_layer\_relation.nterminal\_acetylation.nterminal\_acetylation\_occupancy\_human} is kept as a finite BioReality record rather than as a broad biological law. Its checked scalar surface reports experiment run \texttt{90ea6b5f7fc7}, with $n=1426$ human rows and runtime $5.372$ seconds. The row records that the abundance-controlled flag is false, and its finite conservation readout is the statement that the frozen yeast rule transfers to human under the preregistered gates. This is enough to preserve a bounded occupancy comparison, but it is not enough to promote the row to a statement about translation dynamics, protein folding, physical admissibility, biological function, mechanism, or universality.

\paragraph{Coordinate actually being tested.}
The attached conjectural coordinate is not a direct assay of N-terminal acetyltransferase mechanism. It is a finite coding-sequence window table in which each row may carry an $f_3$-stress residual coordinate, amino-acid composition controls, a wobble-weight asymmetry surface, and a matched position-resolved ribosome-occupancy readout. The admissible row has the form
$$
\begin{aligned}
&(\mathrm{organism},\mathrm{CDS},\mathrm{window},s_{f_3},a_{\mathrm{wobble}},Z,T,\beta),
\end{aligned}
$$
where $s_{f_3}$ is the third-position synonymous residual after amino-acid projection, $a_{\mathrm{wobble}}$ records whether the attached wobble weights break the $f_3$ symmetry, $Z$ contains the amino-acid bin or position bin, GC3, length, M-density, codon-count, and mRNA controls, $T$ is the matched local ribosome-occupancy or translation-window residual, and $\beta$ is the break condition. The row is interpretable only at this local translation-readout boundary. Codon geometry alone, modeled tAI or stAI weights alone, and the availability of a review route do not create an observed ribosome-occupancy statistic.

\paragraph{Human occupancy fact and its limited force.}
For the human N-terminal acetylation occupancy record, the finite scalar evidence is precise but narrow: $1426$ rows entered the checked human panel, the computation completed in $5.372$ seconds, and the stored abundance-control flag is false. The phrase that the frozen yeast rule transfers to human under preregistered gates is therefore read as a rule-survival observation at the occupancy layer, not as a causal explanation of translation, abundance, localization, folding, or downstream phenotype. In particular, the absence of abundance control blocks any inference that the occupancy pattern is independent of protein amount or expression regime. The correct local reading is that a finite human occupancy panel preserves the named rule under its declared gates, while leaving abundance-conditioned and mechanism-conditioned questions outside the row.

\paragraph{Translation-window boundary.}
The $f_3$-stress component is even more restricted. It becomes a candidate dynamical covariate only when three attachments are present on the same finite row: ordered CDS coordinates with codon counts, bounded wobble weights with $w_c \neq w_{c^{f_3}}$ for the asymmetric context being tested, and a matched position-resolved ribosome-footprint or translation-window readout with mRNA abundance available as a control. The intended residual comparison may be written as
$$
\begin{aligned}
T_{\mathrm{window}} &= \alpha + \gamma s_{f_3} + \theta a_{\mathrm{wobble}} + \eta(s_{f_3}a_{\mathrm{wobble}}) + Z\delta + \varepsilon,
\end{aligned}
$$
with the admissible question limited to whether the $f_3$ term improves the bounded translation-window residual after the declared controls and rank checks. If the same-organism, same-CDS, same-window footprint matrix is absent, then $s_{f_3}$ remains a codon-topology residual and not a measured translation variable.

\paragraph{Local no-promotion rule.}
This chapter therefore admits the human N-terminal acetylation occupancy row as a finite, checked, human panel with $n=1426$, runtime $5.372$ seconds, no abundance control, and a recorded yeast-to-human gate-survival statement. It also admits the $f_3$-stress translation-window packet only as a request for a joined position-resolved translation statistic. No translation realization follows from third-position codon topology; no protein abundance follows from the occupancy row while abundance control is false; no folding, physical admissibility, localization, biological function, system phenotype, mechanism, or universal law follows without a separate layer-matched contact. A positive future row would have to join codon counts, wobble weights, matched mRNA abundance, local ribosome occupancy, coverage and mappability filters, rank sufficiency, and simpler-baseline ablations before reporting any bounded translation-window survival.
