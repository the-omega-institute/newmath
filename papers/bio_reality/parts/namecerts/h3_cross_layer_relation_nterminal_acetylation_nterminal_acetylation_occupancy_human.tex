\paragraph{Local object.}
The admissible object here is a finite table row, not a universal biological mechanism.  Each admissible row is keyed by organism, coding sequence or protein identifier, a bounded translation-window or protein-position context, the N-terminal acetylation occupancy readout, and the declared break condition.  The row may be compared with a proposed $f_3$-stress synonymous residual coordinate only after amino-acid composition, position or amino-acid bin, GC3, sequence length, methionine-density, wobble-weight asymmetry, and matched mRNA abundance have been attached as same-row controls.  Without those joins, the $f_3$ coordinate remains a codon-topology descriptor and not a measured translation-layer variable.

\paragraph{Admissible relation.}
The finite human result may be used to state that the N-terminal acetylation occupancy panel survived the preregistered human gate in the specific run above.  It may not be promoted into a claim that synonymous third-position structure explains protein abundance, folding, localization, stability, downstream function, physical admissibility, phenotype, mechanism, or universality.  The abundance flag is especially restrictive: since $A_{\mathrm{control}}=\mathrm{false}$, the chapter cannot separate occupancy from a matched abundance route inside this finite record.  The strongest local reading is therefore conditional:
$$
\begin{aligned}
&\text{human N-terminal occupancy row accepted by its run}\;\\
&\quad \not\Rightarrow\ \text{abundance-controlled protein-output law},\\
&\quad \not\Rightarrow\ \text{folding, stability, localization, function, mechanism, or universality}.
\end{aligned}
$$
This also keeps the $f_3$-stress proposal bounded.  A synonymous residual can enter the chapter only as a candidate covariate for a later matched translation-window statistic.  It is not allowed to stand in for ribosome occupancy, translation efficiency, protein abundance, or N-terminal acetylation state by itself.

\paragraph{Break condition.}
The local claim breaks if the text treats the review route, the contact names, a modeled tAI or stAI surface, a wobble-asymmetry flag, or a symbolic $f_3$ statistic as though it were already a joined same-organism and same-CDS measurement.  It also breaks if the human occupancy run is described as abundance-controlled, because the verified scalar says otherwise.  A valid extension would require a finite joined table with the human N-terminal occupancy row, matched mRNA abundance, position-resolved ribosome occupancy or another explicitly bounded translation readout, codon-window counts, wobble weights, amino-acid-bin controls, GC3, length, methionine-density, coverage, mappability, rank sufficiency, and a simpler-baseline ablation.  Until such a table is supplied, this chapter records a bounded positive human occupancy row and a separate request for translation-window evidence, not a completed cross-layer mechanism.
