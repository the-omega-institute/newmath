\subsection{Translation-conditioned RNA half-life and the bounded RBP bridge test}\label{subsec:bioreality-q6-rna-half-life-rbp-bridge}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite carrier.}
The record considered here is a finite matched transcript or coding-sequence table in which each row carries a synonymous residual coordinate label, an amino-acid quotient class, a translation readout, an RNA half-life readout, matched transcript-abundance controls, and the declared possibility of a local decay-factor RNA-binding-protein readout.  The intended positive relation is not merely that a high-coordinate row has a different RNA half-life from a low-coordinate row.  It is the stricter assertion that the RNA half-life residual remains visible after the translation boundary is attached, then follows an independently measured local occupancy change of a prespecified decay-factor RBP, disappears under loss of that RBP, returns under wild-type rescue, and fails under binding-defective or non-target rescue controls.

The finite evidence currently attached to this subject is not such a perturbation panel.  The directly indexed GLORI-style m6A stoichiometry run has experiment id $2e8583415e99$ and reports
$$
\begin{aligned}
  n &= 60000,\\
  \mathrm{distinct} &= 49668,\\
  \mathrm{coverage\_confound\_r} &= -0.135922814,\\
  \mathrm{cross\_condition\_concordant} &= \mathrm{true},\\
  \mathrm{runtime\_sec} &= 78.316.
\end{aligned}
$$
These numbers show that the finite table is large, nontrivially de-duplicated, and condition-concordant for the measured m6A stoichiometry surface, with a recorded coverage association that must remain visible as a nuisance scalar rather than be erased from the interpretation.  They do not by themselves provide same-window RNA half-life, same-row ribosome occupancy or translation efficiency, local decay-factor RBP occupancy, RBP loss, or rescue rows.

\paragraph{Coordinate contrast.}
Let $q_i$ denote the residual synonymous-coordinate label after quotienting by amino-acid identity, let $T_i$ denote the matched translation readout when present, let $H_i$ denote RNA half-life, and let $O_i$ denote measured local occupancy of the prespecified decay-factor RBP.  The permitted contrast is bounded by the conditional comparison
$$
\begin{aligned}
  \Delta_H
  &= E[H_i \mid q_i \in Q_{\mathrm{high}}, Z_i, T_i]
     - E[H_i \mid q_i \in Q_{\mathrm{low}}, Z_i, T_i],\\
  Z_i
  &= (\mathrm{transcription\ rate},\ \mathrm{local\ RNA\ structure},\ \mathrm{motif\ density},\
      \mathrm{GC/dinucleotide},\ \mathrm{codon\ pair},\ \mathrm{isoform},\
      \mathrm{abundance},\ \mathrm{batch},\ \mathrm{amino\ acid\ class}).
\end{aligned}
$$
A nonzero $\Delta_H$ would still be only an RNA-stability association unless it is accompanied by the local RBP bridge pattern.  The stricter bridge asks for the implication chain
$$
\begin{aligned}
  q_i \leadsto H_i
  \quad \text{only if} \quad
  q_i \leadsto O_i,\quad
  \mathrm{loss}(\mathrm{RBP}) \Rightarrow \Delta_H \to 0,\quad
  \mathrm{WT\ rescue} \Rightarrow \Delta_H \text{ returns},\\
  \mathrm{binding\ defective\ rescue} 
Rightarrow \Delta_H \text{ returns},\quad
  \mathrm{non\ target\ rescue} 
Rightarrow \Delta_H \text{ returns}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The present finite-row material does not instantiate this chain.  The chapter therefore records a precise bounded test rather than a positive RBP-mediated RNA-decay result.

The m6Am flanking-identity Crown-seq run, experiment id $eb689b7dc73c$, reports $n=31886$, $\mathrm{distinct}=19406$, $\mathrm{cross\_cell\_concordant}=\mathrm{true}$, $\mathrm{saturated\_ge\_0\_95\_fraction}=0.343223$, $\mathrm{lowtail\_lt\_0\_8\_n}=3230$, $\mathrm{variable\_subset\_n}=20942$, and $\mathrm{runtime\_sec}=324.086$.  Its recorded partial correlations include
$$
\begin{aligned}
  \rho_{\mathrm{full}} &= 0.268094,\\
  \rho_{\mathrm{lowtail}<0.8} &= 0.098666,\\
  \rho_{\mathrm{variable\ subset}} &= 0.168335,

$$
with a GC-matched null probability recorded as $p=0.000833$.  This adjacent example is useful only as a finite-row demonstration that RNA-modification tables can carry condition-concordant scalar structure under explicit controls.  It is not imported as evidence for the present m6A half-life/RBP bridge.

A separate RBP-binding contact, experiment id $b225890251c2$, records $n_{\mathrm{peaks\ total}}=900$, $\mathrm{cross\_rbp\_concordant}=3$, and $\mathrm{runtime\_sec}=223.648043$.  This gives an example of a finite RBP occupancy or binding surface in the broader corpus, but it is not the same-window decay-factor occupancy panel required here.  The distinction matters: an RBP-binding table in the repository and an m6A stoichiometry table in the repository do not together form a same-row mechanistic bridge unless the identifiers, windows, conditions, perturbations, and rescue contrasts are explicitly joined.

\paragraph{Null model.}
The operational null is row exchangeability of the high- and low-coordinate labels with respect to translation-conditioned RNA half-life after all declared controls are fixed.  In symbols, for any finite matched panel that lacks the required local RBP bridge rows, the admissible null statement is
$$
\begin{aligned}
  (q_i \in Q_{\mathrm{high}}) \perp H_i
  \mid
  (Z_i, T_i, \mathrm{matched\ abundance}, \mathrm{batch}),
\end{aligned}
$$
with the additional rule that a detected half-life difference is demoted to a bounded RNA-stability association whenever $O_i$ is absent, unchanged, not experimentally decoupled from coordinate architecture, or not tested through loss and rescue.  In this chapter the known GLORI finite rows support an m6A stoichiometry/exon-architecture surface with $60000$ rows and $49668$ distinct entries.  They do not support the stronger conclusion that synonymous residual coordinates control RNA decay through a decay-factor RBP.

\paragraph{Readout boundary.}
The attached half-life, mRNA abundance, and ribosome-profiling contacts specify possible readout surfaces, not a completed joined matrix.  RNA half-life alone does not certify translation realization.  A translation readout alone does not certify RNA decay mechanism.  Transcript abundance is a control denominator or covariate, not a half-life measurement.  A contact name for ribosome profiling is not a protein-output, folding, localization, physical-admissibility, function, phenotype, adaptation, causation, or universal-law measurement.  Likewise, codon-window geometry and residual-coordinate notation are mathematical labels on rows; they are not curated biological observations unless an external row supplies the corresponding measurement.

Accordingly, the chapter permits the following finite conclusion and no stronger one: the current evidence defines a bounded, auditable target for a translation-conditioned RNA half-life/RBP bridge, anchored by a large m6A stoichiometry table and by adjacent finite examples of RNA-modification and RBP-binding surfaces.  A positive bridge remains unavailable until a single same-organism, same-window panel supplies residual-coordinate labels, same-row translation readout, matched mRNA abundance, RNA half-life, measured decay-factor RBP occupancy, coordinate/occupancy decoupling, target-RBP loss, wild-type rescue, and binding-defective or non-target controls.  Without that joined panel, the admissible scientific statement is a controlled null boundary: no translation, protein output, folding, physical admissibility, biological function, mechanism, phenotype, adaptation, causation, or universality follows from the present rows.
