\subsection{GLORI m6A Stoichiometry as a Finite Exon-Architecture Readout}\label{subsec:bioreality-glori-m6a-exon-architecture}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite panel.}
The GLORI m6A record is treated here as a finite cross-layer observation surface, not as a mechanism claim.  The verified row panel contains $60000$ measured rows and $49668$ distinct measured sites for the claim identifier $h3.cross\_layer\_relation.rna\_m6a\_stoichiometry.m6a\_stoichiometry\_exon\_architecture\_glori$.  The recorded run id is $2e8583415e99$, with runtime $78.316$ seconds.  Its scalar audit reports cross-condition concordance as true and gives a coverage-confounding correlation of $-0.135922814$.  Thus the finite observation is not merely that an m6A site exists, but that a large site panel retains a reproducible stoichiometry signal while the observed coverage association is small in magnitude and negative:
$$
\begin{aligned}
N_{\mathrm{rows}} &= 60000,\\
N_{\mathrm{distinct}} &= 49668,\\
r_{\mathrm{coverage}} &= -0.135922814,\\
C_{\mathrm{condition}} &= \mathrm{true}.
\end{aligned}
$$
These numbers license a bounded statement about measured m6A stoichiometry against exon-level genomic context in the GLORI-derived panel.  They do not by themselves identify an RNA decay factor, a translation state, a protein output, or a causal biochemical route.

\paragraph{Coordinate distinction.}
The chapter records a finite distinction between high-coordinate and low-coordinate synonymous residual labels after amino-acid quotienting, but that distinction is used only as an indexing discipline for matched rows.  A row is admissible for this chapter only when its transcript or CDS identifier, editable RNA window, m6A stoichiometry readout, exon-architecture annotation, and declared controls can be joined without changing the biological subject of the row.  The verified GLORI panel supplies the m6A stoichiometry and exon-architecture side of this finite relation.  It does not supply same-window measured decay-factor RBP occupancy, target-RBP loss, wild-type rescue, or binding-defective rescue.  Therefore the strongest local conclusion is an exon-architecture-associated m6A stoichiometry readout, not an RBP-mediated RNA half-life bridge.

\paragraph{Methylation-neighbor comparison.}
The m6Am Crown-seq neighbor record provides a useful calibration of what a stronger methylation-specific finite row can look like without importing its conclusion into the GLORI m6A claim.  For $h3.cross\_layer\_relation.rna\_m6am\_methylation.m6am\_flanking\_identity\_stoichiometry\_crownseq$, the verified panel has $31886$ rows, $19406$ distinct sites, $20942$ rows in the variable subset, $3230$ rows in the low-tail subset below $0.8$, and a saturated fraction $0.343223$ at threshold at least $0.95$.  Its run id is $eb689b7dc73c$, and cross-cell concordance is true.  The recorded scalar checks include $p = 0.000833$, partial correlation $0.268094$ for the full panel, $0.168335$ for the variable subset, and $0.098666$ for the low-tail subset.  These finite values show that nearby RNA methylation chapters may carry explicit flank-identity and stoichiometry statistics, but they remain separate row systems.  They are cited here only to make the GLORI boundary sharper: the m6A GLORI panel has its own $60000$-row surface and its own coverage-confounding scalar, while the m6Am Crown-seq panel has different rows, different chemistry, and different flank tests.

\paragraph{Null model.}
The local null is row exchangeability after the declared controls.  Once amino-acid sequence, transcription rate, local RNA structure, motif density, GC and dinucleotide composition, codon-pair context, splice or isoform state, transcript abundance, batch, and same-row translation readout are held fixed, high-coordinate and low-coordinate labels are not allowed to differ in RNA half-life merely by interpretive pressure.  A measured m6A stoichiometry or exon-architecture association may remain a finite RNA modification association, but it is not promoted to a translation-conditioned RNA decay mechanism unless the same finite row also carries the missing bridge evidence.  In particular, an RBP bridge would require measured local occupancy of a prespecified decay-factor RBP at the editable window, experimental separation between coordinate label and occupancy, collapse under target-RBP loss, restoration under wild-type rescue, and failure under binding-defective or non-target controls.

\paragraph{Allowed conclusion.}
The accepted conclusion is therefore narrow.  The GLORI fact supports a finite m6A stoichiometry--exon-architecture readout over $60000$ rows, with $49668$ distinct measured sites, true cross-condition concordance, and coverage-confounding correlation $-0.135922814$.  This is enough for a BioReality chapter because the row counts, concordance flag, and confounding scalar give a concrete measured surface rather than a purely symbolic coordinate assertion.  The readout is bounded to RNA modification stoichiometry and exon architecture.  No translation realization, RNA half-life mechanism, folded structure, physical admissibility, biological function, adaptation, causation, system phenotype, or universal biological law follows from this chapter unless a separate layer-matched contact supplies the corresponding measurement and the finite statistic is recomputed on joined rows.
