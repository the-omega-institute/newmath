\subsection{A tRNA-supply increment beyond the measured dictionary}\label{sec:biorality-q6-trna-supply-increment}\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite-row object.}  This record concerns a yeast matched panel in which the response is log-scaled protein abundance after residualization against the sibling sequence controls, and the candidate increment is a per-gene tAI geometric mean computed from codon weights.  The base dictionary is intentionally already biological and measured: it contains $\log_{10}$ measured translation efficiency, $\log_{10}$ mRNA stability, and turnover, each residualized and standardized after the same control projection.  The full design appends one additional residualized and standardized tRNA-supply coordinate, namely the per-gene tAI geometric mean.  The finite carrier therefore has rows indexed by the active joined yeast protein identifiers and two design matrices, one measured-dictionary matrix and one dictionary-plus-tAI matrix, evaluated by deterministic five-fold prediction.

\paragraph{Observed finite calculation.}  The powered run uses $3290$ joined rows, five folds, ridge value $\lambda=10^{-8}$, and the fold rule given by deterministic index modulo $5$ over sorted active protein identifiers.  The base measured dictionary achieves held-out $R^2=0.4579960005021211$, while the full dictionary with the added tAI coordinate achieves held-out $R^2=0.5186044342149576$.  Thus the observed held-out increment is
$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta R^2_{\mathrm{heldout}}
  &= 0.5186044342149576-0.4579960005021211 \\
  &= 0.06060843371283653.
\end{aligned}
$$
The same comparison gives an in-sample context-only increment of $0.06016972939113374$, so the held-out number is not a training-only artifact of the fitted panel.  The tAI coordinate alone has $R^2=0.33369275999817194$, which is substantial but not the statistic used for the claim; the claim uses the increment after the measured dictionary is already present.

\paragraph{Permutation boundary.}  The null model keeps the readouts and folds fixed and applies deterministic SHA256 permutations of the gene-to-target labels.  With $120$ permutations, the null mean is $-0.0004019510944510909$, the null minimum is $-0.00510268312590334$, the null maximum is $0.0010291561244795133$, and the $95$th percentile is $0.0007222485676979806$.  The observed increment is therefore about $84$ times the $95$th percentile of the permutation null:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\frac{0.06060843371283653}{0.0007222485676979806}
  &\approx 83.9163.
\end{aligned}
$$
This makes the finite-row statement a predictive increment statement: after the measured dictionary consisting of translation efficiency, mRNA stability, and turnover is already included, a codon-weighted tRNA-supply coordinate still improves held-out prediction of residualized yeast protein abundance.

\paragraph{Coordinate and rank context.}  The tRNA-supply calculation is tied to the same nine-dimensional $B^*_{Q6}$ residual-coordinate family used in the surrounding measured translation and protein-readout analyses: $K\_AAA$, $Arg\_AGR$, $Ile\_AUA$, $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$, $Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG$, $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$, $Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG$, $Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY$, and $f3\_stress$.  In the cross-organism joint translation-efficiency and mRNA-stability mediation panel, the same coordinate count is $9$, the control column count is $24$, and the residualized control rank is $23$ in each computed organism.  For the yeast row of that panel, the joined count is $4759$, $R^2_{QP}=0.2715520808117803$, $R^2_{QP\mid T}=0.1439989545940314$, $R^2_{QP\mid T,M}=0.13047582645538838$, the TE-only fraction is $0.4697188319693239$, the stability-only fraction is $0.0626618595344251$, and the joint mediation fraction is $0.5195182225621592$ with right-tail permutation value $0.000999000999000999$.  These numbers provide the local background that measured translation and stability readouts already carry a large part of the yeast protein-abundance association; the present tRNA-supply result asks whether an additional tAI-derived coordinate survives after that measured dictionary is installed.

\paragraph{Measured-translation comparison.}  The measured translation-mediated protein-abundance run supplies a further check on the interpretation.  In yeast, the measured-TE panel has $5128$ joined three-way rows, residual degrees of freedom $5105$ after controls, $S^{QP}=0.3146818503437042$, measured $S^{QT}=0.13681931343730436$, and measured mediation fraction $0.4793069656345167$.  E. coli, human, and danio also have powered measured-TE fits, with joined row counts $3415$, $11554$, and $13140$ respectively.  The tRNA-supply increment in this section is not a replacement for those measured-TE calculations.  It is a separate finite-row statement that a modeled tRNA-supply summary retains predictive information after the measured yeast dictionary has already absorbed measured translation efficiency, mRNA stability, and turnover.

\paragraph{Four-case readback.}  The resulting classification is a bounded protein-readout support statement rather than a free-standing mechanism call.  The observed finite table rules out the null in which the added tAI coordinate contributes no held-out predictive increment beyond the measured dictionary on this yeast join.  It does not, by itself, assign a universal $Q\to T\to P$ causal pathway.  In the four-case language, the result is compatible with a translation-layer contribution only when the matched translation step is separately represented and the direct protein association is shown to drop after conditioning on that translation readout.  Without that accompanying translation-readout computation, a positive protein-abundance increment remains a protein-readout predictive residual.

\paragraph{Local no-promotion rule.}  The finite result authorizes the following narrow sentence: on $3290$ yeast joined rows, adding residualized per-gene tAI to a measured dictionary of translation efficiency, mRNA stability, and turnover improves held-out prediction of residualized log protein abundance by $\Delta R^2=0.06060843371283653$, far above the fixed-fold permutation boundary $0.0007222485676979806$.  It does not authorize claims about direct ribosome occupancy, protein folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, post-translational state, localization, phenotype, mechanism, causation, or universality.  Any such claim requires a separate layer-matched measurement and its own finite statistic.
