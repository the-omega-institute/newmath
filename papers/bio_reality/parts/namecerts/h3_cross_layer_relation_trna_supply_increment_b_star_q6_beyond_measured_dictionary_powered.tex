\subsection{Yeast tRNA-supply increment beyond the measured translation dictionary}\label{sec:bioreality-q6-trna-supply-increment}\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite panel.}
The record tests whether a tRNA-supply coordinate retains held-out predictive content for yeast protein abundance after the measured translation dictionary has already been placed in the design.  The active panel contains $n=3290$ joined yeast protein rows.  The target is $\log_{10}$ protein abundance after residualization against the sibling sequence controls, and the measured dictionary is the standardized residual design with $\log_{10}(\mathrm{measured\ TE})$, $\log_{10}(\mathrm{mRNA\ stability})$, and turnover.  The extended design appends a standardized residual per-gene tAI geometric mean computed from $61$ codon weights, $268$ usable tAI records, and $3290$ joined per-gene tAI rows, with no zero raw weight filled by a geometric mean.

\paragraph{Controlled statistic.}
Let $Y_Z$ denote the protein-abundance target after the declared sibling controls have been removed, let $D_Z$ denote the measured dictionary columns, and let $A_Z$ denote the residualized tAI supply coordinate.  The reported comparison is the held-out difference between a ridge fit using $D_Z$ and a ridge fit using $(D_Z,A_Z)$, with ridge parameter $10^{-8}$.  In the finite run,
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{\mathrm{base}} &= 0.4579960005021211,\\
R^2_{\mathrm{full}} &= 0.5186044342149576,\\
\Delta R^2_{\mathrm{held\ out}} &= 0.06060843371283653.
\end{aligned}
$$
The same contrast, evaluated only as in-sample context, gives $\Delta R^2=0.06016972939113374$, so the held-out value is not a cosmetic restatement of the fitted contrast.  The tAI-only readout has $R^2=0.33369275999817194$, which shows that the supply coordinate is large enough to be visible by itself but is here judged only by the incremental held-out comparison after the measured dictionary has already been used.

\paragraph{Fold and null construction.}
The cross-validation uses $5$ deterministic folds, assigning rows by index modulo $5$ over sorted active protein identifiers.  The null keeps the readouts and folds fixed and permutes gene-to-target labels by deterministic SHA256 permutations with seed $\mathrm{sha256{:}b\_star\_q6\_trna\_supply\_increment\_powered{:}fixed\mbox{-}readout\mbox{-}target\mbox{-}permutation}$.  Across $120$ permutations, the null distribution has mean $-0.0004019510944510909$, minimum $-0.00510268312590334$, maximum $0.0010291561244795133$, and $95\%$ cutoff $0.0007222485676979806$.  Therefore the observed held-out increment exceeds the reported null cutoff by a factor of about
$$
\begin{aligned}
\frac{0.06060843371283653}{0.0007222485676979806} \approx 83.91.
\end{aligned}
$$
The statistic is thus a finite-row positive increment for the yeast panel: after measured TE, mRNA stability, and turnover have been supplied, the tAI supply coordinate still improves held-out prediction of the residual protein-abundance target.

\paragraph{Relation to the measured translation layer.}
The measured-TE mediation audit gives the relevant local scale for interpreting this increment.  In yeast, the measured translation-protein decomposition used $5128$ joined three-way rows and had $S^{QP}=0.3146818503437042$, measured $S^{QT}=0.13681931343730436$, and measured mediated fraction $0.4793069656345167$.  In the stricter four-way yeast panel that also includes mRNA stability, $4759$ rows were joined; the corresponding joint variance partition had $R^2_{QP}=0.2715520808117803$, $R^2_{QP\mid T}=0.1439989545940314$, $R^2_{QP\mid T,M}=0.13047582645538838$, TE-only fraction $0.4697188319693239$, stability-only fraction $0.0626618595344251$, joint mediation fraction $0.5195182225621592$, and permutation right-tail probability $0.000999000999000999$.  The present tRNA-supply increment is narrower: it does not replace those translation and stability decompositions, but asks whether the modeled tRNA-supply coordinate contributes after those measured dictionary variables have already been included.

\paragraph{Boundary.}
This chapter supports only a bounded cross-layer protein-abundance readout on the stated yeast finite panel.  It does not show that tAI is observed ribosome occupancy, does not establish a causal translation mechanism, and does not by itself imply folding, physical admissibility, localization, post-translational state, molecular function, phenotype, or a universal biological rule.  The result is a held-out predictive increment for a residual abundance target under the recorded controls.  Any stronger biological interpretation would require a separate layer-matched measurement panel and its own statistic.
