\subsection{tRNA-supply increment beyond the measured translation dictionary}
\label{subsec:trna-supply-increment-beyond-measured-dictionary}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite panel.}
This record asks whether a tRNA-supply readout still contributes held-out protein-abundance information after a measured dictionary has already been supplied.  The finite panel is the yeast join with $n=3290$ active protein rows.  The response is log protein abundance after residualization against the sibling control design.  The measured dictionary contains three standardized, residualized columns: $\log_{10}$ measured translation efficiency, $\log_{10}$ mRNA stability, and turnover.  The enlarged design appends one further standardized residual coordinate, the per-gene tAI geometric mean computed from codon weights.  The codon-weight table contains $61$ sense-codon weights, the per-gene tAI join covers $3290$ rows, the usable tAI record count is $268$, and no raw zero weight was replaced by the geometric-mean fallback.

\paragraph{Held-out increment.}
The comparison is a deterministic five-fold ridge regression with folds assigned by sorted active protein identifier modulo $5$ and ridge parameter $10^{-8}$.  The measured dictionary alone gives
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{\mathrm{base}} &= 0.4579960005021211,
\end{aligned}
$$
whereas the dictionary plus residualized tAI coordinate gives
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{\mathrm{full}} &= 0.5186044342149576.
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the held-out increment is
$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta R^2_{\mathrm{held\ out}}
  &= R^2_{\mathrm{full}}-R^2_{\mathrm{base}} \\
  &= 0.06060843371283653.
\end{aligned}
$$
The in-sample contextual increment is close, $0.06016972939113374$, so the held-out effect is not merely an in-sample re-expression of the enlarged design.  A tAI-only fit gives $R^2=0.33369275999817194$, which is lower than the measured dictionary, but the important finite-row result is not the tAI-alone score.  It is the positive held-out increment after the measured translation, stability, and turnover dictionary is already present.

\paragraph{Permutation boundary.}
The null model fixes readouts and fold assignments and permutes gene-to-target labels by deterministic SHA256 seeds.  Across $120$ permutations, the null distribution for the held-out full-minus-base statistic has mean $-0.0004019510944510909$, minimum $-0.00510268312590334$, maximum $0.0010291561244795133$, and $95$th percentile $0.0007222485676979806$.  The observed increment is therefore separated from the finite permutation boundary:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta R^2_{\mathrm{held\ out}} &= 0.06060843371283653,\\
q_{0.95}^{\mathrm{null}} &= 0.0007222485676979806,\\
\Delta R^2_{\mathrm{held\ out}}-q_{0.95}^{\mathrm{null}}
  &= 0.05988618514513855.
\end{aligned}
$$
The computation used the seed string \texttt{sha256:b\_star\_q6\_trna\_supply\_increment\_powered:fixed-readout-target-permutation}.  This makes the positive increment a finite, reproducible row-level observation for the declared yeast panel, not a visual trend or a naming consequence.

\paragraph{Relation to measured translation and stability rows.}
The dictionary columns are not empty labels.  In the matched yeast translation--stability--protein panel, the four-way join contains $4759$ rows with a reported join hit rate $0.8818572656921754$.  On that panel the joint $B^*_{{Q6}}$ protein-abundance association is $R^2_{QP}=0.2715520808117803$; conditioning on measured translation alone leaves $R^2_{QP\mid T}=0.1439989545940314$, and conditioning on measured translation plus mRNA stability leaves $R^2_{QP\mid T,M}=0.13047582645538838$.  The corresponding joint mediation fraction is $0.5195182225621592$, with a right-tail permutation value $0.000999000999000999$.  The measured-translation protein-abundance computation also reports a yeast three-way join of $5128$ rows, with $S^{QP}=0.3146818503437042$, measured $S^{QT}=0.13681931343730436$, and measured mediation fraction $0.4793069656345167$.  These numbers justify treating measured translation and stability as real finite-row dictionary terms before asking whether the tRNA-supply coordinate adds anything beyond them.

\paragraph{Readout.}
The chapter-specific conclusion is narrow.  On the yeast finite panel, residualized per-gene tAI supplies a positive held-out protein-abundance increment beyond measured translation efficiency, mRNA stability, and turnover.  The supported statement is the inequality
$$
\begin{aligned}
0.06060843371283653 &> 0.0007222485676979806,
\end{aligned}
$$
with the left side computed from the fixed five-fold held-out design and the right side from the fixed-label-permutation null.  This is a cross-layer protein-abundance readout conditioned on measured dictionary rows.  It is not, by itself, evidence for a translation mechanism, folding state, physical admissibility, biological function, phenotype, or universal law.  Those stronger interpretations would require separate row-matched contacts and statistics at the corresponding layer.  The result also does not turn modelled tAI into observed ribosome occupancy; it only shows that the tAI-derived tRNA-supply coordinate retains predictive protein-abundance information after the measured dictionary used here has been included.
