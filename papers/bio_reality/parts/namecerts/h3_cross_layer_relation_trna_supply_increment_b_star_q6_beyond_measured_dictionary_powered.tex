\subsection{Residual tRNA-supply increment beyond measured translation and stability readouts}
\label{sec:bioreality-trna-supply-increment-beyond-measured-dictionary}

\paragraph{Finite-row question.}
This record asks whether a yeast B$^*_{Q6}$-indexed tRNA-supply coordinate retains held-out predictive content for protein abundance after the measured local dictionary of translation and stability readouts has already been used.  The tested rows are not a symbolic codon table alone.  They are $3290$ active yeast protein rows on which the target is log$_{10}$ protein abundance after residualization against the sibling sequence controls, and the measured dictionary consists of Z-residualized and standardized log$_{10}$ measured translation efficiency, log$_{10}$ mRNA stability, and turnover.  The full design appends a Z-residualized standardized per-gene tAI geometric mean, computed from $61$ codon weights, $268$ usable tAI records, and $3290$ joined per-gene tAI values, with no zero raw $W$ entries requiring geometric-mean filling.

\paragraph{Controlled finite-row statistic.}
The fitted comparison uses five deterministic held-out folds over sorted active protein identifiers, with ridge parameter $10^{-8}$.  Let $R^2_{\mathrm{base}}$ denote the held-out coefficient of determination for the measured dictionary and let $R^2_{\mathrm{full}}$ denote the same statistic after adding the tRNA-supply coordinate.  The recorded values are
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{\mathrm{base}} &= 0.4579960005021211,\\
R^2_{\mathrm{full}} &= 0.5186044342149576,\\
\Delta R^2_{\mathrm{heldout}} &= R^2_{\mathrm{full}}-R^2_{\mathrm{base}} = 0.06060843371283653.
\end{aligned}
$$
The corresponding in-sample context-only increment is $0.06016972939113374$, close to the held-out increment rather than being an isolated training-fit artifact.  As a separate scalar check, the tAI-only design has $R^2=0.33369275999817194$, so the tRNA-supply readout is individually informative but is not being reported here as a replacement for the measured translation, stability, and turnover dictionary.

\paragraph{Permutation scale.}
The null comparison keeps the readouts and fold assignment fixed and applies deterministic SHA256 permutations of gene-to-target labels.  Across $120$ permutations, the null distribution for $\Delta R^2$ has mean $-0.0004019510944510909$, minimum $-0.00510268312590334$, maximum $0.0010291561244795133$, and $95\%$ quantile $0.0007222485676979806$.  Thus the observed held-out increment is about $84$ times the null $95\%$ cutoff:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\frac{0.06060843371283653}{0.0007222485676979806} &\approx 83.92.
\end{aligned}
$$
This supports a finite-row statement that, on this yeast join, the tRNA-supply coordinate contributes predictive signal beyond the measured dictionary used in the base design.  The statistic is a held-out prediction increment, not a causal decomposition.

\paragraph{Relation to measured translation mediation.}
The same biological layer has an independently recorded measured-TE mediation panel across yeast, E. coli, human, and zebrafish.  In that panel, the yeast measured mediation fraction is $0.4793069656345167$, with $S^{QP}=0.3146818503437042$ and measured $S^{QT}=0.13681931343730436$ on $5128$ three-way joined yeast rows.  The E. coli measured fraction is much smaller, $0.04091158340950673$, with $S^{QP}=0.10941535951828074$ and measured $S^{QT}=0.009394266754822262$ on $3415$ joined rows.  Human is smaller still, with measured fraction $0.0027966485036786744$, $S^{QP}=0.04288464996165745$, and measured $S^{QT}=0.018837911344127926$ on $11554$ joined rows.  These comparison numbers matter because the present yeast tRNA-supply increment is not being inferred from modeled tAI alone: it is evaluated after measured translation-efficiency, mRNA-stability, and turnover columns are already in the base dictionary.

\paragraph{Relation to joint TE and mRNA-stability conditioning.}
A second finite-row panel decomposes B$^*_{Q6}$ protein-abundance association by measured translation efficiency and mRNA stability jointly.  In yeast, $4759$ joined rows give $R^2_{QP}=0.2715520808117803$ and $R^2_{QP\mid T,M}=0.13047582645538838$, with joint mediation fraction $0.5195182225621592$ and right-tail permutation value $0.000999000999000999$.  In E. coli, $2517$ joined rows give $R^2_{QP}=0.1181743523064932$, $R^2_{QP\mid T,M}=0.08975732521542036$, joint mediation fraction $0.2404669586626661$, and the same recorded right-tail value $0.000999000999000999$.  In human, $11322$ joined rows give $R^2_{QP}=0.04274178488511215$, $R^2_{QP\mid T,M}=0.0355223930537993$, joint mediation fraction $0.16890712099923358$, and right-tail value $0.000999000999000999$.  The present yeast result is therefore best read as an additional held-out tRNA-supply contribution after measured TE, stability, and turnover have been placed directly in the model, rather than as a substitute for those measured mediators.

\paragraph{Case assignment.}
For the four-case translation-protein discriminant, this record is closest to a bounded case-D-style signal only for the specific yeast held-out dictionary task: a translation-related supply coordinate improves prediction after measured translation and stability readouts are included.  The supported statement is narrower than a general protein-realization claim.  It says that, on the finite yeast join, a Z-residualized per-gene tAI coordinate raises held-out protein-abundance prediction from $0.4579960005021211$ to $0.5186044342149576$, exceeding the fixed permutation cutoff by a large margin.  It does not say that every B$^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinate has a measured translation path, nor that the protein-abundance association is fully mediated by translation.

\paragraph{Local scope boundary.}
The calculation is observational and row-matched, not an intervention.  The tAI coordinate is a modeled tRNA-supply readout, while measured translation efficiency, mRNA stability, turnover, and protein abundance are separate data columns.  From this result alone one may not infer ribosome occupancy for unmeasured rows, protein folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, post-translational state, localization, phenotype, mechanism, or universality.  Any such conclusion would require its own matched contact and its own statistic at that layer.  The finite conclusion retained here is only that the yeast tRNA-supply coordinate gives a positive held-out protein-abundance increment beyond the measured translation/stability/turnover dictionary under the recorded residualization and permutation design.
