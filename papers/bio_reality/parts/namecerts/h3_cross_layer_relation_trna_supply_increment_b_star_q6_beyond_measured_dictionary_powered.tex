\subsection{A measured-dictionary test for residual tRNA-supply increment}
\label{sec:bioreality-q6-trna-supply-increment}

\paragraph{Finite row object.}
The finite object in this chapter is the yeast row set on which protein abundance, measured translation efficiency, mRNA stability, turnover, codon counts, and a per-gene tRNA-adaptation score are simultaneously available.  After the sibling sequence controls have been removed, the target is the residual of $\log_{10}$ protein abundance on this yeast join.  The active joined panel contains $3290$ protein identifiers.  The base dictionary consists of three standardized residual columns, $\log_{10}(\mathrm{measured\ TE})$, $\log_{10}(\mathrm{mRNA\ stability})$, and turnover.  The tested extension appends one standardized residual column: the per-gene tAI geometric mean computed from $61$ codon weights.  The tAI construction joins $3290$ per-gene records, uses $268$ usable tAI records, and requires no geometric-mean fill for zero raw weights.

\paragraph{Controlled regression comparison.}
Let $Y$ denote the residualized yeast protein-abundance target, let $D$ denote the measured dictionary, and let $A$ denote the residualized per-gene tAI geometric mean.  The finite comparison is not a statement about all translation systems; it is a held-out comparison between $D$ and $(D,A)$ on the same finite table.  With ridge parameter $\lambda=10^{-8}$ and five deterministic folds indexed by sorted active protein identifiers modulo $5$, the observed scores are
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2(Y\mid D) &= 0.4579960005021211,\\
R^2(Y\mid D,A) &= 0.5186044342149576,\\
\Delta R^2_{\mathrm{held\ out}} &= 0.06060843371283653.
\end{aligned}
$$
The corresponding in-sample context-only increment is $0.06016972939113374$, so the held-out and fitted increments agree at the scale relevant for this row set.  A tAI-only fit gives $R^2=0.33369275999817194$, which is large enough to show that the tRNA-supply column is not numerically inert, but the claim here is narrower: the added column improves prediction after the measured dictionary has already been supplied.

\paragraph{Permutation boundary.}
The null comparison keeps the readouts and folds fixed and applies deterministic SHA256 permutations of gene-to-target labels.  Across $120$ permutations, the null distribution has mean $-0.0004019510944510909$, minimum $-0.00510268312590334$, maximum $0.0010291561244795133$, and $95$th percentile $0.0007222485676979806$.  Hence the observed held-out increment satisfies
$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta R^2_{\mathrm{held\ out}} &= 0.06060843371283653,\\
q_{0.95}(\Delta R^2_{\mathrm{null}}) &= 0.0007222485676979806,\\
\Delta R^2_{\mathrm{held\ out}} - q_{0.95}(\Delta R^2_{\mathrm{null}}) &= 0.05988618514513855.
\end{aligned}
$$
The finite result is therefore a positive held-out increment of tRNA-supply information beyond the measured dictionary on this yeast table, under the declared fold rule and ridge value.  The deterministic seed for the permutation family is $\mathrm{sha256{:}b\_star\_q6\_trna\_supply\_increment\_powered{:}fixed\text{-}readout\text{-}target\text{-}permutation}$.

\paragraph{Relation to the measured translation and stability rows.}
The same program has nearby yeast readouts that keep the interpretation bounded.  In the measured-TE protein-abundance table, the yeast three-way join has $5128$ matched rows, with $S^{QP}=0.3146818503437042$, $S^{QT}_{\mathrm{meas}}=0.13681931343730436$, and measured mediation fraction $0.4793069656345167$ under a rank-sufficient residual design.  In the joint measured-TE and mRNA-stability decomposition, the yeast four-way join has $4759$ matched rows, $R^2_{QP}=0.2715520808117803$, $R^2_{QP\mid T,M}=0.13047582645538838$, and joint mediation fraction $0.5195182225621592$, with right-tail permutation value $0.000999000999000999$.  These companion numbers explain why the dictionary $D$ is a real competing dictionary rather than a symbolic placeholder: measured translation and measured stability already absorb a substantial part of the protein-abundance residual association.

\paragraph{Case assignment.}
For this chapter the relevant case is a bounded protein-readout increment after measured translation, stability, and turnover are already in the design.  The tRNA-supply column is not being used as an observed ribosome-footprint row.  It is a modeled codon-adaptation readout that survives as an added predictor of the residualized protein-abundance target.  Thus the finite row result supports the statement that, on the $3290$-row yeast join, a per-gene tAI geometric mean carries held-out predictive information beyond the measured dictionary.  It does not by itself assign a full four-case $S^{QT}$, $S^{QP}$, and mediation-drop table for every individual $B^*_{Q6}$ coordinate.

\paragraph{Local non-promotion rule.}
The finite result remains at the protein-abundance readout level.  It does not establish translation realization from tAI alone, does not identify ribosome occupancy, and does not imply folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, post-translational state, phenotype, mechanism, adaptation, causation, or universality.  Any one of those stronger conclusions would need its own matched contact and its own statistic on the corresponding layer.  The present chapter only records that a modeled tRNA-supply column adds held-out protein-abundance predictive power beyond measured TE, measured mRNA stability, and turnover on the specified yeast residual table.
