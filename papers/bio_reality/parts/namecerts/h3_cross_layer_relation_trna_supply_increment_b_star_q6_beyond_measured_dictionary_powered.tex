\subsection{Yeast tRNA-supply increment beyond the measured translation dictionary}
\label{sec:h3-cross-layer-relation-trna-supply-increment-b-star-q6-beyond-measured-dictionary-powered}

\paragraph{Question encoded by the finite rows.}
This BioReality record asks whether a yeast tRNA-supply readout retains predictive mass for protein abundance after the measured dictionary already contains translation efficiency, mRNA stability, and turnover.  The target is the yeast protein-abundance response after residualization against the sibling sequence controls: amino-acid composition, coding-sequence length, GC3, and the residual-coordinate support-density baseline used for the $B^\ast_{Q6}$ packet.  The base design is the standardized residual dictionary
$\log_{10}(\mathrm{measured\ TE})$, $\log_{10}(\mathrm{mRNA\ stability})$, and turnover.  The full design adds the standardized residual per-gene tAI geometric mean computed from tRNA supply.  The finite carrier is therefore not a symbolic statement about codon usage in the abstract; it is a held-out regression comparison over the $3290$ joined yeast protein rows for which the target, measured dictionary, controls, and tAI readout are all simultaneously present.

\paragraph{Numerical certificate.}
The observed base held-out coefficient of determination is $0.4579960005021211$, while the full design reaches $0.5186044342149576$.  Thus the finite increment is
$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta R^2_{\mathrm{held\ out}}
  &= 0.5186044342149576 - 0.4579960005021211 \\
  &= 0.06060843371283653.
\end{aligned}
$$
The same direction appears in the context-only in-sample comparison, where the added tAI column contributes $0.06016972939113374$.  In isolation, the tAI readout has $R^2=0.33369275999817194$ against the same residual protein-abundance target, but the claim here is narrower: it concerns the increment left after the measured dictionary has already been given the first opportunity to explain the target.  The regression uses five deterministic folds, assigned by index modulo $5$ over sorted active protein identifiers, and ridge parameter $10^{-8}$.  The tRNA-supply construction used $61$ codon weights, $268$ usable tAI records, and $3290$ per-gene tAI joins, with $0$ zero raw weights filled by a geometric mean.

\paragraph{Permutation boundary.}
The null model keeps the readouts and fold assignment fixed and permutes gene-to-target labels by deterministic SHA256 permutations.  Across $120$ permutations, the null distribution has mean $-0.0004019510944510909$, minimum $-0.00510268312590334$, maximum $0.0010291561244795133$, and $95$th percentile $0.0007222485676979806$.  The observed held-out increment is therefore far outside the recorded null envelope:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta R^2_{\mathrm{observed}} &= 0.06060843371283653,\\
\Delta R^2_{\mathrm{null},95} &= 0.0007222485676979806,\\
\Delta R^2_{\mathrm{observed}} / \Delta R^2_{\mathrm{null},95} &\approx 83.92.
\end{aligned}
$$
This is the finite-row basis for treating the tRNA-supply coordinate as a real residual predictor in this yeast panel.  The deterministic seed for the null was $\mathrm{sha256{:}b\_star\_q6\_trna\_supply\_increment\_powered{:}fixed\mbox{-}readout\mbox{-}target\mbox{-}permutation}$, so the comparison is tied to a reproducible finite resampling rule rather than to a floating descriptive threshold.

\paragraph{Relation to the measured translation rows.}
The measured-TE protein-abundance record provides the local translation dictionary used by this chapter.  In yeast, that measured three-way join contains $5128$ rows, with $5815$ coding sequences joined to abundance and $5128$ valid measured-TE entries.  In that measured-translation decomposition, the yeast protein-abundance association has $S^{QP}=0.3146818503437042$, the measured translation readout has $S^{QT}=0.13681931343730436$, and the measured mediation fraction is $0.4793069656345167$.  Those numbers show that the measured translation column is not absent or merely nominal before the tRNA-supply increment is tested.  The increment reported here is therefore not a replacement for measured TE; it is the remaining held-out signal after measured TE, mRNA stability, and turnover have already been placed in the base dictionary.

\paragraph{Relation to stability and joint mediation rows.}
The wider yeast joint TE-stability panel supplies an additional check on the local scale of the data.  In the joint yeast panel, $4759$ rows are joined, with $R^2_{QP}=0.2715520808117803$, $R^2_{QP\mid T}=0.1439989545940314$, $R^2_{QP\mid M}=0.25453612246767165$, and $R^2_{QP\mid T,M}=0.13047582645538838$.  The corresponding joint mediation fraction is $0.5195182225621592$, with right-tail permutation value $0.000999000999000999$.  These figures do not prove a causal path, but they make clear that the measured dictionary is already carrying substantial translation and stability information before the tRNA-supply column is asked for additional held-out power.

\paragraph{Local interpretation.}
The ready claim is a bounded cross-layer statement: in this yeast finite-row panel, a tRNA-supply readout adds held-out predictive power for residual protein abundance beyond a measured dictionary containing measured TE, mRNA stability, and turnover.  The appropriate scientific reading is that modeled per-gene tAI retains information not exhausted by those measured covariates in the present joined matrix.  The result is not a proof that tRNA supply causes the observed abundance, nor that the $B^\ast_{Q6}$ residual coordinates realize a universal translation rule.  It also does not promote the residual coordinate to folding, physical admissibility, biological function, localization, post-translational state, phenotype, mechanism, or universality.  Each of those stronger biological layers would require its own matched contact and statistic.

\paragraph{Case assignment boundary.}
For the four-case translation-protein discriminant, this chapter supports only the dictionary-increment component.  The positive $\Delta R^2$ says that the tAI supply coordinate remains useful after measured translation and stability covariates are present.  It does not by itself assign every individual $(q_i,T_j,P_k)$ triple to cases A--D, because that assignment requires the coordinate-level $S^{QT}$, $S^{QP}$, and conditioning drop for each matched triple.  The finite outcome is therefore a ready bounded increment, not a blanket promotion from protein-readout prediction to mechanism.
