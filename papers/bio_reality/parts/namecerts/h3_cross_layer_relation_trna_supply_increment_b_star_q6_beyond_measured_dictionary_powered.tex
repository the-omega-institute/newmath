\paragraph{Interpretation.}
The ready claim is narrow.  On the yeast joined panel, a residualized per-gene tAI geometric mean adds held-out protein-abundance prediction beyond the measured dictionary consisting of measured TE, mRNA stability, and turnover.  The finite result is a dictionary-increment result, not a claim that tAI is an observed ribosome-occupancy measurement.  It also does not by itself establish translation causation, protein folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, post-translational state, localization, phenotype, mechanism, or a universal biological rule.  Such claims require their own layer-matched measurements and their own finite statistics.  Here the supported statement is only that the tRNA-supply coordinate remains predictive after the stated measured readouts and sequence controls have already been included.
