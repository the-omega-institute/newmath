\subsection{tRNA-Supply Increment Beyond the Measured Dictionary}
\label{sec:bioreality-trna-supply-increment-beyond-measured-dictionary}

\paragraph{Finite row object.}
The claim is evaluated on a yeast matched panel in which the target is log protein abundance after residualization against the sibling sequence controls.  The active finite table has $n=3290$ joined protein rows, and the prediction task compares two row-wise designs.  The base design contains the measured dictionary columns
$\log_{10}(\mathrm{measured\ TE})$, $\log_{10}(\mathrm{mRNA\ stability})$, and turnover, each residualized against the same covariate surface and standardized.  The full design appends one additional row coordinate: the residualized and standardized per-gene tAI geometric mean computed from tRNA supply.  The tRNA-supply side is finite as well: $61$ codon weights enter the calculation, $268$ usable tAI records are available, $3290$ per-gene tAI values join to the active rows, and no zero raw $W$ value requires geometric-mean filling.

\paragraph{Controlled increment.}
Let $R^2_{\mathrm{base}}$ denote the held-out coefficient of determination for the measured dictionary, and let $R^2_{\mathrm{full}}$ denote the corresponding value after adding the tRNA-supply coordinate.  The recorded finite-row values are
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{\mathrm{base}} &= 0.4579960005021211,\\
R^2_{\mathrm{full}} &= 0.5186044342149576,\\
\Delta R^2_{\mathrm{held\ out}} &= R^2_{\mathrm{full}}-R^2_{\mathrm{base}} = 0.06060843371283653.
\end{aligned}
$$
The same comparison in the in-sample context gives $\Delta R^2=0.06016972939113374$, so the held-out and in-sample increments agree at the displayed precision scale rather than being a training-only artifact.  The model uses $5$ deterministic folds, assigned by index modulo $5$ over sorted active protein identifiers, and ridge stabilization $\lambda=10^{-8}$.  In isolation, the tAI coordinate has $R^2=0.33369275999817194$ against the same residualized protein-abundance target, but the operative claim is not that tAI alone replaces the measured dictionary.  The operative claim is the positive held-out increment after the dictionary has already been supplied.

\paragraph{Permutation boundary.}
The null model keeps the readouts and folds fixed and applies deterministic SHA256 permutations of the gene-to-target labels.  Across $120$ permutations, the null distribution for $\Delta R^2(\mathrm{full}-\mathrm{base})$ has mean $-0.0004019510944510909$, minimum $-0.00510268312590334$, maximum $0.0010291561244795133$, and $95$th percentile $0.0007222485676979806$.  The observed held-out increment therefore exceeds the displayed null boundary by
$$
\begin{aligned}
0.06060843371283653 - 0.0007222485676979806
  &= 0.05988618514513855.
\end{aligned}
$$
The deterministic seed for this finite audit is $\mathrm{sha256{:}b\_star\_q6\_trna\_supply\_increment\_powered{:}fixed\text{-}readout\text{-}target\text{-}permutation}$.  This makes the record a reproducible row-level increment statement: after the measured translation, stability, and turnover dictionary is already present, the tRNA-supply coordinate still carries held-out protein-abundance information on this yeast join.

\paragraph{Relation to the measured translation dictionary.}
The measured-dictionary context is not empty.  In the measured translation-to-protein decomposition for yeast, the three-way measured TE and abundance join contains $5128$ rows, with $R^2_{QP}=S^{QP}=0.3146818503437042$, measured translation survival $S^{QT}=0.13681931343730436$, and measured mediated fraction $0.4793069656345167$.  On the more restrictive yeast joint TE, mRNA-stability, and protein-abundance panel, the joined row count is $4759$, with $R^2_{QP}=0.2715520808117803$, $R^2_{QP\mid T}=0.1439989545940314$, $R^2_{QP\mid M}=0.25453612246767165$, and $R^2_{QP\mid T,M}=0.13047582645538838$.  The corresponding joint mediation fraction is $0.5195182225621592$, with TE-only fraction $0.4697188319693239$, stability-only fraction $0.0626618595344251$, and right-tail permutation value $0.000999000999000999$.  These numbers show why the present chapter is phrased as an increment beyond a measured dictionary rather than as a first contact with protein abundance: the measured TE and mRNA-stability surfaces already explain a substantial component of the residual association, and the tRNA-supply coordinate is tested only for the remaining held-out improvement.

\paragraph{Readout class.}
This finite-row result belongs to the cross-layer relation class.  It is not a universal codon law and it is not a direct mechanism claim.  The positive quantity is the held-out improvement of one residualized tRNA-supply coordinate when added to a fixed measured-readout dictionary on a yeast protein-abundance target.  The statistic is compatible with a translation-supply contribution to the protein-abundance readout, but only at the level of this controlled predictive relation.  It does not by itself identify elongation kinetics, initiation, ribosome occupancy at particular codons, protein folding, protein stability, localization, post-translational modification, physical admissibility, molecular function, phenotype, causation, or universality.

\paragraph{Local no-promotion rule.}
The chapter therefore records a narrow positive boundary.  A tRNA-supply increment is present because $\Delta R^2_{\mathrm{held\ out}}=0.06060843371283653$ is positive and far above the fixed-label-permutation $95$th percentile $0.0007222485676979806$ on $3290$ yeast rows.  No stronger biological conclusion follows from this table alone.  Any claim about measured translation realization requires its own matched translation-readout computation; any claim about folding, physical admissibility, function, phenotype, mechanism, localization, stability, or a general biological rule requires a separate layer-matched contact and its own finite statistic.
