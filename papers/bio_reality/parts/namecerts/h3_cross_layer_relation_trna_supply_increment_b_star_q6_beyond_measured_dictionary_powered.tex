\subsection{Yeast tRNA-Supply Increment Beyond the Measured Dictionary}\label{sec:bioreality-trna-supply-increment-beyond-measured-dictionary}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite row object.}
This record tests whether a yeast tRNA-supply coordinate still carries held-out protein-abundance information after the measured dictionary has already supplied translation efficiency, mRNA stability, and protein turnover. The finite carrier is the matched yeast protein table on which the target is log-transformed protein abundance after sibling sequence controls have been removed. The base design consists of $Z$-residualized and standardized columns for $\log_{10}(\mathrm{measured\ TE})$, $\log_{10}(\mathrm{mRNA\ stability})$, and turnover. The full design appends one additional $Z$-residualized and standardized per-gene tAI geometric-mean coordinate. The active matched table has $3290$ joined protein rows, uses $5$ deterministic folds, and assigns folds by sorted active protein identifier modulo $5$. The ridge parameter is $10^{-8}$.

\paragraph{Held-out increment.}
On this finite table the base dictionary reaches
$R^2_{\mathrm{base}}=0.4579960005021211$, while the full design reaches
$R^2_{\mathrm{full}}=0.5186044342149576$. Thus the held-out increment assigned to the tRNA-supply coordinate is
$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta R^2_{\mathrm{held\ out}}
&=R^2_{\mathrm{full}}-R^2_{\mathrm{base}}\\
&=0.5186044342149576-0.4579960005021211\\
&=0.06060843371283653.
\end{aligned}
$$
The same contrast computed in sample for context is $0.06016972939113374$, so the held-out estimate is not merely an in-sample-only artifact of the fitted dictionary. The tAI coordinate alone has $R^2=0.33369275999817194$, but the claim here is narrower: the tested signal is the incremental survival of tAI after measured TE, mRNA stability, and turnover are already in the dictionary.

\paragraph{Permutation boundary.}
The null model keeps the readouts and folds fixed and applies deterministic SHA256 permutations of gene-to-target labels; the reported statistic is the held-out $\Delta R^2$ of the full design over the base design. Across $120$ permutations, the null mean is $-0.0004019510944510909$, the null minimum is $-0.00510268312590334$, the null maximum is $0.0010291561244795133$, and the $95\%$ null cutoff is $0.0007222485676979806$. The observed increment therefore clears the finite permutation boundary by a wide margin:
$$
\begin{aligned}
0.06060843371283653 &> 0.0007222485676979806.
\end{aligned}
$$
The deterministic seed for this finite test is $\mathrm{sha256{:}b\_star\_q6\_trna\_supply\_increment\_powered{:}fixed\mbox{-}readout\mbox{-}target\mbox{-}permutation}$. The tRNA supply computation used $61$ codon weights, joined $3290$ per-gene tAI records, used $268$ usable tAI records, and required zero geometric-mean fills for zero raw weights.

\paragraph{Relation to measured translation and protein readouts.}
The increment is interpreted only after the measured translation-protein boundary is kept explicit. In the measured yeast translation-protein table, the three-way measured TE/protein/CDS join has $5128$ joined rows. The measured translation readout gives $S^{QT}_{\mathrm{meas}}=0.13681931343730436$, the protein-abundance residual readout gives $S^{QP}=0.3146818503437042$, and the measured mediation fraction is $0.4793069656345167$. The corresponding residualized rank diagnostics report $\operatorname{rank}(Z)=23$, $\operatorname{rank}(X)=9$, $\operatorname{rank}(T)=1$, and residual degrees of freedom $5105$. These values locate the present tRNA-supply result downstream of an already nondegenerate measured translation readout, not as a replacement for that readout.

\paragraph{Relation to mRNA-stability and joint mediation.}
A separate yeast joint table with measured TE and mRNA stability has $4759$ joined rows. There the protein-abundance residual association for the nine-dimensional $B^*_{Q6}$ coordinate has $R^2_{QP}=0.2715520808117803$, drops to $R^2_{QP\mid T}=0.1439989545940314$ after measured TE, and drops to $R^2_{QP\mid T,M}=0.13047582645538838$ after measured TE and mRNA stability together. The joint mediation fraction is $0.5195182225621592$, the TE-only fraction is $0.4697188319693239$, the stability-only fraction is $0.0626618595344251$, and the right-tail permutation value is $0.000999000999000999$. This context matters because the tRNA-supply increment is not being compared against an empty model; it is tested beyond a measured dictionary whose components already capture a substantial part of the same protein-abundance residual surface.

\paragraph{Readback.}
The finite readback is therefore a positive residual-coordinate increment: after measured TE, mRNA stability, and turnover have been residualized and standardized with the sibling sequence controls, the per-gene tAI geometric mean still adds $0.06060843371283653$ held-out $R^2$ on the $3290$-row yeast join. The result supports a bounded cross-layer relation between the tRNA-supply coordinate and the chosen yeast protein-abundance residual readout. It does not by itself assert that tAI is the causal mediator, that codon usage determines protein abundance, or that the coordinate realizes a molecular mechanism.

\paragraph{Local non-promotion rule.}
This chapter allows only the finite-row statement just described. No translation mechanism, folding statement, physical-admissibility statement, biological-function statement, phenotype statement, localization statement, post-translational-state statement, or universal biological law follows from this increment alone. A stronger biological interpretation would require a separate layer-matched contact and its own controlled statistic. In particular, a protein-abundance increment is not a folding assay, not a function assay, not a perturbation or rescue experiment, and not proof of causal translation control. The admissible claim is the narrower one: in the declared yeast finite table, a residualized tRNA-supply coordinate improves held-out prediction beyond the measured dictionary by a value far above the fixed permutation boundary.
