\subsection{Yeast tRNA-supply increment beyond the measured translation dictionary}
\label{sec:bioreality-q6-trna-supply-increment-beyond-measured-dictionary}

\paragraph{Finite matched-row object.}
This record evaluates whether a modeled tRNA-supply coordinate still carries held-out protein-abundance information after the measured yeast dictionary has already been supplied.  The finite carrier is the yeast join of coding-sequence rows, measured translation efficiency, measured mRNA-stability, turnover, protein abundance, and per-gene tAI geometric mean.  The target is $\log_{10}$ protein abundance after residualization against the sibling sequence controls, and the active sample contains $n=3290$ joined protein rows.  The measured dictionary used as the base design consists of the standardized residual columns $\log_{10}(\mathrm{measured\_te})$, $\log_{10}(\mathrm{mrna\_stability})$, and turnover.  The full design appends the standardized residual per-gene tAI geometric mean.  Thus the tested increment is not a raw codon-usage association; it is the additional predictive contribution of tRNA-supply after the measured dictionary has been given first.

\paragraph{Controlled held-out contrast.}
The held-out computation uses five folds, with fold assignment fixed by deterministic index modulo $5$ over sorted active protein identifiers.  The ridge value is $10^{-8}$.  On this finite design the measured dictionary alone gives
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{\mathrm{base}} &= 0.4579960005021211,
\end{aligned}
$$
whereas the dictionary plus tAI-supply coordinate gives
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{\mathrm{full}} &= 0.5186044342149576.
\end{aligned}
$$
The held-out improvement is therefore
$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta R^2_{\mathrm{held\ out}} &= R^2_{\mathrm{full}}-R^2_{\mathrm{base}} \\
&= 0.06060843371283653.
\end{aligned}
$$
The in-sample contextual increment is close, $0.06016972939113374$, which records that the held-out effect is not an artifact of reporting only the fitted contrast.  The same finite run reports $R^2=0.33369275999817194$ for tAI alone, but that scalar is not the promoted statistic; the promoted statistic is the increment after the measured dictionary is already present.

\paragraph{Permutation boundary.}
The null keeps the readouts and folds fixed and applies deterministic SHA256 permutations of gene-to-target labels.  With $120$ permutations, the null distribution for the held-out full-minus-base statistic has mean $-0.0004019510944510909$, minimum $-0.00510268312590334$, maximum $0.0010291561244795133$, and $95$th percentile $0.0007222485676979806$.  The observed held-out increment is therefore far outside the reported upper null quantile:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta R^2_{\mathrm{held\ out}} &= 0.06060843371283653,\\
q_{0.95}^{\mathrm{null}} &= 0.0007222485676979806.
\end{aligned}
$$
The deterministic seed for this null is $\mathrm{sha256{:}b\_star\_q6\_trna\_supply\_increment\_powered{:}fixed\mbox{-}readout\mbox{-}target\mbox{-}permutation}$.  The tRNA-supply construction used $61$ codon weights, joined $3290$ per-gene tAI records into the active table, used $268$ usable tAI records, and required no geometric-mean filling for zero raw $W$ values.

\paragraph{Relation to the measured translation and stability dictionary.}
The measured-dictionary side is not empty background.  In the matched measured-translation protein-abundance run, the yeast three-way join contains $5128$ rows and reports $S^{QP}=0.3146818503437042$, measured $S^{QT}=0.13681931343730436$, and a measured mediation fraction $0.4793069656345167$ for the yeast protein-abundance readout.  In the joint measured-TE and mRNA-stability decomposition on the stricter four-way yeast join, $n=4759$, $R^2_{QP}=0.2715520808117803$, $R^2_{QP\mid T}=0.1439989545940314$, $R^2_{QP\mid M}=0.25453612246767165$, and $R^2_{QP\mid T,M}=0.13047582645538838$.  That run reports a joint mediation fraction $0.5195182225621592$, with TE-only fraction $0.4697188319693239$, stability-only fraction $0.0626618595344251$, and right-tail permutation value $0.000999000999000999$.  The present increment therefore asks a sharper finite question: after measured TE, measured mRNA stability, and turnover are explicitly placed in the base dictionary, does the modeled tRNA-supply readout still improve held-out abundance prediction?  The answer in this finite yeast table is yes, by $\Delta R^2=0.06060843371283653$ against a null $95$th percentile of $0.0007222485676979806$.

\paragraph{Local interpretation and refusal line.}
This chapter records a bounded cross-layer relation: in yeast, a tAI-derived tRNA-supply coordinate adds held-out predictive information for residual protein abundance beyond a measured dictionary containing translation efficiency, mRNA stability, and turnover.  It does not state that the modeled tAI coordinate is observed ribosome occupancy, and it does not state that the residual coordinate causes translation, protein abundance, or any later biological state.  No conclusion about folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, localization, post-translational state, phenotype, mechanism, or universality follows from this table.  Those claims would require their own layer-matched contacts and their own controlled statistics.  The finite verdict is only that the yeast measured-dictionary design leaves a positive, permutation-separated tRNA-supply increment on the declared residual protein-abundance target.
