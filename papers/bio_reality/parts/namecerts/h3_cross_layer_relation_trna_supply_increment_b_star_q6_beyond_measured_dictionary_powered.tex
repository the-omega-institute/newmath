\subsection{A yeast tRNA-supply increment beyond the measured translation dictionary}
\label{sec:bioreality-trna-supply-increment-beyond-measured-dictionary}

\paragraph{Finite panel.}
This chapter records a finite yeast cross-layer calculation for the conjecture that a tRNA-supply coordinate can still improve protein-abundance prediction after the measured translation dictionary has already been placed in the model.  The response is log protein abundance after residualization against the sibling sequence controls $Z$: amino-acid composition, CDS length, GC3, and the codon-support density term used for the projected $B^*_{Q6}$ coordinates.  The active panel contains $3290$ joined yeast protein rows.  The base design uses the measured dictionary columns
$\log_{10}(\mathrm{measured\ TE})$, $\log_{10}(\mathrm{mRNA\ stability})$, and turnover, each $Z$-residualized and standardized.  The full design appends the $Z$-residualized standardized per-gene tAI geometric mean.  The tRNA-supply table contributing this appended coordinate contains $61$ codon weights, $268$ usable tAI records, and $3290$ joined per-gene tAI values, with no zero raw weights requiring geometric-mean filling.

\paragraph{Prediction statistic.}
The statistic is the five-fold held-out change in $R^2$ from the full design over the measured-dictionary base design.  The folds are deterministic, obtained by index modulo $5$ over sorted active protein identifiers.  The fitted ridge constant is $10^{-8}$, so the reported comparison is a rank-stabilized finite-row comparison rather than an unconstrained interpolation.  The observed values are
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{\mathrm{base}} &= 0.4579960005021211,\\
R^2_{\mathrm{full}} &= 0.5186044342149576,\\
\Delta R^2_{\mathrm{held\ out}} &= 0.06060843371283653,\\
\Delta R^2_{\mathrm{in\ sample}} &= 0.06016972939113374,\\
R^2_{\mathrm{tAI\ alone}} &= 0.33369275999817194.
\end{aligned}
$$
The held-out and in-sample increments are nearly the same on this finite panel, and the held-out increment is positive by $0.06060843371283653$.  The permutation null keeps the readouts and folds fixed and permutes gene-to-target labels using deterministic SHA256 permutations.  Across $120$ permutations, the null summary is
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{null}_{\min} &= -0.00510268312590334,\\
\mathrm{null}_{\mathrm{mean}} &= -0.0004019510944510909,\\
\mathrm{null}_{95} &= 0.0007222485676979806,\\
\mathrm{null}_{\max} &= 0.0010291561244795133.
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the observed held-out increment exceeds the recorded null $95$ percent boundary by
$$
\begin{aligned}
0.06060843371283653 - 0.0007222485676979806
  &= 0.05988618514513855.
\end{aligned}
$$
This is the local positive finding: within the finite yeast join, the tRNA-supply coordinate carries held-out predictive information for residual protein abundance beyond the measured dictionary made from measured translation efficiency, mRNA stability, and turnover.

\paragraph{Relation to the measured translation and stability dictionary.}
The dictionary being conditioned away is not empty.  In the matched yeast translation--stability--protein calculation, the four-way join contains $4759$ rows, and the residual degrees of freedom after $Z$ are $4736$.  For the joint $B^*_{Q6}$ protein-abundance decomposition on that panel,
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{QP} &= 0.2715520808117803,\\
R^2_{QP\mid T} &= 0.1439989545940314,\\
R^2_{QP\mid M} &= 0.25453612246767165,\\
R^2_{QP\mid T,M} &= 0.13047582645538838.
\end{aligned}
$$
The same computation reports a measured-translation-only fraction of $0.4697188319693239$, a stability-only fraction of $0.0626618595344251$, and a joint fraction of $0.5195182225621592$, with right-tail permutation value $0.000999000999000999$.  The overlap diagnostic is approximately independent at tolerance $0.05$, since the joint-minus-single-sum value is $-0.012862468941589822$.  These numbers matter because the present tRNA-supply increment is not being read against an absent or weak dictionary; it is tested after measured translation and stability summaries have already absorbed a substantial portion of the $B^*_{Q6}$ to protein-abundance association.

\paragraph{Measured-TE mediation boundary.}
A separate measured-TE protein-abundance computation gives the yeast three-way measured-TE join as $5128$ rows.  In that calculation the measured mediation fraction is $0.4793069656345167$, with
$$
\begin{aligned}
S^{QP} &= 0.3146818503437042,\\
S^{QT}_{\mathrm{measured}} &= 0.13681931343730436.
\end{aligned}
$$
The corresponding residual design has $5105$ residual degrees of freedom after $Z$, $\operatorname{rank}(Z)=23$, $\operatorname{rank}(X)=9$, $\operatorname{rank}(T)=1$, and $\operatorname{rank}(X,T)=10$.  This supports the interpretation that the measured translation readout is a real finite-row component of the dictionary.  The present chapter then asks a narrower question: after that measured dictionary is included together with mRNA stability and turnover, does the tRNA-supply coordinate still add held-out predictive power for residual protein abundance?  The answer on the recorded yeast panel is yes, by $\Delta R^2_{\mathrm{held\ out}}=0.06060843371283653$.

\paragraph{Classification of the finite result.}
The local classification is a bounded cross-layer predictive increment.  It is not a statement that tAI is a direct ribosome-occupancy measurement, and it is not a statement that protein abundance has been explained mechanistically.  The full-design improvement says only that the tRNA-supply coordinate remains useful after the finite measured dictionary has been entered.  Formally, the chapter-level readout is the inequality
$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta R^2_{\mathrm{held\ out}}
  &= R^2_{\mathrm{full}} - R^2_{\mathrm{base}}\\
  &= 0.5186044342149576 - 0.4579960005021211\\
  &= 0.06060843371283653\\
  &> 0.0007222485676979806 = \mathrm{null}_{95}.
\end{aligned}
$$
Because the null model preserves the measured readouts and fold structure, the positive result is not a mere consequence of adding one more standardized column to a fixed response.  It is a finite-row held-out increment over a fixed dictionary, with the target, folds, permutation seed, ridge value, and tRNA-supply construction all recorded.

\paragraph{Local non-promotion rule.}
No translation mechanism, folding state, physical admissibility, molecular function, phenotype, adaptation law, or universal biological principle follows from this chapter.  Measured TE remains a condition-specific translation-efficiency readout; mRNA stability and turnover remain measured or derived covariates; tAI remains a modeled tRNA-supply coordinate.  The positive held-out increment supports only the finite statement that the tRNA-supply coordinate improves prediction of yeast residual protein abundance beyond the measured dictionary on the joined rows.  Any claim about ribosome dynamics, protein folding, protein state, functional realization, perturbational causation, organismal phenotype, or universality would require a separate layer-specific contact and a separately reported statistic.
