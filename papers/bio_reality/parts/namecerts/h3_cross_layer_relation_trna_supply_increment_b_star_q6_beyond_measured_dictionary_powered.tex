\subsection{Yeast tRNA-supply increment beyond the measured translation dictionary}\label{sec:bioreality-q6-trna-supply-increment}\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite panel.} This record asks whether a modeled tRNA-supply coordinate still carries held-out information about protein abundance after the measured dictionary has already been placed in the design.  The finite panel is the yeast matched join with $n=3290$ active protein rows.  The response is log$_{10}$ protein abundance after residualization against the sibling control block $Z$.  The measured dictionary block contains three standardized and $Z$-residualized columns: log$_{10}$ measured translation efficiency, log$_{10}$ mRNA stability, and turnover.  The full block appends one additional standardized and $Z$-residualized per-gene tAI geometric-mean coordinate.  The tRNA-supply computation used $61$ codon weights, joined $3290$ per-gene tAI records, used $268$ usable tAI source records, and required no geometric-mean fill for zero raw weights.

\paragraph{Held-out increment.} The regression comparison was evaluated with five deterministic folds, assigning rows by sorted active protein identifier modulo $5$, and with ridge parameter $10^{-8}$.  On held-out rows the measured dictionary alone gives
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{\mathrm{base}} &= 0.4579960005021211,
\end{aligned}
$$
whereas the dictionary plus the tAI supply coordinate gives
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{\mathrm{full}} &= 0.5186044342149576.
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the held-out increment is
$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta R^2_{\mathrm{heldout}} &= R^2_{\mathrm{full}}-R^2_{\mathrm{base}} \\
&= 0.06060843371283653.
\end{aligned}
$$
The same comparison computed only as an in-sample context check gives $0.06016972939113374$, so the held-out and context-only increments agree at the reported precision scale without using the context value as the evidential statistic.  The tAI-only held-out readout is also substantial, with $R^2=0.33369275999817194$, but the claim here is not that tAI alone explains abundance.  The claim is narrower: after the measured translation, mRNA-stability, and turnover dictionary has been included, the per-gene tAI supply coordinate still adds a positive held-out increment.

\paragraph{Permutation boundary.} The null model fixes the readouts and folds and then applies deterministic SHA256 permutations of gene-to-target labels.  Across $120$ permutations the null distribution for the held-out full-minus-base statistic has mean $-0.0004019510944510909$, minimum $-0.00510268312590334$, maximum $0.0010291561244795133$, and $95$th percentile $0.0007222485676979806$.  The observed held-out increment is therefore far outside the reported permutation envelope:
$$
\begin{aligned}
0.06060843371283653 &> 0.0007222485676979806.
\end{aligned}
$$
The deterministic seed for this null surface is \texttt{sha256:b\_star\_q6\_trna\_supply\_increment\_powered:fixed-readout-target-permutation}.  The comparison is a finite-row predictive increment and not a statement about a universal biological rule.

\paragraph{Relation to the measured translation-protein surface.} The same audit family contains a measured translation-protein abundance decomposition in yeast with $5128$ joined measured-TE/protein/CDS rows.  In that measured-readout surface the yeast values are $S^{QP}=0.3146818503437042$, $S^{QT}_{\mathrm{meas}}=0.13681931343730436$, and measured mediated fraction $0.4793069656345167$, with residual degrees of freedom after $Z$ equal to $5105$.  A stricter joint TE-plus-stability yeast join reports $4759$ rows, $R^2_{QP}=0.2715520808117803$, $R^2_{QP\mid T}=0.1439989545940314$, $R^2_{QP\mid M}=0.25453612246767165$, $R^2_{QP\mid T,M}=0.13047582645538838$, and joint mediation fraction $0.5195182225621592$ with right-tail permutation probability $0.000999000999000999$.  These companion numbers explain why the dictionary block is treated as a measured context rather than as an empty baseline: translation efficiency and RNA stability already absorb a large part of the Q6-to-protein-abundance surface before the tAI supply coordinate is tested.

\paragraph{Local readout.} The ready result is therefore the following finite-row statement.  On the yeast matched join, after amino-acid composition, GC3, CDS length, M-density, measured translation efficiency, mRNA stability, and turnover have been accounted for in the declared residualized design, the per-gene modeled tAI supply coordinate contributes a held-out protein-abundance increment of $0.06060843371283653$, compared with a permutation $95$th percentile of $0.0007222485676979806$.  This supports a bounded cross-layer predictive relation between the Q6 residual setting, measured dictionary covariates, and tRNA-supply readback on this panel.

\paragraph{Claim boundary.} The statistic does not by itself show ribosome occupancy, translation mechanism, folding, physical admissibility, protein function, phenotype, causal mediation, or universality.  Modeled tAI is a supply-weight readout, not a ribosome-profiling measurement.  Protein abundance is a downstream quantitative surface, not evidence for protein state, localization, post-translational modification, structural order, or biological function.  Any such stronger assertion would require a separate layer-matched contact and its own finite statistic.
