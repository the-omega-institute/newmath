\subsection{Yeast tRNA-supply increment beyond the measured translation dictionary}
\label{sec:bioreality-trna-supply-increment-beyond-measured-dictionary}

\paragraph{Finite row object.}
The finite object in this packet is a yeast joined design with $3290$ active protein rows.  The response is $\log_{10}$ protein abundance after residualization against the sibling control design $Z$, and the measured dictionary consists of three standardized residual readouts: $\log_{10}$ measured translation efficiency, $\log_{10}$ mRNA stability, and turnover.  The candidate increment is a separately residualized and standardized per-gene tAI geometric mean computed from a finite codon-weight table with $61$ codon weights.  The tRNA-supply computation used $268$ usable tAI records, joined $3290$ per-gene tAI rows, and required no geometric-mean fill for zero raw weights.  The regression uses five deterministic folds, formed by sorted active protein identifiers modulo $5$, with ridge parameter $10^{-8}$.

\paragraph{Controlled increment statistic.}
Let $Y$ denote the residualized yeast protein-abundance target, let $D$ denote the measured dictionary matrix with columns $\log_{10}(\mathrm{TE})$, $\log_{10}(\mathrm{mRNA\ stability})$, and turnover, and let $A$ denote the residualized tAI supply coordinate.  The reported finite-row statistic is the held-out gain from adding $A$ to $D$:
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{\mathrm{base}} &= 0.4579960005021211,\\
R^2_{\mathrm{full}} &= 0.5186044342149576,\\
\Delta R^2_{\mathrm{held\ out}} &= 0.06060843371283653.
\end{aligned}
$$
The same comparison inside the fitted context gives an in-sample context-only increment of $0.06016972939113374$, close to the held-out value.  The tAI coordinate alone has $R^2=0.33369275999817194$ against the same residualized protein-abundance target, but the claim here is not the marginal tAI association; it is the residual gain after the measured dictionary has already been supplied.

\paragraph{Permutation boundary.}
The null model keeps the readouts and the fold rule fixed, then applies deterministic SHA256 permutations of gene-to-target labels.  Across $120$ permutations the null distribution for $\Delta R^2(\mathrm{full}-\mathrm{base})$ has mean $-0.0004019510944510909$, minimum $-0.00510268312590334$, maximum $0.0010291561244795133$, and $95$th percentile $0.0007222485676979806$.  The observed held-out increment satisfies
$$
\begin{aligned}
0.06060843371283653 &> 0.0007222485676979806,\\
\frac{0.06060843371283653}{0.0007222485676979806} &\approx 83.92.
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the finite panel supports a positive predictive increment of the tRNA-supply coordinate beyond the measured TE, mRNA-stability, and turnover dictionary, under this fold rule and this permutation envelope.  The deterministic seed for the permutation surface is $\mathrm{sha256{:}b\_star\_q6\_trna\_supply\_increment\_powered{:}fixed\mbox{-}readout\mbox{-}target\mbox{-}permutation}$.

\paragraph{Relation to the measured translation panel.}
The same family of $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinates has a measured translation-protein decomposition on organism-matched panels.  In the measured TE/protein-abundance run, yeast had $5128$ joined three-way rows, $S^{QP}=0.3146818503437042$, $S^{QT}=0.13681931343730436$, and measured mediated fraction $0.4793069656345167$.  E. coli had $3415$ joined rows with $S^{QP}=0.10941535951828074$, $S^{QT}=0.009394266754822262$, and measured mediated fraction $0.04091158340950673$; human had $11554$ joined rows with $S^{QP}=0.04288464996165745$, $S^{QT}=0.018837911344127926$, and measured mediated fraction $0.0027966485036786744$; zebrafish had $13140$ joined rows with $S^{QP}=0.04926578454614652$, $S^{QT}=0.04034217614654355$, and a clipped measured mediated fraction $0.0$ from raw value $-0.04801486048721136$.  These numbers place the present yeast tRNA-supply increment inside a broader finite-row pattern: measured translation can absorb part of the protein-abundance association, but a modeled tRNA-supply coordinate still contributes predictive information in the yeast dictionary test.

\paragraph{Stability and joint mediation context.}
The yeast four-way measured TE, mRNA-stability, and protein-abundance panel used $4759$ joined rows.  In that panel, the joint residual association had $R^2_{QP}=0.2715520808117803$, $R^2_{QP\mid T}=0.1439989545940314$, $R^2_{QP\mid M}=0.25453612246767165$, and $R^2_{QP\mid T,M}=0.13047582645538838$.  The joint mediation fraction was $0.5195182225621592$, with TE-only fraction $0.4697188319693239$, stability-only fraction $0.0626618595344251$, and right-tail permutation value $0.000999000999000999$.  This context matters because the present tRNA-supply result is explicitly tested after a measured dictionary containing measured TE, mRNA stability, and turnover.  The positive held-out increment is therefore not merely a restatement of the measured TE column.

\paragraph{Biological reading.}
The supported reading is narrow: on the $3290$-row yeast join, the residualized per-gene tAI supply coordinate improves held-out prediction of residualized protein abundance by $\Delta R^2=0.06060843371283653$ beyond measured TE, mRNA stability, and turnover, and this increment is far above the finite permutation boundary.  This is a cross-layer predictive relation between a synonymous residual coordinate family, a tRNA-supply readout, and a protein-abundance target.  It does not by itself prove translation causation, ribosome occupancy, protein folding, protein physical admissibility, biochemical function, phenotype, mechanism, adaptation, or universality.  Those stronger readings require separate matched contacts and separately reported statistics.  In particular, protein abundance remains a steady-state output surface affected by translation, mRNA level, degradation, localization, measurement platform, and condition; tAI remains a modeled codon-adaptation supply coordinate rather than an observed ribosome footprint.
