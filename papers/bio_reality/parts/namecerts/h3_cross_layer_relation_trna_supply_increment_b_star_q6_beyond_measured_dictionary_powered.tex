\subsection{Yeast tRNA-supply increment after the measured readout dictionary}
\label{sec:bioreality-q6-trna-supply-increment}

\paragraph{Finite panel.}
This record tests whether a bounded tRNA-supply coordinate still predicts yeast protein abundance after the measured readout dictionary has already been placed in the regression design.  The rows are matched yeast protein identifiers with a finite join size of $n=3290$.  The target is log protein abundance after residualization against the sibling control block, and the base dictionary consists of $\log_{10}$ measured translation efficiency, $\log_{10}$ mRNA stability, and turnover, each residualized and standardized after the same control block.  The full design appends one more standardized column: the per-gene geometric-mean tAI coordinate computed from finite codon weights.  The tAI construction used $61$ codon weights, $268$ usable tAI records, $3290$ joined per-gene tAI values, and no zero raw weights filled by a geometric mean.

\paragraph{Held-out increment.}
The decisive statistic is held out rather than only in sample.  Five deterministic folds were assigned by sorted active protein identifier modulo $5$, with ridge $10^{-8}$.  On those folds, the measured dictionary alone gives
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{\mathrm{base}} &= 0.4579960005021211,
\end{aligned}
$$
while the full dictionary including the tAI coordinate gives
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{\mathrm{full}} &= 0.5186044342149576.
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the held-out increment is
$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta R^2_{\mathrm{held\ out}} &= 0.5186044342149576 - 0.4579960005021211 \\
&= 0.06060843371283653.
\end{aligned}
$$
The context-only in-sample increment is close, $0.06016972939113374$, so the effect is not merely an in-sample artifact of appending a flexible column.  The tAI coordinate by itself has $R^2=0.33369275999817194$ against the same target, but the chapter claim is narrower: the tRNA-supply coordinate retains held-out predictive information after the measured TE, mRNA-stability, and turnover dictionary has already been used.

\paragraph{Permutation boundary.}
The null comparison keeps the readouts and folds fixed and permutes gene-to-target labels by deterministic SHA256 permutations.  Across $120$ permutations, the null distribution has mean $-0.0004019510944510909$, minimum $-0.00510268312590334$, maximum $0.0010291561244795133$, and $95$th percentile $0.0007222485676979806$.  The observed held-out increment $0.06060843371283653$ is therefore far above the recorded null boundary:
$$
\begin{aligned}
0.06060843371283653 &> 0.0007222485676979806.
\end{aligned}
$$
This supports a finite-row readout statement: in the yeast join, the tRNA-supply coordinate adds measurable held-out protein-abundance prediction beyond the measured readout dictionary.  The randomization seed is fixed as \texttt{sha256:b\_star\_q6\_trna\_supply\_increment\_powered:fixed-readout-target-permutation}, so the reported null is a reproducible finite computation rather than a verbal plausibility argument.

\paragraph{Relation to measured TE and protein abundance.}
The measured translation-efficiency/protein-abundance panel supplies the relevant background scale.  In yeast, the measured-TE join contains $5128$ three-way rows before the stricter dictionary join, with $5815$ CDS rows reported and $6351$ valid abundance proteins.  On that measured-TE panel, the joint nine-coordinate residual block has $S^{QP}=0.3146818503437042$, $S^{QT}_{\mathrm{measured}}=0.13681931343730436$, and measured mediation fraction $0.4793069656345167$.  The rank diagnostics for that fit report $\operatorname{rank}(Z)=23$, $\operatorname{rank}(X)=9$, $\operatorname{rank}(T)=1$, $\operatorname{rank}(XT)=10$, residual degrees of freedom $5105$, translation residual energy $753.5781168800304$, and protein residual energy $2146.0645009193045$.  These numbers show that the dictionary used by the present increment test is not a symbolic label: it is attached to a nondegenerate measured-readout surface.

\paragraph{Stability and joint-readout context.}
The stricter four-way yeast panel that also includes mRNA stability has $n=4759$ joined rows.  In that panel, the joint TE-plus-stability decomposition reports $R^2_{QP}=0.2715520808117803$, $R^2_{QP\mid T}=0.1439989545940314$, $R^2_{QP\mid M}=0.25453612246767165$, and $R^2_{QP\mid T,M}=0.13047582645538838$.  The recorded joint mediation fraction is $0.5195182225621592$, with TE-only fraction $0.4697188319693239$, stability-only fraction $0.0626618595344251$, and right-tail permutation value $0.000999000999000999$.  Its overlap ledger classifies the single-mediator sum as approximately independent: the single-mediator sum is $0.532380691503749$ and the joint-minus-single-sum value is $-0.012862468941589822$, within the stated descriptive tolerance $0.05$.  The tRNA-supply increment is therefore read as an additional finite-row coordinate beyond this measured dictionary, not as a replacement for measured TE or stability.

\paragraph{Case assignment.}
For the four-case translation-protein discriminant, the present row belongs to the bounded protein-readout side only after the measured readout dictionary is made explicit.  It is not a direct claim that a B*$_{Q6}$ residual coordinate realizes translation by itself.  The positive held-out increment says that modeled tRNA supply contributes protein-abundance prediction after measured TE, mRNA stability, and turnover have been included.  Since the result is conditioned on the measured dictionary rather than proving that the tRNA coordinate propagates through a new measured TE step, the safest local classification is a dictionary-conditioned protein-readout increment.  It is compatible with a case-D style interpretation only where the separate measured $Q\to T$ step has already been computed and the conditioning-on-$T$ reduction is reported; outside that finite measured panel, the statement remains an increment in a controlled protein-abundance regression.

\paragraph{Local boundary.}
The chapter does not infer folding, localization, post-translational state, molecular function, phenotype, physical admissibility, mechanism, or a universal biological rule from the tAI increment.  It also does not treat modeled tAI as observed ribosome occupancy.  The finite computation licenses only this bounded assertion: on the yeast joined rows, a tRNA-supply coordinate improves held-out prediction of protein abundance beyond the measured dictionary by $\Delta R^2=0.06060843371283653$, exceeding the fixed-label-permutation $95$th percentile $0.0007222485676979806$.  Any stronger biological statement would require its own layer-matched contact and its own finite statistic.
