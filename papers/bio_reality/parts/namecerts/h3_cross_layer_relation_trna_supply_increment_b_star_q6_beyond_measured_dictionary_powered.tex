\subsection{A finite tRNA-supply increment beyond the measured yeast dictionary}
\label{sec:bioreality-trna-supply-increment-beyond-measured-dictionary}

\paragraph{Finite row object.}
The object studied here is a yeast matched-row regression packet in which the response is log protein abundance after residualization against the sibling sequence controls, and the competing readout dictionary is already allowed to contain measured translation and RNA-stability information.  The active joined panel has $n=3290$ proteins.  The base design consists of three standardized columns after the same residualization step: $\log_{10}(\mathrm{measured\ TE})$, $\log_{10}(\mathrm{mRNA\ stability})$, and turnover.  The full design appends one further standardized residual column, the per-gene tRNA-adaptation geometric mean.  The five folds are fixed by deterministic index modulo $5$ over sorted active protein identifiers, with ridge parameter $10^{-8}$.  Thus the comparison is not between codon geometry and an empty response, but between a measured readout dictionary and that same dictionary enlarged by a finite tRNA-supply coordinate.

\paragraph{Held-out increment.}
Let $Y_Z$ denote the residualized log protein-abundance target, let $D_Z$ denote the residualized measured dictionary, and let $A_Z$ denote the residualized per-gene tRNA-supply coordinate.  The reported quantities are the fold-held-out coefficients of determination
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2(D_Z;Y_Z) &= 0.4579960005021211,\\
R^2(D_Z,A_Z;Y_Z) &= 0.5186044342149576,\\
\Delta R^2_{\mathrm{heldout}} &= 0.06060843371283653.
\end{aligned}
$$
The corresponding in-sample contextual increment is $0.06016972939113374$, close to the held-out value and therefore not driving the claim by a visible train/test split artifact.  The tRNA-supply coordinate alone has $R^2=0.33369275999817194$ on the same target scale, but the chapter uses the conditional increment as the primary number because the biological question is whether tRNA supply contributes after measured translation, RNA stability, and turnover are already present.

\paragraph{Permutation boundary.}
The null surface keeps the readouts and folds fixed and applies deterministic SHA256 permutations of gene-to-target labels.  With $120$ permutations, the null distribution for $\Delta R^2(\mathrm{full}-\mathrm{base})$ has mean $-0.0004019510944510909$, minimum $-0.00510268312590334$, maximum $0.0010291561244795133$, and $95$th percentile $0.0007222485676979806$.  The observed held-out increment satisfies
$$
\begin{aligned}
0.06060843371283653 &> 0.0007222485676979806,
\end{aligned}
$$
so the finite-row verdict is that the tRNA-supply coordinate adds held-out predictive information beyond the measured dictionary on this yeast join.  The tRNA-supply construction used $61$ codon weights, joined $3290$ per-gene tAI records, used $268$ usable tRNA records, and required no geometric-mean filling for zero raw weights.

\paragraph{Relation to the measured translation-protein packet.}
The measured-TE protein-abundance audit supplies the local scale on which this increment should be read.  In yeast, the measured TE/protein join has $5128$ three-way rows and, after residualization, $5105$ degrees of freedom beyond the sequence-control design.  Its measured translation-protein decomposition reports $S^{QP}=0.3146818503437042$, $S^{QT}=0.13681931343730436$, and measured mediated fraction $0.4793069656345167$.  Separately, the joint TE and mRNA-stability audit on the four-way yeast join has $4759$ rows and reports
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{QP} &= 0.2715520808117803,\\
R^2_{QP\mid T,M} &= 0.13047582645538838,\\
\mathrm{joint\ fraction} &= 0.5195182225621592,
\end{aligned}
$$
with right-tail permutation value $0.000999000999000999$.  These numbers explain why the present chapter is deliberately conditional: the tRNA-supply coordinate is tested after a dictionary that already carries measured TE, mRNA stability, and turnover, rather than being used as a substitute for those readouts.

\paragraph{Interpretation.}
The supported finite-row statement is narrow.  On the yeast joined panel, a residualized per-gene tRNA-supply coordinate improves held-out prediction of residualized log protein abundance by about $0.0606$ in $R^2$ beyond the measured dictionary, and that increment is far above the fixed-label-permutation $95$th percentile.  This is a cross-layer predictive increment for the specified finite matrix.  It does not by itself establish translation realization, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, mechanism, phenotype, adaptation, causation, or universality.  Those stronger claims would require separate layer-matched contacts and statistics: a direct translation readout for translation realization, structural or physical assays for folding or admissibility, perturbational or functional measurements for mechanism or function, and broader controlled panels for any organism-level generality.  The present result should therefore be read as a bounded finite-row survival of tRNA-supply information beyond a measured yeast readout dictionary, not as a biological law.
