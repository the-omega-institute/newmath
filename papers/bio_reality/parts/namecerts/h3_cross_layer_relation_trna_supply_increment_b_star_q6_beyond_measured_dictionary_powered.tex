\subsection{Yeast tRNA supply after measured translation, RNA stability, and turnover controls}
\label{sec:bioreality-trna-supply-increment}

\paragraph{Finite row object.}
This record concerns a yeast matched panel in which the response is log protein abundance after residualization against the sibling sequence controls.  The tested increment is not the raw association between codon usage and abundance.  It is the additional held-out explanatory power of a per-gene tRNA-adaptation coordinate after a measured readout dictionary has already been placed in the design.  The measured dictionary is the standardized residual design with columns $\log_{10}(\mathrm{measured\ TE})$, $\log_{10}(\mathrm{mRNA\ stability})$, and turnover.  The full design appends the standardized residual per-gene tAI geometric mean.  The joined finite table has $n=3290$ active yeast protein rows, with deterministic five-fold splitting by sorted protein identifiers and ridge $10^{-8}$.

\paragraph{Coordinate and readout separation.}
Let $Y$ denote the target residual, namely yeast $\log_{10}$ protein abundance after the sequence-control regression.  Let $D$ denote the measured dictionary consisting of translation-efficiency, RNA-stability, and turnover readouts, and let $A$ denote the residual per-gene tAI geometric mean computed from codon weights.  The finite-row comparison is the held-out difference
$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta R^2_{\mathrm{heldout}} &= R^2(Y\mid D,A)-R^2(Y\mid D),\\
R^2(Y\mid D) &= 0.4579960005021211,\\
R^2(Y\mid D,A) &= 0.5186044342149576,\\
\Delta R^2_{\mathrm{heldout}} &= 0.06060843371283653.
\end{aligned}
$$
The in-sample contextual increment is $0.06016972939113374$, close to the held-out increment, while tAI alone has $R^2=0.33369275999817194$.  Thus the chapter-specific claim is not that tAI replaces measured translation, RNA stability, or turnover.  The claim is narrower: after those measured dictionary columns have already been used, a tRNA-supply coordinate still carries a finite held-out residual increment on this yeast protein-abundance target.

\paragraph{Permutation boundary.}
The null model fixes the readouts and folds, then applies deterministic SHA256 permutations of gene-to-target labels.  Across $120$ permutations, the null distribution for $\Delta R^2(\mathrm{full}-\mathrm{base})$ has mean $-0.0004019510944510909$, minimum $-0.00510268312590334$, maximum $0.0010291561244795133$, and $95$th percentile $0.0007222485676979806$.  The observed held-out increment is therefore far outside the descriptive null band:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta R^2_{\mathrm{heldout}} &= 0.06060843371283653,\\
q_{0.95}^{\mathrm{null}} &= 0.0007222485676979806,\\
\Delta R^2_{\mathrm{heldout}}-q_{0.95}^{\mathrm{null}} &= 0.05988618514513855.
\end{aligned}
$$
The seed string for the deterministic permutation family is $\mathrm{sha256{:}b\_star\_q6\_trna\_supply\_increment\_powered{:}fixed\mbox{-}readout\mbox{-}target\mbox{-}permutation}$.  The tRNA-supply construction used $61$ codon weights, joined $3290$ per-gene tAI records, used $268$ usable tAI records, and required no zero raw $W$ entries to be filled by a geometric mean.

\paragraph{Relation to measured translation and stability rows.}
The measured dictionary is meaningful because the same yeast program already supplies nondegenerate measured-readout rows.  In the measured translation-to-protein decomposition, yeast had $5128$ three-way joined rows, residual rank $\operatorname{rank}(X)=9$, residual translation rank $\operatorname{rank}(T)=1$, and residual degrees of freedom $5105$.  Its measured translation quantities were $S^{QT}=0.13681931343730436$, $S^{QP}=0.3146818503437042$, and measured mediation fraction $0.4793069656345167$.  In the joint measured-TE and mRNA-stability decomposition, the yeast four-way join had $4759$ rows, residual degrees of freedom $4736$, $R^2_{QP}=0.2715520808117803$, $R^2_{QP\mid T}=0.1439989545940314$, $R^2_{QP\mid M}=0.25453612246767165$, and $R^2_{QP\mid T,M}=0.13047582645538838$.  The corresponding joint mediation fraction was $0.5195182225621592$, with right-tail permutation value $0.000999000999000999$.  These earlier finite rows justify treating measured TE and mRNA stability as real dictionary entries for this yeast panel, not as symbolic names.

\paragraph{Structure proxy is not the source of the increment.}
A separate yeast structure-proxy decomposition used the same $4759$-row five-way join and treated the first $39$ nt coding-region RNAfold MFE as a structure readout.  Its structure-only fraction was $0.003284564452662019$, and the structure increment beyond measured TE plus mRNA stability was $0.0005507778820193332$, with permutation value $0.1258741258741259$ for the incremental structure statistic.  This keeps the present tRNA-supply increment from being read as a folding claim.  The present positive held-out increment belongs to the finite protein-abundance prediction problem after the measured dictionary, not to a structural order statement.

\paragraph{Four-case interpretation.}
For the four-case translation-protein discriminant, this row set is compatible with the case-D style pattern only at the bounded statistical level: the measured translation row is nonzero, the protein readout is nonzero, and conditioning on measured readouts reduces but does not erase the residual signal.  The present chapter then asks a different question: after the measured translation, stability, and turnover dictionary is already present, does tRNA supply still add held-out predictive power?  The answer on the finite yeast join is yes, with $\Delta R^2_{\mathrm{heldout}}=0.06060843371283653$ against a null $95$th percentile of $0.0007222485676979806$.

\paragraph{Local no-promotion rule.}
No translation mechanism follows merely from this positive increment.  No folding, physical admissibility, biological function, localization, post-translational state, phenotype, causal pathway, adaptation story, or universal biological law follows from this table.  The finite claim is limited to residual protein-abundance prediction on matched yeast rows under the stated measured dictionary, sequence controls, deterministic folds, and permutation boundary.  Any stronger biological reading would require its own layer-matched contact and its own statistic on the corresponding rows.
