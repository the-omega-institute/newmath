\subsection{Yeast tRNA-supply increment beyond the measured readout dictionary}
\label{sec:bioreality-trna-supply-increment-beyond-measured-dictionary}

\paragraph{Finite-row object.}
This record tests whether an organism-matched tRNA-supply coordinate retains predictive mass for yeast protein abundance after the measured dictionary already contains translation efficiency, mRNA stability, and turnover.  The active table has $3290$ joined yeast protein rows.  The response is $\log_{10}$ protein abundance after residualization against the sibling sequence controls.  The measured dictionary is the $Z$-residualized and standardized triple
$\log_{10}(\mathrm{measured\ TE})$, $\log_{10}(\mathrm{mRNA\ stability})$, and turnover.  The enlarged design appends one additional column: the $Z$-residualized and standardized per-gene tAI geometric mean computed from the tRNA-supply table.  The tRNA-supply construction used $61$ codon weights, joined $3290$ per-gene tAI values to the active protein rows, retained $268$ usable tAI records, and required no geometric-mean replacement for zero raw weights.

\paragraph{Controlled held-out comparison.}
The comparison is a fixed five-fold prediction test over sorted active protein identifiers, with fold assignment given by deterministic index modulo $5$.  The fit uses ridge parameter $\lambda=10^{-8}$.  Let $R^2_{\mathrm{base}}$ denote the held-out coefficient of determination for the measured dictionary and let $R^2_{\mathrm{full}}$ denote the same quantity after appending the per-gene tAI coordinate.  The observed finite-row values are
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{\mathrm{base}} &= 0.4579960005021211,\\
R^2_{\mathrm{full}} &= 0.5186044342149576,\\
\Delta R^2_{\mathrm{held\ out}} &= R^2_{\mathrm{full}}-R^2_{\mathrm{base}} = 0.06060843371283653.
\end{aligned}
$$
The in-sample contextual increment is similar but not used as the promotion statistic:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta R^2_{\mathrm{in\ sample}} &= 0.06016972939113374.
\end{aligned}
$$
The isolated tAI column has a finite-row single-design value $R^2_{\mathrm{tAI\ alone}}=0.33369275999817194$, but the chapter claim is not that tAI alone explains protein abundance.  The asserted contact is narrower: after the measured dictionary has already spent the three measured readout columns, the tRNA-supply coordinate still contributes a positive held-out increment.

\paragraph{Permutation boundary.}
The null model fixes the readouts and folds and permutes gene-to-target labels by deterministic SHA256 permutations.  Across $120$ permutations, the null held-out increment distribution is
$$
\begin{aligned}
\min(\Delta R^2_{\mathrm{null}}) &= -0.00510268312590334,\\
\mathrm{mean}(\Delta R^2_{\mathrm{null}}) &= -0.0004019510944510909,\\
\mathrm{q}_{0.95}(\Delta R^2_{\mathrm{null}}) &= 0.0007222485676979806,\\
\max(\Delta R^2_{\mathrm{null}}) &= 0.0010291561244795133.
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the observed held-out gain $0.06060843371283653$ is far above the $95\%$ null boundary $0.0007222485676979806$.  The seed for the deterministic null is \texttt{sha256:b\_star\_q6\_trna\_supply\_increment\_powered:fixed-readout-target-permutation}.  With $120$ permutations this is a bounded finite-row separation, not an asymptotic sampling theorem.

\paragraph{Relation to the measured readout dictionary.}
The measured-dictionary context is essential.  In the companion measured-TE protein-abundance decomposition, yeast has $5128$ joined CDS--TE--protein rows, $S^{QP}=0.3146818503437042$, $S^{QT}=0.13681931343730436$, and measured mediation fraction $0.4793069656345167$.  In the joint measured-TE and mRNA-stability yeast table, the matched four-way join has $4759$ rows, $R^2_{QP}=0.2715520808117803$, $R^2_{QP\mid T,M}=0.13047582645538838$, joint mediation fraction $0.5195182225621592$, and right-tail permutation value $0.000999000999000999$.  Those numbers show that translation efficiency and stability already form a nontrivial measured dictionary.  The present $3290$-row test asks a different question: whether per-gene tRNA supply adds held-out protein-abundance information after that dictionary is installed.  The answer in this finite table is positive by $\Delta R^2=0.06060843371283653$.

\paragraph{Local interpretation boundary.}
The admissible conclusion is a controlled cross-layer readout relation: in this yeast join, the tRNA-supply coordinate improves held-out prediction of residualized protein abundance beyond measured translation efficiency, mRNA stability, and turnover.  The same numbers do not by themselves prove a translation mechanism, folding effect, physical admissibility, molecular function, phenotype, or universal biological law.  They also do not identify whether the extra predictive mass reflects elongation, initiation, codon-specific selection, unmeasured abundance covariates, or another confounded route.  Any such higher-layer interpretation requires a separate layer-matched contact and its own statistic.  The present chapter therefore records a finite, rank-powered protein-readout increment with a measured-dictionary control, and keeps mechanistic and universal claims outside the result.
