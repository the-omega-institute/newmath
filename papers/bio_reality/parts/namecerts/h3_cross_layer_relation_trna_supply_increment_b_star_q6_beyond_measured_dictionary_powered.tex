\subsection{A tRNA supply increment beyond the measured dictionary surface}\label{subsec:h3-cross-layer-relation-trna-supply-increment-b-star-q6-beyond-measured-dictionary-powered}\origin{ai}
\paragraph{Finite witness.} On the yeast join, the target is log10 protein abundance residualized against sibling Z controls. The baseline matrix uses the residualized and standardized measured-dictionary columns $\log_{10}(\mathrm{measured\_te})$, $\log_{10}(\mathrm{mrna\_stability})$, and turnover. The augmented matrix adds a residualized and standardized per-gene tAI geometric mean. This is a finite panel with $n_{\mathrm{join}} = 3290$ joined rows, $61$ codon weights, $268$ usable tAI records, and zero raw $W$ values filled by geometric mean. The split is deterministic, using $5$ folds given by the index modulo $5$ over sorted active protein ids, with ridge $10^{-8}$. The null model uses $120$ deterministic SHA256 permutations of gene-to-target labels.

$$\begin{aligned}
R^2_{\mathrm{base}} &= 0.4579960005021211,\\
R^2_{\mathrm{full}} &= 0.5186044342149576,\\
\Delta R^2_{\mathrm{held\text{-}out}} &= 0.06060843371283653,\\
\Delta R^2_{\mathrm{context\text{-}only}} &= 0.06016972939113374,\\
R^2_{\mathrm{tAI\ alone}} &= 0.33369275999817194,\\
\mathrm{null}_{95} &= 0.0007222485676979806.
\end{aligned}$$
Across the permutation envelope, the null mean is $-0.0004019510944510909$, with range from $-0.00510268312590334$ to $0.0010291561244795133$. The held-out increment is positive, and the observed gain is far above the null band.

\paragraph{Readback.} The concrete readback is simple: adding the tRNA-supply surface to the measured dictionary baseline strengthens the residual fit on the joined protein-abundance target. The improvement is not symbolic; it is a finite cross-validated increment on the exact joined panel. The claim therefore has a real data footprint at the level of abundance residualization, not just a formal coding convenience.

\paragraph{Boundary.} This witness stays at the level of a measured, residualized abundance relation. It does not, by itself, establish translation, protein folding, physical admissibility, function, mechanism, or universal behavior. Those higher-layer statements need their own matched contact and their own statistic. Here the supported statement is narrower: a tRNA-supply increment beyond the measured dictionary baseline improves prediction of residual protein abundance on the yeast join, and it does so with a held-out gain of $0.06060843371283653$ against a $0.0007222485676979806$ null threshold.
