\subsection{Yeast tRNA-supply increment beyond the measured translation dictionary}
\label{sec:bioreality-q6-trna-supply-increment}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Question.}
This record tests whether a modeled tRNA-supply coordinate adds held-out protein-abundance information after the measured readout dictionary has already been supplied.  The finite carrier is the yeast join on active protein identifiers for which codon counts, protein abundance, measured translation efficiency, mRNA-stability readout, turnover readout, and per-gene tAI can all be placed in the same row.  The target is log10 protein abundance after residualization against the sibling control block $Z$, and the competing designs are a measured dictionary and the same dictionary augmented by a tRNA-supply summary.

\paragraph{Finite design.}
The measured dictionary is the $Z$-residualized and standardized matrix with columns log10 measured TE, log10 mRNA stability, and turnover.  The augmented design appends the $Z$-residualized standardized per-gene tAI geometric mean.  The joined panel contains $3290$ usable rows.  The fold rule is deterministic: active protein ids are sorted and assigned to $5$ folds by index modulo $5$.  The ridge value is $10^{-8}$, so the reported comparison is a stabilized held-out linear prediction comparison rather than an unconstrained interpolation score.

\paragraph{Readout statistic.}
Let $R^2_{\mathrm{base}}$ denote held-out predictive power for the measured dictionary and $R^2_{\mathrm{full}}$ the corresponding value after adding the tRNA-supply coordinate.  The computed finite-row values are
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{\mathrm{base}} &= 0.4579960005021211,\\
R^2_{\mathrm{full}} &= 0.5186044342149576,\\
\Delta R^2_{\mathrm{held\ out}} &= 0.06060843371283653.
\end{aligned}
$$
The in-sample contextual increment is $0.06016972939113374$, close to the held-out increment, while the tAI-alone score is $0.33369275999817194$.  Thus the result is not merely that tAI is individually predictive; the finite-row claim is that the modeled tRNA-supply coordinate still contributes after the measured TE, stability, and turnover dictionary has already been made available.

\paragraph{Permutation boundary.}
The null model keeps the readouts and folds fixed and applies deterministic SHA256 permutations to the gene-to-target labels.  Across $120$ permutations, the null mean is $-0.0004019510944510909$, the null minimum is $-0.00510268312590334$, the null maximum is $0.0010291561244795133$, and the $95\%$ null cutoff is $0.0007222485676979806$.  The observed held-out increment satisfies
$$
\begin{aligned}
0.06060843371283653 &> 0.0007222485676979806,
\end{aligned}
$$
so the increment is well outside the recorded permutation boundary.  The computation uses the deterministic seed sha256:b\_star\_q6\_trna\_supply\_increment\_powered:fixed-readout-target-permutation.  The tRNA-supply construction reports $61$ codon weights, $3290$ joined per-gene tAI records, $268$ usable tAI records, and $0$ zero raw weights filled by the geometric mean.

\paragraph{Relation to measured translation and stability rows.}
The measured-TE protein-abundance run supplies the local context for the dictionary term: in yeast, the matched measured-TE/protein-abundance join used $5128$ three-way rows before the stricter stability and turnover requirements of the present increment test.  In that measured-TE decomposition, yeast had $S^{QP}=0.3146818503437042$, measured $S^{QT}=0.13681931343730436$, and measured mediated fraction $0.4793069656345167$.  In the joint measured-TE plus mRNA-stability decomposition, the yeast four-way join contained $4759$ rows, with $R^2_{QP}=0.2715520808117803$, $R^2_{QP\mid T}=0.1439989545940314$, $R^2_{QP\mid M}=0.25453612246767165$, and $R^2_{QP\mid T,M}=0.13047582645538838$.  Those values define the measured dictionary context; the present result asks whether the tRNA-supply coordinate still leaves an additional held-out trace after such measured readout classes are included.

\paragraph{Classification.}
The finite computation supports a cross-layer relation at the level of controlled protein-abundance prediction in the yeast matched panel.  Its positive content is narrow: the per-gene tAI geometric mean gives a held-out $
\Delta R^2$ of $0.06060843371283653$ beyond the measured dictionary under the stated folds, ridge, residualization, and permutation null.  This is a row-level statistical survival result for the chosen protein-abundance target.  It should not be rewritten as a claim that tAI is observed ribosome occupancy, that measured TE has captured every translation process, or that the tRNA-supply term is a direct biochemical cause.

\paragraph{Local no-promotion rule.}
No claim about translation realization, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, phenotype, mechanism, or universality follows from this chapter by itself.  Measured TE, mRNA stability, turnover, protein abundance, and modeled tAI are distinct contacts and distinct numerical columns.  The present chapter only records that, in the finite yeast join, the modeled tRNA-supply coordinate improves held-out prediction of residualized log10 protein abundance beyond the measured dictionary.  Any stronger biological interpretation requires a separate layer-matched contact and a separate statistic for that layer.
