\subsection{A residual tRNA-supply increment beyond the measured readout dictionary}
\label{sec:bioreality-q6-trna-supply-increment}

\paragraph{Question.}
This record tests whether a per-gene tRNA-supply coordinate still carries held-out information about yeast protein abundance after the measured readout dictionary has already been placed in the regression surface.  The dictionary used here is not a symbolic proxy: its columns are the $Z$-residualized and standardized measured quantities $\log_{10}(\mathrm{TE})$, $\log_{10}(\mathrm{mRNA\ stability})$, and turnover.  The augmented surface adds one further $Z$-residualized and standardized coordinate, the per-gene tAI geometric mean.  The response is $\log_{10}$ protein abundance after residualization against the sibling control block $Z$ on the yeast join.  The finite panel has $n=3290$ active joined protein rows, and the fold rule is deterministic: sorted active protein identifiers are assigned by index modulo $5$.

\paragraph{Finite-row statistic.}
The base dictionary explains held-out protein-abundance residuals with
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{\mathrm{base}} &= 0.4579960005021211,\\
R^2_{\mathrm{full}} &= 0.5186044342149576,\\
\Delta R^2_{\mathrm{held\ out}} &= 0.06060843371283653.
\end{aligned}
$$
The corresponding in-sample context-only increment is $0.06016972939113374$, so the held-out increment is not a training-only artifact in this finite computation.  The tAI coordinate alone has $R^2=0.33369275999817194$ against the same target, but the claim made here is narrower: after the measured dictionary is already present, adding tAI improves the five-fold held-out surface by $0.06060843371283653$.

\paragraph{Null comparison.}
The null model keeps the readouts and fold assignments fixed and permutes gene-to-target labels by deterministic SHA256 permutations.  Across $120$ permutations, the null distribution has mean $-0.0004019510944510909$, minimum $-0.00510268312590334$, maximum $0.0010291561244795133$, and $95$th percentile $0.0007222485676979806$.  Thus the observed held-out increment is larger than the reported null $95$th percentile by
$$
\begin{aligned}
0.06060843371283653 - 0.0007222485676979806 &= 0.05988618514513855.
\end{aligned}
$$
The ridge parameter is $10^{-8}$, the deterministic seed is \texttt{sha256:b\_star\_q6\_trna\_supply\_increment\_powered:fixed-readout-target-permutation}, and the computed tRNA-supply table contains $61$ codon weights, $268$ usable tAI records, $3290$ joined per-gene tAI values, and no zero raw $W$ values requiring geometric-mean filling.

\paragraph{Relation to measured translation and protein readouts.}
This result should be read beside the measured-TE protein-abundance decomposition rather than substituted for it.  In the four-organism measured-TE run, the same nine $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinates were used: \texttt{K\_AAA}, \texttt{Arg\_AGR}, \texttt{Ile\_AUA}, \texttt{Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR}, \texttt{Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG}, \texttt{Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY}, \texttt{Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG}, \texttt{Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY}, and \texttt{f3\_stress}.  For yeast, the measured-TE/protein join had $5128$ three-way rows before the stricter dictionary join used above, with $S^{QP}=0.3146818503437042$, measured $S^{QT}=0.13681931343730436$, and a measured mediation fraction $0.4793069656345167$.  The other measured-TE fractions were smaller: $0.04091158340950673$ for \textit{E. coli}, $0.0027966485036786744$ for human, and $0$ for danio after truncating a negative raw value of $-0.04801486048721136$.  These numbers show why the present chapter is not merely repeating the earlier measured-TE decomposition: it asks whether tRNA supply still improves held-out prediction after measured TE, mRNA stability, and turnover have already been included.

\paragraph{Stability and mediation context.}
The same yeast residual coordinate family also appears in the mRNA-stability mediation computation.  There the yeast three-way join had $5085$ rows, $R^2_{QP}=0.2761140373962526$, $R^2_{QP\mid M}=0.24851799532219632$, absorbed $R^2=0.027596042074056304$, and a stability mediation fraction $0.09994436477872035$ with permutation right-tail value $0.000999000999000999$.  In the joint measured-TE plus mRNA-stability computation, yeast had $4759$ joined rows, $R^2_{QP}=0.2715520808117803$, $R^2_{QP\mid T}=0.1439989545940314$, $R^2_{QP\mid M}=0.25453612246767165$, and $R^2_{QP\mid T,M}=0.13047582645538838$.  The joint mediation fraction was $0.5195182225621592$, with TE-only fraction $0.4697188319693239$, stability-only fraction $0.0626618595344251$, and right-tail permutation value $0.000999000999000999$.  Against that background, the present held-out tAI increment is a residual supply-side signal beyond the measured dictionary, not a replacement for the measured translation or RNA-stability surfaces.

\paragraph{Interpretation.}
The supported finite-row conclusion is that, on the yeast join used here, adding the per-gene tAI geometric mean to the measured dictionary produces a positive held-out increment in protein-abundance residual prediction.  The increment is $0.06060843371283653$, while the fixed-fold permutation $95$th percentile is only $0.0007222485676979806$.  This is evidence for a bounded predictive relation between the tRNA-supply coordinate and the protein-abundance residual after the stated measured readouts have been controlled.  It is not evidence that the tAI coordinate is an observed ribosome-occupancy measurement, and it is not by itself a causal translation mechanism.

\paragraph{Local boundary.}
No stronger biological layer follows from this statistic alone.  The chapter does not infer translation realization, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, phenotype, mechanism, or universality from the $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinate or from the tAI increment.  Translation-level statements require their own matched measured translation contact or an explicitly bounded modeled-readout statement; structure, localization, stability, function, perturbation, phenotype, and physical admissibility each require their own layer-matched rows and statistic.  Protein abundance here is a measured downstream readout, and the reported increment remains a finite predictive surface on that readout.
