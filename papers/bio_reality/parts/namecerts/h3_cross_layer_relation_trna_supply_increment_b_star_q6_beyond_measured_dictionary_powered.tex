\subsection{tRNA supply increment beyond the measured translation dictionary}\label{subsec:trna-supply-increment-beyond-measured-dictionary}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite row object.}
This record isolates a yeast finite-row question: after the protein-abundance target has already been read through measured translation efficiency, measured mRNA stability, and turnover, does an explicitly computed tRNA-supply coordinate still improve held-out prediction?  The carrier is the active yeast join of $n=3290$ protein identifiers.  The target is log protein abundance after residualization against the sibling sequence controls.  The base dictionary is the standardized residual design with columns $\log_{10}(\mathrm{TE}_{\mathrm{meas}})$, $\log_{10}(\mathrm{mRNA\ stability})$, and turnover.  The extended design appends one standardized residualized per-gene tAI geometric mean computed from the codon-weight table.

The finite computation reports $5$ deterministic folds, assigned by index modulo $5$ after sorting the active protein identifiers.  The ridge parameter is $10^{-8}$, so the numerical fit is regularized only to stabilize finite matrix inversion rather than to impose a large prior.  The tRNA-supply table has $61$ codon weights, $268$ usable tAI records, $3290$ per-gene tAI rows joined into the active panel, and no zero raw weights filled by the geometric-mean fallback.

\paragraph{Measured dictionary and increment.}
Let $Y$ denote the residual protein-abundance target, let $D$ denote the measured dictionary, and let $A$ denote the residualized per-gene tAI geometric mean.  The reported finite-row scores are
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2(Y\sim D) &= 0.4579960005021211,\\
R^2(Y\sim D,A) &= 0.5186044342149576,\\
\Delta R^2_{\mathrm{held\ out}} &= 0.06060843371283653.
\end{aligned}
$$
The corresponding in-sample context-only increment is $0.06016972939113374$, close to the held-out increment and not larger in the direction that would suggest an obvious fold artifact.  In the same run the tAI-only score is $R^2=0.33369275999817194$.  Thus the tRNA-supply coordinate is not merely a replacement for the measured dictionary: it carries a measurable additional component when appended after measured TE, mRNA stability, and turnover.

\paragraph{Permutation boundary.}
The null model fixes the readouts and fold assignment, then applies deterministic SHA256 permutations of gene-to-target labels.  The statistic is the held-out difference $R^2(D,A)-R^2(D)$.  Across $120$ permutations the null summary is
$$
\begin{aligned}
\min(\Delta R^2_{\mathrm{null}}) &= -0.00510268312590334,\\
\mathrm{mean}(\Delta R^2_{\mathrm{null}}) &= -0.0004019510944510909,\\
\mathrm{null}_{95} &= 0.0007222485676979806,\\
\max(\Delta R^2_{\mathrm{null}}) &= 0.0010291561244795133.
\end{aligned}
$$
The observed held-out increment $0.06060843371283653$ is above both the listed $95\%$ null boundary and the largest listed null draw.  Since the permutation count is $120$, the finite audit can say that no listed permutation reaches the observed statistic; it should not be reworded as an asymptotic biological law.

\paragraph{Relation to the measured translation and stability layers.}
The same yeast branch has already separated several neighboring readouts.  In the measured-TE protein-abundance run, yeast has $n=5128$ joined measured TE/protein/CDS rows, $S^{QP}=0.3146818503437042$, $S^{QT}_{\mathrm{meas}}=0.13681931343730436$, and a measured mediation fraction $0.4793069656345167$.  In the joint TE and mRNA-stability decomposition on the stricter yeast join, $n=4759$, with
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{QP} &= 0.2715520808117803,\\
R^2_{QP\mid T} &= 0.1439989545940314,\\
R^2_{QP\mid M} &= 0.25453612246767165,\\
R^2_{QP\mid T,M} &= 0.13047582645538838.
\end{aligned}
$$
That run reports a joint mediation fraction $0.5195182225621592$, a TE-only fraction $0.4697188319693239$, a stability-only fraction $0.0626618595344251$, and a right-tail permutation value $0.000999000999000999$.  The present tRNA-supply increment is therefore placed after the measured dictionary rather than in place of it: it asks whether a tRNA-copy-derived codon adaptation coordinate remains predictive once those measured columns are already present.

The mRNA-stability-only yeast decomposition uses $n=5085$ rows and reports $R^2_{QP}=0.2761140373962526$, $R^2_{QP\mid M}=0.24851799532219632$, mediation fraction $0.09994436477872035$, and right-tail permutation value $0.000999000999000999$.  The 5-prime CDS MFE audit on the yeast five-way join reports $n=4759$, $R^2_{QP}=0.2715520808117803$, $R^2_{QP\mid S}=0.2706601505000995$, $R^2_{QP\mid T,M}=0.13773967696311287$, $R^2_{QP\mid T,M,S}=0.13759011208318542$, structure-only fraction $0.003284564452662019$, and structure-incremental fraction $0.0005507778820193332$.  These neighboring values keep the present chapter narrow: the powered tRNA-supply term is a residual predictive increment beyond a measured translation/stability/turnover dictionary, not a folded-structure result.

\paragraph{Local conclusion.}
The finite-row verdict is positive for the stated statistical readout.  On $3290$ yeast rows, adding residualized per-gene tAI to the measured dictionary raises held-out $R^2$ from $0.4579960005021211$ to $0.5186044342149576$, with observed increment $0.06060843371283653$ against a $120$-draw deterministic permutation null whose $95\%$ boundary is $0.0007222485676979806$ and whose maximum is $0.0010291561244795133$.  The result supports a bounded cross-layer relation between the B-star-Q6 residual coordinate family, the measured translation/stability dictionary, and a tRNA-supply readout on the matched yeast protein-abundance target.

\paragraph{No-promotion boundary.}
This chapter does not infer a translation mechanism from the tAI column, does not treat modeled tAI as observed ribosome occupancy, and does not infer folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, phenotype, localization, post-translational state, or universality from the held-out protein-abundance increment.  The measured dictionary columns, the tRNA-supply coordinate, and the protein-abundance target are finite readouts in one controlled regression design.  Any stronger biological claim would require its own matched contact and its own finite statistic.
