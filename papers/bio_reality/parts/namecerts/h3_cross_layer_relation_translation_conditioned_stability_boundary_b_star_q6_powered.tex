\subsection{Translation-conditioned stability boundary for $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinates}
\label{sec:bior-reality-q6-translation-conditioned-stability-boundary}

\paragraph{Finite panel and gate.}
This record treats $B^*_{Q6}$ as a finite family of synonymous residual coordinates rather than as a direct biological cause.  The admissible panel consists of coding-sequence rows carrying amino-acid-quotient residualized $B^*_{Q6}$ coordinates, matched translation readouts, matched mRNA controls, and a downstream protein-stability or degradation-rate surface when such a surface can be joined at the same organismal scale.  The gate is ordered: a coordinate $q_i$ is first tested against a translation readout $T_j$ under amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, CDS length, $M$-density, and mRNA controls; only after that translation step is present may a downstream stability readout be interpreted.  The downstream number is therefore a translation-conditioned residual association, not a statement that codon topology directly determines folding, degradation, function, phenotype, or an organism-wide law.

\paragraph{Joined stability rows.}
The computed stability boundary used three organism-level joins with a minimum joined-row threshold of $300$ and five held-out folds.  The finite readout surface was organism-specific: \textit{Escherichia coli} K-12 MG1655 contributed $2517$ joined rows with mRNA half-life as the primary readout; \textit{Homo sapiens} contributed $11322$ mRNA half-life rows and $6697$ protein-turnover rows, with protein turnover used as the primary readout; and \textit{Saccharomyces cerevisiae} contributed $4759$ mRNA half-life rows and $3537$ protein-turnover rows, again with protein turnover used as the primary readout.  The run was recorded under seed \texttt{sha256:1deaaafe98cd16982d810af72f6389a20be0dc19cbd46bc966593dd507bad122}.  Across the three organism entries, the unconditioned $B^*_{Q6}$-to-stability predictive surface was positive in all three cases, with summary
$$
\begin{aligned}
\min &= 0.018837736273789707,\\
\operatorname{median} &= 0.027727034742559664,\\
\operatorname{mean} &= 0.02753157569086806,\\
\max &= 0.03602995605625481.
\end{aligned}
$$
This establishes a finite association surface before the translation gate is applied, but it is not yet a mediated stability claim.

\paragraph{Translation-conditioned readback.}
After full controls and conditioning on the matched translation readout, the aggregate stability increment was weak and mixed.  The full-control change in predictive value had $n=3$, with two negative entries and one positive entry:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta R^2_{\mathrm{full},\min} &= -0.004953854025603666,\\
\Delta R^2_{\mathrm{full},\operatorname{median}} &= -0.0033500708066446228,\\
\Delta R^2_{\mathrm{full},\operatorname{mean}} &= -0.0025553576666936064,\\
\Delta R^2_{\mathrm{full},\max} &= 0.0006378518321674687.
\end{aligned}
$$
The row-weighted full-control value was
$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta R^2_{\mathrm{weighted}}=-0.001572140012981015,
\end{aligned}
$$
and the sign-flip test for a positive full-control effect gave
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{>0}=0.946441650390625.
\end{aligned}
$$
The permuted full-control comparison was uniformly negative, with $n=3$, three negative entries, no positive entry, minimum $-0.004840148172275405$, median $-0.002926202164063474$, mean $-0.002753469686061827$, and maximum $-0.0004940587218466019$.  The organism calls were split into two partial entries and one mediated-blocked null entry.  The finite verdict is therefore \emph{partial}: the data contain a positive pre-conditioning residual association with stability-like readouts, but the translation-conditioned boundary does not support a stable positive mediated effect across the joined organisms.

\paragraph{Relation to nearby $Q6$ surfaces.}
The same family of $B^*_{Q6}$ residuals behaves differently on adjacent surfaces.  A structural-order boundary over two organisms reported positive full-control order increments with $n=2$, minimum $0.002688284523492457$, median and mean $0.005284160876443678$, maximum $0.007880037229394898$, weighted value $0.004764742175882462$, and sign-flip value $p_{>0}=0.0029296875$.  In the corresponding structural-order contact, \textit{E. coli} joined $3739$ codon rows to $3736$ matched proteins with hit rate $0.999198$, while yeast joined $5815$ codon rows to $5804$ matched proteins with hit rate $0.998108$; both organisms retained rank $9$ for the projected $q$ coordinates after a rank-$23$ control design.  The stability boundary should therefore not be collapsed into the structural-order boundary: the latter crosses its finite readout in the supplied data, whereas the stability test remains only partial after translation conditioning.

\paragraph{Compressibility background.}
A broader residual-compressibility test also argues against treating the measured downstream readouts as a complete explanation of $P_Q$.  The measured low-dimensional readout dictionary left most residual energy in place: \textit{Danio rerio} had $DL_{D_o}=1$, raw baseline dimension $17$, $R_{\mathrm{final}}$ fraction $0.9756319042010854$, and compressibility $0.024368095798914635$; \textit{E. coli} had $DL_{D_o}=2$, raw baseline dimension $2$, $R_{\mathrm{final}}$ fraction $0.9942823785118229$, and compressibility $0.005717621488177116$; human had $DL_{D_o}=3$, raw baseline dimension $19$, $R_{\mathrm{final}}$ fraction $0.9736442326306781$, and compressibility $0.02635576736932188$; yeast had $DL_{D_o}=2$, raw baseline dimension $19$, $R_{\mathrm{final}}$ fraction $0.8676586345294415$, and compressibility $0.13234136547055853$.  Each organism-level permutation value in that descriptive test was $0.000999000999000999$, but the cross-organism conclusion was that the measured readouts did not highly compress $P_Q$; the median compressibility was $0.026$.  This supports the local caution used here: a downstream readout may be finite and useful without being a complete mechanism for the residual coordinate family.

\paragraph{Local interpretation.}
The supported statement is narrow.  In this finite panel, $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinates show a small positive direct association with stability-like readouts, while the translation-conditioned stability increment is mixed and has a negative weighted aggregate.  The chapter therefore records a boundary condition: $q_i \to T_j \to$ stability may be reported only as a controlled, translation-conditioned residual surface, and in the present data that surface is partial rather than confirmed.  No translation mechanism, folding pathway, physical admissibility statement, molecular function, phenotype, degradation mechanism, or universal biological rule follows from these numbers.  Any such stronger claim would require a separate layer-matched contact and its own controlled statistic.
