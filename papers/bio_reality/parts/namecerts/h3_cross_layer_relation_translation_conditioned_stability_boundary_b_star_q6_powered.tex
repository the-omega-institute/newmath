\subsection{Translation-conditioned protein-stability boundary for $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$}\label{sec:biorality-q6-translation-conditioned-stability-boundary}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite panel.}
The record tests whether synonymous residual coordinates from $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ can be read at a protein-stability or degradation boundary only after a matched translation readout has survived the declared controls. The finite carrier is an organism-by-CDS panel with amino-acid-quotient residualized $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ coordinates, matched translation readouts, matched mRNA controls, and a downstream stability surface where the dataset supplies one. The relevant downstream surface is intentionally heterogeneous: Escherichia coli K12 MG1655 is represented by an mRNA half-life readout with $2517$ joined rows, Homo sapiens by protein turnover with $6697$ joined turnover rows and $11322$ joined mRNA-half-life rows, and Saccharomyces cerevisiae by protein turnover with $3537$ joined turnover rows and $4759$ joined mRNA-half-life rows. The run used five folds, required at least $300$ joined rows, and was keyed by seed $\mathrm{sha256{:}1deaaafe98cd16982d810af72f6389a20be0dc19cbd46bc966593dd507bad122}$.

\paragraph{Statistic.}
For each organism the statistic first estimates whether the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual block has predictive contact with the available stability-like readout after amino-acid composition, organism scope, $GC3$, CDS length, $M$-density, matched mRNA controls, and the translation readout have been accounted for. The downstream quantity is not a direct codon-to-stability mechanism. It is a translation-conditioned residual increment: the test asks whether adding the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual block after the full control surface changes held-out explanatory power for the available stability readout. In symbols, with $q$ denoting the residual coordinate block, $T$ the matched translation readout, $Y_{\mathrm{stab}}$ the stability-like readout, and $Z$ the remaining controls, the reported finite quantity is the held-out increment
$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta R^2_{\mathrm{full}} &= R^2(Y_{\mathrm{stab}} \mid Z,T,q)-R^2(Y_{\mathrm{stab}} \mid Z,T).
\end{aligned}
$$
A positive direct $q \to Y_{\mathrm{stab}}$ association before this conditioning is therefore only a screening observation; it does not by itself pass the boundary.

\paragraph{Observed row-level outcome.}
The unconditioned or weakly conditioned stability screen was positive in all three organism entries. Across the three available entries, the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ block predicting the stability-like readout had minimum $R^2=0.018837736273789707$, median $R^2=0.027727034742559664$, mean $R^2=0.02753157569086806$, and maximum $R^2=0.03602995605625481$, with $3$ positive and $0$ negative entries. After full translation conditioning, the aggregate increment moved toward a blocked or weak boundary: the full-control $\Delta R^2$ distribution over the three entries had minimum $-0.004953854025603666$, median $-0.0033500708066446228$, mean $-0.0025553576666936064$, maximum $0.0006378518321674687$, $2$ negative entries, and $1$ positive entry. The row-weighted full-control increment was $-0.001572140012981015$. The sign-flip probability for being greater than zero was $0.946441650390625$, which is not supportive of a positive translation-conditioned stability effect. Under permutation, the corresponding $\Delta R^2$ values were all negative, with minimum $-0.004840148172275405$, median $-0.002926202164063474$, mean $-0.002753469686061827$, and maximum $-0.0004940587218466019$.

\paragraph{Call.}
The finite verdict is partial rather than positive. The organism calls were split into $2$ partial entries and $1$ mediated-blocked-null entry. Thus the panel sees a small direct association between $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates and stability-like readouts, but the association does not survive as a robust positive translation-conditioned residual after the full control surface is imposed. The local classification remains cross-layer relation, not mechanism. A concise readback of the finite result is
$$
\begin{aligned}
\operatorname{median} R^2(q \to Y_{\mathrm{stab}}) &= 0.027727034742559664,\\
\operatorname{median} \Delta R^2_{\mathrm{full}} &= -0.0033500708066446228,\\
\operatorname{weighted}\,\Delta R^2_{\mathrm{full}} &= -0.001572140012981015,\\
 p_{\mathrm{signflip}}(\Delta R^2_{\mathrm{full}}>0) &= 0.946441650390625.
\end{aligned}
$$
This is best read as a boundary result: direct residual predictivity is present at small scale, while the translation-conditioned stability claim is not established as a stable positive downstream relation.

\paragraph{Relation to neighboring finite rows.}
The stability boundary differs from the structural-order boundary in the same $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ family. The structural-order conditioned run had two positive full-control entries, a positive weighted full-control increment of $0.004764742175882462$, and sign-flip probability $0.0029296875$, whereas the stability run has weighted full-control increment $-0.001572140012981015$ and sign-flip probability $0.946441650390625$. The structural-order screening packet also reports a concrete nine-coordinate $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual block: $K\_AAA$, $Arg\_AGR$, $Ile\_AUA$, $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$, $Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG$, $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$, $Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG$, $Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY$, and $f3\_stress$. In the broader compilation test, the residual source was not compressed by the measured low-dimensional readout set: the cross-organism median compressibility was $0.026$, with per-organism compressibility $0.024368095798914635$ for Danio rerio, $0.005717621488177116$ for Escherichia coli K12 MG1655, $0.02635576736932188$ for Homo sapiens, and $0.13234136547055853$ for Saccharomyces cerevisiae. These neighboring rows support the same conservative reading: $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates can carry finite associations, but each layer must be separately tested.

\paragraph{Local no-promotion rule.}
Nothing in this chapter licenses a direct claim of translation realization, folding quality, physical admissibility, protein function, organismal phenotype, degradation mechanism, or universal biological law. Modeled tAI or stAI contact is not survival evidence for stability. Matched mRNA abundance is a control surface, not a translation readout. Bulk protein abundance is not a stability or folding assay. A stability or degradation statement is allowed only when a same-organism, same-CDS panel supplies a matched translation readout, the $q \to T$ entry survives under the controls, and the downstream $q \to Y_{\mathrm{stab}}$ association is reported after conditioning on that translation readout. Under the finite rows reported here, the scientifically accurate conclusion is therefore: small direct residual association is observed, but the translation-conditioned protein-stability boundary remains only partially supported and is blocked against mechanistic promotion.
