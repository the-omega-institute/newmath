\subsection{Translation-Conditioned Stability Boundary for $B^{*}_{Q6}$ Residual Coordinates}
\label{sec:biorreality-q6-translation-conditioned-stability-boundary}

\paragraph{Finite panel and admissible interpretation.}
The record concerns a finite organism-by-CDS panel in which $B^{*}_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates are read only after amino-acid quotient residualization. The admissible route is sequential: a coordinate $q_i$ may be considered for a downstream protein-stability or degradation-rate surface only after the matched translation readout $T_j$ has been controlled against amino-acid composition, organism identity, $GC3$, coding length, $M$-density, and matched mRNA abundance. The stability layer is therefore not a direct codon-topology claim. It is a conditioned readout question of the form
$$
\begin{aligned}
q_i &\longrightarrow T_j \longrightarrow S_k,\\
\Delta R^2_{\mathrm{full}} &= R^2(S_k\mid Z,T_j,q_i)-R^2(S_k\mid Z,T_j),
\end{aligned}
$$
where $S_k$ denotes the available stability, degradation, turnover, or half-life surface and $Z$ denotes the declared controls. A positive unconditioned $q_i \to S_k$ association is not enough. The stability boundary is crossed only when the same finite rows also preserve the translation step and show that the downstream association is appropriately interpreted after conditioning on translation.

The powered stability run is identified by experiment run $18a9672b4384$, with $5$ folds, a minimum joined-row requirement of $300$, and seed \texttt{sha256:1deaaafe98cd16982d810af72f6389a20be0dc19cbd46bc966593dd507bad122}. In \textit{Homo sapiens} the primary readout is protein turnover with $6697$ joined turnover rows, together with $11322$ mRNA half-life rows. In \textit{Saccharomyces cerevisiae} the primary readout is protein turnover with $3537$ joined turnover rows, together with $4759$ mRNA half-life rows. Across the three computed organisms, the direct $B^{*}_{Q6}$ stability-prediction surface is positive but small: the reported summary has $n=3$, minimum $0.018837736273789707$, median $0.027727034742559664$, mean $0.02753157569086806$, maximum $0.03602995605625481$, and $3$ positive entries.

\paragraph{Conditioned verdict.}
The conditioned test does not support promotion to an unqualified protein-stability mechanism. The full-control conditioned increment has
$$
\begin{aligned}
\min \Delta R^2_{\mathrm{full}} &= -0.004953854025603666,\\
\operatorname{median}\Delta R^2_{\mathrm{full}} &= -0.0033500708066446228,\\
\operatorname{mean}\Delta R^2_{\mathrm{full}} &= -0.0025553576666936064,\\
\max \Delta R^2_{\mathrm{full}} &= 0.0006378518321674687.
\end{aligned}
$$
There are $2$ negative organism entries and $1$ positive entry, with weighted full-control $\Delta R^2=-0.001572140012981015$. The sign-flip probability for being greater than zero is $0.946441650390625$, so the positive direction is not supported. A permutation control gives an entirely nonpositive comparison surface, with maximum $-0.0004940587218466019$, median $-0.002926202164063474$, mean $-0.002753469686061827$, minimum $-0.004840148172275405$, and $3$ negative entries. The finite call distribution is $1$ mediated-blocked null and $2$ partial calls, so the chapter-level verdict is \emph{partial}: the panel contains weak direct stability association, but the translation-conditioned stability boundary is not established as a stable positive passage.

\paragraph{Relation to the measured translation and structural-order panels.}
The stability boundary should be read beside the finite translation and structural-order contacts, not as a replacement for them. The translation-gated structural-order run used $9$ $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates: $K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $Leu_{CUN\,vs\,UUR}$, $Leu_{UUA\,vs\,UUG}$, $Ser_{UCR\,vs\,AGY}$, $Ser_{UCA\,vs\,UCG}$, $Thr_{ACR\,vs\,ACY}$, and $f_3$-stress. In that panel, the control design had $24$ control columns, $20$ amino-acid composition controls, rank $23$ for $Z$, and powered residual rank $9$ for the $q$ coordinates in both \textit{E. coli} and yeast. The \textit{E. coli} structural-order contact joined $3739$ codon rows to $3736$ matched structural-confidence proteins, with hit rate $0.999198$, residual degrees of freedom $3713$, explained energy $4178.935925251874$, residual response energy $136607.52873622958$, and $R^2_{SO}=0.03059081709413562$. The yeast contact joined $5815$ codon rows to $5804$ matched proteins, with hit rate $0.998108$, residual degrees of freedom $5781$, explained energy $44421.14342043593$, residual response energy $620506.1490869389$, and $R^2_{SO}=0.0715885627979685$. Those numbers show that controlled finite contacts can be assembled, but they do not transfer stability, folding, function, or phenotype status to the stability panel.

\paragraph{Compression background.}
The broader residual-compression calculation also argues against treating the $Q6$ source as already explained by the currently measured readout set. The minimal-compilation run reports that the measured low-dimensional $D_o$ set does not highly compress $P_Q$: median compressibility is $0.026$. Per organism, the reported compression values are $0.024368095798914635$ for zebrafish with $DL(D_o)=1$ and raw baseline $17$, $0.005717621488177116$ for \textit{E. coli} with $DL(D_o)=2$ and raw baseline $2$, $0.02635576736932188$ for human with $DL(D_o)=3$ and raw baseline $19$, and $0.13234136547055853$ for yeast with $DL(D_o)=2$ and raw baseline $19$. The corresponding $P_Q$ energies and row counts are $605.0092168669606$ over $13140$ zebrafish rows, $225.84556319953032$ over $2517$ \textit{E. coli} rows, $286.3673197177688$ over $11250$ human rows, and $229.86450794006544$ over $4759$ yeast rows. Each permutation probability is $0.000999000999000999$, but the descriptive verdict remains low compression rather than a causal compilation mechanism.

\paragraph{Boundary of what follows.}
This chapter supports only a finite, controlled, translation-conditioned stability-boundary record with a partial verdict. It does not assert that $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual geometry causes translation, folding, degradation, physical admissibility, molecular function, organismal phenotype, or any universal biological rule. Modeled tAI or stAI weights are not treated as ribosome occupancy. mRNA abundance is a control surface rather than a translation observation. Bulk protein abundance is not a stability, folding, localization, or function assay. A later claim about protein stability or degradation would require a separate organism- and CDS-matched stability contact, the same-row translation step, matched mRNA controls, protein-abundance controls where relevant, and a finite statistic showing that conditioning on translation changes the direct $q_i \to S_k$ association in the declared direction.
