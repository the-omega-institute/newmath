\subsection{Translation-conditioned protein-stability boundary for $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$}\label{sec:bior reality-q6-translation-conditioned-stability-boundary}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite panel.}
This record treats the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ coordinates as finite residual covariates on coding sequences, after first removing the amino-acid quotient contribution.  The statistical object is not a direct codon-to-stability law.  It is a gated matrix in which a coordinate $q_i$ is allowed to enter a protein-stability or degradation-rate boundary only after the same row has a translation readout $T_j$ that survives the declared controls.  The finite run used five folds, a minimum joined-row threshold of $300$, and seed $\mathrm{sha256{:}1deaaafe98cd16982d810af72f6389a20be0dc19cbd46bc966593dd507bad122}$.  The stability-facing joined surfaces were organism-specific: in \emph{Escherichia coli} K-12 MG1655 the available primary readout was mRNA half-life with $2517$ rows; in \emph{Homo sapiens} the primary readout was protein turnover with $6697$ turnover rows and $11322$ mRNA-half-life rows; in \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae} the primary readout was protein turnover with $3537$ turnover rows and $4759$ mRNA-half-life rows.

\paragraph{Controlled statistic.}
Let $Q$ denote the residualized $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ design on the joined coding-sequence rows, let $T$ denote the matched translation readout, let $Y$ denote the available stability or turnover-facing readout, and let $Z$ collect amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, coding-sequence length, $M$-density, mRNA controls, and the remaining declared row-level controls.  The boundary statistic compares the contribution of $Q$ to $Y$ before and after the translation readout is included.  In schematic finite-sample notation the quantity reported by the run is
$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta R^2_{Q\mid T,Z}
  &= R^2(Y \sim Z+T+Q)-R^2(Y \sim Z+T).
\end{aligned}
$$
A positive direct $Q \to Y$ association is therefore insufficient for a protein-stability interpretation.  The only admissible reading is translation-conditioned residual association on the joined rows, and even that reading remains descriptive unless a separate stability, degradation, turnover, pulse-chase, or perturbation contact supplies the corresponding biological layer.

\paragraph{Observed finite-row behavior.}
Across the three joined organisms, the direct $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ stability-facing prediction summary was positive but small: minimum $0.018837736273789707$, median $0.027727034742559664$, mean $0.02753157569086806$, and maximum $0.03602995605625481$, with $3$ positive entries and $0$ negative entries.  After the full translation-conditioned control set was imposed, the aggregate $\Delta R^2$ summary no longer supported a robust positive boundary: minimum $-0.004953854025603666$, median $-0.0033500708066446228$, mean $-0.0025553576666936064$, maximum $0.0006378518321674687$, with $2$ negative entries and $1$ positive entry.  The weighted full-control value was $-0.001572140012981015$, and the sign-flip probability for a positive full-control effect was $0.946441650390625$.  The permuted full-control comparison was entirely non-positive in this finite run, with minimum $-0.004840148172275405$, median $-0.002926202164063474$, mean $-0.002753469686061827$, maximum $-0.0004940587218466019$, and $3$ negative entries.  The organism-level calls were $2$ partial cases and $1$ mediated-blocked-null case, so the recorded verdict is partial rather than a crossing of the stability boundary.

\paragraph{Relation to neighboring finite tests.}
The stability boundary should be read alongside the measured residual-compressibility and structural-order checks only as context for scope, not as proof of a common mechanism.  In the minimal-compilation run, the residual source was not strongly compressed by the measured low-dimensional readout set: the reported cross-organism median compressibility was $0.026$, with organism values $0.024368095798914635$ for \emph{Danio rerio}, $0.005717621488177116$ for \emph{E. coli}, $0.02635576736932188$ for human, and $0.13234136547055853$ for yeast; each listed permutation value was $0.000999000999000999$.  The structural-order boundary behaved differently from the stability boundary: the translation-conditioned order run had full-control $\Delta R^2$ values with minimum $0.002688284523492457$, mean and median $0.005284160876443678$, maximum $0.007880037229394898$, and sign-flip probability $0.0029296875$, while the corresponding permuted values were negative.  A separate structural-order survival computation reported $9$ residual coordinates on AlphaFold mean pLDDT readouts, with joined hit rates $0.999198$ for \emph{E. coli} and $0.998108$ for yeast, and $R^2_{SO}=0.03059081709413562$ and $0.0715885627979685$, respectively.  These neighboring values show that the $Q6$ residual basis can have measurable finite-row contacts in other bounded surfaces, but they do not convert the stability result into a mechanism.

\paragraph{Boundary statement.}
The supported statement is therefore narrow.  The $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates may be carried into a protein-stability-facing matrix only under the translation-conditioned design described above, and the observed stability-facing run is partial: direct residual signal exists, but the full conditioned increment is small, mostly negative, and not sign-flip-supported as positive.  No translation realization, folding statement, physical admissibility claim, molecular function claim, phenotype claim, mechanistic explanation, or universal biological rule follows from this record.  Such claims require their own layer-matched contact and their own controlled finite-row statistic.
