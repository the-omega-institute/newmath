\subsection{Translation-Conditioned Stability Boundary for $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ Residual Coordinates}
\label{sec:bioreality-q6-translation-conditioned-stability-boundary}

\paragraph{Finite panel.}
This record tests whether amino-acid-quotient-residualized $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ coding-sequence coordinates can be read at a protein-stability or degradation-rate surface only after passing through a matched translation readout.  The carrier is a finite organism-by-CDS table with residual coordinates $q_i$, translation measurements $T_j$, matched mRNA controls, and a downstream stability-side readout where the table contains one.  The stability-side readout is intentionally heterogeneous: in \emph{Escherichia coli} K12 MG1655 the joined surface uses mRNA half-life with $2517$ rows, while in human and yeast it uses protein turnover with $6697$ and $3537$ rows respectively.  The same joined memory also contains mRNA half-life rows for human and yeast, with $11322$ and $4759$ rows.  The finite run used five folds, required at least $300$ joined rows, and was evaluated under the seed \texttt{sha256:1deaaafe98cd16982d810af72f6389a20be0dc19cbd46bc966593dd507bad122}.

\paragraph{Gate.}
The admissible interpretation is not $q_i \to$ stability.  It is the gated chain $q_i \to T_j \to P_k$, where $P_k$ denotes the stability-side or degradation-side readout and the direct residual coordinate is first tested against translation under the declared controls.  The control surface contains amino-acid composition, organism identity, GC3, CDS length, $M$-density, and matched mRNA covariates.  Formally, the reported boundary has the schematic form
$$
\begin{aligned}
q_i &\perp\!\!\!\perp \mathrm{AA\ quotient}\quad\text{by construction},\\
S^{QT}_{ij} &> 0\quad\text{is required before a stability-side readout is interpreted},\\
\Delta R^2_{i,k\mid T,Z} &= R^2(P_k \sim q_i + T_j + Z)-R^2(P_k \sim T_j + Z),
\end{aligned}
$$
with $Z$ denoting the full control table.  A positive direct association between $q_i$ and a stability-side readout is therefore insufficient unless the corresponding translation step survives and the conditioned statistic is reported as a residual association after $T_j$, not as a folding or degradation mechanism.

\paragraph{Observed stability-side result.}
Before conditioning on the translation layer, the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ coordinates have positive stability-side prediction in all three organisms: the cross-organism summary has $n=3$, minimum $0.018837736273789707$, median $0.027727034742559664$, mean $0.02753157569086806$, and maximum $0.03602995605625481$, with $3$ positive and $0$ negative entries.  After the full translation-conditioned control step, the aggregate full-control $\Delta R^2$ is not positive as a cross-organism claim: it has $n=3$, minimum $-0.004953854025603666$, median $-0.0033500708066446228$, mean $-0.0025553576666936064$, and maximum $0.0006378518321674687$, with $1$ positive and $2$ negative entries.  The weighted full-control value is $-0.001572140012981015$.  The sign-flip probability for a positive full-control effect is $0.946441650390625$, so the finite table does not support a general positive translation-conditioned stability transfer.  The organism calls are mixed: two organisms are classified as partial and one as mediated blocked null.  A permutation comparison remains negative, with minimum $-0.004840148172275405$, median $-0.002926202164063474$, mean $-0.002753469686061827$, and maximum $-0.0004940587218466019$ across $n=3$.

\paragraph{Relation to neighboring finite checks.}
The stability boundary is sharper when placed beside the other finite $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ contacts. Thus the residual source is not compressed into the available low-dimensional readout set.  By contrast, a translation-conditioned structural-order boundary has a positive full-control profile on two organisms: $n=2$, minimum $0.002688284523492457$, median and mean $0.005284160876443678$, maximum $0.007880037229394898$, sign-flip probability $0.0029296875$, and weighted value $0.004764742175882462$.  The structural-order contact used $3412$ joined \emph{E. coli} rows and $5119$ joined yeast rows.  A still more direct structural-order survival table reported AlphaFold mean pLDDT only as predicted structure confidence, with codon joins of $3739$ in \emph{E. coli} and $5815$ in yeast, matched-protein counts of $3736$ and $5804$, hit rates $0.999198$ and $0.998108$, and $R^2$ values $0.03059081709413562$ and $0.0715885627979685$.  These neighboring checks show that the stability boundary is not merely refusing every downstream surface; rather, the specific stability-side chain fails to become a robust translation-conditioned positive effect in the present finite panel.

\paragraph{Boundary statement.}
The chapter therefore records a partial finite result.  The residual coordinates can be tested at a stability-side surface, and direct stability-side prediction is present at small positive magnitudes before the full conditioning step.  However, the claimed cross-layer route is not licensed as a mechanism: the translation-conditioned $\Delta R^2$ is negative on average, negative after row weighting, and has a high sign-flip probability for positivity.  No translation realization, protein folding account, physical admissibility claim, biological function, degradation mechanism, phenotype, or universal biological rule follows from this table.  Any such stronger assertion would require its own layer-matched contact and its own finite statistic beyond the present $q_i \to T_j \to P_k$ boundary.
