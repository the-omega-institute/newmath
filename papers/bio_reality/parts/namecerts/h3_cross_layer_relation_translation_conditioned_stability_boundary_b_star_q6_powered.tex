\subsection{Translation-conditioned stability boundary for $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates}
\label{sec:bioreality-q6-translation-conditioned-stability-boundary}

\paragraph{Finite panel.}
This record treats $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ as a finite panel of synonymous residual coordinates rather than as a direct biological cause.  Each coordinate is first residualized against amino-acid quotient structure, and every downstream protein-state reading is allowed to enter only after the same finite row has a translation readout and the declared controls are present.  The operative finite carrier is an organism-by-CDS table with $q_i$ columns for the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates, a translation readout $T_j$, matched mRNA controls, and a stability or degradation-rate surface where such a surface is available.  The control block contains amino-acid composition, organism identity, $GC3$, coding-sequence length, the $M$-density baseline, and matched mRNA abundance; protein abundance is used only as a downstream or auxiliary surface, not as a substitute for stability.

\paragraph{Experiment readback.}
The computed stability-boundary run is identified by experiment id \texttt{b\_star\_q6\_translation\_conditioned\_stability\_boundary\_powered} with run id \texttt{18a9672b4384}.  It used five folds, a minimum joined-row requirement of $300$, and seed \texttt{sha256:1deaaafe98cd16982d810af72f6389a20be0dc19cbd46bc966593dd507bad122}.  Three organisms supplied joined stability-side rows.  For \textit{Escherichia coli} K12 MG1655 the primary readout was mRNA half-life with $2517$ joined rows.  For \textit{Homo sapiens} the primary readout was protein turnover, with $6697$ protein-turnover rows and $11322$ mRNA-half-life rows.  For \textit{Saccharomyces cerevisiae} the primary readout was protein turnover, with $3537$ protein-turnover rows and $4759$ mRNA-half-life rows.  Thus the evidence is a finite mixed stability surface: one bacterial entry is still an RNA-half-life proxy, while the human and yeast entries use protein-turnover surfaces.

\paragraph{Direct residual association.}
Before translation conditioning, the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual block had positive predictive contact with the selected stability-side readout in all three joined organism panels.  The reported cross-validated values were
$$
\begin{aligned}
\min R^2_{q\to S} &= 0.018837736273789707,\\
\operatorname{median} R^2_{q\to S} &= 0.027727034742559664,\\
\operatorname{mean} R^2_{q\to S} &= 0.02753157569086806,\\
\max R^2_{q\to S} &= 0.03602995605625481,
\end{aligned}
$$
with $n=3$, $3$ positive entries, and no negative entries in this direct summary.  This number is not interpreted as a stability mechanism.  It is only the pre-conditioning surface that must be compared against the translation-conditioned surface.

\paragraph{Translation-conditioned boundary statistic.}
After the full control block and translation-conditioning step are imposed, the aggregate delta-$R^2$ surface changes sign in the finite panel:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\min \Delta R^2_{\mathrm{full}} &= -0.004953854025603666,\\
\operatorname{median} \Delta R^2_{\mathrm{full}} &= -0.0033500708066446228,\\
\operatorname{mean} \Delta R^2_{\mathrm{full}} &= -0.0025553576666936064,\\
\max \Delta R^2_{\mathrm{full}} &= 0.0006378518321674687.
\end{aligned}
$$
There are two negative organism calls and one positive organism call in this full-control delta surface.  The weighted full-control delta is
$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta R^2_{\mathrm{weighted}}=-0.001572140012981015,
\end{aligned}
$$
and the sign-flip test for being greater than zero gives
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{\mathrm{signflip}}=0.946441650390625.
\end{aligned}
$$
The permuted comparison remains negative in all three organism entries, with minimum $-0.004840148172275405$, median $-0.002926202164063474$, mean $-0.002753469686061827$, maximum $-0.0004940587218466019$, and $3$ negative entries out of $3$.  The finite classification is therefore \texttt{partial}: two organism entries are partial and one is a mediated blocked null, while the aggregate sign test does not support a positive translation-conditioned stability effect.

\paragraph{Relation to the broader $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ panel.}
The same coordinate family has a separate minimal-compilation diagnostic on four organisms.  There the residual source $P_Q$ was not strongly compressed by the measured low-dimensional readout set: \textit{Danio rerio} had $P_Q$ energy $605.0092168669606$, $n=13140$, $DL_{D_o}=1$, raw baseline description length $17$, final residual fraction $0.9756319042010854$, and compressibility $0.024368095798914635$; \textit{E. coli} had $P_Q$ energy $225.84556319953032$, $n=2517$, $DL_{D_o}=2$, raw baseline description length $2$, final residual fraction $0.9942823785118229$, and compressibility $0.005717621488177116$; human had $P_Q$ energy $286.3673197177688$, $n=11250$, $DL_{D_o}=3$, raw baseline description length $19$, final residual fraction $0.9736442326306781$, and compressibility $0.02635576736932188$; yeast had $P_Q$ energy $229.86450794006544$, $n=4759$, $DL_{D_o}=2$, raw baseline description length $19$, final residual fraction $0.8676586345294415$, and compressibility $0.13234136547055853$.  The median compressibility was $0.026$, with permutation $p=0.000999000999000999$ in each computed organism.  This places the stability-boundary result in the same descriptive regime: the residual coordinates are measurable, but they are not collapsed into a simple low-dimensional biological explanation by the available readouts.

\paragraph{Comparison with structural-order contacts.}
The stability boundary is weaker than the corresponding translation-conditioned structural-order boundary.  In the structural-order run, only two organisms were joined, but both had positive full-control delta-$R^2$ entries: minimum $0.002688284523492457$, mean and median $0.005284160876443678$, maximum $0.007880037229394898$, weighted value $0.004764742175882462$, and sign-flip $p=0.0029296875$.  Its classification was \texttt{crosses\_to\_structural\_order\_layer}.  The more primitive structural-order survival run also reported nine residual coordinates, namely \texttt{K\_AAA}, \texttt{Arg\_AGR}, \texttt{Ile\_AUA}, \texttt{Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR}, \texttt{Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG}, \texttt{Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY}, \texttt{Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG}, \texttt{Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY}, and \texttt{f3\_stress}.  In that run, \textit{E. coli} used $3736$ matched proteins out of $3739$ codon-joined rows, with hit rate $0.999198$, rank $9$ after controls, residual degrees of freedom $3713$, and $R^2_{SO}=0.03059081709413562$; yeast used $5804$ matched proteins out of $5815$ codon-joined rows, with hit rate $0.998108$, rank $9$ after controls, residual degrees of freedom $5781$, and $R^2_{SO}=0.0715885627979685$.  These numbers are included only to localize the stability result among neighboring readout surfaces: structural confidence and protein turnover are different measurement layers, and a positive structural-order contact cannot be used to promote the stability boundary.

\paragraph{Allowed conclusion.}
The finite result supports only a bounded statement.  The $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual block can show a small direct association with available stability-side readouts, but after translation conditioning and full controls the aggregate boundary is not positive: $\Delta R^2_{\mathrm{weighted}}=-0.001572140012981015$ and $p_{\mathrm{signflip}}=0.946441650390625$.  Therefore this chapter records a partial, translation-conditioned boundary rather than a protein-stability mechanism.  A legitimate positive stability claim would require the same $q_i$ coordinate to survive the translation readout $T_j$ on the matched row panel and then show a stability or degradation-rate association whose direct $q_i\to S_k$ component is reduced by conditioning on $T_j$.

\paragraph{Local no-promotion rule.}
No translation mechanism, folding claim, physical admissibility claim, biological function, phenotype, causal pathway, or universal biological law follows from these rows.  A modeled adaptation weight, a ribosome-profiling route name, a matched mRNA control, a bulk proteomics contact, a direct $q_i\to S_k$ association, or the presence of the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ coordinates is not enough.  Each stronger assertion needs its own layer-matched contact and a finite statistic on the joined rows.  In this chapter the admissible readout is only the controlled, translation-conditioned stability-boundary statistic reported above.
