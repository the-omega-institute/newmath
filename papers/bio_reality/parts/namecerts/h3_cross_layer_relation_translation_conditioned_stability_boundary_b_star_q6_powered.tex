\subsection{Translation-Conditioned Stability Boundary for B$^*_{Q6}$ Residual Coordinates}\label{sec:bioreality-q6-translation-conditioned-stability-boundary}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite panel.}
The record concerns a finite organism-by-CDS panel in which each B$^*_{Q6}$ coordinate is used only after amino-acid-quotient residualization.  The admissible readout path is not a direct codon-to-stability assertion.  The tested path first asks whether a residual coordinate $q_i$ survives a matched translation readout $T_j$ under controls, and only then asks whether a downstream protein-stability or degradation-rate surface retains an association after conditioning on the translation readout.  The control set includes amino-acid composition, organism identity, GC3, CDS length, M-density baseline, and matched mRNA controls; where protein abundance enters the downstream surface, it is treated as a control or readout layer rather than as a stability mechanism.  The finite run used five folds, required at least $300$ joined rows, and was seeded by the recorded hash sha256:1deaaafe98cd16982d810af72f6389a20be0dc19cbd46bc966593dd507bad122.

\paragraph{Joined stability surfaces.}
Three organism-level joined surfaces entered the stability boundary calculation.  For \textit{Escherichia coli} K-12 MG1655, the primary stability-side readout was mRNA half-life with $2517$ joined rows.  For \textit{Homo sapiens}, the primary readout was protein turnover with $6697$ joined turnover rows, and the same organism also carried $11322$ mRNA half-life rows.  For \textit{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}, the primary readout was protein turnover with $3537$ joined turnover rows, together with $4759$ mRNA half-life rows.  These rows are sufficient for a finite boundary test, but their biological meaning is deliberately narrow: mRNA half-life is not protein degradation, and protein turnover is not a folding assay, a functional assay, or a phenotype measurement.

\paragraph{Direct residual association.}
Before translation conditioning, the B$^*_{Q6}$ residual block had a small positive association with the available stability-side surfaces in all three organism entries.  The distribution of the direct stability-side score was
$$
\begin{aligned}
\min &= 0.018837736273789707,\\
\operatorname{median} &= 0.027727034742559664,\\
\operatorname{mean} &= 0.02753157569086806,\\
\max &= 0.03602995605625481.
\end{aligned}
$$
All three entries were positive at this descriptive stage.  This is not the endpoint of the claim.  A direct $q_i$ to stability-side association is treated as a candidate signal that must pass through the translation-conditioned gate before any cross-layer interpretation is allowed.

\paragraph{Translation-conditioned result.}
After the full control set and translation conditioning were applied, the aggregate delta-$R^2$ surface changed sign in most organism entries.  The full-control delta-$R^2$ summary was
$$
\begin{aligned}
\min &= -0.004953854025603666,\\
\operatorname{median} &= -0.0033500708066446228,\\
\operatorname{mean} &= -0.0025553576666936064,\\
\max &= 0.0006378518321674687.
\end{aligned}
$$
Two of the three organism entries were negative and one was positive.  The weighted full-control delta-$R^2$ was $-0.001572140012981015$, and the sign-flip test for a positive full-control effect gave $p=0.946441650390625$.  The permuted full-control delta-$R^2$ surface was also negative in all three entries, with minimum $-0.004840148172275405$, median $-0.002926202164063474$, mean $-0.002753469686061827$, and maximum $-0.0004940587218466019$.  The finite call is therefore partial rather than promoted: one organism was classified as mediated-blocked-null and two were classified as partial.

\paragraph{Relation to the wider Q6 readout block.}
The same finite memory also records that the measured low-dimensional downstream readout dictionary did not compress the projected residual source strongly across the computed organisms.  The residual-compressibility test reported compressibility $0.024368095798914635$ for \textit{Danio rerio}, $0.005717621488177116$ for \textit{E. coli} K-12 MG1655, $0.02635576736932188$ for \textit{H. sapiens}, and $0.13234136547055853$ for \textit{S. cerevisiae}, each with permutation $p=0.000999000999000999$.  The corresponding residual source energies were $605.0092168669606$, $225.84556319953032$, $286.3673197177688$, and $229.86450794006544$, with full row counts $13140$, $2517$, $11250$, and $4759$.  Thus the stability-boundary result should be read together with an irreducibility warning: the measured downstream dictionary absorbs only a small part of the B$^*_{Q6}$ residual source in the available descriptive tests.

\paragraph{Structural-order contrast.}
A nearby structure-confidence test gives a useful contrast but not a stability mechanism.  The translation-conditioned structural-order boundary used two organisms and reported B$^*_{Q6}$ order-side scores with minimum $0.03664544696481631$, mean and median $0.09855549686555976$, and maximum $0.1604655467663032$.  Its full-control delta-$R^2$ values were positive in both entries, with minimum $0.002688284523492457$, mean $0.005284160876443678$, maximum $0.007880037229394898$, weighted value $0.004764742175882462$, and sign-flip $p=0.0029296875$.  The underlying structure-confidence contact had $3736$ matched proteins in \textit{E. coli} with join hit rate $0.999198$, and $5804$ matched proteins in \textit{S. cerevisiae} with join hit rate $0.998108$.  Those numbers show that the Q6 residual block can reach a different downstream readout under controls, but they do not upgrade the stability boundary.  AlphaFold mean pLDDT is a predicted confidence surface, not a degradation, folding-kinetics, physical-admissibility, function, or phenotype measurement.

\paragraph{Local interpretation.}
The finite stability result supports only the following bounded statement.  B$^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinates can be evaluated against stability-side or degradation-side readouts only after the same coordinate has been tested through a translation readout under the declared controls.  In the present run the direct residual-to-stability-side association was positive but small, while the translation-conditioned full-control delta-$R^2$ was negative on average and statistically did not support a positive aggregate effect.  Therefore the correct paper-level status is a partial boundary: the panel is informative enough to prevent a direct codon-topology stability claim, but it is not strong enough to claim a translation-mediated stability mechanism.

\paragraph{Claims excluded by this finite record.}
No translation mechanism, protein folding statement, physical-admissibility statement, molecular-function statement, phenotype statement, or universal biological rule follows from this stability-boundary panel.  Modeled tAI or stAI coupling is not accepted as stability survival evidence.  Bulk protein abundance is not accepted as degradation-rate evidence.  mRNA half-life is not accepted as protein turnover unless the readout is explicitly labeled at that layer.  A mechanism would require the matched translation readout to survive first and then to reduce the direct $q_i$ to stability-side association in the joined organism-by-CDS rows.  The present finite result records a controlled boundary, not a folding model or a biological law.
