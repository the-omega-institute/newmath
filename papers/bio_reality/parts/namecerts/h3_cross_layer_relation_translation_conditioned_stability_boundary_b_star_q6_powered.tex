\subsection{Translation-conditioned stability boundary for $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinates}\label{subsec:bioreality-q6-translation-conditioned-stability-boundary}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite panel.}
The record tests whether first-amino-acid-quotient residual coordinates from $B^*_{Q6}$ can be read at a protein-stability or degradation boundary only after a matched translation layer has been accounted for.  The carrier is a finite organism-by-CDS panel with nine residual coordinates inherited from the $B^*_{Q6}$ construction, matched translation readouts, matched mRNA controls, and a downstream stability-like surface where such rows exist.  The stability-side join is not uniform across organisms: \emph{Escherichia coli} K-12 MG1655 contributes $2517$ rows through an mRNA-half-life readout used as the primary available stability-side proxy, human contributes $6697$ protein-turnover rows and $11322$ mRNA-half-life rows, and budding yeast contributes $3537$ protein-turnover rows and $4759$ mRNA-half-life rows.  The computation used five folds, required at least $300$ joined rows, and was recorded under seed \texttt{sha256:1deaaafe98cd16982d810af72f6389a20be0dc19cbd46bc966593dd507bad122}.

\paragraph{Gated statistic.}
For each admissible organism the residual coordinate block is first evaluated against the translation readout under amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, CDS length, M-density, and matched mRNA controls.  Only after that gate is present is the downstream readout summarized as a translation-conditioned residual association.  The intended finite-row contrast is therefore not a direct map from codon topology to stability.  It is the change in held-out explanatory power for a stability-side response after the translation readout and the full covariate set have been supplied:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta R^2_{\mathrm{full}}(o)
  &= R^2\bigl(P_k \mid Z_o,T_j,q_i\bigr)-R^2\bigl(P_k \mid Z_o,T_j\bigr),\\
Z_o
  &= \{\text{amino-acid composition},\text{organism},\mathrm{GC3},\text{CDS length},\text{M-density},\text{mRNA controls}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
Here $P_k$ denotes the available stability-side or degradation-side readout, not a claimed folding mechanism.  A positive direct $q_i\to P_k$ association is allowed to be reported only as an empirical association; it does not become a mechanism unless the matched translation readout both survives and absorbs or reduces the direct residual association.

\paragraph{Observed finite-row values.}
Across the three organism entries, the unconditioned statement that the $B^*_{Q6}$ block predicts the available stability-side surface is small but positive in every row: the summary has $n=3$, minimum $0.018837736273789707$, median $0.027727034742559664$, mean $0.02753157569086806$, maximum $0.03602995605625481$, with $3$ positive and $0$ negative entries.  After the full translation-conditioned control set is imposed, the aggregate $\Delta R^2$ surface changes sign in most organisms: $n=3$, minimum $-0.004953854025603666$, median $-0.0033500708066446228$, mean $-0.0025553576666936064$, maximum $0.0006378518321674687$, with $1$ positive and $2$ negative entries.  The weighted full-control value is $-0.001572140012981015$.  A sign-flip test for being greater than zero gives $p=0.946441650390625$, so the finite panel does not support a robust positive translation-conditioned stability effect.

\paragraph{Permutation check and call.}
The permuted full-control comparison is also negative in all three organism entries: minimum $-0.004840148172275405$, median $-0.002926202164063474$, mean $-0.002753469686061827$, maximum $-0.0004940587218466019$, with $0$ positive and $3$ negative entries.  The organism-level calls consist of two partial entries and one mediated-blocked null entry.  The appropriate classification is therefore \emph{partial}: the data contain a weak direct residual association with the available stability-side readouts, but the translation-conditioned residual gain is not positive at the aggregate level and is not supported by the sign-flip statistic.

\paragraph{Relation to neighboring finite tests.}
The stability boundary is stricter than a generic cross-layer association.  In the separately computed structural-order boundary, the full-control $\Delta R^2$ was positive in both organisms, with $n=2$, minimum $0.002688284523492457$, mean and median $0.005284160876443678$, maximum $0.007880037229394898$, weighted value $0.004764742175882462$, and sign-flip $p=0.0029296875$.  By contrast, the present stability boundary has $n=3$ but a negative weighted full-control value and a sign-flip value near one.  The minimal-compilation finite test also warns against overcompression of the residual source: across computed organisms the median compressibility was $0.026$, with organism values $0.024368095798914635$ for zebrafish, $0.005717621488177116$ for \emph{E. coli}, $0.02635576736932188$ for human, and $0.13234136547055853$ for yeast, each with permutation $p=0.000999000999000999$.  These comparisons keep the stability result in a narrow empirical lane: the residual coordinates are measurable, but this panel does not show that the downstream stability-side boundary is crossed by a translation-conditioned positive effect.

\paragraph{Local interpretation boundary.}
This record supports only a finite, controlled readback statement: $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinates show small direct associations with the available stability-side readouts, while the translation-conditioned full-control statistic is partial and aggregate-negative.  It does not establish translation realization by itself, does not establish protein folding, does not establish physical admissibility, does not establish protein function or phenotype, and does not establish a universal biological rule.  Modeled tAI or stAI coupling is not survival evidence for this stability boundary.  Bulk protein abundance is not a stability or degradation-rate measurement unless a layer-matched stability, turnover, pulse-chase, degradation-rate, or perturbation contact is actually joined to the same CDS panel.  Any future mechanism statement must show that the $q_i\to T_j$ step survives under the declared controls and that conditioning on $T_j$ reduces the direct $q_i\to P_k$ association.
