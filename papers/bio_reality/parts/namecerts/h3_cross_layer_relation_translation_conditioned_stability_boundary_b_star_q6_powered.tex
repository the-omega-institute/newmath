\subsection{Translation-conditioned stability boundary for $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates}
\label{sec:bioreality-q6-translation-conditioned-stability-boundary}

\paragraph{Finite panel.}
The stability-boundary record is a finite organism-by-coding-sequence panel in which each $B^{*}_{Q6}$ coordinate is first residualized against amino-acid composition before it is allowed to face a downstream protein-state readout. The admissible route is ordered: a residual coordinate $q_i$ is tested against a matched translation readout $T_j$ under controls, and only after that gate is examined may a stability or degradation-rate surface be read as a conditioned residual association. The controls include amino-acid composition, organism identity, GC3, CDS length, an $M$-density baseline, matched mRNA controls, and the available protein-level control surface. This chapter therefore treats the stability surface as a boundary statistic, not as a folding explanation or a phenotypic claim.

\paragraph{Joined readout rows.}
The powered stability run used five folds and imposed a minimum joined-row threshold of $300$. Three organism/readout contacts survived the finite-row join. In \emph{Escherichia coli} K-12 MG1655, the primary stability-side readout was mRNA half-life with $2517$ joined rows. In \emph{Homo sapiens}, the primary readout was protein turnover, with $6697$ protein-turnover rows and $11322$ mRNA-half-life rows available in the joined table. In \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}, the primary readout was protein turnover, with $3537$ protein-turnover rows and $4759$ mRNA-half-life rows. The run identifier was $18a9672b4384$, and the deterministic seed was recorded as $\mathrm{sha256{:}1deaaafe98cd16982d810af72f6389a20be0dc19cbd46bc966593dd507bad122}$.

\paragraph{Direct stability-side association.}
Before translation conditioning is interpreted, the raw stability-side association of $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates is only a finite statistical surface. Across the three organism entries, the unconditioned stability-side summary was positive in all three cases: minimum $0.018837736273789707$, median $0.027727034742559664$, mean $0.02753157569086806$, and maximum $0.03602995605625481$. Thus the panel detects a small direct residual association with the available stability-side readouts, but this number is not yet a stability mechanism. It is the quantity that the translation-conditioned step is required to interrogate.

\paragraph{Conditioning statistic.}
The declared boundary statistic asks whether the direct $q_i$ to stability-side association remains after the matched translation readout has been admitted to the model. Let $\Delta R^2_{\mathrm{full}}$ denote the full-control change in explained stability-side variance assigned to $B^{*}_{Q6}$ after conditioning on the translation route and the declared controls. The finite summary was
$$
\begin{aligned}
\min \Delta R^2_{\mathrm{full}} &= -0.004953854025603666,\\
\operatorname{median}\Delta R^2_{\mathrm{full}} &= -0.0033500708066446228,\\
\operatorname{mean}\Delta R^2_{\mathrm{full}} &= -0.0025553576666936064,\\
\max \Delta R^2_{\mathrm{full}} &= 0.0006378518321674687.
\end{aligned}
$$
Two of the three organism entries were negative and one was positive. The weighted full-control value was $-0.001572140012981015$, and the one-sided sign-flip probability for being greater than zero was $0.946441650390625$. The permuted full-control surface was also negative in all three organism entries, with minimum $-0.004840148172275405$, median $-0.002926202164063474$, mean $-0.002753469686061827$, and maximum $-0.0004940587218466019$.

\paragraph{Boundary call.}
The finite call is \emph{partial}. The organism-call ledger contains two partial entries and one mediated-blocked-null entry. This is a stability-boundary result rather than a positive stability theorem: the direct $B^{*}_{Q6}$ stability-side association is small and consistently positive before conditioning, but the translation-conditioned full-control increment is not positive in aggregate. The strongest local statement is that the available finite panel detects a weak residual contact with stability-side readouts and then mostly loses positive support when the translation-conditioned route is imposed. The panel therefore supports a gated boundary interpretation: $q_i$ may be brought to the stability surface only through the tested $q_i \to T_j$ route, and the downstream result is reported as conditioned residual attenuation or partial survival, not as direct codon-topology control of protein stability.

\paragraph{Relation to neighboring readouts.}
The stability boundary should be read alongside the neighboring structural-order tests only as context for scale, not as a transfer of evidence. In the translation-conditioned structural-order boundary, the joined structural-order panel had two organism entries and a positive weighted full-control value $0.004764742175882462$, with a sign-flip probability $0.0029296875$ and positive calls in both organisms. The underlying structural-order contact used AlphaFold mean pLDDT only as predicted structure-confidence readout; the earlier structural-order survival run joined $3736$ of $3739$ bacterial codon-matched proteins, giving hit rate $0.999198$, and $5804$ of $5815$ yeast codon-matched proteins, giving hit rate $0.998108$. Those numbers show that a nearby protein-state surface can be row-rich and positive under its own rules, but they do not upgrade the stability-boundary call. The stability panel remains governed by its own joined turnover and half-life rows, its own conditioning statistic, and its own partial verdict.

\paragraph{Compression context.}
The broader compilation test also argues against collapsing the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ source into a small measured readout dictionary. In the cross-organism residual-compressibility run, the median compressibility was $0.026$, with organism values $0.024368095798914635$ for zebrafish, $0.005717621488177116$ for \emph{E. coli}, $0.02635576736932188$ for human, and $0.13234136547055853$ for yeast. Each listed organism had permutation probability $0.000999000999000999$ in that descriptive partition. These values say that the residual coordinate source is not well compressed by the measured low-dimensional readouts used there. They do not create a stability claim; they only explain why the stability boundary is treated as a separate finite-row contact rather than as a consequence of a general compression law.

\paragraph{Local cannot-claim boundary.}
No translation realization, protein folding claim, physical admissibility claim, molecular function claim, biological mechanism, phenotype statement, or universal biological law follows from this chapter unless a separate layer-matched contact supplies the relevant measurement and the corresponding controlled statistic is computed. Modeled tAI or stAI weights are not ribosome-occupancy observations. Matched mRNA abundance is a control surface, not a translation readout by itself. Bulk protein abundance is not a stability, turnover, folding, or function assay. The finite result here is exactly narrower: in the available joined panel, $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates show small direct association with stability-side readouts, but the translation-conditioned full-control boundary is partial and weighted negative.
