\subsection{Translation-conditioned stability boundary for $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates}
\label{sec:biorreality-q6-translation-conditioned-stability-boundary}

\paragraph{Finite panel.}
This record treats $B^{*}_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates as finite coding-sequence covariates that may approach a protein-stability or degradation-rate surface only after a matched translation readout has been tested on the same row family. The carrier is a finite organism-by-CDS panel containing first-amino-acid-quotient-residualized $B^{*}_{Q6}$ coordinates, translation readouts, matched mRNA controls, and a downstream stability readout where such a readout is available. The rule is directional: a coordinate $q_i$ may be considered at the stability boundary only through the gated chain $q_i \to T_j \to P_k$, where $T_j$ is a translation readout and $P_k$ is a stability, turnover, or degradation-related readout. A direct $q_i \to P_k$ association is not by itself interpreted as a stability mechanism.

\paragraph{Rows and readout availability.}
The powered stability experiment used run \texttt{18a9672b4384}, five folds, and a minimum joined-row threshold of $300$. Three organism panels entered the stability-side calculation. For \textit{Escherichia coli} K-12 MG1655, the available primary downstream readout was mRNA half-life with $2517$ joined rows. For \textit{Homo sapiens}, the primary downstream readout was protein turnover with $6697$ joined rows, while the matched mRNA half-life surface contained $11322$ rows. For \textit{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}, the primary downstream readout was protein turnover with $3537$ joined rows, while the matched mRNA half-life surface contained $4759$ rows. The finite panel therefore has real downstream contact, but its contact geometry is not uniform across organisms: one organism is represented at the RNA-half-life side, and two organisms are represented at the protein-turnover side.

\paragraph{Direct residual-to-stability surface.}
Before translation conditioning, the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual block had a small positive predictive surface against the available stability-side readouts in all three organism panels. The reported cross-validated stability summary was
$$
\begin{aligned}
 n &= 3,\\
 \min R^2(q \to P) &= 0.018837736273789707,\\
 \operatorname{median} R^2(q \to P) &= 0.027727034742559664,\\
 \operatorname{mean} R^2(q \to P) &= 0.02753157569086806,\\
 \max R^2(q \to P) &= 0.03602995605625481.
\end{aligned}
$$
All three entries were positive at this direct level. This direct positivity is not promoted to a biological stability claim. In this chapter it is only the upstream surface that must be tested for attenuation after the translation readout and the declared controls are introduced.

\paragraph{Translation-conditioned residual test.}
The decisive finite statistic is the full-control delta after conditioning on the matched translation layer. The aggregate full-control delta-$R^2$ distribution was
$$
\begin{aligned}
 n &= 3,\\
 \min \Delta R^2_{\mathrm{full}} &= -0.004953854025603666,\\
 \operatorname{median} \Delta R^2_{\mathrm{full}} &= -0.0033500708066446228,\\
 \operatorname{mean} \Delta R^2_{\mathrm{full}} &= -0.0025553576666936064,\\
 \max \Delta R^2_{\mathrm{full}} &= 0.0006378518321674687.
\end{aligned}
$$
Two of the three organism calls were negative after full controls, and one was positive. The weighted full-control delta was $-0.001572140012981015$. The sign-flip test for a positive full-control delta gave $p=0.946441650390625$, so the finite panel does not support a positive cross-organism stability increment after translation conditioning. The permutation-side control was also negative throughout, with
$$
\begin{aligned}
 \min \Delta R^2_{\mathrm{perm}} &= -0.004840148172275405,\\
 \operatorname{median} \Delta R^2_{\mathrm{perm}} &= -0.002926202164063474,\\
 \operatorname{mean} \Delta R^2_{\mathrm{perm}} &= -0.002753469686061827,\\
 \max \Delta R^2_{\mathrm{perm}} &= -0.0004940587218466019.
\end{aligned}
$$
The organism-level calls were classified as two partial cases and one mediated blocked-null case. The chapter verdict is therefore partial rather than positive: there is finite direct residual signal against stability-side readouts, but the translation-conditioned statistic does not establish a stable downstream passage.

\paragraph{Control geometry.}
The finite-row interpretation is conditioned on the declared controls: amino-acid composition, organism identity, GC3, CDS length, M-density baseline, matched mRNA abundance, and protein-abundance controls where the downstream surface requires them. The first-amino-acid quotient residualization is essential, because the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ coordinates are not allowed to inherit their interpretation from amino-acid composition. The translation readout is also essential, because a modeled codon-adaptation contact, a codon-frequency table, or a direct residual-to-turnover association does not count as stability evidence unless the same row family contains a surviving translation entry.

\paragraph{Relation to the broader finite landscape.}
The stability boundary should be read beside two nearby finite facts. First, the minimal compilation calculation did not compress the residual source into the measured low-dimensional readouts: per-organism compressibility was $0.024368095798914635$ for \textit{Danio rerio}, $0.005717621488177116$ for \textit{E. coli}, $0.02635576736932188$ for human, and $0.13234136547055853$ for yeast, with median compressibility $0.026$ and per-organism permutation values $0.000999000999000999$. Second, a related structural-order boundary did cross its declared layer in a different experiment: its full-control delta-$R^2$ values were positive in two of two organisms, with mean $0.005284160876443678$ and sign-flip value $0.0029296875$. These nearby results show that the stability boundary is not being rejected because the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates are numerically inert. Rather, this particular downstream surface fails to produce a robust positive translation-conditioned stability increment under the finite controls.

\paragraph{Local boundary.}
No claim of translation realization, protein folding, physical admissibility, biological function, degradation mechanism, phenotype, or universality follows from this chapter. A positive direct $q_i \to P_k$ surface is insufficient. Modeled tAI or stAI coupling is insufficient. Bulk protein abundance is insufficient as a stability assay. The only permitted reading is narrower: in the available finite organism panels, $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates show small direct association with stability-side readouts, but after the matched translation layer and controls are introduced, the aggregate stability boundary remains partial and non-promoted. A stronger claim would require a separate organism- and CDS-matched stability or degradation-rate contact, a surviving $q_i \to T_j$ translation entry on the same rows, and attenuation of the direct $q_i \to P_k$ association after conditioning on $T_j$.
