\subsection{Translation-conditioned stability boundary for $B^\ast_{Q6}$ residual coordinates}
\label{sec:bioreality-q6-translation-conditioned-stability-boundary}

\paragraph{Finite panel.}
This record tests whether synonymous $B^\ast_{Q6}$ residual coordinates can be carried into a protein-stability or degradation-rate layer only after passing through a matched translation readout.  The carrier is a finite organism-by-CDS panel with amino-acid-quotient residual coordinates, translation readouts, matched mRNA controls, and a downstream stability surface where available.  The admissible readback is not a direct map from codon topology to protein stability.  It is the gated chain
$$
\begin{aligned}
q_i &\longrightarrow T_j,\\
(q_i,T_j) &\longrightarrow Y_{\mathrm{stab}},
\end{aligned}
$$
where $q_i$ is a residual $B^\ast_{Q6}$ coordinate, $T_j$ is the matched translation readout, and $Y_{\mathrm{stab}}$ is the stability or degradation-rate readout after the stated controls.  The control set includes amino-acid composition, organism identity, GC3, CDS length, $M$-density, matched mRNA controls, and the available protein-abundance controls.  The run used five folds, required at least $300$ joined rows, and is identified by seed $\mathrm{sha256{:}1deaaafe98cd16982d810af72f6389a20be0dc19cbd46bc966593dd507bad122}$.

\paragraph{Joined stability rows.}
The finite stability surface contains three organism-level entries.  In \emph{Escherichia coli} K--12 MG1655, the primary downstream readout is mRNA half-life with $2517$ joined rows.  In \emph{Homo sapiens}, the primary downstream readout is protein turnover with $6697$ joined protein-turnover rows, while mRNA half-life is also available with $11322$ rows.  In \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}, the primary downstream readout is protein turnover with $3537$ joined rows, with an additional mRNA half-life surface of $4759$ rows.  These row counts are the finite support of the boundary test; they do not by themselves license any mechanism, phenotype, folding, or universal biological conclusion.

\paragraph{Direct residual association.}
Before translation conditioning, the $B^\ast_{Q6}$ residual block has a small positive held-out association with the downstream stability surface in all three organism entries.  Across $n=3$ entries, the direct $B^\ast_{Q6}$-to-stability statistic has minimum $0.018837736273789707$, median $0.027727034742559664$, mean $0.02753157569086806$, and maximum $0.03602995605625481$, with $3$ positive entries and $0$ negative entries.  This direct statistic is useful only as a boundary pressure: it says that the residual coordinates touch the joined downstream readout weakly but nontrivially before the translation gate is imposed.  It is not interpreted as protein folding quality, degradation mechanism, functional realization, or organism-level phenotype.

\paragraph{Translation-conditioned statistic.}
After full controls and translation conditioning, the aggregate incremental stability statistic is not a positive cross-organism mediation result.  The full-control $\Delta R^2$ distribution has minimum $-0.004953854025603666$, median $-0.0033500708066446228$, mean $-0.0025553576666936064$, maximum $0.0006378518321674687$, and $n=3$, with $2$ negative entries and $1$ positive entry.  The row-weighted full-control $\Delta R^2$ is $-0.001572140012981015$.  The sign-flip probability for being greater than zero is $0.946441650390625$, so the finite panel does not support a positive translation-conditioned stability crossing.  The permuted full-control comparison is also negative in all entries: minimum $-0.004840148172275405$, median $-0.002926202164063474$, mean $-0.002753469686061827$, maximum $-0.0004940587218466019$, with $3$ negative entries and $0$ positive entries.  The organism calls are $2$ partial entries and $1$ mediated-blocked null entry, so the module-level classification is partial rather than closed crossing.

\paragraph{Interpretation of the boundary.}
The correct finite-row reading is therefore asymmetric.  The direct residual block has weak positive association with the downstream stability readouts, but the translation-conditioned full-control surface does not show a positive aggregate mediation signal.  The boundary is kept as a guarded frontier: $B^\ast_{Q6}$ coordinates may be tested against stability only through a surviving translation entry, and any downstream report must be phrased as a conditioned residual association.  A modeled codon-adaptation readout, a contact name, or a direct residual-to-stability score cannot replace the translation gate.  In this panel, the gate leaves a partial record, not a mechanism.

\paragraph{Comparison with neighboring finite tests.}
The same coordinate family is not globally compressible into the measured low-dimensional readout dictionary.  In the minimal-compilation run, the residual source has organism-level compressibilities $0.024368095798914635$ for zebrafish, $0.005717621488177116$ for \emph{E. coli}, $0.02635576736932188$ for human, and $0.13234136547055853$ for yeast, with median compressibility $0.026$ and permutation value $0.000999000999000999$ in each computed organism.  Thus the stability-boundary result should not be read as a collapse of $B^\ast_{Q6}$ into a simple biological layer.  A separate structural-order boundary is stronger: its translation-conditioned order statistic has weighted full-control $\Delta R^2=0.004764742175882462$, positive entries in both tested organisms, and sign-flip probability $0.0029296875$.  The stability boundary differs from that structural-order case because its full-control stability statistic is centered below zero after conditioning.

\paragraph{Local no-promotion rule.}
This chapter only records a finite translation-conditioned stability boundary.  It does not claim that $B^\ast_{Q6}$ causes translation, protein degradation, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, phenotype, or a universal biological law.  No stability mechanism follows from $B^\ast_{Q6}$ geometry, tAI or stAI weights, ribosome-profiling contact labels, mRNA controls, or bulk proteomics contacts alone.  A stronger claim would require a separate layer-matched contact with same-organism and same-CDS rows, a surviving $q_i\to T_j$ entry under the full control set, and attenuation of the direct $q_i\to Y_{\mathrm{stab}}$ association after conditioning on $T_j$.
