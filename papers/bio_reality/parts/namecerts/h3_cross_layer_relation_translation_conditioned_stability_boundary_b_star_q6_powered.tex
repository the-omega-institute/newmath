\subsection{Translation-conditioned stability boundary for $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinates}\label{subsec:bioreality-q6-translation-conditioned-stability-boundary}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Panel object.}
The record considered here is a finite organism-by-CDS panel in which each $B^*_{Q6}$ coordinate is used only after first removing amino-acid-quotient structure.  The rows carry coding-sequence residual coordinates, matched translation readouts, matched mRNA controls, and a downstream stability-side surface when such a surface is available.  The intended readback is therefore not a direct map from codon topology to protein fate.  It is a gated matrix of entries of the form $q_i \to T_j \to Y_k$, where $q_i$ is a residual codon coordinate, $T_j$ is a translation readout, and $Y_k$ is a stability or turnover-side readout.  The admissible controls include amino-acid composition, organism identity, GC3, coding-sequence length, support-density baseline for the projected coordinates, and matched mRNA controls; protein-abundance controls are included when the downstream readout requires them.

\paragraph{Finite run.}
The stability-boundary run is identified by experiment id \texttt{b\_star\_q6\_translation\_conditioned\_stability\_boundary\_powered} and run id \texttt{18a9672b4384}.  It used five folds and required at least $300$ joined rows for an organism to contribute.  Three organism-level entries were available.  In \textit{Escherichia coli} K-12 MG1655, the primary downstream readout was mRNA half-life with $2517$ joined rows.  In \textit{Homo sapiens}, the primary downstream readout was protein turnover with $6697$ protein-turnover rows and $11322$ mRNA-half-life rows available.  In \textit{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}, the primary downstream readout was protein turnover with $3537$ protein-turnover rows and $4759$ mRNA-half-life rows available.  The run status was passed, but its biological call was not a full crossing of the boundary; the recorded verdict was partial.

\paragraph{Unconditioned stability-side association.}
Before the translation-conditioning test is interpreted, the finite rows show that the residual coordinate block has a small positive association with the available stability-side readouts in all three organism entries.  Across the three entries, the $B^*_{Q6}$ stability-side prediction summary has minimum $0.018837736273789707$, median $0.027727034742559664$, mean $0.02753157569086806$, and maximum $0.03602995605625481$, with $3$ positive entries and $0$ negative entries.  These numbers only say that the residual coordinate block carries weak finite predictive signal for the available downstream readouts.  They do not by themselves establish a stability mechanism, a degradation-rate law, a folding statement, or a phenotype statement.

\paragraph{Translation-conditioned test.}
The decisive boundary check asks whether the downstream association remains meaningful after the matched translation readout and the declared controls are imposed.  The full-control change in $R^2$ is the relevant finite statistic.  In aggregate over the three organism entries, the full-control $\Delta R^2$ summary has minimum $-0.004953854025603666$, median $-0.0033500708066446228$, mean $-0.0025553576666936064$, and maximum $0.0006378518321674687$.  The weighted full-control $\Delta R^2$ is $-0.001572140012981015$.  Two entries are negative and one entry is positive.  The sign-flip probability for a greater-than-zero full-control effect is $0.946441650390625$, so the finite matrix does not support a robust positive translation-conditioned stability crossing.  A permuted comparison is also negative throughout: its $\Delta R^2$ summary has minimum $-0.004840148172275405$, median $-0.002926202164063474$, mean $-0.002753469686061827$, and maximum $-0.0004940587218466019$, with $3$ negative entries and $0$ positive entries.

\paragraph{Organism calls.}
The organism-level classification has two partial entries and one mediated-blocked-null entry.  This is the correct reading of the matrix: the coordinate block can be discussed as touching a stability-side frontier, but the downstream claim is not promoted to a direct codon-to-stability mechanism.  The panel allows a bounded statement that some weak residual association is present before conditioning, and that the translation-conditioned surface fails to become a stable positive aggregate after full controls.  Thus the scientifically admissible claim is a boundary statement: $B^*_{Q6}$ may be carried into a stability or turnover analysis only through a surviving translation-readout gate, and the present finite rows give a partial, not decisive, result for that gate.

\paragraph{Comparison with neighboring finite records.}
The contrast with the structural-order boundary sharpens the interpretation.  A separate structural-order run on two organisms reported positive full-control $\Delta R^2$ values in both entries, with minimum $0.002688284523492457$, mean and median $0.005284160876443678$, maximum $0.007880037229394898$, weighted full-control $\Delta R^2$ $0.004764742175882462$, and sign-flip probability $0.0029296875$.  That neighboring matrix was classified as crossing to a structural-order layer.  The stability-boundary matrix here does not behave that way: its weighted full-control statistic is negative, its sign-flip probability is high, and its organism calls remain partial or blocked.  A second neighboring finite record, the minimal-compilation-law test, also warns against over-compression: the measured low-dimensional readout set left median compressibility $0.026$, with organism values $0.024368095798914635$ for zebrafish, $0.005717621488177116$ for \textit{E. coli}, $0.02635576736932188$ for human, and $0.13234136547055853$ for yeast.  Those values keep the residual source from being treated as already explained by the measured readouts.

\paragraph{Local interpretation.}
The finite result is therefore a translation-conditioned stability-boundary record with a partial call.  It supports neither a direct stability law nor a degradation-rate mechanism.  It records that the residual coordinate block has small finite downstream association before full interpretation, and that the translation-conditioned aggregate is not positive under the declared controls.  The admissible language is mediation-boundary language: if a future organism- and CDS-matched stability, turnover, pulse-chase, or perturbation contact supplies stronger rows, then the first required step remains $q_i \to T_j$ survival under the controls, followed by a test of whether conditioning on $T_j$ reduces the $q_i \to Y_k$ association.

\paragraph{Boundary of non-claims.}
No translation realization follows merely from the $B^*_{Q6}$ coordinate geometry, and no protein stability, degradation, folding quality, physical admissibility, function, phenotype, mechanism, or universal biological rule follows from the finite associations reported here.  Modeled tAI or stAI coupling is not survival evidence for a stability readout.  Matched mRNA abundance is a control surface, not a translation readout by itself.  Bulk proteomics abundance is not a substitute for a stability or turnover measurement.  A downstream stability statement is admissible only when a separate layer-matched contact supplies the stability-side rows and the same-coordinate translation-readout gate survives before the downstream association is interpreted.
