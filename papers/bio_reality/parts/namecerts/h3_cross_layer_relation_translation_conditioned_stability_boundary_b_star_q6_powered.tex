\subsection{Translation-Conditioned Stability Boundary for $B^*_{Q6}$ Residual Coordinates}
\label{sec:bioreality-q6-translation-conditioned-stability-boundary}

\paragraph{Finite panel and admissible readout.}
This record concerns a finite organism-by-CDS panel in which each $B^*_{Q6}$ coordinate is first residualized against amino-acid composition before it is allowed to meet any downstream molecular readout.  The admissible route is not a direct map from codon topology to protein fate.  The panel first asks whether a coordinate $q_i$ has a translation-layer contact $T_j$ after controls for amino-acid composition, organism, $GC3$, CDS length, $M$-density, and matched mRNA terms.  Only after that gate is considered can a protein-stability or degradation-rate surface be read as a translation-conditioned residual association.  The finite run used five folds, a minimum joined-row threshold of $300$, and seed \texttt{sha256:1deaaafe98cd16982d810af72f6389a20be0dc19cbd46bc966593dd507bad122}.  The joined stability-side rows were organism-specific: \textit{Escherichia coli} K--12 MG1655 contributed $2517$ rows for \texttt{mrna\_half\_life}; \textit{Homo sapiens} contributed $11322$ rows for \texttt{mrna\_half\_life} and $6697$ rows for \texttt{protein\_turnover}; and \textit{Saccharomyces cerevisiae} contributed $4759$ rows for \texttt{mrna\_half\_life} and $3537$ rows for \texttt{protein\_turnover}.  The \textit{E. coli} entry is therefore a boundary contact through an RNA-stability surface, while the human and yeast entries carry protein-turnover rows.

\paragraph{Controlled stability statistic.}
The unconditioned $B^*_{Q6}$ stability-side prediction was finite but small across the three organism entries.  Its summary was positive in all three entries, with minimum $0.018837736273789707$, median $0.027727034742559664$, mean $0.02753157569086806$, and maximum $0.03602995605625481$.  The translation-conditioned full-control surface did not preserve a uniformly positive downstream gain.  Its aggregate $\Delta R^2$ list had one positive and two negative entries, summarized by
$$
\begin{aligned}
\min(\Delta R^2) &= -0.004953854025603666,\\
\operatorname{median}(\Delta R^2) &= -0.0033500708066446228,\\
\operatorname{mean}(\Delta R^2) &= -0.0025553576666936064,\\
\max(\Delta R^2) &= 0.0006378518321674687.
\end{aligned}
$$
The row-weighted full-control value was $-0.001572140012981015$, and the sign-flip probability for a positive full-control effect was $0.946441650390625$.  The permutation comparator was also negative in every entry, with minimum $-0.004840148172275405$, median $-0.002926202164063474$, mean $-0.002753469686061827$, and maximum $-0.0004940587218466019$.  The finite classification is therefore \texttt{partial}: two organism entries were called \texttt{partial}, and one was called \texttt{mediated\_blocked\_null}.  This is a boundary result, not a stability mechanism.

\paragraph{Interpretation of the boundary.}
The numerical pattern says that $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinates can show small direct association with the available stability-side surfaces, but the mandatory translation-conditioning step does not promote that association into a stable cross-layer law.  The correct reading is local and asymmetric: the panel allows a finite residual association to be reported, but it blocks the stronger claim that codon residual geometry by itself explains protein stability, degradation, folding quality, mature abundance, molecular function, phenotype, or organism-wide rule.  In symbols, the reported statistic is constrained to the gated form
$$
\begin{aligned}
q_i &\longrightarrow T_j,\\
(q_i,T_j) &\longrightarrow P_k^{\mathrm{stab}},
\end{aligned}
$$
where the second line is interpreted only after the first line survives its controls.  A direct $q_i \to P_k^{\mathrm{stab}}$ association is not enough for mechanism, because conditioning on $T_j$ must reduce or absorb the direct association before a mediation claim is biologically meaningful.

A nearby structural-order check is stronger but belongs to a different readout: its full-control $\Delta R^2$ values were positive in two entries, with minimum $0.002688284523492457$, mean and median $0.005284160876443678$, maximum $0.007880037229394898$, and sign-flip probability $0.0029296875$. They do not convert the present stability-side \texttt{partial} call into a folding or function claim.

\paragraph{Local cannot-claim boundary.}
This chapter records a finite, controlled contact between amino-acid-quotient residualized $B^*_{Q6}$ coordinates, translation readouts, mRNA controls, and available stability-side surfaces.  It does not establish translation realization unless the matched translation readout survives its own controls.  It does not establish folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, phenotype, mechanism, degradation law, or universality.  Bulk protein abundance, modeled tAI or stAI weights, mRNA half-life, and workflow eligibility are not substitutes for a layer-matched stability, turnover, pulse-chase, degradation-rate, or perturbation contact.  The present result is best kept as a translation-conditioned boundary with a \texttt{partial} finite verdict: it permits cautious reporting of small controlled residual associations and refuses direct promotion from $B^*_{Q6}$ geometry to protein-stability mechanism.
