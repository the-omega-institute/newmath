\subsection{Translation-conditioned stability boundary for $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residuals}
\label{sec:bior reality-q6-translation-conditioned-stability-boundary}

\paragraph{Finite panel.}
The object tested here is a finite organism-by-CDS panel in which each $B^{*}_{Q6}$ coordinate is first residualized against amino-acid quotient structure before it is allowed to meet a biological readout.  The residual coordinates are not read as protein-stability variables.  They enter the analysis only through a gated chain
$$
\begin{aligned}
q_i \longrightarrow T_j \longrightarrow H_k,
\end{aligned}
$$
where $q_i$ is a residual synonymous coordinate, $T_j$ is a matched translation readout, and $H_k$ is a downstream protein-stability, degradation, turnover, or half-life surface when such a surface is actually present on the same finite row set.  The required controls are amino-acid composition, organism identity, GC3, CDS length, $M$-density, matched mRNA abundance where available, and the translation readout itself before any downstream interpretation is attempted.

\paragraph{Observed row support.}
The powered stability run used five folds and required at least $300$ joined rows.  Three organisms supplied usable downstream rows.  In \textit{Escherichia coli} K12 MG1655 the available downstream surface was mRNA half-life, with $2517$ joined rows.  In \textit{Homo sapiens} the primary downstream surface was protein turnover, with $6697$ protein-turnover rows and $11322$ mRNA-half-life rows available.  In \textit{Saccharomyces cerevisiae} the primary downstream surface was protein turnover, with $3537$ protein-turnover rows and $4759$ mRNA-half-life rows available.  Thus the contact is finite and organism-scoped; it is not a universal biological table, and it is not a direct assay of folding, function, phenotype, or physical admissibility.

\paragraph{Direct residual-to-stability association.}
Before conditioning on translation, the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual block did show small positive predictive contact with the selected stability surfaces.  Across the three organism entries the direct stability statistic had minimum $0.018837736273789707$, median $0.027727034742559664$, mean $0.02753157569086806$, and maximum $0.03602995605625481$, with $3$ positive entries and $0$ negative entries.  This is a finite association signal, not a mechanism.  Its role is to test whether a downstream association remains once the mandatory translation gate is inserted.

\paragraph{Translation-conditioned result.}
After the full control set and translation conditioning are applied, the aggregate downstream increment is not positive as a stable cross-organism effect.  The full-control delta-$R^2$ summary has
$$
\begin{aligned}
\min &= -0.004953854025603666,\\
\operatorname{median} &= -0.0033500708066446228,\\
\operatorname{mean} &= -0.0025553576666936064,\\
\max &= 0.0006378518321674687.
\end{aligned}
$$
There are $2$ negative organism entries and $1$ positive entry, and the weighted full-control delta-$R^2$ is $-0.001572140012981015$.  The sign-flip probability for being greater than zero is $0.946441650390625$, so the observed finite panel does not support a positive translation-conditioned stability boundary as a clean cross-organism result.  The permutation control is also centered below zero: its delta-$R^2$ values have minimum $-0.004840148172275405$, median $-0.002926202164063474$, mean $-0.002753469686061827$, and maximum $-0.0004940587218466019$, with $3$ negative entries and no positive entry.

\paragraph{Call structure.}
The run-level verdict is partial.  The organism-call ledger contains $2$ partial entries and $1$ mediated-blocked-null entry.  This means that the direct $q_i \to H_k$ surface is visible at small effect size, but the stricter chain $q_i \to T_j \to H_k$ does not close as a positive translation-conditioned stability mechanism on the available rows.  The correct reading is therefore a boundary statement: $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residuals may be carried forward into stability analysis only after the translation step has survived its own controlled test, and any stability quantity must be reported as a translation-conditioned residual association rather than as codon topology explaining degradation, folding, or protein state.

\paragraph{Relation to neighboring finite tests.}
The same coordinate family behaves differently on nearby readout surfaces.  In the structural-order boundary run, only two organisms were joined, \textit{E. coli} with $3412$ rows and yeast with $5119$ rows, and the translation-conditioned order statistic was positive: full-control delta-$R^2$ minimum $0.002688284523492457$, mean and median $0.005284160876443678$, maximum $0.007880037229394898$, weighted value $0.004764742175882462$, and sign-flip probability $0.0029296875$.  In the translation-gated structural-order run, the residual block had $9$ coordinates: $K\_AAA$, $Arg\_AGR$, $Ile\_AUA$, $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$, $Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG$, $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$, $Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG$, $Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY$, and $f3\_stress$.  The rank checks were sufficient in both organisms, with $\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde X)=9$, $23$ ranked control columns, and residual degrees of freedom $3713$ in \textit{E. coli} and $5781$ in yeast.  Those structure-confidence rows had join hit rates $0.999198$ and $0.998108$, and the structural-order partial $R^2$ values were $0.03059081709413562$ and $0.0715885627979685$.  These neighboring results explain why the present stability chapter must be kept narrow: a positive boundary on one downstream surface does not license promotion to another surface.

\paragraph{Compression check.}
The minimal-compilation diagnostic also argues against over-reading the stability result.  Across computed organisms the residual source was not highly compressed by the measured low-dimensional readout dictionary.  The reported compressibilities were $0.024368095798914635$ for zebrafish, $0.005717621488177116$ for \textit{E. coli}, $0.02635576736932188$ for human, and $0.13234136547055853$ for yeast, with median approximately $0.026$.  The corresponding residual-energy fractions after the readout dictionary were $0.9756319042010854$, $0.9942823785118229$, $0.9736442326306781$, and $0.8676586345294415$.  Each organism-level permutation probability in that diagnostic was $0.000999000999000999$, but the verdict was still that the measured dictionary did not compress $P_Q$ strongly.  The stability boundary should therefore be read as a finite controlled interface, not as a compact law of protein state.

\paragraph{Local no-promotion rule.}
The chapter supports only the following statement: on the available finite rows, $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates show small direct association with the selected stability or turnover surfaces, but the translation-conditioned downstream increment is partial and aggregate-negative under the declared controls.  No translation mechanism follows from the coordinate geometry alone.  No folding, physical admissibility, biological function, phenotype, degradation mechanism, localization mechanism, or universal law follows from these numbers.  Any such claim would require its own matched contact and its own controlled statistic on the relevant layer.
