\subsection{Translation-Conditioned Stability Boundary for $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ Residuals}\label{sec:biorality-q6-translation-conditioned-stability-boundary}

\paragraph{Finite panel.}
This chapter records a finite cross-layer test for the claim that a $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinate may be interpreted near a protein-stability or degradation-rate surface only after a same-entry translation readout has first been admitted. The carrier is a finite organism-by-CDS panel with $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates, matched translation readouts, matched mRNA controls, and a downstream stability-side readout where the row join exists. Each $q_i$ coordinate is first residualized against amino-acid composition, and the downstream readout is not read as a direct codon-topology effect. The admissible chain is instead
$$
\begin{aligned}
q_i \longrightarrow T_j \longrightarrow U_k,
\end{aligned}
$$
where $T_j$ is the translation readout and $U_k$ is the available stability-side surface, such as protein turnover when present. A direct $q_i \to U_k$ association is therefore only a screening quantity. The reported object is the residual association left after the translation step and the declared controls have been applied.

\paragraph{Joined readout rows.}
The powered run used five folds, required at least $300$ joined rows per organism, and was recorded under seed \texttt{sha256:1deaaafe98cd16982d810af72f6389a20be0dc19cbd46bc966593dd507bad122}. Three organism-level stability-side joins were available. For \emph{Escherichia coli} K--12 MG1655 the primary readout was mRNA half-life with $2517$ joined rows. For \emph{Homo sapiens} the primary readout was protein turnover with $6697$ protein-turnover rows and $11322$ mRNA-half-life rows. For \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae} the primary readout was protein turnover with $3537$ protein-turnover rows and $4759$ mRNA-half-life rows. The finite-row status of the run was \texttt{passed}; the biological verdict was \texttt{partial}.

\paragraph{Direct stability-side screening.}
Before translation conditioning, the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual block had a positive stability-side predictive surface in all three organism entries. The direct screening statistic had
$$
\begin{aligned}
\min R^2 &= 0.018837736273789707,\\
\operatorname{median} R^2 &= 0.027727034742559664,\\
\operatorname{mean} R^2 &= 0.02753157569086806,\\
\max R^2 &= 0.03602995605625481,
\end{aligned}
$$
with $n=3$, $3$ positive entries, and $0$ negative entries. This is evidence that the residual coordinate block can touch the available stability-side readout surface in the joined panels. It is not, by itself, evidence for stability control, degradation mechanism, folding quality, translation dynamics, function, phenotype, physical admissibility, or a universal biological rule.

\paragraph{Translation-conditioned boundary statistic.}
The decisive statistic is the full-control change after the translation readout has been included. The aggregate full-control $\Delta R^2$ distribution was
$$
\begin{aligned}
\min \Delta R^2 &= -0.004953854025603666,\\
\operatorname{median}\Delta R^2 &= -0.0033500708066446228,\\
\operatorname{mean}\Delta R^2 &= -0.0025553576666936064,\\
\max \Delta R^2 &= 0.0006378518321674687.
\end{aligned}
$$
Across the three organism entries, two were negative and one was positive. The weighted full-control value was
$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta R^2_{\mathrm{weighted}}=-0.001572140012981015,
\end{aligned}
$$
and the sign-flip probability for a positive full-control shift was $0.946441650390625$. A permuted comparison remained non-positive in all three entries, with minimum $-0.004840148172275405$, median $-0.002926202164063474$, mean $-0.002753469686061827$, and maximum $-0.0004940587218466019$. The organism calls were two \texttt{partial} entries and one \texttt{mediated\_blocked\_null} entry. Thus the direct $q_i \to U_k$ surface is real enough to be finite-row visible, but it does not cross the stronger boundary required for a stable translation-conditioned mechanism claim.

\paragraph{Comparison with adjacent finite tests.}
The same residual family was not highly compressed by the measured low-dimensional readout dictionary in the minimal-compilation test. The organism-level compressibilities were $0.024368095798914635$ for \emph{Danio rerio}, $0.005717621488177116$ for \emph{E. coli} K--12 MG1655, $0.02635576736932188$ for \emph{H. sapiens}, and $0.13234136547055853$ for \emph{S. cerevisiae}, with median compressibility $0.026$ and permutation value $0.000999000999000999$ in each computed organism. This supports a conservative reading: the residual source is not exhausted by the measured readouts used in that finite descriptive test. By contrast, the translation-conditioned structural-order boundary had a positive full-control $\Delta R^2$ surface in both available entries, with weighted value $0.004764742175882462$ and sign-flip probability $0.0029296875$. The structural-order survival panel also reported $R^2_{SO}=0.03059081709413562$ for \emph{E. coli} and $R^2_{SO}=0.0715885627979685$ for \emph{S. cerevisiae}, with structural-order join hit rates $0.999198$ and $0.998108$, respectively. These adjacent facts show that the stability boundary is not being rejected by a blanket inability of the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ block to meet downstream readouts; it is specifically the translation-conditioned stability or degradation interpretation that remains only partial.

\paragraph{Local interpretation.}
The finite conclusion is that $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates may be carried to a protein-stability boundary only as a gated, translation-conditioned association. The direct residual block predicts the available stability-side surface weakly but consistently across the three joined organism entries, while the conditioning statistic does not show a robust positive translation-mediated shift. The chapter therefore allows the statement that a stability-side association exists in the finite joined panel and blocks the stronger statement that the association is explained by the measured translation readout.

\paragraph{Local no-promotion rule.}
No unconditioned translation realization, ribosome-occupancy claim, folding claim, protein-stability mechanism, degradation mechanism, physical-admissibility claim, molecular-function claim, phenotype claim, or universal biological law follows from this panel. A modeled tAI or stAI surface is not a measured stability assay. Bulk protein abundance is not a protein-stability or turnover assay unless a layer-matched stability contact is actually present. A direct $q_i \to U_k$ surface is not a mechanism unless the same row also carries a surviving $q_i \to T_j$ translation entry and conditioning on $T_j$ reduces the direct downstream association under amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, CDS length, M-density, matched mRNA, and protein-abundance controls. The present record stops at a partial translation-conditioned boundary.
