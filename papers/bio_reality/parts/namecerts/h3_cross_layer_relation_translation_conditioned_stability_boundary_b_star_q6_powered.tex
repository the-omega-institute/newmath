\subsection{Translation-conditioned stability boundary for $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates}\label{sec:bioreality-q6-translation-conditioned-stability-boundary}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite panel.}
This record tests whether $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates can be allowed to touch a protein-stability or degradation-rate surface only after passing through a matched translation readout.  The carrier is a finite organism-by-CDS panel with amino-acid-quotient residual coordinates, translation readouts, matched mRNA controls, and a downstream stability-like readout where the row join is available.  The tested run used five folds, required at least $300$ joined rows, and is identified by the deterministic seed $\mathrm{sha256{:}1deaaafe98cd16982d810af72f6389a20be0dc19cbd46bc966593dd507bad122}$.  The joined stability surface contains three organism entries.  In \emph{Escherichia coli} K-12 MG1655 the primary downstream readout is mRNA half-life with $2517$ rows.  In \emph{Homo sapiens} the primary downstream readout is protein turnover with $6697$ protein-turnover rows and $11322$ matched mRNA-half-life rows.  In \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae} the primary downstream readout is protein turnover with $3537$ protein-turnover rows and $4759$ matched mRNA-half-life rows.

\paragraph{Controlled statistic.}
For each organism, the statistic first asks whether the residual coordinate block $q$ predicts the downstream stability-like readout after the local controls have been applied.  It then asks whether this association remains after the matched translation readout is included.  The object reported here is therefore not a direct codon-to-stability mechanism.  It is the residual change in held-out explanatory power after translation conditioning.  Writing $\Delta R^2_{\mathrm{full}}$ for the full-control translation-conditioned increment, the aggregate surface is
$$
\begin{aligned}
\min \Delta R^2_{\mathrm{full}}&=-0.004953854025603666,\\
\operatorname{median}(\Delta R^2_{\mathrm{full}})&=-0.0033500708066446228,\\
\operatorname{mean}(\Delta R^2_{\mathrm{full}})&=-0.0025553576666936064,\\
\max \Delta R^2_{\mathrm{full}}&=0.0006378518321674687.
\end{aligned}
$$
Across the three organism entries, two increments are negative and one is positive.  The weighted full-control increment is $-0.001572140012981015$.  A sign-flip test for positivity gives $p=0.946441650390625$, so the finite panel does not support a positive translation-conditioned stability boundary in aggregate.  The matched permutation surface is also negative throughout, with minimum $-0.004840148172275405$, median $-0.002926202164063474$, mean $-0.002753469686061827$, maximum $-0.0004940587218466019$, and $3$ negative entries out of $3$.

\paragraph{Direct downstream association before conditioning.}
Before the translation-conditioning step, the same coordinate block has a small positive downstream association in all three organism entries.  The summary for the unconditioned $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ association with the stability-like surface is
$$
\begin{aligned}
\min R^2_{q\to S}&=0.018837736273789707,\\
\operatorname{median}(R^2_{q\to S})&=0.027727034742559664,\\
\operatorname{mean}(R^2_{q\to S})&=0.02753157569086806,\\
\max R^2_{q\to S}&=0.03602995605625481.
\end{aligned}
$$
The positive count is $3$ out of $3$.  This is enough to say that the coordinate block has a finite downstream statistical footprint before conditioning, but it is not enough to promote the footprint to a protein-stability claim.  The translation-conditioned surface reverses the interpretation: once the matched translation readout is included, the aggregate increment is negative and the positive sign test fails.

\paragraph{Verdict.}
The finite-row verdict is \emph{partial}.  The organism-level calls consist of two partial entries and one mediated-blocked-null entry.  The correct local statement is that $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates show small downstream associations on the available stability-like surfaces, while the full-control translation-conditioned increment does not remain positive across the panel.  In matrix language, the admissible readback has the form $q_i \to T_j \to S_k$, but the observed summary does not license a direct $q_i \to S_k$ biological mechanism.  The result is compatible with a boundary condition: downstream stability-like readouts may be inspected only after translation survival and conditioning have been recorded, and the present aggregate evidence is not a positive stability-promotion result.

\paragraph{Local no-promotion rule.}
This chapter records a bounded finite-row relation.  No translation realization follows from codon usage, modeled adaptation weights, or residual geometry alone.  No folding claim, physical-admissibility claim, biological-function claim, degradation mechanism, phenotype claim, or universal biological law follows from the observed $R^2$ summaries.  Such claims would require their own layer-matched contact, their own joined rows, and their own controlled statistic.  The only positive statement retained here is the guarded one: the available finite panel permits a partial translation-conditioned stability-boundary readback, and its aggregate conditioned increment is not positive.
