\subsection{Translation-conditioned stability boundary for $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates}\label{sec:bioreality-q6-translation-conditioned-stability-boundary}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite panel.}
This record tests whether a synonymous residual coordinate in the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ family may be read against a downstream stability or degradation surface only after the same coordinate has passed through a measured translation layer.  The carrier is a finite organism-by-CDS table: each row carries amino-acid-quotient-residualized $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ coordinates, CDS length, amino-acid composition, GC3, the $M$-density baseline induced by the projected $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ support, matched mRNA controls, matched translation readouts, and a stability-side readout where available.  The readout is not a direct codon-to-stability assertion.  It is a gated matrix of the form $q_i \to T_j \to U_k$, where $q_i$ is a residual coordinate, $T_j$ is a translation readout, and $U_k$ is a stability or degradation-rate surface.

\paragraph{Coordinate and rank conditions.}
The nine $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ coordinates used by the surrounding finite panel are $K\_AAA$, $Arg\_AGR$, $Ile\_AUA$, $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$, $Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG$, $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$, $Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG$, $Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY$, and $f3\_stress$.  In the matched structural-order computation, the same coordinate family had full residual rank after controls: for \emph{Escherichia coli} K-12 MG1655, $\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde X)=9$ with $\operatorname{rank}(Z)=23$, residual degrees of freedom $3713$, and residual response energy $136607.52873622958$; for \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}, $\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde X)=9$ with $\operatorname{rank}(Z)=23$, residual degrees of freedom $5781$, and residual response energy $620506.1490869389$.  Thus the boundary considered here is not an $n=3$ saturated organism-level fit; it is a CDS-row computation whose stability interpretation is restricted by the translation gate.

\paragraph{Joined stability readouts.}
The stability-side joins are finite and organism-specific.  For \emph{E. coli} K-12 MG1655, the primary stability-side readout is mRNA half-life with $2517$ joined rows.  For \emph{Homo sapiens}, the primary readout is protein turnover with $6697$ joined rows, while the matched mRNA half-life table contains $11322$ rows.  For \emph{S. cerevisiae}, the primary readout is protein turnover with $3537$ joined rows, while the matched mRNA half-life table contains $4759$ rows.  The computation uses $5$ folds and requires at least $300$ joined rows before an organism-level entry is interpreted.

\paragraph{Direct and conditioned scores.}
Before the translation-conditioning test is imposed, the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ coordinate block has a positive direct association with the stability-side surface in all three reported organism entries: the direct score summary has $n=3$, minimum $0.018837736273789707$, median $0.027727034742559664$, mean $0.02753157569086806$, maximum $0.03602995605625481$, and $3$ positive entries.  The relevant boundary test is stricter: after the matched translation readout and full controls are included, the residual gain for the stability-side association is summarized by
$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta R^2_{\mathrm{full}} &: \min=-0.004953854025603666,\\
\operatorname{median}(\Delta R^2_{\mathrm{full}}) &= -0.0033500708066446228,\\
\operatorname{mean}(\Delta R^2_{\mathrm{full}}) &= -0.0025553576666936064,\\
\max(\Delta R^2_{\mathrm{full}}) &= 0.0006378518321674687.
\end{aligned}
$$
There are $2$ negative full-control entries and $1$ positive full-control entry.  The row-weighted full-control value is $-0.001572140012981015$.  The sign-flip test for a positive full-control effect gives $p=0.946441650390625$, so the finite panel does not support a stable positive translation-conditioned stability gain across the joined organisms.  The permuted full-control comparison is also nonpositive in all three entries, with minimum $-0.004840148172275405$, median $-0.002926202164063474$, mean $-0.002753469686061827$, maximum $-0.0004940587218466019$, and $3$ negative entries.

\paragraph{Boundary call.}
The organism calls are mixed: $2$ entries are classified as partial and $1$ as mediated-blocked null.  The chapter-level verdict is therefore partial, not positive closure.  The data show that the residual coordinates can register finite direct association with a stability-side readout, but that association is not promoted into a codon-topology mechanism.  The admissible statement is narrower: in this finite panel, $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ may be carried to the stability boundary only through a matched translation-readout gate, and the measured full-control stability increment is weak enough that the downstream layer remains boundary-level rather than mechanism-level.

\paragraph{Relation to adjacent finite rows.}
The same coordinate family crossed more cleanly into a predicted structural-order surface in the separate translation-conditioned structural-order computation: that test had $n=2$, direct order scores with minimum $0.03664544696481631$, maximum $0.1604655467663032$, mean and median $0.09855549686555976$, and a positive full-control structural-order increment with mean $0.005284160876443678$, weighted value $0.004764742175882462$, and sign-flip $p=0.0029296875$.  That contrast matters locally: it prevents this stability boundary from being read as a generic cross-layer success rule.  The minimal-compilation row also gives a negative caution.  Across four organisms the measured low-dimensional readout dictionary failed to compress $P_Q$ strongly: the median compressibility was $0.026$, with organism values $0.024368095798914635$ for \emph{Danio rerio}, $0.005717621488177116$ for \emph{E. coli}, $0.02635576736932188$ for human, and $0.13234136547055853$ for yeast; all reported permutation values there were $0.000999000999000999$.  Hence the stability-boundary result is best read as a constrained finite-row contact, not as an explanation of the residual source.

\paragraph{Local no-promotion rule.}
No translation mechanism, folding pathway, physical admissibility statement, molecular function, phenotype, degradation mechanism, or universal biological rule follows from the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ coordinates themselves.  Modeled tAI or stAI coupling is not survival evidence for protein stability.  Bulk protein abundance is not a stability or turnover assay unless a separate matched stability contact supplies that layer.  A direct $q_i \to U_k$ association is reportable only as a preconditioning or residual association; it becomes a translation-conditioned statement only when the same finite row supports the $q_i \to T_j$ step and conditioning on $T_j$ is explicitly evaluated under amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, CDS length, $M$-density, matched mRNA, and protein-side controls.
