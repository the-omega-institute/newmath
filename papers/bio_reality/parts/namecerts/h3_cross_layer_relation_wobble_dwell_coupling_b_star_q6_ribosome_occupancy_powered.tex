\subsection{Position-Controlled Wobble--Dwell Coupling for $B^*_{Q6}$}\label{sec:bioreality-wobble-dwell-coupling-b-star-q6-ribosome-occupancy}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite row under test.}
The finite object in this record is a codon-window occupancy table joined to ordered coding sequence rows and read back through the nine synonymous residual coordinates of $B^*_{Q6}$.  The tested families are the same nine-coordinate list used throughout the $B^*_{Q6}$ translation interface:
$K\_AAA$, $Arg\_AGR$, $Ile\_AUA$, $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$, $Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG$, $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$, $Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG$, $Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY$, and $f3\_stress$.  The occupancy file supplied $5754$ genes, the ordered coding-sequence table supplied $6013$ genes, and the overlap after the length check was $5754$ genes, with length-mismatch count $0$.  Thus the row set is not an abstract codon-frequency comparison: it is a same-gene, ordered-CDS occupancy comparison on $5754$ joined genes.

\paragraph{Statistic.}
For each synonymous family $q$, the local readout is the relative ribosome occupancy assigned to the same codon windows as the ordered CDS row.  The analysis removes a positional confound before reading any family effect: occupancy is demeaned inside exact file-provided window/codon-index and amino-acid bins, and the null distribution shuffles synonymous labels only within the same bin.  In symbols, for a codon instance $a$ lying in bin $b(a)$, the effect statistic has the form
$$
\begin{aligned}
E(q)
  &= \Delta_q\bigl(y_a - \overline{y}_{b(a)}\bigr), \\
Null95(q)
  &= \operatorname{quantile}_{0.95}\{E_\pi(q):\pi \text{ permutes synonymous labels within } b(a)\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
Here $y_a$ is the supplied local occupancy value, and the permutation is not allowed to move a codon instance across its position/amino-acid bin.  This is the important finite-row control: the comparison asks whether a synonymous choice separates local ribosome occupancy after codon position and amino-acid identity have already been accounted for.

\paragraph{Finite outcome.}
The null calculation was run for all $9$ families, and all $9$ families had at least $200$ within-bin permutations; the requested permutation count was $200$.  The decision rule used a description-length penalty with $DL=2$ and $\lambda=0.01$, together with the sign gate $C_H>0$.  Under this rule exactly three $B^*_{Q6}$ families passed all gates:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathcal{P}_{\mathrm{wobble\mbox{-}dwell}}
  = \{K\_AAA,\ Arg\_AGR,\ f3\_stress\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The finite verdict is therefore positive but narrow: at least one, and in this row exactly three, synonymous families show position-bin-controlled local ribosome-occupancy separation exceeding the within-bin label-permutation $Null95$ while also passing the $\lambda DL$ and $C_H$ filters.  This supports a bounded wobble--dwell coupling statement for local occupancy windows, not a general statement about every coordinate or every organism.

\paragraph{Relation to the broader $B^*_{Q6}$ translation panel.}
This row sits downstream of earlier finite checks that established the $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinate table as non-vacuous.  In the proxy coupling matrix, all $9$ synonymous residual coordinates were computed across three organisms, the break condition $|A_{ij}|<0.01$ for all entries was false, and the largest reported absolute coupling was $1882464.1106377328$ with $9$ nonzero entries.  A separate modeled-tAI panel used $40$ organisms, residualized against $24$ control columns of rank $23$, retained residual degrees of freedom $17$, and obtained a $9\times 9$ modeled translation-readout survival matrix with $R^2_{QT}=0.9003849000036579$.  Those companion checks do not prove measured ribosome behavior, but they make the present occupancy row interpretable as a direct finite contact with a ribosome-position readout rather than as an isolated naming coincidence.

\paragraph{Coordinate specificity.}
The passing set is biologically and mathematically asymmetric.  $K\_AAA$ and $Arg\_AGR$ are individual synonymous-family residual coordinates, whereas $f3\_stress$ is a composite third-base stress coordinate.  The later coordinate-decomposition audit reported $9$ single-coordinate entries on a yeast abundance target with $n=1258$, fold count $5$, permutation count $120$, and $z$-basis rank $24$; it found real-source evidence for $K\_AAA$, $Arg\_AGR$, $Ile\_AUA$, $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$, $Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG$, $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$, $Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY$, and $f3\_stress$, while $Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG$ was not listed in that real-source subset.  The $f3\_stress$ family decomposition then split the composite coordinate into $21$ synonymous-family subcoordinates on $n=1800$ joined rows, with $P_{f3}$ norm squared $52.76911713166682$, target norm squared $430.42705205115925$, and $P_{f3}$ fraction of target $0.12259712041843235$.  Nineteen of the $21$ listed subfamilies were reported as real-source families under the held-out/permutation gate.  These related rows make the present occupancy result more precise: the passing $f3\_stress$ entry should be read as a family-structured signal, not as a claim that every third-position contrast has the same dwell-time behavior.

\paragraph{Local interpretation.}
The supported statement is descriptive: synonymous codon choices in the $B^*_{Q6}$ coordinate system co-vary with local relative ribosome occupancy after exact window/codon-index and amino-acid bin controls.  The finite row is strong enough to reject the local null that every tested family is absorbed by the within-bin synonymous-label permutation baseline under the stated $\lambda DL$ rule.  It is not strong enough to assign a causal dwell-time mechanism, and it is not a population-level estimate over all organisms or all ribosome-profiling protocols.  The observed support is tied to the $5754$ joined genes, the supplied $5'$ and $3'$ occupancy arrays, the exact binning rule, the $200$-permutation null, $DL=2$, $\lambda=0.01$, and $C_H>0$.

\paragraph{Non-promotion boundary.}
This subsection only licenses the bounded occupancy-separation statement just described.  It does not by itself establish a full translation-realization pathway, protein abundance output, folding, structural order, physical admissibility, biological function, molecular mechanism, phenotype, or universal biological law.  Any stronger assertion would need its own layer-matched measurement and its own statistic: for example, direct translation-efficiency rows with matched mRNA controls for a TE claim, matched proteomics for a protein-output claim, perturbation or biochemical data for mechanism, and separate structure or function assays for higher biological interpretation.  The present row is therefore a finite, position-controlled ribosome-occupancy contact for $K\_AAA$, $Arg\_AGR$, and $f3\_stress$, and nothing above that boundary is inferred from it.
