\subsection{Position-controlled wobble dwell contact for $B^*_{Q6}$}
\label{sec:b-star-q6-wobble-dwell-ribosome-occupancy}

\paragraph{Finite row object.}
This record tests a narrowly bounded ribosome-occupancy contact for the nine synonymous residual coordinates of $B^*_{Q6}$.  The measured object is not a protein-level outcome and not a pathway-level claim.  It is a local codon-window statistic: for each synonymous family represented in the $B^*_{Q6}$ coordinate list, codon labels are compared against local relative ribosome occupancy in the same supplied footprint windows.  The joined table contains $5754$ genes after the ordered CDS table is matched to the ribosome-occupancy file.  The occupancy file contains $5754$ genes, the ordered CDS side contains $6013$ genes, the gene-id overlap is $5754$, and the length-mismatch count after the join is $0$.  Thus the finite row is not an identifier-only route; it is a same-gene, length-consistent occupancy/CDS contact.

\paragraph{Coordinates and controlled comparison.}
The analysis uses the nine $B^*_{Q6}$ residual families
$K\_AAA$, $Arg\_AGR$, $Ile\_AUA$, $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$, $Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG$, $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$, $Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG$, $Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY$, and $f3\_stress$.  The position control is local rather than global: occupancy is demeaned inside the exact file-provided window and codon-index bins, with amino-acid bins retained, and the null distribution shuffles synonymous labels only within the same bin.  The windows are the supplied $5'$ and $3'$ arrays, so the statistic asks whether codon choice separates occupancy inside matched positional contexts rather than whether a codon family is globally enriched in high-occupancy genes.

\paragraph{Null design.}
For each computed family the null is a within-position-bin synonymous-label permutation.  The run requested $200$ permutations and obtained at least $200$ permutations for all $9$ computed families.  The finite-row threshold is therefore not a free sign readout.  A family must exceed its own within-bin $Null95$ value, must have positive orientation support $C_H>0$, and must pass the stated description-length penalty with
$$
\begin{aligned}
\lambda_{DL} &= 0.01,\\
DL &= 2.
\end{aligned}
$$
Under these gates, exactly three families pass all filters: $K\_AAA$, $Arg\_AGR$, and $f3\_stress$.

\paragraph{Readout verdict.}
The supported finite statement is that at least one $B^*_{Q6}$ synonymous family, and in fact the three families $K\_AAA$, $Arg\_AGR$, and $f3\_stress$, shows local ribosome-occupancy separation after controlling by exact position/window and amino-acid bin, with the separation exceeding the within-bin label-permutation $Null95$ and satisfying the $\lambda_{DL}=0.01$, $DL=2$, $C_H>0$ gate.  In compact form, the positive finite row is
$$
\begin{aligned}
\{q_i : q_i \hbox{ passes the occupancy gate}\}
  &= \{K\_AAA,\ Arg\_AGR,\ f3\_stress\},\\
N_{\mathrm{joined\ genes}} &= 5754,\\
N_{\mathrm{families}} &= 9,\\
N_{\mathrm{permutations\ requested}} &= 200.
\end{aligned}
$$
This is a position-resolved occupancy co-variation result, not a causal dwell-time experiment.  The data show that the local ribosome-occupancy surface is not indifferent to all tested $B^*_{Q6}$ synonymous families under the declared finite controls.

\paragraph{Relation to broader $B^*_{Q6}$ translation readouts.}
The occupancy contact is consistent with earlier bounded translation-layer probes but is not interchangeable with them.  A separate codon-usage/tRNA-copy coupling run computed all nine $B^*_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates and found $9$ nonzero proxy coupling entries above the $0.01$ threshold, with maximum absolute coupling $1882464.1106377328$, while explicitly refusing to promote that proxy coupling to a survival score without controlled partial-$R^2$ estimation.  Another modeled-tAI run used $40$ organisms, a $9\times 9$ modeled readout matrix, residual control rank $23$, residual degrees of freedom $17$, and reported $R^2_{QT}=0.9003849000036579$ for the modeled codon-adaptation surface.  Those two rows show that $B^*_{Q6}$ has bounded contact with modeled or proxy translation-related surfaces, while the present row supplies a different contact: measured local ribosome occupancy in matched codon windows.  The present chapter therefore reports the occupancy result as its own finite statistic rather than as a consequence of the proxy coupling or the modeled-tAI fit.

\paragraph{Scope boundary.}
The only claim licensed here is local, descriptive co-variation between $B^*_{Q6}$ synonymous codon choices and relative ribosome occupancy in the supplied windows, after the stated position and amino-acid controls.  This does not establish a general translation law, a universal direction of codon effect, a causal ribosome-pausing mechanism, protein abundance, folding, structural order, physical admissibility, molecular function, phenotype, or pathway behavior.  It also does not turn tRNA gene-copy counts, modeled tAI, stAI, or codon-usage geometry into measured ribosome dynamics.  Any stronger assertion would require its own matched contact and its own controlled statistic.
