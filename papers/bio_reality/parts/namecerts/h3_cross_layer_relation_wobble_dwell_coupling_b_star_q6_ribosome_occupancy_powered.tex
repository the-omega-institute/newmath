\subsection{Local ribosome-occupancy dwell separation for the $B^*_{Q6}$ residual family}\label{subsec:bioreality-wobble-dwell-coupling-b-star-q6-ribosome-occupancy}\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Claim tested.}
This record tests a bounded translation-layer question: whether synonymous choices inside the nine $B^*_{Q6}$ residual families co-vary with local relative ribosome occupancy after the obvious positional confound is removed.  The row under consideration is not a global codon-usage statistic and is not a protein-output statistic.  It is a within-window, within-position readback: codons are compared only against synonymous alternatives that occur in the same supplied ribosome-occupancy window and the same codon-index bin, with amino-acid identity retained in the binning.  The tested family list is
$K\_AAA$, $Arg\_AGR$, $Ile\_AUA$, $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$, $Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG$, $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$, $Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG$, $Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY$, and $f3\_stress$.

\paragraph{Finite row and join.}
The occupancy file supplied both $5'$ and $3'$ codon-window arrays, and these arrays were joined to ordered coding sequences by gene identifier.  The finite join had $5754$ overlapping genes, all $5754$ of which survived the length check, with $0$ length mismatches.  The ribosome-occupancy side contained $5754$ genes and the ordered-CDS side contained $6013$ genes.  Thus the tested matrix is not a synthetic organism-level summary: it is a same-gene codon-window table in which local codon labels and local relative occupancy can be compared after positional demeaning.

\paragraph{Position-controlled statistic.}
For each synonymous family, local occupancy was first centered inside the exact file-provided window/codon-index and amino-acid bin.  The null model then shuffled synonymous labels only within that same bin.  This keeps the tested effect from being a trivial consequence of where a codon appears in a transcript window.  In the notation of the chapter, the observed family statistic is a bin-residual separation score $R_f$, and the permutation reference is the within-bin $95\%$ threshold $N_{95,f}$.  The support gate is not merely $R_f>N_{95,f}$; it also applies a description-length penalty and a direction filter:
$$
\begin{aligned}
R_f &> N_{95,f},\\
C_H(f) &> 0,\\
R_f - \lambda\,DL &> N_{95,f},\\
\lambda &= 0.01,\\
DL &= 2.
\end{aligned}
$$
The requested permutation budget was $200$ within-bin shuffles per family.  All $9$ families were computed, and all $9$ had at least $200$ permutations available for the null summary.

\paragraph{Observed support.}
Three families passed all gates: $K\_AAA$, $Arg\_AGR$, and $f3\_stress$.  The reported gate was the joint condition $C_H>0$ with $\lambda=0.01$ and $DL=2$, after comparison to the within-position-bin permutation $N_{95}$ value.  This supplies a concrete positive row for local ribosome-occupancy separation: at least one, and in this run three, $B^*_{Q6}$ synonymous families exhibit occupancy separation that is larger than the same-bin label-shuffle reference after the stated penalty.

\paragraph{Relation to modeled translation readouts.}
This row is narrower than the modeled tAI survival matrix, but it is compatible with the broader translation-readout pattern already measured for the same nine-coordinate residual family.  In the modeled per-codon tAI calculation, $40$ organisms were loaded, the $B^*_{Q6}$ design had $9$ residual coordinates, the control matrix had $24$ columns with rank $23$, and the residual degrees of freedom after controls were $17$.  The modeled translation-readout matrix had shape $9\times 9$, rank $9$ after residualization, and total $R^2_{QT}=0.9003849000036579$.  A wider modeled-tAI organism sweep covered $41$ organisms; its per-organism $R^2_{QT}$ distribution had minimum $0.1579271837024072$, first quartile $0.26353954700074694$, median $0.33934845811598124$, mean $0.38466555440501393$, third quartile $0.5143951545826744$, and maximum $0.8213457220900503$.  Those modeled rows do not substitute for measured ribosome occupancy, but they explain why the present local dwell-style test is placed in the translation-readout layer rather than in a folding or function layer.

\paragraph{Relation to downstream abundance decompositions.}
Separate abundance-side rows show that $B^*_{Q6}$ coordinates also carry protein-abundance residual components in several organisms, but those rows are not used here as proof of ribosome dwell.  For example, the complement-support calculation reported $R^2_{QP}=0.29837624987287115$ in $Saccharomyces\ cerevisiae$, with modeled-tAI complement fraction $d=0.21173219572706703$ and modeled-tAI-aligned fraction $m=0.788267804272933$.  In the same complement table, $Homo\ sapiens$ had $R^2_{QP}=0.04454565117192484$, $d=0.9208949355679455$, and $m=0.07910506443205445$, while $Escherichia\ coli\ K$-$12$ MG1655 had $R^2_{QP}=0.10767526667768725$, $d=0.6629960038578532$, and $m=0.3370039961421468$.  Direction-aware candidate ranking likewise treated measured translation efficiency, mRNA, modeled tRNA-tAI, localization, and other readouts as separate $H$ candidates rather than as interchangeable biological causes.  In that ranking, $Danio\ rerio$ selected measured translation efficiency as $H_o^*$ with $A_H=0.017594290015070787$, $C_H=0.6573753096184687$, $A_HC_H=0.011566051846174291$, and Bonferroni threshold $0.000719994971498518$ on $13140$ joined rows; $Homo\ sapiens$ selected complex membership as $H_o^*$, while $Saccharomyces\ cerevisiae$ selected mRNA.  These contrasts matter because the present row is specifically a ribosome-occupancy window test, not a general declaration that every abundance or candidate-$H$ signal is translationally mediated.

\paragraph{Interpretation.}
The finite conclusion is that the same-gene occupancy table contains a position-controlled synonymous-label signal for the $B^*_{Q6}$ family.  The strongest local statement is therefore:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\exists f\in B^*_{Q6}\quad R_f>N_{95,f}\quad\text{and}\quad C_H(f)>0\quad\text{after}\quad \lambda=0.01,\ DL=2.
\end{aligned}
$$
The computed passing set is $\{K\_AAA, Arg\_AGR, f3\_stress\}$.  This supports a bounded dwell-coupling reading: synonymous residual choices in those families are not fully absorbed by local position bins in the supplied ribosome-occupancy windows.

\paragraph{Local boundary.}
No stronger biological layer follows from this row alone.  The calculation does not establish a causal translation mechanism, does not measure tRNA abundance or charging, does not prove mature protein abundance, and does not imply folding, structural order, physical admissibility, molecular function, phenotype, or a universal biological rule.  It also does not turn modeled tAI or stAI rows into observed ribosome occupancy.  Any such stronger claim would require its own matched contact and its own statistic.  The present record is limited to a finite, position-confound-controlled ribosome-occupancy separation for three named $B^*_{Q6}$ synonymous families in a $5754$-gene joined table.
