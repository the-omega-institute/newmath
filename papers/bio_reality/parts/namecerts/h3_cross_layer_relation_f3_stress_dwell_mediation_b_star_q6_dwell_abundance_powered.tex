\subsection{Stress-dwell mediation for the $f_3$ residual component}
\label{sec:bioreality-f3-stress-dwell-mediation}

\paragraph{Finite panel.}
This record isolates the $f_3$-stress coordinate inside the nine-coordinate $B^*_{Q6}$ synonymous residual surface and asks whether a measured dwell readout carries part of the abundance component aligned with that coordinate.  The active organism is \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}.  The tested rows are not an organism-level codon table and not a three-organism rank surface: they are $1257$ joined gene rows on a yeast panel whose base design was trained on $1800$ rows with $5$ held-out folds.  The dwell variable is a per-gene local relative ribosome-occupancy summary computed from file-provided $5'$ and $3'$ A-site occupancy windows.  The target is the projection $P_{f3}$ of $Z$-residualized $\log_{10}$ protein abundance onto the $Z$-residualized $f_3$-stress coordinate on the active base-plus-dwell join.  The nuisance basis has rank $24$, while the tested $f_3$ direction has rank $1$.  The measured abundance join available for yeast in the surrounding protein-omics surface used $5815$ CDS-protein rows with join hit rate $0.9156038419146592$, so the dwell mediation calculation is a stricter same-row subset rather than a free-standing abundance screen.

\paragraph{Controlled readout.}
The primary held-out comparison is between the base abundance design and the base-plus-dwell design on the projected $P_{f3}$ target.  The finite scalars are
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{\mathrm{base}} &= 0.1855578757644517,\\
R^2_{\mathrm{full}} &= 0.20412261520502883,\\
\Delta R^2_{\mathrm{held\ out}} &= 0.01856473944057712,\\
R^2_{\mathrm{dwell\ alone}} &= 0.034336580953121976.
\end{aligned}
$$
The coordinate-target norm is $\|P_{f3}\|^2=44.20095750389323$, and $P_{f3}$ accounts for $0.1999063916718794$ of the target component used in this test.  The simple $f_3$--dwell correlation is $0.1615441450901653$, with approximate $p=7.873916057196935\times 10^{-9}$.  These values are read as finite-row association and held-out predictive increment only.  They do not say that dwell is the complete mediator, nor that $f_3$ directly measures elongation speed, ribosome pausing biophysics, protein folding, or organismal fitness.

\paragraph{Permutation boundary.}
The null model keeps the base and full designs and the fold assignment fixed while deterministically permuting row labels of $P_{f3}$.  Across $120$ permutations, the null distribution has mean $-0.0032911642620130563$, minimum $-0.013799622026666476$, maximum $0.005001350039772926$, and $95$th percentile $0.0013115362189692937$.  The observed held-out increment $0.01856473944057712$ is therefore above the fixed-readout null boundary by more than one order of magnitude.  In the finite panel, this supports the statement that the local dwell summary carries a non-null incremental readback of the $f_3$-aligned protein-abundance component after the declared residualization and fold structure.

\paragraph{Relation to the larger $B^*_{Q6}$ surface.}
The same nine residual coordinates are $K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $Leu_{CUN\,vs\,UUR}$, $Leu_{UUA\,vs\,UUG}$, $Ser_{UCR\,vs\,AGY}$, $Ser_{UCA\,vs\,UCG}$, $Thr_{ACR\,vs\,ACY}$, and $f_3\text{-stress}$.  On the measured protein-abundance surface, yeast has $R^2_{QP}=0.29837624994140827$ with all $9$ coordinates surviving the positive-threshold screen.  On the measured ribosome-TE surface, yeast has $R^2_{QT,\mathrm{meas}}=0.13681931343730436$, again with all $9$ coordinates surviving.  In the measured translation-conditioned abundance decomposition, yeast has $S_{QP}=0.3146818503437042$, $S_{QT,\mathrm{meas}}=0.13681931343730436$, raw measured mediation fraction $0.4793069656345167$, and clipped measured mediation fraction $0.4793069656345167$.  In the joint TE-plus-mRNA-stability decomposition, yeast has $R^2_{QP}=0.2715520808117803$, $R^2_{QP\mid T}=0.1439989545940314$, $R^2_{QP\mid M}=0.25453612246767165$, and $R^2_{QP\mid T,M}=0.13047582645538838$, giving TE-only fraction $0.4697188319693239$, stability-only fraction $0.0626618595344251$, and joint mediation fraction $0.5195182225621592$.  The dwell result is narrower than these whole-coordinate summaries: it identifies a local ribosome-occupancy readout that improves held-out prediction of the $f_3$-projected abundance component.

\paragraph{Positional refinement.}
A companion positional dwell test on the same organism and the same $P_{f3}$ norm reports the leading segment as $5'$ with $n=1257$, $5$ folds, and rank $1$ for the $f_3$ direction.  At the primary $50$-codon ramp setting, the weighted ramp increment is $0.0011165640103326878$, the weighted body increment is $-0.00026157313923957926$, and the ramp-minus-body contrast is $0.001378137149572267$.  The $5'$ ramp signal passes its positive gate, while the $3'$ body signal does not.  The positional conclusion is therefore weaker and more specific than a general localization claim: the data show positive ramp evidence but do not pass the paired ramp-body localization gate.

\paragraph{Compression and residual remainder.}
A yeast readout-dictionary reduction on the same family uses $n=1800$ joined rows, $5$ folds, $\lambda_{DL}=0.01$, and $120$ permutations.  Its best selection-aware coverage is $0.24538211439730545$, with selection-aware null $95$th percentile $0.029149007242956115$, leaving unexplained fraction $0.7546178856026946$.  This keeps the dwell result in the correct scale: the local dwell readout is a real incremental carrier of the $f_3$-aligned abundance component, but it is not a complete reduction of the $B^*_{Q6}$ abundance residual.

\paragraph{Verdict.}
The claim is ready at the finite-row statistical level.  The decisive inequality is
$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta R^2_{\mathrm{held\ out}} &= 0.01856473944057712 \\
&> 0.0013115362189692937 \\
&= \mathrm{Null}_{0.95}.
\end{aligned}
$$
Together with the positive $f_3$--dwell correlation and the fixed-fold permutation design, this supports a bounded statement: in the yeast joined panel, local ribosome-occupancy dwell summaries mediate a measurable part of the $f_3$-stress protein-abundance projection.  No claim about translation mechanism, folded structural order, physical admissibility, molecular function, phenotype, causal direction, or universality follows from this chapter by itself.  Any such stronger statement would require a separate layer-matched contact and its own finite statistic.
