\subsection{F3-stress dwell mediation against measured protein abundance}\label{sec:bioreality-f3-stress-dwell-mediation}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite surface.}
This record tests one rank-one coordinate of the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual surface, the coordinate named $f3\_stress$, against a yeast protein-abundance component that has first been isolated along the same residual direction.  The finite panel is not a symbolic codon argument.  It is a row-level Saccharomyces cerevisiae join with codon-derived residual coordinates, a protein-abundance target, and local ribosome-occupancy dwell summaries.  The active mediation fit used $n=1257$ joined genes from an available yeast context of $1800$ base rows.  The control basis had rank $24$, and the tested source coordinate had rank $1$.  The protein target was defined as $P_{f3}$, the projection of $Z$-residualized $\log_{10}$ protein abundance onto the $Z$-residualized $f3\_stress$ direction on the active base-plus-dwell join.

\paragraph{Controls and readouts.}
The dwell readout was built from per-gene local relative ribosome occupancy summaries using file-provided $5'$ and $3'$ A-site occupancy windows.  The sibling abundance surface establishes that the same $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual family contains nine coordinates, namely $K\_AAA$, $Arg\_AGR$, $Ile\_AUA$, $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$, $Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG$, $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$, $Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG$, $Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY$, and $f3\_stress$.  On the yeast direct protein-omics abundance surface, the CDS-abundance join used $5815$ proteins with hit rate $0.9156038419146592$, and the full nine-coordinate direct abundance fit had $R^2_{QP}=0.29837624994140827$ with all nine coordinates surviving the positive threshold.  For measured ribosome translation efficiency in yeast, the ribosome-TE join used $5128$ genes, had hit rate $0.8818572656921754$, and gave $R^2_{QT,\mathrm{meas}}=0.13681931343730436$ after the same broad family of length, amino-acid composition, $GC3$, and $M$-density controls.  These sibling readouts justify treating the dwell test as a measured cross-layer row calculation, while not by themselves supplying a causal mechanism.

\paragraph{Dwell increment.}
The registered dwell mediation fit compares a base design to a base-plus-dwell design under fixed folds.  The base held-out model explained
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{\mathrm{base}} &= 0.1855578757644517,
\end{aligned}
$$
whereas the dwell-augmented model explained
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{\mathrm{full}} &= 0.20412261520502883.
\end{aligned}
$$
The observed held-out increment was therefore
$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta R^2_{\mathrm{held\ out}}
  &= R^2_{\mathrm{full}}-R^2_{\mathrm{base}} \\
  &= 0.01856473944057712.
\end{aligned}
$$
The dwell readout alone had $R^2=0.034336580953121976$, and the empirical correlation between the $f3\_stress$ coordinate and the dwell summary was $0.1615441450901653$, with approximate $p=7.873916057196935\times 10^{-9}$.  The isolated abundance component was substantial but not the whole protein-abundance target: $\|P_{f3}\|^2=44.20095750389323$, and the $P_{f3}$ fraction of the target was $0.1999063916718794$.

\paragraph{Permutation calibration.}
The null model was a deterministic row-label permutation of $P_{f3}$, keeping the base design, full design, and five folds fixed.  It used $120$ permutations with deterministic seed $\mathrm{sha256{:}b\_star\_q6\_f3\_stress\_dwell\_mediation\_powered{:}deterministic}$.  The null distribution had mean $-0.0032911642620130563$, minimum $-0.013799622026666476$, maximum $0.005001350039772926$, and $95$th percentile $0.0013115362189692937$.  Thus the observed held-out increment exceeded the fixed-readout null boundary by more than an order of magnitude:
$$
\begin{aligned}
0.01856473944057712 &> 0.0013115362189692937.
\end{aligned}
$$
The finite verdict is consequently positive for a dwell-mediated increment on the $f3\_stress$ abundance component in this yeast panel.

\paragraph{Position and dictionary context.}
A companion positional decomposition on the same organism and the same $P_{f3}$ target used $n=1257$, rank $1$ for $f3\_stress$, control-basis rank $24$, and $120$ fixed-design permutations.  It found the leading segment by $\Delta R^2$ to be the $5'$ window: the $5'$ ramp passed, the $3'$ body did not, and the dominant segment was $5'$; the primary ramp statistic at window size $50$ had weighted $f3$ ramp held-out $\Delta R^2=0.0011165640103326878$ while the body value was $-0.00026157313923957926$.  This positional result is weaker than the aggregate dwell mediation fit, but it is directionally consistent with a local ribosome-occupancy contribution rather than a purely global abundance artifact.  A separate yeast readout-dictionary certificate used $\lambda_{DL}=0.01$, $\rho_{join}=0.05$, $n=1800$, five folds, and $120$ permutations; it reported coverage $C^{*}=0.24538211439730545$ for at most three measured readouts from the dictionary $\{\mathrm{measured\_te},\mathrm{mrna\_stability},\mathrm{turnover}\}$, leaving unexplained fraction $0.7546178856026946$.  The dwell signal therefore belongs to a partially reducible measured-readout layer, not to a completed reduction of the full abundance residual.

\paragraph{Local conclusion.}
For the registered finite matrix, the claim is supported in the following narrow sense.  In Saccharomyces cerevisiae, after the stated residualization and row join, local ribosome-occupancy dwell summaries add held-out predictive power for the $f3\_stress$-aligned protein-abundance component.  The observed $\Delta R^2=0.01856473944057712$ exceeds the permutation null $95$th percentile $0.0013115362189692937$, with $n=1257$, five folds, source rank $1$, and control-basis rank $24$.  This is evidence for a measured dwell-abundance mediation pattern attached to one $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual coordinate in one yeast finite-row panel.

\paragraph{Boundary.}
The positive dwell result is not a general translation law and is not a molecular mechanism proof.  It does not by itself establish translation realization in other organisms, folded structural order, physical admissibility, protein function, phenotype, evolutionary direction, or universality.  Bulk protein abundance, ribosome occupancy summaries, measured TE, modeled adaptation weights, and codon residual coordinates are separate layers of measurement.  A stronger biological interpretation would require an additional matched contact at that layer, with the same-row statistic recomputed under the appropriate controls.  In particular, direct $q_i\to P_k$ association is not promoted to mechanism unless a measured $q_i\to T_j$ step survives and conditioning on $T_j$ removes or reduces the corresponding $q_i\to P_k$ signal on the same finite panel.
