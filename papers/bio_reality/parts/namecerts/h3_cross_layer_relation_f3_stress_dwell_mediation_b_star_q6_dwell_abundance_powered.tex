\subsection{A dwell-mediated abundance component for the $f_3$ stress coordinate}
\label{sec:bioreality-f3-stress-dwell-mediation}

\paragraph{Finite surface.}
The record is a finite matched-row test in \textit{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}.  The active coordinate is the one-dimensional $f_3\_stress$ projection inside the nine-coordinate $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual family, and the target is the component of $Z$-residualized protein abundance lying along the $Z$-residualized $f_3\_stress$ direction.  The control basis has rank $24$ after including the intercept-level control surface, and the tested $f_3$ block has rank $1$.  On the active base-and-dwell join there are $n=1257$ matched CDS rows, drawn from a base availability count of $1800$ rows.  The abundance component has squared norm $44.20095750389323$, and it accounts for fraction $0.1999063916718794$ of the residualized abundance target on this joined surface.

\paragraph{Dwell readout and fitted comparison.}
The dwell variable is not a modeled codon-adaptation score.  It is a per-gene local relative ribosome-occupancy summary computed from file-provided $5'$ and $3'$ A-site occupancy windows.  The test compares a base design against the same design augmented by the dwell readout, with five held-out folds and deterministic row-label permutations of $P_{f3}$ keeping the base/full designs and folds fixed.  The base held-out fit gives
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{\mathrm{base}} &= 0.1855578757644517,\\
R^2_{\mathrm{full}} &= 0.20412261520502883,\\
\Delta R^2_{\mathrm{held\ out}} &= 0.01856473944057712.
\end{aligned}
$$
The dwell readout alone has $R^2=0.034336580953121976$ against the same abundance component.  The observed $f_3$--dwell correlation is $0.1615441450901653$, with approximate $p=7.873916057196935\times 10^{-9}$.

\paragraph{Permutation boundary.}
The empirical null uses $120$ deterministic permutations under seed \texttt{sha256:b\_star\_q6\_f3\_stress\_dwell\_mediation\_powered:deterministic}.  Its observed range is
$$
\begin{aligned}
\min(\Delta R^2_{\mathrm{null}}) &= -0.013799622026666476,\\
\mathrm{mean}(\Delta R^2_{\mathrm{null}}) &= -0.0032911642620130563,\\
\mathrm{Null}_{95} &= 0.0013115362189692937,\\
\max(\Delta R^2_{\mathrm{null}}) &= 0.005001350039772926.
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the observed held-out increment $0.01856473944057712$ is positive and exceeds the fixed-readout permutation $95\%$ boundary by more than an order of magnitude.  In finite-row terms, this supports a dwell-associated reduction of the $f_3\_stress$ abundance component on the yeast join, not merely an in-sample correlation.

\paragraph{Relation to the broader measured surfaces.}
The same yeast organism is strongly non-null in the direct protein-abundance surface: the PAXdb abundance join has hit rate $0.9156038419146592$ with $5815$ joined proteins, and the direct $B^{*}_{Q6}\to P$ residual fit reports $R^2_{QP}=0.29837624994140827$ with all $9$ residual coordinates surviving the positive partial-$R^2$ threshold.  Yeast also has a measured ribosome-TE surface with $5128$ genes used, ribosome-TE join hit rate $0.8818572656921754$, and $R^2_{QT,\mathrm{meas}}=0.13681931343730436$ with all $9$ coordinates surviving.  In the measured mediation decomposition for yeast, the reported quantities are
$$
\begin{aligned}
S_{QP} &= 0.3146818503437042,\\
S_{QT,\mathrm{meas}} &= 0.13681931343730436,\\
M^{QTP}_{\mathrm{meas}} &= 0.4793069656345167.
\end{aligned}
$$
These supporting rows place the dwell result inside a measured translation-and-protein panel rather than an isolated codon-usage table.  They do not by themselves specify the local dwell route; the present chapter-specific claim is the finite held-out improvement of the $f_3$ abundance component after adding the dwell readout.

\paragraph{Position and case readback.}
The positional dwell companion classifies the same yeast readout as $5'$-dominant: the $5'$ ramp window passes, the $3'$ body window does not, and the leading segment by held-out increment is \texttt{5prime}.  The finite four-case discriminant for yeast gives counts $A=0$, $B=0$, $C=0$, and $D=9$ under its modeled-translation/protein-abundance classification, with no unclassified coordinates.  The partial readout dictionary on the unified yeast context join uses $\lambda_{\mathrm{DL}}=0.01$, $\rho_{\mathrm{join}}=0.05$, $n_{\mathrm{join}}=1800$, $5$ folds, and $120$ permutations; its best selection-aware coverage is $C^{*}=0.24538211439730545$, leaving unexplained fraction $0.7546178856026946$.  These auxiliary numbers are consistent with the dwell result being a real but partial readout of the abundance residual, not a closed account of the full $B^{*}_{Q6}$ protein-abundance signal.

\paragraph{Scientific verdict.}
The ready claim is therefore narrow.  On the yeast finite-row panel, the $f_3\_stress$-aligned abundance component is better predicted by adding local ribosome-occupancy dwell summaries to the base design, and the held-out increment exceeds the fixed-design permutation boundary.  This is evidence for dwell mediation in the statistical sense of an added measured readout reducing residual abundance uncertainty for the $f_3$ component.  It is not a claim of causal translation control, codon-edit rescue, folded structural order, physical admissibility, molecular function, organismal phenotype, or universality.  Any such stronger statement would require its own layer-matched contact and a separate finite statistic at that layer.
