\subsection{Stress-Dwell Mediation for the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ Abundance Component}
\label{sec:bioreality-f3-stress-dwell-mediation}

\paragraph{Finite row surface.}
The row object is a matched finite surface over coding-sequence rows in \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}.  The active coordinate is the one-dimensional $f_{3}$-stress residual inside the nine-coordinate $B^{*}_{Q6}$ synonymous residual panel.  The nine coordinates used by the surrounding panel are $K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $Leu_{CUN\,vs\,UUR}$, $Leu_{UUA\,vs\,UUG}$, $Ser_{UCR\,vs\,AGY}$, $Ser_{UCA\,vs\,UCG}$, $Thr_{ACR\,vs\,ACY}$, and $f_{3}$-stress.  The protein target is not the full protein-abundance vector; it is the component
$$
\begin{aligned}
P_{f3} &= \operatorname{proj}_{\widetilde f_{3}}(\widetilde P),
\end{aligned}
$$
where $\widetilde P$ is $\log_{10}$ PAXdb protein abundance after residualization by the control matrix, and $\widetilde f_{3}$ is the corresponding residualized $f_{3}$-stress column.  The dwell readout is formed from file-provided local relative A-site occupancy summaries in the $5'$ and $3'$ windows.  The joined analysis uses $n=1257$ rows, with a training base size of $1800$, five folds, $\operatorname{rank}(f_{3})=1$, and a residual control basis of rank $24$.

\paragraph{Controls and rank condition.}
The control design removes the same elementary covariates used by the neighboring $B^{*}_{Q6}$ protein-abundance analyses: coding length, amino-acid composition, GC3, and the $M$-density baseline on the support of the projected residual coordinates, together with the intercept.  On the full yeast direct protein-abundance surface, the PAXdb join reports $5815$ CDS rows with join hit rate $0.9156038419146592$, and the $Z$-residualized direct $Q \to P$ fit has $R^{2}_{QP}=0.29837624994140827$ with all $9$ coordinates surviving as direct abundance predictors.  On the measured ribosome-efficiency surface, yeast has $5128$ genes with measured TE, ribosome-TE join hit rate $0.8818572656921754$, and $R^{2}_{QT,meas}=0.13681931343730436$ with all $9$ coordinates surviving against measured TE.  These neighboring row counts do not by themselves establish the dwell claim, but they show that the yeast row system is rank-powered rather than an all-fit low-rank artifact.

\paragraph{Dwell mediation statistic.}
The dwell test asks whether the local A-site occupancy summaries improve held-out prediction of $P_{f3}$ beyond the base design.  The recorded $R^{2}$ list is
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^{2}_{base} &= 0.1855578757644517,\\
R^{2}_{dwell\ only} &= 0.034336580953121976,\\
R^{2}_{full} &= 0.20412261520502883,\\
\Delta R^{2}_{held\ out} &= 0.01856473944057712.
\end{aligned}
$$
The $f_{3}$-stress dwell correlation is $0.1615441450901653$, with approximate $p=7.873916057196935\times 10^{-9}$.  The target component has squared norm $44.20095750389323$ and accounts for fraction $0.1999063916718794$ of the active abundance target.  The null model keeps the base and full designs and the fold assignment fixed while applying deterministic row-label permutations to $P_{f3}$.  Across $120$ permutations, the null range is
$$
\begin{aligned}
\min(null) &= -0.013799622026666476,\\
\operatorname{mean}(null) &= -0.0032911642620130563,\\
\operatorname{Null}_{95} &= 0.0013115362189692937,\\
\max(null) &= 0.005001350039772926.
\end{aligned}
$$
Since $0.01856473944057712>0.0013115362189692937$, the finite-row verdict for this module is passed: the $f_{3}$ abundance component has a dwell-mediated increment above the fixed-readout permutation boundary.

\paragraph{Positional sharpening.}
The companion positional run uses the same organism, the same $P_{f3}$ fraction $0.1999063916718794$, the same target norm $44.20095750389323$, the same $n=1257$ join, five folds, $\operatorname{rank}(f_{3})=1$, and $Z$-basis rank $24$.  Its window-level classification is $5'$-dominant: the $5'$ window passes, the $3'$ body window does not, and the leading segment by $\Delta R^{2}$ is $5'$.  In the structural-order ramp analysis, the primary ramp size is $50$ codons; the weighted $f_{3}$ ramp increment is $0.0011165640103326878$, the weighted body increment is $-0.00026157313923957926$, and the paired ramp-minus-body increment is $0.001378137149572267$.  The sign-flip values are $p=0.0478515625$ for a positive ramp increment, $p=0.54296875$ for a positive body increment, and $p=0.05859375$ for paired ramp greater than body.  This gives a local ramp-positive but not fully localized structural-order verdict.  Thus the dwell signal is positionally biased toward the $5'$ window, while the stricter ramp-localization gate remains short of the registered paired criterion.

\paragraph{Relation to measured translation and abundance mediation.}
The broader measured-TE mediation analysis over yeast reports $S_{QP}=0.3146818503437042$, $S_{QT,meas}=0.13681931343730436$, raw measured mediation fraction $0.4793069656345167$, and accepted bounded mediation fraction $0.4793069656345167$.  Its residual-energy diagnostics include $X$ explaining $103.10404057290246$ energy units on measured TE and $675.3275481062249$ energy units on protein abundance, while measured TE explains $593.5847863333478$ energy units on abundance and improves by $269.89558844115504$ after $X$.  In the joint TE-plus-mRNA-stability decomposition, yeast has $R^{2}_{QP}=0.2715520808117803$, $R^{2}_{QP\mid T}=0.1439989545940314$, $R^{2}_{QP\mid M}=0.25453612246767165$, and $R^{2}_{QP\mid T,M}=0.13047582645538838$.  The corresponding mediator list is measured TE and mRNA stability, with TE-only fraction $0.4697188319693239$, stability-only fraction $0.0626618595344251$, joint mediation fraction $0.5195182225621592$, and right-tail permutation value $0.000999000999000999$.  These figures place the dwell increment inside a measured translation-abundance row system rather than in a purely modeled codon-adaptation readout.

\paragraph{Reduction and remaining residual.}
The partial reduction dictionary over the yeast context uses the candidate readouts $\{\text{measured TE},\text{mRNA stability},\text{turnover}\}$ with description-length penalty $\lambda_{DL}=0.01$ and join threshold $\rho_{join}=0.05$.  It reports $n_{join}=1800$ from $4758$ available rows, five folds, $120$ permutations, raw best coverage $0.2745002077400602$, selection-aware null $95$ value $0.029149007242956115$, accepted coverage $C^{*}=0.24538211439730545$, and unexplained fraction $0.7546178856026946$.  The irreducibility diagnostic is therefore not a proof that the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual is primitive: in the registered current test, the source signal is real and at least one listed reduction family refutes the original irreducibility hypothesis.  The dwell-mediated increment should be read as one concrete reduction route for the $f_{3}$ abundance component, not as a complete account of the remaining $B^{*}_{Q6}$ abundance residual.

\paragraph{Local no-promotion boundary.}
The passed finite-row claim is narrow.  It says that, on the joined yeast rows, local relative A-site dwell summaries add held-out predictive power for the $f_{3}$-projected protein-abundance component, above a deterministic row-label permutation boundary, after the declared sequence and composition controls.  It does not establish causal mediation, elongation mechanism, folded structure, protein physical admissibility, molecular function, phenotype, or a universal biological rule.  A direct $q_i\to P_k$ abundance association remains only a protein-omics association unless a measured translation readout survives and conditioning on that readout removes or reduces the direct signal on matched rows.  Likewise, a positionally biased dwell increment is not a structural or functional claim unless a separate layer-matched contact supplies the corresponding structure, physical, localization, stability, perturbation, or function measurement.
