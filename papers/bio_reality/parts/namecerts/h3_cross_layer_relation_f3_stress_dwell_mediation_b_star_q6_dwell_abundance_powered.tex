\subsection{The $f_3$-Stress Dwell Mediation Certificate}
\label{sec:bioreality-f3-stress-dwell-mediation}

\paragraph{Finite panel.}
This record isolates the $f_3$-stress coordinate inside the nine-dimensional $B^*_{Q6}$ synonymous residual surface and asks whether the protein-abundance component aligned with that coordinate is better predicted when local ribosome-dwell summaries are added to the baseline model. The tested organism is \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}. The active join contains $n=1257$ genes from a base availability pool of $1800$ rows. The abundance target is not raw protein abundance: it is the projection $P_{f3}$ of $Z$-residualized $\log_{10}$ protein abundance onto the $Z$-residualized $f_3$-stress coordinate on the same active join. The control basis has rank $24$, and the tested $f_3$ source has rank $1$. The finite target norm is $\|P_{f3}\|^2=44.20095750389323$, and this component accounts for $0.1999063916718794$ of the residualized abundance target.

\paragraph{Dwell readout and fitted contrast.}
The dwell readout is a per-gene local relative ribosome-occupancy summary computed from file-provided $5'$ and $3'$ A-site occupancy windows. The five-fold held-out comparison fixes the baseline design, then asks whether adding the dwell columns improves prediction of $P_{f3}$. The finite score table is
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{\mathrm{base}} &= 0.1855578757644517,\\
R^2_{\mathrm{full}} &= 0.20412261520502883,\\
\Delta R^2_{\mathrm{held\ out}} &= 0.01856473944057712,\\
R^2_{\mathrm{dwell\ alone}} &= 0.034336580953121976.
\end{aligned}
$$
The empirical association between the $f_3$ coordinate and the dwell summary is positive, with correlation $0.1615441450901653$ and approximate $p=7.873916057196935\times 10^{-9}$. The row-label null fixes the base/full designs and the fold assignment, permutes $P_{f3}$ deterministically, and uses $120$ permutations. Its mean is $-0.0032911642620130563$, its minimum is $-0.013799622026666476$, its maximum is $0.005001350039772926$, and its $95$th percentile is $0.0013115362189692937$. The observed held-out increment exceeds that null boundary by more than an order of magnitude.

\paragraph{Readback.}
The finite conclusion is therefore a narrow mediation readback: for yeast, the $f_3$-aligned protein-abundance component has positive incremental predictability from local ribosome-dwell summaries after the declared residualization and fold discipline. In compact form, the certified inequality is
$$
\begin{aligned}
0.01856473944057712
  &= \Delta R^2_{\mathrm{held\ out}}(P_{f3};\mathrm{dwell}\mid\mathrm{base})\\
  &> 0.0013115362189692937\\

$$
The deterministic seed for the run is $\mathrm{sha256{:}b\_star\_q6\_f3\_stress\_dwell\_mediation\_powered{:}deterministic}$, and the run status is passed. This is not an all-coordinate or all-organism theorem. It is a finite-row certificate for one coordinate, one organism, one abundance projection, and one dwell-summary family.

\paragraph{Local cannot-claim boundary.}

It does not show a causal translation mechanism, does not identify elongation kinetics as the unique cause, does not establish protein folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, phenotype, adaptation, or a universal biological law, and does not license promotion from direct $q_i\to P_k$ association to mechanism.
