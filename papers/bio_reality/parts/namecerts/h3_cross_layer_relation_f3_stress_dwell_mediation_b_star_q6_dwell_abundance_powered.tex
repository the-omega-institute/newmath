\subsection{F3-Stress Dwell Mediation in Yeast Protein Abundance}
\label{sec:bioreality-f3-stress-dwell-mediation}

\paragraph{Finite-row claim.}
This record isolates the $f_3\_stress$ coordinate inside the nine-dimensional $B^*_{Q6}$ synonymous residual surface and asks whether its protein-abundance component is partially carried by a measured ribosome-dwell readout.  The finite carrier is the yeast same-row join whose rows contain coding-sequence codon counts, the residual coordinate basis, the sibling control design $Z$, PAXdb protein abundance, and per-gene local relative ribosome occupancy summaries computed from file-provided $5'$ and $3'$ A-site occupancy windows.  The active organism is \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}; the fitted claim uses $n=1257$ joined rows from a base availability of $1800$ rows, five held-out folds, $\operatorname{rank}(f_3)=1$, and $\operatorname{rank}(Z)=24$.

\paragraph{Controlled target.}
The response is not the raw protein abundance vector.  It is the one-coordinate abundance component
$P_{f_3}$, defined as the projection of $Z$-residualized $\log_{10}$ protein abundance onto $Z$-residualized $f_3\_stress$ on the active base+dwell join.  The target component has squared norm $44.20095750389323$ and accounts for $0.1999063916718794$ of the corresponding target energy.  The dwell readout alone explains held-out variance $R^2=0.034336580953121976$, while the base design without dwell reaches
$R^2_{\mathrm{base}}=0.1855578757644517$ and the base+dwell design reaches
$R^2_{\mathrm{full}}=0.20412261520502883$.  Thus the measured increment is
$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta R^2_{\mathrm{dwell}}
  &= R^2_{\mathrm{full}}-R^2_{\mathrm{base}} \\
  &= 0.20412261520502883-0.1855578757644517 \\
  &= 0.01856473944057712 .
\end{aligned}
$$
The rowwise correlation between the residual $f_3\_stress$ coordinate and the dwell summary is $0.1615441450901653$, with approximate $p=7.873916057196935\times 10^{-9}$.

\paragraph{Permutation boundary.}
The null model keeps the base and full designs and the five folds fixed, then deterministically permutes the $P_{f_3}$ row labels.  Across $120$ such permutations, the null increments range from $-0.013799622026666476$ to $0.005001350039772926$, with mean $-0.0032911642620130563$ and $95$th percentile $0.0013115362189692937$.  The observed held-out increment $0.01856473944057712$ is therefore above the fixed-readout null boundary by more than an order of magnitude:
$$
\begin{aligned}
0.01856473944057712 &> 0.0013115362189692937 .
\end{aligned}
$$
The finite-row verdict is consequently positive for dwell-mediated carry of the $f_3\_stress$ abundance component in this yeast panel.

\paragraph{Relation to the surrounding readouts.}
The result is narrower than a general protein-output law.  In the broader direct protein-omics survival run, yeast has $R^2_{QP}=0.29837624994140827$ with all nine residual coordinates surviving against measured PAXdb abundance after the same family of length, amino-acid composition, GC3, and M-density controls.  In the measured ribosome-TE run, yeast has $R^2_{QT,\mathrm{meas}}=0.13681931343730436$ with nine surviving coordinates.  The present dwell result tests a different question: whether a position-derived ribosome occupancy summary accounts for a selected $f_3\_stress$ abundance projection.  The linked positional dwell run keeps the same $n=1257$, $P_{f_3}$ norm $44.20095750389323$, and $P_{f_3}$ target fraction $0.1999063916718794$, and reports a $5'$-dominant segment, with the $5'$ window passing and the $3'$ window not passing.  A separate structural-order ramp analysis classifies the related ramp signal as $\mathrm{ramp\_positive\_but\_not\_localized}$: at the primary ramp length $50$, the weighted ramp increment is $0.0011165640103326878$, the weighted body increment is $-0.00026157313923957926$, and the paired ramp-minus-body sign-flip value is $0.05859375$.  These values are compatible with a localized dwell-like statistical surface, but they do not turn the dwell statistic into a structural-order conclusion.

\paragraph{Readback.}
The finite readback is a positive dwell increment for the yeast $f_3\_stress$ abundance component.  It is meaningful because the joined matrix is powered, the target is residualized before projection, the dwell increment is assessed out of sample, and the observed $\Delta R^2$ exceeds a row-label permutation boundary computed with the same folds and designs.  The readback should be stated as: in this yeast same-row panel, local ribosome dwell summaries carry additional predictive information for the $f_3\_stress$ component of protein abundance after the base controls.

\paragraph{Local no-promotion rule.}
This chapter does not claim that $f_3\_stress$ causes translation, fixes elongation speed, determines folding, establishes physical admissibility, realizes molecular function, or states a universal biological law.  It also does not claim that bulk abundance alone is a mechanism.  A stronger statement would require a separate layer-matched contact for the proposed layer: direct perturbation or rescue for causality, structure or stability measurements for folding or physical state, functional assays for molecular function, and broader controlled panels for universality.  The present result is a finite-row statistical mediation readback for one measured dwell surface in yeast.  It remains compatible with residual unexplained signal: the linked partial-mediation certificate on the same yeast context reports $n=1800$, $\lambda_{DL}=0.01$, $\rho_{\mathrm{join}}=0.05$, raw best coverage $0.2745002077400602$, selection-aware coverage $C^*=0.24538211439730545$, selection-aware null $95$th percentile $0.029149007242956115$, and unexplained fraction $0.7546178856026946$.
