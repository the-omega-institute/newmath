\subsection{F3-stress dwell mediation in the yeast abundance panel}\label{sec:bioreality-f3-stress-dwell-mediation}\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite panel.} The claim is evaluated on a Saccharomyces cerevisiae panel in which the active joined surface contains $1257$ gene-level rows.  The source design begins from a base availability count of $1800$ rows and keeps a single residual coordinate, $f3\_stress$, after the same sibling control basis used by the surrounding $B^{*}_{Q6}$ abundance tests.  The control basis has rank $24$, and the tested coordinate has rank $1$.  The dwell readout is not a generic codon-usage score: it is a per-gene local relative ribosome-occupancy summary computed from file-provided $5'$ and $3'$ A-site occupancy windows.  The protein target is also explicit: $P_{f3}$ is the projection of $Z$-residualized $\log_{10}$ protein abundance onto the $Z$-residualized $f3\_stress$ coordinate on the active base-plus-dwell join.

\paragraph{Residual coordinate and fitted quantities.} On this finite surface the $f3\_stress$ abundance component has squared norm $44.20095750389323$ and accounts for $0.1999063916718794$ of the target component.  The dwell readout alone has $R^{2}=0.034336580953121976$.  The base design has held-out $R^{2}=0.1855578757644517$, while the base-plus-dwell design has held-out $R^{2}=0.20412261520502883$.  Thus the dwell increment is
$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta R^{2}_{\mathrm{held\ out}}
  &= 0.20412261520502883 - 0.1855578757644517 \\
  &= 0.01856473944057712 .
\end{aligned}
$$
The observed correlation between the $f3\_stress$ coordinate and the dwell readout is $0.1615441450901653$, with approximate $p=7.873916057196935\times 10^{-9}$.  The cross-validated fit uses $5$ folds.

\paragraph{Permutation boundary.} The null comparison is a deterministic row-label permutation of $P_{f3}$ with the base design, full design, and folds held fixed.  Across $120$ permutations, the null distribution has mean $-0.0032911642620130563$, minimum $-0.013799622026666476$, maximum $0.005001350039772926$, and $95$th percentile $0.0013115362189692937$.  The observed held-out increment $0.01856473944057712$ is therefore well above the registered null boundary:
$$
\begin{aligned}
0.01856473944057712 &> 0.0013115362189692937 .
\end{aligned}
$$
This is the finite-row reason the record is classified as passed: the local ribosome-occupancy dwell columns add predictive information for the $f3\_stress$-aligned abundance component beyond the base design, and the increment is not reproduced by the fixed-design row-label null.

\paragraph{Relation to the broader yeast readout surface.} The result sits inside a larger $B^{*}_{Q6}$ readout context but should not be inflated beyond the registered rows.  In the direct yeast protein-omics survival surface, $5815$ Saccharomyces cerevisiae coding-sequence rows join to measured PAXdb abundance with join hit rate $0.9156038419146592$, all $9$ residual coordinates survive the positive-threshold direct $Q\to P$ screen, and the direct abundance statistic is $R^{2}_{QP}=0.29837624994140827$. In the measured mediation surface for yeast, $S_{QP}=0.3146818503437042$, $S_{QT,\mathrm{meas}}=0.13681931343730436$, and the measured mediated fraction is $0.4793069656345167$.  These surrounding numbers show that yeast is not a rank-degenerate toy setting; however, the present claim is narrower: it concerns the $f3\_stress$ abundance component and the added value of local dwell readouts on the $1257$-row joined panel.

\paragraph{Positional refinement.} A companion finite-row localization check uses the same organism, the same base availability count $1800$, the same active join size $1257$, the same $P_{f3}$ norm $44.20095750389323$, the same $P_{f3}$ target fraction $0.1999063916718794$, and the same $5$-fold design.  It separates the dwell summaries into $5'$ head and $3'$ tail windows.  The leading segment by held-out increment is the $5'$ window; the $5'$ ramp gate passes, while the $3'$ body gate does not.  The weighted primary ramp increment is $0.0011165640103326878$, the weighted body increment is $-0.00026157313923957926$, and the ramp-minus-body increment is $0.001378137149572267$.  This supports a positional reading of the dwell signal as $5'$-leading rather than a symmetric whole-body occupancy effect, although the paired ramp-body localization gate remains below the stronger localization threshold in the structural-order check.

\paragraph{Local interpretation.} The scientific content of the record is a finite statistical statement: in yeast, after the declared residualization, the $f3\_stress$-aligned protein-abundance component is partly predicted by local ribosome-occupancy dwell summaries, and the out-of-sample increment clears a row-permutation boundary by more than an order of magnitude.  The admissible interpretation is therefore a dwell-mediated abundance readback for this coordinate and this finite panel.  It is not, by itself, a causal translation mechanism, a proof of elongation dynamics, a folding statement, a physical-admissibility statement, a molecular-function result, or a universal biological rule.  Any such stronger layer would require a separate matched contact and its own finite statistic.  The present chapter records only the controlled $f3\_stress$ dwell-to-abundance relation on the specified Saccharomyces cerevisiae rows.
