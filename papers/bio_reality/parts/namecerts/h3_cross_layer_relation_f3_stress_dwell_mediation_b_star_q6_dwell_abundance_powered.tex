\subsection{F3-stress dwell mediation for protein abundance}\label{sec:bioreality-f3-stress-dwell-mediation}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Claim scope.}
This record concerns the $f_3$-stress coordinate inside the nine-dimensional $B^*_{Q6}$ synonymous residual surface.  The finite row object is a matched yeast table in which each retained coding sequence carries a residualized $f_3$ coordinate, the usual sequence-composition controls, a protein-abundance target, and a measured dwell readout derived from local ribosome-occupancy summaries.  The biological question is deliberately narrow: after amino-acid composition, CDS length, $GC3$, and $M$-density style controls have been removed, does the local dwell readout improve held-out prediction of the part of protein abundance aligned with the residualized $f_3$ coordinate?  The answer for this finite table is positive, but only at the level of a statistical readout.  It is not a causal translation claim, not a folding claim, not a physical-admissibility claim, not a molecular-function claim, and not a universal biological law.

\paragraph{Finite carrier and target.}
The active organism is \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}.  The experiment identifier is \texttt{b\_star\_q6\_f3\_stress\_dwell\_mediation\_powered}, with run identifier \texttt{946f33083caf}.  The joined table contains $1257$ rows on the active base-plus-dwell join, drawn from a base context with $1800$ rows.  The control basis has rank $24$, and the tested $f_3$ source has rank $1$.  The protein target is not raw abundance itself; it is
$$
\begin{aligned}
P_{f3} &= \operatorname{proj}_{\widetilde f_3}\bigl(\widetilde{\log_{10}(\operatorname{protein\ abundance})}\bigr),
\end{aligned}
$$
where tildes denote residualization against the sequence-control design on the active join.  The measured dwell readout is a per-gene local relative ribosome-occupancy summary computed from file-provided $5'$ and $3'$ A-site occupancy windows.  Thus the carrier is a finite residual matrix with one source coordinate, one dwell readout family, and one projected abundance target.

\paragraph{Controlled prediction numbers.}
The base design has held-out coefficient of determination
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{\operatorname{base}} &= 0.1855578757644517.
\end{aligned}
$$
Using the dwell readout alone gives
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{\operatorname{dwell}} &= 0.034336580953121976,
\end{aligned}
$$
while the combined base-plus-dwell design gives
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{\operatorname{full}} &= 0.20412261520502883.
\end{aligned}
$$
The registered increment is therefore
$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta R^2_{\operatorname{held\ out}} &= R^2_{\operatorname{full}}-R^2_{\operatorname{base}} \\
&= 0.01856473944057712.
\end{aligned}
$$
The finite target has squared norm
$$
\begin{aligned}
\lVert P_{f3}\rVert^2 &= 44.20095750389323,
\end{aligned}
$$
and the $f_3$-aligned component accounts for
$$
\begin{aligned}
0.1999063916718794
\end{aligned}
$$
of the target scale reported by the run.  The direct association between residualized $f_3$ and the dwell summary is positive, with
$$
\begin{aligned}
\operatorname{corr}(f_3,\operatorname{dwell}) &= 0.1615441450901653, \\
p_{\operatorname{approx}} &= 7.873916057196935\times 10^{-9}.
\end{aligned}
$$
These values support a finite-row mediation-style readback: the measured dwell summary carries predictive information for the $f_3$-aligned abundance component beyond the base design.

\paragraph{Permutation boundary.}
The null model keeps the base and full designs, as well as the five cross-validation folds, fixed, and deterministically permutes the row labels of $P_{f3}$.  The run used $120$ permutations.  The null distribution has
$$
\begin{aligned}
\operatorname{Null}_{\min} &= -0.013799622026666476, \\
\operatorname{Null}_{\operatorname{mean}} &= -0.0032911642620130563, \\
\operatorname{Null}_{95} &= 0.0013115362189692937, \\
\operatorname{Null}_{\max} &= 0.005001350039772926.
\end{aligned}
$$
The observed held-out increment $0.01856473944057712$ is larger than both the null $95$th percentile and the observed null maximum.  The finite status is therefore passed: the dwell readout adds more held-out explanatory power for $P_{f3}$ than expected under the fixed-design row-label null.

\paragraph{Relation to the wider Q6 readout table.}
The same nine-coordinate $B^*_{Q6}$ basis used in the surrounding protein-omics and translation-readout runs consists of $K\_AAA$, $Arg\_AGR$, $Ile\_AUA$, $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$, $Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG$, $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$, $Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG$, $Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY$, and $f3\_stress$.  In the direct protein-abundance survival table, yeast has $5815$ joined coding sequences, residual control rank $23$ under $24$ control columns, and direct $B^*_{Q6}$ to protein-abundance survival
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{QP}(\operatorname{yeast}) &= 0.29837624994140827,
\end{aligned}
$$
with all $9$ coordinates surviving the positive-entry threshold.  In the measured ribosome-TE table, yeast has $5128$ genes with measured TE, a join hit rate $0.8818572656921754$, and
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{QT,\operatorname{measured}}(\operatorname{yeast}) &= 0.13681931343730436.
\end{aligned}
$$
Those neighboring facts place the dwell result in a concrete cross-layer setting: the same coordinate system has protein-abundance and measured-translation readouts, while this chapter isolates a local ribosome-occupancy dwell increment for the $f_3$-aligned abundance component.

\paragraph{Positional refinement.}
A companion positional run, \texttt{6db78e1bf746}, keeps the same yeast organism, $1257$ active joined rows, $1800$ base rows, five folds, $120$ permutations, rank-$1$ $f_3$ source, and rank-$24$ control basis, but separates $5'$ and $3'$ occupancy windows.  Its dominant segment is $5'$: the five-prime ramp passed, the three-prime body did not, and the leading segment by held-out increment was $5'$.
$$
\begin{aligned}
\operatorname{dominant\ segment} &= 5', \\
\operatorname{five\ prime\ ramp\ passed} &= \operatorname{true}, \\
\operatorname{three\ prime\ body\ passed} &= \operatorname{false}.
\end{aligned}
$$
This does not convert the dwell result into a mechanism.  It only refines the finite readout by showing that the detectable increment is localized to the ramp-side occupancy summary rather than to the body-side summary in that positional design.

\paragraph{Comparison with partial reduction.}
A separate yeast partial-mediation certificate on the same broader context reports $4759$ base rows, $1800$ joined rows, five folds, $120$ permutations, $\lambda_{\operatorname{DL}}=0.01$, $\rho_{\operatorname{join}}=0.05$, and a best selection-aware coverage
$$
\begin{aligned}
C^* &= 0.24538211439730545,
\end{aligned}
$$
leaving unexplained fraction
$$
\begin{aligned}
1-C^* &= 0.7546178856026946.
\end{aligned}
$$
That neighboring result is consistent with the interpretation here: measured readouts can reduce part of the residual protein-abundance signal, but the finite table does not collapse the whole $B^*_{Q6}$ abundance residual into dwell, measured TE, mRNA stability, turnover, or any single named biological layer.

\paragraph{Local verdict.}
The ready claim is the following finite-row statement.  In the yeast joined table with $1257$ rows, rank-$24$ controls, a rank-$1$ residual $f_3$ source, and five fixed folds, local ribosome dwell summaries improve held-out prediction of the projected abundance component $P_{f3}$ by $0.01856473944057712$ in $R^2$.  This exceeds the deterministic permutation boundary $\operatorname{Null}_{95}=0.0013115362189692937$ and also exceeds the sampled null maximum $0.005001350039772926$.  The observed $f_3$--dwell correlation is $0.1615441450901653$ with approximate $p=7.873916057196935\times 10^{-9}$.  Therefore the finite readback supports a dwell-mediated statistical component for $f_3$-aligned protein abundance in this yeast table.

\paragraph{No-promotion boundary.}
The result remains a bounded association among residual codon geometry, a measured dwell summary, and a projected protein-abundance component.  It does not establish that $f_3$ changes translation rate in a causal sense, that ribosomes are the unique mediating path, that the effect transfers across organisms, that protein folding or structural order follows, that the proteins become physically admissible in any new sense, that molecular function or phenotype is realized, or that a general biological law has been found.  Any such stronger statement would require a separate layer-matched contact and a fresh controlled statistic on the same rows or on a clearly specified matched panel.
