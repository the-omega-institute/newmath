\subsection{F3-stress dwell mediation for measured yeast protein abundance}\label{subsec:bioreality-f3-stress-dwell-mediation}\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite surface.} The finite record is a yeast same-row panel on $1257$ joined coding-sequence entries, extracted from an available base context of $1800$ rows.  The coordinate under test is the one-dimensional $f3\_stress$ axis inside the nine-coordinate $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual family.  The protein target is not the full abundance vector itself, but the component
$$
\begin{aligned}
P_{f3} &= \operatorname{proj}_{\widetilde f3}\bigl(\widetilde{\log_{10} P}\bigr),
\end{aligned}
$$
where both $\widetilde f3$ and $\widetilde{\log_{10} P}$ are residualized against the same $Z$ basis.  The reported control basis has rank $24$, the active coordinate has rank $1$, and the resulting component has squared norm $44.20095750389323$.  Its fraction of the controlled abundance target is $0.1999063916718794$, so the row object is large enough to test whether a measured ribosome-occupancy dwell readout carries part of the $f3\_stress$ abundance component.

\paragraph{Dwell readout and regression numbers.} The mediator is a per-gene local relative ribosome-occupancy summary computed from file-provided $5'$ and $3'$ A-site occupancy windows.  On the active join, the base model gives
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{\mathrm{base}} &= 0.1855578757644517,\\
R^2_{\mathrm{dwell\ alone}} &= 0.034336580953121976,\\
R^2_{\mathrm{full}} &= 0.20412261520502883,\\
\Delta R^2_{\mathrm{held\ out}} &= 0.01856473944057712.
\end{aligned}
$$
The observed correlation between the residual $f3\_stress$ coordinate and the dwell readout is $0.1615441450901653$, with approximate $p=7.873916057196935\cdot 10^{-9}$.  The validation used $5$ folds.  The null distribution was generated by $120$ deterministic row-label permutations of $P_{f3}$ while keeping the base and full designs, and the folds, fixed.  The permutation null has mean $-0.0032911642620130563$, minimum $-0.013799622026666476$, maximum $0.005001350039772926$, and $95$th percentile $0.0013115362189692937$.  Thus the observed held-out gain satisfies
$$
\begin{aligned}
0.01856473944057712 &> 0.0013115362189692937,
\end{aligned}
$$
and the finite-row verdict is that the dwell readout carries a positive incremental component of the $f3\_stress$-projected protein-abundance residual on this yeast panel.

\paragraph{Position of the signal.} The companion positional run uses the same $P_{f3}$ norm $44.20095750389323$, the same target fraction $0.1999063916718794$, the same base context size $1800$, and the same active join size $1257$.  It separates dwell into $5'$ head and $3'$ tail windows.  The $5'$ segment passes while the $3'$ segment does not; the leading segment by held-out gain is $5'$ and the recorded dominant segment is $5prime$.  This localizes the measurable dwell increment to the early coding-window side of the available ribosome-occupancy summaries.  A further structural-order ramp run is compatible with this direction but weaker as a localization claim: at primary ramp length $50$, the weighted $f3$ ramp held-out gain is $0.0011165640103326878$, the body gain is $-0.00026157313923957926$, and the ramp-minus-body value is $0.001378137149572267$; the ramp signal is positive with sign-flip $p=0.0478515625$, while the paired ramp-body localization gate does not pass with sign-flip $p=0.05859375$.  Therefore the dwell result should be read as an observed positional ribosome-occupancy mediation signal, not as a folded-structure localization theorem.

\paragraph{Relation to the broader $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ abundance surface.} The dwell result sits inside a larger direct protein-omics surface.  Across the direct $S^{QP}$ panel, the nine coordinates are $K\_AAA$, $Arg\_AGR$, $Ile\_AUA$, $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$, $Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG$, $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$, $Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG$, $Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY$, and $f3\_stress$.  For Saccharomyces cerevisiae, the protein-abundance join uses $5815$ CDS rows with join hit rate $0.9156038419146592$, residual rank $9$ for the $Q$ design, residual degrees of freedom $5792$ after $Z$, and direct $R^2_{QP}=0.29837624994140827$ with all $9$ coordinates surviving the positive threshold.  The same organism has measured ribosome-TE survival $R^2_{QT,\mathrm{meas}}=0.13681931343730436$ on $5128$ genes, and the measured abundance mediation run reports $S_{QP}=0.3146818503437042$, $S_{QT,\mathrm{meas}}=0.13681931343730436$, and measured mediation fraction $0.4793069656345167$.  These numbers justify asking a more local question: whether a position-resolved dwell readout can account for a defined part of the $f3\_stress$ abundance component.

\paragraph{Comparison with other mediators.} The joint TE and mRNA-stability decomposition on yeast joins $4759$ rows and reports $R^2_{QP}=0.2715520808117803$, $R^2_{QP\mid T}=0.1439989545940314$, $R^2_{QP\mid M}=0.25453612246767165$, and $R^2_{QP\mid T,M}=0.13047582645538838$.  Its joint mediation fraction is $0.5195182225621592$, with TE-only fraction $0.4697188319693239$, stability-only fraction $0.0626618595344251$, and right-tail permutation $p=0.000999000999000999$.  A readout-dictionary certificate on yeast uses $n=1800$, $5$ folds, $\lambda_{DL}=0.01$, $\rho_{join}=0.05$, and $120$ permutations; it finds selection-aware coverage $C^{\ast}=0.24538211439730545$ against a selection-aware null $95$th percentile $0.029149007242956115$, leaving unexplained fraction $0.7546178856026946$.  The irreducibility check uses the same $n=1800$, $5$ folds, $\lambda_{DL}=0.01$, and $\rho_{join}=0.05$, and its status records that at least one listed reduction family refutes the strongest irreducibility reading.  The dwell run is therefore not an isolated abundance correlation: it is a finite local mediation certificate for one coordinate component within a partially reducible, not fully explained, abundance residual surface.

\paragraph{Four-case interpretation.} The modeled-tAI four-case discriminant gives Saccharomyces cerevisiae $R^2_{QT}=0.8213457220900503$ and $R^2_{QP}=0.29837624994140827$ on $5815$ joined rows, with all $9$ coordinates classified as case $D$ and no positive-positive-without-drop coordinates.  The measured-ribosome mediation surface gives a more conservative same-row readout, but still keeps yeast positive for both $S^{QT}$ and $S^{QP}$ and records a measured mediation fraction of $0.4793069656345167$.  The dwell chapter refines this by projecting specifically onto $P_{f3}$ and showing that local ribosome occupancy adds held-out predictive power above the base design.  In the local four-case language, this is evidence for a measured readout-mediated component of the $f3\_stress\to P$ relation on the yeast dwell panel, not a declaration that every $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ coordinate acts through dwell.

\paragraph{Local claim.} The admissible mathematical claim is the following finite statement.  On the recorded Saccharomyces cerevisiae join of $1257$ rows, after the shared $Z$ residualization and projection to $P_{f3}$, adding the measured dwell readout raises held-out $R^2$ by $0.01856473944057712$, which is larger than the fixed permutation $95$th percentile $0.0013115362189692937$.  Consequently the available data support a positive dwell-mediated component for the $f3\_stress$ abundance projection.  The support is finite, organism-specific, readout-specific, and conditioned on the stated residualization and permutation protocol.

\paragraph{Local boundary.} Nothing in this chapter turns codon-coordinate geometry, modeled adaptation weights, ribosome occupancy, or steady-state protein abundance into a general biological law.  The dwell readout is a measured translation-layer contact for this finite yeast panel, but it does not by itself establish causal translation, elongation mechanism, folding order, physical admissibility, molecular function, phenotype, universality, or evolutionary direction.  Any such stronger statement would require a separate layer-matched contact and its own finite statistic.  Here the result is only that a concrete local ribosome-occupancy dwell summary absorbs a positive, permutation-separated part of the $f3\_stress$-projected protein-abundance residual.
