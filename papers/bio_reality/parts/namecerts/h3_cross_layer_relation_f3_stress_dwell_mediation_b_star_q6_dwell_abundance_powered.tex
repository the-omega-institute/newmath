\subsection{Stress-Dwell Mediation of the $f_3$ Protein-Abundance Component}
\label{sec:bioreality-f3-stress-dwell-mediation}

\paragraph{Finite row surface.}
The finite object in this record is a joined yeast matrix whose active rows are coding-sequence units with three simultaneously available quantities: the $f_3$ coordinate from the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ synonymous residual basis, a protein-abundance readout, and a local dwell readout derived from file-provided A-site occupancy windows. The organism is \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}; the organism key is \texttt{saccharomyces\_cerevisiae}. The active mediation join contains $n=1257$ rows, while the base cross-validation frame records \texttt{base\_n}$=1800$. The coordinate rank tested here is $\operatorname{rank}(f_3)=1$, and the residual-control basis has rank $24$. The target is not total protein abundance. It is the projection $P_{f_3}$ of $Z$-residualized $\log_{10}$ protein abundance onto the $Z$-residualized $f_3$ axis on the active base-plus-dwell join. Numerically,
$$
\begin{aligned}
\|P_{f_3}\|_2^2 &= 44.20095750389323,\\
P_{f_3}\text{ fraction of target} &= 0.1999063916718794.
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the tested target is a one-coordinate abundance component, not the whole protein-output layer.

\paragraph{Dwell readout and fitted relation.}
The dwell readout is a per-gene local relative ribosome-occupancy summary computed from the available $5'$ and $3'$ A-site occupancy windows. The direct empirical association between the $f_3$ coordinate and the dwell readout is positive but modest:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\operatorname{corr}(f_3,\mathrm{dwell}) &= 0.1615441450901653,\\
p_{\mathrm{approx}} &= 7.873916057196935\times 10^{-9}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The dwell-only fit gives
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{\mathrm{dwell}} &= 0.034336580953121976.
\end{aligned}
$$
The base design and the base-plus-dwell design were evaluated on fixed five-fold splits. Their held-out coefficients of determination are
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{\mathrm{base}} &= 0.1855578757644517,\\
R^2_{\mathrm{full}} &= 0.20412261520502883,\\
\Delta R^2_{\mathrm{held\ out}} &= 0.01856473944057712.
\end{aligned}
$$
This is the finite-row mediation signal: after the base abundance design is fixed, the dwell surface adds held-out predictive mass for the $f_3$-projected abundance component.

\paragraph{Permutation calibration.}
The null model is a deterministic row-label permutation of $P_{f_3}$ with the base design, full design, and fold assignment held fixed. The permutation count is $120$, using seed \texttt{sha256:b\_star\_q6\_f3\_stress\_dwell\_mediation\_powered:deterministic}. The null range and cutoff are
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{Null}_{\min} &= -0.013799622026666476,\\
\mathrm{Null}_{\mathrm{mean}} &= -0.0032911642620130563,\\
\mathrm{Null}_{95} &= 0.0013115362189692937,\\
\mathrm{Null}_{\max} &= 0.005001350039772926.
\end{aligned}
$$
The observed held-out increment $0.01856473944057712$ is positive and exceeds the fixed $95\%$ permutation boundary by more than an order of magnitude relative to the cutoff. The registered run status is \texttt{passed}, with experiment id \texttt{b\_star\_q6\_f3\_stress\_dwell\_mediation\_powered} and run id \texttt{946f33083caf}.

A companion positional run on the same $f_3$ abundance component reports the same $P_{f_3}$ target norm, $\|P_{f_3}\|_2^2=44.20095750389323$, and the same target fraction $0.1999063916718794$. Its active join again has $n=1257$, base frame $1800$, five folds, rank-$1$ $f_3$, and a rank-$24$ residual basis. The positional result classifies the leading dwell segment as \texttt{5prime}: the $5'$ ramp window passes, the $3'$ body window does not, and the dominant segment is \texttt{5prime}. In the structural ramp comparison, the primary ramp size is $50$ codons, the weighted $f_3$ ramp held-out increment is $0.0011165640103326878$, the weighted body increment is $-0.00026157313923957926$, and the paired ramp-minus-body increment is $0.001378137149572267$. The classification there is \texttt{ramp\_positive\_but\_not\_localized}: the ramp signal is positive, but the stricter paired localization gate does not pass. The dwell-mediation result therefore supports a local ribosome-occupancy increment for the $f_3$ abundance component, with a $5'$-leaning positional signature, but it does not license a stronger localization claim.

\paragraph{Reduction and irreducibility context.}
The broader yeast reduction dictionary gives a useful scale for the present signal. A partial-mediation certificate on the unified yeast context uses $n_{\mathrm{join}}=1800$ out of $4758$ available joined rows, five folds, $\lambda_{\mathrm{DL}}=0.01$, $\rho_{\mathrm{join}}=0.05$, and $120$ permutations. Its best selection-aware coverage is $C^{*}=0.24538211439730545$, with selection-aware null $95\%$ boundary $0.029149007242956115$ and unexplained fraction $0.7546178856026946$. The irreducibility test on the same yeast context also uses $n_{\mathrm{join}}=1800$, five folds, $\lambda_{\mathrm{DL}}=0.01$, $\rho_{\mathrm{join}}=0.05$, $120$ permutations, and a rank-$24$ control basis. These rows say that measured readouts can account for part of the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ abundance residual, but not all of it. The present dwell increment is one concrete component of that partial reduction: it is a statistically calibrated local ribosome-occupancy contribution to the $f_3$ abundance projection, not a complete explanation of the residual family.

\paragraph{Admissible reading.}
The admissible conclusion is narrow. On the registered yeast finite-row panel, the $f_3$-projected protein-abundance component has a positive dwell-mediated held-out increment:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta R^2_{\mathrm{held\ out}} &= 0.01856473944057712 
  > \mathrm{Null}_{95} = 0.0013115362189692937.
\end{aligned}
$$
Together with the positive $f_3$--dwell correlation and the five-fold held-out design, this supports a finite statistical link between the $f_3$ residual coordinate, local ribosome-occupancy dwell, and the $f_3$ component of measured protein abundance in yeast.

\paragraph{Local boundary.}
No broader biological claim follows from this row set alone. The result does not establish translation causation, elongation mechanism, folded structural order, physical admissibility, molecular function, phenotype, adaptation, or universality. It is not a perturbation, rescue, synonymous-edit, or time-ordered causal experiment. The dwell readout is a measured ribosome-occupancy summary on joined yeast rows, and the protein readout is a steady-state abundance measurement. Claims about folding, physical feasibility, protein function, localization mechanism, turnover mechanism, or general biological law require their own layer-matched contacts and their own controlled statistics. Direct $q_i\to P_k$ association is therefore kept as a prediction surface unless a measured $q_i\to T_j\to P_k$ chain is explicitly fitted and shown to reduce the direct abundance signal under the declared controls.
