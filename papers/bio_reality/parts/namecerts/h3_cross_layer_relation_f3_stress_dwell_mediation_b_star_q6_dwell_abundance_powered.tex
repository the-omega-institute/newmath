\subsection{The $f_3$-Stress Dwell Mediation Certificate}\label{sec:bioreality-f3-stress-dwell-mediation}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite row surface.}
The certificate concerns the $f_3$-stress coordinate inside the nine-coordinate $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual family.  The active surface is a matched Saccharomyces cerevisiae table in which coding-sequence codon counts, the usual residual controls, local ribosome-occupancy dwell summaries, and measured protein abundance are all available on the same gene rows.  The abundance readout is not used raw: the target is the component $P_{f_3}$ obtained by projecting $Z$-residualized $\log_{10}$ protein abundance onto the $Z$-residualized $f_3$-stress direction on the active base-plus-dwell join.  The control basis has rank $24$, the $f_3$ source rank is $1$, and the joined finite table contains $n=1257$ rows drawn from a base availability of $1800$ rows.

\paragraph{Controls and readouts.}
The dwell readout is explicitly local: per-gene relative ribosome occupancy is summarized from file-provided $5'$ and $3'$ A-site occupancy windows.  Thus the readout is closer to a ribosome-position dwell summary than to a global translation-efficiency scalar.  The matching background is the same organism in which the measured ribosome-TE packet reports $5128$ genes with measured TE against $5815$ CDS rows, a join hit rate of $0.8818572656921754$, and $R^2_{QT,\mathrm{meas}}=0.13681931343730436$ after the codon residuals and controls are applied.  The measured protein-abundance background for the same organism joins $5815$ proteins at hit rate $0.9156038419146592$ and gives a direct $B^{*}_{Q6}$ to abundance survival value $R^2_{QP}=0.29837624994140827$ with all nine coordinates surviving the positive partial-$R^2$ threshold.  Those surrounding rows justify studying whether the $f_3$ component of the abundance signal is partially carried by a local ribosome-dwell readout rather than treating the direct protein association as self-explanatory.

\paragraph{Regression certificate.}
On the active yeast join, the base design alone gives
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{\mathrm{base}} &= 0.1855578757644517,\\
R^2_{\mathrm{dwell\ alone}} &= 0.034336580953121976,\\
R^2_{\mathrm{full}} &= 0.20412261520502883.
\end{aligned}
$$
The held-out gain from adding dwell to the base design is
$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta R^2_{\mathrm{held\ out}} &= 0.01856473944057712.
\end{aligned}
$$
This is the finite-row mediation signal recorded for the packet: the dwell variables add predictive power for the $f_3$-aligned abundance component after the base controls are already present.  The norm of the target component is $\lVert P_{f_3}\rVert^2=44.20095750389323$, and $P_{f_3}$ accounts for $0.1999063916718794$ of the target energy on the active join.  The direct empirical association between the $f_3$ coordinate and the dwell summary is positive, with correlation $0.1615441450901653$ and approximate $p=7.873916057196935\times 10^{-9}$.

\paragraph{Permutation boundary.}
The null model holds the base and full designs and the five folds fixed, then applies a deterministic row-label permutation of $P_{f_3}$.  Across $120$ permutations, the null distribution has mean $-0.0032911642620130563$, minimum $-0.013799622026666476$, maximum $0.005001350039772926$, and $95$th percentile $0.0013115362189692937$.  Therefore the observed held-out gain satisfies
$$
\begin{aligned}
0.01856473944057712 &> 0.0013115362189692937,
\end{aligned}
$$
so the packet is classified as passed for this finite-row dwell-mediation test.  The fold count is $5$, and the deterministic seed is \texttt{sha256:b\_star\_q6\_f3\_stress\_dwell\_mediation\_powered:deterministic}.

\paragraph{Positional refinement.}
A companion positional readback on the same organism and target separates the dwell summaries into $5'$ head and $3'$ tail windows.  It retains the same base availability $1800$, active join $1257$, source rank $1$, target norm $44.20095750389323$, and target fraction $0.1999063916718794$.  The recorded dominant segment is $5'$: the $5'$ ramp gate passes, the $3'$ body gate does not pass, and the leading segment by held-out gain is the $5'$ window.  This positional result does not by itself explain why the signal exists, but it sharpens the finite readback: the dwell increment is localized to the head/ramp side of the occupancy summaries rather than being a symmetric whole-CDS effect.

\paragraph{Interpretation.}
The admissible conclusion is narrow.  In the finite yeast table, the $f_3$-stress component of residualized protein abundance is partially reducible to measured local ribosome dwell summaries, with a held-out gain far above the fixed permutation boundary.  This supports a translation-near statistical mediation readout for this coordinate and organism.  It does not turn the codon residual into a causal translation mechanism, does not prove elongation kinetics, and does not establish folding, physical admissibility, protein function, organism phenotype, evolutionary direction, or a universal biological rule.  Those stronger claims would require separate layer-matched contacts and their own finite tests.  The broader yeast dictionary packet is consistent with this restraint: a selection-aware readout dictionary covers $C^{*}=0.24538211439730545$ of the residual signal with unexplained fraction $0.7546178856026946$ at $\lambda_{\mathrm{DL}}=0.01$, so the dwell-mediated piece is a real but partial reduction, not a complete explanation of the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ abundance residual.
