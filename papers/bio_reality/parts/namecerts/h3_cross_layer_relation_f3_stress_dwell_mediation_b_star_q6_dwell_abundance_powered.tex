\subsection{F3-stress dwell mediation for the yeast abundance component}
\label{sec:bioreality-f3-stress-dwell-mediation}

\paragraph{Question.}
The finite-row question is whether the $f_3\_stress$ coordinate in the $B^*_{Q6}$ residual family has a protein-abundance component whose prediction improves when a measured ribosome-dwell readout is added. The test is deliberately narrower than a general translation claim. It projects the residualized protein-abundance target onto the residualized $f_3\_stress$ direction, then asks whether local relative ribosome occupancy summaries add held-out predictive power for that projected abundance component. The organism is \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}; the readout is a per-gene dwell summary computed from file-provided $5'$ and $3'$ A-site occupancy windows; and the response is
$$
\begin{aligned}
P_{f3} &= \operatorname{proj}_{\widetilde f_3}(\widetilde P),
\end{aligned}
$$
where $\widetilde P$ is log-scaled protein abundance after the stated covariate residualization and $\widetilde f_3$ is the corresponding residualized $f_3\_stress$ coordinate on the active base-plus-dwell join.

\paragraph{Finite row surface.}
The verified run is `946f33083caf`, with experiment id `b\_star\_q6\_f3\_stress\_dwell\_mediation\_powered` and status `passed`. The active join contains $n=1257$ yeast genes out of a base availability of $1800$ rows. The fitted $f_3$ source has rank $1$, the control basis has rank $24$, and the cross-validation uses $5$ folds. The projected target has squared norm $44.20095750389323$, and this projected component accounts for $0.1999063916718794$ of the target energy used by the test. The finite-row quantities are
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{\mathrm{base}} &= 0.1855578757644517,\\
R^2_{\mathrm{dwell\ alone}} &= 0.034336580953121976,\\
R^2_{\mathrm{full}} &= 0.20412261520502883,\\
\Delta R^2_{\mathrm{held\ out}} &= 0.01856473944057712.
\end{aligned}
$$
The observed correlation between the residualized $f_3\_stress$ coordinate and the dwell readout is $0.1615441450901653$, with approximate $p=7.873916057196935\times 10^{-9}$. The null model is a deterministic row-label permutation of $P_{f3}$ with the base design, full design, and folds held fixed. Across $120$ permutations, the null summary is
$$
\begin{aligned}
\operatorname{Null}_{\min} &= -0.013799622026666476,\\
\operatorname{Null}_{\mathrm{mean}} &= -0.0032911642620130563,\\
\operatorname{Null}_{95} &= 0.0013115362189692937,\\
\operatorname{Null}_{\max} &= 0.005001350039772926.
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the observed held-out increment exceeds the permutation $95$th percentile by more than an order of magnitude:
$$
\begin{aligned}
0.01856473944057712 &> 0.0013115362189692937.
\end{aligned}
$$
This is the finite-row content of the positive call: the dwell summary carries additional held-out information about the $f_3\_stress$ abundance component beyond the base design.

\paragraph{What the dwell increment adds.}
The dwell result is more specific than a generic protein-abundance association. The target is not total protein abundance; it is the $P_{f3}$ component obtained by projecting residualized protein abundance onto residualized $f_3\_stress$. The predictor added to the base design is not modeled tAI and not total ribosome efficiency; it is a local relative occupancy summary from $5'$ and $3'$ A-site windows. The positive increment therefore says that, on the active yeast join, local ribosome-dwell information accounts for a reproducible part of the $f_3\_stress$-aligned abundance component:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta R^2_{\mathrm{held\ out}} - \operatorname{Null}_{95}
&= 0.01856473944057712 - 0.0013115362189692937\\
&= 0.017253203221607827.
\end{aligned}
$$
The deterministic seed is `sha256:b\_star\_q6\_f3\_stress\_dwell\_mediation\_powered:deterministic`, so the finite comparison is keyed to a fixed fold and permutation construction rather than to an informal visual trend.

\paragraph{Local verdict.}
The chapter-level call is ready for the narrow dwell-abundance statement. In yeast, the $f_3\_stress$ abundance projection has positive held-out improvement when the dwell readout is added to the base design, and the improvement exceeds the fixed permutation boundary. The positive statement is therefore
$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta R^2_{\mathrm{held\ out}}(P_{f3};\ \mathrm{base+dwell}) &= 0.01856473944057712,\\
\operatorname{Null}_{95} &= 0.0013115362189692937,\\

\end{aligned}
$$
This supports a finite-row mediation-style readback from $f_3\_stress$ to an abundance component through measured local ribosome occupancy summaries on the registered yeast rows.

\paragraph{Interpretive boundary.}
No broader biological promotion is licensed by this result alone. The dwell statistic is observational, cross-sectional, and finite-row; it is not a perturbation, rescue, causal mediation, or synonymous-edit experiment. It does not by itself establish translation as a universal mechanism, protein folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, phenotype, evolutionary direction, or a global law. A claim at any of those layers would need its own matched contact and its own finite statistic. The present result should be read only as a controlled yeast statement about the $f_3\_stress$-aligned protein-abundance component and the added predictive value of local ribosome-dwell summaries.
