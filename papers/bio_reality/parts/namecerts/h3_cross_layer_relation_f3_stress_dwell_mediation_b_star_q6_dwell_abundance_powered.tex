\subsection{The f3-Stress Dwell Mediation Record}
\label{sec:bioreality-f3-stress-dwell-mediation}

\paragraph{Finite panel.}
This record concerns the $f3\_stress$ coordinate inside the nine-coordinate $B^*_{Q6}$ residual surface.  The coordinate family used in the surrounding measurements is
$K\_AAA$, $Arg\_AGR$, $Ile\_AUA$, $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$, $Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG$, $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$, $Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG$, $Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY$, and $f3\_stress$.  The specific dwell experiment is the Saccharomyces cerevisiae panel with $n=1257$ joined rows on the active base-plus-dwell join, drawn from a larger base context of $1800$ rows.  The target is the projection $P_{f3}$ of $Z$-residualized $\log_{10}$ protein abundance onto the $Z$-residualized $f3\_stress$ direction.  The control basis has rank $24$, the tested coordinate rank is $1$, and the dwell readout is a per-gene local relative ribosome-occupancy summary computed from file-provided $5'$ and $3'$ A-site occupancy windows.

\paragraph{Controlled prediction surface.}
The base design explains held-out abundance variation with
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{\mathrm{base}} &= 0.1855578757644517,\\
R^2_{\mathrm{full}} &= 0.20412261520502883,\\
\Delta R^2_{\mathrm{held\ out}} &= 0.01856473944057712.
\end{aligned}
$$
The dwell-only readout has $R^2=0.034336580953121976$, and the observed correlation between $f3\_stress$ and the dwell readout is $0.1615441450901653$ with approximate $p=7.873916057196935\times 10^{-9}$.  The signal is judged against $120$ deterministic row-label permutations of $P_{f3}$ with the base and full designs and the five folds fixed.  The null distribution has mean $-0.0032911642620130563$, minimum $-0.013799622026666476$, maximum $0.005001350039772926$, and $95$th percentile $0.0013115362189692937$.  Thus the observed held-out increment is about $14.15$ times the permutation $95$th percentile:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\frac{0.01856473944057712}{0.0013115362189692937} &\approx 14.15.
\end{aligned}
$$
On this finite panel the dwell readout therefore carries positive incremental information about the $f3\_stress$ abundance component beyond the base design.

\paragraph{Abundance coordinate being explained.}
The same protein-abundance program reports that direct $B^*_{Q6}$ residuals survive against measured PAXdb abundance across a rank-powered organism panel.  For Saccharomyces cerevisiae, the PAXdb join contains $5815$ CDS-protein rows with join hit rate $0.9156038419146592$, rank $\operatorname{rank}(X_{\tilde{}})=9$, control rank $23$, residual degrees of freedom $5792$, and direct abundance survival $R^2_{QP}=0.29837624994140827$ with all $9$ coordinates surviving.  The broader direct-abundance range includes $R^2_{QP}=0.019256877907572044$ in Halobacterium salinarum and $R^2_{QP}=0.29837624994140827$ in Saccharomyces cerevisiae, so the yeast dwell result is not an isolated statement about a zero direct-abundance coordinate; it refines one measurable component of a real direct protein-abundance residual.

\paragraph{Relation to measured translation and mediation readouts.}
The measured ribosome-TE panel gives Saccharomyces cerevisiae $5128$ genes with TE, a ribosome-TE join hit rate $0.8818572656921754$, residual degrees of freedom $5105$, and $R^2_{QT,\mathrm{meas}}=0.13681931343730436$ with $9$ surviving coordinates.  In the measured protein-abundance mediation calculation, the same organism has $5128$ three-way joined CDS, TE, and abundance rows, $S_{QP}=0.3146818503437042$, $S_{QT,\mathrm{meas}}=0.13681931343730436$, raw measured mediation fraction $M^{QTP}_{\mathrm{raw}}=0.4793069656345167$, and clipped measured mediation fraction $0.4793069656345167$.  The joint TE and mRNA-stability decomposition on the yeast four-way join reports $n=4759$, $R^2_{QP}=0.2715520808117803$, $R^2_{QP\mid T}=0.1439989545940314$, $R^2_{QP\mid M}=0.25453612246767165$, $R^2_{QP\mid T,M}=0.13047582645538838$, TE-only fraction $0.4697188319693239$, stability-only fraction $0.0626618595344251$, and joint fraction $0.5195182225621592$ with right-tail permutation probability $0.000999000999000999$.  These numbers place the dwell increment inside a larger measured-readout landscape: the $f3\_stress$ abundance component is not merely a direct codon-abundance association, because measured translation-related covariates absorb a substantial fraction of the same yeast abundance residual surface.

\paragraph{Position of the dwell effect.}
A companion positional dwell test also uses Saccharomyces cerevisiae, $n=1257$ joined rows, base size $1800$, five folds, coordinate rank $1$, and $120$ fixed-design permutations.  It identifies the leading dwell segment as $5'$ and reports that the five-prime ramp passes while the three-prime body does not.  In the structural-order positional screen the primary ramp length $50$ gives weighted $f3$ ramp increment $0.0011165640103326878$, weighted body increment $-0.00026157313923957926$, and weighted ramp-minus-body increment $0.001378137149572267$; the classification is $ramp\_positive\_but\_not\_localized$.  That classification is important: the evidence supports a translation-readout-positioned abundance increment, not a structural-order claim.

\paragraph{Dictionary and reduction context.}
The partial mediation certificate on the yeast context uses $n=1800$ from $4758$ available joined rows, five folds, $\lambda_{\mathrm{DL}}=0.01$, join penalty $\rho_{\mathrm{join}}=0.05$, and $120$ permutations.  Its selected measured-readout dictionary covers $C^*=0.24538211439730545$ of the residual signal after selection-aware correction, with raw best coverage $0.2745002077400602$, selection-aware null $95$th percentile $0.029149007242956115$, naive fixed-subset null $95$th percentile $0.00598374993486675$, and unexplained fraction $0.7546178856026946$.  The dwell mediation record is consistent with this partial-reduction picture.  It gives a strong finite increment for one local ribosome-occupancy readout, while still leaving most of the abundance residual outside the small measured-readout dictionary.

\paragraph{Decision.}
The finite-row decision is positive for the stated local claim.  On the yeast joined panel, the base-plus-dwell model improves held-out prediction of the $f3\_stress$ abundance projection by $\Delta R^2=0.01856473944057712$, far above the fixed-design permutation threshold $0.0013115362189692937$, with $f3$-dwell correlation $0.1615441450901653$ and approximate $p=7.873916057196935\times 10^{-9}$.  The admissible statement is therefore narrow: local ribosome-occupancy dwell summaries carry incremental predictive information for the $f3\_stress$ component of residualized protein abundance in Saccharomyces cerevisiae under the declared finite controls and joins.

\paragraph{Local no-promotion rule.}
This chapter does not infer a translation mechanism, protein folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, organismal phenotype, causality, evolutionary direction, or universality from the dwell increment.  The readout is a finite, condition-bound ribosome-occupancy summary, the abundance target is a residualized PAXdb-derived protein-abundance component, and the fit is observational.  Any stronger conclusion would require an additional layer-matched contact and a separate statistic at that layer.  In particular, the positive dwell result does not by itself establish elongation mechanism, folded structural order, localization, turnover, function, perturbation rescue, or a global biological law.
