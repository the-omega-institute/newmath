\subsection{F3-stress dwell mediation for the abundance component}
\label{sec:bioreality-f3-stress-dwell-mediation}

\paragraph{Finite row surface.}
This record tests whether the $f_3$-stress coordinate in the $B^*_{Q6}$ residual family has a measurable path through local ribosome-occupancy dwell summaries into the protein-abundance component aligned with the same coordinate.  The active surface is a finite yeast join: $n_{\mathrm{join}}=1257$ joined coding-sequence rows from \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}, evaluated inside a larger available base context with $n=1800$ rows.  The target vector is not raw abundance.  It is the projection $P_{f3}$ of $Z$-residualized $\log_{10}$ protein abundance onto the $Z$-residualized $f_3$-stress coordinate on the active base-plus-dwell join.  The control basis has rank $24$, and the tested source coordinate has rank $1$.

\paragraph{Readout and fitted quantities.}
The dwell readout is a per-gene summary of local relative ribosome occupancy computed from file-provided $5'$ and $3'$ A-site occupancy windows.  On this finite surface the $f_3$-aligned protein-abundance component has squared norm
$$
\begin{aligned}
\|P_{f3}\|^2 &= 44.20095750389323,\\
\frac{\|P_{f3}\|^2}{\|P\|^2} &= 0.1999063916718794.
\end{aligned}
$$
The dwell-only regression accounts for
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{\mathrm{dwell}} &= 0.034336580953121976,
\end{aligned}
$$
while the base design accounts for
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{\mathrm{base}} &= 0.1855578757644517.
\end{aligned}
$$
Adding the dwell readout raises the fitted value to
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{\mathrm{base+dwell}} &= 0.20412261520502883,
\end{aligned}
$$
and the five-fold held-out increment is
$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta R^2_{\mathrm{held\ out}} &= 0.01856473944057712.
\end{aligned}
$$
The row-level correlation between $f_3$ stress and the dwell readout is positive, with
$$
\begin{aligned}
rho(f_3,\mathrm{dwell}) &= 0.1615441450901653,\\
p_{\mathrm{approx}} &= 7.873916057196935\times 10^{-9}.
\end{aligned}
$$
These numbers give a finite, row-indexed mediation signal: the local ribosome-dwell summary improves prediction of the $f_3$-aligned abundance component beyond the base design.

\paragraph{Permutation boundary.}
The null model keeps the base and full designs, the five folds, and the dwell rows fixed, and deterministically permutes the labels of $P_{f3}$.  Across $120$ permutations the null distribution has
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mu_{0} &= -0.0032911642620130563,\\
q_{0.95} &= 0.0013115362189692937,\\
\min_{0} &= -0.013799622026666476,\\
\max_{0} &= 0.005001350039772926.
\end{aligned}
$$
The observed held-out increment $0.01856473944057712$ exceeds the fixed-readout null $95\%$ threshold by more than an order of magnitude.  The registered finite-row verdict is therefore passed for the narrow claim that the $f_3$-stress abundance component carries dwell-mediated incremental signal in the yeast panel.

\paragraph{Relation to the positional check.}
The companion positional computation keeps the same organism, the same $n=1800$ base context, the same $n_{\mathrm{join}}=1257$ active join, the same rank-one $f_3$ source, and the same $\|P_{f3}\|^2=44.20095750389323$.  It separates the dwell summaries into $5'$ and $3'$ windows.  The leading segment is $5'$, with the positional verdict recording $5'$ ramp passage and no $3'$ body passage.  The primary $50$-codon ramp comparison gives
$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta R^2_{5'\ \mathrm{ramp}} &= 0.0011165640103326878,\\
\Delta R^2_{3'\ \mathrm{body}} &= -0.00026157313923957926,\\
\Delta R^2_{5'-3'} &= 0.001378137149572267.
\end{aligned}
$$
The sign-flip probability for a positive $5'$ ramp signal is $0.0478515625$, while the paired ramp-minus-body probability is $0.05859375$.  Thus the positional evidence is informative but weaker than the main dwell-mediation result: it supports a ramp-leading pattern, but it does not by itself establish a fully localized positional mechanism.

\paragraph{Placement among adjacent readouts.}
The dwell result is consistent with a broader finite-row picture in which $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinates are visible against protein abundance and translation-related surfaces, while remaining bounded to the readouts actually measured.  In the direct protein-omics scan, yeast has $5815$ joined protein rows, all $9$ residual coordinates survive the positive threshold, and the direct abundance statistic is
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{QP} &= 0.29837624994140827.
\end{aligned}
$$
For measured ribosome translation efficiency in yeast, the joined TE surface uses $5128$ genes with ribosome-TE coverage reported at $0.8818572656921754$, and the measured TE survival statistic is
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{QT,\mathrm{meas}} &= 0.13681931343730436.
\end{aligned}
$$
The measured TE-to-abundance decomposition in yeast reports
$$
\begin{aligned}
S_{QP} &= 0.3146818503437042,\\
S_{QT,\mathrm{meas}} &= 0.13681931343730436,\\
M^{QTP}_{\mathrm{meas}} &= 0.4793069656345167.
\end{aligned}
$$
A separate joint TE and mRNA-stability decomposition on $4759$ yeast rows reports $R^2_{QP}=0.2715520808117803$, $R^2_{QP\mid T,M}=0.13047582645538838$, and joint mediation fraction $0.5195182225621592$.  The partial readout-dictionary certificate on the same yeast context uses $\lambda_{\mathrm{DL}}=0.01$, $\rho_{\mathrm{join}}=0.05$, $120$ permutations, and $n_{\mathrm{join}}=1800$, with selection-aware coverage $0.24538211439730545$ and unexplained fraction $0.7546178856026946$.  These adjacent finite computations do not replace the dwell test; they locate it inside a family of measured translation and abundance readbacks.

\paragraph{Local interpretation.}
The supported statement is deliberately narrow.  In yeast, after residualizing the abundance target and the $f_3$ source against the declared controls, local ribosome-occupancy dwell summaries add held-out predictive power for the $f_3$-aligned protein-abundance component.  The positive increment survives a fixed-design row-label null and is accompanied by a positive $f_3$--dwell correlation.  This warrants the claim that dwell is a measured mediator-like readout for this finite surface.

\paragraph{No promotion beyond the measured surface.}
The computation does not by itself establish a causal translation mechanism.  It also does not imply folded structural order, physical admissibility, molecular function, organismal phenotype, adaptation, or a universal biological law.  Protein abundance is a steady-state proteomics readout; dwell is a ribosome-occupancy summary from the available yeast windows; both are condition- and dataset-bound.  Any stronger claim would require a separate layer-matched contact, such as perturbation or rescue data for mechanism, structure-level measurements for folding or admissibility, functional assays for molecular role, or phenotype assays for organism-level effect.  Without that additional contact, the result remains a finite statistical mediation record for the $f_3$-stress abundance component only.
