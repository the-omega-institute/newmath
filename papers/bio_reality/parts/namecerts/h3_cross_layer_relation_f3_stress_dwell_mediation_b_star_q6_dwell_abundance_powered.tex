\subsection{Stress-Dwell Mediation of the $f_3$ Protein-Abundance Component}
\label{sec:bioreality-f3-stress-dwell-mediation}

\paragraph{Finite row surface.}
This record tests a single $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinate, $f_3\_stress$, on a matched \textit{Saccharomyces cerevisiae} row surface in which codon-derived residual coordinates, local ribosome-occupancy dwell summaries, and measured protein abundance are present on the same gene rows.  The active target is not total abundance.  It is the one-dimensional protein-abundance component
$$
\begin{aligned}
P_{f_3} &= \operatorname{proj}_{\widetilde f_3}\bigl(\widetilde{\log_{10} P}\bigr),
\end{aligned}
$$
where both $f_3\_stress$ and $\log_{10} P$ have already been residualized against the sibling control basis $Z$.  The control rank on the active join is $24$, the $f_3$ source rank is $1$, and the joined finite surface has $n=1257$ rows inside a larger base pool of $1800$ rows.  The abundance component has norm squared $44.20095750389323$ and accounts for $0.1999063916718794$ of the residual protein-abundance target on this join.

\paragraph{Readout and fitted contrast.}
The dwell readout is a per-gene local relative ribosome-occupancy summary computed from file-provided $5'$ and $3'$ A-site occupancy windows.  The base design reaches held-out $R^2=0.1855578757644517$, while the base-plus-dwell design reaches held-out $R^2=0.20412261520502883$.  Thus the finite-row dwell increment is
$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta R^2_{\mathrm{dwell}} &= 0.20412261520502883 - 0.1855578757644517 \\
&= 0.01856473944057712.
\end{aligned}
$$
The dwell-only readback is $R^2=0.034336580953121976$.  The direct $f_3$--dwell correlation is $0.1615441450901653$, with approximate $p=7.873916057196935\times 10^{-9}$.  The computation used five folds, and the deterministic null kept the base design, full design, and folds fixed while permuting the row labels of $P_{f_3}$.  Across $120$ permutations, the null mean was $-0.0032911642620130563$, the lower and upper observed null range was $[-0.013799622026666476,\,0.005001350039772926]$, and the $95\%$ null cutoff was $0.0013115362189692937$.  Since $0.01856473944057712>0.0013115362189692937$, the dwell increment clears the finite-row permutation boundary.

\paragraph{Local interpretation.}
The supported statement is narrow: in the yeast row surface used here, local ribosome-occupancy dwell summaries carry predictive information for the $f_3\_stress$-aligned protein-abundance component beyond the base controls.  This is a translation-near readout because the dwell variables are derived from A-site occupancy windows, but the result is still an observational held-out prediction statement.  It is not a causal mediation effect, not a perturbation result, and not a proof that $f_3\_stress$ controls ribosome dwell time.  The result says that, after the declared residualization, the dwell channel explains an additional fraction of the abundance component selected by $f_3\_stress$.

\paragraph{Position check.}
A sibling positional computation separates the dwell evidence by window.  On the same organism, the positional run used $n=1257$ joined rows, base pool $1800$, five folds, source rank $1$, the same $P_{f_3}$ norm squared $44.20095750389323$, and the same $P_{f_3}$ target fraction $0.1999063916718794$.  The leading segment by held-out increment is $5'$; the recorded dominant segment is $5'$, the $5'$ ramp gate passed, and the $3'$ body gate did not pass.  In the structural-order companion readback, the primary ramp length $50$ gives weighted $f_3$ ramp held-out increment $0.0011165640103326878$ and weighted body increment $-0.00026157313923957926$, with ramp-minus-body $0.001378137149572267$.  The module-level conclusion is \emph{ramp-positive but not localized}: the $f_3$ ramp signal is positive, but the stricter paired localization gate does not pass.

In the measured abundance mediation surface, yeast has $5128$ three-way joined rows, $S_{QP}=0.3146818503437042$, $S_{QT,\mathrm{meas}}=0.13681931343730436$, and measured mediated fraction $0.4793069656345167$.  In the joint TE and mRNA-stability variance partition, yeast has $n=4759$ rows, $R^2_{QP}=0.2715520808117803$, $R^2_{QP\mid T}=0.1439989545940314$, $R^2_{QP\mid M}=0.25453612246767165$, and $R^2_{QP\mid T,M}=0.13047582645538838$, giving joint mediation fraction $0.5195182225621592$ with right-tail permutation $p=0.000999000999000999$.  These surrounding rows make the dwell result biologically well placed: it is one member of a yeast-centered translation and abundance readout family, not an isolated numerical coincidence.

\paragraph{Classification against alternative readbacks.}
The modeled-tAI four-case discriminant places all nine yeast coordinates in case $D$ and no yeast coordinate in cases $A$, $B$, or $C$ under the stated threshold $10^{-12}$; the same run reports $R^2_{QT}=0.8213457220900503$ and $R^2_{QP}=0.29837624994140827$ for yeast.  This modeled-readout classification should not be substituted for the dwell window result, because the dwell computation uses file-provided local A-site occupancy windows rather than modeled tAI.  The partial mediation certificate on yeast uses $\lambda_{\mathrm{DL}}=0.01$, $\rho_{\mathrm{join}}=0.05$, $n_{\mathrm{join}}=1800$, five folds, and $120$ permutations; it reports coverage $C^*=0.24538211439730545$, selection-aware null $95\%$ cutoff $0.029149007242956115$, and unexplained fraction $0.7546178856026946$.  The irreducibility companion uses the same $\lambda_{\mathrm{DL}}=0.01$, $\rho_{\mathrm{join}}=0.05$, $n_{\mathrm{join}}=1800$, and five-fold protocol, and records that the current reduction family refutes the original irreducibility hypothesis rather than certifying a primitive residual law.  Thus the dwell result is best read as a positive, finite-row reduction through a measured occupancy-derived readout, while substantial residual structure remains outside this single mediator.

\paragraph{No promotion beyond the measured layer.}
The admissible conclusion is finite and local.  The chapter supports a positive dwell increment for the yeast $f_3\_stress$ abundance component under the declared controls and deterministic permutation test.  It does not by itself establish translation causality, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, phenotype, mechanism, evolutionary direction, or universality.  Any such stronger statement would require an additional layer-matched contact and a separate statistic on matched rows.  In particular, protein abundance is a steady-state omics readout, dwell is a ribosome-occupancy summary, and their association after residualization is not a rescue experiment, not a synonymous-edit perturbation, and not a global biological law.
