\subsection{The $f_3$-stress dwell increment for yeast protein-abundance residuals}
\label{sec:bioreality-f3-stress-dwell-abundance}

\paragraph{Finite surface.}
This record fixes a finite row surface in \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}.  The source coordinate is the one-dimensional $f_3$-stress member of the nine-coordinate $B^*_{Q6}$ residual basis, and the target is not total protein abundance itself but the $f_3$-aligned component of the abundance residual.  The target vector is defined as the projection of $Z$-residualized $\log_{10}$ protein abundance onto the $Z$-residualized $f_3$-stress direction on the active join.  The control basis has rank $24$ and includes the usual coding-sequence covariates: length, amino-acid composition, GC3, and the $M$-density baseline used by the surrounding $B^*_{Q6}$ experiments.  The dwell readout is a per-gene local relative ribosome-occupancy summary computed from file-provided $5'$ and $3'$ A-site occupancy windows.  The experiment used $n=1257$ joined genes for the active dwell fit, with a base design size recorded as $1800$, five held-out folds, and rank $1$ for the tested $f_3$ direction.

\paragraph{Observed increment.}
The base model explains $R^2=0.1855578757644517$ on the held-out target scale.  Adding the dwell readout gives $R^2=0.20412261520502883$, so the observed held-out gain is
$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta R^2_{\mathrm{dwell}}
  &= 0.20412261520502883 - 0.1855578757644517 \\
  &= 0.01856473944057712 .
\end{aligned}
$$
The dwell readout alone has $R^2=0.034336580953121976$ against the same projected target.  The finite-row correlation between the residual $f_3$ coordinate and the dwell readout is $0.1615441450901653$, with approximate nominal probability $7.873916057196935\times 10^{-9}$.  The target projection has squared norm $44.20095750389323$ and accounts for $0.1999063916718794$ of the abundance target component on this surface.  Thus the fitted signal is not merely a sign annotation: it is a positive held-out increment on the explicitly projected abundance component.

\paragraph{Permutation boundary.}
The null comparison keeps the base and full designs and the five folds fixed, and permutes row labels of $P_{f_3}$ deterministically.  Across $120$ permutations the null summary is
$$
\begin{aligned}
\operatorname{mean}(\Delta R^2_{\mathrm{null}}) &= -0.0032911642620130563,\\
\operatorname{min}(\Delta R^2_{\mathrm{null}}) &= -0.013799622026666476,\\
\operatorname{max}(\Delta R^2_{\mathrm{null}}) &= 0.005001350039772926,\\
\operatorname{Null}_{95} &= 0.0013115362189692937 .
\end{aligned}
$$
The observed gain $0.01856473944057712$ is larger than both the $95\%$ null cutoff and the largest recorded null draw.  This is the local reason the record is classified as passed: the dwell row family adds predictive information for the $f_3$-aligned abundance component beyond the fixed base design on held-out folds.

\paragraph{Layer placement.}
This is a cross-layer relation in a narrow sense.  It joins codon-derived $B^*_{Q6}$ residual geometry, ribosome-occupancy dwell summaries, and protein-abundance residuals, but the positive statement is only the finite variance increment just described.  The larger protein-abundance scan supplies the downstream context: in the rank-powered direct protein-omics panel, yeast had $5815$ joined CDS/protein rows with join hit rate $0.9156038419146592$, and all nine $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinates survived against measured PAXdb abundance, with $R^2_{QP}=0.29837624994140827$.  The measured translation-efficiency panel for yeast used $5128$ genes, hit rate $0.8818572656921754$, and $R^2_{QT,\mathrm{meas}}=0.13681931343730436$ with all nine coordinates surviving.  In the measured TE to protein-abundance mediation surface, yeast had $5128$ three-way joined rows, $S_{QP}=0.3146818503437042$, $S_{QT,\mathrm{meas}}=0.13681931343730436$, and a measured mediation fraction $0.4793069656345167$.  Those surrounding finite facts make the dwell result biologically positioned, but they do not change its mathematical content: the present claim is the incremental dwell explanation of the $P_{f_3}$ abundance component.

\paragraph{Positional refinement.}
The companion positional dwell run uses the same organism, the same base size $1800$, the same $n=1257$ active join, the same five-fold design, the same rank-$1$ $f_3$ source, and the same target norm $44.20095750389323$.  It separates file-provided $5'$ head and $3'$ tail A-site occupancy windows.  At the primary ramp length $50$, the weighted $5'$ ramp increment is $0.0011165640103326878$, while the weighted body increment is $-0.00026157313923957926$; the paired ramp-minus-body increment is $0.001378137149572267$.  The sign-flip probability for the ramp being positive is $0.0478515625$, whereas the body probability is $0.54296875$ and the paired ramp-minus-body probability is $0.05859375$.  The positional module therefore reports a positive ramp signal but does not certify a fully localized ramp-over-body gate.  In the language of this chapter, the dwell increment is present, and its strongest visible window is the $5'$ side, but the spatial refinement remains weaker than the main dwell-abundance mediation statistic.

\paragraph{Reduction boundary.}
A separate yeast readout-dictionary reduction run gives a useful scale for what should not be overread here.  On a unified yeast context with $n=1800$ sampled rows out of $4758$ available joins, five folds, $\lambda_{\mathrm{DL}}=0.01$, and $\rho_{\mathrm{join}}=0.05$, the best selected family among measured TE, mRNA stability, and turnover covers $C^*=0.24538211439730545$ of the residual signal, leaving $0.7546178856026946$ unexplained after selection-aware calibration.  The selection-aware null $95\%$ cutoff is $0.029149007242956115$, and the naive fixed-subset null $95\%$ cutoff is $0.00598374993486675$.  The dwell result should be read in that same disciplined way: it is a real finite-row increment for one projected abundance component, not a complete decomposition of the abundance residual.

\paragraph{Local no-promotion rule.}
The admissible conclusion is that, on this yeast finite surface, file-derived local ribosome-occupancy dwell summaries carry held-out information about the $f_3$-stress projection of protein-abundance residuals, and the observed increment exceeds the fixed-design row-permutation boundary.  No claim about translation rate as a causal mediator follows from this alone.  No protein folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, organismal phenotype, evolutionary direction, or universal biological law follows from the dwell increment.  Such claims would require separate layer-matched measurements and their own controlled finite-row statistics.  The present chapter remains exactly at the level of a rank-sufficient, cross-sectional, controlled, non-causal relation among $f_3$ residual geometry, local ribosome-occupancy dwell summaries, and the projected abundance component.
