\subsection{The $f_3$-stress dwell-abundance mediation readback}
\label{sec:bioreality-f3-stress-dwell-abundance}

\paragraph{Finite row surface.}
The record considered here is the $f_3$-stress coordinate inside the nine-coordinate $B^*_{Q6}$ synonymous residual surface.  The active organism is \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}, and the finite join used for the mediation test contains $n=1257$ genes after matching the codon-derived residual coordinate, the protein-abundance target, the control design, and the dwell readout.  The readout named ``dwell'' is not an abstract translation label: in this run it is a per-gene local relative ribosome-occupancy summary computed from file-provided $5'$ and $3'$ A-site occupancy windows.  The protein target is the $f_3$ component of the controlled protein-abundance vector,
$$
\begin{aligned}
P_{f_3} &= \operatorname{proj}_{\widetilde f_3}\bigl(\widetilde{\log_{10} P}\bigr),\\
\|P_{f_3}\|_2^2 &= 44.20095750389323,
\end{aligned}
$$
where both abundance and $f_3$ are residualized against the same $Z$ controls used by the surrounding $B^*_{Q6}$ abundance tests.  The control basis has rank $24$, while the tested $f_3$ module has rank $1$.  The projected $f_3$ component accounts for $0.1999063916718794$ of the active target surface, so the test is not asking whether all abundance variation is explained by local dwell.  It asks the narrower finite question: whether the $f_3$-aligned abundance component has out-of-sample increment when local ribosome-occupancy summaries are added to the base model.

\paragraph{Held-out mediation statistic.}
The base design uses $1800$ rows in the common yeast context and is evaluated with $5$ fixed folds.  The base held-out coefficient of determination is
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{\mathrm{base}} &= 0.1855578757644517,
\end{aligned}
$$
while the base-plus-dwell design gives
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{\mathrm{full}} &= 0.20412261520502883.
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the finite held-out increment is
$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta R^2_{\mathrm{dwell}}
  &= R^2_{\mathrm{full}}-R^2_{\mathrm{base}}\\
  &= 0.01856473944057712.
\end{aligned}
$$
The dwell readout alone has $R^2=0.034336580953121976$ against the same target, and the direct correlation between $f_3$ and dwell is $0.1615441450901653$ with approximate $p=7.873916057196935\times 10^{-9}$.  These numbers are reported as finite-row diagnostics, not as a causal estimate.  The central claim is the held-out increment after the base terms are already present.

\paragraph{Permutation boundary.}
The null model keeps the base design, full design, and fold assignment fixed, and permutes row labels of $P_{f_3}$ deterministically.  Across $120$ permutations the null distribution has
$$
\begin{aligned}
\min(\Delta R^2_{\mathrm{null}}) &= -0.013799622026666476,\\
\operatorname{mean}(\Delta R^2_{\mathrm{null}}) &= -0.0032911642620130563,\\
\max(\Delta R^2_{\mathrm{null}}) &= 0.005001350039772926,\\
Q_{0.95}(\Delta R^2_{\mathrm{null}}) &= 0.0013115362189692937.
\end{aligned}
$$
Since $0.01856473944057712 > 0.0013115362189692937$, the finite row surface supports a positive dwell increment for the $f_3$-aligned abundance component.  In the four-case language of the paper, this is a measured-readout absorption result for a specific residual component, not a promotion of $f_3$ geometry into a general biological rule.

\paragraph{Adjacent row checks.}
The same $B^*_{Q6}$ program records that direct protein-omics survival is visible across a rank-powered panel, but that direct survival is not itself mediation.  For example, the direct $S^{QP}$ abundance fit has $R^2_{QP}=0.29837624994140827$ in yeast with $5815$ joined CDS rows and join hit rate $0.9156038419146592$; the same direct panel reports $R^2_{QP}=0.10767526677985896$ in \emph{E. coli} K12 MG1655 with $3739$ joined rows and hit rate $0.9978649586335735$, and $R^2_{QP}=0.04454565118019448$ in human with $19053$ joined rows and hit rate $0.9779294769799313$.  The measured ribosome-TE survival surface is also positive but organism-specific: yeast has $R^2_{QT,\mathrm{meas}}=0.13681931343730436$ on $5128$ TE genes with ribosome-TE join hit rate $0.8818572656921754$, \emph{E. coli} has $0.009394266754822262$ on $3415$ TE genes with hit rate $0.9133458143888741$, human has $0.018837911344127926$ on $11554$ TE genes with hit rate $0.6064136881331024$, and zebrafish has $0.04034217614654355$ on $13140$ TE genes with hit rate $0.9118667591950035$.  These surrounding surfaces justify treating the yeast dwell calculation as a concrete measured-readout refinement of a broader residual-abundance program, while preserving the distinction between direct abundance prediction and a translation-conditioned absorption statistic.

\paragraph{Position and dictionary context.}
A related positional run on the same yeast object reports the $5'$ segment as the leading segment by held-out increment.  With primary ramp length $50$, the weighted $f_3$ ramp increment is $0.0011165640103326878$, the weighted body increment is $-0.00026157313923957926$, and the weighted ramp-minus-body contrast is $0.001378137149572267$; its sign-flip probabilities are $0.0478515625$ for the ramp, $0.54296875$ for the body, and $0.05859375$ for the paired ramp-minus-body contrast.  The reported module classification is therefore ``ramp positive but not localized'': the ramp signal is positive, but the paired localization gate does not pass.  Separately, the partial-mediation certificate on the yeast context uses $\lambda_{\mathrm{DL}}=0.01$, $\rho_{\mathrm{join}}=0.05$, $n_{\mathrm{join}}=1800$, and $120$ permutations.  Its selection-aware null $95\%$ cutoff is $0.029149007242956115$, the fixed-subset null $95\%$ cutoff is $0.00598374993486675$, and the certified coverage is $C^*=0.24538211439730545$, leaving unexplained fraction $0.7546178856026946$.  The irreducibility check uses the same $\lambda_{\mathrm{DL}}=0.01$, $\rho_{\mathrm{join}}=0.05$, $n_{\mathrm{join}}=1800$, and $120$ permutations, and its own status semantics explicitly say that the current reduction families refute the original irreducibility hypothesis rather than proving a universal primitive law.  These companion facts place the dwell result in a finite reduction landscape: some residual abundance is absorbed by measured or dictionary readouts, and a substantial remainder is still outside the present readout family.

\paragraph{Local interpretation.}
The accepted statement is the following bounded one.  In a yeast finite-row join, after the specified residualization, adding local A-site dwell summaries improves held-out prediction of the $f_3$-projected protein-abundance component by $\Delta R^2=0.01856473944057712$, well above the deterministic row-permutation $95\%$ boundary $0.0013115362189692937$.  This licenses the phrase ``dwell-associated absorption of the $f_3$ abundance component'' for this row surface.  It does not license a standalone claim about translation as a whole, protein folding, physical viability, molecular function, cellular phenotype, causal mechanism, adaptation, or universality.  Any such stronger assertion would need its own matched contact and its own controlled statistic at that layer.
