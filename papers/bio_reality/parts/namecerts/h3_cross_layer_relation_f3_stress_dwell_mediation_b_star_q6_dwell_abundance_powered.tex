\subsection{Stress-coordinate dwell mediation for yeast protein abundance}
\label{sec:bioreality-f3-stress-dwell-mediation}

\paragraph{Finite row surface.}
This record tests a single $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual coordinate, $f3\_stress$, on a Saccharomyces cerevisiae matrix in which codon-derived residual geometry, local ribosome-occupancy dwell summaries, and measured protein abundance are all keyed to the same per-gene rows before fitting.  The active abundance target is not the full abundance vector.  It is the component
$$
\begin{aligned}
P_{f3}=\operatorname{proj}_{\widetilde f3}(\widetilde P),
\end{aligned}
$$
where $\widetilde P$ is $\log_{10}$ protein abundance after the sibling control basis $Z$, and $\widetilde f3$ is the same $f3\_stress$ coordinate after $Z$ residualization.  The control basis has rank $24$, the tested coordinate has rank $1$, and the active base-plus-dwell join contains $n=1257$ rows.  The row design was drawn from a base availability pool of $1800$ rows and evaluated with $5$ held-out folds.  The dwell readout is a per-gene local relative ribosome-occupancy summary computed from file-provided $5'$ and $3'$ A-site occupancy windows.

\paragraph{Controlled mediation statistic.}
The base design explains $R^2=0.1855578757644517$ on the held-out target, while the base plus dwell design explains $R^2=0.20412261520502883$.  Thus the finite-row increment is
$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta R^2_{\mathrm{dwell}}&=0.20412261520502883-0.1855578757644517\\
&=0.01856473944057712.
\end{aligned}
$$
The dwell readout alone has $R^2=0.034336580953121976$.  The sample correlation between the residual $f3\_stress$ coordinate and the dwell summary is $0.1615441450901653$, with approximate right-tail probability $7.873916057196935\times 10^{-9}$.  The projected abundance component has squared norm $44.20095750389323$ and accounts for $0.1999063916718794$ of the controlled abundance target used in this test.

\paragraph{Permutation boundary.}
The null comparison fixes the base design, full design, and fold assignment, then permutes the row labels of $P_{f3}$ deterministically.  Across $120$ permutations the null increments range from $-0.013799622026666476$ to $0.005001350039772926$, with mean $-0.0032911642620130563$ and $95$th percentile $0.0013115362189692937$.  The observed held-out increment satisfies
$$
\begin{aligned}
0.01856473944057712>0.0013115362189692937,
\end{aligned}
$$
so the dwell term is not merely a same-design degree-of-freedom artifact.  The deterministic seed for the row-label null is \texttt{sha256:b\_star\_q6\_f3\_stress\_dwell\_mediation\_powered:deterministic}.  This is a finite statistical mediation readback: local ribosome-occupancy dwell carries additional held-out information about the $f3\_stress$-projected abundance component after the declared controls.

\paragraph{Relation to adjacent measured readouts.}
The same yeast protein-abundance contact has a high CDS join: $5815$ joined proteins with hit rate $0.9156038419146592$.  On the direct protein-omics surface, all $9$ $B^{*}_{Q6}$ coordinates survive against measured PAXdb abundance in yeast, with $R^2_{QP}=0.29837624994140827$.  A separate measured ribosome-TE surface joins $5128$ yeast genes, with ribosome-TE join hit rate $0.8818572656921754$ and $R^2_{QT,\mathrm{meas}}=0.13681931343730436$ across the same $9$ coordinates.  In the measured abundance mediation fit for yeast, $S_{QP}=0.3146818503437042$, $S_{QT,\mathrm{meas}}=0.13681931343730436$, and the measured mediated fraction is $0.4793069656345167$.  These neighboring readouts are consistent with the dwell result in the limited sense that translation-associated measurements absorb a nonzero part of the codon-residual abundance association; they do not by themselves identify a biochemical pathway.

\paragraph{Position and module classification.}
The position-resolved dwell test localizes the stronger finite signal to the $5'$ window.  In the positional readout, $n=1257$ rows again enter the active join, $P_{f3}$ has squared norm $44.20095750389323$, the leading segment by $\Delta R^2$ is \texttt{5prime}, and the recorded dominant segment is \texttt{5prime}.  The $5'$ ramp gate passed, while the $3'$ body gate did not.  A separate ramp-versus-body structural-order screen reports module classification \texttt{ramp\_positive\_but\_not\_localized}: at primary ramp length $50$, the weighted $f3$ ramp held-out increment is $0.0011165640103326878$, the weighted body increment is $-0.00026157313923957926$, and the paired ramp-minus-body increment is $0.001378137149572267$.  The ramp sign-flip probability is $0.0478515625$, but the paired localization gate does not pass at the recorded threshold.  The correct reading is therefore narrower than a structure claim: the dwell-associated abundance component is strongest near the $5'$ occupancy window, while the position screen does not promote the observation to folded order.

\paragraph{Readout dictionary and reduction limits.}
The yeast partial-mediation certificate uses $n=1800$ rows from $4758$ available join rows, $5$ folds, $120$ permutations, $\lambda_{DL}=0.01$, and $\rho_{\mathrm{join}}=0.05$.  Its selected readout dictionary $\{\texttt{measured\_te},\texttt{mrna\_stability},\texttt{turnover}\}$ covers $0.24538211439730545$ of the source signal after selection-aware correction, leaving unexplained fraction $0.7546178856026946$.  The raw best coverage over the candidate family is $0.2745002077400602$, the fixed-subset null $95$th percentile is $0.00598374993486675$, and the selection-aware null $95$th percentile is $0.029149007242956115$.  The irreducibility certificate uses the same $\lambda_{DL}=0.01$, $\rho_{\mathrm{join}}=0.05$, $n=1800$, and $120$ permutations on a yeast abundance target.  Its status means that the source signal is real and that at least one listed reduction family defeats the stronger irreducibility alternative; it is not a certificate that the residual has no lower-level explanation.

\paragraph{Local conclusion and blocked promotions.}
The finite-row conclusion is that the $f3\_stress$ abundance component in Saccharomyces cerevisiae has positive dwell-mediated evidence: adding local ribosome-occupancy dwell summaries to the controlled base design raises held-out $R^2$ by $0.01856473944057712$, far above the fixed-readout permutation threshold $0.0013115362189692937$.  The result is a rank-powered cross-layer association among a codon residual coordinate, local ribosome occupancy, and measured protein abundance.  It does not assert universal translation law, causal mediation, folded structure, physical admissibility, molecular function, phenotype, or evolutionary direction.  Those stronger readings require separate layer-matched contacts and their own controlled finite-row statistics.  Here the permitted claim is exactly the bounded one: in the recorded yeast rows, the dwell readout carries held-out information about the $f3\_stress$-projected abundance component after the declared residualization and null comparison.
