\subsection{Stress-Dwell Mediation of the $f_3$ Abundance Component}
\label{sec:bioreality-f3-stress-dwell-mediation}

\paragraph{Finite panel.}
The finite row surface for this record is the yeast matched panel for the $B^*_{Q6}$ coordinate named $f_3\_stress$.  The tested organism is \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}.  The active join contains $1257$ genes after matching coding-sequence residual coordinates, protein abundance, and file-provided ribosome-occupancy dwell summaries.  The base design is drawn from a larger yeast context with $1800$ rows, while the fitted mediation readback uses $5$ fixed held-out folds.  The coordinate entering the mediation test has rank $1$, and the control basis has rank $24$.  The protein target is not raw abundance itself; it is the component $P_{f_3}$ obtained by projecting $Z$-residualized $\log_{10}$ protein abundance onto the $Z$-residualized $f_3\_stress$ direction on the same base-plus-dwell join.  In this panel the target component has squared norm $44.20095750389323$ and accounts for fraction $0.1999063916718794$ of the residual protein-abundance target used by the test.

\paragraph{Readout and null design.}
The dwell readout is a per-gene local relative ribosome-occupancy summary computed from file-provided $5'$ and $3'$ A-site occupancy windows.  The base model, without the dwell summary, reaches held-out $R^2=0.1855578757644517$.  The dwell-only surface has $R^2=0.034336580953121976$.  Adding dwell to the base model raises the held-out score to $R^2=0.20412261520502883$, so the measured increment is
$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta R^2_{\mathrm{held\ out}} &= 0.20412261520502883-0.1855578757644517 \\
&= 0.01856473944057712.
\end{aligned}
$$
The fixed null keeps the base design, full design, and fold assignment unchanged, and permutes row labels of $P_{f_3}$ deterministically.  Across $120$ permutations the null mean is $-0.0032911642620130563$, the null minimum is $-0.013799622026666476$, the null maximum is $0.005001350039772926$, and the $95$th percentile is $0.0013115362189692937$.  The observed increment exceeds that percentile by more than an order of magnitude.  The direct correlation between the $f_3\_stress$ coordinate and the dwell summary is $0.1615441450901653$, with approximate $p=7.873916057196935\times 10^{-9}$.

\paragraph{Mediation readback.}
The finite-row conclusion is therefore a positive dwell-mediated abundance readback for the $f_3\_stress$ component in yeast.  The word ``mediated'' is used here in the statistical sense fixed by the row design: the dwell readout explains held-out variation in the $P_{f_3}$ protein-abundance component beyond the base covariates, and the observed gain is larger than the row-label null boundary.  It is not a perturbation claim and it is not a causal pathway estimate.  The row data say that the local ribosome-occupancy dwell summary carries additional predictive information for the abundance component aligned with $f_3\_stress$; they do not by themselves identify a molecular event, a rescue mechanism, or a universal translation law.

\paragraph{Relation to the surrounding finite facts.}
The same yeast coordinate family is already visible in broader readouts.  In direct protein-omics survival, yeast has $R^2_{QP}=0.29837624994140827$ with all $9$ $B^*_{Q6}$ coordinates surviving after length, amino-acid composition, GC3, and M-density controls.  In measured ribosome-TE survival, yeast has $R^2_{QT,\mathrm{meas}}=0.13681931343730436$, again with $9$ surviving coordinates.  In the measured TE to protein-abundance decomposition, yeast has $S_{QP}=0.3146818503437042$, $S_{QT,\mathrm{meas}}=0.13681931343730436$, and measured mediated fraction $0.4793069656345167$.  The dwell test is narrower: it isolates the $f_3\_stress$ abundance component and asks whether a local ribosome-occupancy dwell surface improves held-out prediction of that component.  The positive result is compatible with the larger measured-TE mediation facts, but it is not a replacement for them.

\paragraph{Position and reduction context.}
A related positional dwell test reports the same $P_{f_3}$ target norm $44.20095750389323$ and the same target fraction $0.1999063916718794$ on the yeast panel.  Its finite verdict identifies $5'$ as the leading segment by held-out $\Delta R^2$, with the $5'$ ramp passing and the $3'$ body not passing.  The present chapter does not need that positional split to pass; the pooled dwell mediation already clears its row-label null.  The split nevertheless sharpens the readback: the measurable dwell signal is not merely an arbitrary protein-abundance association, but is consistent with a position-sensitive ribosome-occupancy contribution.  A separate partial-mediation certificate on the yeast context reports coverage $C^*=0.24538211439730545$ for a measured-readout dictionary with unexplained fraction $0.7546178856026946$, using $\lambda_{DL}=0.01$, $\rho_{\mathrm{join}}=0.05$, $5$ folds, $120$ permutations, $n_{\mathrm{join}}=1800$, and $4758$ available joined rows.  That certificate keeps the present result in the right scale: the dwell readout supplies real finite predictive mass, while a large residual component remains outside the measured dictionary.

\paragraph{Local boundary.}
This record supports only the finite statement that, in the matched yeast rows, the $f_3\_stress$-aligned protein-abundance component receives a positive held-out increment from local ribosome-occupancy dwell summaries beyond the base design, and that this increment exceeds the deterministic row-label null.  It does not imply translation realization in general, folded structure, physical admissibility, molecular function, organismal phenotype, causal mechanism, adaptive direction, or universality.  Any such higher-layer statement would require its own matched contact and its own controlled finite statistic.  Direct $q_i\to P_k$ association, even when positive, remains insufficient for mechanism unless the relevant translation readout is measured and conditioning on it removes or reduces the direct signal on the same controlled rows.
