\subsection{F3-stress dwell mediation in yeast protein abundance}
\label{sec:bioreality-f3-stress-dwell-mediation}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite-row object.}
This record tests whether the $f_3\_stress$ coordinate inside the nine-dimensional $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual basis has a measured dwell-mediated component in yeast protein abundance.  The row surface is not a symbolic extrapolation: it is a finite Saccharomyces cerevisiae join in which codon residual geometry, protein abundance, and local ribosome-occupancy dwell summaries are all present on the same genes after the declared controls are formed.  The active organism is Saccharomyces cerevisiae, with organism key $saccharomyces\_cerevisiae$.  The dwell readout is defined as per-gene local relative ribosome occupancy from file-provided $5'$ and $3'$ A-site occupancy windows.  The target is the component $P_{f3}$ obtained by projecting $Z$-residualized $\log_{10}$ protein abundance onto the $Z$-residualized $f_3\_stress$ direction on the active base-plus-dwell join.

\paragraph{Controlled design.}
The fitted surface uses $n=1257$ joined rows out of a base availability scale of $1800$ rows, with $5$ held-out folds.  The $f_3$ direction has rank $1$, while the control basis has rank $24$.  The control basis is the same family used by the surrounding $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ protein-abundance residual tests: coding length, amino-acid composition, GC3, and the M-density baseline are used before the $f_3$ protein-abundance component is read.  In the larger yeast abundance contact, the PAXdb-to-CDS join contains $5815$ joined proteins with join hit rate $0.9156038419146592$; the direct $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ protein-abundance survival surface for yeast has
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{QP}(\text{yeast}) &= 0.29837624994140827,\\
\#\{\text{surviving }B^{\ast}_{Q6}\text{ coordinates}\} &= 9.
\end{aligned}
$$
For the measured ribosome-TE contact in yeast, $5128$ genes with TE are used, with ribosome-TE join hit rate $0.8818572656921754$ and measured translation-readout survival
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{QT,\mathrm{meas}}(\text{yeast}) &= 0.13681931343730436,\\
\#\{\text{surviving coordinates against measured TE}\} &= 9.
\end{aligned}
$$
These background surfaces establish that the yeast finite rows are not rank-degenerate and that the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ basis has measurable protein-abundance and translation-readout contact on the same biological system.  They do not by themselves prove dwell mediation; the dwell claim is tested by the separate held-out increment below.

\paragraph{Dwell-mediated increment.}
The primary dwell fit compares a base model to a base-plus-dwell model for the $P_{f3}$ target.  The recorded finite-row values are
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{\mathrm{base}} &= 0.1855578757644517,\\
R^2_{\mathrm{dwell\ alone}} &= 0.034336580953121976,\\
R^2_{\mathrm{full}} &= 0.20412261520502883,\\
\Delta R^2_{\mathrm{held\ out}} &= 0.01856473944057712.
\end{aligned}
$$
The sign and scale are therefore specific: adding the dwell summaries to the base design improves held-out prediction of the $f_3$-aligned abundance component by about $1.856\times 10^{-2}$ in $R^2$ units.  The observed correlation between the $f_3$ coordinate and dwell readout is
$$
\begin{aligned}
r(f_3,\mathrm{dwell}) &= 0.1615441450901653,\\
p_{\mathrm{approx}} &= 7.873916057196935\times 10^{-9}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The $P_{f3}$ component has squared norm $44.20095750389323$ and accounts for fraction $0.1999063916718794$ of the target.  Thus the reported increment is not an all-coordinate protein-abundance statement; it is the dwell increment for the one-dimensional $f_3$ projection of controlled protein abundance.

\paragraph{Permutation boundary.}
The null model is a deterministic row-label permutation of $P_{f3}$ with the base and full designs, and the held-out folds, kept fixed.  The permutation count is $120$, using seed $sha256:b\_star\_q6\_f3\_stress\_dwell\_mediation\_powered:deterministic$.  The null distribution is recorded as
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{Null}_{\min} &= -0.013799622026666476,\\
\mathrm{Null}_{\mathrm{mean}} &= -0.0032911642620130563,\\
\mathrm{Null}_{95} &= 0.0013115362189692937,\\
\mathrm{Null}_{\max} &= 0.005001350039772926.
\end{aligned}
$$
The observed held-out increment satisfies
$$
\begin{aligned}
0.01856473944057712 &> 0.0013115362189692937,\\
0.01856473944057712 &> 0.005001350039772926.
\end{aligned}
$$
The first inequality clears the declared $95\%$ permutation boundary, and the second also exceeds the largest value among the recorded deterministic permutations.  On this finite surface, the dwell readout therefore carries a positive held-out increment for the $f_3$-aligned protein-abundance component.

\paragraph{Position and reduction context.}
A related positional dwell test on the same organism and the same $P_{f3}$ target records the same $P_{f3}$ norm $44.20095750389323$, the same fraction $0.1999063916718794$, and the same $n=1257$ active join with $5$ folds.  Its dominant segment is $5prime$: the $5'$ window passes while the $3'$ body window does not.  This makes the dwell contact more specific than an undifferentiated ribosome-footprint aggregate, although the positional call is still a statistical localization of the predictive increment rather than a mechanistic assay.  In the broader yeast reduction dictionary, the partial mediation certificate records $\lambda_{DL}=0.01$, $\rho_{join}=0.05$, $n=1800$, selection-aware null $95\%$ value $0.029149007242956115$, and coverage $C^{\ast}=0.24538211439730545$ for the candidate readout family $\{\mathrm{measured\_te},\mathrm{mrna\_stability},\mathrm{turnover}\}$.  The corresponding unexplained fraction is $0.7546178856026946$.  The irreducibility audit for the same source design also uses $\lambda_{DL}=0.01$, $\rho_{join}=0.05$, $n=1800$, and $120$ permutations, and its registered status states that at least one listed reduction family refutes the original irreducibility hypothesis.  The dwell result should therefore be read as one measured contributor to a partially reducible abundance residual, not as a complete reduction of the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ signal.

\paragraph{Four-case interpretation.}
For this chapter, the finite-row verdict is case-specific.  The $f_3\_stress$ abundance component has a positive dwell increment because $\Delta R^2_{\mathrm{held\ out}}=0.01856473944057712$ exceeds the fixed permutation boundary.  In the measured-TE mediation surface for yeast, the matched three-way join has $5128$ rows, measured translation survival $S_{QT,\mathrm{meas}}=0.13681931343730436$, protein-abundance survival $S_{QP}=0.3146818503437042$, and measured mediated fraction $0.4793069656345167$.  Those numbers support the local statement that yeast has a measured translation-layer readout and protein-abundance readout on a rank-powered joined surface.  The dwell chapter narrows this further: for the $f_3$-aligned abundance projection, local ribosome-occupancy dwell summaries improve held-out prediction beyond the base design and beyond the deterministic row-label null.

\paragraph{Local boundary.}
The result is a finite statistical mediation readback, not a general law of coding sequence biology.  It does not by itself assert translation causation, elongation mechanism, folding order, physical admissibility, molecular function, phenotype, adaptation, or universality.  The dwell readout comes from local relative ribosome occupancy windows; it is not a perturbation, rescue, synonymous-edit experiment, folding assay, localization assay, or functional assay.  A stronger biological claim would require a separate layer-matched contact and a new finite statistic at that layer.  In particular, no statement about folded structure, protein function, physical admissibility, system phenotype, or evolutionary direction follows from the positive dwell increment alone.  The supported claim is only this: on the recorded yeast joined rows, after the stated residualization and with folds fixed, dwell summaries add a positive held-out component to the controlled $f_3\_stress$ projection of protein abundance, and that component exceeds the deterministic permutation boundary.
