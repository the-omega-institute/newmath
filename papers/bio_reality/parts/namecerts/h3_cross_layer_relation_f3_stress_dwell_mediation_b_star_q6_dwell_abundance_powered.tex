\subsection{The $f_3$-stress dwell mediation certificate for yeast abundance}
\label{sec:f3-stress-dwell-mediation-yeast-abundance}

\paragraph{Finite row surface.}
This record isolates the $f_3$-stress coordinate inside the nine-dimensional $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual basis and tests whether a measured ribosome-dwell readout carries incremental predictive information for the $f_3$-aligned component of protein abundance.  The active organism is \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}.  The response is not total abundance itself, but the projection $P_{f3}$ of $Z$-residualized $\log_{10}$ protein abundance onto the $Z$-residualized $f_3$-stress coordinate on the active base-plus-dwell join.  The control basis has rank $24$, the tested $f_3$ direction has rank $1$, and the joined finite matrix contains $n=1257$ genes after the dwell, abundance, sequence, and control rows are matched.  The registered base sampling frame is $1800$ rows, evaluated with $5$ held-out folds.  The dwell readout is a per-gene local relative ribosome occupancy summary computed from file-provided $5'$ and $3'$ A-site occupancy windows.

\paragraph{Observed numerical certificate.}
The base model reaches held-out $R^2=0.1855578757644517$ for $P_{f3}$.  Adding the dwell readout gives full held-out $R^2=0.20412261520502883$, hence the finite-row increment is
$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta R^2_{\mathrm{dwell}} &= 0.20412261520502883-0.1855578757644517 \\
&=0.01856473944057712.
\end{aligned}
$$
The dwell readout alone has $R^2=0.034336580953121976$ against the same target component.  The sample correlation between $f_3$-stress and the dwell readout is $0.1615441450901653$, with approximate $p=7.873916057196935\times 10^{-9}$.  The target component has squared norm $\|P_{f3}\|^2=44.20095750389323$ and accounts for $0.1999063916718794$ of the target surface used by the certificate.  These values identify a positive finite-row association between local ribosome dwell and the $f_3$-aligned abundance component; they do not identify a causal chain.

\paragraph{Permutation boundary.}
The null model fixes the base and full designs and the fold assignment, then deterministically permutes the row labels of $P_{f3}$.  Across $120$ such permutations, the null increment has mean $-0.0032911642620130563$, minimum $-0.013799622026666476$, maximum $0.005001350039772926$, and $95$th percentile $0.0013115362189692937$.  The observed increment exceeds this finite null threshold:
$$
\begin{aligned}
0.01856473944057712 &> 0.0013115362189692937.
\end{aligned}
$$
The run therefore passes the registered readout-permutation test.  The deterministic seed is \texttt{sha256:b\_star\_q6\_f3\_stress\_dwell\_mediation\_powered:deterministic}, and the experiment identifier is \texttt{b\_star\_q6\_f3\_stress\_dwell\_mediation\_powered}.  The module is classified at the cross-layer relation level because the tested surface joins codon residual geometry, local ribosome occupancy, and protein abundance within one finite row table.

\paragraph{Position and reduction context.}
A separate positional dwell run on the same organism reports that the leading segment by held-out increment is the $5'$ window.  For the primary ramp length $50$, the weighted $f_3$ ramp increment is $0.0011165640103326878$, the weighted body increment is $-0.00026157313923957926$, and the paired ramp-minus-body increment is $0.001378137149572267$.  The positional conclusion is \texttt{ramp\_positive\_but\_not\_localized}: the ramp signal is positive, but the stricter localization gate does not pass.  A separate partial-reduction certificate on the unified yeast context join used $n=1800$, $5$ folds, $120$ permutations, $\rho_{\mathrm{join}}=0.05$, and $\lambda_{\mathrm{DL}}=0.01$; its best selection-aware coverage by at most three measured readouts was $C^{*}=0.24538211439730545$, leaving unexplained fraction $0.7546178856026946$.  Thus the dwell result is positive but partial: it marks a real finite-row reduction of an $f_3$-aligned abundance component, while most of the broader residual signal remains outside that small readout dictionary.

\paragraph{Local interpretation.}
The passed dwell certificate supports the following bounded statement.  In yeast, after the stated sequence and composition controls, local ribosome dwell summaries add held-out predictive power for the $f_3$-stress-aligned component of protein abundance, and this increment is larger than the fixed row-label permutation threshold.  The admissible readback is therefore a finite statistical relation among the $f_3$ residual coordinate, measured dwell summaries, and the abundance component $P_{f3}$.
