\subsection{The f3-stress dwell mediation certificate}\label{sec:bioreality-f3-stress-dwell-mediation}\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite matrix surface.} The claim is evaluated on a finite Saccharomyces cerevisiae row surface in which the active coordinate is the one-dimensional $f3\_stress$ projection inside the nine-coordinate $B^*_{Q6}$ synonymous residual system. The target response is not total protein abundance itself, but the component $P_{f3}$ obtained by projecting $Z$-residualized $\log_{10}$ protein abundance onto the $Z$-residualized $f3\_stress$ direction on the active join. The control basis has rank $24$, and the tested coordinate block has rank $1$. The active dwell-abundance experiment used $n=1257$ joined rows, with a base design size parameter $1800$ and five held-out folds. The dwell readout is a per-gene local relative ribosome-occupancy summary computed from file-supplied $5'$ and $3'$ A-site occupancy windows, so the recorded readout is a bounded ribosome-position statistic rather than an unrestricted translation mechanism.

\paragraph{Observed scalar certificate.} The fitted base model explains held-out abundance-component variation at $R^2=0.1855578757644517$. Adding the dwell readout gives $R^2=0.20412261520502883$, hence the registered held-out increment is $\Delta R^2=0.01856473944057712$. Dwell alone has $R^2=0.034336580953121976$. The direct finite-row association between the residual coordinate and the dwell readout is positive, with $\operatorname{corr}(f3,dwell)=0.1615441450901653$ and approximate $p=7.873916057196935\times 10^{-9}$. The projected target component has squared norm $44.20095750389323$ and accounts for $0.1999063916718794$ of the abundance target on this active surface. These numbers support a specific statement: the $f3\_stress$ abundance component is better predicted when the local dwell summary is added to the base design.

\paragraph{Permutation boundary.} The null comparison holds the base and full designs and the folds fixed, then permutes the row labels of $P_{f3}$ deterministically. Across $120$ permutations the null distribution has mean $-0.0032911642620130563$, minimum $-0.013799622026666476$, maximum $0.005001350039772926$, and 95th percentile $0.0013115362189692937$. The observed increment therefore exceeds the fixed-readout permutation threshold by more than an order of magnitude:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta R^2_{\mathrm{obs}} &= 0.01856473944057712,\\
\Delta R^2_{\mathrm{null},95} &= 0.0013115362189692937,\\
\Delta R^2_{\mathrm{obs}}-\Delta R^2_{\mathrm{null},95} &= 0.017253203221607825.
\end{aligned}
$$
This is the finite-row reason the packet is marked passed. The claim is not that the dwell variable is the unique biological cause of the abundance component; it is that, on the registered yeast join and with the registered controls, the dwell readout carries out-of-fold information about the $f3\_stress$ protein-abundance component beyond the base design.

\paragraph{Contact consistency.} The same organism has a measured protein-abundance join in the direct protein-omics surface: Saccharomyces cerevisiae contributes $5815$ joined CDS/protein rows with join hit rate $0.9156038419146592$, and its direct $B^*_{Q6}$ to protein-abundance residual survival is $R^2_{QP}=0.29837624994140827$ with all nine residual coordinates surviving at the positive threshold. The measured ribosome-TE surface for the same organism reports $5128$ genes with TE, a ribosome-TE join hit rate $0.8818572656921754$, and $R^2_{QT,meas}=0.13681931343730436$ with nine surviving coordinates. In the measured TE-to-abundance mediation decomposition, the yeast three-way join also has $5128$ rows, $S_{QP}=0.3146818503437042$, $S_{QT,meas}=0.13681931343730436$, and measured mediated fraction $0.4793069656345167$. These related finite surfaces do not replace the dwell certificate, but they show that the yeast row universe contains matched abundance, measured ribosome-derived readouts, and codon-residual coordinates at nondegenerate rank.

\paragraph{Dwell localization and module reading.} The positional dwell companion separates the local ribosome-occupancy summaries into $5'$ and $3'$ windows on the same organism and the same $P_{f3}$ target norm. It again uses $n=1257$, five folds, rank $1$ for $f3$, and control-basis rank $24$. Its recorded dominant segment is $5prime$: the $5'$ ramp window passes, while the $3'$ body window does not. The structural-order ramp companion records the classification $ramp\_positive\_but\_not\_localized$: at primary ramp size $50$, the weighted $f3$ ramp held-out increment is $0.0011165640103326878$, the body increment is $-0.00026157313923957926$, and the ramp-minus-body value is $0.001378137149572267$, with ramp sign-flip probability $0.0478515625$ and paired ramp-body probability $0.05859375$. Thus the local dwell signal is compatible with an early-window ribosome-occupancy contribution, but it is not a license to assert a folded-structure or localization mechanism.

\paragraph{Reduction status.} The broader yeast readout dictionary certificate places the dwell result in a partial-reduction landscape. On a unified yeast context join with $n=1800$ out of $4758$ available rows, five folds, $\rho_{join}=0.05$, and $\lambda_{DL}=0.01$, the best registered family of at most three measured readouts covers $C^*=0.24538211439730545$ of the residual signal, against a selection-aware null 95th percentile $0.029149007242956115$ and a naive fixed-subset null 95th percentile $0.00598374993486675$. The remaining unexplained fraction is $0.7546178856026946$. This matters locally: the dwell certificate is a positive finite readout, but it is not an exhaustion theorem for $B^*_{Q6}$ residual protein-abundance structure.

\paragraph{Local boundary.} The admissible conclusion is a bounded cross-layer statement: on the registered yeast rows, a local ribosome-occupancy dwell summary mediates a positive held-out increment for the $f3\_stress$ component of residualized protein abundance. No stronger biological layer is obtained from this chapter alone. In particular, the finite result does not establish translation causality, protein folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, organismal phenotype, a perturbation mechanism, or a universal biological rule. Each of those interpretations would require a separate layer-matched measurement surface and its own controlled finite-row statistic.
