\subsection{Proteostasis turnover residual audit for the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ coordinates}\label{sec:bioreality-proteostasis-turnover-residual-b-star-q6}\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite surface.}
This record asks whether the nine $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates carry any remaining information about measured protein turnover after the more immediate expression, translation, and protein-state controls have already been entered. The finite rows are genes with matched residual coordinates, expression-level information, translation-efficiency information, structural-confidence or order proxies where available, $N$-end and related degradation controls, and a measured turnover readout. The admissible question is deliberately narrow: after the control surface is fitted, does the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ block improve the turnover description? It is not a claim that synonymous geometry causes degradation, not a claim about folding, and not a claim about molecular function.

\paragraph{Coordinate block.}
The residual block used in the audit is the same nine-coordinate $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ list used in the translation and protein-abundance rows: $K\_AAA$, $Arg\_AGR$, $Ile\_AUA$, $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$, $Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG$, $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$, $Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG$, $Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY$, and $f3\_stress$. In the larger matched protein-abundance surface, the same block was evaluated across twelve organisms with joined protein counts ranging from $1656$ in \emph{Sulfolobus solfataricus} to $19395$ in mouse, and the statistical mediation scalar $M^{QTP}$ stayed in the unit interval in all reported organisms. For example, the mediated fraction was $0.9491551041216973$ in yeast, $0.612922252793667$ in \emph{E. coli} K12 MG1655, $0.27206289160119157$ in human, and $0.01850668564589326$ in \emph{Sulfolobus}. Those rows show that the coordinate block can be placed on a controlled translation-to-protein abundance surface, but they do not by themselves test turnover.

\paragraph{Turnover residual test.}
The turnover audit used $n=3290$ genes and $200$ null draws. Let $L2$ denote the turnover model after expression, translation-efficiency, structural-confidence or order, $N$-end, and matching controls have been entered, and let $L3$ denote the same model with the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual block added. The observed fit moved in the wrong direction for a positive residual claim:
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{L2} &= 0.055740851626933985,\\
R^2_{L3} &= 0.050278872193227464,\\
\Delta R^2 &= R^2_{L3}-R^2_{L2} = -0.005461979433706483,\\
\Delta DL &= -13.68819707902732\ \mathrm{bits}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The matched-bin null gave $p=0.8855721393034826$, and the recoded null gave $p=1.0$. The corresponding $99$th percentile null description-length improvements were $3.51918934107672$ bits for the matched-bin null and $1.9038288477880905$ bits for the recoded null. Thus the observed $\Delta DL$ is not merely below the null thresholds; it is negative. The finite-row verdict is therefore a bounded negative one: with these rows and controls, there is no evidence that $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ improves measured turnover once expression, translation, structural-confidence or order, $N$-end, and allied covariates are already present.

\paragraph{Relation to nearby positive contacts.}
This negative turnover result is not a global null for the coordinate block. In the observable execution decomposition, the registered yeast rate-slot run used $3289$ genes and returned the finite conclusion that at least one observed rate-proxy slot passed both effective null classes. In the dynamic mass-balance run, the fixed subsample used $120$ genes over $718$ intervals; the model had $R^2_{L0,M}=0.9960628201518571$, $R^2_{L1,M,E}=0.996410628851051$, $R^2_{L2}=0.9712443377743347$, and $R^2_{L3}=0.8953999568421264$, with $\Delta R^2=-0.07584438093220836$, $\Delta DL=-668.8046070381928$ bits, $p_{TEbin}=0.6831683168316832$, and $p_{timeshuffle}=0.9306930693069307$. These neighboring rows separate two facts. First, $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ can enter bounded translation, rate-proxy, and protein-abundance decompositions. Second, the present turnover target does not inherit those successes unless its own turnover residual improves under its own null classes.

\paragraph{Readback.}
The correct readback is a controlled refusal of turnover promotion. The finite statement is: for the current matched turnover data, adding the nine-dimensional $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual block after expression, translation-efficiency, structural-confidence or order, $N$-end, and related controls decreases held-out explanatory performance and fails both null comparisons. The admissible conclusion is therefore current-data closure for the negative turnover residual: no positive $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ turnover-prediction claim is supported here. The residual coordinates remain available for translation-readout and protein-abundance mediation surfaces where those rows are separately joined and tested, but they are not promoted into proteostasis turnover from this audit.

\paragraph{Local boundary.}
No stronger biological interpretation follows from this chapter. A turnover residual audit does not establish translation realization, ribosome mechanism, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, organismal phenotype, selection, causation, or universality. A direct $Q_i \to P_k$ or $Q_i \to$ turnover association is not enough for protein realization when the $Q_i \to T_j$ translation step is null, missing, or untested. Any future positive claim above this layer would need a separate layer-matched contact and a new controlled statistic on the same rows or on a clearly declared finite replacement surface.
