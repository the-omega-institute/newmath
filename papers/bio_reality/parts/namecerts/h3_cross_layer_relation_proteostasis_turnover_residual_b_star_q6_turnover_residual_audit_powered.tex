\subsection{Proteostasis turnover residual audit for $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$}
\label{sec:bioreality-proteostasis-turnover-residual-b-star-q6}

\paragraph{Finite row surface.}
This BioReality record tests whether the nine-dimensional synonymous residual packet $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ carries measurable information about protein turnover after the already available expression, translation, structural-confidence, and N-end covariates have been given priority. The finite surface has $3290$ matched genes and compares a lower model $L_2$ against an augmented model $L_3$ that adds the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates after the proteostasis controls are present. The rows are not read as a perturbation experiment, a degradation mechanism, or a folding assay. They are a controlled residual audit: the only admissible positive conclusion would be an improvement of the turnover fit after the declared covariates, with null comparisons showing that the improvement is not a coding-baseline artifact.

\paragraph{Test statistic.}
The powered turnover run reports
$$
\begin{aligned}
 n_{\mathrm{genes}} &= 3290,\\
 R^2(L_2) &= 0.055740851626933985,\\
 R^2(L_3) &= 0.050278872193227464,\\
 \Delta R^2 &= R^2(L_3)-R^2(L_2)=-0.005461979433706483,\\
 \Delta \mathrm{DL}_{\mathrm{bits}} &= -13.68819707902732.
\end{aligned}
$$
The bootstrap budget is $B=200$. Against the matched-bin null, the upper reference point is $3.51918934107672$ bits and the reported tail probability is $p_{\mathrm{matchedbin}}=0.8855721393034826$. Against the recoded null, the upper reference point is $1.9038288477880905$ bits and the reported tail probability is $p_{\mathrm{recoded}}=1.0$. Thus the augmented turnover model is not merely small; it is worse on the registered $R^2$ comparison and has no positive description-length support relative to either null class. The finite-row verdict is therefore a bounded negative: in the current data domain, $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ does not predict measured turnover beyond expression, translation efficiency, structural-confidence, N-end, and associated proteostasis covariates.

\paragraph{Relation to translation and protein-abundance contacts.}
This negative turnover result is not in tension with the separate protein-abundance mediation run. In the cross-organism $Q \to T \to P$ surface, twelve organisms were joined to real CDS codon counts, modeled per-protein tAI, and PAXdb protein abundance. The joined row counts were $4042$ for \emph{Bacillus subtilis}, $11661$ for \emph{Caenorhabditis elegans}, $14410$ for \emph{Danio rerio}, $13295$ for \emph{Drosophila melanogaster}, $3739$ for \emph{Escherichia coli} K12 MG1655, $12418$ for chicken, $19053$ for human, $19395$ for mouse, $4129$ for \emph{Mycobacterium smegmatis}, $13322$ for rat, $5815$ for yeast, and $1656$ for \emph{Sulfolobus solfataricus}. In that surface, every organism had rank-sufficient residualized $Q$ and $T$ design matrices, and all reported mediated fractions lay in $[0,1]$. For example, yeast had $c_{\mathrm{total}}=0.29837624994140827$, $c'_{\mathrm{direct}}=0.0151709093608293$, indirect contribution $0.28320534058057895$, and mediated fraction $0.9491551041216973$; human had $c_{\mathrm{total}}=0.04454565118019448$, $c'_{\mathrm{direct}}=0.03242643251185274$, indirect contribution $0.012119218668341744$, and mediated fraction $0.27206289160119157$. Those values support a bounded statistical protein-abundance mediation readout under modeled tAI, not a turnover result. The turnover audit asks a stricter downstream question and returns a negative answer.

\paragraph{Axis context.}
The structural-order axis audit also helps locate the negative turnover result. The nine coordinates were $K\_AAA$, $Arg\_AGR$, $Ile\_AUA$, $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$, $Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG$, $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$, $Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG$, $Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY$, and $f3\_stress$. In the five-fold structural-order run, exactly one coordinate passed the fold-level sign-flip gate: $f3\_stress$, with weighted held-out increment $0.0031744502294756994$ and sign-flip probability $0.03125$. The whole $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ signal was significant there, but the turnover run did not inherit that axis as a proteostasis predictor. The bounded reading is that an axis can be visible in one measured layer while failing to improve another layer after appropriate controls.

\paragraph{Observable execution context.}
A separate rate-slot bridge run in yeast used $3289$ genes and reached the registered conclusion `observable\_execution\_subblock': at least one observed rate proxy slot passed both effective null classes. That result says that the residual packet can touch an observed execution-rate surface under the relevant design. It does not license a turnover claim. The present turnover surface has one additional matched gene, $3290$ rather than $3289$, and its direct comparison is adverse: $R^2$ decreases by $0.005461979433706483$ when the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ turnover residual terms are added. The two records together separate execution-rate contact from proteostasis-turnover prediction.

\paragraph{Local boundary.}
This chapter therefore records a negative proteostasis-turnover conclusion, not a general biological null. It does not show that synonymous residual coordinates are biologically irrelevant, and it does not erase the bounded translation, protein-abundance, structural-order, or rate-slot contacts listed above. It only says that, on the current finite turnover rows, the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ coordinates add no positive predictive power beyond the declared proteostasis controls. No translation realization, folding pathway, physical admissibility, molecular function, causal mechanism, organismal phenotype, selection law, or universal rule follows from this audit unless a separate layer-matched contact supplies the corresponding rows and passes its own controlled statistic.
