\subsection{B*\_Q6 Turnover Residual Audit}
\label{sec:bioreality-bstar-q6-turnover-residual-audit}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite surface.}
This record tests whether the $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinates add predictive information for measured protein turnover after the already admitted expression, translation, structural-order, and degradation-baseline covariates have been placed in the model.  The finite carrier is a joined gene table with $n=3290$ genes.  Each row carries a coding-sequence residual vector in the nine-coordinate $B^*_{Q6}$ basis, matched expression and translation-efficiency information, a pLDDT-like structural confidence slot, an N-end degradation baseline, and a measured turnover readout.  The audit asks only whether the residual coordinate block improves the turnover fit beyond those controls.  It is therefore a turnover-residual test, not a direct translation test and not a protein-function or mechanism assay.

\paragraph{Model comparison.}
Let $L_2$ denote the controlled turnover model before adding the $B^*_{Q6}$ residual block, and let $L_3$ denote the corresponding model with that residual block included.  On the registered joined table, the observed coefficients of determination were
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2(L_2) &= 0.055740851626933985,\\
R^2(L_3) &= 0.050278872193227464.
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the residual block did not improve the held-out turnover description.  The registered difference was
$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta R^2 &= R^2(L_3)-R^2(L_2)\\
&= -0.005461979433706483,
\end{aligned}
$$
and the description-length comparison gave
$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta \mathrm{DL}_{\mathrm{bits}} = -13.68819707902732.
\end{aligned}
$$
The sign is the important datum: after the expression, translation-efficiency, pLDDT, and N-end slots are present, the $B^*_{Q6}$ block moves the turnover fit in the wrong direction rather than supplying a residual gain.

\paragraph{Null calibration.}
The audit used $B=200$ registered null draws.  The matched-bin null gave a right-tail value $p_{\mathrm{matched\ bin}}=0.8855721393034826$, and the recoded null gave $p_{\mathrm{recoded}}=1.0$.  The corresponding $99\%$ null reference levels for the description-length statistic were
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{null99}_{\mathrm{matched\ bin}} &= 3.51918934107672\ \mathrm{bits},\\
\mathrm{null99}_{\mathrm{recoded}} &= 1.9038288477880905\ \mathrm{bits}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The observed $\Delta \mathrm{DL}_{\mathrm{bits}}=-13.68819707902732$ is not merely below those positive reference levels; it is negative.  In this finite panel, the residual coordinates fail the turnover-support gate under both null views.  The registered runtime for the audit was $272.531$ seconds, and the run identifier was \texttt{eab3c5dee757}.

\paragraph{Relation to translation-mediated abundance.}
The turnover audit is downstream of a separate protein-abundance mediation surface, where the $B^*_{Q6}$ block was tested through a translation readout before being read against abundance.  That larger mediation surface used the same nine residual coordinates and included organism-level joined protein counts such as $5815$ proteins for \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}, $19053$ for \emph{Homo sapiens}, $19395$ for \emph{Mus musculus}, and $3739$ for \emph{Escherichia coli} K12 MG1655.  On that abundance surface, the yeast mediated fraction was reported as $M^{QTP}=0.9491551041216973$, with $R^2_{QP}=0.29837624994140827$ and direct conditioned value $R^2_{QP\mid T}=0.0151709093608293$.  Those numbers justify treating translation-mediated protein abundance as a separately tested statistical route.  They do not license a turnover claim: turnover is a different downstream readout, and the present audit explicitly checks whether the residual block survives after the turnover controls are already present.

\paragraph{Verdict.}
The finite-row verdict is negative for turnover residual realization.  The admissible statement is that, in the current joined data domain, there is no evidence that $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinates predict measured protein turnover beyond expression, translation efficiency, pLDDT-like structural confidence, N-end information, and the other controls used by the audit.  Equivalently, the packet closes as a bounded current-data refusal for turnover promotion: the residual basis may remain useful for codon-count, translation-readout, or protein-abundance mediation questions, but this run does not move it into an independent proteostasis-turnover signal.

\paragraph{Local boundary.}
No translation, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, degradation mechanism, phenotype, selection law, or universality follows from this audit.  A positive turnover claim would require a separate layer-matched turnover contact with a surviving controlled statistic after the translation step and after matched abundance and transcript controls.  A direct $Q_i\to P_k$ or $Q_i\to$ turnover association is not admissible when the $Q_i\to T_j$ route is absent, untested, or absorbed by simpler baselines.  The present numbers therefore support only a constrained negative: $B^*_{Q6}$ is not promoted to a turnover-realization coordinate on this finite surface.
