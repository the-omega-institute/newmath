\subsection{Proteostasis Turnover Residual Audit}
\label{sec:bioreality-proteostasis-turnover-residual-audit}

\paragraph{Finite surface.}
This record tests whether the nine-dimensional $B^*_{Q6}$ residual panel has any remaining predictive contact with measured protein turnover after the protein-side covariates already visible to the assay have been accounted for.  The finite row surface has $3290$ genes.  The added block is not allowed to enter the readback as a direct biological mechanism: it is only the residual coordinate block tested after expression, translation-efficiency, structure-quality, and N-end-rule style controls.  In the registered run the control model had
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{L2} &= 0.055740851626933985,\\
R^2_{L3} &= 0.050278872193227464,\\
\Delta R^2 &= R^2_{L3}-R^2_{L2}=-0.005461979433706483.
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the residual block decreases held-out explanatory power on the turnover readout rather than improving it.  The same conclusion appears in description-length form: the observed increment was $\Delta DL=-13.68819707902732$ bits, below both calibrated positive null boundaries, $3.51918934107672$ bits for the matched-bin null and $1.9038288477880905$ bits for the recoded null.  With $B=200$ effective resamples, the empirical tail probabilities were $p_{\mathrm{matchedbin}}=0.8855721393034826$ and $p_{\mathrm{recoded}}=1.0$.

\paragraph{Turnover verdict.}
The admissible conclusion is therefore negative and bounded: on this finite panel there is no evidence that $B^*_{Q6}$ predicts measured turnover beyond the expression, translation-efficiency, pLDDT-like structure-quality, N-end, and related protein-side controls used by the audit.  This is not evidence that synonymous residual geometry is biologically inert.  It says only that the present turnover contact does not support promotion of the residual coordinates into a proteostasis-turnover realization claim.  The local statistic is a refusal of the stronger claim:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta R^2 < 0,\quad \Delta DL < 0,\quad
p_{\mathrm{matchedbin}}=0.8855721393034826,\quad
p_{\mathrm{recoded}}=1.0.
\end{aligned}
$$
The audit ran to completion in $272.531$ seconds, so the result is a completed finite-row negative rather than a missing-contact deferral.

\paragraph{Relation to translation and protein-abundance contacts.}
The turnover refusal must be read together with the separate translation-mediated protein-abundance matrix.  That matrix used the same nine coordinates $K\_AAA$, $Arg\_AGR$, $Ile\_AUA$, $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$, $Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG$, $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$, $Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG$, $Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY$, and $f3\_stress$, and it joined matched protein-abundance rows across twelve organisms.  Its per-organism joined row counts included $3739$ proteins for \emph{Escherichia coli} K12 MG1655, $5815$ for \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}, $19053$ for human, $19395$ for mouse, and $11661$ for \emph{Caenorhabditis elegans}.  In that protein-abundance surface the statistical mediation fraction was positive and in the unit interval for every reported organism, for example $M^{QTP}=0.612922252793667$ in \emph{E. coli}, $0.9491551041216973$ in yeast, $0.27206289160119157$ in human, and $0.15488799907323988$ in mouse.  Those protein-abundance decompositions are not contradicted by the turnover audit; they simply occupy a different readout.  A residual can be useful for a steady-state abundance decomposition and still fail to add predictive power for turnover once turnover-specific covariates are present.

\paragraph{Mass-balance comparison.}
A separate dynamic mass-balance contact also warns against promoting residual coordinates beyond their measured surface.  On a fixed $120$-gene subsample with $718$ intervals, the mass-balance run reported $R^2_{L0,M}=0.9960628201518571$, $R^2_{L1,M,E}=0.996410628851051$, $R^2_{L2}=0.9712443377743347$, and $R^2_{L3}=0.8953999568421264$, with $\Delta R^2=-0.07584438093220836$ and $\Delta DL=-668.8046070381928$ bits.  Its null probabilities, $p_{TEbin}=0.6831683168316832$ and $p_{timeshuffle}=0.9306930693069307$, likewise do not support a stronger residual-driven closure on the dynamic protein surface.  The turnover audit is consistent with that pattern: the residual coordinates do not earn an additional proteostasis claim merely because translation and abundance contacts exist elsewhere.

\paragraph{Execution boundary.}
The observable execution decomposition supplies a positive but narrower contact: in yeast it used $3289$ genes and concluded that at least one observed rate-proxy slot passed the effective null classes.  This justifies keeping translation and rate-proxy readouts as real finite surfaces.  It does not by itself license the turnover claim tested here.  The turnover surface must still pass its own controlled increment, and it does not.  The local readback is therefore: $B^*_{Q6}$ may remain a coordinate system for codon-count residuals, modeled translation contacts, and some protein-abundance decompositions, but on the present proteostasis-turnover audit it stays below the threshold for a positive turnover-residual result.

\paragraph{Cannot-claim boundary.}
No translation mechanism, ribosome dynamics, folding pathway, physical admissibility, molecular function, organismal phenotype, selection law, or universal biological rule follows from this audit.  A direct $Q_i\to P_k$ or $Q_i\to$ turnover association is not admissible as a protein-realization claim unless the same finite row system also supplies the required translation-readout step and the downstream readout survives its own controls.  Here the turnover increment is negative after the declared controls.  The chapter therefore closes only the current data-domain question: under the available turnover contact and its finite controls, the stronger residual proteostasis claim is unsupported.
