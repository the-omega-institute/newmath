\subsection{Proteostasis turnover residual after translation and structural controls}\label{sec:bioreality-proteostasis-turnover-residual}\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite surface.} The turnover audit is a finite-row test of whether the nine $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinates retain predictive content for measured protein turnover after the obvious cross-layer covariates have already been charged to their own rows.  The evaluated rows are proteins for which coding-sequence residuals, expression, translation-efficiency information, protein-structure proxy information, N-end information, and measured turnover can be joined.  In the completed run, the eligible matrix contains $3290$ genes and uses $200$ null replicates.  The coordinate block is the same nine-dimensional residual panel used in the surrounding translation and protein-abundance audits: $K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $Leu_{CUN\,vs\,UUR}$, $Leu_{UUA\,vs\,UUG}$, $Ser_{UCR\,vs\,AGY}$, $Ser_{UCA\,vs\,UCG}$, $Thr_{ACR\,vs\,ACY}$, and $f_3$-stress.  The test is deliberately downstream: it asks whether this residual panel improves turnover prediction beyond expression, translation efficiency, pLDDT-like structural-order information, N-end terms, and the existing simpler baselines.

\paragraph{Model comparison.} Let $L_2$ denote the controlled turnover model before the $B^*_{Q6}$ block is admitted, and let $L_3$ denote the corresponding model with the residual block added.  The observed finite-row scores are
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2(L_2) &= 0.055740851626933985,\\
R^2(L_3) &= 0.050278872193227464,\\
\Delta R^2 &= R^2(L_3)-R^2(L_2) = -0.005461979433706483,\\
\Delta DL_{bits} &= -13.68819707902732.
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the added residual block does not improve the turnover fit in this joined dataset.  The direction is negative both in explained variance and in description-length accounting.  The matched-bin null gives $p=0.8855721393034826$ with a $99\%$ null threshold of $3.51918934107672$ bits, while the recoded null gives $p=1.0$ with a $99\%$ null threshold of $1.9038288477880905$ bits.  The observed $\Delta DL_{bits}$ is below both null boundaries, so the finite evidence supports a current-data refusal rather than a positive turnover residual claim.

\paragraph{Relation to upstream readouts.} This negative turnover result is not a global negative about the residual panel.  Other finite contacts place the same residual panel at different layers.  A rate-slot audit in yeast reports an observable execution subblock with $3289$ genes and a completed runtime of $2667.02$ seconds.  A dynamic RPF--protein closure audit uses $120$ genes over $718$ intervals and gives $R^2(L_0)=0.9960628201518571$, $R^2(L_1)=0.996410628851051$, $R^2(L_2)=0.9712443377743347$, $R^2(L_3)=0.8953999568421264$, with $\Delta R^2=-0.07584438093220836$, $\Delta DL_{bits}=-668.8046070381928$, $p_{TEbin}=0.6831683168316832$, and $p_{timeshuffle}=0.9306930693069307$.  A separate protein-abundance mediation run has organism-matched protein rows ranging from $1656$ in \textit{Sulfolobus solfataricus} to $19395$ in mouse, and reports positive modeled mediation fractions such as $M^{QTP}=0.9491551041216973$ in yeast, $0.612922252793667$ in \textit{E. coli} K-12 MG1655, and $0.27206289160119157$ in human.  A structural-order axis audit identifies $f_3$-stress as the sole significant held-out axis, with weighted held-out increment $0.0031744502294756994$ and sign-flip $p=0.03125$.  These contacts show that the coordinate panel can touch translation, abundance, or structural-order proxies under their own finite surfaces, but they do not transfer a positive verdict into measured turnover.

\paragraph{Local conclusion.} The permitted statement is narrow: in the present joined turnover data, $B^*_{Q6}$ does not predict measured protein turnover beyond expression, translation efficiency, pLDDT-like structural-order information, N-end terms, and the simpler baseline terms already in the model.  The row count, null design, and negative score make this a completed audit for the current finite dataset rather than an untested conjecture.  The result should be read as a bounded failure of residual turnover improvement, not as a biological impossibility theorem.

\paragraph{Layer boundary.} No claim about translation, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, mechanism, organismal phenotype, selection, or universality follows from this turnover audit by itself.  A translation claim requires its own translation-readout contact; a folding or structural claim requires a structure-level contact; a physical-admissibility or function claim requires the corresponding assay or perturbation surface; and a universal statement requires multi-context evidence beyond this finite join.  Direct associations from $Q_i$ to a downstream protein quantity are not promoted unless the intervening readout layer has been separately tested and survives its own controls.
