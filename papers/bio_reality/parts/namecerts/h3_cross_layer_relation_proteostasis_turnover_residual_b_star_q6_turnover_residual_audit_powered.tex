\subsection{Proteostasis turnover residual audit for $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$}
\label{sec:bioreality-proteostasis-turnover-residual-audit}

\paragraph{Finite question.}
This record asks whether the nine-dimensional $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ synonymous residual surface carries additional information about measured protein turnover after the main upstream and protein-state covariates have already been read.  The finite rows are genes with a measured turnover endpoint, expression information, translation-efficiency information, structural-confidence information, N-end information, and the projected $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates.  The tested coordinates are the same residual axes used in the surrounding translation and protein-abundance audits: $K\_AAA$, $Arg\_AGR$, $Ile\_AUA$, $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$, $Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG$, $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$, $Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG$, $Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY$, and $f3\_stress$.  The endpoint here is not protein abundance itself and not translation efficiency itself.  It is the residual turnover question: after the established explanatory blocks have been admitted, does the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ block still improve the finite prediction surface?

\paragraph{Nested finite models.}
Let $Y$ denote the measured turnover readout on the joined gene set.  Let $L_{2}$ denote the model level containing the admitted expression, translation, structural-confidence, and terminal-state controls, and let $L_{3}$ denote the same model with the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual block added.  On the audited panel the number of genes was $3290$, and the permutation budget was $B=200$.  The fitted comparison was
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^{2}(L_{2}) &= 0.055740851626933985,\\
R^{2}(L_{3}) &= 0.050278872193227464,\\
\Delta R^{2} &= R^{2}(L_{3})-R^{2}(L_{2})=-0.005461979433706483.
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the added synonymous residual block did not improve held-out turnover explanation on this finite surface.  It reduced the fitted explanatory score relative to the already admitted turnover controls.  The description-length comparison agrees with the same direction:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta DL &= -13.68819707902732\ \mathrm{bits},\\
\Delta DL_{0.99}^{\mathrm{matched\ bin}} &= 3.51918934107672\ \mathrm{bits},\\
\Delta DL_{0.99}^{\mathrm{recoded}} &= 1.9038288477880905\ \mathrm{bits}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The observed value is below both reported $99\%$ null reference levels.  The matched-bin permutation tail was $p=0.8855721393034826$, and the recoded null tail was $p=1.0$.  The finite-row verdict is therefore negative for additional turnover prediction by $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ after the listed controls.

\paragraph{Relation to translation and protein-abundance contacts.}
The negative turnover result is not a denial that $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ can touch lower protein-production surfaces.  A separate translation-mediated protein-abundance audit joined twelve organism panels and found positive statistical mediation fractions for modeled per-protein tAI to protein abundance, including $0.9491551041216973$ in \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}, $0.612922252793667$ in \emph{Escherichia coli} K12 MG1655, $0.6165167748739756$ in \emph{Drosophila melanogaster}, and $0.27206289160119157$ in human.  Those quantities belong to a modeled translation-to-abundance decomposition, not to turnover.  The present audit asks a stricter downstream question: after expression, translation efficiency, pLDDT-like structural confidence, N-end state, and related turnover covariates are already present, does the same residual geometry add predictive power for measured turnover?  On $3290$ genes, the answer is no.

\paragraph{Consistency with adjacent audits.}
The neighboring finite results sharpen the interpretation.  A dynamic mass-balance audit reported $R^{2}_{L0,M}=0.9960628201518571$, $R^{2}_{L1,M,E}=0.996410628851051$, $R^{2}_{L2}=0.9712443377743347$, $R^{2}_{L3}=0.8953999568421264$, and $\Delta R^{2}=-0.07584438093220836$ on $120$ genes and $718$ intervals, again giving no promotion of the residual block beyond the stronger dynamic controls.  In contrast, an observable execution audit on \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae} reported an execution-subblock verdict on $3289$ genes, and a structural-order audit identified $f3\_stress$ as the only fold-level sign-flip-passing coordinate with weighted held-out increment $0.0031744502294756994$ and sign-flip $p=0.03125$.  These facts can coexist: $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ may have bounded contact with some translation, execution, or structural-order readouts while still failing to add measurable information to turnover once stronger turnover covariates have been admitted.

\paragraph{Local boundary.}
The allowed conclusion is deliberately narrow.  On the current finite turnover panel there is no evidence that $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ predicts measured turnover beyond expression, translation-efficiency, structural-confidence, N-end, and associated controls.  This is a current-data closure statement for the turnover residual surface, not a universal biological absence theorem.  It does not imply that synonymous residuals have no translation contact, no protein-abundance contact, or no perturbational effect in other experiments.  It also does not license any stronger positive claim.  No translation mechanism, folded structure, physical admissibility, molecular function, organismal phenotype, proteostasis mechanism, adaptive law, or universal biological rule follows from these rows unless a separate layer-matched contact supplies that readout and a finite statistic survives its own controls.

\paragraph{Readback.}
The scientific readback is the bounded negative sentence
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{TurnoverGain}\bigl(B^{\ast}_{Q6}\mid L_{2}\bigr) &< 0,\\
\Delta R^{2} &=-0.005461979433706483,\\
\Delta DL &=-13.68819707902732\ \mathrm{bits}.
\end{aligned}
$$
Consequently the residual block remains outside protein-turnover realization on this dataset.  The coordinate system may still be carried forward as a controlled covariate or as a hypothesis generator for other readout layers, but the turnover chapter itself closes with a negative finite-row verdict.
