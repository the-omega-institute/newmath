\subsection{Proteostasis-Turnover Residual Audit for $B^\ast_{Q6}$}
\label{sec:bioreality-proteostasis-turnover-residual-bstar-q6}

\paragraph{Finite row surface.}
The audited object is a finite cross-layer table whose rows join $B^\ast_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates to measured turnover readouts only after the lower-layer translation and abundance covariates have already been represented.  The coordinate side has the nine residual directions
$K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $Leu_{CUN\,vs\,UUR}$, $Leu_{UUA\,vs\,UUG}$, $Ser_{UCR\,vs\,AGY}$, $Ser_{UCA\,vs\,UCG}$, $Thr_{ACR\,vs\,ACY}$, and $f_3$-stress.  The turnover audit used $3290$ genes and $B=200$ null draws.  The tested question is not whether synonymous residuals can be drawn, nor whether codon usage has any lower-layer translation contact.  It is narrower: after expression, translation efficiency, predicted structural confidence, N-end information, amino-acid composition, GC3, length, and the matched baseline controls have been used, does the residual block still improve measured turnover?

\paragraph{Nested residual comparison.}
Let $L_2$ denote the controlled turnover model before adding the $B^\ast_{Q6}$ residual block and let $L_3$ denote the corresponding model after adding that block.  The finite audit reported
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2(L_2) &= 0.055740851626933985,\\
R^2(L_3) &= 0.050278872193227464,\\
\Delta R^2 &= R^2(L_3)-R^2(L_2) = -0.005461979433706483.
\end{aligned}
$$
The description-length comparison is also adverse to promotion:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta DL &= -13.68819707902732\ \mathrm{bits},\\
q_{0.99}^{\mathrm{matched\ bin}} &= 3.51918934107672\ \mathrm{bits},\\
q_{0.99}^{\mathrm{recoded}} &= 1.9038288477880905\ \mathrm{bits}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The two null readbacks give $p_{\mathrm{matched\ bin}}=0.8855721393034826$ and $p_{\mathrm{recoded}}=1.0$.  Thus the observed increment is not merely below the empirical upper-tail thresholds; it has the wrong sign for a turnover-residual support claim.  On the finite rows available here, adding the $B^\ast_{Q6}$ block after the declared turnover controls reduces held-out explanatory fit rather than improving it.

\paragraph{Relation to lower-layer positive contacts.}
This negative turnover verdict is compatible with positive or bounded signals at lower layers.  In the multi-organism protein-abundance mediation audit, the same nine-coordinate $B^\ast_{Q6}$ block had organism-specific mediated fractions $M^{QTP}$ in the unit interval, including $0.9491551041216973$ for \textit{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}, $0.612922252793667$ for \textit{Escherichia coli} K12 MG1655, $0.6165167748739756$ for \textit{Drosophila melanogaster}, $0.27206289160119157$ for human, and $0.15488799907323988$ for mouse.  Those values describe a statistical $Q\to T\to P$ protein-abundance decomposition using modeled per-protein tAI and PAXdb abundance rows; they do not license a turnover conclusion.  Likewise, the mass-balance audit on $120$ genes and $718$ intervals reported $R^2(L_0)=0.9960628201518571$, $R^2(L_1)=0.996410628851051$, $R^2(L_2)=0.9712443377743347$, and $R^2(L_3)=0.8953999568421264$, with $\Delta R^2=-0.07584438093220836$, $p_{TEbin}=0.6831683168316832$, and $p_{timeshuffle}=0.9306930693069307$.  These neighboring finite-row audits keep translation, abundance, and turnover as separate readout surfaces rather than merging them into one biological assertion.

\paragraph{Coordinate identity and non-promotion.}
A separate structural-order axis audit identified $f_3$-stress as the only coordinate passing its fold-level sign-flip gate, with weighted held-out increment $0.0031744502294756994$ and sign-flip right-tail probability $0.03125$.  That fact is useful for naming which residual direction can dominate one structural-order contact, but it is not a turnover result.  The turnover audit therefore treats the nine-coordinate block as admissible covariates and then asks for fresh predictive gain on measured turnover.  The answer on the present data is negative: the allowed statement is that there is no evidence, in this finite surface, that $B^\ast_{Q6}$ predicts measured turnover beyond expression, translation-efficiency, pLDDT, N-end, and the other stated controls.

\paragraph{Boundary.}
The chapter therefore closes only a current-data turnover residual question.  It does not assert translation realization, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, mechanistic causation, organismal phenotype, or universality.  A direct $Q_i\to$ turnover or $Q_i\to P_k$ association is not interpretable as protein-state realization unless an appropriate translation step and a layer-matched downstream contact are both present and survive their own controls.  Protein abundance contacts, modeled tAI contacts, mRNA controls, and turnover measurements remain distinct finite readouts.  If a future dataset supplies matched translation, abundance, stability, degradation, localization, perturbation, or pulse-chase rows, the same residual block can be retested; until such rows overturn the negative increment above, the $B^\ast_{Q6}$ turnover-residual claim remains bounded at a negative current-data audit verdict.
