\subsection{Proteostasis Turnover Residual Audit for $B^*_{Q6}$}\label{sec:bioreality-proteostasis-turnover-residual-b-star-q6}\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite audit surface.} The turnover packet is a finite cross-layer audit over matched protein rows rather than a direct claim that synonymous residual geometry realizes a protein-state mechanism. The tested surface has $n=3290$ genes and compares a turnover model already carrying ordinary upstream and protein-state controls against the same model with the nine $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinates added. The residual coordinates are
$K\_AAA$, $Arg\_AGR$, $Ile\_AUA$, $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$, $Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG$, $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$, $Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG$, $Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY$, and $f3\_stress$. The baseline side includes expression, translation-efficiency information, structural-confidence information such as pLDDT, N-end information, and the ordinary sequence-composition covariates used to prevent amino-acid composition, GC3, coding length, and residual support density from being mistaken for a synonymous-coordinate effect.

\paragraph{Model comparison.} Let $L_2$ denote the controlled turnover model before adding the $B^*_{Q6}$ residual block, and let $L_3$ denote the same audit after adding that block. The finite run reports
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2(L_2) &= 0.055740851626933985,\\
R^2(L_3) &= 0.050278872193227464,\\
\Delta R^2 &= R^2(L_3)-R^2(L_2) = -0.005461979433706483.
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the added synonymous-residual block does not improve the held audit surface; it slightly lowers the fitted explanatory score in the registered comparison. The description-length difference is also unfavorable:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta DL &= -13.68819707902732\ \textrm{bits}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The sign is the important finite-row fact: the turnover audit did not find positive residual power for $B^*_{Q6}$ after the named controls were present.

\paragraph{Null calibration.} The calibration used $B=200$ null draws. Two effective null classes were recorded. The matched-bin null gave
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{\mathrm{matched\ bin}} &= 0.8855721393034826,\\
\mathrm{null99}_{\mathrm{matched\ bin}}(\Delta DL) &= 3.51918934107672\ \textrm{bits},
\end{aligned}
$$
and the recoded null gave
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{\mathrm{recoded}} &= 1.0,\\
\mathrm{null99}_{\mathrm{recoded}}(\Delta DL) &= 1.9038288477880905\ \textrm{bits}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The observed value $-13.68819707902732$ bits is not merely below the positive null thresholds; it is on the non-supporting side of the comparison. The finite conclusion is therefore an honest negative for turnover prediction: in the current joined data, there is no evidence that the $B^*_{Q6}$ residual block predicts measured protein turnover beyond expression, translation-efficiency, pLDDT, N-end, and related baseline controls.

\paragraph{Relation to nearby protein readouts.} This negative turnover result is narrower than a general denial of all protein-layer contact. A separate matched protein-abundance mediation run reported positive statistical mediation fractions for the same nine-dimensional residual block when modeled per-protein tAI was used as the intermediate readout. For example, the reported $M^{QTP}$ fractions were $0.9491551041216973$ in \textit{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}, $0.612922252793667$ in \textit{Escherichia coli} K-12 MG1655, $0.6165167748739756$ in \textit{Drosophila melanogaster}, $0.27206289160119157$ in human, and $0.15488799907323988$ in mouse. Those rows concern abundance mediation through a modeled translation-readout surface, not protein turnover. The turnover audit asks a stricter downstream question: after the ordinary turnover covariates and protein-state controls are present, does the synonymous residual block still buy predictive power for measured turnover? The finite answer is no.

\paragraph{Consistency with other bounded signals.} The broader finite record also contains bounded positive contacts at other layers. A structural-order audit identified $f3\_stress$ as the dominant axis, with weighted held-out increment $0.0031744502294756994$ and sign-flip $p=0.03125$. A mass-balance audit, by contrast, showed a decline from $R^2=0.9712443377743347$ at $L_2$ to $R^2=0.8953999568421264$ at $L_3$, with $\Delta R^2=-0.07584438093220836$ and $\Delta DL=-668.8046070381928$ bits. These neighboring facts keep the turnover chapter from overreading either direction. The residual system can leave detectable bounded traces in some controlled readouts, while the turnover audit specifically closes against a positive turnover-residual claim in the current data.

\paragraph{Local readback.} The admissible readback is only this: on the finite $3290$-gene turnover surface, adding the nine $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinates after expression, translation-efficiency, pLDDT, N-end, and baseline sequence controls gives $\Delta R^2=-0.005461979433706483$ and $\Delta DL=-13.68819707902732$ bits, with null-calibrated $p$-values $0.8855721393034826$ and $1.0$. The packet therefore supports a current-data refusal of residual turnover prediction, not a new turnover mechanism.

\paragraph{Boundary.} No translation realization, folding consequence, physical admissibility statement, molecular function, causal mechanism, phenotype, selection law, or universal biological rule follows from this audit. A direct $Q_i\to P_k$ or $Q_i\to$ turnover association is not enough unless the same coordinate first survives a controlled translation-readout step and the downstream layer has its own matched measurement surface. Here the turnover surface itself is negative after controls, so the only warranted statement is bounded non-support for an extra $B^*_{Q6}$ turnover residual effect in the present finite rows.
