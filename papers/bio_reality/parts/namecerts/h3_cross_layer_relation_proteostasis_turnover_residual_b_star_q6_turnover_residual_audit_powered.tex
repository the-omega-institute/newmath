\subsection{Proteostasis turnover residual audit for $B^*_{Q6}$}
\label{sec:biorg-proteostasis-turnover-residual-b-star-q6-audit}

\paragraph{Finite surface.}
The audit surface is the finite joined panel on which each coding sequence has a $B^*_{Q6}$ residual vector, expression and translation covariates, structural-order covariates when available, and a measured protein-turnover readout.  The residual coordinates are the nine computed axes
$K\_AAA$, $Arg\_AGR$, $Ile\_AUA$, $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$, $Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG$, $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$, $Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG$, $Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY$, and $f3\_stress$.  The turnover audit does not ask whether these coordinates have any previously observed association with abundance, translation, or structural order; it asks the narrower residual question: after the known lower-layer explanatory slots have already been admitted, is there remaining predictive mass for measured turnover that is specifically attributable to $B^*_{Q6}$?

\paragraph{Registered comparison.}
The fitted comparison used $n=3290$ genes and $B=200$ null resamples.  Let $L_2$ denote the model containing the admitted expression, translation-efficiency, pLDDT, N-end, and related turnover controls, and let $L_3$ denote the same model after adding the $B^*_{Q6}$ residual block.  The observed held-out coefficients of determination were
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2(L_2) &= 0.055740851626933985,\\
R^2(L_3) &= 0.050278872193227464,\\
\Delta R^2 &= R^2(L_3)-R^2(L_2) = -0.005461979433706483.
\end{aligned}
$$
The description-length comparison also moved in the wrong direction for a positive residual claim:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta DL &= -13.68819707902732\ \text{bits},\\
q_{0.99}^{\mathrm{matched\ bin}}(\Delta DL) &= 3.51918934107672\ \text{bits},\\
q_{0.99}^{\mathrm{recoded}}(\Delta DL) &= 1.9038288477880905\ \text{bits}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The two effective-null checks gave $p_{\mathrm{matched\ bin}}=0.8855721393034826$ and $p_{\mathrm{recoded}}=1.0$.  Thus the added residual block neither improves the held-out fit nor clears either null family.  In this finite panel, the honest conclusion is not a small positive turnover effect; it is the absence of evidence that $B^*_{Q6}$ predicts measured turnover beyond the admitted expression, translation, pLDDT, N-end, and associated controls.

\paragraph{Relation to the translation and protein-abundance rows.}
This negative turnover result is not a global erasure of all lower-layer contact.  The translation-mediated protein-abundance matrix had already produced positive bounded mediation fractions on a larger matched protein-abundance surface: for example $M^{QTP}=0.9491551041216973$ in \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}, $0.612922252793667$ in \emph{Escherichia coli} K12 MG1655, $0.6165167748739756$ in \emph{Drosophila melanogaster}, and $0.27206289160119157$ in human, with organism-specific joined row counts including $5815$ yeast proteins, $3739$ \emph{E. coli} proteins, $13295$ fly proteins, and $19053$ human proteins.  Those rows justify a bounded statistical mediation readback through modeled translation adaptation and matched abundance; they do not by themselves certify turnover.  The turnover audit is deliberately placed after such lower-layer results, so that a positive finding would have required extra residual predictive power after the already admitted explanatory routes were conditioned away.

\paragraph{Checks against nearby biological interpretations.}
Several linked finite rows explain why the current turnover verdict must be read narrowly.  The dynamic mass-balance run used $120$ genes and $718$ intervals, with $R^2(L_0)=0.9960628201518571$, $R^2(L_1)=0.996410628851051$, $R^2(L_2)=0.9712443377743347$, and $R^2(L_3)=0.8953999568421264$; its reported $\Delta R^2=-0.07584438093220836$, $\Delta DL=-668.8046070381928$ bits, $p_{\mathrm{TEbin}}=0.6831683168316832$, and $p_{\mathrm{timeshuffle}}=0.9306930693069307$ again do not support an added $B^*_{Q6}$ turnover-like gain.  The observable-execution decomposition did find an execution subblock in yeast on $3289$ genes, but that is a rate-proxy slot, not a turnover-residual proof.  The structural-order identity row found a dominant $f3\_stress$ coordinate with weighted held-out increment $0.0031744502294756994$ and sign-flip $p=0.03125$; this is a structural-order readout, not a degradation-rate or proteostasis mechanism.  Likewise, the yeast structure-mediation abundance row had $n=4759$, $R^2_{QP}=0.2715520808117803$, $R^2_{QP\mid S}=0.2706601505000995$, $R^2_{QP\mid T,M}=0.13773967696311287$, and $R^2_{QP\mid T,M,S}=0.13759011208318542$, with a structure-only fraction of $0.003284564452662019$ and a structure-incremental fraction of $0.0005507778820193332$.  These neighboring rows show that the coordinate system can touch translation, abundance, and structural-order summaries under bounded statistical readings, while the turnover residual test itself remains negative.

\paragraph{Local decision.}
The finite decision rule is therefore
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{TurnoverResidual}(B^*_{Q6}) &= \mathbf{1}\{\Delta R^2>0\ \text{and}\ \Delta DL\ \text{exceeds the effective-null boundary}\},\\
&=0\quad\text{on the registered turnover panel.}
\end{aligned}
$$
The chapter records a closed current-data verdict: no evidence that $B^*_{Q6}$ contributes additional measured-turnover prediction once expression, translation-efficiency, pLDDT, N-end, and related controls are present.  This is a bounded negative result, not a claim that synonymous residuals are biologically irrelevant in every layer.

\paragraph{Boundary of interpretation.}
The result licenses only the current-data statement above.  It does not establish translation realization, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, degradation mechanism, organismal phenotype, adaptation, causation, or universality.  Any such stronger reading would need its own layer-matched contact: measured translation rows for translation, structural or stability assays for folding and physical state, perturbation or rescue assays for mechanism, and phenotype or function assays for higher biological consequence.  A direct $Q_i\to P_k$ or $Q_i\to$ turnover association is not admissible as a protein-realization claim unless a surviving $Q_i\to T_j$ translation step and the required downstream contact are both present under the declared controls.
