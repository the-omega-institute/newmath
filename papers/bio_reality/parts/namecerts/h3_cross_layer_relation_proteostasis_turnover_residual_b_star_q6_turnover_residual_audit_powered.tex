\subsection{Proteostasis Turnover Residual Audit for $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$}\label{sec:bioreality-q6-proteostasis-turnover-residual-audit}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite question.}
The proteostasis-turnover audit asks whether the nine-dimensional $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ synonymous residual panel contributes measurable information about protein turnover after the already attached expression, translation-efficiency, structural-confidence, and N-end-rule style covariates have been taken into account. The finite surface is a matched gene table with $n=3290$ rows. Each row carries a coding-sequence residual vector $q$, expression and translation readouts, a protein-structure-confidence proxy, N-terminal degradation features, and a measured turnover response. The test is intentionally downstream of the translation-mediated protein-abundance surface: a direct residual-to-turnover association is not treated as a proteostasis statement unless the translation and abundance layers have already been separately bounded.

\paragraph{Model comparison.}
Let $Y$ denote the measured turnover readout on the joined rows. Let $L_2$ be the control model using the non-$B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ covariate block: expression, translation efficiency, pLDDT-style structural-confidence information, N-end information, and the associated baseline controls. Let $L_3$ be the corresponding model after adding the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates. The observed held-out coefficients of determination were
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2(L_2) &= 0.055740851626933985,\\
R^2(L_3) &= 0.050278872193227464,\\
\Delta R^2 &= R^2(L_3)-R^2(L_2) = -0.005461979433706483.
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the residual block decreased held-out explanatory power on this finite panel. The description-length comparison gives the same direction:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta \mathrm{DL} &= -13.68819707902732\ \mathrm{bits}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The audit used $B=200$ null draws. The matched-bin null gave $p=0.8855721393034826$ and a $99\%$ reference value of $3.51918934107672$ bits. The recoded null gave $p=1.0$ and a $99\%$ reference value of $1.9038288477880905$ bits. Since the observed description-length increment is negative and lies below both positive reference cutoffs, the finite-row verdict is not a weak positive hidden under a conservative threshold; it is a direct failure of incremental turnover prediction on the available joined data.

\paragraph{Readout boundary.}
The allowed reading is narrow. On this dataset there is no evidence that $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ predicts measured protein turnover beyond expression, translation efficiency, pLDDT-style information, N-end features, and the declared baselines. The current finite contact therefore closes only the current-data turnover question: it says that the audited residual block does not improve the measured turnover fit on the joined panel. It does not say that synonymous residual geometry is biologically irrelevant in general, and it does not rule out other turnover assays, perturbation designs, stress conditions, organism panels, pulse-chase protocols, localization-specific turnover, or client-specific degradation routes.

\paragraph{Relation to neighboring finite contacts.}
The negative turnover result is compatible with the other bounded contacts in the same finite program. The translation-mediated protein-abundance audit used a twelve-organism protein-abundance surface with organism-specific joined row counts including $5815$ Saccharomyces cerevisiae proteins, $3739$ Escherichia coli K12 MG1655 proteins, $19053$ Homo sapiens proteins, and $19395$ Mus musculus proteins. On that surface the yeast mediation fraction was $M^{QTP}=0.9491551041216973$, with $c=0.29837624994140827$, $c'=0.0151709093608293$, and indirect component $0.28320534058057895$ under modeled per-protein tAI conditioning. Those numbers are protein-abundance mediation statistics, not turnover statistics. Likewise, the structural-order axis audit identified $f_3$-stress as the dominant coordinate, with weighted held-out increment $0.0031744502294756994$ and sign-flip $p=0.03125$; that is an order-axis identity result, not a degradation-rate result. The observable-execution decomposition reported $n=3289$ yeast genes and a bounded positive rate-proxy subblock, while the mass-balance dynamic audit on $n=120$ genes and $718$ intervals found $\Delta R^2=-0.07584438093220836$ and $\Delta \mathrm{DL}=-668.8046070381928$ bits for the tested closure step. The chaperone-client absorption audit on $n=3290$ rows also refused promotion, with held-out $\Delta R^2=-0.025094043993987934$ and verdict composition artifact. These neighboring contacts show that the same coordinate family can have bounded contacts at translation, abundance, structural-order, and rate-proxy layers while still failing the specific proteostasis-turnover increment tested here.

\paragraph{Local cannot-claim boundary.}
This chapter makes no claim of translation realization from turnover alone. It also makes no claim of folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, degradation mechanism, organismal phenotype, selection, or universality. Any such claim would require its own layer-matched contact and its own finite statistic. In particular, a residual coordinate $q_i$ may not be read as a turnover mechanism merely because it appears in a codon residual basis, because it participates in a modeled translation-weight surface, or because a different protein-abundance mediation table is positive. The present result is only the audited statement that adding the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates to the specified turnover model does not improve held-out turnover prediction on $3290$ matched genes.

\paragraph{Finite verdict.}
For the current joined turnover panel, the promoted proteostasis interpretation is refused. The supported statement is
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2(L_3) &< R^2(L_2),\\
\Delta R^2 &=-0.005461979433706483,\\
\Delta \mathrm{DL} &=-13.68819707902732\ \mathrm{bits},\\
p_{\mathrm{matched\ bin}}&=0.8855721393034826,\\
p_{\mathrm{recoded}}&=1.0.
\end{aligned}
$$
Therefore $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ remains outside protein-turnover realization on this audited finite surface. Its status here is a controlled negative result: current data support no incremental turnover prediction after the expression, translation-efficiency, structural-confidence, N-end, and baseline covariates are already present.
