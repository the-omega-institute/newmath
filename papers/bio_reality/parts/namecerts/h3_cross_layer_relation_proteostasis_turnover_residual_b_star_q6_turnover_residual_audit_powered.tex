\subsection{Proteostasis Turnover Residual Audit for $B^*_{Q6}$}\label{subsec:bioreality-proteostasis-turnover-residual-b-star-q6}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite audit surface.}
The turnover audit asks whether the nine-dimensional $B^*_{Q6}$ synonymous residual packet carries any remaining predictive content for measured protein turnover after the ordinary expression and translation-adjacent covariates have already been admitted. The finite row surface is a matched gene panel with $n=3290$ genes. Each row contains a coding-sequence residual vector $q_i$ drawn from the $B^*_{Q6}$ basis, expression and translation-efficiency controls, a structural-confidence covariate through pLDDT, an N-end-rule-style boundary covariate, and the measured turnover readout. The point of the test is deliberately narrow: it is not a search for a new folding, function, or phenotype law. It is a residual audit of whether $B^*_{Q6}$ still improves the turnover fit once the already admitted protein-expression, translation, and stability-proxy slots have taken their share.

\paragraph{Model comparison.}
The registered comparison used two nested finite designs. Let $L_2$ denote the baseline turnover model after the declared controls and let $L_3$ denote the same design after adjoining the $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinates. The observed held-row coefficients of determination were
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2(L_2) &= 0.055740851626933985,\\
R^2(L_3) &= 0.050278872193227464,\\
\Delta R^2 &= R^2(L_3)-R^2(L_2) = -0.005461979433706483.
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the residual packet did not improve the turnover model. The added coordinates slightly reduced predictive performance on the evaluated surface, so the finite readback is not a weak positive hidden below a nominal threshold; it has the wrong sign for a turnover-realization claim.

\paragraph{Description-length and null calibration.}
The same conclusion appears in the description-length audit. The observed description-length change was
$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta \mathrm{DL}_{\mathrm{obs}} &= -13.68819707902732\ \mathrm{bits}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The permutation and recoding controls did not rescue the signal. With $B=200$ audit draws, the matched-bin comparison gave $p_{\mathrm{matched\ bin}}=0.8855721393034826$, and the recoded comparison gave $p_{\mathrm{recoded}}=1.0$. Their $99\%$ null reference values were positive,
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{null}_{99}^{\mathrm{matched\ bin}} &= 3.51918934107672\ \mathrm{bits},\\
\mathrm{null}_{99}^{\mathrm{recoded}} &= 1.9038288477880905\ \mathrm{bits}.
\end{aligned}
$$
A positive turnover-residual claim would require the observed finite improvement to clear these null surfaces in the favorable direction. Instead, the observed value is negative and lies below both positive reference cutoffs. The calibrated readback is therefore an honest negative: no evidence is present that $B^*_{Q6}$ predicts measured turnover beyond expression, translation efficiency, pLDDT, N-end boundary information, and the other declared controls in the current finite panel.

\paragraph{Relation to the protein-abundance route.}
This result does not erase the separate protein-abundance mediation surface. In the organism-matched abundance experiment, the $B^*_{Q6}$ packet was evaluated as a nine-coordinate $Q$ surface through modeled translation readouts and matched protein abundance, with rank-sufficient organism panels such as $n=5815$ proteins in \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}, $n=19053$ in human, and $n=19395$ in mouse. That route was explicitly a $Q \to T \to P$ abundance decomposition, not a turnover claim. The turnover audit is stricter in a different direction: after expression, translation-efficiency, pLDDT, and N-end-style slots are already present, it asks whether $Q$ adds residual information for turnover itself. The answer on the audited rows is no.

\paragraph{Mass-balance context.}
A nearby dynamic mass-balance audit also warns against promoting translation-adjacent fits into turnover or proteostasis conclusions without the exact row-level statistic. In that panel the fixed subsample used $n=120$ genes and $718$ intervals; the mass-only baseline had $R^2=0.9960628201518571$, adding estimated expression gave $R^2=0.996410628851051$, and the later residual layers fell to $R^2=0.9712443377743347$ and $R^2=0.8953999568421264$, with $\Delta R^2=-0.07584438093220836$, $\Delta\mathrm{DL}=-668.8046070381928$ bits, $p_{\mathrm{TEbin}}=0.6831683168316832$, and $p_{\mathrm{timeshuffle}}=0.9306930693069307$. That supporting audit is consistent with the present turnover result: protein-level dynamics require their own finite contact and do not follow from a codon residual coordinate merely because translation or abundance surfaces can be named.

\paragraph{Allowed statement.}
The local conclusion is a bounded closure statement for the present data: $B^*_{Q6}$ supplies no supported residual predictor of measured protein turnover once expression, translation efficiency, pLDDT, N-end boundary information, and the declared controls are included. The strongest admissible reading is therefore negative and domain-scoped. The residual coordinates may remain useful for codon-count geometry, modeled translation readouts, or separate protein-abundance mediation audits, but this finite turnover surface does not promote them into a proteostasis-turnover realization.

\paragraph{Local no-promotion boundary.}
No translation mechanism, ribosome dynamics, folding pathway, physical admissibility, molecular function, organismal phenotype, evolutionary law, or universal biological principle follows from this audit. A positive statement at any one of those layers would require a separate layer-matched contact and its own controlled finite statistic. In particular, a direct $Q_i\to P_k$ or $Q_i\to$ turnover association is not admissible as protein realization unless the appropriate translation-readout step survives first, and the present turnover audit gives no residual improvement after the declared turnover controls.
