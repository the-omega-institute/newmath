\subsection{Proteostasis Turnover Residual Audit for $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$}\label{subsec:bioreality-proteostasis-turnover-residual-bstar-q6}\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite row surface.}
This record tests whether the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates add measurable predictive content for protein turnover after the already attached expression, translation-efficiency, structural-confidence, and terminal-degron controls have been accounted for.  The finite carrier is the matched gene table used by the turnover audit: $n=3290$ genes, with residual coordinates evaluated only after the control layer containing expression, TE, pLDDT, N-end features, and the declared baseline variables has been fitted.  The relevant comparison is not a direct assertion that a synonymous coordinate reaches proteostasis.  It is a residual audit asking whether adding the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ block improves the turnover model beyond the prior controlled layer.

\paragraph{Controlled statistic.}
Let $L_2$ denote the controlled turnover model before the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual block is allowed to enter, and let $L_3$ denote the model after that block is included.  The observed finite-row values are
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2(L_2) &= 0.055740851626933985,\\
R^2(L_3) &= 0.050278872193227464,\\
\Delta R^2 &= R^2(L_3)-R^2(L_2) = -0.005461979433706483,\\
\Delta \mathrm{DL} &= -13.68819707902732\ \mathrm{bits}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The audit used $B=200$ realized null draws.  Against the matched-bin null the observed result has $p=0.8855721393034826$, and against the recoded null it has $p=1.0$.  The corresponding $99\%$ null reference levels for description-length improvement are $3.51918934107672$ bits for the matched-bin null and $1.9038288477880905$ bits for the recoded null.  Thus the observed residual block is not merely below the positive reference surface; it moves the held-out turnover fit in the wrong direction on the recorded $R^2$ scale.

\paragraph{Relation to the translation and protein-abundance records.}
The negative turnover verdict is local to measured turnover.  It does not erase the earlier bounded protein-abundance mediation surface, where the same nine-coordinate $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ block was evaluated through a modeled translation readout and matched protein abundance.  In that separate finite matrix, the joint mediation fraction $M^{QTP}$ was positive and in the unit interval for all twelve organisms.  Examples include $M^{QTP}=0.9491551041216973$ for \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}, $0.612922252793667$ for \emph{Escherichia coli} K12 MG1655, $0.4872268357227724$ for \emph{Caenorhabditis elegans}, $0.27206289160119157$ for \emph{Homo sapiens}, and $0.15488799907323988$ for \emph{Mus musculus}.  Those values concern a modeled $q_i\to T_j\to P_k$ protein-abundance decomposition, not a turnover effect.  The present audit asks whether the residual coordinate block continues into proteostasis turnover after expression, TE, pLDDT, N-end, and related controls are present; the answer in the recorded finite rows is no.

\paragraph{Compatibility with other bounded contacts.}
The turnover audit also sits below a broader execution-readout landscape.  A separate rate-slot bridge over \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae} reported $n=3289$ genes and concluded that at least one observed rate-proxy slot passed both effective null classes.  A dynamic mass-balance contact used $n=120$ genes over $718$ intervals and found $R^2(L_0)=0.9960628201518571$, $R^2(L_1)=0.996410628851051$, $R^2(L_2)=0.9712443377743347$, and $R^2(L_3)=0.8953999568421264$, with $\Delta R^2=-0.07584438093220836$, $\Delta \mathrm{DL}=-668.8046070381928$ bits, $p_{\mathrm{TEbin}}=0.6831683168316832$, and $p_{\mathrm{timeshuffle}}=0.9306930693069307$.  These neighboring rows are consistent with the same rule: a rate or abundance contact can be reported at its own measured layer, but a proteostasis claim must be earned by a layer-matched turnover statistic.  The present turnover statistic does not earn that promotion.

\paragraph{Admissible conclusion.}
The admissible readback is therefore a bounded negative result.  In the current data, there is no evidence that $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ improves prediction of measured turnover beyond expression, translation-efficiency, pLDDT, N-end, and the associated baseline controls.  The coordinate block remains usable for codon-residual, modeled translation-readout, or protein-abundance mediation questions when those surfaces are explicitly measured or bounded, but this audit closes the turnover branch negatively for the recorded table.

\paragraph{Local no-promotion rule.}
No direct $q_i\to$ turnover interpretation is licensed from the residual coordinates alone.  A translation claim, a folding claim, a physical-admissibility claim, a molecular-function claim, a mechanistic claim, or a universal biological law would require its own matching measured contact and its own controlled statistic.  In particular, the high null probabilities $p=0.8855721393034826$ and $p=1.0$, together with $\Delta R^2=-0.005461979433706483$, block any statement that the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual block predicts measured proteostasis turnover in the present finite-row surface.
