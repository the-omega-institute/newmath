\subsection{Proteostasis turnover residual audit for $B^*_{Q6}$}\label{sec:bioreality-proteostasis-turnover-residual-bstar-q6}\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite audit surface.}
This record tests whether the nine-dimensional $B^*_{Q6}$ synonymous-residual surface carries information about measured protein turnover after the obvious upstream and structural covariates have already been admitted. The row set has $3290$ genes. The tested increment compares a control layer $L_2$ against a larger layer $L_3$ that adds the $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinates after expression, translation-efficiency information, predicted structural confidence, $N$-end information, and the declared baseline controls have been used. The coordinate list is the same residual basis used in the translation and protein-abundance audits: $K\_AAA$, $Arg\_AGR$, $Ile\_AUA$, $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$, $Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG$, $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$, $Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG$, $Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY$, and $f3\_stress$. The audit therefore asks a narrow question: once turnover has been given expression, translation, structural-order proxies, and elementary sequence baselines, do the residual codon coordinates still improve the finite turnover fit?

\paragraph{Observed residual comparison.}
The answer in the registered finite run is negative. The control fit has
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2(L_2) &= 0.055740851626933985,
\end{aligned}
$$
whereas the larger model with the $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinates has
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2(L_3) &= 0.050278872193227464.
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the measured increment is not a gain but a loss,
$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta R^2 &= R^2(L_3)-R^2(L_2)\\
&= -0.005461979433706483.
\end{aligned}
$$
The description-length comparison points the same way:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta DL &= -13.68819707902732\ \text{bits}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The audit used $B=200$ null draws. The matched-bin null has $p=0.8855721393034826$ and a $99\%$ reference value of $3.51918934107672$ bits, while the recoded null has $p=1.0$ and a $99\%$ reference value of $1.9038288477880905$ bits. Since the observed description-length increment is negative and sits below both positive null-reference thresholds, the finite rows do not support a turnover residual effect for these coordinates.

\paragraph{Relation to upstream positive surfaces.}
The negative turnover result is not a denial that the same coordinate family can touch earlier readout layers. In the translation-mediated protein-abundance run, twelve organism-specific joined surfaces were evaluated with positive bounded mediation fractions: for example, $Saccharomyces\ cerevisiae$ had $M^{QTP}=0.9491551041216973$ with $c=0.29837624994140827$, $c'=0.0151709093608293$, and indirect component $0.28320534058057895$; $Escherichia\ coli$ K-12 MG1655 had $M^{QTP}=0.612922252793667$ with $c=0.10767526677985896$, $c'=0.04167869969498872$, and indirect component $0.06599656708487024$; $Homo\ sapiens$ had $M^{QTP}=0.27206289160119157$ with $c=0.04454565118019448$, $c'=0.03242643251185274$, and indirect component $0.012119218668341744$. Those values concern a modeled translation-readout and protein-abundance decomposition, not a degradation-rate or turnover readout. They therefore motivate the turnover audit but do not substitute for it.

\paragraph{Coordinate identity and mass-balance checks.}
The structural-order audit also identifies a particular coordinate direction without giving it a turnover claim. In that run, $f3\_stress$ was the only coordinate passing the fold-level sign-flip gate, with weighted held-out increment $0.0031744502294756994$ and sign-flip $p=0.03125$; the remaining coordinates had larger right-tail probabilities or weaker increments. The mass-balance run likewise gives a separate negative warning for a dynamic closure interpretation: on $120$ genes and $718$ intervals, the comparison reported $R^2(L_0)=0.9960628201518571$, $R^2(L_1)=0.996410628851051$, $R^2(L_2)=0.9712443377743347$, $R^2(L_3)=0.8953999568421264$, $\Delta R^2=-0.07584438093220836$, $\Delta DL=-668.8046070381928$ bits, $p_{TEbin}=0.6831683168316832$, and $p_{timeshuffle}=0.9306930693069307$. These two neighboring audits sharpen the local interpretation: the same residual coordinates may have bounded contacts with translation, abundance, or structural-order proxies, but the present turnover table does not preserve a positive residual increment after the relevant controls.

\paragraph{Local verdict.}
The permitted statement is therefore deliberately small. In the current finite data domain, there is no evidence that $B^*_{Q6}$ predicts measured protein turnover beyond expression, translation-efficiency, predicted structural confidence, $N$-end information, and the listed baseline covariates. Equivalently, the turnover residual contact closes negatively for this row set: the larger model is worse by $0.005461979433706483$ in $R^2$ and worse by $13.68819707902732$ description-length bits. The result should be read as a bounded failure of this residual-to-turnover increment, not as a universal biological theorem and not as a claim that synonymous residuals are irrelevant at all other biological layers.

\paragraph{Layer boundary.}
No claim about translation, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, mechanism, phenotype, selection, or universality follows from this turnover audit unless another layer-matched contact supplies the corresponding measurement and statistic. A direct $Q_i\to P_k$ or $Q_i\to$ turnover association is not admissible as protein realization when the intervening translation readout is absent, null, or not tested under the declared controls. The present record is only a controlled finite-row refusal for the proteostasis-turnover increment.
