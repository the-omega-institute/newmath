\subsection{Proteostasis turnover residual audit for $B^*_{Q6}$}
\label{sec:b-star-q6-turnover-residual-audit}

\paragraph{Finite row surface.}
The turnover audit asks whether the residual coordinates of $B^*_{Q6}$ pass beyond translation and steady-state abundance into a measured proteostasis readout.  The row object is a finite joined table whose rows are proteins with a coding sequence, residual codon-coordinate values, expression and translation covariates, structural-confidence covariates, N-end covariates, and an experimentally reported turnover or degradation-rate readout.  The relevant coordinate family is the same nine-dimensional residual list used in the translation and abundance analyses:
$K\_AAA$, $Arg\_AGR$, $Ile\_AUA$, $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$, $Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG$, $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$, $Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG$, $Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY$, and $f3\_stress$.
The audit does not treat these coordinates as a direct proteostasis mechanism.  They are only tested as additional finite predictors after the lower-layer controls have already been charged to the model.

\paragraph{Model comparison.}
The powered turnover run used $n=3290$ joined genes and $B=200$ null resamples.  Let $L_2$ denote the controlled turnover model containing the non-$B^*_{Q6}$ explanatory block, including expression, translation-efficiency, pLDDT, and N-end information.  Let $L_3$ denote the same controlled model after adding the $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinates.  The registered comparison returned
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2(L_2) &= 0.055740851626933985,\\
R^2(L_3) &= 0.050278872193227464,\\
\Delta R^2 &= R^2(L_3)-R^2(L_2) = -0.005461979433706483.
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the residual coordinate block did not improve held-out turnover prediction on this finite table.  The description-length contrast agreed with the held-out $R^2$ direction:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta DL &= -13.68819707902732\ \text{bits}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The negative sign records that the proposed residual-coordinate extension is not rewarded by the current turnover readout once the lower-layer and proteostasis-adjacent covariates are present.

\paragraph{Null calibration.}
The audit used two effective null classes.  The matched-bin null gave
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{\mathrm{matched\ bin}} &= 0.8855721393034826,\\
Q_{0.99}^{\mathrm{matched\ bin}}(\Delta DL) &= 3.51918934107672\ \text{bits}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The recoded null gave
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{\mathrm{recoded}} &= 1.0,\\
Q_{0.99}^{\mathrm{recoded}}(\Delta DL) &= 1.9038288477880905\ \text{bits}.
\end{aligned}
$$
Since the observed $\Delta DL=-13.68819707902732$ lies below both positive null cutoffs and the two null $p$-values are large, the finite-row result is a negative proteostasis-turnover audit rather than a weak positive.  The run completed in $272.531$ seconds, so the negative conclusion is a completed registered computation, not a missing-data placeholder.

\paragraph{Relation to translation and abundance rows.}
The turnover result is deliberately narrower than the translation-mediated protein-abundance result.  In the abundance mediation run, twelve organisms had joined CDS/protein rows, with examples including $5815$ proteins in \textit{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}, $19053$ in human, $19395$ in mouse, $3739$ in \textit{Escherichia coli} K12 MG1655, and $1656$ in \textit{Sulfolobus solfataricus}.  That earlier surface computed positive statistical mediation fractions for modeled tAI and protein abundance, for example $M^{QTP}=0.9491551041216973$ in yeast, $0.612922252793667$ in \textit{E. coli}, $0.27206289160119157$ in human, and $0.01850668564589326$ in \textit{S. solfataricus}.  Those values only license a bounded statistical statement about a codon-residual, modeled-translation, and abundance table.  They do not license a turnover conclusion.  The present audit asks the next question and answers it negatively on the available measured turnover rows.

\paragraph{Execution and structural context.}
The broader execution record contains a positive bounded rate-slot result in yeast, where $3289$ genes were sufficient for at least one observed rate-proxy slot to pass both effective null classes.  The structural-order audit also found that the dominant structural-order coordinate was $f3\_stress$, with weighted held-out increment $0.0031744502294756994$ and sign-flip right-tail probability $0.03125$.  These neighboring facts explain why a turnover audit is scientifically natural: the same residual basis has finite contacts with translation-rate and structural-order summaries.  They do not, however, force a turnover signal.  The current $3290$-gene turnover table shows that adding the nine residual coordinates after expression, translation-efficiency, pLDDT, and N-end controls worsens the registered predictive score.

\paragraph{Local verdict.}
The local conclusion is therefore a bounded negative statement:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta R^2 &< 0,\\
\Delta DL &< 0,\\
p_{\mathrm{matched\ bin}} &\approx 0.886,\\
p_{\mathrm{recoded}} &= 1.0.
\end{aligned}
$$
On the present finite rows, there is no evidence that $B^*_{Q6}$ predicts measured turnover beyond expression, translation-efficiency, pLDDT, N-end information, and the other registered controls.  The correct readback is domain-bounded closure for this data surface: $B^*_{Q6}$ may remain relevant to codon residuals, modeled translation readouts, abundance mediation, or specific perturbation settings, but this run does not move it into a proteostasis-turnover predictor.

\paragraph{Boundary of interpretation.}
No translation mechanism, folding pathway, physical admissibility claim, molecular function claim, organism-level phenotype claim, or universal biological rule follows from this turnover audit.  A direct coordinate-to-turnover association would be inadmissible unless the translation-readout step first survived on the same controlled row surface and the downstream turnover readout then improved after the declared covariates.  Here the measured turnover comparison fails in the opposite direction.  Any stronger proteostasis claim would require a separate layer-matched contact, such as pulse-chase degradation rates, perturbation-rescue data, localization or stability assays, or condition-matched protein-state measurements, joined to the same coordinate and translation rows under rank-sufficient controls.
