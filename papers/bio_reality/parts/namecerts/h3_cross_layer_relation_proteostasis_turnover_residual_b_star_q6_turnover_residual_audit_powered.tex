\subsection{Proteostasis Turnover Residual Audit for the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ Coordinates}\label{subsec:bioreality-proteostasis-turnover-residual-b-star-q6}\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite surface.}  The turnover audit asks whether the nine-dimensional $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual surface still carries information about measured protein turnover after the ordinary expression, translation, structure-confidence, and degradation-tag controls have already been given first access to the data.  The finite table has one row per protein with a joined coding-sequence residual vector, an expression layer, a translation-efficiency layer, a structure-confidence proxy through pLDDT, an N-end rule or terminal-residue control, and a measured turnover readout.  The coordinate set used by the linked $B^{*}_{Q6}$ construction is
$$
\begin{aligned}
Q=\{&K\_AAA,\ Arg\_AGR,\ Ile\_AUA,\ Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR,\ Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG,\\
&Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY,\ Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG,\ Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY,\ f3\_stress\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
These symbols name internal residual coordinates, not biochemical causes.  The turnover readout is allowed to test only the residual increment that remains after the control surface is fitted; it is not allowed to turn a codon residual into translation, folding, degradation mechanism, function, physical admissibility, or a universal law.

\paragraph{Audit statistic.}  The powered run used $n=3290$ genes and $B=200$ null draws.  The control model before adding the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates had
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{L2} &= 0.055740851626933985,\\
R^2_{L3} &= 0.050278872193227464.
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the residual coordinate block lowered the fitted explanatory score rather than improving it:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta R^2 &= R^2_{L3}-R^2_{L2}\\
&= -0.005461979433706483,\\
\Delta DL &= -13.68819707902732\ \text{bits}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The matched-bin null placed its $99\%$ reference value at $3.51918934107672$ bits, and the recoded null placed its $99\%$ reference value at $1.9038288477880905$ bits.  The observed increment did not approach either positive tail.  The reported tail probabilities were
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{\mathrm{matchedbin}} &= 0.8855721393034826,\\
p_{\mathrm{recoded}} &= 1.0.
\end{aligned}
$$
The finite-row verdict is therefore negative for a turnover residual increment: in the available table, $B^{*}_{Q6}$ does not improve measured turnover prediction beyond expression, translation efficiency, pLDDT, N-end information, and the other fitted controls.

\paragraph{Relation to the translation and protein-abundance rows.}  The negative turnover result is not a denial that the same residual basis can touch other bounded readouts.  A separate protein-abundance mediation run joined twelve organisms, with $n=4042$ proteins in \emph{Bacillus subtilis}, $n=11661$ in \emph{Caenorhabditis elegans}, $n=14410$ in zebrafish, $n=13295$ in fly, $n=3739$ in \emph{Escherichia coli} K-12 MG1655, $n=12418$ in chicken, $n=19053$ in human, $n=19395$ in mouse, $n=4129$ in \emph{Mycobacterium smegmatis}, $n=13322$ in rat, $n=5815$ in yeast, and $n=1656$ in \emph{Sulfolobus solfataricus}.  In that separate surface, the mediated fraction $M^{QTP}$ ranged from $0.01850668564589326$ in \emph{S. solfataricus} to $0.9491551041216973$ in yeast, with every listed organism reporting the fraction inside the unit interval.  Those numbers justify treating translation-conditioned protein abundance as a bounded statistical contact in its own table.  They do not license import into turnover, because turnover is a different downstream layer and the current turnover table gives a negative residual increment.

\paragraph{Mass-balance and structural-order context.}  Two neighboring audits help locate the boundary.  In the dynamic mass-balance closure run, the finite subset had $n=120$ genes and $718$ intervals; the reported values were $R^2_{L0}=0.9960628201518571$, $R^2_{L1}=0.996410628851051$, $R^2_{L2}=0.9712443377743347$, and $R^2_{L3}=0.8953999568421264$, with $\Delta R^2=-0.07584438093220836$, $\Delta DL=-668.8046070381928$ bits, $p_{\mathrm{TEbin}}=0.6831683168316832$, and $p_{\mathrm{timeshuffle}}=0.9306930693069307$.  The structural-order axis identity run, by contrast, found a bounded positive axis call carried by $f3\_stress$: its weighted held-out increment was $0.0031744502294756994$ with sign-flip $p=0.03125$, while the other eight coordinates did not pass that fold-level gate.  These contacts show why the turnover chapter must be read as a local audit rather than a blanket biological judgment.  A coordinate can be visible in one finite readout and absent in another.

\paragraph{Allowed conclusion.}  The only positive statement supported here is a closure of the current turnover table against the proposed extra residual block.  After expression, translation efficiency, pLDDT, N-end information, and related controls are included, the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ block gives $\Delta R^2<0$, $\Delta DL<0$, and null-tail probabilities far from a positive detection.  The chapter therefore records a current-data refusal for measured turnover residual prediction.  It does not say that synonymous residuals are biologically irrelevant, and it does not erase the bounded translation or protein-abundance contacts in other finite surfaces.

\paragraph{No-promotion boundary.}  Nothing in this audit establishes translation realization, ribosome dynamics, folding state, protein physical admissibility, molecular function, degradation mechanism, phenotype, selection, causation, or universality.  Any such layer would require its own matched contact and its own finite statistic.  In particular, a direct $Q_i\to P_k$ or $Q_i\to$ turnover association is not admissible as protein realization unless the same coordinate first survives an appropriate translation-readout step, and the present turnover result supplies no such survival.  The local readback is only this: on $3290$ joined genes, the measured turnover residual does not improve when the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ coordinates are appended to the existing control surface.
