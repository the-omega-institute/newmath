\subsection{Proteostasis turnover residual audit for $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$}\label{sec:bioreality-proteostasis-turnover-residual-bstar-q6}\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite surface.}
This record tests whether the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates retain independent contact with measured protein turnover after the lower explanatory layers have already been admitted. The finite table has $n=3290$ genes. Its row coordinates include the residual block $Q$, expression and translation controls, protein-structure and terminal-degradation controls, and a measured turnover readout. The intended readback is not a direct statement that a codon residual changes degradation. It is the narrower audit question: after expression, translation-efficiency information, pLDDT-like structural-order information, N-end features, and the usual sequence controls are already present, does the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ block still improve the turnover model?

\paragraph{Layered regression audit.}
The registered run compares a control surface $L2$ against a surface $L3$ in which the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual block is added after the turnover-relevant controls. The measured values are
$$
\begin{aligned}
 n_{\mathrm{genes}}&=3290,\\
 R^2_{L2}&=0.055740851626933985,\\
 R^2_{L3}&=0.050278872193227464,\\
 \Delta R^2&=R^2_{L3}-R^2_{L2}=-0.005461979433706483,\\
 \Delta DL_{\mathrm{bits}}&=-13.68819707902732.
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the added residual coordinates do not improve held-out turnover prediction on this finite surface. The sign is adverse in both reported summaries: the fitted $R^2$ decreases from $0.055740851626933985$ to $0.050278872193227464$, and the description-length difference is negative by $13.68819707902732$ bits. The audit therefore licenses a negative statement, not a weak positive one.

\paragraph{Null calibration.}
The permutation and recoding checks reinforce the same boundary. The run used $B=200$ resamples. Against the matched-bin null, the $99$ percent reference value for $\Delta DL$ is $3.51918934107672$ bits, with $p_{\mathrm{matched\ bin}}=0.8855721393034826$. Against the recoded null, the $99$ percent reference value is $1.9038288477880905$ bits, with $p_{\mathrm{recoded}}=1.0$. These numbers put the observed $\Delta DL=-13.68819707902732$ far from any positive-tail turnover signal. The finite-row conclusion is therefore a current-data closure: there is no evidence here that $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ predicts measured turnover beyond expression, translation, pLDDT-like order, N-end information, and the other admitted controls.

\paragraph{Relation to adjacent positive surfaces.}
This negative turnover audit is compatible with other finite contacts in the same biological program. A separate protein-abundance mediation surface contains twelve organism-matched protein-abundance panels, with per-organism mediated fractions such as $0.9491551041216973$ in \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}, $0.612922252793667$ in \emph{Escherichia coli} K12 MG1655, $0.4872268357227724$ in \emph{Caenorhabditis elegans}, and $0.27206289160119157$ in human. Those numbers concern a $Q\to T\to P$ abundance decomposition, not turnover. A mass-balance audit on $n=120$ genes and $718$ intervals also reported a negative increment, with $R^2_{L2}=0.9712443377743347$, $R^2_{L3}=0.8953999568421264$, $\Delta R^2=-0.07584438093220836$, and $\Delta DL=-668.8046070381928$ bits. An execution-decomposition run on $3289$ yeast genes found at least one observed rate-proxy subblock, and a structural-order audit identified $f_3$-stress as the only fold-level sign-flip axis, with weighted held-out increment $0.0031744502294756994$ and sign-flip $p=0.03125$. The turnover result should be read against that mixed finite record: $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ can touch translation-adjacent and structure-adjacent summaries, but this particular turnover surface refuses promotion.

\paragraph{Admissible conclusion.}
The allowed conclusion is deliberately local. On the verified table with $3290$ genes, adding $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ after the stated controls gives $\Delta R^2=-0.005461979433706483$, $\Delta DL=-13.68819707902732$ bits, $p_{\mathrm{matched\ bin}}=0.8855721393034826$, and $p_{\mathrm{recoded}}=1.0$. Hence the protein-turnover claim is closed negatively for the present data: $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ is not promoted to an independent measured-turnover predictor on this audit.

\paragraph{Local boundary.}
No translation-realization claim follows from this turnover audit by itself. No folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, degradation mechanism, organism-level phenotype, selection law, or universal biological rule follows from the residual coordinates or from the negative regression outcome. A positive statement at any one of those layers would require its own layer-matched contact and its own controlled statistic. In particular, a direct $Q_i\to P_k$ or $Q_i\to$ turnover association is not admissible as protein realization unless the relevant $Q_i\to T_j$ translation step is present and survives its controls; this audit reports that the downstream turnover residual does not gain predictive support after the stronger controls are included.
