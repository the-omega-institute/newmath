\subsection{Proteostasis turnover residual audit for $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$}
\label{sec:bioreality-proteostasis-turnover-residual-bstar-q6}

\paragraph{Finite surface.}
This audit treats the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ synonymous residuals as a finite row-wise surface over genes for which a measured turnover response can be joined to expression, translation, structural-confidence, and degradation-context controls.  The coordinate family is not promoted to a protein-state claim by its definition.  It is tested only after the lower readouts have already been placed in the control design: expression, translation efficiency, pLDDT-like structural-confidence information, N-end information, and the simpler codon-composition baselines remain covariates against which any residual turnover contribution must compete.  The powered turnover run used $n=3290$ genes and $B=200$ null draws.  Its intended question is therefore narrow: after the observable execution and protein-abundance routes have been accounted for, does $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ still improve prediction of measured turnover?

\paragraph{Nested residual test.}
Let $Y$ denote the measured turnover readout and let $Z$ denote the finite control matrix containing the expression, translation, pLDDT, N-end, amino-acid, length, GC3, and codon-density terms available to the run.  The audit compares a control model $L_2$ with an enlarged model $L_3$ that appends the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual block.  The reported values were
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2(L_2) &= 0.055740851626933985,\\
R^2(L_3) &= 0.050278872193227464,\\
\Delta R^2 &= R^2(L_3)-R^2(L_2) = -0.005461979433706483.
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the residual block did not improve held-out turnover fit on this finite panel.  The description-length comparison agreed with the same direction:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta \mathrm{DL} &= -13.68819707902732\ \mathrm{bits},\\
\mathrm{null}_{0.99}^{\mathrm{matched\ bin}} &= 3.51918934107672\ \mathrm{bits},\\
\mathrm{null}_{0.99}^{\mathrm{recoded}} &= 1.9038288477880905\ \mathrm{bits}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The observed increment is not merely below the null thresholds; it is negative.  The matched-bin and recoded null tests gave $p_{\mathrm{matched\ bin}}=0.8855721393034826$ and $p_{\mathrm{recoded}}=1.0$, so the finite-row evidence supports a bounded negative readback rather than a turnover realization claim.

\paragraph{Relation to the lower protein route.}
The negative turnover audit is compatible with the separate protein-abundance mediation surface.  In that earlier finite matrix, $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ was represented by nine coordinates, and each organism-level design kept amino-acid composition, GC3, length, and the $M$-density baseline in the residualization set.  For example, the protein-abundance mediation run included $5815$ yeast proteins, $19053$ human proteins, $19395$ mouse proteins, and $3739$ Escherichia coli K12 MG1655 proteins.  Several of those rows had positive statistical mediation fractions for modeled translation readout to protein abundance, including $M^{QTP}=0.9491551041216973$ in yeast, $0.612922252793667$ in Escherichia coli, $0.6165167748739756$ in Drosophila, and $0.27206289160119157$ in human.  Those numbers justify keeping the protein-abundance route as a bounded cross-layer statistical object.  They do not force a turnover effect after turnover-specific controls have been inserted.

\paragraph{Execution context.}
Other finite contacts also delimit the interpretation.  The observable execution decomposition reported $3289$ Saccharomyces cerevisiae genes and classified the result as an observable execution subblock, with at least one observed rate-proxy slot passing both effective null classes.  The dynamic mass-balance audit used $120$ genes and $718$ intervals; there the enlarged model decreased fit from $R^2(L_2)=0.9712443377743347$ to $R^2(L_3)=0.8953999568421264$, with $\Delta R^2=-0.07584438093220836$, $\Delta\mathrm{DL}=-668.8046070381928$ bits, $p_{\mathrm{TEbin}}=0.6831683168316832$, and $p_{\mathrm{timeshuffle}}=0.9306930693069307$.  The structural-order audit located its strongest coordinate at $f_3$-stress, with weighted held-out increment $0.0031744502294756994$ and sign-flip $p=0.03125$.  These neighboring facts show that $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ can contact some bounded readout surfaces, but the turnover residual audit is not one of the positive surfaces under the current controls.

\paragraph{Local verdict.}
The permitted claim is a current-data closure statement: there is no evidence in this finite run that $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ predicts measured turnover beyond expression, translation efficiency, pLDDT, N-end context, and the simpler sequence controls.  Formally, the observed comparison is
$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta R^2 &< 0,\\
\Delta \mathrm{DL} &< 0,\\
p_{\mathrm{matched\ bin}} &\gg 0.05,\\
p_{\mathrm{recoded}} &= 1.0.
\end{aligned}
$$
Consequently the residual block remains bounded at the turnover-audit boundary for this dataset.  A direct $Q_i\to P_k$ association, a translation-mediated $Q_i\to T_j\to P_k$ association, or a protein-abundance mediation fraction is not by itself a degradation-rate result.  No translation mechanism, folded-state assertion, physical admissibility claim, molecular function, organism-level phenotype, causal pathway, selection law, or universal biological rule follows from this turnover audit unless a separate layer-matched contact supplies the corresponding measurements and passes its own controlled finite-row statistic.
