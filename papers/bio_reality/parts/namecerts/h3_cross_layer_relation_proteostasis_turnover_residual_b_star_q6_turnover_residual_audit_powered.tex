\subsection{Proteostasis turnover residual audit for $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$}
\label{subsec:bioreality-proteostasis-turnover-residual-audit}

\paragraph{Finite surface.}
This record tests whether the nine-dimensional $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual surface carries a measurable protein-turnover increment after stronger, nearer readouts have already been accounted for. The finite rows are protein-indexed genes with matched turnover measurements and a control design that includes expression, translation-efficiency information, structure-order proxy information through pLDDT, N-end features, amino-acid and sequence-derived controls, and the usual codon-composition baselines. The admissible question is therefore not whether codon residuals can be associated with proteins in an uncontrolled projection, but whether the residual coordinates still improve a turnover model after the nearer explanatory layers have absorbed the obvious routes.

\paragraph{Tested coordinates and model comparison.}
The residual family is the same nine-coordinate $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ basis used in the adjacent translation and protein-abundance audits:
$$
\begin{aligned}
Q &= \{K\_AAA,\ Arg\_AGR,\ Ile\_AUA,\ Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR,\ Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG,\\
&\qquad Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY,\ Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG,\ Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY,\ f3\_stress\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
On the turnover panel the registered run used $n=3290$ genes and $B=200$ null replicates. The base layer with the non-$Q$ explanatory variables achieved
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{L2} &= 0.055740851626933985,\\
R^2_{L3} &= 0.050278872193227464,\\
\Delta R^2 &= R^2_{L3}-R^2_{L2}=-0.005461979433706483.
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ block did not improve held-out turnover prediction; it reduced the fitted explanatory score on the registered comparison. The description-length comparison points in the same direction:
$$
\begin{aligned}

The observed increment is below both positive-null reference thresholds, with $p_{\mathrm{matched\ bin}}=0.8855721393034826$ and $p_{\mathrm{recoded}}=1.0$. The finite-row verdict is therefore a bounded negative result: on the current data, $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ supplies no evidence of turnover prediction beyond the expression, translation, pLDDT, N-end, and matched baseline controls.

\paragraph{Readback.}
The permitted readback is narrow. The result closes the current finite turnover surface as an honest negative: the tested $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ coordinates do not improve measured turnover prediction under the registered controls. It does not say that synonymous residuals are irrelevant to all protein-state questions, nor does it refute the bounded translation or protein-abundance surfaces. It says only that this turnover panel gives no positive residual increment after the nearer controls have been placed in the model.

\paragraph{Local no-promotion rule.}
No claim about translation, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, mechanism, phenotype, selection, or universality follows from this turnover audit. A translation claim requires a separate translation readout; a folding or structural claim requires a structure-level contact; a physical-admissibility or function claim requires its own assay; and a universal law would require multi-context evidence with simpler-baseline ablation. The present finite-row statement is a controlled non-promotion result for measured turnover, not a higher-layer biological mechanism.
