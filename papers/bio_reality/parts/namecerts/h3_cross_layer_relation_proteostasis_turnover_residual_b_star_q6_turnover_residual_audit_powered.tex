\subsection{Turnover Residual Audit for $B^\ast_{Q6}$ Coordinates}
\label{sec:bioreality-q6-turnover-residual-audit}

\paragraph{Finite surface.}
This record tests whether the nine $B^\ast_{Q6}$ residual coordinates add measurable explanatory power for protein turnover after a protein-state control surface has already absorbed the ordinary expression, translation, structural-order, and terminal-rule readouts available in the finite rows.  The tested coordinate list is
$$
\begin{aligned}
Q = \{&K\_AAA,\ Arg\_AGR,\ Ile\_AUA,\ Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR,\ Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG,\\
&Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY,\ Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG,\ Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY,\ f3\_stress\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The audit uses $n=3290$ genes and compares a controlled model $L_2$ with a $B^\ast_{Q6}$-augmented model $L_3$.  The measured turnover fit decreases from
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2(L_2) &= 0.055740851626933985,\\
R^2(L_3) &= 0.050278872193227464,
\end{aligned}
$$
so the finite improvement score is negative:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta R^2 &= R^2(L_3)-R^2(L_2)\\
&= -0.005461979433706483.
\end{aligned}
$$
The paired description-length score is also unfavorable, with $\Delta DL=-13.68819707902732$ bits.  The permutation audit used $B=200$ draws.  Its matched-bin right-tail value was $p=0.8855721393034826$, and its recoded-control right-tail value was $p=1.0$.  The corresponding $99\%$ null thresholds were $3.51918934107672$ bits for the matched-bin null and $1.9038288477880905$ bits for the recoded null.  Since the observed $\Delta DL$ is below zero and far below both positive null thresholds, the finite rows do not support a turnover-residual gain for $B^\ast_{Q6}$ beyond the named controls.

\paragraph{Relation to the translation and abundance rows.}
The negative turnover result is not a denial that the same coordinate family can enter lower-layer readouts.  A separate protein-abundance mediation surface joined twelve organisms with organism-matched CDS and protein-abundance rows.  The joined row counts were $4042$ for \textit{Bacillus subtilis} subsp. subtilis str. 168, $11661$ for \textit{Caenorhabditis elegans}, $14410$ for \textit{Danio rerio}, $13295$ for \textit{Drosophila melanogaster}, $3739$ for \textit{Escherichia coli} K12 MG1655, $12418$ for \textit{Gallus gallus}, $19053$ for \textit{Homo sapiens}, $19395$ for \textit{Mus musculus}, $4129$ for \textit{Mycobacterium smegmatis} str. MC2 155, $13322$ for \textit{Rattus norvegicus}, $5815$ for \textit{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}, and $1656$ for \textit{Sulfolobus solfataricus}.  In that abundance surface, every organism had a finite-rank residualized design with $9$ $Q$ coordinates, $23$ control rank, and one translation readout column.  The mediated fractions ranged from $0.01850668564589326$ in \textit{Sulfolobus solfataricus} to $0.9491551041216973$ in \textit{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}.  Thus the turnover audit is read as a layer-specific refusal: abundance mediation under modeled tAI does not authorize an additional claim that the same coordinates predict measured turnover after expression, translation efficiency, structural confidence, and N-end style controls are already present.

\paragraph{Control hierarchy.}
The background execution rows show why the turnover conclusion is deliberately narrow.  In a mass-balance closure run, the sequence from $L_0$ through $L_3$ had
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2(L_0\;M\text{-only}) &= 0.9960628201518571,\\
R^2(L_1\;M,E_{stat}) &= 0.996410628851051,\\
R^2(L_2) &= 0.9712443377743347,\\
R^2(L_3) &= 0.8953999568421264,
\end{aligned}
$$
with $\Delta R^2=-0.07584438093220836$, $\Delta DL=-668.8046070381928$ bits, $n=120$ genes, $718$ intervals, $p_{TEbin}=0.6831683168316832$, and $p_{timeshuffle}=0.9306930693069307$.  A distinct observable-execution decomposition in yeast used $3289$ genes and returned the finite verdict that at least one observed rate-proxy slot passed both effective null classes.  These companion rows keep the turnover audit from becoming a global rejection of rate-linked observability.  They say that rate proxies can be detected in some finite slots, while the present turnover slot does not receive a positive residual contribution from $B^\ast_{Q6}$ after the stronger controls are in place.

\paragraph{Axis localization.}
The structural-order axis audit computed the same nine coordinates and isolated $f3\_stress$ as the only coordinate passing the fold-level sign-flip gate.  Its weighted held-out increment was $0.0031744502294756994$, its sign-flip right-tail value was $0.03125$, and its weighted absolute unit loading was $0.5109586606724208$.  The remaining coordinate increments did not pass that gate: for example $Arg\_AGR$ had $0.0020139270616115154$ with $p=0.142578125$, $Ile\_AUA$ had $0.0013067592718559024$ with $p=0.0693359375$, and $Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY$ was negative at $-0.00045340330387383447$ with $p=0.529296875$.  This localization matters for the turnover audit because it prevents the protein-state result from being paraphrased as a broad nine-axis biological law.  The only finite structural-order axis with a positive gate remains an order-axis readout, not a degradation-rate or proteostasis mechanism.

\paragraph{Verdict.}
The allowed reading is a current-data closure of the turnover residual route: in the available $3290$-gene turnover matrix, $B^\ast_{Q6}$ does not improve the measured turnover model once expression, translation efficiency, pLDDT-like structural confidence, N-end style controls, and the finite baseline design have been applied.  The audit therefore records no evidence for a residual $B^\ast_{Q6}$ turnover effect beyond those controls.  It also records what is not licensed.  A direct $Q_i\to P_k$ or $Q_i\to$ turnover association is not treated as protein realization unless a same-coordinate translation readout first survives.  No translation mechanism, folding pathway, physical admissibility claim, molecular function, organism-level phenotype, selection law, or universal biological principle follows from this negative turnover row.  Any such statement would need its own layer-matched contact and its own finite statistic rather than being inherited from codon residual geometry, modeled tAI, protein abundance, structural order, or workflow eligibility.
