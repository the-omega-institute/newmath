\subsection{Translation-accuracy refusal for the fourth residual coordinate}
\label{sec:bioreality-q6-translation-accuracy-refusal}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite coordinate under test.}
This record isolates the finite coding-sequence surface obtained after amino-acid projection and restricts it to the $f_{3}$-stress coordinate inside the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual basis. The carrier is not a protein-level observable. It consists of coding-sequence rows, organism labels, synonymous codon coordinates, tRNA or wobble weights, and matched translation-readout candidates. The relevant $f_{3}$ saturation fact is finite: the ambient set $M = \mathrm{Med}(R) \cup \mathrm{ACA}$ contains
$$
\begin{aligned}
M = \{&\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{ACA},\mathrm{ACG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\\
&\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The saturated $f_{3}$ image removes the two boundary entries $\mathrm{ACA}$ and $\mathrm{ACG}$ and leaves
$$
\begin{aligned}
\operatorname{Sat}_{f_{3}}(R) = \{&\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\\
&\mathrm{CUU},\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\},
\end{aligned}
$$
with empty $f_{3}$ boundary. This is the finite combinatorial input to the translation-accuracy test; it is not itself an observation of mistranslation, ribosome dwelling, protein abundance, or phenotype.

\paragraph{Powered translation-accuracy assay.}
The tested claim is \texttt{h3.cross\_layer\_relation.fourth\_mechanism\_identity.b\_star\_q6\_translation\_accuracy\_powered}. Its powered assay completed with status \texttt{passed}, experiment run \texttt{0dc44df59ba7}, five folds, $41$ synonymous-design columns, $11$ organisms computed out of $16$ requested, and $200$ permutations. The recorded conclusion is \texttt{not\_accuracy}. In this finite row, translation accuracy is therefore rejected as the fourth-coordinate identity under the declared assay, rather than promoted as a candidate explanation of the residual axis. The conclusion is a negative identification statement: the available rank-powered residual coordinate does not select translation accuracy as the identity of the fourth residual component.

\paragraph{Position among neighboring finite tests.}
The refusal is not an isolated lack of measurement. The same finite program reports that the three-axis optimality basis is insufficient: the powered sufficiency test concluded \texttt{fourth\_mechanism\_present} with five folds, $41$ synonymous-design columns, and $11$ organisms computed out of $16$ requested. The per-organism axis-strength spectrum also used five folds and $41$ synonymous-design columns; it computed $11$ organisms out of $16$ requested and found that captured spectra were led by $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$ on average, with per-organism dominant counts $\{\texttt{d\_resid4}:11\}$. Domain means were only descriptive because the domain counts were imbalanced, namely $\{\texttt{archaea}:1,\texttt{bacteria}:2,\texttt{eukaryote}:8\}$, and the largest GC3 rank effect was reported for $d_{\mathrm{usage}}$ with $\rho = 0.136$. Thus the fourth coordinate is present in the residual geometry, but the translation-accuracy assay does not identify it.

\paragraph{Comparison with other candidate identities.}
Other finite rows narrow the interpretation without converting it into a downstream biological claim. The universal residual-axis assay concluded that the non-tRNA residual optimality aligns most with \texttt{f3\_translation\_selection}, again over five folds, $41$ synonymous-design columns, and $11$ computed organisms out of $16$. The third-axis identity assay reported provisional alignment to \texttt{usage\_frequency\_within\_family}, with mean cosine $0.128$ and one-sided sign-test $p = 0.000488$. The corresponding third-axis mechanism row concluded \texttt{selection-not-GC; not-wobble-clustered; CSC/positional identity needs\_external}. These neighboring facts keep the residual surface translation-facing and selection-facing, but they do not rescue translation accuracy as the fourth-coordinate identity. The codon-pair powered assay separately concluded \texttt{not\_codon\_pair}; the mRNA-stability powered assay concluded \texttt{fourth\_axis\_not\_mrna\_stability}; and the elongation-dwell row was only \texttt{partial} in \textit{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}. The local finite picture is therefore discriminating rather than omnibus: there is a fourth residual component, several candidate explanations are excluded or limited, and translation accuracy is one of the excluded candidates.

\paragraph{Readout boundary.}
The admissible readback is bounded by the translation row. A positive biological interpretation would require a non-vacuous controlled survival entry $S_{f3\text{-}\mathrm{stress},T_{j}}$ for a translation readout such as tAI, stAI, ribosome occupancy, or translation efficiency, computed after controls for amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, CDS length, $M$-density, and matched mRNA where the readout requires it. A rank-degenerate all-$1.0$ surface on only three organisms is a null boundary rather than survival. Likewise, modeled tAI or stAI weights are bounded codon-adaptation readbacks, not observed ribosome occupancy. In this row, the translation-accuracy test is already finite and powered, and its conclusion is negative: $B^{*}_{Q6}$ $f_{3}$-stress does not identify translation accuracy as the fourth residual mechanism in the assayed surface.

\paragraph{Local non-promotion rule.}
Nothing in this subsection establishes translation realization in measured ribosome data, protein folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, localization, stability, phenotype, mechanism, or universality. Those claims require their own layer-matched contacts and their own controlled statistics. The finite coordinate geometry, the $S$ notation, tRNA or wobble asymmetry, tAI or stAI weights, and the powered \texttt{not\_accuracy} result are sufficient to state the local refusal of translation accuracy as the fourth-coordinate identity; they are not sufficient to infer downstream structure, function, organismal consequence, or a general biological law.
