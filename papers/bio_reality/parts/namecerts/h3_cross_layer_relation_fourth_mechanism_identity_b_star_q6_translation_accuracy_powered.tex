\subsection{Translation-accuracy refusal for the fourth $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual coordinate}
\label{sec:bioreality-q6-translation-accuracy-refusal}

\paragraph{Finite carrier.}
This record concerns a finite coding-sequence surface after amino-acid projection. The coordinate under inspection is the $f_3$-stress component of the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual basis, paired only with translation-layer readouts that can be kept distinct from amino-acid composition, organism identity, GC3, coding length, $M$-density, and matched mRNA abundance. The finite upstream saturation row fixes the relevant codon support. The set $M = \operatorname{Med}(R) \cup \{\mathrm{ACA}\}$ is
$$
\begin{aligned}
M = \{&\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{ACA},\mathrm{ACG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\\
&\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The $f_3$-saturated $R$ row removes exactly $\mathrm{ACA}$ and $\mathrm{ACG}$ from this carrier:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\operatorname{Sat}_{f_3}(R) = \{&\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\\
&\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\},
\end{aligned}
$$
and the recorded $f_3$ boundary set is empty. Thus the chapter is not asking whether a new codon class appears at the boundary. It asks whether a residual imbalance already present inside this finite coding surface is better read as translation accuracy once the amino-acid quotient and the standard sequence covariates have been imposed.

\paragraph{Powered finite row.}
The direct powered test for translation accuracy was run as \texttt{b\_star\_q6\_fourth\_mechanism\_translation\_accuracy\_powered}. It used $5$ folds, $41$ synonymous-design columns, $200$ permutations, and a requested panel of $16$ organisms, of which $11$ satisfied the computation gate. The deterministic seed was \texttt{sha256:97e0f836e1a0e4fb71a81bc6201b6e67d532adce9d7fa9c348b5df160e3ae2e1}. The finite conclusion is \texttt{not\_accuracy}. In the notation of this record, the reported outcome is
$$
\begin{aligned}
N_{\mathrm{requested}} &= 16, & N_{\mathrm{computed}} &= 11,\\
K_{\mathrm{fold}} &= 5, & P_{\mathrm{perm}} &= 200,\\
D_{\mathrm{syn}} &= 41, & \operatorname{call} &= \texttt{not\_accuracy}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The negative call is local and finite. It says that, under this assay, translation accuracy is not the supported identity of the fourth residual coordinate. It does not say that the coordinate has no biological contact, and it does not say that every possible translation-accuracy assay is impossible. It only rejects the proposed accuracy interpretation for the declared powered design.

\paragraph{Relation to the residual spectrum.}
The surrounding finite rows constrain how this refusal should be read. The axis-strength spectrum was computed on $11$ organisms out of $16$ requested organisms, again with $5$ folds, at least $500$ proteins per organism, and $41$ synonymous-design columns. Its descriptive summary says that $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$ leads the captured spectra on average, followed by $d_{\mathrm{tRNA}}$, and that the per-organism dominant count is $\{\texttt{d\_resid4}:11\}$. The same row reports imbalanced domain counts $\{\texttt{archaea}:1,\texttt{bacteria}:2,\texttt{eukaryote}:8\}$ and a largest GC3 rank effect of $\rho = 0.136$ for $d_{\mathrm{usage}}$. Therefore the fourth coordinate is not being dismissed as numerical noise merely because the translation-accuracy candidate failed. The finite record instead separates two statements: a residual direction is present in the powered spectrum, while the accuracy label is not the supported explanation for that direction.

\paragraph{Adjacent finite constraints.}
Other rows sharpen the local interpretation. The three-axis sufficiency test, also using $5$ folds, $11$ computed organisms out of $16$, at least $500$ proteins per organism, and $41$ synonymous-design columns, concluded \texttt{fourth\_mechanism\_present}. The non-tRNA residual optimality row concluded that the dominant non-tRNA residual axis is \texttt{f3\_translation\_selection}. The third-axis identity row identified $d_{\perp}$ provisionally with usage frequency within family, with mean cosine $0.128$ and one-sided sign-test $p = 0.000488$. The third-axis mechanism row concluded \texttt{selection-not-GC; not-wobble-clustered; CSC/positional identity needs\_external}. These rows make the accuracy refusal more specific: the finite panel keeps residual structure, selection-aligned signal, and an unresolved fourth direction, but the translation-accuracy interpretation does not survive the declared powered test.

\paragraph{Bounded readout rule.}
For this chapter, a positive translation-layer claim would require a nondegenerate controlled survival entry $S_{f_3\text{-stress},T_j}$ for a bounded readout such as tAI, stAI, ribosome occupancy, or translation efficiency. The current translation-accuracy row is not such a positive survival row; it is an exclusion of one proposed identity. A same-row translation-efficiency claim would require joined codon counts, wobble or tRNA weights, measured ribosome-profiling or translation-efficiency rows, and matched mRNA abundance under the declared controls. In symbols, the positive condition would have the form
$$
\begin{aligned}
S_{f_3\text{-stress},T_j} > 0
\end{aligned}
$$
after amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, coding length, $M$-density, mRNA, rank, and simpler-baseline controls. That statistic is not supplied by the translation-accuracy refusal itself.

\paragraph{Cannot-claim boundary.}
The finite conclusion \texttt{not\_accuracy} must not be promoted into a broader biological verdict. It does not establish translation realization, ribosome occupancy, protein abundance, folded structure, localization, stability, physical admissibility, molecular function, organismal phenotype, causation, or universality. It also does not turn modeled tAI or stAI weights into observed ribosome dynamics. Those claims require their own layer-matched contacts and their own controlled statistics. The only local conclusion is that, on the rank-powered $11$-organism computed panel with $41$ synonymous-design columns, $5$ folds, and $200$ permutations, translation accuracy is not the supported identity for the fourth $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual coordinate.
