\paragraph{Controlled survival interpretation.}
The admissible readback for this chapter is a bounded translation-row statistic of the form $S_{f_3\text{-}\mathrm{stress},T_j}$, where $T_j$ may be a modeled tAI or stAI surface, a ribosome-occupancy surface, or a translation-efficiency surface when the rows are organism-matched and controlled. The finite conjectural support condition is that at least one controlled entry is strictly positive after the declared covariates and simpler-baseline ablations:
$$
\begin{aligned}
S_{f_3\text{-}\mathrm{stress},T_j} > 0
\end{aligned}
$$
for some admissible $T_j$. The break condition is the opposite finite statement: all such entries remain non-positive after amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, CDS length, $M$-density, and matched mRNA controls, or the fitted matrix is rank-degenerate. The three-organism all-$1.0$ surface is therefore not treated as survival; it is a saturation boundary.

\paragraph{Local non-promotion rule.}
The translation-accuracy row supplies only a mechanism refusal inside the translation-readout layer. It does not establish translation realization by itself, because the mandatory measured or explicitly bounded translation row must be present before any downstream interpretation is allowed. It also does not establish protein abundance, folded structure, localization, stability, physical admissibility, molecular function, phenotype, causation, or a universal biological law. Each of those would require a separate layer-matched contact and its own controlled finite statistic. In particular, modeled tAI or stAI weights may serve as bounded codon-adaptation readouts, but they are not observed ribosome footprints; matched mRNA abundance is a control covariate, not a translation output; and codon-window geometry is not biological evidence unless paired with the appropriate measured or explicitly bounded contact row.

\paragraph{Chapter verdict.}
The finite verdict is a ready negative identification statement. The powered row passes its own audit and reports $\operatorname{not\_accuracy}$ for the fourth mechanism identity on $11$ computed organisms, $41$ synonymous design columns, $5$ folds, and $200$ permutations. The result should be cited as a translation-accuracy refusal for the fourth $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual mechanism, while preserving the open requirement for a non-vacuous controlled translation-readout survival entry before any higher-layer claim is made.
