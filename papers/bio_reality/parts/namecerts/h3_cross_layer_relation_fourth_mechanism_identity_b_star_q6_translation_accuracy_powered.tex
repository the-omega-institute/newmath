\subsection{Translation-accuracy refusal for the fourth residual mechanism}\label{sec:bioreality-q6-translation-accuracy-refusal}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite carrier.}
The finite object considered here is the coding-sequence residual coordinate surface obtained after amino-acid projection, restricted to the $B^*_{Q6}$ fourth-residual family and to the $f_3$-stress direction. The codon-level ground row fixes the relevant $M$ list as
$$
\begin{aligned}
M=\{&AAA,AAG,ACA,ACG,AGA,AGG,AUA,AUG,CUA,CUC,\\
& CUG,CUU,UAA,UAG,UCA,UCG,UGA,UGG,UUA,UUG\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The reported $f_3$ saturation removes $ACA$ and $ACG$, giving
$$
\begin{aligned}
\operatorname{Sat}_{f_3}(R)=\{&AAA,AAG,AGA,AGG,AUA,AUG,CUA,CUC,CUG,CUU,\\
& UAA,UAG,UCA,UCG,UGA,UGG,UUA,UUG\},
\end{aligned}
$$
and the finite boundary row is empty: $\partial_{f_3}R=\varnothing$. Thus the accuracy test is not a free codon-topology assertion. It is a finite-row discrimination problem on an already quotient-normalized residual coordinate, with the two excluded $ACA/ACG$ entries marking the saturation difference between the raw $M$ carrier and the $f_3$-closed row.

\paragraph{Question tested.}
The biological question is whether the fourth residual mechanism in the powered $B^*_{Q6}$ panel is translation accuracy. The relevant finite experiment is \texttt{b\_star\_q6\_fourth\_mechanism\_translation\_accuracy\_powered}. It used $41$ synonymous design columns, $5$ folds, $200$ permutations, and computed $11$ organisms out of $16$ requested organisms. Its recorded status is \texttt{passed}, but the substantive conclusion is \texttt{not\_accuracy}. In symbols, if $d_4$ denotes the fourth residual direction and $a$ denotes the translation-accuracy candidate surface, the tested identity is not accepted:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\operatorname{Id}(d_4,a) &= 0 \quad \text{on the powered finite panel},\\
\operatorname{conclusion}(d_4) &= \text{not translation accuracy}.
\end{aligned}
$$
This is a refusal of a mechanism label, not a refusal of the existence of a fourth residual axis. The separate optimality-axis sufficiency row reports \texttt{fourth\_mechanism\_present} on the same $11$ computed organisms out of $16$ requested, with $41$ synonymous design columns and $5$ folds. Hence the local conclusion is sharper than a null coordinate result: the fourth residual signal remains a finite candidate for biological explanation, but translation accuracy is not the supported identity in this assay.

\paragraph{Relation to neighboring mechanism rows.}
The powered mechanism table separates several candidate explanations. The mRNA-stability row reports \texttt{fourth\_axis\_not\_mrna\_stability}, with the stated reason that the yeast $d_{\mathrm{opt,halflife}}$ direction has low alignment with the abundance residual $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$ under the fixed gates. The codon-pair row reports \texttt{not\_codon\_pair} with $50$ shuffle nulls. The elongation-dwell row is only \texttt{partial} and is organism-specific for \textit{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}. The accuracy row is therefore part of a finite exclusion pattern: accuracy is rejected, mRNA stability is rejected as the dominant identity in its assay, codon-pair identity is rejected, and elongation dwell remains partial rather than a full identity call. The axis-strength spectrum also keeps the fourth residual direction visible: across $11$ computed organisms, the captured spectra are led by $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$ on average, and the per-organism dominant counts are $\{d_{\mathrm{resid4}}:11\}$, while the largest GC3 rank effect is only $\rho=0.136$ for $d_{\mathrm{usage}}$.

\paragraph{Controlled readout boundary.}
The $f_3$-stress conjecture becomes a translation-layer claim only after a matched translation readout is present. The admissible readout is a controlled survival entry $S_{f_3\text{-stress},T_j}$ for a bounded translation surface such as tAI, stAI, ribosome occupancy, or translation efficiency. The intended statistic has the form
$$
\begin{aligned}
S_{f_3\text{-stress},T_j} > 0
\end{aligned}
$$
after amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, CDS length, $M$-density, matched mRNA abundance, rank, and simpler-baseline controls. The present accuracy experiment does not supply such a measured ribosome or TE survival row. It supplies a powered classification against one proposed fourth-mechanism identity. Consequently, the positive content is limited to the finite statement that translation accuracy is not the supported identity of the fourth residual mechanism under the recorded design.

\paragraph{What follows and what does not.}
The finite row supports the following chapter-local assertion: in the powered $B^*_{Q6}$ fourth-mechanism assay, translation accuracy is not the accepted identity, despite the broader finite table indicating that a fourth mechanism is present. This statement is backed by $11$ computed organisms, $16$ requested organisms, $41$ synonymous design columns, $5$ folds, and $200$ permutations for the accuracy test. It does not establish translation realization, ribosome occupancy, protein abundance, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, phenotype, mechanism universality, or a general biological law. It also does not promote modeled tAI or stAI weights into observed ribosome dynamics. Any such stronger conclusion requires a separate layer-matched contact and its own controlled finite statistic. In particular, a direct association between the residual coordinate and a downstream protein, structure, localization, stability, function, or phenotype row is not interpretable until the same coordinate first has a non-vacuous positive translation-readout survival entry under the declared controls.

\paragraph{Falsifiability.}
The refusal is finite and reversible in principle. A future panel could overturn the local identity call only by joining same-organism or same-CDS codon counts, wobble or tRNA weights, measured translation readouts, matched mRNA controls, and accuracy measurements, then showing that the accuracy surface absorbs or explains the fourth residual direction under held-out controls. Conversely, the present row is falsified as a positive accuracy claim because the recorded powered conclusion is exactly \texttt{not\_accuracy}. The correct current reading is therefore a bounded negative mechanism identity: $B^*_{Q6}$ retains a fourth residual problem, but translation accuracy is not its supported identity in the finite experiment recorded here.
