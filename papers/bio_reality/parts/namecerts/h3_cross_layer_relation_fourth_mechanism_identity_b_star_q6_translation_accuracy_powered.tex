\paragraph{Finite coordinate surface.}
This record isolates the proposed fourth mechanism identity for the $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinate after amino-acid projection. The local carrier is the finite CDS-by-organism residual surface restricted to the $f_3$-stress coordinate and to matched translation-readout rows. Its codon-level background is not an unrestricted codon table: the recorded $M=\operatorname{Med}(R)\cup\{\mathrm{ACA}\}$ row contains
$$\begin{aligned}

\paragraph{Readout rule.}
The allowed biological readback is a controlled survival entry $S_{f3\text{-}stress,T_j}$ for a bounded translation readout such as tAI, stAI, ribosome occupancy, or translation efficiency. The entry is meaningful only after controls for amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, CDS length, $M$-density, and matched mRNA abundance where TE is used. A row with modeled tAI or stAI weights may help define a bounded translation-layer coordinate, but it is not by itself an observed ribosome-occupancy row. Likewise, codon usage, tRNA copy number, wobble weights, and residual geometry do not by themselves establish translation realization, protein abundance, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, mechanism, phenotype, or universality. Each stronger interpretation requires a separate layer-matched measurement and its own controlled statistic.

\paragraph{Finite powered result.}
The translation-accuracy identity test is passed as a negative identification. The recorded experiment $b\_star\_q6\_fourth\_mechanism\_translation\_accuracy\_powered$ used $5$ folds, $41$ synonymous design columns, $200$ permutations, and computed $11$ organisms out of $16$ requested organisms. Its conclusion is $\mathrm{not\_accuracy}$. In local terms, the fourth residual direction detected in the $B^*_{Q6}$ surface should not be named as translation accuracy on the present finite panel. The refusal is not a refusal of all translation-layer structure: the separate optimality-sufficiency row reports $\mathrm{fourth\_mechanism\_present}$ on the same $11$ of $16$ organism panel with $5$ folds and $41$ synonymous design columns. It is a narrower statement: among the tested candidates, translation accuracy is not the identity of that fourth component.

\paragraph{Neighboring discriminants.}
The same finite dossier gives useful boundaries around the negative call. The codon-pair test reports $\mathrm{not\_codon\_pair}$ with $5$ folds, $41$ synonymous design columns, and a $50$-shuffle null. The mRNA-stability test reports $\mathrm{fourth\_axis\_not\_mrna\_stability}$ because the yeast $d_{opt,halflife}$ direction has low alignment with the abundance residual $d_{resid4}$ under the fixed gates. The elongation-dwell test is only $\mathrm{partial}$ for \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}. These three rows keep the fourth mechanism unresolved in a disciplined way: not accuracy, not codon-pair, not dominant mRNA stability in the stated assay, and only partial dwell support in one organismal setting.

\paragraph{Relation to the residual spectrum.}
The broader powered spectrum helps interpret why the negative accuracy call is not vacuous. Across $11$ computed organisms, the captured spectra are led by $d_{resid4}$ in all $11$ per-organism dominance counts, with $d_{tRNA}$ next on average; the largest GC3 rank effect is reported for $d_{usage}$ with $\rho=0.136$. The universal core tRNA-adaptation row is passed, while the non-tRNA residual optimality row names $f_3$ translation selection as the dominant axis on the same $11$ of $16$ organism panel. The two-axis closure test reports $\mathrm{third\_axis\_present}$, and the third-axis identity row aligns $d_\perp$ to within-family usage frequency with mean cosine $0.128$ and one-sided sign-test $p=0.000488$. After GC orthogonalization, the third-axis mechanism row remains selection-not-GC and not wobble-clustered, with CSC or positional identity still needing additional evidence. These neighboring rows support the existence of structured residual translation pressure while preventing the fourth component from being relabeled as accuracy.

\paragraph{Wobble-breaking condition.}
The $f_3$ coordinate becomes dynamically interpretable only where third-position symmetry is broken by context tRNA or wobble weights, expressed locally as $w_c\neq w_{c^{f_3}}$. The tRNA-poverty row supplies a related finite mechanism contact: R sense codons are systematically tRNA-poor under GtRNAdb tGCN plus dos Reis wobble weights, with $40$ organisms computed out of $46$ requested, $6$ skipped, $1000$ permutations, and gate level $\alpha=0.05$. This supports a tRNA-supply boundary for R avoidance, but it does not convert the translation-accuracy test into a positive accuracy mechanism. It only says that a translation-layer asymmetry route is available where the external tRNA or wobble surface breaks the $f_3$ pairing.

\paragraph{Local conclusion.}
The finite claim is therefore a refusal with positive surrounding structure. The $B^*_{Q6}$ $f_3$-stress residual coordinate is allowed to seek a controlled $S_{f3\text{-}stress,T_j}$ entry in tAI, stAI, ribosome-occupancy, or translation-efficiency rows, and the larger powered panel shows nontrivial residual axes. However, the specific fourth-mechanism label ``translation accuracy'' is rejected by the finite powered assay: $11/16$ organisms computed, $5$ folds, $41$ synonymous design columns, $200$ permutations, conclusion $\mathrm{not\_accuracy}$. A future measured TE or ribosome row may still test survival of the $f_3$-stress coordinate under the declared controls, but no translation, folding, physical admissibility, function, mechanism, or universal biological claim follows here without an additional contact and statistic at that layer.
