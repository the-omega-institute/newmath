\paragraph{Finite coordinate surface.}
This record isolates the $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinate attached to third-position $f_3$ stress after amino-acid projection. The finite carrier is not a protein-level object. It is a CDS-by-organism residual surface restricted to the $f_3$-paired synonymous imbalance coordinate, with rows matched only to translation-layer readouts such as tAI, stAI, ribosome occupancy, or translation efficiency when such rows are present. The starting saturation datum is finite and explicit: the ambient marked set $M=\operatorname{Med}_R\cup\operatorname{ACA}$ is

The recorded $f_3$ boundary list is empty. Thus the finite row system begins with an eighteen-codon saturated surface inside a twenty-codon marked ambient list, and the accuracy question is asked only after this residual coordinate has been paired with an organism-scoped translation readout.

\paragraph{Accuracy mechanism test.}
The powered fourth-mechanism experiment for translation accuracy was run as \texttt{b\_star\_q6\_fourth\_mechanism\_translation\_accuracy\_powered}. It reports status \texttt{passed}, with $11$ computed organisms out of $16$ requested organisms, $5$ folds, $41$ synonymous design columns, and $200$ permutations. Its recorded conclusion is \texttt{not\_accuracy}. In the notation of this chapter, this means that the fourth residual direction seen in the $B^*_{Q6}$ panel is not identified with a translation-accuracy axis by the finite test that was actually run. The result is a negative classification of one candidate mechanism, not a rejection of the residual coordinate itself.

\paragraph{Relation to the surrounding finite spectrum.}
The negative accuracy call is consistent with the broader finite-row spectrum. The per-organism axis-strength computation used $11$ computed organisms out of $16$ requested organisms, again with $5$ folds, at least $500$ proteins per organism, and $41$ synonymous design columns. Its descriptive summary says that captured spectra are led by \texttt{d\_resid4} on average, followed by \texttt{d\_tRNA}; the per-organism dominant count is $\{\texttt{d\_resid4}:11\}$, and the largest GC3 rank effect is reported for \texttt{d\_usage} with $\rho=0.136$. The three-axis sufficiency experiment, on the same $11$-of-$16$ organism scale with $5$ folds and $41$ synonymous design columns, reports \texttt{fourth\_mechanism\_present}. Hence the finite situation is not that the fourth axis disappears. Rather, a fourth residual component is present, but the powered accuracy assay classifies it as not being translation accuracy.

\paragraph{Comparison with nearby mechanism calls.}
The neighboring mechanism records sharpen the interpretation. The mRNA-stability assay reports \texttt{fourth\_axis\_not\_mrna\_stability}; the codon-pair assay reports \texttt{not\_codon\_pair}; the elongation-dwell assay reports \texttt{partial}. The universal residual-axis computation identifies the dominant non-tRNA residual optimality with \texttt{f3\_translation\_selection}. The third-axis identity record gives a provisional alignment to usage frequency within family, with mean cosine $0.128$ and one-sided sign-test $p=0.000488$. The third-axis mechanism record classifies the axis as selection-not-GC, not wobble-clustered, with CSC or positional identity still requiring a separate source. These rows collectively support a narrow finite statement: the $B^*_{Q6}$ residual geometry contains a detectable fourth-mechanism problem, but the supplied accuracy-specific row resolves that problem away from translation accuracy.

\paragraph{Controlled readout rule.}
Let $q_{f_3}$ denote the $B^*_{Q6}$ $f_3$-stress residual coordinate and let $T_j$ range over bounded translation readouts. The only survival quantity licensed here is a controlled entry of the form
$$
\begin{aligned}
S_{f_3,T_j}\;=\;\Delta \operatorname{Score}\bigl(T_j\mid q_{f_3},Z\bigr),
\end{aligned}
$$
where $Z$ contains amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, CDS length, M-density, matched mRNA abundance when translation efficiency is computed, and simpler codon baselines. A positive biological translation statement would require a non-vacuous, non-rank-degenerate entry with $S_{f_3,T_j}>0$ under those controls. The present powered accuracy row does not provide such a positive accuracy entry; it supplies the finite classification \texttt{not\_accuracy}. Therefore accuracy is excluded as the identified fourth-mechanism label in this assay.

\paragraph{Local no-promotion boundary.}
This section licenses only the finite negative mechanism classification just stated. It does not say that the $f_3$-stress coordinate changes translation in vivo, and it does not infer protein abundance, folded structure, physical admissibility, molecular function, localization, stability, phenotype, causation, adaptation, or a universal biological law. Modeled tAI or stAI weights are bounded readout surfaces, not direct ribosome-occupancy observations. Ribosome profiling or translation-efficiency rows would become decisive only after same-organism and same-CDS joining with codon counts, wobble or tRNA weights, matched mRNA controls, and the declared covariates. Without that separate contact and statistic, the finite record remains an accuracy-refusal result for the fourth-mechanism identity problem.
