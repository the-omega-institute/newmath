\subsection{Translation-accuracy refusal for the $B^*_{Q6}$ fourth mechanism}
\label{sec:bioreality-q6-translation-accuracy-refusal}

\paragraph{Finite carrier.}
The record considered here is the finite coding-sequence residual surface obtained after amino-acid projection, restricted to the $f_3$-stress coordinate of $B^*_{Q6}$ and to organism-matched translation readouts. The relevant biological object is not a free assertion about translation as such. It is a row-bounded question: whether the $f_3$-paired synonymous imbalance, after controls for amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, CDS length, $M$-density, and mRNA abundance, survives specifically as a translation-accuracy identity for the fourth residual mechanism. The powered run for this claim is \texttt{0dc44df59ba7}. It evaluated $11$ organisms from $16$ requested organisms, used $5$ folds, $200$ permutations, and a synonymous design with $41$ columns. The recorded conclusion is \texttt{not\_accuracy}. Thus the finite row does not support translation accuracy as the identity of the fourth mechanism in this assay.

The recorded $f_3$ boundary is empty, so the accuracy test is not being driven by an unaccounted codon-boundary residue. It is a mechanism-identification test inside the already finite residual coordinate system.

\paragraph{Powered accuracy verdict.}
The powered fourth-mechanism accuracy experiment carries the seed \texttt{sha256:97e0f836e1a0e4fb71a81bc6201b6e67d532adce9d7fa9c348b5df160e3ae2e1} and passed its computation gate. Its finite numerical content is
$$
\begin{aligned}
N_{\mathrm{computed}} &= 11, & N_{\mathrm{requested}} &= 16,\\
K_{\mathrm{folds}} &= 5, & N_{\mathrm{perm}} &= 200,\\
D_{\mathrm{syn}} &= 41, & \operatorname{conclusion} &= \mathrm{not\_accuracy}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The conclusion is therefore negative in the narrow sense required here: the fourth mechanism should not be named translation accuracy on the basis of this powered surface. This does not erase the fourth mechanism itself. A separate powered test for three-axis sufficiency reported \texttt{fourth\_mechanism\_present} on the same scale of $11$ computed organisms from $16$ requested organisms, with $5$ folds and $41$ synonymous design columns. The accuracy row only rejects one proposed identity for that remaining component.

\paragraph{Relation to neighboring finite rows.}
The refusal is consistent with the surrounding powered records. The axis-strength spectrum reported $11$ computed organisms, $16$ requested organisms, $5$ folds, and $41$ synonymous design columns; its descriptive summary says the captured spectra are led on average by \texttt{d\_resid4}, with per-organism dominance count $\{\texttt{d\_resid4}:11\}$ and a largest GC3 rank effect of $\rho=0.136$ for \texttt{d\_usage}. The universal residual-axis row reported that the non-tRNA residual optimality aligns most with \texttt{f3\_translation\_selection}, again on $11$ computed organisms from $16$ requested organisms. The third-axis identity row gives a different positive calibration: $\operatorname{mean\ cosine}=0.128$ and one-sided sign-test $p=0.000488$ for alignment to usage frequency within family. Finally, the boundary-set tRNA-poverty row computed $40$ organisms from $46$ requested organisms, skipped $6$, used $1000$ permutations at $\alpha=0.05$, and reported systematic tRNA poverty for $R$ sense codons under GtRNAdb tGCN plus dos Reis wobble weights. These rows show that the finite system has enough positive structure to distinguish mechanism candidates; the negative accuracy call is not a blanket null for $B^*_{Q6}$.

\paragraph{Interpretive boundary.}
The admissible readback is only the controlled survival or refusal of a translation-row statistic. A positive tAI, stAI, ribosome-occupancy, or translation-efficiency entry would still be a bounded translation-layer row, not an automatic statement about mature protein abundance, protein folding, localization, stability, molecular function, organismal phenotype, physical admissibility, or universality. In the present packet the stricter statement is even narrower: translation accuracy is not the supported fourth-mechanism identity under the recorded powered test. The all-control question may still be pursued with measured ribosome profiling or translation-efficiency rows, but any such row must be joined to codon counts, wobble or tRNA weights, and matched mRNA controls before it can count as a non-vacuous $S_{f_3\text{-}\mathrm{stress},T_j}$ entry.

\paragraph{Local conclusion.}
The finite evidence supports a refusal, not a promotion. The $B^*_{Q6}$ residual system contains a fourth component beyond the three-axis closure, and neighboring rows identify translation-selection and tRNA-supply structure as live biological interfaces. The powered accuracy-specific experiment, however, returns \texttt{not\_accuracy} with $11/16$ organisms computed, $5$ folds, $200$ permutations, and $41$ synonymous design columns. The chapter therefore records translation accuracy as an excluded fourth-mechanism identity for this finite assay, while leaving other translation-readout mechanisms open only where their own controlled rows are supplied.
