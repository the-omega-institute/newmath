\subsection{Fourth-Mechanism Accuracy Refusal for the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ $f_3$ Translation Surface}
\label{sec:bior reality-q6-f3-translation-accuracy-refusal}

\paragraph{Subject.}
This record concerns the finite coding-sequence surface obtained after amino-acid projection, restricted to the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual coordinate that measures $f_3$-paired synonymous imbalance.  The biological question is deliberately narrow: whether the residual direction that remains visible in the rank-powered multi-organism panel can be identified as a translation-accuracy mechanism.  The tested row is not a free biological story about codon usage.  It is the coordinate-level question whether the $f_3$-stress direction, together with organism-scoped codon usage, tRNA or wobble weights, and matched translation readouts, supports an accuracy identity rather than another fourth mechanism.

The codon-side support is anchored by the verified $f_3$ saturation row.  The finite ambient list $M = \operatorname{Med}(R) \cup \{ACA\}$ contains twenty codons, while the saturated $f_3$ image removes the two $ACA,ACG$ boundary entries and leaves eighteen entries:

The recorded $f_3$ boundary is empty, so the residual coordinate is not being carried by an unaccounted boundary codon.  Its possible biological expression must therefore enter through a paired readout such as tAI, stAI, ribosome occupancy, or translation efficiency, and only after controlling amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, CDS length, $M$-density, and matched mRNA abundance where a measured translation-efficiency denominator is used.

The appropriate reading is a refusal of translation accuracy as the fourth-mechanism identity for this row, not a refusal of all translation-layer structure.

\paragraph{Relation to neighboring powered rows.}
The surrounding finite rows make the refusal more specific.  A three-axis sufficiency assay on the same $11$ of $16$ organism panel, with $5$ folds, at least $500$ proteins per organism, and $41$ synonymous design columns, concluded that a fourth mechanism is present.  The per-organism axis spectrum also used $11$ computed organisms and reported that $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$ led on average, with dominant counts $\{d_{\mathrm{resid4}}:11\}$ and domain sample sizes $\{\mathrm{archaea}:1,\mathrm{bacteria}:2,\mathrm{eukaryote}:8\}$; the largest GC3 rank effect there was only $\rho=0.136$ for $d_{\mathrm{usage}}$.  The third-axis identity row identified $d_{\perp}$ with usage frequency within family, with mean cosine $0.128$ and one-sided sign-test $p=0.000488$, while the third-axis mechanism row classified the signal as selection-not-GC and not-wobble-clustered.  Together these rows say that the coordinate system contains real rank-powered residual structure, but the accuracy row says that this particular fourth-mechanism label is not the right one.

\paragraph{Discriminating against nearby candidates.}
Other fourth-mechanism probes also constrain interpretation.  The mRNA-stability row concluded $\operatorname{fourth\_axis\_not\_mrna\_stability}$ because the yeast $d_{\mathrm{opt\_halflife}}$ direction had low alignment with abundance $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$ under the fixed gates.  The codon-pair row concluded $\operatorname{not\_codon\_pair}$ using $5$ folds, $41$ synonymous design columns, a shuffle null of $50$, and a minimum of $500$ proteins per organism.  The elongation-dwell row was only partial in \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}.  Hence the accuracy refusal should be recorded as one member of a finite exclusion pattern: accuracy is not the fourth-mechanism identity; codon-pair structure is not the identity; mRNA stability is not the dominant identity in the assayed form; elongation dwell has only partial support in the yeast row.

\paragraph{Readout boundary.}
The row remains bounded by its translation-readout contacts.  Codon-usage tables report sequence composition; tRNA-copy and modeled tAI or stAI weights report organism-scoped supply or adaptation surfaces; ribosome profiling and matched mRNA abundance would be needed for measured translation efficiency.  None of these contacts, by name alone, proves observed translation survival for this exact coordinate.  A positive survival entry would require a same-organism or same-CDS joined matrix and a controlled statistic $S_{f3\text{-}stress,T_j}>0$ after the declared covariates and simpler-baseline ablation.  In symbols, the admissible support condition is only
$$
\begin{aligned}
S_{f3\text{-}stress,T_j} &> 0\\
\text{after controls } Z &= \{\text{amino-acid composition},\text{ organism},GC3,\text{ length},\\
&\quad M\text{-density},\text{ matched mRNA where applicable}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The present accuracy assay does not supply such a positive measured translation-efficiency row; it supplies a negative mechanism label for accuracy within the powered design.

\paragraph{Local cannot-claim rule.}
The finite result permits the following statement and no stronger one: in the powered $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ fourth-mechanism panel, the $f_3$-stress residual coordinate is not identified with translation accuracy.  It does not follow from this row that the coordinate has no translation relevance, nor that it changes protein abundance, folding, physical admissibility, localization, stability, molecular function, organismal phenotype, mechanism in the broad causal sense, or universality.  Each of those claims would require its own layer-matched contact and its own controlled statistic.  Modeled tAI or stAI weights may bound a codon-adaptation readout; they are not direct ribosome-occupancy measurements.  Rank-degenerate all-$1.0$ surfaces are null boundaries, not survival evidence.  The chapter therefore records an accuracy-identity refusal inside a finite translation-readout program, while keeping all higher biological promotions closed until separate data open them.
