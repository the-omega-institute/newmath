\paragraph{Finite object.}
The object tested here is the finite residual coordinate surface obtained from coding-sequence rows after amino-acid projection, with attention restricted to the $B^*_{Q6}$ coordinate whose sign is sensitive to the $f_3$ third-position pairing. The recorded $f_3$ boundary is empty.  Thus the tested coordinate is not an unrestricted codon-count statistic; it is a finite residual contrast on a declared codon surface after the amino-acid quotient has removed amino-acid-composition effects.

\paragraph{Tested accuracy claim.}
The accuracy hypothesis asks whether the fourth residual mechanism detected in the $B^*_{Q6}$ powered panel is explained by translation accuracy.  The experiment identified as $\mathrm{b\_star\_q6\_fourth\_mechanism\_translation\_accuracy\_powered}$ returned status $\mathrm{passed}$ with conclusion $\mathrm{not\_accuracy}$.  Its design used $41$ synonymous design columns, $5$ folds, $11$ computed organisms out of $16$ requested organisms, and $200$ permutations, with deterministic seed
$\mathrm{sha256:97e0f836e1a0e4fb71a81bc6201b6e67d532adce9d7fa9c348b5df160e3ae2e1}$.
In the notation of this chapter, the finite call is therefore
$$
\begin{aligned}
&\text{organisms computed}=11,\qquad \text{organisms requested}=16,\\
&\text{folds}=5,\qquad \text{permutations}=200,\\
&\text{synonymous design columns}=41,\qquad \text{classification}=\mathrm{not\_accuracy}.
\end{aligned}
$$
This is a negative identity result, not a negative result for all translation-layer biology.  It says that, under the declared finite powered assay, the fourth residual direction was not identified with the tested translation-accuracy mechanism.

\paragraph{Surrounding finite context.}
The refusal is not isolated from the rest of the finite panel.  A separate optimality-axis sufficiency run on the same $11$ of $16$ organism scale returned $\mathrm{fourth\_mechanism\_present}$ with $41$ synonymous design columns and $5$ folds.  Thus the accuracy result is best read as an identity exclusion inside a nontrivial fourth-direction setting: a fourth mechanism is present in the powered residual geometry, but this particular accuracy assay did not name it.  The per-organism axis-strength spectrum also used $11$ computed organisms, $16$ requested organisms, $5$ folds, a minimum of $500$ proteins per organism, and $41$ synonymous design columns; its descriptive summary recorded that $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$ led on average and was dominant in all $11$ computed organisms, with domain counts imbalanced as $\{\mathrm{archaea}:1,\mathrm{bacteria}:2,\mathrm{eukaryote}:8\}$ and largest GC3 rank effect $\rho=0.136$ for $d_{\mathrm{usage}}$.  These rows justify treating the fourth axis as a finite residual phenomenon requiring mechanism discrimination, while still denying the specific translation-accuracy label.

\paragraph{Relation to third-position stress.}
The $f_3$ stress coordinate becomes biologically testable only after a context-dependent asymmetry is supplied, for example by tRNA-copy or wobble-weight rows in which $w_c\ne w_{c^{f_3}}$.  A boundary-set tRNA-poverty run computed $40$ organisms out of $46$ requested organisms, skipped $6$, used $1000$ permutations at $\alpha=0.05$, and classified the $R$ sense codons as systematically tRNA-poor under GtRNAdb tGCN plus dos Reis wobble weights.  This supports a tRNA-supply route for $R$ avoidance, but it does not convert the translation-accuracy assay into a positive accuracy result.  The third-axis identity row separately recorded an alignment to usage frequency within family with mean cosine $0.128$ and one-sided sign-test $p=0.000488$ over the powered $11$ of $16$ organism panel.  These finite rows place the accuracy refusal in a broader codon-usage and tRNA-supply landscape without promoting the refusal into a universal mechanism theorem.

\paragraph{Readout rule.}
The bounded readout for this chapter is a controlled survival entry $S_{f3\text{-}\mathrm{stress},T_j}$, where $T_j$ may be a modelled codon-adaptation weight such as tAI or stAI, a ribosome-occupancy readout, or a translation-efficiency readout.  The intended statistic is only meaningful after controls for amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, CDS length, $M$-density, and matched mRNA abundance where translation efficiency is used.  The three-organism all-$1.0$ controlled matrix is not counted as survival in this chapter, because rank saturation at that size gives a null boundary rather than evidence.  Likewise, a modelled tAI or stAI row is a bounded translation-layer proxy unless it is calibrated against organism-matched measured ribosome or translation-efficiency rows.

\paragraph{Local non-promotion boundary.}
The finite conclusion $\mathrm{not\_accuracy}$ has a narrow scope.  It does not assert that the fourth residual direction has no biological interpretation, and it does not assert that $f_3$ stress has no translation-layer contact.  It only excludes translation accuracy as the fourth-mechanism identity in the recorded powered assay.  No observed translation realization, ribosome occupancy, protein abundance, folded structure, physical admissibility, molecular function, phenotype, detailed mechanism, or universal biological law follows from the residual coordinate, the codon lists, the wobble asymmetry condition, the tAI or stAI route, or the accuracy refusal alone.  Any such stronger statement must be supplied by its own layer-matched measured contact and its own controlled statistic.  In particular, a downstream protein or function claim remains blocked until a positive same-coordinate translation-readout row is first established and then connected to a separate downstream measurement under the declared controls.

\paragraph{Chapter verdict.}
The finite chapter verdict is therefore a mechanism-identity refusal: the $B^*_{Q6}$ $f_3$-stress residual coordinate remains part of a powered fourth-axis landscape, but the powered translation-accuracy test classifies the fourth mechanism as $\mathrm{not\_accuracy}$.  The surviving scientific content is the separation between a real residual direction and an excluded mechanism label.  The next admissible question is not whether the refusal proves absence of translation biology, but whether a same-organism or same-CDS table joining codon counts, wobble-breaking weights, measured translation readouts, and matched mRNA controls yields a nondegenerate positive $S_{f3\text{-}\mathrm{stress},T_j}$ row after the stated baseline ablations.
