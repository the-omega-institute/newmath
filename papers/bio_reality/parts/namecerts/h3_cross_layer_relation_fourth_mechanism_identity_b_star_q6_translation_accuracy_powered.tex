\subsection{Translation-accuracy refusal for the fourth Q6 mechanism}\label{sec:bioreality-q6-translation-accuracy-refusal}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite coordinate being tested.}
This BioReality record concerns the finite coding-sequence residual surface obtained after amino-acid projection, restricted to the $B^*_{Q6}$ coordinate family and, more specifically, to the $f_3$-stress direction where synonymous third-position pairing can still be probed. The finite saturation row fixes the relevant codon support before any biological interpretation is allowed. The recorded $M$ row is
$$
\begin{aligned}
M=\{&AAA,AAG,ACA,ACG,AGA,AGG,AUA,AUG,CUA,CUC,\\
& CUG,CUU,UAA,UAG,UCA,UCG,UGA,UGG,UUA,UUG\},
\end{aligned}
$$
while the $f_3$-saturated $R$ row removes $ACA$ and $ACG$:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\operatorname{Sat}_{f_3}(R)=\{&AAA,AAG,AGA,AGG,AUA,AUG,CUA,CUC,CUG,CUU,\\
&UAA,UAG,UCA,UCG,UGA,UGG,UUA,UUG\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The boundary list for $f_3$ is empty, so this packet is not using an unaccounted boundary codon as evidence. The question is narrower: after amino-acid composition, organism, $GC3$, coding-sequence length, $M$-density, and matched transcript controls are accounted for, does the $f_3$-stress residual behave like a translation-accuracy axis?

\paragraph{Accuracy test outcome.}
The finite powered run for the fourth-mechanism accuracy hypothesis returned status passed with conclusion \emph{not\_accuracy}. The run used $5$ folds, requested $16$ organisms, computed $11$ organisms, used $41$ synonymous-design columns, and evaluated $200$ permutations. Thus the row does not license the statement that the residual fourth mechanism is translation accuracy. It says the opposite within the tested surface: the accuracy interpretation failed the declared discriminant on the available powered panel.

\paragraph{Relation to the surrounding Q6 axis facts.}
The refusal is not an empty or rank-only artifact. The neighboring finite rows show that there is real Q6 residual structure to explain. The three-axis sufficiency run reported \emph{fourth\_mechanism\_present} on $11$ computed organisms out of $16$ requested, again with $5$ folds and $41$ synonymous-design columns. The per-organism axis-strength spectrum likewise computed $11$ organisms; all $11$ dominant-count calls were assigned to $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$, with the descriptive ordering led by $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$ on average, followed by $d_{\mathrm{tRNA}}$. Domain means were explicitly only descriptive under the imbalanced domain count $\{\mathrm{archaea}:1,\mathrm{bacteria}:2,\mathrm{eukaryote}:8\}$, and the largest reported $GC3$ rank effect was $d_{\mathrm{usage}}$ with $\rho=0.136$. These rows make the negative accuracy call informative: a fourth residual direction was present in the finite design, but this run did not identify it with translation accuracy.

\paragraph{Competing mechanism rows.}
The same finite memory separates the accuracy refusal from other candidate interpretations. The codon-pair test returned \emph{not\_codon\_pair} with $5$ folds, $41$ synonymous-design columns, and $50$ shuffle-null draws. The mRNA-stability test returned \emph{fourth\_axis\_not\_mrna\_stability}, because the yeast $d_{\mathrm{opt\_halflife}}$ alignment with abundance $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$ was low under the fixed gates. The elongation-dwell test returned \emph{partial} for \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}, so the current table leaves a bounded elongation-related possibility but does not promote it to a universal mechanism. The translation-accuracy row therefore has a specific role: it removes one named interpretation from the fourth-mechanism candidate set without erasing the fourth residual axis itself.

\paragraph{Third-axis and selection background.}
The accuracy refusal is also consistent with the prior third-axis rows. The $d_\perp$ identity run provisionally aligned $d_\perp$ to usage frequency within family, with mean cosine $0.128$ and one-sided sign-test $p=0.000488$. The third-axis mechanism run classified the signal as selection-not-$GC$ and not wobble-clustered, while noting that the CSC or positional identity still needs an additional contact. A separate residual optimality run reported that the non-tRNA residual optimality aligned most with $f_3$ translation selection. These finite rows support a careful reading: the residual geometry may encode translation-selection pressure in a broad bounded sense, but the present accuracy assay does not identify the fourth mechanism as mistranslation accuracy.

\paragraph{Supply-side boundary.}
The tRNA-poverty row gives one concrete supply-side anchor. In a powered permutation run with $1000$ permutations, $40$ organisms computed out of $46$ requested and $6$ skipped, the $R$ sense codons were systematically tRNA-poor under GtRNAdb tGCN plus dos Reis wobble weights. This supports a tRNA-supply interpretation for $R$ avoidance at that boundary. It does not turn the failed accuracy call into a hidden positive result. The distinction matters because tRNA supply, wobble asymmetry, elongation dwell, modeled codon adaptation, observed ribosome occupancy, and translation accuracy are different readout classes.

\paragraph{Local survival statistic.}
The bounded readout for this chapter is the controlled survival entry $S_{f3\text{-stress},T_j}$, where $T_j$ may be a modeled tAI or stAI row or a measured ribosome-occupancy or translation-efficiency row when such data are joined to the same organism and coding-sequence identifiers. In the present finite packet, the accuracy experiment is the available fourth-mechanism discriminant, and it returns \emph{not\_accuracy}. A positive biological translation claim would require a non-vacuous row such as
$$
\begin{aligned}
S_{f3\text{-stress},T_j} > 0
\end{aligned}
$$
after amino-acid composition, organism, $GC3$, length, $M$-density, matched mRNA, rank, and simpler-baseline controls. The all-positive or saturated appearance of a small rank-degenerate matrix is not enough; it is a null boundary unless the panel has enough rank and the controlled statistic survives.

\paragraph{Interpretation.}
The chapter-level conclusion is a negative identity statement. The finite run does not say that the $B^*_{Q6}$ $f_3$-stress coordinate is biologically irrelevant, and it does not say that translation-layer effects are absent. It says that, in the powered fourth-mechanism identity assay recorded here, the candidate label \emph{translation accuracy} is rejected. The remaining scientifically admissible content is therefore: a fourth residual direction is present in the Q6 design, codon-pair and mRNA-stability identities are also rejected in their respective assays, elongation dwell has only partial support in yeast, and tRNA or wobble-weight asymmetry remains relevant only where it is tied to an explicitly bounded translation row.

\paragraph{No-promotion boundary.}
No downstream claim follows merely from this coordinate geometry or from the accuracy refusal. In particular, this chapter does not establish translation realization, ribosome occupancy, protein abundance, folded structure, physical admissibility, molecular function, localization, protein stability, phenotype, mechanism, or universality. Each of those would need its own layer-matched contact and its own controlled statistic. The only local verdict is that the fourth-mechanism accuracy hypothesis was tested on the finite powered panel and returned \emph{not\_accuracy}.
