\subsection{The $f_3$-stress coordinate does not identify translation accuracy}\label{subsec:bioreality-q6-f3-translation-accuracy}\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite coordinate surface.}
The record tested here is the $B^*_{Q6}$ synonymous residual surface restricted to the $f_3$-stress coordinate after amino-acid projection. The finite saturation row fixes the admissible codon support before any biological interpretation is attempted. The marked set $M=\mathrm{Med}_R\cup\mathrm{ACA}$ contains
$$
\begin{aligned}
M=\{&\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{ACA},\mathrm{ACG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\\
&\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The $f_3$-saturation row removes exactly $\mathrm{ACA}$ and $\mathrm{ACG}$ from this marked set:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{Sat}_{f_3}(R)=\{&\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\\
&\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The recorded $f_3$ boundary is empty, so the coordinate has no remaining boundary codon row in this finite test surface. This is why the accuracy question is not a free biological extrapolation from codon spelling: it is a controlled readout question on a residual coordinate that has already been quotiented by amino-acid identity.

\paragraph{Powered accuracy assay.}
The specific fourth-axis identity test for translation accuracy was run as experiment $\mathrm{b\_star\_q6\_fourth\_mechanism\_translation\_accuracy\_powered}$ with run id $\mathrm{0dc44df59ba7}$. The finite design used $41$ synonymous design columns, $5$ folds, $200$ permutations, and computed $11$ organisms out of $16$ requested organisms. The deterministic seed was $\mathrm{sha256:97e0f836e1a0e4fb71a81bc6201b6e67d532adce9d7fa9c348b5df160e3ae2e1}$. The run passed its computational gate, and its conclusion was
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{fourth\ axis\ identity}\ne \mathrm{translation\ accuracy}.
\end{aligned}
$$
Equivalently, within the finite powered panel actually computed, the fourth residual component of the $B^*_{Q6}$ surface was not supported as a translation-accuracy axis. This is a negative identity result, not a failed computation and not a null about the whole residual geometry.

\paragraph{Relation to the surrounding finite spectrum.}
The negative accuracy call is consistent with the surrounding powered rows. A separate three-axis sufficiency row, run on $11$ computed organisms out of $16$ requested with $41$ synonymous design columns and $5$ folds, concluded that a fourth component was still present. The axis-strength spectrum, again over $11$ computed organisms out of $16$ requested with $41$ synonymous design columns and $5$ folds, reported that captured spectra were led on average by $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$, followed by $d_{\mathrm{tRNA}}$, with all $11$ per-organism dominance calls assigned to $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$ and the largest GC3 rank effect only $\rho=0.136$ for $d_{\mathrm{usage}}$. Thus the fourth component is not erased by the accuracy result; rather, this row removes one proposed identity for it.

\paragraph{Distinction from the third axis.}
The third-axis identity row gives a useful contrast. In the same powered style, $d_{\perp}$ was provisionally aligned to within-family usage frequency, with mean cosine $0.128$ and one-sided sign-test $p=0.000488$. That statement concerns the third residual direction and its usage-frequency alignment. It does not license an accuracy interpretation for the fourth residual component. The present row is narrower: after the declared controls and permutation procedure, the fourth mechanism candidate named translation accuracy is rejected for this finite surface.

\paragraph{Readout boundary.}
The only bounded biological interpretation available here is the finite negative identity call for translation accuracy. The row does not by itself establish translation survival for the $f_3$-stress coordinate on measured ribosome occupancy or translation efficiency. Such a statement would require same-organism and same-CDS codon rows, wobble or tRNA-weight rows where the $f_3$ symmetry is broken, measured translation readouts such as ribosome-profiling or translation-efficiency rows, and matched mRNA controls. The relevant statistic would have to be non-vacuous and positive after amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, CDS length, $M$-density, mRNA, rank, and simpler-baseline controls.

\paragraph{No promotion beyond the measured layer.}
This record therefore has a precise scientific content: the powered fourth-axis assay found $\mathrm{not\_accuracy}$ on $11$ computed organisms, $41$ synonymous columns, $5$ folds, and $200$ permutations. It does not imply protein abundance, folded structure, localization, stability, molecular function, physical admissibility, phenotype, causal mechanism, or universality. None of those higher-layer interpretations follows from the codon residual surface, from modeled tAI or stAI weights, from the empty $f_3$ boundary, or from the negative accuracy classification. Each would require its own matched contact and its own controlled statistic before it could be stated as a biological result.
