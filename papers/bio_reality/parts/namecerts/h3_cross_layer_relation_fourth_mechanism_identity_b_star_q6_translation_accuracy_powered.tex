\paragraph{Finite coordinate surface.}
This record concerns the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinate after amino-acid projection, restricted to the $f_3$-stress direction and tested against translation-layer readbacks.  The finite codon boundary used by the packet is not an unrestricted codon table.  Its marked set is

&UAA,UAG,UCA,UCG,UGA,UGG,UUA,UUG\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The remaining $f_3$ boundary is empty in the audited row, so the coordinate is being tested as a residual stress after the named finite surface has already closed its local $f_3$ boundary.  The relevant biological question is therefore narrower than whether codon usage matters in general: it asks whether the fourth residual direction in this finite surface is specifically a translation-accuracy axis.

\paragraph{Observed finite assay.}
The powered translation-accuracy assay reports status passed with conclusion \emph{not\_accuracy}.  The run computed $11$ organisms out of $16$ requested organisms, used $5$ folds, $41$ synonymous design columns, and $200$ permutations, with seed \texttt{sha256:97e0f836e1a0e4fb71a81bc6201b6e67d532adce9d7fa9c348b5df160e3ae2e1}.  In the local notation of this chapter, the tested decision is
$$
\begin{aligned}
H_{\mathrm{acc}} &: \text{the fourth } B^{*}_{Q6} \text{ residual direction is explained by translation accuracy},\\
\widehat{H}_{\mathrm{acc}} &: \text{not\_accuracy}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The finite row chooses $\widehat{H}_{\mathrm{acc}}$.  This is not a claim that translation accuracy is biologically irrelevant; it is a claim that, under this $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual design and the available powered organism panel, translation accuracy is not the identity of the fourth mechanism.

\paragraph{Relation to the surrounding residual spectrum.}
The refusal is meaningful because the same powered family of rows does not collapse the fourth direction as a whole.  The three-axis sufficiency assay reports \emph{fourth\_mechanism\_present} on $11$ of $16$ requested organisms, with $5$ folds, at least $500$ proteins per organism, and $41$ synonymous design columns.  The per-organism axis-strength spectrum also computed $11$ organisms out of $16$ and reports that captured spectra are led by $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$ on average, with dominant-count summary $\{d_{\mathrm{resid4}}:11\}$.  Thus the finite evidence separates two statements:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\text{there is residual fourth-axis signal} &\quad \text{supported in the powered rows},\\
\text{the fourth-axis identity is translation accuracy} &\quad \text{rejected in this row}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The negative call is therefore an identity refinement, not a disappearance of the residual coordinate.

\paragraph{Comparison with other proposed fourth-axis identities.}
The neighboring identity assays constrain the interpretation.  The mRNA-stability row reports \emph{fourth\_axis\_not\_mrna\_stability}, because yeast $d_{\mathrm{opt\_halflife}}$ has low alignment with abundance $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$ under the fixed gates.  The codon-pair row reports \emph{not\_codon\_pair}, using $5$ folds, at least $500$ proteins per organism, $41$ synonymous design columns, and $50$ shuffle-null replicates.  The elongation-dwell row in \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae} reports \emph{partial}.  These rows leave a sharper finite picture: the fourth direction is present, but the current named candidates mRNA stability, codon-pair structure, and translation accuracy do not identify it, while elongation dwell supplies only partial contact in one organismal setting.

\paragraph{Lower-axis anchoring.}
The refusal also sits above a nontrivial lower-axis scaffold.  The third-axis identity row identifies $d_{\perp}$ provisionally with usage frequency within synonymous family, with mean cosine $0.128$ and one-sided sign-test $p=0.000488$ across the powered assay.  The third-axis mechanism row reports \emph{selection-not-GC; not-wobble-clustered; CSC/positional identity needs\_external}, with the reason that GC-orthogonalized $d_{\perp}$ keeps significant cross-species alignment and held-out $R^2$.  Separately, the non-tRNA residual optimality row identifies the dominant residual axis as $f_3$ translation selection, and the boundary-set assay reports that $R$ sense codons are systematically tRNA-poor under GtRNAdb tGCN plus dos Reis wobble weights, using $40$ computed organisms out of $46$ requested and $1000$ permutations at $\alpha=0.05$.  These numbers justify treating the translation-accuracy result as a targeted fourth-axis exclusion rather than as evidence against the whole codon-translation interface.

\paragraph{Readout boundary.}
The only bounded readback allowed here is the controlled survival or identity entry attached to translation-layer rows such as tAI, stAI, ribosome occupancy, or translation efficiency.  A positive translation-readout statistic would still be a statement about that row after amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, CDS length, M-density, and matched mRNA controls.  The present finite row instead says that the powered fourth-axis identity test returned \emph{not\_accuracy}.  No claim about protein abundance, folded structure, localization, physical admissibility, biological function, organismal phenotype, causal mechanism, or universality follows from this result unless a separate layer-matched contact supplies the relevant measurements and controls.  In particular, modeled tAI or stAI weights cannot be read as observed ribosome occupancy, and a residual coordinate cannot be promoted into a protein-level or phenotype-level conclusion by notation alone.

\paragraph{Chapter verdict.}
The chapter-level conclusion is a finite exclusion: within the audited $B^{*}_{Q6}$ powered panel, translation accuracy is not the fourth-mechanism identity.  The result is compatible with a real fourth residual direction, compatible with lower-axis translation-selection structure, and compatible with partial elongation-dwell evidence, but it blocks any sentence that identifies the fourth residual axis with translation accuracy.  The next admissible step is not to reinterpret the negative call as a broader biological law, but to attach a row-matched translation or post-translation measurement surface and test a new, explicitly controlled statistic.
