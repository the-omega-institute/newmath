\subsection{Translation-accuracy refusal for the fourth residual axis}
\label{sec:q6-translation-accuracy-refusal}

\paragraph{Finite carrier.}
The record concerns a finite coding-sequence residual surface: synonymous codon rows are first quotiented by amino-acid identity, then restricted to the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual coordinate family and, within it, to the $f_3$-stress direction whenever third-position pairing is relevant. The saturation audit fixes the local codon support. The finite set $M=\operatorname{Med}(R)\cup\{\mathrm{ACA}\}$ has $20$ codons,
$$
\begin{aligned}
M=\{&\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{ACA},\mathrm{ACG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\\
&\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
After $f_3$ saturation of $R$, the surviving set has $18$ codons and omits exactly $\mathrm{ACA}$ and $\mathrm{ACG}$ from the displayed carrier. The recorded $f_3$ boundary is empty, so the present row is not a boundary-population artifact. It asks whether the fourth residual component, already visible in controlled synonymous designs, is specifically a translation-accuracy direction.

\paragraph{Powered accuracy assay.}
The translation-accuracy test was run as \texttt{b\_star\_q6\_fourth\_mechanism\_translation\_accuracy\_powered}. Its finite design used $41$ synonymous design columns, $5$ folds, $200$ permutations, and $11$ computed organisms from $16$ requested organisms. The stored run identifier is \texttt{0dc44df59ba7}, with deterministic seed \texttt{sha256:97e0f836e1a0e4fb71a81bc6201b6e67d532adce9d7fa9c348b5df160e3ae2e1}. The assay status is passed, and its conclusion is \texttt{not\_accuracy}. Thus the chapter records a negative identity call: within the powered finite panel, the fourth residual mechanism is not identified with translation accuracy.

\paragraph{Surrounding residual geometry.}
The negative accuracy call is not a claim that the residual geometry is absent. Other finite rows show that the residual system remains structured. The per-organism axis-strength spectrum computed $11$ organisms from $16$ requested, again with $41$ synonymous design columns and $5$ folds; every per-organism dominance count was assigned to $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$ among the $11$ computed organisms, while the reported GC3 rank effect for $d_{\mathrm{usage}}$ was only $\rho=0.136$. The three-axis sufficiency test also computed $11$ organisms from $16$ requested and concluded \texttt{fourth\_mechanism\_present}. The two-axis closure test concluded \texttt{third\_axis\_present}, with the recorded reason that $d_{\perp}$ had systematically positive held-out $R^2$ and mutually same-signed directions across organisms. In the separate identity row, $d_{\perp}$ aligned to within-family usage frequency with mean cosine $0.128$ and one-sided sign-test $p=0.000488$. These rows make the accuracy result sharper: the fourth direction is present in the finite residual analysis, but the tested accuracy readout does not identify it.

\paragraph{Translation-selection and wobble context.}
The residual-optimality row computed $11$ organisms from $16$ requested, with $41$ synonymous design columns and $5$ folds, and assigned the dominant non-tRNA residual optimality axis to \texttt{f3\_translation\_selection}. This is compatible with an $f_3$-stress hypothesis only as a bounded coordinate statement. The tRNA-poverty row gives a separate supply-side constraint: in a powered permutation test with $40$ computed organisms from $46$ requested organisms and $1000$ permutations at $\alpha=0.05$, $R$ sense codons were systematically tRNA-poor under GtRNAdb tGCN together with dos Reis wobble weights. Hence the biologically meaningful break condition is not merely the presence of codon topology. The coordinate can become a translation-readout candidate only where tRNA or wobble weights distinguish paired third-position alternatives, and only after a matched translation row survives the declared controls.

\paragraph{Excluded identities.}
Three neighbouring fourth-axis tests constrain the interpretation. The mRNA-stability assay passed with conclusion \texttt{fourth\_axis\_not\_mrna\_stability}; its reason was that yeast $d_{\mathrm{opt\_halflife}}$ had low alignment with abundance $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$ under the fixed gates. The codon-pair assay passed with conclusion \texttt{not\_codon\_pair}, using $41$ synonymous design columns, $5$ folds, and $50$ shuffle nulls. The elongation-dwell assay, restricted to \texttt{saccharomyces\_cerevisiae}, returned \texttt{partial}. Together with the present \texttt{not\_accuracy} result, these rows say that the fourth residual axis is not settled by accuracy, mRNA stability, or codon-pair structure in the tested panels, while elongation dwell remains only partly aligned in the single-organism row.

\paragraph{Local mathematical statement.}
Let $q_{f_3}$ denote the $f_3$-stress residual coordinate after amino-acid quotienting, and let $T_j$ range over bounded translation-layer readouts such as tAI, stAI, ribosome occupancy, or translation efficiency when the corresponding finite rows exist. The admissible survival statement has the form
$$
\begin{aligned}
S_{f_3,T_j} > 0
\end{aligned}
$$
after controls for amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, coding-sequence length, $M$-density, matched mRNA abundance when translation efficiency is used, rank sufficiency, and simpler codon baselines. The present powered accuracy row instead records
$$
\begin{aligned}
\operatorname{Id}_{\mathrm{fourth}}(q_{f_3},\mathrm{accuracy})=\mathrm{false}
\end{aligned}
$$
for the finite assay summarized above. This refusal is a positive finite result about identity exclusion, not a positive survival result for a measured translation-efficiency matrix.

\paragraph{Scope boundary.}
No translation realization follows from the codon-coordinate geometry alone. No folded structure, physical admissibility, biological function, downstream phenotype, causal mechanism, or universal biological law follows from this row. A later positive statement at any of those layers would require its own matched contact and its own controlled statistic. The current chapter records only the finite negative call for translation accuracy, the surrounding evidence that the residual axis is real within the synonymous coordinate analysis, and the condition that any future $f_3$-stress readout must pass through a nondegenerate, matched translation row before it can support downstream interpretation.
