\paragraph{Question tested.}
The biological question is whether the fourth residual mechanism in the powered $B^{*}_{Q6}$ panel is translation accuracy.  The tested object is narrower than generic translation.  It asks whether the $f_3$-stress coordinate, after amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, coding-sequence length, $M$-density, and simpler codon baselines are controlled, aligns with an accuracy-like translation-readout surface.  The finite assay used $41$ synonymous design columns, $5$ folds, and an organism panel with $11$ computed organisms out of $16$ requested organisms.  The translation-accuracy discriminant used $200$ permutations and returned status passed with conclusion \emph{not\_accuracy}.  In formula form, the assayed refusal is the claim that the accuracy candidate is not the surviving fourth-mechanism identity:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\operatorname{FourthMechanism}(B^{*}_{Q6}, f_3\text{-stress}) \neq \operatorname{TranslationAccuracy}
\end{aligned}
$$
under the stated finite controls and the powered panel actually computed.

\paragraph{Relation to the residual-axis spectrum.}
The refusal is not a refusal of the fourth axis itself.  A separate powered sufficiency run on the same design scale reported \emph{fourth\_mechanism\_present}, again with $5$ folds, $41$ synonymous design columns, and $11$ computed organisms out of $16$ requested organisms.  The axis-strength spectrum places the fourth residual direction at the head of the captured spectrum: the descriptive dominant count is $\{\mathrm{d\_resid4}:11\}$ across the $11$ computed organisms.  That same spectrum records the domain imbalance as $n=\{\mathrm{archaea}:1,\mathrm{bacteria}:2,\mathrm{eukaryote}:8\}$ and therefore treats domain means as descriptive rather than universal.  The largest reported GC3 rank effect is $\rho = 0.136$ on $\mathrm{d\_usage}$, which is too small and too differently located to identify the fourth residual direction with a GC3 artifact by itself.

\paragraph{Nearby negative and partial identifications.}
The accuracy refusal sits inside a finite elimination pattern.  The codon-pair powered test concluded \emph{not\_codon\_pair} on $5$ folds with $41$ synonymous design columns and $50$ shuffle-null replicates.  The mRNA-stability powered test concluded \emph{fourth\_axis\_not\_mrna\_stability}; its recorded reason is that the yeast $d_{\mathrm{opt\_halflife}}$ direction has low alignment with abundance $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$ under the fixed gates.  The elongation-dwell test returned \emph{partial} for \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}.  The combined finite reading is therefore asymmetric: the fourth mechanism is present, accuracy is rejected as its identity in the powered assay, codon-pair and mRNA-stability explanations are also rejected under their stated tests, and elongation-dwell remains only partial rather than decisive.

\paragraph{What survives mathematically.}
The residual geometry remains informative at the coordinate level.  The non-tRNA residual optimality run identifies its dominant axis as $f_3\_translation\_selection$, while the third-axis identity run reports alignment to usage frequency within family with mean cosine $0.128$ and one-sided sign-test $p=0.000488$. These facts justify keeping the $f_3$ residual coordinate in the finite biological agenda.  They do not identify the fourth residual component with translation accuracy.

\paragraph{Bounded readout rule.}
The only permitted translation-layer readback for this chapter is a controlled survival entry $S_{f_3\text{-stress},T_j}$ for a bounded translation readout such as tAI, stAI, ribosome occupancy, or translation efficiency, and even that entry must be organism-matched and nondegenerate.  A three-organism all-$1.0$ rank surface is a saturation boundary, not evidence of survival.  A modelled tAI or stAI row is a codon-adaptation readout, not an observed ribosome-occupancy row.  Matched mRNA abundance is a control covariate for translation-efficiency residualization, not a translation output.  The local support condition is therefore strict:
$$
\begin{aligned}
S_{f_3\text{-stress},T_j} > 0
\end{aligned}
$$
only after amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, length, $M$-density, matched mRNA controls where relevant, rank checks, and simpler-baseline ablation.  The current accuracy packet does not supply such a positive measured translation-survival row; it supplies a powered negative identity call for the accuracy candidate.

\paragraph{Cannot-claim boundary.}
This chapter does not claim that the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ $f_3$ coordinate causes translation, alters protein abundance, changes folded structure, establishes physical admissibility, realizes molecular function, determines localization or stability, explains phenotype, or states a universal biological law.  None of those conclusions follows from codon topology, residual coordinates, wobble-weight asymmetry, tAI or stAI modeling, or the powered \emph{not\_accuracy} outcome.  Each higher layer requires its own matched contact and its own finite statistic.  The chapter's positive content is narrower: within the powered fourth-mechanism identity screen, the fourth residual component remains present, but translation accuracy is not the supported identity.
