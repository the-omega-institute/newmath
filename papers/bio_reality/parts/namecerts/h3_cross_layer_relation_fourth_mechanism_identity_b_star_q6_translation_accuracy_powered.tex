\subsection{The translation-accuracy boundary for the fourth $B^*_{Q6}$ mechanism}
\label{sec:bioreality-q6-translation-accuracy-boundary}

\paragraph{Finite row under test.}
This BioReality row concerns the claim \texttt{h3.cross\_layer\_relation.fourth\_mechanism\_identity.b\_star\_q6\_translation\_accuracy\_powered}.  The tested object is the $B^*_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinate after amino-acid quotienting, with special attention to the $f_3$-stress direction and to translation-layer readouts.  The coordinate is finite: the recorded $M=\mathrm{Med}\,R\cup\mathrm{ACA}$ surface consists of
$$
\begin{aligned}
M=\{&\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{ACA},\mathrm{ACG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\\
&\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The corresponding $f_3$ saturation row removes the two exceptional ACA-family entries and records
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{Sat}_{f_3}(R)=\{&\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\\
&\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The boundary list for $f_3$ is empty, so this row is not a claim about an unresolved codon-edge exception.  It is a claim about whether the residual direction, once projected away from amino-acid identity, can be identified with translation accuracy rather than another fourth mechanism.

\paragraph{Powered accuracy assay.}
The accuracy-specific assay was run as \texttt{b\_star\_q6\_fourth\_mechanism\_translation\_accuracy\_powered}.  Its finite row reports status \texttt{passed}, fold count $5$, $41$ synonymous design columns, $11$ organisms computed out of $16$ requested, and $200$ permutations under seed \texttt{sha256:97e0f836e1a0e4fb71a81bc6201b6e67d532adce9d7fa9c348b5df160e3ae2e1}.  The conclusion field is \texttt{not\_accuracy}.  Thus the present chapter records a negative mechanism identity result: within this powered finite panel, the fourth $B^*_{Q6}$ mechanism should not be named as translation accuracy.

\paragraph{Local mathematical statement.}
Let $q_{f_3}$ denote the $f_3$-stress residual coordinate after amino-acid projection, and let $A$ range over translation-accuracy candidate readouts in the tested organism panel.  The finite row does not support an identity
$$
\begin{aligned}
q_{f_3}\equiv A
\end{aligned}
$$
under the declared controlled design.  More precisely, the row records that the powered accuracy discriminant returned the classification \texttt{not\_accuracy}.  This is stronger than merely saying that translation accuracy is unmeasured here: the assay specifically evaluated the fourth-mechanism identity against an accuracy candidate and rejected that name for the tested surface.  The result is nevertheless bounded by the row design.  It does not prove that no synonymous codon feature can ever influence accuracy; it says that the detected fourth $B^*_{Q6}$ residual mechanism in this powered panel is not identified with the translation-accuracy candidate used in the assay.

\paragraph{Relation to the surrounding finite spectrum.}
The negative accuracy call sits inside a broader finite spectrum rather than in isolation.  The three-axis sufficiency test reported \texttt{fourth\_mechanism\_present} with $5$ folds, $41$ synonymous design columns, and $11$ computed organisms out of $16$ requested.  The per-organism axis-strength row reported that captured spectra were led by \texttt{d\_resid4} on average, with dominant counts $\{\texttt{d\_resid4}:11\}$ and a largest GC3 rank effect of $\rho=0.136$ for \texttt{d\_usage}.  The residual optimality row identified the dominant non-tRNA residual axis as \texttt{f3\_translation\_selection}.  The third-axis identity row aligned $d_\perp$ to usage frequency within family with mean cosine $0.128$ and one-sided sign-test $p=0.000488$.  These rows make the accuracy verdict interpretable: the fourth mechanism is not absent from the finite spectrum, but the accuracy candidate is not its supported identity.

\paragraph{Competing fourth-mechanism calls.}
Other mechanism rows sharpen the boundary.  The mRNA-stability assay concluded \texttt{fourth\_axis\_not\_mrna\_stability}, with the stated reason that the yeast \texttt{d\_opt\_halflife} direction has low alignment with abundance \texttt{d\_resid4} under the fixed gates.  The codon-pair assay concluded \texttt{not\_codon\_pair} using $5$ folds, $41$ synonymous design columns, and $50$ shuffle-null draws.  The elongation-dwell assay in \textit{Saccharomyces cerevisiae} returned \texttt{partial}.  The present accuracy row therefore belongs to a finite exclusion pattern: translation accuracy, mRNA stability, and codon-pair structure are not the supported names for the dominant fourth-axis identity under their respective assays, while elongation dwell remains only partial in the single-organism row.  The chapter should be read as a mechanism-name refusal for accuracy, not as a universal biological negation.

\paragraph{Readout boundary.}
The only admissible translation-layer interpretation is a bounded survival or non-survival statement for matched translation readouts.  A positive downstream interpretation would require a non-vacuous controlled entry $S_{f_3,T_j}>0$ for a translation readout such as tAI, stAI, ribosome occupancy, or translation efficiency, after amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, CDS length, $M$-density, mRNA control, rank, and simpler-baseline controls.  The present row instead reports the accuracy mechanism identity as negative.  Consequently, no claim about protein abundance, folded structure, localization, stability, molecular function, system phenotype, physical admissibility, causal mechanism, or universality follows from this row.  Those layers require their own matched contacts and their own controlled statistics.  In particular, modeled tAI or stAI weights are not observed ribosome occupancy, ribosome profiling is not protein abundance, mRNA abundance is only a control unless paired into a translation-efficiency statistic, and a codon-coordinate geometry is not by itself biological realization.

\paragraph{Falsification and use.}
The falsifiable content of the row is finite and operational.  If a later organism-matched panel joins codon counts, wobble or tRNA weights, measured translation readouts, matched mRNA abundance, and an explicit accuracy readout, then the accuracy name can be reconsidered only by recomputing the controlled statistic on that joined matrix.  Until such a row overturns the present powered result, the correct local use of this chapter is to exclude \texttt{translation\_accuracy} as the supported identity of the fourth $B^*_{Q6}$ mechanism in the recorded finite panel.  The result may coexist with a translation-selection residual axis and with translation-readout boundary tests; it only blocks the stronger naming step that would identify the fourth mechanism itself as translation accuracy.
