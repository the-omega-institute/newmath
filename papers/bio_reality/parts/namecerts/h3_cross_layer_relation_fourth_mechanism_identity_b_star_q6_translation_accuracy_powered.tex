Its finite conclusion is
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{fourth\ mechanism\ identity} &\neq \mathrm{translation\ accuracy},\\

\end{aligned}
$$
This is a negative identity result rather than a failed computation. The assay has enough finite rows to reject translation accuracy as the named fourth mechanism in this panel, while still allowing the broader $B^*_{Q6}$ residual program to retain translation-layer relevance under other readouts.

\paragraph{Relation to the fourth-axis search.}
The refusal is not isolated. The three-axis sufficiency experiment computed $11$ of $16$ requested organisms, used $5$ folds and $41$ synonymous-design columns, and concluded $\mathrm{fourth\_mechanism\_present}$. The per-organism spectrum also computed $11$ organisms, again with $5$ folds and $41$ synonymous-design columns, and reported that $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$ led the captured spectra on average, with per-organism dominant counts $\{d_{\mathrm{resid4}}:11\}$. These rows say that the residual surface still contains a fourth component; the present chapter says that the component is not explained by translation accuracy in the tested sense. Other identity rows narrow the same space: the mRNA-stability assay returned $\mathrm{fourth\_axis\_not\_mrna\_stability}$, the codon-pair assay returned $\mathrm{not\_codon\_pair}$, and the elongation-dwell assay returned $\mathrm{partial}$ for $\mathrm{saccharomyces\_cerevisiae}$. The finite reading is therefore a partition, not a collapse: accuracy is excluded, mRNA stability is excluded in the fixed assay, codon-pair structure is excluded in the fixed assay, and elongation dwell remains only a partial candidate.

\paragraph{Controlled readback convention.}
The coordinate may be compared with translation readouts only through bounded rows such as tAI, stAI, ribosome occupancy, or translation efficiency, and only after controls for amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, coding-sequence length, $M$-density, and matched mRNA abundance where the readout requires it. The relevant statistic has the form
$$
\begin{aligned}
S_{f_3\text{-stress},T_j} &> 0
\end{aligned}
$$
for a specified translation row $T_j$. A modeled codon-adaptation weight is not the same object as observed ribosome occupancy, and a rank-degenerate all-$1.0$ surface is a null boundary rather than survival. The current accuracy row does not by itself supply a positive measured translation-efficiency or ribosome-occupancy survival entry; it only rules out translation accuracy as the fourth-mechanism identity under the finite powered assay.

\paragraph{Local biological boundary.}
No protein abundance, folded structure, physical admissibility, localization, stability, molecular function, phenotype, mechanism, or universal biological law follows from this coordinate or from the accuracy refusal. Even a future positive $S_{f_3\text{-stress},T_j}$ entry would remain a translation-layer statement unless a separate layer-matched contact supplies protein, structure, physical, functional, phenotypic, or perturbational data. In the present record, the justified conclusion is narrower: within the computed $11$-organism, $5$-fold, $41$-column, $200$-permutation assay, the fourth $B^*_{Q6}$ mechanism is not translation accuracy, while the existence of a fourth residual component remains supported by the associated powered rows.
