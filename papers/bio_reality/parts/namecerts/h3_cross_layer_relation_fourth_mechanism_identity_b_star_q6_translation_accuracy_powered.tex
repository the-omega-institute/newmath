\paragraph{Mechanism discrimination.}
The nearby exclusion and partial-identification rows sharpen the interpretation. The mRNA-stability assay returned \texttt{fourth\_axis\_not\_mrna\_stability}: in yeast, $d_{\mathrm{opt\_halflife}}$ had low alignment with abundance $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$ under the fixed gates. The codon-pair assay returned \texttt{not\_codon\_pair} with $5$ folds, $50$ shuffle-null samples, and $41$ synonymous-design columns. Together with the present \texttt{not\_accuracy} call, the finite pattern is that the fourth component is not accounted for by translation accuracy, mRNA stability, or codon-pair structure in these assays, while elongation dwell remains a bounded partial route rather than a settled identity. A separate tRNA-supply result computed $40$ organisms out of $46$ requested, skipped $6$, used $1000$ permutations at $\alpha=0.05$, and found that $R$ sense codons are systematically tRNA-poor under GtRNAdb tGCN plus dos Reis wobble weights. That row supports a tRNA-supply route for $R$ avoidance, but it is not a translation-accuracy rescue.

\paragraph{Readout gate.}
For this chapter, a positive translation claim requires a nondegenerate survival entry $S_{f_3\text{-stress},T_j}$ for a bounded translation readout such as tAI, stAI, ribosome occupancy, or translation efficiency, after amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, CDS length, $M$-density, matched mRNA abundance, rank, and simpler-baseline controls. The present finite result supplies a negative identity decision for translation accuracy, not a measured translation-efficiency survival surface. A rank-degenerate all-$1.0$ matrix on too few organisms would remain a null boundary, because saturation of the fitted surface is not survival. Likewise, a modeled wobble or adaptation weight may identify where $f_3$ symmetry is broken, but it is not itself an observed ribosome-occupancy row.

\paragraph{Local boundary of interpretation.}
The claim is therefore narrow. The $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ $f_3$-stress coordinate remains a finite residual coordinate with a powered exclusion of translation accuracy as the fourth mechanism identity. No statement about actual translation output, protein abundance, folded structure, physical admissibility, molecular function, organismal phenotype, causation, or universal biological law follows from this chapter alone. Such a statement would need its own same-organism or same-CDS contact and its own controlled statistic. In particular, the conclusion \texttt{not\_accuracy} should be read as a refusal to name the fourth component by translation accuracy under the stated finite rows, not as proof that synonymous residual geometry has no translation-layer relevance.
