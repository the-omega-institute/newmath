The exclusion is coherent with the other fourth-mechanism audits. The codon-pair audit used $5$ folds, $41$ synonymous-design columns, $50$ shuffle nulls, and returned \texttt{not\_codon\_pair}. The finite picture is therefore not a generic failure of all translation-adjacent probes. \paragraph{Local non-promotion rule.}
The only positive statement licensed here is the finite exclusion of translation accuracy as the fourth-mechanism identity in the audited $B^*_{Q6}$ design. No measured translation survival follows from the accuracy row alone. No ribosome-occupancy realization, protein abundance, folded structure, physical admissibility, molecular function, system phenotype, mechanism, causation, or universality follows from this chapter unless a separate layer-matched contact supplies rows for that layer and an explicit controlled statistic is computed there. In particular, modeled tAI or stAI weights are bounded translation-weight readbacks, not observed ribosome footprints; matched mRNA abundance is a control surface for translation-efficiency residualization, not a translation output; and codon geometry is a finite coordinate description, not curated biological observation by itself.

\paragraph{Falsification and retained opening.}

Until such a row exists, the finite state is stable: the tested powered assay returns \texttt{not\_accuracy}, and downstream claims remain outside the warranted scope.
