\subsection{Motif Compression as an ORF-Eligibility Boundary Record}\label{subsec:h0-r-motif-compression}\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite motif packet.}
The packet named $h0.R.motif.compression$ records a finite codon-level compression fact, not a biological realization. Its verified motif row states that the set $R$ is exactly the following thirteen codons:
$$
\begin{aligned}
R=\{&\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\\
&\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UUA}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The same run reports no missing elements and no surplus elements: the finite comparison fields $\mathrm{no\_missing}$ and $\mathrm{no\_extra}$ are both empty. This makes the row a closed enumeration certificate for the motif compression itself. It does not say that any nucleotide interval in a genome, transcript, or experiment realizes the compressed pattern.

\paragraph{Relation to the larger codon census.}
A linked finite census verifies that the ambient motif universe $M$ has cardinality $20$ and is exactly the union named $WNR \cup CUN$ in the row. The listed elements are
$$
\begin{aligned}
M=\{&\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{ACA},\mathrm{ACG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\\
&\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\\
&\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the local compression row occupies $13$ of the $20$ codons in the verified ambient census. A second linked closure computation reports that $\mathrm{Sat}_{f3}(R)$ contains $18$ codons, namely all members of $M$ except $\mathrm{ACA}$ and $\mathrm{ACG}$, and that the recorded $f3$ boundary is empty. These numbers are useful for the internal geometry of the motif table: $13$ initial compressed members, $18$ members after the recorded saturation operation, and $20$ members in the ambient census. They are not observations of expression, translation, folding, molecular stability, or organismal role.

\paragraph{Quotient geometry.}
The same finite neighborhood records a quotient check for $M$. The verified $f$-vector for $M$ is $(20,38,26,8,1)$, while the quotient has $10$ vertices and $f$-vector $(10,14,6,1)$. The quotient representatives are ten binary rows, and the row count $10$ is explicitly reported as $\pi_M$ size. These data show that the motif census admits a finite combinatorial compression under the recorded quotient map. They do not add biological semantics to $R$; they only sharpen the internal account of how the codon enumeration has been grouped.

\paragraph{ORF-eligibility reading.}
The biological phrase attached to this packet is open-reading-frame eligibility from start and stop codon enumeration. In this chapter that phrase is deliberately read as a boundary test. A start--stop window can be enumerated as a candidate interval pattern, but the enumeration alone is not an observed open reading frame. The local carrier remains at the status of awaiting contact with sequence, transcript, or curated interval evidence. Consequently, the codon motif row may define what would be checked, but it does not by itself certify that a biological interval exists.

\paragraph{Separate finite contact rows.}
The linked start--stop-window row reports one loaded window and one classified window, with scope restricted to external-window start--stop enumeration and a snapshot date of $2026$-$05$-$25$. A stronger linked interval row reports a curated interval dataset with $168$ rows from RefSeq accession $\mathrm{NC\_000913.3}$, split into $72$ positive curated ORF intervals, $72$ matched non-ORF decoys, and $24$ boundary cases. In that row the positive accept rate is $1.0$ over $72$ positives, the matched decoy rejection rate is $1.0$ over $72$ decoys, and the exact one-sided binomial upper-tail value is $2.117582368135751\times 10^{-22}$ for both the positive and decoy tests. Three held-out splits are also recorded; each split has $24$ positives accepted at rate $1.0$ and $24$ decoys rejected at rate $1.0$. These finite contact rows are relevant evidence for their own interval task, but they remain distinct from the motif compression row. They do not retroactively turn the $13$-codon compression fact into translation, folding, physical admissibility, biological function, mechanism, or a universal biological law.

\paragraph{Local boundary.}
The present chapter therefore claims exactly this: the finite row for $h0.R.motif.compression$ verifies a precise motif union for $R$, with no missing or extra codons, inside a $20$-codon ambient census and alongside linked saturation and quotient summaries. The chapter does not claim that the motif is translated, that any protein folds from it, that the resulting molecule is physically admissible, that it has a biological function, that it explains a mechanism, or that it holds universally across biological systems. Any such statement would require an additional contact row that supplies the relevant observed biological interval and a protocol appropriate to that higher layer. The self-check row is consistent with this reading: it reports three checked gates and states that no codon geometry is promoted across biological layers.
