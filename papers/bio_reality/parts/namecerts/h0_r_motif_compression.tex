\paragraph{Finite motif row.}
The row $h0.R.motif.compression$ records a finite codon motif compression. Its verified motif set is
$$
\begin{aligned}

&UAA, UAG, UCA, UGA, UUA\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The certificate reports no missing elements and no additional elements: $\mathrm{no\_missing}=\varnothing$ and $\mathrm{no\_extra}=\varnothing$. Thus the row is not a statistical extrapolation from an open alphabet; it is a finite equality check over exactly thirteen displayed codons, verified in experiment run $338799579a87$ at $2026$-$06$-$01T07:28:18+00:00$.

\paragraph{Ambient codon packet.}

This pins down the local combinatorial position of $R$: saturation reaches eighteen codons, while the two codons $ACA$ and $ACG$ remain outside that saturated part of the row.

\paragraph{ORF-eligibility status.}
The biological object under discussion is open-reading-frame eligibility from start and stop codon enumeration. At the seed stage, the packet deliberately has no independent biological interval contact attached to the motif row itself. Its minimal biological readout is therefore pending: the carrier, distinctions, and readback are all held at the level of awaiting contact with a sequence, transcript, or curated interval annotation. The admissible statement is only that the displayed start/stop-window geometry supplies a finite boundary condition for a later biological test.

A separate row does show that the start/stop enumeration code can classify a supplied window. In $h2.orf.start\_stop.window\_eligibility$, one external window dataset was loaded, with $\mathrm{window\_count}=1$ and $1$ of $1$ input windows classified. Its declared scope is external window start/stop enumeration only, and the row explicitly records $\mathrm{no\_geometry\_to\_translation\_promotion}=\texttt{orf\_eligibility\_only}$. This means the classifier can act on an externally supplied window, not that the motif compression row itself has already observed a biological interval.

The matched decoy test rejects $72$ of $72$ decoys, giving reject rate $1.0$ with the same probability $2.117582368135751\times 10^{-22}$. The predeclared paired comparison records $72$ wins in $72$ pairs, again with one-sided binomial upper-tail probability $2.117582368135751\times 10^{-22}$.

The held-out stability check uses three splits. In each split there are $24$ positives and $24$ decoys; the positive accept rate is $1.0$, the decoy reject rate is $1.0$, and the split passes. The boundary-case guard records $24$ boundary cases with $0$ promoted, so the promoted fraction is $0.0$. These finite numbers support the curated interval-contact row, but they do not retroactively turn the motif compression row into an observed ORF claim unless the specific contact relation is attached to the packet being asserted.

\paragraph{Local boundary.}
The local rule is strict. Motif compression, saturation, quotient geometry, and start/stop-window enumeration may define a finite candidate boundary for ORF eligibility. They do not establish translation, folding, physical admissibility, biological function, mechanistic explanation, or a universal biological law. Any such promotion requires a separate contact that supplies the relevant biological observation. The self-check row reinforces this separation: it records $3$ checked gates and states that no codon geometry is promoted across biological layers. Consequently, the present chapter treats $h0.R.motif.compression$ as a finite codon-geometry certificate whose biological role is conditional on later interval evidence, not as a completed biological realization claim.
