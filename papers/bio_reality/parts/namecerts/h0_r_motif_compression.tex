\paragraph{Layer discipline.}
These contact rows show how the motif packet may meet biological data without changing what the motif packet itself claims.  The finite equality $R=\{AAA,AGA,AGG,AUA,CUA,CUC,CUG,CUU,UAA,UAG,UCA,UGA,UUA\}$ is a codon-level compression result.  The curated interval study is an outside annotation contact with its own frozen protocol: classifier rules were fixed before observation, held-out splits were fixed, and the recorded observation time was $2026$-$05$-$30T10{:}29{:}31+00{:}00$.  The protocol reports three held-out splits, each with $24$ positives accepted at rate $1.0$ and $24$ decoys rejected at rate $1.0$.  This supports ORF-window eligibility only where the curated interval data supply the interval.  It does not license a jump from motif compression to translation, protein folding, physical admissibility, biological function, molecular mechanism, or a general law about genomes.
