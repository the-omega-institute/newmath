In the finite row \texttt{h2.orf.start\_stop.window\_eligibility}, run \texttt{c26a0d6656f7} loaded one external window dataset, with \texttt{window\_count} equal to $1$ and \texttt{input\_windows} equal to $1$; that one input was classified by start/stop enumeration. The recorded scope is \texttt{external\_window\_start\_stop\_enumeration\_only}, and the row explicitly stores \texttt{no\_geometry\_to\_translation\_promotion} as \texttt{orf\_eligibility\_only}. Hence the admissible conclusion is only that the supplied window passed the declared boundary classifier. The conclusion is not that translation occurred, not that a protein folded, not that the product is physically admissible, and not that the interval has biological function.
