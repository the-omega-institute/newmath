\paragraph{Local non-promotion rule.}
The finite packet must be read with a strict layer boundary.  The motif equality for $R$, the twenty-codon carrier $M$, the eighteen-codon saturation, the quotient vectors, the one-window enumeration, and the $168$-row curated interval contact are compatible pieces of finite evidence for ORF-window eligibility under supplied interval data.  They do not imply that a ribosome translates the interval, that a folded protein exists, that the product is physically admissible, that any function is realized, that a mechanism has been identified, or that the result holds beyond the observed and curated contact scope.  Any such stronger assertion would require its own separately attached observation record and its own controls.
