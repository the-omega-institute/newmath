\subsection{Motif Compression as an ORF-Eligibility Boundary Record}

\paragraph{Finite codon row.}
The finite row attached to \texttt{h0.R.motif.compression} records a motif-compressed codon subset
$R$ and checks it against the declared motif union.  The verified row gives
$$
\begin{aligned}
R = \{&\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UUA}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The same verification reports no missing members and no extra members, so the row is an exact finite equality between the recorded set and the motif-union presentation.  This is a combinatorial equality on enumerated codon symbols only.  It is not, by itself, an observation of an open reading frame, a translated product, a folded molecule, a physically admissible object, a biochemical role, a molecular mechanism, or a universal biological regularity.

&\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\}.

$$
Within this ambient row, the motif-compressed set $R$ has $13$ listed codons.  A separate saturation row reports
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{Sat}_{f_3}(R) = \{&\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\\
&\mathrm{CUU},\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\},

$$

Thus the finite data distinguish the exact motif row, the $20$-element ambient codon row, and the $18$-element saturation row.  These distinctions are internal to the enumerated symbols and do not create a biological interval unless an independent biological contact supplies one.

\paragraph{ORF-eligibility boundary.}
The associated ORF-eligibility seed is deliberately weaker than an ORF claim.  Its own minimal readout is marked as awaiting contact with observed biological data, and its listed distinction is also awaiting such contact.  The local rule is this: start and stop codon enumeration may describe a window that could be tested for eligibility, but it cannot certify eligibility without an external sequence, transcript, or curated interval annotation.  Consequently, the packet may say that a boundary test is defined; it may not say that an open reading frame exists.

One linked start-stop enumeration row reports a single external window dataset with window count $1$, classified count $1$, and scope \texttt{external\_window\_start\_stop\_enumeration\_only} at snapshot date $2026$-$05$-$25$.  A stronger curated-interval contact row reports $168$ rows from NCBI RefSeq GenBank XML CDS intervals for \emph{Escherichia coli} K-$12$ MG1655, accession NC\_000913.3, at snapshot date $2026$-$05$-$30$.  That contact includes $72$ accepted positive intervals, $72$ rejected matched decoys, and $24$ boundary cases with $0$ promotions.  The positive exact one-sided binomial value is $2.117582368135751\cdot 10^{-22}$, the matched-decoy rejection exact one-sided binomial value is also $2.117582368135751\cdot 10^{-22}$, and the predeclared paired baseline sign-test advantage has $72$ wins out of $72$ pairs with the same reported value $2.117582368135751\cdot 10^{-22}$.  Three held-out splits are recorded; each has $24$ positives accepted at rate $1.0$ and $24$ decoys rejected at rate $1.0$.

\paragraph{Local verdict.}
For this row, the scientifically usable conclusion is narrow.  The motif-compressed set $R$ is exactly the verified $13$-codon set above, it sits inside the verified $20$-codon ambient enumeration $M$, and its linked saturation and quotient summaries have the finite values stated above.  These facts support a bounded ORF-eligibility probe once a separate curated interval contact is attached.  They do not establish translation, folding, physical admissibility, biological function, mechanism, or universality.  Any statement at one of those higher layers would require its own contact and cannot be inferred from the motif compression, quotient geometry, or start-stop enumeration alone.

\paragraph{Self-check.}
The packet-level self-check records three checked gates and states that no codon geometry is promoted across biological layers.  This chapter adopts that restriction as a local reading rule: finite codon geometry may organize a testable boundary, while biological realization belongs only to rows that explicitly carry an appropriate external contact.
