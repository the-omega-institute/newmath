\subsection{Motif compression and the ORF-eligibility boundary}
\label{sec:bioreality-h0-r-motif-compression}

\paragraph{Finite codon record.}
The finite row named \texttt{h0.R.motif.compression} records a closed codon packet rather than an observed biological outcome. Its verified motif union is
$R=\{\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UUA}\}$.
The audit reports no missing members and no extra members, so the row is exact at cardinality $13$. This exactness is the only content licensed by the motif-compression row itself: it identifies which codons lie in the compressed motif set and verifies that the enumerated set has not drifted from the declared list.

\paragraph{Ambient finite universe.}
The related row \texttt{h0.M.equals.WNR.CUN} fixes the surrounding codon universe used by the packet. It verifies a set $M$ of size $20$:
$\{\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{ACA},\mathrm{ACG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\}$.
Thus the motif row occupies $13$ of the $20$ available members in this finite universe. A second related saturation calculation gives $\mathrm{Sat}_{f3}(R)=M\setminus\{\mathrm{ACA},\mathrm{ACG}\}$, a set of $18$ codons, and records an empty $f3$ boundary. The companion decomposition row verifies
$M=\mathrm{Med}(R)\cup\{\mathrm{ACA}\}$
with the same $20$ codons. These are finite closure facts about codon enumeration and neighborhood completion; they are not observations of expression, translation, folding, stability, or cellular role.

\paragraph{Quotient geometry.}
The quotient row supplies a second finite check on the same ambient object. It reports an $M$ face vector $(20,38,26,8,1)$ and a quotient face vector $(10,14,6,1)$, with quotient size $10$. The verified quotient coordinate list contains ten rows, beginning with $(0,0,0,0,1)$ and ending with $(1,0,1,1,1)$. This gives a compact combinatorial description of how the finite codon universe is identified under the quotient map. The row therefore supports the statement that the codon packet has a reproducible finite geometry, but it still remains a geometry of codon labels and not a biological assay.

\paragraph{ORF-eligibility contact boundary.}
The conjectural biological layer attached to this row is open-reading-frame eligibility from start/stop codon enumeration. The seed statement deliberately withholds any such biological claim until an independent interval contact is attached. A separate start/stop-window row reports one loaded window, one classified input window, scope restricted to external-window start/stop enumeration, and the explicit classification tag \texttt{orf\_eligibility\_only}. That one-window contact can test an interval boundary; it does not turn motif compression into translation evidence.

A stronger curated-interval contact is also listed in the finite data. It loads $168$ curated interval rows from RefSeq accession \texttt{NC\_000913.3}. The row separates $72$ positive curated ORF intervals, $72$ matched non-ORF decoys, and $24$ boundary cases. The positives are accepted at rate $1.0$ with exact one-sided binomial upper-tail value $2.117582368135751\times 10^{-22}$; the matched decoys are rejected at rate $1.0$ with the same upper-tail value; the predeclared paired comparison records $72$ wins in $72$ pairs, again with upper-tail value $2.117582368135751\times 10^{-22}$. Three held-out splits are reported, each with $24$ positives accepted at rate $1.0$ and $24$ decoys rejected at rate $1.0$. Boundary cases have $n=24$ and promoted count $0$.

\paragraph{What the row can and cannot support.}
Taken together, these facts justify a narrow claim: the finite motif set $R$ is exactly the verified $13$-codon list, it sits inside a verified $20$-codon universe $M$, its related saturation contains $18$ codons, and independent interval rows can test start/stop-window eligibility when a curated biological interval is supplied. The self-check row records three checked gates and states that no codon geometry is promoted across biological layers. Consequently, no translation event, folded molecular structure, physical admissibility, biological function, mechanism, or universal genomic law follows from the motif row, the quotient row, or the start/stop enumeration alone. Such stronger assertions require their own contact with biological observation and must remain outside the scope of this packet unless that contact is separately supplied.
