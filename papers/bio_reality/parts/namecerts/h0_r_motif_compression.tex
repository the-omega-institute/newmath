\begin{aligned}

&UAA, UAG, UCA, UGA, UUA\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The audit reported no missing row and no additional row: $no\_missing=\varnothing$ and $no\_extra=\varnothing$.  Thus the packet is not a biological realization claim.  It is a precise finite compression claim about which codon rows are in the named motif union.

\paragraph{Ambient codon universe.}
The same boundary is sharpened by the finite ambient row $M$.  Experiment run $a53ef0f36703$ verified that $M$ has cardinality $20$ and is exactly the union named by $WNR$ and $CUN$:
$$
\begin{aligned}

&CUA, CUC, CUG, CUU, UAA, UAG, UCA, UCG,\\

The row $R$ is therefore a thirteen-element motif compression inside a twenty-element enumerated codon domain.  This is enough to support the finite statement that the listed motif rows are exactly present, but it is not enough to assert that any listed codon occurs in a transcript, occupies a genomic interval, is translated, folds into a protein product, satisfies physical constraints, has function, provides a mechanism, or holds universally across organisms.

\paragraph{ORF-eligibility contact discipline.}
The biological object attached to this row is open-reading-frame eligibility from start/stop codon enumeration.  The seed statement is intentionally conservative: a start/stop-window enumeration may define what would be checked, but it does not become an observed ORF-eligibility assertion until a separate curated sequence, transcript, or interval annotation supplies the tested interval.  The start/stop-window experiment $c26a0d6656f7$ loaded one external nucleotide-window record from the $2026$-$05$-$25$ snapshot and classified all $1$ of $1$ input windows by start/stop enumeration.  Its declared scope was $external\_window\_start\_stop\_enumeration\_only$, and its promotion field was $orf\_eligibility\_only$.  This keeps the row at the intended layer: interval eligibility under explicit contact, not translation or downstream biological realization.

\paragraph{Local boundary of interpretation.}
The biological conclusion is deliberately narrow.  The finite rows support a motif-compression statement for $R$, the twenty-row ambient enumeration for $M$, the listed saturation and quotient summaries, and, where the curated interval contact is invoked, ORF-eligibility performance on the specified external interval dataset.  They do not establish translation, folded molecular structure, physical admissibility, biological function, molecular mechanism, organism-wide universality, or a global law.  Any such stronger claim would require a new contact whose observed data are suitable for that higher layer.  The self-check audit $0f47d2f0cf41$ is consistent with this boundary: it recorded $3$ checked gates and stated that no codon geometry is promoted across biological layers.
