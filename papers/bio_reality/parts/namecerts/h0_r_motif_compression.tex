\subsection{Motif Compression as an ORF Eligibility Boundary Packet}
\label{sec:bioreality-h0-r-motif-compression}

\paragraph{Finite motif record.}
The packet attached to claim $h0.R.motif.compression$ records a finite codon-level compression event and keeps its biological reading deliberately narrow. The verified compressed set is
$$
\begin{aligned}
R = \{&AAA, AGA, AGG, AUA, CUA, CUC, CUG, CUU, UAA, UAG,\\
     &UCA, UGA, UUA\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The run reports no omitted element and no surplus element: the two mismatch lists are both empty. Thus the row does not merely name a motif family; it certifies that the thirteen displayed codons are exactly the recorded motif union for this claim. The experiment identifier for this finite equality is $338799579a87$, verified at $2026$-$06$-$01T07{:}28{:}18+00{:}00$.

\paragraph{Ambient codon set.}
The same packet is linked to a larger verified codon carrier $M$ of cardinality $20$. Its displayed equality is

\begin{aligned}

     &CUG, CUU, UAA, UAG, UCA, UCG, UGA, UGG, UUA, UUG\}.

The reported scalar $M\_cardinality\_equals\_20=20$ agrees with the listed size $20$. Relative to this ambient set, the motif set $R$ occupies $13$ of the $20$ displayed codons. The complementary codons inside $M$ are $AAG$, $ACA$, $ACG$, $AUG$, $UCG$, $UGG$, and $UUG$. This complement is not interpreted as a biological negative class; it is only the finite residue left after the verified motif compression is read inside the stated $20$-element carrier.

\paragraph{Saturation and boundary behavior.}
A linked saturation check gives an additional finite comparison. The reported value $Sat\_{f3}(R)$ is

\begin{aligned}

&UAA, UAG, UCA, UCG, UGA, UGG, UUA, UUG\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
This set has $18$ displayed codons. The same run states that $M$ is recovered as $Med(R)\cup\{ACA,ACG\}$, and the reported $f3$ boundary is empty. In concrete terms, the finite saturation expands the thirteen-codon motif record by adding $AAG$, $AUG$, $UCG$, $UGG$, and $UUG$, while $ACA$ and $ACG$ remain outside the displayed $Sat\_{f3}(R)$ list but appear in the reported recovery of $M$. These are finite set facts only. They do not assert that any codon in the list is translated in an organism, nor that any listed interval is expressed, folded, physically tolerated, functional, mechanistic, or universal.

The row data therefore gives a finite combinatorial shape for the motif environment, but this shape is not itself a biological observation. It is a coordinate-level organization of codon records. Any move from this organization to a statement about a real open reading frame requires a separate contact with sequence, transcript, or curated interval evidence.

\paragraph{ORF eligibility reading.}
The biological object named by the packet is open-reading-frame eligibility from start and stop codon enumeration. The seed status is therefore intentionally restrained: before contact, the packet says what kind of outside observation would be needed, not that an observed biological interval has already been found. The linked start-stop window check reports one input window and one classified window, with scope limited to external window start-stop enumeration and snapshot date $2026$-$05$-$25$. Its own output states that the classification remains at the $orf\_eligibility$ layer only. This is compatible with the motif compression record because both are finite eligibility filters rather than claims of realized translation.

\paragraph{Layer discipline.}
The curated interval contact strengthens only the local eligibility claim associated with that contact. It does not alter what the motif compression row alone can say. The motif row and its linked start-stop enumeration provide a finite screening context for ORF eligibility; they do not establish translation, folded molecular structure, physical admissibility, biological function, causal mechanism, or a global law across genomes. Those stronger statements require their own reality contacts and their own finite audits. The self-check row records this restriction explicitly: $3$ gates were checked, and the boundary text says that no codon geometry is promoted across biological layers. Consequently, the correct reading of $h0.R.motif.compression$ is a precise finite motif compression with an ORF-eligibility testing role, not a biological realization claim by itself.
