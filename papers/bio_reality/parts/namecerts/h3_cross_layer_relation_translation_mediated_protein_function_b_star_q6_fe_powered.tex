\paragraph{Finite carrier.}
This record tests a finite cross-layer carrier whose rows are indexed by residual codon coordinates $q_i$, possible translation readouts $T_j$, protein-omics readouts $P_k$, and bounded downstream readback slots $F_l$.  The tested coordinate side is not raw synonymous codon usage.  It is the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ amino-acid-quotient residual basis after composition control, with nine coordinates:
$$
\begin{aligned}
R = \{&K\_AAA,\ Arg\_AGR,\ Ile\_AUA,\ Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR,\ Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG,\\
&Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY,\ Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG,\ Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY,\ f3\_stress\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The run identified by $\mathrm{ebba7436a62a}$ joined real CDS codon counts, measured protein abundance, and binary laboratory knockout essentiality for two organisms.  Thus the finite data presently support a controlled residual association with an essentiality readout after protein-abundance residualization; they do not yet supply the stronger chain $q_i \to T_j \to P_k \to F_l$ because the measured translation-readout row $T_j$ is not present in the verified table.

\paragraph{Controls and rank condition.}
The control matrix $Z$ contains an intercept, $\log(\mathrm{CDS\ length\ in\ nt})$, the twenty amino-acid composition proportions $A,C,D,E,F,G,H,I,K,L,M,N,P,Q,R,S,T,V,W,Y$, GC3, the $M$-density baseline on the union support of the nine projected $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ vectors, and $\log_{10}(\mathrm{abundance\ ppm})$ as a measured protein-abundance control.  Hence the tested signal is a beyond-expression residual association rather than a protein-output mechanism.  In both organisms the projected residual design has full coordinate rank after these controls:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{rank}(\widetilde X) &= 9,\\
\#\{q_i\} &= 9.
\end{aligned}
$$
For \emph{Escherichia coli} K-12 MG1655, $\mathrm{rank}(Z)=24$ with $25$ listed control columns, residual degrees of freedom $3713$, explained residual-design energy $2.243840654793728$, and residual response energy $232.13814987247684$.  For \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}, $\mathrm{rank}(Z)=24$ with $25$ listed control columns, residual degrees of freedom $5487$, explained residual-design energy $2.741892921952373$, and residual response energy $826.488384003322$.  The degeneracy threshold used for rank-saturation refusal is $0.999999$, and both organism rows are marked rank-sufficient rather than rank-degenerate.

\paragraph{Observed finite rows.}
The essentiality join supplies both knockout classes in each organism.  In \emph{E. coli}, $3737$ genes were used, with $285$ essential and $3452$ nonessential genes, giving essential fraction $0.07626438319507627$; the abundance join reported $3739$ rows and $3737$ unique essentiality labels.  In yeast, $5511$ genes were used, with $1168$ essential and $4343$ nonessential genes, giving essential fraction $0.2119397568499365$; the abundance join reported $5815$ rows and $5511$ unique essentiality labels.  The positive-survival threshold for the partial-$R^2$ statistic is $10^{-12}$.

Against the binary essentiality readout, all nine residual coordinates are counted as surviving in both organism rows after the declared controls.  The fitted finite effects are small but positive:
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{FE}(\mathrm{E.\ coli}) &= 0.00966597112980509,\\
R^2_{FE}(\mathrm{S.\ cerevisiae}) &= 0.003317521425614316,\\
\#\{\mathrm{surviving\ coordinates}\} &= 9\quad\text{in each organism.}
\end{aligned}
$$
These numbers are read as controlled cross-sectional associations between $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates and laboratory knockout essentiality after amino-acid composition, GC3, length, $M$-density, and measured protein abundance have been removed by ordinary least squares residualization.  The binary phenotype is handled with a linear-probability approximation, so the statistic is a finite residual readback, not a generative model of essentiality.

\paragraph{Mediation boundary.}
The intended mediation test has the ordered form
$$
\begin{aligned}
q_i &\longrightarrow T_j \longrightarrow P_k,\\
q_i &\not\longrightarrow F_l\quad\text{until a separate function or phenotype assay is attached and evaluated.}
\end{aligned}
$$
The verified run reaches only a bounded downstream boundary: it demonstrates that the nine $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates have nonzero controlled association with a laboratory essentiality label after measured protein abundance is included as a control.  It does not show that a matched translation readout absorbs a direct $q_i \to P_k$ association, because no organism-matched ribosome-profiling or translation-efficiency row is part of the verified finite matrix.  It also does not show that protein abundance mediates function; abundance is used here as a confounding control.  A future mediation row would have to join the same CDS-level codon counts to measured translation efficiency, matched mRNA abundance, and matched proteomics, then show both survival of $q_i \to T_j$ and attenuation of $q_i \to P_k$ after conditioning on $T_j$.

\paragraph{Local no-promotion rule.}
The finite readback therefore has four local cases.  A coordinate may fail all controlled readouts; it may survive only a translation-layer readout; it may show a protein-omics association not explained by the measured translation row; or it may pass the ordered translation-mediated protein-omics test.  The present run belongs to a narrower powered boundary case: positive beyond-expression association with essentiality is observed, but translation mediation is not measured in the same matrix.  Consequently no translation mechanism, ribosome dynamics, folding pathway, physical admissibility, molecular function, organism-general law, or causal synonymous-edit effect follows from these rows.  Modeled tAI or stAI weights, contact names, codon geometry, residual notation, and small positive $R^2_{FE}$ entries are insufficient for such claims.  Each higher layer requires its own matched biological contact and its own controlled statistic before it can be asserted.
