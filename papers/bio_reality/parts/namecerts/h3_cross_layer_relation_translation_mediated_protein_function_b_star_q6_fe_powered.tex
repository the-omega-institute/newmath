\subsection{Translation-Conditioned Protein-Function Boundary for $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ Residuals}
\label{sec:bioreality-q6-translation-mediated-protein-function-boundary}

\paragraph{Finite carrier.}
This record is a finite cross-layer association surface for synonymous residual coordinates in coding sequences.  The carrier consists of per-gene cells indexed by a residual coordinate $q_i$, an admissible translation-side readout boundary $T_j$, an intermediate protein-omics readout $P_k$, and a bounded downstream readback surface $F_l$ only when a separate function or phenotype measurement is actually joined to the same biological rows.  In the present run the residual coordinate family has exactly nine coordinates,
$$
\begin{aligned}
Q = \{&K\_AAA,\ Arg\_AGR,\ Ile\_AUA,\ Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR,\ Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG,\\
&Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY,\ Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG,\ Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY,\ f3\_stress\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The coordinate source is recorded as the imported $q$-vector output of \texttt{run\_b\_star\_q6\_translation\_survival\_powered.py}.  These coordinates remain amino-acid-quotient residual coordinates: their interpretation is not a codon-count statement before adjustment, and it is not a protein-function statement after adjustment.  The run identified the claimed layer as \texttt{cross\_layer\_relation} and the conjectural boundary as \texttt{q6.translation-mediated.protein-function-boundary.cross-layer}.

\paragraph{Readout and controls.}
The realized finite readout in this run is a binary laboratory knockout essentiality label joined by protein identifier to real CDS codon counts and measured protein abundance.  The estimation is an ordinary least-squares linear-probability residualization of that binary readout.  The control block contains the intercept, $\log(\mathrm{cds\_len\_nt})$, the twenty amino-acid composition proportions $A,C,D,E,F,G,H,I,K,L,M,N,P,Q,R,S,T,V,W,Y$, GC3, the $M$-density baseline defined as the fraction of sense codons on the union support of the nine projected $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ vectors, and $\log_{10}(\mathrm{abundance\_ppm})$ as the measured protein-abundance control.  Protein abundance is therefore used here as a confounding control for a beyond-expression association, not as the effect whose mechanism is being established.

\paragraph{Organism rows.}
Two organism panels are present.  For \textit{Escherichia coli} K-12 MG1655, $3739$ abundance-joined rows were reported, $3737$ genes entered the finite regression surface, and the essentiality labels contained both classes: $285$ essential genes and $3452$ nonessential genes, with essential fraction $0.07626438319507627$.  The controlled design has $25$ control columns, $\operatorname{rank}(Z)=24$, $\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde X)=9$, residual degrees of freedom $3713$, explained energy $2.243840654793728$, and residual response energy $232.13814987247684$.  For \textit{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}, $5815$ abundance-joined rows were reported, $5511$ genes entered the finite regression surface, and the essentiality labels again contained both classes: $1168$ essential genes and $4343$ nonessential genes, with essential fraction $0.2119397568499365$.  Its controlled design has $25$ control columns, $\operatorname{rank}(Z)=24$, $\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde X)=9$, residual degrees of freedom $5487$, explained energy $2.741892921952373$, and residual response energy $826.488384003322$.  In both organisms the recorded degenerate-$R^2$ threshold is $0.999999$, the $q$-coordinate count is $9$, and the design is marked rank-sufficient for the powered finite-row check.

\paragraph{Finite statistic.}
Let $Y^{FE}$ denote the residualized binary essentiality readout, and let $\widetilde X_Q$ denote the nine-column residual coordinate matrix after projection away from the control block $Z$.  The finite association statistic is read as a controlled partial contribution of $\widetilde X_Q$ to $Y^{FE}$:
$$
\begin{aligned}
S^{FE}_{Q} &> 0 \quad \text{only when the controlled partial } R^2 \text{ exceeds } 10^{-12},\\
Z &= (1,\log(\mathrm{cds\_len\_nt}),\mathrm{AA}_{20},\mathrm{GC3},M\text{-density},\log_{10}(\mathrm{abundance\_ppm})).
\end{aligned}
$$
The recorded positive threshold is $10^{-12}$.  Under that threshold, all nine projected residual coordinates survive in both organism panels as a finite association with the essentiality surface: \textit{E. coli} has $R^2_{FE}=0.00966597112980509$ with $3737$ genes and nine surviving coordinates, while \textit{S. cerevisiae} has $R^2_{FE}=0.003317521425614316$ with $5511$ genes and nine surviving coordinates.  These are small, explicit, powered association values; their small size is part of the result and is not upgraded into a mechanism.

\paragraph{Cross-layer interpretation.}
The admissible reading is narrow.  The finite rows support the statement that the nine $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates have a nonzero controlled association with a laboratory knockout essentiality readout after amino-acid composition, GC3, length, $M$-density, and measured protein abundance have been residualized.  The data do not show that the coordinate changes translation; they do not show that translation mediates the essentiality association; and they do not show that a protein-omics intermediary absorbs a direct $q_i \to F_l$ contribution.  A true mediation call would require same-row translation measurements $T_j$, same-row protein-omics measurements $P_k$, and a demonstrated reduction of the direct $q_i \to P_k$ or $q_i \to F_l$ contribution after conditioning on the measured $T_j$ surface.

\paragraph{Local boundary.}
No claim of translation realization, ribosome dynamics, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, causal mechanism, organism-general law, or universal biological principle follows from these rows.  Modeled tAI or stAI weights, contact names, workflow readiness, codon geometry, and nonzero residual association are not interchangeable with measured ribosome occupancy, protein abundance mediation, structural order, function realization, or phenotype causation.  The present finite result remains a bounded beyond-expression essentiality association: it is cross-sectional, observational, computed with a linear-probability approximation to a binary phenotype, and explicitly separated from synonymous-edit causality, expression causality, translation causality, pathway mechanism, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, and universality.  A higher-layer statement becomes admissible only after a separate layer-matched contact supplies the corresponding measurement and the declared same-row statistic is computed under the stated controls.
