\subsection{Translation-mediated protein-function boundary for $B^*_{Q6}$ residuals}\label{subsec:bireality-q6-translation-mediated-protein-function-boundary}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite carrier.}
The finite table for this record is built from nine $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinates evaluated against organism-matched coding-sequence rows, amino-acid-composition controls, measured protein abundance, and a binary laboratory knockout essentiality readout. The coordinate list is
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathcal Q=\{&K\_AAA,\ Arg\_AGR,\ Ile\_AUA,\ Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR,\ Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG,\\
&Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY,\ Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG,\ Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY,\ f3\_stress\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The rows come from real CDS codon counts joined by protein identifier to measured protein abundance and essentiality labels. The computation was run as experiment $beb31e9e1742$. It does not attach a ribosome-profiling matrix, a same-row translation-efficiency matrix, a protein-function assay, or a perturbation-rescue panel. Consequently the finite object is a controlled association surface at a protein-function boundary, not a completed translation-mediated functional mechanism.

\paragraph{Controls and rank.}
For each organism, the residualization design used an intercept, $\log(\mathrm{CDS\ length\ in\ nt})$, the $20$ amino-acid composition proportions $A,C,D,E,F,G,H,I,K,L,M,N,P,Q,R,S,T,V,W,Y$, $GC3$, an $M$-density baseline computed as the fraction of sense codons on the union support of the nine projected $B^*_{Q6}$ vectors, and $\log_{10}(\mathrm{abundance\_ppm})$ as a measured protein-abundance control. Thus the reported association is beyond amino-acid composition, sequence length, $GC3$, the declared $M$ baseline, and measured abundance. In \emph{Escherichia coli} K-12 MG1655, the control matrix had $25$ columns, rank $24$, residual degrees of freedom $3713$, residual response energy $232.13814987247684$, and the residualized $B^*_{Q6}$ coordinate matrix retained rank $9$ across the nine coordinates. The explained energy for the residualized coordinate block was $2.243840654793728$, below the degeneracy threshold $0.999999$ for rank-saturation refusal and marked rank-sufficient. In \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}, the same $25$-column control design had rank $24$, residual degrees of freedom $5487$, residual response energy $826.488384003322$, residualized coordinate rank $9$, and explained energy $2.741892921952373$, again with rank sufficiency recorded for all nine coordinates.

\paragraph{Observed finite rows.}
The essentiality joins used $3737$ \emph{E. coli} genes after abundance joining, with $285$ essential and $3452$ nonessential labels, giving essential fraction $0.07626438319507627$. The yeast table used $5511$ genes, with $1168$ essential and $4343$ nonessential labels, giving essential fraction $0.2119397568499365$. Both organisms contained both essentiality classes. The computed finite response was a binary linear-probability residualization of lab knockout essentiality. After the declared controls, all nine residual coordinates were counted as surviving under the positive partial-$R^2$ threshold $10^{-12}$ in both organisms. The finite essentiality association strengths were
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{FE}(\mathrm{E.\ coli\ K12\ MG1655})&=0.00966597112980509,\\
R^2_{FE}(\mathrm{S.\ cerevisiae})&=0.003317521425614316.
\end{aligned}
$$
These values are small, positive, and powered by nondegenerate residual rank. They should be read as controlled cross-sectional associations between $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinates and a lab knockout phenotype after abundance residualization, not as direct evidence that codon residuals cause essentiality.

\paragraph{Layer separation.}
The intended mediation chain has the form $q_i\to T_j\to P_k\to F_l$, where $q_i$ is a residual codon coordinate, $T_j$ is a matched translation readout, $P_k$ is a protein-omics intermediary, and $F_l$ is a function or phenotype readout admitted only after an independent functional contact is supplied. The present finite table does not contain $T_j$ rows from ribosome profiling or translation efficiency, and it does not contain a measured $q_i\to T_j\to P_k$ attenuation statistic. It also does not contain a separate function assay, phenotype perturbation panel, folded-structure measurement, physical assay, localization assay, stability assay, or pathway mechanism readout. Therefore the observed $R^2_{FE}$ entries cannot be promoted to translation realization, protein mediation, folded structure, physical admissibility, biological function, mechanism, or organism-general law.

\paragraph{Readback verdict.}
The finite readback is a boundary-positive but mechanism-null result. It supports the statement that, in two rank-sufficient organism tables, all nine listed $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinates retain a small positive association with binary essentiality after amino-acid composition, length, $GC3$, $M$-density, and measured protein-abundance controls. It does not support the stronger statement that translation mediates the association, because no same-row translation readout has been attached. It also does not support a function-realization claim, because essentiality here is an observational knockout label and not a molecular function contact. The admissible conclusion is restricted to a controlled protein-function boundary: the residual coordinates may be carried forward as candidates for later translation-readout and protein-omics mediation testing, while every claim about translation, folding, physical admissibility, function, mechanism, or universality remains blocked until a separate layer-matched contact supplies the relevant finite rows and statistic.
