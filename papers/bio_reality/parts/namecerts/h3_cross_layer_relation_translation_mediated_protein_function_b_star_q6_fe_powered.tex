\subsection{Rank-powered $B^\ast_{Q6}$ residual association at the protein-function boundary}\label{sec:bior-reality-q6-translation-mediated-protein-function-boundary}\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Object and finite carrier.} This record concerns a finite gene-row experiment in which nine $B^\ast_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates are evaluated against a laboratory knockout essentiality readout after amino-acid composition and measured protein abundance have already been held fixed. The biological object is not a free codon-usage summary. It is the amino-acid-quotient residual profile left after coding sequences have been represented by real CDS codon counts and then compared under a control surface containing length, amino-acid composition, $GC3$, an $M$-density baseline, and measured protein abundance. The finite coordinate list is
$$
\begin{aligned}
Q=\{&K\_AAA,\ Arg\_AGR,\ Ile\_AUA,\ Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR,\ Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG,\\
&Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY,\ Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG,\ Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY,\ f3\_stress\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The readout in the computed rows is a binary laboratory knockout essentiality phenotype, joined by protein identifier to CDS codon counts and measured protein abundance. Thus the observed response is a phenotype row, but the fitted association is deliberately narrower: it is a codon residual association after amino-acid and abundance residualization, not a causal statement about editing, expression, translation, protein mechanism, folded state, physical admissibility, function, or organism-general law.

\paragraph{Control surface.} For each organism, the nuisance matrix $Z$ contains an intercept, $\log(\mathrm{CDS\ length\ in\ nucleotides})$, the twenty amino-acid composition proportions $A,C,D,E,F,G,H,I,K,L,M,N,P,Q,R,S,T,V,W,Y$, $GC3$, the $M$-density baseline defined as the fraction of sense codons on the union support of the nine projected $B^\ast_{Q6}$ vectors, and $\log_{10}(\mathrm{abundance\_ppm})$ as a measured protein-abundance control. The protein-abundance term is part of the confounding adjustment, not a protein-function conclusion. In both organisms the control table has $25$ columns and numerical rank $24$, so the finite-row calculation is not a three-organism saturated surface. The residual design keeps all nine $q$ coordinates available after projection away from $Z$:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{rank}(\widetilde X) &= 9,\\
|Q| &= 9,\\
\tau_{\mathrm{degenerate}} &= 0.999999.
\end{aligned}
$$
The positive-entry threshold used for the reported partial residual association is $10^{-12}$. No reported p-values, likelihood-ratio values, lambda parameters, or hit-rate summaries accompany this run; the scalar evidence available here is the finite rank, residual degrees of freedom, explained energy, residual response energy, row counts, essentiality fractions, and partial $R^2$ values.

\paragraph{Escherichia coli row block.} In the \emph{Escherichia coli} K-12 MG1655 block, $3739$ abundance-joined records are reported, of which $3737$ genes enter the fitted table with $3737$ unique essentiality labels. The essentiality split is $285$ essential and $3452$ nonessential genes, giving essential fraction $0.07626438319507627$. After projection against the $25$-column control surface, the residual degrees of freedom are $3713$, the response residual energy is $232.13814987247684$, and the nine-coordinate residual design has explained energy $2.243840654793728$. The fitted finite association has
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{FE}(\mathrm{E.\ coli}) &= 0.00966597112980509,\\
\mathrm{surviving\ coordinate\ count} &= 9,\\
\mathrm{residual\ df\ after\ }Z &= 3713.
\end{aligned}
$$
This is a powered beyond-abundance association surface for the nine residual coordinates in that organism. The value is small in absolute response-energy terms and should be read exactly at that scale: it records residual association with the knockout binary after the declared controls, not a molecular mechanism and not a direct translation observation.

\paragraph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae row block.} In the \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae} block, $5815$ abundance-joined records are reported, while $5511$ genes are used in the fitted table with $5511$ unique essentiality labels. The split is $1168$ essential and $4343$ nonessential genes, for essential fraction $0.2119397568499365$. The same control architecture has numerical rank $24$, leaves all nine $B^\ast_{Q6}$ residual coordinates rank-available, and gives $5487$ residual degrees of freedom. The response residual energy is $826.488384003322$ and the explained energy is $2.741892921952373$. The fitted finite association is
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{FE}(\mathrm{S.\ cerevisiae}) &= 0.003317521425614316,\\
\mathrm{surviving\ coordinate\ count} &= 9,\\
\mathrm{residual\ df\ after\ }Z &= 5487.
\end{aligned}
$$
The yeast block therefore supplies a second rank-sufficient organism-row table for the same nine-coordinate residual design, again with a small but positive beyond-abundance residual association. The calculation does not identify which biological route produces the remaining association, and it does not license promotion to translation, protein function, phenotype mechanism, or universality.

\paragraph{Boundary of the cross-layer relation.} The intended layer relation is ordered as $q_i \to T_j \to P_k \to F_l$, but the computed finite rows in this record do not instantiate the whole chain. The available response is lab knockout essentiality with measured protein abundance included as a control. A matched translation readout $T_j$ is named as the required route for later mediation tests, and protein-omics $P_k$ is represented here only by measured abundance used in residualization. No external function or phenotype contact is attached as a separate functional assay, perturbation assay, physical assay, localization assay, folded-structure measurement, or pathway mechanism readout. Consequently $F_l$ remains null in this record. A direct $q_i \to F_l$ interpretation is not admitted, and neither is a direct $q_i \to P_k$ mechanism interpretation without first showing a same-row translation step and attenuation of the residual-to-protein contribution after conditioning on that step.

\paragraph{Four-case readback.} The finite evidence supports a local four-case discriminant for later joined matrices. Case A is no survival: every controlled residual score is null or nonpositive. Case B is translation-only survival: a coordinate survives against a translation readout but has no downstream protein-omics continuation. Case C is protein-omics survival not mediated by the measured translation readout: a downstream association remains after controls, but the measured translation step fails to absorb it. Case D is translation-mediated protein-omics survival: the same coordinate first survives against $T_j$, and conditioning on $T_j$ reduces the direct $q_i\to P_k$ contribution. The present essentiality computation is not itself a completed case-D mediation matrix, because it lacks same-CDS joined translation rows and a demonstrated $q_i\to T_j\to P_k$ attenuation surface. It is instead a rank-powered beyond-abundance finite-row contact at the protein-function boundary: it shows that all nine $B^\ast_{Q6}$ residual coordinates remain estimable and yield positive residual association against the binary knockout readout under the declared controls in two organisms.

\paragraph{Cannot-claim boundary.} The calculation is observational and cross-sectional. The binary readout is handled through an ordinary least-squares linear-probability residualization, so the positive entries are finite-row associations rather than causal estimates. From these numbers alone one may not claim synonymous-edit causality, translation realization, ribosome occupancy, folding, protein physical admissibility, molecular function, phenotype realization, pathway mechanism, or a universal biological rule. Modeled tAI or stAI couplings, contact names, coordinate geometry, and survival notation do not supply those layers. Each higher layer requires its own matched data contact and its own controlled statistic. In particular, function remains unentered until a separate function or phenotype readout is attached after a positive translation-readout step and a demonstrated translation-conditioned protein-omics mediation test.
