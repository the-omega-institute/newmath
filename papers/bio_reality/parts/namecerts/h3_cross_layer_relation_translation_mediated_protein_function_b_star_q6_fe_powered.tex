\subsection{Rank-powered essentiality contact for the $B^*_{Q6}$ protein-function boundary}
\label{sec:bioreality-q6-translation-mediated-protein-function-fe-powered}

\paragraph{Finite carrier.}
This record concerns a finite matrix whose rows are coding genes and whose coordinate side is the nine-dimensional $B^*_{Q6}$ residual basis. The computed residual coordinates are $K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $Leu_{CUN\,vs\,UUR}$, $Leu_{UUA\,vs\,UUG}$, $Ser_{UCR\,vs\,AGY}$, $Ser_{UCA\,vs\,UCG}$, $Thr_{ACR\,vs\,ACY}$, and $f_3\text{-}stress$. Each coordinate is retained only as an amino-acid-quotient codon residual: it is not a protein readout, not a translation readout, and not a function measurement by itself. The attached finite rows join real CDS codon counts, measured protein abundance, and a laboratory knockout essentiality label. Thus the observed surface is a residual association between $q_i$ and essentiality after composition and abundance controls, not a direct realization of translation, folding, physical admissibility, or biological function.

\paragraph{Controls and rank condition.}
The residualization design used an intercept, $\log(\mathrm{cds\_len\_nt})$, the twenty amino-acid composition proportions $A,C,D,E,F,G,H,I,K,L,M,N,P,Q,R,S,T,V,W,Y$, GC3, the $M$-density baseline on the union support of the nine projected $B^*_{Q6}$ vectors, and $\log_{10}(\mathrm{abundance\_ppm})$ as a measured protein-abundance control. The abundance term is essential to the interpretation: a surviving codon residual is only a beyond-expression association after this control, not a protein-output mechanism. For \emph{Escherichia coli} K-12 MG1655 the control design had $25$ columns, $\operatorname{rank}(Z)=24$, $\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde X)=9$, residual degrees of freedom $3713$, explained energy $2.243840654793728$, and residual response energy $232.13814987247684$. For \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae} the same $25$-column control design had $\operatorname{rank}(Z)=24$, $\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde X)=9$, residual degrees of freedom $5487$, explained energy $2.741892921952373$, and residual response energy $826.488384003322$. Both organisms therefore pass the finite rank-sufficiency check for the nine residual coordinates under the stated control set.

\paragraph{Observed finite rows.}
The \emph{E. coli} joined table used $3737$ genes, with $285$ essential and $3452$ nonessential genes; the essential fraction was $0.07626438319507627$, and both essentiality classes were present. The \emph{S. cerevisiae} joined table used $5511$ genes, with $1168$ essential and $4343$ nonessential genes; the essential fraction was $0.2119397568499365$, again with both classes present. The abundance joins reported $3739$ rows for \emph{E. coli} and $5815$ rows for \emph{S. cerevisiae}, while the modeled binary response analysis retained the gene counts just listed after identifier and row filtering. The finite-row conclusion is therefore organism-scoped and table-scoped: the record speaks about these joined gene panels, not about all organisms or all coding sequences.

\paragraph{Essentiality residual readback.}
Using an ordinary least-squares linear-probability residualization for the binary knockout label, all nine $B^*_{Q6}$ coordinates produced positive entries above the stated positive partial-$R^2$ threshold $10^{-12}$ in both organisms. The finite residual scores were $R^2_{FE}=0.00966597112980509$ for \emph{E. coli} and $R^2_{FE}=0.003317521425614316$ for \emph{S. cerevisiae}. In the notation of this chapter, the only supported statement is the following bounded one:
$$
\begin{aligned}
&\text{real CDS codon counts} + \text{protein abundance control} + \text{essentiality label} \\
&\qquad \leadsto \text{positive residual association of the nine } B^*_{Q6} \text{ coordinates with essentiality}.
\end{aligned}
$$
This is not a mediated translation result, because the same finite contact does not yet include an observed or explicitly bounded translation readout $T_j$ that survives before the protein or phenotype surface is considered. It is also not a protein-function result, because the readout is a laboratory knockout binary label analyzed observationally, and no independent molecular-function assay, perturbation rescue, folded-structure assay, physical-stability assay, localization assay, or pathway mechanism contact is attached here.

\paragraph{Layer verdict.}
The chapter therefore admits a guarded cross-layer boundary. The data are strong enough to record a rank-powered, abundance-controlled association between the nine $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinates and essentiality in two organism-specific finite panels. The data are not strong enough to promote that association to codon causality, synonymous-edit causality, translation realization, ribosome dynamics, protein abundance mediation, folded structure, physical admissibility, biological function, organism-general law, or mechanism. A genuine translation-mediated protein-function claim would require a same-row chain in which $q_i \to T_j$ first survives against the amino-acid, GC3, length, $M$-density, organism, and mRNA controls, and in which conditioning on $T_j$ then absorbs or sharply reduces the direct $q_i \to P_k$ contribution before any separate function or phenotype contact is interpreted. Until those contacts exist on one finite joined panel, this record remains a bounded essentiality-association result with an explicit no-promotion boundary.
