\subsection{A powered $B^{*}_{Q6}$ boundary for residual essentiality association}
\label{sec:bq6-fe-powered-boundary}

\paragraph{Finite row object.}
This record studies a finite cross-layer table whose row variables are $B^{*}_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates and whose present readout is a laboratory knockout essentiality binary joined to real coding-sequence codon counts and measured protein abundance.  The coordinate side contains exactly nine residual directions:
$K_{\mathrm{AAA}}$, $\mathrm{Arg}_{\mathrm{AGR}}$, $\mathrm{Ile}_{\mathrm{AUA}}$, $\mathrm{Leu}_{\mathrm{CUN\;vs\;UUR}}$, $\mathrm{Leu}_{\mathrm{UUA\;vs\;UUG}}$, $\mathrm{Ser}_{\mathrm{UCR\;vs\;AGY}}$, $\mathrm{Ser}_{\mathrm{UCA\;vs\;UCG}}$, $\mathrm{Thr}_{\mathrm{ACR\;vs\;ACY}}$, and $f_{3}$-stress.  These coordinates are treated as amino-acid-quotient residual quantities: they are not amino-acid composition, and their admissible test is only after the amino-acid composition columns have been included in the control design.  The row object was computed in run $\mathrm{beb31e9e1742}$ and verified at 2026-06-12T11:06:10+00:00.

\paragraph{Control surface.}
For each organism, the residualization design contains an intercept, $\log(\mathrm{CDS\ length\ in\ nt})$, the twenty amino-acid composition proportions $A,C,D,E,F,G,H,I,K,L,M,N,P,Q,R,S,T,V,W,Y$, $\mathrm{GC3}$, the $M$-density baseline formed as the fraction of sense codons on the union support of the nine projected $B^{*}_{Q6}$ vectors, and $\log_{10}(\mathrm{abundance\ ppm})$ as a measured protein-abundance control.  Thus the finite statistic is a beyond-expression association test, not a direct expression test.  In both organisms the control matrix has $25$ columns and rank $24$, while the residualized $B^{*}_{Q6}$ coordinate matrix has rank $9$.

$$
\begin{aligned}
Z &= (1,\log L,\mathrm{AA}_{20},\mathrm{GC3},M\text{-density},\log_{10} A_{\mathrm{protein}}),\\
X &= (q_{1},\ldots,q_{9}),\\
S^{FE} &= R^{2}(Y_{\mathrm{essential}}\mid Z,X)-R^{2}(Y_{\mathrm{essential}}\mid Z).
\end{aligned}
$$

The positivity cutoff for the partial signal is $10^{-12}$.  The degeneracy threshold for rejecting rank-saturated fits is $0.999999$, and both organism rows are marked rank-sufficient rather than rank-degenerate.

\paragraph{Layer interpretation.}
The present readout is a binary lab knockout phenotype, not a direct translation measurement.  The calculation also includes protein abundance as a confounding control; it does not show that a translation readout mediates a protein-omics readout.  A translation-mediated chain would require same-row quantities $q_i\to T_j\to P_k$, where $T_j$ is a measured or explicitly bounded translation readout and $P_k$ is a protein-omics intermediary, followed by attenuation of the direct $q_i\to P_k$ term after conditioning on $T_j$.  This finite table instead supplies a stricter boundary statement: after abundance control, a small but positive residual association with essentiality remains in both organisms, and that association is not promoted into a molecular route.

\paragraph{Four-case readback.}
The admissible readback has four local outcomes.  First, if no $q_i$ survives the controlled readout, the table is null for this boundary.  Second, if $q_i$ survives only against a translation readout $T_j$, the result is confined to translation-layer association.  Third, if $q_i$ predicts a protein-omics readout $P_k$ but the signal is not absorbed by a measured $T_j$, the result is a protein-omics association without demonstrated translation mediation.  Fourth, if $q_i\to T_j$ survives and conditioning on $T_j$ reduces the $q_i\to P_k$ term, the record may state translation-conditioned protein-omics propagation.  The current finite rows belong to an essentiality-after-abundance-control boundary, not to the fourth case, because no matched translation readout and no $q_i\to T_j\to P_k$ mediation surface are present.

\paragraph{Local cannot-claim boundary.}
No translation realization follows from these rows unless a separate ribosome-profiling, translation-efficiency, or explicitly bounded translation-weight contact supplies the translation readout and the controlled statistic is recomputed.  No folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, mechanism, organism-general rule, or universality follows from the positive $S^{FE}$ values.  Essentiality is a phenotype-level laboratory binary, the model is cross-sectional and observational, and the residualization is a linear-probability approximation.  The finite result is therefore a powered two-organism association statement: all nine listed $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates retain positive beyond-expression association with knockout essentiality in the two tested organisms, but the record remains blocked from translation, protein-mediation, structure, function, causal, mechanistic, and universal interpretations until separate layer-matched contacts supply those measurements.
