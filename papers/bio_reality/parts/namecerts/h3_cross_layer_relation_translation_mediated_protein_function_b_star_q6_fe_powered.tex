\subsection{Powered essentiality readback for the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ protein-function boundary}\label{subsec:bioreality-q6-fe-powered-protein-function-boundary}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite carrier.}
This record uses a finite table whose rows are coding genes and whose columns join four kinds of bounded coordinates: $q_i$ coordinates from the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ synonymous residual basis, organism-specific control columns $Z$, measured protein-abundance controls, and a binary laboratory knockout readout. The nine residual coordinates are
$K\_AAA$, $Arg\_AGR$, $Ile\_AUA$, $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$, $Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG$, $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$, $Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG$, $Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY$, and $f3\_stress$. They are not amino-acid-composition columns; the fitting table first removes the amino-acid quotient, so a surviving $q_i$ records residual synonymous structure after the 20 amino-acid proportions have already been included in $Z$.

\paragraph{Observed rows and controls.}
The powered run $ebba7436a62a$ joins real CDS codon counts, essentiality labels, and measured protein abundance for two organisms. In \emph{Escherichia coli} K--12 MG1655, $3737$ genes enter the fitted table, with $285$ essential genes and $3452$ nonessential genes, giving an essential fraction of $0.07626438319507627$. The protein-abundance join reports $3739$ rows before the final fitted gene count. In \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}, $5511$ genes enter the fitted table, with $1168$ essential genes and $4343$ nonessential genes, giving an essential fraction of $0.2119397568499365$; the abundance join reports $5815$ rows. Both organisms contain both essentiality classes, so the binary readout is not class-degenerate.

\paragraph{Residualization model.}
For each organism the control design contains an intercept, $\log(\mathrm{cds\_len\_nt})$, the 20 amino-acid composition proportions $A,C,D,E,F,G,H,I,K,L,M,N,P,Q,R,S,T,V,W,Y$, $GC3$, the $M$-density baseline on the union support of the nine projected $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ vectors, and $\log_{10}(\mathrm{abundance\_ppm})$. The nominal control-column count is $25$, while the realized control rank is $24$ in both fitted organisms. The residualized $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ design has rank $9$ in both organisms, matching the nine tested coordinates. The residual degrees of freedom after $Z$ are $3713$ for \emph{E. coli} and $5487$ for yeast. The corresponding residual readout energies are $232.13814987247684$ and $826.488384003322$, and the explained energies assigned to the $q_i$ block are $2.243840654793728$ and $2.741892921952373$.

\paragraph{Finite statistic.}
The fitted readout is a binary linear-probability residualization of laboratory knockout essentiality, not a mechanistic assay. With the positive partial-$R^2$ threshold fixed at $10^{-12}$, all nine tested residual coordinates have positive entries in both organisms. The finite essentiality readback is therefore
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{FE}(\mathrm{E.\ coli}) &= 0.00966597112980509,\\
R^2_{FE}(\mathrm{S.\ cerevisiae}) &= 0.003317521425614316,\\
\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde X_{Q6}) &= 9,\\
\operatorname{rank}(Z) &= 24.
\end{aligned}
$$
The result is powered in the narrow rank sense: each organism has enough residual design rank to test the nine $q_i$ columns after the declared controls, and the degenerate-$R^2$ threshold used to reject saturated fits is $0.999999$. The observed $R^2_{FE}$ values are small, but they are nonzero finite-row associations after amino-acid composition and measured protein abundance have been controlled.

\paragraph{Readback classification.}
This table does not yet certify a translation-mediated protein-function chain. It supplies a downstream essentiality association after protein abundance has been included as a confounding control. The appropriate local readback is: positive beyond-abundance essentiality association for the nine-coordinate $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ block in two powered organism tables. It is not a $q_i \to T_j$ translation survival result, because no measured ribosome-profiling or translation-efficiency column is present in the fitted statistic. It is not a $q_i \to T_j \to P_k$ mediation result, because no same-gene translation readout has been shown to absorb a direct $q_i \to P_k$ association. It is also not a molecular function realization, because laboratory essentiality is a phenotype-level binary knockout label rather than a direct function assay for a protein product.

\paragraph{Boundary.}
The finite row evidence may be carried only as a bounded cross-layer association: residual synonymous coordinates have small positive association with laboratory essentiality after amino-acid composition, $GC3$, coding length, $M$-density, and measured protein abundance controls. No translation realization, ribosome mechanism, protein folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, organism-general rule, or causal biological mechanism follows from this table alone. Each of those claims requires its own matched measurement layer: a translation contact for $q_i \to T_j$, a protein-omics mediation table for $q_i \to T_j \to P_k$, and a separate function or phenotype assay before any function-realization statement is licensed. The present record therefore strengthens the protein-function boundary by showing that the finite design is not rank-degenerate and that the residual block can be tested, while keeping the higher-layer interpretation null until the missing contacts are attached.
