\paragraph{Finite carrier.}
The record is a finite cross-layer assay over $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates and a bounded downstream readback.  The coordinate side contains nine projected residual directions: $K_{\mathrm{AAA}}$, $\mathrm{Arg}_{\mathrm{AGR}}$, $\mathrm{Ile}_{\mathrm{AUA}}$, $\mathrm{Leu}_{\mathrm{CUN\ vs\ UUR}}$, $\mathrm{Leu}_{\mathrm{UUA\ vs\ UUG}}$, $\mathrm{Ser}_{\mathrm{UCR\ vs\ AGY}}$, $\mathrm{Ser}_{\mathrm{UCA\ vs\ UCG}}$, $\mathrm{Thr}_{\mathrm{ACR\ vs\ ACY}}$, and $f_3$-stress.  These are not amino-acid composition variables: each $q_i$ is interpreted only after quotienting against the amino-acid profile and the declared sequence controls.  The finite rows used here join real coding-sequence codon counts, a laboratory essentiality label, and measured protein abundance; they do not yet supply a matched ribosome-profiling translation readout.  For this reason the observed surface is written as a bounded $S^{FE}$ association surface rather than as a completed $q_i \to T_j \to P_k \to F_l$ mediation chain.

\paragraph{Control surface.}
The residualization uses an intercept, $\log(\mathrm{CDS\ length})$, the twenty amino-acid composition proportions $A,C,D,E,F,G,H,I,K,L,M,N,P,Q,R,S,T,V,W,Y$, $GC3$, an $M$-density baseline on the union support of the nine $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ directions, and $\log_{10}(\mathrm{abundance\ ppm})$ as a measured protein-abundance control.  Thus the tested signal is beyond amino-acid composition, nucleotide-position composition, sequence length, the declared codon-support baseline, and measured protein abundance.  The two organism blocks are rank-powered after these controls: in \textit{Escherichia coli} K-12 MG1655, $\operatorname{rank}(Z)=24$, $\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde X)=9$, the residual degrees of freedom after $Z$ are $3713$, the residual essentiality energy is $232.13814987247684$, and the explained energy is $2.243840654793728$.  In \textit{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}, $\operatorname{rank}(Z)=24$, $\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde X)=9$, the residual degrees of freedom after $Z$ are $5487$, the residual essentiality energy is $826.488384003322$, and the explained energy is $2.741892921952373$.  Both blocks use $25$ control columns and the same degeneracy cutoff $0.999999$.

\paragraph{Readback verdict.}
The finite evidence supports a narrow association statement: after the declared controls, the nine-coordinate $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual panel carries nonzero predictive association with laboratory knockout essentiality in two powered organism blocks.  The effect sizes are small, and that smallness is part of the result rather than a defect to be hidden.  The binary phenotype is modeled by an ordinary least-squares linear-probability residualization, so the output is a controlled cross-sectional association with essentiality, not a causal synonymous-edit experiment.  Protein abundance is included here as a confounding control; it is not used as a demonstrated mediator from codon residuals to function.

\paragraph{Layer boundary.}
This chapter therefore stops at a protein-function boundary readback.  It does not establish translation realization, because the finite rows used here do not contain a matched $q_i\to T_j$ ribosome or translation-efficiency survival step.  It does not establish protein mediation, because conditioning on a measured translation readout has not yet been shown to absorb a direct $q_i\to P_k$ association.  It does not establish folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, mechanism, phenotype causality, or an organism-general law.  Any such statement would require its own layer-matched measurement: a direct translation readout for translation, protein-omics mediation rows for $q_i\to T_j\to P_k$, structural or physical assays for folded or admissible state, and a separate function or phenotype contact before function-realization language is allowed.

\paragraph{Local no-promotion rule.}
The admissible conclusion is finite and conditional: $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates survive as beyond-composition and beyond-abundance associations with laboratory essentiality in the two recorded organism panels.  The same numbers do not license a shortcut from codon residual geometry to translation mechanism or biological function.  A future completed mediation record must first attach matched translation rows and then test whether the same $q_i$ coordinates survive into $T_j$ and whether $T_j$ attenuates the downstream $P_k$ term before any higher-layer functional reading is considered.
