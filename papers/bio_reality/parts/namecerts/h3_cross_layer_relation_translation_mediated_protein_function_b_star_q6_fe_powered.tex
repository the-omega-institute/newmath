\subsection{Powered residual association at the protein-function boundary}
\label{sec:bioreality-q6-translation-mediated-protein-function-boundary}

\paragraph{Finite row object.}
This record tests a bounded cross-layer question for $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates.  The finite carrier is the per-gene matrix whose coordinate side consists of nine residual directions, namely $K\_AAA$, $Arg\_AGR$, $Ile\_AUA$, $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$, $Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG$, $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$, $Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG$, $Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY$, and $f3\_stress$.  These directions are not treated as amino-acid composition, because the fit first removes the amino-acid composition surface.  The readout in the verified row is a laboratory knockout essentiality binary phenotype, joined by protein identifier to real coding-sequence codon counts and measured protein abundance.  Thus the row is a powered association test at a protein-function boundary, not a direct observation of translation, protein folding, molecular activity, or organismal mechanism.

\paragraph{Controls and rank.}
The control matrix contains an intercept, $\log(\mathrm{CDS\ length\ in\ nt})$, the twenty amino-acid composition proportions $A,C,D,E,F,G,H,I,K,L,M,N,P,Q,R,S,T,V,W,Y$, $GC3$, the $M$-density baseline defined on the union support of the nine projected $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ vectors, and $\log_{10}(\mathrm{abundance\ ppm})$ as the measured protein-abundance control.  For \emph{Escherichia coli} K-12 MG1655, the loaded row set contains $3737$ genes, with $285$ essential and $3452$ nonessential genes; the essential fraction is $0.07626438319507627$.  The abundance join reports $3739$ entries before the final used set, and both essentiality classes are present.  The residualized design has $25$ control columns, $\operatorname{rank}(Z)=24$, $\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde X)=9$, residual degrees of freedom $3713$, residual phenotype energy $232.13814987247684$, and explained residual energy $2.243840654793728$.  For \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}, the used row set contains $5511$ genes, with $1168$ essential and $4343$ nonessential genes; the essential fraction is $0.2119397568499365$.  The abundance join reports $5815$ entries before the final used set.  The residualized design again has $25$ control columns, $\operatorname{rank}(Z)=24$, $\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde X)=9$, residual degrees of freedom $5487$, residual phenotype energy $826.488384003322$, and explained residual energy $2.741892921952373$.  In both organisms the rank check is powered for the nine-coordinate residual panel, using degeneracy threshold $0.999999$.

\paragraph{Computed boundary statistic.}
The positive-entry threshold for the partial $R^2$ readout is $10^{-12}$.  With the stated controls, all nine $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ coordinates survive the positive-entry screen in each organism for the essentiality readout, but the magnitude is small.  The computed finite-row summaries are
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{FE}(\mathrm{E.\ coli}) &= 0.00966597112980509,\\
R^2_{FE}(\mathrm{S.\ cerevisiae}) &= 0.003317521425614316,\\
\#\{q_i\ \mathrm{surviving\ in\ E.\ coli}\} &= 9,\\
\#\{q_i\ \mathrm{surviving\ in\ S.\ cerevisiae}\} &= 9.
\end{aligned}
$$
The row therefore supports only the following narrow statement: after amino-acid composition, coding length, $GC3$, $M$-density, and measured protein abundance have been residualized, the nine-coordinate $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ panel retains a nonzero association with the binary essentiality phenotype in the two powered organism-specific matrices.  The ordinary least-squares linear-probability residualization is a numerical readback for a binary laboratory phenotype, not a causal model of essentiality.

\paragraph{Readback classification.}
The intended translation-mediated chain has the form $q_i \to T_j \to P_k \to F_l$, where $T_j$ is a matched translation readout, $P_k$ is a protein-omics intermediary, and $F_l$ is a function or phenotype contact.  The verified row supplied here does not contain a matched translation-efficiency matrix, a ribosome-profiling matrix, or a mediation matrix showing that conditioning on $T_j$ absorbs a direct $q_i \to P_k$ term.  It also does not attach a separate molecular-function assay.  Consequently the present finite row is classified as a powered boundary association with an essentiality phenotype after abundance control.  It is not a completed translation-mediated protein-function realization.  The correct local readback is: nonzero residual association at a bounded protein-function boundary, with no promotion to translation mechanism, protein mediation, folding, physical admissibility, function realization, or universality.

\paragraph{Local exclusion boundary.}
Several interpretations are excluded by the finite row itself.  Essentiality is a laboratory knockout binary phenotype; it is not synonymous-codon causality and is not a molecular mechanism.  Positive $S^{FE}$ entries are associations of residual codon coordinates after amino-acid composition and measured protein-abundance controls.  The cross-sectional design does not establish synonymous-edit causation, expression control, translation control, pathway mechanism, protein folding, physical admissibility, or a general biological law.  Protein abundance is used here as a confounding control, so the residual signal is a beyond-expression association rather than a protein-output mechanism.  A small but positive $R^2_{FE}$ is therefore an honest powered outcome: it may be reported as a finite association under the declared controls, but it cannot be upgraded to translation, folding, physical admissibility, biological function, mechanism, or universality unless a separate layer-matched contact supplies that evidence.
