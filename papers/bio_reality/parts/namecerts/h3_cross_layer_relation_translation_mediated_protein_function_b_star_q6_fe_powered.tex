\paragraph{Finite carrier.}
This record treats the finite row as a cross-layer boundary test, not as a completed biological explanation.  The carrier is a finite table whose active coordinates are the nine $B^*_{Q6}$ residual directions
$K\_AAA$, $Arg\_AGR$, $Ile\_AUA$, $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$, $Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG$, $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$, $Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG$, $Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY$, and $f3\_stress$.  Each coordinate is kept as an amino-acid-quotient residual: it is evaluated only after the amino-acid composition of the coding sequence has been placed in the control surface.  The intended cross-layer table has cells $(q_i,T_j,P_k,F_l)$, where $q_i$ is one of these residual coordinates, $T_j$ is a matched translation readout, $P_k$ is a protein-omics intermediate, and $F_l$ is a later function or phenotype readout.  In the supplied finite run, the available measured outcome is a lab knockout essentiality binary joined by protein identifier to real coding-sequence codon counts and measured protein abundance.  Thus the row is admissible as a bounded protein-function-boundary observation, while the translation-mediated chain $q_i \to T_j \to P_k$ is not yet fully instantiated by measured same-row translation and protein-omics mediation data.

\paragraph{Observed design.}
The computation identified nine $q$ coordinates and evaluated them in two organisms: \emph{Escherichia coli} K-12 MG1655 and \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}.  The control set contained an intercept, $\log(\mathrm{cds\_len\_nt})$, twenty amino-acid composition proportions, GC3, an $M$-density baseline over the union support of the nine projected coordinates, and $\log_{10}(\mathrm{abundance\_ppm})$ as a measured protein-abundance covariate.  For both organisms the residualized coordinate matrix retained full coordinate rank:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde X) &= 9,\\
\#\{q_i\} &= 9.
\end{aligned}
$$
For \emph{E. coli}, the control matrix had $25$ columns, $\operatorname{rank}(Z)=24$, residual degrees of freedom $3713$, explained energy $2.243840654793728$, and residual outcome energy $232.13814987247684$.  The joined finite row used $3737$ genes, with $285$ essential and $3452$ nonessential genes, giving essential fraction $0.07626438319507627$.  For yeast, the control matrix also had $25$ columns and $\operatorname{rank}(Z)=24$, with residual degrees of freedom $5487$, explained energy $2.741892921952373$, and residual outcome energy $826.488384003322$.  The joined finite row used $5511$ genes, with $1168$ essential and $4343$ nonessential genes, giving essential fraction $0.2119397568499365$.

\paragraph{Finite readback.}
The readback statistic is an ordinary least-squares linear-probability residualization of the binary essentiality outcome after the declared control surface.  The positive threshold for partial-$R^2$ survival was $10^{-12}$, and both organism rows were rank-sufficient under the declared degeneracy threshold $0.999999$.  The computed beyond-abundance essentiality association was
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{FE}(\textit{E. coli}) &= 0.00966597112980509,\\
R^2_{FE}(\textit{S. cerevisiae}) &= 0.003317521425614316.
\end{aligned}
$$
Each organism retained all nine coordinates as positive entries under this threshold.  The run therefore supplies a finite, powered association between the nine residual coordinates and essentiality after amino-acid composition, length, GC3, $M$-density, and measured protein-abundance controls.  It does not by itself decide which entries would survive against ribosome occupancy, translation efficiency, or a same-row protein-omics mediation statistic.

\paragraph{Four-case interpretation.}
The appropriate local readback has four cases.  First, no survival occurs when the residual coordinates fail against every attached readout after controls.  Second, translation-only survival occurs when a coordinate survives against $T_j$ but does not propagate to $P_k$.  Third, protein-omics survival without measured translation mediation occurs when a direct $q_i \to P_k$ association persists but is not absorbed by $T_j$.  Fourth, translation-mediated protein-omics survival occurs only when $q_i \to T_j$ survives and conditioning on $T_j$ reduces the direct $q_i \to P_k$ contribution.  The present finite row is not a fourth-case certificate, because the supplied verified facts contain no same-row measured $T_j$ matrix and no mediation attenuation calculation for $q_i \to T_j \to P_k$.  It is a bounded essentiality-associated row after measured protein abundance has been used as a confounding control, not a completed mediation chain.

\paragraph{Local boundary.}
The essentiality readout is a laboratory knockout binary phenotype, and the fitted model is cross-sectional and observational.  A positive $R^2_{FE}$ entry is therefore only a residual association after the declared controls.  It is not evidence that synonymous edits cause essentiality, that expression or translation has been mechanistically altered, or that a pathway mechanism has been identified.  Protein abundance is included here as a required control for beyond-expression residualization; it is not promoted to a protein-function mechanism.  Likewise, modeled tAI or stAI weights, ribosome-profiling contact names, mRNA-control names, and protein-abundance routes do not become observations of the missing layers merely by being named.  No translation realization, folding statement, physical admissibility claim, function realization, molecular mechanism, organism-general law, or universality statement follows from this finite row unless a separate layer-matched contact supplies that measurement and the corresponding controlled statistic is computed.

\paragraph{Conclusion.}
The run $beb31e9e1742$ supports a narrow boundary claim: in two rank-sufficient organism panels, all nine $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinates have a positive beyond-abundance association with binary lab essentiality after amino-acid composition, GC3, length, $M$-density, and measured protein-abundance controls, with $R^2_{FE}=0.00966597112980509$ in \emph{E. coli} and $R^2_{FE}=0.003317521425614316$ in yeast.  The result is informative because it survives a finite controlled design; it remains bounded because the measured row does not contain the translation readout and protein-omics mediation surface required for a $q_i \to T_j \to P_k$ propagation claim, and it does not contain the external functional assay required for function realization.
