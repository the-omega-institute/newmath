\subsection{B-star-Q6 residual association with essentiality after abundance control}
\label{sec:bioreality-q6-translation-mediated-protein-function-boundary-fe-powered}

\paragraph{Finite row system.}
The record fixes a finite cross-layer matrix whose coordinate side is the nine-coordinate $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual basis
$\{K\_AAA, Arg\_AGR, Ile\_AUA, Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR, Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG, Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY, Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG, Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY, f3\_stress\}$.
These coordinates are not raw amino-acid usage variables. They are residual codon coordinates read after amino-acid composition is present in the control surface. The observed finite readout in this run is a binary laboratory knockout essentiality label joined by protein identifier to real coding-sequence codon counts and measured protein abundance. Thus the realized finite rows are not translation rows, folded-protein rows, or molecular-function rows. They are gene-level essentiality rows equipped with codon residual coordinates and abundance controls.

\paragraph{Control surface and rank.}
The fitted control matrix uses an intercept, $\log(cds\_len\_nt)$, twenty amino-acid composition proportions $(A,C,D,E,F,G,H,I,K,L,M,N,P,Q,R,S,T,V,W,Y)$, $GC3$, the $M$-density baseline defined as the fraction of sense codons on the union support of the nine projected $B^{*}_{Q6}$ vectors, and $\log_{10}(abundance\_ppm)$ as the measured protein-abundance covariate. \paragraph{Observed finite association.}
The statistic is an ordinary least-squares linear-probability residualization of the binary knockout label, not a causal model for gene function. With positive partial-$R^{2}$ threshold $10^{-12}$, all nine residual coordinates survive in both organism panels against the essentiality readout after amino-acid composition and measured protein abundance are included. The reported finite association strengths are
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^{2}_{FE}(\textit{E. coli}) &= 0.00966597112980509,\\
R^{2}_{FE}(\textit{S. cerevisiae}) &= 0.003317521425614316.
\end{aligned}
$$
The small magnitudes are part of the result. They show a powered residual association surface for this binary phenotype after the declared controls, not a large-effect mechanism and not an organism-general law. The run identifier is $beb31e9e1742$, with verification timestamp $2026$-$06$-$12T11:06:10+00:00$.

\paragraph{Interpretation boundary.}
The finite rows support only the following bounded statement: per-gene $B^{*}_{Q6}$ codon residual coordinates retain nonzero association with laboratory knockout essentiality in the two powered panels after amino-acid composition, coding-sequence length, $GC3$, $M$-density, and measured protein abundance are controlled. This does not establish synonymous-edit causality, expression causality, ribosome dynamics, translation efficiency, protein abundance mediation, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, pathway mechanism, phenotype mechanism, or universality. The abundance covariate is a confounding control in this run; it is not itself evidence that the residual coordinate acts through protein production.

\paragraph{Translation-mediated protein-function status.}
The name of the conjecture points toward a possible route $q_i \to T_j \to P_k$ before any function readback, but this finite run does not contain a matched translation readout $T_j$ or a mediation table showing that conditioning on $T_j$ absorbs a direct $q_i \to P_k$ contribution. It also does not attach an independent molecular-function or phenotype-mechanism contact beyond the binary essentiality label. Consequently the result remains at a bounded association boundary: the residual coordinates are allowed to be recorded as powered essentiality associations beyond measured abundance in two organisms, while the stronger translation-mediated protein-function interpretation remains unavailable until a separate same-row translation contact, protein-omics mediation surface, and function or phenotype contact are supplied.
