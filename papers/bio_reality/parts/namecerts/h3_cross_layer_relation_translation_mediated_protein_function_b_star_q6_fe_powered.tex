\paragraph{Readback classification.}

Instead, the available readback is a protein-abundance-controlled essentiality association.  Its proper placement is at a bounded protein-function boundary: the finite table has a phenotype-like knockout label and a protein-abundance covariate, but it lacks the layer-matched translation and functional-assay contacts required to make a translation-mediated protein-function claim.

No folding, physical admissibility, molecular mechanism, protein function, organism-general rule, or universal biological law follows from the reported $R^2_{FE}$ values.
