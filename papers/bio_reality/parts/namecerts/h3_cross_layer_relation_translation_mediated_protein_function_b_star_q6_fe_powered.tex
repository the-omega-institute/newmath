\paragraph{Layer discipline.}
The biological interpretation is deliberately narrow. Essentiality here is a laboratory knockout binary phenotype, and the regression used for the binary readout is an ordinary least-squares linear-probability residualization. Positive $S^{FE}$ entries mean only that the codon residual block contributes measurable association after the stated controls. Protein abundance enters as a required confounding control, so the remaining signal is a beyond-expression association. It is not a protein-mediated translation chain, because no matched $q_i \to T_j \to P_k$ mediation matrix is computed here. It is also not a molecular mechanism, because no synonymous-edit perturbation, ribosome-profiling survival step, mRNA-matched translation-efficiency estimate, pathway assay, structural assay, or function assay is attached in this finite row.

\paragraph{No-promotion boundary.}
The local conclusion is a boundary statement. The two powered organisms show that the nine-coordinate $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual block has nonzero finite-row association with essentiality after amino-acid composition and measured abundance controls, with $R^{2}_{FE}=0.00966597112980509$ in \textit{E. coli} and $R^{2}_{FE}=0.003317521425614316$ in yeast. This permits the record to remain open as a bounded cross-layer association surface. It does not license a claim of translation realization, folded structure, physical admissibility, biochemical function, causal mechanism, phenotype mechanism, or universality. Those stronger statements require separate layer-matched contacts: a measured translation readout for $q_i \to T_j$, a protein-omics readout for $T_j \to P_k$, and an independent function or phenotype assay before any function-realization language is allowed.
