\subsection{A powered essentiality readback for the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ protein-function boundary}
\label{sec:bioreality-q6-translation-mediated-protein-function-fe-powered}

\paragraph{Finite carrier.}
This record fixes a finite matrix whose rows are coding sequences and whose coordinate columns are the nine projected $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual directions
$K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $Leu_{CUN\_vs\_UUR}$, $Leu_{UUA\_vs\_UUG}$, $Ser_{UCR\_vs\_AGY}$, $Ser_{UCA\_vs\_UCG}$, $Thr_{ACR\_vs\_ACY}$, and $f3\_stress$.  The rows are joined to a binary laboratory knockout essentiality readout and to measured protein abundance.  The contact is therefore not a direct measurement of translation dynamics, folding, molecular activity, or organism-level law.  It is a finite association test for whether amino-acid-quotiented synonymous residual coordinates retain any essentiality-facing signal after composition and abundance controls.

\paragraph{Control surface.}
The estimation uses an ordinary least-squares linear-probability residualization for the binary essentiality readout.  The nuisance design $Z$ contains an intercept, $\log(\mathrm{CDS\ length\ in\ nt})$, the twenty amino-acid composition proportions $A,C,D,E,F,G,H,I,K,L,M,N,P,Q,R,S,T,V,W,Y$, $GC3$, the $M$-density baseline on the union support of the nine projected coordinates, and $\log_{10}(\mathrm{abundance\ ppm})$ as a measured protein-abundance control.  Thus the reported signal is beyond amino-acid composition and beyond measured expression-level abundance; it is not permitted to stand in for a translation mechanism.

\paragraph{Rank and row counts.}
Two organism-specific row sets pass the finite-rank admissibility check.  In \textit{Escherichia coli} K-12 MG1655, $3737$ genes are used, with $285$ essential and $3452$ nonessential genes, giving essential fraction $0.07626438319507627$.  The design has $25$ control columns, $\operatorname{rank}(Z)=24$, residual degrees of freedom $3713$, $\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde X)=9$, explained coordinate energy $2.243840654793728$, and residual readout energy $232.13814987247684$.  In \textit{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}, $5511$ genes are used, with $1168$ essential and $4343$ nonessential genes, giving essential fraction $0.2119397568499365$.  The same $25$-column control design has $\operatorname{rank}(Z)=24$, residual degrees of freedom $5487$, $\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde X)=9$, explained coordinate energy $2.741892921952373$, and residual readout energy $826.488384003322$.  In both organisms the declared degeneracy threshold is $0.999999$, both essentiality classes are present, and the nine-coordinate residual block remains rank sufficient after the controls.

\paragraph{Finite readback.}
Let $Y_{FE}$ denote the residualized knockout-essentiality readout and let $X_Q$ denote the residualized nine-coordinate $B^{*}_{Q6}$ block after projecting away $Z$.  The positive readback condition is only the existence of nonzero finite partial explanatory power above threshold $10^{-12}$:
$$
\begin{aligned}
S^{FE}_{Q} &> 10^{-12}.
\end{aligned}
$$
For \textit{E. coli}, all $9$ coordinates survive this finite positivity threshold and the reported partial readout is $R^{2}_{FE}=0.00966597112980509$.  For \textit{S. cerevisiae}, all $9$ coordinates also survive and the reported partial readout is $R^{2}_{FE}=0.003317521425614316$.  These values are small but nonzero finite associations under the stated controls.  They are therefore read as powered residual essentiality contacts, not as causal effects.

\paragraph{Interpretation.}
The admissible conclusion is narrow.  The $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual block has a powered, organism-specific association with laboratory knockout essentiality after amino-acid composition, $GC3$, CDS length, $M$-density, and measured protein abundance have been residualized.  Because protein abundance is included as a control rather than established as a mediator in a same-row $q_i \to T_j \to P_k$ chain, the result does not certify translation-mediated protein function.  It only says that the residual codon-coordinate block retains finite signal against an essentiality phenotype beyond the listed covariates.

\paragraph{Local no-promotion rule.}
No stronger biological reading follows from these rows.  The binary essentiality endpoint is a laboratory knockout phenotype, not a synonymous-edit experiment, not a ribosome-profiling readout, not a translation-efficiency measurement, not a protein-folding assay, and not a molecular-function assay.  A positive $S^{FE}$ entry does not establish expression causality, translation causality, pathway mechanism, physical admissibility, folded structure, function realization, phenotype mechanism, or universality.  Any such claim requires a separate layer-matched contact with its own joined rows and its own controlled statistic.

\paragraph{Chapter verdict.}
This finite-row record is ready as a bounded cross-layer association chapter.  It supports the statement that the nine $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates remain rank-powered and positively associated with essentiality in two organism-specific panels after the declared controls.  It does not support direct promotion from codon residual geometry to translation, protein mediation, molecular function, mechanism, or general biological law.
