\subsection{Amino-acid-quotient residuals at an essentiality boundary}
\label{sec:bioreality-q6-essentiality-boundary}

\paragraph{Finite row object.}
The record considered here is a finite cross-layer matrix whose rows are coding sequences and whose columns separate three kinds of quantities: amino-acid-quotient codon residuals, observed protein abundance used as a control, and a binary laboratory essentiality readout. The residual side contains nine $B^*_{Q6}$ coordinates:
$$
\begin{aligned}
q \in \{&K_{AAA},\ Arg_{AGR},\ Ile_{AUA},\ Leu_{CUN\,vs\,UUR},\ Leu_{UUA\,vs\,UUG},\\
&Ser_{UCR\,vs\,AGY},\ Ser_{UCA\,vs\,UCG},\ Thr_{ACR\,vs\,ACY},\ f3_{stress}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
These coordinates are not amino-acid composition itself. They are retained only after the coding sequence has already exposed its amino-acid composition, length, third-position GC content, and a baseline density on the union support of the nine projected residual vectors. The protein-abundance column is not treated as the response of interest in this row object; it is part of the conditioning surface. The response is a laboratory knockout essentiality label joined through protein identifiers to real coding-sequence codon counts and measured protein abundance.

\paragraph{Control surface.}
For each organism, the conditioning design contains an intercept, $\log(\mathrm{cds\_len\_nt})$, the twenty amino-acid proportions $A,C,D,E,F,G,H,I,K,L,M,N,P,Q,R,S,T,V,W,Y$, $GC3$, the $M$-density baseline, and $\log_{10}(\mathrm{abundance\_ppm})$. Thus the fitted residual signal is a beyond-abundance association, not a direct protein-output effect. The positive-entry threshold used for the finite screen is $10^{-12}$ in partial-$R^2$ units. The run identifier is \texttt{beb31e9e1742}, verified at \texttt{2026-06-12T11:06:10+00:00}.

\paragraph{Readback.}
The finite result is a controlled association between the nine $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinates and a lab knockout essentiality label after amino-acid composition and measured protein abundance have been residualized. It is properly read as an essentiality-bound row contact, not as a completed translation-mediated protein-function chain. In particular, the row object does not contain a measured translation readout $T_j$ such as ribosome occupancy or translation efficiency, and it does not contain a mediation estimate of the form $q_i \to T_j \to P_k$. The measured protein-abundance term is used to reduce expression-level confounding; it is not promoted to a mechanism-bearing intermediate in this table.

\paragraph{Boundary of the claim.}
The finite screen permits the following bounded statement and no stronger one: after the declared controls, the two organism tables retain nonzero essentiality associations for the nine amino-acid-quotient residual coordinates, with small partial-$R^2$ values and full residual coordinate rank. From these numbers alone one cannot infer translation realization, ribosome mechanism, folded structure, physical admissibility, molecular function, phenotype causality, or a universal biological rule. A later claim about translation would require a matched translation contact, such as ribosome profiling or a translation-efficiency matrix, joined to the same coding-sequence rows. A later claim about protein mediation would require an explicit attenuation test in which the translation readout absorbs a direct residual-to-protein association. A later claim about function or phenotype would require its own functional, perturbational, or phenotypic contact. Until such rows are attached, the present result remains a finite, controlled essentiality association at the boundary before mechanism and before function.
