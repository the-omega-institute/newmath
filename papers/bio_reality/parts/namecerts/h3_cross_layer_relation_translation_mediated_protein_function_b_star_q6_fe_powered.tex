\subsection{A Translation-Mediated Boundary for Protein-Function Claims}\label{subsec:q6-translation-mediated-protein-function-boundary}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite carrier.}
This record treats the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ synonymous-residual coordinates as finite row variables attached to coding sequences, not as biological causes by themselves.  The carrier consists of cells $(q_i,T_j,P_k,F_l)$ in which $q_i$ is one of nine residual coordinates, $T_j$ is a translation-layer readout, $P_k$ is a protein-omics readout, and $F_l$ is admitted only after a separate function or phenotype measurement has been joined.  The nine computed coordinates are
$$
\begin{aligned}
R_{Q6}=\{&K\_AAA,\ Arg\_AGR,\ Ile\_AUA,\ Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR,\ Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG,\\
&Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY,\ Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG,\ Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY,\ f3\_stress\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The coordinate count is therefore $|R_{Q6}|=9$.  These rows were imported from the powered $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ translation-survival computation, but the finite experiment reported here is not yet a translation-mechanism result.  It is a residual association test against a lab knockout essentiality readout after amino-acid composition and measured protein abundance have been controlled.

\paragraph{Controls and rank.}
The control matrix $Z$ contains an intercept, log CDS length in nucleotides, twenty amino-acid composition proportions, GC3, an $M$-density baseline on the union support of the nine projected $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ vectors, and $\log_{10}$ protein abundance in ppm.  Thus the declared control family has $25$ columns before rank loss.  For \emph{Escherichia coli} K-12 MG1655 the fitted control rank is $\operatorname{rank}(Z)=24$, the residualized coordinate rank is $\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde X)=9$, the residual degrees of freedom after $Z$ are $3713$, the residual response energy is $232.13814987247684$, and the coordinate-explained energy is $2.243840654793728$.  For \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae} the corresponding values are $\operatorname{rank}(Z)=24$, $\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde X)=9$, residual degrees of freedom $5487$, residual response energy $826.488384003322$, and coordinate-explained energy $2.741892921952373$.  Both organisms pass the stated nondegenerate rank condition under the threshold $0.999999$ and both retain all nine residual coordinates after the controls.

\paragraph{Observed finite rows.}
The bacterial table joins $3737$ genes after the abundance and essentiality merge, with $285$ essential genes, $3452$ nonessential genes, and essential fraction $0.07626438319507627$.  The yeast table joins $5511$ genes, with $1168$ essential genes, $4343$ nonessential genes, and essential fraction $0.2119397568499365$.  In both organisms the binary essentiality response has both classes present, so the readout is not a constant-label artifact.  The reported positive partial-$R^2$ threshold is $10^{-12}$, and the finite readback is
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{FE}(\textit{E. coli}) &= 0.00966597112980509,\\
R^2_{FE}(\textit{S. cerevisiae}) &= 0.003317521425614316.
\end{aligned}
$$
The positive values are small, but they are not zero under the declared numerical rule.  Their interpretation is deliberately narrow: they report beyond-abundance codon-residual association with lab knockout essentiality after the listed controls, using an ordinary least-squares linear-probability residualization of a binary phenotype.

\paragraph{Mediation order.}
The admissible biological path is ordered.  A residual coordinate $q_i$ must first be tested against a matched translation readout $T_j$, such as a measured ribosome-profiling or translation-efficiency surface, before any downstream protein-omics quantity $P_k$ can be treated as translation-conditioned.  A direct $q_i\to P_k$ association is not enough.  The mediation claim becomes stronger only if conditioning on $T_j$ reduces the direct $q_i\to P_k$ contribution while the $q_i\to T_j$ step survives the amino-acid, GC3, length, $M$-density, organism, mRNA, and abundance controls.  The present finite table does not contain that joined $(q_i,T_j,P_k)$ matrix.  It contains an essentiality readout with measured abundance in the control set, so it is a bounded cross-layer association table rather than a completed translation-mediated protein-omics mechanism.

\paragraph{Four-case readback.}
The local readback has four mutually exclusive cases.  Case $0$ is no residual survival after controls.  Case $T$ is survival only at a translation readout.  Case $P$ is protein-omics survival that is not absorbed by measured translation.  Case $T\!	o\!P$ is translation-mediated protein-omics survival, where the measured translation readout carries the downstream association.  The current rows do not assign a function case, because no separate function assay, phenotype perturbation, physical assay, folding measurement, or structure contact is attached.  Essentiality is a laboratory knockout binary phenotype in this table, but the model used here treats it as a residualized association endpoint; it does not establish synonymous-edit causality, expression causality, ribosome mechanism, pathway mechanism, or organism-general law.

\paragraph{Local boundary.}
The result may be cited as a powered finite association between nine $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates and lab knockout essentiality after amino-acid composition and measured protein-abundance controls in two organisms.  It may not be cited as direct translation realization, protein folding, physical admissibility, biological function, mechanism, or universality.  Those claims require their own layer-matched measured contacts and their own controlled statistics.  In particular, modeled tAI or stAI weights do not become observed ribosome occupancy, protein abundance does not become a mechanism, and the positive $R^2_{FE}$ values do not promote the residual coordinates into function realization without a later external function or phenotype readout.
