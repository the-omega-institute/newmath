\subsection{A powered essentiality readback for the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ protein-function boundary}
\label{sec:biorality-q6-translation-mediated-protein-function-powered}

\paragraph{Finite carrier.}
The record is built from a finite matrix of cells $(q_i,T_j,P_k,F_l)$, where $q_i$ ranges over amino-acid-quotient $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates, $T_j$ denotes a translation-layer readout when such a readout is actually attached, $P_k$ denotes a protein-omics intermediary, and $F_l$ denotes a downstream function or phenotype surface only after a separate assay is supplied. In the present finite run, the attached measured surface is a laboratory knockout essentiality binary joined by protein identifier to real CDS codon counts and measured protein abundance. Thus the realized row is a bounded phenotype-side association screen with protein-abundance control, not a completed translation-mediated function claim.

The nine residual coordinates used in the run were
$$
\begin{aligned}
R_{Q6}=\{&K\_AAA,\ Arg\_AGR,\ Ile\_AUA,\ Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR,\ Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG,\\
&Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY,\ Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG,\ Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY,\ f3\_stress\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
These coordinates were imported from the projected $q$-vectors used for the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ translation-survival computation, and the coordinate count was $9$. Each coordinate remains a codon-residual coordinate after amino-acid composition has been controlled; it is not itself a translation observation, a protein measurement, or a function assay.

\paragraph{Controls and rank admissibility.}
For each organism, the control design contained an intercept, real-CDS length through $\log(\mathrm{cds\_len\_nt})$, the $20$ amino-acid composition proportions $A,C,D,E,F,G,H,I,K,L,M,N,P,Q,R,S,T,V,W,Y$, GC3, an $M$-density baseline defined as the fraction of sense codons lying on the union support of the nine projected residual vectors, and measured protein abundance through $\log_{10}(\mathrm{abundance\_ppm})$. The protein-abundance term is part of the confounding-control surface: it makes the readback a beyond-expression association screen rather than a protein-realization mechanism.

For \emph{Escherichia coli} K-12 MG1655, the control column count was $25$, the control rank was $24$, the residualized $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ design had rank $9$, and the residual degrees of freedom after controls were $3713$. The explained energy was $2.243840654793728$, the residual response energy was $232.13814987247684$, and the design was rank-sufficient under the degeneracy threshold $0.999999$. For \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}, the corresponding values were control column count $25$, control rank $24$, residualized $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ rank $9$, residual degrees of freedom $5487$, explained energy $2.741892921952373$, residual response energy $826.488384003322$, and the same degeneracy threshold $0.999999$. Both organisms therefore pass the finite-rank condition required for reporting a powered residual association surface rather than a saturated fit.

\paragraph{Finite rows and essentiality surface.}
The \emph{E. coli} table joined $3739$ abundance rows as reported, used $3737$ genes after the join and filtering, and contained $285$ essential genes and $3452$ nonessential genes. Its essential fraction was $0.07626438319507627$, with both essentiality classes present. The yeast table joined $5815$ abundance rows as reported, used $5511$ genes, and contained $1168$ essential genes and $4343$ nonessential genes. Its essential fraction was $0.2119397568499365$, again with both classes present.

The binary readout was residualized with an ordinary least-squares linear-probability approximation. With positive partial-$R^2$ threshold $10^{-12}$, all nine residual coordinates survived as positive entries in the finite association screen for both organisms. The finite readback was
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{FE}(\mathrm{E.\ coli}) &= 0.00966597112980509,\\
R^2_{FE}(\mathrm{S.\ cerevisiae}) &= 0.003317521425614316.
\end{aligned}
$$
The surviving-coordinate count was $9$ in \emph{E. coli} and $9$ in yeast. These values are deliberately small in absolute magnitude; their role is to record a controlled residual association after amino-acid composition and measured protein-abundance controls, not to assert a large-effect biological law.

\paragraph{Four-way readback.}
The chapter distinguishes four possible outcomes for a joined $(q_i,T_j,P_k)$ design. First, no survival occurs when $q_i$ fails against every admissible readout after the control surface is applied. Second, translation-only survival occurs when a measured or explicitly bounded translation readout $T_j$ carries a positive controlled residual association but no downstream protein-omics readback is attached or supported. Third, protein-omics survival without measured translation mediation occurs when $q_i$ is associated with a protein-omics surface $P_k$ but the matched $q_i \to T_j$ step is absent or negative. Fourth, translation-mediated protein-omics survival occurs only when the same-coordinate $q_i \to T_j$ step survives and conditioning on $T_j$ removes or reduces the direct $q_i \to P_k$ contribution.

The present finite run belongs to the boundary side of this classification. It gives a powered essentiality association after amino-acid composition, GC3, length, $M$-density, and measured abundance controls. It does not yet supply the same-CDS joined translation readout matrix needed to call the $q_i \to T_j$ step, and it does not supply a controlled mediation matrix showing $q_i \to T_j \to P_k$. Consequently the result can be used as evidence that the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates have a finite, rank-admissible beyond-expression phenotype association surface in these two organisms, while the proposed translation-mediated protein-function route remains gated.

\paragraph{Local non-promotion rule.}
No translation realization follows merely from the codon residuals, the rank check, the essentiality association, or modeled tAI/stAI-style contacts. No folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, mechanism, phenotype mechanism, or organism-general law follows from the finite $R^2_{FE}$ values. A stronger claim would require a separate layer-matched contact: measured ribosome occupancy or translation efficiency for translation, matched protein-omics rows for protein output, structural or physical assays for folding and admissibility, and function or perturbation assays for function realization. The current record therefore stops at a controlled, finite, two-organism association readback with an explicit prohibition against promoting the association to causality or mechanism.

\paragraph{Interpretation.}
The usable conclusion is narrow. In two organisms with nondegenerate rank after the declared control surface, the nine $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates jointly retain positive essentiality association after amino-acid composition and measured protein-abundance residualization. This is compatible with the protein-function-boundary program because it identifies a bounded downstream surface worth testing, but it does not complete the mediation chain. The next decisive contact would be an organism- and CDS-matched table containing codon counts, a measured translation readout, matched mRNA abundance, matched protein abundance, and the same essentiality or function assay, so that $q_i \to T_j$, $q_i \to P_k$, and the attenuation of $q_i \to P_k$ after conditioning on $T_j$ can be estimated in one finite design.
