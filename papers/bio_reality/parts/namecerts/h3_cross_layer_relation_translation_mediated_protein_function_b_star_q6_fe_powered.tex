\paragraph{Finite row surface.}
This record concerns the claim identifier \texttt{h3.cross\_layer\_relation.translation\_mediated\_protein\_function.b\_star\_q6\_fe\_powered} and the conjecture identifier \texttt{q6.translation-mediated.protein-function-boundary.cross-layer}.  The finite data table used here is not a direct translation assay and not a molecular-function assay.  It is a per-gene association surface in which $B^*_{Q6}$ synonymous-residual coordinates are tested against a laboratory knockout essentiality binary readout after amino-acid composition and measured protein-abundance controls.  The residual coordinate list has nine entries,
$$
\begin{aligned}
R_{Q6}=\{&K\_AAA,\ Arg\_AGR,\ Ile\_AUA,\ Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR,\ Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG,\\
&Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY,\ Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG,\ Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY,\ f3\_stress\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The coordinate source is recorded as \texttt{q\_vectors} imported from \texttt{run\_b\_star\_q6\_translation\_survival\_powered.py}.  The readout is essentiality joined by \texttt{protein\_id} to real CDS codon counts and measured protein abundance.  Thus the observed rows sit at a bounded protein-function edge: the table may report residual association with a phenotype-coded laboratory knockout endpoint, but it does not by itself identify a translation mechanism, a protein-omics mediation chain, or a realized molecular function.

Both organism rows have both essentiality classes and satisfy the recorded powered-rank condition for the nine-coordinate residual surface.

\paragraph{Observed finite association.}
With the positive partial-$R^2$ threshold set at $10^{-12}$, all nine residual coordinates are reported as surviving in both powered organism rows.  The finite essentiality association values are
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{FE}(\textit{E. coli})&=0.00966597112980509,\\
R^2_{FE}(\textit{S. cerevisiae})&=0.003317521425614316.
\end{aligned}

These values are small but nonzero under the recorded threshold and are interpreted only as controlled residual associations on this finite table.  The binary endpoint is handled through an ordinary least-squares linear-probability residualization, so the statistic is a finite readback of association after the named controls rather than a causal estimate.  The protein-abundance term is part of the confounding-control surface; it is not treated as a downstream mediator proved by these rows.

\paragraph{Protein-function boundary.}
The intended cross-layer form asks whether a residual coordinate $q_i$ first survives against a matched translation readout $T_j$, and whether a downstream protein-omics association $P_k$ is then absorbed by that same translation readout.  The verified finite rows here do not supply that mediation chain.  They contain essentiality, codon counts, amino-acid composition, GC3, length, $M$-density, and measured protein abundance, but they do not contain same-row ribosome profiling, translation efficiency, modeled-to-observed translation calibration, or a measured $q_i\to T_j\to P_k$ mediation statistic.  Consequently this chapter records a bounded protein-function contact only in the following sense: $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinates have a powered finite association with a lab knockout binary endpoint after amino-acid composition and measured abundance controls, while the function-realization interpretation remains withheld.

\paragraph{Local no-promotion rule.}
No translation, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, mechanism, phenotype causation, or organism-general biological law follows from this table alone.  A direct path $q_i\to F_l$ is not admitted from these rows, because no external function or phenotype contact is attached as an independently interpreted functional assay.  A direct path $q_i\to P_k$ is also not promoted to mechanism unless a matched translation readout $T_j$ is present and conditioning on $T_j$ reduces the direct $q_i\to P_k$ contribution.  Modeled tAI or stAI weights, ribosome-profiling contact names, matched mRNA controls, and matched proteomics routes remain distinct readout surfaces; naming them does not create their rows.  The present finite result is therefore a controlled association at a boundary before function, with the higher-layer claims held null until separate layer-matched contacts and statistics are supplied.
