\paragraph{Finite carrier.}
This chapter records a finite cross-layer test for the conjecture identified as \texttt{q6.translation-mediated.protein-function-boundary.cross-layer}.  The carrier is a row matrix whose columns are $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ synonymous-residual coordinates, measured coding-sequence controls, measured protein abundance, and a bounded downstream readout.  In the available run, the downstream readout is not a molecular-function assay.  It is a binary laboratory knockout-essentiality phenotype joined to coding-sequence codon counts and measured protein abundance by protein identifier.  The run identifier is \texttt{beb31e9e1742}, verified at \texttt{2026-06-12T11:06:10+00:00}.

The residual coordinate list has nine entries:
$$
\begin{aligned}
R_{Q6}=\{&\mathrm{K\_AAA},\mathrm{Arg\_AGR},\mathrm{Ile\_AUA},\mathrm{Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR},\mathrm{Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG},\\

These coordinates remain amino-acid-quotient residuals: they are tested after amino-acid composition is included in the control block, not as replacements for amino-acid composition itself.

\paragraph{Layer discipline.}
The matrix does not by itself establish the intended chain $q_i\to T_j\to P_k\to F_l$.  In the available finite rows, $F_l$ is represented only by knockout essentiality, and protein abundance is used as a covariate rather than as a mediated protein-omics endpoint.  A translation readout $T_j$ would require organism-matched ribosome occupancy, translation efficiency, or an explicitly bounded codon-adaptation readout; a protein-omics intermediary $P_k$ would require a matched protein readout tested before function language is introduced.  The present run therefore supports only a bounded association surface at the protein-function boundary: $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residuals retain nonzero explanatory mass for essentiality after amino-acid composition, length, $GC3$, $M$-density, and measured protein abundance are residualized.

\paragraph{No-promotion clause.}
No translation realization follows from these finite rows alone.  No folding, physical admissibility, molecular mechanism, biological function, organism-general law, or causal synonymous-edit claim follows from them.  The essentiality variable is a laboratory knockout binary phenotype, not a direct molecular mechanism measurement.  The model is cross-sectional and observational.  Positive $S^{FE}$ entries mean codon-residual association after the declared controls; they do not show that a codon edit changes expression, that translation mediates the signal, that protein abundance transmits the signal, or that a pathway function has been realized.  Any claim at one of those layers requires a separate matched contact and a new controlled statistic at that layer.

\paragraph{Interpretive status.}
The correct finite interpretation is a scoped positive boundary result.  The two powered organism rows rule out the trivial rank-degenerate refusal for this essentiality surface, because both organisms retain $\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde X)=9$ and positive residual degrees of freedom after the full control block.  They do not rule in translation mediation or function realization.  The next admissible empirical step would be a same-organism, same-CDS matrix joining codon counts, the nine $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ coordinates, a measured or explicitly bounded translation readout, matched mRNA abundance, matched protein abundance, and only then a function or phenotype contact.  Until that joined matrix is evaluated, the chapter remains a finite association record at the boundary before function.
