\subsection{Powered B$^*_{Q6}$ Residual Contact at the Protein--Function Boundary}
\label{sec:b-star-q6-fe-powered-protein-function-boundary}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite rows and residual coordinates.}
This record tests a finite cross-layer contact for the B$^*_{Q6}$ synonymous-residual coordinate system. The finite coordinate set has nine rows,
$K\_AAA$, $Arg\_AGR$, $Ile\_AUA$, $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$, $Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG$, $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$, $Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG$, $Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY$, and $f3\_stress$. Each row is read as an amino-acid-quotient codon residual, not as an amino-acid composition effect. The row universe is therefore deliberately narrow: it asks whether these nine residual directions retain finite explanatory contact with a downstream laboratory readout after the ordinary coding-sequence covariates and measured protein abundance have already been partialled away.

\paragraph{Controls and rank sufficiency.}
The control block $Z$ consists of an intercept, $\log(\mathrm{cds\_len\_nt})$, the twenty amino-acid composition proportions $A,C,D,E,F,G,H,I,K,L,M,N,P,Q,R,S,T,V,W,Y$, GC3, the M-density baseline defined as the fraction of sense codons on the union support of the nine projected B$^*_{Q6}$ vectors, and $\log_{10}(\mathrm{abundance\_ppm})$ as the measured protein-abundance control. Thus the tested signal is not a raw codon-usage effect and not an expression proxy. In \emph{Escherichia coli} K-12 MG1655, the joined table contains $3737$ genes, with $285$ essential and $3452$ nonessential genes; the essential fraction is $0.07626438319507627$. The control design has $25$ columns, $\operatorname{rank}(Z)=24$, $\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde X)=9$, and $3713$ residual degrees of freedom after $Z$. In \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}, the joined table contains $5511$ genes, with $1168$ essential and $4343$ nonessential genes; the essential fraction is $0.2119397568499365$. The same $25$-column control design has $\operatorname{rank}(Z)=24$, $\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde X)=9$, and $5487$ residual degrees of freedom after $Z$. Both organism rows pass the declared powered-rank check for the nine-coordinate residual block.

\paragraph{Finite statistic.}
The readout is a binary laboratory knockout essentiality phenotype joined by protein identifier to real CDS codon counts and measured protein abundance. The binary response is handled by an ordinary least-squares linear-probability residualization, so the statistic is a finite association surface rather than a causal model. The positive-entry threshold is $10^{-12}$ for partial $R^2$. The observed residual energies and explained energies are
$$
\begin{aligned}
\textit{E. coli}:\quad &\|y_Z\|^2 = 232.13814987247684,\quad E_{Q}=2.243840654793728,\\
\textit{S. cerevisiae}:\quad &\|y_Z\|^2 = 826.488384003322,\quad E_{Q}=2.741892921952373.
\end{aligned}
$$
The corresponding finite essentiality-contact scores are
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{FE}(\textit{E. coli}) &= 0.00966597112980509,\\
R^2_{FE}(\textit{S. cerevisiae}) &= 0.003317521425614316.
\end{aligned}
$$
In both organisms all nine B$^*_{Q6}$ residual directions are counted as surviving coordinates under the positive-entry rule. The result is therefore a powered finite association between the nine residual coordinates and the essentiality surface after amino-acid composition, GC3, coding-sequence length, M-density, and measured protein abundance controls.

\paragraph{Layer interpretation.}
The finite calculation reaches only a bounded protein--function boundary. It does not by itself show translation realization, ribosome occupancy, protein folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, mechanism, organism-general law, or synonymous-edit causality. The protein-abundance column is used here as a confounding control, not as a mediated protein-output endpoint. Likewise, the essentiality readout is a laboratory knockout phenotype, not a molecular mechanism and not a direct function assay. The result can be reported as a beyond-abundance association between B$^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinates and knockout essentiality in two powered organism-specific matrices. It cannot be promoted to a translation-mediated protein-function claim until a same-row translation readout is attached and the mediation route $q_i \to T_j \to P_k$ is tested before any relation to a function or phenotype row is asserted.

\paragraph{Four-case readback.}
For this finite contact the appropriate readback is conservative. The observed rows rule out a vacuous rank failure for the two organism matrices, since both have $\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde X)=9$ for the nine-coordinate block. They support a positive residual association with essentiality after the stated controls. They do not distinguish translation-only survival, direct protein-omics survival not mediated by translation, or translation-mediated protein-omics survival, because the verified matrix does not include a matched translation-efficiency or ribosome-profiling variable as the intermediate $T_j$. The present row therefore occupies the fourth-boundary preparation state only in the weak sense that it defines what must be tested next: attach matched translation readouts and protein-omics intermediates, then ask whether conditioning on $T_j$ absorbs the direct $q_i \to P_k$ contribution. Until that added matrix exists, the function readback remains null.
