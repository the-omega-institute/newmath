\subsection{Rank-powered essentiality readback for the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates}\label{subsec:bioq6-fe-powered-essentiality-boundary}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite carrier.}
This record fixes a finite matrix whose rows are real coding-sequence genes and whose active coordinate columns are the nine projected $B^{*}_{Q6}$ synonymous-residual directions
$K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $Leu_{CUN\,vs\,UUR}$, $Leu_{UUA\,vs\,UUG}$, $Ser_{UCR\,vs\,AGY}$, $Ser_{UCA\,vs\,UCG}$, $Thr_{ACR\,vs\,ACY}$, and $f3_{stress}$.  Each coordinate is retained as an amino-acid-quotient residual: the row statistic is not allowed to be an amino-acid-composition surrogate, and the finite regression surface explicitly subtracts amino-acid composition before the residual coordinate is read.  The observed response in this run is a laboratory knockout essentiality label joined by protein identifier to real CDS codon counts and measured protein abundance.  It is therefore a phenotype-side readback of essentiality association, not a direct translation readout, not a protein-function assay, and not a molecular mechanism.

\paragraph{Control surface.}
For each organism, the control matrix $Z$ contains an intercept, $\log(\mathrm{cds\_len\_nt})$, the twenty amino-acid composition proportions $A,C,D,E,F,G,H,I,K,L,M,N,P,Q,R,S,T,V,W,Y$, GC3 computed from the real CDS codon counts, an $M$-density baseline given by the fraction of sense codons on the union support of the nine projected $B^{*}_{Q6}$ directions, and $\log_{10}(\mathrm{abundance\_ppm})$ as a measured protein-abundance control.  Thus the fitted residual association is beyond amino-acid composition, GC3, length, the shared support-density baseline, and measured abundance.  In both organisms the control column count is $25$, while the observed control rank is $24$; the residualized $B^{*}_{Q6}$ design has full coordinate rank $9$.

\paragraph{Escherichia coli finite row.}
For \emph{Escherichia coli} K-12 MG1655, the abundance-and-essentiality join reports $3739$ abundance-linked entries and yields $3737$ genes used in the regression.  The essentiality ledger contains $285$ essential genes and $3452$ nonessential genes, with essential fraction $0.07626438319507627$, and both essentiality classes are present.  After the $Z$ projection, the residual degrees of freedom are $3713$, the residual response energy is $232.13814987247684$, and the explained energy assigned to the nine residual coordinates is $2.243840654793728$.  The powered-rank check is positive: $\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde X)=9$ for $9$ candidate coordinates, and the reported degenerate threshold is $0.999999$.  The finite essentiality association score is
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^{2}_{FE}(\mathrm{E.\ coli}) &= 0.00966597112980509,\\
N_{genes}(\mathrm{E.\ coli}) &= 3737,\\
N_{essential}(\mathrm{E.\ coli}) &= 285.
\end{aligned}
$$
All nine residual coordinates are counted as surviving positive association at the declared positive partial-$R^2$ threshold $10^{-12}$.

\paragraph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae finite row.}
For \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}, the abundance-and-essentiality join reports $5815$ abundance-linked entries and yields $5511$ genes used in the regression.  The essentiality ledger contains $1168$ essential genes and $4343$ nonessential genes, with essential fraction $0.2119397568499365$, and both essentiality classes are present.  After projection against the same kind of $Z$ surface, the residual degrees of freedom are $5487$, the residual response energy is $826.488384003322$, and the explained energy assigned to the nine residual coordinates is $2.741892921952373$.  The powered-rank check is again positive: $\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde X)=9$ with $9$ residual coordinates and degenerate threshold $0.999999$.  The finite essentiality association score is
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^{2}_{FE}(\mathrm{S.\ cerevisiae}) &= 0.003317521425614316,\\
N_{genes}(\mathrm{S.\ cerevisiae}) &= 5511,\\
N_{essential}(\mathrm{S.\ cerevisiae}) &= 1168.
\end{aligned}
$$
As in the bacterial row, all nine residual coordinates are reported as positive at threshold $10^{-12}$ after the stated residualization.

\paragraph{Readback verdict.}
The finite result is a powered, organism-scoped association between the nine $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates and a binary laboratory essentiality label after amino-acid composition, CDS length, GC3, support-density, and measured abundance have been controlled.  The two organism rows are not rank-degenerate: each has both response classes, thousands of genes, residual degrees of freedom far above the nine-coordinate surface, and full residual coordinate rank.  The result may therefore be recorded as a positive finite association with essentiality under the stated linear-probability residualization.

\paragraph{No-promotion boundary.}
This chapter deliberately stops at the observed finite row.  The response is an essentiality label analyzed by ordinary least-squares residualization of a binary outcome; it is not a synonymous-edit experiment, not a causal perturbation of codons, and not evidence that a ribosome readout has survived.  Because no matched translation-efficiency or ribosome-profiling matrix $T_j$ is supplied here, the association cannot be read as translation realization.  Because no controlled $q_i\to T_j\to P_k$ mediation matrix is supplied, a direct $q_i\to P_k$ or $q_i\to FE$ association cannot be promoted to protein mediation.  Because no separate structure, physical assay, molecular-function assay, phenotype perturbation assay, or mechanism contact is supplied in this finite row, the result does not establish folding, physical admissibility, biological function, mechanism, or universality.  The admissible interpretation is narrower: the nine amino-acid-quotient $B^{*}_{Q6}$ coordinates show small but positive powered association with knockout essentiality in two organism-specific matrices after the declared controls, while all higher-layer claims remain unavailable until a separate layer-matched measurement is attached and analyzed.
