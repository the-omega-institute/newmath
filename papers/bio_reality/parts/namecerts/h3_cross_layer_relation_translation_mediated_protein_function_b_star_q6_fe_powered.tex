\subsection{Rank-powered $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual rows before protein-function promotion}\label{subsec:biorality-q6-translation-mediated-protein-function-boundary}\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite row object.} The record is a finite cross-layer matrix whose active coordinates are nine $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual rows: $K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $Leu_{CUN\,vs\,UUR}$, $Leu_{UUA\,vs\,UUG}$, $Ser_{UCR\,vs\,AGY}$, $Ser_{UCA\,vs\,UCG}$, $Thr_{ACR\,vs\,ACY}$, and $f3_{stress}$. Each row is read only after quotienting away amino-acid composition and then conditioning on sequence and abundance covariates. The finite carrier is therefore not a codon-count table by itself. It is the controlled table of residual coordinates $q_i$, possible translation readouts $T_j$, protein-omics intermediates $P_k$, and a bounded downstream readback surface $F_l$. In the present run, the measured attached endpoint is a laboratory knockout essentiality binary joined to real CDS codon counts and measured protein abundance; it is not a direct ribosome, folding, physical, molecular-function, or organism-general law measurement.

\paragraph{Control surface and rank check.} The residualization design used an intercept, $\log(\mathrm{CDS\ length\ in\ nt})$, the twenty amino-acid composition proportions $A,C,D,E,F,G,H,I,K,L,M,N,P,Q,R,S,T,V,W,Y$, $GC3$, an $M$-density baseline given by the fraction of sense codons on the union support of the nine projected $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ vectors, and $\log_{10}(\mathrm{abundance\ ppm})$ as a measured protein-abundance control. Thus the tested signal is beyond amino-acid composition and beyond the measured abundance covariate, not a raw synonymous-codon association. For Escherichia coli K-12 MG1655 the design had $25$ control columns, $\operatorname{rank}(Z)=24$, $\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde X)=9$, residual degrees of freedom $3713$, residual response energy $232.13814987247684$, and explained energy $2.243840654793728$. For Saccharomyces cerevisiae the corresponding values were $25$, $24$, $9$, $5487$, $826.488384003322$, and $2.741892921952373$. Both organisms passed the finite rank test for all nine residual coordinates and had both essentiality classes present.

$$\begin{aligned}
\mathcal Q &= \{K_{AAA}, Arg_{AGR}, Ile_{AUA}, Leu_{CUN\,vs\,UUR}, Leu_{UUA\,vs\,UUG},\\
&\qquad Ser_{UCR\,vs\,AGY}, Ser_{UCA\,vs\,UCG}, Thr_{ACR\,vs\,ACY}, f3_{stress}\},\\
|\mathcal Q| &= 9,\\
\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde X_{E.\ coli}) &= 9,\qquad \operatorname{rank}(\widetilde X_{S.\ cerevisiae})=9.
\end{aligned}$$

\paragraph{Observed finite rows.} The Escherichia coli table used $3737$ genes after joining the essentiality labels, CDS codon counts, and abundance rows. It contained $285$ essential and $3452$ nonessential genes, giving essential fraction $0.07626438319507627$. The Saccharomyces cerevisiae table used $5511$ genes, with $1168$ essential and $4343$ nonessential genes, giving essential fraction $0.2119397568499365$. The abundance joins reported $3739$ Escherichia coli rows and $5815$ Saccharomyces cerevisiae rows before the final finite analysis set. The positive-entry threshold for the partial coefficient-of-determination readback was $10^{-12}$, and the computed finite-essentiality statistic retained all nine residual coordinates in both organisms.

$$\begin{aligned}
R^2_{FE}(E.\ coli) &= 0.00966597112980509,\\
R^2_{FE}(S.\ cerevisiae) &= 0.003317521425614316,\\
N_{E.\ coli} &= 3737,\qquad N_{S.\ cerevisiae}=5511,\\
S^{FE}_{\mathrm{surviving}}(E.\ coli) &= 9,\qquad S^{FE}_{\mathrm{surviving}}(S.\ cerevisiae)=9.
\end{aligned}$$

These numbers are small but nonzero finite-row associations after the declared controls. They support a bounded cross-layer association with the laboratory essentiality readout, not a mechanistic explanation of why a synonymous residual coordinate matters.

\paragraph{Mediation gate.} The intended cross-layer ordering is $q_i\to T_j\to P_k\to F_l$, where $q_i$ is the amino-acid-quotient residual coordinate, $T_j$ is a matched translation-layer readout, $P_k$ is a protein-omics intermediary, and $F_l$ is admitted only when a separate function or phenotype contact is attached. The present powered table does not by itself establish the $q_i\to T_j$ step, because no organism-matched ribosome-profiling or translation-efficiency row is reported in this finite result. It also does not establish that a measured translation readout absorbs a $q_i\to P_k$ protein-omics association. The correct readback is therefore narrower: the nine residual coordinates remain estimable and show finite essentiality association after amino-acid, sequence, $M$-density, and measured abundance controls in two organisms. That statement is compatible with later translation-mediated protein-omics testing, but it is not already that mediation result.

\paragraph{Local non-promotion rule.} No claim of measured translation, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, mechanism, universality, or organism-general survival follows from these rows alone. A modeled tAI or stAI weight can serve only as a bounded codon-adaptation readout, not as observed ribosome occupancy. Matched mRNA abundance is only a transcription-layer control for translation-efficiency residualization. Bulk protein abundance is an intermediary or confounding control, not a folding assay, functional assay, perturbation assay, or phenotype mechanism. The laboratory knockout endpoint supplies a finite phenotype association surface, but the binary readout was fit through an ordinary least-squares linear-probability residualization and remains cross-sectional observational evidence.

\paragraph{Finite verdict.} The experiment identified a rank-sufficient two-organism table in which all nine $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates survive the declared positive threshold against the finite essentiality readback after amino-acid composition and measured protein abundance are controlled. The result is best recorded as a protein-function boundary row: it allows the residual coordinates to be carried to a bounded downstream testing surface, while keeping the translation-mediated protein-omics and function-realization claims closed until matched translation, protein-state, function, phenotype, or perturbation contacts are attached and evaluated with the same coordinate discipline.
