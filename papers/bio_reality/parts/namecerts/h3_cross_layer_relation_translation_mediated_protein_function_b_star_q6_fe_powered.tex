\paragraph{Finite row object.}
This record treats the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ synonymous-residual coordinates as a finite row system rather than as a free biological interpretation. The carrier is a matrix of cells $(q_i,T_j,P_k,F_l)$, where $q_i$ ranges over amino-acid-quotient codon residual coordinates, $T_j$ ranges over translation-layer readouts when such readouts are supplied, $P_k$ ranges over protein-omics intermediary readouts, and $F_l$ ranges over a bounded function or phenotype readout only when an independently measured functional contact is attached. In the present finite run the active observed surface is a lab knockout essentiality readout joined by protein identifier to real coding-sequence codon counts and measured protein abundance. It is therefore a protein-function-boundary association test, not a completed translation-mediated mechanism test.

The computed residual basis has nine coordinates:
$K\_AAA$, $Arg\_AGR$, $Ile\_AUA$, $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$, $Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG$, $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$, $Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG$, $Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY$, and $f3\_stress$. These coordinates were imported from the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ translation-survival computation, but in this chapter they are evaluated against the finite essentiality surface after amino-acid composition and abundance controls. The coordinate count is therefore
$$
\begin{aligned}
|Q| &= 9 .
\end{aligned}
$$
The positive-entry threshold used by the run is $10^{-12}$ for partial-$R^2$ style survival, and the readout model is explicitly an ordinary least-squares linear-probability residualization of a binary knockout phenotype.

The abundance term is not a mechanism term. It is a confounding control that separates beyond-expression residual association from steady-state protein quantity.

\paragraph{Observed finite outcome.}
The finite computation reports that all nine $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates survive with positive residual contribution under the stated linear-probability approximation for both organisms. The finite-effect readback values are
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{FE}(\mathrm{E.\ coli}) &= 0.00966597112980509,\\
R^2_{FE}(\mathrm{S.\ cerevisiae}) &= 0.003317521425614316.
\end{aligned}
$$
The corresponding surviving coordinate counts are
$$
\begin{aligned}
N_{\mathrm{surv}}(\mathrm{E.\ coli}) &= 9,\\
N_{\mathrm{surv}}(\mathrm{S.\ cerevisiae}) &= 9.
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the row-level conclusion is narrow: after the specified amino-acid composition, GC3, length, $M$-density, and measured abundance controls, the nine-dimensional $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual block retains a small positive association with the binary essentiality readout in each of the two organism-specific tables. The effect sizes are small, and small positive values are reported as bounded finite-row associations rather than as explanatory biological causes.

\paragraph{Four-case interpretation.}
The intended cross-layer discriminant separates four possibilities. First, no survival would mean that the residual block does not improve the bounded readout after the controls. Second, translation-only survival would require a measured or explicitly bounded $q_i\to T_j$ readout without downstream protein-omics propagation. Third, protein-omics survival not mediated by measured translation would require a $q_i\to P_k$ association that remains after translation conditioning or appears without a surviving translation step. Fourth, translation-mediated protein-omics survival would require a positive $q_i\to T_j$ step and attenuation of the direct $q_i\to P_k$ contribution after conditioning on $T_j$.

The present run does not instantiate the full fourth case. It supplies a finite boundary association between the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual block and a lab knockout essentiality surface after measured protein abundance has been controlled. It does not contain a matched ribosome-profiling or translation-efficiency row matrix, and it does not show that a translation readout absorbs a direct residual-to-protein association. Consequently the chapter may record a powered beyond-expression essentiality association, but it may not record a completed $q_i\to T_j\to P_k$ mediation chain.

\paragraph{Local no-promotion rule.}
The computation is cross-sectional and observational. Essentiality is a lab knockout binary phenotype; it is not synonymous-edit causality, not codon-level intervention evidence, and not a molecular mechanism. The abundance covariate is a required control, not proof of protein mediation. The absence of matched translation readouts means that neither ribosome occupancy nor translation efficiency is established by this finite table. The absence of a structure, physical assay, functional assay beyond the knockout label, perturbation mediation design, or multi-organism law contact means that no folding, physical admissibility, biological mechanism, organism-general rule, or universal biological law follows from the reported residual associations.

The admissible statement is therefore the following bounded one: in experiment $beb31e9e1742$, the nine-coordinate $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual block is rank-powered and retains small positive association with binary essentiality in \emph{E. coli} K-12 MG1655 and \emph{S. cerevisiae} after amino-acid composition, GC3, length, $M$-density, and measured protein-abundance controls. Any stronger statement about translation, folding, physical admissibility, function realization, mechanism, phenotype realization beyond the attached knockout readout, or universality requires a separate layer-matched contact and a separately computed statistic.
