\subsection{B-star Q6 residuals at the essentiality boundary}\label{sec:b-star-q6-essentiality-boundary}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite row system.}
This record tests a finite cross-layer matrix whose coordinate side is the nine-dimensional $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual basis after amino-acid quotienting.  The coordinate list used in run $\mathrm{beb31e9e1742}$ is
$$
\begin{aligned}
R_{Q6} = \{&K\_AAA,\ Arg\_AGR,\ Ile\_AUA,\ Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR,\ Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG,\\
&Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY,\ Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG,\ Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY,\ f3\_stress\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
Each row is a gene-level cell in which the residual coordinate vector is evaluated against a bounded readout surface.  The actual readout in the verified row is not ribosome occupancy, translation efficiency, folded structure, or molecular function.  It is a binary laboratory knockout essentiality phenotype joined by protein identifier to real CDS codon counts and measured protein abundance.  Thus the finite matrix has a concrete downstream phenotype boundary, while the translation-mediated protein-function claim remains blocked until a matched translation readout and a separate function or phenotype contact are jointly available.

\paragraph{Controls and residualization.}
The estimation uses an ordinary least-squares linear-probability residualization for the binary essentiality readout.  The control family $Z$ contains an intercept, $\log(\mathrm{CDS\ length\ in\ nt})$, the twenty amino-acid composition proportions $A,C,D,E,F,G,H,I,K,L,M,N,P,Q,R,S,T,V,W,Y$, GC3 from the real CDS codon counts, an $M$-density baseline defined as the fraction of sense codons on the union support of the nine projected $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ vectors, and $\log_{10}(\mathrm{abundance\_ppm})$ as a measured protein-abundance control.  The last term is essential: the reported residual association is beyond-expression association, not a protein-output mechanism.

For \emph{Escherichia coli} K-12 MG1655, $3737$ genes entered the fitted table after joining essentiality, codon, and abundance rows; $285$ were essential and $3452$ were nonessential, giving essential fraction $0.07626438319507627$.  The joined abundance report listed $3739$ rows, and the unique essentiality-label count was $3737$.  The residual design had $25$ control columns, $\operatorname{rank}(Z)=24$, $\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde X)=9$, and residual degrees of freedom $3713$.  The response residual energy was $232.13814987247684$, and the coordinate explained energy was $2.243840654793728$.

For \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}, $5511$ genes entered the fitted table; $1168$ were essential and $4343$ were nonessential, giving essential fraction $0.2119397568499365$.  The abundance-joined report listed $5815$ rows, and the unique essentiality-label count was $5511$.  The residual design again had $25$ control columns, $\operatorname{rank}(Z)=24$, $\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde X)=9$, and the residual degrees of freedom were $5487$.  The response residual energy was $826.488384003322$, and the coordinate explained energy was $2.741892921952373$.

\paragraph{Observed finite-row outcome.}
Both organisms passed the finite-rank admissibility check used by this row: the residual coordinate matrix retained rank $9$ against a nine-coordinate residual basis, and the degeneracy threshold was $0.999999$.  With positive partial-$R^2$ threshold $10^{-12}$, all nine coordinates were counted as surviving entries for the essentiality boundary in each organism.  The finite numerical readback is
$$
\begin{aligned}
S^{FE}_{\mathrm{E.coli}} &: n=3737,\quad R^2_{FE}=0.00966597112980509,\quad |R_{\mathrm{surv}}|=9,\\
S^{FE}_{\mathrm{S.cerevisiae}} &: n=5511,\quad R^2_{FE}=0.003317521425614316,\quad |R_{\mathrm{surv}}|=9.
\end{aligned}
$$
These numbers say that the nine residual coordinates retain a small but positive association with the binary essentiality boundary after amino-acid composition, CDS length, GC3, $M$-density, and measured protein-abundance controls.  They do not say that synonymous codons cause essentiality, that the residuals act through translation, or that the associated genes instantiate a molecular function.  The measured effect sizes are deliberately read as bounded residual associations: the stronger organism-level statement is not licensed by the two-organism table, and the small $R^2_{FE}$ values are part of the result rather than a reason to promote the row to a mechanism.

\paragraph{Mediation status.}
The proposed mediation chain has the form $q_i \to T_j \to P_k \to F_l$, where $q_i$ is a $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual coordinate, $T_j$ is a matched translation readout, $P_k$ is a protein-omics intermediary, and $F_l$ is a function or phenotype readout admitted only when a separate contact supplies it.  In the present finite row, $F_l$ is represented only by a laboratory essentiality boundary and $P_k$ enters as a measured abundance control.  There is no organism-matched ribosome-profiling or translation-efficiency surface in the verified row, and no statistic showing that conditioning on a measured $T_j$ absorbs a direct $q_i \to P_k$ association.  Consequently the observed $S^{FE}$ entries must be classified as essentiality-boundary residual survival after abundance control, not as translation-mediated protein-function realization.

\paragraph{Local claim boundary.}
The chapter therefore permits one narrow assertion: in the two verified organism tables, the nine $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates remain rank-sufficient and show positive finite-row association with lab knockout essentiality after the declared controls, including measured protein abundance.  Nothing in this row establishes translation realization, ribosome dynamics, folded structure, physical admissibility, molecular function, pathway mechanism, organism-general law, or universal biology.  A future promotion would require a same-gene matrix joining codon counts, matched mRNA abundance, a direct translation readout such as ribosome profiling or translation efficiency, matched protein-omics rows, and an independently attached function or phenotype assay, followed by an explicit mediation test in which $q_i \to T_j$ survives and the conditional $q_i \to P_k$ contribution is reduced by $T_j$.
