\subsection{Rank-powered $B^*_{Q6}$ residual association at the protein-function boundary}
\label{subsec:b_star_q6_fe_powered_boundary}

\paragraph{Finite row carrier.}
This record tests a cross-layer association surface whose rows are genes with real coding-sequence codon counts, matched protein identifiers, measured protein abundance, and a binary laboratory knockout essentiality label. The residual coordinates are the nine $B^*_{Q6}$ projections
$K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $Leu_{CUN\,vs\,UUR}$, $Leu_{UUA\,vs\,UUG}$, $Ser_{UCR\,vs\,AGY}$, $Ser_{UCA\,vs\,UCG}$, $Thr_{ACR\,vs\,ACY}$, and $f3_{stress}$. These coordinates are not amino-acid composition variables: they are tested only after quotienting away the ordinary amino-acid composition basis and after including additional sequence and abundance controls. The measured response in this run is a lab knockout essentiality indicator, joined by protein identifier to the coding-sequence rows and to measured protein abundance. Thus the finite matrix is a bounded association matrix over residual codon coordinates and a phenotype-like readback, not a direct translation, folding, mechanism, or molecular-function assay.

\paragraph{Control design.}
For each organism, the covariate block $Z$ contains an intercept, $\log(\mathrm{CDS\ length\ in\ nt})$, twenty amino-acid composition proportions $A,C,D,E,F,G,H,I,K,L,M,N,P,Q,R,S,T,V,W,Y$, GC3, the $M$-density baseline defined as the fraction of sense codons on the union support of the nine projected $B^*_{Q6}$ vectors, and $\log_{10}(\mathrm{abundance\ ppm})$. The abundance term is part of the design because the claimed finite contact is beyond-expression residual association; protein abundance is treated as a confounding control rather than as a mediator or mechanism. The residualized design keeps nine $q$ columns in both organisms. In \textit{Escherichia coli} K-12 MG1655, the effective control rank is $24$, the residualized $q$ rank is $9$, and the residual degrees of freedom after $Z$ are $3713$. In \textit{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}, the effective control rank is again $24$, the residualized $q$ rank is $9$, and the residual degrees of freedom after $Z$ are $5487$. The degeneracy threshold used for the rank screen is $0.999999$ in both organisms, and both organisms pass the powered-rank condition.

$$
\begin{aligned}
Z &= \{1,\log L,AA_{20},GC3,M\text{-density},\log_{10}(\mathrm{abundance\ ppm})\},\\
\operatorname{rank}(X_{\mathrm{res}}) &= 9 \quad \text{for both tested organisms},\\
\operatorname{rank}(Z) &= 24 \quad \text{for both tested organisms}.
\end{aligned}
$$

\paragraph{Observed finite rows.}
The \textit{E. coli} table contains $3737$ genes after joining codon counts, abundance, and essentiality labels. It has $285$ essential genes and $3452$ nonessential genes, with essential fraction $0.07626438319507627$. The reported abundance join contains $3739$ rows before the final gene-use filter, and the essentiality labels contain $3737$ unique entries. The \textit{S. cerevisiae} table contains $5511$ used genes, with $1168$ essential genes and $4343$ nonessential genes, giving essential fraction $0.2119397568499365$. Its reported abundance join contains $5815$ rows, and the essentiality labels contain $5511$ unique entries. Both datasets therefore contain both essentiality classes and have nonzero response energy after residualization: $232.13814987247684$ in \textit{E. coli} and $826.488384003322$ in yeast.

\paragraph{Association statistic.}
The tested score is the residual contribution of the nine $B^*_{Q6}$ coordinates to the binary knockout essentiality readout after projecting out $Z$. The binary response is handled as an ordinary least-squares linear-probability residualization, so the scalar $R^2_{FE}$ is an association measure on the residualized finite table, not a logistic causal estimand. The positive partial-$R^2$ threshold is $10^{-12}$. In \textit{E. coli}, the residualized $q$ block explains energy $2.243840654793728$ and gives
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{FE}(\textit{E. coli}) &= 0.00966597112980509,\\
n_{\mathrm{genes}}(\textit{E. coli}) &= 3737,\\
\#\{q_i:\ S^{FE}_i>0\} &= 9.
\end{aligned}
$$
In \textit{S. cerevisiae}, the residualized $q$ block explains energy $2.741892921952373$ and gives
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{FE}(\textit{S. cerevisiae}) &= 0.003317521425614316,\\
n_{\mathrm{genes}}(\textit{S. cerevisiae}) &= 5511,\\
\#\{q_i:\ S^{FE}_i>0\} &= 9.
\end{aligned}
$$
The finite conclusion is therefore narrow but nonempty: in both organism-specific joined tables, all nine named $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinates survive the positive residual-association threshold against the essentiality readout after amino-acid composition, GC3, length, $M$-density, and measured protein-abundance controls.

\paragraph{Layer interpretation.}
The result is a powered cross-layer association with a lab knockout binary phenotype, but it is not a translation-readout mediation result. No ribosome-profiling row, translation-efficiency denominator, matched mRNA row, or measured $q_i\to T_j\to P_k$ mediation matrix is present in this finite run. Consequently, the score should be read as $q_i\to F_l$ residual association under strict covariate control, where $F_l$ is the knockout essentiality readout, while the translation and protein-omics intermediates remain unmeasured for this specific table. The presence of measured protein abundance in $Z$ strengthens the beyond-expression interpretation, but it does not by itself show that translation absorbed a protein-omics association. In particular, conditioning on abundance is not the same as observing a surviving translation step or proving that protein abundance mediates the residual coordinate.

\paragraph{Four-case readback.}
For this finite row set, the admissible readback is a boundary case rather than a full mediation case. The rank screen is powered in both organisms, the essentiality response has both classes, and the residual $q$ block has positive partial association in both tables. However, the translation coordinate $T_j$ and the protein-omics intermediary $P_k$ are not both joined as measured mediation layers. The appropriate classification is therefore: residual essentiality association after abundance control is observed; translation-only survival is not tested here; protein-omics survival not mediated by measured translation is not established here; translation-mediated protein-omics survival is not established here. A later table may test the stronger chain only if it joins the same coding-sequence rows to a direct translation readout, matched mRNA abundance, protein abundance, and the same residual coordinate block.

\paragraph{Local no-promotion rule.}
The finite numbers above do not license a claim of synonymous-edit causality, ribosome mechanism, translation realization, folded structure, physical admissibility, molecular function, organism-general law, or universal biological rule. Essentiality is a laboratory knockout binary phenotype, not a codon-edit intervention and not a molecular mechanism. The small positive values $R^2_{FE}=0.00966597112980509$ and $R^2_{FE}=0.003317521425614316$ are honest finite outcomes: they show residual association in the two joined organism tables and nothing stronger. Any claim about translation must add a separate measured translation contact; any claim about protein mediation must show attenuation of a direct $q_i\to P_k$ term after conditioning on the matched translation readout; any claim about folding, physical admissibility, function, mechanism, phenotype generality, or universality must be supplied by its own layer-matched contact and statistic.
