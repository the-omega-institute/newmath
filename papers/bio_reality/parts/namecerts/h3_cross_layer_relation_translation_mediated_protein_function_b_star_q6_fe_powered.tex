\subsection{A powered $B^{*}_{Q6}$ boundary for translation-mediated protein-function claims}
\label{sec:bioreality-q6-translation-mediated-protein-function-boundary}

\paragraph{Finite-row carrier.}
This record concerns a finite matrix of coding-sequence rows whose columns separate three layers: amino-acid-quotient $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates $q_i$, translation readouts $T_j$, protein-omics readouts $P_k$, and a later function or phenotype surface $F_l$ only when such a surface is separately measured. The residual coordinate list is finite and explicit. The nine coordinates are $K\_AAA$, $Arg\_AGR$, $Ile\_AUA$, $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$, $Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG$, $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$, $Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG$, $Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY$, and $f3\_stress$. The coordinate count is therefore $9$, and every reported survival statistic below is read as a statement about these nine projected residual directions after the declared controls are applied. The coordinate source is the imported $q$-vector table from the powered $B^{*}_{Q6}$ translation-survival computation, but the present finite-row readback is an essentiality association readback rather than a ribosome-dynamics observation.

\paragraph{Readout and control surface.}
The observed response is a binary laboratory knockout essentiality phenotype, joined by protein identifier to real coding-sequence codon counts and measured protein abundance. The fitted statistic is a linear-probability residualization, not a causal edit experiment. The control surface $Z$ contains an intercept, $\log(\mathrm{CDS\ length\ in\ nt})$, the $20$ amino-acid composition proportions $A,C,D,E,F,G,H,I,K,L,M,N,P,Q,R,S,T,V,W,Y$, $GC3$, an $M$-density baseline defined as the fraction of sense codons on the union support of the nine projected $B^{*}_{Q6}$ vectors, and $\log_{10}(\mathrm{abundance\ ppm})$ as a measured protein-abundance control. Thus the survival tested here is beyond amino-acid composition, beyond simple coding-sequence length and third-position GC composition, beyond the declared $M$-density baseline, and beyond measured protein abundance. It is not a direct translation-efficiency experiment.

\paragraph{Finite organism rows.}
Two organism-specific powered rows are present. In \textit{Escherichia coli} K-12 MG1655, $3737$ genes are used after the joins, with $285$ essential and $3452$ nonessential genes, giving essential fraction $0.07626438319507627$. The abundance join reports $3739$ rows and the essentiality label table has $3737$ unique labels. The residualized design has $25$ control columns, $\operatorname{rank}(Z)=24$, $\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde X)=9$, residual degrees of freedom $3713$, explained energy $2.243840654793728$, and response residual energy $232.13814987247684$. In \textit{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}, $5511$ genes are used, with $1168$ essential and $4343$ nonessential genes, giving essential fraction $0.2119397568499365$. The abundance join reports $5815$ rows and the essentiality label table has $5511$ unique labels. The residualized design again has $25$ control columns, $\operatorname{rank}(Z)=24$, $\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde X)=9$, residual degrees of freedom $5487$, explained energy $2.741892921952373$, and response residual energy $826.488384003322$. Both organism rows have both essentiality classes and are marked powered-rank sufficient under the degeneracy threshold $0.999999$.

\paragraph{Residual association statistic.}
The finite readback reports a positive, but small, residual association between the nine $B^{*}_{Q6}$ coordinates and knockout essentiality after the stated controls. The positive partial-$R^2$ threshold is $10^{-12}$, and all nine coordinates are counted as surviving coordinates in each organism row. The reported values are
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^{2}_{FE}(\textit{E. coli}\ K\text{-}12\ MG1655) &= 0.00966597112980509,\\
R^{2}_{FE}(\textit{S. cerevisiae}) &= 0.003317521425614316.
\end{aligned}
$$
These values are finite-row association scores for an essentiality phenotype after abundance residualization. They are not estimates of ribosome speed, elongation pausing, translation efficiency, folding yield, pathway activity, molecular function, or organism-general law. The small magnitudes are part of the result: the powered matrix is allowed to return a weak survival score without being promoted to a stronger biological interpretation.

\paragraph{Layer interpretation.}
The admissible reading is that amino-acid-quotient synonymous residual geometry can retain a detectable association with a downstream knockout phenotype even after measured protein abundance and the declared coding-sequence controls are included. Since the response is essentiality and the model includes abundance as a control, this is best described as a beyond-expression association at a protein-function boundary. It does not show a $q_i \to T_j$ translation step, because no matched ribosome-profiling or translation-efficiency measurement is present in the verified rows. It also does not show a $q_i \to T_j \to P_k$ mediation chain, because the fitted surface controls for protein abundance rather than proving that a translation readout absorbs a residual-to-protein association. Consequently, the result may be carried only as a boundary statistic: it licenses a finite, controlled association statement and blocks direct promotion to mechanism or function realization.

\paragraph{Four-case readback.}
For this dataset the four-case readback is not a translation-mediated protein-function success call. It is also not a pure null, because positive $R^{2}_{FE}$ values are recorded in powered organism rows. The correct local classification is a bounded essentiality association after amino-acid, sequence, $M$-density, and measured-abundance controls, with translation mediation unresolved. In symbols, the present finite-row information supports only
$$
\begin{aligned}
q_i &\leadsto F_{\mathrm{essentiality}} \mid Z,\ \log_{10}(\mathrm{abundance\ ppm}),\\
q_i &\not\Rightarrow T_j,\\
q_i &\not\Rightarrow T_j \to P_k,\\
q_i &\not\Rightarrow \mathrm{function\ realization}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The arrows with negation are not biological disproofs; they state that the required measurements for those stronger statements are absent from this finite-row contact.

\paragraph{No-promotion boundary.}
No translation, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, mechanism, phenotype causation, or universality follows from this chapter unless another layer-matched contact supplies it. A modeled tAI or stAI surface would still be only a bounded codon-adaptation readout unless matched to observed ribosome or translation-efficiency rows. A ribosome-profiling contact would be needed before making a measured translation claim. A protein-omics contact plus a matched translation readout would be needed before claiming mediation through protein abundance. A functional assay, perturbation assay, phenotype assay, structural measurement, stability measurement, localization measurement, or physical assay would be needed before moving beyond the present essentiality-bound association. The verified fact here is therefore deliberately narrow: in two powered organism rows, nine $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates have nonzero finite-row association with knockout essentiality under the stated controls, while all mechanism and function-realization claims remain blocked.
