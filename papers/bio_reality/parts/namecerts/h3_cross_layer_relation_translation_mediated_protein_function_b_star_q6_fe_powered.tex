Thus the protein-abundance column is not an outcome promotion; it is a confounding control required for the beyond-expression residual statement.

\paragraph{Observed finite-row signal.}
With positive partial-$R^2$ threshold $10^{-12}$, all nine residual coordinates survived the finite association test in both organisms.  The reported finite-effect summaries are
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{FE}(\textit{E. coli}) &= 0.00966597112980509,\\
R^2_{FE}(\textit{S. cerevisiae}) &= 0.003317521425614316.

These values are small but positive within the declared powered rank surface.  Their correct interpretation is an association of amino-acid-quotiented codon residual coordinates with a binary essentiality readout after length, amino-acid composition, GC3, M-density, and measured protein abundance have been controlled.  The result is not a mediation proof, because the table contains no matched translation variable $T_j$ and no downstream protein-omics mediator $P_k$ tested by conditioning on $T_j$.  It is also not a direct function proof, because essentiality is a laboratory knockout phenotype and not a molecular-function measurement.

\paragraph{Boundary verdict.}
The finite rows justify carrying this record as a powered cross-layer boundary rather than as a function-realization result.  The admissible local statement is that the $B^*_{Q6}$ coordinate block has nondegenerate residual association with knockout essentiality in two organism-specific matrices after the declared controls, including measured protein abundance.  The result remains observational and cross-sectional.  No synonymous edit, translation-rate change, expression mechanism, pathway mechanism, folding property, physical admissibility, molecular function, organism-general law, or universal biological rule follows from these rows.

A positive translation claim would require a separate matched translation readout, such as ribosome occupancy or translation efficiency, joined to the same CDS records and tested as $q_i \to T_j$ under the same control discipline.  A protein-omics mediation claim would additionally require a measured protein readout $P_k$ and attenuation of the direct $q_i \to P_k$ association after conditioning on $T_j$.  A function or phenotype-realization claim would require a further layer-matched assay beyond abundance and beyond the binary essentiality association reported here.  Until those contacts are attached, the correct four-case readback is: no demonstrated translation mediation, no demonstrated protein-omics mediation, no demonstrated function realization, and a finite powered essentiality association that remains bounded to the observed rows.
