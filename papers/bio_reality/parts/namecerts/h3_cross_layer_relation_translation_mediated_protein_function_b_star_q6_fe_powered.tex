\subsection{Rank-Powered $B^*_{Q6}$ Residual Association at a Protein-Function Boundary}
\label{sec:bq6-translation-mediated-protein-function-boundary}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Claim being tested.}
This record tests whether amino-acid-quotient synonymous residual coordinates in $B^*_{Q6}$ have a finite, controlled association with a downstream laboratory readout after composition and abundance controls are imposed.  The biological object is not a codon-frequency table by itself, and it is not a direct function assay.  The carrier is the finite matrix whose rows are genes and whose columns combine residual coordinates $q_i$, possible translation readouts $T_j$, protein-omics intermediates $P_k$, and a downstream readback surface $F_l$ only when such a downstream contact is separately supplied.  The present run attaches a lab knockout essentiality binary as the finite readout and includes measured protein abundance as a control.  It therefore reaches only a bounded association boundary near protein function; it does not establish translation dynamics, protein folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, mechanism, phenotype causality, or a universal biological rule.

\paragraph{Residual coordinate list.}
The computed $B^*_{Q6}$ residual basis contains $9$ coordinates after amino-acid quotienting.  The finite coordinate list is
$$
\begin{aligned}
R_{Q6}=\{&K_{AAA},\; Arg_{AGR},\; Ile_{AUA},\; Leu_{CUN\,vs\,UUR},\; Leu_{UUA\,vs\,UUG},\\
&Ser_{UCR\,vs\,AGY},\; Ser_{UCA\,vs\,UCG},\; Thr_{ACR\,vs\,ACY},\; f3_{stress}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
These coordinates remain residual coordinates after conditioning on amino-acid composition; they are not themselves translation measurements, protein abundance measurements, function measurements, or phenotype interventions.

\paragraph{Control design.}
For each organism, the regression control matrix $Z$ used an intercept, $\log(\mathrm{CDS\ length})$, $20$ amino-acid composition proportions, $GC3$, the $M$-density baseline on the union support of the $9$ projected residual vectors, and $\log_{10}(\mathrm{abundance\ ppm})$ as a measured protein-abundance control.  Thus the tested residual surface is beyond amino-acid composition, codon-position GC, gene length, the coarse $M$-support baseline, and steady-state abundance.  The decision threshold for a positive partial-$R^2$ entry was $10^{-12}$, so the run was not allowed to promote a numerically zero or rank-saturated fit as survival.

\paragraph{Finite rows and rank checks.}
The two powered organisms were \emph{Escherichia coli} K-12 MG1655 and \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}.  In \emph{E. coli}, $3737$ genes were used after joining codon records, essentiality labels, and protein abundance; the joined abundance report listed $3739$ entries, with $285$ essential and $3452$ nonessential genes among the used rows.  The essential fraction was $0.07626438319507627$.  The control matrix had $25$ declared columns and numerical rank $24$, while the residualized $B^*_{Q6}$ design retained rank $9$ across $9$ coordinates.  The residual degrees of freedom after $Z$ were $3713$, the residual readout energy was $232.13814987247684$, and the explained energy assigned to the residual coordinate block was $2.243840654793728$.

In yeast, $5511$ genes were used from $5815$ abundance-joined reported entries, with $1168$ essential and $4343$ nonessential genes.  The essential fraction was $0.2119397568499365$.  The same $25$-column control specification had rank $24$, and the residualized coordinate design again retained rank $9$ for the $9$ residual coordinates.  The residual degrees of freedom after $Z$ were $5487$, the residual readout energy was $826.488384003322$, and the explained energy assigned to the residual coordinate block was $2.741892921952373$.  Both organisms were therefore rank-powered for this finite residual association test rather than degenerate at the residual-coordinate layer.

\paragraph{Association readback.}
The fitted essentiality readout is a binary lab knockout phenotype analyzed through an ordinary least-squares linear-probability residualization.  The finite readback is
$$
\begin{aligned}
S^{FE}_{o}
  &= R^2\bigl(y_o \sim Z_o + Q_o\bigr)-R^2\bigl(y_o \sim Z_o\bigr),\\
Z_o
  &= \{1,\log(\mathrm{length}),AA_{1..20},GC3,M\text{-density},\log_{10}(\mathrm{abundance\ ppm})\},\\
Q_o
  &= \text{the residualized }9\text{-coordinate }B^*_{Q6}\text{ block.}
\end{aligned}
$$
For \emph{E. coli}, the observed residual association was $R^2_{FE}=0.00966597112980509$ with $9$ surviving coordinates.  For yeast, the observed residual association was $R^2_{FE}=0.003317521425614316$ with $9$ surviving coordinates.  These values are finite, positive, and rank-admissible under the declared control design, but they are small association readbacks rather than causal estimates.

\paragraph{Layer interpretation.}
The strongest local conclusion is that the $9$-coordinate $B^*_{Q6}$ residual block carries a measurable association with the lab knockout essentiality surface in both finite organism panels after amino-acid composition and measured abundance are controlled.  Because measured protein abundance is included in $Z$, the readback is not simply a steady-state protein abundance claim.  At the same time, the run does not contain a matched ribosome-profiling or translation-efficiency matrix showing a same-coordinate $q_i \to T_j$ step, and it does not contain a mediation matrix showing $q_i \to T_j \to P_k$ with attenuation of a direct $q_i \to P_k$ term after conditioning on $T_j$.  The correct reading is therefore a bounded cross-layer association at the protein-function boundary, not a verified translation-mediated function realization.

\paragraph{Four-case boundary.}
The finite result should be placed in the following local decision table.  Case $0$ is no residual survival.  Case $T$ is survival only against a measured or explicitly bounded translation readout.  Case $P$ is protein-omics or phenotype-side association that is not mediated by a measured translation contact.  Case $D$ is translation-mediated downstream propagation, requiring a positive $q_i \to T_j$ step and reduced direct $q_i \to P_k$ contribution after conditioning on $T_j$.  The present run is closest to case $P$ for an essentiality readout with abundance in the control set: it reports positive residual association with lab essentiality after abundance control, but no measured translation step and no mediation matrix are attached.

\paragraph{No-promotion rule.}
No translation, folding, physical admissibility, function, mechanism, phenotype causality, or organism-general law follows from this result alone.  Modeled tAI or stAI weights would be only bounded codon-adaptation readbacks unless ribosome or translation-efficiency rows are separately joined.  Protein abundance is used here as a confounding control and cannot be reinterpreted as a mechanism.  The essentiality phenotype is a laboratory knockout binary and does not by itself identify synonymous-edit causality, pathway causality, molecular mechanism, protein folding, localization, stability, or function.  A stronger claim would require a same-organism, same-CDS table joining codon counts, matched translation readout, matched mRNA abundance, matched protein-omics rows, and then a separate function or phenotype contact if the word function is to be used as a realized biological layer.

\paragraph{Finite verdict.}
The finite-row evidence is sufficient for a ready boundary statement: both organisms have nondegenerate residual-coordinate rank, both contain both essentiality classes, and both report positive partial-$R^2$ values for the $9$-coordinate $B^*_{Q6}$ block after the declared controls.  The evidence is not sufficient for translation mediation or function realization.  The scientific statement is therefore: $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinates show a small but powered association with knockout essentiality after amino-acid composition, $GC3$, length, $M$-density, and measured protein abundance controls in the two tested organisms; this remains a controlled association at a downstream boundary until measured translation and function-layer contacts are joined and tested.
