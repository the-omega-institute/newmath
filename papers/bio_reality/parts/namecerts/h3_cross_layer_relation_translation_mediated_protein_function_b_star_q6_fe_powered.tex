\subsection{Rank-powered $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual association at the protein-function boundary}
\label{sec:bioreality-q6-translation-mediated-protein-function-boundary}

\paragraph{Finite row carrier.}
The record fixes a finite matrix of coding-sequence rows in which the residual coordinate side is the nine-coordinate $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ basis
$K\_AAA$, $Arg\_AGR$, $Ile\_AUA$, $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$, $Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG$, $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$, $Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG$, $Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY$, and $f3\_stress$.  These coordinates are not amino-acid-composition signals: the test projects them after controlling for the twenty amino-acid proportions $A,C,D,E,F,G,H,I,K,L,M,N,P,Q,R,S,T,V,W,Y$, coding-sequence length through $\log(cds\_len\_nt)$, $GC3$, the $M$-density baseline on the union support of the nine projected coordinates, and measured protein abundance through $\log_{10}(abundance\_ppm)$.  The finite readout attached in this run is a binary laboratory knockout essentiality phenotype joined by protein identifier to real CDS codon counts and measured protein abundance.  Thus the observed outcome is a phenotype-side finite row, but the accepted interpretation is narrower than function realization: it is a residual association with essentiality after abundance and sequence controls, not a mechanistic translation or function claim.

\paragraph{Rank and row sufficiency.}
The two organism blocks are nondegenerate under the declared residualization.  For \emph{Escherichia coli} K-12 MG1655, the joined essentiality-abundance table contributes $3737$ genes, with $285$ essential and $3452$ nonessential genes, giving essential fraction $0.07626438319507627$.  The control matrix has $25$ named columns and rank $24$; after projecting away the controls, the nine $q$ coordinates retain rank $9$, with residual degrees of freedom $3713$, residual response energy $232.13814987247684$, and explained coordinate energy $2.243840654793728$.  For \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}, the joined table contributes $5511$ genes, with $1168$ essential and $4343$ nonessential genes, giving essential fraction $0.2119397568499365$.  Its control matrix again has $25$ named columns and rank $24$; the projected $q$ block again has rank $9$, with residual degrees of freedom $5487$, residual response energy $826.488384003322$, and explained coordinate energy $2.741892921952373$.  In both organisms the powered-rank flag is true, the degenerate-$R^2$ cutoff is $0.999999$, and the positive partial-$R^2$ threshold is $10^{-12}$.

\paragraph{Observed finite statistic.}
The run records a finite essentiality statistic $S^{FE}$ rather than a translation statistic $S^{QT}$ or a mediation statistic $M^{QTP}$.  The measured values are
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{FE}(\mathrm{E.\ coli\ K12}) &= 0.00966597112980509,\\
R^2_{FE}(\mathrm{S.\ cerevisiae}) &= 0.003317521425614316.
\end{aligned}
$$
Each organism reports all nine residual coordinates as surviving the strictly positive threshold in this essentiality readback.  The finite facts therefore support the following bounded statement: after amino-acid composition, length, $GC3$, $M$-density, and measured protein abundance have been residualized, the nine-coordinate $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ block still carries a small positive association with knockout essentiality in the two powered organism blocks.  The effect size is explicitly small; the statistic is an ordinary least-squares linear-probability residualization of a binary phenotype, not a causal editing experiment and not a direct biochemical measurement.

\paragraph{Translation and protein mediation status.}
The intended cross-layer route has the shape $q_i \to T_j \to P_k \to F_l$, where $q_i$ is a residual codon coordinate, $T_j$ is a matched translation readout, $P_k$ is a protein-omics intermediary, and $F_l$ is a later function or phenotype surface.  The present finite rows do not instantiate that whole route.  They include protein abundance as a confounding control and essentiality as the finite phenotype readout, but they do not include an organism-matched ribosome-profiling or translation-efficiency matrix joined on the same CDS rows, and they do not show that conditioning on a measured $T_j$ absorbs a direct $q_i \to P_k$ association.  Consequently the readback is not translation-only survival and not translation-mediated protein-omics survival.  It is a powered beyond-abundance essentiality association, held at the boundary where a later translation and protein-mediation contact would have to be attached before the claim could move into a mechanistic cross-layer chain.

\paragraph{Local no-promotion rule.}
The finite rows forbid several stronger readings.  A positive $S^{FE}$ entry does not imply translation realization, because no measured $q_i \to T_j$ translation step is supplied here.  It does not imply protein-folding order, physical admissibility, molecular function, pathway mechanism, organism-general law, or universality.  Measured protein abundance is used as a control, not as evidence that the residual coordinate causes protein production; modeled tAI or stAI weights, where used elsewhere as bounded codon-adaptation annotations, would also not count as ribosome occupancy or proteomics evidence.  The essentiality phenotype is a laboratory knockout label, and cross-sectional residual association cannot identify synonymous-edit causality, expression causality, translation causality, or pathway causality.  Function readback therefore remains null until a separate function or phenotype contact is attached and evaluated after the same-coordinate translation and protein-omics gates have been passed.

\paragraph{Chapter verdict.}
The finite evidence is adequate for a bounded BioReality entry: the nine $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates are rank-sufficient after the stated controls in two organism blocks, and both blocks show small positive essentiality residual association beyond measured abundance.  The entry is not adequate for the stronger title phrase if that phrase is read as already proving translation-mediated protein function.  Its precise current content is a protein-function-boundary record: $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residuals survive as controlled essentiality associations, while translation, protein mediation, folding, physical admissibility, function, mechanism, and universality remain unclaimed pending separate layer-matched contacts.
