\paragraph{Readback classification.}
The natural four-case readback for the broader translation--protein boundary distinguishes no survival, translation-only survival, protein-omics survival not absorbed by measured translation, and translation-mediated protein-omics survival.  The present run does not occupy the final mediated case, because the finite table contains no matched ribosome-profiling or translation-efficiency column $T_j$ and no demonstrated attenuation of a $q_i\to P_k$ association after conditioning on $T_j$.  It also does not attach a molecular function or organismal phenotype assay in the sense needed for a function-realization claim.  Instead, it gives a powered beyond-abundance essentiality association: $q_i$ is tested after amino-acid composition and measured protein abundance have already been put into $Z$.

Accordingly, the local classification is a bounded cross-layer association before function.  The two organisms have adequate residual rank for the nine-coordinate block, both have both binary classes, and each reports a positive finite $R^2_{FE}$ surface.  The evidence is therefore useful as a gatekeeping datum: it prevents the coordinate block from being dismissed as rank-vacuous on these panels, while also preventing promotion to a mechanism claim.

\paragraph{Local no-promotion rule.}
Nothing in this chapter establishes translation realization, folded structure, physical admissibility, molecular function, pathway mechanism, phenotype causation, or a universal biological rule.  A modeled tAI or stAI surface would still be only a bounded codon-adaptation readout; a ribosome-profiling surface would be needed for a direct translation claim; a matched protein-omics surface would be needed for a protein-output claim; and a separate function, perturbation, structure, physical, or phenotype contact would be needed before any higher-layer assertion.  The present finite-row result is therefore best read as follows: the nine $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinates have rank-sufficient, abundance-controlled essentiality associations in \textit{E. coli} and yeast, but the route from those associations to translation mediation and protein function remains unclaimed until a same-row $q_i\to T_j\to P_k\to F_l$ contact is actually measured.
