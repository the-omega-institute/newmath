\paragraph{Finite carrier.}
This record uses a finite per-gene matrix whose coordinate columns are the nine projected $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual directions
$K\_AAA$, $Arg\_AGR$, $Ile\_AUA$, $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$, $Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG$, $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$, $Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG$, $Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY$, and $f3\_stress$.  The rows are real coding-sequence records joined to a binary laboratory knockout essentiality readout and to measured protein abundance.  The contact therefore reaches a phenotype-facing boundary, but its mathematical object is still a controlled association surface: each $q_i$ remains an amino-acid-quotient codon residual coordinate, and the response is an essentiality label rather than a translation-efficiency, protein-folding, molecular-function, or mechanism measurement.

\paragraph{Control surface.}
For each organism the residualization design used an intercept, $\log(\mathrm{cds\_len\_nt})$, the twenty amino-acid composition proportions $A,C,D,E,F,G,H,I,K,L,M,N,P,Q,R,S,T,V,W,Y$, $GC3$, an $M$-density baseline defined as the fraction of sense codons on the union support of the nine projected $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ vectors, and $\log_{10}(\mathrm{abundance\_ppm})$ as a measured protein-abundance control.  Thus the finite test is beyond amino-acid composition, length, third-position GC, the coarse $M$-density baseline, and measured steady-state protein abundance.  It is not a direct test of the chain $q_i \to T_j \to P_k$, because no same-row translation readout $T_j$ is present in this finite run.

\paragraph{Observed finite statistic.}
The ordinary least-squares linear-probability residualization yielded positive finite residual association for all nine projected coordinates in both powered organism panels under the stated positive partial-$R^2$ threshold $10^{-12}$.  The essentiality-facing residual score was
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{FE}(\mathrm{E.\ coli\ K12}) &= 0.00966597112980509,\\
R^2_{FE}(\mathrm{S.\ cerevisiae}) &= 0.003317521425614316.
\end{aligned}
$$
These values are small, but they are not rank artifacts in the recorded design: each panel retained $\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde X)=9$ after the control projection, and each retained thousands of residual degrees of freedom.  The correct readback is therefore a powered, low-amplitude association between the nine $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual directions and a lab knockout essentiality label after amino-acid composition and measured protein abundance have been controlled.

\paragraph{Layer interpretation.}
The finite row object supports only a bounded protein-function-boundary statement.  It says that, in these two organism panels, the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual block retains a nonzero association with essentiality after the listed composition, sequence, $M$-density, and abundance controls.  It does not show that synonymous residual geometry changes translation, because no matched ribosome-profiling or translation-efficiency row is included.  It does not show protein mediation, because the protein-abundance column is used here as a confounding control, not as a mediated endpoint.  It does not show that a translation readout absorbs a downstream protein-omics association, because the required paired $q_i \to T_j \to P_k$ matrix is absent.  It also does not identify a molecular function, folding state, physical admissibility surface, pathway mechanism, causal synonymous-edit effect, or organism-general biological law.

\paragraph{No-promotion boundary.}
The admissible conclusion is deliberately narrow: the experiment\_run\_id $beb31e9e1742$ records a finite, rank-powered, cross-sectional association of nine amino-acid-quotient codon residual coordinates with a binary knockout phenotype after measured abundance and sequence-composition controls.  Any stronger statement requires a separate layer-matched contact.  A translation claim requires same-gene ribosome occupancy or translation-efficiency rows with matched mRNA controls; a protein-mediation claim requires a joined $q_i,T_j,P_k$ panel in which the $q_i \to T_j$ step survives and the direct $q_i \to P_k$ contribution is reduced after conditioning on $T_j$; a function or phenotype-realization claim requires an independent functional, perturbational, or phenotype assay beyond the present binary essentiality association; and claims about folding, physical admissibility, mechanism, causation, or universality require their own measurements and finite tests.  Without those additional contacts, the chapter remains a controlled boundary record rather than a promotion to translation, structure, mechanism, or global biological law.
