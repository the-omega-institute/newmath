\subsection{Translation-mediated protein-function boundary for $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residuals}
\label{sec:b-star-q6-translation-mediated-protein-function-boundary}

\paragraph{Finite coordinate surface.}
This record concerns a finite cross-layer audit of $B^{*}_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates before any promotion to protein function. The residual coordinate list has cardinality $9$:
$$
\begin{aligned}
Q = \{&K\_AAA,\ Arg\_AGR,\ Ile\_AUA,\ Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR,\ Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG,\\
&Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY,\ Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG,\ Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY,\ f3\_stress\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The rows are not amino-acid composition rows and are not raw codon-count rows. They are residual coordinates kept after the amino-acid quotient, then tested against a bounded readback surface. The present finite run uses the experiment identifier \texttt{beb31e9e1742} and the conjecture identifier \texttt{q6.translation-mediated.protein-function-boundary.cross-layer}. Its observed endpoint is a laboratory knockout essentiality binary joined by \texttt{protein\_id} to real CDS codon counts and measured protein abundance. This is a boundary test for residual association after controls; it is not a direct measurement of translation, folded structure, molecular function, or organism-level law.

\paragraph{Control design.}
For each organism the control matrix $Z$ contains an intercept, $\log(\mathrm{cds\_len\_nt})$, the $20$ amino-acid composition proportions $A,C,D,E,F,G,H,I,K,L,M,N,P,Q,R,S,T,V,W,Y$, GC3 from real CDS codon counts, the $M$-density baseline defined as the fraction of sense codons on the union support of the $9$ projected $B^{*}_{Q6}$ vectors, and $\log_{10}(\mathrm{abundance\_ppm})$ as a measured protein-abundance control. Thus the finite statistic asks whether $Q$ retains residual explanatory energy beyond amino-acid composition, GC3, length, the $M$-baseline, and measured abundance. The positive partial-$R^2$ threshold used by the run is $10^{-12}$.

\paragraph{Escherichia coli row.}
For \emph{Escherichia coli} K-12 MG1655, the abundance and essentiality join reports $3739$ abundance-joined rows, with $3737$ genes used after the finite filters. The essentiality labels contain both classes: $285$ essential genes and $3452$ nonessential genes, giving essential fraction
$$
\begin{aligned}
\frac{285}{3737} = 0.07626438319507627.
\end{aligned}
$$
The controlled design has $25$ control columns, $\operatorname{rank}(Z)=24$, $\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde X)=9$, residual degrees of freedom $3713$, and powered rank sufficiency marked true under the degenerate-$R^2$ threshold $0.999999$. The residual endpoint energy is $232.13814987247684$, while the explained energy attributed to the residual coordinate block after controls is $2.243840654793728$. The finite linear-probability readback reports
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{FE} &= 0.00966597112980509,\\
|Q_{\mathrm{surviving}}| &= 9,\\
N_{\mathrm{genes}} &= 3737.
\end{aligned}
$$
This is a positive residual association surface for the finite endpoint used here, but it is still observational and cross-sectional.

\paragraph{Boundary interpretation.}
The two organism rows support a restricted statement: in this finite audit, the $9$ listed $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates have positive residual association with knockout essentiality after amino-acid composition, GC3, CDS length, $M$-density, and measured protein abundance are included in the control surface. The computation uses an ordinary least-squares linear-probability residualization of a binary endpoint. No $\lambda$ parameter, p-value table, module class label, or hit-rate scalar is present in the finite row data for this record, so none is asserted here.

\paragraph{Mediation gap.}
The intended biological route is $q_i \to T_j \to P_k$ before any contact with a function or phenotype surface. The present verified rows do not yet contain a matched translation readout $T_j$, such as organism- and CDS-matched ribosome-profiling translation efficiency, nor do they contain a mediation matrix showing that conditioning on $T_j$ absorbs a direct $q_i \to P_k$ association. The measured protein-abundance term is used here as a confounding control, not as proof that the residual coordinate changes protein production. Consequently, the finite result is best read as beyond-abundance residual association with an essentiality endpoint, not as a completed translation-mediated protein-omics mechanism.

\paragraph{Local no-promotion rule.}
Nothing in this chapter licenses a direct transition from $B^{*}_{Q6}$ codon residual geometry to translation realization, ribosome dynamics, protein folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, mechanism, phenotype causation, or universality. A modeled tAI or stAI weight would be only a bounded codon-adaptation readout unless paired with a separate measured translation contact. A ribosome-profiling contact would still need a joined same-organism or same-CDS matrix and the declared controls before a translation survival claim. A proteomics contact would still need a surviving $q_i \to T_j$ step before it could support mediated protein output. A functional or phenotype claim would require its own layer-matched assay or perturbation contact. The present finite row is therefore a guarded positive association result at the protein-function boundary, with the higher-layer interpretations deliberately left unclaimed.
