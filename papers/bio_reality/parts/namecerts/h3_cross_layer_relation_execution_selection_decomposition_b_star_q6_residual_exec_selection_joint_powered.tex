\subsection{Rank-admissible translation-survival refusal for $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinates}\label{subsec:biorreality-q6-rank-admissible-translation-survival}\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite carrier.} The finite carrier is the residualized $B^*_{Q6}$ design surface together with organism-indexed translation readouts and the control columns used to test whether a proposed entry $S^{QT}_{ij}$ is statistically falsifiable. The gate is not a translation claim by itself. It is a precondition on the row space after residualizing a synonymous coordinate $q_i$ and a translation readout $T_j$ against controls $Z$, where $Z$ includes amino-acid composition, organism identity when present, GC3, coding-sequence length, and the $M$-density baseline. A proposed survival entry is admissible only when the residual design has unsaturated rank, remaining degrees of freedom, and held-out support sufficient to reject a null model rather than merely interpolate the finite panel.

\paragraph{Rank rule.} For each proposed translation-survival entry, let $\widetilde q_i=(I-\Pi_Z)q_i$ and $\widetilde T_j=(I-\Pi_Z)T_j$. The gate asks whether the finite design formed from $\widetilde q_i$, the residualized readout, and the held-out split has nontrivial degrees of freedom after the control projection. In the notation of this chapter the admissibility condition is
$$
\begin{aligned}
\operatorname{rank}(Z, q_i) &> \operatorname{rank}(Z),\\
 n - \operatorname{rank}(Z, q_i) &> 0,\\
 S^{QT}_{ij} &\text{ is estimated on held-out rows rather than on a saturated fit.}
\end{aligned}
$$
If these conditions fail, a positive in-sample coefficient, a nonzero coupling value, or an all-$1.0$ fitted surface is recorded as a projection artifact or a coupling-only contact, not as translation survival. This is the central refusal in the chapter: the gate can return \emph{needs data} even when the finite score looks numerically large.

\paragraph{Observed finite rows.} The strongest directly computed execution-selection panel in the supplied record is the Saccharomyces cerevisiae run with $n=5684$ active genes, $5815$ base rows, $5810$ ordered coding-sequence joins, and $5684$ occupancy-dwell joins. It used five folds and $240$ target permutations. The canonical protein-omics control surface gave $R^2_{QP}=0.30732228260918726$ on the active gene set and $R^2_{QP}=0.29837624994140827$ on the full gene set. The active squared norms were $\lVert P_Q\rVert^2=864.0124192674945$ and $\lVert P_{\mathrm{residual}}\rVert^2=2811.4213259512776$. Under the stated decision rule, the run passed computation but received the verdict \emph{composition\_artifact}, not cross-boundary translation survival. Thus the available finite rows support the rank gate as a refusal discipline: there is enough panel structure to compute an execution-selection decomposition, but the recorded decision does not promote the coordinate to a surviving translation-mediated biological effect.

\paragraph{Coupling is not survival.} A separate three-organism codon and tRNA-copy proxy calculation loaded Homo sapiens, Saccharomyces cerevisiae, and Escherichia coli K12, with codon-count space $61$. It computed nine $B^*_{Q6}$ synonymous residuals: Arg\_AGR, Ile\_AUA, K\_AAA, Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR, Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG, Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG, Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY, Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY, and $f_3$-stress. The proxy matrix had $9$ nonzero entries above the $0.01$ coupling threshold, with maximum absolute coupling $1882464.1106377328$, and the break condition requiring all nine $|A_{ij}|<0.01$ was not met. That result is a contact with a tRNA-Leu CUN-versus-UUR log-copy spectrum, not a direct ribosome or translation-efficiency survival matrix. The chapter therefore keeps $A^{QT}$ and $S^{QT}$ distinct: $A^{QT}$ may show a finite proxy contact, while $S^{QT}$ requires residualized rank, held-out support, and an explicit survival statistic.

\paragraph{Residual-basis context.} The broader residual-basis extraction record also supports caution. With the stated $\lambda=0.01$ description-length regularizer, four organisms were computed and two were marked as requiring data. The computed panels were persistent rather than compressible: Danio rerio had $n=14410$, candidate count $1$, $d_{\mathrm{modeled}}=0.874628721049266$, $d_{\mathrm{difference}}=0.0$, and $\rho=(1.0,0.9791828971490305)$; Escherichia coli K12 MG1655 had $n=3415$, candidate count $2$, $d_{\mathrm{modeled}}=0.6628211177352172$, $d_{\mathrm{difference}}=0.00017488612263594217$, and $\rho=(1.0,0.9982293045445091,0.9979478314995476)$; Homo sapiens had $n=16008$, candidate count $6$, $d_{\mathrm{modeled}}=0.9020707321191321$, $d_{\mathrm{difference}}=0.018824203448813415$, and final $\rho=0.9723341658092314$; Saccharomyces cerevisiae had $n=3046$, candidate count $9$, $d_{\mathrm{modeled}}=0.2199040339633322$, $d_{\mathrm{difference}}=0.008171838236265161$, and final $\rho=0.9798667977250413$. Gallus gallus and Mycobacterium smegmatis str. MC2 155 were not computed and remained in the data-needed class. The persistent residual floor, with minimum final $\rho=0.9723341658092314$, is a negative finite-row result: the selected readout blocks do not dissolve the residual coordinates into a clean translation-realization story.

\paragraph{Other finite contacts.} The same memory contains cross-layer row counts that are useful as calibration but not as promotion evidence for this gate. The branchpoint motif run used $n=10584$ introns with expression controlled from UCSC hg19 gtexGeneV8 and runtime $138.47155$ seconds. The uORF/Kozak ribosome-load packet had $41862$ fixed rows and $16739$ varying rows, with two-sample concordance recorded. The codon-composition mRNA-half-life packet had $16500$ human rows, $18924$ mouse rows, raw $\rho=0.126224$, runtime $46.349$ seconds, and a non-CSC tAI circularity guard. These numbers show that finite biological contacts exist elsewhere in the memory, but none supplies the same-row, rank-admissible $B^*_{Q6}$ translation-survival matrix required here.

\paragraph{Local boundary.} The chapter therefore records a rank-admissibility certificate rather than a positive survival theorem. Codon usage, tRNA gene-copy counts, and tAI or stAI weights can bound a translation-readout question; they do not observe ribosome occupancy unless a matched measured readout is attached. A residual coordinate, a modeled adaptation weight, a nonzero proxy coupling, or a saturated in-sample score does not imply translation realization, protein abundance, folded structure, physical admissibility, molecular function, phenotype, mechanism, or universality. Any such claim must be supplied by a separate layer-matched contact and recomputed with its own controls. For the present finite carrier the scientifically admissible conclusion is that the $B^*_{Q6}$ translation-survival entry remains at the gate: computable proxy contacts and execution-selection decompositions exist, but the available record does not contain a non-saturated, held-out, same-row $S^{QT}_{ij}>0$ statistic.
