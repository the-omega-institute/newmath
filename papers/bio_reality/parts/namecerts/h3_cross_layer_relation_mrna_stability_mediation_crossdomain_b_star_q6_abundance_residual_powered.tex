\subsection{Rank-powered mRNA-stability mediation for the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ abundance residual}
\label{sec:bioreality-q6-mrna-stability-mediation-crossdomain}

\paragraph{Finite panel and coordinate basis.}
This record tests whether the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ synonymous-residual coordinates retain a protein-abundance association after conditioning on an mRNA-stability readout.  The finite carrier is a two-organism panel with coding sequences joined to protein abundance, coding-sequence covariates, and a half-life or stability measurement.  The coordinate side is the same nine-dimensional residual basis used for the abundance residual: $K\_AAA$, $Arg\_AGR$, $Ile\_AUA$, $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$, $Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG$, $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$, $Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG$, $Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY$, and $f3\_stress$.  The shared control matrix contains an intercept, $\log(\mathrm{cds\_len\_nt})$, twenty amino-acid composition proportions from real CDS codon counts, GC3, and an $M$-density baseline defined as the fraction of sense codons on the union support of the nine projected $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ vectors.  In both organisms the control column count is $24$ and the control rank is $23$.

\paragraph{Joined rows.}
For \emph{E. coli}, the half-life join uses the Esquerre GeneId b-number against the CDS match id or locus tag at growth rate $0.40\,h^{-1}$.  The run reports $3739$ CDS rows available for the CDS join, $2947$ valid half-life records by gene id, and $2720$ three-way joined rows; the reported CDS join hit rate is $0.9978649586335735$.  The skipped CDS ledger contains $1019$ rows with no half-life match and zero rows skipped for duplicate locus tag, invalid length, missing locus tag, empty sense-codon counts, non-object records, or nonpositive abundance.  For human, the join uses Ensembl gene id without version against the CDS header gene field, with a unique gene-symbol fallback.  The run reports $19053$ CDS joined rows, $19480$ consensus records, and $16165$ joined rows; $16150$ rows join through Ensembl id and $15$ through the fallback, with no ambiguous symbol fallback keys.  The reported human CDS join hit rate is $0.9779294769799313$, and the skipped CDS ledger records $2876$ rows with no half-life match and $12$ duplicate-gene rows.

\paragraph{Rank diagnostics.}
The residualized design is nondegenerate in both organisms.  In \emph{E. coli}, after removing the $Z$ controls the residual degrees of freedom are $2697$, the residualized $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ block has rank $9$, the residualized mediator has rank $1$, and the combined residualized coordinate-plus-mediator block has rank $10$.  The corresponding residual energies are $p$-residual energy $2551.728810042108$ and $m$-residual energy $215.43086017169205$.  In human, the residual degrees of freedom are $16142$, the residualized $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ block again has rank $9$, the residualized mediator has rank $1$, and the combined block has rank $10$, with $p$-residual energy $22233.302987773684$ and $m$-residual energy $1422.0291144063049$.  These ranks stay below the degeneracy threshold $0.999999$ and therefore do not have the saturated-row-space form rejected for tiny panels.

\paragraph{Mediation readback.}
Let $X$ denote the residualized nine-coordinate $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ block, $M$ the residualized mRNA half-life or stability readout, and $P$ the residualized protein-abundance readout after the control matrix $Z$.  The reported scalar decomposition is
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{fraction}\_{M} &= \frac{R^{2}_{QP}-R^{2}_{QP\mid M}}{R^{2}_{QP}}.
\end{aligned}
$$
For \emph{E. coli}, $R^{2}_{QP}=0.11632311854858134$ and $R^{2}_{QP\mid M}=0.0875945621412319$, giving a mediation fraction $0.24697202727892137$ with right-tail permutation value $0.000999000999000999$.  The same run reports $X$ explained energy on $P$ equal to $296.8250528743585$, $M$ incremental improvement energy given $X$ equal to $325.3466399519295$, $X$ direct improvement energy given $M$ equal to $223.51756781880516$, and $R^{2}_{M\mid X}=0.12750047680284674$.  For human, $R^{2}_{QP}=0.04095215557813418$ and $R^{2}_{QP\mid M}=0.03517257842112907$, giving a mediation fraction $0.1411299863319291$ with the same right-tail permutation value $0.000999000999000999$.  The human energies are $X$ explained energy on $P$ equal to $910.5016829711035$, $M$ incremental improvement energy given $X$ equal to $819.8980203765552$, $X$ direct improvement energy given $M$ equal to $782.0025928981933$, and $R^{2}_{M\mid X}=0.036877022762988716$.

\paragraph{Comparison to the mRNA-abundance control run.}
The same abundance-residual program was previously evaluated with residualized log10 mRNA abundance as an $H$-candidate rather than half-life or stability.  In that run the organism-specific mRNA absorption values were small: $0.011973033879985792$ for zebrafish, $0.0017706954554908886$ for \emph{E. coli}, $0.007858222847072218$ for human, and $0.0018976054721605224$ for yeast.  The computed modeled complements were $0.8424312412078521$, $0.6628211177352172$, $0.8566190269974241$, and $0.22386566043976477$ respectively.  That comparison matters here because the present half-life/stability run is not merely repeating a transcript-abundance adjustment: it asks whether a distinct stability-like mediator removes part of the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ to protein-abundance residual association in organisms where a joined stability panel exists.

\paragraph{Interpretation.}
The finite result is a descriptive variance partition.  In \emph{E. coli}, conditioning on the stability readout reduces the nine-coordinate protein-abundance association from $R^{2}=0.11632311854858134$ to $0.0875945621412319$, while in human it reduces $R^{2}=0.04095215557813418$ to $0.03517257842112907$.  Thus the computed rows support a cross-domain statistical mediation entry in which the mRNA-stability readout accounts for approximately $24.697\%$ of the residual $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ abundance association in \emph{E. coli} and $14.113\%$ in human, under the stated finite controls and joins.  The module classification is cross-layer relation: the coordinate is sequence-derived, the mediator is transcript-stability-like, and the terminal readout is protein abundance.

\paragraph{Scope limit.}
No causal pathway is established by these rows.  The calculation is observational, per organism, and tied to one \emph{E. coli} half-life dataset and one human consensus stability dataset, while abundance and half-life measurements may differ in platform and condition.  The mediation fraction is a variance partition, not a molecular mechanism.  It does not by itself show translation survival, ribosome occupancy, folding, physical admissibility, protein function, post-translational state, phenotype, or a universal biological rule.  Any such higher-layer statement would require a separate layer-matched contact and its own controlled statistic.  In particular, a $Q\rightarrow P$ residual association is not promoted to a translation-mediated mechanism unless a direct $Q\rightarrow T$ translation-readout step survives first and conditioning on $T$ demonstrably reduces the direct $Q\rightarrow P$ component under the same control discipline.
