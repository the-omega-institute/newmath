\subsection{Translation-Bounded RNA-Stability Mediation for $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ Residual Coordinates}
\label{sec:bioreality-q6-rna-stability-mediation}

\paragraph{Finite panel and readout order.}
This record tests a finite cross-layer question about the nine $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates after amino-acid quotienting. The coordinate block is
$K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $Leu_{CUN\,vs\,UUR}$, $Leu_{UUA\,vs\,UUG}$, $Ser_{UCR\,vs\,AGY}$, $Ser_{UCA\,vs\,UCG}$, $Thr_{ACR\,vs\,ACY}$, and $f_3$-stress. These coordinates are not treated as amino-acid sequence effects: they are the remaining synonymous directions after controlling amino-acid composition. The verified mediation computation uses a joint nine-dimensional coordinate matrix $X$, a one-dimensional residualized RNA half-life or stability readout $M$, and a residualized protein-abundance readout $P$. The admissible order is first to ask whether a coordinate survives a translation readout, then to ask whether a direct protein-omics association remains, and only then to partition the association through a mediator. In the finite computation reported here, the mediator is RNA half-life or stability rather than a direct ribosome or translation-efficiency measurement, so the result is a bounded statistical decomposition and not a translation-realization result.

\paragraph{Controls and row geometry.}
The control matrix $Z$ has $24$ declared columns: an intercept, $\log(\mathrm{CDS\ length\ in\ nt})$, the twenty amino-acid composition proportions $A,C,D,E,F,G,H,I,K,L,M,N,P,Q,R,S,T,V,W,Y$, $GC3$, and an $M$-density baseline defined as the fraction of sense codons lying on the union support of the nine projected $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ coordinate vectors. In both organisms the numerical rank of $Z$ is $23$, so the controlled residual space is nontrivial. For \emph{E. coli} the joined panel contains $2720$ stable identifiers, $24$ control columns, $\operatorname{rank}(Z)=23$, residual degrees of freedom $2697$, $\operatorname{rank}(X)=9$, $\operatorname{rank}(M)=1$, and $\operatorname{rank}(X,M)=10$. For human the joined panel contains $16165$ stable identifiers, $24$ control columns, $\operatorname{rank}(Z)=23$, residual degrees of freedom $16142$, $\operatorname{rank}(X)=9$, $\operatorname{rank}(M)=1$, and $\operatorname{rank}(X,M)=10$. The degeneracy threshold used by the diagnostic is $0.999999$, and neither organism is reduced to a rank-saturated all-fit surface.

&\mathrm{mediation\ fraction}=0.24697202727892137,\quad p_{\mathrm{perm}}=0.000999000999000999,\\

The right-tail permutation value is the same reported finite-grid value in both organisms, $0.000999000999000999$, using deterministic residual-mediator permutation seeds tied to the organism and row index.

\paragraph{Interpretation.}
The supported statement is narrow. On the verified finite rows, the joint $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual block has a protein-abundance residual association, and a measurable fraction of that association is absorbed after conditioning on an organism-matched RNA half-life or stability readout. This is evidence for a descriptive RNA-stability mediation decomposition in the two reported panels. It is not evidence that the synonymous coordinates cause RNA stability, that RNA stability causes protein abundance, or that a ribosome-mediated translation step has been observed. The computation is observational, uses one half-life source per organism, and compares organisms without a phylogenetically controlled model. Differences in abundance and half-life platform, condition, and normalization remain part of the statistical surface.

\paragraph{Local no-promotion boundary.}
No translation claim follows from this chapter unless a separate translation contact supplies a matched ribosome-occupancy or translation-efficiency matrix and the corresponding controlled $S^{QT}$ statistic. No folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, phenotype, mechanism, adaptation claim, causation claim, or universal biological law follows from the finite rows above. Protein abundance here is a downstream readout; RNA half-life or stability is a mediator used for variance partitioning; the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ coordinates remain synonymous residual coordinates. A stronger claim would require a same-row panel containing codon counts, the nine residual coordinates, direct translation readout, matched mRNA abundance, RNA half-life or stability, protein abundance, the same $Z$ controls, rank sufficiency diagnostics, and a declared break condition showing that the $Q\rightarrow T$ step survives before downstream interpretation is promoted.
