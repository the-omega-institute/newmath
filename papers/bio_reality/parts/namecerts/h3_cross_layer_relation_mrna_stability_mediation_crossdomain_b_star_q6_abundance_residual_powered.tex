\subsection{Q6 residual coordinates under RNA half-life conditioning}
\label{sec:q6-rna-half-life-conditioning}

\paragraph{Finite panel.}
This record concerns a finite panel in which coding sequences are indexed by organism, a nine-dimensional $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual coordinate vector, a protein-abundance residual readout, and an RNA half-life or stability readout used as a conditioning variable. The coordinate list is
$K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $Leu_{CUN\,vs\,UUR}$, $Leu_{UUA\,vs\,UUG}$, $Ser_{UCR\,vs\,AGY}$, $Ser_{UCA\,vs\,UCG}$, $Thr_{ACR\,vs\,ACY}$, and $f_{3}$-stress. The control block contains an intercept, $\log(\mathrm{CDS\ length\ in\ nt})$, twenty amino-acid composition proportions computed from real CDS codon counts, GC3, and an $M$-density baseline defined on the union support of the nine projected $B^{*}_{Q6}$ coordinates. In both computed organisms the control matrix has $24$ columns and rank $23$, so the reported effects are not read as raw codon-count associations but as residual associations after this amino-acid, length, GC3, and density adjustment.

\paragraph{Joined rows and readout construction.}
The cross-domain RNA-stability run joins two organisms. In \textit{E. coli} K-12 MG1655, the half-life source contributes $2947$ valid half-life records at growth rate $0.40\,h^{-1}$, the CDS side reports $3739$ joined coding sequences, and the three-way stable panel contains $2720$ rows. The reported CDS hit rate is $0.9978649586335735$, with $1019$ CDS records skipped for lack of a half-life match and no skipped records from duplicate locus tags, invalid length, empty sense-codon counts, nonpositive abundance, or malformed objects. In human, the consensus half-life source contributes $19480$ valid Ensembl-gene records, the CDS side reports $19053$ joined sequences, and the stable panel contains $16165$ rows. The human join uses $16150$ Ensembl-gene-id matches and $15$ unique gene-symbol fallback matches, with $12$ duplicate-gene CDS records skipped and $2876$ CDS records skipped for lack of a half-life match. The reported human CDS hit rate is $0.9779294769799313$.

\paragraph{Rank diagnostics.}
Let $X$ denote the residualized nine-coordinate $B^{*}_{Q6}$ block, $M$ the residualized RNA half-life or stability readout, $P$ the residualized protein-abundance readout, and $Z$ the stated control block. The diagnostic ranks after removing $Z$ are nondegenerate in both organisms:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{rank}_{Z} &= 23,\\
\mathrm{rank}(X\mid Z) &= 9,\\
\mathrm{rank}(M\mid Z) &= 1,\\
\mathrm{rank}(X,M\mid Z) &= 10.
\end{aligned}
$$
For \textit{E. coli}, the residual degrees of freedom after $Z$ are $2697$, with $p$-residual energy $2551.728810042108$ and $m$-residual energy $215.43086017169205$. The joint path check gives $R^{2}_{QM}=0.02298556640056076$, while $R^{2}_{M\mid X}=0.12750047680284674$. The explained-energy audit reports $X$ on $P$ as $296.8250528743585$, $M$ on $P$ as $398.65412500748283$, the incremental improvement of $M$ given $X$ as $325.3466399519295$, and the direct improvement of $X$ given $M$ as $223.51756781880516$. For human, the residual degrees of freedom after $Z$ are $16142$, with $p$-residual energy $22233.302987773684$ and $m$-residual energy $1422.0291144063049$. The joint path check gives $R^{2}_{QM}=0.010503670362051522$, while $R^{2}_{M\mid X}=0.036877022762988716$. The corresponding explained energies are $910.5016829711035$ for $X$ on $P$, $948.3971104494655$ for $M$ on $P$, $819.8980203765552$ for the incremental improvement of $M$ given $X$, and $782.0025928981933$ for the direct improvement of $X$ given $M$.

\paragraph{Mediation scalar.}
The reported statistic is a variance-partitioning calculation, not a causal experiment. In \textit{E. coli}, the unconditioned $B^{*}_{Q6}$-to-protein residual association is $R^{2}_{QP}=0.11632311854858134$, and after conditioning on the RNA half-life readout it is $R^{2}_{QP\mid M}=0.0875945621412319$. The corresponding finite-row mediation fraction is
$$
\begin{aligned}
\frac{R^{2}_{QP}-R^{2}_{QP\mid M}}{R^{2}_{QP}}
&= 0.24697202727892137,
\end{aligned}
$$
with right-tail permutation value $0.000999000999000999$ under the deterministic residual-mediator permutation seed recorded for the organism. In human, the same computation gives $R^{2}_{QP}=0.04095215557813418$, $R^{2}_{QP\mid M}=0.03517257842112907$, and
$$
\begin{aligned}
\frac{R^{2}_{QP}-R^{2}_{QP\mid M}}{R^{2}_{QP}}
&= 0.1411299863319291,
\end{aligned}
$$
again with right-tail permutation value $0.000999000999000999$. Thus the finite panels show a measurable attenuation of the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ protein-abundance residual association after adding RNA half-life or stability, larger in \textit{E. coli} than in human on this scalar.

\paragraph{Relation to the mRNA-abundance control run.}
A separate mRNA-abundance control computation used the same nine coordinates and the same $24$-column control design in four organisms. Its joined row counts were $13140$ for zebrafish, $3415$ for \textit{E. coli}, $11554$ for human, and $5128$ for yeast, with rank $23$ for $Z$ in every organism. That run projected the modeled-tAI complement abundance residual onto residualized $\log_{10}$ mRNA abundance. The absorbed fractions were small: $0.011973033879985792$ in zebrafish, $0.0017706954554908886$ in \textit{E. coli}, $0.007858222847072218$ in human, and $0.0018976054721605224$ in yeast. These values matter here because they separate ordinary mRNA abundance from the RNA half-life or stability mediation surface. The half-life computation is therefore not licensed by transcript abundance alone; it is a distinct finite-row conditioning calculation with its own joins, ranks, and permutation audit.

\paragraph{Interpretation boundary.}
The admissible conclusion is narrow. The panels support a descriptive statement: after the declared residualization, adding RNA half-life or stability reduces the observed $B^{*}_{Q6}$ association with protein abundance by about $24.70\%$ in \textit{E. coli} and $14.11\%$ in human on the reported scalar, with nondegenerate coordinate rank and finite joined rows. This does not establish that codon-coordinate residuals cause RNA stability, that RNA stability causes protein abundance, or that a ribosome-mediated translation mechanism has been identified. It also does not license claims about translation realization, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, phenotype, or universality. Each of those layers would require its own matched measurement surface and its own controlled statistic. The present chapter records a bounded cross-layer decomposition: $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates, RNA half-life or stability, and protein abundance can be placed in one finite regression panel, and the conditioning readout measurably attenuates the protein-abundance residual association in the two computed organisms.
