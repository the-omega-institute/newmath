\paragraph{Local verdict.}
The finite-row result supports a narrow statement: in the two computed organisms, conditioning on residualized mRNA half-life or stability reduces the observed association between the nine-dimensional $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual block and protein-abundance residuals.  The reduction is approximately $24.7\%$ in \emph{E. coli} and $14.1\%$ in human under the recorded controls, with deterministic permutation right-tail values $0.000999000999000999$ in both cases.  This is a statistical mediation-style partition of residual variance.  It is not evidence that the synonymous coordinate causes mRNA stability, that mRNA stability causes the protein-abundance association, or that a translation-mediated biochemical pathway has been identified.
