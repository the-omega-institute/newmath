\paragraph{Finite panel.}
This record concerns a two-organism finite panel in which nine amino-acid-quotient-surviving $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinates are tested against protein-abundance residuals after conditioning on an mRNA half-life or stability readout. The coordinate list is
$K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $Leu_{CUN\_vs\_UUR}$, $Leu_{UUA\_vs\_UUG}$, $Ser_{UCR\_vs\_AGY}$, $Ser_{UCA\_vs\_UCG}$, $Thr_{ACR\_vs\_ACY}$, and $f3_{stress}$. The control design uses an intercept, $\log(\mathrm{cds\_len\_nt})$, the twenty amino-acid composition proportions $A,C,D,E,F,G,H,I,K,L,M,N,P,Q,R,S,T,V,W,Y$, real-CDS $GC3$, and an $M$-density baseline defined as the fraction of sense codons on the union support of the nine projected $B^*_{Q6}$ vectors. In both organisms the control column count is $24$ and the control rank is $23$.

\paragraph{Rank checks.}
After residualizing by the shared control block, the bacterial row space has residual degrees of freedom $2697$, $\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde X)=9$, $\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde M)=1$, $\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde X,\widetilde M)=10$, and $\operatorname{rank}(X\text{ for }M)=9$. The degeneracy threshold is $0.999999$, so neither final panel is being read as a saturated all-fit surface. The finite rank statement is therefore not a protein mechanism claim; it only says that the residualized $Q$ block and the residualized mRNA-stability scalar have enough independent row directions to support a variance-partition calculation.

\paragraph{Interpretation.}
The supported statement is narrow. In this finite panel, the $B^*_{Q6}$ residual block has a detectable association with protein-abundance residuals, and part of that association is removed when a same-row mRNA half-life or stability readout is added to the residualized regression design. The reported module classification is therefore a cross-layer statistical mediation record for $q6.mrna\text{-}stability\text{-}mediation.cross\text{-}domain.abundance\text{-}residual.cross\text{-}layer$, not a causal pathway result. The bacterial fraction $0.24697202727892137$ is larger than the human fraction $0.1411299863319291$, but this comparison is descriptive only: the organisms use different single-source half-life panels and are not evaluated under a phylogenetically controlled design.

\paragraph{Local boundary.}
No translation-realization conclusion follows from this chapter by itself. No folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, phenotype, mechanism, adaptation, causation, or universality follows from the variance partition. A positive protein-abundance residual association is not a translation survival matrix entry, and an mRNA half-life conditioner is not a ribosome-readout contact. Any claim about $Q_i\to T_j$ translation survival would require a separate same-row translation readout such as ribosome profiling or translation efficiency under the stated rank-powered controls. Any claim about folded structure, physical assays, function, phenotype, or global biological law would require its own layer-matched measurement and statistic.
