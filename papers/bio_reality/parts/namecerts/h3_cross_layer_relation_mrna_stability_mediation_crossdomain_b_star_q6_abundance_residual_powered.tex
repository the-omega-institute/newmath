\subsection{Rank-powered mRNA-stability mediation for the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ protein-abundance residual}
\label{subsec:biorereality-q6-mrna-stability-mediation-crossdomain}

\paragraph{Finite panel and coordinate scope.}
This record tests a cross-layer claim in which nine $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates are evaluated against protein-abundance residuals after conditioning on an mRNA stability or half-life readout.  The coordinate block is
$K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $Leu_{CUN\,vs\,UUR}$, $Leu_{UUA\,vs\,UUG}$, $Ser_{UCR\,vs\,AGY}$, $Ser_{UCA\,vs\,UCG}$, $Thr_{ACR\,vs\,ACY}$, and $f3_{stress}$.  The control matrix $Z$ has $24$ declared columns: an intercept, $\log(\mathrm{cds\_len\_nt})$, the $20$ amino-acid composition proportions $A,C,D,E,F,G,H,I,K,L,M,N,P,Q,R,S,T,V,W,Y$, GC3 from real CDS codon counts, and an $M$-density baseline equal to the fraction of sense codons on the union support of the nine projected $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ vectors.  In the realized residual designs, both organisms have $\operatorname{rank}(Z)=23$, $\operatorname{rank}(X)=9$, $\operatorname{rank}(M)=1$, and $\operatorname{rank}(X,M)=10$, so the reported mediation scalar is not an all-ones rank-saturated surface.

\paragraph{Joined rows and reality contacts.}
The bacterial panel joins $2720$ stable identifiers.  Its half-life contact is the Esquerre growth-rate $0.40\,\mathrm{h}^{-1}$ table, with $2947$ valid half-life records by gene identifier, $3739$ CDS rows reported before the three-way join, and a CDS join hit rate of $0.9978649586335735$.  The join key is the equality between the Esquerre GeneId b-number and the E. coli CDS $cds\_match\_id/locus\_tag$ field.  The human panel joins $16165$ stable identifiers.  Its stability contact is the Agarwal consensus table, using an unlogged mean normalized relative mRNA half-life consensus across $54$ dataset columns, with $19480$ valid consensus records by Ensembl gene identifier, $19053$ CDS rows reported before the three-way join, and a CDS join hit rate of $0.9779294769799313$.  The human join uses Ensembl gene identifiers without version numbers, with $16150$ rows matched by Ensembl gene id and $15$ rows supplied by the unique gene-symbol fallback.  These two joined panels are observational and organism-specific; they do not, by themselves, provide a perturbation design or a causal intervention.

\paragraph{Mediation readback.}
The finite-row statistic is a variance-partition readback, not a mechanism assignment.  The reported scalar compares the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ association with protein abundance before and after conditioning on the residualized stability readout:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{mediation\ fraction} &= \frac{R^2_{QP}-R^2_{QP\mid M}}{R^2_{QP}} .
\end{aligned}
$$
For E. coli, $R^2_{QP}=0.11632311854858134$ and $R^2_{QP\mid M}=0.0875945621412319$, giving a mediation fraction of $0.24697202727892137$ with right-tail permutation value $0.000999000999000999$.  The corresponding joint check for the $Q\to M$ path is $R^2_{QM}=0.02298556640056076$, and $R^2_{M\mid X}=0.12750047680284674$; the fitted coefficient check for $P$ on $M$ after $X$ is $-1.2432793356009886$.  For human, $R^2_{QP}=0.04095215557813418$ and $R^2_{QP\mid M}=0.03517257842112907$, giving a mediation fraction of $0.1411299863319291$ with the same right-tail permutation value $0.000999000999000999$.  The human $Q\to M$ joint check is $R^2_{QM}=0.010503670362051522$, $R^2_{M\mid X}=0.036877022762988716$, and the fitted coefficient check for $P$ on $M$ after $X$ is $0.7633409850900272$.

\paragraph{Relation to the earlier mRNA-abundance absorption run.}
A prior matched mRNA-abundance computation on the same nine-coordinate family supplies a useful negative-control context.  In that run, the organism-specific modeled-complement values were $0.8424312412078521$ for zebrafish, $0.6628211177352172$ for E. coli, $0.8566190269974241$ for human, and $0.22386566043976477$ for yeast.  The mRNA-abundance absorption fractions were small: $0.011973033879985792$ for zebrafish, $0.0017706954554908886$ for E. coli, $0.007858222847072218$ for human, and $0.0018976054721605224$ for yeast.  The current stability-mediated computation is therefore not a re-labeling of transcript abundance as translation evidence; it is a separate conditioning experiment using half-life or stability rows in two organisms.

\paragraph{Local interpretation.}
The finite result supports the following bounded statement: in two joined organism panels, the nine-dimensional $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual block retains a protein-abundance association after the declared sequence-composition controls, and a measurable part of that association is reduced when an mRNA stability or half-life mediator is added.  The reduction is about $24.6972\%$ in E. coli and $14.1130\%$ in human under the recorded variance-partition statistic, with permutation right-tail values $0.000999000999000999$ in both panels.  The sign and size of the mediator coefficient are organism-specific, and the two panels use different measurement platforms and biological conditions.  Thus the record is best read as a cross-domain statistical decomposition over finite matched rows.

\paragraph{No-promotion boundary.}
No translation-survival claim follows from this chapter alone, because the tested mediator is mRNA half-life or stability rather than a direct ribosome-profiling or translation-efficiency readout.  No folded-structure, physical-admissibility, molecular-function, phenotype, mechanism, or universal biological-law conclusion follows from the reported numbers.  A separate layer-matched contact would be required for each such promotion: measured translation rows for $Q\to T$, protein-state or structure rows for post-translational claims, functional assays for function, perturbation or phenotype assays for organismal outcomes, and an explicitly designed causal study for pathway direction.  The present chapter records a finite observational mediation decomposition only.
