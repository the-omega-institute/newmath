\subsection{Cross-Domain mRNA-Stability Mediation for the $B^*_{Q6}$ Abundance Residual}
\label{subsec:bioreality-q6-mrna-stability-mediation-crossdomain}

\paragraph{Finite panel and layer class.}
This record treats the $B^*_{Q6}$ residual basis as a finite coordinate family tested against a protein-abundance residual after conditioning on an mRNA half-life or stability readout.  The module class is \emph{cross-layer relation}: the rows join coding-sequence codon counts, the nine $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinates, protein-abundance measurements, and one matched mRNA-stability mediator.  The nine coordinates are $K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $Leu_{CUN\,vs\,UUR}$, $Leu_{UUA\,vs\,UUG}$, $Ser_{UCR\,vs\,AGY}$, $Ser_{UCA\,vs\,UCG}$, $Thr_{ACR\,vs\,ACY}$, and $f_3$-stress.  The control block has rank $23$ in both organisms and is built from an intercept, $\log(\mathrm{CDS\ length})$, the twenty amino-acid composition proportions $A,C,D,E,F,G,H,I,K,L,M,N,P,Q,R,S,T,V,W,Y$, GC3, and an $M$-density baseline equal to the fraction of sense codons lying on the union support of the nine projected $B^*_{Q6}$ vectors.

These numbers support a descriptive statement only: part of the $B^*_{Q6}$ to protein-abundance residual association is removed when the mRNA-stability mediator is conditioned on.  They do not identify the molecular route by which that removal occurs.

\paragraph{Boundary of interpretation.}
The readout is a statistical decomposition over two organism-specific panels, not a pathway claim.  The E. coli half-life input is a single growth-rate condition, and the human input is an unlogged mean normalized relative mRNA half-life consensus across $54$ dataset columns.  Abundance and half-life measurements need not be platform-matched or condition-matched across organisms, and the cross-domain comparison is descriptive rather than phylogenetically controlled.  No translation claim follows from this chapter alone: the computation conditions on mRNA half-life or stability, not on an organism-matched translation-efficiency survival matrix.  No folding, physical admissibility, function, mechanism, phenotype, adaptation, causation, or universal biological law follows unless a separate layer-specific contact supplies the appropriate rows and the corresponding controlled statistic.
