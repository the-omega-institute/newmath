\subsection{Rank-powered mRNA-stability mediation for the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ abundance residual}
\label{subsec:bq6-mrna-stability-mediation-crossdomain}

\paragraph{Finite panel and coordinate role.}
This record fixes a finite cross-layer panel in which the nine $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates are evaluated after quotienting away amino-acid composition and after conditioning on ordinary sequence controls.  The coordinate list is
$K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $Leu_{CUN\_vs\_UUR}$, $Leu_{UUA\_vs\_UUG}$, $Ser_{UCR\_vs\_AGY}$, $Ser_{UCA\_vs\_UCG}$, $Thr_{ACR\_vs\_ACY}$, and $f3\_stress$.  The control matrix contains an intercept, $\log(\mathrm{CDS\ length})$, the twenty amino-acid composition proportions $A,C,D,E,F,G,H,I,K,L,M,N,P,Q,R,S,T,V,W,Y$, GC3, and the $M$-density baseline, for $24$ declared control columns and rank $23$ in the finite rows reported here.  The present computation is not a raw codon-use correlation.  It asks whether the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ coordinate block $X$ explains a protein-abundance residual $P$ and how much of that finite association is reduced when a residualized mRNA half-life or stability mediator $M$ is added.

\paragraph{Joined rows.}
Two organism panels are present.  In the \emph{E. coli} panel, the GeneId--b-number join against coding-sequence identifiers produces $2720$ joined rows from $3739$ reported CDS rows and $2947$ valid half-life records at growth rate $0.40\,h^{-1}$.  The CDS join hit rate is $0.9978649586335735$, with $1019$ CDS records skipped for no half-life match and no reported duplicate, invalid-length, empty-codon-count, or nonpositive-abundance skips.  In the human panel, the Ensembl-gene join, with unique gene-symbol fallback, produces $16165$ joined rows from $19053$ reported CDS rows and $19480$ valid consensus half-life records.  The human CDS join hit rate is $0.9779294769799313$; $16150$ rows join by Ensembl gene id and $15$ by gene-symbol fallback, with $12$ duplicate-gene CDS records and $2876$ no-half-life-match CDS records excluded.  These are large finite-row panels, not the rank-saturated $n=3$ surface that must be refused for survival interpretation.

\paragraph{Rank diagnostics.}
After the control residualization, the \emph{E. coli} panel has residual degrees of freedom $2697$, $\operatorname{rank}(X)=9$, $\operatorname{rank}(M)=1$, and $\operatorname{rank}(X,M)=10$.  The corresponding residual energies are $\|P\|^2=2551.728810042108$ and $\|M\|^2=215.43086017169205$.  The human panel has residual degrees of freedom $16142$, $\operatorname{rank}(X)=9$, $\operatorname{rank}(M)=1$, and $\operatorname{rank}(X,M)=10$, with $\|P\|^2=22233.302987773684$ and $\|M\|^2=1422.0291144063049$.  Thus the mediator column is not absorbed into the coordinate block: in both organisms the joint rank is the sum of the $9$-dimensional coordinate block and the one-dimensional mediator readout.

\paragraph{Variance decomposition.}
The reported scalar decomposition is
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{QP} &= \frac{\text{energy explained by }X\text{ on }P}{\text{residual energy of }P},\\
R^2_{QP\mid M} &= \frac{\text{direct energy explained by }X\text{ after conditioning on }M}{\text{residual energy of }P},\\
\mu &= \frac{R^2_{QP}-R^2_{QP\mid M}}{R^2_{QP}}.
\end{aligned}
$$
For \emph{E. coli}, $R^2_{QP}=0.11632311854858134$, $R^2_{QP\mid M}=0.0875945621412319$, and the mediation fraction is $\mu=0.24697202727892137$, with right-tail permutation value $0.000999000999000999$.  The energy audit gives $X$-explained energy on $P$ equal to $296.8250528743585$, $M$ incremental improvement given $X$ equal to $325.3466399519295$, $M$ explained energy on $P$ equal to $398.65412500748283$, and direct $X$ improvement given $M$ equal to $223.51756781880516$.  For the human panel, $R^2_{QP}=0.04095215557813418$, $R^2_{QP\mid M}=0.03517257842112907$, and $\mu=0.1411299863319291$, again with right-tail permutation value $0.000999000999000999$.  The human energy audit gives $X$-explained energy on $P$ equal to $910.5016829711035$, $M$ incremental improvement given $X$ equal to $819.8980203765552$, $M$ explained energy on $P$ equal to $948.3971104494655$, and direct $X$ improvement given $M$ equal to $782.0025928981933$.

\paragraph{Mediator direction and cross-domain status.}
The mediator path is descriptive and organism-specific.  In \emph{E. coli}, the coefficient check for $P$ on $M$ after $X$ is $-1.2432793356009886$, while in human it is $0.7633409850900272$.  The sign difference already blocks any single universal biological reading of the mediator.  The panel is therefore classified only as a cross-layer relation with a joint $9$-coordinate mediation scalar and a per-coordinate audit surface, not as a mechanism theorem.  The finite result says that a residualized mRNA half-life or stability readout removes a measurable fraction of the $B^{*}_{Q6}$--protein-abundance residual association in these two joined panels: about $24.70\%$ in \emph{E. coli} and about $14.11\%$ in human under the stated controls.

\paragraph{Connection to the mRNA-abundance control run.}
The companion mRNA-abundance control computation is useful as a boundary check.  In the four-organism abundance-control run, the same nine coordinates were evaluated with the same $24$-column control design and rank $23$ control matrix.  The joined-row counts were $13140$ for zebrafish, $3415$ for \emph{E. coli}, $11554$ for human, and $5128$ for yeast.  The modeled complement distances were $0.8424312412078521$, $0.6628211177352172$, $0.8566190269974241$, and $0.22386566043976477$, respectively, while the fractions absorbed by residualized mRNA abundance were only $0.011973033879985792$, $0.0017706954554908886$, $0.007858222847072218$, and $0.0018976054721605224$.  That earlier control surface therefore does not supply a causal transcript mechanism; it supplies a finite check that transcript abundance alone absorbs little of the modeled complement in those rows.  The present half-life/stability panel is stronger as a mediation decomposition because it uses a distinct stability readout, but it remains an observational variance partition.

\paragraph{Local admissible claim.}
The admissible claim is limited to the following finite statement: under the specified controls, the $9$-coordinate $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual block has a protein-abundance residual association in \emph{E. coli} and human, and residualized mRNA half-life or stability accounts for a nonzero fraction of that association in the reported joined rows.  The permutation values are small for the recorded statistic, and the rank diagnostics are nondegenerate.  This supports a bounded cross-layer mediation readout, not a pathway assertion.

\paragraph{No-promotion boundary.}
No translation-realization conclusion follows from these rows unless a separate measured translation contact supplies a controlled $S^{QT}$ surface.  The present $M$ column is mRNA half-life or stability, not ribosome occupancy, translation efficiency, tAI, stAI, protein folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, phenotype, adaptation, or a universal law.  Likewise, the protein-abundance readout is steady-state abundance, not a folding assay, not a physical assay, not a perturbation rescue, and not evidence of mechanism by itself.  Any future claim that the same $B^{*}_{Q6}$ coordinate propagates through translation must first estimate $S^{QT}$ under amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, CDS length, $M$-density, mRNA, and simpler-baseline controls; any downstream protein, stability, localization, structure, function, or phenotype statement then requires its own layer-matched contact and statistic.
