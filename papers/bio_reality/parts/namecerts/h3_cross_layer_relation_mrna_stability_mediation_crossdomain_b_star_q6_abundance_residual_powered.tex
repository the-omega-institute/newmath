\subsection{B exorpdfstring{$^*_{Q6}$}{* Q6} residual mediation through mRNA stability}\label{sec:bioreality-q6-mrna-stability-mediation-crossdomain}\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite panel.} The record concerns a finite cross-domain panel in which coding sequences are indexed by organism, a nine-coordinate $B^*_{Q6}$ synonymous residual basis, a protein-abundance residual readout, and an mRNA half-life or stability mediator.  The nine residual coordinates are
$$
\begin{aligned}
&K\_AAA,\quad Arg\_AGR,\quad Ile\_AUA,\quad Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR,\quad Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG,\\
&Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY,\quad Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG,\quad Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY,\quad f3\_stress.
\end{aligned}
$$
These coordinates are not amino-acid composition effects: the estimation residualizes against an intercept, $\log({\rm cds\_len\_nt})$, the twenty amino-acid composition proportions $A,C,D,E,F,G,H,I,K,L,M,N,P,Q,R,S,T,V,W,Y$, GC3, and an $M$-density baseline given by the fraction of sense codons lying on the union support of the nine projected $B^*_{Q6}$ vectors.  In both computed organisms the control matrix has $24$ columns and rank $23$, so the reported quantities are read as residual finite-row associations after these sequence-level controls, not as raw codon-usage effects.

\paragraph{Joined rows and rank checks.} The computed cross-domain instance contains two organisms.  For \emph{Escherichia coli} K-12 MG1655, the half-life join uses Esquerre GeneId $b$-numbers against CDS locus tags at growth rate $0.40\,h^{-1}$.  It reports $3739$ joined CDS records upstream, $2947$ valid half-life records, and $2720$ rows in the final three-way panel, with a CDS join hit rate of $0.9978649586335735$.  The residual degrees of freedom after the control matrix are $2697$.  For human, the join maps Agarwal Ensembl Gene Ids, with a unique gene-symbol fallback for $15$ records; it reports $19053$ upstream CDS joins, $19480$ consensus records, and $16165$ final rows, with join sources $16150$ by Ensembl identifier and $15$ by symbol fallback.  The human CDS join hit rate is $0.9779294769799313$, and the residual degrees of freedom after controls are $16142$.  The rank diagnostics are non-vacuous in both organisms: $rank(X)=9$, $rank(M)=1$, and $rank(X,M)=10$, below the residual row counts and above the degenerate threshold logic used to reject saturated fits.  Thus the panel is large enough to report variance partitions rather than an all-fitted rank artifact.

\paragraph{Residual decomposition.} Let $X$ denote the residualized $9$-coordinate $B^*_{Q6}$ design, $M$ the residualized mRNA half-life or stability readout, and $P$ the residualized protein-abundance readout.  The statistic is a descriptive decomposition of the $X\to P$ association before and after conditioning on $M$:
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{QP} &= R^2(P\mid X,Z),\\
R^2_{QP\mid M} &= R^2(P\mid X,M,Z)-R^2(P\mid M,Z),\\
\mu &= \frac{R^2_{QP}-R^2_{QP\mid M}}{R^2_{QP}}.
\end{aligned}
$$
Here $\mu$ is a mediation fraction only in the variance-partition sense.  In \emph{E. coli}, $R^2_{QP}=0.11632311854858134$, $R^2_{QP\mid M}=0.0875945621412319$, and $\mu=0.24697202727892137$, with a deterministic right-tail permutation value $p=0.000999000999000999$.  In human, $R^2_{QP}=0.04095215557813418$, $R^2_{QP\mid M}=0.03517257842112907$, and $\mu=0.1411299863319291$, with the same reported right-tail permutation value $p=0.000999000999000999$.  The two organisms therefore both show that conditioning on the mRNA half-life/stability readout reduces, but does not eliminate, the finite-row $B^*_{Q6}$ association with protein abundance.

\paragraph{Energy diagnostics.} The scalar energy checks give the same bounded interpretation.  In \emph{E. coli}, the protein residual energy is $2551.728810042108$, the mediator residual energy is $215.43086017169205$, $X$ explains $296.8250528743585$ units on $P$, $M$ explains $398.65412500748283$ units on $P$, and the incremental improvement of $M$ given $X$ is $325.3466399519295$.  The direct $X$ improvement after $M$ is $223.51756781880516$, while $R^2_{M\mid X}=0.12750047680284674$ and the joint $Q$--$M$ path check is $0.02298556640056076$.  In human, the protein residual energy is $22233.302987773684$, the mediator residual energy is $1422.0291144063049$, $X$ explains $910.5016829711035$ units on $P$, $M$ explains $948.3971104494655$ units on $P$, and the incremental improvement of $M$ given $X$ is $819.8980203765552$.  The direct $X$ improvement after $M$ is $782.0025928981933$, with $R^2_{M\mid X}=0.036877022762988716$ and joint path check $0.010503670362051522$.  These values support a finite-row attenuation statement: the stability readout absorbs part of the residual protein-abundance association, and the remaining direct component stays measurable in the same controlled design.

\paragraph{Relation to the mRNA-abundance control check.} A separate finite-row check treated matched mRNA abundance as an $H$-candidate by projecting the modelled translation-complement abundance residual onto residualized $\log_{10}$ mRNA.  That check used the same nine coordinates and the same $24$-column control design.  It reported, for example, $d_{\rm modeled}=0.6628211177352172$ and mRNA absorption $0.0017706954554908886$ in \emph{E. coli}, $d_{\rm modeled}=0.8566190269974241$ and absorption $0.007858222847072218$ in human, $d_{\rm modeled}=0.8424312412078521$ and absorption $0.011973033879985792$ in zebrafish, and $d_{\rm modeled}=0.22386566043976477$ and absorption $0.0018976054721605224$ in yeast.  This earlier abundance-control readout is not the same object as the present half-life mediator: it is used here only to mark that transcript abundance controls and RNA stability or half-life readouts are distinct finite-row surfaces.  The present two-organism result is specifically about conditioning the protein-abundance residual on an mRNA half-life/stability coordinate.

\paragraph{Local interpretation.} The chapter therefore records a cross-layer residual association with partial attenuation through mRNA stability in two organism-specific finite panels.  The admissible statement is descriptive: after amino-acid composition, length, GC3, $M$-density, and organism-specific joins are fixed, the $B^*_{Q6}$ residual block retains protein-abundance association, and an mRNA stability or half-life readout explains a measurable part of that association.  The result is not a causal pathway.  It uses one half-life source per organism, is observational, and compares organisms without a phylogenetic design.  It also does not establish translation survival: no $S^{QT}$ translation-readout matrix with same-row ribosome occupancy or translation efficiency is computed in this record.  Consequently no claim about translation realization, protein folding, physical admissibility, biological function, molecular mechanism, phenotype, or universality follows from this chapter unless a separate layer-matched contact supplies the corresponding measurement and statistic.
