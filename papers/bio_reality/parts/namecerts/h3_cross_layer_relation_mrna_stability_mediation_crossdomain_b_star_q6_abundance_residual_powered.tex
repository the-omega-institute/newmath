\subsection{Cross-domain $B^*_{Q6}$ mRNA-stability mediation of protein-abundance residuals}
\label{sec:bior-reality-q6-mrna-stability-mediation-crossdomain}

\paragraph{Finite panel.}
The row object is a matched coding-sequence panel in which each retained row carries an organism key, a coding-sequence identifier, a nine-dimensional $B^*_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinate vector, the usual sequence controls, a protein-abundance readout, and an mRNA half-life or stability readout. The nine coordinates are
$K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $Leu_{CUN\_vs\_UUR}$, $Leu_{UUA\_vs\_UUG}$, $Ser_{UCR\_vs\_AGY}$, $Ser_{UCA\_vs\_UCG}$, $Thr_{ACR\_vs\_ACY}$, and $f_3\_stress$. The control block has $24$ columns: an intercept, $\log(\mathrm{cds\_len\_nt})$, the $20$ amino-acid composition proportions $A,C,D,E,F,G,H,I,K,L,M,N,P,Q,R,S,T,V,W,Y$, $GC3$, and the $M$-density baseline defined by the fraction of sense codons on the union support of the nine projected $B^*_{Q6}$ vectors. After residualizing against this block, the two organisms with a three-way abundance--half-life--CDS join are \emph{E. coli} and human. For \emph{E. coli}, $2720$ stable identifiers remain, with $3739$ CDS rows reported before the half-life join, $2947$ valid half-life records, and a CDS join hit rate $0.9978649586335735$. The half-life readout is taken at growth rate $0.40\,h^{-1}$, using the b-number join between the Esquerre GeneId field and the CDS locus-tag key. For human, $16165$ stable identifiers remain, with $19053$ CDS rows reported before the half-life join, $19480$ consensus half-life records, and a CDS join hit rate $0.9779294769799313$. Of those human rows, $16150$ join by Ensembl gene identifier and $15$ by the unique gene-symbol fallback; the fallback has $0$ ambiguous keys.

\paragraph{Residual ranks.}
The residual design is nondegenerate in both organisms. The control rank is $\operatorname{rank}(Z)=23$, the residualized $B^*_{Q6}$ block has $\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde X)=9$, the residualized mRNA-stability mediator has $\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde M)=1$, and the joint residual block has $\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde X,\widetilde M)=10$. The residual degrees of freedom after the sequence controls are $2697$ for \emph{E. coli} and $16142$ for human, so the all-one or saturated-fit failure mode is not the explanation for the reported fractions. The numerical rank boundary used for degeneracy is $R^2 \geq 0.999999$; neither organism approaches that boundary in the joint mediation computations.

\paragraph{Abundance association before the mediator.}
Let $P$ denote residualized protein abundance, $X$ the residualized nine-coordinate $B^*_{Q6}$ block, and $M$ the residualized mRNA half-life or stability readout. The direct abundance readback before conditioning on $M$ is measured by $R^2_{QP}$. The finite rows give
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{QP}(\textit{E. coli}) &= 0.11632311854858134,\\
R^2_{QP}(\mathrm{human}) &= 0.04095215557813418.
\end{aligned}
$$
The corresponding residual-energy diagnostics are consistent with a real but organism-dependent projection: for \emph{E. coli}, $p$-residual energy is $2551.728810042108$, $X$ explains $296.8250528743585$ units of energy on $P$, and $M$ explains $398.65412500748283$ units of energy on $P$. For human, $p$-residual energy is $22233.302987773684$, $X$ explains $910.5016829711035$ units of energy on $P$, and $M$ explains $948.3971104494655$ units of energy on $P$. These numbers are not interpreted as a causal pathway; they only say that, in the finite matched panels, the residual coordinates and the residual half-life/stability column both carry descriptive projection mass against the protein-abundance residual.

\paragraph{Conditioning on mRNA half-life or stability.}
The mediation statistic asks how much of the $B^*_{Q6}$ abundance association is removed after conditioning on the residualized mRNA-stability column. The reported conditional fits are
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{QP\mid M}(\textit{E. coli}) &= 0.0875945621412319,\\
R^2_{QP\mid M}(\mathrm{human}) &= 0.03517257842112907.
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the variance-partition fraction attributed to the residualized half-life or stability readout is
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mu_{QMP}(\textit{E. coli})
  &= \frac{0.11632311854858134-0.0875945621412319}{0.11632311854858134}
   = 0.24697202727892137,\\
\mu_{QMP}(\mathrm{human})
  &= \frac{0.04095215557813418-0.03517257842112907}{0.04095215557813418}
   = 0.1411299863319291.
\end{aligned}
$$
Both right-tail residual-mediator permutation checks report $p=0.000999000999000999$ under deterministic seeds keyed by organism and permutation index. The finite conclusion is therefore asymmetric but positive in both domains: roughly $24.7\%$ of the residual $B^*_{Q6}$--protein-abundance association in \emph{E. coli} and $14.1\%$ in human is partitioned through the residualized mRNA half-life or stability column. The supporting path-a joint checks are small, $R^2_{QM}=0.02298556640056076$ for \emph{E. coli} and $0.010503670362051522$ for human, which is compatible with a weak mediator-side coordinate association rather than a saturated mediator fit. The fitted coefficient of $P$ on $M$ after $X$ differs in sign across domains, $-1.2432793356009886$ in \emph{E. coli} and $0.7633409850900272$ in human, so the chapter treats the result as a cross-domain descriptive decomposition, not as a shared biochemical direction.

\paragraph{Readout boundary.}
The computation uses organism-matched sequence controls, protein abundance, and an mRNA half-life or stability readout. It does not compute a direct ribosome-profiling or translation-efficiency survival matrix $S^{QT}$, and it does not show that any $B^*_{Q6}$ coordinate survives as a translation-readout coordinate after matched mRNA controls. Consequently, the chapter only licenses the finite statement that $B^*_{Q6}$ abundance residual mass is partially reduced by conditioning on the available mRNA half-life or stability column in the two joined organisms. It does not certify translation, folding, physical admissibility, biological function, molecular mechanism, phenotype, adaptation, causation, or universality. Any such claim would require a separate layer-matched contact: for example, a same-row translation-efficiency or ribosome-footprint panel for translation, a structural or physical assay for folded or admissible state, a perturbation or binding assay for mechanism, and a function or phenotype assay for higher biological readback.

\paragraph{Local conclusion.}
On the finite rows, the admissible statement is a bounded statistical one. The $B^*_{Q6}$ residual block is rank-powered after the stated sequence controls; the mediator is nondegenerate; the protein-abundance association decreases after conditioning on mRNA half-life or stability in both organisms; and the computed mediation fractions are $0.24697202727892137$ for \emph{E. coli} and $0.1411299863319291$ for human, each with permutation right-tail $p=0.000999000999000999$. The result is therefore ready to report as a cross-domain residual-abundance decomposition through mRNA stability, while remaining blocked against promotion to translation survival, protein mechanism, structure, function, or general biological law.
