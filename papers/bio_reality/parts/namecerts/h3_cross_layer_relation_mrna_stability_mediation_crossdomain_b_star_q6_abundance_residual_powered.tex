\subsection{Rank-powered mRNA-stability mediation for B$^*_{Q6}$ abundance residuals}\label{sec:biorality-q6-mrna-stability-mediation-crossdomain}\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite panel.} The record is a cross-layer relation, not a mechanism claim. Its rows are coding sequences joined to organism-specific codon counts, nine B$^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinates, protein-abundance residuals, and an mRNA half-life or stability readout. The coordinate list is $K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $Leu_{CUN\_vs\_UUR}$, $Leu_{UUA\_vs\_UUG}$, $Ser_{UCR\_vs\_AGY}$, $Ser_{UCA\_vs\_UCG}$, $Thr_{ACR\_vs\_ACY}$, and $f3_{stress}$. The control block contains an intercept, $\log(CDS\ length)$, the twenty amino-acid composition proportions $A,C,D,E,F,G,H,I,K,L,M,N,P,Q,R,S,T,V,W,Y$, $GC3$, and the M-density baseline defined as the fraction of sense codons lying in the union support of the nine projected B$^*_{Q6}$ vectors. In both computed organisms the control-column count is $24$ and the control rank is $23$.

\paragraph{Joined rows.} The Escherichia coli K-12 MG1655 join uses Esquerre GeneId b-number against the CDS locus tag or match id. It reports $3739$ CDS rows, $2947$ valid half-life records, and $2720$ three-way joined rows, with CDS join hit rate $0.9978649586335735$. The skipped-CDS ledger is concentrated in $1019$ rows with no half-life match, while duplicate locus tags, empty sense-codon counts, invalid lengths, missing locus tags, non-object rows, and nonpositive abundances are all $0$. The human join uses Ensembl gene id without version against the CDS header gene field, with a unique gene-symbol fallback. It reports $19053$ CDS rows, $19480$ consensus records, and $16165$ joined rows, with hit rate $0.9779294769799313$. The human join source split is $16150$ rows through Ensembl gene id and $15$ rows through gene-symbol fallback; ambiguous symbol fallback keys are $0$, unique symbol fallback keys are $19479$, and skipped CDS rows include $2876$ rows with no half-life match and $12$ duplicate-gene rows. These row counts are large enough for a rank-powered residual calculation rather than an all-fitted small-panel artifact.

\paragraph{Residual ranks.} After removing the $Z$ controls, the E. coli residual degrees of freedom are $2697$. The residual mediator rank is $rank(M\_{\sim})=1$, the B$^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinate rank is $rank(X\_{\sim})=9$, and the joint rank is $rank(XM\_{\sim})=10$. Human has the same rank pattern, with $16142$ residual degrees of freedom. The recorded degenerate-$R^2$ threshold is $0.999999$, so the reported mediation numbers are not accepted merely because a fitted row space has saturated. The key rank audit is therefore finite and explicit:
$$\begin{aligned}
rank_Z &= 23,\\
rank(X\_{\sim}) &= 9,\\
rank(M\_{\sim}) &= 1,\\
rank(XM\_{\sim}) &= 10.
\end{aligned}$$
This is the local reason the statistic may be read as a controlled variance partition, while still not being read as causal biology.

\paragraph{Mediation surface.} Let $X$ denote the residualized nine-coordinate B$^*_{Q6}$ block, $M$ the residualized mRNA half-life or stability readout, and $P$ the residualized protein-abundance readout. The computed finite-row decomposition is the loss of the direct $X \to P$ residual association after conditioning on $M$. In E. coli the protein residual energy is $2551.728810042108$, the mediator residual energy is $215.43086017169205$, the $X$-explained energy on $P$ is $296.8250528743585$, and the $M$ incremental improvement energy given $X$ is $325.3466399519295$. The corresponding values are $R^2_{QP}=0.11632311854858134$, $R^2_{QP\mid M}=0.0875945621412319$, and mediation fraction $0.24697202727892137$, with right-tail permutation value $0.000999000999000999$. In human the protein residual energy is $22233.302987773684$, the mediator residual energy is $1422.0291144063049$, the $X$-explained energy on $P$ is $910.5016829711035$, and the $M$ incremental improvement energy given $X$ is $819.8980203765552$. The corresponding values are $R^2_{QP}=0.04095215557813418$, $R^2_{QP\mid M}=0.03517257842112907$, and mediation fraction $0.1411299863319291$, again with right-tail permutation value $0.000999000999000999$.

\paragraph{Cross-domain comparison.} The two organisms agree in sign at the level of variance partition: conditioning on the residualized mRNA-stability readout reduces the B$^*_{Q6}$ association with the protein-abundance residual in both panels. The magnitude is larger in E. coli, where approximately $24.6972\%$ of the controlled $Q \to P$ residual association is assigned to the mRNA-stability mediator, than in human, where the assigned fraction is approximately $14.1130\%$. The auxiliary rank diagnostics also show that the mediator is not simply identical to the B$^*_{Q6}$ block: $R^2_{M\mid X}=0.12750047680284674$ in E. coli and $0.036877022762988716$ in human, while the joint $Q,M$ path-a check is $0.02298556640056076$ in E. coli and $0.010503670362051522$ in human. The fitted coefficient of $P$ on $M$ given $X$ differs by organism, with $-1.2432793356009886$ in E. coli and $0.7633409850900272$ in human, so the record should be read as a descriptive cross-domain comparison rather than as one conserved signed pathway.

\paragraph{Relation to the abundance-control companion run.} The same B$^*_{Q6}$ coordinate and $Z$-control geometry was also used in the mRNA-abundance H-candidate residual check. That companion run reports four organism panels: Danio rerio with $n=13140$, E. coli with $n=3415$, Homo sapiens with $n=11554$, and Saccharomyces cerevisiae with $n=5128$, each using $24$ control columns and $rank_Z=23$. Its modeled-complement abundance residual values were $d=0.8424312412078521$ for Danio rerio, $0.6628211177352172$ for E. coli, $0.8566190269974241$ for human, and $0.22386566043976477$ for yeast. The mRNA-abundance absorption terms were small in all four panels: $0.011973033879985792$, $0.0017706954554908886$, $0.007858222847072218$, and $0.0018976054721605224$, respectively. Those values are not the half-life mediation statistic above; they are used here only to localize the new half-life result against a nearby transcript-layer control run that did not promote itself to causal explanation.

\paragraph{Readback verdict.} The finite-row verdict is that the B$^*_{Q6}$ block has a measurable controlled association with protein-abundance residuals in both the E. coli and human panels, and that a nonzero fraction of that association is statistically partitioned through a residualized mRNA half-life or stability readout. The computation is rank-powered because the mediator and coordinate ranks remain below the residual row counts by thousands of degrees of freedom, and because the reported statistic is not an $n=3$ saturated fit. The operational summary is
$$\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{E.coli}:\quad &R^2_{QP}=0.11632311854858134,\quad R^2_{QP\mid M}=0.0875945621412319,\quad \phi_M=0.24697202727892137,\\
\mathrm{human}:\quad &R^2_{QP}=0.04095215557813418,\quad R^2_{QP\mid M}=0.03517257842112907,\quad \phi_M=0.1411299863319291.
\end{aligned}$$
The module classification is therefore a cross-layer relation with statistical mediation semantics: $Q$ is a residual codon-coordinate block, $M$ is a transcript-stability mediator readout, and $P$ is a protein-abundance residual readout.

\paragraph{Local non-promotion rule.} Nothing in this subsection establishes translation survival, ribosome occupancy, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, organismal phenotype, mechanism, or universality. The half-life mediator is observational, per organism, and condition-sensitive; the abundance and half-life measurements do not come from a perturbation design that can order causes. The mediation fraction is a variance partition after the declared controls, not a biochemical pathway proof. In particular, a separate direct translation readout is still required before any $S^{QT}$ claim is made, and a separate layer-specific contact is required before any claim about folding, function, physical admissibility, protein state, localization, phenotype, or global biological law is allowed.
