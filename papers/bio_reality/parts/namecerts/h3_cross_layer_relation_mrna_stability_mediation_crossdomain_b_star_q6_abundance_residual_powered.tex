\subsection{Cross-domain mRNA-stability mediation for the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ abundance residual}
\label{sec:bior-b-star-q6-mrna-stability-mediation-crossdomain}

\paragraph{Finite row system.}
This record concerns a finite matched panel in which each row is a coding sequence with an organism label, a nine-dimensional $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual coordinate, a protein-abundance readout, and an mRNA half-life or stability readout whenever the identifier join exists.  The coordinate block is
$K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $Leu_{CUN\,vs\,UUR}$, $Leu_{UUA\,vs\,UUG}$, $Ser_{UCR\,vs\,AGY}$, $Ser_{UCA\,vs\,UCG}$, $Thr_{ACR\,vs\,ACY}$, and $f_{3}$-stress.  The control block $Z$ contains an intercept, $\log(\mathrm{cds\_len\_nt})$, the twenty amino-acid composition proportions computed from real CDS codon counts, $GC3$, and the $M$-density baseline defined as the fraction of sense codons on the union support of the nine projected $B^{*}_{Q6}$ vectors.  Thus the finite computation separates an amino-acid-quotient-surviving synonymous residual block from the amino-acid sequence component before the stability readout is allowed to enter the protein-abundance analysis.

\paragraph{Residualized matrices.}
Let $X$ denote the $Z$-residualized $n\times 9$ matrix of $B^{*}_{Q6}$ coordinates, let $M$ denote the $Z$-residualized mRNA half-life or stability column, and let $P$ denote the $Z$-residualized protein-abundance column.  The reported mediation statistic is not a causal path coefficient.  It is the finite variance partition obtained by comparing the protein-abundance energy explained by $X$ before and after the stability column is added.  In the notation of this chapter, the descriptive fraction is
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mu_{QMP}
  &= \frac{R^{2}_{QP}-R^{2}_{QP\mid M}}{R^{2}_{QP}},
\end{aligned}
$$
where $R^{2}_{QP}$ is the residual protein-abundance energy explained by the nine-dimensional $B^{*}_{Q6}$ block after $Z$, and $R^{2}_{QP\mid M}$ is the remaining direct $B^{*}_{Q6}$ contribution after conditioning on residualized mRNA stability.  A positive value of $\mu_{QMP}$ says only that the stability column absorbs part of the $Q$--to--protein association in this finite design.

\paragraph{Identifier joins and rank checks.}
Two organisms have complete finite-row computations.  In \emph{E. coli} K-12 MG1655, the join uses Esquerre GeneId b-numbers against CDS locus tags at growth rate $0.40\,h^{-1}$.  The panel has $2720$ joined rows, $3739$ reported joined CDS records, $2947$ valid half-life records, and a reported CDS join hit rate $0.9978649586335735$.  The $Z$ block has $24$ control columns and rank $23$; after residualizing by $Z$, the remaining degrees of freedom are $2697$.  The ranks are $\operatorname{rank}(X)=9$, $\operatorname{rank}(M)=1$, and $\operatorname{rank}(X,M)=10$, so the calculation is not a saturated all-fit row-space artifact.  In human, the join uses Ensembl gene identifiers with a unique gene-symbol fallback.  It has $16165$ joined rows, $19053$ reported joined CDS records, $19480$ valid consensus half-life records, $16150$ Ensembl-gene matches, $15$ symbol-fallback matches, no ambiguous symbol-fallback keys, and a reported CDS join hit rate $0.9779294769799313$.  The human $Z$ block again has $24$ control columns and rank $23$; the residual degrees of freedom are $16142$, with ranks $\operatorname{rank}(X)=9$, $\operatorname{rank}(M)=1$, and $\operatorname{rank}(X,M)=10$.

\paragraph{Readout boundary.}
These numbers support a bounded statement: in two finite organism-specific panels, residualized mRNA half-life or stability absorbs a nonzero fraction of the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ to protein-abundance residual association, with permutation right-tail value $0.000999000999000999$ in both reported panels.  The computation does not by itself supply a direct ribosome-profiling translation-efficiency matrix $S^{QT}$, nor does it turn the modeled tAI or stAI contact into observed ribosome occupancy.  It also does not identify a molecular mechanism for mRNA decay.  The stability column is a measured or consensus readout used inside an observational variance decomposition; it is not a perturbational proof that codon residuals cause mRNA half-life differences, and it is not a proof that mRNA half-life causes the downstream protein-abundance component.

\paragraph{Local non-promotion rule.}
No translation realization, folding claim, physical admissibility claim, molecular-function claim, mechanistic pathway, phenotype claim, adaptation claim, or universal biological law follows from this finite-row record alone.  Such a conclusion would require a separate layer-matched contact: for translation, same-row ribosome or translation-efficiency observations; for folding or physical admissibility, structure or physical-assay contacts; for function or phenotype, functional, perturbational, or system-level assays.  The present chapter records only a rank-checked, controlled, organism-specific statistical mediation surface for $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates, mRNA stability, and protein abundance.
