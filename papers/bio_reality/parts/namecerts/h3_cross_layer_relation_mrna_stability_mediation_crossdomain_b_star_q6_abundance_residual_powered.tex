\subsection{B-star Q6 mRNA-Stability Mediation Across Protein-Abundance Residuals}\label{sec:h3-cross-layer-relation-mrna-stability-mediation-crossdomain-b-star-q6-abundance-residual-powered}\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite panel and scope.} This record treats the $B^*_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates as a finite cross-layer covariate family, not as an autonomous biological mechanism. The panel contains nine residual coordinates, namely $K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $Leu_{CUN\_vs\_UUR}$, $Leu_{UUA\_vs\_UUG}$, $Ser_{UCR\_vs\_AGY}$, $Ser_{UCA\_vs\_UCG}$, $Thr_{ACR\_vs\_ACY}$, and $f3_{stress}$. The control block has $24$ columns: an intercept, $\log(\mathrm{CDS\ length\ in\ nt})$, the $20$ amino-acid composition proportions $A,C,D,E,F,G,H,I,K,L,M,N,P,Q,R,S,T,V,W,Y$, $GC3$, and the $M$-density baseline given by the fraction of sense codons lying in the union support of the nine projected $B^*_{Q6}$ vectors. The classification is cross-layer relation, and the finite readout is restricted to residual protein abundance together with residual mRNA half-life or stability. It does not by itself assert translation realization, folding behavior, physical admissibility, molecular function, causal mechanism, or biological universality.

\paragraph{Three-way row joins.} The computation uses two organism panels. In the \emph{E. coli} panel, the join key is Esquerre GeneId b-number matched to the CDS locus tag or match identifier. The reported CDS join count is $3739$, the half-life table has $2947$ valid gene-id records, and the resulting three-way joined panel has $2720$ stable rows, with reported CDS hit rate $0.9978649586335735$. The half-life readout is the growth-rate $0.40\,h^{-1}$ condition. In the human panel, the join key is Ensembl Gene Id without version matched to CDS header $gene:ENSG$, with a unique gene-symbol fallback. The reported CDS join count is $19053$, the consensus half-life table has $19480$ valid Ensembl records, and the resulting panel has $16165$ stable rows, with reported CDS hit rate $0.9779294769799313$. The human join uses $16150$ Ensembl-gene matches and $15$ gene-symbol fallback matches, with $0$ ambiguous symbol-fallback keys. These row counts are large enough to avoid the rank-saturation pathology that invalidates tiny all-perfect residual fits.

\paragraph{Rank diagnostics.} After removing the $Z$ controls, the \emph{E. coli} panel has residual degrees of freedom $2697$, $\operatorname{rank}(Z)=23$, $\operatorname{rank}(X)=9$, $\operatorname{rank}(M)=1$, and $\operatorname{rank}(X,M)=10$. The human panel has residual degrees of freedom $16142$ with the same ranks: $\operatorname{rank}(Z)=23$, $\operatorname{rank}(X)=9$, $\operatorname{rank}(M)=1$, and $\operatorname{rank}(X,M)=10$. Here $X$ denotes the nine-dimensional $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinate block and $M$ denotes the residualized mRNA half-life or stability mediator. The declared degeneracy threshold is $0.999999$, and the observed mediator-on-coordinate checks remain far below it: $R^2_{QM}=0.02298556640056076$ in \emph{E. coli} and $R^2_{QM}=0.010503670362051522$ in human. Thus the mediation computation is rank-powered rather than a saturated coordinate artifact.

These numbers support a descriptive statement: conditioning on mRNA half-life or stability reduces part of the $B^*_{Q6}$ to protein-abundance residual association in both organisms. The larger fraction appears in \emph{E. coli}; the human panel has a weaker but still nonzero attenuation.

\paragraph{Ordered interpretation.} The admissible reading is an ordered residual decomposition. First, $B^*_{Q6}$ is a synonymous residual coordinate block after amino-acid quotienting and after the stated sequence controls. Second, the mRNA half-life or stability readout is a single residual mediator attached by organism-specific data joins. Third, the protein-abundance residual is decomposed into an $X$ component, an $M$ component, and the remaining direct $X$ component after $M$ is included. In compact notation, the reported finite-row result is
$$\begin{aligned}
\Delta_{E.coli} &= R^2_{QP} - R^2_{QP\mid M} = 0.02872855640734944,\\
\Delta_{human} &= R^2_{QP} - R^2_{QP\mid M} = 0.00577957715700511.
\end{aligned}$$
The associated mediation fractions are therefore descriptive variance partitions, not causal pathway coefficients. The data show that a residual stability readout absorbs part of the coordinate-to-protein association, but the computation does not decide whether codon choice causes stability, whether stability causes protein abundance, or whether a hidden condition-specific variable explains both.

\paragraph{Local exclusion rule.} No translation, ribosome dynamics, folded structure, physical admissibility, molecular function, organismal phenotype, mechanism, adaptation claim, or universal law follows from these finite rows. The mRNA half-life/stability readout is observational and organism-specific: one \emph{E. coli} half-life dataset and one human consensus half-life dataset are used, and abundance and half-life measurements need not share platform, condition, or perturbation design. A downstream protein-abundance signal may be discussed only as the residual variance partition displayed above. Any stronger assertion requires a separate layer-matched measurement: direct translation efficiency or ribosome profiling for translation, protein-state or turnover data for stability mechanisms, structural measurements for folding, perturbation or rescue data for causation, and functional or phenotype assays for biological function.
