\subsection{Cross-domain mRNA-stability mediation for the $B^*_{Q6}$ abundance residual}
\label{sec:q6-mrna-stability-mediation-crossdomain}

\paragraph{Finite panel and coordinate scope.}
This record treats the $B^*_{Q6}$ residual basis as a finite coding-sequence panel rather than as a free biological explanation.  The rows are coding sequences with organism-specific joins to protein abundance and an mRNA half-life or stability readout.  The tested coordinate block has nine columns, namely $\mathrm{K}_{AAA}$, $\mathrm{Arg}_{AGR}$, $\mathrm{Ile}_{AUA}$, $\mathrm{Leu}_{CUN\,vs\,UUR}$, $\mathrm{Leu}_{UUA\,vs\,UUG}$, $\mathrm{Ser}_{UCR\,vs\,AGY}$, $\mathrm{Ser}_{UCA\,vs\,UCG}$, $\mathrm{Thr}_{ACR\,vs\,ACY}$, and $f_3$-stress.  The nuisance block $Z$ contains an intercept, $\log(\mathrm{CDS\ length})$, twenty amino-acid composition proportions from the real coding-sequence codon counts, GC3, and an $M$-density baseline defined on the union support of the nine projected $B^*_{Q6}$ vectors.  In both computed organisms the control matrix has $24$ columns and rank $23$, while the residualized $B^*_{Q6}$ block has rank $9$.

\paragraph{Joined half-life panels.}
Two organism-specific three-way joins are available for this mediation calculation.  For \textit{Escherichia coli} K-12 MG1655, the half-life table is read at growth rate $0.40\,\mathrm{h}^{-1}$ and is joined by the rule that the Esquerre GeneId b-number equals the CDS locus tag or matching identifier.  The reported CDS join has $3739$ CDS rows, the half-life table has $2947$ valid gene-id records, and the final three-way panel contains $2720$ joined rows.  The reported CDS hit rate is $0.9978649586335735$; the missing half-life match count among skipped CDS records is $1019$, with no duplicate locus tags, invalid lengths, empty sense-codon counts, or nonpositive abundance rows recorded.  For the human panel, Agarwal Ensembl gene identifiers without versions are joined to CDS headers, with a unique gene-symbol fallback.  The reported CDS join has $19053$ CDS rows, the consensus half-life table has $19480$ valid Ensembl-gene records across $54$ dataset columns, and the final joined panel contains $16165$ rows.  Of these human joins, $16150$ use Ensembl gene id and $15$ use the unique gene-symbol fallback; the reported CDS hit rate is $0.9779294769799313$, with $2876$ CDS rows lacking a half-life match and $12$ duplicate-gene CDS records skipped.

\paragraph{Residual rank and energy checks.}
After removing $Z$, the \textit{E. coli} panel has residual degrees of freedom $2697$, protein residual energy $2551.728810042108$, mediator residual energy $215.43086017169205$, $\operatorname{rank}(M)=1$, $\operatorname{rank}(X)=9$, and $\operatorname{rank}(X,M)=10$.  The human panel has residual degrees of freedom $16142$, protein residual energy $22233.302987773684$, mediator residual energy $1422.0291144063049$, $\operatorname{rank}(M)=1$, $\operatorname{rank}(X)=9$, and $\operatorname{rank}(X,M)=10$.  Thus neither organism is an $n=3$ saturated display: both have many more residual rows than fitted coordinate directions, and the rank diagnostics leave a non-vacuous residual subspace.

The corresponding variance-partition fractions are $0.24697202727892137$ for \textit{E. coli} and $0.1411299863319291$ for human.  The right-tail permutation value is $0.000999000999000999$ in both organisms under the recorded deterministic residual-mediator permutation seeds.  These values support a descriptive statement: part of the residual association between the nine-dimensional $B^*_{Q6}$ block and protein abundance is reduced after conditioning on residualized mRNA half-life or stability.  The calculation does not by itself identify a causal path.

\paragraph{Mediator-specific diagnostics.}
The scalar rank checks give the mediator a visible but limited role.  In \textit{E. coli}, the mediator explains $398.65412500748283$ units of protein residual energy, while the incremental mediator improvement after the nine $B^*_{Q6}$ coordinates is $325.3466399519295$; the direct coordinate improvement after the mediator is $223.51756781880516$.  The joint check for the coordinate-to-mediator path has $R^2=0.02298556640056076$, and the coefficient check for protein on mediator after coordinates is $-1.2432793356009886$.  In human, the mediator explains $948.3971104494655$ units of protein residual energy, the incremental mediator improvement after coordinates is $819.8980203765552$, and the direct coordinate improvement after the mediator is $782.0025928981933$.  The corresponding coordinate-to-mediator path check has $R^2=0.010503670362051522$, and the protein-on-mediator coefficient check after coordinates is $0.7633409850900272$.

$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{M\mid X}(\textit{E. coli}) &= 0.12750047680284674, &
R^2_{QM}(\textit{E. coli}) &= 0.02298556640056076, \\
R^2_{M\mid X}(\mathrm{human}) &= 0.036877022762988716, &
R^2_{QM}(\mathrm{human}) &= 0.010503670362051522.
\end{aligned}
$$

These numbers make the cross-domain status precise.  The mediator is not merely named; it is joined to the same finite coding-sequence rows and changes the residual protein-abundance fit.  At the same time, the $Q$-to-mediator path is weak in ordinary $R^2$ terms, so the result is best read as a controlled variance decomposition, not as a molecular mechanism.

\paragraph{Relation to the mRNA-abundance control run.}
A separate abundance-control computation places transcript abundance in the control role rather than in the half-life mediator role.  That calculation used the same nine $B^*_{Q6}$ coordinates and the same $24$-column control design.  The joined row counts were $13140$ for zebrafish, $3415$ for \textit{E. coli}, $11554$ for human, and $5128$ for yeast.  The modeled-complement distances on the all-readout joins were $0.8424312412078521$, $0.6628211177352172$, $0.8566190269974241$, and $0.22386566043976477$, respectively.  The additional absorption by residualized $\log_{10}$ mRNA was small: $0.011973033879985792$ in zebrafish, $0.0017706954554908886$ in \textit{E. coli}, $0.007858222847072218$ in human, and $0.0018976054721605224$ in yeast.  This companion run keeps ordinary mRNA abundance separate from mRNA half-life or stability: abundance is a transcript-level covariate, whereas the present calculation asks whether a stability readout partitions the residual protein-abundance association.

\paragraph{Local admissible claim.}
The admissible claim is narrow.  In the two computed organisms, a finite row-wise panel joins coding-sequence codon counts, nine amino-acid-quotient-surviving $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinates, protein abundance, and an mRNA half-life or stability readout.  Under the stated amino-acid composition, length, GC3, and $M$-density controls, conditioning on the residualized stability mediator reduces the $B^*_{Q6}$-to-protein residual association from $0.11632311854858134$ to $0.0875945621412319$ in \textit{E. coli} and from $0.04095215557813418$ to $0.03517257842112907$ in human.  The recorded mediation fractions are therefore finite-row descriptive partitions, not universal constants.

\paragraph{Boundary of interpretation.}
No translation-readout survival entry $S^{QT}$ is established by this half-life mediation run.  No ribosome occupancy, translation-efficiency matrix, folding state, physical admissibility assay, molecular function, phenotype, adaptation, or universal biological law follows from these rows.  The protein-abundance readout is steady-state and condition-dependent; the half-life and abundance datasets differ by organism, platform, and experimental setting.  A mechanism claim would require an additional contact that measures the relevant process directly, and a translation-mediated protein claim would require a same-row translation readout showing that $Q_i \to T_j$ survives before interpreting any $Q_i \to T_j \to P_k$ component.  The present result is therefore a cross-domain statistical mediation record: it says that the residual protein-abundance association is partly partitioned by an mRNA stability coordinate in the available finite panels, while leaving translation, folding, physical realization, function, mechanism, and universality unclaimed.
