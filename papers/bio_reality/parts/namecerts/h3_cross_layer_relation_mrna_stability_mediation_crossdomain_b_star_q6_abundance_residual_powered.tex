\paragraph{Finite row carrier.}
The record concerns a finite panel of coding sequences with organism labels, nine $B^*_{Q6}$ synonymous-residual coordinates, matched control columns, a protein-abundance residual readout, and an mRNA half-life or stability mediator.  The coordinate list is
$K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $Leu_{CUN\_vs\_UUR}$, $Leu_{UUA\_vs\_UUG}$, $Ser_{UCR\_vs\_AGY}$, $Ser_{UCA\_vs\_UCG}$, $Thr_{ACR\_vs\_ACY}$, and $f3_{stress}$.  The control block has 24 columns: intercept, $\log(\mathrm{cds\_len\_nt})$, the 20 amino-acid composition proportions $A,C,D,E,F,G,H,I,K,L,M,N,P,Q,R,S,T,V,W,Y$, GC3, and the $M$-density baseline defined as the fraction of sense codons lying in the union support of the nine projected $B^*_{Q6}$ vectors.  The empirical classification is a cross-layer relation, but only in the narrow statistical sense that the same finite rows carry sequence-derived coordinates, an RNA stability variable, and a protein abundance variable.

\paragraph{Abundance association before and after the mediator.}
Let $X$ denote the nine-dimensional $B^*_{Q6}$ residual-coordinate block, $M$ the residualized mRNA half-life or stability readout, and $P$ the residualized protein-abundance readout, all after the stated controls.  The computed mediation summary is the finite-row comparison
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{QP} &\quad \text{versus} \quad R^2_{QP\mid M},\\
\mathrm{fraction\ mediated} &= \frac{R^2_{QP}-R^2_{QP\mid M}}{R^2_{QP}}.
\end{aligned}
$$
For \emph{E. coli}, $R^2_{QP}=0.11632311854858134$ and $R^2_{QP\mid M}=0.0875945621412319$, giving mediation fraction $0.24697202727892137$.  The right-tail residual-mediator permutation value is $0.000999000999000999$, using the deterministic seed $\mathrm{sha256{:}b\_star\_q6\_mrna\_stability\_mediation\_crossdomain\_powered|ecoli|residual\_mediator\_permutation|perm\_index|index|n}$.  For human, $R^2_{QP}=0.04095215557813418$ and $R^2_{QP\mid M}=0.03517257842112907$, giving mediation fraction $0.1411299863319291$ with the same right-tail value $0.000999000999000999$ under the analogous human deterministic seed.  The numerical result is therefore directionally consistent across the two joined panels: conditioning on the RNA stability readout reduces, but does not erase, the $B^*_{Q6}$ block's protein-abundance association.

That row used the same nine coordinates and the same 24-column control design, with joined row counts $13140$ for zebrafish, $3415$ for \emph{E. coli}, $11554$ for human, and $5128$ for yeast; all four panels report $\operatorname{rank}(Z)=23$. This earlier row is cited here only as a control-boundary companion: it shows that transcript abundance can be placed in the control design without turning transcript abundance into translation survival or causal mechanism evidence.

\paragraph{Scientific readout.}
The admissible conclusion is narrow.  In the supplied finite panels, the joint nine-dimensional $B^*_{Q6}$ residual block has a protein-abundance residual association, and residualized RNA half-life or stability accounts for approximately $24.697202727892137\%$ of that association in \emph{E. coli} and $14.11299863319291\%$ in human under the recorded controls and row joins.  The permutation values mark the observed mediation fractions as large relative to the recorded residual-mediator permutation procedure.  The result is not a claim that a specific codon residual causes RNA decay, that RNA decay causes abundance changes, or that the same molecular pathway operates across domains.  It is a finite-row statistical decomposition of $Q \to P$ association after adding an RNA stability mediator.

\paragraph{Local no-promotion boundary.}
No translation-realization claim follows from this row, because the mediator is mRNA half-life or stability rather than a measured ribosome occupancy or translation-efficiency surface.  No folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, phenotype, mechanism, adaptation, causation, or universal biological law follows from the variance partition.  A protein-abundance contact is not a folded-structure assay, an RNA half-life contact is not an RBP-occupancy perturbation experiment, and a matched transcript-control column is not a ribosome-mediated mechanism.  Any stronger interpretation would require a separate layer-matched contact, such as measured translation rows for $S^{QT}$, perturbation and rescue rows for an RNA-decay mechanism, or structural, functional, localization, stability, phenotype, or physical-assay rows for the corresponding higher layer.
