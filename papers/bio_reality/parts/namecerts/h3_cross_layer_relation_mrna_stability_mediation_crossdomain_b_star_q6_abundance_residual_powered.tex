\subsection{B-star Q6 abundance residuals mediated by mRNA stability}
\label{sec:bior-q6-mrna-stability-mediation-crossdomain}

\paragraph{Finite row universe.}
This BioReality record treats $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ as a finite residual-coordinate panel over coding sequences, not as a direct biological mechanism.  The active coordinate block has nine entries,
$K\_AAA$, $Arg\_AGR$, $Ile\_AUA$, $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$, $Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG$, $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$, $Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG$, $Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY$, and $f3\_stress$.  Each coordinate is interpreted only after quotienting away the amino-acid sequence component and after residualizing against the control block
$$
\begin{aligned}
Z=\{&1,\log(\mathrm{CDS\ length}),\mathrm{AA}_{A},\ldots,\mathrm{AA}_{Y},\mathrm{GC3},\Delta_M\},
\end{aligned}
$$
where the twenty amino-acid composition coordinates are computed from real CDS codon counts and $\Delta_M$ is the M-density baseline on the union support of the nine projected $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ vectors.  The control matrix has $24$ columns, with reported residual control rank $23$ in both measured organisms.  The readout is not a translation-efficiency matrix.  It is a cross-domain abundance residual decomposition in which the direct $Q\to P$ association is compared before and after conditioning on an mRNA half-life or stability mediator $M$.

\paragraph{Joined measurement panels.}
Two organism panels are available for this finite-row claim.  In \emph{Escherichia coli} K-12 MG1655, the join uses the Esquerre GeneId b-number matched to the CDS locus tag.  The run reports $3739$ CDS records, $2947$ valid half-life records at growth rate $0.40\,\mathrm{h}^{-1}$, and $2720$ joined rows, with CDS join hit rate $0.9978649586335735$.  The E. coli join skipped $1019$ CDS records for absent half-life matches and no records for duplicate locus tag, invalid length, empty sense-codon counts, missing locus tag, non-object payload, or nonpositive abundance.  In human, the join uses Ensembl Gene Id without version against the CDS header gene field, with fallback through a unique gene symbol.  The human panel reports $19053$ CDS records, $19480$ valid consensus half-life records, and $16165$ joined rows, with $16150$ rows joined through Ensembl gene id and $15$ through symbol fallback.  The reported CDS join hit rate is $0.9779294769799313$, with $2876$ CDS records lacking a half-life match and $12$ duplicate-gene CDS records skipped.  These row counts define the finite support of the claim; no inference is made outside the two joined panels.

\paragraph{Rank and residual diagnostics.}
The E. coli residual problem has $2697$ residual degrees of freedom after $Z$, rank $9$ for $\widetilde X$, rank $1$ for $\widetilde M$, and rank $10$ for $[\widetilde X,\widetilde M]$.  Its protein-residual energy is $2551.728810042108$ and its mediator-residual energy is $215.43086017169205$.  The joint check reports $R^2_{QM}=0.02298556640056076$, $R^2_{M\mid X}=0.12750047680284674$, $X$-explained energy on $P$ equal to $296.8250528743585$, $M$-explained energy on $P$ equal to $398.65412500748283$, and incremental $M$ improvement given $X$ equal to $325.3466399519295$.  The direct $X$ improvement given $M$ remains $223.51756781880516$, and the coefficient check for $P$ on $M$ given $X$ is $-1.2432793356009886$.

For human, the residual problem has $16142$ residual degrees of freedom after $Z$, again with rank $9$ for $\widetilde X$, rank $1$ for $\widetilde M$, and rank $10$ for $[\widetilde X,\widetilde M]$.  Its protein-residual energy is $22233.302987773684$ and mediator-residual energy is $1422.0291144063049$.  The joint check reports $R^2_{QM}=0.010503670362051522$, $R^2_{M\mid X}=0.036877022762988716$, $X$-explained energy on $P$ equal to $910.5016829711035$, $M$-explained energy on $P$ equal to $948.3971104494655$, and incremental $M$ improvement given $X$ equal to $819.8980203765552$.  The direct $X$ improvement given $M$ is $782.0025928981933$, and the coefficient check for $P$ on $M$ given $X$ is $0.7633409850900272$.  The rank threshold for degeneracy is $0.999999$; the recorded ranks show that these two panels are not the three-row all-$1.0$ saturation case excluded by the claim.

\paragraph{Mediation readback.}
The finite statistic is the reduction in the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ abundance-residual association after conditioning on the residualized mRNA stability mediator.  In E. coli,
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{QP}&=0.11632311854858134,\\
R^2_{QP\mid M}&=0.0875945621412319,\\
\mathrm{mediation\ fraction}&=0.24697202727892137.
\end{aligned}
$$
In human,
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{QP}&=0.04095215557813418,\\
R^2_{QP\mid M}&=0.03517257842112907,\\
\mathrm{mediation\ fraction}&=0.1411299863319291.
\end{aligned}
$$
Both permutation audits use deterministic residual-mediator permutation seeds and report right-tail value $p=0.000999000999000999$.  Thus the finite-row conclusion is descriptive and ordered: the E. coli panel assigns about $24.7\%$ of the controlled $Q\to P$ association to the mRNA-stability-conditioned component, while the human panel assigns about $14.1\%$.  The E. coli signal is larger in fraction and in the joint mediator rank check; the human signal is weaker but remains visible on a much larger joined panel.

\paragraph{Relation to the mRNA abundance control run.}
The preceding abundance-control computation is used only to fix the local interpretation of transcript covariates.  In that run, E. coli has $3415$ all-readout joined rows and $d_{\mathrm{modeled}}=0.6628211177352172$, with only $0.0017706954554908886$ absorbed by residualized mRNA abundance.  Human has $11554$ all-readout joined rows and $d_{\mathrm{modeled}}=0.8566190269974241$, with $0.007858222847072218$ absorbed by residualized mRNA abundance.  These small transcript-abundance absorptions do not explain the present half-life mediation fractions.  They instead separate two covariates: abundance is a control-like transcript level, while half-life or stability is the mediator readout used in the present two-organism decomposition.

\paragraph{Local claim and limit.}
The claim supported here is that a finite, rank-powered $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual-coordinate block has a reproducible statistical association with protein-abundance residuals, and that conditioning on mRNA half-life or stability removes a nonzero fraction of that association in E. coli and human under the stated controls.  The claim is not causal.  The rows are observational, the abundance and half-life measurements come from organism-specific platforms and conditions, and the cross-domain comparison is not a phylogenetic analysis.  The mediator fraction is a variance partition, not a pathway proof.  No translation survival, ribosome mechanism, protein folding claim, physical admissibility claim, biological function claim, phenotype claim, or universal biological law follows from these numbers.  Any such stronger statement would require its own matched measurement layer and its own controlled statistic after the $Q\to T$ translation-readout boundary has been separately estimated.
