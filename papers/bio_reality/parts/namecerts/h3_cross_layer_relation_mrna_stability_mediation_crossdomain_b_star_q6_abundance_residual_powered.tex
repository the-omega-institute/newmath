\subsection{Rank-powered mRNA-stability mediation for $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual abundance}
\label{sec:bioreality-q6-mrna-stability-mediation-crossdomain}

\paragraph{Finite panel and coordinate scope.}
This record treats $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ as a finite residual coordinate family on coding sequences, not as an amino-acid sequence effect and not as a biological mechanism by itself. The analyzed coordinate block has nine residual directions, namely $K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $Leu_{CUN\,vs\,UUR}$, $Leu_{UUA\,vs\,UUG}$, $Ser_{UCR\,vs\,AGY}$, $Ser_{UCA\,vs\,UCG}$, $Thr_{ACR\,vs\,ACY}$, and $f_3$-stress. Every row is conditioned on the same control family: an intercept, $\log(\mathrm{CDS\ length})$, twenty amino-acid composition proportions computed from real CDS codon counts, GC3, and the $M$-density baseline defined as the fraction of sense codons on the union support of the nine projected coordinates. The resulting control matrix has $24$ columns, with residual control rank $23$ in both organisms used for the mRNA half-life mediation computation.

\paragraph{Observed finite joins.}
The cross-domain mediation computation is available for two organisms. In the $E.\ coli$ panel, the Esquerre half-life table at growth rate $0.40\,h^{-1}$ is joined to CDS rows by matching the Esquerre GeneId b-number to the CDS locus tag. The join contains $2720$ stable rows, with $3739$ CDS rows reported and $2947$ valid half-life records by gene id. The reported CDS join hit rate is $0.9978649586335735$, and the half-life payload hash is $87499a22f8699b15d2daf96f826e3345b1707a8360be122f1ec0114dc934469b$. In the human panel, the Agarwal consensus half-life table is joined by Ensembl gene id without version, with a unique gene-symbol fallback. The join contains $16165$ stable rows, including $16150$ Ensembl-gene-id matches and $15$ symbol-fallback matches. The source table has $54$ dataset columns, $19480$ valid consensus records by Ensembl gene id, and a reported CDS join hit rate of $0.9779294769799313$, with payload hash $ddf9441d1fe85d091d4b7f0a444b8dcf3658aa1d4158f66948bff00d5406de98$.

\paragraph{Rank diagnostics.}
Let $Z$ denote the control space, $X$ the residualized nine-coordinate $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ block, $M$ the residualized mRNA half-life or stability readout, and $P$ the residualized protein-abundance readout. The rank diagnostics are nondegenerate in both panels. For $E.\ coli$, the residual degrees of freedom after $Z$ are $2697$, $\operatorname{rank}(X)=9$, $\operatorname{rank}(M)=1$, $\operatorname{rank}(X,M)=10$, and $\operatorname{rank}_X(M)=9$. For human, the corresponding values are $16142$, $9$, $1$, $10$, and $9$. Thus the mediation readback is not an $n=3$ saturated fit and is not an all-ones rank artifact.

\paragraph{Energy decomposition.}

In $E.\ coli$, the protein residual energy is $2551.728810042108$ and the mRNA residual energy is $215.43086017169205$. The $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ block explains $296.8250528743585$ units of energy on $P$, while $M$ explains $398.65412500748283$ units on $P$. Conditional diagnostics give $M$ incremental improvement $325.3466399519295$ given $X$, $X$ direct improvement $223.51756781880516$ given $M$, $R^2_{M\mid X}=0.12750047680284674$, and the joint path-$a$ check $R^2_{QM}=0.02298556640056076$. The fitted conditional coefficient of $P$ on $M$ given $X$ is $-1.2432793356009886$.

For human, the protein residual energy is $22233.302987773684$ and the mRNA residual energy is $1422.0291144063049$. The $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ block explains $910.5016829711035$ units of energy on $P$, while $M$ explains $948.3971104494655$ units on $P$. \paragraph{Relation to the antecedent mRNA-control abundance run.}
The antecedent abundance-control run computed the modeled-tAI complement residual and then projected it onto residualized $\log_{10}$ mRNA. Its finite panels used the same nine coordinates and the same $Z$ controls. The available row counts were $13140$ for zebrafish, $3415$ for $E.\ coli$, $11554$ for human, and $5128$ for yeast, again with control rank $23$ and $24$ control columns. In that run, the modeled-complement values were $0.8424312412078521$ for zebrafish, $0.6628211177352172$ for $E.\ coli$, $0.8566190269974241$ for human, and $0.22386566043976477$ for yeast. The mRNA absorption fractions were small: $0.011973033879985792$, $0.0017706954554908886$, $0.007858222847072218$, and $0.0018976054721605224$ in the same order. The present half-life mediation record is therefore not a repetition of transcript-abundance absorption. It replaces transcript amount by an explicit stability or half-life mediator and asks how much of the residual protein-abundance association is partitioned through that mediator in matched finite joins.

\paragraph{Interpretation.}
The mathematical claim established by the finite rows is a rank-powered statistical decomposition. It says that, after amino-acid composition, length, GC3, and $M$-density controls, the nine-dimensional $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual block has a measurable association with protein-abundance residuals, and that conditioning on residualized mRNA half-life reduces that association by $24.697202727892137\%$ in $E.\ coli$ and $14.11299863319291\%$ in human. The computation is cross-domain because the same coordinate family is evaluated in a bacterial panel and a human panel, but it is not a phylogenetically controlled universality statement.

\paragraph{Local no-promotion boundary.}
No translation-realization claim follows from this section. The mediator is mRNA half-life or stability, not ribosome occupancy or translation efficiency, and the protein readout is abundance, not folding, structure, activity, localization, phenotype, or physical admissibility. The result also does not prove a causal pathway from synonymous residual geometry to RNA decay and then to protein abundance. It is observational, single-dataset per organism on the half-life side, and platform-conditioned by the joined abundance and stability sources. A stronger claim would require separate finite contacts for measured translation readout, perturbation, rescue, structure, function, or phenotype, together with the same rank and control discipline used here. Until those contacts exist, the admissible conclusion is only the reported variance partition on the two joined panels.
