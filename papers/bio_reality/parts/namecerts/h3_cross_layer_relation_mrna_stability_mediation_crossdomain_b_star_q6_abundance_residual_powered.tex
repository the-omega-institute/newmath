\paragraph{Finite panel and coordinate scope.}
This record concerns the cross-layer relation claim $q6.mrna\text{-}stability\text{-}mediation.cross\text{-}domain.abundance\text{-}residual.cross\text{-}layer$.  The finite carrier is a joined coding-sequence panel in which each row has codon-count-derived controls, one of nine $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinates, a protein-abundance residual readout, and an mRNA half-life or stability readout.  The coordinate block has size $9$ and consists of $K\_AAA$, $Arg\_AGR$, $Ile\_AUA$, $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$, $Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG$, $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$, $Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG$, $Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY$, and $f3\_stress$.  The shared control matrix uses an intercept, $\log(\mathrm{cds\_len\_nt})$, the $20$ amino-acid composition proportions $A,C,D,E,F,G,H,I,K,L,M,N,P,Q,R,S,T,V,W,Y$, $GC3$, and the $M$-density baseline on the union support of the nine projected $B^*_{Q6}$ vectors.  In both computed organisms the control-column count is $24$ and the control rank is $23$.

\paragraph{Joined-row evidence.}
The computation is finite and organism-specific, with two joined panels.  In the \emph{E. coli} panel, the half-life source is keyed by the Esquerre GeneId $b$-number against the CDS locus tag. The reported CDS join has $19053$ rows, the consensus stability table has $19480$ valid Ensembl rows, and the three-way joined analysis retains $16165$ rows, with a reported CDS join hit rate $0.9779294769799313$.  The human join sources split as $16150$ Ensembl-gene-id matches and $15$ gene-symbol fallback matches, with $0$ ambiguous symbol fallback keys.

\paragraph{Mediation decomposition.}
The measured object is a variance-partition statistic, not a causal pathway.  The implemented scalar compares the $B^*_{Q6}$ association with protein abundance before and after conditioning on the residualized mRNA stability readout:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{mediation\ fraction}
&=\frac{R^2_{QP}-R^2_{QP\mid M}}{R^2_{QP}}.
\end{aligned}
$$
For \emph{E. coli}, $R^2_{QP}=0.11632311854858134$, $R^2_{QP\mid M}=0.0875945621412319$, and the mediation fraction is $0.24697202727892137$, with right-tail permutation value $0.000999000999000999$.  The same diagnostic reports $R^2_{M\mid X}=0.12750047680284674$, $R^2_{QM}=0.02298556640056076$, $X$ explained energy on $P$ equal to $296.8250528743585$, and incremental $M$ improvement energy given $X$ equal to $325.3466399519295$.  For human, $R^2_{QP}=0.04095215557813418$, $R^2_{QP\mid M}=0.03517257842112907$, and the mediation fraction is $0.1411299863319291$, again with right-tail permutation value $0.000999000999000999$.  The human diagnostics give $R^2_{M\mid X}=0.036877022762988716$, $R^2_{QM}=0.010503670362051522$, $X$ explained energy on $P$ equal to $910.5016829711035$, and incremental $M$ improvement energy given $X$ equal to $819.8980203765552$.

\paragraph{Relation to the mRNA-abundance control run.}
The earlier mRNA-abundance control computation is useful as a local negative check.  It used the same nine-coordinate $B^*_{Q6}$ block and the same $24$-column control design, but projected the modeled-tAI complement protein-abundance residual onto residualized $\log_{10}$ mRNA abundance.  In that run the joined row counts were $3415$ for \emph{E. coli}, $11554$ for human, $13140$ for zebrafish, and $5128$ for yeast, all with control rank $23$.  The mRNA-absorption values were small: $0.0017706954554908886$ in \emph{E. coli}, $0.007858222847072218$ in human, $0.011973033879985792$ in zebrafish, and $0.0018976054721605224$ in yeast.  The stability-mediated panel is therefore not merely a restatement of transcript abundance; it uses a separate half-life or stability coordinate and obtains finite attenuation of the $Q$-to-protein residual association in the two organisms for which this joined stability panel was computed.

\paragraph{Readback verdict.}
The finite result supports a descriptive cross-layer statement: after amino-acid composition, length, $GC3$, and $M$-density controls, the nine-dimensional $B^*_{Q6}$ residual block has a protein-abundance association whose explained fraction is reduced when the matched mRNA half-life or stability readout is included.  The estimated reduction is about $24.6972\%$ in \emph{E. coli} and about $14.1130\%$ in human under the stated residual design.  This is a cross-domain statistical mediation surface with $2$ computed organisms and concrete joined row counts, not a universal biological rule.

\paragraph{Local non-promotion rule.}
No translation-readout survival $S^{QT}$ is established by this chapter.  The half-life or stability readout is not a ribosome-occupancy measurement, and the protein-abundance residual is not a folding, physical-admissibility, molecular-function, phenotype, or mechanism assay.  The finite numbers above also do not prove codon-to-mRNA-stability causation, because the panels are observational, the abundance and half-life measurements are condition- and platform-specific, and the cross-domain comparison is not a phylogenetically controlled design.  Any later claim about translation survival, folding, physical admissibility, function, mechanism, or universality must be supplied by an additional layer-matched contact and its own controlled statistic.
