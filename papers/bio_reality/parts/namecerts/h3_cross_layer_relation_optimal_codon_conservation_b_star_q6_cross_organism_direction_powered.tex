\subsection{Direction-filtered conservation of the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ non-translation residual}
\label{sec:biorality-q6-direction-filtered-conservation}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite object.}
This record concerns the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ modeled-translation-complement abundance residual.  For each organism and each available readout $H$, the finite ledger keeps the absorbed residual energy $A_H$, the residual coordinate support $a_{\perp}$ over the nine requested $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ coordinates, the readout-projected support $a_H$, the direction cosine $C_H$, the degree-of-freedom matched Bonferroni null, and the description-penalized score.  The score used in the ranking is
$$
\begin{aligned}
\operatorname{Score}(H) &= A_H C_H - \lambda\,DL(H),\\
\lambda &= 0.01,\\
DL(H) &= \log_2(1+n_{\mathrm{columns}}).
\end{aligned}
$$
Single-column readouts therefore pay $DL=1$, while the twelve-column localization panel pays $DL=\log_2(13)=3.700439718141092$.  The coordinate list is fixed at nine entries: $K\_{AAA}$, $Arg\_{AGR}$, $Ile\_{AUA}$, $Leu\_{CUN\,vs\,UUR}$, $Leu\_{UUA\,vs\,UUG}$, $Ser\_{UCR\,vs\,AGY}$, $Ser\_{UCA\,vs\,UCG}$, $Thr\_{ACR\,vs\,ACY}$, and $f3\_{stress}$.

\paragraph{Conservation readout.}
The cross-organism optimal-codon conservation run completed with status passed.  It requested $16$ organisms and computed $15$ of them under a minimum of $500$ proteins per organism.  The synonymous design matrix had $41$ columns, the validation used $5$ folds, and the deterministic seed was recorded as \texttt{sha256:b\_star\_q6\_optimal\_codon\_conservation\_powered:deterministic}.  The mean pairwise cosine among the organism-level optimal-codon direction summaries was $0.2629282074265475$, and the run classified the resulting geometry as clustered rather than universal.  Thus the finite outcome is not a shared conserved direction of large magnitude.  It is a weak clustering signal in a finite panel, compatible with organism-specific codon and residual structure.

\paragraph{Boundary set comparison.}
The fixed Window6 boundary set $R$ gives a separate sign check on the same biological neighborhood.  Across all $15$ computed organisms, the $R$ set was abundance-avoided in all $15$ directional non-tie cases and preferred in $0$ cases.  The one-sided avoided sign-test probability was $3.0517578125\times 10^{-5}$ and the two-sided probability was $6.103515625\times 10^{-5}$.  The all-computed avoided list was Escherichia coli K-12 MG1655, Mycobacterium smegmatis str. MC2 155, Saccharomyces cerevisiae, Homo sapiens, Danio rerio, Gallus gallus, Pseudomonas aeruginosa PAO1, Bacillus subtilis subsp. subtilis str. 168, Mus musculus, Caenorhabditis elegans, Drosophila melanogaster, Halobacterium salinarum, Sulfolobus solfataricus, Dictyostelium discoideum, and Rattus norvegicus.  Among individually significant organisms, $6$ of $6$ were avoided: Homo sapiens, Danio rerio, Gallus gallus, Pseudomonas aeruginosa PAO1, Mus musculus, and Rattus norvegicus.  This supports a finite abundance-avoidance signature for the fixed $R$ set, but it does not convert the optimal-codon clustering result into a mechanism.

\paragraph{Null gates and missingness.}
The finite table separates missing rows from negative rows.  Bacillus subtilis, Caenorhabditis elegans, Drosophila melanogaster, Mus musculus, Mycobacterium smegmatis, Rattus norvegicus, and Sulfolobus solfataricus had empty $H$ rankings in this packet rather than zero-valued evidence.  Bonferroni status is also distinct from rank and score.  Danio rerio exceeded the Bonferroni threshold for measured TE, measured tRNA tAI, mRNA, and PTM density, but not for localization, complex membership, domain count, or transmembrane count.  Homo sapiens exceeded for complex membership, localization, measured TE, mRNA, PTM density, and transmembrane count, but not for measured tRNA tAI, turnover, or domain count.  Saccharomyces cerevisiae exceeded for localization and mRNA, but not for turnover, measured TE, measured tRNA tAI, complex membership, PTM density, domain count, or transmembrane count.  Escherichia coli did not exceed for either mRNA or measured TE.  The ranked readback is therefore mixed and organism-specific: some entries exceed a finite null in some organisms, yet the penalized direction-aware score does not produce a shared high-confidence absorber.

\paragraph{Cross-organism verdict.}
The conservation experiment reports a clustered mean pairwise cosine of $0.2629282074265475$ across $15$ computed organisms, not a universal conserved absorber.  The strongest raw direction-aware products in the finite H table stay small: Danio measured TE reaches $0.011566051846174291$, Danio mRNA reaches $0.01073458857797759$, human complex membership reaches $0.006566030115340095$, human mRNA reaches $0.006224777217900546$, yeast mRNA reaches $0.0011462622274400686$, and E. coli mRNA reaches $0.0015297976209788734$.  The best readout changes by organism, high absorbed energy can point in the wrong or weak direction, and the description penalty eliminates most apparent gains.  The minimal-compilation check agrees: the median compressibility across the computed organisms was only $0.026$, with residual fractions $0.9756319042010854$ for Danio rerio, $0.9942823785118229$ for Escherichia coli, $0.9736442326306781$ for Homo sapiens, and $0.8676586345294415$ for Saccharomyces cerevisiae.  This chapter therefore records an organism-specific, direction-filtered residual geometry with a finite avoided-boundary signature and weak clustering of optimal-codon directions, but no shared universal $H$.

\paragraph{Local scope of the claim.}
The quantities $A_H$, $C_H$, $A_HC_H$, rank, Bonferroni status, and $\operatorname{Score}$ are statistical summaries of finite joined rows.  They do not establish translation realization beyond the measured translation contacts, do not identify folding or physical admissibility, do not prove protein function or phenotype, and do not describe a molecular mechanism.  A localization row is a compartmental annotation readout, not a trafficking mechanism.  A proteomics abundance row is an abundance measurement, not a protein-state, structure, or functional assay.  The Window6 $R$ sign result, the optimal-codon clustering result, and the H-ranking table are therefore admissible here only as bounded descriptive readbacks.  Any stronger biological statement would require a separate matched contact and a separate statistic designed for that stronger layer.
