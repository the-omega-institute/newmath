\subsection{Direction-aware conservation of the optimal-codon residual}
\label{sec:bioq6-optimal-codon-conservation-direction}

\paragraph{Finite carrier.}
The finite record for this packet is a per-organism ledger over the nine $B^*_{Q6}$ coordinates
$$
\begin{aligned}
&K\_AAA,\ Arg\_AGR,\ Ile\_AUA,\ Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR,\ Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG,\\
&Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY,\ Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG,\ Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY,\ f3\_stress .
\end{aligned}
$$
For each organism with enough matched coding sequences, the experiment builds a synonymous design with $41$ columns, uses $5$ folds, and requires at least $500$ proteins per organism. The powered run requested $16$ organisms and produced finite rows for $15$ of them. Its cross-organism summary is a mean pairwise cosine
$$
\begin{aligned}
\bar c &= 0.2629282074265475,
\end{aligned}
$$
with the classification `clustered'. This is a weak positive conservation readback in the coordinate direction space, not a claim that a single codon mechanism, pathway, or biological program is shared by all organisms.

\paragraph{What is being compared.}
The object compared across organisms is not raw codon usage alone. It is the direction of the $B^*_{Q6}$ optimal-codon signal after the per-organism finite construction has reduced the row to a direction in the $41$-column synonymous design and then summarized the resulting direction by pairwise cosine. The value $0.2629282074265475$ therefore says that the computed organisms are not directionally independent in this finite coordinate representation, but it also falls far below a near-identity conservation value. The finite label `clustered' should be read as partial direction grouping across the computed organism panel.

\paragraph{Relation to the residual support ledger.}
The direction result sits beside, and does not replace, the non-translation abundance residual ledger. In that ledger the modeled-translation-complement residual $P_{Q\perp T\mathrm{mod}}$ was available for $12$ organisms in the H-ranking run, with per-organism control rank $23$ and $24$ control columns. The complement fractions were highly organism-specific: for example $d=0.21173219572706714$ in \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}, $d=0.6629960038578534$ in \emph{Escherichia coli} K-12 MG1655, $d=0.874628721049266$ in \emph{Danio rerio}, $d=0.9208949355679455$ in \emph{Homo sapiens}, $d=0.9400341806302775$ in \emph{Gallus gallus}, and $d=0.988614641876908$ in \emph{Sulfolobus solfataricus}. These values show that the optimal-codon direction clustering is not the same as a shared downstream explanation of the abundance residual. It is a conserved-shape observation in one finite direction space while the residual energy and its readout absorption remain organism-dependent.

\paragraph{Direction-aware H ranking.}
The associated finite H-ranking table used
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{Score}(H) &= A_H C_H - \lambda\,DL(H),\\
\lambda &= 0.01,\\
DL(H) &= \log_2(1+n_{\mathrm{columns}}).
\end{aligned}
$$
Raw alignment and penalized score separate sharply. In \emph{Danio rerio}, measured TE had $A_H=0.017594290015070787$, $C_H=0.6573753096184687$, and $A_HC_H=0.011566051846174291$, giving the only clearly positive reported penalized score, $0.001566051846174291$. The same organism had mRNA at $A_HC_H=0.01073458857797759$ and Score $0.000734588577977589$. In \emph{Homo sapiens}, complex membership ranked first by the one-column penalized rule, with $A_H=0.007527199987780841$, $C_H=0.8723071163246565$, $A_HC_H=0.006566030115340095$, and Score $-0.0034339698846599055$; mRNA followed with $A_HC_H=0.006224777217900546$ and Score $-0.0037752227820994546$. The human PTM-density readout had larger absorbed energy, $A_H=0.014884825920744861$, but weaker directional agreement, $C_H=0.3604440741749213$, so its raw aligned value was only $0.0053651472982577525$. This is the practical role of $C_H$: it prevents a high-energy readout from being promoted when its coordinate support is poorly aligned.

\paragraph{Absence of a universal absorber.}
The best finite absorber is not shared. The ranking chose measured TE for \emph{Danio rerio}, complex membership for \emph{Homo sapiens} and \emph{Gallus gallus}, mRNA for \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}, and no positive best absorber for the computed \emph{Escherichia coli} row. In \emph{E. coli}, mRNA had $A_H=0.0017706954554909877$, $C_H=0.8639529831258776$, $A_HC_H=0.0015297976209788734$, and Score $-0.008470202379021127$; measured TE had Score $-0.009446333814243976$. Thus the conservation call for the optimal-codon direction should not be read as a shared H channel. It records a clustered cross-organism direction over the optimal-codon coordinate test, while the finite residual absorber is organism-specific or absent.

\paragraph{Boundary R diagnostic.}
The Window6 boundary set supplies an independent directional check on a fixed codon set rather than on the H-ranking table. Across the $15$ computed organisms, the $R$ sense-codon set had $10$ codons out of $61$ sense codons and showed an avoided abundance-preference signature in all $15$ non-tie organisms. The one-sided avoided sign-test value was $3.0517578125\times 10^{-5}$ and the two-sided value was $6.103515625\times 10^{-5}$. Among individually significant organisms, $6$ of $6$ were also avoided. This strengthens the statement that the $B^*_{Q6}$ coordinate construction has reproducible finite direction readbacks, but it still does not identify a translation mechanism, folding constraint, functional program, phenotype, or universal law.

\paragraph{Mediation and specificity checks.}
Measured translation-efficiency mediation remains heterogeneous. In the measured-TE decomposition, the measured mediated fraction was $0.4793069656345167$ in yeast, $0.04091158340950673$ in \emph{E. coli}, $0.0027966485036786744$ in human, and $0.0$ in danio after truncating a negative raw fraction of $-0.04801486048721136$. The organism-specificity meta table also did not resolve a simple covariate law: the test of $M^{QTP}$ fraction versus GC3 used $n=12$ organisms and gave $r=-0.3062616500469447$ with two-sided permutation $p=0.35264735264735264$; versus log proteome size it gave $r=-0.033628115789504955$ with $p=0.913086913086913$; versus log tRNA gene count it gave $r=0.06029405206037823$ with $p=0.8621378621378621$. The domain test had variance ratio $0.8889980452277374$ and right-tail permutation $p=0.4155844155844156$. The dominant-coordinate domain contingency had Cramer's $V=0.6258327785172862$, but its permutation value was $p=0.5814185814185814$. These diagnostics keep the direction-conservation result in a finite descriptive register.

\paragraph{Compression check.}
The measured low-dimensional readout dictionaries do not compress the abundance residual into a small common explanation. The minimal compilation test reported compressibility $0.024368095798914635$ in danio, $0.005717621488177116$ in \emph{E. coli}, $0.02635576736932188$ in human, and $0.13234136547055853$ in yeast, with cross-organism median about $0.026$. In the same rows the residual fractions were $0.9756319042010854$, $0.9942823785118229$, $0.9736442326306781$, and $0.8676586345294415$. Thus the computed readouts leave most of $P_Q$ unexplained. The optimal-codon direction is clustered, but the residual remains largely outside the measured translation, mRNA, turnover, localization, and feature panel.

\paragraph{Local interpretation boundary.}
The chapter-level conclusion is therefore deliberately narrow. The finite evidence supports a clustered cross-organism direction in the optimal-codon coordinate test over $15$ computed organisms, with mean pairwise cosine $0.2629282074265475$, and it is consistent with other finite $B^*_{Q6}$ direction readbacks such as the avoided $R$-set sign test. It does not show that codon usage realizes translation dynamics, does not establish folding or physical admissibility, does not infer function or phenotype, and does not turn $A_H$, $C_H$, Score, rank, $H_o^*$, or the clustered label into a mechanism. Any stronger biological statement would require a separate matched contact with its own statistic, on the same organism and row system, and would have to be reported independently of this direction-conservation ledger.
