\subsection{Direction-aware conservation of the optimal-codon residual panel}
\label{sec:bioreality-q6-direction-aware-optimal-codon-conservation}

\paragraph{Finite row.}
The record tests whether the $B^*_{Q6}$ modeled-translation-complement residual has a conserved cross-organism direction when it is read through a finite panel of organism-matched codon and readout tables. The conservation experiment was run as \texttt{b\_star\_q6\_optimal\_codon\_conservation\_powered} with deterministic seed \texttt{sha256:b\_star\_q6\_optimal\_codon\_conservation\_powered:deterministic}. It requested 16 organisms, computed 15, used 41 synonymous-design columns, required at least 500 proteins per organism, and used 5 folds. The resulting mean pairwise cosine was $0.2629282074265475$, and the finite classification was \texttt{clustered}, not universal. Thus the admissible reading is weak directional clustering of codon-conservation coordinates across the computed organisms, not a shared biological channel.

\paragraph{Coordinate ledger.}
The residual panel is the nine-coordinate $B^*_{Q6}$ ledger with coordinates \texttt{K\_AAA}, \texttt{Arg\_AGR}, \texttt{Ile\_AUA}, \texttt{Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR}, \texttt{Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG}, \texttt{Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY}, \texttt{Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG}, \texttt{Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY}, and \texttt{f3\_stress}. For each organism and candidate readout $H$, the finite row stores absorbed energy $A_H$, direction cosine $C_H$, the raw directional product $A_H C_H$, and a description-length penalized score
$$
\begin{aligned}
\operatorname{Score}(H) &= A_H C_H - 0.01\,\log_2(1+n_{\mathrm{columns}}(H)).
\end{aligned}
$$
The penalty is a declared ranking convention only. It does not estimate a mechanism, and it does not rewrite the Bonferroni null status. Single-column candidates have $DL_H=1$, while the 12-column localization readout has $DL_H=3.700439718141092$.

\paragraph{Cross-organism boundary signal.}
A separate fixed boundary-set diagnostic gives the strongest finite sign result in this packet. The Window6 $R$ set contained 10 sense codons, and all 15 computed organisms fell in the avoided direction: \texttt{escherichia\_coli\_k12\_mg1655}, \texttt{mycobacterium\_smegmatis\_str\_mc2\_155}, \texttt{saccharomyces\_cerevisiae}, \texttt{homo\_sapiens}, \texttt{danio\_rerio}, \texttt{gallus\_gallus}, \texttt{pseudomonas\_aeruginosa\_pao1}, \texttt{bacillus\_subtilis\_subsp\_subtilis\_str\_168}, \texttt{mus\_musculus}, \texttt{caenorhabditis\_elegans}, \texttt{drosophila\_melanogaster}, \texttt{halobacterium\_salinarum}, \texttt{sulfolobus\_solfataricus}, \texttt{dictyostelium\_discoideum}, and \texttt{rattus\_norvegicus}. The one-sided avoided-sign binomial value was $3.0517578125\times 10^{-5}$, the two-sided value was $6.103515625\times 10^{-5}$, and the majority fraction was $1.0$ over 15 non-tie organisms. Among individually significant organisms, 6 of 6 were avoided: human, danio, gallus, \textit{P. aeruginosa}, mouse, and rat. This is a finite abundance-preference signature for the fixed $R$ boundary, not a claim that the $R$ codons realize translation, structure, function, phenotype, or a universal law.

\paragraph{Measured translation comparison.}
The measured-TE mediation row shows why the conservation call must remain descriptive. For the measured-protein-abundance decomposition, the measured mediated fractions were $0.4793069656345167$ in yeast, $0.04091158340950673$ in \textit{E. coli}, $0.0027966485036786744$ in human, and $0.0$ in danio after the negative raw value was clipped to the unit interval. The matched row counts were 5128 for yeast, 3415 for \textit{E. coli}, 11554 for human, and 13140 for danio. The same table reports $S_{QP}=0.3146818503437042$ for yeast, $0.10941535951828074$ for \textit{E. coli}, $0.04288464996165745$ for human, and $0.04926578454614652$ for danio. These values do not align to one conserved translation-mediated direction; they show organism-specific measured-readout behavior.

The finite $H$ table reinforces the negative cross-organism conclusion. In danio, the top raw directional entries were measured TE with $A_H=0.017594290015070787$, $C_H=0.6573753096184687$, $A_HC_H=0.011566051846174291$, and $\operatorname{Score}=0.001566051846174291$, followed by mRNA with $A_H=0.011973033879985887$, $C_H=0.896563785384548$, $A_HC_H=0.01073458857797759$, and $\operatorname{Score}=0.000734588577977589$. In human, complex membership led the single-column candidates with $A_H=0.007527199987780841$, $C_H=0.8723071163246565$, and $A_HC_H=0.006566030115340095$, while mRNA followed with $A_HC_H=0.006224777217900546$. Human PTM density had higher absorbed energy, $A_H=0.014884825920744861$, but its direction cosine was only $0.3604440741749213$, giving $A_HC_H=0.0053651472982577525$; the direction filter therefore demotes a high-energy but weakly aligned readout. Human measured TE is still more sharply rejected directionally: $A_H=0.002894714925796667$ with $C_H=-0.10813537216189718$, so $A_HC_H=-0.00031302107580362116$.

\paragraph{Organism-specific winners.}
The per-organism best absorber is not shared. Danio selects \texttt{measured\_te}; human selects \texttt{complex\_member}; yeast selects \texttt{mrna}; gallus selects \texttt{complex\_member}; \textit{E. coli} has no positive score after the penalty despite mRNA having $C_H=0.8639529831258776$ and $A_HC_H=0.0015297976209788734$. Several organisms in the larger panel have no ranked $H$ entries in this finite table. The highest raw product listed in the ranking table is danio measured TE at about $0.011566$, below $0.012$. Under the penalized score, only the two danio single-column readouts have positive scores in the displayed ranking; the other organism-level leaders remain negative after the $0.01\,DL_H$ subtraction. This is the concrete reason the packet reports no shared $H^*_{\mathrm{shared}}$.

\paragraph{Meta-structure and compression.}
The organism-specific meta row computed 12 complete-feature organisms and 16 total rows, with missing $M^{QTP}$ inputs for \texttt{pseudomonas\_aeruginosa\_pao1}, \texttt{halobacterium\_salinarum}, \texttt{dictyostelium\_discoideum}, and \texttt{arabidopsis\_thaliana}. The $M^{QTP}$ fraction versus GC3 test had Pearson $r=-0.3062616500469447$ with two-sided permutation value $0.35264735264735264$ over 1000 trials; versus log proteome size it had $r=-0.033628115789504955$ with value $0.913086913086913$; versus log tRNA gene count it had $r=0.06029405206037823$ with value $0.8621378621378621$. Domain comparison had variance ratio $0.8889980452277374$ and right-tail permutation value $0.4155844155844156$. The minimal-compilation row also fails to compress the residual strongly: compressibility was $0.024368095798914635$ in danio, $0.005717621488177116$ in \textit{E. coli}, $0.02635576736932188$ in human, and $0.13234136547055853$ in yeast, with median reported as $0.026$. Thus the finite panel is not just non-universal; it is also poorly compressed by the available measured low-dimensional readouts.

\paragraph{Reach and coverage diagnostics.}
The $f3$ stress readout reach calculation in yeast considered all 512 subsets of the nine requested independent readouts. The base coverage was $0.1897720314893696$, the best coverage was $0.22618445168998694$, and the increment over base was $0.03641242020061733$. The selection-aware null had mean $0.018736269159555625$, 95th percentile $0.02697141300639046$, and maximum $0.03878006220013416$ over 120 permutations. This confirms that a finite subset can exceed a selection-aware null for one coordinate component, while $0.7738155483100131$ of that component remains unexplained after the full readout set. Turnover rows give the same caution. Yeast turnover joined 3640 proteins, with reported CDS join hit rate $0.9156038419146592$ and turnover join hit rate $0.8818572656921754$, but absorbed only $0.0009086031889966327$. Human Mathieson turnover joined 8057 proteins, loaded 8164 valid turnover rows, and had absorbed energy $3.5740764900116538\times 10^{-6}$, below its dof-matched null 95th percentile $0.0004342285066808464$.

\paragraph{Local exclusion rule.}
The chapter therefore supports a narrow finite statement: the optimal-codon conservation panel is clustered across 15 computed organisms, the fixed Window6 $R$ boundary has a strong avoided abundance-preference sign result, and the direction-aware $H$ ranking shows weak, organism-specific absorbers rather than one conserved channel. Nothing in this row establishes translation realization beyond the measured readouts, protein folding, physical admissibility, biological function, a causal pathway, a localization mechanism, phenotype, or universality. Those stronger statements would require separate layer-matched contacts and statistics. Here $A_H$, $C_H$, rank, score, missingness, and $H^*_o$ are finite descriptive entries only.
