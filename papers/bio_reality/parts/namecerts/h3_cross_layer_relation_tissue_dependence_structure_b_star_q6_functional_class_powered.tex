\subsection{B-star Q6 Tissue Dependence Under a Functional-Class Prior}
\label{sec:h3-cross-layer-relation-tissue-dependence-structure-b-star-q6-functional-class-powered}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite panel.}
This record evaluates whether the synonymous residual coordinates denoted $B^*_{Q6}$ leave a repeatable tissue-dependent trace in a protein-readout panel before any claim about translation mechanism or protein function is made.  The empirical rows are human coding sequences joined to the Kim et al. 2014 SEQUEST adult-tissue PAXdb files, with the response variable $\log_{10}(\mathrm{abundance\_ppm})$.  Thirteen adult tissues were loaded: Adult\_Bcells, Adult\_CD4Tcells, Adult\_CD8Tcells, Adult\_NKcells, Adult\_Monocytes, Adult\_Liver, Adult\_Kidney, Adult\_Pancreas, Adult\_Frontalcortex, Adult\_Retina, Adult\_Colon, Adult\_Lung, and Adult\_Heart.  The usable row counts are not small: the joined panels range from $5179$ rows for Adult\_Monocytes to $10914$ rows for Adult\_CD8Tcells, with Adult\_Bcells at $9244$, Adult\_CD4Tcells at $7604$, Adult\_Colon at $7948$, Adult\_Frontalcortex at $9301$, Adult\_Heart at $6468$, Adult\_Kidney at $6726$, Adult\_Liver at $8664$, Adult\_Lung at $6577$, Adult\_NKcells at $8787$, Adult\_Pancreas at $9695$, and Adult\_Retina at $10373$.

\paragraph{Residual coordinates and controls.}
The tested coordinate family has nine columns: K\_AAA, Arg\_AGR, Ile\_AUA, Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR, Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG, Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY, Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG, Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY, and f3\_stress.  Each tissue regression uses the same biological control design: an intercept, $\log(\mathrm{cds\_len\_nt})$, twenty amino-acid composition proportions computed from real CDS codon counts, GC3 from real CDS codon counts, and the M-density baseline, defined as the fraction of sense codons lying on the union support of the nine projected $B^*_{Q6}$ vectors.  The recorded control-column count is $24$ for every tissue.  After residualization, the fitted coordinate rank is $9$ for every tissue, while the control rank is $23$ for every tissue.  Thus the finite rows do not collapse the nine-coordinate residual family into a lower-rank panel.

The panel therefore supports only a modest descriptive protein-abundance association, not a strong tissue law.  The dominant residual coordinate also varies by tissue: Arg\_AGR dominates Adult\_Bcells, Adult\_CD8Tcells, Adult\_Colon, Adult\_Kidney, Adult\_NKcells, Adult\_Pancreas, and Adult\_Retina; Ile\_AUA dominates Adult\_CD4Tcells, Adult\_Frontalcortex, Adult\_Liver, Adult\_Lung, and Adult\_Monocytes; Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY dominates Adult\_Heart.  No single coordinate is therefore promoted to a universal driver.

\paragraph{Rank diagnostics.}
The row-level degrees of freedom confirm that the readout is estimated in powered panels rather than in a saturated toy table.  The residual degrees of freedom after controls are $9221$ for Adult\_Bcells, $7581$ for Adult\_CD4Tcells, $10891$ for Adult\_CD8Tcells, $7925$ for Adult\_Colon, $9278$ for Adult\_Frontalcortex, $6445$ for Adult\_Heart, $6703$ for Adult\_Kidney, $8641$ for Adult\_Liver, $6554$ for Adult\_Lung, $5156$ for Adult\_Monocytes, $8764$ for Adult\_NKcells, $9672$ for Adult\_Pancreas, and $10350$ for Adult\_Retina.  All of these sit far below the degenerate cutoff $R^2=0.999999$ and use $9$ residual-coordinate columns after a $23$-rank control residualization.  The fitted readout is therefore a controlled finite-panel statistic, not an all-one rank artifact.

\paragraph{Functional-class prior test.}
The tissue labels were grouped by a manually specified functional-class prior: immune, metabolic, neural, and epithelial\_or\_other.  This prior is not learned from the abundance matrix.  The cosine comparison used all $78$ tissue pairs, with $17$ within-class pairs and $61$ between-class pairs.  The observed within-class mean cosine is $0.9015393659102923$, the observed between-class mean cosine is $0.8936124027169124$, and the observed within-minus-between contrast is
$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta_{\mathrm{within-between}}
  &= 0.9015393659102923 - 0.8936124027169124\\
  &= 0.007926963193379821.
\end{aligned}
$$
A class-size-preserving permutation test with $1000$ permutations gives one-sided $p=0.3006993006993007$ for the hypothesis that within-class similarity is larger than label-shuffle expectation.  The null deltas ranged from $-0.043787962870935226$ to $0.08858262592759103$, with null mean $0.0007739569961830541$.  This finite row therefore does not support a functional-class-structured tissue law.  The appropriate readback is idiosyncratic or weakly descriptive tissue variation in $S^{QP}$, not class-level organization.

\paragraph{Relation to modeled translation readouts.}
A separate rank-powered modeled-translation sweep over $41$ organisms computed modeled tAI-based $S^{QT}$ summaries for the same residual coordinate family with $24$ controls and reached the target of at least $40$ organisms.  In that organism sweep, the $R^2_{QT}$ distribution had minimum $0.1579271837024072$, first quartile $0.26353954700074694$, median $0.33934845811598124$, mean $0.38466555440501393$, third quartile $0.5143951545826744$, and maximum $0.8213457220900503$, using $1000$ permutations.  Those values show that the residual coordinates can align with a modeled codon-adaptation readout in a broad organism panel.  They do not by themselves establish ribosome occupancy, translation efficiency, or protein output, because modeled tAI or stAI weights are annotations rather than measured ribosome-profiling rows.

\paragraph{Local verdict.}
The finite-row result for this chapter is ready as a bounded descriptive claim.  In human adult PAXdb tissue rows, the $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinates retain small controlled associations with $\log_{10}(\mathrm{abundance\_ppm})$ after length, amino-acid composition, GC3, and M-density controls.  The tissue-level coordinate signatures are heterogeneous: Arg\_AGR is most often dominant, Ile\_AUA is dominant in several tissues, and Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY is dominant in Adult\_Heart.  The functional-class prior does not pass the recorded permutation test, since $p=0.3006993006993007$ and the observed contrast $0.007926963193379821$ lies well within the shuffled range.  The chapter therefore records a powered protein-readout association surface and a negative class-structure result.

\paragraph{Boundary of interpretation.}
Nothing in this panel licenses a claim about measured translation efficiency, ribosome occupancy, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, mechanism, phenotype, or universality.  The PAXdb readout is a draft tissue proteome abundance readout from one study, not ribo-seq and not mRNA-controlled TE.  A translation claim would require same-organism and same-CDS codon-count rows joined to ribosome-profiling RPF rows and matched mRNA rows, followed by an explicit controlled TE statistic such as $\mathrm{TE}=\mathrm{RPF}/\mathrm{mRNA}$.  A structure, localization, stability, interaction, function, perturbation, physical-assay, or phenotype claim would require its own layer-matched measurement.  The present chapter stops at controlled residual association with tissue protein abundance and at the observed failure of the functional-class prior to separate the tissue signatures.
