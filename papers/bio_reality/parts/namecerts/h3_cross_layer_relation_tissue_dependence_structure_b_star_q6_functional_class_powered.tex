\subsection{Rank-Powered $B^{*}_{Q6}$ Translation-Readout Calibration Boundary}\label{sec:biorerality-q6-modeled-observed-translation-calibration}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite carrier.}
The finite object in this chapter is a panel of coding sequences carrying nine synonymous residual coordinates after amino-acid quotienting, paired with modeled codon-adaptation readouts where available and with downstream observed readout tables when those tables have been joined to the same coding-sequence identifiers. The coordinate list is
$K\_AAA$, $Arg\_AGR$, $Ile\_AUA$, $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$, $Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG$, $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$, $Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG$, $Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY$, and $f3\_stress$.
The control design used in the tissue computation has $24$ columns per tissue: an intercept, $\log(cds\_len\_nt)$, the $20$ amino-acid composition proportions computed from real CDS codon counts, GC3, and an $M$-density baseline defined as the fraction of sense codons lying on the union support of the nine projected $B^{*}_{Q6}$ vectors. Thus the chapter is about a finite residual-and-control matrix, not about a completed biological mechanism.

\paragraph{Modeled translation surface.}
A rank-powered organism sweep supplies the modeled codon-adaptation side of the record. It covers $41$ organisms and reaches the target of $40$ organisms. For the modeled $S^{QT}$ computation, the distribution of $R^{2}_{QT}$ over the organism panel has minimum $0.1579271837024072$, first quartile $0.26353954700074694$, median $0.33934845811598124$, mean $0.38466555440501393$, third quartile $0.5143951545826744$, and maximum $0.8213457220900503$, with $1000$ permutations. This is a modeled tAI or stAI readout surface, derived from codon adaptation weights and codon-count joins. It is not a ribosome-profiling survival surface and it is not a measured translation-efficiency matrix.

\paragraph{Observed protein-readout surface.}
The finite observed table currently attached to the tissue-dependence computation is not ribosome profiling. It is a human tissue-specific draft proteome readout from Kim et al. 2014 SEQUEST PAXdb files, using $\log_{10}(abundance\_ppm)$ as the response. Thirteen adult tissues are loaded: Adult\_Bcells, Adult\_CD4Tcells, Adult\_CD8Tcells, Adult\_NKcells, Adult\_Monocytes, Adult\_Liver, Adult\_Kidney, Adult\_Pancreas, Adult\_Frontalcortex, Adult\_Retina, Adult\_Colon, Adult\_Lung, and Adult\_Heart. The usable joined row counts range from $5179$ in Adult\_Monocytes to $10914$ in Adult\_CD8Tcells. The full row-count vector is
$$\begin{aligned}
&n_{Bcells}=9244,\quad n_{CD4T}=7604,\quad n_{CD8T}=10914,\quad n_{NK}=8787,\quad n_{Monocytes}=5179,\\
&n_{Liver}=8664,\quad n_{Kidney}=6726,\quad n_{Pancreas}=9695,\quad n_{Frontalcortex}=9301,\quad n_{Retina}=10373,\\
&n_{Colon}=7948,\quad n_{Lung}=6577,\quad n_{Heart}=6468.
\end{aligned}$$
In every tissue the residualized coordinate matrix has rank $9$, and the control matrix has rank $23$ after the listed control columns are assembled. The finite computation therefore avoids the three-row saturated case: its tissue rows are numerous and its $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual block remains full rank after control residualization.

This coordinate pattern is therefore heterogeneous across tissues rather than a single functional-class law.

\paragraph{Functional-class test.}
The tissue cosine matrix contains $13$ tissues and $78$ tissue pairs. A class-size-preserving label-shuffle test compares a functional-class prior with the observed cosine structure. There are $17$ within-class pairs and $61$ between-class pairs. The observed within-class mean cosine is $0.9015393659102923$, the observed between-class mean cosine is $0.8936124027169124$, and the observed within-minus-between difference is $0.007926963193379821$. Under $1000$ deterministic permutations, the one-sided within-greater $p$-value is $0.3006993006993007$, with null mean difference $0.0007739569961830541$, null minimum $-0.043787962870935226$, and null maximum $0.08858262592759103$. Hence the finite table does not support a functional-class-structured conclusion at this resolution. The appropriate reading is idiosyncratic or at most weakly descriptive tissue structure, not a data-driven immune, metabolic, neural, or epithelial module assignment.

\paragraph{Modeled-to-observed calibration status.}
The modeled side and the observed side do not yet form a same-row modeled-to-ribosome calibration matrix. The modeled organism sweep gives a rank-powered $S^{QT}$ surface against tAI or stAI weights. The tissue computation gives a human $S^{QP}$ surface against draft proteome abundance. Neither table supplies same-CDS rows containing codon counts, modeled tAI or stAI weights, ribosome-profiling RPF counts, matched mRNA abundance, and computed translation efficiency $TE=RPF/mRNA$ under the same controls. The present finite evidence can therefore be written as a separated pair of bounded surfaces:
$$\begin{aligned}
&Q_i \longrightarrow T^{model}_{j}\quad \text{is represented by the modeled organism sweep},\\
&Q_i \longrightarrow P^{draft}_{k}\quad \text{is represented by the human tissue protein-abundance table},\\
&Q_i \longrightarrow T^{observed}_{j}\quad \text{is not yet represented by a joined ribosome-profiling and mRNA-control matrix}.
\end{aligned}$$
This is a calibration boundary: agreement or mismatch between modeled adaptation and observed ribosome occupancy remains unmeasured until the same coding sequences are joined to RPF and mRNA rows and the declared controlled statistic is recomputed.

\paragraph{Local claim boundary.}
The chapter supports only a finite descriptive statement: after amino-acid composition, GC3, CDS length, and $M$-density controls, the nine $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates have small but nonzero descriptive associations with human draft tissue protein abundance, and the functional-class prior is not supported by the reported permutation test. The chapter also records that modeled tAI or stAI readouts have a separate rank-powered organism-level surface. No claim of measured translation realization follows from these facts alone. No folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, phenotype, mechanism, causation, or universal biological rule follows from the modeled weights, from the tissue protein-abundance projections, from the coordinate names, or from the survival notation. Those claims require their own layer-matched measurements and a fresh controlled finite statistic.
