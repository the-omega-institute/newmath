\subsection{Tissue Dependence of the $B^*_{Q6}$ Residual Projection}\label{sec:bioreality-q6-tissue-dependence-functional-class}\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite row object.}  The finite panel for this record is the human adult-tissue table obtained by joining coding-sequence rows to tissue-specific draft proteome readouts from the Kim 2014 SEQUEST PAXdb files.  The organism is Homo sapiens, and the readout used here is $\log_{10}(\mathrm{abundance\_ppm})$.  The tested coordinate block consists of the nine $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinates
$$
\begin{aligned}
&K\_AAA,\ Arg\_AGR,\ Ile\_AUA,\ Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR,\ Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG,\\
&Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY,\ Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG,\ Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY,\ f3\_stress .
\end{aligned}
$$
For each tissue, the control block has 24 columns: an intercept, $\log(\mathrm{cds\_len\_nt})$, the 20 amino-acid composition proportions $A,C,D,E,F,G,H,I,K,L,M,N,P,Q,R,S,T,V,W,Y$, GC3 from the observed coding sequence, and an $M$-density baseline equal to the fraction of sense codons on the union support of the nine projected $B^*_{Q6}$ vectors.  The projection is therefore a residual protein-abundance statistic, denoted here as $S^{QP}$, not a direct translation-efficiency, ribosome-occupancy, structure, or function measurement.

\paragraph{Tissue rows and ranks.}  Thirteen adult tissue files were usable, with no skipped tissues.  The joined row counts were: Adult\_Bcells $n=9244$, Adult\_CD4Tcells $n=7604$, Adult\_CD8Tcells $n=10914$, Adult\_Colon $n=7948$, Adult\_Frontalcortex $n=9301$, Adult\_Heart $n=6468$, Adult\_Kidney $n=6726$, Adult\_Liver $n=8664$, Adult\_Lung $n=6577$, Adult\_Monocytes $n=5179$, Adult\_NKcells $n=8787$, Adult\_Pancreas $n=9695$, and Adult\_Retina $n=10373$.  In every tissue, the residualized $B^*_{Q6}$ coordinate design retained rank $9$, while the control design had rank $23$ after the 24-column control specification.  Thus the finite rows are not an $n=3$ saturated surface: the residual degrees of freedom after controls range from $5156$ in Adult\_Monocytes to $10891$ in Adult\_CD8Tcells, and the declared degeneracy threshold was $R^2=0.999999$.

\paragraph{Per-tissue residual power.}  The controlled $S^{QP}$ values are small but nonzero across the panel.  The largest value is Adult\_Retina, with $R^2_{QP}=0.04805730109298326$ and dominant coordinate $Arg\_AGR$.  Adult\_CD8Tcells follow with $R^2_{QP}=0.03928541767885904$, also dominated by $Arg\_AGR$, and Adult\_Bcells have $R^2_{QP}=0.03626117434944674$, again with $Arg\_AGR$ dominant.  Adult\_CD4Tcells show $R^2_{QP}=0.033532527035959644$ with dominant coordinate $Ile\_AUA$.  The remaining tissues have Adult\_Pancreas $0.027616177587166925$ with $Arg\_AGR$, Adult\_NKcells $0.02709444741593442$ with $Arg\_AGR$, Adult\_Liver $0.026133456228248052$ with $Ile\_AUA$, Adult\_Frontalcortex $0.025874798845290918$ with $Ile\_AUA$, Adult\_Colon $0.023732563331619574$ with $Arg\_AGR$, Adult\_Lung $0.0232738640400933$ with $Ile\_AUA$, Adult\_Heart $0.02320896321347296$ with $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$, Adult\_Monocytes $0.021350299623075428$ with $Ile\_AUA$, and Adult\_Kidney $0.018499903301809693$ with $Arg\_AGR$.  These numbers support only a controlled descriptive residue in the draft proteome readout.  They do not by themselves identify a translation mechanism or a protein-level causal route.

\paragraph{Functional-class comparison.}  The tissue vectors were compared by a 13-tissue cosine matrix, producing $78$ tissue pairs.  The manually assigned prior classes were immune, metabolic, neural, and epithelial-or-other.  There were $17$ within-class pairs and $61$ between-class pairs.  The observed within-class mean cosine was $0.9015393659102923$, while the observed between-class mean cosine was $0.8936124027169124$, giving a within-minus-between difference of $0.007926963193379821$.  A class-size-preserving label-shuffle null with $1000$ permutations gave one-sided $p=0.3006993006993007$ for within-class similarity greater than the null.  The null differences ranged from $-0.043787962870935226$ to $0.08858262592759103$, with null mean $0.0007739569961830541$.  The finite result is therefore idiosyncratic or weakly descriptive, not functional-class structured at the usual level of evidence.  The classification labels are prior annotations, not data-derived modules.

\paragraph{Relation to modeled translation readouts.}  A linked modeled-readout sweep supplies useful context but not a substitute for observed ribosome profiling.  In that sweep, modeled tAI was computed over $41$ organisms, reaching the target of $40$ organisms, with $1000$ permutations per organism.  The $R^2_{QT}$ distribution across organisms had minimum $0.1579271837024072$, first quartile $0.26353954700074694$, median $0.33934845811598124$, mean $0.38466555440501393$, third quartile $0.5143951545826744$, and maximum $0.8213457220900503$.  That record is rank-powered and modeled; it uses codon adaptation weights from gene-copy-derived tAI-style data.  It is not a measured ribosome-footprint table, and it is not the same readout as the Kim 2014 draft proteome abundance rows used in this tissue panel.

\paragraph{Local scientific verdict.}  The finite evidence says that the nine $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinates retain a small controlled projection onto human adult tissue draft proteome abundance after length, amino-acid composition, GC3, and $M$-density controls.  The strongest tissue-level residuals occur in retina, CD8 T cells, B cells, and CD4 T cells, but the tissue-vector similarity test does not support a clean functional-class organization: $p=0.3006993006993007$ under the declared class-size-preserving permutation null.  The chapter should therefore be read as a bounded cross-layer protein-abundance projection on one human proteome study, with explicit negative scope above that layer.  No translation realization, ribosome dynamics, folding, physical admissibility, biological function, mechanism, phenotype, or universal biological rule follows from these rows unless a separate layer-matched contact supplies the appropriate measurements and a controlled statistic for that layer.
