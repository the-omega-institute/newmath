\subsection{Rank-Powered Tissue Dependence of $B^{*}_{Q6}$ Residual Coordinates}
\label{sec:bioreality-q6-tissue-dependence-functional-class-powered}

\paragraph{Finite panel.}
The record considered here is a finite human coding-sequence panel in which nine $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates are evaluated against tissue-specific draft proteome readouts.  The coordinates are $K\_AAA$, $Arg\_AGR$, $Ile\_AUA$, $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$, $Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG$, $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$, $Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG$, $Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY$, and $f3\_stress$.  Each tissue regression uses the same controlled residual design: an intercept, $\log(\mathrm{CDS\ length\ in\ nt})$, the twenty amino-acid composition proportions computed from real CDS codon counts, GC3, and an $M$-density baseline equal to the fraction of sense codons on the union support of the nine projected $B^{*}_{Q6}$ vectors.  This gives twenty-four control columns in every tissue file, with rank $23$ for the control block and rank $9$ for the residual-coordinate block after projection.

\paragraph{Observed readout and scope.}
The observed finite rows are not ribosome-footprint rows and are not mRNA-controlled translation-efficiency rows.  They are $\log_{10}(\mathrm{abundance\_ppm})$ values from the Homo sapiens PAXdb adult tissue files labelled as Kim et al. 2014 SEQUEST draft proteome readouts.  The statistic is therefore written as a bounded $S^{QP}$ projection from $Q$-coordinates to a protein-abundance readout $P$, not as a measured $Q \to T$ translation-efficiency claim.  The result may describe whether the residual codon coordinates leave a small controlled trace in tissue proteome abundance; it does not by itself identify a translation mechanism, folding constraint, physical-admissibility rule, molecular function, phenotype, or universal biological law.

\paragraph{Per-tissue controlled energies.}
Thirteen adult tissues pass the join.  The usable row counts are $9244$ for B cells, $7604$ for CD4 T cells, $10914$ for CD8 T cells, $7948$ for colon, $9301$ for frontal cortex, $6468$ for heart, $6726$ for kidney, $8664$ for liver, $6577$ for lung, $5179$ for monocytes, $8787$ for NK cells, $9695$ for pancreas, and $10373$ for retina.  All thirteen regressions are nondegenerate under the declared threshold $0.999999$: the residual degrees of freedom after the control block range from $5156$ in monocytes to $10891$ in CD8 T cells. \paragraph{Dominant residual coordinates.}
The leading coordinate is not uniform across tissues.  $Arg\_AGR$ is dominant in B cells, CD8 T cells, colon, kidney, NK cells, pancreas, and retina; $Ile\_AUA$ is dominant in CD4 T cells, frontal cortex, liver, lung, and monocytes; $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$ is dominant only in heart.  Thus the finite panel does not reduce to a single universal codon residual.  It records a tissue-indexed pattern in which arginine AGR and isoleucine AUA residual directions are most often selected, with one serine-family exception.  This is compatible with a heterogeneous tissue-abundance projection and is not evidence that the same coordinate controls translation or downstream protein state in every biological setting.

\paragraph{Functional-class label test.}
The packet also computes a $13 \times 13$ cosine comparison among the tissue $S^{QP}$ vectors, giving $78$ tissue pairs.  The functional-class prior supplies $17$ within-class pairs and $61$ between-class pairs.  The observed within-class mean cosine is $0.9015393659102923$, the observed between-class mean cosine is $0.8936124027169124$, and the observed within-minus-between difference is therefore $0.007926963193379821$.  A class-size-preserving label-shuffle test with $1000$ permutations gives a one-sided within-greater $p$-value of $0.3006993006993007$, with null differences ranging from $-0.043787962870935226$ to $0.08858262592759103$ and null mean $0.0007739569961830541$.  The finite result is consequently not a powered positive claim for functional-class organization.  The correct reading is narrower: tissue vectors are highly mutually similar in this projection, but the imposed immune, metabolic, neural, and epithelial-or-other labels do not obtain a small permutation probability in this run.

\paragraph{Relation to modeled translation weights.}
A separate rank-powered modeled-readout sweep over $41$ organisms supplies bounded $S^{QT}$ context for tAI or stAI-style codon-adaptation weights.  In that sweep the per-organism modeled $R^{2}_{QT}$ distribution has minimum $0.1579271837024072$, first quartile $0.26353954700074694$, median $0.33934845811598124$, mean $0.38466555440501393$, third quartile $0.5143951545826744$, and maximum $0.8213457220900503$, using $1000$ permutations and the same nine residual coordinates with twenty-four controls.  Those numbers show that the $Q$-residual coordinate system can have nontrivial alignment with modeled codon-adaptation readouts in a rank-powered organism set.  They do not convert the human tissue PAXdb abundance analysis into ribosome profiling, and they do not replace a same-CDS matrix containing codon counts, RPF rows, matched mRNA abundance, and a computed translation-efficiency statistic.

\paragraph{Local conclusion.}
The finite human tissue packet is ready as a descriptive cross-layer relation from $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates to draft proteome abundance.  Its strongest local facts are the thirteen nondegenerate tissue fits, the small controlled $R^{2}_{QP}$ interval from $0.018499903301809693$ to $0.04805730109298326$, the repeated dominance of $Arg\_AGR$ and $Ile\_AUA$, and the non-significant functional-class prior test with $p=0.3006993006993007$.  The correct scientific statement is therefore deliberately bounded: $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates leave a measurable but small controlled tissue-abundance projection in this finite panel, while the available rows do not establish translation efficiency, ribosome occupancy, protein folding, physical admissibility, biological function, mechanism, phenotype, or universality.  Any such stronger layer requires its own matched measurement surface and its own controlled statistic before it can be stated.
