\subsection{Human tissue dependence of $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual protein-readout projections}
\label{subsec:biorality-q6-tissue-dependence-functional-class}

\paragraph{Finite panel.}
This record evaluates whether the nine projected $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates have a stable tissue-level pattern when they are projected against a human protein-abundance readout.  The finite carrier is the set of human coding sequences that join to the Kim et al. 2014 SEQUEST adult-tissue PAXdb files, with the response fixed as $\log_{10}(\mathrm{abundance\_ppm})$.  The residual coordinate block is
$$
\begin{aligned}
Q=\{&K\_AAA,\ Arg\_AGR,\ Ile\_AUA,\ Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR,\ Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG,\\
&Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY,\ Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG,\ Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY,\ f3\_stress\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
For every tissue row, the control side contains an intercept, $\log(\mathrm{cds\_len\_nt})$, twenty amino-acid composition proportions computed from the same coding-sequence codon counts, GC3, and an $M$-density baseline defined as the fraction of sense codons lying on the union support of the nine projected $B^{*}_{Q6}$ vectors.  The reported control-column count is $24$ in every loaded tissue, while the fitted residual coordinate rank is $9$ and the control rank is $23$ in every tissue-specific fit.  Thus this is a controlled protein-readout projection, not a free comparison of codon counts to abundance.

\paragraph{Loaded rows.}
Thirteen adult human tissue readouts were loaded, and no loaded tissue was skipped.  The usable joined row counts were $9244$ for Adult\_Bcells, $7604$ for Adult\_CD4Tcells, $10914$ for Adult\_CD8Tcells, $8787$ for Adult\_NKcells, $5179$ for Adult\_Monocytes, $8664$ for Adult\_Liver, $6726$ for Adult\_Kidney, $9695$ for Adult\_Pancreas, $9301$ for Adult\_Frontalcortex, $10373$ for Adult\_Retina, $7948$ for Adult\_Colon, $6577$ for Adult\_Lung, and $6468$ for Adult\_Heart.  The smallest finite panel is therefore still above five thousand joined coding-sequence rows, and the largest is above ten thousand rows.  The tissue-pair cosine matrix contains $78$ pairwise comparisons across the $13$ tissue vectors.

\paragraph{Dominant residual coordinates.}
The dominant coordinate is not uniform across the tissue panel.  Adult\_Bcells, Adult\_CD8Tcells, Adult\_Colon, Adult\_Kidney, Adult\_NKcells, Adult\_Pancreas, and Adult\_Retina are led by $Arg\_AGR$.  Adult\_CD4Tcells, Adult\_Frontalcortex, Adult\_Liver, Adult\_Lung, and Adult\_Monocytes are led by $Ile\_AUA$.  Adult\_Heart is led by $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$.  This coordinate heterogeneity is the main finite-row reason to classify the result as idiosyncratic or not functional-class structured, rather than as a clean module law.  No module classification, hit-rate statistic, $\lambda$ value, or separate $M$-list is present in the supplied rows; the only $M$ quantity in this record is the declared $M$-density control used in every fit.

\paragraph{Functional-class label test.}
The tissue labels used for the descriptive prior were immune, metabolic, neural, and epithelial-or-other.  There were $17$ within-class tissue pairs and $61$ between-class tissue pairs.  The observed within-class mean cosine was $0.9015393659102923$, the observed between-class mean cosine was $0.8936124027169124$, and the observed difference was
$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta_{\mathrm{within-between}}&=0.9015393659102923-0.8936124027169124\\
&=0.007926963193379821.
\end{aligned}
$$
Against a class-size-preserving label-shuffle null with $1000$ permutations, the one-sided probability for within-class similarity being larger was $0.3006993006993007$.  The null distribution had mean $0.0007739569961830541$, minimum $-0.043787962870935226$, and maximum $0.08858262592759103$.  The finite conclusion is therefore not that the tissue vectors sort by the supplied functional labels.  The small positive observed difference is not rare under the stated label-null procedure.

\paragraph{Relation to modeled translation readouts.}
A linked modeled-readout sweep used $41$ organisms and reached the declared target of $40$ organisms.  In that sweep the modeled $S^{QT}$ distribution had mean $0.38466555440501393$, median $0.33934845811598124$, lower quartile $0.26353954700074694$, upper quartile $0.5143951545826744$, minimum $0.1579271837024072$, and maximum $0.8213457220900503$, with $1000$ permutations and $24$ control columns.  Those values show that modeled tAI-style surfaces can be computed at rank-powered organism scale, but they are not ribosome-profiling measurements.  The present tissue record instead uses draft proteome abundance in one human study.  Consequently the modeled translation sweep and this protein-abundance tissue panel should be read as adjacent bounded readouts, not as a calibrated measured translation-to-protein mechanism.

\paragraph{Local interpretation.}
The finite result is a controlled, human, tissue-specific $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual projection against $\log_{10}(\mathrm{abundance\_ppm})$.  It supports the narrow statement that, after amino-acid composition, coding-sequence length, GC3, and $M$-density controls, the nine-coordinate residual block leaves weak but measurable tissue-specific abundance projections in the Kim et al. 2014 SEQUEST PAXdb adult tissue files.  It also supports the negative statement that the supplied immune/metabolic/neural/epithelial-or-other labels do not give a strong class-structured explanation of the tissue cosine matrix, since the observed within-minus-between difference has one-sided permutation probability $0.3006993006993007$.

\paragraph{Boundary of use.}
This chapter does not claim translation realization, ribosome occupancy, protein folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, system phenotype, mechanism, adaptation, causation, or universality.  The readout is a draft tissue proteome abundance surface, not ribosome profiling, not an mRNA-controlled translation-efficiency matrix, and not a perturbation assay.  A claim at any higher biological layer would require a separate layer-matched contact and a separately recorded statistic.  In particular, no inference from $S^{QP}$ to translation efficiency is licensed here, and no inference from a tissue-specific abundance projection to folded structure, localization, stability, post-translational state, biological function, or a general biological law is licensed here.
