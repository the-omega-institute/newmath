\subsection{Rank-powered $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ tissue readout boundary}\label{sec:bioreality-q6-tissue-dependence-functional-class}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite panel.}
This record treats the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates as a bounded cross-layer readout test, not as a protein mechanism.  The finite coordinate panel contains nine residual coordinates after amino-acid quotienting: $K\_AAA$, $Arg\_AGR$, $Ile\_AUA$, $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$, $Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG$, $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$, $Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG$, $Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY$, and $f3\_stress$.  For the human tissue panel, each tissue fit used the same controlled design with an intercept, $\log(\mathrm{cds\_len\_nt})$, the twenty amino-acid composition proportions from coding-sequence codon counts, GC3, and an $M$-density baseline defined on the union support of the nine projected $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ vectors.  The recorded control-column count is $24$ for every loaded tissue, with $\operatorname{rank}(Z)=23$ and $\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde X)=9$ in every tissue-specific fit.

\paragraph{Observed tissue readout.}
The observed readout in this finite run is $\log_{10}(\mathrm{abundance\_ppm})$ from human Kim et al. 2014 SEQUEST adult-tissue PAXdb files, not ribosome profiling and not an mRNA-controlled translation-efficiency matrix.  Thirteen tissue files were loaded with no skipped tissues.  The joined row counts were $9244$ for Adult B cells, $7604$ for Adult CD4 T cells, $10914$ for Adult CD8 T cells, $8787$ for Adult NK cells, $5179$ for Adult monocytes, $8664$ for adult liver, $6726$ for adult kidney, $9695$ for adult pancreas, $9301$ for adult frontal cortex, $10373$ for adult retina, $7948$ for adult colon, $6577$ for adult lung, and $6468$ for adult heart.  The fit is therefore powered as a descriptive proteome-abundance projection, but it is not a measured translation-efficiency calibration.

\paragraph{Per-tissue residual survival surface.}
The tissue-specific $S^{QP}$ values are small and nondegenerate.  The minimum recorded value is $0.018499903301809693$ in adult kidney, and the maximum is $0.04805730109298326$ in adult retina.  The immune tissues show $R^2_{QP}=0.03626117434944674$ for Adult B cells, $0.033532527035959644$ for Adult CD4 T cells, $0.03928541767885904$ for Adult CD8 T cells, $0.02709444741593442$ for Adult NK cells, and $0.021350299623075428$ for Adult monocytes.  The metabolic-labelled tissues show $0.026133456228248052$ for adult liver, $0.018499903301809693$ for adult kidney, and $0.027616177587166925$ for adult pancreas.  The neural-labelled tissues show $0.025874798845290918$ for adult frontal cortex and $0.04805730109298326$ for adult retina.  The epithelial-or-other-labelled tissues show $0.023732563331619574$ for adult colon, $0.0232738640400933$ for adult lung, and $0.02320896321347296$ for adult heart.

\paragraph{Dominant coordinates.}
The dominant coordinate is not constant across tissues.  $Arg\_AGR$ is dominant in Adult B cells, Adult CD8 T cells, adult colon, adult kidney, Adult NK cells, adult pancreas, and adult retina.  $Ile\_AUA$ is dominant in Adult CD4 T cells, adult frontal cortex, adult liver, adult lung, and Adult monocytes.  $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$ is dominant only in adult heart.  This pattern supports an idiosyncratic tissue readout surface rather than a single coordinate law.  The retina has the largest $R^2_{QP}$ value while sharing the $Arg\_AGR$ dominant coordinate with several immune, metabolic, and epithelial-or-other tissues; hence the coordinate label alone does not identify a functional tissue class.

\paragraph{Functional-class prior test.}
The cosine matrix contains $78$ tissue pairs across $13$ tissues.  The prespecified class labels give $17$ within-class pairs and $61$ between-class pairs.  The observed within-class mean cosine is $0.9015393659102923$, the observed between-class mean cosine is $0.8936124027169124$, and the observed within-minus-between contrast is
$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta_{\mathrm{obs}} &= 0.9015393659102923 - 0.8936124027169124 \\
&= 0.007926963193379821 .
\end{aligned}
$$
A class-size-preserving permutation null with $1000$ permutations gives one-sided $p=0.3006993006993007$ for the within-class mean being larger.  The observed contrast is positive but weak relative to the label-shuffle null; the finite row therefore records descriptive tissue variation, not functional-class structure.  The null range seen in the permutations runs from $-0.043787962870935226$ to $0.08858262592759103$, with null mean $0.0007739569961830541$, so the measured contrast lies well inside the finite null envelope.

\paragraph{Relation to modeled codon-adaptation readout.}
A separate rank-powered modeled-readout sweep computed modeled tAI over $41$ organisms and reached the target of $40$ organisms.  In that sweep the $S^{QT}$ distribution had minimum $0.1579271837024072$, first quartile $0.26353954700074694$, median $0.33934845811598124$, mean $0.38466555440501393$, third quartile $0.5143951545826744$, and maximum $0.8213457220900503$, using $1000$ permutations per organism.  Those numbers show that modeled codon-adaptation surfaces can be computed on a rank-powered organism panel, but they do not turn the human tissue $S^{QP}$ proteome-abundance projection into observed ribosome occupancy or measured translation efficiency.  The modeled readout and the PAXdb tissue readout remain two distinct finite contacts with different measurement semantics.

\paragraph{Local boundary.}
The finite evidence supports only the following bounded statement: after amino-acid composition, CDS length, GC3, and $M$-density controls, the nine $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates have small tissue-specific projections onto a human draft proteome-abundance readout, and those projections do not show a statistically supported functional-class clustering under the recorded label-shuffle test.  No translation mechanism, ribosome-occupancy claim, folded-structure claim, physical-admissibility claim, molecular-function claim, system-phenotype claim, or universal biological law follows from this table.  A measured translation-efficiency calibration would require same-organism and same-CDS codon counts, modeled tAI or stAI weights, ribosome-profiling RPF rows, matched mRNA rows, and the corresponding controlled statistic on the joined matrix.  Until that contact is present, disagreement between modeled codon adaptation and observed ribosome-profiling translation efficiency can only be recorded as a pending readout mismatch, not promoted to protein-level interpretation.
