\subsection{Rank-powered $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual readouts across modeled adaptation and tissue proteome panels}\label{subsec:bior reality-q6-tissue-dependence-functional-class}\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite carrier.}
The record is a finite cross-layer panel built from nine $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates after amino-acid quotienting, namely $K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $Leu_{CUN\_vs\_UUR}$, $Leu_{UUA\_vs\_UUG}$, $Ser_{UCR\_vs\_AGY}$, $Ser_{UCA\_vs\_UCG}$, $Thr_{ACR\_vs\_ACY}$, and $f3\_stress$.  Each coordinate is evaluated only as a residual codon-composition direction against finite readouts.  The control block contains an intercept, $\log(\mathrm{cds\_len\_nt})$, the twenty amino-acid composition proportions $A,C,D,E,F,G,H,I,K,L,M,N,P,Q,R,S,T,V,W,Y$, $GC3$, and an $M$-density baseline defined as the fraction of sense codons lying on the union support of the nine projected $B^{*}_{Q6}$ vectors.  In the human tissue panel, each loaded tissue uses 24 control columns, while the fitted residual design has $\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde X)=9$ and $\operatorname{rank}(Z)=23$ in every reported tissue.

\paragraph{Modeled adaptation reference panel.}
A separate rank-powered modeled-readout sweep uses organism-scoped tAI-style codon-adaptation weights for 41 organisms, reaching the declared target of 40 organisms.  The modeled $S^{QT}$ projection is descriptive: the $R^2_{QT}$ distribution over organisms has minimum $0.1579271837024072$, first quartile $0.26353954700074694$, median $0.33934845811598124$, mean $0.38466555440501393$, third quartile $0.5143951545826744$, and maximum $0.8213457220900503$, with 1000 permutations used in the organism sweep.  These values establish that the modeled-adaptation panel is rank-powered and nontrivial, but they do not measure ribosome occupancy, translation efficiency, or protein output.

$$\begin{aligned}
\min R^2_{QT} &= 0.1579271837024072,\\
\operatorname{median} R^2_{QT} &= 0.33934845811598124,\\
\operatorname{mean} R^2_{QT} &= 0.38466555440501393,\\
\max R^2_{QT} &= 0.8213457220900503.
\end{aligned}$$

\paragraph{Human tissue proteome readout.}
The tissue calculation uses Homo sapiens draft tissue proteome readouts, specifically $\log_{10}(\mathrm{abundance\_ppm})$ from Kim et al. 2014 SEQUEST adult tissue PAXdb files.  Thirteen tissues are loaded and none are skipped: Adult\_Bcells, Adult\_CD4Tcells, Adult\_CD8Tcells, Adult\_NKcells, Adult\_Monocytes, Adult\_Liver, Adult\_Kidney, Adult\_Pancreas, Adult\_Frontalcortex, Adult\_Retina, Adult\_Colon, Adult\_Lung, and Adult\_Heart.  The usable joined row counts are 9244, 7604, 10914, 8787, 5179, 8664, 6726, 9695, 9301, 10373, 7948, 6577, and 6468 in that order.  Thus every tissue has thousands of joined rows, and the smallest panel is Adult\_Monocytes with 5179 rows, while the largest is Adult\_CD8Tcells with 10914 rows.

\paragraph{Per-tissue residual power.}
The controlled $S^{QP}$ values remain small in absolute magnitude and are interpreted as tissue-specific statistical projections, not as causal biology.  The reported $R^2_{QP}$ values are $0.03626117434944674$ for Adult\_Bcells, $0.033532527035959644$ for Adult\_CD4Tcells, $0.03928541767885904$ for Adult\_CD8Tcells, $0.02709444741593442$ for Adult\_NKcells, $0.021350299623075428$ for Adult\_Monocytes, $0.026133456228248052$ for Adult\_Liver, $0.018499903301809693$ for Adult\_Kidney, $0.027616177587166925$ for Adult\_Pancreas, $0.025874798845290918$ for Adult\_Frontalcortex, $0.04805730109298326$ for Adult\_Retina, $0.023732563331619574$ for Adult\_Colon, $0.0232738640400933$ for Adult\_Lung, and $0.02320896321347296$ for Adult\_Heart.  Adult\_Retina is the largest reported tissue projection at $0.04805730109298326$, and Adult\_Kidney is the smallest at $0.018499903301809693$.

$$\begin{aligned}
\min R^2_{QP} &= 0.018499903301809693\quad\text{Adult\_Kidney},\\

\paragraph{Dominant residual coordinates.}
The dominant coordinate is not uniform across tissues.  $Arg_{AGR}$ is dominant in Adult\_Bcells, Adult\_CD8Tcells, Adult\_NKcells, Adult\_Pancreas, Adult\_Retina, Adult\_Colon, and Adult\_Kidney.  $Ile_{AUA}$ is dominant in Adult\_CD4Tcells, Adult\_Monocytes, Adult\_Liver, Adult\_Frontalcortex, and Adult\_Lung.  $Ser_{UCR\_vs\_AGY}$ is dominant only in Adult\_Heart.  This distribution is a finite descriptive signature of the readout panel.  It is not promoted to an explanation of immune, metabolic, neural, epithelial, or cardiac function.

\paragraph{Functional-class label test.}
The cosine matrix contains 78 tissue pairs over the 13 tissues.  The prior class labels produce 17 within-class pairs and 61 between-class pairs.  The observed within-class mean cosine is $0.9015393659102923$, the observed between-class mean cosine is $0.8936124027169124$, and the observed within-minus-between contrast is $0.007926963193379821$.  A class-size-preserving label permutation test with 1000 permutations gives one-sided $p=0.3006993006993007$ for within-class similarity being larger than the shuffled-label null.  The null distribution spans from $-0.043787962870935226$ to $0.08858262592759103$, with null mean $0.0007739569961830541$.  Therefore the functional-class prior is not supported as a strong organizing law in this panel; the record is better read as idiosyncratic or weakly structured descriptive tissue variation.

$$\begin{aligned}
\overline c_{\mathrm{within}} &= 0.9015393659102923,\\
\overline c_{\mathrm{between}} &= 0.8936124027169124,\\
\Delta c &= 0.007926963193379821,\\
p_{\mathrm{one\mbox{-}sided}} &= 0.3006993006993007.
\end{aligned}$$

\paragraph{Boundary of the claim.}
This chapter records two bounded facts: a 41-organism modeled codon-adaptation sweep has nondegenerate $R^2_{QT}$ values, and a 13-tissue human proteome panel has controlled $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual projections against $\log_{10}(\mathrm{abundance\_ppm})$ with a weak functional-class contrast.  The chapter does not claim observed ribosome-profiling translation efficiency, because the same-CDS joined matrix containing codon counts, modeled tAI or stAI weights, ribosome-profiling RPF rows, matched mRNA abundance rows, and computed $TE=RPF/mRNA$ is not present here.  It also does not claim realized translation, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, mechanism, phenotype, or universality.  Any such assertion would require an additional layer-matched contact and a new controlled statistic on the corresponding finite rows.
