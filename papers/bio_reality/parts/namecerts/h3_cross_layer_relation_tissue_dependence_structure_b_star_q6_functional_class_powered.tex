\subsection{B-star Q6 Tissue Dependence Structure under Functional-Class Labels}
\label{sec:bioreality-b-star-q6-tissue-dependence-functional-class}

\paragraph{Finite panel.}
This BioReality record concerns a finite human tissue panel in which nine $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates are compared with tissue-specific draft proteome readouts after the amino-acid quotient and after a shared control layer has been applied.  The coordinates are $K\_AAA$, $Arg\_AGR$, $Ile\_AUA$, $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$, $Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG$, $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$, $Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG$, $Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY$, and $f3\_stress$.  The biological rows are $Homo\ sapiens$ coding sequences joined to Kim et al. 2014 SEQUEST adult tissue PAXdb files, with the response variable $\log_{10}(abundance\_ppm)$.  The control matrix contains an intercept, $\log(cds\_len\_nt)$, the twenty amino-acid composition proportions $A,C,D,E,F,G,H,I,K,L,M,N,P,Q,R,S,T,V,W,Y$, GC3 computed from real CDS codon counts, and an $M$-density baseline defined on the union support of the nine projected $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ coordinate vectors.  Each tissue reports 24 control columns, rank $rank(Z)=23$, and residualized coordinate rank $rank(\widetilde X)=9$.

\paragraph{Per-tissue readback.}
The powered tissue panel contains 13 loaded adult tissues and no skipped tissue file.  The joined row counts are 9,244 for B cells, 7,604 for CD4 T cells, 10,914 for CD8 T cells, 8,787 for NK cells, 5,179 for monocytes, 8,664 for liver, 6,726 for kidney, 9,695 for pancreas, 9,301 for frontal cortex, 10,373 for retina, 7,948 for colon, 6,577 for lung, and 6,468 for heart.  The fitted $S^{QP}$ summaries are small in magnitude: retina has the largest recorded $R^2_{QP}=0.04805730109298326$, followed by CD8 T cells at $0.03928541767885904$, B cells at $0.03626117434944674$, CD4 T cells at $0.033532527035959644$, pancreas at $0.027616177587166925$, NK cells at $0.02709444741593442$, liver at $0.026133456228248052$, frontal cortex at $0.025874798845290918$, colon at $0.023732563331619574$, lung at $0.0232738640400933$, heart at $0.02320896321347296$, monocytes at $0.021350299623075428$, and kidney at $0.018499903301809693$.  Thus the finite record is not a high-explanation proteome law; it is a low-amplitude residual projection whose values remain inside a descriptive tissue readout surface.

\paragraph{Dominant-coordinate pattern.}
The dominant residual coordinate varies across tissues rather than collapsing to a single universal coordinate.  $Arg\_AGR$ is dominant in B cells, CD8 T cells, colon, kidney, NK cells, pancreas, and retina.  $Ile\_AUA$ is dominant in CD4 T cells, frontal cortex, liver, lung, and monocytes.  $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$ is dominant only in heart.  The largest explained-energy entries are CD8 T cells with $491.7590959574601$, retina with $396.28840522457523$, and B cells with $290.65227005813716$; the smallest listed explained-energy entry is monocytes with $57.0282547084279$.  The corresponding residual response energies range from $2671.075147198014$ in monocytes to $12517.598768514408$ in CD8 T cells.  Since every tissue has the same nine-coordinate residual rank after the control projection, this variation is read as a tissue-indexed descriptive geometry, not as evidence that the codon residuals cause a tissue program.

\paragraph{Functional-class check.}
A cosine matrix was computed for all 78 tissue pairs across the 13 tissues.  The human prior labels used for the descriptive comparison group immune tissues together, metabolic tissues together, neural tissues together, and epithelial-or-other tissues together.  There are 17 within-class tissue pairs and 61 between-class tissue pairs.  The observed within-class mean cosine is $0.9015393659102923$, while the observed between-class mean cosine is $0.8936124027169124$, giving
$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta_{within-between} &= 0.9015393659102923 - 0.8936124027169124 \\
&= 0.007926963193379821 .
\end{aligned}
$$
A class-size-preserving label-shuffle test with 1000 permutations gives one-sided $p=0.3006993006993007$ for within-class similarity being larger than the shuffled-label baseline.  The null distribution recorded mean $0.0007739569961830541$, minimum $-0.043787962870935226$, and maximum $0.08858262592759103$.  This does not support a functional-class-structured tissue law.  The finite conclusion is therefore idiosyncratic or not functional-class-structured under this prior label partition.

\paragraph{Relation to modeled translation readouts.}
A separate rank-powered modeled-readout sweep gives organism-level context only.  In that sweep, modeled tAI was computed for 41 organisms, with a target of 40 organisms reached, 1000 permutations per organism, and $R^2_{QT}$ distribution minimum $0.1579271837024072$, first quartile $0.26353954700074694$, median $0.33934845811598124$, mean $0.38466555440501393$, third quartile $0.5143951545826744$, and maximum $0.8213457220900503$.  Those values say that the residual coordinate family can be paired with a modeled codon-adaptation readout in a rank-powered organism sweep.  They do not supply the joined same-CDS ribosome-footprint, matched mRNA, and codon-count table required for an observed translation-efficiency calibration in the tissue panel.

\paragraph{Local boundary.}
The record is intentionally bounded.  The tissue calculation uses draft proteome abundance, not ribosome profiling, and it is not mRNA-controlled.  The functional-class labels are a human prior partition, not a discovered causal module.  Modeled tAI or stAI weights are codon-adaptation annotations, not measured ribosome occupancy.  No translation realization, protein folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, mechanism, phenotype, or universal biological rule follows from the $S^{QP}$ tissue surface, the modeled $S^{QT}$ organism sweep, the cosine matrix, or the coordinate names.  Any such claim would require a separate layer-matched contact and a new finite statistic on joined rows.  In the present chapter the admissible statement is only this: after amino-acid composition, length, GC3, and $M$-density controls, the nine $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates leave a small tissue-dependent draft-proteome projection in 13 adult human tissue files, and the observed within-versus-between functional-class difference is not significant under the recorded label-shuffle test.
