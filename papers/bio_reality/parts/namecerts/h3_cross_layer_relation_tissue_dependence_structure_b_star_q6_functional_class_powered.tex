\subsection{Tissue Dependence of the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ Residual Panel}\label{sec:bioreality-q6-tissue-dependence-functional-class}\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite panel.}
The record concerns a finite human tissue panel in which the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates are tested against tissue-specific protein-abundance readouts before any interpretation at the level of protein mechanism.  The coordinate source is the nine-vector residual panel imported from the powered $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ translation-survival construction: $K\_AAA$, $Arg\_AGR$, $Ile\_AUA$, $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$, $Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG$, $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$, $Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG$, $Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY$, and $f3\_stress$.  The observed readout used here is $\log_{10}(\mathrm{abundance\_ppm})$ from the PAXdb $9606$ Adult Kim 2014 SEQUEST tissue files.  The organism is $Homo\ sapiens$, and the loaded panel contains $13$ adult tissues with no skipped tissue files.

\paragraph{Controls and rank.}
Each tissue regression uses the same controlled residual design: an intercept, $\log(\mathrm{CDS\ length\ in\ nt})$, the $20$ amino-acid composition proportions $A,C,D,E,F,G,H,I,K,L,M,N,P,Q,R,S,T,V,W,Y$ from real CDS codon counts, GC3 from the same codon counts, and an $M$-density baseline defined as the fraction of sense codons lying on the union support of the nine projected $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ vectors.  The reported control-column count is $24$ for every tissue.  After residualization, every tissue has $\mathrm{rank}(\widetilde X)=9$ for the $Q6$ coordinate block and $\mathrm{rank}(Z)=23$ for the controls, so the calculation is not an all-fitted rank-saturation artifact.  The usable joined row counts are $9244$ for Adult B cells, $7604$ for Adult CD4 T cells, $10914$ for Adult CD8 T cells, $7948$ for Adult Colon, $9301$ for Adult Frontal cortex, $6468$ for Adult Heart, $6726$ for Adult Kidney, $8664$ for Adult Liver, $6577$ for Adult Lung, $5179$ for Adult Monocytes, $8787$ for Adult NK cells, $9695$ for Adult Pancreas, and $10373$ for Adult Retina.

\paragraph{Per-tissue residual strength.}
The computed quantity is a controlled $S^{QP}$-style statistical projection from the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ coordinate block to the tissue protein-abundance readout, not a causal translation or protein-production mechanism.  The observed partial $R^2_{QP}$ values are small but nonzero across all $13$ tissues:

The smallest value is in Adult Kidney, $0.0184999033$, and the largest value is in Adult Retina, $0.0480573011$.  Thus the finite calculation supports only a weak descriptive tissue-dependent protein-abundance association for this residual panel, with no promotion to translation efficiency, folding, physical admissibility, biological function, mechanism, or universality.

\paragraph{Dominant-coordinate readback.}
The dominant residual coordinate varies by tissue.  $Arg\_AGR$ is dominant in Adult B cells, Adult CD8 T cells, Adult Colon, Adult Kidney, Adult NK cells, Adult Pancreas, and Adult Retina.  $Ile\_AUA$ is dominant in Adult CD4 T cells, Adult Frontal cortex, Adult Liver, Adult Lung, and Adult Monocytes.  $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$ is dominant only in Adult Heart.  This distribution is best read as idiosyncratic tissue dependence of the controlled projection surface: the calculation records which residual coordinate contributes most to the fitted abundance projection after the declared controls, but it does not identify a translation apparatus, a protein-stability route, a localization route, or a molecular function.

\paragraph{Functional-class label test.}
The tissue-label comparison used immune, metabolic, neural, and epithelial-or-other priors as a descriptive label partition.  The cosine matrix contains $78$ tissue pairs over $13$ tissues.  Within-class pairs number $17$, and between-class pairs number $61$.  The observed within-class mean cosine is $0.9015393659$, the observed between-class mean cosine is $0.8936124027$, and the observed within-minus-between difference is only $0.0079269632$.  Against $1000$ class-size-preserving permutations with the deterministic seed recorded for this run, the one-sided value for within-class similarity above the label-shuffle null is $p=0.3006993007$.  The null mean difference is $0.0007739570$, with the sampled null range from $-0.0437879629$ to $0.0885826259$.  Therefore the finite panel does not support a functional-class-structured tissue law; the stronger reading is rejected in favor of a descriptive, weak, tissue-specific association surface.

\paragraph{Relation to the modeled translation sweep.}
A separate rank-powered organism sweep computed modeled tAI-based $S^{QT}$ readouts over $41$ organisms, reaching a target of $40$ organisms with $1000$ permutations per organism.  In that modeled sweep, the $R^2_{QT}$ distribution had minimum $0.1579271837$, first quartile $0.2635395470$, median $0.3393484581$, mean $0.3846655544$, third quartile $0.5143951546$, and maximum $0.8213457221$.  Those values show that the same $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual panel can produce substantial modeled codon-adaptation projections under an organism-sweep design.  They do not, however, turn the present tissue-abundance calculation into a ribosome-profiling or translation-efficiency result.  Modeled tAI or stAI weights, PAXdb protein abundance, ribosome profiling, and matched mRNA controls are separate readout routes and must not be conflated.

\paragraph{Local boundary.}
The finite evidence supports the following bounded statement.  In the Kim 2014 SEQUEST adult human PAXdb panel, the nine $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates have small controlled associations with tissue-specific $\log_{10}(\mathrm{abundance\_ppm})$ readouts, and the tissue-specific fitted vectors are not convincingly organized by the manually supplied functional-class labels.  This is a cross-layer descriptive relation between synonymous residual coordinates and a protein-abundance readout.  It is not evidence for observed translation efficiency, ribosome occupancy, protein folding, structural order, physical admissibility, localization, post-translational state, biological function, system phenotype, mechanism, or a universal biological law.  Any such stronger claim requires its own matched measurement contact and a separately recorded controlled statistic.
