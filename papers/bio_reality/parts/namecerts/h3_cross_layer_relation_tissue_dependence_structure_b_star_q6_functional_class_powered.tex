\subsection{Tissue-Indexed Protein-Abundance Readback for $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ Residual Coordinates}\label{sec:bior reality-q6-tissue-dependence-functional-class}\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite panel and coordinate scope.}
This record treats the nine projected $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates as finite sequence-level regressors and asks how much of a tissue-indexed human protein-abundance readout remains associated with them after ordinary composition and length controls have been removed.  The coordinate list is
$K\_AAA$, $Arg\_AGR$, $Ile\_AUA$, $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$, $Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG$, $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$, $Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG$, $Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY$, and $f3\_stress$.  The readout is $\log_{10}(\mathrm{abundance\_ppm})$ from human Kim et al. 2014 SEQUEST adult-tissue PAXdb files.  The panel is therefore not a ribosome-footprint panel, not a matched mRNA-controlled translation-efficiency panel, and not a structural or functional assay.  It is a finite protein-abundance projection for Homo sapiens, used only to test whether the residual coordinates leave a small, controlled, tissue-dependent statistical trace in a downstream abundance surface.

\paragraph{Controls and rank diagnostics.}
For each tissue, the control block contains an intercept, $\log(\mathrm{cds\_len\_nt})$, twenty amino-acid composition proportions computed from real CDS codon counts, GC3 from the same codon counts, and an $M$-density baseline equal to the fraction of sense codons lying in the union support of the nine projected $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ vectors.  The declared control-column count is $24$ in every loaded tissue, while the numerical rank of the control matrix is $23$ in every tissue.  The residualized coordinate block has rank $9$ in every tissue, so the reported $R^2_{QP}$ values are not produced by an all-saturated coordinate block.  The usable row counts range from $5179$ in Adult Monocytes to $10914$ in Adult CD8 T cells, with all thirteen loaded tissues retained and no skipped tissue entries.

$$
\begin{aligned}
|Q| &= 9,\\
\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde X) &= 9,\\
\operatorname{rank}(Z) &= 23,\\
\#\mathrm{controls} &= 24,\\
\#\mathrm{tissues} &= 13,\\
\#\mathrm{pairwise\ cosines} &= 78.
\end{aligned}
$$

Adult CD8 T cells follow with $R^2_{QP}=0.03928541767885904$, dominant coordinate $Arg\_AGR$, and $10914$ joined rows. Adult CD4 T cells have $R^2_{QP}=0.033532527035959644$ with dominant coordinate $Ile\_AUA$ and $7604$ rows.  The remaining values are Adult Pancreas $0.027616177587166925$, Adult NK cells $0.02709444741593442$, Adult Liver $0.026133456228248052$, Adult Frontal Cortex $0.025874798845290918$, Adult Colon $0.023732563331619574$, Adult Lung $0.0232738640400933$, Adult Heart $0.02320896321347296$, Adult Monocytes $0.021350299623075428$, and Adult Kidney $0.018499903301809693$. \paragraph{Functional-class label check.}
A class-size-preserving permutation test compares within-class and between-class cosine similarity among the thirteen tissue coefficient profiles.  The observed within-class mean cosine is $0.9015393659102923$, and the observed between-class mean cosine is $0.8936124027169124$, giving observed within-minus-between contrast $0.007926963193379821$.  The permutation count is $1000$, with deterministic seed recorded for the label-shuffle scheme.  The one-sided probability for within-class similarity exceeding the shuffled-label null is $0.3006993006993007$.  The null deltas range from $-0.043787962870935226$ to $0.08858262592759103$, with null mean $0.0007739569961830541$.  Thus the tissue profiles are descriptively present but not convincingly organized by the imposed immune, metabolic, neural, and epithelial-or-other labels.  The local conclusion is therefore idiosyncratic-or-not-functional-class-structured, not a positive functional-class law.

$$
\begin{aligned}
\overline c_{\mathrm{within}} &= 0.9015393659102923,\\
\overline c_{\mathrm{between}} &= 0.8936124027169124,\\
\Delta_{\mathrm{obs}} &= 0.007926963193379821,\\
p_{\mathrm{one\ sided}} &= 0.3006993006993007,\\
N_{\mathrm{perm}} &= 1000.
\end{aligned}
$$

\paragraph{Relation to modeled translation readouts.}
A separate rank-powered organism sweep supplies context for the modeled-readout side: $41$ organisms were swept, the target of $40$ organisms was reached, and modeled tAI was computed for $41$ organisms.  In that sweep, the $S^{QT}$ distribution under $1000$ permutations has mean $0.38466555440501393$, median $0.33934845811598124$, first quartile $0.26353954700074694$, third quartile $0.5143951545826744$, minimum $0.1579271837024072$, and maximum $0.8213457220900503$.  That result is useful only as a bounded modeled codon-adaptation readout.  It does not make the present tissue-abundance panel a measured translation-efficiency panel, and it does not substitute for same-CDS ribosome-profiling and matched mRNA rows.

\paragraph{Local claim and boundary.}
The supported statement is finite and narrow: in thirteen human adult PAXdb tissue readouts, the nine $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates retain small controlled associations with $\log_{10}(\mathrm{abundance\_ppm})$, and the dominant residual coordinate varies by tissue.  The functional-label organization test does not pass a strong label-structured threshold because the observed contrast is small and the permutation probability is $0.3006993006993007$.  No translation realization follows from this chapter.  Modeled tAI or stAI weights are not observed ribosome occupancy; protein abundance is not a ribosome-footprint or translation-efficiency measurement; matched mRNA abundance is only a control when present; and neither the residual coordinates nor the $S^{QP}$ notation imply folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, mechanism, phenotype, or universality.  Any such claim would require a separate layer-matched contact and a new controlled statistic on the corresponding finite rows.
