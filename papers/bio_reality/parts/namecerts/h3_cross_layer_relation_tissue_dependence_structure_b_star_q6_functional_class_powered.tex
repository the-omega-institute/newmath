\subsection{B$^*_{Q6}$ tissue dependence in a controlled human protein-abundance panel}
\label{sec:bioreality-q6-tissue-dependence-functional-class}

\paragraph{Finite panel and readout.}
This record tests whether the nine B$^*_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates retain a tissue-dependent statistical relation to a bounded downstream readout after ordinary coding-sequence covariates have been removed.  The finite carrier is a human coding-sequence panel joined to Kim et al. 2014 SEQUEST adult-tissue PAXdb draft proteome files.  The readout is $\log_{10}(\mathrm{abundance\_ppm})$, not ribosome occupancy and not translation efficiency.  The thirteen loaded tissues are Adult\_Bcells, Adult\_CD4Tcells, Adult\_CD8Tcells, Adult\_NKcells, Adult\_Monocytes, Adult\_Liver, Adult\_Kidney, Adult\_Pancreas, Adult\_Frontalcortex, Adult\_Retina, Adult\_Colon, Adult\_Lung, and Adult\_Heart.  No tissue was skipped.  The joined row counts range from $5179$ in Adult\_Monocytes to $10914$ in Adult\_CD8Tcells, with the full list
$$
\begin{aligned}
&n_{\mathrm{Bcells}}=9244,\quad n_{\mathrm{CD4T}}=7604,\quad n_{\mathrm{CD8T}}=10914,\quad n_{\mathrm{Colon}}=7948,\\
&n_{\mathrm{Frontalcortex}}=9301,\quad n_{\mathrm{Heart}}=6468,\quad n_{\mathrm{Kidney}}=6726,\quad n_{\mathrm{Liver}}=8664,\\
&n_{\mathrm{Lung}}=6577,\quad n_{\mathrm{Monocytes}}=5179,\quad n_{\mathrm{NKcells}}=8787,\quad n_{\mathrm{Pancreas}}=9695,\\
&n_{\mathrm{Retina}}=10373.
\end{aligned}
$$
The tested residual coordinates are $K\_AAA$, $Arg\_AGR$, $Ile\_AUA$, $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$, $Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG$, $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$, $Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG$, $Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY$, and $f3\_stress$.

\paragraph{Controls and rank.}
Each tissue fit uses the same control design: an intercept, $\log(\mathrm{cds\_len\_nt})$, the twenty amino-acid composition proportions $A,C,D,E,F,G,H,I,K,L,M,N,P,Q,R,S,T,V,W,Y$ computed from real CDS codon counts, GC3, and an M-density baseline defined as the fraction of sense codons on the union support of the nine projected B$^*_{Q6}$ vectors.  The control column count is $24$ in every tissue.  The control rank is $23$, the residualized B$^*_{Q6}$ coordinate rank is $9$, and the degeneracy threshold is $R^2=0.999999$ in every reported fit.  Thus the panel is not the refused $n=3$ saturated case; it is a high-row-count descriptive protein-abundance calculation with explicit covariate removal.  The residual degrees of freedom after the controls range from $5156$ to $10891$ across tissues.

\paragraph{Per-tissue residual protein-abundance signal.}
The controlled B$^*_{Q6}$ to protein-abundance projections are small but nonzero in all thirteen tissues.  The observed $R^2_{QP}$ values are
$$
\begin{aligned}
&0.036261\;\mathrm{(Bcells)},\quad 0.033533\;\mathrm{(CD4T)},\quad 0.039285\;\mathrm{(CD8T)},\quad 0.023733\;\mathrm{(Colon)},\\
&0.025875\;\mathrm{(Frontalcortex)},\quad 0.023209\;\mathrm{(Heart)},\quad 0.018500\;\mathrm{(Kidney)},\quad 0.026133\;\mathrm{(Liver)},\\
&0.023274\;\mathrm{(Lung)},\quad 0.021350\;\mathrm{(Monocytes)},\quad 0.027094\;\mathrm{(NKcells)},\quad 0.027616\;\mathrm{(Pancreas)},\\
&0.048057\;\mathrm{(Retina)}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The minimum is Adult\_Kidney at $0.0184999033$, and the maximum is Adult\_Retina at $0.0480573011$.  The dominant coordinate is $Arg\_AGR$ in Bcells, CD8Tcells, Colon, Kidney, NKcells, Pancreas, and Retina; $Ile\_AUA$ in CD4Tcells, Frontalcortex, Liver, Lung, and Monocytes; and $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$ in Heart.  This distribution is tissue-specific in the descriptive sense that the leading coordinate changes across tissues, but the numerical scale remains a low-$R^2$ protein-abundance projection rather than a mechanistic explanation.

\paragraph{Functional-class label test.}
The tissue vectors were also compared by cosine similarity under a hand-assigned functional-class prior: immune, metabolic, neural, or epithelial\_or\_other.  The cosine matrix contains $78$ tissue pairs from $13$ tissues.  Of these, $17$ pairs are within the same prior class and $61$ pairs are between classes.  The observed within-class mean cosine is $0.9015393659$, the observed between-class mean cosine is $0.8936124027$, and the observed difference is
$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta_{\mathrm{within-between}} &= 0.9015393659 - 0.8936124027\\
&= 0.0079269632.
\end{aligned}
$$
A class-size-preserving label-shuffle test with $1000$ permutations gives one-sided $p=0.3006993007$ for the within-class mean exceeding the between-class mean.  The permutation null has mean $0.0007739570$, minimum observed delta $-0.0437879629$, and maximum observed delta $0.0885826259$.  Therefore the finite panel does not support a strong functional-class organization of the B$^*_{Q6}$ protein-abundance vectors.  The better local description is idiosyncratic tissue dependence: the coordinates leave weak controlled traces in protein-abundance readouts, but the imposed immune/metabolic/neural/epithelial labels do not explain those traces at conventional strength.

\paragraph{Relation to modeled translation readouts.}
A separate rank-powered organism sweep computed modeled tAI-style translation projections for $41$ organisms, reaching the target of $40$ organisms.  In that modeled-readout calculation, the $R^2_{QT}$ distribution across organisms had minimum $0.1579271837$, first quartile $0.2635395470$, median $0.3393484581$, mean $0.3846655544$, third quartile $0.5143951546$, and maximum $0.8213457221$, with $1000$ permutations.  Those numbers are relevant only as bounded modeled codon-adaptation readouts.  They do not turn the present PAXdb tissue-protein calculation into ribosome profiling, and they do not identify a measured translation-efficiency bridge.

\paragraph{Local conclusion and boundary.}
The finite result is a controlled, descriptive cross-layer relation from B$^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinates to human adult-tissue draft protein abundance.  It justifies the statement that, after length, amino-acid composition, GC3, and M-density controls, the nine residual coordinates produce small tissue-specific protein-abundance projections with $R^2_{QP}$ between $0.0185$ and $0.0481$.  It does not justify a claim of translation realization, ribosome occupancy, translation efficiency, protein folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, causal mechanism, phenotype, or universality.  The source is a single Kim et al. 2014 SEQUEST PAXdb proteome panel, the functional-class labels are prior labels rather than data-derived modules, and the observed label enrichment has $p=0.3006993007$.  Any stronger biological interpretation would require a separate matched contact at the relevant layer: ribosome-profiling and matched mRNA rows for translation efficiency, structure or physical assays for folding and admissibility, perturbation or functional assays for mechanism and function, and independent panels before any general law is asserted.
