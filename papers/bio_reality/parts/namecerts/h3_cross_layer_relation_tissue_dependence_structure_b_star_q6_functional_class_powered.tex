\paragraph{Finite panel.}
The record tests whether the nine projected $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates carry any finite, tissue-indexed structure when read against a bounded protein-abundance surface. The organism is $\mathrm{Homo\ sapiens}$, and the readout is $\log_{10}(\mathrm{abundance\_ppm})$ from the PAXdb $9606$ adult tissue Kim et al. 2014 SEQUEST files. Thirteen adult tissues enter the panel: B cells, CD4 T cells, CD8 T cells, NK cells, monocytes, liver, kidney, pancreas, frontal cortex, retina, colon, lung, and heart. The joined row counts are not small: $9244$, $7604$, $10914$, $8787$, $5179$, $8664$, $6726$, $9695$, $9301$, $10373$, $7948$, $6577$, and $6468$, respectively. The coordinate list is
$$
\begin{aligned}

&Ser_{UCR\_vs\_AGY},\ Ser_{UCA\_vs\_UCG},\ Thr_{ACR\_vs\_ACY},\ f3\_stress\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
Each tissue regression uses the same declared control family: an intercept, $\log(\mathrm{cds\_len\_nt})$, the twenty amino-acid composition proportions computed from real CDS codon counts, GC3, and the $M$-density baseline, where the latter is the fraction of sense codons lying on the union support of the nine projected $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ vectors. The nominal control column count is $24$ in every tissue; the effective control rank is $23$, the residualized $Q$-rank is $9$, and the degeneracy threshold for $R^2$ is $0.999999$. Thus the calculation is not an all-fit rank artifact.

\paragraph{Functional-class label test.}
The tissue labels used for the finite comparison are immune, metabolic, neural, and epithelial-or-other. The cosine matrix contains $13$ tissues and $78$ tissue pairs. Among these, $17$ pairs lie within the assigned functional class and $61$ lie between assigned classes. The observed mean within-class cosine is $0.9015393659102923$, while the observed mean between-class cosine is $0.8936124027169124$, giving
$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta_{\mathrm{within-between}}
&=0.9015393659102923-0.8936124027169124\\
&=0.007926963193379821.
\end{aligned}
$$
A class-size-preserving label permutation test with $1000$ permutations gives one-sided $p=0.3006993006993007$ for the alternative that within-class similarity is larger. The deterministic null generator has seed $\mathrm{sha256{:}b\_star\_q6\_tissue\_dependence\_structure\_powered|functional\_class\_label\_null|perm\_index|index}$; over the null draws the mean delta is $0.0007739569961830541$, the minimum delta is $-0.043787962870935226$, and the maximum delta is $0.08858262592759103$. The finite conclusion is therefore descriptive: the tissue projection surface exists as a computed panel, but the functional-class prior is not supported as a strong organizing law by this test.

Those numbers are useful context for the existence of a rank-powered modeled codon-adaptation surface, but they do not supply ribosome-footprint rows, translation-efficiency rows, or a same-CDS modeled-to-observed calibration matrix for the human tissue protein-abundance panel.

\paragraph{Local claim boundary.}
The chapter supports only the following finite statement: after amino-acid composition, CDS length, GC3, and $M$-density controls, the nine $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates have small tissue-specific projections to a Kim et al. 2014 PAXdb adult-tissue protein-abundance readout, and the resulting tissue vectors do not show a statistically persuasive functional-class clustering signal under the stated permutation test. This statement does not assert measured translation efficiency, ribosome occupancy, translation mechanism, protein folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, phenotype, causation, or a universal biological law. A claim at any of those layers would require a separate matched contact: for translation, same-CDS ribosome profiling with matched mRNA abundance and an explicit TE statistic; for structure, a structure-level readout; for physical admissibility, a physical or stability assay; for function or phenotype, a layer-matched assay or perturbation panel. The present finite-row record remains a controlled descriptive protein-abundance projection, not a mechanism.
