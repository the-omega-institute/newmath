\subsection{Tissue Dependence of the $B^*_{Q6}$ Residual Projection}
\label{sec:biorality-q6-tissue-dependence-functional-class-powered}

\paragraph{Finite panel.}
This record tests whether the nine $B^*_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates form a reproducible tissue-patterned projection when compared with a bounded protein-abundance readout in human adult tissues. The rows are coding sequences joined to tissue-specific PAXdb Kim 2014 SEQUEST draft proteome files for $\textit{Homo sapiens}$, with the response recorded as $\log_{10}(\mathrm{abundance\_ppm})$. The coordinate block is
$K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $Leu_{CUN\_vs\_UUR}$, $Leu_{UUA\_vs\_UUG}$, $Ser_{UCR\_vs\_AGY}$, $Ser_{UCA\_vs\_UCG}$, $Thr_{ACR\_vs\_ACY}$, and $f3\_stress$. Each tissue regression uses the same control family: an intercept, $\log(\mathrm{cds\_len\_nt})$, the twenty amino-acid composition proportions computed from real CDS codon counts, GC3, and the $M$-density baseline defined by the fraction of sense codons lying on the union support of the nine projected $B^*_{Q6}$ vectors. The observed control-column count is 24 in every loaded tissue; the residualized coordinate rank is 9, and the observed control rank is 23 in every tissue-specific fit.

\paragraph{Joined tissue rows.}
Thirteen adult tissue files are usable. The joined row counts are 9,244 for B cells, 7,604 for CD4 T cells, 10,914 for CD8 T cells, 8,787 for NK cells, 5,179 for monocytes, 8,664 for liver, 6,726 for kidney, 9,695 for pancreas, 9,301 for frontal cortex, 10,373 for retina, 7,948 for colon, 6,577 for lung, and 6,468 for heart. No loaded tissue is skipped. These row counts are large enough to avoid the small-rank saturation failure that would make an all-perfect controlled matrix biologically uninterpretable; the residual degrees of freedom after controls range from 5,156 in monocytes to 10,891 in CD8 T cells.

\paragraph{Per-tissue projection strength.}
The finite statistic is a controlled $S^{QP}$-style projection from $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinates to the protein-abundance readout. The fitted partial energies are small but nonzero across all thirteen tissues. The largest reported value is in retina, with $R^2_{QP}=0.04805730109298326$, dominant coordinate $Arg_{AGR}$, and 10,373 joined rows. CD8 T cells follow with $R^2_{QP}=0.03928541767885904$ and dominant coordinate $Arg_{AGR}$ on 10,914 rows. B cells give $R^2_{QP}=0.03626117434944674$ with dominant coordinate $Arg_{AGR}$ on 9,244 rows, and CD4 T cells give $R^2_{QP}=0.033532527035959644$ with dominant coordinate $Ile_{AUA}$ on 7,604 rows. The lowest value is kidney, with $R^2_{QP}=0.018499903301809693$ and dominant coordinate $Arg_{AGR}$ on 6,726 rows. The other tissues remain in the same low-effect band: colon has $0.023732563331619574$, frontal cortex has $0.025874798845290918$, heart has $0.02320896321347296$, liver has $0.026133456228248052$, lung has $0.0232738640400933$, monocytes have $0.021350299623075428$, NK cells have $0.02709444741593442$, and pancreas has $0.027616177587166925$.

\paragraph{Dominant residual coordinates.}
The dominant coordinate is not constant across tissues. $Arg_{AGR}$ is dominant in B cells, CD8 T cells, colon, kidney, NK cells, pancreas, and retina. $Ile_{AUA}$ is dominant in CD4 T cells, frontal cortex, liver, lung, and monocytes. Heart is the only loaded tissue whose dominant coordinate is $Ser_{UCR\_vs\_AGY}$. This supports an idiosyncratic tissue-dependence record rather than a single universal coordinate rule. The result is therefore best read as a finite controlled projection landscape: the same nine-coordinate residual basis has measurable but weak tissue-dependent alignment with one draft proteome abundance readout.

\paragraph{Functional-class label test.}
A cosine matrix was computed for all 78 tissue pairs. The tissue labels were grouped by a prior functional-class annotation: immune, metabolic, neural, and epithelial-or-other. There are 17 within-class pairs and 61 between-class pairs. The observed within-class mean cosine is 0.9015393659102923, the observed between-class mean cosine is 0.8936124027169124, and the observed within-minus-between difference is 0.007926963193379821. A class-size-preserving permutation test with 1,000 permutations gives a one-sided value $p=0.3006993006993007$ for within-class similarity being greater than the label-shuffle null. The null distribution spans from $-0.043787962870935226$ to $0.08858262592759103$, with mean $0.0007739569961830541$. Thus the functional-class prior is not supported at a conventional small-$p$ threshold in this finite panel. The numerical outcome is compatible with weak tissue structure, but it does not justify a claim that the hand-assigned tissue classes explain the projection geometry.

\paragraph{Relation to the modeled translation-weight sweep.}
A separate bounded modeled-readout sweep over organism-level tAI-style weights reached 41 organisms with 1,000 permutations per organism. Its $R^2_{QT}$ distribution has minimum 0.1579271837024072, first quartile 0.26353954700074694, median 0.33934845811598124, mean 0.38466555440501393, third quartile 0.5143951545826744, and maximum 0.8213457220900503. That companion statistic shows that the same residual basis can be used against modeled codon-adaptation weights in a rank-powered organism panel, but it is not the same empirical object as the thirteen-tissue protein-abundance projection. The tissue calculation here uses draft human proteome abundance, not ribosome profiling, not measured translation efficiency, and not matched mRNA residualization.

\paragraph{Local boundary.}
The contact used here is a tissue-specific draft proteome abundance surface. It is not a ribosome-occupancy measurement, not a direct translation-efficiency matrix, and not an mRNA-controlled TE estimate. The calculation therefore does not establish translation realization. It also does not establish folded structure, physical admissibility, biochemical function, localization, post-translational state, mechanism, phenotype, adaptation, causation, or universality. Those claims would require separate layer-matched contacts and their own finite statistics. Within the scope of this chapter, the supported statement is narrower: after amino-acid composition, CDS length, GC3, and $M$-density controls, the nine $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinates have small tissue-dependent projection values against one human draft proteome abundance readout, while the functional-class label contrast remains statistically weak in the recorded permutation test.
