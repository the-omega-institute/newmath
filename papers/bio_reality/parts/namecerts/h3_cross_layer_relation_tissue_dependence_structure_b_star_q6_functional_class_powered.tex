\subsection{Rank-powered $B^*_{Q6}$ tissue readout before translation promotion}
\label{sec:bioreality-q6-tissue-dependence-functional-class-powered}

\paragraph{Finite carrier.}
This record concerns a finite human coding-sequence panel in which nine $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinates are evaluated against tissue-specific protein-abundance readouts before any claim is made about translation efficiency, protein mechanism, folded structure, physical admissibility, cellular function, phenotype, or universality.  The coordinate list is $K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $Leu_{CUN\,vs\,UUR}$, $Leu_{UUA\,vs\,UUG}$, $Ser_{UCR\,vs\,AGY}$, $Ser_{UCA\,vs\,UCG}$, $Thr_{ACR\,vs\,ACY}$, and $f_3$-stress.  Each tissue fit uses the same control grammar: an intercept, $\log(\mathrm{CDS\ length\ in\ nt})$, the twenty amino-acid composition proportions $A,C,D,E,F,G,H,I,K,L,M,N,P,Q,R,S,T,V,W,Y$, GC3 computed from real CDS codon counts, and an $M$-density baseline defined on the union support of the nine projected $B^*_{Q6}$ vectors.  The recorded control-column count is $24$ in every tissue, while the fitted residual-coordinate rank is $9$ and the control rank is $23$ in every reported tissue fit.

\paragraph{Observed finite panel.}
The readout used here is $\log_{10}(\mathrm{abundance\_ppm})$ from the human Kim et al. 2014 SEQUEST adult-tissue PAXdb files.  Thirteen adult tissues enter the panel: B cells, CD4 T cells, CD8 T cells, NK cells, monocytes, liver, kidney, pancreas, frontal cortex, retina, colon, lung, and heart.  No tissue listed in the finite input was skipped.  The usable joined row counts are $9244$ for B cells, $7604$ for CD4 T cells, $10914$ for CD8 T cells, $8787$ for NK cells, $5179$ for monocytes, $8664$ for liver, $6726$ for kidney, $9695$ for pancreas, $9301$ for frontal cortex, $10373$ for retina, $7948$ for colon, $6577$ for lung, and $6468$ for heart.  Thus the record is powered as a descriptive proteome-readout panel, but it is not a ribosome-profiling or mRNA-controlled translation-efficiency panel.

\paragraph{Per-tissue residual signal.}
The fitted $S^{QP}$ surface is small but nonzero across all thirteen tissues when read only as a controlled protein-abundance projection.  Retina has the largest reported $R^2_{QP}$, $0.04805730109298326$, with dominant coordinate $Arg_{AGR}$ and $10373$ joined rows.  CD8 T cells follow with $R^2_{QP}=0.03928541767885904$, also dominated by $Arg_{AGR}$, on $10914$ rows.  B cells have $R^2_{QP}=0.03626117434944674$ with $Arg_{AGR}$ dominance on $9244$ rows, while CD4 T cells have $R^2_{QP}=0.033532527035959644$ with $Ile_{AUA}$ dominance on $7604$ rows.  The remaining reported values are $0.027616177587166925$ for pancreas, $0.02709444741593442$ for NK cells, $0.026133456228248052$ for liver, $0.025874798845290918$ for frontal cortex, $0.023732563331619574$ for colon, $0.0232738640400933$ for lung, $0.02320896321347296$ for heart, $0.021350299623075428$ for monocytes, and $0.018499903301809693$ for kidney.  These values do not assert causal explanation; they only say that, after the listed sequence-composition controls, a finite residual coordinate block accounts for a small fraction of the tissue proteome-readout variance.

\paragraph{Tissue-class comparison.}
The cosine matrix was computed for all $78$ tissue pairs.  A descriptive functional-class prior was then tested with $17$ within-class pairs and $61$ between-class pairs under a class-size-preserving permutation null with $1000$ permutations.  The observed within-class mean cosine is $0.9015393659102923$, the observed between-class mean cosine is $0.8936124027169124$, and the observed within-minus-between contrast is $0.007926963193379821$.  The permutation distribution has mean contrast $0.0007739569961830541$, minimum observed null contrast $-0.043787962870935226$, maximum observed null contrast $0.08858262592759103$, and one-sided $p=0.3006993006993007$ for within-class similarity being larger than the shuffled-label baseline.  This is not strong evidence that the hand-assigned immune, metabolic, neural, and epithelial-or-other labels explain the residual-projection geometry.  The safer reading is idiosyncratic or weakly structured tissue dependence, not a functional-class law.

\paragraph{Relation to modeled translation readouts.}
A linked rank-powered organism sweep supplies a separate modeled-adaptation context: $41$ organisms were swept, the target of $40$ organisms was reached, and each organism-level modeled tAI calculation used the same nine $B^*_{Q6}$ coordinates with $24$ controls.  Across those modeled $S^{QT}$ organism fits, the $R^2_{QT}$ distribution has minimum $0.1579271837024072$, first quartile $0.26353954700074694$, median $0.33934845811598124$, mean $0.38466555440501393$, third quartile $0.5143951545826744$, and maximum $0.8213457220900503$, with $1000$ permutations recorded per organism.  Those numbers are useful as bounded modeled-adaptation readbacks, but they are not ribosome occupancy and they do not license replacing observed translation-efficiency rows with tAI or stAI weights.

\paragraph{No-promotion boundary.}
The finite tissue result is therefore a cross-layer boundary record, not a protein-mechanism claim.  It may say that a nine-coordinate $B^*_{Q6}$ residual block has small controlled associations with one draft human proteome abundance readout, and that the tissue-label cosine contrast does not pass a strong label-shuffle test.  It may also place this beside a separate organism-level modeled tAI sweep.  It may not say that translation has been measured, because no same-CDS ribosome-profiling RPF rows and matched mRNA rows have been joined here to compute $TE=RPF/mRNA$.  It may not infer folded structure, physical admissibility, localization, stability, post-translational state, molecular function, organismal phenotype, mechanism, or a universal biological law.  Any such assertion would require a distinct layer-matched measurement contact and a new controlled statistic on the same biological rows.
