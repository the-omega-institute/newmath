\subsection{Tissue Dependence of $B^*_{Q6}$ Residual Protein-Readout Geometry}
\label{sec:bioreality-q6-tissue-dependence-functional-class}

\paragraph{Finite panel.}
This record concerns a finite human panel in which nine $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinates are tested against tissue-specific draft proteome readouts after quotienting by amino-acid composition and other sequence controls.  The coordinates are
$$
\begin{aligned}
&K\_AAA,\ Arg\_AGR,\ Ile\_AUA,\ Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR,\ Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG,\\
&Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY,\ Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG,\ Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY,\ f3\_stress.
\end{aligned}
$$
For each tissue, the response is $\log_{10}(\mathrm{abundance\_ppm})$ from the Homo sapiens Kim 2014 SEQUEST adult tissue PAXdb files.  The control block has $24$ columns per tissue: an intercept, $\log(\mathrm{cds\_len\_nt})$, the twenty amino-acid composition proportions $A,C,D,E,F,G,H,I,K,L,M,N,P,Q,R,S,T,V,W,Y$, GC3 computed from real coding-sequence codon counts, and an $M$-density baseline equal to the fraction of sense codons lying on the union support of the nine projected $B^*_{Q6}$ vectors.  Thus the experiment is a finite residual readout comparison, not a claim about protein mechanism.

\paragraph{Rows and rank.}
Thirteen adult tissues enter the joined panel, with no skipped tissue files.  The joined row counts are $9244$ for B cells, $7604$ for CD4 T cells, $10914$ for CD8 T cells, $7948$ for colon, $9301$ for frontal cortex, $6468$ for heart, $6726$ for kidney, $8664$ for liver, $6577$ for lung, $5179$ for monocytes, $8787$ for NK cells, $9695$ for pancreas, and $10373$ for retina.  In every tissue the residualized coordinate rank is $9$ and the residualized control rank is $23$, with degeneracy threshold $0.999999$.  The residual degrees of freedom after controls range from $5156$ in monocytes to $10891$ in CD8 T cells, so the reported surfaces are not the rank-saturated $n=3$ boundary.

\paragraph{Per-tissue residual protein-readout surface.}
The finite statistic is the controlled $S^{QP}$ surface from $B^*_{Q6}$ coordinates to draft protein abundance.  The observed $R^2_{QP}$ values are small but nonzero: $0.03626117434944674$ in B cells, $0.033532527035959644$ in CD4 T cells, $0.03928541767885904$ in CD8 T cells, $0.023732563331619574$ in colon, $0.025874798845290918$ in frontal cortex, $0.02320896321347296$ in heart, $0.018499903301809693$ in kidney, $0.026133456228248052$ in liver, $0.0232738640400933$ in lung, $0.021350299623075428$ in monocytes, $0.02709444741593442$ in NK cells, $0.027616177587166925$ in pancreas, and $0.04805730109298326$ in retina.  The largest value is therefore the retina value $0.04805730109298326$, while the smallest is the kidney value $0.018499903301809693$.

\paragraph{Dominant residual coordinates.}
The dominant coordinate is not constant across tissues.  $Arg\_AGR$ is dominant in B cells, CD8 T cells, colon, kidney, NK cells, pancreas, and retina.  $Ile\_AUA$ is dominant in CD4 T cells, frontal cortex, liver, lung, and monocytes.  $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$ is dominant only in heart.  This split is the main finite-row reason to describe the panel as tissue-dependent and idiosyncratic rather than as a universal codon-to-protein rule.

\paragraph{Functional-class prior test.}
The tissue labels used for the descriptive prior are immune, metabolic, neural, and epithelial-or-other.  The cosine matrix contains $13$ tissues and $78$ tissue pairs.  Within-class pairs number $17$, between-class pairs number $61$, and the label-null is class-size preserving with $1000$ permutations.  The observed within-class mean cosine is $0.9015393659102923$, the observed between-class mean cosine is $0.8936124027169124$, and the observed within-minus-between contrast is
$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta_{\mathrm{class}} = 0.9015393659102923 - 0.8936124027169124 = 0.007926963193379821.
\end{aligned}
$$
The one-sided permutation value for within-class similarity exceeding the label-shuffle null is $0.3006993006993007$.  The null distribution has mean contrast $0.0007739569961830541$, minimum $-0.043787962870935226$, and maximum $0.08858262592759103$.  Hence the observed contrast is positive but not strong evidence for functional-class organization under this prior.  The local conclusion is descriptive: the surface is compatible with weak tissue structure, but the functional labels do not supply a decisive organizing law.

\paragraph{Modeled translation readout comparison.}
A linked rank-powered organism sweep supplies a separate modeled-translation context rather than a measured ribosome context.  In that sweep, modeled tAI was computed for $41$ organisms, with a target of $40$ organisms reached, $1000$ permutations per organism, and the same nine-coordinate residual family with $24$ control columns.  The $R^2_{QT}$ distribution over organisms has minimum $0.1579271837024072$, first quartile $0.26353954700074694$, median $0.33934845811598124$, mean $0.38466555440501393$, third quartile $0.5143951545826744$, and maximum $0.8213457220900503$.  These values show that modeled codon-adaptation readouts can carry substantially larger residual signal than the human tissue protein-abundance readouts in this finite collection.  They do not identify ribosome occupancy, translation efficiency, or protein mechanism; they only provide a bounded modeled-readout comparison.

\paragraph{No-promotion boundary.}
The finite evidence in this chapter is a controlled residual comparison between $B^*_{Q6}$ sequence coordinates and draft tissue protein-abundance readouts, plus a separate modeled tAI organism sweep.  It does not establish observed translation survival, because the tissue panel is not ribosome profiling and is not mRNA-controlled.  It does not establish folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, cellular mechanism, phenotype, or universality.  Such conclusions would require separate layer-matched contacts: measured ribosome occupancy or translation efficiency with matched mRNA abundance for translation, structural or physical assays for folding and admissibility, functional or perturbation assays for biological role, and independently powered panels before any general law is asserted.  Within the data actually present here, the admissible claim is narrower: after amino-acid composition, GC3, length, and $M$-density controls, the nine-coordinate $B^*_{Q6}$ residual family leaves a small, tissue-dependent, nondegenerate imprint on one human draft proteome readout, while the functional-class prior test remains descriptive rather than decisive.
