\subsection{Rank-controlled tissue dependence of the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual panel}
\label{sec:b-star-q6-tissue-dependence-functional-class-powered}

\paragraph{Finite carrier.}
The carrier in this chapter is a finite human coding-sequence panel joined to tissue-specific draft proteome readouts from the Kim et al. 2014 SEQUEST PAXdb adult tissue files.  The response variable is $\log_{10}(\mathrm{abundance\_ppm})$, and the predictor block is the nine-coordinate $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual panel
$K\_AAA$, $Arg\_AGR$, $Ile\_AUA$, $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$, $Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG$, $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$, $Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG$, $Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY$, and $f3\_stress$.  The control block contains an intercept, $\log(\mathrm{cds\_len\_nt})$, the twenty amino-acid composition proportions $A,C,D,E,F,G,H,I,K,L,M,N,P,Q,R,S,T,V,W,Y$, $GC3$, and the $M$-density baseline computed as the fraction of sense codons lying on the union support of the nine projected residual vectors.  Each adult tissue therefore has $24$ control columns before the nine residual coordinates are tested.

\paragraph{Per-tissue residual readout.}
For each tissue $t$, the statistic reported here is the controlled residual projection strength $R^2_{QP}(t)$ of the nine-coordinate residual block against the tissue protein-abundance readout after the stated controls.  The finite rows are not sparse: the joined sample sizes range from $5179$ coding sequences in Adult\_Monocytes to $10914$ in Adult\_CD8Tcells.  The measured controlled values are
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{QP}(\mathrm{Adult\_Bcells}) &= 0.0362611743,\\
R^2_{QP}(\mathrm{Adult\_CD4Tcells}) &= 0.0335325270,\\
R^2_{QP}(\mathrm{Adult\_CD8Tcells}) &= 0.0392854177,\\
R^2_{QP}(\mathrm{Adult\_Colon}) &= 0.0237325633,\\
R^2_{QP}(\mathrm{Adult\_Frontalcortex}) &= 0.0258747988,\\
R^2_{QP}(\mathrm{Adult\_Heart}) &= 0.0232089632,\\
R^2_{QP}(\mathrm{Adult\_Kidney}) &= 0.0184999033,\\
R^2_{QP}(\mathrm{Adult\_Liver}) &= 0.0261334562,\\
R^2_{QP}(\mathrm{Adult\_Lung}) &= 0.0232738640,\\
R^2_{QP}(\mathrm{Adult\_Monocytes}) &= 0.0213502996,\\
R^2_{QP}(\mathrm{Adult\_NKcells}) &= 0.0270944474,\\
R^2_{QP}(\mathrm{Adult\_Pancreas}) &= 0.0276161776,\\
R^2_{QP}(\mathrm{Adult\_Retina}) &= 0.0480573011.
\end{aligned}
$$
The strongest finite row is Adult\_Retina, with $R^2_{QP}=0.0480573011$ over $10373$ joined coding sequences.  The weakest is Adult\_Kidney, with $R^2_{QP}=0.0184999033$ over $6726$ joined coding sequences.  All thirteen tissue fits have $\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde X)=9$, so the residual coordinate block is not collapsed by the control projection.  The control design has rank $23$ in every tissue despite the $24$ listed control columns, reflecting the finite design dependency induced by the composition-style controls rather than a loss of the residual block itself.

\paragraph{Dominant coordinate ledger.}
The dominant residual coordinate is tissue-dependent.  $Arg\_AGR$ is dominant in Adult\_Bcells, Adult\_CD8Tcells, Adult\_Colon, Adult\_Kidney, Adult\_NKcells, Adult\_Pancreas, and Adult\_Retina.  $Ile\_AUA$ is dominant in Adult\_CD4Tcells, Adult\_Frontalcortex, Adult\_Liver, Adult\_Lung, and Adult\_Monocytes.  $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$ is dominant only in Adult\_Heart.  Thus the finite record does not support a single universal residual direction for all tissues; it records a modest, tissue-indexed protein-abundance projection in which the leading coordinate changes across the panel.

\paragraph{Functional-class comparison.}
The thirteen tissues were also grouped by a prior functional label: immune, metabolic, neural, or epithelial-or-other.  The cosine matrix contains $78$ tissue pairs, split into $17$ within-class pairs and $61$ between-class pairs.  The observed within-class mean cosine is $0.9015393659$, while the observed between-class mean cosine is $0.8936124027$, giving
$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta_{\mathrm{within-between}}
  &= 0.9015393659 - 0.8936124027\\
  &= 0.0079269632.
\end{aligned}
$$
A class-size-preserving label-shuffle test with $1000$ permutations gives a one-sided $p$-value of $0.3006993007$ for within-class similarity being greater than the shuffle null.  The permutation ledger records a null mean delta of $0.0007739570$, a minimum seen delta of $-0.0437879629$, and a maximum seen delta of $0.0885826259$.  This is therefore a descriptive tissue-structure signal, not evidence for strong functional-class organization.  The correct finite conclusion is idiosyncratic or not functional-class structured, rather than function-class powered.

\paragraph{Rank and non-saturation.}
The diagnostic rows rule out the vacuous all-fit boundary for this tissue panel.  Each tissue keeps all nine $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates after the control projection, and the degeneracy threshold is $0.999999$, whereas the observed $R^2_{QP}$ values lie between $0.0184999033$ and $0.0480573011$.  The residual degrees of freedom after controls range from $5156$ in Adult\_Monocytes to $10891$ in Adult\_CD8Tcells.  For example, Adult\_CD8Tcells has explained energy $491.7590959575$ and residual response energy $12517.5987685144$, while Adult\_Retina has explained energy $396.2884052246$ and residual response energy $8246.1643956622$.  These rows are finite, non-saturated protein-abundance projections; they are not rank-degenerate perfect fits.

\paragraph{Modeled translation context.}
A separate modeled translation sweep over $41$ organisms computed tAI-style $S^{QT}$ readouts for the same residual family with $1000$ permutations per organism.  In that sweep, the $R^2_{QT}$ distribution has minimum $0.1579271837$, first quartile $0.2635395470$, median $0.3393484581$, mean $0.3846655544$, third quartile $0.5143951546$, and maximum $0.8213457221$.  That finite row is useful as a modeled codon-adaptation comparison surface, but it is not the same object as the human adult-tissue $S^{QP}$ protein-abundance ledger above.  The chapter therefore keeps the modeled tAI or stAI surface, the observed tissue proteome surface, and any future ribosome-profiling translation-efficiency surface as separate finite readouts.

\paragraph{Local boundary.}
The chapter establishes only a controlled association between the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual coordinate panel and tissue-specific draft human protein-abundance readouts in one PAXdb/Kim 2014 SEQUEST data source.  It does not establish ribosome occupancy, translation efficiency, translation mechanism, protein folding, physical admissibility, localization, post-translational state, molecular function, phenotype, causation, or a universal biological rule.  In particular, the tissue protein-abundance readout is not ribosome profiling and is not mRNA-controlled translation efficiency.  A claim about modeled-to-observed translation calibration would require same-organism and same-CDS codon rows, modeled tAI or stAI weights, ribosome-footprint rows, matched mRNA abundance rows, and a controlled $TE=RPF/mRNA$ statistic.  Until that separate contact is attached, disagreement between modeled codon-adaptation survival and observed ribosome-profiling survival must be recorded as a readout mismatch, not promoted into a protein-level mechanism.
