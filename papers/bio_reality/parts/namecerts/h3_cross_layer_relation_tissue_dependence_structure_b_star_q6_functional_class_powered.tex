\subsection{Tissue Dependence of the $B^*_{Q6}$ Residual Protein-Readout Projection}\label{subsec:bioq6-tissue-dependence-protein-readout}\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite panel.}
The finite object considered here is the human $B^*_{Q6}$ residual panel projected against tissue-specific draft proteome readouts before any structural, functional, phenotypic, or mechanistic interpretation is made. The row universe is $\mathrm{Homo\ sapiens}$ coding sequence data joined to Kim et al. 2014 SEQUEST adult tissue PAXdb abundance files. The response coordinate is $\log_{10}(\mathrm{abundance\_ppm})$, and the tested residual coordinates are
$$
\begin{aligned}
&K\_AAA,\ Arg\_AGR,\ Ile\_AUA,\ Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR,\ Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG,\\
&Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY,\ Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG,\ Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY,\ f3\_stress.
\end{aligned}
$$
For each tissue, the residual regression uses an intercept, $\log(\mathrm{cds\_len\_nt})$, the twenty amino-acid composition proportions $A,C,D,E,F,G,H,I,K,L,M,N,P,Q,R,S,T,V,W,Y$, real-CDS $GC3$, and an $M$-density baseline given by the fraction of sense codons on the union support of the nine projected $B^*_{Q6}$ vectors. Each tissue has 24 control columns, $\operatorname{rank}(Z)=23$, and $\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde X)=9$. Thus the numerical object is a controlled protein-abundance projection surface $S^{QP}$, not a ribosome-efficiency, translation-mechanism, folding, physical-admissibility, functional, or universal-law claim.

\paragraph{Dominant residual coordinates.}
The dominant coordinate varies by tissue rather than collapsing to a single global residual. Adult\_Bcells, Adult\_CD8Tcells, Adult\_Colon, Adult\_Kidney, Adult\_NKcells, Adult\_Pancreas, and Adult\_Retina are Arg\_AGR-dominant. Adult\_CD4Tcells, Adult\_Frontalcortex, Adult\_Liver, Adult\_Lung, and Adult\_Monocytes are Ile\_AUA-dominant. Adult\_Heart is Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY-dominant. This dominant-coordinate split is the strongest local reason to keep the record at tissue-dependent descriptive scope: the finite surface does not identify one universal $B^*_{Q6}$ protein-output direction.

\paragraph{Functional-class prior test.}
A cosine matrix was computed for the thirteen tissue coefficient surfaces, producing 78 tissue pairs. The manually assigned prior classes were immune, metabolic, neural, and epithelial-or-other. There were 17 within-class pairs and 61 between-class pairs. The observed within-class mean cosine was $0.9015393659102923$, the observed between-class mean cosine was $0.8936124027169124$, and the observed within-minus-between difference was
$$
\begin{aligned}
0.9015393659102923-0.8936124027169124=0.007926963193379821.
\end{aligned}
$$
A class-size-preserving label-shuffle test with 1000 permutations gave one-sided $p=0.3006993006993007$ for within-class similarity exceeding the shuffle null. The null differences ranged from $-0.043787962870935226$ to $0.08858262592759103$, with null mean $0.0007739569961830541$. Therefore the finite readout does not support a functional-class-structured interpretation. The proper conclusion is idiosyncratic or not functional-class structured at this resolution: the tissue surfaces are highly similar in cosine scale, but the small class-prior separation is not exceptional under the stated permutation test.

\paragraph{Relation to modeled translation weights.}
A related rank-powered organism sweep computed modeled tAI-style $S^{QT}$ surfaces for 41 organisms, reaching the target of 40 organisms. Its $R^2_{QT}$ distribution had minimum $0.1579271837024072$, first quartile $0.26353954700074694$, median $0.33934845811598124$, mean $0.38466555440501393$, third quartile $0.5143951545826744$, and maximum $0.8213457220900503$, using 1000 permutations and the same nine residual coordinates with 24 controls. That modeled-readout result supplies a bounded comparison surface for codon-adaptation weights, not observed ribosome profiling. The present tissue chapter uses PAXdb protein-abundance readouts instead. Consequently the two finite panels can be placed side by side as modeled translation-weight and draft-proteome projections, but they cannot be fused into a measured modeled-to-ribosome calibration without same-organism, same-CDS rows joining codon counts, modeled tAI or stAI weights, ribosome-profiling RPF values, and matched mRNA abundance.

\paragraph{Local boundary.}
This record deliberately stops at controlled residual agreement between $B^*_{Q6}$ coordinates and a tissue-specific protein-abundance surface. It does not claim that $B^*_{Q6}$ residual geometry is curated biological observation, that tAI or stAI weights are measured ribosome occupancy, or that PAXdb abundance is a translation-efficiency denominator. It also does not claim protein folding, structural order, physical admissibility, molecular function, localization, stability, phenotype, mechanism, adaptation, causation, or universality. Any such higher-layer assertion would require a separate layer-matched contact and its own controlled statistic. For the present finite panel, the allowed statement is narrower: across thirteen adult human draft proteome tissues, the nine $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinates retain small controlled protein-abundance projections, with tissue-specific dominant coordinates and no significant support for the supplied functional-class grouping.
