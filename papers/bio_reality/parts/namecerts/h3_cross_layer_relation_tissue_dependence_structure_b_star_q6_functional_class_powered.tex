\subsection{Finite tissue readout for the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual panel}
\label{subsec:bioreality-q6-tissue-dependence-structure}

\paragraph{Finite carrier.}
This record uses a finite human tissue panel to ask whether the nine projected $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates retain a controlled relation to a tissue-specific protein-abundance readout. The organism is Homo sapiens, and the readout is $\log_{10}(\mathrm{abundance\_ppm})$ from the Kim et al. 2014 SEQUEST adult tissue PAXdb files. The joined tissue rows are Adult B cells, Adult CD4 T cells, Adult CD8 T cells, Adult NK cells, Adult monocytes, adult liver, kidney, pancreas, frontal cortex, retina, colon, lung, and heart. The coordinate list is
$$
\begin{aligned}
&K\_AAA,\ \mathrm{Arg}\_AGR,\ \mathrm{Ile}\_AUA,\ \mathrm{Leu}\_{CUN\,vs\,UUR},\ \mathrm{Leu}\_{UUA\,vs\,UUG},\\
&\mathrm{Ser}\_{UCR\,vs\,AGY},\ \mathrm{Ser}\_{UCA\,vs\,UCG},\ \mathrm{Thr}\_{ACR\,vs\,ACY},\ f_{3}\text{-stress}.
\end{aligned}
$$
Each tissue fit uses the same control design: an intercept, $\log(\mathrm{cds\_len\_nt})$, twenty amino-acid composition proportions computed from real coding-sequence codon counts, GC3, and the $M$-density baseline given by the fraction of sense codons on the union support of the nine projected residual vectors. The reported control-column count is $24$ for every tissue, while the effective control rank is $23$ and the residualized $Q6$ design has rank $9$ in every tissue. Thus the finite readout is a controlled residual association panel, not an unrestricted protein-level interpretation.

This pattern supports the narrow statement that the controlled projection is tissue-dependent and coordinate-dependent in this finite panel.

These values make the readout a controlled descriptive surface over observed protein-abundance files, rather than a claim about translation dynamics or molecular mechanism.

\paragraph{Functional-class comparison.}
The tissue vectors were compared through a cosine matrix with $13$ tissues and $78$ tissue pairs. The prior tissue labels were immune, metabolic, neural, and epithelial-or-other; these labels are an imposed grouping, not a clustering result. The within-class mean cosine is $0.9015393659$, the between-class mean cosine is $0.8936124027$, and the observed difference is
$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta_{\mathrm{within-between}} = 0.9015393659 - 0.8936124027 = 0.0079269632.
\end{aligned}
$$
A class-size-preserving label-shuffle test with $1000$ permutations gives one-sided $p=0.3006993007$ for within-class cosine being greater than between-class cosine. The permutation envelope includes a null minimum $-0.0437879629$, null mean $0.0007739570$, and null maximum $0.0885826259$. Therefore the finite record does not support a functional-class structure call. Its appropriate classification is idiosyncratic, or at least not resolved by the supplied functional-class prior.

\paragraph{Relation to modeled translation readouts.}
The broader rank-powered organism sweep supplies useful context but not a replacement for the tissue experiment. In that sweep, modeled tAI was computed for $41$ organisms, reaching the target of $40$ organisms. The $S^{QT}$ distribution over organisms has mean $0.3846655544$, median $0.3393484581$, lower quartile $0.2635395470$, upper quartile $0.5143951546$, minimum $0.1579271837$, and maximum $0.8213457221$, using $1000$ permutations. Those numbers describe a modeled codon-adaptation readout surface over organism rows. They do not supply the ribosome-profiling or matched mRNA rows needed for measured translation-efficiency calibration, and they do not convert the tissue protein-abundance projection into an observed translation-survival result.

\paragraph{Local scope boundary.}
This chapter records a finite controlled association between $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates and a human tissue protein-abundance readout. It does not establish measured translation efficiency, ribosome occupancy, protein folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, system phenotype, mechanism, adaptation, causation, or universality. A modeled tAI or stAI surface is a bounded codon-adaptation readout, not a ribosome-profiling measurement. A PAXdb abundance file is a protein-abundance surface, not a folding assay, perturbation assay, functional assay, phenotype assay, or global biological law. Any promotion beyond the finite $S^{QP}$ tissue projection would require a separate layer-matched contact and a new controlled statistic on joined rows.

\paragraph{Finite verdict.}
The finite evidence is ready for a descriptive BioReality statement: in thirteen adult human tissue proteome files, the nine-coordinate $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual panel has small controlled $S^{QP}$ values, full residualized coordinate rank, nondegenerate row counts, and tissue-specific dominant coordinates. The strongest tissue in this panel is retina with $R^{2}_{QP}=0.0480573011$ and $n=10373$; the weakest is kidney with $R^{2}_{QP}=0.0184999033$ and $n=6726$. The functional-class prior is not supported at the declared threshold, since the within-minus-between cosine difference is only $0.0079269632$ with one-sided permutation $p=0.3006993007$. The correct finite conclusion is therefore tissue-dependent descriptive structure without functional-class promotion and without downstream biological promotion.
