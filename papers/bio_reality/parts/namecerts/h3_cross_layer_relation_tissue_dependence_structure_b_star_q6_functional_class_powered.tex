\subsection{Rank-Powered Tissue Dependence of $B^*_{Q6}$ Residual Coordinates}\label{subsec:bioreality-q6-tissue-dependence-functional-class}\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite panel.}
The finite object studied here is a human coding-sequence panel joined to tissue-specific draft proteome readouts from the Kim et al. 2014 SEQUEST PAXdb adult tissue files.  The response variable is $\log_{10}(\mathrm{abundance\_ppm})$, and the coordinate family is the nine-dimensional $B^*_{Q6}$ residual basis after amino-acid quotienting.  The nine coordinates are $K\_AAA$, $Arg\_AGR$, $Ile\_AUA$, $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$, $Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG$, $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$, $Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG$, $Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY$, and $f3\_stress$.  Each tissue fit used the same control design: an intercept, $\log(\mathrm{cds\_len\_nt})$, twenty amino-acid composition proportions computed from real CDS codon counts, GC3, and the $M$-density baseline defined as the fraction of sense codons lying on the union support of the nine projected $B^*_{Q6}$ vectors.  This gives $24$ control columns in each loaded tissue, with control rank $23$ and residualized $B^*_{Q6}$ rank $9$ in every reported tissue.

\paragraph{Observed tissue rows.}
Thirteen adult human tissues were joined without skipped tissue files: B cells, CD4 T cells, CD8 T cells, NK cells, monocytes, liver, kidney, pancreas, frontal cortex, retina, colon, lung, and heart.  The usable joined row counts were $9244$, $7604$, $10914$, $8787$, $5179$, $8664$, $6726$, $9695$, $9301$, $10373$, $7948$, $6577$, and $6468$, respectively.  Thus the smallest fit was Adult Monocytes with $5179$ joined coding-sequence rows, and the largest was Adult CD8 T cells with $10914$ joined rows.  The statistic is not a protein-function or mechanism claim; it is a controlled residual association between synonymous residual coordinates and a tissue proteome abundance readout.

\paragraph{Per-tissue residual power.}
The fitted $S^{QP}$ surface was weak but nonzero across all thirteen tissue readouts.  The observed $R^2_{QP}$ values were $0.036261$ for Adult B cells, $0.033533$ for Adult CD4 T cells, $0.039285$ for Adult CD8 T cells, $0.027094$ for Adult NK cells, $0.021350$ for Adult Monocytes, $0.026133$ for Adult Liver, $0.018500$ for Adult Kidney, $0.027616$ for Adult Pancreas, $0.025875$ for Adult Frontal cortex, $0.048057$ for Adult Retina, $0.023733$ for Adult Colon, $0.023274$ for Adult Lung, and $0.023209$ for Adult Heart.  The largest value in this panel is the retina value $0.048057$, and the smallest is the kidney value $0.018500$.  In all tissues the fit is far below the declared degeneracy threshold $0.999999$, so the row does not collapse into a saturated all-explaining surface.

\paragraph{Dominant-coordinate pattern.}
The leading coordinate is not tissue-universal.  $Arg\_AGR$ dominates Adult B cells, Adult CD8 T cells, Adult NK cells, Adult Kidney, Adult Pancreas, Adult Retina, and Adult Colon.  $Ile\_AUA$ dominates Adult CD4 T cells, Adult Monocytes, Adult Liver, Adult Frontal cortex, and Adult Lung.  $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$ dominates Adult Heart.  This gives a finite categorical readback with seven $Arg\_AGR$ leading tissues, five $Ile\_AUA$ leading tissues, and one $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$ leading tissue.  The pattern is compatible with idiosyncratic tissue dependence, but it is not strong evidence that the manually assigned immune, metabolic, neural, and epithelial-or-other classes organize the residual vectors.

\paragraph{Class-label test.}
A cosine matrix over the thirteen tissue-specific $S^{QP}$ coefficient profiles produced $78$ tissue pairs.  The functional-class comparison used $17$ within-class pairs and $61$ between-class pairs, preserving class sizes under $1000$ deterministic permutations.  The observed within-class mean cosine was $0.9015393659$, while the observed between-class mean cosine was $0.8936124027$, giving
$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta_{\mathrm{within-between}} &= 0.9015393659 - 0.8936124027 \\
&= 0.0079269632 .
\end{aligned}
$$
The permutation null had mean $0.0007739570$, minimum observed null delta $-0.0437879629$, and maximum observed null delta $0.0885826259$.  The one-sided class-label $p$-value for within-class similarity greater than the label-shuffle null was $0.3006993007$.  Consequently this finite panel supports the descriptive conclusion that the $B^*_{Q6}$ tissue profiles are present but not cleanly organized by the supplied functional-class labels.

\paragraph{Relation to modeled translation readouts.}
A separate rank-powered organism sweep computed modeled tAI readouts for $41$ organisms, reaching the target of $40$ organisms.  In that sweep the $R^2_{QT}$ distribution over organisms had minimum $0.1579271837$, first quartile $0.2635395470$, median $0.3393484581$, mean $0.3846655544$, third quartile $0.5143951546$, and maximum $0.8213457221$, with $1000$ permutations.  This confirms that the same residual coordinate family can be paired with modeled codon-adaptation readouts at organism scale.  It does not convert the human PAXdb tissue abundance result into ribosome occupancy, translation efficiency, or a measured tAI-to-ribo-seq calibration.

\paragraph{Local boundary.}
The chapter records a finite cross-layer relation between $B^*_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates and tissue-specific draft human proteome abundance readouts under amino-acid composition, length, GC3, and $M$-density controls.  It does not establish translation realization, ribosome dynamics, protein folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, tissue mechanism, phenotype, or universality.  Modeled tAI or stAI weights remain modeled codon-adaptation readouts, and PAXdb abundance remains a proteome abundance readout from one study.  A separate matched contact would be required before claiming measured translation efficiency: same-organism and same-CDS codon rows, ribosome-profiling RPF rows, matched mRNA rows, and an explicit controlled statistic for $TE = RPF/mRNA$.  Until such rows are joined, disagreement or agreement between modeled adaptation and observed ribosome profiling must remain a translation-readout calibration target rather than a protein-level interpretation.
