\subsection{B$^*_{Q6}$ Tissue Dependence at a Protein-Readout Boundary}
\label{sec:bior-reality-b-star-q6-tissue-dependence-protein-readout}

\paragraph{Finite panel.}
This record evaluates a finite human tissue panel in which nine $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinates are projected against tissue-specific protein-abundance readouts.  The coordinates are $K\_AAA$, $Arg\_AGR$, $Ile\_AUA$, $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$, $Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG$, $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$, $Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG$, $Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY$, and $f3\_stress$.  The response is $\log_{10}(abundance\_ppm)$ from the human Kim et al. 2014 SEQUEST adult tissue PAXdb files, treated here only as a draft tissue-specific proteome readout.  Each tissue fit uses the same covariate design: an intercept, $\log(cds\_len\_nt)$, twenty amino-acid composition proportions computed from real CDS codon counts, GC3, and an $M$-density baseline defined by the fraction of sense codons lying on the union support of the nine projected $B^*_{Q6}$ vectors.  The declared control column count is $24$ for every loaded tissue, with rank $23$ for the control block and rank $9$ for the residual-coordinate block after control removal.

\paragraph{Per-tissue readback.}
The panel contains $13$ adult tissues and $78$ pairwise tissue cosines.  The usable joined row counts are $9244$ for B cells, $7604$ for CD4 T cells, $10914$ for CD8 T cells, $8787$ for NK cells, $5179$ for monocytes, $8664$ for liver, $6726$ for kidney, $9695$ for pancreas, $9301$ for frontal cortex, $10373$ for retina, $7948$ for colon, $6577$ for lung, and $6468$ for heart. \paragraph{Dominant residual coordinates.}
The leading coordinate is not uniform across tissues.  $Arg\_AGR$ is dominant in B cells, CD8 T cells, colon, kidney, NK cells, pancreas, and retina.  $Ile\_AUA$ is dominant in CD4 T cells, frontal cortex, liver, lung, and monocytes.  $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$ is dominant only in heart.  This distribution is the main finite-row reason to record the result as idiosyncratic rather than as a stable functional-class law.  The tissue labels used as prior classes are immune for B cells, CD4 T cells, CD8 T cells, monocytes, and NK cells; metabolic for liver, kidney, and pancreas; neural for frontal cortex and retina; and epithelial-or-other for colon, lung, and heart.

\paragraph{Functional-class permutation check.}
The functional-class comparison tests whether tissues with the same prior class have more similar $S^{QP}$ residual profiles than tissues with different prior classes.  There are $17$ within-class pairs and $61$ between-class pairs.  The observed within-class mean cosine is $0.9015393659102923$, the observed between-class mean cosine is $0.8936124027169124$, and the observed within-minus-between contrast is $0.007926963193379821$.  A class-size-preserving label permutation with $1000$ permutations gives one-sided $p=0.3006993006993007$ for within-class greater-than-between-class similarity.  The null distribution has mean $0.0007739569961830541$, minimum observed contrast $-0.043787962870935226$, and maximum observed contrast $0.08858262592759103$.  Thus the finite panel does not support a class-structured tissue law at the chosen descriptive boundary; the recorded conclusion is a weak, tissue-specific protein-readout association surface with no persuasive functional-class organization.

\paragraph{Relation to modeled translation readouts.}
A separate rank-powered modeled-readout sweep supplies context but not promotion.  In that sweep, $41$ organisms were evaluated against modeled tAI-style readouts with $1000$ permutations, using the same nine residual coordinates and $24$ control columns.  The reported $R^2_{QT}$ distribution has minimum $0.1579271837024072$, first quartile $0.26353954700074694$, median $0.33934845811598124$, mean $0.38466555440501393$, third quartile $0.5143951545826744$, and maximum $0.8213457220900503$.  Those values are modeled codon-adaptation readbacks, not measured ribosome occupancy.  They may motivate a later same-CDS calibration against ribosome profiling, but they do not turn the present protein-abundance tissue panel into a translation-efficiency result.

\paragraph{Local boundary.}
The finite observation is therefore narrow.  It says that, after amino-acid composition, CDS length, GC3, and $M$-density controls, the nine $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinates retain small tissue-dependent associations with a Kim 2014 PAXdb abundance readout, and that the resulting tissue profile is not convincingly organized by the supplied immune, metabolic, neural, and epithelial-or-other labels.  It does not establish measured translation efficiency, ribosome occupancy, protein folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, system phenotype, causal mechanism, or a universal biological law.  Any such assertion would require an additional layer-matched dataset and a statistic computed on joined rows at that layer.  In particular, an observed translation claim would require same-organism and same-CDS codon counts, ribosome-profiling RPF rows, matched mRNA abundance rows, and a controlled translation-efficiency statistic such as $TE=RPF/mRNA$ before any modeled-to-observed calibration could be reported.
