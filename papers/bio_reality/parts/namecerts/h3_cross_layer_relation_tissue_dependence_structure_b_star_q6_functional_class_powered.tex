\subsection{Tissue-Resolved $B^{*}_{Q6}$ Residual Protein-Readout Geometry}
\label{sec:bioreality-q6-tissue-dependence-functional-class}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite panel and readout.}
This record fixes a finite human tissue panel for the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates after amino-acid quotienting. The tested rows are coding sequences joined to tissue-specific draft proteome measurements from the Kim 2014 SEQUEST adult-tissue PAXdb files for \emph{Homo sapiens}. The response is $\log_{10}(\mathrm{abundance\_ppm})$, not ribosome occupancy and not translation efficiency. The residual coordinate block has nine coordinates,
$$
\begin{aligned}
Q=\{&K\_{AAA},\ Arg\_{AGR},\ Ile\_{AUA},\ Leu\_{CUN\,vs\,UUR},\ Leu\_{UUA\,vs\,UUG},\\
&Ser\_{UCR\,vs\,AGY},\ Ser\_{UCA\,vs\,UCG},\ Thr\_{ACR\,vs\,ACY},\ f3\_{stress}\},
\end{aligned}
$$
and each tissue fit uses the same control design: intercept, $\log(\mathrm{cds\_len\_nt})$, the twenty amino-acid composition proportions $A,C,D,E,F,G,H,I,K,L,M,N,P,Q,R,S,T,V,W,Y$, GC3 from the real CDS codon counts, and an $M$-density baseline defined as the fraction of sense codons on the union support of the nine projected $B^{*}_{Q6}$ vectors. The reported control-column count is $24$ in every tissue. The fitted residual rank is nondegenerate in the recorded runs: $\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde X)=9$ for the $Q$ block and $\operatorname{rank}(Z)=23$ for the controls in all thirteen tissues.

\paragraph{Per-tissue finite rows.}
Thirteen tissue rows are present, with no skipped tissue in the loaded panel. The joined row counts are $9244$ for Adult B cells, $7604$ for Adult CD4 T cells, $10914$ for Adult CD8 T cells, $8787$ for Adult NK cells, $5179$ for Adult monocytes, $8664$ for Adult liver, $6726$ for Adult kidney, $9695$ for Adult pancreas, $9301$ for Adult frontal cortex, $10373$ for Adult retina, $7948$ for Adult colon, $6577$ for Adult lung, and $6468$ for Adult heart. The controlled $S^{QP}$ protein-readout projections are small but finite: Adult retina has the largest recorded $R^{2}_{QP}=0.04805730109298326$, followed by Adult CD8 T cells at $0.03928541767885904$, Adult B cells at $0.03626117434944674$, Adult CD4 T cells at $0.033532527035959644$, Adult pancreas at $0.027616177587166925$, Adult NK cells at $0.02709444741593442$, Adult liver at $0.026133456228248052$, Adult frontal cortex at $0.025874798845290918$, Adult colon at $0.023732563331619574$, Adult lung at $0.0232738640400933$, Adult heart at $0.02320896321347296$, Adult monocytes at $0.021350299623075428$, and Adult kidney at $0.018499903301809693$. Thus the observed protein-readout surface is neither an all-zero refusal row nor a saturated all-one matrix; it is a low-amplitude descriptive tissue surface.

\paragraph{Dominant-coordinate pattern.}
The dominant residual coordinate is tissue-dependent but sparse in its winners. $Arg\_{AGR}$ is dominant in Adult B cells, Adult CD8 T cells, Adult colon, Adult kidney, Adult NK cells, Adult pancreas, and Adult retina. $Ile\_{AUA}$ is dominant in Adult CD4 T cells, Adult frontal cortex, Adult liver, Adult lung, and Adult monocytes. $Ser\_{UCR\,vs\,AGY}$ is dominant only in Adult heart. No other coordinate is the recorded dominant coordinate in this finite run. This supports only a coordinate-level descriptive readout: the data say that different adult tissue protein-abundance panels place their largest controlled residual projection on different members of $Q$; they do not say that the winning coordinate is a causal translation mechanism.

\paragraph{Functional-class label test.}
The finite cosine matrix contains $13$ tissues and $78$ tissue pairs. Using the prior labels immune, metabolic, neural, and epithelial-or-other, the within-class mean cosine is $0.9015393659102923$ and the between-class mean cosine is $0.8936124027169124$, giving
$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta_{within-between}
  &=0.9015393659102923-0.8936124027169124\\
  &=0.007926963193379821.
\end{aligned}
$$
The label-preserving finite test has $17$ within-class pairs, $61$ between-class pairs, $1000$ permutations, and one-sided $p=0.3006993006993007$ for within-class cosine being larger than the class-size-preserving label-shuffle null. The null deltas range from $-0.043787962870935226$ to $0.08858262592759103$, with null mean $0.0007739569961830541$. The observed positive delta is therefore a weak descriptive offset, not a statistically persuasive functional-class structure in this panel.

\paragraph{Relation to modeled translation readouts.}
A linked rank-powered organism sweep gives a bounded comparison point for modeled codon-adaptation readouts. In that run, $41$ organisms were swept, the target of $40$ organisms was reached, $1000$ permutations were used per organism, and the modeled $S^{QT}$ distribution over organisms had $R^{2}_{QT}$ minimum $0.1579271837024072$, first quartile $0.26353954700074694$, median $0.33934845811598124$, mean $0.38466555440501393$, third quartile $0.5143951545826744$, and maximum $0.8213457220900503$. This comparison is deliberately bounded: the modeled tAI or stAI surface is a codon-adaptation readout, whereas the tissue run here is a human protein-abundance projection. The two finite facts motivate a calibration question between modeled translation weights, observed ribosome-profiling translation efficiency, and downstream protein abundance, but the present tissue rows do not themselves supply ribosome-profiling or matched mRNA-controlled translation-efficiency measurements.

\paragraph{Local claim boundary.}
The finite conclusion is a narrow one. The human adult-tissue panel records a controlled $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual association with $\log_{10}(\mathrm{abundance\_ppm})$ across thirteen PAXdb tissue readouts, with small $R^{2}_{QP}$ values and a non-significant functional-class cosine test. It does not establish translation realization, ribosome occupancy, translation efficiency, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, system phenotype, mechanism, or universality. A translation claim would require same-organism and same-CDS ribosome-profiling or translation-efficiency rows, matched mRNA abundance rows, and the declared controls before any $Q_i\to T_j$ survival statement is made. A protein-mechanism claim would then require a separate layer-matched contact beyond these abundance rows. In this chapter the recorded object is only the finite, controlled, tissue-specific protein-readout geometry of the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates.
