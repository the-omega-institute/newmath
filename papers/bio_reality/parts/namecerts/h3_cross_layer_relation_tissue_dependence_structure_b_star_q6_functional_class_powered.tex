\subsection{Powered tissue dependence of $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates}
\label{sec:bioreality-q6-tissue-dependence-functional-class-powered}

\paragraph{Finite panel.}
The record tests whether the nine projected $B^{*}_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates leave a reproducible tissue-indexed trace in a human protein-abundance readout after controlling for elementary sequence composition.  The carrier is the finite panel of human coding sequences joined to the Kim et al. 2014 SEQUEST adult-tissue PAXdb files, with the response variable $\log_{10}(\mathrm{abundance\_ppm})$.  Thirteen adult tissues were loaded: B cells, CD4 T cells, CD8 T cells, NK cells, monocytes, liver, kidney, pancreas, frontal cortex, retina, colon, lung, and heart.  The usable joined row counts were $9244$, $7604$, $10914$, $8787$, $5179$, $8664$, $6726$, $9695$, $9301$, $10373$, $7948$, $6577$, and $6468$, respectively.  No tissue in the declared panel was skipped.

\paragraph{Residual coordinates and controls.}
The tested residual basis consists of $K\_AAA$, $Arg\_AGR$, $Ile\_AUA$, $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$, $Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG$, $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$, $Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG$, $Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY$, and $f3\_stress$.  In every tissue the design used twenty-four control columns: an intercept, $\log(\mathrm{cds\_len\_nt})$, twenty amino-acid composition proportions computed from real CDS codon counts, GC3, and the $M$-density baseline formed from the union support of the nine projected $B^{*}_{Q6}$ vectors.  The residualized coordinate block retained rank $9$ in all tissues, while the control block had rank $23$ in all tissues.  Thus the finite row is not the degenerate $n=3$ surface: the residual degrees of freedom after controls ranged from $5156$ in monocytes to $10891$ in CD8 T cells.

The dominant residual coordinate was $Arg\_AGR$ in B cells, CD8 T cells, colon, kidney, NK cells, pancreas, and retina; $Ile\_AUA$ in CD4 T cells, frontal cortex, liver, lung, and monocytes; and $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$ in heart.  The largest explained-energy entry was $491.7590959575$ in CD8 T cells, while the smallest was $57.0282547084$ in monocytes.

\paragraph{Functional-class label check.}
The panel also compared similarity of tissue coefficient profiles within and between the declared functional-class priors immune, metabolic, neural, and epithelial-or-other.  The cosine matrix had $78$ tissue pairs across $13$ tissues.  The observed within-class mean cosine was $0.9015393659$, the observed between-class mean cosine was $0.8936124027$, and the observed within-minus-between contrast was
$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta_{\mathrm{within-between}} &= 0.9015393659 - 0.8936124027 \\
&= 0.0079269632.
\end{aligned}
$$
A class-size-preserving permutation test with $1000$ permutations gave one-sided $p=0.3006993007$ for within-class similarity exceeding the label-shuffle null.  The null distribution had mean $0.0007739570$, minimum $-0.0437879629$, and maximum $0.0885826259$.  This is therefore a weak descriptive tissue-shape observation rather than evidence for a functional-class law: the direction is positive, but the finite-row label test does not separate the declared tissue classes at conventional evidential strength.

\paragraph{Modeled translation contrast.}
A separate rank-powered modeled tAI sweep supplies a useful boundary for interpretation.  That sweep used $41$ organisms, reached the target of $40$, and computed $S^{QT}$-style modeled translation-adaptation projections with $1000$ permutations.  Its $R^2_{QT}$ distribution had minimum $0.1579271837$, first quartile $0.2635395470$, median $0.3393484581$, mean $0.3846655544$, third quartile $0.5143951546$, and maximum $0.8213457221$.  These values are larger than the human tissue protein-abundance $R^2_{QP}$ range reported here, but the two panels do not measure the same layer.  Modeled tAI or stAI weights are adaptation annotations, whereas the tissue panel uses steady-state draft proteome abundance; neither panel by itself supplies measured ribosome occupancy, translation efficiency, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, or phenotype.

\paragraph{Local conclusion.}
The finite result supports the following bounded statement.  In the declared human tissue panel, the nine $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates have small but computable controlled projections onto tissue-specific draft protein abundance, with $R^2_{QP}$ between $0.0184999033$ and $0.0480573011$ after length, amino-acid composition, GC3, and $M$-density controls.  The coefficient-shape comparison across functional-class priors is positive but not decisive under the $1000$-permutation label null.  The chapter therefore records an idiosyncratic or weakly organized tissue-abundance pattern, not a causal tissue program.

\paragraph{Boundary.}
No translation claim follows from this chapter, because the readout is not ribosome profiling and the panel is not mRNA-controlled.  No folding, physical admissibility, biological function, mechanism, phenotype, or universal rule follows from the controlled $S^{QP}$ values.  Those interpretations would require their own layer-matched measurements and a new controlled statistic on joined rows.  The present record is limited to the finite association between $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates and human adult-tissue draft proteome abundance under the stated controls.
