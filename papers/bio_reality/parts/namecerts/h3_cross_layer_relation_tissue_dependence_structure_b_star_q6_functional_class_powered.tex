\subsection{Tissue dependence of the $B^*_Q6$ protein-readout projection}
\label{sec:bioreality-b-star-q6-tissue-dependence-structure}

\paragraph{Finite panel.}
This record concerns a human coding-sequence panel in which nine $B^*_Q6$ residual coordinates were evaluated against tissue-specific draft proteome readouts.  The coordinates were
$K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $Leu_{CUN\_vs\_UUR}$, $Leu_{UUA\_vs\_UUG}$, $Ser_{UCR\_vs\_AGY}$, $Ser_{UCA\_vs\_UCG}$, $Thr_{ACR\_vs\_ACY}$, and $f_3\_stress$.  The observed readout was $\log_{10}(\mathrm{abundance\_ppm})$ from Homo sapiens adult tissue PAXdb files attributed to the Kim 2014 SEQUEST draft proteome collection.  The finite tissue set contained thirteen rows at the tissue level: Adult B cells, Adult CD4 T cells, Adult CD8 T cells, Adult NK cells, Adult Monocytes, Adult Liver, Adult Kidney, Adult Pancreas, Adult Frontal cortex, Adult Retina, Adult Colon, Adult Lung, and Adult Heart.  The joined coding-sequence counts ranged from $5179$ in Adult Monocytes to $10914$ in Adult CD8 T cells, with the other counts equal to $9244$, $7604$, $7948$, $9301$, $6468$, $6726$, $8664$, $6577$, $8787$, $9695$, and $10373$ for the listed tissues.

\paragraph{Controlled residual statistic.}
For each tissue, the protein-readout projection was estimated after residualizing against a fixed control block containing an intercept, $\log(\mathrm{cds\_len\_nt})$, twenty amino-acid composition proportions computed from real CDS codon counts, GC3, and the $M$-density baseline defined by the fraction of sense codons on the union support of the nine projected $B^*_Q6$ vectors.  The reported control block had $24$ columns in every tissue, while the realized control rank was $23$; after this residualization the nine $q$ coordinates retained rank $9$ in every tissue.  Thus the finite computation was not an all-fit rank-saturation surface.  The tissue-specific residual degrees of freedom after controls were $9221$, $7581$, $10891$, $7925$, $9278$, $6445$, $6703$, $8641$, $6554$, $5156$, $8764$, $9672$, and $10350$.

\paragraph{Observed protein-readout strengths.}
The controlled $S^{QP}$ values were small but nonzero across the thirteen tissues.  Adult Retina had the largest reported value, $R^2_{QP}=0.04805730109298326$, followed by Adult CD8 T cells at $0.03928541767885904$, Adult B cells at $0.03626117434944674$, Adult CD4 T cells at $0.033532527035959644$, Adult Pancreas at $0.027616177587166925$, Adult NK cells at $0.02709444741593442$, Adult Liver at $0.026133456228248052$, Adult Frontal cortex at $0.025874798845290918$, Adult Colon at $0.023732563331619574$, Adult Lung at $0.0232738640400933$, Adult Heart at $0.02320896321347296$, Adult Monocytes at $0.021350299623075428$, and Adult Kidney at $0.018499903301809693$.  The dominant residual coordinate was $Arg_{AGR}$ in Adult B cells, Adult CD8 T cells, Adult Colon, Adult Kidney, Adult NK cells, Adult Pancreas, and Adult Retina; it was $Ile_{AUA}$ in Adult CD4 T cells, Adult Frontal cortex, Adult Liver, Adult Lung, and Adult Monocytes; it was $Ser_{UCR\_vs\_AGY}$ in Adult Heart.  In compact form, the observed range was
$$
\begin{aligned}
0.018499903301809693 \leq R^2_{QP} \leq 0.04805730109298326 .
\end{aligned}
$$
This is a descriptive protein-abundance readout, not a ribosome-occupancy or translation-efficiency measurement.

\paragraph{Functional-class comparison.}
The tissue-level cosine matrix contained $13$ tissues and $78$ tissue pairs.  The prespecified class labels were immune, metabolic, neural, and epithelial-or-other.  The within-class comparison used $17$ pairs, the between-class comparison used $61$ pairs, and the label-shuffle null preserved class sizes across $1000$ permutations using the recorded deterministic seed.  The observed within-class mean cosine was $0.9015393659102923$, the observed between-class mean cosine was $0.8936124027169124$, and the observed within-minus-between contrast was $0.007926963193379821$.  The permutation null had mean $0.0007739569961830541$, minimum $-0.043787962870935226$, and maximum $0.08858262592759103$, giving a one-sided within-greater $p$-value of $0.3006993006993007$.  Consequently, the finite rows support an idiosyncratic or weakly descriptive tissue structure, but they do not support a functional-class law.  The numerical contrast is positive, yet it sits comfortably inside the label-randomized range:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta_{obs} &= 0.9015393659102923 - 0.8936124027169124 \\
             &= 0.007926963193379821, \\
p_{within>between} &= 0.3006993006993007 .
\end{aligned}
$$

\paragraph{Relation to modeled translation readouts.}
A separate finite computation for the modeled tAI route swept $41$ organisms and reached the target of at least $40$ organisms.  It used the same nine residual coordinates and a $24$-column control block, and computed an organism-level $S^{QT}$ distribution with $1000$ permutations.  The modeled-tAI $R^2_{QT}$ distribution had minimum $0.1579271837024072$, first quartile $0.26353954700074694$, median $0.33934845811598124$, mean $0.38466555440501393$, third quartile $0.5143951545826744$, and maximum $0.8213457220900503$.  This companion result shows that the $B^*_Q6$ coordinates can be evaluated against a bounded codon-adaptation model across many organisms, but that model is not a measured ribosome-profiling survival matrix.  The present tissue result is instead a human draft-proteome projection $S^{QP}$, and the modeled organism sweep is retained only as a nearby translation-readout comparison.

\paragraph{Local boundary.}
The chapter therefore records a finite cross-layer relation between $B^*_Q6$ residual coordinates and human tissue draft-proteome abundance, with all claims restricted to the stated panel, controls, and readout.  It does not establish translation realization, ribosome occupancy, translation efficiency, protein folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, phenotype, mechanism, or universality.  A claim at any of those layers would require its own layer-matched contact: for translation, same-organism and same-CDS ribosome-profiling or translation-efficiency rows with matched mRNA abundance; for folding or physical admissibility, a structure or physical assay; for function or phenotype, a functional or perturbational assay.  The finite evidence here is a controlled descriptive projection from residual synonymous-coordinate geometry to a tissue-specific protein-abundance readout, and no stronger biological interpretation is licensed by these rows.
