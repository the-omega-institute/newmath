\subsection{Tissue dependence of the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ protein-readout projection}\label{subsec:b-star-q6-tissue-dependence-functional-class}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite panel.}
This record fixes a human tissue panel in which the residual coordinates $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ are evaluated against tissue-specific draft proteome readouts before any functional, mechanistic, or structural interpretation is allowed. The organism is Homo sapiens. The observed readout is $\log_{10}(\mathrm{abundance\_ppm})$ from the PAXdb 9606 adult tissue files attributed to the Kim 2014 SEQUEST draft proteome panel. The coordinate source is the same nine-coordinate $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual basis used by the powered translation-readout sweep:
$$
\begin{aligned}
Q=\{&\mathrm{K\_AAA},\mathrm{Arg\_AGR},\mathrm{Ile\_AUA},\mathrm{Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR},\mathrm{Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG},\\
&\mathrm{Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY},\mathrm{Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG},\mathrm{Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY},f_3\text{-}\mathrm{stress}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
For each tissue, the control block contains $24$ columns: an intercept, $\log(\mathrm{cds\_len\_nt})$, the $20$ amino-acid composition proportions $A,C,D,E,F,G,H,I,K,L,M,N,P,Q,R,S,T,V,W,Y$, GC3 computed from real CDS codon counts, and the $M$-density baseline given by the fraction of sense codons on the union support of the nine projected $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ vectors. The computation therefore asks a narrow finite-row question: after these controls, how much protein-abundance residual energy is captured by the nine synonymous residual coordinates in each tissue?

\paragraph{Per-tissue projection values.}
Thirteen adult tissues were loaded, with no skipped tissues. The usable joined row counts were $9244$ for B cells, $7604$ for CD4 T cells, $10914$ for CD8 T cells, $8787$ for NK cells, $5179$ for monocytes, $8664$ for liver, $6726$ for kidney, $9695$ for pancreas, $9301$ for frontal cortex, $10373$ for retina, $7948$ for colon, $6577$ for lung, and $6468$ for heart. In every tissue the residualized coordinate block has rank $9$, while the control block has rank $23$; the full control-column count is $24$ because the intercept is included before rank reduction. The computed $S^{QP}$ values, reported as controlled $R^2_{QP}$ against the protein-abundance readout, are

The largest controlled projection in this panel is retina, with $R^2_{QP}=0.04805730109298326$ over $10373$ joined rows and residual degrees of freedom $10350$ after $Z$. The smallest is kidney, with $R^2_{QP}=0.018499903301809693$ over $6726$ joined rows and residual degrees of freedom $6703$. These values are finite descriptive projections, not a statement that any residual coordinate causes abundance or mediates translation.

\paragraph{Dominant residual coordinates.}
The dominant coordinate is not constant across tissues. Arg\_AGR is dominant in B cells, CD8 T cells, colon, kidney, NK cells, pancreas, and retina. Ile\_AUA is dominant in CD4 T cells, frontal cortex, liver, lung, and monocytes. Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY is dominant only in heart. Thus the finite panel does not support a single coordinate-wide rule; it records tissue-specific residual directions under the stated controls. The corresponding explained and residual energies also remain tissue-local. For example, retina has explained energy $396.28840522457523$ against residual response energy $8246.16439566217$, CD8 T cells have explained energy $491.7590959574601$ against residual response energy $12517.598768514408$, and monocytes have explained energy $57.0282547084279$ against residual response energy $2671.075147198014$.

\paragraph{Functional-class label test.}
The functional-class labels used here are prior labels rather than discovered clusters. The classes are immune for B cells, CD4 T cells, CD8 T cells, NK cells, and monocytes; metabolic for liver, kidney, and pancreas; neural for frontal cortex and retina; and epithelial or other for colon, lung, and heart. A cosine matrix was computed on the thirteen tissue projection profiles, giving $78$ tissue pairs. Of these, $17$ are within-class pairs and $61$ are between-class pairs. The observed within-class mean cosine is $0.9015393659102923$, the observed between-class mean cosine is $0.8936124027169124$, and the observed within-minus-between contrast is
$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta_{\mathrm{class}}&=0.9015393659102923-0.8936124027169124\\
&=0.007926963193379821.
\end{aligned}
$$
A class-size-preserving label-shuffle test with $1000$ permutations gives one-sided $p=0.3006993006993007$ for within-class similarity exceeding the shuffled null. The shuffled contrast range seen in the run is from $-0.043787962870935226$ to $0.08858262592759103$, with null mean $0.0007739569961830541$. Therefore the finite evidence is idiosyncratic rather than functional-class structured: the tissue profiles are computable and nondegenerate, but the prior class labels do not produce a statistically compelling within-class excess in this panel.

\paragraph{Relation to modeled translation readouts.}
A separate powered organism sweep computed modeled tAI readouts across $41$ organisms and reached the target of $40$ organisms. In that sweep, the $S^{QT}$ distribution for modeled tAI has $R^2_{QT}$ minimum $0.1579271837024072$, first quartile $0.26353954700074694$, median $0.33934845811598124$, mean $0.38466555440501393$, third quartile $0.5143951545826744$, and maximum $0.8213457220900503$, with $1000$ permutations per organism. That modeled-readout result is useful as a bounded translation-layer comparison surface, but it is not an observed ribosome-profiling or translation-efficiency surface. The present tissue computation is also not ribosome profiling and is not mRNA controlled. It reads draft protein abundance, not RPF counts, not $\mathrm{TE}=\mathrm{RPF}/\mathrm{mRNA}$, and not a same-CDS modeled-to-observed calibration matrix.

\paragraph{Local boundary.}
The finite conclusion is exactly this: in a thirteen-tissue human draft proteome panel, the nine $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates have small but computable controlled projections onto $\log_{10}(\mathrm{abundance\_ppm})$, with $R^2_{QP}$ values between $0.018499903301809693$ and $0.04805730109298326$, and the prior functional-class grouping has a weak within-minus-between cosine contrast with $p=0.3006993006993007$. No translation-realization claim follows from this. No ribosome occupancy, translation efficiency, folding, physical admissibility, biological function, causal mechanism, phenotype, or universal biological law follows from these rows. Such claims would require their own layer-matched contact: for translation, same-organism and same-CDS codon counts, RPF rows, matched mRNA rows, and a declared controlled statistic; for protein mechanism or higher layers, separate protein-state, structure, function, perturbation, or phenotype measurements.
