\subsection{E. coli mRNA-stability readout for the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ codon-residual panel}\label{sec:bioreality-ecoli-mrna-stability-sqm-bstar-q6}\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite row and layer.}
This record tests whether the nine $B^{*}_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates retain statistical contact with an $E. coli$ mRNA-stability readout after the same CDS-derived covariates used in the protein-abundance survival analyses have been removed.  The biological row is $E. coli$ K-12 MG1655, and the recorded layer is \emph{cross-layer relation}: the statistic compares a codon-residual coordinate system to an mRNA half-life readout, without upgrading that association to translation, protein output, folding, physical admissibility, function, mechanism, or a universal biological rule.

\paragraph{Joined measurement table.}
The half-life contact is the Esquerré et al. 2015 mRNA half-life table at growth rate $0.40\,h^{-1}$, read as $\log_{10}$ mRNA half-life in minutes.  The join key identifies the Esquerré GeneId $b$-number with the $E. coli$ CDS locus tag.  The finite join reports $3739$ CDS rows, $2947$ valid half-life records, and $2720$ joined rows, with a CDS join hit rate of $0.9978649586335735$.  The skipped CDS accounting is explicit: $1019$ CDS records have no half-life match, while duplicate locus tags, missing locus tags, empty sense-codon counts, invalid lengths, non-object rows, and nonpositive abundances are all reported as $0$.  On the half-life side, duplicate GeneIds, missing GeneIds, missing growth-rate entries, non-object rows, and nonpositive half-lives are all reported as $0$.  A second growth-rate value, $0.10\,h^{-1}$, is available for robustness but is not the selected row in this computation.

\paragraph{Residual coordinates and controls.}
The tested design has $9$ codon-residual coordinates:
$K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $Leu_{CUN/UUR}$, $Leu_{UUA/UUG}$, $Ser_{UCR/AGY}$, $Ser_{UCA/UCG}$, $Thr_{ACR/ACY}$, and $f_{3}$-stress.  The control block $Z$ has $24$ columns and rank $23$.  It consists of an intercept, $\log(\mathrm{CDS\ length\ in\ nt})$, the $20$ amino-acid composition proportions $A,C,D,E,F,G,H,I,K,L,M,N,P,Q,R,S,T,V,W,Y$, real-CDS $GC3$, and the $M$-density baseline given by the fraction of sense codons lying in the union support of the nine projected $B^{*}_{Q6}$ vectors.  After removing these controls, the residual degrees of freedom are $2697$, and the residualized coordinate matrix has rank $9$.

\paragraph{Computed $S^{QM}$ statistic.}
The mRNA-stability surface for this row is summarized by
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^{2}_{QM} &= 0.02298556640056076,\\
\mathrm{explained\ energy}_{QM} &= 4.951800341206348,\\
\mathrm{residual\ half\mbox{-}life\ energy} &= 215.43086017169205.
\end{aligned}
$$
The permutation audit uses $1000$ permutations and reports right-tail probability
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{QM} &= 0.000999000999000999.
\end{aligned}
$$
The dominant $S^{QM}$ coordinate is $Arg_{AGR}$.  Thus, within this finite $E. coli$ row, the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual panel is not fully absorbed by amino-acid composition, CDS length, $GC3$, and the $M$-density baseline when the readout is residualized log half-life.

\paragraph{Same-join protein comparison.}
On the same joined support, the protein-abundance comparison reports
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^{2}_{QP} &= 0.11632311854858134,\\
\cos(S^{QM},S^{QP}) &= -0.7784923375569599.
\end{aligned}
$$
The dominant same-join $S^{QP}$ coordinate is also $Arg_{AGR}$, but the negative cosine records that the mRNA-stability vector and protein-abundance vector point in substantially different directions in the nine-coordinate residual space.  The scalar contrast is therefore not a single monotone codon-optimality story: the same named coordinate can dominate both readouts while the full nine-dimensional signatures oppose each other.

\paragraph{Yeast anchor used only as a descriptive contrast.}
The companion yeast mRNA-stability row gives $R^{2}_{QM}=0.05179429044975085$, $R^{2}_{QP}=0.2761140373962526$ on its same join, permutation probability $0.000999000999000999$, dominant mRNA-stability coordinate $Leu_{CUN/UUR}$, and cosine $\cos(S^{QM},S^{QP})=0.8040935468384264$.  This contrast is useful because the yeast row is positive-aligned while the $E. coli$ row is negative-aligned.  It is not used as a phylogenetic estimate, and it does not turn either organism-specific result into a cross-organism law.

\paragraph{Local conclusion.}
The finite $E. coli$ result supports a bounded $S^{QM}$ statement: after amino-acid, length, $GC3$, and $M$-density controls, the nine $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates explain a small but nonzero fraction of residualized mRNA half-life variation, with $Arg_{AGR}$ as the dominant coordinate and a permutation probability near $10^{-3}$.  The same finite row also reports that the mRNA-stability signature is directionally opposed to the same-join protein-abundance signature, despite the shared dominant coordinate.  Nothing in this row establishes translation realization, ribosome dynamics, protein abundance mediation, folding, physical admissibility, biochemical function, causal mechanism, or universality.  Each such claim would require its own layer-matched measurement contact and its own controlled statistic.
