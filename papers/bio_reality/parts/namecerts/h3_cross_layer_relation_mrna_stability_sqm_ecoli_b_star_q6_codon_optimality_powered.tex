\subsection{E. coli mRNA-stability survival of the $B^\ast_{Q6}$ codon residuals}
\label{subsec:bioreality-ecoli-mrna-stability-sqm-bstar-q6}

\paragraph{Finite-row object.}
This record tests whether the nine $B^\ast_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates retain a measurable association with an $E.\ coli$ mRNA-stability readout after the ordinary CDS-derived controls have already been removed.  The tested readout is $\log_{10}$ mRNA half-life in minutes from the Esquerr\'e et al. 2015 table at growth rate $0.40\,\mathrm{h}^{-1}$.  The joined table contains $2720$ CDS rows.  The source join reports $3739$ CDS rows available on the codon side, $2947$ valid half-life records by gene id, and a CDS join hit rate $0.9978649586335735$ under the key matching Esquerr\'e GeneId b-numbers to the $E.\ coli$ CDS locus-tag identifier.  The readout also records an available robustness growth-rate slice at $0.10\,\mathrm{h}^{-1}$, but the statistic below is the selected $0.40\,\mathrm{h}^{-1}$ row.

\paragraph{Residual design.}
The coordinate block is the full nine-dimensional list
$K\_AAA$, $Arg\_AGR$, $Ile\_AUA$, $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$, $Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG$, $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$, $Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG$, $Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY$, and $f3\_stress$.  The control block has $24$ columns: an intercept, $\log(\mathrm{CDS\ length})$, the twenty amino-acid composition proportions, GC3, and the $M$-density baseline given by the fraction of sense codons lying on the union support of the nine projected $B^\ast_{Q6}$ vectors.  Its realized rank is $23$, leaving residual degrees of freedom $2697$ for the $E.\ coli$ mRNA-stability fit.  The residualized $B^\ast_{Q6}$ design has rank $9$, so the reported statistic is not a one-coordinate proxy and is not a rank-collapsed surface.

\paragraph{Observed survival statistic.}
On the $2720$ joined rows, the $B^\ast_{Q6}$ block explains $4.951800341206348$ units of residual half-life energy out of $215.43086017169205$, giving
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{QM}(E.\ coli,0.40\,\mathrm{h}^{-1}) &= 0.02298556640056076,\\
\operatorname{rank}(X_{\perp Z}) &= 9,\\
\operatorname{rank}(Z) &= 23.
\end{aligned}
$$
The dominant coordinate in this mRNA-stability projection is $Arg\_AGR$.  Against $1000$ deterministic label permutations, the right-tail value is $p=0.000999000999000999$, so the finite row supports a positive statistical survival entry for the mRNA-stability readout in this organism and condition.  The corresponding same-join protein-abundance statistic is larger,
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{QP,\mathrm{same\ join}} &= 0.11632311854858134,\\
\cos(S^{QM}_{E.\ coli},S^{QP}_{E.\ coli}) &= -0.7784923375569599.
\end{aligned}
$$
The negative cosine is part of the result: the mRNA-stability direction and the same-row protein-abundance direction are strongly opposed in the nine-coordinate residual space, even though both have $Arg\_AGR$ as their dominant named coordinate on this finite table.

\paragraph{Cross-organism anchor.}
The yeast mRNA-stability row gives a useful comparison without making a phylogenetic claim.  In the yeast half-life table, the corresponding statistic is $R^2_{QM}=0.05179429044975085$ on $5085$ joined rows, with $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$ as the dominant coordinate and $\cos(S^{QM},S^{QP})=0.8040935468384264$.  The $E.\ coli$ result therefore has two chapter-specific features: the survival magnitude is lower than the yeast anchor, and the mRNA-versus-protein alignment changes sign.  This comparison is descriptive only; it is not a population-level estimate and it is not adjusted as an evolutionary independence analysis.

\paragraph{Interpretation.}
The finite row says that $B^\ast_{Q6}$ synonymous residual structure is not completely absorbed by amino-acid composition, GC3, length, and the $M$-density baseline when the readout is $E.\ coli$ mRNA half-life at $0.40\,\mathrm{h}^{-1}$.  The strongest named residual direction is $Arg\_AGR$, and the fitted survival is small but nonzero under the recorded permutation check.  The statistic is a controlled association with an mRNA-stability measurement.  It is not evidence that the codon residuals cause decay-rate changes, and it is not a translation-efficiency, ribosome-occupancy, or protein-output measurement.  No statement about translation realization, protein folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, biological mechanism, or universal law follows from this row unless an additional layer-matched contact supplies that separate measurement and its own controlled statistic.
