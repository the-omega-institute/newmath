\subsection{E. coli mRNA-Stability Readout for the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ Codon Residual}
\label{sec:bioreality-ecoli-mrna-stability-bstar-q6}

\paragraph{Question.}
This record tests whether the nine-dimensional $B^{*}_{Q6}$ synonymous residual system leaves a measurable trace on an $E.\ coli$ mRNA-stability readout after ordinary sequence-composition explanations have been removed.  The response variable is $\log_{10}$ mRNA half-life, in minutes, from the Esquerr\'{e} et al. 2015 table at growth rate $0.40\,h^{-1}$.  The codon side is the fixed list
$$
\begin{aligned}
q \in \{&K\_AAA,\ Arg\_AGR,\ Ile\_AUA,\ Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR,\ Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG,\\
&Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY,\ Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG,\ Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY,\ f3\_stress\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The tested layer is a cross-layer relation from codon residual coordinates to mRNA stability, not a direct translation, protein, folding, or function statement.

\paragraph{Rows and joining.}
The finite table is organism-specific: $E.\ coli$ K-12 MG1655 only.  The half-life table contains $2947$ valid gene-id records, and the CDS side reports $3739$ joined coding sequences before the half-life intersection.  Matching uses the rule that the Esquerr\'{e} GeneId b-number equals the CDS locus-tag or matching CDS identifier.  The final statistical join contains
$$
\begin{aligned}
n &= 2720,\\
\text{CDS hit rate} &= 0.9978649586335735.
\end{aligned}
$$
The loaded payload is recorded by SHA-256 digest $87499a22f8699b15d2daf96f826e3345b1707a8360be122f1ec0114dc934469b$.  The skipped CDS counts are explicit: $1019$ rows lack a half-life match, while duplicate locus tags, empty sense-codon counts, invalid lengths, missing locus tags, non-object records, and non-positive abundance rows each contribute $0$.  On the half-life side, duplicate gene ids, missing gene ids, missing growth-rate entries, non-object records, and non-positive half-lives each contribute $0$.  A second growth-rate contact at $0.10\,h^{-1}$ is available for robustness, but the finite row summarized here uses the declared $0.40\,h^{-1}$ readout.

\paragraph{Residual design.}
The control matrix $Z$ has $24$ columns and rank $23$.  Its columns are the intercept, $\log(\mathrm{CDS\ length\ in\ nt})$, the twenty amino-acid composition proportions $A,C,D,E,F,G,H,I,K,L,M,N,P,Q,R,S,T,V,W,Y$ computed from real CDS codon counts, GC3 from the same CDS counts, and the $M$-density baseline, defined as the fraction of sense codons lying on the union support of the nine projected $B^{*}_{Q6}$ vectors.  After residualizing by $Z$, the remaining degrees of freedom are $2697$.  The residual codon design has rank $9$, so the nine-coordinate test is not collapsed by the controls:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\operatorname{rank}(Z) &= 23,\\
\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde X) &= 9,\\
\mathrm{df}_{\mathrm{resid}} &= 2697.
\end{aligned}
$$
This construction asks whether the nine residual coordinates improve the description of the mRNA half-life residual beyond amino-acid composition, GC3, length, and the $M$-density baseline.

\paragraph{Finite statistic.}
The $E.\ coli$ mRNA-stability statistic is positive but modest:
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^{2}_{QM} &= 0.02298556640056076,\\
\text{explained energy} &= 4.951800341206348,\\
\text{residual response energy} &= 215.43086017169205.
\end{aligned}
$$
The permutation calculation uses $1000$ permutations and gives a right-tail value
$$
\begin{aligned}
p &= 0.000999000999000999.
\end{aligned}
$$
The dominant coordinate for the mRNA-stability surface is $Arg\_AGR$.  On the same joined rows, the corresponding protein-abundance residual surface has
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^{2}_{QP} &= 0.11632311854858134,
\end{aligned}
$$
and its dominant coordinate is also $Arg\_AGR$.  However, the direction of the full nine-coordinate mRNA-stability vector is strongly opposed to the same-row protein-abundance vector:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\cos(S^{QM},S^{QP}) &= -0.7784923375569599.
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the $E.\ coli$ row is not a simple propagation of the protein-abundance statistic.  It says that $Arg\_AGR$ is the largest coordinate in both projections, while the full signed nine-coordinate profile points in a substantially different direction for mRNA half-life and protein abundance.

\paragraph{Cross-organism context.}
The yeast mRNA-stability anchor supplied with this experiment has $R^{2}_{QM}=0.052$, dominant coordinate $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$, permutation value $p=0.001$, and cosine $0.8$ against the yeast same-row protein-abundance surface.  The human consensus relative-half-life row has $R^{2}_{QM}=0.010503670362051522$, dominant coordinate $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$, $16165$ joined rows, $54$ consensus dataset columns, and positive same-row cosine $0.6351183883924848$ against its protein-abundance surface.  Against these two anchors, the $E.\ coli$ result is qualitatively distinct: its mRNA-stability strength lies between human and yeast, its dominant coordinate is $Arg\_AGR$ rather than $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$, and its same-row abundance comparison has negative cosine rather than positive cosine.

\paragraph{Interpretation.}
The finite row supports a bounded statistical statement: after the declared CDS-derived controls, the nine $B^{*}_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates explain a nonzero portion of residualized $E.\ coli$ mRNA half-life, with $R^{2}_{QM}=0.02298556640056076$ and permutation value $0.000999000999000999$.  The effect is small in absolute variance terms, but it is not numerically empty under the recorded null.  The readout is growth-rate-specific, organism-specific, and tied to a single half-life dataset; the cross-domain comparison to yeast and human is descriptive rather than a phylogenetically controlled population estimate.

\paragraph{Boundary.}
No translation realization follows from this row by itself.  The response is mRNA half-life, not ribosome occupancy, translation efficiency, tAI, stAI, protein abundance, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, phenotype, mechanism, or a universal biological law.  In particular, a positive $S^{QM}$ entry is a residual statistical association with an mRNA-stability readout, not a codon-optimality mechanism and not evidence that $Arg\_AGR$ controls decay.  Any claim about ribosome-mediated translation, downstream protein output, structure, physical admissibility, function, causal mechanism, or universality requires its own matched contact and its own controlled statistic.
