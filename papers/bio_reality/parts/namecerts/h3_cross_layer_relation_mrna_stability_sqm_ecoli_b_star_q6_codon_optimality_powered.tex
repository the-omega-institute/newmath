\subsection{Escherichia coli mRNA-stability survival of the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates}
\label{sec:bioreality-ecoli-mrna-stability-sqm-bstar-q6}

\paragraph{Finite row and readout.}
This record tests whether the nine synonymous residual coordinates of $B^{*}_{Q6}$ retain predictive content for an $E.\ coli$ mRNA-stability readout after the usual coding-sequence controls have already been removed.  The biological row is restricted to $E.\ coli$ K-12 MG1655 and uses the Esquerr\'e et al. mRNA half-life table at growth rate $0.40\,h^{-1}$.  The readout is $\log_{10}$ mRNA half-life in minutes.  The join is keyed by matching the Esquerr\'e gene identifier, in b-number form, to the CDS locus tag or matched CDS identifier.  The loaded table contains $2947$ valid half-life records, $3739$ CDS records in the organism-level coding-sequence panel, and $2720$ joined rows for the regression surface.  The reported CDS join hit rate is $0.9978649586335735$.  The half-life payload hash recorded for the row is \texttt{87499a22f8699b15d2daf96f826e3345b1707a8360be122f1ec0114dc934469b}.  A second growth-rate column, $0.10\,h^{-1}$, is available as a robustness direction but is not the selected row used in the statistic below.

\paragraph{Residual design.}
The design matrix consists of the nine $B^{*}_{Q6}$ coordinates
$K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $Leu_{CUN/UUR}$, $Leu_{UUA/UUG}$, $Ser_{UCR/AGY}$, $Ser_{UCA/UCG}$, $Thr_{ACR/ACY}$, and $f3$-stress.  These coordinates are tested only after residualizing against a $24$-column control family consisting of an intercept, $\log$ CDS length, the $20$ amino-acid composition proportions computed from real CDS codon counts, GC3, and the $M$-density baseline defined as the fraction of sense codons lying on the union support of the nine projected $B^{*}_{Q6}$ vectors.  The control matrix has rank $23$, leaving residual degrees of freedom $2697$.  The residualized coordinate block has rank $9$, so the statistic is not a saturated or rank-trivial fit.

\paragraph{Survival statistic.}
Let $M$ denote the residualized mRNA-stability readout and let $Q$ denote the residualized nine-coordinate $B^{*}_{Q6}$ block after the stated controls.  The finite-row statistic is the controlled coefficient of determination
$$
\begin{aligned}
S^{QM}_{E.\ coli} &= R^{2}(M \mid Q, Z) \\
&= 0.02298556640056076.
\end{aligned}
$$
The explained residual energy is $4.951800341206348$ against readout residual energy $215.43086017169205$.  The permutation comparison used $1000$ permutations and reports right-tail value $p=0.000999000999000999$, so the row is treated as a positive statistical survival entry for the $E.\ coli$ mRNA-stability surface, not as a mechanistic explanation of decay.  The dominant coordinate in this row is $Arg_{AGR}$.

\paragraph{Relation to the same-row abundance surface.}
On the same joined row set, the protein-abundance surface gives
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^{2}_{QP,\ same\ join} &= 0.11632311854858134,\\
R^{2}_{QM} &= 0.02298556640056076,\\
\cos(S^{QM}_{E.\ coli},S^{QP}_{E.\ coli}) &= -0.7784923375569599.
\end{aligned}
$$
The same coordinate, $Arg_{AGR}$, is dominant for both the mRNA-stability row and the same-join abundance row, but the negative cosine records that the two nine-coordinate directions are opposed as vectors.  This is a cross-layer relation in the narrow statistical sense: a codon-residual direction survives in an mRNA half-life readout, and the corresponding abundance direction on the same join is not aligned with it.  It is not a statement that mRNA half-life causes the abundance signal, nor that $Arg_{AGR}$ is a biochemical mechanism.

\paragraph{Comparison rows.}
The yeast mRNA-stability row supplies a useful contrast: $R^{2}_{QM}=0.05179429044975085$, $R^{2}_{QP,\ same\ join}=0.2761140373962526$, permutation value $p=0.000999000999000999$, dominant coordinate $Leu_{CUN/UUR}$, and cosine $0.8040935468384264$ between the mRNA-stability and abundance surfaces.  The human consensus mRNA-stability row reports $R^{2}_{QM}=0.010503670362051522$, $R^{2}_{QP,\ same\ join}=0.04095215557813418$, permutation value $p=0.000999000999000999$, dominant coordinate $Leu_{CUN/UUR}$, and positive cosine $0.6351183883924848$.  Against those comparison rows, the $E.\ coli$ result is neither a universal sign rule nor a conserved dominant-coordinate claim: it is a finite organism-specific survival row with a different dominant coordinate and an opposite relation to the abundance surface.

\paragraph{Local boundary.}
This chapter only records a controlled association between $B^{*}_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates and a growth-rate-specific $E.\ coli$ mRNA half-life measurement.  It does not report a ribosome-profiling translation-efficiency measurement, does not infer translation realization, does not identify a decay mechanism, does not establish codon-optimality causation, and does not support protein folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, organismal phenotype, or a universal biological law.  Any such stronger claim would require a separate matched contact and its own controlled statistic.  The row is therefore ready as an $S^{QM}$ cross-layer finite statistic, while downstream translation, mechanism, structure, function, and universality remain outside the asserted content.
