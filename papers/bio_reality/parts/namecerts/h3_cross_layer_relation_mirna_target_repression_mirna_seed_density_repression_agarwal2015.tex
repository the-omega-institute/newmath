\subsection{miRNA seed-density repression as a finite cross-layer readout}
\label{subsec:bioreality-mirna-seed-density-repression-agarwal2015}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite surface.}
The miRNA seed-density repression packet records a finite human post-transcriptional readout surface rather than a universal mechanism.  Its present computed row set contains $11$ miRNAs and $56682$ miRNA--target pairs, produced in $1$ second under experiment run $45f083dea984$.  The carrier is the matrix whose rows are miRNA--CDS pairs and whose measured coordinates include seed-density information and target repression annotations derived from the Agarwal-style target-repression surface.  In this chapter the object is not the whole codon-to-protein mediation program; it is the bounded question whether an externally measured miRNA seed-density surface can be attached as a same-row repression readout without promoting synonymous residual geometry into translation, folding, protein function, or biological universality.

\paragraph{Readout form.}
For each retained pair $(m,g)$, with miRNA $m$ and target transcript or CDS unit $g$, the finite row stores a seed-density coordinate and a repression-side readout.  The minimal matrix shape is
$$
\begin{aligned}
  R_{m,g} &= (d_{m,g},\rho_{m,g},z_g),\\
  m &\in \{1,\ldots,11\},\\
  (m,g) &\in \{1,\ldots,56682\},
\end{aligned}
$$
where $d_{m,g}$ denotes the seed-density coordinate, $\rho_{m,g}$ denotes the repression readout, and $z_g$ denotes the local control vector carried by the row when matched controls are present.  The run supplies row cardinality and execution provenance, not yet a published coefficient table inside this packet.  Consequently the current chapter treats the computed surface as a finite repression-contact attachment and not as a quantitative law about all miRNAs or all transcripts.

\paragraph{Relation to the translation-conditioned boundary.}
The same research branch already contains measured translation and protein-abundance controls that fix the no-promotion discipline for this miRNA contact.  In the measured ribosome-TE survival run $58611670ec19$, the B$^*_{Q6}$ coordinate set has $9$ residual coordinates: $K\_AAA$, $Arg\_AGR$, $Ile\_AUA$, $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$, $Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG$, $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$, $Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG$, $Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY$, and $f3\_stress$.  The measured TE joins are organism-specific: $13140$ genes for zebrafish with $R^2_{QT,meas}=0.04034217614654355$, $3415$ genes for \emph{E. coli} with $R^2_{QT,meas}=0.009394266754822262$, $11554$ genes for human with $R^2_{QT,meas}=0.018837911344127926$, and $5128$ genes for yeast with $R^2_{QT,meas}=0.13681931343730436$.  Those TE rows show how a translation readout is admitted: the row must be joined, residualized, and interpreted as a measured readout only at its own layer.

\paragraph{Protein mediation comparison.}
The measured protein-abundance mediation run $d71384d16e71$ gives the neighboring standard for a cross-layer claim.  It uses the same $9$ residual coordinates and reports three-way joins of $13140$ rows in zebrafish, $3415$ in \emph{E. coli}, $11554$ in human, and $5128$ in yeast.  The measured mediation fractions are $0.0$ for zebrafish, $0.04091158340950673$ for \emph{E. coli}, $0.0027966485036786744$ for human, and $0.4793069656345167$ for yeast.  These values are not imported as miRNA effects.  They define the local standard for saying that a downstream readout has crossed a layer: the direct coordinate-to-output association must be evaluated together with an explicit measured intermediate.  The miRNA seed-density packet therefore remains a repression readout surface unless a same-row model later connects seed density, mRNA abundance, translation readout, and protein abundance in one controlled finite panel.

\paragraph{Stability and mediator context.}
A second neighboring run, $81f728964641$, decomposes codon-residual protein-abundance association by measured TE and mRNA stability.  It reports $R^2_{QP}=0.1181743523064932$ for \emph{E. coli}, $0.04274178488511215$ for human, and $0.2715520808117803$ for yeast.  Joint mediation fractions are $0.2404669586626661$, $0.16890712099923358$, and $0.5195182225621592$, with right-tail permutation values $0.000999000999000999$ in all three organisms.  This matters for the miRNA seed-density record because miRNA repression may act through transcript abundance, translation, decay, or other post-transcriptional channels.  The chapter therefore does not identify seed density with translation inhibition by definition.  It records a finite repression surface and requires additional same-row readouts before assigning the repression signal to translation, RNA stability, protein abundance, or any further biological layer.

\paragraph{Four local cases.}
Given a pair-level seed-density statistic $d_{m,g}$, a repression readout $\rho_{m,g}$, and optional matched translation or abundance readouts, the finite readback separates four cases:
$$
\begin{aligned}
  A &: d_{m,g}\ \hbox{does not survive against repression or any downstream readout},\\
  B &: d_{m,g}\ \hbox{survives against repression only},\\
  C &: d_{m,g}\ \hbox{survives against a downstream output without a measured intermediate},\\
  D &: d_{m,g}\to T_g\to P_g\ \hbox{is supported by a measured intermediate and attenuation of the direct term}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The current Agarwal-style packet establishes that the pair matrix exists at scale: $11$ miRNAs and $56682$ pairs.  It does not by itself place any row in case $D$.  A case-$D$ call would require the same target units to carry miRNA seed density, matched mRNA abundance, a measured translation readout, matched protein abundance, and enough residual rank after controls for an attenuation test.

\paragraph{Interpretive boundary.}
This chapter locally fixes the cannot-claim boundary.  The finite miRNA seed-density repression surface may support a bounded repression-contact statement: a computed pair matrix exists and can be joined to later row-level controls.  It does not imply translation realization, ribosome dwell, protein folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, system phenotype, causal mechanism, evolutionary direction, or a universal biological rule.  Those claims require separate measured contacts at their own layers.  A positive seed-density association, even on $56682$ pairs, remains a repression-side association until same-row translation and protein-abundance measurements show the required mediation or attenuation pattern under explicit controls.
