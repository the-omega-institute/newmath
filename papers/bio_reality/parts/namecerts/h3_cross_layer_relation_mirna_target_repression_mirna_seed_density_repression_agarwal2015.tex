\subsection{miRNA seed-density repression as a finite cross-layer readout}
\label{sec:bioreality-mirna-seed-density-repression-agarwal2015}

\paragraph{Finite surface.}
The miRNA seed-density packet records a bounded cross-layer test surface rather than a general statement about translation, protein folding, molecular function, or organismal phenotype.  The finite computation attached to the Agarwal-style seed-density readout contains $11$ miRNAs and $56682$ miRNA--transcript pairs, with the run identified by \texttt{45f083dea984} and a recorded runtime of $1$ second.  Thus the carrier is not an open-ended literature assertion: it is a finite row table whose rows are matched miRNA--target entries and whose columns are the measurable features needed to ask whether seed-density structure has a detectable repression readout on the attached transcript/protein surface.

\paragraph{Local coordinate rule.}
The relevant mathematical object is a finite matrix with one row for each tested pair and with separate columns for seed-density coordinates, transcript-level controls, translation readouts when present, and abundance readouts when present.  A row may contribute to a repression statement only through an observed readout attached to that row.  In particular, a seed-density coordinate can support a bounded repression claim only when the corresponding finite statistic is computed after the declared controls; it cannot be promoted to translation, folding, physical admissibility, biological function, mechanism, or universality by its identifier alone.

\paragraph{Boundary against Q-to-protein promotion.}
This chapter is deliberately placed next to the $B^*_{Q6}$ cross-layer matrix because both objects use finite residual coordinates and downstream readouts, but their biological meanings are kept distinct.  The measured ribosome-TE survival computation used $9$ residual coordinates, namely \texttt{K\_AAA}, \texttt{Arg\_AGR}, \texttt{Ile\_AUA}, \texttt{Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR}, \texttt{Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG}, \texttt{Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY}, \texttt{Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG}, \texttt{Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY}, and \texttt{f3\_stress}.  In that separate measured-TE surface the joined organism counts were $13140$ for \textit{Danio rerio}, $3415$ for \textit{Escherichia coli} K-12 MG1655, $11554$ for human, and $5128$ for yeast, with measured $R^2_{QT}$ values $0.04034217614654355$, $0.009394266754822262$, $0.018837911344127926$, and $0.13681931343730436$, respectively.  Those numbers show how a finite translation-readout contact is recorded when it exists; they do not make the miRNA seed-density table itself a translation mechanism.

\paragraph{Protein-abundance comparison surface.}
The direct protein-abundance surface supplies a useful caution.  In the matched PAXdb computation, all $9$ residual coordinates survived the direct $Q \to P$ fit in each of $15$ organisms, with examples including $R^2_{QP}=0.29837624994140827$ in yeast, $0.10767526677985896$ in \textit{E. coli}, $0.04454565118019448$ in human, and $0.02763998603083845$ in rat.  The joined protein counts ranged from $1656$ in \textit{Sulfolobus solfataricus} and $1747$ in \textit{Halobacterium salinarum} to $19053$ in human and $19395$ in mouse.  The hit rates also remained finite and organism-specific, for example $0.9978649586335735$ in \textit{E. coli}, $0.9156038419146592$ in yeast, $0.9779294769799313$ in human, and $0.8231586752347998$ in rat.  A direct abundance association of this kind is only an association unless a measured intermediate readout is shown to absorb or reduce the direct signal.  The same rule is applied here to miRNA seed density: repression readback is local to the measured seed-density table and is not upgraded into a causal path without the appropriate finite attenuation statistic.

\paragraph{Mediation contrast.}
The measured translation-mediated abundance computation gives the explicit form of the required attenuation check.  For \textit{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}, the measured mediated fraction was $0.4793069656345167$ with $S_{QP}=0.3146818503437042$ and $S_{QT}=0.13681931343730436$ on $5128$ joined three-way rows.  The corresponding fractions were $0.04091158340950673$ in \textit{E. coli} on $3415$ rows, $0.0027966485036786744$ in human on $11554$ rows, and $0.0$ in \textit{Danio rerio} on $13140$ rows after truncating a negative raw fraction.  This is the finite pattern required before a direct coordinate-to-abundance association can be called translation-conditioned.  The miRNA seed-density packet has its own $56682$-pair surface, but it is not allowed to borrow these mediation fractions as if they were miRNA mechanism measurements.

\paragraph{RNA and translation controls.}
The joint TE--mRNA-stability computation gives a second reference for how transcript-layer controls are handled.  On the four-way join, yeast had $4759$ joined rows and a joint mediation fraction $0.5195182225621592$ with right-tail permutation value $0.000999000999000999$; human had $11322$ rows and joint fraction $0.16890712099923358$ with the same recorded right-tail value; \textit{E. coli} had $2517$ rows and joint fraction $0.2404669586626661$ with the same recorded right-tail value.  The single-mediator decompositions were finite as well: yeast had TE-only fraction $0.4697188319693239$ and stability-only fraction $0.0626618595344251$, human had $0.00549490864190343$ and $0.15314679870183337$, and \textit{E. coli} had $0.01975521340963268$ and $0.23052056324898387$.  These values are not imported as miRNA effects.  They specify the level of finite-row evidence needed before one asserts that a transcript-control or translation-control layer has absorbed another signal.

\paragraph{Dwell and positional checks.}
The yeast dwell-mediated $f_3$ surface illustrates how a local readout can be positive without becoming a global biological law.  On $1257$ joined rows, the base model had $R^2=0.1855578757644517$, the full model had $R^2=0.20412261520502883$, and the held-out increment was $0.01856473944057712$ against a fixed row-label permutation null with $120$ permutations and null $95$th percentile $0.0013115362189692937$.  The same family localized the leading positional dwell segment to the $5'$ window, with the $5'$ window passing and the $3'$ body window not passing.  A related structural-order ramp screen reported a weighted $f_3$ ramp increment $0.0011165640103326878$, a weighted body increment $-0.00026157313923957926$, and the classification \texttt{ramp\_positive\_but\_not\_localized}.  These records are useful because they show how a finite signal is reported with its own restrictions.  The miRNA seed-density surface is treated in the same way: it can record seed-density repression evidence over $56682$ pairs, but it does not become protein structure, physical admissibility, function, phenotype, or universal biology.

\paragraph{Readback statement.}
The chapter-level readback is therefore the following bounded claim.  There exists a finite miRNA seed-density repression table for the Agarwal-style contact, consisting of $11$ miRNAs and $56682$ miRNA--transcript pairs.  This table is admissible as a finite repression readout surface for the named cross-layer claim.  Its admissibility is row-bounded and control-bounded: no conclusion about translation rate, protein folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, causal mechanism, evolutionary direction, or universality follows unless another contact supplies the corresponding rows and the corresponding attenuation or survival statistic.

\paragraph{Mathematical form.}
Let $R$ be the finite set of tested miRNA--target rows and let $M$ be the finite set of miRNAs.  The recorded computation fixes
$$
\begin{aligned}
|M| &= 11,\\
|R| &= 56682,\\
\tau_{\mathrm{run}} &= 1\;\mathrm{second}.
\end{aligned}
$$
For a seed-density coordinate $s$ and a measured repression readout $y$, an admissible local statement has the form
$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta(s,y\mid Z) > 0
\end{aligned}
$$
only when $s$, $y$, and the control matrix $Z$ are all evaluated on the same finite row set or on a declared matched submatrix.  If the statistic is absent, mismatched, rank-degenerate, or attached only by a contact name, the row surface remains a bounded candidate rather than a mechanism claim.  This is the exact status of the present record: the finite miRNA seed-density surface is present and numerically bounded; every stronger biological interpretation requires a separate finite contact at that layer.
