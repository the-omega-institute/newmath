\subsection{miRNA seed-density repression as a cross-layer readout}
\label{sec:bioreality-mirna-seed-density-repression-agarwal2015}

\paragraph{Finite surface.}
The miRNA seed-density packet records a finite target--regulator surface rather than a free biological law.  The attached computation for the Agarwal-style seed-density repression readout used $11$ miRNAs and $56682$ miRNA--target pairs, and the finite pass completed in $1$ second under run id $45f083dea984$.  The record is therefore a row-level cross-layer contact: it says that a bounded table of seed-density rows has been materialized, not that any synonymous residual coordinate, translation state, folded structure, molecular function, or phenotype has been explained.

The row object may be written as a finite matrix
$$
\begin{aligned}
D_{ab} &= (m_a,g_b,s_{ab},r_{ab}),\\
1 &\leq a \leq 11,\\
1 &\leq (a,b) \leq 56682,
\end{aligned}
$$
where $m_a$ is a miRNA label, $g_b$ is a transcript or target label, $s_{ab}$ is the seed-density descriptor, and $r_{ab}$ is the attached repression readout when present in the source table.  The verified row count fixes the size of the matrix.  It does not by itself fix a positive repression coefficient, a mediation coefficient, or a causal direction.  Those must be read from the fitted surface, not from the existence of the table.

\paragraph{Relation to the translation-conditioned protein panel.}
This miRNA chapter is deliberately kept separate from the $B^*_{Q6}$ synonymous-residual protein-abundance panel.  The related protein-omics computations show why that separation is necessary.  Direct codon-residual association with measured PAXdb abundance was computed over $15$ organisms with $9$ residual coordinates.  The observed direct $R^2_{QP}$ values ranged from $0.019256877907572044$ in \textit{Halobacterium salinarum} to $0.29837624994140827$ in \textit{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}, with all $9$ residual coordinates surviving the direct protein-omics screen in each listed organism.  Those direct associations are not miRNA repression and are not translation mechanisms.

The measured ribosome-TE contact gives a stricter boundary.  Four organisms had measured TE survival against the same $9$ residual coordinates after the stated controls: \textit{Danio rerio} had $R^2_{QT,\mathrm{meas}}=0.04034217614654355$, \textit{Escherichia coli} K12 MG1655 had $0.009394266754822262$, \textit{Homo sapiens} had $0.018837911344127926$, and \textit{Saccharomyces cerevisiae} had $0.13681931343730436$.  The joined row counts were $13140$, $3415$, $11554$, and $5128$, respectively.  These numbers establish that a translation readout can be attached on finite rows, but they do not turn miRNA seed density into a translation readout.  A miRNA density column is a post-transcriptional regulatory descriptor unless a same-row mediation fit explicitly connects it to translation and protein abundance.

\paragraph{Mediation boundary.}
The strongest related translation-conditioned protein computation still remains statistical.  In the measured $Q \to T \to P$ panel, the mediated fractions were $0.0$ for \textit{Danio rerio}, $0.04091158340950673$ for \textit{E. coli}, $0.0027966485036786744$ for \textit{Homo sapiens}, and $0.4793069656345167$ for \textit{S. cerevisiae}.  The corresponding three-way joined row counts were $13140$, $3415$, $11554$, and $5128$.  Thus the paper may say that measured translation conditioning has been evaluated on finite matched panels, but it may not transfer those mediation fractions to the miRNA seed-density table.

The joint TE and mRNA-stability panel gives the same warning in another form.  For \textit{S. cerevisiae}, $R^2_{QP}=0.2715520808117803$ and $R^2_{QP\mid T,M}=0.13047582645538838$, giving joint mediation fraction $0.5195182225621592$ with right-tail permutation value $0.000999000999000999$.  For \textit{E. coli}, the analogous values were $R^2_{QP}=0.1181743523064932$, $R^2_{QP\mid T,M}=0.08975732521542036$, and joint fraction $0.2404669586626661$.  For \textit{Homo sapiens}, they were $0.04274178488511215$, $0.0355223930537993$, and $0.16890712099923358$.  These are translation and stability partitions, not miRNA seed-density repression estimates.

\paragraph{Readback rule.}
The miRNA seed-density surface is admitted as a finite cross-layer readout only in the following local sense.  It can support statements of the form: among $56682$ evaluated miRNA--target pairs and $11$ miRNAs, a seed-density descriptor was available for a repression-oriented table.  It can also be placed beside translation, abundance, and stability surfaces as a possible regulatory covariate.  It cannot be read as a proof that $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinates cause repression, that codon choice realizes a miRNA mechanism, or that protein output is mediated through miRNA targeting.

A permissible finite statement is therefore
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{Rows}_{\mathrm{miRNA}} &= 56682,\\
\mathrm{miRNAs}_{\mathrm{miRNA}} &= 11,\\
\mathrm{runtime}_{\mathrm{sec}} &= 1.
\end{aligned}
$$
A non-permissible strengthening would replace this row certificate by an unmeasured mechanism claim such as $Q_i \to T_j \to P_k$ through seed density.  That strengthening is blocked unless the same row panel contains codon residuals, miRNA seed-density descriptors, matched transcript abundance, a measured translation readout, matched protein abundance, and an explicit conditioning test showing that the seed-density or translation term absorbs the relevant direct association.

\paragraph{Compression comparison.}
The related partial-mediation dictionary computation shows how a legitimate reduction claim is reported when such a fit exists.  In yeast, the selected measured-readout dictionary had coverage $C^*=0.24538211439730545$, unexplained fraction $0.7546178856026946$, selection-aware null $95$ percentile $0.029149007242956115$, naive fixed-subset null $95$ percentile $0.00598374993486675$, $\lambda_{\mathrm{DL}}=0.01$, $\rho_{\mathrm{join}}=0.05$, $5$ folds, $120$ permutations, $n_{\mathrm{join}}=1800$, and $n_{\mathrm{join,available}}=4758$.  A miRNA seed-density mediation claim would need the same kind of finite reduction statement: a declared target, a fixed candidate family, a selection-aware null, and a measured excess over that null.

The f3-stress dwell computation gives another concrete contrast.  On yeast rows, the held-out dwell increment was $\Delta R^2=0.01856473944057712$, the null $95$ percentile was $0.0013115362189692937$, $n_{\mathrm{join}}=1257$, $5$ folds were used, and the f3--dwell correlation was $0.1615441450901653$ with approximate $p=7.873916057196935\times 10^{-9}$.  These numbers have the shape needed for a readout-specific claim: target, readout, null model, increment, and finite sample size.  The miRNA seed-density table currently supplies the first finite-size certificate but not the full mediation fit.

\paragraph{Local boundary.}
No translation, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, organismal phenotype, mechanism, causality, or universal biological law follows from the $11$-miRNA, $56682$-pair seed-density table alone.  The chapter records a bounded repression-oriented row surface and its relation to already computed translation and protein-abundance panels.  A stronger conclusion would require a separate layer-matched contact and a same-row statistic showing that seed-density information survives the declared controls and explains an otherwise direct association after the appropriate transcript, translation, and abundance variables are conditioned.
