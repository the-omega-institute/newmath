\subsection{A finite seed-density surface for miRNA target repression}
\label{sec:bioreality-mirna-seed-density-repression-agarwal2015}

\paragraph{Finite surface.}
This record isolates a finite miRNA target-repression surface from the broader codon-residual program. The active row set is not a statement about all post-transcriptional regulation. It is a bounded table whose rows are miRNA--target pairs and whose columns are the observed seed-density or target-site quantities needed to test whether a seed-density coordinate carries repression readback on the same target units. The verified computation registered $11$ miRNAs and $56682$ miRNA--target pairs, with a reported runtime of $1$ second. Thus the finite carrier for this chapter is the matrix
$$
\begin{aligned}
  \mathcal{M}_{\mathrm{seed}}&=\{(\mu,g):\mu\in R_{\mu},\ g\in R_g,\ (\mu,g)\ \text{is retained by the finite target-pair parser}\},\\
  |R_{\mu}|&=11,\\
  |\mathcal{M}_{\mathrm{seed}}|&=56682.
\end{aligned}
$$
The claim is deliberately local: the matrix certifies that a seed-density repression readout was assembled over a nonempty and rank-relevant finite surface. It does not by itself claim that every miRNA in the source study is represented, that every transcript has a usable target annotation, or that a causal repression mechanism has been isolated.

\paragraph{Coordinate readback.}
The biological coordinate in this chapter is the miRNA seed-density coordinate on matched target rows. A row $(\mu,g)$ is interpreted only after the target row has an explicit miRNA identity, a transcript or gene target identity, and the row-level repression readout supplied by the finite Agarwal-style target-repression surface. The row language is therefore
$$
\begin{aligned}
  (\mu,g)&\longmapsto (D_{\mu g},Y_{\mu g},Z_{\mu g}),\\
  D_{\mu g}&=\text{seed-density or target-site coordinate for miRNA }\mu\text{ on target }g,\\
  Y_{\mu g}&=\text{matched target-repression readout},\\
  Z_{\mu g}&=\text{available target-level controls and row filters}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The chapter-specific assertion is that the finite surface is large enough to state a genuine seed-density question: whether variation in $D_{\mu g}$ survives as target-repression signal after the row filters used by the computation. The verified rows are the evidence surface; no codon-residual geometry is used as evidence for this miRNA claim.

\paragraph{Relation to translation and protein-abundance readouts.}
The seed-density table is cross-layer only in the weak sense that target repression can affect transcript and protein output downstream. It is not a translation-efficiency table, a ribosome-occupancy table, or a protein-abundance mediation table. This distinction is enforced by the neighboring measured-translation facts. In the measured TE survival computation, the retained organisms had $9$ residual coordinates, and the measured TE joins were $13140$ genes for \textit{Danio rerio}, $3415$ genes for \textit{Escherichia coli} K12 MG1655, $11554$ genes for human, and $5128$ genes for yeast, with measured $R^2_{QT}$ values $0.04034217614654355$, $0.009394266754822262$, $0.018837911344127926$, and $0.13681931343730436$, respectively. Those numbers belong to the ribosome-TE surface, not to the miRNA target table. They are cited here only to make the separation explicit: a finite seed-density matrix does not inherit a measured translation-efficiency result merely because both live near transcript regulation.

The same separation applies to protein abundance. In the measured translation-conditioned protein-abundance decomposition, the three-way joins were $13140$ for \textit{Danio rerio}, $3415$ for \textit{E. coli}, $11554$ for human, and $5128$ for yeast. The measured mediated fractions were $0.0$, $0.04091158340950673$, $0.0027966485036786744$, and $0.4793069656345167$, respectively. These values describe a codon-residual-to-measured-TE-to-protein-abundance decomposition. They do not license a miRNA mechanism claim unless the same miRNA rows are joined to a measured translation readout and the direct seed-density-to-output association is reduced after conditioning on that readout.

\paragraph{Local four-step test.}
The admissible repression statement has four finite checks. First, the seed-density surface must retain the declared row count and miRNA count. Second, the readout column must be present on the same $(\mu,g)$ rows. Third, the seed-density coordinate must be tested against the target-repression readout after row-level controls rather than reported as a raw target-site count. Fourth, any proposed propagation into translation or protein abundance must pass through a measured intermediate table on the same units. In symbols, the local support condition has the form
$$
\begin{aligned}
  &|R_{\mu}|=11,\quad |\mathcal{M}_{\mathrm{seed}}|=56682,\\
  &S^{\mu Y}>0\ \text{on the retained seed-density surface},\\
  &\text{and, for any downstream protein or translation statement,}\quad
    S^{\mu T}>0\ \text{with attenuation of }S^{\mu P}\text{ after conditioning on }T.
\end{aligned}
$$
Only the first finite inventory is established by the registered miRNA computation in this record. The downstream line is written as an admissibility rule, not as a result for the Agarwal seed-density table.

\paragraph{Boundary.}
The registered seed-density computation supplies a finite target-repression surface with $11$ miRNAs and $56682$ target pairs. It does not show translation efficiency, ribosome occupancy, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, organismal phenotype, causation, or universality. A positive seed-density association would remain a target-repression association unless a separate matched contact supplies the missing layer. In particular, no translation, folding, physical-admissibility, function, mechanism, or universal biological law follows from this chapter without an additional finite table at that layer and an explicit conditioning calculation on the same biological units.

\paragraph{Readback verdict.}
The chapter is ready as a finite BioReality record for miRNA seed-density target repression because the row carrier is concrete and nonempty: $11$ miRNAs and $56682$ miRNA--target pairs were registered for the Agarwal-style surface. Its scientific scope is intentionally narrow. The result can be used as a seed-density repression surface and as a request for later same-row mediation, but it cannot be promoted into a translation-mediated protein-output claim without a joined measured-translation and protein-abundance panel.
