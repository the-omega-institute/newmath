\paragraph{Finite row surface.}

The row count is large enough to make the chapter a measured regulatory readout rather than a three-row rank artifact, but the available record at this site only certifies the existence and size of the pair table; it does not by itself supply a full coefficient table, a per-miRNA effect list, a permutation null, or a causal perturbation assay.

In the notation used locally in this chapter, the admissible readout is bounded by
$$
\begin{aligned}

\operatorname{runtime} &= 1\ \mathrm{second}.

These numbers are the entire certified finite support for this record.  They justify treating the miRNA seed-density packet as a concrete cross-layer relation between a transcript-level sequence feature and an observed repression readout, but they do not identify the biological layer of the effect beyond that measured repression surface.

\paragraph{Mediation boundary.}
A measured translation-mediated protein-abundance surface exists elsewhere in the finite data: for yeast, the measured mediation fraction is $0.4793069656345167$ with $S_{QP}=0.3146818503437042$ and $S_{QT}=0.13681931343730436$; for E. coli, the corresponding fraction is $0.04091158340950673$ with $S_{QP}=0.10941535951828074$ and $S_{QT}=0.009394266754822262$; for human, the fraction is $0.0027966485036786744$ with $S_{QP}=0.04288464996165745$ and $S_{QT}=0.018837911344127926$; for danio, the raw fraction is negative and is recorded as $0.0$ after interval clipping.  These mediation numbers are useful only as a contrast class.  The miRNA seed-density chapter has not been given a same-row matrix joining seed density, translation efficiency, mRNA abundance, protein abundance, and target repression.  Consequently, a seed-density association may be reported as a repression readout, but it must not be rewritten as translation mediation, protein folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, organismal phenotype, mechanism, or a universal biological rule.

\paragraph{Four-case readback.}
For this chapter the four local cases are defined only over the miRNA table.  Case A is a seed-density-null row family: no measurable repression association is visible after the declared controls.  Case B is a repression-positive but translation-unresolved family: seed density predicts target repression, while no matched translation readout is available.  Case C is a protein-output-associated family without a surviving measured translation step.  Case D would require the strongest finite evidence: seed density survives against a measured translation or expression readout and the downstream protein signal is reduced after conditioning on that measured intermediate.  The current finite record supports at most the existence of the row surface needed to test these cases; it does not certify a Case D mechanism.

\paragraph{Local verdict.}
The chapter is ready as a bounded BioReality record because the primary miRNA run supplies a concrete finite table with $11$ microRNAs and $56682$ miRNA--target pairs.  The scientific claim should be phrased as a measured seed-density repression surface, not as a causal mechanism.  The coding-sequence residual facts show why this boundary matters: direct protein-omics and measured-TE associations can be computed on large matched panels, and measured mediation can be quantified when the required rows exist.  The miRNA packet has not yet supplied those joined translation and protein-abundance rows.  Therefore the admissible conclusion is finite and local: miRNA seed-density repression is a testable cross-layer relation on the recorded pair table, while translation, folding, physical admissibility, function, mechanism, and universality remain outside the claim until separate layer-matched contacts supply those measurements.
