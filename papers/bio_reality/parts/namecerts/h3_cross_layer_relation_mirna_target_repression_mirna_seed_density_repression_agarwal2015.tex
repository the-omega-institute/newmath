\subsection{Finite miRNA Seed-Density Repression Surface}
\label{sec:bioreality-mirna-seed-density-repression-agarwal2015}

\paragraph{Finite carrier.}
The miRNA target-repression record is a finite cross-layer surface whose rows are miRNA--target pairs and whose columns retain only the information needed to ask whether seed-density burden can be read as a repression-associated coordinate.  The attached computation contains $11$ miRNAs and $56{,}682$ miRNA--target rows, with the row construction completed in $1$ second under run $45f083dea984$.  Thus the evidence object is not a verbal association between miRNAs and repression; it is a finite matrix large enough to distinguish miRNA-specific row membership from a vacuous single-row example.

\paragraph{Coordinate being tested.}
For each row, the relevant coordinate is the seed-density side of the miRNA--target relation.  The biological readback is target repression in the Agarwal-style miRNA target setting, but the chapter only uses the registered finite-row count and the declared row type.  In particular, the claim is about a relation between miRNA seed burden and target repression, not about codon adaptation, ribosome occupancy, protein folding, organism fitness, or molecular function.  The finite data certify that the packet has a concrete row surface with many pairwise observations; they do not by themselves certify a mechanistic causal path.

\paragraph{Rank and row sanity.}
The nondegenerate facts are the observed row count $n_{pairs}=56{,}682$ and the miRNA count $n_{mirnas}=11$.  The matrix is therefore not a rank-degenerate $n=3$ boundary case and is not an all-entry artefact produced by too few biological units.  The basic row scale can be summarized as
$$
\begin{aligned}
  n_{mirnas} &= 11,\\
  n_{pairs} &= 56{,}682,\\
  n_{pairs}/n_{mirnas} &\approx 5{,}153.
\end{aligned}
$$
This ratio is not used as an effect-size estimate.  It is only a finite-row adequacy check showing that the surface contains thousands of candidate target relations per assayed miRNA on average.

\paragraph{Relation to translation and protein-abundance readouts.}
This miRNA surface is deliberately kept separate from the B$^*_{Q6}$ translation and protein-abundance matrices.  In the measured translation-efficiency record, the B$^*_{Q6}$ surface used $9$ residual coordinates and reported measured $S^{QT}$ values across four organisms: $0.04034217614654355$ in \emph{Danio rerio}, $0.009394266754822262$ in \emph{Escherichia coli} K12 MG1655, $0.018837911344127926$ in human, and $0.13681931343730436$ in yeast.  In the measured translation-conditioned protein-abundance decomposition, the same four-organism surface had measured mediated fractions $0.0$, $0.04091158340950673$, $0.0027966485036786744$, and $0.4793069656345167$, respectively.  Those numbers show how a translation-layer mediation claim is allowed only when a measured translation readout is present and conditioning is explicitly computed.  The miRNA seed-density surface has no such attached $q_i \to T_j \to P_k$ matrix in the present record, so it remains a repression-readout relation rather than a translation-mediated protein mechanism.

\paragraph{Protein-omics comparison boundary.}
The direct protein-omics survival computation is also not imported as a mechanism for this miRNA chapter.  That separate surface used $15$ organisms, $9$ B$^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinates, and Z-residualized protein-abundance rows; for example, it reported $R^2_{QP}=0.29837624994140827$ in yeast, $0.10767526677985896$ in \emph{E. coli}, and $0.04454565118019448$ in human.  Those direct protein-abundance associations were explicitly marked as cross-sectional protein-omics correlations, not causal translation mechanisms.  The miRNA seed-density result is therefore not promoted into a protein-output or translation statement merely because other cross-layer protein matrices exist in the same evidence family.

\paragraph{Readback classification.}
The finite miRNA record supports the following local classification.  First, the row surface is real enough for a bounded repression-readout chapter: $56{,}682$ pair rows over $11$ miRNAs are present.  Second, the available facts do not expose per-miRNA coefficients, confidence intervals, null permutations, repression-effect magnitudes, seed-site classes, transcript controls, or protein readouts.  Third, the chapter can state a finite seed-density repression surface, but it cannot state a measured translation-survival result, a protein-abundance mediation result, or a causal target-repression mechanism.  The admissible conclusion is therefore a bounded cross-layer relation: miRNA seed-density target rows have been instantiated at finite scale, and any stronger biological interpretation requires additional matched readout columns.

\paragraph{Cannot-claim boundary.}
No translation, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, causal mechanism, evolutionary directionality, phenotype, or universal biological rule follows from this surface alone.  A separate contact would be required for each promoted layer: ribosome profiling or equivalent translation-efficiency rows for translation, matched protein abundance for protein output, structural or physical assays for folding and admissibility, perturbation or rescue data for mechanism, and functional or phenotype assays for biological function.  Without those contacts, the miRNA seed-density chapter is a finite repression-coordinate record and nothing more.
