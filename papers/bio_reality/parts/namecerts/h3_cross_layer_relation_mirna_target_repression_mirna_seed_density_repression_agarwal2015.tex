\subsection{miRNA seed-density repression over a finite target table}\label{sec:bioreality-mirna-seed-density-repression-agarwal2015}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite surface.}
This record isolates a cross-layer repression question from the translation and protein-abundance route.  The finite data surface is a miRNA--target table rather than a codon-residual organism panel: the attached computation contains $11$ miRNAs and $56682$ miRNA--transcript pairs, with the registered run completing in $1$ second.  Thus the carrier for this chapter is the finite row set
$$
\begin{aligned}
  \mathcal{R}_{\mathrm{miR}} &= \{(m,g): m \text{ is one of } 11 \text{ miRNAs and } g \text{ is a target transcript row}\},\\
  |\mathcal{R}_{\mathrm{miR}}| &= 56682 .
\end{aligned}
$$
Each row is read as a bounded repression datum: a miRNA identity, a transcript or target identity, and a seed-density or target-context quantity usable for a finite repression readout.  The chapter does not identify this row surface with ribosome occupancy, codon adaptation, mature protein abundance, folded protein state, molecular function, phenotype, or evolutionary optimization.  It only registers that a large finite miRNA-target matrix exists for the Agarwal-style seed-density repression contact and can be inspected as a cross-layer regulatory readout.

\paragraph{Claim tested by the row surface.}
The scientific question is whether seed-density information supplies a measurable repression layer on matched transcript targets.  In the finite formulation, the admissible statistic is a relation between miRNA seed density and a repression readout on the same target rows.  If $D_{m,g}$ denotes the seed-density or target-site density assigned to miRNA $m$ and transcript $g$, and $Y_{m,g}$ denotes the attached repression readout, the bounded claim has the form
$$
\begin{aligned}
  D_{m,g} &\leadsto Y_{m,g}
  \quad\text{on the finite set }\mathcal{R}_{\mathrm{miR}},\\
  |\{m\}| &= 11,\qquad |\mathcal{R}_{\mathrm{miR}}| = 56682 .
\end{aligned}
$$
The arrow is statistical and row-local.  It is not a causal perturbation claim, and it is not a statement that any codon-residual coordinate creates repression.  The finite matrix is large enough to record a concrete regulatory readout surface, but its registered summary does not yet include per-miRNA coefficients, held-out losses, p-values, confidence intervals, or a list of surviving seed-context modules.  The correct paper use is therefore a bounded positive contact: the repression table is present and non-vacuous, while any stronger mechanistic statement requires additional row-level summaries.

\paragraph{Separation from codon-residual translation panels.}
The miRNA table is deliberately kept separate from the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ codon-residual route.  In the measured ribosome-TE survival panel, the row counts and survival statistics are organism-specific: Danio rerio has $13140$ genes with measured TE and $R^2_{QT,\mathrm{meas}}=0.04034217614654355$, Escherichia coli K12 MG1655 has $3415$ genes and $R^2_{QT,\mathrm{meas}}=0.009394266754822262$, Homo sapiens has $11554$ genes and $R^2_{QT,\mathrm{meas}}=0.018837911344127926$, and Saccharomyces cerevisiae has $5128$ genes and $R^2_{QT,\mathrm{meas}}=0.13681931343730436$.  Those numbers are translation-efficiency contacts, not miRNA repression entries.  They show why the present record must not promote a seed-density association into a translation mechanism.

The same separation holds for measured protein abundance.  Direct protein-omics survival has positive controlled $R^2_{QP}$ values on many organism panels, including $0.04454565118019448$ for Homo sapiens, $0.049827984062539195$ for Mus musculus, $0.10767526677985896$ for Escherichia coli K12 MG1655, and $0.29837624994140827$ for Saccharomyces cerevisiae, with $9$ surviving codon-residual coordinates in each listed organism.  These protein-abundance figures are useful as a boundary check: even a positive direct protein association is not a miRNA repression mechanism, and the miRNA seed-density table is not evidence for codon-mediated protein abundance unless the same rows, controls, and readout chain are explicitly joined.

\paragraph{Translation-conditioned contrast.}
The measured translation-mediated abundance run also supplies a useful non-promotion rule.  On the measured TE--protein-abundance panels, the mediated fractions are $0.0$ for Danio rerio after clipping a raw suppressive value, $0.04091158340950673$ for Escherichia coli K12 MG1655, $0.0027966485036786744$ for Homo sapiens, and $0.4793069656345167$ for Saccharomyces cerevisiae.  These values come from conditioning codon residuals on measured ribosome-derived TE before reading protein abundance.  The miRNA seed-density table does not contain that three-way $q_i \to T_j \to P_k$ structure.  It therefore cannot be used to assert translation mediation, protein-output mediation, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, phenotype, or universality.

\paragraph{Admissible conclusion.}
The finite conclusion is narrow but real: the Agarwal-style miRNA seed-density contact supplies a concrete matrix with $11$ miRNAs and $56682$ target-pair rows.  The chapter may state that miRNA seed density has an attached repression-readout surface suitable for finite row analysis.  It may not state that this surface explains translation efficiency, protein abundance, codon-residual survival, folded structure, physical admissibility, biological function, mechanism, causation, adaptation, or a general biological law.  Those stronger readings require separate matched contacts and a row-level statistic showing the relevant attenuation or mediation on the same units.
