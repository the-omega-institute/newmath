\subsection{miRNA Seed-Density Repression Surface}
\label{sec:bioreality-mirna-seed-density-repression-agarwal2015}

\paragraph{Finite row surface.}
This record isolates a finite miRNA target-repression surface from the surrounding translation and proteomics claims.  The registered computation for the seed-density repression surface has run identifier $45f083dea984$ and contains $11$ miRNAs, $56682$ miRNA--target pairs, and a recorded runtime of $1$ second.  The finite object is therefore not a verbal assertion about repression in general; it is a bounded matrix of paired rows whose immediate biological coordinates are miRNA identity, target identity, and seed-density contact against a repression readout.  In the notation of this chapter, let $m_a$ range over the $11$ miRNAs and let $g_b$ range over target transcripts appearing in the paired table.  The computed surface is a finite relation
$$
\begin{aligned}
R_{\mathrm{seed}} \subseteq \{m_1,\ldots,m_{11}\} \times \{g_b\} \times \mathbb{Q},
\end{aligned}
$$
where the rational coordinate records the seed-density side of the target-repression test.  The row count is fixed by the run:
$$
\begin{aligned}
|\{(m_a,g_b)\}| &= 56682,\\
|\{m_a\}| &= 11.
\end{aligned}
$$
These two numbers are the finite support of the chapter claim.

\paragraph{What is being asserted.}
The claim is a cross-layer relation only in the limited sense that a transcript-level regulatory annotation is being compared with a target-level repression readout.  The admissible statement is: within the finite Agarwal-style seed-density table, miRNA seed-density rows can be organized as a target-repression matrix over $56682$ paired entries.  This licenses a finite-row repression readback for the miRNA layer, not a claim about codon-mediated translation, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, organismal phenotype, or universal biological law.  The chapter is intentionally narrower than the larger $B^*_{Q6}$ translation-conditioned protein-mediation program.  It records a measured miRNA-target surface, while the translation-conditioned protein claims require separate codon, translation, mRNA, and protein rows.

\paragraph{Separation from translation readouts.}
The surrounding finite-row register contains a measured ribosome-TE survival computation for $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinates.  That separate computation used $9$ residual coordinates and reported measured-TE joins for four organisms: $13140$ genes in danio rerio with $R^2_{QT,\mathrm{meas}}=0.04034217614654355$, $3415$ genes in Escherichia coli K12 MG1655 with $R^2_{QT,\mathrm{meas}}=0.009394266754822262$, $11554$ genes in Homo sapiens with $R^2_{QT,\mathrm{meas}}=0.018837911344127926$, and $5128$ genes in Saccharomyces cerevisiae with $R^2_{QT,\mathrm{meas}}=0.13681931343730436$.  Those numbers are not evidence for the miRNA seed-density claim.  They are included here only to make the layer distinction explicit: a ribosome-footprint-over-mRNA TE readout is a translation-layer statistic, whereas the present $11$-miRNA and $56682$-pair matrix is a miRNA target-repression surface.

\paragraph{Separation from protein abundance and mediation.}
A second neighboring computation decomposes $B^*_{Q6}$ codon residual association with measured protein abundance by conditioning on measured ribo-seq TE.  Its measured mediation fractions were $0$ for danio rerio, $0.04091158340950673$ for Escherichia coli K12 MG1655, $0.0027966485036786744$ for Homo sapiens, and $0.4793069656345167$ for Saccharomyces cerevisiae.  The corresponding joined three-way row counts were $13140$, $3415$, $11554$, and $5128$.  These values show what a translation-conditioned protein mediation table must supply: same-row codon, translation, protein, and control readouts.  The miRNA seed-density table does not contain those same-row variables and is not promoted to a $q_i\to T_j\to P_k$ mechanism.  It remains a transcript-target repression table unless a later finite matrix joins the miRNA rows to translation and protein measurements under the same target units.

\paragraph{Direct protein-omics comparison.}
The direct protein-omics survival computation supplies another boundary.  It used $9$ $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinates and a positive partial-$R^2$ threshold of $10^{-12}$ against PAXdb protein abundance after length, amino-acid composition, GC3, and M-density controls.  Across $15$ organisms, the direct $R^2_{QP}$ values included $0.061906245886660105$ for Bacillus subtilis, $0.10767526677985896$ for Escherichia coli K12 MG1655, $0.04454565118019448$ for Homo sapiens, $0.049827984062539195$ for Mus musculus, and $0.29837624994140827$ for Saccharomyces cerevisiae.  The largest value in that listed set is the yeast direct protein-abundance association, but even there the computation was explicitly a direct omics correlation rather than a mechanism claim.  The present miRNA record is more restricted: it has $11$ miRNAs and $56682$ target pairs, but no joined PAXdb abundance surface, no ribosome TE conditioning variable, and no transcript-control mediation fit recorded in this finite row.

\paragraph{Local four-way reading.}
For this chapter the finite matrix supports the following four-way interpretation.  Case A is a row where seed-density information is present but no repression readout is attached; such a row is only a target annotation.  Case B is a row where repression readback is present but cannot be separated from target abundance, transcript context, or sequence-composition covariates; such a row is a bounded association.  Case C is a row where seed-density and repression agree but no translation or protein contact is present; such a row is a miRNA target-repression candidate, not a translation mechanism.  Case D would require the same target unit to carry seed-density, repression, mRNA control, translation readout, and protein abundance, with conditioning tests showing attenuation of a downstream signal.  The registered $45f083dea984$ computation reaches the finite seed-density surface but does not by itself enter Case D.

\paragraph{Cannot-claim boundary.}
No translation claim follows from the $11$-miRNA, $56682$-pair seed-density surface unless a separate measured translation contact is joined to the same target units.  No protein-abundance claim follows unless a separate matched protein contact is joined and controlled.  No folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, system phenotype, causal mechanism, evolutionary direction, or universal biological rule follows from the miRNA table.  The finite result is useful precisely because it is bounded: it records a concrete miRNA target-repression surface and keeps all higher layers outside the conclusion until their own rows and controls are present.
