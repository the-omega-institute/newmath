\subsection{miRNA seed-density repression as a finite cross-layer readout}\label{subsec:bioreality-mirna-seed-density-repression-agarwal2015}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite surface.}
This record isolates a measured post-transcriptional repression surface rather than a translation-rate, folding, or protein-function claim. The finite rows are indexed by a microRNA seed-density design and a matched target-repression readout derived from the Agarwal-style target-repression setting. The registered computation used $11$ microRNAs and $56{,}682$ microRNA--target rows, with runtime $1$ second, so the packet is not a symbolic assertion about the genetic code; it is a concrete finite matrix whose observed entries are miRNA seed-density features paired with repression readback rows.

The row object may be written as a finite family
$$
\begin{aligned}
  R &= \{(m,g,s_{mg},y_{mg}) : m \in M,\ g \in G_m\},\\
  |M| &= 11,\\
  |R| &= 56{,}682,
\end{aligned}
$$
where $m$ ranges over the assayed microRNAs, $g$ ranges over matched target transcripts or genes for that microRNA, $s_{mg}$ denotes the seed-density or target-site feature row, and $y_{mg}$ denotes the corresponding repression readout. The admissible claim is therefore row-level association between seed-density information and repression response on this finite surface. It is not a claim that a codon residual coordinate has been translated into protein abundance, nor that a protein-level mechanism has been resolved.

\paragraph{Layer separation.}
The miRNA surface sits beside, but does not merge with, the codon-residual translation and protein-abundance surfaces. In the measured ribosome-TE packet, four organisms had residualized measured-TE readouts after the same style of finite-rank control discipline: Danio rerio had $R^2_{QT,\mathrm{meas}}=0.04034217614654355$ with $13{,}140$ genes used, Escherichia coli K12 MG1655 had $R^2_{QT,\mathrm{meas}}=0.009394266754822262$ with $3{,}415$ genes used, Homo sapiens had $R^2_{QT,\mathrm{meas}}=0.018837911344127926$ with $11{,}554$ genes used, and Saccharomyces cerevisiae had $R^2_{QT,\mathrm{meas}}=0.13681931343730436$ with $5{,}128$ genes used. Those numbers are cited here only to mark the neighboring translation-readout scale. They do not make the miRNA seed-density rows into ribosome-occupancy rows.

Likewise, the direct protein-omics packet reports protein-abundance association after controls in a larger organism panel. Its strongest listed direct protein-abundance entry is Saccharomyces cerevisiae with $R^2_{QP}=0.29837624994140827$ and $5{,}815$ joined proteins, while Homo sapiens has $R^2_{QP}=0.04454565118019448$ and $19{,}053$ joined proteins. These direct protein-abundance values are not imported into the miRNA record as mechanism evidence. They show that this BioReality index can hold multiple finite row surfaces at different biological layers without allowing one layer's positive statistic to promote another layer's interpretation.

\paragraph{Admissible readback.}
For the present miRNA packet, the admissible readback is a finite seed-density repression table. A row can support a statement of the following form: after the row-level construction has assigned a seed-density feature $s_{mg}$ and a repression readout $y_{mg}$, the finite matrix can be inspected for whether seed-density variation tracks repression variation over the $56{,}682$ observed microRNA--target pairs. The computation records the row count and microRNA count, but it does not record an effect-size vector, a fitted coefficient list, a null distribution, or a right-tail probability for the miRNA run. Therefore this chapter states the registered finite contact and its layer boundary, not a stronger inferential statistic.

A compact version of the allowed statement is
$$
\begin{aligned}
  \mathrm{Surface}_{\mathrm{miRNA}} &= (M,R,s,y),\\
  |M| &= 11,\\
  |R| &= 56{,}682,\\
  \mathrm{claim} &= \text{seed-density repression readback on the finite row table.}
\end{aligned}
$$
This says that the observed object is a microRNA target-repression surface. It does not say that $s_{mg}$ is a codon-usage coordinate, a ribosome-profiling measurement, a protein-abundance measurement, a folding assay, a physical-admissibility assay, a molecular-function assay, or a universal biological rule.

\paragraph{Relation to translation-conditioned protein mediation.}
A separate measured mediation packet shows why this distinction matters. In the measured translation-conditioned protein-abundance surface, yeast had $S_{QP}=0.3146818503437042$, $S^{\mathrm{meas}}_{QT}=0.13681931343730436$, and measured mediation fraction $0.4793069656345167$ on a three-way join of $5{,}128$ rows; E. coli had $S_{QP}=0.10941535951828074$, $S^{\mathrm{meas}}_{QT}=0.009394266754822262$, and measured mediation fraction $0.04091158340950673$ on $3{,}415$ joined rows; Homo sapiens had $S_{QP}=0.04288464996165745$, $S^{\mathrm{meas}}_{QT}=0.018837911344127926$, and measured mediation fraction $0.0027966485036786744$ on $11{,}554$ joined rows; Danio rerio had $S_{QP}=0.04926578454614652$, $S^{\mathrm{meas}}_{QT}=0.04034217614654355$, and clipped measured mediation fraction $0.0$ on $13{,}140$ joined rows. Those mediation numbers require same-row translation and protein readouts. The miRNA seed-density table has its own same-row repression surface, but the supplied finite facts do not include a same-row ribosome-TE or protein-abundance mediation fit for the miRNA rows.

\paragraph{Local cannot-claim boundary.}
The chapter therefore admits the finite miRNA seed-density repression surface as ready for bounded reporting, while refusing any promotion beyond that surface. From $11$ microRNAs and $56{,}682$ microRNA--target pairs one may report a finite seed-density repression contact and use it as a row-level biological readback. One may not infer translation efficiency, ribosome dwell, codon-residual survival, protein abundance, folding state, physical admissibility, molecular function, organismal phenotype, causal mechanism, evolutionary direction, or universality unless an additional layer-matched contact supplies the corresponding rows and the corresponding controlled statistic. In particular, the presence of neighboring codon-residual translation and protein-omics records does not turn this miRNA repression packet into a translation-mediated protein mechanism. The packet is ready only in the narrower sense: its finite row surface is concrete, counted, and layer-scoped.
