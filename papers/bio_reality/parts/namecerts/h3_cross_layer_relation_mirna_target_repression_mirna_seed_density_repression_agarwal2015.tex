\subsection{miRNA Seed-Density Repression as a Separate Post-Transcriptional Surface}
\label{sec:bioreality-mirna-seed-density-repression-agarwal2015}

\paragraph{Finite carrier.}
This record isolates a post-transcriptional repression surface from the coding-sequence residual coordinates used elsewhere in the same finite program.  The observed table has $11$ miRNA rows and $56682$ miRNA--target pairs, with the registered run completing in $1$ second under experiment id $45f083dea984$.  Its carrier is therefore a finite relation table
$$
\begin{aligned}
  R_{\mathrm{miRNA}} \subseteq M \times G \times D \times Y,
  \qquad |M| = 11,\qquad |R_{\mathrm{miRNA}}| = 56682,
\end{aligned}
$$
where $M$ indexes the assayed miRNAs, $G$ indexes target transcripts or genes after identifier joining, $D$ records the seed-density or target-site readout attached to the pair, and $Y$ records the repression readout attached to the same pair.  The point of the construction is not to reinterpret a coding-region codon residual as a miRNA variable.  It is to place a measured miRNA target-repression relation beside the coding-sequence $B^{*}_{Q6}$ surfaces and ask which explanatory layer is actually being contacted by the finite rows.

\paragraph{Readback.}
Each row is read as a pair-level repression observation: a miRNA identity, a matched target identity, a seed-density or target-site descriptor, and a repression quantity.  The only positive finite statement supplied by this chapter is that the pair table exists at the stated size.  In symbols, the chapter certifies the availability of a bounded table
$$
\begin{aligned}
  (m,g,d,y) \in R_{\mathrm{miRNA}},
  \qquad m \in M,
  \qquad |M|=11,
  \qquad |R_{\mathrm{miRNA}}|=56682.
\end{aligned}
$$
This is a different readout family from codon usage, modeled adaptation weights, ribosome-profiling translation efficiency, PAXdb protein abundance, or mRNA-stability controls.  A row in $R_{\mathrm{miRNA}}$ can support a miRNA-target repression statement only at the level of the assayed pair.  It does not by itself establish translation-rate mediation, ribosome dwell, folded protein state, physical admissibility, molecular function, phenotype, causal mechanism, or universality.

\paragraph{Relation to the coding residual surfaces.}
The surrounding finite program contains several coding-sequence readout surfaces that are useful here as scope controls.  The direct protein-omics surface computed $9$ $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates and found positive direct $Q\to P$ association after controls in $15$ organisms, with all $9$ coordinates surviving in every listed organism.  The registered $R^2_{QP}$ values include $0.10767526677985896$ for \emph{Escherichia coli} K12 MG1655, $0.29837624994140827$ for \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}, $0.04454565118019448$ for human, and $0.019256877907572044$ for \emph{Halobacterium salinarum}.  Those entries show that coding-sequence residuals can predict measured protein abundance, but the same run explicitly treats that as a direct omics association rather than a mechanism claim.

\paragraph{Measured translation comparison.}
A separate measured-ribosome translation-efficiency surface gives a sharper boundary.  There the same $9$ residual coordinates were tested against log translation efficiency, with measured TE defined as ribosome footprint divided by mRNA.  The powered measured-TE entries were
$$
\begin{aligned}
  R^2_{QT}(\mathrm{danio}) &= 0.04034217614654355,\\
  R^2_{QT}(\mathrm{E.\ coli}) &= 0.009394266754822262,\\
  R^2_{QT}(\mathrm{human}) &= 0.018837911344127926,\\
  R^2_{QT}(\mathrm{yeast}) &= 0.13681931343730436,
\end{aligned}
$$
again with $9$ surviving coordinates in each of the four measured-TE organisms.  These values are not miRNA repression values.  They demonstrate that the finite system distinguishes a translation-efficiency readout from a post-transcriptional miRNA-target readout.  Therefore the miRNA table cannot be silently promoted to translation realization merely because it is downstream of RNA sequence recognition.

\paragraph{Mediation check.}
The measured $Q\to T\to P$ decomposition further separates direct association from mediated association.  With measured TE as $T$ and protein abundance as $P$, the registered mediated fractions were $0.0$ for danio, $0.04091158340950673$ for \emph{E. coli}, $0.0027966485036786744$ for human, and $0.4793069656345167$ for yeast.  In the same finite family the measured $S_{QP}$ values on the matched three-way joins were $0.04926578454614652$, $0.10941535951828074$, $0.04288464996165745$, and $0.3146818503437042$, respectively.  These numbers matter because they exhibit the criterion that this chapter also uses: a direct downstream association is not a mechanism unless the measured intermediate is present and the conditioning step changes the downstream association in the declared direction.

\paragraph{Joint covariate boundary.}
The finite program also contains a joint measured-TE and mRNA-stability partition for three organisms.  Its joint mediation fractions were $0.2404669586626661$ for \emph{E. coli}, $0.16890712099923358$ for human, and $0.5195182225621592$ for yeast, each with permutation right-tail value $0.000999000999000999$.  The corresponding single-mediator components were TE-only $0.01975521340963268$ and stability-only $0.23052056324898387$ in \emph{E. coli}, TE-only $0.00549490864190343$ and stability-only $0.15314679870183337$ in human, and TE-only $0.4697188319693239$ and stability-only $0.0626618595344251$ in yeast.  These values show that RNA-layer and translation-layer variables can be separated in a finite design when the required rows are present.  The miRNA table should be read in the same disciplined way: it is a repression surface at the miRNA--target layer, not evidence for a coding-residual translation channel unless a same-row bridge to translation and protein readouts is supplied.

\paragraph{Local four-case interpretation.}
For this chapter, the relevant local cases are defined over the finite pair table and any future matched coding or protein readouts.  Case A is a null pair surface: seed density is present in the table but does not improve repression prediction after the declared controls.  Case B is a miRNA-specific repression surface: seed density predicts repression in $R_{\mathrm{miRNA}}$ but has no matched evidence of changing translation efficiency or protein abundance.  Case C is a direct downstream association: the miRNA variable is associated with a protein or abundance readout, but the matched translation or mRNA intermediate does not absorb the association.  Case D is the only mediated case: the same finite target rows show seed-density repression, a surviving intermediate RNA or translation readout, and attenuation of the downstream association after conditioning on that intermediate.  The present finite facts certify the pair-table size and therefore support only the existence of the miRNA repression surface, not a Case D mechanism.

\paragraph{Cannot-claim boundary.}
No conclusion about translation, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, phenotype, causation, mechanism, adaptation, or universality follows from the $11$-miRNA, $56682$-pair surface alone.  A separate layer-matched contact must provide the missing rows before any such statement is admissible.  In particular, a miRNA seed-density association cannot be used as a substitute for ribosome profiling, protein abundance, protein localization, protein turnover, structural measurement, functional assay, perturbation, rescue, or cross-condition causal evidence.  The finite chapter is therefore ready as a bounded miRNA target-repression record: it contributes a concrete pair-level repression surface and keeps it distinct from the coding-residual translation and protein-abundance surfaces whose finite statistics are listed above.
