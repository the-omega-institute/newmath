\subsection{miRNA seed-density repression as a separated post-transcriptional readout}
\label{sec:bioreality-mirna-seed-density-repression-agarwal2015}

\paragraph{Finite contact surface.}
The miRNA seed-density record attaches a bounded post-transcriptional repression surface to the cross-layer catalogue.  The finite run identified $11$ miRNAs and evaluated $56{,}682$ miRNA--target pairs in $1$ second.  These are the rows actually present for this contact: they support a finite target-repression readout keyed by miRNA seed-density and target-pair identity, not a translation-efficiency, protein-folding, physical-admissibility, molecular-function, mechanism, or universal-law conclusion.  The finite surface can be written as a row matrix
$$
\begin{aligned}
R_{\mathrm{miR}} &= \{(m,g): m \text{ is one of } 11 \text{ miRNAs and } g \text{ is a tested target row}\},\\
|R_{\mathrm{miR}}| &= 56{,}682,\\
\tau_{\mathrm{run}} &= 1\ \mathrm{s}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The intended readback is therefore a seed-density repression screen over a finite pair table.  It is not, by itself, a codon-usage survival matrix, a ribosome-profiling experiment, or a protein-abundance mediation fit.

\paragraph{Separation from the codon residual route.}
The surrounding cross-layer record contains a second family of finite matrices over $B^*_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates.  Those matrices use $9$ coordinates: $K\_AAA$, $Arg\_AGR$, $Ile\_AUA$, $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$, $Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG$, $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$, $Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG$, $Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY$, and $f3\_stress$.  The miRNA contact does not add a tenth codon coordinate and does not replace the measured translation readouts.  Its role is narrower: it records whether a finite target-repression layer can be inspected beside translation and protein-abundance layers without collapsing those layers into one another.

\paragraph{Measured translation comparison.}
The measured ribosome-TE contact supplies the translation-side comparison surface.  In that run the residualized $B^*_{Q6}$ design kept rank $9$ after the shared controls, and the measured-TE associations were positive in four organism panels: $R^2_{QT}=0.04034217614654355$ for danio rerio over $13{,}140$ genes, $0.009394266754822262$ for E. coli over $3{,}415$ genes, $0.018837911344127926$ for human over $11{,}554$ genes, and $0.13681931343730436$ for yeast over $5{,}128$ genes.  The corresponding ribosome-TE join hit rates were $0.9118667591950035$, $0.9133458143888741$, $0.6064136881331024$, and $0.8818572656921754$.  These numbers are translation-readout facts; they do not become miRNA repression facts, and the miRNA table does not become evidence for ribosome occupancy.

\paragraph{Protein-abundance mediation comparison.}
The measured protein-abundance mediation run gives the protein-side contrast.  The same $9$ codon residual coordinates were evaluated against measured TE and local protein abundance.  The mediated fractions were $0$ for danio rerio, $0.04091158340950673$ for E. coli, $0.0027966485036786744$ for human, and $0.4793069656345167$ for yeast.  The paired survival values were $S_{QP}=0.04926578454614652$ and $S_{QT}=0.04034217614654355$ in danio, $S_{QP}=0.10941535951828074$ and $S_{QT}=0.009394266754822262$ in E. coli, $S_{QP}=0.04288464996165745$ and $S_{QT}=0.018837911344127926$ in human, and $S_{QP}=0.3146818503437042$ and $S_{QT}=0.13681931343730436$ in yeast.  These values show why the miRNA seed-density contact must remain a distinct target-repression layer: a direct $q_i \to P_k$ signal is not a repression mechanism, and a miRNA-pair table is not a translation-conditioned protein mediation proof.

\paragraph{Joint mediator context.}
The joint measured-TE and mRNA-stability surface supplies another guardrail.  With $T$ denoting measured TE and $M$ denoting mRNA stability, the joint mediation fractions were $0.2404669586626661$ for E. coli, $0.16890712099923358$ for human, and $0.5195182225621592$ for yeast, each with right-tail permutation value $0.000999000999000999$.  The single-mediator fractions were also finite and organism-specific: in E. coli, $T$ alone contributed $0.01975521340963268$ and $M$ alone $0.23052056324898387$; in human, $T$ alone contributed $0.00549490864190343$ and $M$ alone $0.15314679870183337$; in yeast, $T$ alone contributed $0.4697188319693239$ and $M$ alone $0.0626618595344251$.  These rows are useful because miRNA target repression is also post-transcriptional, but they do not identify the miRNA contact as an mRNA-stability mediator unless the same rows, identifiers, and controls are joined and tested.

\paragraph{Dictionary and dwell controls.}
The dictionary-compression contact reports $\lambda_{\mathrm{DL}}=0.01$, $\rho_{\mathrm{join}}=0.05$, $n_{\mathrm{join}}=1800$, $5$ folds, $120$ permutations, coverage $C^*=0.24538211439730545$, selection-aware null $95\%$ value $0.029149007242956115$, and unexplained fraction $0.7546178856026946$.  The $f3\_stress$ dwell mediation contact reports $n_{\mathrm{join}}=1257$, base $R^2=0.1855578757644517$, full $R^2=0.20412261520502883$, held-out $\Delta R^2=0.01856473944057712$, $f3$--dwell correlation $0.1615441450901653$, approximate $p=7.873916057196935\times 10^{-9}$, and null $95\%$ value $0.0013115362189692937$.  These scalar controls show that other post-transcriptional or dwell-like readouts can be treated as finite measured surfaces with explicit nulls.  The miRNA contact should be read in the same disciplined way: $11$ miRNAs and $56{,}682$ tested pairs are a concrete finite surface, but without same-row mediation or perturbation they are not a causal mechanism.

\paragraph{Four-case placement.}
The four-case translation-conditioned discriminant supplies a local classification grammar for codon residuals.  In E. coli the finite classifier placed $7$ coordinates in case $D$ and recorded $2$ positive-positive coordinates without a mediation drop; in yeast it placed all $9$ coordinates in case $D$.  Both panels had no unclassified coordinates, and the classifier threshold was $10^{-12}$.  The miRNA seed-density surface is not automatically one of these four cases, because its rows are miRNA--target pairs rather than the same $q_i,T_j,P_k$ triples.  It can enter such a grammar only after a same-row join specifies whether seed-density repression is being used as a target-layer readout, a covariate, or a candidate mediator.

\paragraph{Local verdict.}
The finite Agarwal-style miRNA seed-density record is ready as a bounded BioReality chapter because it has a concrete pair matrix: $11$ miRNAs, $56{,}682$ miRNA--target pairs, and a completed run time of $1$ second.  Its positive content is modest and precise: it licenses discussion of a finite target-repression readout next to translation, mRNA-stability, dwell, and protein-abundance readouts.  It does not license translation realization, folded-structure inference, physical admissibility, biological function, causal mechanism, evolutionary direction, or universality.  Any such higher claim requires an additional contact with matched rows and an explicit statistic showing the relevant reduction, attenuation, or perturbation effect under the declared controls.
