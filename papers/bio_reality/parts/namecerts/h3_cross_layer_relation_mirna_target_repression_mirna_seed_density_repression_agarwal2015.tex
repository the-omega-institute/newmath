\subsection{miRNA seed-density repression as a bounded cross-layer relation}\label{sec:bioreality-mirna-seed-density-repression-agarwal2015}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite row surface.}
The miRNA target-repression record is a finite matrix surface whose rows are transcript--miRNA pairs and whose columns record a seed-density repression readout drawn from the Agarwal-style target context.  The computed surface contains $11$ miRNAs and $56{,}682$ transcript--miRNA pairs, and the registered run completed in $1$ second under experiment run $45f083dea984$.  This is enough to certify that the chapter is not describing an empty contact: the row set has a concrete cardinality, a concrete miRNA panel size, and a concrete completed computation.  The finite object is therefore the array
$$
\begin{aligned}
R_{\mu,g} &= \text{seed-density and repression-context entry for miRNA } \mu \text{ and transcript } g,\\
|\{\mu\}| &= 11,\\
|\{(\mu,g)\}| &= 56{,}682.
\end{aligned}
$$
The relevant biological layer is not translation efficiency, protein abundance, folding, localization, or phenotype.  It is a transcript-level post-transcriptional targeting surface: the rows say that seed-density information has been assembled for many candidate miRNA--transcript pairs, while the finite count prevents the statement from floating free of row support.

\paragraph{Local readback.}
For each row $(\mu,g)$, the admissible readback is a bounded statement about miRNA seed-density repression evidence.  A positive row may be used as evidence that the transcript belongs to the finite target-repression surface for that miRNA; it may not be promoted to a claim about ribosome occupancy, codon-mediated translation rate, protein amount, protein state, molecular function, or organism-level outcome.  The row type is therefore distinct from the $B^*_{Q6}$ codon-residual panels used elsewhere in the same research program.  Those panels use nine residual coordinates such as $K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $Leu_{CUN\,vs\,UUR}$, $Ser_{UCR\,vs\,AGY}$, $Thr_{ACR\,vs\,ACY}$, and $f_3$-stress, whereas the present surface is indexed by miRNA identity and transcript target rows.

\paragraph{Separation from measured translation.}
The finite miRNA contact is compared locally against measured translation readouts only to mark the boundary.  In the measured ribosome-TE survival runs, the $B^*_{Q6}$ residual panel had positive controlled translation-readout fits in four organisms: $R^2_{QT,\mathrm{meas}}=0.04034217614654355$ for danio, $0.009394266754822262$ for \textit{E. coli} K12 MG1655, $0.018837911344127926$ for human, and $0.13681931343730436$ for yeast, with all nine residual coordinates surviving in each listed organism.  Those numbers are translation-readout statistics, not miRNA repression statistics.  Conversely, the $56{,}682$ miRNA-pair surface is not a measured TE matrix, does not contain ribosome-footprint denominators, and does not establish a $q_i\to T_j$ path.  The two row types must remain separate unless an additional same-transcript joined panel explicitly contains both miRNA-target rows and translation-efficiency rows.

\paragraph{Separation from protein abundance.}
The protein-abundance boundary is likewise finite and explicit.  Direct $B^*_{Q6}\to P$ protein-omics survival was computed on PAXdb abundance after length, amino-acid composition, GC3, and $M$-density controls; examples include $R^2_{QP}=0.10767526677985896$ for \textit{E. coli}, $0.04454565118019448$ for human, $0.049827984062539195$ for mouse, and $0.29837624994140827$ for yeast, again with nine surviving residual coordinates in those organism-specific protein-abundance fits.  These protein-omics numbers do not validate a miRNA mechanism.  They only show that a different row surface can be attached to a measured protein readout when the identifiers, controls, and abundance columns are present.  The miRNA seed-density matrix here lacks matched protein-abundance rows, matched translation rows, and a mediation fit tying a miRNA row to a downstream protein row.  Hence a seed-density entry may support transcript-level repression contact, but not protein-output realization.

\paragraph{Mediation boundary.}
A mechanism claim would require a joined chain in which the miRNA-target surface, transcript abundance, translation readout, and protein abundance are all observed on the same units and the conditioning step is evaluated directly.  The available $B^*_{Q6}$ measured-mediation runs illustrate the required shape: measured mediation fractions were $0.0$ in danio, $0.04091158340950673$ in \textit{E. coli}, $0.0027966485036786744$ in human, and $0.4793069656345167$ in yeast for codon residuals conditioned on measured ribosome TE before protein abundance.  The joint TE--mRNA-stability partition also reports organism-specific joint mediation fractions of $0.2404669586626661$ in \textit{E. coli}, $0.16890712099923358$ in human, and $0.5195182225621592$ in yeast, each with right-tail permutation value $0.000999000999000999$.  Those statistics demonstrate the kind of finite conditioning calculation needed before a downstream mechanism is asserted.  The present miRNA seed-density record has not supplied such a joined mediation table; it supplies $11$ miRNAs and $56{,}682$ target-pair rows.

\paragraph{Admissible conclusion.}
The chapter therefore certifies a bounded transcript-level contact: a finite Agarwal-style miRNA seed-density repression surface exists at the scale of $11$ miRNAs and $56{,}682$ transcript--miRNA pairs.  The admissible mathematical object is the row-indexed relation $R\subseteq M_{\mathrm{miRNA}}\times G_{\mathrm{transcript}}\times C_{\mathrm{seed}}$, together with the readback that a row belongs to a computed miRNA target-repression surface.  The finite-row fact supports inclusion in the BioReality cross-layer catalogue as a transcript-regulatory relation, but it does not, by itself, say that translation changes, ribosomes move differently, proteins change abundance, proteins fold differently, physical admissibility is established, biological function is realized, a causal mechanism is proven, or a universal rule holds.  Any such stronger claim requires a separate layer-matched contact with its own rows, controls, and attenuation or perturbation statistic.
