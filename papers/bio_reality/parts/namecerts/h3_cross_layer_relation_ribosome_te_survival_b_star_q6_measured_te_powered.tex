\subsection{Measured ribosome-TE survival of the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual surface}\label{sec:bioreality-b-star-q6-measured-te-survival}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite surface.}
This record concerns a finite matrix whose rows are coding sequences with organism-matched codon counts and whose columns contain nine $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates, one measured translation-efficiency readout, and the covariates used to remove ordinary composition effects.  The nine coordinates are $K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $Leu_{CUN\,vs\,UUR}$, $Leu_{UUA\,vs\,UUG}$, $Ser_{UCR\,vs\,AGY}$, $Ser_{UCA\,vs\,UCG}$, $Thr_{ACR\,vs\,ACY}$, and $f_3$-stress.  The measured readout is $\log_{10}(TE)$, with $TE$ computed in the source rows as ribosome footprint divided by matched mRNA.  Thus the translation readout is already mRNA-normalized at construction, and the fitted survival is not a raw transcript-abundance effect.

\paragraph{Controls and rank.}
For each organism, the $Q$ design is residualized against a control block containing length, amino-acid composition, $GC3$, and an $M$-density baseline.  The control block has $24$ columns and rank $23$ in every fitted organism.  The residual $Q$ block has rank $9$ in each case, so the nine-coordinate surface is not a rank-collapsed artifact.  The residual degrees of freedom are $13117$ for \emph{Danio rerio}, $3392$ for \emph{Escherichia coli} K12 MG1655, $11531$ for \emph{Homo sapiens}, and $5105$ for \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}.  The corresponding residual TE energies are $3588.378859021949$, $919.4854026798789$, $2052.594706241978$, and $753.5781168800304$.  The positive threshold used for partial-$R^2$ survival is $10^{-12}$, so the reported positive entries are far above a numerical-zero convention.

\paragraph{Joined translation rows.}
The measured ribosome-TE contact is present on four organism-matched panels.  In \emph{Danio rerio}, $13140$ genes with TE are joined to a CDS panel reporting $14410$ joined CDS rows, giving a TE join hit rate of $0.9118667591950035$.  In \emph{E. coli} K12 MG1655, $3415$ TE genes are joined against $3739$ reported CDS rows, with hit rate $0.9133458143888741$.  In human, $11554$ TE genes are joined against $19053$ reported CDS rows, with hit rate $0.6064136881331024$.  In budding yeast, $5128$ TE genes are joined against $5815$ reported CDS rows, with hit rate $0.8818572656921754$.  These are not three-organism saturated rows: the panel has four measured TE organisms, each with thousands of sequence-matched records and full rank in the residual $Q$ block.

\paragraph{Measured TE survival.}
After the stated controls, the nine-coordinate $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ block has positive measured-TE survival in every organism in this fitted panel.  The aggregate measured translation-efficiency partial-$R^2$ values are
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{QT,meas}(\mathrm{danio}) &= 0.04034217614654355,\\
R^2_{QT,meas}(\mathrm{ecoli}) &= 0.009394266754822262,\\
R^2_{QT,meas}(\mathrm{human}) &= 0.018837911344127926,\\
R^2_{QT,meas}(\mathrm{yeast}) &= 0.13681931343730436.
\end{aligned}
$$
In each organism, all nine residual coordinates have positive survival against the measured TE readout under the finite-row criterion used here.  The strongest measured TE association in this set is yeast, with $R^2_{QT,meas}=0.13681931343730436$; the weakest is \emph{E. coli}, with $R^2_{QT,meas}=0.009394266754822262$.  The result is therefore a measured translation-readout survival statement for the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ coordinate surface, not merely a modeled tAI or stAI readback.

\paragraph{Relation to protein abundance.}
The same coordinate family also has direct protein-abundance survival in a broader PAXdb panel.  For example, the direct $Q\to P$ partial-$R^2$ values include $0.10767526677985896$ in \emph{E. coli}, $0.04454565118019448$ in human, $0.29837624994140827$ in yeast, and $0.04613529807085213$ in \emph{Danio rerio}; all nine coordinates survive in those direct abundance fits.  That direct protein-omics fact is not itself a mechanism.  When the measured TE readout is inserted explicitly into the $Q\to T\to P$ decomposition, the measured mediated fractions are $0.04091158340950673$ for \emph{E. coli}, $0.0027966485036786744$ for human, and $0.4793069656345167$ for yeast, while \emph{Danio rerio} has raw suppression $-0.04801486048721136$ and is clipped to measured mediated fraction $0.0$.  The corresponding measured $S_{QP}$ values in that three-way fit are $0.10941535951828074$, $0.04288464996165745$, $0.3146818503437042$, and $0.04926578454614652$ for \emph{E. coli}, human, yeast, and \emph{Danio rerio}.  Thus the present TE-survival result supplies the required positive $Q\to T$ leg, while the downstream interpretation remains organism-specific and depends on whether conditioning on measured TE reduces the $Q\to P$ association.

In \emph{E. coli}, the corresponding values are $0.1181743523064932$, $0.11583979275713331$, $0.09093273405121653$, and $0.08975732521542036$, with joint fraction $0.2404669586626661$ and the same right-tail permutation value.  In human, they are $0.04274178488511215$, $0.04250692268197657$, $0.036196017359154814$, and $0.0355223930537993$, with joint fraction $0.16890712099923358$ and right-tail permutation value $0.000999000999000999$.  These values support the narrower reading: measured TE can carry part of the $Q\to P$ association, but the amount varies by organism and is not a universal mechanism constant.

\paragraph{Residual readout dictionary.}
The yeast residual is only partly covered by a small readout dictionary.  In the partial-mediation certificate, the active yeast join has $n=1800$ from $4758$ available rows, five folds, $\lambda_{DL}=0.01$, and $\rho_{join}=0.05$.  The best selection-aware readout coverage is $C^{\ast}=0.24538211439730545$, with unexplained fraction $0.7546178856026946$; the selection-aware null $95$th percentile is $0.029149007242956115$, while the naive fixed-subset null $95$th percentile is $0.00598374993486675$.  This places the measured TE survival in a broader finite-row pattern: the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ coordinates are not null at the translation readout, but a large residual component remains after the listed measured readouts.

\paragraph{Position and structure-adjacent boundaries.}
For the $f_3$-stress coordinate in yeast, a dwell-mediated abundance component has $n_{join}=1257$, base $n=1800$, five folds, $R^2_{base}=0.1855578757644517$, $R^2_{full}=0.20412261520502883$, held-out $\Delta R^2=0.01856473944057712$, null mean $-0.0032911642620130563$, null $95$th percentile $0.0013115362189692937$, and approximate $p=7.873916057196935\times 10^{-9}$ for the $f_3$ dwell correlation.  The positional version classifies the leading segment as $5'$ and reports $five\_prime\_ramp\_passed=true$ and $three\_prime\_body\_passed=false$.  A separate structural-order ramp probe has status $passed$ but conclusion $ramp\_positive\_but\_not\_localized$: for the primary $50$-codon ramp, the weighted ramp increment is $0.0011165640103326878$, the body increment is $-0.00026157313923957926$, the paired ramp-minus-body increment is $0.001378137149572267$, and the paired sign-flip value is $0.05859375$.  These numbers may guide later biological targeting, but they do not turn measured TE survival into folding, localization, or physical-admissibility evidence.

\paragraph{Local verdict.}
The finite-row verdict is ready for the translation-readout layer: a rank-powered, organism-matched measured ribosome-TE panel exists, the $Q$ residual block has rank $9$ after controls, and all four measured-TE organisms show positive $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ survival against $\log_{10}(TE)$.  The result should be read as a controlled association from synonymous residual coordinates to an mRNA-normalized ribosome-profiling TE readout.  It is not a claim of universal translation efficiency, elongation speed, protein abundance mechanism, folding order, physical admissibility, molecular function, phenotype, causality, or a biological law.  Any such higher-layer conclusion requires a separate layer-matched contact and its own controlled statistic; direct $Q\to P$ survival is admissible only as direct protein-omics prediction unless the measured $Q\to T$ leg survives and conditioning on $T$ removes or reduces the corresponding $Q\to P$ signal.
