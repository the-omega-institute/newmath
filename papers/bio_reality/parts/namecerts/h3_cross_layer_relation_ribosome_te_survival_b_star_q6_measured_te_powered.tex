\subsection{Measured ribosome-efficiency survival of the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual panel}
\label{sec:bireality-q6-ribosome-te-survival}

\paragraph{Finite contact.}
This record tests a bounded cross-layer question: whether the finite $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ synonymous-residual coordinate panel retains measurable association with ribosome-profiling translation efficiency after the usual sequence-composition explanations have been removed.  The response variable is $\log_{10}(\mathrm{TE})$, with $\mathrm{TE}$ computed in the local organism files as ribosome footprint divided by matched mRNA abundance.  Thus the readout is already mRNA-normalized at source, and the fitted statistic asks only whether codon-residual coordinates still explain part of the measured TE variation after control residualization.  The nine tested coordinates are $K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $Leu_{CUN\,vs\,UUR}$, $Leu_{UUA\,vs\,UUG}$, $Ser_{UCR\,vs\,AGY}$, $Ser_{UCA\,vs\,UCG}$, $Thr_{ACR\,vs\,ACY}$, and $f3_{stress}$.  The coordinate source is the imported $q$-vector table from the powered $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ translation-survival computation, so the contact is a finite CDS-by-coordinate matrix crossed with organism-matched ribosome TE rows.

\paragraph{Controlled model.}
For each organism, the residual test uses amino-acid composition, organism identity at the panel level, GC3, CDS length, M-density, and matched mRNA normalization as separating controls before the $Q6$ residual panel is scored.  In the computed files this appears as a residualized design with $24$ control columns, control rank $23$, $9$ residual coordinates, and full residual coordinate rank $9$ in every organism.  The rank tolerance is $10^{-10}$, and a fit with degenerate $R^{2}$ at or above $0.999999$ is treated as saturated rather than as evidence.  The positivity threshold for the measured partial-$R^{2}$ entry is $10^{-12}$, so the reported survival calls are not rank-saturated all-one artifacts.

$$
\begin{aligned}
Z &= \{\text{amino-acid composition},\ \mathrm{GC3},\ \text{CDS length},\ M\text{-density},\ \text{matched mRNA normalization}\},\\
Y_o &= \log_{10}(\mathrm{RPF}_o/\mathrm{mRNA}_o),\\
S^{QT,\mathrm{meas}}_o &= R^{2}\bigl(Y_o \sim \widetilde{Q}_{6,o}\mid Z_o\bigr),
\end{aligned}
$$

where $o$ ranges over the four measured organisms and $\widetilde{Q}_{6,o}$ denotes the nine-coordinate residual panel after the controls have been projected out.

\paragraph{Joined measured rows.}
The ribosome-TE join is finite and organism-specific.  In \textit{Danio rerio}, $14{,}410$ CDS entries were reported in the joined codon table, $13{,}140$ genes had TE values and were used, and the join hit rate was $0.9118667591950035$.  In \textit{Escherichia coli} K-12 MG1655, $3{,}739$ CDS entries were reported, $3{,}415$ genes were used, and the hit rate was $0.9133458143888741$.  In \textit{Homo sapiens}, $19{,}053$ CDS entries were reported, $11{,}554$ genes were used, and the hit rate was $0.6064136881331024$.  In \textit{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}, $5{,}815$ CDS entries were reported, $5{,}128$ genes were used, and the hit rate was $0.8818572656921754$.  These counts make the contact a measured row table rather than a three-row or rank-saturated demonstration.

\paragraph{Rank and residual energy.}
All four organism designs pass the powered-rank check.  For \textit{Danio rerio}, the residual degrees of freedom after $Z$ are $13{,}117$, with response residual energy $3588.378859021949$ and explained energy $144.76301201119642$.  For \textit{E. coli} K-12 MG1655, the residual degrees of freedom are $3{,}392$, with response residual energy $919.4854026798789$ and explained energy $8.637891149939946$.  For \textit{Homo sapiens}, the residual degrees of freedom are $11{,}531$, with response residual energy $2052.594706241978$ and explained energy $38.66659710161269$.  For \textit{S. cerevisiae}, the residual degrees of freedom are $5{,}105$, with response residual energy $753.5781168800304$ and explained energy $103.10404057290246$.  Since $\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde{Q}_{6})=9$ throughout, no organism is being counted by a collapsed or underpowered residual coordinate surface.

\paragraph{Measured survival values.}
The measured TE survival statistic is positive in each organism and all nine residual coordinates survive the stated positivity screen in each case.  The observed partial-$R^{2}$ values are

$$
\begin{aligned}
S^{QT,\mathrm{meas}}_{\textit{D. rerio}} &= 0.04034217614654355,\\
S^{QT,\mathrm{meas}}_{\textit{E. coli K12}} &= 0.009394266754822262,\\
S^{QT,\mathrm{meas}}_{\textit{H. sapiens}} &= 0.018837911344127926,\\
S^{QT,\mathrm{meas}}_{\textit{S. cerevisiae}} &= 0.13681931343730436.
\end{aligned}
$$

The strongest measured panel-level association in this run is the yeast contact, with partial $R^{2}=0.13681931343730436$; the weakest is the \textit{E. coli} contact, with partial $R^{2}=0.009394266754822262$.  The finite conclusion is therefore not that a universal biological law has been found, but that the same nine-coordinate $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual panel remains non-vacuous against measured, mRNA-normalized TE in all four powered organism tables.

\paragraph{Local interpretation.}
The admissible reading is narrow.  Positive $S^{QT,\mathrm{meas}}$ values mean that the synonymous-residual coordinate panel improves the measured TE fit after the declared controls.  They are codon-usage to measured-TE associations under residualization, not mechanistic derivations of elongation, initiation, ribosome traffic, protein production, or organismal outcome.  Agreement or disagreement with modeled tAI or stAI weights is only coordinate-pattern evidence; it is not a causal validation or falsification of those models.  The measured TE files are condition-specific and cross-sectional, and they come from one or a small number of ribosome-profiling studies per organism.

\paragraph{No upward promotion.}
This chapter does not infer protein abundance, folding, structural order, physical admissibility, molecular function, phenotype, mechanism, or universality.  It also does not infer a general translation law beyond the measured $\log_{10}(\mathrm{RPF}/\mathrm{mRNA})$ contact used here.  Any claim at those higher or different layers would require its own matched contact and its own controlled statistic.  The present result only moves the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual panel across the measured TE boundary for this finite organism set, under the stated controls, ranks, thresholds, row counts, and residual energies.
