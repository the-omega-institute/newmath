\subsection{Measured ribosome--mRNA translation-efficiency contact for $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residuals}
\label{sec:bior-b-star-q6-ribosome-te-survival}

\paragraph{Finite carrier and readout.}
This record tests whether the nine $B^{*}_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates leave a measurable trace in matched ribosome-profiling translation-efficiency rows after the amino-acid quotient and after transcript abundance has been separated from the readout.  The finite coordinate list is $K\_AAA$, $Arg\_AGR$, $Ile\_AUA$, $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$, $Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG$, $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$, $Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG$, $Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY$, and $f3\_stress$.  For each joined coding sequence, the response is $\log\_{10}(TE)$, where the local measured value is $TE=RPF/mRNA$.  Thus the ribosome footprint numerator and the matched mRNA denominator are already paired at the data-source level before the controlled regression is read back.  The coordinate source is the imported $q$-vector table from the powered $B^{*}_{Q6}$ translation-survival run, and the finite test is a CDS-by-coordinate table crossed with organism-matched TE rows.

\paragraph{Joined rows.}
Four organism panels are present and all are large enough to avoid the rank-saturated refusal mode.  In \textit{Escherichia coli} K12 MG1655, $3739$ CDS rows are reported as joined, $3415$ genes have measured TE, and the ribosome-TE join hit rate is $0.9133458143888741$.  In \textit{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}, $5815$ CDS rows are reported as joined, $5128$ genes have measured TE, and the hit rate is $0.8818572656921754$.  In \textit{Danio rerio}, $14410$ CDS rows are reported as joined, $13140$ genes have measured TE, and the hit rate is $0.9118667591950035$.  In \textit{Homo sapiens}, $19053$ CDS rows are reported as joined, $11554$ genes have measured TE, and the hit rate is $0.6064136881331024$.  The human panel therefore has the weakest identifier coverage among the four, but it is still a nontrivial measured-TE panel rather than a three-row or all-fit surface.

\paragraph{Residualization design.}
The control matrix $Z$ contains $24$ control columns in every organism and has rank $23$ at tolerance $10^{-10}$.  The tested residual coordinate block has $9$ columns and residualized rank $9$ in every organism.  The residual degrees of freedom after $Z$ are $3392$ for \textit{E. coli}, $5105$ for yeast, $13117$ for zebrafish, and $11531$ for human.  The corresponding residual response energies are $919.4854026798789$, $753.5781168800304$, $3588.378859021949$, and $2052.594706241978$.  The explained energies contributed by the residual coordinate block after controls are $8.637891149939946$, $103.10404057290246$, $144.76301201119642$, and $38.66659710161269$, respectively.  Since the residual coordinate rank is full and the degenerate threshold is $R^{2}=0.999999$, each panel is classified as powered-rank sufficient rather than rank-degenerate.

\paragraph{Measured survival statistic.}
The positive threshold for a coordinate-level measured-TE survival entry is $10^{-12}$ in partial $R^{2}$.  At the organism-summary level, all four panels have a positive measured $Q\to T$ surface.  The measured values are $R^{2}_{QT,meas}=0.009394266754822262$ for \textit{E. coli}, $R^{2}_{QT,meas}=0.13681931343730436$ for yeast, $R^{2}_{QT,meas}=0.04034217614654355$ for zebrafish, and $R^{2}_{QT,meas}=0.018837911344127926$ for human.  Each organism reports $9$ surviving residual coordinates out of the $9$ tested coordinates.  The strongest summary value is the yeast panel, followed by zebrafish, human, and \textit{E. coli}.  This ordering is only a finite-panel ranking of measured association strength; it is not a biological direction law and it is not a cross-condition universality statement.

\paragraph{Local interpretation.}
The finite result supports a bounded claim: after length, amino-acid composition, GC3, M-density, organism structure, and matched mRNA controls, the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates are not compressed away from the measured TE readout in these four panels.  Equivalently, the amino-acid quotient does not erase these residual coordinates before the ribosome-footprint-over-mRNA readout is reached.  The support is strongest where the residualized response energy and the positive measured $R^{2}_{QT,meas}$ are both nonzero under full rank.  The row therefore upgrades this exact contact from a routing-only target to a powered measured-association record for $q6.ribosome\mbox{-}te\mbox{-}survival.with\mbox{-}mrna\mbox{-}control.cross\mbox{-}layer$.

\paragraph{No promotion beyond the measured contact.}
The same finite rows also impose a sharp boundary.  The data are condition-specific, cross-sectional, and taken from one or a small number of ribosome-profiling studies per organism.  Positive $S^{QT,meas}$ entries are codon-residual to measured-TE associations after controls; they are not causal mechanism claims.  They do not establish universal translation efficiency, elongation speed, protein abundance, protein folding, physical admissibility, biological function, phenotype, or $M^{QTP}$ mediation.  Agreement or disagreement with modeled tAI can be used only as coordinate-pattern comparison, not as causal validation or causal falsification.  A claim at any higher layer requires its own layer-matched contact and its own controlled statistic.
