\subsection{Rank-powered measured translation-efficiency survival for $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates}
\label{sec:bioreality-q6-measured-te-survival}

\paragraph{Finite row surface.}
This record tests whether the nine-dimensional $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ synonymous residual surface survives against directly measured ribosome-profiling translation efficiency. The coordinate list is
$K\_{AAA}$, $Arg\_{AGR}$, $Ile\_{AUA}$, $Leu\_{CUN\,vs\,UUR}$, $Leu\_{UUA\,vs\,UUG}$, $Ser\_{UCR\,vs\,AGY}$, $Ser\_{UCA\,vs\,UCG}$, $Thr\_{ACR\,vs\,ACY}$, and $f3\_stress$. The readout is $\log_{10}(TE)$, where $TE$ is the local measured ribosome-footprint signal divided by matched mRNA signal. Thus the measured translation-efficiency column is already mRNA-normalized before the residual survival fit is made. The four organism panels are rank-powered after residualization: each has $9$ residual $Q$ coordinates, residualized control rank $23$, and residualized $Q$ rank $9$ at rank tolerance $10^{-10}$.

\paragraph{Controls and rank checks.}
The residualization control surface contains $24$ columns: intercept, coding-sequence length, amino-acid composition, $GC3$, and the $M$-density baseline on the union support of the projected $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ coordinates. The organism-level residual degrees of freedom after this control step are $13117$ for \emph{Danio rerio}, $3392$ for \emph{Escherichia coli} K12 MG1655, $11531$ for \emph{Homo sapiens}, and $5105$ for \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}. The corresponding residual TE energies are $3588.378859021949$, $919.4854026798789$, $2052.594706241978$, and $753.5781168800304$. The energy explained by the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ coordinate block on the measured-TE readout is $144.76301201119642$ in zebrafish, $8.637891149939946$ in \emph{E. coli}, $38.66659710161269$ in human, and $103.10404057290246$ in yeast. In all four cases the rank diagnostic is nondegenerate rather than an all-fitted low-rank surface.

\paragraph{Joined measured-TE rows.}
The measured ribosome-efficiency joins are finite and organism-specific. Zebrafish contributes $13140$ genes with measured TE out of $14410$ CDS rows, with join hit rate $0.9118667591950035$. \emph{E. coli} contributes $3415$ measured-TE genes out of $3739$ CDS rows, with hit rate $0.9133458143888741$. Human contributes $11554$ measured-TE genes out of $19053$ CDS rows, with hit rate $0.6064136881331024$. Yeast contributes $5128$ measured-TE genes out of $5815$ CDS rows, with hit rate $0.8818572656921754$. These are same-organism codon/readout joins, not organism-count summaries; the test is therefore a per-gene finite-matrix test after the declared controls.

\paragraph{Measured survival values.}
Let $S^{QT,meas}$ denote the partial survival of the residual coordinate block against measured $\log_{10}(TE)$ after the control projection. The observed organism-level values are
$$
\begin{aligned}
S^{QT,meas}_{Danio} &= 0.04034217614654355,\\
S^{QT,meas}_{Ecoli} &= 0.009394266754822262,\\
S^{QT,meas}_{Human} &= 0.018837911344127926,\\
S^{QT,meas}_{Yeast} &= 0.13681931343730436.
\end{aligned}
$$
The positivity threshold used by the run is $10^{-12}$, and the surviving-coordinate count is $9$ in every organism. The strongest measured-TE survival in this panel is yeast, while the smallest positive entry is \emph{E. coli}. The result is therefore not a rank-degenerate $n=3$ artifact and not a notation-only route: it is a positive, Z-controlled measured-TE association on four joined organism panels.

\paragraph{Relation to measured protein abundance.}
The same measured-TE layer was also used in a downstream protein-abundance decomposition. In the measured $Q \to T \to P$ run, the measured TE survival values above pair with protein-abundance survival values $S^{QP}$ of $0.04926578454614652$ for zebrafish, $0.10941535951828074$ for \emph{E. coli}, $0.04288464996165745$ for human, and $0.3146818503437042$ for yeast. The measured mediation fractions are $0.0$, $0.04091158340950673$, $0.0027966485036786744$, and $0.4793069656345167$, respectively. This comparison is useful because it separates a direct measured-TE survival claim from a stronger protein-abundance mediation claim: measured TE survival is positive in all four organisms, but measured TE absorption of protein-abundance association is large only in yeast, modest in \emph{E. coli}, very small in human, and clipped to zero for zebrafish.

\paragraph{Stability and readout dictionary context.}
A separate three-organism TE-plus-mRNA-stability decomposition gives another finite comparison surface. In that run the direct residual protein-abundance values are $R^2_{QP}=0.1181743523064932$ for \emph{E. coli}, $0.04274178488511215$ for human, and $0.2715520808117803$ for yeast. Conditioning on both measured TE and mRNA stability leaves $R^2_{QP\mid T,M}=0.08975732521542036$, $0.0355223930537993$, and $0.13047582645538838$. The resulting joint mediation fractions are $0.2404669586626661$, $0.16890712099923358$, and $0.5195182225621592$, with right-tail permutation probability $0.000999000999000999$ in each organism. These numbers support the local interpretation that measured TE is one real readout of the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual surface, while protein abundance is partitioned among measured TE, mRNA stability, and remaining components rather than being exhausted by a single translation-efficiency column.

\paragraph{Four-case readback.}
For the present measured-TE survival chapter the relevant case is the translation-positive branch: $S^{QT,meas} > 0$ in each organism under the stated controls. A protein-mediated case requires an additional matched protein-abundance row and a conditioning calculation showing that the $Q \to P$ signal is reduced by the measured $T$ readout. The finite runs satisfy that stronger condition unevenly: yeast shows substantial measured-TE absorption, \emph{E. coli} shows a small but positive fraction, human is nearly direct after measured-TE conditioning, and zebrafish has no positive clipped measured mediation fraction despite positive $S^{QT,meas}$. The chapter therefore records measured translation-efficiency survival first and treats downstream protein mediation as a separate finite statistic.

\paragraph{Interpretive limits.}
The positive entries above mean that the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates retain predictive association with measured ribosome-footprint-over-mRNA translation efficiency after length, amino-acid composition, $GC3$, and $M$-density controls. They do not by themselves establish elongation kinetics, initiation control, causal translation mechanism, protein abundance production, folded structural order, physical admissibility, molecular function, phenotype, evolutionary direction, or a universal biological law. Any such stronger statement needs its own layer-matched measurement and its own conditioning calculation. In particular, a direct $Q \to P$ protein-omics association is not promoted to a mechanism unless a measured $Q \to T$ step is positive and conditioning on $T$ removes or reduces the direct protein signal on the same joined rows.
