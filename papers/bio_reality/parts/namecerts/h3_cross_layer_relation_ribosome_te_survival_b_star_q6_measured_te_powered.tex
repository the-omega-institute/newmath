\subsection{Measured ribosome-TE survival for the $B^*_{Q6}$ residual panel}
\label{sec:bior reality-q6-measured-ribosome-te-survival}

\paragraph{Finite surface.}
The measured ribosome-TE test uses a finite per-CDS matrix whose columns are the nine $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinates $K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $Leu_{CUN\,vs\,UUR}$, $Leu_{UUA\,vs\,UUG}$, $Ser_{UCR\,vs\,AGY}$, $Ser_{UCA\,vs\,UCG}$, $Thr_{ACR\,vs\,ACY}$, and $f_3$-stress.  The row units are organism-matched coding sequences joined to ribosome-profiling translation-efficiency records.  The readout is $\log_{10}(TE)$, with $TE=RPF/mRNA$ in the local source files, so the translation-efficiency column is already normalized by the matched transcript denominator.  The controls are fixed before scoring: intercept, CDS length, amino-acid composition, $GC3$, and an $M$-density baseline on the union support of the nine projected coordinates.  The experiment is therefore a controlled codon-residual to measured-TE association test, not an inference from codon geometry alone.

\paragraph{Joined rows and rank.}
Four organisms supplied powered measured-TE joins.  Danio rerio joins $13{,}140$ TE genes against $14{,}410$ reported CDS rows, with hit rate $0.9118667591950035$.  Escherichia coli K12 MG1655 joins $3{,}415$ TE genes against $3{,}739$ reported CDS rows, with hit rate $0.9133458143888741$.  Homo sapiens joins $11{,}554$ TE genes against $19{,}053$ reported CDS rows, with hit rate $0.6064136881331024$.  Saccharomyces cerevisiae joins $5{,}128$ TE genes against $5{,}815$ reported CDS rows, with hit rate $0.8818572656921754$.  In all four cases the residualized $Q$ design has rank $9$ after the $Z$ controls, with $23$-rank control design, $24$ control columns, rank tolerance $10^{-10}$, and degeneracy threshold $0.999999$.  The post-control residual degrees of freedom are $13{,}117$, $3{,}392$, $11{,}531$, and $5{,}105$, respectively.

\paragraph{Survival readback.}
The measured-TE survival values are strictly positive in every powered organism, with all nine coordinates surviving the positive partial-$R^2$ threshold $10^{-12}$.  The finite readback is
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{QT,meas}(\textit{D. rerio}) &= 0.04034217614654355,\\
R^2_{QT,meas}(\textit{E. coli K12}) &= 0.009394266754822262,\\
R^2_{QT,meas}(\textit{H. sapiens}) &= 0.018837911344127926,\\
R^2_{QT,meas}(\textit{S. cerevisiae}) &= 0.13681931343730436.
\end{aligned}
$$
The corresponding explained energies of the residualized measured-TE readout are $144.76301201119642$, $8.637891149939946$, $38.66659710161269$, and $103.10404057290246$.  The residual TE energies after control removal are $3588.378859021949$, $919.4854026798789$, $2052.594706241978$, and $753.5781168800304$.  The strongest measured-TE surface in this finite panel is yeast, while the weakest is E. coli; both remain positive and rank-powered.

\paragraph{Relation to protein abundance.}
The measured-TE surface does not by itself establish a protein-abundance mechanism.  A separate three-way measured mediation fit joins codon residuals, measured TE, and PAXdb abundance.  In that fit the measured mediated fractions are $0$ for Danio rerio after a negative raw fraction $-0.04801486048721136$, $0.04091158340950673$ for E. coli K12, $0.0027966485036786744$ for Homo sapiens, and $0.4793069656345167$ for Saccharomyces cerevisiae.  The same fit reports $S_{QP}$ values $0.04926578454614652$, $0.10941535951828074$, $0.04288464996165745$, and $0.3146818503437042$, alongside the measured $S_{QT}$ values above.  Thus the ribosome-TE chapter records a real translation-readout association surface; any downstream protein-abundance interpretation must be read through the separate attenuation calculation, where yeast shows the largest measured TE-conditioned fraction and human shows only a small one.

\paragraph{Stability and readout dictionary context.}
A broader abundance decomposition using measured TE and mRNA stability was computed for three organisms.  Its joint mediation fractions are $0.2404669586626661$ in E. coli K12, $0.16890712099923358$ in Homo sapiens, and $0.5195182225621592$ in Saccharomyces cerevisiae, each with right-tail permutation value $0.000999000999000999$ under the fixed pair-permutation audit.  In yeast, a readout dictionary certificate on a unified join of $1{,}800$ rows reports coverage $C^*=0.24538211439730545$ with $\lambda_{DL}=0.01$, $\rho_{join}=0.05$, five folds, $120$ permutations, a selection-aware null $95$th percentile $0.029149007242956115$, and unexplained fraction $0.7546178856026946$.  These linked surfaces show that the measured-TE contact is one finite component of a larger cross-layer readout family, not a complete explanatory closure of the abundance residual.

\paragraph{Boundary.}
The admissible conclusion is narrow.  The finite matrix supports a positive, rank-powered association between the nine $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinates and measured ribosome-derived TE after the stated sequence and composition controls.  It does not assert universal translation efficiency, elongation speed, causal mediation, protein folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, phenotype, or an organism-independent biological law.  A claim at any of those layers requires an additional layer-matched contact and its own controlled statistic.  In particular, a direct $Q_i\to P_k$ association remains a protein-omics association unless a measured $Q_i\to T_j$ readout survives and conditioning on $T_j$ removes or reduces the direct protein-abundance signal in the same joined surface.
