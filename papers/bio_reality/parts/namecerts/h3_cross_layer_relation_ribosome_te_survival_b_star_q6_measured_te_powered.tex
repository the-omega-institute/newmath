\subsection{Measured ribosome-TE survival of the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates}\label{sec:bioreality-q6-measured-ribosome-te-survival}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite surface.}
This record tests a finite row surface whose columns are the nine $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates
$K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $Leu_{CUN\,vs\,UUR}$, $Leu_{UUA\,vs\,UUG}$, $Ser_{UCR\,vs\,AGY}$, $Ser_{UCA\,vs\,UCG}$, $Thr_{ACR\,vs\,ACY}$, and $f_{3}\text{-stress}$.
The readout is measured ribosome-profiling translation efficiency, represented as $\log_{10}(TE)$ with $TE=RPF/mRNA$ in the local organism files. The statistic is therefore not a modeled tAI score: it is a per-gene footprint-over-mRNA readout, already normalized by the matched transcript denominator in the source construction.
For each organism, the codon residual matrix $Q$ and the measured TE vector $T$ are residualized against the same control family $Z$: intercept, CDS length, amino-acid composition, $GC3$, and the $M$-density baseline. The measured survival number is the partial $R^2$ of residualized $T$ explained by the residualized nine-coordinate $Q$ design.

\paragraph{Rank and row admissibility.}
Four organism panels are present and all are nondegenerate for the nine-coordinate test. The joined measured-TE rows are $13{,}140$ for \emph{Danio rerio}, $3{,}415$ for \emph{Escherichia coli} K12 MG1655, $11{,}554$ for \emph{Homo sapiens}, and $5{,}128$ for \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}. These correspond to reported codon-table joins of $14{,}410$, $3{,}739$, $19{,}053$, and $5{,}815$ CDS rows, with ribosome-TE hit rates $0.9118667591950035$, $0.9133458143888741$, $0.6064136881331024$, and $0.8818572656921754$, respectively.
After residualizing against $Z$, every organism has $\operatorname{rank}(Q_{\perp Z})=9$ and $\operatorname{rank}(Z)=23$ with a rank tolerance of $10^{-10}$ and degeneracy threshold $0.999999$. The residual degrees of freedom after controls are $13{,}117$ in danio, $3{,}392$ in \emph{E. coli}, $11{,}531$ in human, and $5{,}105$ in yeast. Thus the all-positive surface is not an $n=3$ saturated artifact: the finite rows have enough post-control rank to evaluate the nine residual coordinates.

\paragraph{Measured TE survival values.}
The measured translation-efficiency survival matrix is positive in each organism under the declared threshold $10^{-12}$. The organism-level values are
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{QT,\mathrm{meas}}(\mathrm{danio}) &= 0.04034217614654355,\\
R^2_{QT,\mathrm{meas}}(\mathrm{E.\ coli}) &= 0.009394266754822262,\\
R^2_{QT,\mathrm{meas}}(\mathrm{human}) &= 0.018837911344127926,\\
R^2_{QT,\mathrm{meas}}(\mathrm{yeast}) &= 0.13681931343730436.
\end{aligned}
$$
All nine residual coordinates survive in each of the four measured-TE panels. The strongest measured TE association is yeast, with $R^2_{QT,\mathrm{meas}}=0.13681931343730436$ on $5{,}128$ joined genes and $5{,}105$ post-control residual degrees of freedom. The weakest is \emph{E. coli}, with $R^2_{QT,\mathrm{meas}}=0.009394266754822262$ on $3{,}415$ joined genes, still above the positive threshold and with full residual coordinate rank.

\paragraph{Energy audit.}
The same computation reports finite residual energies and explained energies for the TE readout after controls. The explained energies of the nine-coordinate surface on measured TE are $144.76301201119642$ in danio, $8.637891149939946$ in \emph{E. coli}, $38.66659710161269$ in human, and $103.10404057290246$ in yeast. The corresponding residual TE energies after $Z$ are $3588.378859021949$, $919.4854026798789$, $2052.594706241978$, and $753.5781168800304$. These numbers show a finite, measured readout contact: the signal is not inferred from codon geometry alone, but from joined CDS rows with ribosome-derived TE values.

\paragraph{Relation to protein abundance.}
The measured-TE survival result is only the $Q\to T$ part of the larger cross-layer question. A separate measured mediation fit on the same family gives $S_{QP}=0.04926578454614652$ and measured-mediated fraction $0.0$ for danio, $S_{QP}=0.10941535951828074$ and fraction $0.04091158340950673$ for \emph{E. coli}, $S_{QP}=0.04288464996165745$ and fraction $0.0027966485036786744$ for human, and $S_{QP}=0.3146818503437042$ and fraction $0.4793069656345167$ for yeast. Thus the measured TE layer is clearly present in all four panels, but downstream absorption of the direct protein-abundance association is organism-dependent and is largest in yeast. The direct protein-omics screen over fifteen organisms likewise reports positive $Q\to P$ associations after controls, including $R^2_{QP}=0.29837624994140827$ in yeast, $0.10767526677985896$ in \emph{E. coli}, $0.04454565118019448$ in human, and $0.04613529807085213$ in danio, but those direct associations are not mechanism claims by themselves.

\paragraph{Four-case readback.}
For this chapter the finite readback is: positive measured translation survival is established for all four powered measured-TE panels; direct protein-abundance survival is available from the linked protein-omics surface; measured mediation is strong only where conditioning on $T$ materially reduces the $Q\to P$ signal. Yeast is therefore the cleanest measured $Q\to T\to P$ candidate among the listed rows. \emph{E. coli} and human have positive measured TE survival but only small measured-mediated fractions. Danio has positive measured TE survival and positive direct protein association, but the measured-mediated fraction is clipped to $0$ because the raw mediation fraction is negative. This is a discriminant, not a causal ordering.

\paragraph{Local boundary.}
The result supports a bounded statement: $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates retain measurable association with ribosome-derived $TE=RPF/mRNA$ after length, amino-acid composition, $GC3$, and $M$-density controls in four rank-powered panels. No broader translation realization, folding claim, physical admissibility claim, biological function claim, mechanistic assertion, or universal law follows from these rows alone. The data are condition-specific, cross-sectional, and derived from one or a small number of ribosome-profiling studies per organism. Protein abundance, localization, structural order, perturbation response, and molecular function each require their own matched contact and statistic before any such layer may be promoted.
