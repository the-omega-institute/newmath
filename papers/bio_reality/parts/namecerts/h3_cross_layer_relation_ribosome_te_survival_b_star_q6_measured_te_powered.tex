\subsection{Measured ribosome-TE survival for the rank-powered $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual surface}\label{sec:bioreality-q6-ribosome-te-survival-measured}\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite surface.}
This record tests a finite matrix surface whose rows are coding sequences and whose columns are the nine residual coordinates $K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $Leu_{CUN\,vs\,UUR}$, $Leu_{UUA\,vs\,UUG}$, $Ser_{UCR\,vs\,AGY}$, $Ser_{UCA\,vs\,UCG}$, $Thr_{ACR\,vs\,ACY}$, and $f_{3}\text{-}stress$.  The translation readout is $\log_{10}(TE)$, with $TE$ defined in the local rows as ribosome footprint divided by matched mRNA.  Thus the assayed translation coordinate is already normalized by a transcript denominator before the residual regression is formed.  The control block has 24 columns, rank $23$, and contains length, amino-acid composition, GC3, and the $M$-density baseline.  In each organism the residualized $Q$ design has rank $9$, so the observed positive entries are not rank-saturated three-organism artifacts.

\paragraph{Joined measured-TE rows.}
Four organism panels have joined codon and measured-TE rows.  For \textit{Danio rerio}, $13{,}140$ genes with measured TE are used against $14{,}410$ reported joined CDS rows, giving hit rate $0.9118667591950035$.  For \textit{Escherichia coli} K12 MG1655, $3{,}415$ genes with TE are used against $3{,}739$ joined CDS rows, with hit rate $0.9133458143888741$.  For \textit{Homo sapiens}, $11{,}554$ genes with TE are used against $19{,}053$ joined CDS rows, with hit rate $0.6064136881331024$.  For \textit{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}, $5{,}128$ genes with TE are used against $5{,}815$ joined CDS rows, with hit rate $0.8818572656921754$.  These row counts fix the scope of the claim: it is a measured-readout survival statement over these finite joins, not a universal property of translation.

\paragraph{Controlled survival values.}
After residualization by the control block, the measured-TE survival values are strictly positive in all four panels:
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^{2}_{QT,meas}(\textit{D. rerio}) &= 0.04034217614654355,\\
R^{2}_{QT,meas}(\textit{E. coli}) &= 0.009394266754822262,\\
R^{2}_{QT,meas}(\textit{H. sapiens}) &= 0.018837911344127926,\\
R^{2}_{QT,meas}(\textit{S. cerevisiae}) &= 0.13681931343730436.
\end{aligned}
$$
The positive-entry threshold is $10^{-12}$, and the surviving coordinate count is $9$ in each organism.  The residual degrees of freedom after the control block are $13{,}117$ for \textit{D. rerio}, $3{,}392$ for \textit{E. coli}, $11{,}531$ for \textit{H. sapiens}, and $5{,}105$ for \textit{S. cerevisiae}.  The corresponding residual readout energies are $3588.378859021949$, $919.4854026798789$, $2052.594706241978$, and $753.5781168800304$.  The largest measured-TE association occurs in yeast, while the smallest occurs in \textit{E. coli}; both remain positive under the same rank and control convention.

\paragraph{Relation to protein-abundance rows.}
A separate direct protein-abundance surface reports positive $R^{2}_{QP}$ values over a larger organism set, including $0.061906245886660105$ for \textit{Bacillus subtilis}, $0.10767526677985896$ for \textit{E. coli}, $0.04454565118019448$ for human, and $0.29837624994140827$ for yeast.  Those protein rows do not by themselves identify a translation route.  In the measured mediation run over the four organisms with TE, the measured fractions are $0.0$ for \textit{D. rerio}, $0.04091158340950673$ for \textit{E. coli}, $0.0027966485036786744$ for human, and $0.4793069656345167$ for yeast.  Therefore the present chapter records measured $Q\to T$ survival as a necessary readout-layer contact, while the downstream $Q\to T\to P$ interpretation remains organism-specific and finite-row dependent.

\paragraph{Companion finite checks.}
The joint TE and mRNA-stability decomposition gives right-tail permutation values $0.000999000999000999$ in \textit{E. coli}, human, and yeast, with joint mediation fractions $0.2404669586626661$, $0.16890712099923358$, and $0.5195182225621592$.  The yeast partial-mediation dictionary uses $\lambda_{DL}=0.01$, $\rho_{join}=0.05$, $n_{join}=1800$, and reports coverage $C^{*}=0.24538211439730545$, leaving unexplained fraction $0.7546178856026946$.  A separate structural-order positional check classifies the ramp result as $ramp\_positive\_but\_not\_localized$, with weighted ramp increment $0.0011165640103326878$, weighted body increment $-0.00026157313923957926$, and paired ramp-minus-body sign-flip probability $0.05859375$.  These companion rows are included only to delimit the interpretation: measured TE survival can coexist with partial downstream reduction and with a non-localized structural-order result, but it does not turn either of those companion outcomes into a mechanistic proof.

\paragraph{Verdict.}
The finite result is ready at the measured-translation-readout layer.  The rank-powered rows show that all nine $B^{*}_{Q6}$ coordinates survive against measured ribosome-derived TE after the stated controls in four organism panels, with no all-$1.0$ rank-degenerate surface and with explicit row counts for each join.  The conclusion is deliberately narrow: the finite data support controlled association between $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates and measured TE.  They do not establish translation causality, elongation mechanism, folded structure, physical admissibility, protein function, phenotype, evolutionary direction, or a universal biological rule.  Any such higher-layer assertion requires a separate matched contact and its own finite statistic.
