\subsection{Measured ribosome-TE survival for the rank-powered $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual surface}\label{sec:bioreality-ribosome-te-survival-b-star-q6-measured-te-powered}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite surface.}
This record tests a measured translation-efficiency readout rather than a modeled codon-adaptation score.  The finite carrier is a per-CDS matrix whose columns are the nine $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates
$K\_AAA$, $Arg\_AGR$, $Ile\_AUA$, $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$, $Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG$, $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$, $Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG$, $Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY$, and $f3\_stress$, and whose readout is $\log_{10}(TE)$ with $TE = RPF/mRNA$ in the local ribosome-profiling files.  The test is therefore not a raw codon-usage comparison: each organism is residualized against a 24-column control design consisting of an intercept, CDS length, amino-acid composition, $GC3$, and the $M$-density baseline before the $Q$ surface is allowed to explain measured TE.

\paragraph{Joined rows and rank.}
Four organism panels pass the powered-rank check.  The measured TE joins contain 13,140 of 14,410 reported Danio rerio CDS rows, with hit rate $0.9118667591950035$; 3,415 of 3,739 Escherichia coli K12 MG1655 rows, with hit rate $0.9133458143888741$; 11,554 of 19,053 Homo sapiens rows, with hit rate $0.6064136881331024$; and 5,128 of 5,815 Saccharomyces cerevisiae rows, with hit rate $0.8818572656921754$.  In every case the residualized $Q$ design has rank $9$ and the control design has rank $23$.  The remaining degrees of freedom after controls are 13,117 for Danio rerio, 3,392 for Escherichia coli, 11,531 for Homo sapiens, and 5,105 for Saccharomyces cerevisiae.  Thus the four reported survival entries are not the rank-degenerate $n=3$ boundary; they are computed on finite, row-powered matrices.

\paragraph{Measured TE survival values.}
The measured translation-efficiency survival list is organism-specific:
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^{2}_{QT,meas}(\mathrm{Danio\ rerio}) &= 0.04034217614654355,\\
R^{2}_{QT,meas}(\mathrm{E.\ coli\ K12\ MG1655}) &= 0.009394266754822262,\\
R^{2}_{QT,meas}(\mathrm{Homo\ sapiens}) &= 0.018837911344127926,\\
R^{2}_{QT,meas}(\mathrm{Saccharomyces\ cerevisiae}) &= 0.13681931343730436.
\end{aligned}
$$
Each of the four organisms reports all nine residual coordinates as surviving at the positive partial-$R^2$ threshold $10^{-12}$.  The residual TE energies after the controls are $3588.378859021949$, $919.4854026798789$, $2052.594706241978$, and $753.5781168800304$, respectively.  The corresponding $Q$-explained energies on the TE residual are $144.76301201119642$, $8.637891149939946$, $38.66659710161269$, and $103.10404057290246$.  These numbers give the chapter its local positive statement: the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual surface survives against a measured ribosome-derived TE readout in four rank-powered organism panels after the declared sequence-composition controls.

\paragraph{Downstream comparison without promotion.}
The measured-TE survival result is narrower than a protein-output mechanism.  In the matched measured-TE and protein-abundance mediation run, the same four organisms have measured mediation fractions
$$
\begin{aligned}
M^{QTP,meas}(\mathrm{Danio\ rerio}) &= 0.0,\\
M^{QTP,meas}(\mathrm{E.\ coli\ K12\ MG1655}) &= 0.04091158340950673,\\
M^{QTP,meas}(\mathrm{Homo\ sapiens}) &= 0.0027966485036786744,\\
M^{QTP,meas}(\mathrm{Saccharomyces\ cerevisiae}) &= 0.4793069656345167.
\end{aligned}
$$
The yeast entry is the only large measured-TE absorption among these four panels; Danio is clipped to zero after a negative raw fraction of $-0.04801486048721136$.  A separate joint TE and mRNA-stability decomposition gives joint mediation fractions $0.2404669586626661$ in Escherichia coli, $0.16890712099923358$ in Homo sapiens, and $0.5195182225621592$ in Saccharomyces cerevisiae, with right-tail permutation value $0.000999000999000999$ for each organism.  In that decomposition the TE-only fractions are $0.01975521340963268$, $0.00549490864190343$, and $0.4697188319693239$, while the stability-only fractions are $0.23052056324898387$, $0.15314679870183337$, and $0.0626618595344251$.  This comparison keeps the measured-TE survival statistic in its proper layer: it is evidence that codon residuals predict a measured TE readout, not by itself a claim that TE mediates protein abundance in every organism.

\paragraph{Four-case placement and residual dictionary context.}
The modeled-tAI four-case discriminant supplies a useful cautionary contrast.  In Escherichia coli the case counts are $A=0$, $B=0$, $C=0$, $D=7$, with two positive-positive coordinates lacking a mediation drop; in Saccharomyces cerevisiae the case counts are $A=0$, $B=0$, $C=0$, $D=9$.  That modeled surface is not the same as the measured ribosome-TE surface used here, but it shows why a positive $Q \to T$ readout must still be separated from protein-abundance and mechanism claims.  The partial readout-dictionary certificate in yeast reports coverage $C^{\ast}=0.24538211439730545$ for the selected readouts $\{measured\_te, mrna\_stability, turnover\}$, with unexplained fraction $0.7546178856026946$, selection-aware null $95\%$ level $0.029149007242956115$, naive fixed-subset null $95\%$ level $0.00598374993486675$, $\lambda_{DL}=0.01$, $n=1800$, five folds, and 120 permutations.  Thus even when TE is included in a compact readout dictionary, most of the yeast abundance residual is not reduced by that dictionary.

\paragraph{The $f3\_stress$ branch.}
The $f3\_stress$ coordinate has additional finite-row context in yeast.  A dwell-mediated abundance run joins $1257$ rows and reports $base\ R^2=0.1855578757644517$, $dwell\ alone\ R^2=0.034336580953121976$, $full\ R^2=0.20412261520502883$, and held-out $\Delta R^2=0.01856473944057712$ against a 120-permutation null with mean $-0.0032911642620130563$, $95\%$ level $0.0013115362189692937$, and approximate $p=7.873916057196935\cdot 10^{-9}$.  The positional dwell run on the same joined size identifies the $5'$ segment as dominant: the $5'$ window passes, the $3'$ body window does not, and the leading segment is $5prime$.  A separate structural-order ramp test classifies the outcome as $ramp\_positive\_but\_not\_localized$, with primary ramp length $50$, weighted ramp held-out $\Delta R^2=0.0011165640103326878$, weighted body held-out $\Delta R^2=-0.00026157313923957926$, paired ramp-minus-body $\Delta R^2=0.001378137149572267$, ramp sign-flip $p=0.0478515625$, and paired ramp-minus-body sign-flip $p=0.05859375$.  These auxiliary records are compatible with the present TE survival result, but they do not upgrade it into folding, structure, or function.

\paragraph{Local boundary.}
The permitted conclusion is deliberately bounded.  The four measured ribosome-TE panels show that the nine-coordinate $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual surface has positive association with $\log_{10}(RPF/mRNA)$ after the stated sequence-composition controls, with all four residualized designs having rank $9$.  Nothing stronger follows from this chapter alone.  The TE readout is condition-specific, cross-sectional, and drawn from one or a small number of ribosome-profiling studies per organism; it is already normalized by mRNA abundance through the $RPF/mRNA$ construction.  No claim about translation as a causal process, elongation mechanism, protein abundance, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, phenotype, or universality is licensed unless a separate matched contact at that layer supplies rows and the corresponding controlled statistic.  Direct protein-omics survival, modeled-tAI agreement, dwell localization, or compact readout coverage may be compared with this result, but none of them converts the measured TE survival statistic into a causal or higher-layer biological law.
