\subsection{Measured ribosome-TE survival of the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual surface}\label{sec:bior-reality-q6-measured-te-survival}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite carrier.}
The record is a finite row matrix whose rows are coding sequences with codon counts and whose columns consist of nine $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates, one measured translation-efficiency readout, and the control block used before estimating survival.  The nine residual coordinates are
$K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $Leu_{CUN\,vs\,UUR}$, $Leu_{UUA\,vs\,UUG}$, $Ser_{UCR\,vs\,AGY}$, $Ser_{UCA\,vs\,UCG}$, $Thr_{ACR\,vs\,ACY}$, and $f3\_stress$.  The readout is $\log_{10}(TE)$, where the local ribosome-profiling files define $TE$ as ribosome footprint divided by matched mRNA.  Thus the tested readout is already mRNA-normalized at the source-file level, and the fitted statistic is a codon-residual-to-measured-TE association rather than a protein-output statistic.

\paragraph{Controls and rank.}
For each organism the residualization block contains $24$ control columns: intercept, coding-sequence length, amino-acid composition, $GC3$, and the $M$-density baseline.  The effective control rank is $23$ in every fitted organism, while the residualized $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ design keeps rank $9$ with rank tolerance $10^{-10}$.  The degenerate-fit cutoff is $0.999999$, and the positive partial-$R^2$ threshold is $10^{-12}$.  The four powered measured-TE rows are:

\paragraph{Measured survival values.}
The computed measured-TE survival statistic is positive in each powered organism, and all nine residual coordinates survive the positive-threshold test in each fitted matrix.  The organism-level partial-$R^2$ list is

The largest measured-TE association in this four-organism panel is the yeast entry, while the smallest is the $E.\ coli$ entry.  The fitted energies attributed to the residual coordinate block on the TE readout are $144.76301201119642$, $8.637891149939946$, $38.66659710161269$, and $103.10404057290246$ for danio, $E.\ coli$, human, and yeast respectively.  These numbers make the record non-vacuous: it is not an all-$1.0$ rank-saturated surface and it is not a three-row boundary artifact.

\paragraph{Relation to protein abundance mediation.}
The same measured-TE route can be compared with a separate protein-abundance mediation fit, but that comparison does not change the meaning of the TE-survival result.  In the measured $Q\to T\to P$ fit, the measured mediated fractions are $0.0$ for danio after a raw suppressive value $-0.04801486048721136$, $0.04091158340950673$ for $E.\ coli$, $0.0027966485036786744$ for human, and $0.4793069656345167$ for yeast.  The corresponding direct codon-residual-to-protein values in that fit are $S_{QP}=0.04926578454614652$, $0.10941535951828074$, $0.04288464996165745$, and $0.3146818503437042$.  Thus the measured-TE survival chapter supports a bounded $Q\to T$ readout surface, while the downstream $Q\to T\to P$ question remains a separate variance-partitioning question.

\paragraph{Auxiliary finite checks.}
A separate joint TE and mRNA-stability decomposition reports right-tail permutation $p=0.000999000999000999$ in yeast, human, and $E.\ coli$, with joint mediation fractions $0.5195182225621592$, $0.16890712099923358$, and $0.2404669586626661$ respectively.  For yeast, the TE-only and stability-only fractions are $0.4697188319693239$ and $0.0626618595344251$; for human they are $0.00549490864190343$ and $0.15314679870183337$; for $E.\ coli$ they are $0.01975521340963268$ and $0.23052056324898387$.  A separate yeast readout-dictionary reduction uses $n=1800$, five folds, $\lambda_{DL}=0.01$, $\rho_{join}=0.05$, and $120$ permutations, yielding coverage $C^{\ast}=0.24538211439730545$ and unexplained fraction $0.7546178856026946$.  For the $f3\_stress$ dwell check, the yeast join has $n=1257$, five folds, held-out $\Delta R^2=0.01856473944057712$, dwell-alone $R^2=0.034336580953121976$, approximate $p=7.873916057196935\times 10^{-9}$, and permutation null $95\%$ value $0.0013115362189692937$.  These auxiliary rows show that the measured-TE surface is part of a finite family of controlled readout decompositions, not a standalone causal claim.

\paragraph{Local boundary.}
The conclusion is deliberately narrow.  The finite matrix supports positive measured-TE survival of the nine-coordinate $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual block after the stated controls in four rank-powered organism joins.  It does not by itself establish a general translation rule, a causal pathway, elongation speed, protein abundance, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, phenotype, adaptation, or universality.  Any stronger biological statement requires its own layer-matched contact and its own controlled finite-row statistic.  In particular, agreement or disagreement with modeled tAI is only a coordinate-pattern comparison, and conditioning on measured TE is a statistical decomposition rather than an intervention or rescue experiment.
