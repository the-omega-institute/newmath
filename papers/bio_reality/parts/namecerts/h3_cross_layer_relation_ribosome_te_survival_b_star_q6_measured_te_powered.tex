\subsection{Rank-powered measured ribosome-TE survival for $B^*_{Q6}$}
\label{sec:bioreality-q6-measured-te-survival}

\paragraph{Finite panel.}
The object tested here is the nine-coordinate $B^*_{Q6}$ synonymous residual surface on real coding-sequence rows, with coordinates $K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $Leu_{CUN\,vs\,UUR}$, $Leu_{UUA\,vs\,UUG}$, $Ser_{UCR\,vs\,AGY}$, $Ser_{UCA\,vs\,UCG}$, $Thr_{ACR\,vs\,ACY}$, and $f_3\text{-stress}$.  The measured readout is $\log_{10}(TE)$, where the local finite files define $TE$ as ribosome footprint divided by matched mRNA abundance.  Thus the response is already mRNA-normalized before the residual survival fit is evaluated.  The control design contains an intercept, CDS length, amino-acid composition, GC3, and an $M$-density baseline, giving $24$ control columns with residualized control rank $23$ in every reported organism.

\paragraph{Rank sufficiency.}
The measured-TE join is nondegenerate in all four organisms.  The active rows are $13140$ for \emph{Danio rerio}, $3415$ for \emph{Escherichia coli} K12 MG1655, $11554$ for \emph{Homo sapiens}, and $5128$ for \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}.  The corresponding TE join hit rates against the CDS panels are $0.9118667591950035$, $0.9133458143888741$, $0.6064136881331024$, and $0.8818572656921754$.  After residualizing the controls, the $B^*_{Q6}$ matrix has rank $9$ in every organism, with rank tolerance $10^{-10}$ and degeneracy threshold $0.999999$.  The residual degrees of freedom are $13117$, $3392$, $11531$, and $5105$, respectively.  The residual TE energies are $3588.378859021949$, $919.4854026798789$, $2052.594706241978$, and $753.5781168800304$.

\paragraph{Measured survival statistic.}
The finite readout is the controlled coefficient of determination for the residualized nine-dimensional coordinate block against measured TE.  In the notation of this chapter, the organism-level survival values are
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{QT,\mathrm{meas}}(\mathrm{danio}) &= 0.04034217614654355,\\
R^2_{QT,\mathrm{meas}}(\mathrm{E.\ coli}) &= 0.009394266754822262,\\
R^2_{QT,\mathrm{meas}}(\mathrm{human}) &= 0.018837911344127926,\\
R^2_{QT,\mathrm{meas}}(\mathrm{yeast}) &= 0.13681931343730436.
\end{aligned}
$$
Each entry has all nine residual coordinates surviving at the declared positive threshold $10^{-12}$.  The strongest measured-TE association is in yeast, where the residual coordinate block explains $103.10404057290246$ units of TE residual energy; the weakest is in \emph{E. coli}, where it explains $8.637891149939946$ units.  These values are not an all-one saturated surface: they are organism-specific finite-row regressions with thousands of joined CDS units and full residual rank.

\paragraph{Readout interpretation.}
The positive entries mean that synonymous residual coordinates remain predictive of a measured ribosome-footprint-over-mRNA TE readout after amino-acid composition, length, GC3, and $M$-density have been removed.  The result is therefore a bounded measured-readout survival statement: $B^*_{Q6}$ is not erased by the standard compositional controls on these four rank-powered panels.  The finite rows do not say that the same coordinate has the same biological magnitude in every organism.  They say only that the controlled residual block is non-null for the measured TE response in each tested panel.

\paragraph{Protein-abundance comparison.}
A separate direct protein-omics run gives positive $R^2_{QP}$ values on measured PAXdb abundance for a broader organism panel.  The four organisms overlapping the measured-TE run have direct protein-abundance values $0.04613529807085213$ for danio, $0.10767526677985896$ for \emph{E. coli}, $0.04454565118019448$ for human, and $0.29837624994140827$ for yeast.  The measured mediation run then conditions the protein-abundance association on measured TE.  Its measured fractions are
$$
\begin{aligned}
M^{QTP}_{\mathrm{meas}}(\mathrm{danio}) &= 0.0,\\
M^{QTP}_{\mathrm{meas}}(\mathrm{E.\ coli}) &= 0.04091158340950673,\\
M^{QTP}_{\mathrm{meas}}(\mathrm{human}) &= 0.0027966485036786744,\\
M^{QTP}_{\mathrm{meas}}(\mathrm{yeast}) &= 0.4793069656345167.
\end{aligned}
$$
The yeast case is the clear finite-row positive mediation example in this quartet: measured TE survives, protein abundance is associated, and conditioning on measured TE absorbs a large fraction of the direct abundance signal.  Danio is the contrasting case: the measured TE survival is positive, but the fitted measured mediation fraction is clipped to $0.0$ because the raw fraction is $-0.04801486048721136$.  This separation is essential.  A $q_i\to T_j$ signal is not automatically a $q_i\to T_j\to P_k$ signal.

\paragraph{Joint mediator context.}
The wider four-way join with measured TE, mRNA stability, protein abundance, and the same $B^*_{Q6}$ design shows that measured TE is one mediator among several finite readouts.  In \emph{E. coli}, human, and yeast the joint TE-plus-stability mediation fractions are $0.2404669586626661$, $0.16890712099923358$, and $0.5195182225621592$, with right-tail permutation values $0.000999000999000999$ in each organism.  The single-mediator TE-only fractions are $0.01975521340963268$, $0.00549490864190343$, and $0.4697188319693239$, while the stability-only fractions are $0.23052056324898387$, $0.15314679870183337$, and $0.0626618595344251$.  Yeast again concentrates much of the measured abundance attenuation in TE, whereas \emph{E. coli} and human place most of the joint attenuation in mRNA stability.  A separate partial readout dictionary on yeast reports coverage $C^*=0.24538211439730545$ for a selected family including measured TE, mRNA stability, and turnover, with unexplained fraction $0.7546178856026946$, selection-aware null $95\%$ value $0.029149007242956115$, fixed-subset null $95\%$ value $0.00598374993486675$, and $\lambda_{DL}=0.01$.

\paragraph{Local substructure.}
The $f_3\text{-stress}$ coordinate gives a more localized readout check in yeast.  On the dwell-abundance panel, the join has $1257$ rows, the base design has $1800$ rows, the full model has held-out $\Delta R^2=0.01856473944057712$, and the fixed row-label permutation null has $95\%$ value $0.0013115362189692937$ over $120$ permutations.  The same coordinate has $P_{f3}$ target fraction $0.1999063916718794$, $P_{f3}$ norm squared $44.20095750389323$, dwell-only $R^2=0.034336580953121976$, and $f_3$--dwell correlation $0.1615441450901653$ with approximate $p=7.873916057196935\times 10^{-9}$.  A positional check assigns the leading segment to the $5'$ window: the $5'$ window passes, the $3'$ window does not, and the dominant segment is recorded as $5prime$.  A separate structural-order positional run is deliberately weaker: the primary $50$-codon ramp has weighted $f_3$ ramp held-out $\Delta R^2=0.0011165640103326878$ and body held-out $\Delta R^2=-0.00026157313923957926$, with conclusion $ramp\_positive\_but\_not\_localized$.  These numbers support a measured ribosome-readout and dwell-context association, not a folded-structure conclusion.

\paragraph{Four-case readback.}
The measured-TE result occupies the translation-survival part of the four-case dictionary.  The local classification rule is: case A is null for both translation and protein readouts; case B survives translation but not protein abundance; case C survives protein abundance without a measured translation-conditioned drop; case D survives both and has a positive measured attenuation after conditioning on $T_j$.  In the finite rows reported here, all four organisms have positive $S^{QT,\mathrm{meas}}$.  The protein and mediation layer then separates them: yeast has a large measured attenuation fraction, \emph{E. coli} has a small but positive measured attenuation fraction, human has a very small positive attenuation fraction, and danio has no positive measured attenuation despite positive measured TE survival.  This is why direct protein-omics survival cannot be promoted to a mechanism statement without the conditioning calculation.

\paragraph{Local boundary.}
The admissible conclusion is narrow.  The finite rows show that $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinates survive against measured ribosome-derived TE in four rank-powered organism panels after the declared controls.  They also show that measured TE explains a substantial part of the yeast abundance association and only a small or null part of the other measured-protein panels.  No statement about translation as a complete biological process, elongation mechanism, protein folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, phenotype, causation, or universal biological law follows from these regressions alone.  Any such layer would require its own matched measurement contact and its own finite statistic.  The present chapter is therefore a measured-readout survival certificate with a carefully bounded mediation comparison, not a general biological mechanism claim.
