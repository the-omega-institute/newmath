\subsection{Measured ribosome-TE survival of the $B^*_{Q6}$ residual panel}\label{subsec:bioreality-ribosome-te-survival-bstar-q6}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite carrier.}
This record concerns a finite matrix surface whose rows are organism-matched coding sequences and whose active columns are the nine $B^*_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates
$K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $Leu_{CUN\,vs\,UUR}$, $Leu_{UUA\,vs\,UUG}$, $Ser_{UCR\,vs\,AGY}$, $Ser_{UCA\,vs\,UCG}$, $Thr_{ACR\,vs\,ACY}$, and $f3_{stress}$.  The measured translation readout is $\log_{10}(TE)$, with $TE$ defined in the local ribosome-profiling files as ribosome footprint divided by matched mRNA.  Thus the readout is already normalized by transcript abundance at the measurement level; the regression still removes the common codon-level control surface before testing whether the nine residual coordinates retain measurable association with $TE$.

\paragraph{Controlled residualization.}
For each organism, the codon residual matrix $Q$ and the measured-TE vector were residualized against the same finite control family: intercept, CDS length, amino-acid composition, $GC3$, and the $M$-density baseline induced by the support of the nine projected residual coordinates.  The fitted control design has $24$ columns and rank $23$ in every measured-TE organism.  After this residualization, the $Q$ block remains full rank: $\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde Q)=9$ for danio, \emph{E. coli}, human, and yeast, with rank tolerance $10^{-10}$ and degeneracy threshold $0.999999$.  The residual degrees of freedom are $13117$ for \emph{Danio rerio}, $3392$ for \emph{Escherichia coli} K12 MG1655, $11531$ for \emph{Homo sapiens}, and $5105$ for \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}.  The corresponding residual TE energies are $3588.378859021949$, $919.4854026798789$, $2052.594706241978$, and $753.5781168800304$.

\paragraph{Joined measurement rows.}
The finite row joins are rank-powered rather than saturated.  The danio TE contact uses $13140$ genes against $14410$ reported CDS joins, giving a ribosome-TE hit rate of $0.9118667591950035$.  The \emph{E. coli} contact uses $3415$ genes against $3739$ reported CDS joins, with hit rate $0.9133458143888741$.  The human contact uses $11554$ genes against $19053$ reported CDS joins, with hit rate $0.6064136881331024$.  The yeast contact uses $5128$ genes against $5815$ reported CDS joins, with hit rate $0.8818572656921754$.  These row counts are large enough that an all-one matrix cannot be dismissed as a small-rank artifact; the test is a finite, controlled survival computation on joined codon and measured-TE rows.

\paragraph{Measured survival values.}
The measured-TE survival statistic is the partial explanatory fraction for $\widetilde Q$ against residualized $\log_{10}(TE)$ after the controls above.  The positive threshold is $10^{-12}$, and all four organisms exceed it:
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{QT,meas}(\textit{D. rerio}) &= 0.04034217614654355,\\
R^2_{QT,meas}(\textit{E. coli}) &= 0.009394266754822262,\\
R^2_{QT,meas}(\textit{H. sapiens}) &= 0.018837911344127926,\\
R^2_{QT,meas}(\textit{S. cerevisiae}) &= 0.13681931343730436.
\end{aligned}
$$
In each organism, all nine residual coordinates have positive measured-TE survival under the declared threshold.  Yeast is the largest measured-TE case in this panel, with $R^2_{QT,meas}=0.13681931343730436$; \emph{E. coli} is the smallest positive case, with $R^2_{QT,meas}=0.009394266754822262$.  This is enough to make the ribosome-TE survival claim ready at translation-readout scope: the $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinates do not collapse to the amino-acid, length, $GC3$, or $M$-density controls when measured TE is used as the response.

\paragraph{Protein-abundance contrast.}
The same residual coordinate family also survives directly against measured protein abundance in a broader PAXdb panel, but that direct protein-omics result is not itself a translation mechanism.  The direct $Q\to P$ partial values include $R^2_{QP}=0.10767526677985896$ for \emph{E. coli}, $0.04454565118019448$ for human, and $0.29837624994140827$ for yeast, with all nine coordinates surviving in those cases.  In the measured-TE plus protein-abundance mediation computation, the three-way joined row counts are $13140$ for danio, $3415$ for \emph{E. coli}, $11554$ for human, and $5128$ for yeast.  The corresponding measured mediation fractions are clipped to $0.0$ for danio from a raw value of $-0.04801486048721136$, $0.04091158340950673$ for \emph{E. coli}, $0.0027966485036786744$ for human, and $0.4793069656345167$ for yeast.  Thus the same finite evidence surface distinguishes three statements that must not be collapsed: measured $Q\to T$ survival is positive in all four organisms; direct $Q\to P$ survival can also be positive; and measured $Q\to T\to P$ absorption is organism-dependent, strong in yeast, weak in human and \emph{E. coli}, and not positive in danio after clipping.

\paragraph{Joint readout comparison.}
A separate three-organism decomposition adds mRNA stability to measured TE and protein abundance.  In that computation, the joined row counts are $2517$ for \emph{E. coli}, $11322$ for human, and $4759$ for yeast.  The joint mediation fractions are $0.2404669586626661$, $0.16890712099923358$, and $0.5195182225621592$, respectively, each with a right-tail permutation value of $0.000999000999000999$.  The single-readout components differ by organism: measured TE contributes $0.01975521340963268$ in \emph{E. coli}, $0.00549490864190343$ in human, and $0.4697188319693239$ in yeast, while stability contributes $0.23052056324898387$, $0.15314679870183337$, and $0.0626618595344251$.  The overlap labels are approximately independent under tolerance $0.05$, with joint-minus-single-sum values $-0.009808817995950458$, $0.010265413655496769$, and $-0.012862468941589822$.  This supports the local reading that measured TE is one real component of the downstream surface, not a universal explanation of every protein-abundance residual.

\paragraph{Dictionary-scale residual accounting.}
For yeast, the partial mediation certificate tests a finite candidate dictionary containing measured TE, mRNA stability, and turnover.  On $n=1800$ sampled joined rows out of $4758$ available rows, using five folds, $\lambda_{DL}=0.01$, and $\rho_{join}=0.05$, the best selection-aware coverage is $C^*=0.24538211439730545$.  The raw best coverage over the candidate family is $0.2745002077400602$, while the selection-aware null $95\%$ value is $0.029149007242956115$ and the naive fixed-subset null $95\%$ value is $0.00598374993486675$ over $120$ permutations.  The unexplained fraction remains $0.7546178856026946$.  This finite certificate is consistent with the measured-TE survival result: there is a real measured-readout component, but most of the yeast protein-abundance residual is not absorbed by this small readout dictionary.

\paragraph{Local scope boundary.}
The ready verdict is restricted to a measured translation-efficiency association after the stated controls.  It does not assert a causal translation pathway, elongation speed, ribosome mechanism, folded structural order, physical admissibility, molecular function, organismal phenotype, or universal biological law.  The ribosome-profiling contact supplies condition-specific, cross-sectional $TE$ values from one or a small number of studies.  Protein abundance, when used, is a downstream steady-state measurement with turnover, localization, sample-preparation, isoform, and platform effects still present.  A higher-layer assertion would require an additional layer-matched contact and a separate finite statistic showing that the same coordinate first survives the measured translation step and then reduces or mediates the downstream signal under the same control discipline.
