\subsection{Measured ribosome-TE survival of the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual panel}\label{sec:bioreality-q6-measured-ribosome-te-survival}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite surface.}
This record tests whether the nine-coordinate $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ synonymous residual panel leaves a detectable signal in measured ribosome translation efficiency after the coding-sequence controls have been removed. The finite carrier is a row-matched matrix whose columns are the residual coordinates
$K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $Leu_{CUN\,vs\,UUR}$, $Leu_{UUA\,vs\,UUG}$, $Ser_{UCR\,vs\,AGY}$, $Ser_{UCA\,vs\,UCG}$, $Thr_{ACR\,vs\,ACY}$, and $f3\_stress$.
The response column is $\log_{10}(TE)$, where the local measurement defines $TE$ as ribosome footprint divided by matched mRNA abundance. Thus the response is already normalized by transcript abundance at the source level; the additional regression controls are used to keep the synonymous-coordinate question separated from length, amino-acid composition, GC3, and the support density of the residual coordinate family.

\paragraph{Controls and rank.}
For each organism the design first residualizes the nine $Q$ coordinates and the measured TE response against a $Z$ block of twenty-four listed controls, with effective $\operatorname{rank}(Z)=23$. The retained $Q$ design has $\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde X)=9$ in every powered organism, using rank tolerance $10^{-10}$ and the degeneracy cutoff $R^2=0.999999$. The finite rank table is
$$
\begin{aligned}
\textit{Danio rerio}:&\quad n_{\mathrm{TE}}=13140,\ h=0.9118667591950035,\ df_Z=13117,\ E_T=3588.378859021949,\\
\textit{Escherichia coli}~K12~MG1655:&\quad n_{\mathrm{TE}}=3415,\ h=0.9133458143888741,\ df_Z=3392,\ E_T=919.4854026798789,\\
\textit{Homo sapiens}:&\quad n_{\mathrm{TE}}=11554,\ h=0.6064136881331024,\ df_Z=11531,\ E_T=2052.594706241978,\\
\textit{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}:&\quad n_{\mathrm{TE}}=5128,\ h=0.8818572656921754,\ df_Z=5105,\ E_T=753.5781168800304.
\end{aligned}
$$
Here $h$ is the ribosome-TE join hit rate against the reported CDS pool: $14410$ reported CDS rows for zebrafish, $3739$ for $E. coli$, $19053$ for human, and $5815$ for yeast. These ranks rule out the small-panel all-fit artifact: the statistic is computed on thousands of CDS-matched rows rather than a rank-saturated three-organism surface.

\paragraph{Measured TE survival.}
The survival statistic is the controlled partial explanatory power of the residualized nine-dimensional $Q$ block against residualized measured TE. With positivity threshold $10^{-12}$, every organism has a positive measured-TE entry and all nine coordinates survive the row-level fit. The observed values are
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{QT,\mathrm{meas}}(\textit{Danio rerio}) &= 0.04034217614654355,\\
R^2_{QT,\mathrm{meas}}(\textit{E. coli}~K12~MG1655) &= 0.009394266754822262,\\
R^2_{QT,\mathrm{meas}}(\textit{Homo sapiens}) &= 0.018837911344127926,\\
R^2_{QT,\mathrm{meas}}(\textit{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}) &= 0.13681931343730436.
\end{aligned}
$$
The yeast surface is the largest measured-TE survival row in this finite panel, followed by zebrafish, human, and $E. coli$. The organism ordering is a descriptive property of this panel only. It does not assert a universal codon law, an organismal adaptation direction, or a causal ordering among synonymous residuals, ribosome traffic, mRNA abundance, and protein output.

\paragraph{Relation to protein abundance.}
A separate protein-abundance surface shows that the same nine-coordinate panel has direct controlled association with measured PAXdb abundance in a larger organism set. In the four organisms overlapping the measured-TE test, direct $Q\to P$ values include $0.04613529807085213$ for zebrafish, $0.10767526677985896$ for $E. coli$, $0.04454565118019448$ for human, and $0.29837624994140827$ for yeast. Those values are not by themselves translation mediation. The measured mediation calculation on the same four-organism route reports
$$
\begin{aligned}
M^{QTP,\mathrm{meas}}(\textit{Danio rerio}) &= 0,\\
M^{QTP,\mathrm{meas}}(\textit{E. coli}~K12~MG1655) &= 0.04091158340950673,\\
M^{QTP,\mathrm{meas}}(\textit{Homo sapiens}) &= 0.0027966485036786744,\\
M^{QTP,\mathrm{meas}}(\textit{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}) &= 0.4793069656345167.
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the present section certifies the upstream measured $Q\to T$ contact, while downstream $Q\to T\to P$ interpretation must be read only through a separate mediation fit. In particular, the positive TE survival row for zebrafish coexists with a clipped zero measured mediation fraction in the abundance decomposition, so measured TE survival alone is not enough to claim protein-abundance propagation.

\paragraph{Readback.}
The finite-row readback is therefore: the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates are not translation-null on this rank-powered measured-TE panel. They retain positive controlled association with $\log_{10}(TE)$ in zebrafish, $E. coli$, human, and yeast after residualizing the declared coding-sequence controls. The matrix is a translation-readout association matrix, not a physical model of ribosome elongation. It says that the residual synonymous coordinates carry information about the measured footprint-per-mRNA response in these matched rows.

\paragraph{Local boundary.}
No stronger biological conclusion is licensed by this finite surface. The measured TE rows are condition-specific and come from one or a small number of ribosome-profiling studies per organism. A positive $S^{QT,\mathrm{meas}}$ entry is not a causal translation mechanism, not a universal translation-efficiency rule, not a protein-abundance claim, not a folding or structural-order claim, not a physical-admissibility assay, and not a function or phenotype claim. Protein abundance, localization, turnover, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, phenotype, and universality each require their own layer-matched contact and statistic. The present claim is exactly the bounded one: under the stated finite controls and ranks, the nine-coordinate $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual panel survives against measured ribosome TE.
