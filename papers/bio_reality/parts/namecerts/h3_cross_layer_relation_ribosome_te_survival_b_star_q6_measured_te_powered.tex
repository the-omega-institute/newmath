\subsection{Measured ribosome-profiling TE survival for the $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinates}
\label{sec:bior reality-q6-ribosome-te-survival-measured}

\paragraph{Finite row set.}
The record tested here is a finite CDS-level table in which nine $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinates are joined to organism-matched ribosome-profiling translation-efficiency measurements.  The coordinate list is
$K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $Leu_{CUN\,vs\,UUR}$, $Leu_{UUA\,vs\,UUG}$, $Ser_{UCR\,vs\,AGY}$, $Ser_{UCA\,vs\,UCG}$, $Thr_{ACR\,vs\,ACY}$, and $f3\_stress$.
The coordinate vectors were imported from \texttt{run\_b\_star\_q6\_translation\_survival\_powered.py}, and the measured readout is $\log_{10}(TE)$ with $TE=RPF/mRNA$ in the local ribosome-TE source files.  Thus the response is already normalized by matched mRNA abundance before the controlled residual calculation is performed.

\paragraph{Joined measured rows.}
Four organism panels have nondegenerate matched rows.  In \textit{Escherichia coli} K12 MG1655, $3739$ CDS rows were joined and $3415$ genes with TE were used, giving a join hit rate of $0.9133458143888741$.  In \textit{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}, $5815$ CDS rows were joined and $5128$ genes were used, with hit rate $0.8818572656921754$.  In \textit{Danio rerio}, $14410$ CDS rows were joined and $13140$ genes were used, with hit rate $0.9118667591950035$.  In \textit{Homo sapiens}, $19053$ CDS rows were joined and $11554$ genes were used, with hit rate $0.6064136881331024$.  The human panel is therefore usable but visibly more lossy at the identifier-join boundary than the other three panels.

\paragraph{Controlled residual design.}
For each organism, the calculation residualizes the TE response and the nine $Q6$ coordinates against the declared control family: amino-acid composition, organism identity as applicable to the panel routing, GC3, CDS length, $M$-density, and matched mRNA handling inherited by the TE denominator.  Each organism has $24$ control columns and rank $23$ for the control matrix.  The residualized $Q6$ design has rank $9$ in all four organisms, equal to the number of tested coordinates, so no panel is accepted merely because of a saturated all-perfect fit.  The rank tolerance is $10^{-10}$ and the degenerate-$R^2$ threshold is $0.999999$.

\paragraph{Power checks.}
The residual degrees of freedom after controls are $3392$ for \textit{E. coli}, $5105$ for yeast, $13117$ for zebrafish, and $11531$ for human.  The response residual energies are respectively $919.4854026798789$, $753.5781168800304$, $3588.378859021949$, and $2052.594706241978$.  The explained energies assigned to the residual $Q6$ block are $8.637891149939946$ for \textit{E. coli}, $103.10404057290246$ for yeast, $144.76301201119642$ for zebrafish, and $38.66659710161269$ for human.  These finite numbers are the reason the result is classified as powered rather than as a rank-defective or underdetermined contact.

\paragraph{Measured survival statistic.}
The positive threshold for a partial $R^2$ entry is $10^{-12}$.  Against this threshold, all nine residual coordinates survive in every organism panel.  The measured $R^2_{QT}$ values are
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{QT}(\textit{E. coli})&=0.009394266754822262,\\
R^2_{QT}(\textit{S. cerevisiae})&=0.13681931343730436,\\
R^2_{QT}(\textit{D. rerio})&=0.04034217614654355,\\
R^2_{QT}(\textit{H. sapiens})&=0.018837911344127926.
\end{aligned}
$$
The strongest measured panel is yeast, with $R^2_{QT}=0.13681931343730436$ after the declared controls.  The weakest positive panel is \textit{E. coli}, with $R^2_{QT}=0.009394266754822262$, still above the positivity threshold and still rank-powered.  The finite-row verdict is therefore that the $B^*_{Q6}$ residual block has measured TE association after amino-acid, GC3, length, $M$-density, and mRNA-normalized TE controls in the four tested panels.

\paragraph{Interpretation.}
This chapter records a controlled association between synonymous residual coordinates and measured ribosome-profiling TE.  It does not identify an elongation mechanism, a causal direction, a universal law of translation, or a protein-level consequence.  The TE values come from one or a small number of condition-specific ribo-seq studies, and the design is cross-sectional.  A positive $S^{QT,meas}$ entry should therefore be read as codon-residual information remaining in measured TE after the stated controls, not as proof of protein abundance, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, organismal phenotype, or mechanism.

\paragraph{Local no-promotion rule.}
The admissible conclusion stops at the measured TE boundary.  No general translation claim, no folding claim, no physical-admissibility claim, no function claim, no mechanism claim, and no universality claim follows from this row set unless another layer-matched contact supplies its own rows and its own controlled statistic.  Agreement or disagreement with modeled tAI is only a comparison of coordinate patterns; it is not causal validation and is not causal falsification.
