\subsection{Measured ribosome-footprint translation-efficiency contact for $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residuals}
\label{sec:biorality-q6-ribosome-te-survival}

\paragraph{Finite carrier.}
The finite object in this record is a CDS-indexed table whose rows are joined coding sequences and whose columns contain two separated blocks of information.  The first block is the nine-coordinate $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual panel
$K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $Leu_{CUN\,vs\,UUR}$, $Leu_{UUA\,vs\,UUG}$, $Ser_{UCR\,vs\,AGY}$, $Ser_{UCA\,vs\,UCG}$, $Thr_{ACR\,vs\,ACY}$, and $f3_{stress}$.  The second block is the organism-matched measured translation-efficiency readout, written as $\log_{10}(TE)$ with $TE=RPF/mRNA$ in the local ribosome-profiling files.  Thus the response is not raw transcript abundance: the measured footprint signal is already divided by matched mRNA abundance before the controlled regression is formed.  The experimental run is $d789816e1ab3$, and the finite contact is restricted to three organism panels: \textit{Escherichia coli} K12 MG1655, \textit{Homo sapiens}, and \textit{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}.

\paragraph{Controlled statistic.}
For each organism, the test residualizes the measured TE response and the nine residual-coordinate columns against a baseline $Z$ containing amino-acid composition, organism-level terms where applicable, $GC3$, CDS length, $M$-density, and the matched mRNA-control construction implicit in the measured TE denominator.  The survival score is the positive partial coefficient of determination contributed by the residualized $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ block after $Z$ has been removed.  The decision threshold for positivity is $10^{-12}$, and rank-saturated or degenerate panels are not allowed to count as survival.  In symbols, if $Y$ is the measured $\log_{10}(TE)$ vector, $X_Q$ is the nine-column residual-coordinate matrix, and $P_Z$ is the projection onto the control span, the fitted object is the residualized pair $((I-P_Z)Y,(I-P_Z)X_Q)$ rather than the unadjusted codon table.  The reported finite statistic is
$$
\begin{aligned}
S^{QT,meas} &= R^2\bigl((I-P_Z)Y \;\big|\; (I-P_Z)X_Q\bigr),\\
\text{survival} &\Longleftrightarrow S^{QT,meas}>10^{-12}\text{ with full residual rank for the nine-coordinate block.}
\end{aligned}
$$
This formulation makes the claim narrow: the row table tests whether the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates improve a measured TE readout after the declared controls.  It does not test protein abundance, elongation mechanism, folded structure, physical admissibility, molecular function, phenotype, or a universal biological law.

\paragraph{Joined rows and rank checks.}
The three organism panels are all rank-powered for the nine-coordinate question.  In \textit{E. coli} K12 MG1655, $3739$ CDS rows were joined to the codon table, $3415$ genes had measured TE and were used, and the ribosome-TE join hit rate was $0.9133458143888741$.  The control matrix had $24$ columns with rank $23$, the residualized $Q$ block had rank $9$, the residual degrees of freedom after $Z$ were $3392$, the response residual energy was $919.4854026798789$, and the explained energy attributed to the residualized $Q$ block was $8.637891149939946$.  In \textit{H. sapiens}, $19053$ CDS rows were joined, $11554$ genes were used, and the hit rate was $0.6064136881331024$; the corresponding rank data were again $24$ control columns, $rank(Z)=23$, $rank(\widetilde X_Q)=9$, residual degrees of freedom $11531$, response residual energy $2052.594706241978$, and explained energy $38.66659710161269$.  In \textit{S. cerevisiae}, $5815$ CDS rows were joined, $5128$ genes were used, and the hit rate was $0.8818572656921754$; the control and residual ranks were again $23$ and $9$, with residual degrees of freedom $5105$, response residual energy $753.5781168800304$, and explained energy $103.10404057290246$.

\paragraph{Measured survival values.}
All three organism panels pass the finite-rank screen and have positive measured TE survival for the residual-coordinate block.  The measured partial values are
$$
\begin{aligned}
S^{QT,meas}_{E.\ coli} &= 0.009394266754822262,\\
S^{QT,meas}_{H.\ sapiens} &= 0.018837911344127926,\\
S^{QT,meas}_{S.\ cerevisiae} &= 0.13681931343730436.
\end{aligned}
$$
Each value is above the declared $10^{-12}$ positivity threshold, and each panel reports $9$ surviving coordinates rather than a lower-rank or all-degenerate fit.  The strongest finite entry is the yeast panel, where the residual-coordinate block accounts for $0.13681931343730436$ of the post-control measured TE variance.  The human and bacterial panels are smaller but still positive under the same decision rule and full-rank residual design.  The survival statement is therefore a bounded cross-layer association: after amino-acid composition, $GC3$, length, $M$-density, and matched mRNA normalization are accounted for, the nine $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates retain measurable power against $\log_{10}(RPF/mRNA)$ in these organism-specific tables.

\paragraph{Interpretation boundary.}
The result promotes the residual-coordinate packet only to a measured TE association, not beyond it.  The finite rows are condition-specific, cross-sectional, and drawn from one or a small number of ribosome-profiling studies per organism.  A positive $S^{QT,meas}$ entry says that codon-residual coordinates improve the controlled TE readout in the joined table; it does not identify a causal ribosome mechanism, a universal direction of effect, an elongation-speed law, protein abundance, protein function, physical admissibility, folded structure, localization, phenotype, or a mediated $M^{QTP}$ pathway.  Agreement or disagreement with modeled tAI or stAI weights is only coordinate-pattern evidence inside this measured-TE comparison.  Any claim about actual translation mechanism, protein output, folding, physical admissibility, function, or universality would require a separate layer-matched contact and its own controlled statistic.
