\subsection{Measured ribosome-TE survival of the $B^*_{Q6}$ residual surface}
\label{sec:bioreality-b-star-q6-measured-te-survival}

\paragraph{Finite row surface.}
The measured ribosome-TE test attaches the nine-dimensional $B^*_{Q6}$ synonymous residual surface to organism-matched coding-sequence rows with a direct ribosome-profiling translation-efficiency readout.  The coordinate set is
$K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $Leu_{CUN\,vs\,UUR}$, $Leu_{UUA\,vs\,UUG}$, $Ser_{UCR\,vs\,AGY}$, $Ser_{UCA\,vs\,UCG}$, $Thr_{ACR\,vs\,ACY}$, and $f_3\_stress$.  The readout is $\log_{10}(TE)$, with $TE$ computed in the local rows as ribosome footprint divided by matched mRNA abundance.  Thus the translation readout is already mRNA-normalized before the residual coordinate regression is evaluated.

The finite organism panel has four powered rows.  The joined TE/codon surfaces use $13140$ genes for \emph{Danio rerio} from $14410$ reported CDS joins, hit rate $0.9118667591950035$; $3415$ genes for \emph{Escherichia coli} K12 MG1655 from $3739$ reported CDS joins, hit rate $0.9133458143888741$; $11554$ genes for \emph{Homo sapiens} from $19053$ reported CDS joins, hit rate $0.6064136881331024$; and $5128$ genes for \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae} from $5815$ reported CDS joins, hit rate $0.8818572656921754$.  In each row the residual design retains full $B^*_{Q6}$ rank: $rank(X_{\sim})=9$ for $9$ coordinates after residualization against $rank(Z)=23$ controls.  The control basis has $24$ columns and contains coding length, amino-acid composition, GC3, and the $M$-density baseline; the degeneracy threshold is $0.999999$ and the rank tolerance is $10^{-10}$.

\paragraph{Observed survival values.}
The powered statistic is the partial translation-readout survival of the residual coordinate block after the control basis has been removed.  The measured finite-row values are
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{QT,meas}(\textit{D. rerio}) &= 0.04034217614654355,\\
R^2_{QT,meas}(\textit{E. coli}) &= 0.009394266754822262,\\
R^2_{QT,meas}(\textit{H. sapiens}) &= 0.018837911344127926,\\
R^2_{QT,meas}(\textit{S. cerevisiae}) &= 0.13681931343730436.
\end{aligned}
$$
All four rows report $9$ surviving coordinates at the positive threshold $10^{-12}$.  The residual degrees of freedom after $Z$ are $13117$ for \emph{D. rerio}, $3392$ for \emph{E. coli}, $11531$ for \emph{H. sapiens}, and $5105$ for \emph{S. cerevisiae}.  The corresponding residual TE energies are $3588.378859021949$, $919.4854026798789$, $2052.594706241978$, and $753.5781168800304$.  The control-explained energies on the measured TE readout are $144.76301201119642$, $8.637891149939946$, $38.66659710161269$, and $103.10404057290246$, respectively.

These numbers establish a bounded translation-readout result: at finite rank, the $B^*_{Q6}$ residual block is not annihilated by length, amino-acid composition, GC3, or the $M$-density baseline when the response is measured ribosome-profiling TE.  The strongest row in this panel is yeast, with $R^2_{QT,meas}=0.13681931343730436$ on $5128$ genes; the weakest positive row is \emph{E. coli}, with $R^2_{QT,meas}=0.009394266754822262$ on $3415$ genes.  Since every row has $rank(X_{\sim})=9$, the result is not the all-$1.0$ rank-saturated failure mode that appears in underpowered organism-count surfaces.

\paragraph{Relation to measured protein abundance.}
The measured-TE survival row is a translation-layer contact, not by itself a protein-output or mechanism result.  A separate matched TE/protein-abundance decomposition on the same four organisms gives the following finite mediation scalars:
$$
\begin{aligned}
M^{QTP,meas}(\textit{D. rerio}) &= 0, & S^{QP}(\textit{D. rerio}) &= 0.04926578454614652,\\
M^{QTP,meas}(\textit{E. coli}) &= 0.04091158340950673, & S^{QP}(\textit{E. coli}) &= 0.10941535951828074,\\
M^{QTP,meas}(\textit{H. sapiens}) &= 0.0027966485036786744, & S^{QP}(\textit{H. sapiens}) &= 0.04288464996165745,\\
M^{QTP,meas}(\textit{S. cerevisiae}) &= 0.4793069656345167, & S^{QP}(\textit{S. cerevisiae}) &= 0.3146818503437042.
\end{aligned}
$$
The associated raw mediation fractions are $-0.04801486048721136$ for \emph{D. rerio}, $0.04091158340950673$ for \emph{E. coli}, $0.0027966485036786744$ for \emph{H. sapiens}, and $0.4793069656345167$ for \emph{S. cerevisiae}; the reported bounded fractions are clipped to the unit interval.  This comparison shows why measured TE survival and protein-abundance mediation must be kept distinct.  A positive $q_i \to T_j$ row licenses only a measured translation-readout association.  A downstream $q_i \to T_j \to P_k$ interpretation requires the separate protein-abundance fit and the observed attenuation of the direct $q_i \to P_k$ component after conditioning on $T_j$.

A broader three-mediator decomposition on yeast, human, and \emph{E. coli} further separates measured TE from mRNA stability.  The joint mediation fractions are $0.5195182225621592$ in yeast, $0.16890712099923358$ in human, and $0.2404669586626661$ in \emph{E. coli}; the right-tail permutation value is $0.000999000999000999$ in all three rows.  The TE-only fractions are $0.4697188319693239$, $0.00549490864190343$, and $0.01975521340963268$, while the mRNA-stability-only fractions are $0.0626618595344251$, $0.15314679870183337$, and $0.23052056324898387$.  These finite values confirm that the ribosome-TE surface can be positive without collapsing all downstream abundance structure into a single translation explanation.

\paragraph{Local boundary of the claim.}
The admissible conclusion is narrow.  In a finite, rank-powered, organism-matched panel, the nine $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinates have positive measured ribosome-TE survival after the stated controls, with all four organisms retaining full residual coordinate rank.  The claim does not say that the residual coordinates cause translation efficiency, encode a universal translation law, determine protein abundance, specify elongation speed, imply folded structural order, establish physical admissibility, realize molecular function, or explain phenotype.  The readout is condition-specific and cross-sectional; it is based on one or a small number of ribosome-profiling studies per organism.  Agreement or disagreement with modeled tAI is only a coordinate-pattern comparison.  Protein abundance, folding, physical viability, function, mechanism, and universality require separate layer-matched measurements and their own controlled statistics.
