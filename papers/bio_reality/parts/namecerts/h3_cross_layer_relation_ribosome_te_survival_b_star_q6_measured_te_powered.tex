\subsection{Measured ribosome-TE survival for the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual surface}
\label{sec:bireality-q6-measured-ribosome-te-survival}

\paragraph{Finite surface.}
This record tests whether the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates survive against a measured translation-efficiency readout after the ordinary coding-sequence covariates have already been removed. The carrier is a finite row matrix whose rows are CDS-matched genes and whose columns contain nine residual coordinates, one measured readout $T$, and a control block $Z$. The coordinate block has exactly nine columns:
$K\_AAA$, $Arg\_AGR$, $Ile\_AUA$, $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$, $Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG$, $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$, $Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG$, $Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY$, and $f3\_stress$. The measured readout is $\log_{10}(TE)$, with $TE$ defined in the local ribosome files as ribosome footprint divided by matched mRNA. Thus the readout is already mRNA-normalized before the residual survival fit is made.

\paragraph{Control geometry.}
The control block removes length, amino-acid composition, $GC3$, and the $M$-density baseline before the $Q$ block is tested. In every organism used here the residualized $Q$ block has full rank $9$, while the control block has rank $23$ from $24$ supplied columns. The rank tolerance is $10^{-10}$ and the degeneracy threshold is $0.999999$. The resulting per-organism residual degrees of freedom after $Z$ are $13117$ for Danio rerio, $3392$ for Escherichia coli K12 MG1655, $11531$ for Homo sapiens, and $5105$ for Saccharomyces cerevisiae. The corresponding residual readout energies are $3588.378859021949$, $919.4854026798789$, $2052.594706241978$, and $753.5781168800304$. These numbers certify that the displayed survival values are not the all-fitted surface produced by a rank-degenerate panel.

\paragraph{Joined measured-TE rows.}
Four organism panels have same-CDS codon and measured-TE joins. Danio rerio joins $13140$ genes out of $14410$ reported CDS rows, with hit rate $0.9118667591950035$. Escherichia coli K12 MG1655 joins $3415$ genes out of $3739$ reported CDS rows, with hit rate $0.9133458143888741$. Homo sapiens joins $11554$ genes out of $19053$ reported CDS rows, with hit rate $0.6064136881331024$. Saccharomyces cerevisiae joins $5128$ genes out of $5815$ reported CDS rows, with hit rate $0.8818572656921754$. These are the finite rows on which the measured ribosome-TE test is evaluated.

\paragraph{Measured survival statistic.}
For each organism the statistic is the partial explanatory power of the residualized nine-dimensional $Q$ block on residualized $\log_{10}(TE)$. With the positivity threshold fixed at $10^{-12}$, all nine coordinates survive in each of the four organism panels. The measured translation-readout survival values are
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{QT,\mathrm{meas}}(\mathrm{Danio\ rerio}) &= 0.04034217614654355,\\
R^2_{QT,\mathrm{meas}}(\mathrm{E.\ coli\ K12\ MG1655}) &= 0.009394266754822262,\\
R^2_{QT,\mathrm{meas}}(\mathrm{Homo\ sapiens}) &= 0.018837911344127926,\\
R^2_{QT,\mathrm{meas}}(\mathrm{Saccharomyces\ cerevisiae}) &= 0.13681931343730436.
\end{aligned}
$$
The largest measured-TE survival in this row set is therefore yeast, and the smallest is E. coli, but all four panels remain strictly positive under the declared threshold and full-rank residualized design.

\paragraph{Relation to protein abundance.}
The same coordinate family also has a direct protein-abundance readout in a wider PAXdb panel, where every listed organism has nine surviving coordinates against $\log_{10}$ protein abundance after the same type of controls. For the four measured-TE organisms, the direct protein-abundance $R^2_{QP}$ values in the protein-only surface are $0.10767526677985896$ for E. coli K12 MG1655, $0.04454565118019448$ for Homo sapiens, and $0.29837624994140827$ for Saccharomyces cerevisiae; Danio rerio has $R^2_{QP}=0.04613529807085213$ in the direct protein-omics panel. These direct protein associations are not read as translation mechanisms. They are downstream associations that require a separate mediation fit before they can be linked to measured TE.

\paragraph{Measured mediation check.}
The paired measured-TE and protein-abundance mediation run supplies that separate finite fit. Its measured $S_{QP}$ and $S_{QT}$ surfaces give raw mediated fractions $-0.04801486048721136$ for Danio rerio, $0.04091158340950673$ for E. coli K12 MG1655, $0.0027966485036786744$ for Homo sapiens, and $0.4793069656345167$ for Saccharomyces cerevisiae. After truncation to the reported unit interval, the Danio value is recorded as $0.0$, while the other three remain positive. The associated three-way row counts are $13140$, $3415$, $11554$, and $5128$, respectively. Hence this chapter's measured-TE survival result is strongest as a bounded translation-readout association, and only the separate mediation surface can discuss whether conditioning on $T$ absorbs any part of $Q \to P$.

\paragraph{Adjacent readout dictionary.}
The broader yeast readout-dictionary certificate keeps the same caution visible. On a unified yeast context join with $n=1800$, five folds, $\lambda_{DL}=0.01$, and $\rho_{join}=0.05$, the best selected family from measured TE, mRNA stability, and turnover covers $C^{\ast}=0.24538211439730545$ of the residual protein-abundance signal, leaving $0.7546178856026946$ unexplained. The raw best coverage over the candidate family is $0.2745002077400602$, the selection-aware null $95$ percentile is $0.029149007242956115$, and the naive fixed-subset null $95$ percentile is $0.00598374993486675$. This confirms that measured readouts can carry real downstream signal while still leaving most of the abundance residual outside the small dictionary.

\paragraph{Four-case readback.}
The modeled-tAI four-case classifier is not the measured-TE result, but it supplies a useful local contrast. In its two-organism run, E. coli has $R^2_{QT}=0.5416736993691318$ and $R^2_{QP}=0.10767526677985896$, with seven coordinates in case D and two positive-positive coordinates without a mediation drop. Yeast has $R^2_{QT}=0.8213457220900503$ and $R^2_{QP}=0.29837624994140827$, with all nine coordinates in case D. Because that readout is modeled tAI rather than measured ribosome TE, it is treated as a coordinate-pattern comparison, not as direct ribosome evidence.

\paragraph{Boundary of interpretation.}
The finite conclusion is narrow. The rows support positive $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ survival against measured ribosome-derived $\log_{10}(TE)$ after length, amino-acid composition, $GC3$, and $M$-density controls in four rank-powered organism panels. This does not by itself establish a causal translation mechanism, elongation speed, protein abundance mediation, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, phenotype, evolutionary direction, or a universal biological rule. Protein abundance, localization, turnover, structure, function, and perturbation claims require their own matched contacts and their own controlled statistics. Direct $Q \to P$ association remains a prediction surface unless a measured $Q \to T$ step is present and conditioning on $T$ removes or reduces the corresponding $Q \to P$ signal.
