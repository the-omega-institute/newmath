\subsection{Rank-Powered Measured-TE Survival for $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ Residual Coordinates}
\label{sec:bioreality-q6-ribosome-te-survival-measured}

\paragraph{Finite surface.}
This record tests whether the nine-dimensional $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ synonymous residual surface has a nonzero measured translation-efficiency readout after sequence-composition controls.  The finite carrier is the matrix whose rows are organism-matched CDS units and whose columns are the nine residual coordinates
$K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $Leu_{CUN\,vs\,UUR}$, $Leu_{UUA\,vs\,UUG}$, $Ser_{UCR\,vs\,AGY}$, $Ser_{UCA\,vs\,UCG}$, $Thr_{ACR\,vs\,ACY}$, and $f_3$-stress.  The readout is $\log_{10}(TE)$, where the local source files define measured $TE$ as ribosome footprint divided by matched mRNA.  Thus the translation readout is already normalized by mRNA in its construction; the regression still residualizes against the declared sequence controls before estimating the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ contribution.

\paragraph{Controls and rank.}
For each organism, the controlled design removes an intercept, CDS length, amino-acid composition, GC3, and the M-density baseline before the residual coordinate block is tested.  The control basis has 24 columns and rank 23 in every measured-TE organism.  The residualized $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ block keeps rank 9 in all four organisms, so the reported positive entries are not rank-degenerate all-ones artifacts.  The finite rank diagnostics are
$$
\begin{aligned}
\operatorname{rank}(Z)&=23,\\
\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde Q)&=9,\\
\tau_{\mathrm{rank}}&=10^{-10},\\
R^2_{\mathrm{degenerate}}&=0.999999.
\end{aligned}
$$
The residual degrees of freedom after the control projection are 13117 for \textit{Danio rerio}, 3392 for \textit{Escherichia coli} K12 MG1655, 11531 for \textit{Homo sapiens}, and 5105 for \textit{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}.  These values are the finite-row reason the packet is treated as rank-powered rather than as a three-organism saturation boundary.

\paragraph{Matched measured-TE rows.}
The organism joins are concrete rather than symbolic.  \textit{Danio rerio} has 14410 CDS rows reported and 13140 genes with measured TE, giving a ribosome-TE join hit rate of 0.9118667591950035.  \textit{E. coli} K12 MG1655 has 3739 reported CDS rows and 3415 measured-TE genes, with hit rate 0.9133458143888741.  \textit{Homo sapiens} has 19053 reported CDS rows and 11554 measured-TE genes, with hit rate 0.6064136881331024.  \textit{S. cerevisiae} has 5815 reported CDS rows and 5128 measured-TE genes, with hit rate 0.8818572656921754.  The lower human hit rate is kept as a finite-row limitation; it does not invalidate the powered rank check, but it makes the human entry a study-conditioned readout rather than a universal translation statement.

\paragraph{Measured-TE survival values.}
The controlled measured-TE survival statistic is positive in every organism in the powered panel.  The reported values are
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{QT,\mathrm{meas}}(\textit{D. rerio})&=0.04034217614654355,\\
R^2_{QT,\mathrm{meas}}(\textit{E. coli}\ K12)&=0.009394266754822262,\\
R^2_{QT,\mathrm{meas}}(\textit{H. sapiens})&=0.018837911344127926,\\
R^2_{QT,\mathrm{meas}}(\textit{S. cerevisiae})&=0.13681931343730436.
\end{aligned}
$$
Each organism reports nine surviving coordinates at the positive partial-$R^2$ threshold $10^{-12}$.  The strongest measured-TE entry is yeast, where the residual coordinate block explains 103.10404057290246 units of measured-TE residual energy out of 753.5781168800304 after controls.  The smallest positive entry is \textit{E. coli}, where the block explains 8.637891149939946 out of 919.4854026798789.  Human and zebrafish sit between these endpoints, with explained energies 38.66659710161269 out of 2052.594706241978 and 144.76301201119642 out of 3588.378859021949, respectively.

\paragraph{Four-case interpretation at the translation layer.}
This finite surface supports a bounded claim: the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates survive against measured ribosome-derived $TE$ after the stated controls.  It does not by itself say that a protein-abundance association is mediated by translation.  The separate measured protein-abundance mediation fit reports measured fractions of 0 for \textit{D. rerio}, 0.04091158340950673 for \textit{E. coli}, 0.0027966485036786744 for human, and 0.4793069656345167 for yeast.  Those values show that a positive $Q\to T$ entry and a positive $Q\to P$ entry need not have the same downstream interpretation.  In the same finite-row language, the direct protein-omics survival surface contains positive $R^2_{QP}$ entries across a larger protein-abundance panel, including 0.04454565118019448 for human, 0.049827984062539195 for mouse, 0.10767526677985896 for \textit{E. coli}, and 0.29837624994140827 for yeast; those are direct abundance associations until an explicit conditioning step shows attenuation through a measured translation readout.

\paragraph{Mediation and stability context.}
The strongest organism-specific downstream contrast is yeast.  In the joint measured-TE and mRNA-stability decomposition, yeast has $R^2_{QP}=0.2715520808117803$, $R^2_{QP\mid T}=0.1439989545940314$, $R^2_{QP\mid M}=0.25453612246767165$, and $R^2_{QP\mid T,M}=0.13047582645538838$, giving a joint mediation fraction of 0.5195182225621592.  The same run gives a measured-TE-only fraction of 0.4697188319693239, a stability-only fraction of 0.0626618595344251, and right-tail permutation value 0.000999000999000999 for the joint pair.  Human and \textit{E. coli} also have positive joint fractions, 0.16890712099923358 and 0.2404669586626661, respectively, with the same recorded permutation value.  These numbers are included only to locate the measured-TE survival row inside the larger abundance-decomposition surface; the present subsection's direct result remains $Q\to TE$, not a causal pathway.

\paragraph{Local verdict.}
The measured-TE survival claim is ready at the finite-row level.  The required readout rows exist for four organisms, the control residualization is nondegenerate, the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ block has full residual rank 9, and every organism has $R^2_{QT,\mathrm{meas}}>10^{-12}$.  The paper may therefore state that $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates retain measurable association with ribosome-derived translation efficiency after mRNA-normalized TE construction and sequence-composition controls.  The same statement must remain bounded: no translation mechanism, elongation mechanism, protein folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, phenotype, evolutionary direction, or universal biological law follows from this surface alone.  Such claims require their own matched contact and their own controlled finite-row statistic.
