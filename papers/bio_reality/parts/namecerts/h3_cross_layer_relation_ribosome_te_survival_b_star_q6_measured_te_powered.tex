\subsection{Measured ribosome-TE survival for the $B^*_{Q6}$ residual surface}
\label{sec:bioreality-q6-measured-ribosome-te-survival}

\paragraph{Finite row object.}
The measured ribosome-TE test uses a finite matrix whose rows are coding sequences with an organism-matched codon-count vector and a measured translation-efficiency readout.  The nine residual coordinates are $K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $Leu_{CUN\,vs\,UUR}$, $Leu_{UUA\,vs\,UUG}$, $Ser_{UCR\,vs\,AGY}$, $Ser_{UCA\,vs\,UCG}$, $Thr_{ACR\,vs\,ACY}$, and $f3_{stress}$.  For each organism the response is $\log_{10}(TE)$, where the local files define $TE$ as ribosome footprint divided by matched mRNA abundance.  Thus the readout is already mRNA-normalized before the residual regression is fit.  The covariate block contains an intercept, coding-sequence length, amino-acid composition, $GC3$, and the $M$-density baseline.  The estimated surface is therefore not a bare codon-composition correlation; it is a residual association after the declared composition and sequence-length controls.

\paragraph{Rank and row support.}
The test is rank-powered in all four measured-TE panels.  Danio rerio joins $13{,}140$ genes with measured TE out of $14{,}410$ reported CDS rows, giving a ribosome-TE hit rate of $0.9118667591950035$; after the control projection it has $rank(Z)=23$, $rank(\widetilde X)=9$, residual degrees of freedom $13{,}117$, and TE residual energy $3588.378859021949$.  Escherichia coli K12 MG1655 joins $3{,}415$ genes out of $3{,}739$ reported CDS rows, hit rate $0.9133458143888741$, with $rank(Z)=23$, $rank(\widetilde X)=9$, residual degrees of freedom $3392$, and TE residual energy $919.4854026798789$.  Homo sapiens joins $11{,}554$ measured-TE genes out of $19{,}053$ reported CDS rows, hit rate $0.6064136881331024$, with $rank(Z)=23$, $rank(\widetilde X)=9$, residual degrees of freedom $11{,}531$, and TE residual energy $2052.594706241978$.  Saccharomyces cerevisiae joins $5{,}128$ measured-TE genes out of $5{,}815$ reported CDS rows, hit rate $0.8818572656921754$, with $rank(Z)=23$, $rank(\widetilde X)=9$, residual degrees of freedom $5105$, and TE residual energy $753.5781168800304$.  The rank tolerance is $10^{-10}$, the degenerate-$R^2$ cutoff is $0.999999$, and every panel preserves all nine residual-coordinate columns after the control projection.

\paragraph{Measured translation-readout survival.}
Let $Q$ denote the nine-column residual-coordinate block and let $T_{meas}$ denote the measured ribosome-derived TE response after the same control residualization.  The finite-row statistic is the controlled explanatory fraction of $T_{meas}$ supplied by $Q$.  The four observed survival values are
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{QT,meas}(\mathrm{Danio\ rerio}) &= 0.04034217614654355,\\
R^2_{QT,meas}(\mathrm{E.\ coli\ K12}) &= 0.009394266754822262,\\
R^2_{QT,meas}(\mathrm{Homo\ sapiens}) &= 0.018837911344127926,\\
R^2_{QT,meas}(\mathrm{S.\ cerevisiae}) &= 0.13681931343730436.
\end{aligned}
$$
The positivity threshold is $10^{-12}$, and the surviving-coordinate count is $9$ in each of the four organisms.  This places the packet on the positive measured-translation-readout side of the four-case dictionary: every coordinate survives against measured TE in every tested rank-powered organism, but the magnitude is organism-dependent.  Yeast is the strongest measured-TE case, with $R^2_{QT,meas}=0.13681931343730436$; E. coli is positive but much smaller, with $R^2_{QT,meas}=0.009394266754822262$.

\paragraph{Relation to protein-abundance mediation.}
The same measured-TE row family can be compared with the downstream abundance decomposition, but the two statements are logically separate.  In the measured $Q \to T \to P$ protein-abundance run, the three-way joins are $13{,}140$ rows for Danio rerio, $3{,}415$ for E. coli, $11{,}554$ for Homo sapiens, and $5{,}128$ for S. cerevisiae.  The measured mediation fractions are $0.0$ for Danio rerio after truncating the raw suppressive value $-0.04801486048721136$, $0.04091158340950673$ for E. coli, $0.0027966485036786744$ for Homo sapiens, and $0.4793069656345167$ for S. cerevisiae.  Hence measured TE survival is not automatically equivalent to a strong protein-abundance mediation claim.  Yeast has both the largest measured TE survival and the largest measured mediation fraction, while Danio rerio has positive measured TE survival but no positive measured protein-abundance mediation fraction under this scalar decomposition.

\paragraph{Direct protein-omics contrast.}
A separate direct protein-abundance surface reports positive $Q \to P$ association in a broader proteomics panel, again without turning that association into a translation mechanism.  In that direct surface the $R^2_{QP}$ values include $0.10767526677985896$ for E. coli, $0.04454565118019448$ for Homo sapiens, $0.049827984062539195$ for Mus musculus, $0.29837624994140827$ for S. cerevisiae, and $0.019256877907572044$ for Halobacterium salinarum, with all nine coordinates surviving at the direct protein-omics threshold in the reported organisms.  These direct protein-omics values show that the $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinates can predict abundance after the composition controls, but the measured-TE chapter only licenses the narrower claim that $Q$ carries measured ribosome-TE signal.  A downstream mechanism statement requires a positive $Q \to T$ step together with attenuation or absorption of the $Q \to P$ signal after conditioning on the measured $T$ readout.

In Homo sapiens the joint mediation fraction is $0.16890712099923358$, TE-only fraction $0.00549490864190343$, stability-only fraction $0.15314679870183337$, and right-tail permutation value $0.000999000999000999$.  The partial readout-dictionary certificate on the yeast context uses $n=1800$, five folds, $\lambda_{DL}=0.01$, permutation count $120$, and reports coverage $C^*=0.24538211439730545$ with selection-aware null $95\%$ level $0.029149007242956115$, leaving unexplained fraction $0.7546178856026946$.  These numbers support a partial-reduction reading, not a complete biological mechanism.

\paragraph{Local boundary.}
The measured ribosome-TE surface establishes a controlled finite-row association between the nine $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinates and measured $\log_{10}(TE)$ in four organism panels.  It does not by itself establish universal translation efficiency, elongation speed, protein abundance, protein folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, system phenotype, causal mediation, or evolutionary direction.  The structural-order positional check illustrates the same rule: the registered structural-order ramp result is classified as $\mathrm{ramp\_positive\_but\_not\_localized}$, with primary ramp size $50$, weighted ramp increment $0.0011165640103326878$, weighted body increment $-0.00026157313923957926$, and paired ramp-minus-body sign-flip value $0.05859375$.  A positive ribosome-TE survival entry therefore remains a measured translation-readout statistic unless a separate layer-matched contact supplies the further protein-state, structure, physical, functional, perturbational, or phenotype evidence.
