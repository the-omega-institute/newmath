\subsection{Measured ribosome-TE survival of the rank-powered $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual surface}
\label{sec:bioreality-ribosome-te-survival-b-star-q6-measured}

\paragraph{Finite row object.}
The record is a finite matrix test over nine $B^{*}_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates, evaluated against measured ribosome-profiling translation efficiency on organism-matched coding sequences.  The coordinate list is $K\_AAA$, $Arg\_AGR$, $Ile\_AUA$, $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$, $Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG$, $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$, $Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG$, $Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY$, and $f3\_stress$.  The readout is $\log_{10}(TE)$, with $TE$ supplied in the local ribosome tables as footprint divided by matched mRNA.  Thus the translation measurement used here is already mRNA-normalized by construction, while the regression still removes the declared sequence covariates before asking whether the residual coordinate block carries measured-TE signal.

\paragraph{Controls and rank check.}
For each organism, the response and the nine-coordinate design were residualized against the same $Z$ family: intercept, coding length, amino-acid composition, GC3, and the $M$-density baseline.  The residualized control matrix had rank $23$ with $24$ control columns in every measured-TE organism, and the residualized $B^{*}_{Q6}$ design had rank $9$ in every case.  The powered rows were therefore nondegenerate rather than saturated: Danio rerio had residual degree count $13117$ and TE residual energy $3588.378859021949$; Escherichia coli K12 MG1655 had $3392$ and $919.4854026798789$; Homo sapiens had $11531$ and $2052.594706241978$; Saccharomyces cerevisiae had $5105$ and $753.5781168800304$.  The corresponding explained energies of the nine-coordinate block on the measured-TE residual were $144.76301201119642$, $8.637891149939946$, $38.66659710161269$, and $103.10404057290246$, respectively.  The positivity cutoff used for the survival call was $10^{-12}$, and all four rank checks used tolerance $10^{-10}$ with the degeneracy guard set at $0.999999$.

\paragraph{Joined measured-TE rows.}
The measured ribosome-TE contact is not an identifier-only route in this record; it is attached to concrete joined coding-sequence rows.  Danio rerio joined $13140$ genes out of $14410$ reported CDS rows, with hit rate $0.9118667591950035$.  Escherichia coli K12 MG1655 joined $3415$ genes out of $3739$ reported CDS rows, with hit rate $0.9133458143888741$.  Homo sapiens joined $11554$ genes out of $19053$ reported CDS rows, with hit rate $0.6064136881331024$.  Saccharomyces cerevisiae joined $5128$ genes out of $5815$ reported CDS rows, with hit rate $0.8818572656921754$.  These row counts are large enough for the reported survival entries to be read as powered finite-row statistics rather than as the all-ones artifact that can occur when an organism panel is rank-degenerate.

\paragraph{Survival values.}
The measured-TE survival surface is positive in all four attached organisms.  The block-level values were
$$
\begin{aligned}
S^{QT,\mathrm{meas}}_{Danio} &= 0.04034217614654355,\\
S^{QT,\mathrm{meas}}_{E.coli} &= 0.009394266754822262,\\
S^{QT,\mathrm{meas}}_{Human} &= 0.018837911344127926,\\
S^{QT,\mathrm{meas}}_{Yeast} &= 0.13681931343730436.
\end{aligned}
$$
In each organism all nine residual coordinates had positive per-coordinate survival at the stated threshold.  The strongest measured-TE block entry is the yeast entry, $0.13681931343730436$; the weakest is the E. coli entry, $0.009394266754822262$.  The correct readback is therefore not a universal translation law, but a finite statement: after the declared sequence controls and $M$-density baseline, the nine-coordinate $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual block retains measurable association with ribosome-derived translation efficiency in each attached powered organism.

\paragraph{Relation to protein abundance mediation.}
The measured-TE survival row is a necessary translation-layer contact for downstream protein-abundance mediation, but it is not itself a protein-output claim.  A separate measured mediation computation on the same four organisms reported $S^{QP}$ values $0.04926578454614652$ for Danio rerio, $0.10941535951828074$ for E. coli, $0.04288464996165745$ for human, and $0.3146818503437042$ for yeast.  Conditioning the protein-abundance fit on measured TE gave clipped mediated fractions $0.0$, $0.04091158340950673$, $0.0027966485036786744$, and $0.4793069656345167$, respectively.  Thus the strongest translation-conditioned absorption in the measured-TE data is yeast, where nearly half of the direct $B^{*}_{Q6}$ to abundance block signal is absorbed by the measured TE readout; the human and E. coli rows show much smaller absorption, and the Danio row is suppressive before clipping.  A broader joint TE and mRNA-stability decomposition agrees that the translation readout is not the whole downstream story: in yeast, the joint measured-TE plus mRNA-stability mediation fraction was $0.5195182225621592$, with TE-only fraction $0.4697188319693239$ and stability-only fraction $0.0626618595344251$; in E. coli the joint fraction was $0.2404669586626661$, mostly stability-only at $0.23052056324898387$ with TE-only $0.01975521340963268$; in human the joint fraction was $0.16890712099923358$, mostly stability-only at $0.15314679870183337$ with TE-only $0.00549490864190343$.  Those numbers keep the translation-survival fact and the protein-abundance mediation fact distinct.

\paragraph{Readout dictionary boundary.}
A yeast readout-dictionary certificate further constrains the interpretation.  On a unified yeast join with $n=1800$, five folds, dictionary penalty $\lambda=0.01$, and $120$ deterministic row-label permutations, the best selection-aware coverage was $C^{*}=0.24538211439730545$ against a selection-aware null $95$ percent point of $0.029149007242956115$.  The raw best coverage over the candidate family was $0.2745002077400602$, the naive fixed-subset null $95$ percent point was $0.00598374993486675$, and the remaining unexplained fraction was $0.7546178856026946$.  This finite-row result is compatible with the measured-TE survival row: measured TE is a real attached readout, but the abundance residual is only partially reducible to a small measured-readout dictionary.

\paragraph{Local verdict.}
The claim is ready at the translation-readout level.  The finite evidence supports a positive $B^{*}_{Q6}\rightarrow TE$ association after length, amino-acid composition, GC3, and $M$-density controls in four powered organism joins.  The evidence does not, by itself, establish translation mechanism, elongation speed, protein abundance, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, phenotype, evolutionary direction, or universality.  Those stronger assertions require separate layer-matched contacts and their own controlled finite-row statistics.  In particular, a direct $Q\rightarrow P$ association remains only a downstream omics association unless the same-row measured translation readout both survives and removes or reduces the direct protein-abundance signal under the declared controls.
