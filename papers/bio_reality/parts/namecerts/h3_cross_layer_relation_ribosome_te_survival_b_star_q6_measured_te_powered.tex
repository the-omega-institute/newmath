\paragraph{Local non-promotion boundary.}
This chapter does not infer protein abundance, folded structure, physical admissibility, molecular function, phenotype, mechanism, elongation speed, or a universal biological law from the measured $TE$ association.  The ribosome-profiling contact is condition-specific, cross-sectional, and taken from one or a small number of source studies.  Agreement or disagreement with modeled adaptation weights would be coordinate-pattern evidence only, not causal validation and not causal falsification.  Any claim about actual translation mechanism, protein output, structure, admissibility, function, phenotype, or universality must be supplied by a separate layer-matched measurement and its own controlled statistic.
