\subsection{Measured ribosome-TE survival for $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinates}\label{sec:bioreality-ribosome-te-survival-b-star-q6-measured-te-powered}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite row object.}
This record concerns a finite matrix surface whose rows are matched coding sequences and whose columns are the nine $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinates
$K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $Leu_{CUN\,\mathrm{vs}\,UUR}$, $Leu_{UUA\,\mathrm{vs}\,UUG}$, $Ser_{UCR\,\mathrm{vs}\,AGY}$, $Ser_{UCA\,\mathrm{vs}\,UCG}$, $Thr_{ACR\,\mathrm{vs}\,ACY}$, and $f3_{\mathrm{stress}}$.
The readout is $\log_{10}(\mathrm{TE})$, with $\mathrm{TE}$ computed in the local ribosome-profiling files as ribosome footprint divided by matched mRNA. Thus the measured translation readout is already mRNA-normalized before the $B^*_{Q6}$ regression is fitted. Each organism-level fit first removes a control design $Z$ consisting of an intercept, coding-sequence length, amino-acid composition, $GC3$, and the $M$-density baseline. The recorded threshold for a positive partial contribution is $10^{-12}$, and the degeneracy cutoff used in the rank audit is $0.999999$.

\paragraph{Rank and row audit.}
The four measured-TE panels are all rank-powered for the nine-coordinate test after residualization. The organism joins are concrete: Danio rerio uses $13140$ genes out of $14410$ reported CDS joins, with hit rate $0.9118667591950035$; Escherichia coli K12 MG1655 uses $3415$ genes out of $3739$, with hit rate $0.9133458143888741$; Homo sapiens uses $11554$ genes out of $19053$, with hit rate $0.6064136881331024$; Saccharomyces cerevisiae uses $5128$ genes out of $5815$, with hit rate $0.8818572656921754$. In every case the residualized $B^*_{Q6}$ design has rank $9$ and the residualized control design has rank $23$, with $24$ control columns before rank reduction. The residual degrees of freedom after $Z$ are $13117$, $3392$, $11531$, and $5105$, respectively. The rank tolerance is $10^{-10}$.

$$
\begin{aligned}
\operatorname{rank}(X_Q \mid Z) &= 9,\\
\operatorname{rank}(Z) &= 23,\\
\operatorname{df}_{\mathrm{res}}(D.\,rerio,E.\,coli,H.\,sapiens,S.\,cerevisiae) &= (13117,3392,11531,5105).
\end{aligned}
$$

The measured-TE residual energies after the $Z$ projection are also nonzero and finite: $3588.378859021949$ for Danio rerio, $919.4854026798789$ for Escherichia coli, $2052.594706241978$ for Homo sapiens, and $753.5781168800304$ for Saccharomyces cerevisiae. The corresponding energy explained by the nine-coordinate residual design is $144.76301201119642$, $8.637891149939946$, $38.66659710161269$, and $103.10404057290246$.

\paragraph{Measured-TE survival result.}
The measured ribosome-TE survival statistic is positive in all four rank-powered organism panels. The recorded values are
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{QT,\mathrm{meas}}(D.\,rerio) &= 0.04034217614654355,\\
R^2_{QT,\mathrm{meas}}(E.\,coli) &= 0.009394266754822262,\\
R^2_{QT,\mathrm{meas}}(H.\,sapiens) &= 0.018837911344127926,\\
R^2_{QT,\mathrm{meas}}(S.\,cerevisiae) &= 0.13681931343730436.
\end{aligned}
$$
For each organism the surviving-coordinate count is $9$, so the finite-row readback is not a rank-degenerate $n=3$ all-one surface. It is a measured, joined, organism-specific codon-residual association with $\log_{10}(\mathrm{TE})$ after the stated controls. The strongest measured-TE survival among the four panels is the yeast value $0.13681931343730436$; the weakest is the Escherichia coli value $0.009394266754822262$. Both remain strictly positive relative to the declared $10^{-12}$ positivity threshold.

\paragraph{Relation to protein abundance mediation.}
The same coordinate family was also tested against measured PAXdb protein abundance and against measured-TE-conditioned abundance decompositions. Direct protein-omics survival alone is not interpreted as a mechanism, but it gives the downstream comparison surface: $R^2_{QP}$ is $0.04613529807085213$ in Danio rerio, $0.10767526677985896$ in Escherichia coli, $0.04454565118019448$ in Homo sapiens, and $0.29837624994140827$ in Saccharomyces cerevisiae in the broader direct abundance panel, with nine surviving coordinates in each listed organism. On the measured-TE and abundance three-way joins, the statistical mediation fractions are organism-specific: Danio rerio has $S_{QP}=0.04926578454614652$, $S_{QT,\mathrm{meas}}=0.04034217614654355$, and a clipped measured mediated fraction $0.0$ from raw value $-0.04801486048721136$; Escherichia coli has $S_{QP}=0.10941535951828074$, $S_{QT,\mathrm{meas}}=0.009394266754822262$, and measured mediated fraction $0.04091158340950673$; Homo sapiens has $S_{QP}=0.04288464996165745$, $S_{QT,\mathrm{meas}}=0.018837911344127926$, and measured mediated fraction $0.0027966485036786744$; Saccharomyces cerevisiae has $S_{QP}=0.3146818503437042$, $S_{QT,\mathrm{meas}}=0.13681931343730436$, and measured mediated fraction $0.4793069656345167$.

$$
\begin{aligned}
M^{QTP,\mathrm{meas}}_{\mathrm{clip}}(D.\,rerio,E.\,coli,H.\,sapiens,S.\,cerevisiae)
&= (0.0,0.04091158340950673,0.0027966485036786744,0.4793069656345167).
\end{aligned}
$$

These numbers separate two claims. First, $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinates survive against a measured ribosome-TE readout in all four rank-powered panels. Second, absorption of a codon-to-protein abundance signal by the measured TE readout is a separate organism-level decomposition, strongest here in yeast and weak in the human and bacterial measured-TE mediation rows. A positive $Q \to P$ association is therefore not promoted to a translation mechanism unless the $Q \to T$ step is present and conditioning on $T$ reduces the direct abundance association.

\paragraph{Neighboring finite-row constraints.}
The joint measured-TE and mRNA-stability abundance decomposition supplies an additional check on this interpretation. In the three organism four-way join, the joint mediation fractions are $0.2404669586626661$ for Escherichia coli, $0.16890712099923358$ for Homo sapiens, and $0.5195182225621592$ for Saccharomyces cerevisiae; the right-tail permutation values are $0.000999000999000999$ in all three cases. The single-mediator split is not uniform: yeast has a TE-only fraction $0.4697188319693239$ and stability-only fraction $0.0626618595344251$, whereas Escherichia coli has TE-only fraction $0.01975521340963268$ and stability-only fraction $0.23052056324898387$. This reinforces the local reading: measured TE is a real translation-layer readout, but the downstream abundance relation can be shared with stability or other non-TE readouts.

The $f3_{\mathrm{stress}}$ row gives the same warning from a different finite test. In yeast, the dwell-mediated abundance component has base $R^2=0.1855578757644517$, full $R^2=0.20412261520502883$, held-out $\Delta R^2=0.01856473944057712$, and a deterministic permutation null $95$th percentile $0.0013115362189692937$, with approximate $f3$-dwell $p=7.873916057196935\times 10^{-9}$. The partial readout dictionary test on the yeast context join used $\lambda_{DL}=0.01$, $\rho_{\mathrm{join}}=0.05$, $120$ permutations, $n_{\mathrm{join}}=1800$, and found selection-aware coverage $C^*=0.24538211439730545$, leaving unexplained fraction $0.7546178856026946$. These scalar audits make the chapter finite rather than universal: measured TE explains part of the interface, while a substantial residual remains for other readouts or unmeasured structure in the data-generating process.

\paragraph{Local boundary.}
The conclusion is deliberately narrow. The chapter supports measured ribosome-TE survival of the nine $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinates on four organism panels after length, amino-acid composition, $GC3$, and $M$-density controls. It does not by itself establish universal translation efficiency, elongation speed, a causal pathway, protein abundance mediation in every organism, folded structure, physical admissibility, molecular function, phenotype, or an evolutionary direction. Those stronger statements require separate matched contacts and their own finite statistics. Here the only positive claim is that the measured footprint-over-mRNA TE rows carry a nonzero, rank-powered $B^*_{Q6}$ codon-residual signal under the declared controls, and that downstream protein-abundance interpretation must pass through an explicit measured-TE conditioning test rather than through direct $Q \to P$ association alone.
