\subsection{Measured ribosome-efficiency survival for $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinates}
\label{sec:bioreality-q6-measured-ribosome-te-survival}

\paragraph{Finite carrier.}
This record tests whether the nine-dimensional $B^*_{Q6}$ synonymous residual surface remains visible in measured ribosome-efficiency data after the usual compositional controls have been removed.  The finite carrier consists of per-CDS rows joined across codon counts and a measured translation-efficiency readout $T$, with $T=\mathrm{RPF}/\mathrm{mRNA}$ in the local ribosome-profiling files.  The residual coordinate list is
$$
\begin{aligned}
Q=\{&\mathrm{K\_AAA},\ \mathrm{Arg\_AGR},\ \mathrm{Ile\_AUA},\ \mathrm{Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR},\ \mathrm{Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG},\\
&\mathrm{Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY},\ \mathrm{Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG},\ \mathrm{Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY},\ f3\_\mathrm{stress}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
For every organism in the measured-TE panel, the residualized $Q$ design has rank $9$ after the $Z$ controls, while the control design has rank $23$ from $24$ supplied columns.  The controls remove length, amino-acid composition, GC3, and the $M$-density baseline before any $Q\to T$ association is read.

\paragraph{Matched rows and rank.}
Four organism panels are present and rank-powered.  In \textit{Danio rerio}, $13140$ measured-TE genes are used out of $14410$ CDS rows, with ribosome-TE join hit rate $0.9118667591950035$; after residualization, the design has residual degrees of freedom $13117$, residual readout energy $3588.378859021949$, and $Q$-explained energy $144.76301201119642$.  In \textit{Escherichia coli} K-12 MG1655, $3415$ measured-TE genes are used out of $3739$ CDS rows, with hit rate $0.9133458143888741$; the residual degrees of freedom are $3392$, residual readout energy is $919.4854026798789$, and $Q$-explained energy is $8.637891149939946$.  In \textit{Homo sapiens}, $11554$ measured-TE genes are used out of $19053$ CDS rows, with hit rate $0.6064136881331024$; the residual degrees of freedom are $11531$, residual readout energy is $2052.594706241978$, and $Q$-explained energy is $38.66659710161269$.  In \textit{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}, $5128$ measured-TE genes are used out of $5815$ CDS rows, with hit rate $0.8818572656921754$; the residual degrees of freedom are $5105$, residual readout energy is $753.5781168800304$, and $Q$-explained energy is $103.10404057290246$.

\paragraph{Survival readback.}
The measured-TE survival statistic is positive in all four powered panels.  With positive partial-$R^2$ threshold $10^{-12}$, each panel reports all nine coordinates as surviving against $\log_{10}(T)$ after the $Z$ controls.  The observed measured-TE survival values are
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{QT,\mathrm{meas}}(\textit{D. rerio}) &= 0.04034217614654355,\\
R^2_{QT,\mathrm{meas}}(\textit{E. coli}) &= 0.009394266754822262,\\
R^2_{QT,\mathrm{meas}}(\textit{H. sapiens}) &= 0.018837911344127926,\\
R^2_{QT,\mathrm{meas}}(\textit{S. cerevisiae}) &= 0.13681931343730436.
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the finite row supports a measured translation-readout statement: $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinates are not erased by amino-acid composition, length, GC3, or $M$-density controls when the response is an mRNA-normalized ribosome-profiling translation-efficiency readout.  The strongest entry in this measured-TE panel is yeast, with $R^2_{QT,\mathrm{meas}}=0.13681931343730436$; the weakest is \textit{E. coli}, with $R^2_{QT,\mathrm{meas}}=0.009394266754822262$, still above the declared positivity threshold.

\paragraph{Relation to protein abundance.}
The same coordinate family also has direct protein-abundance survival in the broader PAXdb row family.  For example, direct $Q\to P$ residual survival reports $R^2_{QP}=0.10767526677985896$ in \textit{E. coli}, $0.04454565118019448$ in human, $0.29837624994140827$ in yeast, and $0.04613529807085213$ in zebrafish in the corresponding abundance panels, with all nine coordinates surviving in each listed organism.  However, direct $Q\to P$ survival is not a mechanism statement.  The measured mediation row distinguishes the translation-conditioned part: the measured $Q\to T\to P$ fractions are $0.04091158340950673$ in \textit{E. coli}, $0.0027966485036786744$ in human, and $0.4793069656345167$ in yeast, while zebrafish has raw suppressive sign $-0.04801486048721136$ and is reported as clipped measured fraction $0.0$.  The measured-TE chapter therefore licenses a translation-readout survival claim, and only the separate mediation fit licenses a limited variance-partition statement about how much of the protein-abundance association is reduced by measured TE.

\paragraph{Four-case interpretation.}
On this finite row, the translation side is positive for the four measured-TE organisms: $S^{QT,\mathrm{meas}}>0$ in zebrafish, \textit{E. coli}, human, and yeast.  The protein side must be read organism by organism and only when matched abundance rows are present.  The relevant distinction is not whether a residual coordinate predicts protein abundance somewhere, but whether the same controlled coordinate first survives against a measured translation readout and then loses direct explanatory power after conditioning on that measured readout.  Yeast is the clearest finite-row example of a sizeable measured-TE component, with $S^{QT,\mathrm{meas}}=0.13681931343730436$, $S^{QP}=0.3146818503437042$ on the matched measured-mediation row, and measured mediated fraction $0.4793069656345167$.  Human has positive measured-TE survival but a small measured mediation fraction, $0.0027966485036786744$.  Zebrafish has positive measured-TE survival and positive direct $Q\to P$ association in the matched mediation row, but the measured-TE conditioning does not yield a positive mediated fraction.  This separates translation-readout survival from protein-output mediation rather than merging them.

\paragraph{Local no-promotion boundary.}
The readout is measured ribosome-profiling TE, but it is still condition-specific, cross-sectional, and drawn from one or a small number of studies per organism.  Because $T$ is already $\mathrm{RPF}/\mathrm{mRNA}$, the statistic is mRNA-normalized by construction; it is not an independent causal perturbation.  Positive $S^{QT,\mathrm{meas}}$ entries do not establish universal translation efficiency, elongation speed, protein abundance, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, system phenotype, mechanism, or evolutionary direction.  A higher-layer assertion requires its own layer-matched measurement and its own controlled finite-row statistic.  In particular, a direct $Q\to P$ association remains a direct protein-omics association unless a measured translation readout survives and conditioning on that readout removes or reduces the direct $Q\to P$ signal in the matched mediation model.
