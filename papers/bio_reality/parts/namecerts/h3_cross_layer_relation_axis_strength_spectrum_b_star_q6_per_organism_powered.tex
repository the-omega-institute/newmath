\subsection{Per-organism axis-strength spectrum for the $B^*_{Q6}$ residual surface}
\label{sec:bioreality-q6-axis-strength-spectrum}

\paragraph{Finite coordinate surface.}
The finite row considered here is the coding-sequence-by-organism residual coordinate surface restricted to the $B^*_{Q6}$ synonymous design and, within that surface, to the $f_3$-stress direction after amino-acid projection. The coordinate is not read as a protein property. It is a residual synonymous-codon imbalance direction whose possible biological contact is limited to matched translation readouts, and only after the usual nuisance geometry has been removed: amino-acid composition, organism identity, GC3, CDS length, $M$-density, matched mRNA abundance where translation efficiency is used, and simpler codon baselines.

The finite codon support used by the linked saturation row is explicit. The marked set $M={\rm Med}(R)\cup\{\mathrm{ACA}\}$ is
$$
\begin{aligned}
M=\{&\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{ACA},\mathrm{ACG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\\
&\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The $f_3$-saturation row removes the two ACA/ACG boundary entries and gives
$$
\begin{aligned}
{\rm Sat}_{f_3}(R)=\{&\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\\
&\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\},
\end{aligned}
$$
with an empty recorded $f_3$ boundary. Thus the present row is about a finite codon-coordinate distinction, not an unrestricted molecular mechanism.

\paragraph{Observed finite spectrum.}
The powered axis-strength computation has status passed for experiment \texttt{b\_star\_q6\_axis\_strength\_spectrum\_powered}, with deterministic seed \texttt{sha256:b\_star\_q6\_axis\_strength\_spectrum\_powered:deterministic}. It requested 16 organisms and computed 11, using five folds, at least 500 proteins per organism, and 41 synonymous design columns. Within that finite panel, the captured spectra are led on average by $d_{\rm resid4}$, followed by $d_{\rm tRNA}$. The per-organism dominance count is concentrated entirely on $d_{\rm resid4}$, with 11 of 11 computed organisms having that dominant axis. Domain summaries are kept descriptive because the panel is imbalanced, with $n_{\rm archaea}=1$, $n_{\rm bacteria}=2$, and $n_{\rm eukaryote}=8$. The largest recorded GC3 rank effect is the $d_{\rm usage}$ effect with $\rho=0.136$.

This row therefore supports a finite rank-powered statement: in the computed multi-organism panel, the strongest captured $B^*_{Q6}$ residual axis is not evenly distributed among all candidate directions, but is concentrated on the fourth residual direction. The statement is deliberately narrower than a mechanistic claim. It says that the controlled synonymous design contains a reproducible per-organism spectrum, and that the leading finite coordinate in this assay is $d_{\rm resid4}$.

\paragraph{Relation to the $f_3$ translation-readout conjecture.}
The nearby residual-axis rows constrain how the $f_3$-stress coordinate may be interpreted. The universal residual-axis computation reports that the non-tRNA residual optimality aligns most with \texttt{f3\_translation\_selection}; the two-axis closure row reports \texttt{third\_axis\_present}; and the third-axis identity row identifies $d_\perp$ with usage frequency within family, with mean cosine $0.128$ and one-sided sign-test $p=0.000488$. The mechanism row then records that the $d_\perp$ signal is GC-orthogonalized, selection-not-GC, not wobble-clustered, and that CSC or positional identity still needs an additional contact. Together these rows allow a precise reading: $f_3$-stress is a finite residual direction whose translation relevance must be earned by a matched readout row, not by codon topology alone.

The support condition is consequently conditional. A positive translation-readout entry would require a non-vacuous controlled score $S_{f3\text{-stress},T_j}>0$ for tAI, stAI, ribosome occupancy, or translation efficiency after all declared controls. A rank-degenerate surface, including an all-1.0 matrix on only three organisms, is treated as a null boundary rather than survival. The coordinate becomes dynamical only in contexts where tRNA or wobble weights break the third-position $f_3$ symmetry, for example through a context weight asymmetry $w_c\ne w_{c^{f_3}}$.

\paragraph{Fourth-axis context and exclusions.}
The axis-strength row sits beside several finite fourth-mechanism tests. The three-axis sufficiency row reports \texttt{fourth\_mechanism\_present}, so the residual spectrum is not exhausted by the first three axes in that assay. However, the candidate identities are sharply bounded by the recorded rows. The mRNA-stability test concludes \texttt{fourth\_axis\_not\_mrna\_stability}, because the yeast $d_{\rm opt,halflife}$ direction has low alignment with abundance $d_{\rm resid4}$ under the fixed gates. The codon-pair test concludes \texttt{not\_codon\_pair}; the translation-accuracy test concludes \texttt{not\_accuracy}; and the elongation-dwell row is only \texttt{partial} in \textit{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}. Separately, the boundary-set tRNA-poverty computation passes on 40 computed organisms out of 46 requested, with 6 skipped, $\alpha=0.05$, and 1000 permutations, and reports that $R$ sense codons are systematically tRNA-poor under GtRNAdb tGCN plus dos Reis wobble weights. That tRNA-poverty result is compatible with a tRNA-supply pressure, but it does not by itself identify the fourth residual axis or prove translation survival for $f_3$-stress.

\paragraph{Local negative scope.}
The finite result is a controlled axis-spectrum record and a trigger for a translation-readout test. It does not assert that $f_3$-stress changes protein abundance, folded structure, localization, stability, molecular function, phenotype, physical admissibility, or a universal biological rule. It also does not turn tAI or stAI weights into observed ribosome occupancy, nor does it turn mRNA abundance into a translation output. Those interpretations require separate layer-matched contacts and their own statistics. Until a matched translation row gives a nondegenerate positive $S_{f3\text{-stress},T_j}$ under the declared controls, the readback remains bounded to the finite coordinate spectrum and its stated null conditions.
