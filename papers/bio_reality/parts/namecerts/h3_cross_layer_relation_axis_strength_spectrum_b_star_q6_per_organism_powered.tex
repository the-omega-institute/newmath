\subsection{Per-organism strength spectrum for the $B^*_{Q6}$ $f_3$-stress coordinate}
\label{subsec:bioreality-q6-f3-stress-axis-strength-spectrum}

\paragraph{Finite coordinate surface.}
This record fixes a finite coding-sequence surface in which the only admissible coordinate of interest is the $B^*_{Q6}$ residual direction associated with third-position $f_3$ stress, paired with organism-matched codon-usage, tRNA or wobble-weight, and translation-readout rows. The amino-acid quotient is part of the definition: the relevant distinction is not raw codon frequency, but the residual imbalance between synonymous codons after the amino-acid projection has removed composition effects. The finite $f_3$ saturation row gives the concrete boundary. The set
$$
\begin{aligned}
M &= \{AAA,AAG,ACA,ACG,AGA,AGG,AUA,AUG,CUA,CUC,CUG,CUU,\\
&\qquad UAA,UAG,UCA,UCG,UGA,UGG,UUA,UUG\},
\end{aligned}
$$
whereas the saturated $f_3$ row removes only $ACA$ and $ACG$:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\operatorname{Sat}_{f_3}(R) &= \{AAA,AAG,AGA,AGG,AUA,AUG,CUA,CUC,CUG,CUU,\\
&\qquad UAA,UAG,UCA,UCG,UGA,UGG,UUA,UUG\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The recorded $f_3$ boundary set is empty, so the test is not a missing-boundary search. It is a controlled readout question: whether the residual $f_3$ coordinate has any positive survival against translation-layer observations once the usual sequence covariates have been paid for.

\paragraph{Powered spectrum row.}
The powered finite-row experiment used $5$ folds, at least $500$ proteins per organism, $41$ synonymous design columns, and computed $11$ organisms out of $16$ requested organisms. Across these rows the captured spectra were led by $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$ on average, followed by $d_{\mathrm{tRNA}}$. The per-organism dominance count was concentrated entirely on $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$, with
$$
\begin{aligned}
\#\{\text{organisms dominated by } d_{\mathrm{resid4}}\}=11.
\end{aligned}
$$
The domain split is descriptive rather than a domain-level law, because the computed panel is imbalanced: $n_{\mathrm{archaea}}=1$, $n_{\mathrm{bacteria}}=2$, and $n_{\mathrm{eukaryote}}=8$. The largest GC3 rank effect in this row is small, with $d_{\mathrm{usage}}$ attaining $\rho=0.136$. Thus the finite-row conclusion is not that GC3 explains the spectrum. The stronger finite statement is that, in the computed panel, the residual fourth direction is the leading per-organism strength coordinate, while the tRNA-weighted direction remains the next named translation-adaptation axis.

\paragraph{Relation to neighboring mechanism rows.}
The nearby powered rows constrain how this spectrum may be read. The tRNA-poverty row computed $40$ organisms out of $46$ requested organisms, used $1000$ permutations at $\alpha=0.05$, and recorded systematic tRNA poverty for the $R$ sense codons under GtRNAdb tGCN plus dos Reis wobble weights. The universal-core row used the same $5$-fold, $500$-protein, $11/16$-organism, $41$-column design class and passed the claim that the core optimal direction is tRNA adaptation. At the same time, the non-tRNA residual optimality row identified $f_3$ translation selection as the dominant residual axis. The two-axis closure row did not close the surface: it reported a third-axis-present outcome, with positive held-out $R^2$ and mutually same-signed $d_\perp$ directions across organisms. The third-axis identity row then aligned $d_\perp$ to usage frequency within family, with mean cosine $0.128$ and one-sided sign-test $p=0.000488$. After GC orthogonalization, the third-axis mechanism row retained significant cross-species alignment and held-out $R^2$, recording the status as selection-not-GC and not-wobble-clustered while leaving the CSC or positional identity in need of a separate contact.

\paragraph{Translation-readout gate.}
The admissible cross-layer statistic is a bounded survival entry $S_{f3\text{-}stress,T_j}$, where $T_j$ is a tAI, stAI, ribosome-occupancy, or translation-efficiency readout. The support condition is strictly row-based: at least one controlled entry must satisfy
$$
\begin{aligned}
S_{f3\text{-}stress,T_j} > 0
\end{aligned}
$$
after amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, coding-sequence length, $M$-density, matched mRNA, rank, and simpler-codon-baseline controls where those controls are available. A modeled tAI or stAI weight may supply a bounded codon-adaptation readback, but it is not the same thing as observed ribosome occupancy. A three-organism all-$1.0$ controlled matrix is therefore a rank-degenerate boundary, not survival evidence. Likewise, codon topology and wobble-weight asymmetry can nominate where the coordinate might become dynamic, but they do not by themselves produce the survival entry.

\paragraph{Local no-promotion rule.}
This chapter asserts only a finite per-organism spectrum and a conditional translation-readout test. It does not infer protein abundance, folded structure, physical admissibility, localization, stability, molecular function, phenotype, mechanism, or universality from the $B^*_{Q6}$ coordinate, from the $S$ notation, from codon-window geometry, or from modeled adaptation weights. Those layers require their own matched empirical rows and their own controlled statistics. If all controlled $S_{f3\text{-}stress,T_j}$ entries remain non-positive, or if the available readout matrix is rank-degenerate, the $f_3$-stress translation-readout conjecture is null at this boundary even though the finite codon-coordinate spectrum remains well defined.
