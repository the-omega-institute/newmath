\subsection{Per-organism strength spectrum for the $B^*_{Q6}$ $f_3$-stress coordinate}
\label{sec:bioreality-q6-f3-axis-strength-spectrum}

\paragraph{Finite coordinate surface.}
This record concerns the finite CDS-by-organism residual surface obtained after amino-acid quotienting and restriction to the $B^*_{Q6}$ $f_3$-stress coordinate.  The operative biological question is not whether codon geometry exists as a formal coordinate, but whether that coordinate remains visible in a translation-layer row after controls for amino-acid composition, organism identity, GC3, CDS length, $M$-density, and matched transcript abundance where translation efficiency is used.  The finite $f_3$ saturation row fixes the codon support.  The set $M={\rm Med}_R\cup\{\mathrm{ACA},\mathrm{ACG}\}$ is
$$
\begin{aligned}
M=\{&\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{ACA},\mathrm{ACG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\\
&\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The saturated $f_3$ image of $R$, with the two added $AC$ codons removed, is
$$
\begin{aligned}
{\rm Sat}_{f_3}(R)=\{&\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\\
&\mathrm{CUU},\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The recorded $f_3$ boundary set is empty.  Thus the coordinate has a closed finite support in this assay: the possible third-position pairing imbalance is carried by an explicit eighteen-codon saturated row inside a twenty-codon ambient list, not by an unlisted codon class.

\paragraph{Axis-strength measurement.}
The powered spectrum assay requested $16$ organisms and computed $11$ organisms with at least $500$ proteins per organism.  It used $41$ synonymous-design columns and $5$ folds.  On this finite panel the captured spectra are led by $d_{\rm resid4}$ on average, followed by $d_{\rm tRNA}$.  The per-organism dominance count is concentrated entirely on $d_{\rm resid4}$:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\#\{\hbox{organisms dominated by }d_{\rm resid4}\}=11,
\end{aligned}
$$
with no recorded organism in this run dominated by another listed axis.  The domain partition is strongly imbalanced, with $1$ archaeal organism, $2$ bacterial organisms, and $8$ eukaryotic organisms.  Consequently, domain means are descriptive only and are not treated as comparative domain laws.  The largest recorded GC3 rank effect is small, $\rho=0.136$, and it occurs on $d_{\rm usage}$.  This scalar matters because it keeps the readback from being collapsed into a GC3 story: the observed leading axis is $d_{\rm resid4}$, while the largest GC3 rank association is attached to $d_{\rm usage}$ and remains modest.

\paragraph{Translation-gated interpretation.}
The chapter-specific claim is a survival claim at the translation-readout interface.  Let $Q_i$ denote the $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinate component represented by the $f_3$-stress row, and let $T_j$ range over bounded translation readouts such as tAI, stAI, ribosome occupancy, or translation efficiency.  A positive entry is allowed only when the controlled statistic survives after the declared covariates:
$$
\begin{aligned}
S_{ij}>0
\quad\hbox{after controls for}\quad
Z=\{&\hbox{amino-acid composition},\hbox{ organism},\hbox{ GC3},\\
&\hbox{CDS length},\hbox{ $M$-density},\hbox{ matched mRNA where applicable}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The rank-degenerate boundary is also part of the local statement: a three-organism all-$1.0$ controlled surface is not survival evidence, because it has no remaining rank resolution.  Similarly, modeled tAI or stAI coupling is a bounded codon-adaptation readout, not an observed ribosome-occupancy row.  The $f_3$ coordinate becomes biologically informative only in organisms or sequence contexts where the relevant tRNA or wobble weights distinguish a codon $c$ from its $f_3$ partner $c^{f_3}$, that is, where $w_c\neq w_{c^{f_3}}$.

\paragraph{Neighboring finite evidence.}
Several linked powered rows constrain the interpretation of the strength spectrum.  The $R$-sense boundary assay computed $40$ of $46$ requested organisms, skipped $6$, used $1000$ permutations at $\alpha=0.05$, and called the $R$ sense codons systematically tRNA-poor under GtRNAdb tGCN plus dos Reis wobble weights.  The universal core tRNA-adaptation assay computed $11$ of $16$ organisms with $5$ folds and $41$ synonymous-design columns.  The non-tRNA residual optimality assay on the same $11$ computed organisms identified $f_3$ translation selection as the dominant residual axis.  The two-axis closure assay found a third axis present, with positive held-out $R^2$ and same-signed $d_\perp$ directions across organisms.  The third-axis identity assay aligned $d_\perp$ to usage frequency within family with mean cosine $0.128$ and one-sided sign-test $p=0.000488$.  Its mechanism row records selection-not-GC, not-wobble-clustered, and a CSC or positional identity still needing an outside layer-specific row.  The three-axis sufficiency assay concluded that a fourth mechanism is present.

\paragraph{Fourth-axis exclusions and partial row.}
The fourth-axis checks narrow what may be said about the leading $d_{\rm resid4}$ spectrum.  In yeast, the elongation-dwell row is recorded as partial.  The mRNA-stability assay concludes that the fourth axis is not mRNA stability in that assay, because the yeast $d_{\rm opt\_halflife}$ row has low alignment with the abundance $d_{\rm resid4}$ row under the fixed gates.  The codon-pair assay concludes not codon pair, using $50$ shuffle-null replicates.  The translation-accuracy assay computes $11$ of $16$ organisms, uses $200$ permutations, and concludes not accuracy.  These rows make the spectrum sharper but do not promote it beyond its measured layer.  They say that the observed per-organism spectrum is not exhausted by the tested mRNA-stability, codon-pair, or translation-accuracy explanations, while one elongation-dwell row remains partial.

\paragraph{Local falsification rule.}
The falsifier is direct.  If every controlled entry $S_{f3\hbox{-}{\rm stress},T_j}$ is non-positive, or if the available panel remains rank-degenerate after the controls, then this chapter records no translation-readout survival for the $f_3$-stress coordinate.  A single positive entry would be only a bounded row for the corresponding organism and readout; it would not be a universal law.  Negative survival would not show that $B^*_{Q6}$ is biologically irrelevant; it would only close this translation-interface route for the tested contacts and controls.

\paragraph{Scope limit.}
No claim about translation realization, ribosome dynamics, mature protein abundance, protein folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, localization, stability, phenotype, mechanism, or universality follows from the residual coordinate, codon lists, modeled tAI or stAI weights, contact names, or the axis spectrum alone.  Each higher layer requires its own matched biological measurement and its own controlled statistic.  The present chapter therefore reports a finite, controlled axis-strength spectrum and its translation-readout gate: $d_{\rm resid4}$ leads the computed per-organism panel, $d_{\rm tRNA}$ follows, $d_{\rm usage}$ carries only a modest largest GC3 rank effect, and any downstream biological reading remains blocked until a separate layer-specific contact supplies it.
