\subsection{Per-organism axis spectrum for the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ $f_3$-stress coordinate}
\label{subsec:bioreality-q6-axis-strength-spectrum}

\paragraph{Finite row and coordinate surface.}
This BioReality record concerns a finite CDS-by-organism residual coordinate surface after amino-acid projection, restricted to the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ coordinate family and read only through matched translation-layer rows.  The local trigger is the $f_3$-paired synonymous imbalance: two codons that differ by the third-position $f_3$ operation are invisible at the amino-acid quotient, but can become visible again when a context-dependent tRNA or wobble weight satisfies $w_c \ne w_{c^{f_3}}$.  The finite saturation row fixes the codon support on which this question is posed.  The recorded $M=\operatorname{Med}(R)\cup\{\mathrm{ACA}\}$ surface contains
$$
\begin{aligned}
M=\{&\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{ACA},\mathrm{ACG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\\
&\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The $f_3$ saturation row removes exactly $\mathrm{ACA}$ and $\mathrm{ACG}$ from that list and leaves
$$
\begin{aligned}
\operatorname{Sat}_{f_3}(R)=\{&\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\\
&\mathrm{CUU},\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The finite boundary row is empty, $\partial_{f_3}=\varnothing$, so this chapter does not argue from an unfilled codon edge.  It argues from whether a saturated synonymous residual coordinate remains predictive when it is forced through organism-specific translation readouts and controls.

\paragraph{Powered spectrum result.}
The powered spectrum run for this claim used $5$ folds, required at least $500$ proteins per organism, computed $11$ organisms out of $16$ requested, and used $41$ synonymous-design columns.  Its deterministic seed was recorded as \texttt{sha256:b\_star\_q6\_axis\_strength\_spectrum\_powered:deterministic}.  Within that finite panel, the captured spectra were led on average by $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$, followed by $d_{\mathrm{tRNA}}$.  The per-organism dominant count was concentrated entirely on $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$, with
$$
\begin{aligned}
\#\{\text{organisms whose dominant captured axis is }d_{\mathrm{resid4}}\}=11.
\end{aligned}
$$
Domain means are only descriptive in this row because the domain panel is imbalanced: $n_{\mathrm{archaea}}=1$, $n_{\mathrm{bacteria}}=2$, and $n_{\mathrm{eukaryote}}=8$.  The largest recorded GC3 rank effect is small, with $\rho=0.136$ for $d_{\mathrm{usage}}$.  Thus the finite spectrum does not read as a GC3-only account of the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ surface.  It reads as a per-organism residual-strength spectrum in which a fourth residual direction dominates the captured score, while the tRNA direction remains the next visible comparator.

\paragraph{Relation to the surrounding finite evidence.}
The neighboring powered rows constrain how this spectrum can be interpreted.  The boundary-set tRNA-poverty assay computed $40$ organisms out of $46$ requested, skipped $6$, used $1000$ permutations at $\alpha=0.05$, and concluded that $R$ sense codons are systematically tRNA-poor under GtRNAdb tGCN with dos Reis wobble weights.  That row supplies a plausible tRNA-supply pressure for $R$ avoidance, but it is not itself a ribosome-occupancy or translation-efficiency measurement.  The residual-optimality row computed $11$ organisms out of $16$ requested and identified the dominant non-tRNA residual optimality axis as $f_3$ translation selection.  The two-axis closure row, also on $11$ of $16$ requested organisms with $5$ folds and $41$ synonymous-design columns, concluded that a third axis is present because $d_{\perp}$ has systematically positive held-out $R^2$ and mutually same-signed directions across organisms.  The third-axis identity row then aligned $d_{\perp}$ to usage frequency within family, with mean cosine $0.128$ and one-sided sign-test $p=0.000488$.  The mechanism row retained cross-species alignment after GC orthogonalization and reported the boundary as selection-not-GC, not wobble-clustered, with CSC or positional identity still requiring an outside readout.

\paragraph{Fourth-axis exclusion map.}
The finite spectrum is sharpened by the fourth-mechanism rows.  The three-axis sufficiency row concluded that a fourth mechanism is present.  The mRNA-stability assay classified the fourth direction as not mRNA stability because the yeast $d_{\mathrm{opt\_halflife}}$ direction had low alignment with the abundance-side $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$ under the fixed gates.  The codon-pair assay concluded \texttt{not\_codon\_pair} with a shuffle null of $50$.  The translation-accuracy assay concluded \texttt{not\_accuracy} across $11$ computed organisms out of $16$ requested with $200$ permutations.  The elongation-dwell row in \textit{Saccharomyces cerevisiae} was only \texttt{partial}.  Taken together, these rows make the spectrum informative but deliberately non-final: $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$ is the leading captured strength direction in the finite panel, yet its biological identity is not licensed as mRNA stability, codon-pair bias, translation accuracy, or a completed dwell-time mechanism.

\paragraph{Translation-readout gate.}
The chapter therefore records a bounded cross-layer relation, not a promoted biological law.  A positive readback for the $f_3$-stress coordinate requires a non-vacuous entry $S_{f_3\text{-stress},T_j}>0$ for a declared translation readout $T_j$, such as tAI, stAI, ribosome occupancy, or translation efficiency, after amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, CDS length, $M$-density, matched mRNA, rank, and simpler-baseline controls.  The three-organism all-$1.0$ matrix is not survival evidence; it is a rank-degenerate boundary.  Modeled tAI or stAI weights can supply a bounded codon-adaptation readout, but they are not observed ribosome footprints.  Ribosome profiling can test translation efficiency only after same-organism and same-CDS RPF rows are joined to matched mRNA abundance and codon-count rows.  Protein abundance, localization, stability, folded structure, physical admissibility, function, phenotype, mechanism, and universality remain outside the present claim unless another layer-matched contact supplies its own statistic.

\paragraph{Local verdict.}
The finite-row verdict is that the axis-strength spectrum is positive and powered for the stated panel: $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$ leads all $11$ computed organism spectra, $d_{\mathrm{tRNA}}$ is the next comparator, and the largest reported GC3 rank effect is only $0.136$.  The surrounding rows keep the interpretation narrow.  They support a real residual axis spectrum at the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ coordinate level and a tRNA-poverty pressure on the $R$ sense boundary, while refusing any claim that the $f_3$-stress coordinate has already been observed as translation survival in measured ribosome or TE data.  The scientific content is therefore a rank-powered, organism-indexed residual spectrum with an explicit next test: join codon counts, wobble-breaking weights, measured translation rows, and matched mRNA controls, then compute the controlled $S_{f_3\text{-stress},T_j}$ entry before any downstream biological interpretation is allowed.
