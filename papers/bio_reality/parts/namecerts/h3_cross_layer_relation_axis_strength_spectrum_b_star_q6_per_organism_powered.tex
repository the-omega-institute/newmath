\subsection{Rank-powered spectrum of the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ $f_3$-stress axis}\label{sec:bior-axi-strength-spectrum-bstar-q6-per-organism}\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite coordinate surface.}
The finite object in this record is the CDS-by-organism residual coordinate surface restricted to the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ synonymous design after amino-acid projection, with special attention to the third-position $f_3$-stress coordinate and to translation-readout rows that can be matched at organism or CDS resolution. The saturation row fixes the codon-side support before any biological interpretation is attempted. In the finite $f_3$ record, the mediated set $M=\mathrm{Med}(R)\cup\{\mathrm{ACA}\}$ is
$$
\begin{aligned}
M = \{&\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{ACA},\mathrm{ACG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\\
&\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The saturated $f_3$ image of $R$ removes the two ACA/ACG boundary entries and contains the $18$ codons
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{Sat}_{f_3}(R)=\{&\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\\
&\mathrm{CUU},\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The recorded $f_3$ boundary is empty, so the finite codon construction itself does not supply a residual biological readout. It only supplies a closed coordinate on which an organism-matched translation statistic can later be computed.

\paragraph{Powered spectrum row.}
The powered spectrum assay for this claim requested $16$ organisms and computed $11$ organisms with at least $500$ proteins per organism. The design used $41$ synonymous columns and $5$ folds. Its finite conclusion is not that $f_3$-stress is already a measured translation effect; it is that the captured per-organism spectra are dominated by the fourth residual direction. Averaged over the computed organisms, $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$ leads the spectrum, followed by $d_{\mathrm{tRNA}}$, and the per-organism dominant-axis count is
$$
\begin{aligned}
\#\{\hbox{organisms with dominant }d_{\mathrm{resid4}}\}=11.
\end{aligned}
$$
The domain partition in this row is imbalanced, with $1$ archaeal organism, $2$ bacterial organisms, and $8$ eukaryotic organisms. Domain means are therefore descriptive summaries rather than domain-level survival calls. The largest recorded GC3 rank effect in the same assay is the usage direction, with $\rho=0.136$ for $d_{\mathrm{usage}}$. This value is retained as a finite nuisance-alignment fact: it says that GC3 still has a measurable rank relation to one usage direction, but it does not remove the need for explicit GC3 control in any translation-readout survival statistic.

\paragraph{How the axis is allowed to become biological.}
The $f_3$-stress coordinate is biologically admissible only as a bounded translation-readout question. After amino-acid quotienting, the coordinate can matter for translation only where codon-pairing symmetry is broken by organism- or context-specific tRNA or wobble weights. The row that makes this plausible is not the spectrum row alone. The tRNA-poverty assay computed $40$ of $46$ requested organisms, skipped $6$, used $1000$ permutations at $\alpha=0.05$, and called the $R$ sense codons systematically tRNA-poor under GtRNAdb tGCN plus dos Reis wobble weights. That mechanism-facing row supports a supply-side reason to look for a translation readout, but it still does not substitute for the required survival entry.

The surrounding finite results locate the spectrum among other axes. The universal core tRNA-adaptation assay passed on $11$ computed of $16$ requested organisms, again with $5$ folds, at least $500$ proteins per organism, and $41$ synonymous columns. The non-tRNA residual optimality row identifies $f_3$ translation selection as the dominant residual optimality axis. The two-axis closure row reports a third axis present, with systematically positive held-out $R^2$ and mutually same-signed $d_{\perp}$ directions across organisms. The third-axis identity row provisionally aligns $d_{\perp}$ to usage frequency within family, with mean cosine $0.128$ and one-sided sign-test $p=0.000488$. The third-axis mechanism row keeps that axis GC-orthogonal and records the finite call ``selection-not-GC; not-wobble-clustered; CSC/positional identity needs external input.'' These rows explain why the spectrum is treated as an axis-strength record rather than as a completed mechanistic chain.

\paragraph{Fourth-axis interpretation.}
The same finite panel also prevents over-reading the leading residual direction. The three-axis sufficiency assay reports ``fourth mechanism present'' on $11$ computed of $16$ requested organisms, with $5$ folds, at least $500$ proteins per organism, and $41$ synonymous design columns. Candidate mechanism checks then rule out or weaken several immediate interpretations. The mRNA half-life assay calls the fourth axis not mRNA stability because yeast $d_{\mathrm{opt\_halflife}}$ has low alignment with abundance $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$ under the fixed gates. The codon-pair assay calls ``not\_codon\_pair'' with $50$ shuffle nulls. The translation-accuracy assay calls ``not\_accuracy'' after $200$ permutations on $11$ computed of $16$ requested organisms. The elongation-dwell row is only partial and is confined to Saccharomyces cerevisiae. Thus the leading $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$ spectrum is a reproducible residual axis in the finite design, but the listed mechanism rows do not license a single downstream biological identity.

\paragraph{Required survival statistic.}
For this chapter, the intended cross-layer statistic is a controlled entry $S_{f_3\text{-stress},T_j}$, where $T_j$ is a bounded translation readout such as tAI, stAI, ribosome occupancy, or translation efficiency. The admissible support condition is
$$
\begin{aligned}
S_{f_3\text{-stress},T_j} > 0
\end{aligned}
$$
after controls for amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, CDS length, $M$-density, matched mRNA abundance when TE is used, rank sufficiency, and simpler codon baselines. Rank-degenerate all-one surfaces do not count as survival; they are null boundaries. A modeled tAI or stAI surface may be used as a bounded translation-weight readback, but observed ribosome occupancy or TE requires joined same-organism and same-CDS rows. The present finite record has the powered axis spectrum and several mechanism-facing companions, but it does not contain a same-row measured ribosome or TE matrix for the exact $f_3$-stress statistic.

\paragraph{Local no-promotion rule.}
The safe reading is therefore narrow. This chapter records a finite, rank-powered axis-strength spectrum for $B^{*}_{Q6}$ and identifies the $f_3$-stress question that should be tested against translation rows when wobble or tRNA asymmetry breaks third-position symmetry. It does not claim protein abundance, folded structure, physical admissibility, localization, stability, molecular function, phenotype, mechanism completion, or universality. None of those follows from codon geometry, residual axes, tRNA-copy summaries, modeled tAI or stAI weights, or a passed spectrum assay. Each stronger layer requires its own matched contact and its own controlled statistic, and the translation row must be non-vacuous before any downstream interpretation is considered.
