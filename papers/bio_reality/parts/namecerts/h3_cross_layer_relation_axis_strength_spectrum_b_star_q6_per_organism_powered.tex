\subsection{Per-organism strength spectrum for the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual axes}
\label{sec:bioreality-q6-axis-strength-spectrum}

\paragraph{Finite subject.}
This record concerns a finite CDS-by-organism residual coordinate surface, restricted to the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ codon-residual coordinates after amino-acid projection.  The tested row is not a claim that a codon coordinate is already a physiological mechanism.  It is a finite comparison of residual-axis strength across a rank-powered multi-organism panel, with synonymous codon design columns matched to organism-level codon usage and tRNA or wobble-weight summaries.  The powered run used $11$ computed organisms out of $16$ requested organisms, $5$ folds, a minimum of $500$ proteins per organism, and $41$ synonymous design columns.  The recorded conclusion is that the captured spectra are led by $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$ on average, followed by $d_{\mathrm{tRNA}}$; the per-organism dominant-axis count is $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}=11$.  Domain means are only descriptive because the domain counts are imbalanced, with $1$ archaeal, $2$ bacterial, and $8$ eukaryotic organisms.  The largest recorded GC3 rank effect in this run is the $d_{\mathrm{usage}}$ association with $\rho=0.136$.

\paragraph{Coordinate boundary.}
The $f_3$ saturation row fixes the local codon-coordinate boundary used by the surrounding $Q6$ interpretation.  The finite set $M=\operatorname{Med}_{R}\cup\{\mathrm{ACA}\}$ contains $20$ codons:
$$
\begin{aligned}
M = \{&\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{ACA},\mathrm{ACG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\\
&\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The saturated $f_3$ row removes exactly $\mathrm{ACA}$ and $\mathrm{ACG}$ from that list and leaves $18$ codons:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\operatorname{Sat}_{f_3}(R) = \{&\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\\
&\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The corresponding $f_3$ boundary list is empty.  Thus the chapter treats $f_3$ stress as a residual coordinate whose possible biological readback must pass through a separate translation-row test; the coordinate boundary alone is not a ribosome measurement.

\paragraph{Spectrum readback.}
The axis-strength run is a passed finite-row result, but its statistical content is deliberately narrow.  It says that, in this panel, the strongest captured residual direction is $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$, and that every computed organism in the panel has $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$ as its dominant direction under the recorded comparison.  The row is compatible with, but does not by itself prove, a translation-readout connection.  The related powered rows sharpen the interpretation: the $R$-sense tRNA-poverty assay computed $40$ organisms out of $46$ requested organisms, skipped $6$, used $1000$ permutations at $\alpha=0.05$, and called $R$ sense codons systematically tRNA-poor under GtRNAdb tGCN plus dos Reis wobble weights.  A separate third-axis row identified $d_{\perp}$ provisionally with usage frequency within family, with mean cosine $0.128$ and one-sided sign-test $p=0.000488$.  Its mechanism row reports selection-not-GC, not-wobble-clustered, with CSC or positional identity still requiring additional evidence.  The universal residual-axis row reports that the non-tRNA residual optimality aligns most with $f_3$ translation selection, while the two-axis closure row still finds a third axis present.  The three-axis sufficiency row reports a fourth mechanism present, and later finite rows exclude several candidate identities: not codon-pair, not translation accuracy, and not mRNA stability as the dominant fourth-axis identity in the yeast half-life assay; elongation dwell is recorded only as partial.

\paragraph{Local formal statement.}
Let $Q_i$ denote the finite $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates available in the synonymous design, and let $A_o(Q_i)$ denote the per-organism captured strength of coordinate $Q_i$ in organism $o$.  The finite claim supported here is the rank-powered spectrum statement
$$
\begin{aligned}
&|O_{\mathrm{computed}}|=11,\quad |O_{\mathrm{requested}}|=16,\quad K_{\mathrm{fold}}=5,\quad p_{\min}=500,\quad d_{\mathrm{syn}}=41,\\
&\operatorname{dominant}_{o}(A_o)=d_{\mathrm{resid4}}\quad\text{for all }o\in O_{\mathrm{computed}},\\
&\operatorname{count}\{o:\operatorname{dominant}_{o}(A_o)=d_{\mathrm{resid4}}\}=11,\quad \rho_{\mathrm{GC3},d_{\mathrm{usage}}}=0.136.
\end{aligned}
$$
This is a finite strength-spectrum assertion, not an assertion of universal directionality.  The domain summary is retained only as a descriptive stratification because the panel contains $1$ archaeal, $2$ bacterial, and $8$ eukaryotic organisms.

\paragraph{Translation gate.}
The bounded readback for the $f_3$-stress component is a controlled survival entry $S_{f3\text{-}stress,T_j}$ for a translation readout such as tAI, stAI, ribosome occupancy, or translation efficiency.  A positive spectrum row is not enough.  A translation readout must be computed on matched rows after amino-acid composition, organism identity, GC3, CDS length, $M$-density, matched mRNA abundance, rank, and simpler-baseline controls.  The local support condition is therefore
$$
\begin{aligned}
S_{f3\text{-}stress,T_j} > 0
\end{aligned}
$$
for at least one declared translation row $T_j$ on a nondegenerate powered panel.  If the controlled surface is non-positive, or if it collapses into a rank-degenerate all-$1.0$ matrix, the translation survival interpretation fails even though the residual geometry and axis-strength spectrum remain well-defined finite objects.

\paragraph{No promotion beyond the measured layer.}
This chapter does not infer protein abundance, folded structure, physical admissibility, localization, stability, molecular function, organismal phenotype, mechanism, causation, or a universal biological law from the spectrum row.  tAI or stAI weights are bounded codon-adaptation readbacks, not observed ribosome footprints.  Ribosome-profiling or translation-efficiency rows, when attached, test only the declared translation layer unless a further layer-matched contact is supplied.  Matched mRNA abundance is a control for translation-efficiency residualization, not itself a translation-output measurement.  The present finite result is therefore a ranked residual-axis spectrum with an explicit translation-readout gate: it licenses discussion of which $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual directions dominate the computed panel, while withholding all downstream biological interpretation until separate finite contacts and controlled statistics exist at those layers.
