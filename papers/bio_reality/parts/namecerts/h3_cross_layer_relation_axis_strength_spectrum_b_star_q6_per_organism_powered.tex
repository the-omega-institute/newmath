\subsection{The rank-powered $B^*_{Q6}$ axis-strength spectrum}\label{subsec:bioreality-q6-axis-strength-spectrum}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite coordinate surface.}
This record concerns a finite coding-sequence by organism residual surface, restricted to the $B^*_{Q6}$ coordinate family and read only through synonymous codon contrasts after amino-acid projection. The specific stress coordinate is the third-position $f_3$ imbalance: two codons paired by the $f_3$ operation are compared only after the amino-acid identity has already been quotiented out. The finite saturation check fixes the relevant codon rows. The mediated row set is
$$
\begin{aligned}
M={}&\{\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{ACA},\mathrm{ACG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\\
&\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\},
\end{aligned}
$$
while the $f_3$-saturated row set removes the two $\mathrm{AC}$ rows,
$$
\begin{aligned}
\operatorname{Sat}_{f_3}(R)={}&\{\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\\
&\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The reported boundary is empty, $\partial_{f_3}R=\varnothing$, so the finite row check supplies a closed codon-coordinate surface rather than an open-ended biological claim.

\paragraph{Rank-powered spectrum.}
The powered organism panel computed $11$ organisms from $16$ requested organisms, with at least $500$ proteins per organism, $41$ synonymous design columns, and $5$ folds. The deterministic run reports that the captured spectra are led on average by $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$, followed by $d_{\mathrm{tRNA}}$. Per-organism dominance is concentrated in a single counted class: $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$ is dominant in all $11$ computed organisms. Domain summaries are explicitly descriptive rather than balanced comparative claims, since the computed panel has $1$ archaeal organism, $2$ bacterial organisms, and $8$ eukaryotic organisms. The largest reported GC3 rank effect is small, with $d_{\mathrm{usage}}$ having $\rho=0.136$.

\paragraph{Connection to adjacent finite rows.}
The same finite memory places this spectrum inside a wider $B^*_{Q6}$ axis story without turning it into a downstream phenotype claim. The $R$-sense codons are reported as systematically tRNA-poor under GtRNAdb tGCN together with dos Reis wobble weights, using $40$ computed organisms from $46$ requested organisms and $1000$ permutations at $\alpha=0.05$. The third-axis identity row identifies $d_{\perp}$ provisionally with usage frequency within family, with mean cosine $0.128$ and a one-sided sign-test value $p=0.000488$ across an $11$-organism, $41$-column, $5$-fold design. The mechanism row for that third axis reports that the signal remains GC-orthogonalized, is not wobble-clustered, and still needs an external CSC or positional identity. The universal residual optimality row names $f_3$ translation selection as the dominant non-tRNA residual axis, and the two-axis closure row reports that a third axis is present. The three-axis sufficiency row then reports a fourth mechanism as present, but the fourth-axis screens do not identify it as mRNA stability, codon-pair structure, or translation accuracy; elongation dwell is only partial in the yeast row. These finite rows support an axis-strength spectrum, not a completed mechanistic cascade.

\paragraph{Translation-readout gate.}
The biological reading is deliberately narrow. A third-position $f_3$ residual coordinate can become a translation-layer distinction only in an organism or context where the codon-adaptation or wobble weights are asymmetric across an $f_3$ pair, so that $w_c\neq w_{c^{f_3}}$. Even then, the admissible readout is a controlled survival entry such as $S_{f3\text{-}\mathrm{stress},T_j}$ for tAI, stAI, ribosome occupancy, or translation efficiency. The required comparison is not a raw codon-topology association. It must condition on amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, CDS length, $M$-density, matched mRNA abundance when translation efficiency is used, and simpler codon baselines. In symbols, the relevant support has the form
$$
\begin{aligned}
S_{f3\text{-}\mathrm{stress},T_j}>0
\end{aligned}
$$
under the declared controls and with a nondegenerate rank surface. A three-organism all-$1.0$ fitted matrix is therefore a rank-saturation boundary, not survival evidence.

\paragraph{Local no-promotion rule.}
This section does not assert that the $f_3$-stress coordinate changes protein abundance, folded structure, localization, stability, molecular function, physical admissibility, phenotype, mechanism, or a universal biological law. Codon usage, tRNA copy number, and modeled tAI or stAI weights can bound a translation-facing readout, but they do not by themselves observe ribosome occupancy or protein output. Ribosome-profiling or translation-efficiency rows would still need same-organism and same-CDS joins with codon counts and matched mRNA controls before any positive $S_{f3\text{-}\mathrm{stress},T_j}$ entry could be reported. Any higher layer requires its own matched contact and its own controlled statistic after the translation row is already non-vacuous and positive.
