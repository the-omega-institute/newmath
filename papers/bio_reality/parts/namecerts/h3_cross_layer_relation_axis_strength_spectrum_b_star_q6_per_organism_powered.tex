\subsection{Per-organism axis spectrum for the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual surface}\label{sec:bioreality-q6-axis-strength-spectrum}\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite coordinate carrier.}
The record concerns a finite coding-sequence surface indexed by organism and by synonymous residual coordinates after amino-acid projection.  The relevant coordinate is the $f_3$-stress component of the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ construction, read only against translation-layer rows such as tAI, stAI, ribosome occupancy, or translation efficiency.  The underlying $f_3$ saturation computation is explicit: the mediated set is
$M=\{\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{ACA},\mathrm{ACG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\}$,
whereas $\operatorname{Sat}_{f_3}(R)$ removes exactly $\mathrm{ACA}$ and $\mathrm{ACG}$ and leaves
$\{\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\}$.
The reported $f_3$ boundary set is empty.  Thus the BioReality question is not whether the codon-coordinate object is syntactically present; it is whether a finite organism panel contains a nondegenerate translation row on which this coordinate still explains held-out variation after the declared controls.

\paragraph{Powered spectrum readback.}
The powered axis-strength experiment used $5$ folds, at least $500$ proteins per organism, and $41$ synonymous design columns.  It computed $11$ organisms out of $16$ requested organisms.  In that finite panel, the captured spectra were led on average by $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$, followed by $d_{\mathrm{tRNA}}$.  The per-organism dominant-axis count was concentrated entirely at $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$, with count $11$.  The domain partition was imbalanced, with $n_{\mathrm{archaea}}=1$, $n_{\mathrm{bacteria}}=2$, and $n_{\mathrm{eukaryote}}=8$, so domain means are descriptive summaries rather than balanced comparative estimates.  The largest reported GC3 rank effect was on $d_{\mathrm{usage}}$, with $\rho=0.136$.

\paragraph{Relation to the translation axis.}
The same finite memory supports several nearby constraints on interpretation.  The non-tRNA residual optimality result identifies $f_3$ translation selection as the dominant non-tRNA residual axis in the powered panel.  The third-axis identity assay provisionally aligns $d_{\perp}$ with within-family usage frequency, with mean cosine $0.128$ and one-sided sign-test $p=0.000488$.  The mechanism assay reports that the GC-orthogonalized $d_{\perp}$ retains significant cross-species alignment and held-out $R^2$, while its mechanism classification is selection-not-GC and not-wobble-clustered, with CSC or positional identity still requiring an added contact.  The three-axis sufficiency assay reports a fourth mechanism present.  These facts make the axis spectrum biologically pointed, but they do not by themselves produce a measured ribosome or translation-efficiency survival entry.

\paragraph{Local test statistic.}
For an organism $o$, a coding sequence $x$, and a translation readout $T_j$, let $q_{f_3}(x,o)$ denote the amino-acid-quotiented $B^{*}_{Q6}$ $f_3$-stress residual coordinate.  Let $Z$ contain amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, coding-sequence length, M-density, matched mRNA abundance when a TE row is used, and the simpler codon baselines declared for the packet.  The admissible survival score is a controlled finite-row gain, for example
$$
\begin{aligned}
S_{f_3,T_j}
&=\operatorname{Score}(T_j\mid Z,q_{f_3})-\operatorname{Score}(T_j\mid Z),\\
\text{support} &\Longleftrightarrow S_{f_3,T_j}>0\text{ on a nondegenerate powered panel.}
\end{aligned}
$$
A rank-saturated all-$1.0$ surface on only three organisms is therefore a null boundary, not survival evidence.  The coordinate becomes a dynamical translation distinction only where context-specific tRNA or wobble weights satisfy $w_c\neq w_{c^{f_3}}$ and where the same rows also contain a bounded translation readout.

\paragraph{Mechanism-side finite contact.}
The tRNA-poverty assay gives one mechanism-facing contact without completing the survival test.  It computed $40$ organisms out of $46$ requested organisms, skipped $6$, used $1000$ permutations at $\alpha=0.05$, and called the $R$ sense codons systematically tRNA-poor under GtRNAdb tGCN plus dos Reis wobble weights.  This supports a tRNA-supply interpretation for $R$ avoidance, but it remains upstream of the required same-row $S_{f_3,T_j}$ calculation.  A modeled tAI or stAI row may be used as a bounded codon-adaptation readout; ribosome profiling or TE rows are needed when the claim is about measured translation behavior.

\paragraph{Boundary of the claim.}
The present chapter records a finite powered axis spectrum and a constrained translation-readout target.  It does not assert that $f_3$-stress changes protein abundance, folded structure, localization, stability, biochemical function, phenotype, physical admissibility, mechanism, or a universal biological rule.  Those interpretations require their own layer-matched contacts and their own controlled statistics.  In particular, codon geometry, tAI or stAI weights, workflow eligibility, and the notation $S_{f_3,T_j}$ are not substitutes for joined organism- or CDS-level rows containing codon counts, wobble or tRNA weights, measured ribosome occupancy or TE where claimed, matched mRNA controls, and the declared covariates.  The falsifier is equally local: if every controlled $S_{f_3,T_j}$ remains non-positive, or if the design is rank-degenerate, the translation-readout claim fails while the internal residual-coordinate spectrum remains intact.
