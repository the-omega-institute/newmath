\subsection{Per-organism strength spectrum for the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual surface}
\label{subsec:bioreality-q6-axis-strength-spectrum-per-organism}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite carrier.}
The record concerns a finite coding-sequence by organism residual surface restricted to the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ synonymous coordinate family.  The active coordinate is the $f_3$-stress direction after amino-acid projection, together with matched rows that can read out translation-layer quantities such as tAI, stAI, ribosome occupancy, or translation efficiency.  The finite saturation row fixes the relevant codon support.  The set $M=\operatorname{Med}(R)\cup\{\mathrm{ACA}\}$ is
$$
\begin{aligned}
M=\{&\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{ACA},\mathrm{ACG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\\
&\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
After $f_3$ saturation, the active row removes exactly $\mathrm{ACA}$ and $\mathrm{ACG}$ from this support:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\operatorname{Sat}_{f_3}(R)=\{&\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\\
&\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\},
\end{aligned}
$$
and the recorded $f_3$ boundary set is empty.  Thus the chapter is not using an open-ended codon universe: it is evaluating a named residual coordinate on a finite codon row with a declared post-quotient stress surface.

\paragraph{Observed powered spectrum.}
The powered axis-strength run used $5$ folds, required at least $500$ proteins per organism, and computed $11$ organisms out of $16$ requested organisms on a synonymous design with $41$ columns.  Its deterministic seed was recorded for the experiment, and the run passed.  Across the captured organisms, the average spectrum was led by $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$, followed by $d_{\mathrm{tRNA}}$.  The per-organism dominant-axis count was concentrated entirely on $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\#\{\text{organisms dominated by }d_{\mathrm{resid4}}\}=11.
\end{aligned}
$$
The domain summaries were explicitly descriptive rather than inferential because the available panel was imbalanced: $1$ archaeal organism, $2$ bacterial organisms, and $8$ eukaryotic organisms.  The largest GC3 rank association reported in the same row was small, with $d_{\mathrm{usage}}$ giving $\rho=0.136$.  This places the result in the category of a finite powered spectrum over the available rows, not a domain-universal biological law.

\paragraph{Mechanism-adjacent rows.}
Several neighboring finite rows constrain how the strength spectrum should be read.  A separate powered tRNA-poverty test used $40$ computed organisms out of $46$ requested organisms, skipped $6$, used $1000$ permutations at $\alpha=0.05$, and passed with the mechanism call that $R$ sense codons are systematically tRNA-poor under GtRNAdb tGCN plus dos Reis wobble weights.  This supports a tRNA-supply interpretation for the $R$-avoidance component, but it does not by itself produce a measured translation-efficiency survival row for the $f_3$-stress coordinate.

The universal-core row also passed on $11$ computed organisms out of $16$ requested organisms, again with $5$ folds, at least $500$ proteins per organism, and $41$ synonymous design columns.  The non-tRNA residual optimality row identified $f_3$ translation selection as the dominant residual axis under the same computed-organism and design-column counts.  The two-axis closure row did not close the story: it reported a third axis, with the reason that $d_{\perp}$ had systematically positive held-out $R^2$ and mutually same-signed directions across organisms.  The third-axis identity row then provisionally aligned $d_{\perp}$ with usage frequency within family, with mean cosine $0.128$ and one-sided sign-test $p=0.000488$.  The third-axis mechanism row retained the signal after GC orthogonalization and described it as selection-not-GC, not wobble-clustered, with CSC or positional identity still needing a separate contact.

\paragraph{Fourth-axis exclusions.}
The three-axis sufficiency test passed but concluded that a fourth mechanism is present.  The subsequent fourth-mechanism rows delimit what that extra direction is not.  The mRNA-stability row passed and concluded that the fourth axis is not mRNA stability in the assay, because the yeast $d_{\mathrm{opt\_halflife}}$ direction had low alignment with abundance $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$ under the fixed gates.  The codon-pair row passed and concluded \emph{not codon pair}, using $5$ folds, at least $500$ proteins per organism, $41$ synonymous design columns, and $50$ shuffle-null replicates.  The translation-accuracy row passed and concluded \emph{not accuracy}, using $11$ computed organisms out of $16$, $5$ folds, $41$ synonymous design columns, and $200$ permutations.  The elongation-dwell row passed for \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae} with conclusion \emph{partial}.  These rows narrow the interpretation of $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$, but they do not convert the axis-strength spectrum into a protein-level, structure-level, localization-level, stability-level, function-level, phenotype-level, or universal statement.

\paragraph{Translation-readout gate.}
The scientific claim is therefore conditional.  The $f_3$-stress coordinate becomes a translation-readout distinction only where the organism or context supplies an asymmetry between a codon $c$ and its third-position partner $c^{f_3}$, for example through tRNA supply or wobble weights with $w_c\ne w_{c^{f_3}}$.  The admissible readback is a controlled survival entry $S_{f3\text{-}\mathrm{stress},T_j}$ for a translation-layer row $T_j$, such as tAI, stAI, ribosome occupancy, or translation efficiency.  The required statistic must remain non-vacuous after amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, CDS length, M-density baseline, matched mRNA control where the readout is TE, rank checks, and simpler codon-baseline ablation.

A compact statement of the required positive case is
$$
\begin{aligned}
S_{f3\text{-}\mathrm{stress},T_j}>0
\end{aligned}
$$
for at least one controlled translation row $T_j$ in a rank-powered panel.  The corresponding refusal case is
$$
\begin{aligned}
S_{f3\text{-}\mathrm{stress},T_j}\leq 0
\end{aligned}
$$
for every matched translation row, or an all-equal rank-degenerate fitted surface such as the recorded three-organism all-$1.0$ boundary.  Such a saturated or rank-degenerate matrix is a null boundary, not survival evidence.

\paragraph{Local exclusion rule.}
This chapter records a finite cross-layer target and the powered axis spectrum already observed for it.  It does not assert that the $f_3$-stress coordinate changes protein abundance, folded structure, localization, stability, molecular function, organismal phenotype, physical admissibility, mechanism, or a general biological law.  None of those conclusions follows from codon topology, modeled tAI or stAI weights, tRNA-copy asymmetry, identifier routing, or the $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$ dominance count alone.  Each higher layer requires its own matched measurement contact and its own controlled statistic.  Until a same-organism or same-CDS translation-readout matrix supplies a positive, nondegenerate $S_{f3\text{-}\mathrm{stress},T_j}$ entry, the correct reading is a powered residual-axis spectrum with a translation-row test still bounded by its measurement surface.
