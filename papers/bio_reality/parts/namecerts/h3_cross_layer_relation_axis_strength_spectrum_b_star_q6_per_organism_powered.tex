\paragraph{Finite carrier.}
The finite object in this record is a CDS-by-organism residual coordinate table restricted to the $B^*_{Q6}$ synonymous surface after amino-acid quotienting.  Its relevant $f_3$-stress component compares codons paired by third-position $f_3$ action, but it is not treated as a biological effect unless a translation-layer row is attached.  The saturated $f_3$ construction supplies the finite codon boundary used here: the set $M=\operatorname{Med}(R)\cup\{\mathrm{ACA}\}$ is
$$
\begin{aligned}
M=\{&\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{ACA},\mathrm{ACG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\\
&\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The $f_3$-saturated $R$ part removes the two ACA/ACG entries and is
$$
\begin{aligned}
\operatorname{Sat}_{f_3}(R)=\{&\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\\
&\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\},
\end{aligned}
$$
with an empty residual $f_3$ boundary, recorded as $\partial_{f_3}=\varnothing$.  These rows define the coordinate geometry only; they do not by themselves observe ribosome motion, protein amount, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, phenotype, mechanism, or a universal rule.

\paragraph{Powered spectrum readout.}
The powered per-organism run used five folds, required at least $500$ proteins per organism, and evaluated $41$ synonymous design columns.  It requested $16$ organisms and computed $11$ usable organism rows.  In those rows, the captured axis-strength spectra were led on average by $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$, followed by $d_{\mathrm{tRNA}}$.  The per-organism dominance count was concentrated entirely on the fourth residual axis:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\#\{\text{organisms dominated by }d_{\mathrm{resid4}}\}=11.
\end{aligned}
$$
Domain means are therefore descriptive rather than balanced-domain estimates, because the computed panel had $1$ archaeal, $2$ bacterial, and $8$ eukaryotic rows.  The largest reported GC3 rank association was small, with $d_{\mathrm{usage}}$ giving $\rho=0.136$.  The deterministic run identifier was $0146012d8909$, and the deterministic seed was $\mathrm{sha256{:}b\_star\_q6\_axis\_strength\_spectrum\_powered{:}deterministic}$.

\paragraph{Relation to neighboring finite facts.}
The spectrum is consistent with the broader $Q6$ residual-axis packet, but it should be read narrowly.  A separate powered run on the non-tRNA residual optimality axis found $f_3$ translation selection as the dominant residual direction across $11$ computed organisms out of $16$ requested organisms, again with five folds and $41$ synonymous design columns.  Another powered run identified $d_{\perp}$ with usage frequency within synonymous family, reporting mean cosine $0.128$ and one-sided sign-test $p=0.000488$.  Its mechanism run classified that axis as selection-not-GC and not-wobble-clustered, while leaving the CSC or positional identity as requiring a further layer-matched contact.  A three-axis sufficiency run concluded that a fourth mechanism is present, which is why the dominance of $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$ in the present spectrum is treated as a finite spectral fact rather than collapsed into the tRNA and $f_3$ span.

\paragraph{Mechanistic exclusions and bounded support.}
The surrounding fourth-mechanism checks constrain overinterpretation.  The mRNA-stability assay concluded $\mathrm{fourth\_axis\_not\_mrna\_stability}$ because the yeast $d_{\mathrm{opt\_halflife}}$ direction had low alignment with abundance $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$ under the fixed gates.  The codon-pair assay concluded $\mathrm{not\_codon\_pair}$ using $50$ shuffle nulls.  The translation-accuracy assay concluded $\mathrm{not\_accuracy}$ with $200$ permutations, $11$ computed organisms, $16$ requested organisms, five folds, and $41$ synonymous design columns.  The elongation-dwell assay gave only a partial call in Saccharomyces cerevisiae.  Separately, the $R$-sense tRNA-poverty permutation run computed $40$ organisms out of $46$ requested organisms, skipped $6$, used $1000$ permutations at $\alpha=0.05$, and supported a tRNA-supply explanation for $R$ avoidance under GtRNAdb tGCN plus dos Reis wobble weights.  Thus the present claim is not that $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$ has a settled biochemical identity; it is that, on the finite powered panel, the residual strength spectrum has a reproducible fourth-axis lead after the stated synonymous design.

\paragraph{Translation-readout gate.}
For this chapter the only admissible cross-layer readback is a controlled translation-row survival statistic for $f_3$-stress against $tAI$, $stAI$, ribosome occupancy, or translation efficiency.  The intended survival entry has the schematic form
$$
\begin{aligned}
S_{f_3\text{-stress},T_j}>0
\end{aligned}
$$
only after amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, CDS length, $M$-density, matched mRNA abundance, rank, and simpler-baseline controls have been applied.  The current finite rows do not contain that same-organism and same-CDS joined matrix for codon counts, wobble weights, direct translation readout, and matched mRNA control.  Consequently the spectrum supports a bounded residual-coordinate distinction and a prioritized translation-readout target, but it does not establish translation realization in measured ribosome data.  It also does not license any claim about protein abundance, folded structure, physical admissibility, biological function, localization, stability, phenotype, mechanism, or universality.  Those statements require separate layer-specific contacts and their own controlled statistics.

\paragraph{Local falsification rule.}
The claim is rejected at the translation interface if a powered organism panel with matched codon, wobble-weight, translation-readout, and mRNA rows gives no nondegenerate positive entry for $S_{f_3\text{-stress},T_j}$ under the declared controls, or if the fitted surface is rank-saturated in the manner of an all-$1.0$ small-panel matrix.  A positive modeled tAI or stAI association alone is not enough; a bounded model-weight row may motivate the test, but survival is counted only when the controlled readout row remains non-vacuous and positive after ablation of amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, length, $M$-density, mRNA control, rank effects, and simpler codon baselines.
