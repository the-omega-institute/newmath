\subsection{A powered organism panel for the $B^*_{Q6}$ axis-strength spectrum}
\label{sec:bioreality-q6-axis-strength-spectrum-powered}

\paragraph{Finite surface.}
This record treats the $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinate surface as a finite CDS-by-organism table after amino-acid projection.  The coordinate of interest is the $f_3$-stress row: a synonymous third-position imbalance that remains meaningful only after amino-acid composition has been quotiented away.  The finite saturation check fixes the combinatorial boundary.  The set $M=\mathrm{Med}_R\cup\mathrm{ACA}$ contains the $20$ codons
$\{\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{ACA},\mathrm{ACG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\}$.
The $f_3$-saturation of $R$ removes exactly $\mathrm{ACA}$ and $\mathrm{ACG}$, leaving the $18$ codons
$\{\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\}$, and the recorded $f_3$ boundary is empty.  Thus the axis being tested is not an informal codon label; it is a closed finite row of the residual codon-coordinate table.

\paragraph{Powered spectrum.}
The powered axis-strength experiment computed $11$ organisms out of $16$ requested organisms, with a minimum of $500$ proteins per organism, $41$ synonymous design columns, and $5$ folds.  On this finite panel the captured spectra are led by $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$ on average, followed by $d_{\mathrm{tRNA}}$.  The per-organism dominance count is concentrated entirely on the fourth residual direction:
$$
\begin{aligned}
N_{\mathrm{organisms}} &= 11,\\
N_{\mathrm{requested}} &= 16,\\
N_{\mathrm{synonymous\ columns}} &= 41,\\
N_{\mathrm{folds}} &= 5,\\
\operatorname{DomCount}(d_{\mathrm{resid4}}) &= 11.
\end{aligned}
$$
The domain split is explicitly imbalanced, with $1$ archaeal, $2$ bacterial, and $8$ eukaryotic organisms, so domain means are descriptive summaries rather than balanced-domain estimates.  The largest recorded GC3 rank effect is on $d_{\mathrm{usage}}$, with $\rho=0.136$.  This makes the finite result a powered organism-panel spectrum, not a universal domain law.

\paragraph{Relation to nearby axes.}
The surrounding finite rows locate this spectrum inside a constrained translation-adaptation geometry.  A separate powered residual-optimality experiment computed $11$ of $16$ requested organisms and found that the non-tRNA residual optimality direction aligns most with $f_3\_\mathrm{translation\_selection}$.  The two-axis closure test on the $d_{\mathrm{tRNA}}$ and $f_3$ span also computed $11$ of $16$ organisms and reported a third axis, because $d_{\perp}$ had systematically positive held-out $R^2$ and mutually same-signed organism directions.  The powered identity test then provisionally identified $d_{\perp}$ with usage frequency within synonymous family, with mean cosine $0.128$ and one-sided sign-test $p=0.000488$.  The mechanism check for that third axis reported selection-not-GC and not-wobble-clustered, while leaving the CSC or positional identity as a contact-dependent question.  These facts support a finite spectrum of residual directions rather than a single collapsed codon-usage scalar.

\paragraph{Mechanism pressure.}
The boundary-set tRNA-poverty experiment used $40$ computed organisms out of $46$ requested organisms, skipped $6$, and used $1000$ permutations at $\alpha=0.05$.  Its finite call is that $R$ sense codons are systematically tRNA-poor under GtRNAdb tGCN together with dos Reis wobble weights.  This supplies mechanism pressure for an $R$-avoidance interpretation at the codon-adaptation layer.  It does not by itself measure ribosome occupancy or translation efficiency.  The present chapter therefore reads the tRNA and wobble information as a bounded codon-adaptation surface: an organism may expose $f_3$-stress as a dynamical distinction only where the contextual tRNA or wobble weights break the $f_3$ pairing symmetry.

\paragraph{Fourth-direction controls.}
The finite panel also records that the first three directions do not exhaust the observed optimality surface.  The three-axis sufficiency test computed $11$ of $16$ requested organisms with $41$ synonymous design columns and $5$ folds and concluded that a fourth mechanism is present.  Three negative identity checks constrain that fourth direction: the mRNA-stability assay concluded $\mathrm{fourth\_axis\_not\_mRNA\_stability}$ under its fixed gates; the codon-pair assay concluded $\mathrm{not\_codon\_pair}$ using $50$ shuffle nulls; and the translation-accuracy assay concluded $\mathrm{not\_accuracy}$ using $200$ permutations on $11$ computed organisms out of $16$ requested.  The elongation-dwell assay on \textit{Saccharomyces cerevisiae} returned only a partial call.  Consequently the powered spectrum should be stated as a finite axis-strength result whose leading direction is $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$, not as an identified downstream biological mechanism.

\paragraph{Local mathematical statement.}
Let $Q$ be the finite residual-coordinate table after amino-acid projection, and let $T$ denote a matched translation-readout table, when such a table exists.  The only permitted readout statistic for this chapter is a controlled survival entry of the form
$$
\begin{aligned}
S_{f_3,T_j} > 0
\end{aligned}
$$
after controlling amino-acid composition, organism identity, GC3, CDS length, $M$-density, matched mRNA abundance when translation efficiency is used, rank sufficiency, and simpler codon baselines.  The powered spectrum described above does not itself provide this $S_{f_3,T_j}$ row.  It says that the residual coordinate system has a stable finite axis-strength profile on the computed organism panel, with $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$ dominating all $11$ computed organisms and with a small GC3 rank association recorded separately at $\rho=0.136$ for $d_{\mathrm{usage}}$.

\paragraph{Boundary of interpretation.}
No claim about measured translation, ribosome occupancy, protein abundance, folding, localization, physical admissibility, molecular function, organismal phenotype, mechanism, or universality follows from this row alone.  A tAI or stAI weight can bound a codon-adaptation readout, but it is not an observed ribosome footprint.  A ribosome-profiling or translation-efficiency contact can test the next layer only after same-organism and same-CDS codon, translation, and mRNA rows are joined and the controlled statistic is computed.  A protein, structure, localization, stability, function, perturbation, or phenotype claim would require its own layer-matched measurement.  The falsification rule is therefore local and finite: if every controlled $S_{f_3,T_j}$ entry is non-positive, vacuous, or rank-degenerate on a powered matched panel, then the $f_3$-stress coordinate has not survived as a translation-readout distinction, even though the finite codon-coordinate spectrum remains a valid organism-panel result.
