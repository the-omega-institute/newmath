\subsection{Per-organism axis spectrum for the $B^*_{Q6}$ residual surface}\label{sec:bioreality-q6-axis-strength-spectrum-per-organism}

\paragraph{Finite carrier.}
The record concerns a finite CDS-by-organism residual coordinate surface restricted to the $B^*_{Q6}$ synonymous design after amino-acid projection.  The active codon geometry is not the full codon cube: it is the $f_3$-stress surface on the post-quotient residual coordinates, paired only with translation-layer readouts when such readouts are separately present.  The finite saturation calculation fixes the relevant $f_3$ boundary before any biological interpretation is attempted.  In that calculation the mediate set with $R$ and ACA contains
$$\begin{aligned}
M &= \{\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{ACA},\mathrm{ACG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\\
&\qquad \mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\\
&\qquad \mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\},
\end{aligned}$$
whereas the $f_3$ saturation removes ACA and ACG:
$$\begin{aligned}
\operatorname{Sat}_{f_3}(R) &= \{\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\\
&\qquad \mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\\
&\qquad \mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\}.
\end{aligned}$$
The computed $f_3$ boundary set is empty.  Thus this chapter does not begin from an unbounded codon-language claim; it begins from a finite residual surface whose stress coordinate is already pinned down by a named finite codon list.

\paragraph{Axis-spectrum computation.}
The powered spectrum run was evaluated under experiment id \texttt{b\_star\_q6\_axis\_strength\_spectrum\_powered} with deterministic seed \texttt{sha256:b\_star\_q6\_axis\_strength\_spectrum\_powered:deterministic}.  It requested 16 organisms and computed 11 organisms, using 5 folds, a minimum of 500 proteins per organism, and 41 synonymous design columns.  The run status was passed.  Within the captured spectra, the average leading axis was $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$, followed by $d_{\mathrm{tRNA}}$.  The per-organism dominant-axis count was
$$\begin{aligned}
\#\{\text{organisms dominated by }d_{\mathrm{resid4}}\} &= 11,
\end{aligned}$$
so every organism actually computed in this finite panel had $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$ as its dominant captured axis.  The domain partition was too imbalanced to support a domain-level biological generalization: the descriptive counts were one archaeal organism, two bacterial organisms, and eight eukaryotic organisms.  The largest reported GC3 rank association was small, with $d_{\mathrm{usage}}$ giving $\rho=0.136$.

\paragraph{Readback allowed by the finite rows.}
The positive content of the spectrum is an axis-ordering statement on the controlled residual design: in the 11 computed organisms, the fourth residual direction was the strongest captured axis, and the tRNA-oriented direction was next on average.  This is compatible with the surrounding powered records in which the non-tRNA residual optimality axis is reported as $f_3$ translation selection, the two-axis tRNA plus $f_3$ span leaves a third axis present, and $d_{\perp}$ is provisionally identified with usage frequency within family.  The separate third-axis identity run reports mean cosine $0.128$ with one-sided sign-test $p=0.000488$, while the mechanism run keeps the same direction GC-orthogonalized and classifies the result as selection-not-GC, not wobble-clustered, with CSC or positional identity still needing an outside contact.  These companion facts sharpen the interpretation of the axis spectrum: $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$ is not being presented as a universal mechanism, but as the strongest finite residual axis found under the declared powered design.

\paragraph{Translation-row gate.}
The $f_3$-stress coordinate becomes a translation-layer object only after a matched translation readout is joined to the same finite rows.  A valid survival entry has the form $S_{f3\text{-}stress,T_j}$, where $T_j$ is an explicitly bounded tAI, stAI, ribosome-occupancy, or translation-efficiency row.  The intended controlled statistic is evaluated after amino-acid composition, organism identity, GC3, CDS length, $M$-density, and mRNA controls, with simpler codon baselines ablated.  In schematic form the admissible readout test is
$$\begin{aligned}
S_{f3\text{-}stress,T_j} &> 0
\quad \text{after controls } Z=\{\mathrm{AA},\mathrm{organism},\mathrm{GC3},\mathrm{length},M\text{-}\mathrm{density},\mathrm{mRNA}\}.
\end{aligned}$$
The present finite rows do not contain that same-CDS joined survival matrix.  Therefore the axis spectrum is ready as a residual-coordinate spectrum, but it is not by itself a measured translation-survival result.

\paragraph{Local non-promotion rule.}
The chapter makes a deliberately narrow biological commitment.  The finite spectrum may be used to say that, in the computed 11-organism panel, $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$ leads the captured $B^*_{Q6}$ residual spectra and that the $f_3$-related residual surface remains a plausible target for translation-readout testing.  It may not be used to assert observed ribosome occupancy, translation efficiency, protein abundance, folded structure, physical admissibility, biological function, phenotype, mechanism, or universality.  tAI or stAI weights are bounded codon-adaptation readouts, not direct ribosome-footprint observations.  Ribosome-profiling and matched mRNA contacts, when present only as contact classes, are not computed $S^{QT}$ entries.  The three-organism all-$1.0$ controlled matrix is treated as a rank-degenerate null boundary rather than survival evidence.  A downstream statement becomes available only when a separate layer-matched contact supplies the relevant rows and the controlled statistic is non-vacuous and positive.

\paragraph{Falsification condition.}
The finite axis-spectrum claim would be weakened if a powered recomputation on the same declared design failed to recover $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$ as the leading captured axis across the computed organisms, or if the apparent residual strength were absorbed by GC3 rank, amino-acid composition, length, $M$-density, or organism labels.  The stronger translation-readout conjecture has a separate failure condition: if every controlled $S_{f3\text{-}stress,T_j}$ entry remains non-positive, rank-degenerate, or unavailable after the matched translation rows are joined, then the $f_3$-stress coordinate remains a residual spectral distinction only and should not be promoted to a translation-layer survival claim.
