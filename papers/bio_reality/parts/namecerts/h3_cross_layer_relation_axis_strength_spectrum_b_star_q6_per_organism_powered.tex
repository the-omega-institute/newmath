\subsection{Per-organism axis-strength spectrum for the $B^*_{Q6}$ $f_3$-stress coordinate}\label{sec:bior reality-q6-axis-strength-spectrum}\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite carrier.} The finite object in this chapter is the CDS-by-organism residual coordinate surface restricted to the $B^*_{Q6}$ synonymous residual basis, with the $f_3$-stress coordinate separated after amino-acid projection. The row is not a protein-output or phenotype row. It is a finite comparison of residual codon-coordinate directions against organism-scoped translation-adjacent readouts, with controls for amino-acid composition, organism identity, GC3, CDS length, $M$-density, and matched mRNA where a measured translation-efficiency row is used. In the present finite table, the powered spectrum computation requested $16$ organisms and retained $11$ organisms satisfying the row requirements, using $5$ folds, at least $500$ proteins per organism, and $41$ synonymous-design columns. The deterministic seed was recorded as $\mathrm{sha256{:}b\_star\_q6\_axis\_strength\_spectrum\_powered{:}deterministic}$, and the run status was passed.

\paragraph{The $f_3$ boundary row.} The finite $f_3$ saturation datum fixes the codon support on which this chapter is read. The set $M=\mathrm{Med}(R)\cup\mathrm{ACA}$ is the $20$-codon list
$$\begin{aligned}
M=\{&\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{ACA},\mathrm{ACG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\\
&\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\}.
\end{aligned}$$
The saturated $f_3$ image of $R$ removes only the pair $\mathrm{ACA},\mathrm{ACG}$ from that list, giving the $18$-codon support
$$\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{Sat}_{f_3}(R)=\{&\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\\
&\mathrm{CUU},\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\}.
\end{aligned}$$
The recorded $f_3$ boundary set is empty. Thus the row is not asserting a missing codon obstruction at the $f_3$ boundary; it asks whether a residual direction that survives amino-acid quotienting has any nondegenerate translation-layer expression once organism and sequence-composition covariates are held fixed.

\paragraph{Observed spectrum.} The powered per-organism spectrum is decisive about the axis ordering inside the retained finite panel, but only within the assay that produced it. The captured spectra are led by $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$ on average, followed by $d_{\mathrm{tRNA}}$. The per-organism dominant-axis count is
$$\begin{aligned}
\#\{\mathrm{dominant}=d_{\mathrm{resid4}}\}=11,
\end{aligned}$$
with no retained organism reported as dominated by another axis in this run. The domain split is explicitly imbalanced, with $n_{\mathrm{archaea}}=1$, $n_{\mathrm{bacteria}}=2$, and $n_{\mathrm{eukaryote}}=8$; therefore domain means are descriptive rather than a balanced-domain law. The largest recorded GC3 rank effect is the $d_{\mathrm{usage}}$ effect with $\rho=0.136$, which is small enough that the row should be read as a controlled spectrum rather than as a GC3 explanation of the axis ordering.

\paragraph{Relation to adjacent finite rows.} The spectrum is coherent with the surrounding finite rows without being promoted into a universal biological mechanism. A separate powered boundary-row computation found that $R$ sense codons are systematically tRNA-poor under GtRNAdb tRNA gene-copy counts with dos Reis wobble weights, using $40$ computed organisms out of $46$ requested organisms, $6$ skipped organisms, $1000$ permutations, and $\alpha=0.05$. Another powered row identifies the third axis $d_{\perp}$ with usage frequency within family, with mean cosine $0.128$ and one-sided sign-test $p=0.000488$ across the retained design. Its mechanism row records that the signal remains after GC orthogonalization and is not simply a wobble-clustered axis. A further residual-optimality row reports that the non-tRNA residual optimality direction aligns most with $f_3$ translation selection, while the three-axis sufficiency row reports a fourth mechanism as present. The fourth-mechanism checks are bounded: mRNA stability is not the dominant fourth-axis identity in the fixed assay, codon-pair and translation-accuracy tests are negative in their recorded calls, and elongation dwell is only partial in the yeast row.

\paragraph{Interpretation.} The scientific content of the row is therefore a spectrum statement. In the retained panel, after amino-acid projection and the declared controls, the strongest captured per-organism residual direction is $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$, not the tRNA axis, and this dominance is repeated in all $11$ retained organisms. The $f_3$-stress coordinate becomes a candidate translation-readout distinction only where context-dependent tRNA or wobble weights break the third-position $f_3$ symmetry. A modeled tAI or stAI surface may bound a translation-adjacent readout, but it is not the same thing as measured ribosome occupancy. A measured translation-efficiency claim would require same-organism and same-CDS codon rows, ribosome-footprint or translation-efficiency rows, and matched mRNA rows, followed by the declared controlled survival statistic.

\paragraph{Local no-promotion rule.} This chapter does not infer protein abundance, folded structure, physical admissibility, biological function, phenotype, mechanism, or universality from the axis spectrum. It also does not treat the three-organism all-$1.0$ controlled matrix as survival; such a rank-degenerate surface is a null boundary. The only bounded readback available here is the controlled survival entry $S_{f3\text{-}\mathrm{stress},T_j}$ for tAI, stAI, ribosome occupancy, or translation efficiency when the corresponding row is non-vacuous and positive under the declared controls. Without that separate row, the finite spectrum remains an organism-scoped residual-coordinate result, not a downstream biological realization claim.
