\subsection{Per-organism axis-strength spectrum for the $B^*_{Q6}$ residual surface}

\paragraph{Finite coordinate surface.}
This record concerns a finite CDS-by-organism residual coordinate surface after amino-acid projection, restricted to the $B^*_{Q6}$ synonymy geometry and, in particular, to the $f_3$-stress direction whose biological interpretation is allowed only at a bounded translation-readout interface.  The finite codon-side saturation row is explicit.  The ambient marked set is
$$
\begin{aligned}
M &= \{\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{ACA},\mathrm{ACG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\},\\
\operatorname{Sat}_{f_3}(R) &= \{\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\},\\
\partial_{f_3} &= \varnothing.
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the finite row has a closed $f_3$ saturation boundary, while the two codons $\mathrm{ACA}$ and $\mathrm{ACG}$ remain outside the displayed saturated set.  The relevant biological test is not the existence of this codon geometry by itself, but whether a controlled translation row survives after amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, CDS length, $M$-density, and matched mRNA controls.

\paragraph{Powered spectrum row.}
The powered spectrum computation for claim \texttt{h3.cross\_layer\_relation.axis\_strength\_spectrum.b\_star\_q6\_per\_organism\_powered} used five folds, required at least $500$ proteins per organism, and retained $41$ synonymous-design columns.  It computed $11$ organisms out of $16$ requested organisms.  In this finite panel, the captured spectra are led on average by $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$, followed by $d_{\mathrm{tRNA}}$.  The per-organism dominant-axis count is not split: all $11$ computed organisms have $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$ as the dominant captured direction.  The domain composition is imbalanced, with
$$
\begin{aligned}
n_{\mathrm{archaea}} &= 1,\\
n_{\mathrm{bacteria}} &= 2,\\
n_{\mathrm{eukaryote}} &= 8,
\end{aligned}
$$
so domain means are descriptive summaries of this panel rather than balanced-domain estimates.  The largest GC3 rank effect in the spectrum row is the $d_{\mathrm{usage}}$ effect with $\rho=0.136$.

\paragraph{Relation to the translation boundary.}
The spectrum row is a rank-powered, finite-organism statement about which residual axes dominate the fitted synonymous-coordinate surface.  It does not, by itself, assert that $f_3$-stress changes ribosome occupancy, translation efficiency, protein abundance, folding, localization, stability, molecular function, phenotype, mechanism, or a universal biological law.  The permitted readout is narrower: a coordinate $q_i$ may be called translation-surviving only if a matched row $T_j$ has a positive controlled entry
$$
\begin{aligned}
S_{q_i,T_j} &> 0
\end{aligned}
$$
after the declared controls and simpler-codon-baseline ablation.  For the specific $f_3$-stress packet, this requires rows joining codon counts, wobble or tRNA-weight asymmetry, a direct or explicitly bounded translation readout such as tAI, stAI, ribosome occupancy, or translation efficiency, and matched mRNA abundance.  In the present finite memory, those same-row translation-survival entries are not present.  Consequently the spectrum result is a coordinate-strength result and a prioritization signal, not a translation-realization result.

\paragraph{Mechanistic side constraints already present.}
Several adjacent finite rows sharpen what can and cannot be inferred.  The $R$-sense tRNA-poverty computation examined $40$ organisms out of $46$ requested, skipped $6$, used $1000$ permutations at $\alpha=0.05$, and concluded that $R$ sense codons are systematically tRNA-poor under GtRNAdb tGCN plus dos Reis wobble weights.  This supports a tRNA-supply route for $R$ avoidance, but it still does not replace a same-row $S_{f_3\text{-}\mathrm{stress},T_j}$ computation.  The third-axis identity row found alignment of $d_\perp$ to within-family usage frequency, with mean cosine $0.128$ and one-sided sign-test $p=0.000488$.  The third-axis mechanism row retained significant cross-species alignment and held-out $R^2$ after GC orthogonalization, yielding the summary ``selection-not-GC; not-wobble-clustered; CSC/positional identity needs external.''  These rows keep the spectrum interpretable as a structured synonymous-coordinate phenomenon while preserving the requirement that every biological promotion be supplied by its own layer-matched contact.

\paragraph{Falsification rule.}
The local falsifier is finite and operational.  Let $q_{f_3}$ denote the $f_3$-stress residual coordinate and let $T_j$ range over the declared translation readouts.  The cross-layer claim fails at the translation interface if the controlled surface remains non-positive or rank-degenerate across the powered panel:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\forall j,\quad S_{q_{f_3},T_j} \leq 0,
\end{aligned}
$$
or if the apparent survival is produced only by a saturated rank surface such as an all-$1.0$ three-organism matrix.  A positive modeled tAI or stAI association may guide the next assay, but it is not an observed ribosome-occupancy measurement.  A positive ribosome or translation-efficiency row, if later obtained, would still stop at translation unless a separate protein, structure, physical-assay, function, localization, stability, or phenotype contact supplies that higher-layer row under its own controls.

\paragraph{Local conclusion.}
The chapter therefore records a finite axis-strength result: in a five-fold, $11$-organism, $41$-column synonymous design with at least $500$ proteins per organism, $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$ dominates every computed organism and leads the average captured spectrum, with $d_{\mathrm{tRNA}}$ next and with the largest GC3 rank effect only $\rho=0.136$ on $d_{\mathrm{usage}}$.  The associated $f_3$ saturation row has empty displayed boundary and a concrete codon list.  The scientifically admissible reading is that the $B^*_{Q6}$ residual surface supplies a prioritized coordinate family for translation-layer testing.  It does not yet supply the same-row, mRNA-controlled $f_3$-stress translation-survival statistic required for biological promotion beyond bounded coordinate-spectrum evidence.
