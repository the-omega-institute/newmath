\subsection{Rank-Powered Axis Spectrum for the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ $f_{3}$-Stress Coordinate}\label{subsec:bioreality-q6-f3-axis-strength-spectrum}\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite carrier.}
This record concerns a finite CDS-by-organism residual coordinate surface in which the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ coordinate is restricted to the $f_{3}$-stress direction and then compared with matched translation-layer readouts only as bounded rows. The amino-acid quotient is part of the carrier: synonymous imbalance is evaluated after amino-acid identity has been projected away, so the tested object is not a raw codon-frequency table. The row is also conditional on a possible third-position asymmetry: the coordinate can become a biological readout candidate only in organisms or contexts where the tRNA or wobble weights separate a codon $c$ from its $f_{3}$ partner $c^{f_{3}}$.

\paragraph{Saturation boundary.}
The finite $f_{3}$ saturation computation fixes the row support before any organism-level readout is considered. The computed set $M=\operatorname{Med}(R)\cup\{ACA\}$ contains twenty codons:
$$\begin{aligned}
M=\{&AAA,AAG,ACA,ACG,AGA,AGG,AUA,AUG,CUA,CUC,CUG,CUU,\\
&UAA,UAG,UCA,UCG,UGA,UGG,UUA,UUG\}.
\end{aligned}$$
The saturated $f_{3}$ image removes $ACA$ and $ACG$, leaving eighteen codons:
$$\begin{aligned}
\operatorname{Sat}_{f_{3}}(R)=\{&AAA,AAG,AGA,AGG,AUA,AUG,CUA,CUC,CUG,CUU,\\
&UAA,UAG,UCA,UCG,UGA,UGG,UUA,UUG\}.
\end{aligned}$$
The recorded $f_{3}$ boundary is empty, so the finite combinatorial row does not by itself provide a missing-boundary signal. It supplies a closed coordinate surface on which later organism rows may be evaluated.

\paragraph{Axis-strength readout.}
The powered axis-spectrum run computed $11$ organisms out of $16$ requested organisms, using $5$ folds, at least $500$ proteins per organism, and $41$ synonymous design columns. Within this finite panel the captured spectra are led on average by $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$, with $d_{\mathrm{tRNA}}$ next. The per-organism dominant-axis count is concentrated entirely on $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$:
$$\begin{aligned}
\#\{\text{organisms dominated by }d_{\mathrm{resid4}}\}=11.
\end{aligned}$$
This is a panel-level strength statement, not a universal law. The domain means are explicitly descriptive because the panel is imbalanced, with $n_{\mathrm{archaea}}=1$, $n_{\mathrm{bacteria}}=2$, and $n_{\mathrm{eukaryote}}=8$. The largest recorded GC3 rank effect in the spectrum is small, $\rho=0.136$ for $d_{\mathrm{usage}}$, so the finite row does not reduce to a GC3 ranking effect.

\paragraph{Nearby finite constraints.}
Several linked rows delimit how the spectrum should be read. A boundary-set run over $40$ computed organisms out of $46$ requested organisms, with $6$ skipped organisms, $1000$ permutations, and $\alpha=0.05$, reported that $R$ sense codons are systematically tRNA-poor under GtRNAdb tGCN combined with dos Reis wobble weights. A separate residual-optimality run identified the non-tRNA residual optimal direction as $f_{3}$ translation selection across $11$ computed organisms out of $16$ requested organisms, again with $5$ folds, a $500$-protein minimum, and $41$ synonymous design columns. The third-axis identity row aligned $d_{\perp}$ to usage frequency within family with mean cosine $0.128$ and one-sided sign-test $p=0.000488$; the corresponding mechanism-discrimination row reported that the GC-orthogonalized $d_{\perp}$ retained significant cross-species alignment and held-out $R^{2}$, while CSC or positional identity still needed an outside row. The three-axis sufficiency run concluded that a fourth component remains present. The fourth-component screens were not all positive: mRNA half-life was not the dominant fourth-axis identity in the yeast assay, codon-pair structure was reported as not the fourth component, translation accuracy was reported as not the fourth component, and elongation dwell gave only a partial yeast result.

\paragraph{Controlled survival target.}
The only admissible readback for this chapter is the controlled survival entry from the $f_{3}$-stress coordinate to a translation readout such as tAI, stAI, ribosome occupancy, or translation efficiency. In local notation the desired bounded statistic has the form
$$\begin{aligned}
S_{f_{3}\text{-stress},T_{j}}>0
\end{aligned}$$
after amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, CDS length, $M$-density, matched mRNA abundance, rank, and simpler-baseline controls have been applied. A modeled tAI or stAI row may serve as a bounded codon-adaptation surface; it is not the same thing as observed ribosome occupancy. A ribosome-profiling or translation-efficiency row may test the same interface only after same-organism and same-CDS codon, wobble-weight, readout, and mRNA-control rows have been joined.

\paragraph{Falsification rule.}
The conjecture is broken, for this chapter, if the controlled $f_{3}$-stress survival entries remain non-positive across a rank-powered panel, or if the fitted surface is rank-degenerate in the sense that an all-$1.0$ low-rank matrix is the only apparent survival. Such a null result would not show that $B^{*}_{Q6}$ is biologically irrelevant. It would only say that this particular $f_{3}$-stress coordinate has not crossed the translation-readout interface under the declared controls.

\paragraph{Local no-promotion boundary.}
Nothing in the finite axis spectrum, the $f_{3}$ saturation row, the tRNA-poverty row, or the modeled adaptation-weight contact licenses a claim about translation realization beyond the explicitly bounded survival statistic. No protein abundance, folded structure, physical admissibility, molecular function, localization, stability, phenotype, general mechanism, or universality follows from this row. Each such assertion would require its own layer-matched contact and its own controlled statistic after the translation row has first become non-vacuous and positive.
