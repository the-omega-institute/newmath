\subsection{Per-organism strength spectrum for the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual surface}
\label{sec:biorn-axis-strength-spectrum-b-star-q6-per-organism}

\paragraph{Finite residual surface.}
The finite object studied here is the CDS-by-organism residual coordinate surface obtained after amino-acid projection, restricted to the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ family of synonymous coordinates and then summarized per organism.  The specific biological question is whether the $f_3$-stress direction remains visible after ordinary compositional explanations have been removed, and whether that visibility is compatible with a translation-layer readout rather than merely with codon-table geometry.  The powered spectrum run used $5$ folds, required at least $500$ proteins per organism, computed $11$ organisms out of $16$ requested organisms, and used $41$ synonymous-design columns.  Its deterministic seed was recorded as $\mathrm{sha256{:}b\_star\_q6\_axis\_strength\_spectrum\_powered{:}deterministic}$, and the run status was passed.

\paragraph{The $f_3$ saturation boundary.}
The underlying $f_3$ finite row check fixes the ambient codon set before any cross-layer reading is attempted.  The row named $M = \operatorname{Med}(R) \cup \{\mathrm{ACA}\}$ contains
$$
\begin{aligned}
M = \{&\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{ACA},\mathrm{ACG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\\
&\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The saturated $f_3$ row removes the two $AC$ third-position partners from this list and gives
$$
\begin{aligned}
\operatorname{Sat}_{f_3}(R) = \{&\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\\
&\mathrm{CUU},\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The recorded $f_3$ boundary row is empty.  Thus the local finite fact is not that every third-position contrast is biologically active; it is that the coordinate construction has no remaining unsaturated $f_3$ boundary inside the checked finite row.  Any later biological use of this coordinate must still pass through organism-level readout rows.

\paragraph{Observed axis spectrum.}
The powered axis-strength experiment reports that the captured spectra are led on average by $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$, with $d_{\mathrm{tRNA}}$ next.  The per-organism dominant-count table is sharp in this finite panel: all $11$ computed organisms have $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$ as the dominant captured axis.  The recorded domain counts are imbalanced, with $1$ archaeal organism, $2$ bacterial organisms, and $8$ eukaryotic organisms.  For that reason the domain means are descriptive summaries only, not domain-level law claims.  The largest recorded GC3 rank effect is on $d_{\mathrm{usage}}$, with Spearman rank effect $\rho = 0.136$.  The spectrum therefore supports a finite per-organism ordering of residual coordinates, but it does not by itself identify a universal biological mechanism.

\paragraph{Relation to the $f_3$ translation-selection row.}
The companion powered residual-axis result names $f_3\_\mathrm{translation\_selection}$ as the dominant non-tRNA residual optimality direction.  In the same family of finite checks, the two-axis closure test reports that a third axis is still present after the tRNA and $f_3$ span is used: $d_{\perp}$ has systematically positive held-out $R^2$ and same-signed directions across the computed organisms.  The third-axis identity run provisionally aligns $d_{\perp}$ with usage frequency within synonymous family, with mean cosine $0.128$ and one-sided sign-test $p = 0.000488$.  The mechanism run further records that the GC-orthogonalized $d_{\perp}$ keeps significant cross-species alignment and held-out $R^2$, leading to the finite conclusion $\mathrm{selection\text{-}not\text{-}GC}$, while also recording that the CSC or positional identity needs an additional source.  These scalar rows explain why the per-organism spectrum is read as an axis-strength spectrum rather than as a one-coordinate proof of translation dynamics.

\paragraph{Wobble and tRNA asymmetry as the admissible trigger.}
The biological trigger for the $f_3$-stress coordinate is not the formal codon involution alone.  A dynamical interpretation requires an organism or context in which the tRNA or wobble weight assigned to a codon $c$ differs from the weight assigned to its third-position partner $c^{f_3}$, so that $w_c \neq w_{c^{f_3}}$.  The powered boundary-set tRNA-poverty run supplies one relevant mechanism row: across $40$ computed organisms out of $46$ requested organisms, using $1000$ permutations at $\alpha = 0.05$, the $R$ sense codons are systematically tRNA-poor under GtRNAdb tGCN plus dos Reis wobble weights.  This supports a tRNA-supply route for $R$ avoidance, but it remains a route through codon-adaptation and tRNA-copy surfaces.  It is not, by itself, a measured ribosome-occupancy row or a protein-output row.

\paragraph{Fourth-axis exclusions and residual caution.}
The finite panel also prevents over-reading the fourth residual direction.  The three-axis sufficiency run reports $\mathrm{fourth\_mechanism\_present}$ on $11$ computed organisms out of $16$ requested organisms, with the same $5$ folds, $500$-protein minimum, and $41$ synonymous-design columns.  The tested fourth-mechanism identities then narrow what may be said: the mRNA-stability run concludes $\mathrm{fourth\_axis\_not\_mrna\_stability}$ because the yeast $d_{\mathrm{opt\_halflife}}$ has low alignment with abundance $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$ under the fixed gates; the codon-pair run concludes $\mathrm{not\_codon\_pair}$ using a $50$-shuffle null; the translation-accuracy run concludes $\mathrm{not\_accuracy}$ with $200$ permutations over $11$ computed organisms out of $16$ requested organisms; and the elongation-dwell run in \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae} records only a partial conclusion.  Hence the dominant $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$ spectrum is a real finite-row signal in this assay, but the mechanism label is not closed by these rows.

\paragraph{Controlled readout condition.}
Let $Q_{f_3}$ denote the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ $f_3$-stress residual coordinate after amino-acid quotienting, and let $T_j$ be a translation-layer readout such as tAI, stAI, ribosome occupancy, or translation efficiency.  The bounded survival quantity for this chapter is a controlled entry
$$
\begin{aligned}
S_{f_3,T_j} = \Delta \operatorname{Fit}(T_j \mid Q_{f_3}, Z) - \Delta \operatorname{Fit}(T_j \mid Z),
\end{aligned}
$$
where $Z$ contains at least amino-acid composition, organism identity, GC3, CDS length, $M$-density, matched mRNA control when a TE row is used, rank checks, and simpler codon baselines.  A positive biological readout requires a non-vacuous finite matrix with $S_{f_3,T_j} > 0$ after those controls.  A rank-saturated all-$1.0$ surface over only three organisms is not counted as survival, because it cannot distinguish a genuine coordinate effect from design degeneracy.

\paragraph{Local conclusion.}
The current finite evidence is a passed axis-strength spectrum, not a completed measured translation-survival matrix.  The passed part says that, in the computed $11$-organism panel, $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$ leads the captured spectra for every computed organism, $d_{\mathrm{tRNA}}$ is next on average, the largest recorded GC3 rank effect is small and attached to $d_{\mathrm{usage}}$ with $\rho = 0.136$, and the related residual-axis rows keep a non-tRNA $f_3$ translation-selection direction in view.  The open part is the same-row readout matrix: organism- and CDS-matched codon counts, wobble or tRNA weights, translation readouts, and matched mRNA controls must be joined before $S_{f_3,T_j}$ can be reported as positive.  No claim about translation realization, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, downstream mechanism, phenotype, or universality follows from this chapter unless a separate layer-specific contact supplies the corresponding finite row and statistic.
