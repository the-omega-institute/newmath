\paragraph{Finite-row object.}
This record concerns a finite coding-sequence by organism residual surface after amino-acid quotienting, restricted to the $f_3$-stress coordinate of the $B^*_{Q6}$ codon surface and to translation-readout rows that remain explicitly separated from downstream biological claims. The row object is not a protein-level assertion. It is a controlled comparison of synonymous residual coordinates against organism-scoped codon usage, tRNA or wobble-weight summaries, and bounded translation-readout candidates. The current finite computation is rank-powered rather than universal: $16$ organisms were requested, $11$ organisms were computed, each retained organism supplied at least $500$ proteins, the synonymous design used $41$ columns, and the held-out procedure used $5$ folds. The deterministic seed was recorded as $\mathrm{sha256{:}b\_star\_q6\_axis\_strength\_spectrum\_powered{:}deterministic}$, and the run status was passed.

\paragraph{Observed axis spectrum.}
On the computed $11$-organism panel, the captured spectra were led on average by $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$, followed by $d_{\mathrm{tRNA}}$. The per-organism dominant-axis count was concentrated entirely at $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$: all $11$ computed organisms selected that axis as dominant. Domain-level summaries were explicitly descriptive because the panel was imbalanced, with $n=1$ archaea, $n=2$ bacteria, and $n=8$ eukaryotes. The largest recorded GC3 rank effect was attached to $d_{\mathrm{usage}}$, with $\rho=0.136$. These finite numbers support an axis-strength spectrum statement only: in this panel, the residual fourth axis is the strongest captured direction, while tRNA-linked structure remains the next leading coordinate family.

\paragraph{Relation to the surrounding $Q6$ finite rows.}
The spectrum is consistent with the broader finite-row pattern. The tRNA-poverty boundary test for the $R$ sense codons ran on $40$ computed organisms out of $46$ requested organisms, skipped $6$, used $1000$ permutations at $\alpha=0.05$, and passed with the call that $R$ sense codons are systematically tRNA-poor under GtRNAdb tGCN plus dos Reis wobble weights. The two-axis closure row computed $11$ of $16$ organisms with $5$ folds and found a third axis present, because the perpendicular direction had systematically positive held-out $R^2$ and mutually same-signed directions across organisms. The third-axis identity row identified $d_{\perp}$ provisionally with usage frequency within family, with mean cosine $0.128$ and one-sided sign-test $p=0.000488$. The mechanism row then kept that direction GC-orthogonalized, calling it selection-not-GC, not wobble-clustered, and still needing external CSC or positional identity. These related rows make the present axis-spectrum result sharper: $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$ dominates the captured organism-wise spectrum, but its biological reading remains bounded by the translation-row gate.

\paragraph{Fourth-axis discrimination.}
The same finite memory separates $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$ from several tempting downstream interpretations. The mRNA-stability powered test passed with the conclusion that the fourth axis is not mRNA stability in the yeast half-life assay, because $d_{\mathrm{opt\_halflife}}$ had low alignment with abundance $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$ under the fixed gates. The codon-pair powered test passed with the conclusion $\mathrm{not\_codon\_pair}$ after $50$ shuffle-null draws. The translation-accuracy powered test passed with the conclusion $\mathrm{not\_accuracy}$ on the same $11$ computed out of $16$ requested organisms and $41$ synonymous design columns. The elongation-dwell row was narrower: it was run for \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae} and returned a partial conclusion. Thus the present spectrum should not be flattened into a single named mechanism. It is a finite strength ordering over residual directions, with several downstream identities excluded or left bounded rather than promoted.

\paragraph{Break condition and support condition.}
The local support condition is a controlled survival entry $S_{f_3\text{-stress},T_j}>0$ for a translation readout such as tAI, stAI, ribosome occupancy, or translation efficiency, computed only after amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, CDS length, $M$-density, matched mRNA control where applicable, rank, and simpler-baseline controls. In symbolic form the allowed readout is only
$$
\begin{aligned}
S_{f_3\text{-stress},T_j} &> 0
\quad\text{under the declared controls,}
\\
S_{f_3\text{-stress},T_j} &\leq 0
\quad\text{or rank degeneracy across the powered panel breaks the translation-readout claim.}
\end{aligned}
$$
The three-organism all-$1.0$ controlled matrix is therefore a null boundary rather than survival evidence. Likewise, codon topology and modeled tAI coupling do not by themselves create directionality; the $f_3$ coordinate becomes a biological test object only where context tRNA or wobble weights break the $f_3$ symmetry and where a matched readout row survives the controls.

\paragraph{Local no-promotion rule.}
This chapter reports a finite axis-strength spectrum and its controlled translation-readout boundary. It does not assert observed ribosome dynamics from tAI or stAI weights alone. It does not assert protein abundance, folded structure, physical admissibility, localization, molecular function, phenotype, mechanism, causation, adaptation, or universality. Any such statement would require its own layer-matched contact and its own finite statistic after the translation row is already non-vacuous and positive. In the present row, $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$ is the dominant captured axis across $11$ computed organisms, $d_{\mathrm{tRNA}}$ is next in the average spectrum, and $d_{\mathrm{usage}}$ carries the largest recorded GC3 rank effect at $\rho=0.136$; those are the bounded conclusions.
