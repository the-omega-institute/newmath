\paragraph{Powered spectrum readout.}
The rank-powered assay named \texttt{b\_star\_q6\_axis\_strength\_spectrum\_powered} passed on $11$ computed organisms out of $16$ requested organisms, using $5$ folds, at least $500$ proteins per organism, and $41$ synonymous-design columns.  The deterministic seed was \texttt{sha256:b\_star\_q6\_axis\_strength\_spectrum\_powered:deterministic}.  The finite conclusion is spectral rather than universal: the captured spectra are led on average by $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$, followed by $d_{\mathrm{tRNA}}$; every computed organism in the dominance count has $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$ as the leading axis, giving the count $\{d_{\mathrm{resid4}}:11\}$.  Domain means are only descriptive because the panel is imbalanced, with $n_{\mathrm{archaea}}=1$, $n_{\mathrm{bacteria}}=2$, and $n_{\mathrm{eukaryote}}=8$.  The largest reported GC3 rank effect is the usage axis, $d_{\mathrm{usage}}$, with $\rho=0.136$.

\paragraph{Relation to the $f_3$ translation row.}
The biological question is not whether the codon coordinate exists; the finite codon calculation already supplies that coordinate.  The question is whether the $f_3$-stress residual survives in a translation readout after the declared controls.  The admissible readback is therefore a controlled entry $S_{f3\text{-}stress,T_j}$ for $T_j$ equal to a bounded tAI or stAI surface, or to an organism-matched ribosome-occupancy or translation-efficiency row when such rows are actually joined to the coding-sequence table.  The row is positive only if it remains non-vacuous after amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, CDS length, $M$-density, matched mRNA abundance, rank, and simpler-baseline controls.  A three-organism all-$1.0$ fitted matrix is not counted as survival; it is a saturated-rank boundary.

\paragraph{Axis context supplied by adjacent finite assays.}
The axis-strength result sits inside a larger finite panel.  The residual optimality assay reported that the non-tRNA residual optimum aligns most with $f_3$ translation selection, while the third-axis identity assay provisionally aligned $d_\perp$ to within-family usage frequency with mean cosine $0.128$ and one-sided sign-test $p=0.000488$.  The corresponding mechanism assay kept $d_\perp$ significant after GC orthogonalization and described the result as selection-not-GC and not wobble-clustered, while leaving the CSC or positional identity as requiring an outside row.  A separate sufficiency assay concluded that a fourth mechanism is present.  The fourth-axis checks then ruled out several tempting promotions: mRNA stability was not the dominant fourth-axis identity in the yeast half-life assay; codon pair was reported as \texttt{not\_codon\_pair}; translation accuracy was reported as \texttt{not\_accuracy}; elongation dwell gave only a \texttt{partial} yeast result.  These rows sharpen the spectrum but do not turn the $f_3$ coordinate into a protein, structure, function, or phenotype claim.

\paragraph{Local falsification rule.}
The conjecture is supported only at the bounded translation interface if at least one same-organism or same-CDS entry $S_{f3\text{-}stress,T_j}$ is strictly positive under the full control set and is not a rank artifact.  It is falsified for this interface if the controlled $f_3$-stress row stays non-positive, rank-degenerate, or unjoined across a powered panel.  Context tRNA or wobble weights may break the $f_3$ symmetry and thereby make the coordinate testable, but modeled tAI or stAI weights alone are not observed ribosome occupancy.  Codon usage tables, tRNA-copy summaries, and adaptation weights are bounded contacts; they do not by themselves establish translation realization, folded structure, physical admissibility, molecular function, mechanism, phenotype, or universality.  Any such stronger layer requires its own matched measurement and its own controlled statistic.
