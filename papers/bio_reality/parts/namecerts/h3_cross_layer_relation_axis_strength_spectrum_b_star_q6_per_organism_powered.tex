\subsection{A rank-powered axis spectrum for the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ $f_3$ stress coordinate}
\label{sec:bioreality-q6-f3-axis-strength-spectrum}

\paragraph{Finite surface.}
The record concerns a finite CDS-by-organism residual coordinate surface restricted to the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ third-position stress coordinate and to translation-layer readouts that can be matched back to the same organism or the same coding sequence.  The coordinate is not a raw codon-frequency column.  It is formed after amino-acid projection, so the visible distinction is the residual imbalance between synonymous codons paired by the $f_3$ operation.  The finite saturation check fixes the relevant boundary explicitly: the row set $M = \operatorname{Med}(R) \cup \{\mathrm{ACA}\}$ is
$$
\begin{aligned}
M = \{&\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{ACA},\mathrm{ACG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\\
&\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The corresponding $f_3$ saturation of $R$ removes the two boundary codons $\mathrm{ACA}$ and $\mathrm{ACG}$ and gives
$$
\begin{aligned}
\operatorname{Sat}_{f_3}(R)=\{&\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\\
&\mathrm{CUU},\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\},
\end{aligned}
$$
with an empty $f_3$ boundary set.  This makes the present chapter a finite stress-spectrum record rather than a free biological interpretation of codon usage.

\paragraph{Axis spectrum result.}
The powered spectrum run computed $11$ organisms out of $16$ requested organisms, with at least $500$ proteins per organism, $5$ folds, and $41$ synonymous-design columns.  Within that finite panel, the captured spectra were led on average by $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$, followed by $d_{\mathrm{tRNA}}$.  The per-organism dominance count was concentrated entirely on $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$: $11$ of $11$ computed organisms had $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$ as the dominant captured axis.  Domain means were retained only as descriptive summaries because the computed panel was imbalanced, with
$$
\begin{aligned}
n_{\mathrm{archaea}}=1,\qquad n_{\mathrm{bacteria}}=2,\qquad n_{\mathrm{eukaryote}}=8.
\end{aligned}
$$
The largest reported GC3 rank effect in this run was the $d_{\mathrm{usage}}$ association, with $\rho=0.136$.  Thus the row-level conclusion is not that GC3 explains the axis, but that GC3 remains a small ranked covariate that has to stay in the control set before any translation-readout survival claim is made.

\paragraph{Mechanistic pressure from tRNA supply.}
The separate $R$-window tRNA-poverty run gives the strongest local pressure on the biological interpretation of the $f_3$ stress coordinate.  It computed $40$ organisms out of $46$ requested organisms, skipped $6$, used $1000$ permutations at $\alpha=0.05$, and called the $R$ sense codons systematically tRNA-poor under GtRNAdb tRNA gene-copy numbers with dos Reis wobble weights.  This supports a tRNA-supply pressure for $R$ avoidance, but it still remains a bounded codon-to-translation contact.  The $f_3$ coordinate becomes a candidate translation distinction only where the context weights break the pairing symmetry, namely where $w_c \ne w_{c^{f_3}}$.  If the paired codons receive symmetric tRNA or wobble weights, the residual coordinate has no licensed route from topology to a directional translation readout.

\paragraph{Relations to neighboring axes.}
The powered residual program gives a coherent but deliberately bounded spectrum.  The universal-core run computed $11$ of $16$ organisms with $5$ folds, at least $500$ proteins per organism, and $41$ synonymous-design columns, and identified the core optimal direction with tRNA adaptation.  The residual optimality run on the same computed scale reported that the non-tRNA residual optimality aligned most with $f_3$ translation selection.  The two-axis closure run concluded that a third axis is present because $d_{\perp}$ retained systematically positive held-out $R^2$ and mutually same-signed directions across organisms.  The identity run then provisionally identified $d_{\perp}$ with usage frequency within family, with mean cosine $0.128$ and one-sided sign-test $p=0.000488$.  A mechanism run classified the same third-axis signal as selection-not-GC, not wobble-clustered, with CSC or positional identity still requiring a separate contact.  These finite classifications make the $f_3$ stress spectrum plausible as a rank-powered residual axis, but they do not by themselves measure ribosome occupancy or translation efficiency.

\paragraph{Fourth-axis exclusions.}
The finite screen also prevents over-reading the fourth residual component.  The three-axis sufficiency run concluded that a fourth mechanism is present on $11$ computed organisms out of $16$, again with $5$ folds, at least $500$ proteins per organism, and $41$ synonymous-design columns.  But the tested identities were sharply constrained: the mRNA-stability run concluded that the fourth axis is not dominated by mRNA stability in the yeast half-life assay, the codon-pair run concluded $\mathrm{not\_codon\_pair}$ using $50$ shuffle-null replicates, the translation-accuracy run concluded $\mathrm{not\_accuracy}$ using $200$ permutations, and the elongation-dwell run in \textit{Saccharomyces cerevisiae} was only partial.  Therefore the chapter records a spectrum of residual pressures, not a completed mechanistic assignment of every residual component.

\paragraph{Translation-readout gate.}
The finite claim is the following bounded statement.  Let $Q_{f3}$ denote the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ $f_3$ stress residual coordinate after amino-acid projection, and let $T_j$ range over translation-layer rows such as tAI, stAI, ribosome occupancy, or translation efficiency.  A positive readout is licensed only by a controlled survival entry
$$
\begin{aligned}
S_{f3,T_j} > 0
\end{aligned}
$$
after amino-acid composition, organism identity, GC3, CDS length, $M$-density baseline, matched mRNA abundance where a TE row is used, rank sufficiency, and simpler codon-baseline ablation.  A modeled tAI or stAI row is a bounded translation-weight readback, not an observed ribosome-footprint row.  A rank-degenerate all-$1.0$ matrix on a three-organism surface is a null boundary rather than evidence for survival.  The present finite evidence therefore supports the axis-spectrum and tRNA-pressure motivation for testing $S_{f3,T_j}$, but it does not yet supply the same-row measured translation matrix required for a positive survival claim.

\paragraph{Local non-promotion rule.}
No downstream biological layer follows from the axis spectrum alone.  The chapter does not infer protein abundance, folded structure, localization, stability, molecular function, organismal phenotype, physical admissibility, mechanism, causation, or universality from the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual geometry, from codon usage, from tRNA-copy summaries, from modeled tAI or stAI weights, or from the dominance of $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$.  Any such assertion would require its own layer-matched measurement and its own controlled statistic.  The only ready conclusion is narrower: in a powered finite panel, the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual spectrum is led by $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$ across all $11$ computed organisms, the $R$ sense window shows tRNA-poverty pressure in $40$ computed organisms, and the $f_3$ stress coordinate is a justified candidate for a future controlled translation-readout survival test exactly where wobble or tRNA weights break the $f_3$ pairing symmetry.
