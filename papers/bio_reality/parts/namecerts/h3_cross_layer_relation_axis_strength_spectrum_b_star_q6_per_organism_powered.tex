\subsection{Rank-powered per-organism strength spectrum for the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ translation boundary}
\label{sec:bioreality-q6-axis-strength-spectrum-per-organism}

\paragraph{Finite carrier.}
The finite surface used here is a CDS-by-organism residual-coordinate table restricted to the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ synonymous design after amino-acid projection.  Each row is allowed to contribute only through codon-count, tRNA or wobble-weight, and translation-readout coordinates that are explicitly present in the finite table.  The row universe is not a statement about protein function, structure, localization, stability, phenotype, or evolutionary optimality.  It is a bounded coordinate assay: the admissible biological question is whether a named synonymous residual direction leaves a controlled trace in a translation-layer readout.

The $f_3$ saturation check fixes the relevant third-position boundary before any readout is interpreted.  The mediated set $M=\operatorname{Med}(R)\cup\{\mathrm{ACA}\}$ contains the following $20$ codons:
$$
\begin{aligned}
M=\{&\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{ACA},\mathrm{ACG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\\
&\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
After applying $f_3$ saturation, the surviving set removes the two ACA/ACG boundary codons and leaves $18$ codons:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\operatorname{Sat}_{f_3}(R)=\{&\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\\
&\mathrm{CUU},\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The recorded $f_3$ boundary is empty, so the assay treats the $f_3$ residual coordinate as a stress coordinate whose biological reading must come from a separate translation row, not from the codon geometry alone.

\paragraph{Powered spectrum.}
The powered spectrum computation used a deterministic seed, $5$ folds, a minimum of $500$ proteins per organism, and $41$ synonymous-design columns.  It requested $16$ organisms and computed $11$ organism rows.  Within those rows, the captured spectra were led by $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$ on average, followed by $d_{\mathrm{tRNA}}$.  The per-organism dominant count was concentrated entirely on $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$ among the computed organisms:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\#\{\text{organisms dominated by }d_{\mathrm{resid4}}\}=11.
\end{aligned}
$$
The domain partition was imbalanced, with one archaeal, two bacterial, and eight eukaryotic rows, so domain means are descriptive only and are not used as a taxonomic law.  The largest reported GC3 rank effect was assigned to $d_{\mathrm{usage}}$ with $\rho=0.136$, which is small enough that the spectrum cannot be collapsed into a GC3 explanation.  The finite result is therefore a rank-powered axis-strength profile, not a universal direction theorem.

\paragraph{Translation boundary.}
The intended readback for the $f_3$-stress coordinate is a controlled survival entry $S_{f_3\text{-stress},T_j}$ where $T_j$ is a bounded translation readout such as tAI, stAI, ribosome occupancy, or translation efficiency.  The admissible support condition is local and statistical: after amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, CDS length, $M$-density, matched mRNA, rank, and simpler-baseline controls, at least one declared translation row must remain non-vacuous and positive.  In schematic form, the only positive translation statement allowed by this packet has the form
$$
\begin{aligned}
S_{f_3\text{-stress},T_j}>0
\quad\text{after the declared finite-row controls.}
\end{aligned}
$$
The available powered spectrum does not by itself supply that measured survival entry.  It says that the finite residual axes have a reproducible per-organism strength ordering in the computed panel.  It does not say that the $f_3$ coordinate has already been observed as ribosome occupancy, translation efficiency, or protein output.

\paragraph{Mechanistic pressure.}
The surrounding finite facts constrain how a later positive translation row would have to be interpreted.  The tRNA-poverty computation for the $R$ sense codons was performed on $40$ computed organisms out of $46$ requested organisms, skipped $6$, used $1000$ permutations at $\alpha=0.05$, and passed with the conclusion that $R$ sense codons are systematically tRNA-poor under GtRNAdb tGCN plus dos Reis wobble weights.  The residual-optimality computation on the same $11$-organism powered panel identified the non-tRNA residual optimality direction most strongly with $f_3$ translation selection.  The third-axis identity run reported alignment to usage frequency within family, with mean cosine $0.128$ and one-sided sign-test $p=0.000488$.  The third-axis mechanism run retained GC-orthogonalized cross-species alignment and held-out $R^2$, while reporting the mechanism summary as selection-not-GC, not wobble-clustered, and needing an external CSC or positional identity contact.  The three-axis sufficiency run concluded that a fourth mechanism is present.  Together these facts give a finite spectrum with mechanistic pressure, but not a promoted biological conclusion.

\paragraph{Local no-promotion rule.}
This chapter permits only the following bounded statement: in the powered finite panel, the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ axis spectrum is dominated by $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$ across the $11$ computed organisms, with $d_{\mathrm{tRNA}}$ next on average, a small largest GC3 rank effect for $d_{\mathrm{usage}}$, and an empty $f_3$ boundary after the recorded saturation.  No translation realization follows from these facts unless a separate organism-matched translation contact supplies the controlled $S_{f_3\text{-stress},T_j}$ row.  No folding, physical admissibility, protein function, localization, stability, phenotype, mechanism, causation, or universal biological law follows from the spectrum.  Those layers require their own finite contacts and their own controlled statistics.
