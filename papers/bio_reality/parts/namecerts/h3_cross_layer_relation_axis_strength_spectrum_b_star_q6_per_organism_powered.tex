\paragraph{Finite coordinate surface.}
The record concerns a finite coding-sequence by organism residual coordinate surface, restricted to the $B^\ast_{Q6}$ coordinate family and, inside that family, to the third-position $f_3$-stress contrast after amino-acid projection. The finite saturation row gives
$$\begin{aligned}
M = {} & \{\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{ACA},\mathrm{ACG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\\
& \mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\},
\end{aligned}$$
while the $f_3$-saturated $R$ row is
$$\begin{aligned}
\operatorname{Sat}_{f_3}(R) = {} & \{\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\\
& \mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\}.
\end{aligned}$$
Thus the recorded $M$ row has $20$ codons, the saturated $R$ row has $18$ codons, and the displayed difference is the pair $\{\mathrm{ACA},\mathrm{ACG}\}$. The finite $f_3$ boundary row is empty, so this chapter does not treat a nonzero boundary residue as evidence for a biological outcome. Its role is narrower: it isolates which synonymous residual coordinate is allowed to be compared with translation-layer rows.

\paragraph{Axis spectrum.}
The powered spectrum run computed $11$ organisms out of $16$ requested organisms, using $5$ folds, a minimum of $500$ proteins per organism, and $41$ synonymous-design columns. Within the captured panel, the dominant per-organism axis count was
$$\begin{aligned}
\#\{\text{organisms dominated by }d_{\mathrm{resid4}}\}=11.
\end{aligned}$$
The average ordering was led by $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$, followed by $d_{\mathrm{tRNA}}$. The domain summaries are only descriptive because the computed panel is imbalanced, with
$$\begin{aligned}
n_{\mathrm{archaea}}=1,\qquad n_{\mathrm{bacteria}}=2,\qquad n_{\mathrm{eukaryote}}=8.
\end{aligned}$$
The largest recorded GC3 rank effect in this spectrum was on $d_{\mathrm{usage}}$, with $\rho=0.136$. This is a small rank association inside the finite panel, not a free-standing explanation of the axis. The spectrum therefore supports a restricted empirical statement: among the organisms that passed the data gates, the fourth residual direction was repeatedly the strongest captured axis, and the tRNA direction was the next average contributor.

\paragraph{Relation to nearby finite checks.}
The same finite memory places this spectrum beside several bounded checks. The boundary-set tRNA-poverty run computed $40$ organisms out of $46$ requested, skipped $6$, used $1000$ permutations at $\alpha=0.05$, and recorded that the $R$ sense codons are systematically tRNA-poor under tRNA gene-copy plus wobble-weight scoring. The third-axis identity run computed $11$ organisms out of $16$, again with $5$ folds, at least $500$ proteins per organism, and $41$ synonymous-design columns; it recorded $d_\perp$ as aligned to usage frequency within family, with mean cosine $0.128$ and one-sided sign-test $p=0.000488$. A separate third-axis mechanism row classified the same $d_\perp$ signal as selection-not-GC, not-wobble-clustered, and still requiring an outside contact for its CSC or positional identity. The residual-optimality row named $f_3$ translation selection as the dominant non-tRNA residual axis, the two-axis closure row returned third-axis-present, and the three-axis sufficiency row returned fourth-mechanism-present. These rows make the axis spectrum sharper, but they do not license a stronger downstream biological reading.

\paragraph{Translation-readout gate.}
The intended readout is a controlled survival entry $S_{f_3\text{-stress},T_j}$ for a bounded translation-layer row such as tAI, stAI, ribosome occupancy, or translation efficiency. The permitted comparison is only meaningful after amino-acid composition, organism identity, GC3, coding-sequence length, $M$-density, mRNA abundance when available, rank, and simpler codon baselines have been accounted for. In symbolic form, the local support condition is
$$\begin{aligned}
S_{f_3\text{-stress},T_j} > 0
\end{aligned}$$
for at least one matched translation row $T_j$ in a rank-admissible panel. The local break condition is the opposite finite outcome: every controlled $S_{f_3\text{-stress},T_j}$ is nonpositive, or the readout matrix is rank-degenerate. In particular, an all-$1.0$ three-organism controlled surface is treated here as saturated rank geometry rather than survival.

\paragraph{Local cannot-claim boundary.}
This chapter does not infer protein abundance, folded structure, physical admissibility, localization, stability, molecular function, phenotype, causal mechanism, or universality from codon topology, residual geometry, tRNA-copy imbalance, modeled tAI or stAI coupling, or the axis-strength spectrum itself. A translation statement may be made only for the matched translation row after the controls above. Any claim above that row requires its own layer-matched contact and its own finite statistic. Consequently, the present result is best read as a finite axis-strength and routing record: $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$ leads the computed organism panel, $d_{\mathrm{tRNA}}$ follows on average, the $f_3$ coordinate has a concrete codon-level saturation surface, and the next admissible biological test is whether a nondegenerate controlled translation-readout survival entry exists.
