\subsection{Powered organism spectrum for the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual axes}
\label{subsec:bioreality-q6-axis-strength-spectrum-powered}

\paragraph{Finite coordinate surface.}
The record concerns a finite coding-sequence by organism residual coordinate surface, restricted to the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ synonymous layer after amino-acid projection. The operative coordinate is the $f_3$-stress row: it compares paired synonymous codons after quotienting by amino-acid identity, and it is allowed to become biologically readable only where codon-context weights, tRNA supply, or wobble weights distinguish a codon $c$ from its $f_3$ partner $c^{f_3}$. The finite saturation check gives the ambient set
$$
\begin{aligned}
M = \{&\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{ACA},\mathrm{ACG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\\
&\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The $f_3$-saturated subcollection removes the two ACA/ACG boundary items and leaves
$$
\begin{aligned}
\operatorname{Sat}_{f_3}(R)=\{&\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\\
&\mathrm{CUU},\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\},
\end{aligned}
$$
with empty recorded $f_3$ boundary. Thus the chapter starts from a closed finite codon coordinate rather than from a continuous or genome-wide asymptotic object.

\paragraph{Powered spectrum readout.}
The powered axis-strength computation used $11$ organisms out of $16$ requested organisms, five folds, a minimum threshold of $500$ proteins per organism, and $41$ synonymous design columns. On that finite panel, the captured spectra are led on average by $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$, followed by $d_{\mathrm{tRNA}}$. The per-organism dominance count is especially sharp: all $11$ computed organisms have $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$ as the dominant captured axis. The domain summary is deliberately descriptive, since the panel is imbalanced with
$$
\begin{aligned}
n_{\mathrm{archaea}}=1,\qquad n_{\mathrm{bacteria}}=2,\qquad n_{\mathrm{eukaryote}}=8.
\end{aligned}
$$
The largest recorded GC3 rank effect is only $\rho=0.136$ for $d_{\mathrm{usage}}$, so the spectrum should not be reduced to a GC3-only explanation. The associated residual-axis result identifies the non-tRNA residual optimum most strongly with $f_3$ translation selection, and the two-axis closure test reports that a third axis remains present after the tRNA and $f_3$ span is fitted.

\paragraph{Third and fourth axis diagnostics.}
The third-axis identity computation classifies $d_{\perp}$ as aligned to usage frequency within synonymous family, with mean cosine $0.128$ and one-sided sign-test $p=0.000488$ across the powered panel. Its mechanism check reports a selection-not-GC outcome: the GC-orthogonalized $d_{\perp}$ keeps significant cross-species alignment and held-out explanatory value, while the wobble-cluster and CSC/positional alternatives are not closed by the present finite rows. The three-axis sufficiency computation returns a fourth-mechanism-present call, so the measured spectrum is not exhausted by the first three named axes. The fourth-mechanism probes narrow the negative space: codon-pair structure is rejected as the identity in the tested assay, translation accuracy is rejected, and mRNA stability is not the dominant fourth-axis identity because the yeast $d_{\mathrm{opt\,halflife}}$ row has low alignment with abundance $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$ under the fixed gates. Elongation dwell gives only a partial yeast-specific signal. These outcomes support a finite spectrum statement, not a complete mechanism assignment.

\paragraph{Translation-row gate.}
The biological interpretation is intentionally narrower than the coordinate construction. The spectrum may be read as evidence that a rank-powered multi-organism panel contains a stable $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual axis, and that this axis is not explained away by amino-acid composition, synonymous design rank, or a simple GC3 rank trend in the recorded computation. It is not, by itself, a measured translation-efficiency survival row. A positive translation statement would require a non-vacuous controlled entry $S_{f3\text{-}\mathrm{stress},T_j}>0$ for a bounded readout such as tAI, stAI, ribosome occupancy, or translation efficiency, after amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, length, M-density, mRNA, rank, and simpler-baseline controls. The rank-degenerate all-$1.0$ three-organism surface is therefore a null boundary rather than survival evidence.

\paragraph{Local no-promotion rule.}
No conclusion about protein abundance, folded structure, physical admissibility, molecular function, localization, stability, phenotype, mechanism, or universality follows from this chapter alone. Codon usage tables, tRNA copy numbers, and modeled tAI or stAI weights can bound a translation-layer question, but they do not observe ribosome occupancy unless matched ribosome or translation-efficiency rows are joined to the same organisms or CDS identifiers. Matched mRNA abundance is only a control for separating transcription from translation. The valid conclusion is the finite, controlled axis-spectrum statement: in the computed $11$-organism, $41$-column, five-fold surface, $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$ dominates the captured spectra and the residual diagnostics place the $f_3$-stress coordinate on a boundary where translation-readout testing is meaningful but still conditional on a separate same-row readout statistic.
