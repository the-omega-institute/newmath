\subsection{Rank-powered axis spectrum for the $B^*_{Q6}$ residual surface}
\label{sec:bioreality-q6-axis-strength-spectrum}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite carrier.}
This record treats the $B^*_{Q6}$ coordinate system as a finite residual surface over coding-sequence rows grouped by organism.  The rows are restricted to synonymous codon contrasts after amino-acid projection, so the reported axes are not amino-acid composition effects.  The finite design used $41$ synonymous columns, $5$ held-out folds, and a minimum inclusion threshold of $500$ proteins per organism.  Of $16$ requested organisms, $11$ had enough joined codon and organism-level rows to enter the powered computation.  The seed was deterministic, recorded as $\mathrm{sha256{:}b\_star\_q6\_axis\_strength\_spectrum\_powered{:}deterministic}$, and the status of the finite computation was passed.

\paragraph{Axis-strength result.}
Across the $11$ computed organisms, the captured spectra were led by $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$ on average, followed by $d_{\mathrm{tRNA}}$.  The per-organism dominance count was concentrated entirely on $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$: all $11$ computed organisms had $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$ as the dominant captured axis.  Domain summaries are descriptive only, because the retained panel was imbalanced with $1$ archaeal, $2$ bacterial, and $8$ eukaryotic organisms.  The largest recorded GC3 rank effect was small, with $d_{\mathrm{usage}}$ having $\rho=0.136$.  In this bounded sense, the finite panel supports an axis-strength spectrum rather than a universal codon law: the strongest recovered residual direction is a fourth residual component, not a direct tRNA, GC3, or amino-acid-composition coordinate.

\paragraph{Mechanistic neighbors.}
Several companion computations constrain what the spectrum can mean.  The $R$ sense codons were classified as systematically tRNA-poor under GtRNAdb tRNA gene-copy numbers with dos Reis wobble weights, using $40$ computed organisms out of $46$ requested, $6$ skipped organisms, $1000$ permutations, and $\alpha=0.05$.  A separate residual optimality computation identified the non-tRNA residual direction most strongly with $f_3$ translation selection.  The third-axis identity computation provisionally aligned $d_{\perp}$ with usage frequency within family, with mean cosine $0.128$ and one-sided sign-test $p=0.000488$.  After GC orthogonalization, $d_{\perp}$ retained significant cross-species alignment and held-out $R^2$, while the mechanism label remained selection-not-GC and not-wobble-clustered.  These facts make the axis spectrum biologically pointed, but still finite-row and assay-scoped.

\paragraph{Fourth-axis exclusions.}
The powered three-axis sufficiency test concluded that a fourth mechanism was present.  The available fourth-mechanism probes narrow the interpretation without closing it.  The mRNA half-life alignment was low enough under the fixed gates to exclude mRNA stability as the dominant fourth-axis identity in that assay.  The codon-pair test returned $\mathrm{not\_codon\_pair}$ with $50$ shuffle-null samples.  The translation-accuracy test returned $\mathrm{not\_accuracy}$ on $11$ computed organisms out of $16$ requested with $200$ permutations.  The elongation-dwell probe in $\mathrm{Saccharomyces\ cerevisiae}$ returned a partial conclusion rather than a complete mechanism call.  Therefore the finite record supports a remaining residual axis, but it does not identify a single complete molecular mechanism.

\paragraph{Translation-readout boundary.}
The intended readout for the $f_3$-stress coordinate is a controlled entry $S_{f3\text{-}stress,T_j}$ for tAI, stAI, ribosome occupancy, or translation efficiency.  A positive entry would require organism-matched coding-sequence rows, wobble or tRNA-weight asymmetry with $w_c\neq w_{c^{f_3}}$, and controls for amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, length, $M$-density, and matched mRNA abundance.  The present powered axis-spectrum row does not contain a same-CDS ribosome or translation-efficiency matrix, and it does not contain a nondegenerate measured $S^{QT}$ statistic.  The three-organism all-$1.0$ surface, where present in the wider packet memory, is a rank-saturated null boundary and is not survival evidence.

\paragraph{Local no-promotion rule.}
This chapter licenses only the finite statement that the powered $B^*_{Q6}$ residual spectrum is dominated by $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$ in the retained $11$-organism panel, with bounded neighboring evidence for tRNA poverty, residual $f_3$ selection, and a fourth-axis remainder.  It does not assert translation realization, ribosome occupancy, protein abundance, folded structure, physical admissibility, biological function, phenotype, mechanism closure, or universality.  Any such statement would require a separate layer-matched contact and its own controlled statistic.  In particular, codon topology, modeled tAI or stAI weights, and residual-coordinate geometry cannot be promoted into protein, structure, function, or global biological conclusions without an explicit observed row at that layer.
