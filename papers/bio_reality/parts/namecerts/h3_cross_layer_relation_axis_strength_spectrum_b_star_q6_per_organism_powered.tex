\subsection{Rank-powered $f_3$ stress and translation-readout survival}\label{subsec:biorality-q6-f3-stress-axis-strength}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite coordinate surface.}
The finite object studied here is a coding-sequence residual surface indexed by organism, by synonymous codon coordinates after amino-acid projection, and by a distinguished $B^{*}_{Q6}$ coordinate measuring third-position $f_3$ stress.  The coordinate is not a direct biological observable.  It becomes empirically meaningful only after it is paired with an organism-matched translation readout and after amino-acid composition, organism identity, GC3, coding-sequence length, $M$-density, and mRNA abundance have been controlled.  The finite $f_3$ saturation row gives the codon-level boundary of the coordinate.  The computed $M=\operatorname{Med}(R)\cup\{\mathrm{ACA},\mathrm{ACG}\}$ row contains 20 codons:
$$
\begin{aligned}
M=\{&\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{ACA},\mathrm{ACG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\\
&\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
After $f_3$ saturation of $R$, the row has 18 codons and omits exactly $\mathrm{ACA}$ and $\mathrm{ACG}$:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\operatorname{Sat}_{f_3}(R)=\{&\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\\
&\mathrm{CUU},\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The recorded $f_3$ boundary is empty, so the finite codon-side test has no residual unsaturated boundary row.  This is a coordinate fact only; it does not assert translation, folding, function, or organismal effect.

\paragraph{Rank-powered axis spectrum.}
The powered axis-strength computation used 5 folds, required at least 500 proteins per organism, and retained 41 synonymous-design columns.  It computed 11 organisms out of 16 requested organisms.  Within that finite panel, the captured spectra were led on average by $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$, followed by $d_{\mathrm{tRNA}}$, and the per-organism dominant-axis count was $\{d_{\mathrm{resid4}}:11\}$.  The domain partition was imbalanced, with $n=1$ archaeal, $n=2$ bacterial, and $n=8$ eukaryotic organisms, so domain means are descriptive summaries rather than balanced-domain estimates.  The largest reported GC3 rank effect was on $d_{\mathrm{usage}}$, with $\rho=0.136$.  The coordinate therefore has a finite rank-powered spectrum, but the spectrum by itself is not a translation-survival statistic.

\paragraph{Mechanistic pressure already present in finite rows.}
Several linked finite rows constrain how the $f_3$ stress coordinate may be interpreted.  The $R$-sense boundary-set assay computed 40 organisms out of 46 requested organisms with 1000 permutations at $\alpha=0.05$ and reported that $R$ sense codons are systematically tRNA-poor under GtRNAdb tGCN with dos Reis wobble weights.  The residual optimal-axis row computed 11 of 16 requested organisms with 5 folds and identified the non-tRNA residual optimality direction as $f_3$ translation selection.  The two-axis closure row, also on 11 of 16 organisms with 5 folds, concluded that a third axis is present because $d_{\perp}$ has systematically positive held-out $R^2$ and mutually same-signed directions across organisms.  The third-axis identity row assigned $d_{\perp}$ provisionally to usage frequency within family, with mean cosine $0.128$ and one-sided sign-test $p=0.000488$.  The third-axis mechanism row kept the signal after GC orthogonalization and classified it as selection-not-GC, not wobble-clustered, with CSC or positional identity requiring further data.

\paragraph{Fourth-axis exclusions.}
The same finite family of assays prevents over-reading the spectrum.  The three-axis sufficiency row concluded that a fourth mechanism is present on 11 computed organisms out of 16 requested organisms, again with 5 folds, at least 500 proteins per organism, and 41 synonymous-design columns.  Candidate identities were then bounded.  The mRNA-stability assay concluded that the fourth axis is not mRNA stability in the fixed test, because yeast $d_{\mathrm{opt\_halflife}}$ had low alignment with abundance $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$.  The codon-pair assay concluded $\mathrm{not\_codon\_pair}$ under 50 shuffle nulls.  The translation-accuracy assay concluded $\mathrm{not\_accuracy}$ using 200 permutations on 11 computed organisms out of 16 requested organisms.  The elongation-dwell assay in \textit{Saccharomyces cerevisiae} returned a partial conclusion.  These rows support a narrow statement: the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual spectrum contains a persistent nontrivial direction, but the currently recorded finite data do not identify a complete downstream mechanism.

\paragraph{Translation-readout criterion.}
The bounded cross-layer claim is the following.  Let $Q_{f_3}$ denote the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ third-position stress coordinate after amino-acid quotienting, and let $T_j$ be a matched translation readout such as tAI, stAI, ribosome occupancy, or translation efficiency.  The admissible survival entry is a controlled statistic $S_{f_3,T_j}$ computed only after the declared covariates and simpler codon baselines have been applied.  The support condition is
$$
\begin{aligned}
S_{f_3,T_j}>0
\end{aligned}
$$
for at least one nondegenerate translation readout row in a rank-powered panel.  The failure condition is that every controlled $S_{f_3,T_j}$ remains non-positive, or that the apparent survival surface is rank-degenerate, for example an all-$1.0$ three-organism fitted matrix.  Wobble or tRNA-weight asymmetry is therefore a trigger for testing; it is not itself a measured translation outcome.

\paragraph{Local no-promotion rule.}
This chapter permits only a finite coordinate-and-readout statement.  It does not infer protein abundance, mature protein stability, folded structure, localization, molecular function, phenotype, physical admissibility, causation, mechanism, adaptation, or universality from codon topology, from modelled tAI or stAI weights, from the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual spectrum, or from review readiness of the packet.  A stronger biological conclusion would require its own layer-matched contact and its own controlled finite statistic.  In particular, mRNA abundance is only a control covariate for translation-efficiency residualization, and ribosome profiling becomes evidence for this packet only after same-organism and same-CDS codon, wobble-weight, translation-readout, and matched mRNA rows are joined and the declared $S_{f_3,T_j}$ statistic is computed.
