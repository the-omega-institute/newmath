\subsection{Per-organism strength spectrum for the $B^*_{Q6}$ $f_3$-stress axis}
\label{sec:biorality-q6-f3-stress-axis-strength-spectrum}

\paragraph{Finite coordinate surface.}
This record concerns the finite CDS-by-organism residual coordinate surface obtained after amino-acid projection, restricted to the $B^*_{Q6}$ coordinate family and, more narrowly, to the $f_3$-stress direction.  The finite codon support used here is not a free biological interpretation; it is a named residual surface on synonymous coding rows.  The saturated $f_3$ row is recorded by
$$
\begin{aligned}
M &= \{\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{ACA},\mathrm{ACG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\\
&\qquad \mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\},\\
\operatorname{Sat}_{f_3}(R) &= \{\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\\
&\qquad \mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\},\\
\partial_{f_3}R &= \varnothing.
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the finite row separates the two codons $\mathrm{ACA}$ and $\mathrm{ACG}$ from the $f_3$-saturated residual support, while the recorded $f_3$ boundary is empty.  This is the codon-coordinate fact that the chapter uses; it is not yet a statement about ribosome occupancy, protein output, or phenotype.

\paragraph{Spectrum statistic.}
The powered axis-strength computation used a deterministic seed, five folds, a minimum of $500$ proteins per organism, and $41$ synonymous-design columns.  It computed $11$ organisms out of $16$ requested organisms.  Within this computed panel the captured spectra were led on average by $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$, followed by $d_{\mathrm{tRNA}}$.  The per-organism dominant-axis count was
$$
\begin{aligned}
\#\{\hbox{organisms dominated by }d_{\mathrm{resid4}}\} &= 11,
\end{aligned}
$$
so every computed organism in the panel selected $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$ as the dominant captured axis.  The domain counts were imbalanced, with $1$ archaeal, $2$ bacterial, and $8$ eukaryotic organisms, so domain-mean comparisons are descriptive rather than a balanced-domain inference.  The largest recorded GC3 rank effect was small, $\rho=0.136$, and is treated as a covariate pressure rather than as a sufficient explanation of the spectrum.

\paragraph{Interpretation of the leading direction.}
The result says that the per-organism residual spectrum is not exhausted by the tRNA direction.  In the computed panel the strongest captured direction is $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$, while $d_{\mathrm{tRNA}}$ remains the next named direction.  This matters for the $f_3$-stress question because the coordinate becomes biologically readable only when the codon-pairing symmetry at third position is broken by an organism- or context-specific readout, such as tRNA availability, wobble weights, or a measured translation-efficiency row.  The finite spectrum therefore supports a bounded claim: after amino-acid quotienting and the declared baseline controls, the $B^*_{Q6}$ residual surface has a reproducible per-organism direction whose leading axis is not simply the named tRNA axis.

The surrounding finite facts sharpen that statement.  A related third-axis identity computation identified $d_\perp$ with usage frequency within synonymous family, with mean cosine $0.128$ and one-sided sign-test $p=0.000488$.  A related mechanism check reported that the GC-orthogonalized $d_\perp$ kept significant cross-species alignment and held-out $R^2$, yielding the finite classification ``selection-not-GC; not-wobble-clustered; CSC/positional identity needs external data.''  A further residual-axis computation classified the non-tRNA residual optimality direction as most aligned with $f_3$ translation selection, and the three-axis sufficiency check concluded that a fourth mechanism is present.  These rows make the spectrum a ranked residual-coordinate statement, not a downstream biological conclusion.

\paragraph{Readout gate.}
The admissible readback for this chapter is a controlled entry $S_{f_3\hbox{-}\mathrm{stress},T_j}$ where $T_j$ is a bounded translation-layer readout, for example tAI, stAI, ribosome occupancy, or translation efficiency.  The statistic must be evaluated after amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, CDS length, $M$-density, and matched mRNA controls when such controls are part of the readout.  A positive spectrum entry by itself is not enough.  The necessary form is
$$
\begin{aligned}
S_{f_3\hbox{-}\mathrm{stress},T_j} &> 0
\end{aligned}
$$
under the declared controls and under simpler-codon-baseline ablation.  Conversely, if the powered panel remains non-positive or rank-degenerate for every such $T_j$, the translation-readout interpretation of the $f_3$-stress row fails for this packet even though the residual coordinate itself remains a valid finite construction.

\paragraph{Local boundary.}
The chapter does not infer observed translation realization from codon topology alone, and it does not infer folding, physical admissibility, protein function, localization, stability, organismal phenotype, mechanism, or universality from the spectrum.  A modeled tAI or stAI weight may serve as a bounded codon-adaptation readout, but it is not the same thing as measured ribosome occupancy.  A ribosome-profiling or translation-efficiency row may test the translation layer only after same-organism or same-CDS joining and the declared controls.  Matched mRNA abundance is a control row for separating transcriptional and translational signal, not a translation observation by itself.  Protein abundance, localization, stability, structural order, physical admissibility, function, phenotype, and universal biological law each require their own layer-matched contact and statistic.  The present finite result is therefore a per-organism residual-spectrum record: $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$ dominates the computed $11$-organism panel, the GC3 rank effect is recorded as $\rho=0.136$, and the $f_3$-stress axis remains a translation-readout conjecture until a non-vacuous controlled $S_{f_3\hbox{-}\mathrm{stress},T_j}$ row is supplied.
