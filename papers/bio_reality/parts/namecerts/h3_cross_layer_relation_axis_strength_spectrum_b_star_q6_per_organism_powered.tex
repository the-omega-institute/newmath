\paragraph{Finite carrier.}
This record concerns a finite CDS-by-organism residual coordinate surface, restricted to the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ coordinate that detects third-position $f_3$ stress after amino-acid projection. The coordinate is not read as a protein, structure, phenotype, or mechanism. Its local subject is only the controlled survival of a synonymous residual direction against translation-layer readouts, with organism, codon-usage, tRNA or wobble-weight, and matched readout rows kept as distinct finite columns. The saturated $f_3$ row supplied by the finite codon computation has
$$
\begin{aligned}

\paragraph{Powered spectrum.}
The powered axis-strength computation is a finite descriptive result on $11$ computed organisms out of $16$ requested organisms, using $5$ folds, a minimum of $500$ proteins per organism, and $41$ synonymous design columns. The recorded conclusion is that the captured spectra are led on average by $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$, followed by $d_{\mathrm{tRNA}}$. At the per-organism level the dominant-count table is concentrated at
$$
\begin{aligned}
\#\{\hbox{organisms dominated by }d_{\mathrm{resid4}}\}=11.
\end{aligned}
$$
The domain counts in this finite panel are imbalanced, with $1$ archaeal, $2$ bacterial, and $8$ eukaryotic organisms. Consequently, domain means are only descriptive summaries of this row set and are not used here as domain-level laws. The largest recorded GC3 rank effect is attached to $d_{\mathrm{usage}}$ with $\rho=0.136$, which is small enough to keep the spectrum as a controlled residual summary rather than a GC3 explanation.

\paragraph{Relation to the $f_3$ translation row.}
The strongest finite message is not that $f_3$ stress has already been observed as a ribosome-level phenomenon. The message is narrower: in the available powered spectrum, the non-tRNA residual direction $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$ is the leading captured axis across all $11$ computed organisms, while independent linked rows report that the non-tRNA residual optimality direction aligns most with $f_3$ translation selection. The latter row used the same finite scale, $11$ computed organisms out of $16$ requested organisms, $5$ folds, at least $500$ proteins per organism, and $41$ synonymous design columns. In addition, the third-axis identity row identifies $d_{\perp}$ with usage frequency within synonymous family, with mean cosine $0.128$ and one-sided sign-test $p=0.000488$. A mechanism row then reports that GC-orthogonalized $d_{\perp}$ keeps significant cross-species alignment and held-out $R^2$, with the qualitative call ``selection-not-GC; not-wobble-clustered; CSC/positional identity needs external.''

\paragraph{Trigger condition.}
The conjectural trigger is local and conditional. The $f_3$ residual coordinate is allowed to become a translation-readout candidate only where a context tRNA or wobble-weight row distinguishes a codon $c$ from its third-position mate $c^{f_3}$, so that $w_c \ne w_{c^{f_3}}$. In such a row, the admissible statistic is a controlled entry $S_{f3\text{-}stress,T_j}$ for a bounded translation readout $T_j$, such as tAI, stAI, ribosome occupancy, or translation efficiency. The finite test has the form
$$
\begin{aligned}
S_{f3\text{-}stress,T_j} &> 0
\end{aligned}
$$
only after amino-acid composition, organism identity, GC3, CDS length, $M$-density, matched mRNA abundance where applicable, rank sufficiency, and simpler codon baselines have been included. If all such entries are non-positive, or if the fitted surface is rank-degenerate, the chapter reads the outcome as a null boundary for this layer.

\paragraph{Supporting finite rows.}
The tRNA-poverty mechanism row is compatible with the stress interpretation at a bounded level: in a separate powered calculation, $40$ organisms were computed out of $46$ requested organisms, $6$ were skipped, and $1000$ permutations at $\alpha=0.05$ supported the statement that $R$ sense codons are systematically tRNA-poor under GtRNAdb tGCN plus dos Reis wobble weights. The two-axis closure row reports a third axis present because $d_{\perp}$ has systematically positive held-out $R^2$ and mutually same-signed directions across organisms. The three-axis sufficiency row reports a fourth mechanism present on the same $11$-of-$16$ organism panel with $5$ folds and $41$ synonymous design columns. Candidate fourth-mechanism rows narrow the interpretation: mRNA stability is not the dominant fourth-axis identity in the yeast half-life assay; codon-pair structure is recorded as not the fourth mechanism; translation accuracy is recorded as not accuracy across the $11$ computed organisms with $200$ permutations; elongation dwell is only partial in $\mathrm{Saccharomyces\ cerevisiae}$. These rows support a finite residual-spectrum picture, not a completed biological mechanism.

\paragraph{Local refusal of over-interpretation.}
No translation, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, cellular localization, stability, phenotype, mechanism, adaptation claim, causation claim, or universal biological rule follows from this coordinate chapter alone. Modeled tAI or stAI weights are bounded codon-adaptation readouts, not observed ribosome footprints. Ribosome profiling or translation-efficiency rows would count only after same-organism and same-CDS joining with codon counts and matched mRNA controls. The three-organism all-$1.0$ controlled matrix is explicitly treated as a rank-degenerate null boundary rather than survival. A direct association from the residual coordinate to protein abundance, structure, localization, stability, function, or phenotype is inadmissible unless the same coordinate first has a non-vacuous positive $S_{f3\text{-}stress,T_j}$ translation entry under the stated controls and a separate layer-matched contact supplies the downstream readout.

\paragraph{Verdict of this finite record.}
The chapter is ready as a bounded BioReality record because the finite axis-strength spectrum is populated and passed: $11$ organisms were computed, $5$ folds were used, $41$ synonymous design columns were present, $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$ dominated all $11$ per-organism spectra, and the largest GC3 rank effect was $\rho=0.136$ for $d_{\mathrm{usage}}$.
