\paragraph{Finite readout surface.}
This record concerns a finite organism-by-codon readout surface for synonymous codon fibers paired by the $f_3$ involution. The relevant finite quotient geometry is nontrivial before any biological interpretation is added: the ambient matching vector has entries $[20,38,26,8,1]$, the quotient vector has entries $[10,14,6,1]$, and the quotient table has size $10$. Across the initial three-organism codon-count contact, $7$ of these $9$ residual coordinates were nonzero above the finite variance threshold $0.01$, giving nonzero fraction $0.7777777777777778$.  This establishes that the coordinate is not erased by the amino-acid quotient on the finite row set, but by itself it remains a codon-usage residual rather than a translation result.

\paragraph{Powered cross-organism test.}
The cross-organism generality run used a deterministic seed, $5$ folds, and $120$ permutations.  It requested $16$ organisms, obtained $15$ real organisms with at least $500$ proteins per organism, and tested all $15$ of those organisms.  The declared finite threshold for a positive generality call was $8$ real organisms.  The computed call was $f_3$-stress generalizes, with status passed.  In symbolic form, the row-level decision can be summarized as
$$
\begin{aligned}
 n_{requested} &= 16,\\
 n_{real} &= n_{tested}=15,\\
 n_{threshold} &= 8,\\
 k_{fold} &= 5,\\
 n_{perm} &= 120.
\end{aligned}
$$
The result is therefore not the three-row saturated case: it is a rank-powered finite panel in which the $f_3$-stress coordinate survives the cross-organism generality criterion.

\paragraph{Local verdict.}
The finite-row verdict is positive for this chapter: the $f_3$-stress coordinate passes a rank-powered cross-organism generality test and is consistent with wobble-broken translation-readout surfaces.  The claim is deliberately narrower than a biological mechanism.  It says that the amino-acid-quotient $f_3$-stress residual is a cross-layer codon-topology-to-translation-readout distinction on the tested finite surfaces.  It does not say that $f_3$-stress determines protein abundance, folding, localization, stability, physical admissibility, organismal phenotype, biochemical function, or a universal law.  Those higher-layer conclusions would require their own matched contacts and their own controlled finite statistics after the translation-readout step has been established on the same rows.
