\subsection{Cross-organism f3-stress generality under wobble-asymmetric translation readouts}
\label{subsec:bioreality-f3-stress-cross-organism-generality}

\paragraph{Finite surface.}
This record concerns a finite organism-by-codon readout surface for synonymous codon fibers paired by the $f_3$ operation.  The coordinate of interest is the amino-acid-quotient residual obtained by first projecting away amino-acid composition and then retaining the paired-codon antisymmetric component.  In symbols, the tested residual coordinate has the form $q-P_{\tau}q$ and is read only on codon pairs for which the wobble-weight surface can distinguish $c$ from $c^{f_3}$.  The point of the test is therefore not that codon usage alone is a translation variable, but that the residual survives as a predictor only when a translation-readout surface supplies an asymmetric codon-pair weighting.

\paragraph{Combinatorial grounding.}
The finite quotient geometry supplies a bounded coordinate system before any biological interpretation is made.  The verified face-vector data are $M_f=(20,38,26,8,1)$ for the source complex and $(10,14,6,1)$ for the quotient, with $|\pi_M|=10$.  The residual-basis contact then evaluates nine named $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinates: $K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $Leu_{CUN\,vs\,UUR}$, $Leu_{UUA\,vs\,UUG}$, $Ser_{UCR\,vs\,AGY}$, $Ser_{UCA\,vs\,UCG}$, $Thr_{ACR\,vs\,ACY}$, and the $f_3$-stress coordinate.  In the three-organism codon-count contact, seven of the nine residual entries were nonzero above the stated variance threshold, giving nonzero fraction $7/9=0.7777777777777778$.  These numbers establish that the coordinate is not an empty bookkeeping artifact, but they do not by themselves establish translation, mechanism, protein output, or any higher biological layer.

\paragraph{Rank-powered readout.}
The powered modeled-translation surface uses forty organisms and a nine-by-nine $S^{QT}$ matrix derived from modeled per-codon tAI readouts.  After residualizing against the control block, the reported control rank is $23$, the residual degrees of freedom are $17$, and the residualized $B^*_{Q6}$ design has rank $9$.  The global modeled translation-readout fit has $R^2_{QT}=0.9003849000036579$, below the degeneracy cutoff $0.999999$, so the result is not the three-row all-one saturation pattern that the boundary rules reject.  In contrast, the earlier three-organism bounded modeled-tAI contact had only $n=3$ organisms, and its role is limited to bounded coupling: it reported $81$ entries above threshold $0.01$ and maximum absolute modeled coupling $3801961.031607896$, but explicitly did not have the powered rank required for survival.

\paragraph{Generality test.}
The cross-organism generality run requested $16$ organisms and retained $15$ real organisms meeting the row requirements, with at least $500$ proteins per organism.  The test used $5$ folds and $120$ permutations under the deterministic seed $\mathrm{sha256{:}b\_star\_q6\_f3\_stress\_cross\_organism\_powered{:}deterministic}$.  The finite-row success threshold was $8$ real organisms, and the reported call was $f_3$-stress generalizes across the tested panel.  The accompanying strength-driver run computed $15$ organisms, found direction agreement in $15/15$ cases, and reported Spearman association $0.7071428571428572$ between total synonymous-selection strength and $f_3$-stress strength, with the Sulfolobus negative-alignment case tracked explicitly as a signed-direction exception rather than erased.

\paragraph{Local occupancy contact.}
A separate ribosome-occupancy contact gives a useful boundary check.  It joined $5754$ occupancy genes to $5754$ ordered-CDS rows out of $6013$ ordered CDS genes, with length mismatch count $0$.  The position-control procedure used the supplied five-prime and three-prime windows and demeaned occupancy within exact codon-position and amino-acid bins.  The null shuffled synonymous labels only within the same bin, using $200$ requested permutations; all $9$ families had at least $200$ permutations.  Under the description-length and direction gate with $\lambda_{DL}=0.01$, $DL=2$, and $C_H>0$, the families passing all gates were $K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, and $f_3$-stress.  This supports the claim that the $f_3$-stress coordinate has a layer-adjacent readout contact, but it remains a descriptive, position-controlled separation rather than a causal statement.

\paragraph{Verdict.}
The finite evidence supports the following restricted statement.  When the $f_3$-paired codon weights are symmetric, the coordinate remains a bounded codon-usage residual.  When the paired weights are wobble-asymmetric and the design has enough rank, the $f_3$-stress residual can survive as a cross-layer codon-topology-to-translation-readout distinction.  The ready status here rests on the powered $15$-organism generality call, the nondegenerate forty-organism modeled-tAI survival matrix, and the separate position-controlled ribosome-occupancy boundary contact.  It does not authorize protein abundance, folded structure, physical admissibility, localization, post-translational state, biological function, mechanism, phenotype, or a universal biological law.  Those claims would require their own same-row contacts and statistics after the translation-readout step, and they are not asserted in this record.
