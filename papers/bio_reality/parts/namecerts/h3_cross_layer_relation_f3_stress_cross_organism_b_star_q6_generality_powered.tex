\subsection{Cross-organism survival of the $f_3$-stress residual under wobble-broken translation readouts}\label{sec:h3-cross-layer-relation-f3-stress-cross-organism-b-star-q6-generality-powered}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite surface.}
This record studies the $B^*_{Q6}$ coordinate called $f_3$-stress after amino-acid projection.  The coordinate is not a raw codon count and not an amino-acid composition statistic: it is the residual left after the codon-usage vector is projected away from the amino-acid quotient baseline.  The relevant finite carrier is the organism-by-codon readout surface restricted to synonymous codon fibers equipped with paired codon usage, wobble-sensitive tAI or stAI weights, and translation-efficiency or translation-proximal readouts when those rows are actually present.  The finite quotient check gives $|\pi_M|=10$, quotient $f$-vector $(10,14,6,1)$, and ambient matching vector $(20,38,26,8,1)$.  In the three-organism residual-basis contact, the $B^*_{Q6}$ list contains nine coordinates,
$$
\begin{aligned}
&K\_AAA,\ Arg\_AGR,\ Ile\_AUA,\ Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR,\ Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG,\\
&Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY,\ Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG,\ Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY,\ f_3\text{-stress},
\end{aligned}
$$
and seven of the nine coordinates are nonzero above the variance threshold $0.01$, giving nonzero fraction $7/9=0.7777777777777778$.  This finite row count is important because it says that the coordinate exists as residual codon topology, not that it has already crossed into a biological output layer.

\paragraph{Rank and readout gate.}
The powered modeled-translation panel uses $40$ organisms and the same nine residual coordinates.  After residualizing by the control block, the calculation reports $24$ control columns, control rank $23$, residual degrees of freedom $17$, and residual-coordinate rank $9$.  The resulting modeled tAI readout surface has shape $9\times 9$ and total controlled readout fit $R^2_{QT}=0.9003849000036579$, below the degeneracy cutoff $0.999999$.  Thus the powered calculation is not the three-row saturated case.  By contrast, the earlier bounded modeled-tAI contact with only three organisms is explicitly underpowered: it contains all $81$ entries above threshold $0.01$ and a maximum absolute entry $3801961.031607896$, but the finite panel size $n=3$ prevents treating that surface as powered survival.  The chapter therefore uses the three-organism surface only as a bounded coupling check and uses the forty-organism panel for rank-admissible readout survival.

\paragraph{Cross-organism generality call.}
The direct cross-organism experiment for this claim is $b\_star\_q6\_f3\_stress\_cross\_organism\_powered$.  It requested $16$ organisms, found $15$ real organisms meeting the row requirements, and tested all $15$ with at least $500$ proteins per organism.  The procedure used $5$ folds and $120$ permutations, with real-threshold $8$.  Its finite verdict is
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{organisms\_tested} &= 15,\\
\mathrm{real\_threshold} &= 8,\\
\mathrm{fold\_count} &= 5,\\
\mathrm{permutation\_count} &= 120,\\
\mathrm{call} &= \mathrm{f3\_stress\_generalizes}.
\end{aligned}
$$
On these rows, $f_3$-stress is therefore retained as a cross-organism codon-topology-to-translation-readout distinction, conditional on the readout being wobble-sensitive and rank-powered.  The companion strength-driver calculation is consistent with this interpretation: it computes all $15$ requested real organisms, obtains direction agreement $15/15=1.0$, and reports Spearman association $0.7071428571428572$ between total synonymous-selection strength and $f_3$-stress strength, with the Sulfolobus negative alignment flagged as expected rather than silently absorbed into a same-direction rule.

\paragraph{Wobble and ribosome-proximal contact.}
The condition $w_c\neq w_{c^{f_3}}$ is not decorative.  If the codon-paired wobble weights are symmetric, the $f_3$ residual remains a bounded codon-usage coordinate and cannot be interpreted as a translation-layer distinction.  The position-controlled ribosome-occupancy contact gives a local check on the same boundary.  It joins $5754$ ordered-CDS genes to the occupancy file, with zero length mismatches after the join.  Its within-position-bin permutation null asks for $200$ permutations and obtains all $9$ families with at least $200$ permutations.  Under the gate $C_H>0$, description length $DL=2$, and $\lambda_{DL}=0.01$, exactly three families pass all gates: $K\_AAA$, $Arg\_AGR$, and $f_3$-stress.  This supports the claim that $f_3$-stress can remain visible in a translation-proximal readout after position and amino-acid binning, but only at this bounded readout layer.

\paragraph{Local boundary.}
The positive call is a finite-row survival and generality statement, not a license to promote the coordinate into every biological layer.  It says that, in the available powered organism panel, the amino-acid-quotient residual $f_3$-stress coordinate improves a wobble-sensitive translation readout in a non-saturated setting and generalizes across the tested organisms.  It does not by itself establish protein abundance, folded structure, physical admissibility, molecular function, localization, stability, phenotype, mechanism, or a universal biological law.  Each of those stronger interpretations would require its own row-matched contact and its own controlled statistic after the same-coordinate translation-readout step has survived.  Likewise, all-$1.0$ fits in an $n=3$ controlled matrix remain rank-saturated null evidence, not biological survival.
