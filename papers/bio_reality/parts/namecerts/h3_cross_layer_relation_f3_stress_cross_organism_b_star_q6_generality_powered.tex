\subsection{Cross-organism generality of the $f_3$-stress coordinate}
\label{sec:biorreality-f3-stress-cross-organism-generality}

\paragraph{Finite row carrier.}
The record concerns a finite organism-by-codon readout surface whose rows are restricted to synonymous codon fibers paired by the $f_3$ involution.  Each row carries three pieces of information: a codon-usage coordinate, a wobble-weight readout such as tAI or stAI when available, and a translation-proximal response.  The coordinate tested here is the amino-acid-quotient residual $f_3$-stress coordinate.  In symbols, if $q$ is the codon-usage vector and $P_{\tau}$ is the amino-acid projection, the tested residual direction is represented by
$$
\begin{aligned}
q_{f_3} &= q - P_{\tau}q,\\
q_{f_3}(c) &=-q_{f_3}(c^{f_3})
\end{aligned}
$$
inside each admissible paired synonymous fiber.  This construction is finite: the quotient geometry contact reports $\pi_M$ of size $10$, quotient $f$-vector $(10,14,6,1)$, and an ambient $M$-side $f$-vector $(20,38,26,8,1)$.  The residual-basis contact then computes $9$ named $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates, including $\mathrm{K\_AAA}$, $\mathrm{Arg\_AGR}$, $\mathrm{Ile\_AUA}$, the two leucine contrasts, the two serine contrasts, $\mathrm{Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY}$, and $f_3$-stress.  In the initial three-organism contact over human, yeast, and \emph{E. coli} K-12, $7$ of $9$ residual coordinates are nonzero above the stated variance threshold $0.01$, giving nonzero fraction $7/9=0.7777777777777778$.  That row is a codon-usage and quotient-geometry fact, not yet a biological response claim.

\paragraph{Powered generality test.}
The powered cross-organism test is the finite-row claim for this chapter.  The run identified as \texttt{16528255bb3b} requested $16$ organisms, retained $15$ real organisms with at least $500$ proteins per organism, and tested the $f_3$-stress coordinate with $5$ folds and $120$ deterministic permutations.  The declared real-organism threshold was $8$.  The finite call was \texttt{f3\_stress\_generalizes}, with status \texttt{passed}.  Thus the local readback is not merely that the coordinate exists in the quotient surface, but that the same residual direction clears the declared cross-organism generality rule on $15$ tested real organism rows:
$$
\begin{aligned}
N_{\mathrm{requested}} &= 16,\\
N_{\mathrm{real}} &= 15,\\
N_{\mathrm{tested}} &= 15,\\
N_{\mathrm{threshold}} &= 8,\\
K_{\mathrm{folds}} &= 5,\\
K_{\mathrm{perm}} &= 120.
\end{aligned}
$$
The conclusion is therefore a finite-panel generality statement: within the available rows, the $f_3$-stress residual behaves as a reusable cross-organism codon-topology signal under the declared rank and resampling design.  It is not a population theorem over all organisms, and it is not a direction-free biological law.

\paragraph{Translation-readout and wobble context.}
The same family of records supplies the translation-proximal context needed to interpret the word ``cross-layer'' narrowly.  A rank-powered modeled-tAI surface over $40$ organisms computes a $9\times 9$ $S^{QT}$ matrix with $R^2_{QT}=0.9003849000036579$, residualized controls of rank $23$, $24$ control columns, and residual degrees of freedom $17$.  The residualized $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ design has rank $9$.  This establishes that, for a larger modeled translation-readout panel, the residual coordinate system is not rank-collapsed and admits a controlled readout calculation.  By contrast, the earlier three-organism modeled-tAI matrix is explicitly bounded: it reports $81$ entries above the coupling threshold $0.01$ and maximum absolute entry $3801961.031607896$, but it also marks powered survival as unavailable with only $3$ organisms.  The three-row contact is therefore useful as a bounded coupling surface, not as evidence of survival.

The wobble and ribosome-adjacent records sharpen the same boundary.  In the position-controlled ribosome-occupancy contact, $9$ families have within-position-bin permutation nulls computed, all $9$ have at least $200$ permutations, and the stated gate uses $\lambda_{DL}=0.01$, $DL=2$, and $C_H>0$.  The families passing all gates are $\mathrm{K\_AAA}$, $\mathrm{Arg\_AGR}$, and $f_3$-stress.  The joined yeast occupancy surface contains $5754$ genes after length checking, with $5754$ gene overlaps between ribosome occupancy and ordered CDS rows, $6013$ ordered CDS genes, and $0$ length mismatches.  This supports a local, position-bin-controlled association between synonymous codon choice and relative occupancy in supplied windows.  It does not by itself turn the cross-organism generality call into a causal elongation mechanism.

\paragraph{Strength driver.}
A second powered record asks whether the magnitude of the $f_3$-stress effect is explained by total synonymous selection strength together with fixed-direction alignment.  That run requested $16$ organisms, computed $15$, used $5$ folds, and passed with direction agreement count $15$ out of $15$.  Its reported Spearman association between total synonymous selection strength and $f_3$-stress strength is $0.7071428571428572$, with direction agreement rate $1.0$.  The record also marks the Sulfolobus negative alignment and direction flag as true.  Read together with the generality run, this gives a finite interpretation: the powered panel sees the coordinate repeatedly, and its strength is ordered by a synonymous-selection readback under fixed-direction alignment.  The result is still descriptive and panel-bound; it is not a claim that every organism uses the same mechanistic route.

\paragraph{Local interpretation.}
The chapter-level statement is the following.  In the finite rows currently attached to this subject, $f_3$-stress is not just a leftover coordinate after amino-acid projection.  It is a named antisymmetric residual that survives the declared powered generality rule over $15$ tested organisms and is compatible with a larger controlled modeled-tAI calculation where the residual design has rank $9$ after the control set has rank $23$.  The biological reading is therefore cross-layer only in the restrained sense that codon-pair topology, wobble-weight asymmetry, and translation-proximal readouts can be compared on matched finite rows.  Where $w_c=w_{c^{f_3}}$, the $f_3$-paired wobble surface is symmetric and the coordinate remains a codon-usage residual.  Where rank is insufficient, such as the three-organism modeled-tAI surface, all fitted survival language is refused even if bounded couplings are nonzero.

\paragraph{Scope boundary.}
No protein abundance, folded structure, physical admissibility, molecular function, phenotype, biochemical mechanism, or organism-wide universal rule follows from this row by itself.  Translation survival requires a same-row translation readout with controls for amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, CDS length, $M$-density, and matched mRNA abundance where available.  Protein, structure, localization, stability, function, phenotype, and physical assays each require their own layer-matched contact and their own statistic.  The present verdict is therefore positive only for the finite powered cross-organism generality of the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ $f_3$-stress residual under the declared panel, folds, permutation design, and rank-aware readout constraints.
