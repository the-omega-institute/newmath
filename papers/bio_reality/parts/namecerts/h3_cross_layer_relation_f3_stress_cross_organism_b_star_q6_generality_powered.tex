\subsection{Rank-powered cross-organism generality of the $f_3$-stress residual}\label{sec:bioreality-f3-stress-cross-organism-generality}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite carrier.}
This record concerns a finite organism-by-codon readout surface restricted to synonymous fibers paired by the $f_3$ codon involution.  The coordinate under test is the amino-acid-quotient residual component
$ q_{f_3}=q-P_{\tau}q $,
where $P_{\tau}$ denotes projection to the amino-acid composition baseline.  It is not a raw codon-count direction and it is not a protein-level variable.  It becomes biologically testable only after pairing codon usage with an organism-scoped wobble-weight surface and a translation-layer readout.  The finite geometry check records $\pi_M$ with size $10$, quotient $f$-vector $(10,14,6,1)$, and matching $M_f$ vector $(20,38,26,8,1)$.  At the codon-residual level, the $B^*_{Q6}$ list has $9$ coordinates:
$K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $Leu_{CUN\,vs\,UUR}$, $Leu_{UUA\,vs\,UUG}$, $Ser_{UCR\,vs\,AGY}$, $Ser_{UCA\,vs\,UCG}$, $Thr_{ACR\,vs\,ACY}$, and $f_3$-stress.  In the initial three-organism contact, $7$ of $9$ quotient residuals are nonzero above the stated variance threshold $0.01$, giving nonzero fraction $7/9=0.7777777777777778$; this establishes finite residual variation, not a cross-layer conclusion.

\paragraph{Powered readout surface.}
The powered modeled translation-readout panel uses $40$ organisms, a $9\times 9$ $S^{QT}$ surface, and modeled per-codon tAI readouts derived from tRNA copy counts and dos Reis wobble weights.  After residualizing controls, the design reports $24$ control columns, $\operatorname{rank}(Z)=23$, residual degrees of freedom $17$, and $\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde X)=9$.  The aggregate modeled-readout statistic is $R^2_{QT}=0.9003849000036579$, below the declared degeneracy threshold $0.999999$ and therefore not the all-fitted rank-saturation case.  This powered setting is deliberately separated from the three-organism bounded calculation, where the same residual family has only $3$ organisms and is explicitly marked underpowered despite $81$ modeled-tAI coupling entries above threshold $0.01$ and maximum absolute modeled coupling $3801961.031607896$.  The distinction matters: a large bounded coupling is not by itself survival after controls.

\paragraph{Generality test.}
The cross-organism $f_3$-stress generality run is identified by experiment run $16528255bb3b$.  It requested $16$ organisms and retained $15$ real organisms satisfying the finite data requirement of at least $500$ proteins per organism.  The analysis used $5$ folds and $120$ permutations with a real-organism threshold of $8$.  The recorded call is $f_3\text{-stress generalizes}$, with status passed, for claim $h3.cross_layer_relation.f3_stress_cross_organism.b_star_q6_generality_powered$.  Thus the finite panel supports the specific statement that the $f_3$-stress coordinate is not merely a single-organism artifact when tested in the rank-powered cross-organism setting.  The companion strength-driver run used the same $15$ computed organisms, $5$ folds, and the same minimum protein count.  It reports Spearman correlation $0.7071428571428572$ between total synonymous selection strength and $f_3$-stress strength, and direction agreement $15/15=1.0$, with the Sulfolobus negative-alignment case recorded explicitly.  These numbers support a selection-strength alignment readout, not a universal sign law.

\paragraph{Wobble-symmetry condition.}
The biological interpretation depends on the codon-paired wobble surface.  If a codon and its $f_3$ partner have symmetric weights, then the residual coordinate remains a codon-topology or codon-usage boundary and does not become a translation-layer distinction.  The powered modeled-tAI setting is the route where wobble asymmetry, codon usage, and the controlled readout can be tested together.  A separate ribosome-occupancy contact gives a more local check: $5754$ ribosome-occupancy genes join to ordered CDS rows after length checking, with $0$ length mismatches out of $6013$ ordered CDS genes.  Position confounding is controlled by file-provided window and codon-index bins, and the null shuffles synonymous labels only within the same bin.  With $200$ requested permutations, all $9$ families have at least $200$ within-bin permutations, and the families passing the $\lambda_{DL}=0.01$ and $C_H>0$ gates are $K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, and $f_3$-stress.  This is consistent with a local ribosome-occupancy separation for the same residual family, but it remains descriptive rather than causal.

\paragraph{Readback and null cases.}
The admissible readback is a rank-powered $S^{QT}_{f_3\text{-stress},j}$ entry for a tAI, stAI, or measured translation-efficiency surface under controls for amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, CDS length, $M$-density, and matched mRNA abundance where available.  The record is null if the $f_3$-paired wobble weights are symmetric, if the matrix is rank-saturated, or if the panel lacks enough independent rows to test the residual after controls.  In particular, the three-organism all-fitted case is not positive evidence even when fitted values look numerically perfect; it is a refusal boundary caused by insufficient rank.

\paragraph{Local scope boundary.}
The finite result licenses only a controlled cross-layer readout statement about the $f_3$-stress residual under the stated codon-usage, wobble-weight, and translation-readout contacts.  It does not by itself establish measured translation output in every organism, protein abundance, folded structure, post-translational state, localization, stability, physical admissibility, molecular function, phenotype, mechanism, or a universal biological rule.  Each such higher layer would require its own matched contact and its own controlled statistic.  The present chapter therefore records a rank-powered cross-organism distinction at the codon-topology-to-translation-readout boundary, together with explicit refusal of promotion beyond that boundary.
