\subsection{Cross-organism generality of the $f_3$-stress residual}\label{sec:bioreality-f3-stress-cross-organism-generality}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite readout surface.}
This record concerns the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ synonymous-residual coordinate named $f_3$-stress, evaluated only after amino-acid projection has removed the ordinary amino-acid-composition component. The relevant finite surface is not the full biological organism, but a restricted organism-by-codon readout panel: synonymous codon fibers paired by the $f_3$ involution, organism-scoped codon usage, wobble-weight readouts such as tAI or stAI when available, and translation-efficiency or ribosome-occupancy measurements when the row actually contains them. On the quotient side, the finite geometry is nontrivial before any biological interpretation is attempted: the $M_f$ vector is $(20,38,26,8,1)$, the quotient $f$-vector is $(10,14,6,1)$, and $\pi_M$ has size $10$. In the first cross-organism codon-count contact, the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual list has $9$ coordinates: $K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $Leu_{CUN\_vs\_UUR}$, $Leu_{UUA\_vs\_UUG}$, $Ser_{UCR\_vs\_AGY}$, $Ser_{UCA\_vs\_UCG}$, $Thr_{ACR\_vs\_ACY}$, and $f_3$-stress. Across the three initial organisms, $7$ of these $9$ residual coordinates are nonzero above the variance threshold $0.01$, giving nonzero fraction $7/9=0.7777777777777778$. This establishes that the coordinate is not merely a named syntactic slot in the finite codon surface.

\paragraph{Powered cross-organism call.}
The powered generality experiment asked for $16$ organisms and retained $15$ real organisms meeting the row conditions. Each organism contributed at least $500$ proteins, the held-out evaluation used $5$ folds, and the null comparison used $120$ permutations with a real-organism threshold of $8$. The finite decision returned status passed and the call $f_3$-stress generalizes. In compact form, the generality gate can be recorded as
$$
\begin{aligned}
N_{\mathrm{requested}}&=16, & N_{\mathrm{real}}&=15, & N_{\mathrm{tested}}&=15,\\
N_{\mathrm{fold}}&=5, & N_{\mathrm{perm}}&=120, & N_{\mathrm{threshold}}&=8.
\end{aligned}
$$
The interpretation is deliberately narrow: the result says that the amino-acid-quotient $f_3$-stress residual has cross-organism predictive persistence on the declared finite readout, not that the coordinate is a universal biological law or an unconditioned causal variable.

\paragraph{Translation-readout boundary.}
The strongest modeled translation-readout support is rank-powered but still bounded by the readout source. In the $40$-organism modeled-tAI panel, the controlled design used $24$ control columns, rank $Z=23$, residual degrees of freedom $17$, and residualized coordinate rank $9$. The computed $S^{QT}$ matrix has shape $9\times 9$, and the reported controlled $R^2_{QT}$ is $0.9003849000036579$. This is a finite modeled translation-readout survival result for the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ coordinate system under amino-acid, organism, GC3, length, $M$-density, and related baseline controls. It is not ribosome profiling, proteomics, protein folding, or physical admissibility. The earlier $3$-organism modeled-tAI contact is useful as a boundary check: it has all $81$ matrix entries above threshold $0.01$ and maximum absolute entry $3801961.031607896$, but with only $3$ organisms it is not a powered survival panel and its break condition for powered survival is not met. Thus the powered claim is assigned to the $40$-organism rank-sufficient readout, while the $3$-organism surface remains a bounded coupling contact.

\paragraph{Wobble and position evidence.}
The $f_3$-stress coordinate is biologically interpretable only where codon-paired wobble weights or observed translation rows break the $f_3$ symmetry. The ribosome-occupancy contact gives a separate local support check: $5754$ genes overlap between ribosome occupancy and ordered CDS rows after length checking, with $0$ length mismatches. The within-position-bin permutation analysis computed $9$ families, all with at least $200$ permutations requested, and the families passing the $\lambda_{DL}$ and directional gate are $K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, and $f_3$-stress. The penalty parameters for that gate are $DL=2$ and $\lambda_{DL}=0.01$, with directional rule $C_H>0$. This does not by itself promote $f_3$-stress to a mechanism; it only shows that the same coordinate can survive a position-confound-controlled ribosome-occupancy separation in a finite same-window analysis.

\paragraph{Strength driver.}
The companion strength-driver computation reports $15$ computed organisms from $16$ requested, again with $5$ folds and at least $500$ proteins per organism. The direction agreement count is $15$ out of $15$, hence direction agreement rate $1.0$, and the Spearman association between total synonymous selection strength and $f_3$-stress strength is $0.7071428571428572$. A Sulfolobus negative-alignment direction is explicitly flagged, so the record should be read as fixed-direction alignment plus total synonymous selection strength, not as a universal positive-effect direction. This supplies an organism-level explanation of why the $f_3$-stress signal is strong in the finite panel, while preserving the sign and alignment caveats.

\paragraph{Local boundary of the claim.}
The chapter supports the following finite statement: within the declared rank-powered organism panel, the amino-acid-quotient $f_3$-stress residual behaves as a cross-layer codon-topology-to-translation readout distinction when the row supplies wobble asymmetry or a translation readout and the controlled rank is sufficient. The claim stops there. It does not assert protein abundance, protein structure, folded-state admissibility, molecular function, phenotype, mechanism, causation, or universality. Such conclusions require their own same-row contacts and their own controlled statistics. If the wobble weights are $f_3$-symmetric, if the matrix is rank-saturated, or if the translation readout is only named rather than computed, then $f_3$-stress remains a bounded codon-usage residual rather than a promoted biological variable.
