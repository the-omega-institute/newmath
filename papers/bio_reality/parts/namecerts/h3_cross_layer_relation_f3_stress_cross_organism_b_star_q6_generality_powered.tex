\subsection{Cross-organism f3-stress generality after amino-acid quotienting}
\label{sec:bior-reality-f3-stress-cross-organism-generality}

\paragraph{Finite readout surface.}
This record concerns the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ synonymous-residual coordinate called $f_3$-stress. The carrier is a finite organism-by-codon readout surface restricted to synonymous codon fibers paired by the $f_3$ involution, with codon usage, tAI or stAI wobble weights, and translation-efficiency readouts kept as separate measured or modelled surfaces. The coordinate is not a raw codon count. It is the residual left after amino-acid projection, written locally as $q-P_{\tau}q$, and it is informative only when the paired codons do not have symmetric wobble weights. The basic geometry is finite: the quotient surface has $\pi_M$ size $10$, quotient $f$-vector $(10,14,6,1)$, and matching $M$-surface $f$-vector $(20,38,26,8,1)$. The observed quotient representatives include ten finite binary rows, so the object being tested is a bounded codon-topology readout rather than an unbounded biological law.

\paragraph{Residual coordinate content.}
The amino-acid quotient leaves a nontrivial residual panel. In the finite cross-organism codon-count contact, the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual list has $9$ entries: $K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $Leu_{CUN\,vs\,UUR}$, $Leu_{UUA\,vs\,UUG}$, $Ser_{UCR\,vs\,AGY}$, $Ser_{UCA\,vs\,UCG}$, $Thr_{ACR\,vs\,ACY}$, and the antisymmetric $f_3$-stress pair. In the three-organism codon-count contact, $7$ of these $9$ residuals are nonzero above the variance threshold $0.01$, giving nonzero fraction $0.7777777777777778$. The organisms in that seed contact are human, budding yeast, and \emph{E. coli} K-12. This establishes only that the quotient did not erase all synonymous-codon variation. It does not itself establish translation, ribosome dynamics, protein abundance, folding, physical admissibility, function, mechanism, adaptation, or universality.

\paragraph{Rank-powered modelled translation readout.}
The stronger modelled translation-readout panel uses $40$ organisms and projects per-codon tAI readouts, computed from tRNA gene-copy counts and dos Reis wobble weights, onto the same $9$ residual coordinates. After residualizing against $24$ control columns, the control rank is $23$, the residual degrees of freedom after controls are $17$, and the residualized coordinate rank is $9$. The reported $S^{QT}$ matrix has shape $9\times 9$ with $R^2_{QT}=0.9003849000036579$, below the declared degeneracy cutoff $0.999999$ and above the positive partial-$R^2$ threshold $10^{-12}$. This supports a bounded statement: the residual coordinates, including $f_3$-stress, survive in a rank-admissible modelled tAI readout under the stated controls. It is not a ribosome-profiling measurement, not measured elongation speed, not proteomics, and not a protein-realization claim.

\paragraph{Three-organism boundary.}
The earlier three-organism tAI contact is retained as a refusal boundary. It contains $61$ sense codons, local GtRNAdb payloads covering all $20$ standard amino-acid families for the three organisms, and nonzero bounded coupling with $81$ entries above threshold $0.01$. Its maximum absolute entry is $3801961.031607896$. Nevertheless, $n=3$ is explicitly underpowered for rank survival. Any all-perfect or rank-saturated fit on that scale is treated as null or data-insufficient rather than as evidence for a translation layer. The powered interpretation begins only at the larger rank-sufficient panel.

\paragraph{Cross-organism generality call.}
The finite powered generality experiment tests whether the $f_3$-stress signal persists across organisms rather than being a single-organism accident. The run used $5$ folds, requested $16$ organisms, obtained $15$ real organisms, tested $15$ organisms, required at least $500$ proteins per organism, and used $120$ permutations. The real-organism threshold was $8$, and the finite call was \emph{$f_3$-stress generalizes}. In the terminology of this chapter, this means that the $f_3$-stress residual is not merely a local codon-table curiosity once the powered cross-organism panel is used. It is a cross-layer candidate only at the translation-readout boundary, and only because the row set is large enough to avoid the three-organism rank-saturation failure.

\paragraph{Strength driver.}
A companion powered run asks what explains the magnitude of the $f_3$-stress effect. It again requested $16$ organisms and computed $15$ organisms with $5$ folds and at least $500$ proteins per organism. The Spearman association between total synonymous selection strength and $f_3$-stress strength is $0.7071428571428572$. Direction agreement is $15/15$, giving agreement rate $1.0$, with the Sulfolobus negative alignment and direction recorded as true. The finite interpretation is therefore not that $f_3$-stress has a universal signed effect. The stronger statement is narrower: in this powered panel, the apparent strength of the $f_3$ coordinate is explained by total synonymous selection strength together with fixed-direction alignment.

\paragraph{Position and dwell contact.}
The ribosome-occupancy contact supplies a separate bounded check on local occupancy covariation. The ordered CDS and occupancy tables overlap on $5754$ genes after the length check, with $6013$ ordered CDS genes and no length mismatches. The position control uses the file-supplied $5'$ and $3'$ windows together with codon-index and amino-acid bins; the null shuffles synonymous labels only within the same bin. The permutation request is $200$, and all $9$ computed families have at least $200$ permutations. With $\lambda_{DL}=0.01$, description length $DL=2$, and the rule $C_H>0$, the families passing all gates are $K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, and $f_3$-stress. This is evidence for position-bin-controlled local ribosome-occupancy separation in the same codon windows, not a causal proof of stalling, elongation mechanism, protein output, or downstream phenotype.

\paragraph{Ordered downstream decomposition.}
The protein-side decomposition is deliberately descriptive. In the ordered partition of $P_Q=\Pi_{Q_e}P_e$, the computed $P_Q$ norms are $605.0092168669606$ for zebrafish with $13140$ full rows, $225.84556319953032$ for \emph{E. coli} K-12 MG1655 with $2517$ rows, $286.3673197177688$ for human with $11250$ rows in the abundance-ordered run, and $229.86450794006544$ for budding yeast with $4759$ rows. The translation fraction is $0.02436809579891462$ for zebrafish, $0.00035964766234144823$ for \emph{E. coli}, $0.006011907792542833$ for human, and $0.11271394500088064$ for budding yeast. The corresponding unexplained fractions remain large: $0.9756319042010854$, $0.9942823785118229$, $0.9736442326306781$, and $0.8676586345294415$. In the direction-aware dictionary table, no positive $H$ readout is retained for these four organisms. Thus the downstream rows are useful as boundary checks: they prevent the translation-readout signal from being redescribed as protein function, localization, stability, complex membership, or phenotype.

\paragraph{Local verdict.}
The finite evidence supports a bounded cross-layer relation: $f_3$-stress is a rank-powered synonymous residual that generalizes across a $15$-organism powered panel and is compatible with wobble-broken modelled translation readouts. The claim is positive at the codon-topology-to-translation-readout interface, with the important qualification that modelled tAI or stAI is not the same object as measured ribosome profiling. A stronger measured claim would require same-organism and same-CDS rows joining codon counts, wobble weights, measured RPF or translation-efficiency rows, matched mRNA abundance, and the declared controls for amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, CDS length, $M$-density, rank, and simpler baselines. Without such a separate contact, no statement follows about translation mechanism, folded structure, physical admissibility, molecular function, protein abundance, system phenotype, adaptation, causation, or a universal biological law.
