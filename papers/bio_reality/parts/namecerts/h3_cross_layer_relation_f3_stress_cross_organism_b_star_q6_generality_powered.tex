\subsection{Cross-organism $f_3$-stress generality under wobble-broken translation readouts}

\paragraph{Finite carrier and residual coordinate.}
The record concerns the $B^*_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinate called $f_3$-stress after amino-acid projection. The finite carrier is an organism-by-codon readout surface restricted to synonymous codon fibers in which each tested coordinate has already been separated from amino-acid identity. The codon-side quotient is not empty: the finite quotient check reports $|\pi_M|=10$, a quotient face vector $(10,14,6,1)$, and the matching $M_f$ vector $(20,38,26,8,1)$. On the organism contact used for the residual-basis check, the surface contains three codon-count tables, for human, budding yeast, and $E.\ coli$ K-12, and the $B^*_{Q6}$ residual list has nine coordinates:
$$
\begin{aligned}
&K_{AAA},\ \operatorname{Arg}_{AGR},\ \operatorname{Ile}_{AUA},\ \operatorname{Leu}_{CUN\,vs\,UUR},\ \operatorname{Leu}_{UUA\,vs\,UUG},\\
&\operatorname{Ser}_{UCR\,vs\,AGY},\ \operatorname{Ser}_{UCA\,vs\,UCG},\ \operatorname{Thr}_{ACR\,vs\,ACY},\ f_3\text{-stress}.
\end{aligned}
$$
Seven of these nine residual coordinates have nonzero finite variance at the stated threshold $0.01$, so the residual layer is a real finite codon-usage surface rather than a notation-only object. This paragraph only licenses codon-residual geometry. It does not by itself license translation, protein abundance, folded structure, physical admissibility, biological function, mechanism, phenotype, or universality.

\paragraph{Translation-readout route and rank.}
The powered translation-readout surface uses a forty-organism panel with modeled per-codon tAI readouts derived from tRNA gene-copy counts and dos Reis wobble weights, then projected onto the same nine $B^*_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates. The controlled design has $24$ control columns, control rank $23$, residual degrees of freedom $17$, and residualized residual-coordinate rank $9$. The reported powered statistic is
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{QT} &= 0.9003849000036579,\\
\operatorname{rank}(X_{\mathrm{res}}) &= 9,\\
\operatorname{rank}(Z) &= 23,\\
\operatorname{df}_{\mathrm{after}\ Z} &= 17.
\end{aligned}
$$
This is enough rank to treat the modeled $S^{QT}$ surface as powered in the stated finite panel. It should not be confused with the earlier three-organism bounded contact: that smaller matrix had only three organisms, no powered-survival status, $81$ modeled entries above the threshold $0.01$, and maximum absolute entry $3801961.031607896$. The smaller contact demonstrates a bounded modeled coupling surface, whereas the forty-organism panel supplies the rank-bearing readout used here.

\paragraph{Generality call.}
The cross-organism $f_3$-stress generality test is finite and explicitly powered. It requested $16$ organisms, retained $15$ real organisms, tested $15$ organisms, required at least $500$ proteins per organism, used $5$ folds, and used $120$ deterministic permutations. The pass threshold was $8$ real organisms, and the recorded call is $f_3\text{-stress generalizes}$. In compact form, the observed row is
$$
\begin{aligned}
N_{\mathrm{requested}} &= 16,\\
N_{\mathrm{real}} &= 15,\\
N_{\mathrm{tested}} &= 15,\\
N_{\mathrm{threshold}} &= 8,\\
K_{\mathrm{fold}} &= 5,\\
B_{\mathrm{perm}} &= 120.
\end{aligned}
$$
Because $15\geq 8$, the finite decision rule returns a positive generality verdict for the $f_3$-stress coordinate on this panel. The deterministic seed is recorded as a SHA-256-labelled protocol seed, so the row is an auditable computation rather than an informal biological assertion.

\paragraph{Wobble asymmetry and local ribosome-occupancy support.}
A separate position-controlled ribosome-occupancy contact gives a mechanistic boundary check without turning the result into a causal claim. It joins $5754$ genes after length checking, with $5754$ overlapping gene identifiers and no length mismatches. The within-position-bin permutation null was computed for all $9$ residual families, with at least $200$ permutations for every family. The gate used description length $DL=2$, $\lambda_{DL}=0.01$, and the sign-alignment rule $C_H>0$. Under these gates, the passing families are
$$
\begin{aligned}
\{K_{AAA},\operatorname{Arg}_{AGR},f_3\text{-stress}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
This supports the statement that the same $f_3$-stress coordinate can survive a position-bin-controlled ribosome-occupancy separation in the bounded window analysis. The statistic is descriptive and position-confound-controlled: codon labels are shuffled only within the same position or amino-acid bin, and the effect is evaluated on locally demeaned occupancy. It is not a perturbation experiment and does not establish a causal ribosome mechanism.

\paragraph{Strength driver.}
The finite strength-driver row is consistent with the generality row. It computed $15$ organisms out of $16$ requested, used $5$ folds, required at least $500$ proteins per organism, and reported direction agreement in all $15$ tested organisms. The direction agreement rate is therefore
$$
\begin{aligned}
\frac{15}{15} &= 1.0.
\end{aligned}
$$
The same row reports Spearman correlation $0.7071428571428572$ between total synonymous-selection strength and $f_3$-stress strength, with a recorded negative-alignment direction for the Sulfolobus case. The correct reading is that the observed $f_3$-stress strength is organized by total synonymous-selection strength together with fixed-direction alignment on this finite panel. This is stronger than a three-organism residual observation, but it is still a finite cross-organism statistic rather than a universal law.

\paragraph{Verdict and local boundary.}
The chapter-level verdict is that $f_3$-stress is a ready cross-layer relation at the bounded translation-readout level: the residual coordinate is nontrivial after amino-acid quotienting, the powered modeled translation-readout surface has sufficient rank, the cross-organism generality rule passes with $15$ tested organisms against a threshold of $8$, and the position-controlled occupancy analysis independently retains $f_3$-stress among the passing families under $\lambda_{DL}=0.01$. The permissible claim is narrow: $f_3$-stress is a codon-topology residual whose translation-proximal readout survives in wobble-broken, rank-bearing, finite panels. No claim about mature protein abundance, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, phenotype, causal mechanism, or global biological law follows from this row. Any such higher-layer statement would require its own same-row contact and its own controlled statistic.
