\paragraph{Finite carrier.}
The record concerns the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ synonymous-residual coordinate named $f_3$-stress.  The carrier is a finite organism-by-codon readout surface: codon-usage rows are first restricted to synonymous codon fibers, then quotient coordinates remove amino-acid composition before the paired third-position residual is evaluated.  The relevant residual is the amino-acid-quotient coordinate $q-P_{\tau}q$, not a raw codon count and not an amino-acid-composition surrogate.  In the finite quotient geometry used by the surrounding row set, the ambient $M$ face vector is $(20,38,26,8,1)$, the projected quotient has face vector $(10,14,6,1)$, and the quotient image has size $10$.  On the three-organism residual-basis contact, $9$ $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ coordinates were computed and $7$ of them were nonzero above the stated variance threshold, giving nonzero fraction $7/9=0.7777777777777778$.  The coordinate list includes $K\_AAA$, $Arg\_AGR$, $Ile\_AUA$, $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$, $Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG$, $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$, $Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG$, $Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY$, and $f_3$-stress.

\paragraph{Readout gate.}
The biological question is not whether the coordinate exists.  It is whether this third-position residual survives as a cross-layer translation readout only when codon-paired wobble weights distinguish the two $f_3$ partners.  A symmetric wobble surface would leave the coordinate at the codon-usage boundary.  The powered modeled-tAI panel supplies the rank check for this gate: $40$ organisms were loaded, the residual coordinate matrix had rank $9$, the control design had rank $23$, and the residual degrees of freedom after controls were $17$.  The computed $S^{QT}$ matrix had shape $9\times 9$ and total modeled-readout coefficient $R^2_{QT}=0.9003849000036579$, below the declared degeneracy cutoff $0.999999$.  Thus this row is not the rejected $n=3$ all-fit surface; it is a rank-admissible modeled translation-readout panel with nontrivial residual degrees of freedom.

\paragraph{Primary finite result.}
The cross-organism generality run tested the $f_3$-stress coordinate across a requested panel of $16$ organisms, of which $15$ had real usable rows and $15$ were tested.  Each organism required at least $500$ proteins.  The evaluation used $5$ folds and $120$ permutations with deterministic seed $sha256:b\_star\_q6\_f3\_stress\_cross\_organism\_powered:deterministic$.  The recorded threshold for a real generality call was $8$ organisms, and the run returned status $passed$ with call $f_3\_stress\_generalizes$.  In finite-row terms, the chapter-level assertion is therefore the following bounded statement:
$$
\begin{aligned}
&\text{tested organisms}=15,\qquad \text{real threshold}=8,\\
&\text{folds}=5,\qquad \text{permutations}=120,\\
&\text{minimum proteins per organism}=500,\\
&\text{verdict}=\text{passed cross-organism generality for } f_3\text{-stress}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The result supports a cross-layer distinction only at the translation-readout level represented by the attached finite surfaces.  It does not by itself identify a universal biological direction, a causal mechanism, or a protein-level consequence.

\paragraph{Direction and strength check.}
A companion strength-driver row gives the most specific finite direction information available here.  Across the same requested scale, $15$ organisms were computed, again with $5$ folds and at least $500$ proteins per organism.  The total synonymous-selection strength had Spearman association $0.7071428571428572$ with $f_3$-stress strength.  Direction agreement was $15/15$, so the agreement rate was $1.0$, and the row separately marked Sulfolobus negative alignment and direction as true.  This is useful as a consistency check on strength and sign, but it is still an association on the declared readout surface.  The finite row licenses the statement that the observed strength is aligned with total synonymous-selection strength under the fixed-direction test; it does not license a claim that a single direction is universal outside the tested panel.

\paragraph{Position-resolved translation contact.}
The ribosome-occupancy contact supplies a separate, narrower readout involving local ribosome occupancy rather than modeled tAI alone.  In that row, $5754$ genes overlapped between the occupancy table and the ordered coding-sequence table; $5754$ genes remained after the length check, with $0$ length mismatches out of $6013$ ordered coding-sequence genes.  The row computed null envelopes for $9$ families, and all $9$ had at least $200$ within-position-bin permutations requested.  The finite gate used $\lambda_{DL}=0.01$ and $C_H>0$; the families passing all gates were $K\_AAA$, $Arg\_AGR$, and $f_3$-stress.  This contact is important because it keeps the codon-window statistic tied to position-bin-controlled occupancy separation rather than to raw codon composition.  It remains descriptive and position-controlled, not causal.

\paragraph{Boundary of the claim.}
The accepted content of this chapter is a finite cross-layer readout statement: the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ $f_3$-stress coordinate generalizes across the tested organism panel when evaluated through the declared translation-readout surfaces and rank controls.  The claim stops there.  No protein abundance, folded structure, physical admissibility, localization, post-translational state, biological function, phenotype, mechanism, adaptation law, or global biological rule follows from this row unless a separate layer-matched contact supplies those measurements and the same-row statistic is recomputed under the relevant controls.  Modeled tAI or stAI weights are bounded translation annotations, not observed ribosome occupancy.  Ribosome profiling or occupancy rows, when present, are translation-readout contacts, not protein-structure or function assays.  Matched mRNA abundance is a control for separating transcriptional signal from translation efficiency, not a translation claim by itself.

The finite evidence is sufficient for a ready BioReality chapter because the primary generality row is passed, the tested panel is rank-powered rather than saturated, and the supporting rows give explicit row counts, ranks, residual degrees of freedom, thresholds, fold counts, permutation counts, and coordinate names.
