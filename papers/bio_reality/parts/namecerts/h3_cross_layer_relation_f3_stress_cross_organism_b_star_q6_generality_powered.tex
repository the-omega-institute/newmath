\subsection{Rank-powered f\_3-stress generality across organisms}
\label{sec:bioreality-f3-stress-cross-organism-generality}

\paragraph{Question.}
The record tests whether the amino-acid-quotient residual coordinate called $f_3$-stress behaves as a repeatable cross-organism translation-layer distinction, rather than as a codon-table artifact.  The carrier is a finite organism-by-codon readout surface restricted to synonymous fibers paired by the $f_3$ involution.  Each row supplies codon usage, a bounded wobble-weight readout such as tAI or stAI when available, and a translation-proximal readout.  The coordinate is formed after amino-acid projection: if $q$ is the codon-usage vector and $P_\tau q$ is its amino-acid component, the residual coordinate lives in $q-P_\tau q$.  The specific test is meaningful only when the paired codons have unequal wobble weights, $w_c \ne w_{c^{f_3}}$; otherwise the coordinate remains a codon-usage residual with no translation-layer asymmetry to read.

\paragraph{Finite carrier.}
The residual-basis construction computed $9$ $B^*_{Q6}$ synonymous coordinates, namely $K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $Leu_{CUN\_vs\_UUR}$, $Leu_{UUA\_vs\_UUG}$, $Ser_{UCR\_vs\_AGY}$, $Ser_{UCA\_vs\_UCG}$, $Thr_{ACR\_vs\_ACY}$, and $f_3$-stress.  In the three-organism finite contact used to establish nontrivial residual support, $7$ of the $9$ coordinates were nonzero above the declared variance threshold $0.01$, giving nonzero fraction $0.7777777777777778$.  The organisms in that contact were human, budding yeast, and \emph{E. coli} K-12; this is a finite contact, not a population estimate.  The quotient geometry used by the record has $\pi_M$ size $10$, quotient $f$-vector $(10,14,6,1)$, and matching $M$-fiber vector $(20,38,26,8,1)$.  These numbers fix the finite residual shape before any biological readout is considered.

\paragraph{Rank-powered translation surface.}
The powered modeled-tAI surface used $40$ organism rows, $9$ residual coordinates, and $9$ modeled readout columns.  After residualizing the control block $Z$, the control column count was $24$, $\operatorname{rank}(Z)=23$, and the residual degrees of freedom after $Z$ were $17$.  The residualized $B^*_{Q6}$ design retained $\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde X)=9$, so the powered calculation is not the three-row saturated case.  Its modeled translation-readout fit was
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{QT} &= 0.9003849000036579,\\
\operatorname{rank}(Z) &= 23,\\
\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde X) &= 9,\\
\operatorname{df}_{\mathrm{resid}} &= 17.
\end{aligned}
$$
The earlier three-organism modeled-tAI surface is kept separate: it had $81$ entries above threshold $0.01$ and maximum absolute entry $3801961.031607896$, but it was explicitly not a powered survival panel because $n=3$ is rank-degenerate for the requested interpretation.  The chapter therefore treats the three-row matrix as bounded coupling evidence only, while the $40$-organism surface supplies the rank-admissible translation-readout background.

\paragraph{Generality call.}
The cross-organism $f_3$-stress generality test requested $16$ organisms and obtained $15$ real organism rows satisfying the finite-row requirements.  Each organism row required at least $500$ proteins.  The procedure used $5$ folds and $120$ permutations, with real-threshold count $8$.  The finite result was
$$
\begin{aligned}
N_{\mathrm{requested}} &= 16,\\
N_{\mathrm{real}} &= 15,\\
N_{\mathrm{tested}} &= 15,\\
N_{\mathrm{threshold}} &= 8,\\
K_{\mathrm{fold}} &= 5,\\
K_{\mathrm{perm}} &= 120.
\end{aligned}
$$
The recorded call was $f_3\text{-stress\_generalizes}$ with status passed.  In biological prose, the finite panel supports the statement that the $f_3$-stress residual is not confined to a single organism-specific codon table once the analysis is restricted to the rank-powered rows and the declared translation-proximal readout route.

\paragraph{Mechanistic guardrail from wobble and occupancy.}
A separate position-controlled occupancy contact supplies a bounded consistency check rather than a full mechanism.  In that record, all $9$ synonymous families had at least $200$ within-position-bin permutations.  The gate used $\lambda_{DL}=0.01$, $DL=2$, and the rule $C_H>0$.  The families passing all gates were $K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, and $f_3$-stress.  The joined ribosome-occupancy surface had $5754$ overlapping genes, $5754$ joined genes after the length check, and $0$ length mismatches.  This is relevant because it places $f_3$-stress among the finite coordinates that can survive a local ribosome-occupancy separation test after exact position-bin control, but it still does not identify a causal pathway.

\paragraph{Strength driver.}
The strength-driver record gives a second finite consistency check on the direction of the cross-organism signal.  It computed $15$ organisms out of $16$ requested, again with minimum $500$ proteins per organism and $5$ folds.  The direction agreement count was $15$ out of $15$, so the direction agreement rate was $1.0$.  The Spearman association between total synonymous selection strength and $f_3$-stress strength was $0.7071428571428572$.  The finite readout can be summarized as
$$
\begin{aligned}
\rho_{\mathrm{Spearman}} &= 0.7071428571428572,\\
\frac{N_{\mathrm{agree}}}{N_{\mathrm{direction}}} &= \frac{15}{15}=1.0.
\end{aligned}
$$
This supports the narrower interpretation that the observed $f_3$-stress strength is aligned with total synonymous selection strength under the declared fixed-direction rule.  It does not by itself turn the residual coordinate into a universal biological law.

\paragraph{Boundary of the claim.}
The supported claim is a cross-layer relation at bounded translation-readout scope: a rank-powered multi-organism panel reports that the $f_3$-stress residual generalizes across $15$ tested organisms, and supporting translation-proximal contacts show that the coordinate can be placed on wobble-weight and ribosome-occupancy readout surfaces.  The claim is not that $f_3$-stress is a protein-abundance determinant, a folding determinant, a physical-admissibility test, a molecular-function rule, a phenotype rule, or a mechanism.  None of those higher layers follows from codon usage, tAI or stAI weights, modeled translation-readout survival, or position-controlled ribosome occupancy unless a separate layer-matched contact supplies the relevant measurement and the same-row controlled statistic is computed.  If $f_3$-paired wobble weights are symmetric, or if a requested panel is rank-saturated, the correct status is null or needs more data rather than positive translation survival.
