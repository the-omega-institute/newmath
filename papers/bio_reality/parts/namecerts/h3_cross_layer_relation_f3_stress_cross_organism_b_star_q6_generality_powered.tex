\subsection{Rank-powered cross-organism readout for the $f_3$-stress residual}
\label{sec:bior-f3-stress-cross-organism-generality}

\paragraph{Finite carrier.}
This record isolates the $f_3$-stress coordinate on the synonymous-codon side of the amino-acid quotient and asks whether that coordinate remains visible in a translation-facing readout once the panel is large enough not to be explained by rank saturation. The carrier is a finite organism-by-codon readout surface restricted to synonymous fibers paired by the third-position involution $f_3$, with paired codon usage, wobble-weight readouts such as tAI or stAI, and translation-efficiency or ribosome-occupancy measurements when those rows are actually joined. The coordinate is not a raw codon count. It is the residual coordinate obtained after projecting away amino-acid composition, written informally as $q-P_\tau q$, and then reading the antisymmetric component along the $f_3$-paired codon fibers.

\paragraph{Quotient geometry.}
The finite quotient audit gives the combinatorial background for this residual. The checked quotient has $\pi_M$ size $10$, quotient face vector $(10,14,6,1)$, and ambient $M_f$ vector $(20,38,26,8,1)$. Thus the codon-side object used here is already compressed through an amino-acid quotient rather than treated as a full $61$-codon free surface. In the residual-basis finite contact, $3$ organisms were loaded, namely human, yeast, and \emph{E. coli} K-12, and $9$ $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinates were computed. Seven of those nine coordinates exceeded the declared variance threshold $0.01$, giving nonzero fraction $7/9=0.7777777777777778$. The residual list was
$$
\begin{aligned}
&K\_AAA,\; Arg\_AGR,\; Ile\_AUA,\; Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR,\; Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG,\\
&Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY,\; Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG,\; Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY,\; f3\_stress\_pair\_antisymmetric.
\end{aligned}
$$
This establishes that the $f_3$-stress coordinate is a nontrivial finite residual coordinate, but that fact alone does not yet say that it is translated, selected, functional, or mechanistic.

\paragraph{Rank gate.}
The underpowered modeled-tAI contact on $3$ organisms is explicitly not used as positive generality evidence, even though it contains a nonzero bounded coupling matrix. In that smaller contact, the codon-count space had size $61$, the coupling threshold was $0.01$, all $81$ entries of the $9\times 9$ modeled-tAI matrix exceeded the threshold, and the maximum absolute entry was $3801961.031607896$. Those numbers show that the bounded readout can be computed, but the same finite record labels the $3$-organism setting as insufficient for powered survival. The powered modeled-tAI readout instead used $40$ organisms, a $9\times 9$ survival matrix, $R^2_{QT}=0.9003849000036579$, residualized control rank $23$, residual degrees of freedom $17$, and residualized coordinate rank $9$. The control surface contained $24$ control columns and used a positive partial-$R^2$ threshold of $10^{-12}$ with a degeneracy threshold $0.999999$. These numbers are the relevant rank check: the $f_3$-stress question is only meaningful after the residual surface retains rank beyond the controls.

\paragraph{Generality statistic.}
The powered cross-organism generality run for this exact claim had run id $16528255bb3b$ and status passed. It requested $16$ organisms, retained $15$ real organisms, and tested $15$ organisms after enforcing at least $500$ proteins per organism. The test used $5$ folds and $120$ permutations under the deterministic seed recorded as $\mathrm{sha256{:}b\_star\_q6\_f3\_stress\_cross\_organism\_powered{:}deterministic}$. The real threshold was $8$, and the resulting call was $f3\_stress\_generalizes$. In compact form, the finite verdict is
$$
\begin{aligned}
N_{\mathrm{requested}}&=16, & N_{\mathrm{real}}&=15, & N_{\mathrm{tested}}&=15,\\
F&=5, & B_{\mathrm{perm}}&=120, & N_{\min\,\mathrm{proteins}}&=500,\\
\theta_{\mathrm{real}}&=8, & \mathrm{call}&=\mathrm{f3\_stress\_generalizes}.&&
\end{aligned}
$$
The content of the call is narrow: the $f_3$-stress residual has cross-organism predictive persistence on the tested finite readout surface. It is not a claim that all organisms share one direction, one mechanism, or one downstream biological consequence.

\paragraph{Wobble asymmetry condition.}
The biological interpretation is conditioned on a symmetry break in codon-paired wobble weights. If the paired weights satisfy $w_c=w_{c^{f_3}}$ throughout a fiber, then the $f_3$-stress residual remains a codon-usage or code-topology boundary coordinate. It becomes translation-facing only where the paired codon readout distinguishes the two sides of the $f_3$ pair. A separate local ribosome-occupancy finite contact supports the plausibility of such a boundary without promoting it beyond translation: $5754$ genes were joined between ribosome occupancy and ordered CDS rows, with no length mismatches after the length check. The position-confound control used the supplied $5'$ and $3'$ codon-index windows and amino-acid bins, and the null shuffled synonymous labels only within the same bin. With $200$ requested permutations per family, all $9$ families had at least $200$ permutations, the description-length parameter was $\lambda_{DL}=0.01$, $DL=2$, and the families passing the gate were $K\_AAA$, $Arg\_AGR$, and $f3\_stress$.

\paragraph{Strength driver.}
A second powered finite run tested whether the observed $f_3$-stress strength tracks total synonymous selection strength rather than an arbitrary direction label. That run had status passed, computed $15$ organisms from $16$ requested, used $5$ folds, enforced the same minimum of $500$ proteins per organism, and found Spearman correlation $0.7071428571428572$ between total synonymous selection and $f_3$-stress strength. Direction agreement was $15/15$, hence rate $1.0$, with the Sulfolobus negative-alignment case recorded explicitly. This supports a strength-driver reading: the coordinate generalizes as a rank-powered translation-facing residual when the synonymous-selection surface is strong and directionally aligned, not as an unrestricted universal biological law.

\paragraph{Local boundary.}
The present chapter therefore makes one finite claim. On the tested finite panels, after amino-acid quotienting and rank controls, the $f_3$-stress residual is not merely a decorative coordinate: it generalizes across $15$ tested organisms under the powered criterion, and it is compatible with wobble-broken translation-facing readouts. The claim stops there. No protein abundance, folded structure, physical admissibility, molecular function, phenotype, causal mechanism, or universal biological principle follows from these rows. Any such statement would require its own layer-matched contact and its own finite statistic. In particular, modeled tAI or stAI weights are bounded translation annotations, not direct observations of ribosome motion; ribosome occupancy is a translation-layer readout, not a folding or function assay; and a rank-powered $S^{QT}$ entry does not by itself create a protein-output or mechanism claim.
