\paragraph{Wobble asymmetry and strength.}
The $f_3$-stress coordinate is interpreted as a translation-adjacent distinction only where paired codons do not remain equivalent under the wobble-weight surface. If the paired weights satisfy $w_c=w_{c^{f_3}}$ across the relevant fiber, the coordinate is only a codon-usage residual. When the weights break that symmetry and the rank condition is adequate, the coordinate can be tested against a translation readout. The strength-driver test provides a finite companion check: across $15$ computed organisms, direction agreement was $15/15$, hence rate $1.0$, and the Spearman association between total synonymous selection strength and $f_3$-stress strength was $0.7071428571428572$. The recorded interpretation was that $f_3$-stress strength is explained by total synonymous selection strength together with fixed-direction alignment, including the recorded negative alignment and direction for Sulfolobus.

\paragraph{Claim admitted.}
The admitted claim is therefore the following finite statement. In a rank-powered multi-organism panel, the amino-acid-quotient residual $f_3$-stress coordinate generalizes across $15$ retained organisms under a $5$-fold, $120$-permutation protocol with real threshold $8$, and it is coherent with bounded translation-readout surfaces only when the paired wobble weights distinguish $c$ from $c^{f_3}$. The claim is cross-layer in the limited sense that a codon-topology residual survives contact with translation-adjacent readouts under the declared finite controls. It is not a claim that codon topology alone measures translation dynamics.
