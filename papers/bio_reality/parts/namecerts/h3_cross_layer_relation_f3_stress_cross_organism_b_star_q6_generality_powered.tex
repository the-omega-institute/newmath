\subsection{Cross-organism generality of the $f_3$-stress coordinate}
\label{sec:bioreality-f3-stress-cross-organism-generality}

\paragraph{Finite surface.}
The readout surface for this record is the organism-by-codon panel restricted to synonymous fibers paired by the $f_3$ involution.  The coordinate under test is the amino-acid-quotient residual
$ q_{f_3}=q-P_{\tau}q $,
read on the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual family after amino-acid projection has removed the coarse amino-acid composition.  The finite quotient geometry used by the record has $M$-vector counts $(20,38,26,8,1)$, quotient counts $(10,14,6,1)$, and $|\pi_M|=10$.  Thus the coordinate is not a free biological variable: it is a residual direction available only after collapsing codons along the amino-acid quotient and then asking whether the remaining $f_3$-paired asymmetry survives a separate translation-level readout.

\paragraph{Residual basis before promotion.}
The residual-basis computation contains nine $B^{*}_{Q6}$ coordinates:
$K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $Leu_{CUN\,vs\,UUR}$, $Leu_{UUA\,vs\,UUG}$, $Ser_{UCR\,vs\,AGY}$, $Ser_{UCA\,vs\,UCG}$, $Thr_{ACR\,vs\,ACY}$, and $f_3$-stress.  In the three-organism contact panel, seven of these nine residuals are nonzero above the stated variance threshold, giving nonzero fraction $7/9=0.7777777777777778$.  That finite contact uses human, budding yeast, and \textit{E. coli}; it is a coordinate and codon-count contact, not a population estimate and not a translation experiment.  The residual coordinate can therefore enter the later test only as a candidate explanatory row in a controlled matrix.

\paragraph{Rank-powered modeled translation readout.}
A separate forty-organism modeled-tAI panel supplies the powered translation-readout surface.  The design has nine $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual columns and nine modeled readout columns, with controls residualized in a $Z$ matrix of $24$ columns and rank $23$.  After the control projection, the residual degrees of freedom are $17$ and the residualized $X$ rank is $9$.  The powered matrix has
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{QT} &= 0.9003849000036579,\\
\operatorname{rank}(Z) &= 23,\\
\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde X) &= 9,\\
\operatorname{df}_{\mathrm{resid}} &= 17.
\end{aligned}
$$
The corresponding three-organism modeled-tAI surface is explicitly not promoted to survival: it has only $n=3$ organisms, although all $81$ modeled entries exceed the bounded-contact threshold $0.01$ and the largest absolute entry is $3801961.031607896$.  The powered call therefore rests on the forty-organism rank condition, not on the saturated three-row fit.

\paragraph{Cross-organism generality call.}
The finite cross-organism experiment for this record requested $16$ organisms and obtained $15$ usable real organisms, each with at least $500$ proteins.  The held-out procedure used $5$ folds and $120$ permutations, with real-threshold $8$.  The recorded status is passed and the call is $f_3$-stress generalizes.  In symbols, the operative support is the finite inequality
$$
\begin{aligned}
N_{\mathrm{real}} &= 15,\\
N_{\mathrm{threshold}} &= 8,\\
N_{\mathrm{folds}} &= 5,\\
N_{\mathrm{perm}} &= 120,
\end{aligned}
$$
paired with the rank-powered modeled-translation surface above.  This is the point at which the $f_3$ residual moves from a bounded codon-topology distinction to a cross-layer relation: the same residual direction is tested across organisms on a translation-readout surface that is not rank-saturated.

\paragraph{Wobble asymmetry and local occupancy contact.}
The relevant mechanism-facing discriminator is not the residual alone, but the residual together with broken symmetry in codon-paired wobble weights.  A position-controlled ribosome-occupancy analysis joined $5754$ genes after length checking, with zero length mismatches from $6013$ ordered CDS genes and $5754$ occupancy genes.  Its permutation null requested $200$ within-position-bin permutations for each of nine families, and all nine families had at least $200$ permutations.  The local gate used description length $DL=2$, $C_H>0$, and $\lambda_{DL}=0.01$; the families passing all gates were $K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, and $f_3$-stress.  This does not by itself identify a molecular mechanism, but it supplies a same-window occupancy-shaped contact showing that the $f_3$ row is not merely an algebraic artifact of codon naming.

\paragraph{Strength driver.}
The strength-driver record further reports that the $f_3$-stress strength is aligned with total synonymous selection strength across the powered organism set.  The direction agreement is $15/15$, hence rate $1.0$, and the Spearman correlation between total synonymous-selection strength and $f_3$ strength is $0.7071428571428572$.  The finite summary is
$$
\begin{aligned}
\rho_{\mathrm{Spearman}} &= 0.7071428571428572,\\
\frac{N_{\mathrm{agree}}}{N_{\mathrm{tested}}} &= \frac{15}{15}=1.0.
\end{aligned}
$$
Together with the generality call, this supports the interpretation that the coordinate is strongest where a broader synonymous-selection signal has a fixed-direction alignment with the $f_3$ residual.

\paragraph{Local verdict.}
The ready claim is therefore narrow.  In a rank-powered multi-organism panel, the amino-acid-quotient $f_3$-stress residual has a positive cross-layer readout when paired with wobble-weight asymmetry and translation-facing measurements or modeled translation weights.  The evidence is finite-row, controlled, and rank-aware: $15$ real organisms pass the cross-organism generality procedure; the powered modeled-tAI matrix uses $40$ organisms with residualized rank $9$ and residual degrees of freedom $17$; and the position-controlled occupancy contact independently keeps $f_3$-stress among the families passing the $\lambda_{DL}=0.01$ gate.  The result does not assert that $f_3$-stress universally controls translation.  It says that, under these finite readout surfaces, $f_3$-stress is a codon-topology-to-translation distinction when the paired wobble weights break $f_3$ symmetry and the matrix is not rank-saturated.

\paragraph{Boundary of interpretation.}
No claim about translation realization beyond the stated readout, folded structure, physical admissibility, molecular function, biological mechanism, phenotype, or universality follows from this record alone.  Modeled tAI or stAI weights are bounded translation annotations, not observed ribosome dynamics.  Ribosome occupancy and translation-efficiency contacts are readout contacts, not protein-output, folding, function, or physical-assay contacts.  A higher-layer claim would require a separate matched contact and its own controlled statistic after the same-row translation-readout step has passed.
