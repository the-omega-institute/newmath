\subsection{Rank-powered cross-organism generality of the $f_3$-stress coordinate}
\label{sec:bioreality-f3-stress-cross-organism-generality}

\paragraph{Finite carrier.}
This chapter records a finite cross-layer test for the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ $f_3$-stress residual coordinate.  The carrier consists of organism-indexed synonymous codon readouts restricted to $f_3$-paired codon fibers, together with paired codon usage, wobble-weight surfaces, and translation-adjacent readouts.  The coordinate is not an amino-acid composition effect: it is formed after quotienting away the amino-acid projection, so the tested residual has the form $q-P_{\tau}q$ on the synonymous side.  The finite quotient geometry used by the surrounding packet has $M$-vector counts
$$
\begin{aligned}
M_f&=(20,38,26,8,1),\\
\#\pi_M&=10,\\
f_{\mathrm{quot}}&=(10,14,6,1).
\end{aligned}
$$
The residual-basis contact computed $9$ $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates.  Seven of those nine coordinates were nonzero above the variance threshold $0.01$, giving nonzero fraction $7/9=0.7777777777777778$ on the finite three-organism codon-count contact.  The coordinate list included $K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, two leucine coordinates, two serine coordinates, $Thr_{ACR}$ versus $Thr_{ACY}$, and the antisymmetric $f_3$-stress pair.  This establishes that the $f_3$-stress axis is a genuine finite synonymous residual coordinate before any translation-layer interpretation is attempted.

\paragraph{Powered readout criterion.}
The positive call in this chapter uses the rank-powered cross-organism run with identifier $b\_star\_q6\_f3\_stress\_cross\_organism\_powered$.  The run requested $16$ organisms and obtained $15$ real organisms satisfying the data filters, with at least $500$ proteins per organism.  It used $5$ folds and $120$ permutations, and the declared real-count threshold was $8$.  The finite outcome was
$$
\begin{aligned}
N_{\mathrm{requested}}&=16,\\
N_{\mathrm{real}}&=15,\\
N_{\mathrm{tested}}&=15,\\
N_{\mathrm{folds}}&=5,\\
N_{\mathrm{perm}}&=120,\\
N_{\mathrm{threshold}}&=8.
\end{aligned}
$$
The recorded call was $f_3\text{-stress generalizes}$, and the run status was passed.  Thus the finite-row result is stronger than a three-organism contact: it is a rank-powered panel in which the $f_3$-stress coordinate survives a multi-organism generality test rather than being an artifact of a saturated tiny matrix.

\paragraph{Why rank matters.}
The same packet contains a useful null boundary.  A three-organism modeled-tAI contact produced a bounded coupling matrix, but it was explicitly not a powered survival result.  That contact had only $3$ organisms, while the break condition required at least $40$ matched species for the powered modeled-readout claim.  Its matrix had $81$ entries above threshold $0.01$ and maximum absolute entry $3801961.031607896$, but the row itself marked the scope as underpowered and blocked promotion to ribosome profiling, proteomics, or protein realization.  By contrast, the powered modeled-tAI panel used $40$ organisms, a $9\times 9$ $S^{QT}$ matrix, residualized $24$ control columns with $\operatorname{rank}(Z)=23$, left residual degrees of freedom $17$, and had $\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde X)=9$.  Its total controlled readout fit was
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{QT}&=0.9003849000036579,\\
\operatorname{rank}(Z)&=23,\\
\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde X)&=9,\\
df_{\mathrm{resid}}&=17.
\end{aligned}
$$
The cross-organism $f_3$-stress result should be read against this distinction: bounded coupling is not enough; a nondegenerate finite panel is required before the residual coordinate can be treated as a cross-layer translation-readout distinction.

\paragraph{Wobble asymmetry condition.}
The biological reason to test $f_3$-stress is that an $f_3$-paired synonymous codon pair can be invisible to translation only when its wobble weights remain symmetric.  The tested distinction therefore requires a break of the form $w_c\neq w_{c^{f_3}}$ before the residual can enter a translation-readout statistic.  If the paired wobble weights are symmetric, the coordinate remains a codon-usage or code-read residual boundary; it is not a dynamical biological variable.  The finite contact can therefore be summarized as the implication
$$
\begin{aligned}
&\operatorname{rank\ sufficient}\ \wedge\ (w_c\neq w_{c^{f_3}})\ \wedge\ S^{QT}_{f_3,j}>0\\
&\hspace{1.0cm}\Longrightarrow\ \text{bounded cross-layer codon-topology-to-translation readout},
\end{aligned}
$$
with the reverse failure modes treated as null: symmetric wobble weights, saturated rank, or absence of controlled readout survival prevent promotion.

\paragraph{Independent finite-row consistency.}
A second powered row tested whether $f_3$-stress strength tracks total synonymous selection strength.  That run again requested $16$ organisms and computed $15$ real organisms with at least $500$ proteins per organism and $5$ folds.  It recorded a Spearman association
$$
\begin{aligned}
\rho_{\mathrm{syn\ strength},f_3\mathrm{\ strength}}&=0.7071428571428572,\\
\frac{N_{\mathrm{direction\ agreement}}}{N_{\mathrm{direction\ denominator}}}&=\frac{15}{15}=1.0.
\end{aligned}
$$
The same row records the Sulfolobus negative-alignment case as true, so the finite result is not phrased as a universal positive sign.  It is a strength-and-alignment statement: the $f_3$-stress magnitude follows total synonymous selection strength while the direction is controlled by the fixed alignment convention of the coordinate.  This supports the generality call without turning it into a universal biological direction law.

\paragraph{Ribosome-occupancy contact.}
A separate position-controlled ribosome-occupancy row gives a narrower same-layer consistency check.  It joined $5754$ ordered CDS genes with ribosome-occupancy genes, with zero length mismatches out of $5754$ joined genes.  The null used within-position-bin label permutations; all $9$ synonymous families had at least $200$ permutations requested.  The description-length gate used $\lambda_{DL}=0.01$ with $DL=2$ and required positive coordinate-support agreement.  The families passing all gates were $K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, and $f_3\text{-stress}$.  In symbols, the row records
$$
\begin{aligned}
N_{\mathrm{joined}}&=5754,\\
N_{\mathrm{length\ mismatch}}&=0,\\
N_{\mathrm{families}}&=9,\\
N_{\mathrm{perm\ requested}}&=200,\\
\lambda_{DL}&=0.01,\\
DL&=2.
\end{aligned}
$$
This is still descriptive and position-confound-controlled, not causal.  Its value here is that the same $f_3$-stress coordinate appears among the families passing the local occupancy separation gate under codon-position and amino-acid-bin controls.

\paragraph{Local boundary.}
The finite evidence licenses only the following statement.  In a nondegenerate multi-organism panel, the amino-acid-quotient $f_3$-stress residual coordinate generalizes as a bounded cross-layer codon-topology-to-translation-readout distinction when paired wobble weights break $f_3$ symmetry and controlled rank is sufficient.  It does not by itself assert protein abundance, protein folding, physical admissibility, function, phenotype, localization, stability, post-translational state, molecular mechanism, adaptation, causation, or a universal biological law.  Those claims require their own matched measurements and their own controlled statistics.  The chapter therefore treats the passed $15$-organism generality row as evidence for a finite translation-readout boundary, not as evidence for any higher biological layer.
