\paragraph{Interpretation.}
The supported statement is narrow.  In the finite records above, the $f_3$-stress residual coordinate survives as a cross-organism codon-topology-to-translation-readout distinction when the panel is rank-powered and when the readout route includes wobble-weight asymmetry or a measured occupancy surface.  The claim is therefore a cross-layer relation between a synonymous residual coordinate and bounded translation-layer readouts.  It is not a statement that $f_3$-stress is itself a dynamical biological variable in every context.  If the paired wobble weights satisfy $w_c=w_{c^{f_3}}$, or if the fitted surface is rank-saturated, the same coordinate remains a codon-usage residual and the translation-readout promotion fails.

\paragraph{Local boundary.}
No translation mechanism, protein abundance, folded structure, physical admissibility, molecular function, phenotype, adaptation theorem, or biological universality follows from this chapter alone.  Such claims require their own same-row contacts and statistics.  The present row licenses only the finite, controlled statement that $f_3$-stress generalizes across the tested organism panel as a residual coordinate associated with bounded translation-readout surfaces under the recorded design.
