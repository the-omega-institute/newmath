\subsection{Rank-powered cross-organism readout for the $f_3$-stress coordinate}
\label{sec:h3-cross-layer-relation-f3-stress-cross-organism-b-star-q6-generality-powered}

\paragraph{Claim.}
This record isolates the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ coordinate called $f_3$-stress after quotienting away amino-acid identity.  The finite object is not a protein-level assertion.  It is a codon-topology residual tested against organism-scoped translation readouts, with the positive claim restricted to the following situation: after amino-acid projection, the residual coordinate survives as a translation-readout predictor across a rank-powered organism panel, and the relevant paired codon weights are not invariant under the $f_3$ pairing.  When the paired weights remain symmetric, or when the design matrix is rank-saturated, the same coordinate remains only a codon-usage residual.

\paragraph{Finite row basis.}
The quotient geometry used here has $|\pi_M|=10$, quotient $f$-vector $(10,14,6,1)$, and ambient $M_f$ vector $(20,38,26,8,1)$.  The amino-acid quotient residual experiment used three organism codon-count contacts, namely human, budding yeast, and \emph{E. coli} K-12, and computed nine $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates:
$$
\begin{aligned}
&K\_{AAA},\ \operatorname{Arg}\_{AGR},\ \operatorname{Ile}\_{AUA},\ \operatorname{Leu}\_{CUN\,vs\,UUR},\ \operatorname{Leu}\_{UUA\,vs\,UUG},\\
&\operatorname{Ser}\_{UCR\,vs\,AGY},\ \operatorname{Ser}\_{UCA\,vs\,UCG},\ \operatorname{Thr}\_{ACR\,vs\,ACY},\ f_3\text{-stress}.
\end{aligned}
$$
Seven of the nine residual coordinates were nonzero above the variance threshold $0.01$, giving nonzero fraction $7/9=0.7777777777777778$.  This first row set is only a finite contact with codon-count tables; it does not by itself measure ribosome motion, protein output, folded structure, phenotype, or mechanism.

\paragraph{Powered translation-readout surface.}
The rank-powered modeled-tAI surface used $40$ organisms and the same nine residual coordinates as both codon-side and modeled-readout coordinates.  After residualizing $24$ control columns, the control rank was $23$, leaving residual degrees of freedom $17$.  The residualized $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ design had rank $9$, and the computed $S^{QT}$ matrix had shape $9\times 9$ with $R^2_{QT}=0.9003849000036579$.  The degenerate-fit cutoff was $0.999999$, so this row is not an all-ones rank artifact.  Its interpretation is bounded: the readout is modeled per-codon tAI from tRNA gene-copy counts and dos Reis wobble weights, projected onto the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates.  It is not ribosome profiling, measured elongation speed, proteomics, protein folding, or a direct gene-level mechanism.

\paragraph{Three-organism coupling row and rank refusal.}
The smaller modeled-tAI contact over human, yeast, and \emph{E. coli} K-12 computed codon adaptation weights for all $61$ sense codons in each organism, with zero raw-weight fallback count $0$ for each organism.  It also found all $81$ entries of the $9\times 9$ modeled coupling surface above threshold $0.01$, with maximum absolute entry $3801961.031607896$.  That row is useful as a bounded coupling contact, but it is explicitly underpowered: with only $3$ organisms, it cannot support a powered survival statement.  The powered claim therefore depends on the $40$-organism row and on nondegenerate rank, not on the small all-contact surface.

\paragraph{Cross-organism generality test.}
The direct generality experiment for this claim requested $16$ organisms, obtained $15$ real organism panels, and tested $15$ of them with at least $500$ proteins per organism.  The experiment used $5$ folds and $120$ permutations under the deterministic seed \texttt{sha256:b\_star\_q6\_f3\_stress\_cross\_organism\_powered:deterministic}.  The declared real-organism threshold was $8$, and the recorded call was \texttt{f3\_stress\_generalizes}.  Thus the finite verdict is a cross-organism readout statement for $f_3$-stress, not a universal law about all organisms or all translation systems.

\paragraph{Strength and direction.}
A companion strength-driver row computed $15$ organisms out of $16$ requested and found direction agreement count $15$ over denominator $15$, hence direction agreement rate $1.0$.  The Spearman association between total synonymous selection strength and $f_3$-stress strength was $0.7071428571428572$.  The same row records a negative alignment and direction flag for \emph{Sulfolobus}, so the chapter should not be read as asserting a single naive sign rule.  The supported statement is narrower: the observed $f_3$-stress strength follows total synonymous selection strength multiplied by a fixed-direction alignment in the finite organism panel.

\paragraph{Ribosome-occupancy contact.}
A separate ribosome-occupancy analysis joined $5754$ genes after length checking, with $5754$ gene identifiers overlapping between ordered CDS records and the occupancy file, and length mismatch count $0$.  The null used within-position-bin synonymous-label permutations, requested $200$ permutations, and computed nulls for all $9$ families.  With description-length penalty $\lambda_{DL}=0.01$, $DL=2$, and the rule $C_H>0$, the families passing all gates were $K\_{AAA}$, $\operatorname{Arg}\_{AGR}$, and $f_3$-stress.  This contact supports a local position-controlled occupancy separation for the named families, but it remains descriptive and position-confound controlled rather than causal.

\paragraph{Downstream partition check.}
The ordered residual decomposition rows keep this chapter from overpromoting the coordinate.  In the organism-level ordered partition, yeast had $T=0.11271394500088064$, $S=0.019627420469677935$, and unexplained fraction $0.8676586345294415$; human had $T=0.006011907792542833$, $S=0.006163991439034291$, and unexplained fraction $0.9736442326306781$; \emph{E. coli} K-12 MG1655 had $T=0.00035964766234144823$, $S=0.005357973825835613$, and unexplained fraction $0.9942823785118229$; zebrafish had $T=0.02436809579891462$, skipped $S$, and unexplained fraction $0.9756319042010854$.  The direction-aware table similarly leaves large unexplained fractions: $0.8676586345294415$ for yeast, $0.9942823785118229$ for \emph{E. coli}, $0.9798666209022441$ for human, and $0.9756319042010854$ for zebrafish.  These values show that the $f_3$-stress result is not an all-layer explanation of protein state or organism phenotype.

\paragraph{Verdict and local boundary.}
The finite-row verdict is that $f_3$-stress is a rank-powered cross-organism codon-topology-to-translation-readout distinction under wobble-weight symmetry breaking.  It is supported by the $15$-organism generality call, the $40$-organism nondegenerate modeled-tAI surface, the $15/15$ direction-agreement strength row, and the separate ribosome-occupancy contact in which $f_3$-stress passes the within-bin null and description-length gate.  No stronger biological conclusion follows here.  In particular, this chapter does not claim protein abundance, folded structure, localization, stability, post-translational state, physical admissibility, biological function, mechanism, phenotype, or universality.  Those claims would require layer-matched empirical contacts and their own controlled statistics after the translation-readout step has survived on the same row family.
