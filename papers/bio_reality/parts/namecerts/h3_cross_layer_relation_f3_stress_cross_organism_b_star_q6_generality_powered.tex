\subsection{Cross-organism survival of the $f_3$-stress residual}
\label{sec:biorality-f3-stress-cross-organism-generality}

\paragraph{Finite carrier.}
This record concerns the finite organism-by-codon surface obtained after amino-acid projection.  The coordinate under test is the $f_3$-stress residual, written schematically as $q_{f_3}=q-P_{\tau}q$ after the amino-acid component has been removed.  The biological question is deliberately narrow: whether this residual coordinate has repeatable translation-layer readback across organisms when synonymous codon pairs related by the $f_3$ involution are not assigned equal wobble weights.  The row set is not a population theorem over life; it is a bounded panel with codon usage, modeled tAI or stAI wobble weights, and translation-efficiency or translation-proximal readouts where the finite joining procedure permits it.

\paragraph{Coordinate basis and quotient size.}
The upstream quotient calculation supplies a concrete finite shape.  The quotient surface has $\pi_M$ size $10$ and quotient $f$-vector $(10,14,6,1)$, while the corresponding $M_f$ vector is $(20,38,26,8,1)$.  In the codon residual panel, nine $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ synonymous coordinates are computed, including $K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, two leucine contrasts, two serine contrasts, $Thr_{ACR}$ versus $Thr_{ACY}$, and $f_3$-stress.  Across the three initial organisms, seven of nine residual coordinates exceed the variance threshold $0.01$, giving nonzero fraction $7/9=0.7777777777777778$.  This establishes that the coordinate is not merely a formal label on the finite codon table; it has nontrivial residual variation after amino-acid quotienting.

\paragraph{Rank-powered translation readback.}
The powered modeled-tAI panel contains $40$ organisms and a $9\times 9$ survival surface.  After residualizing against $24$ control columns, the control matrix has rank $23$ and residual degrees of freedom $17$.  The residualized coordinate matrix has rank $9$, and the reported controlled translation-readout fit is $R^2_{QT}=0.9003849000036579$, below the stated degeneracy threshold $0.999999$.  Thus the powered modeled-readout calculation is not the rejected three-row saturated case.  The earlier bounded three-organism tAI computation is retained only as contact evidence: it has $81$ entries above threshold $0.01$ and maximum absolute modeled coupling $3801961.031607896$, but because $n=3$ it is explicitly not counted as powered survival.

\paragraph{Cross-organism generality row.}
The finite generality test for this claim requested $16$ organisms and retained $15$ real organisms after the data filters.  Each retained organism has at least $500$ proteins.  The test used $5$ folds and $120$ permutations, with a real-organism threshold of $8$.  The recorded call is \texttt{f3\_stress\_generalizes}, and the run status is \texttt{passed}.  The deterministic seed is \texttt{sha256:b\_star\_q6\_f3\_stress\_cross\_organism\_powered:deterministic}.  In finite-row terms, the positive statement is therefore:
$$
\begin{aligned}
&n_{\mathrm{requested}}=16,\qquad n_{\mathrm{real}}=n_{\mathrm{tested}}=15,\\
&k_{\mathrm{folds}}=5,\qquad N_{\mathrm{perm}}=120,\qquad n_{\mathrm{threshold}}=8,\\
&\mathrm{call}=\mathrm{f3\_stress\_generalizes}.
\end{aligned}
$$
This is the chapter's main finite-row support: in the admitted panel, the $f_3$-stress coordinate passes the declared cross-organism generality procedure.

\paragraph{Strength driver.}
A companion powered row explains the magnitude of the $f_3$-stress signal by total synonymous selection strength together with fixed-direction alignment.  It retained $15$ computed organisms out of $16$ requested, again with at least $500$ proteins per organism and $5$ folds.  The direction agreement count is $15$ out of $15$, so the direction agreement rate is $1.0$.  The Spearman association between total synonymous selection and $f_3$-stress strength is $0.7071428571428572$.  The row also records a negative alignment and direction case for \textit{Sulfolobus}, which is important: the result is not a claim that every organism has the same signed biological effect.  It is a claim that the finite panel's strength pattern is organized by selection strength and alignment rather than by an unconstrained universal sign.

\paragraph{Position-resolved contact.}
A separate ribosome-occupancy row gives translation-proximal contact without promoting the result to protein output.  The joined ordered-CDS and occupancy surface has $5754$ overlapping genes, $5754$ joined genes after length checking, and zero length mismatches.  It uses the file-provided $5'$ and $3'$ windows, demeans occupancy within exact position and amino-acid bins, and computes nulls by shuffling synonymous labels within the same bin.  All nine families have at least $200$ within-bin permutations.  Under the gate $C_H>0$ and description-length value $DL=2$ with $\lambda_{DL}=0.01$, the families passing all gates are $K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, and $f_3$-stress.  This supports a bounded statement about local ribosome-occupancy separation under position controls; it does not by itself identify a causal ribosome mechanism.

\paragraph{Interpretation.}
The ready claim is that $f_3$-stress behaves as a cross-layer codon-topology-to-translation distinction on the finite admitted surfaces: it is nontrivial after amino-acid quotienting, rank-powered under the $40$-organism modeled-tAI readout, repeated across $15$ real organisms in the generality test, and consistent with a translation-proximal occupancy contact under position-bin controls.  The wobble condition is essential.  If paired codons have symmetric weights, then the coordinate remains a codon residual and should not be read as a translation variable.  If the residualized design is rank-saturated, as in rejected all-$1.0$ three-row fits, the correct verdict is null or data-insufficient rather than positive survival.

\paragraph{Local boundary.}
The result does not assert protein abundance, folded structure, molecular function, localization, post-translational state, physical admissibility, phenotype, mechanism, causation, or a universal biological law.  Those layers require their own same-row measurements and controls.  In particular, tAI or stAI weights are modeled codon-adaptation readouts, not observed ribosome footprints; ribosome occupancy is a translation-proximal contact, not protein structure; and matched mRNA abundance is a control for translation-efficiency residualization, not a translation readout by itself.  The present chapter is therefore closed only for the finite cross-organism $f_3$-stress generality claim under the stated rank, wobble, control, and row-admission conditions.
