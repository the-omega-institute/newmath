\subsection{A finite cross-organism test for the $f_3$-stress residual}

\paragraph{Finite carrier.}
The object tested here is the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinate called $f_3$-stress, evaluated only after amino-acid projection has been removed.  The finite carrier is not a universal codon law.  It is a readout table whose rows are organisms or organism-matched coding sequences, whose codon columns are collapsed to $f_3$-paired synonymous fibers, and whose translation-side columns are bounded by tAI, stAI, or explicitly supplied translation-efficiency measurements.  In the supporting residual-basis contact, the cross-organism codon-count table contains three named organisms, Homo sapiens, Saccharomyces cerevisiae, and Escherichia coli K-12, and the computed $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ coordinate list has nine entries: $K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $Leu_{CUN\,vs\,UUR}$, $Leu_{UUA\,vs\,UUG}$, $Ser_{UCR\,vs\,AGY}$, $Ser_{UCA\,vs\,UCG}$, $Thr_{ACR\,vs\,ACY}$, and $f_3$-stress.  Seven of the nine residual entries are nonzero above the stated variance threshold $0.01$, giving a nonzero fraction of $0.7777777777777778$.  The ambient quotient check is also finite: the $M_f$ vector is $(20,38,26,8,1)$, the quotient $f$-vector is $(10,14,6,1)$, and $|\pi_M|=10$.

\paragraph{Tested statistic.}
The cross-organism generality test asks whether the $f_3$-stress coordinate continues to carry translation-proximal information beyond the amino-acid quotient and simpler baseline structure.  The powered run requested $16$ organisms, obtained $15$ real organisms, and tested all $15$ with at least $500$ proteins per organism.  The procedure used $5$ folds and $120$ permutations, with a real-organism threshold of $8$.  The recorded call is $f_3\text{-stress\_generalizes}$ and the status is passed.  In compact form, the finite decision can be written as
$$
\begin{aligned}
N_{\mathrm{tested}} &= 15,\\
N_{\mathrm{threshold}} &= 8,\\
K_{\mathrm{fold}} &= 5,\\
K_{\mathrm{perm}} &= 120,\\
\mathrm{call} &= \mathrm{f3\_stress\_generalizes}.
\end{aligned}
$$
This is a rank-powered finite-panel statement.  It says that the residual coordinate survives the declared cross-organism test often enough to pass the stated threshold; it does not say that the sign, magnitude, or biological effect is invariant across all organisms.

\paragraph{Wobble asymmetry and local ribosome occupancy.}
The biological reason this coordinate is not treated as a bare codon-usage artifact is the required break in $f_3$-paired wobble symmetry.  If paired codons have equal wobble weights, then the residual is only a codon-usage or code-read boundary.  In the supplied position-controlled ribosome-occupancy run, $5754$ genes overlap between the occupancy and ordered-CDS files, all $5754$ pass the length check, and there are $0$ length mismatches.  The null is built from within-position-bin synonymous-label permutations; all $9$ families have at least $200$ permutations.  The gate uses $\lambda_{DL}=0.01$, $DL=2$, and the rule $C_H>0$.  Exactly three families pass all gates: $K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, and $f_3$-stress.  This supplies a bounded, position-confound-controlled translation-proximal contact for $f_3$-stress, but only within the tested window and occupancy construction.

\paragraph{Strength driver.}
A separate powered row explains why the cross-organism signal is not merely an arbitrary residual coordinate.  The strength-driver run computes $15$ organisms out of $16$ requested, again with at least $500$ proteins per organism and $5$ folds.  The Spearman association between total synonymous-selection strength and $f_3$-stress strength is $0.7071428571428572$.  Direction agreement is $15/15$, hence the direction-agreement rate is $1.0$, with the Sulfolobus negative alignment recorded explicitly.  The finite interpretation is that $f_3$-stress strength is captured by total synonymous-selection strength multiplied by a fixed-direction alignment in this panel.  This is stronger than a one-row contact and weaker than a universal mechanism claim.

\paragraph{Local boundary.}
The chapter therefore records a positive finite result: in the supplied powered rows, $f_3$-stress is a cross-layer codon-topology-to-translation distinction under wobble-breaking and rank-sufficient readout conditions.  The claim is deliberately bounded.  It does not infer protein abundance, folded structure, physical admissibility, molecular function, phenotype, mechanism, or a global biological law.  It also does not turn modeled tAI or stAI weights into observed ribosome occupancy.  Those stronger interpretations would require separate layer-matched contacts and their own controlled statistics.  If future rows make the $f_3$-paired wobble weights symmetric, collapse the residual rank, or reduce the design to rank-saturated all-$1.0$ fits, the correct status for this coordinate is null or needs more data, not survival.
