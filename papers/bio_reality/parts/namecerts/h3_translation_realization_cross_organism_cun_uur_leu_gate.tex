\subsection{Cross-Organism CUN/UUR Leucine Usage Gate}
\label{sec:h3-translation-realization-cross-organism-cun-uur-leu-gate}

\paragraph{Finite carrier.}
This record treats the leucine codon split between the CUN block and the UUR block as an organism-indexed finite statistic, not as an unbounded biological mechanism.  The carrier has one row coordinate for the signed and scaled codon-usage residual $\log(\#CUN/\#UUR)$, one row coordinate for the organism-level tRNA-Leu gene-copy ratio, one external organism label, and one control coordinate for amino-acid composition.  The finite code-side support set used by the accompanying check has cardinality $20$ and is exactly
$$
\begin{aligned}
M = \{&AAA,AAG,ACA,ACG,AGA,AGG,AUA,AUG,CUA,CUC,\\
     &CUG,CUU,UAA,UAG,UCA,UCG,UGA,UGG,UUA,UUG\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The CUN portion appearing in this support is $\{CUA,CUC,CUG,CUU\}$, while the UUR leucine portion is $\{UUA,UUG\}$.  The row therefore asks only whether an externally labelled organism changes the relative use of these two synonymous leucine blocks in a way that tracks an independently recorded tRNA-Leu copy-number spectrum after the amino-acid-composition coordinate has been held in scope.

\paragraph{Observed finite rows.}
The validation row loaded three organism labels:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{Org} = \{&\mathrm{homo\_sapiens},\ \mathrm{saccharomyces\_cerevisiae},\\
&\mathrm{escherichia\_coli\_k12}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
For these three rows the recorded values of $\log(\#CUN/\#UUR)$ were
$$
\begin{aligned}
(1.3514980212896148,\ -0.24869078488829677,\ 1.0086640520051702),
\end{aligned}
$$
with sign vector
$$
\begin{aligned}
(+1,\ -1,\ +1).
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the sign is not uniform across the measured organisms.  The finite-row flag for sign uniformity is $\mathrm{false}$, and the corresponding run status is $\mathrm{passed}$.  The paired statistic reports a Pearson value of $1.0$ for the available rows and a positive partial correlation with the tRNA-Leu ratio, with
$$
\begin{aligned}
n = 3, \qquad r_{\mathrm{partial}} = 1.0.
\end{aligned}
$$
These numbers are read as a bounded row-level contact: the organism labels, codon-usage summaries, and tRNA-Leu copy annotations jointly supply a finite comparison in which the CUN/UUR sign changes across organisms and the controlled statistic is positive on the three-row table.

\paragraph{Contact interpretation.}
The codon-usage contact is a per-organism sequence-summary contact: it reports codon-count or codon-frequency information for coding sequences.  The tRNA contact is a genome-annotation contact: it reports organism-level tRNA-Leu gene-copy counts by anticodon family.  Their joint use in this record is narrower than a direct observation of translation.  The admitted reading is that the CUN/UUR leucine usage residual can be paired with an external tRNA-Leu copy-number coordinate and tested for a controlled association across the listed organisms.  The finite result is therefore an organism-level usage-and-copy-number association, not a measurement of ribosome motion, peptide production, folding, or downstream biological activity.

\paragraph{Blocked readings.}
Several stronger interpretations are explicitly outside the row.  A CUN/UUR sign by itself is not a translation claim.  A tRNA-Leu gene-copy table by itself is not a ribosome-mediated mechanism.  A codon-usage table by itself is not evidence for folded structure, physical admissibility, or biological function.  The three-row sign reversal is also not a universal cross-organism law.  In particular, no translation, folding, physical admissibility, function, mechanism, or universality follows from this packet unless a separate contact supplies that stronger layer directly.  The mixed biological spelling of descriptive terms such as tRNA-Leu is also not itself evidence; identifier spelling and codon-label readback remain administrative or code-table matters unless they are joined to organism-matched usage and tRNA-copy measurements.

\paragraph{Local conclusion.}
Within its stated scope, the record is ready as a finite cross-organism leucine-gate statistic.  It uses $20$ listed code-side elements, $3$ organism rows, the measured log-ratio vector $(1.3514980212896148,-0.24869078488829677,1.0086640520051702)$, the nonuniform sign vector $(+1,-1,+1)$, and the reported controlled association $r_{\mathrm{partial}}=1.0$ at $n=3$.  The scientific content is the bounded association between organism-labelled CUN/UUR codon usage and organism-labelled tRNA-Leu copy-number spectra under the amino-acid-composition control coordinate.  The record should not be cited as a general theory of translation, a protein-level result, a structural claim, a physical-admissibility claim, a functional claim, or a universal rule.
