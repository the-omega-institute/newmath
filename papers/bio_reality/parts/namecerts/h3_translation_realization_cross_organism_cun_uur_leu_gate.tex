\subsection{Cross-organism CUN/UUR Leu copy gate}
\label{sec:bior-reality-cross-organism-cun-uur-leu-gate}

\paragraph{Finite row.}
This record isolates a Leu-module statistic rather than a protein-level assertion. The carrier is the organism-indexed residual for the synonymous Leu split between the CUN block and the UUR block, paired with an organism-level tRNA-Leu gene-copy ratio and an amino-acid-composition control. The finite codon set used by the companion code-table row is
$$
\begin{aligned}
M=\{&AAA,AAG,ACA,ACG,AGA,AGG,AUA,AUG,CUA,CUC,\\
& CUG,CUU,UAA,UAG,UCA,UCG,UGA,UGG,UUA,UUG\},
\end{aligned}
$$
with $|M|=20$. The Leu comparison in this row uses the two synonymous families $CUN=\{CUA,CUC,CUG,CUU\}$ and $UUR=\{UUA,UUG\}$ as an externally checked per-organism statistic, not as a direct observation of translation chemistry.

\paragraph{Measured organisms and readout.}
The finite experiment loaded three organism rows: Homo sapiens, Saccharomyces cerevisiae, and Escherichia coli K-12. For these rows the reported log-ratios for $\log(\#CUN/\#UUR)$ were
$$
\begin{aligned}
&1.3514980212896148,\quad -0.24869078488829677,\quad 1.0086640520051702,
\end{aligned}
$$
with signs
$$
\begin{aligned}
&+1,\quad -1,\quad +1.
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the sign is not uniform across the measured organisms. The recorded scalar correlation between the CUN/UUR log-ratio statistic and the tRNA-Leu copy-ratio statistic is $1.0$, and the amino-acid-composition-controlled partial Pearson value is also $1.0$ on $n=3$ organism rows. The finite pass condition is therefore narrow: the packet sees a non-uniform CUN/UUR sign together with a positive organism-level association to the tRNA-Leu copy spectrum after the stated composition control.

\subsection{Admissible interpretation}
\label{sec:bior-reality-cross-organism-cun-uur-leu-gate-interpretation}

\paragraph{What the row supports.}
The row supports an organism-level codon-usage contact. It says that, in the three loaded organisms, the CUN-versus-UUR Leu usage balance changes sign and that the recorded controlled statistic tracks the corresponding tRNA-Leu copy-ratio coordinate. The admissible biological reading is therefore a bounded translation-layer proxy: codon-usage summaries and tRNA-gene-copy annotations agree with a cross-organism Leu-family gate in this finite table. The result is not a universal law; it is a finite external check over exactly the loaded organism rows and exactly the recorded variables.

\paragraph{Control role.}
The amino-acid-composition control is essential to the scope of the claim. A raw CUN/UUR log-ratio by itself is only a sequence-composition statistic. It becomes usable in this row only because the packet records a controlled partial-correlation readout against the tRNA-Leu copy-ratio coordinate. The relevant quantity is not merely
$$
\begin{aligned}
\log(\#CUN/\#UUR),
\end{aligned}
$$
but the organism-indexed relation between that quantity and the tRNA-Leu copy spectrum after the synonymous-class composition control has been applied.

\paragraph{Boundary.}
No independent conclusion about translation in the physical ribosome, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, mechanism, or universality follows from this row. The codon-usage table contact records sequence counts or frequencies, and the tRNA contact records genome-annotation copy numbers. Those contacts do not observe ribosome traffic, stAI dynamics, protein output, folded structure, cellular function, or a general biological principle. Any stronger statement would require a separate contact with that stronger resolution. In particular, the negative yeast-side sign is treated as a context-dependent reversal inside the finite table, not as evidence for a global direction of effect.

\subsection{Failure modes excluded by the row}
\label{sec:bior-reality-cross-organism-cun-uur-leu-gate-exclusions}

\paragraph{Identifier and code-table limits.}
The normalized probe identifier is cross-organism.cun-uur-sign-correlates-with-trna-leu. Its spelling is a routing convention, not biological evidence. Mixed-case biological tokens such as tRNA-Leu may appear in prose, but identifier shape does not supply an organism-matched statistic. \paragraph{Null edges retained.}
The blocked-null records remain informative because they mark where the table would be overread. A CUN/UUR sign alone is not a translation-layer result. Separate codon-usage and tRNA-copy contacts are not enough unless they are paired by organism and evaluated through the stated control. A code-table lookup is not a substitute for cross-organism measurement. The present row is accepted only at the paired-statistic level because the finite experiment explicitly reports three loaded organisms, non-uniform signs, Pearson $=1.0$, partial Pearson $=1.0$, and $n=3$ for the controlled tRNA-Leu association.
