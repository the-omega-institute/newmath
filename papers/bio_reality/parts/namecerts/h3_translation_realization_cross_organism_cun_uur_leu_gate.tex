\subsection{Cross-organism CUN/UUR leucine gate}
\label{sec:bior-eality-cross-organism-cun-uur-leu-gate}

\paragraph{Packet scope.}
This record isolates an organism-level leucine codon-usage comparison.  The finite carrier is the residual contrast between the CUN and UUR leucine codon families, paired with an organism label and with an external tRNA-Leu gene-copy readout.  The biological object is therefore not a folded protein, a ribosome trajectory, or a cellular function.  It is a small cross-organism table asking whether the sign and magnitude of $\log(\#\mathrm{CUN}/\#\mathrm{UUR})$ vary with the organism-level tRNA-Leu copy-number ratio after controlling for amino-acid composition through synonymous-class comparison.

\paragraph{Codon window.}
The accompanying code-table contact fixes the internal codon window used by the packet.  The recorded set $M$ has cardinality $20$ and is exactly
$$
\begin{aligned}
M=\{&\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{ACA},\mathrm{ACG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\\
&\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
Inside this window, the leucine comparison uses the CUN family $\{\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU}\}$ against the UUR family $\{\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\}$.  The role of $M$ is coordinate selection: it supplies codon labels and a finite readback domain.  It does not itself report an organism-level translation process.

\paragraph{Measured rows.}
The finite run loaded three organisms: $\mathrm{homo\_sapiens}$, $\mathrm{saccharomyces\_cerevisiae}$, and $\mathrm{escherichia\_coli\_k12}$.  Their recorded CUN/UUR log-ratios are
$$
\begin{aligned}
(1.3514980212896148,\;-0.24869078488829677,\;1.0086640520051702),
\end{aligned}
$$
with corresponding signs
$$
\begin{aligned}
(+1,\;-1,\;+1).
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the sign is not uniform across the measured organisms.  The experiment identifier is $\mathrm{cross\_organism\_cun\_uur\_leu\_gate\_validation}$, and the run status is recorded as passed.  The loaded table is deliberately small, so the numerical result is best read as a finite cross-organism contact rather than as a population-level law.

\paragraph{Paired tRNA-Leu statistic.}
The paired readout compares the CUN/UUR usage contrast with the organism-level tRNA-Leu gene-copy spectrum.  On the three loaded organisms, the recorded Pearson value is $1.0$.  The amino-acid-composition-controlled statistic is reported as a positive partial correlation with
$$
\begin{aligned}
n=3,\qquad r_{\mathrm{partial}}=1.0.
\end{aligned}
$$
This is the entire translation-layer evidence admitted by this packet: a finite association between codon-usage residuals and tRNA-Leu copy-number ratios under the stated synonymous-class control.  The result does not assert that the sign direction is universal, because the observed signs already include a reversal.  The result also does not assert that the three-row value estimates a stable global parameter; no confidence interval or p-value is recorded in the finite row.

\paragraph{Contacts and noise.}
Two external contact types are used.  First, per-organism codon-usage resources report codon counts or frequencies for coding sequences.  These tables are affected by sequence sampling, annotation choices, and the fact that codon usage is a compositional summary rather than a direct observation of ribosome motion.  Second, genome annotation resources report tRNA-Leu gene-copy counts by anticodon family.  Those copy-number summaries are likewise annotation-level data: they are useful for this paired statistic, but they do not by themselves measure decoding kinetics, translation efficiency, or protein output.  The controlled statistic is therefore a bounded contact between sequence composition and tRNA-gene annotation, not a direct mechanistic assay.

\paragraph{Local boundary.}
The packet permits only the following statement: in the loaded three-organism table, $\log(\#\mathrm{CUN}/\#\mathrm{UUR})$ is non-uniform in sign and is recorded as positively associated with the tRNA-Leu copy-number ratio after the stated amino-acid-composition control.  No realized translation event, no ribosome-mediated mechanism, no folded structure, no physical admissibility claim, no biological function, and no universal cross-organism rule follows from this record unless an additional contact supplies that separate evidence.  In particular, raw CUN/UUR sign alone is not treated as a translation claim, and the code-table label readback cannot repair a missing organism-matched statistic.

\paragraph{Verdict.}
The finite evidence supports a narrow organism-level leucine codon-usage gate.  The support is strongest as a checked pairing between codon-usage residuals and tRNA-Leu copy-number annotation in the three loaded organisms, with $\mathrm{sign\_uniform}=\mathrm{false}$, Pearson $=1.0$, and partial Pearson $=1.0$ at $n=3$.  The correct scientific reading is therefore conditional and local: the packet records a real finite association under the stated contacts, while leaving mechanism, higher biological layers, and universality outside the claim.
