\paragraph{Local interpretation.}
The packet supports only an organism-level codon-usage and gene-copy co-readback.  The codon-usage contact reports sequence-composition summaries; the gene-copy contact reports genome-annotation summaries by anticodon family.  Together they can test the bounded relation between the $\mathrm{CUN}/\mathrm{UUR}$ leucine split and the $\mathrm{tRNA}$-Leu copy-number spectrum.  They do not observe ribosome motion, elongation kinetics, protein output, folding, biochemical admissibility, cellular function, or evolutionary necessity.  Consequently, the sign reversal is a context-dependent finite-row observation, not a rule that must hold for all organisms.

\paragraph{Boundary.}
The admissible conclusion is limited to the recorded three-row paired statistic.  No translation event, folding state, physical admissibility statement, biological function, ribosome-mediated mechanism, or universal law follows from the code-table row, the identifier shape, the raw $\mathrm{CUN}/\mathrm{UUR}$ sign, or the $\mathrm{tRNA}$-Leu wording unless another separately recorded contact supplies that layer. The present row is therefore ready only as a scoped translation-realization statistic: it records a controlled codon-usage/gene-copy association and explicitly stops before mechanism, structure, function, physical admissibility, and universality.
