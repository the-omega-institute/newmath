\subsection{Cross-Organism Leucine Codon-Usage Gate}
\label{sec:bioreality-cross-organism-cun-uur-leu-gate}

\paragraph{Claim frame.}
This BioReality record concerns the leucine codon pair formed by the $CUN$ and $UUR$ synonymous families. The packet does not treat the codons as a protein-level object. It treats them as a finite organism-indexed statistic: for each measured organism, compare the sign and magnitude of $\log(\#CUN/\#UUR)$ with an independently recorded tRNA-Leu gene-copy ratio, while keeping amino-acid composition from being mistaken for a translation-layer signal. The carrier is therefore a paired table whose rows are organism labels and whose columns record a codon-usage residual, a tRNA-Leu copy-number contrast, and the control coordinate for amino-acid composition.

\paragraph{Finite codon set.}
The code-table contact supplies the finite set used by the lower-level codon readback. The verified row gives
$$
\begin{aligned}
M=\{&AAA,AAG,ACA,ACG,AGA,AGG,AUA,AUG,CUA,CUC,\\
& CUG,CUU,UAA,UAG,UCA,UCG,UGA,UGG,UUA,UUG\},
\end{aligned}
$$
with $|M|=20$. Within this set, the leucine coordinate used here is the contrast between the four $CUN$ codons $CUA,CUC,CUG,CUU$ and the two $UUR$ codons $UUA,UUG$. This finite codon list is only a label-level and count-level input. It can verify that the symbols used by the organism rows are well formed, but it does not by itself create an organism-matched usage statistic or a biological mechanism.

\paragraph{Organism rows.}
The experiment identified by $675fdddb3187$ loaded three organism rows: $homo\_sapiens$, $saccharomyces\_cerevisiae$, and $escherichia\_coli\_k12$. The recorded log-ratio vector for $\log(\#CUN/\#UUR)$ was
$$
\begin{aligned}
(1.3514980212896148,\;-0.24869078488829677,\;1.0086640520051702).
\end{aligned}
$$
Its sign vector was
$$
\begin{aligned}
(+1,\;-1,\;+1),
\end{aligned}
$$
so the sign is not uniform across the three measured organisms. This is the first finite check: the CUN-over-UUR preference is not being reported as a single direction shared by every row in the table.

\paragraph{Paired statistic.}
The same run records a positive association with the tRNA-Leu copy-number contrast. The ordinary Pearson value is $1.0$. The amino-acid-composition-controlled statistic is reported as a partial Pearson value of $1.0$ on $n=3$ organism rows. In the notation of this chapter, the finite row therefore supports the bounded assertion
$$
\begin{aligned}
\operatorname{signUniform}(\log(\#CUN/\#UUR))&=\mathrm{false},\\
\operatorname{partialPearson}(\log(\#CUN/\#UUR),\operatorname{tRNA\mbox{-}LeuRatio}\mid AA)&=1.0,\\
n&=3.
\end{aligned}
$$
This is a small-row contact rather than a population law. The value $1.0$ is exact as recorded in the finite run, but with only three organism rows it should be read as a passed finite check of the packet, not as a stable estimate of a universal biological parameter.

\paragraph{Reality contacts.}
Two external data interfaces are used. The first is a per-organism codon-usage table interface, which reports codon-count or codon-frequency summaries over coding sequences. The second is an organism-level tRNA-Leu gene-copy interface, which reports genome-annotation summaries by anticodon family. Their shared resolution is organism-level rather than molecule-level. The contact is consequently about a paired usage-and-copy-number statistic. It is not a direct observation of ribosome motion, peptide elongation dynamics, protein folding, biochemical activity, or organismal phenotype.

\paragraph{Scope limitation.}
The passed row permits only the following local reading: in the three measured organisms, the CUN/UUR leucine usage contrast changes sign, and the recorded controlled association with tRNA-Leu copy-number ratio is positive. No broader biological conclusion follows from that row alone. In particular, no realized translation event, no ribosome-mediated mechanism, no folded structure, no physical admissibility claim, no biological function, and no universal cross-organism rule is established unless a separate contact directly supplies the missing measurement. A raw CUN/UUR log-ratio without the amino-acid composition control is also insufficient for the claim in this chapter.

\paragraph{Decision.}
The chapter is admitted as a bounded translation-realization record because the finite run contains the organism labels, the non-uniform sign vector, the positive controlled tRNA-Leu association, and an explicit statement that no promotion to folding, function, physical admissibility, mechanism, or global law is being made. The result is therefore a compact organism-level statistical contact: $CUN$ and $UUR$ define the leucine codon coordinate, the three organism rows show sign reversal, and the paired tRNA-Leu copy-number readback supplies the constrained positive correlation recorded by experiment $675fdddb3187$.
