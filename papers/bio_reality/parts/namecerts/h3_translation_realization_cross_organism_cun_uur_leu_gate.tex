\subsection{Cross-organism CUN--UUR leucine usage gate}
\label{sec:biorality-cross-organism-cun-uur-leu-gate}

\paragraph{Packet and measured question.}
This record isolates a small organism-level test around the leucine synonymous split between the CUN and UUR codon families.  The carrier is the residual coordinate obtained from a per-organism comparison of CUN usage against UUR usage, paired with organism labels and with a separate tRNA-Leu gene-copy spectrum.  The measured statistic is not a protein-output measurement and is not a ribosome-dynamic trace.  It is a finite coding-sequence and genome-annotation contact asking whether the sign and size of $\log(\#\mathrm{CUN}/\#\mathrm{UUR})$ vary with an organism-level tRNA-Leu copy-number ratio after amino-acid composition is controlled inside the synonymous-codon comparison.

\paragraph{Finite codon support.}
The auxiliary code-table check fixes the finite codon window used by the packet.  The verified set $M$ has cardinality $20$ and is exactly
$$
\begin{aligned}
M=\{&\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{ACA},\mathrm{ACG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\\
&\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
Within this support, the leucine comparison uses the CUN family $\{\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU}\}$ against the UUR family $\{\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\}$.  The role of the larger finite set is to keep the codon labels attached to a checked discrete window; the biological test remains the organism-matched CUN--UUR residual paired with tRNA-Leu copy-number data.

\paragraph{Observed finite rows.}
The run loaded three organism rows: $\mathrm{homo\_sapiens}$, $\mathrm{saccharomyces\_cerevisiae}$, and $\mathrm{escherichia\_coli\_k12}$.  The recorded CUN-over-UUR log-ratios were
$$
\begin{aligned}
(1.3514980212896148,\ -0.24869078488829677,\ 1.0086640520051702),
\end{aligned}
$$
with signs
$$
\begin{aligned}
(+1,\ -1,\ +1).
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the sign is not uniform across the three organism rows.  The recorded value $\mathrm{sign\_uniform}=\mathrm{false}$ is the first finite obstruction to treating the CUN--UUR direction as a universal cross-organism direction.  The same run records a Pearson value of $1.0$ for the paired statistic and a positive partial correlation with the tRNA-Leu ratio of $1.0$ at $n=3$ after the stated amino-acid-composition control.

\paragraph{Interpretation of the contact.}
The chapter-specific readout is therefore narrow.  In this finite table, the CUN--UUR leucine statistic changes sign across organisms, and the paired statistic is recorded as positively aligned with the organism-level tRNA-Leu copy-number ratio under the available control.  The admissible conclusion is a bounded translation-layer contact in the sense of organism-level codon-usage and tRNA-copy annotation: the coding-sequence residual has a non-uniform sign, and the finite paired statistic is reported as positive for the three loaded organisms.  The observed values do not make the CUN--UUR sign into a global directionality rule.

\paragraph{Local exclusion boundary.}
No broader biological conclusion is licensed by these rows alone.  The table does not establish ribosome-mediated translation, protein folding, physical admissibility, biological function, a mechanism, or a universal law.  A separate ribosome-profiling, protein-output, structural, functional, or physical contact would be needed before any such claim could be stated.  Raw CUN--UUR sign without the amino-acid-composition control is also outside the admissible claim.  The record is therefore retained as an organism-level codon-usage and tRNA-copy-number contact only: it reports the finite sign reversal and the positive controlled association in the available three-row dataset, while refusing promotion to structure, function, mechanism, physical admissibility, or universality.
