\subsection{Cross-organism CUN/UUR leucine usage as a bounded organism-level contact}
\label{sec:h3-translation-realization-cross-organism-cun-uur-leu-gate}

\paragraph{Packet and finite carrier.}
This chapter records a finite organism-level test for the leucine codon pair $CUN/UUR$.  The carrier is the residual coordinate consisting of $\log(CUN/UUR)$, an organism label, an organism-level $tRNA$-Leu gene-copy ratio, and an amino-acid-composition control.  The intended readout is not a direct observation of ribosomal motion.  It is a checked comparison between codon-usage summaries and genome-annotation summaries, both indexed by organism.  The finite code-side anchor records the set
$$\begin{aligned}
M={}&\{AAA,AAG,ACA,ACG,AGA,AGG,AUA,AUG,CUA,CUC,CUG,CUU,\\
&UAA,UAG,UCA,UCG,UGA,UGG,UUA,UUG\},
\end{aligned}$$
with $|M|=20$.  Within this packet the relevant leucine contrast is the split between the four $CUN$ codons $CUA,CUC,CUG,CUU$ and the two $UUR$ codons $UUA,UUG$.  The biological question is therefore deliberately narrow: after controlling for amino-acid composition through synonymous-class accounting, does the signed $CUN/UUR$ residual vary with the corresponding $tRNA$-Leu copy-number spectrum?

\paragraph{Reality contacts.}
Two finite contacts are used.  The first contact is per-organism codon usage: curated organism tables give codon-count or codon-frequency summaries for coding sequences.  The second contact is organism-level $tRNA$-Leu gene-copy annotation: genome resources give copy-number summaries by anticodon family.  These contacts have different noise profiles.  Codon-usage tables aggregate sequence composition and database sampling choices; copy-number tables aggregate genome annotation calls.  Their pairing is therefore treated as a statistical contact between two organism-level summaries, not as a direct experimental trace of translation dynamics.

\paragraph{Finite rows.}
The checked row loaded three organisms: $homo\_sapiens$, $saccharomyces\_cerevisiae$, and $escherichia\_coli\_k12$.  The recorded $\log(CUN/UUR)$ values were
$$\begin{aligned}
(1.3514980212896148,\;-0.24869078488829677,\;1.0086640520051702),
\end{aligned}$$
with signs
$$\begin{aligned}
(1,\;-1,\;1).
\end{aligned}$$
Thus the sign is not uniform across the three organisms.  The experiment row reports $sign\_uniform=false$ and $status=passed$.  For the organism-matched comparison against the $tRNA$-Leu copy-number ratio, the recorded Pearson value is $1.0$.  The amino-acid-composition-controlled statistic is reported as a positive partial correlation with $n=3$ and partial Pearson coefficient $1.0$.  The experiment identifier is $cross\_organism\_cun\_uur\_leu\_gate\_validation$, with run id $675fdddb3187$.

\paragraph{Interpretation.}
The finite result supports only the following bounded statement.  In the three-organism row, the $CUN/UUR$ leucine residual is context-dependent rather than sign-uniform, and the recorded residual is positively aligned with the organism-level $tRNA$-Leu copy-number ratio after the stated amino-acid-composition control.  The sign reversal matters because it prevents the packet from being read as a one-direction rule about $CUN$ or $UUR$ preference.  Human and $E.\ coli$ carry positive logged contrasts in this row, whereas yeast carries a negative logged contrast.  The statistic is therefore an organism-indexed relation among codon usage, synonymous leucine splitting, and copy-number annotation.

\paragraph{Local boundary.}
No broader biological realization is licensed by this row alone.  The row does not establish translation as a molecular process, ribosome-mediated mechanism, folded structure, physical admissibility, protein function, or a universal cross-organism law.  It also does not turn a raw $\log(CUN/UUR)$ value into a translation-layer claim unless the organism-matched $tRNA$-Leu contact and amino-acid-composition control are present.  A future ribosome-profiling contact, protein-output contact, folding contact, or functional assay would be a different piece of evidence and would have to be recorded separately.  The present packet remains a finite, organism-level statistical contact: codon usage and $tRNA$-Leu copy-number summaries are paired, the non-uniform signs are recorded, and the controlled correlation is kept inside that limited scope.
