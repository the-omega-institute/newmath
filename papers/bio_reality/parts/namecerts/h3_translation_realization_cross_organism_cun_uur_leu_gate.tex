\subsection{Cross-organism CUN/UUR Leu gate}
\label{subsec:h3-translation-realization-cross-organism-cun-uur-leu-gate}

\paragraph{Finite object under test.}
This record isolates a Leu-module statistic at the organism level.  The carrier is not a protein, a ribosome trace, or a dynamical model of translation.  It is the finite paired datum consisting of a CUN-versus-UUR codon-usage residual, an organism label, an organism-level tRNA-Leu gene-copy ratio, and an amino-acid-composition control.  The intended readout is therefore restricted to a signed and scaled comparison of Leu synonymous usage after the Leu composition background has been held fixed.

The accompanying code-table contact fixes the relevant module name.  In the verified row $h0.M.equals.WNR.CUN$, the set $M$ has cardinality $20$ and is recorded as
$$
\begin{aligned}
M = \{&AAA,AAG,ACA,ACG,AGA,AGG,AUA,AUG,CUA,CUC,\\
     &CUG,CUU,UAA,UAG,UCA,UCG,UGA,UGG,UUA,UUG\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
Within this packet the Leu subcomparison is the contrast between the CUN family and the UUR family, expressed as $\log(\#CUN/\#UUR)$ for each organism after the stated control.  The code-table row supplies codon labels and module membership only; it does not itself establish an organism-level translation claim.

\paragraph{Organism rows and numerical readback.}
The validation row $h3.translation_realization.cross_organism.cun_uur_leu_gate$ loaded three organism labels: $homo\_sapiens$, $saccharomyces\_cerevisiae$, and $escherichia\_coli\_k12$.  The recorded log-ratio values were
$$
\begin{aligned}
(1.3514980212896148,\;-0.24869078488829677,\;1.0086640520051702),
\end{aligned}
$$
with sign vector
$$
\begin{aligned}
(1,\;-1,\;1).
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the sign is not uniform across the three measured organisms.  The finite row explicitly reports $sign\_uniform=false$, so the record does not collapse to a single direction of Leu codon preference across the sampled organisms.

The same row records a Pearson value of $1.0$ for the paired statistic available in the validation table.  After the amino-acid-composition control, the recorded partial-correlation object has sample count $n=3$ and partial Pearson value $1.0$:
$$
\begin{aligned}
n &= 3,\\
\rho_{partial} &= 1.0.
\end{aligned}
$$
The experiment identifier is $cross\_organism\_cun\_uur\_leu\_gate\_validation$, the run identifier is $675fdddb3187$, and the row status is $passed$.  These scalars are interpreted only as a finite-row contact between the observed CUN/UUR sign-and-magnitude statistic and the recorded tRNA-Leu copy-ratio statistic in the three-organism table.

\paragraph{Admissible conclusion.}
The supported conclusion is narrow.  In this finite table, the CUN/UUR Leu log-ratio changes sign across organisms, and the available controlled paired statistic reports a positive partial correlation with the tRNA-Leu copy-ratio coordinate.  This is enough to keep the packet as a bounded translation-realization measurement at the codon-usage and tRNA-copy summary level: the statistic has an organism-indexed readout, the sign reversal is explicitly present, and the controlled scalar is recorded as positive in the finite validation row.

This conclusion is not a claim that the raw CUN/UUR sign alone realizes translation.  The raw ratio becomes relevant here only because it is paired with organism labels, the tRNA-Leu copy-ratio coordinate, and the amino-acid-composition control.  Without those companion data, the same codon-window contrast would remain an internal indexing cue rather than a biological measurement.

\paragraph{Local exclusion boundary.}
No stronger biological conclusion is licensed by this packet.  The codon-usage table and the tRNA gene-copy table are sequence and genome-annotation summaries; they do not observe ribosome dynamics.  The passed row therefore does not establish a ribosome-mediated mechanism, a folded structure, a physical-admissibility fact, or a biological function.  It also does not give a universal law about Leu codon usage.  The recorded sign vector $(1,-1,1)$ is treated as context-dependent behavior in the sampled organisms, not as directionality that must persist across organisms, tissues, environments, or future datasets.

The same restriction applies to the code-table component.  The finite equality $|M|=20$ and the displayed membership list certify the codon-label universe used by this packet, but they do not repair missing organism-level measurements and they do not promote a label readback into a translation, folding, structure, physical, functional, mechanistic, or universal claim.  Any such promotion would require a separate contact of the appropriate kind, recorded with its own measured rows.

\paragraph{Failure modes retained by the record.}
The packet would fail if a broader organism panel showed uniform CUN/UUR sign independent of the tRNA-Leu copy spectrum, or if the sign varied while the controlled paired statistic lost its positive association with the tRNA-Leu copy-ratio coordinate.  It would also fail as a translation-level record if the amino-acid-composition control were removed and only the raw log-ratio were retained.  The present row avoids those failures only for the finite three-organism table summarized above; it does not claim robustness beyond that table.
