\subsection{Cross-Organism Leucine CUN/UUR Usage Gate}
\label{sec:cross-organism-cun-uur-leu-gate}

\paragraph{Finite packet.}
This chapter records a bounded organism-level contact for the leucine codon families $\mathrm{CUN}$ and $\mathrm{UUR}$.  The finite carrier is the residual comparison of $\log(\#\mathrm{CUN}/\#\mathrm{UUR})$ against an organism label and a matched $\mathrm{tRNA}$-Leu gene-copy ratio, with amino-acid composition treated as a control rather than as a biological conclusion.  The rows loaded for the checked packet are the three organism labels
$$
\begin{aligned}
&\mathrm{homo\_sapiens},\quad \mathrm{saccharomyces\_cerevisiae},\quad \mathrm{escherichia\_coli\_k12}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The code-window support used by the packet also carries the finite codon set
$$
\begin{aligned}
M = \{&\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{ACA},\mathrm{ACG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\\
&\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\},
\end{aligned}
$$
with $|M|=20$.  This list is not itself a biological mechanism; it only fixes the codon-side coordinate through which the organism-level leucine comparison is expressed.

\paragraph{Measured statistic.}
For the three loaded organisms the recorded leucine-family log ratios are
$$
\begin{aligned}
(1.3514980212896148,\;-0.24869078488829677,\;1.0086640520051702),
\end{aligned}
$$
with sign vector
$$
\begin{aligned}
(+1,\;-1,\;+1).
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the sign is not constant across the finite row set: the packet records $\mathrm{sign\_uniform}=\mathrm{false}$.  The controlled comparison with the organism-level $\mathrm{tRNA}$-Leu copy-number ratio is recorded on $n=3$ rows, with ordinary Pearson value $1.0$ and partial Pearson value $1.0$ after the amino-acid-composition control encoded for the packet.  In this finite table, the claim is therefore not that $\mathrm{CUN}$ always dominates $\mathrm{UUR}$, nor that $\mathrm{UUR}$ always dominates $\mathrm{CUN}$; the reported fact is the context-sensitive sign reversal together with a positive controlled association to the matched $\mathrm{tRNA}$-Leu copy-number ratio.

\paragraph{Admissible reading.}
The scientific reading is deliberately narrow.  A per-organism codon-usage table supplies codon-count or codon-frequency summaries for coding sequences, and an organism-level genome annotation supplies $\mathrm{tRNA}$-Leu gene-copy counts by anticodon family.  When these two contacts are paired, the packet tests whether the sign and magnitude of $\log(\#\mathrm{CUN}/\#\mathrm{UUR})$ track the corresponding $\mathrm{tRNA}$-Leu copy-number ratio once amino-acid composition has been controlled within synonymous codon classes.  On the recorded rows the test status is $\mathrm{passed}$ for the finite statistic, with experiment identifier $\mathrm{cross\_organism\_cun\_uur\_leu\_gate\_validation}$ and run identifier $675\mathrm{fdddb}3187$.

\paragraph{Local null alternatives.}
The packet would fail in either of two finite ways.  First, the signs of $\log(\#\mathrm{CUN}/\#\mathrm{UUR})$ could be uniform across all loaded organisms, which would remove the intended cross-organism reversal.  Second, a sign reversal could be present while the controlled relation to the matched $\mathrm{tRNA}$-Leu copy-number ratio vanished after amino-acid-composition control.  The recorded rows avoid both nulls: the signs are $+,-,+$, and the controlled partial Pearson statistic is $1.0$ on $n=3$ rows.  The small row count makes this a finite contact, not a population law.

\paragraph{Boundary of the claim.}
Nothing in this chapter asserts that a ribosome-level translation process has been observed.  The finite statistic also does not establish protein folding, physical admissibility, biological function, a mechanistic law, or a universal cross-organism rule.  The row set is a codon-usage and $\mathrm{tRNA}$-copy-number contact only.  Any later statement about translation dynamics, folded structure, molecular function, physical realization, mechanism, or universality would require a separate contact of the appropriate kind rather than reuse of the codon-usage statistic.
