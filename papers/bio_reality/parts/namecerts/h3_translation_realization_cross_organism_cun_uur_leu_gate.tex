\subsection{Cross-organism CUN/UUR Leu gate}
\label{sec:h3-translation-realization-cross-organism-cun-uur-leu-gate}

\paragraph{Finite packet.}
This packet treats the leucine codon split between the $\mathrm{CUN}$ family and the $\mathrm{UUR}$ family as an organism-level usage statistic paired with an organism-level tRNA-Leu copy-number statistic.  The admissible carrier is not a ribosome trace and not a protein-output measurement.  It is the finite row consisting of the coordinate $\log(\#\mathrm{CUN}/\#\mathrm{UUR})$, a tRNA-Leu gene-copy ratio, an organism label, and an amino-acid-composition control.  The biological readout is therefore deliberately narrow: per organism, compare the sign and magnitude of the leucine codon-family residual with the corresponding tRNA-Leu copy-number spectrum after controlling for amino-acid composition.

\paragraph{Code-window coordinate.}
The auxiliary code-window fact attached to the packet records a finite set $M$ of size $20$.  In the row supplied here, $M$ is the union of the $\mathrm{WNR}$ window and the $\mathrm{CUN}$ window, with the following codons:
$$
\begin{aligned}
M=\{&\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{ACA},\mathrm{ACG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\\
&\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\},\\
|M|&=20.
\end{aligned}
$$
This list is used only as an internal coordinate for the codon-family comparison.  It does not by itself observe translation, enzyme action, folding, organism fitness, or any universal biological law.

\paragraph{Observed finite rows.}
The realized cross-organism row loads three organism labels: $\mathrm{homo\_sapiens}$, $\mathrm{saccharomyces\_cerevisiae}$, and $\mathrm{escherichia\_coli\_k12}$.  The recorded leucine-family log ratios, in that order, are
$$
\begin{aligned}
\ell_{\mathrm{human}}&=1.3514980212896148,\\
\ell_{\mathrm{yeast}}&=-0.24869078488829677,\\
\ell_{\mathrm{ecoli}}&=1.0086640520051702.
\end{aligned}
$$
The corresponding signs are $+1,-1,+1$, so the finite packet explicitly records $\mathrm{sign\_uniform}=\mathrm{false}$.  The row therefore rejects the overly strong reading that the $\mathrm{CUN}/\mathrm{UUR}$ sign is fixed across the measured organisms.

\paragraph{Paired statistic.}
For the paired codon-usage and tRNA-Leu copy-number comparison, the row reports a Pearson value of $1.0$.  It also reports a positive partial correlation after amino-acid-composition control:
$$
\begin{aligned}
n&=3,\\
r_{\partial}&=1.0.
\end{aligned}
$$
Within this finite table, the admissible claim is therefore that the non-uniform $\log(\#\mathrm{CUN}/\#\mathrm{UUR})$ sign and magnitude are aligned with the organism-level tRNA-Leu copy-ratio statistic under the stated control.  The result is a bounded contact between codon usage and tRNA gene-copy annotation, not a mechanistic explanation of how ribosomes choose codons and not a statement about protein products.

\paragraph{Null boundaries.}
The finite success status is $\mathrm{passed}$ for the statistic just described, but the scope remains local to the supplied contacts.  No process-level translation realization follows beyond the attached codon-usage and tRNA-copy summaries.  No folded structure, physical admissibility, biochemical function, ribosome-mediated mechanism, or universal cross-organism law follows from the sign pattern or from the perfect finite-row correlations.  In particular, the sign reversal is recorded as context-dependent evidence against uniform directionality, not as a new global rule.  A stronger biological assertion would require a separate organism-matched contact, such as ribosome profiling, direct translation-efficiency measurements, protein abundance, folding assays, or functional readouts.

\paragraph{Status of the packet.}
The chapter-level record is ready as a finite, externally checked organism-level statistic.  Its scientific content is the controlled three-row comparison: $n=3$, signs $+,-,+$, $\mathrm{sign\_uniform}=\mathrm{false}$, Pearson $1.0$, and partial Pearson $1.0$ after amino-acid-composition control.  Its non-content is equally important: the row is not licensed to climb from codon-family usage and tRNA-Leu copy number to translation dynamics, structure, physical possibility, function, mechanism, or universality without additional contact data.
