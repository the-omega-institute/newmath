\subsection{Cross-organism CUN/UUR Leu gate}

\paragraph{Finite carrier.}
This packet records a bounded organism-level test for the leucine codon split between the $CUN$ family and the $UUR$ family.  The carrier is not a molecular mechanism and not a protein-output observation.  It is the finite row consisting of an organism label, a codon-usage residual for the leucine module, a matched $tRNA$-Leu gene-copy ratio, and an amino-acid-composition control.  The coordinate readout is the signed quantity $\log(\#CUN/\#UUR)$, interpreted only after the synonymous-class control has been applied.  The recorded organisms are $homo\_sapiens$, $saccharomyces\_cerevisiae$, and $escherichia\_coli\_k12$, so the row count for the cross-organism statistic is $n=3$.

The finite codon window used by the companion code-table readback has cardinality $20$.  The recorded set is

Within that window, the leucine comparison used here is the internal split between $CUN=\{CUA,CUC,CUG,CUU\}$ and $UUR=\{UUA,UUG\}$.  The larger $M$ row is cited only to identify the finite codon context in which the $CUN$ entries and $UUR$ entries are present; it does not by itself supply organism-matched expression, ribosome, folding, function, or fitness information.

\paragraph{Bounded interpretation.}
The packet therefore admits only the following narrow statement.  In the checked three-organism table, the $CUN/UUR$ leucine log-ratio is not sign-uniform, and the supplied controlled statistic is positively aligned with the organism-level $tRNA$-Leu copy-ratio coordinate.  This supports a translation-layer contact only in the weak statistical sense that codon usage and a matched $tRNA$-Leu copy-number coordinate have been compared across organisms after the amino-acid-composition control.  It does not assert that the ribosome realizes a particular causal mechanism, that any protein folds, that any protein function is produced, that a physical admissibility condition has been met, or that the sign direction is a universal biological law.

\paragraph{Local failure modes.}
The claim would fail in either of two finite ways.  First, if the measured organisms all had the same sign for $\log(\#CUN/\#UUR)$ independently of the $tRNA$-Leu copy spectrum, the non-uniformity branch would be absent.  Second, if a sign reversal were present but the controlled paired statistic lost its positive association with the $tRNA$-Leu coordinate, the row would remain a codon-usage observation rather than a checked cross-organism Leu gate.  A raw ratio without amino-acid-composition control is also insufficient for this packet.  The identifier spelling and the standard code-table contact are likewise insufficient: they can delimit codon labels, but they cannot replace the organism-matched codon-usage and $tRNA$ copy-number table.

\paragraph{Conclusion.}
The finite evidence is ready for inclusion at bounded scope.  The checked content is the three-row organism comparison with log-ratio signs $(1,-1,1)$, non-uniform sign status, Pearson value $1.0$, and controlled partial Pearson value $1.0$ against the $tRNA$-Leu copy-ratio coordinate.  The conclusion remains deliberately local: it records a small, externally paired codon-usage/$tRNA$-copy statistic for the leucine module and refuses any promotion to mechanism, folding, physical admissibility, function, or universality without another contact designed for those layers.
