\subsection{Cross-organism CUN/UUR leucine gate}
\label{sec:h3-translation-realization-cross-organism-cun-uur-leu-gate}

\paragraph{Packet and observed coordinates.}
This record concerns the leucine synonymous split between the $\mathrm{CUN}$ family and the $\mathrm{UUR}$ family. The finite carrier is an organism-indexed residual for leucine codon usage, paired with an organism-indexed tRNA-Leu gene-copy comparison. The measured readout is not a protein-level object. It is the sign and magnitude of $\log(\#\mathrm{CUN}/\#\mathrm{UUR})$ after the packet has retained the organism label, the tRNA-Leu copy-ratio coordinate, and an amino-acid-composition control. Thus the object being tested is a bounded translation-layer statistic: codon usage and tRNA gene-copy spectra may be compared as external organism summaries, while no structural, functional, or mechanistic conclusion is licensed by the statistic alone.

The associated code-window datum records the finite set
$$
\begin{aligned}
M=\{&\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{ACA},\mathrm{ACG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\\
&\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The verified cardinality is $|M|=20$. In this chapter the role of $M$ is only to record the finite codon-label support that includes the $\mathrm{CUN}$ and $\mathrm{UUR}$ leucine labels. It does not itself supply an organism-matched usage statistic, a tRNA copy-number statistic, or any ribosome-level observation.

\paragraph{Finite organism rows.}
The checked cross-organism run loaded three organism rows: $\mathrm{homo\_sapiens}$, $\mathrm{saccharomyces\_cerevisiae}$, and $\mathrm{escherichia\_coli\_k12}$. For these rows the recorded leucine log-ratios are
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{homo\_sapiens}:&\quad 1.3514980212896148,\\
\mathrm{saccharomyces\_cerevisiae}:&\quad -0.24869078488829677,\\
\mathrm{escherichia\_coli\_k12}:&\quad 1.0086640520051702.
\end{aligned}
$$
The corresponding signs are
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{signs}=(1,-1,1),
\end{aligned}
$$
so the sign is not uniform across the three measured organisms. The run explicitly reports $\mathrm{sign\_uniform}=\mathrm{false}$.

\paragraph{Controlled tRNA-Leu relation.}
The paired statistic compares the codon-usage residual against the organism-level tRNA-Leu copy-ratio coordinate after the amino-acid-composition control. On the three loaded rows the reported Pearson value is $1.0$. The partial correlation statement is also positive, with
$$
\begin{aligned}
n&=3,\\
\rho_{\mathrm{partial}}&=1.0.
\end{aligned}
$$
Within this finite table, the packet therefore passes the stated counterexample search: the $\mathrm{CUN}/\mathrm{UUR}$ sign reverses across organisms, and the measured magnitude is positively aligned with the tRNA-Leu copy-ratio coordinate under the recorded control. This is a finite-row contact, not a universal biological law. The result is best read as a constrained organism-level association between synonymous leucine usage and genome-annotation summaries for tRNA-Leu families.

\paragraph{Negative scope.}
The experiment status is recorded as $\mathrm{passed}$, but the pass condition is narrow. The contact does not show ribosome-mediated dynamics. It does not show protein folding, physical admissibility, protein function, or any global rule about leucine codon choice. The nonuniform signs $(1,-1,1)$ are evidence against a single context-free direction in this small organism panel, not evidence for a universal reversal principle. Likewise, the value $\rho_{\mathrm{partial}}=1.0$ at $n=3$ should be treated as an exact finite-table statement for the rows supplied here, not as a population-level inference with an attached significance calculation.

\paragraph{Local admissibility verdict.}
The admissible claim is therefore the following: for the three recorded organisms, the leucine $\mathrm{CUN}/\mathrm{UUR}$ codon-usage residual has nonuniform sign and is positively paired with the recorded tRNA-Leu copy-ratio coordinate after the stated amino-acid-composition control. The inadmissible claims are equally local: no translation mechanism, no folded structure, no physical admissibility, no biological function, and no cross-organism universality follows from these rows unless another contact supplies that additional measurement.
