\subsection{Cross-Organism CUN/UUR Leucine Usage Gate}\label{sec:bioreality-cross-organism-cun-uur-leu-gate}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Carrier and finite coordinate.}
This record isolates one organism-indexed leucine module: the contrast between the four CUN leucine codons and the two UUR leucine codons, paired with organism labels and a tRNA-Leu gene-copy readout.  The finite codon-side carrier used by the packet includes the twenty-element set
$$
\begin{aligned}
M = \{&\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{ACA},\mathrm{ACG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\\
&\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\},
\end{aligned}
$$
with $|M|=20$.  Within this carrier the leucine comparison is not treated as a protein-structure assertion.  It is a finite row statistic whose measured coordinate is the organism-level residual sign and magnitude of $\log(\#\mathrm{CUN}/\#\mathrm{UUR})$, read together with the corresponding organism-level tRNA-Leu copy-number ratio and an amino-acid-composition control on synonymous codon classes.

\paragraph{Observed rows.}
The validation run for claim $\mathrm{h3.translation\_realization.cross\_organism.cun\_uur\_leu\_gate}$ loaded three organism rows: $\mathrm{Homo\ sapiens}$, $\mathrm{Saccharomyces\ cerevisiae}$, and $\mathrm{Escherichia\ coli\ K12}$.  The measured CUN/UUR log-ratios were
$$
\begin{aligned}
(1.3514980212896148,\;-0.24869078488829677,\;1.0086640520051702),
\end{aligned}
$$
with signs
$$
\begin{aligned}
(+1,\;-1,\;+1).
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the sign is not uniform across the three-row organism table.  The recorded field $\mathrm{sign\_uniform}$ is false.  This matters because a uniform sign would have collapsed the packet to a one-direction descriptive bias rather than a context-dependent organism relation.

\paragraph{Paired tRNA-Leu statistic.}
The same run reports a positive organism-matched association between the CUN/UUR log-ratio and the tRNA-Leu copy-number ratio after the stated composition control.  The ordinary Pearson value recorded for the paired rows is $1.0$.  The controlled statistic is also reported as a positive partial correlation with $n=3$ and
$$
\begin{aligned}
\rho_{\mathrm{partial}} = 1.0.
\end{aligned}
$$
The finite result therefore supports only the following bounded reading: in this three-organism table, the CUN-versus-UUR leucine usage coordinate reverses sign across organisms and is aligned with the available tRNA-Leu copy-number ratio under the packet's composition-controlled comparison.  No p-value, lambda parameter, module-classification scalar, or hit-rate field is present in the supplied row, so none is inferred here.

\paragraph{Reality contact.}
Two empirical contacts are used.  The first is a per-organism codon-usage table contact: it supplies codon-count or codon-frequency summaries for coding sequences.  The second is an organism-level tRNA-Leu gene-copy contact: it supplies genome-annotation counts by anticodon family.  Both contacts are sequence-annotation summaries.  They are adequate for testing the paired codon-usage and tRNA-copy statistic described above, but they are not measurements of ribosome motion, nascent-chain behavior, protein folding, biological task performance, or thermodynamic admissibility.

\paragraph{Local boundary.}
The verified row is a narrow translation-layer contact in the sense of organism-level codon usage paired with tRNA-Leu copy spectra.  It does not establish a complete translation event, a ribosome-mediated mechanism, a protein structure, a folding pathway, physical admissibility, biological function, or a universal law of codon usage.  The sign reversal is reported as context-dependent: $\mathrm{Homo\ sapiens}$ and $\mathrm{Escherichia\ coli\ K12}$ are positive in the supplied ordering, while $\mathrm{Saccharomyces\ cerevisiae}$ is negative.  A stronger biological conclusion would require an additional contact, such as organism-matched ribosome profiling, direct tRNA abundance or charging measurements, protein-output assays, or structure/function readouts tied to the same coding sequences.

\paragraph{Conclusion.}
The chapter claim is ready at the finite statistic level.  The packet has three organism rows, a non-uniform CUN/UUR sign vector $(+1,-1,+1)$, concrete log-ratio values $(1.3514980212896148,-0.24869078488829677,1.0086640520051702)$, and a recorded positive controlled association with the tRNA-Leu copy-number ratio, $\rho_{\mathrm{partial}}=1.0$ at $n=3$.  The result is therefore a bounded organism-level codon-usage/tRNA-copy finding, not a license to promote the observation into mechanism, structure, function, physical realization, or universality.
