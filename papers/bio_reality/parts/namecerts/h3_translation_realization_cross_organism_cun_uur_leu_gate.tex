\subsection{Cross-organism CUN/UUR Leu usage gate}\label{subsec:h3-translation-realization-cross-organism-cun-uur-leu-gate}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite carrier.}
This record isolates one Leu-module statistic at organism resolution.  The carrier consists of an organism label, a controlled codon-usage residual for the synonymous Leu split $\mathrm{CUN}$ versus $\mathrm{UUR}$, and an organism-level tRNA-Leu gene-copy ratio.  The finite contact is not a statement about all translation.  It is a bounded comparison between codon-usage tables and tRNA-Leu gene-copy summaries for the three loaded organisms
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathcal{O} = \{&\mathrm{homo\_sapiens},\ \mathrm{saccharomyces\_cerevisiae},\ \mathrm{escherichia\_coli\_k12}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The measured readback asks whether the sign and magnitude of $\log(\#\mathrm{CUN}/\#\mathrm{UUR})$ change across these organisms and whether that change is positively associated with the corresponding tRNA-Leu copy-number ratio after an amino-acid-composition control.  The intended coordinate is therefore organism-indexed and relational: a codon-usage residual is paired with an external genome-annotation summary rather than interpreted from codon labels alone.

\paragraph{Codon set anchoring.}
The accompanying code-level anchor records that the internal set $M$ has cardinality $20$ and is exactly the union of the $\mathrm{WNR}$ block with the $\mathrm{CUN}$ Leu block.  The finite list is
$$
\begin{aligned}
M=\{&\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{ACA},\mathrm{ACG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\\
&\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the finite codon bookkeeping is fixed before the organism-level statistic is read.  This matters because the biological question is not whether the string $\mathrm{CUN}$ can be named, but whether the Leu split between $\mathrm{CUN}$ and $\mathrm{UUR}$ has a measured organism-indexed pattern when paired with tRNA-Leu copy spectra.

\paragraph{Observed statistic.}
The validation run loaded three organism rows and returned the log-ratio vector
$$
\begin{aligned}
(1.3514980212896148,\ -0.24869078488829677,\ 1.0086640520051702),
\end{aligned}
$$
with corresponding signs
$$
\begin{aligned}
(+1,\ -1,\ +1).
\end{aligned}
$$
The sign is therefore not uniform across the measured organisms.  The same run reports $\mathrm{sign\_uniform}=\mathrm{false}$, Pearson correlation $1.0$, and a positive partial correlation with the tRNA-Leu ratio after the amino-acid-composition control, with
$$
\begin{aligned}
n=3,\qquad r_{\mathrm{partial}}=1.0.
\end{aligned}
$$
Within this finite table, the packet passes the stated test: non-uniform $\log(\#\mathrm{CUN}/\#\mathrm{UUR})$ signs are observed, and the controlled statistic is positively aligned with the tRNA-Leu copy-number ratio.  The result is deliberately small-row: it is a three-organism contact, not an asymptotic law and not a survey of every lineage.

\paragraph{Reality contacts used.}
Two kinds of external contact are sufficient for this bounded readback.  First, per-organism codon-usage resources provide codon-count or frequency summaries over coding sequences.  Such tables can support an organism-level codon-usage distribution and the associated $\log(\#\mathrm{CUN}/\#\mathrm{UUR})$ residual, but they do not observe ribosome dynamics or protein output.  Second, genome-annotation resources provide tRNA-Leu gene-copy counts by anticodon family.  Those counts can support an organism-level tRNA-Leu copy-spectrum comparison, but by themselves they do not establish a cellular mechanism.  The accepted contact is the paired statistic formed from these two resources under the amino-acid-composition control.

\paragraph{Local interpretation.}
The finite result supports only the following narrow claim.  In the loaded three-row organism table, the Leu-module codon-usage residual for $\mathrm{CUN}$ versus $\mathrm{UUR}$ is not sign-uniform, and its controlled association with the tRNA-Leu copy-number ratio is positive in the recorded run.  This is admissible as a translation-layer usage-and-copy-number contact only because both sides of the pair are externally read at organism level.  Raw codon-window geometry, codon spelling, or the sign of a single organism row would not suffice.

\paragraph{Boundaries.}
No broader biological conclusion is licensed by this row packet.  It does not establish realized translation as a cellular process beyond the attached codon-usage and tRNA-copy summaries.  It does not establish ribosome-mediated mechanism, protein folding, physical admissibility, biological function, or universal directionality across organisms.  The observed sign reversal is context-dependent in the measured rows.  Any claim about translation dynamics, folded structure, function, physical viability, mechanism, or a global biological rule would require a separate contact with the relevant measurements, such as ribosome profiling, protein-output assays, structural evidence, or an independently curated broader organism panel.
