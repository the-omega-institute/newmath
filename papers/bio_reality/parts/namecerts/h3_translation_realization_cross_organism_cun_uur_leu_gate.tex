\subsection{Cross-organism CUN/UUR Leu usage gate}\label{sec:bioreality-cross-organism-cun-uur-leu-gate}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite row.}
This record treats the leucine codon pair $CUN/UUR$ as an organism-level usage-and-copy-number question, not as a protein-level assertion. The finite coordinate under inspection is the signed and scaled residual $\log(CUN/UUR)$, paired with the organism label, the corresponding tRNA-Leu gene-copy ratio, and an amino-acid-composition control on synonymous classes. The available run loaded three organism rows, namely Homo sapiens, Saccharomyces cerevisiae, and Escherichia coli K12. On those rows the measured log-ratio vector is
$$
\begin{aligned}
(1.3514980212896148,\;-0.24869078488829677,\;1.0086640520051702),
\end{aligned}
$$
with sign vector
$$
\begin{aligned}
(+1,\;-1,\;+1).
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the sign is not uniform across the three rows. The paired statistic reported by the run gives Pearson correlation $1.0$ and amino-acid-composition-controlled partial Pearson correlation $1.0$ with $n=3$. The experiment identifier is $cross\_organism\_cun\_uur\_leu\_gate\_validation$, and the recorded status is passed.

\paragraph{Codon coordinate.}
The row also keeps the lower-level codon coordinate explicit. The set $M$ is certified as the union $WNR\cup CUN$ and has cardinality $20$. Its listed members are
$$
\begin{aligned}
M=\{&AAA,AAG,ACA,ACG,AGA,AGG,AUA,AUG,CUA,CUC,\\
& CUG,CUU,UAA,UAG,UCA,UCG,UGA,UGG,UUA,UUG\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The leucine component used by this packet is the contrast between the four $CUN$ codons $CUA,CUC,CUG,CUU$ and the two $UUR$ codons $UUA,UUG$. The biological readout is therefore not a codon-label fact alone. It is the per-organism sign and magnitude of $\log(CUN/UUR)$ checked against the per-organism tRNA-Leu copy-ratio coordinate after controlling for amino-acid composition.

\paragraph{Empirical interpretation.}
The finite table supports a bounded cross-organism statement: within the three loaded organism rows, $\log(CUN/UUR)$ changes sign, and the controlled paired statistic aligns positively with the available tRNA-Leu gene-copy ratio. The result is best read as a contact between codon-usage summaries and genome-annotation summaries. The codon-usage contact reports per-organism codon-count or codon-frequency summaries over coding sequences. The tRNA contact reports organism-level tRNA-Leu gene-copy counts by anticodon family. Their conjunction is sufficient for a narrow translation-realization statistic about codon usage and tRNA-copy spectra, because the measured quantities live at the coding-sequence and genome-annotation level and are matched by organism label.

\paragraph{Scope boundary.}
This packet does not establish direct ribosomal translation dynamics, protein folding, physical admissibility of any protein product, biological function, a molecular mechanism, or a universal cross-organism law. The observed sign reversal is context-dependent in the finite table, not a rule asserted for all organisms. A raw $\log(CUN/UUR)$ value without the amino-acid-composition control is not treated as a translation-layer result here. Likewise, the standard genetic-code membership of $CUN$ and $UUR$ does not repair missing organism-matched data and does not by itself create biological realization. Any future claim about ribosome behavior, protein structure, function, physical admissibility, or universal directionality would require a separate contact with measurements at that level.

\paragraph{Audit conclusion.}
The row is admissible as a finite, externally checked organism-level statistic. Its positive result consists exactly of three loaded organism rows, non-uniform $CUN/UUR$ sign, Pearson correlation $1.0$, controlled partial Pearson correlation $1.0$ with $n=3$, and the explicit $20$-codon $M=WNR\cup CUN$ coordinate. The result remains narrow: it licenses the codon-usage/tRNA-copy contact just described and nothing above that layer.
