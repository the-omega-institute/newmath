\subsection{Cross-organism CUN/UUR Leu gate}\label{sec:h3-translation-realization-cross-organism-cun-uur-leu-gate}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite carrier.}
This record isolates a Leu-module statistic whose rows are indexed by organism labels and whose measured coordinates are the synonymous Leu usage contrast, the corresponding tRNA-Leu copy-number contrast, and an amino-acid-composition control.  The codon coordinate used for the CUN side is tied to the finite set certified by the companion code-table check
$$
\begin{aligned}
M = \{&AAA,AAG,ACA,ACG,AGA,AGG,AUA,AUG,CUA,CUC,CUG,CUU,\\
&UAA,UAG,UCA,UCG,UGA,UGG,UUA,UUG\},\qquad |M|=20.
\end{aligned}
$$
Within this set the CUN Leu block appearing in the present gate is $\{CUA,CUC,CUG,CUU\}$, while the UUR Leu block is $\{UUA,UUG\}$.  The observable coordinate is not a codon label by itself, but the organism-level residual contrast
$$
\begin{aligned}
L_o = \log\left({\#CUN_o \over \#UUR_o}\right)
\end{aligned}
$$
read together with an organism-level tRNA-Leu gene-copy ratio and with a control that prevents total amino-acid composition from being mistaken for a Leu-specific synonymous effect.  Thus the carrier for this packet is the paired finite table consisting of $L_o$, a tRNA-Leu copy-ratio coordinate, an organism label, and the local composition-control relation.

\paragraph{Observed finite rows.}
The checked table contains three organism rows: \texttt{homo\_sapiens}, \texttt{saccharomyces\_cerevisiae}, and \texttt{escherichia\_coli\_k12}.  The recorded Leu log-ratios are
$$
\begin{aligned}
L_{\mathrm{human}} &= 1.3514980212896148,\\
L_{\mathrm{yeast}} &= -0.24869078488829677,\\
L_{\mathrm{ecoli\_k12}} &= 1.0086640520051702.
\end{aligned}
$$
Their signs are $(1,-1,1)$, so the sign is not uniform across the three organisms.  The finite-run flag records \texttt{sign\_uniform = false}.  In the same run, the paired statistic reports Pearson correlation $1.0$ and a positive partial Pearson correlation $1.0$ after the stated amino-acid-composition control, with $n=3$ organism rows.  The experiment identifier is \texttt{cross\_organism\_cun\_uur\_leu\_gate\_validation}, and the recorded status is \texttt{passed}.  These numbers are enough to make the packet a bounded organism-level codon-usage and tRNA-copy contact, not merely a syntactic codon-window observation.

\paragraph{Reality contacts used.}
Two empirical contacts are active.  The first is a per-organism codon-usage readout: curated organism tables report codon-count or codon-frequency summaries over coding sequences.  This contact can test the distributional side of the Leu synonymous contrast, but it does not observe ribosome motion, protein shape, biological role, or a general law.  The second is an organism-level tRNA-Leu gene-copy readout: genome annotations report copy-number spectra by anticodon family.  This contact can test whether the Leu usage contrast is paired with a tRNA-copy contrast at the organism level, but it remains an annotation-level statistic.  The run therefore supports only the paired finite relation
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{sign}(L_o)\ \text{varies by organism},\qquad
\mathrm{partial\ corr}(L_o,\mathrm{tRNA\text{-}Leu\ ratio}_o\mid \mathrm{AA\ composition})=1.0,
\end{aligned}
$$
for the three loaded organisms.  It does not license extrapolation outside those rows.

\paragraph{Interpretation.}
The result is a controlled cross-organism sign-gate for the CUN/UUR Leu pair.  It says that, in the recorded finite table, human and \texttt{E. coli} K-12 have positive CUN-over-UUR log-contrast, yeast has negative contrast, and the measured contrast is perfectly ordered with the recorded tRNA-Leu copy-ratio coordinate under the available three-row control.  The appropriate scientific reading is therefore contextual: the direction of CUN versus UUR usage is not a universal sign rule, and the useful signal is the organism-indexed pairing with tRNA-Leu copy-number data.  A raw CUN/UUR ratio, without the organism-matched tRNA coordinate and the amino-acid-composition control, is not enough for this packet.

\paragraph{Local boundary.}
This packet does not establish actual translation dynamics, ribosome-mediated mechanism, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, or universality.  It also does not turn codon labels into protein-level evidence.  The finite evidence reaches only the attached codon-usage and tRNA-copy table: three organism rows, non-uniform Leu log-ratio signs, Pearson $1.0$, and partial Pearson $1.0$ with $n=3$.  Any stronger biological statement would require a separate contact, such as ribosome profiling, protein-output measurement, structural assay, functional assay, or an independently sampled broader organism panel.
