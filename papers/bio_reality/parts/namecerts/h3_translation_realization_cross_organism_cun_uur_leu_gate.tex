\subsection{Cross-organism CUN/UUR leucine usage as a bounded organism-level signal}\label{sec:bior-reality-cross-organism-cun-uur-leu-gate}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Record.}
This record concerns the leucine codon families $CUN$ and $UUR$ as organism-indexed sequence statistics.  The finite contact is not a direct measurement of ribosome motion or protein output.  It is a paired readout of codon-usage summaries and annotated tRNA-Leu gene-copy spectra, with the organism label retained as an explicit coordinate.  The local object is therefore the residual leucine-module statistic
$$
\begin{aligned}
  L(o) &= \log\left(\frac{\#CUN(o)}{\#UUR(o)}\right),\\
  T(o) &= \log\left(\frac{\hbox{CUN-facing tRNA-Leu copy count in }o}{\hbox{UUR-facing tRNA-Leu copy count in }o}\right),
\end{aligned}
$$
read only after the synonymous leucine class and amino-acid composition have been isolated from broader coding-sequence composition.  The three loaded organisms are $\mathrm{homo\_sapiens}$, $\mathrm{saccharomyces\_cerevisiae}$, and $\mathrm{escherichia\_coli\_k12}$.

\paragraph{Finite coordinate support.}
The accompanying code-table check supplies a finite codon set $M$ of size $20$:
$$
\begin{aligned}
M = \{&AAA,AAG,ACA,ACG,AGA,AGG,AUA,AUG,CUA,CUC,\\
     &CUG,CUU,UAA,UAG,UCA,UCG,UGA,UGG,UUA,UUG\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
This support is used only as a finite indexing and label-control object.  In particular, the presence of $CUA$, $CUC$, $CUG$, and $CUU$ in $M$ marks the $CUN$ side of the leucine pair, while $UUA$ and $UUG$ mark the $UUR$ side.  The code-table row itself does not settle organism-level adaptation; it only confirms that the codon labels used in the organism-level statistic are available as finite discrete coordinates.

\paragraph{Observed organism rows.}
The organism-level calculation reports the three log-ratios
$$
\begin{aligned}
  L(\mathrm{homo\_sapiens}) &= 1.3514980212896148,\\
  L(\mathrm{saccharomyces\_cerevisiae}) &= -0.24869078488829677,\\
  L(\mathrm{escherichia\_coli\_k12}) &= 1.0086640520051702.
\end{aligned}
$$
The corresponding signs are
$$
\begin{aligned}
  (+1,-1,+1),
\end{aligned}
$$
so the sign is not uniform across the three organisms.  The reported flag is $\mathrm{sign\_uniform}=\mathrm{false}$.  Thus the admissible conclusion is a context-indexed reversal in the finite rows, not a universal direction for leucine codon usage.

\paragraph{Paired tRNA-Leu statistic.}
The paired codon-usage and tRNA-Leu copy-number statistic reports Pearson correlation $1.0$ for the available rows.  After the stated amino-acid-composition control, the partial-correlation field reports
$$
\begin{aligned}
  n &= 3,\\
  r_{\mathrm{partial}} &= 1.0.
\end{aligned}
$$
Within this small finite table, larger or smaller $\log(CUN/UUR)$ therefore moves coherently with the organism-level tRNA-Leu copy-ratio coordinate.  The result is recorded as a passed bounded contact for the claim identifier $h3.translation\_realization.cross\_organism.cun\_uur\_leu\_gate$, with experiment id $cross\_organism\_cun\_uur\_leu\_gate\_validation$ and run id $675fdddb3187$.

\paragraph{Local interpretation.}
The allowed interpretation is narrow.  The finite rows support an externally checked association between a leucine codon-usage residual and an organism-level tRNA-Leu gene-copy ratio, under the supplied synonymous-class and amino-acid-composition control.  The non-uniform signs show that a single organism or a raw CUN/UUR ratio is not enough to state a direction-independent biological rule.  The perfect reported correlations are descriptive scalars on three rows, so they should be treated as a compact contact signal rather than as a population-level asymptotic estimate.

\paragraph{Boundary.}
No actual translational event, folded structure, physical admissibility, biological function, molecular mechanism, or universal law follows from this record by itself.  Codon-usage tables are sequence-composition summaries, and tRNA gene-copy counts are genome-annotation summaries.  They make contact with an organism-level translation-adjacent statistic, but they do not observe ribosome dynamics, stalling, aminoacylation efficiency, protein abundance, folding, activity, viability, or selection.  Any such stronger statement would require a separate contact carrying the relevant measurement rather than being inferred from the CUN/UUR sign, the tRNA-Leu wording, or the packet identifier.
