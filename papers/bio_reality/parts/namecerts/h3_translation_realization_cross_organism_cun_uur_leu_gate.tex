\subsection{Cross-organism Leu usage and the CUN/UUR gate}
\label{subsec:bior-h3-translation-realization-cross-organism-cun-uur-leu-gate}

\paragraph{Finite packet.}
This record concerns the organism-level Leu coding split between the CUN quartet and the UUR doublet.  The finite carrier is a paired codon-usage residual together with organism labels and an organism-level tRNA-Leu copy-number comparison.  The measured coordinate is the signed log-ratio $\log(\#\mathrm{CUN}/\#\mathrm{UUR})$, read only after keeping the Leu synonym class separate from unrelated amino-acid composition.  Thus the admissible biological readout is not a protein claim, but a controlled comparison between codon usage and a matched tRNA-Leu gene-copy spectrum.

\paragraph{Codon window.}
The associated code-window check fixes the finite set used by the packet.  The recorded set $M$ has cardinality $20$ and is
$$
\begin{aligned}
M={}&\{\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{ACA},\mathrm{ACG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\\
&\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
Within this finite window the Leu-relevant contrast is the CUN block $\{\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU}\}$ against the UUR block $\{\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\}$.  The window does not by itself measure translation.  It only identifies the codon classes whose organism-level counts can be compared to an independently curated tRNA-Leu annotation.

\paragraph{Observed rows.}
The finite validation row loaded three organisms: $\mathrm{homo\_sapiens}$, $\mathrm{saccharomyces\_cerevisiae}$, and $\mathrm{escherichia\_coli\_k12}$.  Their recorded CUN/UUR log-ratios were
$$
\begin{aligned}
(1.3514980212896148,\;-0.24869078488829677,\;1.0086640520051702),
\end{aligned}
$$
with sign vector
$$
\begin{aligned}
(+1,\;-1,\;+1).
\end{aligned}
$$
Consequently the sign is not uniform across the measured organisms; the stored Boolean value is $\mathrm{sign\_uniform}=\mathrm{false}$.  This is the first finite check required by the packet, since a universal same-sign result would have erased the proposed context dependence.

\paragraph{Paired statistic.}
The same validation row reports a positive organism-matched association with the tRNA-Leu copy-number ratio.  The ordinary Pearson value recorded for the paired statistic is $1.0$.  After the stated amino-acid-composition control, the partial-correlation entry has sample size $n=3$ and value
$$
\begin{aligned}
r_{\mathrm{partial}}=1.0.
\end{aligned}
$$
For this packet, those numbers are used only as a finite-row acceptance test: the sign reversal is present, and the controlled statistic is positive on the three loaded organisms.  The result is therefore a bounded translation-layer usage/contact statement about codon tables and tRNA gene-copy annotations, not a general biochemical law.

\paragraph{Local interpretation.}
The accepted content is the following narrow statement.  In the three-organism row, the Leu CUN/UUR usage residual changes sign across organisms, and the paired tRNA-Leu gene-copy comparison is positively aligned with the residual after the named composition control.  The packet therefore supports an organism-level codon-usage/tRNA-copy contact for the Leu module.  It does not say that the ribosome realizes a particular mechanism, does not establish that any translated protein folds, does not establish physical admissibility, does not assign biological function, and does not license a universal direction for the CUN/UUR contrast.  Any such stronger assertion would require a different measurement channel, for example direct ribosome dynamics, protein-output, folding, or functional data.

\paragraph{Failure modes kept in scope.}
The packet would fail if later organism-matched rows made the CUN/UUR sign uniform across the measured panel, or if the sign remained non-uniform but the amino-acid-controlled association with the tRNA-Leu copy-number ratio vanished.  It would also fail as a translation-layer claim if only raw codon labels or malformed packet identifiers were supplied, because identifier spelling and standard-code lookup do not provide organism-matched usage and tRNA-copy measurements.  The current finite row avoids that failure by recording both the non-uniform signs and the paired partial-correlation value, while still keeping the conclusion below mechanism, structure, physical admissibility, function, and universality.
