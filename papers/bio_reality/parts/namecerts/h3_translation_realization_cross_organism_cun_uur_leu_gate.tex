\subsection{Cross-Organism Leucine Codon-Usage Gate}
\label{subsec:bioreality-cross-organism-cun-uur-leu-gate}

\paragraph{Packet and measured carrier.}
This record treats the leucine codon split between the $CUN$ and $UUR$ synonym families as an organism-level statistical object, not as a direct mechanistic account of translation.  The measured carrier consists of a per-organism residual coordinate for $\log(CUN/UUR)$ paired with an organism label, an amino-acid-composition control, and an external tRNA-Leu gene-copy ratio.  The point of the packet is therefore narrow: it asks whether the sign and magnitude of the leucine codon-usage contrast vary across organisms in the same direction as a curated tRNA-Leu copy-number contrast, after the comparison is kept inside synonymous leucine usage rather than confused with whole-proteome amino-acid composition.

The supporting code-window coordinate used by the packet is the finite set
$$
\begin{aligned}
M=\{&AAA,AAG,ACA,ACG,AGA,AGG,AUA,AUG,CUA,CUC,\\
& CUG,CUU,UAA,UAG,UCA,UCG,UGA,UGG,UUA,UUG\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The verified cardinality is $|M|=20$, and the recorded readback identifies this same list as $WNR\cup CUN$.  This finite coordinate is only a routing and comparison device for the codon-usage packet.  By itself it does not assert translation, folding, physical admissibility, biological function, a ribosome-level mechanism, or a universal biological law.

\paragraph{Finite organism rows.}
The realized cross-organism table contains three organism rows: Homo sapiens, Saccharomyces cerevisiae, and Escherichia coli K12.  For these rows the observed $\log(CUN/UUR)$ values are
$$
\begin{aligned}
&1.3514980212896148,\quad -0.24869078488829677,\quad 1.0086640520051702,
\end{aligned}
$$
with sign vector
$$
\begin{aligned}
&(1,-1,1).
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the finite table rejects uniform sign behavior in the measured rows: the recorded value of \texttt{sign\_uniform} is false.  The sign reversal is not interpreted as an organism-independent directionality rule.  It is only the local observation that these three curated rows do not all place the leucine codon split on the same side of the $CUN/UUR$ balance.

\paragraph{Paired tRNA-Leu statistic.}
The packet also records a paired comparison with organism-level tRNA-Leu gene-copy spectra.  On the three available rows, the ordinary Pearson value is $1.0$.  The amino-acid-composition-controlled statistic is reported as a positive partial correlation with $n=3$ and
$$
\begin{aligned}
r_{\mathrm{partial}}=1.0.
\end{aligned}
$$
This is a finite-row pass for the stated packet because the support condition was not merely that $\log(CUN/UUR)$ varies; it was that the non-uniform leucine codon-usage contrast remains aligned with the tRNA-Leu copy-number ratio after the synonymous-class control.  The experiment identifier attached to the realized row is \texttt{cross\_organism\_cun\_uur\_leu\_gate\_validation}, and the status field is \texttt{passed}.

\paragraph{Interpretation kept inside the measured layer.}
The admissible biological statement is deliberately small.  Per-organism codon-usage tables provide sequence-level summaries of coding regions, and tRNA gene-copy annotations provide genome-level summaries of anticodon-family availability.  Their conjunction can support a bounded translation-realization contact only in the sense of an organism-level codon-usage and tRNA-copy-number statistic.  It does not observe ribosome traffic, elongation kinetics, protein structure, protein folding, molecular function, cellular fitness, or physical admissibility.  A separate contact would be required before any of those claims could be made.

\paragraph{Failure modes excluded by the record.}
The passed statistic also leaves several tempting overreadings outside the chapter.  A raw $CUN/UUR$ log-ratio without amino-acid-composition control is not enough for this packet.  A single organism pair is not enough to state a universal cross-organism law.  A sign reversal is not evidence of a ribosome-mediated mechanism unless ribosome-profiling, stAI, or another direct translation-process contact is added.  Codon-label readback is likewise insufficient: knowing that $CUA,CUC,CUG,CUU$ lie in $CUN$ and $UUA,UUG$ lie in $UUR$ supplies the vocabulary of the comparison, not the biological realization of the comparison.

\paragraph{Local verdict.}
Within the three-row finite table, the leucine codon split is ready as a bounded organism-level translation-realization statistic.  The record supports the claim that $\log(CUN/UUR)$ is not sign-uniform across the measured organisms and that its measured contrast is positively aligned with the tRNA-Leu gene-copy ratio under the stated amino-acid-composition control.  The result remains local to the available organism rows and to the two attached contact types: codon-usage summaries and tRNA-Leu gene-copy annotations.
