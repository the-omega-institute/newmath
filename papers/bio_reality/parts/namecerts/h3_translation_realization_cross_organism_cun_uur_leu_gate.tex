The finite carrier has four recorded distinctions: the coordinate $\log(\#\mathrm{CUN}/\#\mathrm{UUR})$, the organism-level tRNA-Leu gene-copy ratio, the external organism label, and an amino-acid-composition control on synonymous codon classes.  Its internal shape is only a coordinate together with a relation.  The biological readout is therefore narrow: for each organism row, the sign and magnitude of $\log(\#\mathrm{CUN}/\#\mathrm{UUR})$ are compared with the corresponding external tRNA-Leu copy-number spectrum after the amino-acid composition control has been applied.

\paragraph{Admissible reading.}
The passed finite test supports only the following bounded statement.  In the three loaded organism rows, CUN/UUR usage is not sign-uniform, and the measured residual is positively aligned with the organism-level tRNA-Leu copy-number ratio under the recorded control.  This is a contact between codon-usage summaries and tRNA gene-copy annotation summaries.  It is not a direct observation of ribosome dynamics, not a measurement of protein output, and not a claim about the structural or functional consequences of Leu codon choice.

\paragraph{Local boundary.}
No stronger biological conclusion follows from this packet alone.  The row does not establish translation as a physical event, folding, physical admissibility, biological function, a ribosome-mediated mechanism, or a universal cross-organism law.  The sign reversal is reported as context-dependent finite data, not as an invariant direction.  A claim at any of those higher levels would require a separate contact that directly measures the relevant layer, such as ribosome profiling, protein-level output, structural data, functional assay data, or a broader externally paired organism panel.  The present chapter therefore treats the result as a finite organism-level codon-usage/tRNA-copy statistic with a passed status, not as a general biological principle.
