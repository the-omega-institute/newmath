\subsection{Cross-organism CUN/UUR Leu gate}
\label{sec:bioreality-cross-organism-cun-uur-leu-gate}

\paragraph{Object.}
This record treats the leucine codon split between the CUN block and the UUR block as an organism-indexed statistical object, not as a direct statement about ribosomal dynamics.  The finite carrier consists of four named coordinates: the signed value $\log(\#CUN/\#UUR)$, the organism-level ratio of tRNA-Leu gene-copy counts for the matching anticodon families, the organism label, and an amino-acid-composition control that keeps the comparison inside synonymous codon usage rather than total proteome composition.  The readout is therefore a paired per-organism statistic: for each organism with both tables available, one records the CUN-over-UUR log-ratio and compares it with the corresponding tRNA-Leu copy-number ratio after the synonymous-class control has been applied.

\paragraph{Finite codon support.}
The associated code-window check uses the finite set $M=WNR\cup CUN$ with cardinality $20$.  In the recorded run this set is
$$
\begin{aligned}
M=\{&AAA,AAG,ACA,ACG,AGA,AGG,AUA,AUG,CUA,CUC,\\
& CUG,CUU,UAA,UAG,UCA,UCG,UGA,UGG,UUA,UUG\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The four CUN leucine codons appearing in this support are $CUA,CUC,CUG,CUU$, and the two UUR leucine codons appearing in the same support are $UUA,UUG$.  This finite support does not by itself assert any biological realization; it only fixes the codon labels on which the organism-level usage statistic is evaluated.

\paragraph{Measured rows.}
The checked finite run loaded three organism rows: $homo\_sapiens$, $saccharomyces\_cerevisiae$, and $escherichia\_coli\_k12$.  Their recorded $\log(\#CUN/\#UUR)$ values were
$$
\begin{aligned}
1.3514980212896148,\quad -0.24869078488829677,\quad 1.0086640520051702.
\end{aligned}
$$
The corresponding sign vector was
$$
\begin{aligned}
(+1, -1, +1),
\end{aligned}
$$
so the sign was not uniform across the measured organisms.  The stored Boolean value for uniformity is therefore $false$.  This is the first admissible contact: the CUN/UUR split changes direction across the three organism labels rather than presenting a single organism-independent sign.

\paragraph{Paired tRNA-Leu relation.}
The same run reports a positive paired relation with the tRNA-Leu copy-number ratio.  The ordinary Pearson value is $1.0$, and the amino-acid-composition-controlled partial Pearson statistic is also $1.0$ on $n=3$ organism rows.  In formula form, the recorded controlled statistic is
$$
\begin{aligned}
r_{partial}(\log(\#CUN/\#UUR),\,\rho_{tRNA\text{-}Leu}\mid AA\text{-}composition)=1.0,\qquad n=3.
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the finite table supports only the bounded claim that, in these three loaded organisms, the non-uniform CUN/UUR sign and magnitude align positively with the organism-level tRNA-Leu gene-copy ratio after the specified composition control.  The result is a translation-layer usage-and-copy-number contact in the narrow statistical sense: it joins codon usage summaries to tRNA gene-copy annotations.  It is not a measurement of elongation speed, ribosome occupancy, charging state, or codon-context dynamics.

\paragraph{Admissible conclusion.}
The packet is accepted as a finite cross-organism Leu-module gate because the recorded rows satisfy both required checks.  First, the CUN/UUR log-ratio sign is context-dependent across the measured organisms.  Second, the paired statistic against tRNA-Leu copy ratio is positive after the amino-acid-composition control, with the stored partial correlation equal to $1.0$ for $n=3$.  These facts are enough to retain the organism-indexed CUN/UUR statistic as a BioReality translation-realization contact at the codon-usage and tRNA-copy level.

\paragraph{Local limits.}
No stronger biological conclusion is licensed by this table.  The data do not establish protein folding, protein structure, physical admissibility of a molecule, biological function, ribosome-mediated mechanism, or a universal law of leucine codon choice.  A raw CUN/UUR sign without the composition control is also insufficient for the translation-layer statement used here.  Any claim about folding, function, physical mechanism, ribosome dynamics, or universality would require a separate measurement channel aimed at that object.  Within this record, sign reversal is reported only as organism-dependent behavior in a three-row codon-usage and tRNA-copy comparison.
