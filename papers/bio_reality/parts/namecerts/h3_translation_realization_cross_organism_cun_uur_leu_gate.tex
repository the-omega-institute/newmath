\subsection{Cross-organism CUN/UUR Leu gate}
\label{sec:bior-reality-cross-organism-cun-uur-leu-gate}

\paragraph{Question.}
This record isolates a translation-layer contact for the leucine codon split between the $CUN$ family and the $UUR$ family.  The object under test is not a protein, a ribosome trajectory, or a universal rule of the genetic code.  It is a finite organism-indexed statistic: for each organism in the measured row set, compare the sign and magnitude of $\log(CUN/UUR)$ with the organism-level tRNA-Leu gene-copy ratio, while keeping amino-acid composition from doing the explanatory work by itself.  The coordinate therefore has four named readings: $\log(CUN/UUR)$, the tRNA-Leu gene-copy ratio, the organism label, and an amino-acid-composition control.

\paragraph{Finite codon support.}
The companion code-table check fixes the finite codon set used by the packet.  The verified row gives $|M|=20$, with
$$
\begin{aligned}
M={}&\{AAA,AAG,ACA,ACG,AGA,AGG,AUA,AUG,CUA,CUC,CUG,CUU,\\
&UAA,UAG,UCA,UCG,UGA,UGG,UUA,UUG\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The four $CUN$ leucine codons appearing in this finite support are $CUA,CUC,CUG,CUU$, and the two $UUR$ leucine codons are $UUA,UUG$.  This makes the leucine contrast a bounded comparison inside the recorded codon alphabet rather than an open-ended biological assertion.

\paragraph{Measured organism rows.}
The cross-organism validation row reports data loaded for three organisms: $\mathrm{homo\_sapiens}$, $\mathrm{saccharomyces\_cerevisiae}$, and $\mathrm{escherichia\_coli\_k12}$.  The measured $\log(CUN/UUR)$ values are
$$
\begin{aligned}
1.3514980212896148,\quad -0.24869078488829677,\quad 1.0086640520051702.
\end{aligned}
$$
Their signs are
$$
\begin{aligned}
+1,\quad -1,\quad +1,
\end{aligned}
$$
so the finite row explicitly records $\mathrm{sign\_uniform}=\mathrm{false}$.  The sign reversal matters only as a bounded organism-level observation: it says that the $CUN$ versus $UUR$ preference is not constant across the three reported organisms.  It does not say that one direction is biologically privileged in all contexts.

\paragraph{Paired statistic.}
The validation row records $n=3$ for the controlled paired statistic and gives partial Pearson correlation $1.0$ with the tRNA-Leu ratio after the stated amino-acid composition control.  It also records an ordinary Pearson value $1.0$ for the loaded finite row.  In local notation, the reported relation is
$$
\begin{aligned}
r_{\mathrm{partial}}(\log(CUN/UUR),\,\mathrm{tRNA\mbox{-}Leu\ ratio}\mid\mathrm{AA\ composition})=1.0,\qquad n=3.
\end{aligned}
$$
No $p$-value, confidence interval, permutation count, lambda parameter, hit rate, or larger row count is present in the supplied finite record, so none is asserted here.  The statistic is therefore read as a small controlled contact, not as a population-level law.

\paragraph{Contact discipline.}
The codon-usage contact supplies per-organism codon-count or codon-frequency summaries for coding sequences.  The tRNA contact supplies organism-level tRNA-Leu gene-copy counts by anticodon family.  These are compatible contacts for asking whether the codon-usage residual and the tRNA-Leu copy spectrum move together in the observed row set.  They are not direct measurements of elongation rate, ribosome occupancy, aminoacylation state, protein abundance, folding, organismal phenotype, or physical admissibility.  The only accepted reading is that the finite organism-indexed codon statistic passes the stated cross-organism pairing test in the recorded data.

\paragraph{Blocked extensions.}
Several stronger interpretations are explicitly outside the local evidence.  A raw sign for $\log(CUN/UUR)$ is not by itself a translation claim.  A mixed-case packet identifier, spelling convention, or codon-label lookup is not biological evidence.  The standard code table can identify codon labels, but it cannot repair a missing organism-matched usage-versus-tRNA statistic.  The present row also does not provide ribosome profiling, stAI, proteomics, structure, folding, function, physical realization, or a mechanism.  Thus no observed translation event, no folding claim, no physical-admissibility claim, no functional claim, no mechanistic explanation, and no universal cross-organism law follows unless an additional contact records that specific kind of measurement.

\paragraph{Local verdict.}
Within the supplied finite data, the packet is admissible as a small organism-level translation-adjacent statistic.  The support is the combination of the twenty-codon finite support, the three loaded organism rows, non-uniform $CUN/UUR$ signs, and the reported positive controlled correlation with tRNA-Leu copy ratio.  Its scope remains narrow: it records a bounded codon-usage/tRNA-copy relation after amino-acid composition control, and it stops before making claims about actual translation dynamics or any higher biological layer.
