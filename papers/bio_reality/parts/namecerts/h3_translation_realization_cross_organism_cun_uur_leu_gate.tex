\subsection{Cross-organism CUN/UUR Leu usage gate}\label{subsec:biorality-cross-organism-cun-uur-leu-gate}\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Packet and coordinate.} This record treats the leucine codon split between the CUN block and the UUR block as an organism-indexed usage statistic, not as a direct observation of ribosomal motion. The finite carrier is the residual pair consisting of $\log(\#\mathrm{CUN}/\#\mathrm{UUR})$ and an organism-matched tRNA-Leu gene-copy ratio, with organism labels retained as part of the row identity. The intended control is amino-acid composition: the comparison is meaningful only after the Leu module has been separated from whole-proteome amino-acid abundance effects. The coordinate therefore has four named distinctions: the log CUN-over-UUR value, the tRNA-Leu copy-number ratio, the organism label, and the amino-acid-composition control. The readout is a per-organism sign and magnitude comparison, checked against tRNA-Leu copy spectra rather than against protein structure or function.

\paragraph{Finite row.} The checked finite row contains three organisms: \texttt{homo\_sapiens}, \texttt{saccharomyces\_cerevisiae}, and \texttt{escherichia\_coli\_k12}. The reported CUN/UUR log-ratios are
$$
\begin{aligned}
(1.3514980212896148,\;-0.24869078488829677,\;1.0086640520051702),
\end{aligned}
$$
with corresponding signs
$$
\begin{aligned}
(+1,\;-1,\;+1).
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the sign is not uniform across the three-row organism panel. The run records \texttt{sign\_uniform = false} and assigns the validation status \texttt{passed}. The same finite run reports a Pearson value of $1.0$ for the paired statistic and a positive partial correlation with the tRNA-Leu ratio after the stated control, with $n=3$ and partial Pearson value $1.0$. These numbers are not used as population-level inference; they certify only that the finite row realizes the proposed organism-indexed sign reversal and that, within this row, the controlled paired statistic has the recorded positive direction.

\paragraph{Codon-module support.} The companion code-table fact fixes the internal codon module used by the packet. The module $M$ has cardinality $20$ and is recorded as the union of WNR and CUN codons. Its listed elements are
$$
\begin{aligned}
M=\{&\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{ACA},\mathrm{ACG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\\
&\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The finite support fact is simply that $|M|=20$ and that the listed set is the recorded WNR--CUN module. In the present packet, this support is used to keep the CUN side of the Leu comparison explicit; it does not enlarge the biological conclusion beyond the organism-level codon-usage and tRNA-copy contacts.

\paragraph{Contact interpretation.} The two admitted contacts are per-organism codon-usage summaries and organism-level tRNA-Leu gene-copy annotations. The first contact can test whether coding-sequence summaries assign different CUN and UUR usage profiles across organisms. The second can test whether annotated tRNA-Leu copy spectra vary in a way that can be paired with those usage summaries. Both contacts are database-level summaries. They are adequate for a bounded cross-organism usage-and-copy-number statistic, but they are not measurements of translation kinetics, ribosome occupancy, protein folding, biochemical admissibility, cellular function, or an invariant law across life.

\paragraph{Local conclusion.} The finite evidence supports the following bounded statement. In the three-organism row, the CUN/UUR log-ratio changes sign, and the recorded controlled paired statistic with the tRNA-Leu gene-copy ratio is positive with partial Pearson value $1.0$ at $n=3$. This is enough to keep the CUN/UUR Leu gate as a translation-layer contact in the narrow sense of codon-usage and tRNA-copy association. It is not a claim that translation has been directly observed, nor that a ribosome-mediated mechanism has been established. It also says nothing about folded structure, physical admissibility, biological function, or universal directionality. Any such stronger statement would require a separate contact with the relevant measurement type.
