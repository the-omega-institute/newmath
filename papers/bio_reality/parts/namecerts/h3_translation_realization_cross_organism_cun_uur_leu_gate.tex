\subsection{Cross-organism CUN/UUR Leu gate}
\label{sec:bior-h3-cross-organism-cun-uur-leu-gate}

\paragraph{Record.}
This record treats the leucine codon split between the $\mathrm{CUN}$ family and the $\mathrm{UUR}$ family as an organism-indexed statistic rather than as a direct mechanistic statement. The finite carrier is the residual coordinate obtained from a per-organism comparison of $\log(\#\mathrm{CUN}/\#\mathrm{UUR})$, paired with organism labels and with the corresponding tRNA-Leu gene-copy ratio. The admissible readout is therefore narrow: for each organism in the finite table, read the sign and size of the CUN-over-UUR log ratio, then compare that row with the organism-level tRNA-Leu copy-number spectrum after controlling for amino-acid composition inside the synonymous leucine class.

\paragraph{Codon window.}
The associated codon-window certificate uses the finite set
$$
\begin{aligned}
M=\{&\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{ACA},\mathrm{ACG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\\
&\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The verified cardinality is $|M|=20$. In this packet the role of this finite codon list is only to delimit the coordinate window in which $\mathrm{CUN}$ and $\mathrm{UUR}$ are compared. It does not by itself observe a ribosome, a peptide output, a folded structure, a cellular phenotype, or any universal biological law.

\paragraph{Finite organism table.}
The checked row set contains three organisms: $\mathrm{homo\_sapiens}$, $\mathrm{saccharomyces\_cerevisiae}$, and $\mathrm{escherichia\_coli\_k12}$. The recorded log-ratio vector is
$$
\begin{aligned}
(1.3514980212896148,\ -0.24869078488829677,\ 1.0086640520051702),
\end{aligned}
$$
with sign vector
$$
\begin{aligned}
(1,\ -1,\ 1).
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the finite row table does not have a uniform sign: one row is negative while two rows are positive. The stored experiment reports $\mathrm{sign\_uniform}=\mathrm{false}$ and status $\mathrm{passed}$ for the cross-organism CUN/UUR Leu-gate validation.

\paragraph{Paired statistic.}
The organism-matched statistic is recorded with a Pearson value of $1.0$ and a partial Pearson value of $1.0$ for $n=3$ rows after the amino-acid-composition control specified for the synonymous leucine comparison. In local notation, if $x_i$ is the controlled CUN-over-UUR log-ratio residual and $y_i$ is the corresponding tRNA-Leu copy-ratio coordinate, the recorded finite-row claim is the positive association
$$
\begin{aligned}
\rho(x,y)=1.0,\qquad \rho(x,y\mid \mathrm{AA\ composition})=1.0,\qquad n=3.
\end{aligned}
$$
This is a bounded organism-table contact. It says that the three stored rows are consistent with a positive controlled association between the leucine codon-usage split and the tRNA-Leu copy-number spectrum, while also showing that the raw CUN/UUR sign is context-dependent across the row set.

\paragraph{Interpretation.}
The scientific content of the packet is the following restricted statement. A CUN/UUR leucine coordinate becomes admissible at this layer only when it is attached to organism-indexed codon-usage data and organism-indexed tRNA-Leu copy-number data, and only when the amino-acid-composition control is kept in the statistic. Under those conditions, the finite table supports the local claim that CUN/UUR sign and magnitude are not organism-invariant and that the controlled residual is positively aligned with the tRNA-Leu copy-ratio coordinate in the stored three-row sample.

\paragraph{Local boundary.}
No stronger biological conclusion is licensed by this record. The codon-usage table is a sequence-composition summary, and the tRNA-copy table is a genome-annotation summary. From these rows alone, no realized translation event, no ribosome-level dynamics, no folding statement, no physical admissibility statement, no protein function, no mechanism, and no universal cross-organism rule follows. Any such conclusion would require an additional matched contact, such as ribosome profiling, protein-output measurement, structural evidence, functional assay evidence, or another directly relevant observation. The sign reversal is therefore retained as a context-sensitive organism-table fact, not promoted into a general directionality principle.
