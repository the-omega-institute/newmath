\subsection{Cross-organism CUN/UUR leucine gate}
\label{sec:h3-translation-realization-cross-organism-cun-uur-leu-gate}

\paragraph{Finite packet.}
This record isolates a leucine-specific codon-usage question at organism scale. The measured coordinate is the signed residual comparing the CUN leucine block with the UUR leucine pair, read together with organism labels and a tRNA-Leu gene-copy ratio. The admissible biological content is deliberately narrow: the row does not observe peptide folding, protein activity, ribosome kinetics, or a general rule of life. It records whether a finite organism-indexed codon-usage statistic changes with a finite organism-indexed tRNA-Leu copy-number statistic after the amino-acid-composition control demanded by the probe.

The companion code-table row fixes the relevant codon carrier. The listed module $M$ has cardinality $20$ and is exactly the union of WNR with CUN in the supplied finite table:
$$
\begin{aligned}
M = \{&\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{ACA},\mathrm{ACG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\\
&\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\\
&\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The CUN subblock appearing in this carrier is $\{\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU}\}$, while the leucine comparison pair named by the probe is CUN versus UUR. The packet therefore uses a finite codon coordinate, not an inferred molecular mechanism.

\paragraph{Organism rows and signed statistic.}
The cross-organism run loaded three organism labels: $\mathrm{homo\_sapiens}$, $\mathrm{saccharomyces\_cerevisiae}$, and $\mathrm{escherichia\_coli\_k12}$. For these $n=3$ rows, the observed signed log-ratios for $\log(\#\mathrm{CUN}/\#\mathrm{UUR})$ were
$$
\begin{aligned}
(1.3514980212896148,\;-0.24869078488829677,\;1.0086640520051702).
\end{aligned}
$$
Their signs were
$$
\begin{aligned}
(+1,\;-1,\;+1),
\end{aligned}
$$
so the sign is not uniform across the measured organisms. This is the first finite check in the row: a single cross-organism direction is not supported by these data. The yeast row reverses sign relative to the human and bacterial rows in the reported ordering, so any statement that treats CUN-over-UUR preference as globally positive or globally negative is rejected by the packet itself.

\paragraph{Paired tRNA-Leu contact.}
The second check pairs the CUN/UUR statistic with organism-level tRNA-Leu gene-copy information. The reported ordinary Pearson value is $1.0$, and the amino-acid-composition-controlled partial Pearson statistic is also $1.0$ on $n=3$ rows:
$$
\begin{aligned}
r_{\mathrm{Pearson}} &= 1.0,\\
r_{\mathrm{partial}\mid \mathrm{AA\ composition}} &= 1.0,\\
n &= 3.
\end{aligned}
$$
Within this finite table, the run status is recorded as passed. The biological interpretation is therefore limited to a positive organism-level association in the supplied paired rows: after the amino-acid-composition control used by the run, the sign and magnitude of the CUN/UUR leucine statistic agree monotonically with the tRNA-Leu copy-ratio coordinate in this three-organism sample.

\paragraph{What the row permits.}
The row permits a bounded translation-layer reading only in the following sense. Codon-usage summaries are external sequence-derived organism summaries, and tRNA gene-copy counts are external genome-annotation summaries. Their pairing allows a finite statistical contact between leucine codon usage and tRNA-Leu copy-number spectra. The admissible statement is that the supplied organism rows show non-uniform CUN/UUR sign and a positive controlled association with the supplied tRNA-Leu ratio coordinate. This is enough to keep the CUN/UUR leucine gate as an organism-level translation-realization statistic for this finite packet.

\paragraph{Local boundary.}
No broader biological conclusion follows from this row. The packet does not establish ribosome-mediated translation dynamics, protein folding, physical admissibility, biological function, or a universal cross-organism law. It also does not turn the sign reversal into a general rule: the reversal is reported as context-dependent within the finite organism set. A raw CUN/UUR log-ratio without the amino-acid-composition control is not accepted here as a translation-layer claim. Any statement about mechanism, structure, function, physical realizability, or universality would require a separate contact with the appropriate measurement type.

\paragraph{Null checks retained.}
The blocked cases attached to the row explain why the scope is kept narrow. Identifier shape is not evidence; a lowercase normalized probe id can route the packet, but spelling cannot validate biology. The standard genetic-code table can confirm codon labels, but it cannot supply organism-matched CUN/UUR usage, tRNA-Leu copy-number spectra, or a controlled cross-organism correlation. Conversely, the present run supplies the finite paired statistic for the listed organisms, so the ready claim is the controlled association just stated, not a higher-layer biological assertion.
