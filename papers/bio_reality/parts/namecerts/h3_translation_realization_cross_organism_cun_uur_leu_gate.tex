\subsection{Cross-organism CUN/UUR Leucine Usage Gate}\label{sec:h3-translation-realization-cross-organism-cun-uur-leu-gate}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite record.}
This record isolates one organism-level leucine-codon statistic.  The carrier is the residual comparison between the CUN and UUR leucine codon modules, paired with organism labels and an external tRNA-Leu gene-copy summary.  The relevant coordinate is the signed quantity $\log(\#\mathrm{CUN}/\#\mathrm{UUR})$, read only after controlling for amino-acid composition within the synonymous leucine class.  The finite run loaded three organisms: $\mathrm{homo\_sapiens}$, $\mathrm{saccharomyces\_cerevisiae}$, and $\mathrm{escherichia\_coli\_k12}$.  The associated standard-code finite set used by the row has cardinality $20$ and is explicitly
$$
\begin{aligned}
M=\{&\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{ACA},\mathrm{ACG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\\
&\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The CUN side of the leucine pair is therefore represented in this finite row by $\mathrm{CUA}$, $\mathrm{CUC}$, $\mathrm{CUG}$, and $\mathrm{CUU}$, while the UUR side is represented by $\mathrm{UUA}$ and $\mathrm{UUG}$ inside the same enumerated code table.

\paragraph{Measured sign pattern.}
The checked values record the three log-ratios
$$
\begin{aligned}
(1.3514980212896148,\;-0.24869078488829677,\;1.0086640520051702),
\end{aligned}
$$
with signs
$$
\begin{aligned}
(+1,\;-1,\;+1).
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the sign is not uniform across the three organisms.  This is the first finite obstruction to treating the CUN/UUR preference as a single organism-independent direction.  The row also records a Pearson value of $1.0$ for the paired statistic used in the run, and a positive partial correlation with the tRNA-Leu copy-ratio coordinate after amino-acid composition control,
$$
\begin{aligned}
n&=3,\\
r_{\mathrm{partial}}&=1.0.
\end{aligned}
$$
Within this finite dataset, the admissible conclusion is therefore narrow: the organism labels, the controlled CUN/UUR log-ratio, and the tRNA-Leu copy-ratio coordinate co-vary in the recorded three-row table.  The sign reversal is part of the evidence, not an exception to be erased.

\paragraph{Contact interpretation.}
The codon-usage contact contributes per-organism coding-sequence summaries.  Such tables are sequence-composition summaries; they do not directly observe ribosome motion, nascent-chain behavior, folding, selection, or organismal function.  The tRNA contact contributes organism-level tRNA-Leu gene-copy counts by anticodon family.  Those counts are genome-annotation summaries; they can be paired with codon usage, but they are not themselves a dynamic translation assay.  The row is consequently a controlled association between two external summaries: codon usage on one side and tRNA-Leu copy spectrum on the other.  The finite evidence supports the use of this pair as a translation-realization probe only at the level of organism-matched usage and copy-number statistics.

\paragraph{Local limit.}
No stronger biological conclusion is licensed by this row alone.  It does not establish an actual ribosome-mediated translation event, protein folding, physical admissibility of a molecule, biological function, mechanistic causation, or a universal cross-organism law.  A raw CUN/UUR sign without amino-acid composition control is also insufficient for the claim handled here.  Promotion beyond this narrow statistic would require a separate contact, for example ribosome-profiling data for translational dynamics, protein-output data for realized products, structural data for folding, or functional assays for organismal effect.  In the present finite record the only positive statement is that, for the three loaded organisms, the controlled CUN/UUR leucine-codon statistic is non-uniform in sign and aligns with the recorded tRNA-Leu copy-ratio coordinate with partial Pearson value $1.0$.

\paragraph{Verdict.}
The row passes as a bounded cross-organism usage-and-copy-number result.  The passing status belongs to the experiment $\mathrm{cross\_organism\_cun\_uur\_leu\_gate\_validation}$ with run identifier $675\mathrm{fdddb}3187$.  Its usable content is the finite association just stated: three organism rows, non-uniform CUN/UUR log-ratio signs, and a positive controlled tRNA-Leu copy-ratio correlation.  The same record explicitly blocks function, structure, physical admissibility, global law, and context-free mechanism readings.
