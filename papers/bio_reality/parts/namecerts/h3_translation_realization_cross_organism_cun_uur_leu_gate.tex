\subsection{Cross-organism CUN/UUR Leu gate}
\label{subsec:h3-translation-realization-cross-organism-cun-uur-leu-gate}

\paragraph{Finite packet.}
This record treats the leucine codon split between the $\mathrm{CUN}$ family and the $\mathrm{UUR}$ family as an organism-indexed usage statistic, not as a direct observation of ribosomal motion or protein output.  The measured coordinate is the per-organism value $\log(\#\mathrm{CUN}/\#\mathrm{UUR})$, paired with an organism label and checked against the corresponding tRNA-Leu gene-copy spectrum after an amino-acid-composition control.  The standard-code support row fixes the internal codon carrier used by the packet as a twenty-element set,
$$
\begin{aligned}
M=\{&\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{ACA},\mathrm{ACG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\\
&\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\},\qquad |M|=20.
\end{aligned}
$$
Within this carrier, the leucine comparison is concentrated on $\mathrm{CUN}=\{\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU}\}$ and $\mathrm{UUR}=\{\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\}$.  The biological row is therefore a finite cross-organism residual: codon usage supplies the numerator and denominator, tRNA-Leu annotation supplies the paired copy-number direction, and the amino-acid control prevents a raw synonymous-count imbalance from being reported as a translation-layer conclusion by itself.

\paragraph{Observed finite rows.}
The validation row loaded three organism-level records: \texttt{homo\_sapiens}, \texttt{saccharomyces\_cerevisiae}, and \texttt{escherichia\_coli\_k12}.  Their reported log-ratio values were
$$
\begin{aligned}
(1.3514980212896148,\;-0.24869078488829677,\;1.0086640520051702),
\end{aligned}
$$
with signs
$$
\begin{aligned}
(+1,\;-1,\;+1).
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the sign is not uniform across the three organisms.  In the same finite row, the reported Pearson value for the paired statistic is $1.0$, and the amino-acid-composition-controlled partial Pearson statistic is also $1.0$ with $n=3$.  These numbers are read narrowly: in this packet they say that the three loaded organisms exhibit a nonconstant $\log(\#\mathrm{CUN}/\#\mathrm{UUR})$ sign and that, after the stated composition control, the recorded CUN/UUR statistic is positively aligned with the tRNA-Leu copy-ratio coordinate in this three-row table.

\paragraph{Admissible interpretation.}
The finite result supports only an organism-level translation-realization contact in the restricted sense that codon-usage summaries and tRNA-Leu gene-copy annotations can be paired and checked for a controlled cross-organism relation.  The result is not a rule assigning one universal direction to the CUN/UUR sign: the yeast row has the opposite sign from the human and \emph{E. coli} rows in the reported table.  The correct local reading is therefore contextual variation with a positive paired statistic in the available three-organism dataset, not a claim that every organism must prefer the same leucine codon family.

\paragraph{Local exclusion boundary.}
No broader biological conclusion is licensed by these rows without an additional measured contact.  In particular, the packet does not establish translation in the stronger sense of observed ribosome dynamics, and it does not establish folding, physical admissibility, protein function, mechanism, or a universal biological law.  Codon-usage tables are sequence-composition summaries, and tRNA gene-copy counts are genome-annotation summaries; neither one by itself observes a translating ribosome or a folded protein.  The validation row explicitly keeps the CUN/UUR and tRNA-Leu contact below structure and function: it records that protein folding is not established, biological function is not established, the relation is not promoted to a global law, and the sign reversal is context-dependent rather than universal.

\paragraph{Audit verdict.}
The packet is ready as a bounded finite-row claim because the tested row supplies the organism list, the three log-ratio values, the three signs, the nonuniform-sign verdict, and the positive controlled correlation statistic.  Its force is exactly the finite contact just described: across the three loaded organisms, the CUN/UUR leucine usage coordinate is nonuniform and positively paired with the tRNA-Leu copy-ratio coordinate after the stated composition control.  Anything beyond that requires a separate experimental or curated contact at the corresponding biological layer.
