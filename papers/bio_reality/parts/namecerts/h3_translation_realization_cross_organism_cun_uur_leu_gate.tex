\subsection{Cross-organism CUN/UUR Leu usage as a bounded organism statistic}\label{subsec:h3-translation-realization-cross-organism-cun-uur-leu-gate}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite carrier.}
This record concerns the Leu codon pair split between the CUN block and the UUR block, read only at the level of organism-indexed sequence and annotation summaries. The carrier is a finite table whose rows are organisms and whose measured coordinate is the signed quantity $\log(\#\mathrm{CUN}/\#\mathrm{UUR})$, paired with an organism-level ratio of tRNA-Leu gene-copy support. The row labels used in the recorded run are $\mathrm{homo\_sapiens}$, $\mathrm{saccharomyces\_cerevisiae}$, and $\mathrm{escherichia\_coli\_k12}$, so the active table has $n=3$ organism rows. The associated code-window certificate also records the finite codon set
$$
\begin{aligned}
M=\{&\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{ACA},\mathrm{ACG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\\
&\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\},
\end{aligned}
$$
with $|M|=20$. In this chapter the CUN/UUR Leu contrast is not treated as a protein statement. It is a finite organism-level residual: codon-usage summaries and tRNA-Leu copy spectra are compared after the synonymous-class control required by the amino-acid-composition restriction.

\paragraph{Observed row values.}
The run identified the CUN-over-UUR log ratios
$$
\begin{aligned}
(1.3514980212896148,\;-0.24869078488829677,\;1.0086640520051702),
\end{aligned}
$$
with corresponding signs
$$
\begin{aligned}
(+1,\;-1,\;+1).
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the sign is not uniform across the three recorded organisms. The recorded Boolean for uniformity is $\mathrm{false}$, and the experiment status is $\mathrm{passed}$. The organism table therefore realizes the intended finite contrast: the Leu CUN/UUR direction can reverse when the organism label and tRNA-Leu copy context are retained. Since the statistic is row-indexed, the yeast row is not a counterexample to the human or bacterial rows; rather, it is the row that prevents replacing the finite table by a single universal sign.

\paragraph{Controlled relation.}
The paired statistic reports Pearson correlation $1.0$ for the recorded finite comparison and a positive partial correlation with the tRNA-Leu copy ratio after the stated amino-acid-composition control. The controlled partial result is
$$
\begin{aligned}
 r_{\mathrm{partial}}=1.0,\qquad n=3.
\end{aligned}
$$
No $p$-value, confidence interval, or larger sampling distribution is recorded in this finite row, so the result is used here as an exact finite-table contact rather than as an asymptotic population inference. The support condition is consequently narrow: across the three loaded organisms, the sign and magnitude of $\log(\#\mathrm{CUN}/\#\mathrm{UUR})$ are compatible with the organism-level tRNA-Leu copy ratio under the recorded control. The chapter does not infer a law for unmeasured organisms from these three rows.

\paragraph{Reality contact.}
Two external contact types are attached to the row. The first is a per-organism codon-usage summary, which reports codon counts or frequencies from coding-sequence resources. The second is an organism-level tRNA-Leu gene-copy annotation, which reports copy-number spectra by anticodon family. These contacts are sufficient for the bounded question asked here: whether an organism-indexed CUN/UUR residual changes sign and whether the finite controlled statistic is positive against the tRNA-Leu copy-ratio coordinate. They are not measurements of ribosome traffic, translational kinetics, protein abundance, folding state, cellular phenotype, or biochemical activity.

\paragraph{Local scope of the claim.}
The admissible conclusion is only that the finite organism table records a non-uniform CUN/UUR sign and a positive recorded partial correlation with tRNA-Leu copy ratio after the stated amino-acid-composition control. No claim about translation as an active cellular process follows from the codon-usage sign alone. No folding, physical admissibility, biological function, ribosome-mediated mechanism, or universal cross-organism rule follows from this row unless a separate contact supplies the relevant evidence. In particular, raw $\log(\#\mathrm{CUN}/\#\mathrm{UUR})$ without the organism label, the synonymous-class control, and the paired tRNA-Leu copy coordinate is not enough to promote the observation beyond this bounded sequence-and-annotation statistic.

\paragraph{Finite verdict.}
Within the recorded data, the gate is open at the narrow translation-realization-statistic level: the run loaded three organisms, observed signs $(+1,-1,+1)$, recorded $\mathrm{sign\_uniform}=\mathrm{false}$, and reported $r_{\mathrm{partial}}=1.0$ for the tRNA-Leu ratio comparison with $n=3$. The same evidence keeps the stronger readings closed. The row is a controlled cross-organism Leu codon-usage contact, not a protein-structure result, not a functional assay, not a ribosome-dynamics experiment, and not a universal biological principle.
