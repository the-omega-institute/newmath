\subsection{Cross-organism CUN/UUR leucine usage and tRNA-Leu copy contact}
\label{sec:biorreality-cross-organism-cun-uur-leu-gate}

\paragraph{Finite packet.}
This record isolates a leucine-codon statistic at organism resolution.  The measured carrier is the residual pair formed by the CUN-versus-UUR leucine codon-usage log ratio, an organism label, the organism-level tRNA-Leu gene-copy ratio, and an amino-acid-composition control.  The finite coding support used by the packet also records the codon set
$$
\begin{aligned}
M=\{&\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{ACA},\mathrm{ACG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},
\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\\
&\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\},
\end{aligned}
$$
with recorded size $|M|=20$.  Within that finite support, the CUN family is the leucine block $\{\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU}\}$ and the UUR family is $\{\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\}$.  The biological readout is not a codon-label assertion alone; it is the organism-indexed statistic comparing $\log(\#\mathrm{CUN}/\#\mathrm{UUR})$ with the matching tRNA-Leu copy-number spectrum after controlling for amino-acid composition.

\paragraph{Observed rows.}
The finite run loaded three organism rows: $\mathrm{homo\_sapiens}$, $\mathrm{saccharomyces\_cerevisiae}$, and $\mathrm{escherichia\_coli\_k12}$.  Their recorded CUN/UUR log-ratio values are
$$
\begin{aligned}
(1.3514980212896148,\;-0.24869078488829677,\;1.0086640520051702),
\end{aligned}
$$
with corresponding signs
$$
\begin{aligned}
(+1,\;-1,\;+1).
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the sign is not uniform across the measured organisms.  The run records $\mathrm{sign\_uniform}=\mathrm{false}$, so the observed finite table rejects the simple constant-sign reading for these three rows.  It also records a Pearson value of $1.0$ for the paired comparison used in the run.

\paragraph{Controlled tRNA-Leu comparison.}
The organism-matched statistic is reported as a positive partial correlation with the tRNA-Leu copy ratio after the amino-acid-composition control.  The stored value is
$$
\begin{aligned}
n=3,\qquad r_{\mathrm{partial}}=1.0.
\end{aligned}
$$
This is a finite-row contact, not an asymptotic population result.  Its content is that, for the three loaded organisms, the sign and magnitude of $\log(\#\mathrm{CUN}/\#\mathrm{UUR})$ align with the recorded tRNA-Leu copy-ratio coordinate once the codon-usage statistic is read through the stated amino-acid-composition control.  The result is therefore admissible as a bounded translation-layer statistic about codon usage and tRNA gene-copy annotation, because both sides of the comparison are organism-level external summaries.

\paragraph{Scope of the contact.}
The two contacts used here are per-organism codon-usage summaries and organism-level tRNA-Leu gene-copy annotations.  Codon-usage tables summarize coding-sequence composition and sampling choices; tRNA gene-copy counts summarize genome annotation.  These contacts can test a cross-organism codon-usage distribution and a tRNA-Leu copy-number relation.  They do not observe ribosome motion, translation kinetics, protein folding, physical admissibility, biochemical function, or a general biological law.  Consequently, the result may be stated only as a controlled organism-level CUN/UUR--tRNA-Leu statistic.  It does not establish realized ribosome-mediated translation, folded structure, physical feasibility of a protein product, biological function, mechanism, or universality unless a separate contact explicitly measures that layer.

\paragraph{Failure conditions.}
The packet would fail in either of two finite ways.  First, if every measured organism had the same CUN/UUR log-ratio sign independently of its tRNA-Leu copy spectrum, the non-uniform sign condition would be absent.  Second, if sign reversal occurred but the controlled comparison with the tRNA-Leu copy ratio lost its positive association, then the observed reversal would remain only an uncontrolled codon-usage fact.  The stored run avoids both failures for the three loaded rows: the signs are $+1,-1,+1$, $\mathrm{sign\_uniform}=\mathrm{false}$, and the reported partial correlation is $1.0$ with $n=3$.

\paragraph{Verdict.}
The finite data support the narrow claim that the CUN/UUR leucine split is externally visible as an organism-level codon-usage statistic paired with a tRNA-Leu copy-number coordinate.  The validated content is exactly the controlled cross-organism relation among the three loaded organisms, the listed log ratios, the listed signs, and the positive recorded partial correlation.  The chapter therefore keeps the claim at the translation-realization contact surface understood as codon-usage plus tRNA-copy annotation, while withholding every stronger conclusion about process, structure, function, physical admissibility, mechanism, or global directionality.
