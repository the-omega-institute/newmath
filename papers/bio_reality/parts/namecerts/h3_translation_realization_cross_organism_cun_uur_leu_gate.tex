\subsection{Cross-organism CUN/UUR Leu usage gate}
\label{sec:h3-translation-realization-cross-organism-cun-uur-leu-gate}

\paragraph{Packet and readout.}
This record concerns a bounded organism-level statistic on the Leu synonymous module.  The observed coordinate is the signed residual
$\log(\#\mathrm{CUN}/\#\mathrm{UUR})$, paired with organism labels and with an external tRNA-Leu gene-copy ratio.  The admissible reading is deliberately narrow: the packet compares per-organism CUN-versus-UUR usage against organism-level tRNA-Leu copy spectra after an amino-acid-composition control.  It does not identify a ribosome trajectory, a protein fold, a biochemical function, a physical admissibility condition, or a universal biological rule.  Those stronger biological interpretations would require separate measurements at their own resolution.

\paragraph{Finite codon carrier.}
The finite codon-side support includes the verified $20$-element set
$$
\begin{aligned}
M=\{&\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{ACA},\mathrm{ACG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\\
&\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The recorded cardinality is $|M|=20$.  Within this finite support, the Leu component is represented by the two synonymous Leu families $\mathrm{CUN}$ and $\mathrm{UUR}$.  The statistic therefore does not treat a codon label as a biological mechanism; it treats the CUN/UUR split as a coordinate whose sign and magnitude can be checked against organism-matched external summaries.

\paragraph{External rows and measured signs.}
The finite cross-organism run loaded three rows: $\mathrm{homo\_sapiens}$, $\mathrm{saccharomyces\_cerevisiae}$, and $\mathrm{escherichia\_coli\_k12}$.  The recorded log-ratios were
$$
\begin{aligned}
(1.3514980212896148,\;-0.24869078488829677,\;1.0086640520051702),
\end{aligned}
$$
with corresponding signs
$$
\begin{aligned}
(+1,\;-1,\;+1).
\end{aligned}
$$
Hence the sign is not uniform across the three organisms.  This is the first bounded contact: a single direction for CUN-over-UUR is not preserved across the sampled organisms.

\paragraph{Paired tRNA-Leu statistic.}
The paired check reports a positive correlation between the CUN/UUR log-ratio coordinate and the tRNA-Leu copy-ratio coordinate.  The finite run records Pearson correlation $1.0$ and a partial Pearson correlation $1.0$ after the amino-acid-composition control, with $n=3$.  In symbols, the row-level result is summarized as
$$
\begin{aligned}
\rho_{\mathrm{Pearson}} &= 1.0,\\
\rho_{\mathrm{partial}\mid\mathrm{AA\ control}} &= 1.0,\\
n &= 3.
\end{aligned}
$$
The experiment status is recorded as passed.  The content of the pass is not that translation has been mechanically observed; it is that, for the finite organism table supplied here, the controlled CUN/UUR coordinate has a positive paired association with the tRNA-Leu copy-ratio coordinate and the sign is not constant over organisms.

\paragraph{Local interpretation.}
This packet supports a restricted translation-realization contact only in the statistical sense just described: codon-usage summaries and tRNA gene-copy summaries meet at an organism-indexed Leu coordinate.  The result is compatible with context-dependent reversal, since yeast carries the negative sign while the human and bacterial rows carry positive signs.  The record therefore rejects a uniform-sign rule on this finite sample and records a positive controlled association on the same sample.  Because the row count is $3$, the result should be read as a sharply bounded finite-row contact rather than as a population-level law.

\paragraph{Boundary.}
No claim of actual translation dynamics, protein folding, physical admissibility, biological function, ribosome-mediated mechanism, or organism-independent universality is licensed by this packet alone.  Codon-usage tables summarize sequence composition, and tRNA copy numbers summarize genome annotations; neither contact observes translation kinetics, protein output, folded structure, or phenotype.  A stronger biological conclusion would need an additional matched contact, such as ribosome profiling, proteomic abundance, direct tRNA charging or availability measurements, folding assays, or functional readouts at the relevant organismal resolution.
