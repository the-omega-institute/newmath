\paragraph{Object of the record.}
This record concerns the leucine codon split between the $\mathrm{CUN}$ block and the $\mathrm{UUR}$ block, read at organism scale rather than at a single-sequence or protein-output scale.  The finite carrier is the organism-indexed residual consisting of the coordinate $\log(\#\mathrm{CUN}/\#\mathrm{UUR})$, the organism label, a tRNA-Leu gene-copy ratio, and an amino-acid-composition control.  The attached biological object is therefore not a protein, a fold, a translation machine, or a function assay.  It is a paired organism-level statistic: codon-usage summaries for coding sequences are compared with organism-level tRNA-Leu copy spectra, and the only admissible question is whether the sign and magnitude of the leucine block residual track the matched tRNA-Leu copy ratio after controlling for amino-acid composition.

No p-value, confidence interval, additional held-out organism count, or replication table is supplied in the finite row, so the chapter treats the numerical result as a bounded organism-table contact rather than as a population-level law.

\paragraph{Admissible conclusion.}
The record is ready only at the level of the stated organism-scale statistic.  It supports the following limited claim: for the three loaded organisms, the leucine $\mathrm{CUN}/\mathrm{UUR}$ residual has non-uniform sign, and the recorded paired comparison with tRNA-Leu gene-copy ratio is positive after the amino-acid-composition control encoded in the finite run.  The contact is a cross-organism codon-usage and tRNA-copy relation; it is not a direct measurement of ribosome motion, codon dwell time, peptide synthesis rate, protein structure, or organismal phenotype.

\paragraph{Local boundary.}
No ribosome-mediated translation mechanism, folded structure, physical admissibility, biological function, or universal cross-organism rule follows from these rows alone.  A further claim of that kind would require a separate contact with the appropriate resolution: ribosome profiling or matched translation-output data for mechanism, structural assays for folding, physical admissibility evidence for material constraints, and functional experiments for biological role.  The sign reversal is therefore reported as context-dependent in the finite table, not as universal directionality.  Raw $\log(\#\mathrm{CUN}/\#\mathrm{UUR})$ without the organism-matched tRNA-Leu and amino-acid-composition control is also insufficient for the translation-realization layer used here.
