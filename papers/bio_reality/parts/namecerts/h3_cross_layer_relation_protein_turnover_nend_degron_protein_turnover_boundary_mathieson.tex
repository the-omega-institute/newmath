\paragraph{Local conclusion.}
The finite conclusion is negative and useful: within the $3898$-row Mathieson join and the $12000$ recorded permutation draws, the N-end/degron turnover packet does not supply a stable sign-consistent readout that can explain the human non-translation residual.  The row remains a bounded descriptive audit over one human half-life contact.  No translation realization, folded-state assertion, physical admissibility statement, protein-function claim, degradation mechanism, causal pathway, organism-wide universality, or cross-species law follows from these numbers.  Any such stronger statement would require a separate contact with matched perturbational or mechanistic evidence rather than a finite sign summary over steady-state half-life rows.
