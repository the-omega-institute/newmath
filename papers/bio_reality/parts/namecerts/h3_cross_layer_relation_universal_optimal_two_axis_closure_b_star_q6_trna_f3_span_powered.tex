\subsection{The $B^{*}_{Q6}$ $f_{3}$-stress readout boundary}
\label{sec:bq6-f3-stress-translation-readout-boundary}

\paragraph{Finite carrier.}
This record concerns a finite coding-sequence surface in which synonymous codon coordinates are first quotiented by amino-acid identity and then restricted to the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual direction carrying third-position $f_{3}$ stress. The biological rows are organism-indexed CDS observations with codon counts, codon-family composition, organism labels, GC3, CDS length, $M$-density, and a matched translation-layer readout when such a readout is actually present. The intended tested quantity is not a protein, function, phenotype, structure, or organismal law. It is the controlled survival entry $S_{f3\text{-}stress,T_j}$, where $T_j$ is a bounded translation readout such as a tAI/stAI weight surface or a measured ribosome-occupancy or translation-efficiency row.

\paragraph{Closed finite codon rows.}
The $f_{3}$ saturation row is explicit. The finite set $M = \mathrm{Med}(R) \cup \{\mathrm{ACA}\}$ is
$$
\begin{aligned}
M=\{&\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{ACA},\mathrm{ACG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\\
&\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The saturated $f_{3}$ image of $R$ removes the two ACA-side entries and is
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{Sat}_{f_3}(R)=\{&\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\\
&\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The recorded $f_{3}$ boundary is empty, so the local codon-level stress is not a free boundary population waiting to be promoted. It is a residual contrast whose biological meaning must be earned by a separate translation row and by controls.

\paragraph{Powered finite observations.}
The powered two-axis closure run used $5$ folds, required at least $500$ proteins per organism, computed $11$ organisms out of $16$ requested organisms, and used $41$ synonymous-design columns. Its conclusion is \emph{third-axis-present}: after the tRNA and $f_{3}$ span is fitted, $d_{\perp}$ has systematically positive held-out $R^{2}$ and the $d_{\perp}$ directions are mutually same-signed across organisms. The adjacent residual-axis run on the same $11$ of $16$ organism panel, again with $5$ folds, at least $500$ proteins per organism, and $41$ synonymous-design columns, identifies the non-tRNA residual optimality direction as $f_{3}$ translation selection. These two rows justify treating $f_{3}$ stress as a candidate translation-readout coordinate, but only as a candidate.

\paragraph{Spectrum and axis checks.}
The axis-strength spectrum was computed on $11$ organisms with $5$ folds and $41$ synonymous-design columns. The captured spectra are led on average by $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$, followed by $d_{\mathrm{tRNA}}$; all $11$ per-organism dominant counts are assigned to $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$. The domain summary is descriptive rather than balanced, with organism counts $n_{\mathrm{archaea}}=1$, $n_{\mathrm{bacteria}}=2$, and $n_{\mathrm{eukaryote}}=8$. The largest GC3 rank effect in that row is the usage direction, with $\rho=0.136$. Independently, the third-axis identity row reports alignment of $d_{\perp}$ to usage frequency within family, with mean cosine $0.128$ and one-sided sign-test $p=0.000488$. The mechanism row keeps the same third axis GC-orthogonalized: it reports selection-not-GC, not-wobble-clustered, and a CSC/positional identity still requiring additional external contact.

\paragraph{Translation asymmetry condition.}
The local biological test is the following finite implication. A pair of codons $c$ and $c^{f_{3}}$ can contribute to a positive $f_{3}$-stress translation readout only where the relevant organism or context supplies a translation asymmetry,
$$
\begin{aligned}
w_c \neq w_{c^{f_3}}.
\end{aligned}
$$
If the tRNA or wobble-weight surface is symmetric across the third-position $f_{3}$ pair, the residual coordinate remains a codon-topology distinction rather than a translation distinction. The boundary-set tRNA-poverty run supports a nearby supply-side constraint: in $40$ computed organisms out of $46$ requested organisms, with $6$ skipped organisms and $1000$ permutations at $\alpha=0.05$, the $R$ sense codons are systematically tRNA-poor under GtRNAdb tGCN plus dos Reis wobble weights. This supports a tRNA-supply route for $R$ avoidance, but it does not by itself compute $S_{f3\text{-}stress,T_j}$.

\paragraph{Survival statistic.}
For each organism and each matched translation row, the readout claim is the controlled survival inequality
$$
\begin{aligned}
S_{f3\text{-}stress,T_j} > 0
\end{aligned}
$$
after amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, CDS length, $M$-density baseline, matched mRNA abundance when translation efficiency is used, and simpler codon baselines are included. The null condition is the finite boundary
$$
\begin{aligned}
S_{f3\text{-}stress,T_j} \leq 0
\end{aligned}
$$
for every admissible translation row, or a rank-degenerate surface in which the fitted matrix cannot support a held-out distinction. In particular, a three-organism all-$1.0$ controlled matrix is a saturation boundary, not a positive survival result.

\paragraph{Present conclusion.}
The present row is ready as a finite BioReality chapter because the codon carrier, saturation rows, organism counts, fold design, synonymous-design dimension, and axis conclusions are all recorded. The strongest current positive statement is narrower than a translation claim: the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ two-axis span is insufficient, a third axis is present, and the residual non-tRNA optimality direction is most consistent with $f_{3}$ translation selection on the powered panel. A positive translation-readout assertion still requires a joined finite panel containing same-organism and same-CDS codon counts, wobble-breaking tAI/stAI or measured translation rows, matched mRNA abundance when needed, and the controlled survival statistic above.

\paragraph{Local no-promotion boundary.}
Nothing in the residual coordinate, the $f_{3}$ saturation table, the tRNA-copy or wobble-weight route, the $d_{\perp}$ identity row, or the powered two-axis closure row entails observed translation, ribosome occupancy, protein abundance, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, mechanism, phenotype, or universality. Those claims require their own layer-matched observations and their own statistics. This chapter therefore records a bounded candidate interface: $B^{*}_{Q6}$ $f_{3}$ stress may be tested against translation readouts where third-position wobble or tRNA weights break symmetry, and it is falsified at this interface when every controlled $S_{f3\text{-}stress,T_j}$ row is non-positive or rank-degenerate.
