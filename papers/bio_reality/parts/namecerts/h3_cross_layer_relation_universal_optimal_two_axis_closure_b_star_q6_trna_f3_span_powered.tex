\subsection{Wobble-broken $f_3$ stress in the powered $B^*_{Q6}$ translation panel}\label{subsec:bioreality-q6-f3-wobble-broken-translation-readout}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite coordinate surface.}
This record isolates the $f_3$-stress coordinate inside the powered $B^*_{Q6}$ synonymous residual surface after amino-acid projection.  The finite input is not a general claim about translation, protein output, or phenotype.  It is a controlled question about whether a third-position synonymous imbalance can remain visible in a translation-layer row after the ordinary composition axes have been removed.  The saturated $f_3$ calculation fixes the relevant codon support explicitly.  The ambient set $M={\rm Med}_R\cup\{\mathrm{ACA}\}$ contains
$\mathrm{AAA}, \mathrm{AAG}, \mathrm{ACA}, \mathrm{ACG}, \mathrm{AGA}, \mathrm{AGG}, \mathrm{AUA}, \mathrm{AUG}, \mathrm{CUA}, \mathrm{CUC}, \mathrm{CUG}, \mathrm{CUU}, \mathrm{UAA}, \mathrm{UAG}, \mathrm{UCA}, \mathrm{UCG}, \mathrm{UGA}, \mathrm{UGG}, \mathrm{UUA}, \mathrm{UUG}$.
The $f_3$ saturation removes exactly $\mathrm{ACA}$ and $\mathrm{ACG}$, leaving
$\mathrm{AAA}, \mathrm{AAG}, \mathrm{AGA}, \mathrm{AGG}, \mathrm{AUA}, \mathrm{AUG}, \mathrm{CUA}, \mathrm{CUC}, \mathrm{CUG}, \mathrm{CUU}, \mathrm{UAA}, \mathrm{UAG}, \mathrm{UCA}, \mathrm{UCG}, \mathrm{UGA}, \mathrm{UGG}, \mathrm{UUA}, \mathrm{UUG}$,
with empty $f_3$ boundary.  Thus the finite row treats $f_3$ stress as a residual coordinate on a closed codon surface, not as a direct biological observation.

\paragraph{Two-axis closure test.}
The powered two-axis closure run used five folds, at least $500$ proteins per organism, $41$ synonymous design columns, and computed $11$ organisms out of $16$ requested organisms.  Its recorded conclusion is \emph{third-axis-present}: the span generated by the universal tRNA-adaptation direction and the $f_3$ translation-selection residual does not exhaust the held-out signal.  The stated reason is that $d_\perp$ has systematically positive held-out $R^2$ and that the $d_\perp$ directions are mutually same-signed across organisms.  In symbolic form, the tested closure boundary is the failure of
$$
\begin{aligned}
  d_{\rm opt} &\in \operatorname{span}\{d_{\rm tRNA},d_{f_3}\}
\end{aligned}
$$
under the powered held-out design.  The row therefore supports a negative closure statement for the two-axis span, not a positive translation-survival statement for $f_3$ by itself.

\paragraph{Relation to the $f_3$ residual axis.}
The companion residual-axis run computed the same $11/16$ organism panel with five folds, $500$ minimum proteins per organism, and $41$ synonymous design columns.  It identifies the dominant non-tRNA residual optimality direction as \texttt{f3\_translation\_selection}.  The core tRNA-adaptation run on the same finite design passed as the universal core direction, while the two-axis closure run still found a remaining $d_\perp$ component.  The finite interpretation is consequently three-part: a tRNA-weighted direction is present, an $f_3$-sensitive residual direction is present, and their span is not yet the full powered optimum.

\paragraph{Independent shape of the third axis.}
The powered third-axis identity row again used five folds, $500$ minimum proteins per organism, $41$ synonymous design columns, and $11$ computed organisms out of $16$.  It provisionally identifies $d_\perp$ with usage frequency within family, with mean cosine $0.128$ and one-sided sign-test $p=0.000488$.  The mechanism row keeps this third axis GC-orthogonal: its conclusion is selection-not-GC, not-wobble-clustered, with CSC or positional identity left as needing external support.  The axis-strength spectrum sharpens the finite picture: among the $11$ computed organisms, $d_{\rm resid4}$ is dominant in all $11$ per-organism counts, followed on average by $d_{\rm tRNA}$; the descriptive domain counts are imbalanced at archaea $1$, bacteria $2$, and eukaryote $8$, and the largest GC3 rank effect is only $d_{\rm usage}$ with $\rho=0.136$.  These rows prevent the chapter from collapsing the remaining axis into either GC3 composition or a completed wobble mechanism.

\paragraph{Wobble-breaking requirement.}
The $f_3$ coordinate becomes biologically testable only where the codon-pairing context has a non-symmetric translation weight.  The local condition is
$$
\begin{aligned}
  w_c &\neq w_{c^{f_3}},
\end{aligned}
$$
where $c^{f_3}$ denotes the third-position $f_3$ mate of $c$.  Without that asymmetry, an $f_3$-paired synonymous imbalance is a coordinate distinction but not a translation-readout distinction.  The tRNA-poverty mechanism row supplies a bounded mechanism-facing contact: the $R$ sense codons are systematically tRNA-poor under GtRNAdb tRNA gene copy number plus dos Reis wobble weights, with $40$ organisms computed out of $46$, $6$ skipped organisms, $1000$ permutations, and $\alpha=0.05$.  This supports a tRNA-supply pressure for $R$ avoidance, but it still does not by itself measure ribosome occupancy, translation efficiency, or protein output.

\paragraph{Readout statistic and null boundary.}
The admissible readout for this chapter is the controlled survival entry $S_{f_3\text{-stress},T_j}$, where $T_j$ is a bounded tAI, stAI, ribosome-occupancy, or translation-efficiency row.  The statistic must be estimated after amino-acid composition, organism identity, GC3, CDS length, $M$-density, matched mRNA abundance, rank sufficiency, and simpler codon-baseline controls.  A positive row has the form
$$
\begin{aligned}
  S_{f_3\text{-stress},T_j} &> 0,
\end{aligned}
$$
under the declared controls.  A non-positive row, or a rank-degenerate all-$1.0$ surface on too few organisms, is not survival.  It is a boundary case saying that the present panel has not separated the $f_3$ coordinate from the controlled null.

\paragraph{Local cannot-claim boundary.}
The finite rows justify a powered residual-geometry statement: the two-axis span made from universal tRNA adaptation and $f_3$ translation selection leaves a reproducible third direction in the computed panel.  They do not justify a claim that $f_3$ stress causes translation, changes protein abundance, changes folding, establishes physical admissibility, realizes molecular function, determines localization or stability, produces a phenotype, or states a universal biological law.  Any such statement requires a separate layer-matched contact and its own controlled statistic.  In particular, tAI or stAI weights are bounded model readouts; ribosome profiling would be a direct translation contact only after same-organism and same-CDS joining with codon counts and matched mRNA rows; matched mRNA abundance is a control covariate rather than a translation measurement.  The present chapter therefore records an open translation-readout test with strong finite motivation, not a completed downstream biological mechanism.
