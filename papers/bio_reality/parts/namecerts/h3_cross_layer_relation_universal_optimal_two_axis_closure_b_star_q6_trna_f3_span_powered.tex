\subsection{The $B^*_{Q6}$ two-axis closure and the $f_3$ translation boundary}
\label{subsec:bioq6-f3-two-axis-translation-boundary}

\paragraph{Finite coordinate surface.}
This record concerns a finite coding-sequence surface indexed by organism and by synonymous residual coordinates after amino-acid projection.  The distinguished coordinate is the $f_3$-stress component of $B^*_{Q6}$: it compares codons paired by the third-position $f_3$ operation only after the amino-acid class has already been quotiented out.  The finite saturation computation fixes the local combinatorial support.  The mediated set $M=\operatorname{Med}(R)\cup\{\mathrm{ACA}\}$ is
$$
\begin{aligned}
M=\{&\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{ACA},\mathrm{ACG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\\
&\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The $f_3$ saturation removes exactly $\mathrm{ACA}$ and $\mathrm{ACG}$ from that list, giving
$$
\begin{aligned}
\operatorname{Sat}_{f_3}(R)=\{&\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\\
&\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\},
\end{aligned}
$$
with empty $f_3$ boundary.  Thus the chapter is not using an open-ended codon universe: the relevant finite row is the saturated synonymous residual surface together with organism-level codon usage, tRNA or wobble weights, and bounded translation readout rows.

\paragraph{Rank-powered two-axis result.}
The powered two-axis experiment for the claim \texttt{h3.cross\_layer\_relation.universal\_optimal\_two\_axis\_closure.b\_star\_q6\_trna\_f3\_span\_powered} passed on run \texttt{99dc7e2d5abf}.  It requested $16$ organisms and computed $11$, using $5$ folds, a minimum of $500$ proteins per organism, and $41$ synonymous design columns.  The recorded conclusion is \texttt{third\_axis\_present}: the span generated by the tRNA-adaptation direction and the $f_3$-translation-selection residual does not close the observed optimality surface.  In the finite readout, the remaining direction $d_{\perp}$ has systematically positive held-out $R^2$, and the $d_{\perp}$ signs are mutually oriented across organisms.  In compact form, the tested claim is
$$
\begin{aligned}
\operatorname{span}(d_{\mathrm{tRNA}},d_{f_3})\neq \operatorname{span}(d_{\mathrm{opt}})
\end{aligned}
$$
under the stated finite panel and cross-validation gates.  This is a rank statement about residual coordinates and held-out prediction.  It is not yet a same-CDS ribosome or translation-efficiency survival statement.

\paragraph{Relation to the surrounding axis evidence.}
The broader powered panel gives the two-axis claim a coherent finite context.  The core tRNA-adaptation claim passed on $11$ computed organisms out of $16$ requested, again with $5$ folds, at least $500$ proteins per organism, and $41$ synonymous design columns.  The non-tRNA residual optimality experiment also passed on the same scale and identifies its dominant residual axis as \texttt{f3\_translation\_selection}.  The per-organism axis-strength spectrum reports $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$ as the leading mean direction, followed by $d_{\mathrm{tRNA}}$; all $11$ computed organisms have $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$ as the per-organism dominant direction.  Domain means are explicitly descriptive because the sampled domains are imbalanced, with $n=\{\mathrm{archaea}:1,\mathrm{bacteria}:2,\mathrm{eukaryote}:8\}$, and the largest reported GC3 rank effect is only $\rho=0.136$ for $d_{\mathrm{usage}}$.

\paragraph{Third-axis identification.}
The same finite system resolves the residual direction only provisionally.  The third-axis identity run reports that $d_{\perp}$ aligns with usage frequency within family, with mean cosine $0.128$ and one-sided sign-test $p=0.000488$.  The mechanism run classifies the same direction as selection-not-GC and not wobble-clustered; it records that GC-orthogonalized $d_{\perp}$ keeps significant cross-species alignment and held-out $R^2$, while the CSC or positional identity remains dependent on additional empirical resolution.  The optimality-axis sufficiency run concludes \texttt{fourth\_mechanism\_present}, so the two-axis closure result is not being inflated into a complete mechanism.  It says that the tRNA plus $f_3$ span is insufficient and that a residual axis is visible in held-out rank geometry.

\paragraph{Translation readout gate.}
The biological interpretation is narrower than the coordinate result.  The $f_3$ coordinate can become a translation-layer distinction only where the organism or context has asymmetric tRNA or wobble weights, written schematically as $w_c\neq w_{c^{f_3}}$, and where the matched translation row survives the declared controls.  The admissible readback is a controlled entry $S_{f_3\text{-stress},T_j}$ for tAI, stAI, ribosome occupancy, or translation efficiency after amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, CDS length, $M$-density, mRNA, rank, and simpler-baseline controls.  The finite support for tRNA asymmetry is strengthened by the boundary-set tRNA-poverty run: it computed $40$ organisms out of $46$ requested, skipped $6$, used $1000$ permutations at $\alpha=0.05$, and concluded that $R$ sense codons are systematically tRNA-poor under GtRNAdb tGCN plus dos Reis wobble weights.  This is mechanism-facing support for the plausibility of a supply asymmetry, but it still does not replace a joined translation-readout statistic.

\paragraph{No promotion beyond the available rows.}
The present chapter therefore separates three levels.  First, the codon-coordinate computation is finite and saturated: the $f_3$ boundary is empty after the listed codon removal, and the coordinate is evaluated inside the declared synonymous design.  Second, the powered rank evidence is positive for a residual direction outside the two-axis tRNA plus $f_3$ span, with $11$ computed organisms, $5$ folds, and $41$ design columns.  Third, translation realization remains conditional on same-organism and same-CDS contact rows.  A positive tAI or stAI association is a bounded modeled readout, not an observed ribosome-occupancy measurement.  A ribosome-profiling or translation-efficiency row would still have to be joined to codon counts and matched mRNA abundance before $S_{f_3\text{-stress},T_j}>0$ could be reported.

\paragraph{Local falsification rule.}
The chapter is falsified at the translation layer if every controlled $S_{f_3\text{-stress},T_j}$ is non-positive, rank-degenerate, or explained away by the amino-acid, organism, GC3, length, $M$-density, mRNA, rank, or simpler-codon baselines.  The three-organism all-$1.0$ controlled matrix, where it appears, is a saturated-rank boundary and not a survival result.  Conversely, the finite two-axis closure result remains meaningful as a coordinate and rank claim even when the measured translation row is absent.  No claim about translation realization, folding, physical admissibility, biological function, molecular mechanism, phenotype, or universality follows from this chapter unless a separate layer-matched contact supplies the relevant rows and statistic.
