\subsection{Rank-Powered Two-Axis Closure and the $f_3$ Translation Boundary}
\label{sec:bioreality-q6-f3-trna-span-powered}

\paragraph{Finite coordinate under test.}
The record concerns the finite CDS-by-organism residual coordinate surface obtained after amino-acid projection, restricted to the $B^*_{Q6}$ $f_3$-stress direction and to translation-layer readouts that can be matched by organism or by CDS. The biological object is not a protein-function claim and not a structural claim. It is the narrower question whether the $f_3$-paired synonymous imbalance remains visible after standard sequence controls, and only where the translation context supplies an asymmetry between a codon $c$ and its $f_3$ partner $c^{f_3}$ through tRNA or wobble weights.

The finite saturation check fixes the relevant boundary explicitly. The set $M = \operatorname{Med}(R) \cup \{\mathrm{ACA}\}$ is
$$
\begin{aligned}
M = \{&\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{ACA},\mathrm{ACG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\\
&\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The $f_3$ saturation of $R$ removes the two exceptional entries $\mathrm{ACA}$ and $\mathrm{ACG}$:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\operatorname{Sat}_{f_3}(R) = \{&\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\\
&\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The recorded $f_3$ boundary is empty, $\partial_{f_3}R=\varnothing$, so the stress coordinate is not being introduced as an unaccounted boundary residue. It is a finite residual direction whose biological admissibility must be tested through matched translation rows rather than through codon topology alone.

\paragraph{Powered finite-row evidence.}
The powered two-axis closure experiment for this coordinate was run with $16$ requested organisms, $11$ computed organisms, $5$ folds, at least $500$ proteins per organism, and $41$ synonymous design columns. The run status is passed, with conclusion \emph{third\_axis\_present}. Its finite reason is that $d_{\perp}$ has systematically positive held-out $R^2$ and the $d_{\perp}$ directions are mutually same-signed across organisms. This does not say that $f_3$ stress has already survived a ribosome or TE measurement. It says that the two-axis span generated by the tRNA-adaptation direction together with the $f_3$ residual direction is insufficient as a closed description of the powered panel: an additional residual direction is present in the held-out geometry.

The associated residual-axis run, using the same requested and computed organism counts, the same $5$-fold design, the same $500$-protein minimum, and the same $41$ synonymous columns, identifies the non-tRNA residual optimality direction as \emph{f3\_translation\_selection}. This supports the interpretation that the $f_3$ coordinate is a plausible translation-layer candidate, but it still remains a candidate until the readout row is supplied. The third-axis identity run gives a more specific finite reading: $d_{\perp}$ is provisionally aligned to usage frequency within family, with mean cosine $0.128$ and one-sided sign-test $p=0.000488$. Hence the powered panel separates at least three finite directions: a tRNA-related adaptation axis, an $f_3$-translation-selection residual axis, and a usage-frequency-within-family direction.

\paragraph{Wobble asymmetry and the admissible support condition.}
The conjectural support condition is local. For a codon pair $(c,c^{f_3})$, the $f_3$ coordinate can become a dynamical translation-readout coordinate only when the translation context breaks the symmetry
$$
\begin{aligned}
w_c \neq w_{c^{f_3}}.
\end{aligned}
$$
Here $w_c$ may be a bounded tAI, stAI, or wobble-weight entry, but a modeled weight is not the same kind of row as ribosome occupancy or translation efficiency. The boundary-set tRNA-poverty run gives relevant finite background: among $46$ requested organisms, $40$ were computed and $6$ skipped; with $1000$ permutations at $\alpha=0.05$, the mechanism call states that $R$ sense codons are systematically tRNA-poor under GtRNAdb tGCN with dos Reis wobble weights. This is a tRNA-supply constraint on the codon side. It does not by itself compute a controlled $S_{f3\text{-}stress,T_j}$ entry.

The rank-powered translation test therefore has the following finite form. For each organism or same-CDS row where codon counts, wobble or adaptation weights, translation readout, and matched mRNA control are jointly available, estimate
$$
\begin{aligned}
S_{f3\text{-}stress,T_j} = \Delta \mathcal{L}\bigl(T_j \mid Z\bigr) - \Delta \mathcal{L}\bigl(T_j \mid Z, q_{f3}\bigr),
\end{aligned}
$$
where $q_{f3}$ is the $B^*_{Q6}$ $f_3$-stress residual coordinate and $Z$ contains amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, CDS length, M-density, matched mRNA controls when TE is used, rank controls, and simpler codon baselines. A positive finite entry requires $S_{f3\text{-}stress,T_j}>0$ after these controls and after the baseline ablation. A rank-degenerate all-$1.0$ surface on three organisms is not such an entry; it is a null boundary caused by insufficient finite rank.

\paragraph{Readout scope.}
The axis-strength spectrum gives the current descriptive placement of the coordinate family. Across the computed panel, captured spectra are led by $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$ on average, followed by $d_{\mathrm{tRNA}}$; the per-organism dominant count is $\{d_{\mathrm{resid4}}:11\}$. The same report warns that domain means are descriptive only under the imbalanced domain counts $\{\mathrm{archaea}:1,\mathrm{bacteria}:2,\mathrm{eukaryote}:8\}$, and the largest GC3 rank effect is only $d_{\mathrm{usage}}$ with $\rho=0.136$. These numbers keep the chapter finite: the powered residual geometry is nontrivial, but the biological readout must still be earned row by row.

The bounded positive claim is therefore only this: in a rank-powered panel, the $B^*_{Q6}$ $f_3$-stress coordinate is a finite candidate for translation-readout survival in contexts where tRNA or wobble weights break $f_3$ symmetry. The coordinate is falsified as a translation-readout survival claim if every controlled $S_{f3\text{-}stress,T_j}$ remains non-positive, or if the apparent matrix is rank-degenerate under the declared controls.

\paragraph{Local non-promotion rule.}
No conclusion about translation realization, ribosome occupancy, mature protein abundance, folding, localization, physical admissibility, molecular function, phenotype, mechanism, adaptation, causation, or universality follows from the powered coordinate geometry alone. Codon usage, tRNA-copy counts, and modeled tAI or stAI weights may bound a translation-layer hypothesis, but they do not replace same-organism or same-CDS measured readout rows. Ribosome profiling or TE can test the translation row only after it is joined to codon counts and matched mRNA controls. Proteomics, localization, RNA half-life, structure, perturbation, or functional assays would each need their own layer-matched contact and their own controlled statistic before any downstream interpretation is allowed.
