\subsection{A wobble-broken third-position residual as a translation-readout test}
\label{subsec:b-star-q6-f3-stress-translation-readout}

\paragraph{Coordinate being tested.}
The object of this record is the $B^*_{Q6}$ third-position stress coordinate after amino-acid quotienting.  The coordinate is not a raw codon-count contrast.  It is a residual surface on coding sequences, computed after projecting away amino-acid composition and then asking whether an $f_3$-paired synonymous imbalance remains visible when the organism, GC3 content, CDS length, $M$-density, and simpler codon baselines have already been accounted for.  The relevant finite $f_3$ saturation row has
$$
\begin{aligned}
M &= \{\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{ACA},\mathrm{ACG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\},\\
\operatorname{Sat}_{f_3}(R) &= \{\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the finite $M$ row has $20$ codons, the saturated $f_3$ row has $18$ codons, and the recorded $f_3$ boundary is empty.  The missing pair relative to $M$ is $\{\mathrm{ACA},\mathrm{ACG}\}$, so the stress coordinate is a narrow third-position residual rather than a whole-family codon-usage axis.

\paragraph{Finite panel evidence.}
The powered two-axis closure run was evaluated with $5$ folds, a minimum of $500$ proteins per organism, $41$ synonymous design columns, and $11$ computed organisms out of $16$ requested organisms.  Its recorded conclusion is $\mathrm{third\_axis\_present}$: after the tRNA-adaptation direction and the $f_3$ translation-selection direction are used, the residual direction $d_\perp$ still has systematically positive held-out $R^2$, and the $d_\perp$ directions are mutually same-signed across organisms.  This is not a claim that the $f_3$ coordinate alone explains translation output.  It says that the two-axis span is not closed on the available finite panel.

The adjacent powered rows sharpen the interpretation.  The non-tRNA residual optimality row identifies the dominant remaining axis as $\mathrm{f3\_translation\_selection}$ on the same $11$-organism, $5$-fold, $41$-column design.  The universal-core row records a passed tRNA-adaptation run on the same scale.  The third-axis identity row gives a candidate alignment to usage frequency within family, with mean cosine $0.128$ and one-sided sign-test $p=0.000488$.  The third-axis mechanism row records that the GC-orthogonalized $d_\perp$ keeps significant cross-species alignment and held-out $R^2$, while its classification remains selection-not-GC, not-wobble-clustered, and still needing an external CSC or positional identity.  The per-organism strength spectrum reports $11$ computed organisms, with $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$ dominant in all $11$ per-organism counts, followed on average by $d_{\mathrm{tRNA}}$; the largest GC3 rank effect listed there is $d_{\mathrm{usage}}$ with $\rho=0.136$.  These rows jointly place the present coordinate in a residual translation-readout neighborhood, but they do not by themselves create a measured ribosome row.

\paragraph{Why wobble breaking is required.}
The $f_3$ coordinate becomes biologically testable only where the readout layer distinguishes an $f_3$ pair.  In a context with symmetric third-position weights, an $f_3$-paired synonymous imbalance can remain a coordinate of the coding sequence while making no translation-readout distinction.  The finite test therefore asks for a context weight $w_c$ and its $f_3$ mate $w_{c^{f_3}}$ with
$$
\begin{aligned}
w_c \neq w_{c^{f_3}},
\end{aligned}
$$
and then measures whether the residual coordinate survives against a matched translation readout.  The appropriate survival entry is a controlled score $S_{f3\text{-}stress,T_j}$, where $T_j$ may be a bounded tAI/stAI surface, a ribosome-occupancy row, or a translation-efficiency row.  A positive entry must be computed after amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, length, $M$-density, and matched mRNA controls.  Rank-degenerate matrices, including an all-$1.0$ surface on only three organisms, are treated as null boundaries rather than as survival.

\paragraph{What the row supports.}
The chapter-level claim supported here is the following finite statement.  On the powered codon-design panel, the tRNA direction and the $f_3$ translation-selection direction do not exhaust the residual structure: a third residual direction is present, same-signed across the computed organisms, and positive in held-out prediction.  This makes the wobble-broken $f_3$ stress coordinate a legitimate translation-readout target.  It does not yet assert that the target has survived in a same-CDS measured ribosome or translation-efficiency matrix.

A separate tRNA-poverty mechanism row is relevant but narrower: with $40$ computed organisms out of $46$ requested organisms, $1000$ permutations, and $\alpha=0.05$, the $R$ sense codons are recorded as systematically tRNA-poor under GtRNAdb tGCN plus dos Reis wobble weights.  That row supports a tRNA-supply interpretation for $R$ avoidance.  It does not replace the present same-row survival test, because a supply asymmetry and a controlled translation-efficiency survival entry are different empirical objects.

\paragraph{Local exclusion boundary.}
This record stops at the bounded translation-readout interface.  The finite codon geometry, the modeled tAI or stAI weights, the tRNA-copy spectrum, and the positive held-out residual direction do not imply protein abundance, folded structure, physical admissibility, localization, stability, molecular function, organismal phenotype, mechanism, or a universal biological law.  Each of those claims would need its own layer-matched measurement and its own controlled statistic.  In particular, a downstream protein, structure, function, localization, stability, or phenotype interpretation is not admissible unless a non-vacuous and positive $S_{f3\text{-}stress,T_j}$ entry has first been obtained for a matched translation row under the stated controls.

\paragraph{Falsification rule.}
The conjectural readout is falsified at this layer if every controlled entry remains non-positive or rank-degenerate on a powered panel:
$$
\begin{aligned}
S_{f3\text{-}stress,T_j} \leq 0
\end{aligned}
$$
for all admissible translation readouts $T_j$ after the declared controls and simpler-baseline ablations.  In that case the $f_3$ stress coordinate may still be a valid residual coordinate in coding-sequence space, and the broader $B^*_{Q6}$ panel may still contain tRNA, usage, or higher residual structure, but this particular wobble-broken translation-readout claim would not survive.
