\subsection{The $f_3$-stress translation-readout boundary for $B^*_{Q6}$}
\label{sec:bioreality-q6-f3-stress-translation-readout-boundary}

\paragraph{Finite coordinate under review.}
The object of this record is the finite CDS-by-organism residual coordinate surface obtained after amino-acid projection, restricted to the $B^*_{Q6}$ coordinate called $f_3$-stress and paired only with translation-layer rows. The coordinate is not interpreted as a protein, a structure, a phenotype, or a mechanism. It is a controlled synonymous residual direction whose possible biological contact is bounded by translation readouts such as tAI, stAI, ribosome occupancy, or translation efficiency. The finite saturation computation fixes the relevant $f_3$ context before any readout is considered. The ambient set $M=\operatorname{Med}(R)\cup\{\mathrm{ACA}\}$ is
$$
\begin{aligned}
M=\{&\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{ACA},\mathrm{ACG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\\
&\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The $f_3$-saturation of $R$ removes exactly the two ACA/ACG entries and has empty boundary:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\operatorname{Sat}_{f_3}(R)=\{&\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\\
&\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\},\qquad \partial_{f_3}(R)=\varnothing.
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the chapter is not looking for a missing codon-topology boundary. It is asking whether, after the finite codon geometry has been quotiented by amino acid and residualized against standard covariates, a translation row still distinguishes the $f_3$-paired synonymous imbalance.

\paragraph{Powered two-axis test.}
The powered two-axis experiment for this claim was run as \texttt{b\_star\_q6\_universal\_optimal\_two\_axis\_closure\_powered} with 5 folds, at least 500 proteins per organism, 11 computed organisms out of 16 requested organisms, and 41 synonymous design columns. Its conclusion is \texttt{third\_axis\_present}: the span generated by the tRNA direction and the $f_3$ translation-selection direction does not close the observed residual surface. The recorded reason is that $d_\perp$ has systematically positive held-out $R^2$ and the $d_\perp$ directions are mutually same-signed across organisms. The companion residual-axis run, using the same 5-fold and 41-column design scale over 11 of 16 organisms, identifies the dominant non-tRNA residual optimality direction as \texttt{f3\_translation\_selection}. This is the central finite-row fact: $f_3$-stress is strong enough to enter the residual optimality analysis, but the two-axis closure is empirically incomplete.

\paragraph{Third-axis placement.}
The incompleteness is not left as a purely formal failure of span closure. A separate 5-fold run over 11 computed organisms identifies $d_\perp$ provisionally with usage frequency within family, with mean cosine $0.128$ and one-sided sign-test $p=0.000488$. A further mechanism-oriented run reports that the GC-orthogonalized $d_\perp$ keeps significant cross-species alignment and held-out $R^2$, giving the finite classification \texttt{selection-not-GC; not-wobble-clustered; CSC/positional identity needs\_external}. The axis-strength spectrum, again at 5 folds, at least 500 proteins per organism, 11 computed organisms, and 41 synonymous design columns, reports that captured spectra are led by \texttt{d\_resid4} on average and then by \texttt{d\_tRNA}; every one of the 11 computed organisms has \texttt{d\_resid4} as the dominant per-organism axis. The reported domain counts are imbalanced, with $n=\{\mathrm{archaea}:1,\mathrm{bacteria}:2,\mathrm{eukaryote}:8\}$, and the largest GC3 rank effect is only the descriptive value $\rho=0.136$ for \texttt{d\_usage}. These numbers justify treating the $f_3$-stress row as a live translation-readout target while refusing to collapse all residual structure into the two named axes.

\paragraph{Translation-contact restriction.}
The readback allowed here is the controlled survival entry $S_{f3\text{-}stress,T_j}$ for a translation-layer row $T_j$. The admissible rows are bounded codon-adaptation weights such as tAI or stAI, or measured ribosome/translation-efficiency rows when organism- and CDS-matched observations exist. The statistic must be computed after amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, CDS length, $M$-density, matched mRNA, rank, and simpler-baseline controls. In the notation of this chapter, support would require a nondegenerate positive entry
$$
\begin{aligned}
S_{f3\text{-}stress,T_j}>0
\end{aligned}
$$
under those controls. The present finite record does not contain the same-organism and same-CDS joined codon, wobble-weight, measured translation-readout, and matched mRNA-control matrix needed to report that inequality for the exact $f_3$-stress packet. Therefore the chapter records a powered residual-coordinate result and a translation-readout test target, not an observed ribosome-occupancy result.

\paragraph{Mechanism contacts and negative exclusions.}
The wider $B^*_{Q6}$ panel supplies useful constraints on what may and may not be inferred. The R-sense tRNA-poverty test used 1000 permutations at $\alpha=0.05$, computed 40 organisms out of 46 requested, skipped 6, and passed with the finite call that R sense codons are systematically tRNA-poor under GtRNAdb tGCN plus dos Reis wobble weights. The universal-core tRNA-adaptation run also passed at 5 folds over 11 computed organisms out of 16 requested, with at least 500 proteins per organism and 41 synonymous design columns. The three-axis sufficiency run at the same scale concluded \texttt{fourth\_mechanism\_present}, so even a successful $f_3$ translation-readout row would not license a two-axis universal law. The fourth-mechanism screens further narrow overinterpretation: codon-pair identity was called \texttt{not\_codon\_pair} with 50 shuffle nulls, translation accuracy was called \texttt{not\_accuracy} with 200 permutations over 11 computed organisms, mRNA stability was excluded as the dominant fourth-axis identity in the yeast half-life assay, and elongation dwell was only \texttt{partial} in \textit{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}. These classifications are finite restrictions on the search space, not proofs of a complete biological mechanism.

\paragraph{Local no-promotion rule.}
No translation, folding, physical admissibility, protein function, localization, stability, phenotype, mechanism, or universality follows from the $B^*_{Q6}$ codon geometry, from the $f_3$-stress coordinate, from tAI or stAI weights, or from the powered two-axis failure alone. A translation claim requires the positive controlled row $S_{f3\text{-}stress,T_j}$ on matched data. A protein-abundance claim would require a separate matched protein row after the translation step. Structure, localization, stability, function, phenotype, physical admissibility, and general biological-law claims each require their own layer-matched contact and their own controlled statistic. The all-1.0 three-organism surface is treated as rank degeneracy, not survival. Directionality is therefore local: the coordinate becomes a dynamical readout candidate only where context tRNA or wobble weights break third-position $f_3$ symmetry and where the controlled translation row survives.
