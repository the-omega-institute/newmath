\subsection{Rank-powered two-axis closure for the $B^*_{Q6}$ $f_3$ stress surface}
\label{subsec:biorreality-q6-f3-trna-two-axis-closure}

\paragraph{Finite carrier.}
The record considered here is the finite CDS-by-organism residual coordinate surface obtained after amino-acid projection, restricted to the $B^*_{Q6}$ coordinate that detects third-position $f_3$ stress and paired with translation-layer rows only when the readout is organism-matched.  The carrier is not a protein phenotype and not a structural measurement.  It is a finite residual table whose admissible biological reading is limited to whether the $f_3$-paired synonymous imbalance remains visible after controls for amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, CDS length, $M$-density, and matched mRNA abundance when such matched translation data are present.

\paragraph{Saturation boundary.}
The finite $f_3$ saturation calculation fixes the boundary on which the present claim is allowed to operate.  In run $76d3dc408a57$, the set $M=\mathrm{Med}(R)\cup\{\mathrm{ACA}\}$ was
$$
\begin{aligned}
M=\{&\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{ACA},\mathrm{ACG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\\
&\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The computed $f_3$ saturation of $R$ removed the two ACA/ACG boundary entries and returned
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{Sat}_{f_3}(R)=\{&\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\\
&\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The residual boundary was empty, recorded as $f_3\text{-boundary}=\varnothing$.  This matters because the chapter is not testing an arbitrary codon table; it is testing whether a saturated third-position residual coordinate still forces a distinct translation-facing axis once amino-acid identity has been quotiented away.

\paragraph{Two-axis closure test.}
The powered two-axis closure run $99dc7e2d5abf$ used $5$ folds, required at least $500$ proteins per organism, computed $11$ of $16$ requested organisms, and used $41$ synonymous-design columns.  The deterministic seed was $\mathrm{sha256{:}b\_star\_q6\_universal\_optimal\_two\_axis\_closure\_powered{:}deterministic}$.  Its recorded conclusion was $\mathrm{third\_axis\_present}$ rather than two-axis closure.  The reason was finite and explicit: the residual direction $d_{\perp}$ had systematically positive held-out $R^2$, and the $d_{\perp}$ directions were mutually same-signed across organisms.  Thus the proposed span of the tRNA adaptation axis and the $f_3$ translation-selection axis did not exhaust the observed residual geometry in this powered panel.

\paragraph{Neighboring finite checks.}
The adjacent residual-axis run identified the dominant non-tRNA residual optimality direction as $\mathrm{f3\_translation\_selection}$ on the same scale of $5$ folds, $11$ computed organisms out of $16$, a $500$-protein minimum, and $41$ synonymous-design columns.  The third-axis identity run, also on $5$ folds and $41$ synonymous-design columns, provisionally aligned $d_{\perp}$ with usage frequency within family, with mean cosine $0.128$ and one-sided sign-test $p=0.000488$.  The third-axis mechanism run reported that the GC-orthogonalized $d_{\perp}$ retained significant cross-species alignment and held-out $R^2$, giving the finite classification ``selection-not-GC; not-wobble-clustered; CSC/positional identity needs external data.''  The axis-strength spectrum run computed $11$ organisms, found all per-organism dominant counts led by $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$ with count $11$, and reported the descriptive domain sample as $n=\{\mathrm{archaea}:1,\mathrm{bacteria}:2,\mathrm{eukaryote}:8\}$, with the largest GC3 rank effect at $d_{\mathrm{usage}}$ and $\rho=0.136$.  These neighboring rows make the interpretation narrower, not broader: the evidence is for a finite residual axis that survives the modeled design, not for a completed biological mechanism.

\paragraph{Mechanistic pressure from tRNA-poor boundary codons.}
A separate boundary-set test supplies a compatible pressure point.  Run $d7728cc8768e$ used $1000$ permutations at $\alpha=0.05$, computed $40$ of $46$ requested organisms, skipped $6$, and concluded that $R$ sense codons are systematically tRNA-poor under GtRNAdb tGCN plus dos Reis wobble weights.  This supports a tRNA-supply interpretation for $R$ avoidance, but only at the level of codon usage and modeled wobble-weight asymmetry.  It does not by itself make ribosome occupancy, translation efficiency, protein abundance, structure, localization, stability, function, phenotype, or universality follow.

\paragraph{Readout rule.}
The admissible readout is a bounded survival entry $S_{f3\text{-stress},T_j}$ for a declared translation row such as tAI, stAI, ribosome occupancy, or translation efficiency.  The rule is one-directional: the $B^*_{Q6}$ coordinate may be tested against a translation-layer row after the stated controls, but the coordinate is not itself a translation measurement.  In particular, an all-$1.0$ three-organism controlled matrix is treated as a rank-degenerate null boundary, not as survival.  Likewise, codon topology or modeled tAI coupling can motivate the test, but cannot establish directionality unless context tRNA or wobble weights break the $f_3$ symmetry and a non-vacuous controlled readout row is present.

\paragraph{Local conclusion.}
The finite result is therefore a negative answer to the strongest two-axis closure reading.  The tRNA adaptation axis together with the $f_3$ residual selection axis is not sufficient in the powered table: a same-signed, held-out-positive $d_{\perp}$ remains.  The most specific finite reading is that the $B^*_{Q6}$ $f_3$-stress surface exposes a third residual direction in an $11$-organism, $5$-fold, $41$-column synonymous design, with provisional alignment to within-family usage frequency and not to GC alone.  The result is not a claim about folded structure, physical admissibility, protein function, localization, stability, phenotype, mechanism, or a universal biological law.  Those interpretations require their own layer-matched contacts and their own controlled statistics.  Until same-organism and same-CDS codon rows, wobble or tRNA-weight rows, measured ribosome or translation-efficiency rows, and matched mRNA controls are joined, the chapter records powered residual-axis evidence and a constrained translation-readout target, not a completed downstream biological realization.
