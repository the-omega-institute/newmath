\paragraph{Rank-powered two-axis result.}
The powered two-axis experiment for the claim used $5$ folds, required at least $500$ proteins per organism, computed $11$ organisms out of $16$ requested organisms, and used $41$ synonymous-design columns.  Its deterministic seed was recorded as $\mathrm{sha256:b\_star\_q6\_universal\_optimal\_two\_axis\_closure\_powered:deterministic}$.  The finite conclusion was
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{two\_axis\_closure} = \mathrm{third\_axis\_present},
\end{aligned}
$$
with the recorded reason that the residual direction $d_{\perp}$ had systematically positive held-out $R^2$ and that the $d_{\perp}$ directions were mutually same-signed across organisms.  This means that the span generated by the core tRNA-adaptation direction and the $f_3$-translation-selection direction is not sufficient for the powered panel: a further residual direction remains visible after those two axes are accounted for.

The same finite dossier places this result inside a larger residual-axis picture. \paragraph{Translation-readout condition.}
The biological interpretation is therefore deliberately narrow.  The finite coordinate facts support a residual geometry and a rank-powered axis statement, but a translation-layer claim requires a joined row in which the same organism or coding sequence carries codon counts, the $f_3$-stress coordinate, a context tRNA or wobble-weight asymmetry $w_c \ne w_{c^{f_3}}$, a translation readout, and the declared controls.  The bounded survival entry has the form
$$
\begin{aligned}
S_{f_3,T_j} > 0
\end{aligned}
$$
only when the translation readout $T_j$ is estimated after amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, coding-sequence length, $M$-density, matched mRNA abundance where applicable, rank sufficiency, and simpler baseline ablations.  Modeled tAI or stAI weights may serve as bounded translation-weight surfaces, but they are not the same object as observed ribosome occupancy or measured translation efficiency.  A three-organism all-$1.0$ controlled surface is a rank-degenerate boundary and is not counted as survival.

\paragraph{Mechanistic pressure without promotion.}
The strongest finite mechanistic contact available here is not the $f_3$ survival row itself, but the surrounding tRNA-supply evidence.  The $R$-poverty mechanism run computed $40$ organisms out of $46$ requested organisms, skipped $6$, used $1000$ permutations at $\alpha=0.05$, and reported that $R$ sense codons are systematically tRNA-poor under GtRNAdb tGCN together with dos Reis wobble weights.  This supports a bounded supply-side pressure for $R$ avoidance, and it is compatible with the hypothesis that $f_3$-paired synonymous imbalance becomes dynamical where wobble or tRNA weights break third-position symmetry.  It does not by itself establish an observed $S_{f_3,T_j}$ entry.

\paragraph{Local no-promotion rule.}
The chapter therefore certifies a finite residual-coordinate and axis-closure record, not a downstream biological law.  No claim about translation realization, ribosome occupancy, mature protein abundance, folded structure, localization, stability, physical admissibility, molecular function, phenotype, mechanism, causation, or universality follows from the $B^*_{Q6}$ coordinate geometry, the $M$ and $R$ codon rows, the $d_{\perp}$ result, the tAI/stAI route, or the axis-strength spectrum alone.  Any such stronger statement requires its own layer-matched contact and its own controlled statistic.  In particular, the present record is falsified as a translation-readout conjecture if every controlled $S_{f_3,T_j}$ entry remains non-positive or rank-degenerate across a powered panel, even though the residual coordinate geometry and the third-axis finite result may remain valid as internal finite-row facts.
