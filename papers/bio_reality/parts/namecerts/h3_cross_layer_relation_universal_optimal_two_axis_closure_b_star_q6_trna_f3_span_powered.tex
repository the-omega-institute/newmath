\subsection{Rank-powered two-axis refusal for the $B^*_{Q6}$ $f_3$ span}
\label{sec:bioq6-f3-two-axis-closure}

\paragraph{Finite coordinate under review.}
The object in this record is the finite coding-sequence residual surface obtained after amino-acid projection, organism-level normalization, and the restriction to the $B^*_{Q6}$ coordinate in which the third-position involution $f_3$ pairs synonymous codons.  The saturation computation gives the finite ambient set
$$
\begin{aligned}
M={}&\{\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{ACA},\mathrm{ACG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\\
&\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The $f_3$-saturated residual set removes only $\mathrm{ACA}$ and $\mathrm{ACG}$ from this list:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{Sat}_{f_3}(R)={}&\{\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\\
&\mathrm{CUU},\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The computed $f_3$ boundary is empty.  Thus the coordinate is not being treated as a free biological variable; it is a finite residual contrast whose visible degrees of freedom survive only through row-level readouts that can separate paired synonymous codons after amino-acid composition has been quotiented away.

\paragraph{Powered residual geometry.}
The principal powered computation for this record used five folds, required at least $500$ proteins per organism, requested $16$ organisms, retained $11$ organisms, and used $41$ synonymous-design columns.  Its finite conclusion is not that the tRNA axis and the $f_3$-stress axis close the translation problem.  The recorded conclusion is $\mathrm{third\_axis\_present}$: after the fitted tRNA and $f_3$ directions are accounted for, the orthogonal residual direction $d_{\perp}$ still has systematically positive held-out $R^2$, and its directions are mutually same-signed across organisms.  In symbols, the two-axis closure claim is rejected in the finite panel by the existence of a non-vacuous residual component:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\{d_{\mathrm{tRNA}},d_{f_3}\}\ \text{does not exhaust the observed residual span},\qquad d_{\perp}\neq 0\ \text{in held-out readback}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The same data environment also records that the universal core direction is compatible with tRNA adaptation under the same five-fold, $11$-organism, $41$-column design, while the non-tRNA residual optimality aligns most with $f_3\_\mathrm{translation\_selection}$.  These two facts explain why the $f_3$ coordinate is a serious candidate axis, but they do not license a two-axis completion: the separate sufficiency test reports $\mathrm{fourth\_mechanism\_present}$ under the same fold count, organism count, minimum protein threshold, and design-column count.

\paragraph{Identity and mechanism pressure of the remaining direction.}
The finite identity test for $d_{\perp}$ also used five folds, $11$ computed organisms out of $16$ requested, at least $500$ proteins per organism, and $41$ synonymous-design columns.  It provisionally identifies $d_{\perp}$ with usage frequency within synonymous families, with mean cosine $0.128$ and one-sided sign-test $p=0.000488$.  The companion mechanism test reports that the GC-orthogonalized $d_{\perp}$ keeps significant cross-species alignment and held-out $R^2$, giving the local classification ``selection-not-GC; not-wobble-clustered; CSC/positional identity needs external data.''  This matters for the $f_3$ question because it prevents a purely topological interpretation: paired third-position imbalance becomes biologically interpretable only where a translation-facing row, such as tAI, stAI, ribosome occupancy, or translation efficiency, is explicitly joined to the same finite surface and survives the declared controls.

\paragraph{Translation readout as a guarded row, not a downstream promotion.}
The biological readback allowed here is narrow.  A row $T_j$ may be a bounded codon-adaptation weight, an organism-matched stAI or tAI value, or a measured ribosome-occupancy or translation-efficiency quantity.  For the $f_3$-stress coordinate the relevant survival entry is $S_{f3\text{-}stress,T_j}$, computed after amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, CDS length, $M$-density, mRNA abundance where appropriate, rank, and simpler-baseline controls.  The conjectural support condition is
$$
\begin{aligned}
S_{f3\text{-}stress,T_j}>0
\end{aligned}
$$
for at least one admissible translation row in a rank-powered panel with broken third-position wobble or tRNA-weight symmetry.  The break condition is equally finite: if every controlled entry is non-positive, or if the fitted surface is rank-degenerate, then the $f_3$-stress coordinate has not survived as a translation-readout distinction.  In particular, an all-$1.0$ three-organism controlled matrix is a saturated null boundary, not a positive survival result.

\paragraph{Existing biological contacts and their limits.}
The codon-usage contact supplies per-organism codon-count or frequency summaries.  The tRNA-copy contact supplies organism-level tRNA-Leu copy-number summaries by anticodon family.  The tAI or stAI contact supplies modeled codon-adaptation weights, and the boundary-set test over $40$ computed organisms out of $46$ requested, with $1000$ permutations and $\alpha=0.05$, reports that $R$ sense codons are systematically tRNA-poor under GtRNAdb tGCN plus dos Reis wobble weights.  These contacts support a translation-facing hypothesis space, but they are not themselves measured ribosome dynamics.  The ribosome-profiling and matched mRNA contacts name the required measured row shape: per-CDS RPF or translation-efficiency data paired with mRNA abundance so that $\mathrm{TE}=\mathrm{RPF}/\mathrm{mRNA}$ can be residualized.  The current finite memory does not yet contain the same-organism, same-CDS joined matrix for the exact $q6.f3\text{-}stress.wobble\text{-}broken.translation\text{-}readout$ statistic.

\paragraph{No-promotion boundary.}
This record therefore has a positive finite result about the geometry of the residual span and a negative boundary against over-reading it.  The $11$-organism powered panel rejects closure by the tRNA and $f_3$ axes alone, identifies a third residual direction with mean cosine $0.128$ and sign-test $p=0.000488$, and keeps the $f_3$-stress row open as a controlled translation-readout target.  It does not establish translation realization unless a same-row $S_{f3\text{-}stress,T_j}$ value is computed and positive.  It also does not establish protein abundance, folding, physical admissibility, localization, stability, molecular function, system phenotype, mechanism, causation, or universality.  Each of those layers requires its own layer-matched contact and its own controlled finite statistic.  Codon topology, wobble-weight asymmetry, modeled tAI or stAI coupling, and review eligibility are therefore kept as bounded coordinate and readout information, not as downstream biological conclusions.
