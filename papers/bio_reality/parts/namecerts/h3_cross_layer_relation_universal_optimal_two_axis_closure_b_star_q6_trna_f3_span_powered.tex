\subsection{The $B^*_{Q6}$ $f_3$-stress translation-readout boundary}\label{sec:bioreality-q6-f3-stress-translation-readout}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite carrier.}
This record concerns the finite CDS-by-organism residual coordinate surface obtained after amino-acid projection and restricted to the $B^*_{Q6}$ $f_3$-stress coordinate. The coordinate is not treated as a direct biological observation. It is a finite residual axis whose possible biological contact is restricted to matched translation-readout rows: organism-level codon usage, tRNA or wobble weights, bounded tAI or stAI weights, and, where available, same-row ribosome-occupancy or translation-efficiency measurements with matched mRNA controls. The intended readback is therefore a controlled survival entry $S_{f3\text{-}stress,T_j}$ for a translation readout $T_j$, not a claim about protein abundance, localization, stability, folded structure, function, phenotype, mechanism, or a universal biological law.

\paragraph{Closed codon-side finite rows.}
The $f_3$ saturation row fixes the codon-side support before any biological readout is considered. The recorded set
$$\begin{aligned}
M =\{&AAA,AAG,ACA,ACG,AGA,AGG,AUA,AUG,CUA,CUC,CUG,CUU,\\
&UAA,UAG,UCA,UCG,UGA,UGG,UUA,UUG\}
\end{aligned}$$
has $20$ codons. The saturated $f_3$ image of $R$ is
$$\begin{aligned}
\operatorname{Sat}_{f_3}(R)=\{&AAA,AAG,AGA,AGG,AUA,AUG,CUA,CUC,CUG,CUU,\\
&UAA,UAG,UCA,UCG,UGA,UGG,UUA,UUG\},
\end{aligned}$$
with $18$ codons. Thus the finite difference $M\setminus \operatorname{Sat}_{f_3}(R)$ is exactly $\{ACA,ACG\}$ in this row, while the recorded $f_3$ boundary is empty. This is the codon-topological starting point: the $f_3$ residual has a finite support after amino-acid quotienting, but the support alone does not establish a translation-layer effect.

\paragraph{Two-axis test result.}
The powered two-axis closure run for this claim passed with run id $99dc7e2d5abf$. It used $5$ folds, required at least $500$ proteins per organism, computed $11$ organisms out of $16$ requested organisms, and used $41$ synonymous-design columns. Its finite conclusion was \emph{third\_axis\_present}. The stated reason was that $d_\perp$ has systematically positive held-out $R^2$ and that the $d_\perp$ directions are mutually same-signed across organisms. In formula form, the result is a refusal of two-axis closure rather than a positive translation-readout survival claim:
$$\begin{aligned}
\operatorname{span}(d_{tRNA},d_{f_3}) \;\hbox{does not exhaust the observed residual optimum,}
\end{aligned}$$
because a same-signed orthogonal direction remains detectable in held-out evaluation. This makes the $f_3$ stress coordinate biologically interesting as part of a larger residual geometry, but it does not by itself produce a nonzero $S_{f3\text{-}stress,T_j}$ row.

\paragraph{Relation to adjacent finite rows.}
The residual-axis run identified the dominant non-tRNA residual optimum as \texttt{f3\_translation\_selection}; it used $5$ folds, $11$ computed organisms out of $16$ requested organisms, a $500$ protein minimum, and $41$ synonymous-design columns. The core tRNA-adaptation run over the same organism and design scale also passed. The third-axis identity run, again with $5$ folds, $11$ computed organisms, and $41$ synonymous-design columns, identified $d_\perp$ provisionally with \texttt{usage\_frequency\_within\_family}, with mean cosine $0.128$ and one-sided sign-test $p=0.000488$. The third-axis mechanism run retained significant cross-species alignment after GC orthogonalization and reported the finite summary \texttt{selection-not-GC; not-wobble-clustered; CSC/positional identity needs\_external}. These rows jointly say that the residual geometry is not reducible to a two-axis tRNA-plus-$f_3$ span, and that the remaining axis is better read as a selection-like usage-frequency direction than as a simple GC effect.

\paragraph{Spectrum and mechanism context.}
The per-organism spectrum run computed $11$ organisms out of $16$ requested organisms with $5$ folds, $500$ proteins per organism, and $41$ synonymous-design columns. Its captured spectra were led on average by $d_{resid4}$, followed by $d_{tRNA}$; all $11$ per-organism dominant counts were assigned to $d_{resid4}$. The domain counts were imbalanced, with $n=1$ archaeal, $n=2$ bacterial, and $n=8$ eukaryotic organisms, so those domain means are descriptive only. The largest GC3 rank effect was $d_{usage}$ with $\rho=0.136$. A separate tRNA-poverty mechanism run over the $R$ sense-codon boundary passed with $40$ computed organisms out of $46$ requested organisms, $6$ skipped organisms, $1000$ permutations, and $\alpha=0.05$, reporting systematic tRNA poverty under GtRNAdb tGCN plus dos Reis wobble weights. This supports a tRNA-supply interpretation for $R$ avoidance, but still does not convert the $f_3$ residual coordinate into measured ribosome occupancy or translation efficiency.

\paragraph{Survival gate.}
The required finite statistic for this chapter is not merely the existence of codon topology, modeled tAI or stAI weights, or a same-signed residual direction. The row that would support a translation-readout claim is
$$\begin{aligned}
S_{f3\text{-}stress,T_j} > 0
\end{aligned}$$
after controls for amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, CDS length, $M$-density baseline, matched mRNA abundance where translation efficiency is used, residualized rank, and simpler codon baselines. The current finite memory contains no same-organism and same-CDS joined matrix for codon counts, wobble-breaking tAI or stAI surface, measured ribosome-profiling or translation-efficiency readout, and matched mRNA abundance for this exact $f_3$-stress subject. Consequently, the present row is a powered geometric and modeled-readout boundary, not an observed $S^{QT}$ survival row.

\paragraph{Higher-layer non-promotion.}
The optimality-axis sufficiency run passed and concluded \texttt{fourth\_mechanism\_present}, using $5$ folds, $11$ computed organisms out of $16$, $500$ proteins per organism, and $41$ synonymous-design columns. Candidate fourth-mechanism rows then excluded several simple interpretations: mRNA stability was reported as \texttt{fourth\_axis\_not\_mrna\_stability}; codon-pair structure was reported as \texttt{not\_codon\_pair} using $50$ shuffle nulls; translation accuracy was reported as \texttt{not\_accuracy} using $200$ permutations; elongation dwell was only \texttt{partial} in \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}. These finite rows sharpen the residual landscape, but none supplies a separate contact for protein abundance, folding, physical admissibility, localization, stability, biological function, phenotype, mechanism, or universality. Any such assertion would require its own layer-matched measurements and its own controlled statistic after the translation row has first become non-vacuous and positive.

\paragraph{Local conclusion.}
The finite evidence supports the following narrow statement. In a powered multi-organism residual analysis, the $B^*_{Q6}$ tRNA-plus-$f_3$ two-axis span is insufficient: a same-signed $d_\perp$ direction remains, adjacent rows identify the dominant non-tRNA residual optimum with $f_3$-translation-selection, and the third axis is provisionally aligned to within-family usage frequency with mean cosine $0.128$ and sign-test $p=0.000488$. The row does not yet show that $f_3$-stress survives as a measured translation-readout distinction. The next admissible support row must attach same-row measured translation data and compute the controlled $S_{f3\text{-}stress,T_j}$ statistic. Until that row exists, the chapter permits only a bounded residual-geometry and modeled-readout interpretation.
