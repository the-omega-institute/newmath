\paragraph{Rank-powered result.}
The powered two-axis test was run as experiment $\mathrm{b\_star\_q6\_universal\_optimal\_two\_axis\_closure\_powered}$ with deterministic seed $\mathrm{sha256:b\_star\_q6\_universal\_optimal\_two\_axis\_closure\_powered:deterministic}$. It used $5$ folds, required at least $500$ proteins per organism, requested $16$ organisms, computed $11$ organisms, and used $41$ synonymous design columns. The status was passed, but the conclusion was not that the tRNA axis and the $f_3$ axis close the system. The finite conclusion was $\mathrm{third\_axis\_present}$: after the two-axis span is fitted, $d_{\perp}$ has systematically positive held-out $R^2$, and the $d_{\perp}$ directions are mutually same-signed across organisms. In symbols, the recorded outcome is best read as
$$\begin{aligned}
\operatorname{span}(d_{\mathrm{tRNA}},d_{f_3}) \neq \operatorname{span}_{\mathrm{held\mbox{-}out}}(B^*_{Q6}),
\end{aligned}$$
where the inequality means a remaining predictive direction is visible under the finite rank-powered design.

\paragraph{Translation-readout gate.}
The admissible readback for this chapter is narrow. The $f_3$-stress coordinate may be compared with tAI, stAI, ribosome-occupancy, or translation-efficiency rows only as a controlled survival entry $S_{f3\text{-}stress,T_j}$. A positive entry would require a same-organism, and where relevant same-CDS, joined table containing codon counts, tRNA or wobble weights, a direct or explicitly bounded translation readout, and matched mRNA abundance. The survival statistic must remain positive after amino-acid composition, organism identity, GC3, CDS length, $M$-density, mRNA, rank, and simpler-baseline controls. The three-organism all-$1.0$ surface is therefore a rank-degenerate boundary, not survival evidence. Likewise, modeled tAI or stAI asymmetry can identify where $w_c \neq w_{c^{f_3}}$ makes the coordinate testable, but it is not by itself an observed ribosome-occupancy row.

\paragraph{Local biological reading.}
The strongest finite statement is a conditional one. After amino-acid quotienting, the $f_3$-stress residual coordinate is a candidate translation-layer distinction only in organisms or contexts where tRNA or wobble weights break third-position $f_3$ symmetry. The powered two-axis experiment says that the tRNA plus $f_3$ span is insufficient because a third direction remains. The companion rows say that this remaining direction aligns weakly but significantly with within-family usage frequency, survives GC orthogonalization, and is not closed by a wobble-cluster explanation in the current finite table. A separate boundary-set experiment over $40$ computed organisms out of $46$ requested organisms, with $1000$ permutations at $\alpha=0.05$, reports that $R$ sense codons are systematically tRNA-poor under GtRNAdb gene-copy counts plus dos Reis wobble weights; this supports a tRNA-supply interpretation for $R$ avoidance, but it still does not create a measured $S_{f3\text{-}stress,T_j}$ row.
