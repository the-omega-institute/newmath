\subsection{Rank-Powered Two-Axis Closure Fails for the $B^*_{Q6}$ $f_3$ Translation Stress Coordinate}\label{subsec:bior reality-q6-f3-two-axis-closure}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite coordinate being tested.}
This record concerns the finite CDS-by-organism residual surface obtained after amino-acid projection, restricted to the $B^*_{Q6}$ $f_3$-stress coordinate and to rows whose intended biological contact is a translation readout. The coordinate is not a protein-output, structure, function, localization, stability, phenotype, or law-of-life claim. It asks a narrower question: after synonymous codons are compared only inside an amino-acid fibre, does the $f_3$-paired imbalance remain informative for translation-layer readouts when organism, amino-acid composition, GC3, CDS length, $M$-density, and mRNA controls are present? The finite support of the $f_3$ construction is explicit. The saturated $R$-derived set is
$$
\begin{aligned}
\operatorname{Sat}_{f_3}(R)=\{&\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\\
&\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The ambient finite set used for the same row is
$$
\begin{aligned}
M=\{&\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{ACA},\mathrm{ACG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\\
&\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the recorded saturation has $18$ codons inside a $20$-codon ambient list, with the difference represented by $\mathrm{ACA}$ and $\mathrm{ACG}$. The reported $f_3$ boundary list is empty, so this row is not treating a missing boundary codon as evidence. It is treating the finite codon surface as a coordinate on which a later translation contact may or may not survive.

\paragraph{Rank-powered result.}
The powered two-axis closure experiment was run with $5$ folds, $41$ synonymous design columns, a minimum of $500$ proteins per organism, and $11$ computed organisms out of $16$ requested organisms. The recorded conclusion is \emph{third\_axis\_present}. Its stated finite reason is that $d_{\perp}$ has systematically positive held-out $R^2$ and that the $d_{\perp}$ directions are mutually same-signed across organisms. In the notation of this chapter, the tested two-axis span is the span of a tRNA-adaptation direction and an $f_3$-translation-selection residual direction. The finite result says that this span is not sufficient for the observed residual surface:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\operatorname{span}(d_{\mathrm{tRNA}},d_{f_3})\quad \hbox{does not exhaust the held-out residual direction,}\qquad d_{\perp}\neq 0
\end{aligned}
$$
where $d_{\perp}$ is read only as a fitted residual direction in the finite design, not as a biological mechanism by itself. The companion optimality-sufficiency computation, also run with $5$ folds, $41$ synonymous design columns, $500$ proteins per organism, and $11$ computed organisms out of $16$, independently records the conclusion \emph{fourth\_mechanism\_present}. This keeps the two-axis failure from being overread as a complete basis theorem: the result is a positive finite obstruction to the two-axis model, not a closed catalogue of all translation-relevant axes.

\paragraph{Relation to the surrounding spectrum.}
The exact two-axis row is consistent with the recorded neighboring finite facts. The universal core tRNA-adaptation row passed under the same scale of $5$ folds, $41$ synonymous design columns, $500$ proteins per organism, and $11$ computed organisms out of $16$. The non-tRNA residual optimality row identifies the dominant residual direction as $f_3\_translation\_selection$ at that same scale. These two facts justify testing the span of the tRNA direction together with the $f_3$ residual direction. The failure of that span is therefore not a failure to locate the first two axes; it is a statement that the finite row still contains another reproducible direction after those axes are present.

\paragraph{Identity and mechanism of the extra direction.}
The recorded third-axis identity row reports that $d_{\perp}$ is provisionally aligned to usage frequency within family, with mean cosine $0.128$ and one-sided sign-test $p=0.000488$, again over $5$ folds, $41$ synonymous design columns, $500$ proteins per organism, and $11$ computed organisms out of $16$. The associated mechanism row reports \emph{selection-not-GC; not-wobble-clustered; CSC/positional identity needs\_external}, with the reason that GC-orthogonalized $d_{\perp}$ keeps significant cross-species alignment and held-out $R^2$. This is a constrained readback. It permits the chapter to say that the third direction is not explained away by the GC3 control used in the finite design, and that it is not recorded as a simple wobble-cluster effect. It does not permit the chapter to assign a full molecular mechanism, because the row itself keeps the CSC or positional interpretation open.

\paragraph{Per-organism strength profile.}
The per-organism axis-strength spectrum computed $11$ organisms out of $16$ requested organisms, with $5$ folds, $41$ synonymous design columns, and at least $500$ proteins per organism. In that profile, the captured spectra are led on average by $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$ and then by $d_{\mathrm{tRNA}}$; the per-organism dominant count is $11$ for $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$. The domain counts are imbalanced, with $1$ archaeal, $2$ bacterial, and $8$ eukaryotic organisms, so domain-level summaries are descriptive rather than inferential. The largest reported GC3 rank effect in that spectrum is $d_{\mathrm{usage}}$ with $\rho=0.136$. These numbers are important because they keep the two-axis closure claim finite: the evidence is a rank-powered multi-organism design with specific row counts and folds, not a universal species theorem.

\paragraph{Translation-readout gate.}
The biological interpretation is deliberately narrower than the algebraic result. The $f_3$ coordinate becomes a candidate translation distinction only where context tRNA or wobble weights break the $f_3$ symmetry, written locally as $w_c\neq w_{c^{f_3}}$. The recorded boundary-set computation supports the plausibility of a tRNA-supply route at a separate finite scale: $40$ organisms were computed out of $46$ requested organisms, $6$ were skipped, $1000$ permutations were used, and the conclusion says that $R$ sense codons are systematically tRNA-poor under GtRNAdb tGCN plus dos Reis wobble weights. That fact supports a tRNA-supply mechanism for $R$ avoidance, but it does not by itself create a positive translation-survival entry for the present coordinate.

\paragraph{Local survival criterion.}
For this chapter, the bounded readout is the controlled entry $S_{f_3\text{-stress},T_j}$ for a translation row such as tAI, stAI, ribosome occupancy, or translation efficiency. A positive row must be non-vacuous after the declared controls. The intended test can be written as
$$
\begin{aligned}
S_{f_3\text{-stress},T_j}>0
\end{aligned}
$$
only when the matched organism or CDS table supplies the translation measurement and the baseline ablation has already removed amino-acid composition, organism identity, GC3, CDS length, $M$-density, mRNA abundance where applicable, and simpler codon baselines. A rank-degenerate all-$1.0$ matrix over three organisms is therefore a refusal boundary rather than survival. Likewise, modeled tAI or stAI weights are bounded translation-layer annotations, not direct ribosome-occupancy observations.

\paragraph{Cannot-claim boundary.}
The finite facts above support the following chapter-specific statement: in the powered residual design, the tRNA plus $f_3$ two-axis span does not close the $B^*_{Q6}$ synonymous residual surface, and a same-signed held-out $d_{\perp}$ direction remains across the computed organisms. They do not establish translation realization unless an organism-matched translation row gives a positive controlled $S_{f_3\text{-stress},T_j}$. They also do not establish ribosome mechanism, protein abundance, folding, structural order, physical admissibility, localization, stability, molecular function, system phenotype, or universality. Each of those claims requires its own layer-matched contact and its own statistic. The safe conclusion is therefore a finite, rank-powered obstruction to two-axis closure, coupled to an explicit demand for a nondegenerate translation-readout row before any biological promotion is allowed.
