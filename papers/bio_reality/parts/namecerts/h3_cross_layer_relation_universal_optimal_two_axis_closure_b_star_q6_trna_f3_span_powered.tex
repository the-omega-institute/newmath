\subsection{The $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ two-axis closure test at the $f_3$ stress boundary}
\label{sec:bioreality-q6-f3-stress-two-axis-closure}

\paragraph{Finite carrier.}
This record concerns a finite coding-sequence residual surface: rows are organism-indexed CDS observations, columns are synonymous residual coordinates after amino-acid projection, and the distinguished coordinate is the $f_3$-stress component inside the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ panel. The intended biological reading is deliberately narrow. The coordinate may be compared with codon usage, tRNA copy number, tAI or stAI weights, and matched translation readouts; it is not by itself a claim about protein abundance, folded structure, localization, molecular function, phenotype, or a universal biological law. The finite saturation check fixes the relevant codon support. The set $M=\operatorname{Med}_R\cup\{\mathrm{ACA}\}$ is
$$\begin{aligned}
M=\{&\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{ACA},\mathrm{ACG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\\
&\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\}.
\end{aligned}$$
The saturated $f_3$ image of $R$ removes exactly the two ACA/ACG boundary entries:
$$\begin{aligned}
\operatorname{Sat}_{f_3}(R)=\{&\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\\
&\mathrm{CUU},\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\},
\end{aligned}$$
and the recorded $f_3$ boundary is empty. Thus the finite combinatorial surface is already closed enough to ask whether a biological readout distinguishes the $f_3$ residual after the amino-acid quotient has erased ordinary amino-acid composition effects.

\paragraph{Powered finite-row result.}
The direct two-axis closure run for this packet is experiment $\mathrm{b\_star\_q6\_universal\_optimal\_two\_axis\_closure\_powered}$, run id $99\mathrm{dc}7\mathrm{e}2\mathrm{d}5\mathrm{abf}$. It used $5$ folds, required at least $500$ proteins per organism, computed $11$ of $16$ requested organisms, and used $41$ synonymous-design columns. Its finite conclusion is $\mathrm{third\_axis\_present}$, with the reason that $d_{\perp}$ has systematically positive held-out $R^2$ and that the $d_{\perp}$ directions are mutually same-signed across organisms. In formulas, the result says that the span generated by the core tRNA-adaptation direction and the $f_3$ translation-selection residual is not sufficient for all captured organism-level residual structure:
$$\begin{aligned}
\operatorname{span}(d_{\mathrm{tRNA}},d_{f_3}) \;\neq\; \operatorname{span}(d_{\mathrm{tRNA}},d_{f_3},d_{\perp})
\end{aligned}$$
under the recorded held-out design. This is a finite regression-and-rank statement, not a mechanistic law.

\paragraph{Relation to the surrounding coordinate spectrum.}
The neighboring powered rows sharpen the interpretation of the two-axis failure. The core tRNA-adaptation row passed on $11$ computed organisms out of $16$ requested, again with $5$ folds, $500$ minimum proteins per organism, and $41$ synonymous columns. The non-tRNA residual optimality row identified $f_3\_\mathrm{translation\_selection}$ as the dominant residual axis under the same organism and design counts. The third-axis identity row then assigned $d_{\perp}$ provisionally to usage frequency within synonymous family, with mean cosine $0.128$ and one-sided sign-test $p=0.000488$. The third-axis mechanism row reported that GC-orthogonalized $d_{\perp}$ keeps significant cross-species alignment and held-out $R^2$, summarized as selection rather than GC, not wobble-clustered, with CSC or positional identity still requiring an additional contact. Finally, the three-axis sufficiency run concluded $\mathrm{fourth\_mechanism\_present}$ on the same $11/16$ organism panel with $5$ folds, $500$ minimum proteins, and $41$ synonymous columns. These rows jointly place the present packet at a precise finite boundary: the $f_3$ residual is real enough as a coordinate in the powered spectrum, but it does not close the organism-level residual surface with tRNA adaptation alone.

\paragraph{tRNA and wobble break condition.}
The biological trigger is not the mere existence of third-position synonymy. The $f_3$ coordinate becomes a candidate translation-readout distinction only where context tRNA or wobble weights distinguish a codon from its $f_3$ partner, written schematically as $w_c\neq w_{c^{f_3}}$. The independent boundary-set tRNA-poverty run computed $40$ organisms out of $46$ requested, skipped $6$, used $1000$ permutations at $\alpha=0.05$, and passed with the mechanism call that $R$ sense codons are systematically tRNA-poor under GtRNAdb tGCN plus dos Reis wobble weights. This supports a tRNA-supply interpretation of $R$ avoidance, but it still does not supply a measured ribosome or TE survival row for the exact $f_3$-stress coordinate.

\paragraph{Bounded survival statistic.}
The only admissible positive readout for this packet is a controlled survival entry $S_{f_3\text{-}\mathrm{stress},T_j}$ for a translation-layer row $T_j$, where $T_j$ may be a bounded tAI/stAI surface or a measured ribosome-occupancy or translation-efficiency row. The statistic must be evaluated after amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, CDS length, $M$-density, matched mRNA abundance, rank, and simpler-baseline controls. In the notation of this chapter, the required test is
$$\begin{aligned}
S_{f_3\text{-}\mathrm{stress},T_j}>0
\end{aligned}$$
for at least one eligible translation readout $T_j$ on a nondegenerate matched panel. If the surface is all $1.0$ on only three organisms, that is rank saturation and not survival. If all controlled entries are non-positive, or if the fitted matrix is rank-degenerate, the conjecture is falsified at the translation-readout boundary.

\paragraph{No-promotion rule.}
The fourth-mechanism rows show why the boundary is necessary. The mRNA-stability run passed with conclusion $\mathrm{fourth\_axis\_not\_mrna\_stability}$ because yeast $d_{\mathrm{opt\_halflife}}$ had low alignment with abundance $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$ under the fixed gates. The elongation-dwell row gave only a partial conclusion in \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}. The codon-pair row concluded $\mathrm{not\_codon\_pair}$ with $50$ shuffle nulls, and the translation-accuracy row concluded $\mathrm{not\_accuracy}$ using $200$ permutations on $11$ computed organisms out of $16$ requested. These finite negatives and partials prevent promotion from coordinate geometry to biological mechanism. The present chapter therefore asserts only a controlled question: does the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ $f_3$-stress residual survive into an explicitly matched translation readout when wobble or tRNA asymmetry breaks the $f_3$ pairing? No statement about translation realization beyond that bounded statistic, protein amount, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, phenotype, mechanism, or universality follows without a separate layer-matched contact and its own recorded statistic.
