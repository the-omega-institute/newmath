\subsection{The $B^*_{Q6}$ tRNA--$f_3$ Span Does Not Close the Translation Layer}\label{sec:bioreality-q6-f3-trna-two-axis-closure}

\paragraph{Finite carrier.}
This record concerns a finite CDS-by-organism residual coordinate surface after amino-acid projection.  The distinguished coordinate is the $B^*_{Q6}$ third-position stress direction, written here as the $f_3$-stress coordinate, together with organism-scoped codon usage, tRNA or wobble-weight annotations, and only those translation readouts that can be paired to the same organism or coding sequence.  The finite codon boundary inherited by the record is explicit.  The mediated set $M={\rm Med}(R)\cup\{\mathrm{ACA}\}$ contains
$$\begin{aligned}
M=\{&\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{ACA},\mathrm{ACG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\\
&\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\}.
\end{aligned}$$
The $f_3$-saturated $R$-surface removes the two codons $\mathrm{ACA}$ and $\mathrm{ACG}$, giving
$$\begin{aligned}
{\rm Sat}_{f_3}(R)=\{&\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\\
&\mathrm{CUU},\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\},
\end{aligned}$$
and the recorded $f_3$ boundary is empty.  Thus the chapter starts from a finite codon-coordinate object, not from a measured biological law.

\paragraph{Two-axis test and observed failure of closure.}
The powered two-axis closure experiment for this claim used $5$ folds, required at least $500$ proteins per organism, computed $11$ organisms out of $16$ requested organisms, and used $41$ synonymous design columns.  Its deterministic seed was $\mathrm{sha256{:}b\_star\_q6\_universal\_optimal\_two\_axis\_closure\_powered{:}deterministic}$.  The finite result was not that the tRNA axis and the $f_3$ residual axis close the surface.  The recorded conclusion is $\mathrm{third\_axis\_present}$, with the reason that the perpendicular residual direction $d_{\perp}$ has systematically positive held-out $R^2$ and that the $d_{\perp}$ directions are mutually same-signed across organisms.  In local notation, the tested closure assertion has the form
$$\begin{aligned}
Q_{\rm opt}\subseteq {\rm span}(d_{\rm tRNA},d_{f_3})
\end{aligned}$$
up to the declared controls.  The finite readout rejects this as a complete span for the powered panel, because a residual axis outside that span remains detectable on held-out rows.

\paragraph{Relation to the adjacent powered coordinates.}
The surrounding finite records sharpen the interpretation.  The non-tRNA residual optimality experiment, also on $11$ computed organisms out of $16$ requested with $5$ folds, at least $500$ proteins per organism, and $41$ synonymous design columns, identified its dominant axis as $\mathrm{f3\_translation\_selection}$.  The core tRNA-adaptation experiment over the same fold, organism, protein, and design-column counts passed as a bounded tRNA-adaptation coordinate result.  The perpendicular-axis identity experiment again used $5$ folds, $11$ computed organisms out of $16$, at least $500$ proteins per organism, and $41$ synonymous design columns; it provisionally aligned $d_{\perp}$ to usage frequency within family, with mean cosine $0.128$ and one-sided sign-test $p=0.000488$.  Its mechanism companion retained significant cross-species alignment and held-out $R^2$ after GC orthogonalization, giving the finite conclusion $\mathrm{selection\mbox{-}not\mbox{-}GC}$, $\mathrm{not\mbox{-}wobble\mbox{-}clustered}$, and $\mathrm{CSC/positional\ identity\ needs\_external}$.

\paragraph{Spectrum and boundary mechanism.}
The axis-strength spectrum was computed on $11$ organisms out of $16$ requested organisms, with $5$ folds, at least $500$ proteins per organism, and $41$ synonymous design columns.  Its descriptive spectrum was led by $d_{\rm resid4}$ on average, followed by $d_{\rm tRNA}$; the per-organism dominant count was $\{d_{\rm resid4}:11\}$.  Domain means were explicitly marked descriptive because the observed domain counts were imbalanced, $\{\mathrm{archaea}:1,\mathrm{bacteria}:2,\mathrm{eukaryote}:8\}$, and the largest GC3 rank effect was only $\rho=0.136$ for $d_{\rm usage}$.  Separately, the $R$-sense tRNA-poverty mechanism test used $40$ computed organisms out of $46$ requested, skipped $6$ organisms, used $1000$ permutations at $\alpha=0.05$, and passed with the finite mechanism call that $R$ sense codons are systematically tRNA-poor under GtRNAdb tGCN plus dos Reis wobble weights.  These rows support a bounded tRNA-supply pressure for the $R$ boundary, while leaving the two-axis closure itself false on the powered residual surface.

\paragraph{Readout rule for the $f_3$-stress coordinate.}
The permitted readback is narrow.  The $f_3$-stress coordinate becomes a translation-readout candidate only where the context weights break the third-position pairing, so that a codon $c$ and its $f_3$ mate $c^{f_3}$ have unequal translation weights, $w_c\ne w_{c^{f_3}}$.  Even then, the only admissible survival entry is a controlled row such as $S_{f_3\text{-}{\rm stress},T_j}$ for a declared translation readout $T_j$, for example bounded tAI, bounded stAI, ribosome occupancy, or translation efficiency.  The local support condition is
$$\begin{aligned}
S_{f_3\text{-}{\rm stress},T_j}>0
\end{aligned}$$
after amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, CDS length, $M$-density, matched mRNA, rank, and simpler-baseline controls.  A rank-degenerate all-$1.0$ surface on three organisms is treated as a null boundary, not as survival.

\paragraph{What the finite row does not say.}
The powered result is a coordinate and spectrum statement: two axes do not exhaust the finite optimal residual surface, and a third direction remains visible.  It is not a statement that $f_3$-stress changes protein abundance, folded structure, localization, stability, molecular function, phenotype, physical admissibility, or a universal biological rule.  The fourth-mechanism records reinforce this separation rather than removing it: the mRNA-stability assay concluded $\mathrm{fourth\_axis\_not\_mrna\_stability}$ under its fixed gates; the elongation-dwell assay gave $\mathrm{partial}$ in $\mathrm{saccharomyces\_cerevisiae}$; the codon-pair assay concluded $\mathrm{not\_codon\_pair}$ after $50$ shuffle-null draws; and the translation-accuracy assay concluded $\mathrm{not\_accuracy}$ using $200$ permutations over $11$ computed organisms out of $16$.  Therefore any later translation, protein, structure, physical, functional, mechanistic, or universal interpretation must be supplied by its own layer-matched biological contact and by a recorded controlled statistic at that layer.
