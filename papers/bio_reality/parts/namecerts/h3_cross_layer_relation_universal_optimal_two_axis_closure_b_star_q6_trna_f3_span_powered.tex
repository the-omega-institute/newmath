\subsection{Wobble-Broken Third-Position Stress and the Translation-Readout Boundary}
\label{subsec:q6-f3-stress-wobble-broken-translation-readout}

\paragraph{Finite coordinate surface.}
This record isolates the $B^*_{Q6}$ third-position stress coordinate after amino-acid quotienting.  The carrier is a finite CDS-by-organism residual surface: rows are coding sequences in organism-matched panels, columns are synonymous residual coordinates, and the distinguished row tested here is the $f_3$-paired codon-imbalance coordinate after amino-acid composition has been projected out.  The coordinate is not interpreted as an observed translation event by itself.  Its admissible readout is only a bounded survival entry $S_{f3\text{-}stress,T_j}$ against a translation-layer row $T_j$, where $T_j$ may be an explicitly bounded tAI or stAI weight, a ribosome-occupancy readout, or a translation-efficiency readout with matched mRNA control.

The finite saturation data give the local codon support.  The mediate set with the added ACA row contains twenty codons,
$$
\begin{aligned}
M &= \{AAA,AAG,ACA,ACG,AGA,AGG,AUA,AUG,CUA,CUC,CUG,CUU,\\
&\qquad UAA,UAG,UCA,UCG,UGA,UGG,UUA,UUG\},
\end{aligned}
$$
while the $f_3$-saturated residual set removes exactly ACA and ACG:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{Sat}_{f_3}(R) &= \{AAA,AAG,AGA,AGG,AUA,AUG,CUA,CUC,CUG,CUU,\\
&\qquad UAA,UAG,UCA,UCG,UGA,UGG,UUA,UUG\},\\
\partial_{f_3} &= \varnothing.
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the finite boundary is not a missing-codon artifact: the $f_3$ boundary is empty in the recorded table, and the remaining question is whether third-position residual imbalance has any controlled translation readback once amino-acid identity and simpler sequence covariates have been removed.

\paragraph{Powered two-axis result.}
The powered two-axis experiment for \texttt{h3.cross\_layer\_relation.universal\_optimal\_two\_axis\_closure.b\_star\_q6\_trna\_f3\_span\_powered} passed on 2026-06-19 with run id \texttt{99dc7e2d5abf}.  It used five folds, required at least $500$ proteins per organism, computed $11$ organisms out of $16$ requested, and used $41$ synonymous-design columns.  Its conclusion was \texttt{third\_axis\_present}: after the tRNA-adaptation and $f_3$-translation-selection span was fitted, the perpendicular direction $d_{\perp}$ still had systematically positive held-out $R^2$, and the $d_{\perp}$ directions were mutually same-signed across organisms.  In finite-row terms, the two-axis span is therefore insufficient as a complete residual description of the powered organism panel.

This does not overturn the $f_3$ hypothesis; it narrows it.  The companion residual-axis run identified the dominant non-tRNA residual direction as \texttt{f3\_translation\_selection}, again over $11$ computed organisms, $16$ requested organisms, five folds, and $41$ synonymous-design columns.  The third-axis identity run then reported $d_{\perp}$ as provisionally aligned to usage frequency within synonymous families, with mean cosine $0.128$ and one-sided sign-test $p=0.000488$.  The corresponding mechanism run retained significant cross-species alignment and held-out $R^2$ after GC-orthogonalization, reporting the summary \texttt{selection-not-GC; not-wobble-clustered; CSC/positional identity needs\_external}.  These rows say that a third residual direction survives the powered design, but they do not by themselves assign that direction to ribosome occupancy, translation efficiency, protein abundance, or molecular function.

\paragraph{Wobble-breaking condition.}
The biological trigger is the local asymmetry $w_c \ne w_{c^{f_3}}$ for the codon pair under the third-position involution.  If the context tRNA or wobble weights are symmetric, the $f_3$ coordinate remains a codon-topological contrast without a directed translation-layer interpretation.  If the weights are asymmetric, the coordinate becomes eligible for a bounded translation-readout test.  The boundary-set tRNA-poverty run supplies finite support for such asymmetry at the organism level: with $40$ organisms computed out of $46$ requested and $1000$ permutations at $\alpha=0.05$, the R sense codons were reported as systematically tRNA-poor under GtRNAdb tGCN together with dos Reis wobble weights.  This supports a tRNA-supply route for R avoidance, but it still remains a supply-side mechanism candidate rather than a measured $S_{f3\text{-}stress,T_j}$ row.

\paragraph{Spectrum placement.}
Across the powered spectrum, all captured per-organism dominant counts were assigned to $d_{resid4}$, with $11$ dominant instances, while the descriptive average ordering placed $d_{resid4}$ ahead of $d_{tRNA}$.  The domain counts were imbalanced, with $1$ archaeal, $2$ bacterial, and $8$ eukaryotic organisms, and the largest GC3 rank effect was small, $\rho=0.136$ for $d_{usage}$.  The three-axis sufficiency run did not close the space; it concluded \texttt{fourth\_mechanism\_present} on the same finite panel shape of five folds, $11$ computed organisms, $16$ requested organisms, at least $500$ proteins per organism, and $41$ synonymous-design columns.  The fourth-axis probes then restricted several tempting explanations: mRNA stability was excluded as the dominant fourth-axis identity in the yeast half-life assay, codon-pair identity returned \texttt{not\_codon\_pair}, translation accuracy returned \texttt{not\_accuracy}, and elongation dwell was only \texttt{partial}.  The $f_3$-stress record should therefore be read as one bounded component inside a larger residual spectrum, not as a complete law for codon choice.

\paragraph{Local survival statement.}
For each organism $o$, each CDS row $x$, and each admissible translation readout $T_j$, the intended finite statistic is a controlled survival entry
$$
\begin{aligned}
S_{f3\text{-}stress,T_j} &= \Delta \mathcal{L}\bigl(T_j(x,o) \mid Z(x,o)\bigr)
 - \Delta \mathcal{L}\bigl(T_j(x,o) \mid Z(x,o), q_{f3}(x,o)\bigr),\\
Z &= \{\text{amino-acid composition},\text{ organism},GC3,\text{ length},M\text{-density},\text{ mRNA control}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
Equivalently, a partial-$R^2$ version may be used if the same controls and simpler-codon-baseline ablation are enforced.  The support condition is strict: at least one controlled $S_{f3\text{-}stress,T_j}$ entry must be positive in a non-rank-degenerate powered panel.  A three-organism all-$1.0$ surface is not survival evidence; it is a saturated-rank boundary.  The break condition is equally strict: if controlled translation rows remain non-positive or rank-degenerate across a powered panel, the conjecture is falsified at the translation-readout interface.

\paragraph{No-promotion rule.}
The finite rows justify a constrained conjecture: after amino-acid quotienting, the $f_3$ residual coordinate is expected to survive as a translation-readout distinction only where tRNA or wobble weights break third-position symmetry.  They do not justify any stronger biological promotion.  Codon counts, residual coordinates, modelled tAI or stAI weights, and two-axis or three-axis geometry do not by themselves establish translation realization, ribosome dynamics, protein abundance, folding, physical admissibility, localization, stability, function, phenotype, mechanism, or universality.  Each such layer requires its own matched contact and its own controlled statistic.  In this chapter the only permitted positive claim is the finite, bounded eligibility of $q_{f3}$ for a translation-readout survival test under the declared controls.
