\paragraph{Finite coordinate surface.}
This record concerns the finite coding-sequence residual surface obtained after quotienting synonymous codon counts by amino-acid composition and then reading the remaining $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ directions against organism-matched translation-layer rows.  The coordinate singled out here is the $f_3$-stress direction: it compares synonymous codon imbalance across third-position $f_3$ pairing after amino-acid projection, and it is only biologically testable where context tRNA or wobble weights distinguish a codon $c$ from its $f_3$ partner $c^{f_3}$.  The finite saturation row fixes the local codon support.  In the recorded run $76d3dc408a57$, the set $M=\mathrm{Med}(R)\cup\mathrm{ACA}$ is
$$
\begin{aligned}
M=\{&\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{ACA},\mathrm{ACG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\\
&\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The saturated $f_3$ image of $R$ removes precisely the two codons $\mathrm{ACA}$ and $\mathrm{ACG}$ from this list:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\operatorname{Sat}_{f_3}(R)=\{&\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\\
&\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\},
\end{aligned}
$$
with empty recorded $f_3$ boundary set.  Thus the $f_3$ coordinate is not introduced as an unbounded biological assertion; it is a finite residual direction whose possible contact with biology must pass through the translation rows named below.

\paragraph{Rank-powered two-axis result.}
The powered two-axis experiment $99dc7e2d5abf$ tested whether the span generated by the core tRNA-adaptation direction and the $f_3$-stress residual direction exhausts the held-out optimal surface.  The run used $5$ folds, required at least $500$ proteins per organism, computed $11$ organisms out of $16$ requested, and used $41$ synonymous design columns.  Its recorded status is passed, but its conclusion is not that two axes close the surface.  The finite conclusion is $\mathrm{third\_axis\_present}$: after the two-axis fit, the residual direction $d_{\perp}$ retained systematically positive held-out $R^2$, and the $d_{\perp}$ directions were mutually same-signed across organisms.  Consequently the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ $f_3$-stress row is best read as a necessary coordinate in a larger controlled surface, not as a complete translation law by itself.

\paragraph{Relation to the residual spectrum.}
The linked residual-axis run $2a526c9fb7f4$ used the same finite scale, namely $5$ folds, $11$ computed organisms out of $16$ requested, a $500$ protein minimum, and $41$ synonymous design columns.  It identified the non-tRNA residual optimality direction with $f_3\_\mathrm{translation\_selection}$.  The third-axis identity run $377671f96e96$ then gave a separate finite readout: $d_{\perp}$ aligned provisionally with usage frequency within synonymous family, with mean cosine $0.128$ and one-sided sign-test $p=0.000488$, again on $5$ folds, $11$ computed organisms out of $16$, and $41$ synonymous design columns.  The mechanism-discrimination row $d0893337f0dd$ reported the same finite scale and classified the third-axis behavior as selection rather than GC, not wobble-clustered, with CSC or positional identity still requiring additional contact.  A broader axis-strength row, $0146012d8909$, reported that captured spectra were led on average by $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$ and then by $d_{\mathrm{tRNA}}$; all $11$ per-organism dominant counts were $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$, while domain means were only descriptive because the panel was imbalanced with $1$ archaeon, $2$ bacteria, and $8$ eukaryotes.  The largest recorded GC3 rank effect in that row was $\rho=0.136$ for $d_{\mathrm{usage}}$.

\paragraph{Translation contact requirement.}
The finite rows above justify a bounded mathematical and statistical target: compute $S_{f3\text{-}stress,T_j}$ for translation readouts $T_j$ such as tAI, stAI, ribosome occupancy, or translation efficiency, after controlling amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, coding-sequence length, $M$-density, matched mRNA abundance when TE is used, rank sufficiency, and simpler codon baselines.  A positive report requires a non-vacuous same-organism and same-CDS matrix joining codon counts, wobble-breaking tRNA or adaptation weights, measured or explicitly bounded translation readout rows, and the declared controls.  The present data do not contain such a joined measured translation matrix for this exact $f_3$-stress packet.  They contain powered residual-geometry evidence and modeled or bounded contact routes, not an observed ribosome or TE survival row.

\paragraph{Local non-promotion rule.}
The record therefore has a sharp interpretation boundary.  It may say that the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual surface contains a robust non-tRNA component, that the two-axis tRNA plus $f_3$ span is not closed on the powered panel, and that the remaining $d_{\perp}$ direction has finite positive held-out behavior and a usage-frequency alignment with $p=0.000488$.  It may also report that the $R$-sense tRNA-poverty row $d7728cc8768e$ passed on $40$ computed organisms out of $46$ requested, with $6$ skipped organisms and $1000$ permutations, supporting a bounded tRNA-supply reading for $R$ avoidance.  It may not infer translation realization, ribosome occupancy, protein abundance, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, organismal phenotype, mechanism, or biological universality from these rows alone.  Each of those stronger readings requires its own layer-matched contact and its own controlled statistic.  In particular, the three-organism all-$1.0$ controlled surface is a rank-degenerate null boundary, not survival evidence; and modeled tAI or stAI weights remain bounded adaptation readbacks unless a measured ribosome or translation-efficiency row is joined and survives the stated controls.
