\subsection{The $f_3$-stress translation-readout boundary for $B^*_{Q6}$}\label{sec:bioreality-q6-f3-stress-translation-readout}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Coordinate surface.}
The object considered here is the finite CDS-by-organism residual coordinate surface restricted to the $B^*_{Q6}$ third-position stress coordinate and to matched translation-layer rows.  The coordinate is not a protein-level claim.  It is the synonymous residual left after amino-acid projection, with special attention to codon pairs related by the third-position involution $f_3$.  Its biological test is therefore narrow: after the usual sequence controls, does the $f_3$-paired synonymous imbalance leave a non-vacuous entry in a translation readout such as tAI, stAI, ribosome occupancy, or translation efficiency?

\paragraph{Finite codon boundary.}
The finite saturation row fixes the codon surface before any translation interpretation is attempted.  The recorded set
$$
\begin{aligned}
M &= \{AAA,AAG,ACA,ACG,AGA,AGG,AUA,AUG,CUA,CUC,CUG,CUU,\\
&\qquad UAA,UAG,UCA,UCG,UGA,UGG,UUA,UUG\},
\end{aligned}
$$
while the $f_3$-saturated row after removing $ACA$ and $ACG$ is
$$
\begin{aligned}
\operatorname{Sat}_{f_3}(R) &= \{AAA,AAG,AGA,AGG,AUA,AUG,CUA,CUC,CUG,CUU,\\
&\qquad UAA,UAG,UCA,UCG,UGA,UGG,UUA,UUG\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The recorded $f_3$ boundary is empty.  This matters because the chapter is not asking whether a missing codon class can be rescued by prose.  The finite surface is already closed at the codon-list level; the open question is whether a controlled translation row distinguishes the $f_3$ residual after quotienting by amino acid.

\paragraph{Powered residual result.}
The powered two-axis experiment used $5$ folds, required at least $500$ proteins per organism, computed $11$ organisms out of $16$ requested, and used $41$ synonymous design columns.  Its recorded status is passed, with conclusion \emph{third\_axis\_present}.  The finite reason is that $d_\perp$ has systematically positive held-out $R^2$ and the $d_\perp$ directions are mutually same-signed across organisms.  In the adjacent residual-axis row, the non-tRNA residual optimum is recorded as aligning most with \emph{f3\_translation\_selection}.  Thus the two-axis span consisting of tRNA adaptation and the $f_3$-stress residual is not sufficient as a closed explanatory basis on the powered panel; a third direction remains visible.

\paragraph{Interpretation of the third direction.}
The finite identity row for $d_\perp$ used the same $5$-fold, $500$-protein, $11$-of-$16$ organism, $41$-column design and identified the direction provisionally with usage frequency within synonymous family.  The reported mean cosine is $0.128$, and the one-sided sign-test value is $p=0.000488$.  A separate mechanism row records that the direction remains GC-orthogonalized and is classified as selection-not-GC, not wobble-clustered, with CSC or positional identity still requiring an added contact.  The axis-strength row likewise computed $11$ organisms, and its descriptive dominant-count summary is $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}:11$, with domain counts imbalanced as archaea $1$, bacteria $2$, and eukaryotes $8$; the largest reported GC3 rank effect is $d_{\mathrm{usage}}$ with $\rho=0.136$.  These numbers support a finite residual spectrum, not a universal biological rule.

\paragraph{Translation contact requirement.}
The intended survival entry is a controlled statistic $S_{f3\text{-}stress,T_j}$, where $T_j$ is a bounded translation-layer readout.  The support condition is local and explicit: at least one matched row must satisfy
$$
\begin{aligned}
S_{f3\text{-}stress,T_j} &> 0
\end{aligned}
$$
after amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, CDS length, M-density baseline, matched mRNA abundance, rank, and simpler codon-baseline controls.  The row is falsified, for this chapter's purpose, if the controlled statistic remains non-positive or rank-degenerate across the powered panel.  In particular, a three-organism all-$1.0$ fitted matrix is not counted as survival; it is a saturated boundary condition.

\paragraph{Mechanistic pressure from tRNA asymmetry.}
The tRNA-poverty experiment supplies a mechanism pressure but not the translation survival row itself.  It computed $40$ organisms out of $46$ requested, skipped $6$, used $1000$ permutations at $\alpha=0.05$, and passed with the call that $R$ sense codons are systematically tRNA-poor under GtRNAdb tGCN plus dos Reis wobble weights.  That result makes the $f_3$ test biologically pointed: the $f_3$ coordinate should become dynamically visible only where context tRNA or wobble weights break the symmetry between a codon and its $f_3$ mate.  Without such asymmetry, topology and modeled adaptation weights alone do not provide a translation-readout result.

\paragraph{Local no-promotion rule.}
The present record permits only a bounded statement about a residual codon coordinate and its required translation-layer test.  Codon usage tables, tRNA-copy summaries, tAI or stAI weights, ribosome-profiling labels, and mRNA controls are separate data contacts with different resolutions.  None of them, by name alone, establishes translation realization, protein abundance, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, localization, stability, phenotype, mechanism, or universality.  A downstream claim is admissible only after the same coordinate first obtains a non-vacuous positive translation-readout survival entry under the stated controls, and after the downstream layer supplies its own matched contact and statistic.
