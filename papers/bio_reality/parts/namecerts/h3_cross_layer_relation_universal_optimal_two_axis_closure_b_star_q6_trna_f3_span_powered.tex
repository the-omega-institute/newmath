\subsection{A Wobble-Broken Translation Readout Boundary for the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ $f_3$ Coordinate}
\label{sec:bior-wobble-broken-f3-translation-readout-boundary}

\paragraph{Finite carrier.}
The object considered here is the finite coding-sequence-by-organism residual surface obtained after amino-acid quotienting, restricted to the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ third-position stress coordinate and paired only with translation-layer rows. The coordinate is not treated as a protein, phenotype, or molecular function. It is a finite synonymous residual direction whose biological contact is allowed only through an explicitly joined readout row: codon counts, organism identity, tRNA or wobble weights, and a translation-efficiency or ribosome-occupancy response must all be present on the same controlled panel before the coordinate is allowed to count as a surviving translation distinction.

The underlying $f_3$ saturation check fixes the combinatorial boundary. The finite set
$$
\begin{aligned}
M={}&\{AAA,AAG,ACA,ACG,AGA,AGG,AUA,AUG,CUA,CUC,CUG,CUU,\\
&\quad UAA,UAG,UCA,UCG,UGA,UGG,UUA,UUG\}
\end{aligned}
$$
contains twenty codons in the recorded $M=\mathrm{Med}\,R\cup ACA$ surface. The $f_3$ saturation removes exactly $ACA$ and $ACG$, leaving
$$
\begin{aligned}
\operatorname{Sat}_{f_3}(R)={}&\{AAA,AAG,AGA,AGG,AUA,AUG,CUA,CUC,CUG,CUU,\\
&\quad UAA,UAG,UCA,UCG,UGA,UGG,UUA,UUG\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The recorded $f_3$ boundary set is empty. This matters biologically because the coordinate is already closed as a finite codon-surface distinction; what remains open is not whether the coordinate exists, but whether an organism-matched translation row can detect it after controls.

\paragraph{Two-axis test result.}
The powered two-axis closure assay reports status $\mathrm{passed}$ for the claim identifier associated with the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ tRNA--$f_3$ span. The computation used $5$ folds, required at least $500$ proteins per organism, computed $11$ organisms out of $16$ requested organisms, and used $41$ synonymous design columns. Its recorded conclusion is $\mathrm{third\_axis\_present}$, with the reason that $d_{\perp}$ has systematically positive held-out $R^2$ and that the $d_{\perp}$ directions are mutually same-signed across organisms. Thus the finite-row result does not say that tRNA adaptation and the $f_3$ direction exhaust the residual translation-facing surface; it says that a third direction remains after those two axes are supplied.

This is the local algebraic reading:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\operatorname{span}(d_{\mathrm{tRNA}},d_{f_3})\subsetneq
\operatorname{span}(d_{\mathrm{tRNA}},d_{f_3},d_{\perp})
\end{aligned}
$$
within the finite synonymous design surface measured by held-out prediction. The strictness is not asserted as a universal biological theorem. It is the finite-row outcome of the stated multi-organism assay.

\paragraph{Relation to the residual spectrum.}
The neighboring powered assays give the chapter its local interpretation. The universal residual-axis assay, again on $11$ computed organisms out of $16$ requested organisms, with $5$ folds, $500$ minimum proteins per organism, and $41$ synonymous design columns, records that the non-tRNA residual optimality aligns most with $f_3\_translation\_selection$. The axis-strength spectrum assay records that captured spectra are led on average by $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$ and then by $d_{\mathrm{tRNA}}$, with per-organism dominant counts $\{d_{\mathrm{resid4}}:11\}$. Its domain means are explicitly descriptive under the imbalanced domain sample $\{\mathrm{archaea}:1,\mathrm{bacteria}:2,\mathrm{eukaryote}:8\}$, and the largest GC3 rank effect is only $d_{\mathrm{usage}}$ with $\rho=0.136$.

The third-axis identity assay identifies $d_{\perp}$ provisionally with usage frequency within synonymous families: the reported mean cosine is $0.128$ and the one-sided sign-test value is $p=0.000488$. The corresponding third-axis mechanism assay records $\mathrm{selection\mbox{-}not\mbox{-}GC}$, $\mathrm{not\mbox{-}wobble\mbox{-}clustered}$, and a CSC or positional identity requiring further contact; its stated reason is that GC-orthogonalized $d_{\perp}$ keeps significant cross-species alignment and held-out $R^2$. These facts keep the present chapter from collapsing $d_{\perp}$ into GC3, simple wobble clustering, or the two-axis tRNA--$f_3$ span.

\paragraph{Wobble-breaking condition.}
The intended translation-row test is conditional. The $f_3$ coordinate becomes a candidate dynamical readout only in contexts where the codon-side symmetry is broken by context tRNA or wobble weights. In finite terms, the relevant comparison is between a codon $c$ and its third-position $f_3$ partner $c^{f_3}$. A translation readout is admissible for this chapter only when the contact records an asymmetry of the form
$$
\begin{aligned}
w_c\neq w_{c^{f_3}}
\end{aligned}
$$
and joins that asymmetry to the same organism or same coding sequence as the residual coordinate and translation response. A modeled tAI or stAI weight can supply a bounded codon-adaptation row; it is not by itself a measured ribosome-occupancy row. A ribosome-profiling or translation-efficiency contact can supply a measured translation row only after it is joined with codon counts and matched mRNA abundance so that translation efficiency is separated from transcript abundance.

The boundary-set tRNA-poverty assay gives a compatible finite clue but not the final survival row. It computed $40$ organisms out of $46$ requested organisms, skipped $6$, used $1000$ permutations at $\alpha=0.05$, and records that $R$ sense codons are systematically tRNA-poor under GtRNAdb tRNA gene copy number together with dos Reis wobble weights. This supports a tRNA-supply interpretation for $R$ avoidance, but it does not replace the required same-row survival statistic for the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ $f_3$ coordinate.

\paragraph{Survival statistic.}
The bounded readout for this chapter is a controlled entry $S_{f_3\text{-}\mathrm{stress},T_j}$, where $T_j$ is a translation-layer response such as tAI, stAI, ribosome occupancy, or translation efficiency. The statistic is positive only when the $f_3$ residual coordinate improves held-out description or partial prediction of the translation row after amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, coding-sequence length, $M$-density, matched mRNA abundance when applicable, rank sufficiency, and simpler codon baselines have been imposed. Symbolically, the chapter asks for a finite joined panel on which
$$
\begin{aligned}
S_{f_3\text{-}\mathrm{stress},T_j}>0
\end{aligned}
$$
for at least one admissible translation row $T_j$. If every controlled entry is non-positive, or if the fitted surface is rank-degenerate, then the chapter records a null translation boundary rather than a positive biological readout.

The rank warning is essential. A three-organism all-$1.0$ controlled matrix is not counted as survival here. Such a matrix is a saturation boundary: it reports that the design lacks the rank needed to distinguish signal from fit geometry. The powered two-axis result therefore does not authorize promotion from coordinate topology to translation realization. It only says that, in the powered finite design already run, the tRNA--$f_3$ span is insufficient because a same-signed $d_{\perp}$ direction remains.

\paragraph{Higher-layer exclusions.}
No protein-abundance, protein-structure, localization, stability, molecular-function, phenotype, physical-admissibility, mechanistic, or universal-law conclusion is licensed by this chapter. The fourth-mechanism assays illustrate the same caution. The optimality-axis sufficiency assay reports $\mathrm{fourth\_mechanism\_present}$ on $11$ computed organisms out of $16$ requested organisms, with $5$ folds, $500$ minimum proteins per organism, and $41$ synonymous design columns. Candidate exclusions are specific rather than global: the translation-accuracy assay reports $\mathrm{not\_accuracy}$ using $200$ permutations on the same $11$ of $16$ organism scale; the codon-pair assay reports $\mathrm{not\_codon\_pair}$ using a shuffle null of $50$; the mRNA-stability assay reports $\mathrm{fourth\_axis\_not\_mrna\_stability}$ because yeast $d_{\mathrm{opt\_halflife}}$ has low alignment with abundance $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$ under the fixed gates; and the elongation-dwell assay records only $\mathrm{partial}$ for Saccharomyces cerevisiae. These rows sharpen the boundary rather than relaxing it.

Consequently, the present claim is a finite translation-readout conjecture with a strict gate. It may report the existence of a powered residual direction outside the tRNA--$f_3$ span, and it may nominate wobble-broken contexts as the proper place to test the $f_3$ stress coordinate. It may not claim translation survival until a same-row $S_{f_3\text{-}\mathrm{stress},T_j}$ entry is positive under controls. It may not claim folding, physical admissibility, function, downstream mechanism, phenotype, localization, stability, protein abundance, or universality unless a separate layer-matched contact supplies that layer's own finite statistic.
