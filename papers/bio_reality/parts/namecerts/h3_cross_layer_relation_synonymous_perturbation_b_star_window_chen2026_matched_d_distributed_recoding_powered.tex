\subsection{Matched distributed recoding boundary for the Chen panel}\label{sec:biorality-chen-distributed-recoding-boundary}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite surface.}
The Chen distributed-recoding panel is read here as a finite cross-layer test on two matched plasmid classes rather than as a general statement about synonymous design.  The observed rows are $47$ AmpR variants and $53$ gmr variants, stratified into $19$ multi-row $D$ strata.  The coordinate surface used for the audit is the nine-dimensional $B^*_Q6$ window basis with the E. coli CAI baseline and spatial windows named by the run as $B\_star\_window\_Q6\_9D\_Ecoli\_CAI\_spatial\_windows\_full\_nt60\_531\_and\_middle3\_drop\_first\_60\_codons$.  The CAI weights were read from the local E. coli genome-weight file, and the dataset was not rebuilt during the recorded run.  The finite test therefore has the row count
$$
\begin{aligned}
 n_{\mathrm{AmpR}} &= 47,\\
 n_{\mathrm{gmr}} &= 53,\\
 n_D &= 19,
\end{aligned}
$$
with a recorded runtime of $465.144$ seconds for experiment run $36d651ac8538$.

\paragraph{Tested relation.}
The relation tested by this panel is not the unconstrained association $Q_i \to P_k$.  The admissible object is a controlled comparison in which a $B^*_Q6$ window residual is retained only if it survives the declared coding-window and CAI controls, and any downstream protein or resistance interpretation remains conditional on the existence of a separate translation readout.  In the finite Chen audit, the AmpR side produced
$$
\begin{aligned}
 \widehat\beta_{\mathrm{AmpR}} &= 0.0,\\
 p_{\mathrm{AmpR,independent}} &= 1.0.
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the fitted AmpR component gives no independent positive displacement on the registered surface.  The output is a boundary result: the observed finite rows do not support promotion from a $B^*_Q6$ coding-window residual to an independently detected AmpR protein-output effect in this panel.

\paragraph{Matched-distribution reading.}
The role of the $D$ stratification is to keep the recoding comparison inside matched finite slices, not to create a new biological layer.  With $19$ strata and only $47$ AmpR rows, a zero fitted slope and a right-tail probability of $1.0$ are decisive for the registered AmpR direction: the tested AmpR component is null on this surface.  The $53$ gmr rows remain part of the matched panel, but the recorded scalar result supplied for this run is the AmpR independent slope and its associated probability.  No finite value in the row record supplies a positive $\lambda$ coefficient, a positive mediated $R$ list, or a module-level class assignment for this Chen packet.  Consequently the chapter records a controlled non-promotion rather than a positive recoding law.

\paragraph{Layer discipline.}
The same project memory contains a broader modeled translation-to-protein mediation audit in which $12$ organisms had per-protein modeled tAI and PAXdb abundance rows, including $3{,}739$ joined E. coli proteins, $5{,}815$ yeast proteins, and $19{,}053$ human proteins.  That broader audit reports positive statistical mediation fractions in several organisms, for example $M^{QTP}=0.612922252793667$ in E. coli K12 MG1655 and $M^{QTP}=0.9491551041216973$ in yeast, but those numbers are not a substitute for a measured translation step inside the Chen distributed-recoding panel.  They only show that the surrounding coordinate family has a separately bounded translation-mediated abundance surface under modeled tAI and matched protein-abundance rows.  The Chen panel itself supplies the stricter fact: on its registered AmpR test, the independent slope is zero and the probability is $1.0$.

\paragraph{Translation-contact boundary.}
The translation-survival memory also records a modeled tAI contact with $3$ organisms, $61$ sense codons, $9$ computed $B^*_Q6$ residual coordinates, and $81$ entries above the coupling threshold $0.01$.  Its maximum absolute modeled coupling was $3{,}801{,}961.031607896$, but that contact explicitly remains modeled-tAI bounded coupling rather than ribosome profiling or proteomics validation.  Therefore the Chen panel cannot inherit measured ribosome occupancy from the modeled contact.  A direct $Q_i \to P_k$ or $Q_i \to$ resistance statement is not admissible unless the same finite row surface also supplies a surviving $Q_i \to T_j$ translation-readout step under amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, CDS length, M-density, CAI or simpler-codon baselines, and matched mRNA controls.

\paragraph{Verdict.}
The finite verdict for the Chen distributed-recoding packet is a controlled null at the claimed AmpR independent slot.  The test ran to completion on $47$ AmpR rows, $53$ gmr rows, and $19$ matched $D$ strata; it returned $\widehat\beta_{\mathrm{AmpR}}=0.0$ and $p=1.0$.  This is enough to write the BioReality entry as a bounded negative result: the panel preserves the $B^*_Q6$ window coordinate as a codon-residual audit object, but it does not establish translation realization, protein abundance mediation, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, mechanism, selection, phenotype, or universality.  Any such higher-layer claim would require its own matched contact and its own finite statistic on the same or an explicitly joined row surface.
