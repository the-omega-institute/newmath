\paragraph{Finite surface.}
The Chen matched distributed-recoding record tests a narrow perturbation question: whether a $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ window statistic, computed on an explicitly bounded synonymous-recoding surface, carries independent readout power once the design is stratified by the matched recoding class.  The finite carrier has two assayed recoding arms, $53$ Gmr rows and $47$ AmpR rows, together with $19$ matched $D$ strata.  The window basis used in the run is the recorded nine-coordinate surface
$\texttt{B\_star\_window\_Q6\_9D\_Ecoli\_CAI\_spatial\_windows\_full\_nt60\_531\_and\_middle3\_drop\_first\_60\_codons}$,
with the first $60$ codons omitted and the remaining coding region represented by spatial windows through nucleotide coordinate $531$ plus the middle-three-codon summary.  The codon-adaptation weights are drawn from the local Escherichia coli genome CAI table at
$\texttt{tools/bio\_reality/data/ecoli\_genome\_cai\_weights.json}$.
The dataset was not rebuilt in this run, so the calculation is a deterministic readback over the already materialized finite table rather than a new data-ingestion event.

\paragraph{Registered statistic.}
Let $Q$ denote the finite $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ window matrix, let $D$ denote the matched distributed-recoding stratum, and let $Y_{\mathrm{AmpR}}$ be the AmpR arm readout.  The audit asks whether the window residual contributes a nonzero AmpR-directed term after the matched $D$ structure has already absorbed the recoding design.  In the run, the independent AmpR slope and its null-calibrated probability were
$$
\begin{aligned}
 n_{\mathrm{Gmr}} &= 53,\\
 n_{\mathrm{AmpR}} &= 47,\\
 n_D &= 19,\\
 \widehat{\beta}_{\mathrm{AmpR}\mid D,Q} &= 0.0,\\
 p_{\mathrm{AmpR,independent}} &= 1.0.
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the finite row calculation supplies a clean negative result for the independent AmpR increment: after the matched distributed-recoding strata are respected, the tested $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ window coordinate contributes no detected AmpR slope in this surface.  The elapsed runtime was $465.144$ seconds, which is consistent with a completed finite-row audit rather than an unexecuted target.

\paragraph{Readout interpretation.}
The row result is best read as a matched perturbation boundary, not as a protein-realization success.  A direct $Q_i \to P_k$ interpretation is not admissible on this record because the recorded AmpR statistic does not pass through a surviving translation readout $T_j$.  In the broader $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ translation-readout memory, the modeled-tAI surface has nine residual coordinates,
$$
\begin{aligned}
Q = \{&\mathrm{Arg\_AGR},\ \mathrm{Ile\_AUA},\ \mathrm{K\_AAA},\ \mathrm{Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR},\\
&\mathrm{Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG},\ \mathrm{Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG},\ \mathrm{Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY},\\
&\mathrm{Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY},\ f3\_\mathrm{stress}\},
\end{aligned}
$$
and a modeled tAI contact over $3$ organisms reports $81$ entries above the bounded-contact threshold $0.01$, with maximum absolute entry $3801961.031607896$.  That contact is explicitly modeled from tRNA gene-copy counts and dos Reis wobble weights; it is not a ribosome-profiling or matched-proteomics measurement.  The $3$-organism surface is also below the powered-survival requirement of at least $40$ matched species.  Consequently, the Chen distributed-recoding row cannot be promoted by borrowing modeled-tAI coupling as though it were measured translation survival.

\paragraph{Relation to protein-abundance mediation memory.}

They do not rescue the Chen AmpR window test, because the present run reports $\widehat{\beta}_{\mathrm{AmpR}\mid D,Q}=0.0$ and $p=1.0$ on its own matched perturbation rows.

\paragraph{Local verdict.}
The finite-row conclusion is therefore bounded and negative.  The Chen matched distributed-recoding packet supports the statement that, on this registered $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ window surface with $53$ Gmr rows, $47$ AmpR rows, and $19$ matched $D$ strata, the independent AmpR term is absent.  It does not establish translation, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, mechanism, phenotype, selection, or universality.  Those claims would require separate layer-matched contacts: a direct translation readout for $Q_i \to T_j$, matched protein abundance for $Q_i \to T_j \to P_k$, and further structural, functional, physical, or phenotype assays for any higher-layer statement.  With the current finite table, the safe readback is a controlled null for the AmpR-directed distributed-recoding window, not a positive biological mechanism.
