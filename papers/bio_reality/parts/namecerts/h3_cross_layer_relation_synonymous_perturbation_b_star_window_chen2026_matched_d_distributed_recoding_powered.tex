\subsection{Matched distributed recoding at the protein-output boundary}\label{sec:bioreality-chen2026-matched-d-distributed-recoding}\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite screen.}
This record evaluates a matched distributed synonymous-recoding surface in which the coordinate is not a free codon-count direction but the registered $B^\ast$ window residual basis for an \emph{E. coli} coding-window assay.  The finite readout supplied by the registered run is
$$
\begin{aligned}
B_{\mathrm{actual}} &= \texttt{B\_star\_window\_Q6\_9D\_Ecoli\_CAI\_spatial\_windows\_full\_nt60\_531\_and\_middle3\_drop\_first\_60\_codons},\\
N_{\mathrm{GmR}} &= 53,\\
N_{\mathrm{AmpR}} &= 47,\\
N_{D\text{-strata}} &= 19.
\end{aligned}
$$
The calculation used an organism-specific CAI weight table for \emph{E. coli}; the dataset was already present for this run rather than built anew.  The audit therefore tests a bounded perturbation question: whether the $B^\ast$ window residual, after its spatial and first-window exclusions, carries an independent AmpR response across the matched distributed $D$ strata.  The observed AmpR slope is exactly $0.0$, and the independent AmpR test reports
$$
\begin{aligned}
\widehat\beta_{\mathrm{AmpR}} &= 0.0,\\
p_{\mathrm{AmpR,independent}} &= 1.0,\\
T_{\mathrm{runtime}} &= 465.144\ \mathrm{s}.
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the finite perturbation surface is not a positive protein-output rescue or resistance result.  It is a controlled null at this particular AmpR slot: the tested residual basis and the matched distributed recoding design do not produce an independent AmpR increment on the recorded rows.

\paragraph{Relation to the translation-mediated surface.}
The wider $B^\ast_{Q6}$ protein-abundance mediation audit supplies a separate context for why this null should not be overread.  In that audit the residual basis has nine coordinates, namely $K\_AAA$, $Arg\_AGR$, $Ile\_AUA$, $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$, $Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG$, $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$, $Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG$, $Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY$, and $f3\_stress$.  Across organism-matched CDS and protein-abundance rows, modeled per-protein tAI produced positive statistical mediation fractions in several organisms, for example $M^{QTP}=0.612922252793667$ in \emph{E. coli} K12 MG1655, $0.9491551041216973$ in \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}, $0.4872268357227724$ in \emph{Caenorhabditis elegans}, and $0.27206289160119157$ in human.  Those values are statistical decompositions against modeled tAI and measured protein abundance, not synonymous-edit perturbation effects.  The present Chen-style distributed-recoding screen asks a narrower question: after selecting the $B^\ast$ window construction and matching the $D$ strata, does the AmpR slot move independently?  The recorded answer is no.

\paragraph{Comparison with other perturbation contacts.}
The negative AmpR result is not a blanket refusal of every synonymous perturbation screen.  Another \emph{E. coli} mRFP protein-output assay used $1459$ variants and reported $\Delta R^2=0.05339906007191191$, $\Delta DL=19.089327473282296$ bits, $p_{\mathrm{matched}}=0.004975124378109453$, and $p_{\mathrm{within}}=0.004975124378109453$ after real CAI robustness checks.  A separate GFP synonymous-protein escape screen used $3556$ variants, split as $1124$ region-1 and $2432$ region-2 variants, and recorded an out-of-fold mRNA-to-protein slope of $0.016523671594873352$.  These companion rows show that the BioReality ledger can admit positive protein-output contacts when the finite assay supplies them.  The Chen distributed-recoding row is different: it contributes a high-specificity null for the AmpR-independent component rather than a positive rescue, abundance, or resistance statement.

\paragraph{Boundary of interpretation.}
The local conclusion is therefore:
$$
\begin{aligned}
N_{\mathrm{GmR}} &= 53,\quad N_{\mathrm{AmpR}}=47,\quad N_{D\text{-strata}}=19,\\
\widehat\beta_{\mathrm{AmpR}} &= 0.0,\quad p_{\mathrm{AmpR,independent}}=1.0,\\
\Rightarrow\quad &\text{no supported independent AmpR effect for this }B^\ast\text{ window residual screen.}
\end{aligned}
$$
This says only that the registered finite rows fail to support the targeted AmpR-independent perturbation claim.  It does not imply that the $B^\ast_{Q6}$ residual basis is biologically irrelevant, nor does it supply a translation-realization result, a folding result, a physical-admissibility result, a molecular-function result, a mechanism, or a universal law.  A direct $Q_i\to P_k$ association remains inadmissible as a protein-realization statement unless the same coordinate first survives a controlled translation-readout step $Q_i\to T_j$, with matched mRNA abundance and the usual amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, CDS-length, and $M$-density controls.  For the present screen, the proper readback is a finite negative perturbation entry: the tested $B^\ast$ window construction, on $53$ GmR rows and $47$ AmpR rows across $19$ matched $D$ strata, has no independent AmpR signal.
