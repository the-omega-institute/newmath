\paragraph{Finite surface.}
The finite object in this chapter is the matched perturbation table indexed by a synonymous residual coordinate, a distributed-recoding stratum, and a measured output slot.  The coordinate side is the nine-dimensional B*\_Q6 window basis used for the Escherichia coli coding-sequence audit: $K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $Leu_{CUN\,vs\,UUR}$, $Leu_{UUA\,vs\,UUG}$, $Ser_{UCR\,vs\,AGY}$, $Ser_{UCA\,vs\,UCG}$, $Thr_{ACR\,vs\,ACY}$, and $f_3$-stress.  The registered perturbation readout is not an all-CDS codon count; it is the windowed surface named $B\_star\_window\_Q6\_9D\_Ecoli\_CAI\_spatial\_windows\_full\_nt60\_531\_and\_middle3\_drop\_first\_60\_codons$.  Thus the admissible row has the form $(q_i,d_a,p_k)$, where $q_i$ is one of the nine residual coordinates, $d_a$ is one of the matched distributed-recoding strata, and $p_k$ is the observed output class in the matched construct set.

\paragraph{Registered counts.}
The finite run used $47$ AmpR rows and $53$ GmR rows, with $19$ multi-row distributed-recoding strata.  The run was not a dataset-construction event: the payload was already present for the registered computation, and the coding-adaptation weights were read from the E. coli genome CAI weight table at the recorded local source.  The observed AmpR statistic was exactly null at the registered scalar level:
$$
\begin{aligned}
 n_{AmpR} &= 47,\\
 n_{GmR} &= 53,\\
 n_D &= 19,\\
 \widehat{\beta}_{AmpR} &= 0.0,\\
 p_{AmpR,independent} &= 1.0 .
\end{aligned}
$$
These numbers make the chapter a bounded negative readback for the AmpR component of the matched-D distributed recoding surface.  The finite table exists, the registered B*\_Q6 window geometry was evaluated, and the AmpR slope did not depart from zero under the recorded independent test.

\paragraph{Relation to the translation-mediated protein surface.}
The negative AmpR result is interpreted against the larger B*\_Q6 mediation audit, not as a denial that B*\_Q6 can ever touch protein abundance.  In the separate matched protein-abundance mediation run, the same nine residual coordinates were joined to per-protein modeled tAI and PAXdb abundance across twelve organisms.  That surface had organism-specific mediated fractions in the unit interval, including $0.612922252793667$ for E. coli K-12 MG1655, $0.9491551041216973$ for Saccharomyces cerevisiae, $0.27206289160119157$ for Homo sapiens, and $0.15488799907323988$ for Mus musculus.  For the E. coli K-12 MG1655 row in that audit, the total residual-to-protein abundance $R^2$ was $0.10767526677985896$, the direct residual-to-protein abundance $R^2$ after modeled tAI conditioning was $0.04167869969498872$, and the indirect difference was $0.06599656708487024$:
$$
\begin{aligned}
 R^2_{QP,Ecoli} &= 0.10767526677985896,\\
 R^2_{QP\mid T,Ecoli} &= 0.04167869969498872,\\
 R^2_{indirect,Ecoli} &= 0.06599656708487024,\\
 M^{QTP}_{Ecoli} &= 0.612922252793667 .
\end{aligned}
$$
That comparison matters because the present Chen matched-D run is not allowed to inherit the positive mediation scalar from the cross-organism table.  It must stand on its own finite rows.  On those rows, the AmpR component gives $p=1.0$ and slope $0.0$.

\paragraph{Perturbation contrast.}
A second E. coli synonymous perturbation surface supplies a useful calibration of what a positive protein-output perturbation signal looked like under the same family of B*\_Q6 window audits.  In the Nieuwkoop E. coli mRFP protein-output run, $1459$ variants were evaluated; the controls-only model had $R^2=0.12648386762689323$, the controls-plus-B-perpendicular model had $R^2=0.1798829276988051$, the increment was $\Delta R^2=0.05339906007191191$, and both matched and within-set tests had $p=0.004975124378109453$.  The Chen matched-D AmpR surface does not show that pattern.  Its registered AmpR statistic is the opposite limiting case: zero slope with an independent-test probability of one.

\paragraph{Verdict.}
The chapter therefore records a narrow, finite-row conclusion.  The B*\_Q6 window coordinate system and the matched-D distributed-recoding table were sufficient to run the registered AmpR check, but the AmpR component does not support a positive B*\_Q6 protein-output perturbation statement.  The admissible reading is:
$$
\begin{aligned}
 \text{Chen matched-D AmpR support} &= \text{not positive},\\
 \text{registered slope} &= 0.0,\\
 \text{registered independent }p &= 1.0 .
\end{aligned}
$$
The GmR row count and distributed strata remain part of the finite surface, but the reported scalar values do not by themselves supply a positive GmR effect size, a mediated translation step, or a downstream protein-abundance mediation claim.  The result keeps this specific Chen distributed-recoding packet at a bounded perturbation-audit status rather than promoting it to protein realization.

\paragraph{Local boundary.}
No translation mechanism follows from the window coordinate alone.  No folded structure, physical admissibility, molecular function, organismal phenotype, selection law, or universal biological rule follows from the AmpR null statistic.  A direct association from $q_i$ to a protein-output slot is not enough for a translation-mediated claim unless the same finite rows also contain a surviving $q_i\to T_j$ translation readout and the downstream $q_i\to T_j\to P_k$ relation is evaluated after amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, coding-sequence length, M-density baseline, and matched mRNA abundance are controlled.  The present chapter has a precise finite outcome, but it is a boundary outcome: the Chen matched-D AmpR surface is computed and non-positive, while any translation, folding, physical-admissibility, function, mechanism, or universality statement requires an additional layer-matched measurement.
