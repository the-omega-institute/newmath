\paragraph{Finite surface.}
This record concerns a finite perturbation surface in which $B^\ast_{Q6}$ residual coordinates are read on synonymous recoding windows and then tested against a matched-$D$ distributed design.  The concrete run used the nine-dimensional window carrier named \texttt{B\_star\_window\_Q6\_9D\_Ecoli\_CAI\_spatial\_windows\_full\_nt60\_531\_and\_middle3\_drop\_first\_60\_codons}.  The carrier is not a free protein claim: it is a codon-window residual surface with an \emph{E. coli} CAI background, spatial windows over nucleotide positions $60$ through $531$, and a middle-three-window audit after dropping the first $60$ codons.  The CAI weights were taken from \texttt{tools/bio\_reality/data/ecoli\_genome\_cai\_weights.json}.  The finite rows available to the run were $47$ ampicillin-resistance rows and $53$ gentamicin-resistance rows, with $19$ multi-row $D$ strata.

The local question is whether a $B^\ast_{Q6}$ synonymous-window coordinate supplies an independent distributed-recoding signal after the matched-$D$ organization and CAI window construction have already been imposed.  In the notation of the record, the tested surface is bounded by
$$
\begin{aligned}
  n_{\mathrm{AmpR}} &= 47, \\
  n_{\mathrm{GmR}} &= 53, \\
  n_{D\text{-strata}} &= 19, \\

These finite numbers make the record a completed bounded readout, not an open promise for later row acquisition.

\paragraph{Verdict on the Chen 2026 panel.}
On this surface the matched-$D$ distributed recoding claim is not promoted to an independent positive ampicillin-resistance effect.  The decisive local pair is
$$
\begin{aligned}
  \widehat{\beta}_{\mathrm{AmpR}} &= 0.0, \\
  p_{\mathrm{AmpR,independent}} &= 1.0.
\end{aligned}

Thus the observed finite table supports a null boundary for this particular perturbation readout: the $B^\ast_{Q6}$ window construction can be carried through the matched design, but this run does not show an independent AmpR gain attributable to the residual-window coordinates.  The GmR rows are part of the same finite panel, with $n_{\mathrm{GmR}}=53$, but the scalar output supplied here does not give a positive GmR slope, likelihood gain, or permutation tail that would license a separate positive GmR paragraph.

The finite result is deliberately narrow.  From $n_{\mathrm{AmpR}}=47$, $n_{\mathrm{GmR}}=53$, $19$ matched-$D$ strata, $\widehat{\beta}_{\mathrm{AmpR}}=0.0$, and $p=1.0$, one may say that this registered Chen 2026 AmpR test gives no independent positive support for the $B^\ast_{Q6}$ distributed-recoding window on the supplied finite rows. Any such statement would require its own matched contact surface and its own finite statistic, with the translation step separated from transcription controls and from downstream protein or phenotype measurements.

The Chen 2026 matched-$D$ distributed-recoding run is complete as a bounded negative or non-promoting record.
