\subsection{Chen distributed synonymous recoding as a matched-D boundary test}\label{sec:chen-distributed-synonymous-recoding-matched-d-boundary}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite surface.}
This record concerns a finite perturbation surface in which the residual coordinate is not a free codon-count statistic but a windowed $B^*_{Q6}$ readout evaluated on distributed synonymous recoding rows.  The registered surface used the basis
$B=\mathrm{B\_star\_window\_Q6\_9D\_Ecoli\_CAI\_spatial\_windows\_full\_nt60\_531\_and\_middle3\_drop\_first\_60\_codons}$,
with the Escherichia coli genome CAI table as the codon-adaptation control source.  The run did not construct a new dataset in place: the finite table was already present for the audit, and the computation completed in $465.144$ seconds.  The two assayed recoding groups in the local table have sizes
$$
\begin{aligned}
 n_{\mathrm{Gmr}} &= 53,\\
 n_{\mathrm{AmpR}} &= 47,\\
 n_{D\text{-strata}} &= 19.
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the claim is evaluated as a matched finite-row comparison, not as an unrestricted population statement about synonymous codons.

\paragraph{AmpR null readback.}
For the AmpR arm, the fitted independent slope is exactly reported as
$$
\begin{aligned}
 \widehat\beta_{\mathrm{AmpR}} &= 0.0,\\
 p_{\mathrm{AmpR,independent}} &= 1.0.
\end{aligned}
$$
The finite-row conclusion is therefore negative for an AmpR-specific independent $B^*_{Q6}$ window effect on this registered surface.  The result is not a weak positive and not a trend: under the recorded statistic the AmpR component supplies no independent slope after the matching structure has been applied.  The natural readback is that this Chen distributed-recoding table remains a boundary case for the perturbation route.  It can be kept as a finite stress test for whether a windowed $B^*_{Q6}$ coordinate survives matched-D controls, but it does not itself supply a positive AmpR realization claim.

\paragraph{Relation to mediated protein abundance.}
The negative AmpR entry is compatible with the broader cross-layer rule used throughout this record: a direct $Q_i\to P_k$ association is not admitted as protein realization unless the same residual first passes through a controlled translation readout $Q_i\to T_j$.  The separate mediated protein-abundance computation used $12$ organisms and a joint $9$-coordinate $B^*_{Q6}$ residual surface.  In that table the residual coordinates were
$$
\begin{aligned}
&K\_AAA,\ \mathrm{Arg}\_AGR,\ \mathrm{Ile}\_AUA,\ \mathrm{Leu}\_CUN\_vs\_UUR,\ \mathrm{Leu}\_UUA\_vs\_UUG,\\
&\mathrm{Ser}\_UCR\_vs\_AGY,\ \mathrm{Ser}\_UCA\_vs\_UCG,\ \mathrm{Thr}\_ACR\_vs\_ACY,\ f3\_stress.
\end{aligned}
$$
The mediated fractions there were finite and organism-scoped, for example $0.9491551041216973$ in Saccharomyces cerevisiae, $0.612922252793667$ in Escherichia coli K12 MG1655, $0.6084594553474505$ in Bacillus subtilis 168, $0.4872268357227724$ in Caenorhabditis elegans, and $0.01850668564589326$ in Sulfolobus solfataricus.  Those numbers show why the present Chen table should not be overread: a protein-abundance mediation surface can be finite and positive in a matched cross-organism abundance analysis, while a particular distributed synonymous perturbation arm can still be null once its own rows and matched-D strata are used.

\paragraph{Translation-readout gate.}
The modeled translation-readout audit computed the same $9$ residual coordinates over $3$ organisms with a modeled tAI readout derived from GtRNAdb tRNA copy counts and dos Reis wobble weights.  It found $81$ coupling entries above threshold $0.01$, with maximum absolute entry $3801961.031607896$, but it also records that $3$ organisms are insufficient for a powered survival claim requiring at least $40$ matched species.  The local Chen statistic is therefore not allowed to inherit translation survival from a modeled-tAI coupling matrix.  A true mediated claim for this perturbation surface would need the same-row chain
$$
\begin{aligned}
 Q_i &\longrightarrow T_j \longrightarrow P_k,
\end{aligned}
$$
where $T_j$ is an explicitly bounded translation readout such as tAI, stAI, ribosome occupancy, or translation efficiency, and $P_k$ is a matched protein-abundance readout.  Amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, coding-sequence length, the $M$-density baseline, and matched mRNA abundance remain controls rather than promoted coordinates.

\paragraph{Comparison to other synonymous perturbation rows.}
The Chen distributed-recoding outcome is also distinct from the E. coli mRFP perturbation table, where $n=1459$ variants gave $\Delta R^2=0.05339906007191191$, $\Delta\mathrm{DL}=19.089327473282296$ bits, $p_{\mathrm{matched}}=0.004975124378109453$, and $p_{\mathrm{within}}=0.004975124378109453$, with the real CAI control not absorbing the residual signal.  The cross-dataset axis-transfer audit completed with $B=200$ permutations and runtime $974.407$ seconds, but that meta-level completion does not turn the Chen AmpR null into a positive result.  The scientific role of the Chen table is narrower: it tests whether a distributed matched-D recoding design supplies an independent finite-row perturbation signal for the registered $B^*_{Q6}$ window basis.  In the recorded AmpR arm it does not.

\paragraph{Local cannot-claim boundary.}
This chapter makes no claim that the Chen distributed synonymous recodings establish translation realization, folded structure, physical admissibility, molecular function, mechanism, organismal phenotype, selection, or a universal biological rule.  A modeled codon-adaptation table is not ribosome occupancy; matched mRNA abundance is a control, not a translation readout; bulk protein abundance is not folding, function, or physical admissibility.  The only local conclusion is the finite one: on the registered Chen matched-D surface, the AmpR component has $\widehat\beta=0.0$ with independent $p=1.0$ across $47$ AmpR rows, while the whole audit surface contains $53$ Gmr rows and $19$ matched-D strata.  Positive promotion would require a separate layer-matched contact and a surviving $Q_i\to T_j$ step before any downstream $Q_i\to T_j\to P_k$ statement is admitted.
