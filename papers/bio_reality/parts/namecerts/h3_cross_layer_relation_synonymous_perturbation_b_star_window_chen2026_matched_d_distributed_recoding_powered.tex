\subsection{Matched distributed recoding at the AmpR/GmR boundary}\label{sec:bioreality-chen2026-matched-d-distributed-recoding}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite row surface.}
This record tests a narrow perturbation question: whether the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ window coordinates carry additional explanatory power on a matched distributed-recoding surface after the relevant local codon-adaptation baseline has already been attached.  The realized finite table has two assay arms, with $47$ AmpR rows and $53$ GmR rows, and the multi-stratum design retains $19$ distinct $D$ strata.  The active coordinate basis is the named window surface
$B^{\ast}_{Q6}$--$9D$ E. coli CAI spatial windows on full nucleotide positions $60$ through $531$, together with a middle-three window after dropping the first $60$ codons.  The E. coli CAI weights were read from the genome-level CAI weight file used by the run, and the dataset was not rebuilt during this execution.  The computation completed in $465.144$ seconds under experiment run $36d651ac8538$.

\paragraph{Observed perturbation verdict.}
On this finite matched surface, the AmpR arm gives no detectable independent slope for the $B^{\ast}$ window coordinate once the declared distributed-recoding controls are present.  The fitted AmpR slope is exactly $0.0$, and the independent-tail probability is $1.0$.  Thus the run does not license a positive synonymous-perturbation claim for the AmpR component of this Chen2026 matched-$D$ design.  In the notation of the local audit, the measured outcome is
$$
\begin{aligned}
 n_{\mathrm{AmpR}} &= 47,\\
 n_{\mathrm{GmR}} &= 53,\\
 n_{D\text{-strata}} &= 19,\\
 \widehat\beta_{\mathrm{AmpR}} &= 0.0,\\
 p_{\mathrm{AmpR,independent}} &= 1.0.
\end{aligned}
$$
The correct readback is therefore a bounded null on this perturbation surface, not a rescue signal, not a protein-output mechanism, and not evidence that distributed synonymous recoding has a stable sign across antibiotic modules.

\paragraph{Relation to the broader translation-mediated surface.}
The null result is meaningful because the same $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ family is not globally empty.  In the larger translation-mediated protein-abundance computation, the nine residual coordinates were $K\_AAA$, $Arg\_AGR$, $Ile\_AUA$, $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$, $Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG$, $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$, $Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG$, $Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY$, and $f3\_stress$.  The mediated fraction surface $M^{QTP}$ stayed in $[0,1]$ for all listed organisms, but with strongly heterogeneous magnitudes: Saccharomyces cerevisiae had $M^{QTP}=0.9491551041216973$, Escherichia coli K12 MG1655 had $M^{QTP}=0.612922252793667$, Bacillus subtilis 168 had $M^{QTP}=0.6084594553474505$, Homo sapiens had $M^{QTP}=0.27206289160119157$, Mus musculus had $M^{QTP}=0.15488799907323988$, and Sulfolobus solfataricus had $M^{QTP}=0.01850668564589326$.  These values show that the coordinate family can enter a controlled translation-to-abundance decomposition in matched organism-level tables, but they do not override the AmpR-specific null in the Chen2026 matched-$D$ perturbation table.

\paragraph{Modeled translation contact.}
The translation-readout side also has a bounded modeled contact rather than a direct ribosome measurement in the relevant upstream audit.  The modeled tAI survival computation used $3$ organisms, a $61$-sense-codon space, and a coupling threshold of $0.01$.  It found $81$ entries above threshold, with maximum absolute $S_{ij}=3801961.031607896$, but the run explicitly remained underpowered for a powered survival claim because $3$ organisms are fewer than the required $40$ matched species.  The local contact is therefore a codon-adaptation readout computed from GtRNAdb tRNA copy counts and dos Reis wobble weights, not observed ribosome occupancy.  This distinction matters for the Chen2026 perturbation table: the CAI-weighted window surface can be tested as a controlled finite coordinate, but it cannot be promoted to an observed translation mechanism merely because a modeled adaptation route exists.

\paragraph{Comparison with positive and decomposed protein-output rows.}
A different E. coli synonymous perturbation table, the Nieuwkoop mRFP protein-output run, had $1459$ variants and showed a positive controlled increment, with base $R^2=0.12648386762689323$, full $R^2=0.1798829276988051$, $\Delta R^2=0.05339906007191191$, $\Delta DL=19.089327473282296$ bits, and both matched and within-null probabilities equal to $0.004975124378109453$.  That positive table is useful as a contrast class: the Chen2026 matched-$D$ AmpR component does not reproduce that pattern, since its slope is $0.0$ and its independent probability is $1.0$.  The yeast structure-mediation table gives another calibration point: with $4759$ joined rows, $R^2_{QP}=0.2715520808117803$, $R^2_{QP\mid S}=0.2706601505000995$, $R^2_{QP\mid T,M}=0.13773967696311287$, and $R^2_{QP\mid T,M,S}=0.13759011208318542$, while the structure-only fraction is only $0.003284564452662019$.  These comparisons support a conservative interpretation: positive protein or mediation behavior is dataset- and contact-dependent, not a consequence of the coordinate name alone.

\paragraph{Local non-promotion rule.}
The finite Chen2026 run licenses only the following statement: on the matched distributed-recoding table with $47$ AmpR rows, $53$ GmR rows, and $19$ multi-strata $D$ bins, the AmpR independent window coordinate has no positive fitted slope under the registered baseline, with $p=1.0$.  No translation realization follows from this result.  No folding statement follows.  No physical admissibility, biochemical function, antibiotic mechanism, organismal phenotype, or universal biological rule follows.  A stronger downstream claim would require a separate same-row contact supplying an observed translation readout, matched mRNA control, matched protein abundance or perturbation readout, and a statistic showing that the same $q_i$ first survives the $q_i \to T_j$ step before any $q_i \to P_k$ interpretation is entertained.

\paragraph{Readback.}
The chapter-level readback is a controlled refusal rather than a positive perturbation certificate.  The $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ window family remains admissible as a finite coordinate system for testing synonymous recoding surfaces, and other matched surfaces show nonzero mediation or protein-output increments.  This particular Chen2026 matched-$D$ AmpR audit, however, contributes a bounded null: $\widehat\beta_{\mathrm{AmpR}}=0.0$ with $p=1.0$ on $47$ AmpR rows against $53$ GmR rows and $19$ strata.  The result therefore constrains the coordinate family at this perturbation boundary instead of expanding it into a general translation, mechanism, or phenotype claim.
