\paragraph{Finite row surface.}
The Chen 2026 matched-$D$ distributed-recoding packet is read as a finite perturbation surface rather than as a direct biological mechanism.  The registered row set contains two recoding arms, with $n_{\mathrm{AmpR}}=47$ and $n_{\mathrm{GmR}}=53$, stratified across $19$ multi-row $D$ strata.  The tested coordinate family is explicitly recorded as
$\mathrm{B\_star\_window\_Q6\_9D\_Ecoli\_CAI\_spatial\_windows\_full\_nt60\_531\_and\_middle3\_drop\_first\_60\_codons}$.
Thus the finite carrier is not the whole coding sequence, not the full organismal phenotype, and not a protein-function assay.  It is a matched table of synonymous recoding windows in which the $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinates are projected through an \emph{E. coli} CAI-weighted spatial-window basis and then checked against the two antibiotic-marker arms.

\paragraph{Observed bounded statistic.}
The powered run completed under experiment id $36d651ac8538$ with runtime $465.144$ seconds.  The CAI weights were loaded from the local \texttt{ecoli\_genome\_cai\_weights.json} source, and the dataset was not rebuilt during the run.  The decisive finite statistic for the AmpR arm is null at the level reported here:
$$
\begin{aligned}
 n_{\mathrm{AmpR}} &= 47,\\
 n_{\mathrm{GmR}} &= 53,\\
 n_{D\text{-strata}} &= 19,\\
 \widehat\beta_{\mathrm{AmpR}} &= 0.0,\\
 p_{\mathrm{AmpR,independent}} &= 1.0.
\end{aligned}
$$
A slope of exactly $0.0$ together with an independent-arm $p$-value of $1.0$ gives no finite-row support for an AmpR-specific positive recoding response on this surface.  The correct local readback is therefore a bounded negative for that arm: the table was large enough to run the registered audit, but the registered statistic did not expose an AmpR effect.

\paragraph{Why the row table still belongs in the cross-layer register.}
The absence of an AmpR slope does not make the coordinate system meaningless.  It says that this particular matched-$D$ perturbation surface does not promote the $B^*_{Q6}$ window coordinate to a positive AmpR protein-output claim.  The wider protein-abundance mediation audit gives the reason for keeping the distinction sharp.  In the separate multi-organism mediation surface, the same nine-coordinate $B^*_{Q6}$ family was evaluated against modeled translation readout and matched protein abundance over $12$ organisms.  That audit used the coordinates $K\_AAA$, $Arg\_AGR$, $Ile\_AUA$, $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$, $Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG$, $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$, $Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG$, $Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY$, and $f3\_stress$.  It reported positive statistical mediation fractions on its own surface, for example $M^{QTP}=0.612922252793667$ in \emph{E. coli} K-12 MG1655 with $n=3739$ joined proteins, $M^{QTP}=0.9491551041216973$ in yeast with $n=5815$ joined proteins, and $M^{QTP}=0.27206289160119157$ in human with $n=19053$ joined proteins.  Those numbers show that translation-conditioned protein-abundance decompositions can be finite and nontrivial when the required joined rows exist.  They do not rescue the Chen 2026 AmpR statistic, because this chapter's registered perturbation row gives $\widehat\beta_{\mathrm{AmpR}}=0.0$ and $p=1.0$.

\paragraph{Translation readout gate.}
The translation layer is also bounded by the modeled-tAI survival audit.  That audit computed the same nine residual coordinates across three organisms, with $61$ sense codons in the codon-count space, a coupling threshold of $0.01$, and $81$ modeled-tAI entries above threshold.  The maximum absolute modeled entry was $3801961.031607896$, but the audit explicitly marked the three-organism surface as insufficient for powered survival: $3$ organisms are not enough for the requested powered translation-survival claim, and the result is modeled-tAI bounded coupling rather than ribosome profiling or proteomics validation.  This matters locally because the Chen 2026 matched-$D$ row is a synonymous perturbation table, not a measured ribosome-occupancy table.  Its AmpR null result must not be converted into a statement about observed elongation, initiation, folding, turnover, or selection.

\paragraph{Matched controls and admissible interpretation.}
The admissible interpretation is a controlled finite-row refusal of promotion.  Amino-acid sequence, organism identity, GC3, coding-sequence length, CAI-derived baseline, and matched-$D$ stratification remain controls or matching structure; they are not promoted to biological coordinates.  A direct residual-to-protein association would only be admissible after a residual coordinate first survives a translation-readout step, and a mediated protein-abundance claim would additionally require matched protein abundance and matched mRNA abundance on the same finite panel.  In this packet, the recorded AmpR arm supplies no such positive route.  The most precise statement is that the Chen 2026 matched-$D$ distributed-recoding audit ran to completion and returned a null AmpR readout under the declared $B^*_{Q6}$ spatial-window basis.

\paragraph{Local no-promotion boundary.}
No translation realization, folded structure, physical admissibility, molecular function, mechanism, organismal phenotype, selection law, or universal biological rule follows from this chapter.  The row table can constrain a registered synonymous-perturbation question, but it cannot by itself observe ribosome dynamics, mature protein abundance, protein folding, functional rescue, antibiotic mechanism, or evolutionary selection.  A later positive claim would need a separate layer-matched contact: measured translation readout for $Q_i\to T_j$, matched mRNA control for transcription separation, and matched protein abundance for any $Q_i\to T_j\to P_k$ mediation.  Until those rows are attached for this subject, the chapter remains a finite negative boundary for AmpR-specific distributed recoding rather than evidence for protein realization.
