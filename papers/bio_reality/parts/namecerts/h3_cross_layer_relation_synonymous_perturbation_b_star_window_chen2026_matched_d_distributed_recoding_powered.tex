\subsection{Matched distributed recoding as a negative protein-output boundary}
\label{sec:bior reality-chen-distributed-recoding-boundary}

\paragraph{Finite surface.}
The finite record considered here is a matched synonymous-recoding panel in which the active coordinate family is the nine-dimensional $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ window basis, evaluated on an \emph{Escherichia coli} CAI-weighted spatial-window construction.  The recorded active basis is
$$
\begin{aligned}
B &= \texttt{B\_star\_window\_Q6\_9D\_Ecoli\_CAI\_spatial\_windows\_full\_nt60\_531\_and\_middle3\_drop\_first\_60\_codons}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The joined finite rows split into $47$ ampicillin-resistance rows and $53$ gentamicin-resistance rows, with $19$ multi-row $D$ strata available for the matched distributed-recoding comparison.  The CAI weights used by the run are the \emph{E. coli} genome CAI weights stored at \texttt{tools/bio\_reality/data/ecoli\_genome\_cai\_weights.json}.  The dataset was not rebuilt during the recorded run, so the numerical verdict is a readback of the existing finite panel rather than a new data-acquisition event.

\paragraph{Tested relation.}
The intended relation is not a direct assertion that a synonymous coordinate realizes protein abundance.  The admissible surface has the form
$$
\begin{aligned}
q_i &\longrightarrow t_j \longrightarrow p_k,
\end{aligned}
$$
where $q_i$ is one of the residual coordinates, $t_j$ is a translation-layer readout such as modeled tAI, stAI, ribosome occupancy, or translation efficiency, and $p_k$ is a matched downstream protein-abundance or protein-output readout.  A direct $q_i \to p_k$ association is therefore not promoted unless the same coordinate first survives the $q_i \to t_j$ step after the declared controls.  In this panel the computed ampicillin-resistance slope is
$$
\begin{aligned}
\widehat{\beta}_{\mathrm{AmpR}} &= 0.0,
& p_{\mathrm{independent}} &= 1.0.
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the finite matched-$D$ recoding surface gives no positive ampicillin-direction signal for this coordinate package.  The appropriate reading is a bounded negative: the tested $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ window basis does not enter a protein-output interpretation through this ampicillin-resistance slice.

\paragraph{Relation to the broader mediation matrix.}
The negative Chen-distributed-recoding result is consistent with the stricter mediation rule used elsewhere in the same claim family.  In the organism-matched modeled-tAI/protein-abundance matrix, the nine coordinates are $\texttt{K\_AAA}$, $\texttt{Arg\_AGR}$, $\texttt{Ile\_AUA}$, $\texttt{Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR}$, $\texttt{Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG}$, $\texttt{Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY}$, $\texttt{Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG}$, $\texttt{Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY}$, and $\texttt{f3\_stress}$.  That separate matrix reports $12$ organisms with joined CDS and protein-abundance rows, including $3739$ \emph{E. coli} K12 MG1655 proteins and $5815$ yeast proteins.  For \emph{E. coli} K12 MG1655, the modeled mediation decomposition has
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{QP} &= 0.10767526677985896,\\
R^2_{QP\mid T} &= 0.04167869969498872,\\
R^2_{\mathrm{indirect}} &= 0.06599656708487024,\\
M^{QTP} &= 0.612922252793667.
\end{aligned}
$$
Those numbers show why the present chapter must keep translation conditioning explicit: protein-output language is only licensed when the translation-readout component is present in the same finite decomposition.  The Chen distributed-recoding panel, by contrast, records $\widehat{\beta}_{\mathrm{AmpR}}=0.0$ and $p=1.0$ for the ampicillin slice, so it does not add a positive perturbational confirmation to the mediated protein-output route.

\paragraph{Contrast with a positive perturbation surface.}
A nearby \emph{E. coli} mRFP synonymous-variant panel has $1459$ variants and reports a positive held-out increment when the same family of residual-window coordinates is added after controls:
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{\mathrm{controls}} &= 0.12648386762689323,\\
R^2_{\mathrm{controls}+B_{\perp}} &= 0.1798829276988051,\\
\Delta R^2 &= 0.05339906007191191,\\
\Delta \mathrm{DL}_{\mathrm{bits}} &= 19.089327473282296,\\
p_{\mathrm{matched}} &= 0.004975124378109453.
\end{aligned}
$$
The contrast is important.  The mRFP surface shows that this coordinate family can survive a protein-output perturbation assay in at least one finite setting, while the Chen matched-$D$ distributed-recoding surface does not show the same behavior for the recorded ampicillin-resistance slice.  The chapter therefore records a discriminating boundary rather than a global success or global failure of the coordinate family.

\paragraph{Local verdict.}
The finite Chen panel is ready as an evaluated boundary claim.  Its admissible conclusion is:
$$
\begin{aligned}
(n_{\mathrm{AmpR}}, n_{\mathrm{GmR}}, n_D) &= (47, 53, 19),\\
\widehat{\beta}_{\mathrm{AmpR}} &= 0.0,\\
p_{\mathrm{independent}} &= 1.0,
\end{aligned}
$$
so the recorded matched distributed-recoding data do not support a positive ampicillin-side $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ protein-output effect.  The result remains useful because it blocks over-reading: a residual coordinate, a CAI-weighted spatial window, or a matched recoding label is not by itself a translation, protein-abundance, or mechanism readout.

\paragraph{Layer boundary.}
No conclusion about translation realization, ribosome dynamics, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, mechanism, organismal phenotype, selection, or universality follows from this panel alone.  Such a conclusion would require a separate layer-matched contact and a same-row statistic: for translation, a translation-efficiency or ribosome-occupancy readout with matched mRNA control; for protein abundance, a matched proteomics readout after a surviving translation step; for structure, physical state, function, or phenotype, a distinct assay at that layer.  The present record therefore closes only the finite matched-$D$ perturbation readback described above.
