\subsection{Chen-style distributed recoding as a bounded perturbation readout}\label{sec:bioreality-chen-distributed-recoding-bstar-window}

\paragraph{Finite surface.}
This record concerns a finite perturbation table in which the coding sequence is not replaced by an abstract codon-frequency vector, but by a windowed $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual readout for distributed synonymous recoding.  The registered coordinate surface used the actual basis
$B\_\mathrm{run}=\texttt{B\_star\_window\_Q6\_9D\_Ecoli\_CAI\_spatial\_windows\_full\_nt60\_531\_and\_middle3\_drop\_first\_60\_codons}$,
so the admissible coordinates are the nine residual axes evaluated on the stated Escherichia coli codon-adaptation and spatial-window design.  The finite row counts in the completed run were $n_{\mathrm{GmR}}=53$ and $n_{\mathrm{AmpR}}=47$, with $19$ multi-row $D$ strata available for the distributed-recoding comparison.  The local CAI weights were read from \texttt{tools/bio\_reality/data/ecoli\_genome\_cai\_weights.json}; the run did not rebuild the dataset, and the recorded runtime was $465.144$ seconds.

\paragraph{Null calculation.}
The AmpR side of the recoding table gave a slope exactly recorded as $0.0$ and an independence $p$-value of $1.0$.  In the notation of this chapter, the fitted recoding contrast can therefore be summarized as
$$
\begin{aligned}
 n_{\mathrm{GmR}} &= 53,\\
 n_{\mathrm{AmpR}} &= 47,\\
 n_{D} &= 19,\\
 \widehat\beta_{\mathrm{AmpR}} &= 0.0,\\
 p_{\mathrm{AmpR}} &= 1.0.
\end{aligned}
$$
This is a completed negative finite-row result for the AmpR component, not a missing experiment.  The table supplies no positive AmpR recoding effect under the registered windowed $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ readout.  Consequently the admissible statement is only that this particular distributed-recoding surface was evaluated and that the AmpR contrast did not move away from zero in the recorded statistic.

\paragraph{Relation to the broader translation-contact packet.}
The negative AmpR perturbation outcome is evaluated against a wider collection of finite contacts that keep translation, protein abundance, and higher biological layers separated.  A modeled translation-readout packet computed nine synonymous residual coordinates, namely $K\_AAA$, $Arg\_AGR$, $Ile\_AUA$, $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$, $Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG$, $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$, $Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG$, $Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY$, and $f3\_stress$.  In the three-organism modeled-tAI contact, the codon-count space had $61$ sense codons, the organism count was $3$, the coupling threshold was $0.01$, and $81$ modeled entries exceeded that threshold, with maximum absolute $S_{ij}$ equal to $3801961.031607896$.  That contact is explicitly bounded: three organisms are not enough for powered survival, and the rows are modeled tAI weights rather than ribosome-profiling or proteomics validation.

\paragraph{Mediation context.}
A separate twelve-organism protein-abundance mediation audit provides useful scale without changing the Chen perturbation verdict.  Its readout used per-protein modeled tAI and matched PAXdb protein abundance after amino-acid composition, GC3, length, organismal controls, and an $M$-density baseline.  The computed mediation fractions were positive and in the unit interval for all twelve organisms: $0.6084594553474505$ for Bacillus subtilis, $0.4872268357227724$ for Caenorhabditis elegans, $0.3157053953614641$ for Danio rerio, $0.6165167748739756$ for Drosophila melanogaster, $0.612922252793667$ for Escherichia coli K12 MG1655, $0.1976903964724546$ for Gallus gallus, $0.27206289160119157$ for Homo sapiens, $0.15488799907323988$ for Mus musculus, $0.08397602919861291$ for Mycobacterium smegmatis, $0.17489681798543982$ for Rattus norvegicus, $0.9491551041216973$ for Saccharomyces cerevisiae, and $0.01850668564589326$ for Sulfolobus solfataricus.  The joined row counts ranged from $1656$ proteins in Sulfolobus to $19395$ in mouse, with Escherichia coli contributing $3739$ joined proteins.  These values show that a protein-abundance mediation matrix can be finite and nondegenerate, but they do not rescue the Chen AmpR recoding slope, because the Chen statistic is its own perturbation table with its own zero effect.

\paragraph{Neighboring perturbation scale.}
The most directly comparable positive perturbation-scale contact is the E. coli mRFP protein-output table, which had $1459$ variants, base $R^2=0.12648386762689323$, full $R^2=0.1798829276988051$, $\Delta R^2=0.05339906007191191$, $\Delta DL=19.089327473282296$ bits, and matched-null and within-null $p$-values both equal to $0.004975124378109453$.  The real-CAI comparison there did not absorb the $B^{\ast}$ signal, with real-CAI versus proxy correlation $0.17884920449487354$.  This neighboring result matters only as a calibration of scale: a finite perturbation table can produce a detectable protein-output increment, whereas the Chen distributed-recoding AmpR contrast reported here has $\widehat\beta=0.0$ and $p=1.0$.

\paragraph{Residual axes and non-promotion.}
The structural-order audit found that the only fold-level sign-flip-passing coordinate was $f3\_stress$, with weighted held-out $\Delta R^2=0.0031744502294756994$ and sign-flip $p=0.03125$; the remaining eight axes did not pass that gate.  A yeast structure-mediation audit reported $R^2_{QP}=0.2715520808117803$, $R^2_{QP\mid S}=0.2706601505000995$, $R^2_{QP\mid T,M}=0.13773967696311287$, and $R^2_{QP\mid T,M,S}=0.13759011208318542$, with a structure-only fraction of $0.003284564452662019$ and structure-incremental fraction of $0.0005507778820193332$.  These numbers are compatible with a narrow residual-statistical reading, not with a claim that a synonymous residual by itself determines folding or function.  The Chen distributed-recoding row is therefore kept at perturbation-audit scope: it does not establish translation realization, protein folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, mechanism, phenotype, selection, or universality.  Any such interpretation would require a separate layer-matched contact and a statistic computed on the corresponding rows.

\paragraph{Mass-balance and turnover checks.}
The surrounding powered checks further narrow the interpretation.  In the dynamic mass-balance table, $120$ genes and $718$ intervals gave $R^2_{L0}=0.9960628201518571$, $R^2_{L1}=0.996410628851051$, $R^2_{L2}=0.9712443377743347$, $R^2_{L3}=0.8953999568421264$, $\Delta R^2=-0.07584438093220836$, $\Delta DL=-668.8046070381928$ bits, $p_{TEbin}=0.6831683168316832$, and $p_{timeshuffle}=0.9306930693069307$.  In the turnover audit, $3290$ genes gave $R^2_{L2}=0.055740851626933985$, $R^2_{L3}=0.050278872193227464$, $\Delta R^2=-0.005461979433706483$, $\Delta DL=-13.68819707902732$ bits, $p_{matchedbin}=0.8855721393034826$, and $p_{recoded}=1.0$.  These checks align with the same conservative reading: a residual coordinate may be useful as a controlled finite statistic in one table and absent in another, but absent or negative increments must not be rephrased as hidden mechanism.

\paragraph{Conservative conclusion.}
The Chen distributed-recoding packet is ready as a bounded negative entry.  Its own finite rows give $n_{\mathrm{GmR}}=53$, $n_{\mathrm{AmpR}}=47$, $n_D=19$, $\widehat\beta_{\mathrm{AmpR}}=0.0$, and $p_{\mathrm{AmpR}}=1.0$.  The correct chapter-level conclusion is that this registered $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ windowed perturbation did not support an AmpR recoding effect in the recorded statistic.  The result may coexist with other positive or partially positive residual contacts, including modeled-tAI mediation fractions and the E. coli mRFP perturbation increment, but it does not inherit those outcomes.  The row is a disciplined negative surface in the BioReality ledger: it records where the residual coordinate was tested, what finite counts were present, and which higher biological interpretations remain unavailable without their own measurements.
