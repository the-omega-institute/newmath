\subsection{Window-six spectrum for the standard-code codon table}
\label{sec:bioreality-h0-spectrum}

\paragraph{Finite carrier and observed row.}
The record concerns the standard genetic-code table only at the codon-label level. The carrier is the six-coordinate codon cube $Q_6$, with each codon displayed as a six-bit coordinate and then read against the curated standard-code assignment of codons to amino-acid or stop labels. The distinguished boundary row is the finite set
$R=\{\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UUA}\}$.
The verified cardinality row gives $|R|=13$, and the punctured-cube computation gives the face-vector
$$
\begin{aligned}
(f_0,f_1,f_2)=(13,16,4).
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the finite observation is not merely a count of codons: it records a local tile with thirteen vertices, sixteen one-dimensional adjacencies, and four two-dimensional faces inside the six-bit codon display. The label contact is the curated standard-code table; it supplies the codon-to-label readback, not a biochemical mechanism.

\paragraph{Spectral row.}
The spectral computation attached to this same finite row reports three scalar quantities. The measured boundary eigenvalue is $\lambda_R=0.4758352843197002$, the measured complementary or module-side eigenvalue is $\lambda_M=0.6752479308830213$, and the radial scalar is $\mu=3.051487585298128$. The polynomial residual is numerically negligible at the displayed precision:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\lambda_R&=0.4758352843197002,\\
\lambda_M&=0.6752479308830213,\\
\mu&=3.051487585298128,\\
P_{\mathrm{rad}}(\lambda_R,\lambda_M,\mu)&=-6.224354365258478\cdot 10^{-12}.

The same value is recorded as the cubic residual, so the finite row supplies a stable numerical spectrum rather than a qualitative visual impression. The spectral radius row also returns $0.6752479308830213$, matching $\lambda_M$ within the stated tolerance field.

\paragraph{Shape significance against randomized label rows.}
Two finite null checks support the claim that the observed tile is a non-generic code-label pattern. In the unrestricted shape comparison with seed $42$, $N=2000$ randomized samples were evaluated after $57377$ distinct-sample attempts and $55377$ duplicate retries. Exactly one randomized sample matched the observed shape, giving
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{\mathrm{exact}}&=0.0005,\\
p_{J\geq 0.9}&=0.0125,
\end{aligned}
$$
with $25$ samples reaching Jaccard similarity at least $0.9$. In the amino-acid-class-preserving null with the same $N=2000$ and seed $42$, the observed module size was $20$, the exact-shape count was $0$, and the lower-tail count at or below the observed module size was $7$. This gives
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{\mathrm{class\ exact}}&=0.0,\\
p_{\mathrm{class\ lower\ tail}}&=0.0035.
\end{aligned}
$$
These values are evidence for a finite code-layer regularity in the curated table. They do not make the local codon geometry into a law of molecular translation.

This makes the finite tile useful as a code-read predictor within its tested scope, while keeping the prediction attached to codon labels rather than to protein realization.

The complete module classification assigns all $13$ boundary codons, with no missing codons, no extra module codons, and no unassigned codons. The six modules are
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{agr}&=\{\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG}\},\\

The classification row reports $R$-size $13$ and assigned count $13$. Every module has either predictable or boundary status, so the finite boundary is internally accounted for at the level of codon-label rows.

\paragraph{Local interpretation and boundary of the claim.}
The result is a finite, table-bound observation: six-bit codon coordinates expose a local boundary tile, its spectrum, and its module partition when the curated standard-code labels are supplied. The contact used here can test code-read statements and local codon-label splits. It cannot test translation realization, folded structure, physical admissibility, biological function, a universal mechanism, or any claim that DNA must obey this geometry. Amino-acid label readback is therefore not a folded protein observation. Any claim about structure, function, organism-level mechanism, or universality would require a separate empirical contact with that layer before it could be stated as an observed fact.
