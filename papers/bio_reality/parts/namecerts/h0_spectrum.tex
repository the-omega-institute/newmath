\subsection{Window-six spectral packet for the standard-code label table}
\label{sec:h0-spectrum-window-six}

\paragraph{Claim and finite carrier.}
The packet studies the standard genetic-code table only at the codon-label layer.  Its finite carrier is the six-coordinate codon cube $Q_6$, with each codon viewed as a six-bit display of a three-letter base word.  The admissible distinctions are local: a tile face in the cube, a singleton label corner, and a three-point label class.  The biological contact is the curated standard-code assignment of codons to amino-acid or stop labels.  Thus the object being tested is not translation as a process, not protein folding, not physical admissibility, and not biological function.  The contact says only that the code table supplies labels that can be read against the finite cube.

\paragraph{Boundary set.}
The main boundary row is the $13$-codon set $R$.  The verified list is
$$
\begin{aligned}
R=\{&\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\\
&\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UUA}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The recorded size is $|R|=13$.  The same row identifies this set as a punctured-cube pattern with face-vector
$$
\begin{aligned}
(f_0,f_1,f_2)=(13,16,4).
\end{aligned}
$$
The point of this row is combinatorial: the standard-code labels mark a local codon-tile boundary in $Q_6$.  The row does not assert that the tile is a law of biological organization.

\paragraph{Spectral measurements.}
The spectral row assigns two local radii and one coupling parameter to the observed split.  The measured values are
$$
\begin{aligned}
\lambda_M&=0.6752479308830213,\\
\lambda_R&=0.4758352843197002,\\
\mu&=3.051487585298128.
\end{aligned}
$$
The radial equation is numerically satisfied with residual
$$
\begin{aligned}
-6.224354365258478\times 10^{-12},
\end{aligned}
$$
reported both as the cubic-polynomial residual and as the radial-polynomial value.  The spectral radius in tolerance is again $0.6752479308830213$.  These numbers make the row a finite spectral contact for the window-six codon geometry: $M$ carries the larger local radius, $R$ carries the smaller boundary radius, and $\mu$ records the coupling scale required by the fitted cubic relation.

\paragraph{Shape rarity under coordinate randomization.}
A coordinate-randomized comparison used $N=2000$ sampled tables with seed $42$.  Producing distinct samples required $57377$ attempts and $55377$ duplicate retries.  Only $1$ sampled table matched the exact observed shape, giving
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{\mathrm{exact}}=0.0005.
\end{aligned}
$$
The high-overlap comparison counted $25$ samples with Jaccard score at least $0.9$, giving
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{\mathrm{Jaccard}\ge 0.9}=0.0125.
\end{aligned}
$$
The finite conclusion is therefore limited but sharp: the observed local tile is uncommon among the sampled coordinate controls, while still being only a code-table pattern.

\paragraph{Amino-acid-class preserving null row.}
A second null row preserved amino-acid class information.  It used $N=2000$ samples with seed $42$ and observed $|M|=20$.  The exact-shape count was $0$, so the recorded exact-shape probability was
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{\mathrm{class,exact}}=0.0.
\end{aligned}
$$
The lower-tail count at or below the observed $M$ size was $7$, giving
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{\mathrm{class,lower}}=0.0035.
\end{aligned}
$$
This row is stronger than a bare coordinate control because it keeps label-class information while still finding the observed size and shape unusually constrained in the finite sample.

\paragraph{Leave-one-code-out readback.}
The leave-one-code-out probe evaluated $23$ code tables and $63$ targets.  The baseline hit rate was $0.19821739130434785$.  The rate above baseline was $0.9841269841269841$, the hit rate inside $M\setminus T$ was $0.9365079365079365$, and the hit rate inside $M\setminus T$ plus the boundary was $0.9841269841269841$.  By family, the packet records $5$ families: alternative yeast has rate $0.8333333333333334$, while bacterial-plastid, candidate-other, mitochondrial, and nuclear-ciliate each have rate $1.0$.  This supports code-layer recoverability of the local pattern across the evaluated table family, not a claim about organismal mechanism.

\paragraph{Leave-one-codon-out readback.}
The leave-one-codon-out row records $0$ anchors, $5$ boundary codons, $8$ high-closure-coverage codons, and $8$ predictable codons.  Its recovery rate is $1.0$, and the above-threshold recovery entry is also $1.0$.  The associated module classification is complete for $R$: it assigns all $13$ codons, has no extra module codons, no missing codons, and no unassigned codons.  The modules are
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{agr}&=\{\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG}\},\\
\mathrm{ar\_lys}&=\{\mathrm{AAA}\},\\
\mathrm{cun}&=\{\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU}\},\\
\mathrm{ile\_met}&=\{\mathrm{AUA}\},\\
\mathrm{ssn}&=\{\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UUA}\},\\
\mathrm{stop\_trp}&=\{\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UGA}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
Every listed module has either a predictable or boundary status in the recorded classification.  The classification is therefore exhaustive for the observed $R$ row.

\paragraph{Local scientific reading.}
The finite rows jointly support the following narrow statement.  In the six-bit codon cube, the curated standard-code labels expose a $13$-codon punctured-cube boundary with face-vector $(13,16,4)$, spectral values $\lambda_M=0.6752479308830213$, $\lambda_R=0.4758352843197002$, and $\mu=3.051487585298128$, and unusually low sampled probabilities under both coordinate and amino-acid-class preserving controls.  The leave-one probes then show that the same local boundary is recoverable in code-layer perturbation tests.

\paragraph{Scope limits.}
The contact is a codon-to-label table.  It does not observe ribosomal translation, folded structure, physical feasibility, biochemical function, or an organism-independent mechanism.  Amino-acid label readback is not protein-structure evidence.  CUN, UUR, tRNA-Leu, or cross-organism wording cannot be promoted into structural or functional evidence without a separate matched contact.  The admissible conclusion is that the standard-code labels, when placed on $Q_6$, form a finite local spectral-and-boundary pattern.  Any claim beyond code-label readback requires a different observation channel.
