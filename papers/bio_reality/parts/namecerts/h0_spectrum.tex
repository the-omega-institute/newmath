\subsection{Window-Six Codon Spectrum at the Code-Label Boundary}
\label{sec:bioreality-h0-spectrum}

\paragraph{Finite codon display.}
The record concerns the standard-code codon table viewed only at the codon-label layer. The carrier is the six-coordinate codon cube $Q_6$, where a codon is displayed as a finite six-bit coordinate and then read against the curated standard-code label table. The admissible distinctions used here are the six-bit display, a local tile face, a singleton label corner, and a three-point label class. These distinctions are sufficient to ask whether the standard-code labels leave a small boundary-shaped trace inside the cube, but they are not sufficient to infer translation dynamics, folded structure, physical admissibility, function, mechanism, or biological universality.

\paragraph{Boundary row set.}
The verified boundary row $R$ has cardinality $13$. Its codons are
$$
\begin{aligned}
R=\{&\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\\
&\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UUA}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The same finite row was independently certified as a punctured-cube pattern with $f$-vector $(13,16,4)$. In this chapter the word boundary therefore means this concrete thirteen-codon subset together with its finite face data, not an open-ended biological law. The associated module partition is complete: the two-codon $\mathrm{AGR}$ module is $\{\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG}\}$, the $\mathrm{ar\_lys}$ singleton is $\{\mathrm{AAA}\}$, the $\mathrm{CUN}$ block is $\{\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU}\}$, the $\mathrm{ile\_met}$ singleton is $\{\mathrm{AUA}\}$, the $\mathrm{SSN}$ pair is $\{\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UUA}\}$, and the stop--tryptophan-side module is $\{\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UGA}\}$. The module audit reports $R\_\mathrm{size}=13$, $13$ assigned codons, no extra module codons, no missing codons, and no unassigned codons.

\paragraph{Shape significance.}
The observed $M$-side shape has size $20$ in the amino-acid-class-preserving null audit. In the broader shape test with $N=2000$ samples and seed $42$, the run required $57377$ distinct-sample attempts and recorded $55377$ duplicate retries. Exact shape agreement occurred once, giving $p_{\mathrm{exact}}=0.0005$. High-overlap agreement at Jaccard at least $0.9$ occurred $25$ times, giving $p_{J\geq 0.9}=0.0125$. Under the amino-acid-class-preserving null with $N=2000$ and seed $42$, exact shape agreement occurred $0$ times, so the recorded exact-shape value is $0.0$, while $7$ samples fell in the lower tail at or below the observed $M$ size, giving $p_{\mathrm{lower\ tail}}=0.0035$. These numbers make the local pattern statistically nontrivial as a codon-table row phenomenon, while still leaving all higher biological layers unclaimed.

\paragraph{Prediction checks inside the code layer.}
The leave-one-code-out audit evaluated $23$ code tables and $63$ total targets. Its baseline hit rate is $0.19821739130434785$, while the hit rate above baseline is $0.9841269841269841$. The hit rate inside $M\setminus T$ is $0.9365079365079365$, and the rate inside $M\setminus T$ together with the boundary is $0.9841269841269841$. By family, the hit rate above baseline is $0.8333333333333334$ for alternative yeast and $1.0$ for bacterial plastid, candidate other, mitochondrial, and nuclear ciliate families. The leave-one-codon-out audit records $0$ anchors, $5$ boundary cases, $8$ high-coverage closure cases, $8$ predictable cases, and recovery rate $1.0$. These checks support the claim that the window-six coordinate can recover local code-label structure when one row or codon is withheld. They do not support a claim that the coordinate system performs translation or predicts protein form.

\paragraph{Reality contact and blocked promotion.}
The only contact used here is the curated standard-code table, whose observed fact is a codon-to-amino-acid-or-stop assignment. That contact has resolution at codon label readback. It can test whether a local codon tile splits labels in the standard table, but it cannot test translation realization, structural order, physical admissibility, or functional realization. The recorded mismatches therefore remain local and disciplined: the code table aligns with the code-layer split, but it blocks promotion to protein realization, folding, function, a universal mechanism, or any claim that DNA must obey this coordinate geometry as a biological necessity. Amino-acid label readback is only label readback; without an independent structure contact, it contains no observation of fold, physical stability, or function.

\paragraph{Claim status.}
The finite evidence supports the following restricted conclusion. In the standard-code codon table, the six-bit window exposes a thirteen-codon punctured-cube boundary row with a stable spectral certificate, nontrivial shape statistics, complete module assignment, and strong leave-one-out recovery at the code-label layer. The conclusion stops at that layer. Any assertion about translation machinery, folded protein structure, physical admissibility, biological function, universal law, or mechanism would require a separate observation channel not present in this row.
