\subsection{Window-Six Codon Spectrum at the Code-Read Layer}\label{sec:bireality-h0-spectrum}\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite object and scope.}
The record studied here is the standard-code codon table viewed through a six-bit codon display. The finite carrier is the codon cube $Q_6$: each codon is treated as a coordinate in a binary window, while the amino-acid or stop assignment is read from the curated standard-code table. The construction separates four internal pieces: coordinate data, a closure operation, a finite spectrum, and a relation on selected codons. The biological contact is deliberately narrow. It observes only that a curated table assigns each codon to an amino-acid or stop label; it does not observe translation realization, folded structure, physical admissibility, biological function, a mechanism, or a universal biological law.

\paragraph{Distinguished boundary row.}
The finite relation $R$ has exactly $13$ codons. The verified row is
$$
\begin{aligned}
R=\{&\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\\
&\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UUA}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The same computation identifies this row as a punctured-cube pattern with face-vector
$$
\begin{aligned}
f(R)=(13,16,4).
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the observed row is not a verbal cluster of codon names; it is a finite subcomplex in the six-bit display. The row contains singleton-label corners and three-point label classes inside a local tile face, but that geometric description remains a code-layer description unless another observation supplies a higher biological layer.

The radial polynomial check returns
$$
\begin{aligned}
P(\lambda_M,\lambda_R,\mu)=-6.224354365258478\cdot 10^{-12},

$$
with the same value recorded as the cubic-polynomial residual. At the displayed numerical precision this is a zero-residual certificate for the finite spectral row. The value $0.6752479308830213$ is also the reported spectral radius inside tolerance. The certificate therefore supports the statement that the window-six construction exposes a reproducible local spectral pattern in the codon-label table.

\paragraph{Shape against randomized code-label controls.}
The primary shape run used $N=2000$ sampled controls with seed $42$. It required $57377$ distinct-sample attempts and recorded $55377$ duplicate retries. Only $1$ sampled control matched the exact observed shape, giving
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{\mathrm{exact}}=0.0005.
\end{aligned}
$$
The relaxed high-overlap check found $25$ samples with Jaccard score at least $0.9$, giving
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{J\geq 0.9}=0.0125.
\end{aligned}
$$
A second control that preserves amino-acid class also used $N=2000$ and seed $42$. For that control the observed $M$-row size was $20$, the exact-shape count was $0$, and the lower-tail count at or below the observed $M$ size was $7$. The resulting values were
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{\mathrm{class\ exact}}=0.0,\qquad p_{\mathrm{class\ lower\ tail}}=0.0035.
\end{aligned}
$$
These finite rows justify treating the pattern as a nontrivial code-table regularity. They do not justify replacing the curated label table with a biological mechanism.

\paragraph{Leave-one-code and leave-one-codon checks.}
The leave-one-code experiment evaluated $23$ code tables and $63$ total targets. Its baseline hit rate was
$$
\begin{aligned}
0.19821739130434785,
\end{aligned}
$$
whereas the hit rate above baseline was
$$
\begin{aligned}
0.9841269841269841.
\end{aligned}
$$
The hit rate inside $M\setminus T$ was $0.9365079365079365$, and the hit rate inside $M\setminus T$ plus the boundary row was again $0.9841269841269841$. The leave-one-codon experiment reported no anchors, $5$ boundary cases, $8$ high-closure-coverage cases, $8$ predictable cases, and recovery rate $1.0$. These checks indicate that the local tile is stable as a code-read probe under finite deletion tests, while still remaining a probe of labels rather than a demonstration of translation or function.

\paragraph{Family and module accounting.}
The leave-one-code family run partitioned the comparison into $5$ families. The family hit rates above baseline were $0.8333333333333334$ for alternative yeast, $1.0$ for bacterial plastid, $1.0$ for candidate other, $1.0$ for mitochondrial, and $1.0$ for nuclear ciliate. The module classification accounts for all $13$ codons in $R$ with no extras, no missing entries, and no unassigned codons. The modules are
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{agr}&=\{\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG}\},&
\mathrm{ar\_lys}&=\{\mathrm{AAA}\},\\
\mathrm{cun}&=\{\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU}\},&
\mathrm{ile\_met}&=\{\mathrm{AUA}\},\\
\mathrm{ssn}&=\{\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UUA}\},&
\mathrm{stop\_trp}&=\{\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UGA}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
Every module has either a predictable or boundary status. This is the strongest local reading of the finite packet: the code-table labels form a complete, reproducible module decomposition over the selected punctured-cube row.

\paragraph{Local boundary verdict.}
The finite evidence supports a narrow claim. Window-six coordinates expose a local boundary-tile structure in the standard-code label table, with a $13$-codon row, face-vector $(13,16,4)$, a stable spectral radius $0.6752479308830213$, significant randomized-shape contrasts, high leave-one-code recovery, and complete module accounting. The evidence does not cross the code-read boundary. No statement about actual translation, protein folding, structural order, physical admissibility, biological function, mechanism, or universality follows from this packet unless an independent contact supplies observations at that layer.
