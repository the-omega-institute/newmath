\paragraph{Finite coordinate object.}
The object recorded here is the standard-code codon table viewed through the six-coordinate codon cube $Q_{6}$.  The cube coordinate is not treated as a biological cause.  It is a finite display on which each codon occupies a six-bit position, each codon receives its curated amino-acid or stop label, and a local boundary set can be read as a tile-shaped subset of that display.  The reality contact is the curated standard genetic-code table: it supplies codon-to-label assignments and nothing stronger.  In this row the distinguished boundary set $R$ has cardinality $13$ and is exactly
$$
\begin{aligned}
R=\{&AAA, AGA, AGG, AUA, CUA, CUC, CUG, CUU,\\
&UAA, UAG, UCA, UGA, UUA\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The same finite row verifies that this set has the punctured-cube face data
$$
\begin{aligned}
f(R)=(13,16,4),
\end{aligned}
$$
so the local object is not merely a list of exceptional codons.  It has a reproducible small-complex shape in the window-six cube.  The module classification accounts for every one of the $13$ codons in $R$: $\{AGA,AGG\}$ form the $agr$ module, $\{AAA\}$ the $ar\_lys$ module, $\{CUA,CUC,CUG,CUU\}$ the $cun$ module, $\{AUA\}$ the $ile\_met$ module, $\{UCA,UUA\}$ the $ssn$ module, and $\{UAA,UAG,UGA\}$ the $stop\_trp$ module.  The row reports $R\_size=13$, $assigned\_count=13$, with no missing codons, no extra module codons, and no unassigned codons.

$$
\begin{aligned}

p_{J\geq 0.9}&=0.0125.
\end{aligned}
$$
A second null row preserves amino-acid class structure.  There the observed module-size statistic is $|M|=20$, again with $N=2000$ and seed $42$.  The exact-shape count is $0$, the lower-tail count at or below the observed $M$ size is $7$, and the reported probabilities are

These controls support the narrow statement that the observed codon-label boundary has a rare finite geometry relative to the tested randomizations.  They do not identify a biochemical mechanism.

\paragraph{Local boundary of interpretation.}
The contact used here observes only that the curated standard-code table assigns codons to amino-acid or stop labels.  Therefore the row supports a code-read claim: window-six coordinates expose a local boundary tile with a reproducible spectrum, rare shape statistics under the supplied controls, and strong leave-out recovery in the tested finite rows.  No claim about translation realization, folded structure, physical admissibility, biological function, molecular mechanism, or universality follows from this chapter.  Crossing any of those boundaries would require an additional contact that directly observes the relevant layer.  In particular, amino-acid label sequences are not protein structures, CUN or UUR label behavior is not a structural experiment, and the local tile in $Q_{6}$ is not a law that all biological systems must obey.
