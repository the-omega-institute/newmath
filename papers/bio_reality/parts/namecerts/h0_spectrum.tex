\subsection{Window-six spectrum of the standard-code boundary tile}\label{sec:bioreality-h0-spectrum}

\paragraph{Finite coordinate object.}
The packet treats the standard genetic-code table as a code-layer contact, not as a mechanical account of translation.  The finite carrier is the six-coordinate codon cube $Q_6$, read as a binary display of the three codon positions after fixing the four nucleotide letters as a two-bit alphabet.  The visible distinctions in this record are a window-six codon display, a local tile face, a singleton label corner, and a three-point label class.  The contact supplies only the assignment of codons to amino-acid or stop labels in the curated standard code.  It therefore licenses finite label readback and local split tests, while leaving translation chemistry, folded structure, physical admissibility, biological function, mechanism, and universality outside the present observation.

\paragraph{Boundary set.}
The finite boundary row marks a distinguished set $R$ of thirteen codons:
$$
\begin{aligned}
R = \{&\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\\
&\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UUA}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The cardinality certificate reports $|R|=13$, and the punctured-cube row gives the same codon list with face-vector data
$$
\begin{aligned}
f(R)=(13,16,4).
\end{aligned}
$$
This is the local geometric content of the row: the standard-code labels pick out a thirteen-vertex punctured tile inside the codon cube, with sixteen one-dimensional adjacencies and four two-dimensional faces in the induced finite complex.  The row is not a claim that biological coding is forced by this geometry.  It says only that this particular code-layer readback exposes a reproducible local boundary shape.

\paragraph{Shape significance.}
The shape-significance run used $N=2000$ sampled comparisons with seed $42$.  It records $57377$ distinct-sample attempts and $55377$ duplicate retries, showing that the null sampling was constrained by repeated finite configurations.  There was one exact shape match, giving
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{\mathrm{exact}}=0.0005.
\end{aligned}
$$
There were $25$ matches with Jaccard score at least $0.9$, giving
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{J\geq 0.9}=0.0125.
\end{aligned}
$$
These finite rates say that the observed boundary tile is not a common draw under the recorded sampling procedure.  They do not identify a causal mechanism and do not turn the local codon tile into a universal biological principle.

\paragraph{Amino-acid-class preserving null.}
A second null row preserves amino-acid class information while testing the same kind of local shape.  In $N=2000$ trials with seed $42$, the observed $M$ size is $20$.  The row reports no exact matches, so
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{\mathrm{aa\ class\ exact}}=0.0.
\end{aligned}
$$
It also reports $7$ trials in the lower tail at or below the observed $M$ size, giving
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{\mathrm{lower\ tail}}=0.0035.
\end{aligned}
$$
This strengthens the finite reading that the standard-code local split is not reproduced by a broad class-preserving reshuffle.  The observation remains a code-table fact: it has not measured ribosomal realization, molecular folding, thermodynamic viability, or organismal function.

The leave-one-code-out row evaluated $23$ tables and $63$ targets.  The baseline hit rate is $0.19821739130434785$.  Against this baseline, the finite local rule reports a hit rate of $0.9365079365079365$ inside $M-T$ and $0.9841269841269841$ inside $M-T$ plus the boundary.  The same value, $0.9841269841269841$, is recorded for the rate above baseline.  By family, the hit rates above baseline are $1.0$ for bacterial-plastid, mitochondrial, nuclear-ciliate, and candidate-other families, and $0.8333333333333334$ for the alternative-yeast family.  These rows show that the local code-layer tile has predictive stability across the finite table comparison used here.  They still do not supply a claim about protein structure or function, because all hits are codon-label hits.

\paragraph{Scope of the record.}
The supported claim is that window-six codon coordinates expose a statistically unusual and spectrally coherent local boundary tile in the curated standard-code label table.  The support uses the thirteen-codon set $R$, the punctured-cube vector $(13,16,4)$, the spectral values $\lambda_M$, $\lambda_R$, and $\mu$, the exact-shape and high-Jaccard rates, the amino-acid-class preserving null, and the leave-one-code and leave-one-codon rows.  The contact does not observe translation realization, folded protein structure, physical admissibility, biological function, a universal mechanism, or any law forcing DNA to obey the coordinate geometry.  Any later step toward those layers would require a separate observation at that layer; the present chapter records only finite code-label evidence and the local boundary consequences of that evidence.
