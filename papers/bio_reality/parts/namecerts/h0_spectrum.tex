\paragraph{Finite object.}
The record concerns the standard genetic-code table only at the code-read layer.  The carrier is the six-coordinate codon cube $Q_6$, used as a finite display of the $64$ codon strings.  Its observable labels are not inferred from the cube itself: the label contact is the curated standard-code table assigning each codon to an amino-acid or stop symbol.  The finite distinctions used here are a six-bit codon display, a local tile face, singleton label corners, and three-point label classes.  The claim is therefore a finite spectral and boundary statement about a labelled codon table, not a claim about translation chemistry.

This does not say that DNA must instantiate this geometry as a biological necessity; it says that this curated label table has this finite coordinate pattern.
