\subsection{Window-six spectral boundary for the standard-code codon table}
\label{sec:bioreality-h0-spectrum}

\paragraph{Finite carrier and observed row.}
This record works with the six-coordinate codon cube $Q_6$, where each codon is read as a finite bit display over the standard-code label table. The biological contact used here is only the curated assignment of codons to amino-acid or stop labels. It is therefore a code-layer contact: it can test label readback and local tile splitting, but it does not observe translation, folding, physical admissibility, function, mechanism, or universality. The distinguished boundary set $R$ has size $13$ and is exactly
$$
\begin{aligned}
R=\{&\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\\
&\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UUA}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The same row verifies that this $R$ is a punctured cube with face data
$$
\begin{aligned}
(f_0,f_1,f_2)=(13,16,4).
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the local object is not merely a list of exceptional codons: it has a finite cubical incidence shape with $13$ vertices, $16$ edges, and $4$ two-faces. The companion finite row reports an observed $M$-set size of $20$, but this packet does not include the explicit $M$ codon list; only its size and its comparison statistics are used here.

The residual is small enough for the row to be treated as a numerical satisfaction check for the finite computation, while still remaining a computation over the supplied codon-label data rather than a biological mechanism claim.

\paragraph{Shape comparison against finite null rows.}
The shape-significance run used $N=2000$ sampled comparison rows with seed $42$. It required $57377$ distinct-sample attempts and incurred $55377$ duplicate retries. Among these samples, the exact local shape appeared once, giving
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{\mathrm{exact}}=0.0005.
\end{aligned}
$$
The number of rows with Jaccard score at least $0.9$ was $25$, giving
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{\mathrm{Jaccard}\geq 0.9}=0.0125.
\end{aligned}
$$
A second null row preserving amino-acid class again used $N=2000$ and seed $42$. It recorded observed $|M|=20$, exact-match count $0$, and lower-tail count $7$ for rows with $M$-size no larger than the observed value. Hence
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{\mathrm{aa\ class\ exact}}&=0.0,\\
p_{\mathrm{aa\ class\ lower\ tail}}&=0.0035.
\end{aligned}
$$
These comparisons support the narrower statement that the window-six coordinate presentation exposes a non-generic local boundary pattern in the curated standard-code table. They do not justify promoting the pattern into a law of translation, protein structure, biophysical admissibility, organismal function, or universal biology.

This is the strongest local conclusion available from the supplied rows: the spectral boundary, the punctured-cube shape, the finite null comparisons, and the recovery tests cohere at the code-read layer. Any such stronger assertion would require a separate observation channel designed for that layer.
