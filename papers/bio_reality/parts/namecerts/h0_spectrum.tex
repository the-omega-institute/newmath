\paragraph{Finite codon display.}
The record for claim $\mathrm{h0.spectrum}$ is a finite code-layer observation on the six-bit codon cube $Q_6$.  The carrier is the codon display itself, with local tile faces, singleton label corners, and three-point label classes read against the curated standard-code table.  The biological datum used here is only the standard codon-to-label assignment.  It is not a translation experiment, not a protein-structure contact, not a physical-realizability test, and not a functional assay.

The distinguished boundary set $R$ has cardinality $13$.  In codon notation the verified row is
$$
\begin{aligned}

&\mathrm{CUU}, \mathrm{UAA}, \mathrm{UAG}, \mathrm{UCA}, \mathrm{UGA}, \mathrm{UUA}\}.

\paragraph{Shape significance.}
A separate shape null row, also with $N=2000$ and seed $42$, required $57377$ distinct-sample attempts and incurred $55377$ duplicate retries.  It found $1$ exact shape match and $25$ samples with Jaccard score at least $0.9$.  The row-level probabilities are
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{\mathrm{exact\ shape}} &= 0.0005,\\
p_{\mathrm{Jaccard}\geq 0.9} &= 0.0125.
\end{aligned}
$$
These values support the finite claim that the window-six tile is unusual relative to the tested randomizations.  They do not by themselves explain why the biological code has this form.  They say that, after the codon labels are supplied by the curated table, the resulting coordinate shape is rare in the tested finite ensembles.

\paragraph{Scope of the claim.}
The supported conclusion is narrow.  The six-bit codon coordinate exposes a finite local boundary tile in the curated standard-code label table.  The supporting numbers are the thirteen-codon boundary set, its punctured-cube face-vector $(13,16,4)$, the spectral values $\lambda_M=0.6752479308830213$, $\lambda_R=0.4758352843197002$, and $\mu=3.051487585298128$, the near-zero polynomial residual $-6.224354365258478\times 10^{-12}$, the low shape-null rates $0.0005$ and $0.0125$, the amino-acid-class preserving lower-tail rate $0.0035$, the leave-one-code-out hit rate $0.9841269841269841$, and the complete thirteen-codon module assignment.

Nothing in this finite row upgrades the local coordinate pattern into a global law of biology.  The curated table supplies codon labels, not ribosomal dynamics, not protein folding, not thermodynamic admissibility, not organismal function, and not universality across all genetic systems.  Any claim about translation realization, folded structure, physical admissibility, biological function, mechanism, or universal necessity would require a separate contact that directly observes that higher layer.  Without such a contact, the present record remains a code-read result: a finite, numerically supported local tile in the standard-code codon table.
