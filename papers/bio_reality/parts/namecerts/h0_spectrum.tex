\subsection{Spectral Codon Window Record}
\label{subsec:h0-spectrum}

\paragraph{Finite object.}
This record concerns the standard-code codon table viewed only at the code-read layer. The finite carrier is the six-coordinate codon cube $Q_6$, with codons written in the usual nucleotide alphabet and read as six-bit window positions. The observed contact is a curated standard-code table assigning each codon to an amino-acid or stop label. That contact is enough to test local label splits and boundary-tile behavior, but it is not a measurement of translation dynamics, folded structure, physical admissibility, biological function, mechanism, or universality. Every numerical statement below is therefore a finite code-table statement, not a higher-layer biological conclusion.

\paragraph{Shape rarity.}
The shape-significance run used $N=2000$ sampled alternatives with seed $42$. It required $57377$ distinct-sample attempts and therefore encountered $55377$ duplicate retries. Among those alternatives, the exact local shape appeared once, giving
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{\mathrm{exact}}=0.0005.
\end{aligned}
$$
The high-overlap threshold $J\geq 0.9$ was reached $25$ times, giving
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{J\geq 0.9}=0.0125.
\end{aligned}
$$
These values support the narrower statement that the tested local tile is uncommon under the sampled coordinate-preserving comparison. They do not show that the geometry is a biological law, and they do not turn the codon-label table into an account of molecular realization.

\paragraph{Readback discipline.}
The biological readback is only the curated standard-code assignment of codons to amino-acid or stop labels. In this record, the six-bit window geometry, the punctured boundary set $R$, the spectral values $\lambda_M$, $\lambda_R$, and $\mu$, and the leave-one recovery rows jointly support a finite local code-table phenomenon. They do not license claims about translation realization, folded protein structure, physical admissibility, biological function, a universal mechanism, or any necessity imposed on DNA by the coordinate geometry. Crossing any of those boundaries would require a different observation type, such as a translation experiment, a structure contact, a physical constraint measurement, or a function assay. Without such a separate contact, amino-acid label sequences remain label readbacks and not protein structures.

\paragraph{Verdict.}
The finite row is ready as a local codon-window spectral record. It gives an exact $13$-codon boundary set, a punctured-cube face vector $(13,16,4)$, explicit spectral values $\lambda_M=0.6752479308830213$, $\lambda_R=0.4758352843197002$, and $\mu=3.051487585298128$, rare-shape null values $0.0005$ and $0.0125$, an amino-acid-class-preserving lower-tail value $0.0035$, and perfect leave-one-codon recovery in the recorded test. The strongest warranted conclusion is that the standard-code table contains a finite local boundary-tile and spectral pattern under the tested window-six coordinate system. The warranted conclusion stops at code-read structure.
