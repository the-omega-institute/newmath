\subsection{Window-six spectrum for the standard-code boundary tile}
\label{sec:bioreality-h0-spectrum}

\paragraph{Finite carrier and observed label contact.}
The record studied here is the standard-code codon table viewed through a six-coordinate codon display. The carrier is the finite cube $Q_6$, with one binary coordinate for each displayed codon bit. Its empirical contact is only the curated codon-to-label table: each codon is read as an amino-acid label or a stop label. This contact is sufficient for code-layer comparison, local face detection, singleton-corner detection, and three-point label-class detection. It is not an observation of translation dynamics, protein folding, physical admissibility, biological function, mechanism, or universality.

\paragraph{Boundary set.}
The distinguished boundary row is the thirteen-codon set
$$
\begin{aligned}
R=\{&\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\\
&\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UUA}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The finite audit gives $|R|=13$, and the same row is certified as a punctured-cube pattern with $f$-vector $(13,16,4)$. Thus the local object is not merely a list of exceptional codons: it has a finite face incidence profile with thirteen vertices, sixteen edges, and four two-faces in the induced tile. The three stop codons $\mathrm{UAA}$, $\mathrm{UAG}$, and $\mathrm{UGA}$ occur inside this same boundary row, together with codons from arginine, lysine, isoleucine/methionine, leucine, and serine label neighborhoods. The observation remains a code-table statement, because all labels are supplied by the curated table rather than by the cube geometry itself.

\paragraph{Spectral row.}
The spectral check records the three scalars
$$
\begin{aligned}
\lambda_M &= 0.6752479308830213,\\
\lambda_R &= 0.4758352843197002,\\
\mu &= 3.051487585298128.
\end{aligned}
$$
The recorded residual for the cubic relation is $-6.224354365258478\cdot 10^{-12}$, and the radial residual is the same number. The stored spectral-radius value is $0.6752479308830213$, matching $\lambda_M$ within the stated numerical tolerance. These numbers give a finite spectral fingerprint for the observed local split: the larger radius belongs to the twenty-element $M$ side, while the thirteen-element $R$ row has the smaller listed radius. The data do not say that the radius creates biology; they say that the code-table partition has this reproducible numerical profile under the chosen finite construction.

\paragraph{Null comparisons.}
Two finite null checks separate the observed row from random label-preserving alternatives. In the first shape test, $N=2000$ samples were requested, with $57377$ distinct-sample attempts and $55377$ duplicate retries. Exactly one sample matched the exact shape, giving $p_{\mathrm{exact}}=0.0005$. Twenty-five samples reached Jaccard similarity at least $0.9$, giving $p_{\mathrm{Jaccard}\ge 0.9}=0.0125$. In the amino-acid-class-preserving null check, again with $N=2000$, the observed $M$ size was $20$, the exact-match count was $0$, and the lower-tail count at or below the observed $M$ size was $7$. The resulting values are $p_{\mathrm{class,exact}}=0.0$ and $p_{\mathrm{class,lower\ tail}}=0.0035$. These finite counts support the claim that the listed local boundary is not a typical row under the tested randomizations, while leaving all mechanistic biological claims outside the present evidence.

\paragraph{Leave-one-code and leave-one-codon probes.}
The leave-one-code experiment evaluated $23$ code tables against $63$ targets. Its baseline hit rate was $0.19821739130434785$. The hit rate inside $M\setminus T$ was $0.9365079365079365$, while the hit rate after adding the boundary was $0.9841269841269841$; the same $0.9841269841269841$ value is recorded as the hit rate above baseline. The leave-one-codon experiment records $0$ anchors, $5$ boundary codons, $8$ high-coverage closure cases, $8$ predictable cases, and recovery rate $1.0$. Thus, within these finite probes, the boundary row acts as a strong code-layer recovery aid. This still means recovery of codon labels in a finite table experiment, not recovery of translation, folded structure, function, or any global biological rule.

\paragraph{Family and module decomposition.}
The by-family code-out probe groups the rows into $5$ families. The hit-rate-above-baseline values are $0.8333333333333334$ for alternative yeast, $1.0$ for bacterial and plastid tables, $1.0$ for candidate other tables, $1.0$ for mitochondrial tables, and $1.0$ for nuclear ciliate tables. The thirteen codons are also completely assigned to six named modules:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{agr} &= \{\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG}\},\\
\mathrm{ar\_lys} &= \{\mathrm{AAA}\},\\
\mathrm{cun} &= \{\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU}\},\\
\mathrm{ile\_met} &= \{\mathrm{AUA}\},\\
\mathrm{ssn} &= \{\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UUA}\},\\
\mathrm{stop\_trp} &= \{\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UGA}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The module audit reports $R$ size $13$, assigned count $13$, no extra module codons, no codons missing from modules, and no unassigned codons. Each module has a predictable or boundary explanation in the finite probe. This supplies a clean local decomposition of the boundary row, but it is still only a decomposition of code-table labels inside the six-coordinate display.

\paragraph{Local conclusion and boundary of interpretation.}
The data support a narrow statement: window-six codon coordinates expose a reproducible local boundary tile in the standard-code label table, with a thirteen-codon punctured-cube row, spectral values $\lambda_M=0.6752479308830213$, $\lambda_R=0.4758352843197002$, and $\mu=3.051487585298128$, nontrivial null-test counts, and complete module assignment. The empirical contact is a curated genetic-code table, so the claim is confined to code-layer readback. Nothing in this row proves that DNA is forced to follow this geometry, that the local tile is a biological law, that a label sequence folds into a protein structure, or that translation, admissibility, function, mechanism, or universality has been observed. Any such stronger assertion would require an additional contact at the relevant biological layer.
