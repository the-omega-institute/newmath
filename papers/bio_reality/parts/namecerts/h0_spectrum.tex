\paragraph{Reality contact and scope.}
The only reality contact used here is the curated standard-code table, whose observed fact is that each codon receives an amino-acid or stop label.  That contact can test code-layer readback and the local split of labels across the tile.  It cannot test translation realization, folded structure, physical admissibility, biological function, or a universal mechanism.  Consequently the scientific reading is narrow: window-six coordinates expose a local boundary pattern in the standard-code label table, with a thirteen-codon punctured cubical region, a reproducible spectral signature, significant finite null behavior, and complete module accounting.  No claim about protein folding, molecular feasibility, organism-level function, or necessity across biology follows from these rows unless a separate experiment or curated contact supplies that higher-layer observation.
