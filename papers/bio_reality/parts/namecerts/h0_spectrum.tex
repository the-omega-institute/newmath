\subsection{Window-six spectral boundary for the standard codon table}
\label{sec:bioreality-h0-spectrum}

\paragraph{Finite object under test.}
This record concerns the standard-code codon table only at the code-read layer.  The finite carrier is the six-bit codon cube $Q_6$, read through a curated codon-to-label table whose entries assign each codon to an amino-acid label or a stop label.  The distinctions used here are coordinate distinctions in the codon display, local tile faces in the cube, singleton label corners, and three-point label classes.  The biological contact is therefore narrow: it is a checked label table, not a measurement of translation dynamics, folded structure, thermodynamic admissibility, cellular function, evolutionary necessity, or a universal mechanism.

\paragraph{Interpretation at the code layer.}
The finite evidence supports a precise local statement: the window-six codon coordinates expose a boundary-tile organization whose boundary set, module decomposition, spectral scalars, and readback recovery rates are all recorded by finite rows.  The curated standard code supplies the label contact that lets this tile be read as codon labels.  The contact does not observe ribosomal realization, protein folding, physical admissibility, biological function, or universality across mechanisms.  Any claim crossing from code labels into translation, structure, admissibility, function, mechanism, or universal biological law requires a separate empirical contact designed for that layer.  Without that separate contact, amino-acid label sequences remain code-layer outputs, not folded proteins and not function-bearing objects.
