\subsection{Window-Six Spectral Readback for the Standard Code}\label{sec:bioreality-h0-spectrum}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite object.}
The record concerns the standard-code codon table only at the label-readback layer. The finite carrier is the six-coordinate codon cube $Q_6$, where each codon is represented by a six-bit display of three nucleotide positions. The observed labels are the curated amino-acid or stop assignments of the standard code. The construction separates coordinate information, closure information, spectral information, and the relation between a local tile and its boundary. It does not treat the table as a mechanism for translation, and it does not infer folded structure or biological function from the codon labels.

\paragraph{Boundary set.}
The distinguished boundary relation $R$ has cardinality $13$. The verified codons in $R$ are
$$
\begin{aligned}
R=\{&AAA, AGA, AGG, AUA, CUA, CUC, CUG, CUU,\\
&UAA, UAG, UCA, UGA, UUA\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The same row also verifies that this set has the punctured-cube face data
$$
\begin{aligned}
f(R)=(13,16,4),
\end{aligned}
$$
so the boundary is not merely a list of exceptional codons. It is a finite local shape inside the codon cube: thirteen vertices, sixteen one-dimensional incidences, and four two-dimensional faces after the puncture is imposed. The singleton and three-point label phenomena are read here as local table features, not as a claim that the full biology must obey this geometry.

\paragraph{Spectral row.}
The spectral calculation attached to the same finite carrier gives
$$
\begin{aligned}
\lambda_M&=0.6752479308830213,\\
\lambda_R&=0.4758352843197002,\\
\mu&=3.051487585298128.
\end{aligned}
$$
The reported residual for the radial cubic relation is
$$
\begin{aligned}
-6.224354365258478\cdot 10^{-12},
\end{aligned}
$$
which is within numerical tolerance for the finite computation. In this chapter the spectral row is used only as a compact invariant of the local codon split. It says that the observed boundary tile has a stable numerical signature under the selected finite operator; it does not say that ribosomes, tRNAs, folding pathways, or selection dynamics are generated by that operator.

\paragraph{Shape significance.}
Against the finite null sample for the local shape, the run used $N=2000$ sampled tables with seed $42$. It required $57377$ distinct-sample attempts and recorded $55377$ duplicate retries. The exact shape appeared once, giving
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{\mathrm{exact}}=0.0005.
\end{aligned}
$$
High-Jaccard agreement at threshold $0.9$ occurred $25$ times, giving
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{J\geq 0.9}=0.0125.
\end{aligned}
$$
These values support the narrower statement that the observed local tile is not a typical draw under that finite comparison. They do not convert the local codon display into a general biological law.

\paragraph{Amino-acid-class preserving null.}
A second null row preserves amino-acid class information. In that run, again with $N=2000$ and seed $42$, the observed $M$-size was $20$. The exact-shape count was $0$, while $7$ samples lay in the lower tail at or below the observed $M$-size. The reported values are
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{\mathrm{class\ exact}}&=0.0,\\
p_{\mathrm{class\ lower\ tail}}&=0.0035.
\end{aligned}
$$
This is stronger than the unrestricted exact-shape row for the specific question it tests: even after class preservation, the finite local shape remains unusual in the sampled comparison. The interpretation is still confined to code-label readback. No structural-order, physical-admissibility, or functional conclusion is licensed by this row.

\paragraph{Leave-one-code-out behavior.}
The leave-one-code-out probe evaluated $23$ code tables and $63$ targets. Its baseline hit rate was
$$
\begin{aligned}
0.19821739130434785,
\end{aligned}
$$
whereas the rate above baseline was
$$
\begin{aligned}
0.9841269841269841.
\end{aligned}
$$
The hit rate inside $M-T$ was $0.9365079365079365$, and the rate inside $M-T$ together with the boundary was $0.9841269841269841$. The family-level row reports five families. Their rates above baseline are $0.8333333333333334$ for alternative yeast and $1.0$ for bacterial-plastid, candidate-other, mitochondrial, and nuclear-ciliate families. These numbers show that the local coordinate-and-boundary packet tracks many code-read changes under the tested omissions. They remain readback statistics on curated tables, not evidence for translation realization or a universal mechanism.

\paragraph{Leave-one-codon-out behavior.}
The leave-one-codon-out probe reports $8$ predictable codons, $5$ boundary codons, no anchor codons, high closure coverage $8$, and recovery rate $1.0$. The module classification is complete on the same thirteen-codon boundary: $R$ has size $13$, all $13$ codons are assigned, and there are no extra module codons, missing module codons, or unassigned codons. The modules are
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{agr}&=\{AGA,AGG\},\\
\mathrm{ar\_lys}&=\{AAA\},\\
\mathrm{cun}&=\{CUA,CUC,CUG,CUU\},\\
\mathrm{ile\_met}&=\{AUA\},\\
\mathrm{ssn}&=\{UCA,UUA\},\\
\mathrm{stop\_trp}&=\{UAA,UAG,UGA\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
Every listed module has either predictable support or boundary support in the finite row. This is the main reason the spectrum row is recorded with the boundary and module rows rather than as an isolated numerical curiosity.

\paragraph{Local interpretation.}
The scientific content of the packet is therefore narrow and finite. The six-bit codon display exposes a local tile whose boundary set has thirteen specified codons, punctured-cube face vector $(13,16,4)$, spectral values $\lambda_M=0.6752479308830213$, $\lambda_R=0.4758352843197002$, and $\mu=3.051487585298128$, and nontrivial behavior under two finite null comparisons. The curated standard-code table is the contact that supplies codon-to-label assignments. It supplies no observation of translation machinery, folded protein structure, physical admissibility, biological function, cross-organism universality, or mechanistic necessity. Any such higher-layer claim would require a separate contact designed for that layer.
