\paragraph{Reality contact and scope.}
The only contact used here is a curated standard genetic-code table assigning codons to amino-acid or stop labels.  That contact can test codon-label readback and a local split of labels on a finite codon tile.  It cannot test translation realization, folded structure, physical admissibility, biological function, or a universal mechanism.  In particular, amino-acid label sequences are not folded proteins, and a local window-six boundary is not evidence that all organisms, all code variants, or all biochemical processes obey the same rule.  Crossing from this code-read result to translation, folding, physical chemistry, organismal function, or universality would require a separate contact designed for that layer.  The present record is therefore a finite, reproducible code-table signature: $R$ has thirteen listed codons, the punctured-cube face vector is $(13,16,4)$, the spectral row has $\lambda_M=0.6752479308830213$ and $\lambda_R=0.4758352843197002$, and the recovery and null rows support a local boundary-tile phenomenon without promoting it beyond codon-label readback.
