\subsection{Window-Six Codon Spectrum and Code-Layer Boundary}\label{subsec:h0-spectrum-codon-window-six}\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite codon carrier.}
The record considered here is a finite code-layer object: the six-bit codon cube $Q_6$, with each codon read as a point in a binary coordinate display and then labelled by the curated standard genetic-code table. The biological contact used in this subsection is deliberately narrow. It says that the standard table assigns each codon an amino-acid or stop label; it does not say that the coordinate geometry is a law of biology, nor that a local tile realizes translation, folded structure, physical admissibility, function, mechanism, or universality. The admissible question is therefore only whether the finite coordinate display, together with the externally supplied labels, exhibits a reproducible local boundary pattern.

\paragraph{Boundary set.}
The distinguished boundary set $R$ has cardinality $13$. The verified member list is
$$
\begin{aligned}
R = \{&\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\\
&\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UUA}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The same finite row certifies that $R$ is the punctured-cube boundary with face vector
$$
\begin{aligned}
(f_0,f_1,f_2)=(13,16,4).
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the claim is not that the whole code is generated by a physical mechanism. It is the smaller assertion that, after the codons are placed in the six-coordinate display and labelled by the standard code table, these thirteen codons form a specific local boundary tile.

The spectral row gives three numerical parameters:

\lambda_R &= 0.4758352843197002,\\
\mu &= 3.051487585298128.

$$
The reported spectral radius is again $0.6752479308830213$, and the finite computation evaluates the radial cubic relation to

\begin{aligned}

\end{aligned}
$$
At the precision of the run, this is a numerical zero certificate for the displayed cubic relation. The role of this number is internal to the finite codon display: it records that the measured local spectrum agrees with the claimed algebraic constraint. It is not a claim about ribosomal dynamics, amino-acid chemistry, folding, or organism-level fitness.

\paragraph{Shape significance.}
The finite null experiment for the local shape used $N=2000$ samples with seed $42$. It required $57377$ distinct-sample attempts and encountered $55377$ duplicate retries. Exactly one sampled object matched the observed shape, giving
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{\mathrm{exact}}=0.0005.
\end{aligned}
$$
There were $25$ samples with Jaccard score at least $0.9$, giving
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{\mathrm{Jaccard}\ge 0.9}=0.0125.
\end{aligned}
$$
A second null row preserving amino-acid classes also used $N=2000$ and seed $42$. In that row the observed set size for $M$ was $20$, the exact-match count was $0$, and the lower-tail count at or below the observed $M$ size was $7$. The corresponding reported values were
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{\mathrm{class\ exact}} &= 0.0,\\
p_{\mathrm{class\ lower\ tail}} &= 0.0035.
\end{aligned}
$$
These rows make the boundary tile statistically nontrivial within the tested finite comparison classes. They still do not promote the tile to a biological mechanism; they only support the code-layer geometry visible through the curated label table.

\paragraph{Local verdict.}
The record is ready as a code-read claim. The finite evidence supports a local codon-window boundary with $|R|=13$, face vector $(13,16,4)$, spectral parameters $\lambda_M=0.6752479308830213$, $\lambda_R=0.4758352843197002$, and $\mu=3.051487585298128$, and nontrivial null-model behavior with exact-shape probability $0.0005$ in the shape null and exact amino-acid-class-preserving probability $0.0$ in the class-preserving null. No conclusion about translation realization, folded protein structure, physical admissibility, biological function, mechanism, or universal biological law follows from these rows unless a separate contact is supplied for that higher layer.
