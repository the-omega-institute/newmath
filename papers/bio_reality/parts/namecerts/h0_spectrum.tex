\subsection{Window-Six Spectrum for the Standard Code}

\paragraph{Claim record.}
The claim recorded here is the spectrum row $h0.spectrum$ for the window-six codon display.  The finite carrier is the codon cube $Q_6$, read as a six-bit coordinate presentation of the standard-code codon table.  The biological contact used in this row is only the curated assignment of codons to amino-acid or stop labels.  The mathematical observation is therefore a code-layer observation: a local coordinate pattern can be measured against the standard label table, but the row does not observe translation, folding, physical admissibility, biological function, a mechanism of code evolution, or a universal biological law.

The complementary measured block $M$ is recorded by size in the null-model rows as $|M|=20$; no finite row supplied a full ordered list of the $20$ $M$ codons, so the present chapter uses only the recorded size and the spectral scalars attached to it.

\paragraph{Scope of the result.}
The verified content is a finite mathematical contact with the standard-code table: the codon cube $Q_6$ contains a local boundary tile $R$ of size $13$, that tile has face vector $(13,16,4)$, its spectral profile has $\lambda_M=0.6752479308830213$, $\lambda_R=0.4758352843197002$, and $\mu=3.051487585298128$, and its observed shape is rare in the recorded null comparisons. The row is ready as a code-read chapter because all claims are confined to finite codon-label data and finite coordinate computations.

\paragraph{Boundary of interpretation.}
No higher-layer conclusion follows from this chapter alone.  The curated standard-code table supplies codon-to-label assignments; it does not supply translation kinetics, ribosomal realization, protein folding, thermodynamic admissibility, organism-level function, evolutionary mechanism, or universality.  Any claim crossing one of those boundaries would require a separate contact that directly measures the higher layer.  Until such a contact is attached, the correct reading is narrower: window-six coordinates expose a statistically unusual local tile in the standard-code label table, with a reproducible spectral profile and strong finite readback behavior under the stated probes.
