\end{aligned}$$

$$\begin{aligned}

\end{aligned}$$

These values support the claim that the observed row geometry is uncommon under the recorded finite shuffles.  They do not convert the geometry into a physical law.  The curated table is the contact for labels; the null models compare finite label arrangements; neither source supplies an experimental observation of translation dynamics, folding, energetic admissibility, organismal function, or universality across life.
