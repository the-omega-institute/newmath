\paragraph{Finite object.}
The record concerns the standard-code codon table viewed through a six-coordinate codon display.  Each codon is treated as a point of the codon cube $Q_6$, and the only biological contact used here is the curated table assigning codons to amino-acid or stop labels.  This contact is sufficient for code-layer label readback and for local tile tests.  It is not a translation experiment, not a folding experiment, not a physical-admissibility assay, not a function assay, and not evidence for a universal biological mechanism.

\paragraph{Scope boundary.}
The finite evidence shows that a window-six coordinate display can expose a structured local boundary tile in the standard-code label table.  The contact used here observes codon-to-label assignments and nothing beyond that.  Therefore the chapter may claim a code-layer spectral and combinatorial pattern: $R$ is the recorded $13$-codon punctured-cube row, the modules cover $R$ exactly, the spectral residual is approximately zero, and the sampled null rows make the observed shape rare.  The chapter may not claim that this tile is a global law of biology, that it realizes translation, that it predicts folded structure, that it establishes physical admissibility, that it determines function, or that it supplies a universal mechanism.  Any such higher-layer statement would require a separate empirical contact of the appropriate kind.
