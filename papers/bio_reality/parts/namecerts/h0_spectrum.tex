\subsection{Window-Six Spectral Boundary for the Standard-Code Table}\label{sec:bioreality-h0-spectrum}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite carrier and observed row.}
The object tested here is the standard-code codon table, read only as a codon-to-label table. The finite carrier is the six-coordinate codon cube $Q_6$: each codon is treated as a six-bit display position, and the observed table supplies the amino-acid or stop label attached to that position. This subsection records a local boundary phenomenon inside that finite carrier. It does not treat the codon table as a dynamical translation system, a protein-folding oracle, a physical chemistry model, a functional assay, or a universal law of biology.

The reality contact is a curated standard-code table. Its role is narrow: it tells us that codons have labels in the standard code. It does not observe ribosomal realization, structural order, admissible molecular conformations, or organism-level function. The tested layer is therefore code-label readback. All stronger biological interpretations remain outside the scope of this finite row unless a different contact supplies them.

\paragraph{The boundary set $R$.}
The distinguished boundary row is a set $R$ of thirteen codons. The verified cardinality row gives
$$
\begin{aligned}
|R| &= 13.
\end{aligned}
$$
The listed codons are
$$
\begin{aligned}
R = \{&\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\\
&\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UUA}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
A second verification identifies the same row as a punctured-cube pattern with face data
$$
\begin{aligned}
f(R)=(13,16,4).
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the finite phenomenon is not merely the number thirteen; it is the joint occurrence of the thirteen named codons, sixteen local adjacencies, and four local two-faces in the induced tile. The row is a local geometric boundary inside $Q_6$, not a claim that DNA must obey this geometry as a biological necessity.

\lambda_R &= 0.4758352843197002,\\

$$
The recorded residual for the radial cubic relation is
$$
\begin{aligned}

$$
with the same numerical value recorded for the cubic-polynomial check. At the reported tolerance, the spectral radius entry is $0.6752479308830213$, matching $\lambda_M$. This makes the row a concrete finite spectral observation: the local codon split has a stable numerical signature, but the signature is attached only to the codon-label table and the chosen window-six coordinates.

\paragraph{Shape comparison against finite null rows.}
The main shape comparison used $N=2000$ sampled rows with seed $42$. The sampling log records $57377$ distinct-sample attempts and $55377$ duplicate retries. Among those $2000$ sampled rows, the exact shape matched once, giving
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{\mathrm{exact}} &= 0.0005.
\end{aligned}
$$
The high-overlap comparison at Jaccard threshold $0.9$ matched $25$ times, giving
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{J\geq 0.9} &= 0.0125.
\end{aligned}
$$
A second null that preserves amino-acid classes also used $N=2000$ and seed $42$. In that row, the observed $M$ size is $20$, the exact-match count is $0$, and the lower-tail count at or below the observed $M$ size is $7$. The corresponding values are
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{\mathrm{aa\ class\ exact}} &= 0.0,\\
p_{\mathrm{aa\ class\ lower\ tail}} &= 0.0035.
\end{aligned}
$$
These comparisons support a finite-code statement: the observed local tile is uncommon under the sampled alternatives recorded here. They do not identify a causal mechanism, and they do not promote the local split into translation, folding, function, physical admissibility, or universality.

\paragraph{Leave-one readback checks.}
The leave-one-code-out row evaluated $23$ code tables and $63$ total targets. Its baseline hit rate is $0.19821739130434785$. The hit rate above baseline is $0.9841269841269841$, the hit rate inside $M\setminus T$ is $0.9365079365079365$, and the hit rate inside $M\setminus T$ together with the boundary is $0.9841269841269841$. These numbers show that the local code-layer pattern has predictive strength for held-out codon-label rows in the tested finite regime.

The leave-one-codon-out row records $0$ anchors, $5$ boundary cases, $8$ high-closure-coverage cases, $8$ predictable cases, and recovery rate $1.0$. The recovery value is therefore not an asymptotic biological law; it is a complete recovery result for this finite leave-one-codon-out table under the recorded classifier.

The thirteen codons in $R$ are completely assigned to six named modules. The assignment is
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{agr} &= \{\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG}\},\\
\mathrm{ar\_lys} &= \{\mathrm{AAA}\},\\
\mathrm{cun} &= \{\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU}\},\\

$$
The completeness row reports $R$ size $13$, assigned count $13$, no extra module codons, no missing codons from the modules, and no unassigned codons. Thus every codon in the boundary row is accounted for by the finite module partition.

The family-level leave-one-code-out row has $5$ families. The hit-rate-above-baseline values are $0.8333333333333334$ for alternative yeast, $1.0$ for bacterial-plastid, $1.0$ for candidate-other, $1.0$ for mitochondrial, and $1.0$ for nuclear-ciliate. These family rows are still code-table rows: they compare label behavior across curated code tables and do not by themselves observe translation machinery, protein structure, molecular admissibility, function, or universal mechanism.

\paragraph{Local conclusion.}
The finite evidence supports the following chapter-local claim. In the six-coordinate codon cube, the standard-code label table contains a distinguished thirteen-codon boundary row with punctured-cube face vector $(13,16,4)$, spectral values $\lambda_M=0.6752479308830213$, $\lambda_R=0.4758352843197002$, and $\mu=3.051487585298128$, and nontrivial finite-null separation under the recorded sampled alternatives. The row is stable enough to function as a code-layer local tile, and the leave-one checks show strong finite recovery behavior within the recorded table regime.

The same evidence also fixes the boundary of the claim. The curated code table supplies codon labels, not folded proteins, physical realizability, biological function, translation dynamics, or a universal biological principle. Amino-acid label readback is therefore not structural readback. Any move from this local code-layer tile to translation, folding, admissibility, function, mechanism, or universality requires a separate contact with the relevant biological layer.
