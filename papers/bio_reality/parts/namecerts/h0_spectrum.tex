\paragraph{Scope of the claim.}
The finite row supports the following restrained statement. In the standard-code table, window-six codon coordinates expose a local boundary-tile pattern with a thirteen-codon punctured-cube boundary, a recorded spectral tuple, rare finite-null shape behavior, and strong leave-out recovery at the code-read layer. The curated code table is the reality contact for labels; it is not a contact for ribosomal translation dynamics, protein folding, thermodynamic admissibility, organismal function, or universality. Any claim about those higher layers would require a separate observation source with the relevant resolution. Without such a contact, amino-acid label sequences remain label sequences, and the spectral codon tile remains a local finite code-table record.
