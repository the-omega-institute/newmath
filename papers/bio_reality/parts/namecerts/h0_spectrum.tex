\subsection{Window-six spectral record for the standard-code label table}\label{sec:h0-spectrum-window-six-spectral-record}\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite carrier and observed boundary row.}
This chapter records a finite code-layer packet built on the six-bit codon cube $Q_6$.  The carrier is the codon display in which each nucleotide coordinate contributes a two-bit position, so that the sixty-four codons form a cube-shaped finite coordinate space.  The biological contact used here is only the curated standard-code table: it gives a codon-to-label assignment into amino-acid or stop labels.  The packet therefore concerns code readback and local tile geometry, not translation as a realized biochemical process.

The distinguished boundary row is the thirteen-codon set
$$\begin{aligned}
R=\{&AAA, AGA, AGG, AUA, CUA, CUC, CUG, CUU,\\
&UAA, UAG, UCA, UGA, UUA\}.
\end{aligned}$$
The finite audit records $|R|=13$.  As a subcomplex of the window-six cube, the same row is observed as a punctured-cube tile with face vector
$$\begin{aligned}
f(R)&=(13,16,4).
\end{aligned}$$
Thus the row is not merely a list of exceptional labels; it has a finite local incidence pattern with thirteen vertices, sixteen one-dimensional adjacencies, and four two-dimensional faces.  The row includes the stop cluster $\{UAA,UAG,UGA\}$, the arginine pair $\{AGA,AGG\}$, the leucine-family block $\{CUA,CUC,CUG,CUU,UUA\}$, the isoleucine-methionine corner $\{AUA\}$, the lysine-side corner $\{AAA\}$, and the serine-side point $\{UCA\}$.

$$\begin{aligned}

\lambda_R&=0.4758352843197002,\\

\end{aligned}$$
The same run evaluates the radial cubic residual as
$$\begin{aligned}
P_{\mathrm{rad}}(\mu)&=-6.224354365258478\cdot 10^{-12}.
\end{aligned}$$
The reported cubic residual and radial residual coincide at this numerical precision.  The spectral radius recorded for the measurement is again
$$\begin{aligned}
\rho&=0.6752479308830213,
\end{aligned}$$
matching $\lambda_M$ within the stated tolerance.  The local reading is therefore a finite spectral fact about the code table under the selected window-six coordinates: the $M$-side radius is larger than the $R$-row radius, while the row still satisfies the same cubic constraint numerically up to a residual of about $6.23\cdot 10^{-12}$ in absolute value.

\paragraph{Shape rarity under finite null sampling.}
The shape-significance run used $N=2000$ sampled tables with seed $42$.  To obtain those distinct samples it made $57377$ sample attempts and encountered $55377$ duplicate retries.  Among the $2000$ accepted samples, exactly one had the exact observed shape, giving
$$\begin{aligned}
p_{\mathrm{exact}}&=0.0005.
\end{aligned}$$
The high-overlap threshold $J\geq 0.9$ was met by $25$ samples, giving
$$\begin{aligned}
p_{J\geq 0.9}&=0.0125.
\end{aligned}$$
This does not turn the tile into a law of biology.  It says that, relative to the finite sampling scheme used in this packet, the exact window-six shape is rare and close variants are also uncommon.  The amino-acid-class-preserving null run gives a sharper code-layer check: with $N=2000$ and seed $42$, the observed $M$-side size is $20$, the exact-shape count is $0$, and only $7$ samples lie in the lower tail at or below the observed $M$ size.  The corresponding finite values are
$$\begin{aligned}
p_{\mathrm{aa\ class\ exact}}&=0.0,\\
p_{\mathrm{aa\ class\ lower\ tail}}&=0.0035.
\end{aligned}$$
These numbers are finite-run statistics, not asymptotic biological inevitability claims.  They support the statement that the standard-code label table is unusually aligned with the selected local coordinate geometry under these tested null routes.

\paragraph{Scope of the claim.}
The result is a code-read statement about a curated standard-code label table viewed through the six-bit codon cube.  The contact supplies codon labels and stop labels; it does not supply ribosomal dynamics, tRNA charging, protein folding, thermodynamic admissibility, organism-level function, or a universal mechanism.  Consequently no claim is made here that window-six geometry causes translation, determines folded protein structure, proves physical realizability, explains biological function, or holds as a global law across biology.  Any such higher-layer assertion would need a separate reality contact of the appropriate kind.  Within the present data, the defensible conclusion is narrower and finite: the standard-code label table contains a thirteen-codon local boundary row with punctured-cube incidence vector $(13,16,4)$, spectral values $\lambda_M=0.6752479308830213$, $\lambda_R=0.4758352843197002$, and $\mu=3.051487585298128$, rare null-sampling shape behavior, and strong leave-one-out recovery at the code-label layer.
