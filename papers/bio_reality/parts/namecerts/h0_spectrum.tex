\subsection{Window-six spectral row for the standard-code codon table}
\label{sec:biorality-h0-spectrum}

\paragraph{Finite setting.}
This record studies the standard-code codon table only at the code-read layer.  The carrier is the six-coordinate codon cube $Q_6$, with codons displayed as finite six-bit words induced by the three nucleotide positions.  The observed contact is a curated standard genetic-code table assigning each codon to an amino-acid label or a stop label.  The row does not treat the table as a mechanism of translation; it treats it as a finite label assignment on the cube.  The claim tested here is that the window-six coordinate presentation exposes a local boundary tile whose spectral and combinatorial profile is too sharp to be dismissed as a generic relabelling artifact.

\paragraph{Boundary set.}
The finite boundary set $R$ has cardinality $13$.  In the verified row \texttt{h0.R.cardinality.13}, the codons are
$$
\begin{aligned}
R=\{&AAA, AGA, AGG, AUA, CUA, CUC, CUG, CUU,\\
&UAA, UAG, UCA, UGA, UUA\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The companion row \texttt{h0.R.punctured.cube} verifies that the same set is the punctured-cube boundary instance used by this packet, with face-vector
$$
\begin{aligned}
(f_0,f_1,f_2)=(13,16,4).
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the row is not only a list of exceptional codons; it is a finite cubical tile with thirteen vertices, sixteen one-dimensional incidences, and four two-dimensional faces.  The relevant biological readback is still just codon label readback: for example, the row distinguishes singleton-like corners, local tile faces, and three-point label classes inside the curated table, but it does not assert that these finite cube features are causal biology.

\paragraph{Spectral certificate.}
The spectral row \texttt{h0.spectrum} reports three numerical invariants for the local tile: $\lambda_M=0.6752479308830213$, $\lambda_R=0.4758352843197002$, and $\mu=3.051487585298128$.  The same run evaluates the radial cubic residual as
$$
\begin{aligned}
P(\mu)=-6.224354365258478\cdot 10^{-12}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The recorded residual is numerically negligible at the stated tolerance scale, and the spectral-radius entry repeats $0.6752479308830213$ for the $M$-side value.  The point of this certificate is narrow: the tile has a reproducible finite-row spectral signature under the chosen coordinate construction.  It is not a claim that the spectral radius is a biochemical energy, a folding parameter, or a translation-rate observable.

\paragraph{Shape significance against finite null rows.}
The row \texttt{h0.M.shape.significance} compares the observed shape against $N=2000$ sampled alternatives with seed $42$.  The sampling required $57377$ distinct-sample attempts and incurred $55377$ duplicate retries.  Only $1$ sample matched the exact observed shape, giving
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{\mathrm{exact}}=0.0005.
\end{aligned}
$$
There were $25$ samples with Jaccard score at least $0.9$, giving
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{J\geq 0.9}=0.0125.
\end{aligned}
$$
A stricter amino-acid-class-preserving comparison is recorded in \texttt{h0.null.model.aa\_class\_preserving}.  In that row the observed $M$-size is $20$, the exact-match count is $0$ across $N=2000$ samples, the lower-tail count at or below the observed size is $7$, and the corresponding probabilities are
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{\mathrm{class,exact}}&=0.0,\\
p_{\mathrm{class,lower}}&=0.0035.
\end{aligned}
$$
These finite null checks support the statement that the observed local tile is statistically sharp relative to the sampled comparison families.  They do not by themselves select an evolutionary explanation.

\paragraph{Leave-one readback tests.}
The leave-one-code-out row \texttt{h1.leave.one.code.out} evaluates $23$ code tables and $63$ total targets.  Its baseline hit rate is $0.19821739130434785$, while the hit rate inside $M\setminus T$ is $0.9365079365079365$.  Adding the boundary raises the hit rate to $0.9841269841269841$, and the row records the same value for hit rate above baseline:
$$
\begin{aligned}
h_{\mathrm{baseline}}&=0.19821739130434785,\\
h_{M\setminus T}&=0.9365079365079365,\\
h_{M\setminus T+\partial}&=0.9841269841269841.
\end{aligned}
$$
The leave-one-codon-out row \texttt{h1.leave.one.codon.out} records no anchors, $5$ boundary cases, $8$ high-closure-coverage cases, $8$ predictable cases, and recovery rate $1.0$.  These values are useful because they test whether the tile persists when one codon or one code table is removed from the readback procedure.  The finite rows say that the local code-layer pattern is recoverable under those omissions; they do not say that a ribosome, tRNA population, or protein structure has been reconstructed.

\paragraph{Family and module decomposition.}
The by-family row \texttt{h1.leave\_one\_code\_out.by\_family} partitions the leave-one-code-out evidence into $5$ families.  The hit rates above baseline are $0.8333333333333334$ for alternative yeast, and $1.0$ for bacterial/plastid, candidate-other, mitochondrial, and nuclear-ciliate families.  The module-classification row \texttt{h1.leave\_one\_codon\_out.module\_classification} assigns all $13$ codons of $R$ with no missing or extra module codons:
$$
\begin{aligned}
R_{\mathrm{size}}&=13,\\
\mathrm{assigned}&=13,\\
\mathrm{missing}&=0,\\
\mathrm{extra}&=0.
\end{aligned}
$$
The modules are $\{AGA,AGG\}$ for the AGR block, $\{AAA\}$ for the AR/Lys block, $\{CUA,CUC,CUG,CUU\}$ for the CUN block, $\{AUA\}$ for the Ile/Met block, $\{UCA,UUA\}$ for the SSN block, and $\{UAA,UAG,UGA\}$ for the stop/Trp block.  Every listed module has a predictable or boundary role in the finite classification.  This decomposition makes the row interpretable as a codon-table phenomenon rather than as an undifferentiated numerical coincidence.

\paragraph{Local conclusion and boundary of use.}
The evidence supports a bounded conclusion: the standard-code table, read as a finite codon-to-label assignment on $Q_6$, contains a local boundary tile with $13$ codons, face-vector $(13,16,4)$, a stable spectral signature, significant finite-null shape behavior, high leave-one recovery, and complete module classification.  The contact supplies code labels only.  Therefore no claim about realized translation, folded protein structure, physical admissibility, biological function, generative mechanism, or universal biological law follows from this row.  Any such stronger claim would require a separate observation at the appropriate biological layer, not merely the local codon-tile pattern described here.
