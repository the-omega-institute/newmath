\subsection{Local interpretation and exclusions}
\label{sec:bioreality-h0-spectrum-local-interpretation-exclusions}

\paragraph{What the contact supports.}
The curated standard-code table supports codon-to-label readback.  Within that contact, the six-bit codon display, the thirteen-codon boundary set, the punctured-cube face vector, the spectral constants, the randomization rows, and the leave-one-out rows jointly describe a local boundary-tile phenomenon in the code layer.  The finite evidence is strongest where it remains finite: exact membership of $R$, complete module assignment of $R$, rare sampled shape statistics, and high recovery rates in the stated deletion and alternate-code protocols.

\paragraph{What the contact does not support.}
No translation realization follows from this row.  No folded protein structure follows from an amino-acid label sequence.  No physical admissibility, cellular function, universal mechanism, or global law follows from the coordinate tile.  The code table is a label bridge for the code-read layer; it is not a structural experiment, a kinetic assay, a thermodynamic model, or an organism-level functional contact.  Any future claim that crosses one of those boundaries must bring an additional empirical contact appropriate to that layer.

The row is ready as a finite code-layer record.
