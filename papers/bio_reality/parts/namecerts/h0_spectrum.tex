\paragraph{Claim and finite carrier.}
The claim \texttt{h0.spectrum} records a window-six analysis of the standard-code codon table at the code-read layer. The finite carrier is the six-bit codon cube $Q_6$, used here only as a coordinate display for the $64$ codon positions. A curated standard-code table supplies the codon-to-label contact: each codon is read as an amino-acid or stop label according to the curated table, and the geometry is then asked whether the labels expose a local boundary-tile pattern. The object under study is therefore not translation as a biochemical process, and not protein realization; it is a finite codon-label table viewed through a six-coordinate window.

\paragraph{Observed boundary set.}
The verified boundary set $R$ has cardinality $13$. Its codons are $AAA$, $AGA$, $AGG$, $AUA$, $CUA$, $CUC$, $CUG$, $CUU$, $UAA$, $UAG$, $UCA$, $UGA$, and $UUA$. The same finite row verifies that this $R$ is the punctured-cube boundary detected by the coordinate construction, with face-vector data
$$
\begin{aligned}
(f_0,f_1,f_2) &= (13,16,4).
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the observed object is a small local tile: thirteen codons, sixteen one-dimensional incidences, and four two-dimensional faces. The codons concentrate in recognizable code-table modules: the $AGR$ pair is $AGA,AGG$; the $AR$-lysine singleton is $AAA$; the $CUN$ block is $CUA,CUC,CUG,CUU$; the isoleucine/methionine singleton is $AUA$; the $SSN$ pair is $UCA,UUA$; and the stop/tryptophan block is $UAA,UAG,UGA$. The module-classification row is complete: $R$ has size $13$, the assigned count is $13$, and there are no extra module codons, missing module codons, or unassigned codons.

\paragraph{Shape controls.}
The shape-significance row used $N=2000$ sampled controls with seed $42$. It reports $57377$ distinct-sample attempts and $55377$ duplicate retries. Among the controls, there was $1$ exact shape match and $25$ matches with Jaccard similarity at least $0.9$. The corresponding empirical values are
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{\mathrm{exact}} &= 0.0005,\\
p_{\mathrm{Jaccard}\geq 0.9} &= 0.0125.
\end{aligned}
$$
The amino-acid-class-preserving null row also used $N=2000$ and seed $42$. These controls support the narrower conclusion that the observed local shape is unusual under the stated finite randomizations. They do not turn the six-bit display into a biological law.

\paragraph{Scope boundary.}
The contact used here is a curated standard genetic-code table. It observes codon-to-label assignment at codon-label resolution, and it is intentionally silent about translation realization, folded structure, physical admissibility, and biological function. Therefore the chapter claims only this: window-six codon coordinates expose a statistically unusual and spectrally recorded local tile in the standard-code label table. No conclusion follows about protein folding, structural order, biochemical feasibility, organism-level mechanism, functional effect, or universality unless a separate observation at that higher layer is supplied. Amino-acid label-sequence readback is a code-layer datum, not an observation of a folded protein.
