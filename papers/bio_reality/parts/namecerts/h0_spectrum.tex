\paragraph{Reality contact and scope.}
The only empirical contact used here is the curated standard-code table assigning codons to amino-acid or stop labels.  That contact is sufficient for codon-label readback and for checking whether the thirteen-codon boundary set and its module split match the table.  It is not an observation of ribosomal realization, protein folding, physical viability, molecular function, or biological universality.  The correct reading is therefore narrow: window-six coordinates expose a statistically and spectrally structured local code-layer tile in the curated standard code, while every claim above the label layer remains blocked until another contact supplies the missing biological evidence.
