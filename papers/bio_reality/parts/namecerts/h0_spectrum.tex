\subsection{Window-six spectral record for the standard code}
\label{sec:h0-spectrum-window-six-standard-code}

\paragraph{Finite codon display.}
This record treats the standard genetic-code table as a finite label contact on the six-bit codon cube $Q_6$.  The six coordinates are the binary expansion of the three nucleotide positions, and the only biological observation admitted here is the curated assignment of each codon to an amino-acid label or to a stop label.  The resulting object is a code-layer record: it can test whether a local tile, a boundary set, and a label split are visible in the table, but it does not by itself describe translation, folded structure, physical admissibility, function, a molecular mechanism, or a universal biological law.

\paragraph{Boundary set.}
The distinguished boundary row $R$ has cardinality $13$.  In codon notation the verified row is
$$
\begin{aligned}
R = \{&AAA, AGA, AGG, AUA, CUA, CUC, CUG, CUU,\\
     &UAA, UAG, UCA, UGA, UUA\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The punctured-cube computation returns the same thirteen codons and the face-vector
$$
\begin{aligned}
(f_0,f_1,f_2)=(13,16,4).
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the local object is not merely a count: it has thirteen vertices, sixteen one-dimensional adjacencies, and four two-dimensional faces inside the window-six coordinate cube.  This is the finite geometric datum that the chapter records.

\paragraph{Spectral row.}
The spectral computation associated with the same local tile gives

\lambda_R &= 0.4758352843197002,\\

$$
The reported radial equation is numerically satisfied to tolerance:

The spectral-radius field also returns $0.6752479308830213$, matching $\lambda_M$ in the recorded row.  These numbers are read as a finite spectral signature of the local codon tile, not as a claim about biochemical energy, folding, or translational dynamics.

\paragraph{Shape significance.}
Against the coordinate null sample with seed $42$, the observed local shape is rare.  The run used $N=2000$ sampled alternatives, required $57377$ distinct-sample attempts, and incurred $55377$ duplicate retries.  It found $1$ exact shape match and $25$ matches with Jaccard similarity at least $0.9$.  The corresponding reported probabilities are
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{\mathrm{exact}} &= 0.0005,\\
p_{J\geq 0.9} &= 0.0125.
\end{aligned}
$$
A second null test preserving amino-acid label classes used $N=2000$ alternatives with the same seed.  It observed no exact shape match, gave observed $M$-size $20$, and counted $7$ alternatives in the lower tail at or below the observed $M$-size.  The reported probabilities are
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{\mathrm{class\ exact}} &= 0.0,\\
p_{\mathrm{class\ lower\ tail}} &= 0.0035.
\end{aligned}
$$
These finite values support a code-layer distinction in the curated table.  They do not license a higher-level biological conclusion without an additional experiment or observation at that higher level.

\subsection{Recovery and module checks}
\label{sec:h0-spectrum-recovery-module-checks}

\paragraph{Leave-one-code-out behavior.}
The leave-one-code-out test evaluated $23$ tables and $63$ total targets.  The baseline hit rate was $0.19821739130434785$.  The hit rate inside $M-T$ was $0.9365079365079365$, and the hit rate inside $M-T$ together with the boundary was $0.9841269841269841$.  The same value, $0.9841269841269841$, is reported as the hit rate above baseline.  By family, the above-baseline rates are $0.8333333333333334$ for alternative yeast, $1.0$ for bacterial-plastid tables, $1.0$ for candidate-other tables, $1.0$ for mitochondrial tables, and $1.0$ for nuclear-ciliate tables, across $5$ families.  These recovery numbers are evidence that the local window-six split has predictive force at the label-table layer.

\paragraph{Leave-one-codon-out behavior.}
The leave-one-codon-out test reports no anchors, $5$ boundary cases, $8$ predictable cases, high closure coverage $8$, and recovery rate $1.0$.  The threshold comparison also returns $1.0$.  This means that, for the tested codon omissions, the local finite geometry recovers the recorded code-layer targets in the stated sense.  It still remains a statement about codon labels, not about the realization of an amino-acid chain or about the structure of a protein.

\paragraph{Module classification.}
The thirteen boundary codons are partitioned into six named modules:

\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{agr} &= \{AGA,AGG\},\\
\mathrm{ar\_lys} &= \{AAA\},\\
\mathrm{cun} &= \{CUA,CUC,CUG,CUU\},\\

The module audit reports $R$-size $13$, assigned count $13$, no extra module codons, no missing codons from the modules, and no unassigned codons.  Every listed module has either a predictable member or a boundary member.  This closes the finite bookkeeping for the row: the same thirteen codons seen in the punctured-cube computation are exactly the codons classified by the module table.

\subsection{Scope of the record}
\label{sec:h0-spectrum-scope}

\paragraph{What is supported.}
The supported statement is narrow.  In the standard-code label table, the window-six codon coordinates exhibit a thirteen-codon boundary set with face-vector $(13,16,4)$, a numerical spectral signature with $\lambda_M=0.6752479308830213$, $\lambda_R=0.4758352843197002$, and $\mu=3.051487585298128$, significant rarity under two finite null tests, strong leave-one-code-out recovery, perfect recorded leave-one-codon-out recovery, and complete assignment of the boundary codons into six modules.  All of these are finite claims about codon labels and local coordinate structure.

\paragraph{What is not supported.}
Nothing in this row establishes translation as a physical process, protein folding, structural order, admissibility under biophysical constraints, biological function, a causal mechanism, or universality across organisms.  A codon-to-label table can say that a codon is read as an amino-acid or stop label in the curated standard code; it cannot observe a folded protein or a functioning molecule.  Any claim beyond code-layer readback must be supplied by a separate contact designed for that layer, such as a translation assay, a structure measurement, a physical admissibility test, or a function experiment.

\paragraph{Verdict.}
The row is accepted as a finite code-read record for the standard-code table.  Its force lies in the agreement among the boundary list, punctured-cube face data, spectral values, null-model rarity, recovery tests, and complete module assignment.  Its limitation is equally part of the result: the local codon tile remains a local label-table phenomenon until further biological contacts are attached.
