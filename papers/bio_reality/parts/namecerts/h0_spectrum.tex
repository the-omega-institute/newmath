\subsection{Window-six spectral boundary for the standard codon table}
\label{subsec:bioreality-h0-spectrum}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite coordinate object.}
The record concerns the standard-code codon table viewed through a six-coordinate codon display.  A codon is read as a vertex of a finite cube $Q_6$, and the biological annotation used here is only the curated codon-to-label table: each codon is assigned an amino-acid label or a stop label.  The claim is therefore a code-layer claim.  It asks whether the window-six coordinate display isolates a small local boundary tile, and whether that tile has a reproducible spectral signature.  The contact does not observe ribosomal translation, folded protein geometry, energetic admissibility, cellular function, a mechanistic pathway, or a universal biological law.

\paragraph{Boundary row.}
The finite boundary set $R$ has exactly thirteen codons.  The verified row gives
$$
\begin{aligned}
R = \{&AAA, AGA, AGG, AUA, CUA, CUC, CUG, CUU,\\
     &UAA, UAG, UCA, UGA, UUA\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus $|R|=13$.  The same row identifies this set as a punctured cube pattern with face-vector data
$$
\begin{aligned}
f(R)=(13,16,4).
\end{aligned}
$$
This is a finite statement about the coordinate presentation and the standard-code labels.  It is not a statement that these thirteen codons form a universal biological module outside the selected readback layer.

\paragraph{Shape significance.}
The shape-comparison run used $N=2000$ samples with seed $42$.  It required $57377$ distinct-sample attempts and recorded $55377$ duplicate retries.  The exact shape appeared once, giving
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{\mathrm{exact}} = 0.0005.
\end{aligned}
$$
The high-overlap test found $25$ samples with Jaccard similarity at least $0.9$, giving
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{J\geq 0.9} = 0.0125.
\end{aligned}
$$
A second null model preserving amino-acid class structure also used $N=2000$ and seed $42$.  It found no exact shape matches, so $p=0.0$ for exact shape in that sample.  Its lower-tail count at or below the observed $M$ size was $7$, with observed $M$ size $20$, hence
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{\mathrm{lower\ tail}} = 0.0035.
\end{aligned}
$$
These finite rows support the claim that the displayed local tile is not a routine random occurrence under the tested controls.  They do not turn the tile into a full model of translation or organismal biology.
