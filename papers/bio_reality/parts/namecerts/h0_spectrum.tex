\subsection{Window-Six Codon Spectrum and Local Tile Boundary}\label{subsec:bioreality-h0-spectrum}

\paragraph{Finite codon display.} The record concerns the standard genetic-code table only at the code-read layer. The carrier is the six-coordinate codon cube $Q_6$, used as a finite display for the $64$ codon positions. The readback contact is a curated standard-code table assigning each codon to an amino-acid or stop label. This contact is sufficient for label comparisons, local tile checks, and finite row counts; it is not an observation of translation dynamics, folded molecular structure, physical admissibility, organismal function, a biochemical mechanism, or a universal biological law.

\paragraph{Shape comparison.} The shape-significance run used $N=2000$ samples with seed $42$. It recorded $57377$ distinct-sample attempts and $55377$ duplicate retries. Its recorded probabilities were $p=0.0$ for exact class-preserving shape agreement and $p=0.0035$ for the lower-tail statistic. These rows support the statement that the displayed local shape is nontrivial relative to the tested finite null procedures.

\paragraph{Scope boundary.} The supported claim is therefore narrow. Window-six codon coordinates expose a local boundary-tile structure whose finite rows include the $13$-codon punctured cube, the spectral values above, the nontrivial null-model counts, and the leave-out recovery statistics. The curated standard-code table is the contact that lets those coordinates be read as codon labels. It does not supply translation realization, folding, structural order, physical admissibility, function, mechanism, or universality. Any later claim across those boundaries must bring its own independent contact rather than reusing the codon-label table as if it observed those higher layers.
