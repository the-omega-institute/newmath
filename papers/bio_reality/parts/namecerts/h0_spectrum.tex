\subsection{Window-six spectrum of the standard-code boundary tile}
\label{sec:bioreality-h0-spectrum}

\paragraph{Finite object.}
The claim studied here is the spectral row of the window-six codon packet.  The finite carrier is the six-coordinate codon cube, read as a display of the $64$ codons by three nucleotide positions with two binary coordinates per position.  The biological contact is deliberately narrower than a biological mechanism: it is the curated standard-code table, treated only as a codon-to-label table assigning each codon an amino-acid or stop label.  The construction isolates a local boundary tile inside that display and asks whether its finite geometry has reproducible numerical structure.  It does not ask whether the geometry causes translation, folding, physical admissibility, biological function, or universality.

\paragraph{Boundary row.}
The distinguished boundary row $R$ has cardinality $13$.  In the observed standard-code table the row is
$$
\begin{aligned}

&UAA, UAG, UCA, UGA, UUA\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The same row is reported as a punctured cubical tile with face-vector
$$
\begin{aligned}

\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the local object is not just a list of exceptional codons: it carries a small cell count with $13$ vertices, $16$ one-dimensional incidences, and $4$ square faces in the selected six-coordinate display.  The codons in $R$ are concentrated in recognizable standard-code modules: $\{AGA,AGG\}$, $\{AAA\}$, $\{CUA,CUC,CUG,CUU\}$, $\{AUA\}$, $\{UCA,UUA\}$, and $\{UAA,UAG,UGA\}$.

$$
\begin{aligned}

These values support the claim that the observed tile is a notable finite pattern under the stated coordinate and sampling procedure.  They do not by themselves identify an evolutionary, biochemical, or translational cause.

\subsection{Interpretation at the code-read layer}
\label{sec:bioreality-h0-spectrum-scope}

\paragraph{Positive content.}
The row supports a precise code-layer statement.  In the standard-code codon table, the window-six coordinate display contains a $13$-codon boundary tile with face-vector $(13,16,4)$, a stable module partition, rare sampled shape behavior, and a spectral triple $(\lambda_M,\lambda_R,\mu)$ whose recorded polynomial residual is approximately $-6.224354365258478\cdot 10^{-12}$.  The evidence is finite and table-bound: it concerns labels in the curated standard genetic code, local codon geometry, and derived probes over that display.

\paragraph{Negative content.}
The same row blocks promotion beyond its contact.  A codon-to-label table is not an observation of translation in a cell, not a folded-structure measurement, not a physical-admissibility test, not a function assay, not a mechanism, and not a universal biological law.  Reading an amino-acid label sequence from codons remains a code-layer operation; it does not observe a protein fold.  Cross-organism wording, CUN/UUR notation, or tRNA-Leu language would require their own organism-matched contact before being used as evidence for structure or function.  Thus the safe conclusion is that the standard-code table contains a sharply concentrated local spectral boundary tile, while every higher biological layer remains unclaimed until a separate experiment supplies it.
