\subsection{Window-six spectral codon tile}\label{sec:bioreality-h0-spectrum}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite object.}
The record considered here is the standard-code codon table viewed through a six-coordinate codon display.  The finite carrier is the codon cube on the alphabet $\{A,C,G,U\}^3$, encoded as six binary distinctions.  The observation is not a claim about how translation is physically carried out.  It is a claim about what is visible when the curated codon-to-label table is read as a finite labelled cube and then tested for local boundary structure.

The distinguished boundary row $R$ has size $13$.  Its codons are
$$
\begin{aligned}
R=\{&AAA,AGA,AGG,AUA,CUA,CUC,CUG,CUU,\\
&UAA,UAG,UCA,UGA,UUA\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The same row is recovered as a punctured cubical surface with face data
$$
\begin{aligned}
(f_0,f_1,f_2)=(13,16,4).
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the finite signature is not merely a count: it records thirteen vertices, sixteen one-dimensional adjacencies, and four two-dimensional faces inside the local codon tile.  The verified module partition also assigns every element of $R$ without residue: $\{AGA,AGG\}$ is the $agr$ module, $\{AAA\}$ is the $ar\_lys$ module, $\{CUA,CUC,CUG,CUU\}$ is the $cun$ module, $\{AUA\}$ is the $ile\_met$ module, $\{UCA,UUA\}$ is the $ssn$ module, and $\{UAA,UAG,UGA\}$ is the $stop\_trp$ module.  The module audit reports $R$-size $13$, assigned count $13$, no missing module codons, no extra module codons, and no unassigned codons.

\paragraph{Spectral row.}
The spectral calculation supplies a numerical invariant for the same finite object.  The measured values are
$$
\begin{aligned}
\lambda_M&=0.6752479308830213,\\
\lambda_R&=0.4758352843197002,\\
\mu&=3.051487585298128.
\end{aligned}
$$
The associated radial cubic check has residual
$$
\begin{aligned}
-6.224354365258478\cdot 10^{-12},
\end{aligned}
$$
reported both as the cubic-polynomial residual and as the radial-polynomial value.  At the stated tolerance, the spectral radius is therefore $0.6752479308830213$.  The finite interpretation is that the twenty-label code-side mass $M$ and the thirteen-codon boundary row $R$ have a stable local spectral separation inside the window-six display.  The available row data give the size of $M$ as $20$ in the amino-acid-class preserving audit; no explicit ordered list of the twenty $M$ labels is present in this packet, so the chapter uses the verified cardinality rather than inventing a label list.

\paragraph{Shape checks.}
The same tile shape was tested against sampled alternatives.  In the unrestricted shape comparison, $N=2000$ sampled tables were used with seed $42$.  The run required $57377$ distinct-sample attempts and $55377$ duplicate retries.  There was exactly $1$ exact shape match, giving
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{\mathrm{exact}}=0.0005.
\end{aligned}
$$
There were $25$ samples with Jaccard score at least $0.9$, giving
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{J\ge 0.9}=0.0125.
\end{aligned}
$$
The amino-acid-class preserving null model used the same $N=2000$ and seed $42$.  It produced $0$ exact matches, so the reported exact-shape value is
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{\mathrm{class\ exact}}=0.0.
\end{aligned}
$$
For the lower-tail comparison relative to the observed $M$-size $20$, the count was $7$, with
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{\mathrm{class\ lower\ tail}}=0.0035.
\end{aligned}
$$
These figures support a finite code-layer anomaly: the local boundary shape is rare under the sampled alternatives recorded here.  They do not turn the shape into an organism-independent law.

\paragraph{Readback and prediction tests.}
The curated standard-code table is the reality contact for this record.  It supplies codon-to-amino-acid-or-stop labels and thereby permits code-layer readback.  It does not observe ribosomal translation, folding, thermodynamic admissibility, biochemical activity, or evolutionary necessity.

Two leave-out checks quantify how far the local tile can be used within that restricted layer.  In the leave-one-code-out run, $23$ tables and $63$ total targets were evaluated.  The baseline hit rate was $0.19821739130434785$.  The hit rate above baseline was $0.9841269841269841$, the hit rate in $M-T$ was $0.9365079365079365$, and the hit rate in $M-T$ plus the boundary row was $0.9841269841269841$.  The family-level summary reports five families: alternative yeast has rate $0.8333333333333334$, while bacterial-plastid, candidate-other, mitochondrial, and nuclear-ciliate families each have rate $1.0$.

In the leave-one-codon-out run, the anchor count was $0$, the boundary count was $5$, the predictable count was $8$, and the high-coverage closure count was $8$.  The recovery rate was $1.0$, and the threshold comparison also reports $1.0$.  These numbers say that the local codon display carries recoverable code-layer structure for the tested omissions.  They do not by themselves identify a molecular route by which a cell realizes the table.

\paragraph{Boundary of the claim.}
The conclusion is deliberately narrow.  Window-six coordinates expose a local labelled-tile pattern in the standard-code table, with a thirteen-codon punctured-cube boundary, a stable spectral row, rare sampled shape matches, and strong leave-out recovery within the code-label layer.  The evidence remains tied to curated codon labels.  No statement in this section asserts translation realization, folded protein structure, physical admissibility, biological function, mechanism, universality, or necessity.  Any such stronger statement would require an independent contact that observes the relevant higher layer directly.
