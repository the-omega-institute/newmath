\paragraph{Finite packet.}
The packet studies the standard-code codon table at the code-read layer. Its carrier is the six-coordinate codon cube $Q_6$, where a codon is handled as a six-bit display rather than as a biochemical mechanism. The data split distinguishes a local tile face, singleton label corners, and three-point label classes, with coordinate, closure, spectrum, and relation structure recorded only as finite combinatorial information. The observed contact is a curated standard-code label table: each codon is assigned to an amino-acid label or to stop. This is enough to test whether a local codon-tile pattern is present in the label table, but it is not a contact with ribosomal translation, folded protein geometry, energetic admissibility, biological function, or any universal mechanism.

\paragraph{Boundary set.}
The distinguished relation row $R$ has cardinality $13$. The verified codon list is
$\{\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UUA}\}$.
The same row is recorded as a punctured-cube shape with $f$-vector $(13,16,4)$. Thus the finite observation is not merely that thirteen codons were selected, but that those thirteen codons support sixteen one-dimensional adjacencies and four two-dimensional faces inside the six-coordinate codon display. This gives the local tile its boundary character: it is large enough to carry face data, yet still a proper fragment of the full codon cube.

\paragraph{Shape significance.}
The finite significance row uses $N=2000$ sampled comparison tables with seed $42$. It required $57377$ distinct-sample attempts and $55377$ duplicate retries, so the row records both the tested sample size and the finite-search cost of obtaining distinct samples. The exact shape appeared once, giving $p_{\mathrm{exact}}=0.0005$. High-overlap matches with Jaccard score at least $0.9$ appeared $25$ times, giving $p_{J\geq 0.9}=0.0125$. In the amino-acid-class-preserving null row, again with $N=2000$ and seed $42$, the observed $M$-size is $20$, the exact match count is $0$, and the lower-tail count at or below the observed $M$-size is $7$. The corresponding reported values are $p=0.0$ for exact shape under that null and $p=0.0035$ for the lower tail. These finite rows support the statement that the standard-code label table is not behaving like a typical sampled table under the recorded comparison procedures. They do not identify a causal biological pathway.

The hit rate inside $M-T$ is $0.9365079365079365$, and the hit rate inside $M-T$ plus boundary is $0.9841269841269841$; the recorded hit rate above baseline is also $0.9841269841269841$. These rows say that the finite local structure has strong code-label recovery behavior under the tested omissions. The recovery is label recovery, not translation recovery and not recovery of molecular structure.

\paragraph{Local claim boundary.}
The warranted statement is that window-six codon coordinates expose a finite local boundary-tile structure in the standard-code label table, with a thirteen-codon punctured-cube row, a stable spectral radius, significant shape behavior under recorded nulls, and strong omission recovery at the code-label level. No stronger biological conclusion is licensed here. The curated table supplies codon-to-label readback only. A separate contact would be required before claiming translation realization, folded structure, physical admissibility, biological function, mechanism, or universality.
