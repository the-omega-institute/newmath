\subsection{Window-Six Codon Spectrum for the Standard Code}
\label{sec:bioreality-h0-spectrum}

\paragraph{Claim and finite carrier.}
This record concerns the claim identifier \texttt{h0.spectrum}.  The finite carrier is the six-coordinate codon cube obtained by displaying each nucleotide as a two-bit coordinate and each codon as a six-bit word.  The biological readback used here is only the curated standard-code assignment from codons to amino-acid or stop labels.  The computation does not treat the genetic code table as a mechanism.  It treats the table as a finite contact point at the code-reading layer and asks whether a local boundary pattern in the cube is reproducible by the recorded labels.

The distinguished boundary row is the thirteen-codon set
$$
\begin{aligned}
R = \{&\texttt{AAA},\texttt{AGA},\texttt{AGG},\texttt{AUA},\texttt{CUA},\texttt{CUC},\texttt{CUG},\texttt{CUU},\\
&\texttt{UAA},\texttt{UAG},\texttt{UCA},\texttt{UGA},\texttt{UUA}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The finite audit reports $|R|=13$.  The same row is certified as a punctured-cube boundary with face data
$$
\begin{aligned}
(f_0,f_1,f_2)=(13,16,4).
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the record is not merely a cardinality count: it also fixes the local incidence shape observed in the codon-coordinate display.

\mu &= 3.051487585298128.
\end{aligned}
$$
The reported spectral radius is $0.6752479308830213$, matching $\lambda_M$ in the finite run.  The radial polynomial residual is
$$
\begin{aligned}
-6.224354365258478\cdot 10^{-12},
\end{aligned}
$$
and the same residual is recorded for the cubic polynomial check.  At the numerical precision of the run, this is a near-zero certificate for the cubic relation used by the finite spectral probe.  The important point is local and finite: the codon-coordinate object supplies a reproducible spectrum for the displayed boundary row, not a biological law about translation or molecular dynamics.

\paragraph{Shape significance against label-preserving nulls.}
Two null-model rows were run with seed $42$ and $N=2000$ sampled tables.  In the unconstrained shape comparison, $57377$ distinct-sample attempts were needed because $55377$ draws were duplicate retries.  There was $1$ exact shape match, giving
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{\mathrm{exact}}=0.0005.
\end{aligned}
$$
There were $25$ high-overlap matches at Jaccard threshold $0.9$, giving
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{J\geq 0.9}=0.0125.
\end{aligned}
$$
In the amino-acid-class-preserving null, the observed comparison object has size $20$.  Among $2000$ samples there were no exact shape matches, and $7$ samples landed in the lower tail at or below the observed size.  The corresponding finite frequencies are
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{\mathrm{class,exact}} &= 0.0,\\
p_{\mathrm{class,lower}} &= 0.0035.
\end{aligned}
$$
These values support the narrower statement that the displayed standard-code row is unusual relative to the sampled finite alternatives.  They do not by themselves identify a biochemical cause.

\paragraph{Leave-one tests.}
The leave-one-code-out test evaluated $23$ genetic-code tables and $63$ targets.  The baseline hit rate was $0.19821739130434785$.  The hit rate above baseline was $0.9841269841269841$, the hit rate in $M\setminus T$ was $0.9365079365079365$, and the hit rate in $M\setminus T$ plus the boundary was $0.9841269841269841$.  These values say that the finite window-six row has strong recovery behavior under the recorded code-table perturbations.

The leave-one-codon-out row records $0$ anchors, $5$ boundary codons, $8$ high-coverage closure cases, and $8$ predictable cases.  Its recovery rate is exactly
$$
\begin{aligned}
1.0.
\end{aligned}
$$
The result is again a code-layer recovery result.  It is not evidence for folded structure, physical admissibility, biological function, or a universal mechanism.

\paragraph{Scope limitation.}
The curated standard-code table supports codon-label readback and local split checks.  It does not observe translation in a ribosome, folding of a peptide, physical admissibility of a molecule, biological function, or organism-level universality.  Consequently the present chapter may say that window-six codon coordinates expose a local boundary-tile spectrum aligned with the standard-code labels.  It may not say that DNA obeys this geometry as a biological necessity, that a label sequence is a folded protein, that CUN/UUR wording is structural evidence, or that the pattern is a universal law.  Any claim crossing from code labels into translation, structure, admissibility, function, mechanism, or universality requires a separate contact with that layer.
