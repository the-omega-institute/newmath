\subsection{Window-Six Spectrum of the Standard-Code Boundary Tile}
\label{sec:bioreality-h0-spectrum}

\paragraph{Claim.}
The claim $h0.spectrum$ records a finite code-layer observation about the standard-code codon table. Codons are displayed as points in the six-coordinate codon cube $Q_6$, with the two-bit nucleotide display applied independently at each of the three codon positions. The object under test is not translation as a molecular process, and it is not protein structure. It is the following restricted readback: the curated standard-code table assigns each codon an amino-acid or stop label, and the six-coordinate display exposes a local boundary tile inside that label table.

\paragraph{Finite carrier and distinguished row set.}
The distinguished boundary set $R$ has exactly thirteen codons:
$$
\begin{aligned}
R=\{&AAA, AGA, AGG, AUA, CUA, CUC, CUG, CUU,\\
&UAA, UAG, UCA, UGA, UUA\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The verified row count is $|R|=13$. The same finite set is certified as a punctured-cube tile with face-count vector
$$
\begin{aligned}
(f_0,f_1,f_2)=(13,16,4).
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the local tile is not merely a list of exceptional codons. It has a reproducible finite shape in the six-coordinate codon display. The associated module partition covers all thirteen codons and leaves no unassigned elements: $R_{size}=13$, $assigned_{count}=13$, with no missing codons, no extra module codons, and no unassigned codons. The modules are
$$
\begin{aligned}
agr&=\{AGA,AGG\}, & ar\_lys&=\{AAA\},\\
cun&=\{CUA,CUC,CUG,CUU\}, & ile\_met&=\{AUA\},\\
ssn&=\{UCA,UUA\}, & stop\_trp&=\{UAA,UAG,UGA\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
Every listed module has either predictable or boundary status in the leave-one-codon readback audit.

\paragraph{Spectral certificate.}
The spectrum attached to the local tile is numerical and finite. The verified spectral quantities are
$$
\begin{aligned}
\lambda_M&=0.6752479308830213,\\
\lambda_R&=0.4758352843197002,\\
\mu&=3.051487585298128.
\end{aligned}
$$
The reported spectral radius in tolerance is $0.6752479308830213$. The cubic residual and the radial polynomial residual coincide numerically at
$$
\begin{aligned}
-6.224354365258478\times 10^{-12},
\end{aligned}
$$
which is treated here as a floating-point zero at the resolution of the finite computation. The chapter-level meaning is narrow: the coordinate tile has a stable spectral signature relative to the computed relation, not that the biological system is forced by that geometry.

\paragraph{Shape tests against finite null models.}
The observed shape is rare in the reported randomization audit. In the shape-significance run with $N=2000$ samples and seed $42$, the pipeline needed $57377$ distinct-sample attempts and recorded $55377$ duplicate retries. There was one exact shape match, giving
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{exact}=0.0005.
\end{aligned}
$$
There were $25$ samples with Jaccard similarity at least $0.9$, giving
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{Jaccard\ge 0.9}=0.0125.
\end{aligned}
$$
A second audit preserved amino-acid class. In that run, again with $N=2000$ and seed $42$, the observed comparison set had $|M|=20$, the exact-match count was $0$, and the lower-tail count at or below the observed $M$ size was $7$. The recorded probabilities were
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{aa\ class\ exact}=0.0,\qquad p_{aa\ class\ lower\ tail}=0.0035.
\end{aligned}
$$
These values support the narrower statement that the six-coordinate shape is not easily reproduced by the tested finite null procedures. They do not identify a physical cause.

The leave-one-codon-out audit separately records $anchor_{count}=0$, $boundary_{count}=5$, $predictable_{count}=8$, $closure_{coverage\_high}=8$, and $recovery_{rate}=1.0$. These finite rows show that the local tile has predictive organization at the code-label level, while also marking a boundary component rather than a full generative law.

\paragraph{Reality contact and scope.}
The only biological contact used here is a curated standard genetic-code table assigning codons to amino-acid or stop labels. That contact tests code readback and local label splitting. It does not observe translation realization, folded structure, structural order, physical admissibility, biological function, or a universal mechanism. In particular, amino-acid label sequences read from codons are still label sequences; they are not folded proteins. CUN and UUR wording, tRNA-Leu notation, or cross-organism vocabulary cannot by themselves convert this local standard-code tile into a structure claim. Any assertion about translation, folding, admissible molecular physics, function, mechanism, or universality must be supplied by a separate empirical contact at that layer.

\paragraph{Conclusion.}
The finite evidence for $h0.spectrum$ is therefore a bounded code-layer result. The row set $R$ is a thirteen-codon punctured-cube tile with vector $(13,16,4)$, complete module coverage, a stable numerical spectrum, rare occurrence under the reported shape tests, and strong leave-one readback behavior against the finite audits. The accepted content is local: window-six codon coordinates expose a structured boundary tile in the standard-code label table. The rejected promotion is equally part of the record: the same finite rows do not establish translation, folding, physical admissibility, function, mechanism, or biological universality.
