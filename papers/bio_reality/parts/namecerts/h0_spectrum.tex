\subsection{Window-six spectral readback for the standard code}
\label{sec:bioreality-h0-spectrum}

\paragraph{Finite carrier and observed contact.}
This record treats the standard-code codon table as a finite code-layer contact, not as a mechanism for translation or as a physical model of protein formation.  The coordinate carrier is the six-bit codon cube $Q_6$, with each codon displayed as a finite binary-window address and then read against the curated standard-code assignment of codons to amino-acid or stop labels.  The table contact is therefore a label contact: it says which label is attached to each codon in the standard code, and it allows local boundary checks inside the codon cube.  It does not by itself observe ribosomal realization, folded structure, admissible chemistry, biological function, a universal law, or any organism-wide mechanism.

\paragraph{The thirteen-codon boundary row.}
The finite boundary set $R$ has cardinality $13$.  The verified row is
$$
\begin{aligned}
R=\{&AAA, AGA, AGG, AUA, CUA, CUC, CUG, CUU,\\
&UAA, UAG, UCA, UGA, UUA\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The same computation identifies this row as a punctured-cube pattern with $f$-vector $(13,16,4)$.  Thus the contact is not merely a list of exceptional labels: it is a finite cubical shape in the window-six address system, with thirteen vertices, sixteen one-dimensional incidences, and four square faces after the relevant local deletion.  This is the concrete boundary object used by the spectral calculation.

The point of the row is local: the spectral calculation is evidence that the codon-window geometry and the curated code labels meet in a rigid finite pattern.  It is not evidence that geometry alone causes the biological code.

\paragraph{Shape significance.}
The finite randomization check used $N=2000$ samples with seed $42$.  It required $57377$ distinct-sample attempts and encountered $55377$ duplicate retries.  In that sample, exact shape agreement occurred once, giving
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{\mathrm{exact}} &= 0.0005,\\
p_{J\geq 0.9} &= 0.0125.
\end{aligned}
$$
The high-Jaccard count was $25$, so the near-match tail is larger than the exact-match row but still small on this finite sample.  A second null model preserving amino-acid classes used $N=2000$ samples with seed $42$, observed $M$-size $20$, found zero exact matches, and placed only $7$ samples in the lower tail at or below the observed $M$-size.  Its reported values are $p_{\mathrm{class,exact}}=0.0$ and $p_{\mathrm{class,lower\ tail}}=0.0035$.  These numbers support a code-layer shape anomaly relative to the tested finite null models; they do not license a claim about folding, fitness, or biochemical necessity.

The hit rate inside $M-T$ was $0.9365079365079365$, and the hit rate inside $M-T$ plus boundary was $0.9841269841269841$.  The leave-one-codon-out row reports boundary count $5$, anchor count $0$, predictable count $8$, high closure coverage $8$, and recovery rate $1.0$.  The finite reading is that the local coordinate system has strong recovery behavior for the tested code-layer omissions.  The reading remains confined to codon-label recovery.

It remains a decomposition of codon labels, not a measurement of tRNA populations, translation kinetics, protein structure, or organismal adaptation.

\paragraph{Scope verdict.}
The claim is ready at the code-read layer.  The finite rows jointly support the following statement: window-six codon coordinates expose a stable local boundary-tile pattern in the standard-code label table, with a thirteen-codon punctured-cube row, spectral radius $0.6752479308830213$, small exact-shape null probability $0.0005$, amino-acid-class-preserving lower-tail probability $0.0035$, and strong leave-out recovery.  The same evidence also fixes the stopping point.  No translation realization, folded structure, physical admissibility, biological function, mechanism, or universality follows from these rows unless an additional contact is supplied that directly measures the higher layer.
