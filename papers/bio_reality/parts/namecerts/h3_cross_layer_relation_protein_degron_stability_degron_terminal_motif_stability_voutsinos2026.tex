\subsection{Translation-Bounded RNA Decay as a Cross-Layer RBP Bridge}
\label{sec:bioreality-q6-translation-bounded-rna-decay-rbp-bridge}

\paragraph{Finite panel.}
This record concerns a finite matched RNA/CDS window panel in which each row carries a $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual-coordinate label, a same-row translation readout, a matched RNA half-life readout, a matched mRNA-abundance control, and, only when the external experiment supplies it, prespecified decay-factor RBP occupancy and perturbation columns. The intended row has the form
$$
\begin{aligned}
  r = (&q_{Q6},\, w_{RNA/CDS},\, T,\, H,\, A,\, O_{RBP},\, L_{RBP},\, R^{wt},\, R^{bd},\, C_{nt},\, Z),
\end{aligned}
$$
where $q_{Q6}$ is the residual coordinate, $w_{RNA/CDS}$ is the local synonymous RNA/CDS window, $T$ is the translation readout, $H$ is RNA half-life, $A$ is matched mRNA abundance, $O_{RBP}$ is measured occupancy by the declared decay-factor RBP, $L_{RBP}$ is target-RBP loss, $R^{wt}$ is wild-type rescue, $R^{bd}$ is binding-defective rescue, $C_{nt}$ is a non-target control, and $Z$ contains the declared controls. The controls include transcription rate, local RNA structure, motif density, GC and dinucleotide composition, codon-pair context, splice or isoform state, transcript abundance, amino-acid composition, translation readout, batch, rank sufficiency, and simpler-baseline terms.

These numbers show that the BioReality register is finite and keyed, but they do not substitute for the missing RNA half-life/RBP perturbation matrix.

\paragraph{Permitted readback.}
The only positive interpretation allowed at this stage is a bounded design rule for what would count as a cross-layer RNA-decay bridge. High-coordinate and low-coordinate synonymous RNA windows may be compared for RNA half-life only after amino-acid composition and local sequence controls are applied, and the comparison is promoted beyond a finite RNA-stability association only if the effect tracks measured decay-factor RBP occupancy under a coordinate/occupancy decoupling design. The required finite statistic is a controlled contrast such as
$$
\begin{aligned}
  \Delta_H
  &= \Big(H \mid q_{Q6}=\mathrm{high}, O_{RBP}=1, Z\Big)
     - \Big(H \mid q_{Q6}=\mathrm{low}, O_{RBP}=0, Z\Big),\\
  \Delta_H^{loss}
  &= \Delta_H \mid L_{RBP}=1,\\
  \Delta_H^{rescue}
  &= \Delta_H \mid R^{wt}=1,
\end{aligned}
$$
with the additional requirement that the binding-defective rescue and the non-target control do not reproduce the wild-type restoration. A bridge reading is therefore conditional on three finite events: occupancy follows the declared decay-factor RBP, target-RBP loss collapses the half-life contrast, and wild-type rescue restores it while binding-defective or non-target controls fail to do so.

\paragraph{Null model.}
The null condition is exchangeability of the high-coordinate and low-coordinate labels with respect to RNA half-life after the stated controls and the same-row translation-readout boundary are present. Formally, the chapter treats
$$
\begin{aligned}
  H \perp q_{Q6}\;\mid\; Z, T, A
\end{aligned}
$$
as the baseline unless the declared RBP occupancy and perturbation columns break that exchangeability in the prespecified direction. RNA half-life alone is not enough. A coordinate-associated half-life difference without measured RBP occupancy remains an RNA-stability association. An occupancy association without coordinate/occupancy decoupling remains a confounded binding association. A perturbation effect without wild-type rescue, or with the same behavior under binding-defective or non-target controls, remains mechanistically unassigned.

\paragraph{Boundary.}
No translation, protein output, folded structure, physical admissibility, biological function, system phenotype, adaptation, causation, mechanism, or universal biological law follows from this row type by itself. The same-row translation readout is a gate on interpretation, not proof of protein realization. The mRNA-abundance row is a control, not an RNA half-life measurement. The RNA half-life row is a stability readout, not an RBP occupancy measurement. The RBP bridge can be claimed only when the same finite panel supplies measured decay-factor RBP occupancy, coordinate/occupancy decoupling, target-RBP loss, wild-type rescue, binding-defective or non-target controls, and the declared statistic. Until then, the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ coordinate remains a bounded descriptor attached to finite readouts rather than a biological mechanism.

\paragraph{Current verdict.}
For the present standalone BioReality record, the finite protein-side audits are concrete, but the RNA-decay RBP bridge itself is not yet positively observed. The chapter is ready as a bounded mathematical statement because it specifies the exact finite carrier, the permitted statistic, the null model, and the local cannot-claim boundary. Its empirical content is therefore conditional: a future same-organism, same-window panel with the required RNA half-life, translation, mRNA-control, RBP occupancy, loss, rescue, binding-defective, and non-target columns can decide the bridge; the currently attached finite counts only constrain the surrounding register and cannot produce the bridge claim.
