\subsection{Translation-bounded RBP decay panel for Q6 residual windows}\label{subsec:bioreality-q6-rbp-decay-bridge}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite object.}
The record concerns a finite matched RNA/CDS window panel in which each row carries a synonymous residual coordinate label from $B^{*}_{Q6}$, a same-row translation readout when available, an RNA half-life readout, matched mRNA abundance, and, only if supplied by a separate measurement table, decay-factor RBP occupancy or perturbation columns. The claim is not that the coordinate itself is a molecular cause. The claim is a stricter finite-row test: a coordinate-associated RNA half-life contrast may be treated as a bounded cross-layer relation only if it survives the translation-readout boundary and then follows a prespecified measured decay-factor RBP route under the required controls.

The principal verified row set for the named degron-terminal-motif run has experiment id \texttt{1a932ef5684e}. It contains $5303$ C-terminal rows over $5303$ proteins and was checked with runtime $40.3029$ seconds. These numbers certify the finite protein-window surface used by the named record; they do not, by themselves, certify an RNA half-life, RBP occupancy, or rescue experiment. The larger audit context also includes the independent thermal-stability run \texttt{8873440304f9}, with $6961$ finite rows and runtime bucket $70.0$ seconds, and the ubiquitylation-occupancy run \texttt{0b96a8e4da3c}, with $10410$ sites over $2883$ proteins and $2000$ actual permutations. Those two companion rows are cited here only to keep the scale of the finite-row audits explicit; they are not evidence for this RNA-decay bridge.

\paragraph{Row grammar.}
A usable row has the schematic form
$$
\begin{aligned}
 r_i = (&q_i, T_i, H_i, A_i, O_i, L_i, W_i, C_i),
\end{aligned}
$$
where $q_i$ is the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual coordinate label, $T_i$ is the same-row translation readout, $H_i$ is RNA half-life, $A_i$ is matched mRNA abundance, $O_i$ is measured decay-factor RBP occupancy, $L_i$ records target-RBP loss or matched control state, $W_i$ records wild-type rescue or binding-defective rescue state, and $C_i$ is the declared control block. The control block must include transcription rate when available, local RNA-structure summaries, motif density, GC and dinucleotide composition, codon-pair context, splice or isoform state, transcript abundance, amino-acid composition, translation readout, batch, rank sufficiency, and simpler baseline terms. Rows lacking $T_i$ remain outside the cross-layer interpretation even if $H_i$ is present.

\paragraph{Admissible statistic.}
The statistic must compare high-coordinate and low-coordinate synonymous windows after the declared controls, then ask whether the RNA half-life contrast is explained through the measured RBP occupancy route rather than through coordinate labels alone. In finite notation, the intended positive pattern is
$$
\begin{aligned}
&\Delta_H(q \mid C,T,A) \neq 0,\\
&\Delta_H(q \mid C,T,A,O) \text{ is attenuated in the prespecified direction},\\
&\Delta_H(q \mid C,T,A,O,L=\text{target loss}) \text{ collapses},\\
&\Delta_H(q \mid C,T,A,O,L=\text{target loss},W=\text{wild type}) \text{ returns},\\
&\Delta_H(q \mid C,T,A,O,L=\text{target loss},W=\text{binding defective or non-target}) \text{ does not return}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The display is a rule for finite readout organization, not a declaration that the present payload already contains the occupancy, loss, and rescue columns. The available contacts name RNA half-life, matched mRNA abundance, and ribosome-profiling translation efficiency as possible readout surfaces. They do not supply same-window measured decay-factor RBP occupancy, coordinate/occupancy decoupling, target-RBP loss, wild-type rescue, or binding-defective and non-target rescue behavior for the named row set.

\paragraph{Null model.}
The local null is exchangeability of high-coordinate and low-coordinate synonymous windows with respect to RNA half-life after the mandatory controls. In finite terms,
$$
\begin{aligned}
H_i \perp q_i \mid C_i,T_i,A_i
\end{aligned}
$$
unless the joined panel shows a controlled half-life difference that tracks measured RBP occupancy and passes the perturbation/rescue requirements. This null is deliberately stronger than a simple no-association null because the coordinate is not allowed to select its own mechanism after inspection. A positive RBP bridge requires the RBP identity and occupancy route to be specified before the bridge statistic is read.

\paragraph{Present evidence.}
The verified fact for \texttt{h3.cross\_layer\_relation.protein\_degron\_stability.degron\_terminal\_motif\_stability\_voutsinos2026} supplies a finite protein-terminal audit surface with $n_{\mathrm{cterm}}=5303$ and $n_{\mathrm{proteins}}=5303$. This is compatible with using the named record as a bounded finite-row object, but it is not enough for the proposed RNA-decay RBP bridge. The thermal-stability and ubiquitylation companion facts show other independently checked cross-layer surfaces, respectively $6961$ rows and $10410$ sites over $2883$ proteins, but neither supplies the RNA half-life and RBP perturbation columns required here. Therefore the evidence state is a ready chapter record with a negative bridge verdict for the RBP mechanism: the finite-row identity is present, while the mechanism-bearing measurement panel is missing.

\paragraph{Local boundary.}
No translation realization follows from RNA half-life alone. No protein output, folding, structural order, physical admissibility, biological function, system phenotype, adaptation, causation, or universal biological law follows from the residual coordinate geometry, from codon/window labels, from review eligibility, or from an RNA half-life association. No RNA-decay mechanism follows from the presence of a translation readout unless the same rows also contain measured decay-factor RBP occupancy, coordinate/occupancy decoupling, target-RBP loss, wild-type rescue, and binding-defective or non-target controls. If those columns are absent, the coordinate remains a bounded descriptor, or at most a finite RNA-stability association after controls.

\paragraph{Chapter verdict.}
The chapter is ready as a self-contained BioReality record because the finite row counts and the missing-mechanism boundary can both be stated without placeholders. Its scientific verdict is restrictive. The named degron-terminal-motif audit contributes a checked finite protein-window scale, but the proposed $B^{*}_{Q6}$ translation-bounded RNA-decay RBP bridge is not positively established on the supplied data. The next admissible measurement would be a same-organism, same-window table joining $q_i$, $T_i$, $A_i$, $H_i$, $O_i$, target-RBP loss, wild-type rescue, binding-defective or non-target rescue, and the full control block. Only after that finite table exists may the bridge statistic be evaluated as a positive cross-layer relation.
