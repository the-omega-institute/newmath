\subsection{Translation-Bounded RNA Decay Bridge for Terminal Degron Windows}
\label{sec:translation-bounded-rna-decay-bridge-terminal-degron-windows}

\paragraph{Finite panel.}
The record concerns a finite matched RNA/CDS window panel in which each row carries a residual synonymous coordinate label $B^{*}_{Q6}$, a CDS or transcript key, a local window position, a same-row translation readout when available, a matched mRNA abundance control, and an RNA half-life readout.  The concrete terminal-degron audit surface contains $5303$ C-terminal protein rows, with $n_{\mathrm{cterm}}=5303$ and $n_{\mathrm{proteins}}=5303$, computed in run $1a932ef5684e$ with runtime $40.3029$ seconds.  These rows are sufficient to define the terminal-window carrier and to test whether the row accounting remains finite, keyed, and reproducible.  They are not, by themselves, sufficient to assert an RNA-decay mechanism.

\paragraph{Coordinate and readout separation.}
Let $i$ range over the finite terminal-window rows.  The coordinate column is written $q_i \in B^{*}_{Q6}$, the translation readout is $T_i$, the matched transcript abundance control is $A_i$, and the RNA half-life readout is $H_i$.  A decay-factor RBP occupancy column, when present, is written $O_i$, and perturbation or rescue labels are written $P_i$.  The admissible row shape is therefore
$$
\begin{aligned}
  i \longmapsto (q_i,T_i,A_i,H_i,O_i,P_i,Z_i),
\end{aligned}
$$
where $Z_i$ denotes the prespecified controls: transcription rate, local RNA structure, motif density, GC and dinucleotide composition, codon-pair context, splice or isoform state, transcript abundance, amino-acid composition, batch, rank sufficiency, and simpler baseline terms.  The symbol $q_i$ is only a finite coordinate label.  It is not a measurement of translation, RNA decay, protein abundance, folding, physical admissibility, biological function, phenotype, adaptation, causation, or a universal law.

\paragraph{Claim tested.}
The chapter tests whether a coordinate-associated RNA half-life difference may be treated as a bounded cross-layer relation.  The positive form is deliberately narrow: high-coordinate and low-coordinate synonymous windows may be compared only after amino-acid composition and local sequence controls are fixed; any half-life difference must follow a prespecified measured decay-factor RBP occupancy; occupancy must be experimentally separated from coordinate state; target-RBP loss must collapse the effect; wild-type rescue must restore it; and binding-defective or non-target controls must fail to restore the same pattern.  The same-row translation readout is a boundary condition, not a license to infer protein output.

\paragraph{Finite statistic.}
The intended statistic is a controlled contrast between high-coordinate and low-coordinate windows, conditioned on translation and abundance controls, and then checked against the RBP occupancy and perturbation structure.  In schematic finite-row form, the admissible comparison is
$$
\begin{aligned}
  \Delta_H
  &=
  \widehat{H}(q_i=\mathrm{high}\mid T_i,A_i,Z_i)
  -
  \widehat{H}(q_i=\mathrm{low}\mid T_i,A_i,Z_i),\\
  \Delta_{H\mid O}
  &=
  \widehat{H}(O_i=1\mid q_i,T_i,A_i,Z_i)
  -
  \widehat{H}(O_i=0\mid q_i,T_i,A_i,Z_i).
\end{aligned}
$$
A bridge is supported only if the coordinate contrast is carried by the measured occupancy term and by the target-RBP perturbation and rescue arms.  If $\Delta_H$ persists without measured occupancy, if occupancy is not decoupled from coordinate state, if target-RBP loss does not collapse the half-life effect, or if wild-type rescue does not separate from binding-defective and non-target controls, then the result remains an RNA-stability association or a coordinate descriptor.

\paragraph{Observed finite facts.}
The terminal-degron run supplies a finite row count and reproducible execution envelope, not a completed RBP-decay bridge.  Its $5303$ terminal rows establish that the relevant C-terminal window surface can be enumerated.  The same audit bundle also contains two neighboring cross-layer finite surfaces: a human thermal-stability surface with $n=6961$ rows and runtime recorded in the deterministic $70.0$ second bucket under run $8873440304f9$, and a ubiquitylation-occupancy surface with $n_{\mathrm{sites}}=10410$, $n_{\mathrm{prots}}=2883$, and $2000$ actual permutations under run $0b96a8e4da3c$.  These additional row counts are cited only to locate the scale of finite cross-layer auditing in the bundle.  They do not supply RNA half-life, decay-factor occupancy, or perturbation-rescue evidence for the terminal-degron RNA-decay question.

\paragraph{Null form.}
The null hypothesis is exchangeability of high-coordinate and low-coordinate labels with respect to RNA half-life after the declared controls and the same-row translation boundary are attached.  In finite-row language, the null says
$$
\begin{aligned}
  H_i \perp q_i
  \mid
  (T_i,A_i,Z_i)
\end{aligned}
$$
unless a measured occupancy and perturbation structure breaks that exchangeability in the prescribed direction.  This null is not weakened by the existence of a terminal-degron row set.  A row set gives an eligible carrier; it does not give the missing RBP occupancy column, the target-RBP loss column, the wild-type rescue column, or the binding-defective and non-target control columns.

\paragraph{Boundary.}
The local boundary is strict.  RNA half-life alone does not imply translation realization, protein output, folded structure, physical admissibility, function, phenotype, adaptation, mechanism, causation, or universality.  Matched mRNA abundance is a transcription-layer control, not an RNA half-life measurement.  Ribosome profiling or translation efficiency is a translation-layer readout, not an RBP occupancy assay.  A same-row translation readout can prevent an RNA-only association from being promoted too far, but it cannot create an RNA-decay mechanism.  The bridge becomes positive only when the same finite rows contain $B^{*}_{Q6}$ labels, translation readout, matched abundance, RNA half-life, measured decay-factor RBP occupancy, coordinate/occupancy decoupling, target-RBP loss, wild-type rescue, and binding-defective or non-target controls.

\paragraph{Readback.}
The present readback is therefore bounded and conditional.  The terminal-degron surface is finite and reproducible at $5303$ rows, and it can host a translation-bounded RNA half-life test.  The available payload does not yet contain the measured RBP occupancy or perturbation-rescue structure needed for a positive cross-layer RBP-decay bridge.  The correct conclusion is that the coordinate remains a finite descriptor, or at most a candidate RNA-stability association, until the missing same-window RBP and rescue columns are joined and the declared controlled statistic is computed.
