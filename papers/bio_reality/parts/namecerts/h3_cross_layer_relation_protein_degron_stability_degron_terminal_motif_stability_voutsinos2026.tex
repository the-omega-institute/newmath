\subsection{Translation-bounded RNA-decay RBP bridge at a Q6 residual-coordinate boundary}\label{sec:bioreality-q6-rna-decay-rbp-bridge}\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite carrier.} The claim is formulated on a finite matched panel of RNA/CDS windows. Each row carries a $B^\ast_{Q6}$ residual-coordinate label, a synonymous-window label, the same-row amino-acid sequence context, an RNA half-life readout, matched mRNA abundance, and a declared translation readout when that readout is present. A positive RBP-decay bridge is not part of the carrier by naming alone. It requires additional row columns for measured decay-factor RBP occupancy, coordinate/occupancy decoupling, target-RBP loss, wild-type rescue, and binding-defective or non-target controls. Without those columns, the object is only a bounded RNA-stability association panel.

\paragraph{Available finite checks.} The row-count fact currently attached to the named degron-terminal-motif record is the protein-side finite audit $h3.cross_layer_relation.protein_degron_stability.degron_terminal_motif_stability_voutsinos2026$: it reports $5303$ C-terminal records over $5303$ proteins, with deterministic runtime $40.3029$ seconds in run $1a932ef5684e$. Two neighboring cross-layer audits give scale checks rather than RBP-decay support: the human meltome charge-patterning audit reports $6961$ rows with a runtime bucket of $70.0$ seconds in run $8873440304f9$, and the ubiquitylation acidic-microenvironment audit reports $10410$ sites over $2883$ proteins with $2000$ permutations in run $0b96a8e4da3c$. These numbers establish that the surrounding BioReality packet family is finite and row-indexed, but none of them supplies same-window RBP occupancy or perturbation-rescue evidence for the RNA half-life bridge.

\paragraph{Statistic to be read.} The admissible finite statistic compares high-coordinate and low-coordinate synonymous RNA windows only after the declared controls are attached. The null model is exchangeability of the coordinate labels with respect to RNA half-life once transcription rate, local RNA structure, motif density, GC and dinucleotide composition, codon-pair context, splice or isoform state, transcript abundance, batch, amino-acid composition, and the same-row translation-readout boundary are conditioned on. In symbolic form the required row relation is:
$$
\begin{aligned}
&\text{row}_i=(q_i,T_i,A_i,H_i,O_i,L_i,R_i,C_i,Z_i),\\
&q_i \in \{\text{high }B^\ast_{Q6},\text{ low }B^\ast_{Q6}\},\\
&T_i=\text{same-row translation readout},\\
&A_i=\text{matched mRNA abundance},\\
&H_i=\text{RNA half-life},\\
&O_i=\text{measured decay-factor RBP occupancy},\\
&L_i=\text{target-RBP loss},\quad R_i=\text{wild-type rescue},\\
&C_i=\text{binding-defective or non-target control},\\
&Z_i=\text{declared local and transcript-level controls}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The bridge can be reported only if the coordinate-associated half-life contrast follows $O_i$ under coordinate/occupancy decoupling, collapses under $L_i$, and returns under $R_i$ while failing under $C_i$.

\paragraph{Local interpretation.} The present evidence does not reach that positive state. The available RNA half-life contacts supply organism-scoped transcript or gene half-life estimates with provenance and join keys; the matched mRNA contact supplies abundance controls for separating transcript level from translation efficiency; the ribosome-profiling contact supplies a possible translation-efficiency boundary. These are necessary surfaces, not the full RBP-decay experiment. In particular, an RNA half-life difference by itself is not a translation result, not protein output, not folded structure, not physical admissibility, not biological function, not phenotype, not adaptation, and not a general sequence law. A same-row translation readout prevents promotion from RNA half-life into downstream protein realization; it does not prove an RNA-decay mechanism.

\paragraph{Decision boundary.} The finite packet is therefore ready as a bounded negative or boundary statement, not as a positive RBP-mediated cross-layer mechanism. It may say that the current $B^\ast_{Q6}$ coordinate remains a descriptor until a same-organism, same-window panel adds measured decay-factor RBP occupancy, coordinate/occupancy decoupling, target-RBP loss, wild-type rescue, binding-defective or non-target controls, and a declared finite RBP-decay half-life statistic. A positive bridge would require the following implication to survive in the attached panel:
$$
\begin{aligned}
&\Delta H(q_i\mid Z_i,T_i,A_i)\text{ tracks }O_i,\\
&\Delta H(q_i\mid Z_i,T_i,A_i,O_i)\text{ is lost under target-RBP loss},\\
&\Delta H(q_i\mid Z_i,T_i,A_i,O_i)\text{ returns under wild-type rescue},\\
&\Delta H(q_i\mid Z_i,T_i,A_i,O_i)\text{ does not return under binding-defective or non-target controls}.
\end{aligned}
$$
Until those rows exist, the scientific content is the restriction itself: codon-window geometry and $B^\ast_{Q6}$ residual labels do not select an RBP mechanism, and finite RNA half-life readback cannot be promoted to translation, folding, physical admissibility, function, mechanism, causation, or universality without a separate layer-matched contact.
