\paragraph{Claim shape.} The admissible object is a matched RNA/CDS window table. Each row must carry a $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual-coordinate label, a same-row translation readout, an RNA half-life readout, matched mRNA abundance, and externally measured decay-factor RBP columns whenever the RBP mechanism is asserted. The intended comparison is not merely high-coordinate versus low-coordinate RNA half-life. It is the stricter question of whether a coordinate-associated half-life difference tracks a prespecified decay-factor RBP occupancy after amino-acid composition, local sequence composition, local RNA structure, motif density, GC and dinucleotide composition, codon-pair context, splice or isoform state, transcript abundance, batch, and the same-row translation boundary have already been represented.

\paragraph{Finite statistic.} The row-level test can be written as a bounded comparison over a finite index set $W$ of matched RNA/CDS windows. For each window $w \in W$, let $q(w)$ be the residual coordinate class, $T(w)$ the attached translation readout, $A(w)$ the matched mRNA abundance control, $H(w)$ the RNA half-life readout, and $O(w)$ the measured decay-factor RBP occupancy. A positive cross-layer readback is only in scope when the following finite tuple exists on the same row:
$$\begin{aligned}
  w \mapsto \bigl(q(w), T(w), A(w), H(w), O(w), Z(w), P(w), R(w)\bigr),
\end{aligned}$$
where $Z(w)$ denotes the prespecified covariates, $P(w)$ denotes target-RBP loss, and $R(w)$ denotes the rescue/control condition. The decisive pattern is coordinate-associated half-life separation that follows measured RBP occupancy, collapses under target-RBP loss, and returns under wild-type rescue while failing under binding-defective or non-target controls. Without this full row tuple, the observation remains an RNA-stability association or a coordinate descriptor.

A stronger RBP-null says that any remaining half-life separation must not be called an RBP-mediated bridge unless measured decay-factor occupancy explains the separation under coordinate/occupancy decoupling and unless target-RBP loss and rescue behave as specified.
