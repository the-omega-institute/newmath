\paragraph{Claim tested.}
The permitted claim is narrow.  A $B^*_{Q6}$ coordinate-associated RNA half-life difference may be treated as a bounded cross-layer relation only if the same row also contains a translation readout and the half-life difference follows the prespecified decay-factor RBP occupancy after coordinate and occupancy are experimentally separated.  The effect must collapse after target-RBP loss and return under wild-type rescue, while failing to return under binding-defective or non-target controls.  In finite-row notation, the positive case asks for a controlled contrast of the form
$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta_H(q,o \mid Z,t,m) &= E[H \mid q=\mathrm{high}, o=1, Z,t,m]
 - E[H \mid q=\mathrm{low}, o=0, Z,t,m],\\
\Delta_H^{\mathrm{loss}} &\approx 0,\\
\Delta_H^{\mathrm{WT\ rescue}} &\approx \Delta_H,\\
\Delta_H^{\mathrm{binding\ defective}} &\approx 0,
\end{aligned}
$$
where $Z$ includes transcription rate, local RNA structure, motif density, GC and dinucleotide composition, codon-pair context, splice or isoform state, transcript abundance, batch, amino-acid composition, rank sufficiency, and simpler-baseline controls.  The displayed equations are a specification of the finite statistic required by the chapter, not a report that the statistic has been computed.

\paragraph{Null condition.}
The default reading is exchangeability.  After transcription-rate control, local RNA-structure control, motif-density control, GC and dinucleotide control, codon-pair context, splice or isoform state, transcript abundance, batch, amino-acid composition, and the same-row translation boundary are attached, high-coordinate and low-coordinate windows are treated as exchangeable with respect to RNA half-life unless the measured RBP bridge breaks that null.  If the coordinate remains predictive only in rows without measured occupancy, or if occupancy cannot be decoupled from coordinate state, the result is not an RBP mechanism.  If target-RBP loss does not collapse the effect, or if wild-type rescue does not restore it against binding-defective or non-target controls, the record remains an RNA half-life association at most.

\paragraph{Boundary.}
The finite protein-degron run supplies $5303$ matched terminal protein rows for the neighboring degron-stability subject, not a translation-realization or RNA-decay mechanism for this $B^*_{Q6}$ question.  Likewise, the $6961$-row thermal-stability audit and the $10410$-site ubiquitylation audit are finite contact-scale checks, not evidence that an RNA half-life difference realizes protein output, folded structure, physical admissibility, function, phenotype, adaptation, causation, or a universal biological law.  RNA half-life alone does not certify translation.  Translation readout alone does not certify RBP-mediated decay.  Matched mRNA abundance is a control surface, not a half-life or occupancy measurement.  Ribosome profiling is a translation surface, not an RNA-decay perturbation panel.  The chapter therefore records a ready finite specification for the required test, while the positive biological bridge remains conditional on the missing same-window RBP occupancy and perturbation/rescue data.
