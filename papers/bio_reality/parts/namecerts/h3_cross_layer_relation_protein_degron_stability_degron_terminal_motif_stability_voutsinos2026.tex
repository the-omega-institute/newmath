\subsection{Translation-Bounded RNA Decay Bridge for Terminal-Motif Stability Records}\label{subsec:bioreality-degron-terminal-motif-rna-decay-rbp-bridge}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite panel.}
This record treats the Voutsinos terminal-motif stability run as a finite-row BioReality subject, not as a free-standing molecular mechanism. The verified terminal panel contains $5303$ C-terminal protein rows, reported as $n_{\mathrm{cterm}}=5303$ and $n_{\mathrm{proteins}}=5303$ for experiment run $1a932ef5684e$, with runtime $40.3029$ seconds. The rows are therefore suitable as a bounded terminal-window carrier, but the carrier by itself is not an RNA-decay experiment. The coordinate side is a matched RNA/CDS window table whose possible entries include a $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinate label, a same-row translation readout, an RNA half-life value, matched mRNA abundance, and, only when supplied by a separate assay, a measured decay-factor RBP occupancy or perturbation column.

\paragraph{Claim form.}
The admissible question is whether a coordinate-associated half-life difference can be read as a cross-layer RNA-decay relation after the translation boundary has already been represented on the same row. The local object is not a protein-output theorem. It is a finite comparison between high-coordinate and low-coordinate synonymous windows, with amino-acid composition, local sequence composition, transcript abundance, splice or isoform state, batch, and translation-readout status treated as controls. In symbolic form, the intended row has the shape
$$
\begin{aligned}
r_i=(q_i,T_i,H_i,A_i,O_i,P_i),
\end{aligned}
$$
where $q_i$ is the residual coordinate label, $T_i$ is the same-row translation readout when present, $H_i$ is the RNA half-life readout, $A_i$ is matched mRNA abundance, $O_i$ is measured decay-factor RBP occupancy when available, and $P_i$ records target-RBP perturbation or rescue status when available. A positive bridge is allowed only for the subpanel in which $T_i$, $A_i$, $O_i$, and $P_i$ are all present with the prescribed controls.

\paragraph{Required separation.}
The terminal-motif run supplies a finite protein-terminal row count, while the RNA half-life contacts supply transcript or gene half-life estimates. These are different measurement surfaces. The $5303$-row terminal panel may anchor the subject, but it does not supply same-window RBP occupancy, target-RBP loss, wild-type rescue, or binding-defective control behavior. Likewise, the matched mRNA abundance contact is a denominator or covariate for translation-efficiency separation, not an RNA half-life mechanism. The ribosome-profiling contact can represent translation efficiency after matched mRNA control, but it is not an RNA-decay assay and does not measure decay-factor binding.

\paragraph{Decision statistic.}
For a finite eligible subpanel $E$, the guarded statistic compares the residual coordinate term before and after the measured RBP-decay columns are introduced. One acceptable schematic summary is
$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta_H &= \widehat{H}(q=\mathrm{high})-\widehat{H}(q=\mathrm{low}),\\
\Delta_{H\mid O,P} &= \widehat{H}(q=\mathrm{high}\mid O,P,T,A,Z)-\widehat{H}(q=\mathrm{low}\mid O,P,T,A,Z),
\end{aligned}
$$
where $Z$ abbreviates transcription-rate, local RNA-structure, motif-density, GC and dinucleotide composition, codon-pair context, splice or isoform state, amino-acid composition, batch, rank sufficiency, and simpler-baseline controls. The bridge survives only if the half-life contrast tracks measured decay-factor occupancy under coordinate/occupancy decoupling, collapses after target-RBP loss, and returns under wild-type rescue but not under binding-defective or non-target controls. If those columns are absent, the record remains a bounded coordinate association or an RNA-stability boundary, not a mechanism.

\paragraph{Cross-run calibration context.}
Two additional verified BioReality runs show the scale of finite-row evidence used elsewhere in the same audit family without changing the present RNA-decay boundary. The thermal-stability contact records $6961$ rows for run $8873440304f9$ with a deterministic runtime bucket of $70.0$ seconds. The ubiquitylation microenvironment contact records $2883$ proteins, $10410$ sites, and $2000$ actual permutations for run $0b96a8e4da3c$. These numbers demonstrate that the paper accepts finite empirical matrices with explicit row counts and permutation budgets; they do not import thermal-stability or ubiquitylation conclusions into the terminal-motif RNA-decay subject.

\paragraph{Local null.}
The null state is exchangeability of high-coordinate and low-coordinate labels with respect to RNA half-life after transcription rate, local RNA structure, motif density, GC and dinucleotide composition, codon-pair context, splice or isoform state, transcript abundance, batch, amino-acid composition, and the same-row translation boundary are controlled. The null is also retained whenever measured RBP occupancy is absent, unchanged, or not experimentally separated from coordinate state. A target-RBP perturbation that fails to erase the half-life contrast, or a wild-type rescue that fails to restore it under binding-defective and non-target controls, keeps the bridge closed.

\paragraph{Cannot-claim boundary.}
This chapter does not infer translation realization, protein abundance, folded structure, physical admissibility, biological function, phenotype, adaptation, causation, mechanism, or universality from RNA half-life alone. A same-row translation readout is necessary even to discuss translation-bounded RNA stability, and a separate measured RBP occupancy plus perturbation/rescue panel is necessary before any decay-factor RBP relation can be asserted. The $B^*_{Q6}$ coordinate is therefore a finite label on the row system until the relevant empirical columns are attached and the declared statistic is computed.
