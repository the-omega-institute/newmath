\subsection{Translation-bounded RNA-decay bridge for $B^{*}_{Q6}$ terminal-window records}\label{sec:bioreality-q6-rna-decay-rbp-bridge}\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite panel and coordinate object.}
The record considered here is a finite matched RNA/CDS window panel in which each row carries a $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual coordinate label, a synonymous-window description, a same-row translation readout when available, an RNA half-life readout, matched mRNA abundance, and, for a positive mechanism-grade reading, a prespecified decay-factor RBP occupancy and perturbation block. The motivating finite run for the terminal-window stability contact contains $5303$ C-terminal entries over $5303$ proteins, with run identifier $1a932ef5684e$ and deterministic runtime $40.3029$ seconds. This number is used only as a row-count anchor for the terminal-window BioReality packet: it does not by itself assert RNA decay, translation output, protein folding, or a degron mechanism. The chapter therefore treats the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ coordinate as a finite label on matched rows, not as biological evidence unless the corresponding measured contact is actually present in the same finite table.

\paragraph{Local null model.}
The local null hypothesis is exchangeability of high-coordinate and low-coordinate synonymous windows with respect to RNA half-life after the relevant design variables have been conditioned away. In words, once transcription rate, local RNA structure, motif density, GC and dinucleotide composition, codon-pair context, splice or isoform state, transcript abundance, batch, amino-acid composition, and the same-row translation-readout boundary have been accounted for, the coordinate labels should not retain a half-life distinction. A compact form of the intended finite comparison is
$$
\begin{aligned}
H_0 &: \Delta_{1/2}(Q_{\mathrm{high}},Q_{\mathrm{low}}\mid Z,T,A)=0,\\
Z &:=(\hbox{composition, structure, isoform, abundance, batch, and local-context controls}),\\
T &:=(\hbox{same-row translation readout}),\\
A &:=(\hbox{matched mRNA abundance control}).
\end{aligned}
$$
Here $\Delta_{1/2}$ denotes the finite controlled half-life contrast between the coordinate classes. The equation is not a claim of mechanism; it is the boundary condition that must be defeated before the packet can leave descriptor status.

\paragraph{Required RBP-decay gate.}
A positive cross-layer reading requires more than an RNA half-life difference. The finite rows must also contain measured occupancy by a prespecified decay-factor RBP, and the occupancy must be experimentally separated from the coordinate label. The same comparison must collapse under target-RBP loss and return under wild-type rescue, while binding-defective rescue or non-target controls must fail to restore the effect. The intended bridge statistic is therefore not merely $Q\mapsto$ half-life; it is a constrained pattern over coordinate, occupancy, perturbation, and rescue:
$$
\begin{aligned}
Q &\rightsquigarrow R_{\mathrm{occ}},\\
R_{\mathrm{occ}} &\rightsquigarrow \Delta_{1/2},\\
\hbox{target loss} &\rightsquigarrow \hbox{collapse of }\Delta_{1/2},\\
\hbox{wild-type rescue} &\rightsquigarrow \hbox{return of }\Delta_{1/2},\\
\hbox{binding-defective or non-target control} &\rightsquigarrow \hbox{no restoration}.
\end{aligned}
$$
Only this finite pattern would justify treating the RNA half-life association as a bounded cross-layer RNA-decay/RBP bridge. Without the measured occupancy and perturbation-rescue rows, the same terminal-window table remains an RNA-stability association or a coordinate-labeled descriptor.

\paragraph{Translation boundary.}
The translation readout is a mandatory boundary variable, not a license to infer protein output. The same-row translation field is required because the conjecture is explicitly translation-bounded: an RNA half-life signal is not allowed to migrate into protein realization unless translation has been separately observed or bounded in the same matched row design. The matched mRNA abundance field is likewise a control denominator, not evidence for translation by itself. Thus transcript abundance, ribosome-profiling route names, or codon-window geometry do not establish protein abundance, folded structure, physical admissibility, biological function, adaptation, causation, or a universal biological law. Those claims require separate layer-matched contacts and their own finite statistics.

They do not supply measured decay-factor RBP occupancy, target-RBP loss, wild-type rescue, binding-defective controls, or same-window RNA half-life perturbation evidence for the present conjecture.

\paragraph{Name-certified reading.}
The finite name-certified reading is therefore conditional. If a same-organism, same-window table contains $B^{*}_{Q6}$ coordinate labels, same-row translation readout, matched mRNA abundance, RNA half-life, measured decay-factor RBP occupancy, coordinate/occupancy decoupling, target-RBP loss, wild-type rescue, and binding-defective or non-target controls, then the declared statistic may test whether coordinate-associated RNA half-life differences follow the measured RBP-decay route. If any of those fields are absent, the permitted conclusion is strictly weaker: the coordinate labels may organize a finite RNA half-life comparison, but they do not identify an RBP mechanism and they do not promote RNA stability into translation, protein output, folding, physical admissibility, function, phenotype, adaptation, causation, or universality.
