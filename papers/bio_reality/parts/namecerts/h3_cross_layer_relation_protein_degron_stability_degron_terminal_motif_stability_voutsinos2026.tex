\subsection{Translation-Bounded RNA-Decay Bridge for Degron-Adjacent Windows}\label{sec:bioreality-q6-rna-decay-rbp-bridge-degron-terminal-motif}\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite panel.}
This record concerns a finite matched RNA/CDS window panel in which each row carries a $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual coordinate label, a same-row translation readout when available, a matched mRNA abundance control, an RNA half-life readout, and, for a positive bridge claim, a prespecified decay-factor RBP occupancy and perturbation column.  The row universe is not an unrestricted sequence law.  It is a bounded comparison table whose admissible claims are determined by which measured columns are actually present on the same rows.  The degron-terminal-motif run supplies a concrete row scale for the protein-side anchor: $5303$ C-terminal windows across $5303$ proteins, with recorded runtime $40.3029$ seconds under run identifier $1a932ef5684e$.  This count fixes the finite protein-window surface used by the chapter, but it does not by itself supply translation efficiency, RNA half-life, RBP occupancy, or perturbation rescue data.

\paragraph{Coordinate split.}
The intended contrast is between high-coordinate and low-coordinate synonymous RNA windows after amino-acid composition and local sequence controls.  The coordinate is written only as a finite label on a row.  It is not interpreted as a molecular force, a translation mechanism, or a protein-state measurement.  A valid RNA-decay bridge requires the following row type:
$$
\begin{aligned}
  r = (&q_r,\ T_r,\ A_r,\ H_r,\ O_r,\ L_r,\ W_r,\ C_r),
\end{aligned}
$$
where $q_r$ is the residual coordinate label, $T_r$ is the same-row translation readout, $A_r$ is matched mRNA abundance, $H_r$ is RNA half-life, $O_r$ is measured decay-factor RBP occupancy, $L_r$ records target-RBP loss, $W_r$ records wild-type rescue, and $C_r$ records binding-defective or non-target controls.  If $T_r$ is absent, the row may still be used as an RNA-stability association row, but it cannot be read as a translation-bounded cross-layer bridge.

\paragraph{Bridge statistic.}
The finite statistic asks whether the half-life contrast associated with $q_r$ follows measured decay-factor occupancy after the mandatory controls are present.  In schematic form, the positive pattern would have the shape
$$
\begin{aligned}
  \Delta_H(q \mid Z,T,A,O) &\ne 0,\\
  \Delta_H(q \mid Z,T,A,O,L=\mathrm{loss}) &\approx 0,\\
  \Delta_H(q \mid Z,T,A,O,W=\mathrm{wildtype}) &\text{ returns},\\
  \Delta_H(q \mid Z,T,A,O,C=\mathrm{binding\ defective\ or\ non\ target}) &\text{ does not return},
\end{aligned}
$$
with $Z$ containing transcription rate, local RNA structure, motif density, GC and dinucleotide composition, codon-pair context, splice or isoform state, transcript abundance, amino-acid composition, batch, rank sufficiency, and simpler baseline terms.  The statistic is finite-row and conditional: it is not licensed by an RNA half-life column alone, nor by a coordinate label alone, nor by a contact name that merely states that ribosome profiling or mRNA abundance could in principle be joined later.

\paragraph{Observed anchors.}
The present evidence table contains three useful scale checks.  The degron-terminal-motif surface has $n_{\mathrm{cterm}}=5303$ and $n_{\mathrm{proteins}}=5303$.  A separate human meltome charge-patterning anchor records $n=6961$ with a deterministic runtime bucket of $70.0$ seconds under run identifier $8873440304f9$.  A ubiquitylation acidic-microenvironment anchor records $10410$ sites over $2883$ proteins with $2000$ actual permutations under run identifier $0b96a8e4da3c$.  These numbers show that the BioReality table is operating at finite empirical row scales rather than at an abstract universal level.  They do not substitute for the missing RNA-decay RBP bridge columns.  In particular, neither the meltome count nor the ubiquitylation permutation count measures decay-factor RBP occupancy in the degron-adjacent RNA windows.

\paragraph{Null model.}
The local null states that, after the declared controls are attached, high-coordinate and low-coordinate labels are exchangeable with respect to RNA half-life.  Equivalently, if $\pi$ ranges over coordinate-label permutations preserving the matched control strata, the finite readout should not distinguish the observed labeling from the permuted labelings:
$$
\begin{aligned}
  S(q,H \mid Z,T,A,O,L,W,C) &\in \{S(\pi q,H \mid Z,T,A,O,L,W,C): \pi \in \Pi_Z\}
\end{aligned}
$$
up to the declared finite tolerance.  This null is broken only when the same-row translation boundary is present, the half-life difference tracks measured RBP occupancy rather than coordinate alone, target-RBP loss collapses the effect, wild-type rescue restores it, and binding-defective or non-target controls fail to restore it.

\paragraph{Local boundary.}
The present record is therefore a bounded research-ready chapter, not a positive mechanism claim.  The degron-terminal-motif protein-window count gives a concrete finite surface, and the additional verified anchors give neighboring finite scales, but the required same-window decay-factor RBP occupancy, coordinate/occupancy decoupling, target-RBP loss, wild-type rescue, and binding-defective or non-target controls are not present in the supplied row data.  RNA half-life alone cannot be promoted to translation realization, protein output, folding, physical admissibility, biological function, phenotype, adaptation, causation, mechanism, or a universal biological law.  Those conclusions require separate layer-matched contacts, measured on the relevant rows, with their own declared finite statistics.
