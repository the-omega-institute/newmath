\subsection{A bounded translation-window contact for the solubility-linked hydrophobic-clustering route}
\label{sec:bioreality-f3-stress-solubility-esol-translation-window}

\paragraph{Finite object.}
The finite object considered here is a table of coding-sequence windows in which each row is allowed to carry only the data needed for a local translation-window test: organism, coding-sequence or protein identifier, codon-position bin, an $f_3$-stress residual coordinate, a wobble-weight asymmetry flag, amino-acid-bin controls, GC3, length, $M$-density, matched mRNA abundance, a position-resolved ribosome-occupancy residual, and a break condition. The intended biological contact is narrow. The row is not a protein-solubility measurement, a folding assay, a structure annotation, or a mechanistic statement about translation. It is a proposed same-window readout surface on which a synonymous third-position residual may be tested against ribosome-footprint occupancy after the amino-acid projection has already removed ordinary protein-composition information.

\paragraph{Claim-local coordinate.}
For a window $i$, let $q_i$ denote the $f_3$-stress residual left after quotienting by amino-acid composition, and let $T_i$ denote the matched position-resolved ribosome-occupancy statistic, if such a statistic has been joined to the same organism, same CDS, and same local window. The admissible regression target has the form
$$
\begin{aligned}
T_i &= \alpha + \beta q_i + \gamma^{\mathsf T} Z_i + \varepsilon_i,\\
Z_i &= (\mathrm{AA\ bin},\mathrm{GC3},\mathrm{length},M\text{-}\mathrm{density},\mathrm{mRNA},\mathrm{coverage},\mathrm{mappability}).
\end{aligned}
$$
The coefficient $\beta$ is meaningful only inside wobble-asymmetric contexts, where the third-position weight assigned to codon $c$ is not equal to the weight assigned after the $f_3$ move. In wobble-symmetric contexts the coordinate remains a codon-topology residue rather than a translation-layer variable. Thus the survival test is conditional: it asks whether $q_i$ improves a bounded position-resolved translation-window statistic after simpler amino-acid, GC3, length, density, and mRNA explanations have been attached to the same row.

\paragraph{Relation to the protein-solubility record.}
The linked ESOL-facing protein-solubility run supplies a finite scale but not the translation-window evidence required by this chapter. Its recorded run identifier is $8742a224b538$, with $n=3198$ rows and a runtime of $60.0$ seconds for the protein hydrophobic-clustering solubility surface. Those numbers are used here only to identify the size and computational status of the neighboring solubility-facing contact. They do not, by themselves, provide codon-indexed ribosome footprints, wobble weights, matched mRNA rows, or a same-window $f_3$-stress occupancy statistic. The solubility record is therefore a downstream comparator, not a license to promote the synonymous residual to folding, solubility mechanism, physical admissibility, or biological function.

\paragraph{Cross-check against another finite biological contact.}
A second linked row, the human N-terminal acetylation occupancy contact, records run identifier $90ea6b5f7fc7$, $n=1426$, runtime $5.372$ seconds, and $\mathrm{abundance\_controlled}=\mathrm{false}$. Its finite note says that a frozen yeast rule transfers to human under preregistered gates. This is useful as a scope comparison because it shows what this chapter does not yet have: an independently matched downstream readout with its own gate conditions and a declared abundance-control status. The acetylation row is not evidence for the $f_3$-stress translation-window claim; it is a neighboring cross-layer example that makes the local absence of a joined position-resolved ribosome-occupancy matrix explicit.

\paragraph{Support condition.}
The positive claim would require a finite panel satisfying all joins simultaneously. Each admissible row must attach ordered coding-sequence coordinates, codon counts, bounded tAI or stAI wobble weights, matched mRNA abundance, and codon-indexed or local-window ribosome-footprint occupancy. The statistic must remain rank-sufficient after amino-acid-bin or position controls, GC3, CDS length, $M$-density, codon-count controls, matched mRNA, coverage, mappability, and simpler-baseline ablation. The minimal survival statement is then:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta(q;T\mid Z,\mathrm{asym}) &> 0,\\
\Delta(q;T\mid Z,\mathrm{sym}) &\approx 0,
\end{aligned}
$$
where $\Delta$ denotes the controlled improvement of the bounded translation-window readout and $\mathrm{asym}$ marks $w_c \ne w_{c^{f_3}}$. This is a row-local test, not an organism-level law.

\paragraph{Break condition.}
The chapter fails its own contact discipline if review readiness, the contact name, modeled tAI or stAI weights, $S^{QT}$ notation, codon-window geometry, or the $f_3$-stress coordinate is treated as the measured ribosome-occupancy matrix. It also fails if the ESOL solubility run is used as a substitute for the missing translation-window statistic. The only admissible promotion is from a joined finite panel to a bounded position-resolved translation readout. No translation realization, protein abundance, folding, solubility mechanism, physical admissibility, localization, biological function, system phenotype, adaptation claim, causation claim, or universal biological rule follows unless a separate layer-matched contact supplies that specific readout and the corresponding controlled statistic survives.
