\subsection{A Translation-Window Boundary for Third-Position Stress}\label{sec:bioreality-f3-stress-position-resolved-translation-window}\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite record.}
This chapter records an ai-discovered BioReality boundary for the conjecture that a third-position synonymous residual coordinate, written here as $f_3$-stress, may improve a bounded position-resolved translation-window readout only after amino-acid composition and matched transcript-level controls have already been imposed. The available finite row attached to the present record is the protein-solubility experiment run $8742a224b538$, with $n=3198$ rows and a recorded runtime of $60.0$ seconds. That run is useful as a row-count anchor for the linked BioReality packet, but it does not by itself contain the same-organism, same-CDS, codon-indexed ribosome-footprint matrix needed for the translation-window claim. No finite $R$ list, $M$ list, fitted $\lambda$ value, $p$-value, module label, hit rate, or controlled translation-window effect size is present in the supplied row. Consequently the present chapter is a bounded null-facing record rather than a positive biological assertion.

\paragraph{Object under test.}
The admissible object is a finite coding-sequence window table whose rows would have the form
$$\begin{aligned}
  &(\mathrm{organism},\ \mathrm{CDS},\ b,\ S_{f_3},\ A,\ W,\ G_3,\ L,\ M,\ X_{\mathrm{mRNA}},\ Y_{\mathrm{RPF}},\ \beta),
\end{aligned}$$
where $b$ is a codon-position or local-window bin, $S_{f_3}$ is the third-position synonymous residual coordinate after amino-acid projection, $A$ records amino-acid-bin or position controls, $W$ records whether the attached wobble-weight surface is asymmetric under the $f_3$ flip, $G_3$ is the GC3 control, $L$ is the CDS-length control, $M$ is the methionine-density or baseline-density control, $X_{\mathrm{mRNA}}$ is the matched transcript-abundance denominator or covariate, $Y_{\mathrm{RPF}}$ is the position-resolved ribosome-footprint occupancy readout, and $\beta$ records whether a predeclared break condition has fired. The finite row supplied here has only the linked solubility-row count $n=3198$ and runtime $60.0$ seconds; it lacks the translation-window variables in this display.

\paragraph{Admissible statistic.}
A positive test would require comparing a baseline model that already contains amino-acid composition, codon-position or amino-acid-bin effects, GC3, length, $M$-density, codon-count controls, matched mRNA abundance, coverage filters, mappability filters, and simpler codon-usage terms against a model that additionally contains $S_{f_3}$ and its wobble-asymmetry restriction. In finite-row form the permitted comparison is only
$$\begin{aligned}
  \Delta_{f_3}
  &= Q\bigl(Y_{\mathrm{RPF}}\mid Z, S_{f_3}, W\bigr)-Q\bigl(Y_{\mathrm{RPF}}\mid Z\bigr),\\
  Z
  &= (A,G_3,L,M,X_{\mathrm{mRNA}},\mathrm{coverage},\mathrm{mappability},\mathrm{codon\ counts},\mathrm{baseline\ codon\ terms}),
\end{aligned}$$
with the additional requirement that the design matrix has sufficient rank inside the tested stratum. The statistic is meaningful only where $W$ marks a wobble-asymmetric context; if the third-position flip is weight-symmetric, the residual coordinate remains a codon-topology boundary and is not promoted to a translation readout.

\paragraph{Current contact status.}
The position-resolved ribosome-occupancy contact names the needed readout surface: codon-indexed or fixed-window ribosome-footprint occupancy joined to ordered coding-sequence coordinates in the same organism and the same CDS. The matched mRNA contact names the denominator or covariate needed to separate translation-layer signal from transcript abundance. The tAI or stAI contact may supply bounded codon-adaptation weights, but such modeled weights are not observed ribosome occupancy. These contacts specify what must be joined; they do not supply the joined matrix in the present finite row. The supplied run $8742a224b538$ therefore cannot certify $\Delta_{f_3}>0$, cannot provide a controlled $p$-value, and cannot determine whether the effect survives simpler baselines.

\paragraph{Local boundary.}
The chapter permits one narrow interpretation: $f_3$-stress is a candidate residual coordinate whose relevance to translation must be tested on matched, position-resolved footprint rows after the stated controls. It does not follow from the present row that $f_3$-stress realizes translation, changes protein abundance, alters folding, establishes physical admissibility, determines function, explains a mechanism, produces a phenotype, or states a universal biological law. Those claims require separate layer-matched contacts and finite statistics at their own readout surfaces. Until the same-window ribosome-footprint, mRNA, codon-count, and wobble-weight rows are present, the admissible conclusion is only that the packet identifies a specific missing translation-window experiment.
