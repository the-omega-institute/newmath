\subsection{Finite-window status of the protein-solubility route}
\label{sec:bioreality-f3-stress-solubility-esol}

\paragraph{Claim record.}
The record considered here is indexed by \texttt{h3.cross\_layer\_relation.protein\_solubility.protein\_hydrophobic\_clustering\_solubility\_esol}.  Its biological surface is not a direct protein-solubility assay for the $f_3$ coordinate.  The admissible object is narrower: a finite coding-sequence window table in which each row carries a synonymous third-position residual coordinate, amino-acid composition controls, a wobble-weight asymmetry flag, and a matched position-resolved ribosome-occupancy readout.  The current finite audit row for the named protein-solubility route reports $n=3198$ rows and a bounded run time of $60.0$ seconds under run \texttt{8742a224b538}, verified at \texttt{2026-06-27T00:47:31+00:00}.  A separate finite audit row in the same evidence packet, \texttt{h3.cross\_layer\_relation.nterminal\_acetylation.nterminal\_acetylation\_occupancy\_human}, reports $n=1426$ rows, run time $5.372$ seconds, and an abundance-control flag equal to false under run \texttt{90ea6b5f7fc7}.  The second row is cited only as an additional finite audit surface demonstrating that the packet distinguishes row-counted workflow evidence from downstream biological interpretation; it is not used as evidence for solubility, translation, folding, or localization.

\paragraph{Finite row type.}
A valid row has the form
$$
\begin{aligned}
r = (&o, c, b, q_{f_3}, a_{\mathrm{wobble}}, z_{\mathrm{aa}}, z_{\mathrm{GC3}}, z_{\mathrm{len}}, z_M, \\
&x_{\mathrm{mRNA}}, y_{\mathrm{RPF}}, e_{\mathrm{rank}}, e_{\mathrm{break}}),
\end{aligned}
$$
where $o$ is the organism, $c$ is the coding-sequence or protein identifier, $b$ is the codon-position bin or local window, $q_{f_3}$ is the third-position synonymous residual after amino-acid projection, $a_{\mathrm{wobble}}$ records whether the attached codon weight surface breaks the $f_3$ symmetry, $z_{\mathrm{aa}}$ is the amino-acid-bin control, $z_{\mathrm{GC3}}$ is the third-position GC control, $z_{\mathrm{len}}$ is the length control, $z_M$ is the methionine-density baseline, $x_{\mathrm{mRNA}}$ is the matched transcript abundance control, $y_{\mathrm{RPF}}$ is the same-window ribosome-footprint or occupancy readout, and $e_{\mathrm{rank}}$ and $e_{\mathrm{break}}$ record rank sufficiency and the declared stopping condition.  Rows lacking the same organism, same coding sequence, same local window, codon counts, wobble weights, mRNA abundance, and footprint occupancy are outside the admissible row type for the translation-window test.

\paragraph{Local statistic.}
The intended finite comparison is a controlled residual test, not a global biological law.  In a valid panel $P$, the admissible score has the form
$$
\begin{aligned}
S_{f_3}(P)
  &= \Delta\mathcal{L}\bigl(y_{\mathrm{RPF}} \mid z_{\mathrm{aa}},z_{\mathrm{GC3}},z_{\mathrm{len}},z_M,x_{\mathrm{mRNA}},q_{f_3},a_{\mathrm{wobble}}\bigr) \\
  &\quad - \Delta\mathcal{L}\bigl(y_{\mathrm{RPF}} \mid z_{\mathrm{aa}},z_{\mathrm{GC3}},z_{\mathrm{len}},z_M,x_{\mathrm{mRNA}}\bigr),
\end{aligned}
$$
with the additional condition that the positive term is evaluated only on wobble-asymmetric contexts.  The statistic is meaningful only after simpler codon-count and amino-acid-bin baselines are present and after rank checks pass.  The number $3198$ therefore certifies the size of the finite protein-solubility audit surface available to this record, but it does not by itself certify a nonzero $S_{f_3}(P)$, a $p$-value, an effect size, a list of residual coordinates, or a protein-level consequence.

\paragraph{Interpretation boundary.}
The working hypothesis is that a synonymous third-position residual may improve a bounded position-resolved translation-window readout in contexts where codon adaptation weights are not invariant under the $f_3$ flip.  This is a translation-readout boundary claim.  It is not a claim that the same coordinate measures protein abundance, solubility, folding, hydrophobic clustering, localization, stability, physical feasibility, molecular function, organismal phenotype, mechanism, adaptation, causation, or universality.  A protein-solubility conclusion would require a separate protein-level contact joined to the same coding-sequence identifiers and evaluated after the translation-readout step survives.  A folding or hydrophobic-clustering conclusion would require a structure-level or biophysical contact, again joined at the relevant protein rows and controlled against amino-acid composition and abundance.  The present finite packet does not supply those contacts.

\paragraph{Current finite verdict.}
The record is ready as a self-contained boundary chapter because its admissible carrier, controls, readout, and forbidden promotions are finite and explicit.  The verified scalar evidence actually available here is limited to row counts and run times: $3198$ rows in the named protein-solubility audit route and $1426$ rows in the auxiliary acetylation audit route.  No $R$ list, $M$ list, $\lambda$ value, module classification, hit rate, controlled $p$-value, matched footprint matrix, or computed same-window $f_3$ ribosome-occupancy statistic is present in the supplied finite data.  Consequently, the chapter records a precise empirical request: assemble the same-organism, same-CDS, same-window panel and compute the controlled translation-window statistic.  Until that row-joined statistic exists and passes the rank and break-condition checks, the $f_3$ residual remains a codon-topology boundary variable rather than an observed translation, solubility, folding, physical-admissibility, function, mechanism, or universal-law result.
