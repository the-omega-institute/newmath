\paragraph{Row schema.}
The admissible row is a window-level tuple keyed by organism, coding sequence or protein identifier, and codon-position bin.  Its mathematical coordinate is the residual $f_3$-stress left after amino-acid projection, together with amino-acid-bin controls, GC3, length, $M$-density, codon-count controls, a wobble-weight asymmetry indicator, matched mRNA abundance, and a position-resolved ribosome-occupancy residual.  In this chapter the coordinate is not allowed to stand alone.  The finite row is meaningful only when the codon window, wobble-weight surface, mRNA control, and ribosome-footprint occupancy are all joined at the same organism, same coding sequence, and same local window.  A contact name or workflow eligibility event is therefore insufficient; the contact must supply the rows on which the statistic is computed.

\paragraph{Statistic to be tested.}
Let $Q_i$ denote the $f_3$-stress coordinate for window $i$, let $A_i$ collect amino-acid composition or amino-acid-bin controls, let $G_i$ denote GC3, let $L_i$ denote coding-sequence length, let $M_i$ denote the $M$-density baseline, let $W_i$ record whether the third-position wobble weights break $f_3$-symmetry, let $R_i$ be the matched position-resolved ribosome-occupancy residual, and let $X_i$ collect coverage, mappability, batch, and matched mRNA controls.  The intended finite test is not a global biological law; it is a bounded same-window comparison of whether $Q_i$ contributes to $R_i$ only inside wobble-asymmetric contexts after the declared controls have been included.  In schematic form the admissible comparison is
$$
\begin{aligned}
R_i &= \beta_Q Q_i + \beta_W W_i + \beta_{QW}(Q_i W_i) + \gamma_A A_i + \gamma_G G_i + \gamma_L L_i + \gamma_M M_i + \gamma_X X_i + \varepsilon_i,

A positive result would require nondegenerate rank, sufficient coverage, mappability limits, and a simpler-baseline ablation showing that the $f_3$-stress term improves the translation-window readout beyond amino-acid composition, GC3, length, $M$-density, codon counts, wobble weights, and matched mRNA abundance.  The supplied finite facts give the row count and runtime, but they do not provide coefficients, $p$-values, confidence intervals, rank diagnostics, module classification, hit rate, or lambda values.

The verified table size $n=3198$ is enough to state that the run was finite and auditable, but it is not enough to promote the packet to a measured translation result.
