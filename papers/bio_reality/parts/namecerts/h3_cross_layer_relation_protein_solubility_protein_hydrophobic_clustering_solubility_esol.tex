\paragraph{Admissible reading.}
The scientific reading is deliberately narrow.  The $f_3$ coordinate may be treated as a
codon-topological residual only until it is attached to a measured or explicitly bounded
translation readout.  A translation-window claim would require same-organism, same-CDS,
same-window joins among ordered CDS coordinates, codon counts, bounded tAI or stAI wobble
weights, matched mRNA abundance, and codon-indexed ribosome-footprint occupancy.  After
that join, the test would ask whether the $f_3$ residual improves a bounded
position-window translation statistic in wobble-asymmetric contexts after controls for
amino-acid bin or position, GC3, CDS length, methionine density, codon counts, mRNA
abundance, coverage, mappability, rank sufficiency, and simpler codon baselines.  The row
as supplied does not yet contain that joined matrix, so the valid conclusion is a boundary
status: the packet names the finite surface needed for a translation-window test but does
not itself supply the test statistic.

\paragraph{Solubility boundary.}
The solubility phrase in the claim id does not by itself create a protein-solubility
measurement.  A positive hydrophobic-clustering--ESOL result would need a same-protein
finite table containing the hydrophobic-clustering feature, the ESOL or ESOL-derived
solubility readout, and the control matrix used to separate codon-window effects from
amino-acid composition and protein-level confounding.  None of the supplied finite fields
contains the corresponding coefficient, residual, confidence interval, $p$-value,
classification, or hit-rate.  Thus the $3198$-row verified run can be cited only as the
available finite experiment footprint for this claim id, not as evidence that $f_3$-stress,
hydrophobic clustering, or wobble asymmetry predicts solubility.

\paragraph{Local non-promotion rule.}
The row does not license translation realization from codon geometry alone.  It does not
license measured ribosome occupancy from modeled tAI or stAI weights alone.  It does not
license protein abundance, folding, physical admissibility, localization, biological
function, mechanism, phenotype, or universality from the $f_3$ residual, the wobble flag,
the review status, or the solubility-linked claim id.  Each of those layers would require a
separate matched contact carrying its own finite readout and its own control matrix.  In
particular, a protein-solubility statement would need an explicit solubility or ESOL row;
a folding statement would need a structure-level row; a mechanism statement would need a
perturbational or kinetic contact; and a universal statement would require a design that is
not present in this finite record.
