\paragraph{Finite surface.}
This record fixes a bounded organism-level readout for B$^{*}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates after the amino-acid quotient has already been taken.  The finite surface has sixteen organism rows at assembly time, with twelve rows complete for the $M^{QTP}$ cross-layer feature summaries.  The complete rows are \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}, \emph{Mycobacterium smegmatis} str. The missingness is not treated as a biological signal; it is only the present boundary of the finite table.

\paragraph{Domain comparison.}
The domain test compares the $M^{QTP}$ fraction against the three coarse domains represented in the complete twelve-row table.  The group summaries are small and unbalanced: archaea has $n=1$ with mean $0.01850668564589326$, bacteria has $n=3$ with mean $0.4351192457799102$, and eukaryotes have $n=8$ with mean $0.3960177769015294$.  The largest-minus-smallest group-mean contrast is $0.41661256013401693$, with bacteria above archaea by this descriptive contrast.  The deterministic permutation null does not support a domain-level call: the variance-ratio statistic is $0.8889980452277374$ and the right-tail permutation value is $p=0.4155844155844156$ over $1000$ trials.  Thus the table records visible heterogeneity, not a resolved domain law.

\paragraph{Dominant-coordinate contingency.}
The dominant-coordinate table also uses twelve rows.  The contingency statistic is Pearson chi-square $9.399999999999999$, with Cramer's $V=0.6258327785172862$ and a right-tail permutation value $p=0.5814185814185814$ across $1000$ trials.  The observed cell counts are sparse: the single archaeal row is Arg\_AGR; the bacterial rows split one each among Arg\_AGR, K\_AAA, and Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY; the eukaryotic rows place three rows at Arg\_AGR, one at Ile\_AUA, one at Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY, and three at f3\_stress.  The top coordinate by domain is therefore Arg\_AGR for archaea with count $1$ of $1$, Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY for bacteria with count $1$ of $3$ under the recorded tie handling, and f3\_stress for eukaryotes with count $3$ of $8$.  Because the permutation value is large and the table is sparse, the correct reading is coordinate heterogeneity without a settled domain preference.

In the measured translation-efficiency contact, four organisms have joined CDS-level TE rows after matched mRNA availability: \emph{D. rerio} has $n=13140$, \emph{E. coli} K--12 MG1655 has $n=3415$, \emph{H. sapiens} has $n=11554$, and \emph{S. cerevisiae} has $n=5128$, all with control-column count $24$ and rank $23$.  The measured-TE absorption fractions are $0.01759429001506974$, $0.0010982956489726373$, $0.0028947149257963897$, and $0.0002762783754375593$ respectively.  The tRNA-gene-copy tAI contact uses complete CDS joins for \emph{D. rerio}, \emph{H. sapiens}, and \emph{S. cerevisiae}, with joined row counts $14410$, $19053$, and $5815$ and tRNA gene counts $8846$, $431$, and $273$.  Its absorption fractions are $0.020817102845637092$, $0.00005426229300865148$, and $0.001530747712165342$.  These numbers show that the meta table is not merely a label list; it is tied to finite joined panels with ranks, coordinates, and row counts.

\paragraph{Downstream bounded contacts.}
Protein-feature and turnover readouts remain descriptive contacts rather than mechanism claims.  The feature panel joins $2242$ \emph{D. rerio} proteins, $18335$ human proteins, and $5682$ yeast proteins against four coarse feature candidates.  Only selected candidate absorptions exceed their degree-of-freedom matched Bonferroni thresholds, for example \emph{D. rerio} PTM density with absorption $0.014211240113472575$ above threshold $0.003775603188484381$, human complex membership with $0.0075271999877804495$ above $0.0006605662314432479$, human PTM density with $0.014884825920540636$ above $0.00042094892238120357$, and human transmembrane count with $0.0015569064882873212$ above $0.0002697448894057371$.  Yeast turnover is a single-organism half-life contact with $n=3640$ joined rows; its modeled-complement value is $0.2232704509412724$, the full-join reference is $0.21173219572706703$, the absolute difference is $0.011538255214205373$, and the turnover absorption is only $0.0009086031889966327$.  A localization-boundary run is also present with $n=8696$ joined rows, $657$ mitochondrial rows, and $2000$ permutations.  These contacts can inform bounded downstream summaries, but they do not turn the B$^{*}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates into protein-function, folding, or phenotype evidence.

\paragraph{Local verdict.}
The finite result is a cross-organism specificity record with computed descriptive tests and explicit null controls.  It supports the statement that organism-level B$^{*}_{Q6}$ cross-layer summaries are heterogeneous and can be compared to domain, GC3, tRNA-pool size, and proteome-size proxies on the present finite table.  It does not support a causal rule, a phylogenetically corrected inference, a ribosome mechanism, a universal codon-usage law, a protein-abundance law, folding or physical admissibility, molecular function, system phenotype, or any general biological mechanism.  Such claims would require separate layer-matched contacts and statistics beyond the finite rows reported here.
