\paragraph{Local boundary.}
The chapter's positive content is limited to a finite cross-organism comparison: a $16$-row assembled table, a $12$-row complete-feature testing set, domain and coordinate summaries, GC3 entries, tRNA/proteome covariate tests, and permutation-calibrated descriptive statistics.  It does not assert translation realization from codon composition, does not infer ribosome occupancy from tAI or stAI weights, and does not infer protein abundance, folding, physical admissibility, biological function, mechanism, phenotype, phylogenetic correction, or universality.  Any such statement would require its own layer-matched contact and a statistic computed on rows joined at the relevant biological resolution.  In the present finite panel, the strongest defensible conclusion is negative in scope: the $B^*_{Q6}$ organism-specific summaries are heterogeneous and computable, but the reported permutation probabilities do not license a resolved domain, GC3, tRNA-pool, or proteome-size law.
