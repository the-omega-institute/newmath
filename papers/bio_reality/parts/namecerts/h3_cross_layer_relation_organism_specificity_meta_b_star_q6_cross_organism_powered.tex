\subsection{Organism-specific B$^*_{Q6}$ cross-layer summaries}\label{subsec:bioreality-q6-organism-specificity-meta}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite readout surface.}
This record tests whether organism-level summaries of the $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinates show a stable cross-organism pattern when compared with domain membership, GC3, tRNA-pool size, and proteome-size proxies. The finite table contains $16$ organism rows at assembly time, of which $12$ rows have the complete feature set needed for the $M^{QTP}$ comparisons. The complete rows are \texttt{saccharomyces\_cerevisiae}, \texttt{mycobacterium\_smegmatis\_str\_mc2\_155}, \texttt{escherichia\_coli\_k12\_mg1655}, \texttt{homo\_sapiens}, \texttt{danio\_rerio}, \texttt{gallus\_gallus}, \texttt{bacillus\_subtilis\_subsp\_subtilis\_str\_168}, \texttt{mus\_musculus}, \texttt{caenorhabditis\_elegans}, \texttt{drosophila\_melanogaster}, \texttt{sulfolobus\_solfataricus}, and \texttt{rattus\_norvegicus}. Four rows remain outside the complete $M^{QTP}$ comparison: \texttt{pseudomonas\_aeruginosa\_pao1}, \texttt{halobacterium\_salinarum}, \texttt{dictyostelium\_discoideum}, and \texttt{arabidopsis\_thaliana}. Thus the powered statement is a finite descriptive meta-analysis on the $12$ complete rows, not a population law over organisms.

\paragraph{Domain comparison.}
The $M^{QTP}$ fraction was compared across domain classes by the ratio of between-domain mean square to within-domain mean square. The statistic was $0.8889980452277374$ on $12$ complete rows, with a deterministic permutation right-tail value $p=0.4155844155844156$ over $1000$ trials. The observed domain means were $0.01850668564589326$ for archaea with $n=1$, $0.4351192457799102$ for bacteria with $n=3$, and $0.3960177769015294$ for eukaryotes with $n=8$. The max-minus-min group-mean contrast was $0.41661256013401693$, with bacteria the highest-mean group and archaea the lowest-mean group. Because the permutation value is not small and the archaeal group has only one complete row, this comparison reports heterogeneity in the finite table rather than a resolved domain effect.

\paragraph{Continuous proxy comparisons.}
The same $12$ complete rows were tested against GC3, tRNA gene count, and proteome size. For GC3, Pearson correlation between $M^{QTP}$ fraction and GC3 was negative, with $r=-0.3062616500469447$, $|r|=0.3062616500469447$, and two-sided permutation value $p=0.35264735264735264$ over $1000$ trials. For log tRNA gene count, the direction was positive but small, with $r=0.06029405206037823$, $|r|=0.06029405206037823$, and $p=0.8621378621378621$. For log proteome size, the direction was negative and near zero, with $r=-0.033628115789504955$, $|r|=0.033628115789504955$, and $p=0.913086913086913$. These three tests agree in scope: the table supports numerical comparison of $M^{QTP}$ against organism-level proxies, but it does not isolate a cross-organism explanatory axis.

\paragraph{Dominant-coordinate contingency.}
The dominant-coordinate table used $12$ rows and compared domain against the leading $B^*_{Q6}$ coordinate. The Pearson chi-square statistic was $9.399999999999999$, Cramer's $V$ was $0.6258327785172862$, and the right-tail permutation value was $p=0.5814185814185814$ over $1000$ trials. The archaeal row assigned \texttt{Arg\_AGR} once. The bacterial rows assigned \texttt{Arg\_AGR}, \texttt{K\_AAA}, and \texttt{Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY} once each, with \texttt{Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY} reported as the top coordinate only by tie-broken finite count. The eukaryote rows assigned \texttt{Arg\_AGR} three times, \texttt{f3\_stress} three times, \texttt{Ile\_AUA} once, and \texttt{Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY} once; \texttt{f3\_stress} was the reported eukaryote top coordinate with count $3$ among $8$ rows. The chi-square and Cramer's $V$ describe a visibly uneven finite table, but the permutation result blocks a claim of domain-specific dominant-coordinate enrichment.

\paragraph{GC3 coverage.}
GC3 was computed for $15$ named organisms while \texttt{arabidopsis\_thaliana} remained missing. The computed values included $0.3809678913179245$ for \texttt{saccharomyces\_cerevisiae}, $0.8720662993842282$ for \texttt{mycobacterium\_smegmatis\_str\_mc2\_155}, $0.5642064879164637$ for \texttt{escherichia\_coli\_k12\_mg1655}, $0.5807133823494395$ for \texttt{homo\_sapiens}, $0.5450598668769285$ for \texttt{danio\_rerio}, $0.5467395284798344$ for \texttt{gallus\_gallus}, $0.4467139786396889$ for \texttt{bacillus\_subtilis\_subsp\_subtilis\_str\_168}, $0.577302922262734$ for \texttt{mus\_musculus}, $0.3973579615210404$ for \texttt{caenorhabditis\_elegans}, $0.6313215697461184$ for \texttt{drosophila\_melanogaster}, $0.3061845898275272$ for \texttt{sulfolobus\_solfataricus}, and $0.5817750962555773$ for \texttt{rattus\_norvegicus}. Additional computed GC3 values outside the complete $M^{QTP}$ set were $0.8806155914396909$ for \texttt{pseudomonas\_aeruginosa\_pao1}, $0.8944468418285529$ for \texttt{halobacterium\_salinarum}, and $0.14471498077638234$ for \texttt{dictyostelium\_discoideum}. The missing \texttt{arabidopsis\_thaliana} value is a coverage gap, not negative evidence about the residual coordinates.

\paragraph{Local interpretation.}
The finite result is a bounded cross-organism summary. It records that $B^*_{Q6}$-derived quantities can be tabulated against domain, GC3, tRNA-pool size, and proteome-size proxies, and that deterministic permutation nulls were computed on the available complete rows. It does not establish causality, a translation mechanism, phylogenetic independence, or a universal codon-usage rule. The organisms are not treated as independent evolutionary replicates, and no phylogenetic comparative correction was applied. The modeled $M^{QTP}$, $R^2_{QP}$, and dominant-coordinate quantities are statistical projections from prior finite experiments; they are not themselves ribosome-profiling measurements.

\paragraph{Scope boundary.}
No translation-efficiency survival, ribosome occupancy, protein abundance, folding state, physical admissibility, molecular function, organismal phenotype, mechanism, or universal biological law follows from this meta table alone. Such claims require a separate layer-matched contact and its own controlled statistic. In particular, a domain contrast or GC3 correlation in this finite table cannot be promoted to a translation-layer survival statement, and the absence of a small permutation value cannot be promoted to biological irrelevance of $B^*_{Q6}$. The supported statement is narrower: on the $12$ complete organism rows, the tested cross-organism summaries were computed, the main permutation values were $0.4155844155844156$, $0.35264735264735264$, $0.8621378621378621$, $0.913086913086913$, and $0.5814185814185814$, and none of these finite tests licenses a resolved organism-domain, GC3, tRNA-pool, or proteome-size explanation for the residual-coordinate summaries.
