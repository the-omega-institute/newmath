\subsection{Organism-specific cross-layer readout for the $B^*_{Q6}$ residual basis}
\label{sec:bioreality-q6-organism-specificity-meta}

\paragraph{Finite surface.}
The organism-specificity packet records a bounded cross-organism readout for the $B^*_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates. The carrier is a finite table whose rows are organisms and whose columns summarize the behavior of residual-coordinate projections after amino-acid composition, GC3, length, and codon-support controls have already been used in the coordinate-level experiments. The assembled meta table contains $16$ organism rows, of which $12$ have the complete feature set used for the reported $M^{QTP}$ comparisons. The four rows still outside the complete $M^{QTP}$ feature panel are \texttt{pseudomonas\_aeruginosa\_pao1}, \texttt{halobacterium\_salinarum}, \texttt{dictyostelium\_discoideum}, and \texttt{arabidopsis\_thaliana}. Thus the numerical tests below are not a population law over life; they are finite descriptive summaries over the complete rows.

\paragraph{Per-organism covariates.}
The GC3 coordinate was computed for $15$ named organisms, with \texttt{arabidopsis\_thaliana} still requiring data. The observed GC3 values span a wide range: \texttt{dictyostelium\_discoideum} has $0.14471498077638234$, \texttt{sulfolobus\_solfataricus} has $0.3061845898275272$, \texttt{saccharomyces\_cerevisiae} has $0.3809678913179245$, \texttt{caenorhabditis\_elegans} has $0.3973579615210404$, \texttt{bacillus\_subtilis\_subsp\_subtilis\_str\_168} has $0.4467139786396889$, \texttt{danio\_rerio} has $0.5450598668769285$, \texttt{gallus\_gallus} has $0.5467395284798344$, \texttt{escherichia\_coli\_k12\_mg1655} has $0.5642064879164637$, \texttt{mus\_musculus} has $0.577302922262734$, \texttt{homo\_sapiens} has $0.5807133823494395$, \texttt{rattus\_norvegicus} has $0.5817750962555773$, \texttt{drosophila\_melanogaster} has $0.6313215697461184$, \texttt{mycobacterium\_smegmatis\_str\_mc2\_155} has $0.8720662993842282$, \texttt{pseudomonas\_aeruginosa\_pao1} has $0.8806155914396909$, and \texttt{halobacterium\_salinarum} has $0.8944468418285529$. These values are used only as organism-level covariates for the finite meta test; they do not by themselves certify any translation, protein, structural, or functional interpretation.

\paragraph{Domain comparison of the mediated fraction.}
On the $12$ complete rows, the $M^{QTP}$ fraction was compared across domain labels by the statistic between-domain mean square divided by within-domain mean square. The observed variance ratio is $0.8889980452277374$ with a right-tail deterministic-permutation value $p=0.4155844155844156$ over $1000$ trials. The group means are $0.01850668564589326$ for archaea with $n=1$, $0.4351192457799102$ for bacteria with $n=3$, and $0.3960177769015294$ for eukaryotes with $n=8$. The largest-minus-smallest group-mean contrast is $0.41661256013401693$, with bacteria above archaea by group mean. Because the permutation value is not small and the archaeal group has only one complete row, this result is read as heterogeneous organism dependence rather than a resolved domain-level rule.

\paragraph{Dominant-coordinate contingency.}
The dominant-coordinate table was also tested against domain. The contingency table has $n=12$ complete rows and six observed dominant-coordinate labels. Archaea contributes one \texttt{Arg\_AGR} row. Bacteria contributes one \texttt{Arg\_AGR}, one \texttt{K\_AAA}, and one \texttt{Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY} row. Eukaryotes contribute three \texttt{Arg\_AGR}, one \texttt{Ile\_AUA}, one \texttt{Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY}, and three \texttt{f3\_stress} rows. The Pearson chi-square statistic is $9.399999999999999$, Cramer's $V$ is $0.6258327785172862$, and the right-tail permutation value is $p=0.5814185814185814$ over $1000$ trials. The top coordinate by domain is \texttt{Arg\_AGR} for archaea with count $1$ of $1$, \texttt{Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY} for bacteria with count $1$ of $3$, and \texttt{f3\_stress} for eukaryotes with count $3$ of $8$. The finite table therefore shows coordinate heterogeneity, but not a statistically resolved domain enrichment under this null.

\paragraph{Connection with lower readouts.}
The meta surface is constrained by the measured and modeled readout experiments that feed it. In the measured ribosome-profiling translation-efficiency panel, four organisms were joined with matched abundance controls: \texttt{danio\_rerio} has $n=13140$, \texttt{escherichia\_coli\_k12\_mg1655} has $n=3415$, \texttt{homo\_sapiens} has $n=11554$, and \texttt{saccharomyces\_cerevisiae} has $n=5128$, each with rank $23$ for the $Z$ controls. The measured-TE absorption values are $0.01759429001506974$, $0.0010982956489726373$, $0.0028947149257963897$, and $0.0002762783754375593$ for those organisms respectively. The measured-tRNA tAI comparison gives absorption values $0.020817102845637092$ for \texttt{danio\_rerio}, $5.426229300865148\times 10^{-5}$ for \texttt{homo\_sapiens}, and $0.001530747712165342$ for \texttt{saccharomyces\_cerevisiae}. Protein-feature absorptions are candidate-specific and small: for example human \texttt{ptm\_density} absorbs $0.014884825920540636$, human \texttt{complex\_member} absorbs $0.0075271999877804495$, and zebrafish \texttt{ptm\_density} absorbs $0.014211240113472575$, while the yeast turnover panel absorbs only $0.0009086031889966327$ on $3640$ joined turnover rows. These quantities support the meta table as a descriptive cross-layer summary, not as a mechanism.

\paragraph{Local verdict.}
The finite result is a ready organism-specificity record with a negative finding at the meta-inference level: the available cross-organism rows show heterogeneity in $B^*_{Q6}$ readout summaries, but the reported permutation tests do not resolve a domain, GC3, tRNA-pool-size, or proteome-size explanation. The strongest apparent categorical contrast is the bacteria-versus-archaea group-mean difference in $M^{QTP}$ fraction, but it is carried by $n=3$ bacterial rows against $n=1$ archaeal row and has $p=0.4155844155844156$. The strongest continuous magnitude among the three reported correlations is the GC3 comparison with $|r|=0.3062616500469447$ and $p=0.35264735264735264$, which remains exploratory.

\paragraph{Boundary of interpretation.}
This chapter licenses only a finite descriptive statement about organism-specific summaries of residual-coordinate projections. It does not establish translation realization, ribosome mechanism, protein abundance, folding, structural order, physical admissibility, molecular function, organism phenotype, adaptation, causation, phylogenetically corrected inference, or a universal biological law. Any such statement would require a separate layer-matched contact and its own controlled statistic. In particular, modeled-tAI mediation is not ribosome profiling, a dominant-coordinate table is not a mechanism table, and a cross-organism correlation is not independent biological replication when the rows are not phylogenetically corrected.
