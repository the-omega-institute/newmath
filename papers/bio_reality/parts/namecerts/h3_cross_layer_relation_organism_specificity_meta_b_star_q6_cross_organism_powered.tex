\paragraph{Local interpretation.}
The finite organism profile supports the following narrow reading. The $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinates produce heterogeneous organism-specific summaries, and the observed summaries can be compared to domain, GC3, tRNA-pool size, and proteome-size covariates on the available complete panel. None of the recorded permutation tests licenses a strong cross-organism explanatory law: dominant coordinate versus domain has $p=0.5814185814$, $M^{QTP}$ versus domain has $p=0.4155844156$, $M^{QTP}$ versus GC3 has $p=0.3526473526$, $M^{QTP}$ versus log proteome size has $p=0.9130869131$, and $M^{QTP}$ versus log tRNA gene count has $p=0.8621378621$. The correct conclusion is therefore bounded heterogeneity with unresolved organism specificity, not a resolved domain mechanism.

\paragraph{Non-promotion rule.}
No translation, folding, physical admissibility, function, mechanism, phenotype, or universality follows from this record by itself. The table contains statistical projections and finite readout comparisons. A measured translation claim requires a matched translation-efficiency or ribosome-footprint contact with the declared controls; a protein-output claim requires a separate protein-abundance contact; structure, physical state, molecular function, phenotype, and mechanism each require their own layer-appropriate measurements. The present chapter records where the organism-specific $B^*_{Q6}$ summaries vary and where the finite null tests fail to resolve a predictor, while deliberately stopping before any higher-layer biological interpretation.
