\subsection{Organism-specific cross-layer power of the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual surface}
\label{sec:bioreality-q6-organism-specificity-meta}

\paragraph{Finite surface.}
The record considered here is the finite cross-organism readout surface for $B^{*}_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates after amino-acid quotienting.  Its rows are organisms, its columns are organism-level summaries of the already computed residual projections, and its admissible comparisons are deliberately narrow: domain membership, GC3, tRNA-gene-copy scale, proteome-size scale, and the dominant residual coordinate selected by the fitted summaries.  The assembled table contains $16$ organism rows, but the complete feature panel used for the reported $M^{QTP}$ tests contains $12$ rows.  The complete rows are $\mathrm{saccharomyces\_cerevisiae}$, $\mathrm{mycobacterium\_smegmatis\_str\_mc2\_155}$, $\mathrm{escherichia\_coli\_k12\_mg1655}$, $\mathrm{homo\_sapiens}$, $\mathrm{danio\_rerio}$, $\mathrm{gallus\_gallus}$, $\mathrm{bacillus\_subtilis\_subsp\_subtilis\_str\_168}$, $\mathrm{mus\_musculus}$, $\mathrm{caenorhabditis\_elegans}$, $\mathrm{drosophila\_melanogaster}$, $\mathrm{sulfolobus\_solfataricus}$, and $\mathrm{rattus\_norvegicus}$.  The incomplete rows for the $M^{QTP}$ panel are $\mathrm{pseudomonas\_aeruginosa\_pao1}$, $\mathrm{halobacterium\_salinarum}$, $\mathrm{dictyostelium\_discoideum}$, and $\mathrm{arabidopsis\_thaliana}$.

\paragraph{Domain contingency.}

The observed statistic is $\chi^2=9.4$, the permutation right-tail value over $1000$ deterministic trials is $p=0.5814185814185814$, and Cramer's association value is $V=0.6258327785172862$.  The finite counts are

The top coordinate by domain is $\mathrm{Arg\_AGR}$ for the single archaeal row, $\mathrm{Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY}$ for the bacterial block with count $1$ out of $3$, and $f3\_\mathrm{stress}$ for the eukaryotic block with count $3$ out of $8$.  This is a heterogeneous finite profile, not a resolved domain preference, because the permutation value is large relative to any ordinary enrichment threshold.

\paragraph{Continuous meta-readouts.}
The same $12$ complete rows were tested against continuous organism-level covariates.  For $M^{QTP}$ fraction versus GC3, the Pearson correlation is $r=-0.3062616500469447$ with two-sided permutation value $p=0.35264735264735264$ over $1000$ trials.  For $M^{QTP}$ fraction versus log proteome size, $r=-0.033628115789504955$ and $p=0.913086913086913$.  For $M^{QTP}$ fraction versus log tRNA-gene count, $r=0.06029405206037823$ and $p=0.8621378621378621$.  The domain comparison of $M^{QTP}$ fraction has variance ratio $0.8889980452277374$ and right-tail permutation value $p=0.4155844155844156$ over $1000$ trials.  Its group means are $0.01850668564589326$ for the single archaeal row, $0.4351192457799102$ for the three bacterial rows, and $0.3960177769015294$ for the eight eukaryotic rows; the largest-minus-smallest group-mean contrast is therefore $0.41661256013401693$, with bacteria numerically above archaea in this finite table.  These values are descriptive coordinates of the sampled panel.  They do not supply a population-level law.

\paragraph{Measured translation-efficiency contact used as a boundary check.}
The measured translation-efficiency computation supplies the local readout discipline for this meta chapter.  In that computation, each organism used $9$ residual coordinates, namely $\mathrm{K\_AAA}$, $\mathrm{Arg\_AGR}$, $\mathrm{Ile\_AUA}$, $\mathrm{Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR}$, $\mathrm{Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG}$, $\mathrm{Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY}$, $\mathrm{Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG}$, $\mathrm{Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY}$, and $f3\_\mathrm{stress}$. The joined measured-TE row counts were $13140$ for $\mathrm{danio\_rerio}$, $3415$ for $\mathrm{escherichia\_coli\_k12\_mg1655}$, $11554$ for $\mathrm{homo\_sapiens}$, and $5128$ for $\mathrm{saccharomyces\_cerevisiae}$.  The modeled-complement fractions on those joins were $0.8424312412078521$, $0.6628211177352172$, $0.8566190269974241$, and $0.22386566043976477$, respectively, while the measured-TE absorbed fractions were $0.01759429001506974$, $0.0010982956489726373$, $0.0028947149257963897$, and $0.0002762783754375593$.  These small absorptions are not used here as causal estimates; they only record how much of the residual projection is removed by the measured-TE coordinate under the declared controls.

\paragraph{Local conclusion and boundary.}
The finite evidence supports a bounded statement: organism-specific $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual summaries can be assembled and compared against domain, GC3, tRNA-pool size, and proteome-size proxies, and the resulting permutation tests are computed on an explicitly declared finite panel.  The strongest-looking categorical association in the table, the dominant-coordinate by domain contingency, has $p=0.5814185814185814$ and therefore remains heterogeneous rather than resolved.  The continuous tests likewise remain descriptive, with $p=0.35264735264735264$ for GC3, $p=0.913086913086913$ for log proteome size, and $p=0.8621378621378621$ for log tRNA-gene count.  No claim of translation realization, protein folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, mechanism, phenotype, universality, or phylogenetically corrected inference follows from this chapter.  Any such claim would require its own layer-matched contact and its own statistic; the present record only licenses the finite cross-organism comparison just described.
