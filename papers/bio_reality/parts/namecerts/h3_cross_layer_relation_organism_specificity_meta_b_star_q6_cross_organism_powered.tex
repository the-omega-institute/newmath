\paragraph{Finite readout surface.}
This record treats the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates as organism-indexed statistical readouts, not as direct biological mechanisms.  The assembled table has $16$ organism rows, with $12$ rows complete enough for the $M^{QTP}$ fraction comparisons. Thus the panel is broad enough to expose organism-level variation, but not broad enough to license phylogenetic or universal claims.

\paragraph{Domain comparison for $M^{QTP}$.}
The domain-level test compares $M^{QTP}$ fraction by domain on the $12$ complete rows.  The group means are $0.01850668564589326$ for archaea with $n=1$, $0.4351192457799102$ for bacteria with $n=3$, and $0.3960177769015294$ for eukaryotes with $n=8$.  The largest observed mean is bacterial and the smallest is archaeal, giving a max-minus-min group-mean contrast of $0.41661256013401693$.  The corresponding variance-ratio statistic is $0.8889980452277374$, with deterministic permutation right-tail value $p=0.4155844155844156$ over $1000$ trials.  This is a descriptive heterogeneity entry: it records that the bacterial rows have the highest sample mean in this finite table, while the permutation result does not resolve a domain-level enrichment.

For $M^{QTP}$ fraction against log proteome size, the statistic is $r=-0.033628115789504955$ with $p=0.913086913086913$. In particular, the GC3 comparison is the largest of the three continuous effects in absolute value, with $|r|=0.3062616500469447$, but it remains a finite-panel association rather than a rule about codon usage, translation efficiency, or protein output.

The contingency surface has one archaeal row, assigned to $\mathrm{Arg\_AGR}$; three bacterial rows, with one each in $\mathrm{Arg\_AGR}$, $\mathrm{K\_AAA}$, and $\mathrm{Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY}$; and eight eukaryotic rows, with three in $\mathrm{Arg\_AGR}$, one in $\mathrm{Ile\_AUA}$, one in $\mathrm{Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY}$, and three in $f_3\text{-}\mathrm{stress}$.  The Pearson chi-square value is $9.399999999999999$, the Cramer statistic is $0.6258327785172862$, and the right-tail permutation value is $p=0.5814185814185814$ over $1000$ trials.  The top coordinate by domain is $\mathrm{Arg\_AGR}$ for archaea with count $1$ out of $1$, $\mathrm{Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY}$ for bacteria with count $1$ out of $3$, and $f_3\text{-}\mathrm{stress}$ for eukaryotes with count $3$ out of $8$.  The table is therefore visibly heterogeneous, but the permutation result does not justify a resolved domain-specific dominant-coordinate law.

\paragraph{Relation to translation and protein-side contacts.}
This organism-specific summary is downstream of several bounded cross-layer computations, and it should be read through their finite-row limitations.  The measured translation-efficiency computation joined $13140$ zebrafish CDS rows, $3415$ \emph{E. coli} rows, $11554$ human rows, and $5128$ yeast rows, each with rank $23$ controls.  Its measured-TE absorption values were $0.01759429001506974$ for zebrafish, $0.0010982956489726373$ for \emph{E. coli}, $0.0028947149257963897$ for human, and $0.0002762783754375593$ for yeast.  The measured tRNA-gene-copy tAI computation used full CDS joins for zebrafish, human, and yeast, with $8846$, $431$, and $273$ tRNA genes respectively, and produced absorption values $0.020817102845637092$, $0.00005426229300865148$, and $0.001530747712165342$.  The protein-feature computation was more coverage-sensitive: zebrafish had only $2242$ joined feature rows from $3180$ feature records, while human had $18335$ joined rows from $18842$ records and yeast had $5682$ joined rows from $6156$ records.  These side computations explain why the meta-table is reported as a cross-organism descriptive summary instead of a mechanistic closure.

\paragraph{Local interpretation boundary.}
The finite panel supports the following restrained reading: organism-specific $B^{*}_{Q6}$ summaries can be tabulated against domain, GC3, tRNA-pool size, proteome-size proxies, and dominant-coordinate categories, and the resulting null tests can be reported exactly.  The panel does not show a resolved domain effect for $M^{QTP}$ fraction, does not show a resolved continuous association with GC3, log tRNA gene count, or log proteome size, and does not show a resolved domain preference for dominant coordinate.  No translation-realization claim follows from this meta-analysis alone.  No folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, biological mechanism, organismal phenotype, or universal biological law follows from these rows unless a separate layer-matched measurement supplies that evidence and is evaluated under its own controls.  In this chapter the numbers are evidence for finite organism-level heterogeneity of the computed summaries, not evidence that the residual coordinates themselves cause translation, protein abundance, structure, function, or phenotype.
