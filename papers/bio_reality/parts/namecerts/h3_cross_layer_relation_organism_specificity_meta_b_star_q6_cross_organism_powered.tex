\overline{M}^{QTP}_{bacteria} &= 0.4351192457799102,\\

The bacterial mean is the largest and the archaeal mean is the smallest, giving a max-minus-min group-mean contrast of $0.41661256013401693$. The finite readout therefore records heterogeneity in the observed group means, but it does not resolve a domain-level enrichment under this null.

\paragraph{Interpretive boundary.}
The verified result is a finite descriptive meta-analysis: organism-specific $B^{*}_{Q6}$ summaries were compared against domain, GC3, tRNA-pool size, and proteome-size proxies using deterministic permutation nulls.  The result is not causal, is not phylogenetically corrected, and uses a small non-independent organism panel.  Shared ancestry may explain apparent similarities or differences, and modeled-tAI mediation is not a ribosome-profiling measurement.  No translation mechanism, protein folding, physical admissibility, biological function, phenotype, or universal rule follows from these rows.  Any such claim would require a separate layer-matched measurement and a separately stated statistic.
