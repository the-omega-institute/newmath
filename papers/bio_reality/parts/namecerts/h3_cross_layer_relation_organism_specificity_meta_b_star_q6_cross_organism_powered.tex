\paragraph{Local exclusion principle.}
The finite results above license only the following statement: organism-level $B^*_{Q6}$ cross-layer summaries were assembled and tested against domain, GC3, tRNA-pool size, proteome-size scale, and dominant-coordinate labels by finite permutation checks.  They do not establish translation realization by themselves, do not establish folding or structural order, do not establish physical admissibility, do not establish molecular function or phenotype, do not identify a causal mechanism, and do not establish universality.  Any such stronger claim would require a separate layer-matched contact and its own finite statistic.  The small row count is also material: the complete-feature tests use $12$ organisms, the full assembled table has $16$ rows, organisms are not phylogenetically independent, and no phylogenetic comparative correction is included in this record.

\paragraph{Verdict.}
The organism-specificity packet is ready as a bounded BioReality chapter because all reported scalars are finite-row outputs: row counts, domain counts, GC3 values, Pearson coefficients, permutation values, Cramer's $V$, the chi-square statistic, and the $M^{QTP}$ group means are explicitly present.  The chapter therefore records a negative-to-bounded outcome: the panel shows organism heterogeneity, but the reported permutation tests do not support a resolved domain, GC3, tRNA-count, or proteome-size law for the $B^*_{Q6}$ cross-layer summaries.
