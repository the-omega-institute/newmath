\subsection{Punctured Codon Cube at the Code-Read Layer}
\label{sec:bioreality-h0-r-punctured-cube}

\paragraph{Packet scope.}
This packet records a finite window-six codon observation on the standard genetic-code label table.  The carrier is the six-bit codon cube $Q_6$, read only as a coordinate display for the sixty-four codons.  The biological contact is narrower than the cube itself: a curated standard-code table assigns each codon an amino-acid or stop label, and the present packet tests a local tile-shaped split inside that table.  The claim is therefore a code-read claim.  It does not assert that the coordinate geometry is a biological cause, that translation is realized by this geometry, or that any resulting amino-acid string has a folded structure, physical admissibility, cellular function, mechanism, or universality.  Those stronger assertions would require separate contacts of the appropriate kind.

\paragraph{Shape rarity checks.}
Against the finite null model used for the shape test, $N=2000$ sampled tables were evaluated with seed $42$.  The sampler required $57{,}377$ distinct-sample attempts and encountered $55{,}377$ duplicate retries.  Only one exact shape match appeared, giving
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{\mathrm{exact}}=0.0005.
\end{aligned}
$$
There were twenty-five matches at Jaccard similarity at least $0.9$, giving
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{\mathrm{Jaccard}\ge 0.9}=0.0125.
\end{aligned}
$$
A second null model preserving amino-acid classes used $N=2000$ and seed $42$.  It recorded observed $M$-size $20$, exact-match count $0$, and lower-tail count $7$, hence
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{\mathrm{aa\text{-}class\ exact}}&=0.0,\\
p_{\mathrm{aa\text{-}class\ lower\ tail}}&=0.0035.
\end{aligned}
$$
These tests support the statement that the local tile is unusual under the sampled finite rearrangements.  They still do not promote the packet beyond codon-label readback.

\paragraph{Reality contact and exclusions.}
The contact named for this packet is the curated standard-code table.  It supplies codon-to-label assignments and supports local split checks at codon-label resolution.  It is not a measurement of ribosomal translation, folding, structural order, physical admissibility, biological function, or a universal biological law.  The packet also blocks mixed identifier forms and any attempt to reinterpret CUN/UUR or tRNA-Leu wording as structural evidence.  Cross-organism usage and tRNA-copy questions require their own organism-matched contact route.  Within its stated scope, the packet is aligned: the finite codon set, the punctured-cube face data, the spectral values, the sampled null-model rarity, and the removal tests all describe the same local code-read phenomenon.
