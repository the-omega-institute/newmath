\paragraph{Reality contact and local scope.}
The only contact used here is the curated standard-code label table. It says that a codon receives an amino-acid or stop label in the standard-code readout. It does not observe translation as a physical process, a folded protein, an energetic admissibility condition, a biological function, a universal mechanism, or a law governing all organisms. Consequently, the punctured-cube result should be read as follows: window-six coordinates expose a specific local boundary pattern in the standard-code label table, and the finite audits make that pattern numerically inspectable. Any passage from this local code-read pattern to translation, folding, physical admissibility, function, mechanism, or universality would require a separate contact whose measurements actually live at that higher layer. Without such a contact, the higher-layer assertions remain blocked even when the finite codon-label geometry is aligned.
