\subsection{A Punctured Codon Cube in the Standard Code}\label{sec:biorality-h0-r-punctured-cube}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite carrier.}
This record treats the standard-code codon table as a finite labelled object on a six-bit codon display. The carrier is the codon cube $Q_6$, with each RNA codon represented by its window-six coordinate and then read against the curated standard-code assignment of codons to amino-acid or stop labels. The finite row isolates a distinguished set $R$ of thirteen codons:
$$
\begin{aligned}
R = \{&\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\\
&\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UUA}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The independent cardinality row reports $|R|=13$ and gives the same ordered membership list. The punctured-cube row reports that the same set is the selected punctured-cube region and that its face-count vector is
$$
\begin{aligned}
f(R)=(13,16,4),
\end{aligned}
$$
so the observed local object has thirteen vertices, sixteen one-dimensional adjacencies, and four two-dimensional faces in the finite cubical presentation used by the probe.

\paragraph{Local tile meaning.}
The claim is not that the biological code is generated by this geometry. The mathematical observation is narrower: when the standard-code labels are placed on the window-six codon cube, the distinguished label-boundary region $R$ is not an arbitrary-looking subset in the observed coordinate system. It forms a local cubical tile with a punctured-cube profile. The distinctions used by the record are the six-bit codon display, local tile faces, singleton label corners, and three-point label classes. These distinctions are enough to describe a code-layer split, but they are not enough to describe translation chemistry, folded protein geometry, physical admissibility, realized function, a mechanism, or a universal biological law.

\paragraph{Spectral check.}
The finite spectral row gives a second numerical signature for the same coordinate-level object. It reports
$$
\begin{aligned}
\lambda_M &= 0.6752479308830213,\\
\lambda_R &= 0.4758352843197002,\\
\mu &= 3.051487585298128.
\end{aligned}
$$
The associated radial cubic residual is $-6.224354365258478\times 10^{-12}$, also recorded as the cubic polynomial residual. This value is numerically zero at the recorded tolerance and therefore supports the claim that the finite coordinate construction is satisfying its stated spectral consistency check. The row should be read as a finite diagnostic on the codon-coordinate presentation, not as evidence for any higher biological layer.

\paragraph{Null comparisons.}
The shape-significance row uses $N=2000$ sampled alternatives with seed $42$. It records $57377$ distinct-sample attempts and $55377$ duplicate retries. Among these alternatives, there is $1$ exact shape match and $25$ matches with Jaccard similarity at least $0.9$. The corresponding empirical probabilities are
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{\mathrm{exact}} &= 0.0005,\\
p_{\mathrm{Jaccard}\geq 0.9} &= 0.0125.
\end{aligned}
$$
The amino-acid-class-preserving null row is stricter about label-class preservation. It again uses $N=2000$ and seed $42$, records observed $M$-size $20$, finds $0$ exact matches, and gives a lower-tail count of $7$ alternatives with size at most the observed $M$-size. Its reported probabilities are
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{\mathrm{aa\ class\ exact}} &= 0.0,\\
p_{\mathrm{aa\ class\ lower\ tail}} &= 0.0035.
\end{aligned}
$$
These numbers support a code-read statement: the observed local shape is uncommon under the finite null procedures supplied in the row data. They do not by themselves identify a causal biological process.

\paragraph{Reality contact and scope.}
The only reality contact used here is the curated standard-code table, at codon-label resolution. It supports the observation that codons are assigned amino-acid or stop labels and permits local checks of how those labels sit on the six-bit codon cube. It does not observe ribosomal translation, molecular folding, structural order, thermodynamic admissibility, cellular function, organism-level mechanism, or universality across biological settings. Therefore the supported conclusion is deliberately local: the standard-code label table, when expressed in the window-six coordinate system, contains a thirteen-codon region with the recorded punctured-cube face vector, spectral consistency numbers, low empirical null rates, and complete module assignment. Any statement beyond code-layer label readback requires a separate contact at the relevant biological layer.
