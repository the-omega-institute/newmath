\subsection{The punctured codon cube at the code-read layer}
\label{sec:h0-r-punctured-cube}

\paragraph{Finite coordinate record.}
The object considered here is the standard-code codon table viewed through a six-bit codon coordinate display.  The coordinate construction isolates a local boundary tile inside the codon cube $Q_6$ and reads it against the curated codon-to-label table.  The verified subset $R$ has cardinality $13$ and is exactly
$$
\begin{aligned}
R=\{&AAA, AGA, AGG, AUA, CUA, CUC, CUG, CUU,\\
&UAA, UAG, UCA, UGA, UUA\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The same run verifies that this subset has the punctured-cube face count
$$
\begin{aligned}
(f_0,f_1,f_2)=(13,16,4).
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the finite observation is not merely a list of exceptional codons; it is a thirteen-vertex local cubical pattern with sixteen one-dimensional incidences and four square faces.  The row is still a code-table observation: the labels used to identify the codons come from the curated standard-code table, while the cubical pattern comes from the six-bit coordinate representation.

\paragraph{Label classes and modules.}
The thirteen codons are completely assigned by the supplied module classification.  The modules are
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{AGR}&=\{AGA,AGG\},&
\mathrm{ar\_lys}&=\{AAA\},\\
\mathrm{CUN}&=\{CUA,CUC,CUG,CUU\},&
\mathrm{ile\_met}&=\{AUA\},\\
\mathrm{SSN}&=\{UCA,UUA\},&
\mathrm{stop\_trp}&=\{UAA,UAG,UGA\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The classification row reports $R\_size=13$, $assigned\_count=13$, no missing codons, no extra module codons, and no unassigned codons.  In particular, the local tile is not being completed by an implicit biological assumption: every displayed codon in $R$ is explicitly accounted for in the finite module partition.  The three-point stop--tryptophan class $\{UAA,UAG,UGA\}$ and the four-point $CUN$ class are the largest visible components of the tile, while the singleton classes $\{AAA\}$ and $\{AUA\}$ mark label corners in the finite display.

\paragraph{Spectral check.}
The spectral row supplies independent numerical structure for the same coordinate object.  The measured values are
$$
\begin{aligned}
\lambda_M&=0.6752479308830213,\\
\lambda_R&=0.4758352843197002,\\
\mu&=3.051487585298128.
\end{aligned}
$$
The cubic relation is numerically satisfied with residual $-6.224354365258478\times 10^{-12}$, and the radial polynomial has the same residual.  The reported spectral-radius value in tolerance is $0.6752479308830213$.  These data make the punctured-cube row more constrained than a bare cardinality claim: the subset $R$, its ambient comparison object of observed size $20$, and the spectral summary are mutually tied by finite computations rather than by descriptive similarity alone.

\paragraph{Null comparisons.}
The shape-significance run used $N=2000$ samples with seed $42$.  It required $57377$ distinct-sample attempts and encountered $55377$ duplicate retries.  Among those samples, there was $1$ exact shape match, giving
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{\mathrm{exact}}=0.0005.
\end{aligned}
$$
There were $25$ matches with Jaccard score at least $0.9$, giving
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{J\geq 0.9}=0.0125.
\end{aligned}
$$
The amino-acid-class-preserving null run, also with $N=2000$ and seed $42$, reported observed $M$ size $20$, exact-match count $0$, and $7$ samples in the lower tail at or below the observed $M$ size.  Its reported probabilities are
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{\mathrm{aa\ class\ exact}}&=0.0,\\
p_{\mathrm{aa\ class\ lower\ tail}}&=0.0035.
\end{aligned}
$$
These values support a finite significance statement about the local code-table geometry.  They do not by themselves identify a mechanism of translation or a physical cause for the codon table.

\paragraph{Leave-one tests.}
The leave-one-code-out row evaluated $23$ tables and $63$ total targets.  Its baseline hit rate is $0.19821739130434785$, while the hit rate above baseline is $0.9841269841269841$.  The hit rate inside $M-T$ is $0.9365079365079365$, and the rate inside $M-T$ plus the boundary is $0.9841269841269841$.  By family, the row reports $5$ families: alternative yeast has hit rate $0.8333333333333334$ above baseline, while bacterial plastid, candidate other, mitochondrial, and nuclear ciliate each have hit rate $1.0$ above baseline.  The leave-one-codon-out row reports anchor count $0$, boundary count $5$, high closure coverage $8$, predictable count $8$, and recovery rate $1.0$.  These tests indicate that the local tile is stable under finite deletion probes at the code-read level, with the boundary playing a visible role in recovery.

\paragraph{Reality contact and scope.}
The only biological contact used here is the curated standard-code table assigning codons to amino-acid or stop labels.  This contact is sufficient for codon-label readback and for checking the local split induced by the six-bit coordinate display.  It is not an observation of ribosomal translation, protein folding, structural order, physical admissibility, biochemical function, or a universal biological law.  Therefore the chapter claims only that the standard-code labels, when placed in the window-six codon coordinate system, exhibit the verified punctured-cube boundary tile $R$ with the numerical and deletion-test behavior recorded above.  Any promotion from this code-layer statement to translation, folded structure, physical realization, function, mechanism, or universality would require a separate empirical contact designed for that higher layer.
