\subsection{The punctured codon cube at the code-read layer}
\label{sec:h0-r-punctured-cube}

\paragraph{Finite claim.}
The packet concerns a six-coordinate codon display $Q_6$ for the standard genetic-code table. The finite object under inspection is the distinguished codon region $R$ obtained from the local window-six coordinate construction. In the verified row, $R$ is not a vague enriched neighborhood: it is exactly the following thirteen-codon set,
$$
\begin{aligned}
R = \{&\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\\
&\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UUA}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The same run records $|R|=13$ and identifies the local cell count as
$$
\begin{aligned}
(f_0,f_1,f_2)=(13,16,4).
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the claim is a finite combinatorial claim about a punctured cube-shaped codon patch, with thirteen displayed vertices, sixteen local adjacencies, and four square faces. The biological readout used here is only the curated standard-code assignment of codons to amino-acid or stop labels. The contact is therefore at the label-table level: it checks which codons receive which code labels, and it does not observe translation in a cell, protein folding, molecular admissibility, biological activity, a mechanism, or universality.

\paragraph{Coordinate content.}
The local window separates three visible features. First, the codons in $R$ occupy a six-bit coordinate region rather than an unordered list. Second, the region has a tile boundary with the recorded face count $4$, so the support is not just a cardinality coincidence. Third, the code labels place singleton and small-class behavior at specific corners and edges of the patch. The row therefore supports the more precise sentence that the standard-code label table can be read against a punctured finite cube, where the puncture and boundary are part of the tested geometry.

\paragraph{Spectral check.}
The same finite construction has a numerical spectral signature. The verified spectrum records

\begin{aligned}
\lambda_M &= 0.6752479308830213,\\
\lambda_R &= 0.4758352843197002,\\
\mu &= 3.051487585298128.

$$
The radial polynomial residual is $-6.224354365258478\times 10^{-12}$, and the cubic residual is the same number. This is small at the numerical tolerance of the run, so the spectral row is consistent with the finite tile calculation rather than an independent biological mechanism. The value $\lambda_R=0.4758352843197002$ is the spectral quantity attached to the thirteen-codon punctured region, while $\lambda_M=0.6752479308830213$ belongs to the larger module-scale object whose observed size is $20$ in the null-model row.

\paragraph{Randomization evidence.}
The shape was tested against finite random alternatives with seed $42$. In the general shape-significance run, $N=2000$ samples required $57377$ distinct-sample attempts and $55377$ duplicate retries. Only $1$ sample matched the exact observed shape, giving $p_{\mathrm{exact}}=0.0005$. A high-overlap threshold using Jaccard score at least $0.9$ was met by $25$ samples, giving $p_{\mathrm{Jaccard}\geq 0.9}=0.0125$. A separate amino-acid-class-preserving null with $N=2000$ reported no exact shape matches, so $p_{\mathrm{aa\ class\ exact}}=0.0$ at the sampling resolution of the run. The same null recorded $7$ samples in the lower tail at or below the observed module size $20$, giving $p_{\mathrm{aa\ class\ lower\ tail}}=0.0035$. These numbers support the statement that the punctured-tile geometry is not typical under the tested finite reshufflings. They do not convert the geometry into a biological law.

The finite readback is also stable under two leave-out probes. In the leave-one-code-table test, $23$ tables and $63$ targets were evaluated. The baseline hit rate was $0.19821739130434785$, while the hit rate above baseline was $0.9841269841269841$. The hit rate inside $M-T$ was $0.9365079365079365$, and the hit rate inside $M-T$ plus boundary was $0.9841269841269841$. In the leave-one-codon test, the recovery rate was $1.0$, with $8$ predictable cases, $5$ boundary cases, $8$ cases of high closure coverage, and $0$ anchor cases. These rows say that the finite tile has strong internal recovery behavior at the code-label layer. They do not say that a missing codon is biologically produced, translated, folded, or functionally realized by the coordinate construction.

\paragraph{Local verdict.}
The ready claim is that the standard-code label table supports a finite punctured-cube pattern in the six-coordinate codon display. The strongest finite evidence is the exact thirteen-codon region, the $f$-vector $(13,16,4)$, the spectral residual near zero, the sampled exact-shape probability $0.0005$, the amino-acid-class-preserving exact-match rate $0.0$ at $2000$ samples, and the complete module assignment of all thirteen codons. The interpretation must remain local. The contact used here is a curated codon-to-label table, so the chapter may discuss code-read structure, local boundary behavior, and finite recovery probes.
