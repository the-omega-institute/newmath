\subsection{The punctured local codon cube}
\label{sec:h0-r-punctured-cube}

\paragraph{Finite object.}
The record concerns a window-six display of the standard genetic-code table. Each codon is treated as a six-bit coordinate word after the four nucleotide letters are placed in the chosen binary presentation. The finite subset under inspection is the code-layer set
$R \subset Q_6$, where the observed row is
$$
\begin{aligned}
R=\{&\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\\
&\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UUA}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The cardinality check gives $|R|=13$, and the same thirteen codons occur in the direct cardinality row. The combinatorial summary of the induced tile is the face vector
$$
\begin{aligned}
(f_0,f_1,f_2)=(13,16,4).
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the row is not merely a named codon list: it carries a local cubical incidence profile with thirteen vertices, sixteen one-dimensional adjacencies, and four two-dimensional faces. The phrase ``punctured cube'' is used here only for this finite local geometry inside $Q_6$; it does not assert that the biological code is generated by cubical necessity.

\paragraph{Label contact.}
The only biological contact used in this row is the curated standard-code label table. That contact assigns each codon an amino-acid or stop label and therefore supports readback at the code layer. It supplies enough information to ask whether the thirteen-coordinate tile has a coherent label-side boundary pattern, but it does not observe translation dynamics, molecular structure, physical viability, cellular function, or a universal generating mechanism. The local contact is therefore deliberately narrow: a codon-to-label table can check a label split, while higher biological layers require their own measurements.

The sampling process attempted $57377$ distinct draws and had $55377$ duplicate retries, so the finite row is also marked by a heavily constrained search space. These numbers support a restricted statement: the displayed coordinate-and-label shape is unusual under the recorded finite null models. They do not by themselves identify a biochemical cause.
