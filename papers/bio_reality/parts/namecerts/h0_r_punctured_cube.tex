\subsection{Punctured Codon Cube Boundary}
\label{subsec:h0-r-punctured-cube-boundary}

\paragraph{Finite codon carrier.}
This record treats the standard-code codon table as a code-layer observation over the six-coordinate codon cube $Q_6$.  The relevant finite row is the boundary set
$$
\begin{aligned}
R=\{&AAA, AGA, AGG, AUA, CUA, CUC, CUG, CUU,\\
&UAA, UAG, UCA, UGA, UUA\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The cardinality check gives $|R|=13$, and the same thirteen codons are returned by the independent expected-codon readout: $AAA$, $AGA$, $AGG$, $AUA$, $CUA$, $CUC$, $CUG$, $CUU$, $UAA$, $UAG$, $UCA$, $UGA$, and $UUA$.  In the local cubical display the row has face-vector
$$
\begin{aligned}
(f_0,f_1,f_2)=(13,16,4),
\end{aligned}
$$
so the observed object is not only a list of labels but a finite punctured-cube tile with thirteen vertices, sixteen one-dimensional adjacencies, and four two-dimensional faces.  The table contact used here is deliberately narrow: it supplies codon-to-label assignments for the standard code, and it does not itself observe translation dynamics, folded structure, physical admissibility, biological function, a mechanism, or a universal biological law.

\paragraph{Local spectral and sampling checks.}
The spectral row attached to the same finite geometry gives
$$
\begin{aligned}
\lambda_M&=0.6752479308830213,\\
\lambda_R&=0.4758352843197002,\\
\mu&=3.051487585298128.
\end{aligned}
$$
The cubic residual reported for the radial calculation is $-6.224354365258478\cdot 10^{-12}$, which is at numerical zero for the recorded tolerance.  The shape comparison against the coordinate null sampled $N=2000$ tables with seed $42$.  It found one exact shape match, giving $p_{\mathrm{exact}}=0.0005$, and twenty-five matches at Jaccard threshold at least $0.9$, giving $p_{J\ge 0.9}=0.0125$.  The sampling log also records $57377$ distinct-sample attempts and $55377$ duplicate retries, so the low tail is being read as a finite empirical pressure inside a constrained discrete space rather than as an asymptotic law.  Under the amino-acid-class-preserving null with $N=2000$ and seed $42$, the observed module size was $20$, the exact shape count was $0$, and the lower-tail count at or below the observed size was $7$, giving $p=0.0035$ for that lower-tail event.  These checks support the claim that the local tile is unusually specific relative to the tested shuffles; they do not license movement beyond the code-read layer.

\paragraph{Leave-one recovery rows.}
Two finite recovery probes test whether the local boundary is useful as a code-label predictor while remaining inside the label table.  In leave-one-code-out evaluation, $23$ code tables supplied $63$ targets.  The baseline hit rate was $0.19821739130434785$, while the hit rate inside $M\setminus T$ was $0.9365079365079365$ and the hit rate inside $M\setminus T$ plus the boundary row was $0.9841269841269841$.  The recorded above-baseline hit rate is therefore $0.9841269841269841$.  In leave-one-codon-out evaluation the anchor count was $0$, the boundary count was $5$, the high-coverage closure count was $8$, the predictable count was $8$, and the recovery rate was $1.0$.  These numbers say that the punctured tile is a strong finite guide for missing code labels in the tested table setting.  They do not say that the recovered amino-acid labels have thereby been realized as translated molecules, folded proteins, stable physical structures, or functional biological systems.

\paragraph{Family and module accounting.}
The family split contains five families.  The recorded hit rates above baseline are $1.0$ for bacterial-plastid tables, $1.0$ for candidate-other tables, $1.0$ for mitochondrial tables, $1.0$ for nuclear-ciliate tables, and $0.8333333333333334$ for alternative-yeast tables.  The module classification is complete: $R$ has size $13$, the assigned count is $13$, and there are no extra module codons, no codons missing from modules, and no unassigned codons.  The modules are
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{agr}&=\{AGA,AGG\},\\
\mathrm{ar\_lys}&=\{AAA\},\\
\mathrm{cun}&=\{CUA,CUC,CUG,CUU\},\\
\mathrm{ile\_met}&=\{AUA\},\\
\mathrm{ssn}&=\{UCA,UUA\},\\
\mathrm{stop\_trp}&=\{UAA,UAG,UGA\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
Every module has either a predictable or boundary role in the recorded classification.  This is the chapter-specific content of the punctured-cube claim: the standard-code label table exposes a thirteen-codon local boundary with a stable cubical face count, nontrivial spectral row, low null frequencies, strong leave-one recovery, and complete module accounting.  The scientific boundary is equally local.  The observation remains a code-label readback over a curated standard-code table.  Any assertion about translation, folding, physical admissibility, biological function, generative mechanism, or cross-organism universality would require an additional contact that observes that higher layer directly.
