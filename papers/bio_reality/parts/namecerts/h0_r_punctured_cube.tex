\subsection{The Punctured-Cube Codon Tile}\label{subsec:bioreality-h0-r-punctured-cube}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite codon carrier.}
The packet treats the standard-code codon table as a finite code-level contact on the six-coordinate codon cube $Q_6$.  Each codon is read as a three-letter word over the four nucleotide symbols, equivalently as a six-bit display after choosing a two-bit presentation of each nucleotide.  The distinguished row $R$ is the thirteen-codon set
$$
\begin{aligned}
R=\{&AAA, AGA, AGG, AUA, CUA, CUC, CUG, CUU,\\
&UAA, UAG, UCA, UGA, UUA\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The finite audit records $|R|=13$, with the same thirteen entries appearing in the independent cardinality row and in the punctured-cube row.  The face count of the induced local tile is
$$
\begin{aligned}
f(R)=(13,16,4),
\end{aligned}
$$
so this is not merely a list of labels: it has thirteen vertices, sixteen one-dimensional adjacencies, and four square faces inside the codon-coordinate cube.  The geometric content is therefore a local boundary pattern in codon-coordinate space, while the amino-acid and stop labels remain supplied by the curated standard-code table.

\paragraph{Spectral and shape checks.}
The spectral row gives two measured radii, $\lambda_M=0.6752479308830213$ and $\lambda_R=0.4758352843197002$, together with $\mu=3.051487585298128$.  The recorded cubic residual is $-6.224354365258478\times 10^{-12}$, the same value listed as the radial-polynomial residual, so the numerical witness is within floating tolerance rather than a free narrative fit.  Against the reported shape null with $N=2000$ samples and seed $42$, there was exactly one exact shape match, giving $p_{\mathrm{exact}}=0.0005$.  The high-overlap statistic found $25$ samples with Jaccard score at least $0.9$, giving $p_{J\geq 0.9}=0.0125$.  The sampling process attempted $57377$ distinct draws and rejected $55377$ duplicates, which matters because the small tail values are attached to a concrete finite enumeration protocol rather than to an unspecified asymptotic model.

\paragraph{Label-class control.}
A separate amino-acid-class-preserving null used $N=2000$ samples with seed $42$.  In that row the observed companion size was $|M|=20$, the exact-shape count was $0$, and the lower-tail count at or below the observed $M$ size was $7$.  Thus the exact-shape probability in that constrained null is recorded as $0.0$, while the lower-tail probability is $0.0035$.  This does not say that the codon table is forced by the cube.  It says only that, under the finite class-preserving control that was actually run, the observed local configuration lies in a small counted tail.

\paragraph{Recovery rows.}
The leave-one-code-out row evaluated $23$ code tables and $63$ total targets.  Its baseline hit rate was $0.19821739130434785$, while the hit rate above baseline was $0.9841269841269841$.  The hit rate inside $M-T$ was $0.9365079365079365$, and the rate inside $M-T$ together with the boundary was again $0.9841269841269841$.  The family breakdown used five families: alternative yeast had rate $0.8333333333333334$, and bacterial plastid, candidate other, mitochondrial, and nuclear ciliate each had rate $1.0$.  The leave-one-codon-out row records $8$ predictable codons, $5$ boundary codons, no anchors, high closure coverage $8$, and recovery rate $1.0$.  These rows support the reading that the local tile is useful for code-layer recovery tests; they do not convert the tile into a biological mechanism.

\paragraph{Module accounting.}
The thirteen $R$ codons are completely assigned by the module-classification row.  The modules are
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{agr}&=\{AGA,AGG\},&
\mathrm{ar\_lys}&=\{AAA\},&
\mathrm{cun}&=\{CUA,CUC,CUG,CUU\},\\
\mathrm{ile\_met}&=\{AUA\},&
\mathrm{ssn}&=\{UCA,UUA\},&
\mathrm{stop\_trp}&=\{UAA,UAG,UGA\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The module audit reports $R\_\mathrm{size}=13$, assigned count $13$, no extra module codons, no missing entries, and no unassigned codons.  Every listed module has either predictable or boundary support in the finite leave-one-codon analysis.  This is the precise sense in which the row is a punctured local cube: the named codons form a finite coordinate object with complete module coverage, not a claim that all codon biology is exhausted by this object.

\paragraph{Reality contact and boundary.}
The only biological contact used here is the curated standard-code label table: it assigns codons to amino-acid or stop labels and permits code-level readback.  It does not observe ribosomal translation as a process, folded protein structure, physical admissibility of a molecule, biological function, evolutionary mechanism, or universality across all organisms.  Consequently the supported conclusion is deliberately narrow: window-six codon coordinates expose a statistically sharp local boundary tile whose labels can be read against the standard code.  No claim about translation realization, folding, structural order, physical admissibility, function, global mechanism, or universal biological law follows from these rows unless a separate contact of the appropriate kind is supplied.
