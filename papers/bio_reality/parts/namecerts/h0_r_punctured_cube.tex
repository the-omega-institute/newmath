\subsection{Interpretation boundary}
\label{sec:h0-r-punctured-cube-boundary}

\paragraph{What is supported.}
The supported statement is that a thirteen-codon subset of the six-bit codon cube forms a verified local punctured-cube pattern with incidence vector $(13,16,4)$, complete module assignment, nontrivial spectral values, and favorable finite sampling statistics.  The curated standard-code table supplies the codon labels needed for the readback. \paragraph{What is not supported.}
No statement about translation realization follows from this chapter.  No folded protein structure follows from an amino-acid label sequence.  No physical admissibility, biochemical function, organism-level mechanism, or universal biological law follows from the punctured-cube row.  Any such stronger assertion would require a separate contact that observes the relevant layer directly.  In the present record, the local tile may organize a code-table boundary, but it does not explain why translation works, how a protein folds, whether a molecule is physically viable, what function it performs, or whether the same pattern is forced across biology.
