\subsection{A Punctured Window-Six Tile in the Standard Codon Table}
\label{sec:bioreality-h0-r-punctured-cube}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Record.}
This record concerns the finite claim \texttt{h0.R.punctured.cube}.  The carrier is the six-coordinate codon cube, with each codon displayed as a word in the alphabet $\{A,C,G,U\}^3$ and read through a six-bit window.  The biological datum used here is only the curated standard genetic-code table: it assigns each codon to an amino-acid or stop label.  The claim is therefore located at the code-reading layer.  It does not assert translation in a cell, folded structure, physical admissibility, biological function, a mechanism, or a universal biological law.

\paragraph{Observed subset.}
The distinguished set $R$ has thirteen codons.  In lexicographic display order the verified row is
$$
\begin{aligned}
R=\{&AAA, AGA, AGG, AUA, CUA, CUC, CUG, CUU,\\
&UAA, UAG, UCA, UGA, UUA\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The independent cardinality row gives $|R|=13$ and lists the same thirteen codons.  The punctured-cube row verifies that this set has face-count vector
$$
\begin{aligned}
f(R)=(13,16,4),
\end{aligned}
$$
so the reported object is not merely a list of labels: it carries a local cubical boundary profile inside the window-six codon coordinate space.

Within numerical tolerance, this places the local codon tile in a reproducible spectral position rather than treating the thirteen-codon set as an isolated manual selection.

\paragraph{Shape evidence.}
The random-shape comparison used $N=2000$ sampled tables with seed $42$.  It required $57377$ distinct-sample attempts and recorded $55377$ duplicate retries.  Exactly one sampled instance matched the observed shape, giving
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{\mathrm{exact}}=0.0005.
\end{aligned}
$$
There were $25$ samples with Jaccard score at least $0.9$, giving
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{\mathrm{Jaccard}\geq 0.9}=0.0125.
\end{aligned}
$$
These numbers support a finite local-shape anomaly at the code-label layer.  They do not by themselves identify a causal process or a biological optimization principle.

\paragraph{Class-preserving null row.}
A separate amino-acid-class-preserving null run also used $N=2000$ and seed $42$.  The observed comparison size was $20$.  It found no exact shape match, so the recorded exact-shape value is
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{\mathrm{class\ exact}}=0.0.
\end{aligned}
$$
The lower-tail count at or below the observed $M$ size was $7$, yielding
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{\mathrm{class\ lower\ tail}}=0.0035.
\end{aligned}
$$
The row therefore says that the local shape remains uncommon even after preserving amino-acid class information.  The supplied finite data record gives the size of $M$ and its statistical role, but it does not include a member-by-member codon list for $M$.

Thus every element of the punctured-cube set is accounted for by a named local module, and no codon outside the thirteen-member set is introduced by the module bookkeeping.

\paragraph{Interpretation.}
The verified content is a finite relation among a six-bit codon coordinate system, a thirteen-codon subset, cubical face counts, spectral scalars, module assignments, and code-label readback.  The contact with biology is the curated standard-code table, which supplies codon-to-label assignments.  That contact is sufficient for testing whether the local boundary tile aligns with code labels, but it is not an observation of ribosomal translation, protein folding, molecular stability, organismal function, or evolutionary necessity.

\paragraph{Boundary.}
The local tile should therefore be read as a constrained code-layer pattern.  Any future claim that the same pattern governs translation, folded structure, physical admissibility, biological function, mechanism, or universality must bring a separate empirical contact for that higher layer.  Amino-acid label sequences obtained from the table remain labels until connected to an independent structural or functional measurement.  In particular, CUN, UUR, AGR, stop--tryptophan, isoleucine--methionine, or lysine-family wording does not convert this finite codon-table record into a folded-protein or organism-wide claim.
