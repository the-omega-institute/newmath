\subsection{Punctured cube boundary in the standard-code readout}
\label{sec:h0-r-punctured-cube}

\paragraph{Finite object.}
The record concerns the standard genetic-code table only at the level where a codon is read as an amino-acid or stop label.  The coordinate carrier is the six-bit codon cube $Q_6$, with a local face tile singled out by the label split observed in the curated standard-code table.  The distinguished boundary set is
$$
\begin{aligned}
R=\{&\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\\
&\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UUA}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The cardinality check gives $|R|=13$, and the independent punctured-cube check returns exactly the same thirteen codons.  Its finite cell count is
$$
\begin{aligned}
f(R)=(13,16,4),
\end{aligned}
$$
so the observed object is not merely a list of exceptional labels: it has the incidence profile of a small local cubical boundary after one corner-class obstruction is removed.

\paragraph{Local geometry.}
The row data support a chapter-level claim about a codon-window tile, not about the full biological process of translation.  The verified set $R$ occupies a boundary-shaped portion of $Q_6$ built from the codons $\mathrm{AAA}$, $\mathrm{AGA}$, $\mathrm{AGG}$, $\mathrm{AUA}$, the four $\mathrm{CUN}$ codons, $\mathrm{UAA}$, $\mathrm{UAG}$, $\mathrm{UCA}$, $\mathrm{UGA}$, and $\mathrm{UUA}$.  The standard-code contact supplies the labels needed to read these codons, while the coordinate analysis supplies the local shape.  The two roles must remain distinct: the curated table is a biological readout for codon labels, and the cubical computation is a finite geometry over the displayed codons.

\paragraph{Spectral check.}
The same finite row bundle records a spectral separation between the boundary set and its companion mass.  The measured values are
$$
\begin{aligned}
\lambda_M&=0.6752479308830213,\\
\lambda_R&=0.4758352843197002,\\
\mu&=3.051487585298128.
\end{aligned}
$$
The radial cubic residual is $-6.224354365258478\times 10^{-12}$, also reported as the cubic-polynomial residual, so the spectral computation is numerically closed to the stated tolerance.  The radius value attached to the high side is $0.6752479308830213$.  This does not turn the codon tile into a mechanism; it only says that the finite graph-like readout has a reproducible numerical signature for this coordinate presentation.

\paragraph{Null comparisons.}
Two randomization checks make the local shape nontrivial in the finite experiment.  In the shape-significance run with $N=2000$ samples and seed $42$, there was $1$ exact shape match, giving $p_{\mathrm{exact}}=0.0005$.  There were $25$ samples with Jaccard similarity at least $0.9$, giving $p_{J\geq 0.9}=0.0125$.  The run required $57377$ distinct-sample attempts and encountered $55377$ duplicate retries, which is consistent with a constrained finite sampling space rather than an unconstrained continuum.  Under the amino-acid-class-preserving null, again with $N=2000$ and seed $42$, the observed companion size was $20$, the exact-match count was $0$, and the lower-tail count at or below the observed size was $7$.  The corresponding values were
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{\mathrm{class\ exact}}&=0.0,\\
p_{\mathrm{lower\ tail}}&=0.0035.
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the punctured-cube row is supported both by exact finite recognition and by a class-preserving null that rarely reaches the observed compactness.

\paragraph{Leave-out behavior.}
The leave-one-code-out experiment evaluated $23$ alternative code tables over $63$ total targets.  Its baseline hit rate was $0.19821739130434785$, while the observed hit rate above baseline was $0.9841269841269841$.  The hit rate inside $M-T$ was $0.9365079365079365$, and the hit rate inside $M-T$ plus the boundary was $0.9841269841269841$.  By family, the hit-rate-above-baseline values were $1.0$ for bacterial-plastid, mitochondrial, nuclear-ciliate, and candidate-other families, and $0.8333333333333334$ for the alternative-yeast family.  The leave-one-codon-out row gives $8$ predictable codons, $5$ boundary codons, $0$ anchors, closure coverage $8$, and recovery rate $1.0$.  These numbers show that the local boundary has predictive stability under finite deletion probes at the code-label layer.

\paragraph{Module accounting.}
The thirteen boundary codons are fully assigned to six named modules with no missing or extra codons.  The module accounting is
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{agr}&=\{\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG}\},\\
\mathrm{ar\_lys}&=\{\mathrm{AAA}\},\\
\mathrm{cun}&=\{\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU}\},\\
\mathrm{ile\_met}&=\{\mathrm{AUA}\},\\
\mathrm{ssn}&=\{\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UUA}\},\\
\mathrm{stop\_trp}&=\{\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UGA}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The classification row reports $R$-size $13$, assigned count $13$, no extra module codons, no missing module codons, and no unassigned codons.  Every module has either predictable or boundary status in the leave-out probe, so the punctured-cube claim is not carried by an unnamed residue outside the table.

\paragraph{Scope.}
The biological contact is the curated standard-code table: it tells us which codon labels are present in the standard code.  It does not observe ribosomal realization, protein folding, physical admissibility of a molecule, organism-level function, or a universal biological mechanism.  Therefore the supported conclusion is deliberately narrow.  Window-six coordinates expose a local boundary-tile pattern in the standard-code label table, with $13$ codons, cell vector $(13,16,4)$, stable leave-out recovery, and significant null-model separation.  No claim about translation, folded structure, admissible chemistry, biological function, mechanism, or universality follows from this chapter unless another independent contact supplies that higher-layer observation.
