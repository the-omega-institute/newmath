The biological contact is deliberately narrow: it is the curated assignment of each codon to an amino-acid or stop label. Within that contact, the observed boundary set $R$ is the following thirteen-codon row:

\begin{aligned}

\paragraph{Scientific interpretation.} The chapter-level claim is therefore a local code-read claim. In the standard-code table, the thirteen codons above form a punctured-cube boundary pattern with face-vector $(13,16,4)$, rare-shape behavior in the two finite null families, numerical spectral constraints, and high held-out recovery in code-label readback probes. The external contact is the curated standard genetic-code assignment, so the conclusion is about codon labels and their local coordinate pattern.
