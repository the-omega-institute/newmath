\subsection{A punctured codon cube at the code-read layer}
\label{sec:bioreality-h0-r-punctured-cube}

\paragraph{Finite coordinate object.}
The packet records a local window-six pattern in the standard codon table. The carrier is the six-bit codon cube $Q_6$, read as the finite set of codon displays obtained from three nucleotide positions with two binary coordinates per position. The row under test is the subset $R$ selected by the observed label split in the curated standard-code table. In the verified run $e19917012c60$, the selected row is
$$
\begin{aligned}
R=\{&AAA, AGA, AGG, AUA, CUA, CUC, CUG, CUU,\\
&UAA, UAG, UCA, UGA, UUA\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The independent cardinality check $5d4a3459997d$ gives the same list and reports $|R|=13$. Thus the row is not a verbal summary of the code table: it is a concrete thirteen-codon subset in $Q_6$ with an explicitly checked membership list.

\paragraph{Punctured-cube incidence.}
The finite incidence calculation identifies the row as a punctured cubical tile with face-vector
$$
\begin{aligned}
(f_0,f_1,f_2)=(13,16,4).
\end{aligned}
$$
Here $f_0=13$ is the number of codon vertices in the row, $f_1=16$ is the number of one-coordinate adjacencies retained inside the row, and $f_2=4$ is the number of square faces retained by the same local adjacency relation. This is the chapter-specific geometric content of the claim: the code labels single out a local cubical patch with four two-faces, not merely a scattered thirteen-element set. The word local is essential. The calculation concerns the finite tile seen inside $Q_6$ and does not turn that tile into a rule of translation, a statement about molecular folding, a criterion for physical feasibility, a biological function claim, or a universal mechanism.

The companion spectral run $1026324eff41$ gives two normalized row scales and one radial parameter:

The same run reports a radial cubic residual of $-6.224354365258478\cdot 10^{-12}$, also listed as the cubic-polynomial residual. The spectral values should be read as internal diagnostics of the row geometry and its surrounding module, not as biochemical observables. They sharpen the local shape claim but do not supply a contact with ribosomal translation, protein structure, thermodynamic admissibility, or downstream function.

\paragraph{Shape rarity under finite sampling.}
The shape-significance run $0b267c88f8e4$ used seed $42$ and evaluated $N=2000$ sampled alternatives. Because the distinctness filter was strict, the sampler made $57377$ distinct-sample attempts and rejected $55377$ duplicate retries. The exact punctured-cube shape appeared once, giving
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{\mathrm{exact}}&=0.0005.
\end{aligned}
$$
A relaxed comparison using Jaccard similarity at least $0.9$ found $25$ matches, giving
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{J\ge 0.9}&=0.0125.
\end{aligned}
$$
These numbers support the claim that the observed local tile is not typical under the tested coordinate-preserving finite sampler. They do not say that the tile is forced by biology in all genetic codes, nor that its geometry is sufficient to explain why an organism realizes a particular translation system.

\paragraph{Amino-acid-class preserving null.}
A second null calculation, run $e8d5de747a0c$, preserves amino-acid class information and again uses seed $42$ with $N=2000$. In that model the observed module size is $20$. The exact shape count is $0$, so the empirical exact-shape probability is reported as
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{\mathrm{aa\ class,exact}}&=0.0.
\end{aligned}
$$
The lower-tail count for samples with size at most the observed module size is $7$, giving
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{\mathrm{aa\ class,lower\ tail}}&=0.0035.
\end{aligned}
$$
This null is useful because it tests a stronger background than a completely unstructured draw: even when label-class information is protected, the exact local tile does not recur in the finite sample. The result strengthens the code-layer anomaly reading while still staying inside code-label evidence.

These row counts and rates indicate that the finite coordinate pattern has predictive reach across the tested code-table variants. They do not identify a biochemical cause of the variants.

\paragraph{Reality contact and scope.}
The sole external contact used here is the curated standard genetic-code table. That contact states that codons are assigned to amino-acid or stop labels, at codon-label resolution. It supports code-layer readback and local split tests. It does not observe protein production, folded conformation, physical admissibility, biological function, or a universal law of coding. Therefore the chapter claim is deliberately narrow: the standard-code label table, when placed in the six-bit codon cube, exposes a thirteen-codon punctured cubical row with face-vector $(13,16,4)$, rare finite-sampling behavior, spectral diagnostics, and complete module assignment. Any promotion from this code-read statement to translation, folding, physical feasibility, function, mechanism, or universality requires an additional contact of the appropriate kind.
