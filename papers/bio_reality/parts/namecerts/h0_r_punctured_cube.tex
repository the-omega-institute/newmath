\subsection{The punctured codon cube row}
\label{sec:bioreality-h0-r-punctured-cube}

\paragraph{Finite carrier.}
The finite object in this record is the six-coordinate codon cube, read as the $64$ possible codon displays with three nucleotide positions and two binary coordinates per displayed base.  The distinguished row $R$ is not introduced as a biochemical mechanism.  It is the observed finite subset selected by the window-six coordinate test and then compared with the curated standard-code label table.  The computed row is
$$
\begin{aligned}
R=\{&\texttt{AAA},\texttt{AGA},\texttt{AGG},\texttt{AUA},\texttt{CUA},\texttt{CUC},\texttt{CUG},\texttt{CUU},\\
&\texttt{UAA},\texttt{UAG},\texttt{UCA},\texttt{UGA},\texttt{UUA}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The cardinality check gives $|R|=13$, and the independent cardinality row reports the same thirteen codons.  Thus the finite row is not merely a count: the named codons in the punctured-cube computation and the named codons in the cardinality computation coincide exactly.

\paragraph{Punctured-cube shape.}
The row $R$ has the reported face-vector data
$$
\begin{aligned}
(f_0,f_1,f_2)=(13,16,4).
\end{aligned}
$$
This says that the selected codons form a small local tile with thirteen vertices, sixteen one-dimensional adjacencies, and four square faces inside the six-bit display cube.  The word ``punctured'' is used here only for this finite cubical incidence shape: it records a local missing-corner geometry in the codon-coordinate display.  It does not by itself say that a ribosome, tRNA ensemble, organismal lineage, or physical folding process is governed by the same geometry.

\paragraph{Spectral summary.}
The same packet records a numerical spectral profile for the coordinate split.  The two reported spectral radii are $\lambda_M=0.6752479308830213$ and $\lambda_R=0.4758352843197002$, with $\mu=3.051487585298128$.  The radial cubic check is numerically stable at
$$
\begin{aligned}
P(\mu)&=-6.224354365258478\cdot 10^{-12}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The value is treated as a floating-point certificate of the finite computation, not as a symbolic theorem about biology.  The important point for this chapter is the separation between the larger measured coordinate set, whose size row reports $|M|=20$, and the punctured-cube row $R$ of size $13$.

\paragraph{Shape rarity rows.}
Two null comparisons are attached to the row.  In the shape-significance experiment with $N=2000$ samples and seed $42$, the exact shape appeared once, so $p_{\mathrm{exact}}=0.0005$.  The high-overlap condition with Jaccard score at least $0.9$ appeared $25$ times, so $p_{\mathrm{Jaccard}\ge 0.9}=0.0125$.  The sampler required $57377$ distinct-sample attempts and recorded $55377$ duplicate retries, which is itself useful context: the finite search space contains substantial collision pressure under this sampling regime.  A second amino-acid-class-preserving null model, again with $N=2000$ and seed $42$, reports $0$ exact matches to the observed shape and a lower-tail count of $7$ for the observed $M$ size $20$.  Its two reported probabilities are $p_{\mathrm{class,exact}}=0.0$ and $p_{\mathrm{class,lower}}=0.0035$.  These numbers support the statement that the local tile is unusual under the two finite null procedures used here.  They do not upgrade the tile into a universal law.

\paragraph{Code-table contact.}
The biological contact used by this chapter is a curated standard genetic-code table.  It supplies codon-to-label readback at the code layer: each displayed codon receives an amino-acid or stop label according to the standard table.  That contact is sufficient to ask whether the thirteen codons in $R$ occupy a coherent local label boundary.  It is not a contact with translation dynamics, tertiary structure, thermodynamic admissibility, fitness, molecular function, or universality across life.  Consequently, an amino-acid label sequence obtained from the table is still a label sequence.  It is not a folded protein structure, not an experimentally realized protein, and not a functional claim.

\paragraph{Leave-one-code behavior.}
The leave-one-code-out row evaluates $23$ code tables and $63$ total targets.  The baseline hit rate is $0.19821739130434785$.  The boundary-aware recovery row reports a hit rate of $0.9365079365079365$ inside $M\setminus T$ and $0.9841269841269841$ inside $M\setminus T$ plus the boundary.  The same value, $0.9841269841269841$, is recorded as the hit rate above baseline.  By family, the reported hit rates above baseline are $1.0$ for bacterial-plastid, mitochondrial, nuclear-ciliate, and candidate-other families, and $0.8333333333333334$ for alternative yeast.  These rows show that the local coordinate boundary has predictive force for code-label perturbations in the finite table comparison.  The claim remains at the code-read layer.

\paragraph{Leave-one-codon behavior.}
The leave-one-codon-out row reports $8$ predictable codons, $5$ boundary codons, $0$ anchor codons, and high closure coverage $8$, with recovery rate $1.0$.  The module classification accounts for all thirteen codons in $R$: $\texttt{AGA},\texttt{AGG}$ form the $\mathrm{agr}$ module; $\texttt{AAA}$ forms $\mathrm{ar\_lys}$; $\texttt{CUA},\texttt{CUC},\texttt{CUG},\texttt{CUU}$ form $\mathrm{cun}$; $\texttt{AUA}$ forms $\mathrm{ile\_met}$; $\texttt{UCA},\texttt{UUA}$ form $\mathrm{ssn}$; and $\texttt{UAA},\texttt{UAG},\texttt{UGA}$ form $\mathrm{stop\_trp}$.  The classification row gives $R\_\mathrm{size}=13$, assigned count $13$, no missing codons, no extra module codons, and no unassigned codons.  Thus the local tile is not only a thirteen-point set; it is partitioned by a complete finite module accounting.

\paragraph{Local conclusion.}
The chapter claim is therefore narrow and finite.  The window-six codon display selects a thirteen-codon row whose incidence data are $(13,16,4)$, whose spectral row is numerically stable, whose null comparisons are small-probability events under the recorded procedures, and whose code-table behavior is captured by leave-one-code and leave-one-codon tests.  The curated standard-code table permits codon-label readback and local boundary checking.  Nothing in this chapter licenses promotion to translation realization, folded structure, physical admissibility, biological function, mechanism, or universality.  Any such statement would require an additional contact that observes the higher layer directly.
