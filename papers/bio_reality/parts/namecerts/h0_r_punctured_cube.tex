\subsection{A punctured local cube in the standard-code readout}
\label{subsec:bioreality-h0-r-punctured-cube}

\paragraph{Finite coordinate object.}
The record concerns the code-read layer of the standard genetic-code table.  The finite carrier is the six-coordinate codon display $Q_6$, where a codon is treated only as a labeled row in a curated codon table and not as a mechanistic object.  In this coordinate display the distinguished boundary set $R$ is exactly
$$
\begin{aligned}
R=\{&AAA,AGA,AGG,AUA,CUA,CUC,CUG,CUU,\\
&UAA,UAG,UCA,UGA,UUA\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The row count is $|R|=13$, matching the independently recorded cardinality check.  The same finite run identifies this set as a punctured cube tile with face data
$$
\begin{aligned}
f(R)=(13,16,4),
\end{aligned}
$$
so the packet is not merely a list of exceptional codons: it is a local cubical boundary pattern with $13$ vertices, $16$ one-dimensional incidences, and $4$ two-dimensional faces in the induced window-six geometry.  The biological readout attached to this object is only the standard-code label table: each listed codon has its curated amino-acid or stop label, and the geometric grouping is evaluated against that code-layer readout.

These numbers are used only as finite witnesses for the coordinate analysis.  They do not assert a physical spectrum of a molecule, an evolutionary law, or a translation mechanism.

The shape-significance experiment used $N=2000$ sampled comparators with seed $42$.  It required $57377$ distinct-sample attempts and produced $55377$ duplicate retries.  Only $1$ exact shape match occurred, giving $p_{\mathrm{exact}}=0.0005$.  At the looser threshold $J\geq 0.9$, there were $25$ matches, giving $p_{J\geq 0.9}=0.0125$.  A second amino-acid-class-preserving null model again used $N=2000$ and seed $42$; it found $0$ exact matches, observed $|M|=20$, and counted $7$ lower-tail samples at or below that observed size.  The resulting values were $p_{\mathrm{class\ exact}}=0.0$ and $p_{\mathrm{class\ lower\ tail}}=0.0035$.  Thus the finite table supports a non-random-looking local code-layer tile under these two sampled null views, while leaving all higher biological interpretation outside the present contact.

The hit rate inside $M-T$ was $0.9365079365079365$, and the rate inside $M-T$ plus the boundary was again $0.9841269841269841$.  The family-resolved check used $5$ families.  The recorded hit rates above baseline were $0.8333333333333334$ for alternative yeast, $1.0$ for bacterial or plastid codes, $1.0$ for the candidate-other class, $1.0$ for mitochondrial codes, and $1.0$ for nuclear ciliate codes.  These statistics say that the local coordinate boundary remains highly predictive for code-table label differences in the tested finite rows; they do not say that organisms implement the coordinate construction as a biological necessity.

The leave-one-codon-out run found $0$ anchors, $5$ boundary codons, and $8$ high-coverage closure cases.  It marked $8$ codons as predictable and reported recovery rate $1.0$, with the recovery-rate-above-threshold flag also equal to $1.0$.  At the module level all $13$ boundary codons were assigned, with no missing codons, no extra module codons, and no unassigned codons.  The complete module partition was

It covers arginine-related rows, an AAA lysine row, the CUN leucine block, the AUA isoleucine--methionine row, two U-start singleton-shift rows, and the stop--tryptophan block.

\paragraph{Reality contact and local boundary.}
The only external contact used here is the curated standard-code table.  That contact supplies codon-to-label assignments and permits a finite check of local label splits on the six-coordinate codon display.  It does not contain observations of ribosomal dynamics, tRNA availability, aminoacylation chemistry, folded protein geometry, thermodynamic admissibility, phenotype, fitness, or function.  Consequently the admissible conclusion is narrow: window-six coordinates expose a robust local boundary tile in the standard-code label table, with $R$ equal to the $13$ codons listed above and with face vector $(13,16,4)$.

No translation realization follows from this chapter.  No folded structure follows from this chapter.  No physical admissibility, biochemical function, organism-level mechanism, or universal biological law follows from this chapter.  Any claim crossing one of those boundaries requires an additional contact that directly observes the relevant layer.  In particular, reading an amino-acid label sequence from codons is still a code-layer operation; it is not a protein-structure observation.  Likewise, CUN, UUR, stop, tryptophan, arginine, lysine, isoleucine, and methionine labels may organize the finite table, but their presence in this packet does not by itself establish translation, folding, chemistry, selection, or universality.
