\paragraph{Finite codon object.}
This record treats the standard-code codon table only at the level of codon-label readback.  The carrier is the six-coordinate codon cube $Q_6$, where each codon is represented by its window-six display and the curated table supplies the amino-acid or stop label attached to that coordinate.  Inside this finite carrier the distinguished boundary set $R$ is exactly
$$
\begin{aligned}
R=\{&\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\\
&\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UUA}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The verified row count is $|R|=13$.  In the local cell structure the same set has $f$-vector $(13,16,4)$, so the observed object is not merely a list of exceptional codons: it is a punctured local cube pattern with thirteen vertices, sixteen one-dimensional incidences, and four two-dimensional faces.  The finite distinctions used here are the six-bit codon display, a local tile face, a singleton label corner, and a three-point label class.  The internal descriptors are coordinate, closure, spectrum, and relation; the only biological readback used in this subsection is the standard-code label table.

\paragraph{Spectral and sampling checks.}
The spectral row gives $\lambda_M=0.6752479308830213$, $\lambda_R=0.4758352843197002$, and $\mu=3.051487585298128$.  The reported cubic residual is $-6.224354365258478\times 10^{-12}$, the same value recorded for the radial polynomial evaluation, so the finite spectral computation is within numerical tolerance for the cubic relation being tested.  A shape-sampling check with $N=2000$ trials and seed $42$ found one exact shape match, giving $p_{\mathrm{exact}}=0.0005$.  The same run found $25$ samples with Jaccard score at least $0.9$, giving $p_{J\geq 0.9}=0.0125$.  The sampler needed $57377$ distinct-sample attempts and recorded $55377$ duplicate retries, which is relevant because the finite codon universe is small enough for repeated draws to collide heavily.  Under the amino-acid-class-preserving null model, also with $N=2000$ and seed $42$, the observed $M$-size was $20$, the exact-match count was $0$, the lower-tail count at or below the observed $M$-size was $7$, and the reported lower-tail probability was $0.0035$.  These numbers support a finite local-geometry claim about the code table; they do not by themselves identify a biochemical cause.

\paragraph{Scope of the claim.}
The supported statement is narrow: window-six codon coordinates expose a local boundary-tile pattern in the standard-code label table, and the finite rows above verify the punctured-cube set, its cardinality, its $f$-vector, its spectral scalars, its sampling significance, its leave-one behavior, and its module coverage.  The contact used here is a curated codon-to-label table.  That contact observes assignments from codons to amino-acid or stop labels; it does not observe translation as a process, folded protein structure, physical admissibility, biological function, mechanism, or universality.  Consequently no claim about translation realization, structural order, folding, admissible molecular physics, organismal function, or a universal biological law follows from this local tile unless a separate empirical contact supplies that higher-layer information.
