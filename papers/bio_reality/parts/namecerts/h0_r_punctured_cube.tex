\subsection{A punctured-cube boundary row in the standard codon display}
\label{sec:bioreality-h0-r-punctured-cube}

\paragraph{Finite codon coordinate.}
The row recorded here concerns the standard genetic-code label table viewed only as a six-coordinate codon display.  The coordinate carrier is the finite cube $Q_6$ whose vertices are codons in the alphabet $\{A,C,G,U\}^3$ after choosing a fixed six-bit presentation of the three nucleotide positions.  The verified boundary set is not asserted to be a biochemical mechanism.  It is a finite subset $R \subset Q_6$ whose membership is read against a curated standard-code table, so that codon labels are allowed as observations while translation, folding, physical admissibility, function, mechanism, and universality remain outside the present contact.

\paragraph{Punctured-cube row.}
The verified row identifies $R$ with the following thirteen codons:
$$
\begin{aligned}
R=\{&AAA, AGA, AGG, AUA, CUA, CUC, CUG, CUU,\\
&UAA, UAG, UCA, UGA, UUA\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The independent cardinality row gives $|R|=13$ for the same list.  In the standard-code labels these entries cover the arginine pair $AGA,AGG$, the lysine singleton $AAA$, the isoleucine/metionine-side singleton $AUA$ at the code-read level, the four codons $CUA,CUC,CUG,CUU$, the stop-related codons $UAA,UAG,UGA$, and the two-codon row $UCA,UUA$ used by the module classifier.  The geometric audit reports the face data
$$
\begin{aligned}
f(R)=(13,16,4),
\end{aligned}
$$
meaning that the finite object has thirteen vertices, sixteen one-dimensional adjacencies, and four two-dimensional faces in the chosen codon-window coordinate.  This is the sense in which the row is a punctured local cube: the object is a visible boundary tile in $Q_6$, not a law about all biological realizations.

\paragraph{Amino-acid-class preserving null row.}

$$
\begin{aligned}

p_{\mathrm{class\ lower\ tail}}&=0.0035.
\end{aligned}
$$
The zero exact count is a finite-run statement, not a proof of impossibility.  Its role is narrower: within the stated class-preserving resampling, the observed boundary arrangement is not reproduced in the sampled exact-shape channel, and the lower-tail channel is small.

\paragraph{Module partition.}
The module classifier assigns all thirteen members of $R$ and reports no extra module codons, no missing members, and no unassigned codons.  The complete partition is
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{agr}&=\{AGA,AGG\},\\
\mathrm{ar\_lys}&=\{AAA\},\\
\mathrm{cun}&=\{CUA,CUC,CUG,CUU\},\\
\mathrm{ile\_met}&=\{AUA\},\\

\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the row has both a geometric description, through its punctured-cube face vector, and a label-side description, through the module partition supplied by the curated code table.  The two descriptions meet at codon-label readback only.  The table says what labels the codons carry in the standard code; it does not observe ribosomal realization, protein conformation, energetic admissibility, biological role, or cross-organism necessity.

\paragraph{Local verdict.}
The finite evidence supports the following bounded conclusion.  In the six-coordinate codon display, the set $R$ is a thirteen-codon punctured local tile with face vector $(13,16,4)$, nontrivial spectral rows, small sampled shape probabilities, complete module assignment, and high leave-out recovery in the recorded probes.  The conclusion is deliberately local.  No statement about actual translation, folded protein structure, physical possibility, organismal function, causal mechanism, or universal biological law follows from this row unless a separate empirical contact supplies that higher-layer observation.
