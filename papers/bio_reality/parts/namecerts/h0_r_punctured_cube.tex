\paragraph{Finite carrier and observed row.}
The packet studies a six-coordinate codon display $Q_6$ whose rows are read against the curated standard-code label table.  The finite row singled out by the computation is the following thirteen-codon set:
$$
\begin{aligned}
R=\{&\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\\
&\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UUA}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The independent cardinality check returns $|R|=13$ and the listed members match the expected codons exactly.  The same finite row is certified as a punctured cube pattern with face data
$$
\begin{aligned}
f(R)=(13,16,4),
\end{aligned}
$$
so the object being discussed is not an arbitrary codon list: it has thirteen vertices, sixteen one-dimensional incidences, and four two-dimensional tile faces in the local codon geometry.  The contact used here is only the curated assignment of standard-code codons to amino-acid or stop labels; it is a label table, not an assay of translation dynamics or protein structure.

\paragraph{Local geometry.}
The geometric content is a code-layer split inside $Q_6$.  In the verified row, the codons form a local tile with a missing-corner character rather than a complete cube.  This is why the row is described as punctured: the evidence is a finite boundary pattern in the coordinate cube, together with a readout through the standard-code labels.  The claim is therefore deliberately narrow.  It says that the window-six coordinates expose a local boundary-tile configuration whose row is
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},
\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UUA}.

It does not say that this finite shape is a biological necessity.  The observed contact supports codon-label comparison and local split checks only.

\paragraph{Spectral and sampling evidence.}
The spectral probe returns two row-scale values, $\lambda_M=0.6752479308830213$ and $\lambda_R=0.4758352843197002$, together with $\mu=3.051487585298128$.  The radial cubic residual is numerically negligible at $-6.224354365258478\times 10^{-12}$, reported both as the cubic-polynomial residual and as the radial-polynomial value.  Thus the row is not merely combinatorial; it also sits in a finite spectral calculation with a stable numerical certificate.

The shape-significance run sampled $N=2000$ null rows with seed $42$.  It required $57377$ distinct-sample attempts and recorded $55377$ duplicate retries.  Among those samples there was $1$ exact shape match, giving $p_{\mathrm{exact}}=0.0005$, and $25$ samples with Jaccard score at least $0.9$, giving $p_{\mathrm{Jaccard}\ge 0.9}=0.0125$.  A second null model preserving amino-acid classes also used $N=2000$ and seed $42$.  For that control the observed module size was $20$, the exact-match count was $0$, the exact-shape value was $p=0.0$, and only $7$ samples fell in the lower tail at or below the observed size, giving $p_{\mathrm{lower\ tail}}=0.0035$.  These values support a finite code-layer regularity against the supplied controls, while still leaving the biological interpretation bounded by the contact actually used.

The leave-one-code-out run evaluated $23$ tables and $63$ total targets.  Its baseline hit rate was $0.19821739130434785$, while the hit rate above baseline was $0.9841269841269841$.  The hit rate inside $M-T$ was $0.9365079365079365$, and the rate inside $M-T$ plus boundary was again $0.9841269841269841$.  By family, the above-baseline hit rates were $0.8333333333333334$ for alternative yeast, and $1.0$ for bacterial/plastid, candidate-other, mitochondrial, and nuclear-ciliate families.  These figures say that the local coordinate row is useful for code-table readback under omissions; they do not raise the result to a mechanism of translation.

The leave-one-codon-out run reported $0$ anchors, $5$ boundary codons, $8$ high-coverage closure recoveries, $8$ predictable codons, and recovery rate $1.0$.  The complete module classification assigns all thirteen members of $R$ without surplus or omission:
