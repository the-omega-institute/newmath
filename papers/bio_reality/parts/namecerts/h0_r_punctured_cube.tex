\subsection{A punctured local codon cube}
\label{sec:bioreality-h0-r-punctured-cube}

\paragraph{Finite carrier.}
This record concerns the window-six presentation of the standard-code codon table.  The carrier is the six-bit codon cube $Q_6$, read only at the level of codon-to-label assignments.  The distinguished row set $R$ is the finite set
$$
\begin{aligned}
R = \{&\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\\
&\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UUA}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The independent cardinality check returns $|R|=13$, with the same thirteen codons.  In the local cubical description this set is not an arbitrary thirteen-row sample: the verified face count is
$$
\begin{aligned}
f(R)=(f_0,f_1,f_2)=(13,16,4),
\end{aligned}
$$
so the observed object is a punctured codon tile with thirteen vertices, sixteen one-dimensional adjacencies, and four square faces.  This is the finite geometric content of the claim.  It is a statement about the code-read layer of the codon table, not about the biochemical implementation of translation.

\paragraph{Module decomposition.}
The thirteen rows also admit a complete module accounting.  The verified module partition assigns
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{AGR}&=\{\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG}\},\\
\mathrm{AR\_Lys}&=\{\mathrm{AAA}\},\\
\mathrm{CUN}&=\{\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU}\},\\
\mathrm{Ile\_Met}&=\{\mathrm{AUA}\},\\
\mathrm{SSN}&=\{\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UUA}\},\\
\mathrm{Stop\_Trp}&=\{\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UGA}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The classification reports $R\_\mathrm{size}=13$, assigned count $13$, no extra module codons, no missing module codons, and no unassigned codons.  Thus every codon in $R$ is accounted for by one of the six named local modules.  The row set can therefore be discussed simultaneously as a punctured cubical tile and as a finite collection of code-label modules.

\paragraph{Shape significance.}
The finite sampling check used $N=2000$ samples with seed $42$.  It required $57377$ distinct-sample attempts and incurred $55377$ duplicate retries.  The exact-shape match count was $1$, giving
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{\mathrm{exact}}=0.0005.
\end{aligned}
$$
The high-overlap threshold reported $25$ samples with Jaccard score at least $0.9$, giving
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{J\geq 0.9}=0.0125.
\end{aligned}
$$
Both numbers are small relative to the $2000$-sample experiment.  They do not prove a biological law, but they do say that this punctured cubical row shape was rare under the tested finite sampling scheme.

\subsection{Reality contact and local boundary}
\label{sec:bioreality-h0-r-punctured-cube-boundary}

\paragraph{Observed contact.}
The only reality contact used here is the curated standard genetic-code table, treated as a codon-label source.  Its observed fact is that codons are assigned amino-acid or stop labels.  This contact has enough resolution to test code-layer readback and local label splits, including the thirteen-row punctured tile above.  It does not observe ribosomal realization, molecular folding, energetic admissibility, cellular function, or evolutionary universality.

\paragraph{Boundary statement.}
The scientific conclusion is therefore deliberately narrow.  The window-six coordinates expose a finite local tile in the standard-code label table: $R$ has thirteen specified codons, cubical face vector $(13,16,4)$, complete module assignment, small sampled exact-shape probabilities, a near-zero spectral residual, and strong leave-one-out code-layer recovery.  None of these finite rows licenses a claim that biological translation must follow this geometry, that an amino-acid label sequence determines folded protein structure, that the tile is physically admissible as a molecular mechanism, that it realizes biological function, or that it is a universal biological law.  Any assertion at those higher layers would need a separate contact with the corresponding observation type.
