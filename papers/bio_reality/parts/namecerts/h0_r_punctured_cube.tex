\paragraph{Claim and finite carrier.}

The finite carrier is the codon cube $Q_6$, with coordinates used only as a bookkeeping layer for local adjacency, closure, spectral, and relation probes. \begin{aligned}

&\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UUA}\}.

$$
The independent cardinality check returns $|R|=13$, with the same thirteen codons.  The local cubical incidence summary is the face-count vector
$$
\begin{aligned}

$$

This is a code-read statement about a curated codon table.  It is not a statement about ribosomal realization, molecular structure, chemical admissibility, organismal function, a causal mechanism, or a universal biological principle.

The radial cubic check is numerically closed at $-6.224354365258478\cdot 10^{-12}$, and the same residual is reported for the cubic-polynomial test. A separate amino-acid-class-preserving null with $N=2000$ trials found no exact shape matches, so its exact-shape rate is $0.0$, while the lower-tail count at the observed size $|M|=20$ is $7$, giving $p_{\mathrm{lower\ tail}}=0.0035$.

\paragraph{Local boundary discipline.}
The curated standard-code table supplies codon-to-label assignments and permits a local split check between singleton label corners, three-point label classes, and adjacent tile faces.  It does not observe translation as a physical process, does not observe folded protein structure, does not test whether a peptide is chemically admissible, and does not measure biological function.  In particular, amino-acid label sequences read from the table are still labels, not structures.  CUN and UUR wording, tRNA-Leu notation, or cross-organism comparison language must remain on their own organism-matched contact route unless a separate experiment supplies those observations.  The finite evidence therefore supports a precise code-layer statement: the thirteen-row set $R$ forms a punctured local cubical tile with non-random shape signatures and strong held-out label recovery, while every claim above codon-label readback remains blocked until independently contacted.
