\paragraph{Finite codon carrier.} This record treats the standard-code codon table at the code-read layer only. The finite carrier is the six-coordinate codon display obtained by reading each RNA codon as a window-six object, with local distinctions given by coordinate position, closure membership, spectral behavior, and codon-to-label relation. The selected boundary set $R$ is not an inferred biological mechanism; it is the finite row set singled out by the coordinate calculation and then compared against the curated standard-code labels. The verified row is
$$
\begin{aligned}
R = \{&AAA, AGA, AGG, AUA, CUA, CUC, CUG, CUU,\\
&UAA, UAG, UCA, UGA, UUA\},\\
|R| &= 13,\\
f(R) &= (13,16,4).
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the local object has thirteen codon vertices, sixteen one-dimensional adjacencies, and four square faces in the chosen cube complex. The phrase ``punctured cube'' is used here in this finite combinatorial sense: the row set behaves like a local cube tile with a missing or exposed boundary, not like a statement that nucleotide biology must realize that geometry as a law.

\paragraph{Label readback and singleton corner.} The reality contact used here is the curated standard genetic-code table: each codon is assigned an amino-acid or stop label. This contact is sufficient for codon-label readback and local label-split tests. It is not a contact for translation dynamics, folded protein structure, physical admissibility, biological function, or universality. In this row, the local tile contains the singleton-style entries $AAA$ and $AUA$, the arginine pair $AGA,AGG$, the four-codon leucine block $CUA,CUC,CUG,CUU$, the stop-related block $UAA,UAG,UGA$, and the two single-row serine/leucine-side entries $UCA,UUA$. The module classification accounts for every member of $R$:
$$
\begin{aligned}
R_{ar\_lys} &= \{AAA\},\\
R_{agr} &= \{AGA,AGG\},\\
R_{ile\_met} &= \{AUA\},\\
R_{cun} &= \{CUA,CUC,CUG,CUU\},\\

\end{aligned}
$$
The module audit reports $R$-size $13$, assigned count $13$, no missing module codons, no extra module codons, and no unassigned codons. This is a finite completeness statement about the local code-read partition; it does not promote the partition to a statement about organismal translation or protein realization.

They do not measure biochemical rate, thermodynamic feasibility, folding geometry, or biological fitness.

\paragraph{Shape significance against finite nulls.} The shape comparison was run with $N=2000$ sampled null trials and seed $42$. In the unconstrained shape experiment, there was one exact shape match, giving $p_{exact}=0.0005$, and twenty-five samples with Jaccard score at least $0.9$, giving $p_{J\ge 0.9}=0.0125$. The sampler recorded $57377$ distinct-sample attempts and $55377$ duplicate retries. In the amino-acid-class-preserving null, the observed closure-side size was $20$, the exact shape match count was $0$, and the lower-tail count at or below the observed $M$-size was $7$. Hence
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{aa\text{-}class,exact} &= 0.0,\\
p_{aa\text{-}class,lower\ tail} &= 0.0035,\\
|M|_{observed} &= 20.
\end{aligned}
$$
These finite null results support the claim that the observed local tile is not a routine output of the tested randomizations. They still remain code-layer evidence: the calculation observes codon-coordinate and codon-label structure, not cellular implementation.

The baseline hit rate was $0.19821739130434785$, while the hit rate above baseline was $0.9841269841269841$. The hit rate inside $M-T$ was $0.9365079365079365$, and the rate inside $M-T$ plus boundary was $0.9841269841269841$. By family, the hit-rate-above-baseline values were
$$
\begin{aligned}
\text{alternative yeast} &= 0.8333333333333334,\\
\text{bacterial plastid} &= 1.0,\\
\text{candidate other} &= 1.0,\\
\text{mitochondrial} &= 1.0,\\

The leave-one-codon-out row records boundary count $5$, anchor count $0$, predictable count $8$, high closure coverage $8$, and recovery rate $1.0$. These values show that the local coordinate construction has strong finite recovery behavior for the tested code-read tasks. They do not show that the table is generated by the coordinate construction in nature.

\paragraph{Boundary of the claim.} The admissible conclusion is narrow. The window-six codon coordinates expose a local punctured-cube pattern on the thirteen-row set $R$, and the curated standard-code table lets those rows be read as amino-acid or stop labels. The verified finite data include the exact $R$ list, the face vector $(13,16,4)$, the spectral values, the null-model $p$-values, the leave-one-out hit rates, and the complete module assignment. No translation process, folded structure, physical admissibility condition, functional role, universal mechanism, or global biological law follows from this contact. Any such stronger assertion would require an additional experiment or observation whose object is that higher layer itself.
