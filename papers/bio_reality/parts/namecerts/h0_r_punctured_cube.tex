\subsection{A punctured local cube in the standard code}\label{sec:bioreality-h0-r-punctured-cube}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite object.}
This record concerns a six-coordinate codon display of the curated standard genetic code.  The biological contact used here is only the codon-to-label table: each codon is read as an amino-acid or stop label, and no translation apparatus, folded molecule, physical energy model, or organism-level function is part of the contact.  In that restricted code-read layer, the distinguished residual set $R$ is exactly the following thirteen codons:
$$
\begin{aligned}
R=\{&\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\\
&\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UUA}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The independent cardinality check reports $|R|=13$, with the same list of codons.  The local cubical computation reports the face data
$$
\begin{aligned}
(f_0,f_1,f_2)=(13,16,4),
\end{aligned}
$$
so the observed set is not being treated merely as a list of exceptions.  It has a finite local shape: thirteen vertices, sixteen one-dimensional edges, and four square faces in the window-six coordinate display.

\paragraph{Spectral readout.}
The same row gives a numerical spectral profile for the codon-coordinate construction.  The reported values are
$$
\begin{aligned}
\lambda_M&=0.6752479308830213,\\
\lambda_R&=0.4758352843197002,\\
\mu&=3.051487585298128.
\end{aligned}
$$
The radial cubic check is numerically closed at the reported tolerance, with value $-6.224354365258478\cdot 10^{-12}$.  This is evidence that the finite coordinate calculation is internally coherent as a local shape computation.  It is not evidence that molecular translation must realize this geometry, and it is not evidence that any protein structure or biological function follows from the codon labels.

\paragraph{Shape significance.}
Against the reported shape null model, the experiment used $N=2000$ samples with seed $42$.  It required $57377$ distinct-sample attempts and incurred $55377$ duplicate retries.  The exact shape appeared once, giving
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{\mathrm{exact}}=0.0005.
\end{aligned}
$$
The relaxed high-overlap test counted $25$ samples with Jaccard score at least $0.9$, giving
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{J\geq 0.9}=0.0125.
\end{aligned}
$$
A second null model preserving amino-acid classes also used $N=2000$ samples and seed $42$.  In that run the observed module size was $20$, the exact-shape count was $0$, and the lower-tail count at or below the observed size was $7$.  The reported probabilities were
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{\mathrm{class,exact}}&=0.0,\\
p_{\mathrm{class,lower\ tail}}&=0.0035.
\end{aligned}
$$
These numbers support the claim that the observed local tile is unusual under the tested finite reshufflings.  They do not license a claim of a global law of DNA, a universal mechanism, or a physical admissibility theorem.

\paragraph{Predictive rows.}
The leave-one-code-out row evaluated $23$ code tables and $63$ targets.  Its baseline hit rate was $0.19821739130434785$.  The hit rate above baseline was $0.9841269841269841$, the hit rate inside $M-T$ was $0.9365079365079365$, and the hit rate inside $M-T$ plus boundary was $0.9841269841269841$.  The family-level refinement grouped the evaluated codes into $5$ families, with hit rates above baseline equal to $0.8333333333333334$ for alternative yeast and $1.0$ for bacterial/plastid, candidate-other, mitochondrial, and nuclear-ciliate families.  The leave-one-codon-out row reports $8$ predictable codons, $5$ boundary codons, $0$ anchors, high closure coverage $8$, and recovery rate $1.0$.  These rows say that the local code-layer construction recovers held-out codon labels well inside the tested finite regime.

\paragraph{Module classification.}
The thirteen codons of $R$ are completely assigned to six named modules:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{agr}&=\{\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG}\},\\
\mathrm{ar\_lys}&=\{\mathrm{AAA}\},\\
\mathrm{cun}&=\{\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU}\},\\
\mathrm{ile\_met}&=\{\mathrm{AUA}\},\\
\mathrm{ssn}&=\{\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UUA}\},\\
\mathrm{stop\_trp}&=\{\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UGA}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The classification row reports $R$-size $13$, assigned count $13$, no missing codons from the modules, no extra module codons, and no unassigned codons.  Every module has either predictable or boundary status.  Thus the finite record is closed at the level of local codon-label organization: the displayed residual set, its cubical face counts, its spectral values, its null-model rarity, its prediction rows, and its module partition all refer to the same code-read object.

\paragraph{Boundary of the claim.}
The claim established here is deliberately narrow.  The curated standard-code table supplies label readback, and the finite coordinate calculation exposes a punctured local cube on the thirteen displayed codons.  Nothing in this row observes ribosomal realization, kinetic feasibility, folded structure, physical admissibility, biological function, organismal adaptation, or universality across life.  Any assertion at one of those higher layers would require a separate contact with data at that layer.  Until such a contact is supplied, the result remains a finite code-layer record: a statistically unusual local tile in the standard-code label table, not a mechanism of translation and not a theorem about proteins.
