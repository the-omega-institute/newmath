\subsection{Numerical checks on the local tile}
\label{sec:h0-r-punctured-cube-numerics}

\paragraph{Shape significance against unconstrained shuffles.}
The finite shape was tested against $N=2000$ sampled alternatives with seed $42$.  The sampling process made $57377$ distinct-sample attempts and encountered $55377$ duplicate retries, which is already a warning that the null space is small enough to require careful finite accounting.  Exactly one sampled alternative matched the observed shape, giving
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{\mathrm{exact}}=0.0005.
\end{aligned}
$$
A looser comparison using Jaccard threshold at least $0.9$ gave $25$ matches among the $2000$ samples, hence
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{J\geq 0.9}=0.0125.
\end{aligned}
$$
Both numbers support the claim that the local punctured-cube arrangement is not a typical row under this finite null experiment.  They do not by themselves identify a mechanism; they only quantify how exceptional this particular code-label geometry is under the specified resampling procedure.
