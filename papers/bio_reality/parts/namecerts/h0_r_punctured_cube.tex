\paragraph{Finite object.}
This record concerns the code-read layer of the standard genetic-code table, represented as a six-coordinate codon display. Each codon is read as a finite row in the carrier $Q_{6}$, with the observed label supplied by the curated standard-code table. The distinguished set in this row is
$R=\{\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UUA}\}$.
The direct cardinality check gives $|R|=13$, and the independently recorded cardinality row returns the same thirteen codons. The cell-combinatorics row identifies this set as a punctured local cube with face data
$$
\begin{aligned}
  f(R)&=(13,16,4),\\
  |R|&=13.
\end{aligned}
$$
The point of the record is therefore narrow: the finite coordinate system isolates a local boundary tile in the standard-code readback, not a biological law about translation or protein realization.

\paragraph{Local tile reading.}
The punctured-cube row separates the finite codon display into a small family of visible boundary phenomena. The carrier is the six-bit codon coordinate cube; the observed distinctions are a local tile face, a singleton label corner, and a three-point label class. The internal bookkeeping is by coordinate, closure, spectrum, and relation, while the only contact with biology in this row is the standard-code codon-label table. In particular, $\mathrm{AAA}$ is isolated inside the recorded module $\mathrm{ar\_lys}$; $\mathrm{AGA}$ and $\mathrm{AGG}$ form the recorded $\mathrm{agr}$ module; $\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU}$ form the $\mathrm{cun}$ module; $\mathrm{AUA}$ is the $\mathrm{ile\_met}$ module; $\mathrm{UCA}$ and $\mathrm{UUA}$ form the $\mathrm{ssn}$ module; and $\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UGA}$ form the $\mathrm{stop\_trp}$ module. The module audit reports $R\_\mathrm{size}=13$, $\mathrm{assigned\_count}=13$, no extra module codons, no missing codons, and no unassigned codons. Thus the punctured-cube description covers exactly the recorded $R$ rows, with no surplus codon imported by the module names.

\paragraph{Null comparison.}
The coordinate shape was compared against finite null rows. In the recorded shape-significance run, $N=2000$ distinct samples were evaluated after $57377$ distinct-sample attempts and $55377$ duplicate retries. There was $1$ exact shape match, giving $p_{\mathrm{exact}}=0.0005$, and $25$ samples with Jaccard similarity at least $0.9$, giving $p_{\mathrm{Jaccard}\geq 0.9}=0.0125$. The amino-acid-class-preserving null run also used $N=2000$ with seed $42$; it recorded $0$ exact matches, $7$ lower-tail samples at or below the observed $M$ size, observed $|M|=20$, $p_{\mathrm{aa\ class\ exact}}=0.0$, and $p_{\mathrm{aa\ class\ lower\ tail}}=0.0035$. These numbers support the claim that the finite standard-code row has non-generic local geometry under the tested coordinate procedures. They do not turn the geometry into a claim about folding, physical admissibility, biological function, or cross-organism necessity.

The hit rate inside $M-T$ was $0.9365079365079365$, and the hit rate inside $M-T$ plus the boundary was $0.9841269841269841$. Family-level code-out checks give five families: alternative yeast has hit rate $0.8333333333333334$, while bacterial plastid, candidate other, mitochondrial, and nuclear ciliate each have hit rate $1.0$. These audits make the local tile robust as a code-label readback phenomenon: when a codon or related code row is withheld, the recorded boundary and closure data recover the finite label-side pattern at high rate.

\paragraph{Contact and limit of interpretation.}
The only reality contact used here is the curated standard-code table assigning codons to amino-acid or stop labels. That contact can test code-layer readback and the local label split; it cannot test translation realization, folded structure, physical admissibility, biological function, or a universal mechanism. Consequently, the row licenses the following bounded statement: in the six-coordinate codon display, the thirteen listed codons form a punctured local cube with $f$-vector $(13,16,4)$, supported by the recorded spectrum, null comparisons, leave-one recovery, and complete module assignment. It does not license any inference that DNA must obey this geometry as a biological necessity, that amino-acid label sequences determine folded protein structure, or that CUN/UUR-style wording supplies structure evidence. Any claim beyond code\_read requires a separate contact that observes the higher layer directly.
