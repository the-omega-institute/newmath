\subsection{Window-six punctured-cube record for the standard-code label table}
\label{sec:h0-r-punctured-cube}

\paragraph{Finite codon display.}
This record works at the code-read layer only. The carrier is the six-coordinate codon cube obtained by displaying each standard-code codon as a finite window-six object, and the reality contact is the curated standard-code table assigning codons to amino-acid or stop labels. The distinguished boundary set $R$ is the thirteen-codon row
$$
\begin{aligned}
R=\{&\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\\
&\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UUA}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The independent cardinality row reports $|R|=13$ and lists the same thirteen codons. The punctured-cube row records that this set has face-count data
$$
\begin{aligned}
f(R)=(13,16,4),
\end{aligned}
$$
so the observed object is not merely a named collection of labels: it is a finite local tile with thirteen vertices, sixteen one-dimensional adjacencies, and four square faces in the window-six codon geometry.

\paragraph{Label contact and local boundary.}
The contact does not assert that DNA is forced to realize this geometry. It only supplies the codon-to-label table against which the local split can be read. The biological object is therefore the standard-code label table, not translation machinery, folded protein state, thermodynamic admissibility, organismal function, or a universal mechanism. The local boundary set may be inspected for code-layer structure, but any claim above codon-label readback requires a separate contact of the appropriate kind. In particular, amino-acid label sequences produced from the table are not folded structures, and no claim about protein function follows from the presence of the tile.

\paragraph{Randomization checks.}
The shape-significance run used $N=2000$ samples with seed $42$. It required $57377$ distinct-sample attempts and encountered $55377$ duplicate retries. Among those samples, the exact shape appeared once, giving $p_{\mathrm{exact}}=0.0005$, and there were $25$ samples with Jaccard score at least $0.9$, giving $p_{\mathrm{Jaccard}\ge 0.9}=0.0125$. The amino-acid-class-preserving null also used $N=2000$ and seed $42$. It reported an observed $M$ size of $20$, no exact shape matches, and $7$ lower-tail samples at or below the observed $M$ size, giving
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{\mathrm{aa\ class\ exact}}&=0.0,\\
p_{\mathrm{aa\ class\ lower\ tail}}&=0.0035.
\end{aligned}
$$
These rows support the claim that the local configuration is statistically non-generic under the tested finite null models. They do not promote the configuration to a biological law; they only say that this code-layer finite shape is uncommon under the stated randomization schemes.

In the window-six codon cube, the thirteen-codon set $R$ forms a punctured-cube boundary tile with $f$-vector $(13,16,4)$, stable spectral scalars, non-generic shape behavior under the stated null checks, strong leave-out recovery, and a complete six-module partition.
