\subsection{The punctured-cube row of the standard code}\label{sec:bioreality-punctured-cube-row}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite object.}
This record concerns the code-read layer of the standard genetic-code table. The finite carrier is the six-bit codon cube obtained from three nucleotide positions, each read as a two-bit display coordinate. The observed row is the subset $R$ of codons where the local coordinate procedure singles out the boundary tile rather than the ambient cube. In the verified run $e19917012c60$, the row is
$$
\begin{aligned}
R=\{&\texttt{AAA},\texttt{AGA},\texttt{AGG},\texttt{AUA},\texttt{CUA},\texttt{CUC},\texttt{CUG},\texttt{CUU},\\
&\texttt{UAA},\texttt{UAG},\texttt{UCA},\texttt{UGA},\texttt{UUA}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The independent cardinality check $5d4a3459997d$ reports $|R|=13$ and returns the same thirteen codons. The boundary complex has verified face-count vector $(13,16,4)$: thirteen vertices, sixteen local edges, and four local two-faces. Thus the row is not merely a list of exceptional labels; it is a finite punctured-cube pattern in the codon coordinate display.

\paragraph{Code labels and observed contact.}
The reality contact used here is only the curated standard-code table. It assigns each codon to an amino-acid or stop label and thereby permits a readback of the row at the code-label level. It does not observe translation dynamics, a folded protein, chemical admissibility, phenotype, or organismal function. The contact therefore supports the statement that the standard-code labels can be compared against this local tile, but it does not convert the tile into a biological law. In particular, the three-point label classes and singleton corner behavior are code-table facts inside the finite row, not mechanism-level explanations.

\paragraph{Spectral row.}
The spectral measurement $1026324eff41$ gives $\lambda_M=0.6752479308830213$, $\lambda_R=0.4758352843197002$, and $\mu=3.051487585298128$. The same run reports the radial cubic residual as $-6.224354365258478\times 10^{-12}$, which is numerically at zero at the recorded tolerance. A compact way to record the numerical check is
$$
\begin{aligned}
\lambda_M&=0.6752479308830213,\\
\lambda_R&=0.4758352843197002,\\
\mu&=3.051487585298128,\\
P(\mu)&=-6.224354365258478\times 10^{-12}.
\end{aligned}
$$
These values describe the finite coordinate spectrum of the row and its surrounding complement. They do not, by themselves, supply biochemical causation. Their role is to show that the same thirteen-codon row has a reproducible numerical profile in the local cube calculation.

\paragraph{Null comparisons.}
The shape comparison run $0b267c88f8e4$ sampled $N=2000$ distinct null cases after $57377$ sample attempts and $55377$ duplicate retries. It found one exact shape match, giving $p_{\mathrm{exact}}=0.0005$, and twenty-five cases with Jaccard similarity at least $0.9$, giving $p_{\mathrm{Jaccard}\ge 0.9}=0.0125$. The amino-acid-class-preserving null run $e8d5de747a0c$ also used $N=2000$ and reported no exact shape matches, so $p_{\mathrm{class,exact}}=0.0$. It recorded $7$ lower-tail cases at or below the observed $M$-size, with observed $|M|=20$ and $p_{\mathrm{class,lower}}=0.0035$. These tests say that the finite geometry is uncommon under the recorded null procedures. They do not show that the code must have this geometry, and they do not identify a physical process that produced it.

\paragraph{Leave-out checks.}
Two recovery probes measure whether the row remains informative when finite information is withheld. In the leave-one-code-out experiment $429e6e2c4eb4$, $23$ tables were evaluated against $63$ total targets. The baseline hit rate was $0.19821739130434785$, while the hit rate above baseline was $0.9841269841269841$. The hit rate inside $M-T$ was $0.9365079365079365$, and the hit rate inside $M-T$ together with boundary codons was $0.9841269841269841$. In the leave-one-codon-out run $e9e1a6e41633$, the recovery rate was $1.0$, with $8$ predictable codons, $5$ boundary codons, $8$ high-coverage closure cases, and no anchors. These values support a local prediction-and-boundary reading: the row is stable as a finite code-table pattern, but the stability remains inside the code-read layer.

\paragraph{Family and module accounting.}
The family-level leave-one-code-out run $7dda978c6c97$ groups the evaluated cases into five families. Its recorded hit rates above baseline are $0.8333333333333334$ for alternative yeast, $1.0$ for bacterial and plastid tables, $1.0$ for candidate-other tables, $1.0$ for mitochondrial tables, and $1.0$ for nuclear-ciliate tables. The module-classification run $bbe8a4d0455b$ assigns all thirteen row codons to six named modules with no extras, no missing row elements, and no unassigned codons. The modules are $\texttt{agr}=\{\texttt{AGA},\texttt{AGG}\}$, $\texttt{ar\_lys}=\{\texttt{AAA}\}$, $\texttt{cun}=\{\texttt{CUA},\texttt{CUC},\texttt{CUG},\texttt{CUU}\}$, $\texttt{ile\_met}=\{\texttt{AUA}\}$, $\texttt{ssn}=\{\texttt{UCA},\texttt{UUA}\}$, and $\texttt{stop\_trp}=\{\texttt{UAA},\texttt{UAG},\texttt{UGA}\}$. The complete assignment records that the local row is organized by recognizable codon-label modules rather than by an unexplained residue list.

\paragraph{Boundary of interpretation.}
The row may be used as a finite, testable code-layer object: a thirteen-codon punctured-cube tile with face vector $(13,16,4)$, a recorded spectrum, significant null comparisons, and recovery behavior under leave-out probes. The same data must not be promoted beyond its contact. No translation event, folded structure, physical admissibility condition, biological function, universal mechanism, or organism-wide necessity follows from this chapter. Any such higher-layer statement would require a separate observation aimed at that layer, not a reinterpretation of the curated codon-label table.
