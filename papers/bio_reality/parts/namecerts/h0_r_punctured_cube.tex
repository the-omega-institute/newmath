\subsection{The punctured codon cube as a code-read packet}\label{sec:bioreality-h0-r-punctured-cube}

\paragraph{Finite object and observation layer.}
The packet studies a finite codon-window display, not a biochemical mechanism.  The carrier is the six-coordinate codon cube $Q_6$, where the coordinate system is used only to arrange standard-code labels into a local combinatorial object.  The observed contact is a curated standard genetic-code table: it assigns each codon to an amino-acid or stop label.  That contact is strong enough to test codon-label readback and local label splitting, but it does not observe translation dynamics, folded structure, thermodynamic admissibility, organismal function, or a universal biological law.

The word ``punctured'' is used here in this finite, coordinate-level sense: the table-defined label subset fills a cube-like local pattern with a missing or boundary-sensitive part, rather than asserting any global spatial law for DNA or proteins.

\paragraph{Null comparisons.}
The shape-significance run used $N=2000$ sampled alternatives with seed $42$.  The audit reports $57377$ distinct-sample attempts and $55377$ duplicate retries.  There was $1$ exact shape match, giving
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{\mathrm{exact}}=0.0005,
\end{aligned}
$$
and there were $25$ matches with Jaccard score at least $0.9$, giving
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{J\ge 0.9}=0.0125.
\end{aligned}
$$
The amino-acid-class-preserving null used the same $N=2000$ and seed $42$.  It reports observed $M$ size $20$, exact match count $0$, lower-tail count $7$, exact-shape value $0.0$, and lower-tail value
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{\mathrm{lower\ tail}}=0.0035.
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the recorded finite shape is not presented as an unavoidable biological law; it is presented as a low-frequency outcome under the tested finite shuffles, with the exact null model and sample counts kept visible.

\paragraph{Reality contact and blocked promotions.}
The only reality contact used in this chapter is the curated standard-code table.  It supplies codon-to-label assignments and lets the finite coordinate system ask whether the thirteen-codon row, its face counts, and its module coverage are present.  It does not supply a ribosome experiment, an organism-matched translation assay, a protein-folding contact, a physical-admissibility measurement, or a functional phenotype.  Therefore the chapter records a strict boundary: no translation realization, no folded structure, no structural order, no physical admissibility, no biological function, no mechanism, and no universality follows from the punctured cube unless a separate contact supplies that higher-layer observation.
