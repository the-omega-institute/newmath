\subsection{The punctured codon cube at the code-read boundary}
\label{sec:h0-r-punctured-cube}

\paragraph{Finite carrier and observed row.}
This packet treats the standard-code codon table as a finite code-layer contact over the six-bit codon cube $Q_6$.  The distinguished row $R$ is the thirteen-codon set
$$
\begin{aligned}
R=\{&\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\\
&\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UUA}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The finite audit records both the explicit equality $R=\{\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UUA}\}$ and the cardinality assertion $|R|=13$.  Its cubical incidence profile is the row vector $(13,16,4)$, read here as thirteen selected vertices, sixteen selected local one-dimensional adjacencies, and four selected square faces inside the codon display.  The phrase punctured cube is therefore used only for this finite local shape: it names a small face-bearing tile with a missing global completion, not a biological law.

\paragraph{Coordinate interpretation.}
The row is evaluated at the code\_read layer.  A curated standard-code table supplies codon-to-label readback, so the finite object may compare local coordinate shape with amino-acid or stop labels.  The contact does not observe translation, ribosome dynamics, folded protein structure, energetic admissibility, cellular function, or cross-organism universality.  Consequently the admissible conclusion is a local boundary statement: the six-bit coordinate system exposes a compact tile around the listed codons, while the labels attached to those codons remain supplied by the curated table.  No step in this packet converts the tile into a mechanism, and no step infers protein structure from the label sequence.

\paragraph{Spectral row check.}
The same row is accompanied by a scalar spectral record.  The measured values are
$$
\begin{aligned}
\lambda_M&=0.6752479308830213,\\
\lambda_R&=0.4758352843197002,\\
\mu&=3.051487585298128.
\end{aligned}
$$
The radial cubic residual is $-6.224354365258478\cdot 10^{-12}$, and the spectral-radius entry is again $0.6752479308830213$.  This is a numerical consistency check on the finite construction rather than a new biological observation.  It says that the encoded row has a stable coordinate signature under the chosen finite operators; it does not say that organisms must realize the same spectrum as a causal principle.

\paragraph{Shape significance against finite null rows.}
A random-shape comparison with $N=2000$ trials and seed $42$ found one exact shape match.  The resulting exact-shape value is $p=0.0005$.  The same run found twenty-five samples with Jaccard similarity at least $0.9$, giving $p=0.0125$.  The run required $57377$ distinct-sample attempts and incurred $55377$ duplicate retries, which is important because the row lives in a constrained finite space rather than in an unconstrained continuum.  A second null comparison preserving amino-acid classes used $N=2000$ and seed $42$; it found zero exact matches, an exact-shape value $p=0.0$, seven lower-tail samples at or below the observed $M$-size, observed $|M|=20$, and lower-tail value $p=0.0035$.  These numbers support the claim that the row shape is nontrivial among the tested finite alternatives, while leaving the biological scope at codon-label readback.

\paragraph{Leave-one evidence.}
The leave-one-code-out audit evaluated $23$ tables and $63$ targets.  Its baseline hit rate was $0.19821739130434785$, whereas the hit rate above baseline was $0.9841269841269841$.  Inside $M\setminus T$ the hit rate was $0.9365079365079365$, and after adding the boundary it was $0.9841269841269841$.  The leave-one-codon-out audit reported anchor count $0$, boundary count $5$, high closure coverage $8$, predictable count $8$, and recovery rate $1.0$.  These are finite recovery statistics: they show that the local row and its boundary are informative for held-out code-layer labels, not that the held-out codons are translated, folded, or functional because of the geometry.

\paragraph{Family and module resolution.}
The family-level leave-one-code-out record groups the evidence into five families.  The hit rates above baseline are $0.8333333333333334$ for alternative yeast, $1.0$ for bacterial or plastid codes, $1.0$ for the candidate-other family, $1.0$ for mitochondrial codes, and $1.0$ for nuclear ciliate codes.  The module classification covers all thirteen row codons with no extra module codons, no missing row codons, and no unassigned row codons.  The assigned modules are
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{agr}&=\{\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG}\},\\
\mathrm{ar\_lys}&=\{\mathrm{AAA}\},\\
\mathrm{cun}&=\{\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU}\},\\
\mathrm{ile\_met}&=\{\mathrm{AUA}\},\\
\mathrm{ssn}&=\{\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UUA}\},\\
\mathrm{stop\_trp}&=\{\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UGA}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the punctured-cube row is not merely a cardinality coincidence: it decomposes into named finite modules matching the observed boundary behavior.  The packet remains intentionally narrow.  It records a local codon-tile phenomenon over a curated standard-code label table, with explicit refusal to promote the result to translation, folding, physical admissibility, biological function, mechanism, or universality without an independent contact for that higher layer.
