\subsection{Local boundary and non-promotion rule}
\label{sec:bioreality-h0-r-punctured-cube-boundary}

\paragraph{Reality contact.}
The only contact used here is a curated standard-code table assigning codons to amino-acid or stop labels.  That contact is sufficient for code-label readback and for checking the local split induced by the punctured-cube tile.  It is not an observation of ribosomal translation, a measurement of folding, a physical-admissibility experiment, a functional assay, or a proof of universality across biological contexts.

The finite data support the statement that the thirteen-codon set $R$ forms a punctured-cube-like local tile with $f(R)=(13,16,4)$ and nontrivial finite predictive behavior in the supplied leave-one-out rows.  No stronger biological conclusion follows from this record alone.  In particular, the row does not establish translation realization, folded protein structure, structural order, physical admissibility, biological function, a universal biological mechanism, or a global law governing DNA.  Any such statement would require a separate contact at the appropriate biological layer, not merely the standard-code label table used here.
