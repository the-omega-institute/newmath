\subsection{The punctured-cube row in the standard-code display}\label{sec:h0-r-punctured-cube}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite object.}
The record attached to claim $h0.R.punctured.cube$ treats the standard genetic-code table as a labeled finite display on the six-coordinate codon cube $Q_6$.  The biological input used here is only the curated codon-to-label table: each codon is read as a code-layer label, either an amino-acid name or a stop label.  The distinguished row $R$ is not introduced as a biochemical mechanism.  It is the finite set of codon positions selected by the local window-six construction, and in the verified run it is exactly
$$\begin{aligned}
R=\{&AAA,AGA,AGG,AUA,CUA,CUC,CUG,CUU,\\
&UAA,UAG,UCA,UGA,UUA\}.
\end{aligned}$$
The same run records $|R|=13$, with the separately audited cardinality row giving the same thirteen codons.  The point of the row is therefore precise: a thirteen-vertex subobject appears inside the codon cube, and its code labels can be inspected against the curated standard table.

\paragraph{Punctured-cube shape.}
The shape computation reports that this thirteen-codon row has face data
$$\begin{aligned}
(f_0,f_1,f_2)=(13,16,4).
\end{aligned}$$
Thus the observed object is not merely a list of exceptional codons.  It carries a small cubical boundary profile: thirteen vertices, sixteen one-dimensional adjacencies, and four square faces.  The phrase ``punctured cube'' is used in this narrow combinatorial sense.  It names the finite local tile seen in the coordinate display, not a claim that codon reassignment, ribosomal decoding, molecular folding, or organism-level function is governed by this geometry.

\paragraph{Spectral row.}
A companion spectral calculation gives the row a numerical profile.  The verified values are
$$\begin{aligned}
\lambda_M&=0.6752479308830213,\\
\lambda_R&=0.4758352843197002,\\
\mu&=3.051487585298128.
\end{aligned}$$
The cubic check is numerically closed at the recorded tolerance: both the cubic-polynomial residual and the radial-polynomial residual are $-6.224354365258478\cdot 10^{-12}$.  These figures are useful because they give the local tile a reproducible coordinate-and-spectrum signature.  They do not add any biological force beyond the code-table readback; they only say that the same finite subset can be recognized by adjacency and spectral diagnostics.

\paragraph{Randomization checks.}
The shape-significance run used $N=2000$ samples with seed $42$.  It required $57377$ distinct-sample attempts and $55377$ duplicate retries.  Among those samples there was one exact shape match, giving $p_{exact}=0.0005$, and twenty-five samples with Jaccard score at least $0.9$, giving $p_{J\ge 0.9}=0.0125$.  A separate amino-acid-class-preserving null run, again with $N=2000$ and seed $42$, recorded observed $M$-size $20$, exact-match count $0$, lower-tail count $7$, $p=0.0$ for exact shape in that null, and lower-tail $p=0.0035$.  These checks support the statement that the local shape is not a typical accident of the sampled finite controls.  They still remain code-layer tests: the controls shuffle or preserve label classes in a finite table, rather than measuring translation kinetics, structural stability, or biological fitness.

\paragraph{Recovery and module behavior.}
The leave-one-code-out audit evaluated $23$ code tables and $63$ total targets.  Its baseline hit rate was $0.19821739130434785$, while the hit rate above baseline was $0.9841269841269841$.  The hit rate inside $M\setminus T$ was $0.9365079365079365$, and the hit rate inside $M\setminus T$ together with boundary positions was again $0.9841269841269841$.  The leave-one-codon-out audit found no anchor codons, five boundary codons, eight high-coverage closure cases, eight predictable codons, and recovery rate $1.0$.  At family level, five families were recorded: alternative yeast has hit rate $0.8333333333333334$, while bacterial plastid, candidate other, mitochondrial, and nuclear ciliate each have hit rate $1.0$.

The module classification is complete for the thirteen codons: $R$-size is $13$, assigned count is $13$, and there are no extra module codons, no missing entries, and no unassigned codons.  The recorded partition is

Every listed module has either a predictable or boundary status in the leave-one-codon audit.  This is the strongest local reading licensed by the finite data: the same thirteen-position tile, its module grouping, and its recovery behavior cohere inside the curated code-label display.

\paragraph{Scope of the claim.}
The contact used by this chapter is the curated standard-code table.  It supplies codon labels and permits local split checks.  It does not observe translation realization, folded protein structure, physical admissibility, biological function, a molecular mechanism, or a universal biological law.  Consequently, amino-acid label sequences obtained from the table remain label sequences.  They are not folded structures, they are not functional proteins, and they are not evidence that the six-bit cube geometry is a necessary law of biology.  Any later statement about translation, folding, admissibility, function, mechanism, or universality must be supported by a separate empirical contact of the appropriate kind.
