\paragraph{Reality contact and scope.}
The only contact used here is the curated standard-code table assigning codons to amino-acid or stop labels.  It tests codon-label readback and the local split of the displayed tile.  It does not test translation as a biochemical process, folded structure, structural order, physical admissibility, biological function, a mechanism of code origin, or universality across biology.  In particular, reading the amino-acid labels for the codons in $R$ is not a protein-fold claim, and the local punctured-cube geometry is not a global biological law.
