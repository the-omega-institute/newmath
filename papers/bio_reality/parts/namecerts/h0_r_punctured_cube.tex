\paragraph{Finite object.}
This packet records a local window-six pattern inside the codon cube $Q_6$.  The working display is the six-bit codon presentation of the standard genetic-code table: each codon is a point of the finite cube after the three nucleotide positions have been expanded into binary coordinate features.  The distinguished row is the set
$$
\begin{aligned}
R=\{&\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\\
&\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UUA}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The finite audit gives $|R|=13$, with the same thirteen codons recovered in the direct cardinality row and in the punctured-cube row.  The local cell count is not merely a list size: the verified face data are
$$
\begin{aligned}
f(R)=(f_0,f_1,f_2)=(13,16,4),
\end{aligned}
$$
so the row behaves as a thirteen-vertex, sixteen-edge, four-square subcomplex of $Q_6$.  This is the sense in which the packet calls the row a punctured cube: it is a finite cubical trace with a missing-corner profile, not a claim that the biological table is generated by cubical physics.

\paragraph{Code-label readout.}
The contact used here is the curated standard-code table, and it is used only at the label layer.  Under that contact the row contains the singleton or small label corners associated with $\mathrm{AAA}$, $\mathrm{AGA}$, $\mathrm{AGG}$, $\mathrm{AUA}$, the CUN block $\{\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU}\}$, the stop-side block $\{\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UGA}\}$, and the $\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UUA}$ pair.  The standard-code labels may be read as codon labels, but no stronger biological process is read from them.  In particular, the amino-acid or stop assignment is a table contact; it is not an observation of ribosomal realization, folded conformation, thermodynamic admissibility, activity, or organism-level function.

\paragraph{Local boundary.}
The scientific claim is deliberately narrow.  The window-six coordinate pattern exposes a finite punctured-cube row in the standard-code label table, with a verified thirteen-codon support, face vector $(13,16,4)$, spectral values, randomization p-values, leave-out hit rates, and a complete module assignment.  Those facts justify a code-read statement about local boundary-tile structure.  They do not imply that DNA or RNA must obey this geometry as a biological necessity.  They do not establish translation realization, folded protein structure, physical admissibility, biochemical function, a universal mechanism, or any global law of biology.  Any such stronger statement would require a separate empirical contact at the relevant layer.
