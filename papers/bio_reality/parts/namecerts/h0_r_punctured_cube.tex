\subsection{Punctured codon cube at the code-read layer}
\label{subsec:h0-r-punctured-cube}

\paragraph{Finite object.}
This record concerns a six-coordinate codon display for the standard genetic-code table.  The observational contact is the curated assignment of codons to amino-acid or stop labels, and the claim is deliberately kept at that label-readout level.  In the finite row used here, the distinguished codon set $R$ has cardinality $13$ and is exactly
$$
\begin{aligned}
R=\{&AAA, AGA, AGG, AUA, CUA, CUC, CUG, CUU,\\
&UAA, UAG, UCA, UGA, UUA\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The computed cell count for the local punctured-cube complex is
$$
\begin{aligned}
(f_0,f_1,f_2)=(13,16,4),
\end{aligned}
$$
so the row is not merely a list of exceptional labels: it carries a specific finite face pattern with thirteen vertices, sixteen one-dimensional adjacencies, and four two-dimensional faces.  The puncture language is therefore used only for this local combinatorial shape inside the six-bit codon display, not for a global law of molecular biology.

\paragraph{Spectral and null-model coordinates.}
The same row has a finite spectral signature.  The recorded values are
$$
\begin{aligned}
\lambda_M&=0.6752479308830213,\\
\lambda_R&=0.4758352843197002,\\
\mu&=3.051487585298128,
\end{aligned}
$$
and the radial cubic check evaluates to $-6.224354365258478\cdot 10^{-12}$, within numerical tolerance for the stated computation.  Against $2000$ sampled comparison tables with seed $42$, the exact shape appeared once, giving $p_{\mathrm{exact}}=0.0005$.  The high-overlap threshold $J\geq 0.9$ was met $25$ times, giving $p_{J\geq 0.9}=0.0125$.  The sampling row also reports $57377$ distinct-sample attempts and $55377$ duplicate retries, so the empirical significance claim is tied to the actual finite sampling pressure rather than to an asymptotic assertion.

\paragraph{Amino-acid-class preserving comparison.}
A stricter comparison preserving amino-acid label classes used $2000$ samples with the same seed.  In that comparison the observed $M$ size is $20$, the exact-match count is $0$, and the lower-tail count at or below the observed $M$ size is $7$.  Thus the recorded empirical values are
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{\mathrm{class,exact}}&=0.0,\\
p_{\mathrm{class,lower}}&=0.0035.
\end{aligned}
$$
The zero here is a finite-sample statement, not a proof of impossibility: it says that no exact class-preserving sampled table among the $2000$ trials reproduced the observed shape.  Together with the ordinary null row, this supports the narrower statement that the standard-code labels occupy a non-generic local tile in the chosen codon coordinates.

\paragraph{Leave-one readback tests.}
The leave-one-code-out test evaluated $23$ tables and $63$ total targets.  Its baseline hit rate is $0.19821739130434785$, while the observed hit rate above baseline is $0.9841269841269841$.  The hit rate inside $M-T$ is $0.9365079365079365$, and the rate inside $M-T$ plus the boundary is again $0.9841269841269841$.  The family-level split has five families: alternative yeast has hit rate $0.8333333333333334$ above baseline, while bacterial-plastid, candidate-other, mitochondrial, and nuclear-ciliate each record $1.0$.  These numbers are read as code-table stability checks under finite deletion, not as claims about translation dynamics.

\paragraph{Leave-one-codon recovery and module accounting.}
The leave-one-codon-out row records $8$ predictable codons, $5$ boundary codons, no anchors, high closure coverage of $8$, and recovery rate $1.0$.  The module classification accounts for all thirteen codons of $R$ with no missing, extra, or unassigned codons.  The assigned modules are
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{AGR}&=\{AGA,AGG\},\\
\mathrm{AR/Lys}&=\{AAA\},\\
\mathrm{CUN}&=\{CUA,CUC,CUG,CUU\},\\
\mathrm{Ile/Met}&=\{AUA\},\\
\mathrm{SSN}&=\{UCA,UUA\},\\
\mathrm{Stop/Trp}&=\{UAA,UAG,UGA\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
Every listed module has either predictable or boundary status in the finite classification.  This is the strongest chapter-local conclusion: the thirteen-codon punctured tile is not an unstructured residue after the coordinate choice; it decomposes into named code-label modules that remain visible in deletion probes.

\paragraph{Scope of the biological reading.}
The curated standard-code table supplies codon-to-label observations only.  It permits the local statement that the six-bit codon coordinates expose a punctured-cube-like boundary tile whose labels are not generic under the reported null probes.  It does not by itself demonstrate translation realization, folded-protein structure, physical admissibility, biological function, an organism-level mechanism, or universality across biology.  Those stronger readings require separate observational contacts designed for those layers.  In particular, amino-acid label readback is not a structure measurement, CUN/UUR wording is not evidence of folding, and tRNA or organism-specific hypotheses must remain outside this row unless matched to their own data source.
