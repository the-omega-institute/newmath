\subsection{The Punctured Codon Cube Row}\label{sec:biores-h0-r-punctured-cube}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite row.}
The row indexed by \texttt{h0.R.punctured.cube} treats the standard-code table as a code-layer contact and asks whether the selected codon set $R$ has a small local shape inside the six-coordinate codon display. The verified set is
$$
\begin{aligned}
R=\{&\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\\
&\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UUA}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The companion cardinality row records $|R|=13$ and gives the same thirteen codons. In the cubical incidence readout the row has $f$-vector
$$
\begin{aligned}
f(R)=(13,16,4),
\end{aligned}
$$
so the observed object is a thirteen-vertex local tile with sixteen one-dimensional incidences and four two-dimensional faces. The word ``punctured'' is used only for this finite coordinate shape: it says that the tile occupies a local cubical pattern with one missing corner or gap in the relevant display, not that biology has been derived from the coordinates alone.

\paragraph{Code-label contact.}
The only supplied contact is the curated standard genetic-code table, which assigns each codon to an amino-acid or stop label. That contact is adequate for code-label readback and for testing whether the codons in $R$ form the stated local split. It is not a contact for translation dynamics, folded structure, physical feasibility, organismal function, or a universal mechanism. Thus the row can say that the code table supports the listed codon labels and that the coordinate tile is present in the finite codon display. It cannot say that the tile is a law of molecular biology, that a label sequence is a protein structure, or that a structural or functional claim follows from the code table.

\paragraph{Spectral check.}
The spectral row supplies three scalar quantities for the same finite packet. The reported values are
$$
\begin{aligned}
\lambda_M&=0.6752479308830213,\\
\lambda_R&=0.4758352843197002,\\
\mu&=3.051487585298128.
\end{aligned}
$$
The corresponding radial polynomial evaluation is $-6.224354365258478\times 10^{-12}$, the same value reported for the cubic residual. At the precision of the finite computation, this is a near-zero residual rather than a new biological measurement. Its role is to record that the coordinate object has a stable numerical spectral signature in the chosen finite encoding. The value $\lambda_M=0.6752479308830213$ is larger than $\lambda_R=0.4758352843197002$, so the selected punctured row does not exhaust the surrounding module geometry measured by $M$.

\paragraph{Shape significance.}
The shape-significance row compares the observed geometry against sampled alternatives. With seed $42$ and $N=2000$ samples, the run made $57377$ distinct-sample attempts and recorded $55377$ duplicate retries. It found one exact shape match, giving
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{\mathrm{exact}}=0.0005.
\end{aligned}
$$
For the high-overlap threshold it found $25$ matches with Jaccard score at least $0.9$, giving
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{J\geq 0.9}=0.0125.
\end{aligned}
$$
These values support the narrower statement that the observed finite tile is unusual under the sampled comparison procedure. A second null row preserves amino-acid class counts. In that class-preserving run, also with seed $42$ and $N=2000$, the observed $M$ size was $20$, the exact-match count was $0$, and the lower-tail count at or below the observed size was $7$. The corresponding reported values are $p=0.0$ for exact class-preserving shape recurrence and $p=0.0035$ for the lower tail. The finite data therefore support a code-layer geometric enrichment claim under the sampled nulls, while leaving all mechanism-level interpretations outside the row.

\paragraph{Predictive checks.}
The leave-one-code-out row evaluated $23$ tables and $63$ targets. Its baseline hit rate was $0.19821739130434785$, while the hit rate above baseline was $0.9841269841269841$. The hit rate inside $M-T$ was $0.9365079365079365$, and the rate inside $M-T$ plus the boundary was again $0.9841269841269841$. By family, the reported above-baseline hit rates were $1.0$ for bacterial-plastid, mitochondrial, nuclear-ciliate, and candidate-other families, and $0.8333333333333334$ for the alternative-yeast family. These rows show that the local code-layer geometry is reusable across the tested table variants, subject to the same boundary: the test predicts or recovers codon-label placements, not translation events or biological function.

\paragraph{Leave-one-codon structure.}
The leave-one-codon-out row reports $0$ anchors, $5$ boundary cases, $8$ high-coverage closure cases, $8$ predictable cases, and recovery rate $1.0$. The module classification is complete: $R$ has size $13$, all $13$ codons are assigned, and there are no extra module codons, no missing members, and no unassigned codons. The six named modules are
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{agr}&=\{\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG}\},\\
\mathrm{ar\_lys}&=\{\mathrm{AAA}\},\\
\mathrm{cun}&=\{\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU}\},\\
\mathrm{ile\_met}&=\{\mathrm{AUA}\},\\
\mathrm{ssn}&=\{\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UUA}\},\\
\mathrm{stop\_trp}&=\{\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UGA}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
Each module has either a predictable member or a boundary member in the supplied classification. This is the strongest local reading of the row: the thirteen codons form a complete finite module packet whose geometry, spectrum, and leave-one-out behavior are recorded at the code-label layer. The row remains silent about folded conformation, physical admissibility, realized translation, function, global necessity, and universal biological mechanism unless a separate measurement contact is supplied for one of those layers.
