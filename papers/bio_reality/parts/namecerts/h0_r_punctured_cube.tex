\subsection{The punctured-cube row of the standard-code window}
\label{sec:bioreality-h0-r-punctured-cube}

\paragraph{Finite carrier.}
This record concerns the standard-code codon table only at the code-read layer.  The carrier is the window-six codon cube $Q_6$, where each codon is displayed by six binary coordinates and the table contact assigns each codon an amino-acid or stop label.  The row named $h0.R.punctured.cube$ isolates a thirteen-codon region $R$ whose observed members are
$$
\begin{aligned}
R=\{&AAA,AGA,AGG,AUA,CUA,CUC,CUG,CUU,\\
&UAA,UAG,UCA,UGA,UUA\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The same finite run records $|R|=13$ and the cell-complex count
$$
\begin{aligned}
(f_0,f_1,f_2)=(13,16,4).
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the row is not merely a list of codons: it is a local cubical pattern with thirteen occupied vertices, sixteen occupied edges, and four occupied square faces inside the six-coordinate codon display.  The word ``punctured'' is used in this local, combinatorial sense: the observed region behaves as a cube-like tile whose boundary data include a missing or distinguished corner pattern rather than as a biological law about all coding systems.

\paragraph{Label contact.}
The reality contact used here is a curated standard genetic-code table.  It supplies codon-to-label assignments and nothing stronger.  In this chapter, a label is an amino-acid or stop symbol read from that table; it is not a molecular realization of translation, not a fold, not an energetic admissibility check, and not a functional assay.  The table is therefore sufficient for asking whether the thirteen-codon region aligns with a local label split, but it is insufficient for promoting that split to mechanism, organismal universality, protein structure, or biological function.

\paragraph{Shape rarity checks.}
Two null comparisons accompany the row.  In the shape-significance run with seed $42$, $N=2000$ samples were evaluated after $57377$ distinct-sample attempts and $55377$ duplicate retries.  Exactly one sampled object matched the observed shape, giving $p_{\mathrm{exact}}=0.0005$.  Twenty-five samples reached Jaccard similarity at least $0.9$, giving $p_{J\geq 0.9}=0.0125$.  A separate amino-acid-class-preserving null model, also with $N=2000$ and seed $42$, reported observed $M$-size $20$, no exact class-preserving shape matches, and seven samples in the lower tail at or below the observed $M$-size.  Its recorded probabilities are
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{\mathrm{class\ exact}}&=0.0,\\
p_{\mathrm{class\ lower\ tail}}&=0.0035.
\end{aligned}
$$
These finite counts support the statement that the observed local tile is not a routine output of the tested randomized controls.  They do not identify a causal process.

\paragraph{Local boundary.}
The admissible conclusion is narrow.  Window-six coordinates expose a thirteen-codon punctured-cube tile in the curated standard-code table; the tile has $16$ edges and $4$ faces, passes the listed spectral consistency check, is rare under the two reported null controls, and supports strong leave-out recovery at the code-read layer.  The inadmissible conclusions are equally important.  No translation process, folded structure, physical admissibility, biological function, universal mechanism, or necessity claim follows from this row.  Any such conclusion would require a separate contact that directly observes the corresponding layer.  The present chapter records a finite codon-label geometry and its tested boundary, not a general theory of molecular realization.
