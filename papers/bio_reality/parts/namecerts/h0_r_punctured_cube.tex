\subsection{The punctured cube row in the standard-code codon display}\label{sec:bioreality-h0-r-punctured-cube}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite codon carrier.}
The record studied here is the six-coordinate codon display obtained by reading each codon as a finite point in the codon cube.  The biological contact is deliberately narrow: a curated standard-code table supplies codon-to-label assignments, so the admissible readout is a code-layer label split and not a claim about how translation, folding, chemistry, function, or organism-level mechanism is realized.  Within that finite display, the distinguished row $R$ is exactly the following set of thirteen codons:
$$
\begin{aligned}
R=\{&AAA,AGA,AGG,AUA,CUA,CUC,CUG,CUU,\\
&UAA,UAG,UCA,UGA,UUA\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The independent cardinality check records $|R|=13$ and gives the same list of codons: $AAA$, $AGA$, $AGG$, $AUA$, $CUA$, $CUC$, $CUG$, $CUU$, $UAA$, $UAG$, $UCA$, $UGA$, and $UUA$.  The local cell count of this row is not just a set-size observation.  Its finite cubical summary has $f$-vector $(13,16,4)$, meaning that the selected codon row is represented by thirteen vertices, sixteen one-dimensional adjacencies, and four square faces in the local cube geometry.  This is the sense in which the row is a punctured cube: it is a finite local tile with visible boundary structure, not a completed law of biology.

\paragraph{Spectral and sampling checks.}
The same row is accompanied by a spectral summary in which the row-side value is $\lambda_R=0.4758352843197002$, the comparison-side value is $\lambda_M=0.6752479308830213$, and the radial parameter is $\mu=3.051487585298128$.  The cubic residual and the radial-polynomial residual are both recorded as $-6.224354365258478\cdot 10^{-12}$, which is effectively zero at the reported numerical tolerance.  Thus the finite row is not merely a hand-selected list; it also survives a numerical consistency check against the polynomial relation used by the packet.

The shape-significance run used $N=2000$ samples with seed $42$.  It required $57377$ distinct-sample attempts and encountered $55377$ duplicate retries.  Among those samples, there was $1$ exact shape match, giving $p_{\mathrm{exact}}=0.0005$, and $25$ samples reached Jaccard similarity at least $0.9$, giving $p_{J\ge 0.9}=0.0125$.  The amino-acid-class preserving null comparison also used $N=2000$ and seed $42$.  In that run the observed comparison size was $|M|=20$, the exact-match count was $0$, the lower-tail count at or below the observed $M$ size was $7$, the exact-shape probability was $0.0$, and the lower-tail probability was $0.0035$.  These numbers support a finite local irregularity claim at the code-label layer, while leaving the biological interpretation bounded by the table contact.

\paragraph{Module accounting.}
The thirteen codons in $R$ are completely assigned by the module classification check.  The six modules are $ar\_lys=\{AAA\}$, $agr=\{AGA,AGG\}$, $ile\_met=\{AUA\}$, $cun=\{CUA,CUC,CUG,CUU\}$, $stop\_trp=\{UAA,UAG,UGA\}$, and $ssn=\{UCA,UUA\}$.  The classification reports $R\_size=13$, $assigned\_count=13$, no extra module codons, no codons missing from the modules, and no unassigned codons.  In particular, the row is not being described by an informal visual impression; each codon in the row has a named local module and the module inventory exhausts the row.

The leave-one-codon-out check gives a separate recovery diagnostic.  It records $anchor\_count=0$, $boundary\_count=5$, $closure\_coverage\_high=8$, $predictable\_count=8$, and $recovery\_rate=1.0$, with the reported threshold comparison also equal to $1.0$.  This means that, inside the finite coordinate-and-label setup, withholding a codon does not destroy the local recovery signal.  The result is still a code-read result: the recovery is over codon labels and local boundary status, not over ribosomal operation or protein realization.

\paragraph{Code-table readout across related rows.}
The leave-one-code-out run evaluated $23$ code tables and $63$ total targets.  The baseline hit rate was $0.19821739130434785$, while the hit rate above baseline was $0.9841269841269841$.  The hit rate inside $M-T$ was $0.9365079365079365$, and adding the boundary condition gave $0.9841269841269841$.  By family, the check counted $5$ families: alternative yeast had hit rate $0.8333333333333334$, and bacterial plastid, candidate other, mitochondrial, and nuclear ciliate each had hit rate $1.0$ above baseline.  These row counts show that the packet is not confined to a single isolated lookup event, but the permitted conclusion remains local: the curated code table supports codon-label readback and boundary-sensitive comparison.

\paragraph{Interpretive boundary.}
The contact used here is a standard genetic-code table.  It observes that codons are assigned to amino-acid or stop labels.  It does not observe molecular translation as a realized process, folded structure, physical admissibility, biological function, a universal mechanism, or any organism-independent necessity.  Consequently the finite row claim is the following: the thirteen-codon set $R$ forms a punctured-cube local tile with $f$-vector $(13,16,4)$, supported by spectral, sampling, leave-one-codon, leave-one-code, and module-classification checks at the code-read layer.  Any assertion about translation, folding, chemistry, function, mechanism, or universality would require a separate empirical contact directed at that layer.
