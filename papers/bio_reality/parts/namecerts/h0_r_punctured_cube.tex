\subsection{Punctured-cube boundary in the standard-code codon table}\label{sec:bioreality-h0-r-punctured-cube}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite object.}
The record concerns the standard-code codon table after codons are displayed as six binary coordinates inside the codon cube $Q_6$.  The biological contact is deliberately narrow: the table supplies codon-to-label readback for amino-acid and stop labels, and the geometric computation asks whether one finite subset has a reproducible local shape.  The distinguished row set is
$$
\begin{aligned}
R=\{&AAA, AGA, AGG, AUA, CUA, CUC, CUG, CUU,\\
&UAA, UAG, UCA, UGA, UUA\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The verified cardinality calculation gives $|R|=13$.  The same run identifies $R$ with a punctured cube having face data
$$
\begin{aligned}
(f_0,f_1,f_2)=(13,16,4).
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the finite claim is not that the genetic code is globally generated by this cube.  It is the weaker and testable statement that these thirteen standard-code codons occupy a local boundary tile with thirteen vertices, sixteen one-dimensional adjacencies, and four square faces in the chosen six-bit presentation.

\paragraph{Spectral and sampling evidence.}
The associated spectrum separates the tile from a merely arbitrary subset in the tested coordinate model.  The recorded values are $\lambda_M=0.6752479308830213$, $\lambda_R=0.4758352843197002$, and $\mu=3.051487585298128$.  The radial cubic check returns $-6.224354365258478\cdot 10^{-12}$, within numerical tolerance for the stated spectral relation.  A shape-randomization run with seed $42$ used $N=2000$ samples; it found $1$ exact shape match and $25$ matches with Jaccard score at least $0.9$.  The resulting probabilities were
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{\mathrm{exact}}&=0.0005,\\
p_{\mathrm{Jaccard}\geq 0.9}&=0.0125.
\end{aligned}
$$
The sampling process required $57377$ distinct-sample attempts and saw $55377$ duplicate retries, so the estimate should be read as a finite search statistic over a constrained combinatorial space rather than as an asymptotic theorem about biology.

\paragraph{Amino-acid-class preserving null.}
A second null model preserves amino-acid class information.  In $N=2000$ trials with seed $42$, the observed comparison size was $20$, the exact shape count was $0$, and only $7$ samples lay in the lower tail at or below the observed $M$ size.  The recorded probabilities are
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{\mathrm{class\ exact}}&=0.0,\\
p_{\mathrm{class\ lower\ tail}}&=0.0035.
\end{aligned}
$$
This supports the local claim that the punctured-cube row is not easily reproduced by the tested class-preserving randomizations.  It does not turn the coordinate pattern into a translation mechanism, a folding statement, a physical admissibility result, a functional claim, or a universal law.

\paragraph{Leave-out probes.}
The leave-one-code-out experiment evaluated $23$ code tables and $63$ total targets.  Its baseline hit rate was $0.19821739130434785$, while the recorded hit rate above baseline was $0.9841269841269841$.  The hit rate inside $M-T$ was $0.9365079365079365$, and the rate inside $M-T$ plus boundary was $0.9841269841269841$.  The family-level check counted $5$ families and reported hit-rate-above-baseline values $0.8333333333333334$ for alternative yeast, $1.0$ for bacterial/plastid, $1.0$ for candidate other, $1.0$ for mitochondrial, and $1.0$ for nuclear ciliate.  A separate leave-one-codon-out run recorded $0$ anchors, $5$ boundary cases, $8$ high-coverage closure cases, $8$ predictable cases, and recovery rate $1.0$.

\paragraph{Module accounting.}
The thirteen codons are completely assigned to six named modules.  The module inventory is
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{agr}&=\{AGA,AGG\},\\
\mathrm{ar\_lys}&=\{AAA\},\\
\mathrm{cun}&=\{CUA,CUC,CUG,CUU\},\\
\mathrm{ile\_met}&=\{AUA\},\\
\mathrm{ssn}&=\{UCA,UUA\},\\
\mathrm{stop\_trp}&=\{UAA,UAG,UGA\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The classification check reports $R$ size $13$, assigned count $13$, no extra module codons, no codons missing from the modules, and no unassigned codons.  This is the strongest finite-row reading of the packet: the local boundary tile is not only a vertex set, but also a fully accounted collection of codon-label modules in the standard-code readback.

\paragraph{Boundary of interpretation.}
The only direct contact used here is the curated standard genetic-code table.  That contact observes code labels, not ribosomal realization, folded protein structure, physical chemistry, organism-level function, or a necessary law across all biological codes.  The punctured-cube computation may therefore be used to describe a local codon-label pattern and to guide further probes, but any claim about translation, structure, physical admissibility, function, mechanism, or universality requires a separate observational contact designed for that layer.
