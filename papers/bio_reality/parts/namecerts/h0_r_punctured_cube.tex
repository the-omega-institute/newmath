\subsection{The punctured codon cube as a code-layer contact}
\label{sec:bioreality-punctured-codon-cube}

\paragraph{Finite coordinate record.}
The row under consideration is the claim $h0.R.punctured.cube$.  The carrier is the six-coordinate codon cube $Q_6$, read through the standard-code assignment of codons to amino-acid or stop labels.  In this row the distinguished region $R$ is not introduced as a biochemical mechanism.  It is the following finite subset of codon labels:
$$
\begin{aligned}
R = \{&\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\\
&\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UUA}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The direct cardinality check gives $|R|=13$, and the independently recorded expected-codon list is exactly the same list.  The local tile computation records the face data
$$
\begin{aligned}
(f_0,f_1,f_2)=(13,16,4),
\end{aligned}
$$
so the object tested here is a thirteen-vertex, sixteen-edge, four-face local subcomplex of the window-six cube.  The phrase ``punctured cube'' is therefore used only for this finite combinatorial footprint: a local cube-shaped region with one distinguished missing corner pattern, not a claim about molecular necessity.

\paragraph{Spectral and shape checks.}
The same finite row carries a spectral summary.  The recorded values are
$$
\begin{aligned}
\lambda_M &= 0.6752479308830213,\\
\lambda_R &= 0.4758352843197002,\\
\mu &= 3.051487585298128.
\end{aligned}
$$
The radial polynomial residual is $-6.224354365258478\times 10^{-12}$, so the numerical check is at roundoff scale for the row's cubic constraint.  This does not make the codon table derivable from geometry; it says that the finite codon subset and its surrounding closure data satisfy the recorded spectral test to the displayed tolerance.

The shape-significance run used $N=2000$ samples with seed $42$.  It required $57377$ distinct-sample attempts and recorded $55377$ duplicate retries.  Among those samples there was $1$ exact shape match, giving $p_{\mathrm{exact}}=0.0005$.  There were $25$ samples with Jaccard score at least $0.9$, giving $p_{\mathrm{Jaccard}\ge 0.9}=0.0125$.  Thus the local tile is not a common outcome under that finite sampling protocol.  A stricter amino-acid-class-preserving null run, again with $N=2000$ and seed $42$, found $0$ exact matches, an observed $M$ size of $20$, and $7$ samples in the lower tail at or below that observed size.  Its recorded probabilities are $p=0.0$ for exact shape under the sampled class-preserving null and $p=0.0035$ for the lower-tail event.

\paragraph{Readback against the curated code table.}
The contact used by this chapter is a curated standard-code table.  That contact supplies codon-to-label assignments at the code-reading layer.  It does not supply translation dynamics, folded structures, energetic admissibility, biological function, a universal mechanism, or a law governing all organisms.  Consequently the row can test whether the thirteen displayed codons form the recorded local boundary pattern inside the label table, but it cannot promote the pattern beyond that layer.

The leave-one-code-out row gives a separate finite stability check across $23$ evaluated tables and $63$ total targets.  The baseline hit rate is $0.19821739130434785$.  The hit rate inside $M\setminus T$ is $0.9365079365079365$, and the hit rate inside $M\setminus T$ plus the boundary is $0.9841269841269841$.  The recorded hit rate above baseline is also $0.9841269841269841$.  By family, the row records $5$ families: alternative yeast has hit rate $0.8333333333333334$, while bacterial/plastid, candidate other, mitochondrial, and nuclear ciliate each have hit rate $1.0$ above the corresponding baseline comparison.  These values are evidence for a robust local code-layer recovery pattern, not evidence for protein realization.

\paragraph{Local conclusion and scope.}
The evidence supports a precise statement: the standard-code label table contains a thirteen-codon region $R$ whose local cubical footprint has face vector $(13,16,4)$, whose recorded spectral quantities satisfy the row's numerical constraint, and whose removal tests show high finite recovery rates under the supplied protocols.  The strongest allowed interpretation is therefore code-layer geometry with curated label readback.  No conclusion about actual translation, folding, structural order, physical admissibility, biological function, cross-organism universality, or a general biological mechanism follows from this row unless a distinct contact measuring that higher layer is added.
