\subsection{Translation-Conditioned RNA Half-Life and the RBP-Bridge Boundary}
\label{sec:bior-reality-q6-rna-half-life-rbp-bridge}

\paragraph{Finite carrier.}
This record concerns a finite matched RNA panel whose rows must carry four pieces of information before a positive cross-layer statement is allowed: a synonymous residual coordinate label after amino-acid quotienting, a same-row translation readout, an RNA half-life readout, and a prespecified decay-factor RBP measurement at the editable RNA window.  The available poly(A)-tail-length and CPE-grammar audit does not yet supply that full joined panel.  Its finite readout is nevertheless informative as a boundary case: the run for \texttt{h3.cross\_layer\_relation.polya\_tail\_length.cpe\_grammar\_polya\_taillength\_subtelny2014} reports $8854$ human rows and $3990$ yeast rows, with \texttt{cross\_species\_concordant = false} and runtime $31.5212$ seconds.  Thus the present finite evidence is not a conserved cross-species grammar signal that can be lifted into a translation-conditioned RNA-decay mechanism.

\paragraph{Statistic recorded by the row audit.}
The observed finite summary is a bounded transcript-layer surface.  Its relevant scalar form is
$$
\begin{aligned}
N_{\mathrm{human}} &= 8854,\\
N_{\mathrm{yeast}} &= 3990,\\
C_{\mathrm{cross\ species}} &= 0.
\end{aligned}
$$
Here $C_{\mathrm{cross\ species}}=0$ denotes the recorded non-concordance flag, not a proof that no organism-specific effect exists.  It only says that this particular finite comparison does not justify a conserved grammar-level promotion.  The same audit collection contains other finite contact surfaces, but they remain unjoined to this subject.  For example, the RBP-binding contact run records $900$ total peak rows and $3$ cross-RBP concordant cases, while the mTOR translation readout run records $6494$ rows and runtime $9.469$ seconds.  These numbers show that RBP and translation surfaces exist as separable finite assays in the surrounding evidence ledger, but they do not create the same-window, same-transcript perturbation-and-rescue matrix required here.

\paragraph{Positive condition.}
A positive RNA half-life RBP bridge would require a matched panel in which high-coordinate and low-coordinate synonymous residual labels are compared after amino-acid sequence, transcription rate, local RNA structure, motif density, GC and dinucleotide composition, codon-pair context, splice or isoform state, transcript abundance, batch, and the same-row translation readout have been conditioned.  In addition, the half-life contrast must track an experimentally decoupled local occupancy change for a prespecified decay-factor RBP.  The effect must collapse when that target RBP is lost, return under wild-type rescue, and fail to return under binding-defective or non-target rescue.  In compact form, the admissible positive pattern is
$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta H(Q \mid Z,T) &\ne 0,\\
\Delta O_{\mathrm{RBP}}(Q \mid Z,T) &\ne 0,\\
\Delta H_{\mathrm{target\ loss}}(Q \mid Z,T) &\approx 0,\\
\Delta H_{\mathrm{wild\ type\ rescue}}(Q \mid Z,T) &\approx \Delta H(Q \mid Z,T),\\
\Delta H_{\mathrm{binding\ defective}}(Q \mid Z,T) &\approx 0.
\end{aligned}
$$
The covariate block $Z$ includes transcription and abundance controls, sequence-composition controls, local structure controls, isoform controls, and batch controls; $T$ denotes the mandatory same-row translation readout.  Without the occupancy, loss, and rescue rows, a half-life contrast remains an RNA-stability association rather than an RBP-mediated bridge.

\paragraph{Current verdict.}
The present finite facts support a negative or bounded reading.  The poly(A)-tail-length audit has substantial row counts, but its recorded cross-species concordance flag is false.  The RBP-binding and mTOR translation summaries supply independent finite contact examples, with $900$ peak rows and $6494$ translation rows respectively, yet they are not joined to the same transcript windows as measured RNA half-life, local decay-factor RBP occupancy, target-RBP loss, and rescue controls.  Therefore the chapter records a finite cross-layer candidate whose mechanism gate remains closed.  The correct scientific statement is that a $B^{*}_{Q6}$ coordinate-associated RNA half-life pattern may be investigated under translation conditioning, but no RNA-decay RBP mechanism has been established by the currently cited finite rows.

\paragraph{Local non-promotion rule.}
No translation realization follows from RNA half-life alone.  No RBP-decay mechanism follows from a half-life difference unless measured local decay-factor RBP occupancy changes in the required direction and the target-loss and rescue experiments discriminate wild-type binding from binding-defective or non-target controls.  No protein output, folding state, physical admissibility, biological function, phenotype, adaptation, causation, or universal law follows from the coordinate labels, codon-window geometry, poly(A)-tail rows, transcript abundance controls, translation-contact names, or RBP-contact names unless a separate layer-matched contact supplies that readout and the declared finite statistic is computed on joined rows.
