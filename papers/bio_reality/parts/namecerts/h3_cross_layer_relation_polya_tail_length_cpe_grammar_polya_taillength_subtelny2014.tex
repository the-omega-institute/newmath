\subsection{Translation-conditioned RNA half-life and an RBP occupancy boundary at synonymous Q6 windows}
\label{sec:bioreality-q6-rna-half-life-rbp-boundary}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite row object.}
The record considered here is a finite matched transcript or coding-sequence panel in which each row may carry a $B^*_{Q6}$ synonymous residual-coordinate label, a same-row translation readout, an RNA half-life readout, matched abundance and transcription controls, and, if the stronger mechanistic claim is attempted, a measured local occupancy row for a prespecified decay-factor RNA-binding protein. The coordinate label is used only after quotienting away amino-acid identity. Thus the comparison is not between different proteins, but between synonymous residual windows that are high or low in the $Q6$ coordinate while preserving the amino-acid sequence boundary. The target claim is therefore not that a codon coordinate is itself a decay mechanism. It is the narrower question of whether a coordinate-associated RNA half-life residual can be read as a translation-conditioned RBP-mediated RNA stability relation once local RBP occupancy, target-RBP loss, rescue, and control rescue rows are all present in the same finite design.

\paragraph{Observed finite contacts.}
The poly(A)-tail and cytoplasmic-polyadenylation-entry fact currently attached to this subject is finite but negative for cross-species concordance: the parsed run reports $8854$ human rows, $3990$ yeast rows, and $\mathrm{cross\_species\_concordant}=\mathrm{false}$ for run $9581dc27a332$. This makes the record useful as a bounded RNA-level readout surface, not as a universal sequence rule. A separate RBP-binding contact reports $900$ total peak rows and $3$ cross-RBP-concordant cases in run $b225890251c2$; this supplies scale for finite binding evidence, but it is not by itself the required same-window decay-factor occupancy and perturbation panel for the present RNA half-life claim. A further translation contact reports $6494$ rows in run $0c2bbfd3e2d9$ from a translation-readout source. That row count supports the existence of finite translation-readout surfaces in the surrounding BioReality memory, but the present chapter requires a same-row join before any translation-conditioned RNA half-life statement is allowed.

\paragraph{Admissible statistic.}
Let each eligible row be indexed by $i$. Write $q_i \in \{0,1\}$ for the high-versus-low residual-coordinate label after amino-acid quotienting, $T_i$ for the same-row translation readout, $H_i$ for the RNA half-life readout, $A_i$ for transcript abundance or matched mRNA control, and $Z_i$ for the declared control block containing transcription rate, local RNA structure, motif density, GC and dinucleotide composition, codon-pair context, splice or isoform state, batch, length, and amino-acid composition. If an RBP mechanism is claimed, the row must also include local decay-factor occupancy $O_i$, target-RBP loss $L_i$, wild-type rescue $W_i$, and a binding-defective or non-target rescue control $C_i$. A positive finite statistic would have to show a coordinate-associated half-life residual only after conditioning on the same-row translation boundary, together with the required occupancy and perturbation pattern:
$$
\begin{aligned}
H_i &= \alpha + \beta q_i + \gamma T_i + \delta A_i + \eta Z_i + \varepsilon_i,\\
\beta &\neq 0 \quad \text{only in the eligible joined panel},\\
O_i &\text{ changes with the editable local window independently of abundance-only explanations},\\
\beta_{L} &\approx 0 \quad \text{under target-RBP loss},\\
\beta_{W} &\approx \beta \quad \text{under wild-type rescue},\\
\beta_{C} &\approx 0 \quad \text{under binding-defective or non-target rescue.}
\end{aligned}
$$
These equations specify the finite readback required for the stronger RBP interpretation. They do not follow from the observed poly(A)-tail rows alone, nor from translation rows alone, nor from an RBP-binding catalogue unless the binding rows are the same-organism, same-window, decay-factor occupancy rows needed by the statistic.

\paragraph{Null model.}
The local null is exchangeability of high- and low-coordinate labels with respect to RNA half-life once amino-acid sequence, transcription, abundance, isoform state, batch, local RNA structure, motif density, GC and dinucleotide composition, codon-pair context, and the same-row translation readout have been controlled. In compact form, the null says
$$
\begin{aligned}
q_i \perp H_i \mid T_i,A_i,Z_i.
\end{aligned}
$$
For the RBP-specific version, failure of any one of the occupancy, loss, wild-type rescue, or binding-defective/non-target rescue checks returns the result to a bounded RNA-stability association. In particular, a half-life difference without measured local decay-factor RBP occupancy is not an RBP-mediated result; an occupancy association without perturbation and rescue is not a mechanism; and a rescue signal that is also restored by binding-defective or non-target controls is not specific to the proposed RBP bridge.

\paragraph{Interpretation of the current finite evidence.}
The current finite evidence is sufficient to state a disciplined boundary, not a positive mechanistic crossing. The poly(A)-tail fact has substantial row counts, with $8854$ human rows and $3990$ yeast rows, but its cross-species concordance flag is false. That outcome blocks any attempt to promote the observed RNA-level grammar into a general biological law. The RBP-binding fact has $900$ peak rows and only $3$ cross-RBP-concordant cases, which is compatible with finite binding-contact bookkeeping but does not identify the required decay-factor occupancy panel for this subject. The translation-readout fact has $6494$ rows, but those rows must be joined to the RNA half-life and RBP perturbation rows before the statistic above exists. Therefore the present BioReality chapter records a constrained research target: the coordinate may be tested against RNA half-life only through a same-row translation-conditioned design, and the RBP reading is allowed only if the local occupancy and target-RBP perturbation/rescue pattern is observed.

\paragraph{Cannot-claim boundary.}
Nothing in this finite record licenses translation realization from RNA half-life alone. Nothing here licenses protein output, protein folding, structural order, physical admissibility, cellular function, phenotype, adaptation, causation, or a universal biological law. A codon-window coordinate is not an external biological measurement; a translation contact name is not an RNA decay assay; an RNA half-life contact is not an RBP occupancy experiment; and a matched mRNA control is not a mechanism. Each stronger layer requires its own row-matched contact and its own declared finite statistic. Until the same-organism, same-window panel contains $B^*_{Q6}$ labels, translation readout, RNA half-life, measured decay-factor RBP occupancy, target-RBP loss, wild-type rescue, and binding-defective or non-target controls, the scientifically correct status is a bounded RNA-level association target with an explicit RBP-mechanism gap.
