\subsection{Human prime-editor synonymous fitness panel}
\label{sec:bioreality-human-prime-editor-synonymous-fitness-panel}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite surface.}
The human prime-editor panel supplies a finite perturbation surface for asking whether a B$^*_{Q6}$ synonymous-window coordinate can be read as a bounded perturbation predictor rather than as a free biological explanation. The rows are synonymous coding edits, the grouping units are genes, and the measured endpoint is a cell-line fitness readout from the prime-editor screen. The finite run used $1822$ genes, $9000$ synonymous rows in the analysis surface, and a compact synonymous reservoir of $24000$ rows. The analysis was capped at $3000$ rows per cell line and used $B=200$ resampling or null repetitions, with a recorded runtime of $295.329$ seconds. Held-out folds were gene-block folds; direct gene dummy effects were not inserted into the predictive design because the test genes were unseen in the training folds. Thus the row relation being tested is not a within-gene memorization device: it is a finite held-out relation between synonymous-window coordinates and fitness readout after gene-level blocking.

\paragraph{Admissible readback.}
The object of this chapter is the finite relation
$$
\begin{aligned}
(q_i, e_r, y_r, g_r) \in Q_{6}^{*} \times E \times Y \times G,
\end{aligned}
$$
where $q_i$ is a B$^*_{Q6}$ synonymous-window residual coordinate, $e_r$ is a synonymous prime-editor edit row, $y_r$ is the measured fitness response for that row, and $g_r$ is the gene block used for held-out evaluation. The row count fixes the scale of the claim: $n_{\mathrm{gene}}=1822$, $n_{\mathrm{syn}}=9000$, $n_{\mathrm{compact}}=24000$, and $B=200$. The finite table can support only the following kind of statement: a synonymous coordinate is allowed to enter a perturbation-fitness model if it improves held-out prediction across unseen genes under the declared blocking design. It does not, by itself, identify a translation-rate change, a protein-abundance change, a folded-state change, a molecular-function change, or a mechanism.

\paragraph{Relation to the translation gate.}
The same B$^*_{Q6}$ coordinate family has a separate translation-mediated protein-abundance surface with $9$ coordinates: $K\_AAA$, $Arg\_AGR$, $Ile\_AUA$, $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$, $Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG$, $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$, $Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG$, $Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY$, and $f3\_stress$. In that separate multi-organism protein-abundance analysis, the mediated fractions included $0.27206289160119157$ for human, with total $R^2=0.04454565118019448$, direct conditioned $R^2=0.03242643251185274$, and indirect difference $0.012119218668341744$. The human proteomics join in that surface used $19053$ proteins after joining. Those numbers are useful only as boundary calibration: they show that the coordinate family can be placed on a finite translation--protein mediation matrix elsewhere, but they do not convert this prime-editor fitness table into a translation or protein-realization experiment. The human prime-editor panel has its own finite endpoint, its own gene-block split, and its own perturbation rows.

\paragraph{Synonymous perturbation context.}
The perturbation ledger is heterogeneous across organisms and endpoints. A yeast synonymous-mutation panel used $20$ genes and $1783$ synonymous mutants, with $21$ CDS map hits and a real codon-stability-correlation check that did not absorb the B$^*_{Q6}$ proxy signal. An Escherichia coli mRFP protein-output panel used $1459$ variants and reported $\Delta R^2=0.05339906007191191$, $\Delta DL=19.089327473282296$ bits, and matched and within-null probabilities both equal to $0.004975124378109453$. A 5-prime boundary audit on the same variant scale reported $n=1459$, $R^2=0.06748192482378434$ for the first step, $R^2=0.6060702157093043$ after the second step, and $R^2=0.6049438075310105$ after the third step, with downstream $\Delta R^2=-0.001126409133973234$ and block probability $0.1691542288557214$. A distributed E. coli recoding panel recorded $47$ ampicillin-resistance rows, $53$ green-to-red marker rows, $19$ multi-row $D$ strata, and an ampicillin independent probability of $1.0$. These neighboring finite surfaces justify treating the human prime-editor screen as one perturbation endpoint in a family of bounded synonymous-window tests, not as a universal law about codons.

\paragraph{Decision rule.}
The declared decision boundary is local. A positive human fitness readout would mean that at least one B$^*_{Q6}$ synonymous-window coordinate improves held-out prediction for prime-editor fitness across gene-block folds. A null or nonpositive readout would keep the coordinate at synonymous perturbation or codon-window residual status for this endpoint. The row counts and blocking design prevent a stronger interpretation. In particular, the absence of direct gene fixed effects in the held-out design is a discipline of evaluation, not a biological assertion about genes. The compact reservoir of $24000$ synonymous rows is a sampling and representation support, not an independent proof of molecular execution.

\paragraph{Cannot-claim boundary.}
No translation, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, mechanism, phenotype, selection law, or universality follows from this panel unless a separate layer-matched measurement supplies that contact. A direct association from $q_i$ to prime-editor fitness cannot be read as $q_i \to T_j \to P_k$ mediation unless the same coordinate first survives a controlled translation readout and then a matched downstream protein or state readout under the relevant controls. Modeled tAI or stAI weights remain bounded codon-adaptation readbacks; ribosome profiling, matched mRNA abundance, matched protein abundance, localization, stability, structure, and function each require their own finite rows. The safe readback of this chapter is therefore narrow: the human prime-editor screen is a gene-blocked synonymous perturbation fitness surface with $1822$ genes and $9000$ analysis rows, suitable for testing whether B$^*_{Q6}$ windows carry held-out perturbation signal, and unsuitable for promotion to translation or higher biological layers without additional evidence.
