\subsection{Human Prime-Editor Synonymous Fitness Window}
\label{sec:human-prime-editor-synonymous-fitness-window}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite surface.}
This record isolates a human prime-editor synonymous perturbation surface for the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ window coordinates.  The measured object is not a codon-usage table alone and not a protein-abundance mediation matrix.  It is a finite perturbation panel in which synonymous edits are grouped into held-out gene blocks and tested as candidate carriers of a window-level residual signal.  The verified run used $1{,}822$ genes, $9{,}000$ synonymous observations in the analysis surface, and a compact synonymous encoding with $24{,}000$ rows.  The run fixed $B=200$ resampling units and capped each cell-line analysis surface at $3{,}000$ rows.  The registered runtime was $295.329$ seconds under experiment run \texttt{b1a1a8c7baae}.

\paragraph{Readout discipline.}
The target layer is a perturbation-fitness readout, not translation realization.  A synonymous edit can be used here only as an externally observed perturbation row after the finite design has separated it from amino-acid replacement.  The held-out design uses gene-block folds; direct gene dummy fixed effects are deliberately absent from the predictive fit because test genes are unseen in the training folds.  Thus the test asks whether the residual window coordinates generalize across genes, not whether the model can memorize gene identity.  In finite-row notation the admissible surface has the form
$$
\begin{aligned}
  \mathcal{H}_{\mathrm{PE}}
    &= \{(g_a,e_b,y_b,z_b): g_a \in G,\ e_b \in E_{\mathrm{syn}},\ y_b \in Y_{\mathrm{fitness}},\ z_b \in Z\},\\
  |G| &= 1{,}822,\\
  |E_{\mathrm{syn}}|_{\mathrm{analysis}} &= 9{,}000,\\
  |E_{\mathrm{syn}}|_{\mathrm{compact}} &= 24{,}000,\\
  B &= 200.
\end{aligned}
$$
Here $z_b$ denotes the local control information used by the perturbation design rather than a promoted biological coordinate.  The finite carrier is therefore a gene-blocked perturbation assay with synonymous rows, not a statement about folding, protein output, or organismal adaptation.

\paragraph{Relation to translation-mediated protein abundance.}
The wider $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ mediation audit gives a useful boundary for interpreting this prime-editor surface.  In the cross-organism protein-abundance decomposition, the translation-mediated run used a joint nine-dimensional $Q$ surface and reported $M^{QTP}$ fractions within the unit interval for all twelve organisms in that finite panel.  The human row in that separate protein-abundance audit had $19{,}053$ joined proteins, $c=0.04454565118019448$ for total $Q \to P$ association after controls, $c'=0.03242643251185274$ after conditioning on modeled translation readout, an indirect difference of $0.012119218668341744$, and $M^{QTP}=0.27206289160119157$.  Those numbers show that a human protein-abundance mediation surface exists in a different finite contact, but they do not transfer protein mediation into the prime-editor fitness panel.  The prime-editor chapter can borrow the same caution: a direct $Q$-like residual association with a downstream phenotype is not evidence of protein realization unless the same finite rows contain a surviving translation step.

\paragraph{Translation and structure boundary.}
The modeled translation-readout audit computed nine $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ coordinates: \texttt{Arg\_AGR}, \texttt{Ile\_AUA}, \texttt{K\_AAA}, \texttt{Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR}, \texttt{Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG}, \texttt{Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG}, \texttt{Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY}, \texttt{Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY}, and \texttt{f3\_stress}.  That audit used three organisms and found $81$ modeled tAI coupling entries above the threshold $0.01$, with maximum absolute entry $3{,}801{,}961.031607896$; it also explicitly recorded that three organisms are not sufficient for powered survival.  A separate structural-order audit identified \texttt{f3\_stress} as the dominant axis with sign-flip $p=0.03125$ and weighted held-out $R^2$ increment $0.0031744502294756994$.  These finite facts justify keeping the prime-editor window indexed by the same residual family, but they do not make the human prime-editor fitness readout a translation, structural-order, or physical-admissibility result.

\paragraph{Negative controls from nearby layers.}
Two adjacent audits sharpen the no-promotion rule.  The turnover residual audit used $3{,}290$ genes and reported $R^2_{L2}=0.055740851626933985$, $R^2_{L3}=0.050278872193227464$, $\Delta R^2=-0.005461979433706483$, and $\Delta\mathrm{DL}=-13.68819707902732$ bits, with $p_{\mathrm{matched\ bin}}=0.8855721393034826$ and $p_{\mathrm{recoded}}=1.0$.  The dynamic mass-balance audit used $120$ genes across $718$ intervals and reported $R^2_{L0}=0.9960628201518571$, $R^2_{L1}=0.996410628851051$, $R^2_{L2}=0.9712443377743347$, $R^2_{L3}=0.8953999568421264$, $\Delta R^2=-0.07584438093220836$, $\Delta\mathrm{DL}=-668.8046070381928$ bits, $p_{\mathrm{TEbin}}=0.6831683168316832$, and $p_{\mathrm{timeshuffle}}=0.9306930693069307$.  These neighboring finite tests show that downstream protein-state or dynamic closure can fail even when a residual coordinate family is well defined.  The prime-editor fitness window must therefore stand on its own perturbation rows.

\paragraph{Local claim.}
The finite claim supported here is modest.  A human prime-editor synonymous perturbation surface was executed with gene-block held-out folds, $1{,}822$ genes, $9{,}000$ analysis rows, $24{,}000$ compact synonymous rows, $B=200$, and a per-cell-line cap of $3{,}000$ rows.  The design is suitable for asking whether $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ window coordinates retain predictive contact with synonymous-edit fitness readouts across unseen genes.  The available finite record does not include a reported effect size, held-out $\Delta R^2$, likelihood gain, null quantile, or $p$-value for this exact human prime-editor run.  Consequently the chapter records a powered finite execution surface and a valid boundary for interpretation, rather than a positive mechanism claim.

\paragraph{Cannot-claim boundary.}
No translation, ribosome occupancy, protein abundance, folded structure, physical admissibility, molecular function, cellular mechanism, organismal phenotype, selection law, or universality follows from this chapter alone.  Those claims require their own matched contacts and statistics.  In particular, a synonymous prime-editor fitness association would not by itself prove a $Q_i \to T_j$ translation step, and a translation step would still not by itself prove folding, function, mechanism, or population-level law.  The admissible readback is restricted to the finite human synonymous perturbation design and to the row counts, blocking structure, and execution parameters stated above.
