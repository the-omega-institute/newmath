\subsection{Human prime-editor synonymous perturbation window}\label{sec:bior_human_prime_editor_synonymous_perturbation_window}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite perturbation surface.}
This record treats the human prime-editor synonymous-edit panel as a finite perturbation surface rather than as a direct mechanism claim.  The finite rows are the synonymous edits retained by the registered run, with held-out prediction organized by gene blocks.  The run used $n_{\mathrm{genes}}=1822$ genes, $n_{\mathrm{syn}}=9000$ analyzed synonymous edits, and a compact synonymous surface of $n_{\mathrm{syn,compact}}=24000$ rows.  The resampling count was $B=200$, the per-cell-line analysis cap was $3000$, and the registered computation completed in $295.329$ seconds.  The gene-control note is part of the mathematical object: held-out folds are gene-block folds, so direct gene dummy fixed effects are not included in the predictive design because the test genes are absent from the training folds.

\paragraph{Coordinates and admissible readback.}
The coordinate side is the same nine-dimensional $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual basis used by the translation and protein-abundance panels:
$$
\begin{aligned}
Q=\{&K_{AAA},\ Arg_{AGR},\ Ile_{AUA},\ Leu_{CUN\,vs\,UUR},\ Leu_{UUA\,vs\,UUG},\\
&Ser_{UCR\,vs\,AGY},\ Ser_{UCA\,vs\,UCG},\ Thr_{ACR\,vs\,ACY},\ f_{3\text{-}stress}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The human prime-editor panel is therefore read as a finite relation between synonymous-edit windows and these residual coordinates, with the outcome kept at perturbation-fitness scope.  A row-level association is not allowed to become a translation, protein-output, folding, physical-admissibility, functional, mechanistic, or universal statement unless a layer-matched measurement supplies that additional contact.  In particular, an edit-window fitness signal is not a ribosome-occupancy measurement, not a protein-abundance measurement, and not a direct statement about folded molecular state.

\paragraph{Rank and row sufficiency.}
The panel is large enough to support a bounded perturbation readout because it combines thousands of synonymous edits with gene-block held-out folds.  The relevant finite dimensions are not merely decorative: the $1822$ gene blocks prevent the readout from being reduced to memorized gene identity, while the $9000$ analyzed synonymous edits and $24000$ compact rows provide enough edit-level variation for a windowed residual test.  The absence of gene dummy effects is not a weakness of bookkeeping; it is the correct held-out design for unseen test genes.  The result can therefore be stated as a perturbation-window contact with $B=200$ resampling, not as a proof that the residual coordinates cause the edited phenotype.

\paragraph{Relation to translation mediation controls.}
The stronger cross-layer protein-abundance panel supplies the appropriate boundary for interpreting this perturbation result.  In that separate finite matrix, the same nine $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ coordinates were joined to modeled per-protein tAI and PAXdb protein abundance across $12$ organisms.  The human row of that matrix used $19053$ joined proteins, a modeled tRNA-copy total of $432$, zero geometric-mean fallback codons, and residual rank diagnostics with $\operatorname{rank}(X)=9$, $\operatorname{rank}(T)=1$, $\operatorname{rank}(XT)=10$, $\operatorname{rank}(Z)=23$, and residual degrees of freedom $19030$.  The human mediated fraction was
$$
\begin{aligned}
M^{QTP}_{\mathrm{human}}&=0.27206289160119157,\\
R^2_{QP}&=0.04454565118019448,\\
R^2_{QP\mid T}&=0.03242643251185274,\\
R^2_{\mathrm{indirect}}&=0.012119218668341744.
\end{aligned}
$$
Those numbers justify keeping a translation-conditioned protein-abundance route as a separate comparison surface, but they do not convert the prime-editor fitness panel into a protein-realization result.  The prime-editor record remains a perturbation-window test unless the same edited rows are joined to translation and protein readouts.

\paragraph{Modeled translation boundary.}
The modeled-tAI survival audit also fixes the no-promotion rule.  It computed the same nine residual coordinates in a $61$-sense-codon space using three organisms, with $81$ modeled coupling entries above the $0.01$ threshold and maximum absolute modeled coupling $3801961.031607896$.  The organism count was only $3$, and the recorded boundary says that $3$ organisms are insufficient for powered survival; powered survival requires at least $40$ matched species.  Thus modeled tAI can be used only as a bounded translation-readout contact, not as an observed ribosome or fitness mechanism.  The human prime-editor window cannot borrow modeled tAI coupling as if it were measured translation survival.

\paragraph{Axis-level caution.}
The structural-order axis audit identifies $f_{3\text{-}stress}$ as the only significant axis in its own finite surface, with weighted held-out $\Delta R^2=0.0031744502294756994$ and sign-flip $p=0.03125$.  Other axes did not pass that fold-level sign-flip gate, for example $K_{AAA}$ had $\Delta R^2=0.0005307046179682497$ with $p=0.2294921875$, and $Leu_{CUN\,vs\,UUR}$ had $\Delta R^2=-0.0003205780368125179$ with $p=0.779296875$.  This axis result is useful only as a guardrail for reading the prime-editor window: if a human edit-window association concentrates on an axis, the association must still be reported as a finite perturbation association until a translation, protein, structure, or function contact is measured on the same admissible surface.

\paragraph{Local claim.}
The chapter-level claim is therefore bounded and positive only in the finite-row sense.  The human prime-editor synonymous panel supplies a gene-block held-out perturbation surface with $1822$ genes, $9000$ analyzed synonymous edits, $24000$ compact rows, and $B=200$ resampling.  It is admissible to ask whether $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ window coordinates improve held-out perturbation-fitness prediction inside that finite surface.  It is not admissible to report translation, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, mechanism, selection, or universality from this record alone.  Any such promotion requires a separate matched contact and a new finite statistic joining the residual coordinate, the intermediate readout, the downstream readout, and the declared controls on the same row set.
