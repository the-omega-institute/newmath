\subsection{Human Prime-Editor Synonymous Perturbation Window}
\label{sec:bioreality-human-prime-editor-synonymous-perturbation-window}

\paragraph{Finite perturbation surface.}
This chapter records a finite perturbation audit for the claim identifier \texttt{h3.cross\_layer\_relation.synonymous\_perturbation.b\_star\_window\_human\_prime\_editor\_fitness\_powered}.  The observed table is not a free biological law: it is a bounded collection of synonymous prime-edit rows projected onto the same $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinate family used elsewhere in the translation-mediated protein-abundance program.  The exact run used $n_{\mathrm{genes}}=1822$ genes, $n_{\mathrm{syn}}=9000$ analyzed synonymous edits, a compact synonymous surface of $24000$ rows, and at most $3000$ analysis rows per cell line.  The resampling budget was $B=200$, and the registered execution time was $295.329$ seconds.  Held-out evaluation used gene-block folds.  Direct gene dummy terms were therefore not inserted into the predictive design, since test genes were unseen in the training folds.  This makes the row surface suitable for testing whether a codon-window residual has out-of-gene predictive contact, but it also prevents a gene-fixed-effect interpretation of the fitted score.

\paragraph{Coordinate discipline.}
The admissible coordinates are the nine residual axes already computed for the $B^*_{Q6}$ family: \texttt{K\_AAA}, \texttt{Arg\_AGR}, \texttt{Ile\_AUA}, \texttt{Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR}, \texttt{Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG}, \texttt{Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY}, \texttt{Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG}, \texttt{Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY}, and \texttt{f3\_stress}.  In the broader protein-abundance mediation audit, these same nine coordinates were joined to $12$ organism-scoped protein-abundance panels: for example, $19053$ human proteins, $19395$ mouse proteins, $5815$ yeast proteins, $3739$ \emph{E. coli} K12 MG1655 proteins, and $1656$ \emph{Sulfolobus solfataricus} proteins were used after identifier reconciliation.  Those rows showed that the matrix rank was sufficient for the declared nine-dimensional residual block in each organism, with $\operatorname{rank}(X)=9$, $\operatorname{rank}(T)=1$, and $\operatorname{rank}(X,T)=10$ after the stated control residualization.  The human mediation panel had total residual-to-protein $R^2=0.04454565118019448$, direct $R^2$ after conditioning on modeled tAI $0.03242643251185274$, indirect difference $0.012119218668341744$, and mediated fraction $0.27206289160119157$.  These numbers justify carrying the same residual family into a human perturbation audit, but they do not by themselves prove that any prime-edit fitness effect is mediated by translation.

\paragraph{Translation gate.}
The translation-readout contact available to this packet is bounded.  In the modeled tAI survival audit, only $3$ organisms were used, and the run explicitly reported that $3$ organisms are insufficient for powered survival.  The modeled matrix nevertheless had $81$ entries above the threshold $0.01$ and maximum absolute coupling $3801961.031607896$ under GtRNAdb copy-number and dos Reis wobble weights.  The same audit found full standard amino-acid family coverage in the local tRNA payloads: $20$ families for human, yeast, and \emph{E. coli}; the human tRNA FASTA surface had $432$ headers and $432$ matched headers.  This is a useful translation-weight contact, not an observed ribosome-dynamics measurement.  Therefore the prime-editor rows may be read as a perturbation-facing residual audit only after the translation layer is kept separate from protein abundance, folding, physical feasibility, molecular function, organismal phenotype, and universal mechanism.

\paragraph{Perturbation comparison rows.}
The human prime-editor surface is best interpreted against other finite perturbation audits rather than as a stand-alone mechanism claim.  In the \emph{E. coli} mRFP synonymous protein-output window, $1459$ variants were tested with $B=200$ resamples; the real-CAI controlled model had base $R^2=0.12648386762689323$, full $R^2=0.1798829276988051$, $\Delta R^2=0.05339906007191191$, $\Delta \mathrm{DL}=19.089327473282296$ bits, and matched and within-null $p$-values both equal to $0.004975124378109453$.  In the yeast synonymous mutation audit, $1783$ synonymous mutants across $20$ genes were used, with $21$ CDS-map hits, $B=200$, real-CSC robustness marked true, real-CSC absorption marked false, and real-versus-proxy correlation for the CSC delta equal to $0.39717918221186976$.  These companion rows show that synonymous perturbation surfaces can carry measurable output contact in some organisms and assays.  They do not transfer a sign, causal route, or fitness law into the human prime-editor table.

\paragraph{Controlled readback.}
The mathematical readback is a finite matrix problem.  Each edit row contributes a gene-block-held-out observation whose codon-window coordinates may be projected onto the residual basis $Q=(q_1,\ldots,q_9)$.  A positive biological interpretation would require a surviving chain $Q_i \to T_j \to P_k$ or a separately declared perturbation response after controls for amino-acid identity, organism, GC3, coding-sequence length, $M$-density baseline, matched mRNA abundance where relevant, and simpler codon baselines.  The available human prime-editor run supplies the row counts, gene-block design, cap per cell line, compact-row count, and resampling budget, but the finite record quoted here does not include a human prime-editor effect size, null quantile, description-length gain, or $p$-value for a particular residual coordinate.  Consequently this chapter records admissibility of the perturbation surface and not a promoted human fitness mechanism.

\paragraph{Mediation boundary.}
A direct $Q_i \to$ fitness or $Q_i \to$ protein-abundance association is not enough.  The translation-mediated protein-abundance audit gave positive modeled mediation fractions in all listed organisms, including yeast $0.9491551041216973$, \emph{E. coli} K12 MG1655 $0.612922252793667$, human $0.27206289160119157$, mouse $0.15488799907323988$, and \emph{Sulfolobus solfataricus} $0.01850668564589326$.  A separate yeast structure-mediation audit also decomposed residual-to-protein abundance with $R^2_{QP}=0.2715520808117803$, $R^2_{QP\mid S}=0.2706601505000995$, $R^2_{QP\mid T,M}=0.13773967696311287$, $R^2_{QP\mid T,M,S}=0.13759011208318542$, and structure-incremental fraction $0.0005507778820193332$.  These linked finite values constrain the interpretation of the human prime-editor rows: the appropriate question is whether a same-coordinate perturbation window remains predictive after the declared controls and after the translation step is established or explicitly bounded.  They do not license a claim about folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, selection, phenotype, causation, or universality.

\paragraph{Local verdict.}
The finite record is ready as a bounded chapter because the exact human prime-editor audit has a declared row surface, gene-block held-out design, compact-row count, resampling budget, and runtime, and because the surrounding $B^*_{Q6}$ program supplies the coordinate family and a separate translation/protein mediation context.  The local scientific conclusion is deliberately narrow: the human prime-editor synonymous table is an admissible perturbation window for testing $B^*_{Q6}$ residual contact under gene-block evaluation.  It is not, on the present finite facts, a completed proof of translation realization, protein realization, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, mechanism, organismal phenotype, evolutionary selection, or a cross-organism law.
