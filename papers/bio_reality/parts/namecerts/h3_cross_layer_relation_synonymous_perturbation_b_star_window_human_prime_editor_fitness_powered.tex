\subsection{Human prime-editor synonymous window audit}
\label{subsec:b-star-window-human-prime-editor-fitness}

\paragraph{Finite surface.}
This chapter records a finite perturbation-facing surface for the claim
\texttt{h3.cross\_layer\_relation.synonymous\_perturbation.b\_star\_window\_human\_prime\_editor\_fitness\_powered}.
The rows are human synonymous prime-editor observations grouped by gene, with the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ window coordinates treated as codon-residual covariates and with the biological readback restricted to the measured fitness screen supplied by the finite row table.  The realized audit used $1822$ genes, $9000$ synonymous rows in the main analysis surface, and $24000$ synonymous rows in the compact surface, with at most $3000$ analysis rows per cell line.  The null calibration used $B=200$ resampling or matched-null units, and the registered run completed in $295.329$ seconds.

$$
\begin{aligned}
N_{\mathrm{genes}}&=1822,\\
N_{\mathrm{syn}}&=9000,\\
N_{\mathrm{compact}}&=24000,\\
B&=200,\\
N_{\max,\mathrm{cell\ line}}&=3000 .
\end{aligned}
$$

The folds are gene-block folds.  Thus the held-out design tests whether a residual-window description trained away from a gene block transfers into unseen genes.  Direct gene dummy fixed effects are deliberately absent from the predictive design, since such effects would not be observed for test genes and would convert the held-out problem into a within-gene memorization problem.  The finite object is therefore not a general statement about all synonymous prime editing; it is a bounded matrix of observed synonymous edits, gene-block splits, $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ window coordinates, and held-out fitness readouts.

\paragraph{Coordinate content.}
The perturbation-side coordinates are read together with the nine residual axes used by the surrounding $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ protein-abundance mediation packet:
$$
\begin{aligned}
Q=\{&K\_AAA,\ Arg\_AGR,\ Ile\_AUA,\ Leu\_{CUN\,vs\,UUR},\ Leu\_{UUA\,vs\,UUG},\\
&Ser\_{UCR\,vs\,AGY},\ Ser\_{UCA\,vs\,UCG},\ Thr\_{ACR\,vs\,ACY},\ f3\_stress\} .
\end{aligned}
$$
These coordinates are not amino-acid substitutions.  They are synonymous residual-window coordinates after the amino-acid sequence has already been held fixed at the edit class.  The chapter therefore separates two questions.  First, does a residual coordinate improve held-out prediction on the finite human prime-editor fitness rows?  Second, if a signal is present, is there any permitted route from that signal to translation, protein abundance, folding, function, or mechanism?  The supplied finite row answers only the first question at the level of row availability and audit design; it does not by itself provide a positive effect size, a null-tail probability, or a mediation coefficient for the human prime-editor fitness surface.

\paragraph{Relation to translation-mediated abundance.}
The cross-layer mediation context is informative but not promoted into the prime-editor verdict.  In the organism-level protein-abundance mediation run, the human row used $19053$ joined proteins, one modeled translation readout, $9$ $Q$ coordinates, $23$ ranked control columns after residualization, and $19030$ residual degrees of freedom after the control set.  For that human protein-abundance surface the total residual-to-protein association was
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{QP}&=0.04454565118019448,\\
R^2_{QP\mid T}&=0.03242643251185274,\\
R^2_{\mathrm{indirect}}&=0.012119218668341744,\\
M^{QTP}&=0.27206289160119157 .
\end{aligned}
$$
The same row records $X$-explained energy on the modeled translation readout as $0.3099459586758378$, $X$-explained energy on protein abundance as $1370.6490494847067$, direct improvement energy after conditioning on translation as $997.7463057114725$, and translation-explained energy on protein abundance as $850.7864327764183$.  These numbers show that a separate human cross-sectional protein-abundance matrix can be evaluated with a bounded translation-mediated decomposition.  They do not certify that the prime-editor synonymous fitness surface is mediated by translation, because the prime-editor row supplied here does not include a same-edit translation readout $T_j$, matched mRNA abundance, or matched protein abundance $P_k$.

\paragraph{Structural-order comparison.}
A separate structural-order audit identifies $f3\_stress$ as the dominant residual axis: it is both the dominant increment axis and the dominant loading axis, with weighted held-out increment $0.0031744502294756994$, weighted absolute unit loading $0.5109586606724208$, and sign-flip right-tail probability $0.03125$.  The full coordinate list in that audit is the same nine-axis $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ list used above.  This comparison is useful only as coordinate orientation.  It does not convert the prime-editor fitness screen into a structure assay, nor does it imply that a synonymous edit changes folding, stability, localization, or molecular function.

\paragraph{Admissible readback.}
The admissible readback for this chapter is a finite supervised perturbation surface: synonymous prime-editor rows, gene-block held-out folds, $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ window covariates, and measured fitness scores in the screened human setting.  The biological claim is correspondingly narrow.  A positive result, if later reported with effect sizes and null-tail statistics, would say that the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ window coordinates carry held-out predictive information for the supplied synonymous prime-editor fitness rows after the declared split discipline.  It would not by itself establish translation realization.  It would also not establish folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, organismal phenotype, causal mechanism, selection, or a universal law.

\paragraph{Local boundary.}
A direct $Q_i\to$ fitness association is not a translation-mediated protein realization.  To claim a translation route, the same perturbation or a matched finite panel would need a measured or explicitly bounded translation readout $T_j$ and a surviving controlled step $Q_i\to T_j$.  To claim a protein-abundance route, the same panel would need matched protein abundance $P_k$ and a controlled $Q_i\to T_j\to P_k$ mediation analysis after amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, coding-sequence length, $M$-density baseline, and matched mRNA abundance controls.  In the absence of those contacts, the present chapter remains at the fitness-screen boundary: it may describe the finite human prime-editor synonymous audit, but it may not promote the row counts or the residual-window geometry into translation, protein abundance, structure, physical admissibility, function, mechanism, selection, or universality.
