\paragraph{Local no-promotion rule.}
The finite human prime-editor table is therefore a bounded perturbation record.  It can support a statement of the form: within $9000$ synonymous human editing rows over $1822$ genes, with $B=200$ resampling and gene-block held-out folds, a $B^*_{Q6}$ window coordinate is eligible to be tested against the recorded fitness readout.  It cannot by itself assert translation realization, protein abundance, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, mechanism, organismal phenotype, evolutionary selection, or universality.  Any such statement would require a separate layer-matched contact: measured ribosome occupancy or translation efficiency for translation, matched proteomics for protein abundance, structure or stability data for folded or physical state, and a functional or phenotype assay for biological effect.  A direct $q_i\to y$ association in the prime-editor table remains a perturbation association unless a same-row route through $q_i\to T_j$ and then through an appropriate downstream readout is supplied.
