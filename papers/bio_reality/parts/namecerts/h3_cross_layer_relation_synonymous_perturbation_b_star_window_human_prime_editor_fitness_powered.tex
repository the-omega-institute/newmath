\subsection{Human prime-editor synonymous-window fitness boundary}\label{sec:human-prime-editor-synonymous-window-fitness-boundary}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Claim surface.}
This record concerns the human prime-editor synonymous perturbation packet for $B^*_{Q6}$ window coordinates.  The finite surface is not a direct claim that synonymous editing produces a biological function or organism-level phenotype.  It is a row-bounded test surface in which coding-sequence residual coordinates are allowed to touch a perturbation readout only through explicitly measured rows.  The available run contains $9000$ synonymous prime-editor observations over $1822$ genes, with a compact synonymous window table of $24000$ rows and a per-cell-line analysis cap of $3000$ rows.  The resampling depth was $B=200$, and the registered run completed in $295.329$ seconds.  Held-out evaluation used gene-block folds; direct gene dummy effects were therefore not inserted into the predictive held-out design because test genes were absent from the training folds.

\paragraph{Finite variables.}
The coordinate side is the same nine-coordinate $B^*_{Q6}$ residual list used by the surrounding translation and protein-abundance packets:
$K\_AAA$, $Arg\_AGR$, $Ile\_AUA$, $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$, $Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG$, $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$, $Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG$, $Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY$, and $f3\_stress$.
The perturbation rows are human prime-editor synonymous edits, grouped so that gene identity cannot leak as a memorized fixed effect across the train--test split.  The present finite facts establish the dimensions of the experiment but do not provide a signed held-out effect, a null-tail probability, a description-length gain, or a per-coordinate ranking for the fitness readout.  Consequently the chapter can state the admissible contact geometry and the row counts, but it cannot report a positive human fitness result.

\paragraph{Translation gate.}
The perturbation surface inherits the no-promotion rule from the translation boundary.  A residual coordinate $q_i$ may be interpreted downstream only if it first survives a translation readout $T_j$ such as modeled tAI, observed ribosome occupancy, or translation efficiency.  In the finite modeled-tAI contact, only $3$ organisms were available, so the run was explicitly underpowered for a species-powered survival claim.  It nevertheless computed $81$ entries above the bounded coupling threshold $0.01$, with maximum absolute coupling $3801961.031607896$, while recording that $3$ organisms are insufficient for powered survival without at least $40$ matched species.  Thus modeled tAI supplies a bounded translation-layer contact, not a measured ribosome or protein-output result.

\paragraph{Protein mediation context.}
A separate cross-layer protein-abundance run supplies the strongest available controlled mediation context.  Across $12$ organisms, the joined protein counts included $19053$ human proteins, $19395$ mouse proteins, $5815$ yeast proteins, $3739$ \emph{E. coli} proteins, and $1656$ \emph{Sulfolobus solfataricus} proteins.  After amino-acid composition, GC3, coding length, and the $M$-density baseline were controlled, the joint nine-coordinate mediation fractions were positive and in the unit interval for all organisms.  The largest was yeast, with $M^{QTP}=0.9491551041216973$ and indirect $R^2=0.28320534058057895$ from total $R^2=0.29837624994140827$ to direct conditioned $R^2=0.0151709093608293$.  Human had $M^{QTP}=0.27206289160119157$, total $R^2=0.04454565118019448$, direct conditioned $R^2=0.03242643251185274$, and indirect $R^2=0.012119218668341744$.  These numbers justify treating translation-conditioned protein abundance as an already bounded comparison surface, but they still do not turn a human prime-editor fitness association into a mechanism.

\paragraph{Perturbation comparison.}
The closest perturbation-side finite comparison is the yeast synonymous mutation packet.  It had $1783$ synonymous mutants over $20$ genes, with $21$ CDS map hits, $B=200$, and runtime $289.093$ seconds.  Its codon-stability coefficient contact had real-versus-proxy correlation increment $0.39717918221186976$; the real coefficient was marked robust, while the proxy was not said to absorb it.  This yeast result shows that synonymous perturbation rows can be audited as finite perturbation data rather than as coordinate geometry alone.  The human prime-editor run has a larger row surface, but the supplied finite record lacks the corresponding held-out fitness statistic, null envelope, and coordinate-specific effect table.

\paragraph{Associated negative controls.}
Several linked runs constrain how far the human perturbation packet may be read.  The evolutionary imprint run completed on $80$ orthogroups and $240$ rows across $3$ species, with $\Delta R^2=0.03250515015613468$, leave-clade-out $\Delta R^2=-0.0021477729853302424$, null $95\%$ maximum $\Delta R^2=0.05427157066522958$, and positive selection-imprint set to false.  The mass-balance run used $120$ genes and $718$ intervals; it had $R^2_{L0}=0.9960628201518571$, $R^2_{L1}=0.996410628851051$, $R^2_{L2}=0.9712443377743347$, $R^2_{L3}=0.8953999568421264$, $\Delta R^2=-0.07584438093220836$, and $\Delta DL=-668.8046070381928$ bits.  The turnover audit used $3290$ genes and found $R^2_{L2}=0.055740851626933985$, $R^2_{L3}=0.050278872193227464$, $\Delta R^2=-0.005461979433706483$, $\Delta DL=-13.68819707902732$ bits, matched-bin $p=0.8855721393034826$, and recoded $p=1.0$.  These negative or bounded outcomes prevent the prime-editor chapter from borrowing evidence for selection, dynamic mass closure, or turnover realization.

\paragraph{Structure-side separation.}
The structure-related packets also remain separated.  The yeast abundance-residual decomposition joined $4759$ genes and reported $R^2_{QP}=0.2715520808117803$, $R^2_{QP\mid S}=0.2706601505000995$, $R^2_{QP\mid T,M}=0.13773967696311287$, and $R^2_{QP\mid T,M,S}=0.13759011208318542$.  The structure-only fraction was $0.003284564452662019$, the structure-incremental fraction was $0.0005507778820193332$, the $S_Q$ structure $R^2$ was $0.003774248455422586$, the structure-right-tail permutation value was $0.027972027972027972$, and the incremental-structure permutation value was $0.1258741258741259$.  A broader structural-order run identified $f3\_stress$ as the only significant axis, with weighted held-out increment $0.0031744502294756994$ and sign-flip $p=0.03125$.  Those values describe structure-adjacent statistical contacts, not folding or physical admissibility for human prime-editor edits.

\paragraph{Baseline genetic-code contact.}
The locked genetic-code contact gives a lower-layer calibration point.  In the phase-one confirmatory surface, $40$ of $40$ independent replication trials and $40$ of $40$ boundary-probe trials succeeded, each with one-sided binomial upper-tail value $9.094947017729282\times 10^{-13}$.  The negative-control and critical-ablation surfaces each had $20$ trials with $0$ unexpected successes.  This supports the codon-geometry contact at the genetic-code table layer, but it does not by itself supply translation, protein abundance, fitness, mechanism, or universal biological law for the prime-editor rows.

\paragraph{Local formal readback.}
The admissible finite relation for this chapter is the controlled chain
$$
\begin{aligned}
q_i &\longrightarrow T_j \longrightarrow P_k \longrightarrow F_\ell,
\end{aligned}
$$
where $q_i$ is a $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinate, $T_j$ is a translation readout, $P_k$ is matched protein abundance when present, and $F_\ell$ is a human prime-editor fitness readout.  The current finite human run records the size and blocking design of the $F_\ell$ surface but does not provide the numerical fitness increment needed to fill the last arrow.  A direct $q_i \to F_\ell$ association, even if later observed, would remain insufficient unless the same coordinate also passes the translation step under amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, CDS length, $M$-density baseline, matched mRNA abundance, and simpler-baseline controls.

\paragraph{Verdict.}
The chapter is therefore a boundary-ready human perturbation record rather than a positive fitness-realization record.  It can state that the experiment exists as a finite row surface with $1822$ genes, $9000$ synonymous observations, $24000$ compact rows, $B=200$, and gene-block held-out folds.  It can also state that the surrounding translation and protein-abundance packets give bounded comparison data, including human $M^{QTP}=0.27206289160119157$ and yeast $M^{QTP}=0.9491551041216973$.  It cannot claim translation realization, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, mechanism, organismal phenotype, selection, or universality from these rows.  Those interpretations require separate layer-matched measurements and finite statistics not present in the supplied human prime-editor record.
