\subsection{Human Prime-Editor Synonymous Perturbation Window}\label{subsec:human-prime-editor-synonymous-perturbation-window}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite row surface.}
This record treats human prime-editor synonymous edits as a perturbation contact for the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ coding-window coordinates, not as a direct claim about protein realization.  The finite table contains $9000$ synonymous rows in the principal analysis surface and $24000$ synonymous rows in the compact auxiliary surface, distributed over $1822$ genes.  The analysis capped each cell-line slice at $3000$ rows, used $B=200$ resampling or matched-null draws, and completed in $295.329$ seconds.  Held-out evaluation used gene-block folds: genes appearing in the test fold were not present in the training fold, so explicit gene dummy fixed effects were not used as predictive covariates in the held-out design.  This is the correct finite-row reading of the contact: it asks whether the synonymous coding-window residual has predictive contact with edited-cell fitness readout under a gene-block split, while refusing any interpretation that would require the same gene identity to be visible on both sides of the fold.

\paragraph{Coordinate and controls.}
The row coordinate is a synonymous perturbation window decorated by the nine-dimensional $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual basis used throughout the codon residual program:
$$
\begin{aligned}
Q &= \{K\_{AAA},\ Arg\_{AGR},\ Ile\_{AUA},\ Leu\_{CUN/UUR},\ Leu\_{UUA/UUG},\\
&\qquad Ser\_{UCR/AGY},\ Ser\_{UCA/UCG},\ Thr\_{ACR/ACY},\ f3\_{stress}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The row is therefore not a raw codon-count claim.  It is a finite perturbation table whose admissible readback is restricted to synonymous edit fitness under the declared held-out protocol.  Amino-acid sequence is fixed by the synonymous design, while gene identity is handled by gene-block splitting rather than by a train-test leaking fixed-effect column.  The cap of $3000$ rows per cell line prevents a single cell-line slice from dominating the finite matrix.  The $24000$-row compact surface is useful for checking that the coordinate extraction and edit packaging scale beyond the principal $9000$-row analysis table, but it does not by itself promote the result to a mechanism claim.

\paragraph{Perturbation statistic.}
Let $q_i$ be one of the nine residual coordinates and let $F$ be the measured edited-cell fitness readout on the finite prime-editor table.  The admissible question is whether the residualized window coordinate improves held-out prediction of $F$ after the local synonymous-edit design and fold constraints have been imposed.  At this finite-row level the chapter records the following bounded statistic shape:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathcal{D}_{human} &= \{(e_r,g_r,c_r,q_r,F_r): 1\le r\le 9000\},\\
G_{seen}(\mathrm{train})\cap G_{seen}(\mathrm{test}) &= \varnothing,\\
B &= 200,\qquad n_{genes}=1822,\qquad n_{syn}=9000.
\end{aligned}
$$
Here $e_r$ is the synonymous edit row, $g_r$ its gene block, $c_r$ its cell-line or condition slice, $q_r$ the extracted $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ window coordinate vector, and $F_r$ the edited-cell fitness readout.  The finite contact is therefore a perturbation-level association test under gene-block generalization.  It is not an observation of translation rate, protein abundance, folding, molecular function, physical admissibility, or organism-level selection.

\paragraph{Relation to translation-mediated protein abundance.}
A separate multi-organism matrix has already shown that the same nine-coordinate $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ basis can be used in a bounded statistical mediation calculation from modeled per-protein tAI to matched protein abundance.  That surface joined $12$ organisms, including $19053$ human proteins, $19395$ mouse proteins, $5815$ yeast proteins, and $3739$ \textit{E. coli} K12 proteins.  On that separate matrix the human mediated fraction was $M^{QTP}=0.27206289160119157$, with total residual-to-protein $R^2=0.04454565118019448$, direct residual-to-protein after modeled tAI $R^2=0.03242643251185274$, and indirect difference $0.012119218668341744$.  Yeast had the largest listed mediated fraction, $0.9491551041216973$, while \textit{Sulfolobus solfataricus} had $0.01850668564589326$.  These numbers are useful only as a calibration of the coordinate family: they do not convert the human prime-editor fitness table into a protein-abundance mediation table.  The present chapter therefore keeps the prime-editor fitness contact as a synonymous perturbation readout, while using the broader mediation surface only to locate the coordinate in the surrounding finite evidence landscape.

\paragraph{Modeled translation boundary.}
The modeled-tAI survival contact computed the same nine residual coordinates for three organisms and found $81$ modeled coupling entries above threshold $0.01$, with maximum absolute modeled score $3801961.031607896$.  The organism count in that modeled contact was only $3$, and the finite record explicitly states that $3$ organisms are insufficient for powered survival.  Thus the prime-editor chapter may mention modeled translation compatibility as background coordinate contact, but it cannot use it as measured ribosome occupancy or translation-efficiency evidence.  A genuine translation step would require same-row translation readouts such as ribosome occupancy, translation efficiency, tAI or stAI only under its bounded interpretation, and matched mRNA controls.

\paragraph{Local no-promotion rule.}
The human prime-editor table is allowed to support only the finite perturbation claim that a synonymous coding-window residual can be evaluated against edited-cell fitness under gene-block held-out prediction.  No translation, folding, physical admissibility, biochemical function, molecular mechanism, phenotype law, or universality follows from this table alone.  A direct $Q_i\to F$ association is not a $Q_i\to T_j\to P_k$ mediation chain.  To promote the result toward protein realization, a separate contact would have to attach a matched translation readout $T_j$, a matched protein readout $P_k$, and matched mRNA abundance controls on the same finite rows or on a rigorously joined finite panel.  Without that extra contact, the correct reading is bounded: the prime-editor dataset is a perturbation-level fitness surface for synonymous windows, not a translation or protein-mechanism experiment.

\paragraph{Verdict.}
The finite-row evidence is sufficient for a chapter-level BioReality record because the table has concrete row counts, a declared resampling count, a gene-block held-out protocol, and a finite synonymous perturbation surface.  The admissible conclusion is narrow: $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ window coordinates are eligible for controlled human prime-editor synonymous-fitness testing on $9000$ principal rows over $1822$ genes, with $B=200$ null or resampling draws and a $3000$-row cap per cell-line slice.  The record remains bounded below translation and protein realization until a separate matched translation and protein-abundance contact is supplied.
