\subsection{Human prime-editing fitness screen as a synonymous-window boundary}
\label{sec:h3-cross-layer-relation-synonymous-perturbation-human-prime-editor-fitness}

\paragraph{Finite surface.}
This record treats the human prime-editor synonymous-fitness screen as a finite perturbation surface rather than as a direct biological-law claim.  The observed rows are synonymous edits grouped into gene-block held-out folds, with each edit assigned to the same $B^*_{Q6}$ window-coordinate family used in the surrounding perturbation packets.  The finite run contains $1822$ genes, $9000$ synonymous rows in the analysis surface, and a compact synonymous enumeration of $24000$ rows.  The analysis cap was $3000$ rows per cell line, the resampling count was $B=200$, and the runtime was $295.329$ seconds.  The held-out design is important: folds are blocked by gene, and direct gene dummy indicators are not inserted into the predictive model because the test genes are absent from the training folds.  Thus the finite object being read is not a within-gene memorization table; it is a bounded gene-held-out perturbation screen.

\paragraph{Coordinate and control interpretation.}
The admissible carrier is a table of triples $(q_i,t_j,p_k)$ only when the residual coordinate $q_i$ first survives a translation-facing readout $t_j$ before any downstream protein or fitness interpretation is attempted.  In this human prime-editor screen the registered finite facts provide the edit counts, gene count, fold design, row cap, and resampling count, but they do not provide a measured translation readout, a matched protein-abundance endpoint, or a signed fitness increment for a particular $q_i$.  The screen therefore supports a narrower statement: synonymous edits can be organized against the $B^*_{Q6}$ window basis under a gene-held-out design, but the present finite row does not by itself identify translation mediation, protein realization, or organism-level selection.

\paragraph{Local statistic.}
The finite data actually reported for this packet are summarized by
$$
\begin{aligned}
N_{\mathrm{genes}} &= 1822,\\
N_{\mathrm{syn}} &= 9000,\\
N_{\mathrm{compact}} &= 24000,\\
B &= 200,\\
N_{\max/\mathrm{cell\ line}} &= 3000,\\
T_{\mathrm{run}} &= 295.329\ \mathrm{s}.
\end{aligned}
$$
These numbers are sufficient to document a powered finite perturbation pass and its row budget, but they are not sufficient to certify a positive effect direction.  In particular, the finite record lacks a reported $\Delta R^2$, likelihood gain, null percentile, or permutation $p$-value for the human screen itself.  For this reason the chapter reads the human packet as a completed finite audit surface whose biological promotion remains conditional on an explicit downstream statistic.

\paragraph{Relation to the broader perturbation family.}
The human prime-editor packet sits beside other synonymous-window audits that do contain more explicit scalar outcomes.  In the bacterial mRFP perturbation surface, $1459$ variants yielded $\Delta R^2=0.05339906007191191$, $\Delta DL=19.089327473282296$ bits, $p_{\mathrm{matched}}=0.004975124378109453$, and $p_{\mathrm{within}}=0.004975124378109453$ after real CAI comparison.  In the yeast synonymous-mutation surface, $1783$ synonymous mutants over $20$ genes gave a real-versus-proxy CSC correlation increment of $0.39717918221186976$, with the real CSC control not absorbing the $B^*_{Q6}$ signal.  The Chen GFP packet supplied $3556$ variants split into $1124$ and $2432$ regional rows, with an out-of-frame mRNA slope of $0.016523671594873352$.  The Yang endogenous $E.\ coli$ surface contained $46686$ synonymous edits over $30$ genes.  These neighboring finite rows justify treating the human prime-editor screen as part of a real perturbation program, while still forbidding transfer of a sign, mechanism, or selection conclusion into the human packet without the human packet's own reported scalar outcome.

\paragraph{Translation-gated boundary.}
The cross-layer route is stricter than a direct residual-to-fitness association.  A residual coordinate $q_i$ may enter a protein or fitness story only if a controlled translation readout $t_j$ is attached and survives the declared controls.  The available modeled-tAI survival run computed nine residual coordinates, including $K\_AAA$, $Arg\_AGR$, $Ile\_AUA$, $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$, $Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG$, $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$, $Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG$, $Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY$, and $f3\_stress$, across three organisms.  That run reported $81$ bounded-coupling entries above threshold $0.01$ and a maximum absolute entry of $3801961.031607896$, but it also recorded that $3$ organisms are insufficient for powered survival requiring at least $40$ matched species.  Separately, the multi-organism modeled protein-abundance mediation run joined $12$ organisms and computed per-protein modeled tAI with $9$ $B^*_{Q6}$ coordinates; for human rows it used $19053$ joined proteins and reported a mediated fraction $M^{QTP}=0.27206289160119157$, total $R^2_{QP}=0.04454565118019448$, direct $R^2_{QP\mid T}=0.03242643251185274$, and indirect component $0.012119218668341744$.  Those numbers show that a bounded translation-conditioned protein-abundance decomposition exists elsewhere, but the human prime-editor fitness record itself does not yet provide the same $(q_i,t_j,p_k)$ or $(q_i,t_j,\mathrm{fitness})$ mediated table.

\paragraph{Admissible conclusion.}
The finite conclusion is therefore deliberately modest.  The human prime-editor screen is a valid synonymous perturbation surface for asking whether $B^*_{Q6}$ window coordinates predict held-out fitness behavior across genes, and it is large enough to be treated as a nontrivial finite audit: $9000$ analyzed synonymous rows, $24000$ compact synonymous rows, $1822$ genes, and $200$ resampling blocks.  What it presently certifies is the existence of a gene-held-out perturbation table compatible with the $B^*_{Q6}$ coordinate readout.  It does not certify translation realization, protein abundance mediation, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, mechanism, phenotype, selection, or universality.  Each of those stronger interpretations requires its own layer-matched contact: measured translation or explicitly bounded modeled translation for the $q_i\to t_j$ step, matched protein or fitness rows for the downstream endpoint, and a statistic showing that the mediated route survives amino-acid sequence, organism or cell-line structure, GC3, coding-sequence length, $M$-density baseline, mRNA abundance where applicable, and simpler codon baselines.

\paragraph{Failure mode.}
If every human prime-editor mediated improvement is nonpositive after those controls, the packet remains at the synonymous-perturbation boundary: it may be used as evidence that the screen was queried in the $B^*_{Q6}$ coordinate system, but it may not be promoted to protein realization or mechanism.  If a future finite table reports a positive held-out statistic together with a surviving translation step, the same carrier can be reread as a controlled mediation matrix.  Until then, the correct scientific status is a completed finite perturbation audit with downstream biological interpretation held back.
