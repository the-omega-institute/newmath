\subsection{Human Prime-Editor Synonymous Windows as a Bounded Perturbation Readout}
\label{subsec:bior reality-human-prime-editor-synonymous-window}

\paragraph{Finite surface.}
The record considered here is a finite perturbation table whose rows are synonymous prime-editor windows in human coding sequence.  Each row is read with a controlled coordinate tuple $(q_i,t_j,p_k)$ only after the synonymous residual coordinate $q_i$ has been separated from amino-acid identity, organism identity, GC3, CDS length, and the $M$-density baseline.  The verified finite run used $n_{\mathrm{genes}}=1822$ genes, $n_{\mathrm{syn}}=9000$ analyzed synonymous entries, and a compact synonymous scan of $n_{\mathrm{compact}}=24000$ entries, with a per-cell-line analysis cap of $3000$ rows.  The resampling budget was $B=200$, and the run time was $295.329$ seconds.  The folds were gene-block folds: a test fold contains genes not seen in the training fold, so ordinary gene dummy fixed effects were not inserted into the held-out predictor.  This design choice prevents a gene label from becoming a disguised memorization coordinate in the predictive comparison.

\paragraph{Coordinate discipline.}
The admissible residual coordinates are the nine $B^*_{Q6}$ axes
$$
\begin{aligned}
Q=\{&K\_AAA,\ Arg\_AGR,\ Ile\_AUA,\ Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR,\ Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG,\\
&Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY,\ Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG,\ Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY,\ f3\_stress\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
They are not amino-acid substitutions and they are not protein-state annotations.  A synonymous perturbation may enter the present table only as a coding-window residual after the amino-acid sequence is fixed.  The finite run therefore asks whether human synonymous prime-editor windows carry a residual predictive signal inside the registered fitness table; it does not by itself say that the coordinate has reached translation, protein abundance, folding, or organismal phenotype.

\paragraph{Translation gate inherited by the readout.}
The linked translation-readout computation supplies a bounded background for why direct $Q\to P$ promotion is not allowed.  In the modeled tAI survival run, the codon-count space had $61$ sense codons, three organisms were present, and the coupling threshold was $0.01$.  The finite modeled-tAI surface had $81$ entries above threshold and maximum absolute coupling $3801961.031607896$, but the same record explicitly marks three organisms as insufficient for powered survival; it is a modeled-tAI coupling table, not ribosome profiling or matched proteomics validation.  The current human perturbation rows therefore remain below a translation-realization claim unless a same-row translation readout is attached.

\paragraph{Mediation scale for downstream protein abundance.}
The separate $Q\to T\to P$ protein-abundance decomposition gives the finite mediation vocabulary used here, while also showing why the human prime-editor result cannot be promoted by notation alone.  Across the matched protein-abundance table, the human organism had $19053$ joined proteins, residual degrees of freedom $19030$ after controls, $9$ $Q$ coordinates, rank $9$ for the residualized $Q$ block, rank $1$ for the residualized modeled-tAI block, and rank $10$ for the combined $Q,T$ block.  For human protein abundance, the total controlled residual association was
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{QP}&=0.04454565118019448,\\
R^2_{QP\mid T}&=0.03242643251185274,\\
R^2_{\mathrm{indirect}}&=0.012119218668341744,\\
M^{QTP}&=0.27206289160119157.
\end{aligned}
$$
Those numbers belong to a cross-sectional matched CDS/protein-abundance surface with modeled per-protein tAI.  They are useful as a finite scale reference: a direct residual-to-protein association is not enough.  A stronger interpretation would require the same residual coordinate to pass a controlled translation step before a downstream protein readout is considered mediated.

\paragraph{Perturbation verdict.}
The prime-editor finite row is therefore registered as a bounded synonymous-perturbation fitness readout.  Its positive content is the existence of a gene-blocked, finite human synonymous-window analysis with $1822$ genes and $9000$ analyzed synonymous rows under a $B=200$ resampling budget.  Its negative content is equally important: the available row does not include a same-row ribosome-occupancy, translation-efficiency, matched protein-abundance, folding, localization, stability, functional-assay, or organismal phenotype measurement.  Consequently the admissible statement is that the human prime-editor synonymous table is a perturbation-level contact for residual fitness prediction under gene-blocked evaluation.  It is not, by itself, evidence that $B^*_{Q6}$ has been realized as translation output, folded structure, physical admissibility, molecular mechanism, biological function, selection, or a universal biological rule.

\paragraph{Local boundary.}
The chapter keeps the following rule local.  A residual coordinate $q_i$ may be discussed at the synonymous-window fitness layer when the finite prime-editor table supplies the row.  It may be discussed at the translation layer only if a separate translation contact supplies $q_i\to t_j$ rows under the declared controls.  It may be discussed at the protein-abundance layer only after a downstream $p_k$ row is matched and the direct $q_i\to p_k$ association is tested against the translation-conditioned route.  No claim about folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, mechanism, phenotype, selection, or universality follows from the present human prime-editor table without an additional contact at that layer.
