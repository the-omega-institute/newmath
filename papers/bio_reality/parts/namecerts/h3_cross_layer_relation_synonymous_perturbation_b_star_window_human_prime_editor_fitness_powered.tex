\paragraph{Finite perturbation surface.}
This record concerns a finite synonymous-perturbation table in human coding sequence windows.  The designated coordinate family is the nine-dimensional $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual basis, used only as a codon-window coordinate system and not as a direct biological cause.  The audited human prime-editor run completed with experiment id \texttt{b1a1a8c7baae}.  Its finite surface contains $1822$ genes, $9000$ synonymous edit rows in the primary analysis table, and $24000$ synonymous edit rows in the compact table.  The run used at most $3000$ analyzed rows per cell line and evaluated $B=200$ resampling or null draws, with total runtime $295.329$ seconds.  Held-out folds were gene-block folds: direct gene dummy fixed effects were not included in the predictive held-out design because test genes were unseen in the training folds.  Thus the admissible readback is a finite held-out synonymous-edit fitness audit indexed by gene-block generalization, not an in-sample gene-identity regression.

\paragraph{Local mathematical object.}
Let $Q=\{q_1,\ldots,q_9\}$ denote the residual coordinates
$$
\begin{aligned}

\end{aligned}
$$
For the human prime-editor table, an observed row is read as a tuple
$$
\begin{aligned}
r=(g,e,c,y,z),
\end{aligned}
$$
where $g$ is the edited gene, $e$ is a synonymous edit or compact synonymous edit entry, $c$ records the induced local $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ window coordinates, $y$ is the held-out fitness readout supplied by the prime-editor assay, and $z$ is the finite control context used by the audit.  The chapter-level object is therefore the finite relation
$$
\begin{aligned}

with $|G|=1822$, $|E_{\mathrm{primary}}|=9000$, $|E_{\mathrm{compact}}|=24000$, and a held-out partition in which genes, rather than rows alone, define the fold boundary.

\paragraph{Why the gene-block design matters.}
The relevant finite question is not whether a synonymously edited row can be fitted after its gene identity has already been seen.  The question is whether the coordinate signal survives when a test gene is absent from the training fold.  If a model were allowed to encode a direct gene dummy for the same gene in both train and test, then a fitness association could be partly a gene-label memory.  The recorded design blocks that route: gene identity is respected as a fold boundary, so a reported held-out gain must cross from synonymous-window features to unseen genes rather than from an explicit in-sample gene indicator to held-out rows.  This makes the audit appropriate for a bounded cross-layer perturbation question, while still leaving the biological interpretation narrow.

\paragraph{Connection to translation-mediated protein abundance.}
The surrounding finite biological register contains a separate $Q\to T\to P$ mediation calculation on matched coding sequence, modeled translation readout, and protein abundance rows.  In that calculation, the human organism table has $19053$ joined proteins, residual degrees of freedom $19030$ after controls, a rank-$9$ residual coordinate block, a rank-$1$ modeled translation readout, and rank $23$ for the control matrix.  The human mediation scalar was
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{QP}&=0.04454565118019448,\\
R^2_{QP\mid T}&=0.03242643251185274,\\
R^2_{\mathrm{indirect}}&=0.012119218668341744,\\
M^{QTP}&=0.27206289160119157.
\end{aligned}
$$
These numbers are not imported as evidence that a prime-editor synonymous edit changes protein abundance or fitness by a translation mechanism.  They are cited only to state the finite background discipline: when a downstream protein quantity is discussed, the admissible form is a controlled $Q\to T\to P$ matrix with a translation-readout step, not a direct promotion of $Q\to P$ or $Q\to$ fitness association.

\paragraph{Translation-readout boundary.}
A separate modeled-tAI survival audit computed the same nine residual coordinates across $3$ organisms.  It used $61$ sense codons, a coupling threshold of $0.01$, and found $81$ modeled-tAI bounded-coupling entries above threshold, with maximum absolute entry $3801961.031607896$.  The same audit explicitly states that $3$ organisms are insufficient for powered survival and that powered survival would require at least $40$ matched species.  Hence the human prime-editor chapter cannot treat a codon-window residual coordinate as an established translation-realization law.  It may only use the coordinate as a finite edit-window covariate unless the relevant translation readout is measured or explicitly bounded in the same row system.

\paragraph{Axis-specific caution.}
Another finite audit identified $\mathrm{f3\_stress}$ as the only structural-order-associated coordinate under its fold-level sign-flip gate.  Its weighted held-out $R^2$ increment was $0.0031744502294756994$, with sign-flip right-tail probability $0.03125$ and weighted absolute unit loading $0.5109586606724208$.  The remaining coordinates did not pass that gate in the same record.  This is useful for naming a plausible coordinate to inspect in the prime-editor table, but it does not license a statement about folding, function, or phenotype in the human edit assay.  It only sharpens the finite audit language: any observed human prime-editor effect must be reported per coordinate, under its own held-out statistic, rather than transferred from a different biological table.

\paragraph{Admissible conclusion.}
The finite-row conclusion is a bounded audit statement.  The human prime-editor synonymous-window experiment is sufficiently instantiated to support a chapter-level record with $1822$ genes, $9000$ primary synonymous edits, $24000$ compact synonymous edits, $B=200$ null or resampling draws, and gene-block held-out evaluation.  The record is ready as a finite perturbation surface for testing whether $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ window coordinates add held-out fitness information beyond the declared controls.  Since the supplied row does not include the final held-out improvement, null quantile, $p$-value, coefficient list, or per-coordinate ranking for the human prime-editor response, this chapter does not assert a positive synonymous-fitness effect.  It asserts only that the finite audit surface exists and that any positive interpretation must pass through the held-out statistic actually computed for this table.

\paragraph{No higher-layer promotion.}
No translation, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, mechanism, organismal phenotype, selection law, or universality follows from the existence of the human prime-editor synonymous edit table.  A $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ coordinate is not itself a ribosome measurement, protein abundance measurement, folding assay, activity assay, or evolutionary mechanism.  A direct association from a residual coordinate to a fitness readout would remain a perturbation-level statistical association unless the same finite row system also supplies the appropriate intermediate contact: a translation readout for translation claims, matched protein abundance for protein-output claims, structural or physical measurements for folding or admissibility claims, and functional or phenotype assays for function or organismal claims.
