\subsection{Human prime-editor synonymous windows at the fitness boundary}
\label{sec:human-prime-editor-synonymous-window-fitness-boundary}

\paragraph{Finite surface.}
This record concerns a finite perturbation table whose rows are synonymous prime-editor alleles in human coding sequence.  The attached run used $9000$ synonymous rows in the main analysis surface and also retained a compact synonymous surface of $24000$ rows.  The row set covered $1822$ genes, with no more than $3000$ analysis rows taken per cell line, and the randomization or resampling depth was $B=200$.  The finite object is therefore not an abstract codon law; it is a bounded table of edited coding windows, gene-block held-out folds, codon-window covariates, and an observed fitness readout.  Direct gene dummy variables were not placed in the predictive held-out design, because held-out folds were split by gene and the test genes were absent from the training fold.  This matters mathematically: the surface tests whether the synonymous-window coordinate can travel across unseen genes, not whether a gene label can be memorized.

\paragraph{Coordinate being tested.}
Let $q_i$ range over the nine $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinates used throughout the associated codon-window packet:
$$
\begin{aligned}
q_i \in \{&K\_AAA,\ Arg\_AGR,\ Ile\_AUA,\ Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR,\ Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG,\\
&Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY,\ Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG,\ Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY,\ f3\_stress\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
In this chapter the row coordinate is read only as a synonymous perturbation descriptor attached to an edited coding window.  The readout is a human prime-editor fitness measurement, so the admissible claim is a perturbation-level association under the declared held-out design.  The coordinate is not promoted to translation, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, mechanism, selection, or universality merely because it appears in a finite fitness table.

\paragraph{Held-out design and rank boundary.}
The decisive design feature is the gene-block fold split.  If a model can improve prediction on held-out rows only by learning gene-specific constants, then it has not shown a transferable synonymous-window effect.  The stated design blocks that shortcut by withholding genes as units.  With $1822$ genes and $9000$ main synonymous rows, the average main-surface density is approximately
$$
\begin{aligned}
9000/1822 \approx 4.94
\end{aligned}
$$
main synonymous edits per gene, while the compact surface has
$$
\begin{aligned}
24000/1822 \approx 13.17
\end{aligned}
$$
compact synonymous rows per gene.  These counts are large enough to define a finite perturbation audit and small enough that the row geometry must remain visibly conditioned on the split rule, the maximum per-cell-line cap, and the bootstrap depth $B=200$.

\paragraph{Relation to translation mediation.}
A separate cross-layer protein-abundance mediation surface used the same nine-coordinate $B^*_{Q6}$ basis and reported finite organism-specific mediation fractions for modeled translation readouts and matched PAXdb protein abundance.  For example, the modeled $Q \to T \to P$ decomposition gave $M^{QTP}=0.27206289160119157$ for Homo sapiens with $19053$ joined proteins, $M^{QTP}=0.9491551041216973$ for Saccharomyces cerevisiae with $5815$ joined proteins, and $M^{QTP}=0.612922252793667$ for Escherichia coli K12 MG1655 with $3739$ joined proteins.  Those numbers justify using the same coordinate family as a bounded codon-window descriptor, but they do not certify this prime-editor fitness table as a translation-mediated protein-realization result.  The human prime-editor row table lacks, inside this finite record, a same-row measured translation readout $T_j$ and matched downstream protein abundance $P_k$ for each edited allele.  Thus a direct $q_i \to$ fitness association, even when predictive, is reported here as perturbation readout contact, not as proof of a $q_i \to T_j \to P_k \to$ fitness pathway.

\paragraph{Modeled translation contact kept separate.}
The modeled translation-survival packet computed the same residual basis from three organisms and found $81$ modeled-tAI coupling entries above threshold $0.01$, with maximum absolute entry $3801961.031607896$.  It also recorded that $3$ organisms are insufficient for powered survival, and that powered survival would require at least $40$ matched species.  This local prime-editor chapter inherits the same no-promotion discipline.  A modeled tAI or stAI weight may help name a translation-layer contact in another table, but it is not a ribosome-footprint measurement, not a protein-abundance measurement, and not a substitute for a same-row translation step in this human editing assay.

\paragraph{Finite verdict.}
The finite record is admissible as a human synonymous-perturbation fitness audit with $9000$ main synonymous alleles, $24000$ compact synonymous alleles, $1822$ genes, $B=200$, and a gene-block held-out design capped at $3000$ analysis rows per cell line.  Its positive content is that the $B^*_{Q6}$ window coordinate can be tested against an experimentally perturbed human fitness surface without turning gene identity into the fitted explanation.  Its negative content is equally important: the chapter does not infer translation realization, ribosome dynamics, protein folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, mechanism, organism-level selection, or a universal biological law.  Those claims would require their own finite contacts, with a same-row translation readout or protein-state measurement and the relevant controls attached before any higher-layer interpretation is allowed.
