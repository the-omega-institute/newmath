\subsection{Human prime-editor synonymous-window fitness audit}\label{sec:human-prime-editor-synonymous-window-fitness-audit}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite surface.}
This record treats the human prime-editor synonymous perturbation table as a finite perturbation surface, not as a general law about coding sequence.  The rows are synonymous edits grouped by gene, the readout is a held-out fitness response, and the admissible coordinate input is the same nine-dimensional $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual window family used throughout the translation-bounded codon analysis:
$$
\begin{aligned}
Q = \{&K\_AAA,\ Arg\_AGR,\ Ile\_AUA,\ Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR,\ Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG,\\
&Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY,\ Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG,\ Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY,\ f3\_stress\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The completed finite run used $1822$ genes, $9000$ synonymous edits in the main analysis table, and a compact synonymous table of $24000$ rows.  The analysis capped each cell-line slice at $3000$ rows and used $B=200$ resampling units.  The recorded runtime was $295.329$ seconds.  These numbers make the chapter a row-bounded perturbation audit: it is large enough to test whether a synonymous-window encoding carries held-out predictive information in this dataset, but it is not by itself a proof of translation, protein output, folding, mechanism, or evolutionary universality.

\paragraph{Gene-block design.}
The central design restriction is that held-out folds are gene-block folds.  Direct gene dummy fixed effects are deliberately absent from the predictive design, because the test genes are unseen during training.  Thus the finite question is not whether the model can memorize per-gene offsets.  It is whether a codon-window residual description can transport information across genes under the imposed split.  In symbolic form, the tested surface has the form
$$
\begin{aligned}
(q_i, g, e, y) \in Q \times G \times E \times Y,
\end{aligned}
$$
where $q_i$ is a synonymous-window residual coordinate, $g$ is a gene identity used for blocking rather than as a fitted test-gene dummy, $e$ is a prime-editor synonymous edit row, and $y$ is the observed fitness readout.  The gene-block rule is part of the scientific content of the certificate: a positive held-out result, if present in the underlying audit table, would be a cross-gene synonymous-window signal rather than a within-gene identifier effect.

\paragraph{Admissible reading.}
The admissible reading is narrow.  The human prime-editor run can support only a finite-row statement about synonymous perturbation fitness prediction on the audited rows.  It does not establish that the same $q_i$ first survives a direct translation readout $T_j$, and therefore it cannot be read as a completed $Q_i \to T_j \to P_k$ mediation chain.  The linked protein-abundance mediation run gives a separate statistical surface for modeled per-protein tAI and PAXdb abundance across $12$ organisms, with organism-specific joined counts such as $19053$ human proteins, $19395$ mouse proteins, $5815$ yeast proteins, and $3739$ Escherichia coli K-12 MG1655 proteins.  That linked surface reports positive bounded mediation fractions in a modelled-abundance setting, for example $M^{QTP}=0.27206289160119157$ for human and $M^{QTP}=0.9491551041216973$ for yeast.  Those numbers are useful context for why a perturbation readout is worth auditing, but they are not imported as proof that this prime-editor fitness table measures translation or protein abundance.

\paragraph{Translation boundary.}
The translation-contact ledger remains separate from the human prime-editor fitness rows.  A modeled-tAI bounded-coupling audit computed nine $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates across $3$ organisms and found $81$ entries above the threshold $0.01$, with maximum absolute modeled coupling $3801961.031607896$.  The same audit explicitly records that $3$ organisms are insufficient for powered survival when the requested claim requires at least $40$ matched species.  Consequently, the prime-editor chapter cannot upgrade a synonymous fitness association into ribosome occupancy, translation efficiency, or protein-abundance realization unless a same-row or properly matched translation contact supplies the missing $Q_i \to T_j$ step.  A direct $Q_i \to y$ perturbation-fitness association is therefore kept as a perturbation-layer result, not as a translation-mediated protein-output result.

\paragraph{No-promotion rule.}
The local boundary is part of the result.  Synonymous edits can affect a measured human fitness readout in a prime-editor assay, but the finite certificate does not license claims about folded structure, molecular function, physical admissibility, mechanism, organism-wide selection, or a universal codon law.  It also does not license CUN/UUR directionality, an organism-general sign rule, or a population-level law from a single human perturbation surface.  Any such claim requires its own layer-matched contact and its own controlled statistic.  In particular, translation requires ribosome occupancy, translation efficiency, tAI/stAI, or an equivalent translation-layer readout; protein realization requires matched protein abundance or a perturbation-level protein output; folding and physical admissibility require structure or physical assays; function and phenotype require functional or organismal readouts.  The present finite row set stays below those thresholds.

\paragraph{Certificate statement.}
The certified statement is therefore the following.  On a finite human prime-editor synonymous perturbation surface with $1822$ genes, $9000$ main synonymous edit rows, $24000$ compact synonymous rows, a per-cell-line cap of $3000$ rows, and $B=200$ resampling units, the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ window family is admissible as a controlled perturbation-fitness coordinate family under gene-block held-out evaluation.  The certificate records the existence and scale of the powered audit, the cross-gene split rule, and the no-promotion boundary.  It does not assert a completed translation-mediated protein-abundance chain for these rows, because the finite fact record supplied here does not include a same-row translation readout, a matched protein-abundance readout, a mediation fraction, a held-out $R^2$ increment, a likelihood increment, or a null-calibrated $p$-value for this exact prime-editor surface.
