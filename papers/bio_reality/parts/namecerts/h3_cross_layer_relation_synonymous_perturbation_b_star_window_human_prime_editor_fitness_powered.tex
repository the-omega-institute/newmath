\paragraph{Controlled readback.}
The object tested here is not a codon-count association in an unstratified table.  It is a same-claim human perturbation table in which the held-out folds are gene-block folds.  The run explicitly records that direct gene dummy fixed effects are not inserted into the predictive held-out design because test genes are absent from the training folds.  This matters for the interpretation of any $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ window signal: the model is not allowed to win by memorizing a gene label and then replaying that label in the test block.  The admissible readback is instead a finite map from synonymous edit rows to a held-out fitness readout, with gene identity used to define the split boundary rather than as a direct predictive coordinate.

\paragraph{Translation-contact boundary.}
The translation-survival contact also remains bounded.  The modeled-tAI audit computed the same $9$ residual coordinates in a $61$-sense-codon space across $3$ organisms and found $81$ entries above the bounded coupling threshold $0.01$, with maximum absolute modeled coupling $3801961.031607896$.  That record is explicitly underpowered for a powered survival claim because $3$ organisms are not enough for the requested species-level survival standard.  The prime-editor chapter therefore cannot treat modeled tAI, stAI, or residual-window geometry as observed ribosome occupancy.  At most, these records show that the coordinate system has finite contact routes and that the human perturbation table is large enough to be evaluated as a bounded synonymous-edit fitness surface.

\paragraph{Local claim.}
Within this chapter, the supported statement is deliberately narrow.  A human prime-editor synonymous-edit table has been assembled and evaluated on a finite gene-block held-out surface with $1822$ genes, $9000$ main synonymous rows, $24000$ compact synonymous rows, a $3000$ row cap per cell-line analysis, and $200$ resampling units.  The chapter may discuss the existence of a bounded $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ window perturbation audit on human synonymous edits.  It may not state that the residual coordinates realize translation, protein abundance, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, organismal phenotype, mechanism, selection, or a universal biological law.  Those stronger readings would require separate layer-specific contacts: measured translation rows for translation, matched protein rows for protein abundance, structure or physical assays for folding and admissibility, and functional or phenotype assays for biological function.

\paragraph{Null interpretation.}
The finite row counts do not by themselves encode a positive biological direction.  The safest mathematical reading is that the experiment has reached the row-complete audit stage for a human synonymous perturbation surface, while effect direction, effect size, and any mechanism-level promotion remain outside the numbers supplied in this record.  If later tables add signed coefficients, held-out $\Delta R^2$, description-length differences, or calibrated $p$-values, those quantities can refine the verdict.  In the present finite record, the durable content is the controlled perturbation surface and the no-promotion rule separating synonymous fitness prediction from translation, protein, structure, function, mechanism, and universality.
