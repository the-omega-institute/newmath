\subsection{Human prime-editor synonymous-window fitness contact}\label{sec:human-prime-editor-synonymous-window-fitness-contact}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite surface.}
This record treats the human prime-editor screen as a finite perturbation contact for synonymous coding changes, not as a direct protein-output or mechanism contact. The observed row surface is a set of synonymous editing rows grouped by gene-block folds. The finite run used $1822$ genes, $9000$ synonymous rows in the main analysis table, and $24000$ synonymous rows in the compact synonymous table. The analysis cap was $3000$ rows per cell line, and the null calibration used $B=200$ resampling draws. The reported run identifier is \texttt{b1a1a8c7baae}, with runtime $295.329$ seconds.

\paragraph{Coordinate discipline.}
Let $Q$ denote the nine-dimensional $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual coordinate family used by the surrounding translation-boundary records. The coordinate list is
$$
\begin{aligned}
Q=\{&\mathrm{K\_AAA},\mathrm{Arg\_AGR},\mathrm{Ile\_AUA},\mathrm{Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR},\mathrm{Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG},\\
&\mathrm{Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY},\mathrm{Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG},\mathrm{Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY},f_{3}\text{-}\mathrm{stress}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The human prime-editor table is therefore read as a finite perturbation matrix whose rows may carry synonymous-window residual values, edited gene identity, and observed fitness response. It is not read as a direct translation-efficiency matrix unless a matched translation readout is present in the same controlled surface.

\paragraph{Held-out design.}
The finite certificate uses gene-block held-out folds. Direct gene dummy fixed effects are deliberately absent from the predictive held-out design because test genes are unseen in train folds. Thus the admissible interpretation is out-of-gene-block prediction from synonymous-window coordinates and controls, not within-gene memorization. In symbolic form, the tested surface has the form
$$
\begin{aligned}
(q_i, g, e, y) \in Q \times G \times E \times Y,
\end{aligned}
$$
where $q_i$ is a residual coordinate evaluated on the edited coding window, $g$ is a gene block used only for fold assignment, $e$ is the synonymous prime-editor event, and $y$ is the measured fitness readout. The fold rule prevents a fitted gene label from being promoted to a biological explanation.

\paragraph{Relation to translation-boundary evidence.}
The broader translation-readout route supplies a useful guardrail for this perturbation chapter. In the modeled tAI contact, only $3$ organisms were present, while the powered survival route requires at least $40$ matched species. That contact nevertheless computed all $61$ sense codons, found $81$ modeled entries above the coupling threshold $0.01$, and reported maximum absolute modeled coupling $3801961.031607896$. Those numbers show that a bounded modeled-readout surface exists, but they do not turn the human prime-editor fitness rows into observed ribosome occupancy or translation efficiency. The same distinction is essential here: a synonymous edit can be scored for fitness, but a $Q_i \to y$ association is not automatically a $Q_i \to T_j$ translation survival step.

\paragraph{Protein-abundance comparison.}
A separate protein-abundance mediation run joined $12$ organisms and used the same nine residual coordinates. Its human rows contained $19053$ joined proteins, and the human mediation decomposition reported
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{QP} &= 0.04454565118019448,\\
R^2_{QP\mid T} &= 0.03242643251185274,\\
R^2_{\mathrm{indirect}} &= 0.012119218668341744,\\
M^{QTP} &= 0.27206289160119157.
\end{aligned}
$$
These values are useful as a scale comparison for how a same-coordinate protein-abundance mediation surface is reported when matched protein abundance and modeled translation readout are explicitly joined. The human prime-editor screen does not itself contain that joined $Q_i \to T_j \to P_k$ chain. Its readout is fitness under synonymous perturbation, so the chapter keeps protein abundance, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, mechanism, and universality outside the local claim.

\paragraph{Structural-order guardrail.}
The structural-order audit identified $f_{3}$-stress as the only significant coordinate under its fold-level sign-flip gate, with weighted held-out $\Delta R^2=0.0031744502294756994$ and sign-flip right-tail probability $0.03125$. Other listed coordinates did not pass that gate; for example, $\mathrm{Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR}$ had weighted held-out $\Delta R^2=-0.0003205780368125179$ with sign-flip probability $0.779296875$, and $\mathrm{Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY}$ had weighted held-out $\Delta R^2=-0.00045340330387383447$ with sign-flip probability $0.529296875$. This comparison prevents the prime-editor chapter from treating all synonymous residual axes as equally supported. If the prime-editor rows favor one axis, that must be stated as a finite perturbation result for the edited-row fitness surface, not as a general law of coding sequence.

\paragraph{Admissible statement.}
The finite human contact supports the following bounded statement. A synonymous-window prime-editor screen with $1822$ genes and $9000$ main synonymous rows was analyzed under gene-block held-out folds, with $B=200$ null draws and a compact synonymous table of $24000$ rows. The admissible object is the controlled relation between $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ synonymous-window residual coordinates and the measured fitness response of edited human rows. The object may be compared with translation and protein-abundance mediation records only through explicitly joined contacts. A direct residual-to-fitness relation is not a translation claim, not a protein-abundance claim, and not evidence of folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, mechanism, organism-level phenotype law, or universality.

\paragraph{Local refusal rule.}
The chapter uses a strict promotion rule. No translation readout follows from the human prime-editor rows unless matched ribosome occupancy, translation efficiency, modeled tAI/stAI, or another declared translation contact is joined and controlled. No protein realization follows unless a matched protein-abundance contact is joined after the translation step. No folding, physical admissibility, function, mechanism, selection law, or universal biological rule follows from the synonymous perturbation table alone. The row counts and fold design make the perturbation contact finite and auditable; they do not supply the missing higher-layer contacts.
