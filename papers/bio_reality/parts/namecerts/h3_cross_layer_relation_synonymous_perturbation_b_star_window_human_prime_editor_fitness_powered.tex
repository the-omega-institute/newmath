\subsection{Human prime-editor fitness windows for synonymous residual coordinates}
\label{sec:human-prime-editor-fitness-windows-synonymous-residuals}

\paragraph{Finite row surface.}
This record treats the human prime-editor screen as a finite perturbation surface rather than as an unrestricted claim about translation or protein realization.  The rows are synonymous edit windows joined to gene identity and cell-line analysis strata, and the admissible signal is a held-out fitness readout after the synonymous edit has been encoded by the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ window coordinates.  The registered run used $9000$ synonymous rows in the analysis surface and a compact representation with $24000$ synonymous encoded rows, spanning $1822$ genes.  The per-cell-line analysis cap was $3000$ rows, the resampling budget was $B=200$, and the run completed in $295.329$ seconds.  The row accounting is therefore
$$
\begin{aligned}
  n_{\mathrm{syn}} &= 9000,\\
  n_{\mathrm{compact}} &= 24000,\\
  n_{\mathrm{genes}} &= 1822,\\
  B &= 200,\\
  n_{\mathrm{cell\ line\ cap}} &= 3000.
\end{aligned}
$$
The design note is part of the finite claim: held-out folds are gene-block folds, and direct gene dummy effects are not placed in the predictive fit because the test genes are unseen in training folds.  Thus the comparison is not a within-gene memorization exercise; it asks whether a finite synonymous-window encoding carries predictive information that transfers across blocked gene partitions.

\paragraph{Coordinate meaning.}
The coordinates used here are not amino-acid changes.  They are residual synonymous-window coordinates derived from the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ coding-sequence basis after amino-acid identity has been held fixed.  The relevant coordinate family is the nine-dimensional residual list used by the associated translation and protein-abundance audits:
$$
\begin{aligned}
  Q = \{&\mathrm{K\_AAA},\ \mathrm{Arg\_AGR},\ \mathrm{Ile\_AUA},\
  \mathrm{Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR},\ \mathrm{Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG},\\
  &\mathrm{Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY},\ \mathrm{Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG},\
  \mathrm{Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY},\ f3\_\mathrm{stress}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The prime-editor surface supplies a perturbational fitness contact for synonymous edits, while the coordinate list supplies the finite coding-sequence residual basis.  The admissible readback is therefore a relation between edited synonymous windows and held-out fitness response, not a direct assertion that a coordinate changes ribosome occupancy, mature protein amount, folding, or molecular function.

\paragraph{Cross-layer comparison surface.}
The same coordinate family has a bounded cross-layer mediation audit in a matched CDS/protein-abundance setting.  In the human rows of that audit, $19053$ joined proteins were used.  After amino-acid composition, GC3, CDS length, and the $M$-density baseline were controlled, the human protein-abundance decomposition reported
$$
\begin{aligned}
  R^2_{QP} &= 0.04454565118019448,\\
  R^2_{QP\mid T} &= 0.03242643251185274,\\
  R^2_{\mathrm{indirect}} &= 0.012119218668341744,\\
  M^{QTP} &= 0.27206289160119157.
\end{aligned}
$$
The same human block had residual degrees of freedom $19030$, rank $9$ for the residual coordinate matrix, rank $1$ for the modeled translation readout, and rank $10$ after adjoining the translation column.  Its residual energy for the modeled translation readout was $1.174468648693672$, and the protein-abundance residual energy was $30769.5367150478$.  These numbers give a nearby bounded comparison surface: the prime-editor record asks about perturbational fitness, while the matched proteomics audit shows that the coordinate family can be placed in a controlled $Q \to T \to P$ decomposition without promoting the decomposition to a causal mechanism.

\paragraph{Translation-readout gate.}
The translation layer remains a gate, not an automatic consequence of the synonymous edit.  The modeled tAI survival audit computed the same nine residual coordinates for three organisms and obtained $81$ entries above the bounded-contact threshold $0.01$, with maximum absolute modeled coupling $3801961.031607896$.  That run used only $3$ organisms, so it explicitly remained underpowered for a population-level survival claim requiring at least $40$ matched species.  In symbols, the finite distinction is
$$
\begin{aligned}
  \#\{(i,j): |S_{ij}| > 0.01\} &= 81,\\
  \max_{i,j}|S_{ij}| &= 3801961.031607896,\\
  n_{\mathrm{organisms}} &= 3 < 40.
\end{aligned}
$$
For the human prime-editor chapter this means that a synonymous-window fitness association is allowed to be recorded as perturbational evidence, but it is not allowed to bypass the translation-readout step.  A direct $Q_i \to$ fitness relation is not the same object as a controlled $Q_i \to T_j \to P_k$ mediation relation.

\paragraph{Perturbational anchors.}
Two neighboring perturbational records show why the human screen is kept in a finite-window class rather than interpreted as a universal codon law.  In an \emph{E. coli} mRFP protein-output dataset, the same window style used $1459$ variants with $B=200$ and reported a positive improvement $\Delta R^2=0.05339906007191191$, $\Delta \mathrm{DL}=19.089327473282296$ bits, and matched-null and within-null tail probabilities both equal to $0.004975124378109453$.  In a yeast synonymous-mutation dataset, $1783$ synonymous mutants across $20$ genes were used with $B=200$; the real codon-stability coefficient did not absorb the window signal, with real-versus-proxy correlation $0.39717918221186976$.  These perturbational anchors support the choice to represent the human prime-editor data as a controlled finite-row window surface, while also warning against turning one organism, assay, or sign into a general biological rule.

\paragraph{Verdict for this record.}
The human prime-editor run is usable as a finite perturbational fitness chapter because it has a concrete row surface, gene-block held-out design, bounded resampling budget, and recorded scale: $9000$ synonymous analysis rows, $24000$ compact encoded rows, $1822$ genes, $B=200$, and a $3000$ row cap per cell line.  The admissible claim is that the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ synonymous-window encoding has been evaluated on a human prime-editor fitness surface under a blocked predictive design.  The chapter does not claim that the edited synonymous coordinate has been shown to alter translation, ribosome occupancy, protein abundance, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, mechanism, selection, organismal phenotype, or a universal biological law.  Any such stronger statement requires its own matched contact: a translation readout for $Q_i \to T_j$, a protein-abundance readout for $Q_i \to T_j \to P_k$, and separate layer-specific measurements for folding, physical state, function, or phenotype.
