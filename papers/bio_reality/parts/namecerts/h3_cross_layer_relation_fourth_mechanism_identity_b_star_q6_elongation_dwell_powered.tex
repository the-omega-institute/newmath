\subsection{B$^*_{Q6}$ $f_3$-stress and the elongation-dwell boundary}
\label{sec:bioreality-b-star-q6-f3-stress-elongation-dwell}

\paragraph{Finite surface.}
This BioReality record concerns the finite residual coordinate surface obtained by restricting coding-sequence codon counts to the B$^*_{Q6}$ $f_3$-stress coordinate after amino-acid projection, then asking whether that coordinate remains visible in a translation-layer readout. The relevant carrier is not an unrestricted biological story: it is the finite table whose rows are organism-matched coding sequences, whose codon-side columns include the $f_3$-paired synonymous imbalance, and whose readout-side columns are bounded translation measurements or explicitly modelled translation-weight rows. The conjectural break of symmetry is local: if a codon $c$ and its $f_3$ partner $c^{f_3}$ have equal context weight, then the residual coordinate has no translation-dynamical asymmetry to read. The coordinate can become visible only in rows where the context tRNA or wobble weight separates the pair, written as $w_c \neq w_{c^{f_3}}$.

\paragraph{Codon support.}
The finite $f_3$ saturation computation supplies the codon-side boundary. The verified median-R union ACA carrier contains exactly 20 codons,
$$
\begin{aligned}
M = \{&\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{ACA},\mathrm{ACG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\\
&\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
After removing the ACA and ACG boundary pair, the saturated $f_3$ R-surface has 18 codons,
$$
\begin{aligned}
\operatorname{Sat}_{f_3}(R)=\{&\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\\
&\mathrm{CUU},\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The reported $f_3$ boundary set is empty. Thus the codon-side object is finite and sharp: the stress coordinate is not being inferred from an open-ended sequence class, but from a closed residual codon surface with a 20-row support before the ACA/ACG removal and an 18-row saturated support afterward.

\paragraph{Readout rule.}
The readout admitted here is only the controlled survival entry $S_{f3\text{-}stress,T_j}$ for a translation quantity $T_j$, such as tAI, stAI, ribosome occupancy, or translation efficiency. The chapter therefore uses the following local rule:
$$
\begin{aligned}
S_{f3\text{-}stress,T_j} > 0
\end{aligned}
$$
means that the $f_3$-stress coordinate improves a translation readout after amino-acid composition, organism identity, GC3, coding-sequence length, M-density, matched mRNA abundance where applicable, rank sufficiency, and simpler-codon-baseline controls. If the entry is non-positive, absent, or rank-degenerate, the record remains null at the translation boundary. A three-organism all-1.0 controlled matrix is therefore not counted as survival; it is a saturated-rank boundary and carries no positive biological reading.

\paragraph{Empirical finite rows.}
The powered fourth-mechanism assay for elongation dwell has status passed, uses 5 folds, is scoped to \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}, and reports the conclusion \emph{partial}. This is a finite positive contact only in the narrow sense that the elongation-dwell candidate was not eliminated in the yeast assay. It is not a full translation-survival theorem for all organisms or all readout classes. The surrounding powered assays keep the interpretation constrained. The optimality-axis sufficiency run over 11 computed organisms out of 16 requested, with 5 folds, at least 500 proteins per organism, and 41 synonymous design columns, concluded that a fourth mechanism is present. The per-organism axis-strength spectrum used the same 11 of 16 organism panel, 5 folds, and 41 synonymous design columns; it reported d\_resid4 as the leading average axis and as dominant in all 11 computed organisms. Domain means were marked descriptive because the panel was imbalanced, with $n=1$ archaea, $n=2$ bacteria, and $n=8$ eukaryotes, and the largest GC3 rank effect was d\_usage with $\rho=0.136$.

\paragraph{Fourth-axis discrimination.}
The elongation-dwell result is best read against the negative fourth-axis alternatives. The mRNA-stability assay passed but concluded \emph{fourth\_axis\_not\_mrna\_stability}: yeast d\_opt\_halflife had low alignment with abundance d\_resid4 under the fixed gates, so mRNA stability was excluded as the dominant fourth-axis identity in that assay. The codon-pair assay passed with conclusion \emph{not\_codon\_pair}, using 5 folds, 41 synonymous design columns, at least 500 proteins per organism, and 50 shuffle-null replicates. The translation-accuracy assay passed with conclusion \emph{not\_accuracy}, using 11 computed organisms out of 16 requested, 5 folds, 41 synonymous design columns, and 200 permutations. These finite rows do not prove that elongation dwell is the unique mechanism. They do show that, within the supplied powered tests, the yeast elongation-dwell candidate remains as a partial fourth-mechanism identity while three named competitors are not supported under their corresponding gates.

\paragraph{Relation to the residual spectrum.}
The same finite panel places the $f_3$-stress candidate inside a larger residual spectrum. The universal residual-axis assay over 11 computed organisms out of 16 requested, with 5 folds, at least 500 proteins per organism, and 41 synonymous design columns, identified the dominant non-tRNA residual optimality axis as f3\_translation\_selection. The third-axis identity run reported d\_perp aligned to usage\_frequency\_within\_family, with mean cosine $0.128$ and one-sided sign-test $p=0.000488$. These numbers justify treating $f_3$-stress as a serious translation-adjacent residual coordinate, but they do not by themselves supply a measured ribosome-dwell row for every organism. They are coordinate and spectrum evidence, not a license to promote the coordinate beyond the readout actually computed.

\paragraph{Local verdict.}
The finite-row verdict is therefore bounded and asymmetric. On the support side, the $f_3$ codon surface is closed by a 20-codon M carrier, an 18-codon saturated R carrier, and an empty $f_3$ boundary set. On the mechanism side, the powered assays leave elongation dwell as a partial yeast fourth-mechanism identity while excluding mRNA stability, codon-pair structure, and translation accuracy as the named dominant alternatives in their supplied tests. On the translation-readout side, the conjecture still requires a non-vacuous $S_{f3\text{-}stress,T_j}$ entry on organism-matched codon, wobble-weight, ribosome or translation-efficiency, and matched mRNA rows before any stronger statement is allowed.

\paragraph{No-promotion boundary.}
This record does not claim that the B$^*_{Q6}$ $f_3$-stress coordinate causes translation, protein abundance, folding, localization, stability, function, phenotype, physical admissibility, a mechanism in the full causal sense, or a universal biological law. Modeled tAI or stAI weights are bounded codon-adaptation readouts, not observed ribosome occupancy. Matched mRNA abundance is a control covariate for translation-efficiency residualization, not a translation output. Ribosome-profiling or translation-efficiency data, if later attached, can test only the declared translation row unless a separate layer-specific contact supplies protein, structure, physical, functional, perturbational, or phenotypic evidence. The positive content of this chapter is exactly the finite partial elongation-dwell status under the declared controls and the refusal to read beyond that finite row.
