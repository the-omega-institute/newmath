\paragraph{Readout rule.}
The local statistic has the form $S_{f3\text{-}\mathrm{stress},T_j}$, where $T_j$ is a bounded translation readout such as tAI, stAI, ribosome occupancy, or translation efficiency. The support condition is not the existence of codon topology alone. It is the existence of a non-rank-degenerate, controlled survival entry after amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, CDS length, $M$-density, matched mRNA, rank, and simpler-baseline controls. In schematic form, the required positive row is
$$
\begin{aligned}
S_{f3\text{-}\mathrm{stress},T_j} &> 0,\\
T_j &\in \{\mathrm{tAI},\mathrm{stAI},\mathrm{ribosome\ occupancy},\mathrm{translation\ efficiency}\},\\
Z&=\{\mathrm{amino\ acid\ composition},\mathrm{organism},\mathrm{GC3},\mathrm{length},M\text{-}\mathrm{density},\mathrm{mRNA},\mathrm{rank},\mathrm{simpler\ baselines}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
If the controlled entry is non-positive, or if the matrix is all $1.0$ only because the rank is saturated, the packet is a null boundary rather than support.

\paragraph{Boundary of interpretation.}
The present finite row allows the sentence: in \textit{S. cerevisiae}, the fourth-axis identity has a passed five-fold elongation-dwell record with a partial conclusion, and that record is consistent with an $f_3$-stress translation-readout route when wobble or tRNA weights break the $f_3$ pairing. It does not allow the stronger sentence that $f_3$ stress controls translation in general. It also does not allow any conclusion about folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, localization, stability, phenotype, or universality. Those layers require their own contacts and their own controlled finite statistics. Even within translation, modeled tAI or stAI weights are bounded codon-adaptation rows, not direct ribosome-footprint observations unless a measured ribosome or translation-efficiency contact is joined on the same organism or CDS rows.
