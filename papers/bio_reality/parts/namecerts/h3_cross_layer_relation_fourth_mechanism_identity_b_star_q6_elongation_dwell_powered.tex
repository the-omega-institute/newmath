\subsection{A partial elongation-dwell identity for the fourth $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ mechanism}
\label{sec:bq6-elongation-dwell-fourth-mechanism}

\paragraph{Finite coordinate surface.}
This record concerns the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinate after amino-acid projection, restricted to the $f_3$-stress direction and evaluated only against translation-layer readouts.  The finite saturation row fixes the relevant codon boundary before any biological interpretation is attempted.  The reported $M = \mathrm{Med}(R) \cup \mathrm{ACA}$ surface contains
$$
\begin{aligned}
M = \{&\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{ACA},\mathrm{ACG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\\
&\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The $f_3$-saturated $R$ row removes exactly $\mathrm{ACA}$ and $\mathrm{ACG}$ from that twenty-codon list, leaving eighteen codons:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{Sat}_{f_3}(R)=\{&\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\\
&\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The recorded $f_3$ boundary set is empty.  Thus the tested fourth-mechanism question is not whether a new codon class appears at the boundary, but whether the residual $f_3$ imbalance left after amino-acid quotienting is visible in a translation readout when wobble or tRNA weights break third-position symmetry.

\paragraph{Powered fourth-mechanism row.}
The elongation-dwell assay is finite and organism-scoped: experiment \texttt{\detokenize{b\_star\_q6\_fourth\_mechanism\_elongation\_dwell\_powered}} passed with five folds on \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}, using seed \texttt{\detokenize{sha256:f3d542c80eab2ed48839c11e4a4ef75c2286abfe31c9327f7afef47ce7335ab3}}.  Its conclusion is \texttt{partial}.  In this chapter that word is interpreted literally: the yeast row gives evidence that elongation dwell can account for part of the fourth residual mechanism, but it does not close the identity of the fourth axis, does not imply a universal law, and does not license downstream protein or phenotype claims.

\paragraph{Comparison against adjacent mechanism tests.}
The partial elongation-dwell call is sharpened by the other finite rows.  The fourth-axis mRNA-stability assay passed with five folds and classified the tested axis as \texttt{\detokenize{fourth\_axis\_not\_mrna\_stability}}; its stated reason is that yeast \texttt{\detokenize{d\_opt\_halflife}} has low alignment with abundance \texttt{\detokenize{d\_resid4}} under the fixed gates.  The codon-pair assay passed with five folds, fifty shuffle-null replicates, and conclusion \texttt{\detokenize{not\_codon\_pair}}.  The translation-accuracy assay passed with five folds over eleven computed organisms out of sixteen requested organisms, with two hundred permutations and conclusion \texttt{\detokenize{not\_accuracy}}.  These three negative classifications make the elongation-dwell row more specific: the present evidence is not a generic fourth-axis placeholder, nor is it currently assigned to mRNA stability, codon-pair structure, or translation accuracy.

\paragraph{Axis context.}
The surrounding powered panel reports that three axes are not sufficient: the optimality-axis sufficiency run passed over eleven computed organisms out of sixteen requested organisms, with five folds, at least five hundred proteins per organism, and forty-one synonymous design columns; its conclusion was \texttt{\detokenize{fourth\_mechanism\_present}}.  The per-organism strength spectrum also passed on eleven computed organisms and forty-one synonymous design columns, with \texttt{\detokenize{d\_resid4}} leading all eleven per-organism dominant counts.  The same spectrum records the average ordering as \texttt{\detokenize{d\_resid4}} before \texttt{\detokenize{d\_tRNA}}, notes the imbalanced domain counts $\{\mathrm{archaea}:1,\mathrm{bacteria}:2,\mathrm{eukaryote}:8\}$, and reports the largest GC3 rank effect as $\rho=0.136$ for \texttt{\detokenize{d\_usage}}.  This places the elongation-dwell result inside a finite residual-axis analysis rather than inside an unconstrained story about translation.

\paragraph{Mechanistic pressure from tRNA and wobble asymmetry.}
The tRNA-poverty row supplies a separate finite pressure on the same translation layer: forty organisms were computed out of forty-six requested organisms, six were skipped, the alpha level was $0.05$, and the permutation count was $1000$.  The call was that $R$ sense codons are systematically tRNA-poor under GtRNAdb tGCN with dos Reis wobble weights.  This does not itself measure ribosome dwell time, but it explains why an $f_3$-paired synonymous imbalance can become testable only where the weights $w_c$ and $w_{c^{f_3}}$ are not equal.  In formula form, the coordinate is allowed to become a translation-layer distinction only under a broken-weight condition such as
$$
\begin{aligned}
w_c \neq w_{c^{f_3}}.
\end{aligned}
$$
Without such a break, the $f_3$ pair is read as a rank-degenerate or null boundary rather than as survival.

\paragraph{Relation to the earlier residual axes.}
The first two powered translation-selection rows constrain the background interpretation.  The universal-core tRNA-adaptation assay passed over eleven computed organisms out of sixteen requested organisms with five folds, at least five hundred proteins per organism, and forty-one synonymous design columns.  The residual optimal-axis assay passed on the same organism count and design size and identified the dominant non-tRNA residual direction as \texttt{\detokenize{f3\_translation\_selection}}.  The two-axis closure assay then reported \texttt{\detokenize{third\_axis\_present}}, with the stated reason that \texttt{\detokenize{d\_perp}} has systematically positive held-out $R^2$ and mutually same-signed organism directions.  The third-axis identity row gave a provisional alignment to within-family usage frequency, with mean cosine $0.128$ and one-sided sign-test $p=0.000488$; the mechanism row classified the same axis as selection rather than GC, not wobble-clustered, with CSC or positional identity still needing a separate contact.  These values make the fourth-axis claim more disciplined: elongation dwell is being tested after the tRNA, $f_3$ translation-selection, and third-axis residual structure have already been separated.

\paragraph{Readout rule.}
The bounded readout for this chapter is a controlled survival entry $S_{f3\text{-}stress,T_j}$ for a translation-layer row such as tAI, stAI, ribosome occupancy, or translation efficiency.  The intended statistic is positive only when the $f_3$-stress coordinate improves the translation readout after amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, coding-sequence length, $M$-density, matched mRNA abundance, rank, and simpler-baseline controls.  Symbolically, the positive case has the form
$$
\begin{aligned}
S_{f3\text{-}stress,T_j} > 0,
\end{aligned}
$$
under the declared controls.  The current finite row is weaker than that full measured-readout claim: it is a passed, five-fold, yeast-scoped, partial elongation-dwell identity result.  It is therefore suitable as evidence that elongation dwell belongs among the live fourth-mechanism candidates, not as evidence that every organism has a nonzero measured translation-survival entry.

\paragraph{Local non-promotion boundary.}
No translation realization, ribosome-occupancy realization, protein abundance, folding, physical admissibility, biological function, localization, stability, phenotype, mechanism, or universality follows from the coordinate geometry, from modeled tAI or stAI weights, or from the partial elongation-dwell row alone.  A stronger statement would require same-organism and same-CDS joined codon counts, wobble or tRNA weights, measured translation readouts, and matched mRNA controls, followed by the controlled statistic above.  The present chapter therefore records a finite partial mechanism identity for yeast elongation dwell and keeps all higher-layer interpretations outside the claim.
