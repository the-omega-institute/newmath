\paragraph{Why the fourth-axis interpretation is non-vacuous.}
Several surrounding finite rows make the partial dwell call meaningful.  A three-axis sufficiency test over $11$ computed organisms out of $16$ requested organisms, with $5$ folds, at least $500$ proteins per organism, and $41$ synonymous design columns, concluded \texttt{fourth\_mechanism\_present}.  The axis-strength spectrum was computed on the same $11$-organism, $5$-fold, $41$-column design and found the captured spectra led by \texttt{d\_resid4} on average, with per-organism dominant counts $\{\texttt{d\_resid4}:11\}$.  The reported domain counts were imbalanced, $\{\text{archaea}:1,\text{bacteria}:2,\text{eukaryote}:8\}$, and the largest GC3 rank effect was small, \texttt{d\_usage} $\rho=0.136$.  These numbers place the dwell row in a genuine fourth-axis setting rather than in a saturated three-axis restatement.

Thus the local scientific content is not merely that a fourth residual coordinate exists; it is that mRNA half-life, codon-pair structure, and translation accuracy were not the selected identities under these assays, while elongation dwell retained only a partial positive call.

This supports a tRNA-supply route for $R$ avoidance, but it remains a supply-side contact, not a direct ribosome-dwell measurement.
