\subsection{Fourth-axis elongation-dwell contact for the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ $f_3$-stress coordinate}
\label{subsec:b-star-q6-elongation-dwell-powered}

\paragraph{Finite surface.}
The object studied here is the finite coding-sequence residual surface obtained after amino-acid projection, restricted to the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ $f_3$-stress coordinate and to translation-layer rows that can be matched to organism-specific codon usage, tRNA or wobble weights, and a readout for elongation behavior.  The relevant third-position involution is not treated as biologically active merely because it is available on codons.  It becomes testable only where a context weight separates a codon $c$ from its $f_3$ mate $c^{f_3}$, so that $w_c \ne w_{c^{f_3}}$ and the residual imbalance is still visible after amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, CDS length, $M$-density, and transcript-level controls are imposed.

The finite saturation row fixes the local codon domain.  In run $76d3dc408a57$, the row $M=\operatorname{Med}(R)\cup ACA$ contains
$$
\begin{aligned}
M=\{&AAA,AAG,ACA,ACG,AGA,AGG,AUA,AUG,CUA,CUC,CUG,CUU,\\
&UAA,UAG,UCA,UCG,UGA,UGG,UUA,UUG\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The $f_3$-saturated $R$ part removes $ACA$ and $ACG$ and leaves
$$
\begin{aligned}
\operatorname{Sat}_{f_3}(R)=\{&AAA,AAG,AGA,AGG,AUA,AUG,CUA,CUC,CUG,CUU,\\
&UAA,UAG,UCA,UCG,UGA,UGG,UUA,UUG\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The reported $f_3$ boundary is empty.  Thus the chapter does not begin from a loose biological analogy; it begins from a closed finite codon surface in which the residual coordinate is already defined before any translation-row interpretation is attempted.

The run status is \emph{passed}; the fold count is $5$; the organism row is \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}; and the conclusion is \emph{partial}.  The deterministic seed recorded for the computation is
$$
\begin{aligned}

The word \emph{partial} is essential.  It means that the yeast row supplies a bounded positive contact between the fourth residual direction and an elongation-dwell interpretation, but it does not close the interpretation across organisms, readout technologies, or downstream layers.  The result is therefore a finite identification pressure, not a universal biological law.

\paragraph{Position among competing fourth-axis explanations.}
The same finite memory separates this row from several neighboring explanations.  The three-axis sufficiency assay, run $2436cae9d455$, computed $11$ organisms out of $16$ requested organisms with $5$ folds and $41$ synonymous design columns, and concluded that a fourth mechanism is present.  The per-organism axis-strength spectrum, run $0146012d8909$, also computed $11$ organisms out of $16$ requested organisms, again with $5$ folds, a minimum of $500$ proteins per organism, and $41$ synonymous design columns.  In that spectrum, the captured directions are led by $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$ on average, followed by $d_{\mathrm{tRNA}}$; all $11$ per-organism dominant calls are assigned to $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$.  The domain composition is imbalanced, with $1$ archaeal, $2$ bacterial, and $8$ eukaryotic rows, and the largest GC3 rank effect reported there is $\rho=0.136$ for $d_{\mathrm{usage}}$.

The competing fourth-axis identity assays narrow the interpretation.  The mRNA-stability assay, run $377abd67b8b3$, used $5$ folds, a minimum of $500$ proteins per organism, and $41$ synonymous design columns, and concluded \emph{fourth\_axis\_not\_mrna\_stability}; its stated reason is that the yeast half-life direction has low alignment with the abundance-side $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$ under the fixed gates.  The codon-pair assay, run $b519b5ebd34d$, used $5$ folds, $41$ synonymous design columns, and $50$ shuffle-null replicates, and concluded \emph{not\_codon\_pair}.  The translation-accuracy assay, run $0dc44df59ba7$, computed $11$ organisms out of $16$ requested organisms with $5$ folds, $41$ synonymous design columns, and $200$ permutations, and concluded \emph{not\_accuracy}.  Against those excluded or unsupported alternatives, the elongation-dwell row remains only a partial positive identity, not a completed mechanism.

\paragraph{Relation to the residual spectrum.}
The elongation-dwell row also sits inside the broader $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ spectrum.  The third-axis identity assay, run $377671f96e96$, computed $11$ organisms out of $16$ requested organisms with $5$ folds, a minimum of $500$ proteins per organism, and $41$ synonymous design columns.  It provisionally identified $d_{\perp}$ with usage frequency within family, with mean cosine $0.128$ and one-sided sign-test $p=0.000488$.  The non-tRNA residual optimality assay, run $2a526c9fb7f4$, computed the same $11$ of $16$ organism scale with $5$ folds and $41$ synonymous design columns, and reported $f_3$ translation selection as the dominant residual axis.  These rows are not substitutes for the elongation-dwell assay.  They specify why an $f_3$-sensitive residual coordinate is a plausible place to look for a translation-layer effect, while the yeast elongation-dwell result supplies the chapter-specific finite contact.

\paragraph{Local readout rule.}
The admissible readback is a controlled survival entry of the form
$$
\begin{aligned}
S_{f_3\text{-stress},T_j}>0,
\end{aligned}
$$
where $T_j$ is a bounded translation readout such as modeled tAI, stAI, ribosome occupancy, translation efficiency, or an elongation-dwell proxy, and where the controls include amino-acid composition, organism identity, GC3, CDS length, $M$-density, and matched mRNA abundance when TE is estimated.  A rank-degenerate all-$1.0$ matrix is not counted as survival.  A topology-only or tAI-only coupling is not counted as a measured ribosome row.  The present chapter therefore records the yeast elongation-dwell identity as a partial, finite-row result under a passed five-fold assay, while keeping the stronger translation-efficiency and ribosome-profiling survival claims open until organism-matched rows and the declared controlled statistic are available.

\paragraph{No-promotion boundary.}
Nothing in this chapter says that the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ $f_3$-stress coordinate by itself produces translation, protein abundance, folded structure, physical admissibility, biological function, phenotype, mechanism, or universality.  The finite codon surface is a coordinate system; the tRNA and wobble weights are bounded translation-layer annotations; and the yeast elongation-dwell row is a partial identity contact.  Any stronger statement requires its own layer-matched measurement and its own controlled statistic.  In particular, a downstream claim about protein amount, localization, stability, structure, function, or organismal phenotype is blocked unless a separate contact first supplies a non-vacuous positive translation row and then supplies the corresponding downstream readout.
