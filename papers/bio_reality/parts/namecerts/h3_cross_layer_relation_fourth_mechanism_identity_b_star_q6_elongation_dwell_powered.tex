\subsection{Elongation-dwell reading of the fourth residual mechanism}
\label{sec:bioreality-q6-elongation-dwell-fourth-mechanism}

\paragraph{Finite surface.}
The object considered here is a finite coding-sequence residual surface restricted to the $B^*_{Q6}$ fourth-mechanism coordinate, with special attention to the $f_3$-stress row after synonymous codons have been quotiented by amino acid.  The $f_3$ operation is not treated as a biological force by itself.  It becomes a candidate translation-layer coordinate only when the paired codons have unequal context weights, for example when a tRNA, wobble, or dwell-time readout assigns $w_c \ne w_{c^{f_3}}$.  The finite saturation row fixes the relevant codon boundary: the $M = \mathrm{Med}_R \cup \mathrm{ACA}$ surface contains $20$ codons,
$$
\begin{aligned}
M = \{&AAA,AAG,ACA,ACG,AGA,AGG,AUA,AUG,CUA,CUC,\\
& CUG,CUU,UAA,UAG,UCA,UCG,UGA,UGG,UUA,UUG\},
\end{aligned}
$$
whereas the saturated $f_3$ image removes $ACA$ and $ACG$ and leaves $18$ codons,
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{Sat}_{f_3}(R)=\{&AAA,AAG,AGA,AGG,AUA,AUG,CUA,CUC,CUG,CUU,\\
&UAA,UAG,UCA,UCG,UGA,UGG,UUA,UUG\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The reported $f_3$ boundary is empty.  Thus the row is not a missing-codon story; it is a residual imbalance story on an already closed finite codon list.

\paragraph{Powered fourth-axis evidence.}
The powered fourth-mechanism elongation-dwell assay passed with run id $e4edcde459b7$ and seed $\mathrm{sha256{:}f3d542c80eab2ed48839c11e4a4ef75c2286abfe31c9327f7afef47ce7335ab3}$.  Its finite readout was computed for $\textit{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}$ with $5$ folds and returned the conclusion $\mathrm{partial}$.  This is a positive classification only in the narrow sense that elongation-dwell information accounts for part of the fourth residual mechanism under the assay that was run.  It is not a claim that the fourth residual axis has been reduced to a single universal kinetic law.

\paragraph{Competing mechanism exclusions.}
The same claim family gives useful guards on interpretation.  The three-axis sufficiency assay over $11$ computed organisms out of $16$ requested organisms, with $41$ synonymous design columns and at least $500$ proteins per organism, returned $\mathrm{fourth\_mechanism\_present}$.  The per-organism axis-strength spectrum, also over $11$ computed organisms out of $16$ requested organisms with $5$ folds and $41$ synonymous design columns, found that captured spectra were led by $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$ on average and that $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$ was dominant in all $11$ computed organisms.  The largest reported GC3 rank effect in that spectrum was only $\rho = 0.136$ for $d_{\mathrm{usage}}$.  Against that background, three nearby mechanistic readings were explicitly disfavored: the mRNA-stability assay passed with conclusion $\mathrm{fourth\_axis\_not\_mrna\_stability}$; the codon-pair assay passed with conclusion $\mathrm{not\_codon\_pair}$ using $5$ folds, $41$ synonymous design columns, and $50$ shuffle-null draws; and the translation-accuracy assay passed with conclusion $\mathrm{not\_accuracy}$ over $11$ computed organisms out of $16$ requested organisms with $200$ permutations.  The elongation-dwell row therefore sits in a bounded residual niche: it is a partial identity for the fourth mechanism after mRNA half-life, codon-pair structure, and translation accuracy have failed the corresponding identity tests.

\paragraph{Relation to tRNA and residual geometry.}
The supporting finite-row context is consistent with a translation-supply interpretation at the boundary.  The $R$-sense tRNA-poverty assay computed $40$ organisms out of $46$ requested organisms, skipped $6$, used $1000$ permutations at $\alpha=0.05$, and reported that $R$ sense codons are systematically tRNA-poor under GtRNAdb tGCN plus dos Reis wobble weights.  Independently, the non-tRNA residual optimality assay identified $f_3$ translation selection as the dominant residual axis over $11$ computed organisms out of $16$ requested organisms, again with $5$ folds, at least $500$ proteins per organism, and $41$ synonymous design columns.  The two-axis closure assay returned $\mathrm{third\_axis\_present}$, while the third-axis identity assay aligned $d_\perp$ to usage frequency within family with mean cosine $0.128$ and one-sided sign-test $p=0.000488$.  A separate mechanism assay then reported selection rather than GC as the surviving explanation, with GC-orthogonalized $d_\perp$ keeping significant cross-species alignment and held-out $R^2$.  These rows do not prove dwell-time causation.  They do show that the fourth residual mechanism is being evaluated in a rank-powered, translation-adjacent coordinate system rather than in an unconstrained codon-composition table.

\paragraph{Readout rule.}
For this chapter, the only admissible bounded readback is a controlled survival entry $S_{f3\text{-}\mathrm{stress},T_j}$ where $T_j$ is a translation-layer row such as tAI, stAI, ribosome occupancy, translation efficiency, or an explicitly specified elongation-dwell proxy.  The intended statistic has the form
$$
\begin{aligned}
S_{f3\text{-}\mathrm{stress},T_j} > 0
\end{aligned}
$$
after amino-acid composition, organism identity, GC3, coding-sequence length, $M$-density, matched mRNA abundance when applicable, rank sufficiency, and simpler codon baselines have been controlled.  A rank-degenerate all-$1.0$ matrix on three organisms is not survival evidence; it is a null boundary.  Likewise, modeled tAI or stAI weights are bounded codon-adaptation readbacks, not direct ribosome-footprint measurements unless a separate measured contact is attached.

\paragraph{Local refusal boundary.}
The finite result is therefore ready only as a scoped BioReality record: $B^*_{Q6}$ has a fourth residual mechanism; the powered elongation-dwell row is classified as partial in yeast; and several competing fourth-mechanism identities are rejected by their own finite assays.  Nothing in this chapter, by itself, establishes measured translation realization beyond the declared bounded row.  It also does not establish protein abundance, folded structure, physical admissibility, localization, molecular function, phenotype, causation, a general mechanism, or universality.  Each such promotion would require its own layer-matched contact and its own controlled finite statistic.  If the declared $S_{f3\text{-}\mathrm{stress},T_j}$ entries remain non-positive or rank-degenerate after the stated controls on a powered panel, then the $f_3$-stress translation-readout conjecture is falsified at this interface, even though the internal residual coordinate may remain mathematically well defined.
