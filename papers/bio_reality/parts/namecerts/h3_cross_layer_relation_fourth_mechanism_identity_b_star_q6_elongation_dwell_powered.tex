\subsection{The fourth residual mechanism: an elongation-dwell candidate in the $B^*_{Q6}$ $f_3$ coordinate}
\label{sec:h3-cross-layer-relation-fourth-mechanism-identity-b-star-q6-elongation-dwell-powered}

\paragraph{Claim surface.}
This record isolates the $B^*_{Q6}$ fourth residual mechanism at the translation-readout layer.  The carrier is a finite CDS-by-organism residual coordinate surface, restricted to the $f_3$-stress coordinate and to rows where the coding-sequence coordinate can be compared with a translation readout.  The intended biological object is not a protein phenotype, a folded state, or a function assay.  It is a controlled synonymous residual: after amino-acid projection, the paired codons related by third-position $f_3$ are compared only where organism-scoped tRNA or wobble weights can break the formal symmetry.  The readout row is therefore bounded: it is a survival entry $S_{f3\text{-}stress,T_j}$ for tAI, stAI, ribosome occupancy, or translation efficiency, after amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, CDS length, $M$-density, and matched mRNA controls have been kept in the model.

\paragraph{Finite coordinate support.}
The starting finite saturation row is explicit.  The verified $M=\operatorname{Med}(R)\cup\operatorname{ACA}$ list has $20$ codons,
$$
\begin{aligned}
M=\{&\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{ACA},\mathrm{ACG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\\
&\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The verified $f_3$ saturation removes exactly $\mathrm{ACA}$ and $\mathrm{ACG}$ from that list and leaves $18$ codons:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\operatorname{Sat}_{f_3}(R)=\{&\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\\
&\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The corresponding $f_3$ boundary is verified empty.  Thus the coordinate is not introduced as a loose biological narrative: it is a finite residual surface whose codon support and excluded pair are fixed before any translation-layer interpretation is attempted.

\paragraph{Powered translation-layer evidence.}
The direct finite-row result for this mechanism is the experiment $\mathrm{b\_star\_q6\_fourth\_mechanism\_elongation\_dwell\_powered}$, run as $\mathrm{e4edcde459b7}$.  It passed its gate with $5$ folds in $\mathrm{Saccharomyces\ cerevisiae}$ and returned the conclusion $\mathrm{partial}$.  The deterministic seed was $\mathrm{sha256:f3d542c80eab2ed48839c11e4a4ef75c2286abfe31c9327f7afef47ce7335ab3}$.  The word ``partial'' is essential: the finite row supports an elongation-dwell identity as a candidate component of the fourth residual mechanism, but it does not close the entire translation mechanism and does not license promotion to protein abundance or phenotype.

\paragraph{Relation to the residual-axis panel.}
The surrounding powered panel makes the elongation-dwell call more specific.  The three-axis sufficiency test, run as $\mathrm{2436cae9d455}$, passed with $11$ computed organisms out of $16$ requested, $5$ folds, at least $500$ proteins per organism, and $41$ synonymous design columns; its conclusion was $\mathrm{fourth\_mechanism\_present}$.  The axis-strength spectrum, run as $\mathrm{0146012d8909}$ on the same $11$-of-$16$ organism panel and $41$ synonymous columns, found that $d_{resid4}$ led all $11$ organism-wise dominant counts, while the largest GC3 rank effect was only $d_{usage}$ with $\rho=0.136$.  This keeps the fourth direction from being reduced to a simple GC3 rank artifact.  The earlier residual-axis test identified the non-tRNA residual optimality most strongly with $f_3$ translation selection, and the two-axis closure test reported a remaining third axis with systematically positive held-out $R^2$ and same-signed directions across organisms.  Those rows explain why a fourth translation-facing mechanism is worth testing, but the present chapter uses them only as finite context for the elongation-dwell call.

\paragraph{Mechanism discrimination.}
The competing fourth-mechanism rows sharpen the interpretation.  The mRNA-stability run $\mathrm{377abd67b8b3}$ passed with $5$ folds, at least $500$ proteins per organism, and $41$ synonymous design columns, but concluded $\mathrm{fourth\_axis\_not\_mrna\_stability}$ because the yeast $d_{opt\_halflife}$ direction had low alignment with abundance $d_{resid4}$ under the fixed gates.  The codon-pair run $\mathrm{b519b5ebd34d}$ passed with $5$ folds, at least $500$ proteins per organism, $41$ synonymous design columns, and $50$ shuffle-null rows, and concluded $\mathrm{not\_codon\_pair}$.  The translation-accuracy run $\mathrm{0dc44df59ba7}$ passed on $11$ computed organisms out of $16$ requested, used $200$ permutations and $41$ synonymous design columns, and concluded $\mathrm{not\_accuracy}$.  Against those exclusions, the elongation-dwell result remains the positive but incomplete fourth-mechanism identity: not mRNA half-life, not codon-pair structure, not translation accuracy, and only partly captured by dwell-like translation timing in yeast.

\paragraph{Wobble asymmetry and the survival rule.}
The finite biological condition is asymmetric wobble or tRNA supply.  A separate tRNA-poverty test over the $R$ sense codons passed with $40$ organisms computed out of $46$ requested, $6$ skipped organisms, $1000$ permutations, and $\alpha=0.05$; its mechanism call was that $R$ sense codons are systematically tRNA-poor under GtRNAdb tGCN plus dos Reis wobble weights.  This supports the possibility that $f_3$-paired synonymous alternatives become dynamically distinguishable only where the organism-level readout breaks the third-position symmetry.  The operative survival condition is therefore local and controlled:
$$
\begin{aligned}
S_{f3\text{-}stress,T_j}>0
\quad\text{only after controlling for}\quad
Z=\{\text{amino-acid composition},\text{ organism},\mathrm{GC3},\text{ length},M\text{-density},\text{ mRNA}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
If the controlled entry is non-positive, or if the readout matrix is rank-degenerate, then the row is a null boundary rather than evidence for translation survival.

\paragraph{Local no-promotion rule.}
This chapter does not claim that $f_3$-stress causes protein abundance, folded structure, localization, stability, molecular function, physical admissibility, organismal phenotype, or a universal biological law.  It also does not treat modeled tAI or stAI weights as observed ribosome occupancy, and it does not treat a three-organism all-$1.0$ matrix as survival.  The only ready claim is narrower: in the finite powered rows available here, the fourth $B^*_{Q6}$ residual mechanism has a partial elongation-dwell identity in yeast, while the companion tests reject mRNA stability, codon-pair identity, and translation accuracy as the dominant fourth-axis explanation under their stated gates.  Any stronger biological assertion requires a separate layer-matched measurement and a new controlled statistic at that layer.
