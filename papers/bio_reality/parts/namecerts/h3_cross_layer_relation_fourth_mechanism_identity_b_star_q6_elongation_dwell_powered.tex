\paragraph{Observed finite rows.}
The direct fourth-axis assay for elongation dwell was run on \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}; it passed the declared pipeline and returned the finite conclusion \emph{partial} with $5$ folds under experiment id \texttt{b\_star\_q6\_fourth\_mechanism\_elongation\_dwell\_powered}. This is a bounded positive contact with dwell-like translation behavior, not a completed universal identity claim. Its placement is sharpened by neighboring negative mechanism rows. The codon-pair assay passed with $5$ folds, $50$ shuffle-null replicates, $41$ synonymous design columns, and conclusion \emph{not\_codon\_pair}. The mRNA-stability assay passed with $5$ folds, $41$ synonymous design columns, and conclusion \emph{fourth\_axis\_not\_mrna\_stability}; its recorded reason states that yeast \texttt{d\_opt\_halflife} has low alignment with abundance \texttt{d\_resid4} under the fixed gates. The translation-accuracy assay passed with $11$ computed organisms out of $16$ requested, $200$ permutations, $41$ synonymous design columns, and conclusion \emph{not\_accuracy}. Consequently the finite row supports a restricted reading: among the tested fourth-axis candidates, the dwell row is the one that remains partially aligned, while codon-pair, mRNA-stability, and accuracy alternatives are rejected by their own assays.

\paragraph{Axis context.}
The surrounding powered panel gives the dwell result its coordinate context. The three-axis sufficiency experiment passed on $11$ computed organisms out of $16$ requested with $5$ folds, $41$ synonymous design columns, and conclusion \emph{fourth\_mechanism\_present}; hence the fourth direction is not an artifact of stopping at the first two translation axes. The two-axis closure assay also passed on $11$ of $16$ requested organisms and concluded \emph{third\_axis\_present}, with the recorded reason that $d_{\perp}$ has systematically positive held-out $R^2$ and mutually same-signed directions across organisms. The third-axis identity row identified $d_{\perp}$ with usage frequency within family, reporting mean cosine $0.128$ and one-sided sign-test $p=0.000488$. The third-axis mechanism row then classified the same component as selection-not-GC, not-wobble-clustered, with CSC or positional identity still requiring an additional contact. In the strength spectrum, all $11$ per-organism dominant counts were assigned to \texttt{d\_resid4}; the descriptive domain balance was $n=\{\mathrm{archaea}:1,\mathrm{bacteria}:2,\mathrm{eukaryote}:8\}$, and the largest GC3 rank effect was only $\rho=0.136$ for \texttt{d\_usage}. These rows make the yeast dwell result a candidate identity for an already detected residual direction, rather than an isolated post hoc label.

\paragraph{Wobble break and readout condition.}
The biological content of the $f_3$-stress coordinate depends on third-position symmetry being broken by context tRNA or wobble weights. A separate powered tRNA-poverty row computed $40$ organisms out of $46$ requested, skipped $6$, used $1000$ permutations at $\alpha=0.05$, and concluded that $R$ sense codons are systematically tRNA-poor under GtRNAdb tGCN plus dos Reis wobble weights. This supplies a finite asymmetry route for $w_c\neq w_{c^{f_3}}$, but it does not by itself prove a translation-survival entry. The admissible readback is instead a controlled survival score $S_{f_3\text{-stress},T_j}$ for a bounded translation readout such as tAI, stAI, ribosome occupancy, or translation efficiency. The local support condition is:
$$\begin{aligned}
S_{f_3\text{-stress},T_j}>0
\end{aligned}$$
after amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, CDS length, $M$-density, matched mRNA, rank, and simpler-baseline controls. If the controlled entries are non-positive, or if the design collapses to a rank-degenerate all-$1.0$ surface, the same data become a null boundary rather than survival evidence.

\paragraph{Interpretation of the partial dwell call.}
The present finite record therefore licenses a narrow statement. In yeast, the powered fourth-axis screen returns a partial elongation-dwell identity for the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual direction, while three neighboring mechanism candidates are rejected in their own finite assays. Across the broader panel, the fourth direction is motivated by an $11$-organism axis spectrum dominated by \texttt{d\_resid4}, a passed fourth-mechanism-present row, and a prior residual optimality row whose dominant axis is \texttt{f3\_translation\_selection}. The claim remains conditional on the translation row: the $f_3$ coordinate becomes dynamical only where wobble or tRNA weights break the $f_3$ pairing, and the reported dwell identity is not promoted beyond the measured or explicitly bounded translation-readout surface.
