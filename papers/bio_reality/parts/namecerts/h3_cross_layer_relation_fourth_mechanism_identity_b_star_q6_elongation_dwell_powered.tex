\paragraph{Readout statistic.}
For a translation readout $T$ such as tAI, stAI, ribosome occupancy, translation efficiency, or a position-resolved dwell proxy, the admissible statistic is a controlled survival increment rather than an unadjusted association.  The intended finite row has the form
$$
\begin{aligned}
S_{f_3,T}
  &= \operatorname{score}(T \mid Z,Q_{f_3})-\operatorname{score}(T \mid Z),\\
Z
  &= \{\text{amino-acid composition},\text{ organism},\text{ GC3},\text{ length},M\text{-density},\text{ mRNA controls}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
A positive row means only that the controlled translation readout retains information from the $f_3$-stress residual coordinate.  A non-positive row, or a rank-degenerate all-equal fitted surface, is a null boundary.  In particular, the three-organism all-$1.0$ controlled surface is not counted as survival; it is a rank-saturated refusal to promote the coordinate.

\paragraph{Wobble break condition.}
The $f_3$-stress coordinate becomes biologically testable only where the translation apparatus breaks third-position symmetry.  In the finite language used here, a codon $c$ and its $f_3$ mate $c^{f_3}$ can contribute to the row only when their context tRNA or wobble weights differ:
$$
\begin{aligned}
w_c &\neq w_{c^{f_3}},\\
S_{f_3,T} &> 0\quad\text{after the declared controls.}
\end{aligned}
$$
The tRNA-poverty boundary run \texttt{d7728cc8768e} supplies a compatible finite mechanism pressure: across $40$ computed organisms out of $46$ requested, with $6$ skipped, $1000$ permutations, and $\alpha=0.05$, the row states that $R$ sense codons are systematically tRNA-poor under GtRNAdb tGCN plus dos Reis wobble weights.  That supports a tRNA-supply interpretation for $R$ avoidance, but it still does not replace the mandatory translation row.  Wobble-weight asymmetry can nominate the dynamical surface; it cannot by itself count as measured ribosome occupancy or translation efficiency.

\paragraph{Local conclusion.}
The chapter-level conclusion is therefore deliberately bounded.  The stored data justify saying that the $B^*_{Q6}$ $f_3$-stress residual coordinate has a yeast-specific, $5$-fold, passed, partial fourth-mechanism identity row on an elongation-dwell readout, in a panel where mRNA stability, codon-pair structure, and translation accuracy did not carry the same fourth-mechanism identity.  The strongest finite description is not that the coordinate controls translation, but that elongation dwell remains the surviving partial candidate for the fourth residual mechanism under the available controlled tests.

No stronger biological claim follows here.  A translation-realization claim needs a non-vacuous same-row statistic on a matched translation readout.  Protein abundance, folding, physical admissibility, localization, stability, molecular function, organismal phenotype, causation, mechanism, and universality each require a distinct layer-matched contact and its own controlled statistic.  The present row is a finite, controlled, yeast-centered identity signal inside the translation-readout boundary; it is not a license to promote synonymous residual geometry into downstream biology.
