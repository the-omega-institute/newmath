\subsection{The fourth residual mechanism as an elongation-dwell boundary}\label{sec:biorreality-q6-f3-elongation-dwell}\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite coordinate surface.}
This record concerns the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual coordinate after amino-acid projection, restricted to the $f_3$-stress direction and compared only with translation-layer readouts. The finite saturation row fixes the relevant codon support before any biological interpretation is attempted. The mediated carrier with the two added $ACA,ACG$ rows is
$$
\begin{aligned}
M = \{&AAA,AAG,ACA,ACG,AGA,AGG,AUA,AUG,CUA,CUC,CUG,CUU,\\
&UAA,UAG,UCA,UCG,UGA,UGG,UUA,UUG\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The $f_3$-saturated residual support removes the two $ACN$ rows and is
$$
\begin{aligned}
\operatorname{Sat}_{f_3}(R)=\{&AAA,AAG,AGA,AGG,AUA,AUG,CUA,CUC,CUG,CUU,\\
&UAA,UAG,UCA,UCG,UGA,UGG,UUA,UUG\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the finite row is not a genome-wide statement about codon usage. It is a restricted residual surface on $18$ saturated codons inside a $20$-row mediated support, with empty reported $f_3$ boundary. The empty boundary is important: the test does not begin from an unmatched leftover codon class, but from a closed finite stress coordinate whose biological meaning must be supplied by an independent translation readout.

\paragraph{Readout rule.}
The admissible readback is a controlled survival entry $S_{f_3\text{-stress},T_j}$, where $T_j$ is a bounded translation readout such as a tAI or stAI weight, a ribosome-occupancy row, or a translation-efficiency row. The row is interpreted only after amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, CDS length, $M$-density, and matched mRNA controls have been accounted for. In schematic form the only promoted statistic is
$$
\begin{aligned}
S_{f_3\text{-stress},T_j} > 0
\end{aligned}
$$
under the declared controls and under simpler-codon-baseline ablation. If this inequality is absent, non-positive, or rank-degenerate, then the coordinate remains a formal residual distinction rather than an observed translation distinction. In particular, an all-$1.0$ surface on a three-organism rank-degenerate panel is a refusal boundary, not evidence for survival.

\paragraph{Powered finite evidence.}
The finite powered row for the exact elongation-dwell identity reports status passed, conclusion partial, five folds, and the organism $Saccharomyces\ cerevisiae$ under seed $sha256:f3d542c80eab2ed48839c11e4a4ef75c2286abfe31c9327f7afef47ce7335ab3$. This is a positive bounded placement of the fourth residual mechanism near elongation dwell, but only as a partial identity. The row does not certify a universal dwell law, and it does not replace measured same-CDS ribosome or translation-efficiency rows.

The neighboring powered rows constrain that interpretation. The three-axis sufficiency experiment passed on $11$ computed organisms out of $16$ requested organisms, with five folds, at least $500$ proteins per organism, and $41$ synonymous design columns; its conclusion was that a fourth mechanism is present. The per-organism axis-strength spectrum also passed on $11$ of $16$ requested organisms with the same five-fold and $41$-column design, and it reported that captured spectra are led on average by $d_{resid4}$ followed by $d_{tRNA}$, with per-organism dominant counts $\{d_{resid4}:11\}$. Its domain counts were imbalanced, with $1$ archaeal, $2$ bacterial, and $8$ eukaryotic organisms, and the largest GC3 rank effect was small, $\rho=0.136$ for $d_{usage}$. These numbers support treating the fourth axis as a real finite residual problem, while warning against over-reading the taxonomic distribution.

\paragraph{Mechanism localization.}
The surrounding discriminants narrow the fourth mechanism without closing it into a downstream phenotype. The non-tRNA residual optimality row passed on $11$ of $16$ requested organisms and identified $f_3\_translation\_selection$ as the dominant axis. The two-axis closure row, also on $11$ computed organisms out of $16$, concluded that a third axis is present, because $d_{\perp}$ has systematically positive held-out $R^2$ and same-signed directions across organisms. The third-axis identity row provisionally aligned $d_{\perp}$ with usage frequency within family, with mean cosine $0.128$ and one-sided sign-test $p=0.000488$. The third-axis mechanism row kept significant cross-species alignment after GC orthogonalization and concluded selection-not-GC, not-wobble-clustered, with CSC or positional identity still requiring additional evidence.

The fourth-axis exclusion rows are equally important. The mRNA-stability powered row passed and concluded that the fourth axis is not dominantly mRNA stability in the assay, because yeast $d_{opt\_halflife}$ had low alignment with abundance $d_{resid4}$ under the fixed gates. The codon-pair powered row passed with conclusion $not\_codon\_pair$, five folds, at least $500$ proteins per organism, $41$ synonymous design columns, and $50$ shuffle nulls. The translation-accuracy powered row passed on $11$ computed organisms out of $16$ requested organisms, with $200$ permutations and $41$ synonymous design columns, and concluded $not\_accuracy$. Together with the exact elongation-dwell row's partial conclusion, these finite rows make elongation dwell the best bounded fourth-mechanism placement among the tested alternatives, not a completed biological mechanism.

\paragraph{Wobble asymmetry condition.}
The coordinate becomes biologically testable only where third-position symmetry is broken by context tRNA or wobble weights. The required local distinction is
$$
\begin{aligned}
w_c \neq w_{c^{f_3}},
\end{aligned}
$$
with the comparison made after amino-acid projection. If the paired codons have symmetric weights, then the residual $f_3$ distinction has no translation-layer leverage in that context. If the weights are asymmetric, the coordinate may enter a translation-readout test, but the test still requires controlled survival rather than topology alone. The boundary-set tRNA-poverty experiment supplies compatible mechanism pressure: it passed on $40$ computed organisms out of $46$ requested organisms, skipped $6$, used $1000$ permutations at $\alpha=0.05$, and found that $R$ sense codons are systematically tRNA-poor under GtRNAdb tGCN plus dos Reis wobble weights. This supports a tRNA-supply route for $R$ avoidance, but it is not by itself a measured dwell-time row.

\paragraph{Local non-promotion rule.}
This chapter permits only a bounded statement: the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ $f_3$-stress residual has a partial finite alignment with an elongation-dwell fourth-mechanism interpretation, after several alternative fourth-axis identities are disfavored by the available powered rows. Nothing stronger follows locally. Codon topology, tRNA-copy spectra, modeled tAI or stAI weights, and the partial yeast elongation-dwell result do not establish observed ribosome occupancy, realized translation dynamics, protein abundance, folded structure, physical admissibility, molecular function, organismal phenotype, mechanism in the causal sense, or universality. Each of those claims requires its own layer-matched contact and its own controlled statistic. Until a same-organism or same-CDS matrix joins codon counts, wobble-breaking weights, direct translation readouts, and matched mRNA controls, the safe conclusion is a finite fourth-axis boundary with partial elongation-dwell support, not a promoted biological law.
