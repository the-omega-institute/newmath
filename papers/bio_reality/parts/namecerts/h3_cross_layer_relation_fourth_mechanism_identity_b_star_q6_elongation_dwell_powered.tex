\subsection{Fourth Mechanism Identity: Partial Elongation-Dwell Contact for the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ $f_3$ Residual}\label{sec:bioreality-q6-f3-elongation-dwell-powered}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite coordinate surface.}
This record isolates the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinate after amino-acid quotienting and asks whether its fourth residual component can be read as an elongation-dwell effect.  The finite codon surface is not the full codon cube.  The verified $f_3$ saturation row gives
$$
\begin{aligned}
M &= \{\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{ACA},\mathrm{ACG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\},\\
\mathrm{Sat}_{f_3}(R) &= \{\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\},\\
\partial_{f_3}R &= \varnothing .
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the finite comparison contains $20$ codons in $M$, $18$ codons after the $f_3$ saturation operation, and no residual $f_3$ boundary row.  The two codons present in $M$ but absent from the saturated row are $\mathrm{ACA}$ and $\mathrm{ACG}$.  In this chapter the coordinate is used only as a residual codon-composition predictor paired with translation-layer measurements or bounded translation-weight rows; it is not treated as a direct observation of ribosome motion.

\paragraph{Powered residual spectrum.}
The powered cross-organism residual assays give a stable background for asking whether a fourth component is needed.  The two-axis closure assay computed $11$ of $16$ requested organisms, used $5$ folds, required at least $500$ proteins per organism, and used $41$ synonymous-design columns; it returned the conclusion that a third axis is present.  The three-axis sufficiency assay used the same $11/16$ organism coverage, $5$ folds, $500$-protein minimum, and $41$ design columns, and returned $\mathrm{fourth\_mechanism\_present}$.  The per-organism axis-strength spectrum again computed $11$ organisms; every computed organism had $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$ as the dominant captured direction, giving the dominant-count summary $\{d_{\mathrm{resid4}}:11\}$.  Its descriptive domain counts were imbalanced, with $1$ archaeal, $2$ bacterial, and $8$ eukaryotic organisms, and the largest GC3 rank effect reported there was only $\rho=0.136$ for $d_{\mathrm{usage}}$.

\paragraph{Mechanism filters.}
Several candidate interpretations of the fourth direction are rejected or limited before the elongation-dwell row is read.  The mRNA-stability assay passed its gate but concluded $\mathrm{fourth\_axis\_not\_mrna\_stability}$, because the yeast half-life direction had low alignment with the abundance-side $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$ direction under the declared controls.  The codon-pair assay, with $5$ folds, a $500$-protein minimum, $41$ synonymous-design columns, and $50$ shuffle-null samples, concluded $\mathrm{not\_codon\_pair}$.  The translation-accuracy assay, with $11$ computed organisms out of $16$, $5$ folds, $41$ synonymous-design columns, and $200$ permutations, concluded $\mathrm{not\_accuracy}$.  Against those negative screens, the elongation-dwell assay passed and returned the finite conclusion $\mathrm{partial}$ in $\mathrm{Saccharomyces\ cerevisiae}$ with $5$ folds.  The word partial is essential: the row supports a bounded yeast-side contact with elongation-dwell structure, not a universal identification of the fourth axis.

\paragraph{Relation to tRNA and wobble asymmetry.}
The translation interpretation is also constrained by tRNA-side rows.  A separate powered boundary-set assay computed $40$ organisms out of $46$, skipped $6$, used $1000$ permutations at $\alpha=0.05$, and concluded that $R$ sense codons are systematically tRNA-poor under GtRNAdb tGCN plus dos Reis wobble weights.  The universal core tRNA-adaptation assay computed $11$ organisms out of $16$ with $5$ folds, a $500$-protein minimum, and $41$ synonymous-design columns.  The non-tRNA residual optimality assay on the same powered panel identified the dominant residual axis as $f_3\_\mathrm{translation\_selection}$.  The third-axis identity screen provisionally aligned $d_{\perp}$ to within-family usage frequency with mean cosine $0.128$ and one-sided sign-test $p=0.000488$; the third-axis mechanism screen then described that direction as selection-not-GC, not wobble-clustered, with CSC or positional identity requiring another measured layer.  These facts make the elongation-dwell row plausible as a bounded translation-layer candidate, but they do not by themselves measure dwell times.

\paragraph{Finite claim.}
The supported statement is therefore narrow.  On the finite powered surface, the fourth residual direction is not explained by the tested mRNA-stability, codon-pair, or translation-accuracy alternatives, while the yeast elongation-dwell assay returns a passed partial call.  In symbols, the chapter records only the implication
$$
\begin{aligned}
&\mathrm{three\text{-}axis\ insufficiency}\;\wedge\;\mathrm{negative\ screens\ for\ mRNA,\ codon\ pair,\ accuracy}\;\wedge\;\mathrm{yeast\ dwell\ partial}\\
&\qquad\Longrightarrow\quad \mathrm{bounded\ fourth\text{-}axis\ elongation\text{-}dwell\ candidate\ on\ the\ tested\ finite\ panel}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The implication is not reversed, and it is not promoted to a general biological law.  The row says that elongation dwell remains a surviving candidate for the fourth residual identity in the tested yeast assay after the named alternatives are filtered; it does not say that every organism or every coding sequence realizes the same mechanism.

\paragraph{Local non-promotion rule.}
No translation realization, ribosome-occupancy claim, protein-abundance consequence, folded-structure claim, physical-admissibility claim, molecular-function claim, phenotype claim, mechanism claim, or universality claim follows from the residual coordinate alone.  A positive interpretation beyond the bounded elongation-dwell row requires a separate layer-matched contact: codon counts, tRNA or wobble weights, organism-matched translation readout, matched mRNA control, and the declared covariates must be joined on the same finite rows before a survival statistic is read.  The three-organism all-$1.0$ rank-saturated boundary is also not counted as survival.  If a controlled $S_{f_3\text{-}\mathrm{stress},T_j}$ entry remains non-positive, rank-degenerate, or unsupported by same-row translation measurements, the present chapter remains only a finite candidate screen and supplies no downstream biological conclusion.
