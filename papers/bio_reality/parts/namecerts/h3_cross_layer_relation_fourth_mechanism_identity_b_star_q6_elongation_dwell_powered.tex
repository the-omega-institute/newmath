\paragraph{Finite coordinate under test.}
This record concerns the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual coordinate obtained after amino-acid quotienting, with special attention to the $f_3$-paired synonymous imbalance that remains only when third-position symmetry is broken by a context-dependent tRNA or wobble weight. The finite saturation row used here has
$$
\begin{aligned}
M &= \{\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{ACA},\mathrm{ACG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\},\\

\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the two codons $\mathrm{ACA}$ and $\mathrm{ACG}$ are present in $M$ but absent from the saturated $f_3$ row, and the recorded $f_3$ boundary is empty. The biological reading is deliberately narrow: the coordinate is a finite residual surface over coding sequences, organisms, codon usage, tRNA or wobble annotations, and matched translation readouts. It is not, by itself, a claim about protein abundance, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, phenotype, or a universal biological law.

\paragraph{Powered row and finite outcome.}
The dwell-specific experiment for the fourth mechanism identity was run as \texttt{b\_star\_q6\_fourth\_mechanism\_elongation\_dwell\_powered} with status \texttt{passed}, seed \texttt{sha256:f3d542c80eab2ed48839c11e4a4ef75c2286abfe31c9327f7afef47ce7335ab3}, and $5$ folds. In this chapter that word is interpreted literally: the available row supports an elongation-dwell component in the fourth residual direction for the yeast assay, but it does not close the mechanism globally and does not license transfer to organisms or layers not represented by the row.

\paragraph{Position among competing fourth-axis explanations.}
The surrounding finite results make the partial dwell call more specific. The three-axis sufficiency row reports \texttt{fourth\_mechanism\_present} on $11$ computed organisms out of $16$ requested organisms, using $5$ folds, at least $500$ proteins per organism, and $41$ synonymous design columns. The axis-strength spectrum was also computed on $11$ of $16$ requested organisms with $5$ folds and $41$ synonymous design columns; its recorded summary says that spectra are led by $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$ on average, followed by $d_{\mathrm{tRNA}}$, with per-organism dominant counts $\{d_{\mathrm{resid4}}:11\}$ and a largest GC3 rank effect of $\rho=0.136$ for $d_{\mathrm{usage}}$. These rows say that the fourth residual direction is not a decorative coordinate: it is repeatedly visible in the powered panel. They do not, however, identify every mechanistic source of that direction.

The direct alternatives recorded in the same finite packet constrain the interpretation. The mRNA-stability test passed with conclusion \texttt{fourth\_axis\_not\_mrna\_stability}; its reason states that the yeast $d_{\mathrm{opt\_halflife}}$ has low alignment with abundance $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$ under the fixed gates. The codon-pair test passed with conclusion \texttt{not\_codon\_pair}, using $5$ folds, at least $500$ proteins per organism, $41$ synonymous design columns, and $50$ shuffle-null draws. The translation-accuracy test passed with conclusion \texttt{not\_accuracy}, over $11$ computed organisms out of $16$ requested, $5$ folds, $41$ synonymous design columns, and $200$ permutations. Against those exclusions, the elongation-dwell row remains the surviving positive but bounded candidate, and its finite conclusion is only partial.

\paragraph{Translation-readout gate.}
The coordinate becomes biologically interpretable only at rows where a translation readout survives after the declared controls. The intended statistic has the form
$$
\begin{aligned}
S_{f_3\text{-stress},T_j} &> 0
\end{aligned}
$$
for a bounded translation row $T_j$, after controlling amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, CDS length, $M$-density, mRNA abundance when used, rank, and simpler codon baselines. A modeled tAI or stAI surface is allowed as a bounded codon-adaptation readback; it is not the same thing as observed ribosome occupancy. Likewise, ribosome profiling or translation efficiency can support the row only after same-organism and same-CDS joining to codon counts, wobble or tRNA weights, and matched mRNA controls. A rank-degenerate all-$1.0$ surface is a null boundary rather than evidence of survival.

\paragraph{Compatibility with the lower-axis packet.}
The third-axis rows give useful local context without turning the dwell row into a complete theory. The $d_{\perp}$ identity row reports alignment to usage frequency within family, with mean cosine $0.128$ and one-sided sign-test $p=0.000488$, over $11$ computed organisms out of $16$ requested, $5$ folds, at least $500$ proteins per organism, and $41$ synonymous design columns. The mechanism row for $d_{\perp}$ reports \texttt{selection-not-GC; not-wobble-clustered; CSC/positional identity needs\_external}, with the reason that GC-orthogonalized $d_{\perp}$ keeps significant cross-species alignment and held-out $R^2$. Separately, the $R$-sense tRNA-poverty row was computed on $40$ organisms out of $46$ requested, skipped $6$, used $1000$ permutations at $\alpha=0.05$, and calls $R$ sense codons systematically tRNA-poor under GtRNAdb tGCN plus dos Reis wobble weights. These facts place the dwell result in a translation-adaptation neighborhood, but the chapter keeps the layers separated: finite codon geometry and modeled supply weights do not themselves measure ribosome dwell time.

\paragraph{Local conclusion and refusal boundary.}
The finite judgment is therefore a constrained one. In the powered yeast row, the fourth $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ mechanism identity has a partial elongation-dwell signal after the other named alternatives in this packet have been excluded or weakened. The scientifically admissible sentence is that elongation dwell is a surviving bounded candidate for the fourth residual direction in the recorded assay. The inadmissible sentence is stronger: no translation realization, protein abundance effect, folded-structure consequence, physical admissibility statement, biological function, mechanism beyond the bounded readout, organismal phenotype, or universal rule follows from this chapter unless a separate layer-matched contact supplies the corresponding observation and the controlled statistic remains non-vacuous.
