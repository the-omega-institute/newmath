\paragraph{Finite surface.}
This record concerns the $B^*_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinate after amino-acid quotienting, restricted to the $f_3$-stress direction and read only against translation-layer rows. The finite carrier is a CDS-by-organism table in which each row supplies codon composition, organism identity, amino-acid composition controls, GC3, CDS length, M-density, and a matched translation readout when such a row is available. The object is not a protein-structure table and not a phenotype table. Its bounded readback is the controlled entry $S_{f3\text{-}stress,T_j}$ for a translation readout $T_j$, such as a tAI/stAI surface or a ribosome-occupancy or translation-efficiency surface.

\paragraph{Relation to the surrounding finite spectrum.}
The fourth-coordinate test sits inside a larger rank-powered $B^*_{Q6}$ spectrum. A separate powered sufficiency run computed $11$ organisms out of $16$ requested organisms, with $5$ folds, a minimum of $500$ proteins per organism, and $41$ synonymous design columns; its conclusion was that a fourth mechanism is present. In the axis-strength spectrum, again on $11$ computed organisms out of $16$ requested and $41$ synonymous design columns, the captured spectra were led by $d_{resid4}$ on average, with the per-organism dominant count recorded as $\{d_{resid4}:11\}$. The same spectrum reported imbalanced domain counts $\{archaea:1,bacteria:2,eukaryote:8\}$ and a largest GC3 rank effect of $\rho=0.136$ for $d_{usage}$. These numbers support treating the fourth coordinate as a real finite-row residual object in the computed design; they do not, by themselves, identify the biological layer.

\paragraph{Eliminated neighboring identities.}
The finite identity packet is sharpened by nearby negative or exclusionary calls. The mRNA-stability powered run, using $5$ folds, at least $500$ proteins per organism, and $41$ synonymous design columns, concluded $fourth\_axis\_not\_mrna\_stability$ because the yeast $d_{opt\_halflife}$ axis had low alignment with the abundance $d_{resid4}$ axis under the fixed gates. Against those alternatives, the elongation-dwell call is the surviving positive but incomplete identity claim for this specific fourth-mechanism branch.

\paragraph{Wobble and tRNA break condition.}
The coordinate becomes dynamically interpretable only where the third-position pairing is not neutralized by equal readout weights. The boundary-set tRNA-poverty run computed $40$ organisms out of $46$ requested, skipped $6$, used $1000$ permutations at $\alpha=0.05$, and reported that $R$ sense codons are systematically tRNA-poor under GtRNAdb tGCN plus dos Reis wobble weights. This gives the correct kind of finite asymmetry for an $f_3$-stress row: a paired synonymous imbalance can matter only when the context weight satisfies $w_c \ne w_{c^{f_3}}$. Without such a weight break, the residual coordinate remains geometrically present but translation-silent.

\paragraph{Non-promotion rule.}
The chapter records a bounded translation-layer contact, not a promotion to downstream biology. A positive or partial $S_{f3\text{-}stress,T_j}$ row may be read as survival of a residual codon coordinate against a translation readout under amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, length, M-density, and mRNA controls. It does not imply mature protein abundance, folded structure, physical admissibility, molecular function, localization, stability, phenotype, causation, or a universal biological law. Those claims require their own matched contacts and their own controlled finite statistics. In particular, the three-organism all-$1.0$ $S^{QT}$ surface is a rank-degenerate boundary and is not survival evidence.
