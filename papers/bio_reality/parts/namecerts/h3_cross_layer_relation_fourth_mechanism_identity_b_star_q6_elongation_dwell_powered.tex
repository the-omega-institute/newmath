\subsection{The $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ $f_3$-stress row and the elongation-dwell boundary}\label{sec:bioreality-q6-f3-stress-elongation-dwell}\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite coordinate under test.}
The record concerns a finite coding-sequence surface in which synonymous codon information is first quotiented by amino acid and then inspected along the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ coordinate carrying $f_3$-stress.  The saturated $f_3$ row is concrete: the ambient set $M=\operatorname{Med}(R)\cup ACA$ contains the 20 codons $AAA,AAG,ACA,ACG,AGA,AGG,AUA,AUG,CUA,CUC,CUG,CUU,UAA,UAG,UCA,UCG,UGA,UGG,UUA,UUG$.  After applying $\operatorname{Sat}_{f_3}(R)$, the surviving list has 18 codons,
$$
\begin{aligned}
\operatorname{Sat}_{f_3}(R)=\{&AAA,AAG,AGA,AGG,AUA,AUG,CUA,CUC,CUG,CUU,\\
&UAA,UAG,UCA,UCG,UGA,UGG,UUA,UUG\},
\end{aligned}
$$
with $ACA$ and $ACG$ removed from the row and an empty $f_3$ boundary.  Thus the test does not begin from an unconstrained codon table.  It begins from a named finite residual coordinate whose possible biological contact is limited to the rows that can be joined to codon usage, tRNA or wobble weights, and translation readout data.

\paragraph{Fourth-axis question.}
The powered fourth-mechanism assay for elongation dwell is recorded as passed with conclusion \emph{partial}.  Its finite row is narrower than the cross-organism spectra: it uses five folds and is explicitly organism-scoped to \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}, with experiment identifier \texttt{b\_star\_q6\_fourth\_mechanism\_elongation\_dwell\_powered} and seed \texttt{sha256:f3d542c80eab2ed48839c11e4a4ef75c2286abfe31c9327f7afef47ce7335ab3}.  The word \emph{partial} is therefore read literally.  It licenses a yeast-specific finite-row alignment between the residual fourth-axis signal and an elongation-dwell interpretation, but it does not by itself certify a universal rule, a direct ribosome-footprint statistic, a protein-abundance effect, or any higher biological endpoint.

\paragraph{Relation to the powered $Q6$ background.}
Several neighboring finite rows bound how much weight the elongation-dwell result can carry.  The axis-strength spectrum was computed on 11 organisms out of 16 requested, with 5 folds, at least 500 proteins per organism, and 41 synonymous-design columns.  In that spectrum, $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$ led the average ordering and was dominant in all 11 computed organism rows; the largest GC3 rank effect was only $\rho=0.136$ for $d_{\mathrm{usage}}$.  The three-axis sufficiency test, also on 11 of 16 requested organisms with 5 folds, at least 500 proteins per organism, and 41 synonymous-design columns, concluded that a fourth mechanism is present.  The universal non-tRNA residual-axis row identified $f_3$ translation selection as the dominant residual optimality direction on the same 11-organism, 5-fold, 41-column design.  These rows make the yeast elongation-dwell result plausible as a fourth-axis contact candidate, but they do not remove the need for the local readout row.

\paragraph{Competing finite mechanisms.}
The same family of assays restricts alternative explanations.  The mRNA-stability identity test passed with conclusion \texttt{fourth\_axis\_not\_mrna\_stability}; its stated reason is that yeast $d_{\mathrm{opt\_halflife}}$ has low alignment with abundance $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$ under the fixed gates.  The codon-pair test passed with conclusion \texttt{not\_codon\_pair}, using 5 folds, at least 500 proteins per organism, 41 synonymous-design columns, and 50 shuffle-null replicates.  The translation-accuracy test passed with conclusion \texttt{not\_accuracy}, using 11 organisms out of 16 requested, 200 permutations, 5 folds, and 41 synonymous-design columns.  These negative rows do not prove elongation dwell as the unique mechanism.  They only make the partial yeast elongation-dwell row sharper by excluding three named competitors under their declared gates.

\paragraph{Wobble break and translation readout.}
The biological route is conditional on broken third-position symmetry.  The $f_3$ coordinate becomes a translation-layer variable only where context tRNA or wobble weights distinguish a codon from its $f_3$ partner.  The tRNA-poverty row supplies one such bounded contact: for $R$ sense codons, a GtRNAdb tRNA-gene-copy plus dos Reis wobble-weight assay was computed on 40 organisms out of 46 requested, with 6 skipped organisms, $1000$ permutations, and $\alpha=0.05$, and it concluded that $R$ sense codons are systematically tRNA-poor.  That result is a supply-side pressure, not a ribosome-dwell observation.  The elongation-dwell row can therefore be stated only as a finite mechanism identity with partial support, pending direct same-row translation-efficiency or position-resolved ribosome-occupancy contact where such data are required.

\paragraph{Readout formula.}
For a coordinate row $q=f_3\text{-stress}$ and a translation readout $T_j$, the bounded survival statistic is interpreted only after controls $Z$ remove amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, coding length, $M$-density, and matched mRNA effects where applicable:
$$
\begin{aligned}
S_{q,T_j}>0
&\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
q \text{ contributes a controlled translation-readout distinction for } T_j,\\
S_{q,T_j}\leq 0
&\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
\text{no positive translation-readout survival is recorded for that row.}
\end{aligned}
$$
The present finite row is not a full $S_{q,T_j}$ matrix over a rank-powered measured ribosome panel.  It is a passed, yeast-scoped, five-fold mechanism-identity row with a partial conclusion.  Hence it may be used to motivate the next translation-row test, but it cannot be promoted into a completed survival matrix.

\paragraph{Local refusal boundary.}
The chapter asserts no downstream biological layer from this row alone.  A partial elongation-dwell identity does not imply protein abundance, folded structure, physical admissibility, molecular function, localization, stability, phenotype, mechanism in the causal-intervention sense, or universality.  Those claims require their own layer-matched finite contacts and their own controlled statistics.  Likewise, modelled tAI or stAI weights may bound a codon-adaptation surface, but they are not identical to observed ribosome occupancy.  A three-organism all-$1.0$ controlled matrix is treated as rank saturation rather than survival.  The allowed statement is exactly the bounded one: on the supplied finite rows, the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ $f_3$-stress coordinate has a yeast-specific, five-fold, partial elongation-dwell mechanism identity, sharpened by negative mRNA-stability, codon-pair, and translation-accuracy alternatives, and still awaiting a nondegenerate same-row translation-readout statistic before any stronger biological interpretation is made.
