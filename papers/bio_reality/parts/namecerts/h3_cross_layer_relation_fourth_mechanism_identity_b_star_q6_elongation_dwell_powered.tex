\subsection{A partial elongation-dwell identity for the fourth $B^*_{Q6}$ residual axis}\label{sec:brioreality-q6-elongation-dwell-fourth-axis}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite carrier.}
This record concerns a finite residual coordinate surface on coding sequences, restricted to the $B^*_{Q6}$ $f_3$-stress coordinate after amino-acid projection.  The rows are not raw codons alone: they are codon-by-organism residual entries paired with translation-layer readouts and with controls for amino-acid composition, organism identity, GC3, coding-sequence length, $M$-density, and matched mRNA abundance where translation efficiency is computed.  The specific biological object is a rank-powered multi-organism panel with codon usage, tRNA or wobble weights, and matched translation readouts.  The claim tested here is narrower than a full biological law: among the candidate identities for the fourth residual axis, the elongation-dwell route receives a finite positive but incomplete call.

\paragraph{Saturation geometry used by the row.}
The $f_3$ closure memory fixes the finite codon set rather than leaving the coordinate schematic.  The verified $M=\operatorname{Med}_R\cup ACA$ surface is
$$
\begin{aligned}
M=\{&AAA,AAG,ACA,ACG,AGA,AGG,AUA,AUG,CUA,CUC,CUG,CUU,\\
&UAA,UAG,UCA,UCG,UGA,UGG,UUA,UUG\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The corresponding $f_3$-saturated $R$ part removes $ACA$ and $ACG$:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\operatorname{Sat}_{f_3}(R)=\{&AAA,AAG,AGA,AGG,AUA,AUG,CUA,CUC,CUG,CUU,\\
&UAA,UAG,UCA,UCG,UGA,UGG,UUA,UUG\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The verified boundary residue is empty, $\partial_{f_3}=\varnothing$.  Thus the coordinate is not being promoted from an unresolved combinatorial edge: its finite codon support is closed at the recorded $f_3$ boundary, and the biological question is whether this closed residual surface survives into a translation-readout row.

\paragraph{Powered finite evidence.}
The elongation-dwell identity test was run as experiment $b\_star\_q6\_fourth\_mechanism\_elongation\_dwell\_powered$ with seed $sha256:f3d542c80eab2ed48839c11e4a4ef75c2286abfe31c9327f7afef47ce7335ab3$.  It passed its execution gate on $Saccharomyces\ cerevisiae$ with $5$ folds and returned conclusion $partial$.  The word ``partial'' is substantive: the row is not a null, but it also does not identify elongation dwell as a universal or exclusive explanation of the fourth axis.  It says that, in the yeast row available to the assay, the fourth-axis signal has a bounded elongation-dwell component.

\paragraph{Position among competing fourth-axis explanations.}
The partial elongation-dwell call is informative because the neighboring powered tests do not point to the same alternatives.  The mRNA-stability assay passed with $5$ folds and $41$ synonymous design columns but concluded $fourth\_axis\_not\_mrna\_stability$, with the stated reason that yeast $d_{\mathrm{opt,halflife}}$ has low alignment with abundance $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$ under the fixed gates.  The codon-pair assay passed with $5$ folds, $41$ synonymous design columns, and $50$ shuffle-null samples, but concluded $not\_codon\_pair$.  The translation-accuracy assay passed on $11$ computed organisms out of $16$ requested, using $5$ folds, $41$ synonymous design columns, and $200$ permutations, but concluded $not\_accuracy$.  Taken together, these rows do not make elongation dwell a settled mechanism; they make it the remaining positively touched fourth-axis route in this finite comparison.

\paragraph{Relation to the wider $B^*_{Q6}$ spectrum.}
The fourth-axis test sits downstream of a nontrivial residual spectrum.  The three-axis sufficiency experiment passed on $11$ organisms out of $16$ requested with $5$ folds, at least $500$ proteins per organism, and $41$ synonymous design columns, and concluded $fourth\_mechanism\_present$.  The per-organism axis-strength spectrum also used $11$ computed organisms out of $16$ requested, $5$ folds, at least $500$ proteins per organism, and $41$ synonymous design columns; its descriptive summary reports that $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$ leads on average, followed by $d_{\mathrm{tRNA}}$, with per-organism dominant counts $\{d_{\mathrm{resid4}}:11\}$.  The domain means are explicitly descriptive under the imbalanced sample $n=\{archaea:1,bacteria:2,eukaryote:8\}$, and the largest GC3 rank effect reported there is $d_{\mathrm{usage}}$ with $\rho=0.136$.  These finite values matter because the elongation-dwell row is being read as an identity test for an already detected residual direction, not as a first detection of codon usage structure.

\paragraph{Translation-readout interpretation.}
The residual axis has a translation-facing route because the non-tRNA residual optimality experiment passed on $11$ organisms out of $16$ requested and identified $f_3\_translation\_selection$ as the dominant non-tRNA residual optimality axis.  A separate third-axis identity row reports alignment to usage frequency within family, with mean cosine $0.128$ and one-sided sign-test $p=0.000488$; the related mechanism row reports that GC-orthogonalized $d_\perp$ keeps significant cross-species alignment and held-out $R^2$, while its conclusion is selection-not-GC, not-wobble-clustered, with CSC or positional identity still needing further outside rows.  The boundary-set assay supplies another finite contact point: $R$ sense codons are systematically tRNA-poor under GtRNAdb tGCN plus dos Reis wobble weights, in a passed run over $40$ computed organisms out of $46$ requested with $1000$ permutations at $\alpha=0.05$.  These facts justify testing an elongation-dwell reading, but they do not replace measured translation rows.

\paragraph{Local falsification condition.}
For this chapter the bounded readback is the controlled survival entry $S_{f3\text{-}stress,T_j}$, where $T_j$ is a tAI, stAI, ribosome-occupancy, or translation-efficiency row.  The intended support condition is
$$
\begin{aligned}
S_{f3\text{-}stress,T_j}>0
\end{aligned}
$$
after amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, length, $M$-density, matched mRNA, rank, and simpler-baseline controls.  The conjectural route fails locally if the controlled entry remains non-positive, rank-degenerate, or explainable by the simpler baseline.  In particular, a three-organism all-$1.0$ controlled surface is treated as rank saturation, not as survival evidence.

\paragraph{No promotion beyond the row.}
The finite result permits only this statement: in the available powered yeast elongation-dwell assay, the fourth $B^*_{Q6}$ residual axis has a partial elongation-dwell identity.  It does not by itself assert measured ribosome occupancy, protein abundance, protein folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, phenotype, causal mechanism, adaptation, or universality.  Those conclusions require their own layer-matched contacts and their own controlled statistics.  Likewise, modeled tAI or stAI weights are bounded codon-adaptation readbacks; they are not direct observations of ribosome dwell time unless paired with measured ribosome-profiling or translation-efficiency rows.  The chapter therefore records a finite positive direction for the fourth-axis identity while keeping downstream biological interpretations outside the claim.
