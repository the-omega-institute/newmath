\paragraph{Finite coordinate surface.}
The record considered here is the $B^*_{Q6}$ $f_3$-stress residual coordinate after amino-acid projection.  Its carrier is a finite CDS-by-organism residual surface restricted to the $f_3$-stress coordinate and to matched translation-readout rows.  The coordinate is not a raw codon-frequency axis: it is the part of synonymous imbalance that remains after amino-acid identity has been quotiented out, and it becomes biologically testable only when a translation-layer row is present.  The finite $f_3$ saturation row fixes the relevant codon support.  In the recorded run $76d3dc408a57$, the set $M=\operatorname{Med}(R)\cup\{\mathrm{ACA}\}$ is
$$
\begin{aligned}
M={}&\{\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{ACA},\mathrm{ACG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\\

The saturated $f_3$ image removes $\mathrm{ACA}$ and $\mathrm{ACG}$ and leaves
$$
\begin{aligned}

with empty $f_3$ boundary.  Thus the coordinate starts from a closed finite codon support, not from an open-ended biological vocabulary.

\paragraph{Readout rule.}
The controlled readback is the survival entry $S_{f3\text{-}stress,T_j}$ for a translation-layer row $T_j$, where $T_j$ may be a bounded tAI or stAI readout, a ribosome-occupancy row, or a translation-efficiency row.  The row is admissible only after controls for amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, CDS length, $M$-density, and matched mRNA abundance when translation efficiency is estimated from ribosome footprints.  In the intended test, support requires a strictly positive controlled entry,
$$
\begin{aligned}
S_{f3\text{-}stress,T_j}>0,
\end{aligned}
$$
under the declared controls and after simpler codon-baseline ablation.  A rank-degenerate surface, including an all-$1.0$ three-organism matrix, is a refusal boundary rather than survival evidence.

\paragraph{Observed finite row for elongation dwell.}
The elongation-dwell identity row has experiment id $\mathrm{b\_star\_q6\_fourth\_mechanism\_elongation\_dwell\_powered}$ and run id $\mathrm{e4edcde459b7}$. This is a deliberately narrow outcome: the row is compatible with an elongation-dwell component in yeast, but it is not a complete identification of the fourth axis and it is not a cross-organism law.

These rows justify asking what the fourth signal is, but they do not by themselves decide that elongation dwell is the dominant biological cause.

These exclusions leave elongation dwell as a plausible partial identity for the observed yeast row, not as a sole mechanism for every organism or every translation layer.

\paragraph{Wobble breaking and $f_3$ dynamics.}
The $f_3$ coordinate is inert unless the translation environment breaks third-position symmetry.  The relevant asymmetry has the schematic form
$$
\begin{aligned}
w_c \ne w_{c^{f_3}},
\end{aligned}
$$
where $w_c$ is a context tRNA or wobble weight.  The boundary-set tRNA-poverty run $d7728cc8768e$ gives one finite mechanism contact for this idea: it computed $40$ organisms out of $46$ requested, used $1000$ permutations at $\alpha=0.05$, and concluded that $R$ sense codons are systematically tRNA-poor under GtRNAdb tGCN plus dos Reis wobble weights.  That supports a tRNA-supply route for $R$ avoidance, but it remains a bounded supply-side readout.  It does not transform the $f_3$ residual coordinate into measured ribosome occupancy, protein abundance, folded structure, function, phenotype, or a universal rule.

\paragraph{Residual-axis context.}
The residual-axis rows also narrow the scope.  The universal residual optimality row $2a526c9fb7f4$ identified $\mathrm{f3\_translation\_selection}$ as the dominant non-tRNA residual axis over $11$ computed organisms out of $16$ requested, again with $5$ folds, at least $500$ proteins per organism, and $41$ synonymous-design columns.  The third-axis identity row $377671f96e96$ provisionally aligned $d_\perp$ to usage frequency within family, with mean cosine $0.128$ and one-sided sign-test $p=0.000488$.  The third-axis mechanism row $d0893337f0dd$ concluded $\mathrm{selection\text{-}not\text{-}GC}$, $\mathrm{not\text{-}wobble\text{-}clustered}$, and that CSC or positional identity still needs an additional contact.  The elongation-dwell row should therefore be read as a yeast-scoped partial fourth-axis explanation lying downstream of a finite residual spectrum, not as a replacement for the residual-axis decomposition.

\paragraph{Local cannot-claim boundary.}
This chapter licenses only the following finite statement: in the yeast-scoped powered row, the fourth-mechanism identity test for $B^*_{Q6}$ returned a passed, partial elongation-dwell call under $5$-fold evaluation.  It does not license a claim that $f_3$-stress has been observed as a realized translation effect in every organism.  It does not license folded-structure, physical-admissibility, molecular-function, localization, stability, phenotype, causal-mechanism, or universal-law claims.  Such claims require their own layer-matched contacts and their own controlled statistics.  In particular, codon geometry, tAI or stAI weights, ribosome-profiling contact names, mRNA-control contact names, and $S^{QT}$ notation are not enough unless same-organism or same-CDS rows have actually been joined and the relevant controlled statistic has been computed.

\paragraph{Verdict.}
The finite evidence supports a cautious identity statement: elongation dwell is a partial yeast-scoped candidate for the fourth $B^*_{Q6}$ mechanism, consistent with the surrounding residual spectrum and with exclusions of mRNA stability, codon-pair structure, and translation accuracy as dominant explanations in their respective assays.  The strongest current phrasing is therefore not that $f_3$-stress causes translation output, but that the $f_3$-stress residual has a bounded, symmetry-breaking translation-readout test whose yeast fourth-mechanism row points partially toward elongation dwell.  The conjecture remains falsifiable: if powered, same-row panels with codon counts, wobble weights, measured translation readouts, matched mRNA controls, and the declared covariates keep $S_{f3\text{-}stress,T_j}\le 0$ or remain rank-degenerate, the translation-readout interpretation fails at this boundary.
