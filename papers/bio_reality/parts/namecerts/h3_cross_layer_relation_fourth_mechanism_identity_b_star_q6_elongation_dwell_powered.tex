\paragraph{Coordinate under test.}
This record isolates the $f_3$-stress residual coordinate in the $B^*_{Q6}$ coding-sequence surface after amino-acid projection.  The carrier is the finite CDS-by-organism residual surface restricted to the $f_3$-paired synonymous imbalance coordinate, together with matched rows for codon usage, tRNA or wobble weights, and a bounded translation readout.  The question is not whether the codon geometry is visible in sequence space; it is whether the same coordinate remains visible after the nuisance variables for amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, CDS length, $M$-density, and mRNA-level controls have been separated.

\paragraph{Finite saturation row.}
The $f_3$ boundary row used here is anchored in the computed saturation datum
$$
\begin{aligned}
M &= \{\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{ACA},\mathrm{ACG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\\

\mathrm{Sat}_{f_3}(R) &= M\setminus\{\mathrm{ACA},\mathrm{ACG}\},\\

\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the finite surface contains $20$ codons in $M$, $18$ codons in the $f_3$-saturated $R$ row, and an empty residual boundary for the listed saturation test.  This matters because the dwell-time claim is not allowed to rely on an informal third-position story: the row must be read as a concrete residual coordinate whose paired codon imbalance is tested only after the amino-acid quotient has already been imposed.

\paragraph{Powered dwell-time result.}
The dwell-time identity run for the fourth mechanism row passed its execution gate with experiment id \texttt{b\_star\_q6\_fourth\_mechanism\_elongation\_dwell\_powered}. I therefore record the row as evidence that elongation dwell is a surviving component of the fourth-mechanism search, but not as a closed identification of the whole fourth axis. $$
\begin{aligned}
S_{f_3\text{-stress},T_{\mathrm{dwell}}} &> 0\quad \text{on the bounded yeast dwell-time assay row,}

The word \texttt{partial} is essential: it permits a positive translation-layer component while refusing promotion to a unique mechanism label.

\paragraph{Relation to the surrounding fourth-axis screen.}
The same finite audit separates this partial dwell signal from several nearby negatives.  The mRNA-stability row passed a $5$-fold assay with $41$ synonymous design columns and concluded \texttt{fourth\_axis\_not\_mrna\_stability}.  The codon-pair row also used $5$ folds, $41$ synonymous design columns, and $50$ shuffle-null replicates, concluding \texttt{not\_codon\_pair}.  The translation-accuracy row computed $11$ of $16$ requested organisms, used $41$ synonymous design columns and $200$ permutations, and concluded \texttt{not\_accuracy}.  These exclusions make the dwell row more specific than a generic codon-usage association, but they do not by themselves supply a full mechanistic closure.

\paragraph{Axis context.}
The broader powered panel reports that a fourth mechanism is present after the three-axis model: the optimality-axis sufficiency run computed $11$ of $16$ requested organisms, used $5$ folds, required at least $500$ proteins per organism, and used $41$ synonymous design columns.  The axis-strength spectrum computed the same $11$ of $16$ organism panel and found that captured spectra are led by $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$ on average, with per-organism dominant counts $\{d_{\mathrm{resid4}}:11\}$; the domain counts were imbalanced, with $1$ archaeon, $2$ bacteria, and $8$ eukaryotes, and the largest GC3 rank effect was $\rho=0.136$ for $d_{\mathrm{usage}}$.  A related third-axis identity row assigned $d_\perp$ provisionally to within-family usage frequency with mean cosine $0.128$ and one-sided sign-test $p=0.000488$.  These rows constrain the interpretation: the dwell result belongs to a finite residual-axis screen rather than to an unconstrained biological narrative.

\paragraph{Wobble-breaking condition.}
The conjectural biological reading is that the $f_3$-paired coordinate becomes dynamically visible only where context tRNA or wobble weights break the third-position symmetry.  The boundary-set run gives one concrete reason to treat tRNA supply as a live mechanism class: for the $R$ sense-codon set, $40$ of $46$ requested organisms were computed, $6$ were skipped, and a $1000$-permutation test at $\alpha=0.05$ supported systematic tRNA poverty under GtRNAdb tGCN with dos Reis wobble weights.  That row supports the plausibility of a wobble-weight gate, not an automatic translation-readout survival claim for every organism or context.

\paragraph{Readout rule.}
The local survival entry must be a controlled translation-row statistic, written here as $S_{f_3\text{-stress},T_j}$ for $T_j$ equal to a bounded tAI, stAI, ribosome-occupancy, translation-efficiency, or dwell-time readout.  A three-organism all-$1.0$ controlled surface is treated as rank saturation and not as survival.  A modeled adaptation weight is a bounded translation-layer annotation, not an observed ribosome footprint.  A positive yeast dwell component is therefore admissible only at the translation-readout layer and only with the recorded finite assay scope.

\paragraph{Local no-promotion boundary.}
No claim about protein abundance, folded structure, physical admissibility, localization, stability, molecular function, phenotype, a complete mechanism, or a universal biological law follows from this row.  Such claims require their own layer-matched contact and their own controlled statistic.  The present result says only that the $B^*_{Q6}$ $f_3$-stress coordinate has a partial, finite, yeast-scoped elongation-dwell signal inside the fourth-mechanism screen, while neighboring mRNA-stability, codon-pair, and translation-accuracy explanations are disfavored by their own powered rows.
