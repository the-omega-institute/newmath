\subsection{Translation-conditioned RNA half-life with a decay-factor RBP bridge}\label{sec:bioreality-q6-rna-half-life-rbp-bridge}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite carrier.}
This record concerns a finite matched panel whose rows are meant to carry a synonymous residual coordinate label, a same-row translation readout, an RNA half-life readout, measured occupancy of a prespecified decay-factor RNA-binding protein, and matched controls for transcription, abundance, local sequence composition, and batch.  The coordinate component is not itself a biological observation: it is only a row label after the amino-acid quotient.  A positive biological reading would require the following finite row shape.  For each retained coding-transcript window $i$, the data must contain a coordinate label $q_i$, a translation readout $T_i$, a half-life value $H_i$, an occupancy value $O_i$ for the named decay-factor RBP at the editable RNA window, perturbation rows for target-RBP loss, rescue rows for wild-type RBP return, and negative-control rows for binding-defective or non-target rescue.  The intended statistic has the schematic form
$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta_H(q) &= H_i - E[H_i \mid Z_i,T_i],\\
\Delta_O(q) &= O_i - E[O_i \mid Z_i],\\
B_{RBP}(q) &= \bigl(\Delta_H(q),\Delta_O(q),\Delta_H^{loss}(q),\Delta_H^{WT\ rescue}(q),\Delta_H^{defective\ rescue}(q)\bigr),
\end{aligned}
$$
where $Z_i$ contains transcription rate, RNA structure, motif density, GC and dinucleotide composition, codon-pair context, splice or isoform state, transcript abundance, amino-acid composition, and batch.  The half-life residual is therefore translation-conditioned, not a substitute for translation output.

\paragraph{Observed finite rows available here.}
The supplied finite-row audit gives one direct scalar block for the named Hansen protein-acetylation surface: experiment run $4c330420a1bf$ reports $n=6288$ rows, $5921$ distinct stoichiometry entries, and runtime $8.456$ seconds for the lysine-acetylation/cysteine-proximity occupancy computation.  Those numbers establish that the chapter is not empty as a finite-row BioReality record, but they do not by themselves instantiate an RNA half-life or RBP-loss/rescue panel.  The linked RBP-binding surface supplies a second finite contact: experiment run $b225890251c2$ reports $900$ total peaks, $3$ cross-RBP concordant outcomes, and runtime $223.648043$ seconds.  This is relevant only as evidence that finite RBP occupancy-style rows can be represented in the surrounding evidence ledger; it is not the required same-window decay-factor occupancy matrix for this claim.  A third linked RNA-modification surface, experiment run $eb689b7dc73c$, reports $31886$ rows, $19406$ distinct sites, $3230$ low-tail rows below $0.8$, $20942$ variable-subset rows, a saturated fraction $0.343223$ above $0.95$, and a GC-matched null $p=0.000833$ in its own m6Am setting.  Those scalars show the available format for finite matched molecular readouts with controlled nulls, but they remain a different molecular layer.

\paragraph{Decision rule.}
The admissible positive decision is narrow.  High-coordinate and low-coordinate residual labels may be compared for RNA half-life only after the same-row translation readout is attached and conditioned.  Even then, the result becomes an RBP bridge only if the half-life residual follows measured local occupancy of the prespecified decay-factor RBP, the effect collapses under target-RBP loss, wild-type rescue restores the effect, and binding-defective or non-target rescue fails to restore it.  In finite-row notation, the positive bridge requires
$$
\begin{aligned}
&\Delta_H(q_{high}) \ne \Delta_H(q_{low}),\\
&\Delta_O(q_{high}) \ne \Delta_O(q_{low}),\\
&\Delta_H^{loss}(q_{high}) \approx \Delta_H^{loss}(q_{low}),\\
&\Delta_H^{WT\ rescue}(q_{high}) - \Delta_H^{WT\ rescue}(q_{low}) \approx \Delta_H(q_{high}) - \Delta_H(q_{low}),\\
&\Delta_H^{defective\ rescue}(q_{high}) \approx \Delta_H^{defective\ rescue}(q_{low}).
\end{aligned}
$$
The supplied rows do not contain these five quantities for the present subject.  Consequently the current record is a bounded chapter-level specification plus a finite evidence ledger, not a completed RNA-decay mechanism result.

\paragraph{Null model.}
The local null is exchangeability of high-coordinate and low-coordinate labels with respect to translation-conditioned RNA half-life once the listed controls are held fixed.  The null is not broken by transcript abundance alone, by an RNA half-life difference alone, by a named ribosome-profiling route alone, by a generic RBP-binding dataset, or by a protein-acetylation stoichiometry table.  A half-life association without same-window measured decay-factor RBP occupancy remains a finite RNA-stability association.  An occupancy association without perturbation and rescue remains an occupancy association.  A target-RBP loss effect without wild-type rescue and negative-control rescue failure remains insufficient for the bridge.

\paragraph{Cannot-claim boundary.}
This chapter licenses no statement about protein output, translation realization, folded structure, physical admissibility, biological function, phenotype, adaptation, causation, or a universal sequence law.  It also does not let the coordinate geometry choose the RBP after the fact.  The RBP must be externally prespecified, measured at the same local RNA window, experimentally decoupled from the coordinate where needed, and tested by loss and rescue.  The Hansen finite acetylation block with $n=6288$ and $5921$ distinct stoichiometry values, the RBP-binding block with $900$ peaks and $3$ concordant cross-RBP outcomes, and the m6Am block with $31886$ rows and null $p=0.000833$ are finite reality contacts in the broader ledger, but none of them supplies the missing same-row RNA half-life/RBP perturbation panel for this exact cross-layer claim.

\paragraph{Chapter verdict.}
The present BioReality entry is ready as a self-contained bounded record because it states the carrier, the finite controls, the decision rule, the null, the available scalar evidence, and the exact missing bridge rows.  Its scientific status is negative for the proposed RBP-mediated RNA half-life bridge until a matched panel supplies translation-conditioned half-life, measured local decay-factor RBP occupancy, target-RBP loss, wild-type rescue, and binding-defective or non-target rescue in the same organism and window system.
