\subsection{Translation-conditioned RNA half-life as a bounded RBP-bridge record}
\label{sec:bioreality-q6-translation-conditioned-rna-half-life-rbp-bridge}

\paragraph{Finite row carrier.}
The finite object in this record is a matched table of coding-transcript rows.  Each row carries a synonymous residual coordinate label after quotienting by amino-acid identity, a same-row translation readout or an explicit translation-efficiency boundary, an RNA half-life readout, matched transcript-abundance controls, and a declared place where a decay-factor RNA-binding protein could be measured.  The coordinate is not itself a biological measurement.  It is a finite label used to sort rows into high-coordinate and low-coordinate classes after amino-acid sequence has been held fixed.  A positive interpretation would require the same row to contain all of the following finite entries: the coordinate label, translation readout, RNA half-life, measured local occupancy of a prespecified decay-factor RBP, target-RBP loss, wild-type rescue, and binding-defective or non-target rescue controls.

\paragraph{Observed finite scalars.}
The payload supplies several bounded row audits, but it does not yet supply the complete RBP-perturbation table required for the present bridge.  The primary linked finite audit, \texttt{h3.cross\_layer\_relation.protein\_acetylation.lysine\_acetylation\_cys\_proximity\_occupancy\_hansen2019}, reports $n=6288$ rows, $5921$ distinct stoichiometry-bearing entries, and runtime $8.456$ seconds.  This establishes that the BioReality ledger can store a high-cardinality post-translational occupancy-style panel, but it is not an RNA half-life or RBP-loss/rescue panel.  A separate RBP-contact audit, \texttt{h3.cross\_layer\_relation.rbp\_binding.rbns\_affinity\_eclip\_binding\_encode}, reports $900$ total peak rows, $3$ cross-RBP concordant cases, and runtime $223.648043$ seconds.  This is relevant as a finite RBP-binding surface, but it is not the same-window decay-factor occupancy, target loss, and rescue matrix demanded here.  The linked N-terminal acetylation audit, \texttt{h3.cross\_layer\_relation.nterminal\_acetylation.nterminal\_acetylation\_occupancy\_kron2018}, reports $n=657$, $426$ NatA rows, $622$ high-low rows for AUROC evaluation, and AUROC $0.942081$.  The RNA methylation audit, \texttt{h3.cross\_layer\_relation.rna\_m6am\_methylation.m6am\_flanking\_identity\_stoichiometry\_crownseq}, reports $n=31886$, $19406$ distinct entries, cross-cell concordance true, $3230$ low-tail rows below $0.8$, $20942$ variable-subset rows, and saturated fraction $0.343223$ at the $0.95$ threshold.  These scalar contacts show finite biological tables with row counts, concordance flags, and measured stoichiometric or binding readouts; none of them by itself supplies the joined translation-conditioned RNA-decay RBP-bridge matrix.

\paragraph{Local statistic.}
Let $q_i$ denote the synonymous residual coordinate label on row $i$, let $T_i$ denote the same-row translation readout, let $H_i$ denote RNA half-life, let $O_i$ denote measured local occupancy of the prespecified decay-factor RBP, and let $L_i$, $W_i$, and $B_i$ denote target-RBP loss, wild-type rescue, and binding-defective or non-target rescue arms.  The intended bridge statistic is finite and row-local:
$$
\begin{aligned}
  q_i &\in \{\mathrm{high},\mathrm{low}\},\\
  T_i &\text{ is attached before any RNA-half-life interpretation is promoted},\\
  H_i &\text{ is evaluated after transcription, abundance, isoform, structure, motif, composition, codon-pair, batch, and amino-acid controls},\\
  O_i &\text{ must vary with the editable local window},\\
  L_i &\text{ must collapse the half-life effect},\\
  W_i &\text{ must restore the effect},\\
  B_i &\text{ must fail to restore it.}
\end{aligned}
$$
A row panel that lacks any one of $O_i$, $L_i$, $W_i$, or $B_i$ can still be used as a bounded RNA-stability association table, but it cannot be read as an RBP-mediated decay bridge.  The same-row translation readout is a conditioning boundary, not a proof of RNA decay mechanism.

\paragraph{Matched null.}
The local null says that, after controlling transcription rate, local RNA structure, motif density, GC and dinucleotide composition, codon-pair context, splice or isoform state, transcript abundance, batch, amino-acid composition, and the attached translation readout, high-coordinate and low-coordinate labels are exchangeable with respect to RNA half-life.  In finite-row notation the blocked case is
$$
\begin{aligned}
  H_i \perp q_i \mid Z_i,T_i
\end{aligned}
$$
where $Z_i$ collects the declared non-translation controls.  Even when this exchangeability fails, the interpretation remains bounded unless the measured local RBP occupancy and perturbation/rescue arms satisfy the bridge pattern.  A half-life difference without an occupancy shift is not an RBP bridge.  An occupancy association without target-RBP loss is not a decay mechanism.  Loss without wild-type rescue is not a specific bridge.  Rescue by a binding-defective or non-target control breaks the proposed interpretation.

\paragraph{Name certificate readback.}
The certificate therefore names a precise finite readback rather than a broad biological law.  It asks whether a coordinate-associated RNA half-life residual survives only when translation is attached on the same row and whether the residual follows an experimentally decoupled decay-factor RBP occupancy pattern.  The required finite readback has five parts: a translation-conditioned RNA half-life effect, a local RBP occupancy change at the editable RNA window, collapse under target-RBP loss, return under wild-type rescue, and failure under binding-defective or non-target controls.  If the row table supplies only RNA half-life, matched mRNA abundance, ribosome profiling, or a generic RBP-binding surface, the certificate remains open at the mechanism boundary.

\paragraph{Cannot-claim boundary.}
This chapter licenses no claim about protein output, protein folding, structural order, physical admissibility, biological function, phenotype, adaptation, causation, or universality.  It also licenses no translation realization from RNA half-life alone and no RBP-decay mechanism from contact names alone.  The protein-acetylation audit with $6288$ rows, the RBP-binding audit with $900$ peak rows, the N-terminal-acetylation audit with AUROC $0.942081$, and the m6Am audit with $31886$ rows are finite biological contacts, but they are not substitutes for the same-window decay-factor occupancy, loss, and rescue rows demanded by this particular bridge.  The present result is therefore a ready self-contained BioReality record for a bounded cross-layer test: it fixes the carrier, the null, the finite statistic, and the break conditions, while keeping every higher biological interpretation outside the claim unless a separate layer-matched contact supplies it.
