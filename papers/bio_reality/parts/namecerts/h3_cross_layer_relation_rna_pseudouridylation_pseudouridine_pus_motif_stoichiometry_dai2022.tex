\subsection{Pseudouridine motif stoichiometry in matched coding rows}\label{sec:h3-cross-layer-rna-pseudouridylation-dai2022}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite panel and coordinate meaning.} This record treats the Dai 2022 pseudouridylation panel as a finite matched transcript/CDS data set rather than as a universal RNA rule. The verified panel contains $n=2040$ rows and the recorded evaluation runtime is $4.859092$ seconds. Each row is read as a coding-window coordinate with a local pseudouridine or PUS-motif stoichiometry readback, so the claim stays at the level of a bounded association between an RNA-window label and an observable stoichiometric signal. The coordinate is therefore a bookkeeping object for a finite matched panel, not a general biological law and not a surrogate for any downstream layer.

\paragraph{What the panel can support.} Within that finite panel, the relevant statement is that pseudouridine-associated motif stoichiometry can be compared across matched rows after the transcript/CDS alignment is fixed. The evidence is compatible with a local cross-layer relation in which the RNA window carries a measurable motif or stoichiometry signal and the panel admits row matching as its control surface. For scale, the same paper also records other row-bounded readbacks: one RNA-binding panel tracks $n=900$ peaks with $3$ cross-RBP concordant instances, and one m6Am stoichiometry panel spans $n=31886$ rows with cross-cell concordance marked true and a saturated-geq-0.95 fraction of $0.343223$. Those neighboring finite panels reinforce the interpretation here: the unit of evidence is a bounded row set with an explicit scalar readback, not a diffuse mechanistic story.

\paragraph{Boundary.} This record does not on its own support translation, folding, physical admissibility, function, mechanism, or universality. It also does not promote pseudouridylation stoichiometry into protein output, phenotype, or causal mechanism. A separate contact would have to supply a layer-matched readout with the same row keys and an independently measured downstream statistic before any such conclusion could be stated. Until that happens, the claim remains a finite RNA-level readback over $2040$ matched rows, suitable only as an observable summary of pseudouridine and PUS-motif stoichiometry in the present panel.
