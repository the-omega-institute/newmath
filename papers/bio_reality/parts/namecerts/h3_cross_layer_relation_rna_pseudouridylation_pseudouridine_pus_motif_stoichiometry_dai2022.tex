\subsection{Pseudouridine Stoichiometry at a Translation-Conditioned RNA-Stability Boundary}\label{sec:bioreality-pseudouridine-pus-motif-stoichiometry-dai2022}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite panel.}
The record concerns a finite matched RNA panel in which coding transcript rows carry residual $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ coordinate labels, synonymous labels are compared only after the amino-acid quotient, and every promoted RNA-stability interpretation must be conditioned on a same-row translation readout.  The direct pseudouridine finite-row audit for \texttt{h3.cross\_layer\_relation.rna\_pseudouridylation.pseudouridine\_pus\_motif\_stoichiometry\_dai2022} contains $2040$ rows and completed in $4.859092$ seconds under run \texttt{cf233b5f0446}.  This row count is the only positive scalar supplied for the pseudouridine packet: the verified payload gives no $R$ list, no $M$ list, no $\lambda$ value, no $p$-value, no module class, and no hit-rate field.  Consequently the finite evidence can support only a bounded pseudouridine stoichiometry surface, not a completed RNA-decay mechanism.

\paragraph{Local coordinate statement.}
Let $i$ range over the $2040$ audited rows, let $q_i$ denote the post-amino-acid-quotient $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ coordinate label, let $\psi_i$ denote the pseudouridine or PUS-motif stoichiometry readout, let $T_i$ denote the same-row translation readout when present, let $H_i$ denote RNA half-life, and let $O_i$ denote measured local occupancy of a prespecified decay-factor RNA-binding protein at the editable RNA window.  The admissible finite test is not the bare association $q_i \mapsto H_i$.  The admissible test has the form
$$
\begin{aligned}
& q_i \text{ high rather than low after the amino-acid quotient} \\
& \quad \Longrightarrow H_i \text{ shifts only after conditioning on } T_i, \\
& \quad \Longrightarrow O_i \text{ changes for the prespecified decay-factor RBP}, \\
& \quad \Longrightarrow \text{the } H_i \text{ effect collapses after target-RBP loss}, \\
& \quad \Longrightarrow \text{wild-type rescue restores the effect}, \\
& \quad \Longrightarrow \text{binding-defective or non-target rescue fails to restore it.}
\end{aligned}
$$
Without the whole chain, the coordinate remains a finite descriptor of pseudouridine-associated RNA rows or, at most, a translation-conditioned RNA-stability association.

\paragraph{Matched controls.}
The null model keeps high-coordinate and low-coordinate synonymous labels exchangeable with respect to RNA half-life after controlling transcription rate, local RNA structure, motif density, GC and dinucleotide composition, codon-pair context, splice or isoform state, transcript abundance, batch, amino-acid composition, and the same-row translation readout.  The mRNA-abundance control is a denominator or covariate for separating translation efficiency from transcriptional abundance; it is not itself a half-life measurement.  The ribosome-profiling contact can supply a translation-efficiency boundary when it is joined to matched mRNA rows, but it does not supply RNA half-life, RBP occupancy, perturbation, or rescue evidence.

\paragraph{RBP bridge requirement.}
A separate RBP-binding audit gives useful scale for the bridge requirement but does not close this pseudouridine claim by itself.  The verified RBP-binding record \texttt{h3.cross\_layer\_relation.rbp\_binding.rbns\_affinity\_eclip\_binding\_encode} reports $900$ total peaks, $3$ cross-RBP concordant cases, and runtime $223.648043$ seconds under run \texttt{b225890251c2}.  Those numbers show that finite RBP occupancy-style surfaces can be represented in the BioReality ledger, but they are not same-organism, same-window perturbation and rescue rows for the $2040$ pseudouridine panel.  Therefore they cannot be substituted for the required measured decay-factor RBP occupancy, coordinate/occupancy decoupling, target-RBP loss, wild-type rescue, and binding-defective or non-target rescue tests.

\paragraph{Stoichiometry comparison.}
A separate RNA-modification stoichiometry audit, \texttt{h3.cross\_layer\_relation.rna\_m6a\_stoichiometry.m6a\_stoichiometry\_exon\_architecture\_glori}, records $60000$ rows, $49668$ distinct entries, cross-condition concordance equal to \texttt{true}, coverage-confound correlation $-0.135922814$, and runtime $78.316$ seconds under run \texttt{2e8583415e99}.  This comparison is useful only as a calibration point for finite stoichiometry bookkeeping: it shows that a larger RNA-modification panel may carry explicit cross-condition and confound scalars, whereas the pseudouridine packet here exposes only $n=2040$ and runtime.  The absence of a pseudouridine-side $p$-value, concordance flag, or confound scalar keeps the present chapter at a bounded claim surface rather than a positive mechanistic conclusion.

\paragraph{Readback.}
The finite readback is therefore precise but narrow.  The packet may report that the pseudouridine/PUS-motif stoichiometry audit has $2040$ rows and that it is aligned with a proposed translation-conditioned RNA-half-life and decay-factor-RBP bridge design.  It may also state the exact evidence still required for a positive bridge: matched RNA half-life, matched mRNA abundance, same-row translation readout, measured local decay-factor RBP occupancy, coordinate/occupancy decoupling, target-RBP loss, wild-type rescue, binding-defective or non-target rescue failure, and a declared finite statistic computed under the controls above.  It may not infer translation realization from half-life alone, may not infer an RBP-decay mechanism from a half-life shift without the occupancy and perturbation pattern, and may not treat pseudouridine motif density, RNA structure, GC or dinucleotide composition, codon-pair context, isoform state, transcript abundance, or batch behavior as support for the RBP bridge.
