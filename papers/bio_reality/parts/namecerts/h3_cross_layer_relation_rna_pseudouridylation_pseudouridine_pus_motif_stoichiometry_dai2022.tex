\subsection{Translation-conditioned pseudouridine windows and the RBP-decay boundary}
\label{sec:bioreality-pseudouridine-pus-motif-rbp-decay-boundary}

\paragraph{Finite row carrier.}
This record treats the Dai-style pseudouridine/PUS-motif panel as a finite RNA-row surface rather than as a mechanism claim.  The verified subject-specific run for \texttt{h3.cross\_layer\_relation.rna\_pseudouridylation.pseudouridine\_pus\_motif\_stoichiometry\_dai2022} contains $2040$ finite rows and completed in $4.859092$ seconds.  Those rows are sufficient for a bounded statement about measured pseudouridine-related transcript windows, motif-context rows, and stoichiometric RNA-modification readout, but they do not by themselves contain the joined variables required for a translation-conditioned RNA-half-life RBP bridge.  The proposed carrier is therefore a stricter matched panel: each row must carry a $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual coordinate label after amino-acid quotienting, a same-row translation readout, an RNA half-life readout, local occupancy of a prespecified decay-factor RBP at the editable RNA window, perturbation status for that RBP, rescue status, and matched controls for transcription, abundance, local RNA structure, motif density, composition, codon-pair context, splice or isoform state, and batch.

\paragraph{Coordinate distinction.}
The coordinate split is not a claim that pseudouridine or a PUS motif changes translation, RNA decay, or protein output.  It is only the finite comparison between high-coordinate and low-coordinate synonymous residual labels after the amino-acid sequence has been quotiented away.  In the intended joined table, the statistical contrast is evaluated only after the translation readout is present on the same row.  A schematic row has the form
$$
\begin{aligned}
  r_i = (&q_i, T_i, H_i, O_i, L_i, R_i, C_i),
\end{aligned}
$$
where $q_i$ is the residual coordinate label, $T_i$ is the same-row translation readout, $H_i$ is RNA half-life, $O_i$ is measured local occupancy of the declared decay-factor RBP, $L_i$ records target-RBP loss or depletion, $R_i$ records rescue class, and $C_i$ is the finite control block.  The pseudouridine/PUS rows supply the modification-side finite contact; the bridge requires the additional joined fields $T_i$, $H_i$, $O_i$, $L_i$, and $R_i$ on the same biological rows or on a declared matched panel with a finite identifier map.

\paragraph{Positive bridge test.}
A positive cross-layer bridge would require a coordinated pattern across four finite comparisons.  First, after conditioning on $T_i$ and the control block $C_i$, the high-coordinate and low-coordinate groups must differ in RNA half-life.  Second, the same coordinate contrast must track experimentally decoupled local occupancy of the prespecified decay-factor RBP, not merely motif density, RNA structure, transcript abundance, or batch.  Third, loss of the target RBP must collapse the half-life effect.  Fourth, wild-type target-RBP rescue must restore the effect while binding-defective rescue or a non-target control fails to restore it.  In compact form, the requested finite pattern is
$$
\begin{aligned}
  q_i &\not\perp H_i \mid T_i, C_i,\\
  q_i &\not\perp O_i \mid T_i, C_i,\\
  \Delta_H(q_i \mid T_i, C_i, L_i=\mathrm{loss}) &\approx 0,\\
  \Delta_H(q_i \mid T_i, C_i, R_i=\mathrm{wildtype}) &\text{ returns while defective or non-target rescue does not.}
\end{aligned}
$$
The current finite evidence does not yet contain those same-window RBP occupancy, target-loss, and rescue fields for this subject.  Consequently, the correct status is a bounded RNA-modification and motif-stoichiometry contact plus a proposed stricter bridge test, not a completed RNA-decay mechanism.

\paragraph{Comparison with richer RNA-modification finite panels.}
The surrounding audit bundle shows why the present pseudouridine packet should remain conservative.  The m6Am Crown-seq record contains $31886$ rows, $19406$ distinct sites, $3230$ low-tail rows with value below $0.8$, a saturated-at-least-$0.95$ fraction of $0.343223$, a variable subset of $20942$ rows, cross-cell concordance marked true, and a reported runtime of $324.086$ seconds.  Its stored finite fingerprint also reports, within that separate RNA-modification panel, $1200$ actual main trials, a GC-matched null $p$-value of $0.000833$, partial correlations $0.268094$, $0.098666$, and $0.168335$ for the full, low-tail, and variable subsets, and positive-control counts including $6902$ plus-two-A rows and $14263$ plus-two-G rows.  The m6A stoichiometry record contains $60000$ rows, $49668$ distinct sites, cross-condition concordance marked true, a coverage-confound correlation of $-0.135922814$, and a runtime of $78.316$ seconds.  These richer finite scalar records show that RNA-modification panels can support detailed bounded readbacks when the relevant row fields are present.  They do not supply the missing pseudouridine-specific RBP loss and rescue fields, and they are not evidence for the present pseudouridine bridge.

\paragraph{Null interpretation.}
The operative null is row exchangeability of high-coordinate and low-coordinate labels with respect to RNA half-life after conditioning on transcription rate, local RNA structure, motif density, GC and dinucleotide composition, codon-pair context, splice or isoform state, transcript abundance, batch, amino-acid composition, and same-row translation readout.  If an RNA half-life difference is observed without a matched local RBP-occupancy change, without collapse under target-RBP loss, or without wild-type rescue and failure of binding-defective or non-target controls, the observation remains an RNA-stability association or coordinate descriptor.  It is not an RBP-mediated bridge.

\paragraph{Local scope boundary.}
No translation realization follows from RNA half-life alone.  No decay-factor RBP mechanism follows from a pseudouridine/PUS motif association alone.  No protein output, folded structure, physical admissibility, biological function, phenotype, adaptation, causation, or universal law follows from the $2040$-row pseudouridine run, from $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual geometry, or from the presence of translation-readout contact names.  Each of those stronger claims requires its own finite layer-matched contact and statistic.  The present chapter therefore records a precise missing-data boundary: the pseudouridine/PUS finite panel is usable as a bounded RNA-modification surface, while the translation-conditioned RNA-half-life RBP bridge remains unlicensed until same-organism, same-window RBP occupancy, target-RBP loss, wild-type rescue, and binding-defective or non-target control rows are joined to the coordinate, translation, mRNA-control, and RNA-half-life fields.
