\subsection{Pseudouridine Motif Stoichiometry as a Bounded RNA-Side Contact}
\label{sec:bioreality-pseudouridine-pus-motif-stoichiometry-dai2022}

\paragraph{Finite row surface.}
This record treats the Dai2022 pseudouridylation panel as a finite RNA-side measurement surface rather than as a direct claim about translation, protein state, or cellular phenotype. The usable rows are transcript or site records carrying pseudouridine stoichiometry together with motif-window information for the relevant pseudouridine synthase context. The verified run for \texttt{h3.cross\_layer\_relation.rna\_pseudouridylation.pseudouridine\_pus\_motif\_stoichiometry\_dai2022} contains $2040$ finite rows and completed in $4.859092$ seconds under run identifier \texttt{cf233b5f0446}. Those numbers fix the empirical scale of this chapter: the discussion is about a measured collection of RNA modification rows, not an asymptotic sequence law and not a genome-wide universal principle.

\paragraph{Coordinate distinction.}
For each row, the coordinate information is read only as a bounded label on an RNA window after the relevant amino-acid and transcript-level controls have been separated from the RNA modification observation. The intended contrast is between higher-coordinate and lower-coordinate synonymous residual labels after amino-acid quotienting, but the observed datum in this chapter is still pseudouridine stoichiometry at an RNA site. The coordinate label may organize the panel; it does not itself measure ribosome occupancy, RNA decay, protein abundance, folded protein structure, physical admissibility, biological function, adaptation, or a general biological law.

\paragraph{Motif and stoichiometry statement.}
The local scientific statement is the following finite-row assertion. In the $2040$-row Dai2022 pseudouridylation panel, a pseudouridine synthase motif-window association may be reported only as an RNA-modification stoichiometry association when the row carries the measured pseudouridine readout and the motif or window annotation used to stratify it. In symbolic form, the admissible readout has the shape
$$
\begin{aligned}
  r_i &= (q_i, w_i, \psi_i, z_i),\\
  \Delta_\psi &= \operatorname{Stat}\{\psi_i \mid q_i \in Q_{\mathrm{high}}, w_i, z_i\}
              - \operatorname{Stat}\{\psi_i \mid q_i \in Q_{\mathrm{low}}, w_i, z_i\},
\end{aligned}
$$
where $q_i$ is the residual coordinate label, $w_i$ is the local RNA window or motif annotation, $\psi_i$ is measured pseudouridine stoichiometry, and $z_i$ denotes the finite list of matched controls actually available for the panel. The displayed statistic licenses only a bounded difference in pseudouridine stoichiometry across the finite matched rows. It does not license a claim that the same rows establish translation realization, RNA half-life control, RBP-mediated decay, protein output, structure, function, causation, or universality.

\paragraph{RBP bridge is not supplied by the pseudouridine panel.}
The conjectural cross-layer route requires more than a pseudouridine motif signal. A decay-factor RBP bridge would need same-window measured RBP occupancy, a separation between coordinate labels and occupancy, target-RBP loss, wild-type rescue, and binding-defective or non-target controls on the same finite panel used for the RNA half-life statistic. The available RBP-binding verification is a separate finite contact: \texttt{h3.cross\_layer\_relation.rbp\_binding.rbns\_affinity\_eclip\_binding\_encode} reports $900$ total peak rows, $3$ cross-RBP-concordant cases, and runtime $223.648043$ seconds under run identifier \texttt{b225890251c2}. Those rows show that an RBP binding surface exists elsewhere in the evidence ledger, but they do not, by themselves, join the Dai2022 pseudouridine rows to RNA half-life, measured decay-factor occupancy, target-RBP perturbation, or rescue behavior.

\paragraph{Local null.}
The null model is finite and row-wise: after conditioning on the available transcript, motif-window, composition, abundance, batch, and coordinate controls, high-coordinate and low-coordinate labels are exchangeable with respect to pseudouridine stoichiometry unless the declared finite statistic separates them. For an RNA half-life or decay-factor RBP interpretation, the null is stricter. A half-life association remains a bounded RNA-stability association unless measured local decay-factor RBP occupancy changes in the required direction, the effect collapses after target-RBP loss, wild-type rescue restores it, and binding-defective or non-target rescue fails. Without that joined perturbation and rescue panel, the pseudouridine rows cannot be promoted to an RBP-decay mechanism.

\paragraph{Admissible conclusion.}
The chapter therefore records a bounded RNA modification contact with $2040$ measured Dai2022 rows. It may support a finite statement about pseudouridine stoichiometry organized by local motif-window and residual-coordinate labels. It may also motivate a future joined assay in which pseudouridine status, translation readout, RNA half-life, decay-factor RBP occupancy, target-RBP loss, wild-type rescue, and defective-control rescue are measured on matched rows. Until that joined assay exists, the evidential boundary is local: no translation, folding, physical admissibility, protein function, RNA-decay mechanism, adaptation, causation, phenotype, or universal biological law follows from this panel alone.
