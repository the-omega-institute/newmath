\subsection{Reach of the  exorpdfstring{$f3\_stress$}{f3-stress} Coordinate by Independent Readouts}\label{sec:bioreality-f3-stress-readout-reach}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite claim.}
This record tests a single coordinate-level question for \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}: after protein abundance is residualized against the stated background basis, how much of the abundance component carried by the $f3\_stress$ coordinate can be reached by a finite panel of independent readouts?  The target vector is
$$
\begin{aligned}
P_{f3} = \operatorname{Proj}_{x_{f3\_stress}}\bigl(Z\text{-residualized } \log_{10}(\mathrm{protein\ abundance})\bigr),
\end{aligned}
$$
where the unified control basis has rank $24$.  The base dictionary has rank $3$ and is evaluated on $n=1258$ joined rows.  The expanded finite search ranges over all $512$ subsets of the nine requested independent readouts, including the empty subset; the selected and full readout designs both have rank $19$ and are evaluated on $n=1056$ joined rows.  Thus this is a finite projection audit, not an inference about a hidden biological cause.

\paragraph{Projection numbers.}
The base design covers $0.1897720315$ of the $P_{f3}$ target energy.  The best finite subset covers $0.2261844517$, equal to the full-panel coverage in this run, giving an increment over base of
$$
\begin{aligned}
0.2261844517 - 0.1897720315 = 0.0364124202.
\end{aligned}
$$
The same comparison appears in the coordinate-normalized readback: the $P_{f3}$ fraction of the target is $0.1978887245$ for the base design and $0.2073899083$ for the best design.  The reported squared norm of the coordinate residual falls from $43.8313140295$ under the base design to $38.1858753555$ under the best design.  Even after the full independent panel is admitted, the unexplained fraction is $0.7738155483$, so the result is a modest but real reachability gain rather than a resolving explanation.

\paragraph{Selection-aware null.}
The finite search is compared with a deterministic row-label permutation null in which the same best-subset selection procedure is replayed on every trial.  With $120$ permutations and $5$ folds, the selection-aware null has mean $0.0187362692$, $95$th percentile $0.0269714130$, and observed maximum $0.0387800622$.  The naive null, which does not carry the same selection burden, has mean $0.0173762139$ and $95$th percentile $0.0281691691$.  The observed increment $0.0364124202$ exceeds the selection-aware $95$th percentile:
$$
\begin{aligned}
0.0364124202 > 0.0269714130.
\end{aligned}
$$
The finite verdict is therefore that the $f3\_stress$ abundance component is partially reachable by the broader independent-readout panel, with a positive increment beyond the base dictionary.  The size of the remaining unexplained part prevents any stronger reading.

\paragraph{Relation to the surrounding finite biology.}
The coordinate is part of a nine-coordinate $B^*_{Q6}$ list: $K\_AAA$, $Arg\_AGR$, $Ile\_AUA$, $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$, $Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG$, $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$, $Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG$, $Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY$, and $f3\_stress$.  The independent-readout result should be read next to two other bounded facts.  First, the Window6 boundary $R$ set contains $10$ sense codons and shows a cross-organism avoided abundance-preference signature: all $15$ computed organisms fall in the avoided direction, with one-sided binomial $p=3.0517578125\times 10^{-5}$ and two-sided $p=6.103515625\times 10^{-5}$.  Second, measured translation-efficiency mediation is highly organism-specific: the measured $M^{QTP}$ fraction is $0.4793069656$ in yeast, $0.0409115834$ in \emph{E. coli}, $0.0027966485$ in human, and clipped to $0$ for danio from a raw value of $-0.0480148605$.  These numbers justify treating the yeast $f3\_stress$ projection as a real finite readout pattern, but they do not make it universal.

\paragraph{Direction-aware H-ranking boundary.}
The related direction-aware H-ranking table uses the stated regularizer $\lambda=0.01$ with $DL(H)=\log_2(1+n_{\mathrm{columns}})$, and reports both raw $A_H C_H$ and penalized Score.  In yeast, the ranking table gives $H_o^*=\mathrm{mRNA}$ with $A_H=0.0018976055$, $C_H=0.6040571891$, $A_H C_H=0.0011462622$, and Score $=-0.0088537378$.  Yeast localization has larger raw alignment $A_H C_H=0.0047683241$ but pays $DL=3.7004397181$, yielding Score $=-0.0322360731$.  This reinforces the same finite interpretation: simple ranked readouts can absorb or align with small pieces of the residual geometry, but no readout supplies a sufficient explanation of the coordinate.

\paragraph{Local boundary.}
The conclusion is limited to a finite statistical projection statement.  It says that, in yeast, the expanded independent-readout panel reaches more of the $f3\_stress$ abundance component than the base dictionary, and that the gain survives the replayed selection null.  It does not establish translation realization, folding behavior, physical admissibility, protein function, causal mechanism, pathway identity, organismal phenotype, or a universal law.  Those stronger statements would require separate layer-matched contacts and statistics.  Missing rows remain missing, rank and Score do not rewrite null status, and coordinate alignment is not a biological mechanism.
