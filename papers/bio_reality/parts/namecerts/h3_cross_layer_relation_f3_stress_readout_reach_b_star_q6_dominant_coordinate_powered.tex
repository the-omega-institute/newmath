\paragraph{Finite record.}
This record concerns the cross-layer relation claim `h3.cross\_layer\_relation.f3\_stress\_readout\_reach.b\_star\_q6\_dominant\_coordinate\_powered'.  The measured object is the Saccharomyces cerevisiae coordinate component
$P_{f3}=\operatorname{Proj}_{x_{f3\_stress}}(Y_Z)$,

The experiment enumerated all $512=2^9$ subsets of the nine requested independent readouts, including the empty subset, and replayed the same best-subset selection under a deterministic row-label permutation null.

\paragraph{Measured reach.}
The base dictionary used $n=1800$ joined rows, had rank $3$, and covered $0.1897720314893696$ of the $P_{f3}$ target.  The best selected readout subset used $n=1056$ joined rows, had rank $19$, and covered $0.22618445168998694$ of the target; the full independent-readout panel gives the same coverage, $0.22618445168998694$, on the same $n=1056$ rows.  Thus the selected independent readouts add
$$
\begin{aligned}
0.22618445168998694-0.1897720314893696
  &=0.03641242020061733
\end{aligned}
$$
over the base dictionary.  In norm-squared units, the base residual has $\|P_{f3}\|^2=43.831314029512704$, while the best selected subset leaves $\|P_{f3}-\widehat P_{f3}\|^2=38.18587535550906$.  Equivalently, the base row accounts for $0.19788872448081418$ of the target base quantity, while the best selected row accounts for $0.20738990828488693$ of the target best quantity.

\paragraph{Selection-aware null.}
The comparison is not made against a fixed-subset null.  On each of $120$ deterministic permutations, the row labels of $P_{f3}$ were permuted and the full best-subset selection procedure was replayed.  The naive null mean and $95$th percentile were $0.017376213916576056$ and $0.028169169068843265$, respectively.  The selection-aware null mean, $95$th percentile, and maximum were $0.018736269159555625$, $0.02697141300639046$, and $0.03878006220013416$.  The observed increment over the base dictionary, $0.03641242020061733$, is above the selection-aware $95$th percentile but below the largest permuted value.  The finite conclusion is therefore a bounded excess-reach statement, not a discovery of a pathway: yeast $f3\_stress$ abundance mass is partially accessible to a larger independent readout panel, but $0.7738155483100131$ of the full target remains unexplained after the full panel.

\paragraph{Local interpretation boundary.}
The admissible statement is narrow.  The experiment shows that, for Saccharomyces cerevisiae, a best subset selected from nine independent readouts reaches more of the $f3\_stress$ abundance component than the base dictionary and clears the selection-aware $95$th-percentile null.  It also shows that most of the target remains unexplained.  The finite row does not establish translation realization, folding, physical admissibility, protein function, a causal regulatory route, a stress-response mechanism, a conserved pathway, or a universal biological law.  Any such stronger statement would require a separate matched contact with its own statistic, row join, null model, and failure boundary.
