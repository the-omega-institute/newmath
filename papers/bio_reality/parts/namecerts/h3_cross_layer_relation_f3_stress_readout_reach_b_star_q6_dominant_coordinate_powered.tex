\subsection{Stress-coordinate reach by independent readout panels}
\label{sec:bq6-f3-stress-readout-reach}

\paragraph{Finite object.}
The tested object is the yeast $f3\_stress$ coordinate component of the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ abundance residual after the fixed background controls have been removed.  The response is not the whole protein-abundance signal.  It is the one-coordinate projection
$$
\begin{aligned}
P_{f3} = \operatorname{Proj}_{x_{f3\_stress}}(Z\text{-residualized }\log_{10}\text{ protein abundance}),
\end{aligned}
$$
where the unified control basis has rank $24$.  The experiment joined $1800$ yeast rows for the base dictionary and $1056$ rows for the best and full independent-readout dictionaries.  The candidate search was finite: all $512$ subsets of the nine requested independent readouts were replayed, including the empty subset, under a selection-aware deterministic row-label permutation null.  Five folds were used, with $120$ permutations and seed $\texttt{sha256:b\_star\_q6\_f3\_stress\_readout\_reach\_powered:deterministic}$.

\paragraph{Reach calculation.}
The base panel has projection coverage $0.1897720314893696$ and $\|P_{f3}\|^2=43.831314029512704$ on its join.  The selected readout subset has coverage $0.22618445168998694$ and $\|P_{f3}\|^2=38.18587535550906$ on the $1056$-row join.  The full panel gives the same reported coverage, $0.22618445168998694$, so the best finite subset and the full independent-readout panel coincide at the reported precision.  The gain over the base dictionary is
$$
\begin{aligned}
0.22618445168998694 - 0.1897720314893696
  = 0.03641242020061733.
\end{aligned}
$$
The base panel accounts for $0.19788872448081418$ of the target-base energy scale, and the selected panel accounts for $0.20738990828488693$ of the target-best energy scale.  Thus the positive result is a reach result: the independent readout set reaches a little more of the $f3\_stress$ abundance component than the base dictionary does, but it still leaves $0.7738155483100131$ of the selected-panel target unexplained.

\paragraph{Null comparison.}
The naive row-label null has mean $0.017376213916576056$ and $95$th percentile $0.028169169068843265$.  The selection-aware null, which replays best-subset selection on each permutation rather than comparing against a fixed panel, has mean $0.018736269159555625$, $95$th percentile $0.02697141300639046$, and maximum $0.03878006220013416$.  The observed increment $0.03641242020061733$ exceeds the selection-aware $95$th percentile but remains below the observed maximum of the permutation ensemble.  The local conclusion is therefore bounded: yeast $f3\_stress$ is partially reachable by the finite independent-readout panel, with evidence above the declared selection-aware threshold, but the coverage is far from complete.

\paragraph{Relation to the direction-aware H-ranking ledger.}
The broader $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ ledger records nine residual coordinates, namely $K\_AAA$, $Arg\_AGR$, $Ile\_AUA$, $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$, $Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG$, $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$, $Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG$, $Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY$, and $f3\_stress$.  In that ledger, a candidate readout $H$ is summarized by absorbed energy $A_H$, coordinate-support cosine $C_H$, raw directional product $A_H C_H$, and the stated description-length score
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{Score}(H)= A_H C_H - 0.01\log_2(1+n_{\mathrm{columns}}).
\end{aligned}
$$
The same finite policy explains why high energy alone is not promoted.  In human, for example, PTM density has $A_H=0.014884825920744861$ but only $C_H=0.3604440741749213$, giving $A_HC_H=0.0053651472982577525$ and $\mathrm{Score}=-0.004634852701742248$.  Human complex membership has lower absorbed energy, $A_H=0.007527199987780841$, but stronger direction agreement, $C_H=0.8723071163246565$, yielding $A_HC_H=0.006566030115340095$ and $\mathrm{Score}=-0.0034339698846599055$.  In danio, measured TE is the best raw directional entry, with $A_H=0.017594290015070787$, $C_H=0.6573753096184687$, $A_HC_H=0.011566051846174291$, and $\mathrm{Score}=0.001566051846174291$; mRNA follows with $A_H=0.011973033879985887$, $C_H=0.896563785384548$, $A_HC_H=0.01073458857797759$, and $\mathrm{Score}=0.000734588577977589$.  In yeast, the top direction-aware ranking entry is mRNA, with $A_H=0.001897605472160837$, $C_H=0.6040571890503664$, $A_HC_H=0.0011462622274400686$, and $\mathrm{Score}=-0.008853737772559931$.  These entries show the same pattern as the $f3\_stress$ reach test: finite readouts can absorb small pieces of the residual, but there is no shared universal absorber.

\paragraph{Boundary codons and organism context.}
The fixed Window6 $R$ set has a strong avoided-abundance signature across the computed organisms: among $15$ directional non-ties, all $15$ are avoided and $0$ are preferred, with one-sided binomial $p=3.0517578125\times 10^{-5}$ and two-sided binomial $p=6.103515625\times 10^{-5}$.  Among individually significant organisms, $6$ of $6$ point in the avoided direction.  The $R$ support contains $10$ sense codons out of $61$ sense codons.  This cross-organism boundary fact is relevant background for the coordinate system, but it does not explain the yeast $f3\_stress$ readout reach.  Likewise, the optimal-codon direction audit over $15$ computed organisms reports mean pairwise cosine $0.2629282074265475$ over $41$ synonymous-design columns and classifies the pattern as clustered rather than universal alignment.

\paragraph{Translation and residual scale.}
The non-translation residual is large in several organisms and modest in yeast.  The modeled-translation complement fraction $d$ is $0.21173219572706703$ in yeast, $0.6629960038578532$ in E. coli, $0.874628721049266$ in danio, $0.9208949355679455$ in human, $0.9400341806302775$ in gallus, and $0.8972672018746489$ in Mycobacterium smegmatis.  In the measured-TE mediation ledger, yeast has the largest measured mediated fraction among the four reported organisms, $0.4793069656345167$, whereas human has $0.0027966485036786744$, E. coli has $0.04091158340950673$, and danio is clipped to $0$ from a raw value of $-0.04801486048721136$.  This makes the yeast $f3\_stress$ result especially narrow: even in an organism where the measured translation readout carries more of the codon-abundance association than in the other reported cases, the independent-readout panel reaches only $0.22618445168998694$ of the one-coordinate $f3\_stress$ target.

\paragraph{Row coverage and missingness.}
The readout table is not a complete biological atlas.  Yeast turnover uses $3640$ joined proteins, with reported abundance count $6351$, CDS count $5815$, measured-TE count $5128$, join hit rate $0.8818572656921754$, and CDS join hit rate $0.9156038419146592$ under the SGD-to-STRING mapping.  Human turnover uses $8057$ joined proteins from $8164$ valid turnover proteins and $19053$ CDS-joined proteins, with CDS join hit rate $0.9779294769799313$ under the Mathieson gene-name to UniProt reviewed-gene to STRING mapping.  These row-count asymmetries matter because a readout can change the protein set on which the residual is projected.  Missing rows remain missing; they are not zeros and they are not negative evidence.

\paragraph{Local conclusion.}
The finite result is positive but small.  For Saccharomyces cerevisiae, the $f3\_stress$ abundance component is partly reachable by a larger independent-readout dictionary: the best-subset coverage is $0.22618445168998694$, the increment over the base dictionary is $0.03641242020061733$, and the increment exceeds the selection-aware null $95$th percentile $0.02697141300639046$.  At the same time, the unexplained fraction after the full panel is $0.7738155483100131$.  The chapter therefore records a bounded cross-layer relation: independent measured readouts touch the yeast $f3\_stress$ coordinate more than the base dictionary does, but they do not supply a mechanism, a universal absorber, or a complete explanation of the residual.

\paragraph{Non-promotion rule.}
No translation realization, folding statement, physical-admissibility statement, protein-function statement, pathway mechanism, phenotype, or universal biological law follows from this finite calculation.  The quantities $A_H$, $C_H$, $A_HC_H$, coverage, rank, and Score are projection and ranking quantities.  A stronger biological statement would require a separate layer-matched contact with its own statistic and its own null model.  In particular, coordinate alignment is not a mechanism, energy absorption is not causation, and an organism-specific best readout is not a shared biological channel.
