\subsection{Finite reach of the yeast $f3\_stress$ abundance coordinate}
\label{sec:biorality-q6-f3-stress-readout-reach}

\paragraph{Finite object.}
This record tests one sharply delimited question: in \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}, how much of the abundance component carried by the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ coordinate $f3\_stress$ can be reached by a finite panel of independent measured readouts after the same residualization controls are imposed?  The target is not the whole protein-abundance vector and not a biological stress pathway.  It is
$$
\begin{aligned}
P_{f3} = \operatorname{Proj}_{x_{f3\_stress}}\bigl(Z\text{-residualized }\log_{10}(\text{protein abundance})\bigr),
\end{aligned}
$$
where $Z$ contains the declared sequence and composition controls.  The tested readout panel is finite: all subsets of nine requested independent readouts were enumerated, including the empty subset, so the search space contained $2^9=512$ candidate subsets and the implementation reports the same computed count, $512$.

\paragraph{Observed reach.}
The base dictionary used $n=1800$ joined rows and had rank $3$, with coverage $0.1897720314893696$.  The selected best subset used $n=1056$ joined rows, had rank $19$, and reached coverage $0.22618445168998694$; the full nine-readout panel also used $n=1056$, had rank $19$, and reached the same coverage $0.22618445168998694$.  Thus the best selected dictionary adds
$$
\begin{aligned}
0.22618445168998694 - 0.1897720314893696 = 0.03641242020061733
\end{aligned}
$$
over the base construction.  In squared-norm units the target energy is also reduced from $\|P_{f3}\|^2=43.831314029512704$ on the base join to $38.18587535550906$ on the best-subset join.  The fraction of the target recorded on the base row is $0.19788872448081418$, and on the best row it is $0.20738990828488693$.

\paragraph{Selection-aware null.}
Because the subset is selected after inspecting a finite panel, the null comparison must replay the same selection rule.  The row-label permutation null used $120$ deterministic trials and repeated best-subset selection on every trial.  Its mean coverage was $0.018736269159555625$, its $95$th percentile was $0.02697141300639046$, and its maximum was $0.03878006220013416$.  The naive null, which does not carry the same selection accounting, had mean $0.017376213916576056$ and $95$th percentile $0.028169169068843265$.  The observed increment $0.03641242020061733$ is larger than the selection-aware $95$th percentile, but it remains below the observed selection-aware null maximum.  The finite verdict is therefore a modest but nonzero reach statement, not a decisive explanatory closure.

\paragraph{Relation to the residual dictionary ledger.}
The same $B^{*}_{Q6}$ finite coordinate list contains nine coordinates: $K\_AAA$, $Arg\_AGR$, $Ile\_AUA$, $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$, $Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG$, $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$, $Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG$, $Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY$, and $f3\_stress$.  The direction-aware H-ranking table uses the stated regularizer $\lambda=0.01$ with $DL(H)=\log_2(1+n_{\mathrm{columns}})$ and ranks readouts by $A_H C_H-\lambda DL(H)$ while also reporting raw $A_H C_H$.  In yeast, the leading raw ranking entry in that table is mRNA with $A_H=0.001897605472160837$, $C_H=0.6040571890503664$, and $A_HC_H=0.0011462622274400686$; localization has larger absorbed energy, $A_H=0.011225105608516642$, but with $12$ columns and $DL=3.700439718141092$ its penalized score is $-0.03223607310225219$.  These numbers show why the $f3\_stress$ reach test is not a hidden mechanism claim: even when a finite readout panel reaches part of the coordinate-specific component, the broader residual dictionary remains weak, join-sensitive, and organism-specific.

\paragraph{Cross-checks from neighboring finite rows.}
The yeast translation-complement row reports $P_Q\_\perp T$ energy $186.88597748267532$, $R^2_{QP}=0.29837624987287115$, complement fraction $d=0.21173219572706703$, and modeled-translation-mediated fraction $m=0.788267804272933$.  In the independent proteostasis-mismatch row, yeast uses $3277$ genes after a dictionary-active set of $3290$, obtains $A_{\mathrm{prot}}=0.02163422878638817$, held-out model $R^2=0.016738843160479133$, held-out increment $0.018001228420258173$, null $95$th percentile $0.015564370826515692$, and verdict $\mathrm{crosses\_boundary}$.  These supporting rows are consistent with a small reachable component inside a much larger unresolved abundance residual.  The boundary-set row gives a separate sequence-side signature: the fixed Window6 $R$ set is avoided in all $15$ computed organisms, with one-sided binomial $p=3.0517578125\times 10^{-5}$ and two-sided $p=6.103515625\times 10^{-5}$, but that is a codon-window abundance-preference signature, not a statement that the yeast $f3\_stress$ abundance coordinate is a biological stress mechanism.  The cross-organism optimal-codon direction row is likewise finite and descriptive: $15$ organisms were computed out of $16$ requested, the mean pairwise cosine was $0.2629282074265475$, the synonymous design had $41$ columns, and the classification was reported as clustered.

\paragraph{Readback.}
The only positive assertion here is the finite reach statement:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{coverage}_{\mathrm{best}} &= 0.22618445168998694,\\
\mathrm{coverage}_{\mathrm{base}} &= 0.1897720314893696,\\
\Delta_{\mathrm{best-base}} &= 0.03641242020061733,\\
\mathrm{Null}_{0.95}^{\mathrm{selection}} &= 0.02697141300639046,\\
\mathrm{unexplained}_{\mathrm{full}} &= 0.7738155483100131.
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the finite readout panel partially reaches the yeast $f3\_stress$ abundance component beyond a selection-aware $95$th-percentile null, while roughly $77.38\%$ of the component remains outside the full readout span under this metric.  The result is best read as a bounded coordinate/readout compatibility fact: a measured panel can touch part of the $f3\_stress$ component, but most of that component remains unaccounted for by the tested readouts.

\paragraph{Local boundary.}
No translation realization, folding statement, physical admissibility statement, function statement, causal mechanism, shared pathway, or universal law follows from these rows.  The variables $A_H$, $C_H$, raw $A_HC_H$, penalized Score, $H_o^{*}$, and the best-subset coverage are projection and ranking quantities.  They do not certify biological causation; they do not identify a stress-response mechanism; and they do not turn a coordinate label into a molecular pathway.  Missing rows remain missing rather than negative evidence, and any later promotion to translation, localization, turnover, folding, function, phenotype, or mechanism would require a separate matched measurement and a separate statistic at that layer.
