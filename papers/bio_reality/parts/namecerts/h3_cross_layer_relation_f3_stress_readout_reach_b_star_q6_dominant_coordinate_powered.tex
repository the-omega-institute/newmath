\subsection{Finite Reach of the Yeast $f3\_stress$ Residual Coordinate}
\label{sec:bioreality-f3-stress-readout-reach}

\paragraph{Finite object.}
This record isolates the $f3\_stress$ coordinate inside the nine-coordinate $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual panel and asks a deliberately bounded question: after the standard control ledger $Z$ has removed length, amino-acid composition, GC3, and the $M$-density baseline, how much of the $f3\_stress$ abundance component is reachable from an independent panel of measured readouts?  The finite row is organism-specific and is computed for \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}.  Its target vector is
$$
\begin{aligned}
P_{f3} &= \operatorname{Proj}_{x_{f3\_stress}}\bigl(Z\text{-residualized }\log_{10}(\text{protein abundance})\bigr),
\end{aligned}
$$
and the readout panel is searched over all subsets of nine requested independent readouts, including the empty subset.  The candidate-subset ledger therefore has $2^9=512$ possible subsets, and the run confirms that all $512$ were replayed under the same deterministic selection rule.

\paragraph{Observed finite reach.}
The base dictionary uses $n=1800$ joined rows and has rank $3$, with base projection coverage $0.1897720314893696$.  The best selected readout subset uses $n=1056$ joined rows and has rank $19$; the full readout panel has the same joined-row count and rank.  The selected subset reaches coverage $0.22618445168998694$, equal to the full-panel coverage in this run.  The measured increment over the base dictionary is
$$
\begin{aligned}
0.22618445168998694 - 0.1897720314893696
  &= 0.03641242020061733.
\end{aligned}
$$
Equivalently, the $f3\_stress$ target energy not reached by the full independent readout panel is $0.7738155483100131$.  The direct coordinate-energy diagnostic is also small in absolute terms: $P_{f3}$ contributes $0.19788872448081418$ of the target under the base construction and $0.20738990828488693$ under the best selected construction, with squared norms $43.831314029512704$ and $38.18587535550906$ respectively.  The unified residual-control basis has rank $24$.

\paragraph{Selection-aware null.}
The positive increment is not read as a mechanism.  It is checked against a row-label permutation null in which the best-subset selection is replayed on every trial.  The run uses $120$ deterministic permutations.  The selection-aware null has mean $0.018736269159555625$, $95$th percentile $0.02697141300639046$, and maximum $0.03878006220013416$.  The naive null, which does not carry the same selection burden, has mean $0.017376213916576056$ and $95$th percentile $0.028169169068843265$.  The observed increment $0.03641242020061733$ exceeds both null $95$th percentiles, while remaining below the largest selection-aware null draw.  Thus the finite statement is only that the selected readout panel gives a detectable, selection-tested increase in orthogonal projection coverage of the yeast $f3\_stress$ abundance component.

\paragraph{Relation to the nine-coordinate residual ledger.}
The surrounding $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual panel uses the coordinates $K\_AAA$, $Arg\_AGR$, $Ile\_AUA$, $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$, $Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG$, $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$, $Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG$, $Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY$, and $f3\_stress$.  In the translation-complement diagnostic for yeast, the residual coordinate support is not concentrated only on $f3\_stress$: the dominant support list is $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$, $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$, $Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG$, $Arg\_AGR$, $Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY$, and $f3\_stress$, with support fractions summing to $1.0$.  The yeast modeled-translation-complement fraction is $d=0.21173219572706703$, while the modeled translation-mediated part is $m=0.788267804272933$.  This makes the present row a targeted reachability test for one named residual coordinate, not a general explanation of the whole non-translation residual panel.

\paragraph{Relation to the direction-aware readout ranking.}
The broader direction-aware ranking ledger uses the stated score
$$
\begin{aligned}
\operatorname{Score}(H) &= A_H C_H - \lambda DL(H),
\end{aligned}
$$
with $\lambda=0.01$ and $DL(H)=\log_2(1+n_{\mathrm{columns}})$.  In yeast, the highest raw one-column direction-aware product in that table is mRNA with $A_H=0.001897605472160837$, $C_H=0.6040571890503664$, and $A_H C_H=0.0011462622274400686$, giving $\operatorname{Score}=-0.008853737772559931$.  Localization has larger raw absorption, $A_H=0.011225105608516642$, but its $12$-column description length is $3.700439718141092$, so its score is $-0.03223607310225219$.  These values keep the $f3\_stress$ reachability result in the same descriptive regime: measured readouts can recover a small, statistically visible part of a coordinate component, but the ranking ledger does not produce a compact shared biological channel.

\paragraph{Background biological contact scale.}
The independent coordinate geometry is not treated as biological mechanism by itself.  A separate boundary-set contact reports that the Window6 $R$ set has $10$ sense codons and a cross-organism avoided abundance-preference signature across $15$ computed organisms: $15$ avoided, $0$ preferred, $0$ zero, one-sided avoided sign-test $p=3.0517578125\times 10^{-5}$ and two-sided $p=6.103515625\times 10^{-5}$.  Another cross-organism diagnostic reports clustered optimal-codon direction with mean pairwise cosine $0.2629282074265475$ over $15$ computed organisms and $41$ synonymous design columns.  These contacts show that the coordinate system has finite external readbacks, but they do not license a stronger conclusion for the yeast $f3\_stress$ row.

\paragraph{Local conclusion.}
The finite conclusion is positive but narrow.  For \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}, the $f3\_stress$ abundance component is partially reachable by a selected subset of independent readouts: coverage rises from $0.1897720314893696$ to $0.22618445168998694$, an increment of $0.03641242020061733$, against a selection-aware null $95$th percentile of $0.02697141300639046$.  Most of the target remains unreached, since the full-panel unexplained fraction is $0.7738155483100131$.  The result therefore supports a finite cross-layer descriptive readout: some measured readout combination aligns with a small part of the yeast $f3\_stress$ coordinate component after controls and selection replay.

\paragraph{Local boundary.}
No stronger biological claim follows from this row.  The numbers above do not establish translation realization, protein folding, physical admissibility, protein function, localization mechanism, stress-response causation, a pathway, a universal $H$, or a global biological law.  They also do not make $C_H$, $A_H$, a score, a rank, or a selected subset into a mechanism.  Any such statement would require a separate layer-matched contact with its own statistic and its own null model.  Missing or unavailable readouts remain missing; they are not counted as zero, negative evidence, or within-null biological evidence.
