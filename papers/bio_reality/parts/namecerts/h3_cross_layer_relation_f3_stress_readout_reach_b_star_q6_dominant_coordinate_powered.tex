\subsection{Finite readout reach for the yeast $f3\_stress$ coordinate}
\label{sec:bior reality-f3-stress-readout-reach}

\paragraph{Finite object.}
This record isolates one coordinate-level question inside the $B^{\ast}\_{Q6}$ abundance residual: whether the $f3\_stress$ component in \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae} is reached by a finite panel of independently fetched readouts.  The response vector is not the whole abundance residual.  It is
$$
\begin{aligned}
P\_{f3} &= \operatorname{Proj}\_{x\_{f3\_stress}}\bigl(Z\text{-residualized }\log\_{10}\text{ protein abundance}\bigr),
\end{aligned}
$$
where $Z$ is the stated control basis and the projection coordinate is the $f3\_stress$ coordinate from the nine-coordinate $B^{\ast}\_{Q6}$ frame.  The finite search considered all $512$ subsets of the nine requested independent readouts, including the empty subset.  The replayed null was selection-aware: each permutation relabelled $P\_{f3}$ and then repeated the same best-subset selection rule, so the reported threshold is not the easier fixed-subset threshold.

\paragraph{Numerical readback.}
The base dictionary used $n=1800$ joined rows, had rank $3$, and covered $0.1897720314893696$ of the $f3\_stress$ target.  Its squared target norm was $43.831314029512704$, and the fraction of target carried by $P\_{f3}$ at the base step was $0.19788872448081418$.  The best selected readout subset used $n=1056$ joined rows and rank $19$; the full nine-readout panel also had $n=1056$ and rank $19$.  The best and full coverages agree at
$$
\begin{aligned}
\operatorname{coverage}\_{\mathrm{best}} &= 0.22618445168998694,\\
\operatorname{coverage}\_{\mathrm{full}} &= 0.22618445168998694.
\end{aligned}
$$
The increment over the base dictionary is therefore
$$
\begin{aligned}
0.22618445168998694 - 0.1897720314893696 &= 0.03641242020061733.
\end{aligned}
$$
On the same best-subset join, the squared norm is $38.18587535550906$, the best fraction of target is $0.20738990828488693$, and the unexplained fraction after the full panel remains $0.7738155483100131$.

\paragraph{Null comparison.}
The deterministic permutation audit used $120$ permutations.  The naive null mean was $0.017376213916576056$ and its $95\%$ cutoff was $0.028169169068843265$.  The selection-aware null mean was slightly larger, $0.018736269159555625$, with a $95\%$ cutoff of $0.02697141300639046$ and a maximum observed null value of $0.03878006220013416$.  The observed best-subset increment, $0.03641242020061733$, exceeds the selection-aware $95\%$ cutoff while remaining below the maximum null trial.  The finite conclusion is consequently directional and bounded: the added readout panel has measurable reach into the yeast $f3\_stress$ abundance component, but most of the target is still outside the panel.

\paragraph{Local interpretation.}
The ready result is narrow.  In yeast, a richer independent readout panel improves finite orthogonal coverage of the $f3\_stress$ abundance component from $0.1897720314893696$ to $0.22618445168998694$, beating the selection-aware $95\%$ permutation cutoff by about $0.00921300719422687$. \paragraph{Boundary of the claim.}
No translation realization, folding statement, physical admissibility statement, functional statement, mechanism, causal pathway, or universal biological law follows from these numbers.  The quantities $A\_H$, $C\_H$, $A\_H C\_H$, score, rank, selected subset, and coordinate name are finite projection readbacks.  They may order rows under the declared policy, but they do not identify a biological process.  Missing organisms or unavailable readouts remain unavailable data, not negative evidence.  Any future promotion to translation dynamics, protein state, localization mechanism, folded structure, organismal function, phenotype, or universal channel would require a separate matched contact and a separate statistic for that stronger layer.
