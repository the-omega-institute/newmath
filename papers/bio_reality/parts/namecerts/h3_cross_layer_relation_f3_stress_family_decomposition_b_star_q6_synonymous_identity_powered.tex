\subsection{Synonymous-family decomposition of the $f_3$ stress coordinate}
\label{sec:bioreality-f3-stress-family-decomposition}

\paragraph{Question.}
The $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual basis contains a single coordinate denoted $f_3\text{-stress}$, but that coordinate is not biologically atomic. It is assembled from synonymous third-position contrasts across many amino-acid families. The finite-row question here is whether the observed abundance contact carried by the full residualized $f_3\text{-stress}$ coordinate can be decomposed back into synonymous-family subcoordinates, and whether any family-level subcoordinate remains a real source of the same abundance residual after the sibling controls are imposed. The test is not allowed to infer a ribosome mechanism, a folding property, a molecular function, a physical-admissibility claim, or an organism-independent biological rule from this decomposition alone.

\paragraph{Finite design.}
The computation was run on \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}, keyed as \texttt{saccharomyces\_cerevisiae}. The base input table contained $4759$ candidate rows, and the unified yeast context join used $n=1800$ proteins after the minimum-protein gate of $500$ was satisfied. The response variable was log$_{10}$ protein abundance, residualized against the sibling control basis. That control basis had rank $24$ and included the amino-acid composition, length, GC3, and the shared codon-density baseline used by the surrounding $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ abundance tests. The full $f_3\text{-stress}$ direction had rank $1$ after residualization.

The decomposition used $21$ additive synonymous-family subcoordinates. Each subcoordinate retained only the projected $f_3$ weights belonging to one synonymous family block and divided by the norm of the full projected $f_3$ vector before applying the same residualization and held-out test. The reported energy scale was
$$
\begin{aligned}
E_{\mathrm{family}}
  &= \frac{\lVert P_{\mathrm{family}}\rVert_2^2}{\lVert P_{f_3}\rVert_2^2},\\
\lVert P_{f_3}\rVert_2^2
  &= 52.76911713166682,\\
\lVert y\rVert_2^2
  &= 430.42705205115925,\\
\frac{\lVert P_{f_3}\rVert_2^2}{\lVert y\rVert_2^2}
  &= 0.12259712041843235.
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the full residualized $f_3\text{-stress}$ projection accounted for about $12.26\%$ of the target residual energy on this joined yeast abundance table. The family decomposition was evaluated with $5$ folds and $120$ deterministic label permutations. The seed was recorded as \texttt{sha256:b\_star\_q6\_f3\_stress\_family\_decomposition\_powered:deterministic}, so the row split and permutation comparison are part of the finite certificate rather than an informal post hoc reading.

\paragraph{Family-level readback.}
All $21$ synonymous-family subcoordinates were computed. Nineteen families crossed the held-out/permutation source gate: \texttt{F\_UUU\_to\_UUC}, \texttt{L\_UUA\_to\_UUG}, \texttt{S\_UCU\_to\_UCG}, \texttt{Y\_UAU\_to\_UAC}, \texttt{C\_UGU\_to\_UGC}, \texttt{P\_CCU\_to\_CCG}, \texttt{H\_CAU\_to\_CAC}, \texttt{Q\_CAA\_to\_CAG}, \texttt{R\_CGU\_to\_CGG}, \texttt{I\_AUU\_to\_AUA}, \texttt{T\_ACU\_to\_ACG}, \texttt{N\_AAU\_to\_AAC}, \texttt{K\_AAA\_to\_AAG}, \texttt{R\_AGA\_to\_AGG}, \texttt{V\_GUU\_to\_GUG}, \texttt{A\_GCU\_to\_GCG}, \texttt{D\_GAU\_to\_GAC}, \texttt{E\_GAA\_to\_GAG}, and \texttt{G\_GGU\_to\_GGG}. The finite result is therefore stronger than a statement that one hidden family happens to explain the aggregate coordinate. It says that most tested two-codon third-position contrasts can be read as non-vacuous contributors to the yeast abundance residual when each is isolated inside the same $f_3$ construction and compared against its own held-out null.

This should be read as a synonymous-identity decomposition of the $f_3\text{-stress}$ abundance contact. The aggregate coordinate is not merely a smooth third-position average; it has family-resolved support across phenylalanine, leucine, serine, tyrosine, cysteine, proline, histidine, glutamine, arginine, isoleucine, threonine, asparagine, lysine, valine, alanine, aspartate, glutamate, and glycine blocks. The two non-listed families in the $21$-family panel are not promoted by this row, because the provided finite certificate names only the nineteen families that pass the real-source gate.

\paragraph{Relation to the surrounding abundance evidence.}
The family decomposition agrees with the single-coordinate reducibility audit for the same yeast abundance target. In that related audit, the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ table used $n=1258$ joined yeast proteins after a $500$-protein organism gate, decomposed all $9$ residual coordinates, and found a genuine held-out abundance source among the coordinates. That audit listed the source-bearing coordinates as \texttt{K\_AAA}, \texttt{Arg\_AGR}, \texttt{Ile\_AUA}, \texttt{Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR}, \texttt{Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY}, \texttt{Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG}, \texttt{Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY}, and \texttt{f3\_stress}. The present row refines only the last coordinate: it asks which synonymous families inside $f_3\text{-stress}$ preserve source status, not whether the whole $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ block survives.

The same coordinate also appears as the dominant coordinate in the yeast growth-condition abundance comparison. In that comparison, the shared yeast join had $2209$ proteins; the rich-medium YEPD abundance fit reported $R^2_{QP}=0.3280825603424665$, the minimal-medium SD fit reported $R^2_{QP}=0.3050385538305022$, and $f_3\text{-stress}$ was dominant in both conditions. The two condition-specific $S^{QP}$ profiles were nearly collinear, with cosine $0.9993775935182967$ and Pearson correlation $0.9983880256003688$ over the coordinate profile. These numbers do not prove a growth-condition mechanism, but they make the family decomposition scientifically specific: it is decomposing a yeast abundance coordinate that was already stable across two measured abundance contexts.

A separate local ribosome-occupancy contact also classified \texttt{f3\_stress} among the families passing a position-bin-controlled dwell gate with \texttt{K\_AAA} and \texttt{Arg\_AGR}. That row used exact supplied codon-window and amino-acid bins, requested $200$ within-bin synonymous-label permutations, required $C_H>0$, and applied a description-length penalty with $DL=2$ and $\lambda_{DL}=0.01$. This is only supporting context for why the $f_3\text{-stress}$ coordinate is worth decomposing; the present family table remains an abundance-residual result, not a direct translation-efficiency result.

\paragraph{Local claim boundary.}
The accepted finite statement is the following: in the yeast joined abundance table with $n=1800$, the full residualized $f_3\text{-stress}$ projection has squared norm $52.76911713166682$ against a target residual norm of $430.42705205115925$, and the $21$-family synonymous decomposition identifies $19$ family subcoordinates that pass the held-out/permutation real-source gate under the same control residualization. This supports a family-resolved synonymous-identity reading of the $f_3\text{-stress}$ abundance contact.

Nothing in this row by itself establishes translation realization, ribosome causation, protein folding, structural order, biochemical function, physical admissibility, pathway mechanism, phenotype, or universality. The readout is steady-state protein abundance in one yeast context join, and the statistic is a controlled projection/decomposition. Any stronger claim would require a separate layer-matched contact and its own controlled statistic.
