\subsection{Family-resolved support inside the $f3\_stress$ coordinate}
\label{sec:h3-f3-stress-family-decomposition-b-star-q6-synonymous-identity-powered}

\paragraph{Question isolated by the finite row.}
The record tests whether the aggregate $f3\_stress$ direction in the nine-coordinate $B^*_{Q6}$ synonymous residual panel is carried by a single collapsed codon-family contrast or by a decomposable collection of family-specific residual sources.  The biological table is restricted to \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}; the joined target is residualized $\log_{10}$ protein abundance on the unified yeast context join.  The finite design contains $n=1800$ joined proteins after the minimum-protein gate, with $4759$ base coding-sequence rows available upstream, a $24$-rank control basis for the sibling covariates, and a deterministic seed $\mathrm{sha256{:}b\_star\_q6\_f3\_stress\_family\_decomposition\_powered{:}deterministic}$.  The controls are the same kind of codon-derived background used by the surrounding $B^*_{Q6}$ abundance tests: coding length, amino-acid composition, $GC3$, and the $M$-density baseline on the union support of the nine projected coordinates.

\paragraph{Construction of the family decomposition.}
The full $f3\_stress$ coordinate is first residualized against the control basis.  It has rank $1$ in this experiment and explains a target-projection energy
$P_{f3}^{2}=52.76911713166682$.
Relative to the residualized target energy
$\|P\|^{2}=430.42705205115925$,
this is the finite fraction
$$
\begin{aligned}
\frac{P_{f3}^{2}}{\|P\|^{2}}=0.12259712041843235 .
\end{aligned}
$$
The aggregate coordinate is then split into $21$ additive synonymous-family subcoordinates.  Each subcoordinate keeps only the projected $f3$ weights inside one synonymous family block and divides by the full projected $f3$ norm before applying the same residualization.  The reported energy statistic for a family $F$ is therefore
$$
\begin{aligned}
E_F=\frac{P_F^{2}}{P_{f3}^{2}},
\end{aligned}
$$
where $P_F$ is the target projection onto the residualized family subcoordinate.  The row is not a new measurement of translation speed or folding; it is a decomposition of a protein-abundance association already read through synonymous codon residuals.

\paragraph{Observed family support.}
All $21$ family subcoordinates were computed, and $19$ families passed the held-out/permutation source gate with $5$ folds and $120$ deterministic permutations.  The families with positive finite support are
$F\_UUU\_to\_UUC$, $L\_UUA\_to\_UUG$, $S\_UCU\_to\_UCG$, $Y\_UAU\_to\_UAC$, $C\_UGU\_to\_UGC$, $P\_CCU\_to\_CCG$, $H\_CAU\_to\_CAC$, $Q\_CAA\_to\_CAG$, $R\_CGU\_to\_CGG$, $I\_AUU\_to\_AUA$, $T\_ACU\_to\_ACG$, $N\_AAU\_to\_AAC$, $K\_AAA\_to\_AAG$, $R\_AGA\_to\_AGG$, $V\_GUU\_to\_GUG$, $A\_GCU\_to\_GCG$, $D\_GAU\_to\_GAC$, $E\_GAA\_to\_GAG$, and $G\_GGU\_to\_GGG$.
Thus the aggregate $f3\_stress$ abundance component is not supported only by one codon identity contrast.  It is distributed across many two-codon or multi-codon synonymous identity choices, while the test remains bounded to the yeast abundance table and its stated residualization.

\paragraph{Relation to the nine-coordinate abundance panel.}
A companion per-coordinate reducibility check on the same yeast setting used $n=1258$ joined proteins after a $1800$-row base design and a $24$-rank $Z$ basis.  It found real-source support for eight of the nine $B^*_{Q6}$ coordinates: $K\_AAA$, $Arg\_AGR$, $Ile\_AUA$, $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$, $Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG$, $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$, $Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY$, and $f3\_stress$.  The family decomposition sharpens that panel-level result by opening the $f3\_stress$ entry itself.  The scalar abundance association is also compatible with condition-resolved yeast protein-abundance rows: in rich medium $YEPD$, the nine-coordinate $B^*_{Q6}$ panel gives $R^2_{QP}=0.3280825603424665$ with $f3\_stress$ dominant over $2209$ shared proteins, while in minimal medium $SD$ it gives $R^2_{QP}=0.3050385538305022$, again with $f3\_stress$ dominant.  The two condition vectors have Pearson similarity $0.9983880256003688$ and cosine similarity $0.9993775935182967$ under $1000$ permutations, so the yeast abundance contact is not a single-condition accident in this finite comparison.

\paragraph{Nearby readout boundaries.}
The same family-resolved support should not be confused with a translation mechanism.  A separate local ribosome-occupancy analysis reports a position-bin-controlled gate in which $K\_AAA$, $Arg\_AGR$, and $f3\_stress$ pass a rule using $C_H>0$, description-length penalty $DL=2$, and $\lambda_{DL}=0.01$ with $200$ requested within-bin permutations; that is a descriptive codon-window occupancy contact, not a causal derivation from the abundance decomposition.  Likewise, a yeast mRNA-stability mediation row gives $R^2_{QP}=0.2761140373962526$, $R^2_{QP\mid M}=0.24851799532219632$, absorbed fraction $0.027596042074056304$, mediation fraction $0.09994436477872035$, and right-tail permutation value $0.000999000999000999$ over a $5085$-gene three-way join whose CDS-to-half-life hit rate is $0.9156038419146592$.  Those numbers show that other yeast molecular readouts can be joined to the same residual-coordinate style, but they do not turn the present family decomposition into a causal mRNA-stability pathway.

\paragraph{Local conclusion.}
The finite row supports the following bounded statement: in the yeast protein-abundance context, the $f3\_stress$ component of the $B^*_{Q6}$ synonymous residual panel is family-decomposable, with all $21$ family subcoordinates computed and $19$ passing the held-out/permutation source criterion.  The total $f3\_stress$ projection accounts for $0.12259712041843235$ of the residualized target energy, and the observed support is spread across many synonymous identity contrasts rather than collapsing to a single family.  This is evidence for a family-resolved codon-identity signal inside a residual protein-abundance association.  It does not, by itself, establish ribosome-mediated translation realization, protein folding, structural admissibility, biochemical function, organismal mechanism, or a universal biological law.  Each of those stronger readings would require its own layer-matched contact and its own controlled statistic.
