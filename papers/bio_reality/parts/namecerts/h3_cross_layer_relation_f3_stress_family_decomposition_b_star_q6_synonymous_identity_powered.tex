\subsection{Family decomposition of the $f_3$ stress coordinate}\label{sec:bioreality-f3-stress-family-decomposition}\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Object and row set.}
This record isolates the $f_3$ stress component of the nine-dimensional $B^*_{Q6}$ synonymous-residual panel and asks whether its protein-abundance contact is carried by particular synonymous codon families rather than by an undifferentiated aggregate.  The finite computation was run for \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae} on the unified yeast context join.  The base coding-sequence pool had $4759$ rows, the abundance join used $n=1800$ rows after the minimum-protein gate, and the control basis had rank $24$.  The target was residualized $\log_{10}$ protein abundance, with target squared norm $430.42705205115925$.  The full residualized $f_3$ stress projection had rank $1$, squared norm
$$
\begin{aligned}
\|P_{f_3}\|^2 &= 52.76911713166682,
\end{aligned}
$$
and accounted for the fraction
$$
\begin{aligned}
\|P_{f_3}\|^2 / \|y_Z\|^2 &= 0.12259712041843235
\end{aligned}
$$
of the residualized abundance target energy.  The family decomposition then split this one-dimensional projected source into $21$ additive synonymous-family subcoordinates and evaluated them with $5$ fixed folds and $120$ deterministic target-label permutations.

\paragraph{Construction of the family coordinates.}
Each family subcoordinate keeps only one Q9 synonymous-family block of the projected $f_3$ weights and divides by the norm of the full projected $f_3$ vector before the same $Z$-residualization used for the parent coordinate.  Thus the score attached to a family is not a new biological layer: it is an orthogonal readback of how much of the already residualized $f_3$ projection can be localized to that family block.  The reported energy statistic is
$$
\begin{aligned}
E_F &= \|P_F\|^2 / \|P_{f_3}\|^2,
\end{aligned}
$$
where $P_F$ is the target projection onto one residualized family subcoordinate and $P_{f_3}$ is the projection onto the full residualized $f_3$ source.  The computation produced all $21$ family entries.  Nineteen families crossed the held-out/permutation source gate: $F_{UUU\to UUC}$, $L_{UUA\to UUG}$, $S_{UCU\to UCG}$, $Y_{UAU\to UAC}$, $C_{UGU\to UGC}$, $P_{CCU\to CCG}$, $H_{CAU\to CAC}$, $Q_{CAA\to CAG}$, $R_{CGU\to CGG}$, $I_{AUU\to AUA}$, $T_{ACU\to ACG}$, $N_{AAU\to AAC}$, $K_{AAA\to AAG}$, $R_{AGA\to AGG}$, $V_{GUU\to GUG}$, $A_{GCU\to GCG}$, $D_{GAU\to GAC}$, $E_{GAA\to GAG}$, and $G_{GGU\to GGG}$.  The finite-row conclusion is therefore not merely that the aggregate $f_3$ coordinate touches the abundance target; it says that the aggregate can be decomposed into named synonymous-family directions and that many of those directions retain non-null held-out support under the same residual controls.

\paragraph{Relation to the nine-coordinate abundance panel.}
A sibling single-coordinate decomposition on the same yeast abundance context used $n=1258$ joined rows, $5$ folds, $120$ permutations, a rank-$24$ control basis, and a measured-readout dictionary of rank $3$.  That audit found real-source support for eight of the nine $B^*_{Q6}$ coordinates: $K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $Leu_{CUN/UUR}$, $Leu_{UUA/UUG}$, $Ser_{UCR/AGY}$, $Thr_{ACR/ACY}$, and $f_3$ stress.  The present family decomposition refines only the last of those sources.  It should therefore be read as an internal resolution of a surviving yeast protein-abundance residual coordinate, not as a claim that all $B^*_{Q6}$ coordinates have the same family-level anatomy.

\paragraph{Translation-adjacent checks.}
A separate position-controlled ribosome-occupancy computation reported $5754$ joined yeast genes, no length mismatches, $9$ computed families with at least $200$ within-position-bin permutations, and a description-length gate with $\lambda=0.01$ and $DL=2$.  In that row, $K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, and $f_3$ stress passed the combined $C_H>0$, null, and description-length gates.  This is useful consistency pressure because it places $f_3$ stress near a local ribosome-occupancy readout, but it is not the same statistic as the present abundance-family decomposition.  Likewise, a yeast mRNA-stability mediation row on $5085$ joined records reported $R^2_{QP}=0.2761140373962526$, $R^2_{QP\mid M}=0.24851799532219632$, an absorbed fraction $0.027596042074056304$, mediation fraction $0.09994436477872035$, permutation null mean $0.00019674487584783138$, null $95\%$ value $0.0014571003414947088$, and right-tail permutation probability $0.000999000999000999$.  Those values document a nearby transcript-stability decomposition; they do not supply the family-level abundance result by themselves.

\paragraph{Local verdict.}
The finite-row verdict is positive for the stated, bounded task.  With $21$ family subcoordinates computed, $19$ named synonymous families passing the real-source gate, $n=1800$ abundance rows, rank-$24$ controls, $5$ folds, and $120$ deterministic permutations, the $f_3$ stress component of $B^*_{Q6}$ is not an opaque scalar on this yeast abundance contact.  It has a reproducible synonymous-family decomposition under the declared residualization scheme.  The result is a controlled protein-abundance association and a decomposition of one residual coordinate.  It does not establish translation realization, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, mechanism, phenotype, or a universal biological law.  Any such higher-layer statement would require a separate matched contact and its own controlled statistic.
