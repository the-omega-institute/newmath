\subsection{Rank-admissible refusal for $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ translation survival}
\label{sec:bioreality-q6-rank-admissible-translation-survival-gate}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite carrier.}
This record concerns the finite matrix surface obtained from $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates, organism-indexed translation readout columns, and a declared control block $Z$. The intended survival entry is not the raw coupling score. It is a controlled, rank-audited entry $S^{QT}_{ij}$ asking whether a residual coordinate $q_i$ still predicts a translation readout $T_j$ after amino-acid composition, organism identity, GC3, CDS length, $M$-density, and simpler codon baselines have been removed. The finite carrier therefore consists of residualized coordinate columns, residualized translation-readout columns, and the post-control row space on which a held-out test can be falsified.

\paragraph{Rank gate.}
For each proposed pair $(q_i,T_j)$, the admissibility test first forms the residuals
$$
\begin{aligned}
q_i^{\perp Z} &= q_i - \Pi_Z q_i,\\
T_j^{\perp Z} &= T_j - \Pi_Z T_j.
\end{aligned}
$$
The entry may be interpreted as translation survival only when the post-control design has enough residual degrees of freedom and is not already saturated by the controls. In particular, an all-perfect fitted surface on too few organism rows is a refusal case, not evidence of survival. The break condition is local: if the residualized design has saturated rank, insufficient held-out support, or no non-vacuous improvement beyond the baseline, then $S^{QT}_{ij}$ is returned as unavailable rather than positive. This is the distinction between a contact that merely couples to a modeled translation surface and a contact that survives a controlled readout boundary.

\paragraph{Coupling is not survival.}
A separate coupling run, experiment id \texttt{4a783972e74b}, uses a $61$-codon count space over Homo sapiens, Saccharomyces cerevisiae, and Escherichia coli K12. It computes nine $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates: Arg AGR, Ile AUA, K AAA, Leu CUN versus UUR, Leu UUA versus UUG, Ser UCA versus UCG, Ser UCR versus AGY, Thr ACR versus ACY, and $f_3$ stress. Against the proxy tRNA-Leu CUN-versus-UUR log-copy spectrum, the break condition requiring all nine $|A_{ij}|<0.01$ is not met: there are $9$ nonzero entries above the coupling threshold and the maximum absolute entry is $1882464.1106377328$. This establishes only that the finite proxy surface has nonzero coupling. It does not establish $S^{QT}_{ij}>0$, because the available contact is a proxy readout rather than a same-row, held-out, rank-admissible translation-survival statistic.

\paragraph{Local verdict.}
The finite rows support a rank-admissibility rule rather than a positive translation-survival claim. The computed residual-basis run is powered enough to report persistent unexplained residual structure in four organisms, and the coupling run shows nonzero proxy contact in a three-organism codon/tRNA surface. However, the data presently do not contain an organism- or CDS-matched measured translation matrix with non-saturated residual rank for the exact $S^{QT}$ boundary. The correct readback is therefore: proposed $S^{QT}_{ij}$ entries remain testable only after residual rank, degrees of freedom, and held-out support are certified; rank-saturated or underpowered entries remain coupling-only or need more rows even if an in-sample score is positive or equal to $1.0$.

\paragraph{Boundary of interpretation.}
No translation realization, ribosome occupancy, protein abundance, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, phenotype, mechanism, or universal biological law follows from this record alone. Codon-usage tables, tRNA-copy counts, and tAI or stAI weights are admissible bounded readouts for constructing the test surface, but they are not themselves observations of protein output or higher-layer biology. Each higher layer requires its own matched contact and its own controlled statistic before promotion is allowed.
