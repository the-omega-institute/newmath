\subsection{Rank-admissible translation boundary for $B^*_{Q6}$ residuals}
\label{subsec:biorank-admissible-q6-translation-boundary}

\paragraph{Finite carrier.}
The object of this record is a finite residualized design surface whose rows are organism-indexed coding-sequence observations and whose columns separate three roles: $B^*_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates $q_i$, translation-layer readouts $T_j$, and controls $Z$.  The control side contains amino-acid composition, organism identity, $GC3$, coding length, and an $M$-density baseline whenever the joined panel supplies those fields.  A proposed translation entry $S^{QT}_{ij}$ is not accepted merely because an in-sample regression or coupling surface is large.  It is accepted only when the residualized post-control design still has enough rank and independent held-out support to make failure possible.

\paragraph{Observed residual panel.}
The residual-basis computation was carried out for four organisms and returned data-needed labels for two others.  The computed rows were $n=16008$ for \textit{Homo sapiens}, $n=3046$ for \textit{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}, $n=3415$ for \textit{Escherichia coli} K--12 MG1655, and $n=14410$ for \textit{Danio rerio}.  \textit{Gallus gallus} and \textit{Mycobacterium smegmatis} str. MC2 155 had no joined finite panel in this run and are therefore recorded as needs-data cases, not as negative biological outcomes.  The regularized extraction used $\lambda=0.01$ with description-length penalty $DL=\log_2(1+n_{\mathrm{col}})$.

\paragraph{Degree-of-freedom matched checks.}
The selected blocks were compared with deterministic Gaussian null surfaces matched for available degrees of freedom.  In human, the selected sequence was ptm\_density, complex\_member, tm\_count, measured\_trna\_tai, domain\_count, and localization.  The corresponding increments were $0.014780$, $0.004618$, $0.001444$, $0.000503$, $0.000038$, and $0.006528$, with null $95\%$ cutoffs $0.000285$, $0.000206$, $0.000244$, $0.000202$, $0.000219$, and $0.001160$.  The domain-count step did not exceed its matched null.  In yeast, measured\_te, ptm\_density, tm\_count, turnover, complex\_member, measured\_trna\_tai, domain\_count, mrna, and localization had increments $0.003750$, $0.003278$, $0.001421$, $0.001252$, $0.000935$, $0.000824$, $0.000356$, $0.000087$, and $0.008385$, against null $95\%$ cutoffs $0.001127$, $0.001491$, $0.001114$, $0.001040$, $0.001365$, $0.001209$, $0.001439$, $0.001115$, and $0.007085$.  The complex-member, measured-trna-tai, domain-count, and mrna yeast steps did not exceed their matched nulls.  In \textit{E. coli}, mrna exceeded its cutoff with increment $0.001771$ over $0.001337$, while measured\_te did not, with increment $0.000282$ below $0.001027$.  In zebrafish, measured\_trna\_tai exceeded its cutoff with increment $0.020817$ over $0.000231$.

\paragraph{Coupling is not survival.}
A separate three-organism proxy calculation loaded human, yeast, and \textit{E. coli} K--12 codon-count data in a $61$-codon count space and computed nine synonymous residual coordinates: Arg\_AGR, Ile\_AUA, K\_AAA, Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR, Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG, Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG, Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY, Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY, and f3\_stress.  Against the proxy tRNA-Leu CUN-versus-UUR log-copy spectrum, the coupling threshold was $0.01$, the break condition requiring all nine $|A_{ij}|<0.01$ was not met, and the largest absolute coupling was $1882464.110638$ with nine nonzero entries.  This establishes a finite coupling contact only.  It does not establish a surviving $S^{QT}_{ij}$ entry, because the readout was a proxy copy-number spectrum rather than a held-out translation observation under the full residualized rank gate.

\paragraph{Rank-admissibility rule.}
For each proposed pair $(q_i,T_j)$, form residuals after projecting both sides away from $Z$ and from simpler codon baselines.  The entry can be read as translation survival only if the remaining row space is not rank-saturated, the effective degrees of freedom exceed the fitted parameters and held-out split requirements, and the score remains positive outside the fitting surface.  The refusal condition is local and mechanical:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{Refuse}(S^{QT}_{ij})=1
\quad\Longleftarrow\quad
&\operatorname{rank}(X_{q_i,T_j\mid Z})\ \text{is saturated}\;\\
&\text{or the held-out translation readout has insufficient independent support}\;\\
&\text{or the positive score is only an in-sample projection artifact.}
\end{aligned}
$$
Under this rule, an all-$1.0$ fitted surface in a three-organism panel is a warning sign rather than evidence: it may report that the design has spent its rank, not that translation has been observed.

\paragraph{Local conclusion.}
The finite computation supports a narrower claim: $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinates leave persistent cross-layer residual structure in the available organism panels, and some selected readout blocks exceed matched null cutoffs.  It does not support promotion from codon-topology residuals to measured translation realization.  The chapter therefore records a rank-admissibility gate for future $S^{QT}_{ij}$ entries: positive coupling, modeled tAI or stAI contact, tRNA-copy readback, or a saturated fit remains coupling-only until a same-organism or same-CDS translation-readout panel supplies non-saturated held-out survival after $Z$ controls.  No claim about ribosome occupancy, protein abundance, folded structure, physical admissibility, biological function, mechanism, phenotype, causation, or universality follows from this record without a separate layer-matched contact and its own finite statistic.
