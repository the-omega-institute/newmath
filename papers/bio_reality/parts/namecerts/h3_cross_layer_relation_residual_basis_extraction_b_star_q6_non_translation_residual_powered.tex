\paragraph{Finite carrier.}
The record is a finite-row test object for deciding when a proposed $B^*_{Q6}$ coordinate may even be treated as a falsifiable translation-readout survival entry.  The carrier consists of residualized synonymous coordinates $q_i$, organism-indexed translation readout surfaces $T_j$, and control columns $Z$ for amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, coding length, and the $M$-density baseline.  The admissibility question is not whether an in-sample fit is large.  It is whether the residual design left after projecting $q_i$ and $T_j$ away from $Z$ still has enough independent row space to make held-out survival meaningful.  Thus the intended entry $S^{QT}_{ij}$ is only readable after the rank check has separated a testable survival surface from a rank-saturated projection artifact.

\paragraph{Rank-admissible translation gate.}
These numbers justify a boundary result rather than a translation-survival promotion.  The persistence of the residual curves says that the selected readout blocks do not absorb the $B^*_{Q6}$ residual basis to a compressible endpoint.  The degree-of-freedom-matched steps show that some readout blocks are nontrivial under the chosen finite null, but this is still not the same as a held-out $S^{QT}_{ij}>0$ entry for a particular coordinate/readout pair.  A proposed entry must first pass the rank condition
$$
\begin{aligned}
\operatorname{rank}\bigl((I-P_Z)[q_i,T_j]\bigr)&<n_{\mathrm{rows}}-\dim Z,\\
\mathrm{df}_{ij}&>0,
\end{aligned}
$$
with the residualized readout held outside the fitting artifact that created the score.  If the post-control row space is saturated, or if the remaining degrees of freedom make the held-out check vacuous, the entry is assigned to the refusal state even when the fitted in-sample surface is positive or equal to $1.0$.

\paragraph{Coupling is not survival.}
A separate proxy computation reports nine computed synonymous residual coordinates, namely Arg AGR, Ile AUA, K AAA, Leu CUN versus UUR, Leu UUA versus UUG, Ser UCA versus UCG, Ser UCR versus AGY, Thr ACR versus ACY, and $f_3$ stress.  In that proxy matrix, the three-organism contact over human, yeast, and \textit{E. coli} K12 did not meet the all-small break condition: the maximum absolute coupling was $1882464.1106377328$, there were $9$ entries above the $0.01$ threshold, and the break condition requiring all nine $|A_{ij}|<0.01$ was false.  This supports a coupling contact against a tRNA-Leu CUN-versus-UUR log-copy proxy, not a translation survival result.  The proxy readout lacks the matched tAI/stAI, ribosome-profiling, or explicitly controlled translation-efficiency rows needed for a non-saturated $S^{QT}_{ij}$ claim.

\paragraph{Local no-promotion rule.}
The chapter therefore records a rank-admissibility refusal boundary.  The finite data support residual computation, persistent residual classification, and selected readout-block comparisons under a deterministic degree-of-freedom-matched null.  They do not by themselves establish ribosome occupancy, protein abundance, folded structure, physical admissibility, biological function, molecular mechanism, phenotype, or a universal biological rule.  Any such promotion requires a separate layer-matched contact and its own controlled statistic after the $q_i\to T_j$ translation-readout step has survived with non-saturated rank.  Until that condition is met, $B^*_{Q6}$ remains a residual coordinate system with bounded readout contacts and explicit refusal of rank-saturated survival.
