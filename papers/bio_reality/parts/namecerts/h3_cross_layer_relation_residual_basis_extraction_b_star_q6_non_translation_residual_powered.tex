\subsection{Rank-admissible refusal for $B^*_{Q6}$ translation survival}
\label{sec:bioreality-q6-rank-admissible-translation-survival-refusal}

\paragraph{Finite carrier.}
This record treats the proposed $B^*_{Q6}$ translation-survival surface as a finite-row rank question before it is allowed to become a biological interpretation.  The carrier is the residualized design matrix whose columns are $B^*_{Q6}$ synonymous coordinates, organism-indexed translation readout surfaces, and control columns $Z$.  The control set includes amino-acid composition, organism identity, GC3, coding-sequence length, and the $M$-density baseline.  The admissibility question is not whether a fitted in-sample score can be made positive.  It is whether a proposed entry $S^{QT}_{ij}$ still has residual degrees of freedom after $q_i$ and $T_j$ are projected away from $Z$, so that a held-out translation-readout test could fail.

For a coordinate $q_i$ and readout $T_j$, let $\widetilde q_i$ and $\widetilde T_j$ denote the residuals after projection away from $Z$.  The local gate is the following finite-rank condition:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\operatorname{rank}(Z, q_i, T_j) &> \operatorname{rank}(Z),\\
\operatorname{rank}(Z, q_i, T_j) &< n_{ij},\\
\operatorname{df}_{ij} &= n_{ij} - \operatorname{rank}(Z, q_i, T_j) > 0.
\end{aligned}
$$
If the rank reaches the row count, or if the surviving residual comparison has no independent support, the entry is recorded as underpowered rather than as translation survival.  Thus an all-$1.0$ fitted surface, or any saturated regression surface on a three-organism panel, is treated as a projection artifact unless the same entry survives on non-saturated held-out rows.

\paragraph{Coupling evidence and survival refusal.}
A separate finite coupling run computed nine $B^*_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates against a proxy tRNA-Leu CUN-versus-UUR log-copy spectrum.  The computed coordinate list is
$$
\begin{aligned}
&\mathrm{Arg\_AGR},\ \mathrm{Ile\_AUA},\ \mathrm{K\_AAA},\ \mathrm{Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR},\\
&\mathrm{Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG},\ \mathrm{Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG},\ \mathrm{Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY},\\
&\mathrm{Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY},\ f_3\text{-}\mathrm{stress}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The codon-count space has $61$ codons, the loaded organism scope is \emph{H. sapiens}, \emph{S. cerevisiae}, and \emph{E. coli} K-12, and the coupling threshold is $0.01$.  The break condition for that coupling packet would be met only if all nine coordinates had $|A_{ij}|<0.01$ against the proxy readout.  It is not met: there are $9$ nonzero entries above threshold and the maximum reported absolute coupling is $1882464.1106377328$.  This establishes a finite proxy contact with codon usage and tRNA-copy readback, but it does not establish $S^{QT}_{ij}>0$.

The distinction is essential.  A nonzero $A^{QT}_{ij}$ says that a proxy readout has a finite coupling with residual coordinate geometry under the recorded construction.  A survival entry $S^{QT}_{ij}$ requires a non-saturated residual design, positive remaining degrees of freedom after $Z$, and a held-out translation-readout boundary that could reject the proposed entry.  Therefore the present record accepts proxy coupling and residual persistence, but refuses translation-survival promotion whenever the design is rank-saturated, underpowered, or based only on modeled adaptation weights.

\paragraph{Rank-admissibility verdict.}
The chapter-specific conclusion is a refusal rule rather than a positive translation law.  The residual basis is real as a finite computation: four organisms were computed, two organisms remain data-missing, all computed organisms were persistent, and the minimum final residual ratio was $0.9723341658092314$.  The coupling matrix is also real as a finite proxy computation: all nine listed $B^*_{Q6}$ coordinates have nonzero proxy coupling above the $0.01$ threshold in the three-organism codon/tRNA-contact run.  However, neither result supplies a measured, organism- or CDS-matched, non-saturated $S^{QT}$ surface.

Consequently, the admissible readback for each proposed $S^{QT}_{ij}$ entry is local and conditional: report the residualized rank, the degrees of freedom left after $Z$, the row support used for a held-out translation readout, and the failure condition under which the entry remains coupling-only or data-seeking.  No translation realization, ribosome occupancy, protein abundance, folding, physical admissibility, biological function, mechanism, phenotype, or universal biological rule follows from this record unless a separate layer-matched contact supplies the relevant rows and the corresponding controlled statistic is recomputed.
