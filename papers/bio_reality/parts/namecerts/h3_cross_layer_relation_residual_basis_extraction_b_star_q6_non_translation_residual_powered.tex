\subsection{Rank-admissible refusal for $B^*_{Q6}$ translation survival}
\label{sec:biorreality-q6-rank-admissible-translation-survival-refusal}

\paragraph{Finite carrier.}
The record considered here is not a claim that a synonymous codon coordinate has already survived into translation.  It is a finite-row admissibility rule for deciding when such a statement is even testable.  The carrier consists of residualized $B^*_{Q6}$ synonymous coordinates, organism-indexed codon and translation-readout surfaces, and a control block $Z$ containing amino-acid composition, organism identity, GC3, coding length, and an $M$-density baseline.  For a proposed entry $S^{QT}_{ij}$, the gate first residualizes the coordinate $q_i$ and the translation readout $T_j$ against $Z$.  Only the remaining row space is allowed to support a survival statement.  If that row space is rank-saturated, or if the available rows cannot support a held-out comparison, the entry is returned as coupling-only or needs-data, even if an in-sample fit is positive or perfect.

\paragraph{Rank rule.}
The local admissibility condition is the following finite-rank test:
$$
\begin{aligned}
q_i^{\perp Z} &= q_i - \Pi_Z q_i,\\
T_j^{\perp Z} &= T_j - \Pi_Z T_j,\\
S^{QT}_{ij} &> 0 \quad \text{is reportable only if } \operatorname{rank}(q_i^{\perp Z},T_j^{\perp Z}) \text{ leaves non-saturated held-out degrees of freedom.}
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus an all-$1.0$ fitted surface on too few rows is not a translation-survival result.  It is a rank warning.  The biological boundary is before protein abundance, folded structure, physical feasibility, function, mechanism, phenotype, or any universal biological law.  Those layers require their own matched contacts and their own finite statistics.

\paragraph{Observed residual panel.}
The residual-basis computation was run under experiment $c1ef9b455187$.  Four organisms had sufficient joined rows for the recursive extraction: $\mathrm{danio\ rerio}$ with $n=14410$, $\mathrm{escherichia\ coli\ K12\ MG1655}$ with $n=3415$, $\mathrm{homo\ sapiens}$ with $n=16008$, and $\mathrm{saccharomyces\ cerevisiae}$ with $n=3046$.  Two organisms were not promoted into computed status: $\mathrm{gallus\ gallus}$ and $\mathrm{mycobacterium\ smegmatis\ str\ mc^2\ 155}$ were both recorded as needs-data.  The computed organisms were all classified as persistent rather than compressible.  The final residual fractions were $0.9791828971490305$ for $\mathrm{danio\ rerio}$, $0.9979478314995476$ for $\mathrm{E.\ coli}$, $0.9723341658092314$ for human, and $0.9798667977250413$ for yeast.  The minimum final residual fraction was therefore $0.9723341658092314$ against a persistence floor of $0.97$, with no organism classified as compressible or partially compressible.

\paragraph{Greedy extraction readback.}
The extraction used the stated regularizer $\lambda=0.01$ and the description-length term $\log_2(1+n_{\mathrm{col}})$.  In $\mathrm{danio\ rerio}$, a single candidate block was selected, measured\_trna\_tai, with increment $0.02081710285096942$ against a degree-of-freedom matched null $95\%$ level of $0.0002306374780841125$.  In $\mathrm{E.\ coli}$, the first selected block was mrna with increment $0.0017706954554909888$ above null $0.0013371669456573104$, while the second selected block measured\_te had increment $0.0002819723320884449$ below null $0.0010272364744326006$.  In human, six blocks were selected: ptm\_density, complex\_member, tm\_count, measured\_trna\_tai, domain\_count, and localization.  Their increments were $0.014780177462400674$, $0.004618389163869967$, $0.0014438137599134737$, $0.0005032876020244136$, $0.00003840392718095609$, and $0.006528084968494762$; all exceeded their null levels except domain\_count, whose increment was below $0.00021932006910265595$.  In yeast, nine blocks were selected.  measured\_te, ptm\_density, tm\_count, turnover, and localization exceeded their matched null levels, whereas complex\_member, measured\_trna\_tai, domain\_count, and mrna did not.

\paragraph{Proxy coupling is not survival.}
A separate finite coupling computation, experiment $4a783972e74b$, loaded three cross-organism rows: $\mathrm{homo\ sapiens}$, $\mathrm{saccharomyces\ cerevisiae}$, and $\mathrm{escherichia\ coli\ K12}$.  It used a tRNA-Leu CUN-versus-UUR log-copy proxy rather than a direct held-out translation readout.  Nine synonymous residual coordinates were computed: Arg\_AGR, Ile\_AUA, K\_AAA, Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR, Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG, Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG, Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY, Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY, and f3\_stress.  The coupling threshold was $0.01$, the break condition was that all nine $q_i$ have $|A_{ij}|<0.01$, and that break condition was not met: the maximum absolute proxy coupling was $1882464.1106377328$ with nine nonzero entries.  This is evidence that the proxy contact is nontrivial as a bounded coupling surface.  It is not evidence that any $S^{QT}_{ij}$ entry survived the rank-admissible gate.

\paragraph{Decision.}
The finite rows support a negative, rank-aware conclusion.  The $B^*_{Q6}$ residual basis is present and persistent across four computed organisms, and several selected readout blocks beat degree-of-freedom matched null levels.  However, the present record does not contain an organism- or CDS-matched measured translation panel with non-saturated residual rank for this exact $S^{QT}$ boundary.  The correct status is therefore not translation realization, but refusal to promote.  A positive survival entry would require matched codon rows, a direct or explicitly bounded translation readout, the declared controls $Z$, held-out support, and a non-saturated residualized design.  Without that additional contact and statistic, no claim follows about ribosome occupancy, protein abundance, folding, physical admissibility, biological function, mechanism, phenotype, or universality.
