\subsection{Rank-Admissible Translation Survival Gate for $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ Residual Coordinates}
\label{sec:bior-reality-q6-rank-admissible-translation-survival-gate}

\paragraph{Finite carrier.}
This record concerns a finite residualized design matrix whose rows are organism-indexed readout surfaces and whose columns contain $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates together with controls $Z$.  The controls are the variables required before a codon-topology residual may be compared with a translation readout: amino-acid composition, organism identity, GC3, coding-sequence length, and an $M$-density baseline.  The mathematical object tested here is not a protein, structure, pathway, or phenotype.  It is a rank-audited candidate entry $S^{QT}_{ij}$, where $q_i$ is one of the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates and $T_j$ is a translation-facing readout such as a bounded tAI or stAI weight, a measured translation-efficiency surface, or another organism-matched translation readout.

\paragraph{Gate equation.}
For each proposed entry, the admissible object is the residual pair obtained after removing $Z$ from both sides.  If $\widetilde q_i$ and $\widetilde T_j$ denote those residuals, then a survival entry is testable only when the remaining row space has enough independent support for a held-out statistic:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\widetilde q_i &= q_i - \Pi_Z q_i,\\
\widetilde T_j &= T_j - \Pi_Z T_j,\\
S^{QT}_{ij} &> 0 \quad \hbox{is reportable only if the residual design is not rank saturated.}
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus an in-sample fitted value, a positive coupling coefficient, or an all-$1.0$ controlled surface is not by itself a translation-survival entry.  The gate rejects the entry when the residualized design has saturated rank or too few independent rows to make held-out failure possible.  In that case the entry remains a coupling-only contact or a needs-data row.

\paragraph{Computed residual basis.}
The finite-row residual-basis computation was run under experiment \texttt{c1ef9b455187}.  Four organisms had sufficient local readout data and two were classified as needs-data.  The computed organisms were \texttt{danio\_rerio}, \texttt{escherichia\_coli\_k12\_mg1655}, \texttt{homo\_sapiens}, and \texttt{saccharomyces\_cerevisiae}; \texttt{gallus\_gallus} and \texttt{mycobacterium\_smegmatis\_str\_mc2\_155} had no joined finite row count in this run.  The joined row counts were $14410$ for zebrafish, $3415$ for \emph{E. coli} K-12 MG1655, $16008$ for human, and $3046$ for budding yeast.  The regularizer was the stated $\lambda=0.01$ description-length penalty $DL=\log_2(1+n_{\mathrm{col}})$.  The residual energy curves stayed high in every computed organism: zebrafish had $\rho=(1.0,0.9791828971490305)$; \emph{E. coli} had $\rho=(1.0,0.9982293045445091,0.9979478314995476)$; human had final $\rho=0.9723341658092314$ after six selected blocks; yeast had final $\rho=0.9798667977250413$ after nine selected blocks.  The run therefore classified all four computed organisms as persistent, with no compressible or partially compressible organism and with a reported persistence floor of $0.97$.

\paragraph{Null-matched readout blocks.}
The per-step deterministic Gaussian null check gives the finite evidence that this record is a rank and support audit rather than a biological promotion.  Zebrafish selected \texttt{measured\_trna\_tai} with gain $0.02081710285096942$ above a $95\%$ null level of $0.0002306374780841125$.  \emph{E. coli} selected \texttt{mrna} with gain $0.0017706954554909888$ above $0.0013371669456573104$, followed by \texttt{measured\_te} with gain $0.0002819723320884449$ below $0.0010272364744326006$.  Human selected \texttt{ptm\_density}, \texttt{complex\_member}, \texttt{tm\_count}, \texttt{measured\_trna\_tai}, \texttt{domain\_count}, and \texttt{localization}; the corresponding gains were $0.014780177462400674$, $0.004618389163869967$, $0.0014438137599134737$, $0.0005032876020244136$, $0.00003840392718095609$, and $0.006528084968494762$, with only the \texttt{domain\_count} step below its $95\%$ null level.  Yeast selected \texttt{measured\_te}, \texttt{ptm\_density}, \texttt{tm\_count}, \texttt{turnover}, \texttt{complex\_member}, \texttt{measured\_trna\_tai}, \texttt{domain\_count}, \texttt{mrna}, and \texttt{localization}; steps $1$, $2$, $3$, $4$, and $9$ exceeded their matched null levels, while steps $5$ through $8$ did not.  These numbers identify finite residual contacts, but they do not by themselves establish $S^{QT}_{ij}>0$ for a measured translation-survival matrix.

\paragraph{Coupling contact and refusal rule.}
A separate coupling computation under experiment \texttt{4a783972e74b} used three organisms, namely \texttt{homo\_sapiens}, \texttt{saccharomyces\_cerevisiae}, and \texttt{escherichia\_coli\_k12}.  It computed nine synonymous residual coordinates: \texttt{Arg\_AGR}, \texttt{Ile\_AUA}, \texttt{K\_AAA}, \texttt{Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR}, \texttt{Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG}, \texttt{Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG}, \texttt{Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY}, \texttt{Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY}, and \texttt{f3\_stress}.  Against the proxy tRNA-Leu CUN-versus-UUR log-copy spectrum, the maximum absolute coupling was $1882464.1106377328$ and all nine entries exceeded the $0.01$ coupling threshold, so the declared all-small break condition was not met.  This is a coupling result only.  The readout was a proxy copy-number spectrum rather than an organism-matched measured ribosome or translation-efficiency panel under the full control set, and therefore it cannot be read as controlled survival.

\paragraph{Local verdict.}
The admissible conclusion is a finite rank-survival gate: a proposed $S^{QT}_{ij}$ entry must be residualized against $Z$, must retain non-saturated rank, and must have enough independent organism or readout support for a held-out failure test.  The available finite rows show persistent residual structure and several null-exceeding readout-block contacts, but they also preserve the refusal boundary.  No translation realization, ribosome occupancy, protein abundance, folded structure, physical admissibility, biological function, mechanism, phenotype, or universal biological law follows from these rows unless a separate layer-matched contact supplies the relevant measurement and the controlled statistic is recomputed at that layer.
