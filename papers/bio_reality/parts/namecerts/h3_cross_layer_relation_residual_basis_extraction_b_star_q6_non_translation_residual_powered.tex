\subsection{Rank-admissible translation-survival gate for $B^*_{Q6}$ residuals}
\label{subsec:b-star-q6-rank-admissible-translation-survival-gate}

\paragraph{Question.}
The record tests a narrow cross-layer question: when a synonymous residual coordinate $q_i$ in the $B^*_{Q6}$ family is compared with a translation readout $T_j$, when is an entry $S^{QT}_{ij}$ even eligible to be called a survival contact?  The answer used here is not the size of an in-sample fit.  It is a rank and support condition on the residualized design after the declared controls $Z$ have been removed.  The controls are amino-acid composition, organism identity, GC3, coding-sequence length, and the $M$-density baseline, with matched mRNA or simpler codon baselines added when the readout calls for them.  The proposed entry is admissible only if the remaining row space leaves held-out degrees of freedom and does not collapse into a saturated projection.

\paragraph{Finite carrier.}
The finite carrier consists of organism-indexed rows, residual coordinates $q_i$, translation-layer readout columns $T_j$, and control columns $Z$.  For each candidate entry, the gate first computes the residualized pair
$$
\begin{aligned}
q_i^\perp &= q_i - \Pi_Z q_i,\\
T_j^\perp &= T_j - \Pi_Z T_j,
\end{aligned}
$$
and then asks whether the remaining matrix has enough independent rows to make failure possible.  A fitted score of $1.0$ on a rank-saturated surface is therefore treated as a refusal signal, not as biological translation survival.  The admissible status is a statement about falsifiability of $S^{QT}_{ij}$; it is not a claim about ribosome occupancy, protein abundance, folded structure, physical possibility, function, mechanism, phenotype, or a universal biological rule.

\paragraph{Observed residual-basis rows.}
The residual-basis extraction run was computed for four organisms and returned two organisms as needing more data.  The computed rows were \textit{Homo sapiens}, \textit{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}, \textit{Escherichia coli} K12 MG1655, and \textit{Danio rerio}; \textit{Gallus gallus} and \textit{Mycobacterium smegmatis} str. MC2 155 were labelled \texttt{needs\_data}.  The finite row counts and selected-basis sizes were organism-specific: human used $16008$ joined common rows with $6$ candidate blocks; yeast used $3046$ rows with $9$ candidate blocks; \textit{E. coli} used $3415$ rows with $2$ candidate blocks; zebrafish used $14410$ rows with $1$ candidate block.  The regularized extraction used the stated penalty scale $\lambda=0.01$ with description-length term $\log_2(1+n_{\mathrm{col}})$.

\paragraph{Residual persistence.}
The extraction did not produce a compressible residual class.  All four computed organisms were labelled persistent, with no organisms in the compressible or partially compressible lists.  The final residual ratios remained above the declared floor $0.97$:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\rho_{\mathrm{human, final}} &= 0.9723341658092314,\\
\rho_{\mathrm{yeast, final}} &= 0.9798667977250413,\\
\rho_{\mathrm{ecoli, final}} &= 0.9979478314995476,\\
\rho_{\mathrm{zebrafish, final}} &= 0.9791828971490305.
\end{aligned}
$$
The minimum final value was $0.9723341658092314$, so the finite panel reports a negative residual-compression result rather than a clean explanatory absorption.  The human row had $d_{\mathrm{modeled}}=0.9020707321191321$ and $d_{\mathrm{difference}}=0.018824203448813415$; yeast had $d_{\mathrm{modeled}}=0.2199040339633322$ and $d_{\mathrm{difference}}=0.008171838236265161$; \textit{E. coli} had $d_{\mathrm{modeled}}=0.6628211177352172$ and $d_{\mathrm{difference}}=0.00017488612263594217$; zebrafish had $d_{\mathrm{modeled}}=0.874628721049266$ and $d_{\mathrm{difference}}=0.0$.  These values justify keeping the packet as a boundary test: the residual geometry is real enough to audit, but it is not absorbed well enough to promote without a separate readout.

\paragraph{Null-calibrated selected blocks.}
The per-step deterministic Gaussian null shows that some selected readout blocks exceed their matched null while later blocks often do not.  In human, the selected increments for \texttt{ptm\_density}, \texttt{complex\_member}, \texttt{tm\_count}, \texttt{measured\_trna\_tai}, \texttt{domain\_count}, and \texttt{localization} were $0.014780177462400674$, $0.004618389163869967$, $0.0014438137599134737$, $0.0005032876020244136$, $0.00003840392718095609$, and $0.006528084968494762$; the corresponding null $95\%$ thresholds were $0.0002845846156909306$, $0.000206488525646134$, $0.0002435268444123588$, $0.00020173994173736226$, $0.00021932006910265595$, and $0.0011598998794309233$.  The fifth human step did not exceed its null.  In yeast, steps $1$ through $4$ and step $9$ exceeded null, while steps $5$ through $8$ did not; the final localization increment was $0.008385002223710514$ against null $0.00708487840939486$.  In \textit{E. coli}, the first mRNA step exceeded null with increment $0.0017706954554909888$ against $0.0013371669456573104$, but the second measured-TE step did not, with increment $0.0002819723320884449$ against $0.0010272364744326006$.  Zebrafish had a single measured-tRNA-tAI step, increment $0.02081710285096942$ against null $0.0002306374780841125$.

\paragraph{Coupling evidence and survival refusal.}
A separate finite coupling run loaded three organisms, human, yeast, and \textit{E. coli} K12, in a $61$-codon count space.  It computed nine synonymous residual coordinates: \texttt{Arg\_AGR}, \texttt{Ile\_AUA}, \texttt{K\_AAA}, \texttt{Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR}, \texttt{Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG}, \texttt{Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG}, \texttt{Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY}, \texttt{Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY}, and \texttt{f3\_stress}.  Against the proxy tRNA-Leu CUN-versus-UUR log-copy spectrum, the break condition requiring all nine $|A_{ij}|<0.01$ was not met: there were $9$ nonzero entries above the coupling threshold, with maximum absolute coupling $1882464.1106377328$.  This is contact with a proxy readout, not a survival certificate.  The same run explicitly kept true tAI, stAI, ribosome-profiling, and translation-survival readouts outside the coupling claim.

\paragraph{Gate verdict.}
The finite evidence supports the rank-admissibility rule as a necessary filter.  A proposed $S^{QT}_{ij}$ entry is allowed to proceed only when the residualized design has non-saturated rank, enough post-control degrees of freedom, and held-out translation rows capable of falsifying the entry.  If those conditions fail, the output is \texttt{needs\_data} or coupling-only, even when an in-sample score is positive, very large, or exactly $1.0$.  The present panel therefore records a disciplined no-promotion result: $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinates have finite-row residual and proxy-coupling structure, but no translation, folding, physical admissibility, protein function, mechanism, phenotype, or universal law follows unless a distinct layer-matched contact supplies the relevant rows and the controlled statistic survives.
