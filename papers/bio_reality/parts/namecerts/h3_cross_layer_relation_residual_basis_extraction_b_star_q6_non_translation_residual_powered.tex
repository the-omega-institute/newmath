\subsection{Rank-admissible translation-survival gate for $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates}
\label{sec:h3-cross-layer-relation-residual-basis-extraction-b-star-q6-non-translation-residual-powered}

\paragraph{Finite carrier.}
The finite carrier is the residualized $B^{*}_{Q6}$ design surface together with organism-indexed translation readout candidates and the control block $Z$.  The intended entry is not a raw association coefficient.  It is a proposed survival score $S^{QT}_{ij}$ for a residual coordinate $q_i$ and a translation readout $T_j$ after controlling amino-acid composition, organism identity, GC3, coding-sequence length, and the $M$-density baseline.  The gate therefore asks a prior question: whether the post-control row space still has enough independent support to make held-out survival falsifiable.  A fitted value at or near one is not evidence when the design has already spent its degrees of freedom.

\paragraph{Observed finite rows.}
The residual-basis computation was run on six organism entries.  Four organisms had sufficient joined rows and two remained data gaps.  The computed organisms were \emph{Danio rerio} with $14410$ joined rows, \emph{Escherichia coli} K12 MG1655 with $3415$ joined rows, \emph{Homo sapiens} with $16008$ joined rows, and \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae} with $3046$ joined rows.  \emph{Gallus gallus} and \emph{Mycobacterium smegmatis} str. MC2 155 were marked $\mathrm{needs\_data}$.  The regularized extraction used the stated $\lambda=0.01$ description-length penalty $DL=\log_2(1+n_{\mathrm{col}})$, and every computed organism was classified as persistent rather than compressible.

$$
\begin{aligned}
\rho_{\mathrm{Danio}}&=(1.0000000000,0.9791828971),\\
\rho_{\mathrm{Ecoli}}&=(1.0000000000,0.9982293045,0.9979478315),\\
\rho_{\mathrm{Human}}&=(1.0000000000,0.9852198225,0.9806696940,0.9792537896,0.9787609433,0.9787233550,0.9723341658),\\
\rho_{\mathrm{Yeast}}&=(1.0000000000,0.9962498990,0.9929843171,0.9915730985,0.9903314907,0.9894054695,0.9885902294,0.9882386501,0.9881524583,0.9798667977).

$$

The minimum final residual ratio among computed organisms was $0.9723341658$, with persistent organisms $\{\mathrm{danio\_rerio},\mathrm{escherichia\_coli\_k12\_mg1655},\mathrm{homo\_sapiens},\mathrm{saccharomyces\_cerevisiae}\}$ and no organism classified as compressible or partially compressible.  This is a negative finite-row result in the precise sense that the selected readout blocks did not absorb the residual $B^{*}_{Q6}$ geometry below the declared persistence floor $0.97$.

\paragraph{Per-step null readback.}
The degree-of-freedom matched deterministic Gaussian nulls show that some selected blocks exceed their null threshold, but that fact alone does not promote a $q_i$ coordinate to translation survival.  In \emph{Danio rerio}, the single selected measured-trna-tAI step had increment $0.02081710285096942$ against null $p95=0.0002306374780841125$.  In \emph{E. coli}, the mRNA step exceeded the null with increment $0.0017706954554909888$ against $0.0013371669456573104$, while the measured-TE step did not exceed the null, with increment $0.0002819723320884449$ against $0.0010272364744326006$.  In human rows, ptm-density, complex membership, TM count, measured-trna-tAI, and localization exceeded their stepwise nulls, while domain count did not: the domain-count increment was $3.840392718095609\times 10^{-5}$ against $0.00021932006910265595$.  In yeast rows, measured TE, ptm-density, TM count, turnover, and localization exceeded their nulls, while complex membership, measured-trna-tAI, domain count, and mRNA did not.

\paragraph{Rank-admissibility rule.}
For each candidate entry $S^{QT}_{ij}$, let $\widetilde q_i$ and $\widetilde T_j$ be the residuals after projection away from $Z$.  The gate accepts the entry as testable only when the residual design has non-saturated rank and enough independent held-out support.  In local notation the refusal condition is:

$$
\begin{aligned}
S^{QT}_{ij}\ \mathrm{is\ refused} \quad \Longleftarrow \quad
&\operatorname{rank}([Z,q_i,T_j])=n_{\mathrm{rows}}\\
&\quad \text{or the held-out residual readout has insufficient independent support.}
\end{aligned}
$$

When this condition holds, an in-sample positive score, a perfect fitted $R^2$, or a nonzero coupling coefficient is recorded only as coupling or as a data request.  It is not a translation-survival entry.  This distinction is necessary because the related three-organism coupling run loaded human, yeast, and \emph{E. coli} codon/tRNA-copy rows and found nine nonzero proxy couplings above threshold $0.01$, with maximum absolute proxy coefficient $1882464.1106377328$, but that computation explicitly used a tRNA-Leu CUN-versus-UUR log-copy proxy rather than a direct measured translation readout.  Its break condition, all nine $q_i$ having $|A_{ij}|<0.01$, was not met; nevertheless, $A^{QT}$ coupling is still not $S^{QT}$ survival.

\paragraph{Interpretation boundary.}
The chapter records a rank and readout boundary before biological promotion.  The finite rows support the existence of persistent residual $B^{*}_{Q6}$ structure under the stated greedy extraction and show where individual readout blocks do or do not exceed matched null thresholds.  They do not show ribosome occupancy, protein abundance, folded structure, physical admissibility, biological function, mechanism, phenotype, or a universal rule.  A separate organism- or CDS-matched translation contact with non-saturated residual rank is required before any $S^{QT}_{ij}>0$ entry can be stated.  Separate contacts are again required for protein output, structure, function, mechanism, or system-level interpretation.  Thus the scientific content of this gate is a refusal discipline: a rank-saturated or underpowered surface is not allowed to masquerade as translation survival, even when the fitted in-sample surface looks numerically strong.
