\subsection{Rank-Admissible Translation Survival Gate for B-Star-Q6 Residual Coordinates}
\label{sec:bior-q6-rank-admissible-translation-survival-gate}

\paragraph{Finite carrier.}
This record concerns the finite design in which synonymous $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinates $q_i$ are paired with organism-indexed translation readout surfaces $T_j$ and residualized against a control block $Z$.  The intended control block contains amino-acid composition, organism identity, GC3, coding-sequence length, and $M$-density baseline terms, with simpler codon baselines used as an ablation check when the joined rows exist.  The gate is deliberately placed before any protein, structural, functional, phenotypic, or mechanistic reading.  A proposed translation-survival entry $S^{QT}_{ij}$ is admissible only when the post-control residual design has enough remaining rank and enough held-out support to make survival falsifiable.  If the residualized row space is saturated, or if the degrees of freedom are too small to separate a genuine held-out signal from a projection artifact, the entry remains unpromoted even if an in-sample fit is positive or exactly $1.0$.

\paragraph{Residual-basis computation.}
The finite residual-basis run used the rule
$$
\begin{aligned}
R_0 &= P_{Q \perp T_{\mathrm{mod}}},\\
\rho_k &= \frac{\lVert R_k\rVert}{\lVert R_0\rVert},\\
\lambda &= 0.01,\\
\mathrm{DL}(H) &= \log_2(1+n_{\mathrm{col}}),
\end{aligned}
$$
where the selected readout block $H_k$ absorbs part of $R_{k-1}$ and the step is compared with a degree-of-freedom-matched deterministic Gaussian null.  Four organisms had computable joined panels.  For \textit{Homo sapiens}, $n=16008$ joined rows and six selected candidates gave $d_{\mathrm{modeled}}=0.9020707321191321$, $d_{\mathrm{difference}}=0.018824203448813415$, and
$$
\begin{aligned}
\rho &= (1.0,\ 0.9852198225375993,\ 0.9806696939851618,\ 0.9792537895870558,\\
&\quad 0.9787609432955213,\ 0.9787233550315274,\ 0.9723341658092314).
\end{aligned}
$$
For \textit{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}, $n=3046$ rows and nine selected candidates gave $d_{\mathrm{modeled}}=0.2199040339633322$, $d_{\mathrm{difference}}=0.008171838236265161$, and final $\rho=0.9798667977250413$.  For \textit{Escherichia coli} K-12 MG1655, $n=3415$ rows and two candidates gave $d_{\mathrm{modeled}}=0.6628211177352172$, $d_{\mathrm{difference}}=0.00017488612263594217$, and final $\rho=0.9979478314995476$.  For \textit{Danio rerio}, $n=14410$ rows and one candidate gave $d_{\mathrm{modeled}}=0.874628721049266$, $d_{\mathrm{difference}}=0.0$, and final $\rho=0.9791828971490305$.  \textit{Gallus gallus} and \textit{Mycobacterium smegmatis} str. MC2 155 were not assigned residual curves because the joined readout rows were absent.

\paragraph{Coupling contact versus survival contact.}
A separate three-organism proxy coupling computation loaded \textit{Homo sapiens}, \textit{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}, and \textit{Escherichia coli} K-12 over the $61$-codon count space.  It computed nine $B^*_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates: Arg\_AGR, Ile\_AUA, K\_AAA, Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR, Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG, Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG, Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY, Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY, and f3\_stress.  Against the proxy readout given by the tRNA-Leu CUN-versus-UUR log-copy spectrum, the break condition requiring all nine $|A_{ij}|<0.01$ was not met: there were nine nonzero entries above the coupling threshold and the recorded maximum absolute coupling was $1882464.1106377328$.  This is a contact with the translation-readout boundary, not a survival claim.  The readout is a proxy tRNA-copy spectrum; true tAI or stAI weights and measured translation rows were not present in that coupling matrix.

\paragraph{Rank-admissibility rule.}
For each candidate pair $(q_i,T_j)$, the survival gate asks for a residualized statistic of the form
$$
\begin{aligned}
q_i^\perp &= q_i - \Pi_Z q_i,\\
T_j^\perp &= T_j - \Pi_Z T_j,\\
S^{QT}_{ij} &> 0
\end{aligned}
$$
after the declared controls and after a held-out or otherwise non-saturated check.  A positive coupling $A^{QT}_{ij}$, a modeled adaptation-weight contact, or a perfect fitted surface on three organism rows is insufficient.  The admissibility certificate for an entry must name the residualized rank, the remaining degrees of freedom, the readout boundary used for evaluation, and the break condition that rejects the entry when rank is exhausted.  Under the finite evidence available here, the residual-basis computation supports a persistent unexplained residual pattern, and the proxy coupling computation supports bounded contact with a translation-related surface.  Neither computation alone supplies a rank-admissible measured $S^{QT}_{ij}$ entry.

\paragraph{Local boundary.}
The chapter therefore records a guarded cross-layer relation: $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinates can be organized against translation readout surfaces, and finite rows show persistent residual structure plus nonzero proxy coupling, but translation survival is withheld unless a separate organism- or CDS-matched measured translation contact supplies non-saturated rows under the declared controls.  No ribosome-occupancy realization, protein abundance, folded structure, physical admissibility, biological function, mechanism, system phenotype, or universal biological law follows from the present finite rows.  Those claims require their own layer-matched measurements and their own controlled statistics.
