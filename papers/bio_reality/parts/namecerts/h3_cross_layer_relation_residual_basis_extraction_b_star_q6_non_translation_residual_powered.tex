\subsection{Rank-admissible refusal of spurious B$^{\ast}_{Q6}$ translation survival}
\label{sec:biorality-q6-rank-admissible-translation-survival-gate}

\paragraph{Finite carrier.}
This record concerns a finite residualized design whose rows are organism-scoped coding-sequence or organism-readout observations and whose columns contain B$^{\ast}_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates, translation-facing readout surfaces, and controls $Z$.  The controls include amino-acid composition, organism identity, GC3, coding-sequence length, and an M-density baseline when that baseline is available.  The gate is deliberately placed before any protein, structure, function, phenotype, or mechanism interpretation.  Its object is only the proposed entry $S^{QT}_{ij}$, where $q_i$ is a residual synonymous coordinate and $T_j$ is a translation-facing readout.

\paragraph{Rank-admissibility rule.}
For each proposed pair $(q_i,T_j)$, let $\widetilde q_i$ and $\widetilde T_j$ denote the residuals after projection away from the declared control columns $Z$.  The entry is admissible only if the post-control row space has enough independent support to make an out-of-sample or held-out failure possible.  A saturated in-sample fit, including an all-$1.0$ fitted surface on a three-organism panel, is therefore not a survival result.  The finite decision rule is
$$
\begin{aligned}
S^{QT}_{ij}\text{ may be reported} &\Rightarrow \operatorname{rank}([Z,q_i,T_j]) > \operatorname{rank}(Z),\\
S^{QT}_{ij}\text{ may be reported} &\Rightarrow n-\operatorname{rank}([Z,q_i,T_j]) \text{ leaves a nonzero falsification margin},\\
\operatorname{rank}\text{-saturated surface} &\Rightarrow \text{coupling-only or needs-data status.}
\end{aligned}
$$
This is a gate on admissibility, not a claim that any given entry survives.  Positive proxy coupling, modeled tAI or stAI contact, and a perfect in-sample score are all insufficient unless the residualized design retains non-saturated degrees of freedom.

\paragraph{Computed residual panel.}
The residual-basis computation was run under experiment run \texttt{c1ef9b455187}.  Four organisms had computable local readout panels and two organisms were marked as data-incomplete.  The computable rows were \emph{Homo sapiens} with $n=16008$ joined rows and $6$ selected candidates, \emph{Danio rerio} with $n=14410$ and $1$ selected candidate, \emph{Escherichia coli} K12 MG1655 with $n=3415$ and $2$ selected candidates, and \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae} with $n=3046$ and $9$ selected candidates.  \emph{Gallus gallus} and \emph{Mycobacterium smegmatis} str. MC2 155 were not promoted to computed status; both remain \texttt{needs\_data}.

\paragraph{Residual persistence.}
The recursive extraction used the stated regularizer $\lambda=0.01$ with description-length charge $\log_2(1+n_{\mathrm{col}})$.  None of the four computed organisms crossed into a compressible class.  The reported classification was persistent for all four computed organisms, with no compressible and no partially-compressible case.  The final residual floor was
$$
\begin{aligned}
\min \rho_{\mathrm{final}} &= 0.9723341658092314,\\
\#\{\text{computed organisms}\} &= 4,\\
\#\{\text{persistent computed organisms}\} &= 4.
\end{aligned}
$$
The organism-level residual curves were concrete rather than symbolic: \emph{Homo sapiens} had $\rho=(1.0,0.9852198225375993,0.9806696939851618,0.9792537895870558,0.9787609432955213,0.9787233550315274,0.9723341658092314)$; \emph{Danio rerio} had $\rho=(1.0,0.9791828971490305)$; \emph{E. coli} K12 MG1655 had $\rho=(1.0,0.9982293045445091,0.9979478314995476)$; and \emph{S. cerevisiae} had $\rho=(1.0,0.9962498989663637,0.9929843170647626,0.9915730985485388,0.9903314906684438,0.9894054695002641,0.9885902293718664,0.9882386501212531,0.9881524582851271,0.9798667977250413)$.  These numbers support a negative residual statement: the chosen readout blocks did not absorb the B$^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual energy below the declared persistence floor.

\paragraph{Degrees-of-freedom matched readout checks.}
The per-step null comparison gives finite evidence about descriptive readout absorption, not about translation survival.  In \emph{Danio rerio}, the selected measured tRNA/tAI step had increment $0.02081710285096942$ against null $p95=0.0002306374780841125$.  In \emph{E. coli}, the first mRNA step had increment $0.0017706954554909888$ against $0.0013371669456573104$, while the measured-TE step had increment $0.0002819723320884449$ below $0.0010272364744326006$.  In \emph{Homo sapiens}, PTM density, complex membership, transmembrane count, measured tRNA/tAI, and localization exceeded their matched nulls at steps $1$, $2$, $3$, $4$, and $6$, respectively, whereas domain count at step $5$ had increment $3.840392718095609\times 10^{-5}$ below $0.00021932006910265595$.  In \emph{S. cerevisiae}, measured TE, PTM density, transmembrane count, turnover, and localization exceeded their matched nulls, while complex membership, measured tRNA/tAI, domain count, and mRNA did not.  These finite rows show that several readout blocks can describe residual variance, but they do not by themselves satisfy the rank-admissible $S^{QT}_{ij}$ survival condition.

\paragraph{Proxy coupling is not survival.}
A separate three-organism coupling computation under experiment run \texttt{4a783972e74b} constructed nine B$^{\ast}_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates: Arg\_AGR, Ile\_AUA, K\_AAA, Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR, Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG, Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG, Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY, Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY, and f3\_stress.  The codon-count space had $61$ codons, the loaded organisms were \emph{Homo sapiens}, \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}, and \emph{E. coli} K12, and the proxy readout was a tRNA-Leu CUN-versus-UUR log-copy spectrum.  The coupling threshold was $0.01$; all nine entries were nonzero above threshold and the maximum absolute proxy coupling was $1882464.1106377328$.  The recorded break condition for that proxy matrix was not met: it would have returned null if all nine $q_i$ had $|A_{ij}|<0.01$, but at least one entry was above threshold.

\paragraph{Why the proxy matrix still fails the survival gate.}
The coupling matrix establishes a bounded contact between residual codon coordinates and a tRNA-copy proxy.  It does not establish a rank-admissible translation-survival entry.  The reasons are finite and local: the panel has only three organisms; the readout is a copy-number or modeled-adaptation surface rather than a matched measured ribosome or translation-efficiency row; and the matrix is evaluated as proxy coupling $A_{ij}$ rather than a held-out, control-residualized $S^{QT}_{ij}$.  Thus the correct reading is
$$
\begin{aligned}
\max |A_{ij}| &= 1882464.1106377328,\\
\#\{i: |A_{ij}|>0.01\} &= 9,\\
A_{ij}\neq 0 &
Rightarrow S^{QT}_{ij}>0.
\end{aligned}
$$
The last implication is rejected because a coupling-only statistic can be large even when the corresponding survival entry is not testable under the residualized rank gate.

\paragraph{Local conclusion.}
The chapter records a rank-admissibility boundary for B$^{\ast}_{Q6}$ translation-facing claims.  The residual extraction shows persistent unexplained residual energy in all four computed organisms, with final $\rho$ values at or above $0.9723341658092314$.  The three-organism coupling matrix shows nonzero proxy contact for nine synonymous residual coordinates, but it remains below the evidentiary threshold for translation survival.  A proposed $S^{QT}_{ij}$ entry may be discussed only after organism- or CDS-matched translation rows, the declared controls $Z$, a non-saturated residualized rank profile, and a held-out falsification margin are present in the same finite design.  Without those ingredients, the entry is coupling-only or needs-data.

\paragraph{Local no-promotion boundary.}
No translation realization, ribosome occupancy, protein abundance, folded structure, physical admissibility, biological function, mechanism, phenotype, or universal biological rule follows from the residual geometry, from the proxy $A_{ij}$ matrix, from modeled tAI or stAI weights, or from the persistence labels above.  Each such claim requires its own layer-matched measurement and its own controlled statistic.  This section therefore authorizes only the finite rank gate and the associated refusal of saturated or underpowered survival entries.
