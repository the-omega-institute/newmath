\subsection{Rank-admissible translation-survival refusal for $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinates}

\paragraph{Finite carrier.}
The object of this record is not a protein-level claim.  It is a finite residualized design problem whose rows are organism-indexed readout surfaces and whose columns include $B^*_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates together with control variables $Z$.  The intended entry $S^{QT}_{ij}$ asks whether a residual coordinate $q_i$ survives as a translation-readout predictor for a readout $T_j$ after residualizing both sides against the declared controls.  The admissibility test is prior to interpretation: if the post-control row space is saturated, underpowered, or incapable of held-out falsification, the entry is not promoted to translation survival even when an in-sample fit is positive or exactly $1.0$.

The finite run computed residual-basis extraction for four organisms and returned needs-data labels for two organisms.  The computed rows were Homo sapiens with $n=16008$ joined common rows and $6$ selected candidates, Saccharomyces cerevisiae with $n=3046$ and $9$ candidates, Escherichia coli K12 MG1655 with $n=3415$ and $2$ candidates, and Danio rerio with $n=14410$ and $1$ candidate.  Gallus gallus and Mycobacterium smegmatis str. MC2 155 had no joined finite-row statistics in this packet and therefore remain in the needs-data state.  The regularized greedy extraction used the stated penalty scale $\lambda=0.01$ with description-length term $\log_2(1+n_{\mathrm{col}})$, so the selected readout blocks are descriptive absorbers of residual variation rather than mechanistic explanations.

\paragraph{Coupling versus survival.}
A separate finite coupling computation loaded cross-organism rows for Homo sapiens, Saccharomyces cerevisiae, and Escherichia coli K12 and computed nine synonymous residual coordinates: Arg AGR, Ile AUA, K AAA, Leu CUN versus UUR, Leu UUA versus UUG, Ser UCA versus UCG, Ser UCR versus AGY, Thr ACR versus ACY, and $f_3$ stress.  Against a proxy tRNA-Leu CUN-versus-UUR log-copy spectrum, all nine entries were nonzero above the $0.01$ coupling threshold, with maximum absolute coupling $1882464.110638$.  The break condition for absence of proxy coupling was therefore not met.  This is still only an $A^{QT}$-type contact: it is a proxy codon-count and tRNA-copy relation, not a held-out, control-residualized, rank-admissible translation-survival matrix.

\paragraph{Rank gate.}
The local rule is the following.  Let $q_i^{\perp Z}$ and $T_j^{\perp Z}$ denote the two residualized vectors after amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, length, and M-density controls, with any simpler codon baseline included when the packet declares it.  A proposed survival entry is eligible only if the remaining design has enough independent rows to leave a non-saturated test surface:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\operatorname{rank}(X_Z, q_i, T_j) &< n_{\mathrm{effective}},\\
\operatorname{df}_{\mathrm{heldout}}(q_i,T_j\mid Z) &> 0.
\end{aligned}
$$
When these inequalities fail, an in-sample score, a perfect fitted surface, or a positive proxy coupling is recorded as a rank-saturated projection artifact rather than translation survival.  The point of the gate is negative as much as positive: a row-space geometry that cannot be falsified must return needs-data or coupling-only status.

\paragraph{Finite conclusion.}
This record is ready as a bounded BioReality chapter because it has finite organism rows, explicit residual curves, candidate counts, null comparisons, and a separate proxy-coupling contact.  Its conclusion is a refusal rule, not a biological promotion.  The computed residual extraction shows persistent unexplained residual structure in all four computed organisms, and the proxy coupling matrix shows nonzero codon/tRNA-copy contact in a three-organism panel.  Neither result supplies a measured rank-admissible $S^{QT}_{ij}>0$ entry.  Translation realization requires an organism- or CDS-matched translation readout with non-saturated held-out support after the stated controls.  Folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, mechanism, phenotype, protein abundance, ribosome occupancy, and universal biological-law claims require their own layer-matched measurements and statistics; none follows from the residual geometry, the proxy tAI or stAI route, the tRNA-copy spectrum, or the rank notation used here.
