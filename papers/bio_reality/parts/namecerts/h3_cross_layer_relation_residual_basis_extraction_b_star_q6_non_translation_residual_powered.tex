\subsection{Rank-admissible refusal for $B^*_{Q6}$ translation-survival entries}
\label{sec:biorreality-q6-rank-admissible-translation-survival-gate}

\paragraph{Finite carrier.}
The record concerns a finite residualized design over $B^*_{Q6}$ synonymous coordinates, organism-indexed translation-readout surfaces, and control columns $Z$.  The intended entry is not an unconstrained association score.  For each proposed coordinate--readout pair $(q_i,T_j)$, the admissible object is the post-control surface obtained after residualizing both $q_i$ and $T_j$ against amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, coding-sequence length, and the $M$-density baseline.  The finite-row gate asks whether the remaining row space has enough independent support to make a held-out survival statement falsifiable.  If the residualized design is rank-saturated, or if the degrees of freedom are too small after $Z$, then an in-sample fitted score, including an apparent value of $1.0$, is treated as a projection artifact rather than as translation survival.

\paragraph{Proxy coupling is not survival.}
A related coupling run, experiment $4a783972e74b$, computed nine $B^*_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates: $\mathrm{Arg\_AGR}$, $\mathrm{Ile\_AUA}$, $\mathrm{K\_AAA}$, $\mathrm{Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR}$, $\mathrm{Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG}$, $\mathrm{Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG}$, $\mathrm{Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY}$, $\mathrm{Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY}$, and $f_3$ stress.  That run used three organisms, $\textit{Homo sapiens}$, $\textit{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}$, and $\textit{Escherichia coli}$ K-12, in the $61$-codon count space and compared the residual coordinates with a tRNA-Leu CUN-versus-UUR log-copy proxy readout.  The break condition for the proxy matrix was not met: the maximum absolute coupling was $1882464.1106377328$, with nine entries above the threshold $0.01$.  This is a contact with a modeled or copy-number readout surface, not a survival certificate.  The large magnitude of $A_{ij}$ only says that the proxy contact is nonzero under the chosen finite construction; it does not by itself provide a rank-admissible, held-out $S^{QT}_{ij}$ entry.

\paragraph{Rank-admissibility rule.}
The gate can be written as a local refusal principle.  Let $\widetilde q_i$ and $\widetilde T_j$ denote the residuals after projecting away $Z$, and let $X_{ij}$ be the finite design used to estimate the proposed $S^{QT}_{ij}$ entry.  A positive entry is reportable only when the residual row space leaves non-saturated rank and enough independent rows for held-out testing:
$$
\begin{aligned}
S^{QT}_{ij}>0\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
&\operatorname{rank}(X_{ij})<n_{ij}\;\text{after controls},\\
&\mathrm{df}_{ij}>0\;\text{after controls and baseline ablation},\\
&\text{the held-out translation readout improves beyond the agreed null boundary}.
\end{aligned}
$$
If any of these clauses fails, the correct readback is $\mathrm{needs\_data}$ or coupling-only.  This rule is especially important for small panels: an all-$1.0$ fitted surface on a three-organism residualized design is a warning sign for rank saturation, not evidence that translation survival has been observed.

\paragraph{Local exclusion rule.}
The present finite-row record stops at a rank-audited translation-readout boundary.  It does not assert ribosome occupancy, protein abundance, folded structure, physical admissibility, molecular function, organismal phenotype, mechanism, or a universal biological law.  Modeled tAI or stAI weights, tRNA gene-copy summaries, codon-usage tables, and nonzero proxy coupling can help define bounded readout contacts, but none of them replaces a same-organism or same-CDS measured translation panel with the declared controls.  A downstream protein, structure, localization, stability, function, phenotype, or mechanistic claim would require its own matched contact and its own statistic after the translation step has survived this rank-admissibility gate.

\paragraph{Readback.}
The finite conclusion is therefore a refusal with useful positive structure.  The residual-basis computation is real and numerically specified; four organisms were computed, all four retained persistent residuals, and several selected readout blocks exceeded their degree-of-freedom-matched null thresholds.  The proxy coupling matrix is also nonzero.  However, those facts do not license translation-survival promotion for any $S^{QT}_{ij}$ entry unless the post-control design is non-saturated and held-out survival remains positive.  The correct chapter-level status is a rank-admissible boundary: report the residual coordinates, the row counts, the $\rho$ curves, the selected-block null comparisons, and the proxy coupling as finite contacts, while refusing survival whenever the rank or row support makes the proposed entry unfalsifiable.
