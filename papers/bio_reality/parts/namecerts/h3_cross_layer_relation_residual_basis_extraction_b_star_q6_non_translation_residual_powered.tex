\subsection{Rank-admissible translation-survival gate for $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residuals}\label{sec:bioreality-q6-rank-admissible-translation-survival-gate}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite object.}
This record treats a proposed $S^{QT}_{ij}$ entry as admissible only after the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ coordinate $q_i$ and the translation readout $T_j$ have both been residualized against the declared control surface $Z$.  The control surface includes amino-acid composition, organism identity, GC3, coding length, and an $M$-density baseline, with simpler codon baselines used as additional ablations where the row set supports them.  The finite carrier is therefore not a biological mechanism claim; it is a rank-audited residual design matrix whose output is a decision about whether a translation-readout entry is statistically testable.  If the post-control row space is saturated, or if the held-out degrees of freedom are too small to make failure observable, the entry is retained as coupling-only or needs more rows.

\paragraph{Observed residual panel.}
The finite run computed residual-basis extraction for four organisms and left two organisms outside the computed scope.  The computed organisms were $\mathit{Danio\ rerio}$ with $n=14410$ joined rows and one candidate block, $\mathit{Escherichia\ coli}$ K-12 MG1655 with $n=3415$ joined rows and two candidate blocks, $\mathit{Homo\ sapiens}$ with $n=16008$ joined rows and six candidate blocks, and $\mathit{Saccharomyces\ cerevisiae}$ with $n=3046$ joined rows and nine candidate blocks.  $\mathit{Gallus\ gallus}$ and $\mathit{Mycobacterium\ smegmatis}$ str. MC2 155 were marked needs-data, with no joined row count, no modeled distance, and no residual curve.  The recursive extraction used the stated regularization $\lambda=0.01$ with description-length penalty $\log_2(1+n_{\mathrm{col}})$.

\paragraph{Degree-of-freedom checks.}
The deterministic null comparisons separate selected readout contacts from admissible survival entries.  $\mathit{Danio\ rerio}$ selected measured-trna-tai at step $1$ with increment $0.02081710285096942$ against null $p95=0.0002306374780841125$.  $\mathit{E.\ coli}$ selected mRNA at step $1$ with increment $0.0017706954554909888$ against null $p95=0.0013371669456573104$, but its measured-te step had increment $0.0002819723320884449$ below null $p95=0.0010272364744326006$.  Human selected ptm-density, complex-member, tm-count, measured-trna-tai, domain-count, and localization; the domain-count step was below null, with increment $3.840392718095609\times 10^{-5}$ against null $p95=0.00021932006910265595$, while the localization step was above null, with increment $0.006528084968494762$ against null $p95=0.0011598998794309233$.  Yeast selected measured-te, ptm-density, tm-count, turnover, complex-member, measured-trna-tai, domain-count, mRNA, and localization; steps $5$ through $8$ were below their null thresholds, while localization at step $9$ had increment $0.008385002223710514$ against null $p95=0.00708487840939486$.

\paragraph{Translation-readout refusal.}
A separate proxy coupling computation over three organisms loaded human, yeast, and $\mathit{E.\ coli}$ K-12 codon data and computed nine $B^{*}_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates: Arg AGR, Ile AUA, K AAA, Leu CUN versus UUR, Leu UUA versus UUG, Ser UCA versus UCG, Ser UCR versus AGY, Thr ACR versus ACY, and $f_3$ stress.  The proxy readout was a tRNA-Leu CUN-versus-UUR log-copy spectrum, and the coupling matrix had nine nonzero entries with maximum absolute value $1882464.1106377328$ above the threshold $0.01$.  This prevents the trivial all-small null for the proxy contact, but it does not certify $S^{QT}_{ij}>0$.  A coupling entry $A^{QT}_{ij}$, a modeled tAI or stAI weight, or an all-$1.0$ in-sample fit is not a survival entry when rank saturation or insufficient held-out support prevents falsification.

\paragraph{Local verdict.}
The chapter therefore records a rank-admissibility gate rather than a positive translation-survival theorem.  A proposed $S^{QT}_{ij}$ may be reported only when the residualized design has non-saturated rank, enough held-out support, and a positive controlled statistic after the declared baseline ablations.  The finite evidence here supports the need for that gate: the residual curves remain persistent across the computed organisms, several selected readout blocks fail their matched null checks, and the available three-organism proxy coupling is explicitly weaker than a measured translation-survival surface.  No statement about ribosome occupancy, protein abundance, folding, physical admissibility, biological function, mechanism, phenotype, or universality follows from these rows unless a separate layer-matched contact supplies the corresponding measurement and the controlled statistic is recomputed there.
