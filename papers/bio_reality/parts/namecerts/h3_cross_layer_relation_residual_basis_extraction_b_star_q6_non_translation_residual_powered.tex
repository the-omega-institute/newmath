\subsection{Rank-admissible translation-survival gate for $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinates}\label{sec:bior reality-q6-rank-admissible-translation-survival-gate}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite-row question.}
This record isolates the point at which a $B^*_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinate may be tested against a translation readout without being promoted by a saturated fit.  The carrier is a finite residualized design: rows are organism-scoped coding-sequence or organism summaries when available, columns contain the residual coordinates $q_i$, the proposed translation readout surface $T_j$, and controls $Z$ for amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, length, and the M-density baseline.  The gate is not a claim that translation has occurred.  It asks whether the residualized matrix has enough rank left after $Z$ to make a held-out survival test falsifiable.

The admissibility rule is local to each proposed entry $S^{QT}_{ij}$.  Let $\widetilde q_i$ and $\widetilde T_j$ denote the residuals after projecting away the declared controls.  A proposed survival entry is admissible only when the remaining row space is not saturated and the out-of-sample comparison can fail:
$$
\begin{aligned}
S^{QT}_{ij} \text{ may be reported} &\Longrightarrow \operatorname{rank}(\widetilde X_{ij}) < n_{\mathrm{rows}} \text{ with enough held-out degrees of freedom},\\
S^{QT}_{ij} \text{ is refused} &\Longleftarrow \operatorname{rank}(\widetilde X_{ij}) \text{ saturates the post-control rows or leaves the test underpowered}.
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus an in-sample value equal to $1.0$, or a positive coupling score before the held-out test, is treated as a warning sign rather than as survival evidence when the row space is exhausted.

\paragraph{Regularized extraction and null comparison.}
The extraction used the stated regularizer $\lambda=0.01$ with description-length penalty $DL=\log_2(1+n_{\mathrm{col}})$.  For each organism with sufficient local readouts, the procedure began from $R_0=P_{Q\perp T_{\mathrm{mod}}}$, greedily selected readout blocks $H_k$, recorded the residual ratio $\rho_k$, and compared each selected increment with a deterministic Gaussian null matched to the degrees of freedom.  The supplied finite rows report null $95\%$ cutoffs rather than nominal $p$-values.

For \emph{Danio rerio}, the single selected block was \texttt{measured\_trna\_tai}; its increment was $0.02081710285096942$ against null $p95=0.0002306374780841125$, so the step exceeded the null.  For \emph{E. coli}, step $1$ selected \texttt{mrna} with increment $0.0017706954554909888$ over null $p95=0.0013371669456573104$, while step $2$ selected \texttt{measured\_te} with increment $0.0002819723320884449$ below null $p95=0.0010272364744326006$.  For \emph{H. sapiens}, the selected blocks were \texttt{ptm\_density}, \texttt{complex\_member}, \texttt{tm\_count}, \texttt{measured\_trna\_tai}, \texttt{domain\_count}, and \texttt{localization}; five of the six steps exceeded their null cutoffs, while \texttt{domain\_count} had increment $3.840392718095609\times 10^{-5}$ below null $p95=0.00021932006910265595$.  For \emph{S. cerevisiae}, the nine selected blocks were \texttt{measured\_te}, \texttt{ptm\_density}, \texttt{tm\_count}, \texttt{turnover}, \texttt{complex\_member}, \texttt{measured\_trna\_tai}, \texttt{domain\_count}, \texttt{mrna}, and \texttt{localization}; steps $1$, $2$, $3$, $4$, and $9$ exceeded null, while steps $5$ through $8$ did not.

These finite-row comparisons show that some readout blocks can reduce residual energy above a degree-of-freedom matched null.  They do not by themselves establish $S^{QT}_{ij}>0$.  The rank-admissible gate requires a separate per-entry survival surface after the declared controls, with enough remaining rows for a held-out failure condition.

\paragraph{Coupling matrix is not survival.}
A related finite coupling run used a three-organism panel, with cross-organism data loaded for \emph{Homo sapiens}, \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}, and \emph{Escherichia coli} K12.  It computed nine synonymous residual coordinates:
$$
\begin{aligned}
&\texttt{Arg\_AGR},\ \texttt{Ile\_AUA},\ \texttt{K\_AAA},\ \texttt{Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR},\ \texttt{Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG},\\
&\texttt{Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG},\ \texttt{Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY},\ \texttt{Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY},\ \texttt{f3\_stress}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The codon count space had size $61$.  Against a proxy tRNA-Leu CUN-versus-UUR log-copy spectrum, all nine entries were nonzero above the coupling threshold $0.01$, with maximum absolute coupling $1882464.1106377328$ and break condition \texttt{false}.  This supports the presence of a finite coupling contact $A^{QT}$ under a proxy readout, but the readout was not a direct measured ribosome or translation-efficiency matrix.  The record itself states that true survival requires ribo-seq, stAI, or protein-abundance readouts under controls, and that this codon-count and tRNA-copy proxy does not assert ribosome-mediated translation realization.

The rank gate therefore separates two facts.  A large or nonzero $A^{QT}_{ij}$ can motivate a testable entry, but it is not a survival certificate.  A proposed $S^{QT}_{ij}$ entry must still pass residualized rank, post-control degrees of freedom, and held-out readout checks.  If the design is rank-saturated, an all-$1.0$ fitted surface is classified as projection artifact rather than biological survival.

\paragraph{Local no-promotion boundary.}
This chapter stops at the translation-readout boundary.  The finite rows allow the following statements: $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinates were computed for four organisms, two organisms currently need data, the computed residual traces remain persistent above the $0.97$ floor, selected readout blocks were compared with degree-of-freedom matched null cutoffs, and a proxy coupling matrix had nine nonzero entries above $0.01$.  They do not establish translation realization, ribosome occupancy, protein abundance, folding, structural order, physical admissibility, biological function, mechanism, phenotype, or a universal biological law.

The admissible conclusion is a refusal rule for $S^{QT}_{ij}$ entries.  Report survival only when the post-control residual design has non-saturated rank, enough independent support, and a held-out statistic that can fail.  Otherwise the entry remains coupling-only or data-limited, even if the in-sample score is positive, the proxy coupling is large, or the fitted surface appears perfect.
