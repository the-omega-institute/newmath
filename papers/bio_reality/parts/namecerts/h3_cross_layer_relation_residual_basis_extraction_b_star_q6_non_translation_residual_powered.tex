\subsection{Rank-admissible refusal for $B^\ast_{Q6}$ translation survival}
\label{sec:biorality-q6-rank-admissible-translation-survival-gate}

\paragraph{Finite object.}
This record treats $B^\ast_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates as a finite cross-layer testing object, not as a biological conclusion.  The carrier is a residualized design matrix whose rows are organism-indexed coding-sequence or organism-level readout surfaces, whose distinguished columns are $B^\ast_{Q6}$ coordinates, and whose control block $Z$ contains the declared non-translation covariates: amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, length, and the M-density baseline.  A proposed translation-survival entry $S^{QT}_{ij}$ is admissible only after both the coordinate $q_i$ and the translation readout $T_j$ have been projected away from $Z$ and the remaining row space still leaves a falsifiable, non-saturated comparison.  The local decision is therefore not the fitted score alone.  It is the tuple consisting of residual rank, post-control degrees of freedom, the held-out translation boundary, and a refusal rule for saturated designs.

\paragraph{Rank gate.}
For a candidate pair $(q_i,T_j)$, let $Q_i^\perp$ and $T_j^\perp$ denote the residualized coordinate and translation readout after the control block $Z$ has been removed.  The admissibility condition used here can be summarized as
$$
\begin{aligned}
Q_i^\perp &= (I-P_Z)q_i,\\
T_j^\perp &= (I-P_Z)T_j,\\
S^{QT}_{ij} &\text{ is reportable only if } \operatorname{rank}(Z,Q_i,T_j) \text{ leaves nonzero held-out degrees of freedom.}
\end{aligned}
$$
If this condition fails, the entry is not promoted to translation survival, even when an in-sample surface is positive or has a fitted value equal to $1.0$.  Such a case is classified as coupling-only, rank-saturated, or needs data, depending on which finite obstruction is present.  The break condition is thus semantic as well as numerical: a rank-saturated projection cannot be used to claim $S^{QT}_{ij}>0$.

\paragraph{Observed residual-basis computation.}
The finite-row computation recorded under experiment run \texttt{c1ef9b455187} computed the recursive residual-basis extraction for four organisms and returned needs-data labels for two.  The regularizer was stated as $\lambda=0.01$ with description-length penalty $DL=\log_2(1+n_{col})$.  The computed rows were $n=16008$ for \textit{Homo sapiens}, $n=3046$ for \textit{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}, $n=3415$ for \textit{Escherichia coli} K-12 MG1655, and $n=14410$ for \textit{Danio rerio}.  The two non-computed organisms were \textit{Gallus gallus} and \textit{Mycobacterium smegmatis} str. MC2 155, both labelled needs data.

The four computed organisms were all classified as persistent rather than compressible.  The final residual floors were high: \textit{Homo sapiens} ended at $0.9723341658092314$ after six selected readout blocks; \textit{S. cerevisiae} ended at $0.9798667977250413$ after nine blocks; \textit{E. coli} ended at $0.9979478314995476$ after two blocks; and \textit{D. rerio} ended at $0.9791828971490305$ after one block.  The aggregate negative result is that no computed organism entered the compressible or partially compressible class, with a reported residual floor threshold of $0.97$ and a minimum final residual $0.9723341658092314$.

\paragraph{Per-organism readout increments.}
The degrees-of-freedom matched null comparison gives the finite shape of the residual-basis evidence.  In \textit{D. rerio}, the single selected block was measured tRNA/tAI with increment $0.02081710285096942$ against null $95\%$ cutoff $0.0002306374780841125$.  In \textit{E. coli}, the first selected block was mRNA with increment $0.0017706954554909888$ above cutoff $0.0013371669456573104$, while the measured-TE step had increment $0.0002819723320884449$ below cutoff $0.0010272364744326006$.  In \textit{H. sapiens}, the above-cutoff selected blocks were PTM density, complex membership, transmembrane count, measured tRNA/tAI, and localization, with increments $0.014780177462400674$, $0.004618389163869967$, $0.0014438137599134737$, $0.0005032876020244136$, and $0.006528084968494762$ respectively; the domain-count step was below its cutoff, with increment $0.00003840392718095609$ against $0.00021932006910265595$.  In \textit{S. cerevisiae}, measured TE, PTM density, transmembrane count, turnover, and localization were above their matched null cutoffs, while complex membership, measured tRNA/tAI, domain count, and mRNA were below their corresponding cutoffs.

\paragraph{Coupling is not survival.}
A separate proxy coupling computation under experiment run \texttt{4a783972e74b} loaded three organisms: \textit{Homo sapiens}, \textit{S. cerevisiae}, and \textit{E. coli} K-12.  It computed nine synonymous residual coordinates, namely Arg\_AGR, Ile\_AUA, K\_AAA, Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR, Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG, Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG, Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY, Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY, and $f_3$ stress.  Against the tRNA-Leu CUN-versus-UUR log-copy proxy, at least one coupling entry exceeded the $0.01$ threshold, with nine nonzero entries and maximum absolute value $1882464.1106377328$.  This is a contact with a modeled or proxy translation-adjacent surface, not a positive $S^{QT}$ result.  The same finite memory explicitly records that true survival requires ribosome-seq, stAI/tAI, or protein-abundance readouts under controls, and that the proxy contact does not assert ribosome-mediated translation realization.

\paragraph{Local verdict.}
The chapter-specific conclusion is a refusal boundary.  The residual-basis computation is real finite-row evidence that $B^\ast_{Q6}$ residual structure remains persistent after the tested readout blocks, and the proxy coupling matrix shows nonzero contact with a bounded translation-adjacent readout.  Neither fact is sufficient to report a rank-admissible translation-survival entry.  A proposed $S^{QT}_{ij}$ must still be supported by organism-matched or CDS-matched translation rows, the declared control block $Z$, a residualized rank audit, and a non-saturated held-out statistic.  Until those conditions are met, a positive fitted value, a proxy coupling value, or a residual-basis selected block remains below the translation-survival line.

\paragraph{Local cannot-claim boundary.}
This record does not claim translation realization, ribosome occupancy, protein abundance, folding, physical admissibility, biological function, mechanism, phenotype, causation, or a universal biological law.  It also does not interpret the sign of any coupling entry as universal directionality.  The only supported content is the finite audit shape: persistent $B^\ast_{Q6}$ residuals in the computed organisms, needs-data status in two organisms, explicit null comparisons for selected readout blocks, and a rank-admissibility rule that blocks translation-survival promotion when the residualized design is saturated or underpowered.
