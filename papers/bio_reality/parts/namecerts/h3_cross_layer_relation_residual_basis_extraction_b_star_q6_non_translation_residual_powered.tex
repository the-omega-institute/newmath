\subsection{Rank-admissible translation-survival gate for $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates}
\label{sec:bioreality-q6-rank-admissible-translation-survival-gate}

\paragraph{Finite object.}
This record concerns a finite residualized design over $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ synonymous coordinates, organism-indexed translation readout surfaces, and control columns $Z$.  The proposed entry $S^{QT}_{ij}$ is not allowed to mean ``translation survival'' merely because a fitted score is positive, because a proxy coupling matrix has a large coefficient, or because a controlled regression surface is perfectly fit on too few rows.  The admissible object is narrower: after both the coordinate $q_i$ and the translation readout $T_j$ are residualized against $Z$, the remaining row space must have enough independent support to make a held-out failure possible.  The gate therefore distinguishes three cases: a rank-admissible survival entry, a saturated projection artifact, and a coupling-only contact at the translation-readout boundary.

\paragraph{Controls and entry rule.}
The local control set is the declared biological baseline: amino-acid composition, organism identity, GC3, coding length, and the $M$-density baseline, with matched mRNA or simpler codon baselines added when the readout requires them.  A proposed survival entry is only readable as $S^{QT}_{ij}>0$ when the residual design has non-saturated rank and enough degrees of freedom after $Z$ to support out-of-sample falsification.  In schematic form the rule is
$$
\begin{aligned}
q_i^{\perp Z} &= q_i - \Pi_Z q_i,\\
T_j^{\perp Z} &= T_j - \Pi_Z T_j,\\
S^{QT}_{ij} &> 0 \quad \text{is admissible only if } \operatorname{rank}(q_i^{\perp Z},T_j^{\perp Z}) \text{ is not saturated and the held-out test is non-vacuous.}
\end{aligned}
$$
If the residual rank is saturated, if the remaining degrees of freedom are too small, or if all support is consumed by the controls, the entry is retained as coupling-only or marked as needing data.  This is a refusal to promote a rank artifact, not a negative biological law.

\paragraph{Observed finite rows.}
The residual-basis computation ran on four organism panels with sufficient local rows and left two named panels without enough joined data.  The computed panels were \emph{Danio rerio} with $n=14410$ joined rows and one candidate readout, \emph{Escherichia coli} K12 MG1655 with $n=3415$ and two candidates, \emph{Homo sapiens} with $n=16008$ and six candidates, and \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae} with $n=3046$ and nine candidates.  \emph{Gallus gallus} and \emph{Mycobacterium smegmatis} str. MC2 155 remained in the needs-data state.  The extraction used the stated regularizer $\lambda=0.01$ with description-length cost $DL=\log_2(1+n_{\mathrm{col}})$, so selected readout blocks are descriptive compression terms rather than mechanism claims.

\paragraph{Residual persistence.}
All four computed organism panels were classified as persistent rather than compressible.  The recorded residual curves were $R=(1.0,0.9791828971490305)$ for \emph{Danio rerio}, $R=(1.0,0.9982293045445091,0.9979478314995476)$ for \emph{E. coli} K12 MG1655, $R=(1.0,0.9852198225375993,0.9806696939851618,0.9792537895870558,0.9787609432955213,0.9787233550315274,0.9723341658092314)$ for human, and $R=(1.0,0.9962498989663637,0.9929843170647626,0.9915730985485388,0.9903314906684438,0.9894054695002641,0.9885902293718664,0.9882386501212531,0.9881524582851271,0.9798667977250413)$ for yeast.  The minimum final residual over computed panels was $0.9723341658092314$ against a persistence floor of $0.97$, with zero panels labeled compressible and zero panels labeled partially compressible.  Thus the finite data support a negative readback: the available readout blocks do not absorb the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual energy into a stable translation-realization claim.

\paragraph{Degree-of-freedom matched null checks.}
The per-step null ledger gives the local basis contacts that exceeded or failed their deterministic Gaussian null.  In \emph{Danio rerio}, measured tRNA/tAI was selected at step $1$ with increment $0.02081710285096942$ above null $p95=0.0002306374780841125$.  In \emph{E. coli}, mRNA passed at step $1$ with increment $0.0017706954554909888$ above $0.0013371669456573104$, while measured TE failed at step $2$ with increment $0.0002819723320884449$ below $0.0010272364744326006$.  In human, ptm density, complex membership, transmembrane count, measured tRNA/tAI, and localization exceeded their nulls with increments $0.014780177462400674$, $0.004618389163869967$, $0.0014438137599134737$, $0.0005032876020244136$, and $0.006528084968494762$ respectively, while domain count failed with increment $3.840392718095609\times 10^{-5}$ below $0.00021932006910265595$.  In yeast, measured TE, ptm density, transmembrane count, turnover, and localization exceeded their nulls, with increments $0.003750101033636422$, $0.003277874261256318$, $0.0014211891285406705$, $0.0012521597065434749$, and $0.008385002223710514$ respectively; complex membership, measured tRNA/tAI, domain count, and mRNA did not exceed their local null thresholds.  These rows show finite readout contacts and failures, but they do not by themselves establish a rank-admissible $S^{QT}_{ij}$ survival entry.

\paragraph{Proxy coupling is not survival.}
A separate three-organism codon-count and tRNA-copy proxy matrix loaded \emph{Homo sapiens}, \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}, and \emph{E. coli} K12 over a $61$-codon count space.  Nine $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ synonymous residuals were computed: Arg AGR, Ile AUA, K AAA, Leu CUN versus UUR, Leu UUA versus UUG, Ser UCA versus UCG, Ser UCR versus AGY, Thr ACR versus ACY, and $f_3$ stress.  The proxy coupling break condition was not met: not all nine $q_i$ had $|A_{ij}|<0.01$, and the maximum absolute coupling was $1882464.1106377328$ with nine nonzero entries.  This is evidence of a bounded contact between residual codon geometry and a tRNA-Leu CUN-versus-UUR log-copy proxy.  It remains below the survival threshold because true tAI, stAI, ribosome-profiling, or translation-efficiency rows were not paired with the declared controls in a non-saturated held-out matrix.

\paragraph{Adjacent finite contacts.}
Other finite contacts show that the surrounding BioReality ledger contains genuine row-bearing tests, but they do not fill this rank-admissibility gap.  The branchpoint motif contact used $n=10584$ intron rows with expression control from GTEx-derived median tissue expression.  The uORF/Kozak translation-initiation contact reported $n=41862$ fixed rows and $n=16739$ varying rows with two-sample concordance.  The codon-composition mRNA half-life contact used $n=16500$ human rows and $n=18924$ mouse rows with raw $\rho=0.126224$ under a non-CSC tAI circularity guard.  These finite rows are relevant to neighboring cross-layer surfaces, but none is the same organism- or CDS-matched $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ translation-readout matrix required for $S^{QT}_{ij}$.

\paragraph{Local interpretation.}
The chapter therefore records a rank-admissibility gate rather than a positive translation-survival result.  The finite evidence says that residual $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ coordinates persist after the tested readout blocks, that some readout selections exceed degree-of-freedom matched null thresholds, and that a proxy tRNA-copy coupling exists.  It does not say that any $q_i$ survives as an observed translation-realization coordinate.  No claim about ribosome occupancy, protein abundance, folding, physical admissibility, biological function, causal mechanism, phenotype, or universal biological law follows from this packet unless a separate layer-matched contact supplies the corresponding rows and statistic.  A future positive entry must attach measured translation readouts, preserve the controls, report residual rank and held-out support, and reject the saturated all-fit case before using $S^{QT}_{ij}>0$ as a survival statement.
