This is a coupling observation, not a survival certificate.

\paragraph{Rank gate.}
For a proposed entry $S^{QT}_{ij}$, let $q_i$ be a residual coordinate and let $T_j$ be a bounded translation readout. The gate first projects both sides away from the control columns $Z$, where $Z$ contains amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, length, M-density, and any simpler codon baseline declared for the test. The admissible situation is not merely a positive fitted score. It requires a post-control row space with residual degrees of freedom left after the projection, so that held-out survival can fail. The local rule is:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\operatorname{admissible}(i,j)=1
\quad \Longrightarrow \quad
&\operatorname{rank}\big((I-P_Z)[q_i,T_j]\big)<n_Z,\\
&\operatorname{df}_{ij}>0,\\
&\text{the held-out translation boundary is separately evaluated.}
\end{aligned}
$$
If the rank is saturated, or if the remaining degrees of freedom do not permit a falsifiable held-out comparison, the entry is left at coupling-only or needs-data status. An in-sample fit equal to $1.0$, an all-perfect controlled surface from too few rows, or a modeled tAI contact is therefore refused as translation survival.

\paragraph{Readout boundary.}
The rank-admissibility rule is placed before any promotion from codon-topology residuals to translation-realization survival. The available finite evidence shows residualized coordinates, proxy coupling, and bounded adaptation-weight contacts, but it does not supply an organism- or CDS-matched held-out matrix proving any $S^{QT}_{ij}>0$ after all controls. The packet therefore supports a scoped refusal: proxy contact and residual persistence may guide the next measured translation-readout experiment, but they do not by themselves establish ribosome occupancy, protein abundance, folded structure, physical admissibility, biological function, mechanism, phenotype, or a universal biological law. Each such assertion would require its own layer-matched contact and its own statistic after the translation boundary has been passed.

\paragraph{Local verdict.}
The finite-row result is ready as a BioReality boundary chapter because it has concrete rows, concrete residual curves, a stated regularizer, named coordinate entries, a proxy coupling matrix, and degree-of-freedom matched null comparisons. Its mathematical content is not a positive survival claim. It is the stricter statement that $B^*_{Q6}$ translation-survival entries must be refused whenever post-control rank is saturated, underpowered, or evaluated only through modeled or proxy contacts. In the present record, the correct status of a proposed $S^{QT}$ entry is therefore coupling-only or needs-data until a non-saturated, held-out, organism-matched translation readout is attached and recomputed under the declared controls.
