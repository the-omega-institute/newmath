\subsection{Rank-admissible refusal before translation survival}
\label{sec:b-star-q6-rank-admissible-translation-survival-gate}

\paragraph{Object of the finite test.}
This record concerns a finite residualized design matrix whose rows are organism-indexed coding-sequence or readout rows and whose coordinate columns are the $B^*_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates.  The intended translation entry is not a direct biological conclusion.  It is a candidate scalar $S^{QT}_{ij}$ attached to a residual coordinate $q_i$ and a translation readout $T_j$ only after both have been residualized against the declared control block $Z$.  In this chapter the control block includes amino-acid composition, organism identity, $GC3$, coding-sequence length, and the $M$-density baseline.  The gate asks whether a proposed translation-survival entry is statistically testable after these controls, rather than whether a fitted in-sample surface can be made large.

\paragraph{Residual-rank rule.}
Let $Q_i^\perp$ and $T_j^\perp$ denote the residuals of $q_i$ and $T_j$ after projection away from $Z$.  A survival entry is admissible only when the residual design has nonzero remaining degrees of freedom and the fitted readout is not rank-saturated.  The refusal condition is therefore local to the entry:
$$
\begin{aligned}
S^{QT}_{ij} \hbox{ may be reported only if }&\quad \operatorname{rank}(Z,q_i,T_j) > \operatorname{rank}(Z),\\
&\quad n-\operatorname{rank}(Z,q_i,T_j) > 0,\\
&\quad \hbox{and a held-out or null-matched test can still falsify the entry.}
\end{aligned}
$$
If the controlled design is saturated, or if the remaining rows do not support a falsifiable held-out comparison, the entry is left as coupling-only or needs-data.  This is true even when an in-sample fitted score is positive, and especially when an all-$1.0$ fitted surface appears in a low-row panel.

\paragraph{Finite rows actually computed.}
The residual-basis extraction was computed for four organisms and marked needs-data for two.  The computed organisms were \texttt{danio\_rerio} with $n=14410$ joined rows and one candidate readout, \texttt{escherichia\_coli\_k12\_mg1655} with $n=3415$ and two candidates, \texttt{homo\_sapiens} with $n=16008$ and six candidates, and \texttt{saccharomyces\_cerevisiae} with $n=3046$ and nine candidates.  The two unavailable rows were \texttt{gallus\_gallus} and \texttt{mycobacterium\_smegmatis\_str\_mc2\_155}, both labelled needs-data because the joined common row set and candidate count were absent.

The recursive extraction used the stated penalty $\lambda=0.01$ with description-length term $DL=\log_2(1+n_{\mathrm{col}})$.  Its starting object was recorded as $R_0=P_{Q\perp T_{\mathrm{mod}}}$, and each selected readout block $H_k$ was evaluated by the residual ratio curve $\rho_k$.  The final residual ratios stayed high in every computed organism: $0.9791828971490305$ for \texttt{danio\_rerio}, $0.9979478314995476$ for \texttt{escherichia\_coli\_k12\_mg1655}, $0.9723341658092314$ for \texttt{homo\_sapiens}, and $0.9798667977250413$ for \texttt{saccharomyces\_cerevisiae}.  The recorded floor was $0.97$, and all four computed organisms were classified as persistent rather than compressible or partially compressible.  Thus the finite result is a negative boundary: the available readout blocks did not absorb the residual coordinate energy enough to justify a translation-realization promotion.

\paragraph{Null-matched step evidence.}
The degree-of-freedom matched null checks give more granular information about which selected blocks exceeded their local null and which did not.  In \texttt{danio\_rerio}, the single selected block was \texttt{measured\_trna\_tai}, with increment $0.02081710285096942$ against null $p95=0.0002306374780841125$.  In \texttt{escherichia\_coli\_k12\_mg1655}, \texttt{mrna} exceeded its null at step 1 with increment $0.0017706954554909888$ against $0.0013371669456573104$, but \texttt{measured\_te} failed at step 2 with increment $0.0002819723320884449$ against $0.0010272364744326006$.

For \texttt{homo\_sapiens}, the selected blocks \texttt{ptm\_density}, \texttt{complex\_member}, \texttt{tm\_count}, \texttt{measured\_trna\_tai}, and \texttt{localization} exceeded their matched nulls, while \texttt{domain\_count} did not.  The largest human increment was $0.014780177462400674$ for \texttt{ptm\_density}, and the smallest exceeded-null increment was $0.0005032876020244136$ for \texttt{measured\_trna\_tai}.  In \texttt{saccharomyces\_cerevisiae}, \texttt{measured\_te}, \texttt{ptm\_density}, \texttt{tm\_count}, \texttt{turnover}, and \texttt{localization} exceeded their nulls, while \texttt{complex\_member}, \texttt{measured\_trna\_tai}, \texttt{domain\_count}, and \texttt{mrna} did not.  These stepwise checks are useful readout contacts, but they are not yet the same object as a rank-admissible $S^{QT}_{ij}$ entry under the full survival gate.

\paragraph{Coupling is not survival.}
A separate coupling computation used a $61$-codon count space across \texttt{homo\_sapiens}, \texttt{saccharomyces\_cerevisiae}, and \texttt{escherichia\_coli\_k12}.  It computed nine synonymous residual coordinates: \texttt{Arg\_AGR}, \texttt{Ile\_AUA}, \texttt{K\_AAA}, \texttt{Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR}, \texttt{Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG}, \texttt{Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG}, \texttt{Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY}, \texttt{Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY}, and \texttt{f3\_stress}.  The proxy readout was the tRNA-Leu CUN-versus-UUR log-copy spectrum.  The coupling threshold was $0.01$, the maximum absolute entry was $1882464.1106377328$, and all nine entries were nonzero above the threshold, so the stated break condition that all nine $|A_{ij}|<0.01$ was not met.

This is still a coupling result, not a survival result.  The readout was a tRNA-copy proxy, not a matched ribosome, translation-efficiency, or protein-output surface under the full control set.  The three-organism scope is also too small to turn an all-fitted or nearly saturated controlled surface into a falsifiable survival statement.  The rank-admissible rule therefore separates $A^{QT}$ contact from $S^{QT}$ survival: nonzero proxy coupling can motivate a test, but it cannot itself certify that any $B^*_{Q6}$ coordinate survives into translation realization.

\paragraph{Local conclusion.}
The finite-row evidence supports a bounded refusal statement.  Residual extraction was successfully run in four organisms, and several selected readout blocks exceeded degree-of-freedom matched null thresholds.  Nevertheless, the persistent residual ratios, the needs-data organisms, the proxy nature of the tRNA-copy or tAI/stAI contacts, and the absence of a matched non-saturated held-out $S^{QT}_{ij}$ matrix prevent promotion to translation survival.  No claim about ribosome occupancy, protein abundance, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, mechanism, phenotype, or universality follows from these rows.  Each of those layers would require its own matched contact and its own controlled statistic after the translation-readout boundary has first been passed.
