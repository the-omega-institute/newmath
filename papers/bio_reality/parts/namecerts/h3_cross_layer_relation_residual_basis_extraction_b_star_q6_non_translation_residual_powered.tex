\paragraph{Finite carrier.}
The carrier in this record is a finite residualized design over $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ synonymous coordinates, organism-indexed translation readout surfaces, and control columns $Z$.  The intended entry is a putative $S^{QT}_{ij}$ relation from a residual coordinate $q_i$ to a translation readout $T_j$, but the admissible object is not the fitted score alone.  It is the whole certificate: residualized rank after the $Z$ controls, remaining degrees of freedom, a held-out translation-readout boundary, and the rule that rejects survival when the post-control design is saturated or too small to falsify.

For this packet the residual-basis computation was actually run on six named organism slots.  Four slots have finite computed rows and two return data absence.  The computed slots are $\mathrm{Danio\ rerio}$ with $n=14410$ joined rows and one candidate block, $\mathrm{Escherichia\ coli}$ K12 MG1655 with $n=3415$ joined rows and two candidate blocks, $\mathrm{Homo\ sapiens}$ with $n=16008$ joined rows and six candidate blocks, and $\mathrm{Saccharomyces\ cerevisiae}$ with $n=3046$ joined rows and nine candidate blocks. \paragraph{Coupling is not survival.}
A separate finite coupling run supplies the codon-side contact that motivates the gate.  In a $61$-codon count space, nine $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates were computed: $\mathrm{Arg\_AGR}$, $\mathrm{Ile\_AUA}$, $K\_\mathrm{AAA}$, $\mathrm{Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR}$, $\mathrm{Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG}$, $\mathrm{Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG}$, $\mathrm{Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY}$, $\mathrm{Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY}$, and $f_3$-stress.  The three-organism contact loads human, yeast, and $\mathrm{Ecoli}$ K12, using a tRNA-Leu CUN-versus-UUR log-copy proxy as the readout.  The coupling threshold is $0.01$; all nine entries are nonzero above this threshold, with maximum absolute coupling $1882464.1106377328$, so the coupling break condition, namely all nine $|A_{ij}|<0.01$, is not met.  This is only an $A^{QT}$ contact against a proxy readout.  It does not supply a held-out, non-saturated $S^{QT}$ estimate.

\paragraph{Rank gate.}
The chapter-level decision is therefore a rank-admissibility refusal rather than a positive translation-realization claim.  A proposed $S^{QT}_{ij}$ entry may be read as survival only if both residual vectors are formed after the declared controls $Z$ and if the remaining row space has rank below saturation with enough independent rows to make held-out loss meaningful.  In symbols, the admissible test has the form
$$
\begin{aligned}
q_i^{\perp Z}&=(I-P_Z)q_i,\\
T_j^{\perp Z}&=(I-P_Z)T_j,\\
\mathrm{admissible}(i,j)&\Longrightarrow \mathrm{rank}(X^{\perp Z}_{ij})<n_{ij}\ \text{and held-out survival is falsifiable},\\
\mathrm{saturated}(i,j)&\Longrightarrow S^{QT}_{ij}=\mathrm{needs\_data}\ \text{or coupling-only}.
\end{aligned}
$$
This is the point of the boundary: an in-sample score of $1.0$, or any other apparently strong fit, is not evidence when the post-control row space has already spent the available degrees of freedom.  The finite residual extraction shows persistent unexplained structure across four computed organisms, and the coupling run shows that a proxy translation contact is nonzero, but neither fact licenses translation survival without the rank-admissible held-out step.

\paragraph{Local exclusion.}
The local scientific conclusion is narrow.  The record supports a finite rule for refusing promotion from $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual geometry to translation survival when the controlled design is rank-saturated, underpowered, or only proxy-coupled.  It also preserves the negative information in the residual curves: the persistent $\rho_k$ floors are not failed computations, but signs that the current readout blocks do not absorb the residual coordinates.  No translation realization, ribosome occupancy, protein abundance, folded structure, physical admissibility, biological function, mechanism, phenotype, causation, or universal law follows from this chapter.  Any such statement would require its own layer-matched measurement surface and a separate controlled statistic.
