\subsection{Rank-admissible translation-survival gate for $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates}
\label{sec:biorality-q6-rank-admissible-translation-survival-gate}

\paragraph{Finite carrier.}
This record isolates the finite test surface on which a $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinate may be allowed to approach a translation-readout survival claim.  The carrier is a residualized design matrix whose rows are organism- or coding-sequence-indexed observations, whose $q_i$ columns are $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates, whose $T_j$ columns are bounded translation readouts, and whose control block $Z$ contains amino-acid composition, organism identity, GC3, coding-sequence length, and an $M$-density baseline.  The admissible object is not an in-sample fitted score.  It is a per-entry certificate for $S^{QT}_{ij}$ saying that $q_i$ and $T_j$ remain independently testable after residualization against $Z$, that the remaining row space is not rank saturated, and that held-out survival can fail.

\paragraph{Rank and survival criterion.}
For a proposed entry $S^{QT}_{ij}$, let $\widetilde q_i=(I-P_Z)q_i$ and $\widetilde T_j=(I-P_Z)T_j$.  The entry is eligible only when the finite matrix still has residual degrees of freedom after $Z$ and the translation column is not explained by a saturated projection.  The local gate can be written as
$$
\begin{aligned}
\operatorname{admissible}(i,j)&\Longleftrightarrow \operatorname{rank}([Z,q_i,T_j])>\operatorname{rank}(Z) \\
&\quad \wedge\; n-\operatorname{rank}([Z,q_i,T_j])>0 \\
&\quad \wedge\; S^{QT}_{ij}\text{ is evaluated on a held-out or otherwise falsifiable readout surface.}
\end{aligned}
$$
If the residualized row space is saturated, or if the remaining degrees of freedom are too small to make a failed held-out prediction possible, the entry is recorded as coupling-only or $\mathrm{needs\_data}$.  This refusal is triggered even when an in-sample model has positive score or an apparent fitted value of $1.0$.

\paragraph{Greedy residual extraction does not substitute for the gate.}
The residual-basis computation used the stated regularizer $\lambda=0.01$ with description-length cost $\log_2(1+n_{\mathrm{col}})$.  Its recursive object is $R_0=P_{Q\perp T_{\mathrm{mod}}}$, followed by greedy selection of readout blocks $H_k$ to absorb residual energy and record the decay of $\rho_k$.  This is a descriptive compression calculation, not a mechanism claim and not a global optimum guarantee.  The degree-of-freedom matched deterministic Gaussian null shows several finite increments above null: in Homo sapiens, $\mathrm{ptm\_density}$ contributes $0.014780177462400674$ against null $0.0002845846156909306$, $\mathrm{complex\_member}$ contributes $0.004618389163869967$ against $0.000206488525646134$, $\mathrm{tm\_count}$ contributes $0.0014438137599134737$ against $0.0002435268444123588$, $\mathrm{measured\_trna\_tai}$ contributes $0.0005032876020244136$ against $0.00020173994173736226$, and $\mathrm{localization}$ contributes $0.006528084968494762$ against $0.0011598998794309233$; the $\mathrm{domain\_count}$ step, $0.00003840392718095609$ against $0.00021932006910265595$, does not exceed its null.  In Saccharomyces cerevisiae, $\mathrm{measured\_te}$, $\mathrm{ptm\_density}$, $\mathrm{tm\_count}$, $\mathrm{turnover}$, and $\mathrm{localization}$ exceed their matched nulls, whereas $\mathrm{complex\_member}$, $\mathrm{measured\_trna\_tai}$, $\mathrm{domain\_count}$, and $\mathrm{mrna}$ do not.  In Escherichia coli K12 MG1655 the $\mathrm{mrna}$ step exceeds null, but the $\mathrm{measured\_te}$ step does not; in Danio rerio the single $\mathrm{measured\_trna\_tai}$ step exceeds null.  These rows show residual persistence and block-level finite contact, but they do not by themselves certify an admissible $S^{QT}_{ij}$ translation-survival entry.

\paragraph{Coupling-only contact.}
A separate coupling matrix run computed nine $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates: $\mathrm{Arg\_AGR}$, $\mathrm{Ile\_AUA}$, $\mathrm{K\_AAA}$, $\mathrm{Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR}$, $\mathrm{Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG}$, $\mathrm{Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG}$, $\mathrm{Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY}$, $\mathrm{Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY}$, and $\mathrm{f3\_stress}$.  The finite contact used three organisms, Homo sapiens, Saccharomyces cerevisiae, and Escherichia coli K12, in a $61$-codon count space, with a tRNA-Leu CUN-versus-UUR log-copy proxy readout.  The coupling threshold was $0.01$, all nine entries were nonzero above the threshold, the maximum absolute coupling was $1882464.1106377328$, and the declared break condition, all nine $|A_{ij}|<0.01$, was not met.  This establishes a bounded proxy coupling surface only.  It does not license the survival notation $S^{QT}_{ij}$ unless the rank-admissibility gate is passed on a controlled translation-readout design.

\paragraph{Local refusal line.}
The chapter therefore keeps three distinctions explicit.  First, a rank-admissible translation-survival entry is different from a rank-saturated projection artifact.  Second, a coupling coefficient $A^{QT}_{ij}$, a tAI or stAI weight, or a high in-sample fit is not the same as controlled survival.  Third, the readout boundary is translation-facing only: no ribosome-occupancy realization, protein abundance, folded structure, physical admissibility, function, mechanism, phenotype, or universal biological law follows from this finite packet unless a separate layer-matched contact supplies the relevant rows and the corresponding controlled statistic.  The finite evidence is strong enough to define and populate the refusal gate; it is not yet a measured translation-survival result for any specific $S^{QT}_{ij}$ entry.
