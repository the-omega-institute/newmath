\subsection{Rank-admissible boundary for $B^*_{Q6}$ translation survival}
\label{subsec:b-star-q6-rank-admissible-translation-survival-gate}

\paragraph{Finite carrier.}
This record treats the $B^*_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates as a finite residualized design problem, not as a protein-level or phenotype-level claim.  The carrier consists of organism-indexed rows, residual coordinates $q_i$, translation readout surfaces $T_j$, and control columns $Z$ containing amino-acid composition, organism identity, $GC3$, coding length, and the $M$-density baseline where the joined panel supplies it.  A proposed entry $S^{QT}_{ij}$ is admissible only when both sides have first been residualized against $Z$ and the remaining row space still leaves non-saturated degrees of freedom for a held-out test.  The gate therefore distinguishes three cases: a rank-admissible translation-survival entry, a coupling-only contact, and a rank-saturated projection artifact.  The refusal rule is local: a fitted score, including a fitted value equal to $1.0$, is not survival unless the residualized post-control design has enough independent support to make held-out failure possible.

\paragraph{Observed residual-basis panel.}
The finite-row residual-basis run computed four organism panels and left two requested organisms without usable local readout rows.  The computed organisms were \emph{Homo sapiens} with $n=16008$ joined rows and $6$ selected candidate blocks, \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae} with $n=3046$ and $9$ candidates, \emph{Escherichia coli} K12 MG1655 with $n=3415$ and $2$ candidates, and \emph{Danio rerio} with $n=14410$ and $1$ candidate.  \emph{Gallus gallus} and \emph{Mycobacterium smegmatis} str. MC2 155 were marked as needs-data rows because the joined common panel was not present.  The regularized extraction used the stated description-length penalty $\lambda=0.01$ with $DL=\log_2(1+n_{\mathrm{col}})$, and the computed residual curves remained high rather than collapsing.  The terminal values were
$$
\begin{aligned}
\rho_{\mathrm{human,final}} &= 0.9723341658092314,\\
\rho_{\mathrm{yeast,final}} &= 0.9798667977250413,\\
\rho_{\mathrm{ecoli,final}} &= 0.9979478314995476,\\
\rho_{\mathrm{zebrafish,final}} &= 0.9791828971490305.
\end{aligned}
$$
The minimum terminal residual ratio across the computed set was $0.9723341658092314$ against a reported floor of $0.97$, so the module classification is persistent for all four computed organisms, with no compressible and no partially compressible organism in this run.

\paragraph{Per-organism null comparisons.}
The gate keeps the per-step null comparisons as finite-row evidence about residual compression, not as direct survival.  In \emph{Danio rerio}, the only selected block was measured tRNA/tAI, with increment $0.02081710285096942$ above a deterministic null $95$th percentile of $0.0002306374780841125$.  In \emph{E. coli}, the mRNA block had increment $0.0017706954554909888$ above null $0.0013371669456573104$, while the measured-TE block had increment $0.0002819723320884449$ below null $0.0010272364744326006$.  In human rows, the selected sequence was PTM density, complex membership, transmembrane count, measured tRNA/tAI, domain count, and localization; the corresponding increments were $0.014780177462400674$, $0.004618389163869967$, $0.0014438137599134737$, $0.0005032876020244136$, $0.00003840392718095609$, and $0.006528084968494762$, with the domain-count step below its null threshold $0.00021932006910265595$.  In yeast rows, measured TE, PTM density, transmembrane count, turnover, complex membership, measured tRNA/tAI, domain count, mRNA, and localization were selected; only steps $1$, $2$, $3$, $4$, and $9$ exceeded their reported null thresholds.  These comparisons justify the statement that the residual basis was computed and rank-audited, but they do not by themselves certify $S^{QT}_{ij}>0$.

\paragraph{Coupling is not survival.}
A separate finite coupling contact used three organisms, namely human, yeast, and \emph{E. coli} K12, over a $61$-codon count space and computed nine synonymous residual coordinates: $Arg\_AGR$, $Ile\_AUA$, $K\_AAA$, $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$, $Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG$, $Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG$, $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$, $Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY$, and $f3\_stress$.  Against the proxy readout described as the tRNA-Leu CUN-versus-UUR log-copy spectrum, all nine entries crossed the coupling threshold $0.01$, with maximum absolute coupling $1882464.1106377328$.  This establishes a nonzero modeled contact in the finite proxy matrix, not a translation-survival entry.  The stated break condition for that coupling run was failure only if all nine $q_i$ had $|A_{ij}|<0.01$ against the proxy readout, and that break condition was not met.  The rank-admissible survival gate is stricter: it requires a non-saturated residualized design and a held-out translation readout boundary after $Z$, not merely a nonzero in-sample proxy coefficient.

\paragraph{Admissibility rule.}
For each candidate coordinate-readout pair, write $\widetilde q_i$ and $\widetilde T_j$ for residuals after $Z$.  The entry is eligible for survival testing only when the effective post-control design rank is below the available row support and the held-out fold has enough remaining variation to make a negative result observable.  The local decision rule is
$$
\begin{aligned}
S^{QT}_{ij}\ \text{may be read as survival} 
&\Longrightarrow \operatorname{rank}(Z, q_i, T_j)\ \text{is not saturated},\\
&\quad \widetilde q_i\ \text{and}\ \widetilde T_j\ \text{have held-out support},\\
&\quad \Delta R^2_{\mathrm{heldout}}\ \text{or the declared description-length gain clears its null boundary}.
\end{aligned}
$$
If those conditions fail, the entry remains either coupling-only or needs-data.  The three-organism proxy surface is especially useful as a refusal example: a small organism count can fit a controlled surface too tightly, and an all-perfect or near-perfect in-sample surface would be rank evidence against promotion rather than evidence for translation realization.

\paragraph{Cross-layer placement.}
The residual-basis extraction also sits beside a yeast execution-selection run with $n=5684$ active genes, base $R^2_{QP}=0.30732228260918726$ on the active set, base $R^2_{QP}=0.29837624994140827$ on the full gene set, $P_Q$ norm squared $864.0124192674945$, and residual norm squared $2811.4213259512776$.  That run returned the verdict composition artifact under its own decision rule.  A wider translational-selection growth-scaling run requested $20$ organisms, computed $20$ strengths, had doubling-time rows for all $20$, and had $17$ complete strength-doubling rows after $GC3$ and tRNA controls.  These neighboring finite facts support keeping the $B^*_{Q6}$ boundary as a controlled cross-layer statistic rather than a biological law.  They also show why the present gate is needed: cross-layer association, proxy coupling, and residual compression can coexist with persistent residual energy and with composition-artifact verdicts.

\paragraph{Local refusal boundary.}
The present chapter asserts only a rank-admissibility discipline for $B^*_{Q6}$ translation-readout survival.  It does not assert ribosome occupancy, translation realization, protein abundance, folded structure, physical admissibility, molecular function, mechanism, phenotype, causation, or universality.  Those claims require their own layer-matched measurements and their own controlled statistics.  In particular, codon-usage tables, tRNA gene-copy counts, and modeled tAI or stAI weights may bound a translation-readout surface, but they do not observe ribosome dynamics by themselves.  Matched mRNA abundance is a control surface, not translation output.  A positive proxy coupling $A^{QT}_{ij}$, a persistent residual curve, or a fitted in-sample score equal to $1.0$ is therefore insufficient for $S^{QT}_{ij}$ unless the rank-admissible held-out survival test is actually present.
