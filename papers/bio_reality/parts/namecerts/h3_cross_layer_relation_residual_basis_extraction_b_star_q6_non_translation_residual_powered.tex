\subsection{Rank-Admissible Translation-Survival Gate for $B^*_{Q6}$ Residual Coordinates}
\label{sec:bior reality-q6-rank-admissible-translation-survival-gate}

\paragraph{Finite carrier.}
The finite object considered here is a residualized design surface whose rows are organism-indexed coding-sequence or organism-level readouts, whose coordinate columns are the $B^*_{Q6}$ synonymous residual directions, and whose control block $Z$ contains the covariates required to prevent amino-acid composition, organism identity, $GC3$, length, and $M$-density from being silently reintroduced as a translation signal. The gate is deliberately placed before protein abundance, folded structure, molecular function, phenotype, or mechanism language. Its local question is only whether a proposed entry $S^{QT}_{ij}$ is statistically admissible as a held-out translation-readout survival entry after residualization.

\paragraph{Admissibility rule.}
For a residual coordinate $q_i$ and a translation readout $T_j$, write $\widetilde q_i$ and $\widetilde T_j$ for the residuals after projection away from $Z$. A positive in-sample fit is not enough. The post-control design must still have degrees of freedom for a held-out test, and the row space must not be saturated by the controls and selected columns. The operational rule is:
$$
\begin{aligned}
S^{QT}_{ij} \text{ may be reported only if }&\quad \operatorname{rank}(Z,\widetilde q_i,\widetilde T_j) < n,\\
&\quad n-\operatorname{rank}(Z,\widetilde q_i,\widetilde T_j) \text{ leaves held-out support},\\
&\quad \Delta \mathrm{DL}_{ij} \text{ or controlled partial } R^2_{ij} \text{ is positive off sample.}
\end{aligned}
$$
When these conditions fail, the entry is labelled as coupling-only or needs-data even if an in-sample surface gives a positive coefficient, a large proxy score, or a fitted value equal to $1.0$.

\paragraph{Observed finite rows.}
The residual-basis computation used a $\lambda=0.01$ regularizer with $\mathrm{DL}=\log_2(1+n_{\mathrm{col}})$ and produced computed rows for four organisms. The persistent set was $\{\textit{Danio rerio}, \textit{Escherichia coli}~K12~MG1655, \textit{Homo sapiens}, \textit{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}\}$, while $\textit{Gallus gallus}$ and $\textit{Mycobacterium smegmatis}$ str. MC2 155 remained needs-data. The joined-row counts were $14410$ for zebrafish, $3415$ for $E. coli$, $16008$ for human, and $3046$ for yeast. The final residual ratios were $0.9791828971490305$, $0.9979478314995476$, $0.9723341658092314$, and $0.9798667977250413$, respectively; the minimum final ratio among computed organisms was therefore $0.9723341658092314$ against a persistence floor of $0.97$. No organism was classified as compressible or partially compressible, and all four computed organisms were classified as persistent.

\paragraph{Per-organism extraction evidence.}
The selected readout blocks do show finite contact with the residual surface, but they do not by themselves satisfy the translation-survival gate. Zebrafish had one candidate, selected measured tRNA/tAI at step $1$, with increment $0.02081710285096942$ above a null $95\%$ cutoff of $0.0002306374780841125$. $E. coli$ had two candidates: mRNA at step $1$ exceeded its null cutoff with increment $0.0017706954554909888$ versus $0.0013371669456573104$, while measured TE at step $2$ did not exceed its null cutoff, with increment $0.0002819723320884449$ versus $0.0010272364744326006$. Human had six selected steps: PTM density, complex membership, transmembrane count, measured tRNA/tAI, domain count, and localization. Five of these exceeded their matched nulls, while domain count did not: $0.00003840392718095609$ was below $0.00021932006910265595$. Yeast had nine selected steps. Measured TE, PTM density, transmembrane count, turnover, and localization exceeded their matched nulls, whereas complex membership, measured tRNA/tAI, domain count, and mRNA did not.

\paragraph{Proxy coupling is not survival.}
A separate finite coupling computation over the synonymous residual list $\{\mathrm{Arg\_AGR},\mathrm{Ile\_AUA},\mathrm{K\_AAA},\mathrm{Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR},\mathrm{Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG},\mathrm{Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG},\mathrm{Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY},\mathrm{Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY},f_3\text{-stress}\}$ loaded three organisms: human, yeast, and $E. coli$ K12. It found nine nonzero proxy entries against a tRNA-Leu CUN-versus-UUR log-copy spectrum, with maximum absolute proxy coupling $1882464.1106377328$ and a threshold of $0.01$. This defeats the all-small proxy break condition, but it remains an $A^{QT}$ coupling surface, not an admissible $S^{QT}$ survival matrix. The readout was a tRNA-copy proxy rather than a same-row measured ribosome, TE, or protein-output panel under the declared controls.

\paragraph{Rank-saturation refusal.}
The gate is meant to reject exactly the degenerate case in which an apparently perfect controlled fit is only a row-space artifact. A three-organism, all-$1.0$ controlled surface has no automatic biological meaning here: with insufficient post-control degrees of freedom, it is classified as rank-saturated rather than as translation survival. In this chapter, the finite data support a negative conclusion about the boundary: the $B^*_{Q6}$ residual geometry is persistent under the available readout blocks, and several descriptive extraction increments exceed degree-of-freedom matched null cutoffs, but a measured, non-saturated, held-out $S^{QT}_{ij}$ entry is not yet present.

\paragraph{Adjacent finite contacts.}
The surrounding BioReality memory shows that bounded cross-layer computations can be finite without licensing this gate. The branchpoint-motif usage contact used $n=10584$ intronic rows with expression controlled by a UCSC hg19 GTEx V8 expression source. The uORF/Kozak ribosome-load contact reported $41862$ fixed rows, $16739$ varying rows, and two-sample concordance. The codon-composition half-life contact reported $16500$ human rows, $18924$ mouse rows, raw $\rho=0.126224$, and a non-CSC tAI circularity guard. These records are useful as evidence that finite biological panels can be audited, but none supplies the same $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinate, translation readout, control block, rank certificate, and held-out survival statistic required for this chapter.

\paragraph{Local exclusion rule.}
The only present promotion is from residual-coordinate memory to a rank-audited testing obligation. No translation realization, ribosome occupancy, protein abundance, folded structure, physical admissibility, biological function, mechanism, phenotype, or universal biological law follows from the residual-basis extraction, from the tAI or stAI route, from tRNA gene-copy summaries, from mRNA controls, from protein-abundance contact names, or from the proxy coupling matrix. Any such assertion would require its own layer-matched contact and its own statistic after the same controls. Until that panel exists, the correct status of the rank-admissible $B^*_{Q6}$ translation-survival gate is: persistent residual coordinates observed; proxy and descriptive contacts recorded; measured non-saturated $S^{QT}$ survival not established.
