\subsection{Nieuwkoop mRFP Synonymous-Window Protein Output}
\label{sec:bioreality-nieuwkoop-ecoli-mrfp-protein-output}

\paragraph{Finite perturbation surface.}
The Nieuwkoop mRFP packet is a finite synonymous-variant contact in which the measured endpoint is protein output for an engineered \emph{Escherichia coli} reporter.  The admissible rows are not whole-organism fitness claims, structural claims, or universal codon-law claims.  They are variant-level records in which a synonymous window coordinate is compared against a controlled protein-output readout.  The registered run contains $1459$ variants and uses $B=200$ resampling draws.  The controlled model before adding the residual window coordinate has
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{L2} &= 0.12648386762689323,\\
R^2_{L3} &= 0.1798829276988051,\\
\Delta R^2 &= 0.05339906007191191.
\end{aligned}
$$
The associated description-length gain is $19.089327473282296$ bits.  Both the matched null and the within-design null report $p=0.004975124378109453$.  These numbers support a narrow statement: within this finite mRFP perturbation table, the projected $B^*_Q6$ window residual carries protein-output information not absorbed by the listed controls.

\paragraph{Control relation to codon adaptation.}
The run explicitly audits whether the effect is merely a codon-adaptation proxy.  The real-CAI comparison gives correlation $0.17884920449487354$ between the real CAI axis and the proxy used in the compact table, while the run records \texttt{real\_cai\_absorbs=false} and \texttt{real\_cai\_robust=true}.  Thus the finite readout is not summarized as a CAI-only observation.  The claim remains weaker than a mechanism: it says that the tested synonymous window coordinate improves prediction of the mRFP protein-output endpoint after the available controls, not that codon adaptation is the biological cause.

\paragraph{Local statistic.}
For this chapter the admissible numerical summary is the controlled improvement
$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta R^2_{\mathrm{mRFP}}
  &= R^2_{L3}-R^2_{L2}\\
  &= 0.1798829276988051-0.12648386762689323\\
  &= 0.05339906007191191,
\end{aligned}
$$
with the finite-null evidence
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{\mathrm{matched}} &= 0.004975124378109453,\\
p_{\mathrm{within}} &= 0.004975124378109453,\\
\Delta \mathrm{DL} &= 19.089327473282296\ \mathrm{bits}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The result is therefore recorded as a positive finite-row protein-output perturbation contact for the Nieuwkoop mRFP table.  The support is row-local and design-local: it depends on the $1459$ measured variants, the specified controls, and the registered null construction with $200$ draws.

\paragraph{Boundary against start-region overreading.}
A companion boundary audit on the same experimental family separates this protein-output signal from a simple first-window story.  That audit reports $1459$ variants, $B=200$, $R^2_{\mathrm{step1}}=0.06748192482378434$, $R^2_{\mathrm{step2}}=0.6060702157093043$, and $R^2_{\mathrm{step3}}=0.6049438075310105$.  The downstream-window contribution there is negative, with $\Delta R^2=-0.001126409133973234$ and downstream block-null $p=0.1691542288557214$, while the start-window increment is only $1.7624020537495221\times 10^{-9}$.  The flag \texttt{sign\_stable=false} prevents converting the positive mRFP protein-output result into a generic claim that a particular positional boundary or direction of synonymous change is universally privileged.

\paragraph{Relation to the broader translation-mediated matrix.}
The separate cross-organism protein-abundance mediation run gives useful context but is not substituted for the perturbation result.  In the \emph{E. coli} K-12 MG1655 row of that matrix, $3739$ proteins are joined, the modeled per-protein translation readout has residual energy $0.4728468725814029$, and the residualized protein-abundance energy is $4020.333671802061$.  The total $Q\to P$ protein-abundance coefficient is $R^2=0.10767526677985896$, the direct coefficient after conditioning on the modeled translation readout is $R^2=0.04167869969498872$, and the corresponding mediated difference is $0.06599656708487024$, giving $M^{QTP}=0.612922252793667$.  That matrix shows that a translation-conditioned decomposition can be computed in organism-matched protein rows, whereas the Nieuwkoop packet supplies a direct synonymous-perturbation protein-output table.  The two contacts are compatible but not interchangeable.

\paragraph{No-promotion rule for this chapter.}
The admissible readback stops at controlled protein-output prediction in the finite Nieuwkoop mRFP variant surface.  It does not establish translation dynamics, ribosome occupancy, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, organismal phenotype, selection, causation, or a general biological law.  A translation statement would require a separate matched translation readout such as ribosome profiling, translation efficiency, or an explicitly bounded tAI/stAI surface.  A folding or physical-admissibility statement would require a separate structure or physical assay.  A function, mechanism, phenotype, or universality statement would require its own layer-matched contact and a new finite statistic.  The present claim is therefore positive but deliberately confined: the $B^*_Q6$ synonymous window contributes to mRFP protein-output prediction in this controlled finite perturbation table, with $\Delta R^2=0.05339906007191191$ and matched-null $p=0.004975124378109453$.
