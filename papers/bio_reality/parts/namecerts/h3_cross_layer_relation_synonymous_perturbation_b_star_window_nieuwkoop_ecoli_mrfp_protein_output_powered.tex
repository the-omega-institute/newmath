\end{aligned}

$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{\mathrm{within}} &= 0.004975124378109453,\\
p_{\mathrm{matched}} &= 0.004975124378109453,
\end{aligned}

using $B=200$ randomization draws.  On this finite perturbation panel, the $B^{\ast}$ window contribution is therefore not absorbed by the declared control layer and is not explained away by the real codon-adaptation-index control used in the audit.

\paragraph{Translation gate.}
The translation-readout boundary remains local and explicit.  A modeled tAI contact was computed from GtRNAdb tRNA gene-copy counts and dos Reis wobble weights for three organisms, with $81$ entries above the $0.01$ bounded-coupling threshold and organism count $3$.  That computation also records that three organisms are insufficient for a powered survival claim and that the result is modeled-tAI coupling rather than ribosome profiling or proteomics validation.  Consequently, the mRFP perturbation result may be reported as a controlled synonymous-window protein-output association, but not as a completed $Q_i \to T_j \to P_k$ translation-mediated chain unless a matched translation readout is attached on the same finite surface.

\paragraph{Local verdict.}
The finite-row verdict is positive for the registered Nieuwkoop mRFP protein-output test: adding the $B^{\ast}$ window residual to the declared controls raises held-out explanatory power by $0.05339906007191191$ in $R^2$ and improves description length by $19.089327473282296$ bits, with both within-panel and matched-null tail probabilities equal to $0.004975124378109453$.  The chapter therefore admits a bounded protein-output statement for this synonymous perturbation panel.  It does not license translation realization, protein folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, mechanism, phenotype, selection, or universality.  Each of those stronger readings would require its own layer-matched contact and a separate finite statistic; the present contact only says that, after the recorded controls and CAI audit, the $B^{\ast}$ synonymous-window residual carries additional mRFP output information in this \emph{E. coli} perturbation table.
