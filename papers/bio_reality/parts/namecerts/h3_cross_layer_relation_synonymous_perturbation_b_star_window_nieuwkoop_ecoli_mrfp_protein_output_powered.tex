\subsection{Nieuwkoop \textit{E. coli} mRFP Synonymous-Window Protein Output}\label{sec:bioreality-nieuwkoop-ecoli-mrfp-protein-output}\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite row surface.}  This record concerns a finite synonymous-perturbation panel for an \textit{E. coli} mRFP coding sequence.  The observable rows are synonymous coding variants with a protein-output readout, a real-CAI control surface, and a projected $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ window coordinate.  The registered run contains $1459$ variants and $200$ matched null draws.  The coordinate used here is not treated as a free biological cause; it is a finite regressor tested after the simpler codon-adaptation baseline and the declared controls have already been fitted.

\paragraph{Controlled increment.}  Let $L_2$ denote the controlled baseline using the available lower-order covariates and real-CAI information, and let $L_3$ denote the same design with the residual $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ window coordinate added orthogonally to those controls.  The observed finite-row comparison is
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2(L_2) &= 0.12648386762689323,\\
R^2(L_3) &= 0.1798829276988051,\\
\Delta R^2 &= 0.05339906007191191,\\
\Delta \mathrm{DL} &= 19.089327473282296\ \mathrm{bits}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The within-design and matched-null tail probabilities are both $0.004975124378109453$.  The real-CAI check does not absorb the signal: $\mathrm{real\_cai\_absorbs}=\mathrm{false}$, $\mathrm{real\_cai\_robust}=\mathrm{true}$, and the real-CAI versus proxy correlation is $0.17884920449487354$.  Thus the admissible local conclusion is a bounded protein-output association for this synonymous mRFP perturbation panel after the named baseline, not a general law about synonymous coding sequence.

\paragraph{Five-prime boundary audit.}  The companion boundary audit uses the same $1459$ variants and checks whether the effect can be reduced to a trivial start-proximal or downstream-window artifact.  Its first-stage model has $R^2=0.06748192482378434$, the second-stage fit rises to $R^2=0.6060702157093043$ with $\Delta\ell=628.6217154500001$ relative to the first stage, while the downstream $B$ addition has $\Delta R^2=-0.001126409133973234$ and $\Delta\ell=-2.082967365015851$.  The downstream matched null has $95\%$ reference value $0.5254770343527547$ and block probability $0.1691542288557214$, while the start-window contribution is numerically negligible, $\Delta R^2=1.7624020537495221\times 10^{-9}$ and $\Delta\ell=3.26370933437133\times 10^{-6}$.  The sign-stability flag is false.  These values support a narrow readback: the positive mRFP result is not licensed as a generic five-prime directionality rule, and the downstream audit does not provide a second positive window.

\paragraph{Relation to the broader mediation matrix.}  A separate organism-scale $Q\to T\to P$ calibration provides context for why the protein-output readout is kept behind a translation gate.  In the \textit{E. coli} K12 MG1655 row of the matched CDS/protein-abundance analysis, $3739$ proteins are joined.  The finite decomposition reports
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{QP} &= 0.10767526677985896,\\
R^2_{QP\mid T} &= 0.04167869969498872,\\
R^2_{\mathrm{indirect}} &= 0.06599656708487024,\\
M^{QTP} &= 0.612922252793667.
\end{aligned}
$$
The corresponding controlled-rank diagnostics for this \textit{E. coli} row have residual degrees of freedom $3716$, $9$ $Q$ coordinates, $23$ control-rank columns, and a powered-rank-sufficient flag equal to true.  These numbers do not prove that the mRFP perturbation effect is mediated by ribosomes in the local experiment; they only show that the project has a separate finite surface on which translation-conditioned protein-abundance decompositions can be computed.  The mRFP result therefore remains a perturbation-level protein-output association unless the same-row translation readout is attached for that panel.

\paragraph{Allowed claim.}  The admissible claim is the following finite statement.  In the Nieuwkoop \textit{E. coli} mRFP synonymous-window panel, adding the projected $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual coordinate to the controlled real-CAI baseline improves held-out protein-output fit by $\Delta R^2=0.05339906007191191$ and $19.089327473282296$ description-length bits over $1459$ variants, with matched and within-design probabilities equal to $0.004975124378109453$.  The effect survives the real-CAI absorption check but is not promoted beyond this measured panel.

\paragraph{Local no-promotion boundary.}  No statement about translation dynamics, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, mechanism, organismal phenotype, selection, or universality follows from this chapter alone.  A direct $Q_i\to P_k$ association is not enough to claim protein realization in the mediation sense unless a same-row $Q_i\to T_j$ translation readout first survives the declared controls.  The present chapter records a finite synonymous-perturbation protein-output result and its bounded controls; each higher layer requires its own matched contact and statistic.
