The fitted comparison has two nested designs: an $L_2$ control model using the finite background covariates, and an $L_3$ model that appends the residual $B^{*}_{Q6}$ window coordinate $B_{\perp}$. These numbers certify a table-level residual protein-output association for this perturbation surface, not a general synonymous-code theorem.

\paragraph{Translation-readout gate.}
The residual coordinate is not allowed to jump directly from a synonymous sequence edit to a full protein-realization claim.  Translation readout remains a gate.  The modeled tAI survival matrix has only $3$ organisms in the finite support, so it is explicitly not a powered ribosome-seq or proteomics validation panel.  It nevertheless records $81$ entries above the bounded coupling threshold $0.01$, with maximum absolute $S_{ij}=3801961.031607896$, and all three organisms have complete standard amino-acid-family tRNA coverage: $20$ of $20$ for $E.\ coli$ K-12, human, and yeast.  The same record lists $89$ $E.\ coli$ FASTA headers, $87$ matched headers, and $2$ unmatched headers, with all $61$ sense codons assigned modeled tAI weights and zero raw-weight fallback count $0$.  This supports bounded translation-layer routing, but it does not by itself observe ribosome occupancy in the Nieuwkoop variants.

\paragraph{Local verdict.}
The finite-row verdict is positive at the protein-output table level.  The evidence is the improvement from $R^2=0.12648386762689323$ to $R^2=0.1798829276988051$, the $0.05339906007191191$ increment, the $19.089327473282296$ bit gain, and the paired small finite-null values $p_{\mathrm{matched}}=p_{\mathrm{within}}=0.004975124378109453$.  The admissible statement is therefore: in the Nieuwkoop $E.\ coli$ mRFP synonymous-variant table, the residual $B^{*}_{Q6}$ window coordinate contributes protein-output information beyond the declared controls and beyond the real-CAI absorption check.  The result does not establish translation realization, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, causal mechanism, selection, phenotype, or universality.  Each of those stronger layers would require its own matched contact and its own finite statistic before it could be asserted.
