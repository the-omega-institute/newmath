\subsection{Nieuwkoop mRFP Synonymous-Window Protein-Output Contact}
\label{sec:bioreality-nieuwkoop-ecoli-mrfp-protein-output}

\paragraph{Finite surface.}
This chapter records a finite perturbation surface for an \emph{E. coli} mRFP synonymous-window experiment.  The rows are synonymous recoding variants, the primary downstream readout is protein output, and the tested coordinate is the component of the $B^*_Q6$ window signal that remains after the declared lower-dimensional controls and a real codon-adaptation baseline have been fitted.  The finite panel contains $1459$ variants.  The registered resampling count is $B=200$, so the smallest nonzero permutation tail probability available in the recorded run is $1/(200+1)=0.004975124378109453$, which is exactly the reported value for both within-window and matched-null tests.

\paragraph{Control hierarchy.}
Let $Y$ denote the measured mRFP protein-output readout on the finite variant panel, let $Z_2$ denote the level-$2$ control design, and let $b_\perp$ denote the tested $B^*_Q6$ window coordinate after projection away from the fitted controls.  The comparison recorded for this chapter is
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2(Y \sim Z_2) &= 0.12648386762689323,\\
R^2(Y \sim Z_2 + b_\perp) &= 0.1798829276988051,\\
\Delta R^2 &= 0.05339906007191191.
\end{aligned}
$$
The associated description-length improvement is
$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta \operatorname{DL}_{\mathrm{bits}} = 19.089327473282296.
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the finite-row record is not merely a positive in-sample coefficient: the added coordinate improves the controlled protein-output fit by about $5.34$ percentage points of explained variance and by $19.09$ bits on the registered scoring scale.

\paragraph{Null comparison.}
The same run reports
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{\mathrm{within}} &= 0.004975124378109453,\\
p_{\mathrm{matched}} &= 0.004975124378109453.
\end{aligned}
$$
Since both values equal $1/(200+1)$, the observed improvement lies beyond every sampled null replicate in both the within-window and matched-null audits.  This is the strongest tail resolution available from the recorded $B=200$ resampling design; it should be read as a finite-run extremal result, not as an asymptotic theorem.

\paragraph{Real-CAI audit.}
A separate codon-adaptation check was included to prevent the window coordinate from being reduced to an ordinary codon-adaptation proxy.  The run used the compact real-CAI artifact recorded at \texttt{/tmp/nieuwkoop\_mrfp\_compact\_real\_cai.json}.  The reported correlation between the real CAI baseline and the proxy is
$$
\begin{aligned}
\operatorname{corr}(\mathrm{CAI}_{\mathrm{real}},\mathrm{proxy}) = 0.17884920449487354.
\end{aligned}
$$
The audit flags are $\mathrm{real\_cai\_absorbs}=\mathrm{false}$ and $\mathrm{real\_cai\_robust}=\mathrm{true}$.  In finite-row terms, this means that adding the real codon-adaptation baseline did not absorb the $B^*_Q6$ window contribution, and the residual coordinate remains a distinct predictor of the measured mRFP protein-output readout on this panel.

\paragraph{Five-prime boundary check.}
The companion boundary audit on the same $1459$ variants separates a narrow start-region effect from downstream window information.  Its level sequence reports
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{\mathrm{step\,1}} &= 0.06748192482378434,\\
R^2_{\mathrm{step\,2}} &= 0.6060702157093043,\\
R^2_{\mathrm{step\,3}} &= 0.6049438075310105.
\end{aligned}
$$
The downstream window term in that audit has
$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta R^2_{\mathrm{downstream}} &= -0.001126409133973234,\\
\Delta \ell_{\mathrm{downstream}} &= -2.082967365015851,\\
p_{\mathrm{block}} &= 0.1691542288557214,\\
\mathrm{null}_{95} &= 0.5254770343527547.
\end{aligned}
$$
The start-window increment is numerically negligible, with $\Delta R^2_{\mathrm{start}}=1.7624020537495221\times 10^{-9}$ and $\Delta \ell_{\mathrm{start}}=3.26370933437133\times 10^{-6}$.  The boundary audit also records $\mathrm{sign\_stable}=\mathrm{false}$.  This companion result keeps the positive mRFP protein-output finding from being reinterpreted as a stable, signed start-boundary law; the chapter-level claim is therefore limited to the controlled window residual that survives the stated protein-output fit.

\paragraph{Relation to the broader $Q \to T \to P$ surface.}
The general mediation packet for $B^*_Q6$ codon residuals, modeled per-protein tAI, and protein abundance contains an \emph{E. coli} joined surface with $3739$ proteins.  On that surface the \emph{E. coli} mediation decomposition reports
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{QP} &= 0.10767526677985896,\\
R^2_{QP\mid T} &= 0.04167869969498872,\\
R^2_{\mathrm{indirect}} &= 0.06599656708487024,\\
M^{QTP} &= 0.612922252793667.
\end{aligned}
$$
The same \emph{E. coli} record has $4040$-scale protein joins before residual degrees-of-freedom adjustment, with $n_{\mathrm{joined}}=3739$, residual degrees of freedom $3716$, $9$ $Q$-coordinates, $24$ control columns, and a rank-sufficient residual design.  These broader rows do not prove the perturbation result, but they show that the project already keeps the three-layer notation explicit: $Q$ is the codon-residual coordinate family, $T$ is a translation readout such as modeled tAI or measured translation efficiency when available, and $P$ is a matched protein-abundance or protein-output readout.

\paragraph{Local verdict.}
For this chapter, the finite perturbation panel supports a positive controlled protein-output association for the Nieuwkoop \emph{E. coli} mRFP synonymous-window coordinate.  The admissible statement is the following finite assertion: after the level-$2$ controls and real-CAI audit, the residual $B^*_Q6$ window component improves the mRFP protein-output model from $R^2=0.12648386762689323$ to $R^2=0.1798829276988051$, with $\Delta R^2=0.05339906007191191$, $\Delta \operatorname{DL}=19.089327473282296$ bits, and extremal $B=200$ null tails $p_{\mathrm{within}}=p_{\mathrm{matched}}=0.004975124378109453$.

\paragraph{No-promotion boundary.}
This chapter does not by itself establish a general translation law, a ribosome-dynamics mechanism, protein folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, organismal phenotype, selection, or universality.  The measured endpoint here is protein output in a finite synonymous-variant panel.  A stronger statement about translation would require a layer-matched translation contact such as ribosome occupancy, translation efficiency, or a justified modeled-translation readout on the same relevant rows.  A stronger statement about folding, physical feasibility, function, mechanism, or cross-organism law would require separate contacts at those layers and a new finite statistic tying those contacts to the same coordinate under the appropriate controls.
