\subsection{Nieuwkoop \emph{E. coli} mRFP synonymous-window protein-output readback}\label{sec:bioreality-nieuwkoop-ecoli-mrfp-protein-output}\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite perturbation surface.} The Nieuwkoop \emph{E. coli} mRFP packet is a finite synonymous-perturbation surface whose rows are coding-window variants with a measured protein-output response. The registered computation used $n=1459$ variants and $B=200$ matched null resamples. Its coordinate object is not the amino-acid sequence, organism label, GC3, CDS length, or codon-adaptation baseline itself. Those quantities remain controls. The tested coordinate is the projected $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ synonymous-window residual, residualized against the declared baseline before the protein-output readout is evaluated. The local finite comparison is therefore
$$
\begin{aligned}
L_2 &: P \sim Z,\\
L_3 &: P \sim Z + B^{\ast\perp}_{Q6},
\end{aligned}
$$
where $P$ is the mRFP protein-output measurement, $Z$ contains the admissible controls, and $B^{\ast\perp}_{Q6}$ denotes the residual synonymous-window coordinate after the control surface has been removed.

\paragraph{Observed protein-output increment.} The powered row audit reports $R^2(L_2)=0.12648386762689323$ for the control-only fit and $R^2(L_3)=0.1798829276988051$ after adding the residual $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ window coordinate. The observed increment is
$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta R^2 &= 0.1798829276988051-0.12648386762689323\\
&=0.05339906007191191.
\end{aligned}
$$
The description-length improvement is $19.089327473282296$ bits. Both the matched null and the within-design null give $p=0.004975124378109453$, using the same $B=200$ resampling budget. In this finite panel, the synonymous-window residual therefore gives a positive protein-output readback after the stated controls. The result is not a free-standing codon-usage association: it is the improvement of the controlled protein-output fit when the residual coordinate is admitted after the baseline.

\paragraph{Codon-adaptation audit.} The same run records a real-CAI comparison. The correlation between the real CAI axis and the proxy baseline is $0.17884920449487354$. The audit marks the signal as real-CAI robust and records that real CAI does not absorb the residual effect. In the local vocabulary, this means that the mRFP output increment is not dismissed as a simple codon-adaptation-weight duplicate. The conclusion is narrower: the tested residual coordinate survives this CAI check on the finite Nieuwkoop mRFP variant table.

\paragraph{Five-prime boundary control.} A companion boundary audit on the same $n=1459$ variant scale separates the downstream residual window from a trivial start-proximal explanation. That audit reports $R^2=0.06748192482378434$ at the first step, $R^2=0.6060702157093043$ after the main boundary model, and $R^2=0.6049438075310105$ after adding the downstream residual term. The downstream addition has $\Delta R^2=-0.001126409133973234$, $\Delta \ell=-2.082967365015851$, a block-null value $p=0.1691542288557214$, and a null $95\%$ reference of $0.5254770343527547$. The start-local term is effectively absent, with $\Delta R^2=1.7624020537495221\times 10^{-9}$ and $\Delta \ell=3.26370933437133\times 10^{-6}$. The sign-stability flag is false. This does not erase the protein-output increment above; it confines its interpretation. The positive mRFP readback is a controlled finite-row protein-output result, not a license to claim that every positional subdivision of the same construct carries a stable independent effect.

\paragraph{Relation to translation mediation.} The larger cross-organism mediation table gives useful scale but is not itself the perturbation experiment. In its \emph{E. coli} entry, the matched CDS/protein panel has $n=3739$ joined proteins, $R^2_{QP}=0.10767526677985896$, $R^2_{QP\mid T}=0.04167869969498872$, and an indirect difference of $0.06599656708487024$, giving a mediated fraction $M^{QTP}=0.612922252793667$ under modeled per-protein tAI. This background supports the plausibility of asking whether a synonymous residual can pass through a translation-like readout before protein abundance, but it remains a statistical decomposition using modeled tAI. The Nieuwkoop mRFP perturbation result is stronger in one respect, because it observes a synonymous-variant protein-output response in a finite experimental panel; it is weaker in another respect, because the supplied finite rows here do not include a same-row ribosome-occupancy or translation-efficiency measurement for each variant.

\paragraph{Admissible conclusion.} The chapter-level readback is therefore: in the Nieuwkoop \emph{E. coli} mRFP synonymous-variant panel, the controlled residual $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ window coordinate improves protein-output prediction by $\Delta R^2=0.05339906007191191$ and $19.089327473282296$ bits, with matched and within-design $p$-values both equal to $0.004975124378109453$. This is a finite perturbation contact with protein output, not merely a codon-count memory. It also remains bounded: no translation mechanism, ribosome dynamics, folding pathway, physical admissibility, molecular function, organismal phenotype, selection law, or universal rule follows from these rows alone. Such claims would require a separate layer-matched contact, for example variant-matched ribosome profiling for translation, structural or stability measurement for physical state, and functional or phenotype assays for biological action.
