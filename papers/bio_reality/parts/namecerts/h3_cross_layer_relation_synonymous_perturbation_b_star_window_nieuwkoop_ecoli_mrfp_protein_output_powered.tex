\subsection{Nieuwkoop E. coli mRFP synonymous-window protein-output contact}
\label{sec:bioreality-nieuwkoop-ecoli-mrfp-protein-output}

\paragraph{Finite assay surface.}
This record concerns a synonymous perturbation panel in an \emph{E. coli} mRFP coding sequence. The finite rows are not whole-genome correlates: they are engineered variants whose amino-acid sequence is held fixed while synonymous coding-window coordinates vary. The relevant carrier is therefore the row set of $1459$ variants, a matched scalar protein-output readout, a control block containing ordinary coding-sequence summaries, and the residual $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ window coordinate after projection away from the declared controls. The assay is read as a local protein-output perturbation contact, not as a direct claim about translation dynamics, folding, function, or selection.

\paragraph{Controlled increment.}
The registered run used $B=200$ resampling blocks and compared a control-only model against the same control model augmented by the orthogonalized $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ window coordinate. The control layer had
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{\mathrm{L2}} &= 0.12648386762689323,
\end{aligned}
$$
while the augmented layer had
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{\mathrm{L3}} &= 0.1798829276988051.
\end{aligned}
$$
The observed increment is therefore
$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta R^2 &= 0.05339906007191191,\\
\Delta \mathrm{DL}_{\mathrm{bits}} &= 19.089327473282296.
\end{aligned}
$$
Both within-panel and matched-null tests reported the same finite-tail value,
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{\mathrm{within}} &= 0.004975124378109453,\\
p_{\mathrm{matched}} &= 0.004975124378109453.
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the chapter-specific readout is a positive protein-output increment for this engineered mRFP synonym panel: after the declared controls, the residual window coordinate improves prediction of the measured output by about $5.34$ percentage points of held-out variance and by $19.09$ description-length bits.

\paragraph{Real-CAI audit.}
The same run tested whether ordinary codon-adaptation weighting collapses the signal. The correlation between the real \emph{E. coli} CAI surface and the proxy coordinate was only
$$
\begin{aligned}
\rho_{\mathrm{CAI,proxy}} &= 0.17884920449487354,
\end{aligned}
$$
and the audit reported $\mathrm{real\_cai\_absorbs}=\mathrm{false}$ and $\mathrm{real\_cai\_robust}=\mathrm{true}$. This is the key finite-row separation: the protein-output increment is not explained away by replacing the local residual coordinate with the real CAI baseline used in the audit. The result should nevertheless be read narrowly. It supports a synonym-window protein-output association in this engineered mRFP assay; it does not by itself identify the biochemical step that carries the effect.

\paragraph{Boundary against a five-prime-only explanation.}
A paired boundary audit on the same Nieuwkoop mRFP family used the same $1459$ variants and $B=200$ blocks. In that audit, the downstream-window contribution beyond the start-proximal block was negative:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta R^2_{\mathrm{downstream}} &= -0.001126409133973234,\\
\Delta \ell_{\mathrm{downstream}} &= -2.082967365015851,\\
p_{\mathrm{block}} &= 0.1691542288557214.
\end{aligned}
$$
The start-proximal increment itself was essentially zero at
$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta R^2_{\mathrm{start}} &= 1.7624020537495221\times 10^{-9},\\
\Delta \ell_{\mathrm{start}} &= 3.26370933437133\times 10^{-6}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The baseline-to-intermediate step in that boundary check moved from $R^2=0.06748192482378434$ to $R^2=0.6060702157093043$, but the subsequent residual-window addition gave $R^2=0.6049438075310105$ and was not sign-stable. This companion audit prevents over-reading the mRFP protein-output result as a simple universal five-prime rule. The positive protein-output increment in the main run is real within its finite assay, but its localization and mechanistic interpretation remain constrained.

\paragraph{Relation to the wider translation-mediated matrix.}
A separate multi-organism protein-abundance decomposition gives useful scale but is not the evidence source for the mRFP perturbation claim. In that matrix, the \emph{E. coli} entry joined $3739$ proteins, used $9$ residual coordinates, and reported a modeled-tAI-mediated fraction
$$
\begin{aligned}
M^{QTP}_{\mathrm{E. coli}} &= 0.612922252793667,\\
R^2_{QP} &= 0.10767526677985896,\\
R^2_{QP\mid T} &= 0.04167869969498872,\\
R^2_{\mathrm{indirect}} &= 0.06599656708487024.
\end{aligned}
$$
The same matrix recorded $X$-explained energy on modeled translation readout $0.256128714706293$, $X$-explained energy on protein abundance $432.89050065533684$, and residual degrees of freedom $3716$ after $23$ ranked control columns. These numbers show that the broader $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ family can be placed on a controlled $Q \to T \to P$ statistical surface, but the mRFP perturbation result is stronger in one respect and weaker in another: it is experimentally local and synonymous, yet it lacks a same-row ribosome or translation-efficiency measurement. The finite evidence therefore licenses protein-output association under the assay controls, not a completed translation-mediated mechanism.

\paragraph{Translation-contact status.}
The available modeled translation-readout contact supplies only bounded support. The modeled tAI computation used three organisms, all with sense-codon weights present, and produced $81$ entries above the coupling threshold $0.01$ with maximum absolute modeled coupling $3801961.031607896$. The same audit explicitly marked $3$ organisms as insufficient for powered survival and reported the layer as modeled underpowered rather than ribosome-seq or proteomics validation. Consequently, the Nieuwkoop mRFP result is not promoted to measured translation realization. It remains a protein-output perturbation contact whose downstream interpretation requires a separate same-row translation readout.

\paragraph{Admissible conclusion.}
The finite conclusion is that, in the $1459$-variant \emph{E. coli} mRFP synonymous-window panel, the orthogonalized $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ window coordinate improves controlled prediction of measured protein output: $\Delta R^2=0.05339906007191191$, $\Delta \mathrm{DL}=19.089327473282296$ bits, and both reported finite-tail tests have $p=0.004975124378109453$. The real-CAI audit does not absorb the effect, with $\rho_{\mathrm{CAI,proxy}}=0.17884920449487354$ and robustness retained. The result is therefore suitable as a local protein-output readback for this engineered synonymous perturbation assay.

\paragraph{Local no-promotion rule.}
No stronger biological layer follows from these rows alone. The chapter does not claim observed translation efficiency, ribosome occupancy, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, organismal phenotype, biochemical mechanism, selection, or universality. A direct $Q_i \to P_k$ association is not enough to certify a $Q_i \to T_j \to P_k$ chain unless a separate same-row translation contact supplies a surviving $Q_i \to T_j$ step under amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, coding-sequence length, M-density, matched mRNA abundance, and simpler-baseline controls. Until such rows are present, the mRFP assay stays at controlled synonymous protein-output association.
