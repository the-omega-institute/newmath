\subsection{Nieuwkoop E. coli mRFP Synonymous-Window Protein Output Readback}\label{sec:bioreality-nieuwkoop-ecoli-mrfp-protein-output}

\paragraph{Finite row surface.}
This record concerns a finite synonymous-perturbation surface in the Nieuwkoop \emph{E. coli} mRFP experiment.  The observed rows are not species-level codon-usage summaries: they are variant-level synonymous coding changes scored against a protein-output readout.  The registered computation used $1459$ variants and compared a control-only layer $L_2$ against a layer $L_3$ in which the residual synonymous-window component $B_{\perp}$ was admitted after the declared controls.  The control fit had
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2(L_2) &= 0.12648386762689323,\\
R^2(L_3) &= 0.1798829276988051,\\
\Delta R^2 &= 0.05339906007191191,\\
\Delta \mathrm{DL} &= 19.089327473282296\ \mathrm{bits}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The matched and within-null permutation probabilities were both
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{\mathrm{matched}} &= 0.004975124378109453,\\
p_{\mathrm{within}} &= 0.004975124378109453,
\end{aligned}
$$
with $B=200$ null replicates.  The finite verdict is therefore a positive perturbation-level protein-output readback for this experiment: the residual synonymous-window coordinate improves held-out protein-output description after the registered controls.

\paragraph{Coordinate meaning.}
The coordinate tested here is a $B^*_{Q6}$ synonymous-window residual, not an amino-acid substitution coordinate and not a free codon-count direction.  Its admissible biological reading is narrower than a direct geometry-to-protein assertion.  In this chapter it means the following finite relation: among synonymous mRFP variants in \emph{E. coli}, a residual coordinate orthogonalized against the ordinary control surface contributes additional predictive information for protein output.  The readout is a perturbation assay output, so it may support a local synonymous perturbation statement.  It does not by itself identify ribosome dwell time, folding state, molecular activity, cellular fitness, or evolutionary selection.

\paragraph{Real-CAI robustness.}
The run included a real codon-adaptation control audit.  The real CAI proxy was not an absorbing explanation of the $B^*_{Q6}$ window effect:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\operatorname{corr}(\mathrm{real\ CAI},\mathrm{proxy}) &= 0.17884920449487354,\\
\mathrm{real\_cai\_absorbs} &= \mathrm{false},\\
\mathrm{real\_cai\_robust} &= \mathrm{true}.
\end{aligned}
$$
This matters because a synonymous-window protein-output signal could otherwise be only a restatement of ordinary codon-adaptation weighting.  The low proxy correlation and the non-absorption flag keep the result in a residual-coordinate register: the positive $\Delta R^2$ is not licensed merely by conventional CAI direction, but by the finite controlled comparison in the actual mRFP variant table.

\paragraph{Boundary audit on the same variant count.}
A separate five-prime boundary audit used the same $1459$-variant scale and separated start-window and downstream contributions.  The start-window term was essentially null,
$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta R^2_{\mathrm{start}} &= 1.7624020537495221\times 10^{-9},\\
\Delta \ell_{\mathrm{start}} &= 3.26370933437133\times 10^{-6},
\end{aligned}
$$
while the downstream residual addition was not a stable positive explanation:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta R^2_{\mathrm{downstream}} &= -0.001126409133973234,\\
\Delta \ell_{\mathrm{downstream}} &= -2.082967365015851,\\
p_{\mathrm{block}} &= 0.1691542288557214,\\
\mathrm{null95}_{\mathrm{downstream}} &= 0.5254770343527547,\\
\mathrm{sign\_stable} &= \mathrm{false}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The same audit recorded a large improvement from the second modelling layer, with $R^2=0.6060702157093043$ and $\Delta\ell=628.6217154500001$ relative to the first layer, followed by a third-layer decrease to $R^2=0.6049438075310105$.  This boundary information prevents over-reading the positive mRFP result as a generic five-prime law or as a monotone downstream-window law.  The supported statement is local: the registered residual window improves the protein-output fit under its stated control design.

\paragraph{Relation to the broader mediated protein-abundance surface.}
The broader cross-layer protein-abundance audit supplies context but not a substitute for this perturbation result.  In the organism-matched protein-abundance decomposition, the \emph{E. coli} row joined $3739$ proteins, had residual degrees of freedom $3716$ after $23$ control-rank dimensions, and used $9$ $B^*_{Q6}$ coordinates.  Its reported mediated fraction was
$$
\begin{aligned}
M^{QTP}_{\mathrm{E.coli}} &= 0.612922252793667,\\
R^2_{QP} &= 0.10767526677985896,\\
R^2_{QP\mid T} &= 0.04167869969498872,\\
R^2_{\mathrm{indirect}} &= 0.06599656708487024.
\end{aligned}
$$
That organism-level surface uses modelled per-protein tAI and PAXdb abundance, whereas the Nieuwkoop record is a synonymous-variant perturbation assay.  The two surfaces are therefore compatible but not interchangeable.  The mediated abundance table shows that an \emph{E. coli} $Q\to T\to P$ statistical decomposition can be non-vacuous on matched CDS/protein rows; the mRFP result shows a separate finite perturbation contact where synonymous-window residuals improve protein output in an engineered sequence panel.

\paragraph{Admissible claim.}
The finite admissible claim is the following.  In the Nieuwkoop \emph{E. coli} mRFP synonymous-variant table, the $B^*_{Q6}$ residual window contributes a positive protein-output increment after the registered controls: $\Delta R^2=0.05339906007191191$ and $\Delta\mathrm{DL}=19.089327473282296$ bits, with both matched and within-null probabilities equal to $0.004975124378109453$ over $200$ null replicates.  The result remains visible after the real-CAI audit, because the real CAI control does not absorb the effect.  The chapter therefore admits a local protein-output perturbation readback for this finite experiment.

No stronger biological layer follows from this row set alone. It does not establish folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, organismal phenotype, mechanism, selection, or universality.  A direct $Q_i\to P_k$ improvement is not by itself a protein-realization mechanism; it is a controlled association with a protein-output readout in a finite synonymous perturbation panel.  Any higher-layer reading requires its own matched contact and its own finite statistic.
