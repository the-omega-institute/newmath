\subsection{Nieuwkoop \textit{E. coli} mRFP synonymous-window protein-output readback}
\label{sec:bioreality-nieuwkoop-ecoli-mrfp-protein-output}

\paragraph{Finite row and measured response.}
This record concerns a finite perturbation panel of $1459$ synonymous variants in \textit{E. coli} mRFP. The observable response is protein output, not folding, function, fitness, or any organism-level phenotype. The tested coordinate is the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ window residual after the sequence controls have already absorbed the ordinary coding-sequence baselines available in the row. The fitted comparison reports a control-only level
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{L2} &= 0.12648386762689323,
\end{aligned}
$$
and a residual-augmented level
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{L3} &= 0.1798829276988051.
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the observed residual gain is
$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta R^2 &= 0.05339906007191191,\\
\Delta \mathrm{DL}_{\mathrm{bits}} &= 19.089327473282296.
\end{aligned}
$$
The permutation scale used $B=200$ trials. Both the matched test and the within-panel test give
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{\mathrm{matched}} &= 0.004975124378109453,\\
p_{\mathrm{within}} &= 0.004975124378109453.
\end{aligned}
$$
On this finite panel, the row is therefore a positive protein-output readback for the synonymous-window residual, conditional on the declared controls and the actual panel geometry.

\paragraph{Control interpretation.}
The row explicitly records that real \textit{E. coli} CAI does not absorb the signal: $\mathrm{real\_cai\_absorbs}=\mathrm{false}$ and $\mathrm{real\_cai\_robust}=\mathrm{true}$. The correlation between the real CAI surface and the proxy CAI surface is only $0.17884920449487354$, so the positive residual gain is not being reported as a disguised CAI-only effect. The local readback is narrower: a $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$-derived synonymous-window residual improves prediction of the assayed mRFP protein-output measurement beyond the registered controls in this dataset.

\paragraph{Boundary against a trivial start-window explanation.}
The paired five-prime audit uses the same $1459$ variants and gives a separate check on whether the signal should be collapsed to a start-proximal boundary artifact. In that audit, the second-stage model reaches
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{\mathrm{step2}} &= 0.6060702157093043,
\end{aligned}
$$
from
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{\mathrm{step1}} &= 0.06748192482378434,
\end{aligned}
$$
with $\Delta \ell_{2-1}=628.6217154500001$. After the downstream residual term is added, however, the reported value is
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{\mathrm{step3}} &= 0.6049438075310105,
\end{aligned}
$$
with
$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta R^2_{\mathrm{downstream}} &= -0.001126409133973234,\\
\Delta \ell_{\mathrm{downstream}} &= -2.082967365015851,\\
p_{\mathrm{block}} &= 0.1691542288557214.
\end{aligned}

The start-local coefficient itself is numerically negligible in that audit, with $\Delta R^2_{\mathrm{start}}=1.7624020537495221\times 10^{-9}$ and $\Delta \ell_{\mathrm{start}}=3.26370933437133\times 10^{-6}$. The audit also records $\mathrm{sign\_stable}=\mathrm{false}$. This does not erase the positive mRFP protein-output row above; it prevents interpreting the row as a stable universal five-prime law or as a clean start-boundary mechanism.

\paragraph{Relation to translation-conditioned protein abundance.}
A broader finite protein-abundance decomposition gives a useful calibration for what this mRFP perturbation row may and may not be asked to carry. In the cross-organism $Q \to T \to P$ decomposition, the \textit{E. coli} entry has $3739$ joined proteins, $9$ $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ coordinates, control rank $23$, and residual degrees of freedom $3716$. Its total residual-to-protein association is
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{QP} &= 0.10767526677985896,
\end{aligned}
$$
while the direct association after modeled translation readout is
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{QP\mid T} &= 0.04167869969498872.
\end{aligned}
$$
The difference is $0.06599656708487024$, giving a modeled mediation fraction $M^{QTP}=0.612922252793667$. That comparison is statistical mediation using modeled per-protein tAI, not ribosome observation. It supports the local discipline of reading the Nieuwkoop mRFP result as a protein-output perturbation contact with translation-conditioning relevance, while still refusing to identify the coefficient with a direct molecular mechanism.

\paragraph{Admissible conclusion.}
The finite claim is that, in the Nieuwkoop \textit{E. coli} mRFP synonymous-variant panel, the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ synonymous-window residual gives a positive, permutation-supported improvement for protein output: $\Delta R^2=0.05339906007191191$, $\Delta \mathrm{DL}=19.089327473282296$ bits, and $p=0.004975124378109453$ under both matched and within-panel tests. The result is compatible with the wider translation-conditioned protein-abundance route, but it is not itself a proof of ribosome dynamics, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, organismal phenotype, selection, causation, or universality. Any such stronger statement would require a separate layer-matched contact and a finite statistic at that layer. A direct $Q_i \to P_k$ association is therefore allowed here only as a controlled protein-output association for this perturbation panel; it is not promoted to a universal biological rule or to a mechanistic realization claim.
