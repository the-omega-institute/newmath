\paragraph{Biological reading.}
For open-reading-frame eligibility, the scientific role of this packet is narrow.  It gives a finite start/stop-window geometry from which one can formulate a testable eligibility question, but it does not itself observe an open reading frame.  The local seed has no attached sequence database row, transcript evidence, or curated interval annotation in its own contact list.  Therefore the correct biological status is boundary-level: the packet tells us what kind of outside contact would be needed before an ORF-eligibility assertion could be made.

\paragraph{Local boundary.}
The conclusion is therefore deliberately limited.  The finite saturation certificate establishes that $\operatorname{Sat}_{f_3}(R)$ is the $18$-element subset of $M$ obtained by excluding $\mathrm{ACA}$ and $\mathrm{ACG}$, with zero recorded third-face boundary residue and with the quotient checksums stated above.  From this one may build a boundary probe for ORF eligibility. Any such stronger assertion requires an additional contact that supplies the relevant biological interval or measurement and that is evaluated at that stronger layer rather than read off from the finite codon geometry alone.
