\paragraph{Local boundary.}
The finite $f_3$ saturation row therefore has a precise role.  It supplies a codon-table boundary packet that can be compared with start/stop-window eligibility, and it can be joined to a separately supplied curated interval contact.  Before that contact is attached, the codon geometry alone remains a boundary record.
