\paragraph{Finite codon datum.}
The row named \texttt{h0.f3.saturation} records a closed finite codon calculation whose biological use is deliberately narrow. The observed combinatorial carrier is the twenty-element set

The row \texttt{h0.M.equals.WNR.CUN} independently reports $|M|=20$ and lists the same twenty codons as the union assigned to the $WNR$ and $CUN$ classes. These finite equalities are the entire internal substrate used here; they are not, by themselves, an observation of a gene, a transcript, or a translated product.

\paragraph{Saturation calculation.}
The finite saturation check reports
$$
\begin{aligned}
\operatorname{Sat}_{f_3}(R)
&=\{\texttt{AAA},\texttt{AAG},\texttt{AGA},\texttt{AGG},\texttt{AUA},\texttt{AUG},\texttt{CUA},\texttt{CUC},\texttt{CUG},\texttt{CUU},\texttt{UAA},\texttt{UAG},\texttt{UCA},\texttt{UCG},\texttt{UGA},\texttt{UGG},\texttt{UUA},\texttt{UUG}\},\\
M
&=\operatorname{Med}(R)\cup\{\texttt{ACA}\},\\
M\setminus \operatorname{Sat}_{f_3}(R)
&=\{\texttt{ACA},\texttt{ACG}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The same run records the $f_3$ boundary as the empty finite list. Thus the formal content of the row is a saturation relation among enumerated codons: the eighteen saturated codons are all elements of $M$ except \texttt{ACA} and \texttt{ACG}, while the reported median closure together with \texttt{ACA} recovers the full twenty-codon list. This is a codon-enumeration fact, not a biological completion theorem. In particular, the empty boundary value says that no additional boundary codon is recorded by this finite calculation; it does not say that every biological boundary case has been measured.

\paragraph{ORF eligibility boundary.}
The biological object attached to this packet is open-reading-frame eligibility from start and stop codon enumeration. At seed status, the row is only a proposal about what kind of contact would be needed: an interval annotation, sequence observation, or transcript-linked record must be supplied before an ORF eligibility assertion is made. A separate start--stop-window check loaded one finite outside-window manifest, classified one input window by the start/stop enumeration rule, and explicitly kept the scope at \texttt{external\_window\_start\_stop\_enumeration\_only}. That run also recorded \texttt{orf\_eligibility\_only} as the non-promotion marker. Hence the enumeration can say that a window has the start/stop pattern required by the rule, but it cannot turn that window into translation, protein structure, physical viability, biological function, mechanism, or universality.

\paragraph{Curated interval contact.}
The later curated-interval contact supplies the finite biological contact that the seed itself lacked. It loaded \texttt{tools/bio\_reality/data/curated\_orf\_interval\_contact\_dataset.json} with $168$ rows from NCBI RefSeq GenBank XML CDS intervals for \emph{Escherichia coli} K-12 MG1655 accession \texttt{NC\_000913.3}, snapshot date 2026-05-30. The frozen classifier was \texttt{orf\_start\_stop\_window\_eligibility}; the row records that the classifier rules were not modified after the protocol was fixed. The three required input classes were present: boundary cases, matched non-ORF decoys, and positive curated ORFs. In the finite contact audit, $72$ positive intervals were accepted, $72$ matched decoys were rejected, and $24$ boundary cases had zero promotions. The positive exact one-sided binomial upper-tail value was $2.117582368135751\times 10^{-22}$; the matched-decoy rejection value was the same; and the predeclared paired baseline advantage recorded $72$ wins out of $72$ pairs with the same upper-tail value. Three held-out splits were reported: left, middle, and right locus folds, each with $24$ positives accepted at rate $1.0$ and $24$ decoys rejected at rate $1.0$.

\paragraph{Local verdict.}
The finite row is therefore ready as a bounded BioReality record. The codon calculation gives a concrete saturation identity for $R$, $M$, and the two exceptional codons \texttt{ACA} and \texttt{ACG}; the quotient row gives a matching finite coordinate audit; and the curated interval run supplies a separate biological contact for the ORF-eligibility layer. The self-check row records three checked gates and states that no codon geometry is promoted across biological layers. The correct conclusion is restricted: the packet supports ORF-eligibility contact for the curated interval dataset under the frozen start/stop-window rule. It does not establish translation realization, folded protein structure, physical admissibility, biological function, a molecular mechanism, or a general law beyond the stated finite contact.
