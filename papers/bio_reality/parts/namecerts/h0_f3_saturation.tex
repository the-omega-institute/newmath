\paragraph{Local biological boundary.}
The finite codon geometry and its saturation row do not establish translation, folding, physical admissibility, biological function, mechanism, or universality. They also do not turn an arbitrary nucleotide window into an observed open reading frame. Such claims require an independent sequence, transcript, or curated-annotation contact that supplies the biological interval under test. The self-check row records this restriction as a boundary with $3$ checked gates: no codon geometry is promoted across biological layers. Accordingly, the present packet is accepted only as a finite start--stop eligibility boundary together with later, separately scoped contact rows. The calculation tells us what the enumerated geometry permits one to test; it does not tell us that cellular translation, stable protein structure, organismal role, or general biological law has occurred.
