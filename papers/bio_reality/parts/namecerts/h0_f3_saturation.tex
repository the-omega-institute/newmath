\paragraph{ORF-eligibility reading.}
The biological label attached to the conjectural layer is open-reading-frame eligibility from start and stop codon enumeration.  In this chapter that label is read conservatively: a start/stop-window classifier may describe what kind of external interval would have to be checked, but the codon geometry by itself does not assert that an open reading frame exists in an organism or in a curated sequence.  The seed packet has no attached contact rows in its own contact list, and its minimal biological carrier is explicitly unresolved.  Consequently, the saturation equality above is admissible only as a finite boundary condition for a later test.
