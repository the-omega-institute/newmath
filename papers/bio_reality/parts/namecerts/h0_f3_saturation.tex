\subsection{Finite third-face saturation as an ORF-eligibility boundary packet}
\label{subsec:h0-f3-saturation-orf-boundary}

\paragraph{Finite combinatorial content.}
The record named $h0.f3.saturation$ is a finite codon-level saturation statement, not an observed biological interval. Its checked row says that the third-face saturation of $R$ is exactly the twenty-element motif universe $M$ with the two codons $ACA$ and $ACG$ removed. In the same record the finite boundary term is empty. Thus the verified row is the concrete equality
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{Sat}_{f3}(R)&=\{AAA,AAG,AGA,AGG,AUA,AUG,CUA,CUC,CUG,CUU,\\
&\qquad UAA,UAG,UCA,UCG,UGA,UGG,UUA,UUG\},\\
\partial_{f3}&=\varnothing .
\end{aligned}
$$
The associated medial completion statement records
$$
\begin{aligned}
M&=\{AAA,AAG,ACA,ACG,AGA,AGG,AUA,AUG,CUA,CUC,CUG,CUU,\\
&\qquad UAA,UAG,UCA,UCG,UGA,UGG,UUA,UUG\},
\end{aligned}
$$
so that $|M|=20$ and the saturation row omits precisely $ACA$ and $ACG$. The motif-compression row independently lists
$$
\begin{aligned}
R&=\{AAA,AGA,AGG,AUA,CUA,CUC,CUG,CUU,UAA,UAG,UCA,UGA,UUA\},
\end{aligned}
$$
with no missing and no extra entries relative to the stated motif union. These numbers make the packet a finite enumeration claim: it says which codons lie in the third-face saturated set and which two elements of $M$ do not.

\paragraph{Geometric bookkeeping.}
The same finite universe has a quotient geometry row with $M$-vector $(20,38,26,8,1)$ and quotient vector $(10,14,6,1)$. The quotient has ten listed coordinate classes:
$$
\begin{aligned}

This is useful internal bookkeeping for the codon packet, but it is still only a finite codon-coordinate computation. A coordinate class is not a transcript, a translated molecule, a folded structure, a thermodynamic admissibility certificate, a mechanism, a function, or a universal biological law. The BioReality interpretation therefore keeps the finite enumeration separate from any biological layer that would require observation or curated annotation.

\paragraph{ORF-eligibility boundary.}
The biological object attached to this packet is open-reading-frame eligibility from start and stop codon enumeration. The packet's own reality-contact list is empty, and its probes explicitly require a curated ORF interval annotation before an ORF-eligibility claim can be asserted. In local terms, the finite codon computation may specify a start/stop-window test that one could apply, but it does not by itself say that any observed nucleotide interval exists. The support condition is only this: before a separate sequence, transcript, or curated interval contact is attached, the record remains a boundary probe describing what would have to be tested.

\paragraph{Local claim boundary.}
The admissible conclusion for $h0.f3.saturation$ is therefore narrow. The finite row verifies the twenty-element universe $M$, the thirteen-element motif set $R$, the eighteen-element third-face saturation set, the empty third-face boundary, and the listed quotient geometry. It may be paired with a separately observed ORF-interval contact, whose finite dataset and statistics can be reported on their own terms. It does not follow from the saturation row alone that translation occurred, that a protein folded, that a molecule is physically admissible, that a function is present, that a biological mechanism has been identified, or that the pattern is universal. The self-check row records the same gate discipline: three gates were checked, and no codon geometry is promoted across biological layers.
