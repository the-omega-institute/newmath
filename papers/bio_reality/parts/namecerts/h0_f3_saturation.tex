& CUG,CUU,UAA,UAG,UCA,UCG,UGA,UGG,UUA,UUG\}.

with no missing and no extra elements relative to the recorded motif union.  The separate $M$ decomposition row also reports $M=WNR\cup CUN$ as the same $20$ codons.  Thus the saturation row is not an open-ended biological assertion; it is a finite enumeration statement over the displayed codon alphabet.

\paragraph{Saturation calculation.}
The verified $f_3$ row states two concrete equalities.  First,
$$
\begin{aligned}
M=Med(R)\cup\{ACA,ACG\},
\end{aligned}
$$
where the right-hand side is witnessed by the same $20$ codons listed above.  Second, the saturated image omits exactly $ACA$ and $ACG$:
$$
\begin{aligned}
Sat_{f_3}(R)=\{&AAA,AAG,AGA,AGG,AUA,AUG,CUA,CUC,CUG,CUU,\
& UAA,UAG,UCA,UCG,UGA,UGG,UUA,UUG\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The recorded boundary residual is empty: $f3\_boundary\_is\_zero=[]$.  This means that, inside the finite codon packet, the declared saturation boundary has no unaccounted element after the two exceptional codons $ACA$ and $ACG$ are separated.  The statement is about finite symbolic coverage and exclusion; it is not a statement that a molecule is transcribed, translated, folded, tolerated by chemistry, or useful to an organism.

\paragraph{ORF-eligibility boundary.}
The biological object named by this row is open-reading-frame eligibility from start/stop codon enumeration.  In the present packet, the minimal biological carrier is explicitly still waiting for contact with observed reality: the carrier, distinctions, and readback are not populated by sequence evidence in this row, and the row has no attached contact record.  The two probes enforce the same local boundary.  A start/stop-window seed may be retained as a testable boundary item only while it lacks an external interval observation; if the pipeline asserts ORF eligibility, translation, folded structure, physical admissibility, function, mechanism, or universality from the window enumeration alone, the packet is broken.  The row therefore permits the following narrow sentence only: the finite codon computation identifies what would have to be tested by a separate sequence, transcript, or curated-annotation contact.

\paragraph{Local conclusion.}
The $f_3$ saturation packet is ready as a finite codon-boundary record because the displayed lists, the empty boundary residual, the $20$-element ambient set, the $13$-element motif set, the $18$-element saturated set, and the quotient geometry have all been recorded as finite rows. Even when the separate curated interval contact is cited, the result remains ORF-interval eligibility for that dataset and protocol only; it still does not establish translation realization, folded protein structure, physical admissibility, biological function, a causal mechanism, or a universal biological law.
