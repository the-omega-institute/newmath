\paragraph{Self-check boundary.} The self-check row reports $checked\_gate\_count=3$ and states that no codon geometry is promoted across biological layers. Accordingly, this section separates three assertions. The first is the finite saturation equality for $Sat_{f3}(R)$ and $M$. The second is the quotient geometry of the same finite codon row. The third is an externally supplied curated-interval contact that can test ORF eligibility for the stated dataset. None of these assertions licenses a higher biological layer without a separate contact of the corresponding kind. In particular, an eligible start/stop window is not evidence that translation occurred; translated sequence is not evidence of folding; folding is not evidence of physical admissibility in a cellular context; physical admissibility is not biological function; and a finite curated dataset is not a universal biological law.
