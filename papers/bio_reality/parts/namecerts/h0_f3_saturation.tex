\subsection{Saturation Boundary for Start--Stop ORF Eligibility}
\label{sec:h0-f3-saturation-orf-eligibility-boundary}

\paragraph{Finite codon record.}
The row with claim identifier \texttt{h0.f3.saturation} records a finite codon computation that is relevant to open-reading-frame eligibility only as a boundary-level datum.  The motif-compression record gives the finite set
$$
\begin{aligned}
R = \{&\texttt{AAA},\texttt{AGA},\texttt{AGG},\texttt{AUA},\texttt{CUA},\texttt{CUC},\texttt{CUG},\texttt{CUU},\texttt{UAA},\texttt{UAG},\\
&\texttt{UCA},\texttt{UGA},\texttt{UUA}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The companion membership record identifies the twenty-element set
$$
\begin{aligned}
M = \{&\texttt{AAA},\texttt{AAG},\texttt{ACA},\texttt{ACG},\texttt{AGA},\texttt{AGG},\texttt{AUA},\texttt{AUG},\texttt{CUA},\texttt{CUC},\\
&\texttt{CUG},\texttt{CUU},\texttt{UAA},\texttt{UAG},\texttt{UCA},\texttt{UCG},\texttt{UGA},\texttt{UGG},\texttt{UUA},\texttt{UUG}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
This is the same finite list reported as the union of the WNR and CUN classes, with cardinality $20$.  The saturation row then states that the $f_3$ saturation of $R$ reaches
$$
\begin{aligned}
\operatorname{Sat}_{f_3}(R) = \{&\texttt{AAA},\texttt{AAG},\texttt{AGA},\texttt{AGG},\texttt{AUA},\texttt{AUG},\texttt{CUA},\texttt{CUC},\texttt{CUG},\\
&\texttt{CUU},\texttt{UAA},\texttt{UAG},\texttt{UCA},\texttt{UCG},\texttt{UGA},\texttt{UGG},\texttt{UUA},\texttt{UUG}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus $\operatorname{Sat}_{f_3}(R)$ has $18$ listed codons and differs from $M$ exactly by the two excluded codons $\texttt{ACA}$ and $\texttt{ACG}$.  The same row records $M=\operatorname{Med}(R)\cup\{\texttt{ACA}\}$, again yielding the twenty codons displayed above, and the reported $f_3$ boundary residue is the empty list.  These are finite enumeration facts, not biological realization facts.

\paragraph{Geometric scope.}
The quotient-geometry companion record gives an additional check on the same finite coordinate family: the $M$ vector is $(20,38,26,8,1)$, while the quotient vector is $(10,14,6,1)$ and the quotient has $10$ listed classes.  These numbers make the row suitable for a bounded combinatorial account of which codon symbols are present after the stated closure operation.  They do not by themselves identify an observed genomic interval, an expressed transcript, a translated peptide, or a protein product.  The finite enumeration can say that a start and stop pattern is syntactically eligible for a later biological test; it cannot say that any organism realizes that pattern as an open reading frame.

\paragraph{ORF eligibility boundary.}
For the BioReality reading, the biological object is open-reading-frame eligibility derived from start and stop codon enumeration.  The row is therefore kept at the eligibility layer.  A start--stop window can be counted only as a candidate interval whose biological status remains undecided until an independent sequence, transcript, or curated annotation contact is supplied.  The linked window-enumeration check is deliberately narrow: it reports that an external window dataset was loaded, with $1$ input window and $1$ classified window, under the scope string \texttt{external\_window\_start\_stop\_enumeration\_only}, using the snapshot date \texttt{2026-05-25}.  Its own recorded non-promotion value is \texttt{orf\_eligibility\_only}.  This confirms the intended interpretation: the enumeration classifies the window format, not the biological fate of the molecule.

\paragraph{Local cannot-claim boundary.}
The present saturation row and the curated contact row must not be collapsed into one claim.  The saturation computation describes finite codon closure behavior.  The curated interval contact describes an external annotation test with its own frozen protocol: classifier rules unmodified, baseline classes predeclared, held-out splits fixed, protocol frozen at \texttt{2026-05-29T19:11:13+00:00}, and external evidence observed at \texttt{2026-05-30T10:29:31+00:00}.  Even after that contact, the permitted conclusion remains local to ORF interval eligibility.  No translation event, folded structure, physical admissibility, molecular mechanism, biological function, genome-wide law, or universal rule follows from these finite codon and interval records unless another independent contact is introduced for that higher layer.  The explicit role of the row is therefore to preserve the boundary: start--stop codon geometry may define a testable eligibility class, but biological promotion requires evidence outside the finite codon enumeration itself.
