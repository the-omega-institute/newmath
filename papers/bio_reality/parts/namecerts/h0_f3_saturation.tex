\paragraph{Local scientific boundary.}
The conclusion of this chapter is deliberately narrow.  The finite rows establish a codon-enumeration computation: $R$ has $13$ listed codons, $M$ has $20$ listed codons, $\operatorname{Sat}_{f_3}(R)$ has the $18$ listed codons obtained by excluding $\mathrm{ACA}$ and $\mathrm{ACG}$ from $M$, and the recorded $f_3$ boundary ledger is empty. Any such stronger statement requires a separate contact that supplies the relevant biological observation and a separate analysis confined to that contact's declared scope.
