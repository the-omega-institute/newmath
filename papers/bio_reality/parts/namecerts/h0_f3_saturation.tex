\subsection{The $f_3$ saturation boundary for ORF eligibility}\label{subsec:h0-f3-saturation}\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite codon row.} The row fixes a finite RNA-codon calculation whose immediate object is not a biological open reading frame, but a saturation boundary around a codon subset. The verified ambient list is
$$\begin{aligned}
M=\{&AAA,AAG,ACA,ACG,AGA,AGG,AUA,AUG,CUA,CUC,CUG,CUU,\\
&UAA,UAG,UCA,UCG,UGA,UGG,UUA,UUG\},
\end{aligned}$$
with $|M|=20$. The motif-compressed row is
$$\begin{aligned}
R=\{&AAA,AGA,AGG,AUA,CUA,CUC,CUG,CUU,UAA,UAG,UCA,UGA,UUA\},
\end{aligned}$$
and the finite audit reports no missing and no extra codons for this $R$-motif presentation. Thus the row is a controlled enumeration problem: $M$ is the twenty-codon support under consideration, while $R$ is the thirteen-codon motif list from which the $f_3$ closure calculation is tested.

\paragraph{Saturation certificate.} The verified saturation row states that the $f_3$ saturation of $R$ is precisely
$$\begin{aligned}
\operatorname{Sat}_{f_3}(R)=\{&AAA,AAG,AGA,AGG,AUA,AUG,CUA,CUC,CUG,CUU,\\
&UAA,UAG,UCA,UCG,UGA,UGG,UUA,UUG\}.
\end{aligned}$$
Equivalently, the saturation fills the listed codons of $M$ except $ACA$ and $ACG$. The same run records the reconstruction identity for the full twenty-codon list:
$$\begin{aligned}

&UAA,UAG,UCA,UCG,UGA,UGG,UUA,UUG\},

The scientific content of the row is therefore a finite saturation statement: within this enumerated coordinate system, the $f_3$ process recovers eighteen of the twenty listed codons, and the audit gives an empty residual boundary for the tested saturation computation.

\paragraph{Non-promotion boundary.} Nothing in this row establishes translation, folding, physical admissibility, biological function, mechanism, or universality. The saturation identity $\operatorname{Sat}_{f_3}(R)=M\setminus\{ACA,ACG\}$ is a finite codon-coordinate fact. The one-window start/stop audit is an eligibility classification under supplied interval data. The curated interval audit is an external annotation contact for ORF-interval eligibility in the stated dataset. None of these facts shows that a transcript is translated, that a protein product is stable, that a fold is realized, that a biochemical mechanism acts, that a phenotype follows, or that the observed finite pattern extends to all genomes. Any such claim would require an additional contact targeted to that higher biological layer.
