\subsection{F3 saturation as an ORF-eligibility boundary record}\label{subsec:h0-f3-saturation-orf-eligibility-boundary}

\paragraph{Finite codon packet.}
This record concerns the layer named $\mathrm{orf\_eligibility}$, but its first role is deliberately weaker than an ORF assertion.  The finite codon calculation supplies a closed enumeration on codon strings and records where an external biological interval would have to enter before an eligibility claim could be made.  The motif compression row verifies
$$
\begin{aligned}
R = \{&\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UUA}\},
\end{aligned}
$$
with no missing or additional elements.  The ambient $M$ row has cardinality $20$ and is exactly
$$
\begin{aligned}
M = \{&\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{ACA},\mathrm{ACG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\\
&\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The same row records $M=\mathrm{WNR}\cup\mathrm{CUN}$, so the packet is not a vague codon sample: it is a fixed finite set whose membership is already determined by the verified enumeration.

\paragraph{Saturation computation.}
The $f_3$ saturation check verifies that the mediated closure of $R$ together with $\mathrm{ACA}$ recovers all of $M$:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{Med}(R)\cup\{\mathrm{ACA}\}=M.
\end{aligned}
$$
The same experiment reports
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{Sat}_{f_3}(R)=\{&\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\\
&\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus $\mathrm{Sat}_{f_3}(R)$ has $18$ listed elements and is $M$ with $\mathrm{ACA}$ and $\mathrm{ACG}$ absent.  The boundary residue for this finite check is empty, recorded as $\varnothing$.  This empty residue is a statement about the codon enumeration and the selected closure operation only.  It is not a statement that any nucleotide interval is present in an organism, transcribed, translated, folded, chemically admissible as a product, functional, mechanistic, or universal.

These numbers are useful for auditing the internal combinatorial packet, because the enumeration, saturation row, and quotient row agree on a finite object.  They do not by themselves create a biological interval.  The quotient data describe how the codon packet is organized; they do not observe an open reading frame in a genome or transcript.

\paragraph{Contact gap and scoped external check.}
The seed state for $\mathrm{orf\_eligibility}$ remains a boundary state until a separate biological interval contact is supplied.  A derived probe therefore asks whether the pipeline ever asserts ORF eligibility, translation, folded structure, physical admissibility, function, mechanism, or a general biological law from the start/stop-window enumeration alone.  The one-window start/stop enumeration row reports one loaded external nucleotide-window manifest, one input window, and one classified window, with scope restricted to external window start/stop enumeration only.  Its own field states the non-promotion result as $\mathrm{orf\_eligibility\_only}$.

A stronger curated interval contact is present elsewhere in the finite data.  It uses NCBI RefSeq GenBank XML CDS intervals from \textit{Escherichia coli} K-12 MG1655 accession $\mathrm{NC\_000913.3}$, with snapshot date $2026$-$05$-$30$ and $168$ rows.  The required input classes are present: boundary cases, matched non-ORF decoys, and positive curated ORFs.  The contact row reports $72$ accepted positives out of $72$, $72$ rejected matched decoys out of $72$, and $24$ boundary cases with $0$ promoted.  The positive interval exact one-sided binomial value is $2.117582368135751\times 10^{-22}$, the matched decoy rejection value is also $2.117582368135751\times 10^{-22}$, and the predeclared paired baseline sign-test advantage has $72$ wins in $72$ pairs with the same reported value.  Three held-out splits are recorded; each split has $24$ positives, $24$ decoys, positive accept rate $1.0$, decoy reject rate $1.0$, and pass status true.

\paragraph{Local interpretation.}
The finite codon computation is therefore admissible as a precise boundary record: $R$ is fixed, $M$ is fixed with $20$ codons, $\mathrm{Sat}_{f_3}(R)$ is the listed $18$-codon subset, and the finite boundary residue is empty.  The curated interval contact gives a separate external test for scoped ORF-interval eligibility over its declared dataset.  The two roles must remain distinct.  The codon geometry tells us where an interval-contact question can be posed; the curated contact tells us how that question behaved on the recorded external rows.  Neither role licenses promotion to realized translation, protein folding, physical admissibility, biological function, causal mechanism, or universality without an additional contact designed for that stronger layer.

\paragraph{Self-audit boundary.}
The self-check row records $3$ checked gates and states that no codon geometry is promoted across biological layers.  This is the governing interpretation of the present subsection.  The packet is ready as a finite, scoped record for $\mathrm{orf\_eligibility}$ boundary auditing, but it remains intentionally silent about every stronger biological layer unless another independently declared contact supplies the missing observation.
