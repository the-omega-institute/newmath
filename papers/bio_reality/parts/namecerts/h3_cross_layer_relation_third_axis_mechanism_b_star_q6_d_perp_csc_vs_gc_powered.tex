\subsection{The $f_3$-stress coordinate as a third-axis selection boundary}\label{subsec:bioreality-q6-f3-stress-third-axis-mechanism}

\paragraph{Finite row.}
This record concerns the finite coding-sequence surface obtained after amino-acid projection, restricted to the $B^\ast_{Q6}$ residual coordinate called $f_3$-stress and to matched rows that can read codon usage, tRNA or wobble weights, and translation-associated measurements without merging those layers.  The underlying $f_3$ saturation row is completely finite.  The marked set $M=\mathrm{Med}_R\cup\{\mathrm{ACA}\}$ is
$\{\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{ACA},\mathrm{ACG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\}$.
The $f_3$ saturation of $R$ removes exactly the ACA and ACG entries, giving
$\{\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\}$,
with empty $f_3$ boundary.  Thus the coordinate is not being introduced as an open-ended genomic theme; it is a specified residual contrast on a fixed codon row after quotienting away the amino-acid identity.

\paragraph{Mechanism test encoded by the row.}
The powered third-axis assay ran the experiment $\mathrm{b\_star\_q6\_third\_axis\_mechanism\_powered}$ on $11$ computed organisms out of $16$ requested organisms, with $5$ folds, at least $500$ proteins per organism, and $41$ synonymous design columns.  Its deterministic seed was $\mathrm{sha256{:}b\_star\_q6\_third\_axis\_mechanism\_powered{:}deterministic}$.  The reported conclusion is selection-not-GC, not-wobble-clustered, with the CSC or positional identity still requiring an additional contact.  The finite reason attached to the row is that the GC-orthogonalized $d_{\perp}$ coordinate keeps significant cross-species alignment and held-out $R^2$.

\paragraph{Third-axis identity.}
The companion identity row for the same residual axis used $11$ computed organisms out of $16$ requested organisms, $5$ folds, at least $500$ proteins per organism, and $41$ synonymous design columns.  It provisionally aligned $d_{\perp}$ with usage frequency within synonymous families, with mean cosine $0.128$ and one-sided sign-test $p=0.000488$.  In formula form, the finite read is only
$$\begin{aligned}
 d_{\perp} &\leadsto \text{usage-frequency-within-family candidate},\\
 \overline{\cos}(d_{\perp},d_{\mathrm{usage}}) &= 0.128,\\
 p_{\mathrm{sign}} &= 0.000488.
\end{aligned}$$
This supports a third residual direction after the tRNA and $f_3$ span but does not, by itself, identify a biochemical actuator.  It says that the residual direction is consistently oriented in the powered panel and remains visible after the GC control used in the mechanism assay.

\paragraph{Relation to adjacent finite rows.}
The broader $B^\ast_{Q6}$ panel supplies additional constraints on the interpretation.  The two-axis closure row, again on $11$ computed organisms out of $16$ requested organisms with $5$ folds and $41$ synonymous design columns, concluded that a third axis is present because $d_{\perp}$ has systematically positive held-out $R^2$ and mutually same-signed directions across organisms.  The residual optimality row on the same organism and fold counts reported that the non-tRNA residual optimality aligns most with $f_3$ translation selection.  The three-axis sufficiency row, also with $11$ computed organisms out of $16$ requested organisms, concluded that a fourth mechanism is present, so this chapter cannot collapse all remaining codon-usage structure into the third axis.  The per-organism axis-strength spectrum reported $11$ computed organisms, $5$ folds, and $41$ synonymous design columns; it was led by $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$ on average, then $d_{\mathrm{tRNA}}$, with per-organism dominant counts $\{d_{\mathrm{resid4}}:11\}$, imbalanced domain counts $\{\mathrm{archaea}:1,\mathrm{bacteria}:2,\mathrm{eukaryote}:8\}$, and largest GC3 rank effect $\rho=0.136$ for $d_{\mathrm{usage}}$.  These rows keep the third-axis claim narrow: the present row excludes a pure GC explanation for $d_{\perp}$ under the declared assay, while the surrounding rows show that additional residual structure remains.

\paragraph{Translation-readout condition.}
The biological conjecture attached to this coordinate is conditional.  The $f_3$-stress coordinate should become a translation-readout distinction only in organisms or contexts where context tRNA or wobble weights break the third-position $f_3$ symmetry, so that $w_c\ne w_{c^{f_3}}$.  The permitted bounded entry is a controlled survival score $S_{f3\text{-}stress,T_j}$ for a tAI, stAI, ribosome-occupancy, or translation-efficiency row after amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, length, $M$-density, mRNA, and simpler codon baselines have been held fixed.  The boundary-set tRNA-poverty row gives a separate finite pressure in this direction: on $40$ computed organisms out of $46$ requested organisms, with $6$ skipped organisms and $1000$ permutations at $\alpha=0.05$, R sense codons were reported as systematically tRNA-poor under GtRNAdb tGCN plus dos Reis wobble weights.  That row is compatible with a tRNA-supply interpretation for R avoidance, but it does not fill the missing same-row translation survival matrix for this $f_3$-stress coordinate.

\paragraph{Local no-promotion rule.}
The chapter therefore records a finite selection boundary, not a completed biological pathway.  A positive claim about observed translation requires a separate organism-matched translation contact and a non-vacuous controlled $S_{f3\text{-}stress,T_j}$ entry.  Folding, physical admissibility, mature protein abundance, localization, stability, molecular function, phenotype, biochemical mechanism, and universality require their own layer-matched contacts and statistics.  In particular, an all-$1.0$ three-organism rank-degenerate matrix would be a saturation boundary rather than evidence, and modeled tAI or stAI weights alone are not ribosome occupancy.  The present row is ready as a BioReality chapter because it gives a concrete finite residual coordinate, exact finite codon support, powered organism and fold counts, the $p=0.000488$ third-axis identity signal, and the GC-orthogonalized selection-not-GC mechanism exclusion, while explicitly leaving direct translation survival and downstream biology unclaimed.
