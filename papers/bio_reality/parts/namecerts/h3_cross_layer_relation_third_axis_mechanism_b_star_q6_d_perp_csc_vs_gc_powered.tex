\subsection{The $B^{*}_{Q6}$ third-axis stress row under GC and wobble controls}\label{sec:bioreality-q6-f3-stress-third-axis-mechanism}

\paragraph{Finite carrier.}
The record considered here is the finite coding-sequence residual surface obtained after amino-acid projection, restricted to the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ coordinate in which the third-position involution $f_{3}$ is tested against matched translation-layer readouts.  The row is not a claim about protein output or organismal consequence.  It is a bounded coordinate test: after ordinary compositional controls, an $f_{3}$-paired synonymous imbalance may be carried forward only if an organism-scoped translation readout records a non-vacuous survival entry for the same residual coordinate.

The finite $f_{3}$ saturation datum fixes the codon side of the carrier.  In the stored run $76d3dc408a57$, the set $M=\mathrm{Med}(R)\cup\{\mathrm{ACA}\}$ is
$$
\begin{aligned}
M=\{&\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{ACA},\mathrm{ACG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\\
&\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The saturated third-position image removes the two exceptional entries and leaves
$$
\begin{aligned}
\operatorname{Sat}_{f_{3}}(R)=\{&\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\\
&\mathrm{CUU},\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The reported $f_{3}$ boundary set is empty.  Thus the codon side supplies an eighteen-entry saturated surface inside a twenty-entry ambient list, while the two named exceptions remain visible as finite coordinates rather than being absorbed into a continuous or asymptotic argument.

\paragraph{Controlled third-axis evidence.}
The powered third-axis run $d0893337f0dd$ computed the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ third-axis mechanism test on $11$ organisms out of $16$ requested organisms, with $5$ folds, at least $500$ proteins per organism, and $41$ synonymous-design columns.  Its stored classification is
$$
\begin{aligned}
&\text{selection-not-GC;}\\
&\text{not-wobble-clustered;}\\
&\text{CSC/positional identity needs separate observation}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The reason recorded for this call is that the GC-orthogonalized $d_{\perp}$ coordinate retains significant cross-species alignment and held-out predictive signal.  This is a negative control against the simplest GC explanation: the third coordinate is not exhausted by GC3 once the residual axis has been orthogonalized.  It is also a negative control against a pure wobble-cluster interpretation: the observed third-axis signal should not be promoted to a wobble mechanism merely because third-position codon structure is present.

The companion identity run $377671f96e96$ used the same $5$-fold, $11$-of-$16$ organism, $500$-protein, $41$-column design and provisionally aligned $d_{\perp}$ with usage frequency within synonymous family.  The finite scalar attached to that identity call is mean cosine $0.128$ with one-sided sign-test $p=0.000488$.  Taken together, the mechanism and identity rows say that the third axis is reproducibly visible as a residual usage-frequency direction, that this visibility is not reduced to GC, and that a more specific CSC or positional account still requires an additional layer-matched contact.

\paragraph{Translation-readout gate.}
For this chapter the readout variable is not downstream abundance, folding, localization, function, phenotype, physical admissibility, or a universal law.  The only admissible readback is a controlled survival entry
$$
\begin{aligned}
S_{f_{3}\text{-stress},T_{j}}>0
\end{aligned}
$$
for a declared translation readout $T_{j}$, such as organism-scoped tAI, stAI, ribosome occupancy, or translation efficiency, after controls for amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, coding-sequence length, $M$-density, matched mRNA abundance when used, rank, and simpler codon baselines.  If the entry is non-positive, rank-degenerate, or absent, the biological interpretation stops at the coordinate row.

The boundary-set tRNA-poverty experiment $d7728cc8768e$ supplies a related but still bounded contact: it computed $40$ organisms out of $46$ requested, skipped $6$, used $1000$ permutations at $\alpha=0.05$, and reported that $R$ sense codons are systematically tRNA-poor under GtRNAdb gene-copy counts plus dos Reis wobble weights.  This supports a tRNA-supply interpretation for the $R$-avoidance side of the surface, but it does not itself compute $S_{f_{3}\text{-stress},T_{j}}$ for a matched translation-efficiency or ribosome-occupancy row.  The distinction matters: tRNA-copy and wobble-weight asymmetry can define where the $f_{3}$ symmetry is plausibly broken, but a translation survival claim needs the joined readout statistic.

\paragraph{Rank and axis context.}
The broader powered panel places the present coordinate in a finite residual-axis system rather than in an isolated codon anecdote.  The axis-strength spectrum run $0146012d8909$ computed $11$ organisms from $16$ requested, again with $5$ folds, $500$ proteins per organism, and $41$ synonymous-design columns.  Its spectra were led by $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$ on average, followed by $d_{\mathrm{tRNA}}$; the per-organism dominant count was $\{d_{\mathrm{resid4}}:11\}$.  Domain summaries were explicitly descriptive under the imbalanced domain counts $\{\text{archaea}:1,\text{bacteria}:2,\text{eukaryote}:8\}$, and the largest GC3 rank effect reported there was $d_{\mathrm{usage}}$ with $\rho=0.136$.

Two further finite rows locate the third axis in the optimality surface.  The run $2a526c9fb7f4$ reported that the non-tRNA residual optimality axis aligns most with $f_{3}$ translation selection, under the same $11$-organism, $5$-fold, $500$-protein, $41$-column design.  The run $99dc7e2d5abf$ reported a third-axis-present call because $d_{\perp}$ had systematically positive held-out $R^{2}$ and mutually same-signed directions across organisms.  The sufficiency test $2436cae9d455$ then reported a fourth-mechanism-present conclusion, meaning that the three-axis surface is not the end of the residual decomposition.  These rows constrain the present chapter to a narrow conclusion: the $f_{3}$ stress coordinate belongs to a reproducible residual-axis geometry, but its translation-layer biological status is conditional on a direct readout row.

\paragraph{Local falsification rule.}
The claim is supported only in the following bounded sense.  There must be at least one organism or context in which $f_{3}$-paired synonymous imbalance survives amino-acid quotienting and the same rows have a nondegenerate, positive translation-readout statistic after the declared controls.  It is falsified at this layer if the controlled entries remain non-positive across a powered panel, or if the apparent entries collapse to rank saturation, such as an all-$1.0$ surface on a three-organism design.  Modeled tAI or stAI weights may be used as organism-level codon-adaptation readbacks, but they are not observed ribosome occupancy unless a separate measured contact is attached.

\paragraph{No-promotion boundary.}
This chapter deliberately stops before higher biological layers.  The third-axis row does not assert translation realization unless $S_{f_{3}\text{-stress},T_{j}}$ is computed and positive under controls.  It does not assert ribosome-mediated mechanism from codon topology alone.  It does not assert protein abundance, folded structure, localization, stability, molecular function, system phenotype, physical admissibility, or universality.  Each such assertion would need its own layer-matched contact and its own statistic after the translation row has first survived.  The present finite record is therefore a controlled residual-coordinate and readout-gating statement: selection remains after GC control, the third axis is not merely a wobble cluster, and CSC or positional identity is left open until an external readout at that layer is supplied.
