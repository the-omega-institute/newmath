\subsection{Third-position stress as a bounded translation-readout conjecture}
\label{subsec:biorereality-q6-f3-stress-wobble-broken-translation-readout}

\paragraph{Finite carrier.}
This record concerns the finite synonymous residual surface obtained after amino-acid projection, restricted to the $B^*_{Q6}$ coordinate that compares each codon with its third-position $f_3$ partner.  The biological question is deliberately narrow: whether this $f_3$-stress coordinate survives as a distinction in a matched translation readout, after controls for amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, coding-sequence length, $M$-density, and mRNA abundance where a translation-efficiency denominator is used.  The coordinate is not treated as a protein, phenotype, or physical object.  It is a finite row label whose allowed readback is a controlled survival entry $S_{f3\text{-}stress,T_j}$ for a bounded translation-layer row such as tAI, stAI, ribosome occupancy, or translation efficiency.

\paragraph{Third-position saturation boundary.}
The finite $f_3$ saturation audit fixes the codon support on which the stress coordinate is allowed to speak.  The recorded set $M=\operatorname{Med}(R)\cup\{\mathrm{ACA}\}$ contains the $20$ codons
$\{\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{ACA},\mathrm{ACG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\}$.
Its $f_3$ saturation removes only $\mathrm{ACA}$ and $\mathrm{ACG}$ from that list, leaving the $18$-codon support
$\{\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\}$.
The recorded $f_3$ boundary set is empty.  Thus the present chapter does not posit a hidden boundary codon: the finite stress coordinate must earn any biological reading through a separately measured or explicitly bounded translation row.

\paragraph{Observed finite assays.}
The powered third-axis mechanism assay ran with $5$ folds, a minimum of $500$ proteins per organism, $11$ computed organisms out of $16$ requested organisms, and $41$ synonymous design columns.  Its status is passed, and its finite conclusion is: selection-not-GC; not-wobble-clustered; CSC or positional identity still needs an outside contact.  The stated reason is that the GC-orthogonalized $d_{\perp}$ coordinate keeps significant cross-species alignment and held-out $R^2$.  In symbols, the recorded finite classification is the constrained implication
$$
\begin{aligned}
  d_{\perp}\ \perp\ \mathrm{GC3}\quad &\text{does not erase the held-out signal},\\
  d_{\perp}\quad &\text{is not resolved by wobble clustering alone},\\
  \mathrm{CSC/position}\quad &\text{remains an empirical-contact requirement.}
\end{aligned}
$$
This is a positive finite result about the residual axis under the declared design, not a direct observation of ribosome motion.

\paragraph{Axis identity and neighboring rows.}
The neighboring identity audit gives the compatible finite reading of $d_{\perp}$: it is provisionally aligned with usage frequency within synonymous family, with mean cosine $0.128$ and one-sided sign-test $p=0.000488$, again over $5$ folds, $11$ computed organisms out of $16$, at least $500$ proteins per organism, and $41$ synonymous design columns.  The universal residual-axis assay reports that the non-tRNA residual optimality aligns most with $f_3$ translation selection.  The two-axis closure assay reports a third-axis-present result, with the reason that $d_{\perp}$ has systematically positive held-out $R^2$ and mutually same-signed directions across organisms.  The per-organism axis-strength spectrum is led by $d_{resid4}$ on average, followed by $d_{tRNA}$; among the $11$ computed organisms the dominant-count summary is $\{d_{resid4}:11\}$, and the largest GC3 rank effect is $d_{usage}$ with $\rho=0.136$.  These rows support a finite residual-axis story, while keeping the present $f_3$-stress translation claim dependent on the translation-readout row.

\paragraph{Break condition.}
The conjecture is supported only if at least one matched row gives $S_{f3\text{-}stress,T_j}>0$ after the declared controls and simpler-codon-baseline ablation.  It is broken if the controlled entries remain non-positive, or if the matrix is rank-degenerate, for example an all-$1.0$ three-organism surface whose apparent perfection comes from saturation rather than prediction.  A modeled tAI or stAI surface may define a bounded readout class, but it is not the same kind of evidence as a ribosome-profiling or translation-efficiency measurement.  The same distinction applies to mRNA abundance: it is a control surface for translation-efficiency residualization, not itself a translation-output row.

\paragraph{Local no-promotion rule.}
No translation realization beyond the bounded statistic follows from the residual coordinate alone.  No protein abundance, folding, physical admissibility, localization, stability, molecular function, phenotype, adaptation law, or universality follows from codon topology, GC-orthogonalized alignment, tAI or stAI weights, or the passed finite mechanism assay.  Each higher layer requires its own matched empirical contact and its own controlled statistic.  The present chapter therefore records a precise finite status: the $B^*_{Q6}$ third-position residual axis survives GC orthogonalization and is not explained as a wobble cluster in the powered axis assay, but the $f_3$-stress claim becomes a biological translation-readout claim only where a nondegenerate same-row $S_{f3\text{-}stress,T_j}$ entry is computed under the stated controls.
