\paragraph{Finite coordinate surface.}
This record concerns the $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinate obtained after amino-acid projection, with attention restricted to the third-position $f_3$ stress direction and to translation-layer rows that can be matched to the same coding-sequence panel.  The finite saturation check fixes the codon universe used by the packet: the ambient list $M=\operatorname{Med}(R)\cup\{\mathrm{ACA}\}$ contains $20$ codons,
$$\begin{aligned}
M=\{&\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{ACA},\mathrm{ACG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\\
&\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\},
\end{aligned}$$
while the $f_3$-saturated $R$ surface contains $18$ codons,
$$\begin{aligned}
\operatorname{Sat}_{f_3}(R)=\{&\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\\
&\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\}.
\end{aligned}$$
The finite boundary returned by the saturation run is empty, so the two missing members of the ambient list, $\mathrm{ACA}$ and $\mathrm{ACG}$, mark the explicit exclusion rather than an unaccounted boundary.  This is the coordinate side of the claim: it describes where an $f_3$-paired synonymous imbalance can be formed after amino-acid quotienting, but it does not by itself observe translation.

\paragraph{Mechanism test already passed.}
The powered mechanism run for this claim used $5$ folds, required at least $500$ proteins per organism, computed $11$ organisms out of $16$ requested organisms, and used $41$ synonymous design columns.  Its recorded result is that the third residual direction is selection-linked rather than GC-linked: after GC orthogonalization, $d_{\perp}$ keeps significant cross-species alignment and held-out $R^2$.  The finite conclusion is therefore
$$\begin{aligned}
d_{\perp}&:\ \text{selection-not-GC},\\
d_{\perp}&:\ \text{not wobble-clustered},\\
\text{CSC or positional identity}&:\ \text{requires an additional measured contact}.
\end{aligned}$$
This makes the present record a boundary theorem for biological interpretation rather than a completed mechanism assignment.  It says that the third residual coordinate is not explained away by GC content in the powered panel, and that a pure wobble-cluster account is not supported by the finite run.  It also says that the remaining contextual or positional identity is not closed until an external translation row is attached to the same coding sequences.

\paragraph{Relation to the surrounding powered spectrum.}
The adjacent identity run computed the same $11$ of $16$ requested organisms with $5$ folds, the same minimum of $500$ proteins per organism, and the same $41$ synonymous design columns.  It provisionally aligned $d_{\perp}$ with usage frequency within synonymous family, with mean cosine $0.128$ and one-sided sign-test $p=0.000488$.  The residual-optimality run identified the dominant non-tRNA residual optimality direction as $f_3$ translation selection.  The two-axis closure run found that the tRNA-plus-$f_3$ span is insufficient, because $d_{\perp}$ has systematically positive held-out $R^2$ and same-signed organism directions.  The three-axis sufficiency run then reported a fourth mechanism as present, while the per-organism axis spectrum found $11$ of $11$ computed organisms dominated by $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$ on average, with the reported descriptive ordering led by $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$ and then $d_{\mathrm{tRNA}}$.  In that spectrum the largest GC3 rank effect was small, $\rho=0.136$ for $d_{\mathrm{usage}}$, and the domain counts were imbalanced, with $1$ archaeon, $2$ bacteria, and $8$ eukaryotes.  These numbers keep the third-axis mechanism claim finite: it is strong enough to reject a simple GC explanation in this assay, but not strong enough to promote the coordinate into a universal translation law.

\paragraph{Translation-readout condition.}
The only positive biological readback allowed here is a controlled survival entry for a translation-layer row, written locally as $S_{f_3,T_j}$.  The row $T_j$ may be a bounded tAI or stAI surface, or a measured ribosome-occupancy or translation-efficiency surface, provided it is matched to the organism and coding-sequence rows used for the codon coordinates.  A non-vacuous positive entry must be estimated after amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, CDS length, $M$-density, matched mRNA abundance where relevant, rank sufficiency, and simpler codon baselines are controlled.  The intended test is
$$\begin{aligned}
S_{f_3,T_j} &>0
\end{aligned}$$
for at least one admissible translation readout $T_j$ under those controls.  If every controlled entry is non-positive, or if the readout matrix is rank-degenerate, the biological interpretation stops at the coordinate and spectrum level.  In particular, a three-organism all-$1.0$ controlled surface is treated as a saturated null boundary rather than survival.

\paragraph{Wobble-breaking requirement.}
The $f_3$ coordinate becomes biologically dynamical only where a context tRNA or wobble-weight surface distinguishes paired third-position contexts.  In local notation this means that the candidate translation surface must contain a row with
$$\begin{aligned}
w_c\neq w_{c^{f_3}},
\end{aligned}$$
not merely a topological codon pairing.  The $40$-organism tRNA-poverty run supplies relevant background: it computed $40$ organisms out of $46$ requested organisms, skipped $6$, used $1000$ permutations at $\alpha=0.05$, and concluded that $R$ sense codons are systematically tRNA-poor under GtRNAdb tRNA gene-copy numbers with dos Reis wobble weights.  That result supports the plausibility of a tRNA-supply interface for codon avoidance, but it still does not substitute for the same-row $f_3$ translation-readout survival statistic required in this chapter.

\paragraph{Local no-promotion rule.}
The passed mechanism result is therefore read as a finite cross-layer constraint: GC content does not absorb the third residual direction in the powered assay, wobble clustering alone is not the identified explanation, and a contextual or positional translation contact remains necessary.  No claim about translation realization, ribosome occupancy, protein abundance, folding, localization, stability, physical admissibility, molecular function, organismal phenotype, mechanism, causation, or universality follows from the codon coordinate, from the passed GC-orthogonalized run, from modeled tAI or stAI weights, or from contact names alone.  Each higher layer needs its own matched measurement and its own controlled statistic.  The present chapter is ready only as the bounded statement that $B^*_{Q6}$ $f_3$ stress has a finite, powered, non-GC residual signature whose biological readback must pass through a nondegenerate translation row before any downstream interpretation is allowed.
