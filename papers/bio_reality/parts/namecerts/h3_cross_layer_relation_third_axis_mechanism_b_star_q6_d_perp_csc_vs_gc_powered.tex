\subsection{The f\_3-stress coordinate as a translation-readout boundary}\label{sec:bioreality-q6-f3-stress-translation-readout}\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite coordinate surface.}
This record isolates the $B^\ast_{Q6}$ third-position stress coordinate after amino-acid projection. The finite codon surface used by the packet has
$$\begin{aligned}
M &= \{\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{ACA},\mathrm{ACG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\\
&\qquad \mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\},
\end{aligned}$$
with $20$ listed rows. The $f_3$-saturated row set removes the two $\mathrm{ACA}$ and $\mathrm{ACG}$ entries and leaves
$$\begin{aligned}
\operatorname{Sat}_{f_3}(R) &= \{\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\\
&\qquad \mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\},
\end{aligned}$$
with $18$ listed rows and empty $f_3$ boundary residue. Thus the codon-side object is not a free biological assertion: it is a finite residual coordinate surface whose only admissible downstream question is whether a matched translation readout sees a controlled distinction.

\paragraph{Observed finite-row status.}
The powered third-axis mechanism assay completed on $11$ organisms out of $16$ requested organisms, using $5$ folds, a minimum of $500$ proteins per organism, and $41$ synonymous-design columns. Its recorded conclusion is that the third axis is selection-not-GC, not wobble-clustered, and that the CSC or positional identity still needs a separate outside row. The decisive finite observation is that the GC-orthogonalized $d_{\perp}$ coordinate keeps significant cross-species alignment and held-out $R^2$ after GC handling. This makes the third axis incompatible with a purely GC explanation in the assayed panel, but it does not by itself identify a measured translation process.

\paragraph{Relation to the identified third axis.}
The paired identity assay also used $11$ computed organisms out of $16$ requested, $5$ folds, at least $500$ proteins per organism, and $41$ synonymous-design columns. It provisionally aligned $d_{\perp}$ with usage frequency within synonymous families, with mean cosine $0.128$ and one-sided sign-test $p=0.000488$. The two-axis closure assay reported a third axis present because $d_{\perp}$ had systematically positive held-out $R^2$ and mutually same-signed directions across organisms. The residual-optimality assay identified the dominant non-tRNA residual direction as $f_3$ translation selection. Taken together, these finite rows support the following local reading: after amino-acid quotienting, the remaining third-position stress is a real residual coordinate in the rank-powered design, and its most conservative biological test is whether a translation readout survives after the declared controls.

\paragraph{Wobble-breaking trigger.}
The conjectural trigger is not the bare existence of synonymous codons. It is the failure of third-position symmetry in the readout weights: for a codon $c$ and its $f_3$ mate $c^{f_3}$, the translation layer becomes testable only when the relevant tRNA or wobble weight satisfies $w_c \ne w_{c^{f_3}}$. If the weights are symmetric, the $f_3$ coordinate remains a codon-side residual and no translation distinction is licensed. If the weights are asymmetric, the admissible statistic is a controlled survival score for the $f_3$-stress coordinate against a bounded readout such as tAI, stAI, ribosome occupancy, or translation efficiency:
$$\begin{aligned}
S_{f_3,T_j} &> 0
\end{aligned}$$
after amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, coding-sequence length, $M$-density baseline, matched mRNA control where used, rank, and simpler-baseline ablations. The all-$1.0$ three-organism surface is therefore a null rank boundary, not survival evidence.

\paragraph{Panel context.}
The broader axis-strength spectrum gives useful scale. In the $11$ organism panel, $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$ led the captured spectra on average, followed by $d_{\mathrm{tRNA}}$, and the per-organism dominant-count summary assigned all $11$ dominant calls to $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$. The recorded domain composition was imbalanced, with $1$ archaeal, $2$ bacterial, and $8$ eukaryotic rows, so domain-level contrasts are descriptive only. The largest GC3 rank effect in that spectrum was $d_{\mathrm{usage}}$ with $\rho=0.136$. A separate tRNA-poverty assay on the $R$ sense codons computed $40$ organisms out of $46$ requested, skipped $6$, used $1000$ permutations at $\alpha=0.05$, and called the $R$ sense codons systematically tRNA-poor under GtRNAdb tGCN plus dos Reis wobble weights. These rows explain why a tRNA or wobble route is a plausible testing surface, while still leaving the exact $f_3$ translation-readout statistic uncomputed.

\paragraph{Local falsification rule.}
The record is falsified at the translation boundary if every controlled entry remains non-positive or rank-degenerate:
$$\begin{aligned}
\forall T_j,\quad S_{f_3,T_j} \le 0,
\end{aligned}$$
where $T_j$ ranges over the declared translation readouts. It is also not supported if the only apparent signal comes from a saturated low-rank matrix, from codon topology alone, or from modeled adaptation weights without organism-matched readout rows. A positive entry must be non-vacuous under the same controls that define the coordinate test.

\paragraph{Claim boundary.}
This chapter claims only a finite coordinate-and-test relation: the $B^\ast_{Q6}$ $f_3$-stress residual is a controlled candidate for translation-readout survival when third-position wobble or tRNA weights break the $f_3$ pairing. It does not claim translation realization, ribosome occupancy, protein abundance, folded structure, physical admissibility, molecular function, organismal phenotype, mechanism, or universality from the coordinate itself. Each of those stronger readings would need its own matched measurement contact and its own controlled statistic before it could be stated.
