\subsection{Third-position stress as a selection-facing residual coordinate}
\label{sec:h3-cross-layer-third-axis-mechanism-q6-f3-stress}

\paragraph{Finite coordinate surface.}
The record concerns the $B^*_{Q6}$ synonymous residual surface after amino-acid projection, restricted to the $f_3$-stress coordinate and to translation-facing rows for which codon usage, tRNA or wobble weights, and matched readout covariates can be separated. The finite saturation row fixes the relevant codon set before any biological interpretation is made. The marked set $M = \operatorname{Med}(R) \cup \{\mathrm{ACA}\}$ is
$$
\begin{aligned}
M = \{&\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{ACA},\mathrm{ACG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\\
&\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The $f_3$-saturated part removes the two ACA/ACG boundary tokens and is
$$
\begin{aligned}
\operatorname{Sat}_{f_3}(R) = \{&\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\\
&\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The recorded $f_3$ boundary is empty, so the coordinate is not being read as a leftover boundary exception; it is a residual axis inside the finite synonymous design.

\paragraph{Observed finite test.}
The powered mechanism assay used $5$ folds, a minimum of $500$ proteins per organism, $41$ synonymous design columns, and computed $11$ of $16$ requested organisms. Its status is passed. The finite conclusion is: selection-not-GC; not-wobble-clustered; CSC or positional identity still needs an outside measured contact. The recorded reason is that the GC-orthogonalized $d_{\perp}$ direction keeps significant cross-species alignment and held-out $R^2$. Thus the current row rules out a purely GC3 explanation for the third residual axis in this assay, but it does not yet identify a concrete positional ribosome or context-specific codon mechanism.

\paragraph{Relation to the third-axis identity row.}
A neighboring identity assay on the same powered panel also used $5$ folds, a $500$-protein organism threshold, $41$ synonymous design columns, and $11$ of $16$ requested organisms. It provisionally identified $d_{\perp}$ with the candidate axis usage-frequency-within-family, with mean cosine $0.128$ and one-sided sign-test $p = 0.000488$. This supports the interpretation that the third residual coordinate is not merely a codon-topology artifact after amino-acid quotienting. The mechanism row sharpens that reading: the axis remains visible after GC orthogonalization, but its context-specific codon or position-resolved source is not yet supplied by the finite row itself.

\paragraph{Translation-readout condition.}
The intended biological readback is a controlled survival entry $S_{f3\text{-}stress,T_j}$, where $T_j$ may be a tAI or stAI surface, ribosome occupancy, or translation efficiency. The row is admissible only after amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, CDS length, $M$-density, matched mRNA abundance, rank, and simpler codon baselines have been controlled. In symbols, the bounded support condition is
$$
\begin{aligned}
S_{f3\text{-}stress,T_j} > 0
\end{aligned}
$$
for at least one nondegenerate translation readout under those controls. A rank-saturated all-$1.0$ matrix on three organisms is a null boundary, not survival. Likewise, a modeled tAI or stAI weight is a bounded codon-adaptation readout; it is not by itself an observed ribosome footprint, protein-output row, or mechanism measurement.

\paragraph{Cross-row consistency.}
The broader powered panel gives the same caution. The universal residual optimality row reports that the non-tRNA residual optimum aligns most with $f_3$ translation selection, while the two-axis closure row reports a third axis with systematically positive held-out $R^2$ and same-signed $d_{\perp}$ directions across organisms. The per-organism spectrum row computed $11$ organisms and found $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$ leading in all $11$ dominant-count cases, with descriptive domain counts imbalanced as archaea $1$, bacteria $2$, and eukaryote $8$; the largest GC3 rank effect was only $d_{\mathrm{usage}}$ with $\rho = 0.136$. These rows are compatible with the mechanism call: the third axis has reproducible selection-facing structure, but GC3 alone is not the sufficient explanation and the exact codon-context or position-resolved biological source remains open.

\paragraph{Local exclusion boundary.}
This chapter does not claim that $f_3$-stress changes translation, ribosome occupancy, protein abundance, folding, localization, stability, molecular function, phenotype, physical admissibility, mechanism, or a universal biological rule. The present finite result only says that the $B^*_{Q6}$ third residual direction is selection-facing rather than GC-explained in the powered assay, and that it is not resolved as a wobble-clustered mechanism by the available row. Any stronger claim requires a separate layer-matched contact: same-organism and same-CDS codon counts, wobble or tRNA weights, measured translation readouts or an explicitly bounded modeled substitute, matched mRNA controls, and a nondegenerate controlled statistic. Until such a row exists, downstream structure, function, localization, stability, protein abundance, and phenotype interpretations remain outside the claim.
