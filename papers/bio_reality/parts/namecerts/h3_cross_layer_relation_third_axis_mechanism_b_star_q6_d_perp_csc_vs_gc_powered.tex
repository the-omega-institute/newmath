\paragraph{Finite coordinate surface.}
The present record concerns a finite codon-residual surface: coding-sequence rows are projected through amino-acid composition and then inspected along the $B^*_{Q6}$ third-position $f_3$-stress coordinate, with matched translation-readout rows reserved as the only admissible dynamical readback. The relevant saturated $f_3$ codon set is not the full codon alphabet. In the finite saturation audit, the row $M = \operatorname{Med}(R) \cup \{\mathrm{ACA}\}$ contains
$$
\begin{aligned}
M = \{&\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{ACA},\mathrm{ACG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\\
&\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The $f_3$-saturated $R$ row removes the two boundary codons $\mathrm{ACA}$ and $\mathrm{ACG}$, leaving
$$
\begin{aligned}
\operatorname{Sat}_{f_3}(R) = \{&\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\\
&\mathrm{CUU},\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\},
\end{aligned}
$$
and the recorded $f_3$ boundary list is empty. Thus the coordinate has a finite codon-topological definition before any biological readout is invoked. It is a residual coordinate on a controlled finite table, not yet an observed translation effect.

\paragraph{Third-axis mechanism call.}
The powered third-axis mechanism run reports status passed for $11$ computed organisms out of $16$ requested organisms, using $5$ folds, at least $500$ proteins per organism, and $41$ synonymous-design columns. Its finite conclusion is: selection-not-GC; not-wobble-clustered; CSC/positional identity needs external data. The accompanying reason is that the GC-orthogonalized $d_{\perp}$ direction retains significant cross-species alignment and held-out $R^2$. This is a narrow mechanism statement. It says that the third residual axis is not explained away by GC alone in this assay, and it refuses to identify the axis as a wobble-cluster mechanism on the available rows. The same family of finite runs gives the identity-side calibration: $d_{\perp}$ is provisionally aligned to usage frequency within synonymous families, with mean cosine $0.128$ and one-sided sign-test $p = 0.000488$ over the powered organism panel. A separate optimal-residual-axis run names $f_3$ translation selection as the dominant non-tRNA residual direction, while the two-axis closure run reports a third axis present because $d_{\perp}$ has systematically positive held-out $R^2$ and same-signed directions across organisms.

\paragraph{Spectrum and trigger.}
The mechanism record sits inside a larger finite spectrum rather than as an isolated scalar. The axis-strength spectrum computed $11$ organisms out of $16$ requested organisms, again with $5$ folds, at least $500$ proteins per organism, and $41$ synonymous-design columns. In that spectrum, $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$ leads on average, followed by $d_{\mathrm{tRNA}}$; the per-organism dominant count is $\{d_{\mathrm{resid4}}: 11\}$. The domain counts are explicitly imbalanced, with $1$ archaeal, $2$ bacterial, and $8$ eukaryotic organisms, so domain-level averages are descriptive rather than a balanced phylogenetic law. The largest reported GC3 rank effect is small in this table, $\rho = 0.136$ for $d_{\mathrm{usage}}$. The trigger side is also finite: the R-sense tRNA-poverty run used $40$ computed organisms out of $46$ requested, skipped $6$, and used $1000$ permutations at $\alpha = 0.05$. Its mechanism call says that R sense codons are systematically tRNA-poor under GtRNAdb tRNA gene-copy counts plus dos Reis wobble weights. This supplies a plausible tRNA-supply boundary for R avoidance, but it is not the missing same-row translation-readout statistic for the present $f_3$-stress conjecture.

\paragraph{Translation-readout rule.}
The claimed cross-layer question is therefore conditional. After amino-acid projection, an $f_3$-paired synonymous imbalance may become dynamically meaningful only when context tRNA or wobble weights distinguish a codon $c$ from its third-position partner $c^{f_3}$, so that $w_c \ne w_{c^{f_3}}$. The admissible readback is a controlled survival entry $S_{f3\text{-}stress,T_j}$ for a translation-layer row $T_j$, such as tAI, stAI, ribosome occupancy, or translation efficiency. The finite record does not permit a promotion from the codon residual itself to protein abundance, folded structure, localization, stability, function, phenotype, or a general biological law. Such an inference would require a separate layer-matched contact and its own statistic.

\paragraph{Null boundary and falsifier.}
The exact same-row $f_3$-stress translation statistic has not yet been attached. The current memory explicitly lacks joined rows that simultaneously contain codon counts, bounded wobble-breaking tAI or stAI weights, measured ribosome-profiling or translation-efficiency readouts, and matched mRNA controls for the same organism and coding sequence. Consequently, the present chapter is ready as a finite boundary and mechanism-separation record, not as a positive translation-survival result. The falsifier is concrete: compute $S_{f3\text{-}stress,T_j}$ under amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, CDS length, M-density baseline, matched mRNA, rank, and simpler-baseline controls. If the controlled entry remains non-positive or rank-degenerate across a powered panel, the cross-layer conjecture fails at the translation-readout interface. If a non-vacuous positive entry appears, it still licenses only that bounded translation row; translation realization beyond the statistic, folding, physical admissibility, biological function, mechanism, and universality remain outside the claim until independently measured.
