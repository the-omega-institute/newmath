\subsection{Third-position stress as a bounded translation-readout conjecture}
\label{sec:bioreality-q6-f3-stress-third-axis-mechanism}

\paragraph{Finite coordinate being tested.}
The object of this record is the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual coordinate obtained after amino-acid projection, restricted to the third-position $f_{3}$-stress direction and then compared with translation-layer rows. The coordinate is not treated as a protein-output, folding, function, localization, stability, phenotype, or universal-law claim. Its only permitted readout in this chapter is a controlled survival entry $S_{f3\text{-}stress,T_j}$, where $T_j$ is a bounded translation row such as a tAI or stAI weight, a ribosome-occupancy row, or a translation-efficiency row. The finite saturation row fixes the relevant codon support: the recorded set $M=R\cup\{\mathrm{ACA}\}$ is
$$
\begin{aligned}
M=\{&\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{ACA},\mathrm{ACG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\\
&\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The corresponding $f_{3}$-saturation of $R$ removes $\mathrm{ACA}$ and $\mathrm{ACG}$ and leaves
$$
\begin{aligned}
\operatorname{Sat}_{f_3}(R)=\{&\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\\
&\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\},
\end{aligned}
$$
with an empty recorded $f_{3}$ boundary. Thus the proposed signal is not a free codon-list effect: it is a residual contrast on a finite synonymous surface after amino-acid quotienting.

\paragraph{Observed finite rows.}
The powered third-axis mechanism run used experiment id \texttt{b\_star\_q6\_third\_axis\_mechanism\_powered}, run \texttt{d0893337f0dd}. It passed with $5$ folds, at least $500$ proteins per organism, $41$ synonymous-design columns, and $11$ computed organisms out of $16$ requested. Its recorded conclusion is selection rather than GC, no wobble-clustered identity, and an unresolved CSC or positional identity requiring a further empirical row. The operative reason is that the GC-orthogonalized $d_{\perp}$ coordinate keeps significant cross-species alignment and held-out $R^{2}$. This is a finite positive row for a residual axis after GC removal, but it is not yet the same as a measured translation-efficiency survival row.

\paragraph{Relation to the third-axis identity row.}
A neighboring powered identity row gives the provisional interpretation of $d_{\perp}$ as aligned with within-family usage frequency. That run also used $5$ folds, $500$ minimum proteins per organism, $41$ synonymous-design columns, and $11$ computed organisms out of $16$ requested. Its reported mean cosine is $0.128$, with one-sided sign-test $p=0.000488$. In this chapter that number is used only to orient the residual axis: it says that the finite $d_{\perp}$ direction is not arbitrary noise under the recorded design, but it does not by itself identify ribosome motion, protein abundance, or downstream molecular function.

\paragraph{Rank and companion rows.}
The surrounding finite panel supports the claim that this is a real residual-axis question rather than a one-row artifact. The two-axis closure run reports a third axis present, again with $5$ folds, $500$ minimum proteins per organism, $41$ synonymous-design columns, and $11$ computed organisms out of $16$ requested; its reason is that $d_{\perp}$ has systematically positive held-out $R^{2}$ and same-signed directions across organisms. The residual-optimality row identifies the dominant non-tRNA residual optimality axis as \texttt{f3\_translation\_selection} under the same $5$-fold, $11$-organism, $41$-column design. The axis-strength spectrum row records $11$ computed organisms and reports that the captured spectra are led by \texttt{d\_resid4} on average, followed by \texttt{d\_tRNA}; all $11$ per-organism dominant counts are assigned to \texttt{d\_resid4}, while the largest GC3 rank effect is small, with $\rho=0.136$ for \texttt{d\_usage}. These rows justify treating the third-position residual as a finite, rank-powered target, not merely a visual codon-topology observation.

\paragraph{Mechanistic break condition.}
The biological interpretation is deliberately narrow. The conjecture says that an $f_{3}$-paired synonymous imbalance can become a translation-readout distinction only where a context tRNA or wobble surface breaks the $f_{3}$ symmetry, schematically when $w_c\ne w_{c^{f_3}}$. The finite tRNA-poverty row supplies one supporting constraint on the same contact family: the $R$ sense codons are reported as systematically tRNA-poor under GtRNAdb tGCN plus dos Reis wobble weights, with $40$ computed organisms out of $46$ requested, $6$ skipped organisms, permutation count $1000$, and $\alpha=0.05$. This row supports the plausibility of a tRNA-supply interface, but the present chapter still refuses to promote a modeled wobble or tAI/stAI asymmetry into observed ribosome occupancy.

\paragraph{Local support and falsification rule.}
The support condition is finite and testable. For same-organism and, where possible, same-CDS rows, compute the residual survival statistic after amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, coding length, $M$-density, matched mRNA abundance, rank, and simpler codon-baseline controls. A positive result requires
$$
\begin{aligned}
S_{f3\text{-}stress,T_j}>0
\end{aligned}
$$
for at least one bounded translation row $T_j$, with a nondegenerate design. The conjecture is falsified at the translation interface if the controlled entries remain non-positive or rank-degenerate across a powered panel. In particular, an all-$1.0$ three-organism fitted surface is treated as a rank boundary, not as survival evidence.

\paragraph{No promotion beyond the translation row.}
This chapter records a controlled cross-layer target, not a completed biological mechanism. Codon usage tables, tRNA gene-copy summaries, modeled tAI or stAI weights, and the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual geometry do not by themselves establish translation realization, ribosome occupancy, protein abundance, folding, physical admissibility, function, phenotype, mechanism, causation, or universality. A downstream claim can enter only after a separate layer-matched contact supplies the required rows and the translation entry $S_{f3\text{-}stress,T_j}$ is first non-vacuous, positive, and controlled under the declared finite design.
