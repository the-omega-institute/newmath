\subsection{Third-position stress as a bounded translation-readout candidate}
\label{sec:bior reality-q6-f3-stress-wobble-broken-readout}

\paragraph{Finite carrier.}
The record considered here is the finite coding-sequence surface obtained after amino-acid projection, restricted to the $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinate that detects $f_3$-paired synonymous imbalance.  The coordinate is not treated as a phenotype, a protein property, or a general biological law.  It is only a candidate row in a controlled translation-readout table.  Its finite saturation datum fixes the codon support before any biological interpretation is attempted: the set $M = \operatorname{Med}(R) \cup \{\texttt{ACA}\}$ has $20$ codons, namely \texttt{AAA}, \texttt{AAG}, \texttt{ACA}, \texttt{ACG}, \texttt{AGA}, \texttt{AGG}, \texttt{AUA}, \texttt{AUG}, \texttt{CUA}, \texttt{CUC}, \texttt{CUG}, \texttt{CUU}, \texttt{UAA}, \texttt{UAG}, \texttt{UCA}, \texttt{UCG}, \texttt{UGA}, \texttt{UGG}, \texttt{UUA}, and \texttt{UUG}.  The $f_3$-saturation of $R$ removes exactly the two codons \texttt{ACA} and \texttt{ACG}, leaving $18$ codons: \texttt{AAA}, \texttt{AAG}, \texttt{AGA}, \texttt{AGG}, \texttt{AUA}, \texttt{AUG}, \texttt{CUA}, \texttt{CUC}, \texttt{CUG}, \texttt{CUU}, \texttt{UAA}, \texttt{UAG}, \texttt{UCA}, \texttt{UCG}, \texttt{UGA}, \texttt{UGG}, \texttt{UUA}, and \texttt{UUG}.  The recorded $f_3$ boundary is empty, so this chapter uses the finite codon surface as a closed coordinate panel rather than as an open-ended codon discovery claim.

\paragraph{Mechanism-side finite row.}
The powered third-axis mechanism run is the central finite row for this chapter.  It used experiment \texttt{b\_star\_q6\_third\_axis\_mechanism\_powered}, deterministic seed \texttt{sha256:b\_star\_q6\_third\_axis\_mechanism\_powered:deterministic}, $5$ folds, at least $500$ proteins per organism, and $41$ synonymous-design columns.  It computed $11$ organisms out of $16$ requested organisms.  Its conclusion is that the $d_\perp$ signal is selection-not-GC, is not wobble-clustered, and still needs an external CSC or positional identity contact.  The recorded reason is that the GC-orthogonalized $d_\perp$ coordinate keeps significant cross-species alignment and held-out $R^2$.  Thus the finite row separates two claims: the third residual direction is not explained away by GC alone, but the present table does not identify a complete biochemical mechanism.

\paragraph{Axis evidence.}
The paired identity row supplies the sign and scale of the third direction without promoting it beyond the coding-sequence panel.  In the third-axis identity run, the same $5$-fold, $500$-protein, $41$-column design computed $11$ of $16$ organisms and provisionally aligned $d_\perp$ with usage frequency within synonymous family.  The mean cosine was $0.128$, and the one-sided sign-test value was $p = 0.000488$.  The two-axis closure row also computed $11$ of $16$ organisms and reported that a third axis is present: $d_\perp$ has systematically positive held-out $R^2$ and mutually same-signed directions across organisms.  The residual optimality row places the non-tRNA residual direction on $f_3$ translation selection, while the universal core row keeps the core optimal direction on tRNA adaptation.  Together these finite rows justify studying a third-position stress coordinate, but they do not by themselves observe ribosome occupancy, translation efficiency, protein abundance, localization, stability, or function.

\paragraph{Translation-readout rule.}
The proposed readout is the controlled entry $S_{f3\text{-}stress,T_j}$ for a bounded translation layer $T_j$, such as tAI, stAI, ribosome occupancy, or translation efficiency.  A positive table entry may be reported only after amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, CDS length, $M$-density, matched mRNA, rank, and simpler-baseline controls have been applied.  In symbolic form, the local support condition is
$$
\begin{aligned}
&S_{f3\text{-}stress,T_j} > 0 \\
&\text{after the declared controls and baseline ablation.}
\end{aligned}
$$
The row is falsified for this chapter if the controlled entries remain non-positive or if the fitted surface is rank-degenerate across a powered panel.  In particular, an all-$1.0$ matrix on only three organisms is a saturation boundary, not survival evidence.  Likewise, a modeled tAI or stAI weight is a bounded codon-adaptation readout, not a direct footprint measurement.

\paragraph{Supporting finite context.}
Several neighboring finite rows constrain the interpretation.  The axis-strength spectrum run computed $11$ of $16$ organisms and found captured spectra led on average by \texttt{d\_resid4}, followed by \texttt{d\_tRNA}; all $11$ per-organism dominant counts went to \texttt{d\_resid4}, and the largest GC3 rank effect was \texttt{d\_usage} with $\rho = 0.136$.  The boundary-set tRNA-poverty run used $40$ computed organisms out of $46$ requested, skipped $6$, used $1000$ permutations at $\alpha = 0.05$, and concluded that $R$ sense codons are systematically tRNA-poor under GtRNAdb tGCN plus dos Reis wobble weights.  The three-axis sufficiency run computed $11$ of $16$ organisms and concluded that a fourth mechanism is present, so the third-axis row is not claimed to exhaust synonymous selection.  The fourth-mechanism rows further narrow that residual landscape: mRNA stability was excluded as the dominant fourth-axis identity in the yeast assay, elongation dwell was partial in \textit{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}, codon-pair identity was rejected, and translation accuracy was rejected in an $11$-of-$16$ organism, $200$-permutation run.  These rows make the present claim sharper: $f_3$-stress is a plausible bounded translation-readout target only where third-position symmetry is broken by context tRNA or wobble weights, and the existing mechanism row still asks for measured CSC or position-resolved contact before stronger wording is allowed.

\paragraph{Local boundary.}
This chapter therefore records a finite, reviewable biological conjecture rather than a completed biological mechanism.  Codon usage tables, tRNA gene-copy summaries, and tAI or stAI weights can bound a translation-layer question, while ribosome profiling or translation-efficiency rows with matched mRNA controls would be needed for observed survival.  No translation realization, ribosome dynamics, protein abundance, folded structure, physical admissibility, molecular function, phenotype, adaptation law, mechanism, or universality follows from the $B^*_{Q6}$ geometry, the $f_3$ saturation table, the $d_\perp$ alignment, or the modeled wobble-weight surface alone.  Any such claim requires its own layer-matched contact and its own controlled statistic.
