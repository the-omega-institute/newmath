\subsection{Third-position stress as a bounded translation-readout candidate}
\label{sec:bioreality-q6-f3-stress-translation-readout}

\paragraph{Finite coordinate surface.}
The object considered here is the finite CDS-by-organism residual coordinate surface obtained after amino-acid projection, restricted to the $B^*_{Q6}$ third-position stress coordinate and to translation-readout rows that can be matched by organism and coding sequence. The finite $f_3$ saturation record fixes the relevant codon boundary. In the recorded run, the ambient set $M = \mathrm{Med}(R) \cup \mathrm{ACA}$ is
$$
\begin{aligned}
M=\{&\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{ACA},\mathrm{ACG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\\
&\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The corresponding saturated $f_3$ image omits $\mathrm{ACA}$ and $\mathrm{ACG}$:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{Sat}_{f_3}(R)=\{&\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\\
&\mathrm{CUU},\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\},
\end{aligned}
$$
with empty recorded $f_3$ boundary. Thus the chapter is not about discovering a new codon alphabet. It asks whether a residual third-position imbalance, after amino-acid composition has been quotiented out, can remain visible in a bounded translation readout when the local tRNA or wobble weights distinguish an $f_3$-paired codon from its mate.

\paragraph{Recorded powered surface.}
The direct finite assay for the named third-axis mechanism was run as \texttt{b\_star\_q6\_third\_axis\_mechanism\_powered} with deterministic seed \texttt{sha256:b\_star\_q6\_third\_axis\_mechanism\_powered:deterministic}. It used $5$ folds, required at least $500$ proteins per organism, computed $11$ organisms out of $16$ requested organisms, and used $41$ synonymous-design columns. The recorded status is passed. The finite conclusion is: selection rather than GC explains the retained signal; the axis is not organized as a wobble-clustered axis; and the exact CSC or position-resolved identity still needs a separate readout. The reason stored with the run is that the GC-orthogonalized $d_{\perp}$ direction keeps significant cross-species alignment and held-out $R^2$. A companion identity run on the same $11$ of $16$ organism panel and the same $41$ synonymous-design columns reports $d_{\perp}$ as provisionally aligned to usage frequency within synonymous families, with mean cosine $0.128$ and one-sided sign-test $p=0.000488$.

\paragraph{Rank context.}
The surrounding rank-powered records make the finite interpretation sharper. The universal non-tRNA residual optimality assay reports the dominant residual axis as $f_3$ translation selection on the same $11$-organism, $5$-fold, $41$-column design. The two-axis closure assay records a third-axis-present conclusion, with the reason that $d_{\perp}$ has systematically positive held-out $R^2$ and same-signed directions across organisms. The three-axis sufficiency assay records a fourth-mechanism-present conclusion, so the present chapter does not collapse all residual biology into a three-axis explanation. The per-organism axis-strength spectrum computed $11$ organisms and reports that $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$ leads on average, followed by $d_{\mathrm{tRNA}}$; all $11$ per-organism dominant counts are assigned to $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$, while the largest GC3 rank effect is only $\rho=0.136$ for $d_{\mathrm{usage}}$. These rows support a bounded statement: the $f_3$-stress coordinate is a real finite residual direction in the controlled codon-usage geometry, but the biological readout of that direction must be tested separately.

\paragraph{Translation-readout test.}
For a translation row $T_j$, the admissible survival entry is a controlled statistic $S_{f_3\text{-stress},T_j}$. The tested alternatives include modelled codon-adaptation weights such as tAI or stAI, and direct ribosome-occupancy or translation-efficiency measurements when those rows are organism- and CDS-matched. The intended support condition is
$$
\begin{aligned}
S_{f_3\text{-stress},T_j} > 0
\end{aligned}
$$
after amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, CDS length, $M$-density, matched mRNA abundance, rank, and simpler codon-baseline controls. The falsifying condition is that all controlled entries remain non-positive, or that the fitted surface is rank-degenerate rather than informative. In particular, an all-$1.0$ matrix on only three organisms is not survival evidence; it is a saturation boundary.

\paragraph{Permitted interpretation.}
The positive finite statement is limited to the codon-coordinate layer and to the stated powered assays. The $R$-sense tRNA-poverty run supplies compatible background: it computed $40$ organisms out of $46$ requested organisms, skipped $6$, used $1000$ permutations at $\alpha=0.05$, and passed with the call that $R$ sense codons are systematically tRNA-poor under GtRNAdb tGCN plus dos Reis wobble weights. This makes it plausible to seek a translation-facing break of third-position symmetry, but plausibility is not the same as a measured translation survival row. The present record therefore treats tAI or stAI as bounded codon-adaptation readbacks, and treats ribosome profiling or translation efficiency as the layer-matched contact needed for a stronger claim.

\paragraph{Local boundary.}
No translation realization follows from the residual coordinate alone. No protein folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, organismal phenotype, causal mechanism, or universal biological law follows from codon topology, from GC-orthogonalized alignment, from modelled tAI or stAI coupling, or from a review-ready routing event. Each stronger statement requires its own matched empirical contact and its own controlled statistic. The current finite record is therefore a bounded BioReality entry: $B^*_{Q6}$ contains a powered third-position residual direction that is not explained as a GC artifact and is not resolved as a wobble-clustered identity, while the translation-readout claim remains conditional on a non-vacuous, controlled $S_{f_3\text{-stress},T_j}$ entry.
