\subsection{The $f_3$-stress translation-readout boundary for $B^*_{Q6}$}
\label{sec:bioreality-q6-f3-stress-translation-readout-boundary}

\paragraph{Finite coordinate under test.}
This record concerns the $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinate obtained after quotienting coding-sequence rows by amino-acid composition and then isolating the third-position $f_3$ stress direction.  The finite carrier is not a protein phenotype and not a direct ribosome measurement.  It is a residual codon-coordinate surface whose admissible readout is restricted to matched translation-layer rows: modeled tAI or stAI weights, or measured ribosome-occupancy and translation-efficiency rows when those rows are joined to the same organism or coding sequence.  The relevant distinction is the paired imbalance between synonymous codons related by the third-position involution $f_3$, after amino-acid projection has removed the obvious amino-acid-composition explanation.

\paragraph{Saturation frame.}
The finite $f_3$ saturation calculation gives a concrete boundary for the coordinate.  The set $M=\operatorname{Med}(R)\cup\{ACA\}$ contains 20 codons,
$$
\begin{aligned}
M={}&\{AAA,AAG,ACA,ACG,AGA,AGG,AUA,AUG,CUA,CUC,\\
& CUG,CUU,UAA,UAG,UCA,UCG,UGA,UGG,UUA,UUG\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The saturated $R$ surface removes the two $ACA,ACG$ entries and leaves 18 codons,
$$
\begin{aligned}
\operatorname{Sat}_{f_3}(R)={}&\{AAA,AAG,AGA,AGG,AUA,AUG,CUA,CUC,CUG,CUU,\\
& UAA,UAG,UCA,UCG,UGA,UGG,UUA,UUG\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The recorded $f_3$ boundary is empty.  Thus the paper-level object is not a loose biological narrative: it is a finite synonymy-sensitive coordinate whose possible survival must be tested only through explicitly attached translation readouts.

\paragraph{Powered residual facts.}
The powered $d_\perp$ identity run used 5 folds, a minimum of 500 proteins per organism, 41 synonymous-design columns, and 11 computed organisms out of 16 requested.  In that run the $d_\perp$ direction was provisionally aligned with within-family usage frequency, with mean cosine $0.128$ and one-sided sign-test $p=0.000488$.  The separate third-axis-mechanism run used the same 5-fold, 500-protein, 41-column, 11-of-16 organism design and returned the classification
$$
\begin{aligned}
\text{third-axis call} ={}& \text{selection-not-GC;}\\
&\text{not-wobble-clustered;}\\
&\text{CSC/positional identity needs additional outside rows.}
\end{aligned}
$$
Its stated reason was that GC-orthogonalized $d_\perp$ retained significant cross-species alignment and held-out $R^2$.  This is a directional constraint on the residual coordinate, not a completed biological explanation.  It says that the axis is not exhausted by GC content and is not already certified as a wobble-clustered axis in the available finite rows.

\paragraph{Translation-readout survival statistic.}
Let $q_{f_3}$ denote the $B^*_{Q6}$ $f_3$-stress residual coordinate and let $T_j$ be one bounded translation readout: tAI, stAI, ribosome occupancy, or translation efficiency.  The only permitted positive cross-layer entry is a controlled survival score
$$
\begin{aligned}
S_{f_3,T_j}>0,
\end{aligned}
$$
computed after amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, coding-sequence length, $M$-density, matched mRNA abundance when translation efficiency is used, rank sufficiency, and simpler codon baselines have been accounted for.  A modeled tAI or stAI surface may identify a candidate asymmetry $w_c\ne w_{c^{f_3}}$, but it does not by itself observe ribosome motion.  The $f_3$ coordinate becomes biologically testable only where those context weights are asymmetric and where a same-row translation readout survives the controls.

\paragraph{Rank and panel constraints.}
Several nearby finite rows constrain the interpretation.  The universal residual-axis run, also on 11 computed organisms out of 16 requested with 5 folds, 500 proteins per organism, and 41 synonymous-design columns, identified the dominant non-tRNA residual optimality axis as $f_3$ translation selection.  The two-axis closure run reported a third axis, with $d_\perp$ showing systematically positive held-out $R^2$ and same-signed directions across organisms.  The axis-strength spectrum run again used 11 computed organisms out of 16 requested and found all 11 per-organism dominant counts led by $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$, while domain means were explicitly descriptive under the imbalanced domain counts $n=\{\text{archaea}:1,\text{bacteria}:2,\text{eukaryote}:8\}$; the largest GC3 rank effect was $d_{\mathrm{usage}}$ with $\rho=0.136$.  These numbers justify a powered residual-axis question, but they do not replace the missing same-row translation-readout statistic.

\paragraph{Local refusal rule.}
The conjecture is supported only if at least one controlled $S_{f_3,T_j}$ entry is strictly positive in a nondegenerate panel.  It is falsified at this boundary if every controlled entry is non-positive, or if the apparent surface is rank-saturated, for example the known three-organism all-$1.0$ matrix behavior that carries no survival information.  A positive codon-topology fact, a GC-orthogonal residual direction, or a modeled adaptation-weight coupling is therefore insufficient unless the translation row itself survives.  Conversely, a negative translation-readout result would not show that $B^*_{Q6}$ is biologically irrelevant; it would only close this particular translation interface.

\paragraph{No promotion beyond the row.}
This subsection licenses only the bounded question of whether the $f_3$-stress residual coordinate survives against matched translation readouts.  No translation realization, ribosome mechanism, protein abundance, folded structure, physical admissibility, molecular function, localization, stability, phenotype, or universal biological law follows from the codon coordinate, from the $d_\perp$ spectrum, from tAI or stAI weights, or from review-ready packet status.  Each such stronger assertion requires its own layer-matched contact and its own controlled statistic.  Until the joined codon, wobble-weight, translation-readout, and mRNA-control rows exist, the exact scientific status is a powered finite conjecture with a sharp null: $S_{f_3,T_j}\le 0$ under the declared controls, or a rank-degenerate surface, blocks the claimed cross-layer survival.
