\subsection{The $f_3$-stress coordinate as a bounded translation-readout question}\label{sec:bq6-f3-stress-translation-readout}\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite coordinate surface.}
The record fixes a finite CDS-by-organism residual coordinate surface and then restricts attention to the $B^*_{Q6}$ coordinate that is sensitive to third-position $f_3$ pairing. The underlying saturation check is concrete: the ambient marked set is
$$\begin{aligned}
M = \{&AAA,AAG,ACA,ACG,AGA,AGG,AUA,AUG,CUA,CUC,\\
& CUG,CUU,UAA,UAG,UCA,UCG,UGA,UGG,UUA,UUG\},
\end{aligned}$$
while the $f_3$-saturated residual set removes the two codons $ACA$ and $ACG$ and leaves
$$\begin{aligned}
\operatorname{Sat}_{f_3}(R)=\{&AAA,AAG,AGA,AGG,AUA,AUG,CUA,CUC,CUG,CUU,\\
& UAA,UAG,UCA,UCG,UGA,UGG,UUA,UUG\}.
\end{aligned}$$
The reported $f_3$ boundary is empty. Thus the finite combinatorial object has no residual boundary codon after this saturation operation, but it still leaves a testable question after amino-acid quotienting: whether a synonymous imbalance paired by $f_3$ survives in a translation-facing row once amino-acid composition and other baseline covariates have been removed.

\paragraph{Powered mechanism row.}
The relevant powered experiment for the third residual axis was run with $5$ folds, at least $500$ proteins per organism, $41$ synonymous design columns, $16$ requested organisms, and $11$ organisms successfully computed. Its status is passed. The finite conclusion is deliberately narrow: the third-axis mechanism is reported as selection-not-GC, not-wobble-clustered, with CSC or positional identity still needing an additional contact. The recorded reason is that the GC-orthogonalized $d_{\perp}$ coordinate keeps significant cross-species alignment and held-out $R^2$. This supports a distinction between a GC explanation and a non-GC residual selection signal, but it does not by itself identify a direct ribosome-occupancy mechanism.

\paragraph{Position among neighboring powered rows.}
The identity row for the same third axis used the same powered shape, again with $5$ folds, $500$ minimum proteins per organism, $41$ synonymous design columns, $16$ requested organisms, and $11$ computed organisms. It identifies $d_{\perp}$ provisionally with usage frequency within synonymous family, with mean cosine $0.128$ and one-sided sign-test $p=0.000488$. A separate residual-optimality row reports that the non-tRNA residual optimality aligns most with the $f_3$ translation-selection axis. The two-axis closure row reports that a third axis is present because $d_{\perp}$ has systematically positive held-out $R^2$ and mutually same-signed directions across organisms. These rows locate the coordinate as a reproducible residual direction, not merely as a codon-list artifact.

\paragraph{Spectrum and sufficiency constraints.}
The axis-strength spectrum was computed on $11$ of $16$ requested organisms, with $5$ folds, $500$ minimum proteins per organism, and $41$ synonymous design columns. The captured spectra are led by $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$ on average and then by $d_{\mathrm{tRNA}}$; the per-organism dominant count is $\{d_{\mathrm{resid4}}:11\}$. The largest GC3 rank effect in that row is $d_{\mathrm{usage}}$ with $\rho=0.136$, and the domain means are explicitly descriptive because the domain counts are imbalanced, with $1$ archaeal, $2$ bacterial, and $8$ eukaryotic organisms. The optimality-axis sufficiency row, computed on the same $11/16$ organism powered panel with the same fold count and design-column count, reports fourth-mechanism-present. Hence the three-axis picture is not closed by the tRNA and $f_3$ coordinates alone; the finite record already warns that residual axes beyond the first three can carry signal.

\paragraph{Translation readout gate.}
The BioReality question for this subsection is not whether the coordinate is mathematically present, but whether it survives as a translation-readout distinction. Let $Q_{f_3}$ denote the $B^*_{Q6}$ third-position residual coordinate after amino-acid projection, and let $T_j$ range over bounded translation readouts such as tAI, stAI, translation efficiency, or ribosome occupancy. The permitted finite statistic has the form
$$\begin{aligned}
S_{f_3,T_j} = \operatorname{Survival}(Q_{f_3};T_j \mid Z),
\end{aligned}$$
where $Z$ includes amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, CDS length, $M$-density, matched mRNA control when ribosome or TE rows are used, rank sufficiency, and simpler codon baselines. A positive biological readout is allowed only if a nondegenerate same-organism or same-CDS panel yields $S_{f_3,T_j}>0$ after these controls. The finite rows currently justify a sharper search target: wobble or tRNA-weight asymmetry must break the $f_3$ pairing before the residual coordinate can be treated as dynamically relevant for a translation row.

\paragraph{What the existing evidence does and does not say.}
The tRNA-poverty mechanism row supplies a broader constraint: over $40$ computed organisms out of $46$ requested, using $1000$ permutations at $\alpha=0.05$, R sense codons are systematically tRNA-poor under GtRNAdb tGCN plus dos Reis wobble weights. That supports a tRNA-supply interpretation for R avoidance in that finite setting. It does not supply the missing same-row $S_{f_3,T_j}$ statistic for the third-position stress coordinate. Likewise, modeled tAI or stAI weights may be used only as bounded codon-adaptation readbacks unless they are explicitly tied to the declared controlled survival surface. A three-organism all-$1.0$ controlled matrix is a rank-saturated null boundary, not evidence of survival.

\paragraph{Local limitation.}
No claim about translation realization, protein abundance, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, downstream phenotype, mechanism in the strong causal sense, or universality follows from the present finite rows alone. The current record supports a GC-resistant, selection-facing third residual axis and a precise break condition for testing it against translation readouts. It remains blocked from stronger biological promotion until codon counts, wobble or tRNA weights, measured ribosome or translation-efficiency rows, and matched mRNA controls are joined on the same organism or CDS rows and the controlled statistic $S_{f_3,T_j}$ is computed.
