\subsection{Third-position stress, GC separation, and the remaining positional question}
\label{sec:h3-cross-layer-third-axis-mechanism-q6-d-perp-csc-vs-gc}

\paragraph{Finite coordinate surface.}
This record concerns the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual coordinate obtained after amino-acid projection, with attention restricted to the $f_{3}$-paired synonymous imbalance direction and to translation-layer rows that are allowed to be read only as bounded codon-adaptation or measured translation readouts.  The finite codon support used by the saturation row is
$$
\begin{aligned}
M &= \{AAA,AAG,ACA,ACG,AGA,AGG,AUA,AUG,CUA,CUC,CUG,CUU, \\
&\qquad UAA,UAG,UCA,UCG,UGA,UGG,UUA,UUG\},
\end{aligned}
$$
while the $f_{3}$-saturated $R$ support removes the two $ACA/ACG$ entries:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\operatorname{Sat}_{f_{3}}(R) &= \{AAA,AAG,AGA,AGG,AUA,AUG,CUA,CUC,CUG,CUU, \\
&\qquad UAA,UAG,UCA,UCG,UGA,UGG,UUA,UUG\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The recorded $f_{3}$ boundary outside this saturated support is empty.  Thus the object being tested is not a free codon-count association.  It is a finite residual surface in which the amino-acid quotient has already removed ordinary amino-acid composition, and the remaining question is whether the third-position imbalance leaves a translation-readout trace once organism, $GC3$, CDS length, $M$-density, and simpler codon baselines are kept in the control set.

\paragraph{Observed powered row.}
The powered third-axis mechanism run computed $11$ organisms out of $16$ requested organisms, using $5$ folds, a minimum of $500$ proteins per organism, and $41$ synonymous design columns.  Its recorded status is passed.  The finite conclusion is
$$
\begin{aligned}
&\text{selection-not-GC;}\quad \text{not-wobble-clustered;}\quad
\text{CSC/positional identity needs external contact}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The reason attached to the row is that the $GC$-orthogonalized $d_{\perp}$ coordinate retains significant cross-species alignment and held-out $R^{2}$.  This is the main content of the chapter: the third residual direction is not explained away by $GC$ composition, and the observed alignment survives the explicit removal of the $GC$ component in the powered design.  At the same time, the same finite row refuses to identify the direction as a completed wobble-cluster mechanism or as a closed codon-stability or positional mechanism.  The phrase CSC/positional identity needs external contact is treated here as a mathematical boundary, not as a weak positive claim.

\paragraph{Relation to neighboring finite rows.}
The surrounding powered rows constrain the interpretation.  The third-axis identity run also used $11$ computed organisms out of $16$, $5$ folds, a $500$ protein minimum, and $41$ synonymous design columns; it provisionally aligned $d_{\perp}$ with usage frequency within synonymous family, with mean cosine $0.128$ and one-sided sign-test $p=0.000488$.  The two-axis closure run, under the same organism and fold counts, reported a present third axis because $d_{\perp}$ had systematically positive held-out $R^{2}$ and mutually same-signed directions across organisms.  The residual optimality row identified the non-tRNA residual optimality axis most strongly with $f_{3}$ translation selection.  These rows make the third direction a stable residual coordinate candidate rather than a single-table artifact.

The axis-strength spectrum gives the scale of this interpretation.  In the powered spectrum, $11$ organisms were computed from $16$ requested, again with $5$ folds, a $500$ protein threshold, and $41$ synonymous columns.  The captured spectra were led on average by $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$ and then by $d_{\mathrm{tRNA}}$; the per-organism dominant count was $\{d_{\mathrm{resid4}}:11\}$.  Domain means were explicitly descriptive under the imbalanced sample sizes $\{\mathrm{archaea}:1,\mathrm{bacteria}:2,\mathrm{eukaryote}:8\}$, and the largest $GC3$ rank effect was only $d_{\mathrm{usage}}$ with $\rho=0.136$.  This supports the local reading that the mechanism row is about a residual axis after $GC$ control, not a statement that domain-level taxonomic structure or $GC3$ alone explains the signal.

\paragraph{Mechanistic boundary.}
The boundary-set tRNA-poverty row supplies a separate finite constraint on a related $R$-avoidance mechanism: $40$ organisms were computed out of $46$ requested, $6$ were skipped, the permutation count was $1000$, and the test used $\alpha=0.05$.  Its finite call was that $R$ sense codons are systematically tRNA-poor under GtRNAdb tRNA gene-copy numbers plus dos Reis wobble weights.  That row supports a tRNA-supply interpretation for $R$ avoidance, but it does not by itself close the present $d_{\perp}$ mechanism.  The present row is sharper and narrower: $d_{\perp}$ remains after $GC$ orthogonalization, and it is not reduced to a wobble-cluster call in the powered data.

Consequently the admissible local statement is the following.  After amino-acid quotienting, a third-position synonymous residual can remain visible in the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ coordinate surface when organism-level codon usage and translation-readout weights break the $f_{3}$ symmetry.  The finite evidence supports the existence and $GC$-orthogonality of the residual direction, and it supports a usage-frequency-within-family identity candidate for $d_{\perp}$.  It does not yet certify the final biological mechanism of that direction.

\paragraph{Translation-readout rule.}
A positive interpretation of this chapter requires a non-vacuous survival entry
$$
\begin{aligned}
S_{f_{3}\text{-stress},T_{j}} > 0
\end{aligned}
$$
for a declared translation readout $T_{j}$, such as tAI, stAI, ribosome occupancy, or translation efficiency, after amino-acid composition, organism, $GC3$, length, $M$-density, matched mRNA controls where relevant, and simpler-codon-baseline ablation.  Modeled tAI or stAI weights may be used as bounded translation-layer readbacks, but they are not the same thing as observed ribosome occupancy.  A three-organism all-$1.0$ controlled matrix is a rank-degenerate boundary and is not survival evidence.

\paragraph{Local refusal of promotion.}
No statement in this chapter promotes the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual coordinate to protein abundance, folded structure, physical admissibility, molecular function, localization, stability, phenotype, mechanism closure, or universality.  A translation claim needs its own translation contact and a controlled survival statistic.  A protein-output claim needs a separate protein readout.  Structure, physical admissibility, function, localization, stability, phenotype, and global biological-law claims each require their own layer-matched contact and their own statistic.  The finite conclusion here is therefore limited but nontrivial: in the powered $B^{*}_{Q6}$ panel, the third residual direction survives $GC$ orthogonalization and is not closed as a wobble-cluster mechanism; the remaining CSC or positional identity must be tested by additional external rows before it can be named biologically.
