\subsection{The $f_3$-stress third-axis mechanism as a bounded translation-readout target}
\label{sec:bioq6-f3-stress-third-axis-mechanism}

\paragraph{Finite carrier.}
The record considered here is the finite residual-coordinate surface obtained after amino-acid projection of coding sequences, restricted to the $B^*_{Q6}$ third-position stress coordinate and to translation-layer rows that can be matched by organism or by coding sequence. The carrier is not a statement about protein output. It is the finite comparison class in which an $f_3$-paired synonymous codon imbalance is tested against organism-level codon usage, tRNA or wobble weights, and, where available, ribosome occupancy or translation-efficiency rows with matched mRNA controls. The concrete saturation row fixes the relevant codon support. The set $M=\operatorname{Med}(R)\cup\{\mathrm{ACA},\mathrm{ACG}\}$ is
$$
\begin{aligned}
M={}&\{\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{ACA},\mathrm{ACG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\\
&\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\\
&\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The $f_3$-saturated $R$ support removes $\mathrm{ACA}$ and $\mathrm{ACG}$ and leaves
$$
\begin{aligned}
\operatorname{Sat}_{f_3}(R)={}&\{\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\\
&\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\\
&\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The recorded $f_3$ boundary list is empty, so the finite row is not carried by an unresolved boundary codon. It is carried by the residual imbalance left after quotienting away amino-acid identity and after restricting attention to the paired third-position coordinate.

\paragraph{Mechanism row.}
The powered mechanism assay for the present claim was run as `b\_star\_q6\_third\_axis\_mechanism\_powered` with run id `d0893337f0dd`. It used $5$ folds, required at least $500$ proteins per organism, computed $11$ organisms out of $16$ requested organisms, and used $41$ synonymous design columns. The assay status is `passed`. Its finite conclusion is: selection-not-GC; not-wobble-clustered; CSC/positional identity needs an added contact. The stated reason is that the GC-orthogonalized $d_{\perp}$ coordinate keeps significant cross-species alignment and held-out $R^2$. Thus the row separates a third residual axis from a simple GC explanation, but it does not by itself identify a complete physical translation mechanism.

\paragraph{Relation to the third residual axis.}
The companion identity assay for $d_{\perp}$ used the same $5$-fold, $500$-protein, $11$-of-$16$ organism, $41$-column design and reported provisional alignment to usage frequency within synonymous family, with mean cosine $0.128$ and one-sided sign-test $p=0.000488$. This is the numerical reason the present row is treated as a real residual-axis target rather than as a free label. A second powered row reports that the non-tRNA residual optimality direction aligns most with `f3\_translation\_selection`, again on $11$ computed organisms out of $16$ requested organisms, with $5$ folds, a $500$-protein minimum, and $41$ synonymous design columns. A two-axis closure test reports that a third axis is present because $d_{\perp}$ has systematically positive held-out $R^2$ and the $d_{\perp}$ directions are mutually same-signed across organisms. These rows justify the following finite statement: after amino-acid quotienting and ordinary sequence-composition controls, the third coordinate behaves like a selection-associated residual direction rather than like a remaining GC coordinate.

\paragraph{Translation-readout condition.}
The biological conjecture is narrower than the mechanism row. Let $Q_{f_3}$ denote the $B^*_{Q6}$ $f_3$-stress coordinate and let $T_j$ denote a bounded translation readout such as tAI, stAI, ribosome occupancy, or translation efficiency. The admissible survival entry is a controlled score
$$
\begin{aligned}
S_{f_3,T_j}>0
\end{aligned}
$$
after controls for amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, coding-sequence length, $M$-density, matched mRNA abundance where the readout is translation efficiency, rank sufficiency, and simpler codon baselines. The conjecture expects such a positive entry only in organisms or contexts where the context tRNA or wobble weights break the $f_3$ pairing, written locally as $w_c\neq w_{c^{f_3}}$. If the paired codons have symmetric wobble weight in the relevant context, the $f_3$ coordinate has no licensed translation dynamics in this packet.

\paragraph{Empirical support and refusal boundary.}
The available finite evidence supports a residual-axis mechanism row, not a completed translation-survival row. The tRNA-poverty assay for the related $R$-sense boundary was powered across $40$ computed organisms out of $46$ requested organisms, with $6$ skipped organisms, $1000$ permutations, and $\alpha=0.05$; it concluded that $R$ sense codons are systematically tRNA-poor under GtRNAdb tGCN plus dos Reis wobble weights. The per-organism axis-strength spectrum used $11$ computed organisms out of $16$ requested organisms, $5$ folds, a $500$-protein minimum, and $41$ synonymous design columns; it reported that captured spectra are led by $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$ on average, then $d_{\mathrm{tRNA}}$, with all $11$ per-organism dominant counts assigned to $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$ and largest GC3 rank effect $\rho=0.136$ for $d_{\mathrm{usage}}$. These numbers are compatible with a translation-adjacent residual coordinate, but they are not a measured same-row ribosome or translation-efficiency survival matrix for this exact $f_3$ packet.

\paragraph{Local claim.}
The local claim is therefore the following finite implication. If a rank-powered panel supplies matched codon counts, tRNA or wobble weights, translation readouts, and mRNA controls, then the $f_3$-stress coordinate may be tested by the controlled entry $S_{f_3,T_j}$. Positive survival is allowed only when the third-position pair is broken by the translation-weight context and when the readout remains positive after the listed controls. The current finite rows support the selection-not-GC status of the third residual axis and the need for a CSC or positional contact, but they leave the exact same-CDS or same-organism translation-readout statistic open.

\paragraph{Scope restriction.}
No translation realization, ribosome mechanism, protein abundance effect, folding statement, physical admissibility statement, molecular-function statement, phenotype statement, or universal biological law follows from the rows above alone. Modeled tAI or stAI weights are bounded codon-adaptation readbacks, not observed ribosome footprints. A ribosome-profiling or translation-efficiency contact would still have to supply joined rows and a controlled statistic before the packet can report positive translation survival. Any later claim about protein abundance, structure, localization, stability, function, phenotype, mechanism, or universality would require its own layer-matched contact and its own recorded finite statistic.
