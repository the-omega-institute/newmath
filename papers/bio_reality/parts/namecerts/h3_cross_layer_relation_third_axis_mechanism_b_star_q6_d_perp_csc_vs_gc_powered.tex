\subsection{The Third-Axis Mechanism Boundary for the $B^*_{Q6}$ Residual}
\label{sec:bioreality-q6-third-axis-mechanism-csc-vs-gc}

\paragraph{Finite object.}
The finite object considered here is the $B^*_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinate surface after amino-acid projection, restricted to the third residual direction $d_{\perp}$ and to the $f_3$-paired stress coordinate. The coordinate is not read as a protein-level property. It is a coding-sequence residual coordinate whose only admissible biological readout in this record is a controlled translation-layer row such as tAI, stAI, ribosome occupancy, or translation efficiency. The finite saturation table fixes the relevant $f_3$ boundary explicitly. The larger marked set is
$$
\begin{aligned}
M = \{&\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{ACA},\mathrm{ACG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\\
&\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\},
\end{aligned}
$$
whereas the $f_3$-saturated residual set removes exactly $\mathrm{ACA}$ and $\mathrm{ACG}$:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{Sat}_{f_3}(R) = \{&\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\\
&\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The finite boundary check reports no residual codons outside this saturation, so $f_3$-boundary leakage is absent in the recorded row. This makes the coordinate suitable for asking a bounded mechanism question: whether the third residual axis is better explained as GC pressure, wobble clustering, or a selected codon-context and positional signal.

\paragraph{Powered mechanism row.}
The powered assay for this chapter is the row with run identifier \texttt{d0893337f0dd}. It used $5$ folds, required at least $500$ proteins per organism, computed $11$ organisms out of $16$ requested organisms, and used $41$ synonymous design columns. The reported result is \emph{selection-not-GC; not-wobble-clustered; CSC/positional identity needs external}. The decisive finite statement is that the GC-orthogonalized $d_{\perp}$ direction keeps significant cross-species alignment and held-out $R^2$. Thus the observed residual direction is not absorbed by GC3 correction in this assay. In symbolic form the supported local reading is
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{GC\text{-}orthogonalized}(d_{\perp}) \not\equiv 0
\quad\text{under held-out cross-organism prediction,}
\end{aligned}
$$
with the important qualification that this does not identify a complete molecular mechanism. The same finite row also rejects a simple wobble-cluster explanation for the third axis. What remains is a selected codon-context or positional identity candidate, but that candidate is marked as needing an additional external contact before it can be promoted beyond the bounded coordinate-level result.

\paragraph{Relation to the third-axis identity row.}
A neighboring powered row identifies $d_{\perp}$ provisionally with usage frequency within synonymous families. That row computed $11$ organisms out of $16$, used $5$ folds, imposed the same $500$-protein minimum, and again used $41$ synonymous design columns. Its mean cosine was $0.128$, with a one-sided sign-test value $p = 0.000488$. The identity row therefore supplies a finite orientation for the third axis, while the mechanism row supplies the narrower exclusion: the axis is not merely GC and is not merely wobble clustering. The two statements combine only at the level of bounded coordinate interpretation:
$$
\begin{aligned}
 d_{\perp} &\leadsto \text{usage-frequency-within-family orientation},\\
 d_{\perp} &\not\leadsto \text{GC-only explanation},\\
 d_{\perp} &\not\leadsto \text{simple wobble-cluster explanation}.
\end{aligned}
$$
This is enough to make the third residual direction scientifically inspectable, but not enough to claim translation realization, protein output, structure, function, or universality.

\paragraph{Translation-row gate.}
The conjectural biological test is stricter than the mechanism row. The $f_3$-stress residual is allowed to count as a translation-readout distinction only when a controlled entry $S_{f3\text{-}stress,T_j}$ is non-vacuous and positive for a matched translation row $T_j$. Acceptable readouts include bounded tAI or stAI weights and, more strongly, organism- and CDS-matched ribosome occupancy or translation efficiency with matched mRNA abundance. The required control set includes amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, CDS length, M-density baseline, matched mRNA where applicable, rank sufficiency, and simpler codon baselines. The local support condition is therefore
$$
\begin{aligned}
S_{f3\text{-}stress,T_j} > 0
\quad\text{after the declared controls and baseline ablation.}
\end{aligned}
$$
The present finite mechanism row does not by itself supply such an $S$ entry. It says that the residual axis survives a GC test and is not classified as wobble-clustered; it does not say that a measured ribosome or translation-efficiency surface has already been joined to the same coding-sequence rows.

\paragraph{Auxiliary finite constraints.}
The tRNA-poverty mechanism row for the $R$ sense codons gives a separate finite pressure point: $40$ organisms were computed out of $46$ requested, with $1000$ permutations at $\alpha = 0.05$, and the conclusion was that $R$ sense codons are systematically tRNA-poor under GtRNAdb gene-copy counts plus dos Reis wobble weights. This supports a supply-side mechanism for $R$ avoidance, but it is not the same as the third-axis mechanism row. The axis-strength spectrum row computed $11$ organisms out of $16$ and reported that spectra were led on average by $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$ and then by $d_{\mathrm{tRNA}}$, with per-organism dominant counts $\{d_{\mathrm{resid4}}:11\}$ and largest GC3 rank effect $d_{\mathrm{usage}}$ with $\rho = 0.136$. These numbers keep the third-axis statement in scale: it is a real finite residual direction inside a larger residual spectrum, not a stand-alone global biological law.

\paragraph{Local cannot-claim boundary.}
This chapter makes a bounded claim about a finite residual coordinate and a powered mechanism exclusion. It does not claim that the $f_3$-stress coordinate changes translation unless a separate, same-row translation contact supplies a positive controlled survival statistic. It also does not claim protein abundance, folded structure, physical admissibility, molecular function, phenotype, causal mechanism, or universality. Codon topology, modeled adaptation weights, and GC-orthogonal residual prediction are admissible as coordinate and bounded readout evidence only within their stated rows. Any stronger biological interpretation requires a separate layer-matched contact and a recorded statistic at that layer.
