\subsection{Third-position stress as a bounded translation-readout candidate}\label{subsec:bioreality-q6-f3-stress-wobble-broken-translation-readout}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite coordinate surface.}
This record concerns the finite residual surface obtained after amino-acid projection of coding-sequence codon rows, restricted to the $B^*_{Q6}$ third-position stress coordinate and to readouts that are still translation-layer quantities. The relevant saturated $f_3$ row is not the full codon universe. In the recorded finite row, $M$ is
$\{\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{ACA},\mathrm{ACG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\}$,
with $20$ listed codons. Its $f_3$ saturation removes the two $ACA/ACG$ entries and leaves
$\{\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\}$,
with no residual $f_3$ boundary codon recorded. Thus the biological question is not whether a symbolic third-base operation exists; it is whether, after synonymous and amino-acid quotienting, a finite $f_3$ imbalance row has a non-vacuous translation readout in organisms whose tRNA or wobble weights distinguish paired third-position codons.

\paragraph{Observed finite assays.}
The powered third-axis mechanism assay passed on $11$ computed organisms out of $16$ requested organisms, using $5$ folds, at least $500$ proteins per organism, and $41$ synonymous design columns. Its recorded conclusion is that the third residual direction is selection-associated rather than GC-explained, is not simply wobble-clustered, and still needs a separate contact to identify a CSC or positional explanation. The stated finite reason is that the GC-orthogonalized $d_{\perp}$ direction keeps significant cross-species alignment and held-out $R^2$. This places the coordinate on a genuine residual axis after GC control, but it does not yet identify a direct ribosome or translation-efficiency measurement.

\paragraph{Relation to the surrounding finite spectrum.}
Several neighboring rows constrain the interpretation. The third-axis identity assay, also run on $11$ of $16$ organisms with $5$ folds, at least $500$ proteins per organism, and $41$ synonymous design columns, provisionally aligned $d_{\perp}$ with usage frequency within synonymous families, with mean cosine $0.128$ and one-sided sign-test $p=0.000488$. The two-axis closure assay reported a remaining third axis because $d_{\perp}$ has systematically positive held-out $R^2$ and same-signed organism directions. The residual optimality row identified the non-tRNA residual direction most strongly with $f_3$ translation selection, while the three-axis sufficiency row concluded that a fourth mechanism is still present. In the axis-strength spectrum, $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$ led the captured spectra on average, followed by $d_{\mathrm{tRNA}}$; the per-organism dominant count was $11$ for $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$. The domain summary was explicitly descriptive because the panel was imbalanced, with $n=1$ archaea, $n=2$ bacteria, and $n=8$ eukaryotes, and the largest GC3 rank effect reported there was only $\rho=0.136$ for $d_{\mathrm{usage}}$.

\paragraph{Wobble-breaking condition.}
The coordinate becomes biologically testable only where a readout surface can distinguish a codon $c$ from its $f_3$ partner $c^{f_3}$ by a context-dependent weight. In symbols, the admissible trigger is the finite asymmetry
$$
\begin{aligned}
w_c \neq w_{c^{f_3}},
\end{aligned}
$$
where $w_c$ may be a tAI, stAI, or explicitly bounded wobble-weight row, and where the comparison is made only after amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, coding-sequence length, $M$-density, matched mRNA, rank, and simpler-baseline controls have been applied. A separate tRNA-poverty assay supports the plausibility of supply-side asymmetry for the related $R$-sense boundary: it passed at $\alpha=0.05$ on $40$ computed organisms out of $46$ requested, skipped $6$ organisms, and used $1000$ permutations. That row supports a tRNA-supply explanation for $R$ avoidance, but it is not by itself the required same-row $f_3$ translation-survival statistic.

\paragraph{Readout rule.}
The only positive readback permitted here is a controlled survival entry for a translation-layer row, such as tAI, stAI, ribosome occupancy, or translation efficiency. The local statistic has the form
$$
\begin{aligned}
S_{f3,T_j} > 0,
\end{aligned}
$$
after the declared controls and baseline ablations. If all such entries are non-positive, or if the fitted matrix is rank-degenerate, the packet remains a null boundary. In particular, a three-organism all-$1.0$ controlled surface is rank saturation rather than evidence of survival. A modeled adaptation weight can bound a translation-readout hypothesis, but it is not an observed ribosome-occupancy row unless a measured ribosome or translation-efficiency contact is joined at the same organism or coding-sequence resolution.

\paragraph{Local cannot-claim boundary.}
The present record supports only a bounded claim: a finite $B^*_{Q6}$ third-position residual coordinate remains compatible with a wobble-broken translation-readout test, and the powered GC-control assay does not reduce the third residual direction to GC alone. It does not establish translation realization, ribosome occupancy, protein abundance, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, localization, stability, phenotype, a complete mechanism, or a universal biological law. Each of those stronger statements requires its own layer-matched measurement and its own controlled statistic. Until same-organism and same-CDS codon counts, wobble or tRNA weights, measured translation rows, and matched mRNA controls are joined, the correct conclusion is a constrained positive coordinate result with an open translation-survival boundary, not a downstream biological promotion.
