\subsection{The $f_3$-stress readout boundary for the third $B^*_{Q6}$ axis}
\label{sec:bioreality-q6-f3-stress-third-axis-readout-boundary}

\paragraph{Finite carrier.}
The object considered here is the finite coding-sequence residual surface obtained after amino-acid projection and organism matching, restricted to the $B^*_{Q6}$ third-position stress coordinate.  The coordinate is not a claim about a protein state.  It is a row in a controlled codon-residual table: synonymous codon imbalance is first formed inside amino-acid families, then compared with organism-level codon usage, tRNA or wobble weights, and translation readouts when those readouts are available on matched rows.  In the finite $f_3$ saturation calculation, the ambient marked set is
$$
\begin{aligned}
M &= \{\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{ACA},\mathrm{ACG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\},\\
\operatorname{Sat}_{f_3}(R) &= \{\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the displayed saturation removes exactly $\mathrm{ACA}$ and $\mathrm{ACG}$ from the marked set, and the recorded $f_3$ boundary set is empty.  This is a finite combinatorial boundary, not a biological readout by itself.

\paragraph{Third-axis status.}
The powered third-axis identity assay used $5$ folds, a minimum of $500$ proteins per organism, $41$ synonymous design columns, and $11$ computed organisms out of $16$ requested organisms.  It identified $d_\perp$ provisionally with usage frequency within synonymous family, with mean cosine $0.128$ and one-sided sign-test $p=0.000488$.  The companion mechanism assay, run on the same $5$-fold, $41$-column, $11/16$ organism design, returned the classification
$$
\begin{aligned}
\text{mechanism call} &= \text{selection-not-GC; not-wobble-clustered; CSC/positional identity needs external contact},\\
\text{support reason} &= \text{GC-orthogonalized } d_\perp \text{ keeps cross-species alignment and held-out } R^2.
\end{aligned}
$$
This says that the third residual direction is not explained away by GC after orthogonalization, and it is not already resolved as a simple wobble-cluster direction.  It does not say that codon topology alone has become a translation mechanism.  The finite readout remains conditional on a context in which $w_c \neq w_{c^{f_3}}$, so that the third-position paired codons are given asymmetric tRNA or wobble weights.

\paragraph{Relation and trigger.}
The relevant relation is the controlled survival entry from the $f_3$-stress coordinate to a translation readout $T_j$.  In this chapter the admissible readouts are bounded translation-layer rows such as tAI, stAI, or a matched ribosome-occupancy or translation-efficiency estimate.  The support condition is not topological similarity and not a rank-saturated fitted surface.  It is the existence of a non-vacuous positive controlled entry
$$
\begin{aligned}
S_{f_3\text{-stress},T_j} &> 0
\end{aligned}
$$
after amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, CDS length, $M$-density, matched mRNA abundance where applicable, rank, and simpler-baseline controls.  The trigger for expecting such a row is local symmetry breaking in the translation layer: paired codons that are indistinguishable after amino-acid quotienting can become distinguishable only when their context tRNA or wobble weights differ.

\paragraph{Spectrum context.}
The surrounding powered spectrum keeps this claim narrow.  A non-tRNA residual optimality assay over $11$ computed organisms out of $16$ requested organisms classified the dominant residual axis as $f_3$ translation selection.  The two-axis closure assay on the same organism count reported that a third axis is present, because $d_\perp$ had systematically positive held-out $R^2$ and the $d_\perp$ directions were mutually same-signed across organisms.  The per-organism axis-strength spectrum was led by $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$ on average, followed by $d_{\mathrm{tRNA}}$; all $11$ per-organism dominant counts went to $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$, while the largest GC3 rank effect was only $\rho=0.136$ for $d_{\mathrm{usage}}$.  These finite rows motivate the third-axis boundary as a real residual-analysis target, but they do not replace the translation-readout statistic.

\paragraph{Local falsification rule.}
The claim fails at the BioReality interface if every controlled $f_3$-stress to translation-readout entry is non-positive, or if the available translation matrix is rank-degenerate in the sense that it carries no organism- or CDS-matched distinction beyond a saturated fit.  In particular, a three-organism all-$1.0$ controlled surface is treated as a null boundary, not as survival.  The same refusal applies to modeled tAI or stAI coupling when it is not joined to a nondegenerate readout design: modeled adaptation weights may define a bounded translation-layer surface, but they are not observed ribosome occupancy.

\paragraph{No-promotion boundary.}
The chapter therefore licenses only the following finite statement: the $B^*_{Q6}$ $f_3$-stress coordinate is a candidate translation-readout distinction in contexts where third-position wobble or tRNA weights break the paired-codon symmetry, and the current powered third-axis mechanism row says that the residual is selection-aligned rather than GC-explained while its CSC or positional identity still needs layer-matched evidence.  Nothing in this row establishes translation realization beyond the declared $S_{f_3\text{-stress},T_j}$ statistic.  It also does not establish folding, physical admissibility, protein abundance, localization, stability, molecular function, phenotype, mechanism, causation, adaptation, or a universal biological law.  Each of those interpretations would require its own matched contact and its own controlled statistic after the translation row is already non-vacuous and positive.
