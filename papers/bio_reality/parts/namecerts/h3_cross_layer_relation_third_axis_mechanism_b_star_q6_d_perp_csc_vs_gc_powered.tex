\paragraph{Finite carrier.}

\paragraph{Observed finite facts.}
The powered third-axis identity run used $5$ folds, required at least $500$ proteins per organism, computed $11$ organisms out of $16$ requested, and used $41$ synonymous design columns.  It identified $d_{\perp}$ provisionally with within-family usage frequency, with mean cosine $0.128$ and one-sided sign-test $p = 0.000488$.  The mechanism run for the present claim used the same finite scale: $5$ folds, $500$ minimum proteins per organism, $11$ computed organisms out of $16$, and $41$ synonymous design columns.  Its recorded conclusion is selection-not-GC, not-wobble-clustered, with CSC or positional identity still requiring an additional outside assay.  The stated reason is that GC-orthogonalized $d_{\perp}$ retains significant cross-species alignment and held-out $R^2$.

\paragraph{Relation tested.}
The proposed cross-layer relation is not that third-position topology itself is a biological output.  It is the narrower assertion that the $f_3$-paired synonymous imbalance can become visible in a translation readout only when the translation context breaks the $f_3$ symmetry.  In symbols, if $c$ and $c^{f_3}$ are paired codons after amino-acid projection, then a translation-layer distinction requires a context weight asymmetry such as $w_c \ne w_{c^{f_3}}$ and a non-degenerate controlled survival row:
$$\begin{aligned}
S_{f_3\text{-stress},T_j} &> 0,\\
T_j &\in \{\mathrm{tAI},\mathrm{stAI},\mathrm{ribosome\ occupancy},\mathrm{translation\ efficiency}\}.
\end{aligned}$$
The control set is part of the statement rather than a later interpretation: amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, CDS length, $M$-density, matched mRNA abundance where translation efficiency is used, rank sufficiency, and simpler codon baselines must be accounted for before the entry can be read as surviving.

\paragraph{Power and neighboring evidence.}
The broader finite panel is consistent with treating this as a real candidate axis but not yet as a measured translation mechanism.  A separate optimal-residual-axis run on $11$ computed organisms out of $16$, again with $5$ folds, $500$ minimum proteins per organism, and $41$ synonymous design columns, reports that the non-tRNA residual optimality aligns most with $f_3$ translation selection.  The two-axis closure run reports that a third axis is present: $d_{\perp}$ has systematically positive held-out $R^2$ and directions are mutually same-signed across organisms.  The axis-strength spectrum, on the same $11$ of $16$ organism scale and $41$ synonymous design columns, records $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$ as the mean leader, followed by $d_{\mathrm{tRNA}}$; all $11$ per-organism dominant counts are assigned to $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$, while the largest GC3 rank effect is only $\rho = 0.136$ for $d_{\mathrm{usage}}$.  The R-sense tRNA-poverty run is larger, with $40$ computed organisms out of $46$ requested, $6$ skipped organisms, $1000$ permutations, and $\alpha = 0.05$; it supports a tRNA-supply mechanism for R avoidance under GtRNAdb tGCN plus dos Reis wobble weights.  These facts justify asking for a translation-row test, but they do not themselves supply that row.

\paragraph{Break condition.}
The conjecture fails at this layer if the controlled translation-readout surface remains non-positive or rank-degenerate across a powered organism panel.  In particular, an all-$1.0$ three-organism surface is not survival evidence; it is a saturated rank boundary.  The operative test is the finite statement
$$\begin{aligned}
\exists j\quad S_{f_3\text{-stress},T_j} &> 0
\end{aligned}$$
after the declared controls and baseline ablations.  If no such $T_j$ exists for tAI, stAI, ribosome occupancy, or translation efficiency, then the $f_3$-stress coordinate remains a codon-residual coordinate with no established translation-readout survival.
