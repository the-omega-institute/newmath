\subsection{Third-position stress as a bounded translation-readout coordinate}
\label{sec:bioreality-q6-f3-stress-translation-readout}

\paragraph{Finite surface.}
This record concerns a finite coding-sequence surface indexed by organism, amino-acid quotient class, and the $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinate called $f_3$-stress.  The coordinate is not read as a protein-level property.  It is a residual synonymy coordinate after amino-acid projection, tested only against bounded translation-layer rows such as tAI, stAI, ribosome occupancy, or translation efficiency.  The intended finite question is whether a third-position symmetry break survives after controls for amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, coding-sequence length, $M$-density, matched mRNA abundance, and simpler codon baselines.

\paragraph{Saturation data.}
The finite $f_3$ saturation packet gives a concrete support set rather than an open-ended codon vocabulary.  The union $M=\operatorname{Med}(R)\cup\{ACA,ACG\}$ contains
$$
\begin{aligned}
M=\{&AAA,AAG,ACA,ACG,AGA,AGG,AUA,AUG,CUA,CUC,CUG,CUU,\\
&UAA,UAG,UCA,UCG,UGA,UGG,UUA,UUG\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
After $f_3$ saturation of $R$, the two codons $ACA$ and $ACG$ drop out, leaving
$$
\begin{aligned}
\operatorname{Sat}_{f_3}(R)=\{&AAA,AAG,AGA,AGG,AUA,AUG,CUA,CUC,CUG,CUU,\\
&UAA,UAG,UCA,UCG,UGA,UGG,UUA,UUG\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The reported $f_3$ boundary is empty.  Thus the coordinate is finite and closed at this codon-support level: there is no remaining boundary codon in the recorded saturation table.  This closure is a combinatorial fact about the finite codon surface, not a biological conclusion by itself.

\paragraph{Powered third-axis assay.}
The powered third-axis mechanism run for the claim used $5$ folds, required at least $500$ proteins per organism, computed $11$ organisms out of $16$ requested, and used $41$ synonymous design columns.  Its recorded status is passed.  The finite conclusion is
$$
\begin{aligned}
&\text{selection-not-GC;}\\
&\text{not-wobble-clustered;}\\
&\text{CSC/positional identity needs an additional measured contact.}
\end{aligned}
$$
The stated reason is that the GC-orthogonalized $d_{\perp}$ direction keeps significant cross-species alignment and held-out $R^2$.  This is a negative and bounding result: GC alone does not explain the third residual direction in this assay, and simple wobble clustering is not enough to identify the mechanism.

\paragraph{Relation to the third residual direction.}
A companion identity run computed the same $11$ of $16$ requested organisms with $5$ folds, the same minimum of $500$ proteins per organism, and $41$ synonymous design columns.  It provisionally aligned $d_{\perp}$ to usage frequency within synonymous family, with mean cosine $0.128$ and one-sided sign-test $p=0.000488$.  Another powered residual-optimality run identified the dominant non-tRNA residual axis as $f_3$ translation selection.  A two-axis closure run found that a third axis is present because $d_{\perp}$ has systematically positive held-out $R^2$ and same-signed directions across organisms.  These entries justify treating $f_3$-stress as a named residual coordinate on the finite surface, while still keeping the interpretation confined to the tested readout layer.

\paragraph{Translation readout form.}
For a translation readout $T_j$, the bounded statistic has the form
$$
\begin{aligned}
S_{f_3,T_j} &= \Delta\operatorname{Fit}\bigl(T_j; f_3\text{-stress}\mid Z\bigr),\\
Z &= \{\text{amino-acid composition},\text{ organism},GC3,\text{ length},M\text{-density},\text{ mRNA control},\text{ simpler codon baselines}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
A positive entry requires $S_{f_3,T_j}>0$ on organism-matched or coding-sequence-matched rows.  A rank-degenerate all-$1.0$ surface on three organisms is not counted as survival; it is a saturated null boundary.  Likewise, modeled tAI or stAI weights can supply a bounded codon-adaptation surface, but they are not the same thing as observed ribosome occupancy unless a matched ribosome or translation-efficiency dataset is joined to the coordinate table.

\paragraph{Mechanistic break condition.}
The coordinate becomes a candidate dynamical distinction only where third-position symmetry is broken by context tRNA or wobble weights.  In finite-row notation, the required asymmetry is
$$
\begin{aligned}
w_c \neq w_{c^{f_3}},
\end{aligned}
$$
for an $f_3$-paired synonymous codon pair after amino-acid projection.  Without that asymmetry, codon topology alone does not yield a directional translation claim.  With that asymmetry, the claim is still only that the row is testable: it must survive the controlled statistic above before any positive translation readout is reported.

\paragraph{Supporting finite context.}
The tRNA-poverty boundary run computed $40$ organisms out of $46$ requested, skipped $6$, used $1000$ permutations at $\alpha=0.05$, and concluded that $R$ sense codons are systematically tRNA-poor under GtRNAdb tGCN with dos Reis wobble weights.  This supports a supply-side route for $R$ avoidance, but it does not complete the $f_3$-stress translation-readout test.  The axis-strength spectrum run computed $11$ organisms out of $16$ requested and found that captured spectra were led by $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$ on average, followed by $d_{\mathrm{tRNA}}$; all $11$ per-organism dominant counts were $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$, and the largest GC3 rank effect was $d_{\mathrm{usage}}$ with $\rho=0.136$.  The three-axis sufficiency run, also at $11$ computed organisms out of $16$ requested with $5$ folds and $41$ synonymous design columns, concluded that a fourth mechanism is present.  Together these entries make the present record conservative: the third axis is real enough as a finite residual direction to demand readout testing, but the surrounding spectrum warns against compressing every residual effect into one mechanism.

\paragraph{Local no-promotion rule.}
This chapter does not infer protein abundance, folded structure, localization, stability, molecular function, organismal phenotype, physical admissibility, or a universal biological rule from the $B^*_{Q6}$ coordinate, from codon-window geometry, from tAI or stAI weights, or from the passed third-axis mechanism assay.  It also does not claim an observed translation effect unless an organism-matched or coding-sequence-matched translation row supplies a non-vacuous positive $S_{f_3,T_j}$ after the declared controls.  The present finite result is therefore precise: GC is not sufficient for the powered $d_{\perp}$ direction, simple wobble clustering is not the identified mechanism, and the remaining CSC or positional identity requires a separate measured contact before it can be read as a biological mechanism.
