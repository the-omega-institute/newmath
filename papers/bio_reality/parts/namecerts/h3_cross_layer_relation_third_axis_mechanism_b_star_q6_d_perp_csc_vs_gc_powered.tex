\subsection{The $f_3$-Stress Boundary for the Third Residual Axis}
\label{sec:bioq6-f3-stress-third-axis-mechanism}

\paragraph{Finite coordinate under test.}
The record considered here is the finite coding-sequence residual surface obtained after amino-acid projection, restricted to the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ third-position stress coordinate and to rows for which translation-layer readouts are named separately from codon geometry. The coordinate is not a protein-level assertion. It is a finite residual coordinate on synonymous codon imbalance: a row may enter the test only after amino-acid composition, organism identity, GC3, coding-sequence length, $M$-density, and transcript controls have been treated as baseline covariates. The finite $f_3$ saturation row fixes the raw codon support. The set $M=\operatorname{Med}(R)\cup\{ACA\}$ is
$$\begin{aligned}
M=\{&AAA,AAG,ACA,ACG,AGA,AGG,AUA,AUG,CUA,CUC,CUG,CUU,\\
&UAA,UAG,UCA,UCG,UGA,UGG,UUA,UUG\}.
\end{aligned}$$
The $f_3$-saturated part removes the $ACA,ACG$ pair and leaves
$$\begin{aligned}
\operatorname{Sat}_{f_3}(R)=\{&AAA,AAG,AGA,AGG,AUA,AUG,CUA,CUC,CUG,CUU,\\
&UAA,UAG,UCA,UCG,UGA,UGG,UUA,UUG\}.
\end{aligned}$$
The recorded $f_3$ boundary is empty, $\partial_{f_3}=\varnothing$. Thus the coordinate is finite and closed at the codon-support level, but the biological question is deliberately narrower: whether this closed synonymous residual coordinate survives in a matched translation row once the usual compositional explanations have been removed.

\paragraph{Observed finite computation.}
The powered third-axis mechanism run has status passed with run id $d0893337f0dd$. It computed $11$ organisms out of $16$ requested organisms, used $5$ folds, required at least $500$ proteins per organism, and used $41$ synonymous design columns. Its conclusion was: selection-not-GC; not-wobble-clustered; CSC/positional identity needs external data. The finite reason recorded for this call is that the GC-orthogonalized $d_{\perp}$ direction keeps significant cross-species alignment and held-out $R^2$. A paired identity computation, run on the same $11$ of $16$ organism scale with $5$ folds and $41$ synonymous design columns, identified $d_{\perp}$ provisionally with usage frequency within synonymous family, with mean cosine $0.128$ and one-sided sign-test $p=0.000488$. These numbers support a residual third axis after GC removal, but they do not by themselves identify a measured ribosome, translation-efficiency, protein-abundance, localization, stability, structure, function, phenotype, or universal biological law.

\paragraph{Relation to the wider residual spectrum.}
The neighboring finite rows are useful because they constrain the interpretation of the third axis. The axis-strength spectrum was computed for $11$ organisms out of $16$ requested, with $5$ folds, a minimum of $500$ proteins per organism, and $41$ synonymous design columns. In that spectrum, $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$ led on average, followed by $d_{\mathrm{tRNA}}$; every computed organism had $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$ as its dominant axis, giving the count $\{d_{\mathrm{resid4}}:11\}$. The domain split was imbalanced, with $1$ archaeal, $2$ bacterial, and $8$ eukaryotic organisms, so the domain averages are descriptive rather than a domain-level law. The largest GC3 rank effect was small, $\rho=0.136$ for $d_{\mathrm{usage}}$. In the optimal-residual row, the non-tRNA residual optimality aligned most strongly with $f_3$ translation selection. In the two-axis closure row, the finite call was third\_axis\_present because $d_{\perp}$ had systematically positive held-out $R^2$ and mutually same-signed directions across organisms. The three-axis sufficiency row nevertheless concluded fourth\_mechanism\_present, so the $f_3$ third-axis record is not a complete explanation of the translation-associated residual surface.

\paragraph{Wobble-breaking trigger.}
The intended biological trigger is asymmetry between a codon $c$ and its $f_3$ partner $c^{f_3}$ in context tRNA supply or wobble weight, written locally as $w_c\ne w_{c^{f_3}}$. The finite evidence does not license a direct statement that such asymmetry has been observed in ribosome occupancy for this coordinate. It only says that a GC-orthogonal residual axis survives computationally and that the remaining CSC or positional identity needs a separate contact. The tRNA-poverty boundary row gives one relevant bounded supply fact: the $R$ sense codons are systematically tRNA-poor under GtRNAdb tGCN plus dos Reis wobble weights, using $40$ computed organisms out of $46$ requested, $6$ skipped organisms, $1000$ permutations, and $\alpha=0.05$. That row supports a tRNA-supply route for $R$ avoidance, but it is not the same as a same-CDS, same-organism translation-efficiency survival statistic for the $f_3$-stress coordinate.

\paragraph{Survival statistic.}
For this chapter, the admissible readout is a controlled survival entry $S_{f_3\text{-stress},T_j}$ where $T_j$ is a bounded translation-layer row such as tAI, stAI, ribosome occupancy, or translation efficiency. A positive claim requires a non-vacuous finite row after the declared controls. In local notation the permitted test is
$$\begin{aligned}
S_{f_3\text{-stress},T_j}>0
\end{aligned}$$
only after conditioning on amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, coding-sequence length, $M$-density, matched mRNA abundance where a translation-efficiency denominator is used, rank sufficiency, and simpler codon baselines. The current verified finite row for the named claim is not yet that survival row. It is a third-axis mechanism row: selection-not-GC, not-wobble-clustered, with CSC or positional identity still requiring a separate measured contact. Consequently the chapter may state a ready finite conjectural boundary, but it may not report measured translation survival for the exact $f_3$-stress packet.

\paragraph{Local no-promotion rule.}
No translation realization follows from codon support, GC-orthogonal residual alignment, modeled tAI or stAI weights, tRNA copy summaries, or review routing alone. No folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, protein localization, protein stability, phenotype, mechanism, adaptation, causation, or universal biological law follows from this coordinate unless a separate layer-matched contact supplies the corresponding finite rows and statistic. In particular, a three-organism all-$1.0$ rank-saturated matrix is a null boundary rather than survival evidence, and a modeled adaptation weight is not an observed ribosome-footprint or protein-output measurement. The only bounded interpretation available here is the following: the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ $f_3$-stress residual coordinate is a finite candidate axis whose positive translation meaning remains conditional on a nondegenerate controlled $S_{f_3\text{-stress},T_j}$ row. If that row stays non-positive or rank-degenerate across a powered organism panel, then this cross-layer conjecture is falsified at the translation-readout boundary.
