\subsection{Third-position stress as a bounded translation-readout target}\label{sec:biorality-q6-f3-stress-third-axis-mechanism}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite coordinate surface.}
This record concerns the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual coordinate obtained after amino-acid projection of coding-sequence codon counts. The object is not the raw codon table and not a protein-level phenotype. It is a finite residual coordinate surface on synonymous rows, with special attention to the third-position stress coordinate induced by the $f_3$ pairing. The finite saturation row fixes the relevant codon support: the combined $M=\mathrm{Med}_{R}\cup\{\mathrm{ACA},\mathrm{ACG}\}$ row is
$$\begin{aligned}
M=\{&\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{ACA},\mathrm{ACG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\\
&\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\}.
\end{aligned}$$
After $f_3$ saturation the row loses exactly the two ACA/ACG entries, leaving
$$\begin{aligned}
\operatorname{Sat}_{f_3}(R)=\{&\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\\
&\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\},
\end{aligned}$$
and the reported $f_3$ boundary list is empty. This makes the present claim a finite-row question about whether a residual third-position imbalance survives a controlled translation readout, rather than a general assertion about codon biology.

\paragraph{Observed powered row.}
The powered third-axis mechanism assay was run with five folds, at least $500$ proteins per organism, $41$ synonymous design columns, and $11$ computed organisms out of $16$ requested. Its recorded status is passed. The finite conclusion is: GC-orthogonalized $d_{\perp}$ keeps significant cross-species alignment and held-out $R^2$; the resulting mechanism call is selection-not-GC, not-wobble-clustered, while the CSC or positional identity remains dependent on additional contact rows. In formula form, the row supports only the restricted separation
$$\begin{aligned}
 d_{\perp}\not\equiv \mathrm{GC3}\quad\text{under the assayed controls,}
\end{aligned}$$
not a completed biochemical explanation of what the third axis physically is.

\paragraph{Relation to neighboring finite rows.}
The identity assay for $d_{\perp}$ used the same five-fold, $500$-protein, $41$-column, $11/16$ organism design and provisionally aligned $d_{\perp}$ with usage frequency within family, with mean cosine $0.128$ and one-sided sign-test $p=0.000488$. The two-axis closure assay reports a third-axis-present conclusion because $d_{\perp}$ has systematically positive held-out $R^2$ and mutually same-signed directions across organisms. The optimal residual-axis assay reports the dominant non-tRNA residual optimality axis as $f_3$ translation selection. The axis-strength spectrum row reports $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$ as the average leader, followed by $d_{\mathrm{tRNA}}$, with per-organism dominant counts $\{d_{\mathrm{resid4}}:11\}$ and a largest GC3 rank effect on $d_{\mathrm{usage}}$ of $\rho=0.136$. These finite rows are compatible with the present interpretation: the coordinate is not explained away by GC3 alone, but its biological reading remains bounded by the actual readout row used.

\paragraph{Translation-readout gate.}
The $f_3$ stress coordinate becomes a biological readout only where a context tRNA or wobble-weight asymmetry breaks the third-position pairing, written locally as $w_c\neq w_{c^{f_3}}$. Even then, the permitted target is narrow. The entry to be estimated is a controlled survival statistic $S_{f3\text{-}stress,T_j}$ for a translation readout $T_j$, such as tAI, stAI, ribosome occupancy, or translation efficiency. The supporting condition is not a visible codon-topology pattern by itself, but a positive, nondegenerate controlled row after amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, length, M-density, matched mRNA, rank, and simpler-baseline controls. A schematic admissible row has the form
$$\begin{aligned}
S_{f3\text{-}stress,T_j}>0
\quad\text{after } Z=\{\mathrm{AA},\mathrm{organism},\mathrm{GC3},\mathrm{length},M\text{-}\mathrm{density},\mathrm{mRNA},\mathrm{rank},\mathrm{baselines}\}.
\end{aligned}$$
If the same organism and same CDS rows do not contain codon counts, wobble-breaking weights, translation readout, and matched mRNA control, then the statistic has not been supplied. In particular, a three-organism all-$1.0$ controlled matrix is a rank-saturated boundary, not survival evidence.

\paragraph{Mechanistic scope.}
The tRNA-poverty row supplies a separate finite contact: across $40$ computed organisms out of $46$ requested, with $1000$ permutations at $\alpha=0.05$, R-sense codons are systematically tRNA-poor under GtRNAdb tGCN plus dos Reis wobble weights. That row makes tRNA supply a plausible explanatory direction for R avoidance, but it does not by itself complete the $f_3$ stress translation statistic. Likewise, the fourth-axis checks prevent overinterpretation: the mRNA-stability assay reports fourth-axis-not-mRNA-stability; the codon-pair assay reports not-codon-pair; the translation-accuracy assay reports not-accuracy over $11/16$ organisms with $200$ permutations; and the elongation-dwell assay is only partial in Saccharomyces cerevisiae. These rows sharpen the finite boundary: the present third-axis record separates selection-like residual structure from GC3 and from simple wobble clustering, while leaving the exact CSC or position-resolved identity open.

\paragraph{Local refusal boundary.}
No translation realization, ribosome occupancy, protein abundance, folding statement, physical admissibility claim, molecular function, phenotype, detailed mechanism, or universal biological law follows from the residual coordinate alone. No such conclusion follows from tAI or stAI weights alone, from codon usage tables alone, from review readiness, or from the notation $S^{QT}$ alone. Those interpretations require their own layer-matched contacts and their own finite statistics. The strongest local claim is therefore conditional and finite: in a powered multi-organism panel, the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ third-axis residual is not reduced to GC3 and is not established as a simple wobble cluster; it may be tested as an $f_3$-stress translation-readout distinction only after same-row translation data and controls make $S_{f3\text{-}stress,T_j}$ non-vacuous and positive.
