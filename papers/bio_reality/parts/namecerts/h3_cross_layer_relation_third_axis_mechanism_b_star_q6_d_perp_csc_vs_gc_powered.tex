\subsection{Third-position stress as a bounded translation-readout conjecture}
\label{sec:bioreality-q6-f3-stress-translation-readout}

\paragraph{Finite carrier.}
The object considered here is a finite residual coordinate surface on coding sequences, restricted to the $B^*_{Q6}$ third-position stress coordinate and to readouts that can be stated at the translation layer. The codon-side finite rows have an explicit $f_3$ saturation boundary. In the recorded row, the set $M=\mathrm{Med}(R)\cup\{\mathrm{ACA}\}$ contains
$$
\begin{aligned}
&\{\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{ACA},\mathrm{ACG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\\
&\quad \mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The $f_3$ saturation row is the same finite family with $\mathrm{ACA}$ and $\mathrm{ACG}$ removed:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\operatorname{Sat}_{f_3}(R)=\{&\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\\
&\mathrm{CUU},\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The recorded $f_3$ boundary is empty, so this chapter treats the coordinate as a finite residual contrast whose biological use must come from matched organism rows, not from an unbounded codon-topology assertion.

\paragraph{Claim.}
After amino-acid quotienting, the conjectural third-position stress signal is not a direct claim about protein abundance, folded structure, localization, stability, function, phenotype, or a universal biological rule. It is a narrower assertion: in organisms or contexts where tRNA availability or wobble weights break the $f_3$ symmetry between paired synonymous codons, the $B^*_{Q6}$ $f_3$-stress residual may survive as a controlled translation-readout distinction. The admissible readback is a row such as $S_{f_3\text{-}\mathrm{stress},T_j}$ for tAI, stAI, ribosome occupancy, or translation efficiency, estimated after amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, CDS length, $M$-density, matched mRNA, rank, and simpler codon-baseline controls.

\paragraph{Computed mechanism row.}
The finite mechanism run for this subject has status passed. It used $5$ folds, required at least $500$ proteins per organism, computed $11$ organisms out of $16$ requested organisms, and used $41$ synonymous-design columns. Its conclusion is that the third axis is selection-associated rather than a GC artifact, is not explained as a wobble-clustered axis, and still needs an external CSC or positional identity contact. The stated reason is that the GC-orthogonalized $d_\perp$ coordinate retains significant cross-species alignment and held-out $R^2$. Thus the present row supports a controlled residual axis against a simple GC explanation, but it does not by itself produce the mandatory same-row translation statistic.

\paragraph{Neighboring finite rows.}
The companion identity run also computed $11$ organisms out of $16$ requested organisms with $5$ folds, a $500$ protein minimum, and $41$ synonymous-design columns. It provisionally identified $d_\perp$ with usage frequency within synonymous family, with mean cosine $0.128$ and one-sided sign-test $p=0.000488$. A separate residual-optimality row concluded that the non-tRNA residual optimality axis aligns most with $f_3$ translation selection. The two-axis closure row concluded that a third axis is present because $d_\perp$ has systematically positive held-out $R^2$ and organism-level directions are mutually same-signed. These rows make the third-position stress conjecture biologically pointed: the residual axis is not merely a codon list, but it remains bounded by the absence of a direct measured translation-readout survival row for this exact subject.

\paragraph{tRNA and wobble asymmetry.}
The boundary-set tRNA-poverty run supplies the relevant mechanism pressure. It computed $40$ organisms out of $46$ requested organisms, skipped $6$, used $1000$ permutations at $\alpha=0.05$, and concluded that $R$ sense codons are systematically tRNA-poor under GtRNAdb tGCN together with dos Reis wobble weights. This supports a tRNA-supply explanation for $R$ avoidance, but the statement is still weaker than a same-CDS translation-efficiency result. The conjecture therefore asks whether $w_c\ne w_{c^{f_3}}$ is accompanied by a surviving translation row after amino-acid projection and the declared controls.

\paragraph{Spectrum placement.}
In the axis-strength spectrum run, $11$ organisms were computed out of $16$ requested organisms, again with $5$ folds, a $500$ protein minimum, and $41$ synonymous-design columns. The captured spectra were led by $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$ on average, followed by $d_{\mathrm{tRNA}}$, and the per-organism dominant count was $\{d_{\mathrm{resid4}}:11\}$. Domain means were explicitly descriptive because the panel was imbalanced, with $1$ archaeal, $2$ bacterial, and $8$ eukaryotic organisms. The largest GC3 rank effect was small, with $\rho=0.136$ for $d_{\mathrm{usage}}$. This places the $f_3$-stress conjecture inside a finite multi-axis surface rather than a single-organism anecdote.

\paragraph{Falsification rule.}
The conjecture fails at the translation boundary if every controlled entry satisfies $S_{f_3\text{-}\mathrm{stress},T_j}\le 0$, or if the only available matrix is rank-degenerate, including the three-organism all-$1.0$ surface. A non-vacuous positive result must be organism-matched or CDS-matched, must keep codon usage, tRNA or wobble weights, translation readout, and matched mRNA rows distinct, and must survive the stated controls. A positive tAI or stAI association alone is not an observed ribosome-occupancy result; a ribosome-profiling contact name alone is not a joined TE matrix; and matched mRNA abundance is a control covariate rather than a translation output.

\paragraph{Local boundary.}
The chapter therefore records a bounded, testable translation-readout conjecture. The available finite rows support a non-GC third residual axis, a usage-frequency identity signal, a tRNA-poverty mechanism pressure, and a multi-organism residual spectrum. They do not authorize promotion to protein abundance, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, phenotype, mechanism in the strong causal sense, or universality. Each of those claims would require its own layer-matched contact and its own controlled statistic after the translation row is established.
