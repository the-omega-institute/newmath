\subsection{The $f_3$-stress coordinate as a translation-gated residual surface}
\label{sec:bioq6-f3-stress-translation-gated-residual-surface}

\paragraph{Finite coordinate under test.}
This record isolates the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual coordinate that remains after synonymous codon usage has been projected through amino-acid identity and the ordinary organism-scale covariates have been separated. The coordinate of interest is the $f_3$-stress row: it compares codons paired by the third-position $f_3$ action and asks whether a synonymous imbalance remains visible after amino-acid composition has already been accounted for. The finite saturation computation fixes the relevant boundary. The set $M = \mathrm{Med}_{R} \cup \{\mathrm{ACA}\}$ contains exactly the twenty codons
$\{\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{ACA},\mathrm{ACG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\}$.
After $f_3$ saturation of $R$, the two codons $\mathrm{ACA}$ and $\mathrm{ACG}$ are removed from the saturated list, leaving the eighteen-codon set
$\{\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\}$.
The recorded $f_3$ boundary is empty. Thus the candidate axis is not introduced by an unresolved finite boundary row; it is a residual direction inside a closed finite codon surface.

\paragraph{Controlled readout form.}
The intended biological question is not whether codon topology alone exists, but whether the $f_3$-paired residual coordinate survives in a translation readout. For a coding sequence $x$, let $q_{f_3}(x)$ denote the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ $f_3$-stress residual after amino-acid projection, and let $T_j(x)$ be a bounded translation-layer readout such as a tAI score, an stAI score, ribosome occupancy, or translation efficiency. The local survival statistic has the form
$$
\begin{aligned}
S_{f_3,T_j}
  &= \Delta \mathrm{Fit}\bigl(T_j; q_{f_3} \mid Z\bigr),\\
Z
  &= \{\text{amino-acid composition},\text{ organism},\mathrm{GC3},\text{ CDS length},\text{$M$-density},\text{ mRNA control when available}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
A positive claim requires $S_{f_3,T_j} > 0$ under the stated controls and under ablation by simpler codon baselines. A rank-degenerate all-one matrix is a null boundary rather than a survival observation. This is why a three-organism saturated surface is not counted as evidence for the row.

\paragraph{Observed finite power checks.}
The powered third-axis mechanism run used five folds, required at least $500$ proteins per organism, computed $11$ organisms from $16$ requested organisms, and used $41$ synonymous design columns. Its recorded conclusion is selection rather than GC: after GC orthogonalization, $d_{\perp}$ retained cross-species alignment and held-out predictive signal. The same run also rejected a wobble-clustered interpretation at this level and left the CSC or positional identity as requiring an additional layer-matched contact. In symbols, the finite result supports the restricted statement
$$
\begin{aligned}
 d_{\perp} &\not\equiv \text{GC-only axis},\\
 d_{\perp} &\not\equiv \text{wobble-cluster-only axis},\\
 \text{CSC/positional identity} &\text{ remains unresolved without a separate contact.}
\end{aligned}
$$
This should be read as a controlled residual-axis classification, not as a direct translation-mechanism claim.

\paragraph{Relation to the surrounding $Q6$ spectrum.}
The same finite panel gives the $f_3$-stress row its proper scale. The third-axis identity assay, also run with five folds, at least $500$ proteins per organism, $11$ computed organisms from $16$ requested, and $41$ synonymous design columns, provisionally identified $d_{\perp}$ with usage frequency within synonymous families, with mean cosine $0.128$ and one-sided sign-test $p=0.000488$. A separate residual-optimality assay concluded that the non-tRNA residual optimal direction aligns most with $f_3$ translation selection. The two-axis closure assay returned \emph{third-axis-present}, because $d_{\perp}$ had systematically positive held-out $R^2$ and the $d_{\perp}$ directions were mutually same-signed across organisms. The three-axis sufficiency assay returned \emph{fourth-mechanism-present}, so the $f_3$-stress coordinate is not the end of the residual spectrum. The axis-strength survey computed $11$ organisms from $16$ requested organisms and found that the captured spectra were led by $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$ on average, followed by $d_{\mathrm{tRNA}}$; all $11$ per-organism dominant counts were assigned to $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$, while the largest reported GC3 rank effect was only $\rho=0.136$ for $d_{\mathrm{usage}}$. These companion facts keep the present row narrow: $f_3$ stress is a real candidate residual axis inside the powered surface, but it is not promoted to a universal explanatory axis.

\paragraph{Wobble-breaking requirement.}
The row becomes biologically testable only where context tRNA supply or wobble weighting distinguishes an $f_3$-paired codon from its partner. If $c$ and $c^{f_3}$ have equal translation weight in the relevant organismal context, then the $f_3$ contrast can remain a codon-geometry contrast without becoming a translation-readout contrast. The required asymmetry is local:
$$
\begin{aligned}
w_c &\neq w_{c^{f_3}},\\
S_{f_3,T_j} &> 0
\quad\text{after the control set $Z$ is applied.}
\end{aligned}
$$
The tRNA-poverty assay supplies a related finite pressure on the $R$ sense codons: across $40$ computed organisms from $46$ requested organisms, with $6$ skipped organisms and $1000$ permutations at $\alpha=0.05$, $R$ sense codons were systematically tRNA-poor under GtRNAdb tGCN together with dos Reis wobble weights. That result is compatible with a supply-side reading of codon avoidance, but it does not by itself compute $S_{f_3,T_j}$ for the present coordinate.

\paragraph{Reality contacts and refusal boundary.}
The permitted readouts are organism-level codon usage, tRNA gene-copy or wobble-weight summaries, modeled tAI or stAI weights, and matched ribosome-profiling or translation-efficiency rows with mRNA controls. Codon tables and modeled weights may define a bounded readout surface, but they are not ribosome-occupancy observations. Ribosome profiling or translation efficiency can test the row only after same-organism and same-CDS joins have been made and the control set has been fitted. Matched mRNA abundance is a covariate for translation-efficiency residualization, not a translation output. No statement about protein abundance, folding, localization, stability, physical admissibility, biological function, phenotype, mechanism, or universality follows from this chapter unless a separate layer-appropriate contact supplies that readout and a controlled statistic is recorded for it.

\paragraph{Falsification rule.}
The conjecture is falsified at the declared boundary if every powered organism or context with adequate rows satisfies
$$
\begin{aligned}
S_{f_3,T_j} &\leq 0
\end{aligned}
$$
for tAI, stAI, ribosome occupancy, or translation efficiency after amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, length, $M$-density, mRNA, rank, and simpler-baseline controls. It is also held at null if the fitted surface is rank-degenerate, even when the displayed entries are numerically all equal to $1.0$. The present finite evidence therefore supports a scoped residual-axis statement: the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ $f_3$-stress direction is selection-like rather than GC-only in the powered residual geometry, and it becomes a translation-readout claim only at contexts where wobble or tRNA weights break the $f_3$ symmetry and the controlled survival entry is positive.
