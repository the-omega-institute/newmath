\subsection{Leave-One-Code-Out Readback for a Window-Six Codon Tile}
\label{sec:bioreality-h1-leave-one-code-out}

\paragraph{Object and layer.}
The finite object in this record is the standard-code codon table read through a six-coordinate codon display.  The coordinates are used only to organize the sixty-four codons into local faces, corners, closures, spectra, and relation rows.  The biological contact is narrower: a curated standard genetic-code table assigns each codon to an amino-acid label or to a stop label.  This chapter therefore treats the row as a code-read observation.  It does not claim translation dynamics, folded protein geometry, chemical admissibility, cellular function, a mechanism of code origin, or a universal biological law.

\paragraph{Distinguished codon set.}
The finite row singles out a thirteen-codon set $R$.  The row verifies both the cardinality and the explicit membership:
$$
\begin{aligned}
R=\{&\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\\
&\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UUA}\},\\
|R|&=13.
\end{aligned}
$$
The same row records that this set has the punctured-cube face data
$$
\begin{aligned}
f(R)=(13,16,4).
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the observed row is not merely a list of codons: it carries a finite local shape in the six-bit display.  The member list is concentrated in recognizable code-table modules: the AGR pair $\{\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG}\}$, the lysine-side singleton $\{\mathrm{AAA}\}$, the CUN quartet $\{\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU}\}$, the isoleucine--methionine singleton $\{\mathrm{AUA}\}$, the SSN pair $\{\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UUA}\}$, and the stop--tryptophan block $\{\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UGA}\}$.  The module audit is complete: it assigns all thirteen codons, leaves no codon missing from the modules, introduces no extra module codons, and reports no unassigned codon.

This residual is used here only as a finite numerical consistency check for the coordinate construction.  It is not a biochemical energy, a kinetic parameter, or a structural score.

\paragraph{Shape against randomized code-label controls.}
The finite comparison with shuffled code-label rows gives a sharp separation.  In $N=2000$ sampled controls with seed $42$, there was one exact shape match after $57377$ distinct-sample attempts and $55377$ duplicate retries.  The exact-shape probability estimate is therefore
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{\mathrm{exact}}=0.0005.
\end{aligned}
$$
The same experiment found $25$ controls with Jaccard similarity at least $0.9$, giving
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{J\geq 0.9}=0.0125.
\end{aligned}
$$
The row also includes an amino-acid-class-preserving control.  In $N=2000$ such controls, the exact-shape count was $0$, the observed $M$-size was $20$, and the lower-tail count at or below the observed size was $7$.  The corresponding finite estimates are
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{\mathrm{class\ exact}}&=0.0,\\
p_{\mathrm{class\ lower\ tail}}&=0.0035.
\end{aligned}
$$
These figures say that the local codon-tile shape is rare under the stated finite controls.  They do not say that the geometry is a law of molecular biology.

\paragraph{Leave-one-code-out result.}
The claim-specific row evaluates the ability of the local construction to recover targets when one code table is withheld.  Across $23$ evaluated tables and $63$ total targets, the baseline hit rate is
$$
\begin{aligned}
h_{\mathrm{base}}=0.19821739130434785.
\end{aligned}
$$
The observed hit rate above baseline is
$$
\begin{aligned}
h_{\mathrm{above\ base}}=0.9841269841269841.
\end{aligned}
$$
Using the interior row $M\setminus T$ gives
$$
\begin{aligned}
h_{M\setminus T}=0.9365079365079365,
\end{aligned}
$$
and adding the boundary recovers
$$
\begin{aligned}
h_{M\setminus T\,+\,\partial}=0.9841269841269841.
\end{aligned}
$$
The finite conclusion is therefore a local readback statement: the six-coordinate tile is highly predictive for withheld code-label targets under this experiment, with the boundary row accounting for nearly all recoveries above the baseline.

\paragraph{Leave-one-codon-out companion row.}
A companion codon-level row gives a finer boundary reading.  It records anchor count $0$, boundary count $5$, closure-coverage-high count $8$, predictable count $8$, and recovery rate $1.0$.  In symbols,
$$
\begin{aligned}
a&=0, & b&=5, & c_{\mathrm{high}}&=8, & p&=8, & \rho&=1.0.

This is consistent with the module classification above: every module has a predictable or boundary member.  It also explains why the boundary must remain visible in the statement.  Removing the boundary language would make the result sound like an unconditional reconstruction theorem, while the finite row actually describes a local code-read recovery with explicit edge cases.

\paragraph{Reality contact and blocked promotions.}
The only contact used here is the curated standard-code table.  That contact supplies codon-to-label readback and allows local label-split checks.  It does not observe ribosomal translation, tRNA charging kinetics, protein folding, thermodynamic admissibility, organismal function, or a universal mechanism.  Consequently, the correct scientific reading is constrained: the codon coordinates expose a rare and predictive local tile in the code table, while all higher biological layers require separate observations before they can be asserted.

\paragraph{Chapter verdict.}
For claim $\mathrm{h1.leave.one.code.out}$, the finite data support a ready code-layer statement.  The set $R$ has $13$ codons with punctured-cube face vector $(13,16,4)$; the spectral row is numerically coherent with $\lambda_M=0.6752479308830213$, $\lambda_R=0.4758352843197002$, and $\mu=3.051487585298128$; the exact-shape control gives $p=0.0005$; the amino-acid-class-preserving lower tail gives $p=0.0035$; and the leave-one-code-out experiment gives $0.9841269841269841$ hit rate above baseline across $23$ tables and $63$ targets.  The result is therefore a finite, contact-grounded code-read record, not a claim about translation, folding, physical admissibility, biological function, mechanism, or universality.
