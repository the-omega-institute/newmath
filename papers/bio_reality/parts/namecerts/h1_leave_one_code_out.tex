\subsection{Leave-One-Code-Out Recovery in the Window-Six Codon Cube}
\label{sec:bioreality-h1-leave-one-code-out}

\paragraph{Claim.}
The claim \texttt{h1.leave.one.code.out} records a code-layer recovery test on the standard-code codon table. The finite carrier is the six-coordinate codon cube $Q_6$, read as a codon display with local tile faces, singleton label corners, and three-point label classes. The biological contact is not a mechanism of translation; it is the curated standard-code assignment of codons to amino-acid or stop labels. The tested question is whether the window-six coordinate structure exposes a stable local boundary pattern when one genetic-code table is held out, while the label assignments themselves remain supplied by the curated biological table.

\paragraph{Finite codon region.}
The distinguished region $R$ has cardinality $13$. In the verified row it is exactly
$$
\begin{aligned}
R=\{&\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\\
&\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UUA}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The punctured-cube check gives the same codon set and the face data
$$
\begin{aligned}
(f_0,f_1,f_2)=(13,16,4).
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the row is not merely reporting a count; it identifies a specific thirteen-codon subcomplex in $Q_6$ with sixteen one-dimensional adjacencies and four two-dimensional faces. The module classification partitions all thirteen codons with no missing or extra elements: $\{\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG}\}$ as the AGR module, $\{\mathrm{AAA}\}$ as the AR-Lys module, $\{\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU}\}$ as the CUN module, $\{\mathrm{AUA}\}$ as the Ile-Met module, $\{\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UUA}\}$ as the SSN module, and $\{\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UGA}\}$ as the stop-Trp module. The assignment count is $13$, the region size is $13$, and both the unassigned-codon list and the extra-module-codon list are empty.

\paragraph{Spectral and shape checks.}
The row also carries a coordinate-spectrum signature. The reported values are
$$
\begin{aligned}
\lambda_M&=0.6752479308830213,\\
\lambda_R&=0.4758352843197002,\\
\mu&=3.051487585298128.
\end{aligned}
$$
The radial cubic residual is $-6.224354365258478\times 10^{-12}$, which is the same value reported for the cubic-polynomial check. This places the numerical spectrum inside the stated tolerance rather than treating the shape as an informal picture. A separate randomized shape test used $N=2000$ samples with seed $42$. It found $1$ exact shape match, giving $p=0.0005$, and $25$ samples with Jaccard similarity at least $0.9$, giving $p=0.0125$. The sampling log records $57377$ distinct-sample attempts and $55377$ duplicate retries, so the rarity statement is tied to the actual finite sampling process rather than an asymptotic heuristic. In the amino-acid-class-preserving null model, again with $N=2000$ and seed $42$, the observed $M$ size is $20$, the exact-match count is $0$, and the lower-tail count at or below the observed $M$ size is $7$. The corresponding reported probabilities are $p=0.0$ for exact shape and $p=0.0035$ for the lower tail.

\paragraph{Leave-one-code-out row.}
For the claim itself, the leave-one-code-out experiment evaluates $23$ tables and $63$ total targets. The baseline hit rate is $0.19821739130434785$. The hit rate above baseline is $0.9841269841269841$, the hit rate inside $M\setminus T$ is $0.9365079365079365$, and the hit rate inside $M\setminus T$ together with the boundary allowance is $0.9841269841269841$. Equivalently, the finite row says that almost all held-out targets are recovered once the local window-six region and its boundary behavior are used, while the raw baseline is close to one fifth. The family split keeps this pattern visible: the five reported families have above-baseline hit rates $1.0$ for bacterial-plastid, $1.0$ for candidate-other, $1.0$ for mitochondrial, $1.0$ for nuclear-ciliate, and $0.8333333333333334$ for alternative-yeast. The finite test therefore supports a local code-read regularity, with the weakest reported family still well above the baseline rate.

\paragraph{Codon-level recovery.}
The companion leave-one-codon-out row reports $0$ anchors, $5$ boundary codons, $8$ high-coverage closure codons, and $8$ predictable codons. Its recovery rate is $1.0$, and the threshold comparison also reports $1.0$. This is consistent with the module row: every listed module contains codons that are either predictable or boundary-supported, and the module classification is complete over the thirteen-codon region. The result should therefore be read as a local codon-tile recovery statement inside the code table, not as a statement about molecular realization.

\paragraph{Reality contact.}
The only biological contact used here is the curated standard-code table. It supplies codon-to-label assignments at codon-label resolution. It can test code readback and local codon-tile label splitting. It cannot test translation realization, structural order, physical admissibility, or function realization. The table tells us that a codon has a curated amino-acid or stop label; it does not observe ribosomal execution, tRNA availability, kinetic admissibility, folding, stability, phenotype, or organism-level function.

\paragraph{Boundary.}
The evidence supports the following bounded reading: window-six codon coordinates expose a local boundary-tile structure whose held-out code-label recovery is strong in the reported finite rows. The same evidence does not imply that DNA must obey this geometry as a biological necessity, does not turn a local tile into a universal rule, and does not infer folded protein structure from amino-acid label readback. No claim about translation, folding, physical admissibility, biological function, mechanism, or universality follows from this chapter unless a separate contact supplies observations at that higher layer. In particular, cross-organism wording, CUN or UUR notation, and tRNA-Leu terminology do not convert a code-label table into structural or functional evidence. The current row is a code-layer readback record with a strong local recovery signal and an explicit upper boundary on biological interpretation.
