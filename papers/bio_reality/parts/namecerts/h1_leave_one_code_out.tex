\subsection{Leave-One-Code-Out Recovery in the Six-Bit Codon Cube}\label{sec:bioreality-h1-leave-one-code-out}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite object.}
This record concerns a code-layer test on the standard genetic-code table.  The carrier is the six-coordinate codon cube $Q_6$, used only as a finite display for codon labels.  Each codon is read as a six-bit coordinate, and the data distinguish local tile faces, singleton label corners, and three-point label classes inside that finite cube.  The biological contact is the curated standard-code assignment from codons to amino-acid or stop labels.  That contact supplies label readback only: it is not a translation experiment, not a folding observation, not a physical-admissibility assay, and not a function measurement.

\paragraph{Residual set.}
The finite residual set $R$ has cardinality $13$.  In the recorded run it is exactly
$$
\begin{aligned}
R=\{&AAA, AGA, AGG, AUA, CUA, CUC, CUG, CUU,\\
&UAA, UAG, UCA, UGA, UUA\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The same row also identifies $R$ with a punctured-cube pattern with $f$-vector $(13,16,4)$.  Thus the phenomenon is not described merely by a list of exceptional codons; it carries a finite local shape inside $Q_6$, with $13$ vertices, $16$ one-dimensional incidences, and $4$ two-dimensional faces after the relevant puncture.

The radial cubic check is numerically stable in the run: the cubic-polynomial residual and the radial-polynomial residual are both $-6.224354365258478\cdot 10^{-12}$, while the spectral-radius value in tolerance is $0.6752479308830213$.  These numbers are used only as finite signatures of the displayed codon-label geometry.  They do not turn the label table into a mechanism of translation or into a law of biological realization.

\paragraph{Shape significance.}
Against the reported randomization row for the local shape, $N=2000$ samples were evaluated with seed $42$.  The run required $57377$ distinct-sample attempts and incurred $55377$ duplicate retries.  There was $1$ exact shape match, giving
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{\mathrm{exact}}&=0.0005,
\end{aligned}
$$
and there were $25$ matches with Jaccard similarity at least $0.9$, giving
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{\mathrm{Jaccard}\geq 0.9}&=0.0125.
\end{aligned}
$$
A second null row preserves amino-acid class.  In that row $N=2000$, the observed module size is $20$, the exact-match count is $0$, and the lower-tail count at or below the observed $M$ size is $7$.  The corresponding values are
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{\mathrm{class\ exact}}&=0.0,\\
p_{\mathrm{class\ lower\ tail}}&=0.0035.
\end{aligned}
$$
The finite rows therefore support a strong local-shape signal at the label-table level, including under an amino-acid-class-preserving comparison.  They do not identify a folded structure, a reaction pathway, a ribosomal implementation, or a universal biological principle.

\paragraph{Leave-one-code-out row.}
The primary leave-one-code-out test evaluated $23$ variant tables and $63$ total targets.  Its baseline hit rate is $0.19821739130434785$.  The recorded hit rate above that baseline is $0.9841269841269841$.  The hit rate inside $M\setminus T$ is $0.9365079365079365$, and the hit rate inside $M\setminus T$ after adjoining the boundary contribution is $0.9841269841269841$:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{baseline}&=0.19821739130434785,\\
\mathrm{above\ baseline}&=0.9841269841269841,\\
\mathrm{inside}\ M\setminus T&=0.9365079365079365,\\
\mathrm{inside}\ (M\setminus T)\cup \partial&=0.9841269841269841.
\end{aligned}
$$
This is the central finite claim.  Once one code table is withheld, the local six-bit codon geometry recovers almost all targets beyond the baseline rate, with the boundary term raising the rate to the same $0.9841269841269841$ level reported for the above-baseline row.  The result is a code-readback statement: it concerns codon labels in a curated table, not an organism-level causal model.

\paragraph{Codon-level module row.}
The leave-one-codon-out module classification assigns every member of $R$ to a named module and reports no missing or extra codons.  The completed partition is

The classification row records $R\_size=13$, $assigned\_count=13$, $extra\_module\_codons=\varnothing$, $missing\_from\_modules=\varnothing$, and $unassigned\_codons=\varnothing$.  The related leave-one-codon-out recovery row has anchor count $0$, boundary count $5$, high closure coverage $8$, predictable count $8$, and recovery rate $1.0$.  Hence each residual codon lies either in a predictable component or in the recorded boundary behavior, and the module accounting is complete for the $13$-codon residual set.

\paragraph{Local interpretation.}
The finite data support the following constrained reading.  The six-bit cube exposes a local boundary-tile structure in the standard-code label table.  The residual set $R$ is small, explicitly listed, and geometrically organized as a punctured cube with $f$-vector $(13,16,4)$.  The spectral row supplies stable numerical descriptors of the same finite object.  The randomization rows place the observed local shape in a low-tail regime under the reported null comparisons.  The leave-one-code-out and leave-one-codon-out rows then show that the local pattern has high recovery power for withheld code-layer labels.

\paragraph{Scope boundary.}
No stronger biological conclusion is part of this record.  A local codon tile is not, by itself, a global biological law.  Window-six geometry is not translation, ribosomal realization, structural order, physical admissibility, biological function, or a universal mechanism.  Amino-acid label-sequence readback is also not folded protein structure.  Any claim about translation dynamics, molecular structure, admissible chemistry, organismal function, or universality would require a separate contact that directly observes that layer.  The present finite row stops at code-layer readback from a curated codon-label table.
