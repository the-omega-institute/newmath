\subsection{Leave-One-Code-Out Readback on the Six-Bit Codon Cube}
\label{sec:bioreality-h1-leave-one-code-out}

\paragraph{Finite object under test.}
This record studies the standard-code codon table only at the code-read layer. The finite carrier is the six-coordinate codon cube $Q_6$, read as a binary display of codon positions after the usual alphabetic codon labels have been fixed by a curated standard-code table. The claim is not that the cube generates biology. The claim is narrower: after the table has supplied the codon labels, the window-six coordinates expose a small local boundary tile that predicts many leave-one-code-out label changes. The distinguished finite subset $R$ has cardinality $13$ and is exactly
$$
\begin{aligned}
R=\{&\texttt{AAA},\texttt{AGA},\texttt{AGG},\texttt{AUA},\texttt{CUA},\texttt{CUC},\texttt{CUG},\texttt{CUU},\\
&\texttt{UAA},\texttt{UAG},\texttt{UCA},\texttt{UGA},\texttt{UUA}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The same row verifies that this set forms the punctured-cube pattern with $f$-vector $(13,16,4)$. Thus the local geometry is not a verbal analogy: it is a concrete finite subcomplex selected inside the codon cube. The finite data also record an observed ambient marked set size $|M|=20$ in the amino-acid-class-preserving null experiment. The complete member list of $M$ is not included in the supplied finite row, so this chapter uses $|M|$ and the reported hit rates rather than naming unprovided elements.

This matters because the leave-one-code-out table is not being judged only by a visual shape match; the subset $R$ also sits inside a coordinate-and-relation calculation with a stable measured radius.

\paragraph{Null comparison.}
The shape test used $N=2000$ sampled alternatives with seed $42$. Because duplicate samples were common, the run reports $57377$ distinct-sample attempts and $55377$ duplicate retries. Only $1$ exact shape match occurred, giving $p_{\mathrm{exact}}=0.0005$. The high-overlap threshold was also rare: $25$ samples had Jaccard score at least $0.9$, giving $p_{\mathrm{Jaccard}\ge 0.9}=0.0125$. A second null model preserves amino-acid class. In that comparison, again with $N=2000$ and seed $42$, the exact shape count was $0$, so the recorded exact-shape probability is $0.0$. The lower-tail count at or below the observed $M$ size was $7$, giving $p_{\mathrm{lower\ tail}}=0.0035$. These finite rows do not prove a biological mechanism; they show that the displayed local pattern is atypical under two concrete randomization procedures supplied with the record.

\paragraph{Leave-one-code-out result.}
The central finite row evaluates $23$ code tables and $63$ target events. The baseline hit rate is $0.19821739130434785$. Against that baseline, the local window-six construction records
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{hit\ rate\ above\ baseline} &= 0.9841269841269841,\\
\mathrm{hit\ rate\ in}\; M\setminus T &= 0.9365079365079365,\\
\mathrm{hit\ rate\ in}\; M\setminus T\;\mathrm{plus\ boundary} &= 0.9841269841269841.
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the empirical readback is concentrated in the marked-and-boundary region rather than being spread at the baseline rate. The point of the test is local: remove one code table from the comparison, ask whether the remaining coordinate pattern still locates the target label movement, and count success inside the finite marked region. The record reports a large separation between $0.19821739130434785$ and $0.9841269841269841$ on the supplied $63$ targets.

Every listed module has a predictable-or-boundary witness in the finite record. This is the strongest chapter-specific reason to treat the leave-one-code-out result as a structured finite phenomenon rather than as a loose enrichment statistic.

\paragraph{Local boundary of the claim.}
The contact used here is a curated standard genetic-code table. It supplies codon-to-amino-acid or codon-to-stop labels, with resolution at codon label readback. It does not observe translation realization, folded protein structure, physical admissibility, biochemical function, or a universal biological mechanism. Therefore the result must remain at the code-read layer. Amino-acid label-sequence readback is not a folded structure observation. A local codon tile is not a global law of biology. Window-six geometry alone is not translation, structure, physical admissibility, function, or mechanism. Any later claim that crosses one of those boundaries requires a separate contact that actually measures the higher layer.

\paragraph{Record verdict.}
Within the finite data supplied here, the claim is ready as a local code-read statement. The verified rows give the exact $13$-codon boundary set, the punctured-cube $f$-vector $(13,16,4)$, the spectral values $\lambda_M=0.6752479308830213$, $\lambda_R=0.4758352843197002$, and $\mu=3.051487585298128$, two low-probability null comparisons, a $23$-table leave-one-code-out evaluation over $63$ targets, and a complete $13$-codon module partition. The appropriate conclusion is that the curated code labels, when placed in the six-bit codon cube, expose a strong local boundary-tile readback. The appropriate non-conclusion is equally important: no structural, physical, functional, mechanistic, or universal biological statement follows from this row alone.
