\subsection{Leave-one-code-out recovery at the code-read layer}
\label{sec:h1-leave-one-code-out}

\paragraph{Finite object under test.}
The object recorded here is the standard-code codon table viewed through a six-coordinate codon display.  The carrier is the codon cube $Q_6$, and the observed labels are the curated amino-acid or stop labels attached to codons by the standard genetic-code table.  The claim is deliberately local: the window-six coordinate system may expose a boundary-tile pattern in the code table, while the labels themselves remain empirical contacts supplied by the curated table.  The claim therefore lives at the code-read layer.  It does not by itself assert translation realization, folded structure, physical admissibility, biological function, mechanism, or universality.

\paragraph{Distinguished residual row.}
The residual codon set $R$ has cardinality $13$.  In the verified row it is exactly
$$
\begin{aligned}
R=\{&\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UUA}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The same row identifies this set as a punctured-cube trace with face-vector
$$
\begin{aligned}
(f_0,f_1,f_2)=(13,16,4).
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the tested support is not a vague cluster of codons: it is a finite named subset of the $64$ codon cube with $13$ vertices, $16$ local one-step edges, and $4$ local square faces.  The module classification accounts for all $13$ residual codons and leaves no unassigned elements.  The six modules are
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{AGR}&=\{\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG}\},\\
\mathrm{AR\_Lys}&=\{\mathrm{AAA}\},\\
\mathrm{CUN}&=\{\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU}\},\\
\mathrm{Ile\_Met}&=\{\mathrm{AUA}\},\\
\mathrm{SSN}&=\{\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UUA}\},\\
\mathrm{Stop\_Trp}&=\{\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UGA}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The recorded classification has $R$-size $13$, assigned count $13$, no extra module codons, no missing residual codons, and no unassigned codons.

\paragraph{Spectral and shape checks.}
The finite row also records a spectral signature for the same local geometry.  The two displayed eigenvalue-scale quantities are
$$
\begin{aligned}
\lambda_M&=0.6752479308830213,\\
\lambda_R&=0.4758352843197002,\\
\mu&=3.051487585298128.
\end{aligned}
$$
The cubic relation check is numerically centered at
$$
\begin{aligned}
-6.224354365258478\times 10^{-12},
\end{aligned}
$$
with the same value recorded for the radial polynomial residual.  This is treated only as a finite numerical agreement for the codon-window data, not as an independent biological mechanism.

The random-shape control used $N=2000$ samples with seed $42$.  It observed one exact shape match, giving
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{\mathrm{exact}}=0.0005,
\end{aligned}
$$
and $25$ matches at Jaccard similarity at least $0.9$, giving
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{J\geq 0.9}=0.0125.
\end{aligned}
$$
The sampling log records $57377$ distinct-sample attempts and $55377$ duplicate retries.  A separate amino-acid-class-preserving control again used $N=2000$ and seed $42$.  It found zero exact-shape matches and a lower-tail count of $7$ for observed $M$-size $20$, giving
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{\mathrm{aa\ class\ exact}}&=0.0,\\
p_{\mathrm{aa\ class\ lower\ tail}}&=0.0035.
\end{aligned}
$$
These controls support the claim that the local tile is not a generic byproduct of arbitrary relabeling under the tested null models.  They do not upgrade the local code pattern into a claim about proteins, organisms, or chemistry.

\paragraph{Empirical contact and local boundary.}
The empirical contact is the curated standard-code table.  It supplies codon-to-label assignments and thereby permits code-layer readback and local label-split tests.  It does not observe ribosomal translation, protein folding, thermodynamic admissibility, enzymatic activity, organismal fitness, or a universal biological law.  Consequently, amino-acid label sequences read from the table remain label sequences.  They are not folded protein structures, not physical structures, and not functional objects unless a separate experiment supplies such a contact.

The boundary rule for this chapter is therefore strict.  A local codon-tile pattern may be recorded when the six-coordinate geometry, the curated labels, and the finite probes agree.  The same record must not be promoted across layers.  In particular, CUN or UUR wording, tRNA-Leu notation, alternative-code behavior, or residual-module membership does not by itself become structure evidence.  Any future structural, physical, translational, functional, or universal reading must be attached to a different observation type with its own finite row.

\paragraph{Verdict.}
The finite data support the code-read statement that the window-six codon coordinates expose a local boundary-tile pattern in the standard-code label table.  The support comes from the exact residual set of $13$ codons, the punctured-cube face-vector $(13,16,4)$, the module classification with full assignment of $R$, the spectral residual near $10^{-11}$, the exact-shape and high-Jaccard null rates, and the leave-one-code-out hit rates across $23$ evaluated tables and $63$ targets.  The chapter makes no claim beyond that layer.
