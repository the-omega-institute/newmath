\subsection{Leave-one-code-out locality in the six-bit codon cube}\label{sec:h1-leave-one-code-out}\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Object and contact.}
The object studied here is the standard-code codon table as a finite label table on the six-bit codon cube $Q_6$.  Each codon is read as a coordinate in the cube, and the only biological contact used in this record is the curated codon-to-label table assigning amino-acid or stop labels.  The contact is therefore a code-layer contact: it can test whether a local coordinate pattern tracks codon labels, but it does not by itself observe translation in a cell, folded structure, physical admissibility, biological function, mechanism, or universality.  Those higher layers require separate observations.

\paragraph{Distinguished rows.}
The distinguished residual set $R$ has cardinality $13$.  The verified row list is
$$
\begin{aligned}
R=\{&AAA, AGA, AGG, AUA, CUA, CUC, CUG, CUU,\\
&UAA, UAG, UCA, UGA, UUA\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The same computation identifies $R$ as a punctured-cube pattern with face data
$$
\begin{aligned}
(f_0,f_1,f_2)=(13,16,4).
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the row is not merely a list of exceptional codons: it is a finite coordinate subobject with $13$ vertices, $16$ one-dimensional adjacencies, and $4$ two-dimensional square faces inside the codon cube.

\paragraph{Spectral scale.}
The finite coordinate computation also reports the numerical spectral profile
$$
\begin{aligned}
\lambda_M&=0.6752479308830213,\\
\lambda_R&=0.4758352843197002,\\
\mu&=3.051487585298128.
\end{aligned}
$$
The associated radial cubic check is numerically closed at
$$
\begin{aligned}
-6.224354365258478\cdot 10^{-12},
\end{aligned}
$$
which is effectively zero at the stated numerical tolerance.  This makes the window-six pattern a finite spectral row rather than a visual coincidence alone.

\paragraph{Shape significance.}
Under the reported shape comparison with seed $42$, the run used $N=2000$ samples and required $57377$ distinct-sample attempts, with $55377$ duplicate retries.  There was $1$ exact shape match, giving
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{\mathrm{exact}}=0.0005,
\end{aligned}
$$
and there were $25$ samples with Jaccard score at least $0.9$, giving
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{J\geq 0.9}=0.0125.
\end{aligned}
$$
The amino-acid-class-preserving null model, also at $N=2000$ and seed $42$, found no exact shape matches.  Its lower-tail count at or below the observed $M$-size was $7$ for observed $|M|=20$, hence
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{\mathrm{aa\ class,exact}}&=0.0,\\
p_{\mathrm{aa\ class,lower\ tail}}&=0.0035.
\end{aligned}
$$
These finite null rows support a narrow statement: the local tile has measurable coordinate specificity relative to the tested nulls.  They do not turn the coordinate pattern into a biological law.

\paragraph{Leave-one-code-out readback.}
The leave-one-code-out experiment evaluated $23$ code tables and $63$ targets.  Its baseline hit rate was
$$
\begin{aligned}
0.19821739130434785.
\end{aligned}
$$
Against this baseline, the observed hit rate above baseline was
$$
\begin{aligned}
0.9841269841269841.
\end{aligned}
$$
The hit rate inside $M-T$ was
$$
\begin{aligned}
0.9365079365079365,
\end{aligned}
$$
and the rate inside $M-T$ together with the boundary was
$$
\begin{aligned}
0.9841269841269841.
\end{aligned}
$$
The experiment therefore says that the six-bit local tile nearly always recovers the held-out code-label target under this finite protocol, while the same statement remains restricted to label readback.

\paragraph{Family-level distribution.}
The leave-one-code-out row separates into $5$ families.  The family hit rates above baseline are

The weaker alternative-yeast value is still far above the global baseline reported for the same leave-one-code-out run.  The other four family rows are perfect under the finite test as recorded.

\paragraph{Module classification.}
The $13$ codons of $R$ are completely assigned to six modules.  The verified module partition is
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{AGR}&=\{AGA,AGG\},\\
\mathrm{AR\_Lys}&=\{AAA\},\\
\mathrm{CUN}&=\{CUA,CUC,CUG,CUU\},\\
\mathrm{Ile\_Met}&=\{AUA\},\\
\mathrm{SSN}&=\{UCA,UUA\},\\
\mathrm{Stop\_Trp}&=\{UAA,UAG,UGA\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The classification record has $R$-size $13$, assigned count $13$, no extra module codons, no missing codons from the modules, and no unassigned codons.  Each listed module has at least one predictable or boundary codon under the leave-one-codon-out analysis.  In that companion row, the anchor count is $0$, the boundary count is $5$, the high-coverage closure count is $8$, the predictable count is $8$, and the recovery rate is $1.0$.

\paragraph{Interpretation.}
The finite result is a local code-read statement.  Window-six coordinates isolate a punctured-cube residual row, attach a stable spectral scale to it, pass the recorded shape-null tests, and recover held-out code labels at $0.9841269841269841$ above-baseline rate over $63$ targets from $23$ tables.  The curated standard-code table supplies exactly the label contact needed for that claim.  It supplies no direct observation of ribosomal realization, no folding observation, no energetic or steric admissibility test, no phenotype or function assay, and no reason to promote the local tile into a universal biological mechanism.  Any future claim beyond code-label readback must bring a separate contact whose measured object is actually at that higher layer.
