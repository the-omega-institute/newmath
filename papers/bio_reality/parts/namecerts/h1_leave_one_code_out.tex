\subsection{Leave-one-code-out stability of the local codon tile}
\label{sec:h1-leave-one-code-out}

\paragraph{Claim scope.}
This record concerns the finite codon-window claim indexed by \texttt{h1.leave.one.code.out}.  The biological object is the standard-code codon table, viewed only at the code-read layer: a curated table assigns each codon to an amino-acid label or to a stop label.  The mathematical carrier used here is the six-coordinate codon cube $Q_6$, together with local face incidence, singleton label corners, and three-point label classes.  The claim is deliberately local.  It says that window-six codon coordinates expose a boundary-tile pattern that is stable under a leave-one-code-out test; it does not say that the geometry is a biological law, a translation mechanism, a folding rule, a physical-admissibility criterion, or a functional principle.

\paragraph{Finite exceptional row.}
The distinguished codon set $R$ has cardinality $13$.  The recorded codons are
$$
\begin{aligned}
R=\{&AAA,AGA,AGG,AUA,CUA,CUC,CUG,CUU,\\
&UAA,UAG,UCA,UGA,UUA\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The same row is also recorded as a punctured-cube pattern with face-vector $(13,16,4)$.  Thus the contact is not an unrestricted assertion over all $64$ codons; it is a finite local subset of the six-bit codon cube whose incidence data are part of the observation.  The row does not provide an explicit codon inventory for $M$.  It does, however, record the observed size $|M|=20$ in the amino-acid-class-preserving null model and uses that size in the lower-tail comparison.

\paragraph{Spectral coordinates.}
The spectral row fixes the numerical scale of the local tile rather than leaving the shape as a qualitative picture.  The values are
$$
\begin{aligned}
\lambda_M&=0.6752479308830213,\\
\lambda_R&=0.4758352843197002,\\
\mu&=3.051487585298128.
\end{aligned}
$$
The radial cubic check evaluates to $-6.224354365258478\times 10^{-12}$, the same numerical residual recorded for the cubic polynomial condition, so the spectral computation is within the stated tolerance.  These numbers are evidence for a reproducible coordinate signature of the tile; they are not evidence for a chemical or organism-level mechanism.

\paragraph{Shape significance.}
Against the reported random comparison with $N=2000$ samples and seed $42$, the exact shape appears once.  The exact-shape probability is therefore $p=0.0005$.  The high-overlap event with Jaccard score at least $0.9$ appears $25$ times, giving $p=0.0125$.  The run also records $57377$ distinct-sample attempts and $55377$ duplicate retries, so the finite sample is not being described as a continuous distributional calculation.  A second null model preserves amino-acid class.  Under that comparison, the exact-shape count is $0$, while $7$ sampled rows fall at or below the observed $M$-size of $20$, giving lower-tail probability $0.0035$.  These two null rows support the statement that the observed local pattern is not a routine output of the sampled alternatives, while still keeping the inference at the code-label level.

\paragraph{Leave-one-code-out result.}
The leave-one-code-out test evaluated $23$ tables and $63$ total targets.  The baseline hit rate is $0.19821739130434785$.  The observed rate above baseline is $0.9841269841269841$, the rate inside $M-T$ is $0.9365079365079365$, and the rate inside $M-T$ plus the boundary row is $0.9841269841269841$.  In compact form, the recorded comparison is
$$
\begin{aligned}
\text{baseline}&=0.19821739130434785,\\
\text{above baseline}&=0.9841269841269841,\\
\text{inside }M-T&=0.9365079365079365,\\
\text{inside }(M-T)\cup\text{boundary}&=0.9841269841269841.
\end{aligned}
$$
The empirical content is that removing one code table at a time does not destroy the local codon-label recovery pattern.  The strongest number is not promoted into a universal rule: it is a finite recovery statistic over the evaluated rows.

\paragraph{Family split.}
The family-level row partitions the leave-one-code-out evidence into $5$ families.  The hit-rate-above-baseline values are $1.0$ for the bacterial-plastid family, $1.0$ for the candidate-other family, $1.0$ for the mitochondrial family, $1.0$ for the nuclear-ciliate family, and $0.8333333333333334$ for the alternative-yeast family.  This split matters because it prevents the global hit rate from hiding a family-specific weakness.  The alternative-yeast row is still high, but it is not equal to the four perfect family rows, so the table records a structured finite difference rather than a uniform slogan.

\paragraph{Codon-level recovery.}
The corresponding leave-one-codon-out row reports no anchors, $5$ boundary codons, $8$ predictable codons, high closure coverage of $8$, and recovery rate $1.0$.  The recovery threshold entry is also $1.0$.  The module classification is complete: $R$ has size $13$, all $13$ codons are assigned, and the lists of missing, extra, and unassigned codons are empty.  The modules are
$$
\begin{aligned}
\text{agr}&=\{AGA,AGG\},\\
\text{ar\_lys}&=\{AAA\},\\
\text{cun}&=\{CUA,CUC,CUG,CUU\},\\
\text{ile\_met}&=\{AUA\},\\
\text{ssn}&=\{UCA,UUA\},\\
\text{stop\_trp}&=\{UAA,UAG,UGA\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
Every module has either a predictable codon or a boundary codon.  This is the finite module-level form of the local-tile claim: the pattern is not merely a set cardinality statement, because each named module is accounted for.

\paragraph{Reality contact and blocked promotion.}
The only biological contact used in this record is the curated standard-code label table.  It supports codon-to-label readback and local split checks.  It does not observe translation in a cell, protein folding, physical admissibility, biological function, or a universal mechanism.  Amino-acid label sequences therefore remain label sequences in this chapter.  A separate experimental or curated contact would be required before any statement about structure, admissible physics, function, translation realization, or cross-organism universality could be made.

\paragraph{Local verdict.}
The finite rows support a ready code-read claim: a $13$-codon punctured-cube subset with face-vector $(13,16,4)$, stable spectral values, significant shape tests, complete module assignment, and strong leave-one-code-out recovery is present in the supplied data.  The scientific reading is narrow and explicit.  The codon cube supplies a coordinate organization for the local table pattern, while the curated code table supplies the label contact.  No higher biological layer is imported by that agreement.
