\subsection{Leave-One-Code-Out Readback for the Window-Six Codon Tile}

\paragraph{Finite codon display.}
The claim identified by \texttt{h1.leave.one.code.out} concerns the standard-code codon table viewed through a six-coordinate codon display.  The finite carrier is the codon cube $Q_6$, where each codon is read as a six-bit coordinate token and the only reality contact used here is a curated codon-to-label table for the standard genetic code.  The resulting claim is deliberately placed at the code-read layer: the data may expose a local tile boundary in the label table, but the table itself supplies amino-acid and stop labels, not a biochemical mechanism.

\paragraph{Boundary set.}
The finite exceptional region $R$ has cardinality $13$.  The recorded set is
$$
\begin{aligned}
R = \{&AAA, AGA, AGG, AUA, CUA, CUC, CUG, CUU,\\
&UAA, UAG, UCA, UGA, UUA\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The same row certifies that this is the expected codon list and that the associated punctured-cube face data have $f$-vector
$$
\begin{aligned}
(f_0,f_1,f_2)=(13,16,4).
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the boundary is not being inferred from a continuum approximation or from a hidden biological narrative; it is a finite subset of the codon cube with a recorded face count.

\paragraph{Shape significance.}
Against the finite null run with $N=2000$ samples and seed $42$, the observed shape had exactly one exact match.  The recorded exact-shape probability is therefore
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{\mathrm{exact}}=0.0005.
\end{aligned}
$$
For the high-overlap threshold, $25$ samples had Jaccard overlap at least $0.9$, giving
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{J\geq 0.9}=0.0125.
\end{aligned}
$$
The run also reports $57377$ distinct-sample attempts and $55377$ duplicate retries, so the row should be read as a finite randomized comparison with substantial rejection of duplicates, not as an analytic asymptotic theorem.

\paragraph{Reality contact and scope.}
The only admitted empirical contact in this chapter is the curated standard genetic-code table, whose observed fact is that codons are assigned amino-acid or stop labels.  That contact can test code-read behavior and local codon-tile label splits.  It cannot test translation realization, folded structure, physical admissibility, or biological function.  Consequently, amino-acid label readback must remain at the code layer.  No claim about folding, structural order, protein activity, organismal fitness, universal mechanism, or biological necessity follows from the finite tile unless a separate observation supplies that higher-layer contact.

\paragraph{Local conclusion.}
The ready claim is therefore the following finite statement.  In the standard-code codon table, the six-coordinate codon display isolates a $13$-codon boundary region with face vector $(13,16,4)$, nontrivial spectral diagnostics, low null probabilities for the recorded shape, complete module coverage, and high leave-one-code-out recovery across $23$ tables and $63$ targets.  The support is strong for a local code-read boundary pattern.  The same evidence blocks promotion beyond codon labels: the chapter does not assert translation, folding, physical admissibility, function, mechanism, or universality.
