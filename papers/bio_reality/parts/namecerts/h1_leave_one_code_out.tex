\subsection{Leave-one-code-out recovery in a window-six codon display}\label{sec:leave-one-code-out-recovery}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Claim.}
The finite packet for \texttt{h1.leave.one.code.out} tests whether a six-coordinate codon display can recover local standard-code label changes after one genetic-code table is left out. The carrier is the $Q_6$ codon cube: each codon is represented by a six-bit window, and the inspected structure is not the whole biology of translation but a local tile cut through the codon-label table. The distinctions used by the packet are the coordinate position in the cube, the finite closure generated around selected codons, the spectral profile of the induced relation, and the readback of codons to curated amino-acid or stop labels. The biological object touched by the packet is therefore the standard-code codon table as a code-layer table, not a biochemical mechanism.

\paragraph{Finite codon boundary.}
The distinguished residual set $R$ has cardinality $13$. In the verified row it is exactly
$$
\begin{aligned}
R=\{&AAA, AGA, AGG, AUA, CUA, CUC, CUG, CUU,\\
&UAA, UAG, UCA, UGA, UUA\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The same row records that this set is a punctured-cube pattern with $f$-vector
$$
\begin{aligned}
(f_0,f_1,f_2)=(13,16,4).
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the tested object is a small finite boundary in the codon cube, with thirteen codon vertices, sixteen one-dimensional adjacencies, and four two-dimensional faces. The packet treats this boundary as local evidence about code-label separations. It does not treat the boundary as a law of DNA, RNA processing, ribosomal realization, protein folding, or organism-level function.

\paragraph{Spectral row.}
The relation attached to the local display has numerical spectrum data
$$
\begin{aligned}
\lambda_M &= 0.6752479308830213,\\
\lambda_R &= 0.4758352843197002,\\
\mu &= 3.051487585298128.
\end{aligned}
$$
The recorded cubic residual is $-6.224354365258478\cdot 10^{-12}$, with the same value listed as the radial-polynomial residual. This is numerically indistinguishable from zero at the displayed precision and supports the claim that the inspected local relation has a stable finite spectral signature. The number $\lambda_M$ is the larger displayed radius, and $\lambda_R$ is smaller, so the local residual boundary is spectrally separated from the broader measured region in this row.

\paragraph{Shape significance.}
The shape comparison row uses $N=2000$ sampled alternatives with seed $42$. Among these samples, the exact shape match count is $1$, giving

$$
The high-overlap count at Jaccard threshold $0.9$ is $25$, giving
$$
\begin{aligned}

\end{aligned}
$$
The sampling audit also records $57377$ distinct-sample attempts and $55377$ duplicate retries, so the finite comparison is heavily constrained by the combinatorics of the allowed alternatives. In the amino-acid-class-preserving null row, again with $N=2000$ and seed $42$, the observed $M$-size is $20$, the exact match count is $0$, and the lower-tail count at or below the observed $M$-size is $7$. The corresponding values are

\begin{aligned}
p_{\mathrm{aa\ class\ exact}}&=0.0,\\
p_{\mathrm{aa\ class\ lower\ tail}}&=0.0035.
\end{aligned}
$$
These finite rows make the local tile unusual under the recorded null comparisons. They do not by themselves supply a causal account of how any organism realizes the code.

\paragraph{Leave-one-code-out row.}
The main leave-one-code-out probe evaluated $23$ tables and $63$ total targets. Its baseline hit rate is
$$
\begin{aligned}
0.19821739130434785.
\end{aligned}
$$
The observed hit rate above baseline is
$$
\begin{aligned}
0.9841269841269841,
\end{aligned}
$$
while the hit rate inside $M\setminus T$ is $0.9365079365079365$ and the hit rate inside $M\setminus T$ plus the boundary is $0.9841269841269841$. The finite interpretation is direct: when one code table is withheld, the local coordinate-and-boundary construction recovers nearly all of the recorded target labels under this test, and the recovery level is far above the listed baseline. The equality between the above-baseline rate and the $M\setminus T$ plus boundary rate indicates that the boundary contribution is not incidental bookkeeping; it is part of the tested recovery path.

The row therefore supports a code-table recovery statement across the recorded family partition, with the same restriction as before: the statement is about label readback in curated code tables.

\paragraph{Codon-level boundary check.}
The leave-one-codon-out row records anchor count $0$, boundary count $5$, predictable count $8$, high closure coverage $8$, and recovery rate $1.0$. In scalar form,
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{anchors}&=0,\\

\mathrm{predictable\ codons}&=8,\\
\mathrm{recovery\ rate}&=1.0.
\end{aligned}
$$
The absence of anchors matters: the recovery row is not saying that fixed anchor codons carry the result by themselves. Instead, the thirteen-codon residual set is exhausted by predictable or boundary behavior. This is confirmed by the module classification.

The module row assigns all $13$ residual codons and records no missing codons, no extra module codons, and no unassigned codons. The modules are

\begin{aligned}

The classification is complete because $R$ has size $13$ and the assigned count is also $13$. Each module has a predictable-or-boundary status in the row. The largest module is $\mathrm{cun}$ with four codons, the stop-tryptophan module has three codons, the arginine module and the $\mathrm{ssn}$ module each have two codons, and the lysine-side and isoleucine-methionine modules are singleton cases.

\paragraph{Reality contact and scope.}
The only reality contact used here is a curated standard genetic-code table assigning codons to amino-acid or stop labels. That contact can test code-layer readback and local codon-tile label splits. It cannot test translation realization, folded structure, physical admissibility, biological function, or a universal mechanism. Consequently, an amino-acid label sequence read from the table remains a label sequence. It is not a folded protein, not a structure observation, not a thermodynamic admissibility result, and not a functional assay.

\paragraph{Boundary verdict.}
The finite rows support the following bounded conclusion. In the recorded window-six codon display, the thirteen-codon residual set forms a punctured local cube boundary; its spectral row is numerically stable; its shape is rare under the recorded null comparisons; and the leave-one-code-out probe recovers $0.9841269841269841$ of targets above baseline across $23$ evaluated tables and $63$ targets. The family and module rows show that this behavior is distributed across the recorded code-table families and accounts for every residual codon module. The result is ready as a code-read packet: local codon geometry and curated labels jointly expose a finite boundary-tile regularity. No claim about translation, folding, physical admissibility, function, mechanism, or universality follows from these rows unless a separate contact supplies that higher-layer observation.
