\subsection{Leave-One-Code-Out Stability of the Local Codon Tile}\label{sec:bioreality-h1-leave-one-code-out}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite carrier and biological contact.}
This record concerns the standard-code codon table viewed through a six-coordinate codon display.  The finite object is the codon cube $Q_6$, whose vertices are codons written in the ordinary three-letter alphabet $\{\mathrm{A},\mathrm{C},\mathrm{G},\mathrm{U}\}^3$.  The biological contact used here is deliberately narrow: a curated standard genetic-code table assigns each codon to an amino-acid label or to a stop label.  That contact supports code-layer readback only.  It does not observe translation dynamics, folded structure, thermodynamic admissibility, cellular function, evolutionary mechanism, or a universal biological law.

\paragraph{Observed boundary set.}
The distinguished residual set $R$ has exactly thirteen codons.  In the finite row, the cardinality check and the punctured-cube check agree on the same list:
$$
\begin{aligned}
|R| &= 13,\\
R &= \{\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\\
&\qquad \mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UUA}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The associated punctured-cube face data have $f$-vector $(13,16,4)$.  Thus the row is not merely reporting a label collection: it reports a local tile with thirteen vertices, sixteen one-dimensional incidences, and four two-dimensional faces under the chosen six-coordinate display.  The claim tested in this chapter is that this local tile has predictive stability under leave-one-code-out testing while remaining confined to the codon-label layer.

\paragraph{Spectral fingerprint.}
The same finite packet records a numerical spectral profile.  The displayed values are
$$
\begin{aligned}
\lambda_M &= 0.6752479308830213,\\
\lambda_R &= 0.4758352843197002,\\
\mu &= 3.051487585298128.
\end{aligned}
$$
The radial relation is numerically closed to tolerance: the recorded cubic-polynomial residual is $-6.224354365258478\cdot 10^{-12}$, the same value reported as the radial-polynomial value.  This gives a finite coordinate-and-spectrum signature for the local tile.  The number is not interpreted as a physical energy, folding score, or biochemical rate; it is a compact invariant of the chosen codon-coordinate construction.

\paragraph{Null comparisons.}
The shape-significance run used $N=2000$ samples with seed $42$.  It required $57377$ distinct-sample attempts and encountered $55377$ duplicate retries.  Only one exact shape match was found, giving
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{\mathrm{exact}} &= 0.0005,\\
p_{J\geq 0.9} &= 0.0125,
\end{aligned}
$$
with $25$ high-Jaccard matches at threshold $0.9$.  A separate amino-acid-class-preserving null also used $N=2000$ and seed $42$.  In that run the observed comparison size was $|M|=20$, the exact-match count was $0$, and the lower-tail count at or below the observed size was $7$, giving
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{\mathrm{aa\ class\ exact}} &= 0.0,\\
p_{\mathrm{aa\ class\ lower\ tail}} &= 0.0035.
\end{aligned}
$$
These rows support the claim that the observed local tile is not a common outcome of the tested finite randomizations.  They do not identify a biochemical cause.

\paragraph{Leave-one-code-out result.}
The leave-one-code-out experiment evaluated $23$ code tables and $63$ total targets.  The baseline hit rate was $0.19821739130434785$.  Against that baseline, the local construction gave
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{hit\ rate\ above\ baseline} &= 0.9841269841269841,\\
\mathrm{hit\ rate\ in}\ M-T &= 0.9365079365079365,\\
\mathrm{hit\ rate\ in}\ M-T\ \mathrm{plus\ boundary} &= 0.9841269841269841.
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus, when code tables are withheld one at a time, the finite codon tile recovers almost all targets above the recorded baseline.  The stronger boundary-augmented row reaches $62/63$ in ordinary count language, since $0.9841269841269841\cdot 63=62$.  The row therefore supports a stable local label-recovery phenomenon for the curated code layer.

\paragraph{Family-level distribution.}
The family-level leave-one-code-out row partitions the evidence into five families.  The reported family hit rates above baseline are
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{alternative\ yeast} &= 0.8333333333333334,\\
\mathrm{bacterial\ plastid} &= 1.0,\\
\mathrm{candidate\ other} &= 1.0,\\
\mathrm{mitochondrial} &= 1.0,\\
\mathrm{nuclear\ ciliate} &= 1.0.
\end{aligned}
$$
This matters because the aggregate result is not carried solely by a single named family.  Four of the five families are perfect under the recorded finite criterion, and the remaining family still exceeds five successes in six trials.  The row is still a code-table row: it compares codon-label assignments, not translation machinery or organismal phenotype.

\paragraph{Codon-module accounting.}
The module-classification row assigns all thirteen members of $R$ and reports no missing, extra, or unassigned codons.  The six modules are
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{agr} &= \{\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG}\},\\
\mathrm{ar\_lys} &= \{\mathrm{AAA}\},\\
\mathrm{cun} &= \{\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU}\},\\
\mathrm{ile\_met} &= \{\mathrm{AUA}\},\\
\mathrm{ssn} &= \{\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UUA}\},\\
\mathrm{stop\_trp} &= \{\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UGA}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The classification row records $R_{\mathrm{size}}=13$ and assigned count $13$, with empty lists for extra module codons, missing codons, and unassigned codons.  A separate leave-one-codon-out row reports boundary count $5$, anchor count $0$, predictable count $8$, high closure coverage $8$, and recovery rate $1.0$.  Hence every recorded module has either predictable or boundary support in the finite accounting.

\paragraph{Scientific reading.}
The supported conclusion is narrow and positive: within the six-coordinate codon display, the standard-code table exposes a local boundary-tile structure whose finite tests are stable under leave-one-code-out and leave-one-codon-out probes.  The evidence combines a thirteen-codon residual set, a punctured-cube face count $(13,16,4)$, a reproducible spectral profile, low finite-null frequencies, and a $23$-table leave-one-code-out row with $63$ targets.  The biological input is the curated codon-to-label table, so the observed phenomenon is a code-read phenomenon.

\paragraph{Local prohibition on overreach.}
No statement in this chapter promotes the local codon tile beyond the code layer.  In particular, the finite rows do not show that translation occurs, that a protein folds, that a molecular structure is physically admissible, that a biological function is realized, that an evolutionary mechanism is present, or that the tile is a universal law of biology.  Any such claim would require a separate contact that directly observes the relevant layer.  The present row stays with codon labels, local boundary structure, and finite recovery rates.
