\subsection{Leave-one-code-out recovery inside the codon window}
\label{sec:h1-leave-one-code-out}

\paragraph{Finite carrier and observed label contact.}
This record concerns the standard-code codon table at the code-read layer.  The finite carrier is the six-coordinate codon cube $Q_6$, read as a codon display with local faces, singleton label corners, and three-point label classes.  The observed contact is only the curated assignment of codons to amino-acid or stop labels.  Thus the data support codon-label readback and local boundary tests; they do not by themselves assert translation realization, folded structure, physical admissibility, biological function, mechanism, or universality.  Any claim crossing one of those boundaries would need an additional observation of that higher layer.

\paragraph{The punctured-cube row.}
The distinguished row $R$ has cardinality $13$ and is exactly the following finite set:
$$
\begin{aligned}
R = \{&\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\\
&\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UUA}\},\qquad |R|=13.
\end{aligned}
$$
As a local cubical object this same row has the verified face-vector
$$
\begin{aligned}
f(R)=(13,16,4).
\end{aligned}
$$
The row is therefore not being treated as a verbal motif or as a biological law; it is a finite subconfiguration of the codon cube with a measured local shape.  Its codons cover the AGR pair $\{\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG}\}$, the singleton $\mathrm{AAA}$, the CUN block $\{\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU}\}$, the Ile/Met boundary codon $\mathrm{AUA}$, the SSN-side pair $\{\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UUA}\}$, and the stop/Trp boundary triple $\{\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UGA}\}$.

\paragraph{Spectral and shape measurements.}
The finite row also has a verified spectral profile.  The measured values are
$$
\begin{aligned}
\lambda_M &= 0.6752479308830213,\\
\lambda_R &= 0.4758352843197002,\\
\mu &= 3.051487585298128.
\end{aligned}
$$
The same run evaluated the radial polynomial at $-6.224354365258478\times 10^{-12}$, reported equivalently as the cubic-polynomial residual.  This is numerically at tolerance for the recorded finite computation.  A separate finite randomization check used $N=2000$ samples with seed $42$.  It found $1$ exact shape match after $57377$ distinct-sample attempts and $55377$ duplicate retries, giving
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{\mathrm{exact}} &= 0.0005,\\
p_{J\geq 0.9} &= 0.0125,
\end{aligned}
$$
with $25$ samples reaching Jaccard similarity at least $0.9$.  Under the amino-acid-class-preserving null, the observed $M$ size was $20$; among $2000$ samples the exact-shape count was $0$, the lower-tail count at or below the observed $M$ size was $7$, and the reported probabilities were
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{\mathrm{aa\ class\ exact}} &= 0.0,\\
p_{\mathrm{aa\ class\ lower\ tail}} &= 0.0035.
\end{aligned}
$$
These values make the row a concrete finite signal relative to the tested null models, while still leaving its interpretation confined to the code-label layer.

\paragraph{Leave-one-code-out performance.}
The leave-one-code-out probe evaluated $23$ code tables and $63$ total targets.  The baseline hit rate was $0.19821739130434785$.  The recovery rate inside $M\setminus T$ was $0.9365079365079365$, and the rate after adjoining the boundary allowance was $0.9841269841269841$.  The same value, $0.9841269841269841$, was reported for hit rate above baseline.  In finite-count terms this says that almost all tested code-table targets fall back into the predicted local region once the boundary cases are admitted, while the baseline alone explains only about one fifth of the targets.

\paragraph{Codon-level boundary check.}
The leave-one-codon-out probe reports $0$ anchors, $5$ boundary cases, $8$ predictable cases, closure coverage $8$, and recovery rate $1.0$.  The recovery-rate threshold check is also $1.0$.  This should be read literally: in the recorded finite run, every tested codon-level case is classified either as predictable or as a boundary case, not as evidence for protein structure or function.  The module classification is complete on the $13$-codon row: $13$ codons are assigned, with no missing module codons, no extra module codons, and no unassigned codons.  The modules are
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{agr} &= \{\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG}\},\\

Every listed module has a predictable or boundary status, so the local tile is closed as a finite classification of the row.

\paragraph{Local conclusion.}
The claim is ready at the stated scope.  Window-six codon coordinates expose a local boundary-tile structure for the standard-code label table, and the leave-one-code-out run gives a high finite recovery rate against the reported baseline.  The evidence consists of the $13$-codon punctured-cube row, its face-vector $(13,16,4)$, the spectral values $\lambda_M=0.6752479308830213$, $\lambda_R=0.4758352843197002$, $\mu=3.051487585298128$, the shape-null probabilities $0.0005$ and $0.0125$, the amino-acid-class-preserving lower-tail probability $0.0035$, the $23$ evaluated code tables, and the $63$ targets.  The conclusion remains deliberately local: codon labels are being recovered and organized, but no translation process, folded protein geometry, physical admissibility, biological function, universal mechanism, or global biological necessity follows from this contact alone.
