\paragraph{Finite codon carrier and observed boundary row.}
The record is a finite code-read claim over the six-bit codon cube $Q_6$.  Its biological contact is only the curated standard-code label table: each codon is read as an amino-acid or stop label, and the resulting finite row is compared with local coordinate, closure, spectrum, and relation structure.  The distinguished residual set has cardinality $13$ and is exactly
$$
\begin{aligned}
R=\{&AAA,AGA,AGG,AUA,CUA,CUC,CUG,CUU,\\
&UAA,UAG,UCA,UGA,UUA\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The same row is also recorded as a punctured-cube object with face-vector
$$
\begin{aligned}
(f_0,f_1,f_2)=(13,16,4).
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the finite object is not an informal visual analogy: it has a listed vertex set, a listed combinatorial size, and a two-dimensional local face count.  The protein, organismal, and mechanistic readings are deliberately absent from this row.

\end{aligned}
$$
while the hit rate inside $M\setminus T$ alone is

$$
After adjoining the recorded boundary part, the rate returns to
$$
\begin{aligned}

This is the chapter-level finite signal: the local tile is highly effective as a code-label recovery region for the curated table family, but the statement remains a code-layer statement.  It does not say that the codon cube causes translation, folding, physical admissibility, biological function, a molecular mechanism, or a universal biological principle.

\paragraph{Scope boundary.}
The contact table supplies codon-to-label readback and local label-split tests only.  An amino-acid label sequence is not a folded structure, a stop or amino-acid reassignment is not a translation experiment, and a window-six tile is not a biochemical admissibility proof.  Any assertion about translation realization, structural order, physical admissibility, biological function, mechanism, or universality would require a separate observation designed for that layer.  Within its stated scope, the finite row is ready: the leave-one-code-out signal is a high-rate code-read recovery pattern over a completely listed residual codon set, with module closure, spectral numerics, and null-model comparisons all recorded at the code-label level.
