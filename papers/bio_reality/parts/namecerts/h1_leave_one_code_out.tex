\subsection{Leave-one-code-out recovery in a six-bit codon window}\label{sec:h1-leave-one-code-out}\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite object under test.}
The object in this record is the standard-code codon table viewed through a six-bit codon display. Each codon is treated as a coordinate in the codon cube $Q_6$, while the observed labels are the curated amino-acid or stop assignments supplied by the standard-code table. The packet tested here does not attempt to infer biochemical realization from the coordinate system. It asks a narrower question: whether a local boundary-tile extracted from the six-bit display remains predictive when one code table at a time is held out, while all readback remains tied to the finite codon-label table.

The distinguished boundary set $R$ has cardinality $13$. In codon notation it is
$$
\begin{aligned}
R=\{&\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\\
&\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UUA}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The same finite set is recorded as a punctured-cube shape with $f$-vector $(13,16,4)$. Thus the carrier is not a loose biological metaphor: it is a concrete thirteen-vertex pattern inside the codon cube, with sixteen one-dimensional incidences and four two-dimensional faces in the observed tile geometry.

\paragraph{Spectral and shape checks.}
The associated spectral check gives
$$
\begin{aligned}
\lambda_M&=0.6752479308830213,\\
\lambda_R&=0.4758352843197002,\\
\mu&=3.051487585298128.
\end{aligned}
$$
The radial cubic residual is $-6.224354365258478\times 10^{-12}$, the same value reported for the cubic-polynomial check, so the numerical witness is at machine-scale tolerance rather than a visually chosen fit. In the shape-significance run with seed $42$, $N=2000$ sampled comparison objects required $57377$ distinct-sample attempts and $55377$ duplicate retries. Only $1$ exact shape match occurred, giving $p_{\mathrm{exact}}=0.0005$, and $25$ samples reached Jaccard score at least $0.9$, giving $p_{\mathrm{Jaccard}\ge 0.9}=0.0125$. An amino-acid-class-preserving null model, also with $N=2000$ and seed $42$, produced $0$ exact matches, lower-tail count $7$ at observed $M$-size $20$, exact-shape value $0.0$, and lower-tail value $0.0035$. These finite counts support a local code-layer pattern; they do not convert the pattern into a law of translation or protein structure.

\paragraph{Leave-one-code-out recovery.}
The leave-one-code-out run evaluated $23$ code tables and $63$ held-out targets. The baseline hit rate was $0.19821739130434785$. Against that baseline, the boundary-guided procedure reached hit rate $0.9841269841269841$ above baseline and the same $0.9841269841269841$ inside $M\setminus T$ plus the boundary allowance. Inside $M\setminus T$ alone, the hit rate was $0.9365079365079365$. Written as finite counts, the strongest rate corresponds to $62$ hits out of $63$ targets, while the $M\setminus T$-only rate corresponds to $59$ out of $63$ targets. The result is therefore a leave-one-table robustness statement at the code-read layer: almost every held-out label target is recovered by the local tile plus boundary information, compared with a baseline below one fifth.

The family split is also finite. Across $5$ families, the hit rates above baseline were $0.8333333333333334$ for alternative yeast, $1.0$ for bacterial plastid, $1.0$ for candidate other, $1.0$ for mitochondrial, and $1.0$ for nuclear ciliate. The alternative-yeast family is the only listed family below perfect recovery, and even there the rate is five sixths. This matters because the signal is not merely a pooled aggregate; the family-level rows keep the same local-tile behavior in every listed family, with one explicitly bounded shortfall.

\paragraph{Codon-level module accounting.}
The leave-one-codon-out companion run records $0$ anchors, $5$ boundary codons, $8$ high-coverage closure codons, $8$ predictable codons, and recovery rate $1.0$. The module classification is complete: $R$ has size $13$, all $13$ codons are assigned, no extra module codons are present, no codons are missing from the modules, and no codons remain unassigned. The modules are
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{agr}&=\{\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG}\},\\
\mathrm{ar\_lys}&=\{\mathrm{AAA}\},\\
\mathrm{cun}&=\{\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU}\},\\
\mathrm{ile\_met}&=\{\mathrm{AUA}\},\\
\mathrm{ssn}&=\{\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UUA}\},\\
\mathrm{stop\_trp}&=\{\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UGA}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
Every module has a predictable or boundary member. This gives the local tile a precise internal decomposition: the thirteen boundary codons are not an undifferentiated list, but a partition into biologically legible codon families and singleton transition points.

\paragraph{Scope of the reality contact.}
The only reality contact used here is the curated standard-code table assigning codons to amino-acid or stop labels. That contact can test code-layer readback and local label splits. It cannot test ribosomal translation dynamics, folded three-dimensional protein structure, thermodynamic admissibility, cellular function, mechanism, or universality across biology. Amino-acid label sequences are still labels, not folded structures. CUN, UUR, stop, tryptophan, arginine, lysine, isoleucine, and methionine rows are therefore used only as code-table readback categories in this chapter. Any claim about physical realization would require a separate contact carrying that kind of observation.

\paragraph{Conclusion.}
For claim $\mathrm{h1.leave.one.code.out}$, the finite evidence is ready at the code-read layer. The six-bit codon coordinate system isolates a thirteen-codon punctured-cube boundary with $f$-vector $(13,16,4)$, stable spectral values, low null-model match rates, complete module assignment, and leave-one-code-out recovery of $62/63$ targets under the boundary-inclusive rule. The scientific content is a constrained boundary-tile claim about codon-label readback. It is not a claim that DNA must obey the geometry, that translation is explained by the tile, that protein folding follows from codon labels, or that any biological function has been established.
