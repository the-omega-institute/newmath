\subsection{Leave-one-code-out recovery for the window-six codon tile}
\label{sec:h1-leave-one-code-out}

\paragraph{Claim.}
The record \texttt{h1.leave.one.code.out} tests whether the window-six codon coordinate exposes a stable local boundary pattern when whole genetic-code tables are withheld one at a time.  The carrier is the six-bit codon cube $Q_6$, read only as a finite display of codon positions and curated labels.  The biological contact is the standard-code codon table: it supplies codon-to-amino-acid or stop labels, and it does not by itself supply translation dynamics, molecular structure, physical admissibility, biological function, a mechanism, or a universal law.  The claim is therefore a code-layer statement: the window can organize label readback and local split behavior, but any step beyond label readback requires a separate observation.

\paragraph{Finite codon boundary.}
The distinguished boundary set $R$ has exactly thirteen codons.  In the finite row it is
$$
\begin{aligned}
R=\{&\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\\
&\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UUA}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The same record verifies the cardinality $|R|=13$ and identifies $R$ as a punctured-cube boundary with $f$-vector $(13,16,4)$.  This gives a finite combinatorial object inside $Q_6$, not a biochemical cause.  The local modules are also exhausted by the row: $\mathrm{AGR}=\{\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG}\}$, $\mathrm{AR/Lys}=\{\mathrm{AAA}\}$, $\mathrm{CUN}=\{\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU}\}$, $\mathrm{Ile/Met}=\{\mathrm{AUA}\}$, $\mathrm{SSN}=\{\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UUA}\}$, and $\mathrm{Stop/Trp}=\{\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UGA}\}$.  The assignment count is thirteen, with $R$-size thirteen, no missing module codons, no extra module codons, and no unassigned codons.  Thus the named modules cover exactly the boundary set used by the probe.

\paragraph{Spectral and sampling evidence.}
The finite spectrum attached to the window has $\lambda_M=0.6752479308830213$, $\lambda_R=0.4758352843197002$, and $\mu=3.051487585298128$.  The radial cubic check is numerically closed at $-6.224354365258478\times 10^{-12}$, and the same value is reported for the cubic-polynomial residual.  A separate shape-sampling row used $N=2000$ distinct samples, seed $42$, and $57377$ distinct-sample attempts, with $55377$ duplicate retries.  It found one exact shape match, giving $p_{\mathrm{exact}}=0.0005$, and twenty-five matches at Jaccard at least $0.9$, giving $p_{J\geq 0.9}=0.0125$.  The amino-acid-class-preserving null used $N=2000$ and the same seed; it reports observed $M$-size $20$, zero exact matches, lower-tail count $7$, $p_{\mathrm{aa\ class,exact}}=0.0$, and $p_{\mathrm{aa\ class,lower\ tail}}=0.0035$.  The row therefore treats the window geometry as an unusually specific finite pattern relative to the stated nulls, while keeping the curated labels as the reality contact.

\paragraph{Leave-one-code-out test.}
The leave-one-code-out experiment evaluated $23$ code tables and $63$ total targets.  Its baseline hit rate is $0.19821739130434785$.  Against this baseline, the hit rate above baseline is $0.9841269841269841$.  The hit rate inside $M\setminus T$ is $0.9365079365079365$, and the hit rate inside $M\setminus T$ plus the boundary is $0.9841269841269841$.  In finite terms, withholding whole code tables still leaves almost all targets recoverable by the local window-plus-boundary rule, whereas the baseline rate is about one fifth.  The family-level audit is consistent with this: among five families, the hit-rate-above-baseline values are $0.8333333333333334$ for alternative yeast, $1.0$ for bacterial plastid, $1.0$ for candidate other, $1.0$ for mitochondrial, and $1.0$ for nuclear ciliate.

\paragraph{Related leave-one-codon-out check.}
The codon-level companion row records zero anchors, five boundary codons, eight predictable codons at high closure coverage, and recovery rate $1.0$.  This does not upgrade the claim into a mechanism.  It only says that, within the same code-layer finite representation, the local rule recovers the withheld codon cases recorded by the probe.  The module audit strengthens the bookkeeping point: every listed module has a predictable or boundary representative, and the module inventory exactly accounts for the thirteen codons of $R$.

\paragraph{Scope boundary.}
The admissible conclusion is narrow.  The window-six coordinate, together with the curated standard-code labels, supports local code-read structure: a punctured-cube boundary, a finite module partition, spectral values, and high leave-one-code-out recovery relative to the stated baseline and null rows.  It does not show that DNA obeys this geometry as a biological necessity.  It does not show that the codon tile performs translation, determines folded protein structure, satisfies physical constraints, realizes biological function, or constitutes a universal biological mechanism.  Amino-acid label readback remains label readback.  A future claim about folding, molecular realization, or function would need its own contact with those phenomena rather than borrowing authority from the codon table.
