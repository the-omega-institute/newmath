\subsection{Leave-One-Code-Out Recovery in the Window-Six Codon Cube}
\label{sec:h1-leave-one-code-out}

\paragraph{Codon-coordinate setting.}
The finite carrier is the six-coordinate codon cube $Q_6$, read as a $64$-vertex display of standard-code codons.  Each vertex carries a codon word over $\{\mathrm{U},\mathrm{C},\mathrm{A},\mathrm{G}\}$ and a curated code label, either an amino-acid label or a stop label.  The biological contact used here is only the curated standard-code label table.  It licenses codon-to-label readback and local label-split tests, but it does not observe translation dynamics, folded structure, energetic admissibility, realized function, or a universal biological mechanism.  Those higher layers require separate measurements or curated contacts before they can enter the claim.

\paragraph{Boundary set.}
The row isolates a distinguished codon subset $R$ of size $13$.  The verified finite list is
$$
\begin{aligned}
R=\{&\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\mathrm{UAA},\\
&\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UUA}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The same row records this set as a punctured-cube shape with face-vector
$$
\begin{aligned}
(f_0,f_1,f_2)=(13,16,4).
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the object under discussion is not the whole genetic code as a law of biology, but a finite local boundary pattern in the six-bit codon display.  The singleton-corner and three-point label-class distinctions are used as local labels inside this finite coordinate packet.

\paragraph{Spectral and sampling checks.}
The spectral row supplies three scalar coordinates for the local shape: $\lambda_M=0.6752479308830213$, $\lambda_R=0.4758352843197002$, and $\mu=3.051487585298128$.  The reported radial cubic residual is $-6.224354365258478\times 10^{-12}$, so the numerical witness is at tolerance scale rather than a visible polynomial failure.  The shape-significance run used $N=2000$ sampled alternatives with seed $42$.  Among $57377$ distinct-sample attempts, $55377$ were duplicate retries, only $1$ exact shape match appeared, and $25$ samples reached Jaccard similarity at least $0.9$.  The corresponding empirical probabilities were
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{\mathrm{exact}}&=0.0005,\\
p_{J\geq 0.9}&=0.0125.
\end{aligned}
$$
Under the amino-acid-class-preserving null model, again with $N=2000$ and seed $42$, the observed $M$-size was $20$, the exact-match count was $0$, and the lower-tail count at or below the observed $M$-size was $7$.  This gives
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{\mathrm{class\ exact}}&=0.0,\\
p_{\mathrm{class\ lower\ tail}}&=0.0035.
\end{aligned}
$$
These numbers support the claim that the local tile is unusually organized relative to the finite nulls reported here; they do not identify a biochemical cause.

\paragraph{Leave-one-code-out recovery.}
The primary leave-one-code-out row evaluates $23$ code tables and $63$ total targets.  The baseline hit rate is $0.19821739130434785$.  The observed recovery rates are
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{hit}(M\setminus T)&=0.9365079365079365,\\
\mathrm{hit}(M\setminus T\;\mathrm{plus\ boundary})&=0.9841269841269841,\\
\mathrm{hit\ above\ baseline}&=0.9841269841269841.
\end{aligned}
$$
The finite interpretation is that, after one code table is withheld, the coordinate-and-boundary packet still recovers almost all targets above the baseline rate.  The result is therefore a code-read phenomenon: it concerns how curated labels distribute over the local codon tile when a table is omitted from the readback test.

\paragraph{Family-level behavior.}
The family-stratified row separates the $23$ tables into $5$ families.  The above-baseline hit rates by family are
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{alternative\ yeast}&:0.8333333333333334,\\
\mathrm{bacterial\ plastid}&:1.0,\\
\mathrm{candidate\ other}&:1.0,\\
\mathrm{mitochondrial}&:1.0,\\
\mathrm{nuclear\ ciliate}&:1.0.
\end{aligned}
$$
The weakest family is still well above the global baseline $0.19821739130434785$.  This makes the leave-one-code-out result more specific than a single pooled statistic: the recovery survives the reported family partition, with alternative yeast as the finite-row low point.

\paragraph{Codon-module accounting.}
The codon-level module row accounts for every element of $R$ with no extras and no omissions.  It records $R$-size $13$, assigned count $13$, no extra module codons, no missing codons from the modules, and no unassigned codons.  The modules are
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{agr}&=\{\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG}\},\\
\mathrm{ar\_lys}&=\{\mathrm{AAA}\},\\
\mathrm{cun}&=\{\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU}\},\\
\mathrm{ile\_met}&=\{\mathrm{AUA}\},\\
\mathrm{ssn}&=\{\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UUA}\},\\
\mathrm{stop\_trp}&=\{\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UGA}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The same row states that every module has either predictable or boundary status.  The separate leave-one-codon-out row reports anchor count $0$, boundary count $5$, high closure coverage $8$, predictable count $8$, and recovery rate $1.0$.  Thus the local accounting does not leave a residual codon class outside the finite module inventory.

\paragraph{Scientific boundary.}
The conclusion is deliberately narrow.  Window-six coordinates, the punctured-cube boundary $R$, the spectral scalars, the null comparisons, and the leave-one-code-out rates jointly describe a robust local organization in codon-label readback.  They do not show that DNA or RNA must obey this geometry as a biological necessity.  They do not show that a ribosome realizes the coordinate construction, that an amino-acid label sequence folds, that a protein is physically admissible, that a function is expressed, or that the local tile is universal across biology.  The admissible claim is that the curated standard-code labels, when placed on the six-bit codon cube, expose a finite local boundary structure whose leave-one-code-out recovery is far above the reported baseline and whose module accounting exactly covers the $13$ codons in $R$.
