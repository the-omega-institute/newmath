\subsection{Leave-one-code-out stability in the window-six codon tile}
\label{subsec:bioreality-h1-leave-one-code-out}

\paragraph{Claim being tested.}
This record concerns the standard-code codon table as a code-level readback object.  The carrier is the six-coordinate codon cube $Q_6$, where each codon is treated as a finite six-bit display rather than as a biochemical mechanism.  The tested layer is $\mathrm{code\_read}$: the question is whether the window-six coordinate presentation exposes a local boundary-tile pattern that remains stable when whole genetic-code tables are held out.  The contact is a curated standard-code table assigning codons to amino-acid or stop labels.  That contact is sufficient for codon-label comparison, local tile splitting, and leave-one-code-out recovery counts.  It is not a contact for translation in a cell, folded protein structure, energetic admissibility, biological function, mechanistic necessity, or universal law.

\paragraph{Finite codon set.}
The distinguished row set $R$ has cardinality $13$.  In the verified run it is exactly
$$\begin{aligned}
R=\{&\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\\
&\mathrm{CUU},\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UUA}\}.
\end{aligned}$$
The same finite set is recorded as a punctured-cube tile with face-vector
$$\begin{aligned}
(f_0,f_1,f_2)=(13,16,4).
\end{aligned}$$
Thus the local object is not merely a list of exceptional codons: it has a finite incidence shape inside $Q_6$.  The shape is still local.  The data do not show that the surrounding biological system is generated by this geometry; they show that these $13$ codons occupy a reproducible tile with $16$ one-dimensional adjacencies and $4$ two-dimensional faces under the selected coordinate convention.

\paragraph{Spectral readout.}
The numerical spectrum attached to the same finite object gives
$$\begin{aligned}
\lambda_M&=0.6752479308830213,\\
\lambda_R&=0.4758352843197002,\\
\mu&=3.051487585298128.
\end{aligned}$$
The radial polynomial residual is $-6.224354365258478\times 10^{-12}$, the same value reported for the cubic-polynomial check.  At this precision the finite spectral calculation is internally consistent: the displayed eigenvalue data satisfy the recorded polynomial constraint to numerical tolerance.  This supports using the coordinate tile as a stable finite probe, but it does not turn the probe into a physical or functional theory of translation.

\paragraph{Shape significance.}
Against the reported coordinate null, the observed tile shape was rare.  In $N=2000$ sampled controls, there was $1$ exact shape match, giving
$$\begin{aligned}
p_{\mathrm{exact}}=0.0005.
\end{aligned}$$
There were $25$ samples with Jaccard similarity at least $0.9$, giving
$$\begin{aligned}
p_{J\ge 0.9}=0.0125.
\end{aligned}$$
The run required $57377$ distinct-sample attempts and encountered $55377$ duplicate retries, so the sample space was not casually traversed.  A stricter amino-acid-class-preserving null also failed to reproduce the exact shape in $2000$ trials: the exact-match count was $0$, the lower-tail count at the observed $M$-size threshold was $7$, the observed $M$ size was $20$, and the lower-tail probability was $0.0035$.  These finite probabilities support the claim that the tile is not a common accident under the tested controls.  They do not identify a molecular cause.

\paragraph{Leave-one-code-out result.}
The central holdout test evaluated $23$ code tables and $63$ total targets.  The baseline hit rate was
$$\begin{aligned}
0.19821739130434785.
\end{aligned}$$
The hit rate above baseline was
$$\begin{aligned}
0.9841269841269841,
\end{aligned}$$
while the hit rate inside $M\setminus T$ was $0.9365079365079365$ and the hit rate inside $M\setminus T$ plus the boundary allowance returned to $0.9841269841269841$.  The finite interpretation is direct: after leaving out whole code tables, the local coordinate tile nearly always recovers the held-out target at the code-label layer, especially once the boundary codons are counted as legitimate local contacts.  The conclusion is not that all genetic codes obey a single geometric law.  The conclusion is that this finite codon-window representation has strong recovery behavior on the particular curated code-table row data supplied to the test.

\paragraph{Family-level stability.}
The family split contains $5$ families.  The hit rates above baseline were $0.8333333333333334$ for alternative yeast, $1.0$ for bacterial-plastid, $1.0$ for candidate-other, $1.0$ for mitochondrial, and $1.0$ for nuclear-ciliate.  The alternative-yeast family is therefore the only family in this finite report below perfect recovery, and it still substantially exceeds the baseline rate $0.19821739130434785$.  This pattern makes the leave-one-code-out result less likely to be a single-family artifact, while preserving the same scope restriction: these are code-table comparisons, not demonstrations of cellular realization.

\paragraph{Codon-level recovery and modules.}
The companion leave-one-codon-out record reports recovery rate $1.0$, with $8$ predictable codons, $5$ boundary codons, $8$ high-coverage closure cases, and $0$ anchors.  The module classification is complete for all $13$ codons of $R$: $R$ has size $13$, the assigned count is $13$, and the lists of extra module codons, missing codons, and unassigned codons are all empty.  The finite modules are
$$\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{agr}&=\{\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG}\},\\
\mathrm{ar\_lys}&=\{\mathrm{AAA}\},\\
\mathrm{cun}&=\{\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU}\},\\
\mathrm{ile\_met}&=\{\mathrm{AUA}\},\\
\mathrm{ssn}&=\{\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UUA}\},\\
\mathrm{stop\_trp}&=\{\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UGA}\}.
\end{aligned}$$
Every listed module has at least one predictable or boundary member, so the module decomposition accounts for the full punctured tile rather than leaving a residue of unexplained codons.  The stop--tryptophan, CUN, AGR, isoleucine--methionine, lysine-side, and SSN-side pieces are therefore local code-read modules in the finite row data.

\paragraph{Scientific boundary.}
The result is ready as a finite code-layer finding.  The curated table supplies codon-to-label assignments, and the verified finite rows show a stable local tile, a $13$-codon punctured-cube shape, a consistent spectral signature, rare shape occurrence under the recorded nulls, and high leave-one-code-out recovery over $23$ evaluated tables and $63$ targets.  No part of this record licenses promotion beyond that layer.  Translation realization, folded structure, physical admissibility, biological function, causal mechanism, and universality would each require a separate observation channel matched to that higher claim.  In particular, amino-acid label readback is not folded-protein evidence, and organism-specific CUN or UUR wording is not structural evidence unless an independent structure or organism-matched contact is supplied.
