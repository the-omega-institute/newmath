\subsection{Leave-One-Code-Out Boundary for the Standard Code}
\label{subsec:h1-leave-one-code-out}

\paragraph{Finite codon display.}
The claim concerns the standard-code codon table only at the level where codons are read as curated amino-acid or stop labels.  The finite carrier is the six-coordinate codon cube $Q_6$, with each codon represented by a six-bit window coordinate and then compared with the curated standard-code label table.  The observed residual row $R$ has cardinality $13$ and is exactly
$$
\begin{aligned}
R=\{&AAA, AGA, AGG, AUA, CUA, CUC, CUG, CUU,\\
&UAA, UAG, UCA, UGA, UUA\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The same row is reported as a punctured-cube object with face vector $(13,16,4)$.  Thus the local object is not a verbal analogy: it is a concrete finite subset of codon vertices together with the induced edge-and-face data recorded by the cube coordinates.  The readout remains label-level: the external contact is the curated table assigning each codon to an amino-acid or stop label, not an observation of translation dynamics or molecular structure.

\paragraph{Spectral and sampling checks.}
The spectral record separates the residual row from a purely descriptive list.  The reported values are
$$
\begin{aligned}
\lambda_M&=0.6752479308830213,\\
\lambda_R&=0.4758352843197002,\\
\mu&=3.051487585298128,
\end{aligned}
$$
with radial cubic residual $-6.224354365258478\times 10^{-12}$, within the stated numerical tolerance.  Against the sampled null family with seed $42$, the shape experiment used $N=2000$ accepted samples, required $57377$ distinct-sample attempts, and incurred $55377$ duplicate retries.  It found $1$ exact shape match and $25$ matches with Jaccard score at least $0.9$, giving
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{\mathrm{exact}}&=0.0005,\\
p_{\mathrm{Jaccard}\geq 0.9}&=0.0125.
\end{aligned}
$$
A second amino-acid-class-preserving null, also with $N=2000$ and seed $42$, used observed $M$-size $20$, found $0$ exact matches, and found $7$ lower-tail samples at or below the observed $M$-size.  Its recorded probabilities are $p=0.0$ for exact shape and $p=0.0035$ for the lower tail.  These numbers support a finite code-table pattern stronger than a random relabeling baseline, while still saying only that the codon-label table has a local coordinate regularity.

\paragraph{Leave-one-code-out readback.}
The primary leave-one-code-out experiment evaluated $23$ alternative code tables and $63$ targets.  The baseline hit rate was $0.19821739130434785$.  The hit rate above baseline was $0.9841269841269841$; the hit rate inside $M-T$ was $0.9365079365079365$; and the hit rate inside $M-T$ together with the boundary row was $0.9841269841269841$.  In finite-row terms, removing a code table and asking the coordinate-boundary pattern to recover the held-out label behavior succeeds far above the baseline estimate.  The family stratification reports $5$ families: alternative yeast has hit rate $0.8333333333333334$ above baseline, while bacterial-plastid, candidate-other, mitochondrial, and nuclear-ciliate each have rate $1.0$.  This makes the claim local and quantitative: the signal is a code-table readback regularity for known label assignments, not a biological law governing all organisms or all molecular layers.

Each module has either predictable or boundary status in the reported classification.

\paragraph{Interpretive boundary.}
The supported statement is narrow.  Window-six codon coordinates expose a local boundary-tile pattern in the curated standard-code table, and the finite tests show that this pattern recovers held-out code-label behavior at rates far above the recorded baseline.  The contact used here is only the codon-to-label table.  It does not observe ribosomal translation, folding, thermodynamic admissibility, cellular function, evolutionary mechanism, or universality across all biological settings.  Amino-acid label readback is not protein structure.  Cross-organism wording, tRNA notation, or code-family grouping does not by itself supply a structure experiment.  Any promotion from code labels to translation, folded structure, physical admissibility, biological function, mechanism, or universal law would require a separate contact that directly measures that higher layer.  With that restriction in place, the claim is ready as a finite, reality-contacted code-read record: the row $R$, its punctured-cube face vector, its spectral values, its sampling probabilities, and its leave-one-code-out recovery rates all belong to the same bounded standard-code table analysis.
