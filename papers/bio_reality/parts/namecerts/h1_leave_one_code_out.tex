\subsection{Leave-One-Code-Out Stability for the Window-Six Codon Tile}\label{sec:h1-leave-one-code-out}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite coordinate record.}
The claim concerns the standard genetic-code label table viewed only at the code-reading layer.  The carrier is the six-bit codon cube $Q_6$: each codon is represented by three nucleotide positions, each position carrying two bits of display information.  The relevant distinctions are coordinate position, local tile face, singleton label corner, and three-point label class.  The readback is therefore a finite codon-to-label table, not a biochemical process.  The distinguished residual set has cardinality $13$ and is recorded as
$$
\begin{aligned}
R=\{&AAA,AGA,AGG,AUA,CUA,CUC,CUG,CUU,\\
&UAA,UAG,UCA,UGA,UUA\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The same finite row identifies this set as a punctured cube with $f$-vector $(13,16,4)$.  In this chapter, that phrase means exactly that the selected codons form a finite local shape in $Q_6$ with thirteen vertices, sixteen recorded edges, and four recorded two-faces.  It is not a statement about translation dynamics or organismal necessity.

\paragraph{Spectral and sampling checks.}
The associated spectral row gives
$$
\begin{aligned}
\lambda_M&=0.6752479308830213,\\
\lambda_R&=0.4758352843197002,\\
\mu&=3.051487585298128.
\end{aligned}
$$
The cubic relation is numerically satisfied at $-6.224354365258478\times 10^{-12}$, and the same value is reported for the radial polynomial residual.  These numbers are used only as finite diagnostics for the codon-window construction.  They do not assert that the biological code is generated by the spectral calculation.

The shape-significance run used $N=2000$ sampled controls with seed $42$.  It required $57377$ distinct-sample attempts and encountered $55377$ duplicate retries.  Among those controls, exactly one matched the observed shape, giving $p_{\mathrm{exact}}=0.0005$, while $25$ had Jaccard similarity at least $0.9$, giving $p_{J\geq 0.9}=0.0125$.  A separate amino-acid-class-preserving null run, also with $N=2000$ and seed $42$, found observed $|M|=20$, no exact shape matches, and $7$ lower-tail samples at or below the observed $M$ size.  Its reported probabilities were $p_{\mathrm{class\ exact}}=0.0$ and $p_{\mathrm{lower\ tail}}=0.0035$.  These rows support the claim that the local code-layer tile is not a routine output of the tested finite null models.

\paragraph{Leave-one-code-out result.}
The claim identifier $h1.leave.one.code.out$ records the leave-one-code-out test over $23$ evaluated tables and $63$ total targets.  The baseline hit rate was
$$
\begin{aligned}
0.19821739130434785,
\end{aligned}
$$
whereas the hit rate above baseline was
$$
\begin{aligned}
0.9841269841269841.
\end{aligned}
$$
The hit rate inside $M\setminus T$ was $0.9365079365079365$, and the hit rate inside $M\setminus T$ together with the boundary was again $0.9841269841269841$.  Thus the finite record says that, after one code table is withheld, the window-six construction still recovers nearly all target positions when the local boundary information is allowed.  The statement is deliberately narrower than a biological mechanism claim: it is a stability statement about table labels under a finite withholding test.

\paragraph{Reality contact and scope.}
The only reality contact used here is the curated standard-code table assigning codons to amino-acid or stop labels.  That contact can test code readback and local codon-tile label splitting.  It cannot test translation realization, folded structure, physical admissibility, biological function, or a universal mechanism.  Consequently, an amino-acid label sequence read from the table remains a code-layer object.  No claim about protein folding, structural order, function, physical feasibility, organism-level universality, or the machinery of translation follows from the rows above unless another independent contact supplies that higher-layer observation.

\paragraph{Chapter verdict.}
Within its stated scope, the finite record is positive.  The residual set has a fixed $13$-codon membership, the punctured-cube shape has $f$-vector $(13,16,4)$, the spectral residual is numerically near zero, the sampled null checks give small tail probabilities, and the leave-one-code-out run reports $0.9841269841269841$ hit rate above baseline over $63$ targets from $23$ tables.  The chapter therefore records a stable local code-reading pattern in $Q_6$.  Its scientific boundary is equally part of the result: the evidence reaches codon-label readback and local boundary structure, and it stops before translation, folding, physical admissibility, function, mechanism, or universality.
