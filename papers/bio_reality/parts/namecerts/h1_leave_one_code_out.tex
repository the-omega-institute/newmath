\subsection{Leave-one-code-out recovery inside a local codon tile}
\label{subsec:h-one-leave-one-code-out}

\paragraph{Claim under test.}
The record \texttt{h1.leave.one.code.out} concerns a code-layer readback test on the standard-code codon table. The finite carrier is the six-coordinate codon cube $Q_6$, viewed through codon labels supplied by a curated standard genetic-code table. The relevant distinctions are a six-bit codon display, a local tile face, a singleton label corner, and a three-point label class. The claim is deliberately local: the window-six coordinate system may expose a boundary pattern in the code table, but the amino-acid and stop labels themselves enter through the curated biological table. The test therefore asks whether a held-out code table entry is recovered by the local tile and its boundary closure, not whether the coordinate geometry explains translation, folding, physical feasibility, organismal function, a mechanism, or a universal biological law.

\paragraph{Finite codon support.}
The distinguished residual set $R$ has cardinality $13$. The verified codon list is
$$
\begin{aligned}
R = \{&\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\\
&\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UUA}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The same row verifies that $R$ is the punctured-cube support detected by the coordinate construction, with face-vector data
$$
\begin{aligned}
(f_0,f_1,f_2)=(13,16,4).
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the finite object is not a vague enrichment of the codon table: it is a thirteen-codon subset with a four-face local tile incidence pattern. The available finite row does not list every member of the ambient set $M$, but it verifies the observed size $|M|=20$ in the amino-acid-class-preserving null comparison, and the leave-one computations are stated in terms of $M-T$ and $M-T$ plus the boundary layer.

\paragraph{Spectral and shape checks.}
The same finite packet includes a spectrum row. The residual and module radii are
$$
\begin{aligned}
\lambda_M &= 0.6752479308830213,\\
\lambda_R &= 0.4758352843197002,\\
\mu &= 3.051487585298128.
\end{aligned}
$$
The cubic radial check evaluates to $-6.224354365258478\times 10^{-12}$, the same value recorded for the radial polynomial residual, so the numerical witness is at roundoff scale rather than at visible finite-row error. In the unrestricted shape comparison with $N=2000$ random samples and seed $42$, there was exactly $1$ exact shape match, giving
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{\mathrm{exact}} &= 0.0005,\\
p_{J\geq 0.9} &= 0.0125,
\end{aligned}
$$
with $25$ high-Jaccard matches after $57377$ distinct-sample attempts and $55377$ duplicate retries. Under the amino-acid-class-preserving null with the same sample size and seed, the exact match count was $0$, the lower-tail count at or below the observed $M$ size was $7$, and the recorded probabilities were
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{\mathrm{class\ exact}} &= 0.0,\\
p_{\mathrm{class\ lower\ tail}} &= 0.0035.
\end{aligned}
$$
These numbers support a finite enrichment claim for the local code-layer geometry. They do not supply a biochemical dynamics claim.

\paragraph{Leave-one-code-out row.}
The primary leave-one-code-out experiment evaluated $23$ tables and $63$ total targets. Its baseline hit rate was $0.19821739130434785$. The local recovery rate above baseline was
$$
\begin{aligned}
0.9841269841269841,
\end{aligned}
$$
while the stricter $M-T$ rate was $0.9365079365079365$ and the $M-T$ plus boundary rate returned to $0.9841269841269841$. The numerical content of the claim is therefore a sharp recovery contrast: about one fifth of the targets are hit by the baseline, while the local tile with boundary information hits all but one target in the evaluated leave-one-code-out family. By family, the hit rates above baseline were $1.0$ for bacterial-plastid, mitochondrial, nuclear-ciliate, and candidate-other rows, and $0.8333333333333334$ for the alternative-yeast row, across $5$ recorded families. The finite result is strongest as a code-read phenomenon: when labels are withheld in the tested table family, the coordinate-and-boundary pattern often predicts the held-out label class.

\paragraph{Leave-one-codon-out consistency.}
The companion codon-level row provides a local diagnostic on the same support. It records $0$ anchors, $5$ boundary codons, $8$ predictable codons, and $8$ high-coverage closure cases, with recovery rate $1.0$. The module classification is complete on the thirteen-codon residual support:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{AGR} &= \{\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG}\},\\
\mathrm{AR\_Lys} &= \{\mathrm{AAA}\},\\
\mathrm{CUN} &= \{\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU}\},\\
\mathrm{Ile\_Met} &= \{\mathrm{AUA}\},\\
\mathrm{SSN} &= \{\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UUA}\},\\
\mathrm{Stop\_Trp} &= \{\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UGA}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The row reports $R$ size $13$, assigned count $13$, no extra module codons, no missing residual codons, and no unassigned codons. Each module has either a predictable member or a boundary member, so the local picture is not concentrated in only one codon family.

\paragraph{Local boundary of interpretation.}
The curated standard-code table is a contact for codon-to-label readback. It says that codons are assigned to amino-acid or stop labels, and it lets the finite carrier be checked against those labels. It does not observe ribosomal translation, molecular folding, energetic admissibility, cellular role, evolutionary cause, or universality across biological contexts. The blocked folded-structure reading is therefore not a weakly supported conclusion; it is outside the evidence type supplied here. In particular, an amino-acid label sequence is still only a label sequence at this level. Any claim about a realized protein, a fold, a physically admissible chain, or a biological function must be attached to a separate contact of the appropriate kind. The present chapter closes only the finite code-read claim: on the verified rows, the six-coordinate local tile and its boundary recover the held-out standard-code labels at the stated rates, with a thirteen-codon punctured-cube residual support and a complete module classification.
