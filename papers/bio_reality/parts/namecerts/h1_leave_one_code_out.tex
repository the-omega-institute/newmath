\subsection{Leave-One-Code-Out Recovery on a Window-Six Codon Tile}
\label{sec:bior-h1-leave-one-code-out}

\paragraph{Claim and finite display.}
The claim identified by \texttt{h1.leave.one.code.out} concerns a six-coordinate codon display for the standard-code label table. The object under study is not translation itself, but the finite code-layer table assigning each codon to an amino-acid or stop label. The coordinate carrier is the codon cube $Q_6$, read through local tile faces, singleton label corners, and three-point label classes. The finite question is whether local boundary geometry in this display helps recover changed labels when one code table is held out, while keeping curated label assignment as the empirical contact.

\paragraph{Distinguished boundary row.}
The row $R$ has cardinality $13$. Its codons are exactly
$$
\begin{aligned}
R=\{&\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\\
&\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UUA}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The punctured-cube computation gives the same list and the face-vector
$$
\begin{aligned}
f(R)=(13,16,4).
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the finite boundary row is not an informal visual cluster: it is a concrete thirteen-codon subset with sixteen one-dimensional incidences and four two-dimensional faces in the induced local complex.

\paragraph{Spectral finite check.}
The spectral row separates the measured $R$ component from the larger $M$ component. The recorded values are

\lambda_M&=0.6752479308830213,\\

The radial polynomial residual is $-6.224354365258478\cdot 10^{-12}$, the same value recorded for the cubic check. This residual is numerically negligible at the recorded precision, so the spectrum is being used here as a finite diagnostic of the computed coordinate object, not as a biological dynamical law.

\paragraph{Shape significance against randomized rows.}
The shape-significance run used $N=2000$ samples with seed $42$. It required $57377$ distinct-sample attempts and incurred $55377$ duplicate retries. Among the accepted samples there was $1$ exact shape match, giving
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{\mathrm{exact}}=0.0005.
\end{aligned}
$$
There were $25$ samples with Jaccard score at least $0.9$, giving
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{\mathrm{Jaccard}\geq 0.9}=0.0125.
\end{aligned}
$$
The amino-acid-class-preserving null model also used $N=2000$ and seed $42$. It observed $0$ exact matches to the shape, an observed $M$ size of $20$, and $7$ lower-tail samples at or below that size. The resulting values were
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{\mathrm{aa\ class\ exact}}&=0.0,\\
p_{\mathrm{aa\ class\ lower\ tail}}&=0.0035.
\end{aligned}
$$
These tests support the statement that the row geometry is unusually constrained relative to the tested finite null ensembles.

\paragraph{Leave-one-code-out recovery.}
The leave-one-code-out experiment evaluated $23$ code tables and $63$ held-out targets. The baseline hit rate was
$$
\begin{aligned}
0.19821739130434785.
\end{aligned}
$$
The hit rate above baseline was
$$
\begin{aligned}
0.9841269841269841,
\end{aligned}
$$
while the hit rate inside $M\setminus T$ was $0.9365079365079365$ and the hit rate inside $M\setminus T$ plus the boundary was $0.9841269841269841$. Thus the finite row does not merely name a retrospective subset: under the recorded hold-out protocol it nearly always places the changed label in the predicted region or its boundary extension.

\paragraph{Reality contact and local verdict.}
The empirical contact used here is the curated standard genetic-code table: it supplies codon-to-label assignments at code-read resolution. Under that contact, the local tile computation supports a finite claim about label readback and held-out code-table recovery. It does not support a claim that the six-coordinate geometry is a law of DNA, a translation mechanism, a folded-protein structure, a physical-admissibility criterion, a biological-function explanation, or a universal biological principle. Amino-acid label sequences remain label sequences in this chapter. Crossing from code labels to translation realization, folding, physical admissibility, function, mechanism, or universality requires a separate contact carrying observations at that higher layer.
