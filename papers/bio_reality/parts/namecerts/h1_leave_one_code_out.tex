\subsection{Leave-One-Code-Out Codon Boundary Readback}
\label{sec:bioreality-h1-leave-one-code-out}

\paragraph{Finite coordinate record.}
The claim concerns a six-coordinate codon display $Q_6$ whose observable contact is the curated standard-code label table. The data record is deliberately kept at the code-reading layer: a codon is inspected as a finite coordinate point, a local face or corner may be marked by the induced tile geometry, and the readback is only the amino-acid-or-stop label assigned by the curated table. The distinguished punctured region $R$ is the thirteen-codon set
$$
\begin{aligned}
R={}&\{\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UUA}\},\\
|R|={}&13,\qquad f(R)=(13,16,4).
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the finite row is not merely a count: it supplies a concrete support set together with a face-vector signature. The module decomposition accounts for all thirteen codons with no omissions or extras: $\{\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG}\}$ in the $\mathrm{AGR}$ block, $\{\mathrm{AAA}\}$ in the $\mathrm{AR}/\mathrm{Lys}$ block, $\{\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU}\}$ in the $\mathrm{CUN}$ block, $\{\mathrm{AUA}\}$ in the $\mathrm{Ile}/\mathrm{Met}$ block, $\{\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UUA}\}$ in the $\mathrm{SSN}$ block, and $\{\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UGA}\}$ in the stop/tryptophan block. The row reports $R\_\mathrm{size}=13$, assigned count $13$, no extra module codons, no missing codons, and no unassigned codons.

\paragraph{Spectral and shape checks.}
The associated spectral row records
$$
\begin{aligned}
\lambda_M&=0.6752479308830213,\\
\lambda_R&=0.4758352843197002,\\
\mu&=3.051487585298128,
\end{aligned}
$$
with the cubic or radial residual equal to $-6.224354365258478\times 10^{-12}$. This residual is numerically at tolerance scale, so the row supports the stated finite spectral relation rather than merely a qualitative visual resemblance. The shape-significance comparison used $N=2000$ sampled alternatives with seed $42$. It observed one exact shape match, giving $p=0.0005$, and twenty-five samples with Jaccard similarity at least $0.9$, giving $p=0.0125$. The sampling process required $57377$ distinct-sample attempts and encountered $55377$ duplicate retries, which is relevant because the record is testing a constrained finite family rather than an unconstrained continuum of shapes.

\paragraph{Class-preserving null comparison.}
A second null comparison preserves amino-acid class structure. In that test, again with $N=2000$ and seed $42$, the observed $M$ size was $20$. The exact-shape count was $0$, so the recorded exact-shape probability is $0.0$. The lower-tail count at or below the observed $M$ size was $7$, giving a lower-tail probability $0.0035$. These values make the local codon-tile signature harder to dismiss as a generic artifact of amino-acid class preservation. They do not, however, convert the coordinate signature into a claim about translation chemistry or protein behavior; the finite row only evaluates label-table readback and local codon-coordinate structure.

\paragraph{Leave-one-code-out result.}
The leave-one-code-out row evaluated $23$ code tables and $63$ total targets. Its baseline hit rate was $0.19821739130434785$. Against that baseline, the hit rate above baseline was $0.9841269841269841$. The hit rate inside $M-T$ was $0.9365079365079365$, and the rate inside $M-T$ plus the boundary set was $0.9841269841269841$. In finite-row terms, the important point is that the boundary-augmented local tile nearly recovers the leave-one-code-out targets while the baseline rate remains close to one fifth. The family-stratified row gives five families: alternative yeast has hit rate $0.8333333333333334$ above baseline, while bacterial/plastid, candidate other, mitochondrial, and nuclear ciliate each report $1.0$ above baseline. This family split is useful because it shows where the finite record is strongest and where the weaker alternative-yeast family should remain visible rather than averaged away.

\paragraph{Leave-one-codon-out companion check.}
The companion leave-one-codon-out row reports anchor count $0$, boundary count $5$, high closure coverage $8$, predictable count $8$, and recovery rate $1.0$. The recovery rate above threshold is also $1.0$. These values place the local boundary mechanism in the same code-level neighborhood as the leave-one-code-out result: codons can be recovered or classified through the finite coordinate closure and boundary accounting, but the contact remains a codon-to-label table. The row therefore supports a local readback claim: window-six coordinates expose a boundary-tile organization around the standard-code labels, and that organization has measurable predictive content in leave-one-out tests.

\paragraph{Reality contact and local boundary.}
The only reality contact used here is the curated standard genetic-code table, understood as an assignment of codons to amino-acid or stop labels. This contact can test code-layer readback and local label splitting. It cannot test whether translation is realized in a biological system, whether a peptide folds, whether a molecule is physically admissible, whether any biological function occurs, or whether the tile geometry is a universal mechanism. The observed agreement is therefore scoped as follows: the finite coordinate display, punctured set $R$, spectral relation, null comparisons, module decomposition, and leave-one-out hit rates jointly support a local code-read boundary pattern. They do not establish translation, folding, physical admissibility, function, mechanism, or universality unless a separate experiment or curated contact supplies that higher-layer information.

\paragraph{Chapter verdict.}
The finite evidence is sufficient for the code-read claim identified by \texttt{h1.leave.one.code.out}. The row is ready because it gives a concrete carrier $Q_6$, a finite distinguished set $R$ with $13$ codons and face vector $(13,16,4)$, numerical spectrum values, exact and high-Jaccard null probabilities, class-preserving null probabilities, complete module assignment, and leave-one-out rates over $23$ tables and $63$ targets. The scientific content is the boundary-local readback result: a window-six codon coordinate system captures a strong local pattern in curated standard-code labels. The same finite record also fixes the stopping point of the claim. No conclusion about translation, folded structure, physical feasibility, biological function, universal law, or mechanism is licensed by these rows alone.
