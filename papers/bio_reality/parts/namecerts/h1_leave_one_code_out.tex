\subsection{Leave-One-Code-Out Readback on the Window-Six Codon Cube}
\label{sec:h1-leave-one-code-out}

\paragraph{Claim and finite setting.}
This record treats the standard genetic-code table as a finite label contact on the six-coordinate codon cube $Q_6$.  The object under test is not the biochemical execution of translation, but the code-level question of whether a local window-six tile predicts where reassigned labels tend to fall when related code tables are compared with the standard table.  The finite carrier is the codon display itself, with coordinate distinctions, local tile faces, singleton label corners, and three-point label classes.  The observed table supplies codon-to-label assignments only: it tells which codons carry amino-acid or stop labels in the curated standard code, and it does not by itself observe ribosomes, folding, thermodynamic viability, cellular function, or a universal law of biology.

\paragraph{Boundary region.}
The recurrent boundary set has cardinality $13$.  In the verified row it is exactly
$$
\begin{aligned}
R=\{&\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\\
&\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UUA}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The same row records that this is a punctured-cube shaped set with face-vector
$$
\begin{aligned}
(f_0,f_1,f_2)=(13,16,4).
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the finite observation is not merely a list of exceptional codons.  It has a local cubical incidence pattern: thirteen vertices, sixteen one-dimensional adjacencies, and four two-dimensional faces inside the six-bit codon coordinate system.  The table contact then asks whether leave-one-code-out targets concentrate in this region or in its immediate local boundary, rather than spreading according to a baseline label-preserving expectation.

\paragraph{Spectral and shape checks.}
The associated spectral row gives

\begin{aligned}

$$
The radial cubic residual is $-6.224354365258478\times 10^{-12}$, which is also the recorded value of the cubic-polynomial check.  The shape comparison against randomized finite samples used $N=2000$ samples with seed $42$.  It found one exact shape match, so
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{\mathrm{exact}}=0.0005.
\end{aligned}
$$
For the high-overlap threshold, $25$ samples had Jaccard similarity at least $0.9$, giving
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{\mathrm{Jaccard}\ge 0.9}=0.0125.
\end{aligned}
$$
The run needed $57377$ distinct-sample attempts and logged $55377$ duplicate retries, so the finite random comparison was strongly constrained by the combinatorics of the sampled class.  A separate amino-acid-class-preserving null model, again with $N=2000$ and seed $42$, had observed $M$-size $20$, exact-match count $0$, and lower-tail count $7$.  Its recorded probabilities are
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{\mathrm{aa\ class\ exact}}&=0.0,\\
p_{\mathrm{aa\ class\ lower\ tail}}&=0.0035.
\end{aligned}
$$
These numbers support the narrower statement that the observed code-level pattern is not typical under the tested finite null procedures.  They do not identify a physical cause.

\paragraph{Leave-one-code-out result.}
The leave-one-code-out experiment evaluated $23$ tables and $63$ targets.  The baseline hit rate was $0.19821739130434785$.  The tested local region recovered targets at rate
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{hit}(M\setminus T)&=0.9365079365079365,\\
\mathrm{hit}(M\setminus T\;\mathrm{plus\ boundary})&=0.9841269841269841,\\
\mathrm{hit\ above\ baseline}&=0.9841269841269841.
\end{aligned}
$$
The empirical content of the claim is therefore specific: under this finite leave-one-code-out protocol, almost all reassignment targets lie in the predicted local region once the boundary allowance is included, and this rate is far above the recorded baseline.  The result is a code-read phenomenon.  It says that curated label differences between code tables are concentrated near the window-six local tile; it does not say that the geometry performs translation or determines protein behavior.

This is useful for readback because it makes the local tile less likely to be a one-family artifact, while still leaving the biological interpretation deliberately narrow.

The completeness row records $R\_\mathrm{size}=13$, assigned count $13$, and empty lists for extra module codons, missing codons, and unassigned codons.  Every module has either predictable or boundary status.  This matters because the local readback is not achieved by leaving a residue of unexplained exceptional codons outside the named modules.

A related leave-one-codon-out row reports anchor count $0$, boundary count $5$, closure-coverage-high count $8$, predictable count $8$, and recovery rate $1.0$.  The same row marks the recovery rate above threshold as $1.0$.  This gives a second finite stability check: individual codon removal does not collapse the local classification, although the stress test still remains inside the code-label layer.

\paragraph{Interpretation.}
The verified finite rows support the following local conclusion.  In the six-bit codon coordinate display, the standard-code label table induces a recurrent boundary region $R$ of size $13$ with punctured-cube incidence data $(13,16,4)$.  Leave-one-code-out targets across $23$ evaluated tables and $63$ targets are recovered at rate $0.9841269841269841$ when the local region and boundary are used, compared with baseline $0.19821739130434785$.  The shape and amino-acid-class-preserving null checks give small reported probabilities, and the module accounting covers all codons in $R$.  Therefore the claim is ready at the code-read level: the finite evidence supports a local codon-tile boundary pattern for curated label reassignment.

\paragraph{Local limit of the claim.}
No statement about translation realization, folded structure, physical admissibility, biological function, mechanism, or universality follows from these rows.  A codon-to-label table is not a ribosomal process, an amino-acid sequence is not a folded protein, and a local tile in $Q_6$ is not a global biological law.  Any later assertion about structure, function, admissibility, organism-level mechanism, or universality would require a separate contact that directly observes that higher layer.
