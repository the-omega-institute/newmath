\paragraph{Finite codon display.}
The claim $\mathrm{h1.leave.one.code.out}$ is a code-layer record for the standard-code codon table.  The finite carrier is the six-coordinate codon cube $Q_6$, read as the sixty-four codon displays obtained from the three nucleotide positions.  The observed contact is a curated standard-code label table: each codon is assigned an amino-acid label or a stop label.  That contact gives label readback only.  It does not observe a ribosome, a translation process, a folded molecule, an admissible physical conformation, a biological function, a mechanism, or a universal rule.

The distinguished leave-one region $R$ has cardinality $13$.  In the verified finite row it is exactly
$$\begin{aligned}
R=\{&AAA,AGA,AGG,AUA,CUA,CUC,CUG,CUU,\\
&UAA,UAG,UCA,UGA,UUA\}.
\end{aligned}$$
The same row records that this region has the punctured-cube face data
$$\begin{aligned}
(f_0,f_1,f_2)=(13,16,4).
\end{aligned}$$
Thus the chapter concerns a small boundary-like subset of the codon cube, not a whole biological system.  The internal distinctions used here are coordinate position, finite closure, a spectral summary, and relation data among labeled codons.  The biological object remains the curated standard-code table, and the asserted layer is only $\mathrm{code\_read}$.

\paragraph{Scope boundary.}
The supported reading is: window-six codon coordinates expose a finite local tile whose leave-one-code-out behavior is highly concentrated relative to the listed baselines, while the amino-acid and stop labels are supplied by the curated code table.  The table is a real contact for code labels, but it is not a contact for translation realization, structural order, physical admissibility, biological function, mechanism, or universality.  Any future claim crossing one of those boundaries must introduce a separate observation at that layer.  Without such a contact, amino-acid label sequences remain label sequences, not folded proteins, not functions, and not physical realizations.
