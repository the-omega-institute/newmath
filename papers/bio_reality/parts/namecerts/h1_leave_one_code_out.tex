\subsection{Leave-one-code-out recovery on the codon boundary tile}
\label{sec:h1-leave-one-code-out}

\paragraph{Claim packet.}
The packet $\mathrm{h1.leave.one.code.out}$ tests whether a window-six codon coordinate on the codon cube $Q_6$ retains predictive information about standard-code label changes when one code table is withheld.  The mathematical object in the packet is not a model of translation.  It is a finite coordinate-and-label record: codons are displayed as six-bit positions, local faces are inspected as tile neighborhoods, singleton label corners and three-point label classes are separated, and the final readout is compared only with the curated standard-code label table.  The biological contact therefore lives at the code-read layer.  It permits codon-to-label comparisons, and it does not by itself license statements about translation realization, folded structure, physical admissibility, biological function, mechanism, or universality.

\paragraph{Finite carrier and distinguished boundary row.}
The distinguished boundary set $R$ has cardinality $13$.  In codon notation it is
$$
\begin{aligned}
R = \{&\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\\
&\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UUA}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The same row is recorded as a punctured-cube shape with face vector
$$
\begin{aligned}
(f_0,f_1,f_2)=(13,16,4).
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the packet is not using an unrestricted codon list: the active comparison row is the thirteen-codon boundary set displayed above.  The module partition is complete on this set.  The six modules are
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{agr}&=\{\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG}\},\\
\mathrm{ar\_lys}&=\{\mathrm{AAA}\},\\
\mathrm{cun}&=\{\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU}\},\\
\mathrm{ile\_met}&=\{\mathrm{AUA}\},\\
\mathrm{ssn}&=\{\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UUA}\},\\
\mathrm{stop\_trp}&=\{\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UGA}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The module audit reports $R\_\mathrm{size}=13$, $\mathrm{assigned\_count}=13$, no extra module codons, no missing codons, and no unassigned codons.  Every module contains either a predictable codon or a boundary codon under the leave-one-codon diagnostic, so the row is closed as a finite classification of this local tile rather than as a genome-wide biological principle.

\paragraph{Spectral and sampling checks.}
The local geometry is accompanied by scalar witnesses.  The recorded spectral values are
$$
\begin{aligned}
\lambda_M &= 0.6752479308830213,\\
\lambda_R &= 0.4758352843197002,\\
\mu &= 3.051487585298128.
\end{aligned}
$$
The radial cubic residual is $-6.224354365258478\times 10^{-12}$, which is numerically at zero for the finite computation.  The shape comparison uses $N=2000$ sampled controls with seed $42$.  Among $57377$ distinct-sample attempts, $55377$ were duplicate retries, one sample matched the exact observed shape, and $25$ samples reached Jaccard similarity at least $0.9$.  The corresponding probabilities are
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{\mathrm{exact}}&=0.0005,\\
p_{\mathrm{Jaccard}\ge 0.9}&=0.0125.
\end{aligned}
$$
A second null that preserves amino-acid classes also used $N=2000$ and seed $42$.  It found observed $|M|=20$, zero exact shape matches, and $7$ lower-tail samples at or below the observed $M$ size, giving
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{\mathrm{aa\ class,exact}}&=0.0,\\
p_{\mathrm{aa\ class,lower\ tail}}&=0.0035.
\end{aligned}
$$
These numbers support the statement that the recorded codon-tile shape is unusually specific under the tested finite controls.  They do not turn the coordinate geometry into a law of molecular biology.

\paragraph{Leave-one-code-out result.}
The main claim evaluates $23$ code tables and $63$ total targets.  The baseline hit rate is $0.19821739130434785$.  The rate above baseline is $0.9841269841269841$, the hit rate inside $M\setminus T$ is $0.9365079365079365$, and the hit rate inside $M\setminus T$ plus the boundary row is $0.9841269841269841$.  In finite-count terms, the reported rates mean that the boundary-augmented local tile recovers nearly all withheld code-table targets in this data row, while the baseline comparison is near one fifth.  Family-level stratification keeps the same pattern across five recorded families: alternative yeast has hit rate $0.8333333333333334$, while bacterial-plastid, candidate-other, mitochondrial, and nuclear-ciliate families each have hit rate $1.0$ above baseline.

\paragraph{Leave-one-codon companion check.}
The companion leave-one-codon row reports $0$ anchors, $5$ boundary codons, $8$ high-coverage closure codons, and $8$ predictable codons.  Its recovery rate is $1.0$, and the above-threshold recovery rate is also $1.0$.  This is consistent with the leave-one-code-out result: the signal is concentrated in a local codon boundary structure, not in a claimed universal biochemical mechanism.  The module row confirms that the thirteen codons of $R$ are exactly accounted for by the six named modules, and the recovery row says that each module has access to predictable or boundary status.

\paragraph{Reality contact and scope.}
The only external contact used here is the curated standard genetic-code table, treated as a codon-label observation.  It says that codons have amino-acid or stop labels in the standard code.  It does not observe ribosomal translation, tRNA abundance, protein folding, structure, thermodynamic admissibility, organismal function, or evolutionary necessity.  Consequently the packet supports the following bounded conclusion: in the verified finite rows, a window-six local codon tile with the thirteen-codon boundary set $R$ strongly recovers withheld code-label targets across the tested tables and families.  Any stronger biological reading requires a separate observation at the relevant layer before it can be asserted.
