\subsection{Leave-one-code-out codon-window record}
\label{sec:bior-h1-leave-one-code-out}

\paragraph{Claim scope.}
This record concerns a code-read layer claim for the standard-code codon table. The finite carrier is the six-coordinate codon cube $Q_6$, read with a local tile face, singleton label corners, and three-point label classes. The biological contact is only the curated assignment of codons to amino-acid or stop labels in the standard genetic code. The claim is therefore deliberately narrow: window-six coordinates expose a local boundary pattern that predicts many leave-one-code-out targets, while the labels themselves remain empirical inputs supplied by the curated code table.

\paragraph{Distinguished boundary set.}
The finite boundary set $R$ has cardinality $13$. The observed list is
$$
\begin{aligned}
R=\{&AAA, AGA, AGG, AUA, CUA, CUC, CUG, CUU,\\
&UAA, UAG, UCA, UGA, UUA\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The same list is certified as a punctured-cube pattern with face data
$$
\begin{aligned}
(f_0,f_1,f_2)=(13,16,4).
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the window does not merely select thirteen isolated codons; it selects a small cubical boundary object with vertices, edges, and two-dimensional faces visible in the coordinate presentation. This is the finite geometric object used by the leave-one tests below.

\paragraph{Spectral coordinates.}
The numerical spectrum attached to the observed window separates the main tile scale from the boundary scale. The recorded values are
$$
\begin{aligned}
\lambda_M&=0.6752479308830213,\\
\lambda_R&=0.4758352843197002,\\
\mu&=3.051487585298128.
\end{aligned}
$$
The radial cubic residual is $-6.224354365258478\times 10^{-12}$, which is also the recorded value of the cubic-polynomial check. This residual is small enough for the finite-row audit tolerance used in the run, so the spectrum is treated here as a stable numerical descriptor of the observed codon-window shape, not as a biological law.

\paragraph{Shape significance.}
Against the sampled null comparison with seed $42$, the observed $M$ shape was matched exactly once in $N=2000$ trials. The exact-shape probability estimate is
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{\mathrm{exact}}=0.0005.
\end{aligned}
$$
High-overlap matches with Jaccard score at least $0.9$ occurred $25$ times, giving
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{J\geq 0.9}=0.0125.
\end{aligned}
$$
The sampler needed $57377$ distinct sample attempts and recorded $55377$ duplicate retries, so the finite comparison is not being described as a uniform analytic theorem. It is a reproducible finite-row experiment whose outcome says that the observed codon-window shape is rare under this sampled comparison.

\paragraph{Amino-acid-class-preserving null.}
A second null model preserved amino-acid class information. In that run, $N=2000$, the observed $M$ size was $20$, the exact-shape count was $0$, and the lower-tail count at or below the observed $M$ size was $7$. The finite estimates are
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{\mathrm{class\ exact}}&=0.0,\\
p_{\mathrm{class\ lower\ tail}}&=0.0035.
\end{aligned}
$$
This strengthens the local reading: even after preserving amino-acid-class information, the observed tile shape is not a common sampled outcome. The record still remains at code-read level, because class preservation and codon-label assignment do not observe translation dynamics, folded structure, physical feasibility, biochemical function, or a universal mechanism.

\paragraph{Leave-one-code-out performance.}
The direct leave-one-code-out run evaluated $23$ code tables and $63$ total targets. The baseline hit rate was $0.19821739130434785$. The observed rate above baseline was
$$
\begin{aligned}
0.9841269841269841,
\end{aligned}
$$
with hit rate $0.9365079365079365$ inside $M\setminus T$ and $0.9841269841269841$ inside $M\setminus T$ plus the boundary allowance. Thus almost every held-out code target that is recoverable by this finite procedure lies either in the main window difference or in the local boundary extension. The claim is not that codon biology is generated by the six-coordinate presentation; it is that this presentation gives a compact finite predictor for this particular code-read task.

\paragraph{Family split.}
The family-level audit grouped the evaluated tables into $5$ families. The hit-rate-above-baseline values were $1.0$ for the bacterial-plastid, candidate-other, mitochondrial, and nuclear-ciliate families, and $0.8333333333333334$ for the alternative-yeast family. This family split matters because it prevents the aggregate value $0.9841269841269841$ from hiding a single-family artifact. The weaker alternative-yeast row is retained in the record rather than smoothed away; it marks where the finite pattern is less complete.

The associated leave-one-codon-out audit gives a recovery rate of $1.0$. It records $8$ predictable codons, $5$ boundary codons, $0$ anchor codons, and closure coverage $8$ at the high setting. The module classification accounts for all thirteen $R$ codons with no missing, extra, or unassigned codons. The modules are
$$
\begin{aligned}

ar\_lys&=\{AAA\},\\
cun&=\{CUA,CUC,CUG,CUU\},\\

The classification row reports $R_{\mathrm{size}}=13$ and assigned count $13$, with empty extra-module, missing-from-module, and unassigned lists. Every module therefore has a predictable or boundary interpretation inside the finite code-read window.

\paragraph{Reality contact and stopping rule.}
The only reality contact used here is the curated standard genetic-code table as a codon-label source. It supports codon-to-label readback and local split checks. It does not observe ribosomal realization, protein folding, structural order, physical admissibility, biological function, or universality across possible mechanisms. Consequently, no translation claim, folded-structure claim, physical claim, functional claim, mechanistic claim, or universal-law claim follows from this chapter. Any such promotion would require an additional contact that directly measures the promoted layer. In the present record, the correct conclusion is finite and local: the six-coordinate codon window captures a rare, reproducible boundary-tile pattern whose leave-one-code-out recovery is far above the recorded baseline, while the biological interpretation stops at curated code-label readback.
