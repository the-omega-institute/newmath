\subsection{Leave-One-Code-Out Recovery in the Window-Six Codon Tile}
\label{sec:h1-leave-one-code-out}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Claim.}
The claim \texttt{h1.leave.one.code.out} tests whether a six-coordinate codon display can recover held-out code-table changes at the label-reading layer. The carrier is the $Q_6$ codon cube, with each codon represented by a six-bit window coordinate and then read against a curated standard-code table. The point of the test is deliberately narrow: it asks whether local tile membership, singleton label corners, and three-point label classes organize the observed label table strongly enough to predict leave-one-code-out targets. It does not assert that the tile is a biological mechanism, a translation process, a folding model, a physical admissibility test, a functional assay, or a universal law.

\paragraph{Finite codon set.}
The distinguished boundary set $R$ has cardinality $13$. In standard codon notation it is
$$
\begin{aligned}
R=\{&\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\\
&\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UUA}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The same finite row reports that $R$ is a punctured cube with face-vector
$$
\begin{aligned}
(f_0,f_1,f_2)=(13,16,4).
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the local object is not merely a list of exceptional labels. It has a small cubical shape with $13$ vertices, $16$ one-dimensional adjacencies, and $4$ two-dimensional faces after the puncture. The module classification accounts for all $13$ codons and leaves no unassigned residue: the modules are $\mathrm{agr}=\{\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG}\}$, $\mathrm{ar\_lys}=\{\mathrm{AAA}\}$, $\mathrm{cun}=\{\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU}\}$, $\mathrm{ile\_met}=\{\mathrm{AUA}\}$, $\mathrm{ssn}=\{\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UUA}\}$, and $\mathrm{stop\_trp}=\{\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UGA}\}$. The row reports $R$-size $13$, assigned count $13$, no extra module codons, no codons missing from the modules, and no unassigned codons.

\paragraph{Spectral row.}
The spectral summary gives
$$
\begin{aligned}
\lambda_M &= 0.6752479308830213,\\
\lambda_R &= 0.4758352843197002,\\
\mu &= 3.051487585298128.
\end{aligned}
$$
The recorded cubic residual is $-6.224354365258478\times 10^{-12}$, the same value reported for the radial polynomial residual. At this numerical tolerance the local codon object is therefore internally coherent as a finite coordinate-and-relation object. The spectral numbers do not by themselves identify a biochemical cause; they only record the shape and relation data used by the code-label recovery test.

\paragraph{Shape controls.}
The shape-significance row used $N=2000$ sampled controls with seed $42$. The sampling process required $57377$ distinct-sample attempts and recorded $55377$ duplicate retries. Among the $2000$ controls there was $1$ exact shape match, giving
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{\mathrm{exact}}=0.0005.
\end{aligned}
$$
There were $25$ controls with Jaccard similarity at least $0.9$, giving
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{J\geq 0.9}=0.0125.
\end{aligned}
$$
A separate amino-acid-class-preserving null model used $N=2000$ controls with the same seed. It reported $0$ exact matches, $7$ lower-tail controls at or below the observed $M$-size, observed $M$-size $20$, and
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{\mathrm{class,exact}}&=0.0,\\
p_{\mathrm{class,lower\ tail}}&=0.0035.
\end{aligned}
$$
These controls support the claim that the observed window-six organization is not a routine outcome of the tested randomizations. They still remain code-layer controls: they compare codon-label arrangements, not ribosomal realization, structural dynamics, thermodynamics, or organismal function.

\paragraph{Leave-one-code-out row.}
The direct leave-one-code-out experiment evaluated $23$ code tables and $63$ total targets. Its baseline hit rate was $0.19821739130434785$. The window-six recovery rate above that baseline was $0.9841269841269841$. The recovery rate inside $M\setminus T$ was $0.9365079365079365$, and the recovery rate inside $M\setminus T$ plus the recorded boundary was again $0.9841269841269841$. In compact form,
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{baseline} &= 0.19821739130434785,\\
\mathrm{hit}(>\mathrm{baseline}) &= 0.9841269841269841,\\
\mathrm{hit}(M\setminus T) &= 0.9365079365079365,\\
\mathrm{hit}((M\setminus T)\cup \partial) &= 0.9841269841269841.
\end{aligned}
$$
The gap between $0.19821739130434785$ and $0.9841269841269841$ is the finite-row evidence for this claim. It says that the local coordinate packet is highly informative for held-out code-table label changes under the tested protocol.

\paragraph{Family resolution.}
The family-level row partitions the leave-one-code-out evidence into $5$ families. The hit rate above baseline is $0.8333333333333334$ for alternative yeast and $1.0$ for bacterial/plastid, candidate other, mitochondrial, and nuclear ciliate families. This is important because the aggregate score is not carried by a single undifferentiated table pool. The family row shows that the recovery signal is visible across the named code-table families, with alternative yeast the only listed family below perfect recovery in this summary.

\paragraph{Codon-level companion row.}
The related leave-one-codon-out row reports anchor count $0$, boundary count $5$, closure coverage high $8$, predictable count $8$, and recovery rate $1.0$. This companion row is not the same test as leave-one-code-out, but it confirms that the local packet separates predictable codons and boundary codons without requiring hidden anchor codons. Its role here is corroborative: the code-table recovery row and the codon-level row point to the same local boundary phenomenon inside the six-window display.

\paragraph{Reality contact and boundary.}
The biological contact used here is the curated standard genetic-code table: it supplies codon-to-label assignments, including amino-acid and stop labels. That contact is sufficient for code-label readback and for testing local codon-tile splits. It is not a contact with translation realization, folded protein structure, physical admissibility, biological function, or a universal biological mechanism. Consequently, the valid conclusion is restricted to code-layer organization: the six-window codon geometry strongly recovers held-out code-table label targets in the finite rows above. Any claim about translation, folding, admissible molecular structure, functional effect, mechanism, or universality would require a separate observation channel designed for that layer.
