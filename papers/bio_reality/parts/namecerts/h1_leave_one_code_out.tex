\paragraph{Reality contact and local boundary.}
The only contact used here is the curated standard-code table, which assigns codons to amino-acid or stop labels.  That contact is adequate for checking codon-label readback and local label splitting.  It is not a measurement of translation realization, protein conformation, physical admissibility, biological function, or a universal mechanism.  The local boundary test therefore has the following scope: it may support the claim that the window-six codon coordinates expose a finite boundary-tile pattern in the curated code labels, but it must remain silent about higher biological layers until a separate experiment or dataset is attached for those layers.

Amino-acid label readback is especially not folded structure.  A sequence of labels obtained from a code table has no geometric conformation, no energetic admissibility, and no functional assay in this packet.  The cross-organism and tRNA-Leu wording mentioned by the mismatch records also remains outside the present code-layer contact unless it is routed through its own organism-matched evidence.  The present chapter therefore records a finite codon-label pattern with strong leave-one-out recovery, while keeping the biological interpretation confined to the layer actually tested.
