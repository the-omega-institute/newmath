\subsection{Leave-one-code-out recovery on the codon cube}\label{sec:bioreality-h1-leave-one-code-out}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite codon display.}
The object considered here is the standard-code codon table viewed only at the code-read layer.  The finite carrier is the codon cube $Q_6$: each codon is represented by a six-coordinate binary display, and the observable labels are the curated amino-acid or stop labels attached to the codons by the standard genetic code.  The chapter does not treat the table as a mechanism of translation.  It treats it as a finite label table against which local coordinate structure can be checked.

The distinguished recovery set $R$ has cardinality $13$ and is exactly
$$
\begin{aligned}
R=\{&AAA, AGA, AGG, AUA, CUA, CUC, CUG, CUU,\\
&UAA, UAG, UCA, UGA, UUA\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The same finite row identifies this set as a punctured-cube pattern with face-vector data
$$
\begin{aligned}
f(R)=(13,16,4).
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the local object is not an arbitrary list of exceptional codons: it is a small codon subcomplex with $13$ vertices, $16$ one-dimensional incidences, and $4$ two-dimensional faces in the chosen coordinate presentation.

\paragraph{Spectral and shape checks.}
The measured spectral row gives three scalar quantities attached to the split:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\lambda_M&=0.6752479308830213,\\
\lambda_R&=0.4758352843197002,\\
\mu&=3.051487585298128.
\end{aligned}
$$
The reported radial polynomial residual is $-6.224354365258478\times 10^{-12}$, the same value recorded for the cubic-polynomial check.  At the level of this finite computation, the residual is effectively zero relative to the stated tolerance row, and the spectral radius entry repeats $0.6752479308830213$.

The shape comparison against random alternatives used $N=2000$ samples with seed $42$.  The exact shape was seen once, giving
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{\mathrm{exact}}=0.0005.
\end{aligned}
$$
There were $25$ samples with Jaccard similarity at least $0.9$, giving
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{J\geq 0.9}=0.0125.
\end{aligned}
$$
The sampler records $57377$ distinct-sample attempts and $55377$ duplicate retries, so the finite row is also reporting that the tested space contains many repeated proposals under the sampling procedure.  A separate amino-acid-class-preserving null test used $N=2000$ and seed $42$.  In that row the observed $M$-size is $20$, the exact shape count is $0$, and the lower-tail count at or below the observed $M$-size is $7$, giving
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{\mathrm{aa\ class,exact}}&=0.0,\\
p_{\mathrm{aa\ class,lower\ tail}}&=0.0035.
\end{aligned}
$$
These numbers support the narrower statement that the displayed local shape is uncommon under the recorded finite null models.  They do not, by themselves, identify a biochemical cause.

\paragraph{Leave-one-code-out test.}
The leave-one-code-out row evaluates $23$ code tables and $63$ total targets.  The baseline hit rate is
$$
\begin{aligned}
0.19821739130434785.
\end{aligned}
$$
The recovery rate above that baseline is
$$
\begin{aligned}
0.9841269841269841.
\end{aligned}
$$
The hit rate inside $M-T$ is $0.9365079365079365$, while the rate inside $M-T$ plus the recorded boundary is $0.9841269841269841$.  The boundary therefore accounts for nearly all of the recoveries missed by the stricter $M-T$ test.  In finite-row language, the useful claim is local: when one code table is withheld, the window-six tile and its boundary recover almost all of the withheld label targets relative to the baseline.

The family breakdown has $5$ families.  The hit rates above baseline are
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{alternative\ yeast}&:0.8333333333333334,\\
\mathrm{bacterial\ plastid}&:1.0,\\
\mathrm{candidate\ other}&:1.0,\\
\mathrm{mitochondrial}&:1.0,\\
\mathrm{nuclear\ ciliate}&:1.0.
\end{aligned}
$$
The weaker family is the alternative-yeast family, but it still remains far above the baseline row.  The other four family classes are saturated at $1.0$ for this recorded test.

\paragraph{Leave-one-codon-out module structure.}
The companion leave-one-codon-out row reports $0$ anchors, $5$ boundary codons, $8$ predictable codons, $8$ high-coverage closure cases, and recovery rate $1.0$.  The module classification assigns every codon in $R$ and records no missing or extra codons.  The six modules are
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{agr}&=\{AGA,AGG\},\\
\mathrm{ar\_lys}&=\{AAA\},\\
\mathrm{cun}&=\{CUA,CUC,CUG,CUU\},\\
\mathrm{ile\_met}&=\{AUA\},\\
\mathrm{ssn}&=\{UCA,UUA\},\\
\mathrm{stop\_trp}&=\{UAA,UAG,UGA\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The module audit gives $R_{\mathrm{size}}=13$, assigned count $13$, empty extra-module codons, empty missing-from-modules codons, and empty unassigned codons.  Consequently the finite classification is closed over exactly the observed $R$ set.  Each module has a predictable member or a boundary member, so the local tile is not merely a list-level coincidence; it is organized by the same boundary/predictable split used by the leave-one-codon-out row.

\paragraph{Reality contact and boundary of interpretation.}
The only empirical contact used here is the curated standard-code table: a finite assignment of codons to amino-acid or stop labels.  That contact is sufficient for code-layer readback and for checking whether a local codon-tile split matches those labels.  It is not a measurement of translation dynamics, ribosome operation, folded structure, physical admissibility, biological function, or a universal biological mechanism.  Amino-acid label recovery is still label recovery.  It is not protein realization and it is not structural order.

The supported conclusion is therefore deliberately narrow.  Window-six codon coordinates expose a rare local boundary-tile pattern in the standard-code label table, and leave-one-code-out tests recover withheld code labels at $0.9841269841269841$ above-baseline rate across $23$ evaluated tables and $63$ targets.  The finite evidence supports a code-read claim about local codon geometry and curated labels.  Any claim about translation, folding, physical feasibility, function, mechanism, or universality requires an additional contact of the appropriate kind before it can be asserted.
