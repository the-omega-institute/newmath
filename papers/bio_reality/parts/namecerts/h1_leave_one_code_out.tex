\subsection{Leave-One-Code-Out Stability of the Window-Six Codon Tile}
\label{sec:h1-leave-one-code-out}

\paragraph{Finite codon display.}
This record treats the standard-code codon table as a finite code-read contact on the six-bit codon cube $Q_6$.  The display is not a claim about the biochemical realization of translation.  It is a local coordinate test: codons are placed in the window-six cube, the curated table supplies amino-acid or stop labels, and the resulting label pattern is asked whether it has a stable boundary-tile signature under held-out genetic-code rows.  The distinguished local set is the thirteen-codon region
$$
\begin{aligned}
R=\{&AAA, AGA, AGG, AUA, CUA, CUC, CUG, CUU,\\
&UAA, UAG, UCA, UGA, UUA\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The row audit verifies $|R|=13$ and identifies the same thirteen codons as a punctured cube with $f$-vector $(13,16,4)$.  This is the finite object whose local face structure is being read against labels.  In the present terminology, the relevant distinctions are the six-bit codon display, a local tile face, a singleton label corner, and a three-point label class; the internal coordinates are coordinate, closure, spectrum, and relation data over the cube.

\paragraph{Spectral and shape checks.}
The same finite construction has a measured spectral profile.  The recorded values are $\lambda_M=0.6752479308830213$, $\lambda_R=0.4758352843197002$, and $\mu=3.051487585298128$.  The radial cubic check is numerically zero at the recorded tolerance, with value $-6.224354365258478\times 10^{-12}$, and the spectral-radius entry repeats $0.6752479308830213$.  These numbers are not used as a physical spectrum; they are a finite diagnostic of the codon tile.  Against $N=2000$ sampled alternatives with seed $42$, the exact shape match count is $1$, giving $p_{\mathrm{exact}}=0.0005$.  The high-overlap count at Jaccard threshold $0.9$ is $25$, giving $p_{\mathrm{Jaccard}\ge 0.9}=0.0125$.  The sampler required $57377$ distinct sample attempts and encountered $55377$ duplicate retries, so the result should be read as a finite-row Monte Carlo contact with substantial rejection pressure, not as an asymptotic theorem.

\paragraph{Leave-one-code-out result.}
The leave-one-code-out row evaluates $23$ genetic-code tables and $63$ held-out targets.  The naive baseline hit rate is $0.19821739130434785$.  The tile method records a hit rate above baseline of $0.9841269841269841$, which is $62$ of $63$ targets at this resolution.  The stricter interior count inside $M\setminus T$ is $0.9365079365079365$, namely $59$ of $63$, and adding the boundary recovers the same $0.9841269841269841$ rate.  Thus the finite observation is not merely that the standard code can be re-described after the fact.  The held-out table rows remain almost entirely captured by the local tile-plus-boundary pattern while the baseline row is near $0.1982$.

\paragraph{Amino-acid-class preserving null.}
A separate null keeps amino-acid class information while randomizing the relevant shape.  With $N=2000$ and seed $42$, the exact-match count is $0$, so the recorded exact-shape value is $p=0.0$.  The lower-tail count at or below the observed $M$ size is $7$, with observed $|M|=20$, giving $p_{\mathrm{lower\ tail}}=0.0035$.  This is an important control because it says that preserving a coarse amino-acid class partition alone did not reproduce the same tile shape in the finite run.  It supports the code-read claim that the window-six geometry is detecting a local label organization rather than only restating class preservation.

\paragraph{Module classification.}
The thirteen codons in $R$ decompose completely into six named modules, with no missing, extra, or unassigned codons.  The module audit records $R$ size $13$, assigned count $13$, extra-module codons $\emptyset$, missing-from-modules $\emptyset$, and unassigned codons $\emptyset$.  The modules are
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{agr}&=\{AGA,AGG\},\\
\mathrm{ar\_lys}&=\{AAA\},\\
\mathrm{cun}&=\{CUA,CUC,CUG,CUU\},\\
\mathrm{ile\_met}&=\{AUA\},\\
\mathrm{ssn}&=\{UCA,UUA\},\\
\mathrm{stop\_trp}&=\{UAA,UAG,UGA\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
Every module is marked as predictable or boundary-supported.  The companion leave-one-codon-out row has anchor count $0$, boundary count $5$, closure coverage high $8$, predictable count $8$, and recovery rate $1.0$.  This indicates that the local pattern is not concentrated in a single anchor codon; it is carried by closure and boundary behavior across the thirteen-codon tile.

\paragraph{Family-level held-out behavior.}
The family-level audit groups $23$ evaluated tables into $5$ families.  The hit-rate-above-baseline values are $0.8333333333333334$ for alternative yeast, $1.0$ for bacterial and plastid, $1.0$ for candidate other, $1.0$ for mitochondrial, and $1.0$ for nuclear ciliate.  The only family-level drop below full recovery is therefore the alternative-yeast group, while the remaining four families are complete at the recorded resolution.  This is consistent with a robust local code-read signal, not with a universal biological principle.

\paragraph{Reality contact and scope.}
The biological contact used here is the curated standard genetic-code label table.  It supplies codon-to-label assignments and no more.  It can test code-layer readback and local codon-tile label splits.  It cannot test translation realization, folded structure, structural order, physical admissibility, biological function, or a universal mechanism.  Amino-acid label sequence is therefore not treated as protein structure.  Likewise, a local tile in $Q_6$ is not promoted to a law of molecular biology.  Any claim about translation, folding, admissibility, function, mechanism, or universality would require a separate empirical contact designed for that layer.

\paragraph{Claim verdict.}
For claim $h1.leave.one.code.out$, the finite-row evidence is ready at the code-read layer.  The evidence consists of the thirteen-codon punctured-cube region, the verified spectral scalars, the shape-significance and amino-acid-class-preserving null rows, the complete six-module classification, the $23$-table and $63$-target leave-one-code-out row, and the family-level hit-rate audit.  The accepted statement is narrow: window-six codon coordinates expose a local boundary-tile organization in curated code labels.  The accepted statement does not assert translation, folding, physical admissibility, function, mechanism, or universality.
