\subsection{Leave-One-Code-Out Readback for a Window-Six Codon Tile}\label{subsec:h1-leave-one-code-out}\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite object and readback level.}
This record concerns the standard-code codon table viewed only at the code-read layer.  The finite carrier is the six-coordinate codon cube $Q_6$, whose displayed vertices are codons and whose labels are amino-acid or stop labels supplied by the curated standard-code table.  The construction uses four local distinctions: the six-bit codon display, a local tile face, a singleton label corner, and a three-point label class.  The readback is therefore a statement about label placement on a finite codon table, not a statement about translation chemistry, folded protein structure, physical admissibility, biological function, mechanism, or universality.

\paragraph{Distinguished residual set.}
The finite residual set has cardinality $13$.  In codon notation it is
$$
\begin{aligned}
R=\{&\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\\
&\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UUA}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The same run verifies that this set is the punctured-cube residual with face-vector
$$
\begin{aligned}
(f_0,f_1,f_2)=(13,16,4).
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the row is not merely recording thirteen named codons; it records a finite local shape inside the codon cube, with $13$ vertices, $16$ one-dimensional incidences, and $4$ two-dimensional faces after the relevant puncture.

\paragraph{Shape significance under randomization.}
Against the sampled shape null, the observed exact shape appeared once in $2000$ sampled tables, giving
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{\mathrm{exact}}&=0.0005.
\end{aligned}
$$
The high-overlap event with Jaccard score at least $0.9$ occurred $25$ times in $2000$ samples, giving
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{J\geq 0.9}&=0.0125.
\end{aligned}
$$
The sampling trace also records $57377$ distinct-sample attempts and $55377$ duplicate retries at seed $42$.  Under the amino-acid-class-preserving null, the observed local mass has size $20$, the exact-shape count is $0$ among $2000$ samples, and the lower-tail count at or below the observed $M$-size is $7$, so
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{\mathrm{aa\ class\ exact}}&=0.0,\\
p_{\mathrm{aa\ class\ lower\ tail}}&=0.0035.
\end{aligned}
$$
The finite evidence therefore says that the observed local codon-window shape is rare under these two specified null procedures.  It does not say that the codon table is forced by geometry alone.

\paragraph{Leave-one-code-out test.}
The leave-one-code-out run evaluated $23$ genetic-code tables and $63$ held-out targets.  Its baseline hit rate was
$$
\begin{aligned}
0.19821739130434785.
\end{aligned}
$$
The local prediction rate above that baseline was
$$
\begin{aligned}
0.9841269841269841,
\end{aligned}
$$
while the rate inside $M-T$ alone was
$$
\begin{aligned}
0.9365079365079365.
\end{aligned}
$$
After adding the boundary contribution, the rate in $M-T$ plus boundary again becomes
$$
\begin{aligned}
0.9841269841269841.
\end{aligned}
$$
This is the central finite-row content of the claim: when code tables are held out one at a time, the local codon-window structure recovers most of the target placements relative to the stated baseline, and the boundary term accounts for the remaining local recoveries measured by the row.

\paragraph{Local boundary of the claim.}
The evidence supports the following restricted conclusion.  In a six-bit codon display, the standard-code labels expose a rare local residual tile, a complete thirteen-codon module accounting, and strong leave-one-code-out recovery across the finite table sample.  The curated table is the contact that supplies codon-to-label assignments.  It supplies no observation of translation dynamics, no fold, no physical admissibility test, no function assay, no mechanism, and no universal biological law.  Any movement from code-read recovery to those higher biological layers would require a separate contact carrying that layer directly.  Without such a contact, amino-acid label readback remains label readback.
