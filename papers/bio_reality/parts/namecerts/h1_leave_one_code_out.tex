\subsection{Leave-one-code-out recovery in the window-six codon cube}
\label{sec:bioreality-h1-leave-one-code-out}

\paragraph{Object and scope.}
The object in this record is the standard-code codon table read at the code-label layer.  Codons are placed in the six-coordinate codon cube $Q_6$, where the coordinate display records a finite local geometry before any biological mechanism is asserted.  The readback used here is only the curated assignment from codon strings to amino-acid or stop labels.  Thus the tested datum is a code table, not a translation apparatus, not a folded molecule, not a physical admissibility test, not a functional assay, and not a universal biological principle.  The local tile may expose a boundary pattern in the codon-label table, but the contact is exhausted once codon labels have been read.

\paragraph{Finite carrier and distinguished boundary set.}
The finite row set singled out by the coordinate computation is the $13$-element set
$$
\begin{aligned}
R = \{&\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\\
&\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UUA}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The cardinality check gives $|R|=13$.  The same rows are reported by the punctured-cube computation, whose face-count summary is
$$
\begin{aligned}
(f_0,f_1,f_2)=(13,16,4).
\end{aligned}
$$
This is the local geometric substrate used by the recovery test.  It contains a singleton-label corner component, a three-point label class, and a local tile face structure, all expressed as finite distinctions inside the codon cube.  These distinctions are not promoted to organism-level necessity; they are only the coordinates on which the code-label experiment is evaluated.

$$
\begin{aligned}

The finite data therefore support a non-generic local code-label shape under these two comparison protocols.  The support remains at the table layer: it says that the row pattern is rare under the stated row-level nulls, not that the table is forced by translation physics or by protein function.

\paragraph{Leave-one-code-out recovery.}
The main leave-one-code-out row evaluates $23$ code tables and $63$ held-out targets.  The baseline hit rate is $0.19821739130434785$.  The coordinate recovery rate above that baseline is $0.9841269841269841$.  The hit rate inside $M-T$ is $0.9365079365079365$, while the hit rate inside $M-T$ together with the boundary rows is $0.9841269841269841$.  Equivalently, the boundary-augmented local tile recovers almost all held-out code-label targets in this finite experiment:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{hit}_{\mathrm{baseline}} &= 0.19821739130434785,\\
\mathrm{hit}_{M-T} &= 0.9365079365079365,\\
\mathrm{hit}_{M-T+\partial} &= 0.9841269841269841.
\end{aligned}
$$
This is the precise empirical content of the claim.  The calculation tests whether local codon-cube rows and their boundary neighborhood help recover held-out code-label entries.  It does not test whether those labels are translated in vivo, whether a peptide folds, whether a molecular state is physically admissible, or whether an organism realizes a function.

\paragraph{Reality contact and negative boundary.}
The only external contact used here is the curated standard genetic-code table, understood as a codon-to-label bridge.  It supplies the observed fact that codons are assigned amino-acid or stop labels in the standard table.  It does not contain translation realization, structural order, physical admissibility, functional realization, or a general mechanism.  Therefore an amino-acid label sequence read from this table is not a folded protein structure.  Cross-organism wording, CUN/UUR notation, or tRNA-Leu terminology cannot convert the code-label rows into structure evidence.  Any such assertion requires a separate structure or mechanism contact, with its own finite observations and its own null tests.

\paragraph{Conclusion.}
The finite evidence supports the following bounded statement.  In the six-bit codon cube, the standard-code label table contains a $13$-row local boundary set with punctured-cube face vector $(13,16,4)$, a stable spectral row signature, low-probability shape behavior under the stated null comparisons, and high leave-one-code-out recovery across $23$ tables and $63$ targets.  The strongest reported recovery number is $0.9841269841269841$ after adding the boundary rows, compared with the baseline $0.19821739130434785$.  The result is ready as a code-read claim about finite codon-table geometry.  It is not ready, and is not claimed, as a result about translation, folding, physical admissibility, biological function, mechanism, or universality.
