\subsection{Translation-initiation contact inside a direction-aware residual ledger}
\label{sec:bioreality-uorf-kozak-endogenous-te-ribonn}

\paragraph{Finite object.}
The translation-initiation row is treated as a bounded readout contact attached to the direction-aware $B^*_{Q6}$ residual ledger.  The contact itself was evaluated on $n=10982$ rows, with a recorded runtime of $68.392$ seconds.  Its role in this chapter is not to create a mechanism for upstream open reading frames, Kozak context, or ribosome loading; it supplies one externally measured translation-efficiency-facing contact that can be placed beside the finite residual dictionary.  The ledger being read has nine coordinates,
$$
\begin{aligned}
Q_6 = \{&K\_AAA,\ Arg\_AGR,\ Ile\_AUA,\ Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR,\ Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG,\\
&Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY,\ Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG,\ Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY,\ f3\_stress\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
For each organism and each available readout $H$, the finite entry records the absorbed residual energy $A_H$, the coordinate-support cosine $C_H$, the raw directional product $A_H C_H$, and the penalized score
$$
\begin{aligned}
Score(H)=A_H C_H-\lambda DL(H),\qquad \lambda=0.01,\qquad DL(H)=\log_2(1+n\_{columns}).
\end{aligned}
$$
The score is a declared ranking rule.  It is not a biological force, a causal path, or a sufficient explanation.

\paragraph{Observed translation-side scale.}
The measured translation-efficiency mediation panel gives the scale on which the initiation contact should be interpreted.  In the four organisms with measured TE joins, the three-way row counts were $13140$ for \emph{Danio rerio}, $3415$ for \emph{Escherichia coli} K-12 MG1655, $11554$ for \emph{Homo sapiens}, and $5128$ for \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}.  The measured mediated fractions were heterogeneous: yeast had $M^{QTP,meas}=0.4793069656345167$, \emph{E. coli} had $0.04091158340950673$, human had $0.0027966485036786744$, and danio was clipped to $0$ from a raw value $-0.04801486048721136$.  This already prevents a universal translation-initiation reading: a contact may be informative in one measurement regime while being nearly absent or suppressive in another.

\paragraph{Direction-aware ranking.}
The full residual dictionary gives the finite context for the initiation-facing row.  In danio, measured TE was the best ranked readout with
$$
\begin{aligned}
A_H &=0.017594290015070787,\\
C_H &=0.6573753096184687,\\
A_H C_H &=0.011566051846174291,\\
Score &=0.001566051846174291,
\end{aligned}
$$
and it exceeded its dof-matched Bonferroni threshold $0.000719994971498518$ on $13140$ joined rows.  Danio mRNA was close behind with $A_H C_H=0.01073458857797759$ and $Score=0.000734588577977589$.  Human did not have measured TE as a correct-direction leading readout: human measured TE had $A_H=0.002894714925796667$ but $C_H=-0.10813537216189718$, giving $A_H C_H=-0.00031302107580362116$ and $Score=-0.01031302107580362$.  Human complex membership, not measured TE, led the raw single-column ranking with $A_H C_H=0.006566030115340095$ and $Score=-0.0034339698846599055$.  Yeast was weaker still: mRNA led with $A_H C_H=0.0011462622274400686$ and $Score=-0.008853737772559931$, while measured TE had only $A_H C_H=0.00006250444354633724$ and $Score=-0.009937495556453664$.  \emph{E. coli} had no positive selected absorber under the score rule; its mRNA entry had $A_H C_H=0.0015297976209788734$ but $Score=-0.008470202379021127$.

\paragraph{Residual direction versus raw energy.}
The table separates energy capture from coordinate agreement.  Human PTM density illustrates the point: it had a larger raw absorbed energy than human complex membership, $A_H=0.014884825920744861$ versus $0.007527199987780841$, but its direction cosine was only $0.3604440741749213$, producing $A_H C_H=0.0053651472982577525$, below complex membership's $0.006566030115340095$.  Danio measured tRNA-copy tAI similarly had high energy, $A_H=0.02091194580949614$, but weak direction agreement $C_H=0.33399841523960977$, so its directional product was $0.006984556759948309$ and its score was negative.  Thus the initiation-facing translation contact cannot be read from energy alone; the coordinate direction filter is part of the finite readout.

\paragraph{Background codon boundary.}
The same finite program contains an independent codon-boundary signature.  The Window6 boundary $R$ set had $10$ sense codons, and across $15$ computed organisms every directional non-tie was avoided rather than preferred: avoided count $15$, preferred count $0$, zero count $0$, majority fraction $1.0$.  The one-sided avoided binomial probability was $3.0517578125\times 10^{-5}$ and the two-sided value was $6.103515625\times 10^{-5}$.  Among individually significant organisms, $6$ of $6$ were also avoided.  This is a sequence-level abundance-preference signature for the fixed boundary set; it does not convert the initiation row into a translation mechanism or a universal codon law.

\paragraph{Compilation verdict.}
The broader measured dictionary does not compress the residual into a compact universal readout.  The minimal compilation test reported compressibility $0.024368095798914635$ for danio, $0.005717621488177116$ for \emph{E. coli}, $0.02635576736932188$ for human, and $0.13234136547055853$ for yeast, with permutation value $0.000999000999000999$ in each computed organism.  The cross-organism verdict was that the measured low-dimensional dictionary does not strongly compress $P_Q$; the median compressibility was about $0.026$.  In the direction-aware ordered partition, the unexplained fractions remained large: $0.9756319042010854$ in danio, $0.9942823785118229$ in \emph{E. coli}, $0.9798666209022441$ in human, and $0.8676586345294415$ in yeast.  These numbers make the translation-initiation contact useful as a finite empirical row, but not as a closure of the residual.

\paragraph{Local boundary.}
The valid conclusion is narrow.  A $10982$-row uORF/Kozak/endogenous-TE contact can be placed beside the $B^*_{Q6}$ direction-aware ranking ledger, and the surrounding finite tables show that translation-facing readouts sometimes absorb a small, direction-filtered part of the non-translation residual.  The pattern is organism-specific: danio has a positive measured-TE score, human demotes measured TE by wrong direction, yeast leaves measured TE weak, and \emph{E. coli} lacks a scored positive absorber.  No claim about translation realization, folding, physical admissibility, biological function, causal mechanism, shared pathway, organism-independent law, or universal $H$ follows from this row.  Any such stronger statement would require a separate same-layer contact and a statistic designed for that layer, not merely $A_H$, $C_H$, rank, or score from this finite ledger.
