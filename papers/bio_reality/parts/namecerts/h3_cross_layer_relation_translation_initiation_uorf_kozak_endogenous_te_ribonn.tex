\paragraph{Null checks and finite penalties.}
The Bonferroni thresholds show that exceeding a projection null and justifying the penalized score are different tests.  In danio, measured TE exceeded its Bonferroni threshold $0.000719994971498518$, mRNA exceeded $0.000587830414454545$, measured tRNA-copy tAI exceeded $0.0006532601376630386$, and PTM density exceeded $0.0026907130194498644$; nevertheless the description penalty left only measured TE and mRNA with positive scores.  In human, complex membership, localization, measured TE, mRNA, PTM density, and transmembrane count all exceeded their Bonferroni thresholds, but all listed penalized scores were negative.  Yeast localization exceeded its threshold $0.006295217548252396$ and mRNA exceeded $0.0013014749238591248$, yet localization was heavily penalized by its twelve-column description length, giving score $-0.03223607310225219$.  The initiation row must be read under the same policy: an attached initiation contact may be a real finite readout without licensing a mechanism unless it carries the residual in the declared direction and survives the stated null and penalty tests.

\paragraph{Verdict.}
The uORF/Kozak endogenous-TE row is ready as a bounded translation-initiation contact for the direction-aware residual table.  Its direct finite facts are $n=10982$ and runtime $68.392$ seconds.  The broader table says that no single fetchable readout supplies a universal absorber for the modeled-translation-complement residual: the strongest raw products remain near or below $0.012$, the best absorber changes by organism, and the description-length score rejects most apparent absorbers.  The correct conclusion is therefore negative and useful: the residual is organism-specific and largely unexplained by the attached readout panel.  No translation mechanism, folding statement, physical admissibility claim, protein function, causal pathway, phenotype, or universal biological law follows from the initiation row or from the H-ranking table unless a separate layer-matched contact supplies that stronger statistic.
