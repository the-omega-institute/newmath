\subsection{Modeled-tAI complement support in the B*$_{Q6}$ abundance residual}\label{sec:biorality-q6-translation-complement-residual}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Question.}
This record asks whether the part of a protein-abundance signal explained by the nine B*$_{Q6}$ synonymous-residual coordinates is exhausted by the modeled translation-adaptation direction, or whether a residual component remains after the modeled-tAI component is removed.  The tested object is not a biochemical mechanism.  It is a finite projection calculation on organism-matched coding-sequence, tRNA-copy-derived modeled-tAI, and protein-abundance rows.  For each organism, the abundance vector is first residualized against the declared covariates $Z$: amino-acid composition, log CDS length, GC3, and the M-density baseline.  The nine B*$_{Q6}$ coordinates are then fitted to the abundance residual, giving a coordinate-explained abundance component $P_Q$.  The modeled-tAI component $T$ is projected out of $P_Q$, producing $P_{Q\perp T}$, and this complement is read back as a coordinate vector $a_\perp$ in the nine-dimensional B*$_{Q6}$ coordinate system.

\paragraph{Finite construction.}
The computation used the nine coordinates
$K\_AAA$, $Arg\_AGR$, $Ile\_AUA$, $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$, $Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG$, $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$, $Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG$, $Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY$, and $f3\_stress$.  The module classification is $cross\_layer\_relation$, because the calculation relates a codon-coordinate residual basis to modeled translation adaptation and protein abundance, without treating either contact as a structural or functional assay.  All six organism rows used a $24$-column control design with residualized rank $23$.  The joined row counts were $n=14410$ for \textit{Danio rerio}, $n=3739$ for \textit{Escherichia coli} K-12 MG1655, $n=12418$ for \textit{Gallus gallus}, $n=19053$ for \textit{Homo sapiens}, $n=4129$ for \textit{Mycobacterium smegmatis} str. mc2 155, and $n=5815$ for \textit{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}.  The tRNA organism was the same as the CDS organism except for the \textit{E. coli} row, where the CDS label was $escherichia\_coli\_k12\_mg1655$ and the tRNA label was $escherichia\_coli$.

\paragraph{Projection statistic.}
For each organism the calculation reports
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{QP} &= \frac{\|P_Q\|^2}{\|P_{Z\perp}\|^2},\\
d &= \frac{\|P_{Q\perp T}\|^2}{\|P_Q\|^2},\\
m &= 1-d.
\end{aligned}
$$
The finite values were as follows.  For \textit{Danio rerio}, $R^2_{QP}=0.04613529805760884$, $\|P_{Q\perp T}\|^2=1005.3614101653925$, $d=0.874628721049266$, and $m=0.12537127895073397$.  For \textit{E. coli} K-12 MG1655, $R^2_{QP}=0.10767526667768725$, $\|P_{Q\perp T}\|^2=287.00467177017856$, $d=0.6629960038578532$, and $m=0.3370039961421468$.  For \textit{Gallus gallus}, $R^2_{QP}=0.03980793595345331$, $\|P_{Q\perp T}\|^2=695.5324705841906$, $d=0.9400341806302775$, and $m=0.05996581936972245$.  For \textit{Homo sapiens}, $R^2_{QP}=0.04454565117192484$, $\|P_{Q\perp T}\|^2=1262.22376787716$, $d=0.9208949355679455$, and $m=0.07910506443205445$.  For \textit{Mycobacterium smegmatis}, $R^2_{QP}=0.032090091625816136$, $\|P_{Q\perp T}\|^2=164.9629139386272$, $d=0.8972672018746489$, and $m=0.10273279812535108$.  For \textit{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}, $R^2_{QP}=0.29837624987287115$, $\|P_{Q\perp T}\|^2=186.88597748267532$, $d=0.21173219572706703$, and $m=0.788267804272933$.

\paragraph{Coordinate support.}
The complement vector $a_\perp$ was finite and nine-dimensional in every organism, with absolute support fractions summing to $1$ up to floating-point tolerance.  The dominant support coordinates differed by organism.  The \textit{Danio rerio} complement was led by $f3\_stress$, $Arg\_AGR$, $Ile\_AUA$, $K\_AAA$, and $Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG$.  The \textit{E. coli} row was led by $Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY$, $Arg\_AGR$, $Ile\_AUA$, and $f3\_stress$.  The \textit{Gallus gallus} row was led by $Arg\_AGR$, $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$, $Ile\_AUA$, $Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG$, $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$, and $K\_AAA$.  The human row was led by $Arg\_AGR$, $Ile\_AUA$, $Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG$, $K\_AAA$, and $f3\_stress$.  The \textit{M. smegmatis} row was led by $K\_AAA$, $f3\_stress$, $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$, $Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG$, and $Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG$.  The yeast row was led by $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$, $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$, $Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG$, $Arg\_AGR$, $Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY$, and $f3\_stress$.

\paragraph{Mediation comparison.}
The complement proportion $m=1-d$ was also compared with a separate joint-decomposition mediation scalar computed on the same residualized $Q_e,T_e,P_e,Z$ rows.  The match criterion was absolute difference at most $0.02$.  Five of the six organisms did not satisfy that criterion: \textit{Danio rerio} had $m=0.12537127895073397$ versus reference $0.3157053953614641$; \textit{E. coli} had $m=0.3370039961421468$ versus $0.612922252793667$; \textit{Gallus gallus} had $m=0.05996581936972245$ versus $0.1976903964724546$; human had $m=0.07910506443205445$ versus $0.27206289160119157$; and yeast had $m=0.788267804272933$ versus $0.9491551041216973$.  The only within-tolerance case was \textit{M. smegmatis}, with $m=0.10273279812535108$ versus $0.08397602919861291$, absolute difference $0.018756768926738165$.

\paragraph{Related support readouts.}
Several adjacent finite rows constrain how this complement record should be interpreted.  A modeled-tAI B*$_{Q6}$ survival calculation over $40$ organisms reported $R^2_{QT}=0.9003849000036579$, rank $9$ for the residual coordinate matrix, rank $23$ for $Z$, residual degrees of freedom $17$, and a positive-entry threshold $10^{-12}$; this is strong modeled-readout support, but it is still based on tRNA-copy-derived modeled tAI rather than measured ribosome occupancy.  The organism sweep over $41$ modeled-tAI rows gave $R^2_{QT}$ distribution minimum $0.1579271837024072$, first quartile $0.26353954700074694$, median $0.33934845811598124$, mean $0.38466555440501393$, third quartile $0.5143951545826744$, and maximum $0.8213457220900503$.  For yeast abundance across growth conditions, the $S^{QP}$ values were $R^2_{QP}=0.3280825603424665$ in YEPD and $0.3050385538305022$ in SD, with $f3\_stress$ dominant in both and a cross-condition cosine $0.9993775935182967$.  For human tissue abundance, $R^2_{QP}=0.03626117434944674$ in adult B cells, $0.02320896321347296$ in adult heart, and $0.026133456228248052$ in adult liver, with dominant coordinates $Arg\_AGR$, $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$, and $Ile\_AUA$, respectively.

The direction-aware ranking table used the stated regularization $\lambda=0.01$ with $DL_H=\log_2(1+n_{col})$.  In \textit{Danio rerio}, the top ranked candidate was measured translation efficiency with $A_H=0.017594290015070787$, $C_H=0.6573753096184687$, $A_HC_H=0.011566051846174291$, $DL_H=1.0$, score $0.001566051846174291$, Bonferroni threshold $0.000719994971498518$, null $p95=0.0003297458343745959$, and $n=13140$.  The next row was mRNA with score $0.000734588577977589$, Bonferroni threshold $0.000587830414454545$, null $p95=0.00023560090511497663$, and $n=13140$.  In human, complex membership, mRNA, PTM density, transmembrane count, measured TE, and localization exceeded their Bonferroni screens, but all regularized scores were negative under the same penalty.  In yeast, mRNA exceeded its Bonferroni screen with $A_H=0.001897605472160837$, $C_H=0.6040571890503664$, $A_HC_H=0.0011462622274400686$, score $-0.008853737772559931$, threshold $0.0013014749238591248$, null $p95=0.0004745820831535835$, and $n=5128$.  These rows identify plausible auxiliary readouts for the complement, but they do not convert the complement vector into a causal path.

\paragraph{mRNA-stability check.}
A separate yeast mRNA half-life decomposition supplies a useful scale check.  On $n=5085$ joined yeast rows with residual degrees of freedom $5062$, B*$_{Q6}$ explained $R^2_{QP}=0.2761140373962526$ for protein abundance and $R^2_{QM}=0.05179429044975085$ for log mRNA half-life.  Conditioning protein abundance on mRNA half-life reduced the abundance association to $R^2_{QP\mid M}=0.24851799532219632$, so the absorbed part was $0.027596042074056304$ and the mediation fraction was $0.09994436477872035$.  The permutation null mean for this mediation fraction was $0.00019674487584783138$, the null $p95$ was $0.0014571003414947088$, and the right-tail permutation probability was $0.000999000999000999$.  This strengthens the view that some non-modeled-tAI residual directions can be associated with other measured molecular readouts, but the evidence remains observational and dataset-specific.

\paragraph{Verdict.}
The finite-row verdict is positive for the narrow complement-support statement.  In all six organisms, $P_{Q\perp T}$ had finite nonzero norm and a finite coordinate support vector $a_\perp$.  The strongest modeled-tAI removal occurred in yeast, where only $d=0.21173219572706703$ of the B*$_{Q6}$ abundance component remained outside modeled tAI and $m=0.788267804272933$ was aligned with the modeled-tAI direction.  The other five organisms retained larger modeled-tAI-complement fractions, with $d$ ranging from $0.6629960038578532$ to $0.9400341806302775$.  Thus the data support a bounded statement: the B*$_{Q6}$ abundance component is not uniformly exhausted by the modeled-tAI axis, and the residual complement has organism-specific coordinate support.

\paragraph{Local boundary.}
The calculation is a statistical projection and readback.  It does not establish ribosome-mediated translation realization, because the $T$ axis here is modeled tAI rather than measured ribosome profiling or measured elongation.  It does not establish folding, structural order, physical admissibility, molecular function, phenotype, causal mechanism, or a universal biological law.  It also does not permit tRNA gene-copy counts to be read as tRNA abundance, tRNA charging, ribosome occupancy, translation efficiency, or protein output.  Any such stronger statement would require a separate matched measurement contact and its own controlled statistic.  The present chapter only records that, under the stated controls and finite rows, the modeled-tAI complement of the B*$_{Q6}$ abundance component is computable, nonzero, and coordinate-supported.
