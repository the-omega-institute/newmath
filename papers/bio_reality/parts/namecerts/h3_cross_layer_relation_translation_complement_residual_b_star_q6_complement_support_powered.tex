\subsection{Modeled-tAI Complement Support in the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ Residual Panel}
\label{sec:biorreality-b-star-q6-modeled-tai-complement-support}

\paragraph{Finite row object.}
This record studies a six-organism panel in which the nine residual coordinates of $B^{*}_{Q6}$ are first residualized against a covariate block $Z$ and then compared with protein abundance after removing the component aligned with a modeled tAI readout.  The coordinate panel is
$K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $Leu_{CUN/UUR}$, $Leu_{UUA/UUG}$, $Ser_{UCR/AGY}$, $Ser_{UCA/UCG}$, $Thr_{ACR/ACY}$, and $f3$-stress.  For every organism the control matrix has $24$ columns and rank $23$, consisting of an intercept, CDS length, amino-acid composition, GC3, and an $M$-density baseline.  The observed row counts are $14410$ for \emph{Danio rerio}, $3739$ for \emph{Escherichia coli} K-12 MG1655, $12418$ for \emph{Gallus gallus}, $19053$ for \emph{Homo sapiens}, $4129$ for \emph{Mycobacterium smegmatis} str. MC2 155, and $5815$ for \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}.  The tRNA organism labels match the CDS organism labels except for the bacterial row where the tRNA source is recorded as \emph{Escherichia coli}.

\paragraph{Projection definition.}
Let $Q$ denote the residualized nine-column $B^{*}_{Q6}$ design, let $P$ denote residualized protein abundance, and let $T$ denote the modeled tAI readout after the same controls.  The experiment computes the protein-abundance component explained by $Q$, denoted $P_Q$, and then removes the projection of $P_Q$ on the modeled tAI direction.  The remaining vector is read back in the nine-coordinate $Q$ basis as $a_{\perp}$.  The reported scalar pair is the complement fraction $d$ and the modeled-tAI-aligned fraction $m$:
$$
\begin{aligned}
P_{Q,\perp T} &= P_Q - \Pi_T(P_Q),\\
d &= \frac{\|P_{Q,\perp T}\|^2}{\|P_Q\|^2},\\
m &= 1-d.
\end{aligned}
$$
This is a projection and residual-support calculation.  It is not a causal mediation estimate by itself, and it is deliberately separated from direct ribosome profiling, folding, function, and phenotype claims.

\paragraph{Complement magnitudes.}
The strongest modeled-tAI alignment occurs in yeast, where $R^2_{QP}=0.29837624987287115$, $d=0.21173219572706703$, and $m=0.788267804272933$ with $\|P_{Q,\perp T}\|^2=186.88597748267532$.  The other organisms retain larger modeled-tAI complements: \emph{D. rerio} has $R^2_{QP}=0.04613529805760884$, $d=0.874628721049266$, and $m=0.12537127895073397$; \emph{E. coli} has $R^2_{QP}=0.10767526667768725$, $d=0.6629960038578532$, and $m=0.3370039961421468$; \emph{G. gallus} has $R^2_{QP}=0.03980793595345331$, $d=0.9400341806302775$, and $m=0.05996581936972245$; \emph{H. sapiens} has $R^2_{QP}=0.04454565117192484$, $d=0.9208949355679455$, and $m=0.07910506443205445$; and \emph{M. smegmatis} has $R^2_{QP}=0.032090091625816136$, $d=0.8972672018746489$, and $m=0.10273279812535108$.  Thus the panel does not show one uniform relation between $B^{*}_{Q6}$ abundance support and modeled tAI.  Yeast is the row in which most of the $Q$-explained protein-abundance component is aligned with modeled tAI, whereas vertebrate and mycobacterial rows leave most of that component outside the modeled-tAI direction.

\paragraph{Coordinate support.}
The readback vector $a_{\perp}$ is nine-dimensional in every organism, and its absolute support fractions sum to $1.0$ up to floating-point rounding.  The dominant coordinates are organism-specific.  In \emph{D. rerio} they are $f3$-stress, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $K_{AAA}$, and $Leu_{UUA/UUG}$.  In \emph{E. coli} they are $Thr_{ACR/ACY}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, and $f3$-stress.  In chicken they are $Arg_{AGR}$, $Leu_{CUN/UUR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $Leu_{UUA/UUG}$, $Ser_{UCR/AGY}$, and $K_{AAA}$.  In human they are $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $Ser_{UCA/UCG}$, $K_{AAA}$, and $f3$-stress.  In \emph{M. smegmatis} they are $K_{AAA}$, $f3$-stress, $Leu_{CUN/UUR}$, $Leu_{UUA/UUG}$, and $Ser_{UCA/UCG}$.  In yeast they are $Ser_{UCR/AGY}$, $Leu_{CUN/UUR}$, $Ser_{UCA/UCG}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Thr_{ACR/ACY}$, and $f3$-stress.  The finite row therefore supports a coordinate-support statement: after the modeled tAI direction is removed, the remaining abundance-associated component is still concentrated on named $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates, but the coordinate mixture changes by organism.

\paragraph{Mediation comparison.}
The complement fraction $m$ was also compared with a separate joint-decomposition reference computed on the same residualized $Q_e,T_e,P_e,Z$ rows.  Only the \emph{M. smegmatis} row agrees within $0.02$: $m=0.10273279812535108$ versus reference $0.08397602919861291$, with absolute difference $0.018756768926738165$.  The other absolute differences are larger: $0.19033411641073011$ in \emph{D. rerio}, $0.27591825665152014$ in \emph{E. coli}, $0.13772457710273214$ in chicken, $0.19295782716913712$ in human, and $0.16088729984876438$ in yeast.  Consequently the projection complement is a finite descriptive coordinate readback, not an interchangeable proxy for the joint mediation calculation.

\paragraph{Neighboring finite checks.}
The surrounding finite rows place this result in context without promoting it to a higher biological layer.  A direction-aware supplementary-readout ranking used a regularizer $\lambda=0.01$ and $DL_H=\log_2(1+n_{col})$, reporting Bonferroni-filtered readouts such as measured TE and mRNA in zebrafish, complex membership and PTM density in chicken, and multiple significant readouts in human; these rankings were descriptive and did not convert coordinate alignment into a mechanism.  Yeast mRNA half-life mediation on $5085$ joined rows gave $R^2_{QP}=0.2761140373962526$, $R^2_{QP\mid M}=0.24851799532219632$, absorbed $R^2=0.027596042074056304$, mediation fraction $0.09994436477872035$, permutation null mean $0.00019674487584783138$, null $p95=0.0014571003414947088$, and right-tail permutation $p=0.000999000999000999$.  Separate mRNA-stability rows gave $R^2_{QM}=0.05179429044975085$ in yeast with dominant $Leu_{CUN/UUR}$, $R^2_{QM}=0.02298556640056076$ in \emph{E. coli} with dominant $Arg_{AGR}$, and $R^2_{QM}=0.010503670362051522$ in human with dominant $Leu_{CUN/UUR}$.  The yeast half-life join hit rate was $0.9156038419146592$, the \emph{E. coli} half-life join hit rate was $0.9978649586335735$, and the human consensus half-life join hit rate was $0.9779294769799313$.  These values show that related abundance and stability readouts are numerically populated, but they remain distinct tests.

\paragraph{Local boundary.}
The finite result is ready only as a cross-layer residual-support statement: $B^{*}_{Q6}$ coordinates explain a nonzero protein-abundance component under the stated controls, and the modeled-tAI complement of that component has organism-specific support in the same nine-coordinate panel.  It does not by itself establish observed translation efficiency, ribosome occupancy, tRNA abundance, tRNA charging, protein folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, cellular mechanism, phenotype, or any universal biological law.  Such conclusions require separate matched contacts and their own controlled statistics.  In particular, modeled tAI is not ribosome profiling, the coordinate vector $a_{\perp}$ is not a biochemical pathway, and a positive $R^2_{QP}$ or a large complement norm is not evidence for function unless a layer-matched assay supplies that fact.
