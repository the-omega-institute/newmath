\subsection{Modeled-translation complement support in the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ abundance component}
\label{sec:biorality-q6-translation-complement-support}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite object.}
This record tests a bounded cross-layer question about the nine $B^{*}_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates after the amino-acid quotient has already been imposed. The coordinates are $K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $Leu_{CUN/UUR}$, $Leu_{UUA/UUG}$, $Ser_{UCR/AGY}$, $Ser_{UCA/UCG}$, $Thr_{ACR/ACY}$, and $f3$-stress. For each organism, the computation first residualizes both the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ coordinate design and the abundance or modeled-translation readout against the same control block $Z$: an intercept, $\log$ CDS length, the 20 amino-acid composition proportions, GC3, and the $M$-density baseline. In all six organisms of this run the control matrix has 24 columns and rank 23. The joined row counts are 19,053 for human, 14,410 for zebrafish, 12,418 for chicken, 5,815 for yeast, 4,129 for \emph{Mycobacterium smegmatis}, and 3,739 for \emph{E. coli} K-12 MG1655.

\paragraph{Complement statistic.}
Let $Q_e$ denote the residualized $B^{*}_{Q6}$ design, let $P_e$ denote the residualized protein-abundance readout, and let $T_e$ denote the residualized modeled-tAI readout. The run forms the part of $P_e$ explained by $Q_e$, then removes from that part the modeled-translation component. The remaining vector is read back in the nine-dimensional $B^{*}_{Q6}$ coordinate frame:
$$
\begin{aligned}
P_Q &= \operatorname{Proj}_{Q_e}(P_e), \\
P_{Q\perp T} &= P_Q - \operatorname{Proj}_{T_e}(P_Q), \\
a_{\perp} &= \operatorname{Coord}_{Q_e}(P_{Q\perp T}), \\
d &= \frac{\lVert P_{Q\perp T}\rVert^2}{\lVert P_Q\rVert^2}, \qquad m = 1-d.
\end{aligned}
$$
Here $d$ is the modeled-translation-complement fraction of the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ abundance component, and $m$ is the modeled-translation-covered fraction. The statistic is descriptive: it asks how much of the $Q$-explained abundance signal remains after removing the modeled-tAI direction, not whether translation causes abundance.

\paragraph{Observed finite rows.}
The complement values are highly organism-dependent. Yeast has the largest modeled-translation-covered fraction, with $R^2_{QP}=0.29837624987287115$, $d=0.21173219572706703$, $m=0.788267804272933$, and $\lVert P_{Q\perp T}\rVert^2=186.88597748267532$. In \emph{E. coli} K-12 MG1655, the corresponding values are $R^2_{QP}=0.10767526667768725$, $d=0.6629960038578532$, $m=0.3370039961421468$, and $\lVert P_{Q\perp T}\rVert^2=287.00467177017856$. Zebrafish gives $R^2_{QP}=0.04613529805760884$, $d=0.874628721049266$, and $m=0.12537127895073397$. Human gives $R^2_{QP}=0.04454565117192484$, $d=0.9208949355679455$, and $m=0.07910506443205445$. Chicken gives $R^2_{QP}=0.03980793595345331$, $d=0.9400341806302775$, and $m=0.05996581936972245$. \emph{M. smegmatis} gives $R^2_{QP}=0.032090091625816136$, $d=0.8972672018746489$, and $m=0.10273279812535108$. Thus the same nine-coordinate residual basis can leave a small complement in yeast but a large complement in vertebrate and mycobacterial rows.

\paragraph{Coordinate support.}
The non-translation-complement vector is not supported by a single universal coordinate. In yeast the dominant support list is $Ser_{UCR/AGY}$, $Leu_{CUN/UUR}$, $Ser_{UCA/UCG}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Thr_{ACR/ACY}$, and $f3$-stress. In human it is $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $Ser_{UCA/UCG}$, $K_{AAA}$, and $f3$-stress. In zebrafish it is $f3$-stress$, Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $K_{AAA}$, and $Leu_{UUA/UUG}$. In chicken it is $Arg_{AGR}$, $Leu_{CUN/UUR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $Leu_{UUA/UUG}$, $Ser_{UCR/AGY}$, and $K_{AAA}$. In \emph{E. coli} it is $Thr_{ACR/ACY}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, and $f3$-stress. In \emph{M. smegmatis} it is $K_{AAA}$, $f3$-stress, $Leu_{CUN/UUR}$, $Leu_{UUA/UUG}$, and $Ser_{UCA/UCG}$. Each support vector has nine-dimensional readback and absolute support fraction summing to 1.0 up to floating point rounding.

\paragraph{Local verdict.}
The run is ready as a finite cross-layer relation about the modeled-tAI complement of the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ abundance component. It shows that $P_Q$ is finite and nonzero in all six organism rows, that the modeled-translation-covered fraction $m$ varies from 0.05996581936972245 in chicken to 0.788267804272933 in yeast, and that the residual support vector $a_{\perp}$ has organism-specific coordinate content. The record should be read as a controlled projection decomposition over observed codon, modeled-tAI, and protein-abundance tables. It does not by itself establish measured ribosome-mediated translation, protein folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, mechanism, or a universal biological law. Those stronger readings require separate matched contacts and their own controlled statistics.
