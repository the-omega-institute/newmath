\subsection{Modeled-tAI complement support in the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual abundance component}\label{sec:bioreality-q6-translation-complement-residual}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Question.}
This record asks whether the part of protein abundance selected by the nine $B^{*}_{Q6}$ synonymous-residual coordinates is exhausted by the modeled translation-adaptation direction, or whether a remaining component can still be read back in the same nine-coordinate basis.  The computation is deliberately narrower than a biological mechanism claim.  It begins with residualized coding-sequence coordinates $Q$, a modeled tAI translation readout $T$, and a protein-abundance readout $P$, all after the same control family $Z$: amino-acid composition, coding-sequence length, GC3, organism context where applicable, and the $M$-density baseline.  For each organism, the fitted abundance component $P_Q$ is decomposed into the component aligned with modeled tAI and the orthogonal remainder $P_{Q\perp T}$.  The vector $a_{\perp}$ then expresses that remainder back in the nine $B^{*}_{Q6}$ coordinates.

\paragraph{Computed carrier.}
The finite carrier contains six organism rows and nine residual coordinates.  The coordinates are $\texttt{K\_AAA}$, $\texttt{Arg\_AGR}$, $\texttt{Ile\_AUA}$, $\texttt{Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR}$, $\texttt{Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG}$, $\texttt{Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY}$, $\texttt{Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG}$, $\texttt{Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY}$, and $\texttt{f3\_stress}$.  The joined row counts are $14410$ for \emph{Danio rerio}, $3739$ for \emph{Escherichia coli} K-12 MG1655, $12418$ for \emph{Gallus gallus}, $19053$ for \emph{Homo sapiens}, $4129$ for \emph{Mycobacterium smegmatis} str. MC2 155, and $5815$ for \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}.  In every row the control design has $24$ columns and rank $23$.  The tRNA organism label agrees with the coding organism except that the \emph{E. coli} K-12 MG1655 coding row is paired to the broader \emph{Escherichia coli} tRNA label.

\paragraph{Projection statistic.}
For each organism the statistic reports the residual protein-abundance energy explained by $B^{*}_{Q6}$, the norm of the modeled-tAI-complement component, and the split $(d,m)$ between modeled-tAI complement and modeled-tAI-aligned mass.  The finite values are
$$
\begin{aligned}
\textit{Danio rerio}:&\quad R^2_{QP}=0.0461352981,\quad \|P_{Q\perp T}\|^2=1005.3614102,\quad d=0.8746287210,\quad m=0.1253712790,\\
\textit{E. coli}\ \text{K-12}:&\quad R^2_{QP}=0.1076752667,\quad \|P_{Q\perp T}\|^2=287.0046718,\quad d=0.6629960039,\quad m=0.3370039961,\\
\textit{Gallus gallus}:&\quad R^2_{QP}=0.0398079360,\quad \|P_{Q\perp T}\|^2=695.5324706,\quad d=0.9400341806,\quad m=0.0599658194,\\
\textit{Homo sapiens}:&\quad R^2_{QP}=0.0445456512,\quad \|P_{Q\perp T}\|^2=1262.2237679,\quad d=0.9208949356,\quad m=0.0791050644,\\
\textit{M. smegmatis}:&\quad R^2_{QP}=0.0320900916,\quad \|P_{Q\perp T}\|^2=164.9629139,\quad d=0.8972672019,\quad m=0.1027327981,\\
\textit{S. cerevisiae}:&\quad R^2_{QP}=0.2983762499,\quad \|P_{Q\perp T}\|^2=186.8859775,\quad d=0.2117321957,\quad m=0.7882678043.
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the complement is nonzero in all six rows.  The largest complement fractions $d$ occur in \emph{Gallus gallus} and \emph{Homo sapiens}, where more than $92\%$ of the $B^{*}_{Q6}$-explained abundance component is outside the modeled-tAI direction.  The yeast row is the opposite case: $m=0.7882678043$ leaves only $d=0.2117321957$ in the modeled-tAI complement, so yeast is the strongest row for modeled-tAI alignment in this finite panel.

\paragraph{Coordinate support.}
The readback vector $a_{\perp}$ has dimension $9$ in every organism, and the absolute support fractions sum to $1.0$ up to floating-point rounding.  The dominant coordinates are not uniform across organisms.  \emph{Danio rerio} is led by $\texttt{f3\_stress}$, $\texttt{Arg\_AGR}$, $\texttt{Ile\_AUA}$, $\texttt{K\_AAA}$, and $\texttt{Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG}$.  \emph{E. coli} K-12 is led by $\texttt{Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY}$, $\texttt{Arg\_AGR}$, $\texttt{Ile\_AUA}$, and $\texttt{f3\_stress}$.  \emph{Gallus gallus} is led by $\texttt{Arg\_AGR}$, $\texttt{Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR}$, $\texttt{Ile\_AUA}$, $\texttt{Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG}$, $\texttt{Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY}$, and $\texttt{K\_AAA}$.  \emph{Homo sapiens} is led by $\texttt{Arg\_AGR}$, $\texttt{Ile\_AUA}$, $\texttt{Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG}$, $\texttt{K\_AAA}$, and $\texttt{f3\_stress}$.  \emph{M. smegmatis} is led by $\texttt{K\_AAA}$, $\texttt{f3\_stress}$, $\texttt{Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR}$, $\texttt{Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG}$, and $\texttt{Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG}$.  \emph{S. cerevisiae} is led by $\texttt{Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY}$, $\texttt{Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR}$, $\texttt{Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG}$, $\texttt{Arg\_AGR}$, $\texttt{Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY}$, and $\texttt{f3\_stress}$.  This species-specific support pattern is the main positive finite-row content of the record: the modeled-tAI-complement abundance component remains expressible in the residual coordinate system rather than collapsing to numerical zero.

\paragraph{Comparison with the mediation split.}
The same rows were compared to a separate joint-decomposition mediation reference.  Agreement within $0.02$ occurs only for \emph{M. smegmatis}, where $m=0.1027327981$ differs from the reference value $0.0839760292$ by $0.0187567689$.  The other absolute differences are $0.1903341164$ for \emph{Danio rerio}, $0.2759182567$ for \emph{E. coli} K-12, $0.1377245771$ for \emph{Gallus gallus}, $0.1929578272$ for \emph{Homo sapiens}, and $0.1608872998$ for \emph{S. cerevisiae}.  The complement statistic therefore should not be identified with the mediation statistic.  It is a projection readback of the modeled-tAI-complement part of $P_Q$, while the mediation reference answers a different joint-decomposition question.

\paragraph{Relation to modeled translation survival and candidate residuals.}
A companion modeled-tAI survival computation used $40$ organisms, $9$ modeled readout columns, rank $23$ controls, rank $9$ residualized $Q$ coordinates, residual degrees of freedom $17$, and $R^2_{QT}=0.9003849000$ under a positive partial-$R^2$ threshold of $10^{-12}$.  That companion result establishes that the modeled tAI panel is rank-powered for the bounded modeled-readout question, but it remains a modeled readout rather than measured ribosome occupancy or translation efficiency.  A further candidate-readout ranking used the regularizer $\lambda=0.01$ with $DL_H=\log_2(1+n_{\mathrm{col}})$ and reported raw $A_H C_H$ values as well as penalized scores.  In that ranking, \emph{Danio rerio} selected measured TE as $H_o^*$ with score $0.0015660518$ and Bonferroni threshold $0.0007199950$; \emph{Homo sapiens} selected complex membership with score $-0.0034339699$ and threshold $0.0002646171$; \emph{S. cerevisiae} selected mRNA with score $-0.0088537378$ and threshold $0.0013014749$; \emph{Gallus gallus} selected complex membership with score $-0.0057584852$ and threshold $0.0055416579$; \emph{E. coli} K-12 and \emph{M. smegmatis} had no positive selected $H_o^*$ in that table.  These numbers support the interpretation that the complement signal is a real finite-row residual object, but they do not convert it into a single universal downstream module.

\paragraph{Bounded conclusion.}
The claim is supported in the following precise sense.  After the declared controls, each organism has a finite nonzero $P_{Q\perp T}$ component and a dimension-$9$ $a_{\perp}$ coordinate readback, with organism-specific dominant coordinates and reported complement fractions $d$.  This establishes coordinate support for the modeled-tAI-complement part of the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ abundance component.  It does not establish measured translation, ribosome-mediated mechanism, protein folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, phenotype, or a universal biological law.  Those stronger readings require separate layer-matched measurements and their own controlled statistics.  In particular, modeled tAI is not ribosome profiling, tRNA gene copy is not tRNA abundance or charging, and a positive residual projection is not by itself a causal account of protein production.
