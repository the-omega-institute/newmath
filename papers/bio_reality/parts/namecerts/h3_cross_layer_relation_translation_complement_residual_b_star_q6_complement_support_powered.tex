\subsection{Translation-complement residual support for the powered $B^*_{Q6}$ coordinate panel}
\label{sec:h3-cross-layer-relation-translation-complement-residual-b-star-q6-complement-support-powered}

\paragraph{Coordinate panel and residualization design.}
This record concerns the nine-coordinate $B^*_{Q6}$ residual panel
$Q=(q_1,\ldots,q_9)$ with coordinates $K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $Leu_{CUN/UUR}$, $Leu_{UUA/UUG}$, $Ser_{UCR/AGY}$, $Ser_{UCA/UCG}$, $Thr_{ACR/ACY}$, and the $f3$ stress coordinate.  The test is not a direct assertion about a molecular mechanism.  It asks whether the portion of a protein-abundance readout explained by the residual codon panel has a nonzero component orthogonal to the modeled translation readout, after amino-acid composition and sequence-level controls are already removed.  For each organism, the rows were joined at the protein or CDS level, the control matrix had $24$ columns, and the residualized control rank was $23$.  The joined row counts were $19053$ for human, $5815$ for yeast, $3739$ for \textit{E. coli} K-12 MG1655, $14410$ for zebrafish, $12418$ for chicken, and $4129$ for \textit{M. smegmatis} mc2 155.

\paragraph{Projection quantities.}
Let $P_Q$ denote the component of the abundance readout explained by the residualized $B^*_{Q6}$ coordinates, and let $P_{Q\perp T}$ denote the part of that component not accounted for by the modeled-tAI translation coordinate $T$.  The finite-row computation reports the squared norm of $P_{Q\perp T}$, the residual-to-protein coefficient of determination $R^2_{QP}$, and the two fractions $d$ and $m$ for the direct complement and modeled-translation-associated portions.  The observed organism-level values are
$$
\begin{aligned}
\text{human: }&\|P_{Q\perp T}\|^2=1262.22376787716,\quad R^2_{QP}=0.04454565117192484,\quad d=0.9208949355679455,\quad m=0.07910506443205445,\\
\text{yeast: }&\|P_{Q\perp T}\|^2=186.88597748267532,\quad R^2_{QP}=0.29837624987287115,\quad d=0.21173219572706703,\quad m=0.788267804272933,\\
\text{E. coli K-12: }&\|P_{Q\perp T}\|^2=287.00467177017856,\quad R^2_{QP}=0.10767526667768725,\quad d=0.6629960038578532,\quad m=0.3370039961421468,\\
\text{zebrafish: }&\|P_{Q\perp T}\|^2=1005.3614101653925,\quad R^2_{QP}=0.04613529805760884,\quad d=0.874628721049266,\quad m=0.12537127895073397,\\
\text{chicken: }&\|P_{Q\perp T}\|^2=695.5324705841906,\quad R^2_{QP}=0.03980793595345331,\quad d=0.9400341806302775,\quad m=0.05996581936972245,\\
\text{M. smegmatis: }&\|P_{Q\perp T}\|^2=164.9629139386272,\quad R^2_{QP}=0.032090091625816136,\quad d=0.8972672018746489,\quad m=0.10273279812535108.
\end{aligned}
$$
The largest modeled-associated fraction in this complement computation is the yeast value $m=0.788267804272933$, while the smallest is the chicken value $m=0.05996581936972245$.  The same six-organism check compared these $m$ values against a separate joint-decomposition reference.  Only \textit{M. smegmatis} matched within $0.02$, with absolute difference $0.018756768926738165$; the other absolute differences were $0.19033411641073011$ for zebrafish, $0.27591825665152014$ for \textit{E. coli} K-12, $0.13772457710273214$ for chicken, $0.19295782716913712$ for human, and $0.16088729984876438$ for yeast.  Thus the complement readback is finite and structured, but it is not identical to the earlier mediation decomposition except in one organism.

\paragraph{Coordinate support of the complement.}
The complement vector $a_\perp$ was read in the same nine-dimensional residual coordinate system for every organism.  The support fractions summed to $1.0$ in human, yeast, zebrafish, chicken, and \textit{M. smegmatis}, and to $1.0000000000000002$ in \textit{E. coli} K-12, which is numerical roundoff at unit support.  The dominant human coordinates were $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $Ser_{UCA/UCG}$, $K_{AAA}$, and $f3$ stress.  Yeast emphasized $Ser_{UCR/AGY}$, $Leu_{CUN/UUR}$, $Ser_{UCA/UCG}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Thr_{ACR/ACY}$, and $f3$ stress.  \textit{E. coli} K-12 emphasized $Thr_{ACR/ACY}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, and $f3$ stress.  Zebrafish emphasized $f3$ stress, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $K_{AAA}$, and $Leu_{UUA/UUG}$.  Chicken emphasized $Arg_{AGR}$, $Leu_{CUN/UUR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $Leu_{UUA/UUG}$, $Ser_{UCR/AGY}$, and $K_{AAA}$.  \textit{M. smegmatis} emphasized $K_{AAA}$, $f3$ stress, $Leu_{CUN/UUR}$, $Leu_{UUA/UUG}$, and $Ser_{UCA/UCG}$.  This makes the result coordinate-specific rather than a single scalar association hidden behind the panel name.

\paragraph{Relation to the modeled translation readout.}
A separate powered modeled-tAI run used $40$ organisms, a $9\times 9$ modeled readout matrix, residual rank $23$ for the controls, residual degrees of freedom $17$, and rank $9$ for the residualized $B^*_{Q6}$ design.  Its reported $R^2_{QT}$ was $0.9003849000036579$, below the declared degeneracy cutoff $0.999999$, so the modeled translation surface was high-signal but not treated as a rank-saturated identity fit.  Another finite coupling run on human, yeast, and \textit{E. coli} K-12 computed all nine residual coordinates against a proxy tRNA-Leu CUN-versus-UUR log-copy spectrum; the maximum absolute coupling was $1882464.1106377328$, with $9$ nonzero entries above the $0.01$ threshold.  The Leu CUN/UUR organism-level check over the same three organisms gave Pearson $1.0$ and partial Pearson $1.0$ at $n=3$, with log-ratio signs $(+,-,+)$, so the sign was explicitly not uniform across organisms.

\paragraph{Local verdict and boundary.}
The finite-row evidence supports the narrower statement that, after the declared residualization, the modeled-tAI complement of the $B^*_{Q6}$-explained abundance component is nonzero and can be assigned back to named residual coordinates in each of the six organisms tested.  It does not show a measured ribosome-profiling survival matrix, a causal translation mechanism, a protein-folding consequence, physical admissibility, biochemical function, phenotype, or a universal law.  The translation-contact evidence remains bounded: modeled tAI and tRNA-copy contacts are useful readouts for this coordinate-level comparison, but they are not measurements of ribosome occupancy, tRNA abundance, tRNA charging, or protein output unless a separate matched contact supplies those rows.  A stronger survival claim would require an organism-matched matrix of codon counts, direct translation readouts such as TE or ribosome occupancy, matched mRNA controls, and the same $Z$ controls, followed by an explicit partial-$R^2$ or description-length survival computation for each $(q_i,r_j)$ entry.
