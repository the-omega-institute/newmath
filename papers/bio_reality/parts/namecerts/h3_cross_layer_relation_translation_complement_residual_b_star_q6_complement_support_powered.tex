\subsection{Modeled-translation complement support for the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual packet}\label{subsec:b-star-q6-translation-complement-support}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Object of the finite-row calculation.}
This record studies the part of the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ abundance projection that remains after removing the component aligned with a modeled translation readout.  For each organism, the nine residual coordinates
$K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $Leu_{CUN/UUR}$, $Leu_{UUA/UUG}$, $Ser_{UCR/AGY}$, $Ser_{UCA/UCG}$, $Thr_{ACR/ACY}$, and $f3$-stress were residualized against a common control design.  The control block had $24$ columns and rank $23$ in every organism, consisting of an intercept, CDS length, amino-acid composition, $GC3$, and the $M$-density baseline.  The joined row counts were $14410$ for \emph{Danio rerio}, $3739$ for \emph{Escherichia coli} K-12 MG1655, $12418$ for \emph{Gallus gallus}, $19053$ for \emph{Homo sapiens}, $4129$ for \emph{Mycobacterium smegmatis} str. MC2 155, and $5815$ for \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}.  The translation side in this calculation is modeled tAI derived from organism-matched tRNA-copy data; it is not a measured ribosome-profiling contact.

\paragraph{Projection statistic.}
Let $Q_e$ denote the residualized nine-column coordinate matrix, let $T_e$ denote the residualized modeled-tAI readout, and let $P_e$ denote the residualized protein-abundance readout.  The calculation first forms the abundance component $P_Q$ explained by $Q_e$, then removes the modeled-translation component to obtain $P_{Q\perp T}$.  The reported complement fraction is
$$
\begin{aligned}
d &= \frac{\|P_{Q\perp T}\|^2}{\|P_Q\|^2},
& m &= 1-d .
\end{aligned}
$$
The finite rows give the following ordered list $(R^2_{QP},d,m)$: for \emph{D. rerio}, $(0.0461352981,0.8746287210,0.1253712790)$; for \emph{E. coli} K-12 MG1655, $(0.1076752667,0.6629960039,0.3370039961)$; for \emph{G. gallus}, $(0.0398079360,0.9400341806,0.0599658194)$; for \emph{H. sapiens}, $(0.0445456512,0.9208949356,0.0791050644)$; for \emph{M. smegmatis}, $(0.0320900916,0.8972672019,0.1027327981)$; and for \emph{S. cerevisiae}, $(0.2983762499,0.2117321957,0.7882678043)$.  Thus yeast is the exceptional row in which most of the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ abundance projection is aligned with modeled translation, while the vertebrate and mycobacterial rows retain most of that projection in the modeled-translation complement.

\paragraph{Coordinate readback of the complement.}
The complement vector $a_{\perp}$ was read back in the same nine-coordinate basis, with dimension $9$ in every organism and absolute support fractions summing to $1$ up to numerical roundoff.  The dominant support lists were organism-specific.  In \emph{D. rerio} the leading coordinates were $f3$-stress, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $K_{AAA}$, and $Leu_{UUA/UUG}$.  In \emph{E. coli} K-12 MG1655 they were $Thr_{ACR/ACY}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, and $f3$-stress.  In \emph{G. gallus} they were $Arg_{AGR}$, $Leu_{CUN/UUR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $Leu_{UUA/UUG}$, $Ser_{UCR/AGY}$, and $K_{AAA}$.  In \emph{H. sapiens} they were $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $Ser_{UCA/UCG}$, $K_{AAA}$, and $f3$-stress.  In \emph{M. smegmatis} they were $K_{AAA}$, $f3$-stress, $Leu_{CUN/UUR}$, $Leu_{UUA/UUG}$, and $Ser_{UCA/UCG}$.  In \emph{S. cerevisiae} they were $Ser_{UCR/AGY}$, $Leu_{CUN/UUR}$, $Ser_{UCA/UCG}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Thr_{ACR/ACY}$, and $f3$-stress.  The complement is therefore not a single conserved codon-family direction; it is a set of organism-specific residual supports left after the modeled-tAI component is removed.

\paragraph{Comparison with the mediation scalar.}
The complement fraction $m$ is not the same statistic as the joint mediation scalar from the modeled-translation mediation calculation.  The absolute differences between the complement-derived $m$ and the joint-decomposition reference were $0.1903341164$ for \emph{D. rerio}, $0.2759182567$ for \emph{E. coli}, $0.1377245771$ for \emph{G. gallus}, $0.1929578272$ for \emph{H. sapiens}, $0.0187567689$ for \emph{M. smegmatis}, and $0.1608872998$ for \emph{S. cerevisiae}.  Only the \emph{M. smegmatis} row matched within the stated $0.02$ tolerance.  This is the important negative guardrail: the coordinate complement is a projection geometry diagnostic, not a substitute for a mediation estimate.

\paragraph{Supplementary readout context.}
A related direction-aware ranking of candidate supplementary readouts used the score $A_H C_H-\lambda DL_H$ with $\lambda=0.01$ and $DL_H=\log_2(1+n_{col})$.  Its selected top readouts were measured translation efficiency in \emph{D. rerio}, complex membership in \emph{G. gallus} and \emph{H. sapiens}, mRNA in \emph{S. cerevisiae}, and no selected $H^{\ast}$ in \emph{E. coli} or \emph{M. smegmatis}.  The yeast mRNA-stability mediation row gives $R^2_{QP}=0.2761140374$, $R^2_{QP\mid M}=0.2485179953$, absorbed fraction $0.0275960421$, mediation fraction $0.0999443648$, permutation null mean $0.0001967449$, null $95$th percentile $0.0014571003$, and right-tail permutation probability $0.0009990010$ on $5085$ joined rows, with a CDS-to-half-life join hit rate $0.9156038419$.  The separate yeast half-life survival row gives $R^2_{QM}=0.0517942904$ and cosine $0.8040935468$ against the same-join protein-abundance vector.  A local ribosome-occupancy analysis also found that $K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, and $f3$-stress passed the position-bin permutation, positive-support, and $\lambda DL$ gates with $\lambda=0.01$ and $DL=2$ across $5754$ joined genes.  These rows help locate plausible follow-up readouts, but they do not turn the modeled-tAI complement statistic into direct ribosome, mechanism, or function evidence.

\paragraph{Local interpretation.}
The finite result supports a bounded statement: after amino-acid, length, $GC3$, and $M$-density controls, the abundance component associated with $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ has a computable modeled-tAI complement in all six organisms, and that complement can be expressed back in the nine residual coordinates with finite support.  The strongest modeled-translation-aligned row is yeast, with $m=0.7882678043$ and $R^2_{QP}=0.2983762499$; the most translation-complement-heavy rows are chicken and human, with $d=0.9400341806$ and $d=0.9208949356$ respectively.  This establishes an organism-scoped residual-geometry pattern, not a universal biological law.

\paragraph{Boundary of the claim.}
No translation mechanism follows from this chapter by itself.  The $T$ variable is modeled tAI, not ribosome occupancy, elongation speed, or translation efficiency measured by ribosome profiling.  No folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, pathway mechanism, phenotype, or universal directionality follows from the projection, the $a_{\perp}$ support lists, the candidate-readout ranking, or the mRNA-stability comparison.  Any such assertion would require its own matched contact and its own controlled statistic, separate from the modeled-translation complement calculation reported here.
