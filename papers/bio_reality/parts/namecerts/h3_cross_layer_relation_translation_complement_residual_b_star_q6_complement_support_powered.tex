\subsection{Modeled-translation complement of the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ abundance component}\label{sec:bioreality-q6-translation-complement-residual}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Question.}
This record asks a deliberately narrow question.  After the nine $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ synonymous-residual coordinates have been residualized against amino-acid composition, CDS length, GC3, and the $M$-density baseline, and after a modeled tAI readout $T$ has been fitted, how much of the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ protein-abundance component remains outside the modeled-translation direction?  The object is not a claim of causal mediation.  It is the coordinate readback of the modeled-$T$ complement of the abundance component explained by the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual block.

The nine residual coordinates used in the computation are
$$
\begin{aligned}
&K\_AAA,\ Arg\_AGR,\ Ile\_AUA,\ Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR,\ Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG,\\
&Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY,\ Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG,\ Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY,\ f3\_stress .
\end{aligned}
$$
For each organism, the fitted abundance component $P_Q$ is the part of the protein-abundance readout explained by these nine residual coordinates after the declared controls.  The modeled-translation complement $P_{Q\perp T}$ is obtained by removing the fitted modeled-tAI direction from $P_Q$.  The vector $a_{\perp}$ then expresses this remaining component back in the same nine-coordinate basis.  Thus the calculation reports which $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ coordinates carry abundance-associated residual mass that is not absorbed by the modeled tAI projection.

\paragraph{Rows and controls.}
Six organism-specific panels were evaluated.  The joined row counts were $n=19053$ for \emph{Homo sapiens}, $n=14410$ for \emph{Danio rerio}, $n=12418$ for \emph{Gallus gallus}, $n=5815$ for \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}, $n=4129$ for \emph{Mycobacterium smegmatis} str. mc2 155, and $n=3739$ for \emph{Escherichia coli} K-12 MG1655.  In each case the control design had $24$ columns and rank $23$.  The controls included an intercept, log CDS length, the twenty amino-acid composition proportions derived from the CDS codon counts, GC3, and the $M$-density baseline, defined as the fraction of sense codons lying on the union support of the nine projected $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ vectors.

The tRNA organism labels matched the CDS organism labels except for the bacterial K-12 row, where the CDS organism was \texttt{escherichia\_coli\_k12\_mg1655} and the tRNA organism label was \texttt{escherichia\_coli}.  This is a finite identifier join, not a biological equivalence claim beyond the stated data linkage.

\paragraph{Complement projection.}
The complement calculation produced finite values for all six organisms.  The abundance association captured by the nine-coordinate block, measured as $R^2_{QP}$, was largest in yeast and smaller in the vertebrate and bacterial panels:
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{QP}(\textit{S. cerevisiae}) &= 0.29837624987287115,\\
R^2_{QP}(\textit{E. coli K\mbox{-}12}) &= 0.10767526667768725,\\
R^2_{QP}(\textit{D. rerio}) &= 0.04613529805760884,\\
R^2_{QP}(\textit{H. sapiens}) &= 0.04454565117192484,\\
R^2_{QP}(\textit{G. gallus}) &= 0.03980793595345331,\\
R^2_{QP}(\textit{M. smegmatis}) &= 0.032090091625816136 .
\end{aligned}
$$
The modeled-tAI-complement fraction $d$ and modeled-tAI-aligned fraction $m=1-d$ separate the fitted $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ abundance component into the part left outside modeled tAI and the part aligned with modeled tAI.  The observed values were
$$
\begin{aligned}
d(\textit{G. gallus}) &= 0.9400341806302775, & m(\textit{G. gallus}) &= 0.05996581936972245,\\
d(\textit{H. sapiens}) &= 0.9208949355679455, & m(\textit{H. sapiens}) &= 0.07910506443205445,\\
d(\textit{M. smegmatis}) &= 0.8972672018746489, & m(\textit{M. smegmatis}) &= 0.10273279812535108,\\
d(\textit{D. rerio}) &= 0.874628721049266, & m(\textit{D. rerio}) &= 0.12537127895073397,\\
d(\textit{E. coli K\mbox{-}12}) &= 0.6629960038578532, & m(\textit{E. coli K\mbox{-}12}) &= 0.3370039961421468,\\
d(\textit{S. cerevisiae}) &= 0.21173219572706703, & m(\textit{S. cerevisiae}) &= 0.788267804272933 .
\end{aligned}
$$
The corresponding squared norms of the complement component were $1262.22376787716$ in human, $1005.3614101653925$ in zebrafish, $695.5324705841906$ in chicken, $287.00467177017856$ in \emph{E. coli}, $186.88597748267532$ in yeast, and $164.9629139386272$ in \emph{M. smegmatis}.  These numbers show that the complement is not numerically absent.  They also show that modeled tAI absorbs very different fractions of the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ abundance component across organisms: yeast is the strongest modeled-tAI-aligned case in this panel, while chicken, human, \emph{M. smegmatis}, and zebrafish leave most of the fitted abundance component in the complement.

\paragraph{Coordinate support.}
The $a_{\perp}$ readback remained nine-dimensional in every organism, and the absolute support fractions summed to one up to floating-point precision.  The dominant support coordinates were organism-specific.  Human was led by $Arg\_AGR$, $Ile\_AUA$, $Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG$, $K\_AAA$, and $f3\_stress$.  Zebrafish was led by $f3\_stress$, $Arg\_AGR$, $Ile\_AUA$, $K\_AAA$, and $Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG$.  Chicken was led by $Arg\_AGR$, $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$, $Ile\_AUA$, $Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG$, $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$, and $K\_AAA$.  Yeast was led by $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$, $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$, $Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG$, $Arg\_AGR$, $Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY$, and $f3\_stress$.  \emph{E. coli} K-12 was led by $Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY$, $Arg\_AGR$, $Ile\_AUA$, and $f3\_stress$.  \emph{M. smegmatis} was led by $K\_AAA$, $f3\_stress$, $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$, $Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG$, and $Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG$.

The coordinate list is the main scientific content of this chapter.  It says that the modeled-tAI complement is not carried by a single universal codon-family residual.  It is a finite, organism-indexed support pattern over the nine $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ coordinates.  In the present panel, $Arg\_AGR$ appears among the dominant complement coordinates in human, zebrafish, chicken, yeast, and \emph{E. coli}; $f3\_stress$ appears in human, zebrafish, yeast, \emph{E. coli}, and \emph{M. smegmatis}; $K\_AAA$ appears in human, zebrafish, chicken, and \emph{M. smegmatis}.  These repetitions are support observations only.  They do not turn the signs or magnitudes into an invariant biological direction.

\paragraph{Relation to the mediation scalar.}
The complement fraction $m$ was compared with a separate joint-decomposition mediation scalar computed on the same residualized $Q_e,T_e,P_e,Z$ rows.  Only \emph{M. smegmatis} matched the reference mediation scalar within $0.02$: the complement projection gave $m=0.10273279812535108$, the reference value was $0.08397602919861291$, and the absolute difference was $0.018756768926738165$.  The remaining organisms did not match within this tolerance.  The absolute differences were $0.19033411641073011$ for zebrafish, $0.27591825665152014$ for \emph{E. coli} K-12, $0.13772457710273214$ for chicken, $0.19295782716913712$ for human, and $0.16088729984876438$ for yeast.

This mismatch is informative rather than disqualifying.  The complement projection asks how much of the fitted abundance component is orthogonal to modeled tAI.  The joint-decomposition scalar asks a different finite-row question about the same residualized arrays.  Agreement is therefore an empirical outcome, not a definitional requirement.  In this panel, the two summaries coincide within the stated tolerance only for \emph{M. smegmatis}.

\paragraph{Boundary of the claim.}
The chapter supports only the following statement: in six finite organism panels, the modeled-tAI complement of the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ abundance component was computed, had finite nonzero norm, and could be read back into organism-specific support over the nine residual coordinates.  It does not show that modeled tAI is measured ribosome occupancy.  It does not show a direct translation mechanism.  It does not show protein folding, structural order, physical admissibility, molecular function, phenotype, or a universal biological law.  It also does not show that tRNA gene-copy count is tRNA abundance or charging.  Any such stronger conclusion would require a separate organism-matched measurement and its own controlled statistic.

The main negative lesson is equally important: a positive abundance association of $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates is not automatically a modeled-translation association, and modeled-translation complement support is not automatically a causal residual mechanism.  The finite result is a coordinate-level map of what remains after the modeled-tAI direction is removed from the fitted abundance component.
