\subsection{Modeled-translation complement support for the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ abundance component}
\label{sec:bioreality-q6-translation-complement-support}

\paragraph{Object of the row.}
This record concerns a finite cross-layer calculation in which the nine residual coordinates
$K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $Leu_{CUN/UUR}$, $Leu_{UUA/UUG}$, $Ser_{UCR/AGY}$, $Ser_{UCA/UCG}$, $Thr_{ACR/ACY}$, and $f_{3}$-stress are first residualized against the declared control family.  The control family has $24$ columns and rank $23$ in each organism-specific panel: amino-acid composition, CDS length, GC3, and the $M$-density baseline are kept outside the tested $Q$-coordinates.  The readout side is protein abundance, while $T$ is the modeled tAI direction obtained from organism-matched tRNA-copy and wobble-weight inputs.  The computed object is not a direct translation-efficiency measurement.  It is the component $P_{Q}$ of abundance explained by residualized $B^{*}_{Q6}$ coordinates, followed by the part $P_{Q\perp T}$ not aligned with the modeled-tAI direction.

\paragraph{Projection statistic.}
For each organism the finite row reports
$$
\begin{aligned}
P_{Q} &= \operatorname{proj}_{Q\mid Z}(P),\\
P_{Q\perp T} &= P_{Q}-\operatorname{proj}_{T\mid Z}(P_{Q}),\\
d &= \frac{\lVert P_{Q\perp T}\rVert^{2}}{\lVert P_{Q}\rVert^{2}},\qquad
m=1-d.
\end{aligned}
$$
The six organism rows are all finite.  In \emph{Danio rerio}, $n=14410$, $R^{2}_{QP}=0.0461352981$, $\lVert P_{Q\perp T}\rVert^{2}=1005.3614102$, $d=0.8746287210$, and $m=0.1253712790$.  In \emph{Escherichia coli} K12 MG1655, $n=3739$, $R^{2}_{QP}=0.1076752667$, $\lVert P_{Q\perp T}\rVert^{2}=287.0046718$, $d=0.6629960039$, and $m=0.3370039961$.  In \emph{Gallus gallus}, $n=12418$, $R^{2}_{QP}=0.0398079360$, $\lVert P_{Q\perp T}\rVert^{2}=695.5324706$, $d=0.9400341806$, and $m=0.0599658194$.  In \emph{Homo sapiens}, $n=19053$, $R^{2}_{QP}=0.0445456512$, $\lVert P_{Q\perp T}\rVert^{2}=1262.2237679$, $d=0.9208949356$, and $m=0.0791050644$.  In \emph{Mycobacterium smegmatis} str. MC2 155, $n=4129$, $R^{2}_{QP}=0.0320900916$, $\lVert P_{Q\perp T}\rVert^{2}=164.9629139$, $d=0.8972672019$, and $m=0.1027327981$.  In \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}, $n=5815$, $R^{2}_{QP}=0.2983762499$, $\lVert P_{Q\perp T}\rVert^{2}=186.8859775$, $d=0.2117321957$, and $m=0.7882678043$.

\paragraph{Coordinate readback.}
The residual complement is then read in the nine-coordinate $B^{*}_{Q6}$ basis.  The dominant support coordinates differ by organism.  The zebrafish row is led by $f_{3}$-stress, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $K_{AAA}$, and $Leu_{UUA/UUG}$.  The \emph{E. coli} row is led by $Thr_{ACR/ACY}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, and $f_{3}$-stress.  The chicken row is led by $Arg_{AGR}$, $Leu_{CUN/UUR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $Leu_{UUA/UUG}$, $Ser_{UCR/AGY}$, and $K_{AAA}$.  The human row is led by $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $Ser_{UCA/UCG}$, $K_{AAA}$, and $f_{3}$-stress.  The mycobacterial row is led by $K_{AAA}$, $f_{3}$-stress, $Leu_{CUN/UUR}$, $Leu_{UUA/UUG}$, and $Ser_{UCA/UCG}$.  The yeast row is led by $Ser_{UCR/AGY}$, $Leu_{CUN/UUR}$, $Ser_{UCA/UCG}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Thr_{ACR/ACY}$, and $f_{3}$-stress.  The absolute support fractions sum to $1.0$ in every row up to numerical roundoff, with the \emph{E. coli} row reported as $1.0000000000000002$.

\paragraph{Comparison with the mediation row.}
The scalar $m$ in this chapter is a projection-complement fraction, not the same estimator as the separate joint mediation calculation.  The agreement audit is therefore reported explicitly rather than forced.  The absolute differences from the joint-decomposition reference are $0.1903341164$ for zebrafish, $0.2759182567$ for \emph{E. coli}, $0.1377245771$ for chicken, $0.1929578272$ for human, $0.0187567689$ for \emph{M. smegmatis}, and $0.1608872998$ for yeast.  Only the mycobacterial row matches the reference within the $0.02$ tolerance.  Thus the chapter supports a coordinate-support description of the modeled-tAI complement, but it does not collapse the projection statistic into a general mediation statistic.

\paragraph{Adjacent finite checks.}
The rank-powered modeled-tAI row used $40$ organisms, produced a $9\times 9$ $S^{QT}$ matrix, had $\operatorname{rank}(Z)=23$, residual degrees of freedom $17$, $\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde X)=9$, and total $R^{2}_{QT}=0.9003849000$.  This supplies a modeled translation-readout context, not measured ribosome occupancy.  A separate non-translation ranking row used a regularizer $\lambda=0.01$ with $DL=\log_{2}(1+n_{col})$; for example, the zebrafish ranking selected measured TE as $H^{\ast}_{o}$ with $A_{H}=0.0175942900$, $C_{H}=0.6573753096$, $A_{H}C_{H}=0.0115660518$, score $0.0015660518$, Bonferroni threshold $0.0007199950$, and null $p95=0.0003297458$.  The same ranking selected no $H^{\ast}_{o}$ for \emph{E. coli}, complex membership for chicken and human, and mRNA for yeast.  The yeast mRNA-stability companion row had $n=5085$, join hit rate $0.9156038419$, $R^{2}_{QM}=0.0517942904$, $R^{2}_{QP}=0.2761140374$ on the same join, cosine $0.8040935468$, and permutation right-tail $p=0.000999000999000999$; its mediation companion reported $R^{2}_{QP\mid M}=0.2485179953$, absorbed fraction $0.0275960421$, mediation fraction $0.0999443648$, null mean $0.0001967449$, and null $p95=0.0014571003$.  These numbers are useful calibration rows, but they do not substitute for the complement-support calculation above.

\paragraph{Local boundary.}
The finite rows show that the modeled-tAI complement of the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ abundance component is nonzero, organism dependent, and readable in a sparse subset of the nine residual coordinates.  The result is a controlled statistical projection over paired codon, tRNA-copy, modeled-tAI, and abundance tables.  It does not by itself establish measured ribosome-mediated translation, protein folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, biochemical mechanism, organismal phenotype, or a universal biological law.  Such claims require separate layer-matched measurements and their own controlled statistics.
