\subsection{Rank-admissible codon-composition contact with RNA half-life}\label{subsec:biorality-q6-rna-halflife-rank-admissible}\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite panel.}  This record concerns the finite question of whether synonymous residual coordinates in $B^{*}_{Q6}$ can be promoted from codon-composition geometry to a translation-conditioned RNA-stability statement.  The available RNA half-life contact is an Agarwal-style codon-composition panel with $16500$ human rows and $18924$ mouse rows.  The recorded scalar association is
$$
\begin{aligned}
  n_{\mathrm{human}} &= 16500,\\
  n_{\mathrm{mouse}} &= 18924,\\
  \rho_{\mathrm{raw}} &= 0.126224,
\end{aligned}
$$
with runtime $46.349$ seconds and circularity guard recorded as non-CSC tAI.  Thus the paper may say that a weak finite codon-composition/RNA-half-life contact exists on two mammalian row sets, but it may not say that a $B^{*}_{Q6}$ coordinate has survived as a translation-realization variable.

\paragraph{Coordinate side.}  The relevant residual coordinate family is not a single codon-count statistic.  The finite residual list includes $Arg\_AGR$, $Ile\_AUA$, $K\_AAA$, $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$, $Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG$, $Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG$, $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$, $Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY$, and $f_3$-stress.  A separate proxy coupling calculation loaded three organisms, $homo\_sapiens$, $saccharomyces\_cerevisiae$, and $escherichia\_coli\_k12$, in the $61$-codon count space.  Against the proxy tRNA-Leu $CUN$-versus-$UUR$ log-copy spectrum, all nine residual coordinates were nonzero at threshold $0.01$, with maximum absolute coupling $1882464.1106377328$.  This is an $A_{ij}$ contact only.  It is not an $S^{QT}_{ij}$ survival certificate, because the proxy surface is not a held-out translation readout under the required controls.

\paragraph{Rank gate.}  For a proposed translation-survival entry, the design must residualize both the coordinate $q_i$ and the readout $T_j$ against the control block $Z$.  The admissible statistic is not the in-sample fit but the remaining non-saturated row space after controls.  In the notation used here, an entry can be reported only when
$$
\begin{aligned}
  r_{ij} &= \operatorname{rank}\big((I-P_Z)[q_i,T_j]\big),\\
  d_{ij} &= n-r_{ij}-\dim(Z),\\
  d_{ij} &> 0,
\end{aligned}
$$
and the survival score remains positive on a held-out or otherwise falsifiable translation-readout boundary.  If the controlled row space is saturated, then an apparent all-$1.0$ fit or a positive proxy coupling remains a projection artifact.  The finite RNA half-life association above therefore supplies a row-counted stability contact, not a rank-admissible translation-survival entry.

This supports the conservative reading: the residual coordinates remain real finite design objects, but the current contacts do not collapse them into a simple translation or RNA-decay mechanism.

\paragraph{Readback.}  The present readback is therefore a bounded finite-row statement.  There is a two-species RNA half-life contact with $\rho_{\mathrm{raw}}=0.126224$ and a non-CSC tAI guard, and there is a separate multi-organism residual/coupling memory showing that $B^{*}_{Q6}$ coordinates can be computed and can contact modeled translation-adaptation surfaces.  What is not present is a same-row matrix containing $q_i$, a direct translation readout $T_j$, RNA half-life, matched transcript abundance, amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, coding length, M-density, simpler codon baselines, and a recorded non-saturated held-out survival score.  Consequently this chapter records an admissibility boundary rather than a positive $S^{QT}_{ij}$ claim.

\paragraph{Local no-promotion rule.}  The finite association may be used to motivate a test, but it does not by itself establish translation, ribosome occupancy, protein abundance, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, a mechanism, phenotype, causation, or universality.  Any such statement requires its own layer-matched contact and statistic.  In particular, modeled tAI or stAI weights are not observed ribosome footprints, mRNA abundance is a control rather than a translation output, and protein or structure conclusions cannot be inferred from the codon-composition/RNA-half-life contact.  The correct status of the proposed $B^{*}_{Q6}$ translation-survival entry is therefore: finite contact present, rank-admissible translation survival not yet certified.
