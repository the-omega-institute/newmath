\subsection{Rank-admissible codon-composition contact before translation survival}\label{sec:bior-reality-q6-rank-admissible-mrna-halflife}\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite panel and observed statistic.} The record concerns the finite-row question of whether synonymous residual coordinates in $B^{*}_{Q6}$ may be promoted from codon-composition geometry to a cross-layer translation-survival entry. The directly attached mRNA-stability computation uses the Agarwal-style half-life surface with $n_{\mathrm{human}}=16500$ and $n_{\mathrm{mouse}}=18924$ matched rows. Its reported scalar contact is $\rho_{\mathrm{raw}}=0.126224$, computed under the circularity guard recorded as tAI non-CSC, with runtime $46.349$ seconds. This is a bounded association surface for codon composition and mRNA half-life; it is not yet a measured translation-readout survival matrix.

\paragraph{Residual-coordinate carrier.} The finite carrier is a residualized design matrix over $B^{*}_{Q6}$ synonymous coordinates, organism-indexed readout surfaces, and a control block $Z$ containing amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, length, and M-density terms. The admissible entry is not an in-sample score. For a proposed coordinate/readout pair $(q_i,T_j)$, both $q_i$ and $T_j$ must first be residualized against $Z$, and the remaining row space must still have enough independent support for a falsifiable held-out test. In local notation the admissibility condition is the finite-rank condition
$$
\begin{aligned}
  \operatorname{rank}(X_{q_i,T_j\mid Z}) &< n_Z,\\
  \operatorname{df}(q_i,T_j\mid Z) &> 0,\\
  S^{QT}_{ij} &\text{ is reportable only after the held-out readout remains non-vacuous.}
\end{aligned}
$$
If the residualized design is saturated, or if the apparent score is produced only by projection geometry, then the entry remains a coupling or data-request record rather than a survival statement.

\paragraph{Rank and residual evidence.} The related residual-basis computation shows that the available cross-organism coordinates are not exhausted by the present readout families. With $\lambda=0.01$ and description-length penalty $\log_2(1+n_{\mathrm{col}})$, the computed organisms were classified as persistent rather than compressible: human used $16008$ joined rows and ended with residual curve $1.0,0.9852198225375993,0.9806696939851618,0.9792537895870558,0.9787609432955213,0.9787233550315274,0.9723341658092314$; budding yeast used $3046$ joined rows and ended at $0.9798667977250413$ after nine selected candidates; Escherichia coli K12 MG1655 used $3415$ joined rows and ended at $0.9979478314995476$ after two selected candidates; zebrafish used $14410$ joined rows and ended at $0.9791828971490305$ after one selected candidate. Gallus gallus and Mycobacterium smegmatis strain mc2 155 were marked needs-data. Across the computed organisms the minimum final residual fraction was $0.9723341658092314$, so the finite row audit records persistent unexplained residual signal rather than a closed translation-realization account.

\paragraph{Coupling is separated from survival.} A separate three-organism coupling computation loaded Homo sapiens, Saccharomyces cerevisiae, and Escherichia coli K12. It computed nine synonymous residual coordinates, namely Arg\_AGR, Ile\_AUA, K\_AAA, Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR, Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG, Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG, Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY, Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY, and f3\_stress, in a $61$-codon count space. Against the proxy tRNA-Leu CUN-vs-UUR log-copy spectrum, all nine entries exceeded the $0.01$ coupling threshold, with maximum absolute coupling $1882464.1106377328$ and $9$ nonzero entries. This defeats the narrow null that every proxy coupling is below threshold, but it does not pass the stricter $S^{QT}_{ij}$ gate: the readout is a proxy tRNA-copy surface, not a held-out organism- or CDS-matched translation measurement after the declared controls.

\paragraph{Decision rule.} The chapter therefore records a rank-admissibility gate rather than a positive translation-survival claim. The finite mRNA-half-life contact supplies rows and a weak raw association, while the residual-basis and proxy-coupling records show that codon-composition geometry has testable contact with readout surfaces. The promotion rule is deliberately stricter:
$$
\begin{aligned}
  S^{QT}_{ij}>0
  \quad\Longrightarrow\quad
  &\text{non-saturated residual rank, positive degrees of freedom,}\\
  &\text{matched translation readout rows, held-out survival,}\\
  &\text{and survival beyond simpler codon baselines.}
\end{aligned}
$$
The current finite evidence does not supply that full tuple. In particular, an all-fitted or rank-saturated controlled surface, a positive proxy coupling $A^{QT}_{ij}$, or a modeled tAI/stAI contact is not counted as translation survival.

\paragraph{Local no-promotion boundary.} Nothing in this chapter asserts ribosome occupancy, protein abundance, folded structure, physical admissibility, molecular function, phenotype, mechanism, adaptation, causation, or a universal biological law. The mRNA half-life rows test a bounded RNA-stability association; the tRNA-copy and tAI/stAI routes test bounded translation-adaptation readbacks; the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ coordinates remain residual codon-composition variables. Any later claim about measured translation, protein output, folding, physical feasibility, function, mechanism, or universality must attach its own layer-matched contact and recompute the controlled finite-row statistic at that layer.
