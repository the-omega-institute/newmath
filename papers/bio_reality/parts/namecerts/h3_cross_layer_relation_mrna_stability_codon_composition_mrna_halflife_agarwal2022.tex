\subsection{Rank-admissible translation gate for codon-composition half-life contact}
\label{subsec:biorality-q6-rank-admissible-translation-survival-gate}

\paragraph{Finite row surface.}
This record concerns the proposed promotion of synonymous $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates from codon-composition geometry to a controlled translation-readout survival surface. The finite biological contact available for the mRNA-stability side is the Agarwal-style codon-composition and mRNA half-life run with experiment id $74d321d44dd7$. Its recorded row counts are $n_{human}=16500$ and $n_{mouse}=18924$, with a raw rank-order association $\rho=0.126224$ and a circularity guard recorded as non-CSC tAI. The computation therefore supplies a bounded mRNA-stability contact, not a direct ribosome-occupancy or translation-efficiency panel. The rank gate is stated for proposed entries $S^{QT}_{ij}$, where $q_i$ is a residual synonymous coordinate and $T_j$ is a translation readout after controls $Z$ have been applied.

\paragraph{Admissibility object.}
For each candidate pair $(q_i,T_j)$, the usable design is the residualized matrix obtained after removing amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, CDS length, and M-density columns, together with any declared simpler codon baseline. The gate accepts only a non-saturated design whose held-out row support can falsify the survival claim. In local notation, the admissible part of the test is the part for which
$$
\begin{aligned}
  \operatorname{rank}\bigl((q_i,T_j)\mid Z\bigr) &< n_Z,\\
  \operatorname{df}\bigl((q_i,T_j)\mid Z\bigr) &> 0,\\
  S^{QT}_{ij} &\text{ is evaluated on rows not exhausted by the fitted columns.}
\end{aligned}
$$
If the residualized row space is exhausted by the fitted columns, or if the available rows cannot support a held-out statistic, the entry is not a positive translation-survival entry. It remains either a coupling observation or a request for a larger matched panel.

\paragraph{Available positive contacts.}
The codon-side finite contact is not empty. In the translation-coupling run $4a783972e74b$, the cross-organism panel loaded three organisms: $\mathit{Homo\ sapiens}$, $\mathit{Saccharomyces\ cerevisiae}$, and $\mathit{Escherichia\ coli}$ K12. The run computed nine synonymous residual coordinates,
$$
\begin{aligned}
&Arg\_AGR,\ Ile\_AUA,\ K\_AAA,\ Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR,\ Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG,\\
&Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG,\ Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY,\ Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY,\ f_3\text{-}stress.
\end{aligned}
$$
Against the tRNA-Leu CUN-versus-UUR log-copy proxy, the break condition requiring all nine $|A_{ij}|<0.01$ was not met: the threshold was $0.01$, the number of nonzero entries above that proxy threshold was $9$, and the recorded maximum absolute coupling was $1882464.1106377328$. This is meaningful as a proxy contact with codon usage and tRNA-copy geometry, but it is not the same object as a controlled survival statistic $S^{QT}_{ij}$.

\paragraph{Residual-basis pressure.}
The residual-basis extraction run $c1ef9b455187$ gives the rank gate additional negative pressure rather than a promotion. Four organisms were computed and classified as persistent residual cases: $\mathit{Danio\ rerio}$, $\mathit{Escherichia\ coli}$ K12 MG1655, $\mathit{Homo\ sapiens}$, and $\mathit{Saccharomyces\ cerevisiae}$. Two organisms, $\mathit{Gallus\ gallus}$ and $\mathit{Mycobacterium\ smegmatis}$ str. MC2 155, were recorded as needing data. The computed joined-row counts were $14410$ for zebrafish, $3415$ for E. coli, $16008$ for human, and $3046$ for yeast. The final residual ratios stayed high: zebrafish ended at $0.9791828971490305$, E. coli at $0.9979478314995476$, human at $0.9723341658092314$, and yeast at $0.9798667977250413$. The global residual floor was $0.97$, with computed count $4$ and no organism classified as compressible or partially compressible.

\paragraph{Per-step null checks.}
The same residual extraction recorded degree-of-freedom matched deterministic Gaussian null comparisons. In human, selected increments exceeded the null at steps $1$ through $4$ and step $6$: $0.014780177462400674$ versus null $0.0002845846156909306$ for PTM density, $0.004618389163869967$ versus $0.000206488525646134$ for complex membership, $0.0014438137599134737$ versus $0.0002435268444123588$ for transmembrane count, $0.0005032876020244136$ versus $0.00020173994173736226$ for measured tRNA tAI, and $0.006528084968494762$ versus $0.0011598998794309233$ for localization. Human step $5$, domain count, did not exceed its null: $3.840392718095609\times 10^{-5}$ versus $0.00021932006910265595$. In yeast, steps $1$ through $4$ and step $9$ exceeded their nulls, while complex membership, measured tRNA tAI, domain count, and mRNA did not. In E. coli, the first mRNA step exceeded its null, $0.0017706954554909888$ versus $0.0013371669456573104$, while measured TE at step $2$ did not, $0.0002819723320884449$ versus $0.0010272364744326006$. These finite rows show structured residual pressure, but they also show why a survival call cannot be made merely from a positive fitted increment.

\paragraph{Half-life contact does not close translation survival.}
The Agarwal mRNA half-life contact has enough rows to support a bounded mRNA-stability statement at the recorded scope, but the rank-admissible translation gate asks for a different finite object: the same organism or same CDS rows must carry $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual labels, a direct or explicitly bounded translation readout, matched mRNA abundance when TE is formed or residualized, and the declared control columns. The recorded raw $\rho=0.126224$ is therefore not reported as $S^{QT}_{ij}>0$. It is a finite contact with RNA half-life under a non-CSC tAI guard, while $S^{QT}_{ij}$ remains blocked unless the post-control rank is non-saturated and held-out survival is computed on the matched translation surface.

\paragraph{Local verdict.}
This chapter records a rank-admissibility boundary, not a positive translation-survival result. The available finite rows justify three statements. First, codon-side $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residuals and proxy translation-coupling coordinates are present. Second, mRNA half-life has a finite human and mouse contact with $16500$ and $18924$ rows and raw $\rho=0.126224$. Third, the residual-basis runs show persistent unexplained residual structure, with the minimum final residual ratio recorded as $0.9723341658092314$. None of these facts by itself supplies a non-saturated, held-out, Z-controlled $S^{QT}_{ij}$ entry. A fitted all-in-sample score, a modeled tAI or stAI contact, an $A_{ij}$ coupling value, or a positive raw half-life association is not sufficient for translation survival.

\paragraph{Boundary of interpretation.}
The local cannot-promote rule is strict. This record does not assert ribosome occupancy, translation realization, protein abundance, folded structure, physical admissibility, molecular function, system phenotype, causal mechanism, adaptation, or a universal biological law. Any such statement would require a separate layer-matched contact and a statistic computed after the translation-readout gate has survived the declared controls. Until that panel exists, the correct readback is: rank-admissibility is required, the current half-life contact is informative but not decisive for $S^{QT}$, and saturated or underpowered designs return to data-seeking status rather than being promoted.
