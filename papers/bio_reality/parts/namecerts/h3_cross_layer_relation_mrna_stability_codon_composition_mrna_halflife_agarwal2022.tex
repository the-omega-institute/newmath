\subsection{Rank-Admissible Codon Composition Boundary for mRNA Half-Life}
\label{sec:biorality-q6-rank-admissible-mrna-halflife-agarwal2022}

\paragraph{Finite object.}
This record concerns the finite-row question whether synonymous residual coordinates in $B^*_{Q6}$ can be promoted from codon-composition descriptors to a controlled cross-layer relation involving translation readouts and mRNA half-life.  The carrier is a residualized design matrix whose columns include the nine reported synonymous residual coordinates $q_i$, organism-indexed translation readout surfaces $T_j$, RNA half-life readouts, and controls $Z$.  The named coordinates are $\mathrm{Arg}_{AGR}$, $\mathrm{Ile}_{AUA}$, $K_{AAA}$, $\mathrm{Leu}_{CUN\,vs\,UUR}$, $\mathrm{Leu}_{UUA\,vs\,UUG}$, $\mathrm{Ser}_{UCA\,vs\,UCG}$, $\mathrm{Ser}_{UCR\,vs\,AGY}$, $\mathrm{Thr}_{ACR\,vs\,ACY}$, and $f_3$-stress.  The row-level half-life contact used for the Agarwal-style readback contains $16{,}500$ human rows and $18{,}924$ mouse rows, with a reported raw rank-correlation scale $\rho=0.126224$ and a runtime of $46.349$ seconds.  The circularity guard is explicitly recorded as non-CSC $tAI$, so the translation-weight surface is not allowed to smuggle the codon-stability conclusion into the coordinate definition.

\paragraph{Gate condition.}
For each proposed entry $S^{QT}_{ij}$, the admissibility test first residualizes both the coordinate $q_i$ and the translation surface $T_j$ against $Z$, where $Z$ includes amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, length, and M-density controls, with matched mRNA abundance used only as a control when translation efficiency is estimated.  The finite-row gate asks for non-saturated rank and enough degrees of freedom for held-out testing.  In symbols, the admissible part of the claim is only the following implication:
$$
\begin{aligned}
&\operatorname{rank}\bigl((I-P_Z)[q_i,T_j]\bigr)<n_Z
\quad\text{and}\quad
\mathrm{df}_{ij}>0 \\
&\hspace{2em}\Longrightarrow\quad
S^{QT}_{ij}\text{ may be estimated on held-out rows.}
\end{aligned}
$$
If the residualized design is rank-saturated, or if the surviving degrees of freedom are insufficient, the entry remains a coupling or data-request record rather than a translation-survival record.  This rule is needed because the earlier three-organism contact loaded Homo sapiens, Saccharomyces cerevisiae, and Escherichia coli K12 over a $61$-codon count space and produced nine nonzero proxy couplings against a Leu CUN-versus-UUR tRNA-copy spectrum, with maximum absolute proxy coupling $1{,}882{,}464.1106377328$.  Those nine nonzero proxy entries show contact with a modeled or copy-number readout, but they do not by themselves establish $S^{QT}_{ij}>0$.

\paragraph{Cross-layer status.}
The mRNA half-life statistic is finite and organism-scoped, but it is not a completed translation-survival matrix.  The relevant distinction is between a codon-composition or modeled-adaptation association and a controlled survival entry at the translation boundary.  The Agarwal-style row counts and raw $\rho=0.126224$ support a bounded mRNA-stability contact, while the rank-admissibility gate prevents promotion of fitted or saturated surfaces to $S^{QT}$ survival.  The execution-selection decomposition supplies a useful comparator: in Saccharomyces cerevisiae, the active gene set has $n=5{,}684$, base $R^2_{QP}=0.30732228260918726$, full-base $R^2_{QP}=0.29837624994140827$, $\|P_Q\|^2=864.0124192674945$, $\|P_{residual}\|^2=2811.4213259512776$, five folds, $240$ permutations, and the finite verdict is composition artifact.  The translational-selection scaling contact is broader, with $20$ requested organisms, $20$ computed strengths, $20$ organisms with doubling-time rows, and $17$ complete strength--doubling--GC3--tRNA rows, using held-out $R^2$ as the primary strength field.  These supporting finite rows justify the rank gate as a real discriminant: some codon-abundance contacts can be computed, but a specific $q_i\to T_j$ survival entry still requires nondegenerate held-out translation readout rows.

\paragraph{Local boundary.}
The chapter therefore certifies a refusal discipline, not a universal biological law.  A positive in-sample fit, an $A^{QT}$ proxy coupling, a modeled $tAI$ or $stAI$ contact, or an all-$1.0$ saturated surface is not enough for translation survival.  The present finite evidence also does not imply ribosome occupancy, protein abundance, folded structure, physical admissibility, biological function, mechanism, phenotype, causation, or universality.  Those claims require separate layer-matched contacts and their own controlled statistics.  The admissible conclusion is narrower: the Agarwal-style mRNA half-life rows, the non-CSC $tAI$ guard, the persistent residual-basis classifications, and the rank audit jointly define a finite gate for deciding when a proposed $B^*_{Q6}$ codon-composition contact is testable as translation-conditioned RNA-stability survival and when it must remain a bounded association or a request for more matched rows.
