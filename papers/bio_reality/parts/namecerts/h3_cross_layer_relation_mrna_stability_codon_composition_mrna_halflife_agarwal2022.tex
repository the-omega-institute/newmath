\subsection{Rank-admissible codon-composition contact with mRNA half-life}
\label{sec:biorality-q6-rank-admissible-mrna-halflife}

\paragraph{Finite object.}
This record treats the synonymous residual coordinates $B^{*}_{Q6}$ as a finite coordinate family that may be tested against bounded translation and RNA-stability readouts, but only after the design has enough independent residual variation to make a survival claim falsifiable.  The biological surface used here is the codon-composition and mRNA half-life contact indexed by Agarwal-style human and mouse rows.  The finite computation contains $n_{\mathrm{human}}=16500$ human rows and $n_{\mathrm{mouse}}=18924$ mouse rows.  Its recorded raw association is $\rho=0.126224$, with runtime $46.349$ seconds, and it uses the circularity guard recorded as ``tAI non-CSC''.  This is a weak finite contact, not a license to read synonymous geometry as translation, decay mechanism, protein output, structure, or phenotype.

\paragraph{Residualized carrier.}
For a proposed coordinate-readout pair $(q_i,T_j)$, the local object is the residualized two-column design obtained after removing the control span $Z$.  In this chapter $Z$ includes amino-acid composition, organism, $GC3$, transcript or coding length, and the $M$-density baseline whenever the corresponding row field exists.  The gate is the rank condition on the residual columns, not the fitted score itself:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\widetilde q_i &= (I-P_Z)q_i,\\
\widetilde T_j &= (I-P_Z)T_j,\\
X_{ij} &= [\widetilde q_i\;\;\widetilde T_j],\\
\operatorname{df}_{ij} &= n-\operatorname{rank}(Z)-\operatorname{rank}(X_{ij}).
\end{aligned}
$$
The proposed survival entry $S^{QT}_{ij}$ is admissible only when the residualized design is not saturated and when held-out rows can still contradict the fitted relation.  A perfect in-sample fit on a rank-saturated matrix is therefore a refusal event, even if its reported score is positive or equal to $1.0$.

\paragraph{Observed finite contact.}
The mRNA half-life contact supplies a concrete row-bearing surface: human and mouse row counts, a bounded raw correlation, and a circularity guard separating tAI from CSC-style codon composition.  The scalar $\rho=0.126224$ is small but nonzero as an association between codon-composition information and mRNA half-life on the finite panel.  It is not reported here as $S^{QT}_{ij}>0$, because the provided memory does not include the residualized rank table, the held-out split, the post-control partial $R^2$, or a per-entry degrees-of-freedom certificate for any named $B^{*}_{Q6}$ coordinate against the half-life surface.

\paragraph{Relation to the available $B^{*}_{Q6}$ coordinates.}
A separate coordinate computation lists nine synonymous residual coordinates available for the translation-side coupling packet: $\mathrm{Arg\_AGR}$, $\mathrm{Ile\_AUA}$, $\mathrm{K\_AAA}$, $\mathrm{Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR}$, $\mathrm{Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG}$, $\mathrm{Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG}$, $\mathrm{Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY}$, $\mathrm{Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY}$, and $f_3$-stress.  That computation used a coupling threshold $0.01$ and found that the break condition ``all nine entries below threshold'' was false: all nine entries were nonzero against the proxy readout, with maximum absolute coupling $1882464.1106377328$.  The readout in that packet was a tRNA-Leu CUN-versus-UUR log-copy proxy, not a direct ribosome or TE measurement.  The result is therefore useful as evidence that the coordinate family is numerically active, but it remains coupling-only for the present rank-admissibility question.

\paragraph{Residual persistence context.}
The broader residual-basis computation gives additional finite context for why the present chapter insists on the rank gate.  With the stated regularizer $\lambda=0.01$ and description-length penalty $\log_2(1+n_{\mathrm{col}})$, four organisms were computed and all four were labelled persistent rather than compressible: $\mathrm{danio\_rerio}$, $\mathrm{escherichia\_coli\_k12\_mg1655}$, $\mathrm{homo\_sapiens}$, and $\mathrm{saccharomyces\_cerevisiae}$.  The final residual floors were high: $0.9791828971490305$ for zebrafish after one selected readout, $0.9979478314995476$ for E. coli after two selected readouts, $0.9723341658092314$ for human after six selected readouts, and $0.9798667977250413$ for yeast after nine selected readouts.  The minimum final residual among computed organisms was $0.9723341658092314$, above the reported persistence floor $0.97$.  Gallus gallus and Mycobacterium smegmatis were labelled needs-data.  These numbers argue against treating residual activity as automatic biological realization: the coordinates retain large unexplained components even after available readout blocks are used.

\paragraph{Admissibility verdict.}
The local verdict is a bounded readiness result for the gate, not a positive translation-survival result.  The finite half-life contact is real enough to define a test target, and the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ coordinate family is concrete enough to define candidate $q_i$ columns.  However, the chapter refuses to promote any entry to $S^{QT}_{ij}>0$ unless the same finite row table contains the residualized design rank, sufficient post-control degrees of freedom, a held-out translation or explicitly bounded translation-readout boundary, and a break condition showing that rank saturation has not produced a vacuous fit.  The admissibility rule is:
$$
\begin{aligned}
S^{QT}_{ij}\ \text{may be reported} \quad &\Longrightarrow\quad
\operatorname{rank}([Z,q_i,T_j]) < n,\\
&\quad \operatorname{df}_{ij}>0,\\
&\quad \text{the held-out readout can reject the fitted contact},\\
&\quad \text{the entry survives simpler codon-baseline controls.}
\end{aligned}
$$
If any of these conditions fails, the entry is recorded as coupling-only or needs-data.  In particular, all-$1.0$ fitted surfaces, rank-degenerate three-organism panels, modelled tAI or stAI contacts, and proxy coupling matrices are not counted as translation survival.

\paragraph{Local boundary.}
This chapter does not claim ribosome occupancy, translation realization, protein abundance, folded structure, physical admissibility, molecular function, system phenotype, mechanism, causation, or a universal biological law.  The mRNA half-life row counts and raw correlation only identify a bounded cross-layer contact to be tested under controls.  Any stronger statement would require its own matched contact: direct ribosome or TE rows for translation, proteomics rows for protein abundance, structural or physical assays for folding and admissibility, functional or perturbation assays for function, and an independently powered design for generality.  The rank-admissibility gate is therefore a refusal-friendly certificate: it says exactly when a proposed $B^{*}_{Q6}$ translation-survival entry is testable, and it rejects promotion when the finite residual design is saturated or underpowered.
