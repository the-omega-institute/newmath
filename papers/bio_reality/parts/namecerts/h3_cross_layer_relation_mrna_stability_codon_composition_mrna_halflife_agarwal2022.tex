\paragraph{Rank gate.}
For a proposed entry from a residual coordinate $q_i$ to a translation or stability readout $T_j$, the admissibility test is a residualized finite-row rank condition. Let $Z$ contain the declared controls: amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, coding length, and M-density, with matched mRNA abundance used only as a control when the readout requires it. The gate is the following finite check:
$$
\begin{aligned}
q_i^{\perp Z} &= q_i - \Pi_Z q_i,\\
T_j^{\perp Z} &= T_j - \Pi_Z T_j,\\
\operatorname{rank}\bigl([q_i^{\perp Z},T_j^{\perp Z}]\bigr) &< n_Z,\\
\operatorname{df}_{ij} &= n_Z - \operatorname{rank}(Z) - \operatorname{rank}\bigl([q_i^{\perp Z},T_j^{\perp Z}]\bigr) > 0.
\end{aligned}
$$
If this test is saturated, an in-sample score of $1.0$ or any positive fitted association is treated as a projection artifact. The entry can then be recorded only as coupling-only or as requiring additional rows, because there is no remaining held-out degree of freedom in which survival could fail.

A separate three-organism codon and tRNA-copy computation loaded homo sapiens, saccharomyces cerevisiae, and escherichia coli K12. This is useful contact evidence, but it is not a survival result: the readout was a tRNA-Leu CUN-versus-UUR log-copy proxy, not a same-row measured ribosome, translation-efficiency, protein, or RNA half-life surface under the full control set.

\paragraph{Half-life readback.}
The mRNA half-life statistic supplies a real finite-row stability contact, not a translation-realization certificate. The raw association $\rho=0.126224$ is therefore read as a weak but nonzero codon-composition-to-half-life signal over the reported human and mouse panels, subject to the circularity guard. It does not by itself prove that any particular $B^{*}_{Q6}$ coordinate survives as $S^{QT}_{ij}>0$. A positive survival statement would require the same organism and same coding-sequence rows to contain the residual coordinate, the stability or translation readout, all controls in $Z$, the simpler-codon-baseline ablation, and a non-saturated held-out test.

They also show that residual energy remains persistent across the computed organisms under the $\lambda=0.01$ description-length regularizer $DL=\log_2(1+n_{\mathrm{col}})$.
