\subsection{Rank-admissible codon-composition contact with mRNA half-life}
\label{subsec:biorreality-q6-rank-admissible-mrna-halflife}

\paragraph{Finite panel.}
This record studies whether synonymous residual coordinates from $B^{*}_{Q6}$ can be promoted from codon-composition geometry to a controlled translation-survival or RNA-stability readback.  The finite mRNA half-life contact is organism-scoped: the computed panel contains $16500$ human rows and $18924$ mouse rows, with the circularity guard recorded as non-CSC tAI.  The observed scalar contact is a raw rank correlation $\rho = 0.126224$ for the Agarwal-style codon-composition and mRNA half-life surface, with runtime $46.349$ seconds.  This is a real finite contact with RNA stability data, but it is not by itself a certificate of translation survival.

That contact reported $9$ nonzero proxy entries above the threshold $0.01$, with maximum absolute proxy coupling $1882464.1106377328$, and its break condition was not met.  These numbers show finite codon/readout contact, but the proxy matrix remains an $A_{ij}$ surface rather than an $S^{QT}_{ij}$ survival surface.

\paragraph{Admissibility gate.}
For every proposed entry $S^{QT}_{ij}$, the admissibility question is not whether an in-sample fitted score is positive, but whether the residualized post-control design has independent support after $Z$.  Write $q_i^{\perp Z}$ for a residual $B^{*}_{Q6}$ coordinate and $T_j^{\perp Z}$ for the corresponding translation or bounded translation-weight readout after controls.  The gate accepts an entry only when the residual row space is not saturated and has enough degrees of freedom to make held-out failure possible:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\operatorname{rank}(X_{ij}^{\perp Z}) &< n_{ij}^{\perp Z},\\
\operatorname{df}_{ij} &= n_{ij}^{\perp Z}-\operatorname{rank}(X_{ij}^{\perp Z}) > 0,\\
S^{QT}_{ij} &> 0 \quad \text{only after baseline ablation and held-out testing.}
\end{aligned}
$$
If the rank is saturated, an all-$1.0$ in-sample fit is classified as a projection artifact.  The entry is then retained as coupling-only or as requiring more rows, even if the observed proxy contact is large.

\paragraph{Residual-basis context.}
The residual-basis extraction computation used a stated regularizer $\lambda = 0.01$ with description length $\log_{2}(1+n_{\mathrm{col}})$.  It classified four computed organisms as persistent rather than compressible: \emph{Danio rerio}, \emph{E. coli} K12 MG1655, human, and budding yeast.  The final residual floors stayed high: human ended at $0.9723341658092314$ after $6$ selected candidates and $16008$ joined rows, budding yeast ended at $0.9798667977250413$ after $9$ selected candidates and $3046$ joined rows, \emph{E. coli} ended at $0.9979478314995476$ after $2$ candidates and $3415$ joined rows, and zebrafish ended at $0.9791828971490305$ after $1$ candidate and $14410$ joined rows.  Gallus gallus and \emph{Mycobacterium smegmatis} were marked as needing data.  The minimum final residual among computed organisms was $0.9723341658092314$, so the residual-basis packet contributes an important negative constraint: the available readout blocks do not absorb the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual energy strongly enough to justify a broad biological interpretation.

\paragraph{mRNA half-life readback.}
The mRNA half-life contact supplies an RNA-stability surface, not a direct translation-readout survival certificate.  The finite scalar $\rho=0.126224$ is compatible with a weak codon-composition association over the recorded human and mouse rows, but the chapter does not promote it to ribosome occupancy, protein abundance, structure, function, or mechanism.  The correct local readback is narrower: the panel records that a codon-composition and half-life surface exists under a non-CSC tAI circularity guard, while the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ translation-survival gate still demands a rank-audited, held-out, post-control $S^{QT}_{ij}$ entry before any translation claim is made.

\paragraph{Local refusal rule.}
The break rule for this chapter is therefore explicit.  If the residualized design is rank-saturated, if the remaining degrees of freedom vanish, or if the only available support is proxy coupling, modeled tAI or stAI contact, mRNA abundance, or an in-sample perfect fit, then $S^{QT}_{ij}$ is not reported as positive.  In that case the entry remains a bounded contact or a data request.  No translation realization, ribosome-occupancy claim, protein-output claim, folded-structure claim, physical-admissibility claim, molecular-function claim, mechanism claim, phenotype claim, or universal biological law follows from this chapter unless a separate layer-matched finite contact supplies the corresponding rows and statistic.
