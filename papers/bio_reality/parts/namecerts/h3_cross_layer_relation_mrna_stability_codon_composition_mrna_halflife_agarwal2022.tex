\subsection{Rank-admissible codon residuals at the RNA half-life boundary}\label{sec:bior reality-q6-rank-admissible-rna-half-life}\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite-row object.}
This record treats the synonymous residual coordinates $B^{*}_{Q6}$ as a finite design object, not as a direct biological mechanism.  The observed RNA-stability contact is the Agarwal-style codon-composition and mRNA half-life surface with $n_{human}=16500$ and $n_{mouse}=18924$ rows.  The finite run reports a raw rank-order contact $\rho=0.126224$ under the circularity guard ``tAI non-CSC'', with runtime $46.349$ seconds.  This is a real cross-layer contact between codon-composition coordinates and RNA half-life rows, but the contact is deliberately weaker than a translation-survival assertion: it does not by itself contain same-row ribosome occupancy, translation efficiency, protein abundance, folded structure, physical admissibility, function, phenotype, or mechanism measurements.

\paragraph{Coordinate carrier.}
The residual coordinate carrier is the finite matrix whose columns include synonymous $B^{*}_{Q6}$ coordinates and whose controls include amino-acid composition, organism labels, GC3, coding length, and M-density.  The proposed entry $S^{QT}_{ij}$ is allowed to be read only after residualizing a coordinate $q_i$ and a translation readout $T_j$ against the control block $Z$.  The admissibility test is a rank test before it is a biological test.  In local notation the permitted statistic has the form
$$\begin{aligned}
q_i^{\perp Z} &= q_i - \Pi_Z q_i,\\
T_j^{\perp Z} &= T_j - \Pi_Z T_j,\\
S^{QT}_{ij} &> 0 \quad \text{only if the residual design has unused degrees of freedom and held-out falsifiability.}
\end{aligned}$$
If the residual row space is saturated, or if the support is too small to leave an independent held-out check, the same numerical fit is recorded as coupling-only or needs-data rather than translation survival.

\paragraph{Observed half-life contact.}
The half-life run supplies a bounded RNA-stability readout, not a promotion to translation realization.  Its finite facts are specific: $16500$ human rows, $18924$ mouse rows, raw $\rho=0.126224$, and the non-CSC tAI guard preventing a circular codon-stability reuse.  These numbers support the claim that codon-composition residuals can be placed against an RNA half-life surface on a finite panel.  They do not show that a particular $B^{*}_{Q6}$ coordinate survives as $S^{QT}_{ij}$ after the translation-readout residualization step, because the recorded row does not include the declared same-row measured translation matrix.  Thus the local conclusion is a boundary conclusion: RNA half-life contact exists; rank-admissible translation survival remains conditional on a further measured translation readout.

\paragraph{Coupling evidence and refusal rule.}
A separate three-organism codon--tRNA proxy contact computed nine synonymous residual coordinates: $\mathrm{Arg\_AGR}$, $\mathrm{Ile\_AUA}$, $K\_AAA$, $\mathrm{Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR}$, $\mathrm{Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG}$, $\mathrm{Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG}$, $\mathrm{Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY}$, $\mathrm{Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY}$, and $f_3$-stress.  That run loaded Homo sapiens, Saccharomyces cerevisiae, and Escherichia coli K12, used the tRNA-Leu CUN-versus-UUR log-copy spectrum as the proxy readout, and found that the break condition ``all nine $|A_{ij}|<0.01$'' was not met.  The maximum reported proxy magnitude was $1882464.1106377328$ with $9$ nonzero entries above the $0.01$ threshold.  This is strong enough to keep a coupling matrix alive, but it is not enough to report translation survival: $A^{QT}_{ij}$ is a proxy contact, while $S^{QT}_{ij}$ requires rank-admissible, held-out, controlled translation-readout survival.

\paragraph{Residual persistence context.}
The residual-basis extraction run reinforces why the gate must be conservative.  With the regularizer stated as $\lambda=0.01$ and $DL=\log_2(1+n_{col})$, four computed organisms remained persistent rather than compressible.  Homo sapiens had $n=16008$ joined rows, candidate count $6$, $d_{modeled}=0.9020707321191321$, $d_{difference}=0.018824203448813415$, and final residual ratio $0.9723341658092314$.  Saccharomyces cerevisiae had $n=3046$, candidate count $9$, $d_{modeled}=0.2199040339633322$, $d_{difference}=0.008171838236265161$, and final ratio $0.9798667977250413$.  Escherichia coli K12 MG1655 had $n=3415$, candidate count $2$, $d_{modeled}=0.6628211177352172$, $d_{difference}=0.00017488612263594217$, and final ratio $0.9979478314995476$.  Danio rerio had $n=14410$, candidate count $1$, $d_{modeled}=0.874628721049266$, $d_{difference}=0$, and final ratio $0.9791828971490305$.  Gallus gallus and Mycobacterium smegmatis str. MC2 155 were marked needs-data.  The minimum final residual ratio across computed organisms was $0.9723341658092314$, so the extraction did not collapse the residual geometry into a simple known readout family.

\paragraph{Protein-output guardrail.}
The execution-selection decomposition gives a second guardrail against over-reading the RNA half-life surface.  In yeast it used an active gene set of $5684$ rows, base $n=5815$, five folds, and $240$ permutations.  The active-set baseline $R^2_{QP}$ was $0.30732228260918726$, while the full-gene-set baseline was $0.29837624994140827$.  The recorded verdict was composition\_artifact, with $\|P_Q\|^2=864.0124192674945$ and $\|P_{residual}\|^2=2811.4213259512776$.  This prevents the RNA half-life and codon-residual contact from being used as protein-abundance mediation evidence.  At this point the finite record has codon residuals, bounded half-life contact, proxy translation coupling, and a negative protein-output guardrail, but not a measured translation-survival chain.

\paragraph{Local decision.}
The chapter therefore records a rank-admissibility rule rather than a positive universal biology statement.  A proposed $S^{QT}_{ij}$ entry is acceptable only when the post-control residual matrix has non-saturated rank, enough independent rows after $Z$, and a held-out translation readout that can fail.  An all-$1.0$ in-sample score, a modeled tAI or stAI contact, or a nonzero proxy $A^{QT}_{ij}$ is not sufficient.  The present finite evidence permits the following bounded assertion: codon-composition residuals have a finite RNA half-life contact on $16500$ human and $18924$ mouse rows, and related $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates have bounded proxy contacts and persistent residual behavior.  It does not permit any claim of ribosome occupancy, translation realization, protein abundance, folding, physical admissibility, biological function, mechanism, phenotype, causation, or universality unless a separate layer-matched contact supplies those measurements and the controlled statistic is recomputed.
