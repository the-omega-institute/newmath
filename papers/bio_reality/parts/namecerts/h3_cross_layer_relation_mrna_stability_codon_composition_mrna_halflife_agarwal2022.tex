\subsection{Rank-admissible codon residuals at the mRNA half-life boundary}
\label{sec:bioreality-q6-rank-admissible-mrna-halflife-agarwal2022}

\paragraph{Finite object.}
This record concerns the finite question whether synonymous residual coordinates in $B^{*}_{Q6}$ may be read as translation-survival evidence before they are allowed to touch an mRNA half-life interpretation.  The carrier is a residualized design matrix: rows are organism- or transcript-indexed observations, the active columns are $B^{*}_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates, the readout columns are translation-facing surfaces such as tRNA-copy, tAI or stAI, ribosome or translation-efficiency rows when available, and the control block $Z$ contains amino-acid composition, organism labels, $GC3$, coding length, and $M$-density.  The rule is deliberately asymmetric.  A fitted contact may be retained as a coupling contact, but it is not a survival contact unless the residualized row space has non-saturated rank and enough remaining degrees of freedom to make a held-out failure observable.

\paragraph{Observed mRNA half-life contact.}
The finite RNA-stability row actually present here is the Agarwal-style mRNA half-life contact with run id $74d321d44dd7$.  It contains $16{,}500$ human rows and $18{,}924$ mouse rows, and the scalar codon-composition contact reported by the run is $\rho = 0.126224$.  The guard against circularity is explicitly recorded as non-CSC tAI, so the tAI side is not treated as a codon-stability coefficient recycled from the same target.  The computation took $46.349$ seconds.  These numbers support a bounded statement: codon-composition information has a small but nonzero finite association with the mRNA half-life surface under the recorded guard.  They do not, by themselves, say that any particular $B^{*}_{Q6}$ coordinate survives as a translation-mediated RNA-stability mechanism.

\paragraph{Rank gate.}
For a proposed entry $S^{QT}_{ij}$, let $q_i$ be a $B^{*}_{Q6}$ coordinate and let $T_j$ be a translation readout.  Both are first residualized against $Z$.  The gate accepts a survival entry only when the residual design is not rank-saturated and when a held-out statistic can still fail.  In the notation of this chapter, the admissible case is the case in which
$$
\begin{aligned}
\operatorname{rank}\bigl([\widetilde q_i,\widetilde T_j,\widetilde Z]\bigr) &< n,\\
\operatorname{df}_{ij} &= n-\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde Z)-\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde q_i)-\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde T_j) > 0,
\end{aligned}
$$
and the survival score remains positive after baseline ablation.  If the fitted surface reaches an in-sample value such as $R^2=1.0$ only because the design has used up the available row space, the entry is rejected as a survival claim and remains either coupling-only or needs-data.  The break condition is therefore not a biological negation of codon relevance; it is a refusal to confuse projection geometry with a testable translation boundary.

The yeast panel used $3{,}046$ joined rows, selected nine candidate blocks, and ended with

The recorded floor for persistent residuals was $0.97$, and all four computed organisms remained persistent rather than compressible.  The regularizer in the extraction is $\lambda=0.01$ with description-length term $\log_2(1+n_{\mathrm{col}})$.  Thus the rank gate is not a decorative condition: the residual mass is empirically persistent enough that a positive-looking contact must prove that it is not merely explaining the same unresolved geometry in a saturated coordinate system.

\paragraph{Coupling versus survival.}
A separate coupling run, $4a783972e74b$, computed nine synonymous residual coordinates: \texttt{Arg\_AGR}, \texttt{Ile\_AUA}, \texttt{K\_AAA}, \texttt{Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR}, \texttt{Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG}, \texttt{Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG}, \texttt{Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY}, \texttt{Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY}, and \texttt{f3\_stress}.  Against the proxy tRNA-Leu CUN-versus-UUR log-copy spectrum, the break condition was not met: the threshold was $0.01$, all nine entries were not below threshold, the maximum absolute coupling was $1{,}882{,}464.1106377328$, and the nonzero-entry count was $9$.  This is evidence for a finite coupling matrix $A^{QT}$ over three loaded organisms, \texttt{homo\_sapiens}, \texttt{saccharomyces\_cerevisiae}, and \texttt{escherichia\_coli\_k12}.  It is not evidence for $S^{QT}>0$.  The proxy is a tRNA-copy spectrum, not a measured ribosome-occupancy or matched protein-output surface, and the present chapter therefore keeps the result below the survival line.

\paragraph{Neighboring finite contacts.}
Two additional cross-layer runs give scale for what counts as a real finite contact.  The branchpoint-motif run $a27e7c351f67$ used $10{,}584$ rows, controlled expression with UCSC hg19 GTEx V8 median tissue scores, and took $138.47155$ seconds.  The uORF/Kozak ribosome-load run $22595b48828f$ reported $41{,}862$ fixed rows, $16{,}739$ varying rows, a two-sample concordant result, and $131.941$ seconds of runtime.  These records show that row-matched readout surfaces can be present in this finite-data regime.  Their presence elsewhere is exactly why this chapter refuses to promote the mRNA half-life association or the tAI/tRNA coupling contact into a translation-survival statement without the same kind of joined, controlled, layer-specific matrix.

\paragraph{Local verdict.}
The finite conclusion is a guarded mRNA-stability contact plus a rank-admissibility refusal.  The chapter records $n_{\mathrm{human}}=16{,}500$, $n_{\mathrm{mouse}}=18{,}924$, $\rho=0.126224$, non-CSC tAI guarding, persistent $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual curves, and a nine-entry proxy coupling matrix.  It does not record a non-saturated, held-out $S^{QT}_{ij}>0$ entry.  Consequently the correct BioReality reading is: codon-composition and $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates can be carried to the boundary of mRNA half-life and translation readouts, but translation realization is not asserted.  No protein abundance, folding, structural order, physical admissibility, molecular function, phenotype, mechanism, adaptation claim, causal claim, or universal biological law follows from this packet unless a separate matched contact supplies that layer and the corresponding controlled finite statistic is recomputed.
