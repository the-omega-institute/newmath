\subsection{Rank-admissible codon residual contact with mRNA half-life}
\label{sec:biorality-q6-rank-admissible-mrna-halflife}

\paragraph{Finite claim.}
This record concerns the proposed contact between synonymous $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates and an mRNA-stability readout.  The finite observation currently attached to the claim is not a translation-survival result.  It is a bounded mRNA-half-life contact, computed with a non-CSC tAI circularity guard, with $n_{\mathrm{human}}=16500$, $n_{\mathrm{mouse}}=18924$, raw rank correlation $\rho=0.126224$, and runtime $46.349$ seconds.  The role of this chapter is therefore to state the admissibility rule that prevents a positive or visually impressive in-sample surface from being promoted to a translation-realization statement when the post-control design is rank-saturated, underpowered, or missing the translation row itself.

\paragraph{Carrier.}
The finite carrier is a residualized design matrix whose rows are organism- or CDS-indexed entries, whose columns include $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates, translation-readout surfaces when available, and control columns $Z$.  The relevant controls are amino-acid composition, organism identity, GC3, length, and an $M$-density baseline, with matched mRNA abundance used only as a control when a translation-efficiency denominator is being tested.  In the present finite record the external contacts include codon usage, tRNA gene-copy spectra, modeled tAI or stAI weights, and mRNA half-life, but the record does not yet contain the joined same-row ribosome, translation-efficiency, or protein-output panel needed for a survival entry.

\paragraph{Admissibility statistic.}
For a proposed pair $(q_i,T_j)$, let $\widetilde q_i$ and $\widetilde T_j$ denote the residuals after projecting $q_i$ and $T_j$ away from $Z$.  A survival entry $S^{QT}_{ij}$ is reportable only when the remaining row space has enough independent support for a held-out test.  The local rule is
$$
\begin{aligned}
S^{QT}_{ij}\ \hbox{is admissible}
&\Longrightarrow \operatorname{rank}([Z,q_i,T_j])>\operatorname{rank}(Z,q_i) \\
&\quad \hbox{and the held-out score is falsifiable after the declared controls.}
\end{aligned}
$$
If the post-control rank is saturated, or if the degrees of freedom collapse so that the fitted surface can become $1.0$ by construction, the entry is recorded as coupling-only or needs-data.  This refusal applies even when the in-sample score is positive.

\paragraph{Existing finite support.}
The linked codon-topology computation loaded three organisms, namely Homo sapiens, Saccharomyces cerevisiae, and Escherichia coli K-12, in a $61$-codon count space.  It computed nine synonymous residual coordinates: $\mathrm{Arg\_AGR}$, $\mathrm{Ile\_AUA}$, $\mathrm{K\_AAA}$, $\mathrm{Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR}$, $\mathrm{Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG}$, $\mathrm{Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG}$, $\mathrm{Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY}$, $\mathrm{Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY}$, and $f_3$-stress.  Against the proxy tRNA-Leu CUN-versus-UUR log-copy spectrum, all nine coordinates were nonzero above the $0.01$ coupling threshold, with maximum absolute entry $1882464.1106377328$.  That fact is useful as a contact signal, but it is deliberately weaker than $S^{QT}_{ij}>0$: it is a proxy coupling matrix, not a controlled translation-survival matrix.

\paragraph{Residual-basis context.}
The broader residual-basis extraction supports the same boundary.  With the stated regularizer $\lambda=0.01$ and description-length term $\log_2(1+n_{\mathrm{col}})$, four organisms were computed and remained persistent rather than compressible.  Homo sapiens had $n=16008$ joined rows, six selected candidates, and a residual curve
$$
\begin{aligned}
1.0,&\ 0.9852198225375993,\ 0.9806696939851618,\ 0.9792537895870558,\\
&0.9787609432955213,\ 0.9787233550315274,\ 0.9723341658092314 .
\end{aligned}
$$
Saccharomyces cerevisiae had $n=3046$ joined rows, nine candidates, and final residual $0.9798667977250413$; Escherichia coli K-12 MG1655 had $n=3415$ joined rows, two candidates, and final residual $0.9979478314995476$; Danio rerio had $n=14410$ joined rows, one candidate, and final residual $0.9791828971490305$.  The minimum final residual among computed organisms was $0.9723341658092314$, so the observed pattern is persistent residual structure, not a closed translation mechanism.

\paragraph{Neighboring finite contacts.}
Two nearby cross-layer rows show that the data system can support finite bounded contacts without allowing layer promotion.  The branchpoint motif record used $n=10584$ rows and expression control from UCSC hg19 gtexGeneV8, with runtime $138.47155$ seconds.  The uORF/Kozak ribosome-load record reported $n_{\mathrm{fixed}}=41862$, $n_{\mathrm{varying}}=16739$, two-sample concordance, and runtime $131.941$ seconds.  These contacts are useful examples of finite row discipline: a contact may be real at its own layer while still not proving translation survival, protein abundance, folding, function, or organismal phenotype for the present $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ claim.

\paragraph{Break condition.}
The local rejection rule is simple.  If the residualized design has saturated rank, too few independent rows after $Z$, or no held-out translation readout, then $S^{QT}_{ij}$ is not asserted.  The current mRNA-half-life row therefore remains a bounded stability-side contact with raw $\rho=0.126224$, not evidence that a synonymous residual coordinate has survived into translation realization.  A future positive entry would require the same row to contain the codon residual coordinate, a direct or explicitly bounded translation readout, the mRNA control when needed, and the declared covariates, followed by a nondegenerate held-out statistic.

\paragraph{Local boundary.}
No translation event, ribosome occupancy, protein abundance, folded structure, physical admissibility, biological function, mechanism, phenotype, or universal biological law follows from this record alone.  Modeled tAI or stAI weights are adaptation-weight readbacks, not observed ribosome dynamics.  mRNA half-life is an RNA-stability readout, not a translation-output measurement.  Codon-count geometry and $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual labels remain coordinate data until a separate layer-matched contact supplies rows at the claimed layer and the rank-admissibility gate is passed.
