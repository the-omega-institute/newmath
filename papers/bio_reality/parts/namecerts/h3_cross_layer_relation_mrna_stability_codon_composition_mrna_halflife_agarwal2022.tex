\paragraph{Finite subject.}
This record concerns a bounded codon-composition contact for the claim id $h3.cross\_layer\_relation.mrna\_stability.codon\_composition\_mrna\_halflife\_agarwal2022$.  The carrier is a finite residualized design matrix whose columns include $B^{*}_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates, organism-indexed translation or stability readout surfaces, and control columns $Z$. The relevant readback asks whether a proposed entry $S^{QT}_{ij}$ is statistically admissible after residualization, rather than whether an in-sample fit or a modelled adaptation score looks large.

\paragraph{Finite rows and scalar contact.}
The mRNA stability contact has a concrete two-organism row count: $n_{human}=16500$ and $n_{mouse}=18924$.  The recorded scalar association is $\rho=0.126224$, computed under the circularity guard recorded as ``tAI non-CSC'', with runtime $46.349$ seconds.  This is a bounded cross-layer contact between codon composition and mRNA half-life.  It is not, by itself, a translation-realization certificate, because the contact is an RNA-stability readout rather than a matched ribosome or translation-efficiency survival matrix.  The statistic therefore supports the existence of a finite codon-composition versus mRNA-half-life surface, while leaving $S^{QT}_{ij}$ promotion blocked unless a non-saturated translation-readout design is separately supplied.

\paragraph{Rank gate.}
For each residual coordinate $q_i$ and candidate translation readout $T_j$, the admissibility test is defined only after both sides are residualized against the declared controls.  Let $\widetilde q_i$ and $\widetilde T_j$ denote those residuals, and let $X_{ij}$ be the finite post-control design used to estimate the entry.  The entry is reportable as survival only when the remaining design has enough rank to make out-of-sample failure possible:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\widetilde q_i &= q_i - \Pi_Z q_i,\\
\widetilde T_j &= T_j - \Pi_Z T_j,\\
S^{QT}_{ij} &> 0 \quad \text{is admissible only if } \operatorname{rank}(X_{ij}) < n_{ij} \text{ and the held-out test has positive degrees of freedom.}
\end{aligned}
$$
If the rank is saturated, or if the residual design has too few independent rows, the correct output is not a positive survival call.  The entry remains a coupling-only contact or a needs-data cell even when an in-sample score is positive or equal to $1.0$.

\paragraph{Why coupling is not survival.}
The previous paragraph describes an $A^{QT}$-style finite contact, not a controlled survival entry.  Its readout is a proxy tRNA-copy spectrum, and the record explicitly lacks true tAI, stAI, ribosome-profiling, or matched protein-abundance rows at that site.  The rank-admissible gate therefore refuses the inference
$$
\begin{aligned}
A^{QT}_{ij} \neq 0 \quad &
Rightarrow \quad S^{QT}_{ij} > 0.
\end{aligned}
$$
The correct direction is weaker: a nonzero coupling can nominate a row pair for a future controlled survival test, but it does not certify that translation readout survives after amino-acid composition, organism, $GC3$, length, and $M$-density controls.
