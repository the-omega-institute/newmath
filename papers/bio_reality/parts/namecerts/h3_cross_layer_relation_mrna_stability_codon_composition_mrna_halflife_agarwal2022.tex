\subsection{Rank-admissible codon-composition contact with RNA half-life}
\label{sec:bioreality-q6-rank-admissible-mrna-halflife-agarwal2022}

\paragraph{Finite contact.}
The Agarwal-style RNA half-life packet supplies a bounded cross-layer contact between synonymous codon-composition coordinates and transcript decay readouts.  The finite run used $16{,}500$ human rows and $18{,}924$ mouse rows, with the circularity guard recorded as non-CSC tAI and a raw Spearman-scale contact of $\rho=0.126224$ in $46.349$ seconds.  This number is not promoted to a translation-survival score.  It is a weak finite-row association between codon-composition summaries and RNA half-life under the recorded guard, and it remains upstream of any claim about ribosome occupancy, protein abundance, folding, physical admissibility, biological function, mechanism, phenotype, or universality.

\paragraph{Coordinate carrier and controls.}
The carrier for the record is the residualized finite design matrix whose columns are the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates, organism-indexed translation or decay readout surfaces, and control columns $Z$.  The controls named by the gate include amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, coding-sequence length, and an M-density baseline.  A proposed entry $S^{QT}_{ij}$ is admissible only when both the coordinate $q_i$ and the readout $T_j$ have been residualized against $Z$, and the remaining row space has non-saturated rank with enough independent support for a held-out test.  In symbols, the local decision has the form
$$
\begin{aligned}
q_i^{\perp Z} &= q_i - \Pi_Z q_i,\\
T_j^{\perp Z} &= T_j - \Pi_Z T_j,\\
S^{QT}_{ij} &> 0 \quad \text{is reportable only if } \operatorname{rank}(q_i^{\perp Z},T_j^{\perp Z}) \text{ leaves nonzero test degrees of freedom.}
\end{aligned}
$$
If the residualized design is rank-saturated, underpowered, or supported only by an in-sample fitted surface, the record returns a blocked or needs-data state rather than a survival statement.

\paragraph{Why the existing codon--translation contact is not enough.}
A separate finite codon-use run computed nine $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ synonymous residuals: $\mathrm{Arg\_AGR}$, $\mathrm{Ile\_AUA}$, $\mathrm{K\_AAA}$, $\mathrm{Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR}$, $\mathrm{Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG}$, $\mathrm{Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG}$, $\mathrm{Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY}$, $\mathrm{Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY}$, and $f_3$-stress.  It used a three-organism panel consisting of $\mathrm{Homo\ sapiens}$, $\mathrm{Saccharomyces\ cerevisiae}$, and $\mathrm{Escherichia\ coli}$ K-12, and tested a proxy tRNA-Leu CUN-versus-UUR log-copy spectrum.  The proxy coupling threshold was $0.01$; the break condition that all nine $|A_{ij}|<0.01$ was not met, and the largest absolute proxy entry was $1{,}882{,}464.1106377328$ with nine nonzero entries.  These numbers certify a finite proxy coupling contact, not a controlled translation-survival or RNA-stability result.  The proxy surface is organism-level tRNA-copy information, not matched ribosome occupancy, not protein output, and not transcript half-life.

\paragraph{Residual-basis evidence and its negative force.}
The broader residual-basis extraction run reinforces the boundary.  With the stated regularizer $\lambda=0.01$ and description-length penalty $\log_2(1+n_{\mathrm{col}})$, four organisms were computable and all four were classified as persistent rather than compressible: $\mathrm{Danio\ rerio}$, $\mathrm{Escherichia\ coli}$ K-12 MG1655, $\mathrm{Homo\ sapiens}$, and $\mathrm{Saccharomyces\ cerevisiae}$.  Two requested organisms, $\mathrm{Gallus\ gallus}$ and $\mathrm{Mycobacterium\ smegmatis}$ str. MC2 155, remained needs-data.  The final residual floors were high: $0.9791828971490305$ for $\mathrm{Danio\ rerio}$ after one selected readout, $0.9979478314995476$ for $\mathrm{E.\ coli}$ after two selected readouts, $0.9723341658092314$ for human after six selected readouts, and $0.9798667977250413$ for yeast after nine selected readouts.  The smallest final value was still $0.9723341658092314$, above the recorded $0.97$ persistence floor.  Thus the finite evidence says that the residual coordinates retain substantial unexplained mass after the available readout blocks; this is a scientifically useful refusal, not a failure of the coordinate construction.

\paragraph{Admissibility rule for the RNA half-life contact.}
The RNA half-life association can be used as a bounded decay-layer readout only after the same-row matrix contains the relevant $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual coordinate, RNA half-life, matched abundance or transcriptional controls, organism and sequence controls, and the rank audit.  The observed $\rho=0.126224$ is therefore recorded as a finite contact that motivates a controlled test, not as a survival score.  The admissible claim has the weaker form
$$
\begin{aligned}
\text{finite contact} &: \bigl(n_{\mathrm{human}}, n_{\mathrm{mouse}}, \rho, \text{guard}\bigr)\\
&= \bigl(16{,}500,18{,}924,0.126224,\text{non-CSC tAI}\bigr),\\
\text{survival claim} &: \text{withheld until a non-saturated held-out } S^{QT}_{ij} \text{ is computed.}
\end{aligned}
$$
This keeps codon topology, modeled adaptation weights, and transcript half-life in separate readout roles until a single finite panel joins them under the declared controls.

\paragraph{Local no-promotion boundary.}
Nothing in this record asserts translation realization, ribosome occupancy, protein abundance, protein structure, physical admissibility, biological function, mechanism, phenotype, or a universal biological law.  The three-organism proxy coupling surface is too small and too rank-sensitive for survival promotion; the human--mouse half-life contact reports a raw finite association rather than a controlled survival statistic; and the residual-basis extraction reports persistent unexplained residuals rather than a closed mechanism.  A future positive statement would require a same-organism or same-CDS matrix with codon counts, $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual labels, matched RNA half-life, matched translation readout if translation conditioning is claimed, abundance controls, the full $Z$ controls, rank sufficiency, simpler-baseline ablation, and held-out survival.  Until then, the correct readback is a rank-admissibility gate: the contact is real enough to define a test, but not yet strong enough to promote the codon residual into translation survival or decay mechanism.
