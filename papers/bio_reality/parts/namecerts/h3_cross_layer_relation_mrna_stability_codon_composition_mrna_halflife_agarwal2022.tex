\subsection{Rank-admissible codon-composition contact before translation survival}
\label{sec:bioreality-q6-rank-admissible-mrna-halflife-agarwal2022}

\paragraph{Finite row surface.}
The BioReality record for \texttt{h3.cross\_layer\_relation.mrna\_stability.codon\_composition\_mrna\_halflife\_agarwal2022} is a bounded finite-row contact between codon-composition residual coordinates and measured transcript half-life rows. The computed run \texttt{74d321d44dd7} used a non-CSC tAI circularity guard and joined $16500$ human rows with $18924$ mouse rows. The reported raw cross-row association was $\rho = 0.126224$, with runtime $46.349$ seconds. This is a real finite data contact for mRNA stability, but it is not, by itself, a translation-realization contact. The statistic is therefore read as a transcript-stability surface that can motivate a downstream gate, not as a proof that any $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual coordinate survives into ribosome occupancy, translation efficiency, protein abundance, structure, function, phenotype, or a universal rule.

\paragraph{Residual-coordinate side.}
The coordinate side is not empty: the companion $B^{*}_{Q6}$ computation records nine synonymous residual directions, namely $\mathrm{Arg\_AGR}$, $\mathrm{Ile\_AUA}$, $\mathrm{K\_AAA}$, $\mathrm{Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR}$, $\mathrm{Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG}$, $\mathrm{Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG}$, $\mathrm{Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY}$, $\mathrm{Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY}$, and $f_{3}$-stress. The codon count space has $61$ codons, and the loaded cross-organism panel for the proxy coupling calculation contained \texttt{homo\_sapiens}, \texttt{saccharomyces\_cerevisiae}, and \texttt{escherichia\_coli\_k12}. Against the tRNA-Leu CUN-versus-UUR log-copy proxy readout, the finite matrix had $9$ entries above the $0.01$ coupling threshold, with maximum absolute proxy coefficient $1882464.1106377328$. The declared break test for that proxy packet would have returned null only if all nine $q_i$ satisfied $|A_{ij}| < 0.01$; that break condition was not met. This supports the existence of a codon-to-proxy coupling contact. It does not certify a controlled survival entry $S^{QT}_{ij}$.

\paragraph{Rank gate.}
For a proposed translation-survival entry, the admissible object is the residualized row system obtained after removing the control columns $Z$: amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, coding length, M-density baseline, and simpler codon baselines when used. If $q_i^{\perp Z}$ denotes the residualized coordinate and $T_j^{\perp Z}$ denotes the residualized translation readout, the admissibility condition is a finite-rank condition on the design used to estimate $S^{QT}_{ij}$:
$$
\begin{aligned}
S^{QT}_{ij} \text{ is reportable only if }&\ \operatorname{rank}(X_{ij}^{\perp Z}) < n_{ij}^{\perp Z},\\
&\ \operatorname{df}_{ij}^{\perp Z} > 0,\\
&\ \text{and a held-out translation readout can falsify the fitted contact.}
\end{aligned}
$$
A perfect in-sample surface, including an all-$1.0$ fitted score on an underpowered panel, is therefore interpreted as rank saturation unless independent held-out rows remain. The $n=3$ proxy coupling panel is useful as a warning example: it can show a nonzero modeled contact, but it is too small to carry a translation-survival interpretation after the named controls. In that case the correct label is coupling-only, rank-degenerate, or needs more data, not positive translation survival.

\paragraph{Transcript-stability contact.}
The Agarwal-style half-life surface supplies a relevant mRNA-stability readout because codon composition can be associated with transcript persistence. In this record, the observed finite-row quantity is the raw half-life association $\rho = 0.126224$ under the non-CSC tAI circularity guard, not a residualized $S^{QT}$ entry. The guard matters: a tAI-derived quantity that is built from codon usage cannot be allowed to re-enter the design as if it were an independent measurement of translation. The finite-row mRNA result can be used as a control, comparator, or negative boundary for later translation tests. It cannot be promoted into ribosome occupancy or translation efficiency without rows that actually measure those surfaces.

\paragraph{Residual extraction context.}
The residual-basis extraction run \texttt{c1ef9b455187} gives a second reason to keep the gate strict. With the stated regularization $\lambda = 0.01$ and description-length term $\log_2(1+n_{\mathrm{col}})$, the computed organisms remained persistent rather than compressible. The recorded $\rho$ curves were $[1.0,0.9791828971490305]$ for \texttt{danio\_rerio}, $[1.0,0.9982293045445091,0.9979478314995476]$ for \texttt{escherichia\_coli\_k12\_mg1655}, $[1.0,0.9852198225375993,0.9806696939851618,0.9792537895870558,0.9787609432955213,0.9787233550315274,0.9723341658092314]$ for \texttt{homo\_sapiens}, and $[1.0,0.9962498989663637,0.9929843170647626,0.9915730985485388,0.9903314906684438,0.9894054695002641,0.9885902293718664,0.9882386501212531,0.9881524582851271,0.9798667977250413]$ for \texttt{saccharomyces\_cerevisiae}. The minimum final value among the persistent organisms was $0.9723341658092314$, with computed count $4$ and no compressible organism listed. These values say that substantial residual structure remains after the available readout blocks. They do not identify a mechanism; they instead justify requiring an explicit matched translation readout before any cross-layer promotion.

\paragraph{Decision rule.}
The chapter-level rule is local and finite: an $S^{QT}_{ij}$ entry may be called translation survival only when the same organism- or CDS-indexed rows contain codon counts, a direct translation readout such as ribosome profiling or translation efficiency, and the declared controls $Z$, and when the residualized design has non-saturated rank with positive degrees of freedom. If that condition fails, the entry remains a codon-composition, tRNA-copy, tAI/stAI, or mRNA-stability contact depending on which rows are actually present. The finite half-life result with $n_{\mathrm{human}}=16500$, $n_{\mathrm{mouse}}=18924$, and $\rho=0.126224$ is admissible as transcript-stability evidence and as a control surface. It is not admissible as evidence for translation realization.

\paragraph{Local boundary.}
No conclusion about translation, ribosome occupancy, protein abundance, folded structure, physical admissibility, biological function, mechanism, phenotype, adaptation, causation, or universality follows from this packet alone. Those claims require their own layer-matched contacts and their own residualized statistics. The present record is therefore a rank-admissibility gate around a real mRNA half-life contact: it keeps the finite codon-composition signal visible while refusing to convert proxy coupling, modeled adaptation weights, or transcript-stability association into a translation-survival claim.
