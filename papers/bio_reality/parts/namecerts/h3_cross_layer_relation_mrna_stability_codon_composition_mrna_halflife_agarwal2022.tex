\paragraph{Rank gate.}
For an entry $S^{QT}_{ij}$ to count as translation-to-stability survival, both $q_i$ and the readout $T_j$ must be residualized against $Z$, and the post-control design must leave enough independent degrees of freedom for a held-out falsification.  The admissibility condition is local to each proposed entry:
$$
\begin{aligned}
 \operatorname{rank}(X_{q_i,T_j \mid Z}) &< n_{\mathrm{rows}},\\
 \operatorname{df}(X_{q_i,T_j \mid Z}) &> 0,\\
 S^{QT}_{ij} &> 0\quad \text{only if the held-out score survives the same controls.}
\end{aligned}
$$
A saturated all-in-sample fit, including a fitted score equal to $1.0$, is therefore rejected as a projection artifact rather than promoted to a biological survival statement.  The current half-life row records $\rho_{\mathrm{raw}} = 0.126224$ but does not provide the per-coordinate residualized $S^{QT}_{ij}$ matrix, the post-control rank table, held-out scores, or p-values for rank-admissible entries.  Consequently the half-life observation can be cited only as a bounded RNA-stability contact requiring a stricter survival calculation.

\paragraph{Local readback.}
The chapter therefore reads the finite evidence as follows.  There is a real bounded half-life panel with $16500$ human and $18924$ mouse rows, a recorded raw correlation of $0.126224$, and a non-CSC tAI circularity guard.  There is also a persistent residual-basis record in four computed organisms, with $\lambda=0.01$ and final residual curves remaining above $0.97$.  These facts justify keeping the codon-composition-to-RNA-stability question open and scientifically specific.  They do not justify declaring that any $B^{*}_{Q6}$ coordinate has survived as a translation realization, RNA decay mechanism, protein output, structural order, physical admissibility, biological function, phenotype, or universal biological law.

\paragraph{Boundary.}
The allowed statement is narrow: codon-composition residuals have a finite RNA half-life contact and a guarded raw association, while the rank-admissible survival matrix remains uncomputed.  A positive promotion would require same-organism or same-CDS rows joining $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates, measured translation readouts such as ribosome occupancy or translation efficiency, matched mRNA abundance, RNA half-life, amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, length, M-density, batch or dataset controls where relevant, simpler-codon baselines, post-control rank, and held-out survival.  Without that joined finite panel, no claim follows about translation, folding, physical admissibility, function, mechanism, causation, phenotype, or universality.
