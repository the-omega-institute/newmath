\subsection{Rank-Admissible Translation Survival at the mRNA Half-Life Interface}
\label{sec:bioreality-q6-rank-admissible-mrna-halflife}

\paragraph{Claim surface.}
This record concerns the proposed passage from synonymous $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinates to a translation-layer survival entry near the mRNA half-life readout. The biological contact is not a general claim that codon composition determines stability. It is a finite-row audit of whether a proposed entry $S^{QT}_{ij}$ is even admissible after the coordinate $q_i$ and the translation readout $T_j$ have both been residualized against the declared controls $Z$: amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, coding-sequence length, and M-density. The tested Agarwal-style mRNA stability contact contains $16500$ human rows and $18924$ mouse rows, with a recorded raw Spearman contact $\rho = 0.126224$, a non-CSC tAI circularity guard, and a runtime of $46.349$ seconds. These numbers are sufficient for a bounded association surface, but they do not by themselves certify translation survival.

\paragraph{Admissibility rule.}
The gate separates three objects that are easy to conflate: a codon-topology residual coordinate, a modeled or observed translation readout, and a downstream RNA half-life readout. A proposed survival entry is retained only when the post-control design has non-saturated rank and enough independent rows to make held-out failure possible. In local notation the intended test is not the in-sample fitted score alone, but the residualized and rank-audited surface
$$
\begin{aligned}
q_i^\perp &= q_i - \Pi_Z q_i,\\
T_j^\perp &= T_j - \Pi_Z T_j,\\
S^{QT}_{ij} &> 0 \quad \text{only if the held-out residual design is non-saturated.}
\end{aligned}
$$
If the residualized row space is saturated, or if the degrees of freedom collapse after $Z$, the entry is refused as a survival statement even when a fitted score is positive or equals $1.0$. The local readback is therefore a certificate of testability: residualized rank, remaining degrees of freedom, held-out translation boundary, and the explicit break condition that turns an apparent fit into a null result.

\paragraph{Finite contacts already present.}
A separate codon-topology coupling computation supplies a bounded proxy contact rather than survival. It used $61$ codon-count coordinates across Homo sapiens, Saccharomyces cerevisiae, and Escherichia coli K12, and computed nine synonymous residual axes: Arg\_AGR, Ile\_AUA, K\_AAA, Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR, Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG, Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG, Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY, Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY, and $f_3$-stress. The proxy readout was the tRNA-Leu CUN-versus-UUR log-copy spectrum. The coupling break condition would have set the packet null if all nine entries had $|A_{ij}| < 0.01$; that break condition was not met, and the maximum recorded absolute coupling was $1882464.1106377328$, with $9$ nonzero entries above the $0.01$ threshold. This is evidence that the coordinate packet can touch a translation-adjacent proxy. It is not evidence that any $S^{QT}_{ij}$ entry survives the rank-admissible gate.

\paragraph{Null and support behavior.}
The half-life contact is allowed to remain as an RNA-stability surface only if the translation step is kept separate. The recorded human and mouse row counts and the raw $\rho = 0.126224$ establish a finite association contact under the non-CSC tAI guard. They do not establish ribosome occupancy, translation efficiency, protein abundance, RNA-decay mechanism, folding, physical admissibility, biological function, phenotype, causation, or a universal rule. A positive translation-survival claim would require organism- or CDS-matched translation rows, a non-saturated residual design after $Z$, held-out evaluation, and a statistic that remains positive after simpler codon baselines are ablated. If those conditions fail, the correct output is coupling-only or needs-data.

\paragraph{Neighboring finite-row checks.}
Two nearby cross-layer controls illustrate the standard of evidence required before promotion. The branchpoint motif usage contact joined $10584$ rows and used expression control from UCSC hg19 GTEx v8 with a runtime of $138.47155$ seconds. The uORF/Kozak ribosome-load contact recorded $41862$ fixed rows, $16739$ varying rows, two-sample concordance, and a runtime of $131.941$ seconds. These entries are useful because they show that the BioReality table can carry large finite panels with explicit controls, but neither supplies the missing same-row translation-survival statistic for the $B^*_{Q6}$ mRNA half-life gate.

\paragraph{Local boundary.}
The chapter therefore records a rank-admissibility boundary, not a mechanism. The available finite rows support a controlled mRNA half-life association and a separate proxy translation-adjacent coupling surface. They do not license promotion from $B^*_{Q6}$ residual geometry to translation realization. No conclusion about translation, folded structure, physical admissibility, molecular function, biological mechanism, phenotype, or universality follows unless an additional layer-matched contact supplies the relevant measured rows and the rank-audited statistic survives the declared controls.
