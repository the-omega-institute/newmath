\paragraph{Rank gate.}
For a proposed entry $S^{QT}_{ij}$, let $q_i^{\perp Z}$ denote the residualized $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ coordinate and let $T_j^{\perp Z}$ denote the residualized translation readout after the same control matrix $Z$ has been removed.  The entry is admissible only when the remaining design has non-saturated rank and enough independent row support for a held-out test.  The local rule is
$$
\begin{aligned}
\operatorname{Admissible}(i,j) &=
\bigl(\operatorname{rank}([Z,q_i,T_j])>\operatorname{rank}(Z)\bigr)\\
&\quad\wedge\bigl(\operatorname{df}_{ij}>0\bigr)\\
&\quad\wedge\bigl(\text{held-out translation readout is defined}\bigr).
\end{aligned}
$$
If this predicate fails, then any all-1.0 fitted surface, rank-saturated projection, or positive in-sample coupling remains outside survival notation.  The packet may record it as coupling-only, or as requiring more rows, but it may not report a positive $S^{QT}_{ij}$.

The present entry is ready as a rank-admissibility and refusal rule, not as a positive survival result.  It certifies the following finite decision: proxy $A^{QT}$ coupling is available, residual $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ structure persists in several computed organism panels, and RNA half-life rows exist for human and mouse, but $S^{QT}_{ij}$ remains unreported unless the residualized post-control design is non-saturated and held-out translation readout rows exist.  No conclusion about ribosome occupancy, translation realization, protein abundance, folded structure, physical admissibility, molecular function, mechanism, phenotype, causation, or universality follows from this packet.
