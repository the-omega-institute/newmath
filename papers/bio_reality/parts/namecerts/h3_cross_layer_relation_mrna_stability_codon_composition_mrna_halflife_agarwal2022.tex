\subsection{Rank-admissible translation gate before RNA half-life readback}
\label{subsec:bior-reality-q6-rank-admissible-translation-half-life}

\paragraph{Question.}
This record isolates a narrow cross-layer question for the $B^*_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates.  A codon-composition signal may be visible against RNA half-life, and a modeled translation-weight contact may also be present, but neither fact is by itself a translation-survival certificate.  The admissible object is a finite residualized design: rows are organism- or transcript-indexed observations, columns include $B^*_{Q6}$ coordinates and controls $Z$, and the tested readout is a translation-facing surface $T_j$ kept before protein abundance, folded structure, molecular function, phenotype, or mechanism.  The target entry $S^{QT}_{ij}$ is therefore allowed to exist only when the post-control problem has non-saturated rank and enough independent rows to make held-out failure possible.

\paragraph{Finite observations.}
The RNA half-life contact for the codon-composition packet was computed in run \texttt{74d321d44dd7}.  The joined finite panel contains $n_{\mathrm{human}}=16500$ human rows and $n_{\mathrm{mouse}}=18924$ mouse rows, with a reported raw rank correlation $\rho=0.126224$ and runtime $46.349$ seconds.  The circularity guard is recorded as non-CSC tAI, so the modeled adaptation weights are not allowed to be silently identified with codon-stability coefficients.  In symbols, the observed finite readback is only
$$
\begin{aligned}
(n_{\mathrm{human}},n_{\mathrm{mouse}},\rho_{\mathrm{raw}})
  &= (16500,18924,0.126224),\\
\mathrm{guard} &= \mathrm{tAI\ non\mbox{-}CSC}.
\end{aligned}
$$
This is enough to document a bounded association surface between codon composition and RNA half-life, but it is not yet the same object as a controlled $B^*_{Q6}\to T$ translation-survival matrix.

This establishes contact at the level of proxy coupling $A^{QT}$, not survival $S^{QT}$.

\paragraph{Rank gate.}
The admissibility rule is local to each proposed entry.  Let $q_i$ be one residual coordinate and let $T_j$ be a translation readout, modeled or measured.  After residualizing both against $Z$, the entry can be tested only if the remaining row space is not saturated and if a held-out comparison is meaningful.  The gate can be written as
$$
\begin{aligned}
\widetilde q_i &= q_i - \Pi_Z q_i,\\
\widetilde T_j &= T_j - \Pi_Z T_j,\\
S^{QT}_{ij} &> 0
  \quad\text{only if}\quad
  \operatorname{rank}(\widetilde X_{ij}) < n_{ij}
  \quad\text{and held-out loss can increase.}
\end{aligned}
$$
When these conditions fail, an in-sample fitted score, including a perfect value caused by too few rows, is treated as rank saturation rather than biological survival.  Thus the three-organism proxy surface can motivate a test, but it cannot certify translation realization.

\paragraph{Residual-basis context.}
The broader residual-basis extraction confirms why the gate is needed.  Four organisms were computed with persistent residual labels: \texttt{danio\_rerio}, \texttt{escherichia\_coli\_k12\_mg1655}, \texttt{homo\_sapiens}, and \texttt{saccharomyces\_cerevisiae}; \texttt{gallus\_gallus} and \texttt{mycobacterium\_smegmatis\_str\_mc2\_155} remain needs-data cases.  The final residual floors remain high, with minimum final $\rho=0.9723341658092314$ among the computed organisms.  Human has $n=16008$ joined rows, six selected candidates, $d_{\mathrm{modeled}}=0.9020707321191321$, and $d_{\mathrm{difference}}=0.018824203448813415$.  Yeast has $n=3046$ joined rows, nine selected candidates, $d_{\mathrm{modeled}}=0.2199040339633322$, and $d_{\mathrm{difference}}=0.008171838236265161$.  These numbers say that the residual geometry is not exhausted by the available readout blocks; they do not say that a particular translation entry has survived controls.

The execution-selection decomposition gives an additional finite-row guardrail.  In \texttt{saccharomyces\_cerevisiae}, the active gene set has $n=5684$ rows, with $P_Q$ squared norm $864.0124192674945$, residual squared norm $2811.4213259512776$, base $R^2_{QP}=0.30732228260918726$ on the active set, and base $R^2_{QP}=0.29837624994140827$ on the full gene set.  The fold count is $5$, the permutation count is $240$, and the recorded verdict is \texttt{composition\_artifact}.  This reinforces the local rule: residual-coordinate strength and cross-layer contact can coexist with a null or artifact verdict once controls and held-out comparisons are enforced.

\paragraph{Permitted conclusion.}
The chapter-level conclusion is a rank-admissibility statement, not a biological promotion.  The available finite rows support a bounded codon-composition and RNA half-life association, plus a proxy coupling contact between $B^*_{Q6}$ coordinates and modeled translation weights.  They also support a refusal rule: no entry $S^{QT}_{ij}$ should be reported from a saturated residualized design, from an underpowered row space, or from a modeled readout that lacks a nondegenerate held-out survival statistic.  The present packet therefore records a testable gate for translation-facing survival before RNA half-life interpretation.

\paragraph{Local boundary.}
Nothing in this record establishes measured ribosome occupancy, protein abundance, folded structure, physical admissibility, molecular function, cellular phenotype, causal mechanism, or a universal biological law.  A later claim at any of those layers would need its own matched finite rows and its own control set.  In particular, tAI or stAI weights remain modeled translation-adaptation readbacks; codon-usage tables remain sequence-composition summaries; and RNA half-life rows remain RNA-stability readouts unless a same-row translation statistic is computed and passes the rank-admissible gate.
