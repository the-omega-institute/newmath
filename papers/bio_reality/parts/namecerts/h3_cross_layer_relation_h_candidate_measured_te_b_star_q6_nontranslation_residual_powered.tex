\paragraph{Local verdict.}
This subsection is ready as a bounded descriptive contact.  The finite table shows that measured ribosome-profiling TE can be joined to the nine-coordinate $B^*_{Q6}$ residual surface under amino-acid composition, GC3, length, $M$-density, and matched mRNA controls, and it records small positive measured-TE absorptions in all four organism panels.  The result is intentionally narrow: it does not establish translation realization in general, protein abundance, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, organismal phenotype, causal mechanism, or universality.  Those claims require separate layer-matched contacts and their own controlled statistics.  If all controlled entries in a later survival matrix are non-positive, the $Q6$ residual basis remains outside measured translation-efficiency realization for that contact; if one entry is positive, the licensed conclusion is still only a controlled measured-TE survival entry for the stated organism, join, and covariate boundary.
