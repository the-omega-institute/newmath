\paragraph{Finite surface.}

$$
\begin{aligned}

&Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY,\ Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG,\ Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY,\ f3\_stress).

The declared covariate block has 24 columns before rank reduction: an intercept, $\log(\mathrm{cds\_len\_nt})$, the 20 amino-acid composition proportions $A,C,D,E,F,G,H,I,K,L,M,N,P,Q,R,S,T,V,W,Y$, GC3 computed from real CDS codon counts, and an $M$-density baseline equal to the fraction of sense codons lying on the union support of the nine projected $B^*_{Q6}$ vectors.  In every organism listed below the numerical rank of this block is $23$, so the tested projection is not the all-columns-independent formal shape but the finite rank-$23$ residual surface actually present in the data.

\paragraph{Local verdict.}
The finite evidence supports the following bounded statement.  On the joined ribosome-profiling TE surfaces, measured TE can be projected against the modeled-tAI-complement residual after the declared coding-sequence controls, and the resulting absorption values are positive but small in all four organisms.  The numerical contact is strongest in zebrafish and weakest in yeast.  The join-stability failures in zebrafish and human prevent promotion to a stable cross-organism residual-explanation claim.  The result should therefore be read as a measured translation-efficiency boundary for the $B^*_{Q6}$ residual complement: it records a finite projection of ribosome-profiling TE onto a residual coordinate surface, while leaving the non-translation residual substantially open.

\paragraph{No-promotion boundary.}
No protein abundance, folding, structural order, physical admissibility, molecular function, organismal phenotype, mechanism, causation, or universality follows from these TE absorption entries.  Ribosome-profiling TE is a condition-specific translation-efficiency readout, and the absorbed fraction is a descriptive projection.  A stronger claim would require a separate layer-matched contact and its own finite statistic: protein abundance for protein-output claims, structural or physical assays for structure and admissibility, perturbation or functional assays for mechanism and function, and phylogenetically controlled or otherwise powered panels before any global biological law is asserted.
