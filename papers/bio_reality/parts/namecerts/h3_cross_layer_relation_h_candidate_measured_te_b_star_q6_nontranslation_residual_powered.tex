\subsection{Measured translation-efficiency contact for the $B^*_{Q6}$ residual complement}
\label{sec:bioreality-q6-measured-te-contact}

\paragraph{Finite readout surface.}
This record tests whether the $B^*_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates leave a measurable trace in ribosome-profiling translation-efficiency readouts after the transcription layer has been explicitly held in the control set.  The finite surface consists of nine residual coordinates,
$K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $Leu_{CUN\,vs\,UUR}$, $Leu_{UUA\,vs\,UUG}$, $Ser_{UCR\,vs\,AGY}$, $Ser_{UCA\,vs\,UCG}$, $Thr_{ACR\,vs\,ACY}$, and $f_3$-stress, joined to per-CDS translation-efficiency rows where such rows are available.  The control block has $24$ columns and rank $23$ in each measured-TE organism; it contains an intercept, $\log(\mathrm{cds\_len\_nt})$, the twenty amino-acid composition proportions computed from real CDS codon counts, GC3, and the $M$-density baseline given by the fraction of sense codons on the union support of the nine projected $B^*_{Q6}$ vectors.  The mRNA abundance term is not treated as a translation readout.  It is the transcription-layer boundary needed before a TE association can be read as translation-efficiency contact rather than abundance carry-over.

\paragraph{Joined measured-TE panels.}
The measured translation-efficiency join is large enough to avoid the small-panel refusal that affected earlier rank-degenerate surfaces.  The four organism panels are: $n=13140$ for zebrafish, with $14410$ CDS-abundance rows and $13140$ valid TE rows; $n=3415$ for \textit{Escherichia coli} K-12 MG1655, with $3739$ CDS-abundance rows, $3747$ valid abundance rows, and $3415$ valid TE rows; $n=11554$ for human, with $19053$ CDS-abundance rows, $19483$ valid abundance rows, and $11554$ valid TE rows; and $n=5128$ for budding yeast, with $5815$ CDS-abundance rows, $6351$ valid abundance rows, and $5128$ valid TE rows.  Thus the contact is not an organism-level codon-usage summary alone: it is a CDS-matched translation-efficiency surface constrained by composition, length, GC3, $M$-density, organism identity within the panel, and matched mRNA abundance.

\paragraph{Measured-TE absorption values.}
The tested quantity is descriptive absorption of the modeled-tAI-complement abundance residual by residualized measured TE.  The measured-TE absorption fractions are small in all four panels:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{abs}_{TE}(\mathrm{zebrafish}) &= 0.01759429001506974, & d_{combined} &= 0.8276090159036916,\\
\mathrm{abs}_{TE}(\mathrm{E.coli}) &= 0.0010982956489726373, & d_{combined} &= 0.6620871850159149,\\
\mathrm{abs}_{TE}(\mathrm{human}) &= 0.0028947149257963897, & d_{combined} &= 0.854133502300174,\\
\mathrm{abs}_{TE}(\mathrm{yeast}) &= 0.0002762783754375593, & d_{combined} &= 0.2237928123367028.
\end{aligned}
$$
The corresponding modeled-complement residual fractions on the measured-TE joins are $d_{modeled}=0.8424312412078521$ for zebrafish, $0.6628211177352172$ for \textit{E. coli}, $0.8566190269974241$ for human, and $0.22386566043976477$ for yeast.  In norm terms, the projected $Q$ energies and modeled-complement energies are $(1092.0114815505467,919.9445878158525)$ for zebrafish, $(393.78692095732885,261.0102870984463)$ for \textit{E. coli}, $(565.3850201235074,484.31956581711796)$ for human, and $(675.3275479192903,151.18264752811882)$ for yeast.  These numbers support a bounded statement: measured TE accounts for a computed fraction of the modeled-complement residual, but the fraction is modest and is not a causal mediation estimate.

\paragraph{Join-stability self-check.}
The measured-TE rows do not always preserve the full abundance-CDS join used by the complement experiment.  For zebrafish, the current measured-TE join has $13140$ rows rather than the $14410$ complement-reference rows, and the modeled-complement fraction shifts from $0.874628721049266$ to $0.8424312412078521$, an absolute difference of $0.03219747984141397$, exceeding the $0.02$ stability tolerance.  Human has the largest join change: $11554$ measured-TE rows against $19053$ complement-reference rows, moving from $0.9208949355679455$ to $0.8566190269974241$, an absolute difference of $0.06427590857052146$.  By contrast, \textit{E. coli} remains stable within tolerance, with $3415$ current rows against $3739$ reference rows and an absolute difference of $0.00017488612263594217$; yeast also remains within tolerance, with $5128$ current rows against $5815$ reference rows and an absolute difference of $0.012133464712697745$.  The finite conclusion is therefore organism-sensitive: the TE contact is computed, but two organism joins alter the residual target enough that the chapter reports the changed surface rather than pretending that all four organisms share the same abundance-CDS baseline.

\paragraph{Relation to other measured or bounded contacts.}
The measured-tRNA tAI comparison supplies a separate bounded readback, not a replacement for ribosome-profiling TE.  On full CDS joins, tRNA-gene-copy tAI absorption of the modeled complement is $0.020817102845637092$ for zebrafish, $5.426229300865148\times 10^{-5}$ for human, and $0.001530747712165342$ for yeast, with zero join drift relative to the complement reference in those three panels.  The yeast turnover contact is still smaller, absorbing $0.0009086031889966327$ of the modeled-complement residual on $3640$ joined half-life rows, with $d_{modeled}=0.2232704509412724$ and an absolute complement-reference difference of $0.011538255214205373$.  Protein-feature candidates are sparse and annotation-dependent: zebrafish has only $2242$ joined feature rows from $3180$ records, while human has $18335$ joined rows from $18842$ records and yeast has $5682$ joined rows from $6156$ records.  Some Bonferroni comparisons exceed their null thresholds, including zebrafish PTM density with observed absorption $0.014211240113472575$ against threshold $0.003775603188484381$, and human complex membership, PTM density, and transmembrane count with absorptions $0.0075271999877804495$, $0.014884825920540636$, and $0.0015569064882873212$ against thresholds $0.0006605662314432479$, $0.00042094892238120357$, and $0.0002697448894057371$.  Those feature contacts do not retroactively turn measured TE into a protein-state, interaction, localization, or function result.

\paragraph{Cross-organism context.}
A separate organism-specificity survey gives the broader caution for this contact.  Across $12$ complete-feature organisms, the dominant-coordinate-by-domain contingency has Pearson chi-square $9.399999999999999$, Cramer's $V=0.6258327785172862$, and right-tail permutation value $p=0.5814185814185814$ over $1000$ trials, so the apparent domain pattern is not a powered domain-resolution claim.  The $M_{QTP}$ fraction has Pearson $r=-0.3062616500469447$ against GC3 with two-sided permutation $p=0.35264735264735264$, $r=-0.033628115789504955$ against log proteome size with $p=0.913086913086913$, and $r=0.06029405206037823$ against log tRNA-gene count with $p=0.8621378621378621$.  The domain variance-ratio test gives $0.8889980452277374$ with right-tail permutation $p=0.4155844155844156$.  These values keep the measured-TE result in the status of a finite contact calculation, not a cross-domain law.

\paragraph{Local interpretation.}
The ready claim is that a matched, rank-powered measured-TE calculation has been performed for the nine $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinates under the declared controls, and that the measured-TE absorption values are bounded and small: about $1.76\%$ in zebrafish, $0.11\%$ in \textit{E. coli}, $0.29\%$ in human, and $0.028\%$ in yeast.  Since the absorption is descriptive, it does not establish causality, mediation, or mechanism.  Since two measured-TE joins change the modeled-complement target beyond the stated tolerance, the strongest honest read is that measured TE is a tested H-candidate contact but does not close the non-translation residual.  No translation mechanism, protein abundance, folding, physical admissibility, biological function, phenotype, or universal biological rule follows from these entries unless a separate layer-matched contact supplies the relevant rows and statistic.
