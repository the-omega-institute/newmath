\subsection{Measured translation-efficiency contact for the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual complement}\label{sec:bior-h3-q6-measured-te-nontranslation-residual}\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite surface.}
The readout surface for this packet is a finite CDS-by-organism matrix in which nine $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates are tested against matched ribosome-profiling translation-efficiency values after controlling the transcript-abundance denominator.  The coordinate list is
$K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $Leu_{CUN\,vs\,UUR}$, $Leu_{UUA\,vs\,UUG}$, $Ser_{UCR\,vs\,AGY}$, $Ser_{UCA\,vs\,UCG}$, $Thr_{ACR\,vs\,ACY}$, and $f_3$-stress.  The covariate block used for the residualization contains an intercept, $\log(\mathrm{cds\_len\_nt})$, the twenty amino-acid composition proportions $A,C,D,E,F,G,H,I,K,L,M,N,P,Q,R,S,T,V,W,Y$, GC3, and the $M$-density baseline, where $M$-density is the fraction of sense codons lying on the union support of the nine projected $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ vectors.  The reported control-column count is $24$, while the numerical rank of the control matrix is $23$ in every organism.  The joined readout sizes are $13140$ CDS rows for \emph{Danio rerio}, $3415$ rows for \emph{Escherichia coli} K-12 MG1655, $11554$ rows for \emph{Homo sapiens}, and $5128$ rows for \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}.  These counts are not interchangeable with the larger CDS-abundance joins: the corresponding CDS joins before the measured-TE restriction are $14410$, $3739$, $19053$, and $5815$ rows.

\paragraph{Projection statistic.}
The measured-TE test is not a direct codon-to-protein claim.  It asks how much of the modeled-tAI-complement abundance residual $\widehat P_{Q\perp T_{mod}}$ is absorbed when residualized measured translation efficiency is added to the same finite surface.  The organism-specific modeled-complement fractions on the measured-TE joins are
$$
\begin{aligned}
 d_{\mathrm{modeled}}(\mathrm{Danio}) &= 0.8424312412078521,\\
 d_{\mathrm{modeled}}(\mathrm{E.coli}) &= 0.6628211177352172,\\
 d_{\mathrm{modeled}}(\mathrm{human}) &= 0.8566190269974241,\\
 d_{\mathrm{modeled}}(\mathrm{yeast}) &= 0.22386566043976477.
\end{aligned}
$$
The corresponding measured-TE absorbed fractions are
$$
\begin{aligned}
 a_{\mathrm{measTE}}(\mathrm{Danio}) &= 0.01759429001506974,\\
 a_{\mathrm{measTE}}(\mathrm{E.coli}) &= 0.0010982956489726373,\\
 a_{\mathrm{measTE}}(\mathrm{human}) &= 0.0028947149257963897,\\
 a_{\mathrm{measTE}}(\mathrm{yeast}) &= 0.0002762783754375593.
\end{aligned}
$$
After adding the measured-TE axis, the combined remaining fractions are $0.8276090159036916$, $0.6620871850159149$, $0.854133502300174$, and $0.2237928123367028$ for \emph{Danio}, \emph{E. coli}, human, and yeast respectively.  Thus the measured-TE contact is numerically present but weak as an absorber of the modeled-tAI-complement residual in this finite panel.  The strongest measured-TE absorption is the \emph{Danio} value, about $1.76\%$ of the modeled complement, while the yeast value is below $0.03\%$.

\paragraph{Join stability.}
The experiment carries an internal check comparing the modeled-complement fraction on the measured-TE join against the full abundance-CDS complement reference.  The absolute differences are $0.03219747984141397$ for \emph{Danio}, $0.00017488612263594217$ for \emph{E. coli}, $0.06427590857052146$ for human, and $0.012133464712697745$ for yeast.  With the stated $0.02$ tolerance, \emph{E. coli} and yeast pass the join-stability check, while \emph{Danio} and human do not.  This matters mathematically: the test is powered enough to compute the projection, but the measured-TE availability filter changes the residual target in two of the four organisms.  The appropriate conclusion is therefore a bounded descriptive statement about the measured-TE projection on the observed joins, not a stable cross-organism explanation of the non-translation residual.

\paragraph{Relation to adjacent contacts.}
The measured-tRNA-gene-copy tAI contact gives a useful comparison because it uses the full abundance-CDS joins in \emph{Danio}, human, and yeast.  There the current join sizes are $14410$, $19053$, and $5815$, matching the complement references exactly, and the measured-tRNA absorbed fractions are $0.020817102845637092$, $0.00005426229300865148$, and $0.001530747712165342$ respectively.  The tRNA proxy is therefore also a small descriptive absorber, not a replacement for measured ribosome occupancy.  A separate yeast turnover contact joins $3640$ rows, passes the complement-reference tolerance with absolute difference $0.011538255214205373$, and absorbs only $0.0009086031889966327$ of the modeled complement.  These adjacent surfaces agree with the measured-TE result in one important respect: none of the available single contacts absorbs enough of $\widehat P_{Q\perp T_{mod}}$ to close the residual as translation, tRNA supply, or turnover.

\paragraph{Organism-level context.}
The cross-organism meta table remains exploratory rather than explanatory.  Its complete-feature subset has $12$ organisms, while four organisms still need data for the $M^{QTP}$ summary.  The dominant-coordinate contingency test gives $\chi^2=9.399999999999999$, Cramer's $V=0.6258327785172862$, and a right-tail permutation value $p=0.5814185814185814$ over $1000$ permutations.  The continuous proxy checks also do not resolve a cross-organism driver: $M_{QTP}$ fraction versus GC3 has $r=-0.3062616500469447$ with two-sided permutation value $p=0.35264735264735264$, versus log proteome size has $r=-0.033628115789504955$ with $p=0.913086913086913$, and versus log tRNA-gene count has $r=0.06029405206037823$ with $p=0.8621378621378621$.  These values keep the organism-specific variation visible while preventing a domain, GC3, proteome-size, or tRNA-pool narrative from being promoted to a general law.

\paragraph{Local verdict.}
The finite computation supports a narrow statement: measured ribosome-profiling translation efficiency can be joined to the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual surface in four organisms under amino-acid composition, length, GC3, $M$-density, and matched mRNA-abundance controls, and the resulting projection quantities are small but computable.  It does not support the stronger claim that $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates are realized as a translation mechanism, nor that the remaining residual is explained by the measured-TE axis.  If all controlled coordinate entries are non-positive in a stricter per-coordinate survival matrix, the $Q6$ residual basis remains outside the measured translation-efficiency contact for this packet.  Even where an absorbed fraction is positive, it is a descriptive projection on finite joined rows, not a causal mediation test.  No translation realization, folding statement, physical-admissibility statement, functional statement, mechanism, phenotype, or universality follows here unless a separate layer-matched contact supplies the relevant finite rows and statistic.
