\subsection{Measured translation-efficiency contact for the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual complement}
\label{sec:bioreality-q6-measured-te-residual-complement}

\paragraph{Finite readout surface.}
This record tests a bounded cross-layer contact between the nine $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates and measured ribosome-profiling translation efficiency.  The rows are coding-sequence entries joined by organism and protein identifier to codon-derived covariates, measured translation-efficiency values, and matched mRNA abundance controls.  The residual coordinates are
$K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $Leu_{CUN\_vs\_UUR}$, $Leu_{UUA\_vs\_UUG}$, $Ser_{UCR\_vs\_AGY}$, $Ser_{UCA\_vs\_UCG}$, $Thr_{ACR\_vs\_ACY}$, and $f3_{stress}$.
The control block contains an intercept, $\log(\mathrm{cds\_len\_nt})$, twenty amino-acid composition proportions computed from real CDS codon counts, GC3, and an $M$-density baseline defined on the union support of the nine projected $B^{*}_{Q6}$ vectors.  With the amino-acid simplex dependence present, the reported control-column count is $24$ and the effective control rank is $23$ in every organism-specific fit.

\paragraph{Joined panels and rank condition.}
The measured-TE panel has four organism-specific joined surfaces.  In \textit{Danio rerio}, $n=13140$ CDS rows with valid TE are joined against $14410$ CDS-abundance rows, with $14410$ valid abundance identifiers and $\operatorname{rank}(Z)=23$.  In \textit{Escherichia coli} K-12 MG1655, $n=3415$ TE rows are joined against $3739$ reported CDS rows, with $3747$ valid abundance identifiers and $\operatorname{rank}(Z)=23$.  In \textit{Homo sapiens}, $n=11554$ TE rows are joined against $19053$ reported CDS rows, with $19483$ valid abundance identifiers and $\operatorname{rank}(Z)=23$.  In \textit{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}, $n=5128$ TE rows are joined against $5815$ reported CDS rows, with $6351$ valid abundance identifiers and $\operatorname{rank}(Z)=23$.  Thus the tested surfaces are not three-row saturated summaries: each measured-TE fit has many more rows than control degrees of freedom, and the finite-rank warning is instead a join-shift warning, described below.

\paragraph{Statistic.}
Let $P_Q$ denote the abundance-side component carried by the nine $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates after the declared controls, and let $P_{Q\perp Tmod}$ denote the modeled-tAI-complement component on the same joined surface.  The measured-TE contact projects this complement against residualized measured translation efficiency.  The descriptive absorption reported for organism $o$ is the finite-row fraction by which the complement norm is reduced after adding measured TE to the residualized readout block:
$$
\begin{aligned}
  A_{TE}(o) &= 1 - \frac{d_{combined}(o)}{d_{modeled}(o)} .
\end{aligned}
$$
The experiment reports this quantity as a descriptive projection only; it is not a causal mediation estimate and it is not a general biological law.

\paragraph{Measured-TE results.}
For \textit{D. rerio}, $\|P_Q\|^2=1092.0114815505467$, $\|P_{Q\perp Tmod}\|^2=919.9445878158525$, and $d_{modeled}=0.8424312412078521$.  Adding measured TE gives $d_{combined}=0.8276090159036916$ and $A_{TE}=0.01759429001506974$.  For \textit{E. coli} K-12 MG1655, $\|P_Q\|^2=393.78692095732885$, $\|P_{Q\perp Tmod}\|^2=261.0102870984463$, $d_{modeled}=0.6628211177352172$, $d_{combined}=0.6620871850159149$, and $A_{TE}=0.0010982956489726373$.  For \textit{H. sapiens}, $\|P_Q\|^2=565.3850201235074$, $\|P_{Q\perp Tmod}\|^2=484.31956581711796$, $d_{modeled}=0.8566190269974241$, $d_{combined}=0.854133502300174$, and $A_{TE}=0.0028947149257963897$.  For \textit{S. cerevisiae}, $\|P_Q\|^2=675.3275479192903$, $\|P_{Q\perp Tmod}\|^2=151.18264752811882$, $d_{modeled}=0.22386566043976477$, $d_{combined}=0.2237928123367028$, and $A_{TE}=0.0002762783754375593$.

\paragraph{Readback.}
The contact is therefore positive but small in the strict descriptive sense: all four organism-specific TE projections absorb a nonzero fraction of the modeled-tAI-complement residual, with the largest measured-TE absorption in \textit{D. rerio} at $0.01759429001506974$ and the smallest in \textit{S. cerevisiae} at $0.0002762783754375593$.  This supports only the bounded statement that measured TE has a detectable finite-row projection against the residual complement on these joined panels.  It does not license a statement that the residual coordinates have been explained by translation efficiency, because the absorption fractions are small and because the measured-TE datasets are condition-specific.

\paragraph{Join-shift self-check.}
The same computation also records whether the modeled complement on the measured-TE join reproduces the full abundance-CDS complement reference within $0.02$.  The check passes in \textit{E. coli} K-12 MG1655, where the reference value is $0.6629960038578532$, the measured-TE join value is $0.6628211177352172$, and the absolute difference is $0.00017488612263594217$.  It also passes in \textit{S. cerevisiae}, where the reference value is $0.21173219572706703$, the measured-TE join value is $0.22386566043976477$, and the absolute difference is $0.012133464712697745$.  The check fails in \textit{D. rerio}, where the reference value is $0.874628721049266$, the measured-TE join value is $0.8424312412078521$, and the absolute difference is $0.03219747984141397$; it also fails in \textit{H. sapiens}, where the reference value is $0.9208949355679455$, the measured-TE join value is $0.8566190269974241$, and the absolute difference is $0.06427590857052146$.  The failure mode is not a contradiction.  It says that measured-TE availability changes the protein-identifier join enough in two organisms that the projected residual target is not identical to the full abundance-CDS target.

\paragraph{Adjacent contact calibration.}
The measured-TE row is consistent with the neighboring translation-contact checks.  A measured tRNA-gene-copy tAI contact, computed on the full CDS-abundance joins for \textit{D. rerio}, \textit{H. sapiens}, and \textit{S. cerevisiae}, reports exact agreement with the complement reference on those joins: absolute difference $0.0$ in all three organisms.  Its tRNA absorption values are $0.020817102845637092$ for \textit{D. rerio}, $0.00005426229300865148$ for \textit{H. sapiens}, and $0.001530747712165342$ for \textit{S. cerevisiae}.  A yeast turnover contact, joined on $3640$ protein half-life rows with a reported CDS join hit rate of $0.9156038419146592$ and a turnover join hit rate of $0.8818572656921754$, gives $d_{modeled}=0.2232704509412724$, $d_{combined}=0.22306503721327192$, and turnover absorption $0.0009086031889966327$.  These adjacent rows show that the measured-TE contact is part of a bounded sequence of residual readbacks, not a complete mechanistic explanation.

\paragraph{Local conclusion.}
The finite-row verdict is a qualified positive contact: measured ribosome-profiling TE is a nonzero but weak absorber of the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ modeled-tAI-complement residual after amino-acid composition, organism-specific joining, GC3, CDS length, $M$-density, and matched mRNA controls.  The strongest numerical entry is still below $0.02$, and two organisms show complement-reference instability under the measured-TE join.  The result therefore keeps the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual basis in the cross-layer search space while refusing promotion to a complete translation account.

\paragraph{Claim boundary.}
This chapter records a controlled association surface, not an unrestricted biological interpretation.  A measured-TE projection alone does not establish ribosome mechanism, protein abundance, folding, structural order, physical admissibility, molecular function, phenotype, or universality.  Even the word translation is used here only for the specific ribosome-profiling TE readout that is joined to matched mRNA abundance; broader translation realization, protein output, and post-translational interpretation require their own layer-matched contacts and their own controlled statistics.  If all such contacts remain weak or join-unstable, the appropriate conclusion is that the non-translation residual remains open rather than explained.
