\paragraph{Statistic.}
Let $Q$ denote the nine-dimensional residual-coordinate surface after the common controls, let $T_{mod}$ denote the modeled-tAI translation coordinate already removed in the complement computation, and let $T_{meas}$ denote residualized measured ribosome-profiling translation efficiency with matched mRNA abundance in the same control boundary.  The measured-TE contact is not a direct survival matrix for protein output; it is the descriptive projection of the modeled-tAI-complement abundance residual onto $T_{meas}$.  In norm notation the reported quantities can be read as
$$
\begin{aligned}
 d_{mod} &= \frac{\lVert P_{Q\perp T_{mod}}\rVert^{2}}{\lVert P_Q\rVert^{2}},\\
 d_{comb} &= \frac{\lVert P_{Q\perp T_{mod}\perp T_{meas}}\rVert^{2}}{\lVert P_Q\rVert^{2}},\\
 a_{measTE} &= d_{mod}-d_{comb}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The computed values are finite and organism-specific. \paragraph{Readout.}
The measured translation-efficiency surface is a weak absorber of the modeled-tAI-complement residual in all four organisms.  The largest observed measured-TE absorption is in \emph{Danio rerio}, $0.01759429001506974$, while the other three values are approximately $0.0010983$, $0.0028947$, and $0.0002763$.  Thus the finite table supports the narrower statement that the projection has been computed and bounded, not that measured TE explains the residual.  The self-check against the full abundance-CDS complement join is mixed: \emph{E. coli} and yeast match the reference complement within $0.02$, with absolute differences $0.00017488612263594217$ and $0.012133464712697745$, whereas zebrafish and human do not, with absolute differences $0.03219747984141397$ and $0.06427590857052146$.  The failed matches are interpreted as join-sensitivity: measured-TE availability changes the protein-id surface on which the complement projection is evaluated.

\paragraph{Decision.}
The finite record is ready as a bounded cross-layer relation.  It establishes that measured ribosome-profiling translation efficiency, with matched mRNA abundance present in the control boundary, has been tested as an $H$-candidate for the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ modeled-tAI-complement residual on four organism-specific joined panels.  The positive quantities reported here are descriptive absorption fractions and residual norms.  They do not license a claim of translation realization, causal mediation, protein abundance, folding, physical admissibility, biological function, organismal phenotype, molecular mechanism, or universality.  Any such stronger assertion would require its own layer-matched contact and its own finite statistic.  In particular, low measured-TE absorption leaves the non-translation residual open rather than explained; the remaining search space is downstream of transcription and modeled translation, with turnover, localization, protein-state, interaction, and condition-specific contacts treated as separate tests.
