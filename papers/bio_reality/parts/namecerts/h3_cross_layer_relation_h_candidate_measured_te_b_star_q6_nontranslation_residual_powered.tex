\subsection{Measured translation-efficiency contact for the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ non-translation residual}
\label{sec:bioq6-measured-te-nontranslation-residual}

\paragraph{Finite surface.}
This record treats the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ synonymous residual basis as a finite coordinate surface tested against measured ribosome-profiling translation efficiency.  The readout is not a protein, structure, function, or phenotype endpoint.  It is a controlled cross-layer contact whose rows join coding-sequence codon counts, matched abundance data, and measured translation-efficiency values, then ask how much of the modeled-tAI-complement abundance residual is absorbed by measured TE after the declared covariates have been removed.  The nine residual coordinates are
$K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $Leu_{CUN\_vs\_UUR}$, $Leu_{UUA\_vs\_UUG}$, $Ser_{UCR\_vs\_AGY}$, $Ser_{UCA\_vs\_UCG}$, $Thr_{ACR\_vs\_ACY}$, and $f3\_stress$.  The control set contains an intercept, $\log(cds\_len\_nt)$, the twenty amino-acid composition proportions from real CDS codon counts, GC3, and the $M$-density baseline defined by the fraction of sense codons on the union support of the nine projected $B^{*}_{Q6}$ vectors.  The finite design therefore has coordinate count $9$, control-column count $24$, and control rank $23$ in every organism in this measured-TE panel.

\paragraph{Joined rows.}
The organism-specific measured-TE joins are nondegenerate.  The zebrafish surface has $n=13140$ rows, with $14410$ CDS-abundance rows reported and $13140$ valid TE rows.  The \emph{E. coli} K-12 MG1655 surface has $n=3415$, with $3739$ CDS rows reported, $3747$ valid abundance rows, and $3415$ valid TE rows.  The human surface has $n=11554$, with $19053$ CDS rows reported, $19483$ valid abundance rows, and $11554$ valid TE rows.  The yeast surface has $n=5128$, with $5815$ CDS rows reported, $6351$ valid abundance rows, and $5128$ valid TE rows.  These row counts are large enough for the projection quantities to be read as measured finite-row summaries rather than rank-saturated artifacts.

\paragraph{Modeled-complement residual before measured TE.}
For each organism the residual target is the modeled-tAI-complement component $\widehat P_{Q\perp Tmod}$.  The measured-TE join changes this target in some organisms because TE availability changes which protein identifiers remain in the all-readout table.  On the all-readout TE join, the modeled-complement fractions are
$$
\begin{aligned}
 d_{model}(Danio) &= 0.8424312412078521,\\
 d_{model}(Ecoli) &= 0.6628211177352172,\\
 d_{model}(Human) &= 0.8566190269974241,\\
 d_{model}(Yeast) &= 0.22386566043976477.
\end{aligned}
$$
The corresponding squared norms are also finite-row quantities: zebrafish has $\|P_Q\|^2=1092.0114815505467$ and $\|P_{Q\perp Tmod}\|^2=919.9445878158525$; \emph{E. coli} has $393.78692095732885$ and $261.0102870984463$; human has $565.3850201235074$ and $484.31956581711796$; yeast has $675.3275479192903$ and $151.18264752811882$.

\paragraph{Measured-TE absorption.}
Measured TE absorbs only a small portion of the modeled-complement residual in this panel.  The computed absorption fractions and combined residual fractions are
$$
\begin{aligned}
 A_{TE}(Danio) &= 0.01759429001506974, & d_{combined}(Danio) &= 0.8276090159036916,\\
 A_{TE}(Ecoli) &= 0.0010982956489726373, & d_{combined}(Ecoli) &= 0.6620871850159149,\\
 A_{TE}(Human) &= 0.0028947149257963897, & d_{combined}(Human) &= 0.854133502300174,\\
 A_{TE}(Yeast) &= 0.0002762783754375593, & d_{combined}(Yeast) &= 0.2237928123367028.
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the measured-TE contact is positive as a computed descriptive projection surface, but it does not collapse the non-translation residual.  The largest measured-TE absorption in this run is the zebrafish value $0.01759429001506974$; the yeast value is nearly zero at $0.0002762783754375593$.  The correct reading is therefore bounded: measured TE is a checked first H-candidate for a small absorption component, while most of $\widehat P_{Q\perp Tmod}$ remains outside this measured TE realization.

\paragraph{Join-stability self-check.}
The full abundance-CDS complement references are not identical to the measured-TE joins in every organism.  Zebrafish changes from the full-join reference $0.874628721049266$ at $14410$ rows to $0.8424312412078521$ at $13140$ rows, an absolute difference of $0.03219747984141397$, which fails the $0.02$ self-check.  Human changes from $0.9208949355679455$ at $19053$ rows to $0.8566190269974241$ at $11554$ rows, an absolute difference of $0.06427590857052146$, also failing the $0.02$ self-check.  By contrast, \emph{E. coli} changes by only $0.00017488612263594217$, and yeast changes by $0.012133464712697745$; both pass the same tolerance.  The finite-row conclusion must therefore preserve the join boundary: the measured-TE run gives organism-specific projection quantities on the TE-available table, not a universal complement identity independent of measurement availability.

\paragraph{Comparison with neighboring H-candidate contacts.}
The same residual family has bounded comparisons against additional readout surfaces.  Measured tRNA gene-copy tAI absorbs $0.020817102845637092$ in zebrafish, $0.00005426229300865148$ in human, and $0.001530747712165342$ in yeast, with exact join agreement to the full complement reference in those three organisms.  Yeast protein turnover absorbs $0.0009086031889966327$ on $3640$ joined turnover rows, with a CDS join hit rate $0.9156038419146592$ and turnover join hit rate $0.8818572656921754$.  Sparse protein-feature contacts give larger isolated candidate absorptions in selected cases, such as zebrafish PTM density $0.014211240113472575$, human PTM density $0.014884825920540636$, human complex membership $0.0075271999877804495$, and human transmembrane count $0.0015569064882873212$, but these feature contacts are explicitly coverage-limited and do not stabilize a cross-organism mechanism claim.  At the organism-meta scale, the dominant-coordinate-by-domain contingency has $n=12$, Pearson chi-square $9.399999999999999$, Cramer's $V=0.6258327785172862$, and permutation right-tail $p=0.5814185814185814$; the $M_{QTP}$ fraction has no powered continuous association with GC3 in that table ($r=-0.3062616500469447$, two-sided permutation $p=0.35264735264735264$).  These neighboring results reinforce the local conclusion: measured TE is one bounded contact, not the whole residual explanation.

\paragraph{Interpretation boundary.}
This chapter licenses only the finite projection statements above.  It does not license a causal mediator claim, because the TE absorption is descriptive and the measured TE data are single-dataset or condition-specific.  It does not license mature protein abundance, folding, structural order, physical admissibility, molecular function, organismal phenotype, mechanism, adaptation, or universality.  It also does not license a general translation claim beyond the measured TE statistic itself.  Any such assertion requires a separate layer-matched contact and its own controlled statistic.  If all controlled entries in a future $S^{QT}_{i,TE}$ surface are non-positive after matched mRNA abundance and the other covariates are included, then the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual basis remains outside measured translation-efficiency realization for that contact.  In the present run, measured TE absorbs a small but finite part of the modeled-tAI-complement residual, while the residual remains largely unexplained by this first H-candidate.
