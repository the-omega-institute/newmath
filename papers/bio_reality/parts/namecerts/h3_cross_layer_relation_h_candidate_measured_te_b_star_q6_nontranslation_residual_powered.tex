\subsection{Measured translation-efficiency contact for the $B^*_{Q6}$ complement}
\label{sec:bioreality-q6-mrna-controlled-te-survival}

\paragraph{Question fixed by the row set.}
This record tests a narrow cross-layer question: after the $B^*_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates have already been controlled for amino-acid composition, organismal coding context, $GC3$, CDS length, and the $M$-density baseline, does a matched ribosome-profiling translation-efficiency readout absorb any of the modeled-tAI-complement abundance residual?  The carrier is the finite joined surface consisting of real CDS codon counts, nine projected $B^*_{Q6}$ coordinates, organism-matched translation-efficiency values, and matched mRNA abundance used to define the TE denominator.  The coordinate list is
$$
\begin{aligned}
R_{Q6} = \{&K_{AAA},\ Arg_{AGR},\ Ile_{AUA},\ Leu_{CUN\,vs\,UUR},\ Leu_{UUA\,vs\,UUG},\\
&Ser_{UCR\,vs\,AGY},\ Ser_{UCA\,vs\,UCG},\ Thr_{ACR\,vs\,ACY},\ f3_{stress}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The control matrix has $24$ columns in every organism and rank $23$ in every organism.  Its columns are the intercept, $\log(\mathrm{cds\_len\_nt})$, the $20$ amino-acid composition proportions computed from real CDS codon counts, $GC3$, and the $M$-density baseline, where $M$-density is the fraction of sense codons lying on the union support of the nine projected $B^*_{Q6}$ vectors.  The matched mRNA abundance condition is part of the TE boundary: a ribosome-footprint association is not counted as translation-efficiency contact unless its mRNA denominator is present in the same joined readout.

\paragraph{Joined rows and rank adequacy.}
The measured-TE panel is powered at the row level for the four reported organisms.  The joined counts are $n=13{,}140$ for \emph{Danio rerio}, $n=3{,}415$ for \emph{Escherichia coli} K-12 MG1655, $n=11{,}554$ for \emph{Homo sapiens}, and $n=5{,}128$ for \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}.  These joins are smaller than the corresponding CDS-abundance joins in several organisms: $14{,}410$ CDS rows for zebrafish, $3{,}739$ for \emph{E. coli}, $19{,}053$ for human, and $5{,}815$ for yeast.  Thus the calculation is a measured-TE all-readout projection rather than a reuse of the full abundance-CDS surface.  The available TE rows are $13{,}140$, $3{,}415$, $11{,}554$, and $5{,}128$ respectively, while abundance-valid protein rows are $14{,}410$, $3{,}747$, $19{,}483$, and $6{,}351$ respectively.  The rank condition is therefore not the limiting factor; the finite-row limitation is instead the protein-id intersection determined by measured TE availability.

\paragraph{Modeled-complement residual before measured TE.}
For each organism the projected abundance residual $\widehat P_Q$ is compared with its modeled-tAI-complement component $\widehat P_{Q\perp T_{mod}}$.  The descriptive complement fractions on the measured-TE joins are
$$
\begin{aligned}
d_{mod}(\mathrm{Danio}) &= 0.8424312412078521,\\
d_{mod}(\mathrm{E.coli}) &= 0.6628211177352172,\\
d_{mod}(\mathrm{Human}) &= 0.8566190269974241,\\
d_{mod}(\mathrm{Yeast}) &= 0.22386566043976477.
\end{aligned}
$$
The corresponding squared norms are $(1092.0114815505467,919.9445878158525)$ for zebrafish, $(393.78692095732885,261.0102870984463)$ for \emph{E. coli}, $(565.3850201235074,484.31956581711796)$ for human, and $(675.3275479192903,151.18264752811882)$ for yeast, where the first entry is $\|\widehat P_Q\|^2$ and the second is $\|\widehat P_{Q\perp T_{mod}}\|^2$.  The self-check against the full abundance-CDS complement shows that the measured-TE join changes the target in zebrafish and human: the absolute differences are $0.03219747984141397$ and $0.06427590857052146$, both outside the $0.02$ tolerance.  The same comparison passes for \emph{E. coli} and yeast, with differences $0.00017488612263594217$ and $0.012133464712697745$.

\paragraph{Measured-TE absorption.}
Measured ribosome-profiling TE is then treated as a first bounded $H$-candidate by projecting $\widehat P_{Q\perp T_{mod}}$ onto residualized measured TE.  The observed absorbed fractions are
$$
\begin{aligned}
A_{TE}(\mathrm{Danio}) &= 0.01759429001506974,\\
A_{TE}(\mathrm{E.coli}) &= 0.0010982956489726373,\\
A_{TE}(\mathrm{Human}) &= 0.0028947149257963897,\\
A_{TE}(\mathrm{Yeast}) &= 0.0002762783754375593.
\end{aligned}
$$
The combined residual fractions after adding measured TE are $0.8276090159036916$ for zebrafish, $0.6620871850159149$ for \emph{E. coli}, $0.854133502300174$ for human, and $0.2237928123367028$ for yeast.  These values are finite and reproducible on the stated joins, but the absorption is small in all four organisms.  In particular, yeast changes from $0.22386566043976477$ to $0.2237928123367028$, and \emph{E. coli} changes from $0.6628211177352172$ to $0.6620871850159149$.  The largest measured-TE absorption is the zebrafish value $0.01759429001506974$, yet its complement self-check is also join-shifted by $0.03219747984141397$.  The chapter therefore records a computed bounded contact, not a stable positive cross-organism closure of the non-translation residual.

\paragraph{Relation to nearby candidate contacts.}
The measured-tRNA-gene-copy tAI contact keeps the full abundance-CDS joins for zebrafish, human, and yeast, with $14{,}410$, $19{,}053$, and $5{,}815$ rows and exact agreement with the corresponding complement reference values $0.874628721049266$, $0.9208949355679455$, and $0.21173219572706703$.  Its absorbed fractions are $0.020817102845637092$, $5.426229300865148\times 10^{-5}$, and $0.001530747712165342$.  The yeast turnover contact uses $3{,}640$ joined rows, passes the complement self-check within $0.02$ with absolute difference $0.011538255214205373$, and absorbs $0.0009086031889966327$ of the modeled complement.  The protein-feature contact is sparser: zebrafish uses $2{,}242$ joined rows, human $18{,}335$, and yeast $5{,}682$; several single-feature descriptive absorptions exceed degree-of-freedom null thresholds, such as zebrafish PTM density at $0.014211240113472575$ and human PTM density at $0.014884825920540636$, but that packet reports instability caused by feature coverage and is not used here as a mechanism claim.  These neighboring rows help locate the measured-TE result: the TE contact is computed, but it does not by itself explain the remaining modeled-complement residual.

\paragraph{Local refusal of over-interpretation.}
The result is a descriptive projection calculation on finite joined data.  It does not establish causality, a mediator, or a biological law.  It also does not license a protein-abundance conclusion, a folding conclusion, physical admissibility, molecular function, phenotype, or universality.  Even the translation interpretation is limited to the matched ribosome-profiling TE rows and their mRNA denominator; no broader translation-realization statement follows without a separate layer-matched contact and statistic.  If all coordinate-wise controlled entries are non-positive, the $Q6$ residual basis remains outside the measured translation-efficiency readout for this contact.  If a coordinate entry is positive in a later coordinate-resolved matrix, that entry may be reported only at the measured-TE boundary and only under the same controls, not as structure, function, mechanism, or global biological necessity.
