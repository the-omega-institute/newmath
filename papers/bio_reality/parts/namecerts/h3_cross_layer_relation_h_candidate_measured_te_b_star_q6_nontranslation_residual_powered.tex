\subsection{Measured Translation-Efficiency Contact for the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ Residual Complement}\label{subsec:bioreality-q6-measured-te-contact}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite readout surface.}
This record treats measured ribosome-profiling translation efficiency as a bounded contact with the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ synonymous residual basis.  The carrier is the finite coding-sequence by organism table on which nine residual coordinates are evaluated, joined to measured translation-efficiency values and to matched mRNA abundance controls.  The coordinate list is \texttt{K\_AAA}, \texttt{Arg\_AGR}, \texttt{Ile\_AUA}, \texttt{Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR}, \texttt{Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG}, \texttt{Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY}, \texttt{Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG}, \texttt{Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY}, and \texttt{f3\_stress}.  The control surface has 24 named columns: an intercept, $\log(\mathrm{cds\_len\_nt})$, the 20 amino-acid composition proportions computed from real CDS codon counts, GC3, and an $M$-density baseline equal to the fraction of sense codons on the union support of the nine projected $B^{*}_{Q6}$ vectors.  In every organism reported here the control matrix has rank $23$, so the record is not a three-row saturated fit.

\paragraph{Joined rows and rank.}
The measured-TE join contains four organism rows.  For \textit{Danio rerio}, the all-readout join has $n=13140$ CDS entries, compared with $14410$ CDS entries joined to abundance, with $14410$ valid abundance rows and $13140$ valid TE rows.  For \textit{Escherichia coli} K12 MG1655, the join has $n=3415$, compared with $3739$ CDS entries joined to abundance, $3747$ valid abundance rows, and $3415$ valid TE rows.  For \textit{Homo sapiens}, the measured-TE join has $n=11554$, while the abundance-side CDS join reports $19053$ entries, with $19483$ valid abundance rows and $11554$ valid TE rows.  For \textit{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}, the measured-TE join has $n=5128$, compared with $5815$ CDS entries joined to abundance, $6351$ valid abundance rows, and $5128$ valid TE rows.  These row counts are the finite population actually used by the projection; no claim is made for CDS rows lost at the TE-availability boundary.

\paragraph{Projection statistic.}
The statistic is descriptive.  The modeled-tAI complement abundance residual $P_{\widehat Q\perp T_{\mathrm{mod}}}$ is projected onto residualized measured TE after the control surface has already removed amino-acid composition, length, GC3, and $M$-density structure.  The four measured-TE absorption values are small:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{absorb}_{\mathrm{TE}}(\textit{D. rerio}) &= 0.01759429001506974,\\
\mathrm{absorb}_{\mathrm{TE}}(\textit{E. coli}) &= 0.0010982956489726373,\\
\mathrm{absorb}_{\mathrm{TE}}(\textit{H. sapiens}) &= 0.0028947149257963897,\\
\mathrm{absorb}_{\mathrm{TE}}(\textit{S. cerevisiae}) &= 0.0002762783754375593.
\end{aligned}
$$
The corresponding combined residual distances are $0.8276090159036916$, $0.6620871850159149$, $0.854133502300174$, and $0.2237928123367028$.  Thus measured TE, in these finite joins, absorbs only a narrow part of the modeled non-translation complement rather than collapsing the complement into the translation-efficiency readout.

\paragraph{Complement scale.}
Before the measured-TE projection is added, the modeled complement is already organism-specific.  The reported $P_Q$ squared norms are $1092.0114815505467$ for \textit{D. rerio}, $393.78692095732885$ for \textit{E. coli}, $565.3850201235074$ for \textit{H. sapiens}, and $675.3275479192903$ for \textit{S. cerevisiae}.  The corresponding complement squared norms after removing modeled translation are $919.9445878158525$, $261.0102870984463$, $484.31956581711796$, and $151.18264752811882$, giving modeled complement fractions
$$
\begin{aligned}
d_{\mathrm{modeled}}(\textit{D. rerio}) &= 0.8424312412078521,\\
d_{\mathrm{modeled}}(\textit{E. coli}) &= 0.6628211177352172,\\
d_{\mathrm{modeled}}(\textit{H. sapiens}) &= 0.8566190269974241,\\
d_{\mathrm{modeled}}(\textit{S. cerevisiae}) &= 0.22386566043976477.
\end{aligned}
$$
Measured TE therefore contacts a residual component that remains large in zebrafish, human, and \textit{E. coli}, but is already modest in yeast before the TE contact is added.

\paragraph{Join-stability self-check.}
The measured-TE contact is not identical to the full abundance-CDS join.  Relative to the complement experiment reference, the measured-TE join changes $d_{\mathrm{modeled}}$ by $0.03219747984141397$ in \textit{D. rerio} and $0.06427590857052146$ in \textit{H. sapiens}; both exceed the $0.02$ self-check window.  The same comparison is stable for \textit{E. coli}, with absolute difference $0.00017488612263594217$, and for yeast, with absolute difference $0.012133464712697745$.  This is a sampling-boundary fact, not a biological mechanism: TE availability changes the protein-id join in two of the four organisms, so the measured-TE projection must be read as a finite joined-surface statistic rather than as an organism-universal translation law.

\paragraph{Relation to adjacent contacts.}
The companion measured-tRNA tAI contact uses full abundance-CDS joins for three organisms and reports absorption values $0.020817102845637092$ in \textit{D. rerio}, $0.00005426229300865148$ in \textit{H. sapiens}, and $0.001530747712165342$ in yeast, with tRNA gene counts $8846$, $431$, and $273$.  The yeast turnover contact, using $3640$ joined half-life rows and $3537$ rows when measured TE is also required, absorbs $0.0009086031889966327$.  Sparse protein-feature contacts are similarly bounded: reviewed feature joins include $2242$ zebrafish rows, $18335$ human rows, and $5682$ yeast rows, and only selected one-dimensional feature candidates exceed their own permutation-derived Bonferroni thresholds.  The broader organism-specificity survey remains exploratory: its complete-feature meta table has $12$ rows, the domain contingency for dominant coordinate has permutation $p=0.5814185814185814$, and the $M_{QTP}$ fraction correlations have two-sided permutation values $0.35264735264735264$ against GC3, $0.913086913086913$ against log proteome size, and $0.8621378621378621$ against log tRNA-gene count.  A separate localization boundary records $8696$ joined rows, $657$ mitochondrial labels, and $2000$ permutations, but it does not replace the measured-TE statistic.

\paragraph{Local verdict.}
The finite contact is ready as a measured translation-efficiency readout of the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual complement under matched mRNA and sequence-derived controls.  It supports the statement that the TE projection was computed on rank-powered organism joins and that the measured-TE absorption is small in all four reported organisms.  It does not support a stronger promotion.  In particular, no translation mechanism, no protein abundance conclusion, no folding or structural-order claim, no physical-admissibility claim, no functional or phenotypic claim, and no universal biological rule follows from these numbers unless a separate layer-matched contact supplies the relevant observations and the corresponding controlled statistic.
