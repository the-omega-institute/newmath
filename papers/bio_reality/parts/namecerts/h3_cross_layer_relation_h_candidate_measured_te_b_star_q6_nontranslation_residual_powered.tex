\subsection{Measured translation-efficiency contact for the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual complement}\label{sec:bioreality-h3-cross-layer-measured-te-b-star-q6-nontranslation-residual}\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite object.} This record treats the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ synonymous-residual coordinates as a finite readout surface joined to organism-matched ribosome-profiling translation-efficiency values and matched mRNA abundance. The biological object is not a protein, a fold, a molecular function, or a phenotype. It is the controlled relation between nine residual coordinates and a measured translation-efficiency contact after the transcription layer has been made explicit. The coordinate list is
$$
\begin{aligned}
Q = \{&K\_AAA,\ Arg\_AGR,\ Ile\_AUA,\ Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR,\ Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG,\\
&Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY,\ Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG,\ Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY,\ f3\_stress\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The controlled design used an intercept, $\log(\mathrm{cds\_len\_nt})$, the twenty amino-acid composition proportions $A,C,D,E,F,G,H,I,K,L,M,N,P,Q,R,S,T,V,W,Y$, GC3 from real CDS codon counts, and an $M$-density baseline defined as the fraction of sense codons on the union support of the nine projected $B^{*}_{Q6}$ vectors. Each organism had $24$ control columns with rank $23$ in the control matrix.

\paragraph{Joined translation-efficiency rows.} The measured translation-efficiency contact was evaluated on four organism panels. The joined row counts were $n=13140$ for \emph{Danio rerio}, $n=3415$ for \emph{Escherichia coli} K12 MG1655, $n=11554$ for \emph{Homo sapiens}, and $n=5128$ for \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}. These joins are smaller than the corresponding CDS-abundance joins: $14410$, $3739$, $19053$, and $5815$, respectively. The finite readout is therefore a measured-TE surface on the proteins for which coding sequence, abundance, and translation-efficiency identifiers all match; it is not a statement about the missing rows.

\paragraph{Modeled-complement target.} The tested quantity was the modeled-tAI-complement abundance residual $\widehat{P}_{Q\perp Tmod}$ projected onto residualized measured translation efficiency. The modeled-complement fractions on the all-readout joins were
$$
\begin{aligned}
d_{\mathrm{modeled}}(\mathrm{Danio}) &= 0.8424312412078521,\\
d_{\mathrm{modeled}}(\mathrm{E. coli}) &= 0.6628211177352172,\\
d_{\mathrm{modeled}}(\mathrm{human}) &= 0.8566190269974241,\\
d_{\mathrm{modeled}}(\mathrm{yeast}) &= 0.22386566043976477.
\end{aligned}
$$
The corresponding squared-norm pairs $(\|P_Q\|^2,\|P_{Q\perp Tmod}\|^2)$ were $(1092.0114815505467,919.9445878158525)$, $(393.78692095732885,261.0102870984463)$, $(565.3850201235074,484.31956581711796)$, and $(675.3275479192903,151.18264752811882)$ for \emph{Danio rerio}, \emph{E. coli}, human, and yeast. Thus the measured-TE test starts from a sizable non-modeled-translation residual in three panels and a smaller residual target in yeast.

\paragraph{Measured-TE absorption.} The measured translation-efficiency projection absorbed only a small fraction of the modeled-complement residual in each organism:
$$
\begin{aligned}
a_{\mathrm{measTE}}(\mathrm{Danio}) &= 0.01759429001506974,\\
a_{\mathrm{measTE}}(\mathrm{E. coli}) &= 0.0010982956489726373,\\
a_{\mathrm{measTE}}(\mathrm{human}) &= 0.0028947149257963897,\\
a_{\mathrm{measTE}}(\mathrm{yeast}) &= 0.0002762783754375593.
\end{aligned}
$$
After adding measured TE, the combined residual fractions were $0.8276090159036916$, $0.6620871850159149$, $0.854133502300174$, and $0.2237928123367028$. In this finite contact, measured translation efficiency is therefore a weak descriptive absorber of the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ modeled-complement abundance residual. The strongest value occurs in \emph{Danio rerio}, but even there the absorbed component is about $1.76\%$ of the tested residual target.

\paragraph{Join-stability check.} The measured-TE availability changes the target population in two organisms strongly enough that the complement reference does not match within the declared $0.02$ tolerance. For \emph{Danio rerio}, the full abundance-CDS reference had $d_{\mathrm{modeled}}=0.874628721049266$ on $14410$ joined rows, while the measured-TE all-readout join had $d_{\mathrm{modeled}}=0.8424312412078521$ on $13140$ rows, an absolute difference of $0.03219747984141397$. For human, the corresponding values were $0.9208949355679455$ on $19053$ rows and $0.8566190269974241$ on $11554$ rows, an absolute difference of $0.06427590857052146$. \emph{E. coli} and yeast passed this self-check, with differences $0.00017488612263594217$ and $0.012133464712697745$. The chapter therefore reports the measured-TE projection as a finite descriptive contact, not as a stable population-level law.

\paragraph{Comparison contacts.} The same residual family has related bounded contacts that help locate the measured-TE result without promoting it. In the measured tRNA gene-copy tAI contact, full CDS joins were available for \emph{Danio rerio}, human, and yeast, with joined row counts $14410$, $19053$, and $5815$ and tRNA gene counts $8846$, $431$, and $273$. The measured tRNA-tAI absorbed fractions were $0.020817102845637092$, $0.00005426229300865148$, and $0.001530747712165342$. These values are also descriptive projections, since tRNA gene copy number is a proxy rather than direct tRNA abundance or ribosome occupancy. In the yeast protein-turnover contact, the joined half-life panel had $n=3640$, a CDS join hit rate of $0.9156038419146592$, and a turnover join hit rate of $0.8818572656921754$; the turnover absorbed fraction was $0.0009086031889966327$ with $d_{\mathrm{combined}}=0.22306503721327192$. These neighboring contacts agree with the local conclusion: the residual component is only weakly reduced by the available translation-adjacent readouts.

\paragraph{Cross-organism context.} The organism-specific summary remains exploratory. A dominant-coordinate contingency test over $12$ complete-feature organisms gave Pearson $\chi^2=9.399999999999999$, Cramer $V=0.6258327785172862$, and permutation right-tail $p=0.5814185814185814$. Correlations of $M_{QTP}$ fraction with GC3, log proteome size, and log tRNA gene count gave Pearson statistics $r=-0.3062616500469447$, $r=-0.033628115789504955$, and $r=0.06029405206037823$, with two-sided permutation values $0.35264735264735264$, $0.913086913086913$, and $0.8621378621378621$. These numbers do not support a resolved domain, GC3, proteome-size, or tRNA-pool explanation for the residual contact.

\paragraph{Local verdict.} The finite measured-TE packet is a computed cross-layer relation: $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates can be placed against organism-matched ribosome-profiling translation efficiency after amino-acid composition, GC3, CDS length, $M$-density, and mRNA-related readout boundaries are made explicit. The observed absorbed fractions are small, and two organism panels fail the complement-join stability tolerance because measured-TE availability changes the protein set. The correct reading is therefore bounded and negative in strength: measured TE is a tested first $H$-candidate, but it does not by itself close the non-translation residual. No translation mechanism, no ribosome-occupancy mechanism, no protein abundance, no folding or structural-order result, no physical admissibility, no biological function, no phenotype, and no universal biological rule follows from this entry unless another layer-specific contact supplies its own matched rows and controlled statistic.
