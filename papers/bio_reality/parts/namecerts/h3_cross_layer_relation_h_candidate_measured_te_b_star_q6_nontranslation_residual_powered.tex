\subsection{Measured translation-efficiency contact for the $B^*_{Q6}$ residual surface}
\label{sec:h3-cross-layer-relation-h-candidate-measured-te-b-star-q6-nontranslation-residual-powered}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite carrier.}
This record concerns a finite CDS-by-organism readout surface in which the nine $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinates are tested against matched ribosome-profiling translation-efficiency values after sequence and abundance controls have already been imposed. The coordinate list is
$$
\begin{aligned}
R_{Q6}=\{&K_{AAA},\ Arg_{AGR},\ Ile_{AUA},\ Leu_{CUN\,vs\,UUR},\ Leu_{UUA\,vs\,UUG},\\
&Ser_{UCR\,vs\,AGY},\ Ser_{UCA\,vs\,UCG},\ Thr_{ACR\,vs\,ACY},\ f3_{stress}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
For each joined CDS row, the controlled design includes an intercept, $\log(\mathrm{cds\_len\_nt})$, the twenty amino-acid composition proportions $A,C,D,E,F,G,H,I,K,L,M,N,P,Q,R,S,T,V,W,Y$, GC3, and the $M$-density baseline defined as the fraction of sense codons lying on the union support of the nine projected $B^*_{Q6}$ vectors. Thus the control matrix has $24$ columns and rank $23$ in each organism-specific fit. The readout is not a raw codon table and not a modeled adaptation weight; it is the measured ribosome-profiling translation-efficiency quantity, paired with matched mRNA abundance so that the transcription layer is explicitly present as a control boundary.

\paragraph{Joined rows and rank condition.}
The finite panel is rank-powered for the declared controlled projection because the organism-specific joined row counts are large relative to the rank-$23$ control design. The measured-TE joins are $n=13140$ for \emph{Danio rerio}, $n=3415$ for \emph{Escherichia coli} K-12 MG1655, $n=11554$ for \emph{Homo sapiens}, and $n=5128$ for \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}. The same runs report CDS joins of $14410$, $3739$, $19053$, and $5815$, respectively, before measured-TE availability restricts the protein-id surface. The valid abundance counts are $14410$, $3747$, $19483$, and $6351$, while the valid TE counts are $13140$, $3415$, $11554$, and $5128$. This matters because the measured-TE contact changes the active row set in two organisms: the self-check comparing the modeled complement on the current all-readout join to the full-abundance CDS-join reference differs by $0.03219747984141397$ in \emph{D. rerio} and $0.06427590857052146$ in human, both above the $0.02$ tolerance used by the run. In contrast, \emph{E. coli} differs by $0.00017488612263594217$ and yeast by $0.012133464712697745$, both within tolerance.

\paragraph{Measured-TE projection values.}
The measured-TE test is a descriptive projection of the modeled-tAI-complement abundance residual $\widehat P_{Q\perp Tmod}$ onto residualized measured translation efficiency. The modeled complement fractions on the measured-TE all-readout joins are
$$
\begin{aligned}
d_{modeled}(\mathrm{D.rerio})&=0.8424312412078521,\\
d_{modeled}(\mathrm{E.coli})&=0.6628211177352172,\\
d_{modeled}(\mathrm{human})&=0.8566190269974241,\\
d_{modeled}(\mathrm{yeast})&=0.22386566043976477.
\end{aligned}
$$
The corresponding squared-norm data are $P_Q^2=1092.0114815505467$ and $P_{Q\perp Tmod}^2=919.9445878158525$ for \emph{D. rerio}, $393.78692095732885$ and $261.0102870984463$ for \emph{E. coli}, $565.3850201235074$ and $484.31956581711796$ for human, and $675.3275479192903$ and $151.18264752811882$ for yeast. After adding residualized measured TE, the absorbed fractions are small but finite: $0.01759429001506974$ in \emph{D. rerio}, $0.0010982956489726373$ in \emph{E. coli}, $0.0028947149257963897$ in human, and $0.0002762783754375593$ in yeast. The combined residual fractions are therefore $0.8276090159036916$, $0.6620871850159149$, $0.854133502300174$, and $0.2237928123367028$.

\paragraph{Interpretation of survival.}
The finite computation supports a bounded contact statement rather than a biological promotion. Measured TE absorbs a positive but modest part of the modeled-tAI-complement residual in all four organism-specific joined surfaces, with the largest measured-TE absorption in \emph{D. rerio} and the smallest in yeast. The human and \emph{D. rerio} self-check failures also show that TE availability changes the protein-id join enough to alter the complement target, so those organisms should be read as valid measured-TE projections on their own joined matrices, not as exact replicas of the full-abundance complement experiment. The yeast and \emph{E. coli} projections are more stable under this join comparison. In all cases the statistic is a projection quantity, not a causal mediator analysis.

\paragraph{Comparison with modeled tRNA-copy tAI.}
A separate measured-tRNA-gene-copy tAI contact uses the same nine-coordinate residual basis and the same rank-$23$ control design but does not use ribosome-profiling TE rows. Its full-join checks match the complement reference exactly for \emph{D. rerio}, human, and yeast, with joined counts $14410$, $19053$, and $5815$. The tRNA gene-copy tables contain $8846$ genes and $57$ anticodon classes for \emph{D. rerio}, $431$ genes and $47$ anticodon classes for human, and $273$ genes and $41$ anticodon classes for yeast. The absorbed fractions in that modeled-tRNA contact are $0.020817102845637092$, $0.00005426229300865148$, and $0.001530747712165342$ for \emph{D. rerio}, human, and yeast. This comparison keeps the present measured-TE record in scope: tRNA-copy tAI can be used as a bounded codon-adaptation readback, whereas ribosome-profiling TE is the direct measured translation-efficiency contact used here.

\paragraph{Cross-organism caution.}
The broader organism-specific metadata do not license a universal rule. In a twelve-organism summary, the dominant-coordinate contingency test across domain labels gives Pearson chi-square $9.399999999999999$, Cramer's $V=0.6258327785172862$, and permutation right-tail $p=0.5814185814185814$ over $1000$ trials. Continuous tests for $M_{QTP}$ fraction likewise remain exploratory: versus GC3, $r=-0.3062616500469447$ with two-sided permutation $p=0.35264735264735264$; versus log proteome size, $r=-0.033628115789504955$ with $p=0.913086913086913$; versus log tRNA-gene count, $r=0.06029405206037823$ with $p=0.8621378621378621$. The domain comparison has variance ratio $0.8889980452277374$ and right-tail permutation $p=0.4155844155844156$. These finite values say that the organism layer is heterogeneous and underpowered for a domain-level law, even though the measured-TE contact itself is row-rich within each joined organism.

\paragraph{Local boundary.}
The chapter therefore records a measured translation-efficiency contact for the $B^*_{Q6}$ residual surface after matched mRNA and sequence-composition controls. It does not establish translation realization in the mechanistic sense, ribosome mechanism, protein abundance, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, organismal phenotype, or universality. Any such claim requires a separate layer-matched contact and its own controlled statistic. In particular, a positive absorbed fraction for measured TE is not a protein-output claim, and a small absorbed fraction is not evidence that the residual basis is biologically irrelevant; it only says that this particular measured-TE surface leaves most of the modeled-tAI-complement residual outside the tested translation-efficiency coordinate.
