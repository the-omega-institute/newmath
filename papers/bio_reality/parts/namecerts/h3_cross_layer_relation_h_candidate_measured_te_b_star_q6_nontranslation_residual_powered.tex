\subsection{Measured Translation-Efficiency Contact for the $B^*_{Q6}$ Complement Residual}
\label{sec:bioreality-q6-measured-te-complement-residual}

\paragraph{Finite surface.}
This record tests whether a measured ribosome-profiling translation-efficiency readout accounts for any part of the $B^*_{Q6}$ complement residual after the usual coding-sequence controls have already been removed.  The finite carrier is the joined panel of coding sequences with nine residual coordinates,
$K\_AAA$, $Arg\_AGR$, $Ile\_AUA$, $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$, $Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG$, $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$, $Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG$, $Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY$, and $f3\_stress$, together with a matched translation-efficiency value and matched abundance controls.  The tested readout is not a protein, phenotype, structure, or causal intervention.  It is a per-CDS scalar derived from ribosome-footprint signal divided by matched mRNA abundance, used here only as a bounded translation-efficiency coordinate.

\paragraph{Control rank and joined rows.}
The control design $Z$ contains an intercept, $\log(cds\_len\_nt)$, the twenty amino-acid composition proportions computed from real CDS codon counts, GC3, and the $M$-density baseline on the union support of the nine projected $B^*_{Q6}$ vectors.  The declared control-column count is $24$ and the observed rank is $23$ in each organism-specific panel.  The joined TE panels are:
$$
\begin{aligned}
&n_{Danio\ rerio}=13140,\qquad n_{E.\ coli\ K12}=3415,\\
&n_{Homo\ sapiens}=11554,\qquad n_{Saccharomyces\ cerevisiae}=5128.
\end{aligned}
$$
The corresponding reported CDS-abundance joins before the measured-TE restriction are $14410$, $3739$, $19053$, and $5815$.  Thus the measured-TE availability changes the finite row set for zebrafish and human in a visible way, while the bacterial and yeast row restrictions are closer to their complement-reference panels.

\paragraph{Projection statistic.}
For each organism, the measured-TE candidate is evaluated by projecting the modeled-tAI complement abundance residual $\widehat P_{Q\perp Tmod}$ onto residualized measured TE on the same joined rows.  The complement magnitude and the residual magnitude after the modeled translation proxy are reported as
$$
\begin{aligned}
&D_{Danio}=0.8424312412078521,\qquad D_{E.\ coli}=0.6628211177352172,\\
&D_{Human}=0.8566190269974241,\qquad D_{Yeast}=0.22386566043976477.
\end{aligned}
$$
In squared-norm form, the corresponding pairs $(\|P_Q\|^2,\|P_{Q\perp Tmod}\|^2)$ are $(1092.0114815505467,919.9445878158525)$, $(393.78692095732885,261.0102870984463)$, $(565.3850201235074,484.31956581711796)$, and $(675.3275479192903,151.18264752811882)$ for zebrafish, $E.\ coli$, human, and yeast respectively.

\paragraph{Measured-TE absorption.}
The observed measured-TE absorption is positive in all four panels but small:
$$
\begin{aligned}
&A_{TE,Danio}=0.01759429001506974,\qquad A_{TE,E.\ coli}=0.0010982956489726373,\\
&A_{TE,Human}=0.0028947149257963897,\qquad A_{TE,Yeast}=0.0002762783754375593.
\end{aligned}
$$
After adding measured TE to the projection surface, the combined residual fractions are $0.8276090159036916$, $0.6620871850159149$, $0.854133502300174$, and $0.2237928123367028$.  The largest measured-TE absorption in this finite table is therefore zebrafish, at about $1.76\%$ of the modeled complement residual.  The other three panels are below $0.3\%$, with yeast below $0.03\%$.  This supports the narrow statement that a measured TE coordinate can be computed on the joined surface and has nonzero descriptive overlap with the complement residual, but it does not close the residual as a translation-mediated explanation.

\paragraph{Join-stability check.}
The experiment includes a self-check against the full abundance-CDS complement reference.  The differences between the current all-readout join and the complement-reference join are $0.03219747984141397$ in zebrafish, $0.00017488612263594217$ in $E.\ coli$, $0.06427590857052146$ in human, and $0.012133464712697745$ in yeast.  With a $0.02$ tolerance, $E.\ coli$ and yeast pass this join-stability check, while zebrafish and human do not.  The finite conclusion must therefore be organism- and row-set-sensitive: the measured-TE projection is computed and bounded, but the strongest multi-organism reading is weakened by the fact that measured-TE availability changes the residual target in two of the four panels.

\paragraph{Relation to modeled tRNA-copy tAI.}
A linked finite surface tests measured tRNA gene-copy tAI on the full CDS-abundance joins.  In that contact, the organism joins are $14410$ for zebrafish, $19053$ for human, and $5815$ for yeast, with anticodon table sizes $57$, $47$, and $41$ and tRNA gene counts $8846$, $431$, and $273$.  Its absorption values are $0.020817102845637092$ for zebrafish, $0.00005426229300865148$ for human, and $0.001530747712165342$ for yeast.  This comparison matters only as a bounded calibration: the measured-TE contact is not interchangeable with a modeled tAI or stAI contact.  The TE panel measures an observed ribosome-profiling efficiency surface under matched mRNA control; the tRNA-copy panel is a modeled codon-adaptation readback.  Neither surface, by itself, supplies protein output, molecular mechanism, folded structure, or phenotype.

\paragraph{Cross-organism scope.}
The cross-organism meta table gives additional caution against turning the TE result into a broad biological law.  In the assembled organism-specific table, the complete-feature row count is $12$, while the wider row count is $16$ with missing data for $pseudomonas\_aeruginosa\_pao1$, $halobacterium\_salinarum$, $dictyostelium\_discoideum$, and $arabidopsis\_thaliana$.  The dominant-coordinate versus domain contingency has $n=12$, Pearson chi-square $9.399999999999999$, Cramer's $V=0.6258327785172862$, and permutation right-tail $p=0.5814185814185814$ over $1000$ trials.  The $M_{QTP}$ fraction has weak continuous associations in the same finite table: $r=-0.3062616500469447$ against GC3 with two-sided permutation $p=0.35264735264735264$, $r=-0.033628115789504955$ against log proteome size with $p=0.913086913086913$, and $r=0.06029405206037823$ against log tRNA gene count with $p=0.8621378621378621$.  These numbers are descriptive cross-organism summaries, not phylogenetically corrected inference.

\paragraph{Local verdict.}
The finite-row verdict is that the measured-TE H-candidate is computable and bounded on the four organism-specific panels, with positive but small absorption of the modeled-tAI complement residual.  The result is strongest as a negative or limiting record: measured TE does not absorb most of the non-translation residual, and the row-set instability in zebrafish and human prevents a stable population-level claim.  The local boundary is strict.  No translation-realization claim beyond this measured TE statistic, no folding claim, no physical-admissibility claim, no molecular-function claim, no causal mechanism, and no universal biological law follows from these rows unless a separate layer-matched contact supplies the corresponding finite observations and controls.
