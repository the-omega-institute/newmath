\subsection{Measured translation-efficiency contact for the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual complement}
\label{sec:bioreality-q6-measured-te-residual-complement}

\paragraph{Finite readout surface.}
This record treats the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual basis as a finite coordinate system tested against measured ribosome-profiling translation-efficiency readouts under matched transcript-abundance control. The carrier is the joined CDS-by-organism surface on which nine residual coordinates, measured translation efficiency, CDS-derived controls, and matched mRNA abundance are simultaneously available. The nine coordinates are $K_{\mathrm{AAA}}$, $\mathrm{Arg}_{\mathrm{AGR}}$, $\mathrm{Ile}_{\mathrm{AUA}}$, $\mathrm{Leu}_{\mathrm{CUN}\,\mathrm{vs}\,\mathrm{UUR}}$, $\mathrm{Leu}_{\mathrm{UUA}\,\mathrm{vs}\,\mathrm{UUG}}$, $\mathrm{Ser}_{\mathrm{UCR}\,\mathrm{vs}\,\mathrm{AGY}}$, $\mathrm{Ser}_{\mathrm{UCA}\,\mathrm{vs}\,\mathrm{UCG}}$, $\mathrm{Thr}_{\mathrm{ACR}\,\mathrm{vs}\,\mathrm{ACY}}$, and $f_{3}\text{-}\mathrm{stress}$. The control design has $24$ columns and rank $23$ in each organism. Its declared covariates are an intercept, $\log(\mathrm{CDS\ length})$, the $20$ amino-acid composition proportions computed from real CDS codon counts, GC3, and an M-density baseline defined as the fraction of sense codons lying on the union support of the nine projected $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ vectors. In this chapter the measured translation-efficiency readout is not allowed to stand alone: matched mRNA abundance is part of the boundary that separates translation-efficiency residualization from transcription-layer abundance.

\paragraph{Joined organism rows.}
The finite panel is rank-powered for the reported regression geometry. The joined measured-TE counts are $n=13{,}140$ for \emph{Danio rerio}, $n=3{,}415$ for \emph{Escherichia coli} K-12 MG1655, $n=11{,}554$ for \emph{Homo sapiens}, and $n=5{,}128$ for \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}. The corresponding CDS joins before measured-TE filtering are $14{,}410$, $3{,}739$, $19{,}053$, and $5{,}815$. The valid measured-TE counts are the same as the joined rows, while the valid abundance-by-protein counts are $14{,}410$, $3{,}747$, $19{,}483$, and $6{,}351$. Thus the measured-TE contact changes the protein-id join in two organisms by enough to make the self-check visibly negative: \emph{Danio rerio} moves from the full-join modeled-complement reference $0.8746287210$ to $0.8424312412$, an absolute difference of $0.0321974798$, and \emph{Homo sapiens} moves from $0.9208949356$ to $0.8566190270$, an absolute difference of $0.0642759086$. The same check remains within the $0.02$ tolerance for \emph{E. coli} K-12 MG1655, where $0.6629960039$ and $0.6628211177$ differ by $0.0001748861$, and for budding yeast, where $0.2117321957$ and $0.2238656604$ differ by $0.0121334647$.

\paragraph{Projection statistic.}
The measured-TE test is a descriptive projection of the modeled-tAI-complement abundance residual $\widehat{P}_{Q\perp T_{\mathrm{mod}}}$ onto residualized measured translation efficiency. The reported modeled-complement fractions on the all-readout join are $0.8424312412$ for \emph{Danio rerio}, $0.6628211177$ for \emph{E. coli} K-12 MG1655, $0.8566190270$ for \emph{Homo sapiens}, and $0.2238656604$ for \emph{S. cerevisiae}. The underlying squared norms are, respectively,
$$
\begin{aligned}
\lVert P_Q\rVert^2 &= 1092.0114815505, & \lVert P_{Q\perp T_{\mathrm{mod}}}\rVert^2 &= 919.9445878159,\\
\lVert P_Q\rVert^2 &= 393.7869209573, & \lVert P_{Q\perp T_{\mathrm{mod}}}\rVert^2 &= 261.0102870984,\\
\lVert P_Q\rVert^2 &= 565.3850201235, & \lVert P_{Q\perp T_{\mathrm{mod}}}\rVert^2 &= 484.3195658171,\\
\lVert P_Q\rVert^2 &= 675.3275479193, & \lVert P_{Q\perp T_{\mathrm{mod}}}\rVert^2 &= 151.1826475281.
\end{aligned}
$$
Measured TE absorbs only small fractions of that complement: $0.0175942900$ in \emph{Danio rerio}, $0.0010982956$ in \emph{E. coli} K-12 MG1655, $0.0028947149$ in \emph{Homo sapiens}, and $0.0002762784$ in \emph{S. cerevisiae}. The combined residual fractions after adding measured TE are $0.8276090159$, $0.6620871850$, $0.8541335023$, and $0.2237928123$. This is a positive finite computation, but its scale is modest: the measured-TE readout does not remove the non-translation residual component in any organism, and in yeast the already-small upstream complement is essentially unchanged.

\paragraph{Neighboring finite contacts.}
The same residual complement was compared to other bounded readout surfaces. A measured tRNA gene-copy tAI contact used full CDS joins of $14{,}410$, $19{,}053$, and $5{,}815$ in \emph{Danio rerio}, \emph{Homo sapiens}, and \emph{S. cerevisiae}, with tRNA gene counts $8{,}846$, $431$, and $273$, and absorbed fractions $0.0208171028$, $0.0000542623$, and $0.0015307477$. A yeast turnover contact joined $3{,}640$ protein half-life rows, passed the modeled-complement self-check with absolute difference $0.0115382552$, and absorbed $0.0009086032$ of the complement. A reviewed-feature contact joined $2{,}242$ zebrafish rows, $18{,}335$ human rows, and $5{,}682$ yeast rows; its strongest descriptive entries were PTM density in zebrafish at $0.0142112401$ and PTM density in human at $0.0148848259$, with Bonferroni excess also reported for human complex membership and human transmembrane count. These neighboring contacts keep the measured-TE result in scale: they are finite projections on sparse or organism-specific readouts, not a promotion of the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ coordinates into a unified biological mechanism.

\paragraph{Cross-organism caution.}
A broader organism-specific summary assembled $16$ organism rows, of which $12$ had complete feature metadata for the reported tests. The domain test for $M_{\mathrm{QTP}}$ fraction had a variance ratio of $0.8889980452$ and a right-tail permutation value $p=0.4155844156$. Continuous tests were also weak in this finite table: $M_{\mathrm{QTP}}$ fraction versus GC3 had Pearson $r=-0.3062616500$ with two-sided permutation value $p=0.3526473526$, versus log proteome size had $r=-0.0336281158$ with $p=0.9130869131$, and versus log tRNA gene count had $r=0.0602940521$ with $p=0.8621378621$. The dominant-coordinate contingency had $n=12$, chi-square $9.4$, Cramer's $V=0.6258327785$, and right-tail permutation value $p=0.5814185814$. The localization boundary is even narrower: the available mitochondrial-propensity contact joined $8{,}696$ human proteins, with $657$ mitochondrial rows and $2{,}000$ permutations. These scalars support a bounded, organism-aware reading only.

\paragraph{Local verdict.}
The measured-TE contact is ready as a finite BioReality chapter because it reports the exact joined rows, control rank, coordinate list, projection norms, absorbed fractions, and join-stability checks. Its conclusion is deliberately limited. The data say that measured ribosome-profiling translation efficiency can be placed next to the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ modeled-tAI-complement residual and that the observed absorption is small across the four organisms. They do not say that the residual basis has been explained by translation efficiency, nor that the coordinates realize a causal translation process. No claim about translation as a mechanism, protein abundance, folding, structural order, physical admissibility, molecular function, phenotype, adaptation, or universality follows from this projection unless a separate layer-matched contact supplies the relevant measurement and statistic.
