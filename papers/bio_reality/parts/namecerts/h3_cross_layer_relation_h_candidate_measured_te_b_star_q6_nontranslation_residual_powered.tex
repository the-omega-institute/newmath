\subsection{Measured translation-efficiency contact for the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual basis}\label{subsec:bioreality-q6-measured-te-contact}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite surface.}
This record tests whether the nine-coordinate $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ synonymous residual basis is absorbed by an organism-matched ribosome-profiling translation-efficiency readout after the coding-sequence covariates have already been accounted for.  The finite coordinate list is
$K\_AAA$, $Arg\_AGR$, $Ile\_AUA$, $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$, $Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG$, $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$, $Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG$, $Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY$, and $f3\_stress$.  The control design contains an intercept, $\log(\mathrm{cds\_len\_nt})$, the twenty amino-acid composition proportions $A,C,D,E,F,G,H,I,K,L,M,N,P,Q,R,S,T,V,W,Y$, $GC3$, and an $M$-density baseline defined from the union support of the nine projected $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ vectors.  Each organism has $24$ control columns and rank $23$, so the reported readout is not a free comparison of raw codon counts against translation efficiency; it is a projection after a fixed coding-composition boundary has been imposed.

\paragraph{Joined panels.}
The measured translation-efficiency join is finite and organism-specific.  The row counts are $n=13{,}140$ for \emph{Danio rerio}, $n=3{,}415$ for \emph{Escherichia coli} K12 MG1655, $n=11{,}554$ for \emph{Homo sapiens}, and $n=5{,}128$ for \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}.  These rows are smaller than the coding-sequence abundance joins in three organisms: $14{,}410$ CDS-abundance rows for zebrafish, $3{,}739$ for \emph{E. coli}, $19{,}053$ for human, and $5{,}815$ for yeast.  The matched translation-efficiency availability is therefore itself part of the finite surface; the test is evaluated only on proteins for which the coding-sequence row and the measured TE row meet on the same protein identifier.

\paragraph{Projection statistic.}
Let $P\_{Q}$ denote the controlled $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual component and let $P\_{Q\perp Tmod}$ denote the part left after the modeled tAI direction has been removed.  The chapter-level statistic asks how much of this modeled-tAI complement is further absorbed when residualized measured TE is added.  The reported complement fractions are
$$
\begin{aligned}
 d\_{\mathrm{modeled}}(\mathrm{Danio}) &= 0.8424312412078521,\\
 d\_{\mathrm{modeled}}(\mathrm{E.coli}) &= 0.6628211177352172,\\
 d\_{\mathrm{modeled}}(\mathrm{Human}) &= 0.8566190269974241,\\
 d\_{\mathrm{modeled}}(\mathrm{Yeast}) &= 0.22386566043976477.
\end{aligned}
$$
The corresponding measured-TE absorptions are small:
$$
\begin{aligned}
 a\_{\mathrm{TE}}(\mathrm{Danio}) &= 0.01759429001506974,\\
 a\_{\mathrm{TE}}(\mathrm{E.coli}) &= 0.0010982956489726373,\\
 a\_{\mathrm{TE}}(\mathrm{Human}) &= 0.0028947149257963897,\\
 a\_{\mathrm{TE}}(\mathrm{Yeast}) &= 0.0002762783754375593.
\end{aligned}
$$
After adding measured TE, the combined residual fractions are $0.8276090159036916$, $0.6620871850159149$, $0.854133502300174$, and $0.2237928123367028$ in the same organism order.  Thus the measured TE contact is not empty, but it removes only a limited part of the modeled-tAI complement on these joins.  In human and \emph{E. coli}, the complement remains close to its pre-TE value; in yeast the upstream complement is already much smaller, and the extra measured-TE absorption is nearly zero.

\paragraph{Join-stability self-check.}
The same statistic is sensitive to the measured-TE availability boundary.  When the current all-readout join is compared with the full abundance-CDS complement reference, the absolute differences in $d\_{\mathrm{modeled}}$ are $0.03219747984141397$ for zebrafish, $0.00017488612263594217$ for \emph{E. coli}, $0.06427590857052146$ for human, and $0.012133464712697745$ for yeast.  Under the $0.02$ self-check tolerance, \emph{E. coli} and yeast match the reference surface, while zebrafish and human do not.  The failed matches do not invalidate the finite computation; they say that measured-TE availability changes the protein-identifier join enough that a cross-organism biological interpretation must be phrased as a bounded readout rather than as a stable law.

\paragraph{Population-level guardrail.}
The associated organism-specificity survey keeps the population claim exploratory.  In the complete-feature meta table, $12$ organisms are available out of $16$ assembled rows.  The domain test for $M\_{QTP}$ has variance ratio $0.8889980452277374$ with right-tail permutation value $p=0.4155844155844156$.  The continuous checks give $r=-0.3062616500469447$ for $M\_{QTP}$ versus $GC3$ with two-sided permutation value $p=0.35264735264735264$, $r=-0.033628115789504955$ versus log proteome size with $p=0.913086913086913$, and $r=0.06029405206037823$ versus log tRNA-gene count with $p=0.8621378621378621$.  Dominant-coordinate enrichment by domain has chi-square $9.399999999999999$, Cramer's $V=0.6258327785172862$, and permutation value $p=0.5814185814185814$.  These finite values support heterogeneity bookkeeping, not a resolved phylogenetic or domain-wide rule.

\paragraph{Local claim.}
The measured translation-efficiency contact therefore licenses a narrow statement: on the four organism-specific joined panels, the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ modeled-tAI complement can be projected against measured ribosome-profiling TE, and the additional absorption by measured TE is positive but small in all four organisms.  The strongest value is zebrafish at $0.01759429001506974$; the weakest is yeast at $0.0002762783754375593$.  Because two organism joins fail the $0.02$ reference-match check and because the population-level permutation tests do not resolve a domain or continuous driver, the result is best read as a bounded cross-layer contact with residual power remaining outside the measured TE surface.

\paragraph{No-promotion boundary.}
This chapter does not infer translation mechanism, mature protein abundance, folding state, physical admissibility, molecular function, phenotype, causation, or universality from the measured-TE projection.  Ribosome-profiling TE is a condition-specific transcript-normalized readout, and the absorption fraction is a descriptive projection rather than a mediator test.  A later claim about protein abundance, structure, localization, turnover, function, or a general biological rule would require its own layer-matched contact and its own finite statistic.  The present record only says that the measured TE surface has been attached to the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual panel and that, under the declared controls and joins, it leaves most of the modeled-tAI complement unabsorbed.
