\subsection{Measured Translation-Efficiency Contact for the $B^*_{Q6}$ Residual Complement}
\label{sec:bioreality-q6-measured-te-complement}

\paragraph{Finite readout surface.}
The measured translation-efficiency contact considered here is a finite CDS-by-organism surface in which the nine $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinates are joined to organism-matched ribosome-profiling translation-efficiency rows and to the protein-abundance join used by the modeled-tAI complement residual.  The coordinate list is
$$
\begin{aligned}
&K_{AAA},\ Arg_{AGR},\ Ile_{AUA},\ Leu_{CUN\,vs\,UUR},\ Leu_{UUA\,vs\,UUG},\\
&Ser_{UCR\,vs\,AGY},\ Ser_{UCA\,vs\,UCG},\ Thr_{ACR\,vs\,ACY},\ f_3\text{-stress}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The control design has $24$ columns with residual rank $23$ in each measured-TE organism.  The columns are an intercept, $\log(\mathrm{cds\_len\_nt})$, the $20$ amino-acid composition proportions $A,C,D,E,F,G,H,I,K,L,M,N,P,Q,R,S,T,V,W,Y$, GC3 computed from the same CDS codon counts, and an $M$-density baseline equal to the fraction of sense codons lying on the union support of the nine projected $B^*_{Q6}$ vectors.  Translation efficiency is treated as the ribosome-footprint readout normalized by matched mRNA abundance in the same condition, so the result is not a transcript-abundance association by itself.

\paragraph{Joined rows and rank.}
Four organisms have the measured-TE join.  In \emph{Danio rerio}, the all-readout join contains $n=13140$ CDS/protein rows, compared with $14410$ CDS rows joined for abundance and $13140$ valid TE rows.  In \emph{Escherichia coli} K12 MG1655, the join contains $n=3415$ rows, with $3739$ CDS rows joined for abundance and $3415$ valid TE rows.  In \emph{Homo sapiens}, the join contains $n=11554$ rows, with $19053$ CDS rows joined for abundance and $11554$ valid TE rows.  In \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}, the join contains $n=5128$ rows, with $5815$ CDS rows joined for abundance and $5128$ valid TE rows.  The common rank condition is therefore not an under-ranked display artifact: each organism has $\operatorname{rank}(Z)=23$ under the same $24$-column design.

\paragraph{Modeled-complement residual before measured TE.}
The object being tested is not a direct protein, structure, or phenotype output.  It is the portion of the abundance-side $B^*_{Q6}$ residual that remains after the modeled-tAI projection has been removed.  On the measured-TE all-readout join, the squared norms and residual fractions are
$$
\begin{aligned}
\textit{D. rerio}:&\quad \|P_Q\|^2=1092.0114815505467,\quad
\|P_{Q\perp T_{mod}}\|^2=919.9445878158525,\quad d_{modeled}=0.8424312412078521,\\
\textit{E. coli}:&\quad \|P_Q\|^2=393.78692095732885,\quad
\|P_{Q\perp T_{mod}}\|^2=261.0102870984463,\quad d_{modeled}=0.6628211177352172,\\
\textit{H. sapiens}:&\quad \|P_Q\|^2=565.3850201235074,\quad
\|P_{Q\perp T_{mod}}\|^2=484.31956581711796,\quad d_{modeled}=0.8566190269974241,\\
\textit{S. cerevisiae}:&\quad \|P_Q\|^2=675.3275479192903,\quad
\|P_{Q\perp T_{mod}}\|^2=151.18264752811882,\quad d_{modeled}=0.22386566043976477.
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the finite test asks whether observed TE absorbs any of this modeled-tAI complement, after the same sequence-composition and CDS-level controls have already been imposed.

\paragraph{Measured-TE absorption.}
The measured-TE projection is small in all four organisms.  The absorbed fractions and combined residual fractions are
$$
\begin{aligned}
\textit{D. rerio}:&\quad \mathrm{absorbed}_{TE}=0.01759429001506974,\quad d_{combined}=0.8276090159036916,\\
\textit{E. coli}:&\quad \mathrm{absorbed}_{TE}=0.0010982956489726373,\quad d_{combined}=0.6620871850159149,\\
\textit{H. sapiens}:&\quad \mathrm{absorbed}_{TE}=0.0028947149257963897,\quad d_{combined}=0.854133502300174,\\
\textit{S. cerevisiae}:&\quad \mathrm{absorbed}_{TE}=0.0002762783754375593,\quad d_{combined}=0.2237928123367028.
\end{aligned}
$$
The largest measured-TE absorption is the zebrafish value, about $1.76\%$ of the modeled-complement residual target on its TE-available join.  Human, bacterial, and yeast values are much smaller.  The finite-row conclusion is therefore negative for a strong translation-efficiency explanation of the non-modeled component: measured TE is computable and properly joined, but it does not substantially collapse the $B^*_{Q6}$ modeled-complement residual.

\paragraph{Join sensitivity.}
The self-check against the full abundance-CDS complement join shows that TE availability changes the analyzed target for some organisms.  The full-join complement references are $0.874628721049266$ for zebrafish, $0.6629960038578532$ for \emph{E. coli}, $0.9208949355679455$ for human, and $0.21173219572706703$ for yeast.  The measured-TE all-readout joins give $0.8424312412078521$, $0.6628211177352172$, $0.8566190269974241$, and $0.22386566043976477$, respectively.  The absolute differences are $0.03219747984141397$ for zebrafish, $0.00017488612263594217$ for \emph{E. coli}, $0.06427590857052146$ for human, and $0.012133464712697745$ for yeast.  Only \emph{E. coli} and yeast fall within the $0.02$ tolerance.  The finite contact should therefore be read as a measured-TE absorption calculation on the TE-available panel, not as a population-invariant statement about the full abundance join.

\paragraph{Relation to adjacent measured contacts.}
The neighboring finite contacts support the same bounded interpretation.  Measured tRNA-gene-copy tAI absorbs only $0.020817102845637092$ in zebrafish, $0.00005426229300865148$ in human, and $0.001530747712165342$ in yeast on the full tRNA joins.  Yeast turnover absorbs $0.0009086031889966327$ on $3640$ joined turnover rows, with $d_{combined}=0.22306503721327192$.  Sparse protein-feature contacts can show larger feature-specific descriptive absorptions, such as human PTM density at $0.014884825920540636$ and zebrafish PTM density at $0.014211240113472575$, but the protein-feature run reports unstable joins, including a zebrafish complement difference of $0.1384608720516286$.  The organism-specificity screen is also descriptive rather than decisive: the domain test for $M_{QTP}$ fraction has $n=12$ and permutation $p=0.4155844155844156$, the dominant-coordinate contingency has permutation $p=0.5814185814185814$, and the continuous tests give $p=0.35264735264735264$ for GC3, $p=0.913086913086913$ for proteome size, and $p=0.8621378621378621$ for tRNA-gene count.  The localization boundary likewise remains a bounded readout, with $8696$ joined human rows, $657$ mitochondrial labels, and $2000$ permutations in the Itzhak-style contact.

\paragraph{Local interpretation.}
This chapter licenses only the following finite statement: on the joined measured-TE panels, the $B^*_{Q6}$ modeled-tAI complement residual is projected against residualized ribosome-profiling TE, and the observed TE absorption is small under the stated controls.  A positive or negative value here is a descriptive projection statistic, not a causal mediator test.  No claim about translation as a general mechanism, protein abundance, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, phenotype, or universality follows from this surface.  Any such higher-layer interpretation would require its own layer-matched contact and its own finite statistic, joined to the relevant rows rather than inferred from the $B^*_{Q6}$ residual geometry or from the existence of ribosome-profiling measurements alone.
