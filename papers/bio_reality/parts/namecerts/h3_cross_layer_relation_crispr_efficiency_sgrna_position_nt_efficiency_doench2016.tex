\subsection{CRISPR Efficiency as a Finite Row Boundary for Position-Nucleotide Signal}\label{sec:bioreality-crispr-efficiency-sgrna-position-nt-efficiency-doench2016}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite table.}
The Doench-style CRISPR-efficiency record is treated here as a finite row table for guide-level sequence response, not as a translation experiment.  The verified row count is $n=5310$, with a recorded main GC association $r=-0.0582917$ and runtime $24.6506$ seconds for the finite computation.  Thus the available table has enough rows to support a bounded check of guide-sequence features against CRISPR activity, but it does not by itself contain a same-CDS codon-window panel, wobble-weight rows, matched mRNA abundance, or codon-indexed ribosome-footprint occupancy.  The local object is therefore a guide-position nucleotide response surface with a measured efficiency endpoint, while the $f_3$-stress translation-survival proposal remains only conditionally attached.

\paragraph{Coordinate separation.}
Let $G$ denote the finite set of guide rows, let $Y_g$ be the measured CRISPR-efficiency response for guide $g$, and let $X_g$ collect guide-local position and nucleotide features.  The verified scalar $r_{GC,Y}=-0.0582917$ is a weak signed association between a GC summary and the CRISPR endpoint, not a statement about coding-window translation.  The row-level relation available in this chapter is therefore bounded by
$$
\begin{aligned}
  g &\in G,\qquad |G|=5310,\\
  X_g &=(\hbox{guide position features, nucleotide features, GC summary}),\\
  Y_g &=\hbox{measured CRISPR-efficiency endpoint},\\
  r_{GC,Y} &=-0.0582917.
\end{aligned}
$$
This establishes a finite empirical contact for sgRNA sequence-to-efficiency behavior.  It does not establish that a coding-sequence residual coordinate survives against a translation readout, because the rows are keyed to guide design and editing efficiency rather than to ordered CDS windows with matched ribosome occupancy.

\paragraph{Relation to the proposed $f_3$-stress packet.}
The proposed $f_3$-stress survival claim requires a different finite table: each row must contain an amino-acid-quotiented $f_3$ residual coordinate, local synonymous codon counts, a bounded wobble-weight context, a same-window translation readout, matched mRNA control, and position or amino-acid controls.  The CRISPR-efficiency table supplies none of those translation-window columns.  Its relevance is consequently negative but useful: it shows that a sequence-position endpoint can be represented as a finite controlled response table, while also marking the exact place where guide-efficiency evidence stops.

\paragraph{Rank and control condition.}
For the translation-survival conjecture, the admissible statistic would have to be a held-out controlled improvement for a position-resolved translation endpoint.  In local notation this would require a non-vacuous entry
$$
\begin{aligned}
  S_{f_3}(T_{\mathrm{position}}\mid Z) &> 0,\\
  Z &=\{\hbox{amino-acid or position bin},\ GC3,\ \hbox{length},\ M\hbox{-density},\ \hbox{mRNA},\ \hbox{simpler baselines}\},
\end{aligned}
$$
computed on joined same-organism and same-CDS windows.  The present CRISPR fact gives only $n=5310$ guide-efficiency rows and the GC association above.  It does not give $T_{\mathrm{position}}$, a ribosome-footprint matrix, a translation-efficiency denominator, a wobble-weight asymmetry check, or a rank-powered survival score after the required ablations.

\paragraph{Cross-check against a powered translation-side record.}
A separate verified translation-conditioned structural-order record illustrates the kind of scalar evidence that would be needed before a cross-layer promotion could even begin: it reports five folds, a minimum joined size of $300$, organism joined counts $3412$ for \textit{Escherichia coli} K-12 MG1655 and $5119$ for \textit{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}, a weighted full-control $\Delta R^2=0.004764742175882462$, and a full-control sign-flip probability $p=0.0029296875$.  Those values are not imported as support for the CRISPR-efficiency claim.  They are cited only to fix the evidential standard: a positive cross-layer call requires an explicitly joined biological readout table, held-out or permutation-checked improvement, and named controls.  The CRISPR-efficiency row set has a measured endpoint, but it is not the position-resolved ribosome-occupancy endpoint required by the $f_3$-stress survival statement.

\paragraph{Local verdict.}
The chapter supports a bounded finite-row statement: sgRNA position and nucleotide features can be evaluated against a measured CRISPR-efficiency endpoint in a table of $5310$ rows, and the observed GC main association is $-0.0582917$.  The chapter does not support a translation-survival claim for $f_3$-stress.  It also does not support protein abundance, folding, physical admissibility, biological function, mechanism, phenotype, or universality.  Any such claim would require a separate row-matched contact at the relevant layer, with its own endpoint and controls.  Until same-CDS translation rows, mRNA controls, wobble weights, position-resolved ribosome occupancy, and a positive controlled survival statistic are supplied, this record remains a CRISPR-efficiency sequence-response entry and a boundary marker for the missing translation-window experiment.
