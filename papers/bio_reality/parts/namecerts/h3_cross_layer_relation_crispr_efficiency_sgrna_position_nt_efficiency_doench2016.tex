\subsection{Position-resolved sgRNA efficiency as a bounded row certificate}
\label{sec:bioreality-sgrna-position-nt-efficiency-doench2016}

\paragraph{Finite row surface.}
The record indexed by \texttt{h3.cross\_layer\_relation.crispr\_efficiency.sgrna\_position\_nt\_efficiency\_doench2016} is a finite-row contact with a measured CRISPR-efficiency response, not a general biological law. Its primary audited table contains $n=5310$ Doench-style sgRNA rows. The run identifier is \texttt{b6c6363256e0}; the recorded scalar control check gives a main GC correlation of $-0.0582917$, and the finite computation completed in $24.6506$ seconds. These numbers are the row-level surface used here: the certificate says that position-indexed nucleotide features can be tested against sgRNA efficiency on this finite panel, with GC behavior explicitly recorded rather than inferred from sequence grammar alone.

\paragraph{Cross-protocol boundary.}
A second audited row surface, \texttt{h3.cross\_layer\_relation.crispr\_efficiency.sgrna\_position\_nt\_crossdataset\_luo2020}, reports $n_{\mathrm{Luo}}=10543$ rows, marks \texttt{cross\_protocol=true}, and records \texttt{frozen\_source=Doench2016\ repo}. Its run identifier is \texttt{2e9cce255c01}, with runtime $33.705459$ seconds. This companion surface is not merged into the Doench row count, but it matters for the local certificate: the Doench position-nucleotide signal has an explicitly named cross-protocol test route, and the finite evidence is therefore not only an in-table feature enumeration. It remains a row-indexed CRISPR-efficiency contact, because the cross-protocol flag certifies transfer of the sgRNA-position readout setting, not a mechanism for Cas9 binding, DNA repair, chromatin accessibility, or cellular fitness.

\paragraph{Coordinate interpretation.}
For this chapter the admissible mathematical object is a finite table $T$ whose rows carry sgRNA position, nucleotide identity, local sequence controls, and an efficiency readout. The observed scalar $r_{\mathrm{GC}}=-0.0582917$ is treated as a control coordinate for the Doench panel, not as the causal explanation of the efficiency response. A minimal local statistic can be written as
$$
\begin{aligned}
S_{\mathrm{pos}}(T\mid Z)
  &= \Delta\mathcal{L}\bigl(\mathrm{efficiency};\; \mathrm{position\text{-}nt}, Z\bigr),\\
Z
  &= \{\mathrm{GC},\; \mathrm{guide\ length},\; \mathrm{position\ bins},\; \mathrm{simpler\ sequence\ baselines}\},
\end{aligned}
$$
where $S_{\mathrm{pos}}(T\mid Z)$ denotes only the finite improvement or survival of position-nucleotide information after the declared controls. The available audit numbers justify the existence of the finite CRISPR-efficiency row surface and the GC scalar check; they do not by themselves assert a positive value of every possible controlled statistic.

\paragraph{$f_3$-stress relation status.}
The broader conjectural packet asks whether an $f_3$-stress synonymous residual coordinate can survive as a bounded cross-layer coordinate when matched to position-resolved ribosome occupancy or translation-efficiency readouts under wobble-weight asymmetry. The Doench sgRNA-efficiency table is not such a translation readout. It can serve as a finite position-resolved efficiency surface for the CRISPR branch of the program, while the $f_3$ translation-survival packet remains blocked until same-organism, same-CDS, same-window codon counts, wobble-weight rows, matched mRNA controls, and ribosome-footprint or explicitly bounded translation-efficiency rows are joined in one matrix. In particular, the CRISPR table does not supply $S_{f3}(T_{\mathrm{position}}\mid Z)$ for translation.

\paragraph{Local no-promotion rule.}
The finite Doench contact supports only a bounded sgRNA-position-to-efficiency statement on $5310$ audited rows, with a recorded GC main correlation of $-0.0582917$, plus a separate Luo cross-protocol route on $10543$ rows. It does not imply translation realization, protein abundance, folding, physical admissibility, biological function, mechanism, phenotype, or universality. Those claims require their own layer-matched contacts and their own finite statistics. Modeled codon weights, $f_3$ residual geometry, review status, or the presence of a contact name cannot be substituted for measured ribosome occupancy, matched mRNA abundance, proteomics, structure, perturbation, or phenotype rows.
