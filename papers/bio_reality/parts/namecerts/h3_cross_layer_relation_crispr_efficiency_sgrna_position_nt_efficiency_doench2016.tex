\subsection{Position-resolved CRISPR efficiency and the blocked f\_3-stress translation route}\label{sec:bioreality-crispr-sgrna-position-nt-efficiency-doench2016}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite row surface.}
The finite record for this chapter is the Doench position-resolved sgRNA efficiency surface, treated as a sequence-design readout rather than as a translation assay.  The verified Doench run has identifier $\mathrm{b6c6363256e0}$, row count $n=5310$, runtime $24.6506$ seconds, and a recorded GC main-effect correlation $r_{\mathrm{GC}}=-0.0582917$.  A second CRISPR efficiency contact, the Luo cross-protocol comparison, records $n=10543$ rows, runtime $33.705459$ seconds, and the flag $\mathrm{cross\_protocol}=\mathrm{true}$ under run $\mathrm{2e9cce255c01}$.  These numbers are enough to support a bounded statement about finite sgRNA-position data: the object under review has actual rows and a measured nucleotide-position efficiency response, and it is not merely a symbolic packet.

\paragraph{Coordinate being tested.}
The proposed biological coordinate is not the Doench CRISPR response itself.  It is an $f_3$-stress residual coordinate on coding-sequence windows, defined only after the amino-acid quotient, local synonymous codon counts, and a wobble-weight surface have been attached.  The intended finite table has rows of the form
$$
\begin{aligned}
  x &= (c,\,q_{f_3},\,w,\,T_{\mathrm{pos}},\,m,\,Z),\\
  Z &= (\mathrm{AA\ bin},\,\mathrm{position\ bin},\,\mathrm{GC3},\,\mathrm{length},\,M\mathrm{-density},\,\mathrm{codon\ counts},\,\mathrm{baseline}).
\end{aligned}
$$
Here $c$ is an ordered coding-window coordinate, $q_{f_3}$ is the residual $f_3$ coordinate after amino-acid composition has been quotiented out, $w$ is the bounded wobble-weight context, $T_{\mathrm{pos}}$ is a same-window position-resolved ribosome-footprint or translation-efficiency readout, and $m$ is the matched mRNA abundance control.  The survival statistic is only meaningful after these fields occur in the same finite row.  In the notation used by this chapter, the admissible readout has the local form $S_{f_3}(T_{\mathrm{position}}\mid Z)$, and it is positive only if the $f_3$ term improves held-out prediction after the full control set and after simpler codon baselines are removed.

\paragraph{What the CRISPR rows do and do not supply.}
The Doench sgRNA table supplies a real finite design-response panel, but its response layer is CRISPR guide efficiency.  It does not contain same-CDS codon-window rows, wobble-weight asymmetry, matched mRNA abundance, or codon-indexed ribosome occupancy.  The observed value $r_{\mathrm{GC}}=-0.0582917$ is therefore a useful scalar describing a finite sgRNA efficiency surface, not a translation-readout survival value.  Likewise, the Luo cross-protocol row count $n=10543$ confirms a separate CRISPR efficiency comparison surface, not a position-resolved ribosome-occupancy table.  The chapter therefore treats CRISPR efficiency as a finite reality contact for guide-design position effects, while refusing to promote that contact into evidence for $f_3$-stress translation survival.

\paragraph{Break condition.}
The finite survival claim would require a joined coding-window matrix in which the same row carries $q_{f_3}$, local codon counts, a non-symmetric wobble-weight context $w_c \neq w_c^{f_3}$, matched mRNA abundance, and a position-resolved translation readout.  The relevant test is
$$
\begin{aligned}
  S_{f_3}(T_{\mathrm{position}}\mid Z) &> 0,\\
  \Delta_{\mathrm{heldout}}(q_{f_3};T_{\mathrm{position}}\mid Z) &> 0,\\
  \Delta_{\mathrm{baseline\ ablation}}(q_{f_3}) &> 0.
\end{aligned}
$$
The break condition is triggered if review readiness, contact naming, modeled tAI or stAI weights, codon-window geometry, or $S_{f_3}(T_{\mathrm{position}}\mid Z)$ notation is treated as though this matrix already exists.  At present, the supplied finite CRISPR rows do not instantiate that matrix.

\paragraph{Neighboring powered evidence.}
A separate translation-conditioned structural-order run, $\mathrm{1b97387835f7}$, records a passed status with two joined organisms, $3412$ joined rows for \emph{Escherichia coli} K-12 MG1655 and $5119$ for \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}.  Its weighted full-control $\Delta R^2$ is $0.004764742175882462$, with aggregate full-control values between $0.002688284523492457$ and $0.007880037229394898$, and a sign-flip value $p=0.0029296875$.  That result shows that some $B^*_{Q6}$ translation-conditioned surfaces can be finite and powered in a different layer.  It does not supply the missing same-window $f_3$ ribosome-occupancy rows for this CRISPR-position chapter.

\paragraph{Local conclusion.}
This chapter is ready only as a bounded BioReality record.  The Doench and Luo CRISPR efficiency tables provide finite position-resolved guide-design evidence, with $5310$ and $10543$ rows respectively, but they do not test the proposed $f_3$-stress translation readout.  No translation, folding, physical admissibility, biological function, mechanism, phenotype, protein abundance, structural order, or universal biological law follows from these rows.  Such a claim would require a separate layer-matched contact containing same-organism, same-CDS, same-window codon counts, wobble weights, matched mRNA controls, and position-resolved ribosome-footprint or explicitly bounded translation-efficiency measurements, followed by the controlled survival test above.
